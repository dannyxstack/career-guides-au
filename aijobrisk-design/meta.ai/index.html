Direct answers

AI job risk FAQ

Will AI replace most jobs?

AI is more likely to reshape task bundles than erase every role. Jobs with repetitive digital workflows face earlier pressure, while licensed, physical and high-trust work tends to retain a stronger human moat.

What does an AI risk score mean?

The score estimates occupational task exposure on a 0–100 scale. It is a comparison tool, not a prediction that a specific person will lose their job.

Which careers are safest from AI?

Careers combining physical dexterity, regulated responsibility, unpredictable environments and human trust generally rank as more resilient.
