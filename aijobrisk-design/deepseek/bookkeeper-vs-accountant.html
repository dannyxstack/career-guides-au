Frequently Asked Questions

Should a bookkeeper retrain as an accountant?

Given the accountant’s deeper human moat (6.5 vs 3.0) and higher salary ($94,750 vs $53,560), retraining makes sense for many. However, the cost (bachelor’s + CPA) and time (years) are significant. Focus on building analytical, advisory and tech skills.

Will AI eliminate bookkeeping entirely?

Not entirely, but entry-level roles (data entry, reconciliation, report generation) are highly automatable (88–95% per our illustrative task bars). Bookkeepers who move up into analysis, advisory and compliance will remain valuable.

What does the AI risk score mean?

The AI risk rating (0–10) reflects how much of the occupation’s core tasks could be performed or substantially augmented by generative AI and automation, based on task exposure research. A higher score means greater potential disruption.

Where does the data come from?

Official U.S. labour statistics (BLS OEWS, O*NET) combined with generative-AI exposure research: ILO Working Paper 140 and Eloundou et al. “GPTs are GPTs” (OpenAI). Note: the ILO paper is a working paper, not peer-reviewed.

How can I stay employable in these fields?

Build skills in QuickBooks Online, Xero, advanced Excel, Power BI, SQL and RPA basics. For accountants: Python, AI tooling (Xero AI, ACL), and industry specialisation. Focus on judgement, communication and advisory — the human moat.
