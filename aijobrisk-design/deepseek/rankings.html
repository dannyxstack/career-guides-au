Data & methodology

Occupational exposure scores are derived from ILO Working Paper 140 (Gmyrek, Berg & Bescond) and Eloundou et al. "GPTs are GPTs" (OpenAI). Salary, employment, and demand data come from official national labour statistics — for the United States: BLS OEWS and O*NET. Coverage: 6,678 occupations across 14 countries. Exposure is a percentile (0–100); labels (critical, high, moderate, low, very low) use fixed thresholds for accessibility.
