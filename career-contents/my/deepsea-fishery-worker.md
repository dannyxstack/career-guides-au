## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Malaysia?**
A: Deep-sea fishery workers in Malaysia typically earn between RM 1,500 and RM 3,500 per month, depending on experience, vessel size, and catch volume. Senior roles or those on large commercial trawlers may earn higher, often including profit-sharing or bonuses. Salaries vary by state, with East Coast states like Terengganu and Kelantan offering competitive rates.

**Q: What key skills are required to work in deep-sea fishing in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include knowledge of navigation and marine safety, proficiency in operating fishing gear (e.g., nets, lines, and traps), and the ability to work in physically demanding conditions. Basic engine maintenance and first aid are also valuable. Experience with fish identification and sustainable fishing practices is increasingly important due to regulatory requirements.

**Q: What is the career outlook for deep-sea fishery workers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable but challenging due to overfishing and stricter regulations. Demand remains steady for experienced workers, especially in commercial operations. Opportunities may decline slightly as the industry adopts technology, but skilled workers are still needed. Government initiatives promoting sustainable fishing could create jobs in monitoring and compliance roles.
