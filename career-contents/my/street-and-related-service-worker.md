## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for street and related services workers in Malaysia?**
A: Street and related services workers in Malaysia typically earn between RM 1,200 and RM 2,500 per month, depending on location, hours worked, and tips. Those in high-traffic urban areas or with specialized services (e.g., shoe shining, flower selling) may earn more. Earnings are often irregular and cash-based.

**Q: What skills are essential for street and related services workers in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include basic numeracy and literacy for handling transactions, good communication and customer service to attract and retain clients, physical stamina for long hours standing or walking, and knowledge of local languages (Malay, English, Chinese, or Tamil) to interact with diverse customers. Alertness to traffic and safety is also critical.

**Q: What is the career outlook for street and related services workers in Malaysia?**
A: Career prospects are limited, with most workers remaining in informal employment. Growth is influenced by tourism and urban foot traffic. AI and digital payments may reduce demand for some services (e.g., newspaper vending), while others (e.g., food vending) persist. Formalization efforts could improve stability but remain slow.
