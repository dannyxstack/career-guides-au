## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for employment agents and contractors in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level agents earn around RM 2,500–RM 4,000 per month, while experienced contractors or senior agents can make RM 6,000–RM 10,000 or more, often with commission. Large recruitment firms in Kuala Lumpur tend to offer higher pay.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as an employment agent in Malaysia?**
A: Strong communication, negotiation and networking abilities are essential. Agents must understand Malaysia's labour laws, recruitment technology, and candidate sourcing. Bilingual proficiency (English and Malay) and industry-specific knowledge (e.g., IT, manufacturing) also boost effectiveness.

**Q: What is the career outlook for employment agents in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to businesses' need for flexible staffing and the growth of the gig economy. Digitalisation and remote work trends create new opportunities, but competition is high. Agents with niche expertise and strong client relationships will have the best prospects.
