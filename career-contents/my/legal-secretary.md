## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a legal secretary in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level legal secretaries earn between RM 2,000 and RM 3,500 monthly. With 3-5 years of experience, salaries range from RM 3,500 to RM 5,500. Senior legal secretaries or those in larger firms can earn RM 6,000 to RM 8,000 per month. Location and firm size significantly impact pay.

**Q: What skills are essential for a legal secretary in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in legal terminology, drafting and formatting documents, and strong organizational abilities. Familiarity with e-filing systems and court procedures in Malaysia is crucial. Typing speed, attention to detail, and confidentiality are also vital. Knowledge of English and Malay is often required.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of legal secretaries in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like document drafting and transcription, reducing demand for basic administrative work. However, legal secretaries in Malaysia are increasingly focusing on higher-value tasks such as case management, client coordination, and legal research. Adaptability and tech skills are key to remain relevant in this evolving role.
