## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for secondary education teachers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, government secondary school teachers earn a starting salary of RM 2,500 to RM 3,000 per month, depending on qualifications and grade. With promotions and years of service, salaries can reach RM 5,000 to RM 8,000. Additional allowances, such as housing and cost-of-living, may apply.

**Q: What skills are essential for secondary education teachers in Malaysia?**
A: Beyond subject expertise, teachers need strong communication and classroom management skills to engage diverse students. Familiarity with the Malaysian curriculum (KSSM) and assessment methods is crucial. Digital literacy for online teaching and administrative tasks is increasingly important. Adaptability and patience are key for handling different learning paces.

**Q: What is the career outlook for secondary education teachers in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for secondary teachers remains steady, especially in STEM and Bahasa Malaysia. Government jobs offer stability and pension, but competition for permanent posts is high. Private and international schools also provide opportunities. The role is evolving with technology, requiring continuous professional development to integrate AI and digital tools effectively.
