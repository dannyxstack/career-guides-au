## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers typically earn between RM 4,000 and RM 8,000 per month, depending on experience, farm scale, and location. Senior managers in large commercial operations can earn RM 10,000 or more. Bonus structures based on production yields are common.

**Q: What key skills are required for Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of hatchery operations, water quality management, fish health and nutrition, and supply chain logistics. Strong leadership, budgeting, and problem-solving abilities are essential. Familiarity with Malaysian aquaculture regulations and sustainability practices is important. Career outlook is positive due to rising seafood demand and government support for the sector.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is increasingly used in Malaysia for monitoring water quality, feeding systems, and disease detection via sensors and cameras. This allows managers to optimize feed usage and reduce losses. However, human oversight remains crucial for decision-making and troubleshooting. Managers need to adapt to these technologies, but AI is more of a tool than a replacement.
