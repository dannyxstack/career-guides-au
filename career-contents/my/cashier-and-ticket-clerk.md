## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 2,500 for entry-level positions, with experienced workers earning up to RM 3,500. Salaries vary by location, with higher pay in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. Overtime and shift allowances may add to income.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for cashiers and ticket clerks in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes handling cash or card transactions, issuing tickets or receipts, greeting customers, answering queries, and ensuring accurate change. In retail or F&B, they may also scan items or pack purchases. In transport or event venues, they manage queues and operate ticketing systems. Shifts often cover weekends and public holidays.

**Q: How is AI affecting cashier and ticket clerk jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI and automation are introducing self-checkout kiosks and online ticketing systems, reducing demand for manual cashiers. However, in Malaysia, many smaller stores and ticket counters still rely on human staff for customer service and handling complex transactions. Roles are evolving toward multi-tasking, such as assisting with digital systems and customer support.
