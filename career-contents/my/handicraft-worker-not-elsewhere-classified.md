## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for handicraft workers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for handicraft workers in Malaysia ranges from RM1,500 to RM3,500, depending on experience, skill level, and region. Those specializing in high-demand crafts like batik or wood carving may earn more, especially if self-employed or exporting products.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a handicraft worker in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, and knowledge of traditional techniques such as batik, songket weaving, or wood carving. Creativity, basic business skills for marketing and sales, and familiarity with digital platforms for online selling are increasingly important.

**Q: How is the career outlook for handicraft workers in Malaysia, and is AI a threat?**
A: The career outlook is stable but niche, driven by tourism and cultural preservation. AI has limited direct impact on handmade crafts, but digital tools help artisans reach broader markets. Competition from mass-produced items remains a challenge, though growth opportunities exist for entrepreneurial craftspeople.
