## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level social workers and counsellors earn between RM 2,500 and RM 3,500 per month. Mid-level professionals with 3–5 years of experience typically earn RM 4,000–RM 6,000. Those in senior roles or private practice can earn RM 7,000–RM 10,000. Salaries vary by sector, with government and NGOs generally paying less than private healthcare or corporate Employee Assistance Programs.

**Q: What key skills are essential for social work and counselling professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Critical skills include active listening, empathy, cultural competence (given Malaysia’s multiethnic society), and strong verbal communication in Malay and English. Additional languages such as Mandarin or Tamil are advantageous. Clinical skills like assessment, case management, and crisis intervention are vital, along with knowledge of local laws and social welfare systems to navigate referrals and support services effectively.

**Q: What is the career outlook for social work and counselling professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Demand is growing due to increased mental health awareness, government mental health initiatives, and rising social challenges. Job opportunities exist in hospitals, schools, NGOs, government agencies, and private practice. However, competition for limited positions in top organizations can be high. The profession is less affected by AI as human empathy and judgment remain irreplaceable, though digital tools increasingly aid record-keeping and screening.
