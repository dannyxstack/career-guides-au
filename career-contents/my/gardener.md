## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a gardener in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, gardeners typically earn between RM1,200 and RM2,500 per month, depending on experience, location, and type of employer. Those working in private estates or luxury resorts may earn higher, while beginners in landscaping companies might start at the lower range. Overtime and tips can supplement income.

**Q: What key skills are required to work as a gardener in Malaysia?**
A: Gardeners in Malaysia need knowledge of tropical plants, pruning, fertilizing, and pest control. Practical skills in operating gardening tools and machinery, physical stamina, and attention to detail are essential. Basic understanding of landscape design and irrigation systems can be advantageous. Many employers value experience with local conditions.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence (AI) impacting gardening jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI-driven tools like automated irrigation systems, robotic lawn mowers, and drones for plant health monitoring are starting to assist gardeners in Malaysia. However, the role remains hands-on, requiring human judgment for delicate tasks like pruning and creative landscaping. AI is unlikely to replace gardeners but may increase efficiency and demand for tech-savvy workers.
