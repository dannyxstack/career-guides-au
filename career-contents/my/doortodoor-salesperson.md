## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for door-to-door salespersons in Malaysia?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Malaysia typically earn a base salary plus commission. Monthly income ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 4,000, with top earners exceeding RM 5,000. Pay varies by industry (e.g., telecommunications, utilities, insurance) and sales performance. Commission structures are often uncapped, incentivizing higher sales volumes.

**Q: What skills are important for success as a door-to-door salesperson in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include strong communication, persuasion, and resilience to handle rejection. Self-motivation and time management are crucial for covering assigned territories. Knowledge of local languages (e.g., Malay, Mandarin, Tamil) helps connect with diverse households. Basic numeracy for transactions and digital literacy for using sales apps are also valuable.

**Q: What is the career outlook for door-to-door salespersons in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady in sectors like telecommunications and utilities, especially in rural areas with limited digital access. However, growth is slower due to rising online sales. Skilled salespersons can advance to team leader or trainer roles. The role may evolve to include hybrid tasks like lead generation via digital tools, but face-to-face selling persists.
