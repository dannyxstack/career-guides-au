## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for survey and market research interviewers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level interviewers earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,500 per month, while experienced interviewers or team leaders can earn RM 3,000 to RM 4,500. Pay is often project-based with incentives for completed surveys. Some employers offer allowances for transportation and phone expenses.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a survey and market research interviewer in Malaysia?**
A: Interviewers spend most of their day contacting respondents via phone, online platforms, or face-to-face in public areas like malls. They read structured questionnaires, record responses accurately, and handle rejections politely. After fieldwork, they review completed forms for completeness and submit data by deadlines.

**Q: What skills are essential for survey and market research interviewers in Malaysia?**
A: Strong communication and active listening skills are crucial, especially when dealing with diverse Malaysian languages (Bahasa Malaysia, English, Chinese dialects). Patience, attention to detail, and basic computer literacy for data entry are required. Interviewers must also be confident in persuading reluctant respondents while maintaining neutrality.
