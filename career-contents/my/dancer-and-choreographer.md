## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dancers and choreographers in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience and employment type. Freelance dancers may earn RM 1,500 to RM 4,000 per month, while choreographers in commercial or production houses can earn RM 3,000 to RM 8,000. Established professionals in high-demand genres or roles with major productions may earn significantly more.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a dancer or choreographer in Malaysia?**
A: For dancers, days involve rehearsals, technique classes, and performances. Choreographers plan routines, audition dancers, and coordinate with directors. Both may teach classes part-time or work on freelance projects. Evenings and weekends often include shows or events, and schedules can be irregular.

**Q: What skills are essential for dancers and choreographers in Malaysia?**
A: Strong technical dance skills in genres like traditional, contemporary, or hip-hop are crucial. Choreographers need creativity, music interpretation, and leadership. Both benefit from physical stamina, teamwork, and networking abilities. Knowledge of local cultural dances can be an advantage for traditional performances.
