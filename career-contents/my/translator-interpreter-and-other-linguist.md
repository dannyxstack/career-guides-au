## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for translators and interpreters in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level translators earn around RM 2,500 to RM 3,500 monthly, while experienced interpreters can earn RM 5,000 to RM 8,000. Specialization in languages like Mandarin, Japanese, or Korean often commands higher pay. Freelancers may charge per word or hourly, with rates varying by project complexity.

**Q: How is AI affecting translators and interpreters in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools like machine translation assist with speed but lack cultural nuance. In Malaysia, human linguists are still essential for legal, medical, and literary work where accuracy and context matter. AI is more a productivity aid than a replacement, especially for interpreting where real-time judgment is critical.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a translator or interpreter in Malaysia?**
A: Fluency in at least two languages, including English and Bahasa Malaysia, is essential. Specialized knowledge in fields like finance, law, or technology adds value. Strong research, attention to detail, and cultural sensitivity are crucial. Certification from professional bodies like the Malaysian Translators Association is advantageous.
