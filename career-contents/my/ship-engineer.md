## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Ships’ Engineer in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level Ships’ Engineers in Malaysia earn between RM 3,500 and RM 5,500 monthly. With experience, chief engineers on ocean-going vessels can earn RM 10,000 to RM 20,000 per month, depending on vessel type and employer (e.g., offshore vs. cargo). Bonuses and allowances for sea time are common.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a Ships’ Engineer?**
A: A Ships’ Engineer performs engine room watch duties, monitors machinery, conducts preventive maintenance, and troubleshoots mechanical or electrical issues. They oversee fuel, lubrication, and cooling systems, and ensure compliance with safety and environmental regulations. Tasks vary between port stays and underway operations.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the role of Ships’ Engineers?**
A: AI-driven predictive maintenance and automated monitoring systems are becoming more common on modern ships, reducing the need for manual inspections. AI helps diagnose engine faults early and optimizes fuel efficiency. However, Ships’ Engineers are still essential for complex repairs, decision-making, and ensuring system reliability, so AI supplements rather than replaces their expertise.
