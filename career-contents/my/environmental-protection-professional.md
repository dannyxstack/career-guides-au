## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental protection professionals in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level environmental protection professionals earn around RM 2,500–4,000 monthly. Mid-level with 3–5 years experience earn RM 4,500–7,000, while senior roles can reach RM 8,000–12,000. Salaries vary by sector (government, consulting, industry) and location, with higher pay in KL and Penang.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for an environmental protection professional in Malaysia?**
A: Daily tasks include conducting environmental impact assessments, monitoring pollution levels, ensuring regulatory compliance with Department of Environment (DOE) standards, preparing reports, and advising on sustainable practices. They often work on projects like water treatment, waste management, and air quality control for industries such as oil & gas and manufacturing.

**Q: How is AI impacting environmental protection careers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is enhancing data analysis for pollution tracking, predictive modeling for climate impact, and automating routine monitoring. However, human expertise remains crucial for policy interpretation and on-site inspections. Professionals need to upskill in GIS, remote sensing, and data analytics to stay relevant, but AI is not replacing core environmental judgment.
