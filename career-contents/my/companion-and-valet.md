## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for companions and valets in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Malaysia vary based on experience and employer. Entry-level positions earn around RM1,800 to RM2,500 monthly, while experienced individuals can earn RM3,500 to RM6,000, especially with high-net-worth clients or luxury hotels. Additional tips and benefits may increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the main duties and required skills for a companion or valet in Malaysia?**
A: Duties include managing schedules, running errands, driving, and personal care. Essential skills are discretion, excellent communication, time management, and driving proficiency. Familiarity with local customs and etiquette in Malaysia is valued. A professional demeanor, reliability, and strong organizational abilities are critical for success.

**Q: How is technology or AI affecting the role of companions and valets in Malaysia?**
A: AI and automation have minimal direct impact on this role, as it relies on personal interaction and adaptability. However, digital tools for scheduling and communication are used. The demand remains stable among affluent clients who value human touch and personalized service. Upskilling in modern etiquette and tech literacy can enhance career prospects.
