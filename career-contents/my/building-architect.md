## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building architect in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level architects earn around RM 36,000–RM 48,000 annually, while experienced professionals with 5–10 years earn RM 60,000–RM 90,000. Senior architects or those in managerial roles can earn RM 100,000–RM 150,000 or more. Salary varies by location, firm size, and project complexity.

**Q: What is the career outlook for building architects in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady, driven by urban development, infrastructure projects, and the need for sustainable design. Architects with expertise in green building and BIM have strong prospects. Competition is moderate, and the profession is regulated by the Board of Architects Malaysia (LAM). Continuing professional development is essential.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of building architects in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools like generative design and BIM automation streamline drafting and analysis, allowing architects to focus on creative and strategic tasks. However, AI does not replace human judgment in aesthetics, cultural context, or client interaction. Architects who adapt to AI-enhanced workflows will gain efficiency and remain competitive.
