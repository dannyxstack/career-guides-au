## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for structural metal preparers and erectors in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level structural metal preparers and erectors in Malaysia typically earn between RM1,800 and RM2,500 per month. Experienced workers can earn RM3,000 to RM4,500 monthly, depending on the employer, project location, and certifications. Overtime and allowances may increase total compensation.

**Q: What key skills are required for structural metal work in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include reading blueprints, welding, operating hoists and cutting equipment, and knowledge of safety regulations. Physical strength, teamwork, and the ability to work at heights are crucial. Many employers prefer candidates with a Sijil Kemahiran Malaysia (SKM) in related fields.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the role of structural metal erectors in Malaysia?**
A: AI is not expected to replace manual metal erection in Malaysia soon, but it may improve design and prefabrication, reducing on-site adjustments. Workers can benefit from upskilling in computer-aided design and robotic welding systems as the industry gradually adopts smart construction technologies.
