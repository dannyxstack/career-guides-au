## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for forestry labourers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, forestry labourers typically earn between RM1,200 and RM2,000 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage, while experienced workers in remote areas or with specialized skills can earn more. Overtime and allowances may supplement base pay.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a forestry labourer in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes tree planting, thinning, and harvesting using chainsaws and hand tools. Labourers clear undergrowth, maintain trails, and assist in reforestation projects. Work is physically demanding, often in remote jungle areas, and requires teamwork to ensure safety and efficiency under supervision of a foreman.

**Q: What key skills are needed to work as a forestry labourer in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include physical stamina and strength for heavy lifting and long hours in humid conditions. Basic knowledge of tree species and safe chainsaw operation is important. Communication skills for team coordination and ability to follow safety protocols are also required. Many employers provide on-the-job training.
