- Using GPS and drones for forest mapping and boundary marking

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry workers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level forestry workers earn around RM1,500 to RM2,500 per month, while experienced workers or supervisors can earn RM3,000 to RM5,000. Salaries vary by region, employer (government vs private), and specific role (e.g., timber extraction vs conservation). Overtime and field allowances may apply.

**Q: What is the career outlook for forestry workers in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to ongoing reforestation, sustainable forestry practices, and conservation projects by government agencies and private plantations. Workers with skills in tree planting, forest maintenance, and pest control are needed. However, automation in logging may reduce some roles, while eco-tourism and carbon credit projects create new opportunities.

**Q: What skills are essential for forestry workers in Malaysia?**
A: Physical stamina and fitness are crucial for tasks like planting, climbing, and carrying equipment. Knowledge of local tree species, safe use of chainsaws and other tools, basic first aid, and navigation in forests are important. Familiarity with sustainable harvesting methods and environmental regulations is increasingly valued. On-the-job training is common.
