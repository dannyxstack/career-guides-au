## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries vary by specialty and experience. Entry-level positions start around RM 3,000 per month, while experienced professionals in senior or specialized roles can earn RM 8,000 or more. Public sector pay is lower but includes benefits, whereas private hospitals and clinics often offer higher salaries.

**Q: What key skills are required for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include strong clinical knowledge specific to your field (e.g., audiology, dietetics), good communication for patient education, empathy, and teamwork with other healthcare providers. Proficiency in English and Malay is important, and familiarity with local health regulations and electronic health records is increasingly valued.

**Q: What is the career outlook for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: Demand is growing due to Malaysia's aging population and rising chronic diseases. Opportunities exist in hospitals, clinics, community health centers, and private practice. Specialized roles like audiologists and dietitians are in short supply, offering good prospects. Continuing professional development is key for advancement.
