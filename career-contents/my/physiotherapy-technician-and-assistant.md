## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a physiotherapy technician/assistant in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level physiotherapy technicians and assistants earn around RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 per month. With a few years of experience, salaries can rise to RM 3,000–RM 4,000. Those in private hospitals or specialized clinics may earn higher, while public healthcare salaries are generally lower but include benefits.

**Q: What skills are essential for a physiotherapy technician/assistant in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of basic anatomy and physiology, proficiency in therapeutic exercises and manual therapy techniques, good communication for patient interaction, and the ability to follow instructions from physiotherapists. Additionally, patience, empathy, and physical stamina are important for assisting patients with mobility exercises.

**Q: What is the career outlook for physiotherapy technicians/assistants in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for physiotherapy assistants is growing due to Malaysia's aging population and increased awareness of rehabilitation. Opportunities exist in hospitals, clinics, and sports centers. However, advancement may require further education to become a full physiotherapist. The role is relatively stable with moderate growth expected.
