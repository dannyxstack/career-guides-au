## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a valuer or loss assessor in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level valuers and loss assessors in Malaysia earn around RM 2,500 to RM 4,000 monthly. Mid-level professionals with 3-5 years of experience can expect RM 4,500 to RM 7,000, while senior or chartered valuers may earn RM 8,000 to RM 15,000 or more. Salaries vary by location, firm size, and specialization, with commercial and industrial valuations often paying higher.

**Q: What skills are essential for a career as a valuer or loss assessor in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include strong analytical and numerical abilities for property valuation, knowledge of Malaysian valuation standards and regulations, and proficiency in GIS and valuation software. Attention to detail, report writing, and communication skills are crucial for client interactions. Membership with the Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers in Malaysia (BOVAEA) is also important for career advancement.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the valuer and loss assessor profession in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating data collection and preliminary analysis, such as market comparisons and damage assessments, improving efficiency. However, it cannot replace professional judgment, site inspections, or complex valuations requiring local expertise. Valuers must adapt by leveraging AI tools for accuracy while focusing on high-level advisory roles. Continuous learning in data analytics is increasingly valuable.
