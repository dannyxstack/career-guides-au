## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for transport conductors in Malaysia?**
A: Transport conductors in Malaysia earn between RM1,500 and RM3,000 per month, depending on experience and employer. Those working for major public transport operators in cities like Kuala Lumpur may receive higher pay, including allowances and overtime. Entry-level conductors typically start at the lower end of this range.

**Q: What does a transport conductor do on a typical day?**
A: A transport conductor's daily tasks include checking passengers' tickets or passes, assisting with boarding and alighting, announcing stops, ensuring passenger safety, and handling fare disputes. They also maintain order inside the vehicle and may assist passengers with disabilities. The role requires constant interaction with the public and working in shifts.

**Q: What skills are required to become a transport conductor in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills for transport conductors in Malaysia include good communication and interpersonal skills, basic numeracy for handling fares, ability to work under pressure, and knowledge of local routes. Fluency in Bahasa Malaysia and English is advantageous. A secondary school certificate is usually required, with on-the-job training provided.
