## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fitness and recreation instructors in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level fitness instructors earn around RM 2,000 to RM 3,000 per month, while experienced professionals or those managing programmes can earn RM 4,500 to RM 7,000. Income varies by location, type of employer (gyms, resorts, community centres), and specialisation (e.g., yoga, personal training).

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a fitness instructor in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes leading group exercise classes, personal training sessions, designing workout plans, conducting fitness assessments, and ensuring equipment safety. Instructors also update client progress, promote classes via social media, and may travel between multiple studios or outdoor venues. Administrative tasks like scheduling and handling enquiries are common.

**Q: How is AI impacting fitness and recreation instruction jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI-powered apps and wearable tech now provide personalised workout plans and virtual coaching, but human instructors remain essential for motivation, form correction, and creating engaging social environments. AI may reduce demand for basic programme delivery but enhances roles focused on individualised coaching and community building, requiring instructors to adapt and leverage technology.
