## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a freight handler in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for freight handlers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 2,800, depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers may start at around RM 1,200, while experienced handlers can earn up to RM 3,500. Overtime pay and shift allowances are common.

**Q: What skills are essential for freight handlers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills for freight handlers in Malaysia include physical stamina, attention to detail, and basic numeracy. Familiarity with warehouse equipment like forklifts and pallet jacks is advantageous. Good communication and teamwork are essential due to coordination with drivers and warehouse staff. Basic English and Malay proficiency helps.

**Q: How is AI affecting freight handling jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI and automation are impacting freight handling in Malaysia through warehouse robotics and smart inventory systems. While some repetitive tasks may be automated, human handlers are still needed for quality checks and complex logistics. Upskilling in technology can improve job security.
