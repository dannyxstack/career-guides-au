## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Sales Professional in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level ICT Sales Professionals can expect RM 36,000 to RM 54,000 annually, while mid-career professionals earn between RM 60,000 and RM 96,000. Senior roles or those with strong performance records can reach RM 120,000 or more, often with commission or bonuses.

**Q: What key skills are required for ICT Sales Professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include strong technical knowledge of IT products and services, excellent communication and negotiation abilities, and proficiency in CRM software. Familiarity with cloud computing, cybersecurity, and digital solutions is increasingly valued for engaging with Malaysian businesses.

**Q: What is the career outlook for ICT Sales Professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains robust as Malaysian enterprises undergo digital transformation. Opportunities exist in software vendors, telecommunications, and IT services. AI tools are enhancing lead generation and customer insights, but human relationship-building skills remain critical. Professionals who adapt to evolving tech and sales methodologies will thrive.
