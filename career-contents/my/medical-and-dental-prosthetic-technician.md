## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around RM2,000 to RM3,000 monthly, while experienced professionals can earn RM5,000 to RM7,000. Salaries vary based on location, employer (private clinics tend to pay higher than public hospitals), and specialization in complex prosthetics like implant-supported restorations.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as a medical and dental prosthetic technician?**
A: Proficiency in CAD/CAM software and 3D printing is essential for modern digital workflows. Meticulous manual dexterity for finishing and fitting prostheses, attention to detail in color matching, and strong communication with dentists are crucial. A diploma in dental technology or related field is typically required.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence (AI) affecting the role of dental prosthetic technicians in Malaysia?**
A: AI enhances design automation and quality control but does not replace technicians. AI-powered tools assist in creating digital impressions and optimizing restorations, reducing manual drafting time. However, hands-on skills for final adjustments, patient-specific fitting, and aesthetic finishing remain irreplaceable. Technicians who embrace AI tools will have improved productivity and career growth.
