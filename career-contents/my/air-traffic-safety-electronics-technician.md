## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around RM 2,500 to RM 3,500 per month, while experienced ones can earn RM 5,000 to RM 7,000. Those with specialized certifications or working at major airports may earn higher. Salaries in the private sector are generally more competitive than in government agencies.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to the expansion of Malaysia's aviation sector, including new airports and upgrades to air traffic management systems. Technicians are in steady demand, especially those skilled in modern radar and communication technologies. Career progression can lead to senior technician, supervisor, or system engineer roles.

**Q: What skills and qualifications are required to become an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Malaysia?**
A: A diploma or degree in electronics, electrical engineering, or a related field is typically required. Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and communication skills are essential. Familiarity with air navigation systems (e.g., radar, VHF, ILS) is important. Certifications from the Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia (CAAM) or similar bodies enhance employability.
