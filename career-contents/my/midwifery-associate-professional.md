## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for midwifery associate professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level midwifery associates earn around RM 2,000 to RM 3,000 per month in government hospitals, while those in private practice can earn RM 3,500 to RM 5,000. Senior roles with experience may reach RM 6,000 monthly, depending on location and employer.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a midwifery associate professional in Malaysia?**
A: You typically need a Diploma in Midwifery from a recognized institution and registration with the Malaysian Nursing Board. Key skills include clinical competency in antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care, strong communication, empathy, and the ability to handle emergencies in maternal and newborn health.

**Q: What is the career outlook for midwifery associate professionals in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to ongoing government emphasis on reducing maternal and infant mortality and expanding rural healthcare access. Opportunities exist in public hospitals, community clinics, and private maternity centers. Role advancement includes positions as senior midwives, educators, or supervisors with further training.
