## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level technicians earn around RM 2,500–RM 3,500 monthly. With 3–5 years of experience, salaries range from RM 4,000 to RM 6,000. Senior roles or those with specialized certifications (e.g., CCNA) can reach RM 7,000–RM 9,000. Salaries vary by location, with Kuala Lumpur and Selangor offering higher pay.

**Q: What key skills are required to become a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Malaysia?**
A: Employers typically seek proficiency in network configuration (Cisco, Huawei), troubleshooting, and knowledge of operating systems (Windows Server, Linux). Certifications like CCNA, CompTIA Network+, or Huawei HCIA are valued. Soft skills such as problem-solving and teamwork are also essential. Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) is increasingly important.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the role of Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating routine monitoring and fault detection, allowing technicians to focus on complex issues and network optimization. Tools using AI for predictive maintenance are emerging. However, human oversight remains critical for security and adaptability. Technicians who upskill in AI and automation tools will find enhanced career opportunities in Malaysia.
