- Generating preliminary diagnostic suggestions based on symptoms and tongue/pulse images.

- Automated responses for appointment management and patient inquiries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis: Use image recognition to analyze tongue coating, complexion, etc., to assist in syndrome differentiation.
- Personalized treatment plans: recommend optimized herbal combinations or acupuncture plans based on big data.
- Knowledge retrieval: quickly obtain the latest research literature and classic cases

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Practitioners in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and setting. Entry-level practitioners earn around RM 2,500 to RM 4,000 monthly. With 5+ years, salaries range from RM 5,000 to RM 8,000. Those in private practice or specialized fields can earn higher, especially in major cities. Government positions offer fixed scales with benefits.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Professional in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes patient consultations, diagnosing using traditional methods like pulse diagnosis or tongue assessment, prescribing herbal remedies or acupuncture treatments, and maintaining patient records. Many professionals also educate patients on lifestyle modifications and may manage their own clinic schedules, balancing administrative tasks with hands-on care.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Professionals in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to growing interest in holistic health and government recognition through the Traditional and Complementary Medicine Act 2016. Demand is rising in urban centers, with opportunities in private clinics, wellness centers, and hospitals. However, competition exists, and ongoing professional development and registration with the T&CM Council are important for career progression.
