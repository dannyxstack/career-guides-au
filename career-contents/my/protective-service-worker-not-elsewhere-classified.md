## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a security guard in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a security guard in Malaysia ranges from RM1,200 to RM2,500. Factors such as location, experience, and employer type influence pay. Licensed guards with additional training in areas like first aid or fire safety may earn higher wages.

**Q: What skills are required for a protective services worker in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include physical fitness, alertness, good communication, and basic first aid. Knowledge of security equipment, report writing, and conflict resolution are also important. For higher-level roles, surveillance techniques and crowd management skills are beneficial.

**Q: How is AI affecting protective services jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI enhances surveillance and threat detection through automated monitoring systems. However, human judgment and response remain essential. Workers need to adapt by learning to operate AI-driven tools, but the demand for personnel is unlikely to decrease significantly.
