## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building frame and related trades worker in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level workers earn around RM 2,000 per month. Experienced workers can earn RM 3,000 to RM 4,000, with senior roles reaching RM 5,000 or more. Salaries vary by region, employer, and project complexity.

**Q: What skills are important for building frame workers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in using hand and power tools, reading blueprints, and knowledge of construction materials and safety practices. Physical stamina, teamwork, and attention to detail are essential. Certification in scaffolding or formwork can improve job prospects.

**Q: What is the career outlook for building frame workers in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to ongoing infrastructure and housing projects. Opportunities exist in both public and private sectors. With experience, workers can advance to supervisory roles or specialize in areas like steel fixing or concrete formwork.
