## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Malaysia?**
A: Income varies widely by location and scale, but most earn between RM 800 and RM 2,000 monthly from surplus sales. Earnings are supplemented by own consumption. Government subsidies and support programs help stabilize income, though many farmers rely on multiple income sources.

**Q: What skills are essential for a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of local crop cultivation (paddy, vegetables, fruits) and animal husbandry (chickens, goats, cows), pest and disease management, basic financial literacy for budgeting and market sales, and physical stamina. Adaptability to weather and sustainable practices are also important.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable but challenging due to urbanization and climate change. Demand for local organic produce is rising, and government initiatives support farm modernization and agro-tourism. AI impact is currently limited, but precision agriculture tools may become relevant for efficiency gains.
