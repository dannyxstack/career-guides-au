## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for weaving and knitting machine operators in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,000 per month, while experienced operators can earn between RM 2,500 and RM 4,000. Overtime and shift allowances are common in textile factories, especially in major manufacturing hubs like Penang and Johor.

**Q: What skills are essential for weaving and knitting machine operators in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include machine setup, monitoring yarn tension, and basic troubleshooting of common faults like thread breaks or needle issues. Operators must be detail-oriented to detect defects early. Familiarity with different fabric types and safety protocols is also important. Many employers provide on-the-job training.

**Q: What is the career outlook for weaving and knitting machine operators in Malaysia?**
A: The textile industry in Malaysia is stable but increasingly automated. Operators with skills in modern computerized machines have better prospects. While manual roles may decline, demand for operators who can maintain and oversee automated systems is expected to remain steady. Advancement to supervisor or technician roles is possible with experience.
