- Manage raw material inventory and order tracking
- Perform repetitive grinding, polishing, and other finishing operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for handicraft workers in textile and leather in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level handicraft workers earn around RM1,500–RM2,000 per month, while experienced artisans can make RM3,000–RM5,000. Those specializing in high-end batik, songket, or leather goods may earn more through commissions or self-employment. Salaries vary by region, with higher rates in Kuala Lumpur and Penang.

**Q: What skills are essential for handicraft workers in textile and leather in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include hand-sewing, weaving, batik painting, leather cutting, and embroidery. Knowledge of traditional Malaysian techniques like songket weaving or batik tulis is highly valued. Attention to detail, creativity, and patience are crucial. Basic business skills help those selling their own products. Training is often through apprenticeships or craft centers.

**Q: What is the career outlook for handicraft workers in textile and leather in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to tourism and cultural preservation, but competition from mass-produced goods limits growth. Artisans who produce unique, high-quality items or collaborate with fashion designers have better prospects. Government initiatives like crafts expos and online marketplaces offer new opportunities. Automation has minimal impact as handcrafted details are still prized.
