## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level mechanics earn around RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 monthly. With 3–5 years of experience, salaries range from RM 3,000 to RM 5,000. Senior or specialized technicians can earn RM 6,000 to RM 9,000. Overtime and commission from service calls can increase total pay.

**Q: What skills and certifications are required to work as an air conditioning mechanic in Malaysia?**
A: A minimum SPM or vocational certificate in air conditioning/refrigeration is needed. Practical skills in diagnosing, repairing, and installing systems are essential. Knowledge of refrigerant handling and electrical circuits is required. Certifications like the Malaysia Board of Technologists (MBOT) registration or SKM (Sijil Kemahiran Malaysia) are advantageous.

**Q: What is the career outlook for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains strong due to Malaysia's tropical climate and growing construction sector. Opportunities exist in residential, commercial, and industrial maintenance. With experience, mechanics can advance to supervisor roles or start their own business. AI may automate diagnostics but hands-on repair skills will still be needed.
