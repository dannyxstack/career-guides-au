## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Social Work Associate Professional in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level social work associates in Malaysia typically earn between RM 2,000 and RM 3,000 per month. With experience and specialization, salaries can rise to RM 3,500–RM 5,000, particularly in NGOs, government agencies, or private healthcare. Senior roles or those with advanced qualifications may command higher pay.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Social Work Associate Professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for social work professionals is growing in Malaysia due to increased focus on mental health and social welfare. Opportunities exist in NGOs, the Department of Social Welfare, hospitals, and community centres. Career progression includes supervisory roles or further education leading to senior positions.

**Q: What skills are needed to succeed as a Social Work Associate Professional in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include empathy, communication, problem-solving, and cultural sensitivity. Proficiency in Bahasa Malaysia and English is crucial. Organizational abilities and knowledge of local social policies and resources are also important. Training in counselling or case management enhances effectiveness.
