## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for police inspectors and detectives in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries for police inspectors and detectives in Malaysia vary by rank and experience. Entry-level inspectors earn around RM 4,000 to RM 5,000 per month, while senior detectives with over 10 years of service may earn RM 8,000 to RM 12,000 monthly. Allowances for risk, housing, and shift work are common, increasing total compensation.

**Q: What skills are essential for a police inspector or detective in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, effective communication, and physical fitness. Proficiency in Malay and English is important, as is knowledge of Malaysian criminal law. Investigative techniques, evidence handling, and interview skills are critical. Adaptability and teamwork are also highly valued in dynamic policing environments.

**Q: What is the career outlook for police inspectors and detectives in Malaysia?**
A: Career prospects are stable, with opportunities for promotion through the PDRM ranks. Inspectors can advance to Assistant Superintendent, Senior Assistant Commissioner, or specialize in divisions like CID, Narcotics, or Commercial Crime. Demand remains steady due to public safety needs, and technology is increasingly integrated into investigations.
