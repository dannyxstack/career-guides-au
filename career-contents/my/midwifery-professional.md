## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwife in Malaysia?**
A: Midwives in Malaysia earn between RM 2,500 and RM 5,000 per month in public hospitals, depending on experience and qualifications. Private hospitals may offer RM 3,500 to RM 7,000. Additional allowances for night shifts and overtime can increase total pay.

**Q: What key skills are essential for midwifery professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include clinical expertise in antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care, along with emergency obstetric management. Strong communication in Malay and English, cultural sensitivity, empathy, and teamwork are vital. Proficiency with medical equipment and electronic health records is also required.

**Q: What is the career outlook for midwives in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to sustained government focus on maternal health and declining rural access. Opportunities exist in public hospitals, Klinik Kesihatan, and private facilities. Career advancement includes senior midwife, matron, or educator roles. Specialization in neonatal care or midwifery education enhances prospects.
