## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for insulation workers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, insulation workers earn between RM 1,800 and RM 3,500 per month, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around RM 1,800, while skilled workers with certifications can earn up to RM 4,000. Overtime and project-based bonuses may increase total pay.

**Q: What skills are required to become an insulation worker in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of insulation materials (fiberglass, foam, mineral wool), ability to read blueprints, and proficiency with hand and power tools. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and safety awareness are essential. Certification from Malaysian vocational training institutes (e.g., CIDB) is often preferred.

**Q: What is the career outlook for insulation workers in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for insulation workers is steady, driven by construction and industrial projects in Malaysia. Growing focus on energy efficiency and green building standards supports job growth. However, automation in manufacturing may affect some roles. Skilled workers with experience in diverse materials have good prospects.
