## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shelf filler in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a shelf filler in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,200 to RM 1,800, depending on the retailer, location, and experience. Entry-level positions typically start at RM 1,200, while those with more experience or working in larger stores may earn up to RM 1,800. Full-time roles often include overtime pay and benefits.

**Q: What skills are important for a shelf filler in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail for accurate stock arrangement, physical stamina for standing and lifting, basic numeracy for stock counts, and time management to replenish shelves efficiently. Customer service skills are also valued in Malaysian retail, as shelf fillers may assist shoppers. Familiarity with barcode scanners or inventory systems is a plus.

**Q: How is automation affecting shelf filler jobs in Malaysia?**
A: While automation is increasing in Malaysian retail, its impact on shelf filler jobs remains limited. Tasks like stock replenishment still require human dexterity and decision-making. However, automated inventory tracking and robotic stockers are being tested in larger chains. Most shelf filler roles remain secure for now, but workers may benefit from upskilling in technology use.
