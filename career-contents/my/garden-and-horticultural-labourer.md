## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level garden and horticultural labourers in Malaysia earn between RM1,200 and RM1,500 per month. With experience and specialization, wages can rise to RM2,000 or more. Rates vary by region, employer, and type of work (e.g., landscaping vs. nursery). Some positions include accommodation or allowances.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a garden and horticultural labourer in Malaysia?**
A: Physical stamina, ability to work outdoors in tropical heat, and basic knowledge of plants, soil, and gardening tools are essential. Labourers should be familiar with pruning, weeding, planting, and using equipment like lawnmowers and trimmers. Safety awareness and teamwork are also important.

**Q: How is AI affecting garden and horticultural labourers in Malaysia?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact on manual labour in Malaysian horticulture. While robotic mowers and automated irrigation systems are emerging, they mainly assist rather than replace workers. Labourers remain essential for tasks requiring judgment, dexterity, and handling diverse plant care needs. The demand for human labour is expected to persist.
