## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Inquiry Clerk in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, an Inquiry Clerk typically earns between RM 1,800 and RM 3,000 per month, depending on experience, location, and industry. Entry-level positions start around RM 1,500, while senior clerks with specialized knowledge can earn up to RM 3,500. Benefits such as overtime pay and allowances may also be included.

**Q: What skills are essential for an Inquiry Clerk in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include strong verbal and written communication in English and Bahasa Malaysia, proficiency in office software (e.g., Microsoft Office), and the ability to handle high call volumes professionally. Multilingual ability (e.g., Mandarin) is a plus. Attention to detail, patience, and problem-solving skills are also important for managing customer inquiries effectively.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Inquiry Clerks in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for Inquiry Clerks remains steady in sectors like customer service, banking, and telecommunications. Automation and AI may streamline routine tasks, but human interaction is still valued for complex inquiries. Career progression can lead to senior clerk, team leader, or customer service manager roles. Digital skills will be increasingly important for future opportunities.
