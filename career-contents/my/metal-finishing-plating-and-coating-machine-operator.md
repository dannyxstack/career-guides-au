## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for metal finishing machine operators in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,000 per month, while experienced operators can earn RM 2,500 to RM 3,500. Shift allowances and overtime are common in manufacturing hubs like Penang and Johor. Salaries vary by company size and industry specialization.

**Q: What skills are required to operate metal finishing machines in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail, basic math, and mechanical aptitude. Operators must read process sheets, handle chemicals safely, and monitor machine parameters. Certifications in occupational safety and plating technology, such as from the Malaysian Plating Association, enhance employability.

**Q: How is automation affecting metal finishing jobs in Malaysia?**
A: Automation is handling repetitive tasks like loading/unloading parts and controlling plating baths. However, operators remain essential for machine setup, quality checks, troubleshooting, and process optimization. Upskilling in CNC programming or robotics can help workers adapt to evolving roles.
