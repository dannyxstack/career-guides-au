## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for database and network professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level professionals earn between RM 3,500 and RM 5,000 monthly. With 3–5 years of experience, salaries rise to RM 6,000–RM 9,000. Senior roles with certifications like Oracle or Cisco can reach RM 12,000–RM 18,000, depending on industry and company size.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence changing the work of database and network professionals in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like performance tuning, backup management, and network monitoring. However, professionals are needed for strategic planning, security oversight, and handling complex incidents. Upskilling in AI and machine learning is becoming valuable to stay relevant in evolving roles.

**Q: What is the career outlook for database and network professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains strong due to digitalization across banking, e-commerce, and telecommunications. Cloud adoption and data-driven decision-making create new opportunities. Professionals with cloud certifications (AWS, Azure) and cybersecurity skills will have the best prospects over the next five years.
