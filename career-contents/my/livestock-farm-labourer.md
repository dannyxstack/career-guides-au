## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,200 to RM 1,800, depending on the type of livestock, farm size, and location. Overtime and accommodation may be provided. Entry-level positions start around RM 1,000, while experienced workers may earn up to RM 2,200.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a livestock farm labourer in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for manual tasks like feeding, cleaning, and handling animals. Basic knowledge of animal health and hygiene is important. Familiarity with farm tools and machinery, as well as the ability to follow safety protocols, is required. Communication skills and teamwork are also valuable.

**Q: What is the career outlook for livestock farm labourers in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for livestock farm labourers remains steady due to Malaysia's growing agriculture sector. While automation is increasing, manual labour is still needed for animal care. Career progression can lead to supervisory roles or specialized positions in breeding or animal health. The outlook is stable with opportunities in large-scale poultry, swine, and cattle farms.
