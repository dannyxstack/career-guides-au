## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sociologists and anthropologists in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from RM 2,500 to RM 3,500 per month. With experience in government or research institutions, pay increases to RM 4,500–RM 7,000. Senior roles or consulting can earn RM 8,000–RM 12,000 or more, depending on employer and qualifications.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the work of sociologists and anthropologists in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools assist with data analysis and pattern recognition, enabling professionals to handle larger datasets efficiently. However, human interpretation and cultural sensitivity remain essential. AI cannot replace fieldwork and qualitative insights, so a blend of technical and traditional skills is required.

**Q: What key skills are required for sociologists and anthropologists in Malaysia today?**
A: Strong qualitative and quantitative research abilities, cultural awareness, and proficiency in Malay and English are essential. Familiarity with data analysis software (e.g., SPSS, NVivo) is increasingly important. Critical thinking, report writing, and interpersonal skills are vital for fieldwork and community engagement.
