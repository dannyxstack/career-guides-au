**Q: How is AI impacting the day-to-day work of engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools automate routine design, simulation, and data analysis tasks, allowing engineers to focus on complex problem-solving. Professionals need digital literacy and adaptability to use AI-driven software. Roles requiring creativity and strategic thinking remain resilient, while purely manual tasks are increasingly automated.

**Q: What is the career outlook for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
