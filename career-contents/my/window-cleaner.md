## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for a window cleaner in Malaysia?**
A: Window cleaners in Malaysia earn between RM 1,500 and RM 3,500 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Those working for professional cleaning companies in major cities like Kuala Lumpur or Penang may earn higher wages, while freelance cleaners set their own rates, often per job.

**Q: What skills are required to become a window cleaner in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include physical fitness, comfort with heights, attention to detail, and basic knowledge of cleaning equipment and safety procedures. No formal education is needed, but on-the-job training is typical. For high-rise buildings, certification in working at heights or rope access is often required.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting window cleaning in Malaysia?**
A: AI is gradually introducing automated window cleaning systems, such as drones and robotic cleaners, especially for high-rise buildings. However, human cleaners remain essential for complex tasks and smaller premises. AI may reduce demand for routine jobs but creates opportunities for technicians who maintain these systems.
