## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships' deck crews in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level deck crew, such as ordinary seamen, earn between RM 1,500 and RM 2,500 per month. Experienced able seamen or bosuns can earn RM 3,000 to RM 5,000, depending on vessel type and employer. Additional sea-time allowances and overtime are common.

**Q: What skills and certifications are needed for deck crew jobs in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include basic seamanship, safety procedures, cargo handling, and watchkeeping. Communication in English and Malay is important. Mandatory certifications include STCW Basic Safety Training, and for some roles, an Able Seaman certificate. Physical fitness and teamwork are also required.

**Q: What is the career outlook for ships' deck crews in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for deck crew remains stable due to Malaysia's active shipping and offshore oil & gas sectors. Career progression includes roles like able seaman, boatswain, or deck officer through additional training. While automation affects some tasks, hands-on maritime roles remain essential.
