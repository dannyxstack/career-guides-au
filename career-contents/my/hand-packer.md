## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hand packers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for hand packers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,200 to RM 1,800, depending on experience, location, and company. Entry-level positions may start at around RM 1,100, while those in manufacturing hubs like Penang or Selangor might earn slightly more. Overtime pay is common and can boost overall earnings.

**Q: What skills are essential for hand packers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail, manual dexterity, and the ability to work efficiently under time pressure. Basic numeracy for counting and measuring, as well as physical stamina for standing and lifting, are important. Knowledge of safety procedures and teamwork skills are also valued. Prior experience in packing or a production environment is beneficial but not always required.

**Q: What is the career outlook for hand packers in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for hand packers remains steady, especially in manufacturing, food processing, and logistics sectors. However, automation and AI are gradually reducing the need for manual packing in some large factories. Entry-level positions are available, but career progression opportunities are limited without additional skills. Upskilling in machine operation or quality control can improve prospects.
