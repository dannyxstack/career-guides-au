## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for motorcycle drivers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for motorcycle drivers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 2,800, depending on experience and location. Entry-level drivers earn around RM 1,200 to RM 1,800, while those with delivery-based roles may earn more through commissions or per-delivery fees, especially in major cities like Kuala Lumpur and Penang.

**Q: What skills are essential for a motorcycle driver in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include a valid motorcycle license (B2 full license), good road safety knowledge, and strong navigation abilities using GPS apps. Physical stamina is important for long hours on the road, and basic spoken English or Malay helps when communicating with customers or dispatchers. Familiarity with local traffic rules and efficient route planning are also valued.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting motorcycle driving jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI is not significantly replacing motorcycle drivers in Malaysia yet. However, delivery platforms use AI to optimize routes and match orders, which can increase efficiency and earning potential for drivers. Autonomous vehicle technology is still far from affecting motorcycle roles. Instead, AI tools like navigation apps assist drivers, making their work easier rather than obsolete.
