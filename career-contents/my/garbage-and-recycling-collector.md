## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for garbage and recycling collectors in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, garbage and recycling collectors typically earn between RM 1,500 and RM 2,500 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around RM 1,200, while experienced workers can earn higher with overtime or allowances. Salaries vary between private companies and municipal bodies.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a garbage and recycling collector in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for lifting and handling waste, ability to operate collection vehicles, teamwork, punctuality, and basic safety knowledge. Communication skills help coordinate with team members and the public. No formal education is required, but a valid driving license is often needed for vehicle operation.

**Q: How is automation affecting garbage and recycling collection in Malaysia?**
A: Automation in waste collection is still limited in Malaysia, with most municipalities relying on manual labor due to infrastructure and cost constraints. AI and robotics are unlikely to replace collectors soon, though some sorting facilities use automated systems. Demand for human collectors remains stable, especially in residential areas.
