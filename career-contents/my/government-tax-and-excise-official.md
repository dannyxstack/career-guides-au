## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for Government Tax and Excise Officials in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level Tax and Excise Officials in Malaysia typically earn between RM2,500 and RM4,000 per month, depending on qualifications and location. Mid-career officials with 5-10 years of experience can earn RM5,000 to RM8,000 monthly. Senior positions may reach RM10,000 or more, with additional allowances and benefits under the civil service scheme.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a Government Tax and Excise Official in Malaysia?**
A: Day-to-day tasks include auditing tax returns, inspecting excise goods, verifying compliance with tax laws, and conducting investigations on potential tax evasion. Officials also process refunds, issue permits, and advise taxpayers on legal obligations. Field visits to businesses and warehouses for audits or inspections are common, alongside administrative reporting.

**Q: How is AI expected to affect the role of Tax and Excise Officials in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating data analysis and fraud detection, allowing officials to focus on complex cases and strategic enforcement. The Royal Malaysian Customs Department is adopting AI for risk assessment in audits, reducing manual checks. However, human judgment remains critical for decision-making and taxpayer interactions, so AI will augment rather than replace these roles.
