## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for economists in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level economists earn RM 3,500–RM 5,500 monthly. Mid-level (5–10 years) earn RM 7,000–RM 12,000, while senior roles in government or corporations can exceed RM 15,000. Public sector pay includes benefits but may be lower initially.

**Q: What is the job outlook for economists in Malaysia?**
A: Demand is stable, driven by government agencies, Bank Negara Malaysia, research institutes, and private firms. Malaysia's focus on data-driven policy and economic planning creates opportunities in analysis, forecasting, and consulting. Growth is moderate, with preference for quantitative skills.

**Q: What skills are most important for economists in Malaysia today?**
A: Proficiency in data analysis tools like Python, R, and STATA is essential. Econometric modelling, understanding of local policies (e.g., Budget, 12th Malaysia Plan), and ASEAN economic dynamics are key. Strong written communication for reports and presentations is also valued.
