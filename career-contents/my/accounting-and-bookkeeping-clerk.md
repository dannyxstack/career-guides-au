## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an accounting and bookkeeping clerk in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level accounting clerks in Malaysia earn between RM 1,800 and RM 2,800 monthly. With a few years of experience, salaries range from RM 2,800 to RM 4,500. Senior clerks or those in supervisory roles may earn RM 4,500 to RM 6,000 or more, depending on the company size and location, with Kuala Lumpur offering higher pay.

**Q: What key skills are required for accounting and bookkeeping clerks in Malaysia?**
A: Proficiency in accounting software like SQL, Autocount, or MYOB is essential. Strong knowledge of Malaysian tax regulations and SST is important. Attention to detail, numerical accuracy, and good data entry skills are critical. Familiarity with Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables and formulas, is highly valued. Communication skills in English and Bahasa Malaysia are also beneficial.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of accounting and bookkeeping clerks in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like data entry and invoice matching, reducing manual workload. However, it has not eliminated jobs; instead, it shifts focus to analysis, exception handling, and advisory roles. Clerks need to upskill in using AI tools and interpreting outputs. Employers in Malaysia increasingly seek clerks who can complement AI by providing insights and ensuring data accuracy.
