## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, the average monthly salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 2,500. Entry-level positions start around RM 1,200, while experienced workers can earn up to RM 3,000. Overtime and allowances may be available depending on the employer.

**Q: What skills are required for this occupation in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include basic numeracy for reading meters and counting cash, attention to detail, physical fitness for walking or driving between locations, and good time management. Familiarity with handheld devices for data entry is beneficial. Communication skills are needed for interacting with customers and reporting issues.

**Q: How is AI or automation affecting meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Malaysia?**
A: Automated meter reading (AMR) systems and smart meters are gradually reducing the need for manual meter reading, especially in urban areas. However, vending-machine collection remains essential for cash handling and maintenance. Workers may need to adapt to using digital tools for data uploads, but the role is not entirely obsolete.
