- Basic data cleaning and deduplication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI tools to automatically extract document data, with humans handling exceptions.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a data entry clerk in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, data entry clerks typically earn between RM 1,800 and RM 3,000 per month, depending on experience and industry. Entry-level positions start around RM 1,800, while experienced clerks in specialized fields like finance or medical records can earn up to RM 3,500. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total compensation.

**Q: What skills are essential for a data entry clerk in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include fast and accurate typing (at least 40-50 wpm), proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other office software, attention to detail for error-free data input, and basic knowledge of data management systems. Bilingual ability (English and Malay) is often required, and familiarity with accounting software or CRM tools can be advantageous.

**Q: How is AI affecting data entry jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools like optical character recognition (OCR) and robotic process automation (RPA) are automating repetitive data entry tasks. However, human oversight is still needed for quality control and handling unstructured data. To stay valuable, data entry clerks should upskill in data verification, database management, and specialized software, positioning themselves for roles like data analyst or administrative coordinator.
