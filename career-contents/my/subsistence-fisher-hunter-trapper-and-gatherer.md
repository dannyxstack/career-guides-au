## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence fishers, hunters, trappers, and gatherers in Malaysia?**
A: Income is highly variable, typically ranging from RM 800 to RM 1,500 per month, depending on season, location, and catch. Earnings are not fixed and are supplemented by personal consumption of caught or gathered items. Many rely on alternative income sources or government aid due to unpredictability.

**Q: What skills are essential for subsistence fishing and gathering in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of local waterways, tides, and ecosystems; traditional fishing, trapping, or foraging techniques; boat handling and maintenance; weather reading; and basic preservation methods. Physical endurance, patience, and adaptability are vital. Some may also need handicraft skills for tools or nets.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence fishers and gatherers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is challenging due to overfishing, habitat degradation, and climate change affecting marine and forest resources. While demand for local seafood and wild produce remains, younger generations increasingly pursue other livelihoods. Government initiatives for sustainable practices may provide limited opportunities, but overall, the occupation is declining.
