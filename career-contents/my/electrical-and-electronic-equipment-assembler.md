## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level assemblers earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,500 monthly, while experienced workers can earn RM 3,000 to RM 4,500. Pay varies by location, with higher rates in industrial areas like Penang and Johor. Overtime and shift allowances can boost earnings significantly.

**Q: What skills are required to work as an electrical and electronic equipment assembler in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, and ability to read technical diagrams. Basic knowledge of soldering, wiring, and using handheld tools is essential. Familiarity with quality control procedures and safety standards is also important. On-the-job training is common, but a vocational certificate in electronics can be an advantage.

**Q: How is AI impacting electrical and electronic equipment assembler jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI-driven automation is increasingly handling repetitive tasks like soldering and testing, reducing demand for low-skilled assemblers. However, workers who adapt by learning to operate and maintain automated systems remain valuable. Upskilling in robotics and data analysis can enhance job security in Malaysia's evolving electronics manufacturing sector.
