## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for landscape architects in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level landscape architects in Malaysia earn between RM 2,500 to RM 4,000 monthly. With 3-5 years of experience, salaries range from RM 4,500 to RM 7,000. Senior roles or those in managerial positions can earn RM 8,000 to RM 12,000 or more, depending on project complexity and firm size.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for a landscape architect in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes site analysis, design development, preparing drawings and specifications using software like AutoCAD and SketchUp, coordinating with architects and engineers, and attending client meetings. They also inspect project sites to ensure designs are implemented correctly, especially in urban developments, parks, or residential projects.

**Q: How is AI impacting landscape architecture in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools assist with site analysis, generating design alternatives, and automating routine drafting tasks, allowing landscape architects to focus on creative and strategic aspects. While AI enhances efficiency, it cannot replace human intuition for context-sensitive design. Professionals who adapt to using AI for simulation and visualization will stay competitive in Malaysia's evolving market.
