## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for sweepers and related labourers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for sweepers and related labourers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,200 to RM 1,800, depending on location and employer. Entry-level positions may start around RM 1,100, while experienced workers in urban areas can earn up to RM 2,000.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a sweeper or related labourer in Malaysia?**
A: Basic literacy and numeracy are helpful. Physical stamina, ability to follow instructions, and attention to detail are essential. Familiarity with cleaning equipment and safety practices is advantageous. On-the-job training is typically provided.

**Q: What is the career outlook for sweepers and related labourers in Malaysia, including AI impact?**
A: Demand remains stable due to essential cleaning services. Automation is limited as tasks require human judgment in varied environments. Career advancement includes supervisory roles in cleaning or waste management. Job security is moderate, with emphasis on hygiene standards.
