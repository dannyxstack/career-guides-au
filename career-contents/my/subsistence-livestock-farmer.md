## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for subsistence livestock farmers in Malaysia?**
A: Subsistence livestock farmers in Malaysia typically earn between RM 1,000 and RM 2,500 per month, depending on herd size and market prices. Income is irregular and often supplemented by other farm produce. Most operate on small family plots, with minimal external revenue.

**Q: What essential skills are needed for subsistence livestock farming in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include animal husbandry, basic veterinary knowledge, pasture management, and traditional farming techniques. Farmers must also handle feeding, disease prevention, and breeding. Financial literacy for managing small budgets and knowledge of local market conditions are beneficial.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence livestock farmers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook remains stable but challenging, with many farmers continuing traditional practices. Urbanization and demand for organic produce may offer niche opportunities. Government support programs exist, but climate risks and market volatility affect sustainability. Younger generations often shift to other sectors.
