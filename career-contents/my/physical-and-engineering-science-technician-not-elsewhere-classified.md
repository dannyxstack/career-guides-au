## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physical and engineering science technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn between RM 2,500 and RM 3,500 per month, while experienced professionals in specialized fields can earn RM 4,000 to RM 6,000. Salaries vary by sector—oil and gas and pharmaceuticals tend to pay higher than general manufacturing.

**Q: What key skills are required for physical and engineering science technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in operating laboratory and testing equipment, data analysis using software like MATLAB or Python, understanding safety regulations (e.g., DOSH standards), and strong attention to detail. Communication skills are also valued for reporting findings to engineers.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting physical and engineering science technicians in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks such as data logging and basic analysis, reducing manual workload. However, it increases demand for technicians who can calibrate AI-driven instruments, troubleshoot automated systems, and interpret complex results—skills that remain critical in Malaysian industries adopting Industry 4.0.
