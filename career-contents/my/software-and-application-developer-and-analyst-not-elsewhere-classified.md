## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers and analysts in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level software developers earn around RM 3,000–RM 5,000 per month, while mid-level professionals earn RM 6,000–RM 10,000. Senior roles can exceed RM 15,000. Salaries vary by location, with Kuala Lumpur offering higher pay, and by specialization in areas like AI or cybersecurity.

**Q: What technical skills are most in-demand for software developers in Malaysia?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in Python, Java, and JavaScript, along with frameworks like React and Node.js. Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), database management (SQL, MongoDB), and version control systems (Git) are essential. Strong problem-solving and teamwork skills are also highly valued.

**Q: How is the job market for software developers expected to grow in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for software developers in Malaysia is strong, driven by digital transformation and the expansion of tech hubs in Cyberjaya and Penang. Growth is steady, with opportunities in fintech, e-commerce, and government initiatives like MyDIGITAL. The outlook remains positive for skilled professionals.
