## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sheet metal workers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level sheet metal workers in Malaysia earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,500 monthly, while experienced workers can earn RM 3,000 to RM 5,000. Those with specialized skills or supervisory roles may earn higher, up to RM 6,000 or more. Overtime and project-based bonuses can supplement income.

**Q: What skills are essential for sheet metal workers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include reading blueprints and technical drawings, proficiency with hand and power tools, metal cutting, bending, and welding. Knowledge of safety regulations and the ability to work in confined spaces are important. Employers also value physical stamina, attention to detail, and basic math for measurements.

**Q: What is the career outlook for sheet metal workers in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for sheet metal workers is steady, driven by construction, manufacturing, and automotive industries. With infrastructure projects and industrial growth, skilled workers are needed. However, automation may affect repetitive tasks, so developing advanced skills like CNC operation and custom fabrication can improve job security and advancement opportunities.
