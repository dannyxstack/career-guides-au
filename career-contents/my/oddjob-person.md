## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical daily wage for odd-job persons in Malaysia?**
A: Daily wages for odd-job persons in Malaysia vary by task and location, ranging from RM50 to RM150 for general labor like cleaning or gardening. Skilled tasks such as basic plumbing or electrical repairs can fetch RM100 to RM250 per job. Payment is usually cash or e-wallet upon completion, with no fixed monthly salary.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting odd-job work in Malaysia?**
A: AI impact on odd-job work in Malaysia is minimal for manual tasks like cleaning or moving, but digital platforms (e.g., TaskRabbit, local apps) use AI to match workers with clients, increasing job visibility. AI may reduce demand for simple administrative odd jobs but has little effect on physical labor roles.

**Q: What is the career outlook for odd-job persons in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable due to consistent demand for short-term, flexible labor in households and small businesses. Urban areas like Klang Valley offer more opportunities. However, work is irregular and lacks benefits. No formal progression, but experienced workers can charge higher rates or specialize in niches like home renovation.
