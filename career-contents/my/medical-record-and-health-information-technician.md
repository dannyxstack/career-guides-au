## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a medical records technician in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level medical records technicians in Malaysia earn between RM 2,000 and RM 3,000 monthly. With experience and certifications like Certified Coding Specialist (CCS), salaries can rise to RM 4,000–RM 6,000. Senior roles in private hospitals or insurance firms may exceed RM 7,000. Salaries vary by location and employer size.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a medical records technician in Malaysia?**
A: Daily tasks include organising patient health records, ensuring accuracy and confidentiality, coding diagnoses and procedures using ICD-10-CM/PCS, processing discharge summaries, releasing records for audits, and assisting clinicians with data retrieval. They also maintain electronic health record (EHR) systems and ensure compliance with Malaysian healthcare regulations.

**Q: What skills are essential for a medical records technician in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of medical terminology, anatomy, and diagnostic codes (ICD-10-CM/PCS). Proficiency in EHR software, attention to detail, and strong organisational abilities are critical. Communication skills are needed to liaise with healthcare professionals. Certifications like Certified Medical Records Technician (CMRT) enhance employability. Familiarity with Malaysia's Private Healthcare Facilities and Services Act is a plus.
