## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a refuse sorter in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for refuse sorters in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,200 to RM 1,800, with variations based on location, type of waste facility, and experience. Entry-level positions typically start around RM 1,200, while experienced sorters may earn up to RM 2,000. Overtime and shift allowances can supplement base pay.

**Q: What skills are important for a refuse sorter in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for standing and manual labor, attention to detail to correctly separate recyclables from waste, and basic knowledge of sorting categories like plastics, metals, and paper. Familiarity with safety protocols and the ability to work in a team are also essential. Literacy and basic numeracy help in following instructions.

**Q: What is the career outlook for refuse sorters in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for refuse sorters is steady due to ongoing waste management needs and growing recycling initiatives in Malaysia. The job offers limited upward mobility, but experienced sorters may advance to supervisor roles. Automation is gradually being introduced in larger facilities, but manual sorting remains prevalent. Job security is generally stable, especially in urban areas.
