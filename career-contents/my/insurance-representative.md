## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for insurance representatives in Malaysia?**
A: Insurance representatives in Malaysia earn a base salary plus commission. Entry-level representatives can expect RM 3,000 to RM 5,000 monthly, while experienced ones may earn RM 7,000 to RM 10,000 or more. Top performers with strong sales achieve higher income. Bonuses and incentives are also common.

**Q: What skills are essential for insurance representatives in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include strong communication, negotiation, and customer service. Knowledge of financial products and regulations, plus certification from the Malaysian Insurance Institute (MII), is highly valued. Self-motivation, time management, and adaptability are crucial for meeting sales targets in a competitive market.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of insurance representatives in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like policy processing and claims, allowing representatives to focus on client relationships. AI tools provide data-driven insights for personalized recommendations, improving sales effectiveness. However, the human element of trust and advice remains vital, so AI is a complementary tool rather than a replacement.
