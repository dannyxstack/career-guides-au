## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bookmakers and croupiers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, croupiers in casinos typically earn between RM2,500 and RM4,500 per month, plus tips and allowances. Experienced dealers or those in high-limits areas can earn RM6,000 or more. Salaries vary by location; those in major casinos like Genting Highlands may receive additional benefits such as accommodation.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a croupier in Malaysia?**
A: Croupiers need strong math skills for calculating payouts and odds, manual dexterity for handling cards and chips, and excellent customer service to interact with players. Language proficiency in English and Chinese/Malay is often required. Most casinos provide in-house training, but prior gaming knowledge is an advantage.

**Q: How is AI expected to impact gaming workers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is gradually automating some gaming operations, such as electronic roulette and automated betting terminals. However, live table games still require human dealers for interaction and oversight. The outlook for human croupiers remains stable as casinos value the personal touch, but workers may need to adapt by learning to manage technology-integrated tables.
