## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level fumigators earn around RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 per month, while experienced controllers can make RM 3,000 to RM 5,000. Those with specialized certifications or in senior roles may earn up to RM 7,000. Overtime and commission-based bonuses are common.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a pest controller in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes inspecting premises for pests, applying chemical or non-chemical treatments, setting traps, and documenting findings. Controllers often travel between residential, commercial, and industrial sites. They must follow safety protocols, mix pesticides, and advise clients on prevention. Physical stamina and attention to detail are essential.

**Q: What is the career outlook for fumigators in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for pest control remains steady due to tropical climate and urbanization. Job growth is stable with opportunities in agriculture, hospitality, and food processing. Automation may reduce some manual tasks but creates demand for tech-skilled controllers. Licensing requirements and pest resistance drive need for ongoing training.
