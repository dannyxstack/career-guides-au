## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, monthly salaries for these teaching professionals range from RM 2,500 to RM 5,000 for entry-level roles, rising to RM 6,000–RM 10,000 for experienced educators. Pay varies by specialization, such as special education or adult literacy, and institution type (public vs private).

**Q: What key skills are required for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include patience, adaptability, curriculum design, classroom management, and proficiency in Malay and English. For roles in adult education or special needs, additional skills like digital literacy, assistive technology, and differentiated instruction are highly valued.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Malaysia?**
A: AI is increasingly used for personalized learning platforms and administrative tasks like grading, allowing teachers to focus on student interaction. However, it requires upskilling in educational technology. AI is seen as a tool to enhance, not replace, human teachers, especially for roles involving social-emotional support.
