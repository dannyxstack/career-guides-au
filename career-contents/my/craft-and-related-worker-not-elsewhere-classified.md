## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries for craft and related workers in Malaysia vary widely by specialization and experience, typically ranging from RM1,500 to RM4,000 per month. Entry-level positions may start lower, while skilled workers in high-demand trades can earn more, especially with certifications or years of experience.

**Q: What skills are essential for craft and related workers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include technical proficiency in specific trades (e.g., carpentry, welding, or tailoring), attention to detail, manual dexterity, and problem-solving. Basic literacy and numeracy are important. Familiarity with modern tools and safety practices is increasingly valued, as is the ability to adapt to new techniques.

**Q: What is the career outlook for craft and related workers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable, with steady demand for skilled tradespeople across construction, manufacturing, and repair services. Automation may affect repetitive tasks, but complex or customized work remains in demand. Upskilling in specialized areas or supervisory roles can enhance career progression and job security.
