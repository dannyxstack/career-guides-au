## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level stonemasons in Malaysia earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,500 per month, while experienced craftsmen can earn RM 3,000 to RM 5,000. Those specializing in intricate carving or restoration may command higher rates, especially in high-end construction or heritage projects.

**Q: What skills are essential for a stonemason in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include precision in measuring and cutting stone, knowledge of different stone types, and proficiency with hand and power tools like chisels and angle grinders. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to read blueprints are also important. Experience with traditional carving and modern machinery is valued.

**Q: What is the career outlook for stonemasons in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to ongoing construction and infrastructure projects. While automation affects some mass production, custom and restoration work still require manual expertise. Skilled stonemasons are likely to find consistent work, especially in the Klang Valley and tourist areas with heritage buildings.
