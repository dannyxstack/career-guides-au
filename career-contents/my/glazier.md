## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a glazier in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a glazier in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,800 to RM 3,500 for entry-level positions, with experienced glaziers earning up to RM 5,000 or more. Overtime and project-based bonuses can increase total pay. Salaries vary by location, employer, and type of glass work, such as structural or decorative installations.

**Q: What skills are essential for a glazier in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include precise measurement and cutting of glass, knowledge of various glass types and fittings, and proficiency with hand tools like cutters and suction cups. Physical stamina and balance for working at heights are important. Familiarity with safety regulations and basic math for estimating materials is also necessary. On-the-job training often supplements formal apprenticeship programs.

**Q: How is AI impacting the glazier profession in Malaysia?**
A: AI is gradually influencing glazier work through automated glass cutting and design software, which enhances precision and reduces waste. However, hands-on tasks like measuring, fitting, and sealing remain essential and less affected by AI. Glaziers who adapt to using digital tools and computer-aided design (CAD) can improve efficiency and take on more complex projects.
