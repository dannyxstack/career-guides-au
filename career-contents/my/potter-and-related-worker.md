## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for potters in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level potters in Malaysia earn around RM 1,500–RM 2,500 monthly, while experienced workers can earn RM 3,000–RM 5,000. Artisan potters with a strong client base may earn higher. Salaries vary by location, with higher pay in tourist areas like Penang or artisan hubs in Selangor.

**Q: What skills are essential for a pottery career in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include hand-building, wheel-throwing, glazing, and kiln operation. Creativity, attention to detail, and patience are crucial. Knowledge of local clay types and traditional Malaysian ceramic designs is beneficial. Business skills help for self-employed potters selling at markets or online.

**Q: What is the career outlook for potters in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for traditional handcrafted pottery remains steady among collectors and tourists. However, competition from mass-produced ceramics is strong. Potters with unique artistic styles or those who teach workshops have better prospects. The field offers limited full-time positions, with many working freelance or running small studios.
