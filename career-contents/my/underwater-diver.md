- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.

**Where AI augments the role:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for underwater divers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, commercial divers earn between RM 3,000 and RM 8,000 monthly, depending on experience and specialization. Offshore oil and gas divers earn higher rates, while inshore divers in construction or salvage earn less. Overtime and hazard pay may increase total earnings significantly.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for an underwater diver in Malaysia?**
A: Underwater divers in Malaysia often work on offshore oil rigs, bridge inspections, or marine salvage. A typical day involves safety briefings, equipment checks, and performing tasks like welding, cutting, or inspection underwater. Diving depths and durations vary, with strict adherence to decompression schedules to prevent decompression sickness.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting underwater diving jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI is gradually influencing diving through autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) for inspections, reducing the need for human divers in hazardous environments. However, AI cannot replace divers for complex repairs and tasks requiring dexterity and judgment. Divers may need to learn to operate and maintain AUVs to stay relevant.
