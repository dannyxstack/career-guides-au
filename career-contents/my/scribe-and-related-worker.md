## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for scribes and related workers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for scribes in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,800 to RM 3,000 depending on experience and industry. Entry-level positions may start around RM 1,500, while senior scribes in legal or medical fields can earn up to RM 4,000. Salaries are typically higher in major cities like Kuala Lumpur.

**Q: What skills are essential for a scribe in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include fast and accurate typing, proficiency in Bahasa Malaysia and English, good listening and comprehension abilities, and familiarity with industry-specific terminology (e.g., legal or medical). Attention to detail, confidentiality, and the ability to work under time pressure are also critical. Basic digital literacy for transcription software is increasingly expected.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting scribes in Malaysia?**
A: AI-powered transcription tools are automating basic transcription, reducing demand for general scribes. However, specialized fields like court reporting or medical scribing still require human oversight for accuracy, nuance, and context. Scribes who adapt by using AI as a tool and developing higher-order skills like editing and proofreading will remain relevant.
