## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Buyer in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level Buyers earn around RM 2,500–RM 3,500 per month, while experienced Buyers can earn RM 4,500–RM 7,000. Senior positions in multinational companies may exceed RM 8,000. Salaries vary by industry, with manufacturing, retail, and oil & gas typically paying higher.

**Q: What key skills are required for a Buyer in Malaysia?**
A: Buyers need strong negotiation, analytical, and communication skills. Proficiency in procurement software (e.g., SAP, Oracle) and knowledge of supply chain management are essential. Local market awareness, vendor relationship management, and familiarity with Malaysian trade regulations also add value.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Buyers in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like purchase order processing and spend analysis, allowing Buyers to focus on strategic sourcing and supplier relationships. However, human judgment remains critical for negotiations and adapting to local market dynamics. Buyers who upskill in data analytics and AI tools will stay competitive.
