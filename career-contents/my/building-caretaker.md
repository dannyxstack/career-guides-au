## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for building caretakers in Malaysia?**
A: Building caretakers in Malaysia typically earn between RM1,200 and RM2,500 per month. Salaries vary based on experience, location, and the type of building (e.g., condominium, office, or commercial). Those in upscale properties may receive higher pay plus allowances for accommodation or overtime.

**Q: What skills are essential for building caretakers in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include basic maintenance (plumbing, electrical, painting), cleaning, security monitoring, and effective communication with residents and management. A valid security guard license is often preferred. Proficiency in Malay, English, Mandarin, or Tamil helps in dealing with diverse occupants.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of building caretakers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is introducing automated security systems, smart sensors for leak detection or faulty equipment, and digital visitor management. While these reduce some manual tasks, caretakers remain crucial for hands-on repairs, emergency response, and personal interaction with residents, so full replacement is unlikely in the near term.
