## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sewing machine operator in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around RM 1,200 to RM 1,800 monthly, while experienced workers can make RM 2,000 to RM 3,000. Overtime is common in garment factories, boosting take-home pay. Rates may vary by region, with higher wages in Selangor and Penang due to industrial concentration.

**Q: What skills are essential for a sewing machine operator in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in operating industrial sewing machines, knowledge of different stitch types and fabric handling, attention to detail for quality control, and basic maintenance like threading and changing needles. Speed and accuracy are crucial to meet production targets in garment manufacturing.

**Q: How is AI affecting sewing machine operators in Malaysia?**
A: AI-driven automated sewing systems are emerging, but they are expensive and mostly used by large manufacturers for repetitive tasks. Most operators still perform complex and customized work. AI may reduce demand for low-skill operators over time, but skilled operators who can manage automated machines remain valued.
