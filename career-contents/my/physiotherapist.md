## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for physiotherapists in Malaysia?**
A: Fresh graduates typically earn between RM 2,500 and RM 3,500 monthly. With 3–5 years of experience, salaries range from RM 4,000 to RM 6,000. Senior physiotherapists or those in private practice can earn RM 7,000 to RM 10,000 or more, depending on specialization and location.

**Q: What key skills are required for physiotherapists in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include strong manual therapy techniques, patient assessment, and rehabilitation program design. Communication skills are vital for educating patients and collaborating with healthcare teams. Proficiency in Malay and English is important. Adaptability and empathy help in managing diverse patient conditions effectively.

**Q: What is the job outlook for physiotherapists in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for physiotherapists is growing due to an aging population and increased awareness of rehabilitation. Opportunities exist in hospitals, clinics, sports centers, and home care. The government's focus on healthcare expansion supports steady employment growth, though competition in urban areas is moderate.
