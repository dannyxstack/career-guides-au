## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a tailor in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a tailor in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 3,000 for experienced professionals, with entry-level positions starting around RM 1,200. Those specializing in high-end custom garments or bridal wear can earn up to RM 5,000, while self-employed tailors may have variable income depending on client base and reputation.

**Q: What skills are essential for tailors in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include precision in measuring and cutting fabric, proficiency with sewing machines and hand-stitching techniques, pattern making, and alterations. Knowledge of different fabrics and their care, attention to detail, and creativity in design are crucial. In Malaysia, familiarity with traditional attire like baju kurung and kebaya is also valuable for serving local clientele.

**Q: How is AI impacting the tailoring profession in Malaysia?**
A: AI-powered design software and automated cutting machines are streamlining production in large-scale garment factories, but custom tailoring remains skill-intensive. For independent tailors, AI tools assist with pattern digitization and fabric optimization, reducing waste. However, the art of handcrafted fittings is less affected by automation, ensuring demand for skilled tailors who offer personalized service and adjustments.
