## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for professional athletes in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries for professional athletes in Malaysia vary widely by sport and level. Top footballers in the Malaysia Super League earn between RM10,000 to RM50,000 monthly, while in less commercialized sports, monthly pay may range from RM3,000 to RM10,000. Government allowances and sponsorships can supplement income, but many rely on part-time work or endorsements.

**Q: What career opportunities are available for athletes after retiring from professional sports in Malaysia?**
A: Retired athletes in Malaysia often transition into coaching, sports management, or sports medicine. Many join state or national sports associations as administrators or trainers. Others pursue careers in fitness instruction, event management, or media as commentators. Government programs like the Athletes' Career and Education Programme (ACE) support post-retirement planning.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting training and performance for athletes in Malaysia?**
A: AI is increasingly used in Malaysia for performance analytics, injury prevention, and personalized training. National sports institutes employ AI to analyze movement patterns and optimize techniques. Wearable tech and machine learning help monitor biometrics, reducing injury risks. However, AI adoption is still growing, with limited access for lower-tier athletes.
