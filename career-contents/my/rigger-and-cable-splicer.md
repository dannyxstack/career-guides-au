## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for riggers and cable splicers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level riggers and cable splicers in Malaysia earn around RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 per month. With experience, salaries can rise to RM 3,500–RM 5,000. Those with specialized certification or working in high-risk environments like oil and gas may earn up to RM 7,000. Overtime and location also affect pay.

**Q: What skills are essential for riggers and cable splicers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of rigging equipment, load calculation, and cable splicing techniques. Physical fitness, teamwork, and adherence to safety standards are critical. Certification from bodies like NIOSH or CIDB is often required. Experience with fiber optics or high-voltage cables is increasingly valued.

**Q: What is the career outlook for riggers and cable splicers in Malaysia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to ongoing construction, telecommunications expansion, and infrastructure projects like the MRT and 5G rollout. However, automation may reduce some labor-intensive tasks. Skilled workers with updated certifications will have better prospects, especially in specialized sectors like oil and gas or renewable energy.
