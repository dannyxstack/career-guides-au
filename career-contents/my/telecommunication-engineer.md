## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Telecommunications Engineer in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level telecommunications engineers earn around RM 3,000–4,500 monthly. Mid-career engineers with 5–10 years of experience earn RM 6,000–9,000, while senior engineers can exceed RM 12,000. Salaries vary by company, specialization, and location, with higher pay in Kuala Lumpur and tech hubs. Additional certifications can boost earnings.

**Q: What key skills are required for telecommunications engineers in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include network design, RF engineering, and knowledge of 4G/5G technologies. Proficiency in tools like MATLAB, AutoCAD, and network simulation software is important. Soft skills such as problem-solving and teamwork are valued. Certifications like CCNA or CCNP enhance employability. Familiarity with Malaysian regulations from MCMC is also beneficial.

**Q: How is the career outlook for telecommunications engineers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to ongoing 5G rollout and digital infrastructure expansion. Demand is driven by telecom operators, government projects (e.g., JENDELA), and private sector digitalization. Engineers specializing in fiber optics, wireless networks, and cybersecurity have strong prospects. However, automation may reduce demand for routine tasks, so continuous learning is important.
