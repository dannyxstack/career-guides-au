## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for medical imaging technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level medical imaging technicians earn RM 2,500 to RM 3,500 monthly. With experience, salaries rise to RM 4,000 to RM 6,500. Senior roles in private hospitals can exceed RM 8,000. Government hospital pay follows standardized scales with allowances.

**Q: What skills are essential for medical imaging technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Proficiency in operating X-ray, MRI, CT, and ultrasound equipment is vital. Knowledge of radiation safety, patient positioning, and image quality assessment is crucial. Communication and empathy are needed for patient care. Certification from the Malaysian Ministry of Health's Lembaga Ahli Teknologi Makmal Perubatan is mandatory.

**Q: What is the career outlook for medical imaging technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Demand is stable due to an aging population and advances in diagnostics. Opportunities exist in public hospitals, private clinics, and diagnostic centers. Specializing in MRI or CT can improve prospects. AI assists image analysis but is unlikely to replace technicians, who are essential for patient interaction and equipment operation.
