## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Manufacturing Managers in Malaysia?**
A: Manufacturing Managers in Malaysia earn an average monthly salary between RM 8,000 and RM 18,000, depending on experience, company size, and industry. Senior managers in large multinationals may earn up to RM 25,000. Bonuses and allowances, such as shift or travel allowances, are common and can add 15-20% to total compensation.

**Q: What key skills are required to become a Manufacturing Manager in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include lean manufacturing, Six Sigma, and supply chain management. Strong leadership, problem-solving, and communication abilities are essential. Familiarity with Malaysian regulations (e.g., DOSH, DOE) and ISO standards is preferred. Proficiency in data analysis and ERP systems like SAP or Oracle is increasingly important. Continuous improvement and cost reduction expertise are highly valued.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Manufacturing Managers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is transforming manufacturing by enabling predictive maintenance, quality control via computer vision, and process optimization. Manufacturing Managers must now interpret data from AI systems to make decisions. While AI automates routine tasks, it enhances efficiency and reduces downtime. Managers need to upskill in digital tools and data analytics to leverage AI effectively, though human oversight remains critical for complex problem-solving and team management.
