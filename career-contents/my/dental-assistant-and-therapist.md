## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dental assistants and therapists in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level dental assistants in Malaysia earn around RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 per month, while experienced therapists can earn RM 3,000 to RM 5,000. Salaries vary by location, clinic type, and qualifications. Government clinics often offer lower base pay but better benefits, whereas private practices may provide higher pay and performance bonuses.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for a dental assistant or therapist in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day includes preparing treatment rooms, sterilizing instruments, assisting dentists during procedures, taking X-rays, and educating patients on oral hygiene. Therapists may perform scaling, fluoride applications, and simple fillings under supervision. Administrative tasks like scheduling appointments and managing patient records are also common.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence (AI) impacting the role of dental assistants and therapists in Malaysia?**
A: AI is primarily used for diagnostic imaging, such as analyzing X-rays for cavities or gum disease, reducing manual assessment time. However, for assistants and therapists, AI remains a supportive tool rather than a replacement. Routine tasks like sterilization and chairside assistance still rely on human skills, making AI integration gradual and focused on efficiency.
