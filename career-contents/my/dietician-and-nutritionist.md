## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dieticians and nutritionists in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level dieticians in Malaysia earn around RM2,500–3,500 monthly. With 5+ years of experience, salaries range from RM4,500–7,000. Senior roles in hospitals or private practice can exceed RM10,000. Salaries vary by location (Kuala Lumpur typically higher) and employer (private sector pays more than public).

**Q: What is the career outlook for dieticians and nutritionists in Malaysia?**
A: Demand is growing due to rising health awareness and lifestyle diseases. Opportunities exist in hospitals, clinics, the food industry, research, and sports nutrition. The government's focus on preventative healthcare also opens public health roles. Competition is higher in urban areas; continuing education and specialisation improve prospects.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as a dietician in Malaysia?**
A: Besides a recognised degree and registration with the Malaysian Dietitians' Association, essential skills include strong communication for patient counseling, knowledge of local dietary habits, analytical skills for nutritional assessments, and empathy. Cultural competency is important given Malaysia's multi-ethnic cuisine. Staying updated with nutritional science and digital health tools is also beneficial.
