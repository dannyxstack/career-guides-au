## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Research and Development Managers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, Research and Development Managers earn between RM 8,000 and RM 20,000 per month, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior managers in multinational corporations or high-tech sectors can earn above RM 25,000. Bonuses and benefits like medical coverage and stock options are common.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are required to become a Research and Development Manager in Malaysia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in a relevant scientific or engineering field is essential, with many employers preferring a master's or PhD. Key skills include project management, analytical thinking, and familiarity with R&D processes. Proficiency in English and Malay, leadership abilities, and knowledge of industry regulations are also important.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Research and Development Managers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is transforming R&D by accelerating data analysis, simulation, and prototyping, allowing managers to focus on strategic decisions. However, managers must upskill in AI tools and oversee ethical implementation. Demand for R&D managers with AI expertise is growing, particularly in tech, pharmaceuticals, and manufacturing sectors.
