## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for mineral and stone processing plant operators in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary ranges from RM2,500 to RM4,500, depending on experience, location, and the specific mineral processed. Operators in remote mining areas may receive additional allowances. Entry-level positions start around RM2,000, while senior roles can reach RM5,500.

**Q: What key skills are required for mineral and stone processing plant operators in Malaysia?**
A: Operators need technical knowledge of crushing, grinding, and screening machinery, as well as basic troubleshooting and preventive maintenance. Physical stamina, attention to safety protocols, and the ability to work in shifts are essential. Familiarity with digital control systems is increasingly valuable.

**Q: How is AI impacting mineral and stone processing plant operators in Malaysia?**
A: AI is being adopted for automated sorting, real-time monitoring, and predictive maintenance, reducing manual tasks. Operators must learn to manage advanced control systems and interpret data. However, AI is unlikely to fully replace human decision-making in Malaysia's mining sector due to complex site-specific conditions.
