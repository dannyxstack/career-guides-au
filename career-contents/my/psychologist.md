## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for psychologists in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level psychologists earn around RM 3,000–RM 4,500 per month. With 5+ years of experience, salaries can reach RM 6,000–RM 10,000. Clinical psychologists in private practice may earn higher, while those in government hospitals follow civil service pay scales. Specialization and location also affect earnings.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as a psychologist in Malaysia?**
A: Strong interpersonal and active listening skills are essential. Malaysian psychologists must be culturally sensitive to the country's multi-ethnic society. Proficiency in English, Malay, and ideally Mandarin or Tamil is advantageous. Analytical thinking, empathy, and ability to maintain confidentiality are crucial for building trust with clients.

**Q: How is AI impacting the psychology profession in Malaysia?**
A: AI is increasingly used in psychological assessment tools and telehealth platforms, enabling wider access to mental health services in rural areas. However, AI cannot replace human judgment in therapy. Psychologists must adapt by learning to interpret AI-generated data while maintaining empathetic, face-to-face client interactions.
