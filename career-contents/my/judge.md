## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for judges in Malaysia?**
A: Judicial salaries in Malaysia are set by a government schedule. Entry-level magistrates earn approximately RM5,000–RM8,000 per month. Sessions Court judges receive RM10,000–RM15,000, while High Court judges earn RM15,000–RM30,000. Senior positions in the Court of Appeal and Federal Court can exceed RM40,000, including allowances.

**Q: What is the career progression for judges in Malaysia?**
A: The judicial career in Malaysia typically begins as a magistrate or sessions court judge after legal experience. Promotion to the High Court, Court of Appeal, and Federal Court is based on merit, seniority, and performance. Opportunities for advancement are limited due to the small number of senior positions, making the career path highly competitive and selective.

**Q: What key skills are required to become a successful judge in Malaysia?**
A: Judges in Malaysia need strong legal knowledge, analytical reasoning, and impartiality. Excellent communication and writing skills are essential for delivering clear judgments. Integrity, patience, and emotional intelligence are crucial for managing cases and courtroom proceedings. Additionally, familiarity with Malaysian legal procedures and common law principles is mandatory.
