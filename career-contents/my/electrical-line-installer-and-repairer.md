## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for electrical line installers and repairers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level electrical line installers earn around RM 2,000–RM 3,000 per month. With experience, salaries can range from RM 3,500 to RM 6,000. Those with specialized skills or senior roles may earn up to RM 8,000. Overtime and allowances are common.

**Q: What skills are required for electrical line installers and repairers in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include electrical knowledge, physical fitness, ability to work at heights, troubleshooting, and safety compliance. Malaysian employers also value teamwork, communication, and a valid wireman license. Familiarity with local grid standards and basic English is advantageous.

**Q: What is the career outlook for electrical line installers and repairers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable due to ongoing grid maintenance and expansion. Demand is driven by infrastructure projects and renewable energy integration. However, automation may impact routine tasks. Skilled workers with safety certifications and adaptability to new technologies will remain in demand.
