## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for journalists in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level journalists earn between RM2,500 and RM4,000 per month. With 3-5 years of experience, salaries range from RM4,500 to RM7,000. Senior journalists or editors can earn RM8,000 to RM15,000. Print media tends to pay less than broadcast or digital outlets. Freelancers charge per article or project.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting journalism in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools are used for automated news summaries, data analysis, and transcription, freeing journalists for in-depth reporting. However, ethical concerns about bias and accuracy persist. Malaysian newsrooms cautiously adopt AI for routine tasks, emphasizing human oversight to maintain credibility and local context.

**Q: What key skills do journalists need in Malaysia today?**
A: Strong writing in Bahasa Malaysia and English is essential, along with multimedia skills like video editing and podcasting. Proficiency in social media platforms and data journalism tools is increasingly important. Understanding local culture, ethics, and digital security are also critical for modern journalism in Malaysia.
