## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions earn around RM 2,000 to RM 3,500 monthly. With 3-5 years of experience, pay ranges from RM 4,000 to RM 6,000. Senior professionals or those in private practice can earn RM 7,000 or more. Earnings also depend on specialization and client base.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are required to work as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Malaysia?**
A: You need a diploma or degree in traditional medicine from recognized institutions, registration with the Traditional and Complementary Medicine Council, and compliance with the T&CM Act 2016. Language proficiency in Malay and English is important. Practical skills in therapies like acupuncture or herbal medicine are essential, along with good communication and empathy.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Malaysia, including the impact of AI?**
A: Demand is growing due to rising health consciousness and government support for integrative healthcare. AI is unlikely to replace human practitioners, as hands-on diagnostics and personalized care remain key. However, AI tools may assist in treatment planning and record-keeping. The field offers opportunities in clinics, hospitals, or private practice, with steady job growth.
