## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for fruit and vegetable preservers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level preservers earn around RM1,500 to RM2,000 per month, while experienced workers can earn RM2,500 to RM3,500. Salaries vary by location and employer, with those in large-scale processing plants or supervisory roles earning higher pay.

**Q: What is the career outlook for fruit and vegetable preservers in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for preservers is steady due to Malaysia's strong food processing industry, especially in regions like Johor and Penang. Opportunities exist in factories producing canned fruits, pickles, and sauces. Advancement to supervisor or quality control roles is possible with experience.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a fruit and vegetable preserver in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of food safety and hygiene standards (e.g., GMP, HACCP), ability to operate preserving equipment, and attention to detail for quality control. Physical stamina and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment are also important.
