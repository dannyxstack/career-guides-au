## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sales workers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for sales workers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,800 to RM 3,500 for entry-level positions. Experienced workers can earn RM 4,000 to RM 6,000, plus commissions and bonuses. Salaries vary by industry, location (higher in Klang Valley), and performance.

**Q: What key skills are needed for sales jobs in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include strong communication, persuasion, and customer service abilities. Product knowledge, negotiation, and basic digital literacy (e.g., CRM software) are increasingly important. Fluency in multiple languages, especially Malay, English, and Mandarin, is a significant advantage in Malaysia's diverse market.

**Q: How is AI impacting sales roles in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like data entry and lead qualification, freeing time for relationship-building. CRM tools with AI analytics help predict customer behavior. However, human interaction remains crucial for closing deals. Sales workers who adapt to using AI tools can improve efficiency and outperform others.
