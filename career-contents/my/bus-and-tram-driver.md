## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for bus and tram drivers in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,500 to RM 3,000, depending on experience, employer, and city. Drivers working for public transport operators like Rapid KL or Prasarana may earn higher, with overtime and allowances. Senior drivers or those on long-distance routes can earn up to RM 4,000.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Malaysia?**
A: You need a valid Competent Driving Licence (CDL) for buses, typically class D or E, and must pass a medical exam. Tram drivers require specific training from the operator. Key skills include defensive driving, customer service, punctuality, and ability to handle traffic. Knowledge of routes and basic vehicle maintenance is beneficial.

**Q: How is technology like AI affecting bus and tram driving jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI and automation are gradually impacting. Some buses have driver-assist systems, but full autonomy is not imminent. Tram operations remain largely manual. However, technology may create new roles in monitoring and maintenance. The demand for drivers remains stable due to urbanization and public transport expansion, so jobs are secure for the near future.
