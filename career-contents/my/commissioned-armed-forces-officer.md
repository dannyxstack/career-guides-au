## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Malaysia?**
A: Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Malaysia start with a basic salary of approximately RM4,500 to RM6,000 per month, increasing with rank and years of service. Additional allowances for housing, subsistence, and hazardous duty can significantly boost total compensation. Senior officers may earn RM15,000 or more monthly.

**Q: What is the career progression for Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Malaysia?**
A: Officers typically advance from Second Lieutenant to Lieutenant, Captain, Major, and beyond, based on performance, experience, and completion of professional courses. Promotions bring increased responsibility, leadership roles, and higher pay. Career paths include command, staff, and specialist tracks, with opportunities for further education and overseas training.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Malaysia?**
A: AI is enhancing decision-support systems, surveillance, and logistics, allowing officers to focus on strategic planning and leadership. While AI automates data analysis and threat detection, human judgment remains critical. Officers must develop digital literacy and adaptability to integrate AI tools effectively, but the core need for leadership and ethics persists.
