- Manual drafting of mine design and construction drawings

- Manual monitoring of equipment status and energy consumption
- On-site Inspections for Hazard Identification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI for 3D modeling of ore deposits and resource assessment
- Optimizing autonomous truck routes and blasting plans using AI
- Using machine learning to predict rock mass stability and support requirements
- Integrating digital twin technology for real-time monitoring and adjustment of mining operations
- Quickly parse geological reports and compliance documents using natural language processing

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mining and metallurgical technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around RM 2,500 to RM 4,000 per month. With a few years of experience, salaries typically range from RM 4,000 to RM 7,000. Senior technicians or those with specialized skills can earn up to RM 10,000 monthly, depending on the employer and location.

**Q: What key skills are required for mining and metallurgical technicians in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include knowledge of mineral processing, metallurgical testing, and quality control. Technical proficiency with laboratory equipment and data analysis software is important. Strong understanding of safety regulations and environmental compliance is crucial. Problem-solving, teamwork, and communication skills are also valued.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of mining and metallurgical technicians in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating data collection, predictive maintenance, and ore grade estimation, increasing efficiency. Technicians now work alongside AI systems, interpreting outputs and managing automated processes. While some routine tasks are reduced, demand for technicians with data analysis and AI oversight skills is rising.
