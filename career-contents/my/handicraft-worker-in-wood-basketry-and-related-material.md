## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for handicraft workers in wood and basketry in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level handicraft workers in Malaysia earn around RM1,500 to RM2,500 monthly. Experienced artisans with specialized skills can earn RM3,000 to RM5,000 or more, depending on product demand and reputation. Those working in tourist areas or for established brands may earn higher incomes.

**Q: What skills are essential for a career as a handicraft worker in wood and basketry in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, knowledge of traditional techniques (e.g., weaving, carving), and familiarity with tools and materials. Creativity and design sense are important for producing unique items. Business knowledge helps with marketing, especially for independent artisans.

**Q: How does automation and AI affect handicraft workers in wood and basketry in Malaysia?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact on traditional handicrafts, as handmade items are valued for their uniqueness. However, digital tools like 3D modeling and laser cutting aid in design and prototyping, increasing efficiency. Artisans who embrace these technologies can enhance productivity without replacing manual craft.
