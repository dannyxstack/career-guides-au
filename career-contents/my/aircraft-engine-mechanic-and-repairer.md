## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for aircraft engine mechanics in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level mechanics earn between RM2,500 and RM3,500 monthly. With experience and CAAM or EASA licensing, salaries rise to RM6,000-RM8,000. Senior specialists at major airlines or MROs can exceed RM10,000, plus overtime and shift allowances.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become an aircraft engine mechanic in Malaysia?**
A: You need a valid Aircraft Maintenance Engineer license from CAAM (or EASA Part 66), strong mechanical aptitude, knowledge of turbine and piston engines, and ability to interpret technical manuals. Diagnostic troubleshooting, attention to detail, and English proficiency are essential, along with teamwork in a safety-critical environment.

**Q: What is the career outlook for aircraft engine mechanics in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to growing airline fleets and MRO hubs. Demand for licensed mechanics remains high as AI aids diagnostics but cannot replace hands-on repair. Opportunities exist with airlines, third-party MROs, and defense. Continuous training on new engine types ensures career progression.
