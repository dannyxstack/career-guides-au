## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Construction Manager in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, a Construction Manager's monthly salary typically ranges from RM 8,000 to RM 15,000 for mid-level positions, with senior roles earning up to RM 25,000 or more. Pay varies by experience, project scale, and location—urban areas like Kuala Lumpur and Selangor offer higher compensation, while smaller towns may have lower ranges.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a Construction Manager in Malaysia, and what is the career outlook?**
A: A Construction Manager oversees project planning, site coordination, budgeting, and safety compliance. They liaise with architects, engineers, and subcontractors, conduct site inspections, and resolve issues. Career outlook is positive due to ongoing infrastructure projects and property development, with opportunities to advance to senior management, project director, or consultancy roles.

**Q: What key skills are essential for a Construction Manager in Malaysia, and how might AI impact the role?**
A: Essential skills include project management, knowledge of local building codes, budget control, communication, and problem-solving. AI is increasingly used for project scheduling, cost estimation, and safety monitoring via drones and sensors. While AI enhances efficiency, human judgment in site management, stakeholder coordination, and crisis handling remains critical.
