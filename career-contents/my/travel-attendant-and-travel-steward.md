## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for travel attendants in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level travel attendants in Malaysia earn around RM2,500 to RM3,500 monthly, while experienced attendants or those working for full-service airlines can earn RM4,000 to RM7,000 per month. Additional allowances for layovers and flights can increase total compensation significantly.

**Q: What key skills are required to become a travel attendant in Malaysia?**
A: Travel attendants need strong customer service skills, fluency in English and Bahasa Malaysia (additional languages like Mandarin are a plus), excellent communication, problem-solving abilities, and the capacity to remain calm under pressure. Physical fitness, adaptability, and a pleasing personality are also important.

**Q: What is the career outlook for travel attendants in Malaysia?**
A: The career outlook for travel attendants in Malaysia is projected to grow steadily with the recovery of the tourism and aviation sectors. Opportunities exist with local and international airlines. However, automation of routine tasks may reduce demand for some roles, while emphasis on service quality will continue to create opportunities.
