## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for electrical engineers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level electrical engineers earn between RM 2,800 to RM 4,000 per month, while those with 3-5 years of experience can expect RM 4,500 to RM 7,000. Senior engineers or those in managerial roles may earn RM 8,000 to RM 12,000 or more. Salaries vary by location (higher in Kuala Lumpur), industry (oil & gas, semiconductors), and specialization.

**Q: What key skills are required for electrical engineers in Malaysia?**
A: Technical skills include proficiency in circuit design, power systems, PLC programming, and software like AutoCAD, MATLAB, and ETAP. Knowledge of renewable energy systems and industrial automation is increasingly valued. Soft skills such as problem-solving, teamwork, and communication are essential. Registration with the Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM) is required for professional practice.

**Q: What is the career outlook for electrical engineers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is positive, driven by growth in manufacturing, construction, and renewable energy sectors. The government's focus on Industry 4.0, digitalization, and green technology creates demand for skilled engineers. Roles in smart grid development, electric vehicle infrastructure, and automation are emerging. Continuous upskilling is recommended to stay competitive.
