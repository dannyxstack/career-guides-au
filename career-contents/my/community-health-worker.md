## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a community health worker in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, community health workers typically earn between RM 1,500 and RM 3,000 per month, depending on experience and location. Government workers may receive additional allowances, while those in urban areas or with specialized skills can earn higher. Entry-level positions start around RM 1,200.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a community health worker in Malaysia?**
A: Community health workers in Malaysia conduct home visits to monitor health conditions, provide health education on hygiene and nutrition, assist in maternal and child health programs, and refer patients to clinics. They also collect health data, organize community screenings, and collaborate with local clinics and NGOs.

**Q: What skills are essential for a community health worker in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include effective communication in local languages (Malay, Chinese, Tamil), empathy, and cultural sensitivity. Basic knowledge of common health issues like diabetes and hypertension, organizational skills for record-keeping, ability to use mobile health apps, and teamwork with healthcare professionals are also crucial.
