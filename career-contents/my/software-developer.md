- Writing basic unit tests and regression tests

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level software developers in Malaysia earn around RM 3,000–RM 5,000 per month, while mid-level developers with 3–5 years of experience earn RM 6,000–RM 10,000. Senior developers can earn RM 12,000–RM 20,000 or more, especially in Kuala Lumpur and tech hubs. Salaries vary by company size, industry, and specialised skills like AI or cloud computing.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for software developers in Malaysia?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in JavaScript, Python, Java, and frameworks like React, Angular, and Node.js. Cloud skills (AWS, Azure), database management (SQL, MongoDB), and DevOps tools (Docker, Kubernetes) are highly valued. Knowledge of mobile development (Flutter, Swift) and cybersecurity basics also boosts employability in Malaysia’s growing tech sector.

**Q: What is the career outlook for software developers in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is strong, driven by digital transformation, e-commerce, and government initiatives like MyDigital. Demand spans fintech, healthcare, and manufacturing. Roles in AI, data science, and cybersecurity are expanding. While AI automates routine coding, developers who focus on system design, critical thinking, and emerging technologies will have ample opportunities for growth and advancement.
