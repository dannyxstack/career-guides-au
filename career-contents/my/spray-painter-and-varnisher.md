## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a spray painter and varnisher in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level spray painters earn around RM1,500 to RM2,000 per month, while experienced workers can make RM2,500 to RM4,000. Specialists in automotive or industrial finishing may earn up to RM5,000. Overtime and piece-rate work are common, boosting overall pay.

**Q: What skills are required to become a spray painter and varnisher in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include surface preparation, mixing paints and thinners, operating spray equipment, and achieving smooth finishes. Knowledge of safety procedures and ventilation is crucial. Attention to detail, color matching, and patience are essential. Many learn through vocational training or apprenticeships with experienced painters.

**Q: How is the career outlook for spray painters and varnishers in Malaysia given automation?**
A: While automated painting systems are used in large factories, many tasks still require manual skill, especially for custom or repair jobs. Demand remains steady in automotive, furniture, and construction sectors. Skilled painters who adapt to new tools and safety standards will continue to find opportunities.
