## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a lifting truck operator in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a lifting truck operator in Malaysia ranges from RM 1,800 to RM 3,000, depending on experience and industry. Entry-level operators may start lower, while those with certifications or working in heavy industries like logistics or manufacturing earn higher wages. Overtime and allowances can supplement income.

**Q: What are the key skills required to become a lifting truck operator in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include operating forklifts, pallet jacks, or cranes safely; understanding load capacities and balance; basic maintenance checks; and following workplace safety regulations. In Malaysia, a valid forklift operator license from NIOSH or CIDB is often required. Physical stamina and attention to detail are also important.

**Q: How is automation and AI affecting the role of lifting truck operators in Malaysia?**
A: Automation and AI are gradually impacting the role, with automated guided vehicles (AGVs) handling repetitive tasks in warehouses. However, lifting truck operators remain essential for complex maneuvers and irregular loads. In Malaysia, the outlook is stable, with demand in manufacturing, logistics, and construction sectors. Upskilling to operate advanced equipment can enhance job security.
