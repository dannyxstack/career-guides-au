## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level farm labourers earn around RM1,200 to RM1,800 per month. Experienced workers can earn up to RM2,500, depending on farm size and location. Some employers provide accommodation and meals, which can effectively increase take-home pay.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Malaysia?**
A: Daily tasks include feeding and watering livestock, cleaning animal pens, planting and harvesting crops, operating simple farm machinery, and maintaining fences. Labourers also monitor animal health and report issues. Work is physically demanding and often outdoors in varying weather.

**Q: What skills are needed to work as a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Malaysia?**
A: Basic knowledge of crop cultivation and animal care is helpful. Physical stamina, ability to follow instructions, and willingness to work early mornings or weekends are important. On-the-job training is common, but prior experience in farming is advantageous. Basic literacy for record-keeping may be required.
