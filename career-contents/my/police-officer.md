## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a police officer in Malaysia?**
A: A police constable's starting salary is around RM2,500–RM3,000 per month, including allowances. With promotions and years of service, senior inspectors can earn RM8,000–RM10,000. Additional benefits include housing, medical, and pension schemes.

**Q: What is the career progression like for police officers in Malaysia?**
A: Career progression is structured through exams and performance. Officers start as constables and can rise to corporal, sergeant, inspector, and higher ranks. The Royal Malaysia Police offers training programmes and specialist paths in criminal investigation, traffic, or logistics.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the role of police officers in Malaysia?**
A: AI assists in analysing CCTV footage, facial recognition, and crime prediction analytics. Officers now need digital literacy to operate advanced systems. However, human judgment remains essential for investigations and community engagement, ensuring AI complements rather than replaces police work.
