## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Systems Administrators in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level Systems Administrators earn around RM 36,000 to RM 48,000 annually, while mid-level professionals earn RM 60,000 to RM 84,000. Senior roles can exceed RM 108,000, depending on certifications like Microsoft or Linux and experience in cloud platforms.

**Q: What key technical skills are required for Systems Administrators in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include managing Windows/Linux servers, networking (TCP/IP, DNS), virtualization (VMware, Hyper-V), and cloud services (AWS/Azure). Scripting in PowerShell or Python and knowledge of cybersecurity practices are also highly valued in Malaysia’s growing IT sector.

**Q: How is AI impacting Systems Administrators in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks such as patch management and monitoring, allowing admins to focus on strategic projects. However, demand remains strong for professionals who can integrate AI tools and manage hybrid systems. Upskilling in AIOps and automation frameworks is becoming important for career growth in Malaysia.
