**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic refractometer measures prescription and lens selection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted analysis of customer facial features to recommend optimal frame styles.
- Smart system provides personalized lens feature recommendations (blue light filtering, progressive multifocal).
- Automated customer appointment and follow-up system to improve efficiency.
- AI tool training helps opticians quickly learn new product knowledge
- Data-driven optimization of workflows and inventory management

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and adjustment of complex prescriptions (e.g., prisms, high astigmatism)
- Face-to-face customer communication and trust building
- Custom frame adjustment (physical fit and comfort)
- Professional consultation for treating eye abnormalities or heterophoria
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration (optometrists, ophthalmologists) and legal liability

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a dispensing optician in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level dispensing opticians in Malaysia earn between RM 1,800 and RM 2,500 per month. With experience, salaries rise to RM 3,000–RM 5,000. Those in managerial roles or specialized clinics can earn RM 5,500–RM 7,000, depending on location and employer.

**Q: What skills are essential for a dispensing optician in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include precise measurement of pupillary distance and frame fitting, knowledge of Malaysian optical standards, lens selection and ordering, and strong customer service. Familiarity with digital lens edgers, computerized refraction systems, and bilingual communication (English and Malay) is increasingly important.

**Q: What is the career outlook for dispensing opticians in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable, driven by rising vision awareness and an aging population. Opportunities exist in optical retail chains, hospitals, and specialty clinics. Obtaining certification from the Malaysian Optical Council can enhance career advancement. Growth is expected to remain steady over the next decade.
