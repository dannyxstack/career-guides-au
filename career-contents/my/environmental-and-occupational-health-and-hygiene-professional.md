## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental and occupational health professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level positions earn around RM 2,500 to RM 4,000 per month, while experienced professionals can earn RM 5,000 to RM 10,000. Salaries vary by sector (government, private, consulting) and location, with higher pay in urban areas and industries like oil and gas or manufacturing.

**Q: What does a typical workday look like for an environmental health officer in Malaysia?**
A: A typical day involves inspecting workplaces for hazards, reviewing safety data, conducting exposure assessments, and implementing health programs. They also provide training, investigate incidents, and ensure compliance with DOSH regulations. Work is often split between office tasks and field visits to factories, construction sites, or hospitals.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting environmental and occupational health roles in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating data analysis from monitoring sensors, enabling real-time hazard detection and predictive risk assessments. However, professionals still need strong interpersonal skills for training and enforcement. AI tools are supplementing but not replacing the need for expert judgment in complex regulatory and emergency situations.
