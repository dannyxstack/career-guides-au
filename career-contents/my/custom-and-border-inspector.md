## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Customs and Border Inspector in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level Customs and Border Inspectors in Malaysia earn between RM 3,000 and RM 5,500 per month. Senior inspectors with experience can earn up to RM 8,000 monthly, plus allowances for shift work and field duties. Salaries are determined by the Royal Malaysian Customs Department's pay scale and may vary based on qualifications and location.

**Q: What skills are essential for a Customs and Border Inspector in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of Malaysian customs laws and trade regulations, attention to detail for inspecting goods and documents, integrity to handle sensitive information, and communication skills in Malay and English. Physical fitness for patrols and proficiency in using inspection technology are also important. Training is provided by the Royal Malaysian Customs Department.

**Q: How is AI impacting the work of Customs and Border Inspectors in Malaysia?**
A: AI is used for automated risk assessment of cargo and passengers, speeding up clearance processes. However, inspectors still perform physical inspections, enforcement, and public interactions. AI enhances efficiency but does not replace human judgment. The role is evolving to require more data analysis skills alongside traditional inspection duties.
