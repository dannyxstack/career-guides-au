## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for messengers and package deliverers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level messengers and package deliverers earn around RM 1,500 to RM 2,500 per month, depending on location and employer. Experienced workers or those in high-demand areas like Kuala Lumpur may earn up to RM 3,500. Some are paid per delivery or receive allowances for transportation and meals.

**Q: What skills are essential for a messenger or package deliverer in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include navigational knowledge of local areas, time management, and physical stamina for carrying loads. Basic literacy for handling delivery documents and using smartphone apps or GPS is important. Customer service skills are valued for interactions. A valid driving license is often required for motorized deliveries.

**Q: How is AI affecting messengers and package deliverers in Malaysia?**
A: AI-driven route optimization and delivery tracking apps are increasing efficiency, reducing the need for manual planning. Drones and autonomous vehicles are being tested but not widespread. While AI may automate some tasks, human workers remain essential for last-mile delivery in congested areas and handling special requirements.
