## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a farming, forestry and fisheries adviser in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level advisers earn around RM 3,000 to RM 4,500 per month, while experienced advisers in government agencies or large plantations can earn RM 6,000 to RM 8,000. Senior consultants or those with specialized expertise may receive up to RM 10,000 monthly.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for an adviser in Malaysia?**
A: Advisers visit farms, plantations, or fishing communities to provide technical guidance on crop management, pest control, sustainable practices, and compliance with regulations. They analyze soil or water samples, conduct training sessions, and prepare reports for stakeholders.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of agricultural advisers in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools like drone-based crop monitoring, predictive analytics for weather and pests, and automated data collection are becoming common. Advisers must interpret AI-generated insights to give precise recommendations, requiring new skills in digital agriculture and data analysis.
