## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for hunters and trappers in Malaysia?**
A: Hunters and trappers in Malaysia typically earn between RM 1,500 and RM 3,500 per month, depending on experience, location, and the type of game. Those working in remote areas or for licensed wildlife operations may earn higher, but income can be irregular due to seasonal factors and conservation restrictions.

**Q: What essential skills are needed for hunters and trappers in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of local wildlife behavior and habitats, proficiency in using traps and firearms safely, navigation skills in dense forests, and understanding of conservation laws. Physical stamina, patience, and the ability to work independently are crucial. Additionally, basic first aid and survival skills are important for remote work.

**Q: What is the career outlook for hunters and trappers in Malaysia?**
A: Career opportunities are limited and declining due to strict wildlife protection laws and declining forest areas. Most positions are with government agencies for pest control or research, or as licensed guides for traditional hunting. Demand is low, and many hunters transition to ecotourism or conservation roles. AI and drones are increasingly used for monitoring, reducing reliance on traditional trapping.
