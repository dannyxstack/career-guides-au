## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level inspectors earn around RM 2,500–RM 3,500 monthly, while senior professionals with 5+ years can earn RM 5,000–RM 7,000. Those in private sector or with specialized certifications may receive higher pay. Salaries vary by employer, experience, and location, with urban areas offering slightly more.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Malaysia?**
A: Daily tasks include inspecting workplaces for compliance with occupational safety and health regulations, monitoring environmental pollution levels, collecting samples (air, water, noise), reviewing safety documentation, advising employers on corrective actions, and preparing inspection reports. Field visits are common, balancing office documentation with site inspections.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Malaysia?**
A: Demand is steady due to increasing regulatory focus on workplace safety and environmental protection under DOSH and DOE. Growth is driven by industrialization, construction, and manufacturing sectors. Opportunities exist in government agencies, consulting firms, and large corporations. Automation may streamline data collection but professional judgment remains essential.
