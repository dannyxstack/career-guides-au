## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Answer standard FAQ questions (e.g. account balance, business hours)
- Handling simple order changes, returns, and exchange requests
- Transfer complex complaints to human agents
- Record and update customer information in the CRM system
- Send standard confirmation emails or SMS notifications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time translation, supporting multilingual customer service
- Emotion recognition alerts to help agents adjust communication strategies
- Smart knowledge base recommendations for quickly locating solutions to complex issues
- Automatic call summary generation reduces post-call documentation time
- Predict customer intent and prepare response scripts in advance

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly emotional, sensitive, or sudden complaints
- Coordinate across departments to solve complex non-standard issues.
- Build customer trust and maintain long-term relationships
- Respond to unforeseen emergencies (e.g., system failures)
- Deliver personalized, empathetic service experiences

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in major AI customer service platforms (e.g., Zendesk AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Learn basic data analysis (SQL/Excel) to analyze customer trends.
- Enhance conflict management and emotional communication skills
- Master the principles of Conversational Design
- Understand automation process building tools (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere)
- Develop multilingual skills (especially English) to serve international clients

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Contact Centre Information Clerks in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level Contact Centre Information Clerks in Malaysia earn between RM 2,000 and RM 2,800 per month. With 1-3 years of experience, salaries range from RM 2,800 to RM 3,800. Senior clerks or team leaders can earn RM 4,000 to RM 6,000. Salaries vary by city, with Kuala Lumpur offering higher pay, and by industry, such as banking or telecommunications.

**Q: What are the key skills needed to succeed as a Contact Centre Information Clerk in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include fluency in English and Bahasa Malaysia (both spoken and written), good typing speed (at least 35-40 wpm), and basic computer proficiency in CRM software. Strong communication, active listening, and problem-solving abilities are crucial. Many employers prefer candidates with a SPM or diploma, but on-the-job technical training is often provided.

**Q: How is AI impacting Contact Centre Information Clerk roles in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating routine queries through chatbots, reducing the volume of basic calls. However, Malaysian clerks are increasingly handling complex, escalated issues requiring human empathy and judgment. AI tools assist by providing real-time data and script suggestions. While some entry-level tasks may decline, demand remains for skilled clerks who can manage digital channels and work alongside AI.
