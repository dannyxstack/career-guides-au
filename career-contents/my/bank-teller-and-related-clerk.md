## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for bank tellers in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level bank tellers in Malaysia earn between RM 1,800 and RM 2,500 per month. With experience, salaries can reach RM 3,000 to RM 4,000. Major banks may offer slightly higher pay, and performance bonuses are common. Salary also varies by location, with Kuala Lumpur and Penang offering higher ranges.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a bank teller in Malaysia?**
A: Bank tellers in Malaysia need strong numeracy, attention to detail, and customer service skills. Proficiency in Malay and English is essential, while Mandarin or Tamil is advantageous for serving diverse clients. Basic computer literacy and familiarity with banking software are required. Integrity and ability to handle cash accurately are critical.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of bank tellers in Malaysian banks?**
A: AI and automation are reducing routine teller tasks like cash counting and transaction processing through self-service kiosks and digital banking. However, Malaysian banks still employ tellers for complex customer inquiries, relationship building, and fraud detection. Tellers are encouraged to upskill in advisory roles to remain relevant.
