## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Sales Demonstrator in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level sales demonstrators in Malaysia earn between RM 1,800 and RM 2,500 per month, while experienced ones can make RM 3,000 to RM 4,500. Commissions and bonuses based on product sales are common, especially in retail and roadshow roles. Pay varies by location (KL higher) and industry (electronics, cosmetics, FMCG).

**Q: What skills are essential to succeed as a Sales Demonstrator in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include strong communication in Bahasa Malaysia and English, persuasive presentation, and product knowledge. Being outgoing, energetic, and comfortable approaching strangers is crucial. Basic digital skills for POS systems and social media demos are increasingly valued. Multilingual ability (e.g., Mandarin) is an advantage for malls in diverse areas.

**Q: How is AI impacting the career of Sales Demonstrators in Malaysia?**
A: AI is not replacing sales demonstrators yet, but it is changing roles. Automated product kiosks and online demos reduce the need for some in-store staff. However, demonstrators are still valued for personal interaction, trust-building, and real-time Q&A. With AI handling basic queries, demonstrators focus more on upselling, customer experience, and live events—skills that require human empathy.
