## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Building Structure Cleaner in Malaysia?**
A: The monthly salary for building structure cleaners in Malaysia typically ranges from RM1,200 to RM2,500 for entry-level positions. Experienced cleaners, especially those working on high-rise buildings in Kuala Lumpur or Penang, can earn up to RM3,500. Pay varies by employer, location, and scope of work, with some companies providing allowances for safety gear and transportation.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Building Structure Cleaners in Malaysia?**
A: The demand for building structure cleaners is steady due to ongoing urbanization and maintenance needs of commercial and residential buildings. However, automation and robotic cleaning systems are gradually reducing the need for manual labor, especially in routine window cleaning. Workers who upskill in operating advanced equipment or specialize in high-rise safety may find better long-term prospects.

**Q: What skills are required to become a Building Structure Cleaner in Malaysia?**
A: Key skills include physical fitness, comfort with heights, and attention to detail. Knowledge of cleaning chemicals, equipment like water-fed poles or harnesses, and safety protocols is essential. Certification in working at heights or scaffolding is an advantage. Communication skills in Malay or English help coordinate with site supervisors. Most training is on-the-job, but formal courses are available.
