## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for other language teachers in Malaysia?**
A: Salaries for other language teachers in Malaysia vary by experience and institution. At private language centers, entry-level teachers earn around RM 3,000-RM 4,500 monthly. Experienced teachers at international schools can earn RM 6,000-RM 9,000. University lecturers with advanced degrees may earn RM 8,000-RM 12,000. Part-time rates range from RM 50-RM 120 per hour.

**Q: What is the career outlook for language teachers in Malaysia?**
A: Demand for other language teachers in Malaysia is stable, driven by globalization and trade ties with China, Japan, Korea, and Arab countries. Opportunities exist in private language centers, international schools, and corporate training. Growth is steady, especially for teachers of Mandarin, Arabic, and Japanese. However, competition is higher for popular languages like English.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of language teachers in Malaysia?**
A: AI tools like translation apps and adaptive learning platforms are changing language teaching in Malaysia. While AI aids in personalized practice and assessment, it cannot replace human interaction for developing fluency, cultural nuance, and communication skills. Teachers who integrate AI as a supplement remain in demand, especially for advanced conversation and business language training.
