## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for policy administration professionals in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, entry-level policy administration professionals earn around RM 2,500 to RM 4,000 monthly. With 3–5 years of experience, salaries range from RM 4,500 to RM 7,000. Senior roles or those in insurance and financial services can earn RM 8,000 to RM 12,000 per month, depending on the company and qualifications.

**Q: What key skills are required for policy administration professionals in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include attention to detail, knowledge of insurance regulations and policy documentation, proficiency in office software and policy management systems, strong communication, and the ability to interpret complex policy terms. Analytical thinking and customer service skills are also valued.

**Q: How is AI affecting policy administration jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks like data entry, claims processing, and policy renewals. However, professionals are still needed for complex case handling, compliance, and client advisory. Upskilling in data analysis and AI tool management can enhance career prospects. The role is evolving rather than disappearing.
