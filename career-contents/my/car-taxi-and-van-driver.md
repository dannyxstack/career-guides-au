## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for car, taxi, and van drivers in Malaysia?**
A: In Malaysia, car, taxi, and van drivers typically earn between RM 1,500 and RM 3,500 per month, depending on hours, location, and vehicle type. Ride-hailing drivers may earn more but face platform fees. Income is variable; many drivers work on commission or as independent contractors.

**Q: What skills are required to become a successful driver in Malaysia?**
A: Required skills include a valid Malaysian driving license (Class D or E), good knowledge of local roads and traffic conditions, customer service orientation, basic vehicle maintenance, and navigation app proficiency. Physical stamina and patience are important for long hours. Many drivers also benefit from multilingual abilities to serve diverse passengers.

**Q: How is AI affecting car, taxi, and van driving jobs in Malaysia?**
A: Ride-hailing apps like Grab have transformed the industry, increasing competition but also providing flexible income opportunities. Autonomous vehicles are not yet widespread in Malaysia, so driver jobs remain stable. However, drivers should adapt to digital platforms and consider upgrading to commercial licenses for vans to stay competitive.
