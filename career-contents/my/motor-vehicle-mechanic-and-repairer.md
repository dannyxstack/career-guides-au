## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Malaysia?**
A: Entry-level mechanics earn around RM 1,500–RM 2,500 monthly. Experienced mechanics with specialized skills (e.g., air conditioning, electronics) can earn RM 3,000–RM 5,000. Master technicians or those with certification may earn RM 6,000 or more, especially in high-end dealerships or workshops. Overtime and commission on repairs can boost income.

**Q: What key skills are required for motor vehicle mechanics in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include diagnostic proficiency, mechanical aptitude, and knowledge of modern electronic systems. Certifications from institutions like National Occupational Skills Standard (NOSS) or TVET are valued. Soft skills like customer service and problem-solving are important. Familiarity with hybrid/electric vehicles is increasingly beneficial due to growing adoption.

**Q: What is the career outlook for motor vehicle mechanics in Malaysia?**
A: The outlook is stable, with steady demand from the growing vehicle population. However, mechanics must upskill to handle advanced technologies like ADAS, EV systems, and computerized diagnostics. AI-assisted tools are becoming common, but hands-on repair remains crucial. Certification and specialization (e.g., air-conditioning, transmission) enhance employability.
