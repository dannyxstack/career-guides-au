## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Malaysia?**
A: The average monthly salary typically ranges from RM 2,000 to RM 3,500 for entry-level positions, with experienced workers earning up to RM 5,000. Salaries vary by industry, location (e.g., Kuala Lumpur vs. smaller cities), and company size. Additional benefits like overtime pay and allowances may apply.

**Q: What key skills are required for clerical support roles in Malaysia?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel), strong written and verbal English and Bahasa Malaysia, attention to detail, organizational abilities, and basic bookkeeping. Familiarity with office equipment and digital communication tools like email and scheduling software is also important. Multitasking and customer service skills are highly valued.

**Q: How is AI impacting clerical support jobs in Malaysia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like data entry, scheduling, and invoice processing, reducing demand for purely manual roles. However, it also creates opportunities for workers skilled in overseeing AI tools, handling exceptions, and managing digital workflows. Upskilling in areas like data analysis and software management can help clerical workers adapt and remain relevant.
