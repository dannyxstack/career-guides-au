## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for motor vehicle mechanics in Luxembourg vary by experience and employer. Entry-level mechanics earn around €2,800–€3,200 gross per month, while experienced mechanics can earn €3,500–€4,500 or more. The statutory minimum wage for skilled workers is €3,085.11 per month as of 2025. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers and performance bonuses.

**Q: What work visa options are available for motor vehicle mechanics moving to Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU mechanics can work in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a recognized diploma and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. €5,314/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work visa (authorisation de séjour pour salarié) applies for lower-skilled roles, requiring employer sponsorship and proof that no local candidate is available.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for motor vehicle mechanics in Luxembourg?**
A: Mechanics can advance to master mechanic, workshop manager, or specialist in electric/hybrid vehicles. Luxembourg offers vocational training (e.g., DAP in mechanics) and continuing education through the Chamber of Trades. With experience, one can open their own garage or move into automotive sales or technical inspection. The automotive sector also values certifications in new vehicle technologies.
