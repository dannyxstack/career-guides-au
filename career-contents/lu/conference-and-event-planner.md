## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for conference and event planners in Luxembourg?**
A: The average gross salary for conference and event planners in Luxembourg ranges from €45,000 to €70,000 per year, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €38,000, while senior planners can earn up to €85,000. Salaries are generally higher in the financial and business sectors.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU conference and event planners to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized university degree and a job offer with a salary above the threshold (approx. €60,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit (authorisation de séjour) is available for skilled workers, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications. Both routes require approval from the Luxembourg Ministry of Labour.

**Q: Are there specific certifications required to work as an event planner in Luxembourg?**
A: No mandatory certification exists, but many employers prefer a degree in event management, hospitality, or business. Professional certifications like CMP (Certified Meeting Professional) can enhance prospects. Fluency in English is essential; knowledge of French, German, or Luxembourgish is a strong advantage given the multilingual business environment.
