## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned armed forces officer in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries follow the public sector pay scale. For example, a sergeant earns approximately €3,500 gross per month, with increases based on rank and seniority. Additional allowances for housing or hazardous duty may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a non-commissioned officer in Luxembourg?**
A: No. The Luxembourg Armed Forces require Luxembourgish citizenship for all military roles, including non-commissioned officers. The EU Blue Card and standard work visa routes are not applicable, as these positions are reserved for nationals due to national security regulations.

**Q: What training is required to become a non-commissioned officer in the Luxembourg Army?**
A: Candidates must complete basic military training, followed by a specialized NCO course at the Army Training Centre. Promotion to higher ranks requires passing exams, additional courses, and several years of service. Continuous professional development is mandatory.
