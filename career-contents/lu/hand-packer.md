## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand packer in Luxembourg?**
A: Hand packers in Luxembourg earn a gross monthly salary generally between the statutory minimum wage (€2,570.93 as of 2024 for unskilled workers) and €3,200, depending on experience, shift allowances, and company size. Collective bargaining agreements in the packaging and logistics sectors often set slightly higher rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a hand packer in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not available for hand packers because it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Instead, non-EU workers can obtain a standard work permit (autorisation de travail) if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally and meets labor market conditions.

**Q: What are the typical employment settings for hand packers in Luxembourg?**
A: Hand packers are primarily employed in manufacturing, logistics, and warehousing sectors. Common employers include large industrial companies in the steel, automotive, and food processing industries, as well as e-commerce fulfillment centers and third-party logistics providers in the south of the country.
