## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical records technician in Luxembourg?**
A: Entry-level positions start around €35,000–€40,000 gross per year, while experienced technicians can earn €50,000–€60,000. Public sector roles often follow collective agreements with additional benefits. Private clinics and hospitals may offer higher pay for specialized skills.

**Q: Can I work as a medical records technician in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. The EU Blue Card is available for non-EU nationals with a recognized higher education degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€100,800 in 2024). For this occupation, meeting the threshold may require senior or specialized roles.

**Q: What are the main employers for medical records technicians in Luxembourg?**
A: Major employers include the Centre Hospitalier de Luxembourg (CHL), Hôpitaux Robert Schuman, and other public hospitals. Private clinics, health insurance companies, and government agencies like the Caisse Nationale de Santé also hire. Some technicians work for EHR implementation firms.
