## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a taxi or van driver in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for taxi and van drivers in Luxembourg are generally based on collective bargaining agreements. As of 2025, the minimum gross monthly salary for a qualified driver is around €3,200, but actual pay varies by employer, experience, and hours. Drivers may also receive tips and overtime compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a taxi or van driver in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a university degree and a high salary threshold. Taxi and van driver roles do not usually meet these criteria. Non-EU nationals instead need a standard work authorization (authorisation de travail), which requires employer sponsorship and a labor market check.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a taxi driver in Luxembourg?**
A: To drive a taxi in Luxembourg, you must hold a valid driving license (category B for at least two years), pass a medical exam, and obtain a professional capacity certificate from the Ministry of Mobility. You also need a clean criminal record. For van drivers, a standard driving license is usually sufficient, but additional permits may be required for goods transport.
