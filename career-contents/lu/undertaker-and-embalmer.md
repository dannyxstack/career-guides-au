## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an undertaker or embalmer in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for undertakers and embalmers in Luxembourg are typically governed by collective bargaining agreements in the funeral services sector. Entry-level positions may start around €2,500–€3,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €3,500–€4,500 or more. Actual pay depends on employer, qualifications, and experience.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU national to work as an undertaker/embalmer in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit sponsored by a Luxembourg employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a recognized degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average; this occupation may not always meet the threshold. Alternatively, a standard work permit (authorisation de travail) is possible if the employer proves no suitable candidate in the EU.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an undertaker or embalmer in Luxembourg?**
A: Becoming an undertaker or embalmer in Luxembourg usually requires a professional diploma in funeral services or equivalent training, often from a recognized European institution. Embalmers may need specific certification in thanatopraxy. Practical experience through an apprenticeship is common. Knowledge of Luxembourg’s regulations and languages (French, German, Luxembourgish) is advantageous.
