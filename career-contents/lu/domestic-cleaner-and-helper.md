**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Schedule cleaning appointments, send reminders, and collect payments via chatbot

- Automated inventory management: predict cleaning supply needs based on historical data and place orders
- Voice assistants control smart home devices (e.g., robot vacuum cleaners, automatic curtains)

- Smart devices (e.g., stain detection apps) to assist in choosing the best cleaning agent
- Data analysis tools optimize cleaning routes and time allocation

- Building long-term trust relationships and personalized services with employers

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Luxembourg?**
A: The statutory minimum wage in Luxembourg (as of 2023) is €2,570 per month for unskilled workers. Domestic cleaners typically earn at least this amount, though actual wages may vary by employer, experience, or collective agreements. Some positions, especially live-in roles, may include benefits like accommodation.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU nationals to work as domestic cleaners in Luxembourg?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not a typical route for domestic cleaners, as it requires highly qualified workers (university degree or high salary). Non-EU nationals generally need a standard work permit (autorisation de travail) obtained by an employer. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available, and the permit is tied to that specific job.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for domestic cleaners in Luxembourg?**
A: Domestic cleaners can advance to team leader or supervisor roles in cleaning companies, or specialize in areas like industrial or deep cleaning. Further training in facility management or hygiene standards may open doors to higher-paying positions. However, career growth is limited without additional qualifications, and many remain in domestic service long-term.
