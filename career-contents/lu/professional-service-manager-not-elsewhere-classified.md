## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a professional services manager in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for professional services managers in Luxembourg vary by sector, but typically range from €70,000 to €120,000 gross per year. Senior roles in consulting, legal, or financial services command higher pay, often exceeding €150,000. Bonuses and benefits like meal vouchers and health insurance are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for professional services managers moving to Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work as a professional services manager via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, requiring a recognized degree and a job offer with salary above €58,968 (2024 threshold). Alternatively, a standard work permit (authorisation de travail) applies for roles meeting Luxembourg's job market criteria.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for this role in Luxembourg?**
A: Employers usually seek a master's degree in business, finance, or law, plus several years of relevant experience. Fluency in English, French, or German is often essential, with many roles requiring at least two languages. Professional certifications like PMP or CFA can strengthen applications.
