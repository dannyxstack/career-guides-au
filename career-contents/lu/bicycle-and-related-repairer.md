## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bicycle repairer in Luxembourg?**
A: Entry-level bicycle repairers earn around €2,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can expect €3,500 or more. Salaries vary based on employer, certifications, and location, but overall they align with Luxembourg's high cost of living. The minimum wage for skilled workers is about €2,500, so repairers typically earn above that.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU bicycle repairers in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU bicycle repairers need a work permit, which requires an employer to prove the job cannot be filled by a local or EU worker. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for this occupation, as it demands a higher education degree and a salary above a set threshold (approx. €58,000). A standard work visa under Luxembourg’s labour migration scheme is the usual route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bicycle repairer in Luxembourg?**
A: A vocational diploma in bicycle mechanics (e.g., CAP or DAP) is highly valued. Alternatively, on-the-job training combined with relevant experience is accepted. Certification from manufacturers like Shimano or Bosch can improve job prospects. Basic knowledge of French, German, or Luxembourgish is beneficial for customer interaction.
