## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships’ deck crew in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for ships’ deck crew in Luxembourg vary by role and experience. Entry-level deckhands earn around €2,500–€3,000 gross per month, while experienced crew can earn €3,500–€4,500. Due to Luxembourg's landlocked geography, most positions are on inland waterways.

**Q: What is the work visa route for ships’ deck crew to work in Luxembourg as a non-EU national?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Luxembourg as ships’ deck crew via a standard work permit (authorisation de travail) if sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is an option for higher-skilled roles (e.g., officers) requiring a degree and salary meeting the threshold. Lower-skilled deck crew typically do not qualify for the Blue Card.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as ships’ deck crew in Luxembourg?**
A: To work as deck crew in Luxembourg, you need STCW Basic Safety Training and seafarer documents recognized by the Commissariat aux Affaires Maritimes (CMML). For inland waterways, a Rhine navigation certificate is often required. Prior experience on river vessels is an advantage.
