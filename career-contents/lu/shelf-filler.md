## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for shelf fillers in Luxembourg?**
A: Shelf fillers in Luxembourg typically earn at least the statutory minimum wage, which is indexed and adjusted regularly. For unskilled workers, the minimum monthly salary is around €2,500 for a full-time position. Actual pay may vary by employer and experience, and collective bargaining agreements may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as shelf fillers in Luxembourg via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa to take up employment as a shelf filler in Luxembourg. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a highly qualified position with a higher education degree and a salary threshold. Instead, standard work permits are possible but subject to labour market tests proving no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are common working conditions for shelf fillers in Luxembourg?**
A: Shelf fillers in Luxembourg often work part-time or full-time, including weekends and evenings. Shifts typically range from 20 to 40 hours per week. The job involves physical activity such as lifting and stocking shelves. Employment is usually in supermarkets or retail chains, with limited career progression without additional training.
