## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a window cleaner in Luxembourg?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a window cleaner in Luxembourg ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience, employer, and contract type. Entry-level positions start around €2,000, while experienced cleaners can earn up to €4,000. Many employers also provide benefits such as meal vouchers and transportation allowances.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a window cleaner in Luxembourg via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit sponsored by a Luxembourg employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for window cleaners as it targets highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary above €58,968 (2024). Instead, a standard work permit (autorisation de travail) is the typical route, requiring the employer to prove the position cannot be filled by a local or EU candidate.

**Q: What language skills are needed for a window cleaner in Luxembourg?**
A: Fluency in French is essential, as it is the primary language for most workplaces and client interactions. Basic knowledge of German or Luxembourgish is a plus, especially when working in the north or for German-speaking clients. English is less common but can be useful in international settings. Many cleaning companies provide on-the-job language support.
