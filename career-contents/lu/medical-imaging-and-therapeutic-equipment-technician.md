## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for this role in Luxembourg are competitive, typically ranging from €50,000 to €70,000 gross per year, depending on experience, qualifications, and the employing sector (public hospital vs. private clinic). Additional benefits such as meal vouchers and health insurance are common.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as medical imaging technicians in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer in Luxembourg meeting the minimum salary threshold. This process requires recognition of their professional qualifications by the Luxembourg Ministry of Health. The employer must also demonstrate that the position could not be filled by a local or EU candidate.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a medical imaging technician in Luxembourg?**
A: You need a recognized diploma in radiologic technology or equivalent, often a Bachelor’s degree or a professional certification. For non-EU degrees, recognition by the Ministry of Health is mandatory. Fluency in French or German is typically required, and knowledge of Luxembourgish is an advantage.
