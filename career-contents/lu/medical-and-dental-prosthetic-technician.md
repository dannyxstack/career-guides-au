## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical and dental prosthetic technician in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Luxembourg are competitive, reflecting the high cost of living. Entry-level positions typically start around €3,000 gross per month, while experienced technicians can earn up to €5,000 or more, depending on the employer and specialisation. Collective bargaining agreements in the healthcare sector often set pay scales.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU medical and dental prosthetic technicians to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a standard work visa (authorisation de séjour) with a job offer and proof of qualifications. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers holding a recognised diploma and a job offer with a salary meeting the annual threshold (set by national authorities). Both routes require employer sponsorship and labour market testing.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practise as a medical and dental prosthetic technician in Luxembourg?**
A: Practitioners need a recognised diploma in dental or medical prosthetics, typically from an EU/EEA country, or equivalent training. Foreign qualifications must be approved by the Ministry of Health. Luxembourg also offers vocational training (CAP) in dental technology. Continuing education is encouraged for career advancement into specialisations or management roles.
