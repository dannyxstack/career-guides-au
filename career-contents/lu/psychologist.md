## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for psychologists in Luxembourg?**
A: Psychologists in Luxembourg earn competitive salaries, generally ranging from €50,000 to €80,000 gross annually, depending on experience, sector (public, private practice, or corporate), and specialization. Benefits often include additional bonuses and social security coverage. Entry-level positions start around €45,000, while senior roles can exceed €90,000.

**Q: Can non-EU psychologists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Luxembourg?**
A: Yes, non-EU psychologists can work in Luxembourg via an EU Blue Card if they hold a recognised master’s degree in psychology and a job offer meeting the legal salary threshold. Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for shorter contracts or if the salary threshold is not met. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Ministry of Labour.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a psychologist in Luxembourg?**
A: To practice as a psychologist in Luxembourg, you need a master’s degree in psychology from a recognised university and registration with the Ministry of Health. Foreign qualifications must undergo a recognition procedure. Additionally, a certificate of professional competence (CCP) may be required, and ongoing continuing education is mandatory to maintain licensure.
