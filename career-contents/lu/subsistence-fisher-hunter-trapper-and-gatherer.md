## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for subsistence fishers, hunters, trappers and gatherers in Luxembourg?**
A: Since these are subsistence activities for personal consumption rather than paid employment, there is no standard salary. In Luxembourg, such occupations are not recognized as formal employment, and individuals engaging in them do not receive wages. They may require permits from the Administration de la nature et des forêts.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a subsistence fisher, hunter, trapper or gatherer in Luxembourg under the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card and other work visas in Luxembourg are intended for highly skilled or formally employed workers. Subsistence fishing, hunting, trapping and gathering are not considered gainful employment. Non-EU nationals cannot obtain a residence permit solely for these activities. Legal access to hunting and fishing is limited to residents with appropriate permits.

**Q: What regulations apply to subsistence fishing and hunting in Luxembourg?**
A: Subsistence fishing and hunting in Luxembourg are regulated by the Administration de la nature et des forêts. Licenses are required for both activities, and they are generally issued to residents. These activities are not considered a profession but a leisure or subsistence practice. There are seasonal restrictions and species protection laws. For trapping and gathering, additional rules may apply, especially in protected areas.
