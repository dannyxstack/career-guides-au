## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Luxembourg?**
A: Livestock farm labourers in Luxembourg typically earn the minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €2,570.93 per month for unskilled workers. Experienced workers may earn slightly more, but wages generally remain close to minimum wage. Overtime and benefits may be negotiated.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for livestock farm labourers in Luxembourg?**
A: Livestock farm labourers are generally not eligible for the EU Blue Card, as it requires a higher education degree or high-skilled position. Third-country nationals may apply for a temporary work permit (autorisation de séjour pour salarié) if the employer proves no local candidate is available. This is subject to labour market quotas.

**Q: What are the main duties of a livestock farm labourer in Luxembourg?**
A: Livestock farm labourers in Luxembourg handle feeding, watering, and cleaning animals; mucking out stalls; assisting with breeding and births; monitoring animal health; and maintaining farm equipment. Work often involves physical tasks outdoors and adherence to animal welfare and biosecurity regulations.
