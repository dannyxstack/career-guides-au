## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a police inspector or detective in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for police inspectors and detectives in Luxembourg are determined by the public sector pay scale (e.g., state employees). A starting inspector can earn around €4,000–€5,000 gross per month, with experienced detectives earning up to €7,000–€8,000. Additional allowances for shift work and specialization apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a police inspector or detective in Luxembourg?**
A: Police inspector and detective positions in Luxembourg are typically restricted to EU/EEA nationals or Luxembourgish citizens due to national security requirements. Non-EU citizens are generally ineligible for a work visa or EU Blue Card for these roles, as they require nationality or long-term residency.

**Q: What is the career progression for police inspectors and detectives in Luxembourg?**
A: Typically, officers start as police cadets or constables, undergo training at the Luxembourg Police Academy, and after several years of service and competitive exams, can advance to inspector or detective roles. Further progression includes chief inspector, commissioner, and specialized investigative units, often requiring additional certifications.
