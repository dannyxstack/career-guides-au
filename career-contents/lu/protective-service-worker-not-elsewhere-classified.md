## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Luxembourg?**
A: In Luxembourg, salaries for protective services workers (e.g., security guards) generally range from €2,500 to €3,500 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and specific duties. The statutory minimum wage is higher than in most EU countries, and additional benefits may include shift allowances or meal vouchers.

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU nationals to work as protective services workers in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically require a standard work permit (autorisation de travail) sponsored by the employer. The EU Blue Card, designed for highly qualified workers, generally does not apply to protective services roles. Applicants must secure a job offer and the employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for protective services jobs in Luxembourg?**
A: Mandatory qualifications include a security guard certificate (obtained through approved training), a clean criminal record, and often fluency in French or German. Some roles require additional skills like first aid. Career progression can lead to supervisory positions such as team leader or security manager, sometimes requiring further certifications.
