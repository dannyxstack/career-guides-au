## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much do fashion models typically earn in Luxembourg?**
A: Modeling income in Luxembourg varies widely by project, experience, and type (e.g., runway, commercial). Day rates can range from €100 to over €2,000. Most models work freelance, so income is irregular. There is no official minimum for models, but all workers must be paid at least the national minimum wage (€2,508/month for skilled workers in 2025).

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card process for models in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU models need a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers earning at least €56,115.40 gross/year (2025 threshold). If the modeling job meets this salary, you can apply. Otherwise, a standard temporary work permit (autorisation de travail) is required, with employer sponsorship. Both require a job offer and approval from the Ministry of Economy.

**Q: What are the career requirements for models in Luxembourg?**
A: Success in Luxembourg's modeling market requires a strong portfolio, agency representation (e.g., Modelwerk or Viva Models), and fluency in at least one official language (Luxembourgish, French, or German). The market is small but international, so versatility and availability for cross-border work are key. Competition is high, and contracts are often short-term or per-project.
