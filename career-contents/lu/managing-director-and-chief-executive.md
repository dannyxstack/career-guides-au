## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for managing directors and chief executives in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on company size and sector. A managing director in Luxembourg can expect a gross annual salary between €150,000 and €300,000 plus benefits. Large multinationals or financial sector firms often pay at the higher end, while SMEs may offer lower packages. Bonuses and profit-sharing are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for managing directors and chief executives to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals must obtain a work permit through the Luxembourg Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, requiring a minimum salary (currently €54,519/year) and managing directors typically exceed this. Alternatively, a temporary authorisation to stay (ATS) can be obtained for qualified staff. Employment with a company registered in Luxembourg is mandatory.

**Q: What qualifications or experience are typically required to become a managing director in Luxembourg?**
A: A master's degree in business or related field is common, along with 10-15 years of leadership experience. Proficiency in English and French or German is essential. Sector-specific expertise, a strong network, and understanding of Luxembourg's business culture, which values discretion and consensual decision-making, are highly regarded.
