## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for electrical line installers and repairers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries are typically governed by collective bargaining agreements. Entry-level positions start around €2,500–€3,000 gross per month, while experienced workers can earn €4,000–€5,000 or more. Actual pay depends on qualifications, employer, and shift allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU electrical line installers and repairers to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles, requiring a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Alternatively, a standard work permit (autorisation de séjour) may be obtained if the employer proves no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available.

**Q: Which employers typically hire electrical line installers and repairers in Luxembourg?**
A: Main employers include electricity utilities like Enovos and Creos, construction firms such as Tralux and Socom, and infrastructure companies like Spie. The public sector also hires for maintenance of street lighting and public utilities.
