## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Luxembourg?**
A: Software developers in Luxembourg earn between €55,000 and €85,000 per year, depending on experience and skills. Senior developers or those with specialised expertise can earn over €100,000. Salaries are generally higher than in neighbouring countries due to the high cost of living. Many companies also offer additional benefits like meal vouchers and bonuses.

**Q: How can non-EU software developers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU software developers can apply for an EU Blue Card through a Luxembourg employer. Requirements include a recognised university degree (or equivalent professional experience), a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €60,000 in 2024), and a valid work contract. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by a local or EU candidate.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for software developers in Luxembourg?**
A: Luxembourg's tech sector demands expertise in Java, Python, and JavaScript, along with frameworks like Spring Boot and React. Cloud computing (AWS, Azure) and DevOps skills are highly valued. While English suffices in many tech companies, knowledge of French or German is advantageous, especially for roles interfacing with local clients or government. Cybersecurity and fintech experience also stand out.
