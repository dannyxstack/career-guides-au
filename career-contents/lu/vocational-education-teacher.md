## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a vocational education teacher in Luxembourg?**
A: Starting salaries for vocational education teachers in Luxembourg, based on the civil service scale, range from approximately €50,000 to €60,000 gross per year. With experience and advanced qualifications, salaries can rise to €80,000 or more. Additional allowances may apply for special responsibilities or location.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU vocational education teachers to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU teachers require a work visa (autorisation de séjour avec autorisation de travail) with a job offer and approval from the Ministry of Labour. The EU Blue Card is available if the salary is at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €89,000 in 2024), which is uncommon for teachers. Qualifications must be recognized by the Ministry of Education.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a vocational education teacher in Luxembourg?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in the relevant vocational field plus a pedagogical qualification (e.g., from the University of Luxembourg's Institute of School and Learning) are required. Alternatively, candidates may obtain a bachelor's degree with significant professional experience. Fluency in Luxembourgish, German, or French is essential, as teaching languages include these.
