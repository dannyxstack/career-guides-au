## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a landscape architect in Luxembourg?**
A: In Luxembourg, landscape architects earn between €50,000 and €80,000 gross annually, depending on experience and sector. The average gross monthly salary is around €5,500. Public sector and large private firms may offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU landscape architects to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (around €72,000 annually). Alternatively, a standard work permit requires employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a landscape architect in Luxembourg?**
A: A master's degree in landscape architecture or equivalent is required. For independent practice, registration with the Ordre des Architectes et des Ingénieurs-Conseils (OAI) is mandatory. Proficiency in French, German, or Luxembourgish is often necessary.
