- Repeated on-site demonstrations of product standard functions

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sales demonstrators in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for sales demonstrators in Luxembourg generally range from €2,500 to €3,800 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and sector. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage (€2,508 in 2024), while experienced demonstrators in specialized industries can earn higher. Collective bargaining agreements may apply in some sectors.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU sales demonstrators to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as sales demonstrators in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers, requiring a bachelor’s degree or 5+ years experience and salary ≥1.5x national average) or a standard work permit (authorisation de travail). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. A work contract and valid residence permit are mandatory.

**Q: What skills or certifications are most valued for sales demonstrators in Luxembourg's multilingual market?**
A: Luxembourg’s trilingual environment (Luxembourgish, French, German) highly values language proficiency, especially in French and German. Employers prefer candidates with strong interpersonal skills, product knowledge, and experience in retail or B2C sales. A driver’s license is often required for traveling between locations. Certifications in sales techniques or specific product domains can be advantageous.
