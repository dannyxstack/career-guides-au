**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Survey and Market Research Interviewers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for survey interviewers in Luxembourg generally align with the statutory minimum wage, which as of 2025 is around €2,570 per month for unskilled workers. Experienced interviewers may earn slightly more, up to €3,000 monthly, depending on the employer and language skills. Part-time roles are common.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Survey and Market Research Interviewers in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as survey interviewers in Luxembourg via a standard work visa if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card is typically for highly-skilled roles requiring a higher education degree and a salary threshold (usually above €58,000 annually), which this occupation rarely meets. Standard work authorization is the more common route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Survey and Market Research Interviewer in Luxembourg?**
A: No formal degree is mandatory, but fluency in Luxembourgish, French, or German is often required, as interviews may be conducted in these languages. Employers typically value communication skills and previous experience in customer service or market research. A secondary school diploma is usually sufficient.
