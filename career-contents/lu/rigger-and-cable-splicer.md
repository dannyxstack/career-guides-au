## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for riggers and cable splicers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for riggers and cable splicers in Luxembourg range from €3,500 to €5,500 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. The statutory minimum wage for skilled workers is €2,570.73 per month (2024). Higher pay is common for specialized tasks or large infrastructure projects.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as riggers or cable splicers in Luxembourg via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary (€65,400 in 2024). For lower-paid positions, a standard work permit (autorisation de travail) may be available, subject to labor market tests and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a rigger or cable splicer in Luxembourg?**
A: Riggers and cable splicers typically need a vocational qualification (e.g., CAP/BEP in electrical installation or metalwork) or equivalent experience. EU qualifications are recognized; non-EU credentials may require evaluation by the Ministry of Education. Certification in safety (e.g., SST) is often mandatory. The job is in demand due to ongoing construction and telecom projects.
