## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on the specific craft, experience, and employer. Entry-level workers may earn around €2,500-€3,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €3,500-€5,000 or more. Many positions are covered by collective bargaining agreements, and wages are generally higher than in neighbouring countries.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card options for craft workers in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work via a single permit (work visa) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree or at least 5 years of professional experience, plus a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (threshold updated annually, currently around €60,000). For crafts without a degree, a standard work permit with a labour market test may apply.

**Q: How can I start a career as a craft worker in Luxembourg?**
A: Most craft occupations require vocational training or an apprenticeship, often through Luxembourg’s dual system combining work and school. Registration with ADEM (national employment agency) is advised. Demand exists in construction, manufacturing, and services. Language skills in French, German, or Luxembourgish are beneficial. The Ministry of Education guides on recognition of foreign qualifications.
