## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for technical and medical sales professionals in Luxembourg are among the highest in Europe. Entry-level positions start around €50,000–€60,000 gross annually, while experienced professionals earn €80,000–€120,000. Many roles also include performance-based bonuses, company cars, and other benefits.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU Technical and Medical Sales Professionals to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU professionals require a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a job offer meeting a minimum salary threshold. Alternatively, a standard work permit (authorisation de séjour à titre de salarié) applies, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications. Both routes lead to a residence permit.

**Q: Which industries employ Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Luxembourg?**
A: Key employers include pharmaceutical companies (e.g., satellite organizations of major firms), medical device manufacturers, industrial automation firms, and specialized distributors. The healthcare sector and high-tech manufacturing are prominent, with roles involving direct client management and product demonstration.
