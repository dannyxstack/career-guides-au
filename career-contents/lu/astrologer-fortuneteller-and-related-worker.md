## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for this occupation are not officially tracked as most are self-employed. Established practitioners may earn between €2,000 and €3,000 gross per month, but income varies significantly based on clientele, reputation, and services offered.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as astrologers in Luxembourg via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit. For salaried positions, a standard work visa applies. The EU Blue Card is possible if the role meets the high-skilled threshold (e.g., relevant degree and salary conditions). Self-employed astrologers need a business permit from the Ministry of Economy.

**Q: What qualifications or licenses are needed to practice as an astrologer in Luxembourg?**
A: Astrology is not a regulated profession in Luxembourg. No specific license is required, but trust is key. Many practitioners pursue certifications from international astrology schools or join professional associations to enhance credibility. Self-employed individuals must register with the Luxembourg business registry (RCS) and comply with tax and social security rules.
