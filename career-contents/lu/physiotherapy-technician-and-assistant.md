## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patient treatment progress recording and report writing
- Basic vital signs monitoring and data entry
- Setup and calibration of routine treatment equipment
- Appointment scheduling and patient file management.
- Simple Rehabilitation Exercise Guidance (Standardized Movements)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans
- Balance and coordination training assisted by virtual reality (VR)
- Use wearable devices to monitor patient progress in real time and adjust intensity
- AI-assisted generation of treatment plan documents and patient education materials
- Smart voice assistant for patient communication and follow-up

**Human moat:**

- Manual therapy techniques (e.g., massage, joint mobilization)
- Empathy to assess patient mental state and pain
- Clinical judgment in adjusting treatment based on nonverbal cues
- Build trust with patients and motivate them to adhere to rehabilitation
- Handling emergencies (e.g., patient falls or discomfort)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) system operation.
- Virtual reality rehabilitation technology applications
- Patient communication and behavior change coaching
- Wearable device data interpretation.
- Basic programming (e.g., Python scripts for data processing)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for physiotherapy assistants in Luxembourg generally range from €2,500 to €3,500 gross per month for full-time work, depending on experience and employer. The minimum social wage is €2,508.24 (skilled worker) as of 2025. Public sector or hospital roles may offer higher pay and benefits.

**Q: What are the work visa options for physiotherapy technicians and assistants coming to Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa (authorisation to stay for salaried workers). The EU Blue Card is possible if the job meets the minimum salary threshold (€58,968 in 2025) and the worker has a recognised higher education degree (typically bachelor's or master's). For physiotherapy assistants, this threshold may be high; standard work authorisation is more common. Employers must prove no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a physiotherapy technician or assistant in Luxembourg?**
A: Recognition of foreign qualifications is required by the Ministry of Health. Typically, you need a diploma in physiotherapy assistant or a related field, plus language skills (French or German, often B1-B2). Registration with the Ordre des Médecins or the Collège Médical may be necessary. Experience in clinical settings is valued. Continuing education is encouraged for career advancement.
