## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for refuse sorters in Luxembourg?**
A: In Luxembourg, refuse sorters (ISCO 9612) typically earn between €2,200 and €3,000 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. The minimum wage for skilled workers is around €2,508.45 gross per month as of 2024. Shift allowances may apply.

**Q: Can refuse sorters work in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a tertiary education degree and a salary threshold, which refuse sorters generally do not meet. Non-EU workers may instead apply for a work visa as a salaried employee, requiring an employment contract and employer sponsorship, with no specific EU Blue Card route for this occupation.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for refuse sorters in Luxembourg?**
A: Refuse sorters in Luxembourg work in recycling centers or waste facilities, sorting materials manually. Work involves physical effort, dust, and odors. Standard hours are 40 per week, sometimes shifts. Unions are active, and safety regulations are strictly enforced.
