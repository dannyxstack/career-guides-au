## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for door-to-door salespersons in Luxembourg?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Luxembourg often earn a base salary of around €2,500–€3,000 per month, plus commissions. The legal minimum wage for unskilled workers is €2,570.93 (as of 2024). Actual earnings vary widely depending on sales performance and employer.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as door-to-door salespersons in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a tertiary degree and a salary threshold (€77,525 in 2024). Door-to-door sales typically does not meet these criteria. Non-EU applicants usually require a standard work permit, which employers must justify to the Ministry of Labour.

**Q: What are the working conditions for door-to-door salespersons in Luxembourg?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Luxembourg often work irregular hours, including evenings and weekends. They are usually employed on a commission basis. Knowledge of multiple languages (French, German, Luxembourgish) is advantageous given the country's multilingual population.
