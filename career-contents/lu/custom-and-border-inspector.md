## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for customs and border inspectors in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for customs inspectors in Luxembourg follow public service scales, typically ranging from €3,500 to €5,500 gross per month depending on grade and experience. Additional allowances may apply for shift work or seniority.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as customs inspectors in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Customs inspector positions in Luxembourg are generally reserved for EU/EEA nationals due to security clearance requirements. Non-EU nationals may qualify for the EU Blue Card only if the role meets the salary threshold (€64,524 per year in 2025) and is classified as highly skilled, but such opportunities are rare for this occupation.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a customs inspector in Luxembourg?**
A: Candidates typically need a secondary education diploma (baccalaureate) and completion of a competitive civil service exam. Fluency in Luxembourgish, French, and German is essential. Specific training is provided by the Customs Administration.
