## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a teachers' aide in Luxembourg?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for a teachers' aide in Luxembourg ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience and employer. Public-sector aides often follow collective agreements, which may include additional benefits such as meal vouchers and childcare subsidies. Salaries are generally higher in Luxembourg than in neighboring countries.

**Q: Do teachers' aides need a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals require a work permit. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified roles and typically requires a higher salary than most teachers' aides earn. The main route is a temporary work permit (autorisation de travail), which the employer must obtain after proving labor market need. The applicant then applies for a residence permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a teachers' aide in Luxembourg?**
A: Typically, a secondary school diploma is required, along with fluency in Luxembourg's trilingual environment (Luxembourgish, French, German). Some roles prefer a certificate in childcare or education. Prior experience with children is valued. On-the-job training is common, and aides may work in early childhood education or primary schools supporting teachers.
