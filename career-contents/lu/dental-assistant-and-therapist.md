## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dental assistants and therapists in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for dental assistants in Luxembourg generally range from €2,500 to €3,500 gross per month for full-time work, depending on experience and qualifications. Dental therapists may earn slightly higher. The minimum wage (as of 2023) is €2,508 per month for skilled workers. Collective bargaining agreements and clinic size also influence pay.

**Q: Can non-EU dental assistants and therapists work in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU dental assistants and therapists may qualify for the EU Blue Card if they have a recognized higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €58,584 per year, or €46,867 for shortage occupations). Alternatively, a standard work permit (authorisation de travail) is possible with employer sponsorship, subject to labour market checks. Both routes require recognition of qualifications by the Ministry of Health.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a dental assistant or therapist in Luxembourg?**
A: Dental assistants need a certified training diploma (e.g., Certificat de capacité d’assistant dentaire) or equivalent EU qualification. Dental therapists require a recognized degree in dental therapy. All non-EU qualifications must be evaluated by the Ministry of Health for equivalence. Language skills in French, German, or Luxembourgish are often required for patient communication.
