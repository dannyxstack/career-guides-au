## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a social work associate professional in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for social work associate professionals in Luxembourg vary by employer and experience, but generally start above the statutory minimum wage. For skilled workers, the minimum wage is around €2,500 per month, and experienced professionals can earn significantly more. Exact figures depend on the sector (public, private, or non-profit) and qualifications.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for social work associate professionals in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as social work associate professionals in Luxembourg via a standard work visa (authorisation de séjour) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary and a recognised diploma. The standard work visa requires a confirmed job and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a social work associate professional in Luxembourg?**
A: Typically, a relevant bachelor's degree in social work or a related field is required. Additionally, registration with the Ministry of Education, Children and Youth or the professional association may be necessary. Language proficiency in French, German, or Luxembourgish is often required, depending on the client group and employer.
