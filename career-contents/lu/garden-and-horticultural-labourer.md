## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garden and horticultural labourer in Luxembourg?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers in Luxembourg typically earn between €2,500 and €3,200 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. The legal minimum wage for unskilled workers is €2,508.24 as of 2025. Additional benefits may include meal vouchers and transportation allowances.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign garden and horticultural labourers in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU workers can qualify for an EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience. Alternatively, a standard temporary work permit (autorisation de travail) requires employer sponsorship and Ministry of Labour approval. There is no specific shortage occupation list for this role.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for a garden and horticultural labourer in Luxembourg?**
A: Duties include planting, pruning, weeding, mowing, and maintaining gardens and green spaces in municipalities, landscaping firms, or private estates. Career progression may lead to team leader or specialized gardener roles. Obtaining a vocational certificate in horticulture can enhance advancement opportunities.
