## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary of a legislator in Luxembourg?**
A: Legislators in Luxembourg (Chamber of Deputies members) receive a gross annual salary of approximately €105,000, plus allowances for expenses. Pay is set by law and indexed to inflation.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a legislator in Luxembourg under the EU Blue Card?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled employees, not elected officials. Legislators must be Luxembourgish citizens. Non-EU nationals can only enter politics after naturalization, requiring at least five years of residence and language proficiency.

**Q: How are legislators elected in Luxembourg?**
A: Legislators are elected to the Chamber of Deputies every five years through a proportional representation system. Candidates must be at least 18, Luxembourgish citizens, and resident. No specific profession is required, but party affiliation is typical.
