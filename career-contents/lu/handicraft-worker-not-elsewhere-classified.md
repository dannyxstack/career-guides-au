## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for handicraft workers in Luxembourg?**
A: In Luxembourg, the minimum wage for skilled workers (over 18) is €2,508.40 per month as of 2024. Actual earnings vary by craft and experience, with many collective agreements setting higher rates. For example, construction trades often pay €3,000–€4,000 monthly. Specialized crafts may command premium wages.

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU handicraft workers in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit (autorisation de travail) sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is an option for highly qualified workers (university degree and salary ≥ €57,000/year). For most handicrafts, a standard work permit applies, requiring a labour market test proving no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a handicraft worker in Luxembourg?**
A: Many handicraft roles require a vocational diploma, such as the Certificat de Capacité Professionnelle (CCP) or Brevet de Maîtrise. Apprenticeships (dual system) combine on-the-job training with school. The Chambre des Métiers oversees craft professions and can advise on recognition of foreign qualifications.
