## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a police officer in Luxembourg?**
A: The starting salary for a police officer in Luxembourg is around €48,000 gross per year, increasing with seniority and rank. Experienced officers can earn up to €75,000 or more. Salaries are fixed by collective agreement and include allowances for shift work and special duties.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as police officers in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not typically issued for police officer roles, as they are public sector positions requiring Luxembourgish nationality or citizenship of an EU/EEA state. Non-EU nationals may only be recruited under specific bilateral agreements or as part of international cooperation, with strict residency and language requirements.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a police officer in Luxembourg?**
A: Candidates must be Luxembourgish or EU/EEA citizens, aged 18-30, hold at least a secondary school diploma, and pass physical, psychological, and language tests (Luxembourgish, French, German). Training lasts about 18 months at the Police Academy. Non-EU nationals are generally not eligible for direct entry.
