## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Luxembourg?**
A: In Luxembourg, farm labourers generally earn the statutory minimum wage. As of 2025, the minimum wage for unskilled workers is approximately €2,570 per month (index-linked). For skilled workers with a certificate, it is slightly higher. Actual pay may vary by employer and experience.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit under Luxembourg's third-country national scheme. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold. Employers must prove no suitable EU candidate is available. A valid employment contract and sufficient qualifications are needed.

**Q: What are typical duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Luxembourg?**
A: Duties include planting, harvesting crops, feeding and caring for livestock, cleaning barns, and maintaining farm equipment. Work is often seasonal, with peak times during planting and harvest. Labourers may work outdoors in varying weather. Prior experience or on-the-job training is common.
