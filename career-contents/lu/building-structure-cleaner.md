## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building structure cleaners in Luxembourg?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Luxembourg are generally paid at least the statutory minimum wage (Salaire Social Minimum), which is approximately €2,570 per month for unskilled workers as of 2025. Skilled or experienced cleaners may earn between €2,600 and €3,500 monthly, depending on the employer and specific job demands.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for non-EU building structure cleaners in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as building structure cleaners in Luxembourg through a standard work permit sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for this occupation, as it requires a university degree or at least five years of professional experience, plus a minimum salary threshold (around €48,000 in 2025). For less-skilled roles, the temporary work permit (autorisation de travail) is the main route.

**Q: What working conditions and training are typical for building structure cleaners in Luxembourg?**
A: Building structure cleaners often work on construction sites, requiring physical stamina and comfort with heights. No formal qualifications are mandatory, but vocational training in cleaning techniques and safety (e.g., from the Luxembourgish Chamber of Trades) is advantageous. Demand is steady due to ongoing construction projects. Working hours may include early mornings, evenings, or weekends depending on project schedules.
