## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physiotherapist in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for physiotherapists in Luxembourg vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €3,200 per month, while experienced physiotherapists can earn up to €5,500. The standard public sector scale is aligned with the collective agreement for healthcare professionals. Private practice incomes depend on client volume.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for physiotherapists moving to Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists require a work visa to practice in Luxembourg. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals with a recognized university degree (at least three years) and a job offer meeting a minimum salary threshold (currently around €70,000 annually). Alternatively, a standard salaried worker permit is possible below this threshold, but the Blue Card offers family reunification and mobility benefits.

**Q: Are foreign physiotherapy qualifications recognized in Luxembourg?**
A: Yes, but recognition is mandatory. Physiotherapists trained outside the EU must have their qualifications assessed by the Ministry of Higher Education and the Ministry of Health. They may need to complete an adaptation period or pass an aptitude test. EU qualifications are automatically recognized under the Professional Qualifications Directive. Registration with the Order of Physiotherapists is also required.
