## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Medical Secretary in Luxembourg?**
A: Entry-level Medical Secretaries in Luxembourg earn around €35,000–€45,000 gross per year, while experienced professionals can earn €50,000–€60,000. Salaries vary by clinic size, location, and language skills (French, German, Luxembourgish). Many positions include additional benefits such as meal vouchers and health insurance.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Medical Secretaries moving to Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU Medical Secretaries can work in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card (requires a qualified job offer meeting salary thresholds) or a standard work permit (authorisation de travail). Applicants must have a signed employment contract and employer sponsorship. For the Blue Card, the minimum gross annual salary is approximately €58,800 as of 2025.
