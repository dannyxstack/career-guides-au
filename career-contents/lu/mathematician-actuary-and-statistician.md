## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries are competitive, typically ranging from €60,000 to €100,000 per year for experienced professionals. Entry-level positions start around €45,000. Factors include sector (finance/insurance often higher), qualifications, and employer size. Luxembourg's high cost of living is offset by attractive compensation.

**Q: How can non-EU mathematicians and statisticians work in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals with a recognized university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least €58,968 in 2024, or lower for regulated shortage occupations) can apply for an EU Blue Card. The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. After 18 months, holders can move to other EU states.

**Q: Which sectors in Luxembourg employ mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians?**
A: The financial sector (banking, insurance, reinsurance) is the largest employer, requiring risk assessment and data analysis. Other sectors include government statistics (STATEC), research institutes like the Luxembourg Institute of Science and Technology (LIST), and emerging fintech companies. Demand is strong for data scientists and quantitative analysts.
