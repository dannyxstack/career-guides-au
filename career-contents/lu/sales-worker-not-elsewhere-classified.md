## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sales worker in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for sales workers in Luxembourg vary by sector and experience. As of 2024, the statutory minimum wage is €2,508.88 gross per month for unskilled workers. Experienced sales professionals can earn between €2,800 and €4,000 gross monthly, with bonuses or commissions often supplementing base pay.

**Q: How can a non-EU national obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for a sales worker role in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer to apply for a temporary authorisation to stay for salaried employment, processed via the Immigration Directorate. For the EU Blue Card, the job must pay at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (€57,718 in 2023). Sales workers may qualify if the employer meets the criteria.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the role of sales workers in Luxembourg?**
A: AI is enhancing productivity through automated CRM tools, lead generation, and chatbots, allowing sales workers to focus on complex tasks. However, routine roles face transformation. In Luxembourg, upskilling in digital tools is increasingly important, but human interaction remains valued, especially in luxury goods and B2B sectors.
