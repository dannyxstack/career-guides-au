## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Ships’ Engineers in Luxembourg?**
A: Ships’ Engineers in Luxembourg earn between €50,000 and €80,000 annually, depending on experience, vessel type, and employer. Salaries are competitive due to the high cost of living, and additional allowances may apply for overtime or special assignments. The maritime sector is modest but linked to inland waterway transport on the Moselle.

**Q: What visa or EU Blue Card options exist for Ships’ Engineers wanting to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU Ships’ Engineers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, set annually; e.g., approximately €70,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work visa for highly qualified workers is available. Both require recognised professional qualifications and a valid employment contract.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Ships’ Engineer in Luxembourg?**
A: A degree in marine engineering or equivalent, combined with relevant seagoing experience, is typically required. For inland navigation on the Moselle, a specific licence (e.g., Rhinemeister or CCR certificate) may be necessary. Foreign qualifications must be recognised by the Luxembourg Ministry of Education or the competent maritime authority.
