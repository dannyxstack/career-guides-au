## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other language teachers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for language teachers in Luxembourg are governed by collective labour agreements. For private language schools, entry-level pay starts around €3,000 gross per month, while experienced teachers in international schools or public institutions can earn €4,500–€6,000 gross. Benefits often include meal vouchers and pension contributions.

**Q: How can foreign language teachers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU teachers need a work permit, usually via a job offer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a recognised degree and a job paying at least €64,932 gross annually (2025 threshold). Alternatively, a standard work authorisation (autorisation de travail) applies, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from the Ministry of Labour.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an other language teacher in Luxembourg?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in the language taught or in education with a specialisation is required. For teaching in public schools, a master's degree and pedagogical certification are often mandatory. International schools may accept native-level fluency plus a teaching diploma, such as CELTA or TESOL. Knowledge of Luxembourgish, French, or German is an advantage.
