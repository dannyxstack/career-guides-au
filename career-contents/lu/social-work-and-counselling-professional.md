## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. According to 2023 data, the average gross monthly salary for social workers ranges from €4,000 to €5,500. Public sector positions follow fixed scales, with starting salaries around €3,800 and experienced professionals earning up to €6,500. Private practice incomes depend on client base and specialization.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU social work and counselling professionals in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified jobs) or a standard work permit. The employer must demonstrate no suitable EU candidate is available. Recognition of foreign qualifications is required by the Ministry of Education or relevant professional body. Language skills (Luxembourgish, French, or German) are often essential for client-facing roles.

**Q: What are the key requirements for social work and counselling professionals in Luxembourg?**
A: Fluency in at least one of Luxembourg's official languages (Luxembourgish, French, German) is crucial. Registration with the College of Social Workers or equivalent is mandatory for many roles. Employment settings include public administration, hospitals, NGOs, and private practice. A master's degree in social work or counselling is typically required, and continued professional development is encouraged.
