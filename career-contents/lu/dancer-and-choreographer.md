## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dancers and choreographers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for dancers and choreographers in Luxembourg vary widely due to project-based and freelance work. For employees in cultural institutions, gross monthly salaries typically range from €2,500 to €4,500. Freelance rates depend on contracts. The national minimum wage is €2,508.24 per month (as of 2024).

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for dancers and choreographers to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU dancers and choreographers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€58,968 in 2024). Alternatively, a temporary work permit for artists requires a sponsoring employer and approval from the Ministry of Labour, subject to labour market tests.

**Q: What are the main employment opportunities for dancers and choreographers in Luxembourg?**
A: Professional dancers often work with the Grand Théâtre de Luxembourg, the Luxembourg Philharmonic, or independent companies. Choreographers find opportunities in contemporary dance, ballet, and opera. Many work freelance on projects. Training is available at the Conservatoire de Luxembourg or via international programmes. The sector is small but culturally vibrant.
