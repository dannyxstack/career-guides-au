## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for specialist medical practitioners in Luxembourg?**
A: Gross annual salaries for specialist medical practitioners in Luxembourg are among the highest in Europe, typically ranging from €150,000 to €250,000, depending on specialty, experience, and sector (public vs. private). This reflects the country's high cost of living and demand for healthcare professionals.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for specialist medical practitioners to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU/EEA specialists can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid job offer, recognized medical degree, and salary meeting the threshold (~€71,532 in 2025). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible. All routes require recognition of qualifications by the Ministry of Health and, for some roles, knowledge of French, German, or Luxembourgish.

**Q: What are the key requirements to practise as a specialist medical practitioner in Luxembourg?**
A: Practising requires a recognized medical degree, completion of specialty training (validated by the Ministry of Health), registration with the Collège Médical, and proficiency in at least one administrative language (French, German, or Luxembourgish). Licenses from other EU/EEA countries are often recognized via mutual recognition directives.
