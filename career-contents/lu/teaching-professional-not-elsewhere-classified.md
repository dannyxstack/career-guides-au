## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries vary by institution and experience. In public secondary schools, starting annual gross salary is approximately €50,000–€60,000, increasing with seniority. Private international schools may offer different scales. Additional benefits include social security and allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU teaching professionals to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work authorization. Two main routes: a standard work visa (employer must demonstrate labour market shortage) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified roles (minimum gross annual salary threshold of €48,000). Both require a valid job offer and recognized qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a teaching professional in Luxembourg?**
A: For public schools, a master's degree in education or a subject field plus teaching certification is typically needed. Proficiency in French or German is often mandatory. International schools may accept qualifications like a PGCE. Continuous professional development is encouraged.
