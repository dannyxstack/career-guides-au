## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for travel attendants in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for travel attendants in Luxembourg vary by employer and experience. Entry-level positions may start around €2,500-€3,000 gross per month, while experienced stewards can earn €3,500-€5,000. Airlines operating from Luxembourg often offer additional allowances for international flights.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as travel attendants in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens may apply for an EU Blue Card to work in Luxembourg, but travel attendant roles typically require a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least €52,164 annually in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit may be applicable if the position does not meet Blue Card criteria.

**Q: What are the main duties of travel attendants in Luxembourg?**
A: Travel attendants in Luxembourg ensure passenger safety and comfort on flights. Duties include conducting pre-flight safety checks, demonstrating safety procedures, serving meals and beverages, assisting passengers with special needs, and managing emergencies. They must be fluent in English, French, German, and often Luxembourgish.
