**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Long-distance highway driving (autonomous truck pilot programs)
- Basic route planning and GPS navigation adjustments
- Basic vehicle status monitoring and routine inspection recording

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

- Customer relationships and non-standard delivery requirements
- Rapid decision-making in emergencies (accidents, failures)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for heavy truck drivers in Luxembourg?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for heavy truck drivers in Luxembourg ranges between €3,000 and €4,500, depending on experience, employer, and specific cargo. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, bonuses, and overtime pay. Night and weekend shifts may attract higher rates.

**Q: What work visa options are available for non-EU heavy truck drivers to work in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work visa and a labour market certificate obtained by the employer. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers and may not apply to truck drivers. Alternatively, a temporary work permit for seasonal or specific employment can be requested through the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a heavy truck driver in Luxembourg?**
A: You need a valid driving licence (C/CE) and a professional driver qualification certificate (CAP) as per EU Directive 2003/59. For transporting dangerous goods, an ADR certificate is mandatory. Language skills in Luxembourgish, French, or German are often required. Periodic training every five years is mandatory.
