## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and experience. The statutory minimum wage (SMC) for unskilled workers is around €2,571 per month (2025). Many employers offer higher pay, especially for live-in roles or those requiring specialized skills. Benefits such as accommodation or meals may also be included.

**Q: What official work visa routes are available for companions and valets in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. €4,000+ monthly). Alternatively, a standard work permit (autorisation de séjour) may apply for lower-paid roles, but employer sponsorship is mandatory.

**Q: What qualifications are important for becoming a companion or valet in Luxembourg?**
A: Formal qualifications are not required, but relevant experience, discretion, and excellent references are essential. Skills in household management, etiquette, and driving are valued. Language proficiency in French, German, or English is important. Some agencies offer training programs.
