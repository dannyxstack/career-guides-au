## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers and package deliverers in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for messengers and package deliverers in Luxembourg vary by employer and experience. The minimum gross monthly salary for a full-time worker is around €2,500 as of 2025, but many earn between €2,500 and €3,500. Delivery drivers may receive additional allowances for mileage or tips. Part-time roles are common.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a messenger or package deliverer in Luxembourg via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified workers, so it typically does not apply to low-skilled occupations like messengers or package deliverers. Non-EU nationals can apply for a standard work visa or a temporary authorization to work. Employers must demonstrate that no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are the main employers for messengers and package deliverers in Luxembourg?**
A: Major employers include national postal service (POST Luxembourg), courier companies like DHL, UPS, FedEx, and local logistics firms. E-commerce growth has increased demand for delivery drivers. Many positions require a valid driving license and knowledge of Luxembourg's multilingual environment.
