## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for beauticians in Luxembourg?**
A: Salaries for beauticians in Luxembourg generally range from €2,200 to €3,500 gross per month, depending on experience, specialization, and employer. The national minimum wage for skilled workers is around €2,200, so entry-level positions start near this level. Tips and commissions may supplement income. Some high-end salons or self-employed beauticians can earn more.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU beauticians in Luxembourg?**
A: Non-EU beauticians require a work authorization from the Ministry of Labour, based on a job offer and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation as it demands higher education qualifications. Instead, applicants usually apply for a salaried worker permit (authorisation d'établissement). Family reunification provisions may also apply for accompanying family members.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a beautician in Luxembourg?**
A: To work as a beautician in Luxembourg, you typically need a recognized vocational qualification, such as a Certificat de Capacité Professionnelle (CCP) or Diplôme d'État in esthetics. Equivalent foreign qualifications may be accepted after recognition by the Ministry of Education. Certain treatments like tattooing or laser require additional certification. Continuous professional development is recommended to stay current with trends and regulations.
