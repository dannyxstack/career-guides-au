# Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3155 (ISCO08)**

Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians install, maintain, and repair electronic systems used in air traffic control and navigation, including radar, communication, and landing systems. In Iceland, they are primarily employed by Isavia at major airports like Keflavík, ensuring the safety and efficiency of air traffic. This role requires a strong background in electronics, problem-solving skills, and the ability to work under pressure. The Icelandic aviation sector is small but critical for connectivity, with a focus on maintaining high safety standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows a path from technician to senior technician, then to team leader or supervisor within Isavia. Specialization in specific systems such as radar or communications is common, and further education in electrical engineering or aviation management can lead to higher-level roles. Opportunities for advancement are limited by the small size of the organization, but experienced technicians may move into system design, project management, or consultancy with international organizations.
