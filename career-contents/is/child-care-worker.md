# Child Care Worker — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5311 (ISCO08)**

Child care workers in Iceland care for children in preschools, day-care centers, and private homes, supporting their development and well-being. The occupation is in high demand due to Iceland's high female labor participation and strong emphasis on early childhood education. Workers plan activities, supervise play, assist with meals, and ensure a safe environment. While many are employed in municipal preschools, private day-care and family-based care are also common.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Many child care workers also specialize in areas such as special needs support or family-based care. Continuing education is encouraged, and experience can lead to administrative positions.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a child care worker in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary, but typical monthly pay for a child care worker (leikskólaliði) is around 350,000–450,000 ISK before tax. Experienced workers or those in supervisory roles may earn more.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as a child care worker in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. The occupation is on the shortage list, but the salary often falls below the work permit threshold. A job offer is required, and the employer must demonstrate no suitable EEA candidate is available.

**Q: What advancement opportunities are available for child care workers?**
A: With further education (e.g., a bachelor's in preschool teaching), child care workers can become preschool teachers or center managers. Experience may lead to specialization in special needs support or family-based care.
