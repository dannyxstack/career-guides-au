# Signwriter, Decorative Painter, Engraver or Etcher — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 7316 (ISCO08)**

Signwriters, decorative painters, engravers and etchers in Iceland create custom signage, decorative artwork, and engraved designs for businesses, public buildings, and private clients. The occupation is small-scale, often involving self-employment or work in specialized workshops. Demand is driven by tourism (hotel, restaurant, and shop signage), heritage preservation (restoring traditional signs and decorations), and commercial art (engraved gifts and memorials). The role requires artistic talent, precision, and knowledge of materials like paint, glass, and metal. While not a large employer, the occupation offers niche opportunities for skilled craftspeople in Iceland's cultural and tourism sectors.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a signwriter/decorative painter in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, specialization, and employment type (employee vs. self-employed). According to Statistics Iceland, average monthly earnings for craft and related trades workers (including painters) are around 500,000–600,000 ISK before tax. Freelancers may charge hourly rates of 5,000–10,000 ISK. For current official data, consult Statistics Iceland (hagstofa.is).

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as a signwriter in Iceland?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA/EFTA citizen, no visa or work permit is needed – you can live and work freely, but you must register if staying over 3 months. Non-EEA nationals require a work and residence permit tied to a specific job offer, with a labour market test. Since this occupation is not on the shortage list, permits may be harder to obtain.
