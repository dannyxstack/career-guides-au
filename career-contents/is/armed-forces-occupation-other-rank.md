# Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

In Iceland, armed forces occupations are limited due to the country having no standing army; the primary employer is the Icelandic Coast Guard, which handles defence and maritime security. Personnel in other ranks include enlisted members performing operational, technical, and support tasks. These roles require high discipline, physical fitness, and loyalty to national security objectives. The Icelandic Crisis Response Unit also provides opportunities for international peacekeeping missions under the Ministry for Foreign Affairs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary progression for other ranks in the Icelandic Coast Guard?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and include base pay plus allowances for sea duty, special skills, and deployments. Progression with experience and rank leads to increased earnings, but specific figures vary by role.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work in armed forces occupations in Iceland?**
A: Almost never. The positions are typically restricted to Icelandic citizens due to security requirements. Exceptions are extremely rare and only for highly specialized roles that cannot be filled locally, with strict background checks.

**Q: What are the main career paths for other ranks?**
A: Career paths include promotion to non-commissioned officer (NCO) ranks, specialization in technical fields (e.g., engineering, communications), or transition to officer training. Some may move to civilian roles in maritime safety or security.
