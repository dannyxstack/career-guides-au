# Craft and Related Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 7549 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of specialized craft trades not captured by other categories, such as musical instrument makers, jewellery artisans, and other bespoke manufacturers. In Iceland, these workers often operate in small workshops, serving niche markets including tourism, art galleries, and custom orders. The sector blends traditional techniques with modern design, and many practitioners are self-employed or part of micro-enterprises. Demand is driven by tourism, local craftsmanship appreciation, and export of high-quality Icelandic products.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship under a master craftsperson, followed by a journeyman period. Experienced workers may become master craftsmen, open their own workshops, or move into design and teaching. In Iceland, craft workers often diversify into multiple disciplines to sustain their business. Formal recognition through trade certification can enhance career prospects. The field rewards creativity and entrepreneurial spirit, with many successful artisans building strong personal brands.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a craft worker in Iceland?**
A: Earnings vary widely by specialty and experience. Apprentice craft workers may earn around ISK 3.5-5 million annually, while experienced journeymen can earn ISK 5-7 million. Master craftsmen or self-employed artisans may earn higher, depending on demand and reputation.

**Q: Can a non-EEA craft worker get a work permit for Iceland?**
A: Yes, if they have a confirmed job offer from an Icelandic employer and meet the qualification requirements. The employer must demonstrate that no EEA worker is available (labour market test). The occupation is not on the shortage list, so a test is mandatory. A work permit is typically tied to the specific job.
