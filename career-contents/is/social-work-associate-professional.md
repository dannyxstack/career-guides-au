# Social Work Associate Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3412 (ISCO08)**

Social work associate professionals in Iceland provide support and case management to vulnerable groups including children, elderly, immigrants, and individuals with disabilities, working within municipalities, NGOs, and healthcare settings. The role is integral to Iceland's welfare system, which emphasizes community-based care and social inclusion.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a social work associate professional in Iceland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective bargaining agreements in the public sector, usually starting around 5,000,000 ISK annually for entry-level positions, with increases based on experience and specialization.

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as a social work associate professional in Iceland?**
A: EEA/EFTA nationals do not need a visa; they have freedom of movement. Non-EEA nationals require a work and residence permit tied to a specific job offer, which includes a labor market test and proof of qualifications.

**Q: What education is required for this occupation in Iceland?**
A: A diploma (2 years) or bachelor's degree (3 years) in social work or a related field from a recognized institution is typically required. Relevant work experience may be considered.
