# Office Supervisor — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3341 (ISCO08)**

Office supervisors in Iceland oversee administrative staff and daily office operations, ensuring efficient workflow and communication. They are employed across sectors such as government, finance, healthcare, and services. The role combines administrative expertise with team management, requiring strong organizational and interpersonal skills. In Iceland, this occupation is common in both public and private sectors, with positions available in Reykjavík and regional centers. While many supervisors have vocational or secondary education, relevant experience is highly valued. The job market for office supervisors remains stable, with turnover driven by retirements and business growth.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an office supervisor in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, location, and experience. According to Statistics Iceland, the average monthly salary for office supervisors (ISCO 3341) was around 680,000 ISK in 2023, but this includes entry-level and senior roles. For specific figures, consult the latest wage survey.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as an office supervisor in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but must meet the requirements for a work and residence permit for qualified professionals. This includes a job offer, passing a labor market test, and earning at least the threshold salary (around 1,200,000 ISK/month). Office supervisor roles may qualify if the salary meets the threshold.

**Q: Is higher education required to become an office supervisor in Iceland?**
A: No, many supervisors have only secondary education (stúdentspróf) plus experience. However, a vocational diploma or bachelor's degree in business administration can improve job prospects and advancement.
