# Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operator — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 8154 (ISCO08)**

Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators in Iceland are primarily employed in industrial laundries, dry-cleaning services, and the small textile finishing sector. They operate and monitor machines that wash, bleach, dye, or dry-clean fabrics and garments. Given Iceland's limited textile manufacturing, most opportunities arise in hotels, hospitals, and specialized cleaning firms. The role requires attention to detail and adherence to chemical safety protocols. Icelandic employers often prefer workers with vocational training in textile care or related fields.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operator in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer, but typically range from 350,000 to 450,000 ISK per month for full-time work, excluding shift differentials. Entry-level positions may start lower. Always check current collective agreements for the most accurate figures.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national obtain a work permit for this occupation in Iceland?**
A: It is possible but difficult. The occupation is classified as low-skilled, and work permits for non-EEA nationals are subject to a labour market test, quota limitations, and a minimum wage threshold. Priority is given to EEA/EFTA workers. Realistic prospects are limited unless you have specialized experience or a sponsoring employer with a demonstrated need.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist in Iceland for this role?**
A: Advancement may involve becoming a supervisor, quality controller, or trainer. With further training, one could move into equipment sales or maintenance. Starting a dry-cleaning business is an option for experienced operators. However, the occupation generally offers limited upward mobility without additional education.
