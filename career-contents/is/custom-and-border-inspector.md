# Customs And Border Inspectors — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3351 (ISCO08)**

Customs and border inspectors enforce laws regarding imports, exports, and immigration at Iceland's borders, including airports, harbors, and the Schengen external border. They screen passengers, inspect cargo, prevent smuggling, and collect duties. In Iceland, this role is primarily employed by the Directorate of Customs (Tollstjóri) and falls under the Ministry of Finance. The job requires high integrity, attention to detail, and knowledge of customs and immigration regulations. Icelandic language proficiency is essential for communication and legal procedures.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a customs inspector in Iceland?**
A: The starting salary is around 5.5 to 6.5 million ISK per year, increasing with experience and specialization. Senior inspectors can earn up to 8-9 million ISK. Additional allowances for shift work may apply.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national become a customs inspector in Iceland?**
A: It is very difficult. Due to security clearance requirements and the need for Icelandic language proficiency, non-EEA nationals rarely qualify. The job is typically open only to Icelandic citizens or other EEA/EFTA nationals under free movement.

**Q: What training is provided for new customs inspectors?**
A: Recruits undergo a structured on-the-job training program lasting several months, covering customs regulations, inspection techniques, and legal procedures. This is supplemented by courses at the Customs Academy.
