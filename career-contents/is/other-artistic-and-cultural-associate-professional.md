# Other Artistic and Cultural Associate Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3435 (ISCO08)**

This broad category includes roles such as photographers, interior designers, graphic designers, gallery assistants, and cultural event coordinators who support artists and cultural institutions. In Iceland, these professionals are concentrated in Reykjavík and contribute to the vibrant creative sector, often working freelance or for small businesses. The tourism industry also drives demand for cultural experiences, creating opportunities in design, media, and event management.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are typical salaries for artistic and cultural associate professionals in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by role and experience. Entry-level positions may earn around the national average, while experienced professionals in high-demand niches can earn significantly more. Freelancers set their own rates, which fluctuate with project volume.

**Q: Can non-EEA artists easily get a work permit for Iceland?**
A: Non-EEA nationals need a job offer and the employer must demonstrate that no EEA candidate is available. The process is competitive, especially for freelance roles. A specific permit may be available for cultural exchanges or short-term gigs.
