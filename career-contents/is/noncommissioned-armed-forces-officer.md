# Non-Commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Iceland is unique among NATO members in not having a standing army. The role of non-commissioned armed forces officer does not exist as a civilian occupation within Iceland's labour market. The closest equivalent roles are found in the Icelandic Coast Guard, which is a civilian institution under the Ministry of Justice, responsible for maritime security, search and rescue, and fisheries control. These positions are typically filled by Icelandic nationals, with recruitment focused on maritime and technical backgrounds. There is no formal career path for 'armed forces NCOs' in Iceland, as military training abroad is not directly transferable to domestic civilian employment.
