# Fashion and Other Models — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5241 (ISCO08)**

Fashion and other models in Iceland work primarily in the fashion industry, advertising, and media. The sector is small but includes opportunities in e-commerce, social media, and local fashion shows. Most models are freelancers or represented by agencies. The market is competitive and seasonal, with demand peaking during events like Iceland Fashion Week. Non-EEA models face restrictions due to labour market tests and permit requirements.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a model in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely; most models are paid per assignment. Entry-level rates range from 10,000 to 50,000 ISK per hour, but income is irregular. Annual earnings are often low unless combined with other work.

**Q: Can a non-EEA model get a work permit for Iceland?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. A job offer from an Icelandic company is required, and the Directorate of Labour must confirm no EEA candidate is available. Permits are typically short-term and tied to specific contracts.
