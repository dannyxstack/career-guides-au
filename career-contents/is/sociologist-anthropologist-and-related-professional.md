# Sociologist, Anthropologist and Related Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2632 (ISCO08)**

Sociologists and anthropologists in Iceland work primarily in academic research, public policy, social services, and market research. The field is small but stable, with opportunities in government agencies (Statistics Iceland, ministries), universities, and NGOs. Many professionals also hold roles in human resources and organizational development. The work often involves analyzing social trends, conducting surveys, and providing evidence-based recommendations.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sociologists in Iceland?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from 5-7 million ISK per year, while senior professionals in government or academia can earn 8-12 million ISK per year.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to get a work permit as a sociologist in Iceland?**
A: Yes, non-EEA nationals must have a job offer from an Icelandic employer and the position must pass a labour market test to demonstrate that no suitable EEA candidates are available.

**Q: Is Icelandic language proficiency required for sociologist jobs?**
A: For policy and social service roles, proficiency in Icelandic is often required. Academic and research positions may accept English, but knowledge of Icelandic is an advantage.
