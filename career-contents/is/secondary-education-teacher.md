# Secondary Education Teacher — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2330 (ISCO08)**

Secondary education teachers in Iceland work primarily in upper secondary schools (framhaldsskólar), teaching students aged 16–20. The profession requires a master's degree in education or a related subject, combined with a teaching certification. Iceland faces a persistent shortage of qualified teachers, especially in STEM fields, Icelandic, and special education. The role is regulated by the Ministry of Education, and all teachers must hold a teaching license. While EEA/EFTA nationals benefit from free movement, non-EEA teachers need a work permit and labor market test. The job offers stability, union representation, and opportunities for professional development.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for secondary education teachers in Iceland?**
A: Salaries for secondary teachers in Iceland are competitive with other professions requiring a master's degree. Starting salaries are around 700,000 ISK per month, while experienced teachers can earn upwards of 950,000 ISK per month. Additional pay is available for extra duties, such as mentoring or coordinating.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals become secondary teachers in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but the process is more complex. They need a job offer from a school, a work permit, and their qualifications must be recognized by the Ministry of Education. A labor market test is required to prove no EEA/EFTA candidate is available. Icelandic language proficiency is usually mandatory. The profession is on the shortage list for certain subjects, which may facilitate the process.

**Q: Is Icelandic language proficiency required for teaching?**
A: Yes, fluency in Icelandic is generally required, as teaching is conducted in Icelandic. Some international schools may teach in English, but most upper secondary schools require strong Icelandic skills for certification.
