# Shelf Filler — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 9334 (ISCO08)**

Shelf fillers in Iceland are responsible for stocking shelves in retail stores, ensuring products are displayed neatly and inventory is maintained. They work in supermarkets, convenience stores, and larger retail chains across the country. The role requires physical stamina and attention to detail, often as an entry-level position in the retail sector. With Iceland's growing tourism and retail industry, shelf fillers are in steady demand, particularly in Reykjavik and major towns. The job often involves evening or weekend shifts, and it can be a stepping stone to other retail roles.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Iceland?**
A: Salaries for shelf fillers are typically set by collective agreements in the retail sector. Entry-level wages are around the minimum wage level, which is adjusted annually. Weekend and evening shifts may attract additional pay. Exact figures vary by employer and region, but the role is generally considered a low-skilled position with corresponding compensation.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit as a shelf filler in Iceland?**
A: It is very difficult. Iceland's immigration policy prioritizes skilled occupations. For low-skilled roles like shelf filling, the labour market test almost always fails because there are enough EEA/EEA workers available. Non-EEA permits are only issued in exceptional cases, such as for family reunification or if the employer proves a genuine shortage, which is rare.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shelf fillers?**
A: With experience, shelf fillers can become team leaders or shift supervisors. Some move into merchandising, inventory management, or purchasing roles. Larger retail chains offer internal training programs. However, advancement often requires additional education or certifications in business or logistics.
