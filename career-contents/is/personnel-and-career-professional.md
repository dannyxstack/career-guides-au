# Personnel and Careers Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2423 (ISCO08)**

Personnel and careers professionals in Iceland manage human resources functions including recruitment, employee relations, training, and career development. They work in private companies, government agencies, and HR consultancies. The role is critical in Iceland's tight labor market, especially for retaining talent. Icelandic HR professionals often possess university degrees in business or psychology. The occupation is not on the shortage list, but demand remains steady as organizations focus on employee well-being and strategic HR.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Some professionals move into HR consulting or specialize in compensation and benefits. Professional certifications (e.g., CIPD, SHRM) are increasingly valued but not mandatory. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Personnel and Careers Professional in Iceland?**
A: Salaries for HR professionals in Iceland vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around 400,000 ISK per month, while senior managers can earn 800,000–1,200,000 ISK. The average is roughly 600,000 ISK per month, but this does not include bonuses or benefits.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national easily get a work permit as an HR professional in Iceland?**
A: It is challenging. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so employers must prove no suitable EEA candidate is available via a labour market test. A job offer with a salary above the minimum threshold (currently around 400,000 ISK/month) is required. Success rates are moderate.

**Q: What is the most common career path for this role in Iceland?**
A: Many start as HR assistants or recruiters, then advance to HR generalist or specialist roles (e.g., compensation, training). With experience, they become HR managers or directors. Some move into consulting or self-employment.
