# Administrative and Executive Secretaries — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3343 (ISCO08)**

Administrative and executive secretaries in Iceland provide high-level support to managers and executives, handling correspondence, scheduling, and office coordination. They work across sectors such as government, finance, tourism, and creative industries, where organizational skills and discretion are valued. Fluency in Icelandic and English is typically required, with some roles needing additional languages. The profession is adapting to digital tools, requiring proficiency in office software and communication platforms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from junior secretary to executive assistant or office manager, with potential to specialize in fields like legal or financial administration. Further education in business management or specialized certifications can accelerate advancement. Senior roles may involve supervising administrative teams or managing executive office operations. Networking and language skills are key for growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Calendar management and meeting scheduling (AI scheduling tools automatically coordinate times)
- Basic document processing (e.g., formatting documents, generating email templates)
- Data entry and information retrieval (natural language queries replace manual operations)
- Meeting minutes and summary generation (speech-to-text + AI summary)
- Travel booking and expense reimbursement (automated system processing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Executive communication strategy suggestions (AI analyzes email tone, priority, aids decision-making)
- Project progress tracking and early warning (AI integrates multi-source data, automatically flags risks)
- Information organization in cross-departmental coordination (AI extracts key points, assists reporting)
- Data analysis report writing (AI generates charts and initial insights for manual optimization)
- Emergency response (AI provides historical cases and contingency plans)

**Human moat:**

- Maintaining complex interpersonal relationships and emotional support
- Judgment and political sensitivity in high-level decision-making
- Confidential information handling and ethical judgement
- Creative problem-solving for unstructured problems
- Flexible adaptability in cross-cultural and cross-level communication

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced application of AI office tools (e.g., Copilot, Notion AI)
- Data visualization and basic data analysis
- Operation of project management systems (e.g., Asana, Jira)
- Emotional intelligence and conflict management training
- Basic programming (Python script for automating repetitive tasks)
- Information security and compliance awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- Office automation
- Executive support
- Digital transformation
- Tourism industry

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for administrative and executive secretaries in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Average monthly earnings are typically between ISK 450,000 and 600,000, according to Statistics Iceland's wage data.

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as an administrative secretary in Iceland?**
A: EEA/EFTA nationals do not need a visa. Non-EEA nationals require a work permit tied to a job offer, subject to a labour market test. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What training is recommended for this role in Iceland?**
A: A vocational diploma in office administration is common, but many employers prefer candidates with a bachelor's degree in business or equivalent experience. Certifications in executive assistance or language skills (Icelandic and English) are advantageous.
