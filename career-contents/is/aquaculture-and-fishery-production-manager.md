# Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Manager — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 1312 (ISCO08)**

Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers oversee the operations of fish farms, hatcheries, and fishing enterprises in Iceland, ensuring efficient production, compliance with environmental regulations, and high-quality output. With Iceland's growing aquaculture sector—particularly salmon and trout farming—these managers are critical in managing teams, budgets, and production cycles, often in remote coastal areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from technical roles like fish farm technician or supervisor, advancing to shift manager and then production manager. Further progression includes regional management or consultancy. Some managers move into entrepreneurship, starting their own aquaculture ventures. Specialization in areas like recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS) or organic certification offers enhanced career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually record farming data (water temperature, feeding amounts, etc.)
- Generate daily production reports and compliance documents
- Image recognition of initial disease symptoms
- Inventory counting and feed order calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses sensors and AI to predict optimal feeding times and amounts
- Monitor fish health and abnormal behavior through machine vision.
- Simulate the impact of different stocking densities and feed plans on yield
- Real-time water quality monitoring and automatic adjustment of oxygenation and filtration equipment

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making for responding to sudden disease outbreaks or environmental disasters
- Experience-based biosecurity management and overall disease prevention planning.
- Negotiations and relationship management with regulators, suppliers, and sales channels
- Assess and implement new sustainable aquaculture technologies

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (Python/R)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor system management
- Operation and troubleshooting of automated control systems
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainability certification standards
- Inspection skills for drones or underwater robots

**Key growth areas:**

- Salmon farming
- Sustainable aquaculture
- Recirculating systems
- Value-added processing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers in Iceland?**
A: The average annual salary ranges from 7,000,000 to 9,000,000 ISK before tax, depending on experience, location, and company size. Senior managers may earn over 10,000,000 ISK.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals easily obtain a work permit for this occupation?**
A: Non-EEA nationals need a job offer and must pass a labour market test to show no local candidate is available. Since aquaculture managers are often on the shortage list, the process is favorable but still requires a permit application.
