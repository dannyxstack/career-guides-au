# Motorcycle Driver — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 8321 (ISCO08)**

Motorcycle drivers in Iceland primarily work in urban courier and delivery services, transporting food, packages, and documents. The occupation is small but growing due to the rise of e-commerce and on-demand delivery platforms. Most positions are in the Reykjavík capital area, with some in larger towns. The job requires a motorcycle license (category A) and often involves flexible hours, self-employment, or part-time work. While not a high-skilled occupation, it provides entry-level opportunities for both Icelandic residents and EEA nationals.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for motorcycle drivers typically involves moving into dispatch, logistics coordination, or management roles within delivery companies. Some drivers transition to other driving jobs, such as light truck or taxi driving, which may offer better pay and stability. Self-employment as a courier can lead to owning a small delivery business. Further training in logistics or transport management can open up additional opportunities. The role is often seen as a stepping stone rather than a long-term career.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Optimal delivery route planning
- Real-time traffic navigation and adjustment

- Simple repetitive short-distance delivery

**Where AI augments the role:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a motorcycle driver in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and hours. Many drivers are paid per delivery or hourly. Monthly earnings can range from 300,000 to 500,000 ISK before taxes, depending on workload, tips, and season. Self-employed drivers may earn more but have variable income.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit as a motorcycle driver in Iceland?**
A: It is very difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list, and permits require a labour market test proving no EEA worker is available. In practice, non-EEA nationals rarely obtain permits for this occupation.

**Q: Is motorcycle driving in Iceland a full-time job?**
A: Most positions are part-time or freelance. Full-time salaried jobs exist at larger courier companies, but are limited. Many drivers combine it with other work.
