# Technical And Medical Sales Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2433 (ISCO08)**

Technical and medical sales professionals in Iceland promote and sell specialized products such as pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and industrial equipment to healthcare providers, laboratories, and businesses. They combine technical knowledge with sales skills to advise clients, negotiate contracts, and maintain relationships. The role requires deep understanding of complex products, regulations, and client needs. In Iceland, this occupation is concentrated in the Reykjavik capital area but also serves regional hospitals and industries. Professionals often work for international companies or local distributors, and fluency in Icelandic and English is common.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for technical and medical sales professionals in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and commissions. Entry-level positions start around 5-6 million ISK annually, while senior roles can exceed 10 million ISK plus bonuses. Commissions often form a significant part of total compensation.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work visa as a medical sales professional in Iceland?**
A: Yes, provided they have a job offer from an Icelandic employer that meets the salary and qualification thresholds for a work and residence permit (sérfræðileyfi). The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EEA candidate is available (labour market test).

**Q: Is Icelandic language proficiency required for this role?**
A: While some companies operate in English, especially international firms, most clients prefer Icelandic. Basic fluency is often expected, and advanced skills significantly improve job prospects.
