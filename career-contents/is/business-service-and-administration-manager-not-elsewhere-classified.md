# Business Services and Administration Manager Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 1219 (ISCO08)**

In Iceland, this occupation covers managers overseeing business services such as management consulting, administrative support, and facility management. These professionals typically work for private companies, government agencies, or as independent consultants. The sector benefits from Iceland's growing tourism and technology industries, as well as a stable economy. Managers coordinate operations, develop strategies, and ensure service quality. Many have backgrounds in business, economics, or related fields, with several years of experience in specialized roles. The role demands adaptability, leadership, and a solid understanding of Icelandic labour market regulations and business culture.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a business services manager in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level managers earn around 8-10 million ISK annually, while senior managers can earn 15 million ISK or more. Bonuses and benefits are common in larger firms.

**Q: How can a non-EEA national obtain a work permit as a business services manager?**
A: You need a confirmed job offer from an Icelandic employer. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by local or EEA labour (labour market test). A university degree or equivalent experience is required. The permit is initially granted for up to 2 years.

**Q: Is it necessary to speak Icelandic to work as a business services manager?**
A: While English is widely used in the business sector, especially in international companies, proficiency in Icelandic is often required for roles involving local clients or government contracts. Learning Icelandic improves job prospects.
