# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a wide range of clerical support roles not specified elsewhere, including general office clerks, data entry operators, and administrative assistants. In Iceland, these workers are employed across all sectors, from private companies to public institutions. The role typically involves maintaining records, processing documents, handling correspondence, and providing administrative support. Proficiency in Icelandic and basic computer skills are essential, while specialized software knowledge can be an advantage. The job market for clerical workers in Iceland is stable but competitive, with many positions filled through local hires.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for clerical support workers in Iceland?**
A: The average monthly salary for clerical support workers in Iceland is around 450,000 to 550,000 ISK before tax, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior roles can exceed 650,000 ISK.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit for a clerical support job in Iceland?**
A: It is very difficult because clerical support is not on the shortage list. The employer must conduct a labour market test to show no EEA/EFTA worker is available. Approval is rare for this occupation.

**Q: What are the typical career advancement opportunities for clerical support workers in Iceland?**
A: Common paths include moving into office management, executive assistant roles, or specialized admin in HR, finance, or IT. Additional education in business or project management can accelerate advancement.
