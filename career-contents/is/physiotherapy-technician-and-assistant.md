# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3255 (ISCO08)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants support physiotherapists in delivering rehabilitation care, including setting up equipment, guiding patients through exercises, and documenting progress. In Iceland, these roles are essential in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and private clinics, particularly to meet the needs of an aging population and a growing emphasis on physical therapy after injuries or surgeries. Strong Icelandic language skills are often required for patient interaction, and the profession is regulated by health authorities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves gaining experience as an assistant and potentially pursuing further education to become a licensed physiotherapist (requiring a university degree). Some may specialize in areas like sports rehabilitation or elderly care. Leadership roles in clinic management are rare without additional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patient treatment progress recording and report writing
- Basic vital signs monitoring and data entry
- Setup and calibration of routine treatment equipment
- Appointment scheduling and patient file management.
- Simple Rehabilitation Exercise Guidance (Standardized Movements)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans
- Balance and coordination training assisted by virtual reality (VR)
- Use wearable devices to monitor patient progress in real time and adjust intensity
- AI-assisted generation of treatment plan documents and patient education materials
- Smart voice assistant for patient communication and follow-up

**Human moat:**

- Manual therapy techniques (e.g., massage, joint mobilization)
- Empathy to assess patient mental state and pain
- Clinical judgment in adjusting treatment based on nonverbal cues
- Build trust with patients and motivate them to adhere to rehabilitation
- Handling emergencies (e.g., patient falls or discomfort)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) system operation.
- Virtual reality rehabilitation technology applications
- Patient communication and behavior change coaching
- Wearable device data interpretation.
- Basic programming (e.g., Python scripts for data processing)

**Key growth areas:**

- Rehabilitation
- Elderly care
- Sports medicine
- Outpatient clinics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a physiotherapy technician in Iceland?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around 400,000 ISK per month, with experienced assistants earning up to 550,000 ISK. Salaries are higher in private clinics and Reykjavik area.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as a physiotherapy technician in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need a job offer from an Icelandic employer, and the employer must demonstrate that no suitable Icelandic or EEA worker is available. Knowledge of Icelandic is typically required, and the occupation is not on the shortage list.

**Q: What training is needed to become a physiotherapy assistant in Iceland?**
A: A vocational diploma from a technical college, typically 1-2 years, including clinical practice. For non-EEA applicants, the diploma must be recognised by the Icelandic Directorate of Health.
