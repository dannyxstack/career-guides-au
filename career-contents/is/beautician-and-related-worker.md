# Beautician — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5142 (ISCO08)**

Beauticians in Iceland provide skincare, makeup, nail care, and other aesthetic treatments in salons, spas, and hotels. The industry is buoyed by a strong tourism sector, especially in Reykjavik and popular tourist destinations. Most professionals hold a license from the Icelandic Health Authority after completing vocational training. EEA nationals can work freely under free movement rules, while non-EEA applicants face a labour market test and work permit process. The job market is steady with moderate turnover, and many beauticians eventually open their own businesses.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression often begins with entry-level positions in salons or spas. Experienced beauticians can move into management roles, such as salon manager or spa supervisor. Many pursue advanced certifications in areas like laser therapy, permanent makeup, or medical aesthetics. Entrepreneurship is common, with many professionals eventually opening their own studios or mobile services. Continuous education in new techniques and products is valued.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a beautician in Iceland?**
A: The average monthly salary for a beautician is around 400,000 ISK before tax. Experienced professionals in high-end salons or with specializations can earn up to 600,000 ISK per month.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as beauticians in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but they need a work and residence permit. The employer must prove no EEA/EFTA candidate is available (labour market test). The process can be lengthy and is not guaranteed, as beautician is not a shortage occupation.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities?**
A: Beauticians can progress to salon management, open their own business, or specialize in fields like medical aesthetics, laser treatments, or bridal services. Continuing education and certifications are key.
