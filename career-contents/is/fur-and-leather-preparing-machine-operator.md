# Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 8155 (ISCO08)**

Fur and leather preparing machine operators in Iceland operate machinery to clean, tan, dye, and finish fur and leather hides. This occupation is extremely niche, tied to a few small-scale workshops and traditional craft enterprises, primarily in rural areas. The sector is very small and does not constitute a significant part of the Icelandic labour market. Most operations are artisanal or specialized, with limited automation. Given Iceland's mild climate and strong animal welfare focus, fur farming is almost nonexistent, with leather mostly a byproduct of the fishing and meat industries.

Career progression is very limited in this field. Operators typically start as apprentices or assistants in small tanneries or craft studios. With experience, they may become senior operators or shift into related roles such as leather goods manufacturing, shoe repair, or saddlery. Some may branch into self-employment or artisan crafts. However, the narrow scope of the role offers few formal advancement paths, and most workers stay in the same position for many years.
