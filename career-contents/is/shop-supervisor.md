# Shop Supervisor — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5222 (ISCO08)**

Shop supervisors in Iceland oversee daily operations in retail establishments, managing staff, inventory, customer service, and sales targets. The role is vital in Iceland's tourism-driven economy, with many shops in Reykjavík and regional areas serving both locals and visitors. Supervisors ensure compliance with Icelandic labour laws, handle cash management, and coordinate promotional activities. While the occupation is common, competition for positions can be moderate due to a stable retail sector. The job typically requires good communication and leadership skills, as well as familiarity with point-of-sale systems and inventory management.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shop supervisor in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, location, and company size. Generally, shop supervisors earn a competitive wage above the retail average, with additional bonuses or overtime common during tourist seasons. Specific figures are available from Statistics Iceland or salary surveys.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national become a shop supervisor in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer and a work permit. The employer must conduct a labour market test to show the position cannot be filled by an EEA citizen. Permits are employer-specific, and the role is not on the shortage list, so approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for shop supervisors in Iceland?**
A: Many move into store management, regional management, or buying roles. Some gain experience to open their own retail business, particularly in tourism-related sectors. Ongoing training in digital retail can open further opportunities.
