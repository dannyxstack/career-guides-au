# Creative and Performing Artists Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2659 (ISCO08)**

This category encompasses a diverse range of creative professionals such as comedians, choreographers, and independent artists who do not fit neatly into specific performing arts classifications. In Iceland, the creative sector is relatively small but supported by a strong cultural heritage and a growing tourism industry that values unique artistic experiences. Many artists work freelance or on project-based contracts, with opportunities arising from festivals, film productions, and the burgeoning digital content market. The occupation is characterized by high flexibility and self-employment, with income often varying significantly based on reputation and project scope.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a creative performing artist in Iceland?**
A: Income varies widely; most artists earn between 3,000,000 and 6,000,000 ISK annually, but many rely on multiple income streams. Top performers or those with regular institutional jobs may earn higher.

**Q: Can a non-EEA artist get a work permit for Iceland?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer from an Icelandic employer and the position cannot be filled locally. The employer must advertise the job locally first. The artist's qualifications must be recognized as specialized.

**Q: Is it necessary to speak Icelandic to work as an artist in Iceland?**
A: For many performance roles, English is sufficient, especially in music and film. However, knowing Icelandic can help in networking and accessing local funding opportunities.
