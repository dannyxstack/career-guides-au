# Sweeper and Related Labourer — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 9613 (ISCO08)**

Sweepers and related labourers maintain cleanliness in public spaces, streets, buildings, and industrial sites across Iceland. They perform tasks such as manual sweeping, litter picking, snow clearing, and waste collection. Employment is common in municipalities, cleaning companies, and hospitality venues. The work is physically demanding and often seasonal, with higher demand in summer tourism and winter maintenance. It is an entry-level occupation with low formal education requirements, but essential for Iceland's high standards of cleanliness and environmental care.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for sweepers is limited but possible. Experienced workers can advance to supervisory roles, such as team leader or site supervisor, or specialize in areas like industrial cleaning or snow removal. Further training in cleaning techniques or equipment operation can open doors to higher-paying maintenance roles. However, the occupation is generally seen as a stepping stone or short-term employment.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI scheduling and monitoring tools
- Automated cleaning equipment maintenance
- Basic Chemistry and Disinfectant Knowledge
- Data recording and analysis skills
- Communication and customer service skills
- Compliance and safety regulations training

**Key growth areas:**

- urban cleaning
- winter maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sweeper in Iceland?**
A: Salaries are at the lower end of the pay scale, often starting at minimum wage levels. Exact figures depend on the employer and collective agreements, but are generally below average Icelandic wages.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as a sweeper in Iceland?**
A: Generally, no. Work permits for low-skilled occupations like sweeping are rarely granted due to strict labour market tests. Non-EEA nationals typically need a skilled occupation to obtain a residence permit.

**Q: What are the promotion opportunities for a sweeper?**
A: Promotion is limited. With experience, you may become a team leader or move into specialized cleaning, such as industrial or high-rise cleaning. Some transition to maintenance roles with additional training.
