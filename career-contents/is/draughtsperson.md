# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons (teiknari) in Iceland create detailed technical drawings and plans for construction, engineering, and manufacturing projects. They work closely with architects, engineers, and construction managers to translate concepts into precise blueprints, often using CAD software. The role is vital in Iceland's growing infrastructure, renewable energy (geothermal and hydropower), and housing sectors. Most are employed by engineering firms, construction companies, or as freelancers. The labour market demands skilled drafters with proficiency in BIM and 3D modeling.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior drafter, advancing to senior drafter or design coordinator. With further education (e.g., a degree in engineering or architecture), one can move into design engineering or project management. Specialization in fields like geothermal systems or structural steel is common. Continuous learning of new software (Revit, Tekla) is essential for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Geothermal Energy
- Hydropower
- Construction
- BIM/Digital Design

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a draughtsperson in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. A junior draughtsperson can expect around 500,000 ISK per month, while senior or specialized drafters may earn up to 800,000 ISK per month. In-demand skills like BIM can command higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals get a work permit as a draughtsperson in Iceland?**
A: Yes, provided they have a job offer from an Icelandic employer. The employer must prove through a labour market test that no qualified EEA/EFTA worker is available. The occupation is on the shortage list, which facilitates the process. The permit is initially valid for one year and renewable.

**Q: What software do draughtspersons commonly use in Iceland?**
A: AutoCAD is standard, and Revit (for BIM) is increasingly required. Tekla is used for structural steel detailing, and MicroStation appears in some infrastructure projects.
