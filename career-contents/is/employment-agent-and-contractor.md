# Employment Agents and Contractors — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3333 (ISCO08)**

Employment agents and contractors in Iceland facilitate job placement and temporary staffing across industries such as tourism, construction, and healthcare. They act as intermediaries between employers and job seekers, handling recruitment, contracts, and compliance with Icelandic labour laws. The role requires knowledge of EEA/EFTA free movement rules and domestic regulations. Given Iceland's small but dynamic economy, agents often specialize in niche sectors like IT or marine industries. The profession supports labour market flexibility, especially during peak seasons or project-based work.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an employment agent in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level agents can expect around 5-6 million ISK annually, while senior specialists or agency managers may earn 8-12 million ISK. Commission structures are common.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as an employment agent in Iceland?**
A: Yes, if they receive a job offer and the employer demonstrates that no qualified EEA/EEA candidate is available (labour market test). The position must meet minimum salary thresholds (currently around 450,000 ISK per month). A work and residence permit is then granted for a specific employer.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities?**
A: Agents can move into senior recruitment specialist roles, agency management, or start their own staffing firm. Specializing in high-demand sectors (e.g., healthcare, IT) accelerates progression. Additional HR certifications and language skills open further doors.
