# Government Regulatory Associate Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3359 (ISCO08)**

Government regulatory associate professionals in Iceland perform inspections, enforce regulations, and ensure compliance in areas such as customs, taxation, labour standards, and environmental protection. They work for state agencies like the Directorate of Internal Revenue, the Environment Agency, or the Administration of Occupational Safety and Health. These roles require strong knowledge of Icelandic legislation, attention to detail, and the ability to interact with the public and businesses. Most positions are based in Reykjavík, but regional offices exist. The work is largely office-based with occasional field inspections.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a government regulatory associate professional in Iceland?**
A: Starting salaries for entry-level regulatory officers are around 400,000–550,000 ISK per month, while experienced professionals earn between 600,000 and 800,000 ISK. Senior specialists or team leaders may exceed 1,000,000 ISK. Salaries are set by collective agreements and include pension contributions.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national become a government regulatory associate professional in Iceland?**
A: It is difficult but possible. Most public sector jobs require Icelandic citizenship or long-term residence due to language and security clearances. Non-EEA nationals typically need a work permit, but the labour market test may fail if an EEA candidate is available. High-demand fields like data protection or environmental regulation may offer exceptions.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist in this field?**
A: Common progression includes moving from inspector to senior inspector, then to team leader or policy advisor. Some transition to management roles in larger agencies or join the Ministry. Further education (e.g., a master’s in law or policy) can accelerate advancement.
