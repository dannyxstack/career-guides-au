# Engineering Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2149 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers engineers who do not fit into other detailed engineering categories, including environmental, safety, and general engineering roles. In Iceland, these professionals are vital for expanding renewable energy infrastructure, urban development, and environmental protection, often working in consultancy, government, and private firms. The role typically requires a bachelor's or master's degree in engineering.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, but are generally competitive with other engineering roles. Entry-level positions start around 450,000 ISK monthly, while senior roles can exceed 900,000 ISK.

**Q: Can non-EEA engineers easily get a work permit in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but with conditions. The job must be on the shortage list or pass a labour market test. The employer must advertise the position locally first. A work permit is tied to the employer and lasts one year, renewable.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for engineers in Iceland?**
A: Engineers can progress to senior technical roles, project management, or specialize in high-demand fields like geothermal energy. Many also move into consultancy or start their own firms.
