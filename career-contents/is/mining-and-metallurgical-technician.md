# Mining and Metallurgical Technician — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3117 (ISCO08)**

Mining and metallurgical technicians in Iceland work primarily in the country's prominent aluminium smelters and silicon metal plants, such as those operated by Rio Tinto Alcan, Norðurál, and United Silicon. They are responsible for monitoring and optimizing industrial processes, conducting quality control tests, and ensuring safe and efficient operations. The role requires a blend of practical technical skills and theoretical knowledge in metallurgy, with most positions located in rural or industrial areas near geothermal or hydroelectric power plants. While Iceland has limited traditional mining, the metallurgical sector is a vital economic contributor, supported by abundant renewable energy.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence the salary of a mining and metallurgical technician in Iceland?**
A: Salary is primarily determined by experience, education level, and the specific employer. Shift work (including night and weekend shifts) can increase earnings through bonuses. The industry generally offers competitive pay above the national median, with opportunities for overtime.

**Q: Can a non-EEA citizen work as a mining and metallurgical technician in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but a work and residence permit for qualified professionals is required. The employer must prove that the position could not be filled by an EEA/EFTA candidate. This involves advertising the job within the EEA/EFTA for 10 days and receiving labour market approval from the Directorate of Labour.

**Q: What education is typically required for this occupation?**
A: Most technicians hold a vocational diploma in metallurgical technology or a related field (2 years post-secondary). Employers also accept a bachelor's degree in metallurgical engineering or equivalent practical experience. On-the-job training is common.
