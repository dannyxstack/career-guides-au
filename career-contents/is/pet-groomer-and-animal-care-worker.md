# Animal Care Worker — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

Animal care workers in Iceland provide grooming, feeding, and general care for pets and other animals in settings such as kennels, pet shops, veterinary clinics, and animal shelters. The occupation is growing slowly due to high pet ownership rates and increasing demand for professional grooming services, but remains a small niche within the broader agriculture and environment sector. Most workers are self-employed or employed by small businesses, and the role often combines hands-on animal handling with customer service.

Employment in animal care is expected to remain stable with slight growth, driven by urbanization and a cultural emphasis on pet welfare. However, the small population limits expansion, and seasonal variations in tourism can affect demand for boarding services. Automation is unlikely to replace these roles, but competition for jobs is low due to limited formal requirements. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with entry-level positions such as kennel assistant or pet store aide, advancing to senior groomer, shelter manager, or self-employment. Further training in veterinary nursing or animal behavior opens doors to specialized roles. Iceland offers few formal education pathways, so experience and reputation are crucial. Some workers transition into related fields like veterinary support or animal welfare advocacy.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Client appointment and schedule management
- Basic beauty records and billing
- Standardized communication (e.g., reminders, follow-ups)
- Security monitoring (e.g., camera analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze pet fur health for care recommendations
- Use AI tools to optimize inventory and supplies management
- Use AI diagnostic tools to assist in identifying skin issues
- Using AI to generate personalized beauty solutions and recommendations

**Human moat:**

- Calming techniques for handling anxious or aggressive animals
- Adjust grooming techniques flexibly based on individual pet condition
- Building emotional trust and personalized service with customers
- Creativity and aesthetic judgment in hand styling

**Skills to build:**

- Learn animal behavior knowledge to improve soothing skills
- Master the use of AI-assisted diagnostic tools
- Enhancing advanced beauty and styling design skills
- Learn to use customer relationship management software
- Attend professional certification courses to enhance competitiveness
- develop social media marketing skills to expand client base

**Key growth areas:**

- Pet grooming
- Animal boarding
- Veterinary support
- Pet-care services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pet groomer in Iceland?**
A: Salaries are modest compared to other occupations; entry-level positions may earn around the minimum wage, while experienced groomers can earn higher with tips and commission.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as animal care workers in Iceland?**
A: It is difficult because the occupation is not considered skilled; a work permit requires a job offer and a labour market test, which is rarely approved for this type of work.

**Q: Do I need formal education to become an animal care worker in Iceland?**
A: No, formal education is not mandatory, but completing a short course or vocational certificate improves job prospects and may lead to higher pay.
