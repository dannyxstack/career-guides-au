# Cleaners and Helpers in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 9112 (ISCO08)**

Cleaners and helpers in offices, hotels, and other establishments perform routine cleaning tasks such as sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, and emptying trash. In Iceland, this occupation is widespread across the hospitality, tourism, and commercial sectors. Many workers are immigrants, and the job often requires no formal education. The work is typically physical, with flexible hours including evenings and weekends. While wages are moderate, the demand for cleaning services remains stable due to tourism and ongoing building maintenance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for cleaners is limited without additional qualifications. Experienced workers may advance to supervisory roles or start their own cleaning businesses. Specialization in areas like industrial cleaning or eco-friendly cleaning can offer better pay. Some use the job as a stepping stone to other hospitality roles. On-the-job training is common, but formal courses (e.g., cleaning technician certificates) can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean floors using smart vacuum and mop robots
- Operation monitoring of automatic window cleaning robots
- Dust removal in standard areas such as corridors and halls following preset routes.
- Use automatic disinfection robots to disinfect bathrooms and kitchens.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize multi-site cleaning routes and schedules
- Use smart sensors to monitor dirt levels and precisely schedule cleaning frequency
- Receive health and safety alerts via wearable devices to enhance personal protection
- Adjusts service details using a customer review AI system to improve satisfaction
- Use digital tools to manage cleaning supplies, reducing waste

**Human moat:**

- Identification and targeted treatment of complex stubborn stains (e.g., carpet stains, oil stains).
- Communicate with clients to understand specific needs and flexibly adjust cleaning methods
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., breaking items, pet accidents)
- Fine cleaning and care of valuable items (e.g., antiques, silk)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic AI device operation and simple troubleshooting
- Use cleaning management software for scheduling and customer relationship management
- Learn specialized cleaning techniques (e.g., stone care, disinfection)
- Improve communication and customer service skills
- Basic English proficiency to interact with smart devices or serve foreign clients
- Learn about personal protective equipment and eco-friendly cleaning agents

**Key growth areas:**

- Tourism
- Hospitality
- Commercial Cleaning
- Green Cleaning

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners in Iceland?**
A: Salaries for cleaners typically range from 400,000 to 500,000 ISK per month, depending on experience and employer.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals get a work permit as a cleaner in Iceland?**
A: It is difficult as the occupation is not on the shortage list and requires a labour market test. EEA/EFTA nationals can work freely.

**Q: What are the career prospects for cleaners in Iceland?**
A: With experience, cleaners may become supervisors or start their own cleaning businesses. Additional training can lead to specialized cleaning roles.
