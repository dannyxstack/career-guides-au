# Health Services Manager — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 1342 (ISCO08)**

Health Services Managers in Iceland oversee the planning, coordination, and administration of healthcare facilities such as hospitals and clinics, ensuring efficient delivery of services in a system that balances public and private provision. With an aging population and growing demand for integrated care, these managers are critical to implementing digital health solutions and workforce planning. The occupation is typically found in both the public sector (e.g., Landspítali) and private health organizations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression often starts from clinical roles (e.g., nurse, doctor) moving into administration, or from business/management backgrounds in healthcare settings. Advanced education in health management (e.g., Master's from the University of Iceland) is common. Progression can lead to executive roles like CEO of a hospital or chief medical officer. Specialisation in areas like e-health or geriatric care management is increasingly valuable.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a Health Services Manager in Iceland?**
A: Salaries for Health Services Managers range from approximately 7,000,000 ISK to 12,000,000 ISK per year depending on experience, qualifications, and the size of the facility. Public sector salaries are set by union agreements, while private sector may vary.

**Q: Is a Health Services Manager eligible for a work permit in Iceland?**
A: Yes, non-EEA nationals can apply for a work and residence permit if they have a job offer from an Icelandic employer. A labour market test is required to ensure no EEA national can fill the position. The occupation is currently on the shortage list, which may expedite processing.

**Q: Are Icelandic certifications required for foreign health managers?**
A: No specific certification is mandatory, but employers often require a master's degree in health management or related field. Recognition of foreign qualifications is handled by the Icelandic Directorate of Education.
