# Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 1439 (ISCO08)**

Services managers not elsewhere classified oversee operations in a wide range of service industries, such as tourism, event management, cleaning, and facility services in Iceland. They are responsible for planning, organizing, directing, and controlling activities to ensure customer satisfaction and operational efficiency. In Iceland's tourism-driven economy, these roles are vital in maintaining quality standards in hotels, tour companies, and various service providers. They often coordinate with government agencies on compliance and sustainability.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a services manager in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. Entry-level managers earn around 500,000–600,000 ISK per month, while experienced managers can earn 800,000–1,200,000 ISK. Many roles include performance bonuses or tips.

**Q: Do non-EEA services managers need a work permit?**
A: Yes, non-EEA nationals require a work and residence permit tied to a specific job offer. The position must meet the 'qualified professional' criteria (typically requiring a bachelor's degree). The employer must prove no suitable EEA candidate is available.

**Q: Can services managers advance to higher roles in Iceland?**
A: Yes, with experience, managers can move into senior roles like operations director, regional manager, or even start their own businesses. Continuing education in business or niche service areas is beneficial.
