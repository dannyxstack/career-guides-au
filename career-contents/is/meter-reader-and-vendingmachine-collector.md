# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Iceland are responsible for manually or electronically recording utility meter readings (electricity, water) and collecting cash from vending machines. This occupation is small but steady, concentrated in Reykjavík and larger towns, with some remote fieldwork. The role requires a driver's license and basic numeracy, and is often entry-level. Automation and smart meters are gradually reducing manual reading, but vending machine servicing remains stable due to tourism.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression is limited. Workers may advance to senior meter reader or supervisory roles in utility companies, or transition into related field service technician positions (e.g., vending machine repair). Some move into logistics or warehouse work. On-the-job training is typical, and experienced workers may train newcomers. The role is often a stepping stone for those seeking stable employment without formal higher education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings
- On-site inspection of measuring equipment faults (initial judgment)
- Generate manual meter reading reports.
- Reading meters along a fixed route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile apps or handheld terminals to assist with remote confirmation of abnormal readings.
- Identify potential leaks or tampering by analyzing consumption patterns via AI
- Use autonomous inspection drones or robots for meter reading in high-risk areas
- Provide energy advice to clients based on AI-generated energy saving reports.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair in complex on-site environments.
- In-person communication with clients over billing disputes or safety issues
- Decision-making ability in emergencies (e.g., gas leaks)
- Manual calibration and maintenance of old mechanical watch heads

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and anomaly detection
- Mobile device and specialized app operation
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic electrical/mechanical repair knowledge
- Drone or robot operation and maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- energy metering
- smart meters
- vending machine servicing
- field data collection

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a meter reader in Iceland?**
A: Salaries are modest and based on collective agreements. Entry-level pay is lower, while experienced workers may earn slightly more. Overtime for remote routes is common.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit for this job?**
A: It is very difficult because the occupation is not on the shortage list and is considered low-skilled. A labour market test would likely show available EEA workers.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities?**
A: Promotion is limited but possible to senior meter reader or field supervisor. Some transition into vending machine technician or logistics roles with further training.
