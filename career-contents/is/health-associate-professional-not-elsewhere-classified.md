# Health Associate Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3259 (ISCO08)**

This broad category encompasses various health associate professionals in Iceland, such as dental assistants, medical assistants, podiatrists, and orthoptists. They work under the supervision of higher-level health professionals in clinics, hospitals, and private practices. Demand is driven by an ageing population and a focus on preventive care. Most roles require vocational training or a short higher education programme. Proficiency in Icelandic is often necessary for patient interaction, though English may suffice in some settings.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health associate professionals in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by role and experience. Entry-level positions typically start around 350,000–450,000 ISK per month, while experienced specialists can earn 500,000–700,000 ISK. The public sector offers standardised pay scales; private clinics may offer higher rates. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total compensation.

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a health associate professional in Iceland?**
A: EEA/EFTA nationals do not need a permit but must register. Non-EEA nationals need a job offer and a work permit. The labour market test is required unless the occupation is on the shortage list. Many health associate roles are on the shortage list, simplifying the process.

**Q: Are Icelandic language skills required for these roles?**
A: Yes, for most patient-facing roles, basic to intermediate Icelandic (B1–B2 level) is typically required. Some specialist roles in international clinics may accept English, but learning Icelandic is strongly recommended for career advancement and daily life.
