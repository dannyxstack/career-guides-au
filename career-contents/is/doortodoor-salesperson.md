# Door-to-door Salesperson — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5243 (ISCO08)**

Door-to-door salespersons in Iceland typically sell products or services such as telecom subscriptions, energy contracts, or charity memberships directly to households. The role is often commission-based and may be part-time or seasonal, with demand fluctuating based on market campaigns. Given Iceland's small population and increasing digital marketing, this occupation is less common than in larger markets, but still exists for certain industries emphasizing personal contact.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How are door-to-door salespersons paid in Iceland?**
A: Earnings are largely commission-based and vary with performance. Some employers offer a low base salary plus commission, but many roles are entirely commission-driven.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit as a door-to-door salesperson?**
A: It is very difficult. Iceland's labour market test requires that no EEA/EFTA worker is available, and this occupation is not on the shortage list. Permits are rarely granted for low-skilled direct sales roles.

**Q: What career progression exists for door-to-door salespersons in Iceland?**
A: Advancement often involves moving into sales management, telemarketing, or business development after gaining experience. Some transition to other customer-facing roles.
