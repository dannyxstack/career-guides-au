# Information and Communications Technology Services Manager — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 1330 (ISCO08)**

Information and Communications Technology Services Managers in Iceland oversee the strategic direction, implementation, and maintenance of an organization's IT systems, including networks, software, and cybersecurity. They lead teams, manage budgets, and align technology with business goals. In Iceland's small but digitally advanced economy, these managers are crucial in sectors like finance, fisheries, geothermal energy, and the growing tech startup scene.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts from roles like IT specialist, systems administrator, or developer, moving into team lead or project manager before reaching ICT services manager. Further advancement can lead to chief information officer (CIO) or other executive positions. Many managers hold a master's degree in IT management or an MBA with technology focus. Continuous learning in cloud, cybersecurity, and agile methodologies is essential.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Services Manager in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on company size, sector, and experience. Generally, it ranges from 9,000,000 to 14,000,000 ISK annually, with senior roles at larger firms paying more. Benefits often include pension and bonuses.

**Q: Do I need a job offer before applying for a work permit as an ICT Services Manager?**
A: Yes. Non-EEA nationals must have a concrete job offer from an Icelandic employer. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled locally, though the shortage list may expedite the process. The permit is then processed by the Directorate of Immigration.

**Q: Is a university degree mandatory for this role in Iceland?**
A: While not strictly mandatory, a Bachelor's degree in a related field is typically required, and many employers prefer a Master's. Equivalent experience in IT management may substitute in some cases.
