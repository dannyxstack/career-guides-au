# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications engineering technicians in Iceland install, maintain, and repair telecommunications equipment and networks, including fiber-optic cables, mobile infrastructure, and broadband systems. With Iceland's rugged terrain and sparse population, these technicians often work in remote areas to ensure connectivity, supporting the country's high digital penetration and reliance on reliable telecommunications for everything from emergency services to tourism. The role demands both technical expertise and adaptability to harsh weather conditions.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a telecommunications engineering technician in Iceland?**
A: Salaries are competitive and vary based on experience and employer. Entry-level technicians can expect around ISK 5,000,000 to 6,000,000 per year, while senior technicians earn up to ISK 8,000,000 or more. Overtime and travel allowances are common.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as a telecommunications engineering technician in Iceland?**
A: Yes, but a work permit is required. The employer must prove the job cannot be filled locally. The occupation is on Iceland's shortage list, which may expedite the process. The permit is job-specific and must be renewed for changes.

**Q: Is Icelandic language proficiency required for this job?**
A: While not always mandatory, basic Icelandic is beneficial for communicating with colleagues and clients. Many employers require at least conversational Icelandic, especially for customer-facing roles.
