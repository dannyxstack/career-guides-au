# Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 7127 (ISCO08)**

Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics in Iceland install, maintain, and repair cooling and ventilation systems in commercial, industrial, and residential settings. With Iceland's cold climate and reliance on geothermal heating, these professionals work on heat pumps, refrigeration units, and HVAC systems. The role is critical in food storage, fisheries, and hospitality sectors. Demand is steady due to ongoing construction and retrofitting of energy-efficient systems.
