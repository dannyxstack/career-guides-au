# Education Manager — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 1345 (ISCO08)**

Education managers in Iceland oversee the administration, curriculum, and staff of educational institutions from preschools to universities. They ensure compliance with national education standards, manage budgets, and foster a positive learning environment. The role is critical in a system that emphasizes decentralization and quality improvement, with growing demand for leaders in early childhood and special needs education. Fluency in Icelandic and deep knowledge of the local education system are essential.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an education manager in Iceland?**
A: Annual salaries for education managers in Iceland range from 7 to 10 million ISK before tax, depending on experience and institution size. Principals of larger schools earn at the higher end.

**Q: Do non-EEA education managers need a visa to work in Iceland?**
A: Yes, non-EEA nationals require a work and residence permit for qualified professionals. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EEA national, and a job offer is necessary. The process can take several months.

**Q: Is a teaching license mandatory for education managers in Iceland?**
A: For managers in compulsory schools (grades 1-10), a teaching license is mandatory. In other settings like preschools or universities, relevant pedagogical qualifications are required.
