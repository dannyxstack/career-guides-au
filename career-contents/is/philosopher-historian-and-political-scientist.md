# Philosopher, Historian and Political Scientist — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2633 (ISCO08)**

In Iceland, philosophers, historians and political scientists primarily work in academia, research institutes and think tanks. The University of Iceland, Reykjavik University and Bifröst University are main employers. The field is small but intellectually vibrant, with opportunities in teaching, policy analysis and archival work. Roughly a few hundred professionals are active, reflecting the country's emphasis on education and social sciences. EU/EEA citizens benefit from free movement, while others face a competitive, restricted labour market.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a philosopher, historian or political scientist in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely. University professors earn around 8-12 million ISK annually; postdocs and policy analysts earn 5-8 million. Exact figures depend on experience and institution.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA citizen get a work permit as a philosopher/historian/political scientist?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. You need a job offer from an Icelandic employer, and the position must pass a labour market test proving no local candidate available. The permit is tied to that employer.

**Q: What are the main employers for this field in Iceland?**
A: The University of Iceland is the largest, followed by Reykjavik University, Bifröst University, and the University of Akureyri. Government ministries and the National Archives also hire historians and political scientists.
