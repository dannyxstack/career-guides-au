# Environmental Protection Professional — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 2133 (ISCO08)**

Environmental protection professionals in Iceland assess and manage environmental impacts, ensuring compliance with strict regulations. They work on projects related to renewable energy (geothermal, hydropower), waste management, conservation, and climate change adaptation. Typical employers include the Environment Agency of Iceland (Umhverfisstofnun), municipalities, consulting firms, and energy companies. The role requires a master's degree in environmental science or a related field, along with knowledge of Icelandic environmental law and often proficiency in Icelandic.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions may start around the national average for professionals, while senior roles in consulting can be significantly higher. Public sector jobs offer stable income but lower top salaries. Overall, compensation is competitive within Iceland's labour market.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals apply for jobs in this field without a job offer?**
A: Generally no. Non-EEA nationals must have a valid job offer to apply for a work permit. However, if they hold a master's degree from an Icelandic university, they may be eligible for a job-seeking visa for up to 6 months after graduation.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities?**
A: Professionals can advance to senior consultant, project manager, or team leader. Specialization in areas like climate change adaptation or geothermal resource management can open niche opportunities. Some move into regulatory roles at the Environment Agency or pursue PhDs for academic careers.
