# Survey and Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and market research interviewers conduct interviews to collect data for public opinion polls, market research, and government surveys in Iceland. They work for research firms, Statistics Iceland, and private companies, typically via phone, online platforms, or face-to-face.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically moves from entry-level interviewer to team leader, research assistant, or data analyst. Experienced interviewers may specialize in qualitative research or project management. Fluency in Icelandic and English is often required.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Data collection
- Market analysis
- Customer insights
- Public opinion polling

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent communication skills and active listening
- Detail-oriented individuals who can follow scripts accurately
- Comfortable with telephone and online survey tools

**Who is not suited**
- Those who dislike repetitive tasks and strict scripting
- People uncomfortable with cold calling or rejection

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a survey interviewer in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and experience, but typically pay is hourly or project-based. Gross monthly earnings for full-time work range around 350,000–450,000 ISK, but many positions are part-time or seasonal.

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a survey interviewer in Iceland?**
A: EEA/EFTA nationals can work freely without a permit, but must register. Non-EEA nationals require a work and residence permit tied to a job offer. As this occupation is not on the shortage list, approval is less common.
