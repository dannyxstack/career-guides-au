# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

Metal working machine tool setters and operators in Iceland set up and operate machines such as lathes, milling machines, and grinders to shape metal parts. They work in manufacturing, repair, and construction industries, primarily in the Reykjavik capital area and regional industrial hubs. The role requires precision, technical knowledge, and adherence to safety standards. With Iceland's growing emphasis on automation and renewable energy infrastructure, demand for skilled operators remains steady, though reliance on imported talent is common due to limited local training capacity.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, followed by specialization in CNC operation, programming, or quality control. Experienced operators may advance to team leader or supervisor roles, or transition into maintenance and repair of industrial machinery. In Iceland, further education in mechanical engineering or industrial automation can open doors to higher-skilled positions. The field offers relatively stable employment but limited upward mobility without additional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC programming
- Automation
- Renewable energy manufacturing
- Precision engineering

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a metal working machine tool setter and operator in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location but typically range from 450,000 to 600,000 ISK per month before taxes. Overtime and shift differentials can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as a metal working machine tool setter and operator in Iceland?**
A: Yes, if they secure a job offer in a shortage occupation, the employer must conduct a labour market test, and the applicant must obtain a work and residence permit. The occupation is on the shortage list for skilled trades.
