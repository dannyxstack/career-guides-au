# Personal Services Worker — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 5169 (ISCO08)**

Personal services workers in Iceland cover a diverse range of roles including funeral directors, pet groomers, and other personal care providers not elsewhere classified. These positions are often found in small businesses or self-employment, catering to niche demands. The Icelandic labour market for these roles is small but steady, with opportunities linked to demographic trends and lifestyle services. While not a major employment sector, it offers flexibility and specialization for those with relevant skills.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a personal services worker in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialization; funeral directors tend to earn above average due to licensing, while pet groomers earn around average service sector wages. Self-employed workers' income depends on clientele and reputation.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit for a personal services job in Iceland?**
A: It is challenging because these occupations are not considered in shortage. Labour market tests usually find local candidates, and permits are rarely granted unless the role requires specialized skills not available in Iceland.

**Q: What training is necessary to become a funeral director in Iceland?**
A: A vocational diploma in funeral services (1 year) and a license from the Ministry of Health are mandatory. The course covers embalming, regulations, and client management.
