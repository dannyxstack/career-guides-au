# Sewing Machine Operator — career analysis · Iceland

**Occupation code: 8153 (ISCO08)**

Sewing machine operators in Iceland work primarily in small-scale garment manufacturing, upholstery, and outdoor gear production. The industry is niche, with most operators employed by specialized workshops or tourism-related businesses producing custom clothing, costumes, and repair services. The role requires precision and attention to detail, often on a piecework basis.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sewing machine operator in Iceland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer, but are generally in line with collective agreements in the textile industry. Entry-level positions typically earn less than the average manufacturing wage, while experienced operators can earn higher rates.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national get a work permit as a sewing machine operator in Iceland?**
A: It is difficult because the occupation is not on the shortage list and typically does not meet the minimum salary threshold for skilled workers. EEA/EFTA nationals can work freely, but others need a strong job offer and proof that the role cannot be filled locally.

**Q: Is there demand for sewing machine operators in Iceland?**
A: Demand is limited and concentrated in custom garment workshops, upholstery, and tourism-related production. Automation and imports keep overall demand low, but niche areas like outdoor gear repair have steady need.
