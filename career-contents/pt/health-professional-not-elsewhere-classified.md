## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization and sector. In the public sector, entry-level positions start around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals in private practice or hospitals can earn €2,000–€3,000 monthly. Private clinics and specialized roles may offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU health professionals to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for a work visa through the Portuguese consulate if they have a job offer. Alternatively, they may qualify for an EU Blue Card, which requires a binding job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary, recognition of qualifications, and a valid employment contract.

**Q: What are the main employment settings for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Portugal?**
A: These professionals work in public hospitals, private clinics, rehabilitation centers, nursing homes, and research institutions. Many also operate in community health programs or private practice. Registration with the relevant professional order (e.g., Ordem dos Nutricionistas, Ordem dos Biólogos) is often mandatory for regulated roles.
