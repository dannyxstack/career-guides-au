## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for croupiers in Portugal?**
A: Croupiers in Portugal typically earn a starting salary between €1,000 and €1,500 per month, plus tips. Experienced professionals in major casinos can earn higher. Average annual compensation including tips may reach €20,000–€25,000. These figures are estimates and vary by location and employer.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as croupiers in Portugal through a work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as croupiers in Portugal through the D3 visa for skilled workers or the EU Blue Card, provided they have a valid job offer and meet licensing requirements. The occupation may require a professional license from the Portuguese gambling authority (SRIJ). Always consult official sources like SEF for updated procedures.

**Q: What training is required to become a croupier in Portugal?**
A: To become a croupier in Portugal, you must complete a certified training course from an accredited school covering game rules and dealing techniques. You then need to pass a background check by SRIJ to obtain a professional license. Ongoing training is required for career advancement, and experience can lead to supervisory roles or higher-stakes tables.
