## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions often start around €25,000–€35,000 per year. Experienced professionals can earn €40,000–€60,000, with senior roles exceeding €70,000. Bonuses and commissions are common, and industry (e.g., pharmaceuticals, medical devices) significantly influences pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a job offer and apply for a Portuguese work visa or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers meeting salary thresholds). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Self-employed individuals may use the D2 visa. All applications require valid contracts, qualifications, and meeting legal requirements.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are most valued for Technical and Medical Sales roles in Portugal?**
A: Employers seek a bachelor's degree in a relevant field (e.g., life sciences, engineering) and strong sales experience. Fluency in Portuguese and English is often required. Key skills include negotiation, technical knowledge of products, and relationship-building. Certifications in sales or specific medical technologies can enhance prospects.
