## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a police officer in Portugal?**
A: New police officers in Portugal (PSP or GNR) earn a gross monthly salary between €1,200 and €1,400, plus allowances for shifts and risk. After a few years, salaries can rise to €1,600–€2,000. Senior ranks and specialized units earn higher. These figures are pre-tax and may vary by region and experience.

**Q: Can foreign police officers work in Portugal through the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not applicable for police officers as it targets highly qualified roles requiring a degree and high salary. For Portuguese police (PSP or GNR), applicants must be Portuguese or from an EU/EEA country. Non-EU citizens cannot become police officers unless they naturalize as Portuguese citizens first.

**Q: What are the requirements to become a police officer in Portugal?**
A: Candidates must be Portuguese or EU/EEA nationals, aged 18–30, have completed 12th grade, and pass physical, medical, and psychological tests. They also undergo a background check and a training course at the police academy. No prior law enforcement experience is needed; the selection process is competitive.
