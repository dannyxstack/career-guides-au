**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Schedule cleaning appointments, send reminders, and collect payments via chatbot
- Use computer vision to inspect cleaning quality and generate reports
- Automated inventory management: predict cleaning supply needs based on historical data and place orders
- Voice assistants control smart home devices (e.g., robot vacuum cleaners, automatic curtains)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI voice translation tool assists in communicating with foreign employers
- Smart devices (e.g., stain detection apps) to assist in choosing the best cleaning agent
- Data analysis tools optimize cleaning routes and time allocation
- Online platform automatically matches customers and cleaners, improving customer acquisition efficiency

- Building long-term trust relationships and personalized services with employers
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., spills, damage) with flexibility

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Portugal?**
A: The salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Portugal typically ranges from the national minimum wage (€760 per month in 2023) to around €900–1,000 per month, depending on experience, location, and whether employment is full-time or part-time. Many are paid hourly, averaging €5–7 per hour.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as domestic cleaners in Portugal via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as domestic cleaners in Portugal through a regular work visa (D1) if they have a valid job offer. The employer must prove the need to hire a foreign worker. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation, as it requires a high-skilled job with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average.

**Q: What are the main duties of domestic cleaners and helpers in Portugal?**
A: Domestic cleaners and helpers in Portugal perform tasks such as cleaning private homes, sweeping, mopping, dusting, washing dishes, doing laundry, and sometimes ironing. They may also assist with basic household organization. The work is typically physical and may involve using cleaning products and equipment.
