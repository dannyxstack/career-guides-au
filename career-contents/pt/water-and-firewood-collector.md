## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average pay for water and firewood collectors in Portugal?**
A: Water and firewood collectors in Portugal typically earn around the national minimum wage, which in 2024 is €820 per month (14 payments per year). However, many are self-employed or work informally, so incomes vary. Some may earn slightly more in areas with higher demand, but the average remains close to minimum wage.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals get a work visa as a water and firewood collector in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a work visa if they have a job offer from a Portuguese employer. The employer must prove the role cannot be filled locally. For low-skilled jobs like water and firewood collecting, the EU Blue Card is not available as it requires a high-skilled role. Instead, a general work visa (Autorização de Residência para Exercício de Atividade Profissional Subordinada) is the standard route.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for water and firewood collectors in Portugal?**
A: These workers often operate in rural areas, collecting water from natural sources or gathering firewood for sale or personal use. Work is physically demanding, outdoors, and may be seasonal. Many work independently or for small local businesses. Safety hazards include weather exposure and physical strain. Employment is often informal, with limited social protections.
