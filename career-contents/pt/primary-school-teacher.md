## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Marking standardised assignments and exam papers
- Generate basic lesson plans and classroom materials
- Record and analyze student attendance and performance data
- Answering common knowledge questions (e.g., math calculations, spelling)
- Managing classroom multimedia equipment and basic IT troubleshooting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI adaptive learning platforms to customize exercises and feedback for each student
- Use AI to analyze student behavior and performance data to early identify learning difficulties or social-emotional issues
- Quickly generate differentiated teaching resources (e.g., reading materials at varying difficulty levels) using AI tools
- Using AI assistants to plan course progress and evaluate teaching effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- face-to-face emotional communication and building trust between teachers and students
- Managing unexpected classroom situations and behavior
- Tailored teaching based on deep understanding of individual students
- Passing on values, critical thinking, and social skills
- Complex communication and collaboration with parents

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI educational tools (e.g., adaptive learning platforms, intelligent grading systems)
- Data interpretation and student assessment analysis
- Methods for integrating AI resources in instructional design
- Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) and counselling skills
- Interdisciplinary project-based learning (PBL) design
- Digital Literacy and Cybersecurity Awareness

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a primary school teacher in Portugal?**
A: The base salary for primary school teachers in Portugal starts around €1,200–€1,400 per month for a full-time position, with increases based on years of service and additional qualifications. Salaries are set by public sector pay scales, and private schools may offer slightly different rates. Annual earnings typically range from €18,000 to €25,000 before taxes.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a primary school teacher moving to Portugal?**
A: Non-EU teachers can apply for a work visa with a job offer, requiring employer sponsorship and recognition of teaching qualifications by the Portuguese Ministry of Education. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €1,800/month in 2025). The D2 visa for independent workers or the D7 passive income visa may apply for self-employed teaching.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a primary school teacher in Portugal?**
A: To teach in public schools, you need a bachelor's degree in primary education or equivalent, plus a professional teaching qualification (e.g., Mestrado em Ensino). Private schools may accept other degrees if combined with teaching experience. Non-Portuguese qualifications must be recognised by the Ministry of Education (Direção-Geral do Ensino Superior). Fluency in Portuguese is mandatory for all teaching roles.
