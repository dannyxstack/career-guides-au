## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for survey and market research interviewers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for survey and market research interviewers in Portugal vary based on experience and employer. Entry-level positions often pay near the national minimum wage (around €820 per month in 2024), while experienced interviewers can earn up to €1,200 per month. Some roles offer performance bonuses or per-interview commissions.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as a survey interviewer in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer to apply for a Portuguese work visa (D1). The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable as it requires a higher-skilled role and a salary threshold (at least 1.5x the national average). After working legally for several years, one may apply for permanent residency. No specific quotas exist for this occupation.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a survey interviewer in Portugal?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers usually require fluency in Portuguese (and often English) and strong communication skills. A high school diploma is commonly preferred, and prior experience in sales or customer service is advantageous. Some companies provide short training on data collection and interview techniques.
