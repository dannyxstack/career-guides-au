## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for packing, bottling and labelling machine operators in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around €800–€1,000 per month, while experienced workers can make €1,200–€1,500. Overtime, shift premiums, and bonuses are common in manufacturing. Salaries vary by region and industry, with higher pay in industrial zones like Lisbon, Porto, and the north.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as packing machine operators in Portugal via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if they hold a qualifying job offer. The EU Blue Card in Portugal requires a work contract for at least 12 months and salary ≥ 1.5× the national average gross annual wage (approx. €1,500/month in 2025). A regular work visa is also possible for general operators. Both routes require employer sponsorship and meet labor market tests.

**Q: What are the main duties of packing, bottling and labelling machine operators in Portugal?**
A: Operators set up, monitor, and maintain automated packaging lines. Tasks include adjusting fill levels, inspecting labels, checking seals, and logging production data. They ensure compliance with food safety or pharmaceutical standards, perform basic troubleshooting, and report malfunctions. Teamwork and shift work are common.
