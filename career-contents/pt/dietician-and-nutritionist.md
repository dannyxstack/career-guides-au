## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dieticians and nutritionists in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level dieticians in Portugal earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month, while experienced professionals can earn €1,800–€2,500 monthly. Salaries vary by sector: public hospitals, private clinics, and sports nutrition. The average annual salary is approximately €20,000–€30,000. Self-employed nutritionists may have variable income.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign dieticians to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU dieticians can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (threshold updated annually). Alternatively, the D3 visa for highly qualified professionals or a standard work visa (subordinate employment) are options. All require recognition of foreign qualifications by the Portuguese Association of Nutritionists (APN).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a dietician in Portugal?**
A: To practice in Portugal, dieticians must hold a degree in nutrition sciences (BSc or MSc) from an accredited institution, complete a professional internship (usually 6 months), and register with the Portuguese Association of Nutritionists (APN). Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Directorate-General for Higher Education (DGES) and the APN.
