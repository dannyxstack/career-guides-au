## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physiotherapists in Portugal?**
A: The average gross monthly salary ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, with higher pay in private clinics and Lisbon. Senior physiotherapists or those with specializations can earn up to €2,500.

**Q: Can non-EU physiotherapists work in Portugal via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, physiotherapists can apply for an EU Blue Card with a recognized degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. €33,000/year). The D3 visa for highly qualified workers is also an option. Both require qualification recognition.

**Q: What is the career progression for physiotherapists in Portugal?**
A: Physiotherapists can advance to senior roles, specialize in areas like sports or neurological physiotherapy, or move into management or teaching. Many pursue postgraduate education, and private practice is a common path.
