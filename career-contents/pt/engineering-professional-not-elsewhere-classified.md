## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization, experience, and location. In 2025, the typical annual gross salary ranges from €30,000 to €55,000 for mid-career engineers. Lisbon and Porto offer higher pay, often exceeding €50,000, while rural areas are lower. Senior roles or those in high-demand fields like mechatronics can reach €70,000.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign engineering professionals in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the D3 Visa or the EU Blue Card. The D3 is for highly qualified workers and requires a job offer with salary meeting thresholds. The Blue Card requires an EU-regulated contract and minimum salary of €21,481 yearly (updated annually). Both lead to residency and work rights.

**Q: Which sectors employ engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Portugal?**
A: These engineers work in automotive (e.g., component manufacturing), renewable energy (wind, solar), IT (embedded systems, robotics), and aerospace (e.g., Embraer). Consultancies and R&D centers under European innovation programs also hire. Portugal’s growing digital and green industries create substantial demand.
