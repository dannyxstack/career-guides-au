## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a meter reader or vending-machine collector in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Portugal typically range between €700 and €1,100 per month before taxes, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around the national minimum wage (€760 in 2024), while senior roles may reach higher. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: How can a non-EU national obtain a work visa as a meter reader or vending-machine collector in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work visa via the Sistema de Atração de Trabalhadores or the EU Blue Card. For lower-skilled roles, employer-sponsored work permits under the CPLP agreement (for Portuguese-speaking countries) or regulated employment offers are common. The EU Blue Card requires a higher minimum salary (€1,520/month in 2024) and a job contract, which these roles usually do not meet. Always consult the Portuguese consulate or SEF.

**Q: What are the main duties of meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Portugal?**
A: Duties include traveling to residential or commercial sites to read utility meters (gas, water, electricity) or collect cash from vending machines. Workers must record accurate readings, handle basic maintenance, and issue receipts. The job requires walking, driving, and sometimes working outdoors in various weather. Attention to detail and reliability are essential.
