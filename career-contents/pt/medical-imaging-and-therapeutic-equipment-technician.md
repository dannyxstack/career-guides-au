## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, entry-level technicians earn around €20,000–€25,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €30,000–€40,000. Public hospitals generally follow national pay scales, while private clinics may offer higher salaries. Overtime and on-call duties can increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU medical imaging technicians to work in Portugal via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU technicians can apply for a work visa with a job offer from a Portuguese employer, or an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €33,000 in 2024). The employer must prove the role couldn’t be filled by an EU citizen. Both routes require recognition of qualifications by the competent Portuguese authority.

**Q: What are the main career prospects for medical imaging technicians in Portugal?**
A: Technicians can work in public hospitals, private clinics, and diagnostic centers. Specializations in MRI, CT, or nuclear medicine enhance career growth. With experience, one can become a senior technician, department lead, or educator. Continuing education is encouraged, and the profession is regulated by the Health Ministry.
