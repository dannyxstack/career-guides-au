## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Portugal?**
A: Beauticians in Portugal typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Those in high-end salons or self-employed can earn more. Estimates from public employment services suggest a median monthly salary around €900. Pay may include tips and commission.

**Q: Can beauticians obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Portugal?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Portugal is for highly qualified workers and requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€1,900+ in 2024). Beauticians typically do not meet these criteria. Instead, they can apply for a work visa (D1) with a valid job offer, or the D7 Passive Income visa if eligible.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a beautician in Portugal?**
A: To work as a beautician in Portugal, you generally need a professional certification or diploma from an accredited vocational school. Recognized courses include beautician or aesthetician programs. Registration with the Portuguese Institute of Employment and Vocational Training (IEFP) may be required. For EU citizens, qualifications are mutually recognized; non-EU citizens must have credentials validated by Portuguese authorities.
