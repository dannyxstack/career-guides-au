## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Personal Services Workers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary widely by subsector and experience. As of 2024, many earn close to the national minimum wage (€820/month), while experienced workers in fields like hairdressing or funeral services may earn between €1,000 and €1,500 monthly. Tips and commission can supplement income in some roles.

**Q: Can non-EU Personal Services Workers obtain an EU Blue Card or work visa for Portugal?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified roles requiring a degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €1,100/month in 2024), which personal services jobs rarely meet. Instead, non-EU workers can apply for a D1 work visa if they have a job offer from a Portuguese employer and meet general conditions.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Personal Services Workers in Portugal?**
A: Career progression often involves specialization, such as becoming a master hairdresser, senior beautician, or funeral director. Further training or certifications can lead to supervisory roles or self-employment. The Portuguese system recognizes professional experience, so skilled workers may advance through employer-based promotion.
