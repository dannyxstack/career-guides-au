## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Laundry Machine Operator in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for Laundry Machine Operators in Portugal range from the national minimum wage (€820 per month in 2024) to about €1,000, depending on experience and location. Many are paid monthly with 14 installments per year, including holiday and Christmas subsidies.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Laundry Machine Operator in Portugal via the work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: This occupation typically does not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree or equivalent. Instead, non-EU workers may obtain a standard Portuguese work visa (e.g., D3 Visa for skilled workers, but subject to approval). A valid job offer and work contract are necessary; apply through the Portuguese Immigration Service (SEF).

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Laundry Machine Operators in Portugal?**
A: With experience, operators can advance to supervisory roles, quality control, or equipment maintenance. Some transition into industrial laundry management or start their own small laundry businesses. Further training in textile care or business management may improve prospects.
