## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Buyer in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for Buyers in Portugal vary by experience and industry. Typically, entry-level positions earn around €20,000–€25,000 annually, while experienced buyers can earn €35,000–€50,000. Senior or procurement manager roles may exceed €60,000. These figures are gross and may not include bonuses or benefits.

**Q: Can foreign Buyers work in Portugal via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes. Non-EU buyers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €33,000 in 2024) and meet qualification requirements. Alternatively, a regular work visa (Employment Visa) is possible with a valid employment contract and employer sponsorship through the Portuguese consulate.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a Buyer in Portugal?**
A: A bachelor's degree in business, supply chain, or a related field is common. Employers value negotiation, analytical, and communication skills. Proficiency in English and Portuguese is often required. Professional certifications like CIPS or APICS can enhance prospects. Experience in specific industries (e.g., retail, manufacturing) may be preferred.
