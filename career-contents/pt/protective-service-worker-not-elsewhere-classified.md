## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for protective services workers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by role. Security guards typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month, while private investigators may earn €1,200 to €2,000. The national minimum wage for 2024 is €820, but collective agreements often set higher rates for experienced workers.

**Q: Can I work in Portugal as a protective services worker through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, you can apply for a work visa (Autorização de Residência para Exercício de Atividade Profissional Subordinada) with a valid job offer. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Most protective services jobs do not meet Blue Card criteria unless in managerial or specialized roles.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for protective services jobs in Portugal?**
A: For security guards, a vocational course and registration with the Ministry of Administration are required. Private investigators need a license from the Polícia Judiciária. Fire prevention roles require technical training. A criminal record check is mandatory for all positions.
