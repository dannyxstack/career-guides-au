## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate course outlines and lecture drafts
- Basic Q&A and automated responses to common questions
- Preliminary grading and feedback on student assignments
- Literature review and data collation
- Administrative tasks (e.g., class scheduling, grade entry)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted personalised learning path design and adaptive assessment
- Use LLMs to quickly generate teaching cases and simulated discussions
- Assist with hypothesis testing, data analysis, and paper polishing in research
- Virtual classrooms and collaborative teaching with AI teaching assistants
- Knowledge graph construction and interdisciplinary curriculum planning

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face mentorship and emotional support
- In-depth explanation of complex concepts and stimulation of critical thinking
- Formulation of original research questions and method design
- Ethical judgment and academic decision-making
- Creative integration in overall curriculum design

**Skills to build:**

- Application of AI education tools (e.g., Knewton, Carnegie Learning).
- Data analysis and statistical modeling (Python/R)
- Prompt engineering and large model fine-tuning
- Blended instructional design (MOOC/flipped classroom)
- Academic writing and AI-assisted polishing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a university teacher in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for university teachers in Portugal vary by institution and experience. Entry-level assistant professors earn around €2,500–€3,500 per month (14 payments), while full professors can earn €4,500–€6,000+. Public universities follow official pay scales; private institutions may differ.

**Q: How can non-EU teachers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer and apply for a residence visa for employment. High-skilled workers with a valid employment contract and salary at least 1.5 times the national average may qualify for the EU Blue Card. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with Portuguese immigration law.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a university teacher in Portugal?**
A: Typically, a PhD and relevant research output are required. Teaching positions often require post-doctoral experience. Public universities usually hold a public recruitment process (concurso). For certain disciplines, professional experience may substitute academic credentials. Fluency in Portuguese or English depends on the institution.
