## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a companion or valet in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Portugal vary depending on experience and employer. The minimum monthly wage is around €870 (as of 2025). Private care roles typically pay between €900 and €1,300 per month, while valet positions in luxury settings may offer slightly higher pay. Overtime and benefits can supplement income.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for companions and valets in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a D1 work visa if they have a job offer from a Portuguese employer. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly qualified professionals and is unlikely for this occupation. Another route is the D7 passive income visa if you have sufficient funds. Always check with the Portuguese consulate or SEF for current requirements.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to work as a companion or valet in Portugal?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers often prefer relevant experience or training in elderly care, first aid, or housekeeping. For companions, a course in geriatric care or a certificate from a recognized institution is advantageous. Valets may need a valid driver's license and knowledge of luxury service standards. Language skills in Portuguese are highly valued.
