## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an aircraft engine mechanic in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for aircraft engine mechanics in Portugal generally range from €1,500 to €3,000 per month before taxes, depending on experience, certifications, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €1,500, while senior mechanics with EASA Part-66 licenses can earn higher. Major employers include TAP Maintenance & Engineering and other MROs.

**Q: Can an aircraft engine mechanic from outside the EU work in Portugal via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, aircraft engine mechanics may qualify for the EU Blue Card in Portugal if they have a valid job offer, relevant qualifications (e.g., EASA Part-66 license or equivalent), and meet the salary threshold—typically at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. Applications are submitted through the Portuguese consulate or SEF after securing employment.

**Q: What certifications are needed to work as an aircraft engine mechanic in Portugal?**
A: To work as an aircraft engine mechanic in Portugal, you typically need an EASA Part-66 Aircraft Maintenance License (AML) in the B1.1 or B1.2 category. This requires completing an approved training program, passing exams, and gaining practical experience. Many mechanics also pursue type ratings on specific aircraft engines to enhance their qualifications and job prospects.
