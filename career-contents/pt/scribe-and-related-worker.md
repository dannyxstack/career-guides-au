- Transcribing handwritten documents into electronic text.

- Simple proofreading and formatting adjustments

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI-assisted transcription tools to improve efficiency
- Supervise and correct AI-generated transcription results
- Processing complex handwritten or non-standard format documents
- Manage multilingual document translation and localization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a scribe in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for scribes in Portugal vary widely based on specialization and employment. An employed scribe might earn between the national minimum wage (€820/month in 2024) and €1,500/month. Many work freelance, charging per project, with calligraphy pieces ranging from €50 to €200. Income can fluctuate significantly.

**Q: How can a foreign scribe obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU scribes need a job offer and IEFP approval for a work visa. For the EU Blue Card, the role must be highly skilled with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary. Scribes must demonstrate exceptional artistic skills. Self-employed individuals can apply for a residence visa for independent workers.

**Q: How is the role of scribes evolving with technology and AI in Portugal?**
A: AI and digital tools have reduced traditional scribing demand, but a niche market persists in Portugal for hand-lettering, wedding invitations, and artistic calligraphy. Many scribes blend traditional skills with digital design, offering services online. Adaptation to modern contexts is key; the role is unlikely to vanish entirely.
