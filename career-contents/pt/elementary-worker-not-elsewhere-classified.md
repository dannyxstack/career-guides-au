## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers in Portugal?**
A: Elementary workers in Portugal generally earn around the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €820 per month (paid 14 times a year). Actual pay varies by sector and region, with some roles in cleaning or agriculture offering slightly more. Salaries rarely exceed €1,000 per month for entry-level positions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens get a work visa for elementary jobs in Portugal?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can obtain a work visa for elementary jobs if they have a job offer from a Portuguese employer. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a local or EU worker. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold well above elementary-level pay.

**Q: What duties do elementary workers not elsewhere classified perform in Portugal?**
A: These workers perform simple, routine tasks in various sectors such as cleaning, construction, manufacturing, and agriculture. Duties include manual labor, loading/unloading goods, street cleaning, and assisting skilled workers. Usually no formal qualifications are required, but on-the-job training is provided. Physical stamina and reliability are key.
