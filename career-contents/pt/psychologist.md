## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for psychologists in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level psychologists in Portugal earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €2,000–€3,000. Salaries vary by sector: public healthcare offers lower pay but stability, private practice yields higher earnings, and corporate roles can exceed €3,500. The national average gross annual salary is approximately €25,000–€30,000.

**Q: How can non-EU psychologists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU psychologists must secure a job offer from a Portuguese employer, who then applies for a work visa on their behalf. For the EU Blue Card, the job must require a relevant degree and pay at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary (approx. €37,500 in 2024). Both routes require recognition of qualifications by the Portuguese Order of Psychologists.

**Q: What are the main career paths for psychologists in Portugal?**
A: Psychologists in Portugal work in clinical (NHS or private practice), educational (schools), organizational (HR and corporate well-being), forensic (legal settings), and research/academia. Licensure by the Portuguese Order of Psychologists is mandatory. Demand is growing in mental health, though competition is high in urban centers like Lisbon and Porto.
