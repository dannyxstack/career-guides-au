## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a ships' engineer in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for ships' engineers in Portugal vary by rank and vessel type. Entry-level engineers earn around €1,800 gross per month, while experienced chief engineers can make up to €3,500. Many positions also include sea pay and benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU ships' engineers work in Portugal via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average). Alternatively, a regular work visa is available through employer sponsorship. Both require a valid STCW certificate.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a ships' engineer in Portugal?**
A: You need an STCW III/1 (Officer of the Watch) or III/2 (Chief Engineer) certificate, plus relevant sea service. Portuguese language skills are helpful but not mandatory. Job opportunities exist in merchant shipping, fishing, and support vessels operating from Portuguese ports.
