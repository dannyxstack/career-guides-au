## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a social work associate professional in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level social work associates earn around €1,200–€1,400 per month. With experience, monthly salaries can reach €1,600–€1,800. Public sector roles may include additional benefits like meal allowances and seniority increments.

**Q: Can foreign social work associate professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: Yes, a work visa requires a job offer and approval from AIMA. The EU Blue Card is possible if you hold a higher education degree and the salary meets the threshold (€1,580/month in 2025, but social work roles often fall below). For most, the standard work visa is the applicable route.

**Q: What career progression options exist for social work associate professionals in Portugal?**
A: After gaining experience, you can advance to team supervisor, specialize in areas like child protection or addiction services, or move into community project management. Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in social work opens pathways to registered social worker roles.
