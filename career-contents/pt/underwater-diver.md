- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.

- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for underwater divers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. Entry-level commercial divers earn around €1,200–€1,800 per month, while experienced offshore or scientific divers can earn €2,500–€4,000. Freelance rates may be higher but irregular. Many divers also receive additional pay for hazardous conditions or specialized skills.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for non-EU divers to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU divers need a work visa sponsored by a Portuguese employer. The EU Blue Card is an option for highly qualified workers, but diving roles rarely meet the required salary threshold (around €2,280/month in 2024) and degree requirement. A standard work visa (D3 for highly qualified or general employment) is more common, requiring a job contract and proof of qualifications.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as an underwater diver in Portugal?**
A: Divers must hold internationally recognized certifications, such as those from the International Diving Schools Association (IDSA) or equivalent. For commercial diving, the HSE (UK) or IMCA certificates are often preferred. Additionally, a Portuguese medical certificate of fitness for diving is mandatory. For scientific diving, a PhD or equivalency may be required.
