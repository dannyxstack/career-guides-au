## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Portugal?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for a window cleaner in Portugal ranges from €800 to €1,200, varying by experience and region. In major cities like Lisbon or Porto, wages may be higher. Many cleaners are paid per project or on an hourly basis, with rates around €7–€10 per hour.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU window cleaners to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU window cleaners typically apply for a regular work visa (visto de trabalho) rather than an EU Blue Card, as the latter requires higher education or high-skilled criteria. The employer must sponsor the visa and prove no suitable EU worker is available. Self-employment is also possible under a D2 visa for entrepreneurs.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for window cleaners in Portugal?**
A: Window cleaners can progress to supervisory roles, start their own cleaning business, or specialize in high-rise or industrial cleaning. Additional certifications in safety and equipment use (e.g., abseiling techniques) can lead to higher pay and contracts with commercial clients. Experience may also open doors to related fields like building maintenance.
