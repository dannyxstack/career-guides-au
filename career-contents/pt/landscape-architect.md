## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for landscape architects in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level landscape architects earn around €18,000–€25,000 annually. Mid-career professionals typically make €25,000–€40,000, while senior roles can exceed €50,000. Salaries vary by region, with Lisbon and Porto offering higher pay. Public sector roles may have lower but stable income.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU landscape architects to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, or a work visa under the Global Talent or D7 passive income visa if they meet specific criteria. All routes require a valid employment contract and compliance with Portuguese immigration law.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a landscape architect in Portugal?**
A: To practice as a landscape architect in Portugal, you typically need a degree in Landscape Architecture (licenciatura or mestrado) accredited by the Ordem dos Arquitectos. Registration with the professional body is mandatory for signing projects. Relevant experience and proficiency in Portuguese are also important.
