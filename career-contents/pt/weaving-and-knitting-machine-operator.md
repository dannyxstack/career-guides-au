## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Portugal?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Portugal is between €800 and €1,200, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions may start at the national minimum wage (€820 in 2024), while experienced operators can earn more. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as weaving and knitting machine operators in Portugal via the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a regular work visa (D1 Visa) for this occupation, which requires a job offer and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€20,628 annual in 2024), which is rarely met by weaving operators. The D3 Visa for highly qualified work also does not usually apply.

**Q: What training is required to become a weaving or knitting machine operator in Portugal?**
A: Formal education is not always required, but vocational training in textile manufacturing is beneficial. Programs are offered by professional schools (e.g., in Braga or Porto) and some IEFP courses. On-the-job training is common. Proficiency in machine operation, quality control, and basic maintenance are key skills.
