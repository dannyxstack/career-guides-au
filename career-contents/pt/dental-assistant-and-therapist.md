## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dental assistants and therapists in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, dental assistants typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month, while dental therapists earn €1,000 to €1,500. Wages vary by experience, location, and whether in public or private practice. Public sector salaries are generally lower but include benefits like job security.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for dental assistants and therapists to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa (Visto de Trabalho) for employment as a dental assistant or therapist. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals, requiring a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Applications are made through Portuguese consulates abroad.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a dental assistant or therapist in Portugal?**
A: Dental assistants need a vocational diploma in dental assisting. Dental therapists require a higher education degree in oral health, such as a licenciatura. Both qualifications must be recognized by the Ordem dos Médicos Dentistas. Proficiency in Portuguese is typically mandatory for registration and practice.
