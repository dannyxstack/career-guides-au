## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level positions generally earn between €25,000 and €35,000 annually. With experience, salaries can reach €50,000 to €70,000 for senior roles, especially in sectors like insurance, banking, and tech. Actuaries and statisticians in high-demand areas may earn premium rates. Public sector pay is typically lower. Overall, compensation aligns with the Portuguese cost of living.

**Q: Can mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Portugal?**
A: Yes, qualified professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (around €1,880/month in 2024). Alternatively, a regular work visa (Type D) requires a valid employment contract. Both routes allow family reunification and lead to permanent residence. The Portuguese 'Tech Visa' also facilitates hiring for tech roles.

**Q: What industries employ mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Portugal?**
A: Key employers include insurance companies (e.g., Fidelidade, Tranquilidade), banks (Caixa Geral, BPI), and consulting firms (Deloitte, PwC). The tech sector, especially in Lisbon and Porto, hires data scientists and statisticians for analytics roles. Research institutions and universities also offer positions. The Portuguese Statistical Institute (INE) employs many statisticians in the public sector.
