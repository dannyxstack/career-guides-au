## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a house builder in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for house builders in Portugal vary by experience and region. Entry-level workers earn around €1,000–€1,300 per month, while experienced builders can earn €1,500–€2,200. In Lisbon or the Algarve, wages may be higher. Overtime and specialized skills (e.g., bricklaying) can increase pay. Rates are often negotiated per project.

**Q: Can a non-EU house builder obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: Yes, non-EU house builders can apply for a Portuguese work visa through the Employment Attraction System (Sistema de Atração de Talentos). Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is for high-skilled workers with higher education or equivalent experience. House building (ISCO 7111) may qualify if the role meets the salary threshold (around €1,600/month) and contract requirements. Official routes include job offer sponsorship and criteria set by AIMA.

**Q: What are the typical tasks and skills required for a house builder in Portugal?**
A: House builders in Portugal construct and repair residential buildings. Tasks include reading blueprints, framing walls, laying foundations, roofing, and installing structures. Key skills include physical stamina, precision, and knowledge of local construction materials (e.g., concrete and brick). Certification in occupational safety (e.g., Técnico de Segurança) and experience with traditional Portuguese masonry are highly valued.
