## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for building caretakers in Portugal range from €800 to €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience, location, and additional responsibilities. The national minimum wage in 2024 is €820 per month. Some employers provide benefits such as reduced rent or accommodation.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a building caretaker in Portugal via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a regular work visa if they have a confirmed job offer from a Portuguese employer. The EU Blue Card requires a highly-skilled position with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (around €1,500+ per month), which this occupation typically does not meet. Thus, the standard work visa is the applicable route.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building caretaker in Portugal?**
A: Building caretakers in Portugal are responsible for cleaning common areas, minor repairs, managing waste, receiving mail, and ensuring building security. They may also oversee maintenance contracts, monitor heating or water systems, and assist residents with day-to-day needs. Experience can lead to roles in property management or facility supervision.
