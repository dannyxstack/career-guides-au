## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for building structure cleaners in Portugal average between €700 and €1,000 gross per month, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions often pay the national minimum wage (€760 in 2024). Additional benefits may include meal allowance and overtime pay.

**Q: What visa options exist for foreign building structure cleaners to work in Portugal?**
A: Foreign workers can obtain a work visa for employed professionals, requiring a valid job offer and proof of qualifications. The EU Blue Card is typically not applicable as it targets highly skilled workers with higher education and salary thresholds that cleaning roles rarely meet.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building structure cleaner in Portugal?**
A: Tasks include cleaning exterior surfaces like windows, facades, and roofs using cradles, ropes, or scaffolding. Workers often operate pressure washers or apply chemical cleaners, adhering to strict safety protocols. Physical fitness and comfort at heights are essential.
