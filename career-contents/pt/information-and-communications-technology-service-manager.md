## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Information and Communications Technology Services Manager in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for ICT Services Managers in Portugal typically range from €50,000 to €80,000 annually, depending on experience, company size, and location (Lisbon higher). Senior roles in multinationals can exceed €100,000. Data from sources like Teamlyzer and Glassdoor indicate median around €65,000.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for ICT Services Managers to work in Portugal from outside the EU?**
A: Non-EU ICT managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €2,000+ monthly) and meet qualification requirements. Alternatively, a work visa under the D1 category requires employer sponsorship and labor market testing. The D7 passive income visa does not apply.

**Q: What career prospects exist for ICT Services Managers in Portugal?**
A: Portugal's growing tech hub, with startups and multinationals like Google, Microsoft, and remote-friendly companies, offers strong demand. Career progression includes Director of IT, CTO, or consulting roles. The government's Tech Visa and Startup Portugal initiatives support talent. Networking via events like Web Summit and Lisbon's tech ecosystem aids advancement.
