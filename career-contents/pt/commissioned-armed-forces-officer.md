## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries follow military pay scales. Entry-level officers (Alferes) earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross monthly, while senior ranks like Major can earn €2,500–€3,000. Additional allowances for housing, hazard pay, and longevity increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Portugal via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card does not apply to military positions. Non-EU citizens generally require Portuguese nationality to become officers. Limited exceptions exist for citizens of Portuguese-speaking countries with bilateral agreements, who may apply for a residency visa for military service, but no standard work visa route exists.

**Q: How do Commissioned Armed Forces Officers progress through ranks in Portugal?**
A: Promotion is based on time in service, performance evaluations, and completion of mandatory training courses. Officers typically advance from Alferes to Tenente after 4–5 years, then to Capitão after 8–10 years. Higher ranks require competitive selection and staff college attendance.
