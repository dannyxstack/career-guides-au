## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for athletes and sports players in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary greatly by sport and level. In top-tier football, players can earn €1 million+ annually. Lower-tier athletes in less popular sports may earn €1,000–€3,000 per month. Many rely on sponsorships. The national minimum wage is €760/month.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for foreign athletes in Portugal?**
A: Foreign athletes can obtain a work visa under Portugal's Immigration Law (Lei 23/2007). For an EU Blue Card, they need a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average wage (approx. €1,300/month). High-performance athletes may qualify for a specific residence permit under sports activity regulations.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for athletes after their playing career in Portugal?**
A: Many transition into coaching, sports management, or physiotherapy. Portugal has growing sports science programs. Some become agents or work in sports media. Government initiatives support athlete retraining. The Portuguese Football Federation offers courses for former players.
