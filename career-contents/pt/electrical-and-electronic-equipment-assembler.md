## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in Portugal typically range from €900 to €1,400 per month, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start near the national minimum wage (€886 in 2024), while experienced assemblers can earn higher. Overtime and shift bonuses may apply in some manufacturing firms.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU worker to get a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a Portuguese work visa through a job offer from a Portuguese employer. The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Both routes require a valid work contract and compliance with immigration procedures.

**Q: What are the main duties and required qualifications for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Portugal?**
A: Assemblers typically read technical diagrams, use hand tools and soldering equipment to assemble components, test finished products, and maintain quality standards. A high school diploma or vocational training in electronics is often required. Employers may also value prior experience in manufacturing or assembly. Some positions offer on-the-job training.
