**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and vessel, but entry-level workers earn around the national minimum wage (€820/month in 2024). Experienced workers can earn €1,200–€2,000/month, often with bonuses based on catch. Payments may be per trip or monthly, with additional allowances for time at sea.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for deep-sea fishery workers in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a work visa via a Portuguese employer who proves a labour shortage. The EU Blue Card is possible for highly skilled workers, but deep-sea fishing roles typically do not meet the high-skill threshold. A temporary residence permit for employed activities (Autorização de Residência para Exercício de Atividade Profissional Subordinada) is the usual route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a deep-sea fishery worker in Portugal?**
A: Basic training includes a maritime safety course (STCW) and a seaman's book (Cédula Marítima). Practical experience on fishing vessels is valued. Some roles require navigation or fishing gear operation skills. Portuguese language proficiency is helpful but not always mandatory for foreign crew members.
