## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for other language teachers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for other language teachers in Portugal vary by institution and experience, ranging from €1,200 to €2,500 gross per month. Private language schools and international schools generally offer higher pay, while public schools follow a fixed salary scale. Part-time or hourly rates are common, often between €15 and €30 per hour.

**Q: Can non-EU language teachers work in Portugal via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU language teachers can work in Portugal through a work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary meeting the national threshold (updated annually) and a recognized qualification. Alternatively, a standard work visa is available for teaching positions, requiring employer sponsorship and compliance with immigration procedures.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a language teacher in Portugal?**
A: Requirements typically include a bachelor's degree in the target language or education, and a teaching certificate such as TEFL, CELTA, or equivalent. For public schools, Portuguese language proficiency (B2 level or higher) and recognition of foreign qualifications through the DGES are necessary. Private schools may accept native speakers with relevant experience.
