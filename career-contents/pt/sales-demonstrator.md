**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeated on-site demonstrations of product standard functions
- Using pre-recorded videos or AI-generated content to replace on-site explanations
- Frequently asked customer questions; replaced by chatbots
- Product parameter query and comparison, replaced by self-service terminals or AR applications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer data to assist in customizing personalized presentation scripts
- Real-time speech transcription and translation, supporting multilingual audiences
- AR/VR technology enhances the immersion and interactivity of product displays
- Sales data dashboard helps presenters adjust strategies in real time

**Human moat:**

- Soft skills for building emotional trust and client relationships
- Creativity and narrative skills in complex product experiences
- Flexible adaptation for cross-cultural and non-standard scenarios

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for Sales Demonstrators in Portugal generally align with the national minimum wage or slightly above, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start at around €760 per month (2024 minimum wage), while experienced demonstrators can earn up to €1,200 per month. Commissions or bonuses may supplement base pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Sales Demonstrators in Portugal through the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals can obtain a regular work visa sponsored by a Portuguese employer. Alternatively, if they hold a higher education degree and meet the applicable salary threshold, they may qualify for the EU Blue Card. Official routes include the regular work visa (for job offers) and the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers). Requirements and thresholds are set by Portuguese immigration authorities.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Sales Demonstrators in Portugal?**
A: Sales Demonstrators can advance to roles such as sales supervisor, brand ambassador, or retail manager. Additional training in marketing or product management can lead to corporate positions. Proficiency in Portuguese and English, along with strong communication skills, significantly enhance career growth. Some may transition into event coordination or wholesale representation.
