## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shop sales assistants in Portugal?**
A: The typical salary for shop sales assistants in Portugal ranges from €800 to €1,100 per month, depending on experience and location. This includes mandatory bonuses like holiday and Christmas subsidies. The national minimum wage is €760 per month in 2024, so most sales assistants earn slightly above that.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as shop sales assistants in Portugal via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a Portuguese work visa (D1) through a job offer from a local employer. The EU Blue Card is only available for highly-skilled roles with a salary threshold of around €1,500/month, which typically excludes shop sales assistants. Thus, the standard work visa is the relevant route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop sales assistants in Portugal?**
A: Experienced shop sales assistants can advance to senior sales roles, team leader, or store supervisor positions. With additional training, they may move into visual merchandising, inventory management, or retail management. Many large retail chains offer internal promotion programs, and fluency in Portuguese is essential for progression.
