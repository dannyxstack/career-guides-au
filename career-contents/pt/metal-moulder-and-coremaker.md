**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation software assists in predicting casting shrinkage and porosity locations, guiding process improvements.

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for metal moulders and coremakers in Portugal?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for metal moulders and coremakers in Portugal ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and employer. Overtime and shift work can increase earnings. Benefits often include meal allowance and transport subsidies. Salaries are generally aligned with collective bargaining agreements in the metalworking sector.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for non-EU metal moulders to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU metal moulders can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (set annually, typically around €1,500 gross monthly). Alternatively, a General Work Visa requires the employer to obtain a work permit from IEFP and SEF, including a labor market test to prove no suitable EU candidate. Both routes require a valid employment contract.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for metal moulders in Portugal?**
A: Experienced metal moulders can advance to supervisor roles, become master moulders, or specialize in CNC programming and 3D printing for pattern making. Additional training through IEFP or vocational schools can lead to certifications in foundry technology. Some professionals start their own small foundries or consulting businesses.
