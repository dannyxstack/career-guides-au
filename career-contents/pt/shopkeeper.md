## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shopkeeper in Portugal?**
A: The average monthly salary for a shopkeeper in Portugal ranges from €900 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and type of shop. Many shopkeepers are self-employed, so income can vary significantly. In Lisbon or Porto, salaries may be higher, but cost of living also increases.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for a non-EU national to work as a shopkeeper in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as shopkeepers via the D2 Visa for entrepreneurs or the EU Blue Card if employed by a company. The D2 requires a viable business plan, while the Blue Card demands a job offer with salary above the national average. Self-employed shopkeepers may also apply for a residence permit for independent work.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a shopkeeper in Portugal?**
A: Shopkeepers manage daily retail operations, including customer service, inventory management, sales transactions, and store cleanliness. They may also handle bookkeeping, supplier relations, and staff supervision. In small shops, they often perform all tasks themselves. Knowledge of Portuguese is essential for customer interaction.
