## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Administrative and Executive Secretaries in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, industry, and location. Entry-level administrative secretaries earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month gross, while experienced executive secretaries can earn €2,000–€2,500 per month. Larger companies and Lisbon/Porto areas tend to offer higher pay. Benefits often include meal allowance and transport subsidies.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Administrative and Executive Secretaries to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a job offer to apply for a Residence Visa for Subordinate Work or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified roles. The Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold and recognized qualifications. Both routes mandate a valid employment contract, work authorization, and compliance with immigration procedures. Self-employment visas are also possible for freelancers.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities and career progression for Executive Secretaries in Portugal?**
A: Executive Secretaries manage schedules, correspondence, and office administration for senior executives. They often handle confidential information, coordinate meetings, and support project management. Career progression can lead to roles such as office manager, executive assistant to C-level executives, or specialized administrative positions. Fluency in English and Portuguese is highly valued, and digital literacy is essential.
