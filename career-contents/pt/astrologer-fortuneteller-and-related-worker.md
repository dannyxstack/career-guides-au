## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Portugal?**
A: Income varies significantly as most work self-employed or part-time. Official salary data is not available, but typical hourly rates range from €10 to €30. Full-time practitioners may earn between €15,000 and €25,000 annually, though many supplement with related services like tarot readings or event appearances.

**Q: Can astrologers and fortune-tellers get a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled positions requiring a university degree or equivalent higher education, which may not apply to this occupation. However, non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa if they have a job offer or a D2 visa for self-employment, provided they present a viable business plan and meet financial requirements.

**Q: How do astrologers typically practice in Portugal?**
A: Most are self-employed, offering consultations online or in person. Many also work at festivals, private events, and holistic centers. Some combine it with other esoteric services. There is no formal regulation, but practitioners often build reputation through word-of-mouth and social media.
