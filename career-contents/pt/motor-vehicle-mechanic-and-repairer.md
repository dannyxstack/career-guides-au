## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, the average gross monthly salary for a motor vehicle mechanic ranges from €1,200 to €1,500. Experienced mechanics or those specializing in advanced diagnostics can earn up to €2,000. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Lisbon and Porto. Overtime and bonuses may increase total earnings.

**Q: Can a foreign motor vehicle mechanic work in Portugal via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU mechanics can apply for a work visa with a valid job offer. The EU Blue Card is also available for highly skilled workers, requiring a job offer meeting Portugal’s salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Applicants need recognized qualifications and a work contract. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a motor vehicle mechanic in Portugal?**
A: Typically, a vocational qualification (e.g., secondary-level technical course) or a professional certificate in automotive mechanics is required. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Portuguese Ministry of Education. On-the-job training or apprenticeships are common. Certification from manufacturers (e.g., Bosch, Mercedes) can enhance career prospects.
