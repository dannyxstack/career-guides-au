## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a valuer or loss assessor in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level valuers earn around €18,000–€25,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €30,000–€45,000. Senior roles in insurance or specialized property valuation may reach €50,000 or more. Salaries vary by region, employer, and qualifications.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign valuers and loss assessors to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU valuers can apply for a work visa or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles). The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €22,000/year in 2024). Both routes need employer sponsorship, a valid contract, and recognition of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a valuer or loss assessor in Portugal?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in fields like real estate, economics, or civil engineering is required. Professional certification from the Ordem dos Avaliadores (Order of Valuers) is mandatory for certain types of valuation. Loss assessors often need additional insurance sector experience and registrations.
