## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for kitchen helpers in Portugal?**
A: As of 2025, kitchen helpers in Portugal typically earn the national minimum wage, around €870 per month (14 payments per year). Some establishments offer slightly higher pay depending on experience and location, especially in tourist-heavy areas like Lisbon or the Algarve. Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU kitchen helpers get a work visa for Portugal?**
A: Non-EU kitchen helpers can obtain a work visa if a Portuguese employer sponsors them. The process requires a job offer, labor market test (proving no EU candidate available), and application at the Portuguese consulate. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher-skilled role and salary threshold. Instead, apply under the general work visa regime.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a kitchen helper in Portugal?**
A: No formal qualifications are mandatory, but employers prefer basic food hygiene certification (e.g., HACCP). On-the-job training is common. Knowledge of Portuguese is helpful for communication. Kitchen helpers may advance to cook or chef with experience and additional training.
