## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a ships' deck officer in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for deck officers in Portugal vary by experience and rank. A junior deck officer typically earns €2,000–€3,000 per month, while a chief officer or captain may earn €4,000–€7,000 monthly. These figures are gross and can be higher for deep-sea vessels or specialized roles. Seafarer pay is often tax-free for time spent at sea.

**Q: Can a non-EU deck officer work in Portugal via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU deck officers can work in Portugal through the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree (e.g., a Bachelor's in nautical sciences) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. €1,568/month in 2024). Alternatively, a regular work visa (residence permit for dependent employment) is available for seafarers with a valid contract and recognized qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a deck officer in Portugal?**
A: To become a deck officer in Portugal, you need a degree from a recognized maritime school (e.g., Escola Superior Náutica Infante D. Henrique) and STCW certification. After obtaining a Deck Officer Class III license (Watchkeeping Officer), you must complete sea service and exams to progress to Class II (Chief Officer) and Class I (Master). Portuguese is the language of instruction, but English is essential for international voyages.
