## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for garment patternmakers and cutters in Portugal typically range from €800 to €1,200 per month, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage, while experienced professionals in higher-cost areas like Lisbon or Porto can earn towards the upper end. Overtime and bonuses may add to total compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU garment patternmakers and cutters to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU garment patternmakers and cutters can work in Portugal via a standard work visa (applied through a Portuguese consulate with a job offer) or the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher-skilled job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds (currently around €1,040/month). Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with immigration regulations.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a garment patternmaker or cutter in Portugal?**
A: Typically, a vocational diploma in pattern making or tailoring is required, often obtained through professional schools or institutes like CENFIM. Practical experience and on-the-job training are also valued. Proficiency in using computer-aided design (CAD) software for patternmaking is increasingly important. Knowledge of fabrics and garment construction is essential.
