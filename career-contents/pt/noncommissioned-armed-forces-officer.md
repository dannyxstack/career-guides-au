## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Portugal?**
A: Salaries are determined by military pay scales based on rank and seniority. For sergeants (1st class), starting gross monthly pay is around €1,200-€1,500, increasing with promotions and years of service. Benefits include allowances for housing, meals, and risk, plus social security and pension contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Portugal via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled civilian professions. Military roles are generally restricted to Portuguese or EU citizens. Non-EU citizens may join only under specific bilateral agreements or as professional soldiers if they hold a residence permit; the EU Blue Card does not apply.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Portugal?**
A: Non-commissioned officers can advance through ranks from sergeant to sergeant-major, warrant officer, and eventually to officer status via internal exams. Promotions are based on time in grade, performance evaluations, and completion of specialized courses. Opportunities also exist for leadership roles and technical specialties.
