## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Portugal?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers in Portugal typically earn between €700 and €1,000 per month, depending on experience and region. The national minimum wage is €760 as of 2024. Seasonal or temporary positions may offer slightly higher rates. Overtime or additional skills, such as pesticide handling, can increase pay.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as garden and horticultural labourers in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa (Autorização de Trabalho) for paid employment. The EU Blue Card is typically not applicable as it requires higher education qualifications. The CPLP mobility agreement allows easier access for citizens of Portuguese-speaking countries. Seasonal work visas may also be available for temporary horticultural labor.

**Q: What are the main duties of garden and horticultural labourers in Portugal?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers in Portugal perform tasks such as planting, watering, weeding, pruning, and harvesting. They also prepare soil, apply fertilizers and pesticides, operate gardening tools, and maintain green spaces. Work may be in public parks, private gardens, nurseries, or agricultural settings, often requiring physical stamina.
