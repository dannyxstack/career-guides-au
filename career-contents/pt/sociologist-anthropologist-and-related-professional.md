## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sociologists and anthropologists in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. In academia, entry-level researchers earn around €1,200-€1,800 per month. In the private sector, such as market research or consulting, salaries range from €1,500 to €2,500 monthly. Senior roles in public administration or international organizations can exceed €3,000 per month. Source: data from national statistics and job portals.

**Q: What visa routes allow sociologists or anthropologists to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified jobs with salary above €2,000/month) or a D1 work visa (with a job offer from a Portuguese employer). Free-movement rights apply to EU/EEA/Swiss citizens. The D7 passive income visa is for remote workers or retirees, not typical for direct employment.

**Q: What are the main employers for sociologists and anthropologists in Portugal?**
A: Key employers include universities (e.g., University of Lisbon, University of Coimbra), research centers (CIES-ISCTE, ICS-ULisboa), public agencies (INE, Câmaras Municipais), and NGOs. Private sector roles exist in market research firms (Nielsen, Kantar) and consulting companies. International organizations like the UN or European Commission also hire for policy-related positions.
