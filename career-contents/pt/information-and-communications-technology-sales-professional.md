## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an ICT Sales Professional in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for ICT Sales Professionals in Portugal vary based on experience and company size. Entry-level positions typically earn €20,000–€30,000 annually, while mid-level roles range from €35,000–€55,000. Senior or high-performing professionals can earn €60,000–€80,000 or more, often with commission. Lisbon and Porto offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU ICT Sales Professional to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card with a job offer meeting salary thresholds (at least 1.5× average gross salary). Alternatively, a work visa (D1) requires a Portuguese employment contract. The D7 passive income visa is not suitable for employment. All applications are processed through the Portuguese consulate or SEF.

**Q: Which skills are most in demand for ICT Sales Professionals in Portugal?**
A: Employers seek fluency in English and Portuguese, technical knowledge of SaaS or cloud solutions, and experience with CRM tools like Salesforce. Strong communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills are critical. Familiarity with the Portuguese tech ecosystem and European markets is a plus.
