## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, farm labourers generally earn the national minimum wage, which in 2024 is €820 per month (paid 14 times a year). Actual wages may vary by region and employer, ranging between €820 and €1,000 per month. Overtime and additional benefits are uncommon. Salaries are typically lower in the Alentejo and higher in the Algarve during peak seasons.

**Q: How can a non-EU national obtain a work visa to be a farm labourer in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer from a Portuguese employer. They must then apply for a Residence Visa for Employed Workers (D1) at a Portuguese consulate in their home country. This visa allows entry and subsequent application for a residence permit. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation as it requires higher qualifications or a higher salary threshold.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions and duties for this role in Portugal?**
A: Mixed crop and livestock farm labourers perform tasks such as planting, harvesting, feeding animals, and maintaining equipment. Work is often seasonal and outdoors, requiring physical stamina. Hours vary widely, often starting early. In Portugal, many farms are family-owned, and seasonal contracts (3–9 months) are common. Housing may be provided on some farms.
