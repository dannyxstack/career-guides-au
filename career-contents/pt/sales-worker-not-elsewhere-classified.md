## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by sector and commission structure. The national minimum wage is about €760 per month (as of 2024). Entry-level sales workers may earn €800–€1,200 monthly, while experienced professionals with commissions can reach €1,500–€2,000+ per month. Benefits often include meal allowance and performance bonuses.

**Q: What work visa routes exist for foreign sales workers to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU sales workers can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a gross annual salary of at least 1.5 times the national average (approximately €20,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a work visa (subtype D1) requires a labor market test and a valid employment contract. Highly qualified professionals may also use the D3 visa.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for sales roles in Portugal?**
A: Most employers require at least secondary education and relevant sales experience. Fluency in Portuguese is essential; English is a plus for international positions. Some roles may need specific product knowledge or a vocational degree in sales. Strong communication, negotiation, and customer service skills are key for career advancement.
