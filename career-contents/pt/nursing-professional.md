## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for nursing professionals in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for nursing professionals in Portugal vary by sector and experience. In the public sector, entry-level nurses earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month, while senior nurses can reach €2,000. Private hospitals may offer slightly higher pay, but overall, nursing salaries in Portugal are lower than the EU average, with an annual gross range of €18,000–€30,000.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU nurses to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nurses must first have their qualifications recognized by the Ordem dos Enfermeiros and obtain a Portuguese residence visa for work. For the EU Blue Card, they need a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (no specific figure stated). Alternatively, a regular work visa is available through a Portuguese employer, requiring a labor market test and a work contract.

**Q: What are the main professional requirements for nursing in Portugal?**
A: Nurses in Portugal must hold a recognized nursing degree, register with the Ordem dos Enfermeiros, and comply with continuing education requirements. Fluency in Portuguese is typically required for clinical practice. The profession is regulated, and recognition of foreign qualifications is mandatory, which may involve additional training or exams for non-EU applicants.
