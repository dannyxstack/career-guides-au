## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for specialist medical practitioners in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for specialist medical practitioners in Portugal vary by sector and experience. In the public National Health Service (SNS), gross monthly salaries range from €3,000 to €5,500. Private practice can yield higher earnings, often exceeding €7,000 per month. Income depends on specialty, location, and hours worked.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign specialist doctors to practice in Portugal?**
A: Foreign specialist doctors can apply for an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) if they have a recognized medical degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a work visa (D1) for subordinated employment is possible. Both require prior registration with the Ordem dos Médicos (Portuguese Medical Council).

**Q: How can foreign-trained specialists register with the Portuguese Medical Council (Ordem dos Médicos)?**
A: Foreign-trained doctors must have their medical degree recognized by a Portuguese university. After recognition, they apply for registration with the Ordem dos Médicos, providing proof of qualifications, language proficiency (Portuguese B2 or higher), and professional experience. The process may include an assessment or adaptation period. Registration is mandatory to practice legally.
