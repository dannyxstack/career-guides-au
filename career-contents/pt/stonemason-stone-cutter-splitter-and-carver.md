## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Portugal?**
A: The average monthly salary for stonemasons in Portugal ranges from €1,200 to €1,800 gross, depending on experience and region. Lisbon and the Algarve typically offer higher wages due to demand in construction and restoration. Experienced carvers can earn up to €2,200, and overtime pay or meal allowances are common.

**Q: How can non-EU stonemasons obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: Non-EU stonemasons may qualify for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer meeting at least 1.5 times the national average salary. Alternatively, the D1 Work Visa is available with a valid work contract and recognized professional qualifications. Applications are processed by SEF (Serviço de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras).

**Q: What training is required to become a stonemason in Portugal?**
A: Formal training can be obtained through vocational courses at IEFP or trade schools. Many stonemasons learn through apprenticeships with experienced professionals. While no national license is mandatory, IEFP certification enhances job prospects. Specialized restoration skills may require additional courses offered by cultural heritage institutions.
