## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for accountants in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level accountants earn around €1,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €2,500 or more. Senior roles or those with certifications may command higher pay. Salaries vary by region, with Lisbon and Porto generally offering higher compensation.

**Q: Can foreign accountants obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: Yes. Non-EU accountants can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (typically 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary). A work visa is also possible through an employer-sponsored residence permit. Recognition of professional qualifications by the Ordem dos Contabilistas Certificados may be required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as an accountant in Portugal?**
A: To work as a certified accountant, you must register with the Ordem dos Contabilistas Certificados (OCC). Requirements include a degree in accounting or related field, completion of a traineeship, and passing an exam. For non-regulated roles, a degree may suffice, but certification enhances career prospects.
