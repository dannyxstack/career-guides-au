## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a community health worker in Portugal?**
A: The average salary for community health workers in Portugal ranges from €1,100 to €1,500 per month gross, depending on experience, employer (public vs. private sector), and region. Public sector workers may earn slightly more with benefits. Entry-level positions start around €950, while senior roles can reach €1,800. Salaries are regulated by collective bargaining agreements in health sectors.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for community health workers to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU community health workers typically need a work visa sponsored by a Portuguese employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers (salary must exceed ~1.5x the average national wage, around €1,580/month in 2023). Alternatives include the job-seeking visa (valid for 120 days) or the CPLP visa for citizens of Portuguese-speaking countries. All routes require a valid job offer, qualifications recognition, and clean criminal record.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a community health worker in Portugal?**
A: Community health workers in Portugal typically need a secondary education diploma (12th grade) and a certified training course in community health or social support (e.g., Técnico de Apoio Familiar e Apoio à Comunidade). Some employers prefer a bachelor's in nursing, social work, or public health. Registration with the relevant professional order (e.g., Ordem dos Enfermeiros for nursing-related roles) may be required. Fluency in Portuguese is essential.
