## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by sector. In academia, entry-level researchers earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month, while senior professors can earn €2,500–€4,000. In public administration or think tanks, salaries range from €1,500 to €2,500. Private sector roles like policy analysis may offer €1,800–€3,000. Gross annual earnings typically range from €18,000 to €48,000.

**Q: What work-visa or EU Blue Card options exist for philosophers, historians, and political scientists to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with salary at least 1.5x the national average (approx. €33,000/year) and a recognized degree. Alternatively, the D1 Work Visa is for skilled workers with a work contract. Both require employer sponsorship. Self-employed individuals may seek a D2 Visa with a viable business plan.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Portugal?**
A: Common employers include universities, research institutes, museums, archives, government agencies, NGOs, and media. Political scientists often work in policy analysis, consultancy, or international organizations. Historians find roles in cultural heritage, education, or publishing. Philosophers typically pursue academia or ethics advisory roles. Networking and fluency in Portuguese are advantageous.
