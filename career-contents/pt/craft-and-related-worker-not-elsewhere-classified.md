## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Craft and Related Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary widely by skill and sector. Generally, monthly gross earnings range from €800 to €1,500. Entry-level positions may start around the national minimum wage (€820 in 2025), while experienced workers in specialized fields can earn up to €2,000. Exact figures depend on employer, region, and collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as a craft worker in Portugal through visa or EU Blue Card routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work visa sponsored by a Portuguese employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €2,400/month in 2025), which may exceed typical craft worker pay. Most craft workers instead apply for a D1 (work) or D2 (independent professional) visa through the Portuguese consulate, requiring a valid job offer.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed for craft occupations in Portugal?**
A: Many craft roles require vocational training, such as a professional course (ensino profissional) or apprenticeship. The Sistema Nacional de Qualificações offers Certificados de Qualificação (EQF levels 2-4). Some regulated trades (e.g., electricians, construction) need formal certification. Experience and on-the-job training are also valued. Further specialization can lead to supervisory or self-employment opportunities.
