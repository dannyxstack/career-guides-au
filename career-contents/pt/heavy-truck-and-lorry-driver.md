**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Long-distance highway driving (autonomous truck pilot programs)
- Basic route planning and GPS navigation adjustments
- Basic vehicle status monitoring and routine inspection recording
- Standardized cargo handling (integration with automated warehousing)
- Fatigue driving risk detection (AI takeover warning)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Dynamic route optimization (real-time traffic, weather, queue times)

**Human moat:**

- Customer relationships and non-standard delivery requirements
- Rapid decision-making in emergencies (accidents, failures)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a heavy truck driver in Portugal?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for heavy truck drivers in Portugal ranges from €1,100 to €1,500, depending on experience, company, and type of transport. Senior drivers or those handling hazardous materials can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings. Salaries are often supplemented by meal allowances and travel expenses.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as heavy truck drivers in Portugal via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals generally require a work visa sponsored by a Portuguese employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree; heavy truck driving usually does not qualify. However, if the occupation is on Portugal's shortage list, a simplified work visa may apply. Always verify current requirements with the Portuguese immigration authority (SEF).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a heavy truck driver in Portugal?**
A: You need a valid category C or CE driving license, a Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC) for drivers, and a medical certificate. For international transport, a digital tachograph card is required. Experience and additional training for hazardous materials (ADR) are beneficial. The license is obtained after theoretical and practical exams.
