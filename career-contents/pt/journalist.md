## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a journalist in Portugal?**
A: The average salary for a journalist in Portugal ranges from €1,200 to €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, media type, and location. Entry-level journalists typically earn around €1,000-€1,500, while senior positions may exceed €3,000. Freelancers often have variable income.

**Q: Can a non-EU journalist work in Portugal via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, journalists from non-EU countries can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer and meet the salary threshold (currently about €28,000/year). Alternatively, the D3 Visa for Highly Qualified Professionals is available for those with a degree and work contract. Both routes require employer sponsorship and recognition of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a journalist in Portugal?**
A: A degree in journalism, communication, or a related field is typical. Proficiency in Portuguese is essential, as most media outlets operate in Portuguese. Membership in the Portuguese Journalists' Union (SJ) is common but not mandatory. Relevant internships or experience are highly valued.
