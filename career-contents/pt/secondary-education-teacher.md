## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for secondary education teachers in Portugal?**
A: Starting salaries for secondary teachers in public schools range from €1,200 to €1,500 gross per month, according to the Directorate-General for Administration and Public Employment. Pay increases with seniority and performance. Private school salaries vary.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU teacher to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU teachers can obtain an EU Blue Card for highly qualified jobs or a work visa (e.g., D3 visa for regulated professions). Both require a valid job offer and recognition of teaching qualifications by the Portuguese Ministry of Education.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for secondary teachers in Portugal?**
A: Teachers can advance through seniority and performance evaluations, moving up pay scales. Opportunities include becoming head teachers, school coordinators, or administrative roles. Continuing professional development is mandatory for progression.
