## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Portugal?**
A: Salaries vary by modality and experience. In private practice, earnings typically range from €1,200 to €2,500 per month. Public sector positions, if available, follow national pay scales for technical roles, roughly €1,100–€1,800. Self-employed professionals may earn more but have variable income.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals need a job offer and a valid employment contract. For highly qualified roles, the EU Blue Card requires a recognized qualification and salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, the D1 visa for independent work or the D3 visa for qualified professionals are possible, subject to professional recognition by Portuguese authorities.

**Q: What career paths are available for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Portugal?**
A: Many work in private clinics, wellness centers, or run their own practice. Some are employed in hospitals or health spas. Opportunities exist in research or training if combined with formal education. The field is regulated, requiring registration with professional associations for modalities like acupuncture or osteopathy.
