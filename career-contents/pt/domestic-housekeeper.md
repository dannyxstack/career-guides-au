## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a domestic housekeeper in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, domestic housekeepers typically earn between €700 and €1,100 per month, depending on experience, region, and whether the role is full-time or part-time. The national minimum wage is €820 per month (2024). Live-in positions may include accommodation and meals as part of compensation.

**Q: Can domestic housekeepers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU domestic housekeepers can apply for a Portuguese work visa under the general regime for employed workers, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of need. The EU Blue Card is designed for highly-skilled workers, so it generally does not apply to domestic housekeeping roles. Seasonal work visas may also be an option for temporary positions.

**Q: What are the main duties of a domestic housekeeper in Portugal?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Portugal perform cleaning, laundering, ironing, and tidying. They may also cook, run errands, and care for pets or gardens, particularly in live-in roles. Duties vary by employer contract. Experience or certification in cleaning, hospitality, or childcare can enhance job prospects.
