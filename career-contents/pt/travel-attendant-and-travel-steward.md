## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a travel attendant in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level travel attendants in Portugal earn around €900 to €1,200 gross per month, while experienced attendants can earn up to €1,500. Salaries often include flight allowances and per diems. Major airlines like TAP may offer higher base pay, but wages vary by company and contract type.

**Q: Can non-EU travel attendants obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Portugal?**
A: Non-EU travel attendants can apply for a Portuguese work visa (residence visa for employment) with a valid job offer. The EU Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€27,000+ annually), which is typically above travel attendant wages, making the standard work visa the more common route.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for travel attendants in Portugal?**
A: Travel attendants can advance to senior cabin crew, purser, or training instructor roles. Some transition into ground operations or airline management. Bilingual skills and seniority often determine promotion. The role offers limited vertical mobility but opportunities to shift into customer service or tourism sectors.
