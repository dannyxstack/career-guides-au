## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a motorcycle driver in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level motorcycle drivers often earn the national minimum wage (€820 per month in 2024). With experience, salaries can reach €1,200–€1,500 monthly, especially in Lisbon or Porto. Many drivers work per delivery, boosting earnings through volume. Benefits may include meal allowances and tips.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign motorcycle drivers to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Regular Stay Work Visa with a valid job contract approved by the Portuguese labor authority (ACT). The EU Blue Card is also an option, requiring a binding job offer and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (≈€22,000 in 2024). Both lead to a residence permit.

**Q: What are the main duties and requirements for motorcycle drivers in Portugal?**
A: Motorcycle drivers deliver goods like food or documents within urban areas. Requirements include a valid motorcycle license (category A or A2), knowledge of local routes, a smartphone for GPS, and a clean driving record. Employers often prefer experience. The job demands physical stamina and flexibility in work hours.
