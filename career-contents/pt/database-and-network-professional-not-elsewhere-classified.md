## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Database and Network Professionals (NEC) in Portugal?**
A: Salaries typically range from €30,000 to €55,000 annually for mid-level roles, depending on experience, certifications, and location. Senior professionals in Lisbon or Porto may earn €60,000–€80,000. Entry-level positions start around €20,000. Specializations like cloud architecture or cybersecurity can command higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU database and network professionals to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles with a job offer and salary above €28,000/year) or a D1 Work Visa (for regular employment). The D7 Passive Income Visa is for remote workers. All routes require a valid job contract and registration with SEF (Serviço de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras).

**Q: Which industries hire Database and Network Professionals in Portugal?**
A: Key sectors include technology (startups, software companies), finance (banks, fintech), telecommunications, and consulting. Major employers are NOS, Altice, EDP, and multinational tech hubs like Google, Microsoft, and Farfetch. The growing data center and cloud services market also offers opportunities in network infrastructure and database management.
