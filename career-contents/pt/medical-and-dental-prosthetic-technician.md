## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)

- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians in Portugal?**
A: Salaries for Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians in Portugal typically range from €18,000 to €30,000 gross annually, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €14,000, while senior technicians in specialized clinics or labs may earn up to €35,000. Overtime and private-sector work can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU technicians can apply for a work visa (D1) with a job offer from a Portuguese employer, or for the EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (approx. €21,000/year). The employer must prove no suitable EU candidate is available. Self-employed individuals may qualify for a D2 visa. All routes require recognition of qualifications and a residence permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Medical or Dental Prosthetic Technician in Portugal?**
A: A professional diploma or degree in dental prosthetics or medical orthotics is required. The qualification must be recognized by the Portuguese Health Ministry or professional bodies like the Ordem dos Médicos Dentistas. Additional certification for specific prosthetic types may be needed. Experience gained through apprenticeship or supervised practice is highly valued.
