## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Portugal?**
A: The typical monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Portugal ranges from the national minimum wage (€760 in 2024) to around €900, depending on experience and farm type. Overtime and benefits like accommodation may be additional. Salaries can vary by region, with higher pay in areas with intensive livestock production.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a livestock farm labourer in Portugal via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and typically does not apply to livestock farm labourers. Instead, non-EU nationals can apply for a Portuguese work visa if they have a job offer from a Portuguese employer. The employer must prove the position could not be filled by an EU worker. After five years of legal residence, permanent residency may be possible.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a livestock farm labourer in Portugal?**
A: Livestock farm labourers in Portugal handle daily care of animals such as cattle, pigs, sheep, or poultry. Duties include feeding, watering, cleaning enclosures, monitoring animal health, assisting with births, and maintaining farm equipment. They may also milk cows or collect eggs. Physical stamina and comfort working outdoors are essential.
