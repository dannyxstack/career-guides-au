**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Portugal?**
A: The typical salary for a shelf filler in Portugal is close to the national minimum wage, set at €820 per month in 2024, paid 14 times a year (including holiday and Christmas bonuses). Some employers offer slightly higher wages based on experience, location, or store size, but most entry-level positions start at the minimum wage.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU citizen to work as a shelf filler in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as a shelf filler via a Residence Visa for Employment, requiring a job offer and proof that the employer couldn't find a suitable EU candidate. The EU Blue Card route is generally not applicable for this occupation because it requires a higher salary threshold and a degree or equivalent, which shelf filler positions rarely meet.

**Q: What are the main duties and career advancement opportunities for a shelf filler in Portugal?**
A: Main duties include restocking shelves, rotating products to ensure freshness, cleaning aisles, and assisting customers. Advancement opportunities may include moving to team leader, inventory specialist, or department manager roles. Some large retail chains offer internal training programs to help employees progress into supervisory positions.
