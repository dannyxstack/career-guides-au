## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, pet groomers and animal care workers typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. The national minimum wage in 2024 is €887 per month. Self-employed groomers may have variable income.

**Q: Can pet groomers and animal care workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Portugal?**
A: Yes, through the Residence Visa for Work, which requires a job offer. The EU Blue Card is also an option for highly skilled roles meeting a salary threshold (typically over €1,500/month), but most pet grooming positions are medium-skilled, making the standard work visa more common.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a pet groomer in Portugal?**
A: Formal qualifications are not legally required, but certification from recognized grooming schools (e.g., International Professional Groomers) or courses in animal care improve job prospects. Employers often seek practical experience and knowledge of hygiene, handling, and first aid.
