## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for manufacturing labourers in Portugal?**
A: As of 2024, manufacturing labourers in Portugal generally earn around the national minimum wage of €820 per month for full-time work. Actual pay may vary by region, industry, and employer, with some offering additional benefits or overtime. Average salaries often range between €820 and €1,000 monthly.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as manufacturing labourers in Portugal via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a regular work visa, not the EU Blue Card, as this occupation is not considered high-skilled. The employer must obtain a work permit from IEFP, proving no suitable local worker is available. After a job offer, the worker applies for a visa at a Portuguese consulate.

**Q: What are typical working conditions and career paths for manufacturing labourers in Portugal?**
A: Work often involves repetitive tasks in factories, such as assembly or packaging, with shifts including nights or weekends. Career progression may include roles like team leader or machine operator with experience and training. Common sectors include automotive, electronics, and textiles, offering moderate job stability.
