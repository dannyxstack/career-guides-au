## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Portugal?**
A: In Portugal, car, taxi, and van drivers earn an average monthly gross salary between €1,000 and €1,400. Taxi drivers' income can vary significantly based on trips and tips, while self-employed drivers may earn more after expenses. Regional differences exist, with Lisbon and Porto typically offering higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EU drivers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a driver in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa (subtype 1) to drive in Portugal. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals requiring a university degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average—conditions typically not met by drivers. The standard work visa is the applicable route.

**Q: What are the requirements and career prospects for car, taxi, and van drivers in Portugal?**
A: Drivers need a valid category B driver's license (for cars/light vans) and, for taxis, a special municipal license. Experience and knowledge of local routes are advantageous. Career advancement includes owning a vehicle fleet or working for premium services like executive chauffeur or ride-hailing platforms.
