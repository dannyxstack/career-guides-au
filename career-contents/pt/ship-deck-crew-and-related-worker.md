## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships’ deck crew in Portugal?**
A: Entry-level deckhands earn around €1,000–€1,200 gross per month. Experienced able seamen can earn €1,500–€2,000. Salaries vary by vessel type, employer, and contract terms. Overtime and sea allowances may increase total compensation. Crew on large commercial ships may earn more than those on fishing or small vessels.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign deck crew to work in Portugal?**
A: Non-EU deck crew must obtain a Portuguese work visa (Autorização de Residência para Exercício de Atividade Profissional Subordinada) requiring a job offer and labor market clearance. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable due to skill level requirements, but the D2 visa for independent workers may suit self-employed seafarers. Employers often sponsor work visas.

**Q: What are common career paths for deck crew in Portugal?**
A: Deck crew can progress from deckhand to able seaman, then to bosun, and eventually to officer roles with further training. Many attend maritime schools for certifications. Fishing and merchant marine sectors offer distinct pathways. Experience on larger vessels or specialized ships can lead to higher ranks and better pay.
