## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for building architects in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level building architects in Indonesia earn around IDR 5-8 million monthly, while experienced professionals with 5-10 years can earn IDR 10-20 million. Senior architects or firm partners may earn IDR 25 million or more. Salaries vary by location, with Jakarta generally offering higher pay, and by firm size and project scope.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a building architect in Indonesia?**
A: A day often involves site visits, client meetings, and drafting designs using software like AutoCAD or Revit. Architects coordinate with engineers, contractors, and local authorities to ensure compliance with Indonesian building codes (e.g., SNI). They also review progress, adjust plans based on feedback, and prepare permit documents, balancing creative design with technical and regulatory constraints.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of building architects in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools are streamlining tasks like generative design, energy analysis, and automated drafting, allowing architects to focus on creativity and problem-solving. In Indonesia, AI adoption is growing, particularly in large firms for optimizing building performance and reducing errors. However, local expertise in cultural context and regulations remains irreplaceable, so AI is seen as a complement rather than a replacement.
