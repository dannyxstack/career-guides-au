## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a tailor in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, tailors and dressmakers typically earn between IDR 2.5 million to IDR 5 million per month, depending on experience, location, and specialization. Those in high-end boutiques or with custom orders can earn more. Self-employed tailors may have variable income based on clientele and seasonality.

**Q: What skills are essential for a tailor in Indonesia today?**
A: Essential skills include precise hand and machine sewing, pattern making, fabric knowledge, and fitting adjustments. In Indonesia, familiarity with traditional batik and kebaya crafting is valued. Basic business skills for managing orders, pricing, and customer communication are also important. Adaptability to modern fashion trends helps sustain demand.

**Q: How is AI affecting tailoring in Indonesia?**
A: AI impacts tailoring mainly through automated design software and pattern generators, which speed up drafting but cannot replace hands-on craftsmanship. In Indonesia, traditional custom tailoring remains resilient as consumers value personalized fits and hand-finishing. AI tools may assist with inventory and trend forecasting, but human skill remains central for alterations and bespoke work.
