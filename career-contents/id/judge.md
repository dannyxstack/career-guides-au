## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a judge in Indonesia?**
A: Judges in Indonesia earn a monthly salary ranging from approximately IDR 15 million for entry-level positions to over IDR 50 million for senior judges at the Supreme Court. Salaries are regulated by government pay scales and include allowances based on rank and region.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a judge in Indonesia?**
A: To become a judge in Indonesia, you must hold a law degree (S1) and complete a specialized judicial education program at the Supreme Court’s Judicial Training Center. Passing the national judge selection exam and having at least two years of legal practice experience are also required.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the work of judges in Indonesia?**
A: AI is increasingly used in Indonesian courts for case management, document analysis, and legal research, but judges retain final decision-making authority. AI tools help reduce administrative burdens, allowing judges to focus more on complex legal reasoning and judgments.
