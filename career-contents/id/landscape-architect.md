## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for landscape architects in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level landscape architects in Indonesia earn around IDR 5–8 million per month, while mid-career professionals earn IDR 10–15 million. Senior architects with 10+ years’ experience can earn IDR 20–30 million monthly, especially in Jakarta. Salaries vary by region, project scale, and employer (private firms vs. government).

**Q: What is the career outlook for landscape architects in Indonesia?**
A: Demand is growing due to urbanization, infrastructure projects, and green space initiatives. Opportunities exist in private consultancies, property development, and government planning. Specializing in sustainable design or large-scale projects improves prospects. The profession remains competitive, with a positive outlook driven by environmental awareness and smart-city trends.

**Q: What key skills are needed for landscape architects in Indonesia?**
A: Proficiency in design software (AutoCAD, SketchUp, GIS) and knowledge of Indonesian plant species and climate are essential. Strong understanding of local regulations, site analysis, and sustainable drainage systems is required. Communication and project management skills are critical for coordinating with engineers, clients, and contractors.
