## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level shelf fillers in Indonesia earn between IDR 2.5 to 4 million per month in major cities like Jakarta. Larger supermarkets and hypermarkets may offer IDR 3–5 million. Overtime, night shifts, and weekend work can increase total pay. Some retailers pay hourly or daily rates, especially for part-time or temporary positions.

**Q: What skills are required to become a shelf filler in Indonesia?**
A: You need basic literacy and numeracy to read labels and count stock. Physical stamina is essential for standing, lifting up to 15 kg, and moving merchandise. Attention to detail ensures correct product placement and rotation (FIFO). Customer service skills help assist shoppers. Familiarity with barcode scanners or mobile inventory apps is a plus.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting shelf filler jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI-powered cameras and sensors are automating shelf monitoring and inventory tracking in some Indonesian retail chains. This reduces manual counting tasks. However, human shelf fillers remain essential for restocking irregular items, handling fresh produce, and assisting customers. Workers who can operate digital tools and adapt to new technologies will have better prospects.
