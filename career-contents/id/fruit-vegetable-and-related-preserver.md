## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for fruit and vegetable preservers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level preservers earn around IDR 3,000,000 per month, while experienced workers in industrial settings can earn IDR 5,000,000 to IDR 7,000,000. Salaries vary by region and employer, with food processing plants in Java offering higher pay.

**Q: What skills are essential for fruit and vegetable preservers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of food safety and hygiene, ability to operate preserving equipment, and familiarity with local preservation techniques such as pickling, drying, or canning. Attention to detail for quality control and basic literacy for following recipes and safety protocols are also important.

**Q: How is AI impacting fruit and vegetable preserving jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI-driven sorting and quality inspection machines are gradually adopted in large factories, potentially reducing demand for manual sorters. However, small-scale traditional preserving remains common, and AI is not yet widespread. Upskilling in machine operation can help workers stay relevant as automation increases.
