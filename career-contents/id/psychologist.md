## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a psychologist in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level psychologists in Indonesia earn around IDR 5-8 million per month, while experienced professionals in private practice or corporate roles can earn IDR 15-30 million. Salaries vary by sector, with clinical and industrial-organizational psychologists typically earning more, especially in major cities like Jakarta.

**Q: What does a typical workday look like for a psychologist in Indonesia?**
A: A psychologist in Indonesia may conduct client assessments, provide therapy or counseling, and develop treatment plans. Those in organizational settings might design employee wellness programs or conduct training. Many also maintain records, collaborate with healthcare professionals, and engage in community outreach or school consultations.

**Q: How is AI impacting the psychology profession in Indonesia?**
A: AI is beginning to assist with administrative tasks like scheduling and note-taking, and some tools support diagnostic screening. However, human judgment remains essential for therapy and assessment. Psychologists in Indonesia are adapting by learning to integrate AI ethically, emphasizing empathy and critical thinking skills that AI cannot replicate.
