## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a physiotherapist in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level physiotherapists in Indonesia earn around IDR 4-6 million per month in private clinics or hospitals. With 5+ years of experience, salaries range from IDR 8-15 million monthly. Those in specialized fields or owning a practice can earn IDR 20 million or more. Salaries vary by location, with Jakarta typically offering higher pay.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as a physiotherapist in Indonesia?**
A: Strong clinical assessment and manual therapy skills are essential, along with knowledge of musculoskeletal, neurological, and cardiorespiratory conditions. Good communication in Indonesian and basic English helps when working with international patients. Digital literacy for telerehabilitation and electronic medical records is increasingly important, as is empathy and patient education.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence (AI) impacting physiotherapy jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools like motion-capture apps and predictive analytics are assisting diagnosis and treatment planning, but not replacing physiotherapists. AI helps automate administrative tasks and monitor patient progress remotely. However, hands-on manual therapy and personalized care remain irreplaceable. Physiotherapists who adopt AI for data analysis and telerehabilitation will have a competitive advantage.
