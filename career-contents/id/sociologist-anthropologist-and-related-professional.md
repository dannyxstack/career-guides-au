## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sociologists and anthropologists in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level professionals earn around IDR 5–8 million monthly, while mid-level roles pay IDR 10–20 million. Senior consultants or academics can earn IDR 25–40 million or more. Salaries vary by sector, with NGOs and private research firms often offering higher pay than government positions.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a sociologist or anthropologist in Indonesia?**
A: Work often involves designing and conducting fieldwork, such as interviews or surveys in communities across Java or remote areas. Professionals analyze data, prepare reports, and present findings to stakeholders like government agencies or corporations. Many balance office-based analysis with travel for data collection.

**Q: How is AI affecting the work of sociologists and anthropologists in Indonesia?**
A: AI assists in processing large datasets, like social media trends, but fieldwork remains essential for qualitative insights. Professionals now use AI tools for transcription and preliminary analysis, but interpretative skills are still crucial. The demand for combining tech proficiency with cultural understanding is rising.
