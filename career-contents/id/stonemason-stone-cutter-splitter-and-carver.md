## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a stonemason in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level stonemasons in Indonesia earn around IDR 3–4 million per month, while experienced craftsmen can earn IDR 5–8 million. Specialists in stone carving, especially in Bali or Java, may earn more due to demand for cultural and decorative work. Income varies by project and skill level.

**Q: What skills are essential for a stonemason in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in cutting, shaping, and installing stone using hand and power tools. Knowledge of local stone types (e.g., andesite, limestone) and traditional carving techniques is highly valued. Physical stamina, precision, and safety awareness are crucial. Many gain expertise through apprenticeships or vocational training.

**Q: How is AI affecting stonemasonry in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation are mostly used in stone quarrying and large-scale cutting machines, but traditional hand-carving remains valued for custom work, especially in heritage and tourist areas. AI-driven design tools can assist in planning, but the hands-on craft of stonemasons is still essential for detail and restoration. The job is not expected to be fully automated soon.
