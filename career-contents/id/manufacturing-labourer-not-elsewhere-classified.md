## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a manufacturing labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Monthly salaries for manufacturing labourers in Indonesia generally range from IDR 3,000,000 to IDR 5,000,000, depending on the region and company. In major industrial areas like Jakarta, Bekasi, or Surabaya, wages may be higher but often align with the provincial minimum wage. Overtime pay can supplement income.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a manufacturing labourer in Indonesia?**
A: A typical day includes operating simple machinery, assembling products, packing goods, or performing quality checks on a production line. Work is often repetitive and physically demanding, requiring standing for long hours. Labourers follow strict safety protocols and may rotate shifts to meet production targets.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting manufacturing labourer jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation are gradually replacing routine tasks like sorting and basic assembly in Indonesia's manufacturing sector. However, many factories still rely on manual labour for flexible, low-cost operations. Labourers who learn to operate automated systems or perform maintenance tasks may remain employable as roles evolve.
