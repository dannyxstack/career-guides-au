## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn IDR 4-6 million per month, while experienced ones with certifications like CCNA can earn IDR 8-12 million. Specialists in major cities such as Jakarta may reach IDR 15 million. Salaries vary by industry, with telecommunications and banking sectors often offering higher pay.

**Q: What technical skills are essential for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include configuring routers and switches (e.g., Cisco, MikroTik), network security basics, troubleshooting TCP/IP, and managing servers (Windows/Linux). Certifications like CCNA, MTCNA, or CompTIA Network+ are highly valued. Soft skills like communication and problem-solving are also important for coordinating with teams and clients.

**Q: How is the career outlook for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Indonesia?**
A: Demand remains strong due to digital transformation across industries like finance, e-commerce, and government. Cloud computing and IoT growth create new opportunities. Technicians can advance to network engineers, IT managers, or specialize in cybersecurity. Continuous learning is key, as automation and AI handle routine tasks but require human oversight for complex issues.
