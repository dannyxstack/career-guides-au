## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physical and engineering science technicians in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around IDR 4–6 million per month, while experienced professionals in sectors like oil and gas or manufacturing can earn IDR 8–15 million. Salaries vary by industry, location (Jakarta typically offers higher pay), and certification levels.

**Q: How is AI affecting physical and engineering science technician jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating routine data collection and analysis tasks, but technicians are increasingly needed to manage, calibrate, and troubleshoot AI-driven equipment. Upskilling in programming and data interpretation is becoming essential to remain competitive in fields like quality control and laboratory testing.

**Q: What key skills do employers in Indonesia look for in physical and engineering science technicians?**
A: Employers value proficiency in technical drawing, instrumentation, and basic programming (e.g., Python or PLC). Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and familiarity with ISO standards are also important. Hands-on experience with laboratory or field equipment is often required.
