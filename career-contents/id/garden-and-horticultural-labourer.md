## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garden and horticultural labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Monthly wages for garden and horticultural labourers in Indonesia range from IDR 2.5 to 4 million (approx. USD 160–260), depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level workers earn around IDR 2 million, while those with specialized skills in plant care or landscaping may earn higher. Overtime pay is rare but possible for large projects.

**Q: What are the daily tasks of a garden and horticultural labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include planting, weeding, watering, pruning, fertilizing, and mowing lawns. Labourers also rake leaves, prepare soil, apply pesticides, and assist with landscaping installations. Work is outdoor in varying weather, requiring physical stamina. Hand tools like hoes, shears, and rakes are commonly used. Basic knowledge of local plants and pest control is helpful.

**Q: How is AI impacting garden and horticultural labourers in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation have minimal impact on most garden labourers in Indonesia. Manual labour dominates due to low mechanization in small-scale gardens and nurseries. Large plantations may adopt robotic irrigation or AI-based pest monitoring, but this remains rare. Labourers are unlikely to face job displacement soon, though basic digital skills may become useful for operating smart tools.
