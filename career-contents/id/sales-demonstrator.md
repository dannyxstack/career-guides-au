## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level sales demonstrators earn around Rp 3–5 million per month, while experienced demonstrators in major cities like Jakarta can earn Rp 6–10 million. Additional commissions or bonuses from product sales may increase total income. Pay varies by industry, employer, and location.

**Q: What skills are essential for a Sales Demonstrator in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include strong communication and interpersonal abilities to engage customers, product knowledge to explain features clearly, and persuasive selling techniques. Basic language proficiency in Indonesian is essential, and English is a plus for international brands. Adaptability and a friendly demeanor are also important.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the Sales Demonstrator role in Indonesia?**
A: AI is not currently replacing sales demonstrators because the role relies on personal interaction and trust-building. However, AI tools assist in tracking customer preferences and inventory. Demonstrators may use digital tablets for product information, but face-to-face engagement remains irreplaceable in Indonesia's market.
