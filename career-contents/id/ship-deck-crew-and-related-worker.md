## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for ships' deck crews in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary for ships' deck crews in Indonesia ranges from IDR 4 million to IDR 8 million, depending on experience, vessel type (e.g., cargo, tanker, or passenger), and route. Senior crew members or those working on international vessels can earn higher, up to IDR 15 million.

**Q: What are the typical day-to-day duties of a ships' deck crew in Indonesia?**
A: Typical duties include standing watch, steering the ship, maintaining deck equipment (e.g., ropes, fenders, and life-saving appliances), handling mooring lines during port calls, assisting with cargo operations, and performing regular safety drills. Work shifts often last 4-6 hours, with additional tasks as needed.

**Q: What skills and certifications are required for deck crew positions in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina, basic navigation knowledge, communication abilities, and teamwork. Required certifications include Basic Safety Training (BST), Security Awareness Training, and a valid Seaman's Book, as per Indonesian maritime regulations. Experience with rope work and engine room assistance is a plus.
