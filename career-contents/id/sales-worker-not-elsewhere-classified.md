## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level sales workers earn around IDR 3-5 million per month, while experienced staff can earn IDR 6-12 million. Commission and bonuses often double these figures, especially in high-demand sectors like property or automotive. Salaries vary significantly by industry, location, and company size.

**Q: What key skills are essential for sales workers in Indonesia?**
A: Strong communication and negotiation skills in Bahasa Indonesia and basic English are crucial. Local market knowledge, relationship-building (silaturahmi), and adaptability to digital tools like CRM software or WhatsApp Business matter. Resilience and cultural sensitivity also help, especially when dealing with diverse customers across the archipelago.

**Q: How is AI impacting sales roles in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools like chatbots and predictive analytics streamline lead generation and customer follow-ups, but personal interaction remains vital. Sales workers now use AI for data analysis but still rely on human rapport to close deals. Upskilling in basic AI tools is becoming important, though not replacing the need for interpersonal skills.
