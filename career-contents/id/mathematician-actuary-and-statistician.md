## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level positions typically earn IDR 6–10 million per month, while mid-level professionals with 5–10 years of experience can earn IDR 15–30 million. Senior roles in banking, insurance, or fintech may exceed IDR 40 million. Actuaries with professional certification often command higher pay, especially in Jakarta.

**Q: What key skills are essential for actuaries and statisticians in Indonesia?**
A: Strong proficiency in statistical software (R, Python, SAS) and data analysis is crucial. Actuaries need expertise in risk modeling and insurance regulations (OJK). Soft skills like business communication and problem-solving are valued. For statisticians, knowledge of big data tools and machine learning is increasingly important in Indonesia's digital economy.

**Q: What is the career outlook for mathematicians and statisticians in Indonesia?**
A: Demand is growing due to fintech expansion, digital banking, and data-driven decision-making. Actuaries are needed in insurance and pension funds as regulations tighten. Statisticians find opportunities in tech, government, and market research. AI and automation are reshaping roles, but analytical and strategic skills remain highly valued.
