## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dental assistants and therapists in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, dental assistants typically earn between IDR 3,000,000 and IDR 5,000,000 per month, while dental therapists earn IDR 4,000,000 to IDR 6,500,000. Pay varies by experience, location (higher in Jakarta), and type of practice (private clinics often pay more than public health centers). Additional certifications can increase earning potential.

**Q: What skills are essential for dental assistants and therapists in Indonesia?**
A: Dental assistants and therapists in Indonesia need strong clinical skills such as taking radiographs, sterilizing instruments, and assisting in procedures. Communication skills are crucial for patient education and team collaboration. Attention to detail, manual dexterity, and knowledge of infection control are essential. Formal training from accredited institutions and registration with the Indonesian Dental Association are typically required.

**Q: How will AI impact dental assistants and therapists in Indonesia?**
A: AI is beginning to impact dental care in Indonesia through diagnostic imaging analysis and administrative automation. However, AI is unlikely to replace dental assistants and therapists entirely because their roles involve direct patient care, manual tasks, and clinical judgment. Instead, AI may enhance efficiency by assisting with tasks like scheduling and preliminary scans, allowing professionals to focus on patient interaction and complex procedures.
