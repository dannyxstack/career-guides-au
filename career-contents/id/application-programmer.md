## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an applications programmer in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level applications programmers in Indonesia earn around IDR 5–8 million monthly, mid-level IDR 10–20 million, and senior roles IDR 25–40 million. Salaries vary by company, location (Jakarta is higher), and tech stack. Freelancers may charge IDR 100,000–300,000 per hour.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for applications programmers in Indonesia?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in Java, Python, JavaScript (React, Node.js), SQL, and cloud platforms (AWS, Google Cloud). Familiarity with Agile methodologies, version control (Git), and mobile development (Kotlin, Swift) is also valuable. Soft skills like problem-solving and communication are equally important.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the role of applications programmers in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools like GitHub Copilot assist with code generation and debugging, boosting productivity. Programmers are expected to integrate AI APIs (e.g., for chatbots, image recognition) into applications. While routine coding may automate, demand grows for AI-savvy developers who can design intelligent systems.
