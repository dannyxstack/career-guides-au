## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a scribe in Indonesia?**
A: Monthly salaries for scribes in Indonesia vary by experience and location, ranging from IDR 3-5 million for entry-level to IDR 7-10 million for experienced workers. Self-employed scribes may earn more but with inconsistent income. Formal employment in courts or government offices offers stable pay with benefits.

**Q: What skills are essential for a scribe in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include fast and accurate typing (both Bahasa Indonesia and local languages), good listening and comprehension, attention to detail, knowledge of legal or administrative terminology, and proficiency with transcription software. Strong handwriting is also valued since some settings still use manual recording.

**Q: How is AI affecting the career of scribes in Indonesia?**
A: AI-powered transcription tools are increasingly used in Indonesia, automating basic dictation and reducing demand for entry-level scribes. However, courts and formal proceedings still require human scribes for accuracy, context understanding, and legal documentation. Scribes can stay relevant by specializing in technical or legal transcription and developing proofreading skills.
