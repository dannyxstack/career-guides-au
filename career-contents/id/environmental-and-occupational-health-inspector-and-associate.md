## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental and occupational health inspectors in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary for environmental and occupational health inspectors in Indonesia ranges from IDR 5,000,000 to IDR 12,000,000 depending on experience, sector (government or private), and location. Entry-level positions start lower, while senior inspectors in multinational companies can earn more. Additional benefits may include allowances for field inspections.

**Q: What are the day-to-day responsibilities of an environmental and occupational health inspector in Indonesia?**
A: Inspectors conduct workplace inspections to assess compliance with health and safety regulations, collect samples, monitor air and water quality, investigate complaints, and educate employers on hazard prevention. Many work for government agencies like the Ministry of Manpower or private consulting firms. Career progression can lead to supervisory roles or specialization in industrial hygiene.

**Q: What skills and qualifications are needed for this role in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of Indonesian health and safety laws, ability to use testing equipment, analytical thinking, and communication. A diploma or bachelor’s degree in public health, environmental health, or occupational safety is typically required. Certification from recognized bodies like BNSP is advantageous, and practical experience through internships is also valued.
