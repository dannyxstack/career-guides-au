## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for journalists in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level journalists in Indonesia earn around IDR 3-5 million per month, while mid-career reporters with 3-5 years of experience make IDR 6-10 million. Senior editors or special correspondents can earn IDR 12-20 million or more, especially at major media outlets in Jakarta. Salaries vary by region, employer, and specialization.

**Q: What key skills are essential for journalists in Indonesia?**
A: Strong Indonesian and English language proficiency, both written and spoken, is crucial. Digital skills such as multimedia storytelling, data journalism, social media management, and basic video editing are increasingly important. Investigative reporting, fact-checking, and adaptability to fast-paced news cycles are also highly valued by Indonesian media employers.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting journalism in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools are automating routine tasks like transcribing interviews and generating sports or financial reports, allowing journalists to focus on in-depth analysis. However, AI cannot replace human judgment, ethics, or contextual understanding. Journalists in Indonesia must develop skills to collaborate with AI, verify automated content, and maintain editorial integrity.
