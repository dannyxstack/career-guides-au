## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly wage for forestry labourers in Indonesia?**
A: Forestry labourers in Indonesia typically earn between IDR 2.5 million and IDR 4 million per month, depending on the region and employer. Wages are often paid daily or per task, with average daily rates ranging from IDR 80,000 to IDR 150,000. In remote areas, pay may be lower but sometimes includes basic accommodation.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a forestry labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for demanding outdoor work, ability to use hand tools such as axes, machetes, and chainsaws, and basic knowledge of local tree species and forest safety. Most workers learn on the job, but prior experience in plantation maintenance, logging, or reforestation is valued by employers.

**Q: What is the career outlook for forestry labourers in Indonesia?**
A: The demand for forestry labourers remains stable due to ongoing plantation management and reforestation programs, especially in Sumatra and Kalimantan. However, increased mechanization and conservation policies may slowly reduce manual labour needs. Opportunities are tied to the forestry sector's focus on sustainable practices and illegal logging prevention.
