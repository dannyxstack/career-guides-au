## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income for astrologers, fortune-tellers, and related workers in Indonesia?**
A: Income varies widely. In urban areas like Jakarta, experienced practitioners may earn between IDR 3-8 million per month, while those in rural settings earn less. Many rely on a combination of consultations, event bookings, and product sales. Income is often irregular and depends on reputation and client base.

**Q: What skills are essential for success as an astrologer or fortune-teller in Indonesia?**
A: Deep knowledge of traditional Indonesian astrology or divination methods (e.g., Javanese primbon, Chinese zodiac, tarot) is crucial. Strong interpersonal and communication skills help build trust with clients. Business acumen for self-marketing, especially via social media, is increasingly important. Ethical sensitivity is needed to provide guidance without causing harm.

**Q: How is the astrologer, fortune-teller profession evolving in Indonesia?**
A: The profession remains culturally significant, especially for life events like weddings and business decisions. Demand is stable but faces competition from online platforms offering free horoscopes. Some practitioners are adapting by offering online consultations and content creation. However, AI-generated predictions may reduce demand for basic services, while personalized in-person advice retains value.
