## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average daily wage for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Daily wages vary by region and farm type, typically ranging from IDR 50,000 to IDR 100,000. In Java, rates are often lower, while in Sumatra or Kalimantan they may be higher. Payment is usually cash per day, with occasional bonuses for harvesting seasons.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Physical stamina and willingness for outdoor manual labor are essential. Basic knowledge of planting, weeding, fertilizing, and harvesting crops, as well as feeding and cleaning livestock, is needed. Familiarity with simple tools and water management is also useful. No formal education is required, but experience with local crops and animals helps.

**Q: What is the career outlook for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Indonesia?**
A: Demand remains steady, especially in rural areas, as smallholder farms dominate. However, job security is low due to seasonal work and weather risks. Advancement opportunities are limited, but experienced labourers may become farm supervisors or manage their own plots. Mechanization is increasing slowly, so manual jobs persist but may decline.
