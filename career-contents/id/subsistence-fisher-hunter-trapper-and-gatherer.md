## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence fishers and gatherers in Indonesia?**
A: Income varies greatly by region and season, often ranging from IDR 1 to 3 million per month. Many rely on daily catch sales or barter trade. Earnings are inconsistent, with peak seasons during calm seas or fruit harvests. Most have no fixed wage, using cash from surplus catch for essentials.

**Q: What key skills are needed for subsistence fishing and gathering in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include knowledge of local fish behavior, tidal patterns, and forest edibles. Proficiency with traditional gear like nets, traps, and spears is vital. Survival skills, weather reading, and navigation without GPS are also critical. Cultural and ecological knowledge passed down through generations remains indispensable.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence fishers and gatherers in Indonesia?**
A: The outlook is challenging due to overfishing, habitat loss, and climate change. Younger generations often seek urban jobs, reducing transmission of traditional skills. Government policies promoting aquaculture and conservation may shift livelihoods. However, for remote communities, subsistence activities remain a vital safety net.
