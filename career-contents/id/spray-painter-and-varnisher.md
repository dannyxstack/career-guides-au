## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for spray painters in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level spray painters in Indonesia earn around IDR 3-4 million per month, while experienced painters can earn IDR 5-8 million. Pay varies by industry (automotive, furniture, construction) and location, with Jakarta typically offering higher wages. Overtime and piecework can increase earnings.

**Q: What skills are essential for spray painters in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include surface preparation, paint mixing, spraying techniques, knowledge of various paints and thinners, attention to detail, and safety practices. Physical stamina and the ability to work in ventilated areas or with protective gear are important. Experience with spray guns and airless equipment is highly valued.

**Q: Will AI and automation affect spray painters in Indonesia?**
A: Automation and robotic painting are increasing in large-scale manufacturing, reducing demand for manual spray painters in those sectors. However, custom jobs, detailed finishes, and small workshops still require human painters. Upskilling in precision techniques and new paint technologies can help workers stay relevant.
