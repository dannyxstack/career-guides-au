## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Ships’ Engineer in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level ships’ engineers in Indonesia earn around IDR 5–10 million per month, while experienced chief engineers can earn IDR 20–40 million. Salaries vary by vessel type, company, and rank. Offshore and international-flagged ships often pay higher, but be aware that on-board expenses are minimal.

**Q: What does a typical career path look like for a Ships’ Engineer in Indonesia?**
A: Starting as a cadet or junior engineer, one progresses through ranks: 4th, 3rd, 2nd, and finally chief engineer. Each step requires sea-time, exams, and STCW certifications. Many engineers work rotating schedules (e.g., 3 months on, 1 month off). Shore-based roles in shipping companies or maritime agencies are common later.

**Q: What key skills and certifications are needed to work as a Ships’ Engineer in Indonesia?**
A: Mandatory certifications include STCW Basic Safety, Fire Fighting, and Engineering Watch Rating (ERM). A Diploma (D3 or D4) in marine engineering or naval architecture is preferred. Practical skills in diesel engines, electrical systems, and troubleshooting are crucial. Physical fitness and ability to work under pressure are essential.
