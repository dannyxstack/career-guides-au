## 7. FAQ

**Q: How much do subsistence livestock farmers earn in Indonesia?**
A: Subsistence livestock farmers in Indonesia typically earn between IDR 1 million and IDR 3 million per month, though income is often non-monetary, derived from meat, milk, or eggs for household use. Surplus sales provide cash, but earnings are irregular. Many farmers rely on multiple income sources or government assistance to supplement their livelihoods.

**Q: What skills are essential for subsistence livestock farmers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include animal husbandry, disease recognition, basic veterinary care, and knowledge of local feed sources. Traditional breeding techniques, pasture management, and climate adaptation are crucial. Financial literacy for selling surplus, and familiarity with government support programs, also help sustain operations.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence livestock farmers in Indonesia?**
A: The outlook is stable yet challenging. Rural-to-urban migration reduces the workforce, while climate change and land competition increase risks. However, domestic demand for livestock products remains strong. Government initiatives for smallholders and cooperatives may improve access to markets and training, helping farmers sustain their way of life.
