## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for language teachers in Indonesia?**
A: Salaries vary by institution and location. In private language centers or international schools, monthly pay ranges from IDR 4,000,000 to IDR 12,000,000 for native or experienced teachers. Public school salaries are lower, around IDR 3,000,000–6,000,000. Rates for freelance tutoring range from IDR 75,000 to IDR 200,000 per hour.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to teach languages in Indonesia?**
A: A bachelor's degree is generally required, preferably in education or the target language. TEFL/TESOL certification (for English) is commonly expected. For other languages, relevant teaching credentials or a degree in linguistics help. Fluency in Indonesian is not mandatory but beneficial for classroom management. Patience, cultural adaptability, and basic digital literacy are also valued.

**Q: How is the career outlook for language teachers in Indonesia amid AI advancements?**
A: Demand remains stable for in-person instruction, especially for English, Mandarin, Japanese, and Arabic. AI tools assist but cannot replace interactive human teaching. Growth is seen in corporate training and online tutoring. Competition is moderate; however, teachers specializing in exam preparation or business communication have better prospects. The field is adapting rather than shrinking.
