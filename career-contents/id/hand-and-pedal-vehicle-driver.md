## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly income typically ranges from IDR 2,000,000 to IDR 4,000,000, depending on factors like location, number of trips, and vehicle type. Drivers in high-demand areas such as city centers or near markets may earn more. Income is often variable and may include tips.

**Q: What are the daily tasks and key skills required for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include operating vehicles like becak or ojek, navigating through traffic, picking up and dropping off passengers, collecting fares, and performing basic vehicle maintenance. Key skills include physical stamina, knowledge of local routes, customer service, basic financial management, and adherence to traffic rules.

**Q: How is technology affecting the future of hand and pedal vehicle driving in Indonesia?**
A: Ride-hailing apps have increased competition but also expanded job opportunities by connecting drivers with passengers. While AI and automation are advancing, full replacement is unlikely soon due to the need for human judgment in navigating complex traffic and narrow streets. Drivers who adapt to app-based platforms may have better prospects.
