## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for clerical support workers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level clerical support workers in Indonesia earn around IDR 3–5 million per month, while experienced workers can earn IDR 6–9 million. Pay varies by industry, location, and company size. In major cities like Jakarta, salaries tend to be higher, but so is the cost of living.

**Q: What skills are important for clerical support workers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word), data entry accuracy, good communication in Bahasa Indonesia and basic English, time management, and familiarity with office equipment. Increasingly, employers seek basic digital literacy, such as using cloud-based tools and scheduling software.

**Q: How is AI affecting clerical support roles in Indonesia?**
A: AI automation is gradually replacing routine tasks like data entry, scheduling, and document processing. Clerical workers in Indonesia are increasingly expected to use AI tools for efficiency, such as chatbots or automated reporting. To stay relevant, workers should focus on soft skills like problem-solving and adaptability.
