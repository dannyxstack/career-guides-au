## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, weaving and knitting machine operators typically earn between Rp 3,000,000 and Rp 5,500,000 per month, depending on experience, location (textile hubs like Bandung or Tangerang), and the employer. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage, while skilled operators in large factories can earn higher. Overtime pay and bonuses are common.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a weaving or knitting machine operator in Indonesia?**
A: Operators need basic technical skills to set up, monitor, and adjust looms or knitting machines. Key skills include understanding machine controls, detecting fabric defects, performing routine maintenance, and following safety protocols. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to work in shifts are important. Formal training from vocational schools or on-the-job experience is common.

**Q: What is the career outlook for weaving and knitting machine operators in Indonesia?**
A: The textile industry remains a major employer in Indonesia, but automation is reducing demand for manual operators. Entry-level jobs may decline, but skilled operators who can handle modern machinery and perform troubleshooting are still needed. Workers in regions like West Java and Central Java have better prospects. Upskilling in digital knitting or weaving control systems can improve career stability.
