**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using GPS and drones for forest mapping and boundary marking
- Early detection of pests and diseases based on AI image recognition
- Automated felling machinery operation and pruning
- Operation of forest fire monitoring and early warning systems
- data recording (e.g., tree growth, harvest volume)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Develop precision harvesting plans using satellite imagery and AI analysis.
- Using VR simulation for safety training and emergency drills
- AI-assisted Forest Carbon Sequestration Measurement and Reporting
- Real-time monitoring of soil moisture and wind speed via IoT sensors
- Mobile apps to track tools and resource locations

**Human moat:**

- Social skills for collaborating and communicating with landowners and communities

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for forestry workers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level forestry workers in Indonesia earn around IDR 3–4 million per month, while experienced workers can earn IDR 5–8 million. Salaries vary by region and employer, with state-owned companies often offering higher pay. Additional benefits may include housing or transportation allowances.

**Q: What is the career outlook for forestry workers in Indonesia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to Indonesia's vast forests and reforestation efforts. Mechanization and sustainability are changing roles—workers skilled in agroforestry, conservation, or modern logging techniques will have better opportunities. Government programs and private plantations provide stable employment.

**Q: How is AI affecting forestry work in Indonesia?**
A: AI is used for forest monitoring, mapping, and fire detection via satellites and drones. It helps workers focus on technical tasks like data analysis and precision forestry. Training in digital tools enhances job security as the industry adopts technology for sustainable management.
