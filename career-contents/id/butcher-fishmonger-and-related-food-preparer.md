**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Batch cutting and segmentation of standard parts
- Repetitive motions in deboning and skinning processes
- Basic processing like meat grinding and sausage stuffing
- Weighing, labeling, and packaging line operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI vision systems for meat grading and defect detection
- Optimizing cutting plans through data analysis to reduce waste
- Manage meat preservation and turnover using smart inventory systems
- Using augmented reality (AR) to assist training new employees in cutting techniques

**Human moat:**

- Hand-finishing special parts and custom cutting according to client requirements

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for butchers and fishmongers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level butchers in Indonesia earn around Rp 3-4 million per month, while experienced workers in traditional markets or supermarkets can earn Rp 5-8 million. Those with specialized skills, such as halal meat cutting or premium seafood handling, may earn higher wages, especially in upscale outlets.

**Q: What skills are essential for butchers and fishmongers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include precise cutting and deboning techniques, knowledge of meat and fish grading, and strict hygiene standards. Halal slaughter expertise is crucial in Indonesia due to the majority Muslim population. Physical stamina, knife maintenance, and customer service are also important. Formal training from vocational schools or apprenticeships in wet markets provides a strong foundation.

**Q: How is AI impacting butchers and fishmongers in Indonesia?**
A: AI-powered tools, such as automated cutting machines and inventory management systems, are emerging in large processing plants. However, in traditional markets and small shops, manual skills remain dominant. AI may assist with stock optimization and quality control, but it is unlikely to fully replace the craft, especially for halal preparation and customer interaction.
