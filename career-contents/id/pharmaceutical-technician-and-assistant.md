## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pharmaceutical technician in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level pharmaceutical technicians in Indonesia earn around IDR 4–6 million monthly. With experience, salaries can rise to IDR 8–12 million. In major cities like Jakarta or Surabaya, pay may be 10–20% higher. Public sector roles often offer additional allowances, while private pharmacies or hospitals may provide performance bonuses.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a pharmaceutical technician in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include assisting pharmacists in preparing and dispensing medications, managing inventory, labeling prescriptions, and maintaining records. Technicians also counsel patients on dosage and side effects, operate in hospital pharmacies or retail drug stores, and ensure compliance with BPOM regulations. They often interact with doctors and nurses to clarify prescriptions.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of pharmaceutical technicians in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating inventory management and prescription verification, reducing manual errors. Some pharmacies use AI-powered chatbots for patient queries. However, tasks requiring human judgment, such as compounding medications or patient counseling, remain essential. Technicians need to adapt by learning basic data entry and digital literacy to work alongside these tools.
