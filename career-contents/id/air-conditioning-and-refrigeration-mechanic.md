## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level mechanics earn around IDR 3-5 million per month, while experienced professionals can make IDR 7-12 million. In major cities like Jakarta or Surabaya, rates may be higher due to demand. Overtime and project-based work can increase earnings significantly.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for an AC and refrigeration mechanic in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include installing, maintaining, and repairing air conditioning and refrigeration systems in homes, offices, or industrial sites. Mechanics diagnose issues, replace parts, recharge refrigerants, and ensure systems run efficiently. Work often involves climbing ladders, handling tools, and coordinating with clients.

**Q: Is artificial intelligence likely to affect the job of AC and refrigeration mechanics in Indonesia?**
A: AI impacts are minimal for hands-on tasks like repairs and installations. Smart diagnostics may assist troubleshooting, but physical work remains essential. Mechanics who learn to work with smart systems will stay competitive. The demand for skilled technicians is expected to remain stable as cooling systems proliferate.
