## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level fumigators in Indonesia earn around IDR 3-5 million per month, while experienced professionals can make IDR 6-10 million. Those with specialized certifications or working in high-demand sectors like plantation or food processing may earn higher. Salaries vary by region, with Jakarta and industrial areas typically offering higher pay.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a fumigator in Indonesia?**
A: A fumigator's day involves inspecting sites for pests or weeds, selecting appropriate chemicals or methods, and applying fumigants or herbicides. They use protective gear, set up tarps or enclosures, and monitor gas levels. Paperwork includes recording treatments and safety checks. Work often occurs in warehouses, farms, or residential areas, with early morning or late evening hours to minimize public exposure.

**Q: What is the job outlook for fumigators and pest controllers in Indonesia?**
A: Demand remains steady due to Indonesia's agricultural sector and urban pest issues. The rise of integrated pest management and stricter health regulations may increase need for certified professionals. However, automation in fumigation (e.g., remote monitoring) could change some tasks. Overall, skilled workers with knowledge of safe chemical use and environmental practices have stable prospects.
