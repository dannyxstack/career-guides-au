## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic measurement of room dimensions and material quantity calculation, AI can quickly generate optimal layout plans
- Automatic cutting of tiles/stone, CNC waterjet or laser cutting replaces manual cutting
- Automatic detection and adjustment solution generation for ground flatness, reducing manual trial laying
- Inventory management and material ordering processes, AI analyzes project requirements to automatically place orders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to view tiling blueprints and level indicators in real time, improving accuracy
- AI-assisted design of complex patterns and parquet, generating diverse options for clients
- Work collaboratively with automatic mortar mixing and plastering robots to improve efficiency
- AI-driven project progress prediction and scheduling to optimize multi-trade collaboration

**Human moat:**

- Manual cutting and fine placement in irregular areas and corners
- Removal of old flooring, base treatment, and leveling judgment for uneven floors.
- Communicate design preferences with clients and provide personalized advice
- Flexible problem-solving ability for on-site unexpected issues.

**Skills to build:**

- Digital Measurement Tools and BIM Software Operations

- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools for construction assistance.
- Project management and client communication skills

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for floor layers and tile setters in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, floor layers and tile setters typically earn between IDR 3,500,000 and IDR 7,000,000 per month. Experienced workers in large cities or with specialized skills can earn higher rates. Income often depends on project basis, with daily rates ranging from IDR 150,000 to IDR 300,000. Overtime and bonuses may apply for commercial projects.

**Q: What skills are essential for floor layers and tile setters in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include precision in measuring and cutting tiles, knowledge of adhesives and grouting techniques, and ability to level surfaces. Physical stamina and attention to detail are crucial. Familiarity with various tile types (ceramic, porcelain, natural stone) and tools like trowels, cutters, and levels is required. Good communication and time management help in coordinating with contractors and clients.

**Q: What is the career outlook for floor layers and tile setters in Indonesia?**
A: Demand for floor layers and tile setters remains steady due to ongoing construction and renovation projects in residential and commercial sectors. With Indonesia's growing urban population, new housing and infrastructure developments create job opportunities. Certifications from trade associations can enhance prospects. However, automation in cutting and laying may affect basic tasks, so workers skilled in complex installations will have better long-term outlook.
