## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dancers and choreographers in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, dancers and choreographers typically earn between IDR 3 million to IDR 15 million per month, depending on experience, reputation, and performance type (traditional vs modern). Freelance work is common, and income can vary. Many supplement earnings by teaching or choreographing for events, TV, or films.

**Q: What is the career outlook for dancers and choreographers in Indonesia?**
A: The career outlook is growing with increasing interest in performing arts, both traditional and contemporary. Opportunities exist in cultural events, tourism, film, and entertainment. However, competition is high, and many work freelance. Government support for arts and cultural preservation provides some stability, but financial security often requires versatility.

**Q: What skills are essential for dancers and choreographers in Indonesia?**
A: Strong technical proficiency in various dance styles (traditional Indonesian like Balinese or Javanese, as well as modern), creativity, physical fitness, and teamwork. Knowledge of Indonesian cultural contexts is valued. Choreographers need leadership and communication skills. Adaptability and business acumen help manage freelance careers.
