**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, entry-level garment patternmakers and cutters earn around 3–5 million IDR per month. Experienced professionals in major textile hubs like Bandung or Jakarta can earn 6–9 million IDR. Salaries vary by company size, skill level, and production volume.

**Q: How is AI impacting garment patternmaking and cutting jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI-powered software can automate pattern grading and optimize fabric layout, reducing manual work. However, human expertise is still crucial for adjusting designs, handling delicate fabrics, and ensuring fit. AI is a tool that enhances efficiency but does not fully replace skilled workers.

**Q: What key skills are required for garment cutters in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include accurate measuring and cutting using electric or manual tools, reading technical patterns, knowledge of fabric types and grain lines, and attention to detail. Physical stamina and the ability to work in teams are also important in fast-paced garment factories.
