## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for livestock farm labourers in Indonesia?**
A: Livestock farm labourers in Indonesia typically earn between Rp 1.5 to 3 million per month, depending on location, farm size, and experience. Some farms offer additional benefits like housing or meals. Salaries in Java are generally lower than in outer islands due to cost-of-living differences.

**Q: What is the career outlook for livestock farm labourers in Indonesia?**
A: The demand for livestock farm labourers remains steady due to Indonesia's growing meat and dairy consumption. However, work is seasonal and physically demanding. Most positions are informal, with limited advancement opportunities. Some labourers can move to supervisory roles after years of experience.

**Q: What skills are needed to work as a livestock farm labourer in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include animal handling, feeding, cleaning pens, and basic health monitoring. Physical stamina and willingness to work outdoors in all weather are crucial. Knowledge of local livestock practices, such as poultry or cattle care, is beneficial. No formal education is required, but on-the-job training is common.
