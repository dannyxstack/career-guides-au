## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for hand packers in Indonesia?**
A: Hand packers in Indonesia typically earn between IDR 2.5 million and IDR 4.5 million per month. Salaries vary by region, industry, and employer. Higher pay is common in manufacturing hubs like Jakarta, Surabaya, and Batam, especially in electronics and food packaging sectors.

**Q: What basic skills are needed to work as a hand packer in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina, attention to detail, and hand-eye coordination. Basic literacy is required to read labels and tally counts. Most jobs provide on-the-job training, so no formal education beyond primary school is needed. Reliability and speed are highly valued.

**Q: How is automation affecting the hand packing job outlook in Indonesia?**
A: Automation is gradually reducing demand for hand packers in large-scale industries, but manual packing remains essential in small and medium enterprises, as well as in sectors with irregular product shapes. The e-commerce boom sustains demand, especially in logistics and warehousing, but upskilling in basic machine operation can improve job security.
