## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an electronics engineering technician in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around IDR 5–7 million per month, while experienced professionals in sectors like manufacturing or telecommunications can earn IDR 8–12 million. Salaries vary by industry, location (e.g., Jakarta vs. smaller cities), and certifications. Overtime and project bonuses may also apply.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for an electronics engineering technician in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include assembling, testing, and troubleshooting electronic equipment such as circuit boards, communication devices, or control systems. Technicians work under engineers, use multimeters and oscilloscopes, document results, and perform routine maintenance. The role often requires collaboration with production teams and adherence to safety standards.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of electronics engineering technicians in Indonesia?**
A: AI automates routine diagnostic and testing tasks, but technicians are still needed for hands-on repairs, complex troubleshooting, and interpreting AI-generated data. Upskilling in data analysis and AI-based tools is increasingly valuable. The demand for technicians who can integrate AI into existing systems is growing, especially in smart manufacturing and IoT sectors.
