## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for signwriters and decorative painters in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, entry-level signwriters earn around IDR 3-5 million per month, while experienced decorative painters and engravers can make IDR 8-15 million. Freelancers may charge per project, with rates varying by complexity and region. Earnings depend on reputation, portfolio, and demand for custom work.

**Q: What skills are essential for a career as a signwriter or engraver in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include steady hand-eye coordination, knowledge of paint types and application techniques, proficiency with engraving tools, and understanding of color theory. Familiarity with digital design software (e.g., Adobe Illustrator) is increasingly important for creating stencils or templates. Creativity, attention to detail, and patience are essential.

**Q: How is AI affecting the work of decorative painters and engravers in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools are used for generating digital designs, but manual execution by skilled artisans remains valued for high-quality, unique pieces. AI can automate repetitive tasks like pattern creation, but decorative painting and engraving still rely on human craftsmanship. Many clients prefer handcrafted details, limiting AI's impact on premium custom work.
