## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sports, recreation and cultural centre managers in Indonesia?**
A: Monthly salaries for sports, recreation and cultural centre managers in Indonesia typically range from IDR 8 million to 20 million, depending on facility size, location (e.g., Jakarta premiums), and experience. Senior managers at large complexes may earn higher, often including bonuses and allowances.

**Q: What skills are essential for managing a sports or cultural centre in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include leadership, financial management, event planning, marketing, and customer service. Knowledge of local regulations and cultural sensitivity is crucial. Proficiency in English and digital tools for scheduling and budgeting are increasingly important for efficiency.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of sports and recreation centre managers in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating administrative tasks like ticket sales, scheduling, and basic customer inquiries via chatbots. Managers must adapt to using data analytics for visitor trends and maintenance. However, human oversight for safety, creativity, and community engagement remains essential.
