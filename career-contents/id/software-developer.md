## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level software developers in Indonesia earn around IDR 60–120 million annually, while mid-level (3–5 years) can expect IDR 150–250 million. Senior developers and leads can earn IDR 300–500 million or more, especially in Jakarta or at multinational companies. Salaries vary by location, company size, and expertise.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for software developers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include JavaScript (React, Node.js), Python, Java, and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP). Mobile development (Android Kotlin, iOS Swift) is also sought after. Familiarity with databases (SQL, MongoDB), version control (Git), and agile methodologies is expected. Soft skills like communication and problem-solving are highly valued.

**Q: How is AI impacting software developer jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating repetitive coding tasks, but it's creating demand for developers who can integrate AI tools and build AI-powered applications. Roles focusing on data engineering, machine learning, and AI ethics are growing. Developers need to upskill in AI frameworks and stay adaptable to leverage AI as a productivity enhancer rather than a threat.
