## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for software developers and analysts in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level software developers in Indonesia earn around IDR 5-8 million monthly, while mid-level professionals make IDR 10-20 million. Senior roles or specialists in high-demand areas like AI or cloud can earn IDR 25-40 million or more. Salaries vary by location, with Jakarta and Surabaya typically offering higher pay than other regions.

**Q: What is the career outlook for software professionals in Indonesia?**
A: The outlook is very positive due to rapid digital transformation across banking, e-commerce, and government sectors. Demand for mobile and web developers, data analysts, and security specialists is growing. Proficiency in modern frameworks and cloud platforms enhances job prospects. The shortage of skilled talent means competitive salaries and opportunities for advancement.

**Q: How is AI impacting software development roles in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating routine coding tasks, boosting productivity, and creating demand for AI/ML specialists. Developers need to upskill in AI tools and data analysis. However, AI currently complements rather than replaces human creativity and problem-solving. Roles focusing on AI integration, prompt engineering, and ethical AI are emerging as new career paths.
