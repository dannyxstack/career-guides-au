## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for economists in Indonesia?**
A: Fresh graduates earn around IDR 5-8 million monthly. Mid-level economists with 5-10 years experience can earn IDR 15-25 million, while senior roles in government or multinationals may reach IDR 30-50 million. Bonuses and benefits vary.

**Q: What skills are most important for economists in Indonesia?**
A: Strong quantitative analysis using tools like Stata, R, or Python is essential. Understanding Indonesian economic policies, fiscal issues, and regional disparities is crucial. Communication skills to present findings to policymakers or business leaders are highly valued. Familiarity with local data sources like BPS (Statistics Indonesia) is advantageous.

**Q: How is AI affecting the work of economists in Indonesia?**
A: AI automates data collection and basic analysis, allowing economists to focus on interpretation and policy recommendations. Machine learning models are used for forecasting inflation and growth. However, understanding institutional context and providing strategic advice remains irreplaceable. Economists need to upskill in data science to stay relevant.
