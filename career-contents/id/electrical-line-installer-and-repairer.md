## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electrical line installer in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level electrical line installers in Indonesia earn around IDR 4-6 million per month, while experienced workers can earn IDR 8-12 million. Those with specialized high-voltage certifications or working for major utility companies may earn up to IDR 15 million. Overtime and field allowances are common.

**Q: What skills and training are required to become an electrical line installer in Indonesia?**
A: A high school diploma or vocational certificate in electrical engineering is typically required. Practical training at a PLN-authorized training center or through apprenticeship is essential. Key skills include physical fitness, ability to work at heights, knowledge of safety protocols, and basic electrical theory. Certification as a 'Teknisi Listrik' is often demanded.

**Q: How is AI impacting the career of electrical line installers in Indonesia?**
A: AI and drones are increasingly used for line inspections and fault detection, but manual installation and repair remain essential. AI reduces routine inspection workload but does not replace hands-on work. Career prospects remain stable due to Indonesia's ongoing infrastructure expansion, though upskilling in digital monitoring tools is beneficial.
