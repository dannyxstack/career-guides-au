## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for Protective Services Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Indonesia?**
A: Salaries vary by role and experience. Entry-level security guards earn around Rp 3–5 million per month, while private investigators or specialized protective workers can earn Rp 7–15 million. Overtime and night shift allowances are common. Senior roles in corporate or government sectors may exceed Rp 20 million monthly.

**Q: What key skills are needed for protective services jobs in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include physical fitness, observation, communication, and conflict de-escalation. Many roles require a high school diploma; some need certificates from the National Police or private security training. For specialized positions like bodyguards, firearms licensing and first-aid certification are valued. Indonesian language proficiency is mandatory.

**Q: How is AI impacting Protective Services Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Indonesia?**
A: AI enhances surveillance through CCTV analytics and facial recognition, reducing routine monitoring work. However, human judgment remains critical for threat assessment and crisis response. Demand for tech-savvy workers who can operate AI-driven security systems is growing. Overall, AI shifts roles toward oversight rather than replacing them entirely.
