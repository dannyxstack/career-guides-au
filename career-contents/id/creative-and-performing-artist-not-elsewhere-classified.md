## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for creative and performing artists in Indonesia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by discipline and experience. Entry-level artists may earn around IDR 3–5 million per month, while established professionals can earn IDR 10–20 million or more. Freelance income fluctuates; those in commercial arts (e.g., advertising) tend to earn more than those in traditional or fine arts.

**Q: What key skills are essential for creative and performing artists in Indonesia?**
A: Artistic talent is the foundation, but digital literacy is increasingly important, especially for graphic design, video editing, and social media. Networking and self-promotion are vital for securing projects. Understanding local culture and traditional art forms can provide a unique edge in Indonesia's diverse creative scene.

**Q: What is the career outlook for creative and performing artists in Indonesia?**
A: The outlook is cautiously positive. Growth in digital media, film, and advertising boosts demand for skilled artists. However, competition is intense, and many artists work freelance. AI tools are reshaping some tasks, but human creativity remains valued. Artists who adapt to technology and build strong portfolios have the best prospects.
