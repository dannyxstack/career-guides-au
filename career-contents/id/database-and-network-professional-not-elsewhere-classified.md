## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Database and Network Professionals not elsewhere classified in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level positions earn around IDR 6–10 million monthly, while mid-level professionals with 3–5 years of experience can earn IDR 12–20 million. Senior roles at large corporations or tech firms may exceed IDR 30 million per month. Salaries vary by industry, location (especially Jakarta), and specific expertise (e.g., database administration vs. network security).

**Q: What is the career outlook for Database and Network Professionals in Indonesia?**
A: The demand remains strong due to digital transformation across banking, e-commerce, and government sectors. Opportunities exist in system integration, cloud services, and cybersecurity roles. Professionals with skills in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) and AI-driven database management have an edge. however, automation of routine tasks may shift focus toward strategic planning and security.

**Q: How is AI affecting Database and Network Professionals in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools automate monitoring, performance tuning, and anomaly detection, reducing manual work. For instance, AI-driven database management systems (e.g., Oracle Autonomous Database) handle routine optimizations. Professionals must upskill in AI/ML to manage these systems and interpret insights. Network roles leverage AI for predictive maintenance and threat response, emphasizing decision-making over repetitive tasks.
