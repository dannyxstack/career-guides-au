## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Telecommunications Engineer in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level engineers earn IDR 5–8 million per month, mid-level IDR 10–15 million, and senior engineers (10+ years) IDR 20–30 million. Salaries vary by company, location, and expertise. Major telecom operators and Jakarta-based roles typically offer higher pay.

**Q: What technical skills are essential for Telecommunications Engineers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include 4G/5G network design and optimization, RF planning, fiber optics, and proficiency in tools like iBwave, Atoll, or ASSET. Understanding local regulations and infrastructure challenges is also important. Soft skills such as project management and teamwork are valued.

**Q: How is AI affecting the telecommunications engineering field in Indonesia?**
A: AI is used for network optimization, predictive maintenance, and automated fault detection. Engineers need basic AI/ML knowledge to manage intelligent systems. While AI handles routine tasks, engineers focus on complex problem-solving and strategy. Upskilling in AI is important for career growth.
