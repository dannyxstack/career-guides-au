## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for freight handlers in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary for freight handlers in Indonesia ranges from Rp 3,000,000 to Rp 5,000,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while those with forklift certification or in major ports like Tanjung Priok can earn higher.

**Q: What skills are needed to work as a freight handler in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for lifting heavy items, attention to detail for accurate inventory, basic numeracy for counting and labeling, and ability to operate warehouse equipment like pallet jacks. Forklift certification is highly valued. Good teamwork and communication skills are also essential in busy logistics environments.

**Q: What is the career outlook for freight handlers in Indonesia?**
A: The demand for freight handlers remains steady due to Indonesia's growing e-commerce and logistics sectors. While automation in warehouses may reduce some manual roles, skilled handlers who operate machinery or supervise automated systems will still be needed. Career advancement can lead to roles like warehouse supervisor or logistics coordinator.
