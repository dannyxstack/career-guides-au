## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from IDR 4–7 million per month, while experienced professionals can earn IDR 10–20 million. Those in private practice or with specialized certifications may earn more. Salaries vary by location, with Jakarta offering higher pay, and by sector, with NGOs and international organizations typically paying better than government positions.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a social worker or counsellor in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include conducting client assessments, providing individual or group counselling, developing intervention plans, and connecting clients with community resources. Many professionals work in schools, hospitals, or NGOs, handling cases like family issues, mental health, or child protection. Documentation and interagency coordination are also common.

**Q: How is the career outlook for social work and counselling professionals in Indonesia?**
A: Demand is growing due to increased awareness of mental health and social welfare issues. Government initiatives and NGO programs are expanding services, particularly in post-disaster areas and urban centers. However, competition for top positions exists. Professionals with advanced degrees or multilingual skills have better prospects. The field is expected to grow steadily over the next decade.
