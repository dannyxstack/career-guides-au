## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a medical imaging technician in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn IDR 5–8 million per month; experienced professionals earn IDR 10–15 million. Salaries are higher in private hospitals and major cities like Jakarta. Additional certifications in MRI or CT can increase pay.

**Q: What key skills are required for medical imaging technicians in Indonesia?**
A: Proficiency in operating X-ray, MRI, CT, and ultrasound equipment is essential. Strong knowledge of anatomy, radiation safety, and patient care is needed. Technicians must hold a Diploma in Radiography (D3) and register with the Indonesian Radiographers Association (PARI).

**Q: What is the career outlook for medical imaging technicians in Indonesia?**
A: Demand is growing due to healthcare expansion and technology adoption. Job opportunities exist in hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centers, especially for those skilled in advanced modalities like MRI. Urban areas face competition, but remote regions offer more openings.
