## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sewing machine operator in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level sewing machine operators in Indonesia earn around IDR 2.5-4 million per month, varying by location and factory. Experienced operators can earn IDR 6-8 million monthly, plus overtime. Garment factories in Java often offer piece-rate bonuses, increasing total pay.

**Q: What skills are required to become a sewing machine operator in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in industrial sewing machines, pattern following, attention to detail, basic fabric handling, and time management. Most factories provide on-the-job training. Career advancement to supervisor or quality control roles is possible with experience.

**Q: How is AI impacting sewing machine operators in Indonesia?**
A: AI-driven automation is beginning to handle repetitive sewing tasks, but many factories still rely on manual operators for complex garments and quality control. Operators may need to adapt by learning to work with automated systems, which could change job roles but not eliminate demand entirely.
