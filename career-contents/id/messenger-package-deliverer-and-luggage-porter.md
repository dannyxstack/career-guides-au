## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers and package deliverers in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level workers earn around 2.5 to 4 million IDR per month, while experienced deliverers in major cities like Jakarta can earn 4 to 7 million IDR. Pay often includes per-delivery bonuses. Luggage porters at airports or hotels may receive tips, raising total income. Salaries vary by employer, location, and delivery volume.

**Q: What skills are essential for messengers and package deliverers in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include time management, navigation using maps or GPS apps, physical stamina for carrying loads, and basic customer service. For motorcycle-based delivery, a valid SIM C license and safe riding skills are required. Literacy for reading addresses and using mobile apps is important. Honesty and reliability are highly valued.

**Q: How is AI affecting messengers and package deliverers in Indonesia?**
A: AI-powered route optimization and automated sorting reduce manual planning, but delivery jobs remain human-dependent due to last-mile complexity and traffic. Drone delivery trials are limited. AI may shift tasks toward data-driven dispatching, but job displacement is minimal for now. Workers benefit from improved efficiency and app-based platforms that increase delivery opportunities.
