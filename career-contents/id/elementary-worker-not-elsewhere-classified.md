## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers in Indonesia?**
A: Elementary workers in Indonesia typically earn minimum wage, which varies by region. In Jakarta, the minimum wage is around IDR 4.6 million per month, while in rural areas it may be as low as IDR 2 million. Many work informally without benefits, and actual pay often aligns with the regional minimum.

**Q: What skills are required for elementary worker jobs in Indonesia?**
A: Jobs often require physical stamina, basic literacy, and the ability to follow instructions. For cleaning roles, knowledge of cleaning agents is helpful; for helpers, basic customer service skills. Most training is on-the-job, and employers value reliability and a willingness to learn.

**Q: How is AI affecting elementary worker jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact on most elementary jobs due to their manual nature. In manufacturing, automated systems may replace assembly line workers, but service roles like cleaners are less affected. Some hotels use robotic vacuums, but overall, AI's effect on these jobs remains minimal.
