## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building caretaker in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, building caretakers typically earn between IDR 2.5 to 4.5 million per month, depending on location, experience, and building type. Urban areas like Jakarta offer higher pay, while rural regions pay less. Some employers provide free accommodation or meals as additional benefits.

**Q: What skills are required to become a building caretaker in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include basic maintenance (e.g., plumbing, electrical), cleaning, communication with tenants, and problem-solving. Familiarity with building security systems and simple administrative tasks is helpful. Language skills (Bahasa Indonesia and local dialects) are important for engaging with residents and reporting issues.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting the job of building caretakers in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation are gradually impacting the role, with smart building systems handling tasks like monitoring and access control. However, demand remains steady as human oversight is still needed for repairs, personal interactions, and emergency response. Caretakers who upskill in basic tech maintenance can stay relevant.
