## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical earnings potential for door-to-door salespersons in Indonesia?**
A: Earnings vary widely based on industry and location. In major cities like Jakarta, average monthly income ranges from IDR 3 to 8 million, including base salary and commissions. High performers in sectors like insurance or consumer goods can earn up to IDR 15 million. Many positions are commission-only, so income is directly tied to sales results.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting door-to-door sales roles in Indonesia?**
A: AI is being adopted for lead generation, customer profiling, and route optimization, helping salespersons target prospects more efficiently. However, the core role remains highly interpersonal—AI cannot replace the trust and rapport built in face-to-face interactions. Salespeople who learn to use CRM tools and data analytics gain a competitive edge, but human persuasion is still essential.

**Q: What is the career outlook for door-to-door salespersons in Indonesia?**
A: The demand for door-to-door sales remains steady in sectors like insurance, direct selling, and consumer goods, particularly in suburban and rural areas. Career progression includes roles as sales supervisor, area coordinator, or trainer. Many experienced salespersons transition to independent distributors or retail management. The job offers flexible hours but requires persistence and resilience.
