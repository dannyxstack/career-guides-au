## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for secondary education teachers in Indonesia?**
A: Salaries for secondary teachers in Indonesia vary by school type and location. Public school teachers earn around Rp 3-7 million per month, plus certification allowances. Private school salaries range from Rp 2-10 million, with international schools offering higher pay. Housing and other benefits may be provided, especially in remote areas.

**Q: What is the career outlook for secondary teachers in Indonesia?**
A: Demand for qualified secondary teachers remains steady, particularly in STEM and English subjects. The government's focus on improving education quality, including the Kurikulum Merdeka, creates opportunities. Rural areas often face teacher shortages, so mobility can improve job prospects. Competition is higher in urban centers and reputable schools.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of secondary teachers in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools are increasingly used to automate administrative tasks like grading and attendance, and to personalize learning through adaptive platforms. However, teachers remain essential for classroom management and mentorship. The Indonesian government encourages digital literacy, so teachers need to adapt by integrating AI into lessons while focusing on critical thinking and character building.
