## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a child care worker in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a child care worker in Indonesia ranges from IDR 2,000,000 to IDR 4,000,000, depending on location, experience, and employer. In major cities like Jakarta, pay may be slightly higher, while rural areas offer lower wages. Many workers also receive benefits like meals or accommodation.

**Q: What skills are essential for a child care worker in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include patience, strong communication, basic knowledge of child development, and first aid certification. Creativity in planning activities, empathy, and the ability to maintain a safe environment are critical. While formal education is not always required, a high school diploma and training in early childhood care boost employability.

**Q: How is AI impacting child care workers in Indonesia?**
A: AI has minimal impact on child care work in Indonesia due to the high need for human interaction and emotional support. However, digital tools like scheduling apps and educational software are becoming common, helping workers manage tasks. AI cannot replace the nurturing and responsive care essential for young children.
