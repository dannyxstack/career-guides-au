## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a heavy truck driver in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level drivers earn around IDR 3-5 million per month, while experienced drivers can make IDR 6-10 million. Some companies offer per-trip or kilometer-based pay, which can increase earnings. Overtime and long-haul assignments often come with additional allowances. Salaries vary by region, type of goods, and employer.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a heavy truck driver in Indonesia?**
A: A valid SIM B1 (for vehicles over 3,500 kg) or SIM B2 (for trailers) is required. Many employers also require a driver's professional certificate (Surat Izin Mengemudi Profesional). Practical driving skills, knowledge of traffic laws, and basic vehicle maintenance are essential. Experience with manual transmission and long-distance routes is preferred.

**Q: How is the job outlook for heavy truck drivers in Indonesia with the rise of automation?**
A: Current demand remains strong due to Indonesia's growing logistics and e-commerce sectors. While autonomous trucks are emerging, full automation is unlikely to replace drivers in the near future due to road conditions and regulatory hurdles. Skilled drivers with experience in specialized cargo or inter-island routes will continue to be in demand.
