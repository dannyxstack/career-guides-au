## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for bank tellers in Indonesia?**
A: The typical monthly salary for bank tellers in Indonesia ranges from IDR 3,000,000 to IDR 5,500,000 for entry-level positions, varying by bank and location. With experience, salaries can reach IDR 7,000,000 or more. Additional bonuses and allowances may be included.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting bank teller jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating routine transactions like cash deposits and withdrawals through self-service kiosks and mobile banking. However, bank tellers in Indonesia remain essential for complex services, cash handling, and customer assistance. The role is shifting toward advisory and problem-solving tasks, requiring adaptability to new technologies.

**Q: What skills are required to become a bank teller in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include numeracy, attention to detail, customer service, and proficiency in Indonesian and English. Basic computer literacy and knowledge of banking software are essential. Many banks require a high school diploma or associate degree. On-the-job training covers cash handling, transaction processing, and compliance procedures.
