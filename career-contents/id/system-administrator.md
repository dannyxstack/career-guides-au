## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Systems Administrators in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, entry-level Systems Administrators earn around IDR 5-8 million per month, while mid-level roles range from IDR 8-15 million. Senior positions can exceed IDR 20 million, depending on certifications, experience, and industry (e.g., finance or tech).

**Q: What skills are essential for Systems Administrators in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include expertise in Linux/Windows Server administration, networking (TCP/IP, DNS), virtualization (VMware, Hyper-V), and cloud services (AWS, Azure). Scripting (Python, Bash) and security knowledge are increasingly important. Certifications like RHCE, CCNA, or MCSE boost employability.

**Q: How is AI impacting Systems Administrator roles in Indonesia?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like monitoring, patching, and incident response, reducing manual workload. However, strategic oversight, complex troubleshooting, and cybersecurity remain human-driven. Administrators must upskill in AI tools and automation to stay relevant, as AI complements rather than replaces the role.
