## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, building structure cleaners typically earn between IDR 3 million and IDR 5 million per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Workers in major cities like Jakarta may earn on the higher end, while those in smaller towns earn less. Overtime and project-based bonuses can increase total pay.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a building structure cleaner in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for climbing and repetitive tasks, attention to safety when working at heights, and ability to use cleaning equipment like pressure washers and scaffolding. Familiarity with basic building maintenance and ability to work in a team are also valued. No formal education is required, but on-the-job training is common.

**Q: What is the career outlook for building structure cleaners in Indonesia?**
A: The demand for building structure cleaners remains stable due to ongoing construction and maintenance of high-rise buildings. However, AI-powered cleaning robots and drones may eventually replace some manual tasks, especially for repetitive exterior cleaning. Workers who adapt to new technology and specialize in complex structures will have better long-term prospects.
