## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for visual artists in Indonesia?**
A: Visual artists in Indonesia typically earn between IDR 3-8 million per month for salaried positions. Freelancers may earn per project, ranging from IDR 500,000 to 2 million per piece, depending on experience and client. Top artists with established reputations can earn significantly more, but incomes vary widely and are often inconsistent.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a visual artist in Indonesia?**
A: A visual artist's day in Indonesia often involves creating artwork in a studio or at home, managing client communications, and planning exhibitions. Many also handle marketing on social media and collaborate with galleries. Non-art tasks like sourcing materials and bookkeeping are common. Freelancers balance multiple projects, while employed artists may work in design firms or studios.

**Q: How is AI impacting visual artists in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools like image generators are affecting visual artists in Indonesia by increasing competition for commercial work, such as illustration and graphic design. However, many artists use AI to brainstorm ideas or create drafts, saving time. Unique, handcrafted art and personal creativity remain valued in galleries and high-end commissions, so skill differentiation is key.
