## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a window cleaner in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary for a window cleaner in Indonesia ranges from IDR 2.5 million to IDR 5 million, depending on experience and location, with Jakarta offering higher pay. Most work on a contract or per-job basis, and earnings can increase with additional services like high-rise building maintenance.

**Q: What skills are required to become a window cleaner in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina, balance for working at heights, and attention to detail. Familiarity with cleaning equipment and safety harnesses is important. In Indonesia, ability to work in tropical heat and humidity is essential. Basic customer service skills help in building a client base.

**Q: How is AI and technology impacting window cleaning jobs in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact on window cleaning in Indonesia, as most work requires manual dexterity in complex environments. However, robotic cleaners are slowly being used for simple glass surfaces in modern buildings. Overall, demand remains stable due to growth in urban construction and commercial real estate.
