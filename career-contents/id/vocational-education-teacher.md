## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for vocational education teachers in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, vocational education teachers earn between IDR 5-15 million per month, varying with experience, certification, and institution type. Public school teachers have standardized pay scales, while private schools offer flexibility. Additional income from tutorials or industry projects is common, and benefits include health insurance and pension contributions.

**Q: What is the career outlook for vocational education teachers in Indonesia?**
A: The outlook is positive due to government emphasis on vocational education under the 'Merdeka Belajar' policy, which encourages industry partnerships. Teachers can advance to senior roles, curriculum development, or supervision. Demand is high in fields like engineering, hospitality, and IT, but competition is growing.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a vocational education teacher in Indonesia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in the vocational field and a teaching certification (PPG) are required, along with 2-3 years of industry experience. Key skills include hands-on instruction, curriculum adaptation, and digital literacy. AI tools assist in lesson planning but practical mentoring remains essential, so continuous skill updates are vital.
