## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Indonesia?**
A: Monthly salaries for craft workers in Indonesia vary widely by trade and region, typically ranging from IDR 3 million to IDR 8 million. Experience, specialization, and location (e.g., Jakarta vs. rural areas) significantly influence pay. Many work informally or on contract, so income can fluctuate.

**Q: What skills are essential for craft and related workers in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, technical expertise specific to their craft (e.g., woodworking, metalworking, textile production), and safe use of hand and power tools. Problem-solving and creativity are valued, as workers often adapt traditional methods to modern demands. Basic numeracy and communication skills are also helpful.

**Q: How is AI impacting craft and related workers in Indonesia?**
A: AI and automation have limited direct impact on most hands-on craft roles, but advanced tools like computer-aided design and automated cutting machines are changing production. Craft workers may need to learn new digital skills to stay competitive. However, demand for bespoke, artisanal products sustains the value of traditional craftsmanship.
