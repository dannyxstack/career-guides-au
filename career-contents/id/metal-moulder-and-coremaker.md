## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for metal moulders and coremakers in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary for metal moulders and coremakers in Indonesia ranges from IDR 4,000,000 to IDR 7,000,000 depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers earn around IDR 3,500,000, while experienced workers in industrial areas like Bekasi or Surabaya can earn up to IDR 9,000,000. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total pay.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a metal moulder or coremaker in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include pattern reading, knowledge of molding materials (sand, resin, or plaster), and operating molding machines. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and basic math for measurements are essential. Many employers require vocational training or a diploma in foundry technology. Familiarity with safety procedures and quality control is also important.

**Q: What is the career outlook for metal moulders and coremakers in Indonesia?**
A: The demand for metal moulders and coremakers in Indonesia is stable, driven by automotive, machinery, and construction industries. Automation is gradually reducing low-skill jobs, but skilled workers who can operate modern CNC-equipped foundries remain needed. Career progression includes becoming a patternmaker, supervisor, or foundry technician. Continuous learning of new technologies is advisable.
