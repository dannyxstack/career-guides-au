## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shop supervisor in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, shop supervisors typically earn between IDR 4 million to IDR 8 million per month, depending on location, store type, and experience. Entry-level positions start around IDR 4-5 million, while those in major cities like Jakarta or with large retailers can earn up to IDR 10 million. Benefits such as bonuses, transport allowances, and health insurance are common in formal retail.

**Q: What are the typical daily tasks of a shop supervisor in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include overseeing store operations, managing staff schedules, ensuring customer service standards, checking inventory levels, processing sales reports, and handling customer complaints. Shop supervisors also train new employees, coordinate with suppliers, and maintain store cleanliness and visual merchandising. The role requires multitasking, leadership, and attention to detail.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of shop supervisors in Indonesia?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks such as inventory tracking and sales forecasting, allowing supervisors to focus more on customer engagement and team management. However, human skills like problem-solving and interpersonal communication remain essential. Supervisors who embrace technology for efficiency will have better career prospects, while those relying solely on manual processes may face challenges.
