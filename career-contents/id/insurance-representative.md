## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for insurance representatives in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level insurance representatives earn around IDR 4-6 million monthly base salary, plus commissions and bonuses. Experienced representatives can make IDR 8-15 million or more, depending on sales performance. Top performers may exceed IDR 20 million per month. Salary varies by company, product type, and individual targets.

**Q: What key skills are essential for insurance representatives in Indonesia?**
A: Strong communication and persuasion skills are vital for explaining complex policies to clients. Indonesian representatives must be fluent in Bahasa Indonesia and often local dialects. Sales acumen, networking abilities, and persistence are crucial. Basic financial literacy and knowledge of insurance regulations (OJK guidelines) are also required. Many companies provide product training.

**Q: How is AI impacting insurance representatives in Indonesia?**
A: AI automates administrative tasks like policy issuance, claims processing, and client data management, allowing reps to focus on sales and relationships. Chatbots handle initial inquiries, but complex advice still requires human expertise. Reps must now be digitally savvy—using CRM tools and analytics to identify leads. AI is a complement, not a replacement, in Indonesia's relationship-driven market.
