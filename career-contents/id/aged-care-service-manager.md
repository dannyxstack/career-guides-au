## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Aged Care Services Manager in Indonesia?**
A: The monthly salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Indonesia ranges from IDR 8 million to IDR 20 million, depending on experience, facility size, and location. Senior managers in top-tier facilities in Jakarta or Surabaya may earn higher, while entry-level roles in smaller facilities start at the lower end.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for an Aged Care Services Manager in Indonesia?**
A: A typical day includes supervising care staff, coordinating resident admissions and care plans, ensuring compliance with health regulations, managing budgets, and liaising with families. They also handle emergencies, conduct staff training, and oversee daily operations like meal services and activity programs.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Aged Care Services Managers in Indonesia?**
A: AI is streamlining administrative tasks like scheduling, record-keeping, and basic health monitoring, allowing managers to focus more on quality care and staff management. However, human oversight remains crucial for personalized care. Adaptation to new technologies is increasingly valued in the role.
