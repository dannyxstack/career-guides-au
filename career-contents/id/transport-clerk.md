## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Transport Clerks in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level Transport Clerks earn around IDR 4-6 million per month, while experienced clerks in logistics hubs like Jakarta or Surabaya can earn IDR 8-12 million. Salaries vary by company size, location, and responsibilities such as managing export documentation or coordinating fleets.

**Q: What are the main day-to-day responsibilities of a Transport Clerk in Indonesia?**
A: Transport Clerks in Indonesia process shipping documents, track deliveries, coordinate with drivers and warehousing staff, and ensure compliance with local customs or inter-island regulations. They update transport schedules, handle customer inquiries, and reconcile shipment records using logistics software.

**Q: How is AI or automation affecting Transport Clerks in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools automate document processing, route optimization, and delivery tracking, reducing manual data entry. However, Transport Clerks are still needed to handle exceptions, verify customs papers (especially for inter-island trade), and coordinate with partners. Upskilling in logistics software and data analysis helps clerks adapt to these changes.
