## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Indonesia?**
A: Income varies widely by region and season. Most subsistence farmers earn between IDR 2 million and IDR 5 million per month, roughly USD 130–330. Earnings depend on crop yields, livestock sales, and market access. Many also consume part of their produce, so cash income may be lower. Government subsidies and cooperative programs can supplement income.

**Q: What key skills are required for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farming in Indonesia?**
A: Farmers need knowledge of crop rotation, pest control, and soil management for diverse crops like rice, corn, and vegetables. Animal husbandry skills, including feeding, breeding, and basic veterinary care for chickens, goats, or cows, are essential. Practical skills in water management, tool maintenance, and simple financial record-keeping help optimize production and reduce costs.

**Q: How is AI impacting the career outlook for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmers in Indonesia?**
A: AI adoption is minimal in subsistence farming. While technologies like weather forecasting apps and pest detection tools emerge, most farmers rely on traditional methods. AI may improve efficiency through precision agriculture in the future, but adoption faces barriers like cost and digital literacy. The outlook remains stable, with demand for food production ensuring ongoing need for manual farming skills.
