**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Moving construction materials (replaced by AGVs/drones).
- Basic excavation and earthworks (replaced by unmanned excavators)
- On-site cleaning and debris sorting (replaced by cleaning robots)
- Simple bricklaying and plastering (replaced by automatic brick-laying machines/spraying machines)
- Repetitive rebar tying (replaced by tying robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to read construction drawings, improving accuracy.
- Operate semi-automated tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers, laser levels)
- Maintenance and debugging of automated equipment (e.g., drones, robots)
- Report progress and receive instructions via digital platforms
- Using BIM software for collaborative work and problem-solving

**Human moat:**

- Judgment in unstructured environments (e.g., complex terrain, unexpected situations)
- Fine hand-eye coordination (e.g., installing irregular components).
- Team on-site communication and emergency coordination
- Experience in safety supervision and accident prevention
- Mastery of traditional crafts (e.g., stonemasonry, special plastering)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building construction labourer in Indonesia?**
A: In Indonesia, building construction labourers typically earn between 3 to 5 million Indonesian rupiah per month, depending on the region and project complexity. Daily wages range from 100,000 to 200,000 IDR. Overtime and skill level can increase pay. Salaries are often paid weekly or daily, with experienced labourers earning more.

**Q: What skills are essential for building construction labourers in Indonesia?**
A: Essential skills include basic construction knowledge, physical stamina, ability to use hand tools (shovels, hammers, wheelbarrows) and operate simple machinery. Familiarity with safety practices and teamwork are crucial. On-the-job training is common, but prior experience in tasks like concrete mixing, bricklaying, or scaffolding is advantageous.

**Q: What is the career outlook for building construction labourers in Indonesia?**
A: The outlook is stable due to ongoing infrastructure and housing projects. Demand remains strong in urban areas like Jakarta and Surabaya. Labourers can advance to skilled trades or supervisory roles with experience. However, automation may affect repetitive tasks, so upskilling in modern techniques is beneficial for long-term job security.
