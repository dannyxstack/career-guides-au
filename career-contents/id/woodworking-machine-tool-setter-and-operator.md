## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for woodworking machine tool setters and operators in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around 3–4 million IDR per month, while experienced setters or those working with advanced CNC machinery can earn 5–7 million IDR. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in industrial areas like Jepara or Surabaya. Overtime and piece-rate work may increase earnings.

**Q: What key skills are required for woodworking machine setters and operators in Indonesia?**
A: Operators need to read technical drawings, set up and adjust machine tools (such as saws, planers, and routers), and perform basic maintenance. Precision, attention to detail, and knowledge of wood properties are essential. Familiarity with CNC programming is increasingly valued, along with safety awareness to prevent accidents.

**Q: What is the career outlook for woodworking machine operators in Indonesia?**
A: The demand remains steady due to Indonesia's strong furniture and wood product industries, especially in Java and Sumatra. While automation is growing, skilled operators who can manage both traditional and CNC machines are still needed. Career progression includes becoming a senior setter or production supervisor with experience.
