## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a sweeper in Indonesia?**
A: The average monthly salary ranges from IDR 2.5 to 4 million, depending on the region, employer (municipal vs. private), and experience. Municipal workers often receive minimum wage, while private companies may pay slightly more, especially in urban areas like Jakarta.

**Q: What skills are needed to work as a sweeper in Indonesia?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for long hours of manual work, ability to use basic cleaning tools like brooms, dustpans, and trash collectors, and punctuality. Literacy is helpful for reading schedules or safety instructions, but not always required. Reliability and a strong work ethic are valued.

**Q: Will AI or automation replace sweepers in Indonesia?**
A: Automation is unlikely to replace sweepers significantly in the near future, as most cleaning tasks in public spaces, markets, and streets require adaptability to irregular conditions. However, some smart city initiatives may introduce automated sweeping machines in specific areas, potentially reducing demand for manual labor over the long term.
