## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for employment agents and contractors in Indonesia?**
A: Entry-level agents earn around IDR 60–80 million annually, while senior contractors can earn IDR 150–250 million plus commissions. The industry typically offers performance bonuses tied to successful placements. Freelance contractors may have variable income depending on project volume.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for an employment agent in Indonesia?**
A: Daily tasks include sourcing candidates via job portals and social media, screening resumes, conducting initial interviews, coordinating client meetings, negotiating terms between employers and candidates, and maintaining a database of talent. Agents also visit local companies to understand staffing needs and promote their services.

**Q: How is AI affecting employment agents and contractors in Indonesia?**
A: AI tools automate resume screening and initial candidate matching, reducing routine tasks. However, human judgment remains crucial for cultural fit and negotiation. Agents now focus more on relationship-building and strategic consulting. AI is unlikely to replace the role entirely but will shift responsibilities toward higher-value activities.
