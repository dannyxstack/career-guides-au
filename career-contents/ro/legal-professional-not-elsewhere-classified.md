## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, specialization, and employer, ranging from approximately 3,000 to 8,000 RON (600–1,600 EUR) monthly for mid-level roles. Senior positions in international firms or corporate settings can exceed 15,000 RON (3,000 EUR). Entry-level roles typically start around 2,500 RON (500 EUR).

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign legal professionals to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and long-stay visa (D/AM) sponsored by a Romanian employer. For highly qualified roles, the EU Blue Card is available, requiring a work contract of at least one year, a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage, and recognized qualifications. All applications are processed through the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What are the common career paths for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: These professionals often work as in-house legal counsel in corporations, compliance officers, or legal consultants in specialized fields like intellectual property, data protection, or international trade. Progression may lead to senior legal advisor, head of legal department, or partnership in consulting firms. Additional certifications or advanced degrees enhance prospects.
