## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for undertakers and embalmers in Romania?**
A: According to labor market data, undertakers and embalmers in Romania typically earn a gross monthly salary between 2,500 and 4,500 RON (about 500–900 EUR). Pay varies with experience, employer, and location, with higher rates in major cities like Bucharest. Additional income may come from overtime or specialized services.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as undertakers in Romania through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Romania as undertakers via a long-stay work visa (D/AS) for employment. A valid job offer and work permit are required. The EU Blue Card is also available for highly qualified workers, but it requires a high salary threshold (typically above 4,000 EUR/month), which is rarely met in this field. Consult the Romanian Immigration Office for details.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an undertaker or embalmer in Romania?**
A: In Romania, undertakers and embalmers typically need a vocational certificate or diploma in funeral services from an accredited school, plus a license from the Ministry of Health or the National Authority for Qualifications. Practical experience through an apprenticeship is often required. Embalmers may need additional training and registration with the Romanian College of Physicians for medical tasks.
