## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Transport Conductor in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Transport Conductor in Romania ranges from approximately 3,000 to 4,500 RON (€600–€900), depending on experience, employer, and region. Public sector roles may offer additional benefits. Overtime and shift differentials can increase total earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Transport Conductors in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5× the national average gross salary). For Transport Conductors, this route is rarely applicable. Instead, non-EU citizens need a work visa and a valid work permit obtained by the employer from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Transport Conductor in Romania?**
A: Transport Conductors in Romania assist passengers with boarding, validate tickets, announce stops, and ensure fare compliance. They often handle cash or card payments on buses, trams, or trains. Additional duties include providing travel information, reporting delays, and cooperating with drivers to maintain schedule and safety.
