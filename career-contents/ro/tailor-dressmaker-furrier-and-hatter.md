## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for tailors and dressmakers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for tailors and dressmakers in Romania vary by experience and location. The national minimum wage is approximately 3,000 RON gross per month. Entry-level workers may earn around minimum wage, while experienced tailors in cities like Bucharest can earn 4,000–5,000 RON gross. Earnings also depend on employer type (e.g., high-end fashion houses pay more than factories).

**Q: How can a non-EU tailor obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU tailors need a job offer from a Romanian employer, who must apply for a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. After approval, the worker applies for a long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers; tailors may qualify if they have a higher education degree or at least five years of relevant professional experience.

**Q: What are the career prospects for tailors in Romania?**
A: Tailors in Romania can work in bespoke tailoring shops, fashion ateliers, or textile manufacturing. With experience, they may become master tailors, pattern makers, or open their own businesses. The demand for custom-made clothing remains stable, though competition from ready-to-wear brands exists. Continuous training in modern techniques and sustainable fashion can enhance career opportunities.
