## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fashion models in Romania?**
A: Salaries for new fashion models in Romania typically range from 2,000 to 5,000 RON per month (approximately €400–€1,000). Established models with agency representation can earn 10,000–20,000 RON per month for high-profile campaigns, runway shows, or commercial shoots. Payment is often project-based, with additional fees for usage rights.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU models to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU models can work in Romania via a standard work permit, requiring a Romanian employer to prove the role cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a recognized degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national gross average, but it may not apply to most modeling positions. Always consult the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate for current requirements.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for models in Romania beyond traditional runway work?**
A: Models in Romania can pursue commercial print (catalogs, e-commerce), fashion showroom work, fit modeling for local apparel brands, automotive or product shoots, and promotional events. The rise of e-commerce has increased demand for e-retail modeling. Some models transition into acting, brand ambassadorship, or fashion blogging.
