## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly income for a taxi driver in Romania?**
A: Income varies by city and hours worked. In Bucharest, taxi drivers can earn between 2,500 and 4,500 RON net per month after fuel and rental costs. Drivers who own their vehicle and work longer hours may earn more, but competition and platform fees (e.g., Uber, Bolt) affect take-home pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as taxi drivers in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Romania is reserved for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Taxi driving typically does not meet these requirements. Non-EU citizens can apply for a standard work permit and long-stay visa through the single permit procedure, provided a Romanian employer sponsors them.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements for taxi drivers in Romania?**
A: To drive a taxi legally, you need a valid category B driving license held for at least one year, a clean criminal record, and a medical certificate. You must obtain a taxi driver authorization from the local town hall, which involves passing a test on local geography and regulations. The authorization is renewed periodically.
