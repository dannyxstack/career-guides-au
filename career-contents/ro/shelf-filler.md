## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Romania?**
A: Shelf fillers in Romania usually earn the national minimum wage or slightly above, averaging between 3,000 and 3,500 RON per month before tax. Pay varies by employer, city, and experience. Some large retailers offer additional benefits like meal vouchers or transport allowances.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a shelf filler in Romania under the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Romania is reserved for highly skilled workers with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Shelf filler positions do not meet this requirement. Non-EU nationals can instead apply for a standard long-stay work visa and a work permit, typically arranged by the employer.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a shelf filler in Romania?**
A: Shelf fillers restock merchandise, arrange products attractively, rotate stock to ensure freshness, and maintain cleanliness in aisles. They may assist customers in locating items and handle price labeling. The job often requires standing for long periods and working shifts, including evenings and weekends.
