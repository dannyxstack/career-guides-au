## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Romania?**
A: In Romania, meter readers and vending-machine collectors typically earn between 2,500 and 4,000 RON net per month, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage (3,300 RON gross in 2024), while experienced workers can earn higher with bonuses. Overtime and night shifts may increase pay.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU nationals to work as meter readers or vending-machine collectors in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Romanian work visa for employed persons, requiring a job offer and labor market test. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers requiring higher education and salary thresholds; meter readers typically do not qualify. Other routes include intra-corporate transfer or seasonal work permits for short-term assignments. All applications are processed by the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Romania?**
A: Career advancement may include senior meter reader roles, team leader, or supervisor positions. With additional training, workers can move into utility inspection, data analysis, or maintenance roles. Some transition to administrative jobs within utility companies. Continuous education in energy or logistics can open paths to higher paying technical or managerial jobs.
