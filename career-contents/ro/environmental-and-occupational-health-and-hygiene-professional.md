## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions typically earn around 3,000–4,500 RON per month gross. Mid-level professionals earn 5,000–7,500 RON, while senior experts can earn 8,000–12,000 RON or more. Public sector roles may be lower than private industry.

**Q: Can non-EU professionals work as Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a qualifying job offer for at least 1 year, a recognized higher education degree (or 5 years of relevant experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. The employer must obtain a work permit first.

**Q: What are the career prospects for Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Romania?**
A: Demand is growing due to EU regulations and increased workplace safety awareness. Professionals work in manufacturing, construction, mining, and consulting. Opportunities exist in government agencies like the National Public Health Institute, private companies, and international organizations. Continuing education and certifications enhance advancement.
