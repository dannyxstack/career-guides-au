## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for odd-job persons in Romania?**
A: Odd-job persons in Romania are typically paid per task or hourly, often at or slightly above the national minimum wage (which is approximately 3,300 RON per month in 2024 for a full-time schedule). Actual earnings vary widely based on the type of work, location, and demand. For part-time or irregular work, hourly rates range from 20 to 40 RON.

**Q: Can foreign odd-job workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for odd-job persons because it requires a highly skilled position. Non-EU nationals can apply for a Romanian long-stay visa for employment (work permit), which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled by a Romanian or EU citizen. The process involves approval from the Inspectorate General for Immigration.

**Q: What skills and opportunities exist for odd-job persons in Romania?**
A: Odd-job persons in Romania perform various manual tasks like cleaning, moving goods, gardening, or simple repairs. No formal education is required, but physical stamina and basic tool knowledge help. Opportunities are often informal or through agencies. Romanian language skills are beneficial. Career progression is limited, but experience can lead to specialized roles in maintenance or construction.
