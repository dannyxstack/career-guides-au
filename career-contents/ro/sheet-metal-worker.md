## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a sheet metal worker in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sheet metal workers in Romania ranges between 3,500 and 5,500 RON (approximately 700–1,100 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 RON, while experienced workers in industrial hubs like Bucharest or Timișoara can earn up to 6,000 RON. Overtime and specialized skills may increase earnings.

**Q: Can sheet metal workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU sheet metal workers can apply for a Romanian work visa or EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a higher education degree or equivalent experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the gross average wage. Both routes are managed by the Romanian General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a sheet metal worker in Romania?**
A: Sheet metal workers in Romania typically need a vocational school diploma in metalworking or a related field. Apprenticeships or on-the-job training are common. Certification in welding or CNC operation can improve prospects. For regulated work, a professional competence certificate from the Ministry of Labor is required. No formal registration with a professional body is needed, but employers may require specific certifications for safety or specialized tasks.
