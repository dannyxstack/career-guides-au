## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shopkeeper in Romania?**
A: Shopkeeper salaries in Romania vary widely by region and shop type. Many earn the national minimum wage (around 3,700 RON gross per month in 2025), though experienced staff in larger stores or Bucharest may earn 4,500–6,000 RON gross. Most small independent shops offer minimum wage or slightly above.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a shopkeeper in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-term work visa and work permit, processed by the Romanian General Inspectorate for Immigration (IGI) based on an employer application. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, making it unsuitable for shopkeepers. Standard employment permits are the appropriate route.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a shopkeeper in Romania?**
A: No formal qualifications are required, but employers often prefer at least high school education. Key skills include customer service, basic math for cash handling, and, in international shops, English proficiency. Knowledge of relevant laws (e.g., consumer protection) is helpful. Many shopkeepers learn on the job, particularly in small family-run businesses.
