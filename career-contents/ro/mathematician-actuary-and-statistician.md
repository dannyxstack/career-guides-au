## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians in Romania?**
A: In Romania, mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians earn a gross monthly salary ranging from approximately 5,000 to 10,000 RON for mid-level positions, with senior roles reaching up to 15,000 RON or more. Salaries vary by industry, with higher pay in finance, insurance, and IT sectors. Entry-level positions typically start around 3,500 RON.

**Q: Can non-EU mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians work in Romania via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can work in Romania through a standard work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary above the national threshold (approximately twice the average gross salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship and application to the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians in Romania?**
A: In Romania, mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians are in demand in banking, insurance, IT, research and academia. The growing tech sector offers roles in data science and analytics. Actuaries are particularly sought after in insurance and pension funds. Proficiency in English and programming languages like R, Python or SQL significantly boosts career prospects.
