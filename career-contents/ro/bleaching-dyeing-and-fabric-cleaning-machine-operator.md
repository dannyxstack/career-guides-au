## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operator in Romania?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around 3,500–4,200 RON (€700–€850) gross monthly. With experience, salaries can reach 5,000 RON (€1,000). Many textile plants are in Moldavia, Oltenia, or around Bucharest. Pay often includes shift differentials for night or weekend work.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operator in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a highly skilled job with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €18,000/year). This operator role typically does not meet that threshold. Instead, a standard work permit (aviz de muncă) is the official route, requiring employer sponsorship and a positive labour market test.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for this role in Romania?**
A: Operators set up and control machines that bleach, dye or clean fabrics. Work is often in textile factories with exposure to chemicals and noise. Standard shifts are 8 hours, but many plants run 24/7 with rotating schedules. Physical stamina is required. Knowledge of colour chemistry and machine maintenance is valued for advancement.
