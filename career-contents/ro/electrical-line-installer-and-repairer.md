## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electrical line installer in Romania?**
A: Salaries for electrical line installers in Romania vary with experience and employer. Entry-level positions typically earn around the national minimum wage for skilled workers, while experienced installers can earn significantly more, with average gross monthly salaries reported between 4,000 and 7,000 RON. Overtime and hazard pay often increase total compensation.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as an electrical line installer in Romania through a work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work in Romania as electrical line installers by obtaining a work visa. They need a job offer from a Romanian employer, who applies for a work permit on their behalf. The permit is issued based on labor market tests and quotas. After obtaining the work permit, the worker applies for a long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree.

**Q: What training is required to become an electrical line installer in Romania?**
A: In Romania, electrical line installers typically complete a vocational school program in electrical engineering or a related field, followed by an apprenticeship. Certification from the National Authority for Energy Regulation (ANRE) is required for high voltage work. Continuous training is needed to stay updated with safety regulations and new technologies. On-the-job training is common.
