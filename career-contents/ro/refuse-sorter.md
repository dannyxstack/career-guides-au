## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for refuse sorters in Romania?**
A: The average net monthly salary for refuse sorters in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 3,000 RON (approximately 500–600 EUR). Pay varies by employer, location, and experience. Many positions pay the national minimum wage, with some companies offering bonuses for efficiency or overtime.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for refuse sorters in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit (Autorizație de muncă) obtained by the employer. The EU Blue Card is not available for refuse sorting as it requires a high-skilled job. Instead, a long-term visa for employment (D visa) is used. The employer must prove no Romanian candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for refuse sorters in Romania?**
A: Experienced refuse sorters can advance to team leader, shift supervisor, or waste sorting technician. With training, they may move into recycling plant operation or environmental compliance roles. Some workers transition to waste management coordination or quality control positions.
