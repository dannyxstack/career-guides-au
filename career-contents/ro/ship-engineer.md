**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine voyage data recording and report generation
- Basic meteorological route planning and optimization

- Cargo loading calculation and balance adjustment
- Communication relay and standard message forwarding

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted navigation decisions to improve flight safety and fuel efficiency
- Real-time weather and sea condition forecasting, supporting dynamic adjustment of speed and route
- Automated fault diagnosis and predictive maintenance to reduce downtime
- Intelligent Cargo Monitoring Systems to Improve Stowage Accuracy and Safety

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a ships’ engineer in Romania?**
A: Salaries for ships’ engineers in Romania depend on rank and vessel type. Junior engineers earn around €2,000–€3,000 net per month, while chief engineers on large vessels can earn €4,000–€6,000 net. These figures vary by employer, experience, and contract terms.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU ships’ engineers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU engineers can apply for a national long-stay visa for employment with a Romanian job offer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers holding a higher education degree and a job paying at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What is the typical career progression for a ships’ engineer in Romania?**
A: Career progression typically starts as an assistant engineer or junior engineer, advancing to third engineer, second engineer, and finally chief engineer. Each step requires sea time, exams, and certifications. Romanian engineers often work on international vessels, gaining experience that leads to senior ranks.
