## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Education Managers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Education Managers in Romania ranges from 8,000 to 12,000 RON, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and institution size. Public school principals typically earn less, while managers in private universities or training centers may earn higher salaries, often including performance bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Education Managers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU Education Managers can work in Romania via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if the employer demonstrates the role cannot be filled locally. Both routes require valid qualifications and a labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Education Manager in Romania?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in education management or a related field is required, along with several years of teaching or administrative experience. For public schools, candidates must hold a teaching certification. Private institutions may accept equivalent experience. Continuous professional development is highly valued.
