## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry workers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for forestry and related workers in Romania ranges between 2,500 and 3,500 RON (€500–€700), depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced workers in state-owned or large private forestry companies can earn up to 4,500 RON (€900). Overtime and seasonal bonuses can supplement income.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for forestry workers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU forestry workers can obtain a Romanian work permit (aviz de muncă) or an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers). The employer must prove a labor shortage. Workers need a valid job offer, meet qualification requirements, and apply at the Romanian embassy. The Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold (roughly 1.5× the average gross salary). Permanent residence is possible after 5 years.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for forestry workers in Romania?**
A: Forestry workers can advance to roles like forest ranger, technician, or forester after gaining experience and completing vocational training or a degree in forestry. Certification from the National Forest Administration (Romsilva) or private companies enhances prospects. Opportunities exist in conservation, timber management, or supervisory positions, with potential for higher pay and job stability.
