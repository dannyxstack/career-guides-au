## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for psychologists in Romania ranges from 4,000 to 8,000 RON, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions typically start around 3,500 RON, while experienced clinical or organizational psychologists can earn up to 12,000 RON. Salaries in private practice vary widely.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for psychologists to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU psychologists can work in Romania via a work visa (long-stay visa for employment) or the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher-education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What are the typical career paths for psychologists in Romania?**
A: Psychologists in Romania work in clinical, educational, organizational, or forensic settings. Career progression includes specialization through postgraduate training, obtaining certification from the Romanian College of Psychologists, and advancing to roles like senior psychologist, head of department, or private practice. Many also pursue academic or research careers.
