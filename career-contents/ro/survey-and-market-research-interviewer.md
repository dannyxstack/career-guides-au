## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for survey and market research interviewers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, survey and market research interviewers typically earn between 2,500 and 3,500 RON gross per month (approx. 500–700 EUR). Pay often varies by employer, region, and hours worked, with some positions offering bonuses or commission. Entry-level roles may start near the minimum wage, while experienced interviewers or those with language skills can earn higher rates.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for survey interviewers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU workers need a work visa (employment visa) for Romania, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles, but survey interviewing typically does not meet the salary or education thresholds. The most common route is a single work permit under Romanian law, processed by the Immigration Inspectorate after a labor market test.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for survey interviewers in Romania?**
A: Survey interviewers can advance to team leader, fieldwork coordinator, or data analyst roles with experience. Some move into market research management or specialize in qualitative research. Larger firms in Bucharest and Cluj-Napoca offer structured career paths. Additional skills in statistics, foreign languages, or project management improve prospects. Many interviewers also transition to related fields like sales or customer insights.
