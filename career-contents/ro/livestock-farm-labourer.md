## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Romania?**
A: Typical earnings range from the national minimum wage (around 3,000 RON gross per month in 2024) to slightly above, depending on experience, farm size, and type of livestock. Overtime and bonuses may apply, but overall pay is modest compared to other sectors.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for livestock farm labourers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens require a work permit obtained by the employer from the Romanian General Inspectorate for Immigration, followed by a long-stay work visa at a Romanian consulate. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for this occupation, as it requires a higher education and a high-skilled salary threshold.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for livestock farm labourers in Romania?**
A: With experience, workers can progress to senior farm worker, livestock foreman, or farm supervisor roles. Some may specialize in animal health or breeding. Further training or certification in agricultural management can open pathways to farm management positions.
