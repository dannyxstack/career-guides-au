## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Sewing Machine Operator in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Sewing Machine Operator in Romania is between 2,500 and 3,500 RON (approximately 500–700 EUR), depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions start around 2,000 RON, while experienced operators can earn up to 4,000 RON. Overtime and piece-rate pay may increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a Sewing Machine Operator in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Romania through a work permit (permis de muncă) or the EU Blue Card. For skilled jobs, the Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Sewing Machine Operator roles typically qualify for a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the career prospects for a Sewing Machine Operator in Romania?**
A: Career progression includes advancing to senior operator, team leader, or quality inspector. With additional training, operators can move into pattern making, garment design, or production management. The textile industry in Romania has a strong demand for skilled workers, offering stable employment, especially in regions like Bacău, Iași, and Suceava.
