## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an announcer on radio or television in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level announcers typically earn between 2,500 and 4,000 RON per month. Experienced professionals at national stations can earn 5,000–8,000 RON, with top presenters reaching 10,000 RON or more. Pay varies by employer location (Bucharest vs. regional) and media type (radio vs. TV).

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as announcers in Romania via an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a highly qualified job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times Romania's average gross salary. Alternatively, a national long-stay visa for employment (work permit) is available for announcer roles, subject to a labor market test. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a radio or TV announcer in Romania?**
A: Most Romanian employers prefer a degree in journalism, communications, or performing arts. Fluency in Romanian is essential; English is a plus for international media. Practical experience through internships or university radio stations is highly valued. Voice training, diction, and on-camera presence are key skills.
