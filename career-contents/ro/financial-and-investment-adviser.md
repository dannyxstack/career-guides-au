## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Financial and Investment Adviser in Romania?**
A: Salaries for financial and investment advisers in Romania typically range from 4,000 to 8,000 RON per month gross, depending on experience, client portfolio, and the employing institution. Senior advisers or those with strong performance can earn significantly more, often exceeding 10,000 RON monthly.

**Q: Can non-EU financial advisers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Romania as financial advisers via the EU Blue Card or a national long-stay work visa. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (adjusted annually) and relevant qualifications. Alternatively, employers can apply for a standard work permit through the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Financial and Investment Adviser in Romania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in finance, economics, or business is required. Many employers prefer Certified Financial Planner (CFP) or Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) credentials. Registration with the Financial Supervisory Authority (ASF) is mandatory for offering investment advice, and ongoing professional development is expected.
