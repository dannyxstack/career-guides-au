## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a companion or valet in Romania?**
A: In Romania, the average monthly salary for companions and valets ranges from 2,500 to 4,000 RON (net), depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start lower, while those working for high-net-worth individuals or luxury hotels can earn higher. These figures are approximate and subject to change.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign companion or valet to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals seeking to work as companions or valets in Romania typically need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and employer-sponsored application for a long-stay visa (D/AM). The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, but this occupation may not meet the salary threshold. Always consult the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate for current requirements.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a companion or valet in Romania?**
A: Formal qualifications are not strictly required, but previous experience in hospitality or personal service is highly valued. Language skills, especially English and Romanian, are important. Professional training courses in etiquette, household management, or personal assistance can enhance prospects. A valid driver's license is often essential for valets.
