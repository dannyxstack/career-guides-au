## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a civil engineering technician in Romania?**
A: The monthly gross salary for a civil engineering technician in Romania typically ranges from 4,000 to 7,000 RON (approx. €800–€1,400) for entry-to-mid level positions. Experienced professionals or those in senior roles may earn up to 9,000 RON. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Bucharest and major cities.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a civil engineering technician to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU civil engineering technicians can work in Romania via two official routes: a long-stay visa for employment (after obtaining a work permit), or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, approx. 6,800 RON per month).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a civil engineering technician in Romania?**
A: To work as a civil engineering technician in Romania, a post-secondary degree in civil engineering or a related field is typically required. Many professionals hold a diploma from a technical college or university. Certification by the Romanian Register of Civil Engineers (RRS) or similar may be needed for certain roles. Practical experience and knowledge of local regulations are also valued.
