## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Romania?**
A: In Romania, gross monthly salaries for these professions range from 3,000 to 8,000 RON for entry to mid-level roles. Senior positions in academia, NGOs, or public administration can reach 12,000 RON. Pay varies by sector, with private think tanks and international organizations offering higher compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU philosophers, historians, and political scientists to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment if the job offers at least 1.5 times Romania's average gross salary (about 9,000 RON in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible for positions meeting lower salary thresholds, but the Blue Card is typical for degree-requiring roles. The employer applies to the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What are the main career sectors for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Romania?**
A: Key employers include universities, research institutes, museums, government ministries, political parties, think tanks, and media. Many work as teachers, researchers, policy analysts, journalists, or museum curators. Freelance consulting and writing are also viable, especially in political analysis and historical research.
