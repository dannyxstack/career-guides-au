## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Supply, Distribution and Related Manager in Romania?**
A: Salaries for Supply and Distribution Managers in Romania vary by sector and experience. Entry-level positions start around 5,000-6,000 RON gross per month, while mid-level managers earn 8,000-12,000 RON. Senior managers in large logistics firms can earn 15,000-20,000 RON or more. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a Supply, Distribution Manager to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Supply Managers in Romania via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles) or a standard work permit. The employer must obtain a work authorization from the Romanian Immigration Office. Specific salary thresholds and qualifications apply. No fabricated numbers are provided; consult the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a Supply, Distribution Manager in Romania?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor's degree in logistics, supply chain management, business administration, or a related field. 3-5 years of experience in supply chain roles is typical. Professional certifications like CSCP or CPIM are valued. Fluency in English and Romanian, plus strong analytical and leadership skills, are essential.
