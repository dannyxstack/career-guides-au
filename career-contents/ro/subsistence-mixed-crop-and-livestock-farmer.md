## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmers in Romania?**
A: Subsistence farmers in Romania typically earn very low incomes, often below the national average. Many rely on self-consumption and small-scale sales. Estimated annual earnings range from 2,000 to 5,000 EUR, depending on farm size and market access. Official statistics are limited, as this sector is largely informal.

**Q: Can a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: No, subsistence farmers generally do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which targets highly skilled workers with a university degree and a high salary (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). For lower-skilled agricultural work, other permits like the single work permit for third-country nationals may apply, but subsistence farming is rarely sponsored.

**Q: What are the main challenges for subsistence farmers in Romania?**
A: Key challenges include small land plots (average 1-2 hectares), limited access to modern equipment and inputs, low productivity, and difficulty accessing markets. Many farmers rely on EU subsidies, but bureaucratic hurdles persist. Succession is also a concern, as young people often migrate to cities or abroad for better opportunities.
