## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Romania?**
A: In Romania, average monthly salaries for social work and counselling professionals range from 3,500 to 6,500 RON (approximately €700–€1,300) depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and location. Senior roles in nongovernmental organizations or private practice may earn higher, while entry-level positions in state institutions are typically at the lower end.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign social work professionals in Romania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work in Romania via the EU Blue Card (for highly‑qualified roles with a regulated salary threshold) or a standard national long‑stay work visa. Social work and counselling are regulated professions in Romania; recognition of qualifications by the National Authority for Qualifications may be required. Employers must obtain a labor market test, unless a shortage occupation list applies.

**Q: What are the main career prospects for social work and counselling professionals in Romania?**
A: Professionals work in public social services (child protection, elderly care), NGOs, private counselling practices, and corporate employee assistance programs. There is growing demand for specialists in addiction, mental health, and family counselling. Career advancement includes supervisory roles in state agencies or opening a private practice. Master's or specialized certifications enhance opportunities.
