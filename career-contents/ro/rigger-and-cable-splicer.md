- Image recognition-based automatic detection of cable wear

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AR headset displays complex rigging connection steps and safety annotations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for riggers and cable splicers in Romania?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for riggers and cable splicers in Romania typically range from 2,500 to 4,500 RON (approx. €500-€900), depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions start lower, while senior or specialized roles may earn up to 6,000 RON. Overtime and hazard pay can increase total earnings.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU riggers and cable splicers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU workers need a work permit obtained by the employer, followed by a long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for this occupation as it requires a tertiary degree or equivalent high qualification. The work permit is tied to a specific employer and job, renewable annually.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a rigger or cable splicer in Romania?**
A: No formal degree is mandatory, but vocational training or apprenticeship in electromechanics or telecommunications is highly regarded. Certifications in working at height, electrical safety, or fiber optics can improve job prospects. Employers often provide on-the-job training. Experience is key for career advancement to supervisor or specialist roles.
