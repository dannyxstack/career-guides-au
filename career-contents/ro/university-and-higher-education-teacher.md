## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a university teacher in Romania?**
A: Salaries for university teachers in Romania vary by rank and experience. Entry-level lecturers earn around 4,000-5,000 RON (€800-1,000) per month, while full professors can earn 8,000-12,000 RON (€1,600-2,400). Additional income from research grants or private universities is possible.

**Q: Can foreign university teachers work in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU university teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Romania if they have a work contract, a recognized higher education qualification, and meet salary thresholds (at least 1.5x average gross salary). The employer initiates the process. Blue Card holders can also bring family members.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a university teacher in Romania?**
A: A PhD in the relevant field is mandatory. For lecturer positions, teaching and research experience are expected. Professors must have a habilitation certificate and recognized scientific output. Knowledge of Romanian or English (depending on the program) is necessary. Public universities also require passing national competitions.
