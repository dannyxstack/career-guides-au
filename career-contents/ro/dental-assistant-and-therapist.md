## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a dental assistant in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for dental assistants in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 4,500 RON (approx. €500–€900). Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced assistants in private clinics can earn higher. Pay may vary by region, with Bucharest offering higher rates.

**Q: Can non-EU dental assistants obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU dental assistants can work in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card. The employer must apply for a work permit at the Romanian Immigration Office, proving the job vacancy cannot be filled locally. The Blue Card requires a higher-education degree or equivalent experience, plus a salary threshold (e.g., at least 1.5x average gross salary).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a dental assistant in Romania?**
A: To work as a dental assistant in Romania, you typically need a post-secondary degree in dental assistance (2–3 years) from an accredited institution. Registration with the Romanian College of Dental Assistants is mandatory. Some clinics may accept vocational diplomas, but formal education is preferred. Continuing education may be required for career advancement.
