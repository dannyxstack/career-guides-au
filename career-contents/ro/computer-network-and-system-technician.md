## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Romania ranges from 4,000 to 8,000 RON net, depending on experience, certifications, and location. Entry-level positions start around 3,500 RON, while senior technicians with specialized skills can earn over 10,000 RON. Bucharest and IT hubs offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a Computer Network and Systems Technician to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa (viza de muncă) or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer for at least one year, a contract meeting the minimum salary threshold (set by Romanian law, typically twice the average gross salary), and proof of qualifications. The work visa requires a labor market test and employer sponsorship. Both routes lead to a residence permit.
