## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for carpenters and joiners in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for carpenters and joiners in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 RON (approximately 700-1,100 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around 2,800 RON, while highly skilled professionals can earn up to 7,000 RON or more.

**Q: Can non-EU carpenters obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU carpenters can work in Romania via a long-stay work visa for employment, which requires a job offer and work permit from the Romanian Inspectorate General for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. For carpenters without a degree, the standard work permit route is more common.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a carpenter or joiner in Romania?**
A: While formal qualifications are not always mandatory, completing a vocational school or apprenticeship program in carpentry is highly valued. Many employers require a professional certificate (certificat de calificare) from an authorized training provider. Practical experience and a portfolio of work can also help secure jobs. Continuous learning of modern techniques and safety standards is beneficial.
