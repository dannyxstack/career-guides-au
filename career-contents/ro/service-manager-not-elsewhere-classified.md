## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified in Romania?**
A: The gross monthly salary ranges from 6,000 to 12,000 RON (approximately 1,200 to 2,400 EUR) for mid-level positions, depending on industry and location. Senior roles in Bucharest can exceed 15,000 RON. Salaries are often higher in IT services, logistics, and consulting sectors.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for a Services Manager wishing to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania (about 11,250 EUR in 2024). The employer must obtain a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office. A standard work visa also requires employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Services Managers in Romania?**
A: Services Managers can advance to regional director, operations manager, or executive roles such as Chief Operating Officer. With experience, they may oversee multiple service units or transition into consultancy. Further education (e.g., MBA) or industry certifications (e.g., PMP) can enhance prospects. The role is diverse across sectors like IT, hospitality, and logistics.
