## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a craft and related worker in Romania?**
A: In Romania, net monthly salaries for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified typically range from 2,000 to 4,000 RON, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions start around 2,000 RON, while skilled workers with several years of experience can earn up to 4,000 RON or more. Overtime and specialized skills can increase earnings.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU citizens to work as a craft and related worker in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Romania through an EU Blue Card if they have a highly skilled job offer with a salary at least twice the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a national long-stay visa with work permit applies for other craft roles, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of labor shortage. Both routes involve approval from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for craft and related workers in Romania?**
A: Craft and related workers in Romania can advance to team leader or supervisor roles, often after 3-5 years of experience. Specializing in niche areas (e.g., restoration, custom carpentry) can lead to higher pay. Many choose self-employment, especially in construction or artisan trades, where they can build a client base. Continuous training in new techniques is beneficial.
