## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Romania?**
A: In Romania, medical and dental prosthetic technicians typically earn between 2,500 and 4,500 RON net per month, depending on experience, location, and the type of employer. Senior technicians or those with specialized skills may earn higher salaries. The pay can also vary between private clinics and public hospitals.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign medical and dental prosthetic technicians to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Romania via an EU Blue Card or a national long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer, a work permit, and meeting the salary threshold (currently above the average gross salary). Alternatively, a standard work visa requires employer sponsorship and a work permit. All applications are processed through the Romanian Inspectorate General for Immigration.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Romania?**
A: Technicians can progress by specializing in areas like maxillofacial prosthetics or implant-supported restorations. With experience, they may become senior technicians, laboratory managers, or open their own dental laboratory. Continuous training in digital technologies, such as CAD/CAM, enhances career prospects. Certification through the Romanian Order of Medical Technicians is also required for professional practice.
