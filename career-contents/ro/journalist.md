## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for journalists in Romania?**
A: Entry-level journalists earn around 2,500-3,500 RON per month, while experienced journalists can earn 4,000-6,000 RON. Senior editors or specialized correspondents may earn upwards of 8,000 RON. Salaries vary by employer (media outlet type) and location (Bucharest higher). Additional income from freelancing is common.

**Q: Can journalists obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Yes, journalists can apply for an EU Blue Card in Romania if they hold a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and have a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage in Romania. The employment contract must be for at least one year. The employer must obtain a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for journalists in Romania?**
A: Journalists typically start as reporters or editors for local news outlets. With experience, they can advance to senior correspondent, news anchor, or editor-in-chief. Many move into specialized fields (investigative, economic, political journalism) or transition to media management, public relations, or content creation for digital platforms. Continuous training and networking are important.
