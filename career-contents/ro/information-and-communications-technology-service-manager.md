## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Information and Communications Technology Services Manager in Romania?**
A: Salaries for ICT Services Managers in Romania vary by experience and company. Entry-level managers earn around 12,000 RON gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn 20,000–25,000 RON or more. Senior roles in multinational firms may exceed 30,000 RON. Bonuses and benefits are common, especially in Bucharest and IT hubs.

**Q: How can a non-EU professional obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as an ICT Services Manager in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Romanian work visa or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid employment contract with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. 10,000 RON in 2024). The employer must prove no EU/EEA candidate was available. A long-stay visa for employment is another route, requiring a work permit from the Inspectorate General for Immigration.

**Q: What career paths and qualifications are typical for ICT Services Managers in Romania?**
A: ICT Services Managers often start as IT specialists or project managers. Common qualifications include a degree in computer science or engineering, plus certifications like PMP, ITIL, or CISM. Many progress from technical roles to team lead, then to IT manager. Experience in telecommunications, cloud services, or cybersecurity is valued. Continuous learning and leadership skills are essential for advancement.
