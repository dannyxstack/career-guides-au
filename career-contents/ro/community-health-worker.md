## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a community health worker in Romania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for community health workers in Romania ranges from approximately 3,000 to 4,000 RON (600–800 EUR), depending on experience, employer (public sector vs. NGO), and region. Salaries in larger cities like Bucharest may be slightly higher, while rural areas often pay less. The minimum wage in Romania is around 2,550 RON gross per month as of 2024.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as community health workers in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Romania through a standard work visa, requiring a Romanian employer to obtain a work permit. The EU Blue Card, intended for highly qualified workers, demands a university degree and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. 3,300 EUR/month), which is significantly above typical community health worker pay. Thus, the standard work visa is more realistic for this occupation.

**Q: What are the career prospects for community health workers in Romania?**
A: Community health workers often gain experience in preventive care, health education, and patient support. With further training, they can advance to roles like public health nurse, health inspector, or community health coordinator. Many pursue nursing or public health degrees to qualify for higher-level positions in hospitals, NGOs, or government health programs. Experience also opens opportunities in social work and health project management.
