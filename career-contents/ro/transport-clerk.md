## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Transport Clerk in Romania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a Transport Clerk in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 RON (approximately €700–€1,100) for entry to mid-level positions. Senior clerks may earn up to 7,000 RON. Salaries vary by region, with Bucharest offering higher pay. Net income is roughly 70–80% of gross after taxes. Experience and employer size also affect earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Transport Clerks in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally for highly qualified jobs requiring a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. 10,000 RON/month in 2025). Transport Clerk roles typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU workers can instead apply for a standard work permit and long-stay visa, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Transport Clerk in Romania?**
A: Most employers require at least a high school diploma, with vocational training in transport or logistics preferred. Proficiency in Romanian and basic English is often expected. Experience with transport documentation software and knowledge of EU transport regulations are advantageous. On-the-job training is common. Relevant certifications, such as CPC, can improve prospects.
