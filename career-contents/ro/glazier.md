## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in Romania?**
A: Entry-level glaziers earn around 2,500–3,000 RON net per month, while experienced professionals can reach 4,000–5,500 RON. Rates vary by region and employer, with Bucharest offering higher pay. Overtime and specialised skills (e.g., tempered glass installation) may increase earnings.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as a glazier in Romania? What about the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit obtained by a Romanian employer. The EU Blue Card is not typical for glaziers because it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Instead, apply for a long-stay visa for employment; the employer must prove no suitable local candidate.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a glazier in Romania?**
A: Formal training through a vocational school (e.g., in construction or glasswork) or an apprenticeship is common. Optional certification by the National Authority for Qualifications (ANC) can improve job prospects. No university degree is needed; practical skills and safety knowledge are key.
