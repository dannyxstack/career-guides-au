## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for database and network professionals in Romania?**
A: In Romania, database and network professionals typically earn gross monthly salaries between 6,000 and 10,000 RON (approximately €1,200–€2,000), depending on experience and specialization. Senior roles or experts in cloud technologies may exceed this range. Salaries are higher in Bucharest and for multinational companies.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for database and network professionals to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain a Romanian work visa via the EU Blue Card or a national long-stay visa for employment. The Blue Card requires a high-skilled job with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. 9,211 RON in 2024). Employers must obtain a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate. Specific salary thresholds are updated annually.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a database or network professional in Romania?**
A: Employers typically require a bachelor's degree in computer science, information technology, or a related field. Certifications like Oracle, Microsoft, Cisco (CCNA), or AWS are highly valued. Relevant work experience of 2–5 years is often required for senior roles. Romanian language skills are not mandatory for many tech companies, but English proficiency is essential.
