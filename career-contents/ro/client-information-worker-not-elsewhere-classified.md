## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions earn around 2,500-3,500 RON gross per month, while experienced workers can earn 4,500-6,000 RON. Larger cities like Bucharest offer higher pay, and multinational companies may provide additional benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a Client Information Worker in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens need a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross salary, approx. 9,500 RON/month as of 2024). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Alternatively, a standard work visa applies for lower-skilled roles.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for this role in Romania?**
A: A high school diploma is often sufficient, but fluency in Romanian and English is essential. Customer service experience and computer literacy are valued. Certifications in call center or communication skills can enhance prospects. Some employers prefer a bachelor’s degree.
