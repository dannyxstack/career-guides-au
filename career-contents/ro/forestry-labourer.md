## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry labourers in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for forestry labourers in Romania is around the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is 3,300 RON gross. Experienced workers may earn up to 4,500 RON. Pay can vary by region and employer, with some offering additional benefits like accommodation.

**Q: Can foreign forestry labourers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Foreign forestry labourers can work in Romania through a standard work permit (aviz de muncă) sponsored by an employer, provided the job cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience, which forestry labourers typically do not meet.

**Q: What are the main duties of a forestry labourer in Romania?**
A: Forestry labourers in Romania perform tasks such as tree planting, thinning, pruning, and harvesting. They also maintain forest paths, control pests, and operate tools like chainsaws. Work is physically demanding and often seasonal, with peak periods during planting and harvesting seasons.
