## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Business Services Agent in Romania?**
A: Salaries for Business Services Agents in Romania vary by experience and sector. Entry-level agents earn around 3,000 RON net per month, while experienced professionals can earn 6,000-8,000 RON net. Commissions and bonuses are common, significantly increasing total compensation, especially in sales-oriented roles.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a Business Services Agent in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card to work as a Business Services Agent in Romania. Requirements include a valid employment contract for at least one year, a salary meeting the national threshold (approximately 1.5 times the average gross salary, currently around 10,600 RON gross monthly), and recognized qualifications.

**Q: What are common career paths for Business Services Agents in Romania?**
A: Business Services Agents often start in sales or client management roles. With experience, they may become senior agents, team leaders, or branch managers. Others specialize in real estate, insurance, or business brokerage. The role offers flexibility and potential for high earnings through performance-based incentives.
