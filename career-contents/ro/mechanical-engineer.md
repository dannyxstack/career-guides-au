- Smart materials selection and supply chain optimization

**Human moat:**

- Complex nonlinear problem diagnosis and on-site debugging
- Ethical and responsible decision-making for safety-critical systems
- Unstructured communication with clients and suppliers
- Multidisciplinary integration and system architecture decision-making

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Mechanical Engineers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Mechanical Engineers in Romania ranges from €1,200 for entry-level positions to €3,000 for senior roles, with a median around €2,000. Salaries vary by industry, experience, and location, with higher pay in automotive and manufacturing hubs like Bucharest, Timișoara, and Cluj-Napoca.

**Q: How can a foreign Mechanical Engineer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU Mechanical Engineers need an EU Blue Card or a national long-stay visa for employment. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (set annually), a recognized degree, and health insurance. Alternatively, a work permit can be obtained through employer sponsorship. EU/EEA citizens have free access to the Romanian labor market.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Mechanical Engineers in Romania?**
A: Career paths include moving from design or testing engineer to senior engineer, team lead, project manager, or technical specialist. Many transition into related fields like automotive, aerospace, or renewable energy. Advanced degrees and certifications in project management or CAD systems enhance prospects. Large multinationals and research institutes offer structured growth.
