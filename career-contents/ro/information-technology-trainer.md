- Write and update standard training manuals and documentation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Information Technology Trainer in Romania?**
A: Salaries for IT trainers in Romania range from about 5,000 to 10,000 RON per month (approximately 1,000–2,000 EUR) depending on experience, employer, and location. Trainers with specialized certifications or corporate training roles at multinational companies may earn higher, while those in public institutions or smaller firms earn at the lower end.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for an Information Technology Trainer to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as IT trainers in Romania via a long-stay work visa (for employment) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a highly qualified job (including IT trainers) with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. Employers must obtain a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Information Technology Trainer in Romania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in computer science, IT, or a related field is required, plus strong technical skills and teaching ability. Industry certifications (e.g., Microsoft, Cisco) and training-specific credentials (like Certified Technical Trainer) are highly valued. Practical experience in IT combined with pedagogical training (e.g., adult education courses) boosts employability.
