## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships’ deck crew in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for deck crew (e.g., able seaman) in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 RON (approx. 700–1,100 EUR) gross, depending on experience and vessel type. Seafarers may also receive additional allowances. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced crew on international vessels earn more.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for non-EU citizens to work as deck crew in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a Romanian long-stay visa for employment. The employer must obtain a work permit proving no suitable Romanian/EEA candidate. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5x the national average gross salary (approx. 8,500 RON). Deck crew typically do not meet the degree requirement; thus, a standard work permit is the common route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for deck crew in Romania?**
A: Deck crew in Romania can advance to positions such as boatswain, then to deck officer through maritime academies. With experience and required STCW certifications, they can become Chief Officer or Captain. Romanian maritime companies also offer training programs for promotion. Some seafarers transition to onshore roles like port operations or maritime logistics.
