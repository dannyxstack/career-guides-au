## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Romania?**
A: The typical salary for a kitchen helper in Romania ranges from the national minimum wage (approximately 3,000 RON per month in 2024) to around 3,500 RON. Higher pay may be found in upscale restaurants or hotels in major cities like Bucharest. Pay often includes tips and meal allowances.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as kitchen helpers in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as kitchen helpers in Romania through a work permit (obtained by employer) or an EU Blue Card if the job meets the high-skilled salary threshold, which is unlikely for this role. Alternatively, a simple work permit is more common, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What are the main duties of a kitchen helper in Romania?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Romania assist with food preparation, washing dishes, cleaning kitchen equipment and work areas, and storing supplies. They may also help with basic chopping, peeling, and organizing ingredients. Tasks vary by establishment but always support chefs and cooks to maintain cleanliness and efficiency.
