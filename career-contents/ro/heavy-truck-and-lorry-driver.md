## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for heavy truck drivers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, heavy truck drivers typically earn a gross monthly salary between 4,500 and 7,000 RON (approximately 900–1,400 EUR), depending on experience, company, and international routes. Overtime and per diems can increase earnings. Wages are often negotiable, and experienced drivers may earn more.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU citizens to work as a heavy truck driver in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as heavy truck drivers in Romania via a work visa (for employment) or an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers, though truck driving may not qualify). The employer must obtain a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office. After securing a job offer, the employee applies for a long-stay visa at the Romanian consulate.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to drive heavy trucks in Romania?**
A: To drive heavy trucks in Romania, you need a valid category C or C+E driving license, a professional driver qualification (Certificate of Professional Competence - CPC), and a digital tachograph card. Medical and psychological fitness certificates are also required. For international transport, an ADR certificate for dangerous goods may be needed.
