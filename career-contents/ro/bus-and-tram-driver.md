## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Romania?**
A: Monthly net salaries typically range between 3,000 and 5,000 RON (≈€600–€1,000), varying by experience, city, and employer. Drivers in larger cities or with longer tenure may earn higher. Overtime and night shifts often add extra pay.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU citizens to drive buses or trams in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically require a long-stay work visa (viza de lungă ședere) sponsored by a Romanian employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable Romanian or EU worker is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Romania?**
A: Bus drivers need a valid category D or DE driving license and a professional CPC (Certificate of Professional Competence) for passenger transport. Tram drivers require specific tram driving training and a medical certificate. Employers often provide additional on-the-job training.
