## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Buyer in Romania?**
A: For entry-level buyers, monthly gross salaries range from RON 4,000 to RON 6,000. Experienced buyers earn between RON 7,000 and RON 10,000, while senior procurement managers can exceed RON 15,000. Salaries vary by industry, company size, and location, with Bucharest typically offering higher pay.

**Q: What are the work visa options for a foreign Buyer to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-stay visa for employment after the employer obtains a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least twice the average gross salary). A valid employment contract is required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Buyer in Romania?**
A: A bachelor's degree in economics, business administration, or engineering is common. Relevant experience in procurement or supply chain is valued. Certifications like CIPS or APICS can improve prospects. Strong negotiation skills and English proficiency are essential, along with knowledge of local market regulations.
