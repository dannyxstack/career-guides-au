## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Romania?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Romania typically earn between 2,000 and 3,500 RON per month (approximately 400–700 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Hourly rates range from 12 to 20 RON. Salaries are often higher in major cities like Bucharest. Many positions also include meals or accommodation.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a domestic housekeeper in Romania?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for domestic housekeepers, as it requires high-skilled qualifications. Non-EU citizens may obtain a work permit if an employer demonstrates labour shortage and receives approval from the Romanian General Inspectorate for Immigration. The process requires a valid job offer and compliance with annual quotas.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for domestic housekeepers in Romania?**
A: Main duties include cleaning, laundry, ironing, and basic household organisation. Some housekeepers also assist with meal preparation or child care. Career advancement is limited but may include supervisory roles in hotels or private households. Demand remains steady, especially in urban areas and for full-time live-in positions.
