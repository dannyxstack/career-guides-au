## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an ambulance worker in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for ambulance workers in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 RON (approximately €700–1,100), depending on experience, qualifications, and employer (public vs. private). Paramedics and emergency medical technicians may earn slightly higher, while ambulance drivers are at the lower end. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as ambulance workers in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as ambulance workers in Romania through a standard work visa (for employment) or the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education qualification and meet the salary threshold (currently 4,000 RON gross per month). Employers must obtain a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office. Recognized medical qualifications are required, and nostrification of diplomas may be necessary.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an ambulance worker in Romania?**
A: To work as an ambulance worker in Romania, you typically need a high school diploma and a post-secondary qualification in emergency medical services (for paramedics) or a driving license with special training for ambulance drivers. For advanced roles, a degree in nursing or medicine is required. All candidates must pass a medical fitness exam and obtain certification from the Romanian College of Physicians or relevant authority.
