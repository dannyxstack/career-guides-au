## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a landscape architect in Romania?**
A: In Romania, landscape architects earn a gross monthly salary ranging from 4,000 RON to 10,000 RON (approx. €800–€2,000) depending on experience, location, and project complexity. Entry-level positions start around 4,000 RON, while senior roles with 5+ years can reach 10,000 RON or more, especially in private practice or large urban development projects.

**Q: How can a foreign landscape architect obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: A work visa requires a job offer and employer sponsorship, followed by a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled professionals with a contract meeting salary thresholds (approx. 1.5x average gross salary). Both routes require validated education credentials and a clean criminal record. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a landscape architect in Romania?**
A: A university degree in landscape architecture or a related field (e.g., spatial planning) is required. Membership in the Romanian Register of Urban Planners (RUR) may be necessary for certain projects. Fluency in Romanian is often expected, though international firms in Bucharest may accept English. Practical experience through internships or portfolio work is highly valued.
