## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Systems Administrator in Romania?**
A: In Romania, the average gross monthly salary for a Systems Administrator ranges between 6,000 and 10,000 RON (approx. €1,200–€2,000) depending on experience, city, and company size. Entry-level positions start around 4,500 RON, while senior roles can exceed 12,000 RON. IT hubs like Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, and Timișoara offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a non-EU Systems Administrator to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work via the EU Blue Card for highly-qualified employment or a national long-stay visa for work. Requirements include a valid job offer, relevant qualifications (e.g., bachelor’s degree), and a work permit obtained by the employer. The Blue Card offers a pathway to permanent residency after 33 months (or 21 with Romanian language skills).

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of Systems Administrators in Romania?**
A: AI-driven automation is increasingly used for monitoring, incident response, and routine tasks. Romanian sysadmins must upskill in scripting, cloud platforms, and AI/ML basics to manage hybrid environments. Demand remains strong, but roles shift from manual administration to infrastructure automation and strategy.
