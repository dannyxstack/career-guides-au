## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for R&D managers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for R&D managers in Romania range from €30,000–€40,000 per year for entry-level to €60,000–€80,000 for senior roles, with top earners exceeding €100,000 in multinationals. High-tech hubs like Bucharest and Cluj-Napoca offer premium pay. Bonuses and stock options can add 10–20%.

**Q: What official routes allow non-EU R&D managers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a valid employment contract and earn at least 1.5 times Romania's average gross salary. Alternatively, a long-stay work visa (tip D) requires employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Office. Both demand recognized qualifications.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for R&D managers in Romania?**
A: R&D managers can advance to director of R&D, VP of innovation, or CTO roles, especially in IT, automotive, and pharmaceuticals. Professional certifications in project management (PMP, Agile) and active networking via clusters like Cluj IT or RoTech boost career growth.
