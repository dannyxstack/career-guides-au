## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for miners and quarriers in Romania?**
A: Miners and quarriers in Romania earn an average gross monthly salary of around 3,500 to 5,000 RON (€700–€1,000), depending on experience, location, and company. Wages are higher in underground mining and for specialized roles. Overtime and hazard pay are common but vary by employer.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for mining jobs in Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for a Romanian long-stay visa for employment (work permit) if they have a job offer from a Romanian employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a contract of at least one year and salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What are the main tasks and qualifications required for miners and quarriers in Romania?**
A: Miners and quarriers in Romania extract minerals, coal, stone, or ore from underground or surface mines. Tasks include drilling, blasting, loading, and operating heavy machinery. A vocational school certificate or apprenticeship in mining is typically required. Physical fitness, safety training, and a valid blasting license are mandatory. Experience in using mining equipment is highly valued.
