## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, location, and specialization. Entry-level practitioners may earn around 3,500–5,000 RON per month, while experienced professionals can earn 6,000–10,000 RON. Those running private practices often have higher but variable incomes. Public sector wages are generally lower, around 3,000–4,500 RON.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU citizen to work as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a highly qualified job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. Both routes require recognition of qualifications by Romanian authorities.

**Q: What professional requirements exist for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Romania?**
A: Practitioners must comply with Law 118/2007 on alternative therapies. While no single regulator exists, many join recognized associations (e.g., Romanian Association of Acupuncturists). Official recognition of diplomas is needed for regulated modalities. Continuous education is recommended but not legally mandatory for all fields.
