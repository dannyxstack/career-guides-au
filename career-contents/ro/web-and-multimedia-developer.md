## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for web and multimedia developers in Romania?**
A: Salary varies by experience and location. Entry-level developers earn between 2,500–4,000 RON net per month, while mid-level developers earn 5,000–7,000 RON. Senior developers and specialists can earn 8,000–12,000 RON or more. In Bucharest and Cluj-Napoca, salaries are generally higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for web developers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approximately 2 times the national gross average salary) and relevant qualifications. Alternatively, a national long-stay visa for employment is possible. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What career opportunities are available for web developers in Romania?**
A: Romania has a booming IT sector with many multinational companies and startups. Developers can work in full-stack, front-end, or back-end roles. Specializations like mobile development, cybersecurity, or cloud computing offer growth. Remote work is common, and cities like Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara, and Iași have strong tech hubs.
