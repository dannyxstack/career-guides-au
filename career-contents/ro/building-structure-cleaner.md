## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for building structure cleaners in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for building structure cleaners in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 3,500 RON (approximately 500-700 EUR) before taxes, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (around 2,300 RON in 2023), while experienced cleaners in larger cities like Bucharest can earn higher. Overtime and hazardous pay may apply for high-rise work.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as building structure cleaners in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a Romanian work visa for low-skilled occupations like building structure cleaning if an employer obtains a work permit. The EU Blue Card is not applicable, as it requires a highly qualified position with at least a bachelor's degree or equivalent. No specific national quotas exist for this occupation, but the employer must demonstrate labour market need.

**Q: What are the main duties and requirements for building structure cleaners in Romania?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Romania perform cleaning of exterior and interior building surfaces, including windows, facades, roofs, and structural elements using ladders, scaffolding, or industrial rope access. Duties also involve operating pressure washers and handling cleaning chemicals. Requirements typically include physical fitness, no fear of heights, and completion of occupational safety training; formal qualifications are not mandatory.
