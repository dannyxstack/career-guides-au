## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for police inspectors and detectives in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for police inspectors and detectives in Romania ranges from approximately 4,000 to 6,500 RON (800–1,300 EUR) gross, depending on rank, seniority, and location. Additional allowances for overtime, night shifts, and special operations may apply. Salaries are regulated by public sector pay scales.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as police inspectors or detectives in Romania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Romania is for highly qualified non-EU workers. However, police inspector and detective roles are typically restricted to Romanian citizens or EU/EEA nationals due to national security requirements. Non-EU citizens may not be eligible for these positions via the Blue Card route. Official immigration routes for non-EU nationals in law enforcement are very limited.

**Q: What are the entry requirements for becoming a police inspector or detective in Romania?**
A: Candidates must be Romanian citizens, at least 18 years old, with a high school diploma (baccalaureate) for lower ranks or a bachelor's degree for inspector roles. They must pass physical, psychological, and medical tests, as well as background checks. Admission to the Police Academy or a competitive recruitment process is required, followed by specialized training.
