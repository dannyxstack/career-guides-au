## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for signwriters and decorative painters in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions may earn around 2,500–3,500 RON per month, while experienced professionals can earn 4,000–6,000 RON. Freelancers set their own rates. Wages in larger cities like Bucharest tend to be higher.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as signwriters in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Romania through a standard work permit or, if highly skilled, via the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree or equivalent professional experience and a job offer meeting salary thresholds. For most signwriters, a regular work permit is the primary route.

**Q: What career opportunities are available for signwriters and decorative painters in Romania?**
A: Professionals can work in advertising agencies, film or theater set decoration, interior design firms, or as freelancers. Demand exists in retail signage, restoration of historical buildings, and custom artwork. With experience, one can specialize in digital sign-making or start their own business.
