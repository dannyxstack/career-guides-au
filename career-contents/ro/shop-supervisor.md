## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shop supervisor in Romania?**
A: In Romania, shop supervisors earn between 3,000 and 5,500 RON gross per month. Pay depends on experience, store size, and location. Supervisors in Bucharest or in large retail chains may earn toward the higher end. Benefits often include bonuses, meal tickets, and health insurance.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a shop supervisor moving to Romania from outside the EU?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as shop supervisors in Romania via an employment visa, requiring a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available if the gross annual salary meets the threshold (about 54,000 RON in 2023). Both routes require valid qualifications and a clean criminal record.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop supervisors in Romania?**
A: Shop supervisors in Romania can progress to store manager, area manager, or regional operations roles. Advancement often requires proven leadership, sales performance, and inventory management skills. Many retailers offer internal training programs. With experience, supervisors may also move into buying, merchandising, or supply chain management.
