## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sociologists and anthropologists in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level sociologists and anthropologists earn around 3,500–5,000 RON (€700–1,000) per month. Mid-career professionals with experience can earn 5,000–8,000 RON (€1,000–1,600), while senior roles in academia or research may exceed 10,000 RON (€2,000). Salaries vary by sector—private research firms often pay more than public institutions.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for sociologists and anthropologists to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU sociologists and anthropologists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times Romania’s average gross salary) and relevant qualifications. Alternatively, a standard work permit requires employer sponsorship and approval from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. Both routes require a labor market test unless exempted.

**Q: What are the main career paths for sociologists and anthropologists in Romania?**
A: In Romania, sociologists and anthropologists commonly work in academic research, teaching at universities, or conducting social studies for NGOs and government agencies. Private-sector roles include market research, user experience research, and data analysis in companies. Opportunities also exist in international organizations and EU-funded projects focusing on social development.
