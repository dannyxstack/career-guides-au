## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bicycle repairers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for bicycle repairers in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 4,000 RON (approximately 500-800 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while skilled technicians in larger cities like Bucharest can earn higher wages. Additional benefits may include bonuses or commission on sales.

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU bicycle repairers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Romania as bicycle repairers via a work permit, which requires an employer-sponsored application to the General Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable due to skill-level requirements. Alternatively, the EU Intra-Corporate Transfer permit may be available for multinational employees. All routes require a valid job offer and meeting standard conditions.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bicycle repairer in Romania?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but vocational training or a certificate in bicycle mechanics is advantageous. Many repairers learn through apprenticeships or on-the-job experience. Certification from organizations like the International Bicycle Fund can enhance employability. Knowledge of e-bike systems is increasingly valued. The profession is regulated under ISCO-08 code 7234.
