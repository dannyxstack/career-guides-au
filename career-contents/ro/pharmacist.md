## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmacists in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for pharmacists in Romania ranges from 5,000 to 8,000 RON (approx. 1,000–1,600 EUR) for entry-level positions, while experienced pharmacists can earn up to 12,000 RON. Salaries vary by sector, with community pharmacy offering lower pay compared to hospital or pharmaceutical industry roles.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign pharmacists to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU pharmacists can work in Romania via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national long-stay visa for employment. Requirements include a valid job offer, recognized pharmacy diploma (verified by the National Centre for Recognition and Equivalence of Diplomas), and Romanian language proficiency. The employer must obtain a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for pharmacists in Romania?**
A: Pharmacists in Romania work in community pharmacies, hospital pharmacies, pharmaceutical industry (e.g., R&D, quality control, regulatory affairs), academia, and public health institutions. There is growing demand in clinical pharmacy and pharmaceutical care roles. After 3 years of experience, pharmacists can open their own pharmacy, subject to legal requirements.
