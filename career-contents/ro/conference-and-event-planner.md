## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a conference and event planner in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level event planners earn around 3,000–4,500 RON per month, while experienced professionals can earn 6,000–10,000 RON. Salaries vary by city (Bucharest highest), employer type (corporate vs. agency), and event scale. Bonuses and commissions are common in larger firms.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for conference and event planners to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-stay visa for employment, followed by a residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently approx. 2x average gross salary). Employers must apply for a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What qualifications or certifications are valued for event planners in Romania?**
A: While a degree in hospitality, tourism, or business is helpful, many employers value hands-on experience and industry certifications such as CMP (Certified Meeting Professional) or CED (Certified Event Designer). Language skills (English, often French or German) and digital proficiency in event management software are also highly regarded.
