## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for spray painters and varnishers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for spray painters and varnishers in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 RON (approx. 700–1,100 EUR), depending on experience, location, and industry. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced workers in automotive or industrial painting can earn more. Overtime and bonuses may increase total pay.

**Q: What work visa options are available for foreign spray painters to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work in Romania via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers (requiring a recognized degree and minimum salary threshold), or a standard work permit (aviz de muncă) for skilled occupations. The employer must obtain the permit from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate. EU/EEA citizens have free access to the labor market.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a spray painter in Romania?**
A: Typically, employers require a vocational school diploma in painting or a related field, or 1–2 years of on-the-job training. Knowledge of paint types, surface preparation, and safety protocols is essential. Certifications in powder coating or automotive painting may improve prospects. Physical stamina and attention to detail are important traits.
