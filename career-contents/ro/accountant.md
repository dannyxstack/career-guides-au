## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an accountant in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an accountant in Romania ranges from 4,500 to 8,000 RON (approximately 900–1,600 EUR) depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior accountants or those with specialized certifications may earn up to 12,000 RON or more.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU accountant to work in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU accountants can work in Romania via the standard work permit, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals with a recognized degree and a job paying at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Both routes involve approval from the Romanian General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a certified accountant in Romania?**
A: To practice as a certified accountant in Romania, professionals typically need a degree in accounting or finance, membership in the Body of Expert and Licensed Accountants of Romania (CECCAR), and passing the national aptitude exam. Ongoing professional development is mandatory to maintain certification.
