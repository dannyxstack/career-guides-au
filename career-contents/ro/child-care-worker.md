## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for child care workers in Romania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for child care workers in Romania ranges from approximately 3,500 to 4,500 RON, depending on experience, region, and employer type (public vs. private). Salaries in larger cities like Bucharest tend to be higher, while rural areas may offer lower pay. Many workers also receive benefits such as meal vouchers.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as child care workers in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Romania through a standard work permit, requiring an employer to obtain a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is also available for highly qualified workers, but child care workers generally do not meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Both routes require a valid job offer and compliance with immigration procedures.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a child care worker in Romania?**
A: Typically, a high school diploma in pedagogy or a related field is required, along with a certificate of competence from the Ministry of Labor. For roles in licensed daycare centers, additional training in early childhood education (e.g., a post-secondary degree) may be necessary. Continuous professional development is encouraged to meet regulatory standards.
