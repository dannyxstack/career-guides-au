## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for nursing professionals in Romania?**
A: Registered nurses in Romania earn a gross monthly salary between 4,000 and 6,000 RON on average, with variations based on experience, specialization, and hospital location. Public sector salaries follow national scales, while private clinics may offer higher pay. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: Can nursing professionals obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU nursing professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized nursing degree and a valid job offer. The salary must be at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (updated annually). Alternatively, a national long-stay work visa is available for those who do not meet the Blue Card threshold.

**Q: What are the requirements for foreign nurses to work in Romania?**
A: Foreign nurses must have their nursing diploma recognized by the Romanian Ministry of Health. They need to prove Romanian language proficiency at a B1 level or higher and register with the Order of Nurses and Midwives in Romania (OAMGMAMR). A valid work contract is also required for visa application.
