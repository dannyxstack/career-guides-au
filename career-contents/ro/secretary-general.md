## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a secretary in Romania?**
A: Typical gross monthly salaries for secretaries in Romania range from 3,000 to 5,500 RON, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 RON, while senior secretaries can earn up to 5,500 RON. Benefits such as meal vouchers and health insurance are often included.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a foreign secretary to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as secretaries via an employer-sponsored work permit, or through the EU Blue Card if the role requires higher education and meets the salary threshold. The employer must apply for a long-stay employment visa (D visa) followed by a residence permit for employment.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a secretary in Romania?**
A: A high school diploma or vocational training in office administration is typically required. Proficiency in English and Romanian, computer literacy with office software, and strong organizational skills are essential. A bachelor's degree or secretarial certification can be advantageous.
