## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Romania?**
A: As of 2024, the minimum gross monthly salary for a full-time farm labourer in Romania is approximately 3,000 RON (€600). However, many farms pay based on productivity or hourly rates, and earnings vary seasonally. Overtime and in-kind benefits (e.g., accommodation) may supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, making it unsuitable for farm labourers. Non-EU nationals can instead obtain a standard work permit through employer sponsorship or a seasonal work authorization for up to 9 months per year.

**Q: What are the main duties and job prospects for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Romania?**
A: Duties include planting, harvesting, feeding livestock, and maintaining farm equipment. Demand is strongest during planting and harvest seasons, especially in rural areas like Transylvania and the plains. While year-round positions exist on larger farms, many jobs are seasonal, offering flexible work but limited long-term stability.
