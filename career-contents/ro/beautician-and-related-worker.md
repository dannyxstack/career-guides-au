## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for beauticians in Romania ranges from 2,000 to 4,000 RON (approximately 400–800 EUR) depending on experience, location, and employer. In major cities like Bucharest, earnings can be higher. Many beauticians also receive tips, which can supplement income.

**Q: How can non-EU beauticians obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU beauticians typically need a job offer from a Romanian employer, who must apply for a work permit on their behalf. The EU Blue Card is intended for highly qualified workers, usually requiring a higher education degree; beauticians may not qualify. Another option is the Single Permit for long-term employment.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for beauticians in Romania?**
A: Beauticians can advance by specializing in areas like makeup artistry, nail design, or skincare treatments. They may also become salon managers, trainers, or open their own businesses. Continuous learning through professional courses and competitions can enhance career prospects and income potential.
