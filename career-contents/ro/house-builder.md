## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a house builder in Romania?**
A: In Romania, house builders typically earn between 2,500 and 4,500 RON net per month, depending on experience, location, and project complexity. Experienced workers in major cities like Bucharest or Cluj-Napoca may earn higher wages. Overtime and performance bonuses can increase total compensation. The minimum gross wage for skilled construction workers is approximately 3,000 RON.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU house builder to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU house builders can obtain a Romanian work visa through an employer-sponsored work permit. For the EU Blue Card, one needs a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. Alternatively, a long-stay visa for work is possible under bilateral labor agreements. All routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for house builders in Romania?**
A: House builders in Romania can progress to roles such as site foreman, construction supervisor, or project manager. Obtaining certifications in modern techniques, safety standards, or energy-efficient building enhances prospects. Many experienced builders become self-employed contractors. The growing construction sector, driven by infrastructure projects and housing demand, offers stable career paths with potential for higher earnings.
