- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physiotherapy technician or assistant in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level physiotherapy assistants earn around 3,000–4,000 RON per month, while experienced technicians can earn 5,000–6,000 RON. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in private clinics and cities like Bucharest. Public sector roles may include additional allowances.

**Q: Can a foreign physiotherapy assistant work in Romania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Romania if they have a valid job offer as a physiotherapy assistant, meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, updated annually), and have recognized qualifications. The employer must obtain a work permit first. Official routes include the Single Permit procedure.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for physiotherapy technicians in Romania?**
A: Physiotherapy technicians can progress to senior technician roles, specialize in areas like sports or pediatric physiotherapy, or move into management of rehabilitation departments. With further education, some become physiotherapists (requires a bachelor's degree). Continuing professional development is encouraged by the Romanian College of Physiotherapists.
