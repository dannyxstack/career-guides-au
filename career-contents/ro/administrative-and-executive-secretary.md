## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Administrative and Executive Secretary in Romania?**
A: Salaries for administrative and executive secretaries in Romania range from 3,500 to 6,500 RON gross per month, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions may start around 3,000 RON, while senior roles in multinational companies can reach 7,000 RON or more.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as an Administrative and Executive Secretary in Romania through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can obtain an EU Blue Card for this occupation if they have a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the Romanian average gross salary (approx. 9,600 EUR in 2023). Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for lower salaries or non-degree holders.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Administrative and Executive Secretaries in Romania?**
A: Administrative and executive secretaries can progress to roles such as Office Manager, Executive Assistant, or Administrative Coordinator. With experience, they may specialize in areas like human resources or project management. Continuing education in business administration or certifications (e.g., CAP) can further enhance career growth.
