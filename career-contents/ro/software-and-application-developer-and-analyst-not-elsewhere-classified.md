## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a software developer in Romania?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Romania vary by experience and location. Junior developers earn around 800-1,200 EUR net per month, mid-level 1,500-2,500 EUR net, and senior roles can exceed 3,500 EUR net. Major tech hubs like Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, and Timișoara offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for software developers moving to Romania?**
A: Non-EU software developers can work in Romania via the EU Blue Card or a national long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. Alternatively, employers can sponsor a work permit under the Employment of Foreigners law.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for software developers in Romania?**
A: Romania's IT sector offers strong career growth, with paths to senior developer, team lead, architect, or technical manager. Many companies support certifications and training. The market is active, with frequent job changes and skill upgrades common. Remote work options also expand opportunities with international firms.
