## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical imaging technician in Romania?**
A: Salaries for medical imaging technicians in Romania vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions earn around 2,500 RON gross per month, while experienced technicians in private clinics can earn up to 6,000 RON gross. The national average gross monthly salary is approximately 4,000 RON. Public sector salaries are typically lower but include additional benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as medical imaging technicians in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens may obtain an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment in Romania. Requirements include a relevant higher education degree (ISCED 6 or higher) and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. The employer files the application with the General Inspectorate for Immigration. Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible for lower-skilled roles.

**Q: What are the career prospects for medical imaging technicians in Romania?**
A: Career prospects are positive due to increasing demand for diagnostic imaging. Technicians can specialize in MRI, CT, or nuclear medicine, or advance to supervisory roles in hospital radiology departments. Private diagnostic centers offer growth opportunities. Continuous professional development is required through accredited courses to keep up with technological advancements.
