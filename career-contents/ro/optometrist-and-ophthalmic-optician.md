## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an optometrist in Romania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for optometrists in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 6,000 RON (approx. 700–1,200 EUR), depending on experience, location, and type of practice. Entry-level positions start around 2,500 RON, while senior specialists in private clinics can earn up to 8,000 RON. These figures are indicative and may vary.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign optometrist to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can work in Romania via an EU Blue Card or a national long-stay work visa. The Blue Card requires a qualified job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the gross average wage, plus recognized qualifications. Alternatively, a work permit (aviz de muncă) is obtained by the employer. Both routes require diploma recognition and valid registration with the Romanian College of Optometrists.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as an optometrist in Romania?**
A: To practice as an optometrist in Romania, you need a bachelor's or master's degree in optometry from an accredited university. After graduation, registration with the Romanian College of Optometrists (Colegiul Optometriștilor din România) is mandatory. Continuing education credits are required for license renewal. EU diplomas are recognized automatically, while non-EU degrees must undergo a nostrification process.
