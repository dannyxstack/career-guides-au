## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for specialist medical practitioners in Romania?**
A: In Romania, specialist medical practitioners earn between 10,000 and 20,000 RON net per month, depending on experience, specialty, and sector. Public hospital salaries average 12,000-15,000 RON, while private practice can yield higher pay. Additional income from on-call duties and overtime is common.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU specialist medical practitioners to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU doctors can work in Romania via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers or a national long-stay visa for employment. Requirements include a valid job offer, recognition of medical qualifications by the Romanian Ministry of Health, and proof of Romanian language proficiency (at least B1 level). The EU Blue Card requires a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary.

**Q: What is the career progression for specialist medical practitioners in Romania?**
A: After medical school, graduates complete a residency (3-6 years) to become specialists. Progression includes senior specialist and primary physician (medic primar) ranks, achieved through exams and years of service. Many pursue sub-specialties or academic roles. Private practice offers faster advancement, while public hospitals have structured promotion paths based on merit and tenure.
