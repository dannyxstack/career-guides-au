## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for domestic cleaners in Romania vary by region and employer. As of 2024, the national minimum gross wage is approximately 3,300 RON per month, but many domestic workers earn less, especially in informal arrangements. Average net hourly rates range from 15 to 25 RON. Full-time positions in agencies may offer minimum wage or slightly more.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as domestic cleaners in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent, not for domestic cleaners. Non-EU nationals seeking work in this occupation must follow the general work permit route, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The employer must prove no suitable Romanian or EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for domestic cleaners in Romania?**
A: Domestic cleaners and helpers perform cleaning tasks in private homes, such as sweeping, mopping, dusting, laundry, and sometimes cooking. Career progression is limited; experienced workers may become supervisors in cleaning companies or self-employed. Formal training is not required, but certifications in hygiene or cleaning techniques can improve job opportunities.
