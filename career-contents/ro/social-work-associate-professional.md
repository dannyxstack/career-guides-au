## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Social Work Associate Professional in Romania?**
A: Entry-level social work associates earn around 2,500–3,500 RON (€500–€700) per month before tax. With experience, salaries can reach 4,000–5,500 RON (€800–€1,100). Public sector roles often have fixed scales, while private NGOs may offer additional benefits. Net income after taxes and social contributions is roughly 20–30% lower.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a Social Work Associate Professional to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a long-stay visa for employment (work permit) or an EU Blue Card if meeting the higher-skilled criteria (salary threshold approx. 4,000 RON monthly). Both require a valid job offer from a Romanian employer. Intra-corporate transfer permits are also possible for multinational organizations. Always check the latest requirements with the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a Social Work Associate in Romania?**
A: Typically, a post-secondary diploma in social work or a related field is required. For associate roles, a bachelor's degree in social assistance is common. Registration with the National College of Social Workers (Colegiul Național al Asistenților Sociali) is mandatory for practicing. Language skills in Romanian are often required, though some international NGOs may accept English.
