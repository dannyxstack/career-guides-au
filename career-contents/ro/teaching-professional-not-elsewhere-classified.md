## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for these roles ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 RON (approximately 800–1,200 EUR), depending on experience, qualifications, and the institution. Salaries in private sector or specialized fields may be higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU teaching professionals to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU teachers must obtain a work permit through a Romanian employer, followed by a long-stay visa for employment. For highly qualified professionals, the EU Blue Card is available, requiring a recognized qualification and a job offer with a salary above the national threshold set by law.

**Q: Which types of teaching roles fall under 'teaching professionals not elsewhere classified' in Romania?**
A: This category includes specialized teachers not covered by other ISCO codes, such as adult education instructors, special education teachers, language trainers in private institutes, and tutors in non-formal settings. They often work outside the traditional school system.
