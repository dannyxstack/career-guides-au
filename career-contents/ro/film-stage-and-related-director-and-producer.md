## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a film director or producer in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and project. In 2025, a mid-career film director in Romania can expect a monthly gross salary of approximately 4,000 to 12,000 RON (€800–€2,400). Producers often earn similar ranges, with top earners on international co-productions reaching higher figures.

**Q: How can a non-EU film director or producer work in Romania under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer in Romania meeting the minimum salary threshold (at least 1.5x the average gross salary) and hold a higher education degree. The Romanian Immigration Inspectorate processes the application, which also allows family reunification.

**Q: What are the typical career paths for film directors and producers in Romania?**
A: Many start as assistants or in technical roles (e.g., camera, editing) before directing shorts or commercials. Education at national film schools (UNATC Bucharest) is valued. Producers often begin in production companies or as line producers. Networking at festivals like TIFF (Transylvania International Film Festival) is key.
