## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Advertising and Public Relations Manager in Romania?**
A: In Romania, the average gross monthly salary for Advertising and Public Relations Managers ranges between 8,000 and 15,000 RON depending on experience and company size. Senior roles in multinational agencies or large corporations may exceed this range. Salaries are typically higher in Bucharest than in other cities.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as an Advertising and Public Relations Manager in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a valid employment contract with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, they may apply for a long-stay visa for employment if the employer obtains a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office. Both routes require a university degree and relevant professional experience.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are typically required for Advertising and Public Relations Manager roles in Romania?**
A: Employers in Romania usually require a bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, or a related field, plus 3-5 years of experience in advertising or PR. Fluency in Romanian and English is essential. Strong leadership, strategic thinking, and digital marketing skills are highly valued. Professional certifications like CIPR or IABC can be advantageous.
