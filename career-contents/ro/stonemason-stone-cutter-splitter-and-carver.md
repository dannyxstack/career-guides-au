## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for stonemasons and stone cutters in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level stonemasons earn around 2,500 RON net per month, while experienced craftsmen can earn 4,000–6,000 RON. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Bucharest and major construction hubs. Overtime and specialized carving skills can increase earnings further.

**Q: Can non-EU stonemasons obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Yes. Non-EU stonemasons may apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer for a highly qualified position (usually requiring a relevant degree or at least 5 years of professional experience). The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. The Blue Card is valid for 1–4 years and allows family reunification.

**Q: What training or certification is needed to work as a stonemason in Romania?**
A: Formal training is not mandatory but highly valued. Vocational schools offer 3-year programs in stone working. Apprenticeships are common. Certification through the National Authority for Qualifications (ANC) can boost employability. Many stonemasons learn on the job, but advanced carving requires specialized courses.
