## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for town and traffic planners in Romania?**
A: In Romania, town and traffic planners typically earn between 4,000 and 8,000 RON net monthly, depending on experience, sector, and location. Senior planners or those with specialized skills can earn up to 12,000 RON. Public sector salaries are lower but offer stability, while private sector roles may be higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign town planners to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. 4,200 EUR in 2024). Alternatively, a work permit for a specific job is possible. EU/EEA citizens can work freely without a visa.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a town planner in Romania?**
A: A master's degree in urban planning, architecture, geography, or a related field is typically required. Registration with the Romanian Register of Urban Planners (RUR) is mandatory. Knowledge of Romanian spatial planning legislation and experience with local projects are highly valued.
