## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Romania?**
A: The salary for sweepers in Romania generally aligns with the national minimum wage, which is approximately 3,300 RON (around €660) per month gross as of 2024. Net pay is lower after social contributions. Some employers may offer slightly higher wages for night shifts or in larger cities like Bucharest, but overall the pay is entry-level.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for sweeper jobs in Romania?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers and is not applicable to low-skilled occupations like sweepers. Non-EU nationals may still work in Romania through a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The process involves proving the job cannot be filled by an EU citizen. Minimum salary thresholds apply for the permit.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for sweepers in Romania?**
A: Sweepers in Romania perform manual cleaning of streets, public spaces, and sometimes industrial areas. Tasks include sweeping, collecting litter, emptying bins, and operating basic cleaning equipment. Work is often outdoors, in shifts including early mornings or evenings. The job requires physical stamina and tolerance for weather conditions. Safety gear is typically provided.
