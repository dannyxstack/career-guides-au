## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electrical engineer in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an electrical engineer in Romania ranges from 6,000 to 12,000 RON (approx. €1,200–2,400), depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 4,500 RON, while senior engineers can earn over 15,000 RON. Major cities like Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, and Timișoara offer higher pay. Benefits often include meal vouchers and private health insurance.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU electrical engineer to work in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU electrical engineers can obtain a Romanian work visa or an EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and a work permit (aviz de muncă) from the Romanian Immigration Office. The EU Blue Card, for highly skilled professionals, requires a higher salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) and a recognized engineering degree. Both routes allow family reunification and lead to permanent residency after 5 years.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for electrical engineers in Romania?**
A: Electrical engineers in Romania can advance to senior engineer, project manager, or technical director roles. Specializing in renewable energy, automation, or power systems is valued. Obtaining a Professional Engineer (PE) certification or an MBA can accelerate growth. Many firms offer internal training and support for master's degrees. The growing tech and energy sectors provide opportunities in research, design, and consultancy.
