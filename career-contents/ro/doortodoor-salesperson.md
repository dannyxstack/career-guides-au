## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a door-to-door salesperson in Romania?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Romania typically earn a base salary plus commission. The average monthly net salary ranges from 2,500 to 3,500 RON for full-time work, though top performers can earn significantly more. Commissions are often based on sales volume or contracts signed.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a door-to-door salesperson in Romania via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work as door-to-door salespersons in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approximately €24,000 in 2025). Alternatively, a standard work visa is possible if the employer obtains a work permit.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for door-to-door salespersons in Romania?**
A: Entry-level door-to-door salespersons in Romania can advance to sales team leader, regional manager, or transition into inside sales. Experience in direct sales is valued in telecoms, utilities, and insurance. Some move into training or business development roles, especially if they achieve consistent high performance.
