## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for police officers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a police officer in Romania is approximately 4,500-6,000 RON (about 900-1,200 EUR) depending on rank, experience, and location. Starting salaries for new officers are around 3,500 RON gross. Additional allowances for overtime, hazardous duty, and seniority can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as police officers in Romania via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not applicable for police officer positions in Romania. Police officer roles require Romanian citizenship, as stipulated by national law, due to security clearance and public order responsibilities. Non-EU citizens cannot obtain a work visa for this occupation.

**Q: What are the educational requirements to become a police officer in Romania?**
A: To become a police officer in Romania, candidates must have a high school diploma (baccalaureate) and pass a rigorous selection process including physical tests, psychological evaluation, and medical examination. Many attend the Police Academy (Școala de Agenți de Poliție) for a 2-year program. A university degree is not mandatory but can be advantageous for promotion.
