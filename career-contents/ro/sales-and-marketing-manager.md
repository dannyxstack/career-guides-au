## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for Sales and Marketing Managers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, the average gross monthly salary for Sales and Marketing Managers ranges from 8,000 to 15,000 RON (approx. €1,600 to €3,000), depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior roles in multinational firms or high-revenue sectors like IT or pharmaceuticals can exceed 20,000 RON. Bonuses and commissions are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Sales and Marketing Managers moving to Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa (viza de angajare) or an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles). The employer must apply for a work permit at the Romanian Immigration Office, proving job vacancy and compliance. Blue Card requires a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. 9,000 RON/month in 2024). Both require valid passport, employment contract, and health insurance.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Sales and Marketing Managers in Romania?**
A: Sales and Marketing Managers in Romania can advance to Director of Sales, Marketing Director, or Commercial Director roles. Many move into general management (CEO/Country Manager) or specialize in digital marketing, brand management, or business development. The growing startup ecosystem and multinational presence offer pathways to regional or global positions. Continuous education in business or digital skills is valued.
