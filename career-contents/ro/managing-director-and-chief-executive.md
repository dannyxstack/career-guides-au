## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Managing Director or Chief Executive in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and company size. According to local market data, monthly gross salaries for CEOs in Romania typically range from €5,000 to €15,000, with larger multinationals offering higher packages including bonuses and benefits. Performance-related pay is common, and total compensation can include stock options and profit sharing.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a foreign executive to work in Romania?**
A: Foreign nationals can work in Romania as Managing Directors via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid job offer and meeting salary thresholds (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, a Long-Stay Visa for Employment (Type D) is available, followed by a work permit. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are typically required for a Managing Director role in Romania?**
A: Most Managing Director positions in Romania require a master's degree in business or a related field, plus at least 10-15 years of progressive management experience. Fluency in English is essential; Romanian language skills are advantageous. Industry-specific expertise and a strong network are often critical.
