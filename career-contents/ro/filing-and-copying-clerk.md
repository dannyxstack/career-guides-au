## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Filing and Copying Clerks in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Filing and Copying Clerks in Romania ranges from approximately 2,500 to 3,500 RON, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 2,200 RON, while senior clerks may earn up to 4,000 RON. These figures are typical for full-time employment in urban areas like Bucharest.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Filing and Copying Clerks in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Romania as Filing and Copying Clerks by obtaining a work visa (long-stay visa for employment) or an EU Blue Card, provided they meet the criteria. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree or professional experience, but this occupation typically does not meet the salary threshold. The standard work visa is the more common route.

**Q: What are the main duties of Filing and Copying Clerks in Romania?**
A: Filing and Copying Clerks in Romania are responsible for organizing and maintaining paper and electronic files, photocopying documents, sorting and distributing mail, and performing basic data entry. They often work in offices, government institutions, or private companies. Attention to detail and basic computer skills are essential for this role.
