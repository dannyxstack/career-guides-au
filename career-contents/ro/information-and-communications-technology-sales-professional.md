## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ICT Sales Professionals in Romania?**
A: In Romania, ICT sales professionals earn between 7,000 and 18,000 RON net per month, depending on experience, company size, and performance. Entry-level roles start around 5,000 RON, while senior specialists with a strong client portfolio can exceed 20,000 RON. Bonuses and commissions are common, often adding 10–30% to base pay.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU ICT Sales Professionals to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Romania via an employment visa (work permit) or the EU Blue Card. The work permit requires a job offer and approval from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified roles, requiring a higher education degree and a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: Which skills are most valued for ICT Sales Professionals in Romania?**
A: Employers prioritize strong communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills, paired with technical knowledge of IT products like cloud services, cybersecurity, or software solutions. Fluency in English is essential, and additional languages (German, French) are advantageous. Experience with CRM tools and a proven sales track record are highly desirable.
