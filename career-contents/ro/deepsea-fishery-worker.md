## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Romania?**
A: Salaries in Romania are often based on a share of the catch, so earnings vary. Full-time workers typically earn between the national minimum wage (approx. 3,000 RON/month) and 6,000 RON/month, depending on experience, vessel size, and season. Extra bonuses may apply for hazardous conditions.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as deep-sea fishery workers in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not suitable for deep-sea fishery workers as it requires a highly skilled job with a tertiary degree. Instead, non-EU workers can obtain a single work permit or a seasonal work visa, both requiring a confirmed job offer and employer sponsorship. The process is handled by the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for deep-sea fishery workers in Romania?**
A: Advancement typically includes moving from deckhand to boatswain or fishing master with experience and additional certifications. Some workers transition to onshore roles in fish processing, sales, or vessel maintenance. Further training in navigation or marine engineering can open higher-paying positions on larger vessels.
