**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Marking standardised assignments and exam papers
- Generate basic lesson plans and classroom materials
- Record and analyze student attendance and performance data
- Answering common knowledge questions (e.g., math calculations, spelling)
- Managing classroom multimedia equipment and basic IT troubleshooting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI adaptive learning platforms to customize exercises and feedback for each student
- Use AI to analyze student behavior and performance data to early identify learning difficulties or social-emotional issues
- Quickly generate differentiated teaching resources (e.g., reading materials at varying difficulty levels) using AI tools
- Using AI assistants to plan course progress and evaluate teaching effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- face-to-face emotional communication and building trust between teachers and students
- Managing unexpected classroom situations and behavior
- Tailored teaching based on deep understanding of individual students
- Passing on values, critical thinking, and social skills
- Complex communication and collaboration with parents

**Skills to build:**

- Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) and counselling skills

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for primary school teachers in Romania?**
A: Primary school teachers' salaries in Romania are set by public sector pay scales. A beginner teacher with a bachelor's degree earns a gross monthly salary of around 3,500 RON, increasing with experience and additional qualifications. After 10-15 years, gross monthly pay can reach 5,000-6,000 RON, plus allowances for special conditions or rural postings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as primary school teachers in Romania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU teachers can work in Romania through a standard work visa (long-stay for employment) after obtaining a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers earning at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, which may be difficult for teachers given typical pay scales. Additionally, teaching qualifications must be recognized by the Ministry of Education.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a primary school teacher in Romania?**
A: To become a primary school teacher, one needs a bachelor's degree in the field of education (e.g., Pedagogy of Primary and Preschool Education) and must pass a national tenure exam. Registration with the Romanian College of Teachers is mandatory for public schools. Continuous professional development and refresher courses are required to maintain certification.
