## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Woodworking Machine Tool Setters and Operators in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for this occupation in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 3,500 RON (approximately 500–700 EUR), depending on experience, region, and company size. Skilled operators with CNC expertise may earn up to 4,000 RON.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU nationals to work as Woodworking Machine Tool Setters and Operators in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a long-stay visa for employment and a work permit obtained by the employer from the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is typically for high-skilled roles requiring higher education; this occupation generally does not qualify. The employer must demonstrate a labor shortage.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for Woodworking Machine Tool Setters and Operators in Romania?**
A: Experienced operators can advance to team leader, quality inspector, or CNC programmer roles. Additional training in automated machinery or woodworking technology can lead to higher pay and supervisory positions. The Romanian furniture and construction sectors offer stable demand for skilled workers.
