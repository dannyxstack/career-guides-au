## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for messengers and package deliverers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, messengers and package deliverers typically earn between the national minimum wage (approximately RON 3,300 per month in 2024) and RON 4,500 per month. Earnings may vary by employer, location, and delivery volume. Some positions offer tips or performance bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a messenger or package deliverer in Romania under the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree or comparable qualifications, so it does not apply to low-skilled roles like messengers or package deliverers. Non-EU citizens must instead obtain a standard work permit and long-stay visa, which requires a job offer from a Romanian employer.

**Q: What are the typical requirements and career prospects for package deliverers in Romania?**
A: Requirements often include a valid driver’s license (for vehicle delivery), basic Romanian or English skills, and physical fitness. Career progression can lead to team leader, logistics coordinator, or courier franchise owner. Some companies offer training for advanced roles in logistics management.
