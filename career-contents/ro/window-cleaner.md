## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Romania?**
A: In Romania, window cleaners' wages often start at the national minimum gross wage, which is approximately 3,000 RON per month in 2023. Experienced cleaners in cities like Bucharest may earn 4,000–5,000 RON gross, while those working for specialized high-rise cleaning companies can earn more due to safety training requirements.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a window cleaner in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work visa, requiring a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled roles and is not typically issued for window cleaning. Migration routes are limited; candidates must meet Romanian labor market tests, and employers must prove difficulty finding local workers.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for window cleaners in Romania?**
A: Window cleaners can progress to team leader or supervisor roles within cleaning companies. Some pursue specialized high-rise cleaning, requiring safety certifications and offering higher pay. Others start their own cleaning businesses. Continuous training in new techniques and equipment can also open doors to facility management positions.
