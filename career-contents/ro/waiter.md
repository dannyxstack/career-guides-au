## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for waiters in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for waiters in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 4,000 Romanian Lei (RON) gross, depending on the city and establishment. Tips often add 10–20% to take-home pay. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (3,300 RON gross as of 2025), while experienced waiters in high-end restaurants can earn more.

**Q: Can non-EU waiters get a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU waiters can apply for a Romanian work visa if they have a job offer and the employer obtains a work permit. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for waiters, as it requires a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience. Seasonal work permits may also be an option for short-term employment.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for waiters in Romania?**
A: Waiters in Romania can advance to head waiter, restaurant supervisor, or manager roles with experience. Some pursue sommelier or bartending certifications to specialize. Others transition to event coordination or open their own food businesses. Language skills (English, French) and customer service training can boost prospects.
