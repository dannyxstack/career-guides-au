## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand packer in Romania?**
A: Hand packers in Romania typically earn around the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is 3,300 RON per month (gross). Actual pay may vary by employer and region, with experienced workers or those in sectors like manufacturing earning slightly more, up to 4,000 RON gross per month.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a hand packer in Romania via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers and does not apply to hand packers. Non-EU nationals can obtain a standard Romanian work permit for low-skilled labor, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled locally. Processing through Romanian immigration authorities is mandatory.

**Q: What are the career prospects for hand packers in Romania?**
A: Hand packers can advance to supervisory roles like team leader or line manager, especially in larger warehouses or factories. Additional training or experience may lead to positions in logistics, quality control, or inventory management. Advancement often depends on performance, tenure, and willingness to take on extra responsibilities.
