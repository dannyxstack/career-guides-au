## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a language teacher in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by institution and language. Public school teachers earn around 3,000–5,000 RON (€600–1,000) per month. Private language schools offer 4,000–7,000 RON, while international schools may pay higher. Part-time hourly rates range from 50–100 RON. Experienced teachers or those teaching in-demand languages like German can earn more.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU language teachers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and long-stay visa. Employers must demonstrate the job cannot be filled locally. For highly qualified roles, the EU Blue Card applies, requiring a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around 4,000 RON/month in 2023). Approval comes from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What are the career prospects for language teachers in Romania?**
A: Demand for English, French, German, and Spanish teachers is steady. Opportunities exist in public schools, private language centers, corporate training, and online platforms. Experienced teachers can become head of department, curriculum developer, or teach at universities and international schools. The field offers moderate growth and specialization options.
