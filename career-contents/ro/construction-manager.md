## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Construction Manager in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for a Construction Manager in Romania ranges from 8,000 to 15,000 RON (€1,600–€3,000), depending on experience, project size, and location. Senior managers on large projects can earn up to 20,000 RON or more. Benefits may include bonuses, company car, and health insurance.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU Construction Manager to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa for Romania via an employer-sponsored work permit, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers (requires a bachelor’s degree and salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). The employer must obtain prior approval from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Construction Manager in Romania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in civil engineering, construction management, or architecture is required. Professional experience of at least 3–5 years in construction supervision is expected. Certifications like PMP or project management courses are advantageous. Registration with the Romanian Order of Architects or Engineers may be necessary for certain projects.
