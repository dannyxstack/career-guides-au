## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a judge in Romania?**
A: According to the Romanian salary grid for the judiciary, a judge's net monthly salary varies by court level and experience. For example, a first-instance judge earns around 6,000-8,000 RON net, while a High Court judge earns up to 15,000 RON net. Additional bonuses apply. Salaries are set by Legea-cadru nr. 153/2017 and are periodically updated.

**Q: Can a foreign judge work in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU judges may apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer from a Romanian court and meet the salary threshold (currently at least 4 times the average gross salary). However, the legal profession is regulated; candidates must have their qualifications recognized by the Ministry of Justice and pass a language proficiency exam. A work permit is also required before the visa application.

**Q: What are the requirements to become a judge in Romania?**
A: To become a judge in Romania, one must hold a law degree, pass the National Institute of Magistracy entrance exam, complete a two-year training program, and subsequently pass a final exam. Candidates must be Romanian citizens, have full legal capacity, and no criminal record. After graduation, they are appointed by the Superior Council of Magistracy.
