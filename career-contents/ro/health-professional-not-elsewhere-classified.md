## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by field and experience. Entry-level professionals may earn around 3,000–5,000 RON gross per month, while experienced specialists can earn 7,000–12,000 RON or more. Public sector pay is lower than in private practice. Additional allowances may apply for underserved areas.

**Q: How can foreign health professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU professionals need a work permit and long-stay visa. For the EU Blue Card, they must have a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (around 9,800 RON in 2024) and recognized qualifications. The employer initiates the process at the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate. EU/EEA citizens do not need a visa.

**Q: What are the career prospects for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: Demand is high in rural areas and for specific specialties like public health, rehabilitation, or alternative medicine. Opportunities exist in hospitals, clinics, NGOs, and research. Continuing education is required for license renewal. Private practice can be lucrative but requires business registration.
