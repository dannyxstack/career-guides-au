## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for vocational education teachers in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for vocational education teachers in Romania ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 RON (approx. 800–1,200 EUR), depending on experience, qualifications, and school location. Salaries are set by national collective agreements and may increase with seniority or additional responsibilities.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for vocational teachers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU vocational teachers need a work permit obtained by the employer from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate, followed by a long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. 10,500 RON in 2024), which is typically above teacher pay, so the standard work permit is more common.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a vocational education teacher in Romania?**
A: To become a vocational teacher in Romania, you need a bachelor's degree in the relevant field and a teaching qualification (e.g., pedagogical module). Practical experience in the trade is highly valued. Teachers must pass a national exam for tenure and undergo periodic professional development. Foreign qualifications require recognition by the National Centre for Recognition and Equivalence of Diplomas.
