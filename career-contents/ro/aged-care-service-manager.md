## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Romania ranges from 8,000 to 12,000 RON (approximately 1,600 to 2,400 EUR), depending on experience, location, and the size of the facility. Senior managers in private or specialized centers may earn higher.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for a foreign Aged Care Services Manager to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Romania work visa (viza de muncă) or an EU Blue Card if they have a qualifying job offer and meet salary thresholds. The Blue Card requires a higher salary (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) and a recognized degree. Employers must prove labor market testing.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Aged Care Services Manager in Romania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in healthcare management, social work, or nursing is required, along with relevant managerial experience. Registration with the National Authority for Quality Management in Health (ANMCS) may be needed for managerial roles in accredited facilities. Additional training in gerontology is advantageous.
