## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for freight handlers in Romania?**
A: The average net monthly salary for freight handlers in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 3,500 RON (approximately 500-700 EUR). Pay varies by experience, employer, and location, with higher wages in logistics hubs like Bucharest or Constanța. Overtime and night shifts often attract additional allowances.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as freight handlers in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work in Romania through a work visa (long-stay visa for employment) after obtaining a work permit, sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is a valid route but typically requires a higher salary threshold (usually over 4,000 EUR gross/month), which is rare for freight handlers. Both are official channels under Romanian immigration law.

**Q: What are the typical career advancement opportunities for freight handlers in Romania?**
A: Freight handlers in Romania can progress to roles such as team leader, warehouse supervisor, or logistics coordinator. Additional certifications in forklift operation or inventory management can accelerate advancement. With experience, some move into supply chain management or transport planning positions.
