## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fumigator or pest controller in Romania?**
A: In Romania, the average monthly net salary for fumigators and pest controllers ranges between 2,500 and 4,000 RON, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around 2,000 RON, while experienced specialists can earn up to 5,000 RON. Some employers provide hazard pay or transportation allowances.

**Q: How can a foreign worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for fumigator roles in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-stay visa for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers (requires a university degree and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). For lower-skilled fumigator roles, a national work permit is required. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Both routes are official under Romanian immigration law.

**Q: What training or certification is required to become a fumigator in Romania?**
A: In Romania, fumigators and pest controllers must complete a professional qualification course accredited by the Ministry of Labour or the National Authority for Consumer Protection. They need a pesticide handling certificate. Training is available at vocational schools or private institutes. Certification must be renewed periodically through continuing education.
