## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, pet groomers and animal care workers earn an average gross monthly salary of approximately 2,500 to 4,000 RON (500-800 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 2,000 RON, while experienced professionals in Bucharest or high-end salons may earn up to 5,000 RON. Tips and commissions can supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as pet groomers or animal care workers in Romania via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Romania through the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. For pet groomers, the Blue Card is unlikely due to lower qualification requirements. Alternatively, a work permit (aviz de muncă) may be obtained if the employer proves the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Official routes are defined by Romanian Law no. 194/2011 and Government Emergency Ordinance 194/2002.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pet groomer in Romania?**
A: In Romania, formal qualifications are not mandatory, but completing a pet grooming course (e.g., from professional associations or private academies) is highly recommended. Courses cover dog breeds, grooming techniques, and safety. Some employers prefer candidates with a certificate. Experience with animals and good communication skills are essential. No specific license is required, but you must register your activity if self-employed.
