## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for personal services workers in Romania?**
A: As of 2024, the average monthly gross salary for personal services workers in Romania ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 RON (€700–€1,100), depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 RON, while experienced workers in high-demand roles may earn up to 6,500 RON.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for personal services jobs in Romania?**
A: Non-EU workers can obtain a work permit via a national long-stay visa or the EU Blue Card. The employer must apply at the General Inspectorate for Immigration. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and salary meeting national thresholds. Seasonal work permits are also available for temporary roles.

**Q: What are the main duties of personal services workers in Romania?**
A: Personal services workers in Romania provide services such as travel guidance, event assistance, or personal care. Duties may include organizing itineraries, escorting clients, offering local information, or helping with daily tasks. The role varies by employer, often requiring good communication and customer service skills.
