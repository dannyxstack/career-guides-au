## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bank tellers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for bank tellers in Romania ranges between 3,000 and 4,500 RON (approximately €600–€900). Salaries vary based on experience, bank size, and location, with higher pay in cities like Bucharest. Entry-level positions typically start around 2,800 RON gross.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as bank tellers in Romania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Bank teller positions in Romania usually do not meet these requirements. Instead, non-EU citizens can work via a standard work visa, which requires a job offer and an employer-sponsored work permit.

**Q: What are the main duties of a bank teller in Romania?**
A: Bank tellers in Romania handle cash transactions, deposits, withdrawals, and currency exchange. They provide customer service, answer account inquiries, and promote banking products. Accuracy, attention to detail, and basic computer skills are essential. Fluency in Romanian is typically required, though English may be needed in international banks.
