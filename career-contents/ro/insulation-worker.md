## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for insulation workers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, insulation workers earn an average gross monthly salary between 3,500 and 5,500 RON (approx. 700-1,100 EUR). Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced workers or those in construction insulation can earn higher. Pay varies by employer, location, and project type, with larger cities like Bucharest offering higher wages.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as insulation workers in Romania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card in Romania if they have a valid work contract for a highly qualified job. However, insulation worker positions typically require medium-skilled labor, which may not meet the 'highly qualified' threshold. Alternatively, they can obtain a national long-stay visa for employment (work permit) if the employer proves labor shortage and obtains approval from the Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What are the career prospects for insulation workers in Romania?**
A: Insulation workers in Romania can advance to team leaders or supervisors with experience. Additional certifications in energy efficiency or green building materials can boost opportunities. The growing demand for energy-efficient renovations in older buildings offers steady work. Some workers become self-employed contractors serving construction companies.
