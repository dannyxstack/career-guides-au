## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers (e.g., bicycle couriers or rickshaw drivers) in Romania ranges from 2,000 to 3,500 RON gross, depending on location and employer. Many drivers earn near the national minimum wage (2,550 RON in 2023) plus tips. Self-employed drivers may have higher variability. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU drivers can work in Romania via an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. 4,000 RON/month in 2023). Alternatively, a standard work permit (aviz de munca) is available for lower-skilled roles, requiring employer sponsorship and a positive labour market test. These routes follow Romanian Immigration Law.

**Q: What licensing or training is required to become a hand or pedal vehicle driver in Romania?**
A: No formal driver's license is needed for bicycles or pedal vehicles, but employers may require basic bike maintenance skills. For motorized vehicles (e.g., mopeds), a category AM or B license is mandatory. Many companies provide on-the-job safety training. Registration with the local tax authority is required if self-employed. Physical fitness and knowledge of city routes are advantageous.
