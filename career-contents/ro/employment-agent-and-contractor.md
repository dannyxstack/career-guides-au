## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for employment agents and contractors in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for employment agents and contractors in Romania typically ranges from 3,000 to 6,000 RON, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 2,500 RON, while senior agents can earn up to 8,000 RON. Bonuses and commissions are common in this field.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for non-EU employment agents in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as employment agents in Romania via a work visa or the EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. Both routes require approval from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an employment agent in Romania?**
A: Typically, employers prefer candidates with a bachelor's degree in human resources, business administration, psychology, or a related field. Relevant experience in recruitment or sales is highly valued. Professional certifications, such as those from the Romanian Association of Human Resources Specialists (ARHR), can enhance job prospects. Fluency in Romanian and English is often required.
