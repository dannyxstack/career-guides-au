## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physicists and astronomers in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Entry-level physicists earn about 3,500–5,000 RON per month, while experienced professionals can earn 8,000–15,000 RON. Academia and public research institutes (e.g., IFIN-HH) offer lower pay than private-sector roles in technology, energy, or finance. The national average for physicists is roughly 6,000 RON monthly.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for foreign physicists to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU physicists need a work permit and a long-stay visa (type D). The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 4 times the national minimum wage). Researchers can also obtain a National Visa for scientific research (Directive 2005/71/EC), which does not require a work permit.

**Q: Which are the main employers for physicists in Romania?**
A: Key employers include public research institutes such as IFIN-HH (Horia Hulubei National Institute for Physics and Nuclear Engineering), INRNE (Institute for Nuclear Research), and universities like the University of Bucharest and Babeș-Bolyai University. Private-sector opportunities exist in companies like Continental, Honeywell, and Romacril, as well as in IT, renewable energy, and automotive R&D.
