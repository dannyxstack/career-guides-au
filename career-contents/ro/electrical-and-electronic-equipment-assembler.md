## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Romania?**
A: Entry-level positions typically earn around 2,500 RON net per month, while experienced assemblers can earn up to 4,000 RON net. Additional benefits may include meal vouchers and transport subsidies. Overtime is often compensated at higher rates. Salaries vary by region and employer.

**Q: What work visa options are available for foreign electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-stay work visa for employment. The employer must obtain a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Office. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as it requires higher education or specialized skills. Alternatively, intra-company transfers may be possible.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Romania?**
A: With experience, assemblers can become team leaders, quality inspectors, or maintenance technicians. Further training or certifications can lead to roles in production supervision or technical support. Some companies offer internal promotion programs. Advanced knowledge in automation can open doors to more specialized positions.
