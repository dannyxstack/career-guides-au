## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for garden and horticultural labourers in Romania typically range from the national minimum wage (around 3,000 RON gross in 2024) to 3,500 RON gross per month. Actual pay varies by employer, region, and experience. Overtime or seasonal work may offer additional compensation. Benefits like accommodation or meals are sometimes provided.

**Q: Can foreign workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work as garden and horticultural labourers in Romania?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers with higher education qualifications or specialized experience. Third-country nationals can work in this occupation via a standard work permit. The employer must apply to the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate, proving the job cannot be filled locally. The permit is usually tied to one employer and is valid for up to one year.

**Q: What are the main duties of garden and horticultural labourers in Romania?**
A: Duties include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, fertilizing, and harvesting crops, flowers, and plants. They maintain gardens, parks, greenhouses, and nurseries. Tasks also involve operating hand tools and light machinery, applying pesticides, and preparing soil. Knowledge of seasonal cycles and plant care is essential. Physical stamina and outdoor work are typical.
