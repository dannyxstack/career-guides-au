## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a lawyer in Romania?**
A: Entry-level lawyers with 0-3 years of experience earn between 2,000 and 3,000 EUR gross per month. Senior lawyers with over 10 years can earn 5,000 EUR or more. In-house counsel typically earn slightly less than those in private practice. Bonuses and profit-sharing are common in large international firms.

**Q: How can a foreign lawyer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU lawyers need a long-stay work visa supported by a valid employment contract from a Romanian employer. For the EU Blue Card, you must have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (set at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) and recognized qualifications. Diplomas may require nostrification. Apply at the Romanian consulate in your country.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a lawyer in Romania?**
A: You need a law degree (5 years of study), must pass the national bar exam (examen de admitere în barou), and complete a 2-year traineeship. Non-EU lawyers must have their diplomas recognized and may need to pass additional exams. Continuous professional development is required to maintain licensure.
