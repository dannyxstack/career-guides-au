## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Chemical Engineer in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a chemical engineer in Romania ranges from 6,000 to 10,000 RON (approximately €1,200–€2,000), depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions start around 4,500 RON, while senior roles in petrochemicals or pharmaceuticals can exceed 15,000 RON. Salaries are higher in Bucharest and in multinational companies.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign Chemical Engineer to work in Romania under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: Foreign chemical engineers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Romania if they have a qualifying job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €20,000 in 2024) and hold a recognized degree. The employer must prove the vacancy could not be filled locally. The Blue Card is valid for up to 2 years and renewable, allowing family reunification.

**Q: What industries employ most Chemical Engineers in Romania?**
A: Chemical engineers in Romania are primarily employed in petrochemicals (e.g., OMV Petrom), pharmaceuticals (e.g., Zentiva, Bioeel), and food processing. Other sectors include cosmetics, plastics, and environmental engineering. Research institutes and universities also offer opportunities. The industry is concentrated around Bucharest, Ploiești, and Pitești.
