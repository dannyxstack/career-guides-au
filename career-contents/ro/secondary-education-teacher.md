## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for secondary education teachers in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for secondary education teachers in Romania ranges from 4,500 to 5,500 RON (approximately 900–1,100 EUR), depending on experience, qualifications, and school location. Additional income may come from extracurricular activities or bonuses. Salaries are set by the Education Law and are subject to periodic adjustments.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU secondary education teachers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU teachers can work in Romania via a work visa or the EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and a work permit from the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals, requiring a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a secondary education teacher in Romania?**
A: To become a secondary education teacher in Romania, you need a bachelor's degree in the relevant subject and completion of a teacher training program (Module psihopedagogic). After starting teaching, you must pass the national tenure exam (Definitivat) within two years to obtain permanent status. Continuous professional development is also required.
