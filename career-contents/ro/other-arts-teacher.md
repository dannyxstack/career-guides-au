## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for other arts teachers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for other arts teachers in Romania vary by institution and experience. Public school teachers earn around 4,000–6,000 RON (800–1,200 EUR) net per month, while private tutors or freelance artists may earn more. Senior roles or specialized workshops can reach 8,000 RON (1,600 EUR) monthly.

**Q: How can a foreigner work as an other arts teacher in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Foreigners need a job offer from a recognized institution. Apply for a long-stay work visa (type D) at the Romanian embassy. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card requires a high-skilled job with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. 5,400 RON monthly in 2024). Both routes lead to a residence permit.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become an other arts teacher in Romania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in arts education or a related field is required, plus a teaching certification (Definitivat). For private institutions, proven experience and portfolio may suffice. Public schools require passing national exams (titularizare) and fluency in Romanian, though English may be accepted in international schools.
