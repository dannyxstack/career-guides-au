## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: Salaries for elementary workers in Romania vary by sector but generally range from 2,500 to 3,500 RON per month (gross) for full-time employment, depending on experience and location. The national minimum wage is around 3,000 RON gross as of 2023, so many earn near this level.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as an elementary worker in Romania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job with at least 1.5x the average gross salary, so it is not applicable for most elementary roles. Instead, non-EU workers can obtain a standard Romanian work permit, which requires employer sponsorship and proof that no EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are common job duties for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: These roles include manual tasks such as cleaning, packing, basic construction labor, or assisting in warehouses and factories. Duties vary widely but generally require no specialized training. Workers follow simple instructions and perform repetitive tasks, often in sectors like hospitality, manufacturing, or logistics.
