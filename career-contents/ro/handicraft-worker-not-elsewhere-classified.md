## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for handicraft workers in Romania?**
A: Earnings vary by specialization, experience, and region. According to national statistics, average monthly gross salaries for handicraft workers range from 2,500 to 4,500 RON (approximately 500–900 EUR). Self-employed artisans may earn more but face irregular income. Minimum wage is 3,000 RON gross per month as of 2024.

**Q: Can non-EU handicraft workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU workers need a long-stay work visa (viza de muncă) sponsored by a Romanian employer. The employer must obtain a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is rarely applicable, as it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage, which handicraft jobs typically do not meet.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to work as a handicraft worker in Romania?**
A: Formal education is not mandatory, but vocational training or apprenticeships are highly recommended. The National Qualifications Authority (ANC) recognizes specialized certificates in various crafts. Master craftsman exams, offered by professional guilds, can improve career prospects. Completing a state-recognized vocational school program also boosts employability.
