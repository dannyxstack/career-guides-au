## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services and Administration Managers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for business services and administration managers in Romania vary by industry and experience, typically ranging from 8,000 to 15,000 RON gross per month. Senior roles in multinational companies or specialized sectors may exceed this range.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for Business Services and Administration Managers in Romania?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor's degree in business administration, management, or a related field, along with at least 3-5 years of relevant experience. Professional certifications (e.g., PMP, MBA) and fluency in English are advantageous. Knowledge of Romanian labor law and the local market is often expected.
