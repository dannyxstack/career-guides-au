## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a customs inspector in Romania?**
A: The net monthly salary for a customs inspector in Romania typically ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 RON for entry-level positions, increasing with experience and seniority. Additional allowances may apply for overtime or special assignments. Salaries are set by public sector pay scales and are subject to annual adjustments.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals become customs inspectors in Romania through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Customs inspector positions in Romania are part of the public administration, generally requiring Romanian or EU citizenship. The EU Blue Card is intended for highly qualified professionals in private-sector roles, so it is not a viable route for this occupation. Non-EU nationals would need to check with Romanian immigration for any exceptional work permit possibilities.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for customs inspectors in Romania?**
A: Customs inspectors can progress to senior inspector, team leader, or managerial roles within the Romanian Customs Authority. Specialization in areas like risk analysis, anti-fraud, or international trade law can lead to positions in EU institutions or private consultancy. Continuous training and language skills are key for advancement.
