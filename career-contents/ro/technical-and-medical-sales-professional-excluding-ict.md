## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Romania?**
A: Entry-level professionals earn around 3,500-5,000 RON monthly (€700-1,000), while experienced ones can earn 8,000-15,000 RON (€1,600-3,000) plus commissions. Top earners in specialized fields like pharmaceutical sales may exceed 20,000 RON (€4,000) monthly. Bonuses and benefits vary by company.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for foreign Technical and Medical Sales Professionals to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a long-term work visa and a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals with a valid employment contract earning at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. 5,400 RON/month in 2024). Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Romania?**
A: Professionals can progress to senior sales roles, regional sales managers, or key account managers. Many move into marketing or product management. With experience, some become independent consultants. Multinational companies in Romania often offer clear career paths and training programs, enabling advancement within 3-5 years.
