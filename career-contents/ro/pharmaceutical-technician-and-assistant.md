## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a pharmaceutical technician in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a pharmaceutical technician in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 3,500 RON (approximately 500–700 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while those in major cities like Bucharest may earn at the higher end. This is below the EU Blue Card salary threshold.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU pharmaceutical technician to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as pharmaceutical technicians in Romania via two official routes: a standard work permit (obtained by the employer for a specific job) or, if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold, an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Both require a valid job offer and approval from the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pharmaceutical technician in Romania?**
A: To work as a pharmaceutical technician in Romania, you need a post-secondary diploma in pharmacy assistance (3 years) or a similar qualification recognized by the Ministry of Health. Registration with the College of Pharmacists in Romania is mandatory. Proficiency in Romanian is typically required, as communication with patients and doctors is in the local language.
