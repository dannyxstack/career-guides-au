## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Romania?**
A: The average monthly net salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Romania ranges from 3,000 to 5,000 RON, depending on experience, qualifications, and location. Entry-level positions may start around 2,500 RON, while senior mechanics or those working in large cities like Bucharest can earn up to 6,000 RON or more.

**Q: Can foreign air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics work in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU mechanics can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid work contract in Romania with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. They must also hold a recognized qualification or at least five years of professional experience. The application is submitted to the Romanian General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What qualifications or certifications are required to work as an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Romania?**
A: In Romania, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics typically need a vocational school diploma in refrigeration or a related field, or an apprenticeship. Certification such as the European Refrigeration Certificate (Cat I-IV) is highly valued. For handling refrigerants, a national license from the Romanian Environmental Protection Agency is required. Continuous training is recommended to stay updated with new technologies.
