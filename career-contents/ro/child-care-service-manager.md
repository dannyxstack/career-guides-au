## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Child Care Services Manager in Romania?**
A: Salaries for child care services managers in Romania vary widely. Entry-level positions may earn around 3,000–4,500 RON monthly, while experienced managers in private centers can earn 6,000–8,000 RON. The average is approximately 5,000 RON per month, but can be higher in larger cities like Bucharest. Bonuses and benefits depend on the employer.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a foreign Child Care Services Manager to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a long-stay visa for employment, followed by a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a Bachelor's degree and a job offer paying at least the average gross salary (approx. 6,000 RON/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard employment visa applies if the role meets labor market needs. Employer sponsorship is required.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Child Care Services Manager in Romania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in early childhood education, social work, or a related field is required. Additionally, managers often need 3–5 years of experience in child care settings. For registered care facilities, a certificate in management or supervisory training may be mandatory. Continuous professional development is encouraged, and some roles require knowledge of Romanian regulations and child protection laws.
