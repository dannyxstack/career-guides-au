## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwifery associate professional in Romania?**
A: Salaries for midwifery associate professionals in Romania typically range from 2,500 to 4,000 RON per month gross, depending on experience, workplace (public vs. private), and region. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced professionals in private clinics can earn higher.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a midwifery associate professional in Romania via the EU Blue Card or a work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work permit for Romania if an employer sponsors them. The EU Blue Card is available only for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, which typically does not apply to this occupation. A standard work permit is the usual route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a midwifery associate professional in Romania?**
A: To work as a midwifery associate professional in Romania, you need a post-secondary diploma in midwifery from an accredited medical school (typically a 3-year program) and registration with the Romanian Order of Midwives (Ordinul Moașelor din România). The profession is regulated, and practice without registration is not permitted.
