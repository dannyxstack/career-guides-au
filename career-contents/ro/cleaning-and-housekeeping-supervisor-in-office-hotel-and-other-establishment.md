## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a cleaning and housekeeping supervisor in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a cleaning and housekeeping supervisor in Romania ranges from 3,000 to 5,000 RON, depending on experience, location, and establishment type. Senior supervisors in luxury hotels may earn up to 6,000 RON. Benefits often include meals and accommodation in hospitality settings.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign national to work as a cleaning and housekeeping supervisor in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit obtained by the employer, followed by a long-stay visa (Type D). The EU Blue Card is available for highly-skilled workers but typically requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. For this occupation, the standard work permit is more common.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for cleaning and housekeeping supervisors in Romania?**
A: With experience, supervisors can advance to managerial roles such as housekeeping manager or executive housekeeper in hotels, or facilities management in offices. Additional training in hospitality management or sanitation can lead to regional or corporate positions. Fluency in English and Romanian is advantageous.
