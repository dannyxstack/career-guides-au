## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a structural metal preparer and erector in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level structural metal preparers and erectors earn around 2,500-3,500 RON per month (approx. 500-700 EUR). Experienced workers can earn 4,000-6,000 RON (800-1,200 EUR). Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in cities like Bucharest, Timișoara, and Cluj-Napoca. Overtime and project bonuses may increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a structural metal preparer and erector in Romania through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Romania via a standard work visa (long-stay visa for employment) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The EU Blue Card requires a valid employment contract with a salary at least 1.5 times the gross average Romanian salary. For lower-skilled positions, a regular work permit sponsored by the employer is more common. Always check with the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration for current requirements.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a structural metal preparer and erector in Romania?**
A: Typically, a vocational school certificate or completion of a short training program in metalworking or construction is required. Many workers learn through apprenticeships. Certification in welding (e.g., EN 1090 or ISO 9606) is often mandatory for certain tasks. On-the-job training is common, and experience with cranes, rigging, and safety protocols is valued. No university degree is needed.
