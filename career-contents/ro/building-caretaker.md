## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building caretaker in Romania?**
A: In Romania, building caretakers typically earn between 2,500 and 3,500 RON per month gross, depending on location and employer. The national minimum wage in 2024 is about 2,550 RON. Salaries are higher in major cities like Bucharest and lower in rural areas. Some employers may provide housing or utility benefits.

**Q: What are the work visa options for building caretakers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a long-stay visa for employment, requiring a work permit obtained by the employer from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is not typically accessible for building caretakers as it targets highly qualified workers. For temporary assignments, a posted worker permit may be used. All routes require a valid job offer.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a building caretaker in Romania?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers usually require a high school diploma. Basic skills in cleaning, minor repairs, and maintenance are valued. Knowledge of Romanian or English is often necessary. Previous experience in janitorial or caretaking roles is advantageous. On-the-job training is commonly provided.
