## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physiotherapist in Romania?**
A: Salaries for physiotherapists in Romania generally range from 4,000 to 6,000 RON per month gross (approximately 800–1,200 EUR), depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and location. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced specialists in private clinics can earn higher.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for physiotherapists moving to Romania?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Romania via a work permit or an EU Blue Card. Requirements include a recognized physiotherapy degree, a job offer from a Romanian employer, and for the Blue Card, a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Registration with the Romanian College of Physiotherapists is also mandatory.

**Q: How do I get licensed to practice as a physiotherapist in Romania?**
A: To practice in Romania, you need a recognized degree in physiotherapy, then register with the Romanian College of Physiotherapists (Colegiul Fizioterapeuților din România). EU/EEA graduates have automatic recognition; non-EU degrees require equivalency recognition from the Ministry of Education. Continuing education is required to maintain the license.
