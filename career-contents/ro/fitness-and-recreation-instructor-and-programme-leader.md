## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fitness and recreation instructors in Romania?**
A: Salaries for fitness and recreation instructors in Romania vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions earn around 2,000–3,000 RON per month, while experienced instructors can earn 4,000–6,000 RON. Those with specialized certifications or working in high-end gyms may earn more. Self-employed trainers often have higher but variable earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU fitness instructors to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Romania via a work permit (obtained by employer) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a fitness instructor in Romania?**
A: While not legally mandatory, most employers prefer a certification from a recognized body like AFAA, ACE, or the Romanian Federation of Sports. A degree in physical education or sports science is advantageous. First aid certification is often required. Continuous professional development is valued for career advancement.
