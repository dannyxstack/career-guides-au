## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Romania?**
A: Average gross monthly salary for HR specialists in Romania ranges from 5,000 to 8,000 RON (approx. 1,000-1,600 EUR), depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior roles or those in multinational firms can earn up to 12,000 RON. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for foreign Personnel and Careers Professionals to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. 7,000 RON/month in 2024). Alternatively, a long-stay work visa for employment is available, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from the Immigration Office.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Romania?**
A: Typical career paths include advancing to HR manager, recruitment team lead, or talent acquisition specialist. Many professionals move into HR business partnering or organizational development. Certification like CIPD or local HR diplomas can enhance prospects. The demand for HR analytics and digital HR skills is growing.
