## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Entry-level positions may earn around 2,500–3,500 RON gross per month, while experienced sales staff can earn 4,000–6,000 RON or more. Commission and bonuses often supplement base pay. Larger cities like Bucharest tend to offer higher wages.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU sales workers to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a work permit sponsored by a Romanian employer, or for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. 1.5 times average gross salary). Both require employer proof that the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for sales workers in Romania?**
A: Sales workers can advance to senior sales roles, team leader, or sales manager positions. Many companies offer internal training and development programs. Proficiency in English and digital sales tools can accelerate growth. Some transition into key account management or business development.
