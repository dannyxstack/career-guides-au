## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cleaning workers in Romania?**
A: In Romania, cleaning workers typically earn between 2,500 and 3,500 RON (roughly 500-700 EUR) per month before tax. Actual pay varies by employer, location, and hours. Many are paid the minimum wage, which was 3,300 RON gross per month for full-time work as of 2024. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for cleaning workers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa and a job offer to work as a cleaner in Romania. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because it requires a higher education degree or high-skilled job. Instead, employers must apply for a work permit through the General Inspectorate for Immigration. The process involves labour market testing and proof of accommodation.

**Q: What are typical duties for cleaning workers in Romania?**
A: Cleaning workers in Romania perform tasks like sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, cleaning windows, and emptying trash. They work in offices, hotels, hospitals, or private homes. Some use industrial cleaning equipment or handle waste disposal. Duties may vary by sector, but basic hygiene and safety standards are required.
