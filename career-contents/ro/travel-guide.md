## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a travel guide in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for a travel guide in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 4,500 RON (approx. 500-900 EUR) net, depending on experience, language skills, and employer. Seasonal work or tips can increase earnings. Major tourist destinations like Bucharest, Brașov, or the coast may offer higher pay.

**Q: What official routes exist for foreign travel guides to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit sponsored by a Romanian employer, followed by a long-stay visa. EU citizens can work freely. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; travel guide roles typically do not meet the skill threshold. Start-up or intra-corporate transfer visas are less common for this occupation.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a travel guide in Romania?**
A: There is no mandatory national license, but employers prefer certification from the Ministry of Tourism or a recognized course. Fluency in Romanian and at least one foreign language (English, French, German, etc.) is essential. Knowledge of Romanian history, culture, and geography is expected. Many guides hold a degree in tourism or related fields.
