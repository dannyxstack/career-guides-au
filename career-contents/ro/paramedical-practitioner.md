## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for paramedical practitioners in Romania?**
A: In Romania, paramedical practitioners typically earn between 3,000 and 5,500 RON per month (approximately 600–1,100 EUR) depending on experience, location, and employer. Public sector positions may offer lower base pay but include benefits, while private clinics and ambulance services often provide higher salaries. Senior or specialized roles can reach 7,000 RON.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card requirements for paramedical practitioners moving to Romania?**
A: Non-EU paramedical practitioners can work in Romania via an EU Blue Card (for high-skilled roles) or a standard work visa. Requirements include a valid job offer, recognized qualifications (evaluated by the Ministry of Health or CNRED), and proof of professional experience. The Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. All applications are processed through Romanian diplomatic missions.

**Q: What is the process for qualification recognition for paramedical practitioners in Romania?**
A: Paramedical practitioners must have their qualifications recognized by the Romanian Ministry of Health. This involves submitting diplomas, transcripts, and proof of professional experience. For EU degrees, recognition is streamlined under Directive 2005/36/EC. Non-EU qualifications may require nostrification. Practitioners must also register with the relevant professional body, such as the Romanian College of Nurses and Midwives for certain roles.
