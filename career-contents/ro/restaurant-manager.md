## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a restaurant manager in Romania?**
A: The salary for restaurant managers in Romania typically ranges from 5,000 to 10,000 RON per month (approximately 1,000 to 2,000 EUR), depending on experience, location, and the size of the establishment. In major cities like Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, or Timișoara, salaries tend to be higher, with experienced managers in upscale venues earning up to 15,000 RON.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU restaurant manager to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as restaurant managers in Romania via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national long-stay visa for employment (Work Visa). The EU Blue Card requires a recognized diploma and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. The Work Visa process involves securing a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration and a job offer from a Romanian employer.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for restaurant managers in Romania?**
A: Restaurant managers in Romania can advance to regional manager, operations manager, or director of food and beverage within large hotel chains or restaurant groups. Some pursue entrepreneurship, opening their own venues. Certification in hospitality management or business administration can accelerate career growth, as can specialising in fine dining, sustainable gastronomy, or events management.
