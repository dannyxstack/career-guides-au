## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building architect in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for a building architect in Romania ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 RON (approximately 800–1,200 EUR) for mid-level professionals. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 RON, while senior architects can earn over 8,000 RON. Salaries vary by experience, location (Bucharest typically higher), and firm size.

**Q: Can foreign building architects work in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, foreign building architects can work in Romania via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Requirements include a valid employment contract, recognized higher education qualifications (equivalent to at least bachelor's), and a salary meeting the national threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). The employer must apply at the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What are the key steps to become a licensed building architect in Romania?**
A: To practice as a building architect in Romania, you must graduate from a recognized architecture program, complete a supervised internship (stagiu), and pass the national proficiency exam. After passing, you register with the Romanian Order of Architects (OAR) to obtain full practice rights. Foreign qualifications may require equivalency recognition from the National Centre for Recognition and Equivalence of Diplomas (CNRED).
