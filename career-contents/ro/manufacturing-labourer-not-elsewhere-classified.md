## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a manufacturing labourer in Romania?**
A: The salary for manufacturing labourers in Romania usually falls between the national minimum gross wage (approx. 3,300 RON per month in 2024) and 4,500 RON. Overtime and shift differentials may apply. Entry-level positions often start at minimum wage, with slight increases based on experience and employer.

**Q: What visa options are available for non-EU nationals to work as a manufacturing labourer in Romania?**
A: Non-EU workers need a long-stay visa for employment, preceded by a work permit obtained by the employer from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage, so it is typically not used for labourers. The employer must demonstrate a labor shortage.

**Q: What are the main duties of a manufacturing labourer in Romania?**
A: Manufacturing labourers perform tasks such as assembling products, packing goods, operating basic machinery, and cleaning work areas. They work under supervision in factories or workshops. Skills like attention to detail and physical endurance are important. Advancement may lead to team leader or specialized operator roles, often requiring additional training.
