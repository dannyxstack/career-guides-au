## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a software developer in Romania?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Romania vary by experience. Junior developers earn around €1,200–€2,000 per month gross, mid-level professionals €2,500–€4,000, and senior developers can exceed €5,000–€7,000. These figures depend on the company, city (e.g., Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca), and specialization. Many employers also offer bonuses and benefits like meal vouchers and health insurance.

**Q: How can a non-EU software developer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU software developers need a long-stay visa for employment followed by a residence permit. The EU Blue Card requires a higher-education degree (or 5+ years experience) and a job offer paying at least 2× the national average gross salary (approx. €4,500/month in 2024). Alternatively, employers can sponsor a work permit under the annual quota. Both routes follow Romanian Immigration Office procedures.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for software developers in Romania?**
A: Romania’s thriving IT sector offers clear career paths: from junior to senior developer, team lead, engineering manager, or architect. Many companies support upskilling through certifications, tech conferences, and internal mobility. The presence of multinationals and startups allows developers to specialize in areas like AI, cybersecurity, or cloud computing, often with paths to roles like CTO or founder.
