## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Romania?**
A: In Romania, gross monthly salaries for this role typically range from 3,500 to 5,500 RON, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around 3,000 RON, while senior technicians can earn up to 6,500 RON. Net income is lower after social contributions and income tax.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for a work permit or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; for this occupation, you need a job offer with a salary at least twice the average gross salary (approx. 8,000 RON/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit requires employer sponsorship and approval from the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What are the typical duties of a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Romania?**
A: Technicians manage patient health information, ensuring accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance with Romanian data protection laws. Tasks include coding diagnoses and procedures using ICD-10, maintaining electronic health records, preparing reports for healthcare providers, and facilitating data retrieval for audits or research.
