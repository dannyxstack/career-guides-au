## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for environmental protection professionals in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level professionals earn around 4,000–5,500 RON gross monthly, while senior roles reach 9,000–12,000 RON. Public sector pay is lower but stable; private consulting and international companies offer higher compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU environmental protection professionals work in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. You need a job offer from a Romanian employer meeting the EU Blue Card salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). You must have a recognized degree in environmental science or related field. The employer applies for a work permit, then you apply for the Blue Card at the Romanian embassy.

**Q: Which types of employers hire environmental protection professionals in Romania?**
A: Key employers include the Ministry of Environment, local environmental protection agencies (APM), private environmental consulting firms, NGOs like WWF Romania, and companies in energy, mining, or manufacturing with compliance needs. The IT sector also offers roles in environmental data analysis and sustainability reporting.
