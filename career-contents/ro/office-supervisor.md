## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Office Supervisor in Romania?**
A: The average monthly salary for an Office Supervisor in Romania ranges from 4,500 to 7,500 RON (approximately 900–1,500 EUR), depending on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions start around 3,500 RON, while senior supervisors in multinational firms may earn up to 10,000 RON. Benefits often include meal tickets and private health insurance.

**Q: Can an Office Supervisor from outside the EU obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU Office Supervisors can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (currently around 6,792 RON/month). Alternatively, a standard work visa (long-stay visa for employment) is available with a valid work permit, which the employer obtains from the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: What are the typical career progression opportunities for an Office Supervisor in Romania?**
A: Office Supervisors in Romania can advance to senior supervisory roles, office manager, or operations manager positions. With experience, they may move into administration management, human resources, or general management. Multinational companies offer clear career paths, including cross-departmental roles. Professional certifications in management or project management can accelerate promotion.
