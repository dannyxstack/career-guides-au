## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for other ranks in the Romanian Armed Forces?**
A: The average monthly salary for other ranks (soldiers and non-commissioned officers) in the Romanian Armed Forces ranges from approximately 3,500 to 6,000 RON (€700–€1,200), depending on rank, seniority, and specializations. Additional benefits include housing allowance, free medical care, and pension schemes.

**Q: Can foreign nationals join the Romanian Armed Forces as other ranks via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No, service in the Romanian Armed Forces is restricted to Romanian citizens by law. Foreign nationals cannot obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for military roles. The only official route for non-citizens is through naturalization after meeting residency requirements, which does not guarantee military service.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for other ranks in the Romanian military?**
A: Other ranks can advance from soldier to non-commissioned officer (NCO) through promotion exams and training. Career paths include specialization in combat, logistics, or technical roles. Further advancement to warrant officer or officer is possible via military academies or commissioning programs. Service contracts typically range from 4 to 12 years.
