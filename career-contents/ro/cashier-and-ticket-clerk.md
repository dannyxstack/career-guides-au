## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for cashiers and ticket clerks in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Romania is between 2,500 and 3,500 RON (approximately 500-700 EUR). Entry-level positions start around 2,000 RON, while experienced workers can earn up to 4,000 RON. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Bucharest and other major cities.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as cashiers or ticket clerks in Romania via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in these occupations through a standard Romanian work visa, as the EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and higher salary thresholds typically not met by cashier roles. The employer must obtain a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration before the visa application. Permanent residence may be possible after 5 years of continuous legal stay.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for cashiers and ticket clerks in Romania?**
A: With experience, cashiers can advance to senior cashier, team leader, or supervisor roles in retail or transportation companies. Ticket clerks may transition to customer service management, ticket office administration, or revenue control positions. Additional skills in accounting or IT can lead to roles in treasury or systems operations. Some workers move into sales or logistics.
