## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for underwater divers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Romania vary by experience, specialization, and sector. Entry-level divers can earn around 3,500–4,500 RON net per month, while experienced divers in offshore or commercial diving may earn 6,000–9,000 RON net. Rates are often project-based, with additional pay for deep-water or hazardous conditions.

**Q: Can non-EU underwater divers work in Romania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU divers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree or at least 5 years of relevant professional experience. They must secure a job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times Romania’s average gross salary. The employer initiates the work permit process, and the Blue Card allows residence and work in Romania.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for underwater divers in Romania?**
A: Underwater divers in Romania can advance by obtaining higher certifications such as HSE (Health and Safety Executive) or IMCA (International Marine Contractors Association) standards. Career paths include becoming a dive supervisor, saturation diver, or specializing in offshore oil and gas, underwater welding, or marine research. Experienced divers may also start their own diving services company.
