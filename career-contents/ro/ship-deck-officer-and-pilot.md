## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for ships' deck officers and pilots in Romania?**
A: The typical monthly gross salary for ships' deck officers in Romania ranges from 4,000 to 8,000 RON for junior officers and 10,000 to 15,000 RON for senior officers and pilots. Salaries depend on rank, company, and vessel type. Note that earnings can increase with experience and specialization in offshore or large commercial vessels.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign deck officers and pilots to work in Romania via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Foreign deck officers and pilots can work in Romania through a work visa (viza de muncă) or the EU Blue Card. Requirements include a valid job offer from a Romanian shipping company, proof of qualifications recognized by the Romanian Naval Authority, and compliance with salary thresholds. For the EU Blue Card, the gross annual salary must exceed 1.5 times the national average. Official routes are processed through the Romanian Immigration Office.

**Q: What are the career prospects for deck officers and pilots in Romania?**
A: Career prospects for deck officers and pilots in Romania are supported by maritime training institutes in Constanța and Galați. Romanian-flagged vessels and international shipping companies actively recruit. Advancement from deck cadet to captain typically requires 5-10 years of sea service and certification exams. Opportunities also exist in port pilotage and maritime administration.
