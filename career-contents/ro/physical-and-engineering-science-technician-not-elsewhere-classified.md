## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Romania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for technicians in this category in Romania typically ranges from 4,500 to 6,500 RON, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions may start around 3,500 RON, while senior roles or those in high-demand industries such as automotive or IT can exceed 7,000 RON.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign Physical and Engineering Science Technicians to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid employment contract, a recognized degree, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Alternatively, a national long-stay visa for employment (work permit) is available for jobs meeting local labor market needs. Applications are processed by the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration.

**Q: Which industries employ Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Romania?**
A: These technicians work in manufacturing, energy, environmental monitoring, construction, and research laboratories. Key sectors include automotive, aerospace, electronics, and IT. Major employment hubs are Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara, and Iași, where industrial parks and multinational companies are concentrated.
