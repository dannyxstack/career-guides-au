## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Metal Production Process Controllers in Romania?**
A: Salaries for this role in Romania generally range from 3,500 to 6,500 RON per month gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 RON, while senior controllers in large plants may earn up to 8,000 RON. Overtime and shift bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation in Romania?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer as a Metal Production Process Controller, a relevant degree (or 5+ years' experience), and a salary meeting the Romanian threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Alternatively, a national long-stay work visa is possible with a valid employment contract and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a Metal Production Process Controller in Romania?**
A: They monitor and control metal production processes using computerized systems and industrial equipment. Typical duties include adjusting temperature, pressure, and flow rates; inspecting product quality; recording production data; and coordinating with maintenance teams. In Romania, common employers are steel mills, non-ferrous metal plants, and foundries.
