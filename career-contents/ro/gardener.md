## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for gardeners in Romania ranges from 2,500 to 3,500 RON (approximately 500–700 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced gardeners in large cities or specialized roles can earn up to 4,500 RON. Salaries are often higher in regions like Bucharest.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for gardeners to work legally in Romania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA gardeners typically need a long-stay visa for employment, secured via a job offer and work permit obtained by the employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a higher salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) and a tertiary education degree; it rarely applies to gardeners unless the role involves specialized horticulture management.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for gardeners in Romania?**
A: Gardeners can advance to head gardener, landscape designer, or horticultural technician. With experience and certifications (e.g., in landscaping or plant care), they may work for municipalities, hotels, or private estates. Some start their own landscaping businesses. Seasonal work is common, but permanent roles exist in larger companies or public parks.
