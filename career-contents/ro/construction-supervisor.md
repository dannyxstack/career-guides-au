## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for construction supervisors in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for construction supervisors in Romania ranges between 5,000 and 8,000 RON (approximately 1,000–1,600 EUR), depending on experience, project complexity, and region. Senior supervisors or those on large infrastructure projects may earn up to 10,000 RON or more. Pay often includes bonuses tied to project milestones or safety records.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU construction supervisors in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as construction supervisors in Romania via an employment-based work visa or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers). The employer must obtain a work permit from the Romanian Inspectorate for Immigration. For the Blue Card, the job must offer a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage. The application requires a valid job offer, educational credentials, and professional experience. Family reunification is possible.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required to become a construction supervisor in Romania?**
A: Construction supervisors in Romania usually need a post-secondary diploma in civil engineering, construction technology, or a related field. Employers often require at least 3–5 years of relevant experience. Certification from the Romanian Ministry of Development (e.g., technical verifier or project manager) may be required for certain projects. Strong knowledge of safety regulations and Romanian building codes is essential. Soft skills like team coordination and problem-solving are highly valued.
