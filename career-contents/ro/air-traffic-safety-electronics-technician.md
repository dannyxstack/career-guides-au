## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Romania ranges from 8,000 to 12,000 RON, depending on experience and employer. Additional benefits such as shift allowances and overtime pay are common. Entry-level positions start around 6,000 RON, while senior technicians can earn up to 15,000 RON.

**Q: Can foreign Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians work in Romania via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Yes, foreign technicians can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, higher education or five years of relevant experience, and meet the salary threshold set by Romanian law. Alternatively, a national long-stay work visa (tip D) is available for employment, requiring a work permit obtained by the employer.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Romania?**
A: Candidates typically need a diploma in electronics, telecommunications, or a related field. Certification from the Romanian Civil Aeronautical Authority (AACR) is mandatory. Training includes courses on radar, navigation aids, and communication systems. Experience with air traffic control equipment and proficiency in English are highly valued.
