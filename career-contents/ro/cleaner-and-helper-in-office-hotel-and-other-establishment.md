## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cleaners and helpers in offices, hotels, and other establishments in Romania?**
A: The average net monthly salary for cleaners in Romania is between 2,000 and 3,000 RON, depending on location and employer. In hotels, tips may supplement income. The gross minimum wage in 2024 is approximately 2,300 RON. Some employers also provide meal vouchers or accommodation.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for foreign cleaners in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as cleaners via a standard work visa (viza de muncă) obtained by an employer after a labor market test proving no suitable Romanian or EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for low-skilled occupations like cleaning. The employer must also meet minimum salary requirements for the visa.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for cleaners in Romania?**
A: Cleaners can advance to supervisor or team leader roles in cleaning companies, or move into facility management. With additional training, they may specialize in carpet or industrial cleaning. Experienced workers often start their own cleaning businesses. Career progression typically depends on experience, reliability, and language skills.
