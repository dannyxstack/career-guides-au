## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dancers and choreographers in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, location, and employer. In 2024, entry-level dancers earn around 2,500–4,000 RON per month, while experienced choreographers in major cities like Bucharest can earn 5,000–10,000 RON. Freelancers or those in prestigious companies may earn more. Pay is often project-based or seasonal.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign dancers and choreographers in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-stay visa for employment (tip D) or an EU Blue Card if qualified. For the Blue Card, you must have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. 4 times the gross minimum wage). Alternatively, a work permit via employer sponsorship is possible for temporary contracts.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for dancers and choreographers in Romania?**
A: Opportunities include positions in ballet or contemporary companies (e.g., Romanian National Opera, Bucharest National Dance Centre), teaching at private studios or schools, freelance choreography for events/TV, or joining cultural projects. Romania has several annual festivals and competitions that offer exposure and networking.
