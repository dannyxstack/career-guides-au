## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a legislator in Romania?**
A: In Romania, legislators such as deputies and senators receive a base salary equivalent to the average gross salary in the economy, plus allowances. As of 2024, the monthly net salary is approximately 9,000 RON (about 1,800 EUR), with additional benefits for leadership roles and expenses.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as legislators in Romania through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No, there is no work visa or EU Blue Card route for legislators in Romania. Legislators are elected officials and must be Romanian citizens. Non-EU nationals cannot apply for these positions through work migration schemes, as political mandates are reserved for citizens and are not considered regular employment.

**Q: How does one become a legislator in Romania?**
A: To become a legislator in Romania, one must be a Romanian citizen, at least 23 years old for the Chamber of Deputies or 33 for the Senate, and not disqualified by law. Candidates are nominated by political parties or alliances and elected by popular vote in general elections held every four years.
