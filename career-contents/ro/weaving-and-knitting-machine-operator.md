## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Romania?**
A: Salaries generally range between the national minimum wage and the average gross wage in manufacturing, depending on experience, employer, and shift work. According to official data, gross monthly earnings in textile production often fall around 3,500–4,500 RON. Experienced operators or those working night shifts may earn higher.

**Q: How can a non-EU citizen work as a weaving or knitting machine operator in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a long-stay employment visa. The employer must first obtain a work permit from the Inspectorate General for Immigration. The EU Blue Card applies only to highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average; this occupation usually does not meet that threshold, so a standard work permit is the main route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for weaving and knitting machine operators in Romania?**
A: Operators can progress to senior machine operator, shift supervisor, or quality control inspector. With additional training, they may move into maintenance technician roles or production management. The textile industry in Romania also offers opportunities in pattern design or process optimization for those who pursue further education or vocational courses.
