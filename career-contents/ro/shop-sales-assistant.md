## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shop sales assistant in Romania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a shop sales assistant in Romania is between 3,000 and 4,000 RON (approximately €600–€800), depending on experience, location, and employer. Salaries are often supplemented by performance bonuses or commission. The minimum wage for this role is around 2,550 RON gross as of 2024.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for foreign shop sales assistants in Romania?**
A: EU Blue Card is not available for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree. However, non-EU nationals can obtain a long-stay work visa (type D) for employment. The process requires a job offer, employer sponsorship, and a work permit (aviz de angajare) issued by the Romanian Immigration Office, following a labour market test.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop sales assistants in Romania?**
A: Shop sales assistants can advance to roles such as team leader, floor supervisor, or assistant store manager. With experience, they may move into visual merchandising, buying, or branch management. Many retailers offer internal training programs for career progression, and performance-based promotions are common in large chains.
