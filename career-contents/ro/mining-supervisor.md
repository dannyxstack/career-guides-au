## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mining supervisor in Romania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a mining supervisor in Romania ranges from 6,000 to 8,000 RON for experienced professionals, varying by region (e.g., Jiu Valley) and company. Additional underground or hazard allowances often apply. Entry-level supervisors may earn around 4,500 RON.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU mining supervisors to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals must obtain a long-stay visa for employment followed by a work permit from the Romanian Immigration Inspectorate. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a mining supervisor in Romania?**
A: A degree in mining engineering is typically required, along with several years of underground mining experience. Romanian language skills are often necessary for safety communication. Certifications in mine safety and supervisory training from recognized Romanian authorities are mandatory.
