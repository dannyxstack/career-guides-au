## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates in Romania?**
A: In Romania, entry-level inspectors earn around 3,500 RON net monthly, while experienced professionals in the public sector can earn up to 5,500 RON net. Private sector roles, especially in multinational companies, may offer higher pay, ranging from 5,000 to 7,000 RON net. Salaries vary by region and employer.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Romania through the EU Blue Card or work permit?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work in Romania via a standard work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a highly qualified job with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (≈9,750 RON in 2024). For this occupation, the work permit route is more common, but applicants must have their qualifications recognized and a job offer.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Romania?**
A: Inspectors can progress to senior inspector, team leader, or managerial positions within public institutions like the National Public Health Authority or Labor Inspectorate. Specializations in industrial hygiene or environmental auditing also open doors to consulting roles. Further education (e.g., a master's in occupational health) enhances promotion prospects.
