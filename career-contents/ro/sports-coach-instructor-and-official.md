## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sports coaches in Romania?**
A: The salary for sports coaches in Romania varies widely by sport and experience. Entry-level coaches earn around 2,500–3,500 RON per month, while experienced coaches in professional teams can earn 6,000–10,000 RON or more. Public sector and amateur club salaries tend to be lower, while private coaching or high-performance roles offer higher pay.

**Q: How can a non-EU sports coach obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Romania?**
A: Non-EU sports coaches can apply for a Romanian work visa via a job offer from a local employer. The employer must obtain a work permit from the General Inspectorate for Immigration. For the EU Blue Card, the coach must hold a recognized qualification and earn at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. 6,000 RON in 2023). Both routes require a valid contract and compliance with national regulations.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a sports coach in Romania?**
A: To become a sports coach in Romania, you typically need a bachelor's degree in physical education or sports from a recognized university, plus a coaching certification from the National Sports Agency. For specific sports, additional federation-level licenses may be required. Practical experience and ongoing training are highly valued.
