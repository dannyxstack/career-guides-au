- Automatic photo retouching (e.g., one-click beautification, background removal)

- Standard ID photos and product photography automation
- Simple portrait retouching (e.g., skin blemish removal)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted composition and exposure recommendations (e.g., real-time scene analysis)

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for photographers in Romania?**
A: The average salary for photographers in Romania varies by experience and specialization. Entry-level photographers typically earn between 2,500 and 3,500 RON per month, while experienced professionals can earn 5,000 to 8,000 RON or more. Freelancers' income fluctuates based on project volume and client base.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign photographers to work in Romania?**
A: Foreign photographers can work in Romania through a standard work visa (viza de muncă) if they have a job offer from a Romanian employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid employment contract of at least one year and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Romania. Specific conditions and quotas apply.

**Q: What career paths are common for photographers in Romania?**
A: Many photographers in Romania start as assistants or freelancers, then specialize in fields like portrait, wedding, commercial, or fashion photography. Opportunities exist in media outlets, advertising agencies, or photography studios. Some progress to studio management or open their own businesses, while others pursue further education or certifications to enhance their skills and marketability.
