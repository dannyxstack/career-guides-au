## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for stationary plant and machine operators in Romania?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and experience, typically ranging from 2,500 to 4,500 RON net per month (approx. 500–900 EUR). Operators in manufacturing or energy sectors may earn higher. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as stationary plant and machine operators in Romania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, so stationary plant and machine operators usually do not qualify. Instead, non-EU nationals need a work permit obtained by their employer from the General Inspectorate for Immigration, followed by a long-stay visa for employment.

**Q: What are the main industries employing stationary plant and machine operators in Romania?**
A: These operators work in manufacturing (automotive, chemicals, plastics), energy production (power plants, waste treatment), and mining. Key employers include industrial plants in sectors like automotive parts, steel, and cement production.
