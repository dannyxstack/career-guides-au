## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Romania?**
A: Income varies widely; many work independently. Average monthly earnings range from 2,000 to 5,000 RON (approx. 400–1,000 EUR), but top practitioners in Bucharest may earn more. Most are self-employed, and income depends on client base and reputation. Part-time or occasional work is common.

**Q: Can foreign astrologers and fortune-tellers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Romania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a work visa if they have a job offer from a Romanian employer and obtain approval from the Inspectorate for Immigration. The EU Blue Card is typically reserved for highly skilled professions; this occupation is not usually eligible. Self-employment is possible via a business visa if establishing a company.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an astrologer or fortune-teller in Romania?**
A: There are no formal legal requirements or official certifications. Practitioners build credibility through experience, client testimonials, or membership in voluntary associations. Some complete private courses offered by academies. The profession is unregulated, allowing anyone to offer services, but trust and reputation are essential for success.
