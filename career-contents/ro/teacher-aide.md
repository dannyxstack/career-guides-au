## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Teacher's Aide in Romania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a Teacher's Aide in Romania ranges from 2,000 to 3,500 RON, depending on experience, location, and the employing institution. Entry-level positions start around 2,000 RON, while those with several years of experience can earn up to 3,500 RON or more in larger cities like Bucharest.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Teacher's Aide in Romania through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Teacher's Aides in Romania via a standard work visa (employment visa) if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card is only for highly qualified workers requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (currently over 4,500 RON), which is typically not met for this role. Thus, the standard work visa is the primary route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Teacher's Aides in Romania?**
A: Teacher's Aides in Romania can advance by pursuing further education in pedagogy or special education, leading to roles as fully qualified teachers. They may also specialize in supporting children with disabilities or become coordinators of support services. With experience, they can move into administrative positions in schools or work as educational consultants.
