# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 1431 (ISCO08)**

Sports, recreation, and cultural centre managers oversee the operations of facilities such as sports halls, community centres, and cultural venues in Finland. They coordinate activities, manage staff, budgets, and marketing, ensuring compliance with safety regulations and promoting community engagement. The role is dynamic, requiring leadership, event planning, and customer service skills. In Finland, these professionals are employed by municipal services, private sports clubs, and cultural organisations, with opportunities in both urban and rural settings as public investment in wellbeing and culture supports demand.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

Further progression to regional or chain management, or specialisation in large sports complexes or cultural festivals, is possible. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sports and cultural centre manager in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by location, employer, and experience. Entry-level managers might earn around €2,500–€3,000 per month, while experienced managers in large centres can earn €4,000–€5,000 monthly. Public sector roles often follow collective agreements, and private sector may offer performance bonuses.

**Q: Do I need a work visa for this job in Finland as a non-EU citizen?**
A: Yes, unless you are an EU/EEA citizen. You typically need a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) with a job offer meeting minimum salary requirements. If your salary is high and you have a relevant degree, you may qualify for an EU Blue Card, which offers benefits like faster family reunification.

**Q: What education is most common for this role in Finland?**
A: A bachelor's degree in sports management, cultural management, or business administration is common. Many professionals also have vocational qualifications in leisure and recreation. Continuous learning is expected, with certifications in safety and management adding value.
