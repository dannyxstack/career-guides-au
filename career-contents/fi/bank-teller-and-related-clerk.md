# Bank Teller and Related Clerk — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4211 (ISCO08)**

Bank tellers in Finland handle customer transactions, account inquiries, and basic financial services. With the rise of digital banking, the role has evolved to include more advisory tasks, but physical branch banking is declining. Most positions are in major bank branches across urban areas. Finnish language skills are typically required.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 9,374. Employment for bank tellers is projected to decline due to automation and online banking. Branches are consolidating, and routine transactions are increasingly handled digitally. However, there remains demand for customer service in areas like mortgage and loan processing. Overall, the occupation faces a negative outlook with fewer new openings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression from bank teller typically leads to roles such as customer relationship manager, loan officer, or branch manager. With additional qualifications, some transition to specialist roles in wealth management or insurance. However, opportunities are limited as banks restructure, and many tellers seek further education to move into higher-skilled positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard transaction processing such as cash deposits, withdrawals, and transfers
- Account inquiry and balance reconciliation
- Transaction record entry and simple report generation
- Customer identity verification and basic anti-money laundering screening

**Where AI augments the role:**

- analyze customer transaction history via AI to provide personalized financial advice
- Leveraging intelligent quality inspection systems to improve compliance and risk control efficiency
- Collaborate with AI customer service to handle complex customer complaints and disputes
- Automate daily report compilation with RPA to focus on high-value customer service.

**Human moat:**

- Understanding complex customer needs and maintaining deep relationships
- Non-standard business judgment (e.g., special problem solving)
- Interpersonal communication skills for emotional perception and trust building.
- On-the-spot flexibility to handle emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Digital banking and mobile sales skills
- Basic programming and RPA tool application.
- Knowledge of financial products (investment, insurance, loans)
- Data analysis and customer insights.
- Complex communication and conflict resolution

**Key growth areas:**

- customer service
- compliance
- fraud prevention
- digital banking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bank teller in Finland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements. Entry-level tellers earn around €2,500 per month, with experienced tellers earning up to €3,000. Benefits are usually included.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU bank tellers?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a residence permit for employed persons (TTOL) if they have a job offer meeting salary thresholds. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: Can bank tellers advance to higher roles?**
A: Yes, with experience and additional training, many move into customer relationship management or specialist roles such as loan officer or branch manager.
