# House Builder — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7111 (ISCO08)**

House builders construct, repair, and maintain residential buildings including single-family homes, terraced houses, and apartment blocks. In Finland, the occupation is in high demand due to urbanization and a growing need for energy-efficient and affordable housing. Workers often specialize in tasks such as framing, roofing, or finishing, and may be employed by construction companies or work as independent contractors. The sector is cyclical but benefits from government investments in housing and infrastructure.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 37,054. The outlook for house builders in Finland is positive, with a persistent shortage of skilled labor in growing urban regions like Uusimaa, Pirkanmaa, and Varsinais-Suomi. The aging workforce and increasing renovation needs sustain demand. However, the industry is sensitive to economic cycles; downturns can reduce new construction projects. Energy-efficient building and renovation of older housing stock are likely growth areas.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression often starts as a construction worker, then to specialized roles (e.g., master builder, foreman) or site management. Experience and further training can lead to positions such as construction manager or entrepreneur. The vocational qualification (perustutkinto) is common, and many advance through apprenticeships or specialization courses in green building or modular construction.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic generation of bill of materials based on CAD drawings
- Basic building structure calculations and optimization (e.g., load-bearing analysis)
- Construction site safety monitoring and anomaly alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to generate multiple design blueprint options for client selection
- Collision detection and construction simulation optimization through BIM+AI
- Using drones and AI for rooftop inspections and damage identification
- Use machine learning to predict material waste and project delays

**Human moat:**

- On-site fine operations such as manual bricklaying, plastering, and carpentry
- Understanding of individual client needs and designing tailored solutions
- Emergency response to unexpected issues on complex construction sites

**Skills to build:**

- BIM software (e.g., Revit) operation.
- Drone operation and image analysis.
- Basic Programming & AI Collaboration Tools (e.g., ChatGPT)
- Knowledge of green building and energy-saving technologies.
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Green building
- Energy-efficient renovation
- Modular construction
- Smart home technologies

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a house builder in Finland?**
A: Median monthly salary for construction workers is around €2,800–3,200, with experienced workers earning up to €3,800. Overtime and shift allowances can increase pay.

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a house builder in Finland?**
A: EU/EEA citizens do not need a permit. Non-EU nationals need a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). The shortage list may speed up processing.

**Q: What qualifications are most important for house builders?**
A: A vocational qualification in construction is preferred. The occupational safety card is mandatory. Experience and specialization in green building are increasingly valued.
