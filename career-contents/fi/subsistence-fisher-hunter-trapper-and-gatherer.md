# Subsistence Fisher, Hunter, Trapper or Gatherer — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 6340 (ISCO08)**

Subsistence fishers, hunters, trappers and gatherers in Finland harvest wild resources for personal consumption or local trade, operating in rural and remote areas such as Lapland, the archipelago, and forest regions. This occupation is rooted in traditional livelihoods and is closely tied to Finland's natural environment, with practitioners relying on permits and seasonal cycles. Employment is rare in formal labour markets; most work is self-employed or informal, with limited official data due to its small scale.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is informal, typically from apprentice to independent practitioner. Most learn through family traditions or local knowledge. Diversification into guiding tours (e.g., hunting or fishing tourism) or selling surplus at markets is common. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a subsistence fisher or hunter in Finland?**
A: Income is highly variable and often low, as it is supplemented by other work. Most practitioners earn less than €15,000 annually, with sales of surplus products providing additional income. Formal employment in this occupation is extremely rare, so salary data is not publicly available.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a subsistence hunter in Finland?**
A: Only with a job offer from a registered employer, which is very uncommon. The TTOL permit requires a job contract and minimum salary. For self-employed subsistence activities, a startup entrepreneur permit (Residence Permit for a Self-Employed Person) may be possible, but strict criteria apply.

**Q: How do I start a career in subsistence harvesting in Finland?**
A: Start by obtaining necessary permits (hunting license, fishing permit), then gain experience through apprenticeship with a local practitioner. Many combine subsistence with tourism or conservation work. Finnish language skills are important for permits and local networks.
