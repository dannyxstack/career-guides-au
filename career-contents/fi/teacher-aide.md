# Teachers’ Aide — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 5312 (ISCO08)**

Teachers’ aides, known as koulunkäynninohjaajat in Finnish, support teachers in primary, secondary, and vocational schools, as well as in early childhood education. They assist with classroom management, help students with special educational needs, and facilitate inclusive learning. In Finland, the role is integral to the education system, with demand driven by policies promoting equality and individualized support. The occupation typically requires a vocational qualification in educational support, and employment conditions are governed by collective agreements. While the work is rewarding, salaries are modest, and the profession is not a primary target for skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for teachers’ aides often involves gaining experience and additional qualifications, such as a degree in education or social pedagogy, to become a special education teacher or a school social worker. Some may move into coordination roles within schools or transition into early childhood education management. Continuous professional development is encouraged, and experienced aides may mentor new staff. However, upward mobility without further education is limited.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using AI to generate class notes, student attendance reports, and transcripts
- Automated scheduling and classroom resource allocation
- Answer common parent questions (e.g., school calendar, policies) via chatbot
- Automatically Organize Student Files and IEP (Individualized Education Program) Data
- Recording meeting minutes using voice-to-text tools

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to Analyze Student Academic Data to Precisely Identify Students Needing Extra Tutoring
- Assist non-English speaking students and parents using AI translation tools
- Generate personalized learning materials via AI (e.g., differentiated exercises)
- Using AI to simulate classroom management scenarios to enhance behavioral intervention strategies
- Using AI to optimize teacher workflows, freeing up more time for instructional support

**Human moat:**

- Empathy and real-time adaptability in handling student emotional and behavioral issues
- One-on-one physical assistance and emotional connection with students with special needs
- Interpersonal communication in unstructured situations (e.g., sudden conflicts, parent meetings)
- Coordination work requiring flexible judgment in cross-departmental collaboration
- Recognition and response to non-verbal signals in classroom management

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and data interpretation of AI education tools (e.g., Classcraft, Knewton)
- Basic data analysis (intermediate+ Excel/Google Sheets)
- Digital content creation (Canva/Adobe Express to produce learning materials)
- AI-assisted communication tools (e.g., ChatGPT generating parent letter templates)
- Classroom behavior management techniques (positive discipline, trauma-informed practices)
- Project management tools (Asana/Trello) for tracking student support plans

**Key growth areas:**

- Inclusive education
- Special educational needs
- Early childhood education
- Digital learning support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a teachers’ aide in Finland?**
A: The annual pre-tax salary for a teachers’ aide in Finland ranges from €24,000 to €30,000, depending on experience, employer, and location. Municipal collective agreements set the base pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU national become a teachers’ aide in Finland?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer and a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). The salary must meet the collective agreement minimum, which is typically around €2,000 per month. Sponsorship is possible but not common due to the occupation's moderate pay.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to work as a teachers’ aide in Finland?**
A: The standard requirement is a Vocational Qualification in Educational Support (Koulunkäynninohjaajan ammattitutkinto), which takes 2-3 years. Some employers also require a first aid certificate and may prefer additional training in special needs education.
