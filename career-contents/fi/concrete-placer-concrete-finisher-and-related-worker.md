# Concrete Placer, Concrete Finisher and Related Worker — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7114 (ISCO08)**

Concrete placers, finishers, and related workers form the backbone of Finland's construction industry, responsible for pouring, smoothing, and finishing concrete surfaces in buildings, bridges, roads, and infrastructure projects. With a strong emphasis on safety and quality, these professionals work on both new construction and renovation sites across the country. The occupation demands physical stamina, precision, and knowledge of modern concrete technologies. Finland's aging building stock and ongoing urban development ensure steady demand for skilled concrete workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins with a vocational qualification (betonirakentaja). Workers can advance to foreman, site supervisor, or specialist roles in concrete technology. Additional certifications in areas like precast concrete or reinforcement open further paths. Some move into self-employment or entrepreneurial ventures. Continuing education in green building practices is increasingly valued. The trade offers stable employment with potential for steady wage growth through experience and specialization.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- sustainable construction
- infrastructure renovation
- precast concrete
- urban development

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a concrete worker in Finland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements, with entry-level workers earning around €2,500–€3,000 per month, and experienced professionals earning up to €4,000 or more. Overtime and special skills increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU concrete worker get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Yes, through the TTOL residence permit for an employed person. The employer must advertise the job in the EU for 1–2 weeks. If no suitable EU candidate is found, a permit can be issued. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the labour market test applies.
