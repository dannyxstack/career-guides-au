# Street and Related Services Worker — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 9510 (ISCO08)**

Street and Related Services Workers provide various services on streets or in public places, such as selling goods, cleaning, or maintenance. In Finland, this occupation is most common in larger cities, often tied to seasonal work in tourism, events, or municipal cleaning. The role requires minimal formal education and is typically low-paid, with many workers employed part-time or through temporary agencies. EU/EEA citizens have free movement rights, but non-EU workers face strict permit thresholds due to low salary levels.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression is limited for Street and Related Services Workers. Advancement typically involves moving into supervisory roles (e.g., team leader in cleaning) or specialized tasks (e.g., operating street-sweeping vehicles). Some workers transition to related fields like facility maintenance or transport. Most positions are entry-level, with little formal training or certification available.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Parking meter collection (replaced by mobile payments and automatic billing systems)
- Street road cleaning (replaced by autonomous cleaning robots)
- Simple customer inquiries (replaced by AI voice assistants and self-service kiosks)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile payment data analysis to analyze foot traffic and sales timing.
- Post real-time service locations and promotional information through social platforms
- Using voice translation tools to serve multilingual tourists

**Human moat:**

- On-site incident handling and de-escalation in emergencies (e.g., traffic accidents, disputes)
- Ability to build trust and emotional connection with regular customers
- In-depth understanding and flexible application of local regulations and community norms

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (using mobile payment terminals, managing online reviews)
- Basic English or foreign language communication
- Customer service and conflict resolution skills
- Time management and multitasking
- Basic repair skills (e.g., trolleys, cleaning equipment)

**Key growth areas:**

- urban cleaning
- seasonal services
- event support
- public maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Street and Related Services Worker in Finland?**
A: Salaries are modest, usually based on collective agreements for cleaning or retail. Exact figures vary, but the occupation is among the lowest-paid categories, often around the minimum wage.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa for this job?**
A: It is very difficult. Non-EU workers need a residence permit for an employed person, which requires a salary above the threshold (€1,334/month in 2024). This occupation typically pays below that, so the main route is through EU/EEA free movement.

**Q: What career growth is possible?**
A: Limited. With experience, you may become a team leader or shift to equipment operation. Further training in maintenance or cleaning management can open other roles, but advancement is slow.
