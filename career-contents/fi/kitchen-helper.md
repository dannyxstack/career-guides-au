# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers in Finland assist with simple food preparation, cleaning, and dishwashing in restaurants, cafes, and institutional kitchens. The role is entry-level with low barriers, often filled by students, part-time workers, and immigrants. The labour market shows steady demand due to high turnover and seasonal peaks in tourism and hospitality.

**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Finland?**
A: The median monthly salary for kitchen helpers is around €1,800–€2,200 pre-tax, depending on experience and location. Hourly wages for part-time work are typically €9–€12.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa as a kitchen helper?**
A: It is challenging. The TTOL permit requires the job to be full-time and meet income threshold (€1,300/month). While salary often meets this, the role is considered low-skilled, and permits are more easily granted for occupations on the shortage list. EU/EEA nationals have free movement.

**Q: How can a kitchen helper advance to a chef?**
A: Many kitchen helpers attend vocational school for a qualification in cooking (kokki) while working. Apprenticeship programs also exist. With experience and certification, promotion to line cook or chef is possible.
