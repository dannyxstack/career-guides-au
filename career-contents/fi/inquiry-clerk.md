# Inquiry Clerks — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4225 (ISCO08)**

Inquiry clerks handle customer queries, provide information, and process requests in various sectors in Finland, such as call centers, government services, and corporate client support. The role requires strong communication skills and typically involves managing multiple channels like phone, email, and chat. In Finland, these positions are common in both public and private sectors, with a focus on accurate and courteous service.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for inquiry clerks in Finland can lead to team leader, customer service manager, or specialized roles in data analysis, quality assurance, or training. Many clerks also transition into sales or administrative roles. Upskilling in digital tools and languages enhances advancement prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Handle customer inquiries for general information about products or services
- Provides standardized process guidelines and frequently asked questions
- Records and transfers simple complaints to relevant departments
- Update and maintain customer information databases.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI real-time knowledge bases to quickly retrieve answers to complex questions.
- Using sentiment analysis tools to identify customer emotions and adjust communication strategies
- Optimize multilingual customer communication using AI-assisted scripts
- Use automatic summarization tools to quickly record call key points

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly complex or emotionally charged customer complaints
- Creative problem-solving in uncertain situations
- building long-term customer trust and personalised service
- Cross-departmental coordination and internal process optimization.

**Skills to build:**

- Emotional intelligence and conflict resolution skills
- AI customer service system operation and tuning
- Data analysis basics (customer trend identification).
- Multilingual ability (for serving international clients)
- Advanced communication and negotiation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- automation
- digital services
- customer experience
- multilingual support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for inquiry clerks in Finland?**
A: Salary levels are based on collective agreements and vary by experience, sector, and location. Most positions offer competitive compensation with benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as inquiry clerks in Finland?**
A: Yes, with a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold, you can apply for a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). Fluency in Finnish is often required.

**Q: What education is needed to become an inquiry clerk?**
A: A vocational qualification in business or customer service is common, but many employers accept relevant experience and on-the-job training.
