# Midwifery Professional — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 2222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery professionals in Finland provide comprehensive maternal and newborn care, including antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal services in hospitals, health centers, and increasingly in home birth settings. They are regulated by Valvira and require a bachelor's degree from a university of applied sciences, with instruction in Finnish or Swedish. The profession is in high demand due to an aging workforce and a growing emphasis on family-centered care. Midwives work autonomously and in multidisciplinary teams, with opportunities in public and private sectors.

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. —. The employment outlook for midwives in Finland is positive, with a projected shortage over the next decade driven by retirements and increased demand for maternity services. The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health identifies midwifery as a shortage occupation. Growth areas include telehealth, home birth support, and maternal mental health. However, recruitment relies heavily on domestic graduates and licensed EU professionals, as licensing requirements and language barriers limit non-EU entry.

Experienced midwives may advance to managerial roles (e.g., head midwife) or pursue research and teaching positions at universities of applied sciences. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a midwife in Finland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective bargaining agreements and vary by experience, sector (public/private), and location. Entry-level midwives typically earn around €30,000–€35,000 annually, while experienced midwives can earn up to €45,000 or more.

**Q: Can a non-EU midwife get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer from a Finnish employer, recognition of qualifications by Valvira, and a residence permit application (TTOL). Language proficiency in Finnish or Swedish is mandatory. The process can take several months.

**Q: Do I need to re-qualify if I am a midwife from outside the EU?**
A: Yes, your qualifications must be assessed by Valvira. You may need to complete supplementary studies or a bridging program to meet Finnish standards. This often includes language training and clinical practice.
