# Physical And Engineering Science Technician — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3119 (ISCO08)**

Physical and engineering science technicians assist engineers and scientists in research, development, and production across sectors such as electronics, energy, materials, and manufacturing. In Finland, these professionals work in laboratories, industrial plants, and R&D centers, performing tasks like testing, calibration, data collection, and quality control. The role requires practical skills, attention to detail, and knowledge of scientific methods. Finland's strong technology and industrial base offers stable employment, especially in high-tech and green technology fields.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from technician roles after vocational or bachelor's education. With experience, technicians can advance to senior technician, team leader, or specialist positions. Further education, such as a master's degree in engineering, can lead to engineer or research roles. Some move into quality management, sales engineering, or technical consulting. Continuous learning in new technologies is key for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically record experimental data and generate standardized reports
- Use AI algorithms for preliminary data analysis and anomaly detection
- Automatically calibrate and adjust experimental parameters based on historical data
- Classify samples and perform basic inspections using computer vision
- Automatically organize literature and preliminary experimental design

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using machine learning models to optimise experimental design and improve efficiency
- AI-assisted multi-variable data analysis to discover hidden patterns
- Using digital twins to simulate experimental processes and predict outcomes.
- Quick retrieval of technical documents and specifications using natural language processing
- AI-driven predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime.

**Human moat:**

- On-site equipment fault diagnosis and repair decision-making
- Innovative experimental design and non-standard testing.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration, communication, and explaining technical details
- Safety management and compliance assessment
- Customer requirement analysis and customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of Python or R programming
- Introduction to Statistics and Machine Learning
- Digital lab management software (e.g., LabVantage).
- PLC or Embedded Systems basics
- Communication and project management certifications (e.g., PMP)
- Industry-specific regulations (e.g., ISO 17025)

**Key growth areas:**

- Renewable Energy
- Electronics
- Materials Science
- Automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Finland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements in the sector. Entry-level positions start at the base rate, which increases with experience. For exact figures, consult the relevant union agreement.

**Q: Do I need a work visa for this job?**
A: EU/EEA citizens do not need a visa. Non-EU/EEA nationals require a residence permit for employed persons (TTOL) with a job offer. The permit is granted if the salary meets the required threshold.

**Q: What education is required for this role?**
A: Typically a vocational qualification or a bachelor's degree in a related field. Finnish degrees are recognized; foreign degrees may need recognition from the Finnish National Agency for Education.
