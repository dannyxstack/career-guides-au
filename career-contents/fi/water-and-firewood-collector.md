# Water and Firewood Collector — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 9624 (ISCO08)**

Water and firewood collectors perform basic manual tasks to collect water from natural sources and gather firewood for fuel. In Finland, this occupation is extremely rare, confined to remote rural areas, off-grid households, or traditional summer cottages where piped water and central heating are unavailable. The role is often part-time, seasonal, or combined with other elementary work. Due to widespread infrastructure and modern heating solutions, demand is negligible and mainly limited to niche self-employment or communal projects. The occupation has no formal training or qualification requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

There is no established career progression for water and firewood collectors in Finland. Individuals typically enter the role informally and may advance to broader manual labour, forestry work, or related self-employment. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a water and firewood collector in Finland?**
A: This occupation is not formally salaried. Pay is typically piecework or hourly at minimum wage (€10–12 per hour). Annual income is low and often under €15,000, with irregular hours.

**Q: Can I immigrate to Finland as a water and firewood collector?**
A: No. The occupation is considered low-skilled and does not meet the minimum income threshold for a residence permit. Only EU/EEA citizens can work in this role without a permit, but job opportunities are virtually nonexistent.

**Q: Is this a recognized occupation in Finland?**
A: It is listed in the ISCO-08 classification, but in practice it is extremely rare. Most water collection is done via municipal systems, and firewood is bought commercially. The occupation has no formal recognition or training pathways.
