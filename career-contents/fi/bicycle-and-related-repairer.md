# Bicycle and Related Repairers — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Bicycle and related repairers maintain and repair bicycles, e-bikes, and similar vehicles in Finland. With cycling popular in urban areas and for leisure, demand for repair services is steady. Tasks include diagnosing mechanical issues, replacing parts, adjusting brakes and gears, and performing routine servicing. Workers are employed in independent repair shops, sports retailers, or as self-employed professionals. The role requires mechanical aptitude, attention to detail, and familiarity with bike components. As e-bikes become more common, repairers increasingly need electrical skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression includes specialization in e-bikes, high-end racing bikes, or shop management. Some repairers start their own businesses. Vocational training provides a foundation, and experienced workers can advance to master-level roles. There is limited upward mobility without further education, but the trade offers steady work for those skilled with tools.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic software to automatically identify common faults (e.g., circuit issues)
- Handle customer appointments and simple inquiries via chatbot
- Inventory management and parts ordering automated by AI system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of complex electrical faults (e.g., electric bicycle battery management systems)
- AR glasses overlay repair steps and exploded parts diagrams to improve repair accuracy
- Customer needs analysis: AI recommends personalised upgrade options (e.g. performance modifications)
- Remote expert collaboration: obtaining real-time support via AI platforms

**Human moat:**

- Manual fine-tuning (such as wheel truing, suspension adjustment)
- Assess unusual damage and creatively repair
- Face-to-face communication with clients to understand cycling preferences and customize solutions
- Handling replacement and adaptation of old or non-standard parts

**Skills to build:**

- Diagnosis and repair of electric bicycle electrical systems
- Proficient in using digital diagnostic tools and repair apps
- 3D printing part production and repair
- Customer relationship management and personalized service design
- Basic programming skills (e.g., MES system operation in repair stations)
- Sustainable repair concepts (e.g., battery recycling and upgrades)

**Key growth areas:**

- electric bicycle repair
- urban cycling
- servicing
- specialization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary prospects for bicycle repairers in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer, but are generally in line with other repair trades. With specialization in e-bikes or high-end models, earnings can increase significantly.

**Q: Can I get a work visa to work as a bicycle repairer in Finland?**
A: Non-EU workers need a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) with a job offer. Since the occupation is not on the shortage list, visa sponsorship may be challenging and requires employer justification.
