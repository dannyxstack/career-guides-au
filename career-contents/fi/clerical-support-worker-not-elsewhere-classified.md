# Clerical Support Worker Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a wide range of clerical duties not classified elsewhere in ISCO-08, including general office work, data entry, filing, reception, and correspondence. In Finland, these workers are employed across all sectors, with many positions in small and medium-sized enterprises, public administration, and service industries. The role typically requires basic computer skills and fluency in Finnish or Swedish, as many tasks involve communication with local clients and authorities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving from general clerical duties to more specialized roles (e.g., accounting clerk, HR assistant) or supervisory positions (office manager). Many workers gain additional qualifications part-time while working. In Finland, vocational qualifications (e.g., in business administration) can lead to better-paid roles. The path is not rigid, and lateral moves between industries are common, but advancement often depends on language skills and digital competence.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- automation adaptation
- digital administration
- customer service
- specialized clerical roles

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a clerical support worker in Finland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary by sector and experience. Typical monthly pay ranges from about €1,800 to €2,500, but exact figures depend on the employer and region.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work permit for this occupation?**
A: Yes, via the TTOL residence permit for an employed person, but the employer must show that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. The salary must meet the threshold (minimum ~€1,339/month in 2025, but often higher in practice). The process can take 1–3 months.

**Q: What are the opportunities for advancement in this field?**
A: With experience and additional training, you can move into specialized roles (e.g., accounting clerk, HR assistant) or become an office manager. Vocational qualifications in business administration are common stepping stones.
