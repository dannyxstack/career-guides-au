# Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 2132 (ISCO08)**

Farming, forestry and fisheries advisers in Finland provide expert guidance to primary producers on sustainable practices, productivity, and regulatory compliance. They work for public agencies, agricultural cooperatives, and private consulting firms, helping farmers, forest owners, and fishers adopt modern techniques, manage resources, and navigate EU and national policies. Finland's strong focus on bioeconomy and rural development drives demand for these specialists, who often combine field visits with digital advisory tools.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 3,103. Finland's employment outlook for farming, forestry and fisheries advisers is positive due to the aging farmer population, need for climate-smart practices, and growing forest-based industries. The bioeconomy strategy and subsidies for advisory services support demand. However, competition is moderate as graduates from natural resource programs seek roles. Digitalization and precision agriculture create new opportunities, but remote rural areas may face recruitment challenges. Overall, steady growth is expected, especially for specialists in organic farming and forest certification.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a bachelor's or master's in agricultural, forestry or environmental sciences. Entry-level roles involve field advisory; experienced advisers can become senior consultants, team leaders, or specialize in areas like organic farming, forest carbon services, or aquaculture. Many advance into management positions in agricultural extension or government agencies. Continuous learning is essential due to evolving regulations and technology; some pursue doctoral studies or teaching roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate standardized soil test reports and crop growth cycle analyses
- Automated fertiliser recommendation based on historical data and satellite imagery
- Draft basic sustainable forest management plans
- Automatically aggregate fishing catch data and generate compliance reports
- Answer common pest and disease control questions (knowledge base chatbot)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize precision agriculture decision-making recommendations using AI remote sensing analysis and big data
- Simulate long-term environmental impacts of different planting/breeding strategies using AI models
- Real-time monitoring of weather and market data, dynamically adjusting advisor recommendations
- Automates data collection and initial analysis, allowing consultants to focus on farmer communication and customized solutions
- AI-assisted generation of visual reports to improve communication efficiency with farmers

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and long-term relationships with farmers
- On-site problem diagnosis based on local experience and intuition
- Handling complex, unique sustainability conflicts involving multiple stakeholders
- Adaptability to unstructured, low-information-density environments (e.g., remote farms)
- Cross-disciplinary integration (agriculture, ecology, economics) providing customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Application of AI tools (e.g., remote sensing analysis platforms, agricultural decision support systems)
- Data science fundamentals (Python/R, statistical modeling, data visualization).
- Precision agriculture technology (drones, sensors, IoT data analysis)
- Environmental and social impact assessment methodology
- Communication and change management skills (to promote adoption of new technologies among farmers)
- Continuous learning and industry knowledge updates (policy, market, biotechnology).

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable agriculture
- Precision farming
- Forest bioeconomy
- Digital advisory services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a farming adviser in Finland?**
A: Annual pre-tax salaries for experienced farming advisers typically range from €35,000 to €50,000, with higher earnings for senior specialists or managers.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU farming advisers?**
A: Non-EU advisers need a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) with a valid job offer. If the salary exceeds €3,000 per month, the EU Blue Card or specialist route may apply, offering faster processing and family rights.
