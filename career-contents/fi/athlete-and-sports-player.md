# Athlete and Sports Player — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3421 (ISCO08)**

Athletes and sports players in Finland compete professionally in sports such as ice hockey, football, track and field, winter sports, and emerging fields like esports. The sector is small, with most athletes earning through club contracts, sponsorships, or government grants. Many train under national sports federations and may combine sports with other education or work. The labour market is highly competitive and performance-driven, with limited permanent positions.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for athletes in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary enormously by sport, level, and sponsorship. Top ice hockey players may earn over €100,000, while many lower-league athletes earn under €20,000. Most professional athletes have short contracts and supplemental income.

**Q: Can a non-EU athlete get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Yes, via the Residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) if offered a contract by a Finnish club. The salary must meet the threshold (€1,354/month in 2025) but many athlete salaries may qualify. Alternatively, top athletes might use the EU Blue Card if salary exceeds €50,486/year.

**Q: What career options exist after professional sports?**
A: Many athletes transition to coaching, sports management, physiotherapy, or fitness. Some pursue university degrees in sports science or business alongside their careers. Finnish sports federations and the Olympic Committee offer career support programs.
