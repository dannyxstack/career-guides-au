# Garment Patternmaker and Cutter — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7532 (ISCO08)**

Garment patternmakers and cutters create patterns from designs and cut fabric for clothing production. In Finland, the textile and fashion sector is small but specialized, with a growing focus on sustainable and technical textiles. Employment is concentrated in the Helsinki region and a few manufacturing hubs, with most workers employed by small to medium-sized enterprises. The occupation requires precision and creativity, and digital skills are increasingly important.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving from cutter or junior patternmaker to senior patternmaker or production manager. Some workers specialize in sample making or grading. Further education can lead to design or technical roles. The field is small, so advancement may require switching employers or combining patternmaking with design or production planning skills. Digital literacy and sustainability expertise are key for staying relevant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- sustainable fashion
- technical textiles
- digital pattern making
- upcycling

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

- Dislikes working with measurements or tight tolerances

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garment patternmaker and cutter in Finland?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from €28,000 to €33,000 annually. Experienced patternmakers can earn €35,000–€40,000, with senior roles up to €45,000.

**Q: Can a non-EU patternmaker get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Yes, through the Residence permit for an employed person (TTOL), but it's not a shortage occupation, so the employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU/EEA worker is available. The process is possible but not streamlined.
