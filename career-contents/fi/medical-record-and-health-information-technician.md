# Medical Records and Health Information Technicians — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3252 (ISCO08)**

Medical records and health information technicians in Finland manage patient data, ensuring accuracy and compliance with regulations, within a rapidly digitalizing healthcare system. The occupation is increasingly vital due to national eHealth initiatives and the integration of electronic health records across public and private sectors, with strong demand in hospital districts and health tech companies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from data entry or coding roles, advancing to specialist positions in health informatics, data analysis, or system management. With experience and further education, technicians can move into roles such as health information manager, data privacy officer, or consultant. Finnish language skills and knowledge of local regulations are key for advancement, especially in public sector roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

- Health Informatics and HIPAA Compliance
- Data analysis tools (SQL, Python, R)
- AI/ML fundamentals (NLP, predictive modeling)
- Medical coding systems (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI).
- Project management and change management

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital health
- Data analytics
- Electronic health records
- AI in healthcare

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**
