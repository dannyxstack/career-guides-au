# Coding, Proofreading and Related Clerk — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4413 (ISCO08)**

This occupation involves coding data, proofreading texts, and performing related clerical tasks in Finland. Workers may be employed in publishing houses, media companies, data processing firms, or administrative departments. The role requires attention to detail, language proficiency, and basic computer skills. In Finland, these positions are often part-time or project-based, with opportunities in both public and private sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically leads to senior clerk, editorial assistant, or administrative coordinator roles. With additional training, clerks can move into copywriting, editing, or data analysis. In Finland, vocational qualifications in office work or language services can enhance prospects. Some may specialize in technical documentation or digital content management. Upskilling in digital tools and languages (Finnish, English, Swedish) is beneficial.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Text Proofreading and Spell Checking with Real-time High Accuracy by AI Models
- Data Encoding and Classification: Standardized Conversion from Spreadsheets to Databases
- Standardization of format, such as layout and punctuation uniformity
- Simple document template filling and letter generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Logic and semantic proofreading of complex text; AI provides suggestions, but humans must finalize.
- Auxiliary proofreading of multilingual documents, AI draft first then human polishing
- Data quality monitoring, AI flags outliers for human review
- Quickly locate reference sources via AI search to improve proofreading efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Semantic judgment and style grasp in complex contexts
- Authoritative verification of legal or professional terminology
- Responsibility for handling data involving trade secrets or privacy
- Cross-departmental communication and requirement clarification

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced configuration of AI proofreading tools (e.g. Grammarly Business, Acrolinx)
- Basic programming (Python/Excel macros) to automate repetitive tasks
- Data visualization and basic analysis
- Project management and quality control
- Domain expertise (e.g., legal, medical knowledge)

**Key growth areas:**

- digital publishing
- data quality
- editorial services
- language technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary widely; the median monthly pay for clerical workers in Finland is around €2,500–€3,000, but entry-level or part-time positions may be lower. Exact figures depend on employer, experience, and region.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work visa for this job?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The job must meet the minimum salary threshold for a residence permit for an employed person (€1,333/month in 2024), which many such positions do, but employers must also demonstrate that they cannot find suitable EU/EEA candidates. Proofreading roles requiring local language skills may favor locals.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, you can move into senior clerk, editorial assistant, or content management roles. Further education in media, communications, or data management can lead to higher positions like editor or data analyst.
