# Cabinet-Maker and Related Worker — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7522 (ISCO08)**

Cabinet-makers and related workers in Finland design, build, and install wooden furniture, cabinets, and other fixtures. They work in workshops, on construction sites, and in renovation projects. The occupation is part of Finland's strong woodworking tradition, with demand driven by both new construction and the growing interest in custom, sustainable interiors. Skills in traditional joinery and modern CNC machining are valued. The industry is concentrated in Southern Finland and regions with active furniture manufacturing, but opportunities exist nationwide.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a vocational qualification, followed by on-the-job training. Experienced cabinet-makers can advance to master craftsman status, supervisory roles, or start their own businesses. Specialization in areas like kitchen fitting, restoration, or CNC programming can lead to higher pay and project variety. Further education in design or engineering opens doors to management or entrepreneurial opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom Furniture
- Kitchen Installation
- Sustainable Wood
- Renovation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cabinet-maker in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer, but generally range from €2,500 to €3,500 per month for full-time work. Overtime and specialized skills can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as cabinet-makers in Finland?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work with a Residence Permit for an Employed Person (TTOL), provided they have a job offer from a Finnish employer and meet salary requirements. The permit is initially temporary but can lead to permanent residency.
