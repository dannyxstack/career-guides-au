# Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Structural metal preparers and erectors in Finland read blueprints, handle, cut, and position steel beams and columns for buildings, bridges, and industrial structures. They work on construction sites year-round, often at heights, and must ensure precise alignment and safety. The occupation is critical for Finland's large infrastructure projects and growing energy sector, with demand fueled by green building and modular construction trends.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or apprentice, advancing to skilled erector, then to foreman or site supervisor. Specialization in large-scale projects (e.g., wind turbines, bridges) or additional certifications in welding or crane operation can lead to higher pay and leadership roles. Many also move into inspection or teaching vocational skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Precise dimensional measurement and calibration using BIM and laser scanning
- Optimize cutting and welding parameters for steel structures using AI software
- Drones automatically check structural alignment and bolt tightening status

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted crane and rigging configuration planning
- Risk prediction and construction plan optimization based on historical data
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted installation guidance and training
- Real-time tool status monitoring and predictive maintenance alerts.

**Human moat:**

- Physical operation and assembly in complex construction site environments
- Flexible judgment for handling non-standard or damaged materials
- Coordinate with the team and handle emergency situations
- Strict enforcement and supervision of safety regulations

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Building Information Modeling) software operation
- Drone operation and data analysis
- Programming and maintenance of welding robots
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools
- Digital literacy and basic programming concepts
- Data analysis in safety and quality management

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure renewal
- Energy sector construction
- Modular building
- Green steel projects

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is a typical salary for a structural metal erector in Finland?**
A: Salaries for structural metal erectors vary by experience and region. Entry-level workers earn about 2,000–2,400 EUR/month, while experienced erectors can earn 2,600–3,200 EUR/month. Rates are set by collective agreements (e.g., Rakennusliitto) and include extra pay for overtime and hazardous work.

**Q: Can I get a residence permit for this job as a non-EU national?**
A: Yes, the Residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) is available if you have a job offer from a Finnish employer. The job's salary and terms must meet the applicable collective agreement. There is no specific quota, but you must have a fixed-term or permanent contract. The permit is initially granted for up to one year and renewable.

**Q: Is there demand for structural metal erectors in Finland's green transition?**
A: Yes, the green transition creates demand for erectors in wind tower assembly, solar farm structures, and retrofitting steel-framed buildings for energy efficiency. Major projects like Helsinki's Raide-Jokeri light rail and bridge renovations also require skilled erectors.
