# Personnel and Careers Professionals — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 2423 (ISCO08)**

Personnel and Careers Professionals (ISCO-08 2423) in Finland include HR specialists, recruiters, and career counselors who manage staffing, employee relations, and talent development. The role is vital in both private companies and public organizations, with strong demand in Helsinki, Tampere, and Turku. Digitalization and remote work trends are shaping HR practices, emphasizing data-driven decision-making and employee well-being. The profession typically requires a university degree in HR, business, or social sciences, and Finnish language skills are often essential for local roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Personnel and Careers Professional in Finland?**
A: Entry-level salaries start around 30,000 EUR/year, while experienced professionals earn 45,000–65,000 EUR/year. Senior HR managers can exceed 80,000 EUR/year. Salaries are higher in the Helsinki metropolitan area.

**Q: Can a non-EU HR professional get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Finnish employer. You can apply for a Residence Permit for an Employed Person (TTOL). The salary must meet the threshold (1,330 EUR/month in 2025). The EU Blue Card is an option if you have a higher salary (about 4,500 EUR/month) and a degree.

**Q: Do I need to speak Finnish to work in HR in Finland?**
A: For most HR roles, especially those involving employee relations or local compliance, Finnish language proficiency (at least B2 level) is required. However, international companies or roles in talent acquisition for English-speaking positions may accept English only.
