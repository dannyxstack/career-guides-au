# Metal Working Machine Tool Setters and Operators — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

Metal working machine tool setters and operators set up and operate machine tools to shape metal workpieces. In Finland, this occupation is vital to the manufacturing sector, including machinery, shipbuilding, and metal product industries. Work typically takes place in factory settings across industrial hubs such as Tampere, Lahti, and Vaasa. While automation and CNC technology have transformed the role, skilled operators remain in demand for precision tasks and complex setups.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a machine operator, advancing to setter and then to CNC programmer or team leader. Further specialization in automation, robotics, or quality control offers additional pathways. Vocational training and on-the-job experience are key. Some workers move into supervisory roles or technical sales. Upskilling in digital manufacturing is increasingly important for long-term career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC machining
- automation
- robotics
- precision engineering

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Finland?**
A: Salaries are competitive, with median monthly earnings in line with other manufacturing trades. Overtime and shift bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a metal working machine tool setter in Finland?**
A: Yes, but employers must demonstrate that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. A residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) is required, and the job must meet minimum salary thresholds.

**Q: What training is needed to become a machine tool setter in Finland?**
A: A vocational qualification in mechanical engineering is typical, often completed at a vocational school or through an apprenticeship. The education is generally tuition-free.
