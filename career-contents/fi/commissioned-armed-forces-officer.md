# Commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 0110 (ISCO08)**

Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Finland lead and manage military operations, personnel, and resources within the Finnish Defence Forces. They are responsible for strategic planning, command, and training of subordinates, often specializing in areas such as infantry, navy, air force, or logistics. This role requires advanced leadership skills, tactical knowledge, and a strong commitment to national defense. Entry is typically through the National Defence University, with a competitive selection process for Finnish citizens. The work environment is structured and hierarchical, with opportunities for international cooperation through NATO and EU defense initiatives. Finland's geopolitical situation emphasizes the importance of a capable officer corps.

Career progression for Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Finland follows a structured path from junior to senior ranks, with specialization options in command, staff, or technical fields. Officers typically advance through time-in-grade, performance evaluations, and further education at the National Defence University. Paths include branch-specific roles (e.g., infantry, navy, air force) or joint functions (e.g., logistics, intelligence). ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a commissioned armed forces officer in Finland?**
A: Salary varies by rank and experience. A newly commissioned officer (vänrikki) earns approximately €35,000-€45,000 annually, while senior officers (eversti) can earn €70,000-€90,000. Additional allowances for service in remote areas or special duties may apply.

**Q: Can a non-Finnish citizen become a commissioned armed forces officer in Finland?**
A: Generally no. Commissioned officer positions are reserved for Finnish citizens. Exceptions exist for international military attachés or exchange officers through bilateral agreements, but these are not standard employment routes. Only Finnish citizens are eligible for the National Defence University's officer training.

**Q: What education is required to become a commissioned armed forces officer in Finland?**
A: The main pathway is a bachelor's degree from the National Defence University, which combines military training and academic studies. Entry requires passing a competitive entrance exam, physical fitness tests, and security clearance. International students are not accepted.
