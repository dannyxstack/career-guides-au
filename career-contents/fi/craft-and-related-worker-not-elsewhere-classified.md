# Craft and Related Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7549 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of craft workers not classified elsewhere, such as jewellery makers, musical instrument makers, handicraft workers, and other artisans. In Finland, these roles are often small-scale, self-employed, or micro-enterprises operating in niche markets. The labour market is specialized with limited wage data, primarily concentrated in artisan sectors like custom jewellery, instrument restoration, and tourism-related crafts. Demand is stable but modest, driven by tourism and personalized products.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves mastering a specific craft through apprenticeships or vocational training, then building a clientele or starting a business. Some workers advance to supervisory roles in larger workshops or become trainers. Independence and specialization are key; success often depends on networking and online presence.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised jewellery grinding and polishing
- Basic assembly and tuning of musical instruments
- Repeating pattern reproduction in hand carving
- Traditional mold making and casting processes
- Simple wood/stone cutting and shaping

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Conceive personalized jewelry/musical instrument designs using AI design software
- Accelerated prototyping through 3D scanning and modeling
- Using AI quality inspection system to improve product precision
- Using AR/VR for complex assembly guidance
- Customized client communication and quoting via digital platforms

**Human moat:**

- Highly customized artistic creation and aesthetic judgment
- Experience and tactile feel of handling rare materials
- On-site repair and adaptation of complex non-standard parts
- Cultural story interpretation through in-depth communication with clients
- Overall process control through multi-process integration

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM and 3D modeling (e.g., Rhino, Fusion 360)
- Digital fabrication equipment operator (3D printing, CNC engraving)
- AI-assisted design tools (e.g. Midjourney, DALL-E for concept design)
- Materials science and sustainable process knowledge
- Digital customer management and custom platform operations
- Cross-sector collaboration and project management skills

**Key growth areas:**

- custom jewellery
- souvenir crafts
- instrument restoration
- artisan home decor

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft workers in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary widely; median around EUR 30,000 annually, but many self-employed earn less. Income depends on skill, specialization, and client base.

**Q: Can I work in Finland as a craft worker from outside the EU?**
A: Possible via the TTOL residence permit if you have a job offer meeting the income threshold, but competition is high and the salary may be borderline. Self-employment is also an option under the entrepreneur permit.
