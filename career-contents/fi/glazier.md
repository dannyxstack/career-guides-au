# Glazier — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers in Finland install, replace, and repair glass in windows, doors, facades, and other structures, working primarily on construction sites and renovation projects. The field is integral to both new builds and energy-efficiency upgrades, with demand driven by urban development and stricter building regulations. The profession typically requires a vocational qualification in construction with a specialization in glass installation, and ongoing safety training is essential. Most glaziers are employed by construction or glass contracting companies, with opportunities for self-employment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a vocational qualification and apprenticeship, advancing to a journeyman or foreman role. Experienced glaziers may specialize in areas like structural glass, heritage restoration, or energy-saving installations. Self-employment and entrepreneurship are common paths, especially for those with a strong client network. Continued professional development through manufacturer certifications can enhance career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-efficient windows
- Glass facades
- Renovation
- Green building

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Are glaziers in Finland typically paid by the hour or monthly?**
A: Most glaziers are salaried monthly, though temporary or project-based positions may offer hourly pay.

**Q: Do I need a job offer before applying for a residence permit?**
A: Yes, a binding job offer from a Finnish employer is required for the TTOL residence permit.

**Q: Is demand for glaziers growing in Finland?**
A: Yes, demand is stable to growing due to construction, energy renovations, and the need for specialized glass installations.
