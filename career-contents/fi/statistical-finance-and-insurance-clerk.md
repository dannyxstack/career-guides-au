# Statistical, Finance and Insurance Clerk — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4312 (ISCO08)**

Statistical, finance and insurance clerks in Finland handle data processing, financial record-keeping, and insurance documentation in banks, insurance companies, and statistical offices. They ensure accuracy in figures, process claims, and maintain databases. The role is essential for administrative support in Finland's robust financial sector, which employs thousands. Proficiency in Finnish and English is often required, and digitalization is reshaping tasks toward data analysis.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts from junior clerk roles, advancing to senior clerk, team lead, or specialist positions in underwriting, claims, or financial analysis. Further education, such as a bachelor's in business or finance, can lead to managerial roles. Many clerks transition to data analyst or compliance officer roles. Professional certifications in bookkeeping or insurance enhance mobility. The path offers stable income and opportunities in both private and public sectors.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Extracting and entering data from formatted tables or documents
- Preliminary review and basic information verification of insurance claims
- Generate standard statistical reports and financial summaries
- Handling routine premium calculations and claim amount verification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Utilize AI for automatic flagging and preliminary analysis of abnormal transactions or claims
- Quickly retrieve historical cases and policy clauses using natural language query tools
- Generating dynamic visual dashboards through automated reporting platforms
- Using RPA to connect multiple systems for cross-platform data validation and reconciliation
- Use machine learning to predict risk scores, assisting human decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, ambiguous, or anomalous data situations, such as non-compliant claims
- Communicate complex issues with clients or internal departments, providing explanations and recommendations.
- Understand and comply with constantly changing financial and insurance regulations
- Judging business substance behind data anomalies based on industry experience

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and automation scripting in Python or R
- Process design using low-code/RPA tools (e.g. UiPath)
- SQL data querying and database management
- Basic machine learning concepts and model applications (e.g., anomaly detection)
- Knowledge of financial and insurance products and compliance interpretation
- Data visualisation tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).

**Key growth areas:**

- financial services
- insurance
- data analysis
- compliance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Statistical, Finance and Insurance Clerks in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer, but typical annual earnings range from €25,000 to €35,000, with higher pay in the insurance sector.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Statistical Clerk in Finland?**
A: Yes, with a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) if you have a job offer and salary meets the threshold (€1,306/month in 2024). EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience and further education, clerks can move into senior clerk, analyst, or managerial roles in finance or insurance.
