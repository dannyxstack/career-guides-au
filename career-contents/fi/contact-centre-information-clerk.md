# Contact Centre Information Clerk — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4222 (ISCO08)**

Contact Centre Information Clerks handle inbound and outbound calls, emails, and chats to provide information, resolve queries, and support customers across industries such as finance, telecommunications, and public services. In Finland, these roles are concentrated in urban areas and require strong Finnish or Swedish language skills, as customer interaction is predominantly in local languages. The work often involves using multiple communication channels and following scripts, with increasing integration of digital tools and AI assistance. While the role is entry-level for many, it offers a stepping stone into broader customer service and sales careers within the Finnish labour market.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from entry-level clerk to senior advisor, team leader, or supervisor, often within the same organization. Specialization in areas like technical support, sales, or quality assurance can lead to roles in customer experience management or training. Many clerks use the role as a foundation for moving into other business functions such as sales, marketing, or operations. In Finland, additional vocational qualifications can accelerate advancement, and some employers offer internal training programs. The role also provides flexible working arrangements, including remote work, which has become more common post-pandemic.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Answer standard FAQ questions (e.g. account balance, business hours)
- Handling simple order changes, returns, and exchange requests
- Transfer complex complaints to human agents
- Record and update customer information in the CRM system
- Send standard confirmation emails or SMS notifications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time translation, supporting multilingual customer service
- Emotion recognition alerts to help agents adjust communication strategies
- Smart knowledge base recommendations for quickly locating solutions to complex issues
- Automatic call summary generation reduces post-call documentation time
- Predict customer intent and prepare response scripts in advance

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly emotional, sensitive, or sudden complaints
- Coordinate across departments to solve complex non-standard issues.
- Build customer trust and maintain long-term relationships
- Respond to unforeseen emergencies (e.g., system failures)
- Deliver personalized, empathetic service experiences

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in major AI customer service platforms (e.g., Zendesk AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Learn basic data analysis (SQL/Excel) to analyze customer trends.
- Enhance conflict management and emotional communication skills
- Master the principles of Conversational Design
- Understand automation process building tools (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere)
- Develop multilingual skills (especially English) to serve international clients

**Key growth areas:**

- Multichannel service
- AI integration
- Digitalization
- Customer experience management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Contact Centre Information Clerk in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by employer, location, and experience. Entry-level positions start at a modest level, while experienced clerks can earn higher wages. The role is not typically on the high-salary list.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa for a Contact Centre Information Clerk job?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer, you can apply for a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). The process requires the employer to demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally, and the salary must meet the minimum threshold.

**Q: What career progression is possible?**
A: You can advance to team leader, supervisor, or move into related fields like customer experience management or sales.
