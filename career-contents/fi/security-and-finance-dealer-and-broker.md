# Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3311 (ISCO08)**

Securities and finance dealers and brokers in Finland execute orders to buy or sell financial instruments such as stocks, bonds, and derivatives, often working for banks, investment firms, or as independent brokers. They advise clients on financial products, manage portfolios, and ensure compliance with Finnish and EU regulations. The role requires strong analytical skills, market knowledge, and typically fluency in Finnish or Swedish, given the local client base. The sector is concentrated in Helsinki and other urban centers, with growth driven by fintech and digital trading platforms.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 1,065. Employment outlook for securities and finance dealers in Finland is stable, with moderate growth in fintech, sustainable finance, and algorithmic trading. However, automation and technology are reducing demand for traditional brokers. Regulatory changes (MiFID II) and market volatility create opportunities for specialists in risk management and compliance. The sector is sensitive to economic cycles, with hiring fluctuating with market conditions. Overall, demand is expected to grow slowly, especially for roles combining finance with technology.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior dealer or analyst, advancing to senior dealer, team lead, or portfolio manager. Many professionals move into risk management, compliance, or fintech roles. Licensing through FIN-FSA (e.g., investment advisor license) is mandatory and can enhance career prospects. Networking and experience with specific asset classes (e.g., ESG, derivatives) open doors to specialized roles. Some transition to hedge funds, asset management, or start their own advisory firms.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically execute stock, bond, and commodity orders through algorithms, no manual order placement
- AI automatically generates market analysis reports and investment briefs
- AI-driven chatbots handle common customer inquiries (e.g., account inquiries, transaction confirmations)
- Rule-based system automatically identifies potential suspicious transactions and triggers alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer transaction history and risk preferences to assist in generating personalized investment portfolio recommendations
- Real-time market sentiment monitoring and event impact simulation to improve decision quality for advisors
- Automatically generate compliance checklists and regulatory reports to reduce paperwork time
- Predict upselling and cross-selling opportunities through customer behavior models

**Human moat:**

- Build deep trust relationships, understand clients' non-financial needs (e.g., family wealth inheritance, emotional needs)
- Provide qualitative judgment and real-time adjustments to investment strategies in complex market environments
- Take on personalized responsibility for one-on-one consulting and crisis communication with high-net-worth clients
- Ethical judgment and interpretation concerning regulatory grey areas and new financial products

**Skills to build:**

- Financial data analysis tools (e.g., Python, R, SQL).
- Understanding of AI/machine learning applications in portfolio optimization
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and AI-enhanced customer interaction platforms
- Regulatory technology (RegTech) and compliance automation tools
- Understanding of complex financial derivative pricing models
- Soft skills: negotiation, empathy, crisis communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Fintech
- Sustainable Finance
- Digital Trading
- Risk Management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for securities dealers in Finland?**
A: Entry-level dealers earn around €50,000–€60,000 per year, while experienced professionals can earn €70,000–€100,000 or more, often with bonuses. Salaries are highly variable based on firm size, performance, and market conditions.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen become a securities dealer in Finland?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need a job offer from a Finnish employer, meet salary requirements, and pass regulatory exams. EU Blue Card or TTOL permit are common routes. Finnish language skills are often required for client-facing roles.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for Finnish securities dealers?**
A: Proficiency in English and Finnish, knowledge of MiFID II regulations, experience with trading platforms (e.g., Bloomberg), and certifications like CFA or FIN-FSA license are highly valued. Digital and algorithmic trading skills are increasingly important.
