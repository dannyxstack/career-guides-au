# Carpenter and Joiner — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7115 (ISCO08)**

Carpenters and joiners in Finland are skilled tradespeople who construct, install, and repair structures and fixtures made of wood and other materials. They work in residential and commercial construction, renovation, and furniture manufacturing. With Finland's strong tradition in wooden architecture and a growing focus on sustainable building, demand for carpenters remains steady. The profession requires precision, physical stamina, and knowledge of modern tools and safety practices. Many carpenters are employed by construction companies, while others specialize in custom furniture or restoration.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 12,881. The employment outlook for carpenters and joiners in Finland is stable to positive, driven by ongoing construction activity, renovation of aging housing stock, and a policy push for wooden multi-story buildings. The green transition also creates demand for energy-efficient retrofitting. However, the sector is cyclical and sensitive to economic downturns. Skilled carpenters are expected to find good opportunities, especially those with experience in modern building techniques and automation.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for carpenters in Finland typically starts with a vocational qualification and apprenticeship. After several years, one can become a master carpenter (mestari) or specialize in areas like fine joinery, restoration, or construction management. Some carpenters become self-employed or start their own companies. Further training in sustainable building or project management can lead to supervisory or teaching roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use BIM software for formwork design optimization (AI replaces manual calculation)
- Automatic inspection of template dimensions and position accuracy through AI image recognition
- Use drones or laser scanning to automatically measure field data
- AI-generated cutting lists and material optimization plans
- Automated template layout linked with CNC cutting.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted augmented reality (AR) headsets provide real-time template installation guidance
- AI-driven project management tools optimize processes and resource scheduling
- Digital twin simulates template forces to improve safety and quality
- AI voice assistant for on-site documentation and reporting
- Automatically generate construction progress reports and cost estimates

**Human moat:**

- On-site adjustment and manual fabrication of complex irregular formwork
- Multi-trade coordination and on-site problem-solving skills
- Experiential judgment of material properties (wood defects, humidity, etc.)
- Client communication and understanding of customized needs
- Physical adaptability for working at heights and heavy manual labor

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of BIM software (e.g., Revit, Tekla)
- Use of AR/VR construction assistance tools
- CNC woodworking equipment programming and operation.
- Basic data analysis (e.g., material utilization optimization)
- Sustainable materials and green building practices.
- Communication and teamwork (with AI tool assistance)

**Key growth areas:**

- green building
- renovation
- modular construction
- wooden house building

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a carpenter in Finland?**
A: Salary is determined by collective agreements and depends on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level wages are around €2,000–€2,500 per month, while experienced carpenters can earn €3,000–€3,500 or more. Overtime and bonus pay may apply.

**Q: Can a non-EU carpenter get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer and meet the salary threshold (at least €1,399/month in 2023) and the employer demonstrates that the position could not be filled locally. Apply for a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). The process can take several months.
