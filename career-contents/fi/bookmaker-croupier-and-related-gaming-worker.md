# Bookmaker, Croupier and Related Gaming Worker — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4212 (ISCO08)**

Bookmakers, croupiers and related gaming workers operate games of chance and manage betting activities in casinos, gaming halls, and online platforms. In Finland, the sector is dominated by the state monopoly Veikkaus, which operates physical and digital gaming services. Workers typically need good math skills, customer service orientation, and the ability to handle cash. The occupation is concentrated in Helsinki and a few other tourist hubs, with limited but stable demand. Work is often shift-based and requires a license from the police. While not a high-growth field, replacements and seasonal tourism maintain some openings.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 422. Employment outlook is stable but limited. Finland's strict gambling regulations limit expansion, with Veikkaus controlling most operations. The shift toward online gaming may reduce demand for physical croupiers, but live dealer roles and customer service positions persist. Replacement demand due to turnover is the main driver. No major growth is expected, and automation may reduce some entry-level tasks. Opportunities are best near large casino venues and during peak tourist seasons.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a cashier or assistant croupier, advancing to senior croupier, pit boss, or gaming supervisor. Management roles in security or operations require experience and often further training. Some move into online gaming support or compliance. The state monopoly limits career moves to within Veikkaus or a few private operators such as casino ships or international online firms. Continuous learning in customer service and game rules is valued, but upward mobility is modest.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic odds calculation and dynamic adjustment
- Simple bet processing and payment (replaced by self-service terminals)
- Player Identity Verification and Credit Check Automation
- Basic customer inquiries (chatbot)
- Initial card dealing and chip counting on some tables (RFID technology)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time monitoring of abnormal betting patterns and alerts
- Using big data analytics to optimize casino table configurations based on player behavior
- Multilingual AI translation helps dealers handle international clients
- AR/VR training to upskill new croupiers

**Human moat:**

- Authentic emotional interaction and trust building with players
- Human adjudication of disputes over complex rules or cheating
- Entertainment atmosphere creation and personalised service
- Sensitive judgment on legal and regulatory compliance
- Crisis Management (e.g., Intoxicated Customers, Conflict Mediation)

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using electronic table management systems
- Basic data analysis and betting pattern recognition
- Multilingual ability (at least English plus Mandarin or Arabic)
- Dispute resolution and customer psychology
- Familiar with Italian gambling regulations and anti-money laundering compliance
- AI tool operation (e.g., self-service terminal maintenance, chatbot monitoring)

**Key growth areas:**

- Online gaming
- Live dealer
- Regulation compliance
- Customer experience

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a croupier in Finland?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from 22,000 to 28,000 EUR annually, with experienced workers earning up to 35,000 EUR. Tips are common.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a croupier in Finland?**
A: Yes, but a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) is required. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. The job is not on the shortage list, so the process may be more difficult.
