# Clearing and Forwarding Agent — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3331 (ISCO08)**

Clearing and forwarding agents in Finland manage international logistics, including customs clearance, freight forwarding, and supply chain coordination. They ensure compliance with Finnish and EU trade regulations, often working for freight forwarders, shipping companies, or manufacturing firms. The role requires understanding of customs procedures, transport documentation, and Incoterms. Finland's position as a transit hub for Baltic and Russian trade makes this occupation vital, especially in the Helsinki region and ports like HaminaKotka. The work involves liaising with customs authorities, carriers, and clients to optimize cross-border movements. Digitalization and green logistics are reshaping the field, demanding adaptability and continuous learning.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts from operational tasks (documentation, customs clearance) to senior positions like logistics coordinator or team leader. Further advancement leads to management roles such as logistics manager or supply chain director. Specialization in niche areas like dangerous goods, cold chain, or customs advisory offers additional paths. Continuous professional development is valued, including certifications in customs procedures and logistics management. The field also allows movement between employers in freight forwarding, industry, and consultancies. Language skills (Finnish, Swedish, English, Russian) and familiarity with enterprise resource planning systems enhance career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate and check standardized documents such as customs declarations, bills of lading, commercial invoices, etc.
- Use OCR and NLP technologies to automatically extract and input cargo information and document data
- Basic trade compliance checks (e.g., automatic HS code classification, rules of origin verification)
- Real-time tracking of cargo status with automatic system updates and notifications to relevant parties
- Generate standardised transport reports, statistical statements and trade data summaries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI tools to quickly retrieve and interpret the latest trade regulations, tariff changes, and sanction lists of various countries
- AI-assisted analysis of supply chain risks (e.g., port congestion, route disruptions) and recommendation of alternatives
- Use smart chatbots to efficiently handle routine inquiries from customers/freight forwarders about document status and customs clearance progress.
- Use AI to optimize multimodal transport route combinations, balancing cost, time, and compliance requirements

**Human moat:**

- Skills in handling complex, non-standard, or disputed customs clearance anomalies (e.g., customs checks, classification disputes), including negotiation and resolution.
- Maintain long-term trust relationships and industry networks with freight forwarders, clients, and customs officials
- Intuitive judgment and experiential reliance on hidden risks in international trade (e.g., political sanctions, exchange rate fluctuations).
- Develop and implement enterprise-level import/export compliance strategies, train teams to adapt to new regulatory changes

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using AI modules in mainstream international trade management software (e.g., Descartes, eCustoms)
- Master the basics of EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) system configuration and troubleshooting
- New ability to interpret regulatory compliance data (e.g., deciphering customs AI pre-ruling logic)
- Operation and alert interpretation of supply chain visibility platforms (e.g. Project44, FourKites)
- Basic Python or RPA skills for customized data integration
- AI-assisted negotiation and conflict resolution skills (using AI to simulate customs clearance scenarios)

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce logistics
- Green logistics
- Digitalization
- Port operations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a clearing and forwarding agent in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced agents can earn above the national median. Logistics professionals with specialized skills (e.g., customs or dangerous goods) often command higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens become clearing and forwarding agents in Finland?**
A: Yes, through a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). The job must meet the minimum salary threshold and pass labor market testing. EU/EEA citizens have free movement. Finnish language skills and relevant experience improve chances.
