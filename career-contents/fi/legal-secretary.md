# Legal Secretary — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3342 (ISCO08)**

Legal secretaries in Finland provide administrative support to lawyers, judges, and legal departments, handling tasks such as document preparation, case file management, and client communication. They work in law firms, courts, corporate legal departments, and government agencies. The role requires strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and knowledge of legal terminology. In Finland, legal secretaries often hold a vocational qualification in legal and administrative services, with opportunities for further specialization.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for legal secretaries in Finland typically involves gaining experience to move into senior secretarial roles, paralegal positions, or office management within legal environments. Some may specialize in areas like corporate law, litigation, or intellectual property. Further education, such as a bachelor's degree in business or law, can open doors to higher-level administrative or managerial roles. The profession also offers opportunities to work in-house at large corporations or in the public sector.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Draft standardized letters and meeting minutes (AI-generated templates)
- Schedule and manage calendars and reminders (smart scheduling tools)
- Answer and transfer routine calls (AI voice customer service)
- Maintain and retrieve paper/electronic files (document management system)
- Data entry and organization (RPA and OCR automation)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Manage confidential or high-level affairs (trust and confidentiality)

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a legal secretary in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level legal secretaries may earn around €2,000-€2,500 per month, while experienced professionals can earn €3,000-€3,800 per month. In Helsinki, salaries tend to be higher. Collective agreements often set minimums, and bonuses may apply in private law firms.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA national work as a legal secretary in Finland?**
A: Yes, through the residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). You need a job offer from a Finnish employer that meets the salary threshold (€1,300/month in 2025) and complies with collective agreements. The employer must also demonstrate that the position could not be filled by local or EU labor. A D-visa fast track is not typically available for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for legal secretaries in Finland?**
A: With experience, legal secretaries can advance to senior secretary, office manager, or paralegal roles. Further education, such as a bachelor's degree in law or business, can lead to positions as a legal assistant or administrative coordinator. Some also transition into specialized legal fields like intellectual property or corporate law.
