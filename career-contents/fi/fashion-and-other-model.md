# Fashion and Other Models — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 5241 (ISCO08)**

Fashion and other models in Finland work primarily in the domestic fashion, advertising, and e-commerce sectors. The market is small, with most models employed on a freelance basis through agencies. Opportunities are concentrated in Helsinki, with occasional work in other cities for local brands and events. Digitalization has opened new avenues in social media influencing and online retail, but full-time positions are rare. The occupation is highly competitive, with many models combining assignments with other work.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with runway or print work, advancing to commercial modeling or transitioning to related fields like styling, photography, or marketing. Many models build a personal brand via social media to extend their career. Long-term, most exit the profession by their late 20s or early 30s to pursue more stable careers, often in creative industries or business.

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for models in Finland?**
A: Earnings are irregular and project-based. Hourly rates vary greatly by assignment and experience. Full-time annual income is uncommon; most models earn under €30,000 per year, but top earners can exceed that.

**Q: Can non-EU models work in Finland?**
A: Yes, non-EU models need a residence permit for employed persons (TTOL) with a job offer meeting income thresholds, or a D-visa for short assignments. Agency sponsorship is common.

**Q: Do models need formal education?**
A: No formal education is required. A strong portfolio and agency representation are more important. Some models pursue fashion-related studies to enhance career prospects.
