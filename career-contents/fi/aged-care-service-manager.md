# Aged Care Services Managers — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 1343 (ISCO08)**

Aged care services managers oversee residential and home-based care for the elderly in Finland. With the population aging rapidly, demand for high-quality care services is increasing, creating a need for skilled managers who can ensure regulatory compliance, staff management, and budget efficiency. The role combines social care expertise with leadership responsibilities, often in nursing homes or home care organizations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a care professional (e.g., registered nurse or social worker) to supervisory roles, then to unit manager, and finally to director of services. Many pursue a Master's degree in social and health management to advance. Continuing education in leadership, quality management, and elder care legislation is common. Some managers move into regional or municipal administrative positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate shift schedules and personnel assignments
- Automated nursing documentation and review
- Budget and Resource Allocation Report Based on Data Analysis
- Compliance monitoring and automatic alarm system
- Standardized customer satisfaction and service quality statistics

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to predict occupancy and care needs, optimizing bed and staff allocation
- Use analytical tools to improve care quality monitoring and improvement decisions
- Conduct risk management and emergency drills through digital twin simulations
- AI-assisted personalized care plan creation and dynamic adjustment
- Automatically generate compliance reports, freeing up human resources to focus on strategic planning.

**Human moat:**

- Handling complex interpersonal interactions and empathetic communication such as sudden behaviors in dementia patients
- Lead teams and mediate conflicts between staff and families
- Making human judgments and assuming responsibility in ethical dilemmas
- Cross-departmental coordination (medical, government, family) negotiation skills.

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (e.g., Power BI, Tableau)
- Application of AI tools in nursing management (e.g., predictive modeling)
- Digital project management and remote collaboration tools
- Basic programming skills (Python for data cleaning)
- Ethical and compliance knowledge updates (data privacy regulations)
- Change management and digital training skills for employees

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Home care expansion
- Digital care solutions
- Integrated services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an aged care services manager in Finland?**
A: Salaries range from €36,000 to €55,000 annually before tax, depending on experience, employer, and region. Public sector roles may have slightly lower starting pay but offer stability.

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as an aged care services manager in Finland?**
A: EU/EEA nationals can work freely. Non-EU/EEA nationals need a residence permit (TTOL) or EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer, relevant degree, and sufficient salary. Finnish language skills are often a practical requirement.

**Q: What is the career progression for aged care services managers?**
A: Start as care professional, move to unit manager, then to services manager, and potentially to regional director. Further studies in management or social policy can lead to higher positions.
