# Other Artistic and Cultural Associate Professional — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3435 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers professionals who support artistic and cultural activities, such as gallery assistants, museum technicians, cultural event coordinators, and arts administrators. In Finland, they work in museums, galleries, cultural centres, and event organizations. The field is modest but stable, with emphasis on digitalization and audience engagement. Public funding and cultural tourism drive demand, particularly in urban areas like Helsinki, Tampere, and Turku. Multilingual skills and digital competencies are increasingly valued.
