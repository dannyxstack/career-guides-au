# Shelf Filler — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 9334 (ISCO08)**

Shelf fillers in Finland are responsible for stocking shelves, rotating products, and maintaining store displays in retail outlets. The role is physically active and often part-time, with most positions available in grocery chains and large retail warehouses. The job is commonly filled by students, second-job seekers, and new immigrants due to low entry barriers, but turnover is high.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression from shelf filler is limited without additional education. Possibilities include moving to inventory control, logistics, or team leader roles within the store. In Finland, many shelf fillers are students or part-time workers who transition to other fields after studies. Long-term career development typically requires vocational training in logistics or retail management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of retail automation systems
- Inventory data analysis and forecasting tools
- Operation of equipment such as forklifts and electric pallet jacks
- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling
- Basic Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Cross-functional team collaboration and coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce fulfillment
- Inventory management
- Retail automation
- Shelf-ready packaging

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Finland?**
A: Salaries for shelf fillers in Finland are generally at the lower end, often below the national median for full-time workers. The hourly wage for experienced workers can reach €12-14, but part-time hours are common. Exact figures vary by employer and location.

**Q: Can a shelf filler get a work visa for Finland?**
A: Shelf filler is not considered a skilled occupation. Non-EU citizens face very high hurdles because the role typically does not meet salary or labor market test requirements for a residence permit. EU/EEA citizens can work freely without a permit.

**Q: Can I progress from shelf filler to a better job in Finland?**
A: Yes, but it usually requires additional training. Many shelf fillers move into logistics, inventory management, or pursue vocational qualifications in retail or supply chain management to access higher-skilled roles.
