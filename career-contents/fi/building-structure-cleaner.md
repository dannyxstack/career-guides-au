# Building Structure Cleaner — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 7133 (ISCO08)**

Building structure cleaners maintain the cleanliness and appearance of buildings by washing windows, walls, floors, and other surfaces, and removing graffiti. In Finland, this occupation is essential for property maintenance and is often performed by specialized cleaning companies or facility management firms. The work can be physically demanding and may involve working at heights or with chemical cleaning agents. Many workers are immigrants, and the job offers entry-level opportunities for those with limited Finnish language skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for building structure cleaners can lead to supervisory roles, such as team leader or site supervisor, or specialization in industrial cleaning, high-pressure cleaning, or restoration. Some workers advance into facility management or start their own cleaning businesses. On-the-job training and vocational qualifications in cleaning and maintenance can enhance prospects. The occupation often serves as a stepping stone to other roles in building maintenance or property services.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean exterior walls using high-pressure water jets or chemical agents
- Clean glass curtain walls with high-altitude rope work
- Roof debris removal and simple maintenance
- Periodic repetitive facade cleaning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI plans cleaning routes and optimizes chemical ratios.
- UAV inspection to identify dirty areas
- Digital twin simulation of cleaning effectiveness
- Precise application of cleaning agents by automatic spraying equipment
- Real-time sensor feedback adjusts cleaning intensity.

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling complex stains (e.g., bird remains, oil stains)
- Safety risk assessment and emergency rescue capabilities
- Communicating with customers to customize cleaning solutions
- Professional responsibility requiring a high-altitude work permit

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and maintenance
- Fundamentals of programming smart cleaning equipment
- AI image recognition for stain analysis
- High-altitude work safety regulations updated
- Low-voltage fault troubleshooting and basic repairs.

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, employer, and location, but generally align with the median for cleaning occupations in Finland.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa as a building structure cleaner?**
A: Yes, a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) is possible if you have a valid job offer and the salary meets the minimum threshold, typically set by collective agreements.

**Q: Do I need formal qualifications to work as a building structure cleaner in Finland?**
A: No formal education is mandatory, but employers often require an occupational safety card. A hygiene passport may be needed for food-related cleaning.
