# Sweeper and Related Labourer — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 9613 (ISCO08)**

Sweepers and related labourers perform basic cleaning and maintenance tasks such as sweeping streets, parks, public spaces, and industrial sites. In Finland, this occupation is often employed by municipalities, cleaning companies, and facilities management firms. The work is physically demanding and typically requires no formal education. It is a common entry-level job with a high turnover rate.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 113. Employment outlook for sweepers in Finland is stable but limited, driven by ongoing urban maintenance needs and an aging workforce. Automation and mechanization (e.g., street sweeping machines) may reduce demand for manual sweeping over time. However, roles in industrial cleaning and waste management are expected to persist. Overall, job opportunities are moderate with many positions seasonal or part-time.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression from sweeper is limited, but workers can advance to supervisory roles or specialize in industrial cleaning, waste sorting, or green maintenance. Finnish language skills are often required for customer-facing or team leader roles. With experience, some transition to related fields like janitorial services or facilities management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI scheduling and monitoring tools
- Automated cleaning equipment maintenance
- Basic Chemistry and Disinfectant Knowledge
- Data recording and analysis skills
- Communication and customer service skills
- Compliance and safety regulations training

**Key growth areas:**

- urban cleaning
- industrial cleaning
- green maintenance
- waste management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sweeper in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and location, typically around the minimum wage for the cleaning sector, often supplemented by shift work allowances.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU nationals?**
A: Non-EU nationals usually need a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL), but the low salary often makes it difficult to meet income requirements. EU/EEA citizens have free movement and can work without a permit.
