# Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3214 (ISCO08)**

Medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Finland design, fabricate, and repair dental prostheses (crowns, bridges, dentures) and medical devices (orthotics, prosthetics). The field is experiencing technological shifts toward digital workflows (CAD/CAM) and an aging population driving demand for both dental and medical prosthetics. Employment is concentrated in private laboratories, dental clinics, and hospitals, with most technicians holding a vocational or applied science degree. The occupation requires precision, manual dexterity, and knowledge of materials and anatomy.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to senior technician, laboratory manager, or specialist (e.g., implant prosthetics, maxillofacial prosthetics). Some technicians pursue further education to become dental technology teachers or researchers. Self-employment by opening a private laboratory is also common. Digital skills increasingly differentiate candidates and open pathways into product development or sales.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Dentistry
- Implant Prosthetics
- Geriatric Care
- Orthotics and Prosthetics
