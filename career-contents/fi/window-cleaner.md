# Window Cleaner — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 9123 (ISCO08)**

Window cleaners in Finland provide cleaning services for residential and commercial buildings, using equipment like ladders, water-fed poles, and harnesses for high-rise work. The occupation requires physical fitness and attention to safety, with demand driven by urban maintenance and property management needs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is the salary for window cleaners determined in Finland?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements, typically hourly rates that vary by region and employer. Starting pay is lower, but experience and specialized skills can lead to higher rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a window cleaner in Finland?**
A: It is possible but challenging. The employer must prove that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available, and the salary must meet the minimum threshold for a residence permit. Most window cleaners are EU citizens.

**Q: What are the prospects for career growth as a window cleaner?**
A: With experience, you can move into supervisory roles or start your own cleaning business. Additional certifications in safety or specialized cleaning can open opportunities in niche markets like high-rise or solar panel cleaning.
