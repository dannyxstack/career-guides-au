# Data Entry Clerk — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 4132 (ISCO08)**

Data entry clerks in Finland perform routine data input and verification tasks across various sectors, including administration, finance, and logistics. The role requires basic computer skills and attention to detail, often serving as an entry point into office work. Demand has declined due to automation and digitalization, but opportunities remain in smaller enterprises and public sector digitization projects. The occupation is not typically a migration route due to low skill requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for data entry clerks in Finland often involves moving into administrative assistant, office manager, or data processing specialist roles. With additional training, clerks can transition into IT support, database administration, or business process automation. Vocational qualifications in business administration or IT can accelerate advancement. Some employers support further education for motivated employees.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually enter data from paper or electronic documents into databases or spreadsheets
- Check format and basic accuracy of input data
- Repetitive batch data entry, such as invoices and forms
- Basic data cleaning and deduplication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI tools to automatically extract document data, with humans handling exceptions.
- Use automated scripts to verify data consistency and improve efficiency
- Analyze data trends and generate reports through AI-assisted analysis

**Human moat:**

- Complex data judgment and anomaly handling capability
- Client communication and business understanding (e.g., explaining data deviations)
- Cross-system data integration and process optimization
- Maintain data privacy and security compliance

**Skills to build:**

- Learn Python or VBA for automated data processing
- Proficiency in advanced Excel functions (e.g., Power Query, macros)
- Familiar with OCR and RPA tools (e.g., UiPath)
- Data quality management and database basics (SQL)
- Basic data analysis and visualization (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitization
- Data quality
- Remote data entry
- Record management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a data entry clerk in Finland?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience, but are generally below the national average for office workers. Most positions are hourly or monthly, and the annual income typically ranges between €25,000 and €35,000 depending on employer and location.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work visa for a data entry job in Finland?**
A: It is very difficult. The job must meet the minimum salary requirement (around €1,200-1,400 per month) and pass a labor market test. Due to high domestic availability, such permits are rarely granted. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: Is Finnish language proficiency required for data entry jobs?**
A: In most cases, yes. Many positions require Finnish, especially in public sector or local companies. Some international firms may accept English, but fluency in Finnish is strongly recommended.
