# Stationary Plant and Machine Operator Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 8189 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers operators of stationary industrial machinery and plant equipment in sectors such as manufacturing, energy production, water treatment, and mining in Finland. These workers monitor and control processes, adjust settings, and perform basic maintenance to ensure efficient and safe operations. The role requires technical knowledge of specific machinery and adherence to safety protocols.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression often involves moving from operator to senior operator, shift supervisor, or maintenance technician. Further vocational qualifications can lead to specialist roles in automation or process optimization. Experience in high-demand sectors like renewable energy or water treatment enhances advancement prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated production quality inspection using sensors and AI vision
- Predictive maintenance scheduling for equipment based on historical data
- Standardized material handling and packaging processes.
- Adaptive adjustment and optimization of production parameters

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted predictive maintenance improves equipment reliability
- Real-time production data dashboards assist operational decisions
- Smart scheduling system optimizes multi-machine coordination efficiency
- Remote expert collaboration system enables rapid fault diagnosis

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of non-standard mechanical faults.
- Ability to flexibly switch between multiple varieties and small batch production
- Perception of subtle anomalies like unusual equipment noises and vibrations.
- Safety management and emergency shutdown procedures

**Skills to build:**

- Basic programming and PLC debugging.
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data analysis
- Collaborative robot (KUKA/Universal Robots) operation
- Lean production and Six Sigma methods
- Understanding of AI tools in industrial applications

**Key growth areas:**

- automation
- green energy
- water treatment
- process industry

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a stationary plant operator in Finland?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective bargaining agreements and vary by industry, experience, and shift patterns. For exact figures, consult Statistics Finland or the relevant trade union.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a stationary plant operator in Finland?**
A: Yes, through the Residence permit for an employed person (TTOL). You need a valid job offer that meets the minimum salary threshold (usually based on collective agreements) and other requirements.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for stationary plant operators?**
A: With experience and further training, you can become a shift supervisor, maintenance specialist, or process operator in advanced fields like automation or renewable energy.
