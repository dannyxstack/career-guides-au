# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Finland

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons in Finland produce technical drawings and plans for engineering, architecture, and manufacturing, using computer-aided design (CAD) software. They work closely with engineers and architects to convert concepts into detailed schematics. The role is integral to construction projects, industrial design, and product development, with demand linked to investment in infrastructure and technology. Finland's focus on digitalization and sustainable building practices shapes the work, requiring proficiency in BIM and 3D modeling.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a draughtsperson role after vocational training or a bachelor's degree. Advancement can lead to senior drafter, design engineer, or project manager positions with additional education and experience. Specialization in areas like structural engineering, mechanical design, or Building Information Modeling (BIM) enhances prospects. Continuous learning in new software and modular construction methods is common, with some professionals transitioning into engineering roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- BIM
- Renewable Energy
- Infrastructure
- Digitalization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a draughtsperson in Finland?**
A: Salary depends on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions start around competitive levels, while experienced drafters earn higher. Check official statistics for current figures.

**Q: What visa do I need to work as a draughtsperson in Finland?**
A: EU/EEA nationals can work freely. Non-EU nationals typically need a residence permit for an employed person (TTOL) with a job offer. The D-visa fast track is available for selected nationalities.

**Q: Do I need a university degree to become a draughtsperson in Finland?**
A: No, a vocational qualification is sufficient for many roles, but a bachelor's degree can improve career advancement and salary prospects.
