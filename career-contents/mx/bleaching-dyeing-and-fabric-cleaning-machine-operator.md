# Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 8154 (ISCO08)**

Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators control equipment that processes textiles and fabrics through bleaching, dyeing, washing, and finishing stages. They work in textile manufacturing plants, laundries, and industrial cleaning facilities, ensuring consistent color application and fabric quality. This occupation involves monitoring machine operations, adjusting chemical mixtures, and performing routine maintenance. In Mexico, these operators are employed in the textile industry, which accounts for a modest share of manufacturing employment. The role requires attention to detail and physical stamina, as work often involves standing for long periods and handling chemicals.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in Mexico?**
A: Annual salaries for Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators typically range from 96,000 to 156,000 MXN, depending on experience and region. Entry-level workers earn near minimum wage, while experienced operators may earn higher. Overtime and shift differentials can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for this occupation in Mexico?**
A: It is possible but difficult. The employer must sponsor a Temporary Resident Visa with work permit (Oferta de Trabajo). However, due to the low skill level, the INM may consider the position not eligible for skilled migration. Most workers in this role are Mexican citizens or permanent residents.

**Q: What are the main hazards of this job?**
A: Operators are exposed to chemical dyes, bleaches, and cleaning agents, which can cause skin irritation or respiratory issues if safety gear is not used. The work involves heavy lifting, standing for long periods, and potential injuries from machinery. Proper training and personal protective equipment are mandatory.
