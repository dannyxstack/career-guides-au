# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 1431 (ISCO08)**

Sports, recreation and cultural centre managers plan, direct and coordinate the operations of facilities such as sports complexes, gyms, cultural centres, and community recreation spaces. They oversee staff, budgets, programming, and customer service to ensure safe, engaging environments. In Mexico, these roles span public municipal centres, private fitness chains, and cultural institutes, requiring a mix of administrative, marketing, and people-management skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a coordinator or assistant manager in a single facility, advancing to centre manager, then regional or operations manager for multi-site chains. Specialisation is possible in areas like event management or sports tourism. Continuing education in business administration or sports management can accelerate advancement. The path is linear in large organisations, but small centres may offer slower growth.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a sports centre manager in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by location and type of centre. In major cities, annual pre-tax income ranges from 250,000 MXN to 500,000 MXN. Public sector roles may be lower but offer stability; private clubs and gyms often pay higher with performance bonuses.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a sports centre manager in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it is employer-sponsored. The company must register with the National Immigration Institute (INM) and prove no suitable Mexican candidate. The Temporary Resident Visa with work permit is common, but processing can take 2-4 months.
