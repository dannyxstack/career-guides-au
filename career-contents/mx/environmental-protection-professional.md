# Environmental Protection Professional — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2133 (ISCO08)**

Environmental Protection Professionals in Mexico develop and implement policies, programs, and strategies to protect the environment, manage natural resources, and ensure compliance with environmental regulations, working across government, private sector, and NGOs.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Environmental Protection Professional in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. Entry-level roles may start around MXN 200,000-300,000 annually, while senior positions can exceed MXN 800,000. Public sector salaries are lower but offer stability.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as an Environmental Protection Professional without a job offer?**
A: No, Mexico does not have a points-based skilled migration route. You must have a job offer from a Mexican employer who sponsors your Temporary Resident Visa with work permit.

**Q: Is a bachelor's degree mandatory for this profession in Mexico?**
A: Yes, most regulated roles require a bachelor's degree in a related field and registration of the degree (Cédula Profesional) to practice legally.
