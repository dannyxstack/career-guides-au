# Beautician — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 5142 (ISCO08)**

Beauticians provide a range of personal care services including hairstyling, makeup application, skincare treatments, manicures, and pedicures. They work in salons, spas, or as independent contractors, catering to clients' aesthetic needs. In Mexico, the profession is characterized by a mix of formal and informal employment, with many practitioners operating independently to serve local communities.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a beautician in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by location and clientele. Many beauticians work on commission or earn tips, with annual income ranging from 80,000 to 250,000 MXN before taxes. Independent stylists in upscale areas can earn more.

**Q: Can a foreign beautician obtain a work visa for Mexico?**
A: It is challenging. The main route is an employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa, but employers must prove the position cannot be filled by a Mexican worker. Beauticians rarely meet this requirement unless they have exceptional skills or a niche specialty.

**Q: What are the best ways to advance as a beautician in Mexico?**
A: Specializing in high-demand areas (e.g., bridal makeup, chemical treatments), obtaining advanced certifications, and building a strong client base through social media are key. Opening your own salon or becoming a product trainer also offers progression.
