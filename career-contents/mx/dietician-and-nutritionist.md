# Dietitian and Nutritionist — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2265 (ISCO08)**

Dietitians and nutritionists in Mexico assess clients' nutritional needs, develop meal plans, and provide counseling to promote health and manage diseases. They work in hospitals, clinics, public health agencies, food companies, and private practice, collaborating with healthcare teams to address issues like obesity, diabetes, and malnutrition. The role requires strong interpersonal skills, scientific knowledge, and cultural sensitivity to tailor advice to Mexico's diverse dietary traditions.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for dietitians in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and location. Entry-level positions in public hospitals or clinics may start around 8,000-12,000 MXN per month, while experienced professionals in private practice or specialized roles can earn 20,000-35,000 MXN monthly. Private sector roles in food companies or wellness centers tend to pay higher than public sector.

**Q: Can a foreign dietitian work in Mexico without a job offer?**
A: Generally no. To obtain a work permit, you need a job offer from a Mexican employer who sponsors your Temporary Resident Visa. The employer must demonstrate that your skills are not readily available locally. Independent work (e.g., private practice) usually requires Permanent Residence or Mexican citizenship.

**Q: Do foreign dietitians need to revalidate their degrees in Mexico?**
A: Yes. To obtain the Cédula Profesional, your foreign degree must be accepted and registered with SEP. This process can be lengthy and may require exams or additional coursework. Without the cédula, you cannot legally work as a dietitian.
