# Window Cleaner — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 9123 (ISCO08)**

Window cleaners are responsible for cleaning windows, glass surfaces, and sometimes building facades of residential, commercial, and high-rise buildings. They use various tools such as squeegees, ladders, harnesses, and water-fed poles, adhering to safety standards to prevent accidents. Work may involve heights and requires physical fitness and attention to detail.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant or apprentice, then moves to independent cleaner or team leader. Experienced window cleaners can specialize in high-rise or industrial cleaning, start their own cleaning business, or move into supervision and training. Additional certifications in rope access or safety can enhance opportunities and income potential.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Mexico?**
A: Salaries for window cleaners in Mexico are generally low, reflecting the low skill requirements. Earnings can increase with specialization in high-rise cleaning or by starting a cleaning business.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a window cleaner in Mexico?**
A: It is possible but difficult. Employers must prove no local candidate is available. Most window cleaners are Mexican nationals. The visa process is typically not feasible for this occupation due to its low-skill classification.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for window cleaners?**
A: Advancement includes becoming a supervisor, starting a cleaning business, or specializing in high-rise or industrial cleaning with rope access certification. Experience can lead to higher-paying contracts.
