# Professional Services Manager — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 1349 (ISCO08)**

Professional services managers oversee teams delivering expert services such as consulting, legal, accounting, or engineering, ensuring quality, client satisfaction, and operational efficiency in Mexico's growing service sector.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a professional services manager in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and firm size. Entry-level managers may earn around 600,000 MXN annually, while experienced managers in top consulting or legal firms can exceed 2,000,000 MXN. These figures are estimates and should be verified with current market data.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a professional services manager without a job offer?**
A: No, the primary route for skilled migration is the employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa with work permit. A job offer is required. However, if you have sufficient points (age, education, investments, etc.), you may qualify for a Permanent Resident Visa without a job offer.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for professional services managers?**
A: With experience, you can move to senior management, director, or partner roles. Some transition to entrepreneurship, starting their own consulting firm. Earning an MBA or professional certification (e.g., PMP, CPA) can significantly boost prospects.
