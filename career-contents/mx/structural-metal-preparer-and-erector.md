# Structural Metal Preparer and Erector — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors cut, shape, assemble, and install metal frameworks for buildings, bridges, towers, and other structures. They work at heights, use heavy machinery and welding equipment, and read blueprints to ensure precise placement. In Mexico, this role is vital in construction, manufacturing, and infrastructure projects, requiring physical strength and safety awareness.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or apprentice, advancing to journeyman, then supervisor or project manager. Specializations in welding, rigging, or crane operation boost earning potential. Certifications from AWS or STPS enhance job mobility. With experience, some start their own subcontracting businesses. The path is skill-based, with formal training from CONALEP or on-the-job learning.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Precise dimensional measurement and calibration using BIM and laser scanning
- Optimize cutting and welding parameters for steel structures using AI software
- Drones automatically check structural alignment and bolt tightening status

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted crane and rigging configuration planning
- Risk prediction and construction plan optimization based on historical data
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted installation guidance and training
- Real-time tool status monitoring and predictive maintenance alerts.

**Human moat:**

- Physical operation and assembly in complex construction site environments
- Flexible judgment for handling non-standard or damaged materials
- Coordinate with the team and handle emergency situations
- Strict enforcement and supervision of safety regulations

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this role in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by region, experience, and employer. They typically exceed the minimum wage, especially on large industrial projects. For precise figures, consult official statistics such as ENOE or IMSS data.

**Q: Can foreign workers obtain a visa for this occupation in Mexico?**
A: It is possible but challenging. An employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa requires a job offer, labor market test, and proof that no local worker is available. This is not a dedicated skilled-migration route, and most positions are filled locally.

**Q: What training is needed to become a Structural Metal Preparer and Erector?**
A: Common paths include a technical high school diploma from CONALEP (3 years, often free) or on-the-job apprenticeship (1-2 years). Certifications in welding or safety are recommended and sometimes mandatory for larger projects.
