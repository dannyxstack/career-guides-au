# Butcher, Fishmonger and Related Food Preparer — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7511 (ISCO08)**

Butchers, fishmongers and related food preparers are skilled tradespeople who cut, trim, prepare and display meat, poultry and fish for sale in retail or wholesale settings, often working in supermarkets, butcher shops or fish markets, and may also be involved in curing, smoking or marinating products to meet customer preferences and food safety standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or assistant, advancing to journeyman butcher, then master butcher or shop manager. Some specialize in organic, halal or kosher products, or move into roles in food safety inspection, sales representative for meat companies, or start their own business. Continuing education in food handling and emerging trends like plant-based proteins may open new opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Batch cutting and segmentation of standard parts
- Repetitive motions in deboning and skinning processes
- Basic processing like meat grinding and sausage stuffing
- Weighing, labeling, and packaging line operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI vision systems for meat grading and defect detection
- Optimizing cutting plans through data analysis to reduce waste
- Manage meat preservation and turnover using smart inventory systems
- Using augmented reality (AR) to assist training new employees in cutting techniques

**Human moat:**

- Hand-finishing special parts and custom cutting according to client requirements
- Sensory judgment of meat freshness, texture, and abnormalities
- Flexibility in handling unexpected orders and personalized needs.
- Provide cooking advice and demonstrate cutting techniques to customers

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of automated cutting equipment
- Food quality and safety management systems (e.g., HACCP)
- Basic data analysis and inventory management software
- Customer service and communication skills
- Fine hand cutting (for high-end customisation)
- Food safety regulations and traceability knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Organic Butchery
- Meat Processing
- Food Safety
- Specialty Meats

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a butcher in Mexico?**
A: Annual salaries vary by region and experience, but generally range from MXN 90,000 to MXN 180,000, with master butchers or shop owners earning more. Official statistics from INEGI should be consulted for precise figures.

**Q: Can a foreign butcher obtain a work visa for Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. An employer must sponsor a temporary resident visa with a work permit, proving no local worker is available. The occupation is not on priority lists, so approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for butchers?**
A: With experience, butchers can become supervisors, open their own shop, or specialize in premium products like dry-aged meats or charcuterie. Additional training in business management or food technology can open further roles.
