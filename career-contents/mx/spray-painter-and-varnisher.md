# Spray Painter and Varnisher — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7132 (ISCO08)**

Spray painters and varnishers apply paint, varnish, and other coatings to surfaces using spray equipment, ensuring even coverage and a smooth finish. They work in automotive, furniture, industrial, and construction sectors, preparing surfaces by cleaning and masking, mixing paints to specifications, and operating spray guns. Attention to detail, knowledge of safety procedures, and ability to work with various materials are essential.

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals who enjoy detailed, hands-on work and take pride in achieving a smooth, even finish.
- Those who can work in ventilated areas, follow safety protocols, and handle chemical materials responsibly.
- People willing to learn and adapt to new coating technologies and environmental standards.

**Who is not suited**
- Individuals with respiratory conditions or allergies to paint fumes and chemicals.
- Those who dislike repetitive tasks or working in dusty, noisy environments.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a spray painter in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, industry, and region. Entry-level painters may earn near minimum wage, while experienced specialists in automotive or industrial sectors can earn significantly more. According to official statistics, the average monthly income is modest but can increase with specialization.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a spray painter in Mexico?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a local worker and meet other requirements set by the National Immigration Institute (INM).

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for spray painters?**
A: Experienced painters can become supervisors, quality inspectors, or start their own painting businesses. Specializing in high-value coatings like automotive refinishing or aerospace painting can also lead to higher earnings and job stability.
