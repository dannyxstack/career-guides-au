# Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Manager — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 1312 (ISCO08)**

Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers oversee the operations of fish farms, hatcheries, and fishing enterprises in Mexico, managing breeding, feeding, harvesting, and processing to ensure sustainable production. They coordinate teams, monitor water quality, control disease, and comply with environmental regulations. This role is vital for Mexico's growing aquaculture sector, particularly in states like Sinaloa, Sonora, and Veracruz, where shrimp and tilapia farming are key economic activities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from technical or supervisory roles in aquaculture facilities. With experience, managers can advance to regional or national production director, or specialize in quality control, hatchery management, or sustainable certification. Entrepreneurship is also common—starting or consulting for new farms. Continuous education in marine biology, business, or environmental management enhances prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually record farming data (water temperature, feeding amounts, etc.)
- Generate daily production reports and compliance documents
- Image recognition of initial disease symptoms
- Inventory counting and feed order calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses sensors and AI to predict optimal feeding times and amounts
- Monitor fish health and abnormal behavior through machine vision.
- Simulate the impact of different stocking densities and feed plans on yield
- Real-time water quality monitoring and automatic adjustment of oxygenation and filtration equipment

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making for responding to sudden disease outbreaks or environmental disasters
- Experience-based biosecurity management and overall disease prevention planning.
- Negotiations and relationship management with regulators, suppliers, and sales channels
- Assess and implement new sustainable aquaculture technologies

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (Python/R)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor system management
- Operation and troubleshooting of automated control systems
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainability certification standards
- Inspection skills for drones or underwater robots

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable Aquaculture
- Shrimp Farming
- Tilapia Production
- Export Markets

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with a background in marine biology, agribusiness, or environmental science who enjoy hands-on fieldwork and managing teams.
- Entrepreneurial-minded professionals aiming to own or manage a fish farm, and those willing to relocate to coastal or rural areas.

**Who is not suited**

- Individuals uncomfortable with variable schedules, outdoor work in harsh conditions, and dealing with biological risks.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Manager in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and facility size. Entry-level managers earn around MXN 250,000–400,000 annually, while experienced managers in large export-oriented farms can earn MXN 500,000–800,000. Bonuses and profit-sharing may apply.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa as an Aquaculture Production Manager in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it requires a formal job offer from a Mexican employer who will sponsor a Temporary Resident Work Visa. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. The process involves INM approval and labor validation.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities in this field?**
A: With experience, you can become a regional production director, open your own aquaculture business, or specialize in hatchery management, quality control, or environmental compliance. Certifications in sustainable aquaculture can boost prospects.
