# Philosopher, Historian or Political Scientist — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2633 (ISCO08)**

These professionals research and develop theories, concepts, and methods in philosophy, history, and political science, often advising governments, NGOs, or academic institutions on policy, ethics, or historical context. In Mexico, they typically work in universities, think tanks, government agencies, or as independent consultants, conducting research, publishing papers, and teaching.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a political scientist in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector. Entry-level researchers in academia or government earn around 300,000–600,000 MXN annually, while experienced consultants or professors can reach 800,000–1,200,000 MXN. Private sector roles in policy analysis may pay more, but overall, income is moderate compared to other professional fields.

**Q: What visa do I need to work as a historian or political scientist in Mexico?**
A: To work in Mexico in this field, you typically need an employer-sponsored Temporary Residence visa (Residencia Temporal) with a work permit. Your employer must file a petition with the National Immigration Institute (INM). Permanent residence is possible after four years or through family ties, but independent skilled migration points-based routes are not available for this occupation.

**Q: Is a PhD necessary for a career in philosophy or history in Mexico?**
A: A PhD is strongly recommended for university teaching and high-level research positions, but many mid-level roles in government or think tanks only require a master's degree. Publications and networking are crucial for advancement.
