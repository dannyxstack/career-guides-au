# Economist — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2631 (ISCO08)**

An economist analyzes data, trends, and economic policies to forecast economic conditions and advise businesses, governments, and organizations on financial decisions, resource allocation, and market strategies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior analyst or research assistant, advancing to senior economist, lead researcher, or policy advisor. Many pursue a master's or doctoral degree for senior roles. Specialization in fields like behavioral economics, financial modeling, or environmental economics can differentiate candidates.

- Create standard statistical charts and reports

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an economist in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Entry-level economists in the private sector earn around MXN 300,000–450,000 annually, while senior roles in consulting or finance can exceed MXN 1,000,000. Public sector salaries are generally lower but stable.

**Q: Can a foreign economist get a work visa to Mexico?**
A: Yes, with a job offer from a Mexican employer who sponsors a Temporary Resident Visa with work permit. The process requires a job contract, apostilled degree, and other documentation. Alternatively, Permanent Resident status can be obtained via family or points, but not solely for work.

**Q: Do I need a master's degree to work as an economist in Mexico?**
A: A bachelor's degree is sufficient for entry-level roles, but a master's or doctorate is often required for senior positions in research, policy, or academia.
