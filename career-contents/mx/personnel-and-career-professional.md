# Personnel and Careers Professional — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2423 (ISCO08)**

Personnel and careers professionals, commonly known as HR professionals, manage recruitment, training, performance evaluation, and employee relations within organizations. They ensure compliance with labor laws, develop talent management strategies, and foster workplace culture. In Mexico, these roles are concentrated in corporate headquarters, maquiladoras, and service firms, requiring knowledge of local labor legislation and bilingual skills for multinational companies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for HR professionals in Mexico?**
A: Entry-level HR assistants earn 150,000–240,000 MXN annually; mid-level generalists 300,000–500,000 MXN; senior managers/directors 600,000–1,200,000 MXN. Salaries vary by industry, company size, and location (higher in Mexico City, Monterrey).

**Q: Can a foreign HR professional work in Mexico without a degree in HR?**
A: Yes, a bachelor's in any field is generally sufficient, but a related degree or experience in HR, business, or psychology is preferred. For the employer-sponsored temporary work visa, the job offer must prove the role cannot be filled by a Mexican citizen first. Professional certifications can strengthen the case.

**Q: Is a master's degree necessary to advance in HR in Mexico?**
A: Not mandatory, but an MBA or master's in HR can accelerate promotion to leadership roles, especially in multinational companies. Many professionals gain experience and post-graduate diplomas instead.
