# Legal Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2619 (ISCO08)**

Legal professionals not elsewhere classified include lawyers, judges, and legal advisors who provide general legal services, represent clients in court, and advise on legal rights and obligations. In Mexico, they typically work in law firms, corporate legal departments, government agencies, or as independent practitioners. This category covers those who do not specialize in a specific area like criminal or civil law. The role requires a deep understanding of Mexican law, strong analytical skills, and the ability to navigate complex legal frameworks.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a legal professional in Mexico?**
A: Mid-level lawyers in private practice earn between 300,000 and 600,000 MXN annually. Senior partners or in-house counsel at large corporations may earn over 1,000,000 MXN. Government lawyers typically earn less, around 200,000-400,000 MXN.

**Q: Can a foreign lawyer work in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but requires a job offer from a Mexican employer who sponsors a Temporary Resident Visa with work permit. Foreign law degrees must be validated by SEP, and the Cédula Profesional must be obtained, which may involve additional exams or coursework.
