# Dancer or Choreographer — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2653 (ISCO08)**

Dancers and choreographers in Mexico perform or create dance routines for live performances, film, television, and cultural events, often working with dance companies, theater groups, or as freelancers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a performer, then moves into choreography, teaching, or artistic direction. Building a portfolio and networking are crucial. Many dancers also work as instructors or in related fields like fitness or therapy.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a dancer or choreographer in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely: freelance dancers may earn 5,000-15,000 MXN per month, while employed choreographers in theater or TV can earn 15,000-40,000 MXN monthly. Income is often project-based and unstable.

**Q: Can a foreign dancer get a work visa for Mexico?**
A: Yes, through a Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit, but must be sponsored by a Mexican employer (e.g., dance company, event organizer). The process involves a job offer, labor market test, and approval from INM.

**Q: What skills are most important for a dancer in Mexico?**
A: Technical proficiency, creativity, physical endurance, and adaptability to different dance styles (folkloric, contemporary, urban). Networking and teaching skills also help.
