# Services Manager — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 1439 (ISCO08)**

Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified oversee operations, staff, and client relations in a variety of service industries such as cleaning, security, facility management, and call centers. They coordinate resources, ensure quality standards, manage budgets, and drive customer satisfaction.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Services Manager in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by industry, location, and experience. Generally, entry-level managers earn between 250,000 and 450,000 MXN annually, while senior managers in large firms can exceed 800,000 MXN. Benefits and bonuses are common.

**Q: Can a foreign Services Manager obtain a work visa in Mexico?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit. The employer must prove the role cannot be filled by a Mexican national. Points-based residency is not available for this occupation. Family-based permanent residence is an alternative.

**Q: What are the best industries for Services Managers in Mexico?**
A: Top sectors include facility management, outsourced cleaning/security, call centers, and hospitality. Rapid growth in corporate outsourcing and shared services creates steady demand.
