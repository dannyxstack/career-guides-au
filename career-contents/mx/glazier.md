# Glazier — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

A glazier installs, replaces, and repairs glass windows, doors, skylights, mirrors, and other glass fixtures in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. This trade requires precision, physical strength, and comfort working at heights, often using tools such as suction cups, cutters, and sealants. In Mexico, glaziers are essential in the construction and renovation sectors, with opportunities in both new builds and maintenance projects. The occupation is not regulated by a mandatory license, but training through vocational courses or apprenticeships is common to develop safe and skilled work practices.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Glaziers typically start as apprentices or helpers, learning on the job under experienced craftsmen. With experience, they can advance to journeyman glazier, team leader, or supervisor on construction sites. Specialization in areas like automotive glass, stained glass, or structural glazing for skyscrapers can increase earning potential. Some experienced glaziers open their own small businesses or subcontract for larger companies. Formal certifications or vocational training can accelerate career progression, especially in commercial projects requiring compliance with safety standards.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience, ranging from approximately 8,000 to 15,000 MXN per month for skilled workers, with higher pay in industrial settings.

**Q: Can a foreign glazier obtain a work visa in Mexico?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa with work permit. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Mexican worker. It is a restricted route.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for glaziers?**
A: Experienced glaziers can become supervisors, start their own business, or specialize in areas like stained glass, automotive glass, or architectural glass.
