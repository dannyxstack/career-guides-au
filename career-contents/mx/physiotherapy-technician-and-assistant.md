# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 3255 (ISCO08)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants support licensed physiotherapists in delivering rehabilitative care to patients with physical impairments, injuries, or chronic conditions, by implementing exercise programs, applying manual therapies, and operating therapeutic equipment within clinical, hospital, or home health settings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as an assistant or entry-level technician, advancing to senior technician roles or supervisory positions within rehabilitation departments. With additional education (e.g., a bachelor's in physiotherapy), technicians can become licensed physiotherapists. Specialization in areas such as pediatrics, neurology, or orthopedics can further enhance career prospects and earning potential.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a physiotherapy technician in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer, but entry-level technicians typically earn between MXN 120,000 and MXN 200,000 annually, with experienced technicians earning up to MXN 300,000. Public sector roles may offer lower pay but better benefits.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a physiotherapy technician in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but a Mexican employer must sponsor a Temporary Resident Visa with work permit. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable Mexican candidate is available. Additionally, foreign credentials must be validated by the SEP, and Spanish proficiency is essential.

**Q: What educational path is needed to become a physiotherapy technician in Mexico?**
A: The standard pathway is a 2- to 3-year technical degree (Técnico Superior Universitario) in physiotherapy from a SEP-recognized institution. Many technicians later pursue a bachelor's degree to become licensed physiotherapists.
