# Cabinet-maker — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7522 (ISCO08)**

Cabinet-makers and related workers craft, install, and repair wooden cabinets, furniture, and fittings using hand tools and woodworking machinery. They work in workshops or on construction sites, interpreting designs and selecting appropriate materials. The role requires precision, creativity, and knowledge of wood properties.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to journeyman, then senior cabinet-maker or workshop supervisor. Experienced workers may open their own shops or specialize in restoration, custom designs, or kitchen remodeling. Continuing education in new materials and techniques is beneficial.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom furniture
- Kitchen remodeling
- Sustainable materials
- Restoration

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cabinet-maker in Mexico?**
A: Annual pre-tax salaries for experienced cabinet-makers range from MXN 200,000 to MXN 300,000, with variation by region and specialization. Self-employed artisans may earn more or less.

**Q: Can a foreign cabinet-maker work in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but requires a job offer from a Mexican employer. The employer sponsors a Temporary Resident Visa with Work Permit. Permanent residency through points is possible but competitive.

**Q: What skills are most important for a cabinet-maker?**
A: Precision in measuring and cutting, knowledge of wood types and joinery, ability to read blueprints, and creativity for custom designs. Physical stamina and attention to detail are essential.
