# Technical and Medical Sales Professional — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2433 (ISCO08)**

Technical and Medical Sales Professionals promote and sell complex technical or medical products and services (excluding ICT) to businesses, hospitals, and clinics. They require deep knowledge of their product line and industry, often interpreting customer needs and providing post-sale support.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level professionals typically earn competitive base salaries plus commissions, while experienced senior sales representatives can earn well above the national average. Commissions and bonuses often constitute a significant portion of total compensation.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign professionals in this field?**
A: The primary route is the Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit, sponsored by a Mexican employer. Eligible candidates can also apply for a Permanent Resident Visa via a points system if they meet criteria such as age, education, and Spanish proficiency. Employer sponsorship is the most common pathway for skilled sales professionals.

**Q: What is the typical career progression for a Technical or Medical Sales Professional?**
A: Many start as sales representatives, then advance to senior account manager, key account manager, or sales manager. Specialization in a product line (e.g., medical devices, industrial machinery) or moving into marketing, product management, or business development are common paths. Continuous learning of new technologies and sales methodologies is essential.
