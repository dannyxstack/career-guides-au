# Securities and Finance Dealer and Broker — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 3311 (ISCO08)**

Securities and finance dealers and brokers in Mexico execute buy and sell orders for financial instruments, manage client portfolios, and provide investment advice within the country's regulated markets, operating primarily through brokerage firms, banks, and independent advisory firms. They analyze market trends, assess risk, and ensure compliance with regulations set by the National Banking and Securities Commission (CNBV) and the Mexican Stock Exchange (BMV).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a financial analyst or junior broker, advancing to senior broker, portfolio manager, or relationship manager. With experience and certifications (e.g., AMIB or CFA), professionals can move into executive roles such as director of investments or head of trading. The path is performance-driven, with top performers often transitioning to independent advisory or private wealth management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically execute stock, bond, and commodity orders through algorithms, no manual order placement

**Where AI augments the role:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a securities broker in Mexico?**
A: Income is highly variable and performance-driven. Many brokers earn a base salary plus commissions and bonuses. Top performers at major firms can achieve incomes well into six figures (MXN). Experience, client base, and market conditions greatly affect earnings.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign securities brokers?**
A: Foreign brokers typically obtain a Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit (TR-T) via employer sponsorship. The employer must demonstrate that the role cannot be filled by a Mexican candidate. After four years of temporary residency, one may apply for permanent residency.

**Q: Is the AMIB certification mandatory for all brokers?**
A: Yes, the AMIB Investment Advisor Certification is mandatory to legally operate as a securities broker in Mexico. It requires passing an exam and completing continuing education. The CFA is optional but highly recommended for career advancement.
