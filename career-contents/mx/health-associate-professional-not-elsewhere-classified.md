# Health Associate Professional — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 3259 (ISCO08)**

Health associate professionals not elsewhere classified include a diverse group of allied health workers such as dental hygienists, community health workers, nutritionists, and health educators. They support physicians and nurses in clinical settings or work in public health, prevention, and health promotion. In Mexico, these professionals are typically employed in public hospitals, clinics, private practices, and non-governmental organizations. They require specialized training often at the technical or university level, and their roles are critical for expanding access to primary care and community health services.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a health associate professional in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by role and sector. Entry-level technical roles in public institutions earn around 150,000 MXN annually; experienced professionals in private practice can earn 300,000 MXN or more. However, official data is not provided here.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a health associate professional in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it is employer-sponsored. You need a job offer and the employer must obtain a favorable opinion from the National Employment Service. The process is restrictive, and most roles are not on the shortage list. Permanent residency via points is unlikely for this occupation.

**Q: Do I need a Mexican degree to work in this field?**
A: If you have a foreign degree, you can apply for revalidation (revalidación) at SEP. It can be lengthy. Many employers prefer local degrees or professional licenses. Having a Cédula Profesional is mandatory for many roles.
