# Cleaner and Helper in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 9112 (ISCO08)**

Cleaners and helpers perform routine cleaning tasks in various establishments such as offices, hotels, restaurants, and other commercial or residential buildings. Their duties include sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, trash removal, and maintaining sanitation standards. This role is essential for hygiene and comfort in public and private spaces. Typically, no formal education is required, and on-the-job training is provided.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is minimal; cleaners often remain in entry-level roles. Some may become supervisors or start their own cleaning businesses with experience. The work is physically demanding, and wages are low. There is little formal training or certification required, making it accessible but with low mobility. Opportunities for skill development are scarce, and many workers stay in the same position long-term.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean floors using smart vacuum and mop robots
- Operation monitoring of automatic window cleaning robots
- Dust removal in standard areas such as corridors and halls following preset routes.
- Use automatic disinfection robots to disinfect bathrooms and kitchens.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize multi-site cleaning routes and schedules
- Use smart sensors to monitor dirt levels and precisely schedule cleaning frequency
- Receive health and safety alerts via wearable devices to enhance personal protection
- Adjusts service details using a customer review AI system to improve satisfaction
- Use digital tools to manage cleaning supplies, reducing waste

**Human moat:**

- Identification and targeted treatment of complex stubborn stains (e.g., carpet stains, oil stains).
- Communicate with clients to understand specific needs and flexibly adjust cleaning methods
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., breaking items, pet accidents)
- Fine cleaning and care of valuable items (e.g., antiques, silk)

**Skills to build:**

- Use cleaning management software for scheduling and customer relationship management
- Learn specialized cleaning techniques (e.g., stone care, disinfection)
- Improve communication and customer service skills

- Learn about personal protective equipment and eco-friendly cleaning agents

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cleaner in Mexico?**
A: Cleaners earn at or near the minimum wage, which is approximately 172 pesos per day (2024). Monthly income ranges from 5,000 to 6,000 MXN before tax, depending on hours and employer.

**Q: Can a foreign cleaner get a work visa in Mexico?**
A: It is very difficult. Mexico's visa system favors skilled workers. Cleaners typically do not meet salary thresholds or skill requirements for work visas. Most work informally or have legal residence through family ties.

**Q: Is there room for advancement as a cleaner?**
A: Limited. With experience, one may become a supervisor or start an independent cleaning business. But formal career paths are rare, and most positions offer little upward mobility.
