# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers assist with basic food preparation, cleaning, and maintaining hygiene in commercial kitchens. They support cooks and chefs by washing dishes, chopping ingredients, and keeping workspaces orderly. This entry-level role is prevalent in Mexico's restaurants, hotels, and institutional dining facilities, offering a first step into the hospitality industry.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 611,895. Employment for kitchen helpers in Mexico remains stable driven by tourism and food service expansion. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Progression is limited; some workers become line cooks or chefs with experience and training. Advancement often requires formal culinary education. Many kitchen helpers remain in the role long-term due to low formal education requirements.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Mexico?**
A: Salaries are at or just above the minimum wage, typically around 6,000 to 8,000 MXN per month pre-tax, varying by state and employer.

**Q: Can I migrate to Mexico as a kitchen helper?**
A: It is very difficult. The occupation is not on shortage lists and local workers are abundant. Employer sponsorship is possible but rarely approved by INM.

**Q: Do I need prior experience to work as a kitchen helper?**
A: No, most employers provide on-the-job training. However, a food handling certificate is often required for formal positions.
