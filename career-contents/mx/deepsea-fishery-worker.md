# Deep-Sea Fishery Worker — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 6223 (ISCO08)**

Deep-sea fishery workers capture fish and other marine life from ocean waters using commercial fishing vessels, operating in Mexico's Pacific and Gulf waters. Tasks include setting and hauling nets, sorting and preserving catch, maintaining gear, and ensuring vessel safety. The work is physically demanding, seasonal, and often performed in remote conditions, requiring teamwork and endurance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a deckhand or general crew member. With experience, one can become a boat captain, fishing master, or supervisor on larger vessels. Some workers transition into processing, boat maintenance, or start their own small-scale operations. Formal maritime certifications can improve advancement prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Fishing vessel navigation and route planning (AIS + autopilot)
- Fish catch classification and initial weighing (computer vision)
- Fish school detection and net deployment position optimization (AI sonar analysis)
- Predictive maintenance for fishing vessel equipment (IoT + machine learning)
- Catch tracking and compliance report generation (automated data entry)

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: How does income work for deep-sea fishers in Mexico?**
A: Income is typically based on a share of the catch value rather than a fixed salary, so it fluctuates with season, catch volume, and market prices. Beginners may earn less, while experienced crew on large vessels can earn more during peak seasons.

**Q: Can foreigners work as deep-sea fishery workers in Mexico?**
A: It is possible but uncommon. There is no dedicated points-based route, so the main option is an employer-sponsored temporary resident visa with work permit, which requires proving no suitable local candidate. This is difficult due to the availability of local labor.

**Q: What career progression is typical for deep-sea fishery workers?**
A: Advancement usually goes from deckhand to boat captain or fishing master on larger vessels. Some transition into aquaculture, processing, or boat maintenance. Obtaining STCW certifications and a captain's license can accelerate progression.
