# Other Cleaning Workers — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 9129 (ISCO08)**

Other cleaning workers perform a variety of cleaning tasks in settings such as offices, hotels, hospitals, and private homes. They may operate floor-cleaning machinery, dispose of waste, and ensure hygiene standards are met. This occupation is characterized by low entry barriers and high demand in urban areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression is limited without additional training. Workers may move into supervisory roles (e.g., head of cleaning) or specialize in industrial cleaning or hazardous waste management. Some transition to self-employment, offering residential or commercial cleaning services. Formal education is rarely required, but certifications can improve prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic floor scrubbers or robot vacuums for floor cleaning
- Automatic vacuuming and mopping along preset routes
- Timely automatic spraying of disinfectant for environmental disinfection.
- Use AI trash bins to automatically sort and compact waste
- Monitor cleanliness via sensors and generate cleaning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and shifts.
- Using computer vision to detect stains and guide targeted cleaning
- Use voice assistants or apps to log cleaning tasks and feedback
- Use data analysis to predict peak hours and prepare in advance
- Operate semi-automatic cleaning equipment for high-altitude or corner cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Experience in cleaning complex stains and special material surfaces.
- Communicating with clients to confirm cleaning requirements and flexibly adjusting
- Expertise in non-toxic, environmentally friendly cleaning agents
- Identify safety hazards on site and take emergency measures on the spot
- Manual cleaning in narrow, irregular spaces is irreplaceable.

**Skills to build:**

- Learn to operate cleaning robots or smart cleaning equipment
- Basic computer operation and simple programming (e.g., setting cleaning routes)
- Understanding the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the status of cleaning equipment
- Knowledge of Disinfection and Chemical Safety in Learning Environments
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn data analysis fundamentals to optimize cleaning efficiency

**Key growth areas:**

- cleaning
- hygiene
- maintenance
- janitorial

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for other cleaning workers in Mexico?**
A: Salaries typically range from 60,000 MXN to 120,000 MXN annually, depending on location and employer. Higher pay is possible in specialized cleaning or industrial settings.

**Q: Can I get a work visa for Mexico as a cleaner?**
A: Visa approval for low-skilled cleaning positions is extremely unlikely. The Temporary Resident Visa requires employer sponsorship and proof that the job cannot be filled by a local worker. Most cleaning jobs are filled domestically.

**Q: Can cleaning workers advance to better jobs?**
A: With experience and certifications, workers can become supervisors or start their own cleaning business. Some move into related fields like facilities management or environmental services.
