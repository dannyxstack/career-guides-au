# Building Structure Cleaner — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7133 (ISCO08)**

Building structure cleaners are responsible for cleaning the exterior surfaces of buildings, including windows, facades, roofs, and signage, using ladders, scaffolding, or rope access techniques. In Mexico, this occupation is concentrated in urban centers with high-rise construction and tourism infrastructure, requiring physical stamina and comfort with heights. Workers typically learn on the job, with minimal formal education required.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience but generally range from 1 to 1.5 times the minimum wage (approx. 8,000–12,000 MXN per month) for entry-level positions. Supervisors or those with specialized certifications can earn more.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a building structure cleaner in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it is uncommon. The employer must sponsor a Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit, proving the job cannot be filled locally. Due to low skill requirements, immigration authorities often view such applications skeptically.
