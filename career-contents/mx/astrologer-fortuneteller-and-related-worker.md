# Astrologer, Fortune-Teller and Related Worker — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 5161 (ISCO08)**

Astrologers, fortune-tellers and related workers provide esoteric and spiritual guidance by interpreting celestial bodies, tarot cards, palm lines, or other divination methods. In Mexico, this occupation operates informally, often as a self-employed or small-business activity, catering to clients seeking personal advice, entertainment, or cultural traditions. The work is unregulated, with no formal licensing requirements, and income varies widely. While not a mainstream career, it persists due to cultural interest in mysticism and alternative spirituality.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for an astrologer in Mexico?**
A: Income varies widely; most earn per session (MXN 200-1,000) and work part-time. Annual earnings often range MXN 30,000-120,000, depending on reputation and client base.

**Q: Can I migrate to Mexico as an astrologer?**
A: Not directly; this occupation does not qualify for a work visa unless sponsored by a recognized employer (rare). Most practitioners work informally or on a tourist visa, which is illegal for paid work. Consider Permanent Resident via family ties or economic means.

**Q: Do I need formal education to become an astrologer in Mexico?**
A: No, but many take private courses or certifications to build credibility. There are no government-recognized qualifications.
