## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialty, experience, and sector. Entry-level professionals can expect MXN 250,000-400,000 annually, while experienced specialists may earn MXN 500,000-900,000. Private practice can yield higher but with variable income.

**Q: Can a foreign health professional obtain a work visa for Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer from a Mexican employer, validation of foreign degrees by SEP via CENEVAL, and approval from the Health Ministry. The Temporary Resident Visa with work permit is the most common route.

**Q: What education is required to practice as a health professional in Mexico?**
A: At minimum, a bachelor's degree (licenciatura) in the relevant field from an accredited institution, followed by obtaining a professional license (cédula) from SEP. Many specialties require additional postgraduate training.
