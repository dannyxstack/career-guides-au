# Managing Director and Chief Executive — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 1120 (ISCO08)**

Managing Directors and Chief Executives in Mexico lead organizations by setting strategic direction, overseeing operations, and ensuring financial performance. They work across all sectors, from multinational corporations to government entities, and are responsible for high-level decision-making and stakeholder management. This role demands extensive experience, strong leadership, and a deep understanding of the business environment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves advancing from senior management roles such as Director or Vice President to C-suite positions. Many executives hold advanced degrees (e.g., MBA) and have cross-functional experience in finance, operations, or marketing. Board memberships often follow retirement, and some move into consulting or entrepreneurship. Networking and visibility within industry circles are crucial.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What salary range can a Managing Director expect in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary widely by industry, company size, and location. Typical total compensation for CEOs in large firms ranges from MXN 2,000,000 to over MXN 10,000,000 annually, including bonuses and benefits.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a Managing Director in Mexico without a visa?**
A: No, foreign executives need a valid work visa. The most common route is the Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit, sponsored by the Mexican employer. The Intra-Company Transferee visa is also an option if moving from a related foreign entity.

**Q: What is the typical career path to become a Managing Director in Mexico?**
A: Most start in lower management, progress to director or vice president roles, and eventually ascend to the C-suite. An MBA helps, but proven business results and leadership experience are paramount.
