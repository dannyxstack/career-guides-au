# Armed Forces Other Rank — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

Armed Forces Other Ranks form the backbone of Mexico's military, serving in the Army, Navy, or Air Force across all branches. They undergo rigorous basic training and may specialise in combat, logistics, engineering, or administration. Employment is exclusively through enlistment; foreigners are generally barred. The role demands discipline, physical fitness, and loyalty. Service conditions follow military regulations, with stable pay and benefits, but limited personal freedoms. Career progression depends on merit, time in service, and examinations, with opportunities for non-commissioned officer ranks.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Armed Forces Other Rank in Mexico?**
A: Salaries for enlisted personnel start around 8,000–10,000 MXN per month for privates, rising to 15,000–20,000 MXN for senior sergeants. Benefits include housing, food, and healthcare. Annual salaries range from approximately 96,000–240,000 MXN pre-tax, varying by rank and seniority.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an Armed Forces Other Rank in Mexico?**
A: No. Mexican law restricts military service to citizens by birth (Article 32 of the Constitution). Naturalized citizens may serve only in peacetime and after a long residency. There is no visa for foreign recruitment.

**Q: What is the promotion timeline for Other Ranks?**
A: Promotion from private to corporal typically takes 2–4 years, to sergeant 5–10 years, and to senior sergeant 10–15 years. Performance, exams, and vacancies determine pace.
