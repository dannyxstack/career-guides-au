# Poultry Producer — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 6122 (ISCO08)**

Poultry producers manage the breeding, raising, and harvesting of chickens, turkeys, and other domestic fowl for meat or egg production. They oversee feeding, health care, biosecurity, and facility maintenance, often working on large-scale farms or small family operations. In Mexico, poultry farming is a significant agricultural sector, providing affordable protein and supporting rural livelihoods.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level workers start as poultry laborers or assistants, learning on the job. With experience, they can become supervisors, farm managers, or independent producers. Specialization in organic or free-range poultry, hatchery management, or AI-assisted monitoring offers career advancement. Formal training in veterinary science or agribusiness can lead to roles in agritech or quality control.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Environmental parameter monitoring and recording (temperature, humidity, light)
- Basic feed delivery and water supply management
- Mechanized operation of poultry house cleaning and waste disposal
- Preliminary statistics and report generation for production data
- Collection of vital signs data in routine health checks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize feed ratios and feeding times through AI analysis
- Early detection of abnormal behavior in sick poultry using computer vision
- Predicting egg production/slaughter volume based on production data to plan sales
- Efficiency improved in inspecting large poultry houses with drones or ground robots
- AI-assisted epidemic early warning and biosecurity decision support

**Human moat:**

- Acute observation and immediate handling of abnormal behavior in individual poultry.
- veterinary-level disease diagnosis and medication decisions
- Emergency management for sudden weather or equipment failure
- Animal welfare operations in special production systems like organic and free-range
- Regulatory compliance responsibilities related to government quarantine and food certification

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and basic troubleshooting of agricultural IoT devices
- Data analysis and visualization tools (e.g., Excel, Tableau)
- Understanding the application of computer vision systems in farming
- Deepened knowledge of animal behavior and biosecurity
- IT fundamentals and automated systems management skills
- Food safety regulations and sustainable farming practices

**Key growth areas:**

- organic poultry
- biosecurity protocols
- automated feeding systems
- free-range production

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for poultry producers in Mexico?**
A: Salaries for poultry producers vary by experience and farm size. Entry-level workers earn around MXN 5,000–8,000 per month, while experienced managers can earn MXN 15,000–25,000 per month. Official statistics should be consulted for precise figures.

**Q: Can foreigners work as poultry producers in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. Employers must sponsor a Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit, demonstrating that the role cannot be filled by a Mexican worker. The process involves submitting a job offer to the National Immigration Institute (INM) and meeting labor market tests. Most poultry producer positions are filled locally.

**Q: Is poultry production a growing field in Mexico?**
A: Yes, Mexico is one of the top poultry producers globally, with steady demand for chicken and eggs. The industry is modernizing, creating demand for skilled producers who understand biosecurity and automation. However, rural labor shortages may persist.
