# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications Engineering Technicians install, maintain, and repair equipment for voice, data, and video communication systems. They work with fiber optics, wireless networks, and satellite systems for telecom providers, internet service companies, and corporate IT departments. In Mexico, they support the expansion of 4G/5G networks, broadband infrastructure, and IoT connectivity projects.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Mexico?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around MXN 180,000 annually, while experienced technicians can earn up to MXN 360,000. Benefits and bonuses often add 10-20%. For precise figures, consult official INEGI/ENOE statistics.

**Q: Can a foreign Telecommunications Engineering Technician work in Mexico without a job offer?**
A: Generally no. Mexico does not have a points-based skilled migration route for this role. Most work authorizations require an employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit. The employer must file the application with INM and demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: Is a university degree mandatory to become a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Mexico?**
A: Not strictly mandatory, but highly recommended. Many technicians hold a Técnico Superior Universitario (2-year degree) or a Bachelor's in Telecommunications Engineering. Certification and experience can compensate, but a formal degree is often required for visa sponsorship and higher-level positions.
