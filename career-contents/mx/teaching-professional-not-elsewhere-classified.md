# Teaching Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

Teaching professionals not elsewhere classified include specialized instructors in fields such as languages, special education, vocational training, and adult education. They work in public and private schools, language institutes, and training centers. In Mexico, these professionals typically hold a bachelor's degree in education or a related field and require a teaching license (Cédula Profesional) for regulated positions. The role demands strong communication skills and adaptability to diverse student needs.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for teaching professionals in Mexico?**
A: Annual salaries for teachers in Mexico vary widely: public school teachers earn between 150,000 and 300,000 MXN, while private school or specialized teachers may earn 250,000-500,000 MXN. Language teachers often earn 200,000-400,000 MXN.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a teacher in Mexico?**
A: Yes, you can apply for a Temporary Resident Visa with work permit if you have a job offer from a Mexican school or institution. The employer must prove that no local candidate is available. Permanent residence is possible after 4 years of temporary residency.

**Q: Do I need a teaching license to work in Mexico?**
A: For regulated public school positions, a Cédula Profesional (teaching license) is mandatory. Private schools may not require it but often prefer certified teachers. English teaching may only require a bachelor's degree and language proficiency.
