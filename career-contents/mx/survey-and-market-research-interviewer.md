# Survey And Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and market research interviewers conduct interviews to collect data for statistical surveys, opinion polls, or market research. They work in various settings, including telephone call centers, shopping malls, or door-to-door, and may ask questions from a script or use electronic devices. In Mexico, this occupation is common in both public and private sectors, with employment opportunities at research firms, government agencies, and consulting companies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for survey interviewers is limited; advancement often leads to supervisory or team leader roles, or transitioning to data analysis/market research positions. Some interviewers move into sales or customer service. Continuous learning in data collection methods and technology use can improve prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Market Research
- Telephone Interviewing
- Face-to-Face Surveys
- Data Collection

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a survey and market research interviewer in Mexico?**
A: Income varies significantly by employer and hours worked. Most interviewers earn around the minimum wage or slightly above, with average monthly earnings between 4,000 and 8,000 MXN before tax. Full-time positions may offer additional benefits.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a survey interviewer in Mexico?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. Employers must sponsor a Temporary Resident Visa with work permit, proving that no Mexican worker is available for the role. Given the low skill level, such sponsorship is rare and often denied. Most successful applications are for specialized research roles within international firms.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for survey interviewers?**
A: Career progression is limited but possible. Experienced interviewers can become team leaders, supervisors, or quality control monitors. Some transition to market research analyst roles by pursuing further education. The field offers a stepping stone into broader business or research careers.
