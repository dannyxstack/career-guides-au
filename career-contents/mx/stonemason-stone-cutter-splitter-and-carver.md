# Stonemason — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 7113 (ISCO08)**

Stonemasons, stone cutters, splitters, and carvers shape and install natural or artificial stone for construction, restoration, and decorative purposes. They work with granite, marble, limestone, and other materials, using hand tools and power tools to cut, carve, and set stone in buildings, monuments, and landscapes. This trade combines traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques, often requiring precision and physical stamina.

Specialization in carving, restoration, or marble/granite work can lead to higher pay and supervisory roles. Experienced stonemasons may become independent contractors or start their own businesses. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can a foreign stonemason get a work visa for Mexico?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored Temporary Resident Visa with a work permit. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Mexican worker. This route is more feasible for specialized skills (e.g., restoration of historic sites) but is generally restricted compared to higher-skilled professions.

**Q: What is the career progression for a stonemason in Mexico?**
A: Most start as apprentices or laborers, advancing to journeyman after 2-4 years. With experience, one can specialize in carving or restoration, become a foreman, or start an independent business. Certification through CONOCER can improve job opportunities and wages.

**Q: How much does a stonemason earn in Mexico?**
A: According to official sources, average salaries are not specified here, but entry-level workers typically earn around MXN 120,000-180,000 annually, while experienced stonemasons can earn up to MXN 300,000 or more, especially in high-end restoration or commercial projects.
