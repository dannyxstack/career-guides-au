# Sales Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Mexico

**Occupation code: 5249 (ISCO08)**

Sales Workers Not Elsewhere Classified encompass a variety of retail and direct sales roles, including counter attendants, street vendors, and telemarketers, who sell goods or services to customers. In Mexico, these positions are common in the informal economy and small businesses, requiring strong interpersonal skills and resilience to irregular income and working hours.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sales workers in Mexico?**
A: Sales workers in this category typically earn between 6,000 and 12,000 MXN monthly, often with commissions. Annual income is around 72,000 to 144,000 MXN pre-tax, depending on hours and performance.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for a sales job in Mexico?**
A: Generally not, as most sales jobs are considered low-skilled and employers must prove no local candidate is available. Temporary resident visas for work are rarely approved for these roles unless the position involves specialized sales (e.g., high-tech equipment).

**Q: How can a sales worker advance their career?**
A: Advancement often requires additional training in sales techniques or languages, moving into supervisory roles, or transitioning to specialized sales (e.g., insurance, real estate) with licensing. Some pursue higher education to become marketing or business managers.
