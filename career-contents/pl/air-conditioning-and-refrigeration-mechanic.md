## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Poland?**
A: In Poland, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics typically earn between 4,000 and 6,500 PLN gross per month. Experienced professionals or those in senior roles may earn up to 8,000 PLN. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in major cities like Warsaw, Kraków, and Wrocław, and can also depend on certifications and specialization.

**Q: Can foreign air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics work in Poland via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Yes, foreign mechanics can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer and meet salary thresholds (currently around 1.5 times the average Polish salary). Alternatively, a national work visa (type D) is available for non-EU workers. Both require employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications. Self-employment is not eligible for the Blue Card.

**Q: What are the career prospects for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Poland?**
A: Demand for skilled mechanics is growing due to increasing construction and stricter EU environmental regulations on refrigeration systems. Opportunities exist in installation, maintenance, and repair for commercial and residential clients. Advancing to supervisory roles or specializing in industrial systems can lead to higher earnings. Additional certifications in green technologies may improve prospects.
