## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shopkeeper in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for shopkeepers in Poland ranges from PLN 4,000 to 5,500 (€900–€1,200), depending on experience, location, and shop size. Self-employed shopkeepers may earn more. The national minimum wage in 2024 is PLN 4,242 gross.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a shopkeeper in Poland under the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Poland is for highly-skilled workers (university degree or 5 years experience) with a job offering at least 150% of average salary (approx. PLN 12,000 gross in 2024). Shopkeeper roles rarely meet this. Alternative legal routes include a type A work permit or registering a one-person business.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a shopkeeper in Poland?**
A: No formal qualifications are required, but a high school diploma (preferably with vocational training in trade) is beneficial. Employers often value prior experience as a sales assistant. Fluency in Polish is essential. For independent shopkeepers, entrepreneurial skills and knowledge of regulations are important.
