**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using laser rangefinders and digital levels for conventional measurement and marking
- Manually calculating material lists and cutting lists based on BIM models.
- Filling in paper work logs and progress reports
- Repetitive template setup and dismantling work (partially done by robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use BIM and AR glasses for precise positioning and installation guidance
- Structural health monitoring via drones and sensors
- Using AI-assisted design to optimize frame structure layout
- Use automated cutting and welding equipment to improve efficiency
- Coordinate workflows for different trades through digital platforms

**Human moat:**

- Flexible response and problem-solving in complex on-site environments
- Manual adjustments and customization for irregular or old structures
- Monitor automated equipment operation and handle anomalies
- Communication and safety supervision responsibilities in multi-trade coordination

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and report generation tools (e.g. Excel, Power BI)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for building frame and related trades workers in Poland?**
A: In Poland, building frame workers (ISCO 7119) typically earn a gross monthly salary between 4,500 and 6,500 PLN. Experienced workers in large cities like Warsaw can earn up to 8,000 PLN. Overtime and project-based bonuses may increase total pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for building frame workers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU workers can obtain a type A work permit sponsored by a Polish employer, followed by a temporary residence permit. The EU Blue Card is rarely available for this occupation as it requires a high-skilled role with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. 9,000 PLN gross monthly).

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for building frame workers in Poland?**
A: Building frame workers can progress to site supervisor, foreman, or construction manager roles. Further training in steel or concrete framing, or obtaining a construction license (uprawnienia budowlane), opens doors to self-employment or specialized contracting. Polish language skills boost advancement.
