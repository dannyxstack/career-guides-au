## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fitness and recreation instructors in Poland?**
A: The average monthly salary for fitness and recreation instructors in Poland ranges from 3,500 to 6,000 PLN gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Instructors in major cities like Warsaw or Kraków may earn higher salaries, while those in smaller towns earn less. Income can also be supplemented by private lessons or coaching.

**Q: Can fitness instructors from abroad work in Poland via the EU Blue Card or a work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU fitness instructors can work in Poland via a national work visa (type D) if they have a job offer from a Polish employer. The EU Blue Card is also possible but requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 150% of the average national salary (approx. 7,000 PLN gross monthly in 2024), which may be challenging for this occupation. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a fitness instructor in Poland?**
A: Formal qualifications include a certificate from a recognized fitness instructor course, such as those accredited by the Polish Fitness Association (PFA) or international bodies like REPS. Many employers also require a first aid certificate. A degree in sports science or physical education is advantageous but not mandatory. Ongoing professional development is recommended to stay competitive.
