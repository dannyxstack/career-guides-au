## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in Poland?**
A: Pay is generally at or slightly above the Polish national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is 4242 PLN gross monthly. Many employers offer around 4500-5000 PLN gross, often depending on the region and workload. Rates may be piece-based for wood collection. Overtime and seasonal variation can affect total earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for water and firewood collection in Poland?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a Type A work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The process involves a labour market test unless the occupation is on the shortage list. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this low-skilled role, as it requires a high salary and higher education qualifications. Seasonal work permits are also an option for temporary collection jobs.

**Q: What are the working conditions and career prospects for water and firewood collectors in Poland?**
A: Work is often seasonal, physically demanding, and based in rural or forested areas. Tasks include hauling buckets, chopping, and loading wood. Formal career advancement is limited, but experienced workers may become team leaders or supervisors. Most positions are informal or part-time, with limited social benefits. Safety gear and training are rarely provided.
