## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Research and Development Manager in Poland?**
A: Salaries for R&D managers in Poland vary by experience, industry, and company size. Typically, monthly gross pay ranges from 15,000 to 25,000 PLN. Senior roles in global corporations or advanced sectors like biotech or IT may exceed 30,000 PLN. Bonuses, stock options, and benefits are common.

**Q: What work visa options are available for Research and Development Managers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (set at 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Poland). Alternatively, a national work visa (type D) based on a work permit is possible. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the voivodeship office.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a Research and Development Manager in Poland?**
A: A master's or PhD in a relevant technical or scientific field is typically required, along with 5-10 years of R&D experience. Strong project management, leadership, and cross-functional communication skills are essential. Experience in international collaborations, grant writing, and intellectual property management is also valued by Polish employers.
