## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Transport Clerk in Poland?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a Transport Clerk in Poland ranges from 4,000 to 6,500 PLN, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 4,000 PLN, while senior clerks can earn up to 7,000 PLN. Some employers offer additional bonuses or overtime pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a Transport Clerk in Poland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card to work as a Transport Clerk in Poland. Requirements include a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the Polish average (approx. 96,000 PLN in 2024). A standard Type A work permit is another option for specific employers. Both require a valid employment contract and approval from the voivodeship office.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Transport Clerk in Poland?**
A: Transport Clerks in Poland coordinate schedules, prepare shipping documents (e.g., CMR consignment notes), track shipments, and communicate with drivers and clients. They ensure compliance with local and EU transport regulations, resolve operational issues, and maintain records. Strong organizational skills and knowledge of Polish logistics are essential.
