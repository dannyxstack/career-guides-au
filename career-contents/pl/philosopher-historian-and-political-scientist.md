## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary by sector. In academia, assistant professors earn around 5,000 PLN gross monthly, while experienced professionals in think tanks or research institutes can earn 8,000–12,000 PLN. Private sector roles in consulting or analysis may offer higher pay. Senior professors or heads of research units can exceed 15,000 PLN.

**Q: Can foreign philosophers, historians, and political scientists work in Poland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if offered a job requiring higher education and earning at least 150% of the average gross wage (approx. 9,000 PLN gross in 2024). Alternatively, a standard national work visa (type D) is possible for employment. A work permit is required, typically arranged by the employer.

**Q: What are the main employers for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Poland?**
A: Primary employers include universities, research institutes (e.g., Polish Academy of Sciences), think tanks, public administration, NGOs, media, and private sector companies needing policy analysis, market research, or consulting. Many also work as independent experts or in cultural institutions.
