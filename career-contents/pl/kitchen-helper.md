## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Poland?**
A: The median gross monthly salary for a kitchen helper in Poland is approximately 3,200–3,800 PLN (2024 data). Hourly rates range from 17–22 PLN. Pay varies by region, with Warsaw and Kraków offering higher wages, and by employer type (e.g., fast-food vs full-service restaurants). Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: What work visa options are available for kitchen helpers in Poland?**
A: Kitchen helpers typically qualify for a national work visa (type D) based on a work permit (type A). The employer must prove no suitable Polish or EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is not possible for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree or highly skilled position. Seasonal work (up to 9 months) is another route for non-EU nationals.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for kitchen helpers in Poland?**
A: With experience, kitchen helpers can advance to line cook, commis chef, or kitchen supervisor. Many complete formal culinary training (e.g., 2-year vocational programs) to qualify for higher roles. The Polish hospitality sector faces labour shortages, so motivated workers can move up within a few years. Knowledge of Polish and English improves prospects.
