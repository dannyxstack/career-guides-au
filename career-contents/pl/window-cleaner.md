## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for window cleaners in Poland?**
A: Window cleaners in Poland typically earn between 3,500 and 5,500 PLN gross per month, depending on experience and location. Warsaw offers higher rates. Hourly wages range from 20 to 30 PLN. Self-employed cleaners may charge higher rates but must cover their own taxes and insurance.

**Q: Can non-EU window cleaners work in Poland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Window cleaning jobs generally do not meet the EU Blue Card requirements for highly qualified workers, as the job usually requires only vocational training. Non-EU workers can obtain a work permit (type A or B) if sponsored by an employer. The employer must demonstrate that no Polish or EU worker is available.

**Q: What are the career prospects for window cleaners in Poland?**
A: Career progression may involve specializing in high-rise cleaning, starting your own cleaning company, or moving into related fields like building maintenance. Certifications for work at heights or using industrial machinery can enhance opportunities. Demand is steady, especially in cities with many commercial buildings.
