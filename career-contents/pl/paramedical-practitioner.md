## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a paramedical practitioner (ratownik medyczny) in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a paramedic in Poland ranges from 4,500 to 6,500 PLN for entry-level positions, rising to 7,000–9,000 PLN with experience. Public sector pay is lower than private. Salaries vary by region, with Warsaw offering higher pay. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: What work visa options exist for paramedical practitioners to work in Poland?**
A: Paramedical practitioners can work in Poland via a national work visa (type D) or an EU Blue Card if they meet the higher salary threshold. The employer must obtain a work permit from the local voivodeship office. Recognition of professional qualifications is required through the relevant Polish chamber or ministry. No specific paramedic shortage list exists.

**Q: What are the educational requirements to become a paramedic in Poland?**
A: To work as a ratownik medyczny in Poland, you must complete a three-year bachelor's degree (licencjat) in emergency medical services (ratownictwo medyczne) at a recognized university. After graduation, registration with the National Chamber of Paramedics is mandatory. Continuing education is required to maintain licensure.
