## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hotel managers in Poland?**
A: The average monthly salary for hotel managers in Poland ranges from 7,000 to 15,000 PLN gross, depending on hotel size, location, and experience. Major cities like Warsaw, Kraków, and Gdańsk offer higher pay. Senior managers at luxury hotels can earn over 20,000 PLN gross per month. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for hotel managers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU hotel managers can work in Poland via a work permit (Type A) or the EU Blue Card. Requirements include a valid job offer, relevant qualifications (e.g. degree in hospitality), and meeting salary thresholds. The Blue Card requires a contract for at least one year and gross salary at least 1.5 times the average national salary. Employer must obtain a local labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a hotel manager in Poland?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in hospitality management, tourism, or a related field is preferred. Several years of experience in hotel operations, such as front desk, housekeeping, or food & beverage, are essential. Fluency in Polish and English, strong leadership skills, and knowledge of Polish labor law and hospitality standards are also key. Professional certifications may be an advantage.
