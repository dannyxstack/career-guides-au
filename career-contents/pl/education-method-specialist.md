## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Education Methods specialists in Poland?**
A: Salaries for Education Methods specialists in Poland vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions earn around 4,000–6,000 PLN gross per month, while senior specialists can earn 8,000–12,000 PLN gross. Public sector roles may have lower pay but offer stability, whereas private consultancies and international schools often pay higher. Bonuses are uncommon.

**Q: What work visa options are available for Education Methods specialists moving to Poland?**
A: Foreign specialists can apply for a national work visa (type D) or the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting salary thresholds (e.g., at least 150% of average gross wage for Blue Card). A work permit must be obtained by the employer. The process requires approval from the voivode, typically taking 1–3 months. EU citizens do not need a visa.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Education Methods specialist in Poland?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in education, pedagogy, or a related field is required. Additional certifications in curriculum design or instructional technology are advantageous. Experience in teaching or training is often expected. For public schools, recognition of foreign diplomas by the Ministry of Education may be necessary. Polish language proficiency (B2 or higher) is usually required.
