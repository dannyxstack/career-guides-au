## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for police inspectors and detectives in Poland?**
A: Salaries for police inspectors and detectives in Poland vary by rank and experience. A detective (aspirant) earns approximately PLN 6,000–9,000 gross monthly, while higher ranks like commissioner earn PLN 9,000–14,000. Additional allowances for special duties or tenure apply. These figures are before tax and social contributions.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as police inspectors or detectives in Poland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, police and detective positions in Poland are reserved for Polish citizens or citizens of other EU/EEA states who meet specific language and legal requirements. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for law enforcement roles due to national security restrictions. Non-EU citizens would need to pursue naturalization or other paths.

**Q: What are the typical career progression steps for police inspectors in Poland?**
A: Progression typically starts with officer training at the Police Academy, then service as a patrol officer. Promotion to detective requires at least 3 years of service and passing exams. Further promotions include senior detective, inspector, and chief inspector, based on experience, performance, and competitive examinations.
