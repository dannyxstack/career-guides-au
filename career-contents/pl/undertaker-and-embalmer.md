## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for undertakers and embalmers in Poland?**
A: The average monthly salary for undertakers and embalmers in Poland ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 PLN gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 PLN, while experienced professionals in larger cities like Warsaw or Kraków may earn up to 7,000 PLN. Overtime and on-call duties can increase total earnings.

**Q: Can undertakers and embalmers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Poland?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Poland is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer paying at least 150% of the average national salary. Undertakers and embalmers typically do not meet the salary threshold or the educational requirements, as the occupation is not classified as highly skilled. Non-EU workers usually apply for a national work permit (type A) instead.

**Q: What are the licensing and training requirements to work as an undertaker in Poland?**
A: To work as an undertaker in Poland, you need to complete a vocational course in funeral services or have relevant professional experience. A license from the local mayor (starosta) is required to operate a funeral home. Embalming requires additional certification from the Polish Funeral Industry Association. While no university degree is mandatory, knowledge of Polish law and hygiene standards is essential.
