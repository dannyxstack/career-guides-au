## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a registered nurse in Poland?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for nurses in Poland ranges from 4,500 to 7,000 PLN, depending on experience, specialization, and region. Public sector salaries are lower but include benefits such as pension and health insurance. Private clinics and hospitals offering English-language services may pay higher wages.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign nurses to work in Poland via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Foreign nurses can obtain a Polish national work visa (type D) or an EU Blue Card. Requirements include a confirmed job offer, recognition of nursing qualifications (nostrification), proof of Polish language proficiency at B2 level, and registration with the local Chamber of Nurses and Midwives. The Blue Card additionally requires a higher salary threshold.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for nurses in Poland?**
A: Nurses in Poland can specialize in fields such as anesthesiology, pediatrics, or intensive care. They may advance to ward manager, clinical instructor, or nurse educator roles. Pursuing a master's degree in nursing or healthcare management opens doors to higher management positions in hospitals, research, or academia.
