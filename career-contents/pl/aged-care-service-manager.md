## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an aged care services manager in Poland?**
A: Salaries for aged care services managers in Poland vary by region and facility type. The average gross monthly salary ranges from 7,000 to 12,000 PLN. Experienced managers in larger cities like Warsaw or Kraków may earn up to 15,000 PLN. Benefits often include bonuses and housing allowances.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for aged care services managers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU aged care services managers can obtain a Polish work visa (Type D) or an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, relevant qualifications, and meet salary thresholds. The Blue Card requires a minimum gross salary of 1.5 times the average national wage (approximately 9,750 PLN as of 2025). Both routes require employer sponsorship and work permit approval.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an aged care services manager in Poland?**
A: A bachelor's degree in health care management, social work, or nursing is typically required. Relevant experience in aged care management is essential. Many employers prefer candidates with postgraduate qualifications in gerontology or health administration. Fluency in Polish is mandatory, as communication with local authorities and staff is key.
