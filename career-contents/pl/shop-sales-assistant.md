## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shop sales assistant in Poland?**
A: Shop sales assistants in Poland typically earn the national minimum wage or slightly above, which in 2024 is 4,242 PLN gross per month. Some employers offer bonuses or commissions. Salaries may vary by city and type of store, with larger chains often paying more.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a shop sales assistant in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit to work as a shop sales assistant in Poland. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation as it requires higher qualifications. The standard route is a type A work permit, which is employer-sponsored and must be obtained before starting work.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shop sales assistants in Poland?**
A: Shop sales assistants in Poland can advance to senior sales roles, department manager, or store manager. Many retail chains offer internal training programs and language courses. Experience may also lead to opportunities in visual merchandising, purchasing, or customer service management.
