## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Sales Professionals in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and company size, but for experienced ICT sales professionals in Poland, monthly gross pay typically ranges from 12,000 to 25,000 PLN. Entry-level positions start around 7,000-9,000 PLN, while senior roles or those in major tech hubs like Warsaw or Krakow can exceed 30,000 PLN, often with performance bonuses.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as ICT Sales Professionals in Poland, such as the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Poland via a work visa (type D) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid work contract, higher education or 5 years of professional experience, and a salary meeting the annually updated minimum threshold (e.g., for 2024, at least 1.5 times the average national salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit may be obtained if the employer demonstrates that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ICT Sales Professionals in Poland?**
A: ICT sales professionals in Poland can advance from individual contributor roles like Account Executive to management positions such as Sales Manager or Director. Specialization in cybersecurity, cloud solutions, or SaaS can lead to higher earnings. Many also transition into pre-sales engineering or channel management. Continuous upskilling in new technologies and languages (e.g., English, German) is highly valued and accelerates career growth.
