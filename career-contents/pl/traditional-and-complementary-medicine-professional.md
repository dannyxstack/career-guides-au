## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Professionals in Poland?**
A: Salaries for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Professionals in Poland vary by specialization and experience. Entry-level practitioners may earn around 3,500–5,000 PLN gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn 6,000–9,000 PLN or more. Those in private practice or with advanced credentials may earn higher incomes depending on client base and location.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Professionals to work in Poland?**
A: Qualified professionals can obtain a national work visa (type D) or an EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a recognized diploma, a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Poland, and compliance with professional licensing. The standard work visa is for shorter-term employment but also necessitates a job offer. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Professional in Poland?**
A: Practitioners must have a recognized diploma in their field, such as acupuncture, herbal medicine, or homeopathy. Poland does not have a unified licensing system for all complementary therapies, so registration with professional associations (e.g., Polish Acupuncture Society) is often required. Legal practice may also require a business license or certification from the Ministry of Health for certain modalities.
