## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a ships' engineer in Poland?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for ships' engineers in Poland ranges from approximately 10,000 PLN to 15,000 PLN (about 2,200–3,300 EUR) depending on experience, rank, and vessel type. Senior or chief engineers can earn up to 20,000 PLN or more, especially on deep-sea vessels. Salaries at sea are often tax-advantaged under Polish maritime law.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign ships' engineers in Poland?**
A: Foreign ships' engineers can work in Poland via a work permit (type A) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer, a recognized qualification (or 5 years of professional experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times Poland's average gross wage (about 9,000 PLN monthly in 2025). Seafarers may also use a seaman's book and special procedures, but standard routes apply for shore-based roles.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a ships' engineer in Poland?**
A: A ships' engineer in Poland must hold a diploma from a maritime university (e.g., in Gdańsk or Szczecin) and valid STCW certificates. After completing sea service, engineers obtain Polish maritime administration licences (e.g., Officer of the Watch, Chief Engineer). Proficiency in English is essential. Foreign qualifications may need recognition by the Polish Ministry of Maritime Economy.
