## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for nursing associate professionals in Poland?**
A: In Poland, nursing associate professionals (e.g., licensed practical nurses) earn a gross monthly salary ranging from approximately 4,500 to 6,000 PLN, depending on experience, employer (public vs. private), and region. Public sector salaries are often lower but include benefits like annual bonuses and overtime pay.

**Q: Can nursing associate professionals obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Poland?**
A: Yes, non-EU nursing associate professionals may qualify for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized qualification and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Poland (approx. 7,500 PLN monthly as of 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit (type A) is available for shortage occupations, including nursing.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for nursing associate professionals in Poland?**
A: Nursing associate professionals in Poland can advance by completing specialization courses (e.g., in geriatrics or intensive care) or a bridging program to become a registered nurse. Career paths also include supervisory roles such as ward manager, or moving into nursing education or healthcare administration with additional qualifications.
