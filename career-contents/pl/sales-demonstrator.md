## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Poland?**
A: Salaries for Sales Demonstrators in Poland typically range from 4,000 to 7,000 PLN gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and location. Many positions also include performance-based bonuses or commissions. Entry-level roles may start near the minimum wage, while experienced demonstrators can earn higher rates, especially in major cities like Warsaw or Kraków.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a Sales Demonstrator in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work as Sales Demonstrators in Poland. The employer must obtain a work permit (type A) for the employee, who then applies for a national visa. For highly qualified positions, the EU Blue Card is an option, but Sales Demonstrator roles typically do not meet the salary threshold (currently 13,510 PLN gross/month) or require higher education, making the standard work permit route more common.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Sales Demonstrators in Poland?**
A: Sales Demonstrators in Poland can advance to roles such as Sales Manager, Brand Ambassador, or Key Account Manager. Gaining experience in product knowledge and customer engagement often leads to supervisory positions within retail companies or promotional agencies. Some demonstrators transition into permanent sales or marketing roles, especially if they develop skills in training teams or coordinating promotional events.
