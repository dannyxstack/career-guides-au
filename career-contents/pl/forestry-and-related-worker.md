## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry workers in Poland?**
A: Average gross monthly salary for forestry workers in Poland ranges from 3,500 to 5,500 PLN, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level workers earn around 3,000 PLN, while experienced workers can exceed 6,000 PLN. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may increase total earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU forestry workers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU forestry workers need a work permit (type A or seasonal type S) obtained by the employer after a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with higher education and a job offer meeting salary thresholds. Both routes require employer sponsorship and valid documentation.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a forestry worker in Poland?**
A: A vocational diploma in forestry or related field is preferred, but on-the-job training is common. Physical fitness, ability to operate machinery, and knowledge of safety procedures are essential. Certification for chainsaw use and pesticide application may be required. Some employers offer apprenticeships.
