## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level professionals earn around 4,000–5,000 PLN gross monthly, while experienced practitioners in managerial or specialized roles can earn 6,000–8,000 PLN gross. Public sector positions offer stable pay but slower growth; private practice or international organizations may pay higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for social work and counselling professionals in Poland?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Poland via a work permit (type A or B), sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a valid job offer, higher education, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (approx. 7,200 PLN monthly in 2024). Social workers may qualify if their role meets the criteria.

**Q: What career prospects exist for social work and counselling professionals in Poland?**
A: Demand is rising due to aging population, mental health awareness, and EU-funded social programs. Professionals work in public social welfare centers, NGOs, schools, healthcare, or private practice. Specializations in family therapy, addiction counselling, or crisis intervention offer advancement. Polish language proficiency is essential for most roles.
