## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for insulation workers in Poland?**
A: Salaries for insulation workers in Poland vary by experience, region, and employer. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is 4242 PLN gross per month. Experienced workers in larger cities may earn between 5000 and 7000 PLN gross. Overtime and specialist skills can increase earnings.

**Q: Can insulation workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Poland?**
A: Non-EU insulation workers typically need a work permit and national visa (type D) to work in Poland. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree, so insulation workers usually do not qualify. Employers must demonstrate that no local candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an insulation worker in Poland?**
A: Formal qualifications are not always mandatory, but vocational training or apprenticeship in construction or insulation is preferred. Certificates in specific techniques (e.g., thermal insulation) and knowledge of Polish building regulations are advantageous. A health and safety course is often required by employers.
