## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for child care workers in Poland?**
A: Salaries for child care workers in Poland typically range from the national minimum wage (4242 PLN gross monthly in 2024) to around 5000 PLN gross monthly. Actual pay depends on experience, location, and employer type (public vs. private). Wages are often higher in larger cities like Warsaw or Kraków.

**Q: What is the work visa route for foreign child care workers to work in Poland?**
A: Foreigners must obtain a work permit sponsored by a Polish employer, followed by a national visa (type D) or residence permit. The EU Blue Card is unlikely to apply, as it targets highly-skilled workers with high salary thresholds. Recognition of qualifications may be required for nurseries, but not for informal care.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a child care worker in Poland?**
A: For employment in nurseries (żłobek), a minimum of secondary education and a childcare qualification (e.g., a 2-year post-secondary program) is required. Many employers also accept candidates with pedagogical or nursing backgrounds. For babysitting or home care, formal qualifications are not legally required, but first aid training is recommended.
