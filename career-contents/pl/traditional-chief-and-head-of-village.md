## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical remuneration for a Traditional Chief or Village Head in Poland?**
A: In Poland, the role of village head (sołtys) is an elected honorary position with no fixed salary. However, a modest monthly allowance (dieta) is provided, typically ranging from 200 to 800 PLN, depending on the village’s budget and responsibilities. This is not a full-time job; most heads receive additional income from other employment.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a Traditional Chief or Village Head in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No, this role is not eligible for work visas or the EU Blue Card. Village heads (sołtys) are elected officials and must be Polish citizens or, in some cases, EU citizens with permanent residence. Non-EU nationals cannot hold this position as it involves public authority. There is no official route for foreign workers to obtain a visa for this occupation.

**Q: How does one become a Traditional Chief or Village Head in Poland, and what are the career prospects?**
A: Becoming a village head (sołtys) in Poland requires being a resident of the village, at least 18 years old, and winning a direct election by villagers. The term is typically 5 years. Career progression is limited; former heads may enter local municipal councils or pursue roles in community administration. It is a civic duty rather than a career track.
