## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a police officer in Poland?**
A: Starting salary for a police officer in Poland is around 4,500–5,500 PLN gross per month. With seniority and rank, it can reach 8,000–12,000 PLN gross. Additional allowances for overtime, night shifts, and hazardous duty are common, significantly boosting total earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as police officers in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No. Police officer roles in Poland are restricted to Polish citizens or EU/EEA nationals. Non-EU citizens cannot obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation. Only limited administrative support positions may be accessible to foreigners with a valid work permit.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for police officers in Poland?**
A: Police officers can advance through ranks from constable to chief inspector, based on experience, exams, and performance. Specializations include criminal investigation, traffic, counter-terrorism, and cybercrime. Further training at the Police Academy can lead to higher leadership positions.
