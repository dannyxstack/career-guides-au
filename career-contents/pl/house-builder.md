## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a house builder in Poland?**
A: In Poland, house builders earn between 4,000 and 8,000 PLN gross per month, depending on experience and region. Senior workers in cities like Warsaw or Wrocław may earn higher. Overtime and bonuses can increase total pay. The median monthly salary is approximately 5,500-6,000 PLN gross.

**Q: What are the work visa options for house builders wanting to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU house builders can apply for a national work visa (type D) based on a work permit obtained by an employer. Alternatively, if you have a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience, you may qualify for the EU Blue Card, which offers faster family reunification. Both require a valid job offer and meeting Polish immigration requirements.

**Q: What are the career prospects for house builders in Poland?**
A: The construction sector in Poland is growing due to housing demand and infrastructure projects. Experienced house builders can advance to foreman, site supervisor, or start their own contracting business. Certifications in specialized skills like tiling or roofing can improve job opportunities. Continuous learning of modern building techniques is valued.
