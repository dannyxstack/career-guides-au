## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Public sector inspectors typically earn between 4,000 and 7,000 PLN gross monthly. Private sector roles may offer higher pay, with senior specialists earning up to 10,000 PLN gross. Additional benefits such as bonuses or allowances may apply.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work via a standard work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 150% of the national average (approx. 126,000 PLN in 2024). A work permit is available for qualified candidates sponsored by an employer.

**Q: What are typical duties and career paths for this role in Poland?**
A: Inspectors conduct workplace inspections, assess environmental hazards, ensure compliance with health and safety regulations, and investigate incidents. Career progression includes senior inspector, team leader, or specialist roles in government agencies (e.g., PIP) or private consultancy. Additional certifications in occupational health or environmental management enhance prospects.
