## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for commissioned armed forces officers in Poland?**
A: Commissioned officers in the Polish Armed Forces earn a gross monthly salary ranging from approximately 5,000 PLN for junior officers to over 15,000 PLN for senior ranks, depending on rank, years of service, and specializations. Additional allowances for housing, foreign missions, and hazardous duties apply.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as commissioned armed forces officers in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No, this occupation is generally restricted to Polish citizens due to national security requirements. Non-citizens may only serve in limited support roles, and standard work visas or EU Blue Cards are not applicable for commission officer positions. EU citizens may join under specific bilateral agreements.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for commissioned officers in the Polish Armed Forces?**
A: Officers progress through ranks from second lieutenant to general, with promotions based on performance, seniority, and completion of mandatory courses at military academies. Specialization in fields like aviation, cyber defense, or logistics opens leadership roles. Many pursue postgraduate studies funded by the military.
