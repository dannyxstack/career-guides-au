## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a secondary education teacher in Poland?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a secondary school teacher in Poland ranges from 5,000 to 7,000 PLN, depending on experience, qualifications, and school type (public or private). Senior teachers with advanced degrees can earn up to 8,000 PLN or more. Salaries are regulated by the national Teachers' Charter for public schools.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a foreign teacher to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU teachers can work in Poland via a work permit (Type A) obtained by their employer, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Requirements include a recognized teaching qualification, a valid employment contract, and meeting the Blue Card salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross wage). A national visa (D-type) may also be issued for temporary teaching assignments.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a secondary education teacher in Poland?**
A: A master's degree in the subject taught and pedagogical preparation (e.g., a teaching certificate or postgraduate studies in education) are mandatory. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Ministry of Education or NAWA. Fluency in Polish is essential for teaching state schools. Private international schools may have different requirements.
