## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a university teacher in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary by rank and institution. An assistant professor (adiunkt) typically earns 5,000–7,000 PLN gross monthly, while full professors (profesor) may earn 10,000–15,000 PLN. Additional income from research grants or private universities can supplement base pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign university teachers in Poland?**
A: Foreign university teachers can work via a work permit (type A) or EU Blue Card. The employer must obtain a work permit from the voivodeship office. For the Blue Card, the job requires higher education and a salary at least 150% of the national average gross salary. Both routes grant temporary residence, with possible permanent residence after 5 years.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a university teacher in Poland?**
A: A doctoral degree (PhD) is typically required for assistant professor positions. Higher ranks (associate or full professor) require a habilitation, publications, and teaching experience. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by Polish authorities. Professional experience may be considered in some fields.
