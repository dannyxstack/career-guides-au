## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physical and engineering science technicians not elsewhere classified in Poland?**
A: In Poland, physical and engineering science technicians (not elsewhere classified) typically earn between 4,000 and 7,000 PLN gross per month. Salaries vary by experience, industry, and location, with senior roles or those in specialized sectors like R&D potentially exceeding this range. Additional benefits often include bonuses, medical care, or pension contributions.

**Q: What are the official visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU physical and engineering science technicians to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Poland via a work permit (Type A) for roles requiring specific skills, or the EU Blue Card for highly skilled positions. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer, a higher education degree (or 5 years relevant experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross national wage. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with Polish immigration regulations.

**Q: Which employers typically hire physical and engineering science technicians not elsewhere classified in Poland?**
A: Typical employers include industrial manufacturing plants, research institutes, energy companies, and environmental agencies. Technicians often work in quality control, laboratory testing, technical support, or maintenance. Sectors such as automotive, chemicals, electronics, and renewable energy offer abundant opportunities, particularly in industrial hubs like Silesia, Warsaw, and Poznań.
