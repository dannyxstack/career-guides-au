## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Construction Supervisor in Poland?**
A: Construction supervisors in Poland typically earn between 6,000 and 12,000 PLN gross per month. The range varies by experience, project complexity, and region, with higher salaries in cities like Warsaw and for large-scale projects. Senior supervisors or those on major infrastructure works may earn above 14,000 PLN.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Construction Supervisor in Poland via the Blue Card scheme?**
A: Yes, non-EU construction supervisors can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 150% of average gross wage, about 12,000 PLN/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit (type A) is available for lower salaries, requiring employer sponsorship and a local labour market test. Both routes require relevant qualifications and experience.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Construction Supervisors in Poland?**
A: Construction supervisors can progress to senior supervisor, project manager, or construction manager roles. With additional certifications (e.g., in safety or project management) and proven experience, they may oversee larger projects or move into consulting. Polish employers often value continuous learning, so courses in BIM or LEED can boost career growth.
