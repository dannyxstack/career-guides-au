**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout

**Human moat:**

- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Poland?**
A: Shelf fillers in Poland typically earn the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is 4,242 PLN gross per month for full-time employment. Hourly rates often range from 23 to 27 PLN gross, depending on the employer and region. Some large retailers may offer slightly higher pay or bonuses for night shifts.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for shelf fillers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU/EEA citizens can obtain a Polish work permit (Type A or C) for shelf filler roles. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because shelf filler positions are classified as low-skilled and do not meet the salary or qualification thresholds. Employers must demonstrate that no local candidate is available. The process involves employer sponsorship and a work permit application.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shelf fillers in Poland?**
A: Shelf fillers can advance to positions such as team leader, department supervisor, or store manager within retail chains. Some may transition to logistics or warehouse roles. Gaining experience and showing reliability can lead to promotions, though opportunities vary by employer. Additional training in customer service or inventory management can improve chances.
