## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialization and experience. As of 2023, monthly gross earnings typically range from 6,000 to 15,000 PLN. For example, a clinical dietitian might earn around 7,000 PLN, while a radiographer could earn 10,000 PLN. Public sector salaries are often lower than private practices.

**Q: Can health professionals not elsewhere classified obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Poland?**
A: Yes, provided they have a recognized higher professional qualification (at least a bachelor's degree) and a job offer with a salary at least 150% of the average national gross salary (approx. 9,975 PLN in 2023). The employer must obtain a work permit, after which the Blue Card is issued for up to 3 years.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements for health professionals in Poland?**
A: Most health professions require registration with a relevant chamber (e.g., the Chamber of Physicians or Nurses). Recognition of foreign diplomas is handled by the Ministry of Health or the National Center for Education Quality. Non-EU qualifications may need a nostrification procedure. Proficiency in Polish is typically mandatory for direct patient care.
