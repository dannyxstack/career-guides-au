## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an education manager in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for education managers in Poland ranges from 7,000 to 12,000 PLN, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and institution size. Senior roles in large organizations may exceed 15,000 PLN. Salaries in major cities like Warsaw or Kraków tend to be higher.

**Q: Can education managers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Poland?**
A: Yes, education managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (at least bachelor's) and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 150% of the average national salary in Poland. The employer must be recognized and the role must meet skilled professional criteria.

**Q: What qualifications and career progression are typical for education managers in Poland?**
A: Education managers usually hold a master's degree in education, management, or related fields. Career progression often starts from teaching or administrative roles, advancing to department head, then to vice-principal or principal. Additional certifications in school leadership or educational administration can accelerate growth.
