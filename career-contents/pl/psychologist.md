## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Poland?**
A: Salaries for psychologists in Poland vary by sector and experience. In the public sector (e.g., healthcare, education), gross monthly pay ranges from 4,000 to 8,000 PLN. Private practice or corporate roles can earn between 8,000 and 15,000 PLN monthly. Senior clinical psychologists may earn higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for psychologists to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU psychologists can work in Poland via a work permit (type A) or the EU Blue Card, which requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (approx. 9,000 PLN/month in 2025). Both routes require recognition of qualifications by the Polish Psychological Association and a valid employment contract.

**Q: What are the main employment settings for psychologists in Poland?**
A: Psychologists in Poland work in clinical settings (hospitals, mental health clinics), educational institutions (schools, universities), corporate environments (HR, coaching), and private practice. Others are employed in social services, research, or forensic psychology. Demand is growing in mental health and organizational psychology.
