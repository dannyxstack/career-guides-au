## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers in Poland?**
A: Sweepers and related labourers in Poland typically earn the national minimum wage, which in 2024 is 4242 PLN gross per month. Some employers may offer slightly more, but pay generally ranges between 4000 and 5000 PLN gross. Overtime and shift work may add a small bonus, but overall compensation remains at the lower end of the labour market.

**Q: Can foreign workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for sweeper jobs in Poland?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for sweepers as it requires a highly skilled job. Foreign workers can obtain a Polish work permit (type A) if the employer passes a labour market test proving no local candidate is available. Alternatively, seasonal work permits (up to 9 months) are possible for certain nationalities. These are the official legal routes for non-EU citizens.

**Q: What career progression opportunities do sweepers have in Poland?**
A: Sweepers may advance to supervisor roles in cleaning or waste management companies with experience. Some move into related areas like street cleaning or waste collection driving after obtaining a relevant licence. Further training in environmental services can lead to higher positions, though promotion is limited without additional qualifications or language skills.
