## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for journalists in Poland?**
A: Entry-level journalists in Poland earn around 4,000–6,000 PLN gross per month, while experienced journalists can earn 8,000–12,000 PLN. Senior editors or specialists at major outlets may exceed 15,000 PLN. Salaries vary by city, with Warsaw typically offering higher pay.

**Q: What work visa options exist for journalists to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU journalists can work in Poland via a national visa (type D) based on a work permit, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers (requires a university degree and salary of at least 1.5 times the average regional wage). Work permits are tied to a specific employer.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for journalists in Poland?**
A: Journalists typically start as junior reporters or content writers. With experience, they may advance to senior correspondent, editor, or specialist roles (e.g., investigative or data journalist). Many move into media management, PR, or freelance work. Continuous skill development in digital media is key.
