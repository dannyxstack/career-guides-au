## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in Poland?**
A: According to national salary surveys, the average gross monthly salary for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in Poland ranges between 3,500 and 5,500 PLN. Entry-level positions start around 3,000 PLN, while experienced workers can earn up to 6,500 PLN. Salaries vary by region, company size, and specific industry.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a national (D) work visa or an EU Blue Card. The national visa requires a work permit based on a job offer from a Polish employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree or five years of professional experience, plus a salary at least 1.5 times the average national gross wage.

**Q: What does an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler do in Poland?**
A: They assemble, adjust, and inspect electrical and electronic components such as circuit boards, control panels, and wiring harnesses. Tasks include soldering, using hand tools, testing connections, and ensuring compliance with safety standards. Workers are employed in manufacturing plants, often in automotive, electronics, and industrial equipment sectors.
