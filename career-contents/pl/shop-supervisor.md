## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shop supervisor in Poland?**
A: In Poland, shop supervisors earn between 4,000 and 6,500 PLN gross per month, depending on location, store size, and experience. Salaries are higher in major cities like Warsaw and Kraków. Many employers also offer bonuses based on sales targets.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for foreigners to work as a shop supervisor in Poland?**
A: Foreigners can work as shop supervisors via a national work visa (type D) after the employer obtains a work permit. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for those with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average national salary. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop supervisors in Poland?**
A: Shop supervisors can advance to store manager, district manager, or regional operations roles. Many Polish retailers offer internal training programs. Proficiency in Polish and management skills are key. Opportunities also exist in supply chain or merchandising within larger retail groups.
