## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Business Services and Administration Managers in Poland?**
A: According to recent data, the average gross monthly salary for managers in this category in Poland ranges from 15,000 to 25,000 PLN. Factors such as city (Warsaw being highest), company size, and specific industry influence the exact figure. Senior roles at large corporations may exceed 30,000 PLN, while smaller firms or junior positions start lower.

**Q: Can non-EU managers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Poland?**
A: Yes, non-EU managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, a higher education degree (or 5 years’ professional experience), and a salary at least 150% of the average national gross wage (approx. 11,700 PLN in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit (Type A) is available for roles meeting local labor market tests.

**Q: What career prospects exist for Business Services and Administration Managers in Poland?**
A: Poland's growing business services sector offers strong prospects, particularly in shared service centers and BPO hubs in Warsaw, Kraków, and Wrocław. Managers can advance to senior management, director roles, or specialize in areas like finance, HR, or IT services. Networking and certifications (e.g., MBA) enhance mobility.
