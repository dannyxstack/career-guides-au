## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a customs and border inspector in Poland?**
A: In Poland, customs and border inspectors employed by the National Revenue Administration earn a gross monthly salary ranging from approximately 4,500 to 7,000 PLN for entry-level to experienced positions, excluding bonuses and allowances. Salaries increase with seniority and specialization. Public sector pay scales apply.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a customs and border inspector in Poland?**
A: Customs and border inspector positions in Poland typically require Polish citizenship due to security clearance and public service regulations. Foreigners cannot apply for this role via standard work permits or the EU Blue Card. Non-EU citizens may consider related roles in private customs agencies or logistics, which might be accessible with a work visa if sponsored by an employer.

**Q: What are the educational requirements to become a customs and border inspector in Poland?**
A: To become a customs and border inspector in Poland, candidates need a secondary education (matura) or a bachelor's degree, preferably in administration, law, or customs-related fields. After passing a competitive recruitment process, including background checks, new hires undergo mandatory training at the Customs and Tax Training Centre. Language skills and physical fitness are also assessed.
