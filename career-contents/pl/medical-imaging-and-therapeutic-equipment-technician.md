## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians in Poland?**
A: Salaries for medical imaging technicians in Poland typically range from 5,000 to 8,000 PLN gross per month, depending on experience, specialization (e.g., MRI, CT, radiotherapy), and location. Entry-level positions may start around 4,500 PLN, while senior technicians in major cities like Warsaw can earn upwards of 9,000 PLN. Public hospital pay is generally lower than private clinics.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Medical Imaging Technicians to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Poland via a standard work permit (Type A) obtained by an employer, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a recognized degree, a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage, and relevant qualifications. Both routes demand employer sponsorship and registration with Polish authorities.

**Q: What education and registration are required to work as a Medical Imaging Technician in Poland?**
A: You need a diploma from a post-secondary medical imaging school (2-2.5 years) or a bachelor's degree in radiography. After graduation, you must register with the Polish Chamber of Radiology Technicians. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain registration. International qualifications must be nostrified by the Polish Ministry of Health to be recognized.
