## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for technical and medical sales professionals in Poland?**
A: Salaries for technical and medical sales professionals in Poland typically range from 8,000 to 15,000 PLN gross per month, plus commissions and bonuses. Senior roles or those in specialized medical fields can exceed 20,000 PLN. Benefits often include a company car and phone.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card routes are available for non-EU professionals in this field in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a work visa or an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer. For the EU Blue Card, the minimum gross annual salary in 2024 is approximately 68,000 PLN. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable Polish or EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed for technical and medical sales roles in Poland?**
A: A bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as engineering or life sciences is typically required, along with proven sales experience. Fluency in Polish is essential for most domestic roles, though international companies may accept English. Industry-specific certifications are advantageous.
