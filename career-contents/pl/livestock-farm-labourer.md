## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Poland?**
A: Salaries for livestock farm labourers in Poland are generally based on the national minimum wage, which as of 2025 is around 4,300 PLN gross per month for full-time work. However, actual pay can vary by region, farm size, and employer. Some may offer additional benefits like accommodation or overtime pay, but exact amounts depend on individual contracts.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a livestock farm labourer in Poland through a visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Livestock farm labourers typically qualify for a Polish work visa (D-type national visa) based on an employer’s confirmed job offer and work permit (zezwolenie na pracę). The EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled workers, so it is rarely applicable to this occupation. Seasonal work may also be possible under specific simplified procedures for agriculture.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for a livestock farm labourer in Poland?**
A: With experience, livestock farm labourers can advance to senior stockperson or team leader roles, overseeing animal care and daily operations. Some farms offer on-the-job training or courses in animal husbandry, which can lead to supervisory positions or specialist roles like breeding technician. Further education in agriculture may open paths to farm management or agribusiness.
