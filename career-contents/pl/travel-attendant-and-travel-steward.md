## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for travel attendants and stewards in Poland?**
A: Salaries for travel attendants and stewards in Poland vary by employer and experience. Entry-level positions typically earn around 4,000-5,000 PLN gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn 6,000-8,000 PLN gross. Airlines may also provide additional allowances for flights and layovers.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for travel attendants in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as travel attendants in Poland via a work visa (type D) if they have a job offer from a Polish employer. The employer must obtain a work permit. For the EU Blue Card, a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Polish salary are required, though specific airline roles may qualify.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a travel attendant in Poland?**
A: Travel attendants in Poland typically need a high school diploma, fluency in English and Polish, and a valid cabin crew attestation (CCT) or equivalent training. Airlines often require first aid certification and successful completion of their own training program. Prior experience in customer service is beneficial.
