## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Insurance Representatives in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Insurance Representatives in Poland typically ranges from 5,000 to 8,000 PLN, depending on experience, location, and performance. Entry-level positions start around 4,000 PLN, while senior representatives can earn up to 10,000 PLN or more, often including commissions and bonuses.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Insurance Representatives in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a valid job offer from a Polish employer to apply for a work permit, followed by a national visa. For an EU Blue Card, the job must require higher professional qualifications (typically a university degree) and offer a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross national salary. The employer must also demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Insurance Representative in Poland?**
A: A high school diploma is the minimum, but a bachelor's degree in finance, economics, or business is preferred. Licensing is required: candidates must pass an exam administered by the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF). Strong communication and sales skills are essential, and fluency in Polish is typically mandatory, though some international firms may accept English.
