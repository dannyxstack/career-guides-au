## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' deck crew members in Poland?**
A: Salaries for deck crew in Poland vary by experience and vessel type. Entry-level ratings earn around 2,500–4,000 PLN net per month, while experienced deck officers can earn 8,000–15,000 PLN net. In international shipping, pay is often higher and may be tax-free under Polish regulations for seafarers.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for foreign deck crew seeking employment in Poland?**
A: Non-EU deck crew typically need a work permit (type A) sponsored by a Polish employer. For highly qualified positions, the EU Blue Card is available if the job meets salary thresholds (currently at least 6,000 PLN gross/month) and requires higher education or equivalent skills. Seafarers on international vessels may also use a Polish seafarer's document.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as deck crew on Polish-flagged vessels?**
A: Basic requirements include a valid STCW (Standards of Training, Certification, and Watchkeeping) certificate, a seafarer's medical certificate, and a Polish seafarer's book if employed on a Polish-flagged ship. Entry-level positions may require a Deck Rating certificate, while officers need higher-level STCW endorsements and often a maritime academy degree.
