## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Poland?**
A: In Poland, car, taxi, and van drivers earn an average gross monthly salary of 4,000–7,000 PLN, depending on city, experience, and employment type (self-employed vs. contract). Earnings often include tips and bonuses. Drivers in Warsaw or Kraków may earn higher rates. Self-employed drivers typically earn more but must cover their own taxes and insurance.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for non-EU car, taxi, or van drivers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit (type A) and a national visa or temporary residence permit. An employer must prove no local candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is rarely applicable as this occupation usually does not meet the high-skill salary threshold (at least 158,046 PLN gross annually in 2025). Alternative routes include the Poland Business Harbour program for IT professionals, but this does not cover driving roles.

**Q: What skills and licenses are needed to become a taxi or van driver in Poland?**
A: You need a valid driving license (category B for passenger cars; B+E for heavier vans), a clean criminal record, and a taxi driver license (for taxis) from the local city office. Good navigation skills, basic English, and knowledge of local roads are helpful. Professional driver training or certificates (e.g., transport of goods) can improve job prospects. Many drivers become self-employed.
