## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a judge in Poland?**
A: The salary of a judge in Poland is determined by law and varies by court level. As of 2024, a district court judge earns approximately 12,000–15,000 PLN gross per month, with additional seniority and function allowances. Higher court judges earn more, with Supreme Court judges receiving around 20,000 PLN gross.

**Q: Can a foreign judge work in Poland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU judges can work in Poland through the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer and meet requirements: higher professional qualifications (e.g., a law degree), at least three years of relevant professional experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage in Poland. A work permit may also be an option.

**Q: What are the main career steps to become a judge in Poland?**
A: To become a judge in Poland, one must be a Polish citizen with a law degree, complete a three-year judicial traineeship, pass the national judicial exam, and gain experience as a court referendary or assessor for at least three years. Appointment is by the President upon recommendation of the National Council of the Judiciary.
