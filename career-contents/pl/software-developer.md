## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Poland?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Poland vary by experience and location. In 2024, junior developers earn about 8,000–12,000 PLN gross monthly, mid-level 15,000–22,000 PLN, and seniors 25,000–35,000 PLN. Warsaw and Kraków offer higher pay, while remote roles are common. Taxes are progressive, and net pay is roughly 70–75% of gross.

**Q: Can non-EU software developers work in Poland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU software developers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Poland. Requirements include a valid job offer in a highly skilled position, a signed employment contract, a salary at least 150% of the national average gross wage (updated annually), and recognized professional qualifications. The Blue Card grants a residence and work permit with a path to permanent residency.

**Q: Is there high demand for software developers in Poland?**
A: Yes, Poland has a strong demand for software developers, driven by a thriving tech sector and many international companies setting up R&D centers. Specialists in Java, Python, JavaScript, and cloud technologies are particularly sought after. The market is competitive, but opportunities exist across industries like finance, e-commerce, and gaming, with many companies offering flexible work and upskilling programs.
