## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for building architects in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for building architects in Poland ranges from 7,000 to 14,000 PLN, depending on experience, qualifications, and location. Senior architects or those in major cities like Warsaw or Kraków may earn higher. Salaries often increase with membership in the Chamber of Architects and professional experience.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card routes for building architects in Poland?**
A: Non-EU architects can work in Poland via a work permit (Type A) obtained by an employer, or the EU Blue Card, which requires a recognized degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Both routes require a work visa application at a Polish consulate. Blue Card holders may get faster family reunion and residency.

**Q: What qualifications do building architects need to practice in Poland?**
A: To practice as a building architect in Poland, you must hold a master's degree in architecture and complete a 2-year internship under a licensed architect. Then, pass a state exam and register with the Chamber of Architects of Poland (IARP). EU architects can apply for recognition of qualifications, while non-EU architects may need additional training or exams.
