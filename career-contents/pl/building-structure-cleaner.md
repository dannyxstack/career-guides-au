## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building structure cleaners in Poland?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Poland generally earn the national minimum wage or slightly above. As of 2024, the minimum monthly gross wage is about 4242 PLN. More experienced workers or those specializing in high-rise cleaning may earn 5000–6000 PLN. Rates vary by region and employer. Always check current labor market data for updates.

**Q: What are the visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU citizens to work as a building structure cleaner in Poland?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit (type A or B). The EU Blue Card requires a highly skilled job with salary at least 1.5 times the average wage (around 10,000 PLN gross in 2024) and a relevant degree, which cleaning roles rarely meet. Therefore, the standard work permit process is the primary route, with employer sponsorship and a labor market test.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for building structure cleaners in Poland?**
A: Cleaners can advance to team leader or supervisor roles, often after gaining experience and certifications like height safety or industrial cleaning. Some start their own cleaning businesses. Specializing in facade or window cleaning for high-rise buildings can lead to higher pay. The field offers moderate stability, with demand tied to construction and maintenance sectors.
