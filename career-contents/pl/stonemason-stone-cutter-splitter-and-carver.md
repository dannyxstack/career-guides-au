## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Poland?**
A: In Poland, a stonemason's gross monthly salary typically ranges from 4,000 to 7,000 PLN, with senior specialists earning up to 10,000 PLN. Rates vary by region, experience, and project type.

**Q: Can a non-EU stonemason work in Poland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU stonemasons can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Poland. The employer must first obtain a work permit (type A) for the employee. The Blue Card then allows residence and work for skilled workers.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a stonemason in Poland?**
A: Typically, a vocational diploma in stone processing or a related field is required. Apprenticeships or on-the-job training are common. Knowledge of Polish is important for safety and communication. Certification in heritage restoration may be needed for historic works.
