## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a valuer or loss assessor in Poland?**
A: Entry-level valuers in Poland earn between 4,500 and 6,000 PLN gross monthly, while experienced professionals can earn 8,000 to 12,000 PLN. Specialized property valuers or insurance loss assessors may receive higher pay. Salaries vary by region, with Warsaw and Kraków offering the highest compensation.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for valuers and loss assessors to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (approx. 9,000 PLN in 2025), a recognized degree, and professional qualifications. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for work requires a work permit obtained through the local voivodeship office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a valuer or loss assessor in Poland?**
A: A bachelor's degree in finance, economics, or real estate is typically required. For property valuation, a postgraduate program and a professional license from the Ministry of Development are mandatory. Loss assessors often need insurance industry certifications. Both roles demand strong analytical and communication skills.
