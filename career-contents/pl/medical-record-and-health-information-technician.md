## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for medical records and health information technicians in Poland?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for medical records technicians in Poland ranges from 4,000 to 6,500 PLN, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 3,500 PLN, while senior roles can exceed 7,000 PLN. Salaries in private clinics and large hospitals tend to be higher.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for medical records technicians moving to Poland?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a national work visa (type D) for employment. The EU Blue Card, intended for highly skilled workers, requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 150% of Poland's average gross salary (approximately 11,000 PLN per month in 2024). As medical records technician salaries often fall below this threshold, the standard work visa is the more common route.

**Q: What are the career prospects for medical records technicians in Poland?**
A: The digitalization of healthcare is increasing demand for technicians. Career advancement includes moving into health information management, data analysis, or IT roles. A vocational diploma or bachelor's degree in health information management is typically required. Polish language proficiency is essential, as most healthcare documentation is in Polish.
