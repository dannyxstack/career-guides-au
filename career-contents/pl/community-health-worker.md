## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a community health worker in Poland?**
A: Salaries for community health workers in Poland vary by experience and employer, but generally exceed the national minimum wage (4,242 PLN gross/month in 2024). Entry-level positions may start around 4,500 PLN, while experienced workers can earn up to 6,500 PLN gross monthly. Public sector roles often follow fixed salary scales.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as community health workers in Poland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is typically not applicable for community health workers, as it requires higher education and a job meeting a higher salary threshold. The standard route is a national work permit (type A), obtained by the employer after showing no suitable Polish or EU candidate is available. Relevant vocational qualifications are required.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for community health workers in Poland?**
A: Community health workers in Poland can advance to supervisory roles (e.g., team leader) or specialize in areas like addiction prevention, elderly care, or health promotion. Additional certifications or a bachelor's degree in public health or social work can open doors to management or policy positions. Experience is key for progression.
