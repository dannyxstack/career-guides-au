## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Buyer in Poland?**
A: Average monthly gross salary for Buyers in Poland ranges from 5,000 to 10,000 PLN for mid-level positions, with senior buyers earning up to 15,000 PLN. Salaries vary by industry, location (higher in Warsaw), and experience. Many employers offer annual bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Buyers to work in Poland?**
A: Foreign Buyers can obtain a work visa through a Type A work permit or the EU Blue Card if meeting salary thresholds. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer, higher education, and salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage (approx. 7,000 PLN gross monthly). Both routes allow family reunification.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for Buyers in Poland?**
A: Buyers in Poland can advance to Senior Buyer, Procurement Manager, or Supply Chain Director roles. Gaining international sourcing experience and certifications like CIPS improves prospects. Large manufacturing or retail firms often provide clear career paths and skill development programs.
