## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Poland?**
A: Earnings vary widely, often below the national average wage, as most work part-time or freelance. In major cities like Warsaw, fees per session range from 50 to 200 PLN. Annual income typically falls between 15,000 and 40,000 PLN, depending on client base, location, and marketing efforts.

**Q: Can foreign astrologers and fortune-tellers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Poland?**
A: Foreigners can apply for a national work visa (type D) with a work permit, typically if an employer sponsors them. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a high-skilled job; astrologers and fortune-tellers usually do not meet these criteria. Self-employment is possible with a temporary residence permit for business purposes.

**Q: What are the career prospects for astrologers in Poland?**
A: Career prospects are limited, with most practitioners operating as independent freelancers or online consultants. Employment in companies is rare. Growth depends on building a personal brand and clientele through social media or local events. Formal qualifications are not required, but competition from unregulated practitioners is high.
