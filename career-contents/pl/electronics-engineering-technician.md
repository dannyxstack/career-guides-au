## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for electronics engineering technicians in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for electronics engineering technicians in Poland ranges from 5,000 to 8,000 PLN (approx. 1,100–1,800 EUR), depending on experience, location, and industry. Junior technicians earn around 4,500 PLN, while senior roles may exceed 10,000 PLN. Salaries are higher in major cities like Warsaw and Kraków.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for electronics engineering technicians to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as electronics engineering technicians in Poland via the EU Blue Card (for highly skilled workers with a valid job offer and higher education) or a national visa (type D) based on a work permit. The employer must obtain a work permit from the local voivodeship office, confirming labor market demand.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an electronics engineering technician in Poland?**
A: Typically, employers require at least a secondary technical education (technikum) in electronics or a related field, or a bachelor's degree in electronics engineering. Practical experience, knowledge of circuit design, and proficiency with diagnostic tools are essential. Polish language skills are often preferred, though English may suffice in international companies.
