**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CAD drafting and 2D drawing generation
- Standard finite element analysis (FEA) and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations
- Generate Standard BOMs and Technical Documentation
- Routine tolerance analysis and dimensional inspection
- Predictive maintenance planning based on historical data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-driven generative design (e.g., topology optimization)
- Machine learning-based failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)
- Digital twin real-time simulation and optimization
- Smart materials selection and supply chain optimization
- Write automated test and verification scripts

**Human moat:**

- Cross-system physical intuition and innovative design
- Complex nonlinear problem diagnosis and on-site debugging
- Ethical and responsible decision-making for safety-critical systems
- Unstructured communication with clients and suppliers
- Multidisciplinary integration and system architecture decision-making

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a mechanical engineer in Poland?**
A: As of 2024, the average gross monthly salary for a mechanical engineer in Poland ranges from 8,000 to 12,000 PLN, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 6,000 PLN, while senior engineers can earn up to 18,000 PLN. Major industrial regions like Silesia and Warsaw offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for a mechanical engineer to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU mechanical engineers can work in Poland via the EU Blue Card, which requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average, or a national work permit (Type A) based on a labor market test. Both routes require employer sponsorship. Poland also offers a temporary residence permit for highly qualified workers.

**Q: What industries employ mechanical engineers in Poland?**
A: Mechanical engineers in Poland are in demand across automotive, aerospace, machinery, and energy sectors. Major employers include metalworking and industrial machinery companies, as well as foreign firms with R&D centers. The growing automation and renewable energy sectors also offer opportunities.
