## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmacists in Poland?**
A: As of 2024, the average gross monthly salary for pharmacists in Poland ranges from 6,000 to 9,000 PLN (approximately 1,300–2,000 EUR). Salaries vary by experience, location, and sector (e.g., community pharmacy vs. hospital). Senior pharmacists or those in managerial roles can earn up to 12,000 PLN gross per month.

**Q: How can foreign pharmacists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Poland?**
A: Foreign pharmacists can work in Poland via a temporary residence permit for work (type C) or the EU Blue Card. Both require a valid job offer from a Polish employer and recognition of pharmacy qualifications by the Polish Pharmaceutical Chamber. The employer must obtain a work permit. For the Blue Card, a higher salary threshold applies.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a pharmacist in Poland?**
A: To practice as a pharmacist in Poland, you need a Master's degree in Pharmacy (5-year program) from an EU/EEA university or a recognized non-EU degree. Non-EU diplomas must be nostrified. Additionally, you must register with the Regional Pharmaceutical Chamber and be fluent in Polish, as communication with patients and authorities is in Polish.
