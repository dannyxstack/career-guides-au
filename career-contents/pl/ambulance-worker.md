## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ambulance workers in Poland?**
A: In Poland, ambulance workers (ratownicy medyczni) earn between 4,000 and 6,500 PLN gross monthly, with experienced workers earning higher. Salaries vary by region and employer. Additional allowances for night shifts and overtime apply. Public sector pay is regulated.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU ambulance workers to obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card in Poland?**
A: Non-EU ambulance workers can apply for a national work visa (type D) with a job offer from a Polish employer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; ambulance workers typically need a higher education degree and a job paying at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. 9,500 PLN in 2024). Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an ambulance worker in Poland?**
A: To become an ambulance worker in Poland, one must complete a 3-year bachelor's degree in emergency medical services (ratownictwo medyczne) at a medical university, followed by state certification. Continuous professional development is required to maintain licensure.
