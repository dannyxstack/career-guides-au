## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other language teachers in Poland?**
A: Salaries for language teachers in Poland vary by institution and experience. In private language schools, full-time teachers typically earn between 3,500 and 6,000 PLN gross per month. Public schools offer lower base pay but include benefits. Teachers of high-demand languages or with advanced certifications may earn more.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU language teachers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU teachers can work in Poland via a work permit (type A) obtained by their employer after a labor market test, or through the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (currently about 1.5 times the average salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for language teaching positions in Poland?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor's degree and a recognized teaching certificate (e.g., CELTA for English). Native-level proficiency is often expected. For public schools, a teaching license or equivalency is needed. Experience with exam preparation (IELTS, TOEFL) and specialized languages can enhance prospects.
