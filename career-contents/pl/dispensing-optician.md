**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic refractometer measures prescription and lens selection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted analysis of customer facial features to recommend optimal frame styles.
- Smart system provides personalized lens feature recommendations (blue light filtering, progressive multifocal).
- Automated customer appointment and follow-up system to improve efficiency.
- AI tool training helps opticians quickly learn new product knowledge

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and adjustment of complex prescriptions (e.g., prisms, high astigmatism)
- Face-to-face customer communication and trust building
- Custom frame adjustment (physical fit and comfort)
- Professional consultation for treating eye abnormalities or heterophoria
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration (optometrists, ophthalmologists) and legal liability

- Basic data analysis (customer profiling, inventory optimization)
- Advanced optometry techniques (corneal topography, wavefront aberrometry)
- Basic knowledge of ophthalmic diseases (dry eye, glaucoma)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a dispensing optician in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for dispensing opticians in Poland ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 PLN, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 3,500 PLN, while experienced opticians in major cities like Warsaw may earn up to 7,000 PLN. Salaries often include performance bonuses.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for dispensing opticians to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU dispensing opticians can work in Poland via a national work visa (type D) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a recognized diploma, a job offer with a gross salary of at least 6,888 PLN per month (2024 threshold), and a work contract of at least one year. Official routes include employer-sponsored applications through provincial offices.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a dispensing optician in Poland?**
A: To work as a dispensing optician in Poland, you need a secondary education diploma and a vocational qualification in opticianry, typically obtained from a 2-year post-secondary school. Registration with the National Chamber of Opticians is mandatory. For foreign qualifications, recognition by the Ministry of Health or equivalence verification may be required.
