## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Poland?**
A: The salary usually ranges from the national minimum wage (currently around 3,600 PLN gross per month) to about 5,000 PLN gross, depending on experience and employer. Seasonal workers often earn hourly rates, which must comply with Polish labour law.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for foreign farm labourers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a type D national visa for seasonal work (up to 9 months) or a temporary residence permit with a work permit linked to a specific employer. The EU Blue Card is generally reserved for highly skilled workers; farm labourers typically do not meet its salary and qualification thresholds.

**Q: What are the main duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Poland?**
A: Duties include feeding and caring for livestock, planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops, operating and maintaining farm machinery, repairing fences and buildings, and performing general farm maintenance. The work is physically demanding and often follows seasonal cycles.
