## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for underwater divers in Poland?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for commercial underwater divers in Poland ranges from 6,000 to 12,000 PLN, depending on experience, specialization (e.g., offshore vs inland), and employer. Entry-level divers may start around 5,000 PLN, while experienced professionals with advanced certifications can earn significantly more, especially in demanding sectors like offshore wind farm maintenance.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU underwater diver to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU divers can work in Poland via a national work visa (type D) or the EU Blue Card. For a work visa, a Polish employer must obtain a work permit. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or equivalent) and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, approx. 10,000 PLN/month). Relevant diving certifications must be recognized in Poland.

**Q: What qualifications and certifications are required to work as an underwater diver in Poland?**
A: To work as an underwater diver in Poland, you need a recognized commercial diving certificate, such as HSE (UK) or IMCA qualifications, plus a valid medical fitness certificate. Polish language skills are often required for communication on domestic projects. Additional certifications in specialized areas like underwater welding or inspection can enhance job prospects. Registration with the Polish Diving Association may also be necessary.
