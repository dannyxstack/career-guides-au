## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for protective services workers in Poland?**
A: Salaries for protective services workers (ISCO 5419) in Poland vary by role and experience. Entry-level positions earn around 3,500–5,000 PLN gross monthly, while experienced specialists may earn 6,000–8,000 PLN. Security guards, private investigators, and fire inspectors have different pay scales. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings. Actual pay depends on employer, location, and specific duties.

**Q: Can protective services workers work in Poland via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Protective services workers (ISCO 5419) are typically not eligible for the EU Blue Card, which requires a highly skilled job with a university degree or five years of experience. Instead, non-EU citizens can obtain a national work permit (type A) with a job offer from a Polish employer. The employer must prove no suitable local candidate is available. Other routes include seasonal work permits or temporary residence permits for employment.

**Q: What types of employers commonly hire protective services workers in Poland?**
A: Protective services workers in Poland are employed by private security companies, corporate security departments, event management firms, and public institutions. Common roles include security guards, fire safety inspectors, loss prevention officers, and private investigators. Demand is steady in retail, logistics, and industrial sectors. Language skills and relevant certifications can improve job prospects.
