## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Poland?**
A: Average monthly gross salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Poland ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 PLN, depending on experience, location, and employer size. Entry-level positions may start around 3,500 PLN, while senior professionals can earn up to 8,000 PLN. Salaries are typically higher in cities like Warsaw or Łódź.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU garment patternmakers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU garment patternmakers can work in Poland via a work permit (type A) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a gross annual salary at least 150% of the national average (approx. 7,000 PLN/month in 2024) and relevant qualifications. Qualification recognition by Polish authorities is typically needed for both routes.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a garment patternmaker in Poland?**
A: Typically, a vocational diploma in clothing design or patternmaking is required. Many employers also accept candidates with a bachelor's degree in fashion or textile engineering. Practical experience is highly valued. Proficiency in CAD software for pattern making is increasingly important. Foreign qualifications may need nostrification for official recognition.
