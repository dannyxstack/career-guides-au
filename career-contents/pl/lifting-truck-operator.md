## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a lifting truck operator in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a lifting truck operator in Poland ranges from 4,000 to 6,000 PLN, depending on experience, region, and company. Entry-level positions start around 3,500 PLN, while experienced operators can earn up to 7,000 PLN. Overtime and shift work often increase total earnings.

**Q: Can a foreign lifting truck operator work in Poland via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, foreign lifting truck operators can work in Poland through a Type D national visa for employment or an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, meet salary thresholds (e.g., PL 11,664 gross monthly in 2025 for Blue Card), and hold recognized qualifications. Employers must obtain a work permit (type A) before visa application.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to operate lifting trucks in Poland?**
A: Operators must hold a valid UDT (Office of Technical Inspection) license for specific truck types. Training costs 1,500–3,000 PLN and includes theory and practical tests. No formal education is required, but a secondary school diploma is preferred. Certificates must be renewed every 5 years.
