## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Poland?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers in Poland typically earn the national minimum wage or slightly above. As of 2024, the minimum wage is 4,242 PLN gross per month. Hourly rates range from 23 to 30 PLN. Seasonal or part-time work may offer lower pay. Experienced workers or those in specialized tasks can earn up to 5,000 PLN gross per month.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as garden and horticultural labourers in Poland?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit (Type A or seasonal) sponsored by a Polish employer. The seasonal work permit is valid up to 9 months. The EU Blue Card is not suitable for this occupation as it requires higher education or a high salary threshold. For short-term seasonal work, a visa with a declaration of entrusting work to a foreigner may apply, but a full work permit is often required.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for garden and horticultural labourers in Poland?**
A: With experience, labourers can advance to supervisor or team leader roles in landscaping or horticultural companies. Further training in horticulture or landscaping can lead to positions as a gardener, landscaper, or greenhouse manager. Some workers start their own gardening businesses. There are also formal apprenticeship programs and short courses offered by agricultural institutions.
