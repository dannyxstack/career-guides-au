## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sports coaches, instructors and officials in Poland?**
A: Average gross monthly salary for sports coaches in Poland ranges from 4,000 to 8,000 PLN depending on experience, sport, and type of employment (public vs private). Senior coaches or those in popular sports like football may earn upwards of 10,000 PLN, while part-time officials earn less. Income can vary significantly with additional bonuses or event fees.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for foreign sports coaches to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU coaches need a work permit (type A) if employed by a Polish entity. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a recognized university degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross national wage (approx. 12,000 PLN in 2024). Sports coaches may qualify if they hold relevant education and a high-level contract.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for sports coaches in Poland?**
A: Coaches can advance to head coach, sports director, or national team roles with experience and certifications. Further education in sports management or physiotherapy can open administrative or specialist positions. Officials may progress to referee supervisory roles or federation positions. Continuous professional development is key.
