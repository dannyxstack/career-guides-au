## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Poland?**
A: Salaries for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Poland typically range from the national minimum wage (4242 PLN gross monthly in 2024) to around 5000 PLN gross, depending on experience, employer, and location. Some positions may offer additional bonuses or overtime pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a meter reader or vending-machine collector in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa (type D) for employment in this occupation, provided the employer secures a work permit. However, the EU Blue Card is generally not available for these roles, as it requires a highly skilled position with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Thus, the standard work visa route applies.

**Q: How is the job of meter reader and vending-machine collector affected by technology such as smart meters?**
A: Smart meters reduce the need for manual reading, but many older meters remain in Poland, requiring on-site visits. Vending-machine collectors still handle cash collection and basic maintenance. The role is evolving but continues to exist, especially in areas with less advanced infrastructure.
