## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bookmakers, croupiers and related gaming workers in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and location. Starting croupiers often earn the national minimum wage (around 3,600 PLN gross monthly in 2024), while experienced workers in major casinos can earn up to 6,000-8,000 PLN gross monthly including tips. Top earners or those in managerial roles may exceed 10,000 PLN.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as croupiers in Poland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work visa (type D) sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is generally reserved for highly skilled professions with higher education and salary thresholds, and rarely applies to gaming workers. However, standard work permits are available if the employer proves no suitable local candidate.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for croupiers in Poland?**
A: Croupiers can advance to senior table games dealer, pit boss, or casino shift manager. With experience and training, they may also move into tournament coordination, casino marketing, or training new staff. Larger casinos in Warsaw, Krakow or Poznan offer more upward mobility. Some eventually open their own gaming establishments.
