## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Poland?**
A: Median monthly gross salary for these professions in Poland ranges from 8,000 to 15,000 PLN, depending on experience, specialization, and sector. Actuaries in insurance and statisticians in finance or IT tend to earn higher. Entry-level positions start around 6,000 PLN, while senior roles can exceed 20,000 PLN gross monthly.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU mathematicians, actuaries, or statisticians to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain a work permit (type A) with a job offer meeting the market test. For long-term mobility, the EU Blue Card is available for those with a recognized university degree and a salary at least 150% of Poland's average gross wage. The exact threshold is updated quarterly; check with the voivodeship office for current figures.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for mathematicians and statisticians in Poland?**
A: Demand is high in IT, finance, insurance, and research. Mathematicians work in data science, cryptography, and academia. Statisticians find roles in biostatistics, market research, and government agencies. Actuaries are primarily employed by insurance companies and consulting firms. Advanced skills in programming and machine learning open further opportunities in AI and analytics.
