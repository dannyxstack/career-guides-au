## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Poland?**
A: Hourly wages for domestic cleaners in Poland usually range from 20 to 40 PLN, depending on location and employer. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is 28.10 PLN per hour. Full-time cleaners earn roughly 3,500–5,000 PLN gross per month. Earnings can be higher in major cities like Warsaw or Kraków.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU domestic cleaners to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU domestic cleaners can obtain a national visa (type D) for employment, followed by a temporary residence permit. The employer must prove no suitable local candidate exists. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it targets highly skilled workers; domestic cleaning is not considered a high-skill occupation under Polish law.

**Q: What are the typical duties and career prospects for domestic cleaners in Poland?**
A: Duties include cleaning rooms, kitchens, bathrooms; washing windows; sweeping and mopping floors; changing linens; and minor organizing. Most work privately or for cleaning agencies. Career progression is limited but can lead to supervisor roles or starting a cleaning business. Formal training is not required.
