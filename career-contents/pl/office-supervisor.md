## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Office Supervisor in Poland?**
A: Office Supervisors in Poland typically earn between 5,000 and 8,000 PLN gross per month. Salaries vary based on experience, company size, and location, with Warsaw offering higher pay. Senior supervisors or those in specialized industries may earn up to 10,000 PLN gross.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU Office Supervisors to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a national work visa (type D) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education diploma and a salary at least 150% of the national average (approx. 12,000 PLN gross monthly). Both require a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit from the voivode office.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become an Office Supervisor in Poland?**
A: Most positions require at least secondary education, often a bachelor's degree. Relevant experience in administration or team coordination is valued. Key skills include leadership, communication, organization, and basic IT proficiency. Knowledge of English or another foreign language can be an advantage in international companies.
