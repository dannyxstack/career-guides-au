## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an ICT Services Manager in Poland?**
A: According to industry reports, the average gross monthly salary for ICT Services Managers in Poland ranges from 20,000 to 30,000 PLN, with variations based on experience, company size, and location (e.g., Warsaw commands higher pay). Senior managers with over 10 years of experience can earn 35,000 PLN or more, excluding bonuses and benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU ICT Services Managers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU ICT managers can apply for a national work visa (type D) based on a work permit (type A, B, or C) obtained by the employer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a contract meeting salary thresholds (currently at least 150% of average national salary). Temporary residence permits for work are also an option.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for ICT Services Managers in Poland?**
A: ICT Services Managers in Poland can advance to senior leadership roles such as Chief Technology Officer (CTO), IT Director, or Vice President of IT. With Poland's growing tech sector, opportunities also exist in consulting, project management, or starting their own IT service firms. Continuous learning in cloud, AI, and cybersecurity is valued for career progression.
