## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for farming, forestry and fisheries advisers in Poland?**
A: Salaries for farming, forestry and fisheries advisers in Poland typically range from 5,000 to 9,000 PLN gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and region. Public sector positions may offer lower pay but greater stability, while private consultancy roles can be higher. Additional benefits often include bonuses and allowances for fieldwork.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign advisers to work in Poland?**
A: Foreign advisers can work in Poland through a national work visa (type D) or the EU Blue Card. The employer must obtain a work permit from the local labor office. For the EU Blue Card, the applicant needs a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 150% of the average national wage, adjusted annually). Neither route has a quota, and processing times vary.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a farming adviser in Poland?**
A: A bachelor's or master's degree in agriculture, forestry, fisheries, or a related field is typically required. Practical experience in farming or advisory services is highly valued. Additional certifications in sustainable agriculture, EU funding programs, or digital farming tools can improve prospects. Fluency in Polish is essential, as most clients and government bodies operate in Polish.
