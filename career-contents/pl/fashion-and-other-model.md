## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fashion models in Poland?**
A: Earnings vary widely. Entry-level models may earn PLN 3,000–5,000 monthly, while established models can earn much more per assignment. Most work freelance, so income is irregular. Agencies typically take a commission of 15–20%.

**Q: Can non-EU models work in Poland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU models can work through a standard work permit or an EU Blue Card. For the Blue Card, the model must have a higher education degree or five years' professional experience, and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (approx. 150% of the average gross salary). Employer sponsorship is required.

**Q: How can I start a modeling career in Poland?**
A: Join a reputable modeling agency, have professional portfolio photos, and network within the industry. Social media presence can boost visibility. Success requires professionalism, adaptability to different modeling types (fashion, commercial, etc.), and meeting physical requirements set by clients.
