## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for refuse sorters in Poland?**
A: Refuse sorters in Poland earn between 4000 and 5000 PLN gross per month, depending on region and employer. The national minimum wage is 3600 PLN gross (2024). Overtime, night shifts, and weekend work can increase total pay. Salaries are modest, reflecting the low-skilled nature of the job.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as refuse sorters in Poland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent experience. Refuse sorting is low-skilled, so it does not qualify. Non-EU nationals can obtain standard work permits (type A) if an employer sponsors them. The employer must demonstrate a labor shortage. Always verify with the Polish consulate.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for refuse sorters in Poland?**
A: Work takes place in recycling plants or waste sorting facilities. Tasks include manual sorting of recyclables on conveyor belts. Shifts are often 8–12 hours, including nights and weekends. The job is physically demanding with repetitive movements. Personal protective equipment (PPE) is usually provided. Career progression is limited, but experienced workers may become team leaders or quality controllers.
