## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for film, stage, and related directors and producers in Poland?**
A: In Poland, monthly gross salaries for directors and producers typically range from 6,000 to 15,000 PLN, depending on experience, project scale, and region. Freelancers may have variable income. Major productions in Warsaw often offer higher pay. Earnings can exceed 20,000 PLN for established professionals.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for directors and producers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a national work visa (type D) after securing a job offer and employer-filed work permit. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a job contract meeting a gross salary threshold (currently around 7,500 PLN/month). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a film director or producer in Poland?**
A: Most have a degree in film, theatre, or a related field, but practical experience and a strong portfolio are equally vital. Starting as an assistant or in production roles is common. Networking within Polish film institutions (e.g., PISF) is crucial. Fluency in Polish is essential for local productions, though English may suffice for international projects.
