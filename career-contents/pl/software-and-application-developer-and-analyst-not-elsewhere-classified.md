## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Poland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Mid-level developers earn between PLN 15,000 and PLN 20,000 gross monthly. Senior roles can reach PLN 25,000 or more, while juniors start around PLN 7,000–10,000. Warsaw and Krakow offer higher pay, but cost of living is also higher.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for non-EU software developers to work in Poland?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain a type A work permit (if employed by a Polish company) or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a job offer meeting the salary threshold (150% of average wage), recognized qualifications, and allows family reunification. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are common career progression paths for software developers in Poland?**
A: Developers typically advance from junior to mid-level (3–5 years), then senior (5+ years). They can become team leads, architects, or specialize in fields like data science, cloud computing, or mobile development. Many also transition into management or freelance contracting, with strong demand across IT hubs.
