## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building caretakers in Poland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for building caretakers in Poland ranges from 3,000 to 4,500 PLN. Entry-level positions start around 2,800 PLN, while experienced caretakers in larger buildings or cities like Warsaw may earn up to 5,000 PLN. Salaries often include benefits such as housing or utilities.

**Q: Can building caretakers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Poland?**
A: The EU Blue Card typically requires a university degree or at least five years of professional experience, which may not apply to caretakers. However, they can apply for a standard work permit (type A) if sponsored by an employer. Non-EU citizens must secure a job offer, and the employer must prove no suitable Polish candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for building caretakers in Poland?**
A: Caretakers can progress to building manager or facility supervisor roles after gaining experience and additional certifications, such as fire safety or technical maintenance courses. Some move into property management, overseeing multiple buildings. Language skills in Polish and English enhance opportunities, especially in international companies or residential complexes.
