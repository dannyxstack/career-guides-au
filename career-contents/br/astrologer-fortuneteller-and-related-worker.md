# Astrologer and Fortune-Teller — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 5161 (ISCO08)**

Astrologers, fortune-tellers and related workers provide personal guidance and insights through interpretation of astrological charts, tarot cards, palmistry, or other divinatory methods. They often work on a self-employed basis, offering consultations in person, by phone, or online. In Brazil, this occupation is largely informal, with practitioners relying on word-of-mouth and local reputation. While some may combine their practice with other esoteric services, formal recognition is minimal.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: How much can an astrologer or fortune-teller earn in Brazil?**
A: Income varies widely based on reputation, location, and services offered. Most practitioners earn low to modest wages, often below the national average. High earners are rare and typically have a strong online presence or celebrity status. No official salary data is available due to the informal nature of the work.

**Q: Can foreign astrologers work in Brazil?**
A: It is very difficult. There is no specific visa for this occupation. Work authorization requires employer sponsorship (VITEM V) or a Mercosur residence permit, which is unlikely for a self-employed astrologer. Most foreign practitioners operate informally or under tourist visas, which is illegal for paid work.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an astrologer in Brazil?**
A: No formal qualifications are required. Many practitioners learn through informal courses, mentorship, or self-study. Voluntary certifications from esoteric schools exist but are not recognized by the government. Practical experience and client satisfaction are key.
