# Agricultural and Industrial Machinery Mechanic and Repairer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7233 (ISCO08)**

Agricultural and industrial machinery mechanics and repairers maintain, diagnose, and repair equipment used in farming, construction, and manufacturing. They work on tractors, harvesters, conveyor systems, and industrial machinery, using mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic skills. This occupation is essential for keeping Brazil's agribusiness and industrial sectors operational, with roles ranging from independent workshops to large service centers. Mechanics often specialize in specific brands or types of machinery.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or technical course, leading to junior mechanic roles. With experience, mechanics can become senior technicians, supervisors, or open their own workshops. Specialization in electronics, hydraulics, or specific machinery brands (e.g., John Deere, Caterpillar) enhances prospects. Some advance into sales, technical training, or equipment management. Formal education and certifications from SENAI or similar institutions boost career mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic parameter calibration, assisted by automation tools

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a machinery mechanic in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region, experience, and specialization. Entry-level mechanics may earn around minimum wage, while experienced technicians can earn significantly more, especially in agribusiness hubs. Overtime and travel pay may apply. For official figures, consult the IBGE/PNAD Contínua or CBO databases.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a machinery mechanic in Brazil?**
A: Yes, through employer sponsorship under VITEM V visa. The employer must prove that no equally qualified Brazilian is available. Additionally, Mercosur nationals can work freely under the Mercosur Residence Agreement. The process requires a formal job offer and labor ministry approval.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience, mechanics can become senior technicians, workshop supervisors, or independent business owners. Specializing in electronics or specific machinery brands can lead to higher pay and roles in technical training or sales.
