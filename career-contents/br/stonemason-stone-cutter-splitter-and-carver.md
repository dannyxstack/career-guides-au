# Stonemason — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7113 (ISCO08)**

Stonemasons shape, cut, split, carve, and install natural stone for construction, monuments, and decorative purposes. In Brazil, this traditional craft is essential for luxury residential projects, commercial buildings, and restoration of historic structures. Stonemasons work with granite, marble, limestone, and other stones, using both hand tools and power equipment. The role requires physical stamina, precision, and artistic skill, often involving on-site installation or workshop-based fabrication. While modern construction relies on concrete, demand for natural stone in high-end finishes and cultural heritage preservation sustains the trade.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 73,312. Brazil's stonemasonry sector faces moderate employment prospects. Growth is driven by luxury construction in major cities like São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro, as well as restoration projects of colonial-era buildings. However, mechanization and the use of synthetic materials may limit demand for simple cutting and splitting. Skilled carvers specializing in artistic or heritage work are in particular demand. Overall, employment is expected to remain stable, with opportunities concentrated in regions with active construction and tourism sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as an apprentice or helper, advancing to journeyman stonemason after 3–5 years of experience. Master stonemasons can specialize in restoration, artistic carving, or monumental masonry. Self-employment is common, with experienced workers opening their own workshops or contracting directly for projects. Further training in architectural drawing or project management can lead to supervisory roles. Geographic mobility is often required to follow construction booms.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a stonemason in Brazil?**
A: Entry-level stonemasons earn around BRL 18,000–24,000 annually, while experienced professionals can make BRL 36,000–54,000. Specialists in restoration or artistic carving may earn higher, up to BRL 72,000.

**Q: Can a foreign stonemason obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, through employer sponsorship under VITEM V, but it is not a priority occupation. The employer must demonstrate that no qualified Brazilian worker is available. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can work under the Mercosur Residence Agreement with simpler procedures.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities for stonemasons in Brazil?**
A: Stonemasons can progress to supervisor, specialize in heritage restoration or artistic carving, or start their own contracting business. Additional training in project management or architectural drafting can open doors to higher-level roles.
