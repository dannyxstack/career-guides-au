# Services Manager — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 1439 (ISCO08)**

Services managers in Brazil oversee operations in diverse service sectors such as hospitality, retail, leisure, and personal services, ensuring efficient service delivery, managing staff, and maintaining customer satisfaction.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Services Manager in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and location. For a mid-level manager in São Paulo or Rio de Janeiro, annual pre-tax income is typically between BRL 80,000 and BRL 150,000, with senior roles reaching over BRL 200,000.

**Q: Can a foreign Services Manager obtain a work visa for Brazil without a job offer?**
A: No, Brazil does not have a points-based system. You need a job offer from a Brazilian employer who sponsors your VITEM V or, if you are a Mercosur national, you can apply for the Mercosur Residence Agreement without a prior job offer but must later prove employment.

**Q: What are the most in-demand sub-sectors for Services Managers in Brazil?**
A: The tourism and hospitality sector, particularly in popular destinations like Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, and the Northeast coast, shows strong demand. Retail management and business services, such as facility management, are also growing.
