# Physical And Engineering Science Technician — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3119 (ISCO08)**

Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Brazil perform technical tasks supporting research, development, and production in fields such as physics, chemistry, engineering, and environmental science. They work in laboratories, industrial plants, and field settings, operating equipment, collecting data, conducting tests, and ensuring quality control. Common industries include oil and gas, petrochemicals, renewable energy, manufacturing, and environmental consulting. These technicians are essential for bridging the gap between theoretical science and practical application, often working under the supervision of engineers or scientists. The role requires a combination of manual dexterity, analytical skills, and attention to detail, with opportunities in both urban industrial hubs and remote extraction sites.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Physical and Engineering Science Technician in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and sector. Entry-level technicians earn around R$25,000–R$40,000 annually, while experienced professionals in high-demand fields (e.g., petrochemicals) can earn R$50,000–R$80,000. Technicians with a tecnólogo degree may command higher pay. Income is generally higher in industrial hubs like São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro.

**Q: Can a foreign Physical and Engineering Science Technician obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it requires employer sponsorship via the VITEM V visa. The employer must obtain a prior authorization from the CNIg (National Immigration Council) by proving that no qualified Brazilian worker is available. The process can be lengthy (3-6 months). Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can apply for a residence permit without a job offer, though a local contract is still often necessary.

**Q: What are the main differences between a technical course (Técnico) and a technologist degree (Tecnólogo) for this field?**
A: A Técnico course is offered at high school level (2-3 years) and qualifies you as a technician immediately. A Tecnólogo is an undergraduate degree (3 years) that provides broader theoretical knowledge and qualifies you for more advanced roles or eventual registration as an engineer via supplementary study. Both are recognized by CREA, but the Tecnólogo may offer better career progression.
