# Pharmacist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2262 (ISCO08)**

Pharmacists in Brazil are healthcare professionals who dispense medications, provide pharmaceutical care, and ensure safe drug use. They work in community pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, and pharmaceutical industries, playing a key role in public health. The profession requires a university degree and registration with the Regional Council of Pharmacy (CRF).

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for pharmacists in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and region. Hospital and industrial pharmacists tend to earn higher than retail. Public sector salaries are fixed by law, while private sector offers additional benefits.

**Q: How can a foreign pharmacist obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: The employer must apply for a work permit (VITEM V) through the Ministry of Labor, which requires prior registration with the Regional Pharmacy Council (CRF) and diploma revalidation.

**Q: Is diploma revalidation mandatory for foreign pharmacists?**
A: Yes, foreign degrees must be revalidated by a Brazilian university. The process includes document evaluation and possibly exams, depending on the institution.
