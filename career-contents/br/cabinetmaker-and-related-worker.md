# Cabinet-Maker — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7522 (ISCO08)**

Cabinet-makers and related workers craft, assemble, and repair wooden furniture, cabinets, and fixtures using hand tools and woodworking machinery. They work in workshops, factories, or on construction sites, often specializing in custom pieces for residential or commercial clients. The occupation requires manual dexterity, precision, and knowledge of different wood types and finishing techniques. In Brazil, these professionals are commonly employed in the furniture industry, construction sector, or as independent artisans.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or assistant, advancing to skilled cabinet-maker, then to master craftsman or workshop supervisor. Some specialize in restoration, antique reproduction, or sustainable materials. Entrepreneurial paths include opening a custom furniture business. Continuous learning of new techniques and machinery is advantageous, but formal qualifications are not always required for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom Furniture
- Sustainable Wood Products
- Kitchen & Bath Renovation
- Heritage Restoration

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cabinet-maker in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region; entry-level workers earn around minimum wage, while experienced cabinet-makers in custom shops can earn significantly more, especially in São Paulo and other major cities.

**Q: Can a foreign cabinet-maker get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: It is possible but not common. An employer must sponsor a VITEM V visa, demonstrating that the position cannot be filled locally. Alternatively, nationals of Mercosur countries can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement.
