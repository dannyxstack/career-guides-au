# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons in Brazil prepare detailed technical drawings, plans, and blueprints used in engineering, architecture, and construction projects, often using computer-aided design (CAD) software, and work under the supervision of engineers or architects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior draughtsperson or CAD technician, advancing to senior draughtsperson, lead drafter, or project coordinator. Specialization in sectors like oil and gas, mining, or industrial design can lead to higher pay and supervisory roles. Some move into engineering or architecture after further education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Mining
- Oil and Gas
- Civil Construction

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented individuals who enjoy technical drawing and precision work.
- People comfortable with CAD software and willing to update skills regularly.
- Those who can work collaboratively with engineers and architects on large projects.

**Who is not suited**
- Highly creative or artistic individuals seeking open-ended design roles.
- Those averse to repetitive tasks and strict technical standards.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a draughtsperson in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. A junior draughtsperson may earn around 24,000-36,000 BRL per year, while a senior draughtsperson in oil and gas or mining can earn 60,000-90,000 BRL annually. Pre-tax figures.

**Q: Can a foreign draughtsperson get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is not straightforward. The employer must sponsor a VITEM V visa through the Ministry of Labour (CNIg). The role must require proven specialised skills. Mercosur citizens (Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, and associated states) can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement for easier work rights.

**Q: Is drafting being replaced by AI or software?**
A: Yes, AI and automation are impacting routine drafting tasks. However, draughtspersons who master BIM, 3D modeling, and specialised industry knowledge remain in demand. Roles requiring engineering judgment and coordination are less vulnerable.
