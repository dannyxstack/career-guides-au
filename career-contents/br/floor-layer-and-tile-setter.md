# Floor Layer and Tile Setter — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7122 (ISCO08)**

Floor layers and tile setters install and repair floor coverings such as ceramic tiles, marble, granite, wood, and resilient materials. In Brazil, they are essential in residential, commercial, and industrial construction, ranging from basic housing to high-end projects. The role requires manual dexterity, precision, and knowledge of adhesives, grouts, and cutting tools. While most are employed by construction companies or work as self-employed contractors, experienced professionals can advance to supervisory or specialized restoration roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to journeyman after a few years of experience. With added training, one can specialize in marble, granite, or epoxy flooring, or move into project supervision. Some experienced workers become self-employed contractors, managing their own crews. Further specialization in restoration or historic preservation can open higher-paying niches. Formal qualifications, such as SENAI courses, enhance credibility and may lead to managerial roles in larger construction firms.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic measurement of room dimensions and material quantity calculation, AI can quickly generate optimal layout plans
- Automatic cutting of tiles/stone, CNC waterjet or laser cutting replaces manual cutting
- Automatic detection and adjustment solution generation for ground flatness, reducing manual trial laying
- Inventory management and material ordering processes, AI analyzes project requirements to automatically place orders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to view tiling blueprints and level indicators in real time, improving accuracy
- AI-assisted design of complex patterns and parquet, generating diverse options for clients
- Work collaboratively with automatic mortar mixing and plastering robots to improve efficiency
- AI-driven project progress prediction and scheduling to optimize multi-trade collaboration

**Human moat:**

- Manual cutting and fine placement in irregular areas and corners
- Removal of old flooring, base treatment, and leveling judgment for uneven floors.
- Communicate design preferences with clients and provide personalized advice
- Flexible problem-solving ability for on-site unexpected issues.

**Skills to build:**

- Digital Measurement Tools and BIM Software Operations
- CNC cutting machine programming and maintenance
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools for construction assistance.
- Project management and client communication skills
- Knowledge of sustainable materials and environmentally friendly construction methods
- Basic electrical and mechanical troubleshooting

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a floor layer in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level positions pay around R$18,000–R$24,000 annually, while experienced specialists can earn R$30,000–R$48,000. Self-employed workers may earn more during boom periods but lack benefits.

**Q: Can a foreign floor layer get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: It is difficult unless the worker has specialized skills (e.g., restoration of historic tiles) and an employer sponsor via VITEM V. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can obtain residency more easily. Generally, this occupation is not considered a priority for skilled migration.
