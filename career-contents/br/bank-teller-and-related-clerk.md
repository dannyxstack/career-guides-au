# Bank Teller and Related Clerk — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 4211 (ISCO08)**

Bank tellers and related clerks handle cash transactions, customer inquiries, and routine banking services at branches. They process deposits, withdrawals, payments, and assist with account management. While the role requires basic math and customer service skills, it faces declining demand due to digital banking expansion.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from teller to senior teller, then to customer service representative or branch operations officer. Further advancement may lead to bank manager or relationship manager roles. However, lateral moves into other financial services (e.g., loan processing, compliance) are increasingly common as teller roles diminish.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard transaction processing such as cash deposits, withdrawals, and transfers
- Account inquiry and balance reconciliation
- Transaction record entry and simple report generation
- Customer identity verification and basic anti-money laundering screening

**Where AI augments the role:**

- analyze customer transaction history via AI to provide personalized financial advice
- Leveraging intelligent quality inspection systems to improve compliance and risk control efficiency
- Collaborate with AI customer service to handle complex customer complaints and disputes
- Automate daily report compilation with RPA to focus on high-value customer service.

**Human moat:**

- Understanding complex customer needs and maintaining deep relationships
- Non-standard business judgment (e.g., special problem solving)
- Interpersonal communication skills for emotional perception and trust building.
- On-the-spot flexibility to handle emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Digital banking and mobile sales skills
- Basic programming and RPA tool application.
- Knowledge of financial products (investment, insurance, loans)
- Data analysis and customer insights.
- Complex communication and conflict resolution

**Key growth areas:**

- digitization
- branch consolidation
- automation
- customer advisory

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is a typical salary for a bank teller in Brazil?**
A: While exact figures vary by region and bank, median annual salaries for bank tellers are around R$24,000–R$30,000. However, due to automation, wages have stagnated and are expected to decline in real terms.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa to work as a bank teller in Brazil?**
A: It is highly unlikely. Bank teller roles are considered low-skilled and are not eligible for employer-sponsored work visas (VITEM V). The position can be easily filled locally, and migration authorities rarely approve such applications.
