# Potter — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7314 (ISCO08)**

Potters and related workers shape, mold, and fire clay to create ceramic products such as pottery, tiles, and decorative items, combining traditional handcraft techniques with modern production methods in Brazil.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to skilled potter, then master potter or workshop supervisor. Some specialize in artistic ceramics, teaching, or small business ownership. Industrial settings offer roles in production management, while traditional potteries value multigenerational family knowledge.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use a potter's wheel or molds for batch forming of standardized vessels
- Mechanical reproduction of basic carving and patterns
- Automated monitoring and adjustment of kiln temperature and time
- Automatic calculation and mixing of glaze formulas
- Visual inspection of dimensions and defects in product quality inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quickly design complex ceramic shapes using 3D modeling software
- Use AI to generate glaze formulas and pattern ideas
- Use AR technology to show customers virtual finished ceramic products
- Analyze market data to optimize product design and pricing

**Human moat:**

- Irreproducible personal style and artistic expression in handmade pottery
- Ability for manual adjustments to handle customized, non-standard requirements.
- Deep perception and control over clay material properties
- Inheritance and innovation of traditional craft culture

**Skills to build:**

- 3D modeling and digital sculpting (e.g., Blender, Rhino)
- AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Midjourney for creative prototyping)
- Digital manufacturing equipment operation (3D ceramic printer)
- Social media marketing and online portfolio presentation
- Deepening of traditional handicraft skills (e.g., wheel throwing, painting)
- Customer communication and customised service skills

**Key growth areas:**

- artisan ceramics
- custom pottery
- ceramic tile production
- educational workshops

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a potter in Brazil?**
A: Entry-level potters earn around 18,000-24,000 BRL annually, while experienced artisans in high-demand markets can earn 36,000-48,000 BRL. Industrial settings may offer slightly higher but less creative freedom.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a potter in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. Potters are not on a shortage list, so employers must prove that no Brazilian worker can fill the role. VITEM V visa requires a formal job offer and prior approval from the Ministry of Labour. Mercosur nationals have an easier path via the Mercosur Residence Agreement.

**Q: Is pottery a growing career in Brazil?**
A: Stable with niches. The tourist market for handmade ceramics is growing, especially in regions like Minas Gerais and the Northeast. However, automation in industrial pottery limits broad growth. Best prospects are in bespoke, high-quality artisan work and teaching workshops.
