# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

Metal working machine tool setters and operators set up, operate, and monitor various machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and grinders to cut, shape, and finish metal workpieces. They interpret technical drawings, select cutting tools, adjust machine settings, and ensure precision in manufacturing processes. This occupation is vital to Brazil's industrial sector, particularly in automotive, machinery, and metal fabrication industries, where skilled operators maintain quality and efficiency.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a machine operator, advancing to setter or programmer, then to supervisor or quality control specialist. With additional training, operators can move into CNC programming, tooling design, or maintenance roles. Formal technical education (e.g., mechanics or automation) improves advancement prospects. Brazil's SENAI system offers pathways, and experienced setter-operators may transition to industrial engineering or production management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC programming
- industrial automation
- aerospace manufacturing
- renewable energy components

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for metal working machine tool setters and operators in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level operators earn around R$ 18,000–R$ 24,000 annually, while experienced setters or CNC programmers can earn R$ 36,000–R$ 54,000. Industrial hubs like São Paulo and Minas Gerais pay higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa in Brazil as a metal working machine tool setter?**
A: Yes, but it requires employer sponsorship under VITEM V or CNIg authorization. The employer must prove shortage of local labor. Mercosur citizens can work under the regional agreement without employer sponsorship.

**Q: What training is recommended to become a machine tool operator in Brazil?**
A: A technical course in machining from SENAI (1.5-2 years) or an apprenticeship is recommended. CNC programming skills are increasingly important. NR-12 safety certification is mandatory.
