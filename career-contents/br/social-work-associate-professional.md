# Social Work Associate Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3412 (ISCO08)**

Social work associate professionals in Brazil assist qualified social workers in providing support to individuals, families, and communities facing social challenges. They help clients access social services, benefits, and community resources, often working in public agencies, NGOs, or healthcare settings. Roles include case management support, community outreach, and administrative tasks related to social programs.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a social work associate professional in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and sector. In general, monthly income ranges from R$ 2,000 to R$ 4,500 for entry-level to experienced associates, with public sector roles offering higher stability but often lower starting pay. Exact figures depend on the employer and location.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a social work associate in Brazil?**
A: It is difficult because local professionals fill most positions. Employer sponsorship (VITEM V) is possible if no qualified Brazilian is available, but this is rare. Mercosur nationals may use the residence agreement, but a job offer is still needed.

**Q: How can I advance in this career?**
A: Pursuing a full bachelor's degree in Social Work (Serviço Social) is the main path to becoming a qualified social worker (assistente social), which opens higher-level roles and mandatory CRESS registration. Alternatively, gaining experience and specializing in areas like child protection or gerontology can lead to supervisory positions.
