# Teaching Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

This broad occupational group encompasses various teaching professionals not classified in other specific teaching categories, including private tutors, corporate trainers, vocational instructors, and language teachers. They work in diverse settings such as private homes, companies, training centers, and online platforms. Brazil offers opportunities in this field due to growing demand for specialized and lifelong learning, but requires recognition of foreign credentials and proficiency in Portuguese.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a teaching professional not elsewhere classified in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely: private tutors may earn BRL 30-80 per hour, while corporate trainers can earn BRL 60,000-120,000 annually. Full-time vocational instructors might earn BRL 40,000-80,000, depending on experience and location.

**Q: What visa options exist for foreign teaching professionals in Brazil?**
A: The main routes are the VITEM V work visa (employer-sponsored), the Mercosur Residence Agreement (for citizens of neighboring countries), and CNIg professional authorization for specialized expertise. All require a recognized qualification and often Portuguese proficiency.

**Q: Do I need to revalidate my teaching diploma to work in Brazil?**
A: Yes, for regulated positions in accredited schools, a foreign teaching diploma must be revalidated by a Brazilian university. For private tutoring or corporate training, revalidation may not be mandatory but is often required for formal employment.
