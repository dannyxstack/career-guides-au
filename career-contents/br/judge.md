# Judge — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2612 (ISCO08)**

Judges preside over court proceedings, interpret laws, and deliver verdicts in civil, criminal, and administrative cases in Brazil. They ensure fair trials, manage courtrooms, and issue rulings based on legal principles and evidence. The role requires deep knowledge of Brazilian law, impartiality, and strong decision-making skills. Judges are career public servants selected through rigorous public examinations and typically hold law degrees. They work within the federal or state judiciary and may specialize in areas like labor, electoral, or military law.

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. —. Brazil's judiciary faces a backlog of cases, driving demand for more judges despite budget constraints. Overall, employment growth is slow but stable, with periodic openings as older judges retire.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression follows a strict ladder: from substitute judge to titular judge, then to higher courts (appeals, supreme). Advancement depends on seniority, performance evaluations, and competitive internal exams. Early years involve handling simpler cases; later specialization or administrative roles (e.g., court presidency) are possible. Many judges also teach or write legal opinions. Lateral entry from other legal careers is rare; nearly all start at entry level through public exams.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Legal clauses and case law retrieval
- Drafting standardized legal documents (e.g., judgment templates)
- Evidence classification and initial fact sorting
- Court transcript transcription and summary generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use an AI judge assistant to get real-time legal interpretations and procedural reminders.
- Analyzing case law trends through data analysis to assist sentencing decisions

**Human moat:**

- Courtroom scene management and order maintenance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a judge in Brazil?**
A: Entry-level judges earn around 30,000 BRL per month (pre-tax), rising to over 50,000 BRL for appellate judges. Annual pre-tax salaries typically range from 360,000 BRL to 600,000 BRL, plus benefits and allowances.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a judge in Brazil?**
A: Extremely rare. Brazilian law requires judges to be Brazilian citizens (by birth or naturalization with at least 10 years of residence). Mercosur nationals cannot bypass this. Only exceptional cases like international court appointments allow foreign judges, but they do not enter the career judiciary.

**Q: Is a law degree from abroad recognized for becoming a judge?**
A: Yes, but the degree must be revalidated by a Brazilian university. Additionally, candidates must pass the public exam and meet citizenship requirements. Revalidation can be costly and time-consuming.
