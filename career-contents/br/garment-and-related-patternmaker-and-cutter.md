# Garment and Related Patternmaker and Cutter — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7532 (ISCO08)**

Garment and Related Patternmakers and Cutters create patterns and cut fabric for clothing and textile products, ensuring precise measurements and efficient material use. They work in fashion houses, textile factories, and custom tailoring shops, combining technical skills with artistic sensibility to translate designs into wearable garments. Their role is crucial for quality control and cost management in the apparel industry.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a cutter or assistant patternmaker, advancing to senior patternmaker, head of pattern room, or production supervisor. With experience, some transition into fashion design or open their own atelier. Continuous learning in draping, CAD software, and sustainable materials enhances prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom Tailoring
- Sustainable Fashion
- Athleisure Wear
- High-End Apparel

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garment patternmaker in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region, experience, and sector. Entry-level patternmakers earn around the national minimum wage, while experienced professionals in high-end fashion can earn significantly more. Exact figures are not provided here, but it is generally a mid-range skilled trade income.

**Q: How can a foreign patternmaker get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: A foreign patternmaker typically needs a job offer from a Brazilian employer who sponsors a VITEM V work visa. The employer must demonstrate that no qualified Brazilian worker is available. Alternatively, citizens of Mercosur countries can apply for residence under the Mercosur agreement. The process is lengthy and not guaranteed.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a patternmaker in Brazil?**
A: While formal qualifications are not mandatory, a technical diploma in patternmaking from SENAI or a federal institute is highly beneficial. On-the-job training is common. Certifications improve job prospects and earning potential.
