# Landscape Architect — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2162 (ISCO08)**

Landscape architects in Brazil plan, design, and manage outdoor spaces, combining environmental science, art, and urban planning to create functional and sustainable landscapes. They work on projects ranging from public parks and gardens to urban squares, green roofs, and ecological restoration, often collaborating with engineers, urban planners, and architects. The profession is regulated by the Federal Council of Engineering and Agronomy (CONFEA/CREA).

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a landscape architect in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and sector. Entry-level positions generally offer lower pay, while experienced professionals with CREA registration can earn significantly more. The profession is typically well-compensated compared to national averages, especially in large cities like São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign landscape architects seeking to work in Brazil?**
A: Foreign landscape architects can apply for the VITEM V work visa, which requires employer sponsorship and prior approval from the Ministry of Labor. Mercosur citizens may use the Mercosur Residence Agreement for easier access. All applicants must satisfy CREA registration requirements, which may involve credential validation.

**Q: Is CREA registration mandatory for landscape architects in Brazil?**
A: Yes, CREA registration is mandatory for any landscape architect who signs projects or assumes technical responsibility. Without it, one cannot legally practice as a landscape architect. The process involves holding an accredited degree and paying annual fees.
