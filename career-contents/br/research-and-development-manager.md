# Research and Development Manager — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 1223 (ISCO08)**

Research and Development Managers plan, direct, and coordinate the R&D activities of organizations, overseeing teams that develop new products, processes, or scientific knowledge. In Brazil, they are crucial in industries such as pharmaceuticals, agribusiness, information technology, and renewable energy, often working in corporate labs, universities, or government research institutes. They blend technical expertise with management skills to drive innovation, secure funding, and align research with business strategy. The role typically requires an advanced degree and significant experience in a relevant scientific or engineering field.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression often begins as a senior researcher or project lead, moving into managerial roles after 8–12 years. Many R&D managers hold a master’s or doctorate, and those in multinational companies may rotate through global R&D centers. Senior roles include Director of R&D or Chief Innovation Officer. In academia, progression follows a tenure track. The path rewards interdisciplinary skills, mentorship, and a track record of patents or publications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated analysis of literature reviews and patent searches
- Recording experimental data and performing preliminary statistical analysis
- Automatic generation of project documents (e.g., progress reports, technical memos)
- Regular test case design and execution

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted team collaboration and knowledge management, improving cross-department communication efficiency

**Human moat:**

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Research and Development Manager in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by industry and experience. According to recent data, the annual pre-tax salary ranges from about 150,000 BRL to 400,000 BRL for mid-career professionals, with top earners in pharmaceuticals and technology exceeding 500,000 BRL.

**Q: How can a foreign R&D manager obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: The most common route is the VITEM V temporary work visa, requiring an employer sponsor who proves the need for a foreign professional. For citizens of Mercosur countries, the Mercosur Residence Agreement offers a simpler process without employer sponsorship. Additionally, the CNIg Authorization may be used for highly specialized roles.

**Q: Is a PhD necessary to become an R&D manager in Brazil?**
A: A PhD is not strictly required but is highly valued, especially in research-intensive fields like pharmaceuticals and biotechnology. Many R&D managers hold a master's degree with significant experience. In industrial R&D, a bachelor's with an MBA is also common.
