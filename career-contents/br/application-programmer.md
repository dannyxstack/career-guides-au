# Applications Programmer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2514 (ISCO08)**

Applications programmers write, test, and maintain code for software applications across various platforms. In Brazil, they typically develop solutions for finance, e-commerce, and public sector systems, using languages like Java, Python, and C#. Strong demand exists in São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro tech hubs, with remote work opportunities expanding.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for applications programmers in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, location, and company. Junior programmers earn around BRL 60,000-90,000 annually, mid-level BRL 90,000-150,000, and seniors over BRL 150,000. In São Paulo, salaries are higher but offset by cost of living. Multinationals and fintechs tend to pay above market.

**Q: How can a foreign applications programmer get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: The most common route is an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa, requiring a job offer and approval from the Ministry of Labor. The employer must demonstrate that the role cannot be filled by a local worker. Alternatively, the Mercosur Residence Agreement offers a simpler path for citizens of member countries. Highly skilled professionals may also qualify under CNIg authorization.

**Q: Which programming languages are most in demand for applications programmers in Brazil?**
A: Java and Python are widely used, especially in enterprise and fintech. C# is common in corporate environments. For frontend, JavaScript/TypeScript with React or Angular is sought. Mobile development in Kotlin (Android) and Swift (iOS) is growing. Cloud skills (AWS, Azure) and DevOps knowledge (Docker, Kubernetes) add significant value.
