# Midwifery Associate Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery associate professionals in Brazil provide prenatal, childbirth, and postnatal care under the supervision of obstetricians, predominantly within the public health system (SUS) and private hospitals. They play a vital role in reducing maternal and infant mortality, especially in underserved regions, and are key to the government's primary care expansion strategy.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence the salary of a midwifery associate in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by sector (public vs. private), region (urban vs. remote), and experience. Public sector positions offer stability and benefits, while private hospitals may pay higher base salaries. Additional allowances for working in underserved areas are common.

**Q: Can a foreign midwifery associate work in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but the process is complex. The professional must have their technical diploma validated by a Brazilian university and obtain registration with COFEN. For Mercosur nationals, the process is streamlined via the Mercosur Residence Agreement. Others require an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa, plus authorization from the Ministry of Labor and COFEN.

**Q: Is a bachelor's degree required to practice as a midwifery associate?**
A: No, a technical diploma in nursing (with focus on obstetrics) is sufficient for the associate role. However, a bachelor's degree in nursing allows for greater career mobility, including becoming a registered nurse (enfermeiro obstetra) with broader responsibilities.
