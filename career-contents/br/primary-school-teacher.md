# Primary School Teacher — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2341 (ISCO08)**

Primary School Teachers in Brazil are responsible for educating children from 1st to 5th grade, covering subjects like Portuguese, mathematics, science, history, and arts. They work in public and private schools, following the national curriculum (Base Nacional Comum Curricular). The role requires a degree in pedagogy or a specific teaching license (Licenciatura), and registration with the state education council. Teachers often face large classes and limited resources, especially in public schools. The profession offers stable employment with government benefits for public school teachers, while private schools may offer higher salaries but less job security.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 1,609,951. Brazil faces a growing demand for primary school teachers due to population growth in certain regions and a push for universal early education. However, budget constraints and low salaries in public schools have led to teacher shortages, particularly in rural and low-income areas. The government has implemented programs to attract and retain teachers, such as the 'Pé-de-Meia' scholarship for pedagogy students and the 'Professores para o Futuro' initiative. Despite these efforts, the profession remains challenging, with high turnover in some regions.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a classroom teacher, with opportunities to become a pedagogical coordinator, vice-principal, or principal after additional training and experience. Teachers can also specialize in special education, literacy, or educational technology. Many pursue post-graduate degrees (lato sensu or stricto sensu) to advance. Public school teachers may progress through a career ladder with salary increases based on service time and performance. In the private sector, advancement often involves moving to more prestigious schools or administrative roles.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a primary school teacher in Brazil?**
A: Average annual salary ranges from 30,000 to 60,000 BRL for public school teachers (with benefits), and 40,000 to 80,000 BRL in private schools. Salaries vary by state and experience.

**Q: Can a foreign teacher get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa or, if from a Mercosur country, through the Mercosur Residence Agreement. You need a valid job offer, a recognized degree, and Portuguese proficiency (Celpe-Bras).

**Q: Do I need to validate my foreign teaching degree to work in Brazil?**
A: Yes, foreign degrees must be revalidated by a Brazilian public university or through a mutual recognition agreement. This process can take several months.
