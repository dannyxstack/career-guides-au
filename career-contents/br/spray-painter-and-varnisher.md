# Spray Painters and Varnishers — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7132 (ISCO08)**

Spray painters and varnishers apply paint, varnish, and other protective coatings to surfaces using spray guns and related equipment. They work in various industries including automotive refinishing, industrial manufacturing, construction, and furniture production. The role requires knowledge of paint types, surface preparation, masking techniques, and safety procedures to ensure a smooth, durable finish. In Brazil, these professionals often find employment in auto body shops, shipyards, and painting contractors. The occupation demands attention to detail, physical stamina, and the ability to work with potentially hazardous materials while following environmental regulations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, followed by certification as a qualified painter. Experienced workers can become lead painters, supervisors, or quality inspectors. Some specialize in areas like automotive refinishing, industrial coating, or artistic painting. Entrepreneurial painters may open their own businesses. Continuous learning of new materials and application methods is essential for advancement. The occupation offers moderate upward mobility, particularly for those who acquire multiple certifications and demonstrate reliability and precision.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic spraying of batch products.
- Repetitive paint sanding and polishing
- Simple color matching based on preset programs
- Manual adjustment of paint film thickness uniformity
- Routine visual quality inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for spray painters in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region, experience, and sector. For current data, consult official sources such as the Ministry of Labor's CAGED or the PNAD Contínua survey.

**Q: Can a foreign spray painter obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, through employer-sponsored VITEM V visa or Mercosur Residence if from a member country. The employer must prove that the position requires specialized skills not readily available locally.

**Q: What training is needed to become a spray painter in Brazil?**
A: A vocational course from SENAI or similar institution is common, often requiring only basic education. On-the-job training is also typical. Certifications like NR-35 may be required for certain work environments.
