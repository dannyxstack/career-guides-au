# Sweeper — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 9613 (ISCO08)**

Sweepers and related labourers perform manual cleaning of streets, parks, markets, and other public or commercial spaces. They use brooms, shovels, and bins to collect litter, leaves, and debris, and may also operate simple cleaning equipment. This occupation requires physical stamina and the ability to work outdoors in various weather conditions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for sweepers is limited. With experience, some may become team leaders or supervisors in cleaning companies. Others move into related fields such as waste collection, janitorial services, or maintenance. Formal education or vocational training in environmental sanitation can open opportunities in public service or private facility management, though such paths require additional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

- Basic Chemistry and Disinfectant Knowledge
- Data recording and analysis skills
- Communication and customer service skills

**Key growth areas:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sweeper in Brazil?**
A: Salaries are usually close to the minimum wage (around R$1,320 per month in 2024), varying by region and employer. Annual pre-tax income typically ranges from R$15,840 to R$21,600, with some benefits like transportation vouchers.

**Q: Can foreigners work as sweepers in Brazil?**
A: It is difficult because the occupation is low-skilled and employers rarely sponsor work visas. However, nationals of Mercosur countries can apply for residence under the Mercosur Agreement if they have a job offer. The VITEM V visa is generally not an option for this role.

**Q: Are there opportunities for career growth as a sweeper?**
A: Limited. With experience, you may become a team supervisor or move into related areas like waste management or facility maintenance. Additional training in environmental sanitation can improve prospects.
