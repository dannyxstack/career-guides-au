# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 5152 (ISCO08)**

Domestic housekeepers are responsible for cleaning and maintaining private households, including tasks such as sweeping, mopping, dusting, laundry, and organizing. They may also assist with meal preparation and pet care. In Brazil, this occupation is predominantly informal, with many workers lacking formal contracts. Demand is steady due to urbanization and dual-income households, but wages are low and job security limited.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression for domestic housekeepers is limited. Some may advance to supervisory roles in cleaning companies or start independent cleaning businesses. Formal training programs offered by SENAC can improve skills and employability. However, most workers remain in entry-level positions due to low educational requirements and lack of structured career paths. Migration from rural areas to cities is common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Vacuuming/mopping floors (replaced by robotic vacuums/mops)
- Dishwashing (replaced by dishwashers)
- Bed making, clothing folding (replaced by smart bed-making robots/folding machines)
- Regular surface dust removal (replaced by automated dusting robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling tools to plan cleaning routes and task priorities
- Monitoring appliance status and scheduling repairs using smart home systems
- Remote control of cleaning equipment via voice assistant or APP
- Use data analytics to optimize cleaning supplies inventory and procurement

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex cleaning issues such as accidental damage and special material stains
- Communicate with employers about customized service needs and build trust
- Manage multiple smart devices and coordinate troubleshooting.
- Engaging in fine manual work (e.g., antique furniture maintenance)

**Skills to build:**

- Smart home device operation and basic troubleshooting
- Basic data analysis (supply inventory management)
- Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Time management and multitasking coordination
- Knowledge of eco-friendly cleaning technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- urbanization
- aging population
- dual-income households
- cleaning services

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region and formality. As of recent data, the minimum wage (about R$1,320/month in 2024) is common, with informal workers often earning less. Formal workers receive additional benefits like paid leave and 13th salary.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for domestic housekeeping in Brazil?**
A: It is difficult. The VITEM V visa requires employer sponsorship and proof that the worker has specialized skills not available locally, which is rare for housekeeping. However, Mercosur nationals can obtain residence without employer sponsorship.

**Q: How can a domestic housekeeper advance in Brazil?**
A: Advancement is limited but possible through specialization (e.g., eco-friendly cleaning), starting a small cleaning business, or taking courses at SENAC for supervisory roles in cleaning companies.
