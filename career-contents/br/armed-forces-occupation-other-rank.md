# Armed Forces Other Ranks — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

Armed Forces Other Ranks are enlisted personnel in Brazil's military branches (Army, Navy, Air Force) who perform operational, security, and support duties under officer command. They undergo rigorous basic training and serve in roles such as infantry, logistics, communications, or engineering. This career requires Brazilian citizenship, high physical fitness, discipline, and a commitment to national service. Entry is via public recruitment, with advancement through ranks based on merit and time in service.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Armed Forces Other Ranks in Brazil?**
A: Salaries for Other Ranks range from approximately 30,000 BRL to 72,000 BRL annually (pre-tax), depending on rank, tenure, and branch. Benefits include housing allowance, healthcare, and a generous pension.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa to serve as Other Ranks in Brazil?**
A: No. Active-duty enlisted positions in the Brazilian Armed Forces are exclusively for Brazilian citizens. Foreigners may only serve in limited technical advisory roles under special government agreements, typically not as enlisted personnel.

**Q: What is the career progression like for Other Ranks?**
A: Advancement is based on time in service, performance, and completion of required courses. Typical promotions include private to corporal, sergeant, and warrant officer. Specialized training in technical fields can speed progression.
