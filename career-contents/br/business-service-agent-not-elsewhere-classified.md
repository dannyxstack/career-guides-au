# Business Services Agent — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3339 (ISCO08)**

Business Services Agents facilitate commercial transactions, debt collection, purchasing, and other business support services for companies across multiple sectors in Brazil. They act as intermediaries between buyers and sellers, manage accounts receivable, and negotiate contracts, often working in financial institutions, trading companies, or service providers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically advances from junior agent to senior analyst, team leader, or manager in commercial or credit departments. Specialization in areas like credit risk, procurement, or international trade can lead to higher-level roles such as commercial director or supply chain manager. Continuous training in new technologies and regulations is beneficial.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generates standard documents such as business registration forms and permit applications.
- Preliminary screening and matching of potential partners/clients (based on databases and AI recommendations)

- Administrative tasks such as appointments, meeting minutes, email replies, etc.

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Business Services Agent in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector (e.g., finance, trade) and experience. Entry-level agents earn around the national median, while senior specialists or managers can earn significantly more. Benefits often include performance bonuses.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as Business Services Agents in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but they need an employer-sponsored work visa (VITEM V) or, if from Mercosur countries, can apply for residence under the Mercosur Agreement. The process requires a job offer and proof of qualifications.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities?**
A: Agents can move into specialized roles like credit analyst or procurement manager, or into supervisory positions. Further education in business or finance can lead to senior management.
