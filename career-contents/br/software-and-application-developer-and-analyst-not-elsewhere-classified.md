# Software and Applications Developer and Analyst — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2519 (ISCO08)**

Software and Applications Developers and Analysts design, develop, test, and maintain software applications. They work in various industries, from finance to agriculture, using programming languages, frameworks, and tools to create solutions. In Brazil, the role is critical for digital transformation, with demand rising in fintech, agritech, and e-commerce. Professionals often work in teams, following agile methodologies, and may specialize in web, mobile, or cloud development.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from junior developer to mid-level, then senior developer, tech lead, or architect. Many move into product management or entrepreneurship. Continuous learning is essential, with certifications in cloud (AWS, Azure) or agile methodologies boosting employability. Salaries increase significantly with experience, and top developers can command high incomes, especially in São Paulo or in remote roles for international companies.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and location. Junior developers earn around 40,000-80,000 BRL annually, while senior developers can earn 150,000-250,000 BRL or more, especially in major tech hubs like São Paulo.

**Q: Do I need an employer sponsor to work as a software developer in Brazil?**
A: Yes, typically you need a job offer from a Brazilian company that can sponsor your VITEM V work visa. Alternatively, if you are from a Mercosur country, you can apply for residence under the Mercosur Agreement. Digital nomads can also apply for a specific visa.

**Q: Is software development a growing field in Brazil?**
A: Absolutely. Driven by digital transformation, the demand for software developers continues to grow across all sectors, especially fintech, agritech, and e-commerce.
