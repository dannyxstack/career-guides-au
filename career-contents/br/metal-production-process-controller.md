# Metal Production Process Controller — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3135 (ISCO08)**

Metal Production Process Controllers monitor and control the operation of industrial machinery used in metal production, ensuring efficient and safe processing in industries such as steelmaking, aluminum smelting, and foundries. They oversee processes like casting, rolling, and heat treatment, adjusting parameters to meet quality and production targets. This role requires strong technical knowledge, attention to detail, and adherence to safety protocols.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant operator, advancing to senior controller or shift supervisor. Specializations in continuous casting, rolling mills, or quality control offer further growth. With additional education (e.g., metallurgical engineering), roles can move into process optimization or management. Technical training from SENAI or similar is common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- monitor and adjust parameters (temperature, pressure, etc.) of smelting and casting processes
- Records production data and writes reports.
- Perform standardized equipment inspections
- Partial fault diagnosis and alarm response

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI prediction models to optimize process parameters and energy consumption.
- Remote monitoring and simulation drills via digital twins
- Use automated reporting tools to focus on anomaly analysis
- Collaborate with AI for preventive equipment maintenance planning.

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and emergency handling of complex faults
- Experience in cross-process craft adjustments.
- Non-standard coordination with maintenance and quality inspection teams
- On-site judgment and decision-making for security violations
- Intuitive operation of old equipment

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial IoT and sensor data analysis
- PLC/DCS system programming and maintenance
- Python or R for production data analysis
- Application of AI predictive maintenance and fault detection tools
- Process optimization and lean management methods
- Cybersecurity fundamentals (industrial control systems).

**Key growth areas:**

- Automation
- Steelmaking
- Continuous Casting
- Quality Control

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Metal Production Process Controller in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience; in industrial hubs like Minas Gerais or São Paulo, they range from mid-level to high compared to the national average, but exact figures depend on the specific industry and employer.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Metal Production Process Controller in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it requires employer sponsorship and proof of specialized skills. The VITEM V visa is typical. Mercosur nationals have an easier pathway through the Residence Agreement.

**Q: What qualifications are needed?**
A: A technical diploma in metallurgy from a recognized institution like SENAI is often mandatory, along with safety certifications like NR-12.
