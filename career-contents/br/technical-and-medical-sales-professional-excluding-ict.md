# Technical and Medical Sales Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2433 (ISCO08)**

Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Brazil promote and sell sophisticated products and services to businesses, hospitals, clinics, and laboratories, requiring in-depth knowledge of both the technical attributes and the market applications. They bridge the gap between producers and end-users in industries such as pharmaceuticals, medical devices, biotechnology, and industrial equipment. Strong communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills are essential, as is the ability to understand complex regulatory environments. While the role is primarily commercial, success depends on technical credibility, making continuous learning and adaptation key. Many professionals hold degrees in relevant engineering, health sciences, or business fields, and careers often feature performance-based compensation and high mobility.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can foreign professionals easily obtain a work visa for technical and medical sales positions in Brazil?**
A: Work visa sponsorship for sales roles is less common because the occupation typically requires local market knowledge and Portuguese fluency. The VITEM V visa requires employer sponsorship and proof that no suitable Brazilian candidate is available, which can be challenging. Mercosur nationals have an easier path through the residence agreement. Overall, this is not considered a straightforward migration route.

**Q: What salary range can a technical or medical sales professional expect in Brazil?**
A: Compensation varies widely by sector, experience, and commission structures. Entry-level positions may offer around BRL 50,000 to 80,000 annually, while senior roles with strong performance can exceed BRL 200,000. Commissions and bonuses often constitute a significant portion of total earnings. Exact figures should be verified through official salary surveys and job postings.
