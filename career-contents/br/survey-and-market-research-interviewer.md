# Survey and Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and Market Research Interviewers collect primary data from individuals or groups for marketing, social, opinion, or government research. They conduct interviews via telephone, online platforms, or in person, following structured questionnaires to ensure accuracy and consistency. This role is essential for understanding consumer behavior, public opinion, and societal trends, supporting decision-making in business and public policy.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically moves from interviewer to team supervisor, quality control analyst, or research coordinator. With experience and additional training, interviewers can transition into market research analysis, data science, or project management. However, the role is often entry-level with high turnover; advancement depends on performance and further education in statistics or marketing.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Surveys
- Customer Feedback
- Telephone Interviews
- Social Research

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a survey interviewer in Brazil?**
A: Salaries are typically low, around R$1,500 to R$2,500 per month (pre-tax), often with performance bonuses. Annual figures range from R$18,000 to R$30,000.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as survey interviewers in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is rare and usually requires employer sponsorship under VITEM V if the role is specialised. Mercosur citizens can work under the residence agreement without a visa.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for interviewers?**
A: With experience, interviewers can move into supervisory roles, quality assurance, or research analysis. Further education in statistics or marketing enhances prospects.
