# Statistical, Mathematical and Related Associate Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3314 (ISCO08)**

Statistical, mathematical, and related associate professionals support statisticians and data scientists by collecting, processing, and analyzing numerical data, performing mathematical calculations, and preparing reports. They work in diverse sectors such as government, finance, market research, and healthcare, contributing to evidence-based decision-making. In Brazil, these roles often require technical education in statistics or mathematics, with opportunities in both public and private sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant or trainee, advancing to senior analyst, team leader, or specialist roles in areas like data science, market research, or actuarial science. Further education, such as a bachelor's or postgraduate degree, can lead to becoming a full statistician or data scientist. Some professionals move into management positions or consulting. Continuous skill development in programming (R, Python) and statistical software is beneficial for career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Data cleaning and preprocessing (e.g., missing value imputation, outlier detection)
- Automatic execution of basic statistical tests (e.g., t-test, chi-square test)
- Standardized report and dashboard generation (e.g., monthly sales statistics)
- Automatic fitting of simple regression models and predictions

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AutoML tools to quickly iterate complex models and improve experimental efficiency
- Query databases or statistical software via natural language, lowering technical barriers
- AI-assisted generation of data stories and visualization suggestions to enhance presentation effectiveness.
- Automated anomaly monitoring and early warning systems allowing analysts to focus on root causes.

**Human moat:**

- Business understanding: translating statistical results into actionable business recommendations
- Causal inference: designing experiments, distinguishing correlation vs. causality
- Complex model customization: adjusting algorithms or designing new metrics for non-standard problems
- Communication and persuasion: explaining analysis results to non-technical personnel and gaining trust.
- Ethics and compliance: ensuring data use and modeling comply with regulations (e.g., GDPR).

**Skills to build:**

- Python/R programming (focus on pandas, scikit-learn)
- SQL and big data platforms (e.g., Spark, BigQuery)
- Machine learning basics (classification, regression, clustering, hyperparameter tuning)
- Generative AI tools (such as ChatGPT for code assistance, report generation)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI or Python plotting libraries)
- Causal inference methods (e.g., DAG, instrumental variables)

**Key growth areas:**

- Data Analytics
- Market Research
- Public Policy
- Financial Modeling

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Brazil?**
A: Salaries for statistical and mathematical associate professionals in Brazil vary by sector and experience. Entry-level positions typically earn between 30,000 and 50,000 BRL per year, while senior roles can reach 80,000 BRL or more, especially in finance and data analytics.

**Q: Can foreign professionals work in this occupation in Brazil?**
A: Yes, foreign professionals can work as statistical associate professionals in Brazil through employer sponsorship under VITEM V visa. However, employers must prove that no local qualified worker is available, and the occupation is not on the national shortage list, making the process more restrictive.

**Q: What education is required to work in this field in Brazil?**
A: A technical course in statistics (2-3 years) is the minimum requirement for associate positions. A bachelor's degree in statistics, mathematics, or related fields provides better opportunities and is often preferred by employers.
