# Statistical, Finance and Insurance Clerk — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 4312 (ISCO08)**

Statistical, finance and insurance clerks perform a range of clerical duties related to financial transactions, insurance claims, and statistical data. They compile records, process applications, calculate premiums, and maintain databases, often working in banks, insurance companies, or government agencies. This role requires strong numerical skills, attention to detail, and familiarity with financial software.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts with data entry or claims processing, advancing to senior clerk, team leader, or supervisor roles. Further specialization can lead to positions in underwriting, risk analysis, or compliance. Many clerks pursue certifications (e.g., CPA-20) or higher education in finance to move into analyst roles. Lateral moves to banking or insurance sales are also common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Extracting and entering data from formatted tables or documents
- Preliminary review and basic information verification of insurance claims
- Generate standard statistical reports and financial summaries
- Handling routine premium calculations and claim amount verification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Utilize AI for automatic flagging and preliminary analysis of abnormal transactions or claims
- Quickly retrieve historical cases and policy clauses using natural language query tools
- Generating dynamic visual dashboards through automated reporting platforms
- Using RPA to connect multiple systems for cross-platform data validation and reconciliation
- Use machine learning to predict risk scores, assisting human decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, ambiguous, or anomalous data situations, such as non-compliant claims
- Communicate complex issues with clients or internal departments, providing explanations and recommendations.
- Understand and comply with constantly changing financial and insurance regulations
- Judging business substance behind data anomalies based on industry experience

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and automation scripting in Python or R
- Process design using low-code/RPA tools (e.g. UiPath)
- SQL data querying and database management
- Basic machine learning concepts and model applications (e.g., anomaly detection)
- Knowledge of financial and insurance products and compliance interpretation
- Data visualisation tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).

**Key growth areas:**

- fintech
- insurtech
- compliance
- data analysis

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Statistical, Finance and Insurance Clerk in Brazil?**
A: The average annual salary ranges from BRL 25,000 to BRL 45,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level clerks earn around BRL 20,000, while senior clerks can earn up to BRL 55,000. Salaries are higher in São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Statistical, Finance and Insurance Clerk in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but work authorization is employer-sponsored. The most common visa is VITEM V, which requires a job offer and approval from the Ministry of Labor. Mercosur nationals can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement. However, the occupation is not considered high-skilled, so visa approval may be more difficult compared to tech or engineering roles.

**Q: Is this job at risk of automation?**
A: Yes, many routine tasks (like data entry and basic calculations) are increasingly automated. However, roles requiring client interaction, complex problem-solving, and oversight are less vulnerable. Upskilling in data analysis or compliance can mitigate automation risk.
