# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers clerical support workers performing general administrative duties not classified elsewhere, such as filing, data entry, mail handling, and basic office coordination. They work in various sectors including business, government, and services, often as administrative assistants, office clerks, or receptionists. Their role is critical for office efficiency, though tasks are increasingly automated.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression often involves moving into specialized administrative roles (e.g., executive assistant, office manager) or transitioning to other fields like human resources or accounting after gaining experience. Upskilling in software (ERP, Excel), languages, and project management can enhance prospects. Some advance to supervisory positions, but the path is not well-defined and depends on sector and employer size.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- Administrative Support
- Digital Transformation
- Remote Work
- Small Business Services

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a clerical support worker in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region and sector, but typically range from R$1,500 to R$3,000 per month for entry-level positions. Specialized roles in large companies may pay higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for a clerical support job in Brazil?**
A: It is difficult because these jobs are not considered high-skilled. Employer sponsorship under VITEM V is possible but rarely approved for clerical roles. Mercosur nationals have an easier path.

**Q: What are common career paths for clerical workers in Brazil?**
A: Many advance to executive assistant, office manager, or move into HR, finance, or procurement after gaining experience and additional qualifications.
