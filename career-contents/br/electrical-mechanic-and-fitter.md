# Electrical Mechanic and Fitter — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7412 (ISCO08)**

Electrical mechanics and fitters install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair electrical machinery and equipment in industrial, commercial, and residential settings. They work with motors, generators, transformers, switchgear, and control systems, often in manufacturing plants, construction sites, or utility companies. The role combines hands-on electrical work with reading schematics and using testing instruments, requiring a blend of technical knowledge and practical skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or technical course, leading to junior mechanic. With experience, one can advance to senior mechanic, team leader, or specialized roles in automation, high-voltage systems, or renewable energy. Further education (e.g., electrical engineering technology) can open doors to supervisory or design positions. Continuous learning is key due to evolving technology, especially in smart grids and industry 4.0.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Electrical system testing and fault diagnosis, AI automatically analyzes data and outputs maintenance recommendations
- Routine maintenance records and compliance document generation, with AI automatically reporting from sensor data.
- Some repetitive wiring and assembly tasks can be programmed by robots
- Spare parts inventory management and ordering, with AI predicting demand and automatically placing orders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-enhanced fault diagnosis tools quickly locate issues, improving repair efficiency
- Augmented reality (AR) helmet displays wiring diagrams and construction instructions, reducing errors
- AI predictive maintenance analyzes equipment lifespan, optimizes repair schedules
- Cloud-based collaboration platforms share best practices in real time, accelerating troubleshooting

**Human moat:**

- On-site commissioning and adaptive repair of complex electrical systems
- Manual operation judgment in high-risk environments (live work, flammable locations)
- Creative solutions for non-standard modifications and custom installations
- Building trust by communicating with clients about needs and explaining technical solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and sensor integration
- Intelligent motor controller (PLC/VFD) programming
- Use of AI tools for fault prediction and root cause analysis
- Electrical CAD (e.g., AutoCAD Electrical) and AR-assisted systems
- Data-driven maintenance strategy design
- Installation and commissioning of renewable energy systems (solar/storage)

**Key growth areas:**

- Industrial automation
- Renewable energy
- Infrastructure expansion
- Electric vehicle maintenance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an electrical mechanic in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level technicians earn around BRL 25,000-35,000 annually, while senior roles can reach BRL 60,000-80,000.

**Q: Can foreign electrical mechanics easily get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Employer sponsorship is required through the VITEM V visa. The process includes a labor market test and CNIg approval. Demand is high in industrial sectors, so opportunities exist but require a job offer.

**Q: What certifications are essential?**
A: The NR-10 safety certification is mandatory for all electrical work. A technical diploma from SENAI is highly valued.
