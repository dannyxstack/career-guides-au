# House Builder — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7111 (ISCO08)**

House builders construct, repair, and renovate residential buildings using materials such as brick, concrete, and wood. They interpret blueprints, ensure structural integrity, and comply with safety regulations. In Brazil, this occupation is largely informal but offers steady demand in urban areas through new construction and renovation projects tied to housing programs and economic cycles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as an apprentice, advancing to journeyman and master builder. Specialization in finishing, restoration, or green building offers vertical paths. Many house builders become independent contractors or small business owners. Formal recognition through SENAI courses can improve mobility and wages.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic generation of bill of materials based on CAD drawings
- Basic building structure calculations and optimization (e.g., load-bearing analysis)
- Construction site safety monitoring and anomaly alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to generate multiple design blueprint options for client selection
- Collision detection and construction simulation optimization through BIM+AI
- Using drones and AI for rooftop inspections and damage identification
- Use machine learning to predict material waste and project delays

**Human moat:**

- On-site fine operations such as manual bricklaying, plastering, and carpentry
- Understanding of individual client needs and designing tailored solutions
- Emergency response to unexpected issues on complex construction sites

**Skills to build:**

- BIM software (e.g., Revit) operation.
- Drone operation and image analysis.
- Basic Programming & AI Collaboration Tools (e.g., ChatGPT)
- Knowledge of green building and energy-saving technologies.
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban expansion
- Renovation
- Social housing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a house builder in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region and formality. Entry-level workers earn around the minimum wage (about 1,320 BRL per month in 2023), while experienced master builders can earn 2,500–4,000 BRL per month in formal construction companies. Self-employed workers may earn more per project but lack benefits.

**Q: Can a foreign house builder get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: It is uncommon because the occupation is not designated as skilled migration. Work authorization requires a company sponsorship (VITEM V) proving no local worker is available, which is rarely granted for house builders. Mercosur nationals may work under the residence agreement, but formal job offers are still needed.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for house builders?**
A: Typical progression is from apprentice to journeyman to master builder. Specializations in restoration, green building, or safety supervision can lead to higher pay. Many eventually start their own contracting businesses, which offers greater income potential but requires business management skills.
