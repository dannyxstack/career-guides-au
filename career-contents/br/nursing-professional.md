# Nursing Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2221 (ISCO08)**

Nursing professionals provide comprehensive healthcare services, including patient assessment, treatment planning, medication administration, and health education, working in hospitals, clinics, and community settings across Brazil.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 570,399. Brazil faces a growing demand for nurses driven by an aging population, expansion of primary healthcare, and increased focus on pandemic preparedness. However, public sector budget constraints and regional disparities in healthcare access create an uneven employment landscape. Opportunities are strongest in the Southeast and in specialized areas like intensive care, geriatrics, and home care.

Specialization in areas like oncology, pediatrics, or emergency care opens pathways to clinical leadership, while advanced degrees lead to teaching or research positions in universities and hospitals.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for nursing professionals in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region, experience, and specialization. In general, nurses earn a moderate income compared to other professions, with higher pay in private hospitals and urban centers. Government positions offer stability but lower starting pay.

**Q: How can a foreign nurse obtain a work visa in Brazil?**
A: The most common route is the VITEM V temporary work visa, which requires an employer sponsor. The applicant must have their nursing degree validated by a Brazilian university and obtain COREN registration. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement, but still need professional revalidation.

**Q: Is a Brazilian nursing degree recognized abroad?**
A: Recognition depends on the destination country. Brazilian nursing programs are generally well-regarded, but graduates may need to pass equivalency exams or additional training to practice in countries like the US, Canada, or the UK.
