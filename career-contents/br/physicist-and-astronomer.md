# Physicist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2111 (ISCO08)**

Physicists research fundamental laws governing matter, energy, space, and time, applying their findings to advance technology, medicine, and industry. In Brazil, they work in academia, research institutes (e.g., CNPq, CBPF), and sectors like oil & gas, renewable energy, and aerospace. Astronomers study celestial phenomena and often collaborate with international observatories. The role demands strong analytical, mathematical, and computational skills, with a typical career path from research assistant to senior scientist or professor.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 2,682. Brazil's employment outlook for physicists is stable but niche. Growth areas include renewable energy (solar, wind), oil & gas exploration (deep-water), space research (INPE), and data science. Academia remains the largest employer, but competition for tenure-track positions is high. Government funding for research influences opportunities. Private sector demand is growing in tech and energy, but overall hiring volume is limited compared to engineering. The occupation is not on official shortage lists.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physicists in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Entry-level physicists earn around BRL 50,000 annually in academia, while senior professionals in oil & gas can exceed BRL 200,000. Public sector researchers have fixed scales.

**Q: Can a foreign physicist work in Brazil without a specific visa?**
A: No, unless they are from a Mercosur country and use the Mercosur Residence Agreement. Otherwise, employer-sponsored VITEM V or CNIg authorization is required. Self-employment or freelance work is difficult without an employment link.

**Q: Is a PhD mandatory for a physics career in Brazil?**
A: Not for all roles, but it is essential for university research and teaching. In private industry, a master's degree may suffice for some positions, but a PhD gives a significant advantage.
