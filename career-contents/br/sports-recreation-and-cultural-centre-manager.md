# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 1431 (ISCO08)**

Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers plan, organize, direct and coordinate the operations of facilities such as sports stadiums, fitness centers, cultural venues, and recreation clubs. They oversee staff, budgets, programming, marketing, and ensure compliance with safety regulations. In Brazil, these managers work in public and private facilities, often requiring strong leadership and business acumen.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Sports Centre Manager in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and facility size. In major cities, managers earn between BRL 60,000 and BRL 120,000 annually, while in smaller towns it may be BRL 40,000 to BRL 80,000. Public sector roles often have lower but more stable pay.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Sports Centre Manager in Brazil?**
A: Yes, through employer sponsorship under VITEM V visa. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can use the residence agreement. The employer must prove the foreigner has specialized expertise and that no Brazilian candidate is available.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for this role in Brazil?**
A: With experience, managers can move to regional director or executive positions in large chains. Some transition to consulting or open their own facilities. Specializing in high-demand areas like fitness or event management can accelerate growth.
