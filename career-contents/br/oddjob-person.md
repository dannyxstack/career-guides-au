# Odd-job Person — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 9622 (ISCO08)**

Odd-job persons perform a variety of simple, routine, and manual tasks on a casual or temporary basis, such as cleaning, gardening, moving goods, or assisting tradespeople. This occupation is characterized by high informality, low entry barriers, and unstable employment in Brazil's labour market.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is minimal for odd-job persons without additional training. Workers may move into more specialized skilled trades (e.g., construction, cleaning services) or become micro-entrepreneurs. Formalization through cooperatives or digital platforms (e.g., app-based jobs) offers some upward mobility, but most remain in casual, low-wage positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic cleaning robots and drones replace sweeping and window cleaning
- AI scheduling system replaces manual allocation of gig tasks
- Autonomous mobile robots replace manual handling of heavy objects.
- Online platforms replace intermediaries in matching temporary work
- Smart gardening robots replace pruning and weeding

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Smart tools enhance cleaning efficiency (e.g., automatic stain detection)
- AI-assisted optimal route planning
- Online platforms expand opportunities for receiving orders
- Wearable devices to monitor health and safety
- AI training system quickly learns new skills

**Human moat:**

- Handle unexpected and complex cleaning tasks (e.g., special stains)
- Requires interpersonal communication and trust (e.g., family services)
- Handling unstructured environments (e.g., rooms cluttered with items).
- Flexibly adapt to ad hoc client needs
- High-risk or sensitive scenarios (e.g., medical waste disposal)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic equipment operation (e.g., intelligent cleaning robots)
- Use of digital platforms (e.g., order-taking apps)
- Customer Service and Communication
- Time management and multitasking
- Basic repair skills (handling simple faults)
- Health and safety knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- urban services
- gig economy
- cleaning
- handyman services

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an odd-job person in Brazil?**
A: Odd-job persons typically earn between R$ 1,000 and R$ 1,500 per month (often less), with no guaranteed minimum as work is casual and informal.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as an odd-job person in Brazil?**
A: No, this occupation does not qualify for any work visa (VITEM V, Mercosur Residence, or other). Only high-skilled or specialized roles with employer sponsorship are eligible.

**Q: Can an odd-job person become a formal employee?**
A: Yes, but only through skill upgrades (e.g., vocational training in cleaning, maintenance) to qualify for formal positions in service companies, often with CNPJ (micro-entrepreneur).
