# Bicycle And Related Repairer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Bicycle and related repairers maintain, repair, and customize bicycles and similar vehicles, including electric bikes, in shops, workshops, or mobile services. They diagnose mechanical issues, replace parts, tune gears and brakes, and provide advice on bicycle care. In Brazil, this occupation is growing alongside the cycling culture, especially in urban areas and recreational regions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level workers often start as assistants or apprentices, learning from experienced mechanics. Progression includes becoming a senior repairer, supervisor, or shop manager. Specialization in electric bike systems, suspension, or custom builds is a path to higher earnings. Some open their own repair shops after gaining experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic software to automatically identify common faults (e.g., circuit issues)
- Handle customer appointments and simple inquiries via chatbot
- Inventory management and parts ordering automated by AI system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of complex electrical faults (e.g., electric bicycle battery management systems)
- AR glasses overlay repair steps and exploded parts diagrams to improve repair accuracy
- Customer needs analysis: AI recommends personalised upgrade options (e.g. performance modifications)
- Remote expert collaboration: obtaining real-time support via AI platforms

**Human moat:**

- Manual fine-tuning (such as wheel truing, suspension adjustment)
- Assess unusual damage and creatively repair
- Face-to-face communication with clients to understand cycling preferences and customize solutions
- Handling replacement and adaptation of old or non-standard parts

**Skills to build:**

- Diagnosis and repair of electric bicycle electrical systems
- Proficient in using digital diagnostic tools and repair apps
- 3D printing part production and repair
- Customer relationship management and personalized service design
- Basic programming skills (e.g., MES system operation in repair stations)
- Sustainable repair concepts (e.g., battery recycling and upgrades)

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric bicycle repair
- suspension tuning
- wheel building
- bike fitting

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What factors affect the salary of a bicycle repairer in Brazil?**
A: Salary depends on experience, location (urban vs rural), type of employer (independent shop vs large retailer), and specialization (e.g., e-bike repair). Income is generally low to moderate, with experienced mechanics earning more.

**Q: Can a foreign bicycle repairer obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: It is difficult because bicycle repair is not considered highly skilled. Employer-sponsored VITEM V is possible but rare. Mercosur nationals may enter under the Residence Agreement for informal work, but formal employment is limited.
