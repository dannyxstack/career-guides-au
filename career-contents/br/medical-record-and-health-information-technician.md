# Medical Records and Health Information Technician — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3252 (ISCO08)**

Medical records and health information technicians organize, manage, and code patient data in healthcare facilities. They ensure accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance with legal standards. In Brazil, these professionals work in hospitals, clinics, and public health departments, handling electronic health records and supporting clinical decision-making. The role demands attention to detail, familiarity with medical terminology, and proficiency in health information systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a technician with a technical diploma. Experienced professionals can advance to senior technician, supervisor of medical records, or health information manager. Certifications in coding (e.g., ICD-10) or health informatics boost prospects. Some transition to roles in health data analysis, compliance, or IT support. Further education, such as a bachelor's in health information management, opens managerial pathways.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

- Health Informatics and HIPAA Compliance
- Data analysis tools (SQL, Python, R)
- AI/ML fundamentals (NLP, predictive modeling)
- Medical coding systems (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI).
- Project management and change management

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Health
- Data Analytics
- Health Informatics
- Telemedicine

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical records technician in Brazil?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from R$25,000 to R$35,000 annually, with experienced technicians earning R$45,000–R$60,000. Public sector roles often include benefits.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a medical records technician in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. The main route is the VITEM V visa, which requires a Brazilian employer to sponsor and prove no local worker is available. Mercosur nationals have an easier path via the residency agreement.

**Q: What certification is most valued for career advancement?**
A: ICD-10 coding certification from ABRAREC or equivalent is highly valued. Also, a bachelor's in health information management can boost prospects for supervisory roles.
