# Vocational Education Teacher — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2320 (ISCO08)**

Vocational Education Teachers in Brazil instruct students in technical and professional skills, preparing them for specific trades or careers. They work in technical schools, SENAI/SENAC institutions, and federal institutes, blending theoretical knowledge with hands-on training. Teachers must hold a degree in their field plus pedagogical certification. The role is essential for workforce development but requires fluent Portuguese and local credentials, limiting foreign entry.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a classroom instructor, advancing to coordinator, pedagogical supervisor, or department head. Experienced teachers may move into curriculum development or educational consulting. Public-sector opportunities offer stability but competitive entry via exams (concursos). Private institutions provide faster progression but less job security. Continuous professional development is key.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a vocational education teacher in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by institution and region. Public sector teachers (federal institutes) earn roughly R$ 35,000–R$ 70,000 annually, while private institutions may pay less, around R$ 25,000–R$ 50,000. Experience and specialization can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a vocational teacher in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need an employer to sponsor a VITEM V visa, and your credentials must be validated by Brazilian authorities. The process is bureaucratic and Portuguese fluency is essential. Mercosur nationals have an easier pathway via residence agreement.

**Q: Do I need a Brazilian teaching license to work as a vocational teacher?**
A: Yes, a Licenciatura or equivalent pedagogical training is mandatory. Foreign degrees must be revalidated by a Brazilian university, which can take 6–12 months.
