# Messenger, Package Deliverer and Luggage Porter — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 9621 (ISCO08)**

Messengers, package deliverers and luggage porters transport goods, documents, mail and luggage on foot, bicycle, motorcycle or light vehicle, often serving businesses, hotels or private individuals. They ensure timely delivery and may handle basic administrative tasks like signing for parcels.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; experienced deliverers may become supervisors, own a small delivery business, or transition to logistics operators. Many work on a self-employed basis, with variable income. Formal employment offers slightly better stability and social benefits, but opportunities for advancement without additional education are scarce.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually delivering letters and small parcels by address
- Handle and store luggage at hotels or stations
- Operate simple material handling equipment (hand trucks, lifts)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: How much can I earn as a messenger or package deliverer in Brazil?**
A: Earnings vary widely by region and contract type. Formal employees typically earn 1–2 minimum wages (R$1,412–2,824/month as of 2024), while gig workers may earn more during peak hours but have no benefits. In large cities like São Paulo, experienced motorcycle couriers can earn up to R$4,000/month gross.

**Q: Can I get a work visa for Brazil as a delivery person?**
A: It is very difficult. The VITEM V work visa is mainly for skilled workers; low-skilled roles like messenger usually do not meet salary thresholds. Mercosur nationals can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement if they can prove stable income. Otherwise, immigration is not a viable path for this occupation.

**Q: What do I need to work as a delivery person in Brazil?**
A: For bicycle or foot delivery, no formal qualification is needed. For motorcycle delivery, you must have a valid Motorcycle License (CNH A) and complete the mandatory 'Curso de Motofretista' (cargo course). For car delivery, a valid CNH B is required. Most employers also provide on-the-job training.
