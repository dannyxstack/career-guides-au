# Psychologist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2634 (ISCO08)**

Psychologists study mental processes and behavior, providing therapy, assessment, and research. In Brazil, they work in clinical, organizational, educational, and sports settings, helping individuals and groups improve mental health and well-being. The profession requires a bachelor's degree and registration with the Federal Council of Psychology (CFP).

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary outlook for psychologists in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and region. Clinical psychologists generally earn moderate incomes, while those in corporate or specialized fields can earn higher. The profession offers a stable income with growth potential.

**Q: Can a foreign psychologist work in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but the process is restrictive. Foreign psychologists must have their degree validated by the Ministry of Education (MEC) and register with the Federal Council of Psychology (CFP). This often requires additional coursework or exams, making it challenging.

**Q: What are the specializations in psychology in Brazil?**
A: Popular specializations include clinical psychology, organizational psychology, educational psychology, sports psychology, and neuropsychology. Specializing can improve job prospects and income.
