# Gardener — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 6113 (ISCO08)**

Gardeners cultivate and maintain gardens, parks, and other landscaped areas, planting, pruning, watering, and controlling pests. They work in diverse settings, from private residences to public green spaces, and often handle a variety of tools and machinery. The role requires physical stamina, knowledge of plant care, and attention to seasonal cycles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression is limited without formal training; gardeners may advance to head gardener, landscape supervisor, or start their own small business. Specialization in ornamental plants, organic gardening, or landscape design can open higher-paying opportunities. Most workers remain in operational roles with little formal promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic lawnmowers for grass cutting.
- Use robots for precision planting and fertilization
- Automatically detect pests and diseases using AI image recognition
- Scheduling and maintenance of automated irrigation systems
- Use drones for large-area spraying operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze soil data using AI tools to optimize fertilization plans
- Use plant identification apps to quickly diagnose problems and provide care suggestions
- Simulate landscape effects through digital twin technology to assist design
- Use smart devices to monitor environmental conditions and predict plant growth needs.
- Client communication and proposal demonstration using VR/AR

**Human moat:**

- Creative design and aesthetic judgment for complex landscapes
- Empirical diagnosis of atypical plant problems
- Communicate with clients to understand needs and provide personalized services
- Ability to respond to emergencies such as sudden weather and diseases.
- Fine pruning and styling handcraft skills.

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of smart gardening equipment (e.g., automatic pruning robots)
- Data analysis and soil/plant monitoring software usage
- Drone operation and aerial image analysis
- Landscape design software (e.g., AutoCAD, SketchUp)
- Customer relationship management and communication skills
- Basic programming and automation scripting (such as Python)

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a gardener in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and formality; an employed gardener typically earns between R$ 1,200 and R$ 2,000 per month (pre-tax), with formal workers receiving additional benefits like 13th salary and vacation. Self-employed gardeners may earn slightly more per job but lack stability.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a gardener in Brazil?**
A: It is very difficult. Employer-sponsored VITEM V visas require proof that the worker has unique skills unavailable locally, which is rare for this occupation. Mercosur nationals have easier access through the residence agreement, but non-Mercosur foreigners face high barriers.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for a gardener?**
A: With experience, a gardener can become a head gardener, landscape supervisor, or start a small landscaping business. Specializing in ornamental plants, organic gardening, or landscape design can lead to higher earnings and more responsibility.
