# Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7514 (ISCO08)**

This role involves processing and preserving fruits, vegetables, and related products through methods like canning, freezing, drying, and pickling to ensure food safety and extend shelf life. Workers operate machinery, monitor quality, and follow hygiene standards in agro-processing facilities, often in Brazil's agricultural hubs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression is limited without additional training. Entry-level workers can advance to supervisor or quality control roles with experience and certifications. Some move to related fields like food technology or agribusiness management. Specialization in organic or gourmet preservation can open niche markets.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- AI vision systems replace manual sorting and quality inspection
- Automated cutting and peeling equipment replacing manual operations
- Smart packaging lines replace manual canning and sealing
- Monitoring sensors replace manual temperature and humidity adjustment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted optimization of production line scheduling and maintenance plans
- Predict equipment failure and reduce downtime with real-time data analysis
- augmented reality guidance for complex repairs and commissioning
- Robotic collaboration systems improve heavy-lifting efficiency.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair skills
- Emergency handling judgment under abnormal conditions
- Sensory evaluation of food flavor and texture
- Flexible handling and process adjustment for non-standard ingredients.

**Skills to build:**

- Automated equipment operation and basic maintenance
- Introduction to PLC and industrial robot programming
- Data analysis and process monitoring tool usage
- Knowledge of food safety and HACCP systems
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication skills
- Mindset of continuous learning of new technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- organic preservation
- cold chain
- agro-processing
- export quality

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and scale of operation. Entry-level preservers earn around R$18,000–R$25,000 annually, while experienced or supervisory roles can reach R$35,000. Larger agro-processing plants pay slightly more than small artisanal businesses.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for this occupation in Brazil?**
A: It is very difficult. The VITEM V visa requires employer sponsorship and demonstration of specialized skills or higher education, which this low-skilled occupation typically does not meet. Mercosur nationals can work under the Mercosur Residence Agreement with a job offer, but it's still uncommon.

**Q: How can I advance from this role?**
A: Pursue technical courses in food technology or agro-industry, or certifications like GMP and HACCP. Some progress to quality control, production supervision, or start their own small preservation business.
