# Legal and Related Associate Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3411 (ISCO08)**

Legal and related associate professionals in Brazil support lawyers and legal departments by performing tasks such as legal research, drafting documents, managing cases, and assisting in court proceedings. They work in law firms, corporate legal departments, government agencies, or as independent contractors, requiring a strong knowledge of Brazilian legislation and procedures.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a legal associate in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, location, and employer size. Entry-level associates can expect around 2,000-4,000 BRL per month, while senior roles may reach 6,000-10,000 BRL. Large corporate law firms in São Paulo pay the highest.

**Q: Can a foreign legal associate get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The most common route is the VITEM V visa, requiring a job offer from a Brazilian employer and approval from the Ministry of Labor. The employer must prove that the foreign worker has specialized knowledge not available locally. Mercosur nationals have an easier path.

**Q: Do I need to pass the OAB exam to work as a legal associate?**
A: No, the OAB exam is mandatory only to practice as a lawyer. For legal associate roles, it is not required, though it can improve job prospects and salary potential.
