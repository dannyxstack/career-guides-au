# Chemical Engineer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2145 (ISCO08)**

Chemical Engineers design, develop, and optimize processes for the production of chemicals, fuels, pharmaceuticals, and other products, applying principles of chemistry, physics, and engineering. They also ensure safety, environmental compliance, and cost efficiency in industrial operations.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for chemical engineers in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience, but chemical engineers are among the highest-paid engineers in Brazil. Entry-level positions start around BRL 60,000 per year, with senior engineers earning over BRL 150,000. Premium sectors like oil and gas or petrochemicals offer significantly higher compensation.

**Q: How can a foreign chemical engineer obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: The primary route is the VITEM V work visa, requiring a job offer from a Brazilian employer who must obtain prior approval from the Ministry of Labor. Alternatively, citizens of Mercosur countries can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement for a simpler process. Professional registration with CREA is mandatory to practice.

**Q: Is chemical engineering considered a shortage occupation in Brazil?**
A: Yes, chemical engineering is often identified as a shortage area, especially in the petrochemical, biofuels, and environmental sectors. The government periodically publishes lists of professions with insufficient qualified labor, and chemical engineering frequently appears on such lists, facilitating visa approvals.
