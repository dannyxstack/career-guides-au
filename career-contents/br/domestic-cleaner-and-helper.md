# Domestic Cleaner and Helper — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 9111 (ISCO08)**

Domestic cleaners and helpers perform cleaning and basic household tasks in private homes, apartments, and similar settings. Duties include sweeping, mopping, washing dishes, laundry, ironing, and making beds. In Brazil, many work formally under the Domestic Workers Law (Lei das Domésticas), granting rights such as minimum wage, paid leave, and social security. The occupation is predominantly held by women from lower-income backgrounds. Demand remains steady due to urbanization, dual-income households, and an aging population that requires additional support. While the work is often informal, formalization is gradually increasing through registration and compliance with labor laws.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; many remain as general helpers throughout their working lives. Some transition to specialized caregiving roles (elderly or child) or start their own cleaning businesses. Formal training courses in cleaning techniques, hygiene, or elderly care can improve employability and wages. With experience, some move to higher-end clientele or work for cleaning franchises. However, the occupation typically offers little upward mobility without additional education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Schedule cleaning appointments, send reminders, and collect payments via chatbot
- Use computer vision to inspect cleaning quality and generate reports
- Automated inventory management: predict cleaning supply needs based on historical data and place orders
- Voice assistants control smart home devices (e.g., robot vacuum cleaners, automatic curtains)
- Standardized cleaning process documents generated and updated by AI.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI voice translation tool assists in communicating with foreign employers
- Smart devices (e.g., stain detection apps) to assist in choosing the best cleaning agent
- Data analysis tools optimize cleaning routes and time allocation
- Online platform automatically matches customers and cleaners, improving customer acquisition efficiency
- Augmented reality (AR) guidance for cleaning complex furniture.

**Human moat:**

- Fine manual cleaning (e.g., antiques, silk, and other special materials)
- Building long-term trust relationships and personalized services with employers
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., spills, damage) with flexibility
- manual labour and on-site presence (physical contact that cannot be replaced)
- Highly customized home organization and decluttering solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of smart cleaning equipment
- Basic English or a second foreign language (for smart assistants and client communication)
- Time Management and Digital Scheduling Tool Usage
- Knowledge of household chemical safety and intelligent proportioning
- Fundamentals of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software
- Basic data analysis (to optimize service efficiency)

**Key growth areas:**

- Formalization

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic cleaner in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary regionally; in São Paulo, the monthly range is from the minimum wage (about 1,412 BRL in 2024) up to 2,000 BRL for experienced helpers in wealthy households. Formal workers receive benefits like 13th salary and paid vacation.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a domestic cleaner in Brazil?**
A: It is very difficult. Domestic cleaning is not a skilled occupation, so VITEM V or CNIg authorizations are rarely granted. Mercosur nationals may reside and work under the agreement, but they need a formal job offer and registration.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for domestic helpers?**
A: Limited. Some become supervisors for cleaning teams, start independent businesses, or transition to full-time caregiving for the elderly or children. Additional training in caregiving or specialized cleaning (e.g., post-construction) can help.
