# Concrete Finisher — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7114 (ISCO08)**

Concrete finishers and placers shape, smooth, and finish concrete surfaces for buildings, roads, bridges, and other structures. In Brazil, they are essential in the construction industry, working on site to ensure concrete is properly poured, leveled, and cured. This occupation requires physical stamina, attention to detail, and knowledge of concrete mixtures and tools.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or laborer, advancing to journeyman concrete finisher after 2-4 years of experience. With further training, one can become a supervisor, quality inspector, or start their own small contracting business. Additional certifications in safety (NR-18) or specialized techniques (decorative concrete) can enhance advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- infrastructure
- real estate
- industrial construction
- renovation

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for concrete finishers in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience, but entry-level positions pay around the minimum wage, while experienced finishers can earn up to 2-3 times that. Overtime and specialized skills can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a concrete finisher in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Employers must prove no local worker is available. The VITEM V visa requires a job offer and approval from the National Immigration Council (CNIg). Mercosur nationals can use the residence agreement.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience, you can become a supervisor, quality control inspector, or start your own small construction business. Certifications in safety or decorative concrete can open more doors.
