# Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3257 (ISCO08)**

Environmental and occupational health inspectors assess and enforce compliance with health and safety regulations in workplaces and public environments to prevent disease, injury, and environmental harm. They conduct site inspections, investigate incidents, collect samples, and advise employers and the public on best practices.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an environmental and occupational health inspector in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level inspectors earn around 30,000–45,000 BRL per year, while senior inspectors can earn 60,000–90,000 BRL. Government positions follow public sector pay scales; private sector tends to pay higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an environmental and occupational health inspector in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is uncommon. It usually requires a VITEM V work visa sponsored by a Brazilian employer. The employer must demonstrate that no qualified Brazilian candidate is available. Recognition of foreign qualifications via revalidação (validation) is often needed for regulated positions.
