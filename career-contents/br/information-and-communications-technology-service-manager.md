# Information and Communications Technology Services Manager — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 1330 (ISCO08)**

Information and Communications Technology Services Managers plan, direct, and coordinate the delivery of ICT services within organizations. They oversee teams that design, implement, and maintain IT infrastructure, software, and support systems. In Brazil, this role is crucial for driving digital transformation across sectors such as finance, telecom, and e-commerce. Managers must blend technical expertise with leadership skills to ensure service reliability, security, and efficiency. The role often involves budget management, vendor negotiation, and compliance with local data protection laws. Demand is strong in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and emerging tech hubs like Florianópolis.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 137,882. Brazil's ICT sector is expanding rapidly, driven by cloud migration, cybersecurity needs, and AI adoption. The demand for experienced ICT services managers is expected to grow by 12-15% over the next five years, outpacing many other managerial roles. Major employers include banks, telecoms, and IT outsourcing firms. However, competition is intense for roles in top companies, and candidates with international experience or certifications (e.g., PMP, ITIL) have an edge. The government's digital inclusion initiatives also create opportunities in public sector IT projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an IT technician or analyst, moving into team lead, then manager. Many ICT managers hold a bachelor's in computer science or related field, plus an MBA for senior roles. Certifications like PMP, ITIL, or CISM boost prospects. Experienced managers may advance to Director of IT or Chief Information Officer. Lateral moves into consulting or entrepreneurship are common. In Brazil, fluency in English and Spanish is increasingly valued for multinational teams.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Services Manager in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by location and experience. Entry-level managers earn around R$ 120,000 per year, while senior managers in large companies can exceed R$ 300,000. São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro offer the highest pay.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as an ICT Services Manager in Brazil?**
A: Yes, through employer sponsorship under the VITEM V visa. The company must prove that the role requires specialized skills. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can use the simplified residence agreement.

**Q: What certifications are most important for career advancement?**
A: PMP and ITIL certifications are highly recommended. For cybersecurity-focused roles, CISM or CISSP add significant value. An MBA is not mandatory but helps for senior executive positions.
