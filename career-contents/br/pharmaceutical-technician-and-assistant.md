# Pharmaceutical Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3213 (ISCO08)**

Pharmaceutical technicians and assistants work under the supervision of pharmacists, performing tasks such as dispensing medications, preparing compounding formulas, managing inventory, and providing customer service in pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, and pharmaceutical industries. They ensure compliance with health regulations and maintain accurate records. In Brazil, these professionals are known as 'Técnico em Farmácia' (CBO 4211-25) and 'Auxiliar de Farmácia' (CBO 4211-20). The role requires technical training and registration with the Regional Pharmacy Council (CRF).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a pharmacy assistant, advancing to technician after formal training and CRF certification. Experienced technicians can specialize in areas like hospital pharmacy, compounding, or pharmaceutical industry quality control. Some pursue higher education to become pharmacists (graduation in Pharmacy) or move into managerial roles in retail or hospital pharmacy. Continuing education is key, as regulations and drug technologies evolve.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: How much can a pharmaceutical technician expect to earn in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and sector. Metropolitan areas and private hospitals typically pay higher. It is advisable to consult official salary surveys such as the PNAD Contínua or recent job postings for current figures. Benefits often include meal vouchers and transportation allowance.

**Q: Can foreign pharmaceutical technicians obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, through the employer-sponsored VITEM V visa. The employer must obtain prior authorization from the Ministry of Labor. Additionally, CRF registration is mandatory, which may require revalidation of foreign credentials. Mercosur citizens can use the simplified residence agreement.

**Q: Is CRF registration mandatory for all pharmacy technicians?**
A: Yes, it is legally required to work as a pharmacy technician in Brazil. Without it, you cannot legally dispense medications or perform technical tasks in a pharmacy. Registration must be renewed annually.
