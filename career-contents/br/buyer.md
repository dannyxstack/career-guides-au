# Buyer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3323 (ISCO08)**

Buyers, also known as purchasing agents, are responsible for sourcing and procuring goods, raw materials, and services for organizations. In Brazil, they work across industries such as retail, manufacturing, and agribusiness, negotiating contracts, managing supplier relationships, and ensuring cost-effective inventory levels. They analyze market trends, evaluate vendors, and coordinate logistics to meet organizational needs. The role requires strong negotiation skills, analytical ability, and knowledge of supply chain management. Buyers often specialize in categories like industrial inputs, consumer goods, or agricultural commodities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for buyers typically starts from assistant buyer or procurement analyst, advancing to senior buyer, category manager, and procurement manager. Specialization in strategic sourcing, global procurement, or sustainable supply chains can lead to director-level roles. In Brazil, large corporations offer clear advancement paths, while SMEs may provide broader responsibilities. Professional certifications like CPSM or from the Brazilian Association of Purchasing (ABRAS) enhance prospects. Industry switching is common, especially between retail, manufacturing, and agribusiness.

- Automatically Analyze Public Property Data, Generate Market Reports and Comparable Sales Analysis

- Basic clerical work, such as contract drafting and document filing

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a buyer in Brazil?**
A: Entry-level buyers earn around BRL 50,000-70,000 annually; experienced buyers can earn BRL 100,000-150,000; senior managers may exceed BRL 200,000. Varies by industry and region.

**Q: Can a foreign buyer get a work visa in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it's challenging. Employers must sponsor a VITEM V visa, proving no local candidate is available. Buyers are not on priority lists, so the process can be lengthy. Mercosur citizens have an easier route.

**Q: What industries hire the most buyers in Brazil?**
A: Retail, automotive, agribusiness, and pharmaceuticals are top employers. E-commerce is rapidly growing. Industrial and mining sectors also hire procurement professionals.
