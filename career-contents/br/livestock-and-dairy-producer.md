# Livestock and Dairy Producer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 6121 (ISCO08)**

Livestock and dairy producers manage the breeding, raising, and production of cattle, pigs, goats, or other livestock for meat, milk, or other animal products. They oversee feeding, health care, and breeding, and often manage farm staff and finances. In Brazil, this role is critical in the world-leading beef and dairy sectors, with operations ranging from small family farms to large agribusinesses. Producers must adapt to environmental regulations, market fluctuations, and sustainability demands.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression often starts as a farm worker or technician, advancing to farm manager or owner-operator. Specialization in organic or pasture-raised livestock, or in management of large-scale feedlots, can enhance prospects. Many producers expand into related agribusiness, such as processing or direct sales. Formal education in veterinary science or agribusiness is increasingly valued, but hands-on experience remains key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic feeding systems replace manual feeding and water management.
- Smart sensors automatically monitor animal health data and send alerts
- Milking robots replace manual milking operations.
- Automation of data recording and analysis replaces manual ledgers
- Driverless tractors replace some field labor

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted early disease warning system improves veterinary diagnostic accuracy.
- IoT optimizes pasture environment control and feed ratios on farms
- Drone inspection of large-scale pastures to improve management efficiency
- Data analysis tools assist breeding plans and yield prediction
- Blockchain technology tracks dairy supply chains to enhance trust

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment of animal welfare and emergency health conditions
- Unstructured problem-solving ability in complex environments
- Interpersonal communication and team management with veterinarians, staff, and others
- Trade-off decisions on macro factors such as ecology, market, and policy.
- Intuition and feel gained from long-term farming experience

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and sensor technology application
- Automated equipment operation and maintenance
- Use of AI diagnostic tools for animal health
- Sustainable agriculture and precision farming practices
- Digital marketing and supply chain tracking
- Change management and team digital leadership

**Key growth areas:**

- sustainable intensification
- traceability systems
- precision livestock farming
- organic and pasture-raised products

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a livestock producer in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by scale. A farm manager may earn around BRL 60,000 per year, while a large-scale producer can exceed BRL 200,000. Profits depend on market prices and efficiency.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a livestock producer in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but mainly through employer sponsorship (VITEM V) for high-skilled roles like farm manager or veterinarian. Self-employment is restricted; owning a farm requires a separate investor visa.

**Q: Do I need a degree to become a livestock producer in Brazil?**
A: No, hands-on experience is highly valued, but a degree in agronomy or animal science can help with management roles and obtaining credit.
