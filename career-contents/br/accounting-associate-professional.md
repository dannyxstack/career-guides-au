# Accounting Associate Professionals — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3313 (ISCO08)**

Accounting Associate Professionals in Brazil perform bookkeeping, financial recording, and tax compliance tasks under the supervision of senior accountants. They work across diverse sectors, including corporate finance, public accounting firms, and government agencies. This role typically requires a technical diploma or bachelor's degree in accounting, along with registration with the Regional Accounting Council (CRC). The profession is regulated, and practitioners are responsible for ensuring accurate financial reporting and adherence to Brazilian tax laws.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically begins with entry-level positions as bookkeepers or accounting assistants, often requiring a technical diploma. With experience and a bachelor's degree, professionals can move into senior associate roles or supervisory positions. Obtaining a CRC registration is mandatory, and further certifications like CPA Brazil (CFC) can enhance prospects. Many advance to accountant (contador) roles, leading to opportunities in controllership, auditing, or tax management. The career path is structured, with salary growth linked to specialization and employer size.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Accounting Associate Professionals in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer size. Entry-level positions typically offer compensation near the national median for office professionals. Experienced associates with CRC registration and specialization can earn significantly more, especially in large corporations or audit firms.

**Q: Can an Accounting Associate Professional immigrate to Brazil on a work visa?**
A: Yes, but it is employer-sponsored and requires proving that no local candidate can fill the role. The VITEM V visa is the most common route, but the process can be complex due to the availability of local talent. Professionals with specialized skills (e.g., IFRS expertise) may have better chances.

**Q: What is the career progression for Accounting Associate Professionals?**
A: Typical progression starts from bookkeeper or accounting assistant, advancing to senior associate, then to accountant (contador) with a bachelor's degree and CRC registration. Further steps include roles in controllership, tax management, or auditing. Certification like CPA Brazil can accelerate advancement.
