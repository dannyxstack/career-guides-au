# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Brazil are responsible for recording utility consumption data from residential and commercial meters, and collecting cash or cards from vending machines, typically for energy, water, or gas companies. They work outdoors or in public spaces, using handheld devices or manual logs, and often drive between locations. The role requires basic numeracy, attention to detail, and physical stamina.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression is limited; experienced workers may advance to supervisory roles overseeing teams of readers, or transition into customer service or billing departments. Some move into data entry or quality control for meter data. With additional training, a few qualify for technician positions in meter installation or maintenance. The role is often entry-level and offers stability in large state-owned companies, but lacks clear upward mobility without further education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings
- On-site inspection of measuring equipment faults (initial judgment)
- Generate manual meter reading reports.
- Reading meters along a fixed route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile apps or handheld terminals to assist with remote confirmation of abnormal readings.
- Identify potential leaks or tampering by analyzing consumption patterns via AI
- Use autonomous inspection drones or robots for meter reading in high-risk areas
- Provide energy advice to clients based on AI-generated energy saving reports.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair in complex on-site environments.
- In-person communication with clients over billing disputes or safety issues
- Decision-making ability in emergencies (e.g., gas leaks)
- Manual calibration and maintenance of old mechanical watch heads

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and anomaly detection
- Mobile device and specialized app operation
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic electrical/mechanical repair knowledge
- Drone or robot operation and maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- smart metering
- energy utilities
- water utilities
- field services

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a meter reader in Brazil?**
A: Salaries for meter readers and vending-machine collectors are generally low, often starting at the minimum wage level and rising to around 1.5–2 times the minimum wage for experienced workers. Pay varies by company, region, and whether the role is outsourced. In public sector utilities, compensation may include benefits like meal vouchers and transportation.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa for this occupation in Brazil?**
A: It is very difficult for a foreigner to obtain a work visa for meter reading because it is considered a low-skilled occupation. The employer must prove that no qualified Brazilian is available, which is rarely accepted. The Mercosur Residence Agreement is a possible path for nationals of Mercosur countries, but the job itself offers limited migration prospects.

**Q: Is there room for advancement as a meter reader?**
A: Advancement is limited without further education. Some workers become team leaders or move into billing, customer service, or meter installation technician roles. Pursuing technical courses in electricity or water systems can open better opportunities within utility companies.
