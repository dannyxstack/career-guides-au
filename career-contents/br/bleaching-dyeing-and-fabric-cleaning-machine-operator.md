# Bleaching, Dyeing And Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 8154 (ISCO08)**

Bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators control and monitor machinery used to bleach, dye, wash, and finish textiles. They work in textile manufacturing facilities, handling chemicals and ensuring color consistency, fabric quality, and production efficiency. This role requires attention to detail, basic technical knowledge, and adherence to safety protocols.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience, generally ranging from entry-level near the minimum wage to experienced workers earning up to three times the minimum wage, plus benefits.

**Q: Can machine operators get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is restrictive. Employer sponsorship via VITEM V is possible if the company demonstrates need and the worker has relevant experience. Mercosur nationals have an easier path.

**Q: What training is needed to enter this field?**
A: High school diploma is minimal; vocational training in textile processing from SENAI is highly recommended. Chemical safety and machine operation certifications are often required.
