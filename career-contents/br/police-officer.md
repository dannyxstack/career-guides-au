# Police Officer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 5412 (ISCO08)**

Police officers in Brazil are responsible for maintaining public order, preventing and investigating crimes, and enforcing laws at federal, state, and municipal levels. They work in diverse units such as patrol, traffic, investigative, and specialized forces like the Federal Police (PF) and Military Police (PM). The role requires physical fitness, legal knowledge, and crisis management skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with passing a public exam (concurso público) for entry-level positions. Officers can advance through ranks (soldado, cabo, sargento, oficial) based on experience, exams, and training. Specializations in cybercrime, forensics, or intelligence are growing. Senior roles include delegate (for civil police) or commissioner (federal). Leadership paths are limited by hierarchical structures.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for police officers in Brazil?**
A: Starting salaries vary by state and force, typically between R$3,000 and R$7,000 per month for entry-level officers, with higher pay for federal positions and senior ranks. Additional benefits include hazard pay and overtime.

**Q: Can foreigners become police officers in Brazil?**
A: Generally, no. Brazilian police forces require Brazilian citizenship. Exceptions exist for technical cooperation roles or specific agreements with Mercosur countries, but these are rare and usually non-operational.

**Q: What are the main career progression opportunities?**
A: Progression is through public exams for higher ranks (e.g., from patrol officer to investigator, delegate, or commissioner). Specializations in cybercrime, forensics, or command can accelerate advancement.
