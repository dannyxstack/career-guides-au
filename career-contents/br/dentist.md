# Dentist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2261 (ISCO08)**

Dentists diagnose and treat issues related to teeth, gums, and oral tissues, performing procedures such as fillings, extractions, root canals, and cosmetic improvements. In Brazil, they work in private practices, public health clinics, or hospitals. The profession requires a university degree in dentistry, registration with the Regional Council of Dentistry (CRO), and ongoing education. The field is regulated and offers opportunities in both general practice and specialties like orthodontics and implantology.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a general practitioner in a clinic or public service. Many dentists pursue specialization (e.g., orthodontics, periodontics) via postgraduate courses or residency to increase income and demand. Others move into academia, research, or open their own practice. Public sector careers via competitive exams offer stability. Continuous learning is key due to technological advances.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial screening and anomaly marking of X-rays and CT scans (AI reading)
- Administrative paperwork such as patient appointment, billing, and medical record organization
- Standardized preventive procedures like teeth cleaning and fluoride application (automated dental chair)
- Basic intraoral scanning and digital model generation
- Automated review and filling of insurance claim documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic systems provide treatment suggestions, improving diagnosis accuracy
- Simulate surgical paths via digital twin to optimize implant/orthodontic planning
- Real-time voice recording and medical record generation, freeing doctor's attention
- Personalized prevention plan recommendations based on patient historical data
- Virtual reality training systems enhance complex surgical skills

**Human moat:**

- Complex Surgical Decisions and Manual Operations (e.g., Root Canals, Tooth Extractions)
- Patient communication, empathy, and behaviour management (especially for children/anxious patients)
- Interdisciplinary Comprehensive Diagnosis (e.g., Oral-Systemic Disease Links)
- Emergency management and clinical judgement for sudden conditions
- Professional licensing and legal liability

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted Diagnostic Tool Operation and Result Interpretation
- Digital oral scanning and 3D printing design
- Patient interaction via telehealth platforms
- Basic data analysis and clinical research
- Patient communication and digital health education
- Continuous learning of automated workflow integration

**Key growth areas:**

- Elderly dental care
- Cosmetic dentistry
- Dental implants
- Public health programs

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can foreign dentists work in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but they must have their degree revalidated by a Brazilian university and register with the Regional Council of Dentistry (CRO). Employer sponsorship via VITEM V visa is required for non-Mercosur nationals.

**Q: What are the best specialties for dentists in Brazil?**
A: Orthodontics, Implantology, and Oral Surgery are in high demand. Aesthetics and public health also offer opportunities.

**Q: What is the process to practice dentistry in Brazil as a foreigner?**
A: Submit documents to a university with a recognized dentistry program; then take a revalidation exam. After passing, register with the CRO and obtain a work visa.
