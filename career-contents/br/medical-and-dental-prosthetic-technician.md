# Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3214 (ISCO08)**

Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians design, fabricate, and repair prosthetic devices such as dentures, crowns, bridges, and orthotic appliances. They work closely with dentists and medical professionals to create custom devices that restore function and aesthetics. Using materials like acrylic, porcelain, and metal, they employ techniques such as casting, molding, and 3D printing. Precision, manual dexterity, and knowledge of anatomy are essential.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression often starts with a technical diploma and clinic experience. Technicians can specialize in areas like implant prosthetics or maxillofacial prosthetics, or advance to lab management or ownership. Some pursue higher education in biomedical engineering or dentistry. Continuing education in digital technologies is key for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Dentistry
- Implant-Supported Prosthetics
- Geriatric Prosthetics
- 3D Printing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a dental prosthetic technician in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level technicians earn around 1,500-2,500 BRL/month, while experienced professionals in private labs can earn 4,000-8,000 BRL/month or more.

**Q: Can a foreign medical and dental prosthetic technician work in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but requires employer sponsorship (VITEM V visa) and validation of qualifications by the Ministry of Education and CRO. Mercosur nationals have an easier path. The process can be lengthy.

**Q: Is a university degree necessary to become a dental prosthetic technician?**
A: No, a technical diploma from an accredited school (2 years) is sufficient and most common. A technologist degree is optional but may provide a competitive edge.
