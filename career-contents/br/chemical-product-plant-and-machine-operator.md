# Chemical Products Plant and Machine Operator — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 8131 (ISCO08)**

Operate and monitor equipment and machinery in chemical processing plants to produce substances such as petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, fertilizers, and industrial chemicals. Tasks include controlling reactions, adjusting temperatures and pressures, taking samples, and ensuring safety and quality standards. Operators work in shifts in often hazardous environments, requiring strict adherence to protocols. The role demands technical skill, attention to detail, and the ability to respond to alarms and malfunctions quickly.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 79,540. Brazil's chemical industry, especially petrochemicals and biofuels, drives steady demand for plant operators. The expansion of pre-salt oil and gas and the growing biofuels sector create new positions. However, automation may reduce some low-skill roles. Operators with certifications and experience in complex processes will remain in demand. Employment is concentrated in industrial poles like São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Bahia, and Rio Grande do Sul.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a chemical plant operator in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and industry, but a junior operator earns around BRL 30,000–45,000 annually. Senior operators or those in specialty petrochemical plants can earn BRL 60,000–80,000+.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a chemical plant operator in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. You need an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa or qualify under the Mercosur Residence Agreement. The occupation is eligible if you have relevant certifications and local shortage is proven.
