# Electrical Engineer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2151 (ISCO08)**

Electrical engineers design, develop, and supervise the manufacture, installation, operation, and maintenance of electrical systems and equipment, including power generation, transmission, distribution, and control systems. They work in diverse sectors such as energy, telecommunications, manufacturing, and construction, ensuring projects comply with safety standards and technical regulations. In Brazil, they are regulated by the CREA system and require a degree in electrical engineering.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an electrical engineer in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level engineers earn around 50,000-80,000 BRL annually; senior engineers can earn 120,000-200,000 BRL or more. Larger cities and the energy sector tend to pay higher.

**Q: Can a foreign electrical engineer work in Brazil without employer sponsorship?**
A: No, independent work requires a work visa (VITEM V), which is employer-sponsored. The employer must prove the need for a foreign professional. Mercosur citizens have a simplified process.

**Q: Is CREA registration mandatory for foreign engineers?**
A: Yes, CREA registration is mandatory for all professional engineering activities in Brazil. Foreign engineers must validate their diplomas through a recognized university or have their credentials recognized via international agreements.
