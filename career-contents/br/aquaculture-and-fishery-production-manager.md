# Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Manager — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 1312 (ISCO08)**

Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers plan, direct, and coordinate production activities in fish farming, hatcheries, and commercial fishing operations, ensuring sustainable practices, profitability, and compliance with environmental regulations. They oversee breeding, feeding, health management, harvesting, and processing, while managing budgets, staff, and supply chains.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 1,906. Brazil's aquaculture sector is expanding due to growing domestic demand for fish and government incentives like the Plano Safra. The Amazon region and coastal areas offer significant potential, but challenges include environmental licensing, water quality management, and competition from imports. Employment outlook is favourable for experienced managers, especially those skilled in sustainable intensification and value-added processing.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a fish farm technician or supervisor, then advances to production manager, regional director, or consultant. Specialisation in species (e.g., tilapia, shrimp) or systems (recirculating aquaculture, offshore) can enhance prospects. Some managers move into research, policymaking, or entrepreneurship. Continuous learning in sustainability and technology is key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually record farming data (water temperature, feeding amounts, etc.)
- Generate daily production reports and compliance documents
- Image recognition of initial disease symptoms
- Inventory counting and feed order calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses sensors and AI to predict optimal feeding times and amounts
- Monitor fish health and abnormal behavior through machine vision.
- Simulate the impact of different stocking densities and feed plans on yield
- Real-time water quality monitoring and automatic adjustment of oxygenation and filtration equipment

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making for responding to sudden disease outbreaks or environmental disasters
- Experience-based biosecurity management and overall disease prevention planning.
- Negotiations and relationship management with regulators, suppliers, and sales channels
- Assess and implement new sustainable aquaculture technologies

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (Python/R)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor system management
- Operation and troubleshooting of automated control systems
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainability certification standards
- Inspection skills for drones or underwater robots

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable Aquaculture
- Shrimp Farming
- Recirculating Systems
- Fish Processing

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with hands-on experience in aquaculture or fisheries operations.
- Those passionate about sustainable food production and marine biology.
- Skilled managers who enjoy outdoor work and problem-solving.

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer office-based desk jobs and regular hours.
- Those uncomfortable with variable weather, seasonal workload, and regulatory complexity.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and company size, but it is generally above the national average. Mid-level managers can earn a comfortable income, and senior roles in large operations command significantly higher pay.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign aquaculture managers?**
A: Foreign managers typically need employer sponsorship under the VITEM V temporary work visa. Mercosur nationals can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement for simplified free movement. High-skilled professionals may also apply for a permanent visa through CNIg.
