# Engineering Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2149 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers engineering professionals whose specializations are not captured by other ISCO categories, including fields such as environmental, materials, biomedical, and industrial engineering. In Brazil, these engineers work across sectors like infrastructure, manufacturing, energy, and technology, applying scientific principles to design, develop, and optimize systems and processes. They must hold a recognized engineering degree and be registered with the Regional Council of Engineering and Agronomy (CREA) to practice legally.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialization, experience, and region. Entry-level engineers can expect BRL 40,000–60,000 annually, while senior professionals may earn BRL 100,000–180,000. Sectors like oil, gas, and mining offer higher compensation.

**Q: How can a foreign engineer obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: The most common route is the VITEM V work visa, which requires a job offer from a Brazilian employer who must apply with the Ministry of Labor. Mercosur citizens may use the residence agreement. All foreign engineers need CREA registration to practice.

**Q: Is CREA registration mandatory for foreign engineers?**
A: Yes, foreign engineers must have their degree validated by a Brazilian university and then register with CREA to legally practice. The process can be lengthy, so it is advisable to start early.
