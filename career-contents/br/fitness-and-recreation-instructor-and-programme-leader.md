# Fitness and Recreation Instructor and Programme Leader — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3423 (ISCO08)**

Fitness and Recreation Instructors and Programme Leaders, classified under ISCO-08 code 3423, design, lead, and evaluate exercise, fitness, and recreational activities. They work in gyms, sports clubs, community centers, hotels, and as independent personal trainers. In Brazil, this role typically requires a degree in Physical Education and registration with the Regional Council of Physical Education (CREF). The profession is growing due to increasing health awareness and demand for personalized fitness services, offering opportunities in both formal employment and self-employment.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a fitness instructor in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, location, and employment type. Gym employees often earn between 1.5 to 3 times the minimum wage, while established personal trainers can earn significantly more, especially in upscale neighborhoods or through private clients. Income is often supplemented by commissions and bonuses.

**Q: How can a foreign fitness instructor get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: The most common route is the VITEM V visa, requiring employer sponsorship. The employer must prove that no qualified Brazilian worker is available. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can use the Mercosur Residence Agreement, which is simpler and does not require sponsorship. All applicants must present valid credentials, including CREF registration.

**Q: Is a university degree necessary to work as a fitness instructor in Brazil?**
A: Legally, a bachelor's degree in Physical Education is mandatory for professional practice in most settings, and registration with CREF is required. However, some roles in informal or low-intensity settings may accept technical courses. For serious career growth, a degree is highly recommended.
