# Health Services Manager — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 1342 (ISCO08)**

Health Services Managers plan, direct, and coordinate medical and health services in hospitals, clinics, public health agencies, or similar organizations, ensuring efficient delivery of healthcare services while complying with regulations. They oversee budgets, staff, and operations, and often work closely with medical professionals to improve patient care and organizational performance.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Health Services Managers in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region, experience, and type of institution. In major cities and private hospitals, compensation is significantly higher than in public facilities. Senior hospital directors can earn substantial incomes, while entry-level managers in smaller clinics earn less. For precise figures, consult industry salary surveys.

**Q: How can a foreigner get a work visa as a Health Services Manager in Brazil?**
A: Foreigners typically need employer sponsorship under the VITEM V work visa. The employer must obtain authorization from the Ministry of Labor and the National Immigration Council (CNIg). Requirements include a formal job offer, proof of qualifications (degree, experience), and a clean criminal record. Portuguese fluency is highly recommended. The process can take several months.

**Q: What is the career progression like for a Health Services Manager in Brazil?**
A: Many start as department or unit managers in hospitals or clinics. With experience and specialization, they can advance to executive roles such as hospital director, chief operating officer, or regional manager for healthcare networks. An MBA with a focus on health management is often a stepping stone to senior leadership.
