# Midwifery Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery professionals in Brazil, known as enfermeiros obstetras, provide comprehensive care to women during pregnancy, childbirth, and the postpartum period, as well as newborn care. They work in hospitals, birth centers, and community health settings, often within the public health system (SUS) or private practice. This regulated profession requires a nursing degree plus specialized obstetric training, with a focus on natural birth and maternal-infant health.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. —. Brazil's expanding maternal health policies and growing preference for humanized birth are driving demand for midwives. The SUS faces shortages in rural and remote areas, while private hospitals seek professionals for birth centers. Ageing workforce and rising birth rates in some regions further boost openings. However, competition exists in major cities. Job prospects are strong for those willing to relocate or specialize in high-risk care.

Career paths include advancing from staff midwife to senior or supervising positions in hospitals, leading birth centers, or teaching in nursing universities. Specializations in prenatal genetics, lactation consulting, or home birth can differentiate professionals. Some move into public health management or policy. The role offers good work-life balance especially in shift work, with opportunities for entrepreneurial midwives to open private practices.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a midwifery professional in Brazil?**
A: Salaries for midwives in Brazil vary by sector and experience. Entry-level midwives in the public SUS earn around 3,000-5,000 BRL monthly, while senior professionals in private hospitals can earn 8,000-12,000 BRL. Self-employed midwives may charge per birth. Overall, annual pre-tax income ranges from about 50,000 to 120,000 BRL.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign midwives to work in Brazil?**
A: Foreign midwives can apply for a VITEM V work visa with a job offer from a Brazilian employer. Mercosur citizens can obtain permanent residence under the regional agreement. Others may need a CNIg authorization, which requires employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications. Recognition of foreign degrees by the Ministry of Education (revalidação) is also mandatory.

**Q: How long does it take to become a midwife in Brazil?**
A: Typically, it takes 5-6 years: a 4-year nursing bachelor’s degree followed by 1-2 years of postgraduate specialization in obstetrics. Public universities are free; private degrees can cost 120,000-240,000 BRL total. All midwives must register with COREN.
