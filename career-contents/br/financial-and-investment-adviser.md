# Financial and Investment Adviser — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2412 (ISCO08)**

Financial and investment advisers provide personalized guidance to individuals and organizations on financial planning, investment strategies, portfolio management, and wealth optimization. They analyze market trends, assess risk tolerance, and recommend suitable financial products such as stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and insurance. In Brazil, advisers must be registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (CVM) and often hold certifications from Anbima. The role combines analytical skills with client relationship management, aiming to help clients achieve long-term financial goals while navigating regulatory and economic complexities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a junior analyst or trainee at a bank, brokerage, or independent advisory firm. With experience and certifications (e.g., CEA, CFA), one can advance to senior adviser, portfolio manager, or team leader. Some professionals move into private banking, asset management, or open their own advisory firms. Continuous learning and networking are essential. The highest earners often build a loyal client base and specialize in niche areas like high-net-worth individuals or cross-border investments.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a financial adviser in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, client base, and firm type. Entry-level advisers may earn around BRL 60,000-80,000 annually, while experienced senior advisers can exceed BRL 200,000, with top performers earning significantly more through commissions and bonuses.

**Q: How can a foreign professional work as a financial adviser in Brazil?**
A: You need a job offer from a Brazilian company that sponsors a VITEM V work visa. Additionally, you must obtain mandatory certifications (CPA-20) and register with CVM. Proficiency in Portuguese is highly recommended.

**Q: What are the main certifications needed?**
A: CPA-20 is mandatory for selling investment products, and CEA is highly recommended for career advancement. The CFA charter is also valued but not required.
