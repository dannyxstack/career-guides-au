# Non-Commissioned Armed Forces Officers — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Non-commissioned officers (NCOs) in Brazil's Armed Forces are career military personnel who serve as the backbone of the military structure, responsible for leading small units, training soldiers, maintaining discipline, and executing operational missions. They work in the Army, Navy, or Air Force, typically after completing specialized training courses. This occupation demands high discipline, physical fitness, and a strong sense of duty, with career advancement based on time in service, performance, and completion of advanced courses.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a non-commissioned officer in Brazil?**
A: Starting salaries for NCOs (third sergeant) are around 4,500 to 6,000 BRL per month (gross), increasing with rank and time in service. Senior sergeants and warrant officers can earn up to 10,000 BRL monthly. Additional allowances (hazard pay, housing) may apply.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a non-commissioned officer in Brazil?**
A: No. Brazilian Armed Forces NCO positions are restricted to Brazilian citizens by law. Foreigners cannot apply or obtain a work visa for this occupation, except in rare cases of military cooperation agreements, which are highly selective.

**Q: How long does it take to become a sergeant in Brazil's military?**
A: After passing the entrance exam and completing the 2-3 year NCO training course, the graduate is promoted to third sergeant. Further promotions occur every 4-8 years depending on performance and vacancies.
