# Insurance Representative — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3321 (ISCO08)**

Insurance representatives in Brazil are licensed professionals who sell various insurance products—such as life, health, auto, and property insurance—to individuals and businesses. They work for insurance companies, brokerages, or as independent agents, advising clients on appropriate coverage and claims processing. The role requires strong sales skills, knowledge of insurance regulations, and registration with SUSEP (Superintendence of Private Insurance). Employment is widespread across urban centers, with many representatives earning commission-based income.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an insurance representative in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary significantly based on experience, location, and commission structure. On average, representatives earn a mix of fixed salary and commissions, with potential for higher income in metropolitan areas. For specific figures, consult local salary surveys or job portals like Catho or Indeed.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an insurance representative in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but a job offer from a Brazilian company is required. The employer must sponsor a work visa (VITEM V) or the applicant may qualify under the Mercosur Residence Agreement if a citizen of a member country. Additionally, SUSEP registration is mandatory, which may be obtained after arriving in Brazil.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities?**
A: Insurance representatives can move into senior agent, team leader, or branch management roles. Specialization in corporate insurance, reinsurance, or financial planning can lead to higher earnings. Some representatives become independent brokers or agency owners.
