# Well Driller — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 8113 (ISCO08)**

Well drillers operate and maintain drilling machinery to bore into the earth for water, oil, gas, or minerals, working in diverse settings from rural water wells to large-scale mining operations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or roughneck, advancing to driller, then site supervisor or drilling engineer with experience. Specializations in directional drilling, geothermal systems, or offshore operations can increase earnings and opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual adjustment of drilling speed and pressure is taken over by AI adaptive control systems
- Experience-based judgment of strata changes is replaced by real-time data analysis
- Record drilling logs and parameters, replaced by automated sensors and IoT.
- Repetitive maintenance checks (e.g., lubrication, bolt tightening) performed by robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes geological data to assist in optimizing drilling paths and depths
- Drone and camera AI identification of well site safety risks
- Machine learning predicts equipment failures, reducing unplanned downtime
- Augmented Reality (AR) remote guidance for complex operations

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency decision-making and troubleshooting ability
- Adaptability assessment for complex terrain and abnormal geology
- Team coordination and safety leadership responsibilities
- Fine manual skills in equipment repair

**Skills to build:**

- Geological data analysis software (e.g., Leapfrog)
- PLC and automation control system operation
- Drone operation and AI image analysis
- Equipment health monitoring and predictive maintenance
- Basic programming (Python/R) for customized reports
- Safety and compliance management (e.g., ISO 45001)

**Key growth areas:**

- Water Supply
- Geothermal
- Mining
- Infrastructure

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a well driller in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector: water well drilling pays lower, while oil and gas or mining sectors offer higher wages. Experienced drillers can expect above the national average, with significant regional differences.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a well driller in Brazil?**
A: It is possible but rare. The employer must prove a lack of qualified local workers and obtain CNIg authorization. The process is typically limited to specialized offshore or deep-drilling projects, and finding sponsorship is challenging for unskilled roles.

**Q: What qualifications are mandatory for well drillers in Brazil?**
A: Mandatory safety training (NR-20, NR-35) is required for oil and gas sites. While a technical course is not mandatory, it significantly improves job prospects and is often required by large companies.
