# Legal Secretary — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3342 (ISCO08)**

Legal secretaries in Brazil provide administrative and clerical support to lawyers and legal departments. Their duties include drafting and formatting legal documents, managing case files, scheduling hearings, and liaising with clients and courts. Proficiency in Portuguese legal terminology, office software, and organizational skills are essential. The role requires attention to detail and the ability to handle confidential information. While a law degree is not required, many legal secretaries have completed a technical course or higher education in secretarial studies or legal practice.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a legal secretary in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. In major cities like São Paulo, entry-level legal secretaries earn around 2,500 BRL per month (30,000 BRL annual), while experienced professionals can earn up to 5,000 BRL per month (60,000 BRL annual). Large law firms and multinational companies tend to offer higher salaries.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a legal secretary in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it requires employer sponsorship. The employer must obtain a work permit (VITEM V) from the Ministry of Justice and demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Brazilian citizen. Alternatively, citizens of Mercosur countries can apply for the Mercosur Residence Agreement, which simplifies the process.

**Q: Is it necessary to have a law degree to become a legal secretary?**
A: No, a law degree is not required. Most employers prefer candidates with a technical secretarial course or a tecnólogo degree in secretarial studies. Some legal secretaries do study law part-time, but it is not mandatory for the role.
