# Audiologist and Speech Therapist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2266 (ISCO08)**

Audiologists and Speech Therapists in Brazil diagnose and treat communication, swallowing, and hearing disorders. They work in clinics, hospitals, schools, and private practice, requiring a bachelor's degree in Fonoaudiologia and registration with the Conselho Regional de Fonoaudiologia (CRFa). The profession is regulated and growing due to awareness and aging population.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts in clinical settings, moving to supervisory roles, specialized therapies (e.g., pediatric, geriatric), or teaching/research in universities. Some therapists open private practices. Continuing education in specific disorders (e.g., autism, aphasia) enhances opportunities. Hospital management or regulatory roles are less common but possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial screening and automated report generation for standard hearing tests
- Preliminary analysis of voice recordings and marking of abnormal patterns
- Automatic scoring and preliminary suggestions for dysphagia screening questionnaires.
- Standardized speech recognition and feedback for speech therapy exercises
- Basic content generation and customization of patient education materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis: using deep learning models to analyze audiograms, speech spectra, and images to improve diagnostic accuracy
- Enhanced remote therapy: AI-driven real-time voice feedback system improves patient self-training at home
- Personalized treatment plan design: generate customized rehabilitation plans based on patient data (e.g., voice samples, hearing loss patterns)
- Data-Driven Prognostic Assessment: Analyze large clinical datasets to predict treatment responses and optimize treatment pathways
- Multilingual support: AI real-time translation and accent adaptation expand coverage to multicultural populations

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and human empathy for complex communication barriers.
- Empathy for building trust with patients and their families
- Collaborative decision-making and case management in multidisciplinary teams
- Clinical reasoning skills for rare or comorbid conditions
- Ethical decision-making and privacy protection (involving sensitive health data)

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for audiologists and speech therapists in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and region: public healthcare (SUS) offers around R$4,000–R$6,000 monthly, while private practice can reach R$8,000–R$12,000.

**Q: Can I immigrate to Brazil as an audiologist or speech therapist?**
A: Yes, but it's restricted. You need a job offer from a Brazilian employer, a valid VITEM V visa, and CRFa registration (which requires degree revalidation for foreign qualifications). Mercosur nationals may use the Residence Agreement.

**Q: What are the main pathways to advance in this career?**
A: Advancement includes pursuing a specialization (e.g., in audiology, child language), opening a private clinic, or transitioning to teaching and research at universities.
