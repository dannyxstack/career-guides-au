# Ambulance Worker — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3258 (ISCO08)**

Ambulance workers in Brazil, including drivers and attendants, provide emergency medical care and transport. They operate ambulances, assist paramedics, and stabilize patients en route to hospitals. Typically employed by public services (SAMU) or private companies, they require quick decision-making and compassion under pressure.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an ambulance worker in Brazil?**
A: Ambulance workers in Brazil earn between R$1,500 and R$3,000 per month. Public sector jobs (SAMU) may offer slightly higher pay and benefits, while private firms often pay less. Earnings depend on experience, region, and whether the worker is a driver or attendant.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa as an ambulance worker in Brazil?**
A: It is possible but uncommon. The VITEM V visa requires an employer-sponsored job offer and proof of qualifications (e.g., driver's license, certifications). Due to the lower skill level and abundant local workforce, employers rarely sponsor foreign ambulance workers. Mercosur nationals have an easier path via the Mercosur Residence Agreement.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for ambulance workers?**
A: Ambulance workers can progress to roles like ambulance supervisor, nursing technician (after completing a two-year course), or even paramedic (though distinct in Brazil). Some transition to fire services or private security. Additional certifications in emergency care enhance prospects.
