# Shop Sales Assistant — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 5223 (ISCO08)**

Shop Sales Assistants are frontline retail workers who assist customers in finding products, provide information, process payments, and maintain store displays. This entry-level occupation is widespread across Brazil's retail sector, from small local shops to large chain stores and shopping malls. The role requires strong interpersonal skills, basic numeracy, and familiarity with point-of-sale systems. It offers a common starting point for careers in sales, customer service, and retail management.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically moves from sales assistant to senior sales, then to supervisor or team leader. With experience and training, opportunities arise in visual merchandising, inventory management, or store management. Some assistants transition into purchasing, wholesale, or e-commerce roles. The occupation often serves as a stepping stone into broader business careers, especially for those who develop product knowledge and customer relationship skills.

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Shop Sales Assistant in Brazil?**
A: Pre-tax annual salaries generally range from BRL 18,000 to BRL 30,000, depending on region (higher in São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro) and whether commission is included. Formal employment includes benefits like transport vouchers, meal allowance, and 13th salary.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a Shop Sales Assistant in Brazil?**
A: It is very difficult. The VITEM V visa requires employer sponsorship and a labour market test proving no Brazilian worker is available, which is unlikely for this occupation. Mercosur nationals may work freely under the Residence Agreement.

**Q: What career growth opportunities exist for Shop Sales Assistants?**
A: Common paths include promotion to senior sales, supervisor, or store manager. Others move into visual merchandising, inventory control, or e-commerce roles. Additional training in retail management can accelerate advancement.
