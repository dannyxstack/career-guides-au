# Philosopher, Historian or Political Scientist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2633 (ISCO08)**

Social science professionals who study human society, culture, politics, and thought. They conduct research, analyze data, and teach at universities, work in think tanks, government agencies, and NGOs. In Brazil, many are employed in academia, public policy analysis, and cultural institutions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows academic track: PhD → postdoc → professor/researcher. Non-academic paths include policy analyst in government, think-tank researcher, or consultant. Advancement relies on publications, grants, and professional networks. Mid-career shifts into management or independent consulting are common.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a philosopher/historian/political scientist in Brazil?**
A: Entry-level researchers earn around 30,000–48,000 BRL annually; senior professors or high-level policy analysts can earn 70,000–120,000 BRL. Government positions follow statutory pay scales.

**Q: Can a foreign philosopher/historian/political scientist get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but mostly through employer sponsorship (VITEM V) for research/teaching at a university or research institute. The Mercosur Residence Agreement applies to citizens of member countries. Self-employment is not a viable migration route.

**Q: What non-academic careers are available for these professionals in Brazil?**
A: Policy analyst in government or NGOs, data analyst in market research firms, consultant in public policy or corporate social responsibility, journalist or writer specialized in social sciences.
