# Insulation Worker — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7124 (ISCO08)**

Insulation workers apply materials to surfaces to prevent heat loss, cold penetration, sound transmission, or fire spread. They cut, fit, and install insulation in buildings, industrial plants, and pipes. The role demands physical stamina, careful measurement, and compliance with safety standards. In Brazil, they are essential in construction, energy, and petrochemical sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

New entrants often start as assistants after short technical courses. Progression to team lead or supervisor is possible with experience and additional safety certifications. Some specialize in acoustic or fireproofing. Self-employment or small contracting firms are common. Career mobility is limited without formal education in construction management.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an insulation worker in Brazil?**
A: Monthly pre-tax salary ranges from 1,500 to 3,000 BRL, depending on region, experience, and sector. Senior workers or those in industrial plants can earn up to 4,500 BRL.

**Q: Can foreign insulation workers obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but only through employer sponsorship (VITEM V). The employer must prove no local workers are available. Mercosur nationals can work under the Mercosur Residence Agreement without a prior job offer.

**Q: Is insulation work a stable career in Brazil?**
A: Moderately stable, but heavily dependent on construction cycles. Certifications in fireproofing or acoustic insulation can improve job security. Self-employment is common during downturns.
