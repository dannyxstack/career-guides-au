# Sports Coach — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3422 (ISCO08)**

Sports coaches, instructors, and officials train, guide, and officiate athletes and teams in various sports disciplines. They develop training programs, improve performance, ensure safety, and enforce rules during competitions. In Brazil, these professionals work in clubs, schools, academies, and sports federations, often requiring registration with the Regional Council of Physical Education (CREF).

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a sports coach in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary widely by level, sport, and region. Entry-level coaches may earn around R$30,000–R$40,000 per year, while experienced coaches in elite clubs can earn over R$100,000. Public school coaches often have lower salaries but stable employment.

**Q: Can a foreign sports coach get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, via the VITEM V visa. The employer must prove that the coach has specialized qualifications and that no Brazilian candidate is available. The process involves submitting a work contract to the Ministry of Labor. Mercosur nationals have an easier pathway via the Mercosur Residence Agreement.

**Q: What is the career progression for sports coaches in Brazil?**
A: Many start as assistants or instructors in academies. With experience and certifications, they can become head coaches, technical coordinators, or open their own training centers. Officials can advance to referee at national or international competitions.
