# Meteorologist — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2112 (ISCO08)**

Meteorologists study atmospheric processes and weather patterns to forecast conditions, analyze climate trends, and advise on environmental risks. In Brazil, they work in government agencies (INMET, CEMADEN), agricultural consultancy, renewable energy firms, and media. Their work is critical for agriculture, disaster prevention, and aviation, combining data analysis with field observations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career paths progress from operational forecasting to specialized roles in climatology, hydrometeorology, or agrometeorology. Senior meteorologists can lead research teams, become consultants for agribusiness, or occupy management positions in weather services. Postgraduate degrees (master's, PhD) open doors to academia and senior research at institutions like INPE. Public sector often offers structured career ladders; private sector faster advancement for high performers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic generation of routine weather forecast text
- Compilation and preliminary statistical analysis of historical meteorological data
- Standard warning content editing based on numerical model output
- Simple fault diagnosis and standardized reporting of meteorological observation equipment
- Write descriptive reports on basic climate trends

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Interpretation and Probability Calibration of Ensemble Forecasts Using AI
- Combine deep learning to predict local extreme weather (e.g., thunderstorms, heavy rain).
- AI-assisted climate change attribution analysis and scenario simulation
- Automatically generate customized industry weather reports (agriculture, energy).
- Using natural language processing to automatically extract meteorological research progress from literature

**Human moat:**

- Physical intuition and interdisciplinary understanding of complex weather systems
- Deliver authoritative advice to the public and decision-makers during high-impact weather events
- Designing novel observation experiments and scientific methods for integrating multi-source data
- Assume legal and ethical responsibilities (e.g., consequences of failing to issue early warnings)
- Collaborative decision-making with related fields (hydrology, ecology)

**Skills to build:**

- Python/R programming and machine learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Principles and applications of weather AI models (e.g., FourCastNet, GraphCast)
- Explainable AI and uncertainty quantification techniques
- Climate big data visualization and interactive dashboard development
- Cross-disciplinary Skills (Economics, Risk Management, Public Policy)
- AI ethics and social impact assessment of weather warnings

**Key growth areas:**

- Climate change adaptation
- Agribusiness weather services
- Renewable energy forecasting
- Disaster risk management

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a meteorologist in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Entry-level positions in government or private weather services start around BRL 3,000–4,000 per month, while senior meteorologists in agribusiness or energy can earn BRL 10,000–15,000. Academics and researchers may have lower starting salaries but higher long-term potential.

**Q: Can a foreign meteorologist easily obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Direct employer sponsorship (VITEM V) is the main route; it requires a job offer and proof of qualifications. Mercosur nationals can use the Residence Agreement for easier entry. The process is moderately bureaucratic; having a recognized degree and registration with CFM/CREA facilitates approval.
