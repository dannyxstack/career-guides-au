# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications Engineering Technicians install, maintain, and repair telecommunication systems and equipment, including fiber optics, wireless networks, and satellite systems. They work under engineers to ensure network reliability and performance, often in field or office settings. In Brazil, they play a key role in expanding broadband and 5G infrastructure, supporting both urban and rural connectivity.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Brazil?**
A: Annual salaries range from 40,000 to 80,000 BRL for mid-level technicians, with senior roles reaching 110,000 BRL. Entry-level positions start around 30,000 BRL. Earnings vary by region, employer, and specialization.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign technicians to work in Brazil?**
A: Foreign technicians typically need an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa, which requires a job offer and CNIg approval. Alternatively, Mercosur nationals can use the simplified Mercosur Residence Agreement. Specialists may qualify for CNIg authorization with a labor market test.

**Q: What certifications are most valued for career advancement?**
A: Cisco CCNA, Huawei certifications, and ANATEL accreditations are highly valued. Specializations in 5G, fiber optics, or network security can significantly boost career prospects.
