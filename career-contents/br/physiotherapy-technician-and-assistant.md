# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3255 (ISCO08)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants support physiotherapists in treating patients with physical impairments, disabilities, or injuries, by preparing treatment areas, assisting with exercises, operating equipment, and documenting progress. They work in hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centers, and home care settings, playing a key role in patient recovery and wellness.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 57,435. Brazil's aging population and increasing prevalence of chronic conditions drive demand for rehabilitation services, sustaining employment growth for physiotherapy technicians. The occupation benefits from expansion of public healthcare (SUS) and private health plans. However, formal job opportunities are concentrated in urban areas and the Southeast region. Technicians with specialized skills in geriatric, sports, or neurological physiotherapy have better prospects.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins with a technical diploma and registration with CREFITO. Experienced technicians may specialize in areas like cardiorespiratory or manual therapy, or move into supervisory roles overseeing junior staff. Some pursue further education to become licensed physiotherapists (graduação). The occupation offers stable employment but limited upward mobility without higher qualifications.

- Basic vital signs monitoring and data entry

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physiotherapy technician in Brazil?**
A: The average annual salary ranges from R$24,000 to R$36,000 for entry-level positions, with experienced technicians earning up to R$48,000. Public sector positions may have additional benefits. Exact figures vary by region and employer.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a physiotherapy technician in Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The foreigner must have their technical diploma recognized by a Brazilian educational authority, obtain CREFITO registration, and secure an employer-sponsored work visa (VITEM V) or a Mercosur residence if from a member country. The process can take several months and requires demonstration of specialized skills not readily available locally.

**Q: What education level is required to become a physiotherapy technician?**
A: A technical diploma in physiotherapy (ensino técnico) is the standard requirement, typically a 1.5-2 year course. Some employers may accept candidates with no formal title but equivalent experience, but the title is mandatory for CREFITO registration. Higher education in physiotherapy is required to become a licensed physiotherapist.
