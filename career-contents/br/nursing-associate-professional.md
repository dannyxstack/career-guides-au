# Nursing Associate Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 3221 (ISCO08)**

Nursing associate professionals in Brazil, known as Técnicos em Enfermagem, provide direct patient care under the supervision of registered nurses. They work in hospitals, clinics, and community health centers, performing tasks such as administering medications, monitoring vital signs, and assisting with daily activities. The role requires a technical diploma and registration with the Regional Nursing Council (COREN).

Brazil's healthcare sector faces strong demand for nursing associate professionals due to population aging, expansion of primary care, and high turnover rates. The government's focus on strengthening the SUS (Unified Health System) and private healthcare investments will sustain job growth. Career progression typically involves gaining experience in specialized areas like intensive care, geriatrics, or occupational health. With additional training, technicians can become registered nurses or pursue administrative roles. Many take public-sector exams for stable positions in hospitals or clinics. Continuing education is essential for career advancement and higher salaries.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a nursing associate professional in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and sector. In the public sector, entry-level pay ranges from R$ 1,500 to R$ 2,500 per month, while private clinics may offer R$ 1,800 to R$ 3,000. With experience, salaries can reach R$ 3,500–R$ 5,000, especially in hospitals and specialized units.

**Q: Can a foreign nursing associate obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, if sponsored by an employer. The most common route is the VITEM V visa, requiring a job offer, employer approval from the Ministry of Labor, and proof of qualifications (diploma and COREN registration). Mercosur nationals can use the simplified residence agreement.
