# Allied Health Professional — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 2269 (ISCO08)**

Allied health professionals in Brazil include physiotherapists, speech therapists, occupational therapists, dietitians, and other specialists who support patient recovery and preventive care. They work in hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centers, and private practices, often collaborating with physicians and nurses. The field is regulated by professional councils requiring registration for practice.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an allied health professional in Brazil?**
A: Starting salaries for physiotherapists range from 30-50k BRL annually in public clinics, while experienced specialists in private practice can earn 80-120k BRL annually. Income varies by region and specialization.

**Q: Can a foreign allied health professional obtain a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, mainly through an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa. You must have your degree revalidated by a Brazilian university and register with the relevant professional council. The process can take 6-12 months.

**Q: Is the allied health profession regulated in Brazil?**
A: Yes, each branch (physiotherapy, speech therapy, etc.) has a federal council that mandates registration. Without it, you cannot legally practice. Foreign professionals must revalidate their degrees and pass a board exam.
