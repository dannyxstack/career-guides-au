# Fashion Model — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 5241 (ISCO08)**

Fashion models display clothing, accessories, and other products in fashion shows, photo shoots, and commercial advertising. They work with designers, photographers, and agencies to portray a desired image or lifestyle. This occupation requires physical fitness, poise, and the ability to follow directions quickly. In Brazil, the fashion industry is centered in São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro, with strong demand for diverse looks in digital and e-commerce campaigns.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with small local shoots, moving to agencies and larger clients. Many models diversify into acting, hosting, or social media influencing. Specialization in niche segments (e.g., fitness, plus-size, editorial) can improve job security. Some models transition into related roles like styling, photography, or agency scouting.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: How much do fashion models earn in Brazil?**
A: Earnings vary widely; beginner models may earn R$1,500–3,000 per assignment, while top models can earn R$50,000+ per campaign. Most income is project-based, with agency commissions.

**Q: Can foreign models work in Brazil without a visa?**
A: No. Foreign models need a VITEM V work visa sponsored by a Brazilian agency or a Mercosur residence permit if applicable. Independent modeling without authorization is illegal.
