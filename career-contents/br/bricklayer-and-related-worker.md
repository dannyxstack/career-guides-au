# Bricklayer — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7112 (ISCO08)**

Bricklayers and related workers lay bricks, blocks, and other types of building materials to construct and repair walls, partitions, arches, and other structures. They work on residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects, reading blueprints, mixing mortar, and ensuring structures are level and plumb. This occupation requires physical stamina, precision, and familiarity with construction techniques and safety standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level bricklayers typically learn on the job or through vocational courses. With experience, they can advance to foreman, supervisor, or specialized mason (e.g., refractory or decorative brickwork). Some become self-employed contractors. Further training may lead to related trades like stonemasonry or construction management. Certification from SENAI or similar improves career prospects and allows access to larger projects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive wall construction (e.g., straight walls, standard brick walls) is taken over by bricklaying robots or automatic laying equipment.
- Material handling and mortar mixing via automated conveyor systems
- Design and layout of simple masonry structures are automatically generated by BIM software
- On-site measurement and positioning using laser scanners and AI correction, reducing manual work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI visual inspection system assists in checking masonry verticality and mortar joint uniformity, improving quality inspection efficiency
- BIM modeling tools help bricklayers precisely lay bricks in complex curved structures
- Augmented reality (AR) glasses overlay construction drawings, reducing reading errors.
- AI brick layout algorithms optimize material usage and reduce waste

**Human moat:**

- Complex irregular masonry (curved, hollow, historical building restoration) cannot be standardized
- Flexible operation in unstructured environments such as bad weather and confined spaces
- Non-standard requirements from owners or architects for aesthetics and texture, relying on craftsman experience
- On-site safety management and emergency judgment, e.g., support, temporary fixing risks

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated bricklaying equipment and collaborating with robots
- Proficiency in BIM modeling and CAD drawing interpretation.
- Learn drone surveying and laser scanning data analysis
- Proficient in green building and energy-efficient masonry material construction
- Improve project management and multi-trade coordination skills
- Familiarity with building regulations and accessible masonry standards

**Key growth areas:**

- Residential Construction
- Infrastructure Projects
- Renovation
- Green Building

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a bricklayer in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. A starting bricklayer earns around R$1,500–R$2,000 per month, while experienced masons on formal contracts can earn R$2,500–R$4,000. Self-employed bricklayers may earn more during boom periods.

**Q: Can a foreign bricklayer get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, but it is employer-sponsored. The company must prove that no local worker is available. The VITEM V visa is the main route, and for Mercosur nationals, the residence agreement simplifies the process.

**Q: How can a bricklayer advance in their career?**
A: With experience, bricklayers can become foremen, supervisors, or specialize in decorative brickwork. Additional training in construction management or safety can lead to higher-paid roles.
