# Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic — career analysis · Brazil

**Occupation code: 7127 (ISCO08)**

Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics install, maintain, and repair climate-control systems in residential, commercial, and industrial settings across Brazil. They work with cooling and heating units, heat pumps, and refrigeration systems, ensuring efficiency and safety. The role requires technical skills in electrical and mechanical components, diagnostics, and refrigerants handling. With Brazil's tropical climate and expanding urban infrastructure, demand is steady, particularly in construction, hospitality, and food storage sectors. Certification from the National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology (INMETRO) or similar bodies is often required for handling refrigerants.

Experienced mechanics can advance to supervisory roles, start their own businesses, or specialize in industrial refrigeration, commercial HVAC, or solar-assisted systems. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic in Brazil?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. A junior technician may earn around BRL 24,000-30,000 annually, while experienced mechanics can make BRL 48,000-60,000. Self-employed professionals may earn more but have variable income.

**Q: Can a foreign Air Conditioning mechanic get a work visa for Brazil?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored VITEM V visa or the Mercosur Residence Agreement for citizens of Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, and other Mercosur states. The employer must demonstrate that the foreign worker fills a gap.

**Q: What is the career progression for this trade in Brazil?**
A: Many start as apprentices, become technicians, then supervisors or business owners. Specialization in industrial refrigeration or solar HVAC can increase earnings. Some move into teaching or sales.
