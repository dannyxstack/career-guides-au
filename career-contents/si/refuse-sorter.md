## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a refuse sorter in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a refuse sorter in Slovenia ranges from €1,200 to €1,400, which is close to the minimum wage. Pay may vary by employer, region (e.g., Ljubljana vs. rural areas), and experience. Overtime and night shifts may offer additional compensation.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a refuse sorter in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly qualified workers and typically requires a tertiary degree and a salary above the threshold, which refuse sorting does not meet. However, non-EU citizens may obtain a single work permit if an employer sponsors them, subject to a labor market test. EU citizens need no permit.

**Q: What are the main tasks of a refuse sorter in Slovenia?**
A: Refuse sorters in Slovenia manually or mechanically separate recyclable materials from waste, operate sorting equipment, ensure compliance with health and safety regulations, and maintain cleanliness in sorting facilities. They often work in recycling centers or waste management plants.
