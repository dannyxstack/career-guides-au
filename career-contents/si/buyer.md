## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a buyer in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for buyers in Slovenia vary by experience and sector. A mid-career buyer typically earns a gross monthly salary between €1,500 and €2,500. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior buyers can earn over €3,000. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: Can I work in Slovenia as a buyer through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, you can apply for an EU Blue Card in Slovenia if you have a valid job offer as a buyer. The job must require higher professional qualifications and offer a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovenia. The application is submitted to the Employment Service of Slovenia, followed by a residence permit.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for buyers in Slovenia?**
A: Buyers in Slovenia can advance to procurement manager, supply chain director, or strategic sourcing specialist roles. Many companies support further education, such as CIPS certifications. With experience, buyers may also move into international procurement or category management positions.
