## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Government Tax and Excise Official in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for tax and excise officials in Slovenia are determined by the public sector pay system. Entry-level gross monthly salaries range from approximately €1,500 to €2,000, depending on qualifications and experience. Senior officials may earn up to €3,000 or more. Additional allowances may apply for specific roles.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Government Tax and Excise Officials in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment in Slovenia. For tax and excise official roles, applicants typically need a relevant university degree and a job offer from the Financial Administration. Proficiency in Slovenian language is generally required. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EU citizen.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Government Tax and Excise Official in Slovenia?**
A: Duties include enforcing tax laws, collecting taxes and excise duties, conducting audits and inspections, processing tax returns, and preventing tax evasion. Officials work for the Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia (FURS). They may also advise taxpayers and represent the authority in legal proceedings. The role requires strong analytical and legal knowledge.
