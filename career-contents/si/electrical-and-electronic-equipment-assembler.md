## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Slovenia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of Slovenia, the average gross monthly salary for assemblers (ISCO 8212) is approximately €1,200 to €1,500 as of 2023, depending on experience and company. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced assemblers can earn up to €1,800.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a contract meeting the salary threshold (about €1,500 gross monthly in 2024). Alternatively, employers can apply for a single permit for employment. Official routes include the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What qualifications and working conditions are typical for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Slovenia?**
A: Employers usually require at least a vocational secondary education (e.g., in electronics) or equivalent experience. Work involves assembling components, soldering, testing, and quality checks. Conditions often include shift work, standing for long periods, and using protective equipment. On-the-job training is common.
