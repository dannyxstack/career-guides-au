## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships’ deck crews in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for ships’ deck crews in Slovenia ranges from €1,500 to €2,200, depending on experience, vessel type, and employer. Wages are often set by collective agreements in the maritime sector. Overtime and sea allowances may increase total compensation.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for ships’ deck crew jobs in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for the EU Blue Card if they hold a university degree or five years of professional experience and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (around €39,000 gross annually). Alternatively, a national work permit requires the employer to pass a labor market test, proving no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as ships’ deck crew in Slovenia?**
A: Seafarers must hold valid STCW (Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping) certificates, including Basic Safety Training, and a valid medical fitness certificate. Slovenian language skills are not mandatory for foreign crew but basic English is essential; knowledge of Slovenian may be helpful for communication.
