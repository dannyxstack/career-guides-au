## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for riggers and cable splicers in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, riggers and cable splicers typically earn between €1,200 and €1,800 gross per month, depending on experience, certifications, and the sector (construction, telecommunications, or power lines). Overtime and hazardous work allowances can increase total pay. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles or those with specialized skills may exceed €2,000.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU worker to obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for riggers and cable splicers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a single work and residence permit (employment permit) sponsored by a Slovenian employer. The EU Blue Card is available if the role requires higher education or 5+ years of professional experience, and the gross annual salary is at least 1.5 times the average Slovenian wage. Both routes require employer verification and labor market testing.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a rigger or cable splicer in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, vocational training or a secondary education in electrical engineering, construction, or telecommunications is required. Practical experience and certifications (e.g., for working at heights, high-voltage safety) are highly valued. Some employers offer on-the-job training. For senior roles, additional courses in project management or advanced splicing techniques may be beneficial.
