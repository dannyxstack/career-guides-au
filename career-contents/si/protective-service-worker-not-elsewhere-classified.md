## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for protective services workers in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary by specific role and employer. Security guards typically earn between €900 and €1,200 gross per month, while private investigators or specialized roles may earn more. Overtime and night shifts often increase total pay.

**Q: Can I work in Slovenia as a protective services worker through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, requiring a tertiary degree and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovenia. Most protective services roles do not meet these criteria; a standard work permit or single permit is more appropriate, applied through the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work in protective services in Slovenia?**
A: Requirements vary by role. Security guards must complete certified training and register with the Ministry of the Interior. Private investigators need a license from the Chamber of Private Investigators. Basic computer skills, a clean criminal record, and fluency in Slovenian are often required.
