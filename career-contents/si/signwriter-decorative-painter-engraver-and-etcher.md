## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for signwriters, decorative painters, engravers and etchers in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for these occupations in Slovenia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced professionals in specialized fields can earn up to €2,200. These figures are based on data from employment agencies and job portals.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card options for signwriters, decorative painters, engravers and etchers wishing to work in Slovenia?**
A: Foreign nationals typically need a single work and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Slovenian gross wage; this may not apply to this occupation. Alternatively, employers can obtain a work permit under the Employment of Foreigners Act, subject to a labor market test confirming no suitable EU candidate.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a signwriter, decorative painter, engraver or etcher in Slovenia?**
A: A vocational qualification in graphic arts, painting, or woodworking is typically required, often through a 3- or 4-year secondary vocational program. Apprenticeships with certified master craftsmen are common. The Slovenian National Qualifications Framework (SQF) recognizes related certificates. Additional courses in digital design and traditional techniques can enhance employability.
