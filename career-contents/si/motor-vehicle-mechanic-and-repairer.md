## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for motor vehicle mechanics in Slovenia ranges from €1,500 to €2,000, depending on experience and location. Entry-level mechanics may earn around €1,200, while those in specialized workshops or with advanced certifications can earn up to €2,500. Most salaries are paid in 12 monthly instalments plus holiday pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a foreign motor vehicle mechanic to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as motor vehicle mechanics via the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education qualification and the job meets the salary threshold (currently around €37,000 annually). Alternatively, a single work permit can be obtained through a Slovenian employer. EU/EEA citizens have free movement and only need to register residence.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a motor vehicle mechanic in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, becoming a motor vehicle mechanic typically requires completing a 3-4 year secondary vocational education in automotive mechanics, followed by a final examination. Apprenticeships with licensed workshops are common. Further specialization or certification from the Slovenian Chamber of Crafts can enhance career prospects, especially for modern vehicle technologies.
