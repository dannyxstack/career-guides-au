**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate weaving/woodworking design drawings based on customer size parameters
- Use a CNC engraving machine to cut and engrave complex patterns
- Repetitive standardized processes such as basket weaving and wood strip splicing
- Clerical work such as inventory management, order processing, and customer communication
- Product photography, description writing, and online store listing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI generative design to explore more forms and structural possibilities
- Customer preview using 3D scanning and digital twins
- Optimise product style and pricing through social media data analysis
- Quickly label wood defects and recommend cutting plans using AI tools
- Use AI-assisted color/texture matching to improve design consistency

**Human moat:**

- The tactile feel, detail, and irreproducibility of manual craftsmanship
- Customized communication and aesthetic judgment through deep co-creation with clients
- Cultural heritage and innovative interpretation of traditional crafts
- On-site debugging, repair, and flexible adaptation based on material characteristics
- Emotional value of brand story and craftsmanship spirit

**Skills to build:**

- Learn to operate digital manufacturing equipment such as CNC and 3D printing
- Social media marketing and e-commerce platform (Etsy etc.) operations

- Customer experience design and customized service process management
- Basic data analysis and inventory management tools (Excel/Lightroom)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for handicraft workers in wood, basketry and related materials in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for handicraft workers in wood, basketry and related materials in Slovenia ranges from approximately €1,100 to €1,500, depending on experience, employer, and region. Skilled artisans with specialized knowledge may earn higher rates, while entry-level positions start at the lower end. Regional cost-of-living variations can also affect pay.

**Q: What official routes exist for foreign handicraft workers to obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Slovenia?**
A: Foreign workers can apply for a Slovenian work permit via the single permit procedure. The EU Blue Card is typically for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree; for handicraft roles without such qualifications, a standard employment permit (e.g., for seasonal or permanent work) is more common. Employers must demonstrate labor market test requirements.

**Q: Are there opportunities for career advancement or specialization for handicraft workers in Slovenia?**
A: Yes, artisans can advance by mastering techniques, taking master craftsman exams, or starting their own businesses. Specializations like furniture restoration, artistic woodcarving, or traditional basketry are valued in Slovenia's cultural tourism sector. Further training through vocational programs or apprenticeships can enhance skills and lead to higher-paying roles or teaching positions.
