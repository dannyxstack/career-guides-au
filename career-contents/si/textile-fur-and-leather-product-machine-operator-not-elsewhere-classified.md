**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for textile, fur and leather products machine operators in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for machine operators in this sector in Slovenia is around EUR 1,100 to EUR 1,400, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced operators in specialized roles can earn up to EUR 1,600. Overtime and shift work often increase total pay.

**Q: Can a foreign textile machine operator work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovenia is designed for highly skilled workers holding a higher education degree or equivalent professional experience. For ISCO 8159 occupations, which typically do not require tertiary education, the Blue Card is generally not applicable. Workers may instead apply for a single work permit or employment visa through the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What are the main tasks of a textile, fur and leather products machine operator in Slovenia?**
A: Operators set up and monitor machines for cutting, sewing, finishing, or treating textile, fur, and leather materials. They ensure product quality, adjust machine settings, perform basic maintenance, and follow safety protocols. Skills include attention to detail, manual dexterity, and basic technical knowledge. The role is common in manufacturing plants across Slovenia.
