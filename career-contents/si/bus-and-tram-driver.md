## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for bus and tram drivers in Slovenia is around €1,600 to €2,000, depending on experience and employer. Net pay after taxes and social contributions is typically €1,200 to €1,500. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as bus or tram drivers in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Slovenia through a work permit or EU Blue Card. Bus and tram driving qualifies for a single work permit if the employer proves a labor shortage. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree or five years of professional experience, which may not apply to drivers. Alternatively, seasonal or standard employment permits are available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Slovenia?**
A: Bus drivers need a valid D or D1 driving license, a CPC certificate (Certificate of Professional Competence), and a tachograph card. Tram drivers require specific training from the operator, usually including a tram driving license. Both roles typically require a clean record and medical fitness. Courses are available through driving schools.
