## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a secretary in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for secretaries in Slovenia is approximately €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior secretaries can earn up to €1,800. These figures are before tax and social contributions.

**Q: Can a foreign secretary work in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovenia is reserved for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (around €30,000 in 2023). Secretaries typically do not meet these criteria. Instead, non-EU workers can apply for a single work and residence permit if an employer sponsors them.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a secretary in Slovenia?**
A: Most secretary positions in Slovenia require at least secondary education (high school diploma) and proficiency in Slovenian, as well as knowledge of office software. Additional qualifications such as a course in administrative support or fluency in English can improve job prospects. Relevant work experience is highly valued.
