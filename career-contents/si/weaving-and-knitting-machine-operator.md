## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Slovenia is around €1,300 to €1,600, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (€1,203.36 gross as of 2025), while experienced operators in larger textile factories can earn higher rates.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as weaving and knitting machine operators in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovenia is reserved for highly skilled workers with higher education degrees or equivalent, not for machine operators. However, non-EU nationals may obtain a work permit through the Slovenian Employment Service (ZRSZ) under the standard employment permit system, if the employer demonstrates a labor shortage.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for weaving and knitting machine operators in Slovenia?**
A: With experience, operators can advance to senior operator, shift supervisor, or quality control roles. Further training in textile technology or machine maintenance can lead to technician or production manager positions. The sector offers on-the-job training and occasional vocational courses, though opportunities depend on company size and industry demand.
