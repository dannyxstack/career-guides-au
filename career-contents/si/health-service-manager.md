## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health services managers in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for health services managers in Slovenia typically ranges from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience, qualifications, and the sector (public or private). Senior managers in larger institutions may earn higher. Salaries are generally competitive with other management roles in healthcare.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for health services managers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU health services managers can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovenia. Alternatively, a national work permit is possible if the employer demonstrates labour market needs. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a health services manager in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a higher education degree in health management, public health, business administration, or a related field is required. Relevant work experience in healthcare administration is highly valued. Registration with the Slovenian Health Council may be needed for certain roles, especially in public institutions. Continuing education is recommended for career advancement.
