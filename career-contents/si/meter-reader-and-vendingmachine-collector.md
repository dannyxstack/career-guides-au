## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Meter Readers and Vending-machine Collectors in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, the average gross monthly salary for this occupation ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and employer. This is slightly above the national minimum wage (€1,203.83 gross as of 2024). Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Meter Readers in Slovenia via the work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit, usually sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation due to its skill level (ISCO-08 9623 requires no higher education) and income threshold. A standard work permit (e.g., under the Single Permit procedure) is the usual route.

**Q: What are the main duties of Meter Readers and Vending-machine Collectors in Slovenia?**
A: They read utility meters (electricity, gas, water) and collect money from vending machines, parking meters, or coin-operated devices. Duties include recording readings, inspecting meters for tampering, and performing basic maintenance. The job often involves walking routes and using handheld devices for data entry.
