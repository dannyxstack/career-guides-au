## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for tailors and dressmakers in Slovenia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of Slovenia, the average gross monthly salary for workers in textile and leather occupations (including tailors and dressmakers) is around €1,200–€1,500. Actual pay varies by experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while skilled professionals in bespoke tailoring can earn higher rates.

**Q: Can foreign tailors and dressmakers work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Slovenia as tailors or dressmakers through the standard employment permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher-education qualification and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage in Slovenia. For this occupation, the standard work permit, based on a job offer and labor market test, is more common.

**Q: What is the career outlook for tailors and dressmakers in Slovenia?**
A: The demand for tailors and dressmakers in Slovenia is modest, with opportunities mainly in custom tailoring, costume production, and clothing alterations. The profession is declining due to mass-produced clothing, but niche markets (e.g., traditional folk costumes, wedding attire) offer steady work. Skilled artisans with sewing and pattern-making expertise may find self-employment or work in small workshops viable.
