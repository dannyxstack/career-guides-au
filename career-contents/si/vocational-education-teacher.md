## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a vocational education teacher in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for vocational education teachers in Slovenia are determined by the public sector pay scale and vary based on experience and qualifications. Gross monthly salaries typically range from €1,800 to €2,200 for full-time positions. Additional allowances may apply for seniority or specialisations.

**Q: How can a non-EU vocational teacher obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and residence permit to teach in Slovenia. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Employers must first register the vacancy with the Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a vocational education teacher in Slovenia?**
A: Vocational teachers in Slovenia typically need a master's degree in education or their vocational field, plus pedagogical training. They must pass a professional exam and register with the National Education Institute. Practical experience in the relevant trade is often required. Continuous professional development is mandatory.
