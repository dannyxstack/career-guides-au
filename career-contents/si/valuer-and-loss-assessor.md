## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a valuer or loss assessor in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for valuers and loss assessors in Slovenia range from €1,400 to €2,200 gross per month for mid-level positions. Senior professionals or those with specialized certifications can earn up to €3,000 gross. Entry-level roles start around €1,200. Actual pay depends on experience, employer, and region.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a valuer or loss assessor in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Slovenia if they have a valid employment contract, meet the salary threshold (typically at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovenia), and hold a recognized higher education qualification (e.g., degree in economics or real estate). Alternatively, a standard single work permit is possible for lower-skilled roles.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a valuer or loss assessor in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in economics, finance, real estate, or a related field is required. Additionally, certification by the Slovenian Institute of Auditors or similar bodies may be necessary for licensed valuations. Practical experience through internships or apprenticeships is highly valued. Ongoing professional development is expected to maintain certification.
