## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for commissioned armed forces officers in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for commissioned officers in the Slovenian Armed Forces are based on rank and service years. A second lieutenant starts at around €1,500–€1,800 gross per month, while a colonel may earn €3,000–€4,000. Additional allowances for hazardous duty, foreign missions, or housing can apply.

**Q: Can non-Slovenian citizens become commissioned armed forces officers in Slovenia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No, commissioned officer positions in the Slovenian Armed Forces are reserved exclusively for Slovenian citizens. Non-citizens cannot apply via the EU Blue Card or any work visa route. Only individuals who have acquired Slovenian citizenship through naturalisation or other legal means may pursue this career.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a commissioned armed forces officer in Slovenia?**
A: Candidates must be Slovenian citizens, at least 18 years old, have completed secondary education (matura), and pass physical, psychological, and medical tests. They then undergo officer candidate school at the Military Schools Centre, followed by a probationary period. A university degree is preferred for higher ranks.
