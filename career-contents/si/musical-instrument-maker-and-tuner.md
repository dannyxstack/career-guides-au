## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for musical instrument makers and tuners in Slovenia?**
A: Musical instrument makers and tuners in Slovenia earn between €1,200 and €1,800 gross per month, depending on experience and specialization. Entry-level workers may earn less, while highly skilled craftspeople can earn more. For exact figures, consult the Statistical Office of Slovenia or the Ministry of Labour, as wages vary by region and employer.

**Q: How can non-EU musical instrument makers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently around 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, a single work permit (unified permit for work and residence) is possible. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Employment Service of Slovenia. Always check current requirements with the Slovenian embassy.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a musical instrument maker in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a vocational qualification in musical instrument making (e.g., from a program like “Glasbeni izdelovalec instrumentov”) is required, often through apprenticeships. Many artisans join the Chamber of Craft and Small Business of Slovenia. Continuing education through workshops or specialized courses abroad is common to refine skills. Formal certificates from recognized schools enhance employment prospects.
