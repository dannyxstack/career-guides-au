## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building architect in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for building architects in Slovenia ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Senior architects or those with specialized skills may earn higher. Benefits often include bonuses and additional allowances.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for building architects in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU architects can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €27,000 in 2024), a recognized university degree, and employment contract. Alternatively, a single permit for work and residence is issued by the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a building architect in Slovenia?**
A: To practice as an architect in Slovenia, you need a master's degree in architecture from a recognized university and registration with the Slovenian Chamber of Architects (ZAPS). EU qualifications are recognized under Directive 2005/36/EC. Continuing professional development is required to maintain registration and stay updated with local building codes.
