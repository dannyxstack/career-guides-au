## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for stonemasons and related workers in Slovenia ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while skilled carvers or specialists can earn up to €2,200. Pay is often higher in construction-heavy regions like Ljubljana.

**Q: How can a foreign stonemason obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Foreign stonemasons can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card if they hold a relevant tertiary degree (not typical for stonemasons) or via a standard single work permit with employer sponsorship. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable Slovenian or EU candidate is available. The permit is issued for up to one year, renewable. No specific quota for stonemasons exists.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for stonemasons in Slovenia?**
A: Stonemasons in Slovenia can progress to master craftsman (mojster) by passing a trade exam, leading to higher pay and supervisory roles. They may specialize in restoration work on historic buildings or move into business ownership as self-employed artisans. Further training in stone processing or CNC operation opens roles in stone fabrication workshops.
