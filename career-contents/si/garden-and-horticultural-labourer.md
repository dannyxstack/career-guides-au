## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Slovenia ranges from 800 to 1,200 EUR, depending on experience, employer, and seasonality. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is approximately 1,203 EUR gross for full-time work, but many in this sector earn at or near this level. Seasonal work is common, and wages may vary accordingly.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for garden and horticultural labourers in Slovenia?**
A: For non-EU citizens, the standard route is a single work permit (employment permit) sponsored by an employer after demonstrating that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is typically not applicable for this occupation as it requires higher education or a high salary threshold. Short-term seasonal work permits are also available for up to 90 days under bilateral agreements.

**Q: What are the main tasks of garden and horticultural labourers in Slovenia?**
A: Typical tasks include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, mowing lawns, operating garden machinery, and assisting in greenhouse operations. Labourers work in parks, private gardens, nurseries, or agricultural holdings. Physical fitness is essential, and although no formal qualifications are mandatory, vocational certificates in horticulture or landscaping can improve job prospects and earning potential.
