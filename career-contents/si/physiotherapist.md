## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physiotherapists in Slovenia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for physiotherapists in Slovenia typically ranges from 1,200 to 1,800 EUR, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and location. Entry-level positions start around 1,000 EUR, while senior roles in private clinics can exceed 2,000 EUR. Overtime and specializations may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign physiotherapists to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovenia (approx. 26,000 EUR in 2023). Alternatively, a standard work permit via employer sponsorship is possible. EU/EEA citizens have free movement and only need to register residence.

**Q: What are common employment settings for physiotherapists in Slovenia?**
A: Physiotherapists in Slovenia work in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, nursing homes, private clinics, and sports clubs. Many are self-employed, contracting with health insurance funds. The public sector offers stable employment, while private practice provides flexibility. Opportunities are growing in geriatric and sports physiotherapy.
