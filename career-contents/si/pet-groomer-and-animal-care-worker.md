## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level pet groomers typically earn around €1,000-€1,200 gross per month. Experienced workers in well-established salons or veterinary clinics can earn up to €1,500-€1,800 gross. Self-employed groomers may have higher but variable earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as pet groomers or animal care workers in Slovenia through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and usually requires a higher education degree, which may not apply to most pet groomers. Alternatively, a single work permit (combined residence and work permit) is available for jobs where the employer proves no suitable EU candidate exists.

**Q: What qualifications or certifications are needed to work as a pet groomer in Slovenia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not legally mandatory, but employers often prefer completion of a certified grooming course (e.g., from the Slovenian Kennel Club or private schools). Practical experience and a portfolio help. Animal care workers in shelters may need no specific training, though knowledge of animal handling and first aid is beneficial.
