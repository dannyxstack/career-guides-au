## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Construction Manager in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for Construction Managers in Slovenia typically range from €1,800 to €3,200 gross per month, depending on experience, project complexity, and employer. Senior managers or those on large infrastructure projects may earn higher. Benefits often include bonuses, company car, and travel allowances.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Construction Manager in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Slovenia if they have a valid job offer for a highly qualified position (including construction manager) with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. A completed higher education degree (minimum 3 years) and professional recognition of qualifications are required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Construction Manager in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in civil engineering, architecture, or construction management is required. Professional experience and relevant certifications (e.g., project management) are highly valued. Licenses for managing certain infrastructure or hazardous projects may be needed. Membership in the Slovenian Chamber of Engineers is often mandatory for leading large-scale projects.
