## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a domestic housekeeper in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for domestic housekeepers in Slovenia ranges from approximately €900 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and location. Net pay is typically around €700–€950. Salaries may be lower than other sectors but often include benefits like meals or accommodation. Paid overtime and bonuses are rare.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a domestic housekeeper in Slovenia under a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as domestic housekeepers through a Slovenian work permit, typically via the Employment Permit or the Single Permit for third-country workers. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree and a job in a highly skilled occupation. Employers must obtain a labor market test and apply for a work permit on your behalf.

**Q: What are the main duties of a domestic housekeeper in Slovenia?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Slovenia perform cleaning, laundry, ironing, and tidying tasks in private households. They may also assist with meal preparation, light cooking, and running errands. Duties often include making beds, washing dishes, and maintaining household supplies. Some positions require caring for children or elderly relatives, depending on the employer's needs.
