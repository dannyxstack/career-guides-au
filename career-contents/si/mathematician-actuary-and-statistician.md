## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians in Slovenia typically ranges between €2,500 and €3,500, depending on experience, sector and location. Salaries in insurance, finance and IT tend to be higher, while public sector roles may be lower but offer stability.

**Q: How can a non-EU mathematician, actuary or statistician obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Slovenia via a standard work permit if a job offer is approved by the Employment Service. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a valid employment contract and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €2,000/month in 2024). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the career prospects for mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians in Slovenia?**
A: Career prospects are favourable due to growing demand in finance, insurance, IT and research. Opportunities exist in banks, insurance companies, government agencies and academic institutions. Actuaries are particularly sought after in insurance, while statisticians find roles in data analysis and market research. Overall, the field offers stable employment and advancement potential.
