## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for secondary education teachers in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for secondary education teachers in Slovenia ranges from €1,800 to €2,200, depending on experience and qualifications. According to the Statistical Office of Slovenia, teachers with more seniority and additional responsibilities earn higher pay, with the national base salary set by collective agreements.

**Q: What visa or permit routes are available for non-EU secondary teachers to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU teachers can apply for a single work permit or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid employment contract, a recognized teaching qualification, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. The employer must obtain a positive opinion from the Employment Service of Slovenia. Other routes include intra-company transfer or special agreements.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a secondary school teacher in Slovenia?**
A: To teach in Slovenian secondary schools, you need a master's degree in the relevant subject and a pedagogical qualification. Foreign degrees must be recognized by ENIC-NARIC Slovenia. Teachers must also pass a professional exam and register with the Chamber of Education. Continuous professional development is required for career advancement.
