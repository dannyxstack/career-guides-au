## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for optometrists and ophthalmic opticians in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. In 2024, gross monthly pay for optometrists in Slovenia typically ranges from €1,500 to €2,400. Public sector salaries may be lower, while private practice and specialized clinics often offer higher compensation, especially in urban areas like Ljubljana.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for optometrists wanting to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid employment contract, a university degree in optometry (or equivalent), and meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovenia). Alternatively, a national work permit is possible for lower-skilled roles, but the Blue Card is the primary route for highly qualified professionals.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as an optometrist in Slovenia?**
A: You need a completed university degree in optometry (typically 3-5 years) from a recognized institution. Registration with the Slovenian Medical Chamber (Zdravniška zbornica Slovenije) is mandatory. For ophthalmic opticians, additional certification may be required. EU qualifications are generally recognized; non-EU qualifications need verification.
