## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for managing directors and chief executives in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by company size and industry. According to the Statistical Office of the Republic of Slovenia, the gross monthly salary for managers typically ranges from €4,000 to €10,000, with top executives in large corporations earning upwards of €15,000. Bonuses and profit sharing can substantially increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign managing director to work in Slovenia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid employment contract or binding job offer for at least one year, meet the salary threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross salary in Slovenia, approximately €2,300 per month), and have a recognized university degree or five years of relevant professional experience. Alternatively, a single permit for employment and residence can be obtained through employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are typically required for managing director roles in Slovenia?**
A: Most positions require a master's degree in business administration, finance, or a related field, combined with at least 10 years of senior management experience. Fluency in Slovenian and English is essential, as is a deep understanding of local market regulations and business culture. Demonstrable leadership in strategic planning and organizational change is highly valued.
