## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for debt collectors in Slovenia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of Slovenia, the average gross monthly salary for debt collectors and related workers (ISCO 4214) is approximately €1,200 to €1,500. Salaries vary by experience, employer, and region, with entry-level positions starting around €1,000 and senior roles reaching €1,800 or more.

**Q: Can debt collectors obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens seeking to work as debt collectors in Slovenia must obtain a work permit through employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals, but debt collector roles may not meet the required skill level. The Employment Service of Slovenia processes applications, and the employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications or licenses are needed to work as a debt collector in Slovenia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers often prefer candidates with a secondary education in commerce or law. On-the-job training is common. Debt collectors must adhere to Slovenian debt collection regulations and may need to register with the Chamber of Commerce if operating independently. No specific state license is required for employed collectors.
