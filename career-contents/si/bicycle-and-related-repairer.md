## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bicycle repairer in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for bicycle repairers in Slovenia ranges from €1,200 to €1,500. Entry-level positions often start at minimum wage (approximately €1,200 gross), while experienced technicians can earn up to €1,800. Many repairers are self-employed, so income may vary based on client base and season.

**Q: What are the official work visa options for non-EU citizens wanting to work as bicycle repairers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a single work permit through employer sponsorship. For the EU Blue Card, they need a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (currently around €2,300/month). Bicycle repair is not listed as a shortage occupation, so standard permit conditions apply.

**Q: What training or certifications are required to become a bicycle repairer in Slovenia?**
A: No formal degree is required, but vocational training in mechanics or a specialized bicycle repair course is advantageous. The Slovenian Chamber of Commerce offers an optional certification for professional bicycle mechanics. Many employers value practical experience, a driving license (B category), and basic computer skills for parts inventory management.
