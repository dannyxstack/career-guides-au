**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic odds calculation and dynamic adjustment
- Simple bet processing and payment (replaced by self-service terminals)
- Player Identity Verification and Credit Check Automation
- Basic customer inquiries (chatbot)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Multilingual AI translation helps dealers handle international clients

**Human moat:**

- Authentic emotional interaction and trust building with players
- Human adjudication of disputes over complex rules or cheating
- Entertainment atmosphere creation and personalised service
- Sensitive judgment on legal and regulatory compliance

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bookmakers and croupiers in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, the average gross monthly salary for gaming workers (ISCO 4212) ranges from €1,200 to €1,700, depending on experience and employer. Casinos in major cities like Ljubljana or Portorož may offer higher pay. Additional tips and bonuses can also supplement income.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as gaming workers in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovenia is intended for highly qualified workers; gaming occupations typically do not meet the skill threshold. Instead, non-EU nationals may apply for a single work permit (delovno dovoljenje) if an employer sponsors them, subject to labour market test. Specifics are handled by the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a croupier in Slovenia?**
A: Formal education is not mandatory, but employers often require completion of a croupier training course, which covers game rules, dealing techniques, and customer service. Fluency in Slovenian and English is beneficial. Relevant experience in hospitality or dealing can improve job prospects.
