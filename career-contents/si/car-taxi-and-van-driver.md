## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for motor vehicle drivers in Slovenia is between €1,200 and €1,400, according to the Statistical Office. Taxi drivers often earn per trip plus tips, while van drivers may have fixed salaries. Self-employed drivers have variable income. Actual earnings depend on hours, employer, and region.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a driver in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree (ISCED 6+) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. €2,200/month). Most driver positions do not meet the skill or salary threshold, so a standard work permit issued through employer sponsorship is the typical route for non-EU drivers.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a taxi or van driver in Slovenia?**
A: You need a valid driver’s license (category B for cars; higher for vans), a professional competence certificate (CPC) for passenger transport, and a clean criminal record. Taxi drivers require a municipality-issued taxi license. Knowledge of Slovenian or English and local roads is beneficial. Self-employment requires registration with the tax authority.
