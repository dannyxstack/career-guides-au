**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sweepers and related labourers in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sweepers in Slovenia typically ranges from the national minimum wage (€1,203.36 in 2024) to around €1,400. Actual pay depends on employer, location, and experience. Many workers receive additional benefits such as meal vouchers or transport allowance.

**Q: Can sweepers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovenia?**
A: Sweepers and related labourers are classified as low-skilled occupations and generally do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Third-country nationals can instead apply for a standard work permit sponsored by an employer, subject to a labour market test.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for sweepers in Slovenia?**
A: Career advancement for sweepers is limited but possible. With experience, one may become a team leader or supervisor in cleaning services. Further training in specialized cleaning (e.g., industrial or hazardous waste) or environmental services can lead to better-paid positions. Some workers transition to related fields like facility maintenance or waste management.
