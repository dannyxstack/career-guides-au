## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for athletes and sports players in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sport and performance level. Professional athletes in top leagues (e.g., football, basketball) may earn between €2,000 and €10,000 per month, while those in lower-tier or amateur sports earn less. Many athletes also receive bonuses, sponsorships, or support from national federations. Income is often supplemented by coaching or other sports-related work.

**Q: How can a foreign athlete obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Foreign athletes typically need a single work and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, including athletes with a valid contract meeting salary thresholds. Alternatively, a standard employment permit via a Slovenian club is common. Applications are processed by the Ministry of the Interior. Specific requirements depend on nationality and contract terms.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for athletes in Slovenia after retirement from professional sports?**
A: Many retired athletes transition to coaching, sports management, or teaching physical education. Others work in sports administration, event organization, or open private training centers. Slovenia’s strong sports culture also supports roles in fitness, recreation, and sports journalism. Federations and clubs often offer mentorship programs to help athletes prepare for second careers.
