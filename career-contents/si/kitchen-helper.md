## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Slovenia?**
A: The minimum monthly gross salary for kitchen helpers in Slovenia is around €1,203 (as of 2024). However, actual pay varies by employer, location, and experience. Many kitchen helpers earn between €1,200 and €1,500 gross per month, with potential tips and overtime pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a kitchen helper in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers, not for kitchen helpers. Non-EU nationals can apply for a Slovenian work permit via the Employment Service, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. The single work permit allows residence and work. Other routes include the seasonal work permit (up to 9 months) for temporary positions.

**Q: What are the main duties of a kitchen helper in Slovenia?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Slovenia assist chefs with food preparation, washing dishes, cleaning workstations, and storing ingredients. They may peel vegetables, operate dishwashers, and ensure kitchen hygiene. Language skills (Slovene or English) and basic food safety knowledge are often required. Work settings include restaurants, hotels, and institutional catering.
