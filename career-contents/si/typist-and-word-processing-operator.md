- Generating documents via speech recognition and automatic transcription

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted document layout and format optimization to improve aesthetics

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for typists and word processing operators in Slovenia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of the Republic of Slovenia, the average gross monthly salary for administrative and support service activities, which includes typists, was approximately €1,350 in 2023. Salaries vary based on experience, employer, and location, with entry-level positions starting around €1,100 and experienced workers earning up to €1,700 per month.

**Q: What are the work visa options for typists moving to Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU typists can work in Slovenia via a single work permit, which combines a residence and work permit. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as typists typically do not meet the higher education or high-salary requirement. Alternatively, the Slovenia Work Permit for posted workers or intra-company transfers may be possible. Employers must demonstrate that no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for typists and word processing operators in Slovenia?**
A: Employers in Slovenia seek typists with proficiency in Microsoft Office, especially Word and Excel, and fast typing speeds (60+ words per minute). Multilingual ability, particularly in English and German, is highly valued. Attention to detail, accuracy, and experience with document formatting or data entry software further enhance employability. Certification in typing or office administration is beneficial.
