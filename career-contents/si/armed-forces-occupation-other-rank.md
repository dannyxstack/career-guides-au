## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other ranks in the Slovenian Armed Forces?**
A: Basic monthly salary for other ranks ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,800 gross, depending on rank, length of service, and special allowances such as deployment or hazardous duty. Additional benefits include housing, meals, and healthcare.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as other ranks in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not applicable for armed forces occupations in Slovenia. Military service is reserved for Slovenian citizens. Non-EU nationals cannot obtain a work visa or Blue Card for these roles, as they are excluded from the general employment migration routes.

**Q: What training is required for other ranks in the Slovenian military?**
A: Recruits must complete a 6-month basic military training program at the Military School. Training includes physical fitness, weapons handling, tactics, and discipline. After basic training, soldiers undergo specialization courses based on their assigned unit. Annual refresher training is mandatory.
