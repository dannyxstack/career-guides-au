## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners in Slovenia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for domestic cleaners in Slovenia typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,300, depending on experience and employer. The national minimum wage for 2024 is €1,203.80 gross per month. Many cleaners work part-time or on an hourly basis, with hourly rates around €6.50 to €8.00 gross.

**Q: What visa or work permit is required for a foreign national to work as a domestic cleaner in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a single work and residence permit (enotno dovoljenje) to work in Slovenia. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Slovenian or EU citizen. Seasonal work permits are also possible for temporary cleaning jobs. The EU Blue Card is not available for this occupation as it targets highly skilled workers.

**Q: Are there formal qualifications or training needed to become a domestic cleaner in Slovenia?**
A: No formal qualifications are required to work as a domestic cleaner in Slovenia. However, employers may prefer candidates with prior experience or a basic hygiene certificate. On-the-job training is common. Courses in cleaning techniques or customer service can improve job prospects. Proficiency in Slovenian is often expected, especially for roles in private households.
