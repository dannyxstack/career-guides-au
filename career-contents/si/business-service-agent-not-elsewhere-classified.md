**Q: What are the work visa options for Business Services Agents Not Elsewhere Classified in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available if you have a valid job offer, higher education or 5 years of experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times Slovenia's average gross salary (approx. €1,900/month in 2024). Otherwise, a standard single permit is processed by the Employment Service.

They often liaise between companies and external partners, ensuring smooth operations and compliance with company policies and Slovenian regulations.
