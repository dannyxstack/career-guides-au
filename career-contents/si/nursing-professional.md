## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for nursing professionals in Slovenia?**
A: According to official data, the average gross monthly salary for nursing professionals in Slovenia is around €1,800 to €2,200, depending on experience and specialization. Salaries in public healthcare are set by collective agreements, with additional pay for night shifts and overtime.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign nursing professionals to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nursing professionals can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national work permit. Requirements include a recognized nursing degree, registration with the Nurses and Midwives Association of Slovenia, and a job offer. The employer must apply for a work permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a nursing professional in Slovenia?**
A: To practice as a nursing professional in Slovenia, you need a completed nursing degree (at least 3 years of study) recognized by the Ministry of Health. You must register with the Nurses and Midwives Association of Slovenia and pass a language exam in Slovenian at B2 level. Continuous professional development is mandatory.
