- Manually delivering letters and small parcels by address

- Read paper maps to plan delivery routes

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI Route Planning Software to Optimise Delivery Order and Timing
- Use smart handheld devices to receive delivery instructions and communicate with customers in real time
- Remote monitoring of multiple package delivery status via drones/unmanned vehicles
- Use predictive analytics tools to anticipate peak hours and dynamically adjust delivery strategies

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers and package deliverers in Slovenia?**
A: The salary for this occupation in Slovenia generally ranges from the minimum wage (approximately €1,203.36 gross per month in 2024) to around €1,500 gross per month, depending on the employer, hours worked, and any bonuses. Many workers are paid per delivery or on an hourly basis, so earnings can vary.

**Q: What is the visa route for working as a messenger or package deliverer in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU workers require a single work and residence permit, which the employer must apply for. This is a standard process for low-skilled jobs. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, which this occupation does not meet.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for messengers and package deliverers in Slovenia?**
A: With experience, messengers and deliverers can move into supervisory roles such as dispatch coordinator or logistics team leader. Some may transition into customer service or warehouse management. Self-employment as a courier is also an option, allowing for flexible hours and potentially higher earnings.
