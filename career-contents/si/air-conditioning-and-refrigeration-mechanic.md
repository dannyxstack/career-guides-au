## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Slovenia ranges from about 1,200 EUR for entry-level positions to over 2,000 EUR for experienced professionals, with a median around 1,600 EUR. Pay varies based on employer, region, and certifications.

**Q: Can non-EU foreign workers obtain an EU Blue Card for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic jobs in Slovenia?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized higher education qualification and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, a single work and residence permit is available for lower-skilled roles. Slovenia also has a list of shortage occupations that may facilitate work permits.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a vocational secondary education in refrigeration, air conditioning, or a related field is required. Licenses for handling refrigerants and working with electrical systems may be necessary. Professional experience or an apprenticeship is highly valued. Continued training is often needed to stay updated with new technologies and regulations.
