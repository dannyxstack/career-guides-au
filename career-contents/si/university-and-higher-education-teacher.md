## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for university and higher education teachers in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary by rank and institution. According to the Ministry of Education, assistant professors earn roughly €2,500–€3,200 gross monthly, associate professors €3,200–€4,000, and full professors up to €5,000 or more. Additional income may come from research projects or teaching extra courses. Public university salaries are regulated by collective agreements.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for foreign university teachers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Requirements include a job offer as a university teacher, recognized qualifications, and a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €3,200/month in 2024). Alternatively, a single work permit (employment permit) is possible, but the Blue Card offers streamlined family reunification. All applications are processed by the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What are the typical qualifications and career progression for university teachers in Slovenia?**
A: A PhD in the relevant field is required, often followed by postdoctoral experience. Habilitations (venia legendi) are needed for associate and full professorships. Progression from assistant to associate to full professor depends on research output, teaching quality, and securing habilitation. Positions at public universities are filled through open competitions. Some institutions also require proficiency in Slovenian, though English-taught programs may have exceptions.
