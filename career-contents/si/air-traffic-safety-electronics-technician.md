## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians in Slovenia vary based on experience and employer. At Slovenia Control (state air navigation service), entry-level technicians earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while senior technicians can earn up to €3,500 gross. These figures include allowances for shift work and specialist skills.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified employment) or a standard work permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Slovenian gross salary. A work permit requires employer sponsorship and a labor market test showing no suitable EU candidate. Both routes lead to temporary residence.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Slovenia?**
A: Candidates typically need a degree in electronics, telecommunications, or a related field (e.g., from the University of Ljubljana's Faculty of Electrical Engineering). Additional certification from the Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) and passing Slovenia Control’s technical exams are required. Ongoing training in radar, navigation aids, and communication systems is mandatory to maintain licensure.
