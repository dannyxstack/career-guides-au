## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building caretaker in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for building caretakers in Slovenia is approximately 1,200 EUR, varying by location and employer. Net pay after taxes and contributions is around 900-1,000 EUR. Overtime and bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain an EU Blue Card to work as a building caretaker in Slovenia?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is intended for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Building caretaking typically does not meet these criteria. Non-EU citizens can instead apply for a single work permit (dovoljenje za prebivanje in delo) through a Slovenian employer.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building caretaker in Slovenia?**
A: Building caretakers in Slovenia are responsible for cleaning, minor maintenance, waste management, and security checks of residential or commercial buildings. They may also handle mail delivery, supervise heating systems, and coordinate with external service providers. Good physical health and basic technical skills are required.
