## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a travel guide in Slovenia?**
A: Travel guides in Slovenia typically earn between €1,000 and €1,500 gross per month for full-time work, depending on experience, language skills, and seasonality. Hourly rates range from €8 to €15. Many guides work part-time or seasonally, with tips supplementing income. Salaries are regulated by collective agreements in tourism.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for travel guides in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens require a work permit and residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified jobs requiring higher education. Travel guiding typically falls under regulated professions; if you meet educational requirements, you may apply via the Employment Service of Slovenia. Official routes include single permit procedures for temporary residence and work.

**Q: Are there specific requirements to become a travel guide in Slovenia?**
A: Yes, travel guides in Slovenia must pass a national exam and obtain a license from the Slovenian Tourist Board. Fluency in Slovenian and at least one foreign language is required. A secondary education in tourism or related field is beneficial. Guides must renew their license every five years and follow ethical standards.
