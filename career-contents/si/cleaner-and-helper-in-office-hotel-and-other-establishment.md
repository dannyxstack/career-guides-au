## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners in offices and hotels in Slovenia?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for cleaners in Slovenia ranges from the minimum wage (€1,203.36 as of 2024) to around €1,400. Many employers offer additional benefits such as meal vouchers or transport allowance. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as cleaners in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as cleaners via a single work permit (employment permit) obtained by the employer under the Employment, Self-Employment and Work of Aliens Act. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because it targets highly skilled workers. Posted worker status may also apply for temporary assignments.

**Q: What are the main duties of cleaners in offices and hotels in Slovenia?**
A: Duties include sweeping, mopping, vacuuming floors, dusting furniture, cleaning restrooms, emptying trash bins, and restocking supplies. In hotels, tasks also involve making beds, changing linens, and tidying guest rooms. Attention to hygiene and safety standards is expected.
