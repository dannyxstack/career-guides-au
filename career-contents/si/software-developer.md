## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Slovenia?**
A: Average gross monthly salary for mid-level software developers in Slovenia ranges from €3,500 to €5,500. Senior developers can earn over €7,000. Net salary after taxes and social contributions is typically 60-70% of gross. Salaries are higher in Ljubljana and for specialized skills such as AI or cloud computing.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU software developer to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU software developers can obtain an EU Blue Card with a valid job offer, higher education or 5 years professional experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, the Slovenian single permit (combined work and residence) is available; employers may need to pass a labor market test unless the occupation is on the shortage list.

**Q: What career growth opportunities exist for software developers in Slovenia?**
A: Software developers in Slovenia can advance to senior developer, team lead, or architect roles. Many companies support further education and certifications. The growing tech hub in Ljubljana offers opportunities in startups and multinationals. Specializing in AI, cybersecurity, or fintech is encouraged. Networking via meetups and conferences like WebCamp helps career progression.
