## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physicists and astronomers in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, physicists and astronomers earn an average gross monthly salary between €2,800 and €4,500, depending on experience and sector. Senior researchers and those in private R&D may earn higher. Public sector salaries follow collective agreements. This is above the national average, reflecting demand for skilled STEM professionals.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU physicists to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU physicists can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Slovenian salary. Alternatively, a single permit (work and residence) can be applied for by the employer. Both require a university degree and compliance with labor market tests. The Employment Service of Slovenia provides guidance.

**Q: What is the career outlook for physicists in Slovenia?**
A: Physicists in Slovenia find opportunities in research institutes like Jožef Stefan Institute, universities, and high-tech industries (photonics, materials science). The government supports R&D through EU funds. Career progression includes leading projects, teaching, or moving into data science. Networking via Slovenian Physical Society is beneficial.
