## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for companions and valets in Slovenia ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start at the minimum wage (€1,203.36 gross per month in 2024). Experienced staff in private households can earn higher. Tips and overtime may supplement income.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for companions and valets in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as companions or valets via a single work permit (employer-sponsored) or an EU Blue Card if the gross annual salary meets the threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, which was €27,786 in 2023). The employer must first obtain a work permit from the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a companion or valet in Slovenia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers prefer relevant experience, references, and soft skills like discretion, reliability, and communication. Proficiency in Slovenian and additional languages (e.g., English) is beneficial. Training in hospitality, personal assistance, or household management can improve job prospects.
