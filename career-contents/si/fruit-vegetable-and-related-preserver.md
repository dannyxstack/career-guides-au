## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fruit, vegetable and related preserver in Slovenia?**
A: Monthly gross salary typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,300, depending on experience and employer. The national minimum wage for 2024 is €1,203.36 gross, so entry-level positions often start near that figure. Overtime and shift work may increase earnings.

**Q: What work visa options are available for foreign fruit, vegetable and related preservers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a single work and residence permit (employment permit) through the Employment Service of Slovenia. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as this occupation does not meet the required high-skill criteria.

**Q: What training and career progression opportunities exist for this role in Slovenia?**
A: Most employers require a vocational qualification in food processing or related field, though on-the-job training is common. With experience, workers can advance to team leader, quality control inspector, or production supervisor. Further education in food technology can open managerial or specialized roles.
