## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience, but a mid-career teacher in this category typically earns between €1,500 and €2,200 gross per month. Public sector positions follow pay grades, while private institutions may offer higher or lower rates. Additional allowances for specialization or location may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU teaching professionals work in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals must secure a job offer and then apply for a Slovenian work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a tertiary degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage in Slovenia (approx. €34,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard single permit covers work and residence.

**Q: What are the career prospects for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovenia?**
A: Opportunities exist in adult education, special education, or private tutoring. The sector is stable, with demand for multilingual teachers and those with digital skills. Career advancement may involve becoming a head of department, curriculum developer, or moving into educational consultancy. Continuing professional development is encouraged.
