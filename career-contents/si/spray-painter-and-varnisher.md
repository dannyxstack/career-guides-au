## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a spray painter in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly salary for spray painters and varnishers in Slovenia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800 gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced professionals may earn up to €2,200. Overtime and specialization can increase pay.

**Q: Can non-EU spray painters obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a Slovenian work permit if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a job paying at least 1.5 times the average salary, which may not apply to most spray painters. Alternatively, a single work permit combining residence and work authorization is the common route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a spray painter in Slovenia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not always required, but vocational training in painting or a related field is highly valued. Apprenticeships or courses in industrial coating techniques improve job prospects. Certification in specific spray technologies (e.g., HVLP) may be required. Practical experience and a portfolio are often more important than formal education.
