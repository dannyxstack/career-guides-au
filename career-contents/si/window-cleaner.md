## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, the average monthly gross salary for a window cleaner ranges from €1,000 to €1,400, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage (€1,203.80 gross in 2025). Overtime, tips, and seasonal work can increase earnings. Net pay varies after social contributions and income tax.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a window cleaner in Slovenia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and typically not applicable for window cleaners. Non-EU nationals can apply for a single work and residence permit if they have a job offer. The employer must demonstrate that no EU candidate is available. The process involves a labor market test and approval from the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What are the career prospects for window cleaners in Slovenia?**
A: Window cleaning in Slovenia offers limited formal career progression. Experienced cleaners may become team leaders, start their own business, or specialize in high-rise or industrial cleaning. The industry is stable with demand in urban areas like Ljubljana. Additional certifications in safety at height can improve job opportunities.
