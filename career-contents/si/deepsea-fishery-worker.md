## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for deep-sea fishery workers in Slovenia vary based on experience, employer, and type of fishing. Typically, the monthly gross salary ranges from €1,200 to €1,600. Bonuses and overtime can increase earnings, especially during peak seasons. Entry-level workers may start lower, while experienced crew members earn higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as deep-sea fishery workers in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not suitable for deep-sea fishery workers as it requires a higher education degree. Non-EU nationals must instead apply for a single work and residence permit through the Employment Service of Slovenia. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Slovenian or EU worker.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a deep-sea fishery worker in Slovenia?**
A: While formal education is not mandatory, basic seafarer certificates such as STCW (Standards of Training, Certification, and Watchkeeping) are often required. Physical fitness, swimming ability, and experience with fishing gear are important. Some employers provide on-the-job training. Knowledge of Slovenian or English is beneficial for communication.
