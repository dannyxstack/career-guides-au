## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for livestock farm labourers in Slovenia?**
A: The minimum monthly gross wage in Slovenia in 2024 is €1,253. Livestock farm labourers often earn at or slightly above this minimum, depending on the employer, region, and experience. Overtime and bonuses may increase total pay, but actual wages vary widely across farms.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for livestock farm labourers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a standard work permit and employment visa to work as livestock farm labourers. The employer must first obtain a work permit from the Employment Service of Slovenia, proving no suitable EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires higher education qualifications.

**Q: What are typical daily tasks of a livestock farm labourer in Slovenia?**
A: Tasks include feeding and watering animals, cleaning barns and pens, monitoring animal health, assisting with breeding and milking, and maintaining equipment. Labourers also help with herd movement, record-keeping, and ensuring compliance with animal welfare and hygiene standards on Slovenian farms.
