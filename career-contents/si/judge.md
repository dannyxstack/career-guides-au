## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a judge in Slovenia?**
A: Judges in Slovenia are public servants with salaries set by law. The basic monthly gross salary ranges from approximately €2,500 to €4,500, depending on experience and court level. Additional allowances for seniority and specializations may apply, making total compensation competitive within the public sector.

**Q: Can a foreign lawyer work as a judge in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, a foreign lawyer can apply for an EU Blue Card to work as a judge in Slovenia if they have a job offer from a Slovenian court. Requirements include recognition of legal qualifications, proof of Slovenian language proficiency, and a valid employment contract. The process involves applying for a work and residence permit through the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a judge in Slovenia?**
A: To become a judge in Slovenia, one must hold a law degree, pass the state bar exam, and have at least 8 years of legal experience. Candidates undergo a selection process by the Judicial Council and must be Slovenian citizens. Foreign lawyers may face additional language and citizenship barriers.
