## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwifery associate professional in Slovenia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for midwifery associate professionals in Slovenia typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Public sector positions may offer additional allowances. According to the Statistical Office of Slovenia, average gross earnings in health and social work (including midwifery) were around €1,500 in 2023.

**Q: What are the work visa options for midwifery associate professionals from outside the EU seeking employment in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU midwifery associate professionals can apply for a single work and residence permit under the Employment of Foreigners Act, requiring a job offer and positive labor market test. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for this occupation, as it demands higher education qualifications and a salary threshold of at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (€2,250+ monthly in 2024).

**Q: What education and licensing are required to work as a midwifery associate professional in Slovenia?**
A: To practice as a midwifery associate professional, you need a secondary or post-secondary vocational qualification in midwifery (e.g., from a vocational college). Registration with the Nurses and Midwives Association of Slovenia is mandatory. Continuing education is required to maintain licensure. Proficiency in Slovenian (B2 level) is typically needed for patient communication and legal documentation.
