## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for landscape architects in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for landscape architects in Slovenia ranges from €1,800 to €2,800, depending on experience, sector (public or private), and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,500, while senior professionals can earn over €3,500.

**Q: What are the official work visa options for landscape architects moving to Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card or a single work permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree, a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary), and employer sponsorship. Alternatively, a standard work permit may be obtained through employer application to the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What education and registration are required to practice as a landscape architect in Slovenia?**
A: To practice independently, you need a master's degree in landscape architecture or equivalent, and mandatory registration with the Slovenian Chamber of Architecture and Spatial Planning. Graduates from EU countries may have qualifications recognized automatically; non-EU degrees require a recognition procedure by the competent authority.
