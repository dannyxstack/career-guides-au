## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Slovenia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of Slovenia, the average monthly gross salary for professionals in this group (ISCO-08 2633) was approximately €2,100 in 2023. Salaries vary by sector, with academic positions offering lower pay and research roles in public institutions or NGOs providing competitive compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as philosophers, historians, or political scientists in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card. Requirements include a higher education degree (at least three years), a binding job offer in the occupation, and a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the Slovenian average (approx. €32,700 in 2024). A standard work permit is also possible if the Blue Card criteria are not met.

**Q: What are typical employers for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Slovenia?**
A: Common employers include universities and research institutes (e.g., University of Ljubljana, Science and Research Centre Koper), government ministries, think tanks, media organizations, and cultural institutions. Opportunities in the private sector are limited, often in consulting, market research, or policy analysis roles.
