## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Slovenia?**
A: Astrologers, fortune-tellers and related workers in Slovenia typically operate on a self-employed basis. Income varies widely depending on client base and reputation, often charging per consultation. There is no official salary data for this occupation as it is not formally regulated. Earnings can range from modest supplemental income to several hundred euros per session for established practitioners.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as an astrologer in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens wishing to work as astrologers or fortune-tellers in Slovenia must obtain a work permit, which requires a job offer from a Slovenian employer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a tertiary degree and a high-skilled occupation. Alternatively, self-employment through a business visa may be possible if you register a company and meet investment criteria. Always check with the Slovenian Embassy.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an astrologer in Slovenia?**
A: Slovenia does not require formal qualifications for astrologers or fortune-tellers. However, to operate legally, you must register as a self-employed individual or a business. Joining a professional association is optional but may enhance credibility. There are no state-recognized training programs; practitioners typically gain skills through private courses or experience.
