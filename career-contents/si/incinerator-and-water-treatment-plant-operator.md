- Data Recording and Report Generation
- Routine monitoring and alert response based on fixed parameters

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Early warning of equipment failure based on AI prediction models
- Use digital twin simulation to optimize incineration/treatment parameters

- Final decision signing involving legal and environmental responsibilities
- Cross-system coordination and multi-party communication (government, community, contractors)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for incinerator and water treatment plant operators in Slovenia?**
A: The gross monthly salary typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, employer, and location. Public sector jobs may offer slightly higher pay. Overtime and shift allowances can increase earnings. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior operators can earn up to €2,000.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as incinerator or water treatment plant operators in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if you have a tertiary qualification (e.g., relevant degree) or proven skills. Non-EU workers need a job offer with a salary at least 1.2 times the national average gross salary (approx. €22,000 annually in 2024). Apply for the EU Blue Card at the Administrative Unit, which also serves as a work and residence permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an incinerator or water treatment plant operator in Slovenia?**
A: Usually a secondary education in mechanical, electrical, or environmental technology is required. On-the-job training and certification for handling hazardous materials or specific equipment are common. For advanced roles, a vocational college degree (e.g., in water treatment) may be preferred. Licenses from the Slovenian Environment Agency may be necessary.
