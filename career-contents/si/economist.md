## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for economists in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for economists in Slovenia vary by experience and sector, typically ranging from €1,800 to €3,500 gross per month. Senior positions in banking, finance, or public sector can earn more. Entry-level roles start around €1,400 gross.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign economists to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU economists can obtain a Single Permit for employment, requiring a job offer and labor market test. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a degree and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: Which industries and employers in Slovenia hire economists?**
A: Economists in Slovenia are in demand across banking, finance, insurance, public administration, and research institutions. Key employers include Banka Slovenije (central bank), Ministry of Finance, universities, and multinational corporations like Petrol and Krka. Consulting firms and government agencies also hire regularly.
