## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building structure cleaners in Slovenia?**
A: Monthly gross salary for building structure cleaners in Slovenia typically ranges from the minimum wage (approximately €1,200) to €1,500, depending on employer, experience, and specific job requirements. Overtime and night shifts may yield additional pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as building structure cleaners in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree, which generally does not apply to building structure cleaners. Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit and employment visa obtained by their employer after a labor market test.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for building structure cleaners in Slovenia?**
A: With experience, cleaners can advance to team leader or supervisor roles, or start their own cleaning business. Specialized training in areas like hazardous material cleaning or high-rise window cleaning can lead to higher pay and more responsibility.
