## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned officer in the Slovenian Armed Forces?**
A: Salaries are determined by the Public Sector Pay Act and increase with rank and seniority. A junior non-commissioned officer typically earns between €1,500 and €2,000 gross per month, while senior NCOs can earn up to €2,800. Additional allowances for special duties, housing, and family may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as non-commissioned officers in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: No. Military positions in the Slovenian Armed Forces are reserved for Slovenian citizens. The EU Blue Card applies only to highly skilled civilian employment, not military roles. Non-EU nationals cannot obtain a work visa for armed forces service, though rare exceptions exist under bilateral defense agreements.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for non-commissioned officers in Slovenia?**
A: NCOs can progress through ranks from sergeant to sergeant major, with promotions based on performance, experience, and completion of leadership courses. Opportunities include specialized training in areas such as logistics, reconnaissance, or military engineering, and advancement to officer ranks through military academies.
