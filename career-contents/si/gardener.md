## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Slovenia?**
A: Based on available data, the average monthly gross salary for gardeners in Slovenia ranges from €1,000 to €1,400. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced gardeners can earn up to €1,600. Seasonal fluctuations and overtime may affect actual earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as gardeners in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary. Gardener positions typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU gardeners may instead apply for a single work permit under the Employment of Aliens Act, requiring a job offer and a positive labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a gardener in Slovenia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but vocational training in horticulture or landscaping is beneficial. Employers often seek practical experience, plant care knowledge, and equipment handling skills. A driving license may be required. Certification from an agricultural school can improve job prospects.
