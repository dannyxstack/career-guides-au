## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a traditional and complementary medicine associate professional in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions may earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month gross, while experienced professionals can earn up to €2,500. Self-employed practitioners set their own rates. Public sector employment is limited; most work in private clinics or independently.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign traditional and complementary medicine professional to work in Slovenia?**
A: Foreign nationals can obtain a work permit through a Slovenian employer or apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold. Self-employment requires a business visa and registration. Recognition of qualifications from EU/EEA countries is streamlined; non-EU qualifications need verification by the competent authority.

**Q: What is the career outlook for traditional and complementary medicine in Slovenia?**
A: Demand for complementary medicine is growing, with increased public interest in holistic health. Opportunities exist in private practice, wellness centers, and spas. However, regulation is evolving; currently only certain therapies (acupuncture, chiropractic) are officially recognized. Continuing education and certification enhance prospects. Self-employment is common.
