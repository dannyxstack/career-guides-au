## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of retail automation systems
- Inventory data analysis and forecasting tools
- Operation of equipment such as forklifts and electric pallet jacks
- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shelf fillers in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for shelf fillers in Slovenia ranges from approximately €950 to €1,200, depending on employer, location, and experience. The minimum wage is around €1,074 gross per month (as of 2025). Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU citizens to work as shelf fillers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a single work and residence permit. The employer must demonstrate no suitable EU candidate. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers; shelf fillers usually do not qualify. Instead, an employment permit under the national quota system is the common route.

**Q: What are common career progression opportunities for shelf fillers in Slovenia?**
A: With experience, shelf fillers can advance to team leader, stock supervisor, or store manager roles. Additional training in inventory management or logistics can lead to warehouse positions. Some pursue retail management courses. The sector offers stable employment with potential for promotion in larger retail chains.
