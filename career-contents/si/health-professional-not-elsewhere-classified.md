## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary in Slovenia for this group varies by role and experience, ranging from €1,800 to €2,800. Specialists with advanced qualifications or in high demand can earn higher. Entry-level positions start around €1,500 gross.

**Q: Can non-EU health professionals work in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU health professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card. Requirements include a valid work contract, recognized qualifications, and a salary at least 1.5 times the Slovenian average gross annual salary. The employer must first demonstrate that no suitable Slovenian or EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements for health professionals in Slovenia?**
A: Health professionals must have their qualifications recognized by the Ministry of Health or relevant professional chamber. For many roles, registration with the Slovenian Medical Chamber or other regulatory body is mandatory. Language proficiency in Slovene is typically required. The process can take several months.
