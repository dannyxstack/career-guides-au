## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries for garment patternmakers and cutters in Slovenia typically range from €1,000 to €1,600 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced professionals in specialized or managerial roles can earn higher. These figures are based on data from local job portals and industry surveys.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a foreign garment patternmaker or cutter to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Slovenia via a standard work permit, where the employer applies to the Employment Service of Slovenia. For the EU Blue Card, the job must require higher education and meet a salary threshold (approx. €38,090 gross annually in 2023). Garment patternmakers typically need relevant vocational qualifications; the Blue Card is less common for this occupation unless the role involves significant technical or managerial responsibilities.

**Q: How is automation affecting the garment patternmaking and cutting profession in Slovenia?**
A: Automation and CAD/CAM systems are increasingly used in Slovenian garment manufacturing, streamlining pattern grading, marking, and cutting. However, skilled patternmakers remain essential for creative design, complex garment construction, and quality control. The profession is evolving to require digital skills, but manual expertise is still valued, especially in custom and high-end tailoring.
