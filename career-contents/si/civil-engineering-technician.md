- Generates standard construction drawings and 2D/3D models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Structural analysis and simulation testing using AI

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a civil engineering technician in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a civil engineering technician in Slovenia ranges from €1,500 to €2,000, according to the Statistical Office. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while experienced technicians can earn up to €2,500. Overtime and project bonuses may increase total compensation.

**Q: How can a non-EU civil engineering technician obtain a work visa for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU technicians can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card or a single work permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree or five years of professional experience, a job offer, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approximately €24,000). Alternatively, employers can apply for a single permit for employment, which combines work and residence.

**Q: What are the main tasks of a civil engineering technician in Slovenia?**
A: Civil engineering technicians in Slovenia assist engineers in planning, designing, and overseeing construction projects. Tasks include preparing drawings and cost estimates, conducting site surveys, testing materials, and ensuring compliance with Slovenian building codes and EU standards. They often work on infrastructure, residential, and commercial projects.
