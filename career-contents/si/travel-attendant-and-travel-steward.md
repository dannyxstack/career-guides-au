## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for travel attendants in Slovenia?**
A: Travel attendants in Slovenia typically earn between €1,200 and €1,800 gross per month, depending on the airline, experience, and flight hours. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior attendants can earn up to €2,200. These figures include basic pay and allowances for overnight stays and international flights.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU citizens to become travel attendants in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit. The employer must obtain a labor market test showing no suitable EU candidate. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as travel attendant roles are not classified as highly skilled. Alternatively, the Slovenian Single Permit combines work and residence for up to two years, renewable.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a travel attendant in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a secondary education diploma is required, along with fluency in English and Slovene. Airlines also require a valid medical certificate and a clean criminal record. Customer service experience is valued. Training is provided by the airline, including safety and emergency procedures. Some airlines prefer candidates with a certificate from a vocational school for transport services.
