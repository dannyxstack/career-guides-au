## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Slovenia?**
A: The minimum gross monthly salary in Slovenia is around €1,203 in 2024. Many elementary workers earn between minimum wage and €1,500 gross, with variations by sector. Some roles, like seasonal agricultural work, may include additional benefits. Collective agreements can influence actual pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as elementary workers in Slovenia via a work permit or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as elementary workers through a standard work permit under the Slovenian Employment and Work of Aliens Act. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled professionals and typically not applicable for elementary roles. Employers must pass a labor market test to sponsor the permit.

**Q: What sectors commonly employ elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Slovenia?**
A: These workers are found in manufacturing, construction, agriculture, hospitality, cleaning, and logistics. Tasks include manual labor, packing, sorting, or assisting skilled workers. Most training is provided on the job, and employment may be seasonal or temporary in some sectors.
