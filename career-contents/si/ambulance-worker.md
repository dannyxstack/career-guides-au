## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ambulance workers in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for ambulance workers in Slovenia ranges from €1,500 to €2,000, depending on experience and location. Additional shift allowances and overtime pay are common. Salaries are typically set by collective agreements in the healthcare sector.

**Q: Can non-EU ambulance workers get a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU ambulance workers can apply for a Slovenian work permit if they have a job offer and meet qualifications. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree, but ambulance roles may not meet Blue Card salary thresholds unless specialized. The standard single work permit is the common route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an ambulance worker in Slovenia?**
A: Ambulance workers in Slovenia must complete secondary education in nursing or emergency medical care, followed by state registration. Additional certifications in advanced life support are beneficial. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Slovenian Ministry of Health, often requiring language proficiency in Slovene.
