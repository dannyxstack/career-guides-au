## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Slovenia?**
A: Monthly gross salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Slovenia generally ranges from the minimum wage (approximately €1,200) to €1,500, depending on experience and region. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may increase earnings, but net pay is lower after social security and tax deductions.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a single work permit (employer-driven) or a seasonal work permit for up to nine months per year. The EU Blue Card is typically not available for this occupation as it requires higher qualifications. The employer must file the application with the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What are typical tasks and working conditions for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Slovenia?**
A: Labourers perform planting, harvesting, feeding animals, cleaning stables, and operating machinery. The work is physically demanding, often outdoors, with peak seasons in spring and autumn. Employment is often seasonal, with full-time contracts during busy periods. Knowledge of organic farming is an advantage.
