## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmers in Slovenia?**
A: Income varies widely as produce is mainly for personal consumption. Average net monthly income is around €500–€800, with many farmers relying on EU subsidies and off-farm work to supplement. Official statistics are limited due to the informal nature of subsistence farming.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as subsistence farmers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a single work and residence permit if they have a job offer from a Slovenian employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and does not apply to subsistence farming. Self-employment via a business visa is possible if meeting registration and capital requirements.

**Q: What are the main challenges for subsistence farmers in Slovenia?**
A: Challenges include small land plots, aging population, climate change impacts like droughts, and administrative burdens for EU subsidies. Many farmers struggle to sustain livelihoods without additional income from tourism or off-farm employment.
