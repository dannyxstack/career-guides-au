## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, the gross monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers typically ranges from €1,300 to €1,800, depending on experience, employer, and region. Salaries may be higher for specialized services or supervisory roles.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for fumigator jobs in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as fumigators in Slovenia by obtaining a work permit and residence permit sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary above €43,764 gross annually (2024), which typically does not apply to this occupation. A standard employment permit is the relevant route.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a fumigator in Slovenia?**
A: To work as a fumigator in Slovenia, you need a vocational certificate in pest control or related field, typically obtained through an apprenticeship or professional training program. Additionally, you must pass an exam administered by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Food, and obtain a license for handling pesticides. Continuing education is mandatory.
