**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.
- Routine structural measurement: ROV with sensors automatically collects data
- Underwater environment monitoring: AI analyzes parameters such as water temperature and turbidity
- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk
- Augmented reality headset: overlays structural diagrams and repair instructions to improve work accuracy
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes historical data to optimize maintenance schedules.
- Remote specialist collaboration: AI translation and 3D reconstruction help land-based experts guide

**Human moat:**

- Manual operation and quick judgment in emergency situations
- Fine tactile sensation and force feedback (e.g., valve operation)
- Adaptability in harsh environments (current, visibility)
- Legal and safety responsibility attribution (divers must sign on-site)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an underwater diver in Slovenia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for underwater divers in Slovenia typically ranges from €1,500 to €2,500 for entry-level positions, with experienced divers earning up to €3,500 or more. Pay varies by sector: offshore construction, tourism (e.g., lake maintenance), or scientific research. Overtime and specialized certifications (e.g., saturation diving) can increase earnings significantly.

**Q: What work visa routes exist for non-EU underwater divers to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU divers can work in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card if they meet the high-skill criteria (university degree or equivalent experience and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, which in 2023 was around €36,000). Alternatively, a standard single work permit (employed or self-employed) is available, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What are the main industries employing underwater divers in Slovenia?**
A: Underwater divers in Slovenia are employed mainly in freshwater construction (bridges, dams), environmental monitoring (lake and riverbed surveys), aquaculture, and tourism (diving centers at Lake Bled or the Adriatic coast). Rescue and police units also hire divers. Certification through international bodies like ADAS or CMAS is common, and local Slovenian recognition may be required.
