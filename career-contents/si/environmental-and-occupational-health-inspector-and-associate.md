## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovenia is approximately €2,000 to €2,500. Most positions are in the public sector, where pay is determined by fixed pay grades based on experience and qualifications. Private sector roles may vary.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovenia through the EU Blue Card or a single work permit. The profession is regulated, requiring recognition of qualifications by the Ministry of Health. Employers must advertise the job and apply for a work permit. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (currently €1,484.79).

**Q: What are the main duties of an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Slovenia?**
A: In Slovenia, Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors inspect workplaces, public facilities, and environmental sites for compliance with health and safety regulations. They work under the Inspectorate for Health and Safety, issuing warnings, fines, and closure orders. They also investigate occupational diseases and environmental hazards. A degree in public health or a related field is required.
