## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary widely. Most collectors are paid per unit (e.g., per cubic meter of firewood or per liter of water). Earnings can range from minimum wage (approx. €1,203 gross monthly in 2024) to slightly above, depending on volume. Many work informally, so actual income may be lower or irregular.

**Q: Can I get an EU Blue Card to work as a water and firewood collector in Slovenia?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers. Water and firewood collecting is classified as low-skilled work (ISCO-08 9624). To work in Slovenia, non-EU nationals typically need a work permit through a Slovenian employer, often for seasonal or temporary work. No sole Blue Card route exists for this occupation.

**Q: What are the working conditions for water and firewood collectors in Slovenia?**
A: Work is physically demanding, often outdoors in forests or rural areas. Hours are irregular, with peak seasons in autumn and winter for firewood collection. Water collection may be year-round but depends on region. Safety risks include cuts, falls, and weather exposure. Protective gear is recommended but not always provided.
