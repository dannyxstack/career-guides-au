## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for creative and performing artists in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by discipline and experience. As of 2024, monthly gross earnings for artists range from about €1,200 for entry-level to over €2,500 for established professionals. Many artists supplement income through multiple projects, freelance work, or public funding. Official data from the Statistical Office of Slovenia provides sector-specific averages.

**Q: How can non-EU creative and performing artists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU artists can apply for a national work permit (single permit) or an EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (€1,397 gross/month in 2024). The process requires a job offer from a Slovenian employer or engagement by a cultural institution. Self-employment as a sole trader also possible. Applications are submitted via the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What are the main employment opportunities for creative and performing artists in Slovenia?**
A: Employment is diverse, including work in theatres, orchestras, film production, advertising, and event management. Many artists work freelance or on project contracts. Public funding through the Ministry of Culture supports residencies and grants. Permanent full-time positions are limited, so networking and a strong portfolio are essential for career development.
