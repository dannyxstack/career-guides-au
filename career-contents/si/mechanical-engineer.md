## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CAD drafting and 2D drawing generation
- Standard finite element analysis (FEA) and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations
- Generate Standard BOMs and Technical Documentation
- Routine tolerance analysis and dimensional inspection
- Predictive maintenance planning based on historical data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-driven generative design (e.g., topology optimization)
- Machine learning-based failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)
- Digital twin real-time simulation and optimization
- Smart materials selection and supply chain optimization
- Write automated test and verification scripts

**Human moat:**

- Cross-system physical intuition and innovative design
- Complex nonlinear problem diagnosis and on-site debugging
- Ethical and responsible decision-making for safety-critical systems
- Unstructured communication with clients and suppliers
- Multidisciplinary integration and system architecture decision-making

**Skills to build:**

- AI-enhanced design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Python/R for data analysis and automation scripts
- Digital Twin and IoT Integration Knowledge
- Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing) Design Optimization
- Machine Learning Basics and Fault Prediction
- System modeling and simulation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mechanical engineer in Slovenia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office, the average gross monthly salary for mechanical engineers in Slovenia ranges from €2,200 to €2,800, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while senior engineers can earn over €3,500.

**Q: How can a non-EU mechanical engineer work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU mechanical engineers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (1.5 times the national average gross salary). The employer must obtain a work permit, and the application is submitted to the Employment Service of Slovenia.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for mechanical engineers in Slovenia?**
A: Mechanical engineers in Slovenia are in high demand in automotive, manufacturing, energy, and machinery sectors. Opportunities include roles in design, R&D, production management, and quality assurance. Companies like Revoz, Gorenje, and various metal-processing firms are major employers, and experienced engineers can advance to leadership or specialist positions.
