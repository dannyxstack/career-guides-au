## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for medical imaging technicians in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for medical imaging technicians in Slovenia ranges from €1,800 to €2,200, depending on experience and the employer. Public hospitals typically offer slightly lower base pay but include benefits such as additional allowances and job security. Private clinics may offer higher salaries.

**Q: How can foreign medical imaging technicians work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU medical imaging technicians can work in Slovenia through the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree (at least three years) and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the Slovenian average (around €2,700 per month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit may be obtained if the employer sponsors the application.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for medical imaging technicians in Slovenia?**
A: Medical imaging technicians in Slovenia can advance to senior technician roles, specialize in modalities like MRI, CT, or mammography, or move into management positions within radiology departments. Continuous professional development is required, and additional certifications can lead to higher pay and responsibilities.
