## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Slovenia?**
A: The salary for beauticians in Slovenia varies by experience and location. Entry-level beauticians earn around €1,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €1,500 gross. These figures are estimates based on industry data and may not reflect exact market conditions.

**Q: What are the work visa options for beauticians to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit, typically via the Single Permit procedure (work and residence). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree, so beauticians usually do not qualify. EU/EEA nationals can work freely without a permit.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a beautician in Slovenia?**
A: To work as a beautician in Slovenia, you typically need a vocational qualification in beauty therapy or cosmetology, often obtained through a secondary school program or a professional course. Registration with the relevant chamber or obtaining a license may be required, especially for independent practitioners.
