## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for personal services workers in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for personal services workers in Slovenia is around €1,200 to €1,500, depending on the specific role and employer. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (€1,203.80 as of 2024), while experienced workers in specialized services can earn higher. Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU personal services workers to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU workers can obtain a work permit through employer sponsorship, typically via a Slovenian employment permit. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting salary thresholds (at least 1.5 times average gross salary, around €2,250 monthly). Both routes require a valid employment contract and meet labor market test conditions.

**Q: What tasks do personal services workers not elsewhere classified perform in Slovenia?**
A: These workers provide various personal services such as pet grooming, personal shopping, event assistance, or other support not covered by more specific categories. Duties depend on the employer, but may include customer service, handling payments, maintaining cleanliness, and ensuring client satisfaction. The role often requires flexibility and interpersonal skills.
