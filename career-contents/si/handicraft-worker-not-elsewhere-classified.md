## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a handicraft worker in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for handicraft workers in Slovenia is approximately €1,200–€1,500, depending on experience and specialization. Entry-level workers may earn around €1,000, while highly skilled artisans can reach €2,000. Salaries vary by region and employer.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU handicraft worker to obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU handicraft workers can apply for a Slovenian work permit via the Employment Service of Slovenia if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree, which may not apply to most handicraft roles. A single permit combining work and residence is also available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a handicraft worker in Slovenia?**
A: Formal qualifications vary by craft. Many positions require a vocational certificate (e.g., in woodworking, metalworking, or textiles) obtained through secondary vocational schools or apprenticeship programs. Practical experience and a portfolio are often valued. Some crafts may require a master craftsman certificate (mojstrski izpit) for self-employment.
