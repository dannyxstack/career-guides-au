## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sports coaches and instructors in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary by sport, level, and employer. In 2023, sports coaches in Slovenia typically earned between €1,500 and €2,500 gross per month. Instructors in private fitness centers may earn less, while professional team coaches can earn more. Rates for part-time or freelance work depend on experience and location.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for sports coaches to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU sports coaches can work in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles) or a standard single work permit. You need a job offer from a Slovenian employer, who must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Salary thresholds apply: for the Blue Card, gross annual salary must be at least 1.5 times the national average.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a sports coach in Slovenia?**
A: Most coaching roles require at least a relevant sports degree or recognized certification from a national sports body (e.g., the Olympic Committee). Many employers prefer candidates with experience and training in first aid, child protection, and coaching pedagogy. Specific sports may have additional licensing requirements set by their respective federations.
