## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a dental assistant or therapist in Slovenia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for dental assistants in Slovenia ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and location. Dental therapists may earn slightly higher, around €1,500 to €2,000. Salaries are typically higher in private clinics and in larger cities like Ljubljana.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for dental assistants and therapists moving to Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and a residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently around €1,200 gross monthly). Alternatively, employers can apply for a single permit for employment. Recognition of qualifications by the Slovenian Ministry of Health may be required.

**Q: What are the typical duties and career prospects for dental assistants and therapists in Slovenia?**
A: Dental assistants support dentists during procedures, sterilize instruments, and manage patient records. Dental therapists perform basic treatments like fillings and extractions under supervision. Career progression includes specialization in orthodontics or oral surgery, or advancing to practice management. Continuous education is valued for skill development.
