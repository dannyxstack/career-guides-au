## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for child care workers in Slovenia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for child care workers in Slovenia ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Public sector or registered childcare centers may offer higher wages. Note that salaries are often negotiated based on qualifications.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as child care workers in Slovenia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly qualified workers and typically requires a higher education degree and a high salary threshold. Child care worker positions generally do not meet Blue Card criteria. Instead, non-EU nationals can apply for a single work and residence permit (enotno dovoljenje) through employer sponsorship, subject to a labor market test.

**Q: What are the career prospects for child care workers in Slovenia?**
A: Career progression includes advancing to senior child care worker, coordinator, or management roles in childcare centers. Further education can lead to becoming a preschool teacher or special education assistant. Demand is steady due to family-friendly policies and increasing need for early childhood care. Opportunities exist in public and private facilities.
