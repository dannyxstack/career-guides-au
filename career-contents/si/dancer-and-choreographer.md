## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for dancers and choreographers in Slovenia?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on employment type and experience. Dancers in permanent ensembles earn around €1,200-€1,800 gross monthly. Choreographers may earn €1,500-€2,500 per project or contract. Freelancers often have variable income. Slovenia's minimum wage is about €1,074 gross monthly as of 2024.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign dancers and choreographers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a single work permit (Enotno dovoljenje) under the Employment and Work of Aliens Act, or if highly qualified, the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds. Dancers and choreographers may also qualify for a 'seasonal' or 'cultural' worker permit for short-term engagements.

**Q: What are the main employment opportunities for dancers and choreographers in Slovenia?**
A: Employment is primarily in state-funded ballet companies (like SNG Opera in Ljubljana and Maribor), contemporary dance troupes, and private studios. Freelance choreographers often work on project-basis for festivals, theatre productions, or commercial events. Teaching dance in schools or private academies is also common.
