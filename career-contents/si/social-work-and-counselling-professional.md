## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Slovenia?**
A: According to official data, the average gross monthly salary for social work professionals in Slovenia ranges between €1,500 and €2,200, depending on experience and sector. Public sector roles often follow fixed pay scales. Net salary after tax and social contributions is typically 60-70% of gross.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU social workers to work in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU professionals require a work permit, which involves a labour market test by the employer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. Long-term EU residence can be obtained after five years.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a social worker in Slovenia?**
A: A recognized degree in social work (minimum bachelor's level) and registration with the Social Chamber of Slovenia are mandatory. Continuing education and proficiency in Slovene are typically required for client-facing roles. Specializations may demand additional training or certifications.
