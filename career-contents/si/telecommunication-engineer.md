## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Telecommunications Engineers in Slovenia?**
A: Telecommunications engineers in Slovenia earn an average gross monthly salary of approximately €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience and company. Senior roles can reach €4,500+. Net salary is about 60–70% of gross after taxes and social contributions. Salaries tend to be higher in Ljubljana and Maribor.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign Telecommunications Engineers in Slovenia?**
A: Non-EU engineers can work in Slovenia via an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, higher education, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €52,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a single work and residence permit is available for qualified workers, subject to a labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Telecommunications Engineer in Slovenia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in telecommunications, electrical engineering, or a related field is required. Professional experience and certifications (e.g., Cisco CCNA) are valued. Registration with the Slovenian Chamber of Engineers (IZS) may be necessary for certain roles. Knowledge of English and Slovenian is beneficial.
