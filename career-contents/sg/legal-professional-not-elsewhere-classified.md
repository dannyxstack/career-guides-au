# Legal Professional (Not Elsewhere Classified) — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2619 (ISCO08)**

Legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Singapore cover a broad range of roles including corporate lawyers, legal advisors, and compliance specialists. Singapore is a leading legal hub in Asia, with a strong demand for expertise in corporate law, finance, and international arbitration. The sector is supported by a robust regulatory framework and a growing legal tech ecosystem. Legal professionals work in law firms, in-house legal departments, government agencies, and international organisations, handling complex cross-border transactions and disputes.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically follows a path from junior associate to senior associate, partner, or in-house counsel to general counsel. Specialisation in high-growth fields such as fintech, M&A, or international arbitration can accelerate advancement. Networking and continuous learning are crucial, with many professionals pursuing advanced degrees like LLM or attending professional development courses. Lateral moves between law firms and in-house roles are common, and some eventually transition into regulatory or judicial positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a legal professional in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, firm size, and specialisation. Entry-level associates in top firms earn competitive packages with bonuses, while partners and senior in-house counsel command significantly higher compensation.

**Q: Can I obtain Permanent Residence as a legal professional on an Employment Pass?**
A: Yes, legal professionals on an Employment Pass can apply for PR under the Professionals/Technical Personnel & Skilled Workers scheme. Approval is based on factors like salary, duration of stay, and economic contribution, but there is no guaranteed points-based route.

**Q: What are the key skills for career progression in Singapore's legal sector?**
A: Strong technical knowledge, business acumen, and expertise in cross-border matters are essential. Networking, specialisation in growth areas like legal tech or arbitration, and language skills (e.g., Mandarin) can also enhance prospects.
