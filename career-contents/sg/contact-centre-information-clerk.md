# Contact Centre Information Clerk — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 4222 (ISCO08)**

Contact Centre Information Clerks in Singapore handle inbound and outbound customer inquiries, process orders, resolve complaints, and provide information via phone, email, or chat. They are employed across sectors such as banking, telecommunications, retail, and logistics. The role requires strong communication skills, basic computer proficiency, and the ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment. With Singapore's focus on service excellence, these clerks are vital to maintaining customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from junior clerk to team leader, quality analyst, or operations manager. Upskilling in CRM software, data analytics, or digital marketing can lead to roles in customer experience management or sales. Many Singaporean workers pursue WSQ certifications or diplomas in customer service to advance. The trend toward omnichannel support creates opportunities for those adept at integrating phone, chat, and social media interactions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Answer standard FAQ questions (e.g. account balance, business hours)
- Handling simple order changes, returns, and exchange requests
- Transfer complex complaints to human agents
- Record and update customer information in the CRM system
- Send standard confirmation emails or SMS notifications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time translation, supporting multilingual customer service
- Emotion recognition alerts to help agents adjust communication strategies
- Smart knowledge base recommendations for quickly locating solutions to complex issues
- Automatic call summary generation reduces post-call documentation time
- Predict customer intent and prepare response scripts in advance

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly emotional, sensitive, or sudden complaints
- Coordinate across departments to solve complex non-standard issues.
- Build customer trust and maintain long-term relationships
- Respond to unforeseen emergencies (e.g., system failures)
- Deliver personalized, empathetic service experiences

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in major AI customer service platforms (e.g., Zendesk AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Learn basic data analysis (SQL/Excel) to analyze customer trends.
- Enhance conflict management and emotional communication skills
- Master the principles of Conversational Design
- Understand automation process building tools (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere)
- Develop multilingual skills (especially English) to serve international clients

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Transformation
- Omnichannel Customer Service
- AI Integration
- Customer Experience

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical starting salary for a Contact Centre Information Clerk in Singapore?**
A: Starting monthly salaries range from SGD 2,200 to SGD 2,800 for local diploma holders, with experienced workers earning up to SGD 3,500. S Pass holders typically earn at least SGD 3,150 per month.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Contact Centre Information Clerk in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but typically on an S Pass or Work Permit. Employers must satisfy quota and levy requirements. The role is not considered a skilled migration route, so PR eligibility is limited.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for a Contact Centre Information Clerk?**
A: Progression can lead to Team Leader, Quality Assurance Analyst, or Customer Service Manager. Upskilling in data analytics or digital channels improves prospects.
