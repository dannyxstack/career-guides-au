# Dairy Products Maker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7513 (ISCO08)**

Dairy products makers in Singapore operate machinery and perform manual tasks to process raw milk into products such as fresh milk, cheese, yogurt, and ice cream. They work in food manufacturing facilities, often for multinational dairy companies or local artisanal producers. The role involves pasteurizing, homogenizing, packaging, and maintaining hygiene standards. Due to limited local dairy farming, most milk is imported, but the industry supports Singapore's food security goals. Workers must adhere to strict food safety regulations (e.g., Singapore Food Agency) and may require certifications like WSQ Food Safety. The occupation is small but steady, with opportunities in quality control and production supervision.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression for dairy products makers starts with production operator roles, moving to senior operator, team leader, or quality control assistant. With further education (e.g., Diploma in Food Technology from ITE or polytechnic), one can become a food technologist or production supervisor. Some transition into sales or technical support for dairy equipment suppliers. Cross-skilling in automation, process improvement, or food science is valued. The pathway is linear but offers stability, with training often provided on the job.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a dairy products maker in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level dairy products makers (Work Permit) earn about SGD 1,800 to SGD 2,500 per month. With experience and certifications (e.g., S Pass holders), salaries can reach SGD 3,000 to SGD 4,500. Supervisory roles may earn SGD 4,500–6,000.

**Q: What type of work pass do I need to work as a dairy products maker in Singapore?**
A: Most foreign workers in this role hold a Work Permit for semi-skilled employees. If you have a diploma and higher qualifications, you may qualify for an S Pass (minimum salary ~SGD 3,300/month). Employment Pass is rare for this occupation.

**Q: What training is mandatory for dairy products makers in Singapore?**
A: All food handlers must complete the WSQ Food Safety Course Level 2. Additionally, employers often require on-the-job training in HACCP, equipment operation, and cleaning procedures. A Nitec in Food Technology is an advantage.
