# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons in Singapore create detailed technical drawings and plans based on specifications provided by engineers and architects, using computer-aided design (CAD) software. They are employed across engineering, construction, and manufacturing firms, supporting projects in building services, infrastructure, and product design. The role requires precision, spatial awareness, and familiarity with industry standards. With Singapore's ongoing investment in construction and smart infrastructure, draughtspersons remain essential for translating design concepts into actionable blueprints. Local polytechnics and ITE offer relevant diplomas and Nitec courses, and the sector values experience in Building Information Modelling (BIM) and sustainable design practices.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for draughtspersons in Singapore typically starts from junior drafter, advancing to senior drafter or project coordinator. With upskilling in BIM and project management, pathways lead to roles like BIM coordinator, design manager, or even architectural associate. Many pursue further studies in engineering or architecture to move into professional roles. Specializations in M&E (mechanical & electrical), structural, or civil drafting offer clearer career ladders. Some draughtspersons transition into site supervision or quantity surveying. Technical certifications from Autodesk or BCA enhance mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Building Information Modelling
- Green Building Design
- Infrastructure Projects
- Digital Twin Technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for draughtspersons in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level draughtspersons earn around SGD 30,000–36,000 annually, while experienced senior drafters can earn SGD 45,000–55,000. Those with BIM expertise may command higher pay.

**Q: Can foreign draughtspersons work in Singapore?**
A: Yes, mostly under the S Pass for mid-skilled roles. Employers must meet quota and pay at least SGD 3,150 monthly. Higher-skilled roles may qualify for an Employment Pass.
