# Secretary (General) — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 4120 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, secretaries (general) provide essential administrative and clerical support to executives, managers, and teams. They handle correspondence, schedule meetings, manage files, and coordinate office operations. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, strong organisational skills, and discretion are key. The role is common across all sectors, including finance, legal, and corporate services. While automation and digital tools are transforming some tasks, demand for skilled secretaries remains steady due to the need for human oversight and interpersonal coordination.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for secretaries in Singapore typically leads to roles such as senior secretary, executive assistant, office manager, or administrative manager. Some transition into specialised areas like human resources or corporate governance. Professional development through certifications (e.g., Certified Administrative Professional) and training in digital tools can accelerate advancement. The path suits those who enjoy combining support with increasing responsibility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated meeting schedule coordination and conflict detection
- Call screening and standard response generation
- Document Formatting, Spell Checking, and Template Application
- Preliminary review of travel bookings and expense reimbursements
- Simple email classification and auto-reply

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing executive time allocation through AI schedule analysis
- Improving meeting minutes efficiency using voice transcription and summarization tools
- Generate administrative reports and insights using data analysis tools
- Using AI customer management system to enhance external contact management
- Automating workflow design to improve overall team efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Judgment and confidentiality skills when handling highly sensitive or confidential information
- Interpersonal coordination and decision-making in ambiguous or urgent situations
- Emotional intelligence and relationship management in cross-departmental communication
- Understanding and navigating informal power structures within an organization.
- Administrative tasks requiring creativity or personalized handling

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced office suite automation and scripting (e.g., VBA, Power Automate).
- Hands-on AI tools: meeting scheduling AI, speech-to-text, CRM system
- Data analysis basics (advanced Excel functions, BI tools)
- Project management basics (e.g., Agile methodology)
- Advanced communication and emotional intelligence (conflict resolution, executive support)
- Digital security and privacy protection awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Administration
- Executive Support
- Office Technology
- Virtual Assistance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a secretary in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level secretaries typically earn around S$2,500–S$3,500 per month. With experience, salaries can reach S$4,000–S$5,500, and senior roles in MNCs may pay higher. Salary varies by sector and qualifications.

**Q: Can a secretary obtain an Employment Pass in Singapore?**
A: Yes, if they hold a recognised degree and have at least 5 years of relevant experience. They must meet the COMPASS points threshold. Otherwise, the S Pass is more common for diploma-holding secretaries.

**Q: What career progression is available for secretaries in Singapore?**
A: Many progress to roles like executive assistant, office manager, or administrative manager. Some specialise in areas such as human resources, corporate governance, or project coordination, often through additional certifications.
