# Subsistence Livestock Farmer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 6320 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, subsistence livestock farming is minimal due to intense urbanisation and reliance on food imports. The role involves raising animals for personal consumption or small-scale local trade, primarily in designated agricultural areas like Lim Chu Kang. Most farmers are older locals or foreign workers on Work Permits. The occupation aligns with Singapore's '30 by 30' food security goal, but mechanisation and high costs limit viability.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression paths are virtually absent. Subsistence farmers typically operate small family-run plots or work as farmhands. Skills transferability is low; few move to agritech roles without retraining. Younger Singaporeans show little interest, and government schemes (e.g., Agriculture Productivity Fund) favour high-tech farming, not subsistence models. Most workers are older or foreign, with no structured career ladder.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated weighing and recording of livestock weight data
- Sensor-based automatic feed dispensing control
- Remote monitoring and alarm for equipment fault diagnosis
- Automated market data collection and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted farm planning (rotation route optimization)
- Smart wearable devices monitor livestock health and warn of diseases
- Grazing schedule recommendations based on meteorological data
- Automatically generate farming logs and breeding records

**Human moat:**

- Direct animal observation and tactile assessment (e.g., health status, signs of estrus)
- Experience-based decision-making in response to sudden environmental changes (e.g., moving livestock before a storm)
- Traditional herding skills (using terrain and vegetation)
- Informal knowledge exchange with the community (local resource acquisition)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and digital tool application (recording apps, spreadsheets)
- Smart farming equipment operation (automatic feeding, monitoring systems)
- Continuously learn animal science knowledge (disease control, genetic improvement)
- Online information retrieval skills (online markets, policy subsidy information)
- Knowledge of Ecological Conservation and Sustainable Practices
- Basic financial management (costing, revenue optimization)

**Key growth areas:**

- urban farming
- agritech
- food security
- local produce

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a subsistence livestock farmer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries are low, typically around SGD 1,800–2,500 per month for foreign workers on Work Permits. Self-employed subsistence farmers have highly variable income, often below SGD 30,000 annually.

**Q: Can a subsistence livestock farmer get an Employment Pass or PR?**
A: No. This occupation does not meet the COMPASS criteria for Employment Pass (EP) or S Pass. Only Work Permit is possible for foreign workers, with no direct path to permanent residence.

**Q: Is subsistence livestock farming a growing field in Singapore?**
A: No. The sector is shrinking due to land reallocation and focus on high-tech farming. Few new entrants; most existing farmers are ageing.
