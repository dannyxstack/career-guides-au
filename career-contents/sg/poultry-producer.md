# Poultry Producer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 6122 (ISCO08)**

Poultry producers in Singapore manage the breeding, raising, and harvesting of chickens, ducks, and other fowl for meat and egg production. Despite limited land, local farms supply a small fraction of domestic consumption, with government support for high-productivity, biosecure farming methods. Producers oversee feeding, health, and welfare of flocks, comply with strict animal health and food safety regulations, and increasingly adopt technology like automated feeding and climate control. The role is physically demanding and typically involves working on farms in rural areas like Lim Chu Kang or Sungei Tengah.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression in poultry production is limited. Starting as a general farm worker, one can advance to supervisor or farm manager with experience and training. Some producers move into agribusiness roles like hatchery management or feed supply. The government's Agriculture Productivity Fund supports modernisation, so skills in automation and biosecurity are valued. However, the small scale means few senior roles exist, and many workers remain at the same level. Local workers often shift to other sectors; foreign workers may renew permits but rarely achieve permanent residency.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Environmental parameter monitoring and recording (temperature, humidity, light)
- Basic feed delivery and water supply management
- Mechanized operation of poultry house cleaning and waste disposal
- Preliminary statistics and report generation for production data
- Collection of vital signs data in routine health checks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize feed ratios and feeding times through AI analysis
- Early detection of abnormal behavior in sick poultry using computer vision
- Predicting egg production/slaughter volume based on production data to plan sales
- Efficiency improved in inspecting large poultry houses with drones or ground robots
- AI-assisted epidemic early warning and biosecurity decision support

**Human moat:**

- Acute observation and immediate handling of abnormal behavior in individual poultry.
- veterinary-level disease diagnosis and medication decisions
- Emergency management for sudden weather or equipment failure
- Animal welfare operations in special production systems like organic and free-range
- Regulatory compliance responsibilities related to government quarantine and food certification

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and basic troubleshooting of agricultural IoT devices
- Data analysis and visualization tools (e.g., Excel, Tableau)
- Understanding the application of computer vision systems in farming
- Deepened knowledge of animal behavior and biosecurity
- IT fundamentals and automated systems management skills
- Food safety regulations and sustainable farming practices

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban farming
- Automation
- Biosecurity
- Egg production

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a poultry producer in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level poultry farm workers earn around SGD 1,200–1,800 per month. Experienced supervisors can earn up to SGD 2,500–3,000. Salaries are relatively low compared to other sectors.

**Q: Can a poultry producer obtain an Employment Pass or S Pass?**
A: No. This occupation is classified as semi-skilled, so only Work Permit (WP) is available. S Pass is generally not issued for agricultural workers. WP requires employer quota and levy.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for a poultry producer in Singapore?**
A: Progression is limited but possible: farm worker → senior worker → assistant supervisor → farm manager. Some move to hatcheries or feed mills. Advanced roles require managerial experience and possibly a diploma.
