# Pet Groomer and Animal Care Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

Pet groomers and animal care workers provide grooming, boarding, and basic health care for pets, such as dogs and cats. In Singapore, the profession is driven by rising pet ownership and the humanization of pets, leading to demand for quality grooming and care services. Workers may be employed in pet shops, veterinary clinics, boarding facilities, or as mobile groomers. The role requires patience, animal handling skills, and attention to detail. Most workers are Singaporeans or hold work permits, as the role is not typically filled via skilled migration routes.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression in Singapore typically moves from junior groomer to senior or master groomer, with experience and certification. Some specialize in creative grooming, pet nutrition, or animal behavior. Others open their own pet care businesses. The sector offers good prospects for entrepreneurial individuals, though salaries remain modest compared to other services. Formal education is not mandatory but is increasingly valued.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for pet groomers in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, employer type (e.g., pet shop vs. luxury salon), and skill level. Entry-level groomers may earn lower wages, while experienced or master groomers can command higher pay. Many groomers supplement income with tips or commissions.

**Q: Can foreigners work as pet groomers in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but mainly through employer-sponsored Work Permits (for semi-skilled) or S Pass (for more experienced workers). These are tied to a specific employer and subject to quotas and levies. The role is not a typical skilled migration route.

**Q: Do I need formal certification to be a pet groomer?**
A: Not legally mandatory, but employers strongly prefer certified groomers. Certification from recognized programs (e.g., NAPPG or WSQ) can improve job prospects and enable career progression to senior or specialist roles.
