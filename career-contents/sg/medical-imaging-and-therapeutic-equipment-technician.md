# Medical Imaging And Therapeutic Equipment Technician — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3211 (ISCO08)**

Medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians, including radiographers, nuclear medicine technologists, and radiation therapists, operate diagnostic imaging equipment such as X-ray, MRI, CT, and PET scanners, as well as deliver radiation therapy for cancer treatment. In Singapore, these professionals are essential to the healthcare system, working in public hospitals, private clinics, and specialist centres. They require strong technical skills, patient care abilities, and knowledge of radiation safety. Registration with the Allied Health Professions Council (AHPC) is mandatory. The role demands attention to detail, empathy, and the ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced clinical environment.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary with experience, qualifications, and employer (public vs private). Entry-level diploma holders earn around $2,500-$3,200 per month, while experienced degree holders can exceed $5,500. Public hospitals offer structured pay scales with bonuses.

**Q: Can foreign medical imaging technicians obtain a work visa for Singapore?**
A: Yes. Most technicians qualify for an Employment Pass (EP) if they hold a degree and secure a job paying at least $5,000/month. Diploma holders may get an S Pass if the employer has quota. AHPC registration is also required.

**Q: What education is needed to become a radiographer in Singapore?**
A: A diploma in diagnostic radiography from a local polytechnic (e.g., Nanyang Polytechnic) or a bachelor's degree from SIT or abroad is required. Graduates must then register with AHPC before practising.
