# Personal Services Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 5169 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a range of personal services not elsewhere classified, such as pet grooming, funeral directing, and other specialised personal care. In Singapore, the sector is niche but growing, driven by rising pet ownership and demand for premium services. Most roles require practical skills rather than formal degrees, with training available through polytechnics, ITE, or private academies. Workers are typically employed in small businesses or as freelancers, with limited large-scale employer sponsorship.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression is generally flat, from assistant to senior groomer or independent operator. Some workers move into sales of pet products or start their own businesses. Certification from recognised bodies (e.g., International Professional Groomers) can enhance prospects. In funeral services, progression to embalmer or funeral director is possible with experience and licensing. The lack of a formal career structure limits advancement in larger firms.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a personal services worker in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by role. Pet groomers earn around SGD 1,800–3,000 per month gross, while funeral service workers may earn SGD 2,000–4,000 depending on experience and licensing.

**Q: Can foreigners work as personal services workers in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but mainly via the S Pass (mid-skill) or Work Permit (semi-skilled). Employment Pass is rarely issued due to lower salary thresholds. Self-employment is possible with a local business registration.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities?**
A: Progression is limited. Groomers can advance to master groomer or open their own salon. Funeral workers can become licensed embalmers or funeral directors. Certification and business skills are key.
