# Building Structure Cleaner — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7133 (ISCO08)**

Building structure cleaners, also known as facade or window cleaners, specialize in cleaning the exteriors of buildings, including windows, walls, and roofs, often using suspended platforms, ladders, or cherry pickers. In Singapore, this occupation is predominantly filled by foreign workers on Work Permits due to the physically demanding nature and low local interest, with most jobs concentrated in the construction and property maintenance sectors. The work requires adherence to strict safety regulations, including the use of personal protective equipment (PPE) and compliance with the Workplace Safety and Health Act. While no formal education is required, workers typically undergo on-the-job training and may obtain industry-recognized certifications in safety and cleaning techniques.

Career progression for building structure cleaners is minimal, with most workers remaining in the same role. Some may advance to team leaders or supervisors with experience, or move into related fields like cleaning management or safety coordination. In Singapore, the role is often viewed as a stepping stone for foreign workers seeking better opportunities, but locals rarely pursue long-term careers due to low pay and physical demands. Training in specialized areas like high-rise rescue or hazardous material cleaning can improve prospects.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What visa do building structure cleaners in Singapore typically hold?**
A: Most building structure cleaners are foreign workers on a Work Permit (Construction), which requires employer sponsorship and is subject to quota and levy. Only workers from approved source countries are eligible.

**Q: Is the salary for building structure cleaners sufficient to support a family in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for this role are generally low, often at or near the Work Permit minimum salary requirements. They are typically suitable for single individuals or as supplementary income, not for supporting a family.

**Q: What training is required to become a building structure cleaner in Singapore?**
A: No formal education is required, but workers must undergo safety training, such as the Workplace Safety and Health (WSH) course for working at heights. Additional certifications in cleaning techniques can improve job prospects.
