# Economist — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2631 (ISCO08)**

Economists in Singapore analyse data to forecast economic trends, evaluate policies, and advise businesses and government. They work in the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), banks, consultancies, research institutes, and ministries. The role requires strong quantitative skills and an understanding of global and local markets. Singapore's status as a financial hub sustains demand for economic analysis, though competition is keen for top positions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically starts as a research analyst or junior economist, advancing to senior economist, lead economist, or department head. Many move into policy roles at MAS or other agencies. Some transition to academia or private sector strategic roles. Continuing education (e.g., Master's, PhD) is common. The field rewards deep specialisation in areas like monetary policy, trade, or labour economics.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic data cleaning and sorting
- Create standard statistical charts and reports
- Preliminary regression analysis and model runs.
- Automated search and summarization of literature reviews
- Generate periodic economic forecast templates

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Rapid iteration and validation of complex economic models
- Real-time economic indicator monitoring and anomaly warning
- Policy impact simulation and scenario analysis
- Integration and insight extraction from multi-source heterogeneous data.
- Rapid generation of personalized economic advisory reports

**Human moat:**

- Understanding the political and social constraints behind economic policy
- Propose innovative theoretical frameworks and hypotheses
- Make value judgments and ethical considerations.
- Building trust and engaging in high-level communication with decision-makers
- Strategic decision-making in uncertain environments

**Skills to build:**

- Python/R Programming and Data Science Tools
- Machine learning and causal inference methods
- Data visualization and storytelling
- Natural language processing for text data analysis
- Explainability techniques for AI models.
- Interdisciplinary knowledge integration ability

**Key growth areas:**

- Macroeconomic Analysis
- Financial Services
- Public Policy
- Data Analytics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an economist in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience; economists in financial services and consulting often earn more than those in government or academia. Exact figures depend on qualifications and the employer.

**Q: Do foreign economists need a specific visa to work in Singapore?**
A: Yes, foreign economists typically need an Employment Pass (EP) sponsored by a Singapore employer. The EP is assessed under the COMPASS framework, which considers salary, qualifications, and diversity.

**Q: What is the career progression for economists in Singapore?**
A: Progression often starts as a research analyst, then to economist, senior economist, and possibly to head of research or chief economist. Some move to policy roles at MAS or into academia.
