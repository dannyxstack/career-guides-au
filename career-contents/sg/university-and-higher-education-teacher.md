# University and Higher Education Teacher — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2310 (ISCO08)**

University and Higher Education Teachers in Singapore deliver lectures, tutorials, and conduct research at institutions such as the National University of Singapore (NUS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore Management University (SMU), and other autonomous universities. They design curricula, assess students, and publish academic work. This occupation is at the core of Singapore's ambition to be a global education hub, attracting top-tier academics worldwide. Employment is primarily through employer-sponsored Employment Passes, with a focus on research excellence and teaching quality. The role is highly competitive and requires a PhD or equivalent terminal degree in the relevant field.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 17,634. Singapore's higher education sector is stable but selective. Growth is focused on strategic areas such as artificial intelligence, sustainability, digital humanities, and health sciences, with the government investing in research grants and endowed chairs. University faculty openings are limited, especially for tenure-track positions, and hiring is driven by replacement and new initiatives. International candidates face strong competition, but Singapore remains open to top talent through the Employment Pass route. Contract-based teaching positions are more common for early-career academics.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically follows a tenure track: Assistant Professor (tenure-track), Associate Professor (with or without tenure), and Full Professor. In Singapore, universities also offer practice-track and educator-track roles focusing on teaching and industry engagement. Promotion depends on research output, teaching quality, and service. Some faculty move into administrative roles like Dean or Vice Provost. Given Singapore's small size, career advancement requires consistent publications and grants, and inter-university mobility is limited.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate course outlines and lecture drafts
- Basic Q&A and automated responses to common questions
- Preliminary grading and feedback on student assignments
- Literature review and data collation
- Administrative tasks (e.g., class scheduling, grade entry)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted personalised learning path design and adaptive assessment
- Use LLMs to quickly generate teaching cases and simulated discussions
- Assist with hypothesis testing, data analysis, and paper polishing in research
- Virtual classrooms and collaborative teaching with AI teaching assistants
- Knowledge graph construction and interdisciplinary curriculum planning

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face mentorship and emotional support
- In-depth explanation of complex concepts and stimulation of critical thinking
- Formulation of original research questions and method design
- Ethical judgment and academic decision-making
- Creative integration in overall curriculum design

**Skills to build:**

- Application of AI education tools (e.g., Knewton, Carnegie Learning).
- Data analysis and statistical modeling (Python/R)
- Prompt engineering and large model fine-tuning
- Blended instructional design (MOOC/flipped classroom)
- Academic writing and AI-assisted polishing
- Data Privacy and AI Ethics

**Key growth areas:**

- Artificial Intelligence
- Sustainability
- Digital Humanities
- Medical Education

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Singapore's main professional work pass is the Employment Pass, granted to qualifying professionals with a job offer above a salary threshold; long-term holders may apply for Permanent Residence. Criteria are set by the Ministry of Manpower and updated regularly.

**Official immigration source:** [Ministry of Manpower (MOM)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for university professors in Singapore?**
A: For assistant professors, annual salaries range from SGD 120,000 to SGD 150,000; associate professors earn SGD 150,000–200,000; full professors can earn SGD 200,000–250,000 or more, depending on institution, discipline, and experience. Private institutions may offer lower pay.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a foreign university lecturer?**
A: Yes, if you secure a position at a recognised institution and meet the minimum salary threshold (≥ SGD 5,000/month). The employer sponsors the EP, and COMPASS points are typically favourable for academics with relevant qualifications and experience.

**Q: Is there a path to permanent residency for academics?**
A: Yes, many academics obtain PR after 2-3 years on an EP. Applications are assessed on individual merit, including salary, education, and contribution to Singapore. Being sponsored by a local university strengthens the case.
