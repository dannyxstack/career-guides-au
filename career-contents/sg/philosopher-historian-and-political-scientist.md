# Philosopher, Historian and Political Scientist — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2633 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, philosophers, historians, and political scientists primarily work in academia, research institutes, think tanks, government agencies, and the cultural sector. They analyze societal issues, historical events, and political systems, contributing to public policy development, education, and cultural preservation. The field is small but valued for its role in shaping national identity and policy discourse.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a political scientist in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely. Junior research analysts may earn SGD 40,000–60,000 annually, while senior academics or directors can earn SGD 100,000–150,000. Data should be verified with the Ministry of Manpower's Occupational Wage Survey.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a philosopher in Singapore?**
A: Yes, an Employment Pass is common for qualified professionals with a job offer from a local employer. The role must meet COMPASS criteria, and the job should be in academia, research, or policy analysis. Self-employment or freelance work does not qualify for an EP.

**Q: What is the job market like for historians in Singapore?**
A: The market is very niche. Opportunities exist mainly in universities (NUS, NTU, SMU), research institutes (ISEAS, S. Rajaratnam School of International Studies), museums (National Heritage Board), and government agencies (Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth). Networking and publications are essential.
