# Electrical Line Installer and Repairer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7413 (ISCO08)**

Electrical Line Installers and Repairers in Singapore install, maintain, and repair overhead and underground electrical power and telecommunications lines. They are crucial for the nation's grid reliability and expansion, supporting smart grid initiatives and broadband infrastructure. Workers often operate at heights or in confined spaces, adhering to strict safety protocols. The role is typically performed by local or migrant workers holding Work Permits or S Passes, with training provided by employers or through the Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ) system. Demand is steady due to ongoing urban development and renewal of aging networks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a trainee or assistant, advancing to a qualified line installer after 2-4 years of on-the-job training and WSQ certifications. Experienced workers can become senior line installers, supervisors, or specialize in areas like underground cabling or live-line maintenance. Further advancement into project management or engineering roles (e.g., as a licensed electrical worker) is possible with additional education. The sector offers stable employment, but physical demands and safety risks mean not all workers stay long-term.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Uses drones or AI vision systems for line inspection and preliminary fault location
- Automatic generation of predictive maintenance plans based on historical data
- Remote monitoring system automatic alarms and status reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted fault diagnosis and repair plan recommendations to improve on-site efficiency

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an electrical line installer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for entry-level line installers (Work Permit holders) range from SGD 1,500 to SGD 2,200 per month. Experienced workers with certifications can earn SGD 2,500 to SGD 3,500 per month. Supervisors or S Pass holders may earn SGD 3,000 to SGD 4,500 per month. These are gross figures, excluding overtime.

**Q: Can I migrate to Singapore as an electrical line installer?**
A: Yes, but mainly through employer-sponsored Work Permits for semi-skilled workers from approved source countries. The S Pass is possible for those with higher qualifications and experience. There is no direct permanent residency path through this occupation, but long-term work may lead to PR consideration.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for electrical line installers?**
A: With experience and additional certifications, workers can become senior line installers, supervisors, or specialists in areas like high-voltage live-line maintenance. Some progress to become Licensed Electrical Workers (LEW) or move into project management roles in utilities or construction companies.
