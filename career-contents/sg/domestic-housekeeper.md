# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 5152 (ISCO08)**

Domestic housekeepers, commonly referred to as foreign domestic workers (FDWs) in Singapore, provide essential household services such as cleaning, cooking, and caregiving in private homes. They are predominantly women from neighbouring countries like Indonesia, the Philippines, and Myanmar, employed under the Work Permit for FDWs. The demand for domestic housekeepers is driven by Singapore's aging population, high female labour force participation, and busy work schedules. MOM regulates their employment through mandatory security bonds, medical insurance, and a minimum weekly rest day.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for domestic housekeepers is limited, with most remaining in the same role throughout their tenure. However, some may advance to senior positions caring for elderly or disabled family members, or specialise in infant care or pet care. A few agencies offer training programmes that lead to more specialised roles. Beyond household work, there is no clear career ladder, and most FDWs return to their home country after their contract ends. Opportunities to transition into formal caregiving roles in institutions are rare.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a domestic housekeeper in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for domestic housekeepers vary by nationality and experience. As of 2025, the median monthly salary is around SGD 600-800 for new FDWs, with higher pay for experienced or specialized caregivers. Employers also provide free accommodation and meals. Salary is set by bilateral agreements and individual contracts.

**Q: Can a domestic housekeeper switch employers easily?**
A: No. Under the Work Permit for FDWs, a change of employer requires MOM's approval and is subject to conditions such as the worker having served at least 6 months with the current employer, except in cases of employer misconduct or other extenuating circumstances.

**Q: Is there any pathway to permanent residency for domestic housekeepers?**
A: No. The Work Permit for FDWs is a temporary visa with no pathway to permanent residence. Domestic housekeepers must return to their home country after their contract ends. They may only seek long-term stay if they marry a Singapore citizen, which is subject to separate immigration assessment.
