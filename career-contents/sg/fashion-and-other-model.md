# Fashion and Other Models — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 5241 (ISCO08)**

Fashion and other models in Singapore are primarily employed in the retail, fashion, and advertising industries, often on a freelance or project basis. The local market is small but active, with demand driven by commercial shoots, fashion shows, and e-commerce platforms. Most models are represented by agencies and work on short-term contracts. The occupation does not typically offer full-time employment, and income is highly variable. Singapore's modeling scene is cosmopolitan, with a mix of local and international talent, but opportunities are limited compared to major fashion capitals.

**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for models in Singapore is limited; many transition into related fields such as acting, influencer work, or fashion styling after a few years. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a fashion model in Singapore?**
A: Income varies widely; most models earn on a per-project basis. Rates for commercial shoots range from SGD 500 to SGD 3,000 per day, but annual earnings for full-time models are often below SGD 40,000 due to irregular work.

**Q: Can foreign models work in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but they need employer sponsorship via an S Pass or Work Permit. The process is competitive; agencies must show that no suitable local talent is available. Quotas and levies apply for S Passes.

**Q: What are the career prospects for models in Singapore?**
A: Most models have short careers due to age and market saturation. Many transition into related fields like acting, fashion styling, or influencer marketing after a few years.
