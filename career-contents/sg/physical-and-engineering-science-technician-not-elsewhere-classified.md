# Physical and Engineering Science Technician — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3119 (ISCO08)**

Physical and Engineering Science Technicians Not Elsewhere Classified include a broad range of technical support roles in Singapore's manufacturing, precision engineering, and R&D sectors. They assist engineers with experiments, quality control, equipment maintenance, and production troubleshooting. Technicians are vital in supporting Singapore's high-tech manufacturing ecosystem, especially in electronics, chemicals, and biomedical industries. Typical employers include multinational corporations, research institutes, and contract manufacturing firms. The role requires practical skills, attention to detail, and the ability to work with scientific instruments and computerised systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior technician, advancing to senior technician, technical officer, or lead technician. With further education (e.g., part-time degree), one can move into engineering or managerial roles. Many technicians pursue WSQ Advanced Certificates or Specialist Diplomas to specialise in areas like mechatronics or quality assurance. Some transition into technical sales or application support. The Singapore government's SkillsFuture programme provides subsidies for upskilling, making continuous learning accessible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically record experimental data and generate standardized reports
- Use AI algorithms for preliminary data analysis and anomaly detection
- Automatically calibrate and adjust experimental parameters based on historical data
- Classify samples and perform basic inspections using computer vision
- Automatically organize literature and preliminary experimental design

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using machine learning models to optimise experimental design and improve efficiency
- AI-assisted multi-variable data analysis to discover hidden patterns
- Using digital twins to simulate experimental processes and predict outcomes.
- Quick retrieval of technical documents and specifications using natural language processing
- AI-driven predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime.

**Human moat:**

- On-site equipment fault diagnosis and repair decision-making
- Innovative experimental design and non-standard testing.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration, communication, and explaining technical details
- Safety management and compliance assessment
- Customer requirement analysis and customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of Python or R programming
- Introduction to Statistics and Machine Learning
- Digital lab management software (e.g., LabVantage).
- PLC or Embedded Systems basics
- Communication and project management certifications (e.g., PMP)
- Industry-specific regulations (e.g., ISO 17025)

**Key growth areas:**

- Advanced Manufacturing
- Semiconductor
- Biomedical
- Robotics

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with strong practical aptitude and problem-solving skills who enjoy hands-on technical work in manufacturing or research settings.
- Candidates who prefer structured career progression with on-the-job training and certification opportunities.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay range for an Engineering Technician in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level technicians can expect around S$2,500 to S$3,500 per month, while experienced technicians with specialisations may earn S$4,000 to S$5,500. Salaries vary by industry and certifications.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an Engineering Technician in Singapore?**
A: Yes, mainly via the S Pass for mid-skilled workers. Companies must meet quota and levy requirements. A diploma or relevant certification is typically needed. For higher-skilled roles, the Employment Pass is applicable.

**Q: What progression opportunities exist for technicians?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior technician, technical officer, or move into engineering roles by obtaining a degree. Specialised certifications in areas like automation or quality assurance boost prospects.
