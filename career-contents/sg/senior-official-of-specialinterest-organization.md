# Senior Officials of Special-Interest Organizations — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1114 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, senior officials of special-interest organizations lead trade unions, employer associations, professional bodies, and non-profits. They advocate for members' interests, negotiate with government and business, and shape policy. Roles include Secretary-General of NTUC, President of SNEF, or Executive Director of sectoral associations. These positions require deep local understanding and are typically held by experienced Singaporeans or PRs, often with backgrounds in law, economics, or public administration. They work closely with tripartite partners to maintain Singapore's labor harmony and economic competitiveness.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for senior officials of special-interest organizations in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by organization size and sector. For top roles like NTUC Secretary-General or SNEF President, annual pre-tax salary can range from S$250,000 to S$500,000+, including allowances. Mid-level senior officials earn around S$150,000 to S$250,000. These are conservative estimates.

**Q: Can foreigners work as senior officials of special-interest organizations in Singapore?**
A: It is rare. The role requires deep local knowledge and trust. Foreigners may qualify for an Employment Pass only if they have exceptional expertise and no local candidate is available. Most incumbents are Singaporeans or PRs.

**Q: What is the career progression for this role?**
A: Typically, one starts as a union executive, policy analyst, or industry association manager. With experience and leadership, one can rise to senior official. Some move to public service or political roles. Succession is usually internal and carefully managed.
