# Cleaner — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9129 (ISCO08)**

A Cleaner in Singapore performs routine cleaning tasks in commercial, residential, or public spaces, including offices, hotels, hospitals, and shopping malls. The role is essential for maintaining hygiene and cleanliness, with growing demand post-COVID. Most cleaners are foreign workers on Work Permits, with locals increasingly encouraged through the Progressive Wage Model.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited but possible through skills upgrading. Cleaners can move to supervisory roles or specialize in areas like green cleaning or healthcare cleaning. The Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ) framework provides certifications. However, many workers remain in entry-level positions due to limited vertical mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic floor scrubbers or robot vacuums for floor cleaning
- Automatic vacuuming and mopping along preset routes
- Timely automatic spraying of disinfectant for environmental disinfection.
- Use AI trash bins to automatically sort and compact waste
- Monitor cleanliness via sensors and generate cleaning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and shifts.
- Using computer vision to detect stains and guide targeted cleaning
- Use voice assistants or apps to log cleaning tasks and feedback
- Use data analysis to predict peak hours and prepare in advance
- Operate semi-automatic cleaning equipment for high-altitude or corner cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Experience in cleaning complex stains and special material surfaces.
- Communicating with clients to confirm cleaning requirements and flexibly adjusting
- Expertise in non-toxic, environmentally friendly cleaning agents
- Identify safety hazards on site and take emergency measures on the spot
- Manual cleaning in narrow, irregular spaces is irreplaceable.

**Skills to build:**

- Learn to operate cleaning robots or smart cleaning equipment
- Basic computer operation and simple programming (e.g., setting cleaning routes)
- Understanding the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the status of cleaning equipment
- Knowledge of Disinfection and Chemical Safety in Learning Environments
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn data analysis fundamentals to optimize cleaning efficiency

**Key growth areas:**

- Green cleaning
- Healthcare cleaning
- Sanitisation services
- Automation-assisted cleaning

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cleaner in Singapore?**
A: Salaries follow the Progressive Wage Model, which mandates a basic salary that increases with skills and experience. Exact figures depend on sector and employer.

**Q: Can a cleaner get an Employment Pass?**
A: No. Cleaners typically do not qualify for Employment Passes due to the skill level. They are eligible for Work Permits, which require employer sponsorship and are subject to foreign worker quotas and levies.

**Q: Is there a career path for cleaners?**
A: Yes, through the Progressive Wage Model and WSQ certifications, cleaners can become senior cleaners or supervisors, and even move into specialised cleaning like healthcare or industrial cleaning.
