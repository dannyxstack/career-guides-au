# Street and Related Services Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9510 (ISCO08)**

Street and Related Services Workers in Singapore typically include hawker assistants, street food vendors, and service staff at market stalls or pop-up kiosks. These roles are integral to Singapore's vibrant hawker culture, which is recognized as a UNESCO intangible cultural heritage. Workers are often involved in food preparation, serving customers, and maintaining cleanliness. The occupation is largely low-skilled, with many workers employed on a part-time or casual basis. Foreign workers may be hired for these roles under the Work Permit system, but opportunities are limited due to local manpower policies and a preference for locals in service roles.

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 252. The outlook for Street and Related Services Workers in Singapore is stable but challenged by automation and changing consumer habits. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for Street and Related Services Workers is limited. Most start as assistants and may become stall owners or supervisors with experience. Some move into related roles in food and beverage or retail management. Skills upgrading in food hygiene, customer service, and basic business management can lead to better opportunities. However, the occupation generally offers low mobility, and many workers remain in similar roles throughout their careers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Parking meter collection (replaced by mobile payments and automatic billing systems)
- Street road cleaning (replaced by autonomous cleaning robots)
- Simple customer inquiries (replaced by AI voice assistants and self-service kiosks)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile payment data analysis to analyze foot traffic and sales timing.
- Post real-time service locations and promotional information through social platforms
- Using voice translation tools to serve multilingual tourists

**Human moat:**

- On-site incident handling and de-escalation in emergencies (e.g., traffic accidents, disputes)
- Ability to build trust and emotional connection with regular customers
- In-depth understanding and flexible application of local regulations and community norms

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (using mobile payment terminals, managing online reviews)
- Basic English or foreign language communication
- Customer service and conflict resolution skills
- Time management and multitasking
- Basic repair skills (e.g., trolleys, cleaning equipment)

**Key growth areas:**

- hawker culture
- food services
- night markets
- street food tourism

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Street and Related Services Workers in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for street workers vary, but median monthly wages for hawker assistants and stall helpers range from SGD 1,500 to SGD 2,200. Income can be higher for stall owners or self-employed vendors.

**Q: Can foreigners work as street vendors in Singapore?**
A: Foreigners generally cannot work as self-employed street vendors. They may be employed as assistants under a Work Permit, but quotas and levies apply. Employment Pass and S Pass are not typically issued for this occupation.

**Q: Are there any training programs for street food workers in Singapore?**
A: Yes, the WSQ Food Safety Course is mandatory. Additionally, SkillsFuture credits can be used for courses in food hygiene, customer service, and basic entrepreneurship, which are beneficial for career advancement.
