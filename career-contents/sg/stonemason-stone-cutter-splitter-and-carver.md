# Stonemason — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7113 (ISCO08)**

Stonemasons in Singapore are skilled tradespeople specializing in shaping, cutting, and installing natural stone for building facades, interior finishes, monuments, and restoration projects. While the construction industry is dominated by concrete and steel, niche demand exists for high-end residential, commercial, and heritage restoration work. Stonemasons often work for specialized subcontractors on projects like luxury condos, hotels, and conservation areas. The occupation is not formally regulated, but practical skills are learned through on-the-job training or short courses. Due to Singapore's small land area and limited natural stone resources, the craft relies heavily on imported materials and skilled foreign workers.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 227. Employment outlook for stonemasons in Singapore is stable but limited. Growth is tied to the luxury property market and heritage conservation projects. As the construction sector increasingly adopts prefabrication and modular techniques, traditional stonemasonry faces a gradual decline. However, specialized skills in stone cladding, marble finishing, and restoration remain in demand for high-end projects. Foreign workers dominate the trade, with locals rarely entering due to physical demands and competition. The medium-term outlook suggests a niche but steady demand, with occasional surges during major developments.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for stonemasons typically starts as a trainee or apprentice under experienced craftsmen. With several years of experience, one can advance to a senior mason or supervisor role. Some stonemasons eventually become specialist contractors or start their own subcontracting businesses, often focusing on luxury residential or commercial cladding. There is limited formal career path within large firms; most advancement relies on skill mastery and reputation. Additional certifications in workplace safety or stone technology can improve employability. The trade offers a stable income for those who persevere but limited upward mobility into management.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a stonemason in Singapore?**
A: Salaries are highly variable and depend on skill level and project type. The Ministry of Manpower's Occupational Wage Survey provides median salary data for this occupation.

**Q: What work pass do foreign stonemasons need to work in Singapore?**
A: Foreign stonemasons typically require a Work Permit for the construction sector, subject to the employer's quota and levy. S Pass may be possible for those with higher qualifications and salary above the minimum threshold, but Employment Pass is not applicable for this trade.

**Q: Are there opportunities for career advancement as a stonemason in Singapore?**
A: Career advancement is possible through gaining experience and specialized skills. Some stonemasons become supervisors or start their own subcontracting businesses. Certification in stone technology or workplace safety can enhance prospects, but upward mobility within large firms is limited.
