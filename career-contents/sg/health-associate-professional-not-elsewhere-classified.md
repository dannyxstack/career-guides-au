# Health Associate Professional — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3259 (ISCO08)**

Health associate professionals not elsewhere classified include a range of allied health roles such as pharmacy technicians, dental assistants, medical laboratory technologists, and audiology technicians. In Singapore, they are essential to the healthcare system, supporting doctors and nurses in hospitals, clinics, and community care settings. These professionals typically hold diplomas or higher qualifications and are regulated by professional bodies. The ageing population and expanding healthcare services drive demand for these roles, with opportunities in both public and private sectors.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a health associate professional in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by role and experience. A pharmacy technician or medical lab technologist with a diploma can expect a monthly salary between SGD 2,000 and SGD 3,500 for entry-level positions. Senior roles or those with specialised skills may earn higher. Refer to the latest Ministry of Manpower salary benchmarks for details.

**Q: Can foreigners work as health associate professionals in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but typically via the S Pass or Work Permit. Employers must demonstrate efforts to hire local workers first. The S Pass has a minimum salary requirement (currently around SGD 3,000) and is subject to quota and levy. Work Permits are more restrictive and available for certain source countries. These roles are not typically eligible for the Employment Pass.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for health associate professionals?**
A: With experience and further training, one can progress to senior technician, team leader, or trainer. Some pursue part-time degrees to become pharmacists, medical laboratory scientists, or nurses. The healthcare sector offers clear career ladders and support for upskilling through government programmes.
