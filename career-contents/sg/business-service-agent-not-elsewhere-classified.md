# Business Services Agent — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3339 (ISCO08)**

Business services agents in Singapore provide specialized intermediary services across real estate, insurance, recruitment, and business brokerage. They facilitate transactions between parties, advise on market conditions, and ensure compliance with local regulations. The sector is regulated, with licensing requirements for real estate agents (CEA) and employment agents (MOM). These roles often operate on commission-based income, attracting self-starters. Singapore's status as a regional business hub creates steady demand for skilled agents who can navigate multicultural and regulatory landscapes.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a business services agent in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. Entry-level agents earn a base of around SGD 2,000–3,000 per month plus commission. Experienced agents (3-5 years) can earn SGD 4,000–8,000 monthly, while top performers may exceed SGD 10,000. Annual bonuses are common.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a business services agent in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but with restrictions. Real estate agents must hold a valid RES and be employed by a licensed agency; an employer must sponsor an S Pass or Employment Pass. The COMPASS framework for EP requires points on salary, qualifications, and diversity. Most work permits are not issued for this role.

**Q: Is it easy to switch to business services agent without experience?**
A: Many agencies provide training, but you must pass licensing exams. Networking and a client base are crucial; new entrants may find it challenging initially. Previous sales or customer service experience is helpful.
