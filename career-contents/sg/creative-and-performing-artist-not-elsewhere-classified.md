# Creative And Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2659 (ISCO08)**

Creative and performing artists not elsewhere classified encompass a diverse range of talents including visual artists, dancers, musicians, actors, and performance artists who are not covered by more specific categories. In Singapore, this occupation is supported by a vibrant arts scene with government initiatives like the National Arts Council and the Renaissance City Plan, which promote cultural development. Artists often work on a freelance or contract basis, collaborating with galleries, theaters, and event companies. The role demands creativity, versatility, and resilience, as many artists also engage in teaching or commercial projects to sustain their practice.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What salary can a creative and performing artist expect in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, reputation, and employment type. Freelance artists may earn per project, while salaried positions in institutions or companies offer monthly wages. The range is typically from SGD 2,500 to over SGD 8,000 for experienced professionals, but many artists combine multiple income sources.

**Q: How can a foreign artist obtain a work visa in Singapore?**
A: Foreign artists typically need an Employment Pass (EP) sponsored by a Singapore employer, such as a gallery, theatre, or event company. The EP requires a minimum salary of SGD 5,000 and passes the COMPASS points system. Freelance artists may consider the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass (ONE Pass) for high earners. It's important to secure a job offer first.

**Q: What career progression options exist for artists in Singapore?**
A: Artists can advance from independent practitioner to creative director, gallery owner, or arts educator. Some move into arts administration or policy. Government grants and residencies help emerging talents. Networking and a strong digital presence are key.
