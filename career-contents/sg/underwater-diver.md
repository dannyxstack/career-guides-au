# Underwater Diver — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7541 (ISCO08)**

Underwater divers in Singapore perform sub-surface tasks such as inspection, maintenance, repair, and construction of marine structures, including ship hulls, offshore platforms, and port facilities. They are employed by marine services companies, offshore oil and gas firms, and government agencies like the Maritime and Port Authority. The role demands rigorous safety standards and physical fitness, with most divers holding commercial diving certifications.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from entry-level diver to experienced diver, then to dive supervisor or instructor. Divers can specialize in inspection (NDT), welding, or salvage. With experience, some move into project management or safety roles. Certification from recognized bodies like the Association of Diving Contractors International (ADCI) is essential. Locally, the Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ) framework offers progressive pathways.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.
- Routine structural measurement: ROV with sensors automatically collects data
- Underwater environment monitoring: AI analyzes parameters such as water temperature and turbidity
- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk
- Augmented reality headset: overlays structural diagrams and repair instructions to improve work accuracy
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes historical data to optimize maintenance schedules.
- Remote specialist collaboration: AI translation and 3D reconstruction help land-based experts guide

**Human moat:**

- Manual operation and quick judgment in emergency situations
- Fine tactile sensation and force feedback (e.g., valve operation)
- Adaptability in harsh environments (current, visibility)
- Legal and safety responsibility attribution (divers must sign on-site)

**Skills to build:**

- ROV/underwater robot operation and basic maintenance
- AI data analysis tools (e.g., Python image processing libraries)
- Underwater communication and AR system usage
- Advanced welding and cutting qualifications (e.g., underwater welder certificate)
- Diving medicine and physiological monitoring knowledge.
- Project management and team coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Offshore Oil & Gas
- Marine Inspection
- Underwater Welding
- Harbour Works

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an underwater diver in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level commercial divers earn about SGD 2,500–3,500 per month, while experienced divers (e.g., dive supervisors) can earn SGD 5,000–8,000. Specialists like underwater welders command higher rates.

**Q: Can a foreign underwater diver get an Employment Pass or S Pass in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but most divers obtain an S Pass or Work Permit. Employment Pass is possible for highly experienced divers with specialist skills. The employer must sponsor and meet quota/levy requirements.

**Q: What certifications are recognized for underwater divers in Singapore?**
A: The Maritime and Port Authority (MPA) and MOM recognize ADCI, IMCA, and WSQ commercial diving certifications. A valid medical fitness certificate is also required.
