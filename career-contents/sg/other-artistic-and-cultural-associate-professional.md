# Other Artistic and Cultural Associate Professional — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3435 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers associate professionals who support artistic and cultural activities, such as gallery assistants, museum technicians, event coordinators for arts festivals, and heritage interpreters. In Singapore, these roles are integral to the vibrant arts scene supported by the National Arts Council and museums like the National Gallery. Workers often assist in exhibition setup, visitor services, or cultural programming. The role requires strong organisational and communication skills, with opportunities in both public institutions and private galleries.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this role in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start around SGD 2,500–3,500 per month, while senior associates can earn SGD 4,000–5,500. Public sector roles may offer lower pay but better benefits.

**Q: Can foreigners work as artistic associate professionals in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but they must secure an Employment Pass (for higher salaries) or S Pass (for mid-level), and the employer must prove that no suitable local candidate is available. The role is not on the shortage list, so visa approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What are the key skills needed for this career?**
A: Strong communication, organisational, and customer service skills are essential. Familiarity with arts software, basic design tools, and knowledge of Singapore's cultural landscape give an edge.
