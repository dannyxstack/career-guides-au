# Film, Stage and Related Director and Producer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2654 (ISCO08)**

This occupation encompasses directors and producers in film, television, theatre, and related performing arts. In Singapore, the sector is small but dynamic, supported by government initiatives like the Singapore Film Commission and the Media Development Authority. Directors oversee the artistic and technical aspects of productions, while producers manage budgets, schedules, and logistics. The industry includes both independent productions and larger commercial projects, with increasing demand for digital content and streaming platforms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with assistant roles (e.g., assistant director, production coordinator) after gaining relevant education or practical experience. Advancement to director or producer often requires a strong portfolio and industry connections. Some move into larger-budget productions or specialise in areas like commercial advertising or live events. Networking and continuous upskilling in digital tools are crucial for long-term success.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a film director or producer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, project scale, and employment type. Entry-level assistant directors or producers may earn around SGD 30,000–45,000 annually, while experienced directors on major projects can earn over SGD 80,000–120,000. Freelance rates are project-based and can fluctuate.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a foreign film director or producer?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Singapore-registered company and meet the minimum salary threshold (SGD 5,000/month for EP). COMPASS points are awarded for qualifications, diversity, and skills. Having a degree and relevant experience improves your chances. S Pass is possible for lower salaries but less common for senior roles.
