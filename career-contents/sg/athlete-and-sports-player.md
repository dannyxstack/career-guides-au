# Athlete — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3421 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, professional athletes and sports players are primarily supported by national sports associations, private sponsors, or government schemes like the spexCard. The sector is small but growing, with increasing investment in sports science, facilities, and international events. Most athletes combine their sporting careers with coaching or part-time work due to limited full-time positions. Eligibility for foreign athletes is restricted, with employment passes granted only to top-tier talents meeting COMPASS criteria.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Singapore's main professional work pass is the Employment Pass, granted to qualifying professionals with a job offer above a salary threshold; long-term holders may apply for Permanent Residence. Criteria are set by the Ministry of Manpower and updated regularly.

**Official immigration source:** [Ministry of Manpower (MOM)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can foreign athletes work in Singapore professionally?**
A: Yes, but only via an Employment Pass (EP) if they meet COMPASS criteria and have a job offer from a local sports organisation. The EP is tied to the employer and requires a minimum salary of $5,000/month. Training Employment Passes are available for short-term stints.

**Q: What is the salary range for athletes in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on the sport, level, and sponsorship. Most professional athletes earn between $30,000 and $100,000 annually, with elite performers receiving government allowances and endorsements. Many supplement income through coaching or part-time work.
