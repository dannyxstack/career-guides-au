# Personnel and Careers Professional — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2423 (ISCO08)**

Personnel and Careers Professionals in Singapore manage recruitment, employee relations, performance management, and career development within organisations. With a strong emphasis on talent acquisition and retention due to a tight labour market, HR professionals are key to aligning workforce strategies with business goals. The role requires expertise in local employment laws and cultural sensitivity.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level salaries for HR executives range from SGD 30,000 to SGD 45,000 per year, while experienced managers earn SGD 60,000 to SGD 100,000. Senior HR Business Partners or Heads of HR can earn SGD 120,000 to SGD 200,000+. Salaries vary by industry and company size.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a Personnel and Careers Professional?**
A: Yes, if you have a recognised degree and a job offer with salary meeting the EP minimum (SGD 5,600/month). The COMPASS framework awards points for qualifications, salary, and diversity. A degree in HR or related field and relevant experience improve chances.

**Q: What career progression can I expect in Singapore's HR sector?**
A: Typical path: HR Executive → HR Manager → HR Business Partner → Head of HR → CHRO. Specialising in areas like talent acquisition, compensation, or HR analytics can accelerate advancement. Professional certifications (IHRP, SHRM) and a master's degree are advantageous.
