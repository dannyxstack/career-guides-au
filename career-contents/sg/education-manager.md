# Education Manager — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1345 (ISCO08)**

Education Managers in Singapore oversee the administration, curriculum, and operations of educational institutions ranging from preschools to tertiary centres. They lead teams of educators, ensure compliance with Ministry of Education standards, and drive academic excellence. With Singapore's emphasis on lifelong learning and international education hubs, these professionals are in demand for their strategic planning and people management skills. They work in both public and private sectors, including international schools, and must navigate a multicultural environment. The role requires strong communication, leadership, and knowledge of educational policies.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Singapore's main professional work pass is the Employment Pass, granted to qualifying professionals with a job offer above a salary threshold; long-term holders may apply for Permanent Residence. Criteria are set by the Ministry of Manpower and updated regularly.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Education Managers in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for Education Managers in Singapore generally range from $80,000 to $150,000 per year for experienced principals or heads. Senior roles in international schools can exceed $200,000. Starting salaries for assistant principals are around $60,000 to $80,000.

**Q: Can a foreign Education Manager obtain an Employment Pass easily?**
A: Yes, if they meet COMPASS criteria, including a competitive salary, relevant qualifications, and diversity considerations. Prior experience in Singapore or MOE-recognised systems helps. However, local candidates are preferred, so the process is moderately selective.

**Q: What are the key growth areas for Education Managers in Singapore?**
A: International education, EdTech implementation, and lifelong learning (SkillsFuture) are hot. Early childhood education and special needs sectors also offer opportunities. Many move into consultancy or government roles.
