# Armed Forces Other Ranks — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

Armed Forces Other Ranks in Singapore refer to enlisted personnel in the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), including roles such as infantrymen, technicians, medics, and support staff. These positions are critical for national defense and are typically filled by Singapore citizens through National Service (NS) or regular service. The work involves rigorous training, discipline, and operational readiness, with opportunities to specialize in areas like cyber defense, engineering, or logistics. The SAF offers structured career pathways with promotions based on performance and leadership potential.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Armed Forces Other Ranks in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for Other Ranks vary by rank and specialization, but generally range from SGD 2,000 to SGD 5,000 per month for full-time regulars, with additional allowances such as combat pay and housing. National Service recruits receive a monthly allowance according to rank.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as Armed Forces Other Ranks in Singapore?**
A: No, these roles are restricted to Singapore citizens. National Service is mandatory for male citizens, and regular service is only open to citizens. Foreigners cannot obtain a work visa for military positions.

**Q: What are the long-term career prospects for Other Ranks?**
A: Career prospects include promotion to senior specialist or warrant officer, with opportunities for specialization in technical fields. Some may transition to officer roles via commissioning. SAF offers scholarships for further education, and skills gained are transferable to civilian sectors like security, engineering, and logistics.
