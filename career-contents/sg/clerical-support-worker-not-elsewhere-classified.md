# General Clerical Support Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

Clerical support workers not elsewhere classified perform a wide range of administrative and office tasks, including data entry, filing, record keeping, and customer service. In Singapore, these roles are common across all sectors, from corporate offices to government agencies and small businesses. The position typically requires secondary education or vocational training, with an emphasis on basic computer literacy and communication skills. Many employers value relevant experience and familiarity with local documentation processes.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for clerical support workers often moves towards specialised administrative roles, such as executive assistant, office manager, or compliance clerk. Upskilling in areas like Microsoft Office, data analytics, or project coordination can lead to higher-level positions. Continuous learning through WSQ courses or part-time diplomas is common to improve employability and salary prospects. Lateral moves into customer service or sales are also possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- digital administration
- compliance support
- remote office management
- data entry automation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Singapore's main professional work pass is the Employment Pass, granted to qualifying professionals with a job offer above a salary threshold; long-term holders may apply for Permanent Residence. Criteria are set by the Ministry of Manpower and updated regularly.

**Official immigration source:** [Ministry of Manpower (MOM)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a general clerical support worker in Singapore?**
A: Monthly salaries range from S$2,000 to S$3,500 for entry-level positions, depending on qualifications and experience. Those with specialised skills or in high-demand sectors may earn more.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a clerical support worker in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but work pass options are limited. Most foreign clerical workers hold an S Pass (for mid-skilled roles) or Work Permit (for semi-skilled roles). Both require employer sponsorship and are subject to quota and levy. Employment Pass is generally not available as it requires higher salaries (typically >S$5,000) and professional qualifications.
