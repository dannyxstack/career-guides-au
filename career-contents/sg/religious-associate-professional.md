# Religious Associate Professional — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3413 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, religious associate professionals support the spiritual and community activities of religious organisations, such as churches, mosques, temples, and other faith groups. They may assist in conducting services, providing pastoral care, organising events, and offering counselling. This role requires sensitivity to Singapore's multi-racial and multi-religious context, and often involves working closely with volunteers and community leaders. Employment is typically sponsored by religious institutions, and salaries vary based on the size and resources of the organisation. The role is not eligible under the COMPASS framework for Employment Pass unless the candidate meets specific criteria, and alternative passes like the S Pass may be applicable for mid-level roles.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a religious associate professional in Singapore?**
A: Salaries range from SGD 30,000 to SGD 60,000 per year, with higher pay for senior roles in larger organisations. Religious organisations often provide additional benefits such as housing or allowances.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a foreign religious associate professional?**
A: Yes, but it is not straightforward. The role must meet COMPASS points (e.g., salary, qualifications, diversity). Many religious positions are filled locally, and the Ministry of Manpower scrutinises applications to ensure genuine need.

**Q: What are the long-term career prospects?**
A: Prospects include advancement to senior pastoral roles, community service management, or transition to social work. However, promotion may be slow and often requires further theological training. Some professionals move into interfaith organisations or non-profits.
