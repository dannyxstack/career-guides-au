# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers in Singapore assist chefs and cooks with food preparation, cleaning, and maintenance in restaurants, hawker centres, hotels, and central kitchens. This entry-level role is vital to the F&B industry, which employs over 200,000 workers. Most workers are low-skilled and often foreign workers on Work Permits, though locals also enter the role via vocational training or direct hire.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 1,783. The outlook for kitchen helpers remains stable due to Singapore's vibrant food culture and reliance on F&B services. Growth is driven by new eateries, food delivery demand, and central kitchen expansion. However, automation (e.g., dishwashers, vegetable cutters) may slow demand. The role is not on any shortage list, and turnover is high. Opportunities are best in chain restaurants and large catering firms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited but possible: kitchen helpers can move to commis chef or cook roles with experience and certifications (e.g., WSQ in Food Preparation). Some advance to shift supervisor or kitchen manager in larger operations. Many stay in the role for years due to language or educational barriers. Upskilling is key to mobility, especially through SkillsFuture courses.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Weigh ingredients according to recipe
- Manual dishwashing
- Scrub kitchen surfaces
- Cutting standard vegetables
- Transporting ingredient inventory

**Where AI augments the role:**

- use automatic vegetable cutters to improve efficiency
- Uses intelligent inventory system to manage ingredients
- Learn new dishes through digital recipes.
- Operating automated dishwashing line
- Using cleaning robots to maintain floor hygiene

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability in emergency situations
- Sensory assessment of ingredient quality
- Collaboration with chefs and waitstaff.
- Personal responsibility for hygiene checks
- Flexibility for non-standard tasks

**Skills to build:**

- Basic automated equipment operation and maintenance
- Digital inventory management
- Food safety and hygiene monitoring system
- Basic cooking skills
- Multitasking coordination ability
- English communication (tourism and hospitality)

**Key growth areas:**

- Central Kitchens
- Hawker Centres
- Food Delivery Hubs
- Chain Restaurants

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a kitchen helper in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by employer and experience. Refer to MOM's Occupational Wage Survey 2025 for up-to-date data. Entry-level workers typically earn between $1,400 and $2,000 per month, with foreign workers often receiving housing and meals.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a kitchen helper in Singapore?**
A: Yes, mainly via a Work Permit for semi-skilled workers from approved source countries (e.g., Malaysia, China, India). The employer must sponsor and meet quota/levy. S Pass is possible if salary exceeds $3,150/month, but rare for this role.
