# Teaching Professional (Not Elsewhere Classified) — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

This broad ISCO-08 category covers teaching professionals not elsewhere classified, including polytechnic lecturers, adult education trainers, special education teachers, and instructors in private education institutions. In Singapore, the demand is driven by government initiatives like SkillsFuture and lifelong learning, as well as the need for specialized educators in areas such as special needs, vocational training, and corporate education. These professionals work in diverse settings: from public institutions like ITE and polytechnics to private training providers and community organizations. The role typically requires strong subject expertise, pedagogical skills, and adaptability to different learner groups.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a teaching professional in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. For MOE school teachers, entry-level around $3,500–$4,500/month; polytechnic lecturers start around $4,000–$6,000/month. Private sector and corporate training may range from $3,000 to $8,000/month. Refer to MOM's Occupational Wage Survey for detailed figures.

**Q: Can foreign teaching professionals obtain an Employment Pass easily?**
A: Yes, if you have a recognized degree and relevant experience. The Employment Pass is the main route for teaching professionals. The COMPASS framework requires a minimum salary and points for qualifications and diversity. Special education and technical subjects are in higher demand, improving chances.

**Q: What about teaching in MOE schools as a foreigner?**
A: MOE hires foreign teachers but prefers PRs or citizens for permanent roles. Foreigners can work on a contractual basis via Employment Pass. The process is competitive; strong subject knowledge and experience in high-demand areas (e.g., Math, Science) help.
