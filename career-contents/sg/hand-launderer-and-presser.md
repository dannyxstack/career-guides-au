# Hand Launderer and Presser — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9121 (ISCO08)**

Hand launderers and pressers manually wash, dry, and iron garments and textiles in Singapore's hospitality, retail, and commercial laundry sectors. This occupation is predominantly held by low-skilled foreign workers on Work Permits, supporting hotels, dry-cleaning shops, and laundry services. The role requires physical stamina and attention to detail, and typically involves repetitive tasks. In Singapore's labour market, this occupation is part of the broader housekeeping and cleaning workforce, with limited upward mobility. Automation and outsourcing have reduced demand slightly, but tourism and service industry growth sustain a steady need for manual laundry workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for hand launderers and pressers in Singapore is limited. Most workers remain in this role or move to similar low-skilled positions. With additional training, some may advance to supervisory roles in laundry operations or shift to related areas like housekeeping. Self-employment as a laundry service provider is possible but requires capital and clientele. SkillsFuture courses in laundry management can help but are not widely pursued. The occupation is generally a stepping stone for foreign workers on short-term contracts.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual washing operation: automatic washing machines can efficiently complete standard washing according to the program
- Regular ironing: Smart ironing machines batch-process flatwork items such as shirts and bed sheets.
- Manual sorting and folding: Image recognition and automatic sorting systems can identify clothing materials and fold them.
- Drying and sorting: automated hanging systems complete drying and sorting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Advanced stain removal: AI-assisted analysis of stain composition and recommendation of optimal cleaning solutions.
- Precision ironing: AI smart iron adjusts temperature and pressure in real time based on fabric.
- Customer preference management: system records customer laundry care preferences to enhance personalized service quality.
- Quality control: AI visual inspection for stains and wrinkles, assisting manual quality checks

**Human moat:**

- Experience in manual repair and care of delicate fabrics (e.g. lace, silk)
- Communicate with high-end clients, understand special cleaning needs and provide solutions
- Flexibility to handle unexpected situations (such as stains, damage)
- Shaping effects achieved by fine hand ironing (e.g., suits, gowns)

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of smart laundry equipment
- Knowledge of fabric properties and chemical stain removal
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic data analysis (recording efficiency, costs)
- Advanced manual ironing technology (customized)
- Eco-friendly detergents and sustainable practices.

**Key growth areas:**

- Hospitality support
- Manual laundry
- Ironing services
- Hotel housekeeping

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand launderer and presser in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and worker nationality. Foreign workers on Work Permits often earn the minimum salary set by MOM, currently around SGD 1,200–1,500 per month. Locals may earn higher but this is rare.

**Q: Can a hand launderer and presser get an Employment Pass or S Pass?**
A: Typically not. The occupation falls under low-skilled work, eligible only for Work Permit. S Pass requires a minimum salary of SGD 3,150/month, which is rarely met. Employment Pass is for professionals.

**Q: What career progression exists for hand launderers?**
A: Progression is limited. Options include moving to supervisor or team lead roles in commercial laundries, or transitioning to housekeeping. Self-employment as a laundry service provider is possible but requires startup capital.
