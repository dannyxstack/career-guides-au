# Beautician and Related Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 5142 (ISCO08)**

Beauticians and related workers in Singapore provide a range of personal care and aesthetic services, including skincare treatments, makeup application, nail care, and body therapies. The industry is driven by a growing demand for wellness and appearance-enhancing services, with many professionals employed in beauty salons, spas, and freelance roles. The sector is regulated by the Ministry of Health for certain treatments, and practitioners often pursue certifications from recognised bodies such as the Singapore Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ) or international associations like CIDESCO.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for beauticians typically starts from junior roles and advances to senior beautician, salon supervisor, or branch manager. Many professionals diversify into related fields such as makeup artistry, nail technology, or aesthetic consultancy. Entrepreneurial opportunities exist for those who set up their own studios or mobile services. Upskilling through certifications in new techniques (e.g., microblading, laser safety) or management courses can enhance career mobility and earning potential.

- Customer consultation and appointment scheduling (AI chatbot)
- Product recommendations and sales scripts (AI-based personalized recommendations).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for beauticians in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level beauticians earn around SGD 1,800–2,500 per month; experienced professionals with certifications can earn SGD 2,800–4,500, plus commissions. Freelancers may have variable income based on clientele.

**Q: Can foreign beauticians obtain an Employment Pass in Singapore?**
A: Employment Pass is generally not available for beauticians as the role is not considered a professional occupation. Most foreign beauticians work under an S Pass (if they meet the salary threshold and have relevant diplomas) or a Work Permit for semi-skilled roles.

**Q: Is there a pathway to permanent residence for beauticians in Singapore?**
A: Permanent residence is possible but not guaranteed. Applicants typically need an S Pass, a stable income, and strong ties to Singapore, such as family or long-term employment. Work Permit holders rarely qualify.
