# Glazier — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers in Singapore install, maintain, and replace glass in windows, doors, skylights, and facades of residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. They work primarily in the construction sector, often for glazing subcontractors or facade specialists. The role involves cutting, shaping, and fitting glass panels, using sealants and adhesives, and ensuring structural integrity and weatherproofing. With ongoing urban development and a focus on building safety, glaziers are needed for new projects and renovation works.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for glaziers in Singapore typically starts from apprenticeship or NITEC training, advancing to senior glazier or supervisor roles. With experience, one can move into project management, estimating, or quality control. Specialisation in high-performance glass, structural glazing, or heritage restoration can lead to higher pay. Employers often fund certifications like WSQ in Glass Installation and Facade Technology to upskill. However, career advancement beyond team leader may require additional qualifications in construction management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Green building
- Smart glass

- Renovation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience and employer, but typically range from entry-level rates (e.g., $1,800–$2,500 per month for Work Permit holders) to higher for skilled or local staff. Refer to MOM’s Occupational Wage Survey for current figures.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a glazier in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but must be sponsored by an employer. Most glaziers enter on a Work Permit (Construction) for semi-skilled roles, or an S Pass if they have formal qualifications and higher pay. Dependent on employer’s quota and levy.

**Q: What career progression is available for glaziers?**
A: With experience, glaziers can become team leaders, supervisors, or specialists in facade technology. Some move into estimating, project coordination, or start their own glazing businesses. Further training in construction management opens more opportunities.
