# Securities and Finance Dealer and Broker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3311 (ISCO08)**

Securities and finance dealers and brokers in Singapore execute trades in financial instruments such as stocks, bonds, and derivatives, often working for banks, brokerage firms, or asset management companies. Operating in one of the world's leading financial hubs, they facilitate transactions for institutional and retail clients, ensuring compliance with Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) regulations. The role requires strong market knowledge, analytical skills, and the ability to manage risk under pressure.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CMFAS Examination Certificates (Modules 1A, 1B, 5, 6) | Institute of Banking and Finance (IBF) | Required | Mandatory for representatives dealing in securities and futures under MAS regulations. |
| Capital Markets Services (CMS) Representative License | Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) | Required | Required to conduct regulated activities; individuals must be appointed by a licensed firm. |
| Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) Charter | CFA Institute | Optional | Highly valued for advanced investment analysis and portfolio management roles. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically begins as a trainee or junior dealer, advancing to senior dealer or broker, then to team lead or trading desk head. Many move into asset management, private banking, or risk management. Professional certifications like the CFA or CAIA enhance prospects. Networking and a track record of performance are critical. Some transition to fintech roles or start their own advisory firms.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically execute stock, bond, and commodity orders through algorithms, no manual order placement
- AI automatically generates market analysis reports and investment briefs
- AI-driven chatbots handle common customer inquiries (e.g., account inquiries, transaction confirmations)
- Rule-based system automatically identifies potential suspicious transactions and triggers alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer transaction history and risk preferences to assist in generating personalized investment portfolio recommendations
- Real-time market sentiment monitoring and event impact simulation to improve decision quality for advisors
- Automatically generate compliance checklists and regulatory reports to reduce paperwork time
- Predict upselling and cross-selling opportunities through customer behavior models

**Human moat:**

- Build deep trust relationships, understand clients' non-financial needs (e.g., family wealth inheritance, emotional needs)
- Provide qualitative judgment and real-time adjustments to investment strategies in complex market environments
- Take on personalized responsibility for one-on-one consulting and crisis communication with high-net-worth clients
- Ethical judgment and interpretation concerning regulatory grey areas and new financial products

**Skills to build:**

- Financial data analysis tools (e.g., Python, R, SQL).
- Understanding of AI/machine learning applications in portfolio optimization
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and AI-enhanced customer interaction platforms
- Regulatory technology (RegTech) and compliance automation tools
- Understanding of complex financial derivative pricing models
- Soft skills: negotiation, empathy, crisis communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Fintech
- ESG Investing
- Wealth Management
- Digital Assets

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this role in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and firm, but generally competitive with other finance roles. Entry-level dealers may earn a base salary plus bonuses, while senior professionals can command significant packages.

**Q: Can foreigners work as securities dealers in Singapore?**
A: Yes, through an employer-sponsored Employment Pass, subject to meeting COMPASS criteria and obtaining the necessary MAS licensing as a representative.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience, dealers can advance to senior dealer, trading desk head, or move into asset management, private banking, or risk management. Professional certifications and strong performance are key.
