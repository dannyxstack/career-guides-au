# Vocational Education Teacher — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2320 (ISCO08)**

Vocational education teachers in Singapore deliver training and instruction in technical, trade, and vocational subjects at institutions such as the Institute of Technical Education (ITE), polytechnics, and private training centres. They combine practical skills with theoretical knowledge to prepare students for specific occupations or career advancement. With the government's emphasis on SkillsFuture and lifelong learning, vocational teachers play a key role in upskilling the workforce and supporting Singapore's transition to a knowledge-based economy. They design curricula, assess student progress, and collaborate with industry partners to ensure training relevance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a senior teacher or curriculum specialist role, moving to department head or academic manager. With experience and further training, some advance to academic director or dean positions. Others transition into industry training or consultancy. The trend towards lifelong learning also opens opportunities in adult education and Continuing Education and Training (CET). Specialising in high-demand technical areas or obtaining advanced teaching credentials can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generates standardized lesson plans and exam questions.
- Automatically grading multiple-choice questions and simple assignments

- Create adaptive simulation environments for practical training

- Provide after-school tutoring through chatbots

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a vocational education teacher in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level salaries for vocational teachers in polytechnics or ITE typically range from S$3,500 to S$5,000 per month, while experienced teachers and those in senior roles can earn S$7,000 to S$10,000 or more. Factors include qualifications, experience, and the type of institution.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to get a work visa as a vocational teacher in Singapore?**
A: Yes, all work passes require a valid job offer from an employer who will sponsor your Employment Pass or S Pass. The employer must demonstrate that local candidates are not available or that you have specialised skills.

**Q: Can I teach at ITE or polytechnics with only industry experience?**
A: While industry experience is valued, formal teaching qualifications like the PGDE (Vocational) are typically required. Some exceptions may apply, but most positions require at least a diploma or degree in the relevant field and teaching certification.
