# Deep-sea Fishery Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 6223 (ISCO08)**

Deep-sea fishery workers in Singapore operate fishing vessels to catch fish, crustaceans, and molluscs in offshore waters. The industry is small due to limited territorial waters and heavy reliance on imports. Most workers are foreign nationals on Work Permits, employed by local fishing companies. The role demands physical stamina, sea survival skills, and knowledge of navigation and fishing gear. Singapore's aquaculture sector is growing but wild-capture fishing faces challenges from overfishing and marine pollution.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; workers can advance to senior fisherman, boat captain, or supervisor after years of experience. Some move to aquaculture farms or maritime roles. Formal certification (e.g., STCW basic safety training) is increasingly valued. The flat hierarchy and reliance on foreign workers mean few local career paths. Many workers stay in the same role for extended periods. Diversification into seafood processing or marine tourism is possible but uncommon.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Fishing vessel navigation and route planning (AIS + autopilot)
- Fish catch classification and initial weighing (computer vision)
- Fish school detection and net deployment position optimization (AI sonar analysis)
- Predictive maintenance for fishing vessel equipment (IoT + machine learning)
- Catch tracking and compliance report generation (automated data entry)

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

- Judging fish behavior based on experience (limited AI data)
- Communication and coordination with fleet and shore teams
- Flexible operations to adapt to seasonal and regulatory changes

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a deep-sea fishery worker in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and vessel size. Fresh workers earn around SGD 1,200–1,800 per month, while experienced crew or supervisors can earn SGD 2,000–3,000. Employers often provide accommodation and meals during trips, so take-home pay may be higher. Wages are subject to the foreign worker levy, but the worker does not pay the levy.

**Q: Can I get a work visa to Singapore as a deep-sea fishery worker?**
A: Yes, foreign deep-sea fishery workers are eligible for a Work Permit (Marine Fisheries). The employer must sponsor the permit and obtain a quota. Eligibility depends on nationality (typically from approved source countries like Malaysia, China, India, etc.). No minimum salary, but a job offer is required. The permit is tied to the employer and vessel.

**Q: What are the main duties of a deep-sea fishery worker in Singapore?**
A: Duties include setting and hauling nets, sorting and storing catch, maintaining fishing gear and vessel cleanliness, navigating using GPS and charts, and ensuring safety at sea. Workers may also assist in minor engine repairs and fish processing on board.
