# Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operator — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 8154 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators work in the textile manufacturing sector, primarily in factories processing fabrics for apparel, industrial textiles, and niche products. The role involves setting up, operating, and monitoring machines that bleach, dye, or clean fabrics, ensuring colour consistency and quality. Operators also perform routine maintenance and troubleshoot minor issues. With Singapore's shift towards advanced manufacturing and automation, the role now requires basic digital literacy to interface with computer-controlled machinery. The workforce is predominantly made up of older local workers and foreign work permit holders, as the industry faces competition from regional manufacturing hubs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a bleaching machine operator in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, company, and shift patterns. Entry-level operators typically earn between SGD 1,500 and SGD 2,200 per month, while experienced operators can earn up to SGD 3,000. Overtime and shift allowances can increase earnings.

**Q: Can foreigners work as a bleaching machine operator in Singapore?**
A: Yes, foreign workers can work as machine operators under the Work Permit or S Pass schemes, provided the employer has quota and pays the levy. The role is considered semi-skilled, making the Work Permit the most common route. Workers must be at least 18 years old and meet source country requirements.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for a machine operator?**
A: Advancement includes senior operator, shift supervisor, or quality control inspector. With additional training, operators can move into production planning or maintenance. The SkillsFuture programme offers courses in textile technology to support career progression.
