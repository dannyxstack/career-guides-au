# Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7514 (ISCO08)**

Fruit, vegetable and related preservers in Singapore are skilled workers who process, preserve, and package fruits, vegetables, and related products such as jams, pickles, and canned goods. This niche occupation falls within Singapore's small but growing food manufacturing sector, which focuses on artisanal and value-added products to reduce import dependence. Workers are typically employed by small-to-medium enterprises, specialty food producers, and agri-tech startups. The role requires manual dexterity, knowledge of food safety standards, and adherence to hygiene regulations. With Singapore's '30 by 30' food security goal, local production is increasing, but the occupation remains limited in scale.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fruit, vegetable and related preserver in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience and employer, but entry-level workers on a Work Permit may earn around SGD 1,200-1,800 per month, while experienced preservers with certifications can earn up to SGD 2,500 per month. Supervisory roles may command higher pay. These figures are pre-tax.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a fruit preserver in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but work authorisation is employer-sponsored. Most workers will need a Work Permit (semi-skilled) or, if they have a diploma and higher salary, an S Pass. The employer must prove that the role cannot be filled locally. There is no direct path to permanent residency through this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for this role in Singapore?**
A: Progression is limited but possible. Workers can advance to senior preserver, supervisor, or quality control roles. Further education in food science (e.g., WSQ Diploma) can lead to food technologist or production manager positions. However, the small scale of the sector means opportunities are few.
