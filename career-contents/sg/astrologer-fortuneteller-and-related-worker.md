# Astrologer, Fortune-Teller and Related Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 5161 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, astrologers, fortune-tellers, and related workers provide spiritual guidance, horoscope readings, tarot card readings, and other divination services. This occupation is niche, often operated as a self-employed or small business venture. It falls under personal services, with clients ranging from individuals seeking career or relationship advice to corporate groups for team-building events. The practice is not regulated by any professional body, and practitioners typically gain clients through word-of-mouth or online platforms. While not a mainstream career, it persists in Singapore's multicultural society alongside traditional Chinese zodiac and feng shui consultations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression is informal and reputation-based. Beginners often start with basic courses or self-study, then build a client base through social media or referrals. Experienced practitioners may offer premium services like personalised birth chart analyses or corporate workshops. There is no formal hierarchy, but some achieve celebrity status locally. Many astrologers diversify into complementary fields such as tarot, numerology, or feng shui to attract a broader clientele. Formal qualifications are not required, but short certification courses exist from private institutions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate basic horoscope reports and daily fortune analysis
- Calculate planetary positions and houses based on birth information
- Answering common astrology questions (such as zodiac compatibility)
- Writing simple tarot card interpretation copy
- Automatically generate social media posts for astrology/fortune-telling content

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze client historical data, providing more personalized life cycle predictions
- Enhancing online communication efficiency with customers through natural language processing
- Use AI tools to generate visual astrolabes and tarot card spreads to enhance consultation experience
- Identify hidden customer needs using machine learning to assist in-depth interpretation
- Automatically collects and organizes astronomical data, saving research time.

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face emotional resonance and empathetic interaction
- Personalized interpretation combining cultural background and client experience
- building trust and maintaining long-term relationships with high-value clients
- Ability to handle complex, ambiguous, or non-standard advisory issues
- Design of creative rituals or suggestions (e.g., auspicious timing, Feng Shui layout)

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for an astrologer in Singapore?**
A: Income varies widely; part-time practitioners may earn SGD 20,000-40,000 annually, while established full-time astrologers can earn SGD 60,000-100,000. No official MOM data exists.

**Q: Can a foreign astrologer get a work visa in Singapore?**
A: It is very difficult. Most astrologers are self-employed and may qualify for an EntrePass if they set up a business. Employer-sponsored visas like Employment Pass are rare unless the astrologer is hired by a spiritual centre, which is uncommon.

**Q: Are there formal career paths in this field in Singapore?**
A: No formal career paths. Success depends on building a personal brand. Many astrologers also offer feng shui, tarot, or life coaching services to expand their practice.
