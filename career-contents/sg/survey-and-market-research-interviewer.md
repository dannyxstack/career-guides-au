# Survey and Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and market research interviewers collect data from respondents through face-to-face, telephone, or online methods for commercial, social, or government studies. In Singapore, they are employed by market research agencies, polling firms, government departments, and call centres. This role is critical for customer insights, public opinion monitoring, and policy evaluation. The work may involve structured questionnaires or semi-structured interviews, often requiring language skills in English and Mandarin to cover diverse demographic groups. While entry-level, it demands patience, neutrality, and good communication. Many professionals in this field later transition to research analyst roles or market strategy positions.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 240. Singapore's market research industry is stable with moderate demand for interviewers, particularly for specialised face-to-face studies (e.g., healthcare, financial services) and multilingual surveys. Automation and online panels reduce need for routine telephone interviewing, but quality interviewers for complex projects remain valued. Growth opportunities exist in digital survey methods and real-time analytics. The sector is not on the shortage list, and competition is moderate. Job security is average, with turnover common due to project-based work.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior interviewer, advancing to senior interviewer, team leader, or quality control roles. With experience and additional training, one can move into research assistant, data analyst, or market research executive positions. Further education (e.g., MBA or market research certifications) opens paths to consultancy or senior management in insights agencies. In Singapore, cross-sector moves into customer experience, user research, or public policy research are common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- digital surveys
- customer insights
- data collection
- survey technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for survey interviewers in Singapore?**
A: Salaries are competitive and vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions typically start around the median wage for administrative and support roles, with experienced interviewers earning higher. Exact figures depend on company and project scope.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as survey interviewers in Singapore?**
A: Yes, through employer-sponsored work passes. Most interviewers qualify for an S Pass (mid-skilled) if the salary meets thresholds, or a Work Permit if from eligible source countries. Employment Pass is possible for senior roles but rare. Quota and levy apply for S Pass and Work Permit.
