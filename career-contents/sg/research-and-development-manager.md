# Research and Development Manager — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1223 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, Research and Development Managers lead innovation teams to develop new products, processes, or technologies across sectors like biotech, electronics, and AI. They oversee R&D projects, manage budgets, and align research with business goals. With Singapore's strong government push for R&D (e.g., Research, Innovation and Enterprise 2025), demand for these managers is high, particularly in high-tech manufacturing and digital economy clusters. The role typically requires a master's or PhD in a relevant field and several years of research experience.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for R&D managers in Singapore typically starts from senior researcher or team lead roles. With experience, they advance to director of R&D, chief technology officer (CTO), or even executive positions. Many pursue executive education or PMP certification to enhance management skills. Inter-sector mobility is common, moving between MNCs, startups, and public research institutes. Networking through industry conferences and SGInnovate programs is key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated analysis of literature reviews and patent searches
- Recording experimental data and performing preliminary statistical analysis
- Automatic generation of project documents (e.g., progress reports, technical memos)
- Regular test case design and execution

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Feasibility analysis and solution design for R&D projects accelerated by AI
- Optimizes experimental parameters and resource allocation using AI prediction models
- AI-assisted team collaboration and knowledge management, improving cross-department communication efficiency
- Real-Time AI Market Intelligence Analysis Drives Innovation Direction Decisions

**Human moat:**

- Complex technical decision-making and uncertainty risk management
- Deep customer needs insight and productization capability
- Team leadership and talent pipeline development

**Skills to build:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Singapore's main professional work pass is the Employment Pass, granted to qualifying professionals with a job offer above a salary threshold; long-term holders may apply for Permanent Residence. Criteria are set by the Ministry of Manpower and updated regularly.

**Official immigration source:** [Ministry of Manpower (MOM)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Experienced researchers with a strong publication record and leadership skills.
- Professionals comfortable managing cross-functional teams and budgets in fast-paced innovation environments.
- Strategic thinkers who can bridge technical R&D with commercial objectives.

**Who is not suited**
- Individuals who prefer purely individual research without managerial responsibilities.
- Candidates lacking experience in Singapore's regulated industries (e.g., biomedical, electronics) may struggle.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for R&D Managers in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level R&D managers (with 5-8 years experience) earn around SGD 120,000-160,000 per year. Mid-career managers with 10+ years can earn SGD 160,000-250,000. Senior directors may exceed SGD 300,000, plus bonuses.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as an R&D Manager in Singapore?**
A: Yes, if you meet the minimum salary threshold (SGD 5,000/month) and pass COMPASS points. A master's or PhD in a relevant field increases points. Employer sponsorship is required.

**Q: Is R&D Manager a good path to permanent residence?**
A: While no direct PR pathway, holding an EP and working in a shortage role like R&D Manager improves PR application chances under the Professionals/Technical Personnel scheme. Stay at least 6 months, demonstrate skills and salary.
