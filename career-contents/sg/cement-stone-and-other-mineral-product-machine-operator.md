# Cement, Stone and Other Mineral Products Machine Operator — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 8114 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, Cement, Stone and Other Mineral Products Machine Operators are essential in the construction and manufacturing sectors, operating machinery to produce concrete, cement, stone, and other mineral products. They work in precast concrete plants, quarries, and construction material factories, contributing to infrastructure projects such as HDB flats, MRT lines, and commercial buildings. The role requires technical skills in machine operation, basic maintenance, and adherence to safety protocols. With Singapore's ongoing construction boom and focus on quality building materials, these operators play a vital role in the supply chain.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Cement and Stone Products Machine Operator in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, qualifications, and employer. Generally, entry-level operators can expect around SGD 1,800 to SGD 2,500 per month, while experienced operators may earn between SGD 2,500 and SGD 3,500 per month. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can a foreign worker get an S Pass for this occupation?**
A: Yes, if the worker meets the minimum salary requirement (currently SGD 3,000/month) and has a diploma or equivalent qualification. However, most machine operators are on Work Permits due to the semi-skilled nature. S Pass eligibility depends on the employer's quota and the Ministry of Manpower's assessment.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for machine operators?**
A: With experience and additional training, operators can progress to senior operator, team leader, or supervisor. They can also specialize in operating advanced machinery, move into quality control or production planning, or pursue further education to become plant managers or technical instructors.
