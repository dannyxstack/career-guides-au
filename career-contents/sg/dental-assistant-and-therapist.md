# Dental Assistant and Therapist — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3251 (ISCO08)**

Dental assistants and therapists support dentists in clinical procedures, perform dental hygiene treatments, and educate patients on oral care in Singapore. They work in private clinics, public hospitals, and polyclinics. The role requires strong manual dexterity, empathy, and knowledge of infection control. With an aging population and emphasis on preventive care, demand is steady. Registration with the Singapore Dental Council is mandatory for therapists; assistants typically require a relevant diploma.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression includes advancement to senior dental assistant, practice manager, or specialisation in areas like orthodontics or periodontics. Dental therapists may progress to senior therapist or clinical educator. Some pursue further studies in dental surgery or public health. Continuing professional development is mandatory for registration renewal. The field offers stable employment but limited upward mobility without additional qualifications.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a dental assistant in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and qualification. Entry-level assistants earn around SGD 25,000 annually, while experienced ones can earn up to SGD 45,000. Therapists with degrees may earn higher.

**Q: Can foreign dental assistants work in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but they need employer sponsorship. Most qualify for an S Pass if they have a diploma and meet the salary threshold. Work Permits are also possible for certain nationalities. Local registration is required for therapists.

**Q: How can I progress from dental assistant to therapist?**
A: You need to complete a Diploma in Dental Hygiene/Therapy, which is offered locally at Nanyang Polytechnic. After graduation, you must register with the Singapore Dental Council.
