# Mining Supervisor — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3121 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, mining supervisors oversee quarrying and sand mining operations, primarily on offshore islands or in granite quarries. The role involves managing extraction processes, ensuring compliance with safety regulations, and coordinating teams. Due to the limited domestic mining industry, most supervisors work for companies involved in regional mining projects or supply chain management. The occupation requires strong leadership and knowledge of mining engineering principles.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 5,893. Employment outlook for mining supervisors in Singapore is stable but niche, with limited growth due to the country's small land area and lack of mineral resources. Demand is driven by quarrying for construction aggregates and offshore sand mining. Automation and sustainability trends may reduce manual roles, while expertise in environmental compliance and safety will remain valued. Opportunities are mainly with multinational firms managing overseas mining assets from Singapore.

**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Experienced supervisors can advance to mine manager or operations director. Additional certifications in workplace safety and environmental management enhance prospects. The path is narrow in Singapore, but regional roles in Southeast Asia offer broader opportunities.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence the salary of a Mining Supervisor in Singapore?**
A: Salary varies based on experience, qualifications, and the employer's scale. Supervisors with safety certifications and regional project exposure typically command higher pay. Bonuses may be tied to project performance.

**Q: Can a foreign Mining Supervisor obtain an Employment Pass in Singapore?**
A: Yes, if they meet the minimum salary threshold (SGD 5,000/month) and have recognised qualifications. The COMPASS framework assesses individual and firm attributes. Employers must sponsor the pass.
