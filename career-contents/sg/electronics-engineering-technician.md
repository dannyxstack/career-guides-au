# Electronics Engineering Technician — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3114 (ISCO08)**

Electronics engineering technicians in Singapore assist engineers in developing, testing, and manufacturing electronic equipment, such as semiconductors, communications devices, and industrial control systems. They work across the electronics industry, including in R&D labs, fabrication plants, and maintenance departments. In Singapore's advanced manufacturing hub, these technicians are vital for quality control, prototyping, and troubleshooting. With the push towards Smart Nation and Industry 4.0, their role is evolving to include automation and IoT integration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression for electronics engineering technicians in Singapore typically starts from a diploma or Higher NITEC. With experience and further studies (e.g., part-time degree), they can advance to senior technician, technical specialist, or engineering roles. Many technicians move into test engineering, product support, or field service. Certifications in areas like PCB design, embedded systems, or Lean Six Sigma enhance prospects. Some may transition to management or technical sales. The sector values continuous learning, and employers often sponsor certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard circuit board fault diagnosis report writing
- Component selection and parameter matching
- Organization and translation of production documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electronics engineering technician in Singapore?**
A: The median annual salary for electronics engineering technicians in Singapore is around SGD 36,000 to SGD 48,000, depending on experience and qualifications. Entry-level positions with a diploma can start at about SGD 30,000, while senior technicians with a degree may earn over SGD 60,000.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an electronics engineering technician in Singapore?**
A: Yes, foreigners can work as technicians under the S Pass (for diploma holders) or Employment Pass (for degree holders). Employers must sponsor the pass and meet quota/levy requirements for S Pass. Work Permit is possible for lower-skilled roles but less common. There is no direct PR-by-migration route.

**Q: What education is required to become an electronics engineering technician in Singapore?**
A: The minimum requirement is a Diploma in Electronics Engineering from a local polytechnic or equivalent. Higher NITEC from ITE is also accepted. Some employers prefer a bachelor's degree for senior roles. Relevant work experience and certifications can enhance prospects.
