# Structural Metal Erector — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors assemble, install, and repair metal frameworks for buildings, bridges, and other structures. In Singapore, they are essential to the construction, marine, and offshore sectors, working with steel beams, columns, and trusses. The role involves interpreting blueprints, welding, bolting, and ensuring structural integrity. Workers often operate from heights and must adhere to strict safety protocols. The occupation is predominantly held by male workers, and it offers a clear career progression from apprentice to supervisor or specialist welder. It is a hands-on, physically demanding job requiring technical skills and teamwork.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a trainee or apprentice, advancing to skilled erector, then to supervisor or foreman. Specializations include welding (certified welder), rigging, or structural fitting. With experience, one can become a project coordinator or site manager. The Building and Construction Authority (BCA) offers the Construction Registration of Tradesmen (CORE) program, which provides a structured career pathway. Continuing education through WSQ Advanced Certificate in Metal Fabrication (or similar) can lead to higher pay and supervisory roles. Some erectors move into inspection or quality control.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Structural Metal Erector in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and certification. Entry-level erectors earn around SGD 1,800–2,500 per month, while experienced and certified workers can earn SGD 3,000–4,500 per month. Supervisors may earn SGD 5,000 or more.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass (EP) as a Structural Metal Erector?**
A: No, Employment Pass is for professionals, managers, executives, and specialists (PMET). Structural Metal Erectors are considered semi-skilled or mid-skilled, so they typically require a Work Permit or S Pass. Work Permit is the most common, subject to quota and levy.

**Q: Is it easy to become a supervisor or move into project management?**
A: Yes, with experience and additional certifications (e.g., WSQ Advanced Certificate, Supervisor Safety Course), one can progress to supervisor, foreman, or even assistant project manager. Many local erectors take advantage of BCA's training schemes.
