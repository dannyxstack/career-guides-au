# Secondary Education Teacher — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2330 (ISCO08)**

Secondary Education Teachers in Singapore deliver the national curriculum to students aged 13-16, preparing them for the GCE O-Level or N-Level examinations. They are employed by the Ministry of Education (MOE) and teach in government or government-aided schools. The role involves classroom instruction, co-curricular activities (CCA), and student pastoral care. Competition for teaching posts is high, with candidates requiring strong academic records and a passion for education. The National Institute of Education (NIE) provides mandatory teacher training. Teachers enjoy stable employment, structured career pathways, and opportunities for professional development.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 45,176. The employment outlook for Secondary Education Teachers in Singapore remains stable, with steady demand driven by student enrollment and teacher attrition. MOE's emphasis on holistic education and digital literacy may create opportunities for teachers skilled in STEM, special education, and educational technology. However, hiring is tightly controlled, with priority given to Singaporeans and Permanent Residents. Foreign teachers are recruited only for subjects with chronic shortages, such as certain mother tongue languages or specialized STEM fields.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically follows a structured path: classroom teacher to Subject Head, Head of Department (HOD), then to school leadership roles like Vice-Principal or Principal. Alternatively, teachers can move into MOE headquarters as curriculum specialists or policy officers. Performance-based bonuses and accelerated promotion tracks are available for outstanding educators. Many teachers also pursue master's degrees or leadership diplomas through MOE-sponsored programmes.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a secondary school teacher in Singapore?**
A: Salaries start at about SGD 4,000–5,500 per month for fresh graduates and increase with experience. Annual increments and performance bonuses are standard. Exact figures depend on qualifications and the school's budget.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a secondary school teacher in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but it is highly competitive. Foreigners need an Employment Pass sponsored by an MOE school, which requires strong academic credentials and relevant experience. MOE prioritizes citizens and permanent residents, so foreign hires are limited to subjects with shortages.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for teachers?**
A: Teachers can progress to leadership roles such as Subject Head, Head of Department (HOD), Vice-Principal, or Principal. Alternatively, they can become curriculum specialists or policy officers at MOE headquarters. Many also pursue postgraduate degrees.
