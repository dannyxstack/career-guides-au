# Medical Records and Health Information Technician — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3252 (ISCO08)**

Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Singapore manage patient health records, ensure data accuracy, and handle coding for billing and research. They work primarily in hospitals, clinics, and healthcare institutions, supporting clinical decision-making and administrative processes. With the push for electronic health records (EHR) and data interoperability, these professionals play a crucial role in maintaining data integrity and compliance with healthcare regulations. The role requires a mix of administrative, technical, and analytical skills, often involving collaboration with clinicians and IT staff.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a technician role, with advancement to senior technician, team lead, or health information manager. Specialization in clinical coding, data analytics, or health informatics can lead to higher-level positions. Continuing education, such as a degree in health information management or certifications like Certified Health Data Analyst (CHDA), can enhance career prospects. Some professionals transition into healthcare IT or consulting roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

- Health Informatics and HIPAA Compliance
- Data analysis tools (SQL, Python, R)
- AI/ML fundamentals (NLP, predictive modeling)
- Medical coding systems (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI).
- Project management and change management

**Key growth areas:**

- Health Informatics
- Electronic Health Records
- Data Analytics
- Compliance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for entry-level positions start around SGD 2,500 to SGD 3,500 per month for diploma holders, while experienced professionals can earn SGD 4,000 to SGD 6,000 or more. S Pass and Employment Pass holders must meet respective minimum salary thresholds.

**Q: What work passes are available for foreign Medical Records Technicians in Singapore?**
A: Foreign technicians typically apply for an S Pass (mid-skilled) or Employment Pass (professionals). The S Pass requires a minimum salary of SGD 3,000 and a diploma, while the Employment Pass requires at least SGD 5,000 and a degree. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: Is there a shortage of Medical Records Technicians in Singapore?**
A: No, this occupation is not currently on the shortage list (SOL) for Singapore. However, demand is steady due to healthcare expansion and digitalization.
