# Garbage and Recycling Collector — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9611 (ISCO08)**

Garbage and recycling collectors in Singapore are essential workers responsible for the collection, transport, and disposal of household, commercial, and industrial waste. They operate under the National Environment Agency's (NEA) regulated waste management system, often employed by licensed waste collection companies. This occupation is predominantly held by low-skilled migrant workers on Work Permits, with a significant number from South Asia. The role requires physical stamina for lifting heavy bins and operating compactor trucks, as well as basic awareness of safety protocols and recycling segregation. With Singapore's push towards zero waste and circular economy under the Sustainable Singapore Blueprint, the role is evolving to include more recycling and composting duties, though it remains a physically demanding, entry-level position.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for garbage and recycling collectors in Singapore is limited but exists within the waste management hierarchy. Starting as a collector, workers can advance to team leader, driver (if they obtain a Class 4 or 5 driving license), or operations supervisor with experience. Some transition to roles in recycling plants, such as sorting supervisors or machinery operators. The government's SkillsFuture initiative offers subsidized training in WSQ (Workforce Skills Qualifications) for Environmental Cleaning, including modules on waste segregation, safety, and customer service. However, most collectors remain in the same role due to language barriers and limited formal education. For local Singaporeans, the job is often seen as a stepping stone, while for migrant workers, it is a long-term role with little upward mobility.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a garbage collector in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level garbage collectors typically earn between $1,000 and $1,500 per month. With experience or a driving license, salaries can reach $1,800-$2,200. These figures are based on Ministry of Manpower data for Work Permit holders in the cleaning sector.

**Q: Can a foreigner get an Employment Pass as a garbage collector in Singapore?**
A: No. Garbage collectors are classified as semi-skilled workers and are only eligible for a Work Permit. Employment Passes require a minimum salary of $5,000/month and a recognised degree, making this role ineligible for skilled migration routes.

**Q: Are there opportunities for advancement in waste collection?**
A: Yes, limited but possible. With experience and training, collectors can become team leaders, truck drivers, or recycling plant operators. SkillsFuture WSQ certifications can help local workers move into supervisory roles.
