# Ship's Deck Crew and Related Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 8350 (ISCO08)**

Ship's deck crews and related workers in Singapore perform watchkeeping, mooring, cargo handling, and maintenance duties aboard merchant vessels, tugboats, and offshore support craft. The role is vital to the port and maritime logistics sector, which underpins Singapore's status as a global hub. Most employment is on foreign-flagged vessels based in Singapore or local harbour craft, with work authorisation typically via a Work Permit. The job requires physical stamina, seamanship skills, and adherence to international safety standards (STCW).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression usually begins as an Ordinary Seaman, advancing to Able Seaman, then Bosun or deck officer path via further certifications. Singapore-based employers sponsor training through maritime schools or in-house programmes. Transition to shore-based roles (e.g., port operations, vessel traffic management) is possible after sea service. The career is structured with clear rank hierarchies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic navigation and route optimization during navigation watch.
- Sensor-based collision avoidance and alert monitoring
- Automation of cargo loading and stability calculations
- AI tools for daily log and report generation
- Predictive maintenance diagnosis for equipment performance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making system enhances berthing safety under complex port weather conditions
- Augmented reality (AR) systems used for repair guidance and system inspection
- AI-driven energy management system optimizes fuel consumption
- Automatic Identification System (AIS) data fusion provides full maritime situational awareness
- Assisted hull external inspection and rust removal using drones and remote-controlled robots

**Human moat:**

- Rapid decision-making and team coordination skills in emergency situations
- Complex manual operations, such as cable mooring and maintenance
- Unstructured judgment of sea conditions and human factors
- Communication and responsibility as a team leader
- Execution of safety culture in compliance with maritime regulations

**Skills to build:**

- Basic programming and AI tool usage (e.g., route optimization software)
- Data analysis and dashboard interpretation skills
- Skills in remotely operating drones or robots.
- Advanced maritime regulations and certification updates
- Soft skills: cross-cultural communication and crisis management
- Digital forensics and cybersecurity fundamentals

**Key growth areas:**

- Maritime logistics
- Port operations
- Harbour craft
- Safety compliance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a deck crew member in Singapore?**
A: Salary varies by rank and vessel type. Refer to the MOM Occupational Wage Survey for current figures. Entry-level positions are generally lower mid-wage.

**Q: Can a deck crew member obtain an Employment Pass?**
A: Not typically. Deck crew positions are classified as semi-skilled, so the Work Permit is the standard work pass. Higher-ranking officers may qualify for an S Pass or Employment Pass.

**Q: What are the promotion prospects for deck crew?**
A: You can progress from Ordinary Seaman to Able Seaman and then to Bosun. With additional STCW certificates, you may qualify as a Deck Officer (OOW).
