# Building Construction Labourer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9313 (ISCO08)**

Building construction labourers perform manual tasks such as digging, lifting, and carrying materials on construction sites in Singapore. They are employed by construction firms involved in residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects. The role is essential for supporting skilled tradespeople and ensuring smooth site operations. Given Singapore's reliance on foreign workers for this occupation, local uptake is low, and the workforce is predominantly composed of Work Permit holders.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for building construction labourers is limited without upskilling. Labourers may advance to specialised roles like formwork or bricklaying after gaining experience and certifications. Some move into supervisory positions or become construction site supervisors. The government supports SkillsFuture courses for upskilling, but progression often requires additional training and language proficiency. Overall, the role is entry-level with low upward mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Moving construction materials (replaced by AGVs/drones).
- Basic excavation and earthworks (replaced by unmanned excavators)
- On-site cleaning and debris sorting (replaced by cleaning robots)
- Simple bricklaying and plastering (replaced by automatic brick-laying machines/spraying machines)
- Repetitive rebar tying (replaced by tying robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to read construction drawings, improving accuracy.
- Operate semi-automated tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers, laser levels)
- Maintenance and debugging of automated equipment (e.g., drones, robots)
- Report progress and receive instructions via digital platforms
- Using BIM software for collaborative work and problem-solving

**Human moat:**

- Judgment in unstructured environments (e.g., complex terrain, unexpected situations)
- Fine hand-eye coordination (e.g., installing irregular components).
- Team on-site communication and emergency coordination
- Experience in safety supervision and accident prevention
- Mastery of traditional crafts (e.g., stonemasonry, special plastering)

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated construction equipment (e.g., drones, robots)
- Reading digital drawings and BIM basics
- Basic programming or equipment debugging skills
- Enhanced safety and quality management knowledge
- Knowledge of electrical/hydraulic system maintenance
- English or digital tools usage

**Key growth areas:**

- Precast Construction
- Green Building
- Productivity Improvement
- Mechanisation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building construction labourer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and experience, but the median monthly salary for construction labourers is around SGD 1,500 to SGD 2,000, with overtime pay. Work Permit holders may receive allowances. Exact figures depend on the company and contract.

**Q: Can a building construction labourer get an Employment Pass or S Pass in Singapore?**
A: An Employment Pass is not available for this role as it requires high-skilled professionals. The S Pass is possible for those with relevant diplomas or certifications, but very rare. Most workers hold a Work Permit for the Construction sector.

**Q: How can a building construction labourer progress in their career?**
A: By taking up trade certifications (e.g., scaffold erector, form worker) or supervisory courses, labourers can become skilled workers or team leaders. The BCA offers upgrading programmes via SkillsFuture credits.
