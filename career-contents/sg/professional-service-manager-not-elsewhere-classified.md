# Professional Services Manager — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1349 (ISCO08)**

Professional Services Managers oversee the delivery of expert advisory or technical services in fields such as management consulting, accounting, legal, architecture, or engineering. In Singapore, these managers operate in a dynamic hub for regional headquarters and professional firms, coordinating multidisciplinary teams, managing client relationships, and ensuring service quality and profitability. The role requires strong business acumen and sector-specific knowledge, often in a fast-paced, results-driven environment.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Singapore's main professional work pass is the Employment Pass, granted to qualifying professionals with a job offer above a salary threshold; long-term holders may apply for Permanent Residence. Criteria are set by the Ministry of Manpower and updated regularly.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Professional Services Manager in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by industry. For mid-level managers (5-8 years experience), annual base salary ranges from SGD 80,000 to SGD 150,000. Senior managers or directors can earn SGD 180,000 to over SGD 300,000, excluding bonuses.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a Professional Services Manager?**
A: Yes, provided you meet the COMPASS requirements, including a minimum monthly salary of SGD 5,000 (for fresh graduates) to SGD 10,500+ (for experienced managers). An EP is employer-sponsored and renewable.
