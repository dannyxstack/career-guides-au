# Policy Administration Professional — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2422 (ISCO08)**

Policy Administration Professionals in Singapore develop, review, and implement public policies across sectors such as finance, healthcare, and social welfare, working in government ministries, statutory boards, and policy think tanks. They analyze data, conduct research, and draft policy papers to address societal challenges and support national strategies like Smart Nation and sustainability.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a Policy Analyst or Executive, advancing to Senior Policy Manager and Deputy Director. Specialization in fields like healthcare, transport, or finance enhances prospects. Many professionals pursue Master of Public Policy (MPP) degrees to accelerate advancement. Lateral moves to international organizations or private sector consulting are common mid-career.

- Standard Report Generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Policy Administration Professional in Singapore?**
A: Salaries are competitive and tied to public sector pay scales, with entry-level roles generally starting above the median income. Annual increments and performance bonuses are standard. Exact figures vary by ministry and experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as Policy Administration Professionals in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but primarily in private sector policy roles (e.g., consulting firms, international think tanks). Government policy roles are largely restricted to Singapore citizens due to security clearance requirements. Foreigners may also work in statutory boards if deemed highly specialized.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for this occupation?**
A: Progression from Policy Executive to Manager, Deputy Director, and Director. Specialization in high-priority areas like digital government or climate policy accelerates advancement. Many pursue a Master of Public Policy at NUS to move into leadership roles.
