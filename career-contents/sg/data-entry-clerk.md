# Data Entry Clerk — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 4132 (ISCO08)**

Data Entry Clerks in Singapore typically perform routine administrative tasks such as inputting, updating, and maintaining data from various sources into computer systems, often in sectors like finance, logistics, and government. The role is characterised by high demand due to the city-state's status as a global business hub, but it is increasingly automated, leading to slower employment growth. Many positions require only secondary education, with on-the-job training provided.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving into more specialised administrative roles such as data analyst, executive assistant, or office manager. Some may transition into related fields like records management or customer service supervision. Upskilling through courses in data analytics, Microsoft Office advanced skills, or business administration is common. The pathway is linear within office administration, with limited vertical mobility without further education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually enter data from paper or electronic documents into databases or spreadsheets
- Check format and basic accuracy of input data
- Repetitive batch data entry, such as invoices and forms
- Basic data cleaning and deduplication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI tools to automatically extract document data, with humans handling exceptions.
- Use automated scripts to verify data consistency and improve efficiency
- Analyze data trends and generate reports through AI-assisted analysis

**Human moat:**

- Complex data judgment and anomaly handling capability
- Client communication and business understanding (e.g., explaining data deviations)
- Cross-system data integration and process optimization
- Maintain data privacy and security compliance

**Skills to build:**

- Learn Python or VBA for automated data processing
- Proficiency in advanced Excel functions (e.g., Power Query, macros)
- Familiar with OCR and RPA tools (e.g., UiPath)
- Data quality management and database basics (SQL)
- Basic data analysis and visualization (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- digital transformation
- automation
- data management
- administrative support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Data Entry Clerk in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level positions start at around SGD 2,000 per month, while experienced clerks can earn up to SGD 3,000 per month, depending on industry and qualifications.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a Data Entry Clerk in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but options are limited. Work Permits are available for semi-skilled workers from approved source countries, while S Pass is possible for those earning at least SGD 3,150 monthly. Employment Pass is unlikely as the role is not considered professional.

**Q: How can I advance from a Data Entry Clerk role?**
A: Pursue courses in data analytics, business administration, or advanced Excel. Experience may lead to roles like administrative executive, data analyst, or office manager.
