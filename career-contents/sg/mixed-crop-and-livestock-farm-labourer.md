# Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 9213 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop and livestock farm labourers perform manual tasks such as planting, harvesting, feeding animals, cleaning pens, and maintaining farm equipment in Singapore’s small but technologically evolving agricultural sector. With limited land, local farms focus on high-productivity methods like vertical farming and hydroponics. Most workers are foreign Work Permit holders from Malaysia, China, or Bangladesh, employed on short-term contracts. Singapore's '30 by 30' goal (produce 30% of nutritional needs locally by 2030) is driving modest demand, but the occupation remains low-skilled and physically demanding.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is rare; workers typically remain labourers or move to supervisory roles after years of experience. Some transition to higher-skilled agritech roles (e.g., hydroponics technician) with additional training, but this requires education and English proficiency. Singaporeans rarely enter this occupation; it is predominantly a migrant worker path with little upward mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using automated tractors for plowing, sowing, and harvesting
- Monitor crop growth and pests/diseases using sensors and AI systems
- Caring for livestock using automatic feeders and milking robots
- Use drones to inspect farms and produce yield maps
- Optimize irrigation and fertilization plans based on historical data and weather forecasts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operating and maintaining smart agricultural equipment
- Using data analysis to improve crop yields and herd health
- Develop comprehensive agricultural strategies to balance ecology and economy
- Collaborate with AI for precision agriculture management
- Train new employees in using digital tools

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of sudden mechanical faults
- Unstructured work in complex terrain or extreme weather conditions.
- Animal emergency medical care and behavioural intervention
- Compliance with organic certification and animal welfare standards.

**Skills to build:**

- Agricultural drone operation and data analysis certification
- Use of precision agriculture software (e.g., FarmOS, Climate FieldView)
- Basic electrical and mechanical maintenance skills
- Digital literacy: Excel/data recording apps
- Basics of crop and livestock health management
- Training for safe operation of AGV/robot equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban farming
- Agritech
- Vertical farming
- Aquaponics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Singapore?**
A: Monthly salaries range from S$1,200 to S$1,800 for Work Permit holders, depending on experience and employer. Overtime pay may apply.

**Q: Can I get a Work Permit as a farm labourer if I am from a non-traditional source country?**
A: Work Permits are only available for approved source countries (e.g., Malaysia, China, India, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Thailand). You must be sponsored by a Singapore employer who meets quota and levy requirements.

**Q: Are there opportunities to move from labourer to supervisor or technician?**
A: Yes, with experience and additional training (e.g., WSQ certificates), a few workers advance to roles like farm supervisor or hydroponics technician. However, such progression is limited and often requires English proficiency.
