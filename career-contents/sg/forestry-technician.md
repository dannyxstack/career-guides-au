# Forestry Technician — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3143 (ISCO08)**

Forestry technicians in Singapore support the management and conservation of urban greenery, nature reserves, and reforestation projects, typically employed by the National Parks Board, private landscaping firms, or environmental consultancies. Their work involves tree planting, pest control, soil assessment, and data collection to maintain forest ecosystems within a dense urban landscape.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a forestry technician in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level technicians may earn between $2,000 and $3,500 per month, while senior technicians or specialists can earn $4,000 to $6,000. Government roles often include benefits and stable increments.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a forestry technician in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but work passes are employer-sponsored. Mid-skilled roles qualify for the S Pass (minimum salary $3,150/month), while lower-skilled roles fall under the Work Permit (subject to quotas). PR is possible after working on an S Pass, but the field is niche and local preference is strong.

**Q: Who are the main employers of forestry technicians in Singapore?**
A: Key employers include the National Parks Board (NParks), private landscaping companies (e.g., Banyan Tree Services, Nature Landscapes), and environmental NGOs. Some roles exist in research institutions or eco-tourism sites.
