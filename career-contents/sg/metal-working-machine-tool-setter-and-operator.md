# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

Metal working machine tool setters and operators set up and operate a variety of machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and grinders to produce precision metal parts. In Singapore, they are primarily employed in the precision engineering, aerospace, and marine sectors. The role requires technical skills in interpreting blueprints, selecting cutting tools, and performing machine adjustments. Most workers enter through ITE’s Nitec or Higher Nitec programs, and on-the-job training is common. While automation is increasing, skilled operators remain essential for customised and low-volume production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a machine operator, advancing to setter, then to senior setter or team leader. With further training, operators can become CNC programmers or production supervisors. Many upskill through part-time diplomas in mechanical engineering or precision engineering at polytechnics. Those who gain experience in high-precision industries like aerospace or medical devices can achieve higher wages and more stable employment. Certification from institutions like the Singapore Welding Society or WSQ in precision engineering can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision engineering
- Aerospace manufacturing
- Marine and offshore
- CNC programming

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is a typical salary for this role in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around SGD 1,800–2,200 per month, while experienced setters can earn SGD 2,500–3,500. Those with CNC programming skills may earn up to SGD 4,000.

**Q: Can foreigners work as metal working machine tool setters in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but typically under Work Permit or S Pass. The employer must sponsor the visa and meet quota and levy requirements. S Pass requires a diploma and minimum salary of SGD 3,000.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience and upskilling, you can become a CNC programmer, production supervisor, or even a technical specialist in high-precision machining.
