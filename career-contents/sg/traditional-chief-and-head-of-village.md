# Traditional Chief and Head of Village — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1113 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, the role of traditional chiefs and heads of villages does not exist as a formal occupation due to the country's urbanised and modern governance structure. However, related community leadership positions exist within grassroots organisations, such as the People's Association, where volunteers or appointed leaders manage community centres and mediate local issues. These roles are largely honorary and not salaried, reflecting the absence of a traditional village system in Singapore's highly developed city-state context.
