# Commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 0110 (ISCO08)**

Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Singapore serve as leaders in the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), responsible for commanding units, strategic planning, and ensuring operational readiness. This role is primarily filled by Singapore citizens through the Officer Cadet School or university scholarship programs, with limited opportunities for foreign nationals. The position demands strong leadership, discipline, and a commitment to national defense.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary by rank and experience, but are not publicly disclosed due to confidentiality. Entry-level officers receive a competitive monthly salary with allowances, housing benefits, and bonuses. For precise figures, refer to MINDEF career pages or official compensation packages.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a Commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Singapore?**
A: Extremely difficult; the role is restricted to Singapore citizens due to national security. Foreign nationals may occasionally serve under specific exchange programmes or as military experts on short-term contracts, but these are rare and not a migration route.

**Q: What is the career progression for a SAF officer?**
A: Officers typically start as platoon commanders, then progress to company, battalion, and brigade levels. Many pursue advanced education and staff training. Those with potential may attend the Singapore Command and Staff College or overseas military academies.
