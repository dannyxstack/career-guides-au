# Bricklayer and Related Worker — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7112 (ISCO08)**

Bricklayers and related workers in Singapore are skilled tradespeople who construct and repair walls, partitions, arches, and other structures using bricks, blocks, stones, and similar materials. They are employed primarily in the construction industry, which relies heavily on foreign workers. The role involves interpreting blueprints, mixing mortar, laying materials to line and level, and ensuring structural integrity. With Singapore's ongoing public housing and infrastructure projects, demand for these workers remains steady, though subject to economic cycles and government policies on foreign manpower.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for bricklayers in Singapore typically starts as a worker, advancing to supervisor or foreman with experience and certifications. Workers can specialize in heritage restoration or stone masonry, which commands higher pay. The Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ) system offers pathways to Higher Nitec or diploma in built environment, but local uptake is low. Foreign workers often remain at same skill level, while local workers may transition to project management or safety roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive wall construction (e.g., straight walls, standard brick walls) is taken over by bricklaying robots or automatic laying equipment.
- Material handling and mortar mixing via automated conveyor systems
- Design and layout of simple masonry structures are automatically generated by BIM software
- On-site measurement and positioning using laser scanners and AI correction, reducing manual work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI visual inspection system assists in checking masonry verticality and mortar joint uniformity, improving quality inspection efficiency
- BIM modeling tools help bricklayers precisely lay bricks in complex curved structures
- Augmented reality (AR) glasses overlay construction drawings, reducing reading errors.
- AI brick layout algorithms optimize material usage and reduce waste

**Human moat:**

- Complex irregular masonry (curved, hollow, historical building restoration) cannot be standardized
- Flexible operation in unstructured environments such as bad weather and confined spaces
- Non-standard requirements from owners or architects for aesthetics and texture, relying on craftsman experience
- On-site safety management and emergency judgment, e.g., support, temporary fixing risks

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated bricklaying equipment and collaborating with robots
- Proficiency in BIM modeling and CAD drawing interpretation.
- Learn drone surveying and laser scanning data analysis
- Proficient in green building and energy-efficient masonry material construction
- Improve project management and multi-trade coordination skills
- Familiarity with building regulations and accessible masonry standards

**Key growth areas:**

- sustainable construction
- prefabrication
- heritage restoration
- renovation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a bricklayer in Singapore?**
A: Salary varies with experience and project. Entry-level workers earn around $1,200–$1,800 per month, while experienced specialists can earn up to $3,000+. Work Permit holders often receive basic wages plus overtime.

**Q: Can a foreign bricklayer get an Employment Pass?**
A: Unlikely, as the role is classified as semi-skilled. Most bricklayers enter on a Work Permit. S Pass may be possible for those with a diploma and higher salary, but rare.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for bricklayers in Singapore?**
A: Workers can progress to supervisor, safety officer, or even start their own subcontracting business. Certifications like WSQ Higher Nitec can open doors to higher-level roles.
