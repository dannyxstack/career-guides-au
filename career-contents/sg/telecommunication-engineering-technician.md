# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, Telecommunications Engineering Technicians install, maintain, and repair telecom equipment such as fiber optic networks, 5G base stations, and broadband systems. They work for telcos, infrastructure firms, and government agencies supporting the Smart Nation initiative. The role requires technical diplomas or Nitec certifications, with on-the-job training. As Singapore expands its digital infrastructure, these technicians are critical for ensuring connectivity and network reliability.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Singapore?**
A: According to the MOM Occupational Wage Survey, median gross monthly salary for telecommunications technicians (without degree) is around SGD 2,800 to SGD 3,500, depending on experience and certifications. Entry-level may start at SGD 2,200, while senior technicians can earn up to SGD 4,500.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but most will qualify for an S Pass if they have a diploma and at least 2 years of experience, meeting the salary threshold (SGD 3,000 in 2025). Those with only Nitec may be limited to Work Permit, which is more restrictive (country-specific, levy). Employers must demonstrate efforts to hire locals.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for this role?**
A: Technicians can progress to senior technician, team lead, or network specialist. With further education (e.g., part-time degree) and certifications (e.g., CCNA, HCIA), they can become telecommunications engineers, project managers, or network architects.
