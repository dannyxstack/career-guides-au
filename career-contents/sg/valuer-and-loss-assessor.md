# Valuer and Loss Assessor — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 3315 (ISCO08)**

Valuers and Loss Assessors in Singapore determine the value of property, assets, or business interests for purposes such as sale, insurance, taxation, and litigation. They assess damage claims and estimate repair costs. The role is critical in real estate transactions, insurance, and financial reporting, requiring strong analytical skills and knowledge of market trends. Professionals often work for valuation firms, insurance companies, government agencies, or as independent consultants. The Singapore market values accuracy and compliance with professional standards set by the Singapore Institute of Surveyors and Valuers (SISV).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior valuer or trainee loss assessor, advancing to associate or senior valuer after gaining experience and professional certifications. Senior roles involve managing complex assignments, mentoring juniors, and business development. Some professionals move into corporate finance, real estate investment, or property consultancy. Qualification as a Certified Valuer through SISV is highly regarded and can open doors to higher-level positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary data collation and analysis for automated property valuation reports
- Standard loss amount calculation based on historical data
- Online image assessment for minor vehicle damage
- Automatic Classification and Entry of Insurance Claim Documents
- Input parameters for conventional real estate valuation models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to identify anomalies and patterns in loss images.
- Leverage big data analytics to predict market value trends
- AI-assisted rapid generation of complex assessment report drafts
- Using natural language processing to automatically handle customer inquiries
- Improve valuation accuracy through machine learning models

**Human moat:**

- On-site inspection and physical access capability
- Independent judgment for complex or non-standard damage
- Legal and ethical compliance responsibilities
- Client trust building and communication negotiation
- Creativity in handling special cases

**Skills to build:**

- Machine learning fundamentals and model interpretation
- Geographic information systems and drone operation.
- Data visualization and analysis tools (e.g., Python, R)
- AI-assisted assessment software operation
- Enhance communication and client management skills
- Interdisciplinary knowledge (e.g., law, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Real Estate Valuation
- Insurance Claims

- Financial Reporting

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a valuer in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level valuers earn around SGD 36,000 to SGD 48,000 annually. With experience and certification, senior valuers can earn SGD 60,000 to SGD 90,000 or more.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a foreign valuer?**
A: Yes, if you have a recognized degree and job offer with salary meeting EP criteria (SGD 5,000+). COMPASS points system applies, requiring minimum points from factors like qualifications and diversity.

**Q: What are the promotion prospects for a valuer?**
A: Progression to senior valuer, manager, or director roles is possible after gaining experience and professional certifications. Some move to related fields like real estate investment.
