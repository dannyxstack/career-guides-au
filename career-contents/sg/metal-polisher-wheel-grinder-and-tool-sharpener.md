# Metal Polisher, Wheel Grinder and Tool Sharpener — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 7224 (ISCO08)**

Metal polishers, wheel grinders, and tool sharpeners in Singapore are skilled tradespeople who operate grinding and polishing machinery to finish metal surfaces, sharpen tools, and achieve precise dimensions. This role is vital in Singapore's precision engineering, aerospace, marine, and manufacturing sectors, supporting the country's high-value-added industrial base. Workers typically find employment in small-to-medium enterprises or large multinational workshops, where they ensure quality standards in production and maintenance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically moves from apprentice to skilled polisher/grinder, then to senior or lead technician, and possibly to supervisor or quality inspector. Many pursue WSQ or ITE certifications to advance. Some transition into CNC programming or tool-making. Specialisation in aerospace or medical device finishing offers higher wages. Continuous learning is key due to evolving materials and technology.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive metal surface polishing and grinding operations
- Use automated grinding machines for batch workpiece processing.
- Grinding wheel dressing and balancing based on preset parameters
- dimensional inspection and pass/fail determination of simple workpieces
- Automatic grinding of standard tool edges.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted process parameter optimization: automatically recommends grinding parameters based on workpiece material and precision requirements
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes equipment vibration and temperature data to provide early fault warnings
- Visual inspection: AI system checks surface finish in real-time, reducing manual inspection
- Collaborative robots: human-robot collaboration to move heavy workpieces, reducing labor intensity
- Digital Twin: Simulate machining processes, shorten trial cutting cycles

**Human moat:**

- Manual polishing and finishing of non-standard workpieces
- Manual adjustment and grinding of complex-shaped molds and tools
- On-site judgment and process adjustment for material defects
- Manual repair and precision restoration of aging equipment
- Communicate with clients on customized surface treatment requirements

**Skills to build:**

- CNC grinding programming and operation
- CAD/CAM software (e.g., Mastercam)
- Fundamentals of robotics and automation system integration.
- Data analysis and quality control (SPC)
- Additive manufacturing post-processing techniques
- Electrical and mechanical maintenance skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Engineering
- Aerospace MRO
- Medical Device Manufacturing
- Advanced Manufacturing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a metal polisher in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for metal polishers and wheel grinders range from SGD 1,800 to SGD 2,800 per month for entry-level positions, with experienced workers earning up to SGD 3,500 or more. Overtime and shift allowances can increase take-home pay. S Pass holders typically earn at least SGD 3,150 monthly.

**Q: Can a foreign metal polisher get a work visa for Singapore?**
A: Yes, foreign metal polishers can apply for a Work Permit (for semi-skilled workers) or an S Pass if they meet the salary threshold (SGD 3,150) and have relevant qualifications. The employer must sponsor the visa. There is no direct PR path; PR is assessed individually.

**Q: What skills are most valued for this role in Singapore?**
A: Precision finishing, knowledge of different grinding wheels and abrasives, understanding of material properties, and safety practices. Familiarity with CNC grinding and quality control is increasingly valued.
