# Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers (NCOs) in Singapore form the backbone of the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), leading and training soldiers in combat, technical, and administrative roles. They are responsible for the discipline, welfare, and operational readiness of their units. NCOs typically start as enlisted personnel and progress through ranks such as Corporal, Sergeant, and Warrant Officer, often specializing in areas like infantry, armor, artillery, or logistics. The role demands strong leadership, physical fitness, and commitment to national service. In Singapore, NCOs are exclusively Singapore citizens, as military service is reserved for nationals.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a Non-commissioned Officer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries for NCOs in the SAF are competitive within the public sector, with monthly basic pay ranging from about SGD 2,500 for a Corporal to over SGD 6,000 for a Chief Warrant Officer, including allowances. Benefits include housing, medical, and retirement pensions. Exact figures depend on rank, years of service, and specialist qualifications.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Singapore?**
A: No. Only Singapore citizens are eligible to serve as NCOs in the SAF. There are no employment pass or work permit options for this occupation, as it is a reserved position tied to national service and citizenship.
