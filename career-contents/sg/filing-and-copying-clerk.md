# Filing and Copying Clerk — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 4415 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, Filing and Copying Clerks perform routine administrative tasks such as organizing documents, operating photocopiers, and maintaining filing systems. They are employed across various sectors, including government, finance, and legal firms. The role is entry-level and typically requires secondary education. With the digital transformation of office processes, clerks are increasingly expected to manage digital records and use basic office software.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for Filing and Copying Clerks is limited without additional qualifications. With experience, a clerk may advance to senior administrative roles or specialize in records management. Pursuing a diploma in business administration or obtaining certifications in office software (e.g., Microsoft Office) can open pathways to executive assistant or office manager positions. Continuous learning is key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual document sorting and filing, replaced by digital document management systems
- Copying and organizing paper documents, replaced by digital scanning and cloud storage
- Document retrieval and borrowing registration, automatically completed by AI-driven search tools
- Document version control and updates, automated process management system takeover
- Daily office supply management, automated replenishment via smart procurement system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Improve document entry efficiency using OCR and intelligent classification tools
- Use document management platforms (e.g., SharePoint) for team collaboration and permission control
- Use data analysis tools to generate document usage reports and optimize the filing system
- Apply RPA to automate repetitive tasks, freeing up time to handle exceptions or complex documents.
- Conduct document content review and compliance checks with AI assistance.

**Human moat:**

- Professional judgment for handling unstructured or ambiguous documents
- Cross-departmental communication and coordination to resolve document disputes
- Understanding tacit knowledge and unwritten rules within an organization
- Monitor automated systems operations and correct errors
- Maintain security and privacy of sensitive or classified documents

**Skills to build:**

- Document management system (e.g., Alfresco, DocuWare) operation
- Basic programming or RPA tool (e.g., UiPath) application
- Data analysis and reporting (Excel advanced features, Power BI)
- Digital literacy and information management
- English (handling multilingual documents)
- Communication and problem-solving skills.

**Key growth areas:**

- Document Digitisation
- Records Management
- Office Automation
- Administrative Process Improvement

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Filing and Copying Clerk in Singapore?**
A: Salary levels are published in the Ministry of Manpower's Occupational Wage Survey. Generally, entry-level positions start at a basic wage, with incremental increases based on experience and certifications. The exact figures vary by sector and employer.

**Q: Can I work in Singapore as a Filing and Copying Clerk as a foreigner?**
A: Yes, but you must be sponsored by an employer. Most foreign workers in this role come under the Work Permit for semi-skilled workers, or the S Pass if you hold a diploma. Employer sponsorship is mandatory; there is no independent points-based migration route.

**Q: What are the opportunities for advancement?**
A: With experience and additional training, clerks can move to senior administrative roles or specialise in records management. Pursuing a diploma or certifications in office administration can lead to positions such as executive assistant or office manager.
