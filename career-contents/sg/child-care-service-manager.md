# Child Care Services Manager — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1341 (ISCO08)**

Child care services managers oversee the daily operations, curriculum, and staff of early childhood centres in Singapore, including preschools and infant care facilities. They ensure compliance with the Early Childhood Development Agency (ECDA) regulations, manage budgets, and liaise with parents. This role requires a deep understanding of early childhood pedagogy, leadership skills, and a commitment to providing quality care. Singapore's Early Childhood Manpower Plan aims to increase the number of child care centres and enhance professional standards, driving demand for experienced managers.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a child care services manager in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary based on qualifications, experience, and centre type. Entry-level managers with a diploma may earn around SGD 3,500–4,500 per month, while those with a degree and 5+ years of experience can earn SGD 5,000–7,000 or more. Foreigners on an Employment Pass must meet the minimum salary threshold of SGD 5,000.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a child care services manager in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging due to strict ECDA requirements and a preference for local hires. Foreign workers need an Employment Pass (EP) or S Pass, and their qualifications must be assessed by ECDA. The role is not on the shortage list, but there is demand. Many foreigners first obtain an EP after securing a job offer from a licensed centre.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for child care services managers?**
A: Managers can progress to cluster manager, operations director, or even start their own child care centre. ECDA offers leadership programmes and scholarships for further studies. Some move into early childhood curriculum development or training roles.
