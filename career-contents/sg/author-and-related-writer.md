# Author and Related Writer — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2641 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, authors and related writers create original written content for books, articles, scripts, digital media, and corporate communications. The role spans creative writing, technical writing, copywriting, and content strategy. Employment is concentrated in publishing, advertising, media, and corporate communications departments. Many writers work freelance or on project-basis. The industry is supported by Singapore's vibrant literary scene and growing digital content market. Proficiency in English is essential; bilingualism is an advantage.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for authors in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and sector. Entry-level content writers earn around SGD 30,000–40,000 annually, while senior or specialised writers can earn SGD 60,000–100,000. Freelance income is inconsistent.

**Q: Can I get an Employment Pass as a writer?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Singapore employer and meet the minimum salary (SGD 5,000/month) and COMPASS points. Your role must be professional or specialised. Freelance writers typically cannot sponsor their own work pass.
