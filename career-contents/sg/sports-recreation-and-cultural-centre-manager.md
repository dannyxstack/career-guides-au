# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 1431 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, managers of sports, recreation, and cultural centres oversee operations of facilities such as community sports halls, performing arts venues, museums, and integrated resort entertainment areas. They are responsible for programming, staff management, budgeting, and ensuring compliance with regulations. With Singapore's push to become a vibrant cultural and sporting hub under the 'City in Nature' and 'Sports Singapore' initiatives, demand for skilled managers is steady. They typically work under government agencies (e.g., SportSG, National Heritage Board) or private operators in the hospitality and leisure sector.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this role in Singapore?**
A: Annual salaries range from SGD 48,000 for junior managers to SGD 72,000 for experienced managers, with higher pay in integrated resorts or large cultural institutions.

**Q: Can foreign professionals obtain an Employment Pass for this occupation?**
A: Yes, if they meet the minimum salary threshold (SGD 5,000/month) and have a recognised degree and relevant experience. The COMPASS framework assesses points on qualifications, diversity, and support for local employment.

**Q: What are the progression opportunities?**
A: Typical path: Assistant Centre Manager → Centre Manager → Regional Operations Manager. With further experience and certifications, one can move into senior management roles in sports or cultural agencies.
