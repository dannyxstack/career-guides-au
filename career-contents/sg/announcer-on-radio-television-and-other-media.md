# Announcer on Radio, Television and Other Media — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2656 (ISCO08)**

Announcers in Singapore present music, news, sports, and other content on radio, television, and digital platforms. They work for media organizations like Mediacorp, SPH Media, and independent studios. The role requires strong verbal communication, on-air presence, and often bilingual abilities in English and Mandarin or other local languages. Announcers may also host events, produce segments, or engage with audiences through social media.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior announcer or producer, advancing to senior host, programme director, or media executive. Many announcers diversify into voice-over work, corporate communications, or content creation. Continuous skill development in digital media and audience engagement is essential. The career path can be non-linear, with opportunities to move into production or management roles within media groups.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated news reporting (e.g., sports scores, financial reports, weather reports)
- Pre-recorded standard program introductions and linking words
- Simple on-site commentary (e.g. standardised event or ceremony procedures)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Real-time interactive script generation and adjustment (AI provides suggestions based on audience feedback)
- Personalized content recommendations (AI analyzes listener preferences, customizes broadcast highlights)
- Multilingual Simultaneous Interpretation and Dubbing (AI-assisted Real-time Translation)
- Voice cloning backup (AI-generated backup voice to prevent sudden voice loss)

**Human moat:**

- Emotional deep communication and improvisation (e.g., capturing touching moments in interviews)
- Brand persona building and trust establishment (loyalty to authentic voice of the audience)
- Complex topic debate and value judgment (content requiring stance on politics, ethics, etc.)
- Cross-platform community management and fan interaction

**Skills to build:**

- AI broadcasting tool operation and prompt design
- Multi-platform content planning and data analysis
- Video/audio editing and visual storytelling
- Social Media Operations and Personal Brand Building
- Real-time audience interaction skills and improvisation

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Media
- Podcasting
- Live Streaming
- Bilingual Content

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an announcer in Singapore?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on experience, platform, and employer. Entry-level announcers may earn around SGD 30,000–45,000 annually, while experienced hosts can earn SGD 60,000–100,000 or more. Digital creators and freelance announcers have variable income.

**Q: Can foreign announcers get an Employment Pass in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The Employment Pass requires a job offer from a Singaporean employer meeting COMPASS criteria, and the role must be specialized. Most announcers are local due to language requirements, but experienced professionals with a unique voice or bilingual skills may qualify.
