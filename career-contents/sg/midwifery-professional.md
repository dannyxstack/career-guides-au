# Midwifery Professional — career analysis · Singapore

**Occupation code: 2222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery professionals in Singapore provide antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care to women and newborns, promoting natural childbirth and family-centered care. They work in hospital maternity wards, community clinics, and increasingly in integrated care teams alongside obstetricians and nurses. The profession is regulated by the Singapore Nursing Board (SNB), requiring registration and continuing education. Due to Singapore's aging population and emphasis on maternal and child health, midwives are in steady demand, particularly in public healthcare institutions. The role involves clinical skills, emotional support, and health education, with opportunities to specialise in areas such as lactation consultancy or neonatal care.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a midwifery professional in Singapore?**
A: Entry-level midwives in public hospitals earn around SGD 40,000–55,000 annually. Experienced midwives can earn SGD 60,000–80,000, while senior roles (e.g., Nurse Manager) may reach SGD 90,000–110,000. Private hospitals may offer higher pay but require more experience.

**Q: Can foreign midwives work in Singapore?**
A: Yes, but employers must sponsor an Employment Pass (EP) for midwives meeting salary and qualification criteria. SNB registration is mandatory, and foreign qualifications must be assessed. The COMPASS framework evaluates points for qualifications, salary, and diversity. S Pass is possible but less common.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for midwives in Singapore?**
A: Midwives can progress to Senior Midwife, Midwifery Educator, or specialist roles in lactation or neonatal care. Management paths include Nurse Manager or Director of Nursing. Further studies (e.g., Master's) open doors to academic or policy positions in the Ministry of Health.
