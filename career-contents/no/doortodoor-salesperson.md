# Door-to-door Salesperson — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 5243 (ISCO08)**

Door-to-door salespersons in Norway sell products or services by visiting private residences, often on a commission-only basis. This occupation is characterized by low entry barriers and high turnover, with many working part-time or as a secondary income. Norwegian regulations require explicit permission from homeowners, and the industry has faced decline due to digital marketing and stricter sales practices. Most positions are filled by local or EEA workers, as the job does not meet skilled migration criteria.

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for door-to-door salespersons in Norway?**
A: Income is predominantly commission-based, making it highly variable. Average annual earnings typically range from NOK 150,000 to 300,000, with many working part-time. Full-time positions are rare and often yield below NOK 350,000.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as door-to-door salespersons in Norway?**
A: Generally no. Non-EEA nationals require a job offer that meets the salary threshold and educational requirements for a skilled worker permit. Door-to-door sales does not qualify as skilled work, and typical earnings fall below the threshold. EEA/EFTA nationals can work freely.

**Q: Is door-to-door sales a common job in Norway?**
A: It is less common than in the past due to stricter regulations and digital alternatives. It remains a niche occupation, often used for seasonal campaigns (e.g., charity subscriptions or broadband/internet sales).
