# Police Officers — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 5412 (ISCO08)**

Police officers in Norway maintain public order, enforce laws, and investigate crimes. They work under the Norwegian Police Service (Politiet), which is organized into 12 police districts and specialized agencies. The role requires high integrity, physical fitness, and the ability to handle stressful situations. Recruits must complete a three-year Bachelor's degree in Policing at the Norwegian Police University College (PHS), which includes practical training. The profession is regulated, and most positions require Norwegian citizenship due to security clearance requirements. Police officers typically work shifts, including nights and weekends.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for police officers in Norway?**
A: Starting salaries for police officers are around 500,000–600,000 NOK per year, with experienced officers earning up to 700,000–900,000 NOK. Specialists and leaders can earn over 1,000,000 NOK. Salaries are regulated by collective agreements and vary by district and experience.

**Q: Can non-EU/EEA nationals become police officers in Norway?**
A: Generally no, because police officer positions require Norwegian citizenship due to security clearance laws. Non-EU/EEA nationals can study at the Police University College but will face significant hurdles gaining employment. EU/EEA nationals may be eligible if they obtain citizenship after meeting residency requirements.

**Q: What is the career advancement like for police officers?**
A: Officers can advance from patrol to detective, special units (e.g., cyber, narcotics), or management. Further education at PHS (master's) opens leadership roles like police inspector. Promotions are based on performance, seniority, and additional qualifications.
