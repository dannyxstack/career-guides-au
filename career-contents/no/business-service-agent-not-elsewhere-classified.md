# Business Services Agent Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3339 (ISCO08)**

Business services agents in Norway handle a wide range of tasks including debt collection, insurance brokering, real estate agency support, and other commercial activities not covered by more specific agent roles. These professionals are employed by specialized agencies, banks, insurance companies, and consulting firms, often requiring strong communication and negotiation skills. The role demands familiarity with Norwegian business practices and regulatory frameworks, and proficiency in Norwegian and English is typically essential.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts from administrative or sales support roles, advancing to senior agent or team leader positions with experience. Specialization in areas like debt recovery, insurance, or real estate can lead to higher earnings. Further education, such as a master's in finance or law, may open doors to management or consultancy roles. Continuous professional development is encouraged to keep pace with regulatory changes.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generates standard documents such as business registration forms and permit applications.
- Preliminary screening and matching of potential partners/clients (based on databases and AI recommendations)
- Routine market access regulations queries and policy update reports.
- Administrative tasks such as appointments, meeting minutes, email replies, etc.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI market analysis tools to quickly identify high-value target customers and industry trends

**Human moat:**

- Handling negotiations and dispute resolution for non-standard, high-risk commercial agreements
- Cross-cultural sensitivity and tacit knowledge of local business practices
- Creatively resolve unexpected compliance issues in corporate entry.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a business services agent in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and location. Entry-level agents earn near the median for administrative roles, while experienced specialists can earn significantly more. The occupation meets the skilled worker permit salary threshold for most positions.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as a business services agent in Norway?**
A: Yes, if they obtain a Skilled Worker residence permit with a job offer from a Norwegian employer. The applicant must have a relevant degree or trade certificate, and the salary must meet the annual threshold (currently around 500,000 NOK). Proficiency in Norwegian is usually required.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience, agents can move into senior agent roles, team leadership, or specialization (e.g., insurance, debt collection). Further education in finance or law can lead to management positions in banks or consulting firms.
