# Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3230 (ISCO08)**

In Norway, traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals include practitioners such as naprapaths, osteopaths, acupuncturists, and homeopaths. The field is diverse, with some professions (like naprapathy and osteopathy) regulated by the Norwegian Health Personnel Act, while others are unregulated. Practitioners often work in private clinics, wellness centers, or are self-employed. The sector is growing moderately driven by wellness trends, but competition is high, and income can be variable. Most educational programs are offered by private institutions, and many practitioners have backgrounds from abroad.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a traditional and complementary medicine practitioner in Norway?**
A: Income varies widely by profession, clientele, and location. Self-employed practitioners typically earn between 350,000 and 600,000 NOK annually. Those employed by clinics may have a fixed salary plus commission, averaging around 450,000 NOK. Earnings can be higher for established practitioners with a strong client base.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as an alternative therapist for Norway?**
A: It is possible but challenging. For non-EEA nationals, the Skilled Worker permit requires a job offer and an educational background recognized as equivalent to a Norwegian degree (e.g., a bachelor's in naprapathy from an accredited institution). Many alternative medicine diplomas are not recognized, so check with the Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI) and NOKUT. EU/EEA/EFTA nationals can work freely after registration.

**Q: Is alternative medicine regulated in Norway?**
A: Only naprapathy and osteopathy are regulated health professions under the Norwegian Health Personnel Act. Practitioners must have approved education and be registered. Other disciplines like acupuncture, homeopathy, and herbal medicine are unregulated, though voluntary certification exists through professional associations.
