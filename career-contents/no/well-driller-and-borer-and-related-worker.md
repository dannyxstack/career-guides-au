# Well Driller and Borer — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 8113 (ISCO08)**

Well drillers and borers operate drilling machinery to extract water, oil, gas, or geothermal energy. In Norway, this occupation is crucial for the petroleum sector, water supply, and ground-source heat pump installations. Workers typically have a vocational trade certificate and often work in teams on construction sites or offshore platforms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or trainee, advancing to driller, then senior driller or supervisor. Many move into offshore positions or specialize in directional drilling, well servicing, or drilling fluid engineering. With experience, some become consultants or start their own drilling companies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual adjustment of drilling speed and pressure is taken over by AI adaptive control systems
- Experience-based judgment of strata changes is replaced by real-time data analysis
- Record drilling logs and parameters, replaced by automated sensors and IoT.
- Repetitive maintenance checks (e.g., lubrication, bolt tightening) performed by robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes geological data to assist in optimizing drilling paths and depths
- Drone and camera AI identification of well site safety risks
- Machine learning predicts equipment failures, reducing unplanned downtime
- Augmented Reality (AR) remote guidance for complex operations

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency decision-making and troubleshooting ability
- Adaptability assessment for complex terrain and abnormal geology
- Team coordination and safety leadership responsibilities
- Fine manual skills in equipment repair

**Skills to build:**

- Geological data analysis software (e.g., Leapfrog)
- PLC and automation control system operation
- Drone operation and AI image analysis
- Equipment health monitoring and predictive maintenance
- Basic programming (Python/R) for customized reports
- Safety and compliance management (e.g., ISO 45001)

**Key growth areas:**

- Geothermal energy
- Offshore petroleum
- Water supply
- Infrastructure development

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for well drillers in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, but well drillers earn competitive wages, often above the national average. Offshore positions typically pay higher rates due to demanding conditions.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as well drillers in Norway?**
A: Yes, non-EEA nationals can apply for a Skilled Worker Residence Permit if they have a job offer from a Norwegian employer, relevant vocational qualifications, and the salary meets the threshold. The occupation is often in demand, which may facilitate the process.

**Q: What education is needed to become a well driller?**
A: The typical path is a vocational education in drilling (fagbrev) through an apprenticeship, or a technical college program. On-the-job training is also common.
