# Shelf Filler — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 9334 (ISCO08)**

Shelf fillers, also known as stock clerks, are responsible for restocking merchandise, organizing shelves, and ensuring product availability in retail stores across Norway. This entry-level role is common in grocery chains, hypermarkets, and warehouse retailers, often requiring minimal experience. The occupation is part of Norway's large retail sector, which employs many part-time and seasonal workers, especially students and immigrants from the EEA area. Physical stamina and attention to detail are key, as the job involves lifting and precise arrangement.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression from shelf filler is limited without further education or training. Some workers move to supervisory roles such as shift leader or department manager, especially in larger chains. Others transition to logistics or warehouse positions. In Norway, formal qualifications are rarely required, but completing a vocational certificate in retail or logistics can improve advancement prospects. The role is often a stepping stone for students or newcomers to the labour market.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and location, but shelf fillers generally earn between NOK 150 and 200 per hour, with full-time annual earnings around NOK 300,000–350,000 before tax. Figures fluctuate based on shift differentials and collective agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a shelf filler in Norway?**
A: Generally no. Non-EU/EEA citizens need a skilled worker visa, which requires higher qualifications and salary threshold. Shelf filling is not considered skilled work, so only EU/EEA nationals can do this job without a permit.

**Q: Do I need any courses to become a shelf filler?**
A: No formal training is required. Employers provide on-the-job instruction. However, taking a short retail course may improve your chances of being hired or promoted.
