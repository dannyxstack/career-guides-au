# Beautician — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 5142 (ISCO08)**

Beauticians in Norway provide a range of personal care services such as facials, makeup application, manicures, and hair removal, typically working in salons or as self-employed professionals. The profession is regulated through vocational training leading to a trade certificate (fagbrev), with a focus on skin care and hygiene standards. Demand is steady in urban areas, driven by consumer trends toward wellness and appearance. The sector includes many small businesses, and competition for jobs can vary locally. Labour mobility is facilitated for EU/EEA nationals under free movement, while non-EEA applicants face stricter requirements, including a job offer and meeting salary thresholds.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for beauticians in Norway typically begins with a two-year vocational apprenticeship or a one-year course at a private school, leading to a trade certificate. Experienced beauticians may advance to salon management, open their own business, or specialize in areas such as medical aesthetics, permanent makeup, or skincare consultancy. Continuing education is valued, with certifications in advanced techniques enhancing employability. The field offers flexibility but often requires a client base for self-employed success. Union membership (e.g., Fagforbundet) is common in municipal jobs, though private-sector positions dominate.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a beautician in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience and location, but entry-level beauticians often earn around 350,000–400,000 NOK, while experienced professionals can earn up to 500,000 NOK or more if self-employed with a strong clientele. Public-sector positions may offer lower base pay but better benefits.

**Q: Can a beautician from outside the EU/EEA get a work visa for Norway?**
A: It is difficult because the Skilled Worker permit requires a salary above the threshold (508,500 NOK in 2025), which is high for this occupation. Most beauticians earn below that, so non-EEA candidates rarely qualify. EU/EEA nationals face no such restrictions.

**Q: Is a trade certificate (fagbrev) mandatory to work as a beautician in Norway?**
A: Yes, in practice most employers require it. The trade certificate is obtained through a two-year vocational school programme or an apprenticeship. Without it, you cannot legally offer certain services, and insurance may not cover you.
