# Pet Groomer and Animal Care Worker — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

Pet groomers and animal care workers in Norway are employed in a variety of settings including pet shops, kennels, veterinary clinics, and specialized grooming salons, providing essential services such as bathing, trimming, and basic health checks. The profession is niche but stable, with demand driven by high pet ownership rates and a cultural emphasis on animal welfare.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant or trainee, advancing to senior groomer or salon manager. Many professionals become self-employed, opening their own grooming businesses. Some transition into related fields such as veterinary nursing or animal shelter management with additional education. The path is practical and hands-on, with a focus on building a loyal client base.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Client appointment and schedule management
- Basic beauty records and billing
- Standardized communication (e.g., reminders, follow-ups)
- Security monitoring (e.g., camera analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze pet fur health for care recommendations
- Use AI tools to optimize inventory and supplies management
- Use AI diagnostic tools to assist in identifying skin issues
- Using AI to generate personalized beauty solutions and recommendations

**Human moat:**

- Calming techniques for handling anxious or aggressive animals
- Adjust grooming techniques flexibly based on individual pet condition
- Building emotional trust and personalized service with customers
- Creativity and aesthetic judgment in hand styling

**Skills to build:**

- Learn animal behavior knowledge to improve soothing skills
- Master the use of AI-assisted diagnostic tools
- Enhancing advanced beauty and styling design skills
- Learn to use customer relationship management software
- Attend professional certification courses to enhance competitiveness
- develop social media marketing skills to expand client base

**Key growth areas:**

- pet grooming
- animal care
- canine behavior
- pet boarding

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pet groomer in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions may earn around the median for personal service occupations, while experienced or self-employed groomers can earn more. Detailed data is in SSB table 11418.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as pet groomers in Norway?**
A: It is difficult for non-EEA nationals as the occupation typically does not meet the skilled worker permit requirements (degree or trade certificate and salary threshold). EEA nationals have free movement.

**Q: What education is needed to become a pet groomer in Norway?**
A: No formal education is mandatory, but a vocational certificate in animal care or a pet grooming certificate from a recognized school is highly recommended to improve job prospects.
