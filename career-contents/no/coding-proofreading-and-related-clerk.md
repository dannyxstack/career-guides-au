# Coding, Proofreading and Related Clerk — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 4413 (ISCO08)**

Coding, proofreading and related clerks perform a range of clerical tasks involving the coding, verifying and correcting of data and text, often in administrative or publishing settings. In Norway, these roles are typically found in government agencies, publishing houses, and private companies that require accurate data entry and document proofreading. The occupation is generally considered low-skilled and often filled by local candidates or those with basic qualifications. With the digitalisation of administrative processes, demand for such roles has been declining, but niche opportunities exist in specialised fields like legal or medical proofreading.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression is limited without additional education or specialisation. Many workers move into senior clerical roles, administrative management, or specialise in areas like technical writing or data analysis. Others transition to related fields such as content management or digital publishing. Upskilling in languages, software, or IT systems can enhance prospects. In Norway, holding a trade certificate or higher education in documentation or language studies can lead to supervisory positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Text Proofreading and Spell Checking with Real-time High Accuracy by AI Models
- Data Encoding and Classification: Standardized Conversion from Spreadsheets to Databases
- Standardization of format, such as layout and punctuation uniformity
- Simple document template filling and letter generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Logic and semantic proofreading of complex text; AI provides suggestions, but humans must finalize.
- Auxiliary proofreading of multilingual documents, AI draft first then human polishing
- Data quality monitoring, AI flags outliers for human review
- Quickly locate reference sources via AI search to improve proofreading efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Semantic judgment and style grasp in complex contexts
- Authoritative verification of legal or professional terminology
- Responsibility for handling data involving trade secrets or privacy
- Cross-departmental communication and requirement clarification

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced configuration of AI proofreading tools (e.g. Grammarly Business, Acrolinx)
- Basic programming (Python/Excel macros) to automate repetitive tasks
- Data visualization and basic analysis
- Project management and quality control
- Domain expertise (e.g., legal, medical knowledge)

**Key growth areas:**

- Data management
- Legal proofreading
- Medical coding
- Digital publishing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a coding and proofreading clerk in Norway?**
A: Salaries are generally low compared to skilled professions. Entry-level positions may start around 350,000-400,000 NOK per year, while experienced clerks can earn up to 450,000 NOK. The median is around 400,000 NOK, varying by region and sector.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EFTA national work as a proofreading clerk in Norway?**
A: It is very difficult. The occupation is not considered skilled migration, and the salary often falls below the Skilled Worker permit threshold. Non-EU/EFTA nationals typically need a job offer with high salary or an alternative pathway like a jobseeker permit, but chances are slim.

**Q: Is this occupation on Norway's shortage list?**
A: No. Coding, proofreading and related clerks are not listed as a shortage occupation in Norway. The labour supply is generally sufficient from local and EU/EEA workers.
