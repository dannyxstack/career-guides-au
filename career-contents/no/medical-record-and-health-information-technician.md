# Medical Records and Health Information Technician — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3252 (ISCO08)**

In Norway, medical records and health information technicians, commonly known as health secretaries, are crucial for managing patient data, medical records, and health information systems in hospitals, clinics, and GP offices. They ensure accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance with national health regulations while supporting healthcare professionals with administrative tasks and digital health tools.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for health secretaries in Norway typically includes specialization in health informatics, electronic patient records management, or team leadership roles. With experience and further vocational training, they can advance to senior administrative positions, health information management, or transition into health IT consulting. Continuous learning in digital tools and data privacy is essential for career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

- Health Informatics and HIPAA Compliance
- Data analysis tools (SQL, Python, R)
- AI/ML fundamentals (NLP, predictive modeling)
- Medical coding systems (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI).
- Project management and change management

**Key growth areas:**

- Electronic Health Records
- Digitalization
- Aging Population
- Data Management

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Organized and detail-oriented individuals who thrive in structured environments.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a health secretary in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location, but entry-level positions often start around 400,000–450,000 NOK annually, with experienced health secretaries earning up to 550,000 NOK or more, especially in hospitals or with specialization.

**Q: Can a health secretary from outside the EU/EEA get a work visa for Norway?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer and a relevant vocational training or bachelor's degree. The occupation is on the shortage list, which facilitates the process. The job must meet the salary threshold for skilled workers.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for health secretaries in Norway?**
A: With experience and further education, health secretaries can move into health information management, become supervisors, or specialize in electronic health record systems. Some pursue bachelor's degrees in health informatics for higher-level roles.
