# Metal Polisher, Wheel Grinder and Tool Sharpener — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 7224 (ISCO08)**

In Norway, metal polishers, wheel grinders, and tool sharpeners are skilled tradespeople working in manufacturing, engineering, and maintenance sectors, primarily in metalworking, shipbuilding, and renewable energy industries. The role demands precision and attention to detail for finishing surfaces and sharpening tools, often requiring vocational training and certification.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with apprenticeship, moving to journeyman (fagbrev), then senior specialist or supervisor. Some advance to quality control or workshop management. Further specialization in CNC grinding or coating technologies is possible with additional training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive metal surface polishing and grinding operations
- Use automated grinding machines for batch workpiece processing.
- Grinding wheel dressing and balancing based on preset parameters
- dimensional inspection and pass/fail determination of simple workpieces
- Automatic grinding of standard tool edges.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted process parameter optimization: automatically recommends grinding parameters based on workpiece material and precision requirements
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes equipment vibration and temperature data to provide early fault warnings
- Visual inspection: AI system checks surface finish in real-time, reducing manual inspection
- Collaborative robots: human-robot collaboration to move heavy workpieces, reducing labor intensity
- Digital Twin: Simulate machining processes, shorten trial cutting cycles

**Human moat:**

- Manual polishing and finishing of non-standard workpieces
- Manual adjustment and grinding of complex-shaped molds and tools
- On-site judgment and process adjustment for material defects
- Manual repair and precision restoration of aging equipment
- Communicate with clients on customized surface treatment requirements

**Skills to build:**

- CNC grinding programming and operation
- CAD/CAM software (e.g., Mastercam)
- Fundamentals of robotics and automation system integration.
- Data analysis and quality control (SPC)
- Additive manufacturing post-processing techniques
- Electrical and mechanical maintenance skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Manufacturing
- Renewable Energy Maintenance
- Marine Industry
- Tooling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a metal polisher in Norway?**
A: Salary varies by experience and region, but it is competitive with other skilled trades. Entry-level positions start around 450,000 NOK annually, while experienced workers can earn over 550,000 NOK.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as metal polishers in Norway?**
A: Yes, if they obtain a Skilled Worker residence permit with a job offer and a recognized trade certificate (fagbrev). The salary must meet the current threshold.

**Q: Is there room for advancement in this trade?**
A: Yes, with experience you can become a supervisor, quality inspector, or specialize in CNC operations or coating. Further education can lead to engineering roles.
