# Pharmaceutical Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3213 (ISCO08)**

Pharmaceutical technicians and assistants in Norway work under the supervision of pharmacists in community pharmacies, hospital pharmacies, and the pharmaceutical industry. Their duties include dispensing prescription medications, preparing compounded medicines, managing inventory, and providing customer service. The role requires attention to detail, knowledge of pharmaceutical regulations, and proficiency in Norwegian. The profession is regulated, requiring authorization from the Norwegian Medicines Agency.

Many pursue further education to become pharmacists or take on administrative positions in the pharmaceutical industry. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pharmaceutical technician in Norway?**
A: Salaries are regulated by collective agreements. Entry-level positions start around 400,000 NOK annually, with experienced technicians earning up to 500,000 NOK. For the latest figures, consult Statistics Norway or pharmacy union agreements.

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as a pharmaceutical technician in Norway?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA/EFTA national, you can move freely but must register. Non-EEA nationals need a Skilled Worker permit with a job offer and recognized qualifications. Authorization from the Norwegian Medicines Agency is required for all.

**Q: Can I advance to become a pharmacist?**
A: Yes, with additional education. You can apply for a bachelor's degree in pharmacy at a university, which typically takes 3 years, then a one-year internship to become a licensed pharmacist.
