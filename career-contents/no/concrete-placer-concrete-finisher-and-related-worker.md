# Concrete Placer and Finisher — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 7114 (ISCO08)**

Concrete placers, finishers, and related workers are essential in Norway's construction sector, handling the pouring, spreading, and finishing of concrete for foundations, floors, structures, and infrastructure projects. They work on residential, commercial, and public works, requiring physical stamina and precision. The occupation is in steady demand due to ongoing urban development and government investment in transport and housing.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a labourer or apprentice, advancing to skilled finisher after obtaining a trade certificate (fagbrev). With experience, workers can become foremen, supervisors, or self-employed contractors. Specialization in areas like high-strength concrete, bridge construction, or architectural finishes offers further advancement. Additional certifications in safety or operation of concrete equipment can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Residential
- Renovation
- Public works

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a concrete placer and finisher in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and project. Entry-level workers earn around 350,000–400,000 NOK annually, while experienced finishers with a trade certificate can earn 450,000–550,000 NOK. Overtime and project bonuses are common. The skilled worker permit salary threshold applies for non-EEA workers.

**Q: Can a non-EEA concrete worker get a work visa for Norway?**
A: Yes, if you have a concrete job offer and a completed vocational training (e.g., trade certificate) recognized in Norway, you can apply for a Skilled Worker residence permit. The job must meet the salary threshold (around 471,100 NOK in 2024). EU/EEA nationals can work freely under EEA/EFTA free movement rules.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for concrete workers in Norway?**
A: With a fagbrev, you can become a foreman, supervisor, or site manager. Specializing in architectural concrete, bridge work, or restoration can lead to higher pay. Many experienced workers start their own contracting businesses.
