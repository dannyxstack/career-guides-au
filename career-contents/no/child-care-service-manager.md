# Child Care Services Manager — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 1341 (ISCO08)**

Child care services managers in Norway oversee the daily operations of kindergartens and child care centers, ensuring high-quality pedagogical practices, staff management, and compliance with national regulations. This role combines leadership, administrative duties, and educational oversight, requiring a strong background in early childhood education. The profession is highly valued in Norway's welfare system, with a focus on children's development and well-being.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression often starts as a kindergarten teacher (barnehagelærer), then moves to a unit leader or pedagogical leader, and eventually to manager (styrer). Further advancement can lead to roles in municipal administration or educational consultancy. Additional management training and a master's degree can enhance prospects.

- Optimizing employee work schedules based on rostering algorithms

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a child care services manager in Norway?**
A: Typical salaries range from around 550,000 to 700,000 NOK annually, depending on experience and location.

**Q: What visa do I need to work as a child care services manager in Norway?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA citizen, you can work freely. Non-EU/EEA nationals need a Skilled Worker permit with a job offer, a relevant bachelor's degree, and a salary meeting the threshold (around 500,000 NOK).

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities?**
A: After becoming a manager, you can progress to municipal administrative roles, educational consultancy, or overseeing multiple centers. Additional education like a master's degree can help.
