# Personnel and Careers Professional — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 2423 (ISCO08)**

Personnel and Careers Professionals, known in Norway as HR-rådgivere, manage recruitment, training, performance appraisal, and career development within organisations. They work across private and public sectors, often in human resources departments. The role has evolved with digitalisation and increased focus on employee wellbeing and diversity. In Norway, the profession is regulated mainly by company policies and collective agreements, and a bachelor's degree in HR, business administration, or psychology is common. The field offers stable employment, with opportunities in consultancy and specialised HR areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Norway typically starts as an HR assistant or coordinator, advancing to HR advisor, then senior advisor or specialist (e.g., recruitment, compensation). With experience, one can become an HR manager or director. Lateral moves to consulting or specialised roles (e.g., organisational development) are common. Professional certifications (e.g., from Norsk HR-forening) can enhance prospects. The trend is towards more strategic, data-driven HR roles.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What visa do I need to work as an HR professional in Norway?**
A: EU/EEA nationals can work freely after registration. Non-EU nationals need a Skilled Worker permit with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (about 500,000 NOK). A bachelor's degree is typically required.

**Q: What is the typical career path for an HR professional in Norway?**
A: Entry-level as HR assistant or coordinator, then HR advisor, senior advisor, HR specialist (recruitment, compensation), and eventually HR manager or director. Many also move into consulting or specialised areas like organisational development.

**Q: What is the average salary for an HR professional in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary, but a typical HR advisor earns between 550,000 and 700,000 NOK annually. Senior roles can exceed 800,000 NOK. Exact figures depend on experience, sector, and location.
