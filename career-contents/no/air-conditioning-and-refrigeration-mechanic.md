# Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 7127 (ISCO08)**

Air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics install, maintain, and repair cooling and heating systems in residential, commercial, and industrial settings. In Norway, demand is driven by cold climate, green transition, and increasing use of heat pumps. Workers typically hold a trade certificate (fagbrev) from a 4-year vocational programme. The occupation is regulated and requires authorization for handling refrigerants.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: I am a non-EU citizen with a refrigeration qualification from my home country. Can I work in Norway?**
A: Yes, if your qualification is recognized as equivalent to the Norwegian trade certificate and you have a job offer meeting the salary threshold. You can apply for a Skilled Worker permit. You may also need to pass a Norwegian language test.

**Q: How long does it take to become a certified air conditioning mechanic in Norway?**
A: The standard path is 2 years of vocational school followed by 2 years of paid apprenticeship, then a trade test (fagprøve). Total duration is typically 4 years.

**Q: What is the salary range for this occupation in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and sector. Entry-level mechanics can expect around 450,000–500,000 NOK, while experienced specialists may earn over 600,000 NOK. Overtime and on-call work are common.
