# Window Cleaner — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 9123 (ISCO08)**

Window cleaners in Norway are responsible for cleaning windows, glass facades, and other glass surfaces on buildings, often using ladders, scaffolding, or hydraulic lifts. They work in commercial, residential, and industrial settings, ensuring cleanliness and safety. The occupation requires physical fitness, attention to detail, and comfort working at heights. In Norway, demand is driven by ongoing construction, maintenance of existing buildings, and high standards of cleanliness, particularly in urban centres like Oslo and Bergen.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary with experience and location. Beginners earn around the national median, while experienced specialists can earn above average, especially in high-rise work. Overtime and bonuses are common.

**Q: Can non-EEA nationals work as a window cleaner in Norway?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. Non-EEA nationals need a Skilled Worker Residence Permit, which requires a completed vocational training (e.g., fagbrev i renholdsoperatør) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. Without relevant qualifications, it is difficult to qualify.

**Q: Is window cleaning a regulated profession in Norway?**
A: No formal regulation, but safety training for working at heights is often mandatory. Employers may require a trade certificate or driver's license.
