# Signwriter, Decorative Painter, Engraver and Etcher — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 7316 (ISCO08)**

This niche occupation encompasses artisans who create, paint, and etch signs, decorative surfaces, and engravings on various materials. In Norway, these craftspeople often work in small workshops, are self-employed, or are employed by specialized companies. The market is limited but sustained by demand for custom signage, heritage restoration, and high-end interior decoration. Digital printing has reduced the need for traditional hand-painted signs, but there remains a niche for authentic, hand-crafted work in historical buildings, museums, and luxury establishments. Engraving and etching are sought after for awards, memorials, and jewellery. The occupation relies on artistic skill and precision.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows the Norwegian vocational training system: an apprentice (lærling) obtains a trade certificate (fagbrev) after 2-4 years, becomes a journeyman (svenn), and may later earn a master craftsman certificate (mesterbrev). Many practitioners specialise further in restoration, gold leaf work, or advanced engraving techniques. Some work for cultural heritage institutions or run their own studios. Advancement often involves building a reputation and a portfolio; experienced artisans may transition into teaching or consultancy. Self-employment is common, offering flexibility but also income instability.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a signwriter or decorative painter in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary widely. An employed journeyman may earn around 400,000–500,000 NOK annually, while master craftspeople or those in high-end restoration can earn more. Self-employed artisans have variable income; many combine multiple projects. It's advisable to research current wage statistics from SSB or relevant trade unions.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA national immigrate to Norway as a signwriter?**
A: Yes, under the Skilled Worker permit if you have a job offer and a relevant trade certificate (fagbrev). However, the salary threshold (around 456,000 NOK in 2024) may be a barrier. Alternatively, the jobseeker permit allows a 6-month search. Self-employment is possible via the entrepreneur visa, requiring a solid business plan and sufficient funds.

**Q: Is there a shortage of signwriters or decorative painters in Norway?**
A: No, this occupation is not on NAV's shortage list. The market is small and specialised. While there is some demand for restoration work and custom projects, competition exists. It is not considered a high-demand field for migration purposes.
