# Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operator — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 8154 (ISCO08)**

Bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators work in Norway's textile finishing industry, mainly in industrial laundries, textile mills, and chemical processing plants. They operate machinery that treats fabrics with chemicals, water, and heat for coloring, bleaching, or cleaning. The occupation is essential for textile production and recycling, but the industry is modest due to outsourcing. Operators must follow safety protocols for chemical handling and machine operation. The role requires attention to detail and mechanical aptitude.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bleaching/dyeing machine operator in Norway?**
A: Typical salaries for this occupation are moderate and may fall below the skilled worker permit threshold. Precise figures vary by experience, employer, and region. Operators can expect average monthly earnings around the median for production workers.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a machine operator?**
A: For non-EU/EEA nationals, a Skilled Worker permit requires completed vocational training and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. If the salary is below the threshold, the permit may be denied. EU/EEA nationals do not need a visa.
