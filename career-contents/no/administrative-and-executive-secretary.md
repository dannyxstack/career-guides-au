# Administrative and Executive Secretary — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3343 (ISCO08)**

Administrative and executive secretaries in Norway provide high-level administrative support to managers and executives, managing schedules, correspondence, and office operations. They are employed across sectors such as government, finance, law, and private enterprise, often requiring strong organizational and communication skills. The role is stable but evolving with digitalization, valuing proficiency in Norwegian and English.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts from secretary or office assistant, advancing to senior executive secretary or office manager. Many transition into specialized roles like HR coordinator or project manager after gaining experience and further education. Norwegian employers value continuous learning, and upskilling in digital tools or management can accelerate advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Calendar management and meeting scheduling (AI scheduling tools automatically coordinate times)
- Basic document processing (e.g., formatting documents, generating email templates)
- Data entry and information retrieval (natural language queries replace manual operations)
- Meeting minutes and summary generation (speech-to-text + AI summary)
- Travel booking and expense reimbursement (automated system processing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Executive communication strategy suggestions (AI analyzes email tone, priority, aids decision-making)
- Project progress tracking and early warning (AI integrates multi-source data, automatically flags risks)
- Information organization in cross-departmental coordination (AI extracts key points, assists reporting)
- Data analysis report writing (AI generates charts and initial insights for manual optimization)
- Emergency response (AI provides historical cases and contingency plans)

**Human moat:**

- Maintaining complex interpersonal relationships and emotional support
- Judgment and political sensitivity in high-level decision-making
- Confidential information handling and ethical judgement
- Creative problem-solving for unstructured problems
- Flexible adaptability in cross-cultural and cross-level communication

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced application of AI office tools (e.g., Copilot, Notion AI)
- Data visualization and basic data analysis
- Operation of project management systems (e.g., Asana, Jira)
- Emotional intelligence and conflict management training
- Basic programming (Python script for automating repetitive tasks)
- Information security and compliance awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- digitalisation
- executive support
- office management
- public sector

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an administrative and executive secretary in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience, but generally range from 500,000 to 600,000 NOK annually before tax, with public sector and large firms offering higher pay. Exact figures can be found in Statistics Norway's salary statistics.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as an administrative and executive secretary in Norway?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer and meet the Skilled Worker permit requirements: a completed bachelor's degree or vocational qualification, and a salary at or above the threshold (currently 500,000 NOK). The employer must show that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for this role in Norway?**
A: With experience and further training, secretaries can move into senior executive secretary, office manager, or administrative coordinator roles. Many also transition into specialized fields like HR, finance, or project management.
