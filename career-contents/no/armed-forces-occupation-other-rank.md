# Military Personnel (Other Ranks) — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

In Norway, armed forces other ranks (menige) serve in combat, support, and technical roles across the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Home Guard. Entry typically follows conscription (Førstegangstjeneste) or voluntary enlistment, with further training at the Norwegian Armed Forces' schools. The sector is characterised by strong job security, a clear hierarchy, and opportunities for specialisation in areas such as cyber defence, engineering, and international peacekeeping. While not a typical migration route, EEA/EFTA nationals may apply under certain conditions, but citizenship is generally required for security-sensitive positions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression for other ranks typically begins with basic training (Førstegangstjeneste) followed by specialised courses or attending the Non-Commissioned Officer School (Befalsskole) for promotion to sergeant. Exceptional soldiers may later qualify for officer training at the Norwegian Military Academy (Krigsskole). Lateral moves into defence-related civilian roles (e.g., logistics, IT) are possible after service. Many veterans transition to private security or public sector jobs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an other ranks soldier in Norway?**
A: Salaries for other ranks start from around 450,000 NOK annually for privates, increasing with rank and experience. Additional allowances for deployments and specialist roles apply.

**Q: Can foreign nationals join the Norwegian Armed Forces?**
A: Generally, Norwegian citizenship is required for permanent positions due to security clearances. However, EEA/EFTA nationals may enter certain specialist roles or the Home Guard on a limited basis, but most pathways are closed to non-citizens.

**Q: Is prior military experience from other countries recognised?**
A: The Norwegian Armed Forces evaluate foreign military experience on a case-by-case basis. Courses may be credited, but joining as an other rank typically requires completing Norwegian basic training.
