# Non-Commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Non-commissioned armed forces officers in Norway form the backbone of the Norwegian Armed Forces, leading and training enlisted personnel in various branches including Army, Navy, and Air Force. They hold roles such as squad leaders, instructors, and technical specialists, with responsibilities ranging from combat operations to logistics and maintenance. Employment is exclusively through the Norwegian military, requiring Norwegian citizenship or close ties, and is not accessible through standard labour migration pathways. The role demands high discipline, physical fitness, and loyalty to national security.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a non-commissioned officer in Norway?**
A: Salaries for non-commissioned officers follow military pay scales, varying by rank and experience. Starting salaries are competitive with civilian sectors, and include benefits such as housing allowance, pension, and health coverage. Exact figures are set by the Norwegian Armed Forces and are not disclosed here.

**Q: Can a non-Norwegian citizen become a non-commissioned officer in Norway?**
A: Generally no. The Norwegian Armed Forces require citizenship for all officers. Exceptions are rare and only for NATO exchange personnel or very specialized roles, typically for shorter assignments. Standard labour migration routes do not apply.

**Q: What career opportunities exist after leaving the military?**
A: Many former non-commissioned officers transition to civilian roles in security, logistics, project management, or public administration. The military offers certification recognition and re-education programs to ease the transition.
