# Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Manager — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 1312 (ISCO08)**

Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers oversee operations in fish farming, hatcheries, and fisheries, ensuring efficient production, staff management, and regulatory compliance. In Norway, this role is pivotal to the world's largest salmon export industry, with growing emphasis on sustainable practices, technological innovation (IoT, AI, remote monitoring), and strict environmental regulations. Managers must balance biological, economic, and ecological factors, often working in remote coastal areas. The industry is expanding into offshore aquaculture, driving demand for skilled leaders.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from production technician or biologist roles, advancing to shift supervisor, assistant manager, and then production manager. A master's degree in aquaculture or marine biology is common. Many move to regional director or CEO positions within aquaculture companies. Specialization in fish health, genetics, or sustainability opens further opportunities. Some transition into consultancy, regulation, or international roles with Norwegian firms abroad.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually record farming data (water temperature, feeding amounts, etc.)
- Generate daily production reports and compliance documents
- Image recognition of initial disease symptoms
- Inventory counting and feed order calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses sensors and AI to predict optimal feeding times and amounts
- Monitor fish health and abnormal behavior through machine vision.
- Simulate the impact of different stocking densities and feed plans on yield
- Real-time water quality monitoring and automatic adjustment of oxygenation and filtration equipment

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making for responding to sudden disease outbreaks or environmental disasters
- Experience-based biosecurity management and overall disease prevention planning.
- Negotiations and relationship management with regulators, suppliers, and sales channels
- Assess and implement new sustainable aquaculture technologies

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (Python/R)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor system management
- Operation and troubleshooting of automated control systems
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainability certification standards
- Inspection skills for drones or underwater robots

**Key growth areas:**

- Offshore aquaculture
- Digital monitoring
- Sustainable feed
- Biosecurity
