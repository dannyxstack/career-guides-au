# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers in Norway perform basic food preparation, cleaning, and dishwashing tasks in restaurants, hotels, cafeterias, and institutional kitchens. The role is entry-level and often filled by students, seasonal workers, and EU/EEA nationals. Demand is steady due to high turnover and a thriving hospitality sector, but wages are among the lowest in the labour market.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and region, but kitchen helpers generally earn above the minimum wage in the hospitality sector. Pay is typically hourly and may include tips in some establishments.

**Q: Can a non-EEA national work as a kitchen helper in Norway?**
A: Generally no, because the occupation does not meet the requirements for a skilled worker permit (no formal qualification needed and salary threshold too low). Non-EEA nationals would need a different basis, such as family immigration or asylum, to take this job.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for kitchen helpers in Norway?**
A: With experience and vocational training, kitchen helpers can progress to line cook, chef, or kitchen manager roles. Apprenticeships (lærling) in the restaurant and food processing trade are a common pathway.
