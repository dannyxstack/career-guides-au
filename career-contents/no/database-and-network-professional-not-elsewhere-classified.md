# Database and Network Professional — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 2529 (ISCO08)**

Database and network professionals in Norway manage, maintain, and secure data systems and communication networks, supporting the country's highly digitized economy. With a strong focus on cloud migration, cybersecurity, and data analytics, these roles are in high demand across sectors such as finance, telecom, and public administration. Norwegian employers typically require a bachelor's degree or equivalent, plus relevant certifications (e.g., Cisco, Oracle).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression often starts with technical roles (e.g., database administrator, network engineer) and leads to senior positions like IT architect or IT manager. Many professionals specialise further in cloud solutions (AWS, Azure) or cybersecurity. Public sector and oil/gas industries offer long-term stability, while startups and consultancies provide faster advancement for high performers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine database backup, recovery, optimization and other scripted operational tasks
- Automated deployment and compliance checks for network device configurations
- Rule-based log analysis and alert generation
- Routine performance monitoring and generation of capacity planning reports
- Basic data migration and ETL workflow orchestration

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI assistance for database performance tuning (e.g., index recommendations)
- AI-driven network anomaly detection and root cause analysis
- AIOps for predictive maintenance
- Automatically generate operation reports using natural language generation technology
- AI-assisted cybersecurity threat analysis and response

**Human moat:**

- Complex system architecture design and cross-system integration decisions
- Strategic planning for high availability and disaster recovery solutions
- Business understanding and data model design
- Creative problem-solving for non-standard faults
- In-depth communication and collaboration with business departments and management

**Skills to build:**

- AI/ML fundamentals and model deployment skills
- Advanced operations of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible)
- Data security and compliance (GDPR, etc.)
- AIOps tools (e.g. Datadog, Splunk) usage
- Communication and project management skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud computing
- Cybersecurity
- Data analytics
- Automation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EEA country, Norway grants skilled-worker residence permits to qualified professionals who hold a concrete job offer meeting local pay and qualification rules; long-term residence can follow. Requirements are set nationally and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI)](https://www.udi.no/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a database or network professional in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location, but entry-level positions start around NOK 450,000–550,000 per year, while senior roles often exceed NOK 800,000. Specialists in cloud or security can earn more.

**Q: How can a non-EU/EEA database professional get a work visa for Norway?**
A: You need a job offer from a Norwegian employer for a skilled position, a relevant bachelor's degree or trade certificate, and a salary meeting the threshold (NOK 474,000/year in 2024). Apply to UDI for a Skilled Worker residence permit. A Jobseeker permit is also available for up to 6 months.

**Q: Is this occupation on Norway's shortage list?**
A: Yes, database and network professionals are generally considered in high demand, and many roles appear on NAV's list of occupations with shortage. This can facilitate immigration and job searches.
