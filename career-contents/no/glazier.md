# Glazier — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers install, replace, and repair glass in windows, doors, facades, and other structures. In Norway, demand is driven by construction activity, energy-efficient retrofits, and maintenance of existing buildings. The occupation requires precision and adherence to safety standards. Most glaziers work in the building sector, often for small to medium-sized enterprises. The role is recognized with a formal trade certificate (fagbrev) through an apprenticeship.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship (2-4 years) to earn a trade certificate. Experienced glaziers can become forepersons or start their own business. Further specialization includes safety glass, frameless systems, or heritage restoration. The master craftsperson title (mesterbrev) is available for those who complete advanced training and exams, enabling leadership roles and higher earning potential.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-efficient windows
- Commercial construction
- Renovation
- Safety glass

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in Norway?**
A: Entry-level glaziers earn around NOK 450,000–500,000 per year. Experienced glaziers with a trade certificate can earn NOK 550,000–650,000, while master glaziers or those in supervisory roles may exceed NOK 700,000.

**Q: Can a non-EU glazier get a work visa for Norway?**
A: Yes, if you have a completed trade certificate (or equivalent) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (NOK 450,000/year). The job must require skilled workers. You apply for a Skilled Worker Residence Permit.

**Q: Is glazier on Norway's shortage list?**
A: Glazier is not currently on the national shortage list, but demand varies regionally. Skilled workers without a prior job offer may find it easier to apply from within the EEA.
