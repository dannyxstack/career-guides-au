# Social Work Associate Professional — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3412 (ISCO08)**

Social work associate professionals in Norway provide frontline support to individuals, families, and groups facing social challenges, working within municipal services, child welfare, rehabilitation, and NGOs. The role requires a bachelor's degree in social work (sosionom) or child welfare (barnevernspedagog), strong Norwegian language skills, and authorization from the Norwegian Directorate of Health. Norway's comprehensive welfare state ensures steady demand, though competition varies by region and specialization.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a social work associate professional in Norway?**
A: Salaries are determined by municipal pay scales and seniority, with progression over time. Entry-level salaries are around 450,000–500,000 NOK annually, increasing with experience.

**Q: Can I work in Norway as a social worker from outside the EU/EEA?**
A: Yes, but you need a job offer, a recognized bachelor's degree, and authorization from the Norwegian Directorate of Health. You must then apply for a Skilled Worker residence permit, which has a salary threshold.

**Q: Is a Norwegian degree required to work as a social worker?**
A: No, but your foreign degree must be recognized by NOKUT as equivalent to a Norwegian bachelor's in social work or child welfare. You also need to pass language tests and obtain authorization.
