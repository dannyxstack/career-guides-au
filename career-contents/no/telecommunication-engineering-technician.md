# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications engineering technicians install, maintain, and repair telecommunication systems such as broadband, fiber optics, mobile networks, and radio equipment. In Norway, they work for telecom operators, infrastructure companies, and technical service providers, with steady demand fueled by ongoing 5G deployment and rural broadband expansion. The role requires practical technical skills and often involves outdoor or on-site work.

With further training, they can become team leaders, network supervisors, or move into engineering through a bachelor's degree. Many pursue additional certifications to stay current with evolving technology.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much can a telecommunications engineering technician earn in Norway?**
A: Salaries are competitive for skilled technicians. Many employers offer overtime pay and bonuses. Income grows with experience and specialization.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a non-EU/EEA telecommunications technician?**
A: Yes, via the Skilled Worker permit. You need a job offer, relevant qualifications (fagbrev or degree), and a salary above the threshold. The permit is employer-sponsored.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, you can become a senior technician, network specialist, project leader, or pursue further education to become an engineer in telecommunications.
