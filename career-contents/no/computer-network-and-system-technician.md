# Computer Network and Systems Technicians — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3513 (ISCO08)**

In Norway, computer network and systems technicians are in high demand due to the country's rapid digitalization and emphasis on robust IT infrastructure, supporting sectors from oil and gas to public administration, with opportunities in both established firms and startups.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts with technical support roles, advancing to network administrator, systems engineer, or security specialist. With experience and certifications, technicians can move into architecture or management positions, such as IT manager or network architect.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically diagnose common network issues and provide solutions
- Automatically generate network configuration and patch scripts
- Record work orders and categorize/escalate to senior teams
- User password reset and account management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI log analysis to quickly locate fault root causes
- Using network simulation tools to predict change impact.
- Early detection of anomalies through AI-driven monitoring.
- Using natural language queries to accelerate knowledge base resolution

**Human moat:**

- Root cause analysis and manual repair of complex faults
- Network architecture design and security policy formulation
- Effective communication with non-technical users
- Prioritizing urgent matters under pressure

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud network management (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Cybersecurity certifications (CISSP/CEH)
- Automation scripts (Python/Ansible)
- AI/ML basics and network analysis tools
- Hybrid network architecture design

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud computing
- Cybersecurity
- 5G networks
- IoT

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EEA country, Norway grants skilled-worker residence permits to qualified professionals who hold a concrete job offer meeting local pay and qualification rules; long-term residence can follow. Requirements are set nationally and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI)](https://www.udi.no/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Problem-solvers who enjoy hands-on configuration and troubleshooting of networks.
- Detail-oriented individuals with a passion for keeping systems secure and efficient.
- Those who can work both independently and in collaborative team environments.

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike continuous learning, as technology evolves quickly.
- Those who prefer routine tasks without unexpected technical challenges.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a computer network technician in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. According to Statistics Norway (SSB), the average annual salary for IT technicians is competitive. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced technicians can earn well above the median. Check SSB table 11418 for current figures.

**Q: What are the requirements for a skilled worker visa for this occupation?**
A: You need a job offer from a Norwegian employer, a completed degree or vocational training relevant to the role, and your salary must meet the threshold (currently about 450,000 NOK/year). The employer must also comply with working conditions and register the position.

**Q: Is this occupation on Norway's shortage list?**
A: Yes, computer network and systems technicians are often listed as in short supply, according to NAV's business survey. This can streamline visa processing and signal high demand.
