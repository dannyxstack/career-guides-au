# Artistic and Cultural Associate Professional — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 3435 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a range of artistic and cultural support roles, such as photographers, graphic designers, decorators, and assistant producers, often employed in media, advertising, event management, and cultural institutions. In Norway, many work freelance or on short-term contracts, with opportunities concentrated in Oslo and other urban centres. The field is competitive but offers creative fulfilment for those with technical skills and cultural knowledge.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an artistic and cultural associate professional in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary widely by role and sector. According to Statistics Norway, median annual earnings for this group in 2023 were around 450,000–500,000 NOK, but entry-level or freelance roles can be lower. Public sector positions (museums, cultural centres) often pay according to collective agreements, while freelance rates depend on project scope and negotiation.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA national get a work visa as an artistic and cultural associate professional?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need a job offer meeting the skilled worker criteria: the role must require at least a completed vocation or bachelor's degree, and salary must be at least 460,000 NOK per year (2024 threshold). Many associate-level positions fall below this or are temporary, making visa approval uncertain. A strong portfolio and degree in a relevant field improve chances.

**Q: Are there many job opportunities in Norway for this occupation?**
A: The market is modest and competitive. Oslo has the most opportunities in media, design, and events. Demand is steady in digital content creation, but many positions are short-term or freelance. Norwegian language skills are often required for client-facing roles. Networking and a robust portfolio are crucial.
