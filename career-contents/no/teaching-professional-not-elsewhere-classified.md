# Teaching Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Norway

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse group of teaching professionals not specifically classified elsewhere, including adult education teachers, corporate trainers, language instructors, and private tutors. In Norway, they work in public and private institutions, companies, and freelance settings. Demand is driven by lifelong learning initiatives, immigrant integration programs, and the need for digital skills training. Many positions require Norwegian language proficiency and relevant pedagogical qualifications.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression often involves moving into educational management, curriculum design, or specialized training consultancy. Many start as part-time instructors and advance to full-time roles with administrative responsibilities. Further studies in pedagogy or subject-specific fields can open doors to higher-level positions. Some professionals combine teaching with research or move into e-learning development.

- Basic computer skills training
- Generates standardised lesson plans and practice exercises
- Recording and distribution of online courses.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze student learning data through AI to adjust teaching strategies
- Uses AI to assist in personalized tutoring and feedback

**Human moat:**

- Cross-Cultural Understanding and Sensitivity
- Course design and innovative thinking

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a teaching professional not elsewhere classified in Norway?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. Public-sector adult education teachers typically earn 500,000–600,000 NOK per year, while corporate trainers or freelance instructors may earn more or less. No exact figures are provided here due to variability.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA citizen get a work visa as a teaching professional in Norway?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Norwegian employer, a completed degree or trade certificate, and the salary meets the threshold (500,000 NOK as of 2025). Norwegian language skills are often required. The Skilled Worker Residence Permit is the main route.
