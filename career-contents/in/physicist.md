# Physicist — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2111 (NCO2015)**

Physicists and astronomers study the fundamental properties and laws of the universe, including matter, energy, space, and time. They conduct research in academic institutions, government labs, and industries such as aerospace, defense, and technology. In India, physicists contribute to space exploration (ISRO), nuclear research (BARC), and theoretical physics. Employment requires at least a master's degree, often a PhD, and strong analytical skills.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physicist in India with a PhD?**
A: Entry-level postdoctoral fellows earn around INR 4-6 lakhs per annum, but permanent positions like assistant professor or scientist at government labs start at INR 8-12 lakhs. With experience, salaries can reach INR 20-50 lakhs.

**Q: Can foreign physicists work in India easily?**
A: Yes, if sponsored by a recognized institution (e.g., ISRO, IITs). The Employment Visa or Research Visa requires a job offer; OCI is easier for diaspora. India's visa process is bureaucratic but straightforward for qualified researchers.

**Q: Is physics a skilled-migration friendly occupation in India?**
A: Yes, physicists are in demand for specialized research roles, but the pay is lower than in tech. The skill threshold for an Employment Visa is high (advanced degree required), but the visa is not quota-capped.
