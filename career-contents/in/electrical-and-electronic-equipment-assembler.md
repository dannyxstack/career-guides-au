# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 8212 (NCO2015)**

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers put together components such as wiring, circuit boards, and connectors to manufacture items like switchgear, control panels, and household appliances. Work is typically performed in factories or assembly plants, following detailed diagrams and using hand tools or automated machinery. This is a semi-skilled role common in India's expanding electronics manufacturing sector, driven by government initiatives like 'Make in India' and demand from export-oriented units.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for assemblers typically moves from junior assembler to senior assembler, then to team leader or supervisor. Additional training can open roles in quality control, testing, or maintenance. Some workers transition to production planning or process engineering after gaining experience. The profession offers stable employment in large firms, but upward mobility is limited without further education or specialized certifications.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electrical equipment assembler in India?**
A: Entry-level assemblers earn around ₹10,000-₹20,000 per month, while experienced supervisors can earn up to ₹50,000 per month. Annual salaries range from ₹1.2 lakh to ₹6 lakh.

**Q: Can an electrical assembler get a work visa for India?**
A: Generally no, because the occupation is semi-skilled and salaries are well below the Employment Visa threshold (₹2.1 million/year). Only intra-company transfers for multinational employees might be possible, but it's rare.

**Q: What education is needed to become an electrical assembler?**
A: Minimum Class 10; ITI in Electronics or related field is preferred. Short-term vocational training (PMKVY) can also help.
