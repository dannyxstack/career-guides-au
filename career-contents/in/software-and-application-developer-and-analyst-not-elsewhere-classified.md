# Software and Applications Developers and Analysts Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2519 (NCO2015)**

This occupation covers software developers, programmers, and analysts not classified elsewhere, including roles like full-stack developers, DevOps engineers, and quality assurance analysts. They design, develop, test, and maintain software systems and applications across industries. The role demands strong programming skills, problem-solving abilities, and knowledge of modern development frameworks.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a software developer in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from ₹4-9 lakh per annum. Mid-level (4-7 years) earns ₹10-20 lakh, and senior developers (8+ years) earn ₹22-45 lakh. Top talent in product companies or MNCs can earn significantly more.

**Q: Can I work in India as a foreign software developer?**
A: Yes, via the Employment Visa (E-Visa) if you have a job offer from an Indian company meeting salary threshold (~₹24 lakh/yr). Intra-Company Transfer visas are also common. OCI cards simplify work for persons of Indian origin.

**Q: Is a degree mandatory to become a software developer in India?**
A: While not legally mandatory, a bachelor's degree in computer science or related field is highly preferred. Many employers also accept alternative credentials like diplomas, bootcamp certificates, or demonstrated skill through projects.
