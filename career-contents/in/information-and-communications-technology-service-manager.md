# Information and Communications Technology Services Managers — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 1330 (NCO2015)**

ICT services managers plan, direct, and coordinate the delivery of information technology services within organizations. They oversee teams responsible for network infrastructure, software applications, cybersecurity, and user support, ensuring that IT services align with business goals. In India, this role is critical for digital transformation initiatives across industries, including IT services, banking, manufacturing, and government.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a software developer, systems analyst, or network engineer, advancing to project manager, then ICT services manager. With experience, managers can become IT directors or chief information officers (CIOs). Specialization in cloud architecture, cybersecurity, or data analytics accelerates growth. Certifications like PMP, ITIL, or CISSP are valued. Many professionals transition to consulting or entrepreneurship.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate project progress reports and status updates
- Initial code review and test case generation
- Automated resource scheduling and budget tracking
- Automated diagnosis and repair of common operational issues
- Routine compliance checks and document archiving

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Improving system reliability with AI-driven monitoring tools

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an ICT services manager at a global IT company in India?**
A: At global IT firms (e.g., Accenture, TCS, Infosys), entry-level managers earn ₹6-12 lakh; mid-level ₹12-25 lakh; senior directors can earn ₹25-50 lakh, with top performers exceeding ₹1 crore including bonuses.

**Q: Can a foreign national become an ICT services manager in India?**
A: Yes, but requires employer sponsorship under an Employment Visa (minimum salary ~₹20 lakh/year). The role must be specialist or senior managerial. Intra-company transfers are also possible for multinational employees.

**Q: Is ICT services manager on India's skill shortage list?**
A: While India doesn't maintain a formal shortage list, ICT managers are in high demand, and the government's 'Digital India' initiative supports hiring. However, work visa quotas are not tied to shortage lists.
