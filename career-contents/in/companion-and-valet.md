# Companion and Valet — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 5162 (NCO2015)**

Companions and valets provide personal assistance and companionship to individuals, often in private households, hotels, or for elderly or disabled persons. In India, this occupation is typically informal, with limited formal recognition or regulatory framework. Duties include dressing, grooming, and attending to personal needs, requiring discretion, patience, and interpersonal skills. The role is not typically associated with skilled migration pathways.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression for companions and valets in India is limited due to the informal nature of the work. Entry-level workers may advance to senior personal attendants or household managers within wealthy families or luxury hotels. Some may transition into caregiving roles in healthcare facilities. Formal training is rare, and advancement heavily depends on personal networks and employer recommendations.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in India?**
A: Salaries vary widely. Entry-level workers may earn INR 10,000-20,000 per month, often with accommodation. Experienced valets in high-end households or luxury hotels can earn INR 35,000-60,000 per month. Income is generally low compared to skilled occupations.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a companion or valet in India?**
A: No. India's employment visa requires a minimum annual salary of INR 2,000,000 and specialized skills, which this occupation does not fulfill. There are no applicable visa pathways for this role.

**Q: What training is needed to become a companion or valet?**
A: No formal training is required, but a certificate in personal care assistance or first aid can improve job prospects. On-the-job learning is common.
