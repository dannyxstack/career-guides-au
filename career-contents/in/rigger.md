# Rigger — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7215 (NCO2015)**

Riggers and cable splicers set up and repair rigging for moving heavy equipment on construction sites, ships, and factories, and splice electrical and fiber-optic cables. With India's infrastructure boom, demand is steady in telecom, power, and construction sectors.

Employment for riggers in India is projected to grow with infrastructure projects (smart cities, highways, power grids) and telecom expansion (5G). ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Entry as a helper leads to rigger after 2-3 years of on-job training. Experienced riggers become supervisors or safety inspectors. Cable splicers can advance to telecom line managers. Certifications (e.g., from IL&FS, NIELIT) improve prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Image recognition-based automatic detection of cable wear
- Use drones for overhead cable inspection
- Automatically generate rigging load calculations and configuration plans
- Installation path planning using digital twin simulation
- Automated report generation and compliance document filling.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AR headset displays complex rigging connection steps and safety annotations
- AI real-time analysis of tension data and early warning of potential breakage risks
- Smart wearable devices monitor worker posture and fatigue to prevent occupational injuries
- machine learning-based defect identification and repair guidance for cable joints
- Digital twin platforms enable remote expert collaboration for complex lifting operations

**Human moat:**

- Improvise and erect scaffolding in adverse weather or confined spaces
- Manual slinging and adjustment of non-standard components
- Intuitive experience in judging local damage and repair of old cables
- On-site team safety communication and evacuation decision-making in emergencies
- Interpersonal skills for on-site negotiation of installation plans with clients and engineers

**Skills to build:**

- Skilled in operating drones and robots for high-altitude/underground inspections
- Use AI detection software to analyze cable images and sensor data.
- Interpret 3D models and digital twin platforms (such as BIM).
- Master smart wearables and IoT monitoring systems
- Learning automated rigging design software (e.g., OptiRig)
- Basic programming (Python/R) for data cleaning and report automation

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart Cities
- 5G Telecom
- Solar/Wind Power
- Industrial Automation

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a rigger in India?**
A: Entry-level riggers earn ₹1.5-3 lakh/year, mid-level ₹3-5 lakh/year, and senior supervisors ₹5-8 lakh/year. Salaries are higher in oil & gas and telecom sectors.

**Q: Can a foreign rigger get a work visa for India?**
A: Very difficult. India's Employment Visa requires highly skilled positions with salary ≥₹16.25 lakh/year, which is above typical rigger pay. Intra-company transfers or business visas are possible only for managerial or specialist roles.

**Q: What certifications are needed to work as a rigger in India?**
A: While not mandatory, NCVT certificate in rigging and a CSCS safety card are highly valued. For cable splicing, NIELIT certification is beneficial.
