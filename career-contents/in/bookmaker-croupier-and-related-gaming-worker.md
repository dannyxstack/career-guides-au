# Bookmakers, Croupiers and Related Gaming Workers — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 4212 (NCO2015)**

Bookmakers, croupiers, and related gaming workers operate games of chance and manage betting operations in casinos, online platforms, and gambling establishments. They handle chips, cards, dice, and roulette wheels, ensure fair play, calculate winnings, and interact with customers. These roles are heavily regulated in India, with legal gambling limited to state-run lotteries, horse racing, and a few casinos in Goa, Daman, and Sikkim. Most employment is in physical casinos or offshore online betting firms, while domestic online gambling remains largely illegal.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a croupier in Indian casinos?**
A: Base salary ranges from ₹180,000 to ₹600,000 per year depending on experience, but tips can double earnings, especially in busy Goa casinos. Senior supervisors earn up to ₹1,200,000.

**Q: Can a foreign croupier get a work visa for India?**
A: It is very difficult. India's Employment Visa requires sponsorship, a minimum salary of ~₹2,000,000 (often not met by gaming jobs), and clearance from state gambling authorities. Business Visas are for short-term visits only.

**Q: How competitive is this field in India?**
A: Competition is moderate due to the small number of legal casinos in Goa, Sikkim, and Daman. Many workers come from the local population or neighboring states. However, experienced dealers are always in demand for high-end resorts.
