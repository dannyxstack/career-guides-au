# Cabinet-maker — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7522 (NCO2015)**

Cabinet-makers and related workers cut, shape, and assemble wood and other materials to produce furniture, cabinets, and other wooden products. They operate woodworking machines, interpret blueprints, and apply finishes. In India, the craft is concentrated in small-scale workshops and the unorganized sector, with growing demand for skilled artisans in custom furniture and interior design.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry is typically through informal apprenticeship. Career progression involves becoming a master craftsman, workshop supervisor, or starting one’s own furniture business. Skilled workers may specialize in restoration, modular kitchen fitting, or CNC machining. Networking and word-of-mouth are crucial. Certification from ITIs (Industrial Training Institutes) can improve employability and wages.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom furniture
- Modular kitchen fitting
- Restoration crafts
- CNC woodworking

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cabinet-maker in India?**
A: Entry-level cabinet-makers earn ₹12,000-20,000 per month (₹1.44L-2.4L annually). Mid-level skilled workers earn ₹20,000-35,000/month, and experienced master craftsmen can make ₹35,000-60,000/month. Income varies by location, skill level, and whether self-employed.

**Q: Can a foreign cabinet-maker get a work visa for India?**
A: It is extremely difficult. India's Employment Visa is only for highly skilled professionals earning at least ~₹2.1M per year (USD 25k). Cabinet-makers are typically classified as semi-skilled and do not meet this threshold or specialist criteria. Other visa options (Business, e-Visa) do not permit paid employment.

**Q: What is the career progression for a cabinet-maker in India?**
A: Most start as apprentices under a master craftsman. With 5-10 years of experience, they can become supervisors, start their own workshop, or specialize in high-demand areas like modular kitchens or restoration. Formal ITI certification can help in larger firms or government contracts.
