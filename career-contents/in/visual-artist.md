# Visual Artist — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2651 (NCO2015)**

Visual artists create original artwork using various media such as painting, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, or digital techniques. They may work independently, exhibit in galleries, or take commissions for public and private spaces. In India, the field encompasses traditional art forms, contemporary practice, and commercial art for advertising and design.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for visual artists typically moves from emerging artist to mid-career and established artist, often relying on exhibitions, sales, and grants. Many artists diversify into teaching, curating, or art direction. Digital art and NFT markets are opening new revenue streams, but traditional gallery representation remains important for recognition.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary of a visual artist in India?**
A: Salaries vary widely. Entry-level artists may earn ₹1.5-4 lakh/year, mid-career artists ₹4-12 lakh/year, and established artists ₹8-30 lakh/year. Most artists combine multiple income streams.

**Q: Can foreign visual artists work in India?**
A: Yes, via an Employment Visa sponsored by an Indian employer (gallery, institution) with a minimum salary of ₹16.25 lakh, or a Business Visa for short visits. OCI holders can work freely.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to be a visual artist in India?**
A: A BFA or diploma is common but not mandatory; a strong portfolio is essential. Formal education can help with networking and grants.
