# Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 1312 (NCO2015)**

Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers plan, direct, and coordinate the operations of fish farms, hatcheries, and fishing enterprises. They oversee breeding, feeding, health management, and harvesting of aquatic species. In India, they work in both inland and coastal aquaculture, managing teams and ensuring compliance with environmental and food safety regulations. The role combines business management with technical knowledge of aquaculture systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Progression typically starts as a farm supervisor or technician, moving to assistant manager, then production manager. Further advancement to senior management or entrepreneurship in own aquaculture business is common. Specialization in areas like shrimp farming, seed production, or organic aquaculture can enhance career prospects. Professional certifications and training programs (e.g., from ICAR) are valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually record farming data (water temperature, feeding amounts, etc.)
- Generate daily production reports and compliance documents
- Image recognition of initial disease symptoms
- Inventory counting and feed order calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses sensors and AI to predict optimal feeding times and amounts
- Monitor fish health and abnormal behavior through machine vision.
- Simulate the impact of different stocking densities and feed plans on yield
- Real-time water quality monitoring and automatic adjustment of oxygenation and filtration equipment

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making for responding to sudden disease outbreaks or environmental disasters
- Experience-based biosecurity management and overall disease prevention planning.
- Negotiations and relationship management with regulators, suppliers, and sales channels
- Assess and implement new sustainable aquaculture technologies

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (Python/R)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor system management
- Operation and troubleshooting of automated control systems
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainability certification standards
- Inspection skills for drones or underwater robots

**Key growth areas:**

- Shrimp farming
- Recirculating aquaculture systems
- Integrated multi-trophic aquaculture
- Ornamental fish farming

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Aquaculture Production Manager in India?**
A: Starting salaries for entry-level managers range from INR 3-5 lakh annually. With 5+ years of experience, earnings can reach INR 6-12 lakh. Senior roles in large companies or own businesses can exceed INR 15 lakh.

**Q: Is this role eligible for an Indian Employment Visa?**
A: Generally not, as the salary threshold of USD 25,000 is rarely met for domestic hires. However, foreign professionals with specialized skills (e.g., in shrimp breeding) sponsored by a company may qualify. Most work is for Indian nationals.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become a Fisheries Production Manager?**
A: A Bachelor's degree in Fisheries Science (B.F.Sc.) from an ICAR-accredited university is the minimum requirement. A Master's degree is preferred for advancement. Practical experience through internships or farm work is highly valued.
