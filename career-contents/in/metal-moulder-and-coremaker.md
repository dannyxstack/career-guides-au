# Metal Moulder and Coremaker — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7211 (NCO2015)**

Metal moulders and coremakers make moulds and cores for metal casting by hand or using machines. They are essential in foundries for producing components used in automotive, machinery, and infrastructure industries. The work involves preparing sand moulds, assembling cores, and inspecting castings. While automation is growing, skilled moulders are still needed for complex and high-precision parts.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 151,254. The Indian foundry sector, employing about 2.5 million workers, is undergoing modernisation but demand for manual moulders persists in small and medium units. Growth of automotive and construction sectors supports moderate employment. However, automation and shift to precision casting may reduce demand for low-skill moulders, while skilled coremakers remain needed. Overall, employment is expected to grow slowly with churn due to retirements.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Entry typically via ITI (Industrial Training Institute) or apprenticeship. Experienced moulders can become supervisors or pattern makers. With additional training, transition to foundry technician or quality inspector is possible. Self-employment as jobbing moulder is common in rural clusters. Formal certification (NCVT) improves prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate 3D printing paths for sand moulds/cores using CAD models
- AI vision system detects casting defects in real-time and automatically sorts them
- Robots automatically complete sand mold assembly and pouring operations
- AI optimizes pouring parameters and adjusts cooling system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation software assists in predicting casting shrinkage and porosity locations, guiding process improvements.
- AR headset displays sand core assembly sequence and position calibration
- AI-driven quality analysis system generates defect root cause reports
- The digital twin system monitors the entire casting process in real time and warns of anomalies

**Human moat:**

- Process innovation capability for handling non-standard, small-batch castings
- Skills in manual repair and fine-tuning of complex sand moulds/cores
- On-site decision-making ability for multi-variety quick mold changes.
- Team coordination and safety risk management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Process parameter adjustment for 3D printed sand molds/cores
- Casting simulation software (Magma, ProCAST) operation and result interpretation
- Basic maintenance of automated production lines (robots, PLC)
- Metallurgy and Defect Analysis (metallography, X-ray)
- Data-driven process optimization (statistical process control)
- Basic operations and maintenance of digital twin systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Foundry modernisation
- Green sand moulding
- Core shooting
- Aluminium casting

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a metal moulder in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 1.2-1.8 lakhs per annum (approx. USD 1,500-2,200). Experienced moulders can earn INR 2.4-3.6 lakhs, and senior workers up to INR 6 lakhs, especially in supervisory roles. Overtime and piece-rate work can boost earnings.

**Q: Can a metal moulder immigrate to Canada under the Federal Skilled Trades Program?**
A: Yes, metal moulders are listed under NOC 7211 (eligible for FSTP). Requirements include a valid job offer or Canadian trade certificate, language proficiency (CLB 5), and at least 2 years of experience. No minimum education requirement, but IELTS/CELPIP essential.

**Q: Are metal moulders eligible for the Australia Skilled Occupation List?**
A: Metal moulder (ANZSCO 721112) is not on the core skills list, but may qualify under Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (DAMA) for certain regions. Employer sponsorship (TSS visa) is possible but rare due to low demand.
