# Inquiry Clerk — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 4225 (NCO2015)**

Inquiry clerks respond to queries from customers, clients, or the public, providing information about products, services, policies, or procedures. They work in call centers, government offices, utilities, and private companies, handling phone, email, or in-person inquiries. The role requires strong communication skills, patience, and basic computer proficiency.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from inquiry clerk to senior customer service representative, team leader, or quality analyst. Upskilling in CRM software, data entry, or specialized product knowledge can lead to supervisor roles. Lateral moves to sales or support functions are common. Certification in customer service (e.g., from NASSCOM) can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Handle customer inquiries for general information about products or services
- Provides standardized process guidelines and frequently asked questions
- Records and transfers simple complaints to relevant departments
- Update and maintain customer information databases.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI real-time knowledge bases to quickly retrieve answers to complex questions.
- Using sentiment analysis tools to identify customer emotions and adjust communication strategies
- Optimize multilingual customer communication using AI-assisted scripts
- Use automatic summarization tools to quickly record call key points

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly complex or emotionally charged customer complaints
- Creative problem-solving in uncertain situations
- building long-term customer trust and personalised service
- Cross-departmental coordination and internal process optimization.

**Skills to build:**

- Emotional intelligence and conflict resolution skills
- AI customer service system operation and tuning
- Data analysis basics (customer trend identification).
- Multilingual ability (for serving international clients)
- Advanced communication and negotiation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce support
- Government helplines
- Banking/call centers
- Healthcare inquiry

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an inquiry clerk in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range ₹1.8-3 LPA, with mid-level reaching ₹3-5 LPA. Senior roles or managerial positions can earn up to ₹8 LPA. Location and industry affect pay; metro cities and banking/IT offer higher wages.

**Q: Can inquiry clerks migrate to countries like the EU or Canada?**
A: Migration is difficult; the role is not listed as high-skilled. India's Employment Visa requires high salary (₹20L+) for foreign workers, not applicable. For Indian clerks seeking abroad, intra-company transfer or study pathways are more viable.
