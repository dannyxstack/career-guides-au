# Plasterer — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7123 (NCO2015)**

Plasterers apply plaster, cement, stucco, or similar materials to interior and exterior walls and ceilings to create a smooth, decorative, or textured finish. They also install and repair lathing, fix plasterboard, and apply insulating or fireproofing coatings. In India, plasterers are essential in construction, particularly in residential and commercial building projects, where they prepare surfaces, mix materials, and use tools like trowels and floats to achieve a quality finish. The occupation requires physical stamina, attention to detail, and knowledge of material properties.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for plasterers typically starts as a helper, advancing to semi-skilled and then master plasterer with 3-5 years of experience. Further advancement can lead to foreman, site supervisor, or self-employment as a contractor. Specialising in decorative plastering, stucco, or fireproofing opens niche opportunities. Formal certification through the Directorate General of Employment (DGE) or state ITIs enhances credibility and pay.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive wall construction (e.g., straight walls, standard brick walls) is taken over by bricklaying robots or automatic laying equipment.
- Material handling and mortar mixing via automated conveyor systems
- Design and layout of simple masonry structures are automatically generated by BIM software
- On-site measurement and positioning using laser scanners and AI correction, reducing manual work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI visual inspection system assists in checking masonry verticality and mortar joint uniformity, improving quality inspection efficiency
- BIM modeling tools help bricklayers precisely lay bricks in complex curved structures
- Augmented reality (AR) glasses overlay construction drawings, reducing reading errors.
- AI brick layout algorithms optimize material usage and reduce waste

**Human moat:**

- Complex irregular masonry (curved, hollow, historical building restoration) cannot be standardized
- Flexible operation in unstructured environments such as bad weather and confined spaces
- Non-standard requirements from owners or architects for aesthetics and texture, relying on craftsman experience
- On-site safety management and emergency judgment, e.g., support, temporary fixing risks

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated bricklaying equipment and collaborating with robots
- Proficiency in BIM modeling and CAD drawing interpretation.
- Learn drone surveying and laser scanning data analysis
- Proficient in green building and energy-efficient masonry material construction
- Improve project management and multi-trade coordination skills
- Familiarity with building regulations and accessible masonry standards

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban housing
- Infrastructure projects
- Renovation work
- Decorative finishes

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary of a plasterer in India?**
A: Entry-level plasterers earn about INR 14,000-20,000 per month, mid-level INR 20,000-30,000, and senior/master plasterers INR 30,000-50,000 or more, depending on location and project.

**Q: Can an Indian plasterer get a work visa to the EU or USA?**
A: Permanent migration for plasterers is very difficult due to strict skilled visa requirements (high salary thresholds, degrees). Short-term work visas are possible in Gulf countries via employer sponsorship, but not in high-skill migration schemes.

**Q: What are the best locations for plasterer jobs in India?**
A: Major metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Chennai) and rapidly urbanising tier-2 cities offer the highest demand. Infrastructure projects under schemes like Smart Cities Mission also generate work in smaller towns.
