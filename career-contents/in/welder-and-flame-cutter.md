# Welder and Flame Cutter — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7212 (NCO2015)**

Welders and flame cutters join or cut metal parts using various welding and cutting equipment. In India, they work in manufacturing, construction, shipbuilding, and repair industries. The role requires precision, safety awareness, and increasingly, certification under the Indian Standard (IS) codes. While the job is in demand for infrastructure projects, salaries vary widely by skill level and sector.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Entry-level workers typically start as helpers or assistants. With experience and certification, they can become certified welders, supervisors, or quality inspectors. Progression to welding engineer or instructor requires further education. Specialization in underwater welding or pipe welding can increase earning potential. Many welders move to Gulf countries for higher salaries.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Batch standardised metal parts automatic welding (e.g., car body welding, pipe joints)
- Robot programming and path planning based on CAD models replace manual teaching
- Online weld quality inspection (visual inspection + AI defect detection)
- Automatic adjustment of welding parameters and welding process monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted welding parameter optimization (real-time recommendation of current/voltage based on material thickness and ambient temperature)
- Augmented reality (AR) welding guidance system improves novice training efficiency
- Drones/crawler robots equipped with welding modules for high-altitude or hazardous welding
- Digital twin welding simulation reduces material waste from test pieces
- AI-driven multi-robot collaborative welding group control

**Human moat:**

- On-site manual welding of non-standard workpieces and confined spaces (e.g., on ships, inside pipes)
- Welding of pressure vessels and load-bearing structures with high stress and fatigue life requirements
- Welding process development for dissimilar metals and special alloys (titanium, aluminum-magnesium alloys)
- Welding quality responsibility determination and repair decisions

**Skills to build:**

- Programming and debugging of welding robots (e.g., FANUC, KUKA, ABB)
- Welding process simulation software (e.g., Simufact Welding, SYSWELD)
- Automated welding quality control and testing methods
- Digital manufacturing basics (CAD/CAM, BOM management)
- Advanced certifications such as International Welding Engineer (IWE)
- Operation of AR/VR welding simulation training systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Manufacturing
- Oil & Gas
- Shipbuilding

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a certified welder in India?**
A: A certified welder in India can earn between ₹300,000 to ₹480,000 per year with 4-7 years of experience, while senior welders or supervisors earn ₹480,000 to ₹720,000.

**Q: Can foreign welders get an Indian work visa?**
A: It is rare. The Employment Visa requires a specialized skill and salary threshold (~₹2M/year), which is high for welders. Most foreign welders enter on Business Visas for short-term projects.

**Q: What is the career progression for a welder in India?**
A: A welder can progress from apprentice to certified welder, then to supervisor, quality inspector, or instructor. Specializing in pipe welding or underwater welding can boost earnings.
