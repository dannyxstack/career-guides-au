# Business Services Agent Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3339 (NCO2015)**

Business Services Agents Not Elsewhere Classified (NEC) represent a diverse group of professionals who facilitate various business transactions and services, including real estate, intellectual property, and other commercial activities not covered by specific agent categories. They act as intermediaries, negotiators, or consultants, typically requiring strong communication and sales skills. In India, these agents are found in sectors such as real estate, insurance, and financial services, often working on commission-based models.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior agent or associate, advancing to senior agent or team leader with experience and a client base. Specialization in high-demand areas like commercial real estate or patent brokerage can accelerate growth. Many agents become self-employed or start their own agencies. Professional certifications and networking are key for advancement.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a business services agent in India?**
A: Entry-level agents earn around INR 2-4 lakh/year, but top performers in real estate or insurance can exceed INR 10 lakh/year. Income is highly variable due to commissions.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as business services agents in India?**
A: Yes, but it's restricted. They need an Employment Visa with a salary above INR 16.25 lakh/year and employer sponsorship. Business agents typically work on commission, making it hard to meet the salary threshold unless highly experienced.
