# Garment and Related Patternmakers and Cutters — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7532 (NCO2015)**

Garment and related patternmakers and cutters create patterns and cut fabric, leather, or other materials for apparel, upholstery, and related products. They interpret designs, draft patterns, and operate cutting machines to produce garment components. In India, this occupation is concentrated in the textile and apparel manufacturing hubs of Tiruppur, Ludhiana, and Bengaluru, serving both domestic and export markets.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Entry-level workers typically start as cutting assistants or marker makers. With experience, they can advance to senior cutter, pattern master, or supervisor roles. Further progression includes production manager or quality controller in large factories. Upskilling in CAD-based pattern making (e.g., Gerber, Lectra) enhances career mobility. Some experienced workers become entrepreneurs running small tailoring or garment units.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Technical textiles
- Export-oriented units
- Fast fashion retail
- Sustainable fashion

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a garment cutter in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 120,000 to 240,000 per year. Experienced cutters in export units can earn up to INR 420,000, while supervisors may earn INR 720,000 annually.

**Q: Can a garment cutter migrate to India for work?**
A: Skilled migration is not common for this occupation as the salary threshold for an Employment Visa (min INR 20.8 lakh/year) is rarely met. Intra-company transfers are possible for senior roles in multinational firms but are uncommon.
