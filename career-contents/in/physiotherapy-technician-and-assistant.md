# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3255 (NCO2015)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants support physiotherapists in delivering rehabilitative care, including exercise therapy, electrotherapy, and patient mobility training. They work in hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centers, and sports facilities across India, helping patients recover from injuries, surgeries, or chronic conditions. The role is growing due to increased healthcare access and an aging population.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level assistants often start with a diploma and gain experience in hospitals. Career progression includes senior assistant roles, specialization in sports or neuro-physiotherapy, or pursuing a degree to become a physiotherapist. Some move into administrative roles or start own clinics.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patient treatment progress recording and report writing
- Basic vital signs monitoring and data entry
- Setup and calibration of routine treatment equipment
- Appointment scheduling and patient file management.
- Simple Rehabilitation Exercise Guidance (Standardized Movements)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans
- Balance and coordination training assisted by virtual reality (VR)
- Use wearable devices to monitor patient progress in real time and adjust intensity
- AI-assisted generation of treatment plan documents and patient education materials
- Smart voice assistant for patient communication and follow-up

**Human moat:**

- Manual therapy techniques (e.g., massage, joint mobilization)
- Empathy to assess patient mental state and pain
- Clinical judgment in adjusting treatment based on nonverbal cues
- Build trust with patients and motivate them to adhere to rehabilitation
- Handling emergencies (e.g., patient falls or discomfort)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) system operation.
- Virtual reality rehabilitation technology applications
- Patient communication and behavior change coaching
- Wearable device data interpretation.
- Basic programming (e.g., Python scripts for data processing)

**Key growth areas:**

- Geriatric care
- Sports injuries
- Tele-rehabilitation
- Preventive physiotherapy

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a physiotherapy assistant in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 150,000 to 360,000 per year, with mid-level earners making INR 360,000-600,000. Senior assistants can earn up to INR 900,000, especially in metro cities.

**Q: Can a physiotherapy assistant migrate to India for work?**
A: Inbound migration is very restrictive. The Employment Visa requires a job offer from a Indian employer and a minimum salary of about INR 1.5 million, which is far above typical assistant salaries. Alternatives include OCI/PIO for persons of Indian origin, but most assistants are Indian nationals.
