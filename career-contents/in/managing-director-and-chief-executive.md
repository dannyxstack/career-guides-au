# Managing Directors and Chief Executives — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 1120 (NCO2015)**

Managing Directors and Chief Executives formulate and review the policies and plans of enterprises, government departments, and other organizations, direct and control their activities, and represent the organization at official occasions and in negotiations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving up through general management roles, often requiring an MBA or equivalent experience. Many executives have backgrounds in finance, marketing, or operations. Networking and a proven track record of delivering results are crucial. Board directorships and advisory roles are common later in careers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate routine operational reports and data analysis
- Preliminary screening and summarization of large amounts of information

- Basic financial analysis and forecasting
- Preliminary review of compliance documents

- Risk management: AI predicts potential operational or financial risks

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Chief Executive in India?**
A: Salaries vary widely; for large companies, CEOs earn INR 1-5 crore annually including bonuses, while smaller firms may pay INR 20-50 lakhs.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a Managing Director in India?**
A: Yes, but requires an Employment Visa with a sponsoring company. The salary must meet thresholds (~INR 20 lakhs/year). An Intra-Company Transfer visa may be used for internal moves.

**Q: What is the typical career path to become a Chief Executive?**
A: Most CEOs have an MBA, with over 15 years of experience in senior management roles, often across functions like finance, marketing, or operations.
