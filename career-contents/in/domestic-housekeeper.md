# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 5152 (NCO2015)**

Domestic housekeepers in India are primarily employed in private households, hotels, and residential facilities to perform cleaning, organising, and basic maintenance tasks. The role is largely informal, with most workers lacking formal contracts or social security. It is a low-skilled occupation with minimal educational requirements, dominated by women and migrant labour from rural areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is minimal; most workers remain in the same role. A few may advance to supervisory positions in large households or hotels, or specialise in tasks like cooking or childcare. Formal training and certification through government skilling programmes (e.g., NSDC) can improve employability but are not widely adopted.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Vacuuming/mopping floors (replaced by robotic vacuums/mops)
- Dishwashing (replaced by dishwashers)
- Bed making, clothing folding (replaced by smart bed-making robots/folding machines)
- Regular surface dust removal (replaced by automated dusting robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling tools to plan cleaning routes and task priorities
- Monitoring appliance status and scheduling repairs using smart home systems
- Remote control of cleaning equipment via voice assistant or APP
- Use data analytics to optimize cleaning supplies inventory and procurement

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex cleaning issues such as accidental damage and special material stains
- Communicate with employers about customized service needs and build trust
- Manage multiple smart devices and coordinate troubleshooting.
- Engaging in fine manual work (e.g., antique furniture maintenance)

**Skills to build:**

- Smart home device operation and basic troubleshooting
- Basic data analysis (supply inventory management)
- Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Time management and multitasking coordination
- Knowledge of eco-friendly cleaning technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- Urbanisation
- Dual-income households
- Ageing population
- Hospitality sector expansion

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a domestic housekeeper in India?**
A: The average monthly salary ranges from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000, with significant variation by city, employer type, and whether accommodation/meals are provided.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a domestic housekeeper in India?**
A: No, immigration rules restrict Employment Visas to skilled professionals (minimum salary ₹25 lakh/year). Domestic housekeeping is unskilled and ineligible, except for diplomats' household staff under special provisions.

**Q: Are there any formal training programmes for domestic housekeepers?**
A: Yes, the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) offers a 'Domestic Housekeeping' course (about 3 months) covering cleaning, hygiene, and basic home management. It is voluntary but can improve job prospects.
