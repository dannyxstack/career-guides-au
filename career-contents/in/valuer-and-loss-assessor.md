# Valuers and Loss Assessors — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3315 (NCO2015)**

Valuers and loss assessors evaluate property, land, machinery, and other assets to determine their value for insurance, sale, taxation, or legal purposes. In India, they are regulated by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDAI) and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. They work for insurance companies, banks, government agencies, or as independent consultants. The role requires analytical skills, attention to detail, and knowledge of market trends.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a trainee or junior assessor, advancing to senior assessor, then team lead or manager. Experienced professionals may specialize in areas like real estate, machinery, or agricultural assets. Many obtain certifications like IRDAI's 'Licensed Surveyor and Loss Assessor.' Entrepreneurship is common, with senior professionals starting their own firms. Continuous learning of valuation standards (e.g., RICS, IVS) is essential.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary data collation and analysis for automated property valuation reports
- Standard loss amount calculation based on historical data
- Online image assessment for minor vehicle damage
- Automatic Classification and Entry of Insurance Claim Documents
- Input parameters for conventional real estate valuation models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to identify anomalies and patterns in loss images.
- Leverage big data analytics to predict market value trends
- AI-assisted rapid generation of complex assessment report drafts
- Using natural language processing to automatically handle customer inquiries
- Improve valuation accuracy through machine learning models

**Human moat:**

- Legal and ethical compliance responsibilities
- Client trust building and communication negotiation

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a valuer in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from ₹1.8 to ₹4 lakh per annum, depending on the employer and location. With an IRDAI license, earnings can increase.

**Q: Can a foreign valuer work in India?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. An Employment Visa is possible if offered a role by an Indian employer with a salary above USD 25,000/year. You must also obtain an IRDAI license and meet experience requirements. OCI holders can work without a visa.
