# Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9212 (NCO2015)**

Livestock farm labourers perform routine tasks on farms such as feeding, watering, and cleaning livestock; they assist with basic animal care, maintain farm hygiene, and handle farm equipment. This occupation is part of India's large agricultural workforce, which employs a significant portion of the rural population, mostly in subsistence or small-scale commercial farming.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 6,387,022. India's agricultural sector is undergoing slow mechanisation, but livestock farming remains labour-intensive, particularly in dairy and poultry. Employment outlook is stable due to rising demand for milk, meat, and eggs. However, wages are low and job security is limited, with many workers in informal arrangements. Government schemes like dairy cooperatives may offer some support, but overall growth is modest.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression is limited; workers may move to larger farms or become supervisors after years of experience. Some transition to allied sectors like animal feed sales or veterinary support. Formal training is rare, but skill certification through programmes like the PMKVY can improve job prospects. However, upward mobility is constrained by low literacy and lack of formal recognition.

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals from rural backgrounds with animal handling experience.
- Those comfortable with physical labour and irregular hours.
- People with minimal formal education seeking immediate employment.

**Who is not suited**
- Persons seeking high income or career advancement.
- Those preferring urban or desk-based work.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly wage for a livestock farm labourer in India?**
A: Monthly earnings range from INR 6,000 to INR 15,000 depending on region and farm type, often below minimum wage.

**Q: Can a livestock farm labourer migrate to the UK or Canada?**
A: Generally no, as this occupation is not considered skilled. Canada's agriculture stream may accept workers but requires employer-specific LMIA.
