# Fumigator and Pest Controller — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7544 (NCO2015)**

Fumigators and pest controllers apply chemical or biological treatments to eliminate or control pests such as insects, rodents, and weeds in buildings, farms, and other sites. They inspect premises, identify infestations, select appropriate pesticides, and operate fumigation equipment while adhering to safety protocols. In India, this occupation spans agricultural pest control, urban pest management in commercial and residential settings, and specialized fumigation for export goods. Workers may be employed by pest control firms, agricultural cooperatives, or government agencies, and often require certification from the Central Insecticides Board.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a field technician applying treatments, moving to team leader, then supervisor managing multiple sites. With additional training, one can become a certified fumigator (for export goods) or pest management consultant. Further education (B.Sc. in agriculture or entomology) enables roles in research, product development, or regulatory compliance. Self-employment by starting a small pest control business is common. Certification through the Pest Control Association of India (PCAI) enhances prospects.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can I earn a good income as a pest controller in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries are low (INR 10,000-20,000/month), but experienced certified fumigators or business owners can earn INR 35,000-60,000/month. However, the majority work informally.

**Q: Can I migrate to India as a fumigator?**
A: Skilled migration as a fumigator is extremely unlikely. India's Employment Visa requires employer sponsorship and a high salary threshold (INR 16.25 lakh/year), which pest controllers rarely meet. Business or e-visas are only for short stays.
