# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7223 (NCO2015)**

Metal working machine tool setters and operators set up and operate various machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, drilling machines, grinding machines, and CNC machines to cut, shape, or finish metal workpieces. They read blueprints, set cutting speeds and feeds, select cutting tools, and perform routine maintenance. This occupation is critical in India's manufacturing sector, particularly in automotive, aerospace, and heavy engineering industries. Workers typically learn through industrial training institutes (ITIs) or on-the-job training, with opportunities in both organized and unorganized sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a machine operator, advancing to senior operator, then to setter/programmer, and eventually supervisor or maintenance technician. Upskilling to CNC operation and programming (e.g., G-code, CAM software) significantly improves prospects. Some move into quality inspection or production planning. With experience, self-employment through small workshops is possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC Machining
- Automation & Robotics
- Precision Engineering
- Automobile Manufacturing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a CNC operator in India?**
A: A CNC operator with 3-5 years experience earns between ₹2.5-4 lakh per annum, depending on location and industry. Salaries are higher in automotive hubs like Pune and Chennai.

**Q: Can a metal worker move to India under an Employment Visa?**
A: India's Employment Visa requires a minimum salary of ₹25 lakh per annum, which is far above typical earnings for this occupation. Migration is unlikely unless the worker is highly specialized (e.g., CNC programmer) and sponsored by a multinational.

**Q: What certifications are most valued?**
A: NCVT ITI certification is standard. Additional CNC programming certificates (e.g., from Siemens or FANUC) and diploma in mechanical engineering boost prospects.
