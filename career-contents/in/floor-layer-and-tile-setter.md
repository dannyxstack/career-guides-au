# Floor Layers and Tile Setters — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7122 (NCO2015)**

Floor layers and tile setters install and repair various types of flooring, including ceramic, marble, granite, and vinyl tiles, as well as wooden and laminate floors. They work on residential, commercial, and industrial projects, ensuring surfaces are level, durable, and aesthetically pleasing. Tasks include preparing subfloors, cutting and fitting materials, applying adhesives, and finishing edges. This occupation requires physical stamina, precision, and knowledge of different materials and installation techniques.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or apprentice, advancing to skilled tradesperson. Experienced workers can become supervisors, team leaders, or start their own contracting businesses. Specialization in high-end materials (e.g., marble, engineered wood) or techniques (e.g., waterproofing, underfloor heating) can lead to higher pay. Certification from government ITIs or private institutes can improve job prospects. Some migrate to Gulf countries or other regions for better wages.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic measurement of room dimensions and material quantity calculation, AI can quickly generate optimal layout plans
- Automatic cutting of tiles/stone, CNC waterjet or laser cutting replaces manual cutting
- Automatic detection and adjustment solution generation for ground flatness, reducing manual trial laying
- Inventory management and material ordering processes, AI analyzes project requirements to automatically place orders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to view tiling blueprints and level indicators in real time, improving accuracy
- AI-assisted design of complex patterns and parquet, generating diverse options for clients
- Work collaboratively with automatic mortar mixing and plastering robots to improve efficiency
- AI-driven project progress prediction and scheduling to optimize multi-trade collaboration

**Human moat:**

- Manual cutting and fine placement in irregular areas and corners
- Removal of old flooring, base treatment, and leveling judgment for uneven floors.
- Communicate design preferences with clients and provide personalized advice
- Flexible problem-solving ability for on-site unexpected issues.

**Skills to build:**

- Digital Measurement Tools and BIM Software Operations
- CNC cutting machine programming and maintenance
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools for construction assistance.
- Project management and client communication skills
- Knowledge of sustainable materials and environmentally friendly construction methods
- Basic electrical and mechanical troubleshooting

**Key growth areas:**

- Residential construction
- Commercial real estate
- Infrastructure projects
- Renovation and retrofitting

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a tile setter in India?**
A: Average salary ranges from INR 1.5 to 4 lakh per year, depending on experience and location. Daily wages vary from INR 400-800 in smaller towns to INR 800-1500 in metro cities.

**Q: Can floor layers migrate to work abroad from India?**
A: Yes, skilled floor layers often migrate to Gulf countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar) under employer-sponsored work visas. However, meeting the salary threshold for skilled migration to developed countries (e.g., UK, Canada) is difficult due to lower wages in India. Intra-company transfers or business visas are rare for this occupation.
