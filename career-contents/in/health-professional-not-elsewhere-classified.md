# Health Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2269 (NCO2015)**

Health Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified (ISCO 2269) encompass a diverse range of allied health specialists such as audiologists, speech therapists, dietitians, and occupational therapists who provide therapeutic, diagnostic, and preventive healthcare services. They work in hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centers, and community health settings, often collaborating with physicians and nurses. In India, this occupation is growing due to increased awareness of mental health, aging population, and lifestyle diseases, though formal recognition and regulation vary by specialty. Salaries span widely based on sector (public vs. private) and experience, with urban demand higher than rural.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression often starts with junior therapist/dietitian roles, advancing to senior clinician, department head, or private practice. Many pursue postgraduate specialization (e.g., in pediatric oncology or neuro-rehabilitation) for higher earnings. Alternative pathways include healthcare management, academics, or medical writing. The trend towards multi-disciplinary teams and digital health creates opportunities for tech-savvy professionals.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate nutrition assessment reports and meal plans
- Preliminary analysis and classification of hearing test results
- Standard pronunciation practice and feedback in speech therapy
- Medical documentation organisation and medical summary generation
- Preliminary screening and sorting of some diagnostic information

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic tools improve accuracy of hearing and speech disorder identification

- Voice synthesis and recognition technology enhances language therapy interaction.
- AI-driven dynamic adjustment of rehabilitation plans
- Big data analytics supports epidemiology and efficacy studies

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a health professional in India?**
A: For mid-level (4-8 yrs) allied health professionals, average salary ranges from INR 600,000 to 1,200,000 annually, varying by specialty and location. Private practitioners can earn more after establishing a client base.

**Q: Can I migrate to India as a health professional?**
A: Yes, but limited. The Employment Visa requires employer sponsorship and a minimum salary of ~INR 1.8 million per year for specialists. Persons of Indian origin can use the OCI card. Most allied health roles do not qualify unless highly specialized.
