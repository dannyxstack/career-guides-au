# Window Cleaner — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9123 (NCO2015)**

Window cleaners clean windows, glass surfaces, and related fixtures in residential, commercial, and high-rise buildings. In India, this occupation is informal and low-skilled, with no formal certification requirements. Work is often seasonal and physically demanding, with limited career progression and low wages. The role is not a migration route as it does not meet India's skill thresholds for work visas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression is minimal; window cleaners often remain in the same role or move to general cleaning services. Some may start their own small businesses, but earnings remain low. Formal training is absent; most learn on the job. There is no clear pathway to supervisory or managerial roles without additional skills.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a window cleaner in India?**
A: Most window cleaners earn between ₹60,000 and ₹1,20,000 per year. Experienced cleaners in commercial buildings can earn up to ₹2,40,000 per year. Income is highly variable due to informal work.

**Q: Can I migrate to India as a window cleaner?**
A: No. India's employment visas require specialized skills and a minimum salary of ₹2.5 million annually, which window cleaners do not meet. The occupation is not eligible for any work visa category.

**Q: Is there any career progression for window cleaners in India?**
A: Very limited. With experience, one can become a team lead or start a small cleaning service, but earnings remain low. Formal advancement requires additional skills or training.
