# Professional Services Manager — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 1349 (NCO2015)**

Professional Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified oversee and coordinate the delivery of professional services such as consulting, legal, accounting, or engineering services within an organization. They are responsible for managing teams, ensuring client satisfaction, meeting financial targets, and developing business strategies. This role is typical in multinational corporations, consulting firms, and large Indian enterprises, requiring a blend of business acumen and industry-specific expertise.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a specialized role (e.g., consultant, analyst) to team leader, then to manager, senior manager, and director. Many professionals gain experience abroad or in top Indian firms before moving into management. Lateral moves between industries are common. Advanced degrees like an MBA or professional certifications (e.g., CFA, PMP) accelerate progression. The path is competitive but rewarding, with top performers reaching senior leadership within 10-15 years.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Professional Services Manager in India?**
A: Salaries range from ₹6-12 lakhs for entry-level (0-3 years) to ₹40-90 lakhs for senior roles (10+ years), with top consulting firms paying at the higher end.

**Q: Can a foreign professional work as a Professional Services Manager in India?**
A: Yes, via an Employment Visa for roles requiring specialist skills, typically with a salary above ₹20 lakh per year. OCI cardholders can work without restrictions.

**Q: What are the best industries for this role in India?**
A: Management consulting, IT services, legal firms, and financial services are top employers, with high demand in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Hyderabad.
