# Midwifery Professional — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2222 (NCO2015)**

Midwifery professionals in India provide essential maternal and newborn care, including antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal services. They work in hospitals, clinics, and community health centers, often in rural areas. The role requires registration with the Indian Nursing Council (INC) and state nursing councils.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 14,993. India's midwifery workforce is growing due to government initiatives like the 'Midwifery Initiative' to reduce maternal and infant mortality. However, a severe shortage of skilled professionals persists, especially in rural areas. Employment outlook is positive, with increased hiring in public health programs and private hospitals, driven by rising birth rates and healthcare expansion.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwifery professional in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from ₹2.4 to ₹3.6 lakh per year, while experienced midwives can earn up to ₹10 lakh annually in urban private hospitals. Government jobs often have lower base pay but include housing, medical, and pension benefits.

**Q: Can foreign midwives work in India?**
A: Yes, but it is restrictive. Foreign midwives must register with the Indian Nursing Council and obtain an Employment Visa, which requires a job offer and minimum salary of around ₹16.25 lakh (approx USD 20,000). The process is lengthy and typically only feasible for specialists in high-demand areas.
