# Glazier — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7125 (NCO2015)**

Glaziers cut, install, and replace glass in windows, doors, storefronts, and other structures. In India, this trade is primarily concentrated in urban construction and automotive sectors, with demand driven by real estate development and infrastructure projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Entry typically begins as a helper or apprentice under a senior glazier, progressing to a skilled glazier, then supervisor or contractor. Specialization in decorative glass, structural glazing, or automotive glass can enhance career prospects. Self-employment as a small contractor is common after gaining experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban Real Estate
- Infrastructure
- Retail Fit-Outs
- Automotive Glass

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Hands-on workers who enjoy precision and physical tasks.
- Individuals comfortable with heights and working in diverse settings.

**Who is not suited**
- People seeking desk jobs or minimal physical labor.
- Those averse to risk of cuts or heavy lifting.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a glazier in India?**
A: Average annual salary ranges from ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh, depending on experience and location. Metro cities pay higher.

**Q: Can a foreign glazier easily get a work visa for India?**
A: Generally no. Glaziers are not considered high-skilled under India's Employment Visa rules, which require specialized skills and a minimum salary of ~₹20 lakh per year. Only senior technicians or engineers in structural glass may qualify.
