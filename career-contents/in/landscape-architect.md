# Landscape Architect — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2162 (NCO2015)**

Landscape architects plan, design, and oversee the development of outdoor spaces such as parks, gardens, campuses, and urban infrastructure, integrating environmental sustainability with aesthetic and functional requirements. In India, they work for consultancies, government agencies, or as independent professionals, often collaborating with architects and urban planners.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry is via a Bachelor's in Landscape Architecture (B.Arch/B.L.A.) followed by registration with the Council of Architecture (CoA). Career progression leads to senior designer (8-10 yrs), project manager, or partner at a firm. Specializations in ecological restoration, urban design, or landscape planning offer differentiation.

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a landscape architect in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range ₹3.5-6 lakh per year, mid-level ₹7-12 lakh, and senior professionals can earn ₹14-25 lakh annually.

**Q: Can foreign landscape architects work in India?**
A: Yes, but require an Employment Visa with a sponsoring Indian employer; the salary must meet the threshold of ₹25 lakh per annum. Alternatively, OCI holders can work freely.

**Q: Is registration with the Council of Architecture mandatory?**
A: Yes, to practice as a landscape architect in India, you must register with the CoA after completing a recognized degree.
