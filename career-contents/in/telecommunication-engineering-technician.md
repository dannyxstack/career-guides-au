# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3522 (NCO2015)**

Telecommunications engineering technicians install, maintain, and repair telecommunications equipment and systems, including telephone lines, fiber optics, and wireless networks. They work under the guidance of engineers to ensure reliable communication services. In India, they are crucial for expanding digital infrastructure, supporting 4G/5G rollouts, and rural connectivity projects. The role involves fieldwork, troubleshooting, and testing equipment.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a telecommunications engineering technician in India?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn ₹1.5-3 lakh/year, mid-level ₹3-5 lakh/year, and senior technicians ₹5-8 lakh/year. Metro cities and specialized skills can push it higher.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as telecommunications technicians in India?**
A: Yes, but mainly through an employer-sponsored Employment Visa, which requires a high salary threshold (~₹25 lakh/year). OCI cardholders have more flexibility. For most, it's a restricted route.

**Q: What education is needed to become a telecom technician in India?**
A: A minimum ITI certificate in Electronics/Telecom (1-2 years) or a Diploma in Electronics and Communication (3 years) is common. Short-term vocational courses are also available.
