# Shelf Filler — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9334 (NCO2015)**

Shelf fillers, also known as stock clerks or merchandisers, are responsible for restocking shelves, organizing products, and ensuring that store shelves are fully stocked and visually appealing. This role is common in retail environments such as supermarkets, convenience stores, and department stores in India.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for shelf fillers is limited but possible. With experience, one may move into supervisory roles like team leader or department manager. Alternatively, skills in inventory management can lead to logistics or supply chain roles. Many workers view it as a temporary job while pursuing further education or other career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of retail automation systems
- Inventory data analysis and forecasting tools
- Operation of equipment such as forklifts and electric pallet jacks
- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling
- Basic Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Cross-functional team collaboration and coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- organized retail expansion
- e-commerce fulfillment
- evening/weekend shifts
- urban centers

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in India?**
A: Entry-level shelf fillers earn between ₹1.2 to ₹1.8 lakhs per year. Salaries increase modestly with experience, reaching up to ₹3.6 lakhs for supervisory roles.

**Q: Can shelf fillers get a work visa to work abroad?**
A: Shelf filler is not a skilled occupation and typically does not qualify for work visas in countries like the US, UK, or Canada, which require higher skill levels or education.
