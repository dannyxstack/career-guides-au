# Building Construction Labourer — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9313 (NCO2015)**

Building construction labourers perform a variety of manual tasks on construction sites, such as digging trenches, mixing and pouring concrete, carrying materials, and operating basic tools. This occupation is a major source of employment for low-skilled workers in India, particularly in urban areas with ongoing infrastructure and real estate development. The work is physically demanding and often seasonal, with wages determined by minimum wage laws and local market conditions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically moves from labourer to specialised trades such as masonry, carpentry, or plastering, often through on-the-job training or short certificate courses. With experience, workers can become gang supervisors or site foremen. Further advancement to contractor or project manager roles is possible but typically requires formal education in civil engineering or construction management. Most workers remain in labourer roles due to lack of upskilling opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Moving construction materials (replaced by AGVs/drones).
- Basic excavation and earthworks (replaced by unmanned excavators)
- On-site cleaning and debris sorting (replaced by cleaning robots)
- Simple bricklaying and plastering (replaced by automatic brick-laying machines/spraying machines)
- Repetitive rebar tying (replaced by tying robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to read construction drawings, improving accuracy.
- Operate semi-automated tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers, laser levels)
- Maintenance and debugging of automated equipment (e.g., drones, robots)
- Report progress and receive instructions via digital platforms
- Using BIM software for collaborative work and problem-solving

**Human moat:**

- Judgment in unstructured environments (e.g., complex terrain, unexpected situations)
- Fine hand-eye coordination (e.g., installing irregular components).
- Team on-site communication and emergency coordination
- Experience in safety supervision and accident prevention
- Mastery of traditional crafts (e.g., stonemasonry, special plastering)

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated construction equipment (e.g., drones, robots)
- Reading digital drawings and BIM basics
- Basic programming or equipment debugging skills
- Enhanced safety and quality management knowledge
- Knowledge of electrical/hydraulic system maintenance
- English or digital tools usage

**Key growth areas:**

- Urbanisation
- Infrastructure Investment
- Affordable Housing
- Smart Cities

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average daily wage for a building construction labourer in India?**
A: Daily wages vary by state and city, typically ranging from ₹400 to ₹800 per day. Metro cities like Mumbai or Delhi may pay higher, up to ₹1,000 per day for skilled labourers.

**Q: Can foreign building labourers get a visa to work in India?**
A: No, India's Employment Visa requires a minimum salary of USD 25,000/year and is intended for professionals. Manual labour jobs like construction labourer do not qualify. Foreign workers cannot legally take up such roles.

**Q: What are the typical working hours for construction labourers in India?**
A: Standard working hours are 8-10 hours per day, 6 days a week. Overtime is common, paid at 1.5-2 times the regular wage. Conditions vary by employer.
