# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9412 (NCO2015)**

Kitchen helpers in India assist cooks and chefs with food preparation, cleaning, and maintaining hygiene in commercial kitchens. They work in hotels, restaurants, cafeterias, and catering services. The role involves washing dishes, chopping vegetables, cleaning equipment, and organizing storage. It is an entry-level position requiring minimal formal education, but offers opportunities for on-the-job training and progression to cook or chef roles.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in India?**
A: Entry-level kitchen helpers earn ₹1.2–1.8 lakh per year, often with free meals. With experience, salaries can rise to ₹3–5 lakh for supervisory roles.

**Q: Can a kitchen helper get a work visa to India?**
A: No. India's Employment Visa requires a minimum salary of ~₹20 lakh/year, and kitchen helper roles do not qualify as skilled occupations. Such work is typically performed by Indian nationals.

**Q: How can a kitchen helper advance?**
A: By gaining cooking skills on the job and pursuing certificates in food production from ITIs or hotel management institutes, one can progress to commis chef, chef de partie, and eventually head chef.
