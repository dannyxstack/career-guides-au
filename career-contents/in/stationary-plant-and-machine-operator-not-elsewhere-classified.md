# Stationary Plant and Machine Operator Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 8189 (NCO2015)**

Stationary Plant and Machine Operators Not Elsewhere Classified (ISCO-08 8189) run and monitor machinery such as boilers, compressors, or pumps that are not covered by more specific operator roles. In India, these operators are essential in factories, power plants, and industrial facilities, ensuring smooth production by controlling equipment, adjusting settings, and performing minor maintenance. The role demands attention to safety and mechanical aptitude but usually requires only short-term training.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Entry-level operators can advance to senior operator or shift supervisor after 3–5 years of experience. Further specialization (e.g., in boiler or turbine operation) or cross-training in automation can lead to higher pay and roles like plant maintenance manager. Periodic skill upgrades through vocational courses are advised to stay relevant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated production quality inspection using sensors and AI vision
- Predictive maintenance scheduling for equipment based on historical data
- Standardized material handling and packaging processes.
- Adaptive adjustment and optimization of production parameters

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted predictive maintenance improves equipment reliability
- Real-time production data dashboards assist operational decisions
- Smart scheduling system optimizes multi-machine coordination efficiency
- Remote expert collaboration system enables rapid fault diagnosis

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of non-standard mechanical faults.
- Ability to flexibly switch between multiple varieties and small batch production
- Perception of subtle anomalies like unusual equipment noises and vibrations.
- Safety management and emergency shutdown procedures

**Skills to build:**

- Basic programming and PLC debugging.
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data analysis
- Collaborative robot (KUKA/Universal Robots) operation
- Lean production and Six Sigma methods
- Understanding of AI tools in industrial applications

**Key growth areas:**

- Power Generation

- Process Industries

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a stationary plant operator in India?**
A: Entry-level operators earn INR 120,000–240,000 per year, mid-level INR 240,000–420,000, and senior/supervisory roles INR 360,000–600,000. Salaries vary by industry, location, and company size.

**Q: Can a foreigner get an Indian employment visa as a stationary plant operator?**
A: No, this occupation is not eligible for skilled migration. India grants Employment Visas only for roles requiring specialist skills with high salary thresholds (min. USD 25,000/year). Machine operators are considered low-skilled and do not qualify.
