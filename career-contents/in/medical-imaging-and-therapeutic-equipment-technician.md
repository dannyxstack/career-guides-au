# Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3211 (NCO2015)**

Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians operate and maintain diagnostic imaging and radiation therapy equipment such as X-ray, MRI, CT, ultrasound, and nuclear medicine devices. They work in hospitals, diagnostic centres, and clinics, ensuring accurate image capture and patient safety. In India, this occupation is regulated by the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB) for equipment using ionizing radiation. Technicians typically hold a diploma or bachelor's degree in medical imaging technology and must be registered with the appropriate council. The role is critical for modern healthcare, with growing demand due to expanding healthcare infrastructure and rising disease burden.

Some move into equipment sales or application specialist roles for medical device companies. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical imaging technician in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 2-4 lakhs per annum, rising to 7-12 lakhs for senior roles. Specialization (e.g., MRI, CT) and location (metro vs rural) significantly affect pay.

**Q: Can a foreign technician work in India as a medical imaging technician?**
A: Yes, but it requires an Employment Visa sponsored by an Indian employer, meeting a minimum salary threshold (~INR 1.6 million/year). The technician must also obtain AERB registration if operating radiation equipment. E-Business Visas are available for short-term assignments.
