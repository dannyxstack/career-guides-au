# Insurance Representatives — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3321 (NCO2015)**

Insurance representatives sell insurance policies to individuals and businesses, advise on risk management, and handle claims. They work for insurance companies, brokers, or as independent agents. In India, this occupation is regulated by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDAI), requiring licensing for agents. The role involves client acquisition, policy explanation, premium collection, and post-sales service. It is a significant source of employment in the financial services sector, with many entry-level opportunities.

Career progression typically starts as a sales agent, moving to senior agent, then to sales manager or branch manager. Many representatives specialise in life, health, or general insurance. Top performers can become financial advisors or relationship managers. Insurance companies often provide training and performance-based promotions. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an insurance representative in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 2 to 5 lakhs per annum including commissions. Mid-level representatives earn INR 5-12 lakhs, while top performers can earn over INR 30 lakhs. Income is highly variable based on sales performance.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an insurance representative in India?**
A: It is difficult. Foreign nationals would need an Employment Visa, which requires a high salary (typically above INR 20 lakhs per annum) and specialised skills. Insurance sales roles rarely meet these criteria. Most opportunities are for Indian citizens or persons of Indian origin (OCI/PIO).

**Q: Do I need a license to sell insurance in India?**
A: Yes, you must pass the IRDAI agents examination and obtain a license from the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority. This involves a training program and a written exam. The license must be renewed periodically.
