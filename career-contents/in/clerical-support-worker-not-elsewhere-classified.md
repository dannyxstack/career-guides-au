# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 4419 (NCO2015)**

Clerical support workers n.e.c. perform general administrative and clerical duties not covered by other specific clerical roles. Tasks include data entry, filing, photocopying, handling correspondence, and assisting with office logistics. They work in a variety of sectors such as government, healthcare, education, and private enterprises. In India, this occupation is typically at the entry-level and is commonly performed by individuals with secondary education or basic computer skills. The role often serves as a stepping stone to more specialized administrative positions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from general clerical roles to specialized positions such as office manager, executive assistant, or data entry supervisor. With additional training, workers can transition into accounting, human resources, or IT support. The role offers limited vertical mobility without further education, but it provides foundational office experience. Some workers move into government clerical positions through competitive exams.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- Administration
- Data Entry
- Office Support
- Record Keeping

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a clerical support worker in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 1.2 to 2.4 lakhs per year mid-level INR 2.4 to 4.2 lakhs.

**Q: Can I get an Indian work visa for a clerical support role?**
A: Unlikely; the Employment Visa requires specialized skills and a minimum salary of approx. USD 25,000 per year. Clerical roles rarely meet this threshold.

**Q: How can I advance from a clerical support worker?**
A: Pursue higher education or certifications in accounting, HR, or IT. Government exams can also lead to permanent positions.
