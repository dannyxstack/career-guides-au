# Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7127 (NCO2015)**

Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics install, maintain, and repair cooling systems in residential, commercial, and industrial settings. In India, this occupation is crucial due to rising temperatures and expanding infrastructure, with demand growing in construction, hospitality, and food storage sectors. Mechanics typically work for service companies, contractors, or are self-employed.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level mechanics start as apprentices under experienced technicians, progressing to senior or specialist roles (e.g., commercial refrigeration, chiller systems). Experienced mechanics may start their own businesses or move into sales/technical support. Upskilling in energy-efficient systems and smart controls offers better prospects.

- Automatically record equipment operating parameters via remote monitoring system
- Generate routine maintenance reports and work order records
- Developing predictive maintenance plans based on historical data.
- Automatic optimization and adjustment of smart controller parameters

**Where AI augments the role:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic in India?**
A: Entry-level mechanics earn ₹1.2-2.4 lakh per year, while experienced senior mechanics can earn up to ₹7.2 lakh annually. Self-employed mechanics may earn more but have variable income.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as an Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic in India?**
A: It is difficult because India's Employment Visa requires a minimum salary of ₹20 lakh/year, which is far above typical mechanic wages. Business Visas do not permit hands-on work. Thus, migration for this occupation is not feasible.
