# Child Care Services Manager — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 1341 (NCO2015)**

Child Care Services Managers plan, direct, and coordinate the activities of child care centers and programs, ensuring compliance with regulations, managing staff, and overseeing the welfare and development of children. They may work in private daycares, preschools, or government-run facilities in both urban and rural India. The role combines administrative duties with child development expertise, requiring a blend of leadership, financial management, and knowledge of early childhood education.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 30,223. India's child care sector is growing due to rising female workforce participation and government initiatives like the National Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) policy. Demand is strong in metro cities and Tier-2 towns, with urban centers like Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore offering the most opportunities. However, the profession is not considered a shortage occupation under any official list, and migration prospects are limited due to stringent visa requirements.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Child Care Services Manager in India?**
A: The average annual salary ranges from INR 3-5 lakhs for mid-level managers, with senior roles in top cities reaching INR 8 lakhs. Entry-level positions start around INR 2 lakhs.

**Q: Can a Child Care Services Manager migrate to India for work?**
A: It is difficult as the occupation is not on any shortage list. Work visas require employer sponsorship and a high salary threshold (approx USD 25k). Persons of Indian origin may use OCI status.

**Q: What qualifications are mandatory to become a Child Care Services Manager in India?**
A: A Bachelor's degree plus a Diploma in Early Childhood Education (such as NTT) is typically mandatory. Some centers also require a Certificate in Child Care Management.
