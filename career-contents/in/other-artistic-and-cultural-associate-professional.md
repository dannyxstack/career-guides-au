# Other Artistic and Cultural Associate Professionals — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 3435 (NCO2015)**

Other Artistic and Cultural Associate Professionals support the creation, production, and presentation of artistic and cultural works, including roles such as arts administrators, museum technicians, gallery assistants, and cultural event coordinators. They work in galleries, museums, cultural institutions, and event management, handling logistics, curation support, visitor services, and promotional activities. This occupation bridges creative vision with operational execution, requiring organizational skills and cultural awareness.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical starting salary for an arts administrator in India?**
A: Entry-level arts administrators or gallery assistants earn between ₹2.4-4 lakh per annum, depending on the city and institution. Salaries are lower in non-profits and higher in corporate-sponsored venues.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as artistic associate professionals in India?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. Employment e-Visas require a minimum salary of ₹20 lakh/year (≈$24,000), which is above typical pay for this occupation. Most foreign workers enter on business visas for short-term projects or through intra-company transfers.

**Q: Do I need a formal degree to work in this field?**
A: While not mandatory, a degree in arts management, museology, or fine arts is highly preferred for formal employment. Many professionals also have diplomas in event management or cultural studies.
