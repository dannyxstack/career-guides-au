# Creative and Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2659 (NCO2015)**

This occupation covers creative and performing artists not classified elsewhere, including dancers, choreographers, street performers, and other independent artists. In India, the sector is largely informal with many workers engaged in traditional and contemporary performing arts, often on a contractual or freelance basis. Urban centers like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru offer more opportunities, but income volatility is common.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a performing artist in India?**
A: Entry-level artists earn around ₹10,000-30,000 per month, but income can vary greatly. Established performers may earn ₹60,000-1.5 lakh per month or more, depending on reputation and engagements.

**Q: Can a foreign performing artist work in India?**
A: Yes, but for paid performances, an Employment Visa is required, which has a salary threshold of approximately ₹2 million per year. Short-term unpaid events may be possible on a Business Visa or e-Tourist Visa, but performing for remuneration on a tourist visa is prohibited.
