# Elementary Worker Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9629 (NCO2015)**

Elementary workers not elsewhere classified perform simple, routine tasks across various industries such as manufacturing, construction, hospitality, and services. These roles typically require little to no formal education and involve manual labour, cleaning, loading, or assisting skilled workers. The work is often physically demanding and may be temporary or informal.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression is limited for elementary workers without additional skills. With on-the-job training, one may move to semi-skilled roles like machine operator or assistant supervisor. Many workers stay in the same level across different industries. Vocational training courses (e.g., under PMKVY) can lead to better roles in plumbing, electrical work, or hospitality.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple goods handling and stacking: replaced by autonomous mobile robots (AMRs)
- Basic cleaning (e.g., sweeping, mopping): replaced by automatic floor scrubbers or robotic vacuums
- Item classification and sorting: replaced by computer vision systems
- Mail or document distribution: replaced by digital notification systems
- Inventory counting assistance: replaced by RFID and drones

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Task scheduling and priority arrangement: AI optimizes daily work lists
- Equipment failure warning: AI analyzes sensor data to remind of maintenance
- Customer communication records: AI automatic transcription and classification of feedback
- Safety compliance checks: AI-assisted identification of hazardous areas
- Basic communication in multilingual environments: assisted by real-time translation tools

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability to unstructured environments (e.g., ad-hoc assembly tasks)
- Complex emotional communication and customer service (e.g., appeasing dissatisfied customers)
- Cross-task flexible decision-making (when AI recommendations are unclear)
- Fine manual operations (e.g., art packaging)
- Teamwork and on-site coordination skills

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (managing tasks using tablets/mobile phones)
- Operate semi-automated equipment (e.g., cleaning robots)
- Basic Data Analysis (Understanding Task Efficiency Reports)
- Interpersonal communication and customer service skills
- Basic English or a second language (to adapt to multicultural work environments)
- Safety and compliance basic training

**Key growth areas:**

- Informal economy
- Urban construction
- Labour-intensive manufacturing
- Domestic work

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an elementary worker in India?**
A: The average salary ranges from INR 60,000 to INR 120,000 per year for entry-level, but can go up to INR 240,000 with long experience. Most workers earn daily wages, often below minimum wage in informal sectors.

**Q: Can foreigners get a visa for elementary work in India?**
A: No. India does not issue work visas for elementary or unskilled jobs. Work visas require specialized skills or high salary thresholds (e.g., Employment Visa for skilled professionals with salary above ~USD 25,000).

**Q: What career progression is possible?**
A: Without upskilling, progression is limited. With vocational training (e.g., under PMKVY), workers can become semi-skilled in trades like plumbing, electrical work, or hospitality, increasing earnings.
