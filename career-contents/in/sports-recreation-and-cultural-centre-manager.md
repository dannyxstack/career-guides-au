# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 1431 (NCO2015)**

Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers plan, direct, and coordinate the operations of facilities such as sports stadiums, fitness centers, cultural halls, and recreational parks in India. They oversee staff, budgets, programming, marketing, and safety compliance. The role combines business acumen with a passion for sports or culture, requiring skills in event management, people management, and financial planning. With growing awareness of fitness and leisure activities, this occupation is increasingly relevant in urban and semi-urban India.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 3.5–6 lakhs annually, mid-career INR 7–12 lakhs, and senior roles INR 15–30 lakhs, depending on facility size, location, and employer.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Sports Centre Manager in India?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Most managers are Indian nationals; a foreigner would need an Employment Visa with a salary of at least USD 25,000/year and proven expertise. The role is not on the priority shortage list, so sponsorship is challenging.

**Q: What education is needed to become a Sports Centre Manager in India?**
A: A bachelor's degree in sports management, business administration, or a related field is typical. An MBA in event or sports management can accelerate career growth.
