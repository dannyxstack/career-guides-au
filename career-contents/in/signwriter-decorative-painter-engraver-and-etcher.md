# Signwriters, Decorative Painters, Engravers and Etchers — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7316 (NCO2015)**

This occupation covers artisans who design and produce signs, decorative paintings, engravings, and etchings on various materials. They work in traditional and modern settings, often combining artistic skills with technical precision. In India, these craftspeople are found in small workshops, advertising firms, heritage restoration projects, and the growing interior design sector.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a signwriter in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 per month. Experienced artisans can earn ₹20,000-₹35,000 per month, while master craftspeople with their own studios may exceed ₹50,000 per month.

**Q: Can a foreign signwriter get an Indian work visa?**
A: It is very difficult. The Employment Visa requires a salary of at least ₹20 lakh (approx. USD 25,000) per year and proof of specialist skills, which most signwriters cannot meet. OCI cardholders (persons of Indian origin) have easier access.

**Q: Is formal education necessary to become a signwriter in India?**
A: No, many learn through apprenticeships. However, vocational certificates from ITIs or NSQF certifications can improve prospects. A fine arts diploma is beneficial for advanced work.
