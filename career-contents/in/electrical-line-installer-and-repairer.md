# Electrical Line Installer and Repairer — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7413 (NCO2015)**

Electrical line installers and repairers (ISCO-08 code 7413) build, maintain, and repair overhead and underground electrical power transmission and distribution lines. In India, these workers are employed by power utilities, state electricity boards, and private infrastructure firms, ensuring reliable electricity supply across urban and rural networks. The role involves physical work at heights, using specialized equipment to string cables, install transformers, and troubleshoot faults. With India's ongoing grid expansion and renewable energy integration, demand remains steady, though formal training and certification are increasingly valued.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or apprentice, advancing to lineman, senior lineman, and supervisor or charge hand. Experience and certifications (e.g., from the National Power Training Institute) enable moves to project management or safety inspection roles. Some workers transition to transmission network design or utility management. The field remains physically demanding and requires adherence to strict safety protocols. Upskilling in smart grid technology and high-voltage direct current (HVDC) systems can enhance career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Uses drones or AI vision systems for line inspection and preliminary fault location
- Automatic generation of predictive maintenance plans based on historical data
- Remote monitoring system automatic alarms and status reports
- AI-optimised decision-making for load dispatch and grid balancing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted fault diagnosis and repair plan recommendations to improve on-site efficiency
- Smart wearable devices provide real-time safety alerts and construction guidance
- Digital twin simulation helps plan complex line replacement or emergency repair solutions
- Collaborative robots assist with heavy lifting and precise operations

**Human moat:**

- Safety judgment and emergency response in live-line work and high-voltage environments.
- Field operations and decision-making in complex terrain (e.g., mountainous areas, adverse weather)
- Direct communication and coordination with communities, governments, and grid customers
- Team Trust and Leadership in High-Risk Environments

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and maintenance
- Data analysis fundamentals and AI tool usage (e.g., predictive maintenance software)
- Remote monitoring and digital twin platform operation
- Collaborative robot operation and programming
- Upgrade safety management and emergency rescue skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Grid Modernization
- Rural Electrification
- Renewable Energy Integration
- Smart Grid Technology

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electrical line installer in India?**
A: Entry-level workers earn around INR 1.8-3 lakhs per year, mid-level earners INR 3-5 lakhs, and senior supervisors can earn up to INR 8 lakhs annually, depending on employer (government vs. private) and location.

**Q: Can this occupation lead to migration opportunities?**
A: Migration is highly restricted for this role. India lacks a points-based system, and the employer-sponsored Employment Visa requires a high salary threshold (approx. INR 20.8 lakhs), which line installers seldom meet. Intra-company transfers are limited to specialist/managerial roles.

**Q: Is formal certification mandatory for this job in India?**
A: While not strictly mandatory for all employers, an ITI certificate in Lineman or Electrician is highly preferred and often required by government utilities. Practical experience and safety training are critical.
