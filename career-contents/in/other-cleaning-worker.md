# Other Cleaning Worker — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9129 (NCO2015)**

Other cleaning workers perform a variety of cleaning tasks in non-domestic settings such as offices, hotels, hospitals, factories, and public spaces. They use manual and mechanical cleaning equipment to maintain cleanliness and hygiene standards. Duties include sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, trash removal, and sanitizing surfaces. The role may involve specialized cleaning like window cleaning, carpet shampooing, or industrial cleaning. Work is often physical and may require shift work. In India, this occupation is predominantly informal with limited formal employment benefits.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; most workers remain in entry-level positions. Advancement typically requires moving to supervisory roles (e.g., cleaning supervisor, housekeeping manager) after years of experience or additional training in specialized cleaning (e.g., commercial cleaning, industrial cleaning). Some workers may transition to self-employment by starting small cleaning businesses. Formal training and certifications are uncommon but can improve prospects. Overall, the occupation offers low upward mobility and is often a fallback for low-skilled workers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic floor scrubbers or robot vacuums for floor cleaning
- Automatic vacuuming and mopping along preset routes
- Timely automatic spraying of disinfectant for environmental disinfection.
- Use AI trash bins to automatically sort and compact waste
- Monitor cleanliness via sensors and generate cleaning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and shifts.
- Using computer vision to detect stains and guide targeted cleaning
- Use voice assistants or apps to log cleaning tasks and feedback
- Use data analysis to predict peak hours and prepare in advance
- Operate semi-automatic cleaning equipment for high-altitude or corner cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Experience in cleaning complex stains and special material surfaces.
- Communicating with clients to confirm cleaning requirements and flexibly adjusting
- Expertise in non-toxic, environmentally friendly cleaning agents
- Identify safety hazards on site and take emergency measures on the spot
- Manual cleaning in narrow, irregular spaces is irreplaceable.

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cleaning worker in India?**
A: Salaries range from INR 10,000 to 15,000 per month for entry-level, increasing to INR 25,000–33,000 for senior or specialized roles. Most workers earn near minimum wage.

**Q: Can I migrate to India as a cleaning worker?**
A: No. India does not grant work permits for low-skilled occupations like cleaning. Such roles are filled domestically due to abundant labour supply.

**Q: Can a cleaning worker advance to a better job?**
A: Yes, with experience, one can become a cleaning supervisor or start a small cleaning business. However, opportunities are limited and require additional skills or capital.
