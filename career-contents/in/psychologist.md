# Psychologists — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 2634 (NCO2015)**

Psychologists study mental processes and behaviour, providing assessment, therapy, and counselling. In India, they work in clinical, educational, corporate, and research settings, helping individuals cope with mental health issues, stress, and life challenges. The field is regulated by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) for clinical practice. Increasing mental health awareness and government initiatives are driving demand.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 774,417. India faces a severe shortage of mental health professionals, with less than one psychologist per 100,000 people. The National Mental Health Survey estimates a treatment gap of 70-80%. Government schemes like the National Mental Health Programme and the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 are expanding services. Rising awareness, corporate wellness programs, and telepsychology are boosting demand. Employment is expected to grow faster than average, especially in urban areas.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level roles include counselling psychologist or school psychologist. With a Master's degree and RCI registration, one can become a clinical psychologist. Career progression leads to senior therapist, consultant, or private practice. Specialisation in neuropsychology, child psychology, or industrial-organisational psychology offers higher earnings. PhD opens academic and research positions. Many professionals move into corporate wellness or entrepreneurship.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a psychologist in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from INR 300,000 to 600,000 per year. With experience and RCI registration, mid-level psychologists earn INR 600,000 to 1,500,000. Senior professionals and private practitioners can earn over INR 3,000,000 annually.

**Q: Can a foreign psychologist work in India?**
A: Yes, but requires an Employment Visa with a job offer exceeding ~USD 25,000/year. RCI registration is mandatory for clinical practice; foreign qualifications must be recognized by RCI, which may require additional exams or bridging courses.

**Q: Is RCI registration compulsory?**
A: Yes, to practise as a clinical psychologist or in rehabilitation settings. For counselling or non-clinical roles (e.g., corporate or school counselling), registration is not mandatory but preferred.
