# Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 7214 (NCO2015)**

Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors assemble, erect, and install fabricated structural metal frameworks (e.g., steel beams, columns, trusses) for buildings, bridges, industrial plants, and other large structures. They interpret blueprints, position and align components, bolt or weld connections, and ensure stability during construction. In India, this occupation is part of the construction sector, which employs over 50 million workers, with steel fabrication a key skill in infrastructure projects.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

India's infrastructure push under the National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP) and projects like highways, metro rail, and industrial corridors will sustain demand for structural metal erectors. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry typically as a helper or apprentice in construction firms, progressing to skilled erector, then supervisor or foreman. Some move into quality inspection, site management, or start their own subcontracting business. Certification in welding (e.g., from Indian Institute of Welding) or safety (e.g., NEBOSH) aids advancement. Large players like Larsen & Toubro offer structured training. The path is linear but limited without formal qualifications; experience is key.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a structural metal erector in India?**
A: Salaries range from INR 1.5-3 lakhs per annum for entry-level helpers to INR 3-6 lakhs for experienced skilled erectors, and up to INR 6-12 lakhs for supervisors. Daily wage workers earn about INR 500-1000. Large projects in metropolitan areas may pay 20-30% more.

**Q: Can I migrate to India as a structural metal preparer or erector?**
A: Skilled migration is difficult because the salary threshold for an Employment Visa (≈ INR 16.25 lakh/yr) is well above typical earnings. Business Visas allow short stays (≤6 months) for commissioning. Intra-company transfers are possible for multinationals, but general entry is not feasible.

**Q: What education or training do I need to become a structural metal erector in India?**
A: An ITI certificate in Fitter or Welder (1-2 years) is common, but many learn on the job. Apprenticeships under the Apprenticeship Act (1-4 years) also provide entry. Diplomas in civil or mechanical engineering can lead to supervisory roles.
