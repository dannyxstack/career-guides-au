# Data Entry Clerk — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 4132 (NCO2015)**

Data Entry Clerks in India transcribe, verify, and update data into computer systems, often from paper documents or digital sources. They work across sectors like BPO, finance, healthcare, and government, requiring basic computer literacy and typing skills. The role is typically entry-level with high demand for accuracy and speed.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically leads to senior data entry roles, team lead, or quality analyst positions. With additional training, clerks can move into data management, database administration, or business process outsourcing management. Upskilling in software like MS Excel or SQL can improve prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually enter data from paper or electronic documents into databases or spreadsheets
- Check format and basic accuracy of input data
- Repetitive batch data entry, such as invoices and forms
- Basic data cleaning and deduplication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI tools to automatically extract document data, with humans handling exceptions.
- Use automated scripts to verify data consistency and improve efficiency
- Analyze data trends and generate reports through AI-assisted analysis

**Human moat:**

- Complex data judgment and anomaly handling capability
- Client communication and business understanding (e.g., explaining data deviations)
- Cross-system data integration and process optimization
- Maintain data privacy and security compliance

**Skills to build:**

- Learn Python or VBA for automated data processing
- Proficiency in advanced Excel functions (e.g., Power Query, macros)
- Familiar with OCR and RPA tools (e.g., UiPath)
- Data quality management and database basics (SQL)
- Basic data analysis and visualization (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitization
- BPO
- E-commerce
- Government records

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with high typing speed and attention to detail
- Those seeking entry-level office jobs without a degree
- People comfortable with repetitive tasks and working on computers

**Who is not suited**
- Those seeking high income or rapid career growth
- Individuals who dislike routine tasks and prefer creative work

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Data Entry Clerk in India?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 per month (₹120,000-₹240,000 annually), varying by city and employer.

**Q: Can Data Entry Clerks migrate to other countries for work?**
A: Generally no, as the role is considered low-skilled and does not meet the salary thresholds for skilled migration programs.
