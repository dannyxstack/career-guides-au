# Odd-job Person — career analysis · India

**Occupation code: 9622 (NCO2015)**

Odd-job persons, classified under ISCO-08 code 9622, perform a variety of simple and routine manual tasks across different sectors, including cleaning, carrying, and minor maintenance. They are typically employed on a casual or temporary basis and are not specialized in any single trade.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for odd-job persons is limited, often remaining in the same type of work or moving to slightly more skilled manual jobs. Some may transition to semi-skilled roles like helper in construction or services, but formal training is rare. Most work remains informal and low-paid.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic cleaning robots and drones replace sweeping and window cleaning
- AI scheduling system replaces manual allocation of gig tasks
- Autonomous mobile robots replace manual handling of heavy objects.
- Online platforms replace intermediaries in matching temporary work
- Smart gardening robots replace pruning and weeding

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Smart tools enhance cleaning efficiency (e.g., automatic stain detection)
- AI-assisted optimal route planning
- Online platforms expand opportunities for receiving orders
- Wearable devices to monitor health and safety
- AI training system quickly learns new skills

**Human moat:**

- Handle unexpected and complex cleaning tasks (e.g., special stains)
- Requires interpersonal communication and trust (e.g., family services)
- Handling unstructured environments (e.g., rooms cluttered with items).
- Flexibly adapt to ad hoc client needs
- High-risk or sensitive scenarios (e.g., medical waste disposal)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic equipment operation (e.g., intelligent cleaning robots)
- Use of digital platforms (e.g., order-taking apps)
- Customer Service and Communication
- Time management and multitasking
- Basic repair skills (handling simple faults)
- Health and safety knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban informal sector
- Rural odd jobs
- Temporary staffing
- Gig economy

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an odd-job person in India?**
A: The salary varies widely, typically between INR 5,000 and INR 20,000 per month, depending on location and employer. Most are paid daily or weekly in cash.

**Q: Can an odd-job person get an Indian work visa?**
A: No. Indian employment visas require high-skilled roles with a minimum salary of INR 2 million per year. Odd-job persons do not qualify.

**Q: What career progression is possible for an odd-job person?**
A: Progression is very limited. Some may move to semi-skilled roles like construction helper or domestic worker, but formal advancement is rare.
