# Graphic, Communication and Photo Designer（Graphic, Communication and Photo Designer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2322 – Graphic, Communication and Photo Designer。**

This profession involves visual communication design, including graphic design, photography, typography, branding, serving industries such as advertising, media, and publishing.

|---|---|

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for graphic designers in Germany?**
答：Junior: €30,000-38,000, mid-level: €38,000-50,000, senior: €50,000-65,000. Freelancers have variable income.

**问：How can non-EU nationals migrate to Germany as a graphic designer?**
答：You need to have your degree recognized as equivalent, then apply for an EU Blue Card or Skilled Worker visa with a job offer.

**问：What are the career progression paths for graphic designers?**
答：From junior to senior designer, art director, creative director, or starting own design studio.
