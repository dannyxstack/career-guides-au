# Building Cleaner（Building Cleaner）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5411 – Building Cleaner。**

Building cleaners are responsible for cleaning and maintaining the interior and exterior of buildings, including offices, commercial premises, and residential spaces. Tasks include sweeping, mopping, window cleaning, and waste disposal.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 对于非欧盟公民，需有德国雇主提供工作合同，且职业需符合规定。但建筑清洁工通常不被视为技术工人，签证难度大。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 机会卡允许求职者在德国找工作一年，但需满足积分条件。建筑清洁工技能积分较低，成功率不高。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for building cleaners?**
答：According to the German cleaning industry collective agreement, entry-level gross annual salary is about 20,000 to 25,000 EUR, rising to over 30,000 EUR with experience. Actual salary varies by region and job type.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Germany as building cleaners?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation is not considered skilled, visa options are limited, and EU Blue Card or Skilled Worker visas are not applicable. The Opportunity Card may allow a short job search but permanent residence is challenging.

**问：What education is needed to become a building cleaner?**
答：No specific education is required; employers provide on-the-job training. A 2-year dual vocational training (Gebäudereiniger/in) is available but not mandatory.
