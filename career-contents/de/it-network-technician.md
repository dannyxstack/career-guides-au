# IT Network Technician（IT Network Technician）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：4331 – IT Network Technician。**

Plan, install, configure, and maintain computer networks including LAN, WAN, wireless, and security devices to ensure stable and efficient network operations.

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合本科学历且年薪达阈值（2024年约43,800欧元）的申请人，紧缺职业可放宽。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适合有德国认可职业资质（如双元制证书）或本科学历的申请人，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制的求职签证，允许持有人在德国寻找工作，适用于有资质或语言能力者。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an IT network technician in Germany?**
答：Entry-level: €35,000–42,000; mid-level: €45,000–55,000; senior: up to €70,000+, depending on experience and certifications.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany as an IT network technician?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires bachelor's degree and salary threshold, lower for shortage occupations) or Skilled Worker Visa (requires recognized vocational qualification).

**问：What education is needed to become an IT network technician?**
答：Either a 3-year dual vocational training (paid) or a bachelor's degree from a University of Applied Sciences. The dual system is more practice-oriented.
