# Managers in Technical Research and Development（Managers in Technical Research and Development）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2719 – Managers in Technical Research and Development。**

Managers in technical R&D lead research teams, set innovation strategies, manage budgets and timelines, and ensure technology meets market needs.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合年薪不低于43,800欧元（2024年要求）的研发经理，紧缺行业可降至39,683欧元 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 需有认可学历并与雇主签订合同，无薪资下限但需符合行业标准 |

**PR难度：中（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Managers in Technical Research and Development？**
- 拥有工程或自然科学硕士/博士学位，并有志于管理岗位的技术专家
- 具备跨文化沟通能力，愿意在德国长期发展
- 对工业创新和技术趋势有强烈兴趣

**谁不适合学Managers in Technical Research and Development？**
- 不愿承担管理责任，偏好纯技术工作
- 德语水平有限（B2以下），因管理岗需频繁内部沟通

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for R&D managers in Germany?**
答：Junior: €60k-80k, mid: €80k-110k, senior: €110k-150k annual gross, depending on industry, company size, and region.

**问：How can a Chinese R&D manager migrate to Germany?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (salary threshold €43,800) or Skilled Worker Visa, requiring a recognized master's/PhD and job offer. German B1+ helps integration.

**问：Is professional recognition required?**
答：No, R&D management is not regulated. However, degree recognition (via Anabin/ZAB) is recommended for visa.
