## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can advance from events assistant to project manager, or specialize in specific areas like music festivals, corporate events, managing large-scale projects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic scheduling of event agendas and venue bookings
- Automated email notifications for guest lists and attendees
- Automated statistics and report generation for ticketing and registration data
- Generate first drafts of event promotional copy
- Automation of budget tracking and expense classification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyzing past data using AI to optimize event themes and process design
- Leverage AI to generate personalized program recommendations and interactive plans
- Monitor social media feedback in real-time via AI and adjust on-site activities
- Use virtual reality to preview layout effects to reduce trial and error costs
- AI-Assisted Supplier Evaluation and Contract Clause Optimization

**Human moat:**

- Quick decision-making and improvisational creativity for on-site emergencies.
- Deep relationship maintenance with artists and suppliers.
- Understanding client latent needs and designing unique experiences
- Manage multi-stakeholder expectations and conflict mediation
- keen insight into cultural trends and audience psychology

**Skills to build:**

- AI event planning tools (e.g., X.ai, Hootsuite)
- Data analysis and visualisation (Python/Tableau)
- Digital Marketing and Social Media Automation
- Virtual/hybrid event platform management
- Creative and design thinking methods
- Cross-cultural communication and negotiation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Event Management
- Entertainment Industry
- Cultural Events
- Digital Events

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys planning and creative work, with organizational and coordination skills.
- Good at interpersonal communication, adapts to fast-paced event environments.

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes detailed tasks and high-pressure work.
- Unwilling to work on weekends or public holidays.

## 8. FAQ

A: Junior: approx. €30,000-40,000, mid-level: €40,000-55,000, senior: up to €75,000.
