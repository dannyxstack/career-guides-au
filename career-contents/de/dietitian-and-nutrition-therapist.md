# Dietitian and Nutrition Therapist（Dietitian and Nutrition Therapist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：8176 – Dietitian and Nutrition Therapist。**

Dietitians and nutrition therapists provide personalized nutritional counseling and diet plans to treat conditions like obesity and diabetes, working in clinics, hospitals, or rehab centers.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需本科或以上学历，年薪不低于约43,800欧元（2024年），受限；该职业通常达不到门槛 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 需学历认证，双元制或本科认可后可由雇主担保 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，无需雇主即可求职一年，适合资质认证者 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 6个月求职签证，需学历认证证明 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for dietitians and nutrition therapists in Germany?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €32,000-38,000, mid-level €38,000-46,000, and senior can reach €55,000. Private practice may yield higher income.

**问：How can overseas dietitians immigrate to Germany?**
答：Requires qualification recognition (Anerkennung) equivalent to German apprenticeship or bachelor's degree, German at least B2, then skilled worker visa or Opportunity Card.

**问：What are the career progression paths for dietitians and nutrition therapists?**
答：Can advance from clinical to department head, or move to nutrition consulting, health management, or academic research; some open private practices.
