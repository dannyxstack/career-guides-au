# Supervisors in Electrical Engineering — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 2639 (KldB)**

An electrical engineering supervisor oversees electrical installation, maintenance, and repair teams, ensuring projects meet safety codes and quality standards. They coordinate workflows, manage resources, and serve as the contact for technical issue escalation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Common career progression: Electrical technician → Team supervisor → Department manager or project manager. Further education (e.g., technical school or Meister degree) can lead to senior management or professional consulting roles. Some choose to start their own business.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electrical engineering supervisor in Germany?**
A: Entry-level supervisor annual salary about EUR 35,000-45,000, intermediate EUR 48,000-62,000, senior can reach EUR 65,000-85,000. Salaries in East Germany are typically 10-20% lower than in West Germany.

**Q: How can overseas electrical engineers immigrate to Germany to become supervisors?**
A: Requires IHK/HWK certification (Anerkennung), typically German B1-B2. Can apply for EU Blue Card or skilled migration visa shortage occupation pathway. Experienced individuals can also try the opportunity card.

**Q: What steps are needed to become an electrical supervisor?**
A: Usually complete 3.5-year dual training and obtain Meister or technician degree. Then a few years of experience for promotion. Some companies accept internal promotion, but formal supervisor qualification is key.
