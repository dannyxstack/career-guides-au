# Supervisors in Electrical Engineering（Supervisors in Electrical Engineering）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2639 – Supervisors in Electrical Engineering。**

Supervisors in electrical engineering oversee teams in electrical installation, maintenance, and repair, ensuring compliance with safety standards and quality. They coordinate workflows, manage resources, and serve as escalation contacts for technical issues.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for supervisors in electrical engineering in Germany?**
答：Junior supervisors earn about €35k-45k gross annually, mid-level €48k-62k, and senior up to €65k-85k. Salaries in eastern Germany are typically 10-20% lower.

**问：How can overseas electrical engineers immigrate to Germany as supervisors?**
答：Require recognition (Anerkennung) by IHK/HWK, usually with German B1-B2. Can apply for EU Blue Card or Skilled Worker visa (shortage occupation). Experienced candidates may also try the Opportunity Card.

**问：What steps are needed to become an electrical supervisor?**
答：Typically complete a 3.5-year dual apprenticeship and obtain a Meister or technician degree. After several years of experience, promotion is possible. Some companies promote internally, but formal supervisor qualification is key.
