# Production Supervisor - Paper and Packaging Technology（Production Supervisor - Paper and Packaging Technology）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2319 – Production Supervisor - Paper and Packaging Technology。**

Oversees production processes in paper and packaging manufacturing, supervises teams, ensures quality and efficiency, and coordinates machine maintenance and shift schedules.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 如果申请人拥有大学学位（如工程学）并且年薪达到门槛（2024年约43,800欧元；紧缺职业约39,683欧元），可申请。通常主管岗位要求非学位培训，可能不满足蓝卡条件。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适用于完成德国认可的职业培训（双元制）或拥有同等外国资质并通过认证的申请人。需要雇主提供工作合同。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 2024年新选项，基于积分制允许来德国找工作。适合有职业资格和德语技能的外国人。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for a Production Supervisor in Paper and Packaging Technology?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €35,000–42,000 per year, increasing to €55,000–70,000 with experience. Varies by company size, region, and experience.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to Germany for this profession?**
答：The most reliable path is completing a recognized German vocational training (dual system), then finding a job and applying for a skilled worker visa. Foreign qualifications must undergo recognition (Anerkennung).

**问：Is there career advancement in this profession?**
答：Yes. You can advance from production supervisor to production manager or plant manager, or specialize as a technical expert or quality manager through further training.
