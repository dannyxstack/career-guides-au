# Electrical Engineer (General)（Electrical Engineer (General)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2630 – Electrical Engineer (General)。**

Electrical engineers design, develop, and maintain electrical systems and equipment, including power generation, distribution, automation, and building electrical systems. In Germany, this career typically requires a university degree or dual training, offering stable employment and competitive salaries.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for electrical engineers in Germany?**
答：Junior engineers earn around €40,000–52,000 annually, mid-level €52,000–70,000, and senior €70,000–95,000. Automotive and energy sectors offer higher pay, often with performance bonuses.

**问：What are the main migration pathways for electrical engineers to Germany?**
答：Key paths: EU Blue Card (requires bachelor's degree and salary threshold), Skilled Immigration Act (requires recognized qualification or dual training), and Opportunity Card (points-based job seeker visa). Shortage occupations have lower salary thresholds for Blue Card.

**问：What are the career development prospects for electrical engineers in Germany?**
答：Driven by energy transition and digitalization, demand is high. Career paths include project management, technical specialist, or R&D. Additional certifications (e.g., DIN, MBA) accelerate advancement.
