# Dentist and Orthodontist（Dentist and Orthodontist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：8147 – Dentist and Orthodontist。**

Diagnose and treat oral diseases, perform fillings, extractions, root canals, and orthodontics; work in private practices or hospitals.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于学历达标且薪资高于门槛的牙医，需获得资质认可 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 需完成资质认证（Anerkennung），可凭工作合同申请 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德6个月寻找工作，但需先通过资质预审 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Dentist and Orthodontist？**
- 具备出色手部精细操作能力及耐心
- 对口腔医学有浓厚兴趣
- 愿意投入长期学习（6年以上）

**谁不适合学Dentist and Orthodontist？**
- 对血液或口腔异味敏感者
- 无法通过德语C1考试者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for dentists in Germany?**
答：Employed dentists earn about €55k-€100k gross annually; self-employed earn more but bear business risks.

**问：How can a Chinese dentist immigrate to Germany?**
答：You need to have your qualifications recognized (Anerkennung), possibly complete bridging courses/exams, then apply for an EU Blue Card or skilled worker visa with a job offer.

**问：How long does it take to study dentistry in Germany?**
答：The dentistry program typically takes 6 years including practical training, after which you can practice independently.
