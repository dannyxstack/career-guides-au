# Bricklayer（Bricklayer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3212 – Bricklayer。**

Bricklayers in the German construction industry build walls, chimneys, and arches using bricks, stones, and concrete blocks, requiring skills in mortar application, measuring, and reading blueprints.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act Work Visa） | 需德国认可的职业资质和德国雇主合同 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，允许来德国找工作，需资质认证 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 6个月找工作签证，需资质认证和足够资金 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for bricklayers in Germany?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-34,000, mid-level €35,000-45,000, and senior foreman up to €55,000. Salary varies by region, company size, and experience.

**问：How can overseas bricklayers migrate to Germany?**
答：They need to have their vocational qualifications recognized (Anerkennung), obtain a job offer, and apply for a skilled worker visa. Shortage occupation may expedite the process.
