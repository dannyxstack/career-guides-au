# Tobacco Products Manufacturer（Tobacco Products Manufacturer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2927 – Tobacco Products Manufacturer。**

Engaged in the production and processing of tobacco products such as cigarettes, cigars, and pipe tobacco, including raw material handling, machine operation, quality control, and packaging. This occupation is part of traditional manufacturing, with a declining market in Germany.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 需获得德国雇主offer，且职业资格被认可。但该非紧缺职业申请难度较大。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，适合非紧缺职业求职者，需满足一定条件。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for tobacco products manufacturers?**
答：According to the German Federal Employment Agency, entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-32,000, mid-level €32,000-36,000, and senior €36,000-40,000, below the national average.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for skilled migration to Germany?**
答：No. The tobacco industry is not a shortage occupation, with few jobs and low salaries, making it difficult to meet Blue Card or skilled worker visa requirements. Consider other manufacturing roles.

**问：What is the job outlook for the tobacco industry?**
答：Due to tobacco control policies and health awareness, the German tobacco market is shrinking, with jobs decreasing annually. Automation may also replace some positions, leading to a negative outlook.
