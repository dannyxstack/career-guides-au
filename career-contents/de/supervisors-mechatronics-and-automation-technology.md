# Supervisors - Mechatronics and Automation Technology（Supervisors - Mechatronics and Automation Technology）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2619 – Supervisors - Mechatronics and Automation Technology。**

Oversee installation, maintenance, and optimization of mechatronic and automation systems; coordinate technical teams to ensure production efficiency and quality standards.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for mechatronics supervisors?**
答：Entry-level: €35,000-42,000; mid-level: €42,000-52,000; senior: up to €65,000.

**问：Can a Chinese mechatronics technician immigrate to Germany?**
答：Yes, after obtaining recognition of qualifications (Anerkennung) and meeting the Skilled Immigration Act requirements.

**问：What is the career progression?**
答：From technician to master craftsman (Meister), specialist, or management; further education can lead to engineer.
