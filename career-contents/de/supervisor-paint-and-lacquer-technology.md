# Supervisor - Paint and Lacquer Technology（Supervisor - Paint and Lacquer Technology）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2229 – Supervisor - Paint and Lacquer Technology。**

Oversees paint and lacquer production processes, ensuring quality control and safety standards, managing teams, and coordinating production schedules.
