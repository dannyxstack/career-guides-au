## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from technician to supervisor, then to production manager or quality director; dual-system training plus work experience are key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use AI vision systems for coating defect detection and quality report generation
- Automated production scheduling based on order data and equipment status
- Automatic adjustment of paint formulas through sensors and prediction models
- AI-powered automatic writing and version management of compliance documents and SOPs

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted color formula design to quickly match customer needs and optimize costs.
- Real-time data dashboards enhance production monitoring capabilities, detecting anomalies early
- AI-driven training simulators improve new employees' skill acquisition efficiency
- Using AI to analyze historical data to optimize batch switching and cleaning processes
- AI voice assistant assists with on-site inspection and issue recording

**Human moat:**

- Cross-team communication and conflict resolution skills
- On-site judgment and quick decision-making for non-standard process defects
- Formulation of safety procedures and emergency incident handling
- Subordinate skill assessment and personalized development

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial big data analysis and visualization (e.g., Power BI)
- Basic operation of AI quality control tools (e.g., visual inspection systems)
- Use of automated production scheduling software (e.g., MES)
- Cross-departmental digital collaboration and project management (e.g., Jira)
- Basic programming (Python) for data cleaning and report automation
- Leadership and change management skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Green coatings
- Automation
- Quality management
- Regulatory compliance
