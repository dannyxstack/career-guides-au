# Facade Builder（Facade Builder）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3215 – Facade Builder。**

Responsible for installing, maintaining, and renovating building facades, including curtain walls, insulation systems, and decorative panels, ensuring energy efficiency and aesthetics.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 适用于有德国认可职业资格的外墙建造师，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，允许未获担保的求职者入境找工作，需证明语言和资质。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德居留6个月寻找工作，需有相关资质和足够资金。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a facade builder in Germany?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-€35,000, mid-level €35,000-€45,000, and senior master craftsmen can earn up to €60,000.

**问：How can foreign qualifications be recognized in Germany?**
答：You need to undergo the federal recognition procedure (Anerkennung) evaluated by the relevant chamber. In shortage areas, you may work first and complete recognition later.

**问：What career advancements are possible for a facade builder?**
答：You can advance to site foreman, project manager, or through further training become a building technology specialist or energy consultant.
