# Roofers（Roofers）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3214 – Roofers。**

Roofers install, repair, and replace various roofing materials such as tiles, slates, asphalt, and metal sheets to ensure waterproofing and insulation of buildings.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适用于有德国认可职业资格的非欧盟技术工人，需获得工作合同。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制的求职签证，允许在德国寻找工作，需证明语言能力和相关资质。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德国停留6个月寻找工作，需有德国认可的职业资格和足够资金。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for roofers in Germany?**
答：Entry-level roofers earn around €28,000-€35,000 annually, while experienced master roofers can earn €45,000-€60,000, depending on region and company.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become roofers in Germany?**
答：They need a recognized roofing qualification (usually via dual training) or through the equivalency process. Then they can work via the Skilled Immigration Act or Opportunity Card.

**问：What career advancement opportunities exist for roofers?**
答：They can advance to foreman, project manager, or become self-employed after obtaining the master craftsman qualification. Specializing in solar or green roofs offers further growth.
