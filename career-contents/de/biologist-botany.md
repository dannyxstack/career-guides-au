# Biologist (Botany)（Biologist (Botany)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：4124 – Biologist (Botany)。**

Conducts research in plant biology, including taxonomy, physiology, ecology, and genetics, or works in applied sectors such as agriculture, pharmaceuticals, and environmental protection.

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年薪约43,800欧元（2024年）以上的高等学历人才，植物学硕士可申请 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act Work Permit） | 具有德国认可学历，可从事专业相关工作，年薪门槛低于蓝卡 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德国停留6个月找工作，需有足够资金及德语或英语能力 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for botanists in Germany?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €35,000-45,000, mid-level €45,000-60,000, and senior can reach €80,000. Public institutions follow TV-L scale; private sector is higher.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany as a botanist?**
答：Require a recognized degree or obtain credential evaluation. Eligible for EU Blue Card (salary threshold approx. €43,800) or skilled worker visa.

**问：What are the career paths for botanists in Germany?**
答：Work in universities, research institutes, botanical gardens, agricultural companies, pharmaceutical firms, or environmental agencies; also science communication or education.
