# Plastic and Rubber Production Specialist（Plastic and Rubber Production Specialist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2210 – Plastic and Rubber Production Specialist。**

Responsible for manufacturing, processing, and quality control of plastic and rubber products, operating extrusion and injection molding machines to ensure efficient production.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for plastic and rubber production specialists?**
答：Entry-level annual salary in Germany is around €30,000-38,000; experienced technicians can earn up to €48,000-60,000, with shift and overtime bonuses.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate for this profession?**
答：You need a recognized vocational training or degree, verified via credential assessment (Anerkennung). Eligible for EU Blue Card or skilled worker visa; shortage occupation offers priority processing.

**问：What are the career advancement paths in plastics and rubber?**
答：From operator to shift leader, technician, production manager, or via further education to process engineer or quality manager.
