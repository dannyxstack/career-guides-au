# Supervisors and Managers - Wood Processing（Supervisors and Managers - Wood Processing）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2239 – Supervisors and Managers - Wood Processing。**

Supervise and manage wood processing and wood product manufacturing operations, ensuring efficient and safe production processes and team coordination.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于持有大学学历且年薪达到门槛（2024年约43,800欧元）的管理人员，紧缺职业门槛较低。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 适用于有职业培训认证的技术工人，需获得德国企业合同，学历或资质需认证。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制的求职签证，允许在德国寻找工作，适合有资质的管理人员。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a wood processing supervisor in Germany?**
答：Entry-level: €35,000-45,000, mid-level: €45,000-60,000, senior: €60,000-80,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to Germany for wood processing management?**
答：You need to have your vocational qualifications recognized (Anerkennung), secure a job offer, and apply for a skilled worker visa or EU Blue Card.

**问：What is the career path for wood processing management positions?**
答：Typically start as a skilled worker, obtain a Meister certificate to become a supervisor, then advance to production manager or plant manager through further education.
