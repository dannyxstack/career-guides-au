# Salesperson For Electrical, Electronic And Household Goods（Salesperson For Electrical, Electronic And Household Goods）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：6224 – Salesperson For Electrical, Electronic And Household Goods。**

Sells electrical, electronic and household goods in retail stores, providing product advice and technical explanations, handling cash register and inventory management. This is a dual vocational training occupation in Germany with stable employment.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for this occupation in Germany?**
答：Entry-level sales employees earn about 26,000-32,000 EUR gross per year, increasing to over 40,000 EUR with experience.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany through this occupation?**
答：Main path is to complete German dual vocational training or obtain a recognized foreign qualification, then apply for a Skilled Worker Visa. Alternatively, enter with an Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) to search for a job.
