# Plasterers — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 3322 (KldB)**

Plasterers are responsible for plastering, rendering, and decorating internal and external wall surfaces of buildings, including hand-applying plaster, installing plasterboard, and repairing old walls. They are a key skilled trade in building construction.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 70,000. Germany's construction industry continues to boom, with a severe shortage of skilled plasterers, especially in renovation and energy-efficient retrofitting. The Federal Employment Agency lists it as a shortage occupation, with stable positive employment prospects over the next decade.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Starting as an apprentice, can advance to foreman (Vorarbeiter) or site manager (Baustellenleiter); can also become self-employed or move into training after passing the master craftsman exam (Meister).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Large-area wall surface preparation and plastering
- Standard plaster mixing ratios and mixing
- Mechanical spraying of simple decorative lines
- Scaffolding erection and material handling (automated logistics)
- Dimensional error measurement in quality inspection (AI vision)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use plastering robots for rapid large-area application, manual finishing of edges
- AI scans wall flatness and guides manual patching
- AR glasses assist in positioning decorative plaster patterns
- Digital project management and automated material scheduling

**Human moat:**

- Hand-carved and artistic design of complex decorative plaster
- On-site judgment for non-standard shapes and old building restoration
- Tactile Experience of Plaster and Substrate Bonding (e.g., Humidity and Temperature Adjustments)
- Coordination with other trades and on-site problem solving
- Communication and fulfillment of customized customer aesthetic needs

**Skills to build:**

- Operating and maintaining plastering robots
- Building Information Modeling (BIM) basics
- Digital measurement and setting out (laser scanner)
- Knowledge of high-performance plaster materials
- Sustainable construction and green building technology
- Basic electrical and engineering mathematics

**Key growth areas:**

- Skilled trades shortage
- Construction boom
- Energy-efficient renovation
- Aging workforce replacement

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys manual and physical work, with patience and fine motor skills
- Willing to work on construction sites, not bothered by dust and physical exertion
- Seeking stable employment, hoping to immigrate to Germany through skilled migration

**Who is not suited**
- Those sensitive to dust, noise, and climate change
- Prefer desk work and dislike repetitive physical labor.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for plasterers in Germany?**
A: Junior annual salary approx. €28,000-35,000, rising to over €45,000 with experience, higher with Meister certificate.

**Q: Can Chinese plasterers immigrate to Germany?**
A: Yes, but requires qualification recognition (Anerkennung), usually needing to complete supplementary courses or adaptation training, then can apply for a skilled migration visa.

**Q: What certificates are needed for a plasterer in Germany?**
A: Must complete dual training and obtain a Gesellenbrief (journeyman's certificate). Master craftsman (Meister) is not required but recommended for promotion or starting a business.
