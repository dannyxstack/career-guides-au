# Beekeeper（Beekeeper）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：1123 – Beekeeper。**

Beekeepers are responsible for raising bees, managing hives, harvesting honey, and processing bee products, playing a key role in German agriculture and environmental protection.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 需完成德国认可的职业培训并找到工作 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制，允许未找到工作前入境找工 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 6个月求职签证，需自谋出路 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Beekeeper？**
- 热爱自然和动物，特别是昆虫
- 愿意从事户外体力劳动
- 有创业意愿，可自主经营蜂场

**谁不适合学Beekeeper？**
- 对蜂毒过敏或害怕蜜蜂
- 追求高薪和稳定白领工作

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a beekeeper in Germany?**
答：Entry-level beekeepers earn around EUR 24,000-30,000 annually, while master beekeepers can earn up to EUR 48,000. Self-employed income varies significantly.

**问：Can foreign beekeepers immigrate to Germany?**
答：Yes, but they need to complete a recognized beekeeping vocational training (usually 3-year dual system) or obtain qualification recognition. Some federal states have recruitment programs for agricultural workers.

**问：What is the future outlook for beekeeping?**
答：Growing demand for organic food and ecological conservation boosts local honey demand. The workforce is aging, offering opportunities for newcomers.
