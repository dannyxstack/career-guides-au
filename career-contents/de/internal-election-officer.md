# Internal Election Officer（Internal Election Officer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：7123 – Internal Election Officer。**

Internal Election Officers organize, supervise, and execute corporate elections such as works council or supervisory board elections, ensuring legal compliance and fairness.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of internal election officers?**
答：Entry-level around EUR 28,000-35,000, senior up to EUR 55,000, typically aligned with administrative roles.

**问：Can foreigners work in this profession?**
答：Yes, but require fluent German (at least B2) and knowledge of German election laws; generally difficult to obtain work visa due to non-shortage status.

**问：What career paths are available?**
答：Start as administrative assistant, transition to election specialist via training, then advance to compliance or HR management.
