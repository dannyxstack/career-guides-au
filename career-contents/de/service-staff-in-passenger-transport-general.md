# Service Staff in Passenger Transport (General)（Service Staff in Passenger Transport (General)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5140 – Service Staff in Passenger Transport (General)。**

Service staff in passenger transport provide assistance such as ticketing, baggage handling, information, and catering on trains, buses, planes, or ships, ensuring passenger comfort and safety.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合持有大学学历且年薪高于阈值（2025年约45,300欧元）的申请人；但本职业通常不满足学历要求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for service staff in passenger transport?**
答：Entry-level positions earn around €22,000-€28,000 per year, rising to over €35,000 with experience. Aviation and railway services typically pay higher.

**问：How can non-EU citizens work in this occupation?**
答：Main path is completing a recognized vocational training (Ausbildung) in Germany, then obtaining a Skilled Worker Visa with employer sponsorship. The Opportunity Card is also possible for job search, requiring German B1.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities?**
答：You can advance to supervisor or station manager, or move into customer service management. Advancement is relatively limited, but large companies offer internal training and transfers.
