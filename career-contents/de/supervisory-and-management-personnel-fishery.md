# Supervisory and Management Personnel - Fishery（Supervisory and Management Personnel - Fishery）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：1149 – Supervisory and Management Personnel - Fishery。**

Responsible for managing fishery operations, including fishing plans, fleet scheduling, quality control, and regulatory compliance. Works on vessels or in shore-based offices, overseeing catches and ensuring adherence to quotas and environmental regulations.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for fishery management personnel?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 35,000-42,000 EUR, mid-level 42,000-55,000 EUR, and senior up to 70,000 EUR. Salary depends on company size, location, and sea time.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Germany through this occupation?**
答：Yes. With a recognized German vocational qualification (e.g., Fischwirt), you can apply for a work visa under the Skilled Immigration Act. If your qualification is not recognized, you need to go through the recognition procedure.

**问：What is the career progression path?**
答：Start as a crew member or fishery technician, advance to captain or fishery supervisor, then move into corporate management or transition to aquaculture consulting.
