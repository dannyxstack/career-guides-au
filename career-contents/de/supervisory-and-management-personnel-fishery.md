# Supervisory and Management Personnel - Fishery — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 1149 (KldB)**

Responsible for operational management of fishing enterprises, developing catch plans, fleet dispatch, quality control, and compliance monitoring. Usually works on fishing vessels or in shore-based offices, overseeing fishing operations and ensuring compliance with quotas and environmental regulations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can progress from fishing vessel captain or fisheries technician to management, or move into aquaculture management, fisheries policy consulting, etc. Need to accumulate sea experience and business management knowledge.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated recording of fishing data and generation of compliance reports
- AI algorithms optimize fleet scheduling and fishing plans.
- Satellite remote sensing automatically monitors fishing areas and quota usage
- Automatic drone inspection of fish farm conditions

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes catch predictions and market trends to assist decision-making
- Real-Time Alerts for Environmental Violations via Smart Monitoring System
- Automated supply chain management improves efficiency.
- AI assists in training new employees by simulating emergency scenarios

**Human moat:**

- Respond to emergencies on site (e.g., equipment failure, weather changes)
- Interpersonal coordination and leadership with fishing vessel crew and regulatory bodies
- Interpretation and flexible execution of complex regulations
- Experience-based ecological judgment (e.g., anomalous fish migration)

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and AI tool usage (e.g., Python, BI tools)
- Drone operation and remote monitoring technology
- Knowledge of Environmental Regulations and Compliance Management
- Digital supply chain management
- Crisis management and communication skills
- Basic programming and automation process design

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable Fisheries
- Aquaculture Management
- EU Fisheries Policy
- Compliance

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passion for marine and fishery work, able to adapt to offshore working environment
- Possess management skills and a sense of responsibility, good at coordinating teams
- Willingness to learn fishery regulations and sustainable development knowledge

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for fisheries managers?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approx. €35,000-42,000, mid-level €42,000-55,000, senior up to €70,000. Salary is affected by company size, location, and time at sea.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Germany through this occupation?**
A: Yes. Holders of German-recognised vocational qualifications (e.g., Fischwirt) can apply for a work visa under the Skilled Immigration Act. If qualifications are insufficient, credential recognition is required first.

**Q: What is the career progression path?**
A: Start as a deckhand or fisheries technician, gain experience to become a skipper or fisheries supervisor, then enter management or transition to aquaculture consultant.
