# Tree Nursery and Perennial Gardening Specialist（Tree Nursery and Perennial Gardening Specialist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：1212 – Tree Nursery and Perennial Gardening Specialist。**

Tree nursery and ornamental plant cultivation specialists produce, nurture, and sell trees, shrubs, perennials, and ornamental plants, involving seeding, transplanting, pruning, pest control, and customer advice.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act Visa） | 需获得德国雇主offer，职业资格需经德国认可（Anerkennung），可申请技术移民签证。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分系统，允许来德找工一年，需证明语言能力及资金。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德居留6个月寻找工作，需本科以上学历或同等资质。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for tree nursery and ornamental plant cultivation specialists?**
答：Junior annual salary is about €24,000-30,000, mid-level €30,000-38,000, senior up to €48,000. Salary varies by region, company size, and experience.

**问：What conditions are required for non-EU migrants to work in this occupation?**
答：You need a German-recognized horticultural qualification or related degree, employer sponsorship, and German language B1 or higher. Skilled Immigration Act visa or Opportunity Card are options.

**问：What career development paths are available?**
答：You can advance from skilled worker to foreman, technician, or study horticultural engineering, or start your own nursery.
