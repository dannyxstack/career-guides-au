# Transportation Management Clerk（Transportation Management Clerk）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5161 – Transportation Management Clerk。**

Transportation management clerks organize and coordinate freight transport, including route planning, documentation, and customer service. They are key professionals in Germany's logistics sector.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合本科及以上学历，年薪阈值较低（2024年约41,000欧元） |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 适合通过职业资格认证的双元制毕业生 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分系统的求职签证 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**
