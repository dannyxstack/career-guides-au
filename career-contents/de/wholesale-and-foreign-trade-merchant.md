# Wholesale and Foreign Trade Merchant（Wholesale and Foreign Trade Merchant）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：6121 – Wholesale and Foreign Trade Merchant。**

Responsible for commercial processes in wholesale and foreign trade, including procurement, sales, logistics, and customer management, a core profession in Germany's business sector.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Job Seeker Visa（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德停留6个月找工作，需证明经济能力 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker Visa（Skilled Worker Visa） | 需获得雇主合同，职业资质认证，德语B1通常要求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for a wholesale and foreign trade merchant?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about EUR 32,000-40,000, mid-level EUR 40,000-55,000, senior EUR 55,000-75,000, depending on experience and company.

**问：How can foreigners work in Germany through this profession?**
答：Obtain a recognized qualification (e.g., IHK certificate or degree), find an employer, and apply for a Skilled Worker Visa; German B1 recommended.

**问：Is there career advancement?**
答：Yes, you can advance to commercial manager, regional sales director, or move to supply chain or international market departments.
