# Metal Cutting Machining Occupations（Metal Cutting Machining Occupations）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2423 – Metal Cutting Machining Occupations。**

Professionals in metal cutting machining operate lathes, milling machines, grinders, and other machine tools to precisely machine metal workpieces according to drawings. This occupation is vital in German manufacturing.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for metal cutting machining occupations in Germany?**
答：Entry-level skilled workers earn around €26,000-€34,000 gross annually, experienced workers up to €45,000, and master craftsmen or technicians up to €60,000.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany through metal cutting machining occupations?**
答：They need to complete a German-recognized vocational training (e.g., dual system) or pass a qualification recognition (Anerkennung). They can apply for a skilled worker visa, or an EU Blue Card for shortage occupations.

**问：What are the career paths for metal cutting machining occupations?**
答：Progression from skilled worker to foreman, master craftsman, or further education to technician or engineer, or specialization in CNC programming and automation.
