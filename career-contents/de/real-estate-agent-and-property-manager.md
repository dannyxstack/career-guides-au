# Real Estate Agent and Property Manager（Real Estate Agent and Property Manager）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：6131 – Real Estate Agent and Property Manager。**

Manages real estate transactions, leasing, and property administration, including market analysis, client advisory, contract negotiation, and maintenance coordination.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：良好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需大学学历且年薪达标（2024年约43,800欧元），紧缺专业可降低门槛 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 需获得IHK认可的职业资格或大学学历，并有雇主合同 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)） | 基于积分制，允许来德国找工作一年，适合有学历或职业资格者 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德停留6个月寻找工作，需证明经济能力 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income structure for real estate agents?**
答：Income typically consists of a base salary plus commission. Base salary is low initially, but commission increases with experience and performance. Senior agents can earn over €100,000 annually.

**问：How can foreigners become real estate agents in Germany?**
答：They need an IHK license, usually via dual vocational training or a relevant degree and passing an exam. EU citizens can work directly; third-country nationals need a residence permit first.

**问：What career paths are available for real estate agents?**
答：From junior agent to senior broker, team manager, or owning an agency. Also possible to move into property management, real estate appraisal, etc.
