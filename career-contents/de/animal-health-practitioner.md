# Animal Health Practitioner（Animal Health Practitioner）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：8153 – Animal Health Practitioner。**

Tierheilpraktiker are German professionals practicing alternative animal medicine using natural therapies like acupuncture, herbs, and homeopathy. They treat pets and livestock without prescription rights, requiring certification from recognized associations.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 需获得德国雇主offer，且职业需通过资质认证。由于该职业非国家认可，通常难以满足条件。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，允许来德国找工作1年，但需证明语言能力和资金。适合自雇者探索市场。 |
| Subclass Freelancer（Freelancer Visa） | 自雇从业者可申请自由职业者签证，需证明业务可持续性，通常限于柏林等大城市。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

- 希望快速获得德国永居的移民

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of an animal health practitioner?**
答：Entry-level practitioners earn around €18,000-28,000 per year, mostly self-employed, with income varying greatly by client base and region.

**问：How can a foreigner become an animal health practitioner in Germany?**
答：Complete a recognized training program (2-3 years) and pass the industry exam. Non-EU citizens need a residence permit, typically with a job offer or freelancing plan.

**问：Is there career advancement in this field?**
答：Main paths are building a client base as self-employed, specializing in therapies like acupuncture, or opening a training school. Some transition to veterinary assistant roles.
