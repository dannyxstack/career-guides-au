# Commercial and Technical Business Management Specialist（Commercial and Technical Business Management Specialist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：7130 – Commercial and Technical Business Management Specialist。**

Specialist in commercial and technical business management, focusing on planning, organizing, controlling and optimizing operational processes, including cost accounting, project management and process improvement.
