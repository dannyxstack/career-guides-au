# Furnace And Air Heating Construction Specialist（Furnace And Air Heating Construction Specialist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3422 – Furnace And Air Heating Construction Specialist。**

Designs, installs and maintains industrial furnaces, warm air heaters and air heating systems, involving welding, sheet metal work and piping to ensure safe and efficient thermal equipment operation.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a furnace and air heating construction specialist?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-€34,000; experienced workers can earn over €46,000; master craftsmen earn more; varies by region and company size.

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to Germany for this occupation?**
答：Complete a recognized 3.5-year dual vocational training or obtain credential recognition, then apply for a Skilled Worker visa. With B1 German and a job offer, the Skilled Immigration Act route is possible.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for this occupation?**
答：Obtain a master craftsman certificate (Meister) to become a foreman or self-employed, or further qualify as a heating system planner or energy consultant.
