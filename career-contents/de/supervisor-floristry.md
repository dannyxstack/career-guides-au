# Supervisor - Floristry（Supervisor - Floristry）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：1229 – Supervisor - Floristry。**

Responsible for the daily management of a florist shop or floral business, including team leadership, customer consultation, product display, and inventory control, while also participating in floral design creation.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a floral supervisor in Germany?**
答：The gross annual salary ranges from €24,000 to €42,000, with entry-level around €24,000-€30,000 and senior positions up to €42,000+, which is below the German average.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to Germany through a floristry career?**
答：The main route is to obtain a recognized vocational qualification (Ausbildung or equivalent) in Germany, then secure a job offer for a Skilled Worker visa. Without recognized training, you may need to complete an apprenticeship in Germany.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities for a floral supervisor?**
答：You can advance from florist to supervisor, then obtain the Meister certificate to become a store manager or open your own shop, or move into floral wholesale or event planning.
