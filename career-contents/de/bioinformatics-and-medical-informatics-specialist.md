# Bioinformatics and Medical Informatics Specialist（Bioinformatics and Medical Informatics Specialist）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：4313 – Bioinformatics and Medical Informatics Specialist。**

This profession focuses on using information technology to process and analyze biomedical data, including genomics, healthcare data management, and computational modeling in drug development. Practitioners typically have interdisciplinary backgrounds and work in research institutes, hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, and biotech firms.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bioinformatics specialists in Germany?**
答：Entry-level positions earn about 44,000-54,000 EUR annually, mid-level 55,000-70,000 EUR, and senior positions can exceed 70,000 EUR. Salaries are typically higher in pharmaceutical and biotech companies.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany for bioinformatics jobs?**
答：Common paths include EU Blue Card (annual salary threshold €39,682.80) or Skilled Worker visa. A recognized bachelor's degree or higher and a job offer are needed. The Opportunity Card also allows job seekers to enter and search.

**问：What is the career progression for this profession?**
答：Start as a junior data analyst or researcher, progress to senior scientist, team lead, or project manager. In industry, move to R&D management; in academia, become independent PI. A PhD facilitates top positions.
