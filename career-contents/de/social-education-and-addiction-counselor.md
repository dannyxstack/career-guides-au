# Social Education and Addiction Counselor — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 8315 (KldB)**

Professionals engaged in social education, youth support, and addiction counseling, providing psychological support, crisis intervention, and rehabilitation guidance, often serving in public or non-profit organizations.
