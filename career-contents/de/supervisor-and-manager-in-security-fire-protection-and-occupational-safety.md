# Supervisor and Manager in Security, Fire Protection, and Occupational Safety（Supervisor and Manager in Security, Fire Protection, and Occupational Safety）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5319 – Supervisor and Manager in Security, Fire Protection, and Occupational Safety。**

Responsible for monitoring security systems, managing security personnel, and establishing fire protection and occupational safety protocols to ensure compliance with regulations.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 需获得德国雇主合同，且职业资格被认可。适合有相关学位或培训经历者。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制度，允许在德进行求职和资质认证，适合有一定经验者。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高薪职位（2025年薪资门槛约50000欧元），但非紧缺职业，需雇主支持。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary ceiling for this profession?**
答：In large industrial groups or high-risk industries, senior managers can earn up to €80,000-100,000 annually, while in SMEs it's around €60,000.

**问：How can overseas applicants enter this profession in Germany?**
答：They need to have their qualifications recognized (Anerkennung), enter via opportunity card, and then apply for a skilled worker visa after finding a job.

**问：Is there a promotion path to senior management?**
答：Yes, with experience and a degree, you can advance to security director or corporate head of security, often requiring an engineering or management background.
