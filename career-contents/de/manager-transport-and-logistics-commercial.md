# Manager - Transport and Logistics (Commercial)（Manager - Transport and Logistics (Commercial)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5169 – Manager - Transport and Logistics (Commercial)。**

Plans, organizes and manages commercial operations of transport and logistics firms, including fleet management, warehousing, supply chain optimization and cost control.

|---|---|

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for managers in transport and logistics in Germany?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €42,000 (entry-level) to €110,000 (senior), depending on region, company size and experience.

**问：How can a transport and logistics manager immigrate to Germany?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (salary threshold about €45,000) or Skilled Immigration Act visa. A German job offer and recognition of foreign qualifications are required.

**问：What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
答：Typically from operations manager to regional director or head office management; MBA can lead to consulting or entrepreneurship.
