# Supervisors - Photographic Technology and Photography（Supervisors - Photographic Technology and Photography）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2339 – Supervisors - Photographic Technology and Photography。**

Oversee photography studios, technical processes, and teams, ensuring quality of photographic work and coordinating client needs with technical execution.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for photography supervisors in Germany?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-€35,000, mid-level €35,000-€45,000, senior up to €60,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany with this occupation?**
答：Need a recognized vocational training or degree in photography, pass the Anerkennung process, and apply for EU Blue Card (high salary) or Skilled Worker visa.

**问：Will photography be replaced by AI?**
答：Basic photography and post-processing may be automated, but creative, client management, and art direction still require human input.
