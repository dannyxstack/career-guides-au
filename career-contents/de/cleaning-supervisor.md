# Cleaning Supervisor（Cleaning Supervisor）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5419 – Cleaning Supervisor。**

Supervises cleaning staff, ensures hygiene standards, coordinates task assignments, and manages cleaning equipment and supplies.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 需有认可职业资质或大学学位，雇主担保 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制，允许找工作和试用，需德语B1 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 6个月找工作签证，需德语B1和足够资金 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a cleaning supervisor in Germany?**
答：Average gross annual salary is around €35,000-€45,000, depending on experience and company.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Germany as a cleaning supervisor?**
答：It's not a high-skilled shortage occupation, so immigration is limited. However, with recognized German training or qualifications, skilled worker visa may be possible.

**问：What career advancement opportunities exist for cleaning supervisors?**
答：Can advance to regional cleaning manager, facility management supervisor, or start own cleaning company. Obtaining a Meister qualification helps.
