# Blind and Shutter Installer（Blind and Shutter Installer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3335 – Blind and Shutter Installer。**

Blind and shutter installers measure, install, and repair various blinds, shutters, and sun protection systems, including manual and motorized devices. The profession has steady demand in German construction and renovation sectors, typically requiring dual vocational training.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for blind installers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €26,000-32,000, mid-level €32,000-40,000, and master level up to €52,000. Salary varies by region, experience, and company size.

**问：How can Chinese citizens immigrate to Germany for this profession?**
答：You need to complete a German-recognized 3-year vocational training or have equivalent qualifications, and pass qualification recognition (Anerkennung). Then apply for a Skilled Worker Visa. If you have a university degree and meet the salary threshold, you may also apply for an EU Blue Card.

**问：How to become a blind installer in Germany?**
答：Typically complete a 3-year dual vocational training (Ausbildung), with part-time at vocational school and part-time at a company. After passing the IHK exam, you are qualified.
