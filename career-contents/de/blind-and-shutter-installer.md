# Blind and Shutter Installer — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 3335 (KldB)**

Blind and shutter installers measure, install, and repair various blinds, shutters, and sun shading systems, involving manual and motorized devices. The occupation has stable demand in Germany's construction and renovation sector, typically requiring completion of dual vocational training.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for a blind installer?**
A: Junior annual salary approx. 26,000-32,000 euros, intermediate approx. 32,000-40,000 euros, master level up to 52,000 euros. Salary affected by region, experience and company size.

**Q: How can Chinese citizens immigrate to Germany for this occupation?**
A: Must complete a 3-year vocational training recognized in Germany or have equivalent qualifications, and pass qualification recognition (Anerkennung). Then can apply for a skilled worker visa (Skilled Worker Visa). If holding a university degree and earning a certain annual salary, can also apply for the EU Blue Card.

**Q: How to become a blind installer in Germany?**
A: Usually requires completing a 3-year dual vocational training program (Ausbildung), with part-time study at a vocational school and part-time work at a company. After training, passing the IHK exam grants qualification to practice.
