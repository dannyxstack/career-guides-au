# Supervisor and Manager - Mechanical Engineering and Plant Engineering（Supervisor and Manager - Mechanical Engineering and Plant Engineering）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2519 – Supervisor and Manager - Mechanical Engineering and Plant Engineering。**

Oversees teams and operations in mechanical and plant engineering, ensuring efficient, safe, and high-quality production processes.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合本科及以上学历，年薪达到门槛（2024年约43,800欧元，紧缺专业约39,682欧元） |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适合有职业培训或学位，获得德国雇主合同即可申请 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德国停留6个月找工作，需证明经济能力和相关资质 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制，2024年引入，适合尚未找到工作但符合条件者 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Supervisor and Manager - Mechanical Engineering and Plant Engineering？**
- 有机械工程背景并希望转向管理岗位的技术人员

- 英语水平有限且不打算学习德语的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mechanical engineering supervisors?**
答：Entry-level around €45,000-60,000, mid-level €60,000-80,000, senior up to €100,000+, varying by industry and region.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa for this position?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires degree and salary threshold) or Skilled Worker Visa (requires job offer). Qualification recognition may be needed.

**问：What are the promotion opportunities?**
答：From supervisor to department manager, technical director, or plant manager; also possible to move into project management or consulting.
