# Officer (Armed Forces)（Officer (Armed Forces)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：0110 – Officer (Armed Forces)。**

Officers are leadership positions in the German Armed Forces (Bundeswehr), responsible for commanding troops, managing military operations, and logistics. Typically requires military service and officer training.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：非常低（½☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 仅适用于非欧盟军官在特定情况下，如军事交流或合同职位，实际路径极少 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 不适用于军官职位，因为需要德国国籍或特殊身份 |

**PR难度：非常高（★★★★½）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an officer?**
答：Officer salaries depend on rank and years of service: junior officers earn €45,000-55,000/year, senior officers up to €100,000/year, plus benefits and allowances.

**问：Can foreigners become German officers?**
答：Generally requires German citizenship. Non-EU citizens have very limited opportunities, only via special military exchange programs or contractual positions.

**问：What are the requirements to become an officer?**
答：Pass selection by the Bundeswehr, complete at least 3 years of officer training (military and academic), and sign a long-term service contract (typically 13+ years).
