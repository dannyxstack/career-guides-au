# Roofers — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 3214 (KldB)**

Roofers install, repair, and replace various roofing materials such as tiles, slates, asphalt, and metal sheets to ensure waterproofing and insulation of buildings.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Roofers can advance from skilled workers to foremen or project managers, or become roofing technology experts or self-employed entrepreneurs through further education. Some move into emerging fields like solar installation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automation of roofing material quantity and cost estimation
- Inspection Report Generation and Defect Identification
- Digital generation and adjustment of construction drawings
- Standard Tile Laying Path Planning
- Customer communication and quote template generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-enhanced drone or thermal imaging roof inspections
- Waterproofing and insulation recommendations based on historical data
- Real-time voice command assistance for operations and safety reminders
- AR glasses annotation material laying sequence
- Automatically record work hours and material consumption to generate logs

**Human moat:**

- Manual laying and repair of complex roof structures.
- Material bonding and sealing technology under different climatic conditions
- High-altitude safety operations and team coordination
- Assessing experience with special materials and techniques in old buildings

**Skills to build:**

- Operate drones for roof inspections
- Use thermal imaging cameras to detect water leaks
- Learning basic AR tools and digital construction drawings
- Proficiency in project management software and time recording
- Enhance customer communication and on-site quotation skills
- Learn new techniques for waterproof materials and green roofs

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-efficient roofing
- Green roofs
- Solar integration
- Renovation boom

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker (Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)) | Applies to non-EU skilled workers with a German-recognized professional qualification; must have a job contract. |
| Subclass Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)) | A points-based job-seeking visa that allows job searching in Germany, requiring proof of language ability and relevant qualifications. |
| Subclass Job Seeker (Job Seeker Visa) | Allowed to stay in Germany for 6 months to seek employment, must have recognized German vocational qualification and sufficient funds. |

**PR friendliness: medium (★★★☆☆).**

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys outdoor work and physical activity
- Detail-oriented and safety-conscious, with manual skills
- Willing to undergo dual system training and obtain qualifications

**Who is not suited**
- Fear of heights or inability to work at heights
- Dislike outdoor harsh weather conditions.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a roofer in Germany?**
A: Junior roofer annual salary about €28,000-35,000, experienced tradesperson can reach €45,000-60,000, depending on region and company.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens become roofers in Germany?**
A: Must first obtain a German-recognized roofing qualification (usually through dual vocational training) or pass a credential assessment. Then can work in Germany via Skilled Immigration Act or Opportunity Card visa.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for roofers?**
A: Can be promoted to foreman, project manager, or become a master tradesperson and start own business. Can also specialize in high-growth areas like solar roofs, green roofs.
