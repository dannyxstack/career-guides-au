# Chimney Sweep（Chimney Sweep）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：4221 – Chimney Sweep。**

Chimney sweeps clean, inspect, and maintain chimneys, fireplaces, and heating systems to ensure safety and compliance with environmental regulations. In Germany, it is a regulated trade requiring dual vocational training and a master craftsman certificate.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：受规管，较难（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 需获得认可的职业资格，并由德国雇主担保 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制，允许来德找工作，但需证明资质认证启动 |
| Subclass Recognition Partners（Recognition Partnership (Anerkennungspartnerschaft)） | 未完成认证前可先入职，边工作边认证 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for chimney sweeps in Germany?**
答：Entry-level: €25,000-32,000/year, mid-level: €32,000-40,000/year, senior or master: up to €55,000/year. Self-employed income varies.

**问：How can a foreigner become a chimney sweep in Germany?**
答：You need to have your foreign qualification recognized (Anerkennung) and then apply for a skilled worker visa. German language B1-B2 is typically required.

**问：Is dual vocational training mandatory?**
答：Yes, it is a regulated trade. A vocational qualification is required. Dual training is the standard path; foreign qualifications need recognition.
