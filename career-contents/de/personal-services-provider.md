# Personal Services Provider（Personal Services Provider）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：9425 – Personal Services Provider。**

Personal services providers offer customized beauty, hairdressing, and styling services. Typically require dual vocational training, with flexible employment but variable income.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 不适用，因薪资通常低于蓝卡门槛 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 需学历且职业通过资质认证，但个人服务职业非典型高技能岗位 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 可能通过积分制获得居留，但需有雇主合同或培训证明 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average income for personal services jobs in Germany?**
答：Median salary for employed workers is about 28,000-35,000 EUR, while self-employed earnings vary widely, potentially above 50,000 EUR.

**问：How can non-EU citizens enter Germany for personal services occupations?**
答：Require recognized German vocational training and a trade license. Opportunity card visa can be used for job search, but credential recognition is needed first.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for personal services providers?**
答：Opportunities include becoming a master craftsman, opening your own salon, or moving into product training and management.
