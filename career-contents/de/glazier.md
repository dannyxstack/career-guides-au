# Glazier（Glazier）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3334 – Glazier。**

Glaziers cut, install, repair, and replace glass for windows, doors, facades, and furniture, working mainly in construction and renovation.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 学历型工作签证，但玻璃工通常不满足学历要求 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 需通过资质认证，雇主担保，适合双元制毕业生 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，可用于找工作和资质认证，但需满足语言等条件 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a glazier in Germany?**
答：Entry-level glaziers earn around €26,000-32,000 annually, experienced ones up to €40,000, and masters up to €50,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate as a glazier to Germany?**
答：They need to obtain recognition of qualifications (Anerkennung), ideally complete German dual training, then apply for a skilled worker visa.

**问：What are the career progression paths for a glazier?**
答：Glaziers can advance to master, project manager, self-employment, or move into quality control and building materials sales.
