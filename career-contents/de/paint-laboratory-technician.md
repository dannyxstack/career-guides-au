# Paint Laboratory Technician（Paint Laboratory Technician）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2222 – Paint Laboratory Technician。**

Develops and tests paints, varnishes, and coating materials in a laboratory setting, ensuring products meet quality standards and safety regulations.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合本科及以上学历，年薪门槛约43,800欧元（2024年），紧缺职业放宽 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 适合双元制培训毕业生，需雇主担保，无最低工资硬性要求 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，允许求职者在德停留一年找工作，需语言或学历加分 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for a paint lab technician?**
答：Entry-level positions have a gross annual salary of about 32,000-40,000 EUR, increasing with experience.

**问：How can non-EU qualified professionals work in Germany?**
答：They need a credential assessment, then can apply for EU Blue Card or Skilled Worker visa; some may need bridging courses.

**问：Is there career advancement in this field?**
答：Yes, to lab supervisor, R&D manager, or transition to quality assurance or technical sales.
