# Hairdresser（Hairdresser）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：8231 – Hairdresser。**

Hairdressers provide haircutting, coloring, styling, and scalp care services to clients, typically working in salons or barbershops, requiring manual dexterity and customer communication skills.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 如果拥有德国认可的资质，可申请技术移民签证从事该职业。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制的求职签证，可来德国寻找培训或就业机会。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德国停留6个月寻找工作，但需证明有资质且经济能力。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for hairdressers in Germany?**
答：Entry-level hairdressers earn around €20,000-28,000 gross annually, mid-level €28,000-36,000, and senior or self-employed up to €36,000-50,000. Salary varies by region, employer, and client base.

**问：How can a foreigner become a hairdresser in Germany?**
答：You need to have your foreign qualifications recognized (Anerkennung) as equivalent to German standards, typically through completing a dual training or passing a practical exam. Then apply for a skilled worker visa or Opportunity Card. German language B1-B2 required.
