# Technical Drafter（Technical Drafter）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2721 – Technical Drafter。**

Technical drafters create detailed technical drawings and plans using CAD software based on designs from engineers and architects, commonly employed in mechanical, construction, and electrical engineering fields.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a technical drafter?**
答：Gross annual salary in Germany is about €28,000-36,000 for junior, €36,000-45,000 for mid-level, and up to €55,000 for senior positions, varying by region and industry.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany as a technical drafter?**
答：You need a recognized German qualification or dual vocational training. Apply for EU Blue Card (if salary threshold met) or skilled worker visa. Credential recognition (Anerkennung) is crucial, usually handled by IHK.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities for a technical drafter?**
答：Promotion to senior drafter, CAD manager, technical supervisor, or further training to become a technician, engineer, or project manager. Obtaining Meister or Techniker qualification significantly improves prospects.
