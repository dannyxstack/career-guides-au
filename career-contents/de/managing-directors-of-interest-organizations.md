# Managing Directors of Interest Organizations（Managing Directors of Interest Organizations）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：7122 – Managing Directors of Interest Organizations。**

Directs and oversees the operations of trade associations, unions, professional bodies, or non-profit organizations, setting strategic goals, managing staff, and representing the organization's interests.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card for Managers） | 适用于年薪至少58,400欧元（2025年标准）的管理职位，可携带家属，21个月后申请永居。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 适用于具有认可学历匹配的职位，年薪门槛较低（约45,000欧元），但需雇主担保。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Managing Directors of Interest Organizations？**
- 具有商业或公共管理背景、擅长战略规划和人际沟通的管理者
- 在行业协会或非营利领域有多年经验的人士
- 对政策倡导和公共事务有浓厚兴趣的人

**谁不适合学Managing Directors of Interest Organizations？**
- 缺乏领导经验或团队管理能力的人
- 不喜欢政治性工作或频繁对外沟通的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this position?**
答：Entry-level around 50,000-65,000 EUR/year, mid-level 65,000-90,000 EUR/year, senior up to 130,000 EUR/year, depending on organization size and region.

**问：What visa paths are available for foreigners?**
答：Main options are EU Blue Card (salary ≥58,400 EUR) or Skilled Worker Visa (requires matching degree). It is advisable to gain management experience first.

**问：How many years does it take from department head to executive director?**
答：Typically 5-10 years, depending on performance, networks, and organizational hierarchy. Some accelerate with an MBA.
