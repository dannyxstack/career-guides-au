# Market Stall and Market Vendor（Market Stall and Market Vendor）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：6212 – Market Stall and Market Vendor。**

Sells food, beverages, textiles, and other goods at markets, festivals, or temporary stalls; handles display, cashiering, customer inquiries, and stock replenishment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

**AI替代风险：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a market stall vendor?**
答：Full-time gross annual salary is around €24,000–€30,000; part-time is paid hourly at €12–€15.

**问：Are there skilled migration pathways for foreigners?**
答：This occupation is not on the shortage list and does not qualify for Blue Card or skilled migration; only EU free movement applies.
