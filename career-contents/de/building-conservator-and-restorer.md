# Building Conservator and Restorer（Building Conservator and Restorer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3115 – Building Conservator and Restorer。**

This occupation involves maintenance, restoration, and renovation of historical and modern buildings, including masonry, timber, and concrete repair, ensuring structural safety and cultural heritage preservation.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（需资质认证）（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等学历且年薪达标（2024年约43,800欧元）的紧缺职业，建筑维护通常不满足蓝卡薪资门槛。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适用于有德国认可的职业资格（Ausbildung或大学学历）的技术工人，需获得工作合同。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制的求职签证，2024年实施，允许未获得工作合同前赴德找工作，需语言和资质认证。 |

**PR难度：中等（需工作满4年）（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Building Conservator and Restorer？**

- 不愿学习德语或通过认证考试

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for building conservators and restorers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-34,000, rising to €42,000 with experience, and over €50,000 for masters or project managers. Lower than IT but stable.

**问：How can foreigners migrate to Germany for building conservation work?**
答：You need to have your vocational qualifications recognized (Anerkennung). You can then apply for a work visa under the Skilled Immigration Act or use the Opportunity Card to search for a job in Germany.

**问：What are the promotion opportunities?**
答：You can advance to master craftsman (Meister), technician, or project manager, specialize in heritage conservation or energy retrofitting, or start your own business.
