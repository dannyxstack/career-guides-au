## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can advance from skilled tradesperson to foreman (Vorarbeiter) or construction manager (Baustellenleiter), or through further training become a technician (Techniker) or master craftsman (Meister).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic spraying robots and plastering robots perform large-area coating and plastering work, reducing manual operations.
- AI vision system automatically detects quality indicators such as coating thickness and flatness, replacing manual inspection
- BIM-based automatic scheduling system optimizes construction plans, reducing manual scheduling by supervisors
- AI-driven inventory management system automatically tracks material usage and replenishment, replacing manual stocktaking

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AR-assisted layout and measurement, improving construction accuracy and efficiency
- AI quality monitoring system analyzes painting quality in real-time, aiding supervisors in quick decision-making.
- Drones inspect large building facades, providing high-resolution images for AI analysis of cracks and spalling.
- Digital twin technology simulates construction plans to optimize processes and resource allocation
- AI training simulator for skill and accident drills, improving team capability

**Human moat:**

- On-site coordination and communication: manage multi-trade teams and resolve unexpected issues
- Fine manual work: high-end decoration and complex details cannot be fully automated
- Safety and compliance responsibility: ultimate responsibility for construction safety and quality standards
- Customer Relationship Management: Understand client needs, handle complaints and changes
- Material and process experience: knowledge of construction with non-standard substrates and special environments.

**Skills to build:**

- Digital tool operation: tablets, BIM software, project management platforms
- Data analysis basics: understand quality inspection data, use AI tools to assist decisions
- Robot collaboration: familiar with programming and maintenance of painting robots and plastering robots
- Augmented reality (AR) applications: using AR for setting out and acceptance
- Communication and leadership: effectively managing cross-generational, multicultural teams
- Sustainable materials knowledge: master eco-friendly coatings and waterproof material properties

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-efficient renovation
- Building refurbishment
- Waterproofing technology
- Historic preservation
