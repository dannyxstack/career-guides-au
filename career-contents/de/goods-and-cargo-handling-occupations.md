# Goods and Cargo Handling Occupations（Goods and Cargo Handling Occupations）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5133 – Goods and Cargo Handling Occupations。**

Responsible for loading, unloading, moving, sorting, and storing goods and cargo in warehouses, ports, railway stations, and other logistics hubs.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在DE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 若获得双元制培训证书，可作为专业人才申请工作签证，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 可在德国停留6个月寻找工作，需证明经济能力，适合有经验者。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，允许未获工作合同者来德找工作，但需满足条件。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for cargo handling in Germany?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €24,000 to €45,000; entry-level about €24,000-30,000, senior including foreman up to €45,000.

**问：Can I migrate as a cargo handler without a forklift license?**
答：Yes, but employer sponsorship or Opportunity Card is needed; the license can be obtained in Germany for €300-600.

**问：Is there career progression for cargo handlers?**
答：Yes, further training to become a warehouse logistics specialist (Fachkraft für Lagerlogistik) can lead to supervisor or logistics manager roles.
