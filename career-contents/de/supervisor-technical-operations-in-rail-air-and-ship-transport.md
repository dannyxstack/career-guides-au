# Supervisor - Technical Operations in Rail, Air and Ship Transport（Supervisor - Technical Operations in Rail, Air and Ship Transport）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5119 – Supervisor - Technical Operations in Rail, Air and Ship Transport。**

Supervises technical operations in rail, air, or maritime transport, ensuring safety and efficiency, managing technical staff and coordinating maintenance.
