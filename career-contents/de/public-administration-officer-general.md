# Public Administration Officer (General)（Public Administration Officer (General)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：7320 – Public Administration Officer (General)。**

Handles daily administrative tasks in government departments, including document processing, data management, citizen inquiries, and policy implementation.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the entry-level salary for public administration?**
答：The annual gross salary is around EUR 30,000-40,000 (TVöD pay grade E6-E8), depending on the state and qualifications.

**问：How can foreigners enter the German public administration sector?**
答：You need a German recognized degree or vocational training and German language at C1 level. EU Blue Card or skilled worker visa is possible, but some positions require German or EU citizenship.
