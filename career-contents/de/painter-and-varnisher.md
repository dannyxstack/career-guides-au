# Painter and Varnisher（Painter and Varnisher）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：3321 – Painter and Varnisher。**

Applies paints, varnishes, and protective coatings to surfaces of buildings, vehicles, and industrial facilities, including wallpapering and decorative finishes.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 需德国认可的职业资格，雇主担保，德语B1以上 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 通常要求学位，但梅斯特（Meister）可等同，薪资门槛约43,800欧元 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制，无需雇主担保，可来德国找工，需语言A2以上 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 6个月找工作签证，需学历或职业资格，资金自理 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for painters in Germany?**
答：Apprentices receive an allowance; after graduation, starting salary is around 28,000-34,000 EUR; skilled workers 35,000-45,000 EUR; masters or project managers can reach 46,000-60,000 EUR.

**问：Can Chinese painters immigrate to Germany?**
答：Yes, but they need to pass a qualification recognition (Anerkennung) and find an employer sponsor. Dual vocational training or a master certificate is beneficial.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities for painters?**
答：Yes, by becoming a master (Meister), you can start your own business or become a project manager; you can also specialize in architectural or automotive coatings.
