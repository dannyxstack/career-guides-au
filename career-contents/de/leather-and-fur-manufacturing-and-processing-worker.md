# Leather And Fur Manufacturing And Processing Worker（Leather And Fur Manufacturing And Processing Worker）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2830 – Leather And Fur Manufacturing And Processing Worker。**

Workers in tanning, dyeing, cutting, sewing and finishing leather and fur to produce clothing, footwear, bags and other products. Work is mainly in factories or workshops, emphasizing craftsmanship and attention to detail.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适合完成德国双元制培训或学历认证者，需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制的求职签证，可用于来德国找工作 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 6个月求职签证，需证明语言能力和经济来源 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for leather processing workers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 24,000-30,000 EUR, rising to 38,000-45,000 EUR with experience, but growth potential is limited.

**问：How can foreigners enter this profession in Germany?**
答：Usually requires completing German dual training or having qualifications recognized. Can apply for a skilled worker visa (with employer sponsorship) or Opportunity Card.

**问：What training is needed to become a leather processing worker?**
答：Main route is a 3-year dual vocational training (Ausbildung), combining on-the-job training and vocational school. Further specialization courses are also available.
