# Glass And Window Cleaner（Glass And Window Cleaner）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：5412 – Glass And Window Cleaner。**

Professionals responsible for cleaning glass surfaces and windows in buildings, using specialized tools and cleaning agents to ensure safety and cleanliness, commonly employed in commercial, residential, and public facilities.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 若完成德国认可的职业培训，可通过技术移民法申请工作签证。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分系统，允许未获工作合同者来德国寻找工作。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for glass and window cleaners?**
答：Entry-level salary is around EUR 24,000-30,000 per year, while senior supervisors can earn EUR 36,000-42,000, depending on experience and region.

**问：Can Chinese citizens immigrate as glass and window cleaners through skilled migration?**
答：It is difficult as the occupation is not in shortage. Usually requires completing German vocational training or having a job offer; the Opportunity Card may be an option.

**问：What training is required for this occupation?**
答：No mandatory training, but completing a 3-year dual vocational training in building cleaning improves opportunities and salary.
