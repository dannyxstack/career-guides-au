# Farmer (General)（Farmer (General)）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：1110 – Farmer (General)。**

Engages in crop cultivation, livestock farming, and daily farm management, including sowing, fertilizing, harvesting, animal care, and equipment maintenance.

**考证难度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act） | 适用于拥有德国认可的职业资格或大学学历的专业人才，需获得雇主合同。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德居留6个月寻找工作，需提供经济证明。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card） | 基于积分制，允许未获得工作合同的求职者来德寻找工作，具备农业技能可加分。 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Farmer (General)？**
- 热爱户外体力劳动，适应季节性工作节奏
- 对农业技术或有机农业有兴趣
- 愿意在乡村地区长期生活

**谁不适合学Farmer (General)？**
- 偏好城市生活或办公室工作
- 畏惧体力劳动或极端天气

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary of a farmer in Germany?**
答：Junior farmers earn around 24,000-30,000 EUR, while experienced farm managers can earn up to 48,000 EUR gross per year.

**问：How can a foreigner become a farmer in Germany?**
答：Apply under the Skilled Immigration Act with a recognized agricultural qualification; dual vocational training is also a pathway.

**问：Is there career progression in agriculture?**
答：Yes, from farm worker to foreman, master farmer, or farm manager; further training leads to organic farming or agricultural machinery services.
