# Non-Commissioned Officers with Sash（Non-Commissioned Officers with Sash）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：0120 – Non-Commissioned Officers with Sash。**

Non-commissioned officers with sash are senior NCOs in the German Bundeswehr, leading squads, training soldiers, and maintaining discipline. They specialize in technical or combat roles, forming the backbone of the military.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of non-commissioned officers with sash?**
答：Annual salary before tax ranges from about €28,000 to €55,000, depending on rank, years of service, and position allowances. Senior NCOs can reach the upper limit.

**问：Can third-country nationals become non-commissioned officers with sash in Germany?**
答：Almost impossible. The Bundeswehr generally recruits only German or EU citizens. Third-country nationals would need to naturalize or obtain long-term residency first.

**问：What is the career progression for non-commissioned officers with sash?**
答：Start as a junior NCO (Unteroffizier), advance to NCO with sash (e.g., Feldwebel), then to senior NCO (e.g., Oberstabsfeldwebel). Outstanding individuals may become officers.
