# Notary — career analysis · Germany

**Occupation code: 7312 (KldB)**

Notaries are responsible for certifying legal documents, wills, contracts, etc., providing legal attestation services. This is an important profession in the German legal system.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career path: Become a candidate notary after passing national exams and internship, accumulate experience, then apply for official notary position, or open own practice.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Drafting and proofreading standardized contracts and will templates
- Format checking and consistency verification of notarized documents
- Initial verification and data entry of client identity information
- Rule-based legal clause compliance checks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI to quickly search relevant case law and regulations, improving legal consultation efficiency
- Pre-review document completeness using smart tools to reduce manual review time
- Generate initial legal memoranda with AI assistance to aid complex case analysis

**Human moat:**

- On-site confirmation and witnessing of the party's true intent
- Final legal responsibility for notarization involving significant assets or personal rights
- Handle non-standard, complex legal situations requiring professional judgment.
- Face-to-face service to build deep trust with clients

**Skills to build:**

- Application of legal tech tools (e.g., AI for contract analysis, e-notary platforms)
- Data privacy and cybersecurity regulations (DSGVO)
- Basic programming or automation scripting (e.g., Python for batch document processing)
- advanced legal research and knowledge management
- Cross-industry business consulting (e.g., combining finance and immigration law)
- Soft skills: complex communication, judgment, resilience under pressure

**Key growth areas:**

- Legal services
- Aging workforce
- E-notarization
- Cross-border transactions

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a German notary?**
A: Notary public salaries: junior approx. €45,000-60,000 per year, senior can exceed €90,000, and independent practitioners can reach over €150,000.

**Q: Can foreigners become notaries in Germany?**
A: Yes, but requires completing German legal education and passing two state exams, or through a qualification recognition process, which is extremely difficult.

**Q: How long does it take to become a notary?**
A: Typically requires at least 7 years: 5 years of university legal studies (including the first state examination) and 2 years of internship (including the second state examination).
