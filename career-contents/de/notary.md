# Notary（Notary）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：7312 – Notary。**

Notaries certify legal documents, wills, and contracts, providing legal authentication services, playing a key role in the German legal system.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高学历法律专业人士，需获得德国认证的法律学位，并达到薪资门槛（2025年约58,400欧元） |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 适用于已通过德国法律资格认证的申请人，需获得具体工作邀请 |
| Subclass Qualification Recogn（Recognition of Professional Qualifications） | 外国法律资格需通过资质认证程序，完成适应培训或考试 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a notary in Germany?**
答：Entry-level notaries earn about €45,000-60,000 annually, senior notaries over €90,000, and self-employed notaries can exceed €150,000.

**问：Can a foreigner become a notary in Germany?**
答：Yes, but they must complete German legal education and pass two state exams, or go through a qualification recognition process, which is extremely difficult.

**问：How long does it take to become a notary?**
答：Typically at least 7 years: 5 years of university law studies (including first state exam) and 2 years of traineeship (including second state exam).
