## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for corporate organisation and strategy managers in Germany?**
答：Entry-level about 45,000-65,000 EUR, mid-level 65,000-90,000 EUR, senior up to 90,000-150,000 EUR, varying by industry and region.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Germany as corporate managers?**
答：Main paths: EU Blue Card (salary ≥43,800 EUR) and Skilled Worker visa, requiring a university degree and job offer. Opportunity Card is also an option.

**问：What qualifications are needed?**
答：Typically a recognized university degree (e.g., business administration) with credential evaluation (Anerkennung). MBA is beneficial but not mandatory.
