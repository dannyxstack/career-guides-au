# Pipe Installation and Maintenance Technician（Pipe Installation and Maintenance Technician）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2626 – Pipe Installation and Maintenance Technician。**

Responsible for installing, repairing, and maintaining water, heating, gas, and industrial piping systems, including pipe laying, welding, leak detection, and system commissioning.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for pipe fitters?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €30,000-€38,000, mid-level up to €48,000, and senior/Meister up to €60,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Germany as a pipe fitter?**
答：Generally requires recognition of vocational qualifications (Anerkennung), then apply for a skilled worker visa or Opportunity Card.

**问：What career advancement opportunities do pipe fitters have?**
答：By obtaining a Meister certificate, can become a foreman or self-employed, or further study to become an energy consultant or technical planner.
