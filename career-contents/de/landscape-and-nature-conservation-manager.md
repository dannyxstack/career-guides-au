# Landscape and Nature Conservation Manager（Landscape and Nature Conservation Manager）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：1172 – Landscape and Nature Conservation Manager。**

Responsible for the protection and maintenance of natural landscapes and ecosystems, including planning ecological restoration, managing nature reserves, monitoring flora and fauna, and implementing environmental education projects. Demand is stable in federal and state environmental agencies and NGOs in Germany.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需德国认可大学学位，年薪门槛2024年约43,800欧元（短缺职业38,000欧元）；此职业非典型蓝卡目标，但可尝试 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 需资质认证（Anerkennung），有学位或职业培训，雇主担保 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 2024年新路径，基于积分制，无需雇主预聘，允许在德求职1年 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for landscape and nature conservation professionals?**
答：Entry-level around EUR 32,000-40,000, mid-level EUR 42,000-52,000, senior EUR 55,000-70,000. Public sector follows TVöD pay scale; private consulting may be higher.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Germany for nature conservation jobs?**
答：Non-EU citizens need qualification recognition (Anerkennung), then apply for a work visa via the Skilled Immigration Act. The Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) also applies.

**问：What are the career prospects in Germany?**
答：Driven by climate change and biodiversity issues, demand is steadily increasing. Clear career path towards project management or policy consulting.
