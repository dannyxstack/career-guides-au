# Fine Optics Technician（Fine Optics Technician）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2136 – Fine Optics Technician。**

Fine optics technicians manufacture, assemble, and adjust high-precision optical components such as lenses, prisms, and optical systems for medical devices, laser technology, and measurement instruments.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高学历人才，需有德国认可大学学位，年薪门槛约4.5万欧元（2024年） |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适用于有德国认可职业资格的技术工人，需获得雇主担保 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分制的求职签证，适用于有技能和语言能力的申请人 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for fine optics technicians?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about EUR 30,000-38,000, mid-level EUR 38,000-48,000, and senior can reach over EUR 60,000.

**问：How can third-country nationals become fine optics technicians in Germany?**
答：They need a recognized German vocational qualification, either through dual training or credential assessment, then apply for a skilled worker visa or opportunity card.

**问：What qualifications are required?**
答：Completion of German dual training with IHK certification; foreign qualifications must be recognized in Germany.
