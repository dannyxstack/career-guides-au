# Supervisory and Management Personnel - Biology（Supervisory and Management Personnel - Biology）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：4129 – Supervisory and Management Personnel - Biology。**

Supervisory and management personnel in biology lead teams in biotechnology or life sciences, managing R&D projects and lab operations, typically requiring a natural science degree and several years of professional experience.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for biology managers in Germany?**
答：Entry-level managers earn about €50,000-€65,000 gross annually, while senior managers can earn €85,000-€120,000, with higher pay in large corporations or pharma.

**问：How can non-EU citizens apply for biology manager jobs in Germany?**
答：Apply via EU Blue Card or Skilled Employment Visa, requiring a master's degree and job offer. Blue Card salary threshold is about €45,300 in 2024.

**问：What is the career path for biology managers?**
答：Start as a researcher, then progress to team leader, department manager, and eventually R&D director or Chief Scientific Officer (CSO).
