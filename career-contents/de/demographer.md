# Demographer（Demographer）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：9135 – Demographer。**

Demographers analyze population data, studying demographic structures, distributions, and trends to provide policy advice and strategic planning for governments, research institutions, and businesses.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合拥有德国认可大学学位及工作合同，年薪不低于约43,800欧元（2024年门槛）的人口统计学家。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适用于拥有职业资格或学位，并获得德国雇主工作合同者，无需薪资门槛，但需与资质匹配。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa (Chancenkarte)） | 允许来德6个月寻找工作，需有足够资金和学历，但无语言要求，需在机会卡积分系统中达到6分。 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for demographers in Germany?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around EUR 38,000-45,000, mid-level EUR 47,000-58,000, and senior EUR 60,000-80,000. Salaries depend on education, experience, and employer (public sector follows TVöD).

**问：How can demographers migrate to Germany?**
答：Common pathways include EU Blue Card (requires job offer with salary threshold), Skilled Worker Visa (employer sponsorship), and Chancenkarte (6-month job search). A recognized degree or qualification is required.

**问：What is the career progression for demographers?**
答：Start as a research assistant, progress to senior researcher or project manager. Options include market research, urban planning, or policy consulting. A PhD can lead to academic positions.
