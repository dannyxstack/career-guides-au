# Supervisor - Energy Technology（Supervisor - Energy Technology）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2629 – Supervisor - Energy Technology。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for an energy technology supervisor in Germany?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around 40,000-50,000 EUR, rising to 65,000-80,000 EUR with experience, depending on region and company size.

**问：How can overseas applicants obtain a work visa for an energy technology supervisor position in Germany?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (salary threshold ~43,800 EUR) or Skilled Worker Visa (requires employer sponsorship and recognition of qualifications).

**问：What certifications are needed to become a supervisor?**
答：Typically requires completed dual training (e.g., electronics technician for energy) followed by Techniker or Industriemeister qualification. Foreign credentials need recognition.
