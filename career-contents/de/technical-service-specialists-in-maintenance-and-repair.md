# Technical Service Specialists in Maintenance and Repair（Technical Service Specialists in Maintenance and Repair）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：2513 – Technical Service Specialists in Maintenance and Repair。**

Responsible for routine maintenance, inspection, and repair of industrial equipment, machinery, and systems to ensure smooth production. Usually trained via dual vocational training (Ausbildung) as mechatronics or industrial technicians.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在DE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz)） | 适用于具有德国认可的职业资格（如完成Ausbildung）的技术工人，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等教育学历且薪资达门槛的申请人，但此职业通常以职教为主，蓝卡较少适用。 |
| Subclass Chancenkarte（Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)） | 基于积分系统，允许未找到工作前入境找工作，需基本资质和语言能力。 |
| Subclass Job Seeker（Job Seeker Visa） | 允许在德停留6个月寻找工作，需证明经济能力和相关资质。 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €28,000-€36,000, mid-level €36,000-€46,000, and senior can reach over €58,000 (gross). Salary varies by industry, region, and company size.

**问：How can foreign technical personnel immigrate to Germany for this occupation?**
答：First, obtain recognition (Anerkennung) of foreign qualifications as equivalent to German dual training. Then apply for a Skilled Worker Visa or Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte). German language skills of at least B1 are usually required.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for this occupation?**
答：Advance via becoming an Industriemeister or technical specialist, or move into project management, equipment sales, or technical training. Some pursue a bachelor's degree to enter management.
