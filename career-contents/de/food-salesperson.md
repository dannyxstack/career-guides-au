# Food Salesperson（Food Salesperson）职业分析 · DE

**职业代码：6230 – Food Salesperson。**

Works in food retail stores (e.g., supermarkets, butchers, bakeries) handling product display, customer advice, checkout, and inventory management, ensuring food quality and freshness.
