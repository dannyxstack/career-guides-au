# Fishing Crew — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: A1415 (ROME)**

Fishing boat crew members engage in fishing operations at sea, including operating fishing nets, handling catch, and maintaining onboard equipment. This profession requires physical strength, adaptability to offshore operations, and basic navigation skills.

| Stage | Duration | Cost (EUR) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. French fisheries face labor shortages, especially a shortage of young crew members. Automation and sustainable fishing regulations are transforming the industry, but offshore fishing, as a traditional economic sector, still retains key positions and maintains stable demand in the coming years.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Fishing vessel crew members can be promoted to senior crew members (such as chief mate or captain), requiring qualification through navigation training. Some crew members have shifted to land-based positions such as aquaculture, fisheries supervision, or seafood processing. Experienced individuals can also become fishing boat owners.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic navigation and route optimization
- Automated fish detection and net placement
- Automated production line for sorting and cleaning fish
- satellite communication and meteorological data analysis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI fish school prediction models assist in fishing location decisions
- The ship health monitoring system provides early warnings of mechanical faults
- Intelligent net monitoring improves capture efficiency
- Electronic logs and compliance reports are generated automatically

**Human moat:**

- Emergency decision-making and manual equipment operation under harsh sea conditions
- Improvisation repair skills such as repairing damaged fishing nets
- Efficient and intermediary-free communication with captains and shore-based dispatchers
- Localized intuition about changes in sea conditions and fishing grounds

**Skills to build:**

- Basic electronic chart and navigation software operation
- Ship sensor and automation system maintenance
- AI-assisted fishing decision-making tools are used
- Maritime English Terminology and Digital Communication
- Application of small drones in fishery reconnaissance
- Sustainable fishing and data recording compliance capabilities

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable fishing
- Automation
- Shortage of young crew
- Regulatory compliance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is the salary of fishing boat crew?**
A: Junior crew annual salary approx €20,000-28,000, intermediate up to €38,000, captain up to €50,000-80,000. Income often based on catch share, varying greatly between peak and off seasons.

**Q: How can foreigners become crew members of French fishing boats?**
A: Non-EU citizens must first obtain a French work contract, and only after the employer can prove they cannot recruit local nationals can they apply for a work visa. Holding a CAP Maritime or Bac Pro Maritime diploma can increase your chances of success.

**Q: What certificates do fishing vessel crew members need?**
A: Basic safety training in STCW is required; Senior positions such as captains require a professional fishing qualification certificate (Brevet de Patron de Pêche). The CAP Maritime program includes these trainings.
