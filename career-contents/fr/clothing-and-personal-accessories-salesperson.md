# Clothing and Personal Accessories Salesperson（Clothing and Personal Accessories Salesperson）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：D1214 – Clothing and Personal Accessories Salesperson。**

Sells clothing, shoes, bags, and personal accessories in retail stores, offering customer advice, styling tips, and after-sales service.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee work permit） | 需法国雇主担保，岗位需证明无法从欧盟招聘，非紧缺职业获批难度较大。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 仅适用于高级管理或高技能岗位，普通销售员不符合条件。 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Job search and business creation） | 已在法国获学位者可申请12个月求职居留，找到相关工作后可转工签。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for clothing salespeople?**
答：Entry-level salespeople earn €21,000-€24,000 gross per year, including base salary and performance bonuses. Store managers can earn €32,000-€45,000. Salaries vary significantly by brand, location, and individual performance.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation is not in shortage; employers must prove inability to recruit from within the EU, and salary must meet thresholds. Work permit approval is unlikely unless the applicant already holds a French degree or rare language skills.

**问：What are the career progression paths for clothing salespeople?**
答：Can progress from sales advisor to senior sales, department supervisor, store manager, and even regional manager. Alternatively, move into buying, visual merchandising, or brand training. Typically requires 3-5 years of experience and management skills.
