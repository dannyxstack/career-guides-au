# Masonry Worker（Masonry Worker）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1703 – Masonry Worker。**

Masonry workers build and repair structures such as walls, foundations, and chimneys using bricks, stones, and concrete blocks, forming the backbone of the construction industry.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a masonry worker in France?**
答：Entry-level masonry workers earn €22,000-€28,000 per year, experienced workers €28,000-€35,000, and foremen up to €45,000 (gross).

**问：What visa does a foreign masonry worker need to immigrate to France?**
答：Typically a Salarié work permit with employer sponsorship. If the occupation is in shortage, the Passeport Talent may offer a faster route.

**问：What qualifications are needed to work as a mason in France?**
答：The minimum is a CAP Maçon diploma, usually via apprenticeship. A BP Maçon or CQP certification aids promotion. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by ENIC-NARIC.
