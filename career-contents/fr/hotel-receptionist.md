# Hotel Receptionist（Hotel Receptionist）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：G1703 – Hotel Receptionist。**

Welcomes guests, manages check-in/out, handles reservations, and provides information in hotels and other lodging establishments; serves as the first point of contact for guests.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee Work Permit)） | 受雇于法国酒店，需获得工作许可，通过雇主申请。适用于非欧盟公民。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 适用于高技能人才，若年薪达到法国平均工资1.5倍（约4万欧元以上）或持有硕士文凭，可申请。前台岗位通常较难达到薪资门槛。 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Recherche d'emploi (Job Search Visa)） | 完成法国高等教育后，可申请一年期求职签证，找到与学历匹配的工作后转工签。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a hotel receptionist in France?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from 21,000 to 24,000 EUR gross per year, rising to 28,000-35,000 EUR for experienced or supervisory roles, varying by location and hotel category.

**问：How can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a hotel receptionist in France?**
答：The most common path is employer sponsorship for a Salarié work permit. Graduates from French institutions can apply for a one-year job-search visa. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：What are the career prospects for a hotel receptionist?**
答：You can progress to front desk supervisor, assistant manager, or front office manager. Alternatively, move to sales, reservations, or food and beverage, or join management trainee programs in international chains.
