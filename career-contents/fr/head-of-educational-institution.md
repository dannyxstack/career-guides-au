# Head of Educational Institution（Head of Educational Institution）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K2103 – Head of Educational Institution。**

Manages educational institutions (schools, universities, training centers), sets educational policies, oversees teaching quality, and handles budgets and personnel.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 符合高技能人才条件，如管理经验丰富，可申请有效期4年的居留卡 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Visa） | 需获得法国教育机构的工作合同，且雇主证明无法在当地找到合适人选 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Head of Educational Institution？**
- 具有多年教学经验并希望转向管理岗位的教育工作者
- 擅长战略规划、预算管理和团队领导的人才
- 对教育改革和数字化转型有热情的管理者

**谁不适合学Head of Educational Institution？**
- 不喜欢行政工作和繁琐法规的人
- 缺乏长期教育行业承诺的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a head of an educational institution?**
答：In France, public school heads earn about €45,000-€75,000 annually, while private or higher education heads can earn €70,000-€120,000 depending on institution size and experience.

**问：How can foreigners migrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Through the Talent Passport visa, requiring a job offer from a French educational institution and proof of management skills. Immigration is limited due to French language and education system requirements.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a head of educational institution?**
答：Generally requires a Master's degree or higher, French teaching certification (CAPES/Agrégation) or an educational management diploma, and years of teaching or administrative experience.
