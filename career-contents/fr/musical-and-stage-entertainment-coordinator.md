# Musical and Stage Entertainment Coordinator（Musical and Stage Entertainment Coordinator）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：L1101 – Musical and Stage Entertainment Coordinator。**

Organizes and manages live entertainment events such as concerts, shows, and theater productions, coordinating artists, technical crews, and venues to ensure smooth execution.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for musical and stage entertainment coordinators?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from €24,000 to €30,000, mid-level from €30,000 to €42,000, and senior from €42,000 to €60,000 gross per year.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Apply for a Talent Passport or EU Blue Card with a French employer contract or project proof. Some roles require specific certifications.

**问：What are the career progression paths?**
答：From coordinator to project manager, artistic director, or independent producer/entrepreneur.
