# Pipe Layer（Pipe Layer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1705 – Pipe Layer。**

Pipe layers install, maintain, and repair underground piping systems for water, sewage, gas, and industrial fluids. Tasks include excavation, pipe joining, and backfilling, playing a key role in construction and municipal projects.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（非高技能职业）（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Work permit (Salarié)） | 需法国雇主提供长期工作合同（CDI），并证明招聘优先考虑欧盟公民。适用于非紧缺职业，处理周期较长。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 若申请人具有高级技能或项目经验，可通过人才护照获得多年居留，但管道铺设工通常不符合典型人才标准。 |

**PR难度：高（依赖雇主担保，非紧缺清单）（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a pipe layer?**
答：Junior pipe layers earn €22,000-€28,000 gross per year, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior (team leader) up to €35,000-€45,000. Overtime and project bonuses can supplement income.

**问：How can a third-country national migrate to France as a pipe layer?**
答：Pipe layer is not on France's shortage occupation list, making migration challenging. A long-term contract and Salarié work permit are required, with proof that the position could not be filled by an EU citizen. Highly skilled or experienced candidates may consider the Talent Passport, but criteria are strict.

**问：What training or diploma is required to become a pipe layer?**
答：A CAP diploma (2 years), such as CAP Installateur en canalisations, is standard. A BTS in a related field (2 years) can enhance prospects. Apprenticeship (apprentissage) is common, combining study and paid work. Some employers also require a CACES certificate for machinery operation.
