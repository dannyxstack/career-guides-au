# Automotive Mechanic and Vehicle Maintenance（Automotive Mechanic and Vehicle Maintenance）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：I1604 – Automotive Mechanic and Vehicle Maintenance。**

Diagnose, repair, and maintain mechanical and electronic components of passenger cars and light commercial vehicles, including engines, transmissions, brakes, and electrical systems.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee) Work Permit） | 需要法国雇主担保，通过法国劳动局审核，证明无合适欧盟候选人。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 如果符合紧缺职业或高技能标准，可简化申请，有效期4年。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of an automotive mechanic in France?**
答：Entry-level: €22,000-28,000 gross/year; Mid-level: €28,000-36,000; Senior/Supervisor: €36,000-48,000.

**问：How can a foreign automotive mechanic immigrate to France?**
答：Via the Salarié work permit (employer sponsorship) or Passeport Talent (shortage occupation). Requires French-recognized diploma/qualification and proof of French language skills.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities for an automotive mechanic?**
答：Can advance to diagnostic specialist, workshop supervisor, technical manager, or open own repair shop.
