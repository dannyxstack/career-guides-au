# Cinematographic and Audiovisual Production（Cinematographic and Audiovisual Production）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：L1304 – Cinematographic and Audiovisual Production。**

Responsible for planning, shooting, post-production and project management of film, TV and digital audiovisual content, coordinating directors, camera, lighting and sound teams to deliver projects on time and within budget.

---

**学习难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Artistic and Cultural Profession)） | 适用于艺术家和演艺人员，包括电影导演、制片人等，需提供作品和合同，有效期最长4年 |
| Subclass Salarié（Salaried Employee Visa） | 受雇于法国制作公司或机构，需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适合担任高级制片或管理岗位，满足薪资门槛 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Cinematographic and Audiovisual Production？**
- 热爱电影和视听艺术，有创意和项目管理能力
- 适应高强度、快节奏的项目制工作，善于团队协作

**谁不适合学Cinematographic and Audiovisual Production？**
- 偏好稳定朝九晚五工作的人
- 不擅长应对预算压力和时间截止日期

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for film producers in France?**
答：Entry-level positions earn €25,000-35,000, mid-level €35,000-50,000, and senior €50,000-80,000 annually (gross). Large or international co-productions can pay more.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to France through film production work?**
答：The Passeport Talent (Artistic and Cultural Profession) requires a portfolio and a French contract. Alternatively, a Salarié visa can be sponsored by a French employer.

**问：What are the career progression paths for film producers?**
答：Starting as assistant producer or production coordinator, you can advance to production manager, executive producer, or production director. Alternatively, move into content development, distribution, or start your own company.
