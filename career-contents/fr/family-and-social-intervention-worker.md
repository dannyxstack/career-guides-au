# Family and Social Intervention Worker（Family and Social Intervention Worker）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K1305 – Family and Social Intervention Worker。**

Provides social support and educational assistance to families and individuals in their homes or community settings, helping with daily challenges, parenting, budgeting, and fostering social integration and autonomy.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需雇主担保，证明职位无法由法国/欧盟公民填补。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才或创新项目，但该职业通常不满足薪资门槛。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Family and Social Intervention Worker？**
- 喜欢与人沟通、有同理心、注重社会公平的人。
- 愿意在家庭和社区环境工作，能处理多任务和压力。
- 希望从事有社会影响力的职业，不追求高薪。

**谁不适合学Family and Social Intervention Worker？**
- 不适应情绪劳动或频繁面对家庭危机的人。
- 希望高收入或快速晋升的人。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary outlook for family and social intervention workers?**
答：Salaries are relatively low, starting at 22-26K EUR for entry-level and reaching up to 40K EUR for management, with limited growth but stable employment.

**问：Can foreigners become family and social intervention workers in France through skilled migration?**
答：Difficult. The occupation is not in shortage, employer sponsorship is rare; typically limited to EU citizens or those with long-term residence.
