# Electronic Research And Development Technician（Electronic Research And Development Technician）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H1209 – Electronic Research And Development Technician。**

Responsible for designing, testing, and debugging electronic systems; assists engineers in developing new electronic products, involving circuit design, prototyping, and documentation.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an Electronic R&D Technician in France?**
答：Entry-level around €25,000-32,000/year, mid-level €32,000-42,000/year, senior up to €42,000-55,000/year. Varies by region and company size.

**问：Is Electronic R&D Technician suitable for French skilled migration?**
答：Yes. It is a shortage occupation, eligible for EU Blue Card or Talent Passport. Requires at least BTS degree and meeting salary thresholds.

**问：What are the future career prospects?**
答：The electronics industry is growing in France, especially in automotive, aerospace, and IoT. Opportunities to advance to project manager or R&D engineer.
