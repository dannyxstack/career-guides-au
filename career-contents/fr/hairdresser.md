# Hairdresser（Hairdresser）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：D1202 – Hairdresser。**

Hairdressers provide hair care services including washing, cutting, styling, coloring, and perming, typically working in salons or barbershops, with strong manual dexterity and customer service skills.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需获得法国雇主工作合同，且岗位需通过劳动力市场测试（除非紧缺职业）。美容美发不属于紧缺职业，担保较难。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 仅适用于高技能人才或创业者，普通理发师不符合条件。 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Job Search Permit） | 在法完成学业后可申请，寻找美发相关工作。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for hairdressers?**
答：Junior hairdressers earn around €20,000-€25,000 gross per year, rising to €35,000-€50,000 with experience. Income depends on client base, commissions, and salon type.

**问：How can foreign hairdressers immigrate to France?**
答：Immigration for hairdressers is challenging. EU citizens have free movement. Non-EU citizens need a job offer and labor market test. Holding a CAP diploma and French language skills help.

**问：What are the career paths for hairdressers?**
答：Career progression includes becoming a senior stylist, salon manager, or salon owner after obtaining the Brevet de Maîtrise. Alternatively, move into product training or brand consulting.
