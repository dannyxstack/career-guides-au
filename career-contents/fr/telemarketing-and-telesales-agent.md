# Telemarketing and Telesales Agent（Telemarketing and Telesales Agent）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：D1408 – Telemarketing and Telesales Agent。**

Communicates with clients by phone to promote products or services, handle inquiries, overcome objections, and close sales. Common in call centers, insurance, telecom, and banking sectors in France.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需雇主担保，提供CDI合同，薪资须达SMIC以上。中小企业担保难度较大。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于年薪超过53,000欧元的销售专家或管理层，电话销售岗位通常难以达到该薪资门槛。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 需年薪至少54,000欧元（2025年标准），一般电话销售岗位不满足。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Telemarketing and Telesales Agent？**

- 希望高薪且职业前景提升快速的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for telemarketing agents in France?**
答：Entry-level positions pay around €22,000-28,000 gross per year, usually including base salary and commission. Senior roles or team leaders can reach €45,000. Overall, salaries are below the French average.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to France through telemarketing roles?**
答：It is difficult. This occupation is not in shortage, employer sponsorship is rare, and salaries are too low for EU Blue Card or Talent Passport. Consider higher-skilled or shortage occupations.

**问：How to transition from telemarketing to higher-paying careers?**
答：Gain sales experience and move to B2B account management, digital marketing, or customer success. Additional skills in CRM management or data analysis can help.
