# Routing and Folding Machine Operator（Routing and Folding Machine Operator）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：E1302 – Routing and Folding Machine Operator。**

Operates and monitors routing, folding, and binding machines to perform post-press finishing tasks such as folding, collating, and binding, ensuring production quality and efficiency.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee)） | 需法国雇主担保，劳工局审核；职业非紧缺，获批难度中等。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 如为高级技工或管理岗，年薪达38,000EUR以上可申请，需雇主合同。 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Job Search / Business Creation） | 法国毕业留学生可申请12个月找工作居留，找到相关工作后转为Salarié。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level operators earn €24,000-28,000 gross annually, mid-level €28,000-34,000, and senior €34,000-40,000, including bonuses.

**问：Is it easy for third-country nationals to migrate to France for this occupation?**
答：This occupation is not on France's shortage list; the main route is employer-sponsored Salarié visa, requiring proof of no EU candidate. Passeport Talent only for high-salary managerial roles.

**问：What education is required to enter this occupation?**
答：Typically requires a CAP in production machinery operation or a BTS in printing and media; apprenticeship programs are also available.
