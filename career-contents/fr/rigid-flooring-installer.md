# Rigid Flooring Installer（Rigid Flooring Installer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1608 – Rigid Flooring Installer。**

Installs rigid flooring materials such as tiles, marble, and granite, including surface preparation, cutting, laying, and grouting.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a rigid flooring installer?**
答：Entry-level: €22,000-26,000/year; mid-level: €28,000-35,000/year; senior: €36,000-45,000/year. Self-employed or foremen earn more.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate as a rigid flooring installer in France?**
答：Hold a CAP or higher diploma, secure a French employer contract, and apply for a Salarié visa or Talent Passport. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：Is a French certification mandatory to practice?**
答：CAP Carreleur Mosaïste is the basic diploma, highly recommended but not mandatory. Foreign qualifications can be recognized via 'reconnaissance'.
