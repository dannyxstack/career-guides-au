# Buying And Selling Of Art Objects, Antiques Or Second-Hand Goods — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: D1201 (ROME)**

Engaged in the procurement, authentication, appraisal, and sales of artworks, antiques, and second-hand items, possibly operating physical stores or online platforms, requiring knowledge of art history and business acumen.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

You can start as a junior buyer or sales assistant and advance to senior appraiser, gallery manager, or independent shop. After accumulating client resources and industry reputation, we can expand into auction houses or international transactions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Authenticity Authentication and Valuation of Conventional Items (Based on Image Recognition and Database Comparison)
- Product description and listing copy generation (using multimodal large models)
- Responding to customer FAQs and communicating with initial inquiries (chatbot)
- Inventory management and replenishment recommendations (forecasting based on sales data)
- Market trend report generation (automatic data collection and analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to assist in identifying rare artworks, combined with expert experience, enables more accurate judgments
- AI analyzes customer preferences to provide personalized collection recommendations and curatorial suggestions
- AR/VR technology is used for virtual previews to enhance the remote purchasing experience for customers
- Use social media analytics tools to track trends and optimize procurement strategies
- Use AI-assisted pricing models to dynamically adjust prices to maximize profits

**Human moat:**

- Building long-term customer trust and relationship networks (networks and credibility)
- In-depth understanding of the cultural background and non-standard value of artworks (interdisciplinary knowledge)
- Negotiation skills and emotional communication facilitate large-scale transactions
- Development and maintenance of unique supply channels (scarcity control)
- Ethical judgment and source due diligence (avoiding stolen goods and counterfeit goods)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of art appraisal and restoration (to deepen professional knowledge)
- Digital Marketing and Social Media Operations (Expanding Customer Reach)
- Data analytics and business intelligence tools (such as Tableau, Power BI)
- AI tool usage (image recognition platform, ChatGPT-assisted content creation)
- Cross-border trade and e-commerce platform operations (such as eBay, 1stdibs)
- Negotiation and Customer Relationship Management (CRM System)

**Key growth areas:**

- Online Sales Growth
- Art Market Digitalization
- Sustainability & Upcycling
- Luxury Resale

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for this occupation?**
A: Entry-level approx. €22,000-28,000/year, mid-level €30,000-45,000/year, senior €50,000-80,000/year, mainly reliant on commission and client base.

**Q: How can foreigners immigrate to France through this profession?**
A: Quite difficult: this occupation is not in demand, and requires French language skills and industry connections. You can apply for Passeport Talent (cultural category) or obtain a Salarié residence permit by working for a French gallery.

**Q: What is the future career outlook for this occupation?**
A: Due to the impact of online sales and digitalization, traditional brick-and-mortar stores face challenges, but there is still demand in the high-end art and antique markets, so it is recommended to combine online channels.
