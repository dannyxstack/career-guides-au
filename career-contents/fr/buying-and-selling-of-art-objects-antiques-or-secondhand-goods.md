# Buying And Selling Of Art Objects, Antiques Or Second-Hand Goods（Buying And Selling Of Art Objects, Antiques Or Second-Hand Goods）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：D1201 – Buying And Selling Of Art Objects, Antiques Or Second-Hand Goods。**

Engaged in the purchase, appraisal, valuation, and sale of art objects, antiques, or second-hand goods, potentially operating a physical store or online platform, requiring art history knowledge and business acumen.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适合文化、艺术领域杰出人才，需证明经济独立与项目计划 |
| Subclass Salarié | 若被画廊或拍卖行雇佣，需获得工作许可 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高薪管理职位，但此类行业薪资通常不达标 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this profession?**
答：Entry-level around €22,000-28,000/year, mid-level €30,000-45,000/year, senior €50,000-80,000/year, mainly dependent on commissions and client base.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to France through this profession?**
答：It is difficult as the profession is not in shortage and requires French language skills and industry connections. You can apply for a Talent Passport (culture category) or obtain a Salarié residence permit if employed by a French gallery.

**问：What are the future career prospects?**
答：Affected by online sales and digitalization, traditional brick-and-mortar stores face challenges, but high-end art and antique markets still have demand; it's advisable to combine online channels.
