# Leather Goods and Soft Materials Maker (excluding clothing)（Leather Goods and Soft Materials Maker (excluding clothing)）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：B1802 – Leather Goods and Soft Materials Maker (excluding clothing)。**

Crafts leather goods such as bags, belts, and luggage using leather and soft materials, by hand or with machinery.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for leather goods makers in France?**
答：Entry-level: €22,000-€26,000; mid-level: €28,000-€35,000; senior: up to €50,000.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to France as leather goods makers?**
答：Via Passeport Talent or Salarié visa, requiring a job offer and proof of skills.

**问：What education is needed to become a leather goods maker?**
答：Typically a CAP or Bac Pro in leather crafts, often through apprenticeship.
