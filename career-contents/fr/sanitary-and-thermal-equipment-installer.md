# Sanitary and Thermal Equipment Installer（Sanitary and Thermal Equipment Installer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1603 – Sanitary and Thermal Equipment Installer。**

Install, adjust, and maintain sanitary fixtures (bathrooms, piping) and heating systems (boilers, radiators, underfloor heating) to ensure proper water and heat distribution in buildings.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Work Permit)） | 需雇主担保，工作合同>1年，且岗位在人才紧缺职业名单上更易获批 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent – Profession qualifiée） | 适用于高技能人才，需满足学历及薪资条件（≥2倍SMIC） |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Job Search Visa (for graduates)） | 法国毕业留学生可申请，有效期1年，用于找工作 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a sanitary and thermal equipment installer in France?**
答：Entry-level: €22,000-€28,000 gross/year; mid-level: €30,000-€38,000; senior: up to €55,000. Overtime and bonuses can increase earnings.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to France for this occupation?**
答：Best route: obtain a French diploma (CAP/BTS) and then apply for a job search visa, or secure employer sponsorship for a Salarié work permit. Passeport Talent is for highly skilled workers.

**问：What certifications are mandatory for this occupation in France?**
答：A CAP Installateur sanitaire et thermique is typically required. RGE certification is important for energy renovation projects, and Qualibat helps with public contracts.
