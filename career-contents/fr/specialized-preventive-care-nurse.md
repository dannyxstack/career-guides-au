# Specialized Preventive Care Nurse（Specialized Preventive Care Nurse）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：J1505 – Specialized Preventive Care Nurse。**

Specialized preventive care nurses focus on disease prevention and health promotion, including vaccinations, screenings, and health education. They typically work in public health agencies, hospitals, or community health centers.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才，包括医疗专业人员，有效期4年，可带家属 |
| Subclass Salarié（Salaried Employee Visa） | 需雇主担保，工作许可与合同挂钩，有效期1-3年 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于欧盟高技能员工，需年薪达标（约53k+），护士通常符合条件 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a specialized preventive care nurse?**
答：Entry-level around €28k-32k/year, mid-level €35k-42k, senior/management up to €45k-55k/year. Public hospital salaries are lower but stable.

**问：How can a foreign preventive care nurse work in France?**
答：Must first obtain recognition of nursing qualifications in France, then apply for a work visa such as Talent Passport or EU Blue Card. French language B2 level required.

**问：What career advancement opportunities are there for preventive care nurses?**
答：Can advance to nursing supervisor, public health project manager, educator, or become an advanced practice nurse (IPA) through further education.
