# Joinery and Millwork Installer — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: F1607 (ROME)**

Responsible for installing various wooden or metal doors and windows, blinds, rolling blinds, and other closing devices to ensure airtightness, watertightness, and sound insulation. It is a key occupation in building decoration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

You can grow from an apprentice to a skilled worker, and then serve as a project foreman or a small business owner. You can also turn to building energy efficiency consultants or professional installation supervision.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate cutting diagrams and material lists automatically through AI design software, replacing manual measurement and calculation
- CNC equipment replaces manual cutting and repetitive machining
- Prefabricated building components (e.g., modular walls) reduce on-site assembly work
- Laser scanning and BIM technology replace traditional layout and positioning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design tools (e.g., SketchUp + AI plugins) accelerate complex structural design.
- AR/VR technology assists on-site installation guidance, improving accuracy
- Construction management platforms automatically optimize material use and project scheduling.
- AI-driven quality inspection system improves product consistency

**Human moat:**

- Customized installation and adjustment capability in complex on-site environments
- Comprehensive judgment of wood properties, joinery techniques, and structural mechanics
- Skills for handling irregular structures and repairs in old buildings
- Ability to communicate directly with clients and understand personalized needs

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in BIM and 3D modeling software (e.g., Revit, SketchUp)
- Learn CNC programming and digital manufacturing technology
- Familiar with prefabricated building component installation
- Master basic programming (e.g., Python) for automation tool integration
- Improve project management and client communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Renovation
- Energy efficiency
- Smart home
- Timber construction

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for door and window installers in France?**
A: Entry-level annual salary is about 22k-27k euros, and mid-to-senior salary is 28k-45k euros. Overtime and self-employment can significantly increase income.

**Q: How can third-country citizens immigrate to France through this employment?**
A: Mainly through work residence (Salarié) or talent passports (Passeport Talent). Employer sponsorship is required, and the occupation is more advantageous on the Shortage List. French proficiency is a practical requirement.

**Q: What qualifications are required?**
A: At least a CAP or Bac Pro relevant diploma is required. RGE certification is not mandatory but enhances competitiveness. France certifies foreign diplomas (reconnaissance des qualifications).
