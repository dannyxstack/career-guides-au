# Joinery and Millwork Installer（Joinery and Millwork Installer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1607 – Joinery and Millwork Installer。**

Installs various wooden or metal doors, windows, shutters, and rolling closures, ensuring air, water, and sound tightness, essential in building finishing.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee)） | 需法国雇主提供工作合同，且获得劳动局（DIRECCTE）许可，证明该职位难招本地人。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 若具备高级技能或高薪（年薪≥36,500€），可申请人才护照，流程相对简化。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a joinery installer in France?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €22k to €27k, mid-level to senior €28k to €45k. Overtime and self-employment can boost earnings.

**问：How can a third-country national immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Mainly via Salarié (employee) or Passeport Talent (talent passport) visas, requiring employer sponsorship. Being on shortage list helps; French language is essential.

**问：What certifications are required?**
答：At least a CAP or Bac Pro in joinery. RGE certification is optional but beneficial. Foreign qualifications need recognition (reconnaissance des qualifications).
