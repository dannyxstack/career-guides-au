# Preventive Medicine Physician — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: J1101 (ROME)**

Preventive medicine doctors focus on disease prevention, health promotion, and public health, working in French companies, public health institutions, or regional health bureaus, responsible for occupational health checkups, vaccinations, epidemiological surveillance, and health risk assessment.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 6,000. France's demand for preventive medicine doctors is steadily increasing, driven by an aging population, strengthened occupational health regulations, and heightened public health awareness. Job opportunities are mostly in large enterprises, regional health organizations (ARS), and hospitals, with moderate competition.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career paths: Corporate Preventive Medicine Doctor (Médecin du travail) → Chief Preventive Physician (Médecin chef) → Public Health Department Management positions. It can also turn to health policy consulting, epidemiology, or academic research. Continuing study in occupational disease and health management is required.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine vaccination records and reminder management
- Epidemiological data entry and preliminary statistical analysis
- Standardized health screening report generation
- Automated classification of occupational health check results
- Regular health information phone consultation responses

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Disease outbreak prediction and trend analysis based on big data
- Personalized Health Intervention Program Design (Combining Genetic and Environmental Data)
- Real-time risk assessment dashboard generation and interpretation
- Vaccine strategy optimization model construction
- Cross-institutional health data integration and visualization reporting

**Human moat:**

- Communication skills to build trust with patients and the community
- Complex ethical decisions (such as weighing the pros and cons of mandatory vaccination)
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and public health policy-making
- Situational judgments for uncertainty and rare events
- On-site leadership and coordination skills in crisis management

**Skills to build:**

- Python/R and epidemiological statistical modeling
- Natural language processing is used for electronic health record analysis
- Data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Fundamentals of machine learning and causal inference methods
- Understanding AI ethics and privacy regulations
- Communication, collaboration, and cross-disciplinary project management

**Key growth areas:**

- Occupational health
- Public health prevention
- Aging workforce
- Health surveillance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for preventive medicine doctors?**
A: In France, entry-level preventive medicine doctors earn about €45,000–55,000 annually, intermediate doctors €55,000–€75,000, and seniors up to €100,000. Corporate contracts are usually higher than those for public hospitals and include performance bonuses.

**Q: How can non-EU doctors immigrate to France to practice preventive medicine?**
A: You must first pass the French medical qualification (reconnaissance des qualifications), apply for a Temporary Medical Practice Authorization (APT), and then apply for a work visa from an employer, such as Passeport Talent or Salarié. It is recommended to contact the French Health Agency (CNG) or directly with the company.

**Q: What are the career development paths for preventive medicine doctors?**
A: Doctors can be promoted from corporate preventive medicine doctors to chief physicians (Médecin chefs), or move into public health management, epidemiology, or health policy consulting. You can also open a private clinic or pursue academic research. Continuing education should cover occupational diseases, health management, and more.
