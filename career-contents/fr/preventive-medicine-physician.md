# Preventive Medicine Physician（Preventive Medicine Physician）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：J1101 – Preventive Medicine Physician。**

Preventive medicine physicians focus on disease prevention, health promotion, and public health, working in French companies, public health agencies, or regional health authorities, conducting occupational health checks, vaccinations, epidemiological surveillance, and health risk assessments.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才，如医生，需长期合同或资质认可 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Visa） | 需法国雇主提供工作合同（CDI/CDD），并验证资质与经验 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 欧盟蓝卡，要求高等学历+年薪高于阈值（约53,000欧） |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

- 对 bureaucratic 流程缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for preventive medicine physicians?**
答：Entry-level preventive medicine physicians in France earn around €45,000-55,000 gross annually, mid-level €55,000-75,000, and senior up to €100,000. Corporate contracts often pay more than public hospitals and include performance bonuses.

**问：How can non-EU doctors migrate to France for preventive medicine?**
答：They must first undergo French medical qualification recognition (reconnaissance des qualifications), obtain temporary authorization to practice (APT), then find an employer to apply for a work visa such as Talent Passport or Salarié. It is recommended to contact the French Health Authority (CNG) or companies directly.

**问：What are the career development paths for preventive medicine physicians?**
答：You can progress from corporate preventive medicine physician to chief physician (Médecin chef), or move into public health management, epidemiology, health policy consulting. You may also open a private practice or enter academia. Continuing education includes occupational diseases and health management.
