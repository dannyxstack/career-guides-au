# Administrative Operations Officer（Administrative Operations Officer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：M1602 – Administrative Operations Officer。**

Manages daily administrative tasks such as document processing, data entry, scheduling, and office resource optimization to ensure efficient and compliant operations.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|

| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 如作为公司内部调动或创业行政岗位，可申请 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 需年薪≥€53,836（2024），行政岗位通常较难达标 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for an Administrative Operations Officer?**
答：Entry-level gross salary is around €24,000-€28,000 per year, depending on company size and location.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa for administrative operations in France?**
答：The most common path is the Salarié (employee) permit, requiring an employer contract and proof that the position cannot be filled by an EU citizen.
