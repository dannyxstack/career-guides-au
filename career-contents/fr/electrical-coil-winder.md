# Electrical Coil Winder — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: H2601 (ROME)**

Engaged in coil winding, maintenance, and rewinding of electrical equipment such as motors, transformers, and generators, and is a specialized occupation in the electrical field.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Starting as an apprentice or technician, you can gain experience and be promoted to team leader or technical expert. Alternatively, you can pursue higher-level electrical qualifications (such as BTS Electrotechnique) to move into design or management positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Conventional coil winding: automatic winding through a programmed robotic arm, replacing manual operation
- Simple quality inspection: The visual inspection system automatically identifies winding defects
- Data recording: AI automatically generates winding parameters and production logs
- Standard maintenance process: Pre-programmed winding machine performs standardized rewinding
- Material Handling: AGV automatically delivers coil materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex fault diagnosis: AI analyzes electrical parameters to assist in locating winding issues
- Custom design optimization: AI recommends coil turns and wire gauge based on load requirements
- Equipment predictive maintenance: AI analyzes vibration and temperature data to warn of winding machine faults
- Training and guidance: AR-assisted systems display winding steps and key technical points in real time
- Process improvement simulation: AI simulates the impact of different winding processes on performance

**Human moat:**

- Non-standard custom winding: On-site manual adjustment for special shapes or older equipment
- Multi-material handling experience: Feel and tension control under different insulation materials and fine wire diameters
- Root Cause Analysis: Diagnose abnormal noise, overheating, and other comprehensive issues by combining electrical and mechanical knowledge
- Safety and regulatory decision-making: risk assessment and emergency response in high-pressure environments
- Customer communication and on-site service: Explain repair plans and handle emergency shutdown requests

**Skills to build:**

- PLC programming and automation equipment operation
- CAD/CAE software drawings and design adjustments
- Basic programming and teaching of industrial robots
- Fundamentals of Data Analysis (Python/R, for process optimization)
- Advanced reading and troubleshooting of electrical schematic diagrams
- Intelligent maintenance tools (vibration analyzers, thermal imagers)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green energy
- Industrial maintenance
- Electric vehicle
- Automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for coil winders in France?**
A: It varies by region, experience, and employer. The entry-level annual salary is about 22,000-28,000 euros, and the senior can reach up to 45,000 euros. Overtime and performance bonuses are usually included.

**Q: How can foreigners immigrate to France through this profession?**
A: This occupation is considered a shortage occupation and can be applied for through the 'Passeport Talent' (talent passport) or 'Salarié' (work residence). Employer sponsorship is required, and educational or work experience requirements are met.
