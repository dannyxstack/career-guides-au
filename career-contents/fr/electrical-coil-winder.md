# Electrical Coil Winder（Electrical Coil Winder）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2601 – Electrical Coil Winder。**

Wind, rewind, and repair electrical coils for motors, transformers, and generators. A specialized trade within the electrical sector.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for an electrical coil winder in France?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €22,000-28,000 per year. Experienced winders can earn up to €45,000. Overtime and bonuses are common.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to France as an electrical coil winder?**
答：It's a shortage occupation. You can apply for a Talent Passport or Employee Work Permit with employer sponsorship, meeting education or experience requirements.
