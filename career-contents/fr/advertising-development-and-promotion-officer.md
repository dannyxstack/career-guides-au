# Advertising Development and Promotion Officer（Advertising Development and Promotion Officer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：E1401 – Advertising Development and Promotion Officer。**

Responsible for planning, developing, and executing advertising campaigns, coordinating creative and media placements to boost brand awareness.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：一般（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent – Profession Artistique et Culturelle） | 适用于文化、艺术或广告领域的专业人士，需提供项目或雇主合同，有效期最4年。 |
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee) Visa） | 由雇主担保的工作签证，需获得法国公司的工作合同，并符合薪资要求。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟工作者，要求年薪至少53,000欧元（2024年标准），适合高级职位。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an Advertising Development and Promotion Officer in France?**
答：Entry-level: €30,000-€38,000; mid-level: €40,000-€55,000; senior: €60,000-€85,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to France for advertising jobs?**
答：Visa options include Passeport Talent (cultural/artistic) or Salarié (employer-sponsored). The field is not in shortage, but high skills and French language proficiency help.

**问：What are the career progression paths for advertising officers in France?**
答：Advance to project manager, creative director, or account director, or specialize in digital marketing, brand strategy.
