# Executive Assistant（Executive Assistant）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：M1604 – Executive Assistant。**

Executive assistants support senior managers with daily administrative tasks such as scheduling, meeting organization, document preparation, and communication coordination. In France, this role is common in large corporations, public institutions, and SMEs, requiring excellent organizational and communication skills.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 受雇工作居留，需雇主担保，适合已有工作offer的申请人。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 人才护照，适合高技能或高收入职位，行政助理若薪酬达标亦可申请。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 欧盟蓝卡，要求高等学历及高于平均水平的薪资。 |

**PR难度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Executive Assistant？**
- 条理清晰、注重细节的人
- 善于沟通、具备多任务处理能力的人
- 希望在稳定环境中发展职场技能的人

**谁不适合学Executive Assistant？**
- 不喜欢重复性行政工作的人
- 渴望高收入或快速晋升的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an executive assistant in France?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-35,000/year, mid-level €35,000-45,000/year, senior up to €60,000/year.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to France through an executive assistant role?**
答：The most common route is to obtain a job contract and apply for a Salarié work permit or Passeport Talent. EU citizens can move freely.

**问：What key skills are needed for an executive assistant?**
答：Fluency in French and English, proficiency in office software, organizational and communication skills, time management, discretion.
