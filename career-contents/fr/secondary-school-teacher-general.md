# Secondary School Teacher (General) — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K2107 (ROME)**

To teach specialized courses (such as mathematics, French, history, etc.) in regular French junior or senior high schools, one must hold a CAPES or Agrégation teaching certificate, qualifying as a national civil servant or contract teacher.

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAPES (Secondary School Teacher Qualification) | French Ministry of Education | Required | Most public secondary school teachers have a mandatory qualification obtained through the national proficiency exam. |
| Master Degree | French University (INSPE) | Required | Master's degrees in teaching, education, and training are prerequisites for taking the CAPES exam. |
| Academic Credentials (Reconnaissance des diplômes) | ENIC-NARIC | Required | Overseas qualifications must be verified before taking the French teacher qualification exam. |
| Agrégation (Advanced Teacher Qualification) | French Ministry of Education | Optional | Higher-level teaching qualifications allow you to teach in high school or university preparatory classes with higher salaries. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 420,000. French secondary school teachers face regional and subject shortages, especially in subjects such as mathematics, physics, and German. Paris and its suburbs have higher demand. Recruitment of contract teachers (contractuel) increased.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Typically, they start as contract teachers and obtain CAPES or Agrégation through internal exams to become a formal civil servant. They can be promoted to principal, supervisor, or enter educational management positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardized assignment correction and score statistics
- Basic knowledge points are taught and repetitive Q&A sessions are provided
- Student data organization and grade report generation
- Routine classroom management and discipline records
- Administrative document processing (such as course schedules and notices)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized learning path design and intelligent recommendation resources
- Using AI to analyze academic data, it precisely intervenes for underachieving students
- Virtual reality classrooms enhance experimentation and historical experiences
- Automatically generates multi-level exercises and adaptive tests
- AI-assisted lesson preparation to quickly integrate cutting-edge cases

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face emotional communication and psychological counseling
- Guiding critical discussion on complex social issues
- Adjust the teaching pace based on students' nonverbal signals
- Creativity development and project-based learning design
- Parent communication and the transmission of educational values

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI educational tools (such as adaptive learning platforms)
- Data literacy and learning analysis
- Project-based learning and interdisciplinary curriculum design
- Digital Content Creation (Interactive Courseware, Video)
- Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) teaching methods
- Technology ethics and AI literacy education

**Key growth areas:**

- Teacher shortage
- Contract teachers
- STEM focus
- Education reform

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passionate about teaching, highly responsible, and willing to contribute to youth education in France.
- Proficiency in French C1 or above, able to fluently express professional terminology.
- Hold a master's degree in a relevant subject and be willing to pass the French teacher qualification exam.

**Who is not suited**
- Those who cannot accept lower starting salaries and strict civil service management systems.
- Poor at handling adolescent behavioral issues or classroom discipline.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for French secondary school teachers?**
A: Entry-level teachers earn an annual salary of about 30,000-36,000 euros, with over 10 years of experience earning 45,000-60,000 euros. Aggregation-qualified teachers earn higher salaries.

**Q: How can overseas teachers immigrate to France?**
A: You must pass the ENIC-NARIC certification and then take the CAPES or Agrégation exam. After obtaining a work contract, you can apply for a work visa or talent passport.

**Q: What is the difference between CAPES and Agrégation?**
A: CAPES is a secondary school teaching qualification, Agrégation is an advanced qualification, harder to obtain with higher salary, can teach university preparatory classes.
