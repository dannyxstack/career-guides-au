# Meteorologist — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: M1809 (ROME)**

Meteorological information officers are responsible for collecting and analyzing meteorological data, providing weather forecasts and climate analysis, serving industries such as aviation, agriculture, and energy.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

You can be promoted from technician to senior forecaster, or move into roles such as climate research or risk management. Continuous learning of numerical forecasting models and satellite remote sensing technology is required.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic weather station data collection and preliminary quality control
- Simple weather forecast text generation (such as short-term weather forecasts)
- Preliminary manual interpretation of weather radar and satellite images
- Preparation of routine climate statistical reports
- Standardized push of warning information

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis and path prediction for complex weather systems (such as typhoons and severe convection).
- Multi-mode ensemble forecasting interpretation and decision-making based on AI integrated models
- Customized meteorological risk management solutions for industries such as agriculture and aviation
- AI-driven meteorological big data mining and climate trend analysis
- Real-time intelligent early warning of hazardous weather and human-machine collaborative decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Strategic judgment and emergency decision-making for extreme weather events
- Cross-industry meteorological service solution design and communication with clients
- Research and deployment of new observation technologies (such as radar and meteorological satellites).
- Localization tuning and validation of meteorological models
- Manual empirical correction for complex weather phenomena (such as localized strong convection).

**Skills to build:**

- Python/R and Machine Learning Libraries (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Applications of AI weather models (such as FourCastNet, GraphCast).
- Operation of meteorological big data platforms (such as AWS Earth).
- Numerical Forecasting System (WRF) is involved in post-processing
- Risk communication and industry solution design
- Remote sensing and radar data processing (such as Py-ART)

**Key growth areas:**

- Climate change
- Numerical weather prediction
- Data analysis
- Renewable energy

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a meteorological information officer in France?**
A: Entry-level salary is about €25-35k, intermediate salary is 35-50k, and senior-level salary is 50-70k.

**Q: How can foreigners become meteorological informants in France?**
A: Usually, a master's degree recognized by France is required, and after receiving an employer offer, you can apply for a talent passport or work visa. French is a high requirement.

**Q: Is specific certification required?**
A: Public sector prefers engineers graduating from ENM, while private companies accept master's degrees from universities. No mandatory certification, but qualifications are key.
