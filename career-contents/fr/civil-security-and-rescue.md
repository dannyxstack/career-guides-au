# Civil Security and Rescue — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K1705 (ROME)**

Responsible for civil safety, emergency response, and rescue actions, including emergency response and personnel assistance at fires, traffic accidents, and natural disaster sites.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rescue Worker Qualification Certificate (PSE1/PSE2) | French Ministry of the Interior | Required | Basic first aid certificate, essential for professional practice |
| Firefighter selection exam | SDIS (Provincial Fire and Rescue Department) | Required | Physical fitness and theory exams |
| Heavy Vehicle Driver's License (Class C) | French National Driving Permit Center | Optional | Required to drive rescue vehicles |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Civil defense and rescue operations are affected by AI: dispatch and document processing can be automated, but on-site decision-making, complex rescue operations, and interpersonal trust are hard to replace.

Starting as a rescue team member, you can be promoted to team leader or operational commander, or move into specialized fields such as hazardous materials handling and water rescue, or enter training or management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Alert reception and preliminary information entry (AI voice recognition automatic classification)
- Resource scheduling and route planning (AI optimization algorithms replacing manual labor)
- Post-report and document generation (natural language generation and automatic writing)
- Routine Training and Assessment (AI Simulation Scenarios Substitute Part of the Teaching)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Disaster Situation Awareness (AI real-time remote sensing and multi-source data analysis)
- Hazardous environment reconnaissance (drones/robots combined with AI risk identification)
- Rescue decision recommendations (AI simulation and optimal solution)
- Communication Support and Information Integration (AI-Enhanced On-Site Command Efficiency)
- Health Monitoring and Fatigue Alert (Wearable Devices + AI Analysis)

**Human moat:**

- Fast judgment and manual operation under high pressure on site
- Empathy and psychological comfort for disaster victims
- Rescue actions requiring physical intervention (demolition, first aid)
- Real-time cross-team coordination and emergency leadership
- Legal authorization and public safety responsibility

**Skills to build:**

- Drone control and AI-assisted reconnaissance
- Operation of emergency communication systems and command software
- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools
- Psychological first aid and communication skills
- Cross-departmental collaboration and tactical decision-making
- Continuous physical training and maintenance of special equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- Emergency Response
- Risk Management
- Public Safety
- Climate Adaptation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié (Salarié) | For employment residence, you must obtain a work contract from a French employer and prove that the position cannot be held by an EU citizen. |
| Subclass Passeport Talent (Passeport Talent) | The talent passport is suitable for highly skilled professionals, requires qualification certificates and a project plan, and is valid for 4 years. |
| Subclass Recherche emploi (Recherche d'emploi) | Job-seeking residence allows graduates to find work in France, valid for one year, and can be converted to work residence. |

**PR friendliness: medium (★★½☆☆).**

**PR difficulty: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for civil defense and rescue personnel?**
A: Entry-level salaries are about 22,000-28,000 euros, intermediate salaries 28,000-35,000 euros, and seniors up to 50,000 euros. Salaries are influenced by region, seniority, and allowances.

**Q: How can foreign technicians immigrate to France to work in civil defense and rescue?**
A: You can use the Salarié work visa or Passeport Talent passport, but employer sponsorship is required. This profession is subject to administrative access restrictions, with EU citizens given priority.

**Q: What certifications are required for civil defense and rescue?**
A: You must hold a PSE1/PSE2 first aid certificate and pass the SDIS firefighter selection exam. Heavy vehicle driver's licenses and similar items are bonus points.
