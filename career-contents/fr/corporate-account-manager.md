# Corporate Account Manager（Corporate Account Manager）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：D1402 – Corporate Account Manager。**

Responsible for maintaining and developing business relationships with key accounts and corporate clients, formulating sales strategies to achieve performance targets.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才，需年薪达到法国平均工资的1.5倍以上（约45000欧元），可续签 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于非欧盟高技能雇员，需年薪53,748欧元以上（2024年标准），可申请长期居留 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需雇主担保，通过法国劳动局审核，适用于一般雇员，年薪无硬性要求但需与市场水平匹配 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for corporate account managers in France?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €35,000-45,000 per year, mid-level €50,000-70,000, and senior can exceed €100,000, usually including bonuses and commissions.

**问：How can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a corporate account manager in France?**
答：Options include the Talent Passport (requires salary ~€45,000+), EU Blue Card (requires €53,748+ in 2024), or a standard work permit.

**问：What are the career prospects for corporate account managers?**
答：You can advance to regional sales director or business development director, or move into consulting. The B2B market in France is stable, offering good prospects for experienced professionals.
