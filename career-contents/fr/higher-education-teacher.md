# Higher Education Teacher — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K2108 (ROME)**

Engaged in teaching and research at French universities, colleges, or Grande école (Higher Tech Schools), including positions such as lecturer and professor. A doctoral degree is usually required, regulated by the French Ministry of Higher Education and Research.

| Stage | Duration | Cost (EUR) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doctorat | French universities or foreign-accredited institutions | Required | Essential for teaching and research, and requires a thesis defense. |

| Teaching Qualifications (Habilitation à Diriger des Recherches) | French universities | Required | Promotion to professor is essential and requires high-level research achievements. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 95,000. Higher education positions in France are highly competitive, especially tenured teaching positions. The government is promoting digital transformation and internationalization, but fiscal pressures have slowed hiring. STEM fields have high demand, and competition in the humanities and social sciences is fiercer.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Promotion path: Lecturer (Maître de conférences) → Professor (Professeur des universités), must pass national qualification (CNU) and university evaluation. Research output and teaching evaluation are key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generates basic courseware and handouts
- Grade standardized homework and multiple-choice exams
- Answering Common Course FAQs (via chatbot)
- Organize and preliminarily analyze research literature
- Generate student grade reports and basic feedback

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI assistants to design personalized learning paths
- Adjusting teaching strategies through AI analysis of student engagement data
- Use AI tools to accelerate data analysis and thesis writing
- Create adaptive online course modules
- Using AI to simulate complex cases to assist practical teaching

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face in-depth interaction and academic guidance
- Original research and critical thinking
- Intuition and experiential judgment in curriculum design
- Dealing with complex psychological and ethical issues among students
- Interdisciplinary knowledge integration and innovation

**Skills to build:**

- Prompt engineering and AI educational tools (such as ChatGPT, Knewton)
- Learning data analysis and student success prediction
- Online course design and blended teaching methods
- Research design under AI collaboration (such as automated data preprocessing)
- Digital ethics and AI use policymaking
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and project management

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization
- Internationalization
- STEM demand
- Research funding

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for higher education teachers in France?**
A: Lecturers start at about 27,000-40,000 euros per year before tax, professors about 55,000-80,000 euros, with limited additional research rewards, making the Paris region more competitive.

**Q: How can foreign PhDs teach at French universities through immigration routes?**
A: The most commonly used are Passeport Talent or EU Blue Card, which requires a postdoctoral contract or lecturer position first. Non-EU candidates must pass CNU qualification certification, and fluency in French increases their chances.

**Q: Are foreign degrees recognized?**
A: EU degrees can be used directly; third-country diplomas must pass CNU assessment and ENIC-NARIC certification. Doctoral degrees are generally recognized, but supplementary courses may be required.
