# Copy Preparer and Proofreader in Publishing and Press（Copy Preparer and Proofreader in Publishing and Press）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：E1305 – Copy Preparer and Proofreader in Publishing and Press。**

Responsible for proofreading, formatting, and quality control of publications (books, newspapers, digital media) to ensure accuracy and compliance with editorial standards.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee Work Permit)） | 适用于有法国雇主提供CDI合同，且职位符合当地劳动力市场条件。需申请工作许可（Autorisation de travail）。 |

| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等教育学历+年薪超过€54,000的职位。但出版行业薪资通常偏低，较难达到门槛。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a proofreader in France?**
答：Entry-level: €22,000–€28,000/year; mid-level: €28,000–€35,000; senior: up to €45,000. Freelancers charge hourly but income varies.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to France as a proofreader?**
答：Typically need a job offer from a French employer and apply for a Salarié visa. If salary >€40,000, consider Passeport Talent. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：What key skills are needed for a proofreader?**
答：Mastery of French grammar and spelling, knowledge of style guides (e.g., Chicago Manual of Style), proficiency in Adobe InDesign, and strong communication skills.
