# Cultural or Recreational Activity Leader — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: G1202 (ROME)**

Responsible for planning, organizing, and guiding cultural, artistic, or recreational activities aimed at participants of different age groups (children, teenagers, adults), typically working in community centers, resorts, or cultural institutions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

You can advance from event planning assistant to project manager or cultural center director, or transition to tourism or education through training. After gaining experience, you can either be self-employed or establish an event company.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Event Scheduling and Resource Allocation (AI Optimization Algorithm)
- Event promotional copy writing (generative AI)
- Participant registration management and data organization (automated tools)
- Frequently Asked Questions (Chatbot)
- Multimedia material production (such as posters and short video templates)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Creative Event Plan Design (AI-Assisted Brainstorming and Trend Analysis)
- Participant behavior analysis to optimize event content (AI data analysis)
- Cross-language multicultural event planning (real-time translation and localization advice)
- Risk assessment and contingency plan generation (AI simulation scenarios)
- Personalized participation experience design (customized recommendations based on participant data)

**Human moat:**

- Ability to create on-site atmosphere and improvise responses
- Deep cross-cultural and cross-age communication and empathy
- Interpretation and response to participants' nonverbal signals
- The original concept of creative activities
- The ability to build a sense of belonging and trust in the community

**Skills to build:**

- AI event planning tools (such as automatic scheduling and copywriting generation)
- Fundamentals of Data Analysis (Participant Profiling, Effectiveness Evaluation)
- Multimedia content production (short videos, interactive design)
- Project coordination and agile management
- Cross-cultural communication and psychological knowledge
- Narrative and storytelling skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Cultural tourism
- Community engagement
- Digital animation
- Eco-friendly activities
