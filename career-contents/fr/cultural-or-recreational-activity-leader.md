# Cultural or Recreational Activity Leader（Cultural or Recreational Activity Leader）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：G1202 – Cultural or Recreational Activity Leader。**

Plans, organizes, and leads cultural, artistic, or recreational activities for various age groups in community centers, resorts, or cultural institutions.

---

**学习难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用（AUD） |
|---|---|---:|
| BPJEPS 文凭（休闲活动方向） | 2年（BPJEPS） | $3,000~$8,000（可通过学徒制减免费用） |
| DEJEPS 或 licence STAPS | 3年（licence） | $500~$5,000（公立大学费用较低） |
| DESJEPS 文凭 | 2年（DESJEPS） | $5,000~$12,000（高级策划管理方向） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a cultural activity leader?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €20,000 to €40,000, with entry-level around €22,000 and senior over €35,000.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Via a Salarié work permit or Talent Passport, requiring a job offer from a French employer.
