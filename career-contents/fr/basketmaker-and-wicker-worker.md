# Basketmaker And Wicker Worker（Basketmaker And Wicker Worker）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：B1401 – Basketmaker And Wicker Worker。**

Crafts objects such as baskets, furniture, and decorative items using natural plant materials like willow, rattan, and straw. Combines traditional artisanal skills with contemporary design, often in craft workshops, restoration studios, or cultural heritage institutions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the salary for basketmakers high in France?**
答：Not high. Entry-level annual salary is about €20k-25k, senior artisans can reach €35k-50k, but many are self-employed with unstable income.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to France as basketmakers?**
答：Very difficult. The profession is not in shortage, and employers rarely obtain work permits. Usually, one must first obtain a French diploma (e.g., CAP) through apprenticeship to gain local experience before being eligible for a work visa.

**问：What are the career paths for basketmakers?**
答：Common path: apprentice → artisan → senior artisan/workshop manager. Alternatively, move into teaching, heritage restoration, or start your own brand. Some compete in Meilleur Ouvrier de France to gain recognition.
