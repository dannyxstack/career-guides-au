# Design and Management of Public Policy（Design and Management of Public Policy）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K1401 – Design and Management of Public Policy。**

Responsible for designing, coordinating, and evaluating government public policies, ensuring compliance with laws and effective implementation. Typically works in mid-to-senior government roles, involved in strategic planning, budgeting, and cross-departmental collaboration.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适合高学历且年薪达53,000€以上（2023年标准）的非欧盟申请人 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适合具有杰出专业背景或高薪合同的人才，年薪需达约42,000€以上 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 适用于获得法国雇主长期合同且通过劳动市场测试的申请 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for public policy roles in France?**
答：Entry-level positions earn around €35,000-45,000 gross annually, mid-level €45,000-65,000, and senior roles can exceed €90,000. Public sector salaries are fixed with allowances.

**问：How can foreigners enter the public policy field in France?**
答：Highly difficult as senior roles often require French citizenship or competitive exams. However, via EU Blue Card (salary ≥€53,000) or Talent Passport, one can work in think tanks, international organizations, or private consulting firms.

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：Starting as a policy analyst, one can advance to policy director, department head, or senior civil servant (e.g., INSP graduates). Lateral moves to international organizations or private sector are possible.
