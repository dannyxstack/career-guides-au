# Design and Management of Public Policy — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K1401 (ROME)**

Responsible for designing, coordinating, and evaluating government public policies to ensure policies comply with laws and regulations and are effectively implemented. Typically working in mid- to senior-level government departments, involved in strategic planning, budgeting, and cross-departmental collaboration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career paths can gradually advance from policy analyst to policy director, department head, or even senior civil servant (such as members of the Administrative Court). Usually, selection is required through elite institutions such as the French National Academy of Administration (ENA, now INSP).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate preliminary reports on policy impact assessments
- Automatically monitors policy enforcement data and issues abnormal warnings
- Automatically organize checklists for legal and regulatory updates and compliance
- Automatically calculates optimization recommendations for budget allocation plans
- Automatically drafting policy briefs and standardized responses

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to simulate the social and economic impacts under different policy scenarios
- Extracting key demands from public feedback through natural language processing
- Machine learning helps identify causal effects in policy implementation
- Use AI tools to accelerate cross-departmental data integration and visualization
- Dynamically adjust policy implementation priorities based on real-time data

**Human moat:**

- Political judgment and the ability to balance multiple interests
- Public Value Ethical Decision-Making and Responsibility Assumption
- Complex negotiations and cross-departmental collaborative leadership
- Creative anticipation of policy consequences and risk management
- Maintaining credibility and responding to public opinion crises

**Skills to build:**

- Causal Inference and Policy Evaluation Measurement Methods
- Python/R data analysis and machine learning applications
- Natural language processing and text analysis technologies
- Policy simulation model construction
- AI ethics align with public values
- Digital Government and Citizen Participation Platform Design

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital transformation
- Public sector reform
- Green policy
- Data-driven governance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for public policy positions in France?**
A: Entry-level positions earn about €35,000-45,000 before tax annual salary, intermediate positions about €45,000-65,000, and senior positions can earn over €90,000. Civil servant salaries are fixed but include allowances.

**Q: How can foreigners enter the field of public policy in France?**
A: It is very difficult because senior positions usually require French nationality or passing the civil service exam. However, you can work in policy-related positions at think tanks, international organizations, or private consulting firms through the EU Blue Card (annual salary ≥€53,000) or talent passports.

**Q: What is the career progression path for this occupation?**
A: Starting as a policy analyst, you can be promoted to policy director, department head, or even senior civil servant (such as INSP graduates). Laterally, it can also be turned to international organizations or the private sector.
