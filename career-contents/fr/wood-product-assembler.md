# Wood Product Assembler（Wood Product Assembler）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2201 – Wood Product Assembler。**

Wood product assemblers cut, assemble, and finish wooden products like furniture, doors, and stairs using hand tools and machinery, following technical drawings and specifications.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a wood product assembler in France?**
答：Entry-level assemblers earn €20,000-€24,000 gross annually, mid-level €25,000-€30,000, and senior/team leaders €32,000-€40,000, depending on region and experience.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to France as a wood product assembler?**
答：One can apply for a Salarié work permit with employer sponsorship; as a shortage occupation, labor market test may be waived. EU Blue Card and Passeport Talent are options for those meeting higher salary or education criteria.

**问：What qualifications are needed to work as a wood product assembler in France?**
答：A CAP (Certificat d'Aptitude Professionnelle) or Bac Pro in woodworking is commonly required, e.g., CAP Menuisier Fabricant de Menuiserie. These can be obtained through apprenticeships, often at low cost in public schools.
