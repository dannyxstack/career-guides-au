# Short Distance Delivery and Distribution Driver（Short Distance Delivery and Distribution Driver）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：N4105 – Short Distance Delivery and Distribution Driver。**

Drives light commercial vehicles on fixed short-distance routes to deliver goods such as parcels, food, and packages, including loading/unloading and obtaining customer signatures. Often involves last-mile delivery with fast pace.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

**竞争度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee Work Permit)） | 需雇主担保，通过劳工调查证明无法找到欧盟公民。适合非紧缺职业，但配送司机职位常被拒绝。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 仅适用于受过高等培训或高薪岗位，短途配送司机通常不符合条件。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 要求高学历（至少Bac+3）及高薪（2025年门槛约53,000欧元），配送司机不符合薪资要求。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a short-distance delivery driver?**
答：Entry-level drivers earn around €20,000-24,000 gross per year, while experienced drivers can reach €28,000. Earnings depend on hours, region, and employer.

**问：Can non-EU nationals immigrate to France as a short-distance delivery driver?**
答：It is difficult. This occupation is not on the shortage list. Employers must prove no EU candidate is available, and the low salary makes EU Blue Card or Talent Passport unlikely. Consider other logistics roles.

**问：What are the promotion opportunities for short-distance delivery drivers?**
答：Opportunities include promotion to delivery supervisor, fleet dispatcher, or logistics manager. Drivers can also obtain a heavy vehicle license (Category C) for long-haul transport or move into warehouse management.
