# Receptionist and Information Clerk（Receptionist and Information Clerk）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：M1601 – Receptionist and Information Clerk。**

Welcomes visitors, answers phones, provides information, and handles appointments in hotels, companies, and public institutions, serving as the first point of contact.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee (Salarié)） | 需雇主提供工作合同并申请工作许可，适合有法国雇主担保的第三国申请人。由于该类职业非紧缺，获得工作许可难度较大。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 仅适用于高技能或特殊才能人才，普通接待岗位不符合要求。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Receptionist and Information Clerk？**
- 擅长沟通、热情外向的人
- 具备多语言能力者优先
- 希望在服务行业快速入门的人

**谁不适合学Receptionist and Information Clerk？**
- 不喜欢频繁人际互动的人
- 追求高薪或快速晋升的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a receptionist in France?**
答：Entry-level receptionists earn €22,000-€28,000 gross annually, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior up to €45,000, though most positions are modestly paid.

**问：Can third-country nationals immigrate to France as a receptionist?**
答：It is challenging. This occupation is not in shortage; employers must prove no local or EU candidate is available. Fluency in French and a BTS degree or higher is recommended.

**问：What career paths are available for receptionists?**
答：Promotion to front desk supervisor, customer relations manager, or transfer to administration, event planning, or hotel management. Experience can lead to higher salaries in larger firms.
