# Hotel-Restaurant Manager（Hotel-Restaurant Manager）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：G1402 – Hotel-Restaurant Manager。**

Manages daily operations of a hotel-restaurant, including customer service, staff coordination, financial control, and marketing strategies to ensure profitability and guest satisfaction.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Hotel-Restaurant Manager？**
- 具备领导力和团队管理能力
- 热爱服务业，抗压能力强
- 善于财务分析和运营优化

**谁不适合学Hotel-Restaurant Manager？**

- 缺乏人际沟通和客户服务意识

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for hotel-restaurant managers?**
答：Entry-level: €25k-32k; mid-level: €32k-45k; senior: €45k-60k, depending on location and establishment.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate through this job?**
答：Secure a job contract from a French employer, then apply for a Salarié work permit or Passeport Talent. French proficiency is an advantage.
