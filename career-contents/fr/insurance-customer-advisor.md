# Insurance Customer Advisor（Insurance Customer Advisor）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：C1102 – Insurance Customer Advisor。**

Advises clients on insurance products, manages contracts, assists with claims and renewals, working in French insurance agencies, banks or insurance companies.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of an Insurance Customer Advisor?**
答：Junior: 25-32k EUR gross annual; Mid: 32-42k; Senior: 42-55k.

**问：Can a foreigner apply for a work visa as an Insurance Customer Advisor?**
答：Yes, with employer sponsorship. Common routes: Salarié visa, Passeport Talent or EU Blue Card for high salary. ORIAS registration required.

**问：What qualifications are needed?**
答：ORIAS registration mandatory. Recommended: BTS Assurance or Licence. French B2+ required.
