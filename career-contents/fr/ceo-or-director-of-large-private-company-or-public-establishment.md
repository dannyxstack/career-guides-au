# CEO or Director of Large Private Company or Public Establishment（CEO or Director of Large Private Company or Public Establishment）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：M1301 – CEO or Director of Large Private Company or Public Establishment。**

Responsible for strategic decisions, overall management and performance, leading large private companies or public establishments.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent） | 适合高级管理人才，需高学历或高薪合同，有效期4年可续。 |
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié） | 工作居留，需获得工作许可和雇佣合同，通常用于非高管职位。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for CEOs of large French companies?**
答：Entry-level CEO: €120,000-€180,000; mid-level: €250,000-€500,000; senior CAC40 CEOs often several million euros including stock options and bonuses.

**问：How can foreign nationals obtain a work visa for a French large company CEO position?**
答：The most common is the Passeport Talent visa, requiring proof of high skills and salary (usually ≥€53,000/year) with a French company contract. The Salarié work permit is also possible but stricter.

**问：What education and background are needed to become a CEO of a large French company?**
答：Typically a master's degree from a top business school (e.g. HEC, ESSEC) or engineering school (e.g. Polytechnique), plus an MBA. Experience outweighs education; most CEOs have 15-25 years of management experience before promotion.
