# Soft Materials Industrial Design Technician（Soft Materials Industrial Design Technician）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H1205 – Soft Materials Industrial Design Technician。**

Designs and prototypes products using soft materials (textiles, leather, synthetics), combining craftsmanship with technical specifications for fashion, accessories, and furniture industries.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需雇主担保，匹配非欧盟劳动力市场测试，适合有法国文凭或经验者。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Soft Materials Industrial Design Technician？**
- 有艺术审美和手工制作能力的创意型人才
- 对服装、配饰或家具设计感兴趣，注重细节
- 愿意接受持续学习（如数字化工具）的人

**谁不适合学Soft Materials Industrial Design Technician？**
- 对重复性制版工作缺乏耐心的人
- 期望快速高薪或转管理岗位的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for a soft materials design technician?**
答：Entry-level annual gross salary ranges from €24,000 to €30,000, mid-level €30,000-€38,000, and senior up to €48,000, varying by company size, region, and industry sector.

**问：Can non-EU nationals migrate to France for this occupation?**
答：It is difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list, requires employer sponsorship and labor market test, and salary thresholds are challenging for entry-level. A French diploma or prior experience is recommended.
