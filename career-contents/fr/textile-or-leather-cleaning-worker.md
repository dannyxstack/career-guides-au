# Textile or Leather Cleaning Worker（Textile or Leather Cleaning Worker）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：D1205 – Textile or Leather Cleaning Worker。**

Performs dry cleaning, wet cleaning, stain removal and care of textiles (garments, carpets) and leather goods to ensure cleaning and maintenance.

---

**学习难度：容易（★½☆☆☆）。**

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用（AUD） |
|---|---|---:|
| CAP in cleaning techniques | 2年（CAP） | —（公立学校免费，私立学校约2000-5000欧元） |
| Bac Pro in hygiene and cleanliness | 3年（Bac Pro） | —（公立免费，私立约3000-6000欧元） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a textile cleaning worker?**
答：Entry-level around €20,000-24,000/year, mid-level €24,000-28,000/year, senior supervisor up to €35,000/year. Salaries are generally low but stable.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to France as a cleaning worker?**
答：It is possible with a CDI contract and work permit (Salarié visa). However, the profession is not in shortage and competition exists. Obtaining a relevant diploma is recommended.

**问：What skills are required?**
答：Skills in dry cleaning, wet cleaning, stain removal, knowledge of leather and textile care, attention to detail, and physical stamina.
