# Electrical and Telecommunications Network Assembler（Electrical and Telecommunications Network Assembler）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1605 – Electrical and Telecommunications Network Assembler。**

Assembles, connects, and maintains electrical and telecommunications networks (low/high voltage, fiber optics, telecom towers) to ensure reliable energy and data transmission.

| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 要求大学学历或5年专业经验，收入不低于法国平均工资1.5倍；适合有BTS以上学历者 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Recherche d'emploi (Job Search)） | 留法学生毕业后可申请1年求职居留，找到符合条件工作后可转Salarié |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for electrical and telecom network assemblers in France?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €25,000 to €35,000, depending on experience and qualifications. Overtime and allowances can add 10%-20%.

**问：How can a third-country national immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：The direct path is to secure a French employer contract and apply for a Salarié residence permit. With a BTS degree or 5 years of experience, consider the EU Blue Card or Passeport Talent (requires annual salary ~€50,000). Shortage occupations may speed up processing.

**问：Is my non-EU electrician certificate recognized in France?**
答：You need to obtain a qualification recognition (ENIC-NARIC) and possibly take complementary courses to get a CAP or BTS diploma. Passing the Habilitation électrique exam is mandatory. Contact DIRECCTE or France Travail for specific procedures.
