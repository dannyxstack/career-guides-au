# Production Fitter And Assembler（Production Fitter And Assembler）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2901 – Production Fitter And Assembler。**

Adjusts, assembles and tests mechanical components and products in manufacturing, ensuring compliance with technical specifications. Requires precision measurement, machining and assembly skills.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Work Permit)） | 受雇于法国公司，需获得劳动许可。适用于持有CDI合同且雇主履行优先招聘测试的情况。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent - Qualified Employee） | 针对年收入≥42,952欧元的高技能人才，简化流程，可快速获得多年居留。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年收入≥53,940欧元的高技能职位，可带家属，欧盟自由流动。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a Production Fitter And Assembler in France?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from €22,000 to €28,000 per year, experienced workers can earn €35,000-€45,000, and supervisors can exceed €50,000.

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Typically requires a permanent job offer (CDI) from a French employer and obtaining a Salarié work permit. If the salary meets thresholds, one can apply for Passeport Talent or EU Blue Card. This occupation is on the shortage list, but labor market testing applies.
