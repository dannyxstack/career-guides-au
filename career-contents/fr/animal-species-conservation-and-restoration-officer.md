# Animal Species Conservation and Restoration Officer — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: B1701 (ROME)**

Responsible for protecting endangered animal species, restoring populations, managing nature reserves, implementing breeding programs, monitoring ecosystem health, and collaborating with research institutions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career development can be advanced from technician to project leader, protected area manager, or to join the National Park Service, or to join international organizations such as IUCN.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated collection of soil moisture and nutrient data with report generation
- Optimizing fertilization/irrigation plans based on historical yield and weather data.
- Use drones to patrol fields and automatically identify pest and disease areas
- Automated agricultural machinery scheduling and path planning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted precision fertilization decisions to increase crop yield
- Uses computer vision to monitor livestock health in real time and warn of disease.
- Smart sensor network optimizes irrigation and energy consumption
- Machine learning predicts market demand to guide production planning

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency response and complex fault diagnosis
- Unstructured communication and coordination with farm workers and contractors
- Ethical judgment of animal welfare and herd health management
- Comprehensive production decisions across seasons and sites

**Skills to build:**

- Precision agriculture software (e.g., Climate FieldView)
- UAV operation and remote sensing data analysis
- Python/R basics and agricultural data analysis
- IoT device configuration and maintenance
- Project management and team management skills
- Biosecurity protocols and animal health management

**Key growth areas:**

- Biodiversity
- Conservation
- Wildlife Management
- Ecology

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- An ecology graduate passionate about fieldwork and animal conservation
- Patient and meticulous, suitable for long-term monitoring and data collection
- Willing to work in remote areas and adapt to climate challenges

**Who is not suited**
- Those who prefer high salaries or urban office environments
- Not skilled in cross-agency communication and policy coordination

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for animal protection commissioners?**
A: Entry-level salaries are about €25,000–30,000 per year, and seniors can reach €48,000, but growth is slow, and public sector salaries are lower than those in private companies.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to France through this employment?**
A: Requires master's degree + French employer contract, can apply for Salarié or Passeport Talent (annual salary ≥ €53,000), but positions are scarce and competition is intense.

**Q: What is the career advancement path?**
A: From technicians to project leaders, then to protected area managers or researchers, and can also transfer to government departments or international organizations.
