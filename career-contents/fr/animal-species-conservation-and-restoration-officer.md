# Animal Species Conservation and Restoration Officer（Animal Species Conservation and Restoration Officer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：B1701 – Animal Species Conservation and Restoration Officer。**

Responsible for protecting endangered animal species, restoring populations, managing nature reserves, implementing breeding programs, monitoring ecosystem health, and collaborating with research institutions.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适合科学家、研究员，需已获法国机构雇佣且年薪≥53000€ |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Visa） | 需雇主担保，适用于长期合同，且年薪达法国平均工资约28000€ |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Job Search Visa） | 硕士毕业后可申请，有效期1年，允许在法国找工作 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for conservation officers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 25,000-30,000€, senior up to 48,000€, but growth is slow and public sector pays less than private.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Requires a master's degree and a French employer contract; apply for Salarié or Passeport Talent (salary≥53,000€), but jobs are scarce and competitive.

**问：What is the career progression?**
答：From technician to project manager, reserve director or researcher, also possible to join government agencies or international organizations.
