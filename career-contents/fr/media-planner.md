# Media Planner（Media Planner）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：E1402 – Media Planner。**

Media planners develop media strategies for advertising and communication campaigns, including media selection, budget allocation, scheduling, and performance evaluation to maximize client ROI.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Media Planner？**
- 对广告和媒体行业有热情，善于数据分析和策略思考
- 具备创意和商业敏感度，能平衡客户需求与媒介效果

**谁不适合学Media Planner？**
- 不擅长数据分析和数字工具
- 对重复性媒体排期工作缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for a media planner?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €28,000 to €35,000 per year, mid-level from €35,000 to €50,000, and senior positions can reach €50,000 to €70,000. Paris typically pays 10-20% higher.

**问：How can non-EU nationals migrate to France as a media planner?**
答：You can apply for a Salarié work visa (subject to labor market test) or a Talent Passport/EU Blue Card if meeting salary thresholds. French proficiency at B2 level or above improves success.

**问：What career advancement paths exist for media planners?**
答：You can move up to senior planner or media director, transition to digital marketing, data analytics, ad operations, or become a freelance consultant or agency founder.
