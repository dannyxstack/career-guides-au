# Media Planner — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: E1402 (ROME)**

Media planners are responsible for developing media placement strategies for advertising and communication campaigns, including media selection, budget allocation, placement scheduling, and effectiveness evaluation, to maximize clients' return on investment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior media planners can be promoted to senior planner, media director, or shift to digital marketing, data analysis, and other directions. After accumulating client resources and industry experience, you can become an independent consultant or establish a media agency.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Media data collection and cleaning: AI automatically captures audience data and competitor advertising data
- Preliminary media plan generation: AI quickly generates multi-page media scheduling based on budget and goals
- Automatic performance report analysis: AI-generated campaign performance reports and attribution analysis
- Routine Placement Optimization: AI adjusts bids and budget allocation in real time

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Cross-channel strategy integration: After AI provides data, humans engage in creative collaboration and maintain consistency in brand storytelling
- High-value client negotiations: AI simulates negotiation results, and humans collaborate through relationship networks
- Innovative Media Testing: AI predicts the ROI of new media (such as AR ads), and humans design test plans
- Media Response in Crisis PR: AI Monitors Public Opinion, Humans Develop Communication Strategies

**Human moat:**

- In-depth understanding of the media ecosystem: Understand the cultural contexts and user psychology of different media (such as TikTok vs. TV).
- Creative and Data Fusion Capability: Transform complex data into emotionally resonant advertising narratives
- Senior customer trust relationships: long-term stakeholder management that cannot be replaced by algorithms
- Ethics and Risk Assessment: Weigh gray areas such as privacy, brand safety, and social responsibility

**Skills to build:**

- Hands-on with AI media tools: such as Google Ads AI, Meta Advantage+, programmatic buying platforms
- Fundamentals of data science: Python/SQL data analysis, regression analysis, causal inference
- Full-stack media capabilities: Strategic design integrating online-offline and long-tail media (podcasts, communities).
- Visualized storytelling: Turn data into business insight stories with Tableau/Data Studio
- Agile Experiment Methodology: Design A/B testing and incremental effectiveness measurement experiments
- Cross-cultural marketing: Understanding the diverse markets of France and Europe to comply with localized advertising regulations

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Advertising
- Programmatic Buying
- Data-Driven Marketing
- Omnichannel Strategy

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passionate about advertising and media, skilled in data analysis and strategic thinking
- Possess creative and commercial sensitivity, able to balance client needs with media impact
- Adapts to fast-paced work with strong stress tolerance.

**Who is not suited**
- Not skilled in data analysis and digital tools
- Lack of patience for repetitive media scheduling work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for media planners?**
A: Entry-level salaries are about 28,000-35,000 euros per year, intermediate salaries 35,000-50,000 euros, and seniors up to 50,000-70,000 euros. The Paris area is usually 10-20% higher.

**Q: How can non-EU individuals immigrate to France through media planning work?**
A: You can apply for the Salarié work visa (requiring labor market testing) or the Passeport Talent or EU Blue Card that meet the high salary requirements. French proficiency of B2 or above helps improve success rates.

**Q: What career development paths are available for media planners?**
A: Promotion to Senior Planner or Media Director; or shift to digital marketing, data analytics, and advertising operations; You can also become a freelance consultant or start an agency.
