# Port Operations and Maritime Transport Manager（Port Operations and Maritime Transport Manager）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：N3201 – Port Operations and Maritime Transport Manager。**

Manages daily port operations, cargo handling, vessel scheduling, and maritime transport, coordinating logistics chain for efficiency and safety.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €25,000 to €32,000, mid-level from €32,000 to €45,000, and senior up to €65,000.

**问：Is it easy for foreigners to obtain a work visa for port operations in France?**
答：It is somewhat challenging, but employer-sponsored Salarié visa or Talent Passport are viable paths. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：Is there room for career advancement?**
答：Yes, you can progress from operator to supervisor, manager, and regional director. Experienced professionals can move into consulting or international shipping management.
