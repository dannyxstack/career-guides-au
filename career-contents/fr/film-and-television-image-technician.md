# Film and Television Image Technician（Film and Television Image Technician）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：L1505 – Film and Television Image Technician。**

Responsible for image capture, lighting setup, and visual effects in film, television, or digital media to meet the director's artistic vision.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于艺术家或影视行业专业人士，需提供作品集和法国雇主合同。 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需法国雇主担保，工作许可与雇主绑定。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能岗位，要求年薪在法国平均工资1.5倍以上，影视行业较少使用。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a film and television image technician in France?**
答：Junior: €24,000-30,000; mid-level: €30,000-45,000; senior: €45,000-65,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a film and television image technician in France?**
答：Apply via Talent Passport or Employee Work Permit, requiring a French employer contract.

**问：What is the career progression for a film and television image technician in France?**
答：Advance from assistant to cinematographer or lighting director; further to director or VFX supervisor.
