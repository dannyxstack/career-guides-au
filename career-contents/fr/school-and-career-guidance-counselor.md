# School and Career Guidance Counselor（School and Career Guidance Counselor）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K2112 – School and Career Guidance Counselor。**

Provides personalized guidance on educational pathways, career development, and training options for students and adults, helping them formulate academic plans or career transitions, typically working in schools, employment centers, or consulting firms.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a school and career guidance counselor in France?**
答：Junior counselors earn €25,000-32,000 gross annually, mid-level (4-10 years) €32,000-42,000, and senior (10+ years) €42,000-55,000. Public sector pays less but is stable, while private offers more bonuses.

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：They can apply for a Talent Passport or EU Blue Card (requires sufficient salary). Alternatively, study for a French master's degree, then obtain a job-seeking visa (Recherche emploi) and switch to a work permit. The occupation is not in shortage, but certification (e.g., CCO) is crucial.

**问：What certifications are required to become a school and career guidance counselor?**
答：The core certification is CCO (National Diploma of Career Counseling) issued by CNCP. A master's in psychology is an advantage. For regulated positions, CCO is mandatory. Continuous professional development is required to maintain qualification.
