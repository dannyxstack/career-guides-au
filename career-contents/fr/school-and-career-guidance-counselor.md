# School and Career Guidance Counselor — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K2112 (ROME)**

Responsible for providing personalized consultation on academic direction, career development, and training pathways for students and adults, assisting them in formulating educational plans or career transition programs, and often working in schools, employment centers, or consulting agencies.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for academic and career guidance consultants in France?**
A: Junior consultants have a pre-tax annual salary of about EUR 25,000-32,000, intermediate with 4-10 years of experience EUR 32,000-42,000, and senior with 10+ years up to EUR 42,000-55,000. Public sector pay is lower but stable, while private sector offers more flexible bonuses.

**Q: How can third-country citizens immigrate to France through this employment?**
A: Can apply for Talent Passport (high-skilled visa) or EU Blue Card (requires minimum salary). Alternatively, study for a French master's then apply for a job-seeking visa (Recherche emploi), and switch to a work visa upon finding a job. This occupation is not in shortage, but qualification recognition (e.g., CCO) is important for employment.

**Q: What certifications are required to become an academic and career guidance advisor?**
A: The core certification is the CCO (National Diploma in Career Consulting), issued by CNCP. A master's degree in psychology is a plus. For regulated positions, holding a CCO is a statutory requirement. Practitioners also need ongoing training to maintain their qualifications.
