# Retail and Exhibition Space Decorator（Retail and Exhibition Space Decorator）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：B1301 – Retail and Exhibition Space Decorator。**

Designs and decorates retail stores, trade shows, window displays, and other commercial spaces using visual merchandising, lighting, colors, and props to enhance brand image and customer experience.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee (Salarié)） | 需法国雇主提供CDI合同，且职位通过France Travail审核，薪资不低于行业标准。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport (Passeport Talent)） | 适用于设计类杰出人才，需证明有显著成就或项目，薪资通常高于平均水平。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 要求高薪（2024年约54000欧元/年），适合高级装饰师或项目经理。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for retail space decorators in France?**
答：Entry-level earns €24,000-€30,000 gross annually, mid-level €30,000-€40,000, senior €40,000-€55,000. Salaries depend on experience, company size, and region.

**问：What visa options are available for foreigners to work as space decorators in France?**
答：Main options: Salarié (employee visa, requires permanent contract); Talent Passport (for exceptional talent); EU Blue Card (high salary). Non-EU applicants face language and cultural barriers but can succeed with employer sponsorship.

**问：How can a junior decorator advance in career?**
答：Accumulate project experience, build a portfolio, pursue further education (e.g., DSAA) or industry certifications. Advance to visual merchandising manager or creative director, or start your own design studio.
