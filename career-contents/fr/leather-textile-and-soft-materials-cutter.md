# Leather, Textile and Soft Materials Cutter（Leather, Textile and Soft Materials Cutter）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2409 – Leather, Textile and Soft Materials Cutter。**

In France's leather, textile and garment industries, cuts various soft materials (leather, fabric, synthetics) accurately according to patterns or designs, maximizing material yield and serving as a key step in production.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employed Work Permit)） | 受雇工作居留：需法国雇主提供工作合同，并经法国劳动局（DIRECCTE）审核，证明无法在欧盟内招到合适人员。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 人才护照：适用于高技能人才或艺术家，如雇主能证明申请人具备独特手工技艺。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the salary of a cutter sufficient to live in France?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €20,000-24,000, at the level of the French minimum wage (SMIC). Mid-level can reach €30,000. It may be tight in Paris but manageable in other regions.

**问：How can a third-country national immigrate to France as a cutter?**
答：Mainly through the Salarié work permit, requiring a French employer to prove the position cannot be filled by an EU citizen. The Passeport Talent is available for highly skilled artisans. Since the occupation is not in shortage, immigration is moderately difficult.

**问：What training is required to become a cutter in France?**
答：Typically a CAP (2 years) or Bac Pro (3 years) diploma, or through apprenticeship (apprentissage). Industry certification (CQP) is optional but beneficial.
