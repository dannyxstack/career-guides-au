# Wild or Pet Animal Breeder — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: A1408 (ROME)**

Engaging in the breeding, breeding, care, and daily management of wild animals or pets to ensure animal health and welfare, may involve zoos, breeding centers, or pet farms.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior breeders can be promoted to senior breeders or breeding supervisors, and after gaining experience, can transition to animal health management, zoo management, or start their own pet farms.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily animal health data recording and report generation, automatically collected and analyzed by AI
- Automatic feeding systems replace timed and measured feeding
- Automatic monitoring and adjustment of environmental parameters (temperature, humidity, lighting).
- Preliminary identification and alarm of abnormal behaviors based on video analysis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze individual animal health trends and provide early warning about diseases
- Machine vision helps identify individual animals and community relationships
- Automatically generates breeding pairing recommendations to optimize genetic diversity
- The intelligent environmental control system automatically adjusts based on animal behavior feedback

**Human moat:**

- Delicate sensitivity to animal emotions and instant soothing abilities
- Emergency judgment and manual operation in handling sudden animal injuries or illnesses
- Build and communicate deep trust with pet owners or the public
- Interpretation and intervention of non-standardized behaviors based on experience

**Skills to build:**

- Knowledge of animal behavior and welfare science
- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools (such as health monitoring apps)
- Basic veterinary first aid skills
- Customer communication and emotional service capabilities
- Automated equipment maintenance and simple troubleshooting

**Key growth areas:**

- Animal care
- Pet industry growth
- Conservation breeding
- Rural employment

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Loves animals, patient and responsible

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for wildlife or pet keepers?**
A: Junior zookeeper annual salary approximately EUR 19,000-23,000 (pre-tax), senior or management roles up to EUR 38,000. Wages are generally below the French average, but some specialized breeding farms offer higher income.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to France through this employment?**
A: The difficulty is quite high. This occupation is not considered a shortage in France; employers must prove they cannot recruit EU citizens and must hold a Certificat de capacité, which requires high language and cultural adaptability.
