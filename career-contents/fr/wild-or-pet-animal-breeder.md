# Wild or Pet Animal Breeder（Wild or Pet Animal Breeder）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：A1408 – Wild or Pet Animal Breeder。**

Breed, raise, and care for wild or pet animals, ensuring their health and welfare in zoos, breeding centers, or pet farms.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Work Permit (Salarié)） | 需法国雇主担保，证明职位无法由欧盟公民填补，适用于长期工作合同 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Wild or Pet Animal Breeder？**
- 热爱动物，有耐心和责任心
- 愿意在农村或户外工作
- 对动物繁殖和健康管理感兴趣

**谁不适合学Wild or Pet Animal Breeder？**
- 不喜欢体力劳动或不规律作息
- 对动物毛发或气味敏感

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for wild or pet animal breeders?**
答：Entry-level breeders earn around €19,000-23,000 gross annually, while senior or management positions can reach €38,000. Salaries are generally below the French average, but some specialized breeding farms offer higher pay.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：It's difficult. This profession is not on the shortage list; employers must prove no EU candidates are available, and the Certificat de capacité is required, along with high language and cultural adaptation demands.
