# Freight Transport Forwarding（Freight Transport Forwarding）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：N1201 – Freight Transport Forwarding。**

Organizes and coordinates freight transport, optimizes routes and costs, manages shipping documents, and ensures timely delivery.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较友好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才，如具备硕士学历或多年经验，可获4年居留 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需雇主担保，工作合同至少1年，工资不低于SMIC |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等学历+高薪职位，年薪需达约53,000欧元（2024年标准） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for freight forwarding?**
答：Entry-level: €26,000-32,000; mid-level: €33,000-42,000; senior: up to €55,000.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to France as a freight forwarder?**
答：Apply for a Salarié work permit (employer sponsorship) or Talent Passport (high-skilled) with required qualifications and salary.
