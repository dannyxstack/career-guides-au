# Nurse Anesthetist（Nurse Anesthetist）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：J1503 – Nurse Anesthetist。**

Nurse anesthetists provide anesthesia care before, during, and after surgery, including induction, maintenance, and emergence, monitoring vital signs, and managing emergencies, as key members of the surgical team.

---

## 1. 教育路径 / 周期 / 费用

**学习难度：较高（★★★★☆）。**

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用（AUD） |
|---|---|---:|
| 基础护理文凭（DE Infirmier）+ 麻醉专科文凭 | 3年（DE）+ 2年（IADE） | $5,000~$15,000（公立学校学费低，私立稍高；学徒制可获收入） |
| 麻醉护士进修（继续教育） | 2年（IADE） | $8,000~$20,000（需先持有DE Infirmier，部分项目有资助） |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Nurse Anesthetist？**
- 有护理背景且对麻醉感兴趣的人
- 能承受高压、细心负责的医护工作者
- 希望在医疗领域专精发展的人

**谁不适合学Nurse Anesthetist？**
- 无法接受夜间值班和紧急任务的人
- 对高压力环境不适应的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a nurse anesthetist in France?**
答：Entry-level: €28,000-35,000; mid-level: €35,000-45,000; senior: up to €60,000, including night shift and overtime allowances.

**问：How can a foreign nurse anesthetist immigrate to France?**
答：Obtain recognition of qualifications, register with the French nursing order, then apply for a work visa such as Talent Passport or Salarié with employer sponsorship.

**问：What qualifications are required to become a nurse anesthetist in France?**
答：Requires a national nursing diploma (DE Infirmier) and a specialized anesthesia diploma (DE IADE), plus registration with the nursing order.
