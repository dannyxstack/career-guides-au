# Education and Surveillance Officer in Educational Establishments（Education and Surveillance Officer in Educational Establishments）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K2104 – Education and Surveillance Officer in Educational Establishments。**

Responsible for student supervision, discipline, and extracurricular activities in French schools and educational establishments, ensuring campus safety and order while assisting in educational support.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee Work Permit)） | 需法国雇主提供工作合同，并证明职位无法由欧盟公民填补。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 若具备高级技能或硕士以上学历，可申请此签证，有效期4年。 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Recherche d'emploi (Job Search Visa)） | 法国毕业生可申请一年找工作签证，找到工作后转为工作居留。 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation in France?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €20,000-€25,000, senior can reach €35,000, with indexed growth in public schools but overall low.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：Yes, but requires employer sponsorship and proof that no EU citizen can fill the position. Local competition is high.

**问：What diplomas or certificates are required?**
答：At least a BAFA certificate or CAP diploma; some positions require PSC1 first aid certificate. French qualification recognition may be needed.
