# Education and Surveillance Officer in Educational Establishments — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K2104 (ROME)**

Responsible for student supervision, discipline management, and organizing extracurricular activities within French primary and secondary schools or educational institutions, ensuring campus safety and order, while also participating in educational support work.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Assistant Supervisors can be promoted to Senior Supervisors or Educational Consultants, and some become formal teachers or school administrators through internal examinations. You can also transition to youth social workers or community education coordinators.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Real-time patrol and abnormality alarms on campus surveillance footage (AI video analysis replacement)
- Attendance and discipline records are manually entered and summarized (automated system takes over).
- Basic student information consultation and process guidance (chatbot replacement)
- Routine patrol and safety inspection report generation (AI automatically generates logs)
- Extracurricular activity sign-in and check-out management (self-service check-in terminal with AI confirmation)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Design and adjust disciplinary management programs (AI provides data-driven behavioral pattern analysis)
- Organize personalized extracurricular activities (AI recommends event themes and evaluates participation effectiveness)
- Early identification of students' psychological and social risks (AI analyzes abnormal behavioral signals)
- Communicate Student Status Efficiently with Parents (AI Generates Personalized Communication Suggestions and Summaries)
- Handling complex mediation cases (AI provides case databases and script references)

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency decision-making and crisis intervention
- Emotional support and trust among students are established
- One-on-one care for cross-cultural/special needs students
- Creative activity design and improvisational organizational skills
- Moral judgment and grasping legal boundaries

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI monitoring and data analysis tools (such as behavior prediction platforms)
- Basic psychological counseling and conflict mediation techniques
- Use of digital communication and collaboration platforms
- Emergency management and safety plan design
- Interdisciplinary event planning skills
- Understanding data privacy regulations

**Key growth areas:**

- Education
- Youth Supervision
- School Safety
- Social Inclusion

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy communicating with teenagers, are patient, and have a sense of responsibility
- Those aspiring to the education sector but who have not yet obtained a teaching qualification
- Those seeking stable employment who can adapt to school schedules

**Who is not suited**
- People who feel pressured by discipline management or conflict resolution
- People seeking high salary or rapid promotion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for this profession in France?**
A: Junior annual salary: EUR 20,000-25,000, senior up to EUR 35,000. Public school salaries increase by index but are relatively low overall.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to France through this employment?**
A: Yes, but employer sponsorship is required and proof that the position cannot be filled by an EU citizen. There are many local job seekers in France, making competition fierce.

**Q: What diploma or certificate is required?**
A: At minimum, a BAFA certificate or CAP diploma is required; some positions require a PSC1 emergency medical certificate. French academic credential certification may require additional procedures.
