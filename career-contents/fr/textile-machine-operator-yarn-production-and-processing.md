# Textile Machine Operator (Yarn Production and Processing)（Textile Machine Operator (Yarn Production and Processing)）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2404 – Textile Machine Operator (Yarn Production and Processing)。**

Operate and monitor machines for yarn production and processing, such as spinning, twisting, and winding machines, ensuring quality and efficiency in textile manufacturing.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Employee visa） | 受雇于法国企业，雇主需证明无法找到欧盟候选人。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才，但纺织操作员通常不满足条件。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Textile Machine Operator (Yarn Production and Processing)？**
- 喜欢动手操作机器且注重细节的人
- 愿意轮班工作（含夜班）
- 在制造业环境中能适应重复性工作

**谁不适合学Textile Machine Operator (Yarn Production and Processing)？**
- 偏好办公室或创造型工作
- 无法适应噪音和粉尘环境

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for textile machine operators?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €22,000–€26,000 gross, mid-level €26,000–€30,000, senior €30,000–€35,000.

**问：Can third-country nationals immigrate through this occupation?**
答：It is difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list; employers must prove no EU candidates. Consider apprenticeship or obtaining a French diploma.

**问：What are career progression paths?**
答：Promotion to line supervisor, quality inspector, or transition to automation maintenance or textile process engineer via training.
