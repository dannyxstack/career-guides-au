# Art Teacher — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: K2105 (ROME)**

Teach art courses such as fine arts, music, drama, and dance in public or private institutions to cultivate students' aesthetic sense and creativity.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAPES d'arts plastiques | French Ministry of Education | Required | A must-have for public secondary school art teachers, requiring passing competitive exams |
| Agrégation d'arts | French Ministry of Education | Optional | Senior teacher qualification, available for high school and university preparatory courses |
| DAEFLE | Alliance Française | Optional | If teaching French art courses, a French teaching qualification may be required |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 45,000. The French government continues to invest in cultural education, and art education positions remain stable. Due to school budget constraints, competition for positions within the establishment is fierce, but part-time and contract opportunities are increasing.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Assistant teachers can be promoted to senior teachers, subject leaders, or research directors. Some teachers have turned to private studios, cultural institution management, or independent artists.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Teaching basic art history and technical knowledge
- Preliminary grading and feedback on student assignments
- Generation and organization of routine teaching materials
- Simple matters in classroom discipline management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized learning path design and adaptive teaching
- Creative inspiration for student works and collaborative creation with AI
- Planning and implementation of interdisciplinary art projects
- Real-time data analysis and adjustment of teaching effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- In-depth insights into students' emotional and aesthetic development
- Live demonstrations and improvisational artistic expression
- Create a safe and inclusive classroom atmosphere
- Critical thinking and original evaluation

**Skills to build:**

- Digital art tools and AI creation software (such as DALL· E、Midjourney）
- Data analysis and learning management system optimization
- Interdisciplinary Curriculum Design (Art + Technology)
- Digital content creation and multimedia teaching
- Adaptive teaching methods and personalized tutoring
- Innovate classroom management and community development

**Key growth areas:**

- arts education
- cultural policy
- pedagogical innovation
- digital arts

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for art teachers?**
A: Junior annual salary at public schools around €25k–30k, senior up to €50k–55k. Private or association institutions may be slightly lower.

**Q: How can a citizen from a third country become a French art teacher?**
A: A Master MEEF recognized in France and a French C1 level are usually required. After passing the CAPES exam, you can apply for the Passeport Talent or Salarié visa.

**Q: Is a French teaching qualification required?**
A: Teaching in public schools requires holding CAPES or Agrégation. Private or association institutions may only require professional diplomas.
