# Medical Technical Assistant（Medical Technical Assistant）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：J1303 – Medical Technical Assistant。**

Medical technical assistants assist physicians in diagnostic and therapeutic procedures, operate medical equipment, prepare patient records, and ensure smooth technical workflows in healthcare settings.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 针对高技能人才，需雇主担保，合同薪资不低于法国平均工资的1.5倍 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 需雇主提供工作合同，且职位在紧缺职业列表上可简化流程 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适合高薪职位（年薪≥53,000欧元），但医疗技术助理薪资通常不达标 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a medical technical assistant in France?**
答：Entry-level ranges from €25,000 to €30,000, experienced up to €38,000-€45,000 gross annually.

**问：Can foreign medical technical assistants immigrate to France?**
答：Yes, but they need qualification recognition (reconnaissance des qualifications) and job offers in shortage occupations facilitate work permits.

**问：What is the career progression for this role?**
答：Progression to senior assistant, team lead, or specialization in radiography, ultrasound via further education.
