# Leather And Soft Materials Prototype Assembler（Leather And Soft Materials Prototype Assembler）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2411 – Leather And Soft Materials Prototype Assembler。**

Responsible for handcrafting prototypes using leather and soft materials, involving cutting, sewing, and assembly, common in luxury goods, fashion, and automotive upholstery.
