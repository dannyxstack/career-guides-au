# Primary School Teacher（Primary School Teacher）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K2106 – Primary School Teacher。**

Primary school teachers in France educate students aged 6-11 in core subjects, fostering foundational skills and social development. They work within the public education system under national curriculum guidelines.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent - EU Blue Card） | 适用于高级教师或教育专家，需提供法国学校工作合同，年薪不低于42,052欧元 |
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Work permit)） | 普通工作签证，需法国雇主担保，证明该职位无法由欧盟公民胜任 |
| Subclass Recherche emploi（Recherche d'emploi / Création d'entreprise） | 毕业生临时居留，允许在法国寻找工作，有效期1年 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Primary School Teacher？**
- 热爱教育事业，有耐心和责任心的人
- 具有法语C1以上水平，希望获得稳定公共职位的人
- 愿意在法国偏远地区或海外省任教的人

**谁不适合学Primary School Teacher？**
- 无法通过法语教师资格考试（CRPE）的非欧盟人士
- 寻求快速高薪职业的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a primary school teacher in France?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €25,000 to €45,000 (entry to senior level), with net monthly pay of approximately €1,700 to €2,800. Salary is determined by seniority and grade, with limited additional allowances.

**问：How can non-EU nationals become primary school teachers in France?**
答：They must pass the CRPE exam, which is very strict for non-EU teachers, requiring French C1 level and recognized qualifications. Options include Passeport Talent or Salarié visa, but opportunities are limited; consider priority shortage areas.

**问：Is there career progression for primary school teachers in France?**
答：Yes, they can become school management (e.g., deputy principal), pedagogical advisor, or inspector. This requires additional exams and evaluations, with limited salary increase but greater responsibilities.
