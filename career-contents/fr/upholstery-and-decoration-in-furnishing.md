# Upholstery And Decoration In Furnishing（Upholstery And Decoration In Furnishing）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：B1806 – Upholstery And Decoration In Furnishing。**

Upholsterers and furnishing decorators repair, make and decorate furniture, including reupholstery of sofas and chairs, and create interior decor items like curtains and cushions. The work combines craftsmanship with design creativity, often in high-end workshops.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for upholsterers in France?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €21,000-26,000, mid-level €26,000-35,000, and senior up to €50,000. Paris area is slightly higher, but overall it is a moderate-low income.

**问：Can third-country nationals immigrate to France through this occupation?**
答：It is difficult. This occupation is not in shortage, so immigration paths are limited. Usually requires a Salarié work permit with labor market test. Passeport Talent is possible for exceptional artisans.

**问：How to become an upholsterer in France?**
答：The most common path is completing a CAP (2-year apprenticeship) or Bac Pro (2 years), then possibly a BTS. Apprenticeship is mainstream, with salary and free tuition.
