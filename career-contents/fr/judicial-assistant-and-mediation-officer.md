# Judicial Assistant and Mediation Officer（Judicial Assistant and Mediation Officer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：K1901 – Judicial Assistant and Mediation Officer。**

Judicial assistants and mediation officers support judges in case management, organize mediation sessions, and facilitate dispute resolution within the judicial system.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for judicial assistants and mediation officers?**
答：Entry-level around €25,000-€32,000, mid-level €32,000-€42,000, senior up to €55,000+; public sector salaries are stable but lower than private mediators.

**问：How can foreigners become judicial assistants and mediation officers in France through skilled migration?**
答：Require a French law degree or national mediator certification, obtain a Talent Passport or Employee work permit with a job offer from a court or mediation organization, and French proficiency at C1 level.

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：Progress from junior assistant to senior mediator, mediation program manager, or transition to court clerk or judge assistant; also possible to become an independent mediator.
