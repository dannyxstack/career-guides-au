# Actuarial Studies in Insurance（Actuarial Studies in Insurance）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：C1105 – Actuarial Studies in Insurance。**

Actuaries apply mathematics, statistics, and financial theory to assess risks in insurance, pensions, and investments, designing pricing models and ensuring solvency. This role is highly specialized in the French insurance sector and requires rigorous certification.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for actuaries in France?**
答：Entry-level actuaries earn approximately €40,000-€50,000 gross annually, mid-level €55,000-€75,000, senior €80,000-€120,000. Chief actuaries can exceed €150,000. Includes bonuses.

**问：How can non-EU actuaries migrate to France?**
答：Via the EU Blue Card (salary ≥ €53,469.50) or Talent Passport (salary ≥ 2x SMIC ~€36,034). Both require a master's degree and a job offer.

**问：What certifications are required for actuaries in France?**
答：The Actuary Diploma from the Institute of Actuaries (IA) is mandatory. It typically follows a master's in actuarial science and requires passing all IA exams.
