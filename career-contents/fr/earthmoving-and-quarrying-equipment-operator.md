# Earthmoving and Quarrying Equipment Operator（Earthmoving and Quarrying Equipment Operator）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：F1302 – Earthmoving and Quarrying Equipment Operator。**

Operates heavy machinery such as bulldozers, excavators, and loaders for earthmoving, grading, and quarrying, ensuring safety and productivity on construction sites.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié Passport Talent） | 适合有法国雇主担保的熟练操作员，需提供工作合同并证明薪资达一定水平。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 如薪资达标（2024年约53,000欧元），可申请，但本行较低，通常不符合。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an earthmoving equipment operator?**
答：Entry-level around €24,000-€30,000/year, mid-level €30,000-€38,000, senior up to €48,000. Overtime and allowances can boost income.

**问：How can a foreigner obtain a French work visa for this occupation?**
答：Generally requires a French employer to sponsor a Salarié visa. EU Blue Card is usually not applicable due to salary level. CACES certification is essential to prove skills.

**问：Is CACES certification mandatory? How to obtain it?**
答：Yes, CACES R383 is legally mandatory. Obtain it via an INRS-approved training center through theory and practical exams, costing €1,000-€2,000.
