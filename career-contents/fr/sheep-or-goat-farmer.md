# Sheep or Goat Farmer（Sheep or Goat Farmer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：A1410 – Sheep or Goat Farmer。**

Responsible for daily care, breeding, health management, and pasture maintenance of sheep or goats, ensuring animal welfare and productivity as a key link in the French agri-food chain.

| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport – Qualified Employee） | 适用于持有硕士或同等学历，且合同年薪≥38,431欧元（2025标准）的技术人才，但农业领域通常达不到，除非高级技术员。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for sheep or goat farmers in France?**
答：Entry-level employees earn about €18,000-22,000 gross annually; experienced managers or farm owners may earn €28,000-35,000. Self-employed farmers' income varies significantly.

**问：How can foreign workers immigrate to France for livestock farming?**
答：Livestock farming is a shortage occupation; apply for a Salarié work permit with employer attestation of recruitment difficulty. Alternatively, study agriculture (CAP/BTSA) and then switch to a work permit.

**问：What are the career paths for sheep farmers?**
答：Start as a herdsman, advance to technician, farm supervisor, or pursue agricultural training (BTSA) to become an advisor, quality inspector, or start your own farm.
