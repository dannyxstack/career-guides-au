# Prosthetist and Orthotist — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: J1411 (ROME)**

Prosthetists and orthotists are responsible for designing, making, and fitting prosthetics and orthotics to help patients restore function. You will need to work within a medical team, combining technology with patient care.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

You can be promoted from technician to team leader or start your own practice, or specialize in a specific area (such as upper limb prosthetics or spinal orthoses). Further studies can include research and development or teaching.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial design of prosthetics and orthotics was carried out using CAD software
- Automatically generates adapted models from scan data
- Adjust recommendations through AI analysis of gait data
- Automatically generates material lists and production manuals

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI is used to simulate the force distribution of orthotics to optimize structural design
- Machine learning predicts patient rehabilitation progress and adjusts adaptation plans
- Use AR-assisted precise positioning and adjustment of prosthetic components
- Real-time fine-tuning of orthotic parameters using remote monitoring data
- AI assists patient education and self-management

**Human moat:**

- Communicate with patients to understand their life goals and psychological needs
- Fine manual adjustments are made based on patient feedback
- Managing complex complications and individual differences
- Cross-medical team collaboration to develop comprehensive rehabilitation plans
- Follow ethics and regulations to ensure safety and effectiveness

**Skills to build:**

- Master CAD, 3D modeling, and printing technologies
- Learn AI design tools (such as Generative Design)
- Data analysis and gait interpretation skills
- Telemedicine communication and monitoring skills
- Materials Science (Smart Materials, Lightweight Materials)
- Continuous learning of medical device regulations (such as CE marking)

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Orthopedic rehabilitation
- Chronic disease management
- Technological advances

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for prosthetics and orthotists in France?**
A: Entry-level salaries are about €25,000-32,000, intermediate salaries €32,000-42,000, and seniors can earn over €55,000; private institutions or independent businesses earn even higher incomes.

**Q: How can non-EU individuals become French prosthetics and orthotists through skilled migration?**
A: You must first pass the French Ministry of Health's Reconnaissance des qualifications certification, then find a job and obtain Passeport Talent or Salarié residency. Language requirement: French B2 or above.

**Q: What career development paths are available for prosthetics and orthotists?**
A: Can advance to team leader, open own clinic, or specialize in areas such as sports prosthetics or pediatric orthotics. Can also transition to R&D or teaching.
