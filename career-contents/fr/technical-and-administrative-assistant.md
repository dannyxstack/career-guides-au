# Technical and Administrative Assistant（Technical and Administrative Assistant）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：M1605 – Technical and Administrative Assistant。**

Provides technical and administrative support including document management, data entry, meeting scheduling, and client reception. Needed across all sectors in France, especially SMEs.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Work Permit)） | 受雇于法国公司，由雇主担保申请工作许可，需证明职位无法由欧盟公民填补。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent (Talent Passport)） | 适合高技能人才，但行政助理通常不符合条件，除非有特殊背景。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for administrative assistants in France?**
答：Entry-level administrative assistants earn €22,000-€28,000 gross per year, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior up to €45,000. Often includes meal vouchers and transport allowance.

**问：Can third-country nationals immigrate to France as an administrative assistant?**
答：It is difficult. Administrative assistant is not a shortage occupation. Employers must prove inability to hire EU citizens. Local experience or special language skills (e.g., Chinese-French bilingual) may help.
