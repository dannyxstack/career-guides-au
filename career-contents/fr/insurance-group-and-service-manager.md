# Insurance Group and Service Manager（Insurance Group and Service Manager）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：C1108 – Insurance Group and Service Manager。**

Manages insurance business teams, coordinates underwriting, claims, and customer service to achieve performance goals and improve service quality.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an insurance team manager?**
答：Entry-level: €35k-45k, mid-level: €45k-60k, senior: €60k-85k gross annually, including bonuses and profit sharing.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Apply for Talent Passport or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship. Shortage occupation recognition can simplify the process.

**问：What certifications are required to become an insurance team manager?**
答：AMF certification is mandatory for selling insurance investment products; CQP manager certificate is optional but strongly recommended.
