# Navigation Officer（Navigation Officer）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：N3101 – Navigation Officer。**

Responsible for safe ship navigation, route planning, operation of navigation equipment, and crew management, ensuring maritime transport complies with international regulations.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Talent Passport） | 适用于高技能人才，如高级航海官员，需雇主担保及法国海事局认可 |
| Subclass Salarié（Employee Work Permit） | 适用于长期雇佣合同，需通过劳动力市场测试（Opposition au travail） |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高薪职位（年薪≥53,000 EUR），但航海职位常驻船舶，可能不适用岸基要求 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for navigation officers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €30,000 to €40,000 including sea allowance; mid-level (second/first officer) €45,000-€60,000; senior (captain) €65,000-€90,000. Salary varies by vessel type, route, and company.

**问：What immigration pathways exist for non-EU navigation officers to work in France?**
答：Options include Talent Passport or Employee Work Permit, but require a French employer sponsor and qualification recognition by the French Maritime Administration (DMF). EU Blue Card is not applicable as seafaring is not considered high-skilled.

**问：What certifications are required for navigation officers in France?**
答：Must hold a French maritime officer certificate (3rd, 2nd, or 1st class) issued by DMF, and complete STCW training. Non-EU certificates require qualification recognition.
