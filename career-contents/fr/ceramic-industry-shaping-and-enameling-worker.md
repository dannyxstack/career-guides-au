## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

You can be promoted from apprentice (CAP) to skilled worker (compagnon), and then to team supervisor or quality inspector. A few can turn to artisan d'art or open a studio.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine glazing and glaze spraying operations
- Mold pressing and trimming for mass products
- Screen printing or decals with simple patterns
- Standardized processes for temperature control and kiln operation
- Visual dimension inspection in quality inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design of complex shapes and decorative patterns
- Digital twin simulates the firing process to optimize glaze formulations
- Computer vision monitors glaze defects in real time and adjusts parameters
- 3D scanning and modeling to quickly generate custom models
- Collaborative robots assist in handling and finishing heavy items

**Human moat:**

- The tactile and tactile experience of handmade modeling
- Creative design and artistic aesthetic ability
- A deep understanding of traditional craftsmanship and materials
- Personalized communication and service for customized products
- Complex restoration and identification and restoration of antique porcelain

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software (such as Rhino, Fusion 360)
- 3D printing and rapid prototyping technology.
- AI image generation tools (such as Midjourney) are used for design exploration
- Data analysis and process control optimization
- Digital marketing and e-commerce platform operations
- Programming basics for intelligent kilns and industrial robots

**Key growth areas:**

- Automation substitution
- Luxury handicraft niche
- Restoration demand
- Limoges cluster concentration

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for ceramic glazer modeling?**
A: Entry-level salaries are about 22,000-26,000 euros per year, and seniors can reach up to 40,000 euros, which is below the French average.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this profession through skilled migration?**
A: It is more difficult. This occupation is not in short supply and requires a French diploma or certification; the Salarié (employer-sponsored pathway) is viable but highly competitive.

**Q: What are the promotion or development paths for this profession?**
A: You can rise from apprentice to skilled worker, then serve as a team leader or transition to quality inspector; A few have established independent studios.
