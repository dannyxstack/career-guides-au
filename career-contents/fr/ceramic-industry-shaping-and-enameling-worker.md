# Ceramic Industry Shaping And Enameling Worker（Ceramic Industry Shaping And Enameling Worker）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2803 – Ceramic Industry Shaping And Enameling Worker。**

Performs shaping, enameling, and decorating of ceramic products by hand or semi-mechanized processes, common in traditional ceramic regions like Limoges.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在FR技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for ceramic shaping and enameling workers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €22,000-26,000, and experienced workers can reach €40,000, below the French average.

**问：Can foreigners migrate to France for this occupation?**
答：It is difficult. The occupation is not in shortage, requires French certification, and employer sponsorship (Salarié) is possible but competitive.

**问：What are the career progression paths?**
答：From apprentice to skilled worker, then team supervisor or quality inspector; a few open their own studios.
