# Pattern Grader（Pattern Grader）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2412 – Pattern Grader。**

Specializes in scaling base patterns up or down to create size series, ensuring accurate fit and consistent sizing in garment production.

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：一般（★★½☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 6,500。法国服装产业虽面临国际化竞争，但高级定制和快时尚需求持续存在，打版师岗位保持稳定。未来随着自动化技术发展，传统手工作业可能减少，但高端定制领域仍需经验丰富的打版师。目前就业机会主要集中在巴黎及周边地区。

**竞争度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**
**工作强度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for pattern graders in France?**
答：Entry-level pattern graders earn around €22,000-€28,000 per year, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior can reach over €45,000. Salaries are higher in Paris, especially in haute couture and luxury brands.

**问：How can foreign workers obtain a work visa as a pattern grader in France?**
答：Through the Talent Passport or Employee visa, requiring a job offer and contract. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold (around €53,800), typically for senior positions.
