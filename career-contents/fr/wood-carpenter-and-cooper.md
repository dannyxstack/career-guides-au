# Wood Carpenter And Cooper（Wood Carpenter And Cooper）职业分析 · FR

**职业代码：H2206 – Wood Carpenter And Cooper。**

Engages in wood structure fabrication, furniture making, door/window installation, and barrel (oak cask) production. Requires knowledge of wood properties, hand tools, and machinery, involving measuring, cutting, assembling, and finishing.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在FR独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Salarié（Salarié (Employee)） | 需获得法国雇主工作合同，且工资不低于法定最低工资。木工属于紧缺职业，但需通过职业资质认证（如CAP等同）。 |
| Subclass Passeport Talent（Passeport Talent - Highly Skilled Worker） | 适用于具有高技能或独特技艺的木工（如制桶大师），工资须达到一定门槛（约36576欧元/年），需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Carte bleue UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等教育学历（Bac+3以上）且工资达1.5倍平均工资（约53917欧元/年），木工通常难以满足工资要求。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a cooper?**
答：Entry-level coopers earn about 20000-25000 EUR annually; experienced ones can reach 35000-45000 EUR. Coopers generally earn more than general carpenters.

**问：How can foreign carpenters immigrate to France?**
答：Must secure a job offer from a French employer and obtain recognition of professional qualifications (e.g., equivalent CAP). Despite being a shortage occupation, third-country workers still need to meet French language requirements and labor market test.

**问：What are the career development paths for carpenters?**
答：Can advance to foreman, workshop supervisor, or specialize in cooperage, joinery, historic restoration. Also possible to start own workshop or barrel factory.
