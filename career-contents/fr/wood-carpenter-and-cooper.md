# Wood Carpenter And Cooper — career analysis · France

**Occupation code: H2206 (ROME)**

Engaged in wood structure manufacturing, furniture manufacturing, door and window installation, and barrel (oak barrel) production. It requires mastering wood characteristics, hand tools, and mechanical operations, with tasks including measurement, cutting, assembly, and finishing.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

You can start as an apprentice or CAP, gradually advance to skilled worker, foreman, or specialize in cooperage, joinery, and ancient restoration. With experience, you can start a workshop or move into management positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate cutting diagrams and material lists automatically through AI design software, replacing manual measurement and calculation
- CNC equipment replaces manual cutting and repetitive machining
- Prefabricated building components (e.g., modular walls) reduce on-site assembly work
- Laser scanning and BIM technology replace traditional layout and positioning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design tools (e.g., SketchUp + AI plugins) accelerate complex structural design.
- AR/VR technology assists on-site installation guidance, improving accuracy
- Construction management platforms automatically optimize material use and project scheduling.
- AI-driven quality inspection system improves product consistency

**Human moat:**

- Customized installation and adjustment capability in complex on-site environments
- Comprehensive judgment of wood properties, joinery techniques, and structural mechanics
- Skills for handling irregular structures and repairs in old buildings
- Ability to communicate directly with clients and understand personalized needs

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in BIM and 3D modeling software (e.g., Revit, SketchUp)
- Learn CNC programming and digital manufacturing technology
- Familiar with prefabricated building component installation
- Master basic programming (e.g., Python) for automation tool integration
- Improve project management and client communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Traditional crafts revival
- Wine industry demand
- Historic renovation
- Sustainable wood

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for coopers?**
A: Junior coopers earn an annual salary of about 20,000–25,000 euros, and with more experience, can reach 35,000–45,000 euros. Coopers usually earn more than ordinary carpenters.

**Q: How can foreign carpenters immigrate to France?**
A: You must obtain a work contract from a French employer and pass professional qualification certifications (such as CAP certificates). Carpentry is a highly sought-after profession, but third-country immigrants still need to meet French language requirements and labor market tests.

**Q: What career development paths are available for carpenters?**
A: You can be promoted to foreman, workshop supervisor, or specialize in cooperage, joinery, or ancient building restoration. You can also start a business by opening a woodworking workshop or a cooperage.
