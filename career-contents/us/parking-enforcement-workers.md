# Parking Enforcement Workers（Parking Enforcement Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：33-3041 – Parking Enforcement Workers。**

Patrol assigned areas such as public parking lots or city streets to issue tickets for overtime parking and illegally parked vehicles.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Worker） | 季节性临时工作签证，但执法岗通常非季节性，极少使用。 |
| Subclass Green Card (EB-3)（EB-3 Skilled Workers） | 理论上可通过EB-3申请绿卡，但雇主赞助可能性极低，因国内劳动力充足。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for parking enforcement workers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about $32,000 to $40,000, mid-level $40,000 to $50,000, and supervisors up to $60,000. Pay is typically hourly.

**问：Can parking enforcement workers get a US work visa or green card?**
答：It is very difficult. This occupation is not considered high-skilled or in shortage. Employers rarely sponsor H-1B visas, and H-2B seasonal visas are typically not applicable.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities for parking enforcement workers?**
答：Advance to senior officer, supervisor, or traffic management coordinator. Some move into public safety or urban planning with further education.
