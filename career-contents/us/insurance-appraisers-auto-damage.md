# Insurance Appraisers, Auto Damage（Insurance Appraisers, Auto Damage）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：13-1032 – Insurance Appraisers, Auto Damage。**

Appraise automobile or other vehicle damage to determine repair costs for insurance claim settlement, prepare insurance forms, and seek agreement with repair shops.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for insurance appraisers, auto damage?**
答：According to BLS 2024 data, average annual salary is about $65,000, with entry-level around $45,000 and senior up to $95,000.

**问：Can auto damage appraisers apply for US work visas?**
答：Yes, but challenging. Typically require bachelor's degree or equivalent experience. Common paths are H-1B and EB-3, but employers must prove the position qualifies as specialty occupation.

**问：What licenses or certifications are required for this occupation?**
答：Most states require an insurance appraiser license, involving an exam and background check. ASE auto repair certification is optional but beneficial.
