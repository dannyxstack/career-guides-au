# Bailiffs（Bailiffs）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：33-3011 – Bailiffs。**

Bailiffs maintain order in courtrooms, ensuring safety and decorum during legal proceedings. They escort prisoners, manage juries, enforce court orders, and provide security during trials.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常不适用，因法警不要求专业本科学位 |
| Subclass Green Card (EB-3)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-3)） | 极少数情况下，若雇主证明无合适美国人，可能通过EB-3担保，但实际极少 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bailiffs?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for bailiffs is about $47,000, with entry-level around $35,000 and senior positions up to $65,000.

**问：Is there a skilled migration path for bailiffs?**
答：Bailiffs generally have no skilled migration path, as the job does not require a bachelor's degree and employers (government agencies) rarely sponsor visas. H-1B and EB-3 are not applicable.

**问：What certifications are needed to become a bailiff?**
答：Typically, completion of a bailiff training program from a state court or law enforcement agency is required, along with possible firearm certification. Requirements vary by state.
