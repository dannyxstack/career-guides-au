# Cabinetmakers and Bench Carpenters（Cabinetmakers and Bench Carpenters）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-7011 – Cabinetmakers and Bench Carpenters。**

Cut, shape, and assemble wooden articles or operate woodworking machines to fabricate parts for wood products.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for cabinetmakers?**
答：Median annual wage in the US is about $48,000; experienced workers can earn over $70,000, varying by region.

**问：Can cabinetmakers immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
答：Very difficult. It's not a specialty occupation; H-1B is nearly impossible. EB-3 green card requires employer sponsorship, rarely available.
