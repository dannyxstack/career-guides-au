# Transportation Inspectors（Transportation Inspectors）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：53-6051 – Transportation Inspectors。**

Transportation inspectors examine equipment or goods to ensure safe transport of cargo or people, including rail, air, and road vehicle inspections to comply with safety regulations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常需要学士学位，但运输检查员高中毕业即可，H-1B申请难度大 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Workers/Professionals） | 需PERM劳工证，但运输检查员通常不满足技能要求，雇主担保困难 |
| Subclass L-1（L-1 Intracompany Transferee） | 仅限跨国企业内部调动，不普遍 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Transportation Inspectors？**
- 注重细节和安全规范的人
- 愿意接受在职培训和外部工作环境的人
- 有运输行业背景或兴趣的人

**谁不适合学Transportation Inspectors？**
- 追求高薪和高增长前景的人
- 不愿面对重复性检查和户外工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for transportation inspectors?**
答：Entry-level salaries are around $35,000-$45,000, mid-level $45,000-$60,000, and senior $60,000-$80,000, with limited growth potential.

**问：Is it difficult for transportation inspectors to immigrate to the US?**
答：Very difficult. The job typically requires only a high school diploma, not meeting H-1B specialty occupation criteria, and employer-sponsored green cards are rare.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for transportation inspectors?**
答：Progression from junior to senior inspector, supervisor, or roles in safety management and compliance auditing.
