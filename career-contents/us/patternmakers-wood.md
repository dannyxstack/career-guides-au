# Patternmakers, Wood — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 51-7032 (SOC)**

Wood pattern makers plan, lay out, and build wooden units or split patterns for casting sand moulds; this is a traditional craft.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Starts with an apprenticeship, progresses to senior model maker or supervisor, and can move into mould design or CNC programming. Some start their own model workshops.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Hand-drawing and typesetting model drawings: AI/CAD can automatically generate precise 3D models
- Manual cutting and carving of wooden molds replaced by CNC machines and 3D printing
- Hand sanding and finishing of models: automated sanding equipment
- Traditional lofting and scaling techniques: replaced by digital scaling and simulation software
- Simple wood mould assembly: robotic automatic assembly system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Design complex models using digital modeling software (e.g. Fusion 360)
- Use 3D printing to create rapid prototypes for castability verification
- Reverse-copy old models via scanner and digitize them
- Use CNC programming software to optimize machining paths
- Use AI casting simulation to predict defects and adjust mold design

**Human moat:**

- Intuition and long-term experience with complex casting processes
- Manual carving skills for processing non-standard, irregular, or high-precision models
- Process knowledge for communicating with foundry engineers
- Ability to repair and restore antique wooden molds

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360)
- CNC programming and operation
- 3D modeling and reverse engineering
- Casting process simulation software
- Basic knowledge of robot-assisted machining
- Project management and quality control

**Key growth areas:**

- Manual Skilled Trade
- Declining Demand
- Precision Casting
- Automation Impact

## 8. FAQ
