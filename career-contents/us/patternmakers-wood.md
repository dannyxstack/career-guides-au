# Patternmakers, Wood（Patternmakers, Wood）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-7032 – Patternmakers, Wood。**

Patternmakers, Wood plan, lay out, and construct wooden unit or sectional patterns used in forming sand molds for castings.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 木制模型工通常不符合H-1B专业职业要求，极少获批 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Worker） | 理论上有路径，但需PERM劳工证，且雇主赞助困难 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for wood patternmakers?**
答：According to BLS, 2023 median annual wage is about $49,000, ranging from $32,000 to $72,000 depending on experience and location.

**问：Can foreign nationals get an H-1B visa as a wood patternmaker?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation rarely qualifies as a specialty occupation for H-1B, and employer sponsorship is rare. EB-3 green card is theoretically possible but PERM process is complex and seldom successful.

**问：What is the future outlook for this career?**
答：Automation is reducing jobs, but demand remains in precision casting and custom models. CNC skills can expand opportunities.
