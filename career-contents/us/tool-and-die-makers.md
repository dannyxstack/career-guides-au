# Tool and Die Makers（Tool and Die Makers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-4111 – Tool and Die Makers。**

Tool and Die Makers analyze specifications, lay out metal stock, set up and operate machine tools, and fit and assemble parts to make and repair dies, cutting tools, jigs, fixtures, and gauges, essential in manufacturing.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 适用于需要专业知识的模具制造师，但通常要求学士学位，普通技师较难满足 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Workers） | 技术工人绿卡，适合有2年培训或经验的模具制造师，需PERM劳工认证 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 雇主担保绿卡的第一步，证明无美国工人可用，模具制造师可通过此途径申请EB-3 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for Tool and Die Makers in the US?**
答：Entry-level around $35,000-$45,000, experienced $48,000-$62,000, senior $65,000-$85,000, varying by location and industry.

**问：Is it easy for Tool and Die Makers to immigrate to the US?**
答：H-1B is difficult due to degree requirements; EB-3 skilled worker green card via PERM is more feasible, though not easy.

**问：What are the career prospects for Tool and Die Makers?**
答：Slow growth due to automation, but precision tooling and retirements provide opportunities; CNC and automation skills recommended.
