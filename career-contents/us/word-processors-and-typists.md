# Word Processors and Typists — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 43-9022 (SOC)**

Using a word processor, computer, or typewriter to transcribe drafts, revised versions, or recordings into formal documents such as letters, reports, tables, etc., and may perform other clerical duties.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Most practitioners stay in entry-level positions with limited advancement paths. They can move into administrative assistant, secretary, or data entry roles through experience; a few may become office supervisors. Overall upward mobility is weak.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Transcribe audio dictation into documents by typing
- Proofread for spelling, grammar, and formatting errors.
- Create standard letters, reports, and forms from templates.
- Adjust document formatting such as font, spacing, margins
- Enter handwritten or revised manuscripts into the computer.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI transcription tools to speed up processing of non-standard accents or noisy environment recordings
- Quickly generate complex formatted merged documents (e.g., mail merge) using AI templates
- Real-time dictation and editing using AI voice assistants
- Use AI to check document consistency and compliance (e.g., contract clauses)

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly classified or sensitive document content
- Digitization of non-standard formats, handwritten documents, or aging media (e.g., typewriter ribbons)
- Complex layouts requiring manual judgment (e.g., legal documents, sheet music)
- Customer personalized communication and context-based text polishing

**Skills to build:**

- AI transcription and voice tools (e.g. Otter.ai, Rev) operation
- Process automation (e.g., Microsoft Power Automate)
- Document management and compliance (e.g., NDAs, electronic signature platforms)
- Advanced layout and formatting (e.g., InDesign, LaTeX)
- Data privacy and security protocols
- Customer service and communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining
- Automation Replacement
- Speech Recognition
- Administrative Support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for a word processor?**
A: US word processors' median annual salary is about $34,000, below the median for all occupations. Entry-level positions often pay under $30,000, experienced workers can reach over $40,000, but advancement is limited.

**Q: Can a word processor immigrate to the US via a work visa?**
A: Almost impossible. This occupation is not considered a specialty occupation (H-1B requirement), and EB-3 green cards require demonstrating labor shortage, but such positions are easily filled by locals, making PERM certification extremely difficult.
