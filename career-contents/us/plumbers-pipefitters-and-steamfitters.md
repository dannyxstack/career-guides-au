# Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters（Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2152 – Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters。**

Assemble, install, alter, and repair pipelines or pipe systems that carry water, steam, air, or other liquids or gases. May install heating and cooling equipment and mechanical control systems. Includes sprinkler fitters.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Workers） | 用于临时性非农业工作，需雇主担保，受年度配额限制，适用于季节性管道工岗位。 |
| Subclass TN（TN NAFTA Professional） | 仅限加拿大和墨西哥公民，但管道工通常不符合专业职业要求，极少使用。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-3)） | 理论上可通过EB-3 skilled worker申请，但需PERM劳工证，雇主证明无法招到美国工人，难度极高。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary ceiling for plumbers?**
答：Senior plumbers or self-employed contractors can earn over $100,000 per year, but most journeymen earn between $70,000 and $80,000.

**问：Is it easy for plumbers to immigrate to the US?**
答：Very difficult. The job usually requires a US state license and apprenticeship. H-1B is not applicable; EB-3 green card requires PERM and employer to prove labor shortage. H-2B temporary visa is the only possible path but limited by quota.

**问：Which states require plumber licenses?**
答：Most states require a license, with varying exam and experience requirements. For example, California requires 4 years of apprenticeship and passing an exam.
