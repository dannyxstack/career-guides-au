# Separating, Filtering, Clarifying, Precipitating, and Still Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders（Separating, Filtering, Clarifying, Precipitating, and Still Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-9012 – Separating, Filtering, Clarifying, Precipitating, and Still Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders。**

Set up, operate, or tend equipment like filter presses, centrifuges, and stills to separate, filter, or purify liquids, gases, or solids.
