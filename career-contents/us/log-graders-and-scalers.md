# Log Graders and Scalers — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 45-4023 (SOC)**

Grade logs in yards, log ponds, or decks to assess their saleable content or value. Inspect logs for defects and measure volume, excluding agricultural produce purchasing agents.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Can progress from junior grader to senior grader or supervisor, some move to timber procurement or quality inspection roles. With experience, can also start a small forestry service company.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using calipers and tape measures to measure log diameter and length
- Visually inspect logs for defects (e.g., knots, rot) and sort them
- Calculating log volume and grade to determine value
- Record data and fill in inventory forms

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI vision systems to quickly scan and classify logs, improving efficiency
- Assists in accurate log value estimation through sensor data
- Automatically record and analyze data, reducing paperwork
- Use mobile devices and cloud management systems for real-time inventory updates

**Human moat:**

- Professional judgment on abnormal or complex defects
- Ability to manage customer relationships and negotiate prices
- Decision-making authority in handling high-value or special orders
- On-site safety inspection and quality assurance responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and interpretation skills
- AI/automated equipment operation and maintenance
- Use of digital inventory management systems
- Communication and Customer Relationship Management
- Ability to continuously learn new technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- Sawmill Operations
- Logging
- Forestry
- Wood Products

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys outdoor work and has good physical fitness.
- Detail-oriented, able to accurately assess wood quality
- Willingness to work in remote areas or forest plantations

**Who is not suited**
- Those who prefer an office work environment
- Not interested in the timber industry or unable to adapt to physical labor

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a log grader?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approximately USD 32,000-40,000, can reach over USD 50,000 with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation on an H-2B visa?**
A: Yes, the H-2B visa is for seasonal non-agricultural work, but annual quotas are strict and employers must obtain labor certification.

**Q: Are there opportunities for promotion?**
A: Yes, can advance from scaler to senior grader, supervisor, or wood buyer.
