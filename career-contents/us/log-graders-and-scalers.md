# Log Graders and Scalers（Log Graders and Scalers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：45-4023 – Log Graders and Scalers。**

Grade logs or estimate the marketable content or value of logs in sorting yards, millpond, or log deck. Inspect for defects and measure volume. Excludes farm products buyers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for a log grader?**
答：Entry-level positions pay around $32,000-$40,000 annually, increasing with experience to over $50,000.

**问：Can foreigners work as log graders under H-2B visa?**
答：Yes, but H-2B has an annual cap and requires employer to demonstrate labor shortage.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities?**
答：Yes, from grader to senior grader, supervisor, or timber buyer.
