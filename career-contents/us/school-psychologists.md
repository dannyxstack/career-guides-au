# School Psychologists（School Psychologists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：19-3034 – School Psychologists。**

School psychologists diagnose and implement interventions for educational, behavioral, or developmental issues that impact student learning, providing counseling and consulting with school staff.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 学校雇主可申请，但非营利学校免抽签，较易获批 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 要求硕士学位或本科加5年经验，可通过PERM申请 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 学校赞助绿卡，需证明无法找到合格美国工人 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for school psychologists?**
答：Entry-level salary is around $55,000-$70,000, while experienced professionals can earn $90,000-$120,000, depending on district and experience.

**问：Is it easy for school psychologists to immigrate to the US?**
答：Relatively easy due to the occupation being in shortage; non-profit schools are exempt from the H-1B lottery, and EB-2 green cards are available.

**问：What education is required to become a school psychologist?**
答：Usually an Ed.S. or master's degree with additional coursework, plus internship and state licensure exam.
