# Fundraisers（Fundraisers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：13-1131 – Fundraisers。**

Organize activities to raise funds or solicit monetary donations or gifts for an organization; may design promotional materials and raise awareness of the organization's goals and financial needs.

|---|---|

| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 适用于学士学位或技术工人 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for fundraisers?**
答：Entry-level $45,000-60,000, senior up to $120,000, median around $70,000.

**问：Is it easy for fundraisers to get a US work visa?**
答：Usually H-1B or EB-2/EB-3 green card; employer must prove no qualified US worker, moderately difficult.

**问：What is the career path for fundraisers?**
答：From coordinator to manager, director, then Chief Development Officer, or transition to marketing.
