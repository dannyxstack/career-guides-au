# Emergency Medicine Physicians（Emergency Medicine Physicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-1214 – Emergency Medicine Physicians。**

Emergency medicine physicians make immediate medical decisions to prevent death or disability, providing recognition, evaluation, care, stabilization, and disposition of patients in emergency departments.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of an emergency medicine physician?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, the median annual salary is around $330,000, with entry-level at $250,000 and senior exceeding $500,000.

**问：How can international medical graduates become emergency physicians in the US?**
答：They must pass USMLE, obtain ECFMG certification, match into a residency program, then apply for H-1B or J-1 visa, complete training, pass state licensing, and apply for EB-2 green card.

**问：What certifications are required for emergency medicine physicians?**
答：Mandatory: MD/DO degree, USMLE, state medical license. Optional: ABEM board certification (enhances employment).
