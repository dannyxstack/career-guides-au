# Foreign Language and Literature Teachers, Postsecondary（Foreign Language and Literature Teachers, Postsecondary）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-1124 – Foreign Language and Literature Teachers, Postsecondary。**

Teach languages and literature courses in languages other than English at postsecondary institutions, including American Sign Language. Involves a combination of teaching and research activities.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 需大学担任全职教学或研究职位，通常需博士学历，每年配额竞争 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 适合拥有博士学位或杰出能力的教授，可通过PERM或NIW申请绿卡 |
| Subclass O-1（O-1 Extraordinary Ability） | 适用于在语言文学领域有杰出成就的教授，无需劳工证 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Permanent Residence through PERM） | 大学为终身教职或长期职位担保，需证明无合格美国工人 |

**PR难度：中高（★★★½☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Foreign Language and Literature Teachers, Postsecondary？**
- 热爱教学和研究的外语或文学博士毕业生
- 愿意在学术环境中从事长期研究并追求终身教职的人

- 追求快速高薪或工作生活平衡的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for foreign language professors?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage was $79,540 in 2021, but varies by institution type (research universities typically pay more than community colleges) and rank. Adjunct instructors earn lower.

**问：How can a foreign PhD become a professor in the US via H-1B?**
答：You need a full-time teaching or research position at a university. The employer files an H-1B petition; since universities are cap-exempt, you can apply year-round. Afterwards, pursue an EB-2 green card.
