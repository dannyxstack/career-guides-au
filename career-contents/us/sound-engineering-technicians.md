# Sound Engineering Technicians（Sound Engineering Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：27-4014 – Sound Engineering Technicians。**

Assemble and operate equipment to record, synchronize, mix, edit, or reproduce sound for theater, video, film, television, podcasts, sporting events, and other productions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（Specialty Occupation） | 需学士学位，但该职业通常不被视为专业职位，申请H-1B困难。 |
| Subclass O-1（Extraordinary Ability） | 适用于具有杰出能力的音响工程师，需提供大量证据。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Sound Engineering Technicians？**
- 对音频技术有浓厚兴趣的创意人士
- 动手能力强且注重细节者
- 愿意在非固定时间工作以适应演出项目

**谁不适合学Sound Engineering Technicians？**
- 追求稳定早九晚五工作时间者
- 对声音不敏感或缺乏耐心者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary outlook for sound engineering technicians?**
答：Entry-level salary is around $30,000-$45,000, mid-level $45,000-$65,000, and senior can reach up to $100,000 annually. Film and game industries pay higher.

**问：Can sound engineering technicians immigrate to the US via H-1B visa?**
答：Difficult. This occupation typically does not meet H-1B specialty occupation requirements unless a bachelor's degree is required and duties are highly specialized. O-1 visa for extraordinary ability is more suitable for top talents.

**问：Will this occupation be replaced by AI?**
答：Some routine tasks like basic mixing may be automated, but creative decisions and live troubleshooting still require human expertise, so risk is moderate.
