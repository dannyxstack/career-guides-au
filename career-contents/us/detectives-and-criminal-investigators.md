# Detectives and Criminal Investigators（Detectives and Criminal Investigators）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：33-3021 – Detectives and Criminal Investigators。**

Conduct investigations related to suspected violations of federal, state, or local laws to prevent or solve crimes. Excludes private detectives.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for detectives and criminal investigators?**
答：Entry-level: $55,000-$75,000; mid-level: $75,000-$100,000; senior: $100,000-$140,000, with federal agents earning more.

**问：Can non-U.S. citizens become detectives via H-1B?**
答：Possible but competitive. Most positions require U.S. citizenship, but federal agencies may sponsor for specialized skills.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a detective?**
答：Typically require police academy training, several years of police experience, and a bachelor's degree in criminal justice (preferred).
