# Ushers, Lobby Attendants, and Ticket Takers（Ushers, Lobby Attendants, and Ticket Takers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：39-3031 – Ushers, Lobby Attendants, and Ticket Takers。**

Assist patrons at entertainment events by collecting tickets, guiding to seats, searching for lost items, and directing to facilities like restrooms and telephones.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 此职业通常不符合H-1B专业职位要求 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Immigration: Third Preference） | 极少数情况可通过EB-3其他工人类别申请，但需证明劳工短缺 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

答：Annual salary typically ranges from $22,000 to $45,000, mostly part-time with hourly wage between $11 and $22.

答：Very limited; not eligible for H-1B specialty occupations; only rare cases via EB-3 other workers category.

答：Limited advancement; possible to become ticket supervisor or customer service manager, but automation is reducing positions.
