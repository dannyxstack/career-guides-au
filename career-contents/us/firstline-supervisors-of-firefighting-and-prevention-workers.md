# First-Line Supervisors of Firefighting and Prevention Workers（First-Line Supervisors of Firefighting and Prevention Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：33-1021 – First-Line Supervisors of Firefighting and Prevention Workers。**

Directly supervise and coordinate firefighters and fire prevention activities, ensuring efficient and safe fire suppression, rescue, and prevention operations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 不常见，因通常要求学士学位且职位需为专业职业，消防主管通常不满足。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（EB-2/EB-3 Employment-Based Immigration） | 极少，私营消防公司或可能，但公共部门几乎不提供担保。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a fire supervisor?**
答：According to BLS 2023, the median annual wage for first-line supervisors of firefighting workers is about $82,000, ranging from $45,000 to $120,000 depending on region, department size, and experience.

**问：Can foreign firefighters immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Extremely unlikely. Firefighters and supervisors are usually hired by local governments. H-1B requires a specialty occupation, which fire supervision rarely meets. EB-2/EB-3 green cards are also rarely applicable.

**问：How to become a fire supervisor?**
答：Typically start as a firefighter, gain 5-10 years of experience, then pass promotion exams or internal selection. Many departments require EMT certification and an associate degree in fire science.
