# Mathematicians（Mathematicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：15-2021 – Mathematicians。**

Conduct research in fundamental or applied mathematics, solving problems in science, management, and other fields using mathematical methods.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 最常见的非移民工作签证，需雇主担保，学士以上学位，每年配额有限需抽签。 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 适合拥有高等学位或特殊能力的数学家，需PERM劳工证和I-140申请。 |
| Subclass O-1（O-1 Extraordinary Ability） | 适用于在数学领域有杰出成就的从业者，无需劳工证，但标准极高。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Mathematicians？**
- 对抽象数学理论有浓厚兴趣，擅长逻辑推理和问题解决
- 愿意在学术或研究型机构长期发展
- 具备较强的编程能力（如Python、R）

**谁不适合学Mathematicians？**
- 不喜欢长时间独立研究和解决抽象问题
- 希望快速看到实际应用成果或与人频繁打交道

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mathematicians?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for mathematicians in 2023 was about $112,000. Entry-level salaries range from $60,000 to $85,000, senior positions $120,000-$160,000, with PhDs or management earning more.

**问：What are the migration pathways for mathematicians to the U.S.?**
答：Common pathways include H-1B visa (lottery-based) and EB-2 green card (with PERM). Outstanding individuals may apply for O-1 or EB-1A. PhD and research achievements help.
