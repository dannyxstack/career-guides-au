## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for environmental scientists?**
答：Entry-level: $45,000-$65,000; mid-level: $65,000-$90,000; senior: $90,000-$130,000. Federal jobs offer stability, while private consulting can pay higher.

**问：How can environmental scientists immigrate to the US?**
答：Common path: H-1B visa followed by EB-2 or EB-3 green card via PERM. TN visa is available for Canadian and Mexican citizens.

**问：What is the career progression for environmental scientists?**
答：Start as field technician or junior analyst, progress to project manager, senior consultant, or move into environmental policy and regulatory management.
