# Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics（Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：49-3053 – Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics。**

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul small engines used to power lawn mowers, chain saws, recreational sporting equipment, and related equipment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation in the US?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, average annual wage is around $40,000, median about $37,000. Experienced mechanics or shop owners can earn over $60,000.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for H-1B or green card immigration to the US?**
答：No. The occupation typically does not qualify as a H-1B specialty occupation (requires bachelor's degree), and EB-3 green card requires employer to prove no qualified US workers, which is very rare in practice.

**问：Will this occupation be replaced by automation?**
答：Low risk. Although new equipment incorporates electronics, repair work relies on hands-on skills and on-site diagnosis, making it difficult to replace by AI in the short term.
