# Helpers--Pipelayers, Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters（Helpers--Pipelayers, Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-3015 – Helpers--Pipelayers, Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters。**

Assist plumbers, pipefitters, steamfitters, or pipelayers by performing duties requiring less skill, such as using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work areas.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for helpers--pipelayers, plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from $30,000 to $38,000, mid-level $38,000 to $45,000, and senior $45,000 to $55,000, depending on location and employer.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for US work visa or green card applications?**
答：No. This occupation typically does not qualify as a specialty occupation for H-1B and is rarely petitioned for EB-3 green cards due to low skill requirements.
