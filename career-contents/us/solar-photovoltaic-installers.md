# Solar Photovoltaic Installers（Solar Photovoltaic Installers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2231 – Solar Photovoltaic Installers。**

Assemble, install, or maintain solar photovoltaic (PV) systems on roofs or other structures, including measuring, cutting, and bolting structural framing and solar modules.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 此职业通常不属于专业职位，H-1B担保困难 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-3)） | 技术工人绿卡，但需雇主担保且通过PERM劳工认证 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 需要证明美国劳工短缺，但此类岗位通常不需本科，认证难度大 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for solar photovoltaic installers?**
答：The median annual wage for solar photovoltaic installers in the US is around $46,000 (2023), with entry-level salaries ranging from $35,000 to $45,000 and experienced installers earning $60,000 to $80,000.

**问：Is immigration to the US easy for this occupation?**
答：This occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree, making H-1B sponsorship difficult. EB-3 green card is possible but requires employer sponsorship and PERM labor certification, which is challenging.

**问：How to become a solar photovoltaic installer?**
答：A high school diploma is typically required, with on-the-job training or 1-2 year technical programs. NABCEP certification or OSHA safety training can improve job prospects.
