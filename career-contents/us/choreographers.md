# Choreographers（Choreographers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：27-2032 – Choreographers。**

Choreographers create new dance routines, rehearse performers, and may direct and stage presentations. They work in theater, film, television, music videos, and dance companies.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在美国独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass O-1（O-1 Extraordinary Ability Visa） | 适用于在舞蹈领域有杰出成就的编舞师，需证明国际认可。 |
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation Visa） | 少数雇主可担保，通常需本科学位，但编舞师岗位较难符合专业职位要求。 |
| Subclass EB-2（EB-2 Employment-Based Green Card (NIW)） | 国家利益豁免绿卡，适用于其工作符合美国国家利益的杰出编舞师。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for choreographers?**
答：Choreographers' incomes vary widely. Entry-level salaries range from $35,000 to $50,000, mid-level from $50,000 to $75,000, and senior choreographers can earn over $120,000. Many are paid per project, leading to unstable income.

**问：What visa options exist for choreographers to immigrate to the US?**
答：The main route is the O-1 visa for individuals with extraordinary ability. H-1B is rare as the occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree. EB-2 National Interest Waiver is possible for those whose work benefits the US.

**问：What education is required for a choreographer?**
答：No formal degree is mandatory, but many choreographers hold a bachelor's in dance or a related field. However, extensive performance experience and a strong portfolio are more critical.
