# Forest Fire Inspectors and Prevention Specialists（Forest Fire Inspectors and Prevention Specialists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：33-2022 – Forest Fire Inspectors and Prevention Specialists。**

Enforce fire regulations, inspect forests for hazards, recommend prevention or control measures, and report fires and weather conditions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 政府职位通常不提供，私营承包商可能有名额，但竞争激烈。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-2/EB-3)） | 很少申请，因多为政府职位要求公民身份。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for forest fire inspectors?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage is about $47,000 in 2023, ranging from $35,000 to $80,000 depending on experience and employer.

**问：Can foreign nationals become forest fire inspectors via work visa?**
答：Very difficult. Most positions require U.S. citizenship, and employers like the U.S. Forest Service typically do not sponsor visas. Private contractors may offer H-1B but rarely.

**问：Is there room for career advancement?**
答：Yes. You can advance to senior inspector, supervisor, or fire management specialist, or move into related fields like emergency management or environmental compliance.
