# Construction Laborers（Construction Laborers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2061 – Construction Laborers。**

Perform physical labor at construction sites, operating hand and power tools like hammers, tampers, mixers, and hoists. Tasks include site cleanup, trench digging, scaffolding erection, and debris removal.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Worker） | 适用于季节性建筑工作，但名额有限且需雇主担保，通常为期10个月，可续签但最多3年 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for construction laborers?**
答：According to 2023 BLS data, the median annual wage is about $41,000, with entry-level around $31,000 and senior up to $75,000.

**问：Can construction laborers immigrate to the US via H-1B visa?**
答：Typically not, as H-1B requires a specialized degree. H-2B seasonal visa is possible but green card sponsorship is extremely rare.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for construction laborers?**
答：They can advance to foreman, skilled trades (electrician, carpenter), or construction supervisor with additional training and certifications.
