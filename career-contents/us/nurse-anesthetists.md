# Nurse Anesthetists（Nurse Anesthetists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-1151 – Nurse Anesthetists。**

Nurse anesthetists are advanced practice registered nurses who administer anesthesia, monitor patient vitals, and oversee recovery. They require graduate education and national certification.

---

**学习难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

| 麻醉护理硕士/博士 | 2-3年 | $80,000~$200,000（包括学费、书本及临床费用） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for nurse anesthetists?**
答：Nurse anesthetists are among the highest-paid nursing professions in the U.S., with a median salary around $200,000. Entry-level salaries range from $150,000 to $180,000, while experienced practitioners can earn over $250,000.

**问：How can foreign nurses immigrate to the U.S. as a nurse anesthetist?**
答：The typical path is to first immigrate as a registered nurse via H-1B or EB-3 green card, then complete a U.S. nurse anesthesia program and obtain CRNA certification. Direct CRNA visas are rare due to U.S. degree requirements.

**问：How many years of education are required to become a nurse anesthetist?**
答：Typically 6-7 years: 4 years for a BSN plus 2-3 years for a nurse anesthesia master's or doctoral program. Accelerated programs may shorten the timeline.
