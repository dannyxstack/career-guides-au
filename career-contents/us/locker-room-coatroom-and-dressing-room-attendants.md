## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited, can advance to foreman or supervisor, or transfer to hotel front desk or customer service roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually receive and store customer clothing/items
- Issue hand tags or paper credentials and record information
- Sorting and returning stored items for departing customers
- Assist customers with traditional key or padlock lockers
- Handle simple lost and found item registration and inquiries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-assisted management system to quickly locate items
- Linking multiple toll collection points via tablets or smart glasses
- Use voice assistants or mobile devices to book exclusive counters for VIP clients
- Predict peak-hour scheduling using data analysis
- Automated baggage handling robots assist with physical tasks.

**Human moat:**

- Personalized interaction and emotional care for high-end clients
- Responsibility and judgment in handling unexpected incidents like item damage/loss
- Maintain the private atmosphere and etiquette norms of changing rooms
- Experience in manual handling of special (fragile, valuable) items
- Coordinates on-site responses across departments (e.g., catering, cleaning).

**Skills to build:**

- Daily maintenance and troubleshooting of self-service terminals and locker systems
- Basic AI and big data analysis (for people flow prediction, item flow optimization)
- Multilingual customer service skills (especially English, minority languages)
- Mobile CRM and membership management system operation.
- Interpersonal communication and conflict resolution skills
- Quick proficiency with small electronic devices (RFID readers, tablets)

**Key growth areas:**

- Slow growth
- Leisure and hospitality
- Customer service
- Entry-level
