# Shipping, Receiving, and Inventory Clerks（Shipping, Receiving, and Inventory Clerks）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-5071 – Shipping, Receiving, and Inventory Clerks。**

Verify and maintain records on incoming and outgoing shipments involving inventory, including coordinating transportation and preparing items for shipment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
