## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for medical scientists?**
答：Entry-level researchers earn about $60,000-$90,000 per year, while senior researchers can earn $130,000-$200,000, with academia typically lower than industry.

**问：What immigration pathways are available for medical scientists to the US?**
答：Common pathways include H-1B work visa, EB-2 employment-based green card, and O-1 visa for extraordinary ability. Employer sponsorship is required, and a Ph.D. is advantageous.

**问：What educational background is needed to become a medical scientist?**
答：Typically a Ph.D. in biology or a related field is required; some positions may require an M.D.
