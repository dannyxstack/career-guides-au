# Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic（Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-4081 – Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic。**

Set up, operate, or tend more than one type of cutting or forming machine tool or robot for metal and plastic parts, a frontline technical role in manufacturing.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 极少适用，通常要求本科学位，此岗位多数不满足 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled/Unskilled Workers） | 理论上可用，但雇主需证明劳工短缺，实际非常困难 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for multiple machine tool setters in the US?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage in 2023 is about $42,000, with entry-level $30,000-$40,000 and experienced up to $60,000+.

**问：Can this occupation get a H-1B or green card for US immigration?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation usually doesn't qualify as a specialty occupation for H-1B, and EB-3 green card requires PERM which employers rarely sponsor.

**问：What education is needed to become a multiple machine tool setter?**
答：Typically only a high school diploma is required; specialized skills are gained through on-the-job training or a 1-year vocational program.
