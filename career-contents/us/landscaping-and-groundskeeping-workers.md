# Landscaping and Groundskeeping Workers（Landscaping and Groundskeeping Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：37-3011 – Landscaping and Groundskeeping Workers。**

Maintain grounds of property using hand or power tools, including mowing, trimming, planting, and watering; excludes farmworkers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（Temporary Non-Agricultural Workers） | 季节性临时工作签证，常见于园林绿化行业，雇主需证明劳工短缺 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card） | 通过PERM劳工认证申请绿卡，但要求雇主证明无法找到合格美国工人，实际难度大 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for landscaping workers?**
答：Median annual wage about $37,000; entry-level around $29,000, experienced up to $48,000 depending on location.

**问：Can landscaping workers immigrate to the US?**
答：Mainly via H-2B temporary visa; green card is difficult due to strict labor certification and limited quotas.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities?**
答：Yes, to supervisor, project manager, or self-employed. CDL and horticulture certifications help.
