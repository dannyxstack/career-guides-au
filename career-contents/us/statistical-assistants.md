# Statistical Assistants（Statistical Assistants）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-9111 – Statistical Assistants。**

Statistical assistants compile and compute data according to statistical formulas for use in statistical studies. They may perform actuarial computations and compile charts and graphs for use by actuaries, including actuarial clerks.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for statistical assistants?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, the median annual wage for statistical assistants is about $48,000, with entry-level ranging from $35,000 to $45,000 and senior positions up to $80,000.

**问：Can statistical assistants apply for H-1B visas?**
答：Yes, but usually require a bachelor's degree. The employer must prove the position is a specialty occupation, and it is subject to annual caps.
