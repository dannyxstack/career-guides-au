# Physics Teachers, Postsecondary（Physics Teachers, Postsecondary）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-1054 – Physics Teachers, Postsecondary。**

Teach physics courses about the laws of matter and energy at postsecondary level, combining teaching and research.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for physics professors?**
答：Assistant professors earn $55k-$70k; associates $70k-$95k; full professors $95k-$150k; top private universities pay more.

**问：How can foreign physics professors immigrate to the US?**
答：Universities sponsor H-1B visas, then EB-2 or EB-1A green cards; requires PhD and research record.

**问：What is the career path for physics professors?**
答：Start as assistant professor, earn tenure in ~6 years to associate, then ~5-7 years to full professor.
