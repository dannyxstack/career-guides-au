# Amusement and Recreation Attendants（Amusement and Recreation Attendants）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：39-3091 – Amusement and Recreation Attendants。**

Perform various attending duties at amusement or recreation facilities, including maintaining equipment, guiding visitors, operating rides, and selling tickets.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Amusement and Recreation Attendants?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage in 2023 was about $28,000, with entry-level around $24,000 and senior supervisors earning above $50,000. Pay varies by location and season.

**问：Can Amusement and Recreation Attendants immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Generally no. This occupation does not meet H-1B specialty occupation criteria, and PERM labor certification is difficult due to sufficient domestic labor supply.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically only a high school diploma. Some employers may require first aid certification or firearms safety training (e.g., shooting ranges).
