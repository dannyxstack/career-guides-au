# Model Makers, Wood（Model Makers, Wood）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-7031 – Model Makers, Wood。**

Construct full-size and scale wooden precision models of products, including wood jig builders and loft workers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 需证明职位为专业职业，通常不适用，因为木制模型制造通常不要求学士学位。 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Workers/Professionals/Unskilled Workers） | 可尝试通过PERM劳工认证申请，但雇主需证明无法找到美国工人，过程复杂且排期长。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of wood model makers?**
答：The median annual salary for wood model makers in the US is about $45,000, with entry-level around $30,000-$40,000 and senior up to $70,000+, but overall low.

**问：Can wood model makers immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation is not a specialty occupation, usually not eligible for H-1B. EB-3 green card is theoretically possible, but employer must prove labor shortage via PERM, with long backlog and rare cases.

**问：What is the future job outlook for this occupation?**
答：Outlook is average. With 3D printing and CNC machines becoming common, demand for traditional wood models declines, but niche high-end custom and restoration opportunities exist.
