## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Start as an apprentice or junior mold maker, progress to senior mold maker, supervisor, or workshop manager. With experience, can also transition to product design, prototyping, or self-employment.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using digital software for drawing layout and dimension calculations
- Automatic cutting and engraving of regular shapes with CNC machines
- Repetitive cutting, grinding, and drilling tasks
- Batch reproduction based on existing models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-driven 3D modeling software accelerates prototype design iteration
- CNC and robotics enable high-precision cutting and complex surface machining
- Augmented reality (AR)-assisted assembly and inspection improve efficiency
- Simulation software predicts wood stress and deformation, optimizing structures
- Digital twin technology for virtual testing and remote collaboration

**Human moat:**

- Intuition about wood properties and manual finishing skills
- Customer communication and custom design modification skills
- Hand carving and splicing of complex curved surfaces and organic shapes
- Comprehensive judgment of model aesthetics, proportion, and functionality

**Skills to build:**

- CAD and 3D modeling (e.g., Fusion 360, SolidWorks)
- CNC programming and operation
- Augmented Reality (AR) Assembly Assistance Software
- Digital simulation and structural analysis
- Project management and client communication
- Mixed material (wood + plastic/metal) processing

**Key growth areas:**

- stable demand
- custom furniture
- architectural models
- manual craftsmanship

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for wooden model makers?**
A: Median annual salary for US wooden model makers is about $45,000; entry-level: $30,000–$40,000; senior can reach over $70,000, but overall low.

**Q: Can wooden model makers immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
A: Very difficult. This occupation is not a professional specialty and generally cannot apply for H-1B visa. EB-3 green card is theoretically possible, but employer must prove labor shortage via PERM, and waiting period is long; actual cases are rare.

**Q: What is the future employment outlook for this occupation?**
A: Outlook average. With the rise of 3D printing and CNC machines, traditional wooden model demand decreases, but there are still small opportunities in high-end custom and restoration fields.
