# Conservation Scientists（Conservation Scientists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：19-1031 – Conservation Scientists。**

Conservation scientists manage, improve, and protect natural resources to maximize their use without damaging the environment. They conduct soil surveys, develop erosion control plans, and advise farmers on sustainable practices like crop rotation and contour plowing.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for conservation scientists?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for conservation scientists is about $65,000. Entry-level: $45,000-$55,000; mid-level: $58,000-$75,000; senior: $78,000-$100,000. Federal positions typically pay more than private nonprofits.

**问：What is the US work visa path for conservation scientists?**
答：Primary route is H-1B visa, requiring bachelor's degree and employer sponsorship. Universities and non-profit research organizations may be cap-exempt. Green card via EB-2 with PERM labor certification. Some federal agencies may offer J-1 visas.

**问：What is the future career outlook?**
答：Stable, with demand driven by climate change and sustainability policies. Federal agencies (e.g., NRCS) offer many jobs but depend on budget. Private sector (environmental consulting) growing faster. Experienced professionals can move into policy or management.
