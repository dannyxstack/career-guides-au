# Riggers（Riggers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：49-9096 – Riggers。**

Set up or repair rigging for construction projects, manufacturing plants, logging yards, ships and shipyards, or for the entertainment industry.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for riggers?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for riggers was about $56,980 in 2022, with entry-level earning $38,000-$50,000 and experienced up to $85,000.

**问：Can riggers apply for US skilled migration?**
答：Riggers typically qualify for EB-3 Skilled Workers green card via employer sponsorship. H-1B is generally not applicable as it is not a specialty occupation.

**问：How do riggers advance their career?**
答：Start as apprentice, then become journeyman, foreman, or safety supervisor. Further advance by obtaining crane operator certification.
