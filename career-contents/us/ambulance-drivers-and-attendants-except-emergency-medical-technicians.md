# Ambulance Drivers and Attendants, Except Emergency Medical Technicians（Ambulance Drivers and Attendants, Except Emergency Medical Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：53-3011 – Ambulance Drivers and Attendants, Except Emergency Medical Technicians。**

Drive ambulance or assist driver in transporting sick, injured, or convalescent persons; assist in lifting patients, no emergency medical treatment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
