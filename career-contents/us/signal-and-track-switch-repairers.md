# Signal and Track Switch Repairers（Signal and Track Switch Repairers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：49-9097 – Signal and Track Switch Repairers。**

Install, inspect, test, maintain, or repair electric gate crossings, signals, signal equipment, track switches, section lines, or intercommunications systems within a railroad system.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for signal and track switch repairers?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, the average annual salary is about $65,000, with a median of $62,000. Experienced senior workers can earn over $80,000.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for H-1B immigration to the US?**
答：No. The occupation typically requires a high school diploma and on-the-job training, not a bachelor's degree, so it does not meet H-1B specialty occupation requirements. Employer sponsorship is very rare.

**问：How to become a signal and track switch repairer?**
答：Usually requires a high school diploma, followed by on-the-job training or apprenticeship (3-4 years) provided by employers. Some community colleges offer relevant courses.
