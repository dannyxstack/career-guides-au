# Cooks, Fast Food（Cooks, Fast Food）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：35-2011 – Cooks, Fast Food。**

Prepare and cook food in fast food restaurants with limited menus. Duties restricted to basic items and operating large-volume single-purpose cooking equipment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Workers） | 用于临时非农业工作，但快餐厨师通常不符合资格，因属永久性岗位 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 理论上可申请但实际极罕见，因雇主需证明劳工短缺且技能要求低 |
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 不符合，因非专业职业 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for fast food cooks? Is there room for growth?**
答：Fast food cooks earn low wages, median annual wage around $29,000 in 2022. Advancement to manager or other culinary roles can increase income.

**问：Can fast food cooks immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
答：Almost impossible. It is a low-skilled occupation, not qualifying for H-1B or employment-based green cards. Only possible via family or investment immigration.

**问：What career paths exist for fast food cooks?**
答：Can advance to assistant manager, restaurant manager, or move to other culinary roles in full-service restaurants. Some employers offer management training.
