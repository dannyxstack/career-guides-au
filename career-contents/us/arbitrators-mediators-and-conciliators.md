# Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators（Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：23-1022 – Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators。**

Facilitate negotiation and conflict resolution through dialogue. Resolve conflicts outside of the court system by mutual consent of parties involved.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for arbitrators/mediators?**
答：Median annual salary is about $74,000, but entry-level may be below $40,000, while senior experts can exceed $150,000. Income depends on caseload and reputation.

**问：Can international students become arbitrators in the US via H-1B?**
答：Yes, but requires employer sponsorship and demonstration of a specialty occupation. Most mediators are self-employed or at small firms, making H-1B difficult; larger institutions or law firms are more likely to sponsor. EB-2 green card path is also possible.

**问：Is a legal background required to become an arbitrator?**
答：Not mandatory, but most have a background in law, social work, or HR. Many states only require completion of mediation training. Arbitrators typically need legal or industry experience.
