# Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders（Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-9041 – Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders。**

Set up, operate, or tend machines such as glass-forming or plodder machines to shape products like glassware, food, rubber, brick, tile, and cosmetics.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
