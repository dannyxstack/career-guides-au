# Materials Engineers（Materials Engineers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：17-2131 – Materials Engineers。**

Evaluate materials and develop machinery and processes to manufacture materials for products meeting specialized design and performance specifications. Includes composite, metal, ceramic, and polymer specialists.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 适用于专业职位，需学士学位，有年度配额，抽签制 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 需硕士学位或学士加5年经验，通过PERM劳工认证 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference (EB-3)） | 适用于学士学位或技术工人，需PERM劳工认证 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Materials Engineers？**
- 对材料科学有浓厚兴趣，喜欢实验室和工程工作
- 具备分析和解决复杂问题的能力
- 注重细节，有团队合作精神

**谁不适合学Materials Engineers？**
- 不喜欢技术细节和重复性实验
- 对工程数学和物理缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for materials engineers?**
答：Median annual wage in the US is around $100,000, with entry-level about $70,000 and senior up to $150,000. Varies by experience, industry, and location.

**问：How difficult is it for materials engineers to get a US green card?**
答：Materials engineers are STEM, eligible for EB-2/EB-3. Requires employer sponsorship and PERM. Priority dates take 2-4 years; demand is stable.

**问：What are the career paths for materials engineers?**
答：Can advance to senior engineer, project manager, or move to R&D, quality control, consulting. PhD leads to research or academia.
