# Facilities Managers（Facilities Managers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：11-3013 – Facilities Managers。**

Facilities managers plan, direct, or coordinate operations and functionalities of facilities and buildings, including surrounding grounds or multiple facilities. They ensure safe, efficient operations and alignment with organizational goals.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 适用于需学士学位或更高的专业职位，设施经理通常符合条件，但需抽签 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 适用于拥有高等学位或杰出能力的申请人，需PERM劳工认证 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference (EB-3)） | 适用于专业人士、技术工人等，需PERM，排期较长 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 通过雇主赞助的绿卡申请，需证明无合适美国工人 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for facilities managers?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage is around $98,000, with entry-level $55,000-$75,000 and senior $100,000-$140,000+.

**问：Is it feasible for facilities managers to get a US work visa?**
答：Yes, via H-1B (requires bachelor's) or EB-2/EB-3 green card. Requires employer sponsorship, and H-1B has a lottery.

**问：What education is needed to become a facilities manager?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in business, engineering or related. Master's or certifications like CFM are advantageous.
