# Broadcast Announcers and Radio Disc Jockeys（Broadcast Announcers and Radio Disc Jockeys）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：27-3011 – Broadcast Announcers and Radio Disc Jockeys。**

Speak or read from scripts on radio, TV, or other media; may play music, announce performances, or interview guests.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常要求学士学位，但广播主持人需证明特殊性 |
| Subclass O-1（O-1 Extraordinary Ability） | 适用于在广播领域有杰出成就的个人 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary outlook for broadcast announcers?**
答：Salaries range from $25,000 in small markets to $120,000 in large markets, but average is low and affected by automation.

**问：Can broadcast announcers immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Unlikely, as it rarely meets H-1B specialty occupation criteria, and O-1 visa requirements are very high.
