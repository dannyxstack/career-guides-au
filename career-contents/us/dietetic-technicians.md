# Dietetic Technicians（Dietetic Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-2051 – Dietetic Technicians。**

Assist in food service and nutritional programs under the supervision of a dietitian; may plan meals, teach nutrition principles, or counsel individuals.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Dietetic Technicians?**
答：The median annual wage in the US is about $38,000, with entry-level $32,000-$40,000 and senior up to $60,000.

**问：Is it easy for Dietetic Technicians to get a US work visa?**
答：It's relatively difficult as the job typically requires only an associate degree, which may not qualify as a specialty occupation for H-1B. Some employers may sponsor EB-3 green card via PERM.

**问：Can Dietetic Technicians advance to become dietitians?**
答：Yes. They need a bachelor's degree, an AND-accredited internship, and pass the registration exam to become a Registered Dietitian.
