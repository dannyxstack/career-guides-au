# Farmworkers, Farm, Ranch, and Aquacultural Animals（Farmworkers, Farm, Ranch, and Aquacultural Animals）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：45-2093 – Farmworkers, Farm, Ranch, and Aquacultural Animals。**

Attend to live farm animals such as cattle, sheep, swine, poultry, and fish, performing feeding, milking, herding, and cleaning duties to ensure animal health and productivity.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2A（H-2A Temporary Agricultural Worker） | 临时农业工人签证，需雇主担保，季节性工作，但通常不直接通往绿卡 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled/Unskilled Worker） | 职业移民第三优先，但需PERM劳工证，农业工人通常薪资低难以满足要求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for farm animal workers?**
答：Annual salary ranges from $25,000 to $45,000, with average hourly wage around $14, varying by region and farm type.

**问：Can foreigners work in this occupation and immigrate to the US?**
答：Mainly suitable for H-2A temporary work, but green card conversion is very difficult due to employer sponsorship requirements and low wages typically not meeting PERM thresholds.

**问：Is there room for advancement?**
答：Can advance to farm supervisor, livestock manager, or self-employed farmer, but general career progression is limited.
