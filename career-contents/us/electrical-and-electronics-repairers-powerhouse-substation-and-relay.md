# Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Powerhouse, Substation, and Relay（Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Powerhouse, Substation, and Relay）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：49-2095 – Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Powerhouse, Substation, and Relay。**

Inspect, test, repair, or maintain electrical equipment in generating stations, substations, and in-service relays to ensure continuous power supply and safe operation.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for electrical repairers in the US?**
答：The median annual wage is around $70,000, with entry-level at $45,000-$60,000 and senior up to $110,000.

**问：Is immigration easy for this occupation?**
答：Common path is EB-3 green card requiring employer sponsorship and PERM. H-1B is difficult as it usually requires a degree.
