# Health Education Specialists（Health Education Specialists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：21-1091 – Health Education Specialists。**

Provide and manage health education programs to help individuals, families, and communities maximize and maintain healthy lifestyles. Use data to identify community needs, plan, implement, monitor, and evaluate programs that encourage healthy lifestyles, policies, and environments. May link health systems, providers, insurers, and patients to address individual and community health issues.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for health education specialists?**
答：Entry-level around $40,000-$55,000, mid-level $55,000-$75,000, senior up to $100,000. Government and non-profit pay lower, hospitals and private companies higher.

**问：How can international students immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Typical path: F-1→OPT→H-1B→EB-2/EB-3 green card. Requires employer sponsorship, H-1B lottery, PERM and long waiting times.

**问：Is certification required?**
答：Not mandatory, but CHES certification significantly improves job prospects. Many employers prefer or require it.
