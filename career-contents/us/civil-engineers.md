# Civil Engineers（Civil Engineers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：17-2051 – Civil Engineers。**

Civil engineers plan, design, and oversee construction and maintenance of structures and facilities like roads, bridges, dams, and pipelines, including structural, traffic, and geotechnical engineering.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 最常见的非移民工作签证，需雇主担保，每年配额受限 |
| Subclass EB-2/EB-3（Employment-Based Green Card (PERM)） | 职业移民绿卡，通常需PERM劳工证，适合有经验的工程师 |
| Subclass TN（TN Status (USMCA)） | 适用于加拿大和墨西哥公民，作为工程师类专业人士申请 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for civil engineers in the US?**
答：BLS 2023 data shows median annual wage of $89,940, with entry-level $60k-80k and senior over $110k.

**问：What are the main immigration pathways for civil engineers to the US?**
答：Common pathways include H-1B visa (employer-sponsored) and EB-2/EB-3 green card (PERM). TN status available for Canadian/Mexican citizens.

**问：What is the job outlook for civil engineers in the US?**
答：Projected growth of 6% from 2023-2033, driven by infrastructure renewal and sustainability projects.
