# Healthcare Social Workers（Healthcare Social Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：21-1022 – Healthcare Social Workers。**

Provide psychosocial support to individuals and families coping with chronic, acute, or terminal illnesses, including counseling, referrals, and care management.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 适用于持MSW学位并在医疗机构工作的社工，需雇主担保且受年度配额限制。 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 适用于拥有硕士学位的社工，需PERM劳工证，名额充足。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 通过PERM流程申请职业移民绿卡，雇主需证明无法招聘到合格美国工人。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Healthcare Social Workers？**
- 富有同理心、善于沟通，乐于帮助患者应对疾病挑战
- 能承受情感压力并处理复杂病例情况
- 具备跨文化能力，服务多元人群

**谁不适合学Healthcare Social Workers？**
- 无法处理情感压力或面对生死
- 不喜欢多任务处理和文书工作

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for healthcare social workers?**
答：The median annual salary is around $60,000, with entry-level $48,000-$62,000 and senior $78,000-$100,000, varying by location and employer.

**问：What visa options are available for foreign healthcare social workers?**
答：Main options include H-1B visa (requires MSW) and EB-2 green card via PERM. TN visa is available for Canadian or Mexican citizens.

**问：What licenses are required to become a healthcare social worker?**
答：Clinical roles require state licensure (e.g., LCSW), which typically requires an MSW, supervised clinical hours, and passing an exam. Non-clinical roles may only need a master's degree.
