# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers（Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-2022 – Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers。**

Assemble or modify electrical or electronic equipment, such as computers, test equipment, telemetering systems, electric motors, and batteries.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers?**
答：Average annual salary is about $38,000, entry-level around $30,000, senior up to $58,000. Salary varies by experience, location, and industry.

**问：Can foreigners come to the US for this occupation via work visa?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree, so H-1B is not applicable. Only a few may get EB-3 green cards, but PERM is needed and quota is tight.

**问：What training is needed to become an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler?**
答：Typically a high school diploma followed by on-the-job training. Some community colleges offer electronic assembly certificates lasting 6 months to 2 years.
