# Agricultural Inspectors（Agricultural Inspectors）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：45-2011 – Agricultural Inspectors。**

Inspect agricultural commodities, processing equipment, and facilities, and fish and logging operations to ensure compliance with health, quality, and safety regulations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常不适用，因为该职位通常不需要学士学位，但可能有例外 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-2/EB-3)） | 雇主赞助困难，因职位通常由政府资助，需证明无法找到美国工人 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Agricultural Inspectors?**
答：Entry-level: $35,000-45,000; mid-level: $45,000-60,000; senior: $60,000-80,000.

**问：Is it easy to get a work visa or green card as an Agricultural Inspector?**
答：Difficult. The position is often government-based, employer sponsorship is rare, and it usually doesn't qualify as a specialty occupation for H-1B.
