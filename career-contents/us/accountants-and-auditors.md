# Accountants and Auditors（Accountants and Auditors）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：13-2011 – Accountants and Auditors。**

Accountants and Auditors examine, analyze, and interpret accounting records to prepare financial statements, give advice, or audit and evaluate statements. They provide expertise in tax, cost, and financial data systems for businesses, governments, and individuals.

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 注册会计师(CPA) | 美国各州会计委员会 | 可选 | 提供审计意见必备，需通过Uniform CPA Exam |

| 注册内部审计师(CIA) | 国际内部审计师协会(IIA) | 可选 | 内部审计领域权威认证 |

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 会计师可通过H-1B签证在美国工作，需学士学位及雇主担保，每年配额有限需抽签。 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 高级学位或杰出能力会计师可通过EB-2申请绿卡，需PERM劳工证，排期约2-3年。 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference (EB-3)） | 学士学位或同等经验会计师可通过EB-3申请绿卡，需PERM，排期约3-4年。 |
| Subclass TN（TN NAFTA Professional） | 加拿大或墨西哥公民可凭会计师身份（需符合NAFTA要求）获得TN签证，无需抽签。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for accountants?**
答：The median annual wage for accountants in the US is about $78,000. Entry-level salaries range from $50,000 to $75,000, senior positions over $110,000, and partners can exceed $200,000. Salary varies by location, industry, and certifications.

**问：How can accountants immigrate via US work visas?**
答：Common paths include H-1B (lottery) or PERM-based EB-2/EB-3 green cards. Requires employer sponsorship, a bachelor's degree, and CPA certification can improve chances. Canadian/Mexican citizens may qualify for TN status.

**问：What education and certifications are needed to become an accountant?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in accounting or a related field. CPA requires 150 credit hours (some states require a master's). CPA is the gold standard, while CMA and CIA enhance competitiveness.
