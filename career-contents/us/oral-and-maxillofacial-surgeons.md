# Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons（Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-1022 – Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons。**

Perform surgery on oral and maxillofacial regions to treat diseases, injuries, or defects; may improve function or appearance.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the typical salary for an oral and maxillofacial surgeon?**
答：Entry-level: $200k-$300k; mid-career: $300k-$450k; senior: $450k-$600k.

**问：How can international medical graduates become oral surgeons in the US?**
答：Complete a US dental school or credential evaluation, pass NBDE, finish CODA residency, then apply for H-1B or EB-2.

**问：Is immigration to the US difficult for this profession?**
答：Moderately difficult, requiring US licensure and residency, but high demand enables EB-2/EB-3 green card paths.
