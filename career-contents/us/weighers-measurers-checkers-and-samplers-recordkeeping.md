# Weighers, Measurers, Checkers, and Samplers, Recordkeeping（Weighers, Measurers, Checkers, and Samplers, Recordkeeping）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-5111 – Weighers, Measurers, Checkers, and Samplers, Recordkeeping。**

Weigh, measure, and check materials, supplies, and equipment for recordkeeping purposes, primarily clerical duties.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
