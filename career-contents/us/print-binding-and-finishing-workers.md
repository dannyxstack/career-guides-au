# Print Binding and Finishing Workers（Print Binding and Finishing Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-5113 – Print Binding and Finishing Workers。**

Bind books and other publications or finish printed products by hand or machine. May set up binding and finishing machines.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（Specialty Occupations） | 通常不符合专业职业要求，极少使用 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 理论可行但雇主极少担保，因非高技能 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for print binding workers?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage is about $37,000 in 2023, ranging from $28,000 to $58,000, below national average.

**问：Can this occupation get a US work visa or green card?**
答：Very difficult. It generally does not meet H-1B specialty occupation criteria, and EB-3 green card is rarely sponsored due to low skill.
