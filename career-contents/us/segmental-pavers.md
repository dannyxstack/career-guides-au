# Segmental Pavers — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 47-4091 (SOC)**

Lay, cut, and install concrete/stone interlocking paving blocks, including base and restraining material installation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can advance from paver to paving crew leader, project manager, or start own paving company.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic laying of interlocking blocks according to preset patterns, repetitive paving
- Using laser or GPS control for subgrade leveling and slope adjustment
- Manually cut blocks to fit edges and irregular spaces
- Material transport and base gravel spreading
- On-site measurement and layout marking

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operate automated paving equipment and monitor its work quality
- Using BIM and drones for construction site planning and progress tracking
- Use AI vision inspection system to check paving smoothness and gap consistency in real time
- Simulate effects of different paving plans using digital twins

**Human moat:**

- Flexible handling of complex terrain or irregular boundaries
- Repair defects in paved surfaces and aesthetic judgments
- Client communication, on-site coordination, and safety supervision
- Creative paving pattern design and material mixing
- Engineering judgment on special ground conditions and drainage systems

**Skills to build:**

- Programming and operation of automated paving equipment
- BIM (Building Information Modeling) software application
- GPS surveying and laser leveling system operation
- Use and interpretation of AI detection tools
- Basic mechanical repair and troubleshooting
- Project management and team coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Landscaping
- Driveway
- Permeable Pavers

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a paving worker?**
A: According to BLS data, 2023 median annual salary is around $41,000, experienced workers can exceed $60,000.

**Q: Can pavers apply for US work visas or green cards?**
A: This occupation usually does not meet H-1B requirements, and PERM labor certification is difficult to obtain because a bachelor's degree is not required. The only pathway is H-2B seasonal visa, but no green card path.
