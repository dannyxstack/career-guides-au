# Eligibility Interviewers, Government Programs（Eligibility Interviewers, Government Programs）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-4061 – Eligibility Interviewers, Government Programs。**

Determine eligibility of persons applying for government assistance programs such as welfare, unemployment benefits, social security, and public housing through interviews and document review.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常不适用，因该职位学历要求多不高，不符合专业职业标准 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-2/EB-3)） | 极少数情况可能通过劳工证申请，但雇主担保困难 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：Salary is moderate to low, entry-level around $40,000, senior $55,000-$70,000, but benefits and stability are good.

**问：Can foreign nationals apply for H-1B visa for this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. The position typically does not meet H-1B specialty occupation requirements, and employers rarely sponsor foreign workers.

**问：What are the daily work tasks?**
答：Mainly interviewing applicants, reviewing documents, data entry, assessing eligibility, and explaining regulations.
