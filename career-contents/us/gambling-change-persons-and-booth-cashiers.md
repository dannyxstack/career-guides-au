# Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 41-2012 (SOC)**

Exchanges coins, tokens, and chips for customers, provides change services in the slot machine area, and handles initial fund allocation, drawer cash counting, and auditing.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the main sources of income for this occupation?**
A: Basic hourly wage plus customer tips, some casinos also offer bonuses. Annual salary is generally low, with a median of around USD 28,000.

**Q: Is this occupation easy for US work visa or green card applications?**
A: Very difficult. This occupation typically does not meet H-1B specialty occupation requirements, and employers rarely sponsor H-2B visas. There are almost no skilled migration pathways.

**Q: What certificates or licenses are required?**
A: Most states require casino workers to hold a gaming license, which requires a background check. Math or customer service training is not mandatory but useful.
