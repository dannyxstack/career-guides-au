# Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers（Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：41-2012 – Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers。**

Exchange coins, tokens, and chips for patrons; operate booths in slot machine areas, furnish change persons with money banks, and count/audit cash drawers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the main source of income for this occupation?**
答：Hourly wage plus customer tips, with some casino bonuses. Annual salary is typically low, around $28,000 median.

**问：Is it easy to get a US work visa or green card for this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. It typically does not qualify for H-1B specialty occupation, and employer sponsorship for H-2B is rare. Almost no skilled migration path.

**问：What certifications or licenses are required?**
答：Most states require a gaming license for casino workers, involving a background check. Math or customer service training is optional but beneficial.
