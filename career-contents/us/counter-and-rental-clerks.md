# Counter and Rental Clerks（Counter and Rental Clerks）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：41-2021 – Counter and Rental Clerks。**

Counter and rental clerks receive orders for repairs, rentals, and services in person, describe options, compute costs, and process payments, typically at retail or rental counters.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常不符合H-1B要求，因该职业通常不要求学士学位 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Workers/Professionals） | 可能适用但雇主需证明劳动力短缺且支付现行工资，较少见 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Which industries pay higher salaries for counter and rental clerks?**
答：Equipment rental (e.g., tools, vehicles) or specialty rental firms may offer higher pay, but overall income remains low, typically under $47,000.

**问：Can counter and rental clerks immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Extremely difficult. The occupation rarely qualifies for H-1B as it typically does not require a bachelor's degree, and employer sponsorship for EB-3 green cards is uncommon.
