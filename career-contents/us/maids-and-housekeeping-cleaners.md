# Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners（Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：37-2012 – Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners。**

Perform light cleaning duties in private households or commercial establishments like hotels and hospitals to maintain a clean and orderly environment, including making beds, replenishing linens, cleaning rooms, and vacuuming.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for maids and housekeeping cleaners in the US?**
答：According to BLS, median hourly wage is around $13, annual median about $27,000. Experienced workers can earn over $35,000.

**问：Is it easy to get a work visa as a maid or housekeeping cleaner in the US?**
答：Very difficult. H-1B not applicable (lack of specialty), H-2B seasonal but limited quota and employer must prove shortage. EB-3 green card path impractical. Almost no skilled migration route.

**问：What are the advancement opportunities?**
答：Can advance to housekeeping supervisor, manager, or start own cleaning business.
