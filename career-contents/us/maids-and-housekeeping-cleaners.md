# Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 37-2012 (SOC)**

Perform light cleaning tasks in hotels, hospitals, or private residences, such as making beds, restocking linens, cleaning rooms and halls, vacuuming, etc., maintaining a tidy and orderly environment.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Usually starts with informal work experience, no formal education required. Can advance to team leader or supervisor, or move to other cleaning service management roles. Some take over cleaning businesses or start their own after gaining experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily vacuuming, mopping and other floor cleaning tasks (replaced by automated robotic vacuum cleaners)
- Simple bed making and linen changes (replaced by automated bed-making robots)
- Routine bathroom cleaning (replaced by automatic disinfectant sprays and robot scrubbing)
- Scheduled cleaning of open areas such as lobbies (replaced by autonomous floor scrubbers)
- Waste collection and sorting (replaced by smart bins and automated sorting systems)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and task allocation, improving efficiency
- Use sensor data to monitor cleaning quality, precisely locating uncleaned areas.
- Receive customer special cleaning instructions via voice assistant or app and provide real-time feedback
- Operate and maintain cleaning robots, handle exceptions
- Combine image recognition for deep cleaning quality checks to ensure high standards.

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex, irregular scenarios such as accidental stains and areas around fragile items
- Direct communication with clients to understand personalized needs and provide customized services
- Provide meticulous, warm service experience in high-end residences or hotels
- Supervise cleaning robots and manually intervene in emergencies
- Deep cleaning (e.g., carpet stain removal, furniture polishing) requires manual judgment and fine operation

**Skills to build:**

- Cleaning robot operation and maintenance
- Use of smart cleaning system scheduling software
- Basic troubleshooting and repair (cleaning equipment)
- Customer service and communication skills
- Eco-friendly cleaning materials and energy-saving knowledge
- Quality inspection and data analysis (using sensor reports)

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable demand
- Hospitality growth
- Low automation risk
- Entry-level opportunities

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for maids and house cleaners in the USA?**
A: According to BLS, the median hourly wage in 2022 was about $13, and the median annual wage was about $27,000. Experienced workers can earn over $35,000 annually.

**Q: Is it easy for foreign workers to get a maid-type work visa?**
A: Very difficult. H-1B usually not applicable (lack of specialty); H-2B for seasonal workers but with limited quotas and employer must prove shortage. EB-3 green card path unrealistic. Virtually no skilled migration pathway.

**Q: What are the promotion opportunities in this occupation?**
A: Can advance to housekeeping team leader, supervisor, or assistant manager. May also move into cleaning company management, or start own cleaning service.
