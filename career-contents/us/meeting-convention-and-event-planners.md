# Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners（Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：13-1121 – Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners。**

Coordinate activities of staff, convention personnel, or clients to make arrangements for group meetings, events, or conventions, including venue selection, budget management, and vendor coordination.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for event planners?**
答：Entry-level around $35,000-$50,000, senior up to $110,000, depending on experience, location, and employer.

**问：Can event planners apply for US work visas?**
答：Yes, typically via H-1B visa requiring a bachelor's degree and employer sponsorship. EB-2/EB-3 green cards are also possible with PERM certification.

**问：How to advance career?**
答：Obtain certifications like CMP, gain experience with large events, build network, and advance to management or entrepreneurship.
