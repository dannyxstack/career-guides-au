# Home Health Aides（Home Health Aides）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：31-1121 – Home Health Aides。**

Monitor health status and address health-related needs of individuals with disabilities or illness, under direction of licensed nursing staff; provide assistance with routine healthcare tasks and activities of daily living.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 家庭健康助理通常不符合特殊职业要求，极少申请 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Workers/Professionals） | 需要至少2年培训或经验，但家庭健康助理通常不被视为技术职业 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 通过PERM劳工认证的雇主担保绿卡，对家庭健康助理极为困难，需求较低 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Home Health Aides？**
- 有耐心和同理心的人
- 喜欢帮助他人且不介意体力劳动的人

**谁不适合学Home Health Aides？**
- 偏好高薪职业的人
- 无法承受情感或体力压力的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Home Health Aides?**
答：Home Health Aides earn a median annual wage of about $30,180 in 2023. Entry-level positions range from $25,000 to $29,000, while senior or supervisory roles can exceed $37,000.

**问：Is it possible for Home Health Aides to immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
答：Rarely. Home Health Aides are generally not considered specialty occupations or skilled workers, making H-1B and EB-3 green card pathways very difficult, with few employers willing to sponsor.

**问：What career advancement opportunities exist for Home Health Aides?**
答：They can advance to Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN), Registered Nurse (RN), or care coordinator roles, and also become medical assistants or case managers with additional training.
