# Home Health Aides — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 31-1121 (SOC)**

A non-clinical caregiver who provides health monitoring, wound care, medication assistance, and daily living activity support for disabled or ill individuals under the guidance of a registered nurse.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of home health aides?**
A: Home health aide salaries are low; 2023 median annual salary about $30,180, entry-level about $25,000-$29,000, senior or managerial up to $37,000+.

**Q: Is it possible for a home health aide to immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
A: Rare. Home health aides are typically not considered specialty occupations or skilled workers; H-1B and EB-3 green card paths are difficult, and basically no employers are willing to sponsor.

**Q: What positions can a home health aide advance to?**
A: Can advance to Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN), Registered Nurse (RN), or care coordinator, or become a medical assistant or case manager through additional training.
