# Septic Tank Servicers and Sewer Pipe Cleaners（Septic Tank Servicers and Sewer Pipe Cleaners）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-4071 – Septic Tank Servicers and Sewer Pipe Cleaners。**

Clean and repair septic tanks, sewer lines, or drains; may patch tank walls, replace damaged drain tiles, or repair broken underground piping.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage in 2023 was about $43,000; entry-level around $30,000, experienced up to $60,000-$70,000. Overtime and contracting can increase earnings.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for immigration to the US?**
答：No. It is a low-skilled occupation, typically only accessible via H-2B temporary visa; employer-sponsored green card is extremely difficult.

**问：Are special licenses required?**
答：Most states require a Commercial Driver's License (CDL) and a state septic tank servicer license; OSHA safety certification is a plus.
