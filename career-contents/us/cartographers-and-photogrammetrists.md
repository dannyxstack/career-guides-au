# Cartographers and Photogrammetrists（Cartographers and Photogrammetrists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：17-1021 – Cartographers and Photogrammetrists。**

Cartographers and photogrammetrists research, study, and prepare maps and other spatial data in digital or graphic form for various purposes including legal, social, political, educational, and design. They work with Geographic Information Systems (GIS), design algorithms and data structures, and collect, analyze, and interpret geographic information.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 适用于需要学士或更高学位的专业职位，包括制图师和摄影测量师 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference） | 适用于拥有高等学位或特殊能力的移民，常见于该职业 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 适用于技术工人、专业人士，部分雇主通过PERM劳工认证申请 |
| Subclass TN（TN Status under USMCA） | 适用于加拿大或墨西哥公民，专业职业包括制图师，但需符合特定要求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for cartographers?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, median annual wage is around $73,000. Entry-level positions range $45,000-$65,000, while senior roles can exceed $130,000.

**问：What are common visa paths for cartographers to immigrate to the US?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa requiring bachelor's or higher degree, and EB-2/EB-3 immigrant visas through employer sponsorship with PERM labor certification. TN visa is available for Canadian or Mexican citizens.
