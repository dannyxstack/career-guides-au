# Demonstrators and Product Promoters（Demonstrators and Product Promoters）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：41-9011 – Demonstrators and Product Promoters。**

Demonstrate merchandise and answer questions to create public buying interest; may sell demonstrated products. Common in retail shows, supermarkets, or trade exhibitions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常不符合要求，因学历门槛低 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（EB-3 Green Card） | 极少雇主担保，因职业非专业 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for demonstrators and product promoters?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage in 2023 was about $31,000, with entry-level part-time positions below $25,000 and senior or commission-based roles up to $65,000.

**问：Can a foreign national become a demonstrator in the US via work visa?**
答：Extremely unlikely, as the job typically requires only a high school diploma, failing H-1B specialty occupation criteria, and employers rarely sponsor green cards.
