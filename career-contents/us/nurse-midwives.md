# Nurse Midwives — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 29-1161 (SOC)**

Nurse-midwives diagnose and coordinate all aspects of childbirth, work independently or as part of a healthcare team, and provide gynecological care. Requires specialized graduate nursing education.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for nurse-midwives in the United States?**
A: According to BLS, the median annual salary was about $120,000 in 2023, entry-level $90,000-$110,000, senior can exceed $160,000. Salary varies by location, experience, and employer.

**Q: What is the main pathway for foreign midwife nurses to immigrate to the USA?**
A: Common pathways include H-1B work visa (requiring employer sponsorship and subject to quota) or EB-2/EB-3 green card (requiring PERM labor certification). As midwife nurses require US certification, overseas qualifications typically need evaluation or additional education.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a midwife nurse?**
A: Must first become a registered nurse (RN), then complete a master's or doctoral program in nurse-midwifery, pass the AMCB certification exam to obtain CNM certification. State APRN licenses are also required.
