# Cooks, Short Order（Cooks, Short Order）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：35-2015 – Cooks, Short Order。**

Cooks, Short Order prepare and cook to order a variety of foods that require only a short preparation time. They may take orders from customers and serve at counters or tables, typically in fast-food restaurants, cafeterias, and diners.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Worker） | 可用于季节性餐饮岗位，但非移民意图限制，雇主需证明劳动力短缺。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-3)） | 理论上可通过PERM申请，但短时厨师通常不符合特殊技能要求，极难获批。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for short order cooks?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for cooks, short order was about $30,000 in 2023, with lowest 10% earning below $22,000 and highest 10% over $42,000. Pay varies by location, employer, and experience.

**问：Are there immigration pathways for short order cooks to the US?**
答：Immigration pathways are very limited. H-2B visa for temporary non-agricultural workers may be used for seasonal positions, but requires employer certification and non-immigrant intent. EB-3 green card is theoretically possible but requires PERM labor certification and is extremely difficult for low-skilled roles; rarely approved.

**问：What career advancement can a short order cook expect?**
答：Common paths include promotion to chef, kitchen supervisor, or restaurant manager. Some transition to specialty cooking (e.g., pastry, grill) after gaining experience, or start their own small food business.
