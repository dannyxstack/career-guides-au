# Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers（Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：11-9141 – Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers。**

Plan, direct, or coordinate selling, buying, leasing, or governance activities of commercial, industrial, or residential real estate properties, including homeowner and condominium associations.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 适合拥有学士学位的经理，需专业对口（如商业管理），但名额有限。 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 需高等学位或特殊才能，房地产管理可能被视作专业职位，但申请需雇主支持。 |
| Subclass L-1（Intracompany Transferee (L-1)） | 跨国公司内部调派，适合在海外公司担任管理职务后转调美国。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 通过EB-2或EB-3申请绿卡，需证明该职位无合格美国工人，过程复杂。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for property managers?**
答：Entry-level: $45,000-$60,000; mid-level: $60,000-$85,000; senior: $85,000-$120,000, depending on experience and location.

**问：How can international professionals immigrate to the US through this role?**
答：Typically require H-1B visa, but caps are tight; or L-1 intracompany transfer. Green card requires employer-sponsored PERM, which is challenging.

**问：What is the career progression for property managers?**
答：Start as assistant or leasing agent, advance to property manager, regional manager, and then portfolio management or VP.
