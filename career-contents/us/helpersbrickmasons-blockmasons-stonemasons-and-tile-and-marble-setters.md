# Helpers--Brickmasons, Blockmasons, Stonemasons, and Tile and Marble Setters（Helpers--Brickmasons, Blockmasons, Stonemasons, and Tile and Marble Setters）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-3011 – Helpers--Brickmasons, Blockmasons, Stonemasons, and Tile and Marble Setters。**

Assist brickmasons, blockmasons, stonemasons, and tile setters by performing less skilled duties such as supplying materials and cleaning work areas.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Workers） | 临时非农业工人签证，适用于季节性建筑项目，但需雇主担保且配额紧张。 |
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常不适用于助手岗位，因要求至少本科学位；极少使用。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary for helpers of brickmasons?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage in 2022 was about $37,000; entry-level around $29,000; experienced up to $55,000.

**问：Can foreigners get a work visa to become a helper for brickmasons?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation generally doesn't qualify for H-1B; H-2B is temporary with limited quotas and not a direct path to green card.
