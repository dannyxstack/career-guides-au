# Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products（Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：41-4011 – Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products。**

Sell technical and scientific products for wholesalers or manufacturers, requiring knowledge in biology, engineering, chemistry, or electronics, typically with at least 2 years of postsecondary education.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income level for this occupation?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, median annual wage is about $83,000, with top 10% earning over $140,000. Income often includes commissions, with potential for high earnings.

**问：How can non-US citizens immigrate for this career?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa (requires bachelor's degree) and EB-2/EB-3 green card. Intracompany transferees can use L-1 visa. H-1B has quota and lottery.

**问：What education is required for this occupation?**
答：Typically an associate degree, but employers prefer a bachelor's in a technical field like biology, chemistry, engineering, or business. Technical knowledge is crucial.
