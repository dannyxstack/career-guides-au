# Industrial Engineering Technologists and Technicians（Industrial Engineering Technologists and Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：17-3026 – Industrial Engineering Technologists and Technicians。**

Apply engineering theory and principles to problems of industrial layout or manufacturing production, usually under direction of engineering staff; perform time and motion studies to establish standard production rates or improve efficiency.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 需学士学位或同等经验，雇主担保，抽签制。 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference） | 适用于拥有高级学位或特殊能力的专业人士，需PERM劳工证。 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 适用于技术工人、专业人员，需PERM劳工证。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Permanent Residence via PERM） | 雇主赞助的永久绿卡流程，包括劳工证测试。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for industrial engineering technologists and technicians?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, the median annual wage is about $60,000, with a range from $48,000 to $100,000 depending on experience and location.

**问：How can industrial engineering technicians immigrate to the US?**
答：Common path is H-1B visa requiring employer sponsorship and a bachelor's degree or equivalent. Then apply for EB-2 or EB-3 green card with PERM labor certification.
