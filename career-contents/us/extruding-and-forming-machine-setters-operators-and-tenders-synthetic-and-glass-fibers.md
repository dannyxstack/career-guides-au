## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Junior operators can advance to senior operators or team leaders, with some transitioning to quality control or equipment maintenance; requires experience and learning automation systems.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Monitor extruder parameters such as temperature and pressure, and manually adjust
- Regularly record production data to generate reports.
- Start and stop machines for material change or cleaning
- Visually inspect silk thread quality and identify surface defects.
- Change molds and adjust extrusion rates according to production specifications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI predictive maintenance systems to analyze equipment operational data and schedule repairs in advance
- Using machine vision systems to detect fiber defects in real-time and automatically adjust parameters
- Optimizing extrusion process formulations and mold design through digital twin simulation
- Uses an AI scheduling system to coordinate production plans and material supply for multiple extruders.
- Uses augmented reality (AR) to assist in equipment disassembly and fault-finding training

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment and manual repair for unexpected equipment failures and material abnormalities
- Trial extrusion process parameter adjustments for new materials and processes
- Coordination and team management with upstream and downstream departments (e.g., spinning, post-processing)
- Human Responsibility and Decision-Making in Safety and Environmental Compliance Checks
- Experience with operating and maintaining old equipment or systems without AI assistance

**Skills to build:**

- Basic industrial automation and PLC programming knowledge
- Data analysis and statistical process control (SPC) skills
- Skills in using AI operations platforms (e.g., Predix)
- Robot collaboration and maintenance basics
- Digital twin simulation software operation (e.g., Ansys, Simulink)
- Quality and safety management systems (ISO 9001, Six Sigma)

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining employment
- Automation impact
- Specialty fiber demand
- Manufacturing shift
