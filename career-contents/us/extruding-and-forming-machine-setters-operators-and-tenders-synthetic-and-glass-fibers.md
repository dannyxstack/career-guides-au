# Extruding and Forming Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Synthetic and Glass Fibers（Extruding and Forming Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Synthetic and Glass Fibers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-6091 – Extruding and Forming Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Synthetic and Glass Fibers。**

Set up, operate, or tend machines that extrude and form continuous filaments from synthetic materials such as liquid polymer, rayon, and fiberglass.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
