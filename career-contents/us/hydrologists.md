# Hydrologists（Hydrologists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：19-2043 – Hydrologists。**

Study the distribution, circulation, and physical properties of underground and surface waters; analyze precipitation, infiltration, and water flow to support water resource management, environmental protection, and engineering projects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 常见工作签证，需雇主担保，本科学位+专业对口 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference） | 需硕士以上学位或杰出能力，通过PERM劳工认证 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 适用于学士学位或熟练工人，通过PERM |
| Subclass TN（TN NAFTA Professional） | 适用于加拿大或墨西哥公民，水文地质学相关专业 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for hydrologists?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, median annual wage is about $84,000, ranging from $55,000 to $130,000 depending on experience, employer, and location.

**问：What are the immigration pathways for hydrologists to the US?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa (employer-sponsored), EB-2/EB-3 green cards (via PERM), and TN visa for Canadian/Mexican citizens.

**问：What is the job outlook for hydrologists?**
答：BLS projects 4% growth from 2023-2033, with steady opportunities due to climate change and water management needs, but moderate competition.
