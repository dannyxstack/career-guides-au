# Pharmacy Technicians（Pharmacy Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-2052 – Pharmacy Technicians。**

Prepare medications under the direction of a pharmacist, including measuring, mixing, counting, labeling, and recording dosages.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（Specialty Occupation） | 通常不需要学士学位，H-1B不常见。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Immigration） | 雇主担保绿卡难度大，因职业不需高等学历。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for pharmacy technicians?**
答：Median annual salary around $36,000, entry-level about $30,000, senior can exceed $50,000.

**问：Is it easy for pharmacy technicians to immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree, making H-1B sponsorship uncommon and employer-sponsored green cards challenging.

**问：What certifications are required?**
答：Most states require passing the PTCB exam for CPhT certification; some states also require state licensure.
