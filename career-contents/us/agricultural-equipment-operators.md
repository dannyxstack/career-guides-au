# Agricultural Equipment Operators（Agricultural Equipment Operators）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：45-2091 – Agricultural Equipment Operators。**

Operate and control farm machinery such as tractors and harvesters for tilling, planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在美国独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2A（Temporary Agricultural Workers） | 季节性农业工人签证，雇主需证明本地劳动力不足 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card） | 极少情况，需雇主担保并进行劳工证申请，但农业操作员通常不符合高技能要求 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★½）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for agricultural equipment operators?**
答：The median annual wage in the US is about $37,000, with entry-level around $30,000 and experienced operators earning up to $50,000+.

**问：Can agricultural equipment operators immigrate to the US?**
答：Primarily through H-2A seasonal agricultural worker visa, which is non-immigrant. Green card paths are very limited, usually requiring EB-3 but rarely applicable for this occupation.

**问：What are the advancement opportunities?**
答：Operators can advance to crew leader, farm manager, or equipment mechanic. Experienced workers may move into agricultural sales or technical services.
