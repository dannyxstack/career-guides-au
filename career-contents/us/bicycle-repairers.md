# Bicycle Repairers（Bicycle Repairers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：49-3091 – Bicycle Repairers。**

Bicycle Repairers diagnose, repair, and maintain bicycles, including replacing parts, adjusting brakes and gears, and fixing tires. They typically work in bike shops or sporting goods stores.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常不符合H-1B要求，因为不需要学士学位；特殊情况下如赛事团队可尝试，但极难 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-3)） | 技术工人类别，但自行车修理通常不被视为需要学士学位或特殊技能，移民局认定困难 |
| Subclass O-1（O-1 Extraordinary Ability） | 仅适用于极具知名度的自行车赛事技师，几乎不可能 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁不适合学Bicycle Repairers？**

- 不喜欢体力劳动或重复性工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bicycle repairers in the US?**
答：According to BLS data, the median annual wage for bicycle repairers in the US was about $32,000 in 2023, with entry-level positions around $25,000 and senior mechanics over $50,000.

**问：Can bicycle repairers immigrate to the US through employment-based visas?**
答：It is extremely difficult. The occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree, making it ineligible for H-1B or EB-2/EB-3 green cards. Employers would need to prove a labor shortage via PERM, but the Department of Labor rarely certifies such roles as professional positions.

**问：How to become a senior bicycle repairer?**
答：Typically requires 3-5 years of experience, expertise in high-end components (e-bikes, carbon frames), manufacturer certifications, and potential to become a supervisor or shop owner.
