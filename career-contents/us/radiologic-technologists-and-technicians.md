# Radiologic Technologists and Technicians（Radiologic Technologists and Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-2034 – Radiologic Technologists and Technicians。**

Radiologic technologists take x-rays and CAT scans or administer nonradioactive materials into patients' bloodstream for diagnostic purposes. Excludes sonographers and MRI technologists.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for radiologic technologists?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage is about $65,000 in 2023, entry-level around $50,000, and experienced up to $95,000. Salary varies by location, experience, and specialization.

**问：How can radiologic technologists immigrate to the US?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa (employer sponsorship) and EB-3 green card (PERM labor certification). Canadian/Mexican citizens can use TN status. Requires US-accredited education and state license.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities?**
答：Advance to CT/MRI technologist, radiation therapist, imaging supervisor, or educator. Some become radiologist assistants or sonographers with additional education.
