# Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners（Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：39-3012 – Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners。**

Manage wagers on races and sporting events, operate games like keno and bingo, verify and pay out winning tickets.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
