## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can advance from runner to pit supervisor or gaming manager, but advancement is limited.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually enter betting information into the system
- Manually calculate payouts and verify winning lottery tickets
- Handling cash transactions and giving change
- Generate simple betting reports and statistics
- Basic customer inquiries (e.g., explaining rules).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use predictive models to analyze odds and betting trends
- Optimise customer betting suggestions using AI tools
- Automated compliance checks and abnormal transaction alerts
- Using data visualization to present betting data
- multilingual instant support for online betting platforms

**Human moat:**

- Complex dispute handling and customer service
- Security monitoring and problem gambling identification
- On-site decision-making for unexpected situations at events
- Compliance communication with casino management
- Personalized service for high-stakes betting

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical modeling
- Basic Python/SQL programming
- Risk management and compliance knowledge
- Customer relationship management software operation
- Basic machine learning concepts
- Multilingual communication skills.

**Key growth areas:**

- Gambling
- Sports Betting
- Casino Operations
- Regulated Gaming
