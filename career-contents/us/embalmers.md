# Embalmers（Embalmers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：39-4011 – Embalmers。**

Prepare deceased bodies for burial or cremation, including embalming, cosmetics, and dressing, ensuring legal compliance and dignified presentation for viewings.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for embalmers?**
答：Median annual wage around $50,000; entry-level $32,000-$45,000; experienced up to $90,000.

**问：Can international workers obtain a US work visa as an embalmer?**
答：Extremely difficult. H-1B rarely granted, employers seldom sponsor green cards.

**问：What steps are required to become a licensed embalmer?**
答：Complete an ABFSE-accredited program (usually 2 years), pass the National Board Exam (NBE), finish 1-3 years of apprenticeship, and obtain state license.
