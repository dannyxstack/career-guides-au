# Switchboard Operators, Including Answering Service（Switchboard Operators, Including Answering Service）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-2011 – Switchboard Operators, Including Answering Service。**

Operate telephone switchboards or business systems to relay incoming, outgoing, and interoffice calls; supply information and record messages.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
