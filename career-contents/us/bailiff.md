# Bailiffs — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 33-3011 (SOC)**

Bailiffs maintain courtroom order, ensuring legal proceedings are safe and orderly. They escort prisoners, manage juries, enforce court orders, and provide security during trials.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Bailiffs usually start at entry-level positions, gain experience, and can advance to senior bailiff or court security supervisor. Some transition to legal or law enforcement fields, such as becoming a marshal or court administrator.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Monitor court security footage and log unusual events
- Manage prisoner transfer schedules and documentation
- Verifying visitor identity and access (e.g., automated security screening systems replace)
- Process filing and distribution of court orders
- Reports jury management information to judges

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI facial recognition and anomaly behavior detection for early threat warning
- Optimize court security deployment by analyzing historical data with AI
- AI-assisted real-time multilingual court communication
- Use intelligent scheduling systems to efficiently manage prisoner transport
- AI-generated court event log summaries for subsequent review

**Human moat:**

- Use reasonable force during sudden violence or loss of emotional control.
- Understanding of regulations and courtroom procedures, and on-the-spot judgment.
- Communication and psychological support with high-pressure subjects (e.g., defendants, victims)
- Decision-making ability to independently direct crowds during emergency evacuations
- Undertakes the statutory responsibility and moral obligation of escorting prisoners

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis (e.g., interpreting monitoring data)

- Conflict de-escalation and crisis negotiation training
- Court technology tools (electronic filing and scheduling platforms)

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bailiff?**
A: According to the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, median annual salary for bailiffs is about $47,000, entry-level around $35,000, senior up to $65,000.

**Q: Are there skilled migration pathways for a bailiff?**
A: Bailiffs generally have no skilled migration pathway because the occupation does not require a bachelor's degree, and employers (government agencies) rarely provide sponsorship. Common visas like H-1B and EB-3 are not applicable.

**Q: What certification is needed to become a bailiff?**
A: Usually requires completion of a court or law enforcement agency-provided bailiff training course, and may require firearm certification. Specific requirements vary by state.
