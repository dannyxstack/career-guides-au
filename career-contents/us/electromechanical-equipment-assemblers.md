# Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers（Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-2023 – Electromechanical Equipment Assemblers。**

Assemble or modify electromechanical equipment such as servomechanisms, gyros, dynamometers, magnetic drums, tape drives, brakes, control linkage, actuators, and appliances.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for electromechanical equipment assemblers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about $28,000-$35,000, mid-level $35,000-$45,000, and senior up to $55,000. Pay varies by experience, location, and industry.

**问：Can electromechanical equipment assemblers immigrate to the US via H-1B visa?**
答：Yes, but it typically requires at least a bachelor's degree, whereas this occupation often requires only a high school diploma. EB-3 green card through PERM is a more common path.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities in this occupation?**
答：Yes, can advance to senior assembler, production supervisor, or quality control inspector; further education can lead to technician or engineer roles.
