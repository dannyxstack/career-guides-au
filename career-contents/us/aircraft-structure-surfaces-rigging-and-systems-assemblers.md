# Aircraft Structure, Surfaces, Rigging, and Systems Assemblers（Aircraft Structure, Surfaces, Rigging, and Systems Assemblers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-2011 – Aircraft Structure, Surfaces, Rigging, and Systems Assemblers。**

Assemble, fit, fasten, and install parts of airplanes, space vehicles, or missiles, such as tails, wings, fuselage, bulkheads, landing gear, rigging, and control equipment.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for aircraft assemblers in the US?**

**问：Which visa can aircraft assemblers use to immigrate to the US?**
答：Typically EB-3 green card through employer-sponsored PERM labor certification. H-1B is rare due to educational requirements.

**问：Do aircraft assemblers need certification?**
答：No mandatory certification, but FAA A&P license improves job prospects and pay.
