# Cooks, Restaurant（Cooks, Restaurant）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：35-2014 – Cooks, Restaurant。**

Prepare, season, and cook dishes such as soups, meats, vegetables, or desserts in restaurants. May order supplies, keep records, price menu items, or plan menus.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（Temporary Non-Agricultural Workers） | 季节性厨师可申请，但需雇主担保且名额有限 |
| Subclass O-1B（Extraordinary Ability in Arts） | 杰出厨师（如获奖或知名餐厅主厨）可申请，但门槛极高 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for cooks in restaurants?**
答：Median annual wage is around $35,000, with entry-level at $27,000 and experienced chefs earning over $60,000, varying by location, restaurant type, and experience.

**问：Can cooks immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Regular cooks rarely qualify for H-1B; exceptional chefs may use O-1; seasonal cooks can use H-2B. Permanent residency pathways are very limited.

**问：What key skills are needed to become a cook?**
答：Knife skills, cooking techniques, time management, teamwork, creativity, and food safety knowledge (ServSafe certification) are essential.
