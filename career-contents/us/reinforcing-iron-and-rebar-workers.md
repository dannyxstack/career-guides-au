# Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers（Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2171 – Reinforcing Iron and Rebar Workers。**

Position and secure steel bars or mesh in concrete forms to reinforce concrete structures using fasteners, rod-bending machines, blowtorches, and hand tools.
