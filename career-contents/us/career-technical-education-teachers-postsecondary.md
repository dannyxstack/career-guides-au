# Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary（Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-1194 – Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary。**

Teach vocational courses in fields such as construction, mechanics/repair, manufacturing, transportation, or cosmetology at postsecondary institutions, primarily for students who have completed or left high school, providing occupational training below the bachelor's level.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for career/technical education teachers?**
答：Annual salary ranges from $45,000 to $95,000, depending on experience, location, and institution type; public community colleges have transparent pay scales, while private schools may offer bonuses.

**问：What are the main visa routes for career/technical education teachers to immigrate to the US?**
答：Common routes include H-1B work visa (requires bachelor's degree and employer sponsorship) followed by EB-2 or EB-3 green card, but H-1B has a cap and requires lottery.

**问：What is the future demand for this occupation?**
答：Demand is stable with growth at average; vocational education is government-supported, but total positions are limited, leading to moderate competition.
