# Brokerage Clerks（Brokerage Clerks）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-4011 – Brokerage Clerks。**

Perform duties related to the purchase, sale, or holding of securities, including writing orders, computing taxes, verifying transactions, and maintaining records.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 仅限需要学士学位的岗位，但该职业通常不满足专业职位要求。 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Worker） | 需PERM劳工证，但雇主支持难度大。 |
| Subclass L-1（L-1 Intracompany Transferee） | 仅限跨国公司内部调派，机会有限。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a brokerage clerk?**
答：Entry-level: $35,000-$45,000; Mid-level: $45,000-$60,000; Senior: $60,000-$80,000. Bonuses included but lower than investment bankers.

**问：Are there immigration opportunities for brokerage clerks?**
答：Very limited. Employers must prove no US workers qualify, and the occupation typically doesn't require a bachelor's degree, making H-1B and green card sponsorship difficult.

**问：How to advance to trader or investment advisor?**
答：Obtain Series 7 and 63 licenses, gain experience, then transfer internally or switch jobs. Some firms offer training programs.
