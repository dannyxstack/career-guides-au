# Brokerage Clerks — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 43-4011 (SOC)**

Execute duties including securities trading, transfer tax calculation, stock trade verification, securities settlement, share price fluctuation tracking, equity calculation, dividend distribution, and trade holding record keeping.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Junior roles can advance to senior clerical or trader positions, with some transitioning to compliance or investment advisor roles. Series licenses (e.g., Series 7) required to expand career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Entry and verification of stock trading orders
- Automatic calculation of transfer tax and stamp duty
- Automatic updates of holdings data and dividend distributions.
- Generation of standard-format trade confirmations and settlement documents
- Batch processing of stock price fluctuation tracking and equity calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use anomaly detection AI to quickly identify transaction errors and compliance risks
- Efficiently generate customised analysis reports by querying databases in natural language
- Use predictive models to optimize trade clearing time windows and liquidity management
- Use RPA+AI to handle cross-system data synchronization, reducing manual coordination costs.

**Human moat:**

- On-the-spot judgment for handling non-standard, exceptional transactions, or out-of-system instructions
- Provide context-based explanations during regulatory interviews and audit investigations
- Maintain key client relationships, understand their trading intentions and provide human service
- Design and monitor compliance boundaries for AI processes, taking ultimate responsibility

**Skills to build:**

- Automate trading data processing using Python or SQL
- Basic development of RPA tools (UiPath / Automation Anywhere)
- Financial informatics and abnormal transaction detection methodology
- Integration of AI tools (e.g., intelligent document processing) in back-office operations
- Knowledge of financial regulatory compliance (SEC/FINRA rules)
- Process Improvement and Project Management (Lean / Six Sigma)

**Key growth areas:**

- Slow growth
- Automation impact
- Compliance demand
- Fintech transformation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for securities broker clerks?**
A: Entry-level annual salary is about USD 35,000-45,000, mid-level USD 45,000-60,000, senior USD 60,000-80,000. Income is affected by bonuses and is typically lower than investment bankers.

**Q: Is stockbroker clerk eligible for skilled migration?**
A: Very few opportunities. Employers must prove the position cannot be filled by a US worker, and the occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree, making H-1B and green card applications difficult.

**Q: How to advance to trader or investment advisor?**
A: Requires obtaining Series 7 and Series 63 licenses, then internal transfer or job hopping after gaining experience. Some companies offer training programs.
