# Graphic Designers（Graphic Designers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：27-1024 – Graphic Designers。**

Design or create graphics to meet commercial or promotional needs, such as packaging, displays, or logos, using various mediums for artistic or decorative effects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for graphic designers?**
答：Entry-level designers earn around $40,000-$55,000, mid-level $55,000-$75,000, and senior designers $75,000-$100,000 annually, depending on location, industry, and company size.

**问：How can foreign graphic designers immigrate to the US?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa (requires bachelor's degree, lottery-based) and EB-2/EB-3 green cards (employer-sponsored with PERM). O-1 visa is for extraordinary designers.

**问：Will AI replace graphic designers?**
答：AI tools can generate graphics, but designers' creativity, strategic thinking, and client communication are hard to replace. Designers should learn AI tools to enhance efficiency, but full replacement is unlikely.
