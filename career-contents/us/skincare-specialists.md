# Skincare Specialists（Skincare Specialists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：39-5094 – Skincare Specialists。**

Provide skincare treatments to face and body, including hair removal and chemical peels, to enhance appearance. Work in spas, salons, and medical clinics.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常不适用，因为该职业通常不需要学士学位 |
| Subclass O-1（O-1 Extraordinary Ability） | 仅适用于极少数国际知名的护肤专家 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card） | 极难通过PERM获得，因为非技术移民路径 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for skincare specialists?**
答：According to BLS, the average annual wage is about $37,000, but income varies greatly with tips, commissions, and experience.

**问：Can skincare specialists immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation usually does not meet H-1B requirements as a bachelor's degree is not needed. A few exceptional experts may qualify for O-1 visa but the bar is extremely high.

**问：What education is needed to become a skincare specialist?**
答：Typically, completion of a state-approved cosmetology program (6-12 months) and obtaining a state license. Some community colleges offer associate degrees.
