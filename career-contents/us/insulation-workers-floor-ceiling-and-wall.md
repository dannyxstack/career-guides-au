# Insulation Workers, Floor, Ceiling, and Wall（Insulation Workers, Floor, Ceiling, and Wall）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2131 – Insulation Workers, Floor, Ceiling, and Wall。**

Line and cover structures with insulating materials such as batt, roll, or blown insulation to improve energy efficiency and soundproofing.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for insulation workers?**
答：The median annual wage for insulation workers is about $48,000, with apprentices starting around $32,000 and experienced workers up to $68,000. Union positions may pay higher.

**问：Can insulation workers immigrate to the US through employment?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation typically does not qualify for H-1B or EB-2/EB-3 green cards unless exceptionally skilled or managerial. H-2B seasonal visa may apply but requires strong employer sponsorship.

**问：Is there career advancement for insulation workers?**
答：Yes. Apprentices become journeymen, then advance to foremen, supervisors, or inspectors. Some pursue training to become energy auditors or start their own business.
