# Philosophy and Religion Teachers, Postsecondary（Philosophy and Religion Teachers, Postsecondary）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-1126 – Philosophy and Religion Teachers, Postsecondary。**

Teach courses in philosophy, religion, and theology at the postsecondary level, often combining teaching with research.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 适用于大学讲师，但需抽签且配额紧张 |
| Subclass EB-2（EB-2 Advanced Degree） | 要求博士学位，需PERM劳工证，流程较长 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a philosophy professor?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for philosophy and religion teachers was about $86,000 in 2023, with entry-level around $60,000 and senior up to $140,000.

**问：Can foreign scholars teach philosophy in the US on work visas?**
答：Extremely difficult. Universities may sponsor H-1B visas but face lottery odds. Green cards require PERM labor certification, and proving no qualified US workers is challenging due to job market saturation.

**问：What are alternative career paths for philosophy PhDs?**
答：Some move into academic administration, publishing, non-profit research, or legal consulting, but most still pursue teaching positions.
