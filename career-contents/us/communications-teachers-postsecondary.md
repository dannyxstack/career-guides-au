# Communications Teachers, Postsecondary（Communications Teachers, Postsecondary）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-1122 – Communications Teachers, Postsecondary。**

Teach courses in communications, including organizational communication, public relations, broadcast journalism, and journalism at colleges or universities. Combine teaching with research in many cases.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在美国独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（Specialty Occupations） | 高校可申请H-1B，不受名额限制（cap-exempt），但需证明职位专业性 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference） | 通过PERM或国家利益豁免（NIW）申请绿卡，需博士学历和杰出研究 |
| Subclass O-1（Extraordinary Ability） | 少数杰出教授可申请，需国际认可成就 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

- 热爱教学和学术研究，能承受终身教职压力

- 无法接受低薪和频繁流动的兼职现状

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for communications professors?**
答：Entry-level assistant professors earn $55k-$75k, associate professors $65k-$95k, full professors $85k-$150k. Part-time adjuncts earn much less, often per course.

**问：How can foreign communications professors immigrate to the US?**
答：Universities can file cap-exempt H-1B, then pursue EB-2 or EB-1 green cards. PhD and research publications are required. NIW is an option.

**问：What are career paths for communications professors?**
答：Typical path: assistant professor to tenured associate then full professor. Alternatives: academic administration, research institutes, corporate communication. But tenure-track positions are shrinking.
