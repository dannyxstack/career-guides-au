## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bus and truck mechanics in the US?**
答：According to BLS OEWS, the median annual wage in 2023 is about $55,000, ranging from $35,000 to $95,000 depending on experience, certifications, and location.

**问：How can a foreign mechanic immigrate to the US via EB-3 green card?**
答：Requires a US employer to offer a full-time job, obtain PERM labor certification proving no qualified US workers, then file I-140 petition. Wait times vary. No bachelor's degree needed, but at least 2 years experience or training required.

**问：Is there career advancement for this occupation?**
答：Yes. From entry-level mechanic to lead technician, shop foreman, service manager, or owning a repair shop. ASE certification and manufacturer training enhance advancement.
