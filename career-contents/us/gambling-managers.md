## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of a Casino Manager in the US?**
A: According to BLS data, the 2023 median annual salary is about $74,000, entry-level around $45,000, and experienced up to $150,000, depending on casino size and location.

**Q: Is the path to immigrate to the US as a casino manager feasible?**
A: Very difficult. H-1B is usually not considered a professional occupation; L-1 requires intracompany transfer; EB-3 green card requires employer sponsorship and challenging PERM labor certification. Few employers support it.

**Q: What core skills does a casino manager need?**
A: Requires people management, customer service, financial analysis, compliance knowledge, and ability to handle emergencies. Math and statistics skills are also important.
