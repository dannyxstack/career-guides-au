# Bicycle Repairers — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 49-3091 (SOC)**

Bicycle repairers diagnose, repair, and maintain various types of bicycles, including replacing parts, adjusting brakes and gears, and patching tires. They usually work in bicycle shops or sporting goods stores.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career path: start as apprentice or junior repairer, gain experience to advance to senior technician or store manager, some may open own repair shop or become bicycle race technician.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Customer inquiry answering (AI chatbot)
- Inventory management and parts ordering (automated systems)
- Simple fault diagnosis (AI-assisted identification of common issues)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex fault diagnosis: AI analyzes historical data to provide repair suggestions
- Inventory optimization: AI predicts parts demand, reducing shortages
- Customer service: AI schedules appointments and follow-ups
- Training: AR/VR simulated repair scenarios
- Quality control: AI detects vehicle performance after repairs

**Human moat:**

- Fine manual operations (e.g., adjusting spoke tension)
- On-site fault diagnosis (non-standardized problems)
- Customer trust and communication
- Creative modification and customization

**Skills to build:**

- Electronic variable speed system repair
- Knowledge of electric bicycle systems (battery, motor)
- Use of basic AI diagnostic tools
- Customer relationship management software
- Inventory management software
- Sustainable material repair

**Key growth areas:**

- Cycling Popularity
- Green Transportation
- E-bike Maintenance
- Bike Share Programs

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bicycle repairer in the United States?**
A: According to BLS data, the 2023 US median annual salary for bicycle repairers was about $32,000, entry-level around $25,000, and senior technicians over $50,000.

**Q: Can a bicycle repairman migrate to the US through skilled migration?**
A: Almost impossible. The occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree, making it ineligible for H-1B or EB-2/EB-3 skilled immigration standards. Employers need to prove via PERM that they cannot find local workers, but the Department of Labor usually does not certify such positions as professional.

**Q: How to become a senior bicycle mechanic?**
A: Typically requires 3-5 years of experience, mastery of high-end component repair (e.g., electric bicycles, carbon fiber frames), manufacturer certification, and can advance to supervisor or open own shop.
