# Fine Artists, Including Painters, Sculptors, and Illustrators（Fine Artists, Including Painters, Sculptors, and Illustrators）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：27-1013 – Fine Artists, Including Painters, Sculptors, and Illustrators。**

Fine artists create original artworks using various media and techniques, expressing personal perspectives and emotions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass O-1（Extraordinary Ability Visa） | 适用于在艺术领域有杰出成就的美术家，需证明国际认可 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-1A / EB-2 NIW)） | 杰出人才（EB-1A）或国家利益豁免（EB-2 NIW）适用于少数顶尖艺术家 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Fine Artists, Including Painters, Sculptors, and Illustrators？**
- 具有强烈自我驱动力和创作热情的独立艺术家
- 愿意面对收入不稳定且能自我营销的人

**谁不适合学Fine Artists, Including Painters, Sculptors, and Illustrators？**
- 追求稳定高收入和福利的人
- 不喜欢孤独工作和自我推广的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average income for fine artists?**
答：Fine artists' incomes vary widely; median around $40,000-$50,000, but many freelancers earn under $30,000 annually.

**问：Can fine artists immigrate to the US via work visa?**
答：Very limited; only top-tier artists can qualify for O-1 visa or EB-1A green card; regular artists face extremely low chances.
