# Brickmasons and Blockmasons（Brickmasons and Blockmasons）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2021 – Brickmasons and Blockmasons。**

Lay and bind building materials such as brick, concrete block, glass block, and terra-cotta with mortar to construct or repair walls, partitions, arches, sewers, and other structures.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |

| Subclass H-1B（Specialty Occupations） | 通常不适用，因砖瓦工不属于专业职业。但若担任项目经理或特殊项目可能有例外。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of brickmasons?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage is around $60,000 in 2023. Entry-level earns about $40,000, experienced over $85,000. Union members typically earn more.

**问：Is there a migration path for brickmasons?**
答：Brickmasons often use H-2B seasonal visa, not H-1B. EB-3 skilled worker green card is possible but requires PERM and long wait. Some regions with shortages may expedite.

**问：What is the career growth for brickmasons?**
答：Advance to foreman, project manager, or independent contractor. Experienced masons can specialize in historic restoration, large commercial projects, or start their own masonry company.
