# Gas Compressor and Gas Pumping Station Operators（Gas Compressor and Gas Pumping Station Operators）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：53-7071 – Gas Compressor and Gas Pumping Station Operators。**

Operate steam-, gas-, electric motor-, or internal combustion-engine driven compressors to transmit, compress, or recover gases like butane, nitrogen, hydrogen, and natural gas. Monitor equipment, maintain pipeline pressure, and perform routine maintenance.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for gas compressor operators?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage is about $62,000 in 2023, entry-level $42,000-$55,000, senior $70,000-$87,000.

**问：Can foreigners become gas compressor operators via work visa?**
答：This occupation typically does not meet H-1B specialty occupation criteria, but EB-3 green card (skilled worker) is possible with employer-sponsored PERM.

**问：What qualifications are needed for green card?**
答：Typically require at least 2 years of relevant experience or training, and employer must demonstrate inability to find U.S. workers.
