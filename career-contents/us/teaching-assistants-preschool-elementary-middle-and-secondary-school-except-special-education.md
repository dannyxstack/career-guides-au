# Teaching Assistants, Preschool, Elementary, Middle, and Secondary School, Except Special Education（Teaching Assistants, Preschool, Elementary, Middle, and Secondary School, Except Special Education）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-9042 – Teaching Assistants, Preschool, Elementary, Middle, and Secondary School, Except Special Education。**

Assist preschool, elementary, middle, and secondary school teachers with instructional tasks such as preparing materials, supervising students, and grading assignments under teacher supervision.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the salary of a teaching assistant enough to support living in the US?**
答：Salaries are low, generally only sufficient for basic living expenses, especially in high-cost areas. Many positions are part-time or hourly.

**问：Is there an opportunity for a teaching assistant to immigrate to the US through work?**
答：Very difficult. The position rarely meets H-1B specialty occupation criteria, and EB-3 requires employer sponsorship, which is uncommon for assistants.
