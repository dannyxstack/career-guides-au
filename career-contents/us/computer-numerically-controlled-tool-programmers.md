# Computer Numerically Controlled Tool Programmers（Computer Numerically Controlled Tool Programmers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-9162 – Computer Numerically Controlled Tool Programmers。**

Develop programs to control machining or processing of materials by automatic machine tools, equipment, or systems. May also set up, operate, or maintain equipment.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for CNC Programmers?**
答：Entry-level: $40,000-$55,000; Mid-level: $55,000-$75,000; Senior: up to $95,000+. Varies by location, experience, and industry.

**问：Is CNC Programmer a good fit for US work visas?**
答：Eligible for H-1B (requires bachelor's) and EB-3 green card (via PERM). However, must prove specialty occupation; employer sponsorship can be challenging.

**问：Will this job be replaced by AI?**
答：Some programming tasks may be automated, but complex part programming, process optimization, and troubleshooting require human judgment. Medium risk in near term.
