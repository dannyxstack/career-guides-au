## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can move up from operator to senior operator or supervisor, or shift to CNC programming or quality control.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically monitor and adjust forging parameters (such as pressure, temperature)
- Perform repetitive hammering and forming operations
- Read blueprints and convert into basic machine instructions
- Recording production data and workpiece quality inspections

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Reduce downtime through AI predictive maintenance alerts
- Using digital twins to optimize mold design and process parameters
- Integrate machine vision for high-precision quality inspection and feedback
- Remote fault diagnosis and repair using AR assistance systems

**Human moat:**

- Complex fault diagnosis and repair skills
- Non-standard mold debugging and improvement skills
- Safety compliance and emergency incident handling
- Multi-process coordination and production process optimization

**Skills to build:**

- Basic programming (PLC, Python) and automation system operation
- Digital twin or simulation software (e.g., Simufact Forming)
- Industrial robot maintenance and debugging
- Data analysis and statistical process control
- Introduction to additive manufacturing (3D printed molds)

**Key growth areas:**

- Manufacturing
- Automation
- Precision Forging
- Metal Fabrication
