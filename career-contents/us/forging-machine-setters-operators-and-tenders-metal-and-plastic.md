# Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic（Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-4022 – Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic。**

Set up, operate, or tend forging machines to taper, shape, or form metal or plastic parts.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
