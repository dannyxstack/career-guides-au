# Packaging and Filling Machine Operators and Tenders（Packaging and Filling Machine Operators and Tenders）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-9111 – Packaging and Filling Machine Operators and Tenders。**

Operate or tend machines to prepare industrial or consumer products for storage or shipment, including cannery workers who pack food products.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
