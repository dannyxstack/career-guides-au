## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

From junior technician, can advance to senior technician, process engineer, or production supervisor, requiring experience accumulation and relevant certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Real-time monitoring of process parameters and manual adjustment
- Record and report production data
- Perform routine equipment calibration and checks
- Identify and report common alarms
- Operate wafer transfer and loading systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted fault diagnosis and root cause analysis
- Using AI predictive maintenance to reduce downtime
- Optimize process parameters using AI to improve yield.
- AI simulation training for new process operations
- AI-driven quality anomaly early warning and root cause localization.

**Human moat:**

- Creative repair of complex equipment faults
- Cross-process integration and process improvement
- Collaborate with engineers to solve technical problems
- On-site safety and emergency response for anomalies
- Experience in process innovation and material adaptation

**Skills to build:**

- Python Scripting and Data Analysis
- Statistical process control (SPC) and big data analytics
- AI/Machine Learning basics and applications
- Semiconductor process principles and equipment structure
- Human-machine collaboration and anomaly handling capabilities.
- Project management and communication coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Semiconductor industry growth
- CHIPS Act
- Advanced manufacturing
- Automation
