# Lodging Managers（Lodging Managers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：11-9081 – Lodging Managers。**

Lodging managers plan, direct, or coordinate activities of an organization or department that provides lodging and other accommodations. They oversee hotels, motels, resorts, ensuring guest satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for lodging managers?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for lodging managers in the U.S. was $61,910 in May 2022. Salaries vary by property type, location, and experience; general managers at large luxury hotels can earn over $100,000.

**问：How can foreign nationals become lodging managers in the U.S. through employment immigration?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa (requires bachelor's degree) and EB-3 green card (requires PERM labor certification). Multinational hotel chains can use L-1A visa for executive transfers. Competition is high; gaining experience at luxury properties is advised.

**问：What are the advancement opportunities for lodging managers?**
答：Promotion from assistant manager to general manager, then to multi-property or regional management, training, or revenue management. Large chains offer cross-brand or international transfers.
