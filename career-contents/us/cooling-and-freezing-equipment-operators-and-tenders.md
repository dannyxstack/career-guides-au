# Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders（Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-9193 – Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders。**

Operate or tend equipment such as cooling and freezing units, refrigerators, batch freezers, and freezing tunnels to cool or freeze products, food, blood plasma, and chemicals.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for cooling and freezing equipment operators?**
答：According to BLS, the 2023 average annual salary is about $42,000, with entry-level around $32,000 and experienced up to $60,000, plus overtime.

**问：Are there pathways for skilled migration to the US for this occupation?**
答：This is an operator-level job, generally not eligible for high-skilled immigration. H-2B visas are available but capped and require employer sponsorship; EB-3 green cards require PERM, which is difficult.

**问：Is there career growth potential for this occupation?**
答：Job stability is good, but upward mobility is limited. Training can lead to maintenance or quality control roles; certification may help.
