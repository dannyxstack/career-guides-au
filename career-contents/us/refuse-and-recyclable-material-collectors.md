# Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors（Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：53-7081 – Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors。**

Collect and dump refuse or recyclable materials from containers into truck. May drive truck.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（临时非农业工人签证） | 部分季节性垃圾收集可能申请，但配额紧张且职位非长期 |
| Subclass EB-3（职业移民第三优先） | 理论可行，但实际雇主极少担保，因技术要求低、本地劳动力充足 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of refuse collectors?**
答：Entry-level: $28,000-$35,000; mid-level: $37,000-$45,000; senior/foreman: up to $55,000. Overtime can increase pay.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to the US as refuse collectors?**
答：Extremely difficult. It is not a high-skilled occupation; H-2B visas have tight caps and green card sponsorship is rare.

**问：What are the career advancement paths?**
答：Ways to advance include becoming a driver, foreman, or moving into dispatch, waste facility management, or environmental compliance.
