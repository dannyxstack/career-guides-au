# Air Crew Members（Air Crew Members）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：55-3011 – Air Crew Members。**

Perform in-flight duties to ensure successful completion of combat, reconnaissance, transport, and search and rescue missions. Operate aircraft communications and detection equipment, and conduct preflight, in-flight, and postflight inspections.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 极为罕见，通常不适用于军方职位。仅限极少数民用空勤职位。 |
| Subclass EB-2（EB-2 Advanced Degree or Exceptional Ability） | 非典型路径，需要雇主担保，但军方不提供此类担保。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for air crew members?**
答：Salary depends on rank and years of service, ranging from $35,000-$50,000 for entry-level to $70,000-$90,000 for senior positions, plus allowances for housing and medical.

**问：Can air crew members obtain a US green card through employment-based immigration?**
答：Generally no. The occupation is restricted to US citizens, and immigration pathways are virtually nonexistent.

**问：What are the advancement opportunities for air crew members?**
答：Promotion through military ranks, become flight instructors or crew chiefs. Some transition to civilian aviation roles such as aviation safety inspectors.
