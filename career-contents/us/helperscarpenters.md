# Helpers--Carpenters（Helpers--Carpenters）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-3012 – Helpers--Carpenters。**

Help carpenters by performing duties requiring less skill, such as using, supplying, or holding materials or tools, and cleaning work area and equipment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of Helpers--Carpenters?**
答：The median annual wage is around $37,000, with potential to increase with experience.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to the US as a Helper--Carpenter?**
答：No. This occupation typically does not qualify for H-1B visas; only H-2B temporary work visas may be available, with limited quota and no direct green card path.

**问：What career progression is possible for Helpers--Carpenters?**
答：With experience, helpers can advance to carpenters, lead carpenters, or contractors through additional training.
