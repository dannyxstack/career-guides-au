# Airfield Operations Specialists（Airfield Operations Specialists）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：53-2022 – Airfield Operations Specialists。**

Ensure safe takeoff and landing of commercial and military aircraft by coordinating air traffic control and maintenance, using navigational aids, implementing safety procedures, monitoring flight records, and applying weather information.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for airfield operations specialists?**
答：The median annual salary for airfield operations specialists in the US is around $65,000, with entry-level positions earning $40,000-$55,000 and senior roles up to $75,000-$95,000. Salary varies by airport size, location, and experience.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to the US as an airfield operations specialist?**
答：This occupation rarely offers employer-sponsored immigration. Employers prefer US citizens or permanent residents due to security background checks. H-1B visa applicability is low, and green card (EB-3) sponsorship is difficult.

**问：What education is required to become an airfield operations specialist?**
答：A high school diploma is the minimum, but an associate's or bachelor's degree in aviation management is preferred. Some employers provide on-the-job training, but AAAE certification can enhance competitiveness.
