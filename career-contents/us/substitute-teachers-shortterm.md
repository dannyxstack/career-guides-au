# Substitute Teachers, Short-Term（Substitute Teachers, Short-Term）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-3031 – Substitute Teachers, Short-Term。**

Substitute teachers temporarily replace regular classroom teachers, typically following the regular teacher's lesson plan.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**竞争度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**
**工作强度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 代课教师通常不符合H-1B要求，因为该职位通常不需要学士学位。 |
| Subclass J-1（J-1 Exchange Visitor） | 部分学区通过J-1教师交流项目招聘国际教师，但代课教师较少。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：How are short-term substitute teachers paid?**
答：Paid daily or hourly, typically $100-$150 per day, annual salary varies based on days worked, often between $25,000 and $35,000.

**问：Can internationals immigrate to the US as short-term substitute teachers?**
答：Very difficult. Substitute teachers rarely qualify for H-1B or employer-sponsored green cards. J-1 exchange visitor program may apply but has limitations.

**问：Can short-term substitute teachers become regular teachers?**
答：Yes. By gaining experience and obtaining a teaching license, substitutes can apply for full-time positions. Many districts give priority to internal substitutes.
