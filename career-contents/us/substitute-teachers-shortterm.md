# Substitute Teachers, Short-Term — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 25-3031 (SOC)**

Short-term substitute teachers temporarily replace regular teachers, usually using the original teacher's lesson plans, not responsible for long-term planning.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Substitute teachers can gain teaching experience, obtain a formal teaching license through further study, and then transition to full-time teachers or long-term substitutes. A few may move into school administrative positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Deliver lessons according to original teacher's preset lesson plans (AI can generate and broadcast standardized courses)
- Managing daily classroom discipline and processes (AI monitoring systems + automated attendance)
- Distributing and collecting classroom assignments (replaced by automated learning management systems)
- Record student attendance and simple behavior reports (automatically generated by AI)
- Handling routine administrative communication (e.g., class change notices, information confirmations)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to quickly understand class learning situations and special needs (e.g., Individual Education Plans)
- AI real-time translation helps non-native English speaking students understand content.
- Use AI to generate differentiated practice questions or resources to supplement temporary lesson plans
- Analyze student classroom participation through AI to adjust engagement strategies
- Use AI to assist lesson planning, quickly adapt to different subjects and grade levels

**Human moat:**

- Build rapid trust and psychological safety (especially for anxious students)
- On-site response to emergencies (e.g., student meltdowns, equipment failure)
- Non-verbal observation and personalized care (identifying hidden distress)
- Legal and ethical responsibilities (e.g., mandatory reporting of child protection)
- Complex interpersonal relationship management across classes (collaborating with all school staff)

**Skills to build:**

- AI teaching tools (e.g., automatic question generation, classroom real-time feedback systems)
- Basic classroom management and behavioral intervention methods
- Multimodal communication skills (non-verbal, online/offline hybrid)
- Child Psychology and Trauma-Informed Practice
- Rapid learning ability and adaptability to multiple disciplinary frameworks
- Cultural sensitivity (flexibly respond to students from diverse backgrounds)

**Key growth areas:**

- Teacher Shortage
- Flexible Scheduling
- Per Diem Work
- Substitute Teaching

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is salary calculated for short-term substitute teachers?**
A: Usually paid daily or hourly, daily rate approx. 100-150 USD, annual salary varies by number of work days, mostly between 25,000 and 35,000 USD.

**Q: Can internationals immigrate to the US through short-term substitute teaching jobs?**
A: Very difficult. Substitute teachers typically do not meet the professional occupation requirements for H-1B visas, and employers rarely sponsor green cards. The J-1 exchange visitor program may be applicable but has many restrictions.

**Q: Can short-term substitute teachers become regular teachers?**
A: Yes. By accumulating teaching experience and obtaining a formal teaching license, substitute teachers can apply for full-time positions. Many school districts prioritize internal substitute teachers.
