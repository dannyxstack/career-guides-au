# Tax Preparers — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 13-2082 (SOC)**

Prepares and submits tax returns for individuals or small businesses, ensuring compliance with tax laws. Excludes accountants and auditors.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior tax return preparers can advance to senior preparers, tax supervisors, or tax managers, or gain credentials as enrolled agents or CPAs to expand career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract data from bank statements, W-2s, etc., to fill in tax forms
- Calculates standard deductions, allowances, and other basic tax amounts.
- Automatically checks tax return logic errors based on preset rules
- Generate and submit electronic filing documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted quick search of tax law updates and targeted deductions to improve planning efficiency
- Use AI to analyze client financial data and simulate tax outcomes under different filing strategies
- AI automatically generates draft client communication emails, saving clerical time
- Identify audit risk points through machine learning to strengthen compliance reviews

**Human moat:**

- Interpret ambiguous tax code provisions and make professional judgments
- Communicate complex tax issues with clients, build trust relationships
- Customized planning for non-standard situations (e.g., self-employed, multiple income sources)
- Assume legal responsibility, sign to confirm accuracy of declaration

**Skills to build:**

- Master advanced features of mainstream tax software and AI integration modules
- Learn tax law changes, especially related to digital assets and remote work
- Enhance data analysis and visualization skills, using AI to generate client reports
- Strengthen communication and consulting skills to excel in high-end customer service
- Understand basic machine learning principles to evaluate reliability of AI tool recommendations

**Key growth areas:**

- Tax software adoption
- Seasonal demand
- Tax law complexity
- Small business growth

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for tax preparers?**
A: According to BLS 2023 data, median annual salary is about $46,000; junior level $32,000-$45,000, senior level up to $60,000-$80,000. Income is significantly affected by seasonality and client base.

**Q: Can tax return preparers immigrate to the U.S. via EB-3 green card?**
A: Usually very difficult. This occupation does not require a bachelor's degree and many positions are seasonal, making it hard for employers to prove long-term need. Even PERM applications are easily audited and denied. Consider other professional occupations.

**Q: What is the career progression path for a tax return preparer?**
A: Starting as a junior declarant, you can advance to senior tax declarant, tax supervisor, or tax manager with experience. Obtaining an Enrolled Agent (EA) or Certified Public Accountant (CPA) can expand into tax consultant or accountant roles.
