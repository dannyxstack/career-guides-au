# Tax Preparers（Tax Preparers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：13-2082 – Tax Preparers。**

Prepare and file tax returns for individuals or small businesses, ensuring compliance with tax laws. Excludes Accountants and Auditors.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常不适用，因该职业通常不要求学士学位，难以符合专业职位要求。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Tax Preparers？**
- 对税务感兴趣、注重细节的人
- 善于沟通、能独立工作的人
- 愿意季节性加班（报税季）的人

**谁不适合学Tax Preparers？**
- 不喜欢重复性文书工作的人
- 无法承受压力、不擅长数学的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for tax preparers?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, median annual wage is around $46,000; entry-level $32,000-$45,000, senior $60,000-$80,000. Income is highly seasonal and client-dependent.

**问：Can a tax preparer immigrate via EB-3 green card?**
答：Generally difficult. The occupation typically does not require a bachelor's degree and is often seasonal, making it hard for employers to prove permanent need. PERM applications face high audit risk.

**问：What is the career progression for a tax preparer?**
答：Start as a junior preparer, advance to senior preparer, tax supervisor, or tax manager. Obtaining EA or CPA credentials can lead to roles as tax consultant or accountant.
