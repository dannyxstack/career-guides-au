# Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders（Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-9191 – Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders。**

Operate or tend bonding machines that use adhesives to join materials like plywood, paper, rubber, or plastic for further processing or final products.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for adhesive bonding machine operators?**
答：The average annual salary in the US is about $37,000 as of 2023, varying by location and experience. Entry-level around $30,000, experienced up to $50,000.

**问：Can adhesive bonding machine operators immigrate to the US via H-1B visa?**
答：Typically no, because the job does not require a bachelor's degree, failing H-1B standards. Employer-sponsored EB-3 green card is also extremely difficult, with virtually no skilled migration path.

**问：What is the future outlook for this occupation?**
答：Due to automation and offshoring, employment is projected to decline. Promotion opportunities are limited, and long-term prospects are poor.
