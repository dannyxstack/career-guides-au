# First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 47-1011 (SOC)**

Supervise and coordinate activities of construction or mining workers to ensure projects are completed on time, within budget, and safely. Responsibilities include work allocation, progress tracking, quality control, and site safety management.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Usually promoted from skilled workers; with experience can advance to project manager, construction manager, or company executive. Continuing education in construction management can improve promotion prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Plant health diagnosis report writing (auto-generated by AI image recognition)
- Irrigation system daily monitoring and scheduling (IoT+AI automatic optimization)
- Document filing and compliance records (AI auto-organizes)
- Initial client consultation and quote generation (chatbot + fee model)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Crown structure and root risk assessment (AI-assisted 3D scan analysis)
- Early pest and disease warning (AI analyzing historical and weather data).
- Pruning and removal plan optimization (AI simulates best path and tools).
- Sustainable garden design (AI recommends plant combinations and maintenance plans)

**Human moat:**

- On-site safety decisions for high-risk tree removal
- Complex negotiations and trust-building with clients and municipalities
- Improvising in response to unexpected weather or accidents
- On-site management leading teams through high-intensity physical work

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and image analysis.
- GIS and smart irrigation system configuration
- Use of AI pest and disease diagnosis tools
- Basic data analysis and reporting skills
- Customer Relationship Management and Digital Communication
- Knowledge of sustainable horticulture and ecological restoration

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure investment
- Construction management
- Safety regulations
- Project coordination

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Skilled workers with many years of experience in the construction industry seeking promotion to management
- Possess leadership and communication skills, adept at on-site coordination and safety management
- Able to adapt to outdoor and construction site environments, with strong stress tolerance

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes outdoor work or physical activity
- Not good at handling conflict and people management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a construction supervisor?**
A: According to BLS data, the median annual salary for this occupation in 2023 was approx. USD 74,000, junior around USD 50,000, senior up to USD 110,000.

**Q: How can foreign workers obtain an H-1B visa for this occupation?**
A: A bachelor's degree is required, and job duties must meet professional occupation requirements. Employers must submit an application and enter a lottery.

**Q: Is a university degree required to become a construction supervisor?**
A: Typically requires only high school diploma plus years of experience, but associate degree or certificate can enhance promotion opportunities.
