# First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers（First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-1011 – First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers。**

Directly supervise and coordinate the activities of construction or extraction workers, ensuring projects are completed on time, within budget, and safely. Responsibilities include work assignment, progress tracking, quality control, and site safety management.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for construction supervisors?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for this occupation was about $74,000 in 2023, with entry-level around $50,000 and experienced up to $110,000.

**问：How can foreign workers get an H-1B visa for this occupation?**
答：Requires a bachelor's degree and the job must qualify as a specialty occupation. Employer must file a petition and enter the lottery.

**问：Do I need a college degree to become a construction supervisor?**
答：Typically only a high school diploma plus experience is required, but an associate degree or certificate can improve advancement prospects.
