# Occupational Therapy Aides — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 31-2012 (SOC)**

Perform assigned, selected, or routine tasks under the close supervision of an occupational therapist or assistant, including preparing patients and treatment rooms.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Usually start as an assistant role, gain experience, and can advance to occupational therapy assistant or become an occupational therapist through further education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patient appointment and schedule management
- Therapy room preparation and equipment cleaning records
- Patient basic data entry and file maintenance
- Guidance on basic therapeutic activities (e.g., simple exercise instructions)
- Standardized report generation of treatment progress

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze patient progress data and optimize treatment plans
- Using AI tools to personalize patient training videos and materials
- Quickly access treatment guidelines and resources via AI voice assistants
- Automatically generate treatment summaries and insurance documents using AI
- Uses AI to monitor patient movements in real time and provide immediate feedback.

**Human moat:**

- Building trust and empathetic interaction with patients
- Dealing with sudden emotional or behavioral issues
- Adjust treatment pace based on non-verbal cues
- Ensure patient safety and respond quickly in emergencies
- Personalize treatment equipment to suit special needs

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced operation of electronic health records systems
- Basic data analysis and visualization
- Use of AI therapy assistance tools
- Remote therapy platform operation
- Patient communication and empathy training
- Interdisciplinary collaboration

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Increased rehabilitation needs
- Healthcare expansion
- Rehabilitative services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an occupational therapy assistant?**
A: According to BLS data, the average annual salary in 2023 was about $35,000, entry-level about $28,000, and senior up to $48,000.

**Q: Can an occupational therapy assistant immigrate to the US through skilled migration?**
A: This occupation typically does not meet H-1B requirements (needs a bachelor's degree), and EB-3 green cards are difficult. Recommended as a transitional role, then consider immigration after becoming an occupational therapy assistant or therapist.
