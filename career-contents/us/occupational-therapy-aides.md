# Occupational Therapy Aides（Occupational Therapy Aides）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：31-2012 – Occupational Therapy Aides。**

Under close supervision of an occupational therapist or occupational therapy assistant, perform only delegated, selected, or routine tasks in specific situations, including preparing patient and treatment room.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常需要学士学位，该职业通常达不到要求，很少使用。 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（Employment-Based Green Card (EB-3)） | 需要雇主担保，但由于职业门槛低，很难获批。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Occupational Therapy Aides？**
- 对康复治疗有热情，愿意从基层做起
- 细心、耐心，善于与患者沟通
- 希望在医疗领域快速就业

**谁不适合学Occupational Therapy Aides？**
- 追求高薪和高职业声望
- 不愿意在监督下从事重复性任务

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for occupational therapy aides?**
答：According to BLS, the average annual salary in 2023 is about $35,000, with entry-level around $28,000 and experienced up to $48,000.

**问：Can occupational therapy aides immigrate to the US via skilled migration?**
答：This occupation typically does not meet H-1B requirements (needs bachelor's degree), and EB-3 green card is difficult. It is recommended as a stepping stone; consider transitioning to occupational therapy assistant or therapist for immigration.
