# Child, Family, and School Social Workers（Child, Family, and School Social Workers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：21-1021 – Child, Family, and School Social Workers。**

Provide social services to improve social and psychological functioning of children and families, and maximize family well-being and academic performance. Assist parents, arrange adoptions, find foster homes, and address school issues like teenage pregnancy, misbehavior, and truancy.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 适用于需要学士学位的社会工作职位，但受配额限制，雇主需证明无法找到合格美国工人 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference (EB-2)） | 适用于拥有硕士以上学位或特殊能力的社会工作者，需通过PERM劳工证 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference (EB-3)） | 适用于持有学士学位的社工，需PERM，排期较长 |
| Subclass Green Card (PERM)（PERM Labor Certification） | 通过劳工证程序获得永久居留权，雇主需证明招聘努力 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Child, Family, and School Social Workers？**
- 有同理心和耐心，致力于帮助儿童和家庭
- 具备解决问题和危机干预能力
- 能够承受高压工作环境并处理创伤案例

**谁不适合学Child, Family, and School Social Workers？**
- 难以应对情感挑战和较高心理负担
- 不擅长与多系统（学校、家庭、法律）协调沟通

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of child, family, and school social workers?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage in 2023 was about $54,000. Entry-level salaries range $35,000-$48,000, while senior or clinical roles can reach $65,000-$90,000. Salaries vary by location, experience, and certification.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to the US through this occupation?**
答：It qualifies as a specialty occupation, eligible for H-1B and employment-based green cards (EB-2/EB-3). However, H-1B is subject to cap and low lottery odds, and some employers (e.g., government, schools) may not sponsor. Overall, visa paths exist but require employer sponsorship.
