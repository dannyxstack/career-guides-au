# Architectural and Engineering Managers（Architectural and Engineering Managers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：11-9041 – Architectural and Engineering Managers。**

Architectural and Engineering Managers plan, direct, or coordinate activities in architecture, engineering, or R&D, ensuring projects meet time, budget, and quality goals.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 最常见工作签证，需本科学位，受配额限制 |
| Subclass EB-2（Employment-Based Second Preference） | 需硕士学位或本科学位加5年经验，需PERM劳工证 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 适用于本科学位，需PERM劳工证 |
| Subclass O-1（Extraordinary Ability） | 适用于杰出人才，需证明行业成就 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Architectural and Engineering Managers？**
- 具备工程或建筑背景且对项目管理感兴趣的人
- 擅长统筹协调、沟通和团队领导的人
- 能在高压环境下保持决策清晰的人

**谁不适合学Architectural and Engineering Managers？**
- 不喜欢处理行政和预算事务的人
- 对长时间高强度工作不适应的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the typical salary for Architectural and Engineering Managers?**
答：According to BLS 2023 data, the median annual salary is about $158,000, with a range from $85,000 to over $220,000 depending on experience, industry, and location.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to the US through this occupation?**
答：Common path is H-1B visa, then employer-sponsored EB-2 or EB-3 green card with PERM labor certification. Those with extraordinary achievements may try O-1 visa.

**问：What degree is needed to become an Architectural and Engineering Manager?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in engineering or architecture; many have a master's degree (e.g., MBA or engineering management). Some employers require a Professional Engineer (PE) license.
