# Dining Room and Cafeteria Attendants and Bartender Helpers（Dining Room and Cafeteria Attendants and Bartender Helpers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：35-9011 – Dining Room and Cafeteria Attendants and Bartender Helpers。**

Facilitate food service by cleaning tables, removing dishes, setting tables, and supplying items like water, condiments, and coffee to patrons.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
