# Security Guards — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 33-9032 (SOC)**

Security personnel patrol and monitor premises to prevent theft, violence, or violations; may operate X-ray and metal detection equipment.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can progress from junior security officer to supervisor, monitoring center operator, or security manager; also transition to specialized fields like private investigation or cybersecurity.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- AI video surveillance automatically detects abnormal events and alerts
- Automated visitor registration and identity verification systems replace manual registration
- AI-driven patrol robots replacing fixed-route patrols

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI video analysis assisting real-time threat assessment and decision-making
- AI report generation tools quickly summarize event logs
- AI voice assistant supports emergency response communication and coordination
- AI training simulators improve exercise effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency judgment and crisis intervention ability
- Interpersonal communication and persuasion with clients, visitors, and law enforcement
- Legal compliance and licensing liability (e.g., use-of-force boundaries)
- Risk assessment intuition in complex physical environments

**Skills to build:**

- AI monitoring system operation and data analysis
- Basic cybersecurity knowledge (integration of physical security and IT)
- Emergency communication and coordination software (e.g., Duress system).
- Advanced training in conflict resolution and counter-terrorism awareness.
- Data privacy regulations and compliance (Australian Privacy Act)
- Digital reporting and visualization tools (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- Increased demand for private security
- Technology integration (AI surveillance)
- Event security
- Urban security

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a security officer in the US?**
A: 2023 BLS data shows median annual salary ~$37,920, top 10% can exceed $60,000.

**Q: Can security personnel immigrate to the US via H-1B or green card?**
A: Almost impossible. Security is not considered a professional occupation, H-1B not applicable; unless special skills or through other family-based immigration pathways.

**Q: How can security personnel advance?**
A: After gaining experience, can advance to patrol supervisor, control center supervisor, or security manager; obtaining additional certifications (e.g., CPP) can lead to corporate security consulting.
