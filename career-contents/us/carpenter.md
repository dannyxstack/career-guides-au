# Carpenters — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 47-2031 (SOC)**

Carpenters construct, install and repair wooden structures and fixtures, including framing, stairs, doors, windows, cabinets, etc., an important manual skill occupation in the construction industry.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Carpenters can advance to foreman, project manager, or start their own contracting business. With experience, they can move into building inspection, drafting, or related technical roles, and can increase salary through apprenticeships.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate cutting diagrams and material lists automatically through AI design software, replacing manual measurement and calculation
- CNC equipment replaces manual cutting and repetitive machining
- Prefabricated building components (e.g., modular walls) reduce on-site assembly work
- Laser scanning and BIM technology replace traditional layout and positioning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design tools (e.g., SketchUp + AI plugins) accelerate complex structural design.
- AR/VR technology assists on-site installation guidance, improving accuracy
- Construction management platforms automatically optimize material use and project scheduling.
- AI-driven quality inspection system improves product consistency

**Human moat:**

- Customized installation and adjustment capability in complex on-site environments
- Comprehensive judgment of wood properties, joinery techniques, and structural mechanics
- Skills for handling irregular structures and repairs in old buildings
- Ability to communicate directly with clients and understand personalized needs

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in BIM and 3D modeling software (e.g., Revit, SketchUp)
- Learn CNC programming and digital manufacturing technology
- Familiar with prefabricated building component installation
- Master basic programming (e.g., Python) for automation tool integration
- Improve project management and client communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Construction
- Residential Remodeling
- Infrastructure
- Green Building

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B (H-1B Specialty Occupation Visa) | Carpenters generally do not meet H-1B specialty occupation requirements, as the role typically requires only a high school diploma and on-the-job training, not a bachelor's degree or higher. |
| Subclass EB-3 (Employment-Based Third Preference (EB-3)) | Theoretically can apply for a green card through EB-3 skilled worker category, but requires employer PERM labor certification; carpentry positions are often considered unskilled or semi-skilled with limited quotas, making application difficult. |

**PR friendliness: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on work and physical activity
- People who are detail-oriented and have good spatial awareness
- People willing to work outdoors and on construction sites

**Who is not suited**
- Those who feel uncomfortable with physical labor or working at heights
- Those who hope to advance quickly in an office or through higher education

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for carpenters in the US?**
A: Median annual salary for US carpenters is about $50,000 USD (2023). Entry-level around $32,000-44,000, mid-level $44,000-62,000, senior $62,000-85,000. Self-employed or foremen earn more.

**Q: Can carpenters immigrate to the US with a work visa?**
A: Carpenters generally cannot immigrate via H-1B visas as the occupation does not require a bachelor's degree. EB-3 green cards are theoretically possible, but PERM labor certification is difficult to obtain and quotas are tight, making practical immigration paths extremely limited.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for carpenters?**
A: Carpenters can advance to foreman, project manager, building inspector, or start their own contracting business. They can also transition to related fields like drafting, estimating, or teaching.
