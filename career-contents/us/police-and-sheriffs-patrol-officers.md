# Police and Sheriff’s Patrol Officers（Police and Sheriff’s Patrol Officers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：33-3051 – Police and Sheriff’s Patrol Officers。**

Maintain order and protect life and property by enforcing laws, patrolling areas, directing traffic, issuing summonses, investigating accidents, and apprehending suspects.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 不适用，此职业通常不满足专业职业要求，且雇主几乎不担保 |
| Subclass EB-3（Employment-Based Third Preference） | 极少数情况，需赞助，但政府机构很少为警员提供绿卡担保 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for police officers in the US?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for police and patrol officers was about $74,000 in 2023, with entry-level at $45,000-$65,000 and senior roles over $100,000.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to the US as police officers?**
答：Extremely difficult. Most agencies require US citizenship, and work visas do not apply. Green card sponsorship is rare, typically for those with special skills or already permanent residents.

**问：What is the career path for police officers?**
答：Typically start as patrol officers, then advance to detective, sergeant, lieutenant, and up to deputy chief or chief through exams and experience.
