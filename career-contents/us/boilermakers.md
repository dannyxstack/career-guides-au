# Boilermakers（Boilermakers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2011 – Boilermakers。**

Boilermakers construct, assemble, maintain, and repair stationary steam boilers and boiler house auxiliaries, using hand and power tools to align structures, weld plates, and test vessels.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupation） | 通常不适用，因锅炉制造工不属于专业职业 |
| Subclass EB-3（EB-3 Skilled Workers） | 需PERM劳工证，但缺少学士学位要求，申请困难 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for boilermakers?**
答：The median annual salary for boilermakers in the US is around $65,000, with entry-level earning $45,000-$55,000 and experienced up to $95,000, depending on location and industry.

**问：Can boilermakers apply for US work visas?**
答：Boilermakers generally do not qualify for H-1B specialty occupations, and EB-3 green card path is difficult due to lack of bachelor's degree requirements; immigration prospects are low.

**问：What are the advancement opportunities for boilermakers?**
答：Advancement from apprentice to journeyman, then to foreman or supervisor, or transition to boiler inspection, project management, or self-employment.
