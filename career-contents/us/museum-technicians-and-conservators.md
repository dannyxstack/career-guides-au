# Museum Technicians and Conservators（Museum Technicians and Conservators）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：25-4013 – Museum Technicians and Conservators。**

Restore, maintain, and prepare museum collections for storage, research, or exhibit, including fossils, artifacts, textiles, and documents.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can museum technicians' salaries support living in the US?**
答：Entry-level salaries are low, often requiring supplementary income, especially in big cities.

**问：How can foreign professionals immigrate to the US through this occupation?**
答：Employer-sponsored H-1B or green card is typical, but positions are scarce; a master's degree may help for EB-2.

**问：Is the career path clear for this occupation?**
答：Progression from technician to conservator to department head is clear but requires experience and continuing education.
