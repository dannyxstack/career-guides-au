# Ophthalmic Medical Technicians（Ophthalmic Medical Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：29-2057 – Ophthalmic Medical Technicians。**

Assist ophthalmologists by performing clinical functions such as eye exams, administering medications, and instructing patients on corrective lens care.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for ophthalmic medical technicians?**
答：According to BLS 2023, the median annual wage is around $42,000; experienced or certified technicians can earn over $55,000.

**问：Which visas can ophthalmic medical technicians use to immigrate to the US?**
答：Common paths include H-1B visa (requiring a bachelor's degree) or EB-3 green card, both requiring employer sponsorship and PERM labor certification.
