# Ophthalmic Medical Technicians — career analysis · United States

**Occupation code: 29-2057 (SOC)**

Assist ophthalmologists with clinical examinations, including visual acuity tests, intraocular pressure measurement, refraction, and patient education; operate ophthalmic diagnostic equipment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can progress from junior technician to senior technician or department supervisor, or gain experience and take exams to become an ophthalmic assistant or enter optometry.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated refraction and vision measurement
- Automated execution of standard visual acuity chart test
- AI interpretation of intraocular pressure measurement (non-contact)
- Basic patient history collection and triage
- Automated analysis of routine fundus photography.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of difficult cases (e.g., diabetic retinopathy grading)
- Improve accuracy through AI analysis of OCT and visual field data
- Using AI to optimize patient workflow and appointment scheduling
- AI-driven remote eye screening programs expand service scope
- Automated report generation, saving clerical time

**Human moat:**

- Manual calibration and troubleshooting of complex equipment
- Communicate directly with patients and provide empathetic support
- Clinical judgment: identification of atypical symptoms and collaboration with doctors
- Infection control and sterile operating room procedures
- Cross-team coordination and medical decision support

**Skills to build:**

- Use of ophthalmic AI imaging tools (e.g., IDx-DR)
- Ophthalmic equipment maintenance and advanced operation (e.g., OCT, perimeters)
- Data analysis and basic programming (Python)
- Telemedicine and electronic health record optimization
- Patient education and health coaching
- Clinical research design and ethics compliance

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Vision care demand
- Outpatient care
- Technological advancements

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an ophthalmic medical technician?**
A: According to BLS 2023 data, median annual salary is about $42,000; experienced or those with advanced certifications can reach over $55,000.

**Q: What visas can ophthalmic medical technicians use to immigrate to the US?**
A: Common paths are H-1B work visa (requires bachelor's degree) or EB-3 employment-based green card, requiring employer sponsorship and PERM labor certification.
