# Model Makers, Metal and Plastic（Model Makers, Metal and Plastic）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-4061 – Model Makers, Metal and Plastic。**

Set up and operate machines such as lathes, milling and engraving machines, and jig borers to make working models of metal or plastic objects, including template makers.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Model Makers, Metal and Plastic?**
答：According to BLS data, the median annual wage is about $52,000, with entry-level around $35,000-$45,000 and experienced up to $78,000.

**问：Are there viable immigration pathways to the US for this occupation?**
答：You can apply via H-1B visa or EB-3 green card, but require employer sponsorship and a bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.

**问：How to become a Model Maker, Metal and Plastic?**
答：Typically through an apprenticeship after high school or vocational training, gaining about 4 years of experience, and optionally obtaining NIMS certification.
