# Tool Grinders, Filers, and Sharpeners（Tool Grinders, Filers, and Sharpeners）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：51-4194 – Tool Grinders, Filers, and Sharpeners。**

Operate precision grinding machines, files, and polishing tools to smooth, polish, or sharpen metal objects, ensuring accuracy and sharpness of tools and dies.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常要求本科学位，该职业多为技工，难以满足专业职业要求，极少使用 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for tool grinders in the US?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage in 2024 is about $41,000, with experienced workers earning over $50,000.

**问：Can tool grinders immigrate to the US via H-1B visa?**
答：Very unlikely. H-1B requires a specialty occupation (usually bachelor's degree), while this job typically requires only high school and apprenticeship.

**问：How to become a tool grinder?**
答：Typically through a 3-4 year apprenticeship after high school or studying precision machining at a community college.
