# Paperhangers（Paperhangers）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：47-2142 – Paperhangers。**

Cover interior walls or ceilings with decorative wallpaper or fabric, or attach advertising posters on surfaces such as walls and billboards. May remove old materials or prepare surfaces.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of paperhangers?**
答：According to BLS, the median annual wage for paperhangers was about $46,000 in 2023. Entry-level workers earn $30,000-$40,000, while experienced or self-employed can reach $55,000-$70,000.

**问：Are there immigration opportunities for paperhangers in the US?**
答：Very limited. Paperhangers usually do not qualify for H-1B (requires specialty occupation) or EB-2/EB-3 (requires degree or skilled experience). H-2B temporary visa is possible but employer sponsorship is rare.

**问：What education is needed to become a paperhanger?**
答：Typically only a high school diploma or equivalent. Most skills are learned through 3-4 year apprenticeships or on-the-job training. Some community colleges offer related courses.
