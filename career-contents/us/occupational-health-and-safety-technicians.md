# Occupational Health and Safety Technicians（Occupational Health and Safety Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：19-5012 – Occupational Health and Safety Technicians。**

Occupational Health and Safety Technicians collect data on work environments for analysis by specialists, and implement and evaluate programs to limit chemical, physical, biological, and ergonomic risks to workers.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Occupational Health and Safety Technicians?**
答：According to BLS data, median annual salary in 2023 was around $67,000, entry-level around $42,000, and senior over $95,000. Salary varies by industry, experience, and location.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for immigrating to the US via work visa?**
答：Yes. Typically requires a bachelor's degree, can use H-1B visa or EB-3 green card. Employer sponsorship needed, and H-1B subject to lottery cap.

**问：What education is required to become an Occupational Health and Safety Technician?**
答：Typically an associate or bachelor's degree in occupational safety, industrial hygiene, environmental science, etc. Some community colleges offer 2-year associate programs.
