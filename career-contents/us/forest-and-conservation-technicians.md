# Forest and Conservation Technicians（Forest and Conservation Technicians）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：19-4071 – Forest and Conservation Technicians。**

Assist scientists or foresters in managing forest resources, collect data on soil, water, trees, and participate in fire prevention and ecological restoration.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

|---|---|

| Subclass J-1（J-1 Exchange Visitor） | 实习或培训项目，短期 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary ceiling for forest technicians?**
答：Senior or federal management roles can reach $70,000, but the ceiling is low.

**问：Can foreigners become forest technicians via H-1B?**
答：Very difficult, as employers are mostly government or non-profits with low sponsorship willingness.

**问：Is a bachelor's degree required?**
答：Many entry-level positions accept an associate degree, but advancement requires a bachelor's.
