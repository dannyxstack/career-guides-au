# Hotel, Motel, and Resort Desk Clerks（Hotel, Motel, and Resort Desk Clerks）职业分析 · 美国

**职业代码：43-4081 – Hotel, Motel, and Resort Desk Clerks。**

Accommodate hotel, motel, and resort patrons by registering and assigning rooms, handling reservations, answering phones, and providing customer service.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在美国技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass H-2B（H-2B Temporary Non-Agricultural Worker） | 仅限季节性酒店，雇主需证明劳动力短缺，名额有限 |
| Subclass H-1B（H-1B Specialty Occupations） | 通常不适用，因该职业不要求专业学历 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for hotel front desk clerks in the US?**
答：According to BLS, median annual wage was about $29,000 in 2023, entry-level $23,000-$28,000, senior positions up to $35,000+.

**问：Can hotel front desk clerks get a US work visa?**
答：Generally difficult as it does not qualify for H-1B. Some seasonal hotels may use H-2B, but with limited quotas and complex process.

**问：How to advance from hotel front desk?**
答：Gain experience and move to front desk supervisor, guest relations manager, or operations manager. A hospitality degree can accelerate promotion.
