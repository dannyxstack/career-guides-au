## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around 18,000–25,000 THB per month. With 3–5 years of experience, salaries range from 30,000–45,000 THB. Senior roles in private hospitals or insurance firms can reach 60,000+ THB. Certifications like AHIMA or Thai medical records credentials can boost pay.

**Q: What skills are essential for this role in Thailand, and how is AI impacting the field?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in medical coding (ICD-10-TM), data entry accuracy, and knowledge of Thai health data privacy laws. AI is automating basic coding and transcription, but technicians need to manage AI outputs and focus on complex cases, compliance, and interoperability between healthcare systems.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Thailand?**
A: Technicians review patient records for completeness, assign diagnostic and procedure codes, and ensure confidentiality per Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act. They also update electronic health records, assist with audits, and collaborate with doctors to resolve discrepancies. Work is mostly office-based in hospitals or clinics.
