## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a secondary education teacher in Thailand?**
A: Salaries for public school teachers start around 15,000-20,000 THB per month for new hires, increasing to 30,000-50,000 THB with experience. International schools offer higher pay, ranging from 40,000 to 100,000+ THB monthly. Additional income from tutoring or extracurricular activities is common.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a secondary school teacher in Thailand?**
A: A typical day involves teaching 4-6 classes of 50 minutes each, lesson planning, grading assignments, and attending staff meetings. Teachers also supervise morning assemblies, manage classroom behavior, and participate in school events. Extra duties like club advising or exam proctoring are common.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of secondary education teachers in Thailand?**
A: AI is increasingly used for grading, personalized learning platforms, and administrative tasks. However, teachers remain essential for mentoring and interactive instruction. The Thai government promotes digital literacy, so teachers must adapt by integrating AI tools into lessons, but job security remains strong due to the demand for human guidance.
