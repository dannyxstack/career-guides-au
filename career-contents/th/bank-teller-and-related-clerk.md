## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bank teller in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level bank tellers in Thailand earn around 15,000–20,000 THB per month. With experience, salaries increase to 25,000–35,000 THB. Major banks often provide bonuses, health insurance, and overtime pay, but earnings vary by bank and location.

**Q: What skills are essential for bank tellers in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include cash handling accuracy, customer service, proficiency in Thai and basic English, and familiarity with banking software. Attention to detail and the ability to work under pressure are crucial. Most banks require at least a high school diploma and provide on-the-job training.

**Q: How is AI affecting bank teller jobs in Thailand?**
A: Automation and AI have reduced routine cash transactions as ATMs and digital banking expand. However, tellers remain essential for complex services, relationship building, and fraud detection. Thai banks are retraining tellers for advisory roles, making adaptability key for long-term career prospects.
