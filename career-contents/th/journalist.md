## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for journalists in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level journalists in Thailand earn between 15,000 and 25,000 THB per month. Mid-career professionals with 5-10 years of experience typically earn 30,000 to 50,000 THB monthly. Senior journalists or editors at major outlets can earn 60,000 to 100,000 THB or more, depending on the organization and location.

**Q: What key skills are required to become a journalist in Thailand?**
A: Proficiency in Thai and English is essential. Strong writing, research, and interviewing skills are core. Digital literacy, including social media management, multimedia production, and data analysis, is increasingly important. Knowledge of Thai media laws and ethical standards is also required for credibility and compliance.

**Q: How is AI impacting journalism in Thailand?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks like data entry and basic news writing, allowing journalists to focus on in-depth reporting. However, it also raises concerns about misinformation and job displacement. Thai media outlets are adopting AI for content personalization and fact-checking, but human oversight remains crucial for accuracy and ethical standards.
