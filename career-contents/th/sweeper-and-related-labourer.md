## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for sweepers in Thailand?**
A: In Thailand, sweepers typically earn between 9,000 and 15,000 THB per month, depending on location and employer. Government or large private firms may offer slightly higher pay plus benefits. Entry-level positions start near the minimum wage, while experienced workers in busy urban areas can earn more.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a sweeper in Thailand?**
A: No formal education is required, but basic literacy for following safety instructions is helpful. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and reliability are essential. Knowledge of proper waste disposal and use of cleaning tools is often learned on the job. Some employers may require a valid ID card.

**Q: How is AI affecting sweepers in Thailand?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact on manual sweeping jobs in Thailand. While robotic vacuums exist in malls, most public areas rely on human labour due to cost and complexity. However, automated street sweepers are increasingly used on major roads, reducing demand for manual sweepers in those specific areas.
