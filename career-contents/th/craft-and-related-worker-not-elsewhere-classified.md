## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Thailand?**
A: As of 2024, the average monthly salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Thailand ranges from THB 12,000 to THB 25,000, depending on industry, experience, and location. Workers in Bangkok and industrial areas tend to earn higher wages, while those in rural regions may earn less. Specialized skills often command premium pay.

**Q: What key skills are needed for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, and the ability to use hand and power tools safely. Depending on the specific craft, knowledge of materials (e.g., wood, metal, fabric) and basic math for measurements are important. Many employers value vocational training certificates from Thai institutions, and on-the-job training is common.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Thailand?**
A: AI and automation have a moderate impact on this occupation in Thailand. Repetitive tasks in manufacturing are increasingly automated, but many craft jobs requiring customization, repair, or artistic skill remain in demand. Workers can stay competitive by learning digital tools relevant to their trade and focusing on high-quality, bespoke services that machines cannot replicate.
