## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Thailand?**
A: Income varies widely based on reputation and clientele. Many earn 20,000–50,000 THB per month from personal consultations, with top practitioners in Bangkok earning over 100,000 THB. Astrologers may charge 500–3,000 THB per session, while market stall fortune-tellers often earn less but have steady foot traffic.

**Q: What skills are required to work as an astrologer or fortune-teller in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include deep knowledge of Thai astrology (e.g., lunar calendar, zodiac systems), palmistry, or tarot reading. Strong interpersonal and communication skills are essential for building trust with clients. Many learn through apprenticeship with a master or from family tradition. Business acumen helps in marketing and managing a client base.

**Q: How is AI affecting the work of astrologers and fortune-tellers in Thailand?**
A: AI-powered apps for astrology and fortune-telling are growing in popularity, offering free or low-cost automated readings. However, many Thai clients still value personal, human interaction for cultural and spiritual reasons. Professionals are incorporating digital tools for chart calculations while emphasizing the irreplaceable human intuition and empathy in their services.
