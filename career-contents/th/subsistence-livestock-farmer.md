## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence livestock farmers in Thailand?**
A: Income for subsistence livestock farmers in Thailand varies widely, typically ranging from 3,000 to 8,000 THB per month, depending on the number of animals and local market prices. Most farmers rely on livestock for household consumption and occasional sales at local markets, with earnings supplementing other agricultural activities.

**Q: What are the essential skills for a subsistence livestock farmer in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include basic animal husbandry knowledge, such as feeding, breeding, and health care for cattle, pigs, or chickens. Farmers need practical skills in fencing, water management, and fodder cultivation. Knowledge of natural remedies for common livestock diseases and the ability to handle animals safely are also important.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence livestock farmers in Thailand?**
A: The outlook is stable but limited in growth due to small-scale operations. Demand for locally raised livestock remains steady in rural areas, but younger generations often migrate to cities, reducing the workforce. Government programs supporting small-scale farming may offer some opportunities, but overall, the number of subsistence farmers is expected to decline gradually.
