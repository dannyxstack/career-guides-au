## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Thailand?**
A: The average monthly salary for physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Thailand ranges from THB 15,000 to THB 25,000 for entry-level positions, with experienced staff earning up to THB 35,000. Pay varies by location (Bangkok vs. rural areas) and employer type (public hospital vs. private clinic).

**Q: What qualifications and skills are required to become a physiotherapy technician in Thailand?**
A: A vocational certificate or associate degree in physiotherapy or related field is typically required. Key skills include knowledge of therapeutic exercises, manual therapy techniques, patient communication, and basic medical terminology. Certification from the Thai Physical Therapy Council is often preferred. On-the-job training is common.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the work of physiotherapy assistants in Thailand?**
A: AI is mainly used for administrative tasks like scheduling and record-keeping, and for data analysis to track patient progress. While AI-powered rehabilitation devices exist, hands-on patient care still requires human expertise. Assistants must adapt to new software but their core roles remain stable.
