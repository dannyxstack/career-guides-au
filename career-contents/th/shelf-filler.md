## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a shelf filler in Thailand?**
A: The average monthly salary for a shelf filler in Thailand ranges from 9,000 to 15,000 THB, depending on location and employer. In Bangkok, rates may be slightly higher (12,000-18,000 THB). Many positions also include benefits such as social security and overtime pay. Entry-level roles often start at the minimum wage, currently 354 THB per day in most provinces.

**Q: What skills are required to become a shelf filler in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail for proper product arrangement, physical stamina for standing and lifting (up to 15-20 kg repeatedly), and basic literacy to read labels and stock codes. Time management is important for completing tasks within shifts. Most employers provide on-the-job training, so prior experience is not mandatory, but reliability and teamwork are valued.

**Q: What is the career outlook for shelf fillers in Thailand?**
A: The demand for shelf fillers remains steady due to Thailand's extensive retail sector, including supermarkets, convenience stores, and hypermarkets. However, automation (e.g., automated stocking robots) is gradually reducing routine tasks. Career progression includes moving to senior stock roles, inventory management, or store supervision. Upskilling in areas like inventory software can improve long-term prospects.
