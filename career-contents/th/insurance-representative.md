## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for insurance representatives in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level representatives earn 15,000–25,000 THB per month, rising to 30,000–50,000 THB with experience, plus commissions. Top performers in Bangkok or with specialized products (e.g., life insurance) can exceed 100,000 THB. Salaries vary by company, location, and sales performance.

**Q: What skills are essential for insurance representatives in Thailand?**
A: Strong interpersonal and communication skills in Thai are crucial, with basic English helpful. Sales ability, product knowledge, persistence, and digital literacy for CRM tools are key. Building trust and understanding customer needs are vital in Thailand's relationship-driven market.

**Q: How is AI impacting insurance representatives in Thailand?**
A: AI automates administrative tasks like policy comparisons and claims, freeing representatives to focus on advisory and relationship-building. While the human touch remains essential in Thailand's trust-based culture, representatives who embrace digital tools and enhance consultative skills will have a stronger career outlook.
