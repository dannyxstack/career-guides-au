## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmers in Thailand?**
A: Income varies widely depending on location and crop prices, but most subsistence farmers in Thailand earn between 3,000 and 10,000 THB per month. Profits are often reinvested in seeds, feed, and tools, leaving little cash. Additional earnings may come from selling surplus at local markets.

**Q: What skills are essential for subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmers in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of local weather patterns, soil management, and basic animal husbandry. Farmers must be able to plant, harvest, and maintain crops, as well as care for livestock like chickens or pigs. Problem-solving for pests, diseases, and water scarcity is also crucial, along with physical endurance.

**Q: How is the occupation of subsistence mixed crop and livestock farming in Thailand expected to change in the near future?**
A: The number of subsistence farmers is declining as younger generations move to cities for work. Those who remain face challenges from climate change, droughts, and floods. AI and technology are not widely adopted, but some farmers use basic tools like weather apps. Government support and organic farming trends may offer niche opportunities.
