## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dancers and choreographers in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level dancers earn about 15,000–25,000 THB per month; experienced professionals in Bangkok may earn 40,000–80,000 THB. Choreographers for stage shows, TV, or corporate events typically earn 30,000–60,000 THB, with top names commanding over 100,000 THB per project. Income is often irregular and project-based.

**Q: What skills are essential for dancers and choreographers in Thailand?**
A: Technical proficiency in multiple dance styles (e.g., Thai classical, contemporary, hip-hop) is key. Choreographers need strong creativity, teaching ability, and project management. Physical fitness, adaptability to different venues, and collaboration with directors or musicians are important. Fluency in Thai is essential for local work.

**Q: How is AI impacting dance and choreography in Thailand?**
A: AI tools are used for generating movement ideas, analyzing choreography, and creating virtual dance rehearsals. However, the art’s cultural and expressive nature limits AI’s replacement. In Thailand, human creativity remains central, especially for traditional performances. AI serves as a supplementary aid rather than a disruptive force.
