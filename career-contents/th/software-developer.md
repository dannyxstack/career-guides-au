## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Thailand?**
A: For mid-level software developers with 3-5 years of experience, the monthly salary typically ranges from 50,000 to 100,000 THB. Senior developers (5+ years) can earn between 100,000 and 150,000 THB, while junior developers (0-2 years) usually start at 25,000 to 45,000 THB. Salaries vary by city, industry, and tech stack.

**Q: What technical skills are most in-demand for software developers in Thailand?**
A: Proficiency in JavaScript (especially React or Vue.js for frontend, Node.js for backend) and Python is highly sought after. Experience with cloud services (AWS, Azure), databases (SQL, MongoDB), and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) is also valued. Additionally, knowledge of mobile development (Flutter, React Native) and agile methodologies is beneficial.

**Q: What is the career outlook for software developers in Thailand?**
A: The outlook is strong due to digital transformation across finance, e-commerce, and manufacturing. Demand for skilled developers continues to grow, with opportunities in both local companies and international firms with regional hubs. AI and automation are creating new roles focused on integration, but core development skills remain essential for long-term career growth.
