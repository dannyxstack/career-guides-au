## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a building architect in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level architects earn around 25,000-35,000 THB per month. Mid-level architects with 5-10 years of experience earn 50,000-80,000 THB. Senior architects or those with specialized skills can earn 100,000-150,000 THB or more, especially in Bangkok or large firms. Salaries vary based on experience, firm size, and project complexity.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a building architect in Thailand?**
A: A typical day includes reviewing project briefs, creating design concepts, preparing sketches and CAD drawings, coordinating with engineers and contractors, and visiting construction sites. Architects must ensure compliance with Thai building codes, such as the Building Control Act, and often meet with clients to discuss design modifications.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of building architects in Thailand?**
A: AI is used in Thailand for generative design, optimizing layouts for energy efficiency, and automating routine drafting tasks. However, it cannot replace human creativity and local knowledge of Thai architectural styles and regulations. Architects who adopt AI tools can boost productivity and focus on innovative design, but traditional skills remain essential.
