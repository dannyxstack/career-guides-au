## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Parking meter collection (replaced by mobile payments and automatic billing systems)
- Street road cleaning (replaced by autonomous cleaning robots)
- Simple customer inquiries (replaced by AI voice assistants and self-service kiosks)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile payment data analysis to analyze foot traffic and sales timing.
- Post real-time service locations and promotional information through social platforms
- Using voice translation tools to serve multilingual tourists

**Human moat:**

- On-site incident handling and de-escalation in emergencies (e.g., traffic accidents, disputes)
- Ability to build trust and emotional connection with regular customers
- In-depth understanding and flexible application of local regulations and community norms

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (using mobile payment terminals, managing online reviews)
- Basic English or foreign language communication
- Customer service and conflict resolution skills
- Time management and multitasking
- Basic repair skills (e.g., trolleys, cleaning equipment)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly income for street vendors and related services workers in Thailand?**
A: Monthly income varies widely depending on location, goods, and season. In Bangkok, street vendors commonly earn between 10,000 and 30,000 Thai baht. Those in tourist areas or selling popular items like food can earn more. Income is irregular and subject to weather and economic conditions.

**Q: What skills are essential for success as a street vendor in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include customer service, basic math for transactions, and knowledge of local regulations. For food vendors, food safety and cooking skills are crucial. Language abilities, especially English in tourist areas, can boost earnings. Adaptability and stamina for long hours are also important.

**Q: How is the street vendor occupation in Thailand affected by technology and AI?**
A: Technology increasingly influences street vending. Mobile payment options like PromptPay and QR codes are common. AI impacts indirectly through competition from online retail and delivery services. However, street vendors remain prevalent due to low barriers to entry and demand for convenient, affordable goods.
