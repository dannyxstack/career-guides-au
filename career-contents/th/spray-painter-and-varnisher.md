## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for spray painters and varnishers in Thailand?**
A: In Thailand, spray painters typically earn between 12,000 and 25,000 THB per month, depending on experience, industry, and location. Those in automotive refinishing or specialized industrial painting may earn higher wages. Overtime and additional skill certifications can significantly boost monthly income.

**Q: What are the daily responsibilities of a spray painter in Thailand?**
A: Spray painters in Thailand prepare surfaces by cleaning, sanding, and masking. They mix paints, adjust spray equipment, and apply coatings evenly. They also inspect finished work for defects, clean equipment, and follow safety protocols. Work settings include auto body shops, furniture factories, and construction sites.

**Q: How is automation or AI affecting spray painting jobs in Thailand?**
A: While automation is increasing in large-scale manufacturing, many Thai workshops still rely on skilled manual spray painters for custom jobs and fine finishes. Robots handle repetitive tasks, but demand for human painters remains for touch-ups, detailed work, and small-scale operations. Developing advanced hand-eye coordination and color matching skills helps future-proof the career.
