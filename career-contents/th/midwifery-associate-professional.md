**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Auto-fill antenatal check records and birth reports
- Generates basic maternal education materials based on algorithms
- Automatically organize appointment schedules and patient reminders
- Basic postpartum follow-up data entry and analysis
- Use AI to analyze initial abnormal patterns in fetal heart rate monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted identification of early signs of high-risk pregnancy (e.g., abnormal data trends)
- Communicate efficiently with multilingual maternity patients via AI translation tools
- AI-driven patient education platform provides personalized guidance for postpartum women
- AI simulation training system enhances emergency care skills for conditions like postpartum hemorrhage
- Using predictive models to optimize postpartum home visit routes and priorities.

**Human moat:**

- Real-time palpation and manual delivery techniques during childbirth
- Empathy and decision-making in maternal emotional support and family communication
- Rapid assessment and manual intervention in emergencies (e.g., umbilical cord prolapse)
- Ability to provide personalized care considering the psychological and cultural background of the mother.
- On-site coordination across teams (doctors, nurses, anesthetists).

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and AI tool use (e.g. Excel, pregnancy risk models)
- Communication and cross-cultural nursing skills.
- Proficient in electronic health record (EHR) systems

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwifery associate professional in Thailand?**
A: Salaries for midwifery associate professionals in Thailand typically range from 15,000 to 30,000 THB per month in public hospitals, with private clinics offering 20,000–40,000 THB. Experienced professionals or those in specialized roles may earn up to 50,000 THB. Pay also varies by region, with Bangkok and tourism hubs offering higher rates.

**Q: What are the essential skills for midwifery associate professionals in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include competency in antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care, as well as newborn assessment. Proficiency in Thai is required for patient communication. Basic life support, infection control, and familiarity with electronic health records are important. Interpersonal skills and cultural sensitivity are vital for supporting families in diverse Thai communities.

**Q: What is the career outlook for midwifery associate professionals in Thailand?**
A: The demand for midwifery professionals is steady due to Thailand’s focus on maternal health under universal healthcare. Opportunities exist in public hospitals, community health centers, and private clinics. AI is not expected to replace hands-on midwifery care, but may assist with documentation. Career advancement includes supervisory roles or specialization in neonatal care.
