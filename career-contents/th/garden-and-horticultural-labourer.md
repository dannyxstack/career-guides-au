## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Thailand?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers in Thailand typically earn between 300 and 500 THB per day, varying by province and employer. Monthly income ranges from 9,000 to 15,000 THB for full-time work. Wages may be slightly higher in urban areas or for specialized tasks like plant care in commercial gardens.

**Q: What skills are required to become a garden and horticultural labourer in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for outdoor work, basic knowledge of plant types and care, and ability to use hand tools like shovels, pruners, and hoes. Familiarity with watering schedules, weeding, and soil preparation is important. While no formal education is needed, on-the-job training is common.

**Q: How is the career outlook for garden and horticultural labourers in Thailand?**
A: Demand for garden and horticultural labourers remains stable due to Thailand's strong agriculture and landscaping sectors. Job opportunities exist in farms, public parks, resorts, and private estates. Automation has limited impact as many tasks require manual precision. However, seasonal variations may affect employment consistency.
