## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for heavy truck and lorry drivers in Thailand?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level drivers earn around 12,000–18,000 THB per month, while experienced drivers can earn 25,000–40,000 THB. Long-haul drivers often receive additional allowances for overnight trips and mileage. Overtime and bonuses can significantly increase total compensation.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a heavy truck driver in Thailand?**
A: Drivers begin with vehicle inspection and loading, then follow assigned routes, often on highways like Mittraphap or Asian Highway. Long-haul trips may involve overnight stays. They must manage fatigue, traffic, and weather. Day starts early, around 4–6 AM, with shifts lasting 8–12 hours, including mandatory rest breaks.

**Q: What skills are essential for heavy truck and lorry drivers in Thailand?**
A: A valid heavy truck driving license (Type 2 or higher) is mandatory. Key skills include defensive driving, basic vehicle maintenance (checking tires, oil, brakes), route planning to avoid restrictions, and cargo securing. Good physical fitness, patience in traffic, and basic Thai language for communication with dispatchers are also important.
