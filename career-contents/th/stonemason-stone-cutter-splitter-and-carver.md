- Use CAD software for stone cutting and layout design

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using 3D scanning and digital twins for precise stone structure installation

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for stonemasons in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level stonemasons in Thailand earn around 15,000–25,000 THB per month. Experienced workers, especially those skilled in carving and restoration, can earn 30,000–50,000 THB. In tourist areas like Phuket or Chiang Mai, rates may be higher due to demand for decorative stonework. Overtime and project-based work can supplement income.

**Q: How is AI affecting stonemasonry in Thailand?**
A: AI-powered CNC machines are automating repetitive cutting and carving tasks in large-scale stone processing, reducing demand for basic stone cutters. However, intricate carving, restoration of historic temples, and custom artistic work still require human skill and creativity. AI complements rather than replaces skilled artisans, who focus on design and finishing.

**Q: What is the career outlook for stonemasons in Thailand?**
A: Demand remains steady due to ongoing construction, temple restoration, and tourism-related decorative stonework. Skilled carvers and restoration specialists are in higher demand than general cutters. Apprenticeships are common, and experienced stonemasons can advance to supervisory roles or start their own workshops. The outlook is stable with limited growth.
