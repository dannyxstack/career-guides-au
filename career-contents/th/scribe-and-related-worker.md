**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Transcribing handwritten documents into electronic text.
- Copying and transcribing manuscripts
- Simple proofreading and formatting adjustments
- Document archiving and numbering
- Fill in table content following a template

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI-assisted transcription tools to improve efficiency
- Supervise and correct AI-generated transcription results
- Processing complex handwritten or non-standard format documents
- Manage multilingual document translation and localization
- Use OCR and NLP tools to batch process historical documents

**Human moat:**

- Ability to interpret complex, blurred, or damaged manuscripts
- Precise understanding of terminology in legal, medical, or other professional fields
- Client communication and document quality control
- Cross-platform Document Management System Configuration and Maintenance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes and related workers in Thailand?**
A: Scribes and related workers in Thailand typically earn between 15,000 and 30,000 THB per month, depending on experience, employer, and specialization. Those working in legal or government settings may receive higher pay and additional allowances.

**Q: What skills are essential for scribes and related workers in Thailand?**
A: Essential skills include fast and accurate typing in both Thai and English, excellent grammar and spelling, strong attention to detail, and familiarity with legal or business terminology. Proficiency with word processing software and document formatting is also crucial.

**Q: What is the career outlook for scribes and related workers in Thailand?**
A: The demand for scribes may decline due to automation and AI advancements, but specialized roles in legal transcription and certified document preparation remain stable. Workers who update their skills with digital tools and specialize in niche areas can sustain their careers.
