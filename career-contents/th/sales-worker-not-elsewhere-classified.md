## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Sales Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level positions earn around 12,000–18,000 THB per month, while experienced sales workers can make 25,000–40,000 THB. Commission-based roles may yield higher total compensation. Salaries vary by industry and location, with Bangkok offering higher ranges.

**Q: What key skills are needed for a Sales Worker in Thailand?**
A: Essential skills include strong interpersonal communication in Thai (and basic English for some roles), active listening, negotiation, and product knowledge. Adaptability to diverse customer needs and basic digital literacy for CRM tools are increasingly important. Persistence and resilience also matter.

**Q: How is AI affecting Sales Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Thailand?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like data entry and lead sorting, but human skills—such as building rapport and closing deals—remain vital. Sales workers now use AI-powered tools for customer insights and follow-up reminders. The role is evolving toward consultative selling, reducing manual work but requiring digital upskilling.
