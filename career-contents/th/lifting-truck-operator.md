## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a lifting truck operator in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around 12,000–18,000 THB per month, while experienced operators can earn 20,000–30,000 THB. Overtime and bonuses may increase total pay. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in industrial areas like Rayong or Chonburi.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a lifting truck operator in Thailand?**
A: Operators drive and maneuver forklifts or similar trucks to move materials around warehouses, factories, or construction sites. Tasks include loading and unloading trucks, stacking pallets, and performing routine vehicle checks. Safety inspections and coordination with warehouse staff are also daily duties.

**Q: What skills are essential for a lifting truck operator, and is AI affecting this job in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include driving proficiency, spatial awareness, and knowledge of safety protocols. A forklift license from the Department of Labor is required. AI impact is low; automated guided vehicles are emerging but human operators remain essential for complex tasks and safety oversight.
