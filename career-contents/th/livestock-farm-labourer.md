## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a livestock farm labourer in Thailand?**
A: Monthly salaries for livestock farm labourers in Thailand range from 9,000 to 15,000 THB, depending on the farm size, location, and type of livestock. Many farms also provide free accommodation and meals. Overtime pay may apply during peak seasons like births or harvests. Minimum wage laws apply, but actual pay often exceeds the legal minimum.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a livestock farm labourer in Thailand?**
A: Daily tasks include feeding and watering animals, cleaning pens and barns, monitoring animal health, and assisting with breeding or birthing. Labourers may also maintain fences, handle feed storage, and support veterinary visits. Work often starts early, around 6 a.m., with shifts lasting 8-10 hours. Physical stamina and comfort with outdoor work are essential.

**Q: How is AI affecting livestock farm labourer jobs in Thailand?**
A: AI and automation are slowly being adopted on larger Thai farms, such as automated feeding systems, robotic milking, and health monitoring sensors. These technologies reduce the need for manual labour but create demand for workers skilled in operating and maintaining such equipment. Small-scale farms still rely heavily on manual labour, so the immediate impact on employment is limited.
