- Automatically generates preliminary land use analysis report

- Use AI to simulate multiple planning scenarios and assess environmental impacts.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for town and traffic planners in Thailand?**
A: Salaries in Thailand vary by experience and sector. Entry-level planners earn around THB 25,000–35,000 per month, while mid-career professionals earn THB 50,000–80,000. Senior roles in government or private consulting can reach THB 100,000–150,000 monthly. Bonuses and benefits like health insurance are common in larger cities.

**Q: What key skills are required for town and traffic planners in Thailand today?**
A: Planners need GIS proficiency (ArcGIS, QGIS), urban design knowledge, and traffic modeling skills. Analytical ability and communication are crucial for coordinating with agencies and the public. Understanding Thai building regulations and environmental impact assessments is essential. Familiarity with data analytics and smart city technologies gives an edge.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of town and traffic planners in Thailand?**
A: AI enables real-time traffic simulation and predictive modeling, helping planners optimize urban growth and traffic flow. While AI handles data-heavy tasks, planners provide strategic oversight and community engagement. Demand for planners with AI literacy is growing, but human judgment remains vital for decision-making and policy.
