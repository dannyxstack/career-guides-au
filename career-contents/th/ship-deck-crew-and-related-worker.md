## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for ships’ deck crew in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level deck crew (ordinary seaman) earn 15,000–25,000 THB per month. With experience and higher rank (able seaman or bosun), salaries range from 25,000–45,000 THB. Foreign-flagged vessels may pay more, but Thai-flagged ships generally follow this range. Overtime and bonus can add 5,000–10,000 THB.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are required for deck crew jobs in Thailand?**
A: Mandatory certification includes the STCW Basic Safety Training and a Thai seaman's book (from the Marine Department). Physical fitness, swimming ability, and basic English are expected. Many employers prefer completion of a maritime vocational school program. Experience in watchkeeping, mooring, and maintenance is gained on the job.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting deck crew roles in Thailand?**
A: Automation is gradually reducing manual tasks like navigation and cargo monitoring, but human oversight remains essential. In Thailand, most vessels still rely on traditional crew roles. AI may affect long-term employment by increasing demand for tech-savvy crew, but physical tasks (e.g., painting, securing loads) remain unchanged. Upskilling in electronics is advised.
