- Automatically Analyze Public Property Data, Generate Market Reports and Comparable Sales Analysis

- Basic clerical work, such as contract drafting and document filing
- Initial customer inquiries and communication via chatbots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI tools for in-depth data analysis to quickly identify investment opportunities and market trends
- Using AI to generate personalized property valuation models and investment reports

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Buyer in Thailand?**
A: Mid-level Buyers in Thailand earn between 30,000-60,000 THB per month, depending on industry and experience. Senior Buyers can earn 70,000-120,000 THB. Salaries are higher in multinational firms and sectors like retail, manufacturing, and import/export.

**Q: What skills are essential for a Buyer in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include negotiation, supply chain management, data analysis, vendor relationship management, and fluency in English and Thai. Knowledge of local market trends and customs regulations is also important. Increasingly, digital literacy and proficiency in ERP systems are required.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of Buyers in Thailand?**
A: AI automates repetitive tasks like purchase order processing and market price monitoring, allowing Buyers to focus on strategic sourcing and supplier partnerships. However, human skills like negotiation and ethical judgment remain critical. Buyers who upskill in data analytics and AI tools will have a competitive edge.
