## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a medical secretary in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level medical secretaries in Thailand earn 15,000–30,000 THB per month, while experienced professionals in private hospitals or clinics can earn up to 45,000 THB. Salaries are higher in Bangkok, and many positions include health insurance, bonuses, and other benefits.

**Q: What skills are essential for a medical secretary in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in Thai and English, knowledge of medical terminology, appointment scheduling, and patient communication. Experience with electronic health record (EHR) software, attention to detail, confidentiality, and basic billing knowledge are also important. Familiarity with Thai healthcare regulations gives candidates an edge.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of medical secretaries in Thailand?**
A: AI is automating tasks like transcription, scheduling, and data entry, but medical secretaries in Thailand remain essential for patient interaction, complex coordination, and error-checking. AI enhances efficiency but cannot replace human judgment and personalized care, so the role is evolving rather than disappearing.
