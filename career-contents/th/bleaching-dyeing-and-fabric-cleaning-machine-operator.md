## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operator in Thailand?**
A: In Thailand, entry-level operators earn around 9,000–15,000 THB per month. With experience, salaries can rise to 15,000–25,000 THB. Those working in large textile factories in industrial zones like Ayutthaya or Chon Buri may earn higher pay, and overtime is common.

**Q: What skills are required for this role in Thailand?**
A: Operators need skills in machine operation, color matching, chemical handling, and attention to detail. Familiarity with industrial dyeing and bleaching processes is essential. Basic numeracy and literacy are required to follow instructions and record production data.

**Q: How is AI affecting bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Thailand?**
A: AI and automation are gradually being adopted in larger Thai textile factories for color matching and process control. However, manual operation remains common in smaller mills. Operators should learn basic IT skills to adapt, as AI is expected to handle repetitive tasks but not fully replace human oversight.
