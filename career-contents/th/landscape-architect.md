## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a landscape architect in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level landscape architects in Thailand earn around 25,000-35,000 THB per month, while mid-level professionals with 5-10 years of experience earn 50,000-80,000 THB. Senior positions or those in leading consultancies can exceed 100,000 THB monthly. Salaries vary by location, with Bangkok generally offering higher pay.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a landscape architect in Thailand?**
A: A landscape architect in Thailand typically spends the day on site surveys, client meetings, and design development using CAD and 3D modeling software. They coordinate with architects, engineers, and contractors to ensure projects comply with local regulations like the Building Control Act. Preparing presentation materials and cost estimates is also common.

**Q: How is AI impacting the landscape architecture profession in Thailand?**
A: AI tools are streamlining design processes, such as automated site analysis and generative design software that speeds up concept development. However, human creativity and local knowledge of Thai plant species, climate, and cultural preferences remain irreplaceable. AI assists with simulations for flood resilience and microclimate analysis, but the profession emphasizes sustainable, context-sensitive solutions.
