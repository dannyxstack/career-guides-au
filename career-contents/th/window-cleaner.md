## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a window cleaner in Thailand?**
A: Window cleaners in Thailand typically earn between 9,000 and 15,000 THB per month for residential work. Commercial high-rise cleaners can earn 15,000–25,000 THB, often with bonuses. Experienced cleaners with specialized equipment may earn up to 30,000 THB. Overtime and tips can supplement income, especially in tourist areas like Bangkok or Phuket.

**Q: What skills are required to become a window cleaner in Thailand?**
A: Key skills include physical fitness, comfort with heights, and attention to detail. Cleaners must handle ladders, safety harnesses, and water-fed poles. Basic knowledge of cleaning agents and techniques is important. For high-rise work, certification in rope access or working at heights is often required. Good customer service skills help in building repeat clientele.

**Q: Is artificial intelligence (AI) impacting window cleaning jobs in Thailand?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact so far. While robotic window cleaners exist, they are expensive and not widely adopted in Thailand, especially for complex or high-rise buildings. Human cleaners are still preferred for their flexibility and ability to handle irregular surfaces. However, AI scheduling tools are improving route efficiency for cleaning companies.
