## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for physicists and astronomers in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level physicists and astronomers in Thailand earn around 30,000–50,000 THB per month. With experience, salaries can rise to 80,000–150,000 THB monthly in research institutions or universities. Senior roles in private sectors like data science or tech may exceed 200,000 THB. Public sector pay is lower but stable.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for physicists and astronomers in Thailand?**
A: Physicists and astronomers in Thailand work in universities (e.g., Chulalongkorn, Mahidol), research institutes like NARIT (National Astronomical Research Institute of Thailand), and government agencies. Private sector roles include data science, semiconductor engineering, and R&D in industries such as electronics and energy. Teaching and science communication are also common.

**Q: How is AI impacting physics and astronomy in Thailand?**
A: AI is transforming data analysis in Thai research, enabling faster processing of astronomical images and particle physics data. Machine learning helps predict phenomena and optimize experiments. However, AI is a tool, not a replacement—physicists still need strong foundations in theory and modeling. Adapting to AI tools is increasingly important for career competitiveness.
