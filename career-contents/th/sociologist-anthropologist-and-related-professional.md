## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sociologists and anthropologists in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level positions in universities or NGOs pay 30,000–50,000 THB monthly. Mid-career professionals earn 50,000–80,000 THB, while senior roles in research institutes or government can reach 80,000–120,000 THB. Benefits often include health insurance and pension contributions.

**Q: What key skills are required for sociologists and anthropologists working in Thailand?**
A: Proficiency in both Thai and English is essential for fieldwork and report writing. Strong qualitative research skills, including ethnographic observation and in-depth interviews, are highly valued. Familiarity with statistical software like SPSS or NVivo, and knowledge of Thailand’s socio-cultural landscape, are also important.

**Q: What is the career outlook for sociologists and anthropologists in Thailand?**
A: Demand remains steady in academia, NGOs, government agencies, and private sector research. Growth is driven by social impact assessments and cultural heritage work. Competition for university posts is high, but applied roles in policy research and community development offer opportunities. AI may affect data analysis but not fieldwork.
