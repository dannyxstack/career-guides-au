## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for door-to-door salespersons in Thailand?**
A: Earnings vary widely based on commission and product type. Many receive a low base salary (around 8,000–12,000 THB per month) plus commission, with total monthly income ranging from 15,000 to 40,000 THB for experienced sellers. Top performers can earn over 60,000 THB in high-demand sectors like insurance or utilities.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a door-to-door salesperson in Thailand?**
A: Days often start with a team briefing, then canvassing assigned residential areas. Sellers carry product samples, brochures, and payment devices. Each appointment involves a 5–10 minute pitch, handling objections, and closing sales. Afternoons include data entry, follow-ups, and restocking materials. Work is physically demanding with frequent rejection.

**Q: How might artificial intelligence affect door-to-door sales jobs in Thailand?**
A: AI is unlikely to replace the role fully but can enhance efficiency. Customer relationship management tools and predictive analytics help route planning and lead scoring. Automated chatbots may handle initial inquiries, but the human touch remains key for trust-building. Sellers who use AI tools for scheduling and sales scripts may gain a competitive edge.
