- Manual pest and disease identification replaced by AI image recognition

- Paper-based bookkeeping and tax calculations are being replaced by financial software

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI tools analyze soil moisture and meteorological data to optimize irrigation timing.
- Use satellite imagery and drones to monitor crop health and provide early warning of pests and diseases

- Online platforms connecting local consumers with restaurants to expand direct sales channels

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence crop farmers in Thailand?**
A: Subsistence crop farmers in Thailand typically earn between 5,000 and 15,000 THB per month from surplus sales. Income varies by region, crop type, and season. Many farmers supplement through multiple crops or off-farm labor. Government subsidies and cooperative programs may provide additional support, but overall earnings are modest and unpredictable.

**Q: What skills are essential for subsistence crop farmers in Thailand?**
A: Essential skills include knowledge of crop cycles, soil management, pest control, and water management. Physical stamina and ability to work long hours outdoors are crucial. Many farmers rely on traditional techniques passed down through generations, while basic financial literacy helps in managing costs and sales. Some learn modern practices through local agricultural extension services.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a subsistence crop farmer in Thailand?**
A: A typical day involves planting, weeding, watering, and harvesting crops using manual tools or small machinery. Farmers also maintain irrigation systems, tend to livestock if present, and transport produce to local markets. Work starts early to avoid heat and is highly seasonal, with peak labor during planting and harvest times. Weather conditions dictate daily tasks.
