**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard statistical model running and parameter tuning.
- Routine data cleaning and basic report generation
- Simple risk probability calculation and reserve estimation
- Routine forecasting and updating of economic indicators
- Basic regression analysis and hypothesis testing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Identifies complex risk factors and non-linear relationships using machine learning models.
- Analyze economic policy texts and market sentiment via natural language processing
- Uses AI to simulate large-scale economic scenarios and stress tests.
- Automated data exploration and visualization to quickly uncover insights
- Using AI-assisted validation of model assumptions and result robustness

**Human moat:**

- Ability to communicate complex risk models to non-technical management
- Making Professional Judgments Under Uncertainty and Sparse Data

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mathematicians, actuaries and statisticians in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level positions start around 30,000-50,000 THB per month, while experienced professionals in insurance or banking can earn 100,000-200,000 THB monthly. Senior roles in multinational firms or consulting may exceed 300,000 THB. Bonuses often add 2-4 months' pay.

**Q: What key skills are needed for actuaries and statisticians in Thailand, and how is AI affecting the field?**
A: Strong analytical skills, proficiency in Excel, SQL, Python or R, and knowledge of Thai insurance regulations are essential. AI automates routine data tasks but increases demand for strategic interpretation and model validation. Actuaries must adapt by focusing on business insights and ethical AI use.

**Q: What is the day-to-day work and career outlook for actuaries and statisticians in Thailand?**
A: Daily tasks include pricing insurance products, analyzing risk, building predictive models, and preparing reports for regulators. Career outlook is positive due to growing insurance and fintech sectors. Professionals can advance to chief actuary or data science roles, with opportunities in banking and government.
