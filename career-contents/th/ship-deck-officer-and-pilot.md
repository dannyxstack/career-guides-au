## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for deck officers in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level deck officers earn around 30,000–50,000 THB per month, while experienced officers or pilots can earn 80,000–150,000 THB. Senior captains on large vessels may exceed 200,000 THB. Salaries vary by shipping line, vessel type, and rank. Additional bonuses and overtime are common.

**Q: What skills are essential for deck officers in Thai shipping?**
A: Essential skills include navigation, ship handling, cargo operations, and maritime safety. Proficiency in English is critical, as Thai crews often work with international colleagues. Familiarity with the Gulf of Thailand and Singapore Strait routes is valued. Leadership, crisis management, and STCW certifications are mandatory.

**Q: What is the career outlook for deck officers in Thailand?**
A: The outlook is stable, driven by Thailand's role as a maritime hub for container and tanker shipping. Demand is steady for licensed officers, though competition exists for higher ranks. Automation in navigation is growing but human oversight remains essential. Career progression to captain or pilot offers good long-term prospects.
