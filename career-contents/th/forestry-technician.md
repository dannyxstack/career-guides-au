## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for forestry technicians in Thailand?**
A: Entry-level forestry technicians in Thailand earn around 15,000–25,000 THB per month. With 5–10 years of experience, salaries can rise to 30,000–45,000 THB. Government positions often include additional allowances and benefits, while private sector roles in plantations or consulting may offer higher pay, especially for those with specialized skills in GIS or forest certification.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a forestry technician in Thailand?**
A: Daily tasks include conducting field surveys to assess tree health and biodiversity, collecting data on forest resources, and using GPS and GIS for mapping. Technicians may also oversee reforestation projects, monitor fire risks, and assist in enforcing logging regulations. Work often involves hiking in remote areas and collaborating with local communities or government officials.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting forestry technician roles in Thailand?**
A: AI tools like satellite image analysis and drone-based monitoring are streamlining forest inventories and wildfire detection, reducing manual fieldwork. However, technicians remain essential for ground-truthing data, interpreting AI outputs, and making on-site decisions. The role is evolving to require digital skills, but AI is seen as a complement rather than a replacement for human expertise.
