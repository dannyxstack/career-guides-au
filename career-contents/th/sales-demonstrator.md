**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeated on-site demonstrations of product standard functions
- Using pre-recorded videos or AI-generated content to replace on-site explanations
- Frequently asked customer questions; replaced by chatbots
- Product parameter query and comparison, replaced by self-service terminals or AR applications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer data to assist in customizing personalized presentation scripts
- Real-time speech transcription and translation, supporting multilingual audiences
- AR/VR technology enhances the immersion and interactivity of product displays
- Sales data dashboard helps presenters adjust strategies in real time

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sales demonstrators in Thailand?**
A: In Thailand, sales demonstrators typically earn between 15,000 and 30,000 THB per month, with additional commissions or bonuses based on performance. Earnings vary by industry, location, and experience. For example, demonstrators in electronics or high-end cosmetics may earn higher commissions.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a sales demonstrator in Thailand?**
A: Essential skills include strong communication and interpersonal abilities, product knowledge, and persuasion techniques. Fluency in Thai is crucial, with English beneficial for tourist areas. Adaptability, energy, and the ability to engage diverse customers are important.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of sales demonstrators in Thailand?**
A: AI is increasingly used for product information and initial customer interactions via chatbots or kiosks. However, human demonstrators remain vital for creating personal connections and demonstrating product use. The role is evolving to focus more on experiential marketing, where empathy and hands-on interaction are irreplaceable.
