## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for building caretakers in Belgium typically range from €1,800 to €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and sector. The pay is often governed by collective bargaining agreements, such as the paritair comité for cleaning (PC 112) or property management. Additional benefits may include housing allowances or bonuses.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a building caretaker in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified workers and generally requires a higher education degree. Building caretaking is not a high-skilled occupation, so the Blue Card route is not applicable. Instead, non-EU nationals can obtain a single permit (combined work and residence permit) if the employer secures a work permit B, provided the role cannot be filled by a local worker.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for building caretakers in Belgium?**
A: Building caretakers can advance to roles such as facility manager, concierge supervisor, or maintenance coordinator with experience and additional training. Certifications in first aid, fire safety, or property management can boost prospects. Some caretakers transition into cleaning or hospitality management. Knowledge of French, Dutch, or German is advantageous in Belgium's multilingual regions.
