## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers, package deliverers, and luggage porters in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the gross monthly salary for this occupation typically ranges from €1,900 to €2,500 for full-time employment, depending on experience and employer. Many positions pay at or slightly above the national minimum wage (€1,994.71 as of 2024). Additional benefits like meal vouchers and transport allowances are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as a messenger or porter in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers generally require a single permit (combined work and residence permit) sponsored by a Belgian employer. The EU Blue Card, intended for highly qualified workers, is rarely applicable due to the occupation's skill level and the 2024 salary threshold of €60,447 gross annually. Most applicants will use the single permit procedure for low- or medium-skilled roles.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for messengers and porters in Belgium?**
A: Experienced messengers can advance to team leader or logistics coordinator roles. Porters at airports or hotels may move into customer service or operations management. With additional training in logistics or transport management, workers can become supervisors or transition into courier/delivery service management.
