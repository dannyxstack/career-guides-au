## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, water and firewood collectors are typically paid at or slightly above the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is approximately €1,995 per month for full-time work. Actual pay may vary by region and employer, and may include benefits in kind such as accommodation. Collective bargaining agreements may also set higher rates in certain sectors.

**Q: Is it possible to work in Belgium as a water and firewood collector via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled non-EU workers with a university degree or equivalent and a job offer meeting a salary threshold. Water and firewood collecting is a low-skilled occupation and therefore does not qualify for the EU Blue Card. Other work permits exist, such as the single permit (work permit B), which requires a labour market test and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the working conditions for water and firewood collectors in Belgium?**
A: Work is often seasonal and physically demanding, involving outdoor labour in all weather. Tasks include collecting firewood from forests and delivering water in rural areas. Employment may be through temp agencies or directly by local municipalities. Safety training is required. The job offers limited advancement but can provide a steady income for low-skilled workers.
