- AI tool training helps opticians quickly learn new product knowledge

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and adjustment of complex prescriptions (e.g., prisms, high astigmatism)
- Face-to-face customer communication and trust building
- Custom frame adjustment (physical fit and comfort)
- Professional consultation for treating eye abnormalities or heterophoria

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a dispensing optician in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. According to sector agreements, a starting dispensing optician earns between €2,200 and €2,800 gross per month. Experienced opticians can earn up to €3,500 or more. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers and transportation allowances.

**Q: What is the work visa route for a dispensing optician to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as a dispensing optician via the single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Requirements include a job offer, recognition of foreign qualifications through NARIC, and registration with the provincial medical commission. The employer must apply for the permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a dispensing optician in Belgium?**
A: You need a diploma in opticianry from a recognized Belgian institution (e.g., a bachelor's degree) or a foreign qualification approved by NARIC. Registration with the competent provincial commission of the Order of Opticians is mandatory, along with proof of good moral conduct. Continuing education is required to maintain registration.
