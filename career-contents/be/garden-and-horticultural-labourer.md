## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average pay for garden and horticultural labourers in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the minimum wage is €1,954.99 per month (2024). Garden and horticultural labourers typically earn between €1,954 and €2,400 monthly, depending on experience and region. Many are covered by sectoral collective bargaining agreements that set higher wages, especially in Flanders. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may apply.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for non-EU garden and horticultural labourers in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers typically need a single permit (combined work and residence permit). The employer must prove the position could not be filled by an EU candidate (labor market test). The EU Blue Card is not suitable as it targets highly skilled workers with higher education and a minimum salary threshold (€62,000+ annually). For labourers, the single permit is the standard route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for garden and horticultural labourers in Belgium?**
A: Labourers can progress to team leader, supervisor, or specialized roles like pruner or sprayer operator. With experience, they may become independent landscapers. Further training through VDAB (Flanders) or Forem (Wallonia) offers certificates in horticulture, leading to higher pay and responsibility.
