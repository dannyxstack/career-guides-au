## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate treatment records and reports
- Preliminary diagnostic assistance based on images and data
- Patient appointment management and follow-up reminders
- Basic parameter calculation and adjustment of exercise prescriptions

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI motion capture and biomechanical analysis to optimize rehabilitation plans
- Generate clinical documentation quickly using speech recognition and NLP
- Identify complication risks early through AI predictive models
- Remotely monitor patient compliance with home-based training and intervene promptly
- Using digital twins to simulate treatment outcomes

**Human moat:**

- Fine perception in manual therapy and palpation
- Patient emotional support and therapeutic communication
- Complex clinical decision-making and individualised care planning
- Clinical judgment in interdisciplinary collaboration
- Ethics and accountability (e.g., tracing medical errors)

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic tool operation (e.g., Therabot, Kinvent)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a paramedical practitioner in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a paramedic in Belgium typically ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience, region, and employer. Public sector workers may follow official scales, while private ambulance services vary. Additional benefits often include shift premiums and allowances.

**Q: What is the work visa route for paramedical practitioners from outside the EU to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU paramedical practitioners can work in Belgium via the single permit (work and residence). The employer must obtain a work authorization from the regional employment service. Recognition of professional qualifications by the Federal Public Service Health is mandatory, and language proficiency (French, Dutch, or German) is required. The EU Blue Card generally applies to highly qualified workers with a bachelor's degree, which may not always cover paramedics.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for paramedical practitioners in Belgium?**
A: Paramedics can advance to senior paramedic, team leader, or instructor roles. With additional training, they may specialize in emergency medical dispatch, disaster medicine, or paramedical management. Some pursue higher education to become physician assistants or shift to hospital emergency departments. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain registration.
