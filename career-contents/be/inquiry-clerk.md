## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Inquiry Clerk in Belgium?**
A: Inquiry clerks in Belgium earn a gross monthly salary between €2,200 and €3,000, depending on experience and sector. Collective labor agreements (CPCs) set minimum pay scales. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers and transport allowance.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as an Inquiry Clerk in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified roles, requiring a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €46,000 in 2023) and a recognized qualification. Alternatively, a single permit (work + residence) is available for other positions, with labour market checks.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Inquiry Clerks in Belgium?**
A: Inquiry clerks can advance to senior customer service roles, team leader, or specialised positions in complaints handling. With additional training, they may move into sales, administrative management, or data analysis. Fluency in Dutch or French and English enhances prospects in Belgium's multilingual environment.
