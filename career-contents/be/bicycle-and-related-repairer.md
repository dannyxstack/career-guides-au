## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bicycle repairer in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for bicycle repairers in Belgium vary by experience and region, typically ranging from €1,800 to €2,800 gross per month. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while experienced mechanics can earn up to €3,000. Many employers offer additional benefits such as meal vouchers and transport allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa options for non-EU bicycle repairers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU bicycle repairers can work in Belgium via the single permit (combined work and residence permit) if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree or equivalent experience; bicycle repair typically does not meet this threshold, so the single permit is the standard route. The employer must demonstrate labor market need.

**Q: What training is required to become a certified bicycle repairer in Belgium?**
A: While formal certification is not legally required, many employers prefer candidates with a secondary education diploma in mechanics or a vocational training certificate in bicycle repair (e.g., from Syntra or CFI). Apprenticeships are common. Ongoing training on e-bikes and electric systems is increasingly valuable due to market growth.
