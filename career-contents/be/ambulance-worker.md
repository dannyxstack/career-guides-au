## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ambulance workers in Belgium?**
A: Entry-level ambulance workers in Belgium earn between €2,500 and €3,200 gross per month, depending on experience and region. Salaries are often determined by joint committees (paritair comité) and increase with seniority. On-call and overtime pay may apply. Public sector positions offer standardized pay scales.

**Q: Can non-EU ambulance workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers need a single permit (work + residence visa) sponsored by a recognized employer, which requires a labor market test showing no suitable EU candidate. The EU Blue Card is only for highly qualified professionals with a recognized higher education degree and a salary above a threshold (€63,806 in 2024), rarely met by ambulance workers. A more feasible route is the shortage occupation list, but ambulance work is not currently on it.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities and career progression options for ambulance workers in Belgium?**
A: Ambulance workers provide basic and advanced pre-hospital care, transport patients, and maintain equipment. In Belgium, they often work for emergency medical services (Dringende Geneeskundige Hulp) or private companies. Career progression includes becoming a paramedic (after additional training), team leader, or moving into emergency planning or nursing. Continuous education is required to renew licenses.
