## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an insulation worker in Belgium?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for full-time insulation workers in Belgium range from €2,200 to €3,500, depending on experience and region. Sectoral agreements often include extra benefits like meal vouchers, transport allowance, and an end-of-year bonus.

**Q: Can non-EU insulation workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Belgium?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles) or a single permit. The job must meet a salary threshold (currently €60,782/year for Blue Card, lower for shortage occupations), and the employer must pass a labour market test. Recognized qualifications or equivalent experience are required.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for insulation workers in Belgium?**
A: Insulation workers can progress to team leader, estimator, or energy advisor. Certifications from VDAB or SYNTRA enhance prospects. Growing demand due to energy efficiency regulations and ongoing training in advanced materials support career growth.
