## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Employment Agent or Contractor in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for employment agents and contractors in Belgium vary widely by experience and sector. Entry-level agents may earn around €2,500–€3,000 gross per month, while experienced agents can earn €4,000–€6,000 or more. Contractors often negotiate fees based on placements. Benefits and bonuses are common in this field.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU Employment Agents to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a single permit (work + residence) or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. For employment agents, a job offer from a Belgian employer is required. The EU Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Belgium. The employer must also obtain a labour market test in most cases.

**Q: What qualifications or background are commonly required for Employment Agents and Contractors in Belgium?**
A: A bachelor's degree in human resources, business, or a related field is common. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German is essential depending on the region. Experience in sales or recruitment is highly valued. Certifications like the Certified Recruitment Professional (CRP) can be advantageous. Many employers also seek strong networking skills.
