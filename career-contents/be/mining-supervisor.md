## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Mining Supervisor in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a Mining Supervisor in Belgium typically ranges from €3,500 to €5,500, depending on experience, company size, and location. Benefits such as meal vouchers, 13th month pay, and bonuses are common. Salaries are often determined by collective bargaining agreements in the sector.

**Q: Can a non-EU Mining Supervisor work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU Mining Supervisors can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, a higher education degree (or equivalent experience), and a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average Belgian salary. Alternatively, a single permit for work and residence is available for those meeting lower salary thresholds but requiring a labour market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Mining Supervisor in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's or master's degree in mining engineering, geology, or a related field is required. Professional experience in mining operations is essential. Certifications in safety management and knowledge of Belgian mining regulations (e.g., for quarries or subsurface mines) are highly valued. Knowledge of Dutch, French, or German is often necessary depending on the region.
