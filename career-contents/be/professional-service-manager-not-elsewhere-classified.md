## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Professional Services Managers in Belgium?**
A: The typical gross annual salary for Professional Services Managers in Belgium ranges from €55,000 to €95,000, depending on experience, sector, and firm size. Managers in large consulting firms or with specialized expertise may earn higher, with bonuses and benefits commonly included.

**Q: How can a non-EU professional obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this role in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Belgium via the Single Permit or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary above €50,534 (2025 threshold) and a recognized degree. The employer must obtain a work permit. The Single Permit combines work and residence authorization.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Professional Services Managers in Belgium?**
A: Professional Services Managers in Belgium can advance to senior management, director, or partner roles. Pursuing an MBA or professional certifications (e.g., PMP, CFA) enhances prospects. Multilingualism (Dutch, French, English) and international experience are highly valued in the Belgian market.
