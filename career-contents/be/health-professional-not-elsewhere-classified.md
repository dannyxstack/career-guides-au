## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by role and experience, but in Belgium, health professionals (e.g., radiographers, dietitians, physiotherapists) typically earn between €2,800 and €5,000 gross per month. Public sector roles follow fixed scales; private practice may vary. Specialists or those with advanced certifications earn higher end. Benefits often include 13th month pay and healthcare coverage.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for health professionals not elsewhere classified to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU health professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a single permit (combined work and residence). Must have a job offer from a Belgian employer, meet salary thresholds (Blue Card: at least €62,058 gross/year in 2024), and have qualifications recognized by the relevant regulatory body (e.g., for paramedical professions). The employer must also obtain a work permit.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: Career progression includes specialization (e.g., in sports physiotherapy, medical imaging), leadership roles (head of department, clinic management), or transitioning to research or teaching. Many pursue additional certifications (e.g., manual therapy, advanced ultrasound). Networking via professional associations (e.g., AXXON for physiotherapists) and continuing education are key. Public hospitals offer seniority-based promotions.
