## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a landscape architect in Belgium?**
A: A landscape architect in Belgium earns a gross monthly salary between €2,800 and €4,500, depending on experience and region. Senior roles in Brussels or Flanders may reach €5,500. Benefits often include extra holiday pay and a 13th month.

**Q: What work visa options are available for landscape architects from outside the EU?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a single permit for work and residence. Requirements include a valid job offer, a salary threshold (around €50,000 annually for the Blue Card), and recognition of qualifications by the relevant professional body.

**Q: What is the career outlook for landscape architects in Belgium?**
A: Demand for landscape architects in Belgium is growing due to urbanization, climate adaptation, and infrastructure projects. Opportunities exist in private firms, public administration, and NGOs. Registration with the Order of Architects is required for certain tasks, which involves specific educational and experience criteria.
