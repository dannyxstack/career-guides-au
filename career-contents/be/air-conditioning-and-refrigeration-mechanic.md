## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, entry-level mechanics earn around €2,000–€2,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €3,000–€4,000 or more. Wages vary by region (Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels) and employer. Collective bargaining agreements set minimums, and overtime or on-call pay may apply.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic wanting to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a job offer and salary threshold) or a single permit (combining work and residence). The occupation may qualify as a shortage profession, potentially easing procedures. Specific requirements include a valid work contract and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, a secondary education diploma in refrigeration or HVAC (e.g., CESS with technical specialization) is typically required. Vocational training (e.g., through Syntra or IFAPME) and an apprenticeship are common paths. Certification for handling refrigerants (F-gases) is mandatory, and additional certifications may be needed for specific systems.
