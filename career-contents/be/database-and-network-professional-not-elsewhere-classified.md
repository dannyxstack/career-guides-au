## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for database and network professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, sector, and region. Entry-level positions typically start around €2,800 gross per month, while senior professionals can earn over €6,000. In IT consulting and finance, salaries are often higher. Benefits such as meal vouchers and a company car are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa options for non-EU database and network professionals in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer and meet the salary threshold (for highly qualified workers, currently around €60,000 gross annually for non-regulated professions). Alternatively, the single permit (combined work and residence) is available for other skilled positions, subject to a labour market test.

**Q: Which industries in Belgium have the highest demand for database and network professionals?**
A: Demand is strongest in financial services, IT consulting, telecommunications, and e-commerce, especially in Brussels and Flanders. Logistics and manufacturing also rely heavily on database and network infrastructure. The Belgian market values multilingual professionals, particularly those fluent in French, Dutch, and English.
