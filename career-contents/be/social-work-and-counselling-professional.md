## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by sector, experience, and region. In the non-profit and public sectors, entry-level social workers earn around €2,500–€3,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €3,500–€4,500. Counsellors in private practice may have higher or variable income. The net salary is subject to progressive income tax and social security contributions.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU social work and counselling professionals in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals generally need a single permit (work and residence) obtained by an employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a recognized tertiary degree and a salary threshold (€55,991 gross/year in 2025). Social work roles often fall below this threshold, so the single permit is more common. Language skills in Dutch, French, or German are often required for registration.

**Q: Are foreign diplomas in social work or counselling recognized in Belgium?**
A: Yes, but through a recognition procedure. For regulated professions, you must apply to the relevant community (Flemish, French, or German-speaking) for equivalence. Additionally, you may need to register with a professional body, such as the Commission des Psychologues (for psychologists) or the social work council. Language proficiency in the region's language is mandatory for professional practice.
