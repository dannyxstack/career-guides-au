## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physiotherapy assistants in Belgium?**
A: Entry-level physiotherapy assistants in Belgium earn around €1,800-€2,200 gross per month. With experience, salaries can rise to €2,500-€3,000. Public sector and hospitals may offer higher pay, while private practices may vary. Overtime and shift bonuses are common in residential care settings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as physiotherapy assistants in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a single permit (work and residence combined) to work as a physiotherapy assistant. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable, as it requires a higher-education degree and a salary threshold (€52,056 in 2024). This occupation typically requires a vocational diploma. The single permit process involves employer sponsorship and labor market testing.

**Q: What are the main duties of a physiotherapy assistant in Belgium?**
A: Physiotherapy assistants in Belgium support licensed physiotherapists by preparing treatment areas, assisting with exercises, applying modalities (heat, cold, ultrasound), and monitoring patient progress. They document sessions and educate patients on home exercises. Work settings include hospitals, rehabilitation centers, nursing homes, and private clinics. They work under supervision and cannot perform independent assessments.
