## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for vocational education teachers in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by community (Flemish, French, German-speaking) and experience. Starting salaries for a full-time vocational teacher range from €2,800 to €3,500 gross per month. With seniority, this can rise to €4,500–€5,500. Additional allowances may apply for specialized subjects or responsibilities.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for vocational education teachers in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card or a single work permit. Requirements include a valid job offer from a recognized Belgian institution, a bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in the relevant field, and a salary meeting the national threshold. Qualifications must be recognized by the competent community authority. The employer must also prove no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for vocational education teachers in Belgium?**
A: Teachers can progress to head of department, pedagogical advisor, or school director. Further specialization in areas like dual learning (werkplekleren) or adult education is possible. Continuing professional development is encouraged, and some teachers move into curriculum design or inspection roles in the relevant community education ministry.
