## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bank teller in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, bank tellers typically earn between €2,200 and €2,800 gross per month, depending on experience and location. Benefits often include meal vouchers, a 13th month salary, and holiday pay. Collective bargaining agreements in the banking sector set minimum wage scales, and salaries may be higher in Brussels.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for foreign bank tellers in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as bank tellers via a single permit (work permit B) if the employer proves no suitable EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is an option but requires a gross annual salary of at least €60,812 (2024), which is significantly higher than typical teller pay. Applicants must hold a recognized diploma.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for bank tellers in Belgium?**
A: Bank tellers in Belgium can advance to roles such as customer service representative, personal banker, or branch manager. Professional training through Febelfin or banking certifications enhances prospects. Many banks offer internal mobility, and knowledge of Dutch, French, or German is advantageous. Job stability is strong, with opportunities in both retail and business banking.
