## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for nursing professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for nurses in Belgium vary by experience and region. Entry-level gross monthly salaries range from €2,500 to €3,000, while experienced nurses can earn up to €4,500 or more, depending on seniority, shift work, and public vs. private sector employment. Additional allowances for night shifts and weekends are common.

**Q: What visa routes are available for foreign nurses to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nurses can work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or the single permit (combined work and residence). Both require a job offer, recognition of nursing qualifications by the Belgian competent authority, and compliance with language requirements (French, Dutch, or German depending on the region).

**Q: What qualifications and recognition are needed to practice as a nurse in Belgium?**
A: Foreign nurses must have their nursing diploma recognized by the Belgian federal government (SPF Santé Publique). This involves submitting credentials, proof of language proficiency (B2 level in French, Dutch, or German), and possibly completing a bridging program. Registration with the local nursing council (Ordre des Infirmiers or equivalent) is also mandatory.
