## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically diagnose common network issues and provide solutions
- Automatically generate network configuration and patch scripts
- Record work orders and categorize/escalate to senior teams
- User password reset and account management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI log analysis to quickly locate fault root causes
- Using network simulation tools to predict change impact.
- Early detection of anomalies through AI-driven monitoring.
- Using natural language queries to accelerate knowledge base resolution

**Human moat:**

- Root cause analysis and manual repair of complex faults
- Network architecture design and security policy formulation
- Effective communication with non-technical users
- Prioritizing urgent matters under pressure

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, gross monthly salaries for this role typically range from €2,800 to €4,500, depending on experience, sector, and region. Junior technicians may start around €2,500, while senior experts can earn up to €5,500. Employers often add benefits like meal vouchers and transport allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a Computer Network and Systems Technician to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work via the single permit or the EU Blue Card for highly skilled workers. The Blue Card requires a job offer meeting a salary threshold (e.g., at least €49,995 gross annually for 2024). Employers must typically prove the position cannot be filled locally. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are most valued for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Belgium?**
A: Employers seek a bachelor's degree in IT or networking, plus certifications like Cisco CCNA, CompTIA Network+, or Microsoft certifications. Practical experience with network infrastructure, security, and cloud services is highly valued. Dutch or French proficiency may be required depending on the region.
