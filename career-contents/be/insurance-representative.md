## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Insurance Representative in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for Insurance Representatives in Belgium vary by experience, region, and commission structure. A typical monthly gross salary ranges from €2,500 to €4,500, with senior roles or high performance earning more. Bonuses and commissions can significantly increase total compensation.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route to become an Insurance Representative in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Insurance Representatives in Belgium via the single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a gross annual salary meeting the Belgian threshold (updated yearly). Employers must also prove no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Insurance Representative in Belgium?**
A: Insurance Representatives in Belgium need at least a secondary education diploma; a bachelor's in insurance or finance is advantageous. They must register with the Financial Services and Markets Authority (FSMA) and complete mandatory training. Experience in sales or client service is often required, and multilingualism (Dutch, French, English) is a strong asset.
