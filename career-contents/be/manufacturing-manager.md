## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Manufacturing Manager in Belgium?**
A: The gross annual salary for a manufacturing manager in Belgium typically ranges from €70,000 to €120,000, depending on experience, company size, and sector. Benefits often include a company car, bonus, and meal vouchers. Salaries are usually higher in Brussels or for roles in chemicals or pharmaceuticals.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a foreign Manufacturing Manager to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU manufacturing managers can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Belgian average gross annual salary (threshold updated annually). Alternatively, the single permit (work and residence) is available for highly skilled workers through employer sponsorship, with no specific salary floor for managers.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are typically required to become a Manufacturing Manager in Belgium?**
A: A master's degree in engineering, business administration, or a related field is common. Most managers have at least 5–10 years of experience in manufacturing, often starting as production supervisors. Fluency in Dutch or French is essential, with English widely used in multinational companies. Lean Six Sigma certification is a plus.
