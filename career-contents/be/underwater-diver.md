## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an underwater diver in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Belgium vary by experience and specialization. Entry-level divers may earn around €2,500–€3,000 gross per month, while experienced commercial divers can earn €3,500–€5,000 or more. Hourly rates for freelance work range from €30 to €70. Offshore and deep-sea divers typically receive additional allowances.

**Q: What is the work visa route for an underwater diver to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU divers can work in Belgium via the single permit (combined work and residence permit) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The employer must obtain a work permit, proving no suitable Belgian/EU candidate is available. For the Blue Card, a recognized diploma and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Belgian wage are required.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as an underwater diver in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, commercial divers need at least a Class II or III diploma from a recognized training institution, such as the Centre de Formation de Plongée (CFP) or the Hogere Zeevaartschool. Additional certifications like HSE (UK) or ADCI (US) may be accepted. Divers must also pass a medical exam and register with the FOD Werkgelegenheid.
