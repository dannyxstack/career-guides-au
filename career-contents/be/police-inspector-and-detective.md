## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a police inspector or detective in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, police inspectors and detectives earn between €2,800 and €5,200 gross per month, depending on seniority and grade. Starting salaries are around €2,800, while experienced high-ranking inspectors can earn up to €5,200. Annual bonuses and allowances, such as shift or risk premiums, may increase total compensation.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as police inspectors or detectives in Belgium through the EU Blue Card or work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card typically targets highly skilled workers and is not applicable for police occupations, which require Belgian or EU citizenship for most roles. Non-EU nationals may only work in specialized support roles (e.g., forensic experts) via a single permit work visa if sponsored by a police service, but regular police inspector positions are restricted.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a police inspector in Belgium?**
A: Candidates must hold Belgian nationality, be at least 18, and have a clean criminal record. A bachelor's degree in a relevant field is preferred. Recruits pass a competitive exam, psychological tests, and physical fitness assessments, followed by an 18-month training at a police academy. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German (depending on region) is mandatory.
