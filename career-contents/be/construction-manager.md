## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a construction manager in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for construction managers in Belgium vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €3,500 gross per month, while experienced managers can earn up to €7,000 or more. Additional benefits often include a company car, fuel card, and performance bonuses. Employers also contribute to social security and pension plans.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU construction manager to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU construction managers can apply for the EU Blue Card, which requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Belgian salary (around €67,000 annually as of 2024). Alternatively, the single permit combines work and residence authorization. Both require employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a construction manager in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in civil engineering, construction management, or a related field is required. Professional experience and certification such as the 'Erkend Veiligheidscoördinator' (recognized safety coordinator) can enhance prospects. In Wallonia and Brussels, specific regional certifications may be necessary. Fluent Dutch, French, or German is often essential.
