## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a psychologist in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the average gross monthly salary for a psychologist ranges from €2,800 to €4,500 depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and specialization. Clinical psychologists in hospitals or private practice may earn on the higher end, while those in entry-level positions start around €2,500. Additional benefits often include healthcare and pension contributions.

**Q: How can a non-EU psychologist obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU psychologists can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a recognized master's degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €46,568/year or €37,705 for shortage occupations). Alternatively, a single-permit (work visa) is possible via employer sponsorship. Both require approval from the regional employment office, and diplomas must be recognized by the Belgian authorities.

**Q: What are the career prospects for psychologists in Belgium?**
A: Psychologists in Belgium work in clinical, organizational, school, and research settings. Demand is steady, especially in mental health care. Dutch/French language proficiency is often required for client-facing roles. Career progression includes specialization (e.g., neuropsychology), private practice, or managerial positions. Continuous professional development is encouraged.
