## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Child Care Services Manager in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for Child Care Services Managers in Belgium vary by region and experience. On average, gross monthly pay ranges from €3,500 to €5,500. Additional benefits such as meal vouchers and hospital insurance are common. Senior managers or those in larger organisations may earn up to €6,500.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Child Care Services Manager in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Belgium if they have a recognised higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€67,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a single permit for employed work may apply if the role is regulated.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Child Care Services Manager in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in early childhood education, social work, or management is required. Relevant experience in childcare and leadership skills are essential. In Wallonia and Brussels, certification as a 'coordinateur/trice d'accueil' may be needed. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German is expected depending on the region.
