## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the gross monthly salary for a medical imaging technician typically ranges from €2,800 to €4,200, depending on experience, sector (public or private), and region. Salaries are often supplemented with shift allowances and benefits such as hospital insurance. Collective bargaining agreements apply in most healthcare settings.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU Medical Imaging Technicians to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU technicians can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a binding job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (€65,429 gross/year in 2024) and a recognized higher education degree or five years of professional experience. Alternatively, a single permit (work + residence) is possible for shortage occupations. Recognition of your diploma by the Belgian competent authority (e.g., for the profession of 'technologue en imagerie médicale') is mandatory.

**Q: What typical career progression opportunities exist for Medical Imaging Technicians in Belgium?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior or specialized roles in MRI, CT, nuclear medicine, or radiotherapy. With additional training, they may become team coordinators, department heads, or educators in university hospitals. Some pursue further studies to become radiologists (medical doctor) or clinical engineers, though this requires longer academic pathways.
