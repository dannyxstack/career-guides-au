- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €2,000 gross per month, while experienced technicians can earn up to €3,500. The median gross monthly salary is approximately €2,500 to €3,000. Public sector roles may offer different scales.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Medical Records and Health Information Technicians to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals need a Single Permit (work and residence). The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the Belgian average gross annual salary. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a secondary education diploma in medical administration or a related field is required. Knowledge of medical terminology, data protection (GDPR), and electronic health records systems is essential. Some employers prefer a bachelor's in health information management. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German may be required depending on the region.
