## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions earn around €35,000-€45,000 annually, while senior roles can exceed €70,000. Collective bargaining agreements (CCTs) set minimums per sector. Bonuses and benefits like company cars are common. Gross monthly salaries often range €2,800-€5,000.

**Q: What are the work visa options for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a single permit combining work and residence, or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified roles requiring a bachelor’s degree and salary threshold (€47,909 in 2024). Employers must prove labor market need. The Professional Card is for self-employed roles.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Personnel and Careers Professional in Belgium?**
A: A bachelor’s or master’s degree in HR, psychology, or business administration is common. Certifications like HRM or career coaching (e.g., VDAB) are valued. Membership in professional bodies such as HRPro (Flanders) or Solvay HR School enhances credibility. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German is crucial depending on region.
