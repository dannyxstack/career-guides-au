## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary ranges from €3,500 to €5,500 depending on experience and sector. Public sector roles follow PPS salary scales, while private sector positions may offer additional benefits. Salaries vary by region (Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels) and are often higher for senior or specialized inspectors.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card routes are available for non-EU nationals to work as Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Single Permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card for highly skilled workers. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card requires a binding job offer meeting the applicable salary threshold set by Belgian law.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Belgium?**
A: Career progression includes moving to senior inspector, team leader, or policy advisor roles in government agencies such as the Flemish Agency for Care and Health or the federal FPS Employment. Specialization in industrial hygiene, risk management, or consultancy is common, with some inspectors transitioning to private sector advisory roles.
