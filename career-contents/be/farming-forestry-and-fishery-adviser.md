## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers typically earn a gross monthly salary between €3,500 and €5,500, depending on experience, employer, and region. Senior advisers or those with specialized expertise may earn higher. Salaries are often complemented by benefits such as a company car, meal vouchers, and insurance.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card to work as a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Belgium. Requirements include a valid job offer matching the ISCO-08 2132 classification, a recognized higher education degree, and a salary meeting the annual gross threshold (set by the Belgian government, e.g., approximately €56,000 in 2024). The employer must also register the position with the regional employment office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a Master’s degree in agricultural sciences, forestry, fisheries, or a related field is required. Some positions may accept a Bachelor’s degree combined with significant experience. Fluency in Dutch or French is essential, along with knowledge of regional agricultural policies. Continuing education and familiarity with sustainable practices are valued.
