## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dental assistants and therapists in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for dental assistants and therapists in Belgium vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions typically earn €2,000–€2,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €3,500. The salary is often supplemented by benefits such as meal vouchers and transportation allowance. Collective bargaining agreements also influence pay scales.

**Q: Can dental assistants and therapists from outside the EU work in Belgium under the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU dental assistants and therapists can apply for an EU Blue Card in Belgium if they have a valid job offer, meet the salary threshold (around €48,000 gross/year in 2025), and their qualifications are recognized by the Belgian competent authority. A work permit application must also be submitted by the employer. The Blue Card allows for family reunification and mobility within the EU.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a dental assistant or therapist in Belgium?**
A: To work as a dental assistant in Belgium, a secondary school diploma or a recognized professional qualification in dental assisting is typically required. For dental therapists, a bachelor's degree in dental therapy is necessary. Both professions may need to register with the Federal Public Service Health and, for non-EU nationals, have credentials evaluated by the Naric Centre.
