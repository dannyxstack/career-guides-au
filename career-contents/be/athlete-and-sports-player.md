## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for professional athletes in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sport and level. Top footballers in the Pro League may earn €300,000 to €1 million annually, while athletes in less popular sports often earn modest amounts, sometimes supplemented by other jobs. Many semi-professionals receive only expenses or small stipends.

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU athletes coming to Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professional athletes typically apply for a single permit (combined work and residence permit) through their Belgian employer, such as a sports club. The employer must prove the athlete's exceptional skills and that the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is not the standard route for athletes.

**Q: What career transitions are common for athletes in Belgium after retirement?**
A: Many retired athletes move into coaching, sports management, or physiotherapy. Some leverage their networks in sports administration or media. Belgium supports dual careers for student-athletes, and government programs aid transitions, including retraining and education opportunities.
