## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Belgium?**
A: Hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Belgium, such as bicycle couriers, typically earn between €12 and €15 per hour gross. Full-time monthly gross salaries range from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on the employer, city, and experience. Tips and bonuses may supplement income. Pay can also be based on deliveries completed.

**Q: What are the work-visa routes for hand and pedal vehicle drivers coming to Belgium from outside the EU?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for hand and pedal vehicle drivers as it requires a highly skilled job with a university degree and a salary above €56,089 (2024). The main route is the Single Permit for employed workers, but this is subject to a labor market test proving no suitable EU candidate. Self-employment may be possible if meeting specific criteria. Official routes are limited for this occupation.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Belgium?**
A: Career advancement is limited but possible. Drivers may progress to dispatch or logistics coordination roles within courier companies. Others become self-employed or join larger firms offering benefits. Gaining skills in first aid, advanced cycling, or route planning can improve opportunities. Some transition to delivery management or operations roles with experience.
