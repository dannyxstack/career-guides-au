## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for an astrologer or fortune-teller in Belgium?**
A: Income varies widely as most practitioners are self-employed. Monthly net earnings typically range from €1,500 to €3,000, depending on client base and location. Some may earn less, especially when starting out. There is no standardized pay scale.

**Q: Can non-EU astrologers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Belgium?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers with a university degree and high salary, so astrologers rarely qualify. Other routes include the single permit for employed work (if sponsored by an employer) or a professional card for self-employment. Both require demonstrating sufficient resources and a viable business plan.

**Q: What are the career prospects for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Belgium?**
A: Career prospects are limited and niche. Most practitioners are self-employed, relying on word-of-mouth and online presence. There are no formal certification requirements, but building trust is crucial. Competition from online services and apps is growing. Specializing in areas like business astrology can help differentiate.
