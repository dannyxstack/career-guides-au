## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for carpenters and joiners in Belgium?**
A: According to sectoral data, entry-level carpenters and joiners in Belgium earn around €2,000-€2,500 gross per month, while experienced workers can earn €3,000-€3,800. Wages vary by region, experience, and whether working in construction or specialized joinery. Collective bargaining agreements (PC 124) set minimum pay scales.

**Q: Can carpenters and joiners from outside the EU obtain a work visa for Belgium?**
A: Yes, via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers or the single permit for other skilled workers. Carpenter/joiner is not a regulated profession in Belgium, but employers must demonstrate labour market shortage (test) unless the role is on the shortage occupation list. The application requires a valid job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds.

**Q: What certifications are needed to work as a carpenter or joiner in Belgium?**
A: No mandatory national certification, but employers often prefer a CFC (Certificat de Formation Continue) or equivalent vocational diploma. In Brussels and Wallonia, the 'Carte de compétence' (competence card) may be requested. Safety training (VCA) is required on construction sites. Foreign qualifications must be recognized via NARIC or equivalence process.
