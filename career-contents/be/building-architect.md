## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building architect in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a mid-level building architect in Belgium ranges from €3,500 to €5,000, depending on experience, region, and firm size. Starting salaries are typically around €2,800, while senior architects can earn up to €6,500 or more. Salaries are often higher in Brussels and Flanders.

**Q: Can a non-EU building architect work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as a building architect in Belgium through the EU Blue Card. Requirements include a recognized degree in architecture (minimum 3 years), a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€59,850 in 2024), and registration with the Belgian Order of Architects. Applications are submitted to the regional employment authority.

**Q: What are the registration requirements for building architects in Belgium?**
A: To practice as a building architect in Belgium, you must register with the Ordre des Architectes (Order of Architects). This requires a recognized master's degree in architecture, successful completion of a professional traineeship (stage), and passing the professional aptitude exam. Registration is mandatory for using the title 'architect' and for signing architectural plans.
