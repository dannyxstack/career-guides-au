## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for creative and performing artists in Belgium?**
A: Incomes vary widely; many work freelance. Median annual earnings for self-employed artists are around €35,000 (StatBel). Project-based work is common, with some earning €25,000–€50,000. Public subsidies and grants supplement income. Social security contributions are based on declared earnings.

**Q: What work visa options are available for creative and performing artists to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU artists can apply for the EU Blue Card (requires a job offer meeting the salary threshold, €56,443 gross in 2025) or a single permit (combines work and residence). Self-employed artists need a professional card from their region. Official routes include the single permit and Blue Card. Always consult the Immigration Office.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for creative and performing artists in Belgium?**
A: Belgium offers a vibrant cultural scene with roles in theatre, dance, music, visual arts, and digital media. Many work freelance, supported by public funding (e.g., Flemish Arts Council). Tax incentives and social security schemes for artists exist. Networking through local cultural organizations is key. Opportunities in Brussels are especially diverse.
