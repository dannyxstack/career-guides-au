## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for deep-sea fishery workers in Belgium vary by experience and vessel. Entry-level workers earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while experienced workers can earn €2,500–€3,500. Pay often includes profit-sharing or bonuses based on catch. Employers contribute to social security.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for deep-sea fishery workers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card if they meet salary and qualification criteria. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree or equivalent professional experience. Employers must demonstrate labor market shortages. Seafarer-specific visas may also apply through Belgian maritime regulations.

**Q: What are the requirements and working conditions for deep-sea fishery workers in Belgium?**
A: No formal degree is mandatory, but basic safety training (STCW) and medical fitness are required. Workers often start as deckhands. Trips last 1–3 weeks, with long hours. Belgian vessels fish in the North Sea and Atlantic. Knowledge of Dutch or French is helpful but not always required for international crews.
