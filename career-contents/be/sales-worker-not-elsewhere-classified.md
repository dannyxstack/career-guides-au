## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sales workers in Belgium ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while experienced workers can earn up to €4,000. Commission-based roles may offer higher total compensation.

**Q: Can I work as a sales worker in Belgium with an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Sales workers generally need a single permit (work and residence). The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled roles requiring a degree and a job offer with a gross annual salary of at least €45,552 (2024 threshold). For lower-skilled sales positions, a single permit may be issued if the employer proves a labor market shortage.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for sales workers in Belgium?**
A: Sales workers can advance to senior sales roles, team leader, or account manager. Further training in business or management can lead to positions in sales management or business development. Some transition to marketing or customer service management.
