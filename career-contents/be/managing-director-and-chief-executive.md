## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Managing Director or Chief Executive in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and company size, but median annual gross pay for top executives ranges from €150,000 to €250,000. Bonuses and benefits like company cars and stock options are common. Specific data can be found via job boards or salary surveys, as official statistics may not be publicly detailed.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Managing Directors to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU executives can apply for the EU Blue Card, requiring a high-skilled job with a salary threshold (€60,697 in 2024). Alternatively, the single permit combines work and residence. For intra-company transfers, the ICT permit is available. All routes require employer sponsorship and meet specific criteria set by Belgian immigration authorities.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are typically required for a Managing Director role in Belgium?**
A: Most positions require at least a master’s degree in business, economics, or a related field, along with 10–15 years of progressive management experience. Fluency in English is essential, while Dutch and/or French are often required depending on the region. Professional certifications like an MBA or executive education are advantageous.
