## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shop supervisor in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, shop supervisors typically earn a gross monthly salary between €2,500 and €3,500, depending on experience, company size, and region. This often includes benefits such as meal vouchers, 13th-month pay, and holiday allowance. Collective bargaining agreements (PC 312 for retail) set minimum wages, which vary by age and seniority.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a shop supervisor in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified workers and requires a bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) and a gross annual salary above €46,512 (2024 threshold). A shop supervisor role may qualify if it demands managerial responsibilities and meets the salary requirement. Otherwise, a standard single permit (work and residence) is the route, requiring employer proof of labor market shortage.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shop supervisors in Belgium?**
A: Shop supervisors in Belgium can advance to store manager, area manager, or regional manager roles. Further opportunities include buying, merchandising, or operations management within retail chains. Some pursue training in retail management (e.g., via Syntra or VDAB) to accelerate promotion. Experience in large stores or luxury brands can also lead to higher-level positions.
