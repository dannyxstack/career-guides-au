## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Sales Professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for ICT sales professionals in Belgium vary by experience and company. Entry-level roles start around €40,000 per year, while senior professionals can earn €70,000 or more, plus commissions and benefits like a company car. The median salary is approximately €55,000 annually, according to industry surveys.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for ICT Sales Professionals to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work via the Single Permit (combined work/residence) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Belgian gross salary (e.g., around €75,000 in 2024). The Single Permit applies for high-skilled roles, often requiring a recognized diploma or five years' experience. Employer sponsorship is mandatory for both routes.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ICT Sales Professionals in Belgium?**
A: ICT sales professionals can advance to senior roles like Key Account Manager, Sales Director, or Vice President of Sales. Specialization in cloud, cybersecurity, or enterprise solutions is common. Many pursue certifications (e.g., AWS, Cisco) or MBA degrees. The Belgian tech sector offers strong demand, with opportunities to transition into partner management or pre-sales technical roles.
