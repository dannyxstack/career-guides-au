## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Belgium?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Belgium usually earn around the statutory minimum wage, which in 2024 is approximately €1,995.54 gross per month for full-time work. Actual pay can vary by employer and region, but generally ranges from €1,800 to €2,200 gross monthly. Tips or meal benefits may supplement income.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a kitchen helper in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally not suitable for kitchen helpers, as it requires a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience, plus a salary above a high threshold (around €60,000 annually in 2024). The main route for non-EU kitchen helpers is the single permit (work permit B), which employers can request after proving a labor shortage.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for kitchen helpers in Belgium?**
A: Kitchen helpers often work in restaurants, hotels, or catering services. Shifts include evenings, weekends, and holidays, with part-time or full-time contracts. The work is physically demanding, involving cleaning, washing dishes, and basic food prep. Employers must comply with Belgian labor laws on working hours and rest periods.
