## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a domestic housekeeper in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, domestic housekeepers often work via the service voucher system (titres-services), earning a net hourly wage of around €10-12. Average gross monthly salaries range from €1,800 to €2,200, depending on experience and region. Benefits may include meal vouchers, holiday pay, and end-of-year bonuses.

**Q: Do domestic housekeepers qualify for an EU Blue Card or work visa in Belgium?**
A: The EU Blue Card targets highly skilled workers with a higher education degree, so it is not applicable for domestic housekeepers. Non-EU workers may obtain a single permit (work visa) if a Belgian employer proves a labour shortage, but this is rare for domestic roles due to low skill requirements. Alternative routes are limited.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for domestic housekeepers in Belgium?**
A: Domestic housekeepers can advance to team leader or supervisor roles in cleaning companies. With further training, they may specialize in eco-cleaning, carpet care, or move into hotel housekeeping. Some become self-employed offering private cleaning services. Formal education, such as a vocational course in cleaning management, can open doors to facility management.
