## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a town and traffic planner in Belgium?**
A: Mid-level town and traffic planners in Belgium earn a gross monthly salary between €3,500 and €5,500, varying by experience, sector (public or private), and region. Entry-level salaries start around €2,800, while senior roles can exceed €6,000. Benefits often include 13th month pay, meal vouchers, and performance bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU town planners to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU town planners can apply for a work permit (single permit) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a high-skilled job with a salary of at least €50,352 (2024 threshold) and a valid employment contract. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled by an EU worker. A residence permit follows approval.

**Q: What are the career prospects for town and traffic planners in Belgium?**
A: Career prospects are solid due to ongoing urban development, mobility projects, and sustainability initiatives. Planners often work for municipalities, regional authorities, or private consultancies. Advancement to senior planner, project manager, or policy advisor roles is common. A master’s degree in urban planning or a related field is typically required, and Dutch or French proficiency is advantageous.
