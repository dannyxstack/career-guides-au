## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fashion models in Belgium?**
A: Fashion models in Belgium typically work freelance, earning daily rates that vary widely based on experience and assignment. Entry-level models may earn €500-€1,500 per day, while established models can earn €2,000-€5,000 or more. Agencies often take a commission of 20-30%. Income is irregular and seasonal.

**Q: Can fashion models obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Belgium?**
A: Yes, non-EU models can work in Belgium via a single permit (combined work and residence permit) obtained by their employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Belgium, which may not apply to most models. A standard single permit is the common route.

**Q: What are the career prospects for fashion models in Belgium?**
A: Career prospects vary. Most models work on short-term contracts, often with limited duration. Successful models may transition into related fields like fashion styling, photography, or acting. The market is competitive, with many opportunities in Antwerp due to its fashion scene. Agencies like Dominique Models and IMM Bruxelles are key players.
