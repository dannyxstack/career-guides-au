## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for window cleaners in Belgium vary by experience and region but generally range from €1,800 to €2,500 gross per month for full-time work. Collective bargaining agreements may apply. Self-employed cleaners often charge per job, earning potentially more but with variable income. Social benefits and overtime pay are regulated by Belgian labor law.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a window cleaner in Belgium via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as window cleaners in Belgium through a single permit (combined work and residence) if an employer obtains a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation as it requires higher education or high-skilled status. Alternatively, the professional card for self-employment may be an option for independent cleaners.

**Q: What are the typical career progression opportunities for window cleaners in Belgium?**
A: Window cleaners in Belgium can advance to supervisory roles, start their own cleaning business, or specialize in high-rise or industrial cleaning. Training in safety and equipment use (e.g., rope access) can lead to higher pay. The VDAB and other regional employment services offer courses. Experience and certifications in hazardous environments open further opportunities.
