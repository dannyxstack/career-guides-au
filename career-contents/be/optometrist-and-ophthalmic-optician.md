## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an optometrist or ophthalmic optician in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an optometrist in Belgium ranges from €2,800 to €4,000, depending on experience and region. Self-employed practitioners may earn higher but with variable income. Benefits often include extra vacation pay and a 13th month salary. Salary scales are influenced by collective labor agreements (CLA) and are updated annually.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU optometrists to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€51,535 in 2024). Diplomas must be recognized by the Belgian competent authority (e.g., for health professions). The employer typically initiates the process.

**Q: What are the registration requirements for optometrists in Belgium?**
A: Optometrists must hold a recognized diploma (Bachelor or Master) and register with the Federal Public Service (FPS) Health, Food Chain Safety and Environment. For ophthalmic opticians, registration with the Order of Opticians is required. Continuing education is mandatory. Proficiency in Dutch, French, or German is essential for patient communication and legal compliance.
