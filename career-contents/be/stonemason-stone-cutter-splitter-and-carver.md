## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a stonemason in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, stonemasons earn between €2,200 and €3,500 gross per month depending on experience and region. Entry-level workers start around €2,000, while experienced specialists can reach €3,800. The pay is often determined by collective bargaining agreements (PC 124 for construction).

**Q: What is the work visa process for foreign stonemasons in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU stonemasons need a single permit (combined work and residence) or EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (€49,995+ in 2024). The Belgian employer must prove no suitable EU candidate is available. Official routes include the single permit for high-skilled or shortage occupations.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for stonemasons in Belgium?**
A: Stonemasons in Belgium can advance to head mason, restoration specialist, or project foreman. With experience, they may start their own business (independent craftsman) or move into heritage conservation, given Belgium's high demand for restoration of historic buildings. Further specialization in carving or CNC stone cutting is also common.
