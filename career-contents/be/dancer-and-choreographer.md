## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dancers and choreographers in Belgium?**
A: Dancers and choreographers in Belgium often work freelance or on fixed-term contracts. According to sector data, choreographers in cultural institutions earn between €30,000 and €45,000 annually. Dancers may receive per-performance fees or monthly salaries ranging from €1,800 to €3,000 gross, but income varies significantly based on project funding and experience.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU dancers and choreographers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU dancers and choreographers typically need a single permit (work and residence) obtained by an employer. For highly skilled roles, the EU Blue Card applies if the job requires higher education and meets a salary threshold. Alternatively, the 'artist visa' (professional card for cultural workers) is available for specific performances or projects, with the employer proving the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What are the main employment sectors for dancers and choreographers in Belgium?**
A: The primary sectors include dance companies such as the Royal Ballet of Flanders and Charleroi Danse, opera houses, theater productions, and freelance teaching. Belgium also has a strong contemporary dance scene with project-based work funded by regional subsidies. Many choreographers also work in education or community arts, while dancers frequently combine performance with teaching or choreography.
