## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a tailor or dressmaker in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, tailors and dressmakers earn an average gross monthly salary of €2,000 to €2,800. Entry-level salaries start at €1,800, while experienced professionals can earn up to €3,200. Self-employed tailors have variable income depending on clientele. Regional differences exist, with Brussels and Flanders typically offering higher wages.

**Q: Can a non-EU tailor obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU tailors can work in Belgium via the single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a gross annual salary of at least €68,029 (2024) or €54,423 for regulated professions like skilled crafts. The employer must demonstrate the vacancy cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a tailor in Belgium?**
A: To become a tailor in Belgium, formal vocational training is common, such as a secondary education certificate in clothing technology or a three-year apprenticeship. Knowledge of Dutch, French, or German is advantageous. Self-employed tailors must register with a business counter and obtain a professional card if indicated. Continuing education courses are offered by sectoral funds.
