## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an announcer in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for announcers in Belgium vary based on experience and platform. Entry-level radio or TV announcers earn around €2,500 to €3,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals may earn €3,500 to €4,500. Public broadcasters often offer higher pay. Freelance announcers' incomes fluctuate.

**Q: Can non-EU announcers work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Non-EU announcers generally need a single permit (work and residence) sponsored by a Belgian employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers meeting a salary threshold (€60,847 in 2024, or €48,300 for shortage occupations). Announcers rarely qualify unless in senior roles or specialized fields. A standard work permit is more common.

**Q: What is the typical career path for an announcer in Belgium?**
A: Many start with a degree in communication, journalism, or radio/TV arts. Internships at public broadcasters (VRT, RTBF) or local stations are common. Announcers often begin with voice-over or assistant roles, then progress to hosting shows. Networking and multilingualism (French, Dutch, English) are key for advancement.
