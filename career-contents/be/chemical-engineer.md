## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a chemical engineer in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, entry-level chemical engineers earn around €3,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €6,000 or more. Salaries vary by sector, with petrochemicals and pharmaceuticals often offering higher pay. Benefits include bonuses, company cars, and meal vouchers.

**Q: Can non-EU chemical engineers work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU chemical engineers can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer in Belgium and meet the salary threshold. Requirements include a recognized higher education degree and a work contract for at least one year. The employer must obtain a work permit. Details are managed by the regional employment services.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for chemical engineers in Belgium?**
A: Belgium has a strong chemical and pharmaceutical industry, with major hubs in Antwerp and Wallonia. Chemical engineers often work in process design, R&D, manufacturing, or environmental management. The sector offers growth, and knowledge of French/Dutch is beneficial for local roles.
