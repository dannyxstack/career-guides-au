**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI scheduling systems to optimize material handling routes and sequences

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a freight handler in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a freight handler in Belgium ranges from €2,000 to €2,600, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while experienced workers can earn up to €3,000. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, transport allowance, and end-of-year bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as freight handlers in Belgium under the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Belgium is intended for highly skilled workers, typically requiring a higher education degree and a salary threshold (€49,995 in 2024). Freight handler roles rarely meet these criteria. Alternatively, non-EU workers may apply through the single permit for low-skilled labor, but this is subject to labor market tests and quotas.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for freight handlers in Belgium?**
A: Freight handlers can advance to team leader, warehouse supervisor, or logistics coordinator roles with experience. Belgian employers often offer internal training for forklift certification or inventory management. Further education, such as a VPL (competency assessment) or logistics courses, can lead to managerial positions in distribution centers.
