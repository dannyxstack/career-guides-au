## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned officer in the Belgian armed forces?**
A: Salaries are determined by military pay scales from the Ministry of Defence. A starting non-commissioned officer earns a gross monthly salary of around €2,500, increasing with rank and years of service. Additional allowances for specializations, hazard pay, and housing may apply.

**Q: Can a non-EU national obtain an EU Blue Card to work as a non-commissioned officer in Belgium?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not applicable for military roles. Belgian armed forces typically require Belgian or EU/EEA nationality for non-commissioned officer positions. Non-EU nationals may only work in civilian support functions via a single permit or highly skilled migrant permit, provided they meet the relevant education and experience criteria.

**Q: What training is required to become a non-commissioned officer in Belgium?**
A: Candidates must complete basic military training, followed by the Non-Commissioned Officer School at the Royal Military School. The program includes theoretical and practical instruction in leadership, tactics, and specialized skills. Total training duration is typically one to two years, depending on the specialization.
