## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a technical or medical sales professional in Belgium?**
A: Gross monthly salaries typically range from €3,000 to €6,000, varying with experience, sector (e.g., pharma, industrial), and region. Commissions and benefits like company cars are common. Entry-level positions start around €2,500, while senior roles can exceed €7,000.

**Q: What work visa options exist for technical and medical sales professionals in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for a Single Permit (combining work and residence) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Both require a valid job offer and employer efforts to recruit locally. For the Blue Card, the gross annual salary must meet the threshold (at least 1.5 times the average Belgian salary).

**Q: What qualifications and career progression are typical for this role in Belgium?**
A: A bachelor's or master's degree in engineering, life sciences, or a related field is typical, along with sales experience. Multilingualism (French, Dutch, English) is highly valued. Career paths lead to senior account manager, key account manager, or sales director positions.
