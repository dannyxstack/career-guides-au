## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Belgium ranges from €4,500 to €7,000, depending on experience, company size, and region. Senior managers in large logistics firms can earn over €9,000. Benefits often include a company car, fuel card, and meal vouchers.

**Q: Can foreign Supply, Distribution and Related Managers work in Belgium via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Belgium if they have a valid work contract for at least 6 months, a salary meeting the threshold (currently €57,557 gross/year, 2024), and recognized higher education or 5+ years of professional experience. The employer must sponsor the permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Supply, Distribution and Related Manager in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in logistics, supply chain management, or business administration is required. Professional experience in supply chain roles is crucial. Certifications like CSCP or APICS are valued. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German is often necessary depending on the region.
