## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a ships' engineer in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for ships' engineers in Belgium vary based on experience, type of vessel, and employer. Generally, junior engineers earn between €2,800 and €3,500 gross per month, while senior engineers can earn €4,000 to €5,500. Engineers working on internationally flagged vessels may have different pay scales. Additional allowances for sea time and overtime apply.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU ships' engineers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU ships' engineers can apply for a single permit combining work and residence, or the EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. €63,000 gross annually in 2024). Alternatively, a professional card may be required for self-employment. All routes require employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for ships' engineers in Belgium?**
A: Ships' engineers in Belgium can advance to chief engineer or technical superintendent roles. They may also transition to shore-based positions in maritime consultancy, port engineering, or classification societies. Belgian maritime academies offer continuous professional development courses. Engineers with extra qualifications can work on specialized vessels like dredgers or offshore support ships.
