## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for bus and tram drivers in Belgium ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience, employer, and region. Public transport companies like STIB/MIVB in Brussels offer starting salaries around €2,800. Additional benefits often include 13th month pay, meal vouchers, and transport allowances.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for bus and tram drivers to work in Belgium?**
A: The primary route is the single permit (work visa) sponsored by a Belgian employer. The EU Blue Card requires a high salary threshold (€60,000/year in 2024), which exceeds typical bus driver salaries, making it rarely applicable. Non-EU citizens need employer sponsorship and a valid job offer. Intra-company transfers or posted worker status under EU rules are also possible.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Belgium?**
A: You need a valid category D driving licence and a Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC) for passenger transport. Language proficiency in Dutch, French, or German (depending on region) is often required. Many employers provide additional training. A clean driving record and medical fitness are mandatory. Previous experience in driving is an asset.
