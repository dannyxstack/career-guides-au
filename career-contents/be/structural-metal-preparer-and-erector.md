## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a structural metal preparer and erector in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience and region. Benefits often include meal vouchers, transport allowance, and end-of-year bonuses. Wages are governed by sectoral collective bargaining (PC 124 for construction). Overtime and night work are typically compensated at higher rates.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for a foreign structural metal preparer and erector to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence permit) via the regional employment services (Actiris, Forem, VDAB). The EU Blue Card is possible if the role requires a higher education degree or five years of professional experience. A Labour Market Test is usually required. Skilled shortages in construction may ease procedures.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for structural metal preparers and erectors in Belgium?**
A: Starting as an assistant, you can advance to lead erector or team supervisor with experience. Specializations like welding or scaffolding certification increase pay. Further training can lead to project management or quality control roles. Apprenticeships via VDAB or IFAPME and certifications in safety (e.g., VCA) are common career boosters.
