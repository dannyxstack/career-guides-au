## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a spray painter and varnisher in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, a spray painter and varnisher typically earns between €2,000 and €2,800 gross per month for full-time work, depending on experience, sector, and region. Industrial sectors often pay higher wages. Collective bargaining agreements (PCs) set minimum wages, which vary by industry. Additional benefits may include meal vouchers and transportation allowances.

**Q: Can a non-EU spray painter and varnisher obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Belgium?**
A: Belgium offers several work visa routes for non-EU nationals. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a higher education degree or equivalent experience. For spray painters and varnishers, a single permit (combined work and residence) may be more applicable if a labor market test is passed and the employer demonstrates the position cannot be filled locally. Official routes include the single permit procedure and the EU Blue Card, each with specific conditions.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for spray painters and varnishers in Belgium?**
A: Spray painters and varnishers in Belgium can advance to supervisory roles, quality control, or specialized industrial painting (e.g., automotive or aerospace). With additional training, they may become coating inspectors or technical sales representatives. Vocational qualifications (like a CEDEFOP-recognized certificate) and experience in advanced techniques (e.g., electrostatic painting) enhance prospects. Some pursue self-employment or start their own painting company.
