## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for beauticians in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for beauticians in Belgium vary by experience, location, and employer. For full-time work, gross monthly pay typically ranges from €1,800 to €2,500. Entry-level workers earn around €1,800, while experienced professionals in high-end salons may earn up to €3,000. Additional tips can supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU beauticians obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Belgium?**
A: Non-EU beauticians can work in Belgium via a single permit (work permit) sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable, as it requires a highly skilled position with a higher education degree or equivalent experience, which beautician roles typically do not meet. No specific salary thresholds are set for this occupation.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a beautician in Belgium?**
A: To work as a beautician in Belgium, you typically need a recognized vocational qualification, such as a secondary school diploma in beauty care (e.g., from a CEFA or technical school). For non-EU applicants, diplomas may need equivalency recognition by the Flemish or French Community authorities. No additional licensing is required beyond standard salon registration.
