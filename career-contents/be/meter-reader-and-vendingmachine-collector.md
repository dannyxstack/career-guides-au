## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the gross monthly salary typically ranges from €1,900 to €2,500, depending on experience, employer, and region. Collective bargaining agreements may apply, and additional benefits like meal vouchers or travel allowances are common.

**Q: What work visa options are available for non-EU nationals to work as meter readers or vending-machine collectors in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Belgium via the single permit, combining work and residence, if an employer sponsors them. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and typically requires a higher education degree, which this occupation may not meet. Other routes include intra-corporate transfer or the professional card for self-employment.

**Q: What are the main duties and requirements for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Belgium?**
A: Meter readers visit residential or commercial sites to record utility usage, often using handheld devices. Vending-machine collectors refill, collect cash, and perform minor maintenance. A secondary school diploma is typical, and a driving license is often required. Physical fitness and attention to detail are important.
