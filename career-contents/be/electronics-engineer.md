## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electronics engineer in Belgium?**
A: The gross annual salary for an electronics engineer in Belgium typically ranges from €45,000 to €65,000, depending on experience, region, and industry. Entry-level positions start around €38,000, while senior roles can exceed €75,000. Benefits often include meal vouchers, insurance, and a company car.

**Q: What is the official work visa route for non-EU electronics engineers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU electronics engineers typically apply for the EU Blue Card, requiring a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (around €49,000 in 2024). Alternatively, the single permit combines work and residence. The employer must demonstrate labour market shortages. Both routes lead to temporary residence, renewable, with a path to long-term status.

**Q: Which industries employ electronics engineers in Belgium?**
A: Electronics engineers in Belgium find opportunities in telecommunications, automotive (e.g., Audi Brussels), semiconductor manufacturing (e.g., imec), renewable energy, and aerospace. Major employers include research centres, industrial automation firms, and the Port of Antwerp tech sector. Demand is high for R&D roles, embedded systems, and IoT specialists.
