## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a lawyer in Belgium?**
A: The average gross annual salary for a lawyer in Belgium ranges from €50,000 for junior associates to over €100,000 for experienced partners. Mid-level lawyers typically earn between €70,000 and €90,000. Salaries vary by region (Brussels, Flanders, Wallonia), firm size, and specialization.

**Q: How can a foreign lawyer work in Belgium under the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU lawyers can work in Belgium via a work visa or the EU Blue Card, but the legal profession is regulated. To practice as a lawyer, you must register with the local Bar Association (Ordre des Avocats) and often complete a stage (traineeship). The EU Blue Card requires a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€53,514 in 2024) and recognition of qualifications. A standard work permit (B) is also possible if the employer proves no EU candidate is available.

**Q: What is the career progression for a lawyer in Belgium?**
A: Lawyers in Belgium typically start as stagiaires (trainees) for three years, then become associates. After 5–10 years, they may become partners or senior associates. Alternatively, lawyers move to in-house counsel roles in companies or join the judiciary. Career advancement also involves specialization (e.g., corporate, criminal, international law) and continuous legal education.
