## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Human Resource Manager in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the gross annual salary for an HR Manager typically ranges from €60,000 to €90,000, depending on experience, company size, and location (Brussels often pays higher). Benefits often include meal vouchers, bonus, and company car. Collective bargaining agreements (CCT) in some sectors may set minimums.

**Q: What are the work visa options for HR Managers moving to Belgium?**
A: Non-EU HR Managers can work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles, requiring a valid work contract and salary at least 1.5 times average gross annual salary) or the Single Permit (combining work and residence). A labour market test may apply. The employer typically sponsors the permit.

**Q: What are typical career paths for HR Managers in Belgium?**
A: HR Managers in Belgium often progress to senior roles such as HR Director, Head of Talent, or CHRO. Some specialize in compensation & benefits, organizational development, or HR tech. Advanced degrees (e.g., Master in HRM) and certifications (CIPD, VVMI) boost prospects. Multilingualism (NL, FR, EN) is key for top positions.
