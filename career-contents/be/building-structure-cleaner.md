## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Belgium?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Belgium typically earn a gross monthly salary between €1,800 and €2,500, depending on experience and region. Pay is governed by collective labor agreements (PC 125). Overtime and special assignments may increase earnings. Actual rates vary by employer.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as a building structure cleaner in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers can obtain a single permit (combined work and residence) through employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is not available for this occupation due to high salary thresholds (€58,800 in 2024). Alternatively, a professional card is for self-employed cleaners. There is no specific shortage list for this role.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a building structure cleaner in Belgium?**
A: No formal degree is required, but employers often prefer experience and knowledge of cleaning methods and safety. A certificate of professional competence (e.g., from VDAB or Forem) can be helpful. Language skills in Dutch, French, or German are advantageous. On-the-job training is common.
