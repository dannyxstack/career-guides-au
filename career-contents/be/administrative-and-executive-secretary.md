## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Administrative and Executive Secretary in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for administrative and executive secretaries in Belgium typically range from €2,500 to €4,500 gross per month, depending on experience, sector, and location. Executive secretaries in senior roles or with specialized language skills often earn at the higher end. Benefits like meal vouchers and transport allowances are common.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for Administrative and Executive Secretaries in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a single permit (work and residence) sponsored by a Belgian employer. The EU Blue Card requires a high-skilled job with a salary threshold of at least €67,000 gross yearly (2024). Most administrative secretary positions do not meet this level, so the standard work visa (single permit) is the typical route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Administrative and Executive Secretaries in Belgium?**
A: With experience, secretaries can advance to office manager, executive assistant to senior management, or specialist roles in legal, medical, or diplomatic settings. Pursuing certifications in project management or advanced IT skills can open doors to higher-level administrative positions or transitions into HR or event coordination.
