## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary widely, but the average gross monthly salary for craft workers in Belgium ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on sector, experience, and region. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum wages for specific trades.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for craft and related workers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU craft workers typically need a single permit (work + residence) sponsored by an employer, based on labor market checks. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly qualified roles with a higher salary threshold, which may not apply to most craft positions.

**Q: Which sectors commonly employ craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: These workers are found in construction, manufacturing, maintenance, and repair services. Sectors include metalworking, woodworking, textiles, and food processing. Many work in small and medium-sized enterprises, often requiring vocational training or apprenticeships.
