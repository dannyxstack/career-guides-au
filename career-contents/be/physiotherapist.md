## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physiotherapists in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for physiotherapists in Belgium vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions typically earn between €2,200 and €2,800 gross per month. With experience, salaries can reach €3,500 to €4,500. Private practice owners may earn more, but incomes fluctuate. Public sector and hospital roles often follow fixed scales with additional benefits.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for physiotherapists to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Belgium via the Single Permit (combining work and residence) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Both require a job offer, recognition of professional qualifications by the Belgian government, and compliance with language requirements. The EU Blue Card also mandates a salary threshold.

**Q: What language skills are required for physiotherapists in Belgium?**
A: Belgium has three official languages: Dutch, French, and German. Depending on the region (Flanders, Wallonia, or Brussels), physiotherapists need proficiency in the local language for patient communication and registration. Typical requirements include B2 level in Dutch or French, though some employers may accept English in Brussels for international clients.
