## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a valuer or loss assessor in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for valuers and loss assessors in Belgium vary by experience and qualifications. Entry-level professionals earn around €2,800–€3,200 gross per month, while experienced assessors can earn €4,000–€5,000 or more. Certified members of the Belgian Institute of Valuers (BIV) often command higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a valuer or loss assessor in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card or a single permit for highly-skilled work. This requires a valid job offer, relevant qualifications, and meeting the official salary threshold set by the Belgian government (updated annually). Registration with a professional body like the BIV may also be needed.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a valuer or loss assessor in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's or master's degree in real estate, finance, or insurance is required. For valuers, membership in the Belgian Institute of Valuers (BIV) is mandatory. Loss assessors must register with the FSMA (Financial Services and Markets Authority) and have relevant insurance-sector experience. Continuing education is essential to maintain certification.
