## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for motorcycle drivers in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and region. Many motorcycle couriers are paid per delivery or receive hourly wages around the legal minimum. Full-time earnings typically range between €1,800 and €2,500 gross per month depending on experience, hours, and bonuses. Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as motorcycle drivers in Belgium through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a recognized higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience. Motorcycle driving typically does not qualify. Instead, non-EU nationals can apply for a Belgian single permit (work permit) if an employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. The employer initiates the process.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a motorcycle driver in Belgium?**
A: You need a valid motorcycle license (A or A2 depending on engine size), a clean driving record, and often a criminal record extract. Some employers require professional driving experience. For delivery work, a smartphone and knowledge of local areas are essential. No formal higher education is required.
