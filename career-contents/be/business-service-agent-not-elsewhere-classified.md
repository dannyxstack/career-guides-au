## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Business Services Agent in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, but a mid-level Business Services Agent in Belgium typically earns between €2,800 and €4,500 gross per month. Entry-level positions start around €2,300, while senior roles can exceed €5,500. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, insurance, and a company car.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a Business Services Agent in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if you have a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (€61,587 gross annually in 2025) and a recognized higher education degree. Apply through the Belgian region's employment authority. The permit allows work and residence for up to 4 years, renewable, with a path to permanent residency.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Business Services Agents in Belgium?**
A: With experience, you can advance to senior agent or team leader roles, specializing in fields like procurement, logistics, or customer relationship management. Many move into consultancy or management positions. Professional certifications from Belgian chambers of commerce or EU institutions can further boost career growth.
