## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Belgium?**
A: Taxi drivers in Belgium often earn a variable income; average net monthly pay ranges from €1,800 to €2,500 depending on region and company. Van drivers for delivery services typically earn between €2,000 and €2,800 gross per month. Many drivers are self-employed, so earnings can fluctuate based on hours and demand.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a taxi or van driver in Belgium, and what visa routes are available?**
A: Non-EU nationals must obtain a single permit (work and residence). The EU Blue Card is not applicable as drivers do not meet the high-skilled threshold. Employers must prove no suitable EU candidate is available (labor market test). Self-employment is possible but requires a professional taxi card and business visa.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a taxi or van driver in Belgium?**
A: To become a taxi driver, you need a valid driving license (B) and a professional taxi driver's license (taxi card), obtained through exams on local geography and regulations. For van drivers, a standard driving license suffices, but a clean driving record and medical certificate may be required. Some employers also ask for a certificate of good conduct.
