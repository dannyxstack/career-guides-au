## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, protective services workers such as security guards earn between €2,000 and €3,500 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and sector. Shift allowances are common. Salaries are often set by collective bargaining agreements, with entry-level positions starting at the lower end. Public sector roles may offer different scales.

**Q: What are the work visa options for protective services workers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a single permit (work permit B) valid up to 3 years, requiring a job offer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; however, protective services roles typically do not meet the salary threshold unless at management level. Professional cardholders may also apply. Always check the latest rules with the Belgian immigration office.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for protective services workers in Belgium?**
A: Career progression includes moving into supervisory or management roles, specializing in areas like cybersecurity or fire safety, or obtaining certifications. Training is available via VDAB, Forem, or private providers. Experienced workers may advance to positions in private security firms, government agencies, or corporate security departments.
