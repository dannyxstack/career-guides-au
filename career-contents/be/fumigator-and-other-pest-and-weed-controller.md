## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the gross monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers typically ranges between €2,000 and €3,200, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while senior specialists may earn up to €3,800. Additional benefits often include bonuses, meal vouchers, and transport allowances.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as fumigators in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Belgium through a single permit (work visa) issued by the regional employment service. The EU Blue Card is available if the job requires a higher education degree and meets the salary threshold (€60,000/year for regulated professions, updated annually). For fumigators without a degree, a standard work permit is the primary route.

**Q: What certifications are required to become a fumigator in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, fumigators must hold a phytopharmacist certificate (for pesticide use) or a recognized professional qualification. Training is offered by regional agricultural training centres (e.g., Boerenbond in Flanders). Proof of competence in safely handling pesticides and fumigants is mandatory. Renewal of certification is required every five years.
