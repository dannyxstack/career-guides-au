## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for hotel managers in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for hotel managers in Belgium vary based on hotel size, location, and experience. Entry-level managers earn around €2,500–€3,500 gross per month, while experienced managers in luxury or chain hotels can earn €4,000–€6,000 or more. Benefits like meal vouchers and bonuses are common.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for hotel managers in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU hotel managers can work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles with a salary threshold of €1,879/month for 2025) or a single work permit (combined residence/work authorization). Employer must prove labor market shortage. Specific criteria depend on nationality and role.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a hotel manager in Belgium?**
A: A bachelor's degree in hospitality management or equivalent is preferred. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German (depending on region) and English is essential. Experience in hotel operations, leadership skills, and knowledge of Belgian labor laws are key. Certifications like VOKA or IFAPME are valued.
