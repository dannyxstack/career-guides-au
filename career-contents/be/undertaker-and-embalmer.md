## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an undertaker or embalmer in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, undertakers and embalmers earn a gross monthly salary ranging from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience and region. Additional benefits may include seniority bonuses and overtime pay. Self-employed professionals set their own rates. Salaries are often negotiated per collective labor agreements in the funeral sector.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for undertakers wanting to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a single permit (work + residence) from the regional employment service. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a recognized degree and a high salary threshold, generally not applicable to undertakers. Most undertakers qualify for a standard work permit B or a single permit if a Belgian employer sponsors them.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an undertaker or embalmer in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, formal training includes a diploma in funeral sciences or a related field, often from recognized institutes. Embalmers need specific certification. Additionally, a clean criminal record and emotional resilience are essential. Continuing education is mandatory to stay updated on regulations and techniques. Some regions require registration with a professional body.
