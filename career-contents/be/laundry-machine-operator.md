## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Laundry Machine Operator in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for Laundry Machine Operators in Belgium typically range from the national minimum wage (€1,993 gross per month as of 2024) to around €2,500 gross per month for experienced workers. Pay may vary by region, employer, and shift work. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers and transport allowances.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Laundry Machine Operators in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in this role through a single permit (work and residence) if an employer obtains a work permit. The EU Blue Card is also an option but typically requires a higher-skilled occupation with a salary threshold (€65,448 gross/year in 2024). This occupation may qualify for a standard work permit B rather than a Blue Card.

**Q: What are the main tasks and working conditions for Laundry Machine Operators in Belgium?**
A: Laundry Machine Operators in Belgium operate industrial washing, drying, and ironing machines in laundries and dry-cleaning establishments. They sort, load, unload, and fold laundry, and monitor machine performance. Work often involves standing for long hours, handling chemicals, and working in shifts. Knowledge of Dutch or French is beneficial for workplace communication.
