## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a travel attendant in Belgium?**
A: Entry-level cabin crew in Belgium earn a gross monthly salary typically ranging from €2,000 to €2,500, plus allowances. experienced crew can earn up to €3,500 or more. Pay varies by airline, seniority, and flying hours. Additional benefits often include flight perks, meal allowances, and insurance.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as travel attendants in Belgium via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a single permit (work + residence) with a job offer from a Belgian airline. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as the salary threshold (€59,250 gross/year in 2024) typically exceeds cabin crew earnings. Alternatively, intra-company transfer permits may apply for certain cases.

**Q: What language skills are needed to become a travel attendant in Belgium?**
A: Fluency in Dutch and French is highly valued due to Belgium's multilingualism. English is mandatory, and knowledge of German or other languages is an asset. Airlines test language proficiency during recruitment. Most airlines require at least a B2 level in two of the three national languages.
