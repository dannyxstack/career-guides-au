## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Belgium?**
A: The salary for sweepers in Belgium varies by region and employer but generally ranges from €1,600 to €2,000 gross per month for full-time work. Minimum wage regulations apply, with sector-specific collective agreements often determining exact pay. Additional benefits may include meal vouchers and transport allowances.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a sweeper in Belgium under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent, so it does not apply to sweepers. However, non-EU nationals may obtain a single permit for work and residence if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. A labor market test and regional approval are required.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for sweepers in Belgium?**
A: Sweepers can advance to supervisory roles like team leader or site supervisor after gaining experience. Additional training in cleaning management or waste handling may lead to positions in facility management or environmental services. Some transition to related fields such as street cleaning or waste collection.
