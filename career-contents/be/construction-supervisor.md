## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for construction supervisors in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, construction supervisors typically earn between €3,500 and €5,000 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and project complexity. Sectoral collective bargaining agreements set minimum wages, with additional benefits like meal vouchers and transportation allowances often included.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU construction supervisors to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU construction supervisors can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card, requiring a valid job offer, recognized qualifications, and meeting salary thresholds. The Blue Card demands a salary at least 1.3 times the average gross annual salary in Belgium. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a construction supervisor in Belgium?**
A: A bachelor's degree in civil engineering or construction management is typical, along with several years of site experience. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German is essential depending on the region. Certification like Safety Coordination (VCA) may be required, and continuous training is expected to stay updated with regulations.
