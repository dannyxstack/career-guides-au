## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Belgium generally starts at the minimum wage (approximately €1,800 for full-time work) and can reach €2,500 for experienced workers. Pay may vary by region and farm size.

**Q: Is the EU Blue Card a viable route for livestock farm labourers to work in Belgium?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree. Since livestock farm labourers typically do not meet this requirement, the Blue Card is not a suitable route. Instead, employers can apply for a single permit (work and residence) after demonstrating labour market shortages.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for livestock farm labourers in Belgium?**
A: With experience, livestock farm labourers can advance to roles such as team leader, farm supervisor, or specialized animal caretaker. Further training in agricultural management or veterinary skills may lead to positions in farm operations management or self-employment as a farmer.
