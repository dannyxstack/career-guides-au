## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for domestic cleaners in Belgium?**
A: Domestic cleaners in Belgium are often employed via the service voucher system. The minimum gross hourly wage is set by sectoral agreements and is approximately €12.10 per hour as of 2024. Actual pay may vary based on experience and employer, but this is the legal minimum for voucher-based work.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as domestic cleaners in Belgium through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as domestic cleaners by obtaining a single permit (combined work and residence authorization) from the regional employment authority. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by an EU/EEA worker. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it is reserved for highly skilled workers and requires a higher education degree and minimum salary threshold.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for domestic cleaners in Belgium?**
A: Domestic cleaners can progress to supervisory positions, such as team leader or cleaning manager, especially in larger cleaning companies. With experience, they may also start their own cleaning business. Regional employment services like VDAB (Flanders) or Forem (Wallonia) offer training programs in specialized cleaning techniques and business management.
