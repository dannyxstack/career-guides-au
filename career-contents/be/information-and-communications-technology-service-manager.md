## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Services Managers in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, ICT services managers typically earn between €70,000 and €120,000 gross annually, depending on experience, company size, and sector. According to Statbel and job market data, the median salary is around €85,000. Benefits often include a company car, insurance, and performance bonuses. Salaries are higher in Brussels and for roles in finance or tech sectors.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as an ICT Services Manager in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU ICT services managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€50,040 in 2023, adjusted annually) and a recognized higher education degree. Alternatively, the single permit (combined work and residence) is available for roles with a valid work contract. Both routes require employer sponsorship and prior approval from the regional employment office.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for ICT Services Managers in Belgium?**
A: ICT services managers in Belgium can advance to CTO or IT director roles, with many specializing in cybersecurity, cloud computing, or AI leadership. The strong tech ecosystem (e.g., hub.brussels, Agoria) offers networking and training. Bilingualism (French/Dutch + English) boosts prospects. Certifications like PMP or ITIL are valued. Many professionals also transition into consultancy or entrepreneurship.
