## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Research and Development Managers in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, R&D managers typically earn between €70,000 and €120,000 annually, depending on experience, sector (e.g., pharma, tech), and company size. Senior roles in multinationals or Brussels may reach higher. Bonuses and benefits like company cars are common. Salaries are subject to progressive income tax and social security contributions.

**Q: What work-visa routes are available for non-EU Research and Development Managers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU R&D managers can apply for the EU Blue Card (highly skilled workers) or the Belgian single permit (combined work and residence). The EU Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary threshold (€60,747 gross/year in 2024) and a recognized qualification. The single permit is for general employment and involves a labor market test. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the career prospects for Research and Development Managers in Belgium?**
A: Belgium's strong R&D sectors—pharmaceuticals (e.g., Janssen, UCB), chemicals, and tech—offer excellent prospects. R&D managers can advance to director of R&D, CTO, or VP of Innovation. Opportunities also exist in EU-funded research institutions and biotech hubs like Flanders. Continuous learning and multilingualism (Dutch, French) enhance career growth.
