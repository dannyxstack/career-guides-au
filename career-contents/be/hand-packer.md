## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand packer in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a hand packer in Belgium typically ranges from €1,800 to €2,200, depending on experience, region, and sector. Some sectors, like logistics or food processing, may offer slightly higher pay. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum wages, which are updated annually.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a hand packer in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers and is not applicable for hand packers. Non-EU citizens can work as hand packers via a single permit (combined work and residence permit), which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. Labour market tests may apply. Long-term EU residence or family reunification are alternative routes.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions for hand packers in Belgium?**
A: Hand packers in Belgium often work in warehouses, factories, or distribution centers. Common tasks include packing, labeling, and quality checking. Shifts may include nights, weekends, or rotating schedules. Most jobs are full-time, but part-time options exist. Physical stamina and attention to detail are key. Training is usually on the job.
