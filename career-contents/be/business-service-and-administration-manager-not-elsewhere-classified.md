## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services and Administration Managers in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, but business services and administration managers in Belgium typically earn between €55,000 and €85,000 per year gross, plus benefits such as meal vouchers, bonus, and company car. Senior roles in Brussels or in multinational firms may exceed €100,000.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Business Services and Administration Managers in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified non-EU workers with a valid job offer in Belgium. Requirements include a recognized higher education degree (or equivalent experience) and a gross annual salary meeting the legal threshold (adjusted annually; e.g., €46,000 in 2023). The employer must also apply for a single permit.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Business Services and Administration Managers in Belgium?**
A: Managers often progress to senior management roles such as Director of Administration or Chief Operating Officer (COO). Others specialize in areas like project management or consultancy. Continuous professional development in finance, HR, or digital transformation is valued, and fluency in Dutch, French, and English boosts prospects.
