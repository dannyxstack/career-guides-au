## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for forestry and related workers in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for forestry workers in Belgium typically ranges from €1,900 to €2,800 for full-time work, depending on experience, region, and employer. Additional benefits such as bonuses or overtime pay may apply. Salaries are often set by sectoral collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for forestry workers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU forestry workers can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence permit) if employed by a Belgian employer. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers, which most forestry positions do not qualify for. The single permit requires employer sponsorship and compliance with labor market tests.

**Q: What are the main duties of forestry workers in Belgium?**
A: Forestry workers in Belgium perform tasks such as tree planting, thinning, pruning, harvesting, and maintaining forest roads and trails. They operate chainsaws, tractors, and other machinery, monitor forest health, and assist in conservation efforts, including wildfire prevention. Duties vary by season and employer (public or private).
