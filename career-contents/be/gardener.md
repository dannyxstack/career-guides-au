## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a gardener in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a gardener in Belgium ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €1,600, while experienced gardeners can earn up to €3,000. Wages are typically regulated by sector-specific collective bargaining agreements (PC 145 for horticulture).

**Q: Can a non-EU gardener work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU gardeners can work in Belgium through the single permit procedure, which combines work and residence authorization. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers with higher education and a salary threshold; gardening roles rarely meet these criteria. For low- or medium-skilled work, an employer must obtain a labor market test and a work permit (B or C).

**Q: What are the typical tasks and career prospects for a gardener in Belgium?**
A: Gardeners in Belgium perform planting, pruning, lawn care, and maintenance of public or private green spaces. They may specialize in landscaping, arboriculture, or organic gardening. Career advancement includes becoming a head gardener, landscape designer, or starting their own business. Certification through Syntra or regional vocational training is beneficial.
