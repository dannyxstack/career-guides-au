- Generates standard construction drawings and 2D/3D models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Structural analysis and simulation testing using AI

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Civil Engineering Technician in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, Civil Engineering Technicians typically earn between €2,500 and €3,800 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €2,500, while senior technicians can earn up to €4,500. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, 13th-month pay, and transportation allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a foreign Civil Engineering Technician to work in Belgium?**
A: Foreign Civil Engineering Technicians can work in Belgium via a single permit (combined work and residence permit) or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers). The single permit requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary threshold (€48,595 in 2025) and a recognized degree or equivalent experience.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Civil Engineering Technician in Belgium?**
A: Civil Engineering Technicians in Belgium typically need a higher professional education (HBO) or a bachelor's degree in civil engineering or a related field. Many complete a 3-year program at a university of applied sciences (Hogeschool). Relevant experience and knowledge of Dutch, French, or German (depending on region) are also important.
