## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an environmental protection professional in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for environmental protection professionals in Belgium vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions typically pay €40,000–€50,000 annually, while senior roles can earn €70,000–€90,000. The public sector, consulting firms, and large corporations are common employers, with benefits often included.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU environmental protection professionals to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer with a salary above €60,847 (2024 threshold, lower for shortage occupations) or a single permit for work and residence. Both routes need employer sponsorship and recognized qualifications. The single permit is processed regionally (e.g., Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels).

**Q: Which employers hire environmental protection professionals in Belgium?**
A: Key employers include federal and regional environmental agencies (e.g., Flemish Environment Agency), international bodies (e.g., European Commission), engineering consultancies, and private companies in energy, waste management, and sustainability. The growing green economy offers roles in policy, auditing, and research.
