## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for web and multimedia developers in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, junior web developers earn around €35,000–€45,000 gross annually, while senior developers can earn €55,000–€75,000. Location and company size affect pay; Brussels and Antwerp offer higher salaries. Benefits often include meal vouchers, transport, and insurance.

**Q: What work permit options exist for non-EU web developers wanting to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals need either a single permit (type B) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer, a recognized diploma or five years’ experience, and a salary meeting the official threshold (approx. €50,000 gross/year in 2024). The single permit is for other roles; both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What is the career outlook for web and multimedia developers in Belgium?**
A: Demand remains strong due to digital transformation. Opportunities exist in tech hubs like Brussels, Leuven, and Ghent. Skills in front-end, back-end, and full-stack development are valued. Knowing Dutch or French is an asset. Continuous learning is important as technology evolves.
