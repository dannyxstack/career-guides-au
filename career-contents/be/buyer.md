## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for buyers in Belgium?**
A: The gross annual salary for buyers in Belgium typically ranges from €40,000 to €60,000, depending on experience, sector, and company size. Entry-level positions may start around €35,000, while senior buyers can earn up to €80,000. Benefits often include a company car, meal vouchers, and bonuses.

**Q: Can foreign buyers work in Belgium via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, foreign buyers can work in Belgium through the Single Permit (combined work and residence permit) or the EU Blue Card. Both require a valid job offer from a Belgian employer and meeting specific conditions, such as professional qualifications and salary thresholds set by the regional government (no exact figures provided here).

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for buyers in Belgium?**
A: Buyers in Belgium can progress to senior buyer, procurement manager, or supply chain director roles. Advancement often requires additional certifications like CPSM (Certified Professional in Supply Management) or a master's degree in procurement. Fluency in Dutch, French, and English is highly valued for managerial positions.
