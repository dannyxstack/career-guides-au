## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for personal services workers not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on the specific occupation. On average, personal services workers in Belgium earn between €2,000 and €3,000 gross per month. Self-employed individuals may have variable income. Sector-specific collective bargaining agreements often set minimum wages.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as a personal services worker in Belgium through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled roles requiring a degree and a salary threshold (currently €45,000/year). Many personal services jobs may not qualify. Alternative routes include the single permit for work and residence, requiring a job offer and labor market test. Self-employment requires a professional card.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for personal services workers in Belgium?**
A: Career paths include funeral direction, photography, pet care, and travel guiding. Advancement often involves specialization or starting a business. Formal training (e.g., funeral services diplomas) or certifications (e.g., photography courses) can enhance prospects. Self-employment is common, with support from regional agencies.
