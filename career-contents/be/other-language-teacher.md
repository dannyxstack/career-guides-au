## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for other language teachers in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by region and institution. In Flemish community schools, starting gross monthly pay ranges from €2,900 to €3,500 for full-time teachers. Private language schools may offer lower rates, around €25–€40 per hour. Experienced teachers in international schools can earn €3,500–€5,000 per month. Exact figures depend on qualifications and experience.

**Q: How can a non-EU language teacher obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU teachers can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence) if offered a job by a Belgian employer. For the EU Blue Card, the job must be highly qualified (minimum bachelor's degree) and meet a salary threshold (updated annually). The employer applies to the regional employment service. Language teachers in shortage occupations may have slightly streamlined procedures.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a language teacher in Belgium?**
A: Requirements depend on the institution. For official language schools or adult education centers, a teaching degree (e.g., CELTA, DELTA, or a bachelor's in education) is typically needed. For private schools, a university degree in the language and teaching experience may suffice. Registration with the relevant community's education ministry is often required for public schools.
