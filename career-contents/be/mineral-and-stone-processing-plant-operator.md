- Monitor the operating status of crushers and grinders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI predicts equipment failures, guiding preventive maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operator in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operator in Belgium ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €2,200, while experienced operators can earn up to €4,000. Additional benefits often include shift premiums and meal vouchers.

**Q: Can non-EU workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work as a Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operator in Belgium?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Belgium if they have a valid job offer, a recognized higher education degree (or equivalent professional experience of at least 5 years), and a salary meeting the threshold (currently around €60,000 gross annually for most sectors). The employer must also obtain a work permit.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operators in Belgium?**
A: Operators can advance to supervisory roles (shift supervisor, plant foreman) with additional training or experience. Some specialize in quality control or machinery maintenance. Continuous education through sectoral training programs, such as those offered by VDAB or Forem, can lead to higher qualifications and roles in process optimization.
