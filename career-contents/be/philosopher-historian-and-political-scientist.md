## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for philosophers, historians and political scientists in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. In academia, starting salaries for researchers range from €3,000 to €4,500 gross/month. Senior roles or policy advisors in government or NGOs can earn €4,500–€6,500 gross/month. Private sector positions, such as in consulting, may offer higher pay but are less common.

**Q: Can non-EU philosophers, historians or political scientists work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals in these occupations can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, a master’s degree (or higher), and meet the salary threshold (e.g., at least €56,734 gross/year for 2024). The employer must obtain a work permit. Alternatively, a single permit (combined work and residence) is possible for positions below the Blue Card threshold.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for philosophers, historians and political scientists in Belgium?**
A: Common employers include universities, research institutes, think tanks, government agencies, international organizations (e.g., EU institutions, NATO), and NGOs. Roles involve research, policy analysis, teaching, or public administration. The multilingual environment (Dutch, French, English) often gives candidates an advantage but is not always required.
