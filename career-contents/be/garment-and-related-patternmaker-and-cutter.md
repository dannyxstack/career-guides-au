## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garment patternmaker or cutter in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Belgium is between €2,500 and €3,500, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €2,000, while experienced workers in specialized roles can earn up to €4,000.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as a garment patternmaker or cutter in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a single permit (combined work and residence permit) for employment. The EU Blue Card is another route, but it requires a higher education degree and a minimum salary threshold (€56,670 in 2024). Since this occupation typically does not require a degree, the single permit is more common.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a garment patternmaker or cutter in Belgium?**
A: Typically, a vocational secondary education diploma in patternmaking, textile design, or tailoring is required. Apprenticeships and on-the-job training are common. Knowledge of CAD software and sewing techniques is advantageous. The profession is recognized by the Belgian National Employment Office (ONEM/VDAB).
