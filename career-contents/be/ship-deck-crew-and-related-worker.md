## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' deck crew in Belgium?**
A: Entry-level deck crew in Belgium earn between €2,000 and €2,800 gross per month, depending on the employer and vessel type. Experienced crew may earn up to €3,500. Pay is often governed by collective bargaining agreements and includes allowances for sea time.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as deck crew in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree, which deck crew typically do not meet. Instead, non-EU nationals can obtain a single permit (combining work and residence) if a Belgian employer sponsors them. The employer must demonstrate labor market testing and compliance with sectoral conditions.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work on Belgian ships as deck crew?**
A: You need a valid STCW certificate, including basic safety training. For Belgian-flagged vessels, recognition of foreign certificates by the Belgian maritime authority is mandatory. Proficiency in English and basic knowledge of Dutch or French is often required.
