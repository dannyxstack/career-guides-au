## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by role and experience, but for mid-level positions such as compliance officers or legal advisors, the annual gross salary typically ranges from €45,000 to €75,000. Senior professionals may earn up to €90,000. Benefits often include bonuses and meal vouchers.

**Q: Can legal professionals not elsewhere classified work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU legal professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized diploma and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (set annually by Belgian authorities). Alternatively, a single permit (work visa type B) is available for roles not meeting the Blue Card criteria, requiring employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a legal professional not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: A master's degree in law (or equivalent) is typically required. For regulated roles like legal advisors in specific fields, additional certifications or bar membership may be needed. Fluency in at least one national language (French, Dutch, or German) is essential, with English often required in international roles.
