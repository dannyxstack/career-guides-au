## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a door-to-door salesperson in Belgium?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Belgium often earn a base salary plus commission, with total monthly earnings typically ranging from €1,800 to €2,500 gross depending on performance. Many work as self-employed (indépendant/zelfstandige), so income can vary significantly. Sector-specific collective agreements may set minimum pay for employed salespeople.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a door-to-door salesperson in Belgium via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and typically does not apply to door-to-door sales. Non-EU nationals must obtain a single permit (combined work and residence) through a Belgian employer. This requires a labor market test showing no suitable EU candidate is available. Self-employment is also possible via a professional card, but strictly for specific regulated activities.

**Q: What are the working conditions and career prospects for door-to-door salespersons in Belgium?**
A: Door-to-door sales in Belgium involves flexible, often irregular hours and significant travel. Salespeople must comply with privacy laws (e.g., do-not-call lists) and consumer protection rules. Career progression includes becoming a team leader or moving into direct sales management. The role offers autonomy but income instability, as many are paid on commission. Non-EU workers face additional administrative hurdles.
