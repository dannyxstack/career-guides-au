## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a judge in Belgium?**
A: The salary of a judge in Belgium varies by level and seniority. A judge at the court of first instance earns approximately €4,500 to €7,000 gross per month, while higher courts like the court of appeal can pay up to €10,000. Additional allowances and benefits may apply.

**Q: Can foreign judges work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for judicial positions in Belgium, as judges are typically required to be Belgian or EU nationals who have passed the national judicial exam. Foreign lawyers may explore the single permit for highly skilled workers for other legal roles, but not for direct judicial appointments.

**Q: How does one become a judge in Belgium?**
A: To become a judge in Belgium, one must hold a Belgian law degree, complete a judicial traineeship, and pass a competitive exam. Candidates also undergo specialized training at the Institute of Judicial Training (IGO/IFJ). Several years of legal practice as a lawyer or magistrate are typically required before appointment.
