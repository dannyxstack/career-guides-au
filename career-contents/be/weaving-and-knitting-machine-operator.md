## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a weaving or knitting machine operator in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and company size. The gross monthly salary typically ranges from €1,900 to €2,700. Additional benefits may include shift premiums, meal vouchers, and travel allowances. Collective bargaining agreements (CCT) in the textile sector set minimum wages.

**Q: What are the work-visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as a weaving or knitting machine operator in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a single permit (work and residence) through their employer. The job must be registered with the regional employment service (VDAB, Forem, or Actiris) and may require a labour market test unless the occupation is on the shortage list. The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable as it targets highly qualified workers.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for weaving and knitting machine operators in Belgium?**
A: Operators can advance to team leader or production supervisor roles. Further training in textile technology or machine maintenance can lead to technician or process engineer positions. The Belgian textile sector also offers specialization in advanced materials and automation, supported by sectoral training funds.
