## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Belgium?**
A: The salary is generally based on the minimum wage set by the agriculture sector's collective bargaining agreement (PC 144). As of 2024, the gross monthly minimum is around €2,000 for a full-time worker, though actual pay may vary with experience, employer, and region.

**Q: What visa or work permit is needed for a non-EU national to work as a farm labourer in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a single permit (combined work and residence permit) sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card, designed for highly qualified workers, demands a university degree and a salary above €50,000, which is not typical for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for farm labourers in Belgium?**
A: Experienced labourers can progress to team leader or specialized roles in livestock or crop management. With further training, they may become agricultural technicians or farm managers, or even start their own farm, especially if they complete a recognized agricultural qualification.
