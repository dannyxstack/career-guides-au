## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Belgium ranges from €2,100 to €2,800, depending on experience, region, and employer. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, transport allowances, and end-of-year bonuses. Salaries are typically governed by sectoral collective bargaining agreements (CPCs).

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence permit) or an EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (currently €55,000 gross/year for 2024). The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. The single permit is more common for this occupation; the Blue Card is typically for higher-skilled roles.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in Belgium?**
A: With experience, assemblers can advance to team leader, quality controller, or technician roles. Further training (e.g., VDAB or IFAPME courses) can lead to specialization in automation or maintenance. Some transition to production supervisor or technical support. Certification in electronics or PLC programming enhances prospects.
