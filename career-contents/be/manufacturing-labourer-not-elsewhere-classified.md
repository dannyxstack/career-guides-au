## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a manufacturing labourer in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a manufacturing labourer in Belgium typically ranges from €1,800 to €2,400, depending on experience and sector. Minimum wage (as of 2025) is around €1,900/month for full-time work. Collective bargaining agreements may apply, and overtime or shift premiums can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as manufacturing labourers in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers, not low-skilled occupations like manufacturing labourers. Non-EU nationals can apply for a single permit (work + residence) if an employer proves labour market shortage. Official routes include the single permit for workers and the professional card for self-employment, but no simplified visa exists for this role.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for manufacturing labourers in Belgium?**
A: Manufacturing labourers can advance to team leader, machine operator, or quality control roles after gaining experience. Some pursue vocational training (VDAB, Bruxelles Formation) to specialise in sectors like food processing or metalworking. Belgian labour law supports lifelong learning, and internal mobility is common in larger industrial companies.
