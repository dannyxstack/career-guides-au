## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a croupier or gaming worker in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a croupier in Belgium ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience, the casino's location, and the employer. Starting salaries may be lower, around €2,000, while experienced dealers at major casinos can earn up to €4,000. Tips can add a significant amount, sometimes 10-20% extra.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as croupiers in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as croupiers in Belgium through the single permit work visa, if they have a job offer and the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is less common because it requires a high-skilled salary threshold (€52,356 in 2024) which gaming workers rarely meet. However, for specialized roles like casino management, the Blue Card may be possible.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a croupier in Belgium?**
A: To become a croupier in Belgium, you must be at least 18, pass a background check, and complete a training course approved by the Belgian Gaming Commission. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German (depending on the region) is required. A secondary school diploma is usually sufficient, though a certificate from a dealer school is an advantage. Registration with the Gaming Commission is mandatory.
