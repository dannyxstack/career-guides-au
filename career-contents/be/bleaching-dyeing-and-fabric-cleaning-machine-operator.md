## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Belgium?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month. With experience, salaries range from €2,200 to €2,800. Senior operators or those with specialized skills can earn up to €3,200. Additional benefits such as shift premiums and overtime pay are common in the textile industry.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree or at least five years' professional experience, plus a job offer meeting a minimum salary threshold (currently around €46,000/year). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the typical career progression opportunities for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Belgium?**
A: Operators can advance to senior operator, team leader, or shift supervisor roles. With additional training, they may become production managers or quality controllers. The Belgian textile sector, though smaller than in the past, still offers opportunities in specialized finishing plants and technical textile companies.
