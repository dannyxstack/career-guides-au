## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Belgium?**
A: Entry-level pet groomers in Belgium earn around €1,800 gross per month, while experienced workers can earn up to €2,500. Many are paid hourly or on commission, and salaries vary by region and employer. Part-time work is common.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as pet groomers in Belgium via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree, so it generally does not apply to pet groomers. Non-EU nationals can instead apply for a single permit (work and residence) if an employer sponsors them. Other routes include family reunification or intra-company transfer.

**Q: What training is required to become a pet groomer in Belgium?**
A: Formal certification is not mandatory, but employers prefer candidates with a vocational diploma from institutions like Syntra or IFAPME. Courses cover animal handling, grooming techniques, and hygiene. Apprenticeships are also common. Animal care workers may need additional training for specific species.
