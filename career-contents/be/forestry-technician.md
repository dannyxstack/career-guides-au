## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Forestry Technician in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Forestry Technician in Belgium ranges from €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience and region. Public sector positions may follow the scale for technical assistants (A2 level). Additional benefits often include end-of-year bonuses, meal vouchers, and seniority increments. Entry-level technicians earn around €2,000, while senior roles can exceed €4,000.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Forestry Technicians in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU Forestry Technicians can work in Belgium via the EU Blue Card or a single permit. The job must meet the minimum salary threshold (€58,125 gross/year in 2024, or €46,500 for shortage occupations). The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. A degree equivalent to a bachelor's or higher is typically required. Self-employed routes exist for specific projects.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a Forestry Technician in Belgium?**
A: Forestry Technicians in Belgium manage forest inventories, supervise planting and harvesting, advise on sustainable practices, and enforce regulations. They work for public agencies like the Walloon Forest Service or private companies. Tasks include mapping wooded areas, coordinating logging operations, monitoring biodiversity, and ensuring compliance with Walloon or Flemish forestry codes. Fieldwork and office reporting are both common.
