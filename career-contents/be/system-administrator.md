## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Systems Administrator in Belgium?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a Systems Administrator in Belgium typically ranges from €3,200 to €5,500, depending on experience, sector, and region. Senior roles in Brussels or with specialized skills (e.g., cloud, security) can exceed €6,000. Many employers also offer benefits like meal vouchers, insurance, and a company car.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a non-EU Systems Administrator to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work as Systems Administrators in Belgium via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or the single permit (combined work and residence). Both require a job offer meeting salary thresholds (e.g., Blue Card: at least €62,000 gross/year in 2025). Employers must also obtain a work authorization from the regional employment office.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Systems Administrators in Belgium?**
A: Systems Administrators in Belgium often advance to roles like IT Infrastructure Manager, Cloud Architect, or DevOps Engineer. Certifications (e.g., AWS, Azure, Cisco) and experience with automation tools boost prospects. Many move into IT project management or cybersecurity, especially in Brussels' tech hub or major companies in Antwerp and Ghent.
