## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a customs and border inspector in Belgium?**
A: Starting salaries for customs inspectors in Belgium range from €2,500 to €3,200 gross per month, depending on experience and level. With seniority, salaries can reach €4,500–€5,500. Additional allowances for shift work, night hours, and hazardous duties may apply. These figures are based on public sector pay scales for the Belgian Federal Public Service Finance (Customs & Excise).

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU nationals to become a customs inspector in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a single permit (combined work and residence permit) or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. For customs inspector roles, the employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU candidate is available (labor market test). The EU Blue Card route requires a valid job offer, a salary at least 1.5 times the average Belgian wage, and recognized qualifications. Belgian immigration authorities process applications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a customs inspector in Belgium?**
A: Candidates must hold at least a bachelor's degree (e.g., in law, economics, or logistics) and pass a competitive civil service exam organized by SELOR. Language proficiency in Dutch, French, or German is required (depending on the region). Additional requirements include EU citizenship or a valid work permit, a clean criminal record, and passing medical and psychological tests. On-the-job training is provided after recruitment.
