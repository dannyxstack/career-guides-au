## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a travel guide in Belgium?**
A: The average salary for a travel guide in Belgium ranges from €1,800 to €2,800 gross per month, depending on experience, language skills, and employer. Freelance guides may earn €20-€40 per hour. Salaries can be higher in major tourist cities like Brussels, Bruges, or Antwerp, especially for multilingual guides.

**Q: Can non-EU travel guides obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Belgium?**
A: Non-EU travel guides can apply for a single permit (work and residence) if a Belgian employer sponsors them, proving the role cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is possible for highly qualified roles, but travel guiding rarely meets the high-skilled salary threshold (€56,494 gross/year in 2024). Self-employment as a guide requires a professional card and business plan.

**Q: What qualifications or certifications are needed to become a travel guide in Belgium?**
A: Belgium requires travel guides to hold a regional guide license (Brevet de Guide Touristique) from Flanders, Wallonia, or Brussels-Capital Region, involving coursework and exams. Fluency in at least two national languages (Dutch, French, German) plus English is essential. Specialized knowledge of local history, art, or architecture is advantageous for niche tours.
