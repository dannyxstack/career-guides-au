## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Software Developer in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Belgium vary by experience and location. According to recent data, the median gross annual salary is around €60,000 for mid-level developers. Junior roles start at €40,000–€50,000, while senior developers can earn €80,000 or more. Benefits often include meal vouchers, insurance, and a company car.

**Q: What are the work visa options for software developers moving to Belgium?**
A: Non-EU software developers can work in Belgium via the single permit (combined work and residence) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree, a job offer lasting at least one year, and a gross annual salary above the threshold (€57,640 in 2024 for non-regulated professions). The single permit has no set salary minimum but must meet local labor market conditions.

**Q: Which programming skills are in highest demand for developers in Belgium?**
A: Belgium’s tech sector prioritizes full-stack developers proficient in JavaScript (React, Node.js), Python, and Java. Cloud skills (AWS, Azure) and knowledge of DevOps tools (Docker, Kubernetes) are increasingly valued. Employers also seek experience with Agile methodologies and data analysis. Bilingualism (Dutch/French plus English) is a strong advantage, especially for roles in Brussels.
