## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for engineering professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and region. According to industry surveys, the median gross monthly salary for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Belgium is approximately €4,500, with entry-level positions starting around €3,000 and senior roles exceeding €7,000. Benefits often include meal vouchers, insurance, and a company car.

**Q: What work visa routes exist for non-EU engineering professionals to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU engineering professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (€63,902 gross per year in 2024) and a recognized higher education degree. Alternatively, a single permit combines work and residence authorization. Both require employer sponsorship and comply with regional labour market checks.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for engineering professionals in Belgium?**
A: Engineering professionals in Belgium can advance to senior technical roles, project management, or R&D management. The country's strong industrial base (pharma, automotive, tech) offers paths to team lead or innovation manager. Many pursue additional certifications (e.g., PMP) or a master's degree to enhance prospects. Continuing education is often supported by employers.
