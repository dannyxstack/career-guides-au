## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, the average gross monthly salary for an aged care services manager ranges from €4,000 to €5,500, depending on experience, region, and facility size. Most contracts include a 13th-month pay, meal vouchers, and hospital insurance. Salary scales are often set by joint committees (commissions paritaires) for the non-profit healthcare sector.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU Aged Care Services Managers to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card, requiring a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€58,400 in 2024). Alternatively, the single permit combines work and residence authorization. Employers must demonstrate a labor market shortage and provide accommodation. Both routes require a recognized qualification and sufficient language skills.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Aged Care Services Managers in Belgium?**
A: Experienced managers can progress to regional director of care services, policy advisor for health authorities, or consultant for elderly care organizations. Further specialization in dementia care, palliative care, or quality management is possible.
