## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region, but typically range from €2,800 to €4,200 gross per month. Senior technicians may earn up to €5,500. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, 13th month pay, and a company car for field roles.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU Telecommunications Engineering Technicians to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a single permit (combined work and residence). The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€67,000 in 2024). Alternatively, the single permit is for jobs not meeting the Blue Card threshold, but the employer must demonstrate a labour market need.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Belgium?**
A: With experience, technicians can advance to senior technician, team leader, or network specialist. Further education or certifications (e.g., in fiber optics or 5G) can lead to roles like network engineer or project manager. Belgian telecom operators like Proximus, Telenet, and Orange offer internal training and promotion paths.
