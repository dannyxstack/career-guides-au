## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an armed forces other ranks member in Belgium?**
A: Starting basic salary for a recruit (soldier) is around €1,800–€2,000 gross per month, increasing with rank and seniority. Additional allowances (e.g., housing, hazardous duty) apply. Exact pay scales are publicly available via the Belgian Ministry of Defence.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work in Belgium as armed forces other ranks via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No. Belgian armed forces positions are restricted to EU/EEA nationals and Swiss citizens. Non-EU citizens are not eligible for a work visa or EU Blue Card for military roles. Official routes like the single permit or Blue Card apply only to civilian occupations.

**Q: What training is required to become an other ranks member in the Belgian armed forces?**
A: Candidates must pass medical, physical, and psychological tests. Basic training lasts about 12 weeks for soldiers (land component) or 8–10 weeks for other branches. Training is provided by the Belgian Defence. No prior military experience is required, but secondary education completion is typically expected.
