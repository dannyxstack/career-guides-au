## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Social Work Associate Professional in Belgium?**
A: Salaries in Belgium vary by sector and experience, but a typical gross monthly salary ranges from €2,500 to €3,500 for full-time work. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, public transport reimbursement, and holiday pay. Collective bargaining agreements set minimums, so actual pay depends on the employer and region.

**Q: Can a foreign Social Work Associate Professional obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Belgium?**
A: Yes, if you have a valid job offer, a higher education degree (at least Bachelor's), and a salary threshold (approx. €58,000 gross/year in 2025, or €46,000 for regulated professions with labour market test). The employer must apply for a single permit first. The Blue Card allows family reunification and mobility after 12 months.

**Q: What are the key career prospects for Social Work Associate Professionals in Belgium?**
A: Opportunities exist in public social services (CPAS/OCMW), non-profits, and healthcare. With experience, you may advance to team leader or case manager roles. Fluency in Dutch, French, or German is often required. Continuing education (e.g., specialized certificates) can lead to higher-level social work positions (ISCO 2635).
