## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a secretary in Belgium?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a secretary in Belgium ranges from €2,200 to €3,500, depending on experience, sector, and region. Entry-level positions start around €2,000, while senior secretaries can earn up to €4,000. Additional benefits often include meal vouchers, transport, and 13th-month pay.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for non-EU secretaries to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU secretaries typically need a single permit (work and residence) sponsored by a Belgian employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because the salary threshold (€62,000 in 2024) exceeds typical secretary pay. Exceptionally, if the role qualifies as a shortage occupation or the salary meets the lower threshold (€49,000), it may be possible, but it is rare.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for secretaries in Belgium?**
A: Secretaries in Belgium can advance to executive assistant, office manager, or administrative coordinator roles. Further specialization in legal, medical, or multilingual secretarial work is common. With experience and additional training, some move into project management or HR. Professional certifications like the 'Secrétaire de Direction' can enhance prospects.
