## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cleaners and helpers in offices, hotels, and other establishments in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, cleaners and helpers typically earn €1,700–€2,200 gross per month for full-time work, depending on experience, region, and sector. Wages are often set by collective bargaining agreements (CCTs). As of 2024, the minimum wage for employees aged 18+ with 12 months' seniority is €1,995 gross, though younger workers may earn less.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as cleaners and helpers in Belgium, including the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers (typically requiring a bachelor's degree or higher), so cleaners and helpers usually do not qualify. Non-EU nationals may instead apply for a single permit (combined work and residence) if the occupation is on Belgium's shortage list or through family reunification. Official details are available from the Belgian Immigration Office (Dienst Vreemdelingenzaken).

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for cleaners and helpers in Belgium?**
A: With experience, cleaners can advance to supervisory roles like team leader or cleaning manager. Training in specialized cleaning (e.g., industrial disinfection) or hospitality management can lead to higher-level positions. Some workers start their own cleaning companies. Certifications from organizations such as ISSA (International Sanitary Supply Association) may enhance prospects.
