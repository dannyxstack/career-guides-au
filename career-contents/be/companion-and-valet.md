## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in Belgium?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Belgium vary based on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €1,800-€2,200 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €2,500-€3,500. Many positions include benefits like accommodation or meals. Exact pay is often negotiated privately with the employing household.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for a companion or valet to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU companions/valets typically need a single permit (work + residence). The employer must apply for a work authorization, proving the role cannot be filled by an EU citizen. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers (min. salary threshold ~€60,000/year), which is rarely applicable for valets. A professional card may also be required for self-employed services.

**Q: What qualifications or experience are needed to become a companion or valet in Belgium?**
A: Formal qualifications are not legally required, but employers often value discretion, excellent references, and experience in luxury hospitality. Knowledge of multiple languages (French, Dutch, English) is a strong asset. Training in etiquette, butler services, or household management (e.g., from the International Butler Academy) can improve prospects. Background checks are common.
