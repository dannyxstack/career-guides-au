## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health associate professionals in Belgium?**
A: Salaries vary by role and experience. For example, medical assistants or dental hygienists earn between €2,500 and €4,000 gross per month. Public sector positions may have standardized scales. Additional benefits like holiday pay and 13th month are common. Overall, average annual gross income ranges from €30,000 to €55,000.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU health associate professionals to work in Belgium?**
A: Non-EU professionals typically need a work permit via the single permit procedure or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer meeting a salary threshold (€65,000+ in 2024, or €50,000 for regulated professions). Alternatively, the single permit combines work and residence. Employer sponsorship is mandatory.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for health associate professionals in Belgium?**
A: Health associate professionals can advance through specialization or further education. For example, medical assistants may train to become nursing assistants or pursue bachelor's degrees in healthcare. Many employers offer internal promotions to senior roles. Continuous professional development is encouraged, with options like postgraduate certificates in specific healthcare fields.
