## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Belgium?**
A: As of 2024, the national minimum wage in Belgium is €1,995.83 gross per month for full-time employment. Most elementary workers earn at or near this level, though pay may vary by sector or collective agreement. Realistic gross monthly earnings for such roles typically range from €2,000 to €2,200.

**Q: Can non-EU workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for elementary jobs in Belgium?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for elementary occupations because it requires a highly qualified position. Non-EU workers may instead apply for a single permit (work permit) if the employer demonstrates a genuine labor shortage. This involves a labor market test by the regional employment service to confirm no suitable candidate from Belgium or the EU is available.
