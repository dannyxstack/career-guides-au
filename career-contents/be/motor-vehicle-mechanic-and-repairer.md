## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Belgium?**
A: In Belgium, entry-level motor vehicle mechanics earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while experienced mechanics can earn €2,500–€3,500 gross. Salaries vary by region, employer, and specialisation. Collective bargaining agreements (CPs) set minimum scales, and overtime or commissions are common.

**Q: Can motor vehicle mechanics obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Belgium?**
A: Non-EU mechanics typically need a Single Permit (combined work and residence). The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a recognized degree or high salary; mechanics without a degree usually don't qualify. However, if the job is on the shortage list (e.g., heavy vehicle mechanics), a Single Permit may be easier.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a motor vehicle mechanic in Belgium?**
A: Belgium requires a secondary education diploma in vehicle mechanics or a recognised vocational certificate (e.g., CEFA, Syntra). Some employers accept equivalent experience. Certification from the FPS Mobility (for vehicle safety inspections) may be needed. Dutch or French language skills are often required for customer-facing roles.
