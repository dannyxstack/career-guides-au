## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for concrete finishers in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, concrete placers and finishers typically earn between €1,200 and €1,800 gross per month, depending on experience and project complexity. Entry-level workers may start around €1,000, while experienced specialists can earn up to €2,200. Overtime and shift work can increase earnings further. Salaries are subject to standard income tax and social insurance contributions.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign concrete workers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work as concrete workers in Lithuania via a national visa (D visa) for employment, which requires a job offer and a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for non-EU highly qualified workers meeting salary and education criteria. Both routes involve employer sponsorship and compliance with Lithuanian immigration laws. No specific quotas apply.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a concrete finisher in Lithuania?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but many employers prefer candidates with a vocational certificate in construction or concrete works. Accredited programs are offered by vocational schools and training centers. Practical experience is highly valued, and apprenticeship programs combining on-the-job training with theoretical study are common. Some positions may require a health and safety certificate.
