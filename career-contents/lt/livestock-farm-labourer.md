## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience, farm size, and location. Overtime and weekend work may be compensated separately. Minimum wage is €924 per month as of 2025, but many employers offer slightly higher rates for animal care duties.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a livestock farm labourer in Lithuania through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as livestock farm labourers in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) if they have a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card is typically for highly skilled workers, so this occupation may not qualify. Alternatively, seasonal work permits exist for short-term agricultural employment.

**Q: What are the main duties and entry requirements for livestock farm labourers in Lithuania?**
A: Duties include feeding, watering, and monitoring livestock; cleaning pens; assisting with vaccinations or births; and maintaining farm equipment. No formal education is usually required, but on-the-job training is common. Physical fitness and basic animal knowledge are important. Lithuanian or Russian language skills can be beneficial for safety communication.
