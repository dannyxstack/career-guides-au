## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the average gross monthly salary for medical and dental prosthetic technicians ranges from €1,400 to €2,200, depending on experience and employer. Public sector salaries are typically lower, while private dental labs may offer higher pay. Overtime and specialization can increase earnings. Figures are based on 2024 labor market data.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU medical and dental prosthetic technicians to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU technicians can work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €19,584 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) is available for skilled workers meeting specific criteria. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior technician roles, specialize in implants or orthodontics, or become lab managers. Further education in dentistry or prosthodontics is possible. Some open their own dental laboratories. Professional certifications from the Lithuanian Dental Chamber can enhance prospects.
