## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for door-to-door salespersons in Lithuania?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Lithuania earn an average gross monthly salary between €800 and €1,200, combining a base rate with commissions. The national minimum wage is €924 per month as of 2025. Top performers can earn significantly more, but income is highly variable and depends on individual results and product type.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign door-to-door salespersons in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as door-to-door salespersons in Lithuania by obtaining a national work permit through a job offer. The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is generally unsuitable for this occupation due to the typical salary being below the threshold (1.5 times average wage) and higher education requirements.

**Q: What are the main job duties of a door-to-door salesperson in Lithuania?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Lithuania travel to residential areas to present and sell products or services directly to consumers. Key duties include approaching potential customers, demonstrating products, handling objections, completing sales orders, and attending training. They often work independently, managing their own routes and time, and strong communication skills are essential.
