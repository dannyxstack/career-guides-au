## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Child Care Services Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for Child Care Services Managers in Lithuania range from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience, institution size, and location. Public sector roles may offer lower but more stable pay. Senior managers in large private centers can earn at the higher end.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Child Care Services Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a work permit or EU Blue Card. Requirements include a confirmed job offer, a higher education degree in a relevant field, and a salary meeting the Blue Card threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross salary, around €2,100/month). Apply through the Lithuanian Migration Department.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a Child Care Services Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Key duties include overseeing daily operations, ensuring compliance with national childcare regulations, managing staff, developing educational curricula, coordinating with parents, and maintaining safety standards. They typically work in kindergartens, daycare centers, or early childhood education institutions.
