## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a visual artist in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for visual artists in Lithuania vary greatly depending on experience, reputation, and employment type. Freelance artists often have fluctuating income, while those employed in galleries, advertising, or education may earn between €1,000 and €2,500 per month gross. However, many supplement income with commissions, grants, or teaching.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign visual artists to work in Lithuania?**
A: Official routes include the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€1,884/month in 2024) and a higher education degree. Alternatively, artists can apply for a national D visa for self-employment or employment, requiring a work permit and relevant documentation.

**Q: What is the career outlook for visual artists in Lithuania?**
A: Career prospects often depend on networking, portfolio strength, and participation in local art scenes like Vilnius galleries or residency programs. Many artists combine studio practice with teaching, commissions, or commercial art. The market is competitive but offers opportunities in public art projects, cultural institutions, and the growing creative sector.
