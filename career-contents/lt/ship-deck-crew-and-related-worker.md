## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships' deck crew in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for ships' deck crew in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience, vessel type, and rank. Senior positions like boatswain can earn higher. Salaries are often contract-based and may include benefits such as accommodation and meals while at sea.

**Q: How can foreign ships' deck crew obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU crew typically need a national work visa (D visa) and a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; deck crew may not meet the salary threshold. Alternatively, seasonal work permits are available for short-term positions. All applications go through the Migration Department.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as ships' deck crew in Lithuania?**
A: You need a valid STCW certificate (basic safety training, etc.) and a seaman's book. For Lithuanian flag vessels, foreign certificates must be recognized by the Lithuanian Maritime Administration. English is commonly required, and Lithuanian language skills are beneficial. Many employers provide additional training.
