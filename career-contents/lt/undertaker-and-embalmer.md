## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for undertakers and embalmers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for undertakers and embalmers in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced professionals in larger cities can earn higher wages. Additional income may come from on-call duties or overtime.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign undertakers and embalmers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as undertakers/embalmers in Lithuania via a national work permit (requires employer sponsorship and a positive labor market test) or an EU Blue Card (requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage, currently around €30,000 annually). Both routes necessitate a valid job offer and compliance with immigration procedures.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an undertaker or embalmer in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, aspiring undertakers and embalmers typically need a secondary education and a vocational certificate in funeral services or related field. Registration with the State Register of Funeral Services is mandatory. Practical training through apprenticeships is common, and strong communication, empathy, and organizational skills are essential for client interactions and ceremonial duties.
