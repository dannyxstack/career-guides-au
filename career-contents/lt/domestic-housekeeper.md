## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Lithuania?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Lithuania typically earn the minimum wage, which is €730 per month (gross) in 2024, or slightly more depending on employer. Hourly rates range from €4 to €6. Many positions are informal; for formal employment, social insurance contributions are required. Salaries can be higher in urban areas like Vilnius.

**Q: What is the work visa route for domestic housekeepers moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU domestic housekeepers need a Lithuanian work permit (D type national visa) sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because the job is low-skilled and salary requirements (1.5x average wage) are not met. The employer must prove a labor market test. After 5 years, holders may apply for permanent residence.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for domestic housekeepers in Lithuania?**
A: Duties include cleaning, laundry, cooking, and sometimes childcare. Work is often in private homes, hotels, or offices. Hours may be flexible, including weekends. Many positions are part-time or informal, so formal contracts are less common. Experience is valued but formal training is not required. Physical stamina is important.
