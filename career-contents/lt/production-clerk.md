- Using AI prediction tools to optimise production scheduling and material allocation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a production clerk in Lithuania?**
A: According to data from the State Social Insurance Fund Board (SODRA), the average gross monthly salary for production clerks in Lithuania ranges between €1,200 and €1,500, depending on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced clerks can earn up to €1,800.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a production clerk in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card in Lithuania is reserved for highly qualified workers with higher education or five years of professional experience. Production clerks generally do not meet these criteria. Instead, non-EU citizens can obtain a national work permit if the employer secures a confirmation from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange, as per the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for production clerks in Lithuania?**
A: Production clerks in Lithuania manage inventory, process orders, coordinate with production teams, and maintain records. Entry-level positions require secondary education and basic computer skills. career advancement may lead to roles like logistics coordinator or warehouse supervisor, often with additional vocational training.
