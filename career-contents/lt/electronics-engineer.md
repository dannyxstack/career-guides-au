**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI simulation tools to quickly optimize circuit design parameters

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electronics engineer in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an electronics engineer in Lithuania is approximately €1,800–€2,500, depending on experience and industry. Senior engineers in sectors like telecommunications or manufacturing can earn up to €3,500. Entry-level positions typically start around €1,200–€1,500.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU electronics engineers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU electronics engineers can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold) or a National D Visa with a temporary residence permit for employment. Both require a valid job offer and employer sponsorship through the Lithuanian Migration Department.

**Q: Which industries in Lithuania most commonly employ electronics engineers?**
A: Electronics engineers in Lithuania are primarily employed in telecommunications, industrial automation, consumer electronics manufacturing, and research & development. Key employers include companies like Teltonika, Elgama-Elektronika, and startups in the laser and photonics sector. The growing tech hub in Vilnius also offers roles in embedded systems and IoT.
