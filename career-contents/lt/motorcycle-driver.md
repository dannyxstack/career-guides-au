## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for motorcycle drivers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for motorcycle drivers in Lithuania ranges from €900 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start near minimum wage (€924 as of 2025), while experienced drivers, especially those working for delivery platforms, can earn higher with bonuses.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for motorcycle drivers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU motorcycle drivers typically apply for a national work visa (D visa) and a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian salary, which motorcycle driving jobs usually do not meet. Thus, the standard work permit route is the primary option.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a motorcycle driver in Lithuania?**
A: You need a valid motorcycle driving license (category A or A1) and, in many cases, a professional driver qualification certificate. Employers may also require a clean criminal record, basic Lithuanian language skills, and familiarity with local routes. Experience with courier or food delivery services is often preferred.
