## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for hotel managers in Lithuania?**
A: Hotel managers in Lithuania earn between €1,800 and €3,200 gross per month, depending on the hotel size, location, and experience. Salaries in Vilnius and Kaunas tend to be higher. Additional benefits may include bonuses, accommodation, or meal allowances.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card requirements for hotel managers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work visa sponsored by a Lithuanian employer. For an EU Blue Card, you need a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently above 1.5 times the average gross salary). Both routes require a valid passport and health insurance.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for hotel managers in Lithuania?**
A: Hotel managers can advance to regional hotel manager, hospitality director, or open their own hotel. Lithuania’s growing tourism sector offers roles in luxury hotels, resorts, and boutique properties. Professional certifications from hospitality institutes can boost promotion prospects.
