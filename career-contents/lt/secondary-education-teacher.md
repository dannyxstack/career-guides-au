## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for secondary education teachers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for secondary education teachers in Lithuania is approximately 1,800 EUR as of 2023. Salaries vary by experience, school location, and additional duties. Entry-level teachers earn around 1,500 EUR, while those with seniority or in higher-demand subjects may earn up to 2,200 EUR.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU citizens to teach in secondary schools in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a national work permit or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment. The Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (about 2,700 EUR/month in 2023). Standard work permits have no fixed salary threshold but require employer sponsorship and labor market testing.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a secondary education teacher in Lithuania?**
A: To teach in secondary schools, you need a master's degree in education or in a specific subject with a pedagogical qualification. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Lithuanian Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education. Proficiency in Lithuanian is mandatory for teaching state-curriculum subjects, except for foreign language teachers.
