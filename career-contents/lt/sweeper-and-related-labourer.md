**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Lithuania?**
A: The typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Lithuania is around the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is set at €924 per month before taxes. Many employers offer the minimum wage or slightly more, depending on the region and full-time hours. Overtime and bonuses may increase total earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU nationals working as a sweeper in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as a sweeper in Lithuania through a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation, as it requires higher education or 5+ years of professional experience. The permit is issued by the Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior.

**Q: What are the typical working hours and conditions for sweepers in Lithuania?**
A: Sweepers in Lithuania generally work full-time, 40 hours per week, often early mornings or late evenings. Work may be outdoors or in industrial settings, requiring physical stamina. Employers must comply with labor laws regarding breaks, safety gear, and minimum rest periods. Overtime is possible but regulated.
