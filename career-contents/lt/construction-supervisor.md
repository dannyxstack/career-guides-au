## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a construction supervisor in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for construction supervisors in Lithuania ranges from 1,500 to 2,500 EUR, depending on experience, project complexity, and company size. According to the State Social Insurance Fund Board (SoDra), the median salary in 2024 is around 1,800 EUR. Senior supervisors or those managing large projects may earn above 2,500 EUR.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a foreign construction supervisor to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign construction supervisors can obtain a national visa (D) or an EU Blue Card. For the Blue Card, requirements include a higher education diploma (or 5 years of professional experience in the field) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approximately 2,500 EUR in 2024). The employer must also obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Employment Service, though the process may be expedited if the occupation is on the shortage list.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for construction supervisors in Lithuania?**
A: Construction supervisors can progress to roles such as senior supervisor, site manager, project manager, or construction manager. With additional certifications (e.g., from the Lithuanian Association of Civil Engineers) or a degree in construction management, they may lead larger teams or become self-employed contractors. The Lithuanian construction sector offers opportunities in both residential and infrastructure projects, with potential for growth into executive positions.
