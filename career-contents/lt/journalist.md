## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for journalists in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for journalists in Lithuania ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, with higher pay in national media or specialized roles. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while experienced journalists at major outlets can earn up to €3,000 or more, depending on the employer and location.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for journalists to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU journalists can work in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) if they have a job offer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold, which is typically 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania.

**Q: Do journalists need to speak Lithuanian to work in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, most journalistic roles in Lithuania require fluency in Lithuanian, as media primarily serves local audiences. Some international outlets or English-language news platforms may hire journalists without Lithuanian, but such positions are limited. Knowledge of Russian or other languages can be advantageous but is not mandatory.
