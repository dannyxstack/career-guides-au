## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fumigators in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Lithuania ranges from approximately 1000 to 1500 EUR gross, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced specialists can earn more, especially in larger cities like Vilnius.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as a fumigator in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a national visa (D) and a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; it may apply if the role requires higher education and meets salary thresholds, but typically fumigator roles do not qualify. Specific requirements should be checked with the Migration Department.

**Q: Do I need a special license to work as a fumigator in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, fumigators must hold a professional qualification or certificate in pest control, recognized in Lithuania. Training is available through vocational institutions. Additionally, handling certain chemicals requires a phytosanitary certificate or pesticide application license. Employers often provide training for specific methods.
