## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Engineering Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization and experience. Entry-level engineering professionals earn around €1,800–€2,500 gross monthly, while senior roles can reach €4,000–€5,500. The national average gross salary for engineers is approximately €2,500, with higher pay in sectors like IT, energy, and manufacturing.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU engineering professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU engineering professionals can apply for a National Visa (D) or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary. Alternatively, a work permit through a Lithuanian employer is possible for qualified roles.

**Q: What are the career prospects for engineering professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Demand is strong across industries like manufacturing, construction, and information technology. Cities such as Vilnius, Kaunas, and Klaipėda host many multinational companies and startups. Career advancement is possible through specialization in areas like automation, renewable energy, or project management, with opportunities for further qualification.
