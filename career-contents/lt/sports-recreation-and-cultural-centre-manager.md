## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers in Lithuania ranges from €1,800 to €3,000, depending on experience, facility size, and location. Senior managers in large urban centres may earn higher. Benefits and bonuses can also supplement base pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign managers can apply for a National Visa (D) for employment or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified work. Requirements include a valid job offer, relevant qualifications, and meeting salary thresholds. The employer must register the vacancy with the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. Detailed guidance is available from the Migration Department.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Lithuania?**
A: A degree in sports management, business administration, or a related field is commonly required. Relevant work experience in managing recreational or cultural facilities is essential. Proficiency in Lithuanian is often necessary for local operations, though English may suffice in international settings. Additional certifications in event management or facility operations can be advantageous.
