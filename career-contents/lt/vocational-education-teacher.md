## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for vocational education teachers in Lithuania?**
A: Vocational education teachers in Lithuania earn a gross monthly salary between €1,300 and €1,600, depending on experience and qualifications. This base pay can be supplemented with bonuses for extracurricular activities or special projects. Salaries are set by government pay scales, with annual increments for seniority.

**Q: Can non-EU vocational teachers work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU vocational teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer and a higher education qualification. The employer must sponsor the application, and the salary must meet the national threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, updated annually). The Blue Card grants residence and work rights for up to two years, renewable.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for vocational education teachers in Lithuania?**
A: Vocational teachers can progress to senior teacher or head of department roles, often requiring additional certification. They may also move into educational management, curriculum development, or teacher training. Continuous professional development is encouraged through state-funded programs, and experienced teachers can pursue roles in school administration or vocational guidance.
