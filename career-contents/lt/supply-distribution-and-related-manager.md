**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI for demand forecasting and inventory optimization

**Human moat:**

- Cross-departmental coordination and negotiation skills
- Emergency decision-making ability to handle sudden disruptions
- Maintenance of supplier relationships and long-term strategy
- Understand business objectives and set supply chain strategies.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Lithuania?**
A: According to the Lithuanian Department of Statistics, the median gross monthly salary for managers in wholesale, retail, and logistics sectors is approximately €2,500. Salaries vary widely based on company size, experience, and location, ranging from around €1,800 for entry-level to over €4,000 for senior roles in larger firms.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU Supply, Distribution and Related Managers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Alternatively, a national work permit is available for roles not meeting Blue Card criteria; employers must demonstrate a labor market test.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Managers can advance to senior roles such as Logistics Director, Supply Chain Director, or Operations Manager. Key skills include expertise in ERP systems (e.g., SAP), negotiation, and lean management. With experience, many move into executive positions or specialize in international logistics due to Lithuania's strategic trade location.
