## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the average gross monthly salary for this profession ranges from €1,800 to €3,200 depending on experience and sector. Public sector roles may be lower, while private industry and consulting offer higher pay. Senior specialists and managers can earn up to €4,500. Salaries are subject to income tax and social insurance contributions.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU professionals to work in Lithuania as an Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professional?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card with a higher education degree and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania (around €31,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a National Work Permit is available if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. Both require employer sponsorship and registration with the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What are the career prospects for Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Demand is growing due to EU regulatory compliance and increased focus on workplace safety. Opportunities exist in manufacturing, construction, energy, and government agencies. Career advancement includes senior specialist, manager, or consultant roles. Continuous education and certification from the Lithuanian Institute of Occupational Safety and Health can enhance prospects.
