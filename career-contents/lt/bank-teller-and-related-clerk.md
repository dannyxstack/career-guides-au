## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bank teller in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, a bank teller's gross monthly salary typically ranges from €900 to €1,400, depending on experience and the bank. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while experienced tellers can earn up to €1,400. Net salary after taxes and social contributions is roughly €700–€1,100.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as bank tellers in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as bank tellers in Lithuania through a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because bank teller positions usually do not meet the higher education or salary requirements. The Lithuanian National Visa (type D) for employment is the primary route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for bank tellers in Lithuania?**
A: Bank tellers in Lithuania can advance to roles such as customer service representative, personal banker, or branch operations supervisor. With additional training or a degree in finance, they may move into loan processing, financial advising, or management. Major banks also offer internal training programs for skill development.
