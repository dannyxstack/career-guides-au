**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and the healthcare institution. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles may reach €2,000. Salaries are typically higher in private clinics and major cities like Vilnius.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a foreign Medical Records and Health Information Technician to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreigners can work in Lithuania by obtaining a national work permit, sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is also an option if the job meets the required salary threshold (usually higher than the average wage) and the worker holds a higher education degree. However, for this occupation, a standard work permit is the more common route.

**Q: What are typical duties and career prospects for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Typical duties include organizing and managing patient health records, coding diagnoses and procedures, ensuring data accuracy, and handling electronic health record systems. Career prospects are positive due to increasing digitalization in healthcare. Technicians can advance to supervisory roles or specialize in health informatics, with opportunities in hospitals, clinics, and insurance companies.
