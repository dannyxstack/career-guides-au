## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for farm labourers in Lithuania vary widely, often based on hourly or piece-rate pay. The minimum monthly wage (2025) is €924 gross, but many workers earn between €700 and €1,200 gross per month for full-time seasonal work. Pay can be higher during harvest or with overtime.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Lithuania under a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a seasonal work permit (valid up to 9 months) for agricultural labour, including crop and livestock farming, under EU Directive 2014/36/EU. The EU Blue Card is not available for this low-skilled occupation. Employers must prove labour market shortages.

**Q: What are the main duties of mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Lithuania?**
A: Duties include planting, tending, and harvesting crops; feeding and caring for livestock; cleaning animal housing; operating farm machinery; and performing basic repairs. Workers may also assist with fencing, irrigation, and transporting produce. Tasks vary seasonally and are physically demanding.
