## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Mining Supervisor in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for mining supervisors in Lithuania ranges from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior positions or those in key mining regions may earn higher compensation.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU Mining Supervisors in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a National Visa (D) for employment or an EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian salary). The employer must obtain a labor market test and work permit before the application.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for mining supervisors in Lithuania?**
A: Experienced mining supervisors can advance to mine manager, operations director, or health and safety inspector roles. The sector is small but stable, with opportunities in companies like Achema or in consultancy. Further education in mining engineering may support advancement.
