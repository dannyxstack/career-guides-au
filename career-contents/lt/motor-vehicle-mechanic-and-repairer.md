**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults
- Fine disassembly and calibration requiring tactile feedback
- Comprehensive vehicle condition assessment and repair plan decision-making
- Customer trust communication and personalized service.

**Skills to build:**

- Mastery of AI diagnostic tools (e.g., Dealer-Fix, TEXA IDC5)
- Learning hybrid/electric vehicle high-voltage system repair
- Familiar with in-vehicle network diagnostics and software update processes
- Practice using AR-assisted maintenance equipment
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn the basics of data analysis and predictive maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for motor vehicle mechanics in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for motor vehicle mechanics in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level workers may earn around €800, while highly skilled specialists can earn up to €2,000. Overtime and bonuses are common in this field.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU motor vehicle mechanics in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU mechanics can work in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) with a valid employment offer and work permit. The EU Blue Card is an option only if you hold a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with Lithuanian labor market tests.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a motor vehicle mechanic in Lithuania?**
A: Completion of a vocational training program in vehicle repair or a related field is typically required. Many mechanics gain certification through the Lithuanian Labour Exchange or private institutions. Apprenticeships are common, and continuing education is needed for working with modern electronic systems. Language skills in Lithuanian or English are advantageous.
