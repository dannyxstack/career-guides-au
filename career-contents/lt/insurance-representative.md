## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Insurance Representatives in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Insurance Representatives in Lithuania typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, employer, and performance. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced representatives with strong sales records can earn €2,500 or more.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Insurance Representatives in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card to work as Insurance Representatives in Lithuania if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Alternatively, a National Visa (D) for employment is available if the Blue Card conditions are not met. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications or licenses are required to become an Insurance Representative in Lithuania?**
A: Insurance intermediaries must be registered with the Bank of Lithuania and hold a valid license. This requires completing an approved training course and passing a professional exam. Candidates with a higher education degree in finance, economics, or law may be exempt from the exam. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain the license.
