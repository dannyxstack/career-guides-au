- Automatic collection and preliminary analysis of athlete performance data
- Generate basic movement demonstrations and standardized training videos
- Automated Management of Training Schedules and Competition Arrangements

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use motion capture and AI analysis to accurately assess athletes' technical details
- Develop personalized training plans and nutrition programs based on big data models

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal leadership skills such as on-the-spot motivation and team cohesion building

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sports coaches in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sports coaches in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €2,500, depending on experience, sport, and employer. Coaches in popular sports like basketball or football may earn higher, especially in top clubs. Part-time or freelance coaches often earn less.

**Q: Can foreign sports coaches work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or work permit?**
A: Yes, non-EU sports coaches can work in Lithuania through the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (minimum 1.5x average gross salary, about €2,500/month in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit is possible for qualified coaches, subject to labor market needs. Both routes require employer sponsorship.
