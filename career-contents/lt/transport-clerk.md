## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for transport clerks in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for transport clerks in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced clerks in larger logistics companies can earn upwards of €1,800. Salaries are subject to income tax and social insurance contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as transport clerks in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Lithuania via a work permit or an EU Blue Card. For a work permit, an employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage. Both routes involve employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for transport clerks in Lithuania?**
A: Transport clerks can advance to logistics coordinator, dispatcher, or warehouse manager roles. With experience, they may specialize in international freight, customs documentation, or supply chain management. Pursuing certifications in logistics or transport management can enhance promotion prospects. Some clerks progress to operations or fleet management positions.
