## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a wood treater in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for wood treaters in Lithuania generally align with the national average for manufacturing workers. According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary in the wood processing industry is around €1,200–€1,500, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start closer to the minimum wage (€924 per month in 2025), while experienced treaters can earn higher.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work in Lithuania as a wood treater under the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: Non-EU wood treaters may obtain a national work visa (D visa) if they have a job offer and the employer secures a work permit. The EU Blue Card is less common for this occupation, as it requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold (€2,088 gross monthly in 2025). Lower-skilled wood treating typically falls under the national visa route.

**Q: What are the main duties of a wood treater in Lithuania?**
A: Wood treaters in Lithuania apply chemical preservatives to protect wood from decay, insects, and fire. They operate treatment tanks, mix solutions, and monitor processes to ensure compliance with safety and environmental standards. They also inspect finished products, maintain equipment, and record treatment data. The role is common in sawmills, furniture factories, and construction material suppliers.
