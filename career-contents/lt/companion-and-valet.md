**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI can analyze customer preference data to generate personalized service plans.
- Real-time translation tools for steward to communicate with multilingual clients and service staff
- AI security monitoring systems enhance customer privacy protection and security efficiency.
- Virtual concierge database enables fast retrieval of high-end resources (e.g., private doctors, celebrity chefs)

**Human moat:**

- Ultra-high-net-worth clients require a real human butler to provide emotional companionship and trust relationships.

- Privacy-sensitive tasks (e.g., handling confidential client documents, conversation confidentiality)
- Nuances of cultural etiquette (e.g., customs of different noble families) rely on human experience
- High-end social networking (e.g., introducing key contacts) cannot be replaced by AI

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for companions and valets in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Lithuania vary by employer and experience, but typically range from the minimum monthly wage (€924 in 2024) to around €1,200. Higher earnings are possible in luxury hotels or private households with responsibilities beyond basic care.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for companions and valets to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU companions and valets typically need a work permit and temporary residence permit. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as it requires higher education and a higher salary threshold, which this occupation usually does not meet. Employers must sponsor the work permit.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for companions and valets in Lithuania?**
A: Duties include personal care, companionship, household management, and running errands. Career prospects are limited; advancement may come through specialization (e.g., elderly care) or moving into supervisory roles in care facilities. Language skills and certifications can improve opportunities.
