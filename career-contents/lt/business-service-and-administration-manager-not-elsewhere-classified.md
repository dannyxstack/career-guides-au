- Use natural language processing tools to quickly generate business proposals and contracts

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for business services and administration managers in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for managers in this category ranges between €3,000 and €5,000, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior roles in larger firms may exceed this range.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for working as a business services manager in Lithuania?**
A: Qualified non-EU professionals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) is available for those meeting general employment requirements.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for business services and administration managers in Lithuania?**
A: Managers can advance to senior leadership roles such as director of operations, chief administrative officer, or regional manager. Additional certifications in project management or business administration, and experience in international settings, enhance prospects for higher-level positions.
