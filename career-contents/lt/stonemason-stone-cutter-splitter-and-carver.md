## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use CAD software for stone cutting and layout design
- Operating automatic stone cutting machines and CNC engraving machines
- Material quantity calculation and layout using BIM models
- Use robots for repetitive stone handling and laying

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using 3D scanning and digital twins for precise stone structure installation
- Use AR glasses to overlay design drawings on construction sites, reducing errors
- Using AI to optimize stone splicing plans, improving aesthetics and strength
- Conduct drone inspections of large stone structures and remotely monitor maintenance needs.

**Human moat:**

- Artistic judgment for complex stone carving and hand modeling
- Adaptive skills for integrated on-site stone and structure installation
- Empirical judgment of stone texture, color, and physical properties
- Ability to communicate customized design solutions with clients

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/BIM software operation (e.g., AutoCAD, Revit)
- CNC stone processing equipment programming and operation
- 3D scanning and digital modeling skills
- Engineering surveying and precision installation technology
- Drone operation and image analysis basics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Lithuania?**
A: As of 2024, the average gross monthly salary for a stonemason in Lithuania ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and specialization. Overtime and skills in carving can increase earnings. Salaries are typically higher in Vilnius and other major cities.

**Q: Can a foreign stonemason work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or work permit?**
A: Yes. Non-EU stonemasons can obtain a Lithuanian national work permit (D visa) if they have a confirmed job offer. The EU Blue Card is also available for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree or five years of professional experience, and the salary must meet the official national threshold set by the Lithuanian government.

**Q: What is the career outlook for stonemasons in Lithuania?**
A: Lithuania has a strong demand for stonemasons due to ongoing construction and restoration of historic stone buildings. Career paths include advancement to master stonemason, project supervisor, or self-employment. Apprenticeships and vocational training are available. The profession offers stable employment, especially for those skilled in restoration and artistic carving.
