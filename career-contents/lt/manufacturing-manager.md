## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Manufacturing Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for Manufacturing Managers in Lithuania vary by experience and industry. According to official labor statistics, the gross monthly salary typically ranges from €3,500 to €6,500. Entry-level positions start around €2,800, while senior managers can earn over €8,000. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: Can a foreign Manufacturing Manager work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, the EU Blue Card is a valid route for highly qualified non-EU professionals, including Manufacturing Managers. Requirements include a university degree or five years of relevant professional experience, a work contract for at least one year, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €2,700/month in 2024). Apply through the Migration Department.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Manufacturing Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Manufacturing Managers in Lithuania can progress to senior roles such as Plant Manager, Operations Director, or Vice President of Manufacturing. Further advancement may involve moving into corporate strategy or consultancy. Continuous improvement in lean management, automation, and supply chain skills enhances prospects. Many also pursue executive MBA programs.
