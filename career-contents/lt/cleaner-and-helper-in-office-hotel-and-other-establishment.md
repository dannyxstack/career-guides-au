## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners in Lithuania?**
A: The typical salary for cleaners in offices, hotels, and other establishments in Lithuania is based on the national minimum wage, which in 2024 is €924 per month (gross). Many employers pay the minimum wage, though some offer slightly higher rates. Overtime and night shifts may earn additional pay according to labor laws.

**Q: What are the visa options for a cleaner wanting to work in Lithuania?**
A: Cleaners do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which is for highly skilled workers. Instead, non-EU citizens can obtain a national work visa (D visa) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must prove a labor shortage and obtain a work permit. The process requires a job offer and meeting minimum wage requirements. Quotas may apply.

**Q: What are the main duties of cleaners in Lithuanian hotels and offices?**
A: Cleaners in Lithuania ensure cleanliness in offices, hotels, and other establishments. Duties include sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, cleaning restrooms, emptying trash, and replenishing supplies. In hotels, they also make beds and change linens. Attention to detail and reliability are key. Tasks are often performed independently or as part of a team.
