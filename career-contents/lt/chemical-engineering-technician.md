## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Chemical Engineering Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly salary for Chemical Engineering Technicians in Lithuania typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800 gross, depending on experience and industry sector. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior technicians in sectors like pharmaceuticals or oil refining can earn higher. Overtime and bonuses may also apply.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for Chemical Engineering Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work as Chemical Engineering Technicians in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Alternatively, a national work permit is available for specialist roles. Both routes require employer sponsorship and residence permit application.

**Q: What qualifications and career prospects are there for Chemical Engineering Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Chemical Engineering Technicians in Lithuania typically need a vocational or bachelor's degree in chemical engineering or a related field. Proficiency in English or Lithuanian may be required. Career progression includes roles like process technician, quality control supervisor, or laboratory manager. The chemical and pharmaceutical sectors in Lithuania offer growing opportunities.
