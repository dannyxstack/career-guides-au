## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for kitchen helpers in Lithuania ranges from 700 to 900 EUR, with entry-level positions near the national minimum wage of 730 EUR (as of 2024). Salaries may be higher in Vilnius or larger restaurants. Overtime and tips can supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as kitchen helpers in Lithuania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Kitchen helpers are not eligible for the EU Blue Card, as it requires higher-skilled occupations. Non-EU nationals may obtain a national work visa if an employer sponsors them and proves the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Check with the Lithuanian Migration Department for current shortage lists and requirements.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for kitchen helpers in Lithuania?**
A: Kitchen helpers can advance to cook or chef positions through on-the-job training or vocational courses in culinary arts. Many employers offer internal promotion paths. With experience, one may become a sous chef or kitchen manager. Language skills (Lithuanian) often help in career growth.
