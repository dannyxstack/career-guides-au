## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Construction Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Construction Managers in Lithuania typically earn a gross monthly salary between €3,000 and €5,000, depending on experience, project size, and location. Senior managers on large projects can earn up to €6,000 or more. These figures are pre-tax; net income is lower after social security and income tax.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign Construction Manager to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign Construction Managers can work in Lithuania via a work permit or the EU Blue Card. For a work permit, you need a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or equivalent experience) and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Applications are made through the Migration Department.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Construction Manager in Lithuania?**
A: A bachelor's degree in civil engineering, construction management, or a related field is typically required. Professional experience is highly valued, and registration with the Lithuanian Association of Civil Engineers may enhance prospects. Knowledge of local building regulations and project management skills are essential for career advancement.
