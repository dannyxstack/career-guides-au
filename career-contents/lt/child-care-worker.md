## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a child care worker in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, child care workers earn a gross monthly salary ranging from €700 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced workers in private kindergartens may earn up to €1,200. Public sector wages are often lower.

**Q: What are the routes for a foreign child care worker to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as child care workers in Lithuania via a standard work visa, which requires a job offer and a work permit from the Employment Service. The EU Blue Card, designed for highly qualified workers, typically requires a salary threshold higher than child care positions offer, so the standard work visa is the more common route.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for child care workers in Lithuania?**
A: Child care workers can advance to head teacher, kindergarten director, or specialize in early childhood education. Additional qualifications, such as a bachelor's degree in education, can lead to higher positions. In Lithuania, demand for qualified child care staff is growing, offering stable employment and opportunities for advancement.
