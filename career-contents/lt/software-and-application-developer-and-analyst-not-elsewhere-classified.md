## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for software developers in Lithuania?**
A: As of 2023, the average gross monthly salary for software developers in Lithuania ranges from €2,500 to €5,000 depending on experience and specialization. Junior developers earn around €2,000, while senior roles can exceed €6,000. Salaries are competitive within the Baltic region, with Vilnius offering the highest pay.

**Q: What work visa options are available for software developers moving to Lithuania?**
A: Foreign software developers can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage. Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) is available for non-EU nationals. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with immigration procedures.

**Q: What is the job outlook for software developers in Lithuania?**
A: The demand for software developers in Lithuania remains strong, driven by a growing tech sector and foreign investment. Major hubs like Vilnius and Kaunas host numerous startups and global IT companies. Employment growth is expected to continue, with opportunities in fintech, cybersecurity, and AI. English proficiency is often required for international roles.
