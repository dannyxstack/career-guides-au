## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Research and Development Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for R&D Managers in Lithuania typically range from €4,000 to €7,000 gross per month, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior roles in sectors like biotechnology or IT can exceed €8,000. According to state data, the median gross monthly salary for managers in this category is around €5,500.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card routes for R&D Managers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU R&D Managers can apply for a National Visa (D) with a work permit, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania (as of 2025, about €27,000). Both routes need a recognized educational qualification and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become an R&D Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in a relevant scientific or engineering field is required, with a PhD often preferred for advanced R&D roles. Employers seek 5–10 years of progressive experience in research and development, including team leadership. Strong project management skills and familiarity with EU funding programs are advantageous.
