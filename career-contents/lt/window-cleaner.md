## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for window cleaners in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for window cleaners in Lithuania ranges from €700 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. The minimum monthly wage is €924 as of 2024, so entry-level positions may start near that. Experienced cleaners or those working for specialized companies can earn higher wages.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as window cleaners in Lithuania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa to be employed as window cleaners in Lithuania. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, which this occupation typically does not meet. Employers must obtain a work permit on behalf of the worker.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for window cleaners in Lithuania?**
A: Window cleaners in Lithuania can advance to supervisory roles, become team leaders, or start their own cleaning business. Additional certifications in safety or specialized cleaning techniques can lead to higher-paying contracts, such as working on high-rise buildings or industrial sites.
