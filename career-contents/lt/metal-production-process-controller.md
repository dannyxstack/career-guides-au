## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Metal Production Process Controllers in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, Metal Production Process Controllers earn an average gross monthly salary ranging from €1,800 to €2,600, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior specialists in metallurgical plants or with additional qualifications may earn higher wages. Entry-level positions typically start around €1,400.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Metal Production Process Controllers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Metal Production Process Controllers in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education diploma (at least 3 years) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €2,000 in 2023). Alternatively, a national work permit can be obtained for skilled workers meeting local labour market criteria, requiring employer sponsorship and a valid employment contract.

**Q: What qualifications and career progression exist for Metal Production Process Controllers in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, employers require a secondary vocational education in metallurgy or related fields, often complemented by on-the-job training. Advanced roles may need a bachelor's degree in materials engineering or industrial automation. Career progression leads to senior process controller, shift supervisor, or production manager positions in steel mills, foundries, or metal processing plants.
