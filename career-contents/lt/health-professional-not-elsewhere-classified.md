## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: Health professionals in this category, such as dietitians, audiologists, and optometrists, earn an average gross monthly salary of €1,500 to €2,500 in Lithuania. Salaries vary by sector, with private practice often paying more than public healthcare. Experience and specialization also influence earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for non-EU health professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU health professionals can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a recognized degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a national work permit is employer-sponsored and does not require a salary threshold but still needs job market testing.

**Q: What types of jobs are included in the 'health professionals not elsewhere classified' category in Lithuania?**
A: This category covers allied health professionals like dietitians, audiologists, speech therapists, optometrists, and physiotherapists who are not classified elsewhere. In Lithuania, they work in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, schools, or private clinics. Recognition of foreign qualifications by the Lithuanian health ministry is mandatory.
