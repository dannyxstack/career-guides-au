## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Lithuania is approximately €1,000–€1,300, depending on employer and region. Pay may include bonuses for targets or overtime. The minimum wage is currently €924 gross per month. Salaries are generally lower in rural areas.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes to work as a meter reader in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a national visa (D) and a work permit, typically requiring a Labour Market Test. The EU Blue Card is theoretically available but rarely applicable, as it demands a high-salary threshold (about €4,396/month) and higher education, which meter readers usually do not meet. Thus, the standard work permit route is most realistic.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for meter readers in Lithuania?**
A: Meter readers can advance to team leaders, route supervisors, or data analysis roles within energy or vending companies. Additional training in customer service or technical skills may lead to positions in metering system maintenance or billing. Some move into logistics or field service management. Lithuanian companies often support internal promotion.
