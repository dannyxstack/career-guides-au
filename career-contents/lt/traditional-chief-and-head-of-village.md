## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a traditional chief or head of village in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the role of traditional chief or head of village is ceremonial and unpaid. No official salary data exists as it is not a recognized employment category. Any compensation is symbolic and varies by community, often provided as voluntary contributions.

**Q: Can I obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a traditional chief in Lithuania?**
A: No. This occupation is not eligible for the EU Blue Card or a Lithuanian work visa because it is not a standard profession. Work permits require a recognized job offer in an occupation listed in the national classification, which does not include traditional chiefs.

**Q: How does one become a traditional chief or head of a village in Lithuania?**
A: Becoming a traditional chief in Lithuania is typically based on community recognition, heritage, or election within ethnic minority groups. There is no formal education or training required, and the position is usually voluntary or honorary, not a career path.
