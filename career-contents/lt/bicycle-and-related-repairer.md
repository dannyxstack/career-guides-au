## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bicycle repairers in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, bicycle repairers typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage, while skilled technicians in larger cities or specialized shops can earn higher. Overtime and seasonal demand can affect earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card options for bicycle repairers moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU bicycle repairers can work in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) or EU Blue Card. The D visa requires a job offer and a work permit from the Employment Service. The Blue Card is for highly qualified jobs, typically requiring a higher education degree; for repairers, a vocational qualification may suffice if the job is considered highly skilled. Both routes involve employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the career prospects for bicycle repairers in Lithuania?**
A: Bicycle repairers in Lithuania can advance to senior technician roles, open their own repair shops, or specialize in electric bikes and high-end models. With the growing cycling culture and e-bike popularity, demand is stable. Additional certifications in bicycle mechanics can improve job opportunities and salary potential.
