## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a medical secretary in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly salary for medical secretaries in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,500 gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while senior roles in private clinics or hospitals in Vilnius can exceed €1,600. Additional benefits such as health insurance are sometimes included.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a medical secretary in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card for Lithuania if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). However, medical secretary roles typically require vocational training, not a degree, so the standard work permit (D visa) may be more applicable. Check with the Lithuanian Migration Department.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a medical secretary in Lithuania?**
A: Employers usually require a secondary education diploma and a vocational training certificate in medical administration or a related field, often obtainable at professional colleges. Key skills include knowledge of medical terminology, Lithuanian language proficiency, computer literacy, and experience with healthcare management software. Some clinics may prefer prior administrative experience in a healthcare setting.
