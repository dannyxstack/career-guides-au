## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,300, depending on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced operators in larger industrial plants may earn up to €1,500. These figures are based on 2024 industry reports and job postings.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as weaving and knitting machine operators in Lithuania through official routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a Lithuanian work visa or EU Blue Card. For this occupation, the standard work permit process applies: employer obtains a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange, then you apply for a national visa (type D). The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a higher education degree and salary threshold; it may not suit this role. Ensure employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main duties of weaving and knitting machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: Operators set up, monitor, and adjust weaving or knitting machines to produce textiles. Duties include loading yarn, inspecting fabric for defects, cleaning and lubricating machines, and performing minor repairs. In Lithuania, many work in textile factories producing clothing, home textiles, or industrial fabrics. Attention to detail and basic technical skills are important.
