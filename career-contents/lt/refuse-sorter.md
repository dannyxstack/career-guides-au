## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for refuse sorters in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly salary for refuse sorters in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (€730 gross as of 2025). Overtime and shift differentials can increase earnings. Salaries are typically paid monthly.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as refuse sorters in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Refuse sorters are low-skilled occupations, so the EU Blue Card (which requires higher education or 5+ years of professional experience) generally does not apply. Non-EU citizens can obtain a Lithuanian work permit through employer sponsorship under the national immigration scheme, provided the employer proves labor market demand.

**Q: What are the main job duties and working conditions for refuse sorters in Lithuania?**
A: Refuse sorters manually separate recyclable materials from waste at sorting facilities. Work involves standing for long periods, lifting heavy items, and exposure to odors. Shifts often include early mornings, nights, or weekends. Employers provide personal protective equipment. Training is typically on-site and lasts a few days.
