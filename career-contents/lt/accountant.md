## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an accountant in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an accountant in Lithuania ranges from €1,500 to €2,800, depending on experience and certifications. Entry-level accountants earn around €1,200, while senior or ACCA-qualified accountants can earn up to €3,500. Salaries are typically higher in Vilnius and for corporate roles.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign accountant to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU accountants can work via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Alternatively, a national work permit applies for other positions. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an accountant in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or economics is required. Professional certifications such as ACCA, CFA, or membership in the Lithuanian Association of Accountants enhance career prospects. Knowledge of Lithuanian tax laws and fluency in Lithuanian is often expected, though English may suffice in multinational companies.
