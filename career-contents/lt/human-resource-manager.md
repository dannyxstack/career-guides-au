## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Human Resource Managers in Lithuania?**
A: According to the State Social Insurance Fund Board (Sodra), the average gross monthly salary for Human Resource Managers in Lithuania ranges from €3,500 to €4,000. Actual pay varies by experience, company size, and location, with Vilnius typically offering higher compensation.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as Human Resource Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or a National D Visa. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (set annually by the government). A work permit is also mandatory; employers typically handle the application.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an HR Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Employers usually require a bachelor’s degree in human resources, business administration, or a related field, plus several years of HR experience. Professional certifications (e.g., SHRM, CIPD) are valued. Proficiency in Lithuanian and English is often necessary, and strong knowledge of Lithuanian labor law is essential.
