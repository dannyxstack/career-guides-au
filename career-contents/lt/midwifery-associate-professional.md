## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwifery associate professional in Lithuania?**
A: According to the latest data from the Lithuanian State Social Insurance Fund (Sodra), the average gross monthly salary for midwifery associate professionals ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior professionals in private clinics can earn up to €2,200. These figures are approximate and may vary.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for a foreign midwifery associate professional to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign midwifery associate professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card or a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a higher-education diploma and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. Alternatively, a national visa (type D) for employment can be obtained, provided the employer sponsors the application and the applicant has their professional qualifications recognized by the Lithuanian Ministry of Health.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a midwifery associate professional in Lithuania?**
A: To practice, one must hold a recognized diploma in midwifery and obtain a health specialist license from the Lithuanian Ministry of Health. Foreign qualifications require recognition through the Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education (SKVC). Additionally, registration with the State Healthcare Accreditation Service and proficiency in Lithuanian (at least B2 level) are mandatory. Continuing education is also required to maintain the license.
