## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Government Regulatory Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by institution and experience, but the average gross monthly salary for mid-level regulatory associates in Lithuania ranges from €1,600 to €2,500. Public sector roles often include additional benefits. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior roles can exceed €3,200.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a Government Regulatory Associate Professional in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times average gross annual salary). Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) is available for specialized roles. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Migration Department.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for Government Regulatory Associate roles in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in law, public administration, economics, or a related field is required. Fluency in Lithuanian and English is essential, as regulatory work involves drafting documents and liaising with EU institutions. Relevant experience in government or regulatory bodies is highly valued.
