## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Sales Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for ICT sales professionals in Lithuania typically ranges from €2,000 to €5,000, depending on experience and company size. Senior positions can exceed €6,000. Many roles also include performance-based bonuses and commissions, which can significantly increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as an ICT Sales Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid job offer meeting a salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Alternatively, a National Visa (D) for employment is possible after the employer obtains a work permit. Both routes lead to a temporary residence permit.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for ICT Sales Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Employers seek strong technical knowledge of IT products (cloud, cybersecurity, SaaS), combined with sales acumen, communication, negotiation, and client relationship management. Fluency in English and Lithuanian is often required. Experience with CRM software and understanding of B2B sales cycles are highly valued in Lithuania's growing ICT sector.
