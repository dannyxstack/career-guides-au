## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Information and Communications Technology Services Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for ICT Services Managers in Lithuania vary by experience and company size. As of 2023, the gross monthly salary typically ranges from €4,000 to €7,000, with senior roles in larger firms or multinationals reaching higher. According to the State Social Insurance Board (SODRA), the average gross monthly wage in the ICT sector is around €5,500, but managerial positions command a premium.

**Q: How can a foreign national work as an ICT Services Manager in Lithuania through official migration routes?**
A: Foreign nationals can work as ICT Services Managers in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card scheme (for highly qualified workers) or a National Work Permit. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Alternatively, employers can sponsor a work permit if the role meets labor market needs. Applications are processed by the Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for ICT Services Managers in Lithuania's growing tech sector?**
A: Lithuania's booming tech ecosystem offers ICT Services Managers roles in fintech, cybersecurity, and SaaS, with hubs in Vilnius and Kaunas. Career progression includes moving into CTO or CIO positions, or specializing in cloud infrastructure or IT strategy. The country's competitive digital infrastructure and EU market access make it attractive for career growth, with many international companies expanding their operations in Lithuania.
