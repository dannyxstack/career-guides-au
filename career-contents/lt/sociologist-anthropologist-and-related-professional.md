## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sociologists in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for sociologists in Lithuania vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions in research or public sector earn around €1,200–€1,600 gross per month. Senior roles or those in private market research can reach €2,000–€3,000 gross monthly. The national average for social science professionals is approximately €1,800 gross per month.

**Q: Can I work in Lithuania as a sociologist via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified non-EU professionals with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). You need a higher education degree and a work contract of at least one year. The employer must apply for a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for sociologists in Lithuania?**
A: Sociologists in Lithuania work in academic research, public policy analysis, market research, and NGOs. The largest employers are universities, government institutions (e.g., Statistics Lithuania), and private research firms. Demand is steady but competitive, with networking and Lithuanian language skills significantly improving job prospects.
