## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Social Work Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Social Work Associate Professionals in Lithuania ranges from approximately €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, employer (public or private sector), and region. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior roles in specialized fields can exceed €1,800. Salaries are typically higher in Vilnius.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU Social Work Associate Professional to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Social Work Associate Professionals via a standard work permit (issued based on a job offer and labor market test) or the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (approximately 1.5 times the average gross salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a Social Work Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a higher education degree in social work, social pedagogy, or a related field is required. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Lithuanian Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education. Additionally, good Lithuanian language skills (B2 level or higher) are often mandatory, especially for client-facing roles in public institutions.
