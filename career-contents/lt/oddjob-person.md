## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for odd-job persons in Lithuania?**
A: Hourly wages for odd-job persons in Lithuania typically match the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €5.65 per hour (€924 monthly for full-time). Actual pay varies by employer and task complexity, with some assignments offering higher rates. Many workers are paid per assignment rather than on a salary basis, so total monthly income can fluctuate.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as odd-job persons in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals are generally not eligible for the EU Blue Card for odd-job roles as it requires a highly skilled position with salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Instead, they can pursue a national D type work visa or a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a valid job offer from a Lithuanian employer that meets the minimum wage and labor market tests.

**Q: What career prospects do odd-job persons have in Lithuania?**
A: Odd-job persons in Lithuania typically work on short-term contracts in sectors like construction, cleaning, or agriculture. Career progression is limited without further training, but some individuals upgrade their skills through vocational courses in trades such as painting, plumbing, or electrical work, leading to more stable, higher-paying jobs and possible specialization.
