## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for personnel and careers professionals in Lithuania ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience and company size. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior specialists can earn up to €3,500. These figures are based on 2023 data from state statistics and job portals.

**Q: Can Personnel and Careers Professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can apply for a national work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage (approx. €2,200 monthly in 2023). Work visas are employer-sponsored and subject to labor market tests.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Personnel and Careers Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in human resources, psychology, business, or a related field is required. Professional certifications like CIPD or SHRM are valued. Knowledge of Lithuanian labor law and fluency in Lithuanian and English are essential. Employers often prefer candidates with at least 2 years of relevant experience.
