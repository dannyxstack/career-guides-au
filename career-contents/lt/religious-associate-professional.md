## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for religious associate professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Religious associate professionals in Lithuania, such as pastoral workers or religious educators, typically earn between €900 and €1,400 gross per month. Salaries vary based on experience, employer type (e.g., church or non-profit), and location. Urban areas like Vilnius may offer slightly higher pay. This role often includes non-monetary benefits like accommodation.

**Q: Can religious associate professionals obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting a minimum salary threshold (currently about €1,904 gross/month in Lithuania). Religious associate professionals typically fall under a different category, such as a national work permit. Non-EU candidates must secure a job offer and apply for a temporary residence permit for employment.
