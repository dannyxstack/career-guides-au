## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gallery, museum and library technicians in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for these technicians in Lithuania typically ranges from 1,000 to 1,500 EUR, depending on experience, location, and type of institution. Entry-level positions may start around 800 EUR, while senior technicians can earn up to 2,000 EUR. Net salary after taxes and social contributions is approximately 70-75% of gross.

**Q: How can a foreigner obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card as a gallery, museum or library technician in Lithuania?**
A: Foreigners can apply for a national visa (D visa) for employment, which requires a work permit obtained by the employer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average). Both routes lead to a temporary residence permit.

**Q: What education and skills are required for gallery, museum and library technician roles in Lithuania?**
A: Most positions require at least a vocational qualification or bachelor's degree in museology, library science, history, or a related field. Practical experience through internships is highly valued. Key skills include digital cataloging, conservation techniques, customer service, and familiarity with heritage management systems. Knowledge of Lithuanian is often required for public-facing roles.
