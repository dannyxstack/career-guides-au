## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services Agents in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience and location. Entry-level positions may earn around €1,000-€1,300 gross per month, while experienced agents can earn €1,500-€2,500 or more. Major cities like Vilnius often offer higher pay. Additional benefits may be included.

**Q: What work visa options exist for Business Services Agents to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign nationals can apply for a National Visa (D) for employment, requiring a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers meeting a salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Both routes require a job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Business Services Agents in Lithuania?**
A: Business Services Agents can advance to team leader or manager roles within their organizations. Specializing in areas like logistics, finance, or IT services can open up higher-level positions. Continuous professional development and language skills (English, Lithuanian) are beneficial for growth.
