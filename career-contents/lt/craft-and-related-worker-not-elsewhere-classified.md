## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specific trade and experience. According to official statistics, the median gross monthly salary for craft and related workers in Lithuania is approximately €1,200, with entry-level positions starting around €900 and experienced workers earning up to €1,800. Actual pay depends on the employer, region, and specialization.

**Q: How can non-EU craft and related workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU workers typically need a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Labor Exchange. For the EU Blue Card, applicants need a higher education degree or equivalent skills and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary. Specific requirements are set by the Migration Department.

**Q: What are common tasks for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: These workers perform specialized manual tasks not covered by other craft occupations, such as musical instrument making, glass engraving, or textile repair. Duties include interpreting blueprints, using hand tools and machinery, quality inspection, and finishing products. They often work in small workshops or manufacturing settings, requiring precision and attention to detail.
