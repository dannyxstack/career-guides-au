## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Government Social Benefits Officials in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for government social benefits officials in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, region, and specific agency. Public sector salaries are set by law and may include performance bonuses. Net income is lower after taxes and social security contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Government Social Benefits Officials in Lithuania through the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in this role via a national work permit (D visa) or the EU Blue Card if they meet high-skilled criteria. However, government positions often require Lithuanian language proficiency and citizenship or permanent residency. The employer must obtain a labor market test, and the job must be listed in shortage occupations for Blue Card eligibility.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Government Social Benefits Official in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in social work, public administration, law, or a related field is required. Fluency in Lithuanian is mandatory. Entry-level positions may require passing a civil service exam. Ongoing training in social security legislation and case management is provided. Advancement opportunities include senior specialist or managerial roles.
