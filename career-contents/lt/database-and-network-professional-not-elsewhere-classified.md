## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Database and Network Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for Database and Network Professionals in Lithuania typically range from €2,000 to €4,000 gross per month, depending on experience, skills, and company size. Senior roles in Vilnius or with cloud expertise can exceed €5,000. The average is around €3,000 gross monthly, making it competitive within the tech sector.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for foreign Database and Network Professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work via the EU Blue Card, requiring a higher education degree (or 5+ years experience) and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold. Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) is possible if the employer obtains a labor market test. EU/EEA citizens have free movement rights.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for Database and Network Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Lithuania’s growing tech sector offers roles in database administration, network engineering, cloud architecture, and cybersecurity. Major employers include fintech, startups, and global IT service centers. Skills in SQL, Python, AWS/Azure, and network security are in high demand. Progression to senior specialist, architect, or management is common.
