## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for accounting and bookkeeping clerks in Lithuania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for accounting clerks in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €2,000 for entry-level positions, with experienced clerks earning up to €2,800. Salaries vary by city (Vilnius tends to be higher), company size, and specific responsibilities.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign accounting clerks to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as accounting clerks via a national work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage (approx. €1,500 gross/month in 2024). Official routes are managed by the Migration Department.

**Q: What education or qualifications are required to become an accounting clerk in Lithuania?**
A: Most employers require at least a secondary education with specialized accounting courses or a vocational diploma. Knowledge of Lithuanian accounting standards (VAS) and proficiency in Lithuanian are often mandatory. Experience with software like SAP or Oracle is advantageous. Professional certifications (e.g., ACCA) can enhance prospects.
