**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Long-distance highway driving (autonomous truck pilot programs)
- Basic route planning and GPS navigation adjustments
- Basic vehicle status monitoring and routine inspection recording
- Standardized cargo handling (integration with automated warehousing)
- Fatigue driving risk detection (AI takeover warning)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Dynamic route optimization (real-time traffic, weather, queue times)
- Vehicle maintenance prediction (AI analysis engine data for early warning)

- Multimodal transport coordination (rail, port, warehouse system integration)

**Human moat:**

- Complex loading/unloading and on-site flexibility (e.g., construction sites, refrigerated trucks)
- Driving on unstructured roads in remote areas
- Customer relationships and non-standard delivery requirements
- Rapid decision-making in emergencies (accidents, failures)
- Compliance and safety responsibilities (cargo inspection, dangerous goods transport)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for heavy truck drivers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for heavy truck and lorry drivers in Lithuania vary by experience and employer. Typical net monthly earnings range between €1,000 and €1,500. Overtime and international routes can increase pay. The minimum monthly wage in 2025 is €924, but most drivers earn above that.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU heavy truck drivers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a national visa (type D) and a work permit, which the employer must obtain from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. The EU Blue Card is typically not applicable as it requires higher education and a high salary threshold. Seasonal work permits are available for short-term contracts.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to drive heavy trucks in Lithuania?**
A: You need a valid C or CE driving license, a professional driver qualification certificate (CPC), and a medical certificate. For international transport, a digital tachograph card is required. Licenses from other EU countries are recognised; non-EU licenses may need conversion after passing a driving test.
