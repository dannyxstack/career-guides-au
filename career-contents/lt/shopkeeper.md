## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shopkeeper in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a shopkeeper in Lithuania ranges from €900 to €1,300, depending on factors like store size, location, and experience. Entry-level positions may start near the minimum wage (€924 as of 2025), while experienced shopkeepers in larger cities can earn more. Salaries are typically paid 12 times per year.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a shopkeeper moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as a shopkeeper in Lithuania by obtaining a national work visa or temporary residence permit for employment. An employer must offer a job and demonstrate a labor market need. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for shopkeepers as it requires a higher education degree and a salary significantly above the national average. The standard work permit route is the primary option.

**Q: What are typical career progression opportunities for shopkeepers in Lithuania?**
A: Shopkeepers in Lithuania can advance to managing larger stores, become retail chain supervisors, or open independent shops. With experience, they may transition into regional management or purchasing roles. Some pursue further education in retail management to qualify for higher positions. Opportunities are stronger in cities like Vilnius, Kaunas, and Klaipėda.
