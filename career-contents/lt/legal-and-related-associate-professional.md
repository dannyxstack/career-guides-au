## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Legal and Related Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for legal associate professionals in Lithuania ranges from €1,400 to €2,200, depending on experience, specialization, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles in larger firms may exceed €2,500. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: How can a foreigner work as a Legal Associate Professional in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5× average gross salary, currently around €2,100 monthly). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Work permit and residence permit follow.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Legal Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in law or a related field is required. For some roles, such as bailiff assistants, specific certification is needed. Proficiency in Lithuanian is essential, and additional courses in European law or notarial practice may enhance prospects. Experience in legal offices is highly valued.
