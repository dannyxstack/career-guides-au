## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for creative and performing artists in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, salaries for artists vary widely by field and experience. According to 2023 data, median monthly gross earnings for ISCO 265 occupations range from €1,200 to €1,800. Many work freelance or part-time, so actual income may be lower. Top earners in theatre or film can reach €3,000+.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for creative artists to work in Lithuania?**
A: The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified artists with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average annual wage (approx. €1,800/month gross in 2024). Without a degree, a national work visa (type D) may apply if an employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. Freelancers can obtain a temporary residence permit for self-employment.

**Q: Who are the main employers for creative and performing artists in Lithuania?**
A: Key employers include state and municipal theatres, the Lithuanian National Opera and Ballet Theatre, cultural centres, film and TV production studios, advertising agencies, and event management companies. Many artists work as independent contractors or freelancers. The Lithuanian Council for Culture offers project grants, and the creative sector is supported by EU funds.
