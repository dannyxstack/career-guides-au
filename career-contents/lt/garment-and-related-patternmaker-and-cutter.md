## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, garment patternmakers and cutters typically earn between €800 and €1,300 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced professionals in larger companies may earn up to €1,500. Overtime and bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for garment patternmakers and cutters to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU garment patternmakers and cutters need a job offer to apply for a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. Alternatively, an EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree or equivalent experience, requiring a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a garment patternmaker or cutter in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, vocational training in garment technology or pattern making is common, offered by some vocational schools and colleges. Apprenticeships with experienced professionals are also a pathway. Formal qualifications are not always mandatory, but they improve job prospects. Continuous learning about CAD software and new textile techniques is beneficial for career advancement.
