## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for beauticians in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for beauticians in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level workers may earn around €700, while experienced professionals in upscale salons in Vilnius can earn higher, including tips.

**Q: What is the work-visa or EU Blue Card route for beauticians to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign beauticians can work in Lithuania by securing a job offer and obtaining a National Visa (D) and temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable, as it requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold (typically over €2,000 gross/month), which most beautician positions do not meet.

**Q: What are the career prospects for beauticians in Lithuania?**
A: Demand for beauticians is stable and growing in Lithuania, especially in major cities. Career advancement includes specializing in areas like makeup, nail art, or skincare, opening your own salon, or working in high-end hotels and spas. Additional certifications and courses can enhance opportunities.
