## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Inquiry Clerks in Lithuania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for Inquiry Clerks in Lithuania typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start near the minimum wage (€924 in 2025), while experienced clerks can earn around €1,400. These figures are based on industry surveys and job listings.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for Inquiry Clerks in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as Inquiry Clerks in Lithuania via a National Work Visa (D) sponsored by an employer, requiring a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is also possible if the gross annual salary exceeds €45,816 (2025 threshold) and you hold a higher education degree. However, typical Inquiry Clerk salaries often fall below this threshold.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Inquiry Clerks in Lithuania?**
A: Inquiry Clerks can advance to roles such as Customer Service Team Leader, Call Center Manager, or Quality Assurance Specialist. With additional training or language skills, they may move into specialized areas like technical support or sales. Lithuanian employers often promote internally, and experience can lead to supervisory positions within 2–3 years.
