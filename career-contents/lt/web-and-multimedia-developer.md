## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for web and multimedia developers in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for web and multimedia developers in Lithuania ranges from €2,500 for junior roles to over €4,500 for senior positions. Salaries vary by experience, company size, and location, with Vilnius generally offering higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for web developers moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU web developers can apply for a national work visa (D visa) if they have a job offer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the career growth opportunities for web developers in Lithuania?**
A: Lithuania's booming tech scene offers career paths from junior to lead developer, plus roles in project management, DevOps, or full-stack specialization. Many developers progress to senior positions or transition into tech startups. The country's strong IT sector, including fintech and gaming, provides diverse advancement opportunities.
