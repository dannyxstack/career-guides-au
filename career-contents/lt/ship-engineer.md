## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' engineers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for ships' engineers in Lithuania vary by rank and experience. Junior engineers typically earn around €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month, while chief engineers can earn €3,000 to €4,500 or more. Additional allowances for sea service and overtime are common. These figures are pre-tax; net income depends on social contributions and tax rates.

**Q: How can non-EU ships' engineers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible with an employer's sponsorship and a valid job offer. Both routes require meeting STCW certification standards.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a ships' engineer in Lithuania?**
A: Ships' engineers in Lithuania must hold a valid Certificate of Competency (CoC) issued or endorsed by the Lithuanian Maritime Safety Administration, in accordance with STCW standards. This typically requires completion of a maritime academy program and sufficient sea service. Non-Lithuanian CoCs may need endorsement. Continuous professional development is also essential.
