## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a systems administrator in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for systems administrators in Lithuania ranges from €2,500 to €4,500, depending on experience, industry, and location. Senior roles in Vilnius may exceed €5,000. Salaries are generally lower than in Western Europe but competitive within the local market, with strong demand for skilled professionals.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU systems administrator to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU systems administrators can work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5x the national average, currently around €2,850 gross monthly) or a national work visa (D visa) after obtaining a work permit. Both routes require employer sponsorship and meeting qualifications. Official details are on the Migration Department website.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become a systems administrator in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor’s degree in computer science, information technology, or a related field is preferred. Employers also value certifications like CompTIA Server+, Microsoft MCSA, or Linux LPIC. Practical experience with server management, networking, and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) is crucial. Lithuanian language skills are often required for local roles, but English may suffice in international companies.
