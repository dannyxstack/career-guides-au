## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for elementary workers in Lithuania generally align with the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €924 per month gross. Actual earnings may vary by employer and specific duties, but most positions in this category pay around this level. Overtime or additional hours can increase take-home pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for elementary workers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals working as elementary workers in Lithuania typically require a national visa (D visa) and a work permit. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is not available for elementary occupations, as it targets highly skilled workers. Instead, the standard work-based temporary residence permit is the appropriate route.

**Q: What are common job duties for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: Elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania perform simple, routine tasks in sectors like cleaning, construction assistance, warehousing, or manufacturing. Duties may include manual sorting, basic assembly, packaging, or general maintenance. These roles require minimal formal education and typically involve following clear instructions.
