## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for stationary plant and machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and experience. As of 2025, the average gross monthly salary for such operators in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,600. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced operators in sectors like manufacturing or energy can earn up to €2,000.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as stationary plant and machine operators in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Lithuania is reserved for highly qualified workers holding a higher education degree and meeting a salary threshold (currently around €2,000 gross monthly). For this occupation, a standard national work visa (based on employer sponsorship) is more common, as the Blue Card requirements are typically not met. Always check with the Migration Department.

**Q: What industries employ stationary plant and machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: These operators are primarily employed in manufacturing (food processing, textiles, woodworking), energy production (power plants), and waste management. Vocational training in machinery operation is often required, and on-the-job training is common. Opportunities exist in both urban industrial zones and rural factories.
