## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics from the Lithuanian State Social Insurance Fund (SODRA), the average gross monthly salary for gardeners (ISCO 6113) is approximately €800–€1,000. Actual pay varies by region, experience, and employer, with some earning minimum wage (€924 in 2025) and others receiving higher pay in specialized horticulture or landscape management roles.

**Q: What visa routes are available for non-EU gardeners to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU gardeners typically require a temporary residence permit for employment, which mandates a Lithuanian employer to obtain a work permit. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because it requires higher education qualifications. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. After 5 years of legal residence, one may apply for permanent residency.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to work as a gardener in Lithuania?**
A: Formal education is not mandatory, but vocational training in horticulture or landscaping is highly valued. Many employers prefer candidates with practical experience, knowledge of plant care, and relevant certifications (e.g., pesticide application). A valid driver's license is often required for roles involving transport or equipment. Fluency in Lithuanian can also be advantageous for communication.
