## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for survey and market research interviewers in Lithuania?**
A: According to official data from the Lithuanian Department of Statistics, the average gross monthly salary for survey and market research interviewers ranges from €800 to €1,200. Entry-level positions may start at the minimum wage (€924 as of 2025), while experienced interviewers can earn higher, depending on the employer and project complexity.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as survey interviewers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a temporary residence permit for employment if they have a confirmed job offer. The EU Blue Card is also available for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. Neither route has occupation-specific quotas, but general conditions apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a survey interviewer in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, secondary education is sufficient. Employers provide on-the-job training covering interview techniques and data entry. Key skills include fluency in Lithuanian, strong communication, computer literacy, and reliability. Some positions require knowledge of English or Russian. No formal certification is mandatory, but relevant experience is beneficial.
