## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Lithuania?**
A: Starting salaries for non-commissioned officers in the Lithuanian Armed Forces range from approximately €1,200 to €1,600 per month gross, depending on rank and experience. Additional allowances for service conditions, bonuses, and benefits such as housing or health insurance may apply, increasing total compensation.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not applicable for military positions. Foreign nationals wishing to serve as non-commissioned officers in the Lithuanian Armed Forces must meet specific eligibility criteria, including citizenship requirements. Generally, only Lithuanian citizens or citizens of other NATO/EU countries with special agreements may apply, and work visas are handled through bilateral defense cooperation or citizenship-based pathways.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-commissioned officers can advance through ranks such as Sergeant, Staff Sergeant, and Master Sergeant, with corresponding pay increases. Opportunities include specialized training in leadership, logistics, or technical fields. Further promotion to Warrant Officer is possible, along with assignments to NATO or EU missions abroad, requiring continuous professional development.
