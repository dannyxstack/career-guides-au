## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for technical and medical sales professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience and sector. Entry-level positions typically start at €1,500–€2,000 gross per month, while senior roles can exceed €4,000. Bonuses and commissions often significantly increase total compensation. Medical sales may offer higher base pay than technical sales.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for foreign technical and medical sales professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain a National Visa (D) for employment or an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Lithuanian average wage (approximately €2,200 gross/month in 2024). Employer sponsorship is required for both routes.

**Q: Which industries most commonly employ technical and medical sales professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Key industries include pharmaceuticals, medical devices, biotechnology, industrial machinery, electronics, and chemicals. These sectors have strong export markets and often require sales staff with technical or medical backgrounds to liaise with hospitals, manufacturers, and distribution networks.
