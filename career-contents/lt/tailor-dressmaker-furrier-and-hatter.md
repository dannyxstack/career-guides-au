## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for tailors and dressmakers in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, tailors and dressmakers typically earn between €700 and €1,200 gross per month, depending on experience, specialization, and location. Those working in high-end fashion or with fur may earn higher wages. Self-employed professionals often set their own rates, which can vary significantly.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for non-EU tailors to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU tailors need a work permit or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage. Alternatively, a national visa (D) with work permit is possible if no local candidate is available. Employer must apply to the Employment Service.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for tailors in Lithuania?**
A: Tailors can advance to master tailor, pattern maker, or fashion designer. Some open their own ateliers or work in luxury tailoring. Vocational training and apprenticeships are available. The profession also allows branching into costume design for theater or film, or specializing in restoration of historic garments.
