## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for handicraft workers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for handicraft workers in Lithuania ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, specialty, and location. Self-employed artisans may have variable income.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU handicraft workers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU handicraft workers can apply for a national work visa (D type) with employer sponsorship. The employer must pass a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation as it requires higher education qualifications.

**Q: What types of handicraft occupations are common in Lithuania?**
A: Common handicraft occupations in Lithuania include woodcarvers, glassblowers, jewelers, and textile artists. Many work in small workshops, cultural enterprises, or are self-employed. Demand is driven by tourism and traditional crafts preservation.
