## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for musicians in Lithuania?**
A: Income varies widely. Freelance musicians may earn €500–€2,000 per month, while those employed in orchestras or state institutions earn €1,200–€2,500. Many supplement through teaching or gigs. The average gross monthly salary in the arts sector is around €1,400 (2023), but individual earnings fluctuate significantly.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for non-EU musicians in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU musicians can apply for a national work visa (D visa) after securing a job offer from a Lithuanian employer. The EU Blue Card is also possible if the position requires higher education and meets the salary threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage, approx. €2,100 in 2024). Short-term performances may use a Schengen C visa.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for musicians in Lithuania?**
A: Common paths include performing in orchestras (e.g., Lithuanian National Symphony Orchestra), teaching at music schools or universities, composing for film/media, working as session musicians, or joining folk and contemporary ensembles. State funding supports cultural projects, and many musicians also freelance internationally.
