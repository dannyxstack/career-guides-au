**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated management of raw material weighing, mixing, and conveying
- Machine parameter setting and operation status monitoring
- Automatic adjustment and recording of kiln firing temperature curve
- AI visual inspection and sorting of finished product dimensions, color differences, and defects
- Automatic generation of production data reports and anomaly alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI predictive maintenance systems to detect equipment failures early
- Optimising kiln firing curves through digital twins to improve yield
- Using AR glasses to receive remote expert guidance for complex repairs
- Using AI to analyze historical data to optimize raw material formulas and process parameters
- Efficiently schedule multi-variety, small-batch production using smart scheduling systems

**Human moat:**

- Rapid diagnosis and manual repair of sudden equipment failures
- Process debugging for new formulas or non-standard products
- Experienced coordination and emergency decision-making across multiple processes
- Communicate and collaborate with colleagues, quality inspection, and engineering departments
- On-site control and adjustment for special client customization needs.

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial robot programming and commissioning (e.g., ABB, KUKA)
- Basic PLC operation and fault code interpretation
- Basic data analysis skills in Python or R
- Use of digital twin software (e.g., AnyLogic, Simio)
- Quality management tools (e.g. SPC, FMEA)
- IoT device and production management system operation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for glass and ceramics plant operators in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly salary for glass and ceramics plant operators in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,400 gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €800 gross, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,600 gross. Overtime and shift work often provide additional compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as glass and ceramics plant operators in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card in Lithuania if they hold a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience and have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania (€23,184 in 2023). Alternatively, a national work permit (temporary residence permit for employment) may be available for lower-skilled roles.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities and career prospects for glass and ceramics plant operators in Lithuania?**
A: Operators control machinery for manufacturing glass or ceramic products, monitor production processes, ensure quality, and perform basic maintenance. Career advancement includes becoming a team leader or production supervisor. With additional training, operators can specialize in technical support or quality control. The role is in demand in Lithuania due to existing glass and ceramics industries.
