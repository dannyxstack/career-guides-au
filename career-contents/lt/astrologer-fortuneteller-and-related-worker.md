## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Lithuania?**
A: Earnings vary significantly as most work self-employed. Official statistics do not track this occupation; typical income may be low, often near the minimum wage (€924/month in 2025) or slightly above for established practitioners. Many combine it with other jobs to sustain a living. No standard pay scale exists.

**Q: Can foreign astrologers or fortune-tellers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreigners can apply for a Lithuanian national visa (D) if they have a job offer from a local employer. However, the EU Blue Card requires a high-skilled position with a salary at least 1.5 times the average wage (€2,098/month in 2025), which this occupation rarely meets. Self-employed individuals may apply for a temporary residence permit for business activities.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an astrologer or fortune-teller in Lithuania?**
A: No specific official qualifications or licenses are required. Success relies on personal skills, knowledge of esoteric practices, and client trust. Some practitioners complete private courses or obtain certifications from international astrological organizations, but these are not mandated. Building a reputation through word-of-mouth and online presence is key.
