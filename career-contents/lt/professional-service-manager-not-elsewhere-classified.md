## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a professional services manager in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the average gross monthly salary for professional services managers (ISCO 1349) ranges from €3,000 to €5,000 for mid-level roles, with senior positions in fields like law, IT, or consulting earning €6,000 or more. Salaries vary by experience, sector, and company size, with Vilnius typically offering the highest pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a professional services manager to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professional services managers can work in Lithuania via a work permit (temporary residence permit for employment) or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (currently about €2,100 gross). Both routes need employer sponsorship and Labour Market test exemptions may apply.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for professional services managers in Lithuania?**
A: Professional services managers in Lithuania can advance to senior management roles such as director of operations or CEO. Many also move into specialised consultancy, start their own businesses, or pursue roles in multinational corporations. Upskilling in digital transformation, leadership, and industry-specific certifications enhances career prospects.
