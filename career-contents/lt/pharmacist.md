## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmacists in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for pharmacists in Lithuania ranges from €1,500 to €2,200, depending on experience and the sector (public or private). Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced pharmacists in managerial roles can earn higher salaries.

**Q: Can pharmacists obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, pharmacists can apply for an EU Blue Card in Lithuania if they have a recognized higher education degree in pharmacy and a job offer that meets the national salary threshold set by Lithuanian law. The employer must also prove the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements for pharmacists in Lithuania?**
A: To practice as a pharmacist in Lithuania, you must have a recognized pharmacy degree and register with the State Medicines Control Agency (VVKT). EU/EEA qualifications are recognized automatically, while non-EU qualifications require credential evaluation. Proficiency in Lithuanian is mandatory for patient communication.
