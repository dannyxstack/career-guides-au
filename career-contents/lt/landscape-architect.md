## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for landscape architects in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for landscape architects in Lithuania ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and project scope. Entry-level positions start around €1,300, while senior professionals with over 5 years of experience can earn up to €3,500 per month. Salaries may vary between public and private sectors.

**Q: Can landscape architects obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, landscape architects can apply for a National Visa (D) for employment or the EU Blue Card if they have a qualifying job offer and meet the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian salary). The employer must obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. The application process is handled by the Migration Department.

**Q: What are the key opportunities for landscape architects in Lithuania?**
A: Landscape architects in Lithuania work in urban planning firms, municipalities, and private design studios. Major projects include urban green space development, coastal and park restoration, and private garden design. The growing emphasis on sustainable urbanism offers opportunities in eco-design and climate adaptation. Membership in the Lithuanian Landscape Architects' Union is beneficial for networking and certification.
