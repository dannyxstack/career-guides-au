## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for social work and counselling professionals in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for social workers in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and sector. Counselling professionals in private practice may earn higher fees. Public sector positions often follow state pay scales, while NGOs offer varying compensation. Additional benefits may include health insurance and professional development support.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU social work professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Lithuania via a national work permit (D visa) if they have a job offer from a Lithuanian employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers but requires a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, which is typically higher than standard social work wages. Thus, the national work permit route is more common for this occupation.

**Q: What are the career prospects for social workers and counsellors in Lithuania?**
A: Demand for qualified social workers is steady due to an aging population and EU-funded social inclusion projects. Career advancement includes supervisory roles in municipal social services, clinical counselling, or policy development. Licensure through the Social Workers’ Chamber is required for certain roles. Opportunities exist in child welfare, addiction support, and mental health services.
