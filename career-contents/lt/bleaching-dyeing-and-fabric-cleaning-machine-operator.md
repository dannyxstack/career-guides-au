## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: According to official data, the average gross monthly salary for textile machine operators in Lithuania ranges from €900 to €1,300, depending on experience and region. Many earn close to the national minimum wage (€924 in 2024). Overtime and shift bonuses may apply in industrial settings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card in Lithuania requires a higher education degree or equivalent, which this occupation does not typically demand. However, non-EU workers can obtain a national work permit (temporary residence permit for employment) by securing a job offer and approval from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange under local labour market tests.

**Q: What are the main career prospects for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: Career progression typically includes moving to senior operator, shift supervisor, or quality control roles within textile plants. Some operators transition into machine maintenance or technical sales. The Lithuanian textile industry is small but stable, with opportunities in factories near major cities like Vilnius, Kaunas, and Šiauliai.
