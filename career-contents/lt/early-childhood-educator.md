## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for early childhood educators in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for early childhood educators in Lithuania vary by experience and location, typically ranging from €800 to €1,200 gross per month. Public-sector positions may include additional benefits. According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary is around €1,000.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for early childhood educators to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as early childhood educators via a work visa or EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) and a recognized qualification. Both routes necessitate employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an early childhood educator in Lithuania?**
A: To work as an early childhood educator, you generally need a degree in education or a related field, recognized by the Lithuanian Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education (SKVC). Additionally, proficiency in Lithuanian at B2 level is typically required, along with a clean criminal record.
