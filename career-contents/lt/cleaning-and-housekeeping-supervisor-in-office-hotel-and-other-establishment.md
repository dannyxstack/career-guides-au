## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cleaning and housekeeping supervisor in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for cleaning and housekeeping supervisors in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,400, depending on experience, establishment size, and location. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced supervisors in upscale hotels may earn up to €1,700 gross per month.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as cleaning supervisors in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in this occupation through the EU Blue Card (for highly skilled roles requiring higher education or 5+ years of professional experience) or a national work permit, which requires a labor market test. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. The Blue Card salary threshold is at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for cleaning supervisors in Lithuania?**
A: Cleaning supervisors can advance to housekeeping manager, operations manager, or facilities manager positions in hotels, hospitals, or large cleaning companies. With additional training, they may move into quality assurance, staff training, or regional supervision roles. Certifications in hospitality management or facility management can accelerate career growth.
