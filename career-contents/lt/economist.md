## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for economists in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for economists in Lithuania in 2023 is approximately €2,000 to €2,500. Entry-level positions start around €1,500, while senior roles in banking or finance can reach €4,000 or more. Salaries are higher in Vilnius and vary by sector and experience.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign economists to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU economists can work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card, requiring a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a national work permit is available for specific job offers. EU/EEA citizens have free movement and can work without a permit.

**Q: Which sectors employ most economists in Lithuania?**
A: Economists in Lithuania are mainly employed in banking and finance, public administration (e.g., Ministry of Finance, Bank of Lithuania), research and academia, consulting firms, and international organizations. The financial sector, particularly commercial banks and fintech companies, is a major employer, along with government agencies focusing on economic policy.
