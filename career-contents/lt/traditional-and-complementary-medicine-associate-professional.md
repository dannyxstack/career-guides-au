## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and setting. According to Lithuanian labor data, the average gross monthly salary ranges from €1,000 to €1,500. Professionals in private practice or with advanced certifications may earn more, while those in public institutions or with less experience start at the lower end. Benefits and bonuses are uncommon.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit or EU Blue Card. For the EU Blue Card, you require a higher education degree, a job offer in an occupation requiring such qualifications (traditional medicine may qualify), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Alternatively, a national work permit can be obtained with a job offer and employer sponsorship, subject to labor market tests.

**Q: What career opportunities are available for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Professionals can work in private clinics, wellness centers, or start their own practice. Common modalities include acupuncture, herbal medicine, and massage. Registration with the Lithuanian Ministry of Health may be required for certain practices. Continuing education is valued, and collaboration with conventional healthcare providers is growing. The field is regulated, so check legal requirements for each modality.
