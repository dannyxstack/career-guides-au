## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fashion and other models in Lithuania?**
A: Earnings vary widely based on experience, type of modeling, and client. Many models work freelance with project-based fees, while agency-employed models may receive a monthly salary. The national minimum monthly wage (€924 as of 2024) applies to employees, but top models can earn significantly more.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for models to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU models need a work permit and a national visa (D) for employment. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers meeting salary thresholds, but typical modeling contracts rarely qualify. Sponsored employment by a Lithuanian agency is the standard route; self-employment is also possible under certain conditions.

**Q: How do I start a career as a model in Lithuania?**
A: Register with a reputable modeling agency in Vilnius, Kaunas, or Klaipėda. Build a portfolio with professional photos. Attend castings and maintain a strong online presence. Networking and persistence are key. Agencies often provide guidance on contracts and legal requirements.
