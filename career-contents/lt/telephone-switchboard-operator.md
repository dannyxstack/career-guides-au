## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic Call Distribution (ACD) system takes over call routing
- Interactive Voice Response (IVR) handles simple queries and routing
- Speech recognition and natural language processing replace basic manual information consultation services
- Automated attendant software enables 24/7 unattended call handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time customer information and advice to improve service quality
- Natural language processing helps handle multilingual calls, expanding service scope
- Intelligently analyze call data to optimize call routing and staff scheduling
- Automatically record and summarize call content, reducing administrative work

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex, emotional, or contentious customer calls
- Personalized services requiring empathy and interpersonal communication
- Ability to adapt flexibly in emergency or unusual situations
- Multitasking ability (managing multiple lines and systems simultaneously)

**Skills to build:**

- Customer relationship management (CRM) system operation
- Basic data analysis and reporting
- Multilingual communication skills (especially English)
- Emotion management and conflict resolution skills
- Digital communication tools (e.g. chatbot platforms, video customer service)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a telephone switchboard operator in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for telephone switchboard operators in Lithuania typically range from €700 to €1,200 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around the minimum wage, while senior operators may earn higher. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for telephone switchboard operators in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and a temporary residence permit sponsored by a Lithuanian employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers but requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average national salary. Switchboard operators typically do not qualify as highly skilled, so standard work permits apply.

**Q: How is automation affecting telephone switchboard operator jobs in Lithuania?**
A: Increasing automation and AI-driven systems are reducing demand for manual switchboard operators in Lithuania. Many companies now use automated call distribution and voice recognition, shifting the role to hybrid positions with more complex customer service tasks. Upskilling in digital tools and multilingual communication is recommended.
