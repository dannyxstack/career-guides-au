## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics earn a gross monthly salary between €1,300 and €2,200, depending on experience, certifications, and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior specialists in major cities may earn up to €2,500. Overtime and bonuses can increase total pay.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Lithuania via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer from a Lithuanian employer. The employer applies for a work permit at the Migration Department. For the EU Blue Card, the job must require high professional qualifications and meet the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Both routes require valid qualifications, health insurance, and a clean criminal record.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a vocational education in HVAC or refrigeration technology is required. Many employers prefer candidates with a certificate of competence from the Lithuanian Association of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Engineers. Practical experience with installation, maintenance, and repair of systems is essential. Ongoing training on new refrigerants and energy-efficient technologies is recommended for career progression.
