## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Production progress tracking and report generation (automated by MES system)
- Basic quality inspection and defect identification (replaced by machine vision)
- Material requirements calculation and purchasing suggestions (automated by ERP system)
- Employee attendance and working hours statistics (automatically processed by digital systems).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI scheduling algorithms to optimize production plans and improve equipment utilization
- Reduce equipment downtime with predictive maintenance systems
- Monitor production efficiency and bottlenecks in real time via data analytics dashboards
- Using AI-assisted training systems to accelerate new employee onboarding

**Human moat:**

- On-site anomaly handling (equipment failure, personnel conflict, etc.) judgment and decision-making
- Team morale management, employee motivation and communication
- Flexible cross-department coordination (R&D, logistics, sales).
- Safety and compliance on-site management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of Manufacturing Execution System (MES) and ERP systems
- Data analysis tools (e.g., Power BI, basic Python)
- Lean Production and Agile Management Methods
- Basics of human-machine collaboration and industrial IoT
- Cross-cultural team management (trends in manufacturing internationalization)
- Basic programming (e.g., for process automation scripts)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Manufacturing Supervisors in Lithuania?**
A: Manufacturing Supervisors in Lithuania typically earn between €1,800 and €3,200 gross per month, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Entry-level positions start around €1,500, while senior supervisors in large factories can earn up to €4,000. Salaries are often supplemented with bonuses linked to production targets.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for Manufacturing Supervisors moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU Manufacturing Supervisors can apply for a Lithuanian National Visa (D) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian gross wage (currently ~€2,280/month) and a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience. The employer must prove the role could not be filled locally.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Manufacturing Supervisors in Lithuania?**
A: Manufacturing Supervisors can progress to roles like Production Manager, Plant Manager, or Operations Director. Many pursue certifications in Lean Manufacturing or Six Sigma. With experience, supervisors may shift into quality management, supply chain, or process engineering. Large Lithuanian factories (e.g., in electronics or automotive) offer structured career paths and internal promotion programs.
