## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental and occupational health inspectors in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, environmental and occupational health inspectors typically earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. Public sector roles may start lower, while senior positions in private companies can reach higher brackets. Overtime and bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU professionals work as environmental and occupational health inspectors in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania) and have a valid job offer in the field. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is available. Both routes require recognition of qualifications by relevant Lithuanian authorities.

**Q: What are the main duties of an environmental and occupational health inspector in Lithuania?**
A: Inspectors conduct workplace inspections to ensure compliance with occupational safety and health laws, investigate accidents, and assess environmental hazards. They issue permits, provide guidance to employers, and collaborate with the State Labour Inspectorate and the Environmental Protection Agency. Fieldwork and report writing are core activities.
