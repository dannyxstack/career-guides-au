## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a general secretary in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the average gross monthly salary for a general secretary ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and company size. Entry-level positions start near the minimum wage (€730 in 2025), while senior secretaries earn up to €2,000. Salaries in Vilnius tend to be higher than in smaller cities.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for a secretary moving to Lithuania?**
A: Foreign secretaries can work in Lithuania via a National Visa (D) for employment, requiring a work permit obtained by the employer. The EU Blue Card is possible if the applicant holds a higher education degree and the job offer meets the salary threshold (set annually by the Ministry of Social Security and Labour, e.g., 1.5 times average wage). Both routes need employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for secretaries in Lithuania?**
A: Secretaries in Lithuania can advance to executive assistant, office manager, or administrative coordinator roles. With additional skills in project management or languages, they may move into HR, procurement, or specialized support positions. Many also pursue further education to qualify for management or specialized administrative roles.
