## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fitness instructors in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for fitness instructors in Lithuania is around €1,200–€1,800. Entry-level positions may start at €900, while experienced instructors can earn up to €2,500. Additional income from private sessions or classes can supplement base pay.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for fitness instructors to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and temporary residence permit. The employer must apply for a work permit via the Lithuanian Employment Service. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; fitness instructors typically need a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 1.5x average wage). Specific requirements are on the Migration Department website.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a fitness instructor in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a certified fitness instructor qualification is required, such as an accredited personal trainer certificate. Many employers prefer a bachelor's degree in sports science or related field. Registration with the Lithuanian Sports Medicine Centre may be necessary for certain roles. Experience and specializations in group fitness or rehabilitation are advantageous.
