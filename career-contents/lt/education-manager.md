## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Education Managers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for Education Managers in Lithuania vary by institution type and experience. School principals may earn between €2,000 and €3,500 gross monthly, while university deans or directors can earn €3,000 to €5,000. Public sector roles often have lower starting pay but include benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Education Managers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a national work visa (D visa) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Employers must obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Education Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in education management, pedagogy, or a related field is required. School principals must have teaching experience and pass a certification exam. For higher education, a PhD or significant academic experience is often expected. Fluency in Lithuanian is essential.
