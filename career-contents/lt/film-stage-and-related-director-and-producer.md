## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for film and stage directors and producers in Lithuania?**
A: According to the Lithuanian Department of Statistics, the average gross monthly salary for professionals in the arts and entertainment sector, including film and stage directors and producers, ranges from approximately €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, project scope, and employer. Salaries can vary significantly based on individual contracts and project-based work.

**Q: What visa or permit options are available for non-EU directors and producers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card for high-skilled workers or a national work permit. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, a higher education degree, and relevant qualifications. The employer must also prove the vacancy could not be filled locally.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for film directors and producers in Lithuania?**
A: Lithuania's film industry is growing, with increasing international co-productions and a supportive tax incentive system. Directors and producers can work in feature films, documentaries, television, and stage productions. Opportunities are also available at the Lithuanian Film Centre, public broadcaster LRT, and private production companies. Networking through events like the Vilnius International Film Festival is beneficial.
