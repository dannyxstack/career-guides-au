## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physicists and astronomers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. Entry-level physicists in academia or research earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while experienced professionals in industry or senior roles can earn €3,000–€4,500. Astronomers typically work in research and earn €2,000–€3,500. These figures are based on 2023 data from Lithuanian labor statistics.

**Q: How can a foreign physicist or astronomer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Qualified foreign physicists and astronomers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) can be obtained via a Lithuanian employer. Applications are processed by the Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for physicists in Lithuania?**
A: Physicists in Lithuania find roles in academia (Vilnius University, Kaunas University of Technology), research institutes (Center for Physical Sciences and Technology), and the growing laser technology industry. Astronomy opportunities are mainly at the Institute of Theoretical Physics and Astronomy. The private sector also hires for R&D in optics, semiconductors, and renewable energy.
