## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for structural metal preparers and erectors in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for structural metal preparers and erectors in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced workers can earn up to €2,000. Major construction hubs like Vilnius and Kaunas tend to offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for structural metal preparers and erectors to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) or the EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and employer sponsorship, with the employer proving a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers, requiring a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average.

**Q: What are the typical career progression opportunities for structural metal preparers and erectors in Lithuania?**
A: Structural metal preparers and erectors can advance to roles such as foreman, site supervisor, or project manager with experience and additional certifications. Specializing in welding (e.g., certified welder) or structural design also enhances prospects. The construction industry in Lithuania is growing, offering opportunities in large-scale infrastructure projects.
