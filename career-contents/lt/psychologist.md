## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for psychologists in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for psychologists in Lithuania vary by sector and experience. In the public sector, monthly gross wages typically range from €1,200 to €2,000. Private practice or corporate roles can offer €2,000 to €3,500 or more. Full-time employment with mandatory social insurance contributions is standard.

**Q: Can non-EU psychologists work in Lithuania through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU psychologists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania). The application is submitted via the Migration Department, and a work permit is required.

**Q: What are the registration requirements for psychologists in Lithuania?**
A: Psychologists must register with the Lithuanian Psychological Association (LPA) to practice legally. This requires a master's degree in psychology, completion of supervised practice, and passing a professional exam. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain registration. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education.
