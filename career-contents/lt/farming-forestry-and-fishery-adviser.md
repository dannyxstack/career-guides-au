## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Lithuania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for advisers in Lithuania ranges from €1,800 to €3,500, depending on experience, sector, and employer. Public sector and large private firms tend to offer higher pay, while self-employed or smaller advisory services may earn less. Additional benefits may include performance bonuses and professional development allowances.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and temporary residence permit. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Application is through the Lithuanian Migration Department.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Lithuania?**
A: Advisers work in public institutions like the Agricultural Advisory Service, private consultancy firms, or as independent experts. Specializations include organic farming, EU funding project management, environmental conservation, and forestry management. The role often involves fieldwork, training farmers, and policy implementation. Experience can lead to senior advisory or managerial positions.
