- Use automated devices to measure and record vital signs (temperature, blood pressure, etc.)
- Entering and updating patient information in electronic health record systems
- Prepare examination instruments and care supplies according to standard procedures
- Handle routine patient inquiries via chatbots or automated systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Leverage AI-assisted tools for more accurate patient condition assessments (e.g., risk scoring)

**Human moat:**

- Making ethical rapid decisions in emergencies

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Nursing Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Nursing Associate Professionals in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience, location, and employer. Additional allowances may apply for night shifts or specialized care. Salaries in private clinics are often higher than in public hospitals.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU Nursing Associate Professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Lithuania via a national work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Recognition of professional qualifications with the relevant Lithuanian authority is mandatory. Contact the Migration Department for full details.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Nursing Associate Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Nursing Associate Professionals can advance to registered nurse roles by completing additional university education (e.g., a bachelor's degree in nursing). Continuing professional development courses in specialized areas like geriatric care or intensive care are also available. Leadership roles, such as ward manager or clinical instructor, require further training and experience.
