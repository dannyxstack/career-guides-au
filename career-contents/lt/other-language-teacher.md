## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for language teachers in Lithuania?**
A: Language teachers in Lithuania earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, institution type, and language taught. Public sector salaries are lower, while private language schools and corporate training offer higher pay. Part-time tutors may charge €15–€30 per hour.

**Q: Can non-EU language teachers work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: Yes, qualified language teachers can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, a national work permit (D type visa) is available for those with a specific job offer. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are common employment settings for language teachers in Lithuania?**
A: Language teachers in Lithuania work in public and private schools, universities, language centers, and as private tutors. In-demand languages include English, German, French, and Russian. Many teachers also work freelance or combine multiple part-time roles. Professional development opportunities include conferences and workshops offered by the British Council and Goethe-Institut.
