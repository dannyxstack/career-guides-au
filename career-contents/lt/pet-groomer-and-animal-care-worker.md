## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,400, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced groomers in major cities like Vilnius can earn up to €1,600. Self-employed groomers often have variable income based on clientele.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as pet groomers in Lithuania through the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for pet groomers, as it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Non-EU citizens can obtain a Lithuanian national work visa (D type) and temporary residence permit if they have a valid job offer. The employer must demonstrate the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Work permits are issued by the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a pet groomer in Lithuania?**
A: No formal degree is mandatory, but completing vocational courses in pet grooming from accredited institutions like the Lithuanian Veterinary Academy or private schools is highly recommended. Apprenticeships under experienced groomers are common. Certification from international bodies (e.g., IPG) can enhance job prospects. The Lithuanian Labour Exchange also offers subsidized training programs for jobseekers.
