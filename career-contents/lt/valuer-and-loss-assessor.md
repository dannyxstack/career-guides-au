## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for valuers and loss assessors in Lithuania?**
A: Valuers and loss assessors in Lithuania typically earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, specialization, and employer. Senior professionals or those in high-demand sectors may earn more. Private sector valuations often pay higher than insurance loss assessing.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as a valuer or loss assessor in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in this occupation via a national work permit (D visa) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Requirements include a job offer from a Lithuanian employer and relevant qualifications. The Blue Card additionally requires a higher education degree and a salary meeting the annual threshold set by Lithuanian law.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a valuer or loss assessor in Lithuania?**
A: To become a valuer in Lithuania, you typically need a bachelor's degree in economics, finance, real estate, or a related field. For property valuation, certification from the Lithuanian Association of Property Valuers is mandatory. Loss assessors often require insurance-specific training and may need to pass professional exams. Practical experience is highly valued.
