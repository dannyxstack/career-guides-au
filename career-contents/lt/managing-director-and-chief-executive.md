## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Managing Directors and Chief Executives in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for managing directors and chief executives in Lithuania vary widely based on company size, industry, and experience. As of 2025, gross monthly salaries typically range from €5,000 to €15,000. Larger multinational firms or key industries like finance and IT may offer higher compensation, including bonuses and stock options.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card routes for managing directors and chief executives in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as managing directors in Lithuania via a national work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €36,000 in 2024) and a higher education degree or relevant professional experience. A work permit is employer-sponsored and requires a labor market test. Both routes lead to temporary residence.
