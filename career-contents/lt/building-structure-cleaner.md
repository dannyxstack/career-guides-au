## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for building structure cleaners in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for building structure cleaners (ISCO 7133) in Lithuania ranges from 800 to 1000 EUR. Pay varies by region, employer, and experience. Overtime or hazardous work may increase earnings. Some employers offer additional benefits such as accommodation or transportation.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as building structure cleaners in Lithuania? What visa routes are available?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in this occupation via a standard work permit (leidimas dirbti) sponsored by a Lithuanian employer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable, as the role typically does not require higher education or meet the minimum salary threshold. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building structure cleaner in Lithuania?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Lithuania perform exterior cleaning tasks such as washing windows, facades, roofs, and other structural surfaces. They use specialized equipment like scaffolding, lifts, and pressure washers. The job requires physical fitness, safety training, and comfort working at heights. Demand is steady in urban areas.
