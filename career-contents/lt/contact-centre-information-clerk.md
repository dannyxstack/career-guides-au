## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Answer standard FAQ questions (e.g. account balance, business hours)
- Handling simple order changes, returns, and exchange requests
- Transfer complex complaints to human agents
- Record and update customer information in the CRM system
- Send standard confirmation emails or SMS notifications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time translation, supporting multilingual customer service
- Emotion recognition alerts to help agents adjust communication strategies
- Smart knowledge base recommendations for quickly locating solutions to complex issues
- Automatic call summary generation reduces post-call documentation time
- Predict customer intent and prepare response scripts in advance

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly emotional, sensitive, or sudden complaints
- Coordinate across departments to solve complex non-standard issues.
- Build customer trust and maintain long-term relationships
- Respond to unforeseen emergencies (e.g., system failures)
- Deliver personalized, empathetic service experiences

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in major AI customer service platforms (e.g., Zendesk AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Learn basic data analysis (SQL/Excel) to analyze customer trends.
- Enhance conflict management and emotional communication skills
- Master the principles of Conversational Design
- Understand automation process building tools (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere)
- Develop multilingual skills (especially English) to serve international clients

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Contact Centre Information Clerks in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for contact centre information clerks in Lithuania ranges from approximately €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, language skills, and employer. Entry-level positions often start at the minimum wage (€924 in 2025), while those with fluency in multiple languages or specialized knowledge may earn higher. Overtime and performance bonuses are common in larger call centres.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Contact Centre Information Clerks in Lithuania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Lithuania as contact centre information clerks through a national work visa (D visa) or the EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and a work permit, which involves a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. Specific thresholds are set annually by the government.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Contact Centre Information Clerks in Lithuania?**
A: Contact centre information clerks in Lithuania can advance to team leader, quality assurance specialist, or trainer roles within 1-3 years. Some move into sales, account management, or IT support. Large international call centres in Vilnius, Kaunas, and Klaipėda offer clear career paths. Fluency in English and other European languages (German, Scandinavian) significantly boosts promotion prospects.
