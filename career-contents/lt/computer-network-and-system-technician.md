**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically diagnose common network issues and provide solutions
- Automatically generate network configuration and patch scripts
- Record work orders and categorize/escalate to senior teams
- User password reset and account management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI log analysis to quickly locate fault root causes
- Using network simulation tools to predict change impact.
- Early detection of anomalies through AI-driven monitoring.
- Using natural language queries to accelerate knowledge base resolution

**Human moat:**

- Root cause analysis and manual repair of complex faults
- Network architecture design and security policy formulation
- Effective communication with non-technical users
- Prioritizing urgent matters under pressure

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud network management (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Cybersecurity certifications (CISSP/CEH)
- Automation scripts (Python/Ansible)
- AI/ML basics and network analysis tools

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Lithuania ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior technicians can earn up to €3,000 or more. These figures are based on 2023 data from Lithuanian labor market surveys and job portals.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and a national visa (D visa) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or 5 years of relevant experience) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage (currently about €2,200 gross monthly). Employers must prove the position cannot be filled locally. A temporary residence permit is also required.

**Q: What are typical career advancement opportunities for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Technicians can advance to roles like Network Administrator, Systems Administrator, or IT Manager. Further specialization in cybersecurity, cloud computing, or network architecture opens senior positions. Certifications (CCNA, CompTIA Network+) boost prospects. With experience, professionals may move into consulting or freelance work, or pursue higher education to become engineers or IT project managers.
