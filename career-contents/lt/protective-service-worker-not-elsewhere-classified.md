## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for protective services workers in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, specialization, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €750, while experienced professionals in roles such as corporate security or surveillance can earn up to €1,500. Overtime and shift allowances are common.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for protective services workers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit and temporary residence permit, sponsored by a Lithuanian employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers meeting a salary threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross annual wage) and holding a higher education degree. For protective services, the national work permit is more common, as Blue Card criteria may be challenging to meet.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for protective services workers in Lithuania?**
A: With experience, workers can advance to supervisory roles or specialize in fields like cybersecurity, executive protection, or risk management. Further education, such as a bachelor's in security management, opens doors to positions in private security firms or government bodies like the State Security Department. Certifications in areas like CCTV or access control also enhance prospects.
