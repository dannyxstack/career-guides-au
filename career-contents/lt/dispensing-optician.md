- Automatic refractometer measures prescription and lens selection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Smart system provides personalized lens feature recommendations (blue light filtering, progressive multifocal).
- Automated customer appointment and follow-up system to improve efficiency.
- AI tool training helps opticians quickly learn new product knowledge

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and adjustment of complex prescriptions (e.g., prisms, high astigmatism)
- Face-to-face customer communication and trust building
- Custom frame adjustment (physical fit and comfort)
- Professional consultation for treating eye abnormalities or heterophoria
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration (optometrists, ophthalmologists) and legal liability

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a dispensing optician in Lithuania?**
A: The monthly gross salary for dispensing opticians in Lithuania typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while senior opticians in private practices can earn up to €1,800. Salaries in major cities like Vilnius are often higher.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for dispensing opticians in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU dispensing opticians can work in Lithuania via a national work visa or the EU Blue Card. Requirements include a valid job offer, recognition of optician qualifications by the Lithuanian competent authority, and meeting the Blue Card salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Apply through the Migration Department.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a dispensing optician in Lithuania?**
A: To practice as a dispensing optician in Lithuania, you need a recognized diploma in opticianry or equivalent. The qualification must be verified by the Lithuanian Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education. Additionally, registration with the Lithuanian Health Ministry is required. Some employers may ask for proficiency in Lithuanian.
