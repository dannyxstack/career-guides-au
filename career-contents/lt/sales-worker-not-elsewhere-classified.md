## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania ranges from €900 to €1,400, depending on experience, industry, and location. In Vilnius, salaries tend to be higher. Entry-level positions start around €800, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,800. Commission or bonuses may supplement base pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for sales workers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU sales workers can obtain a Lithuanian national visa (D) for employment or an EU Blue Card if meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5x average gross salary, around €1,800/month in 2024). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is available. Employers must prove the position could not be filled locally. Work permits require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for sales workers in Lithuania?**
A: Sales workers in Lithuania can advance to roles like sales supervisor, account manager, or regional sales manager. Gaining experience in B2B or tech sales opens higher-paying opportunities. Certifications from institutions like the Lithuanian Sales Association and fluency in English or Russian are advantageous. Some transition to marketing or business development.
