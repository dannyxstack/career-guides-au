## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for nursing professionals in Lithuania?**
A: As of 2024, the gross monthly salary for nurses in Lithuania ranges from €1,300 to €2,000 for beginners to experienced professionals in public hospitals. Private sector and specialized roles may offer higher pay. Additionally, collective agreements and regional variations influence earnings.

**Q: Can nursing professionals obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: Yes, nursing professionals are eligible for the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education qualification (at least Bachelor's) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. The application is submitted to the Migration Department. Other work permits are also available for non-EU nurses.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a nurse in Lithuania?**
A: To work as a nurse in Lithuania, you need a nursing diploma or Bachelor's degree recognized by the Lithuanian authorities. Non-EU qualifications must be evaluated by the Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education. You must also register with the State Health Care Accreditation Agency and pass a language proficiency exam in Lithuanian.
