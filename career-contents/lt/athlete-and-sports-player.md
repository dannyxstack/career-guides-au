## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for professional athletes in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sport, level, and club. In top-tier Lithuanian basketball, players earn from €30,000 to over €200,000 annually, with stars earning more. Football salaries range from €15,000 to €60,000 per year for domestic league players. Many athletes also have sponsorship deals. Lower-tier sports pay less, often below national average wage.

**Q: What visa routes allow foreign athletes to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign athletes can apply for a national visa (D visa) or temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is not typically used for athletes as it targets highly qualified workers. Instead, athletes may qualify under the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens for employment as a professional athlete, requiring a work permit and sponsorship by a Lithuanian sports club or federation.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for sports players after retirement in Lithuania?**
A: Many former athletes transition into coaching, sports management, or physical education teaching. Lithuania has a strong sports culture, offering roles in federations, clubs, and government sports agencies. Some pursue sports science or become sports commentators. Professional network and sport-specific qualifications are important for a successful second career.
