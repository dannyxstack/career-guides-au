## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Finance Managers in Lithuania?**
A: According to official data from the Lithuanian Department of Statistics, the average gross monthly salary for finance managers ranges between €4,000 and €6,500, depending on experience, company size, and sector. Senior roles in larger firms may exceed this range, and additional bonuses or benefits are common.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for a foreign Finance Manager to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign Finance Managers can work in Lithuania through the EU Blue Card or a national work visa (D visa). The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. The national work visa requires the employer to obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Employment Service. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Finance Manager in Lithuania?**
A: A bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, or economics is typically required, with many employers preferring a master's degree (e.g., MBA). Professional certifications like CFA or ACCA are highly valued. Fluency in Lithuanian and English is often essential, along with several years of experience in financial management, budgeting, and strategic planning.
