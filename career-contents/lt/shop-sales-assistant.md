## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shop sales assistant in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a shop sales assistant in Lithuania is between 800 and 1,200 EUR, depending on experience, location, and employer. As of 2025, the national minimum monthly wage is 1,038 EUR (gross), so entry-level positions typically start at this amount.

**Q: What work visa options are available for non-EU nationals to work as a shop sales assistant in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a national visa (D) for employment, which is obtained via an employer-sponsored work permit after a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree or a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. 2,000 EUR gross per month in 2025).

**Q: What career progression opportunities are there for shop sales assistants in Lithuania?**
A: Experienced shop sales assistants may advance to supervisory roles, such as shift manager or department head. Further opportunities include moving into retail management, visual merchandising, or purchasing. Vocational training and language skills (e.g., Lithuanian, English, Russian) can enhance prospects.
