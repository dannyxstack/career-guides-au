## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hunters and trappers in Lithuania?**
A: The average salary for hunters and trappers in Lithuania is not fixed due to seasonal and commission-based work. Full-time employed hunters may earn between €1,200 and €1,600 gross per month. However, many supplement their income through guided hunting tours and selling game meat or pelts, which can substantially increase annual earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU hunter or trapper obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals seeking work as hunters or trappers must obtain a work permit, which requires an employer to demonstrate a labor market shortage. The EU Blue Card, reserved for highly qualified workers requiring tertiary education or equivalent, is generally not applicable for this occupation. Therefore, the standard work permit route is the only option, though such positions are rare for foreign workers.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a hunter in Lithuania?**
A: To work as a hunter or trapper, individuals must first pass a state hunting exam and obtain a hunting license. Professional trappers may need additional certifications. While no formal education is mandatory, courses offered by the Lithuanian Hunters' Association are highly recommended. Practical experience and knowledge of local wildlife are essential for employment.
