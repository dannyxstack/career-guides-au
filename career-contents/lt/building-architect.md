## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for building architects in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for building architects in Lithuania ranges from €1,500 to €2,500 for mid-level positions, with senior architects earning up to €3,500 or more. Entry-level salaries start around €1,200. Salaries vary by experience, location (higher in Vilnius), and employer type (private vs. public sector).

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU building architects to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU building architects can work in Lithuania via a national work permit (D visa) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree in architecture (or equivalent) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. The employer must apply to the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What licensing is required to practice as a building architect in Lithuania?**
A: To practice as an architect in Lithuania, you must register with the Architects' Chamber of Lithuania. EU/EEA architects can have their qualifications recognized under the Professional Qualifications Directive. Non-EU architects must have their diplomas evaluated by the Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education (SKVC) and may need to complete additional exams or courses.
