## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Accounting Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: The monthly gross salary for accounting associate professionals in Lithuania typically ranges from €1,200 to €2,200 for entry-level to mid-level positions. Senior roles or those with specialized certifications (e.g., ACCA) may earn up to €3,000. Salaries vary by company size, location (Vilnius often higher), and experience.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign Accounting Associate Professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Lithuania as accounting associate professionals via the EU Blue Card (requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian wage) or a National Visa (D) for employment with a work permit obtained by the employer. The employer must prove the vacancy could not be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Accounting Associate Professional in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, employers require a bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or a related field. Professional certifications such as ACCA, CIMA, or Lithuanian accounting certificates (e.g., Apskaitos instituto) enhance prospects. Practical experience with Lithuanian tax laws and accounting software (e.g., Rivile, B1) is highly valued. Fluency in Lithuanian is often essential.
