## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for legislators in Lithuania?**
A: Members of the Seimas (parliament) receive a base monthly salary set by law, currently around €5,000–€6,000 gross before deductions. Additional allowances may apply for committee chairs or leadership roles. Salaries are indexed periodically and are significantly higher than the national average wage.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as legislators in Lithuania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Legislators in Lithuania are typically elected or appointed and must hold Lithuanian citizenship. The EU Blue Card and standard work permits are not applicable to legislative roles. Non-EU citizens may work in political advisory or support positions with a valid work permit, but cannot serve as legislators.

**Q: What qualifications or experience are required to become a legislator in Lithuania?**
A: There are no formal educational requirements, but most legislators hold a university degree, often in law, economics, or political science. Key paths include membership in a political party, prior experience in local government, or civil society leadership. Candidates must be at least 25 years old and meet citizenship and residency conditions.
