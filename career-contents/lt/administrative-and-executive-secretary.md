## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for administrative and executive secretaries in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the gross monthly salary for administrative and executive secretaries typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, industry, and location. Senior roles in larger companies or in Vilnius may earn higher. Entry-level positions start around €1,000 net.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for administrative secretaries to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and a national visa (D visa) to work as administrative secretaries in Lithuania. The employer must prove a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers requiring higher education and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (€2,300+ in 2023), which secretarial roles usually do not meet.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an administrative secretary in Lithuania?**
A: A secondary education with vocational training or a bachelor's degree in business administration is common. Fluency in Lithuanian is mandatory, and English or Russian is often required. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, organizational skills, and experience in office management are key. Some employers prefer candidates with a secretary's certificate.
