## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health associate professionals not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for health associate professionals in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions start around €800, while senior roles in private clinics can reach €2,000. Salaries in public institutions are typically lower than in private healthcare.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for health associate professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU health associate professionals can obtain a Lithuanian National Visa (D) for employment or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a recognized higher education degree (or 5 years relevant experience) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (currently around €2,000 gross monthly). The employer must also register the vacancy with the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: Which employers typically hire health associate professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Health associate professionals in Lithuania are employed by public and private hospitals, outpatient clinics, nursing homes, rehabilitation centers, and public health institutions. Roles include dental hygienists, medical laboratory technicians, physiotherapy assistants, and community health workers. Private practices and specialized diagnostic centers are also common employers.
