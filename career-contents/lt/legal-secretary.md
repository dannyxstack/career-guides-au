## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a legal secretary in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for legal secretaries in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and the firm's size. Senior legal secretaries may earn up to €2,000. These figures are indicative; actual pay varies with qualifications and employer.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign legal secretaries to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign nationals can work as legal secretaries in Lithuania via a national work permit or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or five years professional experience) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approximately 1.5 times the average gross salary). A standard work permit is also available but needs employer sponsorship and a labor market test.

**Q: What are typical duties and career prospects for legal secretaries in Lithuania?**
A: Legal secretaries in Lithuania support lawyers with document preparation, client communication, case file management, and scheduling. Career progression includes senior secretary, paralegal, or office manager roles. Proficiency in Lithuanian is often required, but English is valued in international firms. The role offers stable employment in the legal sector.
