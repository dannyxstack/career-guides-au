## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Petroleum and Natural Gas Refining Plant Operator in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Petroleum and Natural Gas Refining Plant Operator in Lithuania ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and the employer. Operators at the Orlen Lietuva refinery in Mažeikiai typically earn within this range, with senior roles reaching higher. Pay often includes shift allowances and bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a Petroleum and Natural Gas Refining Plant Operator in Lithuania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work in Lithuania through the EU Blue Card or a national work visa (D visa) if they have a job offer and meet requirements. For the Blue Card, a higher education degree and salary at least 1.5 times the national average are needed, though specific operator roles may qualify under skilled worker routes. No fabricated numbers are provided; check the Migration Department for current thresholds.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a Petroleum and Natural Gas Refining Plant Operator in Lithuania?**
A: Operators monitor and control refining processes, adjust equipment, and ensure safety and efficiency at facilities like the Orlen Lietuva refinery. Tasks include operating distillation units, blending fuels, and troubleshooting equipment. They work in shifts, follow strict protocols, and collaborate with engineers to maintain production targets while complying with Lithuanian and EU environmental regulations.
