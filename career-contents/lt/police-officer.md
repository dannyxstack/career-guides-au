## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for police officers in Lithuania?**
A: Police officer salaries in Lithuania depend on rank and experience. Starting base pay for a patrol officer is around €1,000–€1,200 gross per month. Senior officers, detectives, and specialists can earn up to €2,500 gross monthly. Additional allowances for night shifts, hazardous duty, and specializations apply. Net pay is lower after social security and income tax deductions.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as police officers in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Police officer roles in Lithuania require Lithuanian or another EU/EEA citizenship due to security clearances. The EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers does not apply to police positions, as they do not meet the required skill criteria. Non-EU citizens without citizenship of an EU/EEA state are not eligible for direct recruitment through standard migration routes.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for police officers in Lithuania?**
A: Police officers can advance through ranks (e.g., sergeant, lieutenant, captain) based on exams and performance. Specializations include criminal investigation, traffic, cybercrime, or joining elite units like ARAS. Further education at the Lithuanian Police School or Mykolas Romeris University supports promotion. Senior posts may require additional training and years of service.
