## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in Lithuania?**
A: Pay for water and firewood collectors in Lithuania varies widely as the work is often informal, seasonal, or part-time. Hourly rates typically match the national minimum wage (€5.65 per hour as of 2024) or slightly higher. Monthly earnings depend on hours worked, averaging €700–€1,000 gross for full-time effort, but many workers are paid per task or volume.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for water and firewood collection in Lithuania?**
A: This occupation does not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average. Non-EU workers may instead apply for a temporary residence permit for employment based on a job offer, but the employer must prove a labor market shortage. The process involves a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange and consular visa steps.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for water and firewood collectors in Lithuania?**
A: Water collectors typically gather water from public sources for households without piped supply, while firewood collectors gather and sometimes chop wood for heating. The work is physically demanding, often outdoors in rural areas, and may involve carrying heavy loads. Employment is usually informal or through small private contracts. Tools like containers or axes are provided by the worker or employer.
