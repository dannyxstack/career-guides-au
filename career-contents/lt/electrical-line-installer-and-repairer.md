## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for electrical line installers and repairers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for electrical line installers and repairers in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, certification, and region. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior or specialized roles can exceed €2,000. Overtime and hazard pay are common.

**Q: Can electrical line installers and repairers from outside the EU work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, the EU Blue Card is available for non-EU workers with a higher education degree or at least 5 years of relevant professional experience. For this occupation, vocational training and solid work history may qualify. The employer must offer a job meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Standard work permits are also a route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an electrical line installer in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, completion of a vocational training program in electrical engineering or a related field is required. Apprenticeships combining classroom instruction with on-the-job training are common. Certifications in high-voltage safety and first aid are mandatory. Licenses may be needed for work on live lines. Continuous learning is essential due to evolving grid technologies.
