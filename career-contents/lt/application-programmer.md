## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for applications programmers in Lithuania?**
A: According to recent labor market data, the average gross monthly salary for applications programmers in Lithuania ranges from €2,500 for junior roles to over €4,500 for experienced professionals. Senior positions at multinational companies can pay significantly more. Salaries vary by company size and location, with Vilnius offering the highest rates.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU applications programmers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer and a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approximately €29,400 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit is available for those with a valid job offer and relevant qualifications, such as a degree or proven experience.

**Q: What are the career prospects for applications programmers in Lithuania?**
A: Lithuania has a thriving IT sector with high demand for applications programmers. Career paths include advancement to senior developer, team lead, or solution architect. The startup ecosystem and presence of global tech companies offer diverse opportunities. Continuous learning is valued, with many employers supporting professional development.
