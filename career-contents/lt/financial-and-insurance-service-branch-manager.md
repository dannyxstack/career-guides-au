## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Financial and Insurance Services Branch Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for branch managers in Lithuania vary by company size and location, but the average monthly gross salary ranges from €3,500 to €6,000. In Vilnius, rates are often higher. Bonuses and benefits may also be included.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign national to work as a Financial and Insurance Services Branch Manager in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit or EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a higher-education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian salary (currently around €2,100/month gross). A standard work permit may be available for lower salaries. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for this role in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in finance, economics, or business is required, along with several years of experience in financial services. Professional certifications such as CFA or insurance licenses may be advantageous. Fluency in Lithuanian is often preferred, though larger international firms may accept English.
