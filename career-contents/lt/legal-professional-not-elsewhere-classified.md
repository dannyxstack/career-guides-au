## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: According to the State Social Insurance Fund Board (Sodra), the average gross monthly salary for legal professionals in Lithuania is approximately €2,500. However, earnings vary widely by experience, specialization, and employer, ranging from €1,800 for junior roles to over €4,000 for senior in-house counsel or legal advisors.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU legal professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU legal professionals can work in Lithuania via a national work permit (D visa) or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Both routes lead to temporary residence.

**Q: What types of roles are included under legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: This category covers legal specialists not fitting standard occupations, such as in-house corporate lawyers, legal advisors in NGOs or government, legal consultants, and contract managers. It excludes judges, prosecutors, and attorneys-at-law (advokatai). Roles typically require a master's degree in law and often Lithuanian language proficiency.
