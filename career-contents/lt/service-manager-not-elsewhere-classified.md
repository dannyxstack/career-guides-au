## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for services managers not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. As of 2023, the gross monthly salary for services managers in Lithuania typically ranges from €1,500 to €4,000, with a median around €2,200. Higher pay is common in IT, finance, and consultancy services.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for services managers moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a national visa (D visa) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage (approx. €2,100 monthly in 2023). Alternatively, a work permit can be obtained for roles meeting labor market needs.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for services managers in Lithuania?**
A: Services managers can progress to senior management roles such as operations director or general manager. Specialization in high-demand fields like IT services, hospitality, or business process outsourcing offers strong growth. Entrepreneurship is also common, with many starting consultancies or service companies.
