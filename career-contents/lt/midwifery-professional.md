## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for midwifery professionals in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for midwifery professionals in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Senior midwives in hospitals may earn higher. Salaries are typically supplemented by shift allowances and bonuses.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for midwifery professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU midwives need a work permit or EU Blue Card. First, obtain recognition of your professional qualifications from the Lithuanian Ministry of Health. Then, secure a job offer. The employer applies for a work permit, or if you meet salary thresholds (higher than average), you may qualify for an EU Blue Card. Both routes require a valid residence permit.

**Q: What are the career prospects for midwifery professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Midwifery is a regulated profession with strong demand due to an aging workforce and focus on maternal health. Career advancement includes specialization in neonatal care, clinical leadership, or teaching. Midwives can also work in private clinics or pursue further education in obstetrics and gynecology nursing.
