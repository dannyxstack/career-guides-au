## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Lithuania?**
A: Hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Lithuania, such as bike couriers or pedicab operators, often earn hourly wages ranging from €5 to €8, depending on the employer and location. Full-time monthly earnings may fall between the minimum wage (€924 as of 2024) and €1,200. Income varies with delivery volume, tips, and seasonal demand.

**Q: Can hand and pedal vehicle drivers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Lithuania?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers meeting specific salary and education thresholds, which this occupation does not satisfy. Third-country nationals can instead pursue a national work visa (D visa) if they secure a job offer; the employer must demonstrate that no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What are the career prospects for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Lithuania?**
A: Career advancement is limited, as these roles are typically entry-level. Drivers may transition into logistics coordination, dispatch management, or start their own small delivery service. Additional skills, such as route optimization or customer service, can improve opportunities. The demand is seasonal, with higher work in tourism-heavy areas.
