## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for spray painters and varnishers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for spray painters and varnishers in Lithuania vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions earn around €800–€1,000 per month gross, while experienced workers can earn €1,200–€1,600 gross. Overtime and specialized skills (e.g., automotive or industrial painting) may increase pay. These figures are approximate and subject to change.

**Q: Can foreign spray painters and varnishers work in Lithuania through an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for a national work visa (D visa) if they have a job offer from a Lithuanian employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, which typically requires a higher education degree or specialized skills. Spray painters without a degree may qualify for a temporary residence permit for employment if the job meets minimum salary requirements and the employer can justify hiring a foreigner.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a spray painter or varnisher in Lithuania?**
A: Formal qualifications are not always mandatory, but vocational training in painting or surface finishing is highly valued. Many employers prefer candidates with a certificate from a vocational school or apprenticeship. Experience with spray equipment and knowledge of safety regulations are essential. Continuous learning is needed to stay updated on new coatings and techniques.
