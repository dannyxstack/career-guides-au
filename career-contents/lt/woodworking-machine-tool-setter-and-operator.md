**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic programming and path optimization for CNC machines
- Repetitive wood cutting and shaping operations
- Vision-based automatic quality inspection
- Machine status monitoring and basic fault diagnosis
- Automated scheduling of production planning and material handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to optimise cutting paths to reduce waste
- Overlay real-time operational guides through augmented reality
- AI predictive maintenance reduces downtime
- Debug new programs using digital twin simulation
- Collaborative robots assist with heavy object handling to improve efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Real-time decision-making ability to handle natural defects in wood.
- Non-standard process adjustments for custom furniture
- On-site repair experience for mechanical faults in machine tools
- Intuitive judgment of wood characteristics (moisture content, grain)
- Communicate customization needs with designers and clients

**Skills to build:**

- CNC programming (G-code, CAD/CAM software)
- Industrial robot operation and maintenance
- Sensor and IoT device integration
- Data analysis and predictive maintenance basics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for woodworking machine tool setters and operators in Lithuania?**
A: Based on data from Sodra, the average gross monthly salary for woodworking machine operators in Lithuania is approximately €1,200–€1,600 as of 2023. Salaries vary by experience, region, and company size. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,800.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU woodworking machine operators to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card for highly skilled workers or a national work permit. For the Blue Card, a higher education degree and salary at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian salary are required. Employers can also apply for a work permit based on labor market needs. Applications are handled by the Migration Department.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for woodworking machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: With experience, operators can become team leaders, supervisors, or specialize in CNC programming. Further training in wood technology or engineering opens paths to production management or quality control. Vocational schools like VTDK offer relevant courses. Demand in Lithuania's growing wood industry supports career progression.
