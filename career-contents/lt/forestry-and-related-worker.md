- Using GPS and drones for forest mapping and boundary marking

**Human moat:**

- Intuition and experience-based judgment of forest ecosystems

- Social skills for collaborating and communicating with landowners and communities

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry workers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for forestry workers in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may apply.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for forestry workers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU forestry workers can apply for a national work visa (D visa) with a valid job offer from a Lithuanian employer. For higher-skilled roles, an EU Blue Card is possible if the worker holds a higher education degree or at least five years of relevant professional experience. The employer must demonstrate that the vacancy could not be filled locally.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for forestry workers in Lithuania?**
A: Experienced forestry workers can advance to team leader or forest inspector roles, or transition into forest management planning. With additional education, they may become forestry technicians or foresters. The State Forest Service offers professional development courses, and workers can specialize in areas like silviculture or nature conservation.
