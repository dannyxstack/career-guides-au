## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for optometrists and ophthalmic opticians in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, entry-level optometrists earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €2,000–€2,800. Salaries vary by employer (private clinics, optical chains, public hospitals) and location, with Vilnius and Kaunas offering higher pay. Many also receive performance bonuses.

**Q: What are the official routes for optometrists to work in Lithuania as non-EU nationals?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a recognized diploma and job offer) or a national work permit. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Migration Department. The Lithuanian Qualifications and VET Agency must verify your qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as an optometrist in Lithuania?**
A: To practice in Lithuania, you must hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree in optometry from a recognized institution and be licensed by the State Health Care Accreditation Agency. EU professionals can use the Professional Qualifications Recognition Directive; non-EU applicants may need additional examinations and Lithuanian language proficiency (B1 level).
