## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for medical imaging technicians in Lithuania typically range from €1,200 to €1,800 per month gross, depending on experience, workplace (public vs. private sector), and specialization. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior technicians in private clinics may earn up to €2,000.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU medical imaging technicians to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU technicians can apply for a national work visa or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree, a valid job offer meeting Lithuania's minimum salary threshold (set annually), and recognition of qualifications. Employers must obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange before the visa application.

**Q: What education and registration are required to work as a medical imaging technician in Lithuania?**
A: To work as a medical imaging technician in Lithuania, you need a bachelor's degree in radiography or medical imaging technology from an accredited institution. Additionally, registration with the State Health Care Accreditation Service under the Ministry of Health is mandatory. Continuing education is required for license renewal every five years.
