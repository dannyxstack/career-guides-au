## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dairy products maker in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for dairy products makers in Lithuania ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while experienced workers in larger dairies can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and shift bonuses are common.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU dairy products makers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as dairy products makers in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) for employment purposes. Alternatively, if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold, they may qualify for an EU Blue Card. The specific route depends on qualifications and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a dairy products maker in Lithuania?**
A: Most dairy products makers in Lithuania complete vocational training in food technology or dairy science, often through a professional school. Apprenticeships and on-the-job training are common. No university degree is required, but a secondary education and basic food safety knowledge are essential. Certification in HACCP is advantageous.
