## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for clerical support workers in Lithuania generally range from €900 to €1,500 gross per month, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions start around minimum wage (€924 in 2025), while senior roles in larger companies may exceed €1,500. Figures are indicative and subject to change.

**Q: Can I work in Lithuania as a Clerical Support Worker via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Lithuania through employer-sponsored work permits or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education diploma or five years’ professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the statutory salary threshold (1.5 times the average gross wage). Alternatively, a national work visa (D-type) is available with a valid employment contract.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for clerical support workers in Lithuania?**
A: Clerical support workers can advance to administrative specialist, office manager, or executive assistant roles by gaining experience and pursuing certifications in office management or software (e.g., SAP, MS Office). Large companies in Vilnius offer internal training programs. Fluency in Lithuanian is often required for progression, though English-speaking roles exist in international firms.
