## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for buyers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for buyers in Lithuania ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Entry-level positions start around €1,500, while senior buyers in sectors like manufacturing or retail can earn up to €4,500. These figures are based on 2024 data from state statistics and recruitment platforms.

**Q: Can foreign buyers work in Lithuania under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: Yes, foreign buyers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Lithuania if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (around €2,400/month in 2024). The employer must register the vacancy with the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. Alternatively, a national work permit may apply for lower salaries.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a buyer in Lithuania?**
A: Typically, employers require a bachelor's degree in business, economics, logistics, or a related field. Proficiency in English and Lithuanian is essential, and knowledge of additional EU languages is an advantage. Professional experience in procurement, supply chain, or sales is highly valued. Certifications like CIPS or ISM can boost career prospects, especially in multinational companies.
