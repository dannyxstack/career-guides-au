## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmaceutical technicians and assistants in Lithuania?**
A: As of recent data, the average gross monthly salary for pharmaceutical technicians in Lithuania ranges from 1000 to 1300 EUR, depending on experience and location. Senior technicians in major cities may earn higher. Entry-level positions start around 800 EUR. These figures are pre-tax and may vary by employer.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for pharmaceutical technicians to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU pharmaceutical technicians can apply for a national work visa if they have a job offer from a Lithuanian employer. For the EU Blue Card, you need a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently around 1.5 times average wage). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a pharmaceutical technician in Lithuania?**
A: To work as a pharmaceutical technician in Lithuania, you generally need a secondary education in pharmacy or a related field, plus registration with the State Medicines Control Agency. Some positions may require a higher education diploma. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain registration.
