## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for house builders in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for house builders in Lithuania is approximately €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced builders or foremen can earn up to €1,800 or more. Overtime and bonuses can increase total earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU house builders to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a national visa (D) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must first obtain a work permit from the Lithuanian Employment Service. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with higher education and higher salary thresholds, but may not apply to typical house builder roles. The specific route depends on qualifications and job offer.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for house builders in Lithuania?**
A: Experienced house builders can advance to foreman, site manager, or construction project supervisor roles. Additional certifications in specialized trades (e.g., bricklaying, roofing) or safety management can enhance prospects. Some pursue self-employment or start their own construction company. Continuing education at vocational schools also supports career growth.
