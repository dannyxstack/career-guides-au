## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Policy Administration Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, the average gross monthly salary for Policy Administration Professionals ranges from €1,800 to €2,800, depending on experience and employer. Salaries in Vilnius tend to be higher. Entry-level positions start around €1,400, while senior roles can exceed €3,500.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Policy Administration Professionals to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Policy Administration Professionals via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a National Visa (D) with a work permit. Requirements include a university degree, a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 1.5x average Lithuanian wage), and employer sponsorship. The Blue Card facilitates EU mobility.

**Q: What are the main career paths for Policy Administration Professionals in Lithuania?**
A: Career progression often starts from junior policy analyst or assistant roles, advancing to senior policy advisor, team lead, or department head. Many professionals move into strategic planning or government relations. Opportunities exist in public institutions, NGOs, and private sector firms such as insurance or consultancy, with potential to specialize in healthcare, finance, or EU affairs.
