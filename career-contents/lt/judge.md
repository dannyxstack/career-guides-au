## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a judge in Lithuania?**
A: Judges' salaries in Lithuania are determined by law and vary by court level and experience. As of 2023, a district court judge earns around €3,500–€5,000 gross monthly, while Supreme Court judges may earn up to €7,000. Salaries are indexed to the national average wage.

**Q: Can a foreign judge work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: No, because Lithuanian law requires judges to be citizens of Lithuania. The EU Blue Card and other work visas apply to highly skilled workers in private sector roles, but judicial positions are reserved for citizens and not accessible via these routes.

**Q: What are the requirements to become a judge in Lithuania?**
A: To become a judge, one must have a Master's degree in law, at least 5 years of legal practice, pass a qualification exam, and undergo a selection process. Candidates must be Lithuanian citizens of good moral character and complete a mandatory training at the National Courts Administration.
