## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for signwriters, decorative painters, engravers, and etchers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level workers earn around €800–€1,000 gross per month, while experienced specialists can earn €1,200–€1,600. Those with niche engraving or high-end decorative painting skills may earn higher rates, especially in larger cities like Vilnius or Kaunas.

**Q: What are the official work visa and EU Blue Card requirements for foreign signwriters, decorative painters, engravers, and etchers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a national work visa (D visa) after receiving a job offer and a work permit from the Lithuanian Employment Service. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree or equivalent professional experience, and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average Lithuanian salary).

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a signwriter or decorative painter in Lithuania?**
A: Formal education in fine arts, design, or vocational training in decorative painting or engraving is beneficial. Apprenticeships under experienced artisans are common. For sign writing, knowledge of design software and manual lettering is valued. Licenses are not required, but a portfolio showcasing skills helps secure jobs.
