## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for employment agents and contractors in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, employment agents and contractors earn an average gross monthly salary between €1,200 and €2,500, depending on experience, sector, and company size. Commission-based roles may yield higher earnings. The median is around €1,800. Salaries in Vilnius are generally higher than in other regions.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign employment agents to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign nationals can work as employment agents in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers or a national work permit (D visa) with a job offer. The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. Both require a valid employment contract and meeting minimum salary thresholds set by Lithuanian law.

**Q: What qualifications or skills are needed to become an employment agent in Lithuania?**
A: Employment agents in Lithuania typically need fluency in Lithuanian and English, strong negotiation skills, and knowledge of local labor law. A degree in human resources or business is advantageous. Career progression can lead to senior consultant or branch manager roles. Registration with the Lithuanian Labour Exchange may be required for certain activities.
