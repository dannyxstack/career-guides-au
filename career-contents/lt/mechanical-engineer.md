## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CAD drafting and 2D drawing generation
- Standard finite element analysis (FEA) and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations
- Generate Standard BOMs and Technical Documentation
- Routine tolerance analysis and dimensional inspection
- Predictive maintenance planning based on historical data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-driven generative design (e.g., topology optimization)
- Machine learning-based failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)
- Digital twin real-time simulation and optimization
- Smart materials selection and supply chain optimization
- Write automated test and verification scripts

**Human moat:**

- Cross-system physical intuition and innovative design
- Complex nonlinear problem diagnosis and on-site debugging
- Ethical and responsible decision-making for safety-critical systems
- Unstructured communication with clients and suppliers
- Multidisciplinary integration and system architecture decision-making

**Skills to build:**

- AI-enhanced design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Python/R for data analysis and automation scripts
- Digital Twin and IoT Integration Knowledge
- Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing) Design Optimization
- Machine Learning Basics and Fault Prediction
- System modeling and simulation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a mechanical engineer in Lithuania?**
A: Entry-level mechanical engineers earn around €1,800 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €3,200. The average annual gross salary is approximately €28,000, varying by industry, location (e.g., Vilnius, Kaunas), and company size. Senior roles in engineering design or management may exceed €4,000 monthly.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for mechanical engineers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU mechanical engineers can obtain a national work visa (D visa) with a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card requires a higher-skilled job paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania (€33,480 in 2023). Applicants need a recognized degree or 5+ years of experience. Applications are processed by the Migration Department.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for mechanical engineers in Lithuania?**
A: Mechanical engineers in Lithuania find roles in manufacturing, renewable energy, automotive, and machinery design. Major employers include Continental, ABB, and local startups. Career progression often leads to senior engineer, project manager, or R&D specialist. Advanced degrees or specializations in robotics or automation enhance prospects. The sector is growing due to Industry 4.0 investments.
