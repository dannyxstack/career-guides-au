**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shelf fillers in Lithuania?**
A: Shelf fillers in Lithuania typically earn between €600 and €900 per month gross, depending on employer, experience, and location. Minimum wage as of 2024 is €700 per month. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings. Salaries are usually paid monthly, and some employers offer bonuses or meal vouchers.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for shelf fillers in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as shelf fillers in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is typically for high-skilled roles requiring higher education; shelf fillers do not meet the skill threshold, so the Blue Card is not a viable route.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shelf fillers in Lithuania?**
A: Shelf fillers can advance to roles like stockroom supervisor, merchandiser, or department manager with experience and training. Some retail chains offer internal development programs. Additional responsibilities, such as inventory management or ordering, can lead to higher pay and supervisory positions. Proficiency in Lithuanian and digital tools may accelerate progression.
