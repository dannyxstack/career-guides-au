## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior technicians can earn up to €2,200. Salaries are typically higher in Vilnius and Kaunas.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree or relevant experience and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (currently €2,184/month gross for regulated professions). Alternatively, an employer can sponsor a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Lithuania?**
A: With experience, technicians can progress to senior technician, network specialist, or project manager roles. Certifications in optical networks, 5G, or VoIP improve prospects. Many advance into system engineering, IT infrastructure management, or telecommunications consultancy within Lithuania's growing tech sector.
