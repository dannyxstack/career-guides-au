## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for glaziers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for glaziers in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced professionals in major cities like Vilnius can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and specialized skills may increase pay.

**Q: Can glaziers from outside the EU work in Lithuania via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU glaziers can apply for a Lithuanian work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €2,370 gross/month in 2025). A standard work visa is possible for lower salaries. Both routes require employer sponsorship and meet immigration rules.

**Q: What is the job outlook for glaziers in Lithuania?**
A: Demand for glaziers in Lithuania is stable, driven by construction and renovation projects. Opportunities exist in residential and commercial sectors, especially in growing cities. While not high-growth, skilled glaziers are consistently needed. Apprenticeships and vocational training are available, and experienced workers may advance to supervisory roles.
