## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical gross monthly salary for a teaching professional not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for such teaching professionals typically ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience, sector (private vs. public), and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced professionals in specialized fields can earn up to €2,500.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU teaching professional to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU teaching professionals can work in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) with employer sponsorship, or the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€1,625.50/month in 2023). Both require a valid work permit.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Lithuania?**
A: Opportunities include roles in private language schools, international schools, vocational training centers, and corporate training. With increasing demand for English and ICT skills, professionals can also work as tutors, exam preparers, or curriculum developers. Public sector positions are limited mainly to licensed teachers.
