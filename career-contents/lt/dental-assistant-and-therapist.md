## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dental assistants and therapists in Lithuania?**
A: According to official statistics, the average gross monthly salary for dental assistants and therapists in Lithuania typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced professionals in private clinics may earn higher salaries. Overtime and bonuses can also affect total compensation.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as dental assistants or therapists in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain work authorization through the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid job offer, relevant qualifications (e.g., diploma in dental assisting), and a salary meeting the national threshold. Alternatively, a national work permit (D type visa) is available for standard employment. Both require employer sponsorship and compliance with Lithuanian immigration laws.

**Q: What are the typical duties and career progression opportunities for dental assistants in Lithuania?**
A: Dental assistants in Lithuania support dentists by preparing treatment rooms, sterilizing instruments, assisting during procedures, and managing patient records. Career progression may involve specializing in orthodontics or oral surgery, advancing to lead dental assistant, or pursuing further education to become a dental hygienist or therapist. Continuing professional development is encouraged for higher-level roles.
