## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fur and leather preparing machine operators in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for fur and leather preparing machine operators in Lithuania ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and company size. Operators may also receive bonuses or overtime pay. This is comparable to other machine operator roles in the textile and leather industry.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU citizens to work as a fur and leather preparing machine operator in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Lithuania via a national work visa (D visa) for employment. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available if the job requires higher qualifications and meets the salary threshold (set at 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). For machine operator roles, standard work permits are more common, as Blue Cards typically target highly skilled positions.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a fur and leather preparing machine operator in Lithuania?**
A: Most operators complete vocational training in textile or leather technology, or learn through on-the-job training. Employers often value practical experience over formal education. Some may require basic technical skills and knowledge of machine safety. Apprenticeships or short courses are common entry points.
