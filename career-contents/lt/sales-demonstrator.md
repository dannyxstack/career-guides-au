**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeated on-site demonstrations of product standard functions
- Using pre-recorded videos or AI-generated content to replace on-site explanations
- Frequently asked customer questions; replaced by chatbots
- Product parameter query and comparison, replaced by self-service terminals or AR applications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer data to assist in customizing personalized presentation scripts
- Real-time speech transcription and translation, supporting multilingual audiences
- AR/VR technology enhances the immersion and interactivity of product displays
- Sales data dashboard helps presenters adjust strategies in real time

**Human moat:**

- Soft skills for building emotional trust and client relationships
- Creativity and narrative skills in complex product experiences
- Flexible adaptation for cross-cultural and non-standard scenarios

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Sales Demonstrators in Lithuania ranges from €700 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions typically start around the minimum wage (€924 as of 2025), while experienced demonstrators in major cities like Vilnius may earn higher rates plus commissions.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Sales Demonstrators in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for a Lithuanian National Visa (D) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€2,070 gross/month in 2025). Sales Demonstrator roles often do not meet this threshold, so a standard work permit is more common.

**Q: What skills are most important for Sales Demonstrators in the Lithuanian job market?**
A: Key skills include fluency in Lithuanian and Russian (for broader customer reach), strong communication and persuasion abilities, product knowledge, and flexibility with working hours. Experience in retail or event promotions is valued. Employers often seek candidates with a friendly demeanor and ability to handle product samples and point-of-sale materials effectively.
