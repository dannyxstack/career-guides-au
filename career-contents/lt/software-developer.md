## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Lithuania?**
A: Average gross monthly salary for software developers in Lithuania ranges from €3,500 to €6,000, with junior developers earning €2,500–€3,500 and senior developers earning €5,000–€8,000. Salaries are higher in Vilnius and Kaunas, and vary by specialization (e.g., AI, fintech).

**Q: What are the official routes for obtaining a work visa or EU Blue Card as a software developer in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU software developers can apply for an EU Blue Card with a valid job offer, a higher education degree or five years of professional experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Alternatively, a National Work Visa (Type D) is available, requiring employer sponsorship and a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange.

**Q: What career growth opportunities exist for software developers in Lithuania?**
A: Lithuania's thriving tech sector offers advancement to senior, lead, or architect roles, especially in fintech, cybersecurity, and AI. The startup ecosystem provides dynamic career paths, and professional development is supported by local meetups, conferences, and training programs.
