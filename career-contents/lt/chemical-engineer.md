## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for chemical engineers in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for chemical engineers in Lithuania vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,200–€1,600 gross monthly, while mid-career professionals earn €2,000–€3,000. Senior roles or those in management can reach €4,000–€5,000 per month. Additional benefits may include bonuses, health insurance, and paid leave.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for chemical engineers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU chemical engineers can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary) or a national work visa (D visa) for skilled employment. Both require a recognized degree, a valid job contract, and employer sponsorship. Detailed requirements are available from the Migration Department.

**Q: What are the main industries hiring chemical engineers in Lithuania?**
A: Chemical engineers in Lithuania find opportunities in pharmaceuticals (e.g., biotechnology), petrochemicals, food processing, and environmental technology. Major employers include companies like Thermo Fisher Scientific, Lifosa (fertilizers), and various energy firms. Research institutions and universities also offer roles, particularly in sustainable chemistry and materials science.
