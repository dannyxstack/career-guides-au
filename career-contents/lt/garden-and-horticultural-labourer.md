## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Lithuania?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Lithuania ranges between €700 and €1,000, depending on experience and region. Seasonal work may offer slightly higher rates during peak periods. Minimum wage (as of 2024) is €924 gross per month, but many employers provide additional bonuses or accommodation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as garden and horticultural labourers in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Lithuania is for highly qualified workers with higher education and a high salary threshold, typically not applicable to garden labourers. Non-EU nationals can instead apply for a national work visa (D visa) based on a job offer and work permit, which requires proof of qualifications and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What training is required for garden and horticultural labourers in Lithuania?**
A: No formal qualifications are mandatory for entry-level positions, but vocational training in horticulture or landscaping from institutions like the Lithuanian University of Agriculture can improve job prospects. Employers often provide on-the-job training for tasks like planting, pruning, and using equipment. Certificates in pesticide handling may be required.
