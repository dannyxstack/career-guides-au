## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physiotherapists in Lithuania?**
A: The gross monthly salary for physiotherapists in Lithuania typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,600, depending on experience, location, and sector. Public sector salaries are often lower than private practice. According to official statistics, the average gross salary in healthcare is around €1,400, but entry-level positions may start at €900.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for physiotherapists to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or a national work visa. The EU Blue Card requires a recognized higher education degree, a job offer as a physiotherapist, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Lithuania. The national work visa is for shorter-term employment and requires a valid job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a physiotherapist in Lithuania?**
A: To practice as a physiotherapist in Lithuania, you must have a recognized bachelor's or master's degree in physiotherapy. EU/EEA qualifications are automatically recognized under professional directives. Non-EU diplomas must be evaluated by the Centre for Quality Assessment in Higher Education. Additionally, you must register with the State Health Care Accreditation Service.
