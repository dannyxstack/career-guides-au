## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for building caretakers in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and the size of the property. Entry-level positions may start at the minimum wage (€924 as of 2025), while experienced caretakers in larger complexes can earn higher. Some employers provide additional benefits like reduced rent or utilities.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a building caretaker in Lithuania via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit or EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree (or 5 years professional experience) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€2,078/month in 2025), which typically exceeds building caretaker pay. A national work permit is more common for this role, requiring an employer to apply through the Lithuanian Labour Exchange after a labour market test.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building caretaker in Lithuania?**
A: Building caretakers in Lithuania handle routine maintenance, cleaning common areas, minor repairs (plumbing, electrical), and security oversight. They manage waste disposal, coordinate larger repairs with contractors, and ensure compliance with building regulations. In apartment blocks, they often serve as a resident contact and may handle mail delivery or parcel collection.
