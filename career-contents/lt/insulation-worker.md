## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for insulation workers in Lithuania?**
A: As of 2024, the average gross monthly salary for insulation workers in Lithuania ranges from €1,100 to €1,800, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level workers earn around €1,000, while those with specialized skills or certifications can earn up to €2,200. Overtime and project-based bonuses are common, and wages are generally higher in major cities like Vilnius, Kaunas, and Klaipėda.

**Q: How can a non-EU insulation worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU insulation workers typically apply for a national work visa (D visa) after their employer secures a work permit from the Lithuanian Labour Exchange. The EU Blue Card is an option for highly qualified workers, but it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €2,600 gross monthly in 2024). Insulation workers without a degree may not qualify for the Blue Card and should follow the standard work permit route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for insulation workers in Lithuania?**
A: Experienced insulation workers can advance to team leader, site supervisor, or quality inspector. Obtaining certifications in specialized materials (e.g., spray foam, fireproofing) or completing vocational training in construction management can lead to higher-paying roles. Some workers become self-employed contractors or start their own insulation businesses. Continuous professional development through courses offered by the Lithuanian Construction Training Centre is recommended.
