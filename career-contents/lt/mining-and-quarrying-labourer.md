**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Ore handling and transportation: replaced by autonomous trucks and conveyor belts
- Equipment cleaning: handled by automatic cleaning robots
- Site maintenance and inspection: performed by drones and AI vision systems
- Manual crushing and screening: completed by automated crushers
- Safety monitoring records: AI sensors and automated reporting systems replace manual work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operate automated equipment (e.g., remote-controlled excavators, drills).
- Quick troubleshooting of equipment issues using AI fault diagnosis systems
- Optimizing mining processes using digital twin technology
- Real-time ore positioning and quality data obtained through AR glasses

**Human moat:**

- Emergency judgment in complex geological environments.
- On-site coordination for collaborative operation of multiple devices
- Ability to quickly resolve non-routine faults
- Experience working with specialized roles such as blasters

**Skills to build:**

- Automated mining equipment operation and maintenance
- Basic data analysis and AI scheduling system usage
- Drone/robot inspection and control
- Safety protocols and digital report generation
- Mechanical maintenance basics (hydraulics, electrical)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Mining and Quarrying Labourers in Lithuania?**
A: Mining and quarrying labourers in Lithuania typically earn between €700 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience and region. The minimum monthly wage in 2024 is €924, so many entry-level positions start near this level. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total pay, and wages are subject to income tax and social insurance contributions.

**Q: Can mining and quarrying labourers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: For low-skilled labourer roles, the EU Blue Card is not available as it requires higher education or at least 5 years of professional experience. Instead, non-EU workers can apply for a national work visa (D visa) and temporary residence permit based on employment. The employer must obtain a work permit and prove labour market shortage. These permits are granted for specific employers and job roles.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for mining and quarrying labourers in Lithuania?**
A: Duties include extracting materials like sand, gravel, or stone using hand tools or machinery, assisting with blasting, sorting and loading materials, and maintaining equipment. Work is physically demanding, often outdoors and in remote locations. Safety gear is mandatory, and shifts may include weekends. Opportunities for advancement are limited but can lead to machine operator roles with additional training.
