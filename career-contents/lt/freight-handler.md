## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive lifting and stacking in warehouses
- Manual loading and unloading of standardized goods from trucks
- Perform simple sorting and classification according to instructions.
- Use manual forklifts for short-distance transfers

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI scheduling systems to optimize material handling routes and sequences
- Operate automated equipment (e.g., AGVs, robotic arms) for loading and unloading
- Receive and feedback cargo information via smart terminals

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, irregular, or fragile goods
- Flexibly adapt to complex environments (e.g., temporary space adjustments)
- Monitor and intervene in automation system anomalies
- Assume safety responsibilities and implement emergency plans

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of automated equipment like forklifts
- Basic Logistics Information System Operation
- Safety procedures and anomaly handling
- Cargo Classification and Quality Inspection Knowledge
- Team collaboration and communication.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for freight handlers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for freight handlers in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,000 as of 2024. Pay often aligns with the national minimum wage, which is currently €924 gross per month. Overtime and night shift bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU freight handlers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as freight handlers in Lithuania through a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as it requires higher education and a salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average salary). Freight handlers may qualify for a work permit if the employer demonstrates a labor shortage.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for freight handlers in Lithuania?**
A: Freight handlers can advance to supervisory roles such as warehouse team leader or shift manager with experience. Additional training in logistics or forklift operation can lead to positions like logistics coordinator or warehouse manager. Some pursue certifications in supply chain management to move into planning or procurement.
