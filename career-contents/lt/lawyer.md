- AI-based case prediction and litigation strategy optimization

**Human moat:**

- On-the-spot adaptability and persuasion skills in courtroom argument and negotiation

- Empathy to build trust with clients and understand deep needs

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a lawyer in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for lawyers in Lithuania ranges from €2,000 to €3,000, depending on experience, specialization, and location. Senior lawyers in large firms or with niche expertise can earn significantly more. According to official statistics, the median salary for legal professionals is around €2,500 gross per month.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for lawyers moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU lawyers can work in Lithuania via an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary) and a recognized higher education qualification. Alternatively, a national work permit may be required for lower-skilled roles. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with Lithuanian immigration regulations.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for lawyers in Lithuania?**
A: Lawyers in Lithuania can advance from associate to partner in law firms, or specialize in areas like corporate, tax, or EU law. Many also pursue roles in-house at companies, government agencies, or international organizations. Continuing legal education and language skills (especially English) are key for growth. Becoming a judge or prosecutor is another path requiring additional exams.
