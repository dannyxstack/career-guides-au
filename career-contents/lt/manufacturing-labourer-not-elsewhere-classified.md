## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for manufacturing labourers in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for manufacturing labourers in Lithuania ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on industry and region. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced workers may earn more. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total pay. The minimum monthly wage is €924 (as of 2025), but many employers offer higher.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign manufacturing labourers to work in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as manufacturing labourers in Lithuania via an employer-sponsored work permit and temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation due to its skill level. The official route is a national visa (type D) followed by a temporary residence permit, subject to labour market tests.

**Q: What are the main duties of manufacturing labourers in Lithuania?**
A: Manufacturing labourers in Lithuania perform tasks such as assembling products, operating machinery, packing goods, and maintaining cleanliness in factories (e.g., food, textiles, electronics). No formal education is required; on-the-job training is provided. Physical stamina and attention to detail are essential. Career progression may lead to supervisory roles.
