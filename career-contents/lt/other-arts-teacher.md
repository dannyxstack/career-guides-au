## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an arts teacher in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for arts teachers in Lithuania vary widely by institution and hours. Full-time positions at public schools or cultural centers typically pay €800–€1,500 per month before tax. Private lessons or freelancing can yield higher hourly rates but less stability. Self-employed teachers set their own fees, often €15–€40 per hour.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for an arts teacher moving to Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU arts teachers can work in Lithuania via a national work permit (D visa) sponsored by a Lithuanian employer, or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers if the salary meets the threshold (currently 1.5× the average annual wage). Self-employed individuals may apply for a temporary residence permit for business purposes.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to work as an arts teacher in Lithuania?**
A: Most institutions require a bachelor's degree in art education or a related field, plus pedagogical training. For private lessons, no formal qualification is mandatory, but recognized certificates (e.g., from the Lithuanian Ministry of Education) boost credibility. Fluency in Lithuanian is typically expected, especially for school positions.
