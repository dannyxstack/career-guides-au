## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for police inspectors and detectives in Lithuania?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for police inspectors and detectives in Lithuania ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and rank. Entry-level inspectors earn around €1,500, while senior detectives can earn up to €3,000. Benefits include allowances for shift work and special assignments.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a police inspector in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU citizens may apply for an EU Blue Card or work permit for highly skilled roles, but police inspector and detective positions in Lithuania are typically reserved for Lithuanian citizens due to national security requirements. Exceptions are rare and usually require EU/EEA nationality. Thus, the work visa route is not standard for this occupation.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for police inspectors in Lithuania?**
A: Police inspectors can advance to senior detective, chief inspector, or supervisory roles within the Lithuanian Police Force. Further promotions include ranks such as commissioner. Specialization in areas like cybercrime or forensic investigation is possible. Continuous training and passing examinations are required for promotion.
