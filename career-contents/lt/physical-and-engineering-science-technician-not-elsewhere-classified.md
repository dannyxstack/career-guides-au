## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for physical and engineering science technicians in Lithuania?**
A: In Lithuania, physical and engineering science technicians typically earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross monthly, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior technicians can earn up to €2,500. Salaries vary by region and company size.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign physical and engineering science technicians to work in Lithuania?**
A: Foreign nationals can apply for a Lithuanian National Visa (D) with a job offer, or for the EU Blue Card if highly qualified (requires higher education and salary at least 1.5 times the national average). A temporary residence permit for employment, typically employer-sponsored, is another official route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for physical and engineering science technicians in Lithuania?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior technician, team leader, or specialist roles. Further education in engineering or a related field may lead to engineer positions. Lithuania's expanding tech and manufacturing sectors offer opportunities in R&D, quality control, and project management. Professional certifications also enhance career prospects.
