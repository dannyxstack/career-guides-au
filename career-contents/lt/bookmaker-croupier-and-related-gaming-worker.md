## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a croupier in Lithuania?**
A: Salaries for croupiers in Lithuania vary by casino and experience. Entry-level positions often start near the national minimum wage (around €924 gross per month in 2024), while experienced croupiers may earn above the average salary. Tips and bonuses can significantly increase total earnings, particularly in popular casinos.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU nationals to work as a croupier in Lithuania?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit sponsored by a casino. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, but croupier roles typically do not meet the required salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross wage) or higher education criteria. A standard work permit is the usual route.

**Q: What training or qualifications are required to become a croupier in Lithuania?**
A: You must be at least 21, have a clean criminal record, and complete a croupier training course approved by the Gaming Control Authority. Some casinos offer internal training. No specific formal education is required, but fluency in Lithuanian and English is beneficial.
