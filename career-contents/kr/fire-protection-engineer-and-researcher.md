# Fire Protection Engineer and Researcher — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15821 (KECO)**

Professionals who protect lives and property from fire through fire system design, fire safety research, and disaster prevention technology development.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

| Fire Safety Manager | Korea Fire Safety Institute | Required | Person responsible for managing fire safety targets |
| Fire Protection Engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Recommended for advanced engineers |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can advance from junior engineer to mid-level, senior engineer, and project manager. Researchers can grow from postdoctoral to senior researcher and principal researcher.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple repetitive design tasks
- Data collection and organization
- Basic calculations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fire simulation
- Risk assessment
- Design verification
- Accident analysis

**Human moat:**

- Legal interpretation and application
- Emergency response
- Field experience-based judgment

**Skills to build:**

- AI/machine learning basics
- Fire simulation software
- Data analysis
- Risk management

**Key growth areas:**

- Fire safety
- Fire prediction technology
- Smart disaster prevention
- Manufacturing safety

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone thorough with safety and regulations
- Someone who enjoys scientific analysis and problem-solving
- Someone very interested in disaster prevention

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who dislikes fieldwork and regulatory compliance
- Someone who feels burdened by emergency response

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a fire protection engineer?**
A: Entry-level 35-45 million KRW, mid-level 50-70 million KRW, senior 75-100 million KRW.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work in fire protection engineering?**
A: Can work through an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa, and later switch to an F-2 (Residence) visa.

**Q: What are the main certifications required for this job?**
A: Fire-fighting equipment engineer is essential, and fire safety manager qualification is also required. Fire protection engineer is recommended for advanced technicians.
