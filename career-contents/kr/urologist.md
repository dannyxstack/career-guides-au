# Urologist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 301111 (KECO)**

A specialist who diagnoses and treats diseases of the urinary system; obtains a medical license under the Medical Act, completes residency training, and acquires a specialist certificate.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Medical School (6-year program) | 6 years | $40,000,000~$80,000,000 (Varies by national/public/private school and scholarships) |
| Internship (1 year) + Residency (4 years) | 5 years | $30,000,000~$50,000,000 (Receives salary during training period (approx. 30-50 million KRW/year)) |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong passion and endurance for medicine
- People with precise surgical skills and meticulous patient response abilities
- People interested in continuous learning and research

**Who is not suited**
- People who find it difficult to endure long training periods and high work intensity
- People who prefer independent work over collaboration with others

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of a urologist?**
A: For a junior specialist, the annual salary is about 80-120 million KRW, and with experience and opening a private practice, it can exceed 500 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreign doctors work as urologists in Korea?**
A: Yes, but an E-7 visa is required, and you must obtain a Korean medical license and specialist qualification, which is realistically very difficult.

**Q: How long does it take to become a urologist?**
A: It takes at least 11 years: 6 years of medical school, 1 year of internship, and 4 years of residency.
