# Transmission and Distribution Equipment Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15314 (KECO)**

This engineer is responsible for designing, constructing, operating, and maintaining transmission and distribution equipment to stably supply electricity generated at power plants to consumers via substations and transmission lines.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After high school graduation, possible to join public companies like KEPCO, or after associate degree, join engineering firms. After acquiring professional engineer certification, can advance to management; some may enter overseas projects.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of transmission and distribution facility technicians?**
A: It varies by experience, but for entry-level it is about 35-45 million KRW, and for senior level it is 60-90 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, the E-7-1 (Specific Activities) visa is possible, requiring an electrician certification and at least one year of experience.

**Q: What are the required certifications?**
A: The electrician certification is mandatory, and obtaining a senior electrician certification is advantageous in the long term.
