## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone with creative planning and leadership
- Someone who can communicate and coordinate with diverse artists
- A systematic person skilled in budget and schedule management

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who focuses only on administrative work rather than artistic sense
- Someone who prefers autocratic decisions over flexible communication

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for art, design, and broadcast managers?**
A: Approximately 80-150 million KRW for 10+ years of experience, with a median around 60 million KRW. Varies greatly by organization size.

**Q: Can foreigners get an employment visa for this occupation?**
A: Possible with E-7 visa (Specific Activities), but management roles require high expertise and experience, making issuance strict. F-2 visa is more flexible.

**Q: Is there a required certification?**
A: No legally required certification, but a cultural arts manager certification or related master's degree helps with career advancement.
