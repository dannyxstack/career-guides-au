# Electrical Supervision Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15315 (KECO)**

An engineer who supervises whether construction is carried out appropriately according to design documents and specifications of electrical works, and manages quality and safety.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone meticulous and detail-oriented
- Someone with knowledge and experience in electrical equipment
- Someone who communicates well on site

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who dislikes outdoor field work
- Someone lacking responsibility

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an electrical supervisor?**
A: For entry-level, around 40 million KRW pre-tax; with experience and qualifications, up to 80 million KRW or more.

**Q: Can foreigners work as electrical supervisors?**
A: Available on an E-7 visa but requires Korean language ability and relevant qualifications; an employer sponsor is needed.

**Q: Is an electrician's license essential?**
A: It's not mandatory but advantageous for employment and promotion, especially recommended early in your career.
