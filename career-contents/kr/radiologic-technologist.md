# Radiologic Technologist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30621 (KECO)**

A specialized medical professional who operates imaging equipment in medical institutions to capture images for diagnosis and treatment, establishes radiation therapy plans, and performs radiation safety management.

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- A person interested in medical technology and meticulous
- A person with patience and responsibility when dealing with patients
- A person adaptable to operating high-tech equipment

**Who is not suited**
- A person with a strong fear of radiation exposure
- A person who finds repetitive tasks boring

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average starting salary of a radiologic technologist?**
A: The starting salary is about ₩30-38 million depending on the hospital size, and can rise to ₩50-70 million with experience.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a radiologic technologist in Korea?**
A: It is possible, but a Korean Ministry of Health and Welfare license is required, and you can get a job through an E-7 visa. However, it is limited as an immigration path due to language and license requirements.

**Q: What is the main job of a radiologic technologist?**
A: It includes X-ray, CT, MRI scanning and assisting in reading, radiation therapy planning, and radiation safety management.
