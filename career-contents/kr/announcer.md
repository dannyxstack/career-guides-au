# Announcer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41631 (KECO)**

A professional who delivers information and hosts broadcasts such as news, sports commentary, and entertainment programs, requiring excellent language skills and quick wit.


---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Korean Language Proficiency Test (KBS Korean Language Test) | KBS Korean Language Institute | Optional | Additional points given by some broadcasters |
| Broadcast and Communication Engineer | Korea Broadcast and Communication Promotion Agency | Optional | Not required for announcers in non-technical roles |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 4,000. With the expansion of digital media and the growth of personal broadcasting like YouTube, demand for announcers at traditional broadcasters is stagnating, but freelance opportunities are expected to increase.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

A common path is to gain experience at a local broadcaster, then move to a metropolitan broadcaster, or go freelance to work on various platforms.

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone with excellent language skills and seamless hosting ability
- Someone with quick wit and crisis management skills
- Someone who stays calm under pressure

**Who is not suited**
- Someone extremely reluctant to speak in front of people
- Someone who cannot adapt flexibly in improvisational situations

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an announcer?**
A: Approximately 30-45 million KRW for entry-level, over 100 million KRW for freelancers with 10+ years of experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as announcers in Korea?**
A: Possible, but requires an E-7 visa, Korean language proficiency is essential, and competition is very fierce.

**Q: Do I need to attend university to become an announcer?**
A: It varies by broadcaster, but a bachelor's degree is preferred, especially in journalism and broadcasting.
