# Accounting Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02711 (KECO)**

An office worker responsible for accounting and financial tasks such as preparing financial statements, tax reporting, and payroll calculation.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented and strong with numbers
- Person who consistently performs repetitive tasks
- A person who follows the rules well within the organization

**Who is not suited**
- Prefers creative and free work
- Someone who is sensitive to change and dislikes simple repetitive tasks

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an accounting clerk?**
A: Based on 0–3 years of experience, the pre-tax salary is about 24–30 million KRW; for 10+ years, it's around 45–60 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job as an accounting clerk?**
A: It's possible with an E-7 visa, but degree and experience requirements are strict; foreign companies are more favorable than local Korean firms.

**Q: How can an accounting clerk become a tax accountant?**
A: You must pass the tax accountant exam and meet the academic credit conditions or accumulate practical experience before applying.
