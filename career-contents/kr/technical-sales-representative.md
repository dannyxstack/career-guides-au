# Technical Sales Representative — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61211 (KECO)**

Technical Sales Representatives sell specialized technology products (IT, semiconductors, machinery, etc.) to businesses, analyzing customer technical needs and proposing customized solutions.

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 85,000. Growth in AI and semiconductor industries is expected to increase demand for high-value technical sales, but demand in mid-to-low price segments may stagnate due to automation and expanded online channels.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level Sales Associate → Technical Sales Representative → Senior Sales Representative/Team Lead → Branch Manager/Sales Director; building technical expertise and customer relationships is key to promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple Quotation Preparation
- Repetitive Customer Data Entry
- Basic Market Research

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer Data Analysis
- Customized Solution Proposal
- Sales Strategy Development

**Human moat:**

- Customer Trust Relationship
- In-depth Understanding of Technical Products
- Complex Negotiation Skills

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- Basic Understanding of AI/ML
- Presentation
- Project Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Semiconductor
- AI Solution
- Eco-friendly Energy
- Bio

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent technical knowledge and communication skills
- Goal-oriented and self-driven individuals
- People skilled at identifying customer needs

**Who is not suited**
- People who avoid interpersonal relationships
- People who prefer static work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a technical sales representative?**
A: Entry-level annual salary 30-45 million KRW, up to 120 million KRW with experience, with performance incentives as a major variable.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job as a technical sales representative?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but Korean language skills and technical background are required, along with an employer's recommendation.

**Q: What is the outlook for technical sales representatives?**
A: Demand is increasing in high-value-added industries, but some job changes due to AI are expected.
