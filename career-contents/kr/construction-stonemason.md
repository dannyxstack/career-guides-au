# Construction Stonemason — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70151 (KECO)**

A construction stonemason is a skilled professional who cuts and shapes stone for exterior walls, floors, and landscaping of buildings. They work across traditional stone masonry to modern construction.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cultural Heritage Repair Technician (Stonemason) | Cultural Heritage Administration | Required | Mandatory for heritage restoration sites, experienced candidates preferred |
| Stonemason Technician (Seokgong) | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | National technical qualification, not mandatory but advantageous for employment |
| Construction Safety Engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Strengthens site safety management skills |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression: skilled worker → advanced stonemason → site manager → self-employed (stone masonry specialist). Obtaining a Cultural Heritage Repair Technician certification enhances expertise.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent manual dexterity and meticulousness
- People who are not afraid of outdoor/physical labor
- People with strong interest in traditional techniques and modern architecture

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer indoor/stable environment
- People who want office work over precision manual work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a stone mason?**
A: Entry level 24-30 million KRW, mid-level 35-45 million KRW, senior 50-60 million KRW, with high proportion of daily workers causing income fluctuations.

**Q: Can foreigners work as stone masons?**
A: Yes. They can work with an E-7 visa (Skilled Worker) or E-9 visa (partial), and having a stone mason certificate is advantageous.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a stone mason?**
A: Stone Mason Certificate (not mandatory), Cultural Heritage Repair Technician (required for heritage sites), etc.
