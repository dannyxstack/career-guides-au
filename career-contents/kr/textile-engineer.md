## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Promotion typically follows the path of Junior Researcher, Assistant Researcher, Senior Researcher, and Principal Researcher/Team Lead, with a career centered on R&D. Opportunities exist in large corporations and research institutes, as well as SMEs.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Collecting and analyzing standard test data
- Simple property measurement and reporting
- Basic literature review

**Where AI augments the role:**

- New material design and optimization
- Process anomaly detection and prediction
- Development of customer-customized materials

**Human moat:**

- Creative problem-solving skills
- Intuition about material-process-property relationships
- Decision-making based on field experience
- Human-centered design thinking

**Skills to build:**

- Basics of AI/Machine Learning
- Data Analysis (Python, R)

- Professional Technical English for Friends

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary of a textile engineer?**
A: For large companies, entry-level (0-3 years) annual salary is around 35-45 million KRW, while SMEs start in the early 30 million KRW range.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a textile engineer in Korea?**
A: Employment is possible for R&D roles under the E-7 visa (Specific Activities). The employer must apply, and a related bachelor's degree or higher is required.

**Q: How does promotion work?**
A: The typical path is: Junior Researcher → Assistant → Associate → Principal Researcher. In large companies and research institutes, promotion opportunities arise every 5-7 years.
