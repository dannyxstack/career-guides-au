## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a screenwriter?**
A: Entry-level salary is around 12 to 30 million KRW, and depending on experience and hit works, it can go up to 200 million KRW or more. Most are freelancers on contract.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa as a screenwriter?**
A: E-7 visa is possible, but since many are freelancers, you need an employer, and short-term projects are difficult. F-2 (Residence) or F-5 (Permanent Residence) are more realistic.
