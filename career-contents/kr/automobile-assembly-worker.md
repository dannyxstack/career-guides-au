# Automobile Assembly Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 81711 (KECO)**

A field worker who assembles various parts such as the engine, chassis, and interior of an automobile to produce a completed vehicle.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average starting salary for new hires?**
A: For tier-1 automakers, the starting salary for entry-level (0-3 years) is KRW 32-38 million, which can be higher with bonuses and overtime pay.

**Q: Can foreigners work as car assemblers?**
A: Possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment Visa, Manufacturing Quota) or E-7-3 (Skilled Worker) visa; F-2 (Residence) points-based permanent residency route is also available.

**Q: What certifications are needed for promotion?**
A: Automobile assembly technician is not mandatory but preferred; welding technician and industrial safety engineer help with promotion.
