# Air Transportation Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02823 (KECO)**

A clerical professional who manages passenger/cargo transport schedules at airlines and handles reservations, ticketing, check-in, cargo acceptance, etc.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school graduate | 3 years | $0~$0 (Free as compulsory education) |
| Associate degree (Aviation Service/Office) | 2 years | $8,000,000~$20,000,000 (Including tuition and textbook fees) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air Transport Practical Clerk Level 1/2 | Korea Aviation Association | Optional | Preferred for employment, not mandatory |
| Test of Aviation English (TAE) | Korea Aviation Association | Optional | Required or preferred for major airlines |

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 12,000. Employment is stable due to recovery of international/domestic passengers and increase in cargo volume, but growth is limited due to automation and expansion of self-check-in.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Typically start as airline ground staff clerk, gain experience, then advance to specialized departments such as reservations/ticketing/scheduling, or move to airport operations/cargo management positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Booking and ticketing
- Baggage tagging
- Boarding pass issuance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Flight schedule optimization
- Demand-based fare management
- Customer complaint trend analysis

**Human moat:**

- Unstructured customer service
- Emergency situation judgment
- Multinational communication

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Understanding of aviation regulations
- Project management

**Key growth areas:**

- Recovery of air passenger traffic
- Expansion of self-check-in
- Growth in air logistics
- Non-face-to-face services

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented and fast at work
- Proficient in multilingual communication
- Highly interested in the aviation industry

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes face-to-face service
- Finds it difficult to adapt to shift work

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of an air transport clerk?**
A: Starting salary is about 25–32 million KRW, and can rise to 45–60 million KRW with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as air transport clerks?**
A: Theoretically possible with an E-7 visa, but practically difficult due to language barriers and preference for locals.

**Q: What is the promotion path?**
A: After joining, through ticketing and boarding procedures, can advance to team leader or department head, or move to headquarters scheduling.
