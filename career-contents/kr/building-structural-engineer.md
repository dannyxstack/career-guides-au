# Building Structural Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 14021 (KECO)**

A specialist who designs and reviews the structural safety and durability of buildings, responsible for structural calculations and drawings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level supports structural calculations; at 5–10 years, becomes a sectional lead; after 15+ years, with a Professional Engineer license, can advance to design firm representative or public institution supervisor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive structural calculations
- Standard drawing creation
- Preliminary compliance review

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimal structural design alternative generation
- Seismic simulation
- Material/cost analysis

**Human moat:**

- Final safety judgment
- Innovative design of atypical buildings
- Legal/regulatory interpretation and client consultation

**Skills to build:**

- BIM/structural analysis software
- AI/machine learning-based design tools
- Seismic and zero-energy design knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Seismic design
- Zero-energy building
- BIM integrated design
- Reinforcement of aging buildings

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a building structural engineer?**
A: Depending on experience, junior level 32-40 million KRW, mid-level 42-55 million KRW, senior level 55-80 million KRW. It increases significantly upon obtaining a professional engineer license.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job in Korea in this occupation?**
A: Yes, you can apply for an E-7-1 visa, but must meet requirements such as a related degree and 5+ years of experience. Korean language ability and employer recommendation are important.

**Q: What is the minimum education required for this job?**
A: A 2-year college or 4-year university degree is typical, with majors in architecture/civil engineering preferred. Practical experience is essential for obtaining a professional engineer license.
