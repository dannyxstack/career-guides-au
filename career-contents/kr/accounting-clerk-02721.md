# Accounting Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02721 (KECO)**

A job that handles financial affairs such as corporate fund income/expenditure, payroll calculation, and tax reporting. The core is accurate work using accounting programs and Excel.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an accounting clerk?**
A: Entry-level: 24-30 million KRW, mid-level: 32-42 million KRW, senior: 44-60 million KRW, increasing with experience and certifications.

**Q: Can foreigners work as accounting clerks?**
A: Employment is possible with an E-7 visa, but degrees and experience are needed to be recognized as accounting/finance professionals. F-2 or F-4 visa holders can work freely.

**Q: How can an accounting clerk advance to a higher position?**
A: Obtaining Class 1 computerized accounting and computerized tax certifications, and becoming proficient in ERP systems, can lead to promotion to accounting team leader or finance specialist.
