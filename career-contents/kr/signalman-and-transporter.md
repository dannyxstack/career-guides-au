# Signalman and Transporter — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62191 (KECO)**

This occupation guides the movement of heavy equipment and vehicles at construction or industrial sites, transmits signals to prevent safety accidents, and transports various materials and equipment.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 65,000. While jobs are affected by fluctuations in the construction industry, demand is expected to remain steady due to strengthened safety management at large construction sites. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

The impact of AI and automation is limited. Signal work requires field judgment and communication, making full replacement difficult, but remote monitoring using drones or cameras could be introduced as a supplement. AI will contribute to enhancing safety through risk detection alerts.

Initially assigned to assist with signals or simple transport, one can advance to heavy equipment signaling or site manager roles with experience. Obtaining safety certifications is advantageous for promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple Repetitive Signal Transmission
- Manual Equipment Handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based Risk Detection System Operation
- Assisting Field Situation Awareness Using Drones

**Human moat:**

- Field Situation Judgment Skills
- Non-verbal Communication with Workers
- Handling Unexpected Emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Heavy Equipment Operation License
- AI-based Safety Equipment Operation Skills
- Basic English (Communication with Foreign Workers)

**Key growth areas:**

- Safety Reinforcement
- Aging Workforce Replacement
- Construction Site
- Heavy Equipment Operation

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are physically fit and prefer outdoor work
- Thorough safety awareness and meticulous personality
- People with excellent hand-eye coordination

**Who is not suited**
- People sensitive to high-altitude work or dust
- People who find repetitive signaling boring

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a signalman?**
A: It varies from 24 million won to 50 million won depending on experience, and since many are daily workers, it fluctuates greatly depending on the number of working days.

**Q: Can foreigners work as signalmen?**
A: Partially possible with an E-9 visa, but mostly centered on locals; an E-7 visa can be issued to skilled workers.

**Q: How can I get promoted from a signalman?**
A: By obtaining certifications such as Construction Safety Engineer and accumulating field experience, one can be promoted to safety manager or site manager.
