## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of loading and unloading workers?**
A: Starting salary is around 24 million KRW, reaching up to 48 million KRW depending on experience and qualifications. Including night and special work allowances, it can be higher.

**Q: Can foreigners work as loading and unloading workers?**
A: Partially possible with an E-9 visa, but mainly requires experience in logistics centers or ports. E-7 visa is issued to skilled workers, and overseas Koreans with H-2 visa can also work.

**Q: How can one get promoted in this occupation?**
A: Obtaining relevant licenses such as forklift or crane and gaining experience can lead to promotion to team leader or logistics manager.
