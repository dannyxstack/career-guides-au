## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Usually starts as a salesperson or marketing assistant, can be promoted to advertising planning and sales team leader, and can also transition to a digital marketing specialist.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Writing advertising performance reports
- Basic email marketing
- Basic data collection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Setting ad targeting
- Customer segmentation
- Performance prediction

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face sales skills
- Creative ad planning
- Building customer relationships

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- SQL
- Digital advertising platforms (Google Ads, Meta)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital marketing
- Online advertising
- Data-driven sales
- SNS advertising

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Sociable and persuasive individuals
- Goal-oriented and strong with numbers

**Who is not suited**
- Introverted and prefer working alone
- Prefer repetitive and routine tasks

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a product advertising salesperson?**
A: Entry-level: approx. 28~35 million KRW, mid-level: 38~50 million KRW, senior: 50~70 million KRW.

**Q: Can I get a Korean work visa for this occupation?**
A: E-7 visa possible, but sales roles make it hard to prove expertise, so approval rate low. F-2 points system also disadvantageous.
