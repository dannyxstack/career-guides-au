# Traditional Architectural Technician — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70162 (KECO)**

Technician responsible for new construction, repair, and restoration of traditional buildings such as hanok, using traditional techniques including woodworking, plastering, roofing, and decorative painting.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school graduate | 3 years | $0~$5,000,000 (Free general high school education; some vocational schools require partial tuition) |
| Traditional Architecture Department at a Junior College | 2 years | $12,000,000~$20,000,000 (Tuition and practice fees) |
| Department of Traditional Architecture at Korea National University of Cultural Heritage | 4 years | $24,000,000~$40,000,000 (Tuition and practice fees) |
| Apprenticeship training | 3-5 years | $0~$5,000,000 (Low or no pay during apprenticeship period) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Promotion to master craftsman (carpenter, joiner) or independent contracting after obtaining cultural property repair technician certification is common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple drafting
- Material quantity estimation
- Basic structural calculations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Precision restoration design
- Structural safety simulation
- Cultural property documentation

**Human moat:**

- Handicraft skills
- On-site experience and know-how
- Understanding of traditional techniques

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced Traditional Building Techniques
- Utilizing AI Design Tools
- Obtaining Cultural Heritage Repair Certification

**Key growth areas:**

- New construction of Hanok
- Cultural property restoration
- Traditional architecture experience
- Eco-friendly construction

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent manual dexterity
- People interested in traditional culture
- People who are physically fit and can endure outdoor work

**Who is not suited**
- Digital technology enthusiasts
- People seeking high income in a short period

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: For entry-level (0-3 years), the annual pre-tax salary is approximately 24-30 million won, and may be lower during the probation period.

**Q: Can foreigners work as traditional construction craftsmen?**
A: It is possible to invite professional workers with an E-7 visa, but it is realistically difficult due to the need for cultural property repair qualifications and Korean language skills.
