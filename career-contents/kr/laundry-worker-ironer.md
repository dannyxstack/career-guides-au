# Laundry Worker (Ironer) — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 56151 (KECO)**

A job that washes, dries, and irons customers' clothes and fabrics neatly, working in hotels, laundries, hospitals, etc.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a laundry worker?**
A: Based on 2024 minimum wage, annual salary is about 22 million KRW, up to 36 million KRW with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners get a visa as a laundry worker?**
A: Possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa but industry restrictions apply; E-7 is rarely issued for skilled recognition. F-4 (Overseas Korean) visa holders can work freely.

**Q: Is a laundry technician certificate required?**
A: It's not mandatory but advantageous for employment, and preferred by large hotels or professional laundries.
