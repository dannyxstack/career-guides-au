# Asset Manager — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 03121 (KECO)**

A professional who invests and manages diverse assets such as stocks, bonds, and real estate to generate returns, working at banks, securities firms, asset management companies, etc.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Securities Investment Advisory Personnel | Korea Financial Investment Association | Required | Required for fund manager registration |
| Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) | CFA Institute | Optional | International certification, not mandatory but highly beneficial for employment |
| Korean Certified Public Accountant (KICPA) | Financial Supervisory Service | Optional | Useful for accounting and financial analysis |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Typically, after graduating from university, one joins a securities firm or asset management company, gains experience as an analyst or trader, and is promoted to fund manager. Professional certifications like CFA are advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Quantitative data collection and organization
- Basic financial analysis report writing
- General market trend summaries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Portfolio rebalancing
- Risk management
- Investment idea generation

**Human moat:**

- Unique investment philosophy and intuition
- Insight into macroeconomics
- Trust-based client relationships
- Regulatory and market sentiment analysis

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis (Python/R)
- Basics of AI/machine learning
- ESG analysis
- Behavioral economics

**Key growth areas:**

- Pension fund management
- ESG investing
- Hedge fund
- Robo-advisor

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone skilled in numbers and data analysis with a long-term investment vision
- Someone responsible who can take risks but make systematic decisions
- Someone who continuously learns and is interested in macroeconomics and industry trends

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who clings to short-term profits and lacks patience
- Someone who finds it difficult to endure stress and uncertainty

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an asset manager?**
A: Entry-level: about 40-60 million won, mid-level: 60-120 million won, senior: over 300 million won including performance bonuses.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an asset manager?**
A: An E-7 professional visa is required, along with a relevant degree, experience, and an employer sponsor.

**Q: Is it mandatory to obtain the CFA to become an asset manager?**
A: Not mandatory, but it is advantageous for employment and promotion, and preferred by large asset management firms.
