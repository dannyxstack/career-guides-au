# Web Designer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41551 (KECO)**

A professional who designs the visual aspects and user experience of websites and mobile apps, handling UI/UX design.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Starting as a junior designer, career can progress to senior designer, UI/UX director, or freelancer.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Banner Image Production
- Basic Template Design
- Color and Font Combinations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- User Flow Design
- A/B Test Design
- Design System Building

**Human moat:**

- User Empathy
- Brand Strategy Understanding
- Creative Problem Solving

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced Figma Usage
- User Research
- HTML/CSS Basics
- Data-Driven Design

**Key growth areas:**

- UI/UX design
- Responsive web
- Web Accessibility
- Design System

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent visual sense
- People interested in user experience
- People who quickly learn the latest web trends

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike repetitive tasks
- People who prioritize originality over teamwork

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a web designer?**
A: Approximately 30 million won for less than 3 years, 45 million for 5-10 years, 60 million for over 10 years.

**Q: How can foreigners get a job as a web designer in Korea?**
A: An E-7 visa is required, along with a degree and experience.

**Q: What is the outlook for web designers?**
A: Demand exists due to the expansion of the digital environment, but competition has intensified due to AI tools, making expertise important.
