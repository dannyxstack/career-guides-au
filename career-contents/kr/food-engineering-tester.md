# Food Engineering Tester — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15721 (KECO)**

A food engineering tester is a professional who analyzes and evaluates food quality, safety, and nutritional content to support product development and quality management.

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior testers can advance to senior and managerial roles with experience, and further grow to quality control team leaders or research center directors.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive Sample Processing
- Simple Moisture/Ash Analysis
- Data Entry and Management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Data Interpretation and Reporting
- Anomaly Detection
- Statistical Analysis

**Human moat:**

- Sensory Evaluation
- Regulatory Interpretation
- Unstructured Problem Response

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis with Python
- Understanding HACCP and ISO 22000
- Smart Factory System Operation

**Key growth areas:**

- Food Safety
- Health Functional Foods
- HACCP
- Food Tech

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Meticulous and detail-oriented personality
- People who like scientific analysis
- People with high interest in food safety

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike repetitive tasks
- People who don't prefer indoor work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary range for a Food Engineering Tester?**
A: Entry level is 30-40 million won, up to 75 million won with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this job?**
A: Possible with E-7 visa, but requires professional qualifications and degree.

**Q: What is the promotion path like?**
A: Promotion follows: Junior Tester → Senior → Assistant Manager → Manager → Team Leader; large companies are more systematic.
