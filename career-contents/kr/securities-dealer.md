# Securities Dealer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 03141 (KECO)**

A financial professional who generates profits by trading stocks, bonds, derivatives, etc. in the securities market. Typically works at securities firms, asset management companies, banks, etc.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |

| Master's Degree | 2 years | $6,000,000~$12,000,000 (Majors such as Financial Engineering, MBA) |

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investment Asset Manager | Korea Financial Investment Association | Required | Mandatory qualification for financial investment industry |
| CFA | CFA Institute | Optional | Internationally recognized financial analyst certification, preferred |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 3,200. The Korean securities industry is changing the role of dealers due to digital transformation and AI adoption. While high-frequency trading and algorithmic trading are expanding, reducing traditional dealer demand, sophisticated strategic capabilities are still needed for hedge funds and professional investors.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Starting as a trader or analyst, one can advance to senior dealer, department head, or head dealer. At large securities firms, overseas IB experience is advantageous, and professional certifications like CFA aid promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Order execution and trade settlement
- Basic chart analysis
- Simple risk calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Market analysis and modeling
- Algorithmic trading strategy design
- Risk management scenario analysis

**Human moat:**

- Strategic insight and intuition
- Client relationships and trust-building
- Regulatory and legal understanding

**Skills to build:**

- Python
- Machine learning
- Quantitative modeling
- Big data analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- High-frequency trading
- Algorithmic trading
- Derivatives
- ESG Investing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those with high concentration and stress tolerance
- Those strong in math/statistics and quick decision-making
- Those with passion and curiosity for financial markets

**Who is not suited**
- Those with a laid-back personality who find it hard to adapt to change
- Those who prefer independent work over teamwork

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a securities dealer?**
A: Entry-level pre-tax annual salary of 45-70 million KRW, mid-level 70 million-150 million KRW, senior over 150 million KRW, with performance bonuses making up a large portion.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a securities dealer in Korea?**
A: E-7 is common, requiring a securities company sponsor. Can be converted to F-2 or F-5.
