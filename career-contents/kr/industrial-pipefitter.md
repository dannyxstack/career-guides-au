# Industrial Pipefitter — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70321 (KECO)**

A skilled technical professional who installs, maintains, and repairs various piping systems in industrial facilities and factories.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Vocational high school or Meister high school, mechanical/facility department | 3 years | $0~$5,000,000 (Free tuition at national/public high schools, partial burden for private schools) |
| Industrial Pipefitter Technician certification + vocational training | 1 year | $1,000,000~$3,000,000 (Government subsidy possible, small personal contribution) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piping Technician | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Required | Basic national technical qualification, essential for practice |
| Piping Equipment Engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Advantageous for promotion and management positions |
| Welding Technician (TIG/MIG) | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Directly used for pipe welding, high wages |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Employment is stable due to the renovation of aging industrial complexes and new plant demand, and recruitment is steady due to a shortage of skilled workers.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

With 5-10 years of field experience, promotion to piping supervisor or site manager is possible, and obtaining welding/machinery certifications enables growth as a technical expert.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple drawing reading and manual drafting
- General pipe cutting and cutting (replaced by automatic cutters)
- Simple pipe connection work (replaced by modular methods)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex piping system design review (AI suggests optimal routes)
- Safety checks and anomaly detection (sensors + AI analysis for early warnings)
- Project schedule and material management (using AI prediction tools)

**Human moat:**

- Flexible pipe installation and repair according to site conditions
- High-difficulty welding and non-standard pipe work
- Diagnosis and customized renovation of aged pipes in existing buildings

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Revit, etc.) piping modeling
- Welding automation equipment operation
- Safety management and industrial safety and health certification
- Smart piping diagnostic technology

**Key growth areas:**

- Plant
- Semiconductor/battery factory
- Aged-pipe replacement
- Smart piping

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are physically fit and comfortable with outdoor/height work
- People with excellent manual skills and problem-solving abilities
- People who can accept long-term on-site work and travel

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer office work over precise manual tasks
- People who are passive about safety regulations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary for an industrial pipefitter?**
A: Entry-level: approximately 25–35 million KRW pre-tax; with over 10 years of experience, up to 55–80 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as industrial pipefitters in Korea?**
A: Yes, you can work on either an E-9 visa (Non-professional Employment) or an E-7 visa (Specific Activities), and depending on skill level, permanent residency may also be considered.

**Q: What are the promotion paths?**
A: After obtaining a skilled worker certificate and accumulating 5 to 10 years of field experience, you can be promoted to foreman or site manager, or become a high-wage technician by acquiring additional welding certifications.
