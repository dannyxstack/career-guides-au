## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a Western cuisine chef?**
A: For entry-level, annual salary is 24-30 million KRW, varying by hotel or franchise.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a Western cuisine chef with an E-7 visa?**
A: Yes, it is possible. More than 1 year of experience in the field and a cooking license are required, and a contract is mandatory.

**Q: How long does it take to become a head chef from a Western cuisine chef?**
A: Usually, more than 8-10 years of experience are needed, along with intermediate-level skills and leadership.
