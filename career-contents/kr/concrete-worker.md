# Concrete Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70141 (KECO)**

A concrete worker is a skilled occupation responsible for concrete placement, curing, finishing, and other concrete structure construction tasks at construction sites.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 180,000. Although affected by fluctuations in the construction industry, steady employment is expected due to long-term plans from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport and demand for renovation of aging infrastructure. An aging workforce may worsen the shortage of new workers.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Promotion path: entry-level → intermediate → senior (foreman) → site manager; starting a specialized concrete company is also an option.

- Simple concrete pouring
- Repetitive finishing work
- Basic curing management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quality inspection and repair

**Human moat:**

- Skilled hand techniques
- Safety management sense

**Skills to build:**

- 3D printing concrete technology
- Smart Construction Equipment Operation
- Understanding Construction BIM

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban redevelopment and reconstruction

- New city construction

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with good physical strength and able to handle outdoor work
- Those with precise and meticulous personality
- Those who strictly follow safety rules

**Who is not suited**
- People with acrophobia or weak physical strength
- Those who find teamwork difficult rather than working alone

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a concrete worker?**
A: About 24-30 million KRW for beginners, and 48-60 million KRW for senior skilled workers with over 8 years of experience.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job as a concrete worker?**
A: Yes, with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, and you can switch to E-7 if your skills are recognized. Overseas Koreans can work with an F-4 visa.

**Q: Are there any required certifications?**
A: Not mandatory, but having a Concrete Technician certificate helps with employment and salary negotiation.
