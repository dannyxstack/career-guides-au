# Herbal Pharmacist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30302 (KECO)**

A herbal pharmacist is a professional who prepares herbal medicines based on Korean traditional medicine theory and provides drug therapy to patients in oriental medical institutions.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an herbal pharmacist?**
A: Starting salary is around 35-45 million won, and with over 8 years of experience, it can rise to 60-90 million won. It varies by workplace (oriental hospital, pharmacy, etc.) and region.

**Q: Can foreigners work as herbal pharmacists in Korea?**
A: It is possible, but requires obtaining a Korean herbal pharmacist license issued by the Ministry of Health and Welfare, and employment is possible through an E-7 visa. Korean language ability and academic requirements are stringent.

**Q: What is the promotion path for a herbal pharmacist?**
A: Initially, one works as a pharmacist at a Korean medicine hospital or pharmacy, and can be promoted to responsible pharmacist, pharmacy manager, or administrator with experience. It is also possible to advance through graduate school or research positions.
