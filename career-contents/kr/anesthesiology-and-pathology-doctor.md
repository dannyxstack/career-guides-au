# Anesthesiology and Pathology Doctor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 301110 (KECO)**

As a board-certified anesthesiologist, manages pre- and post-operative anesthesia and pain treatment; as a pathologist, performs diagnosis of tissue samples.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI is used to assist in monitoring during anesthesia and pathology diagnosis, but it is difficult to completely replace the doctor's judgment and skills, so the risk of job loss due to AI is low; rather, work efficiency improves.

Intern → Residency (choose Anesthesiology & Pain Medicine or Pathology) → Obtain board certification → Hospital employment or private practice. Promotion to professor or department head.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Quantitative pathology reading (partially automated)
- Basic vital sign monitoring during anesthesia

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimization of anesthetic drug dosage
- Improved speed of pathology image analysis

**Human moat:**

- Emergency judgment during surgery
- Patient communication and trust building

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI-based diagnostic tools
- Data analysis and interpretation

**Key growth areas:**

- Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine
- Increased surgeries
- Aging population
- Demand for specialists

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of an anesthesiologist or pathologist?**
A: Starting salary for board-certified specialists ranges from 80 million KRW to 120 million KRW, and with over 10 years of experience, it can reach 200 to 400 million KRW.

**Q: What type of visa does a foreign doctor need to work in Korea?**
A: The E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is possible, but there are many restrictions such as recognition of the medical license, making it realistically difficult. F-2 (Residence) or F-5 (Permanent Residence) visas are more advantageous.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an anesthesiology and pathology specialist?**
A: A medical license and board certification in anesthesiology and pain medicine or pathology are mandatory. The training period totals 11 years (6 years of medical school + 1-year internship + 4-year residency).
