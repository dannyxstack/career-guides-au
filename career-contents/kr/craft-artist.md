# Craft Artist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 88411 (KECO)**

A professional who creates artistic or practical crafts using various materials such as ceramics, woodworking, metal, and textiles based on hand skills and creativity.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Craft Technician (Craft Skill Worker) | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | National technical qualification, available by field such as ceramics and metalworking |
| Master Artisan of Korean Crafts | Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism | Optional | Certification for highest skill level; experience required |
| Cultural Product Technology Engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Advanced qualification; evaluates design and production skills |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a craftsperson?**
A: For entry-level, it is about 24-30 million won, and it varies greatly depending on career and art sales performance. For self-employed individuals, income fluctuates significantly.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work as a craftsperson in Korea?**
A: If invited as a professional craftsperson, an E-7 visa is possible, but employment contract and qualification requirements must be met. Overseas Koreans can freely work with an F-4 visa.

**Q: How can I develop my career as a craftsperson?**
A: You can diversify your career paths by starting a personal studio, participating in exhibitions, teaching craft courses, or developing cultural products. Obtaining a national certification is helpful.
