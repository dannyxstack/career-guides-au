# IT Tester and QA Specialist (SW Tester) — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 13391 (KECO)**

A role that establishes test plans to ensure software quality, performs manual and automated tests to find and report defects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Starting as a manual tester, one can advance to automation script writing, test architect, and QA manager. Certifications such as ISTQB help career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive manual testing
- Simple regression testing
- Basic defect report writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based test generation
- Automated defect classification
- Performance test optimization
- Test coverage analysis

**Human moat:**

- Business domain understanding
- Exploratory testing ability
- User experience evaluation
- Complex defect cause analysis

**Skills to build:**

- Test automation frameworks (Selenium, Appium)
- CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitLab)
- AI/ML basics
- Security testing (OWASP)

**Key growth areas:**

- SW Testing
- QA Engineer
- Test Automation
- AI Testing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for an IT tester?**
A: Entry-level annual salary ranges from KRW 28 million to 35 million; large companies pay more than small ones, and varies by automation skills.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: It is possible. An E-7 visa is issued if you meet the bachelor's degree and experience requirements, and the IT/QA field has labor demand, so approval is likely.

**Q: How can I transition to a PM role?**
A: After gaining testing experience, you can transition to a PM role by developing project management skills (scheduling, risk) and obtaining certifications such as PMP.
