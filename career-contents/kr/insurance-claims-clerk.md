# Insurance Claims Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 03232 (KECO)**

This is a specialized clerical position in an insurance company that handles loss assessment and compensation tasks, from accident reporting to payment of claims.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression: junior clerk → claims team leader → claims department manager; holding a loss adjuster license is advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- standard compensation calculation
- document review and input
- simple claim intake and processing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- accident investigation and damage assessment
- insurance fraud analysis
- customer consultation and negotiation

**Human moat:**

- human judgment
- on-site accident investigation
- legal interpretation
- customer emotion management

**Skills to build:**

- claims adjusting expertise
- insurance regulation understanding
- data analysis
- communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- claims adjusting
- auto insurance
- AI claim assessment
- insurance fraud investigation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for insurance claims clerks?**
A: For entry-level (0-3 years), the annual pre-tax salary is about 26 million to 32 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job as an insurance claims clerk?**
A: Generally, an E-7 visa is difficult to obtain. It is possible if you have a professional claims adjuster qualification, but this is mostly a local occupation.

**Q: What are the prospects for this job?**
A: Demand remains due to insurance market growth, but simple processing jobs may decrease with the introduction of AI auto-assessment.
