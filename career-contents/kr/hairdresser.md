# Hairdresser — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 51121 (KECO)**

A professional who manages customers' hair and skin, providing beauty services such as cutting, dyeing, perming, and styling.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a beautician?**
A: Entry-level is about 22-30 million won, while experienced can reach 45-70 million won, but it varies greatly depending on individual skills and clientele.

**Q: Can foreigners work as beauticians in Korea?**
A: General employment visa (E-7) issuance is difficult, making it a limited immigration route. However, holders of F-2 and F-4 visas are eligible.

**Q: How long does it take to get a beautician license?**
A: National certification can be obtained through a 2-year college program or a 6-month academy course, with passing the practical exam being crucial.
