# Rubber and Plastic Product Assembler — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 85241 (KECO)**

A production job that processes and assembles rubber or plastic parts to create finished products, mainly producing automotive parts, household goods, and industrial machinery components.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 45,000. Employment is declining due to automation and overseas relocation in Korean manufacturing, but the rubber and plastic processing sector is expected to remain stable due to demand for automotive and electronic parts. Projected annual decline of 1-2% from 2023 to 2033.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Promotion path: assembler → skilled worker → shift leader → process manager. Obtaining a certification (e.g., Rubber and Plastic Industrial Engineer) is advantageous for transitioning to technical roles. Most gain experience at small and medium-sized enterprises.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple assembly work
- Visual inspection
- Packing/loading

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quality control data analysis
- Injection molding condition optimization
- Automation equipment monitoring

**Human moat:**

- Complex manual assembly
- Mold repair
- Abnormal situation response

**Skills to build:**

- Injection molding technology
- Mold repair technology
- PLC programming
- Robot maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Rubber and plastic parts
- Automotive parts assembly
- Quality inspection
- Injection molding

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary?**
A: Approximately 24 million to 30 million KRW for entry-level, up to 45 million KRW with experience and certifications.

**Q: Can foreigners get employed?**
A: Possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa but limited by Employment Permit System quota; conversion to Skilled Worker (E-7-4) also possible.
