# Dental Technician — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30631 (KECO)**

A dental technician is a healthcare professional who manufactures dental prosthetics, orthodontic appliances, and implants according to a dentist's prescription, restoring the patient's oral function and aesthetics.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 35,000. While the introduction of digital technology may reduce simple tasks, demand for skilled technicians remains strong.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Most dental technicians work in dental laboratories. After 5–10 years of experience, they can advance to head technician or open their own laboratory. Specializing in prosthetic areas (crowns, implants, orthodontics, etc.) is advantageous for career development.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple model making
- Basic wax-up
- Investing and casting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- CAD design
- 3D printing production
- Shade matching and esthetic evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Precise manual skills
- Esthetic sense and artistic judgment
- Custom adjustment based on patient oral conditions

**Skills to build:**

- Digital dental laboratory (CAD/CAM) software
- 3D printing operation
- Implant dental laboratory technology

**Key growth areas:**

- Implants
- Digital dental technology (CAD/CAM)
- Orthodontic devices
- Demand related to aging

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of a dental technician?**
A: Approximately 25-35 million KRW for beginners, and 50-70 million KRW for those with over 10 years of experience. Income can increase significantly when opening a practice.

**Q: Can foreigners work as dental technicians in Korea?**
A: Yes, it's possible. Through the E-7 visa (Specific Activities), skilled dental technicians can work, requiring Korean language skills and relevant certifications.

**Q: What education is needed to become a dental technician?**
A: You need to graduate from a junior college's dental technology department (3 years) or a 4-year university's dental technology department and pass the national licensing exam.
