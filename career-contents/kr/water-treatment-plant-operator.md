# Water Treatment Plant Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 85311 (KECO)**

This is a specialized job that involves operating and managing water and wastewater treatment facilities to supply safe water and purify wastewater.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 17,000. Due to climate change and increasing demand for advanced water treatment, demand for specialized personnel continues. However, it is mainly in the public sector and automation is expected to have an impact.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: For public institutions, the annual salary is about 25 to 32 million KRW, increasing with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work here?**
A: Possible with E-7 visa but experience requirements are strict, and E-9 is limited.

**Q: What is the required education level?**
A: High school graduates or above are eligible, but an associate degree or higher in environmental studies is advantageous for employment.
