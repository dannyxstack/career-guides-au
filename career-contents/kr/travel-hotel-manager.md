# Travel/Hotel Manager — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 01413 (KECO)**

This is a management role that oversees overall operations at hotels, resorts, travel agencies, etc., managing service quality and profitability.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Promotion typically progresses from on-site hotel/travel agency experience to department head, then branch manager/general manager; in large chains, advancement to regional head is possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Reservation booking
- Simple customer inquiry response
- Inventory and order management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Demand forecasting
- Rate optimization
- Personalized marketing
- Customer satisfaction analysis

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal skills
- Crisis management skills
- Team management and motivation
- Emotional customer care

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Digital Marketing
- Foreign Languages
- CRM System Utilization

**Key growth areas:**

- MICE industry
- Digital transformation
- Premium and luxury upgrade
- Regional tourism revitalization

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person with outstanding leadership and communication skills
- Person with service mindset and problem-solving skills
- Person skilled in stress management

**Who is not suited**
- Person who is sensitive and has difficulty adapting to change
- Person who avoids night/weekend work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a travel/hotel manager?**
A: Entry-level: about 32–40 million KRW, intermediate: 45–60 million KRW, senior: 65–90 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get this job in Korea?**
A: Yes, hiring for professional careers is possible with an E-7 visa, but Korean language skills and relevant experience are essential.

**Q: What is the minimum education required?**
A: An associate degree or higher is common, with a major in hotel/tourism being advantageous.
