# Weaving and Knitting Machine Operators — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 86121 (KECO)**

Production workers who set up, operate, monitor weaving or knitting machines to produce textile fabrics and manage quality.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

AI and automation technology are rapidly replacing the work of loom and knitting machine operators. With the introduction of smart factories, AI handles monitoring and simple operations, while manpower is expected to be reduced to maintenance and quality control.

Promotion from skilled worker to foreman or field supervisor possible, but technological advances require transition to high-skilled maintenance roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- simple repetitive loom monitoring
- visual inspection of fabric defects
- basic specification input

**Where AI augments the role:**

- analysis of abnormal signs in automation lines
- quality data-based process optimization
- AI-assisted predictive maintenance

**Human moat:**

- diagnosis and repair of complex machinery
- flexible response to high-mix low-volume production
- field experience-based process improvement

**Skills to build:**

- PLC control
- robot maintenance
- data analysis
- automation equipment operation

**Key growth areas:**

- automation
- workforce reduction
- aging population
- smart textiles

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: For beginners (0~3 years), annual pre-tax salary is about 28~34 million KRW, including night shift and overtime allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this job?**
A: Possible at some manufacturers with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa but limited, and transition to E-7 (Skilled Worker) requires long service and skill proof.

**Q: Are there promotion opportunities?**
A: Possible from skilled worker to team leader/site supervisor, but recent automation makes maintenance/management conversion advantageous.
