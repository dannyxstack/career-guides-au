# Building Construction Supervisor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 14025 (KECO)**

A professional who supervises and inspects whether building construction is carried out in accordance with design drawings and specifications, overseeing quality, safety, and regulatory compliance.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building Construction Supervisor | Korean Institute of Registered Architects | Required | From architectural intern to supervisor level |
| Completion of practical training in building supervision | Korea Infrastructure Safety Corporation, etc. | Required | Mandatory training |
| Architectural engineer, Civil engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Additional points, not mandatory |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Progression from junior supervisor → intermediate (responsible supervisor) → senior (supervisor/PM), advancing to construction companies, supervision service firms, public institutions, etc. Experience and qualifications are key to promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic review of design drawings
- Automatic detection of code violations
- Schedule preparation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Remote on-site inspection using drones and CCTV
- AI-based defect prediction
- 3D scanning and BIM integration

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment
- Legal responsibility
- Stakeholder coordination skills

**Skills to build:**

- BIM
- Drone Operation
- Data Analysis
- Smart Construction S/W

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart construction
- Enhanced safety management
- Remodeling
- Reconstruction

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a building inspection engineer?**
A: For beginners with under 3 years of experience, about 30-40 million KRW; for mid-level with 4-9 years, 40-60 million KRW; for senior with 10+ years, 60-90 million KRW.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a building inspection engineer?**
A: The E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is suitable, specifically for the construction industry (E-7-1). Certain experience and Korean language proficiency are required.

**Q: What is the career progression for this job?**
A: Typically, promotion from inspection assistant → responsible inspector → supervisor (PM), with a licensed architect qualification or master's degree being advantageous.
