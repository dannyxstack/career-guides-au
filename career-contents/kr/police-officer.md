# Police Officer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 24011 (KECO)**

A police officer is a public official who protects citizens' lives and property, and prevents and investigates crimes to maintain social order.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Police Academy or Department of Police Administration | 4 years | $0~$0 (National university with free or low tuition) |
| Police Officer Candidate | 1 year | $0~$0 (Salary paid during training) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Police Officer Appointment Exam | National Police Agency | Required | Open competitive recruitment for police officers includes written, physical, and aptitude tests. |
| Police Officer Candidate Selection Exam | National Police Agency | Optional | College graduates or above eligible |
| Class 1 ordinary driver's license | National Police Agency | Optional | Required for practical work |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 140,000. Due to the sophistication of crime patterns and increased safety demands, police personnel are steadily needed, but new recruitment is stable due to quotas.

**Competition: very high (★★★★½).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person with a strong sense of public duty
- Person who remains calm in crisis situations
- Person who adapts to discipline and organizational life

**Who is not suited**
- Person who wants flexible working hours
- Person who values personal time

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting annual salary for a police officer?**
A: For constable level, approximately 36 million to 42 million KRW before tax, plus various allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners become Korean police officers?**
A: Under the Nationality Act, appointment as a public official in the Republic of Korea is restricted to Korean nationals, so foreigners are ineligible.

**Q: What is the competition rate for the police officer exam?**
A: Recently, the average competition rate for the constable recruitment exam is around 10:1.
