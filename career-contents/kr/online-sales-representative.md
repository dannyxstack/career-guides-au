# Online Sales Representative — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61531 (KECO)**

A job that involves planning and selling products through online channels such as internet shopping malls, social media, and live commerce, as well as handling customer service and marketing.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Starting as a junior sales representative, one can grow into a product planning/marketing specialist or develop into a shopping mall founder/manager. With experience, advancement to MD or online marketing manager is possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated customer inquiry responses
- Inventory management data entry
- Simple product image editing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized product recommendations
- Advertising efficiency analysis
- Real-time price fluctuation response

**Human moat:**

- Building trust with customers
- Trend sensitivity
- Negotiation and communication skills

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- SNS marketing
- Using AI tools
- Live Commerce Hosting

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce
- Live commerce
- Social media marketing
- Online MD

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent communication skills
- People sensitive to trends and active online
- People who can multitask

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer working quietly alone
- People who want regular working hours

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an online salesperson?**
A: For entry-level, it's about 24-30 million KRW, and depending on experience and company size, it can go up to 60 million KRW.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work as an online salesperson in Korea?**
A: E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is common, and you need to prove relevant degree or experience. Some simple tasks may allow E-9, but it's limited.

**Q: What skills should I develop to get promoted from an online salesperson?**
A: Product planning, data analysis, and marketing strategy skills are important, and relevant certifications (e.g., E-commerce Manager) help.
