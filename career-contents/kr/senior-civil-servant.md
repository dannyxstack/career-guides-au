# Senior Civil Servant — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 01112 (KECO)**

A high-ranking managerial position responsible for establishing and coordinating policies of ministries or local governments and overseeing organizations, working for the public interest of the entire nation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a high-ranking public official?**
A: The annual salary of high-ranking officials (Deputy Director-General to Director-General) is approximately 100-150 million KRW (pre-tax), varying by rank and step.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a high-ranking public official?**
A: Due to nationality restrictions in the Public Official Appointment Act, foreigners cannot become high-ranking officials in South Korea.

**Q: What is the typical path to becoming a senior civil servant?**
A: After passing the Civil Service Examination (Grade 5 open competition), you enter public service and get promoted for 15-20 years, and must be selected for the Senior Civil Service.
