# Parking Control/Guide — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 56231 (KECO)**

Controls vehicle access, collects parking fees, and guides vehicles to parking spaces in a parking lot.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a parking attendant?**
A: Approximately 24-28 million KRW for entry-level, up to 38 million KRW with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as parking attendants?**
A: Possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, but requires Employment Permit System quota; also possible with F-4 (Overseas Korean) visa.

**Q: Is promotion possible for parking attendants?**
A: Possible to advance to parking lot manager or facility management team leader, but limited in small-scale operations.
