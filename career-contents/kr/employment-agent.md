# Employment Agent — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02994 (KECO)**

A job that connects employers and job seekers, facilitates employment, and performs job counseling and follow-up management.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Typically starts at public employment service agencies or private employment agencies, with potential promotion to team leader, branch manager, etc., based on experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Online job matching
- Automatic resume sorting
- Basic counseling chatbot

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Developing customized job search strategies for job seekers
- Labor market trend analysis
- In-depth competency assessment

**Human moat:**

- Human emotion understanding and empathy
- Complex job negotiation
- Community network

**Skills to build:**

- Digital literacy
- Data analysis
- Coaching skills
- Labor law knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Employment services
- Digital platforms
- Counseling expertise
- Job placement

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who like others and are interested in counseling
- Detail-oriented with excellent communication skills
- Interested in labor market trends

**Who is not suited**
- Prefers working alone
- Prefers routine work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a job placement officer?**
A: Entry-level about 25-32 million KRW, mid-level 32-45 million KRW, senior 45-60 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as job placement officers?**
A: Possible with E-7 visa but rare; Korean proficiency and expertise required.

**Q: What certifications are needed?**
A: A level 2 vocational counselor certification is required, and a level 2 social research analyst is helpful.
