# Plant Mechanical Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15112 (KECO)**

A professional engineer who designs, installs, and maintains mechanical systems for large-scale industrial facilities such as power plants and chemical plants, with key focus on safety optimization considering high-temperature and high-pressure environments.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Due to the replacement of aging coal-fired power plants and new investments in SMR/hydrogen plants, some demand remains, but as of 2024, the plant construction downturn and stagnant new hiring are expected to lead to medium- to long-term employment stagnation or slight decline.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After building a career at a large EPC firm or design office, one can advance to a mid-sized plant company or overseas project PM, or specialize in mechanics, piping, rotating equipment, etc., and become a technical advisor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive piping stress calculations
- Standard drawing review and error detection
- Automatic regular equipment inspection schedule

**Where AI augments the role:**

- 3D modeling automatic optimization
- AI fault prediction (predictive maintenance) data analysis
- Safety regulation review and scenario simulation

**Human moat:**

- Emergency response and on-site safety judgment
- Coordination and communication with clients/partners
- Legal/standard interpretation and design change approval

**Skills to build:**

- Utilizing AI-based CAE (ANSYS, AVEVA)
- Data analysis (Python, R)
- Understanding digital twin and smart plant

**Key growth areas:**

- Hydrogen plant
- SMR (Small Modular Reactor)
- Battery factory equipment
- Carbon capture

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a plant mechanical engineer?**
A: Entry-level approx. KRW 35–48 million, mid-level 48–70 million, senior 70–100 million (excluding field allowances). Additional allowances for large companies and overseas projects.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work in this occupation in Korea?**
A: Professional E-7-1 visa is common; requires mechanical engineering degree and 5+ years of experience. Can convert to F-2 (points-based); not for skilled workers (E-9).

**Q: Are there required certifications?**
A: Not legally required, but Professional Mechanical Engineer or Mechanical Design Engineer helps with promotion/salary. General Mechanical Engineer also benefits treatment.
