# Advertising, Public Relations, and Marketing Specialist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02411 (KECO)**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

AI can replace some tasks like repetitive data analysis, ad targeting optimization, and content creation, but creative strategy and human-based PR still rely heavily on human roles.

Typically builds a career at marketing agencies, corporate marketing departments, or advertising firms; can advance to team leader or department head based on digital and data skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple ad performance report writing
- Basic target analysis
- Some content creation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customized ad message optimization
- Real-time campaign adjustment
- Social media trend analysis

**Human moat:**

- Creative strategy
- Human relationship networking
- Brand storytelling
- Understanding customer psychology

**Skills to build:**

- Digital marketing tool proficiency
- SQL/Python data analysis
- A/B test design
- AI prompt engineering

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Marketing
- Data Analysis
- Content Marketing
- Brand Strategy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an advertising, PR, and marketing specialist?**
A: The average is about 50 million KRW based on 5 years of experience, with large differences between large and small companies.

**Q: What visa can a foreigner use to work in this occupation in Korea?**
A: E-7 visa is most suitable, requiring a marketing-related degree and experience.

**Q: What competencies are important for promotion in this field?**
A: Digital marketing, data analysis, and project management skills are important, and an MBA degree can be helpful.
