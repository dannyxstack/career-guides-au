# Electrical Component and Product Production Machine Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 83511 (KECO)**

This occupation involves operating and managing machines that produce electrical components and products, maintaining quality and enhancing production efficiency. It is typically performed in electronic product manufacturing factories.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Patient with repetitive tasks
- Interested in machine operation with good manual dexterity
- Highly focused on assembly and production processes

**Who is not suited**
- Easily bored by simple repetitive tasks
- Dislikes strictly following safety rules

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for this occupation?**
A: Entry-level: about 24-32 million KRW, mid-level: 32-42 million KRW, senior: 42-55 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job in this occupation in Korea?**
A: Yes, through E-9 (Non-professional Employment) or E-7 (Specific Activities) visas, and via the Employment Permit System.

**Q: What is needed for promotion in this occupation?**
A: Obtaining certifications (e.g., electrician), improving productivity, and completing training are important; can be promoted to team leader or management.
