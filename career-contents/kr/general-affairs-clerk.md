## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a general affairs clerk?**
A: For entry level, about 26-32 million KRW; with over 10 years of experience, 47-60 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as general affairs clerks?**
A: General affairs has low expertise requirements, making E-7 visa issuance difficult; F-2 or higher is required.

**Q: What educational background is needed to become a general affairs clerk?**
A: While high school graduates are eligible, college graduates have an advantage, and office-related certifications are helpful.
