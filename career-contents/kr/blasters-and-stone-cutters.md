# Blasters and Stone Cutters — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70511 (KECO)**

This occupation involves extracting and cutting ore or stone in mines or quarries, and processing construction stone, primarily requiring physical labor and machine operation.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI and automation technology can replace some stone cutting and processing tasks, but tasks requiring on-site judgment and manual labor still need humans. Automation is expected to reduce simple tasks and increase high-value tasks like machine maintenance.

Starting as a beginner, one can obtain heavy equipment licenses such as excavators and cranes with experience and advance to a crew leader, but large-scale promotion paths are limited.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual stone cutting
- Simple transport tasks
- Basic mining processes

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Automated cutting machine operation
- Remote equipment monitoring
- Precision processing control

**Human moat:**

- On-site safety judgment
- Work in irregular environments
- Repair skills for machine breakdowns

**Skills to build:**

- Heavy equipment operation (excavators, etc.)
- Automated equipment operation
- Safety management
- Machine Maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Stone demand
- Infrastructure maintenance
- Automation adoption
- Aging workforce

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for stone cutting workers?**
A: For entry level, approximately 28 to 36 million KRW; with experience, up to 42 to 55 million KRW, though it varies by company size and region.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visas allow hiring in some mining and manufacturing companies, but mostly in manufacturing. Skilled workers may apply for E-7 (Specific Activities) visas, but the process is strict.

**Q: Are there mandatory certifications?**
A: No legally required certificates, but having a excavator operation license or blasting management certificate is advantageous for employment and wages.
