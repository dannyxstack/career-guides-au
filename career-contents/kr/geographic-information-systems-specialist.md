# Geographic Information Systems Specialist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 14062 (KECO)**

A professional who collects, analyzes, and processes spatial data to build, operate, and utilize Geographic Information Systems (GIS), performing spatial analysis, data visualization, and system development tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Begins as a GIS technician or analyst; can advance to project manager or senior researcher with experience; can enter public institutions, research institutes, IT companies, and diverse fields.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic classification of satellite imagery
- Simple editing of digital maps
- Basic spatial query processing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Spatial data quality verification
- Advanced spatial analysis modeling
- 3D visualization and digital twin implementation

**Human moat:**

- Field survey and measurement experience
- Complex location and policy analysis
- Stakeholder consultation and decision support

**Skills to build:**

- Use of Python and GIS libraries
- Spatial databases (PostGIS, etc.)
- AI and deep learning-based image analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart City
- Digital Twin
- Spatial Big Data
- Drone Mapping

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People interested in spatial data and maps
- People who enjoy logical analysis and problem-solving
- People who can combine computer skills with fieldwork

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike repetitive data entry and cleaning
- People who avoid outdoor surveying or site visits

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a GIS specialist?**
A: Entry-level is about 28-35 million KRW, mid-level 35-50 million KRW, and senior 50-75 million KRW.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work in the GIS field in Korea?**
A: For specialized fields, the E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is common, and you must meet degree and experience requirements.

**Q: What are the most important certifications in the GIS field?**
A: GIS Engineer or Surveying and Geospatial Information Engineer are useful, advantageous for public sector jobs.
