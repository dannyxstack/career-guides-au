# Obstetrician/Gynecologist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30114 (KECO)**

A doctor who specializes in female reproductive health, pregnancy, and childbirth, providing prenatal care, delivery, and gynecological disease treatment.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI is used for image reading (ultrasound, MRI) and diagnostic assistance, but the core roles of obstetricians/gynecologists—surgery, delivery, and patient consultation—are difficult to replace with AI. Rather, AI tools are expected to improve diagnostic accuracy and work efficiency.

After graduating from medical school, completing an internship and residency, becoming an OB/GYN specialist, then opening a practice or advancing as a university hospital professor, or specializing in subfields (infertility, oncology, etc.).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple Ultrasound Reading
- Quantitative Test Result Analysis
- Standardized Medical Record Writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Ultrasound/MRI Image Diagnosis
- Surgical Planning
- Drug Prescription Optimization

**Human moat:**

- Patient Trust Relationship
- Manual Surgical Skills
- Complex Clinical Judgment
- Emergency Response

**Skills to build:**

- Robotic Surgery
- Genomics/Precision Medicine
- Data Analysis
- Medical AI Utilization

**Key growth areas:**

- Advanced maternal age
- Women's health
- Obesity Subspecialization
- Robotic Surgery

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of an obstetrician-gynecologist?**
A: Salary varies: junior hospital doctors about 100-200 million KRW, mid-level 200-400 million KRW, private practitioners 400-800 million KRW or more.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an OB-GYN in Korea?**
A: Possible, but obtaining a Korean medical license is mandatory, and employment is possible with an E-7 visa, though the process is strict.

**Q: How long does it take to become an OB-GYN specialist?**
A: Medical school 6 years + internship 1 year + residency 4 years, totaling at least 11 years.
