# Career Counselor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 23141 (KECO)**

A professional counselor who connects job seekers with employers, supporting efficient labor market matching through career planning, job placement, and job information provision.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Department of Social Welfare or Counseling at a junior college or university | 2-4 years | $4,000,000~$20,000,000 (Tuition fees; more affordable at national and public universities) |
| Career Counselor Certification (National Technical Qualification) | 6 months to 1 year | $500,000~$2,000,000 (Includes academy tuition and exam fees) |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a job counselor?**
A: For entry-level, about 24 to 32 million KRW, and with experience and qualifications, it can exceed 40 million KRW.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a job counselor in Korea?**
A: It is very difficult. Due to Korean language ability and the principle of prioritizing native workers, they are mainly jobs for locals and not eligible for E-7 visas.

**Q: Is a job counselor license mandatory?**
A: It is not mandatory but highly advantageous for employment, and public institutions like employment centers give preference to it.
