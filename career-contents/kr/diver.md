## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent physical strength and underwater adaptability
- People who strictly follow safety regulations
- People interested in marine and construction fields

**Who is not suited**
- People with claustrophobia
- People vulnerable to high-altitude work or underwater hazards

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a diver?**
A: From about 35 million KRW for entry-level to 90 million KRW for senior, with additional field and hazard pay.

**Q: Can foreigners work as divers in Korea?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but a Korean diving license and experience are required, and the employer must obtain approval from the Ministry of Labor.

**Q: How do I get a diver's license?**
A: You must pass the written and practical exams administered by the Human Resources Development Service of Korea, usually prepared with 6 months of academy training.
