# Recycling Processing Equipment & Incinerator Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 85321 (KECO)**

A professional environmental facility expert who operates and controls waste recycling processing equipment and incinerators to recover resources or safely incinerate waste.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a recycling processing equipment operator?**
A: Entry-level is approximately 24 to 30 million KRW, including night shift and shift differentials, with a seniority-based pay scale.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, it is possible. You can apply with an E-7-1 (Skilled Worker) visa, which requires experience in the environmental industry.

**Q: What is the working environment like for this job?**
A: Shift work within incineration and recycling facilities, with potential exposure to noise, odors, and high temperatures.
