# Risk Manager — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15842 (KECO)**

A professional who prevents and manages various risks such as fires, chemical spills, and industrial accidents on the manufacturing floor, creating a safe working environment.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Safety engineering department at a college/university | 2–4 years | $6,000,000~$20,000,000 (Tuition and textbook costs) |
| Industrial Safety Engineer certification | 6 months–1 year | $500,000~$1,500,000 (Academy tuition and exam fees) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial Safety Engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Required | Key qualification for performing risk management tasks |
| Industrial Safety Engineer (Hazardous Materials) | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Preferred when handling chemical substances |

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 25,000. Demand is stable due to manufacturing automation and strengthened Serious Accident Punishment Act, but the outlook is positive due to a shortage of skilled workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After obtaining a safety manager certificate and gaining field experience, you can grow into a plant safety general manager or consultant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Risk data collection and basic analysis
- Regular report writing
- CCTV monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Risk prediction modeling
- Safety training simulation
- Real-time risk warning system operation

**Human moat:**

- Complex judgment based on on-site intuition and experience
- Emergency response and leadership
- Communication skills for establishing a safety culture

**Skills to build:**

- Big data analysis
- Machine learning basics
- IoT sensor utilization
- Communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Industrial Safety
- Serious Accidents Punishment Act
- Manufacturing Automation
- Safety Management

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People interested in risk prediction and prevention
- Thorough in following regulations and guiding others
- Practical type who prefers field work

**Who is not suited**
- People who are insensitive to safety
- Personality that often breaks rules or is careless

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a risk manager?**
A: For new hires, annual pre-tax salary is about 30-40 million KRW, and having an industrial safety engineer license is advantageous.

**Q: Can foreigners work as risk managers?**
A: Yes, skilled safety personnel can be hired under an E-7 visa, provided experience and qualification requirements are met.

**Q: Are there required certifications?**
A: Industrial safety engineer is the most common, and hazardous materials industrial engineer is also helpful depending on the site.
