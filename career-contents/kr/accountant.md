# Accountant — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02311 (KECO)**

A professional who records, analyzes, and reports on a company's financial status and business performance, performing tax filing and financial advisory tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Careers are primarily built at accounting firms, corporate finance teams, or public institutions, with promotion to partner or CFO after obtaining CPA.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Journal entries and bookkeeping
- Simple review of financial statements
- Basic tax return preparation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Financial analysis and forecasting
- Tax risk assessment
- Audit sampling and outlier detection

**Human moat:**

- Professional judgment
- Client relations and negotiation
- Complex tax strategy formulation

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Python
- IFRS·KGAAP expertise
- Digital audit tools

**Key growth areas:**

- IFRS Adoption
- Data Analysis
- Tax Consulting
- ESG Accounting

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an accountant?**
A: Junior 40-60M KRW, mid-level 60-90M KRW, senior 100-200M KRW, with differences between accounting firms and general companies.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an accountant in Korea?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is required, along with a Korean CPA license or equivalent experience and an employment contract.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Korean accountant?**
A: A Certified Public Accountant (KICPA) license is essential, requiring passing the first and second exams and completing practical training.
