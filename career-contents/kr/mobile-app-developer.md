# Mobile App Developer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 13325 (KECO)**

A professional who designs, develops, tests, and maintains applications for smartphones and tablets, developing native or cross-platform apps on iOS and Android.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Can advance from junior developer to senior developer to tech lead/CTO, or become a specialist in fields like AI or blockchain. Career development is possible at both startups and large corporations.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple UI code generation
- Basic test case writing
- Standard library call patterns

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Code review and quality inspection
- Performance optimization
- Automated test generation
- User behavior analysis

**Human moat:**

- Creative app design
- Deep understanding of user experience (UX)
- Implementing complex business logic
- Team leadership and communication

**Skills to build:**

- Swift/Kotlin
- Flutter/React Native
- AI/ML Integration
- Cloud Services (AWS, Firebase)
- CI/CD Pipeline

**Key growth areas:**

- iOS
- Android
- Flutter
- Kotlin

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong logical thinking and problem-solving skills
- People who are comfortable continuously learning new technologies
- People skilled in teamwork and communication

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer repetitive tasks and dislike creative challenges
- People unable to adapt to changing technologies

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a mobile app developer?**
A: Beginner: 30-40 million KRW, intermediate: 45-60 million KRW, advanced: 65-90 million KRW, varying greatly by experience and skills.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a mobile app developer in Korea?**
A: An E-7 visa is common, requiring a bachelor's degree or 5+ years of experience. Can switch from a D-10 job-seeking visa.

**Q: What skills should I develop to grow as a mobile app developer?**
A: Learning latest languages like Swift, Kotlin, Flutter is essential; UI/UX design understanding and collaboration skills are also important.
