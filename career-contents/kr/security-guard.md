# Security Guard — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 54201 (KECO)**

A safety occupation that prevents theft, fire, and intrusion in buildings, facilities, factories, etc., and performs patrols, access control, and facility management.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Security Guard New Training | National Police Agency | Required | Mandatory completion of 24 hours for initial employment |
| Mechanical Security Instructor | Korea Security Association | Optional | Enables unmanned security control duties |
| Special Security Guard Certificate | National Police Agency | Optional | Required for escort and critical facility security |
| Fire Safety Manager | Korea Fire Safety Institute | Optional | Enhances fire prevention and response capabilities |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 450,000. Due to aging and labor shortages, demand for security guards remains, but the adoption of AI/IoT-based unmanned security systems is gradually expanding.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

New recruits are assigned after simple on-site training, and with experience, they can be promoted to squad leader or team leader, or transition to special security (mechanical security/escort).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple patrol and access control records
- Simple CCTV monitoring
- Regular facility inspection report writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Drone and robot patrols with human collaboration
- AI anomaly detection system operation supervision
- Decision-making and response in emergency situations

**Human moat:**

- Emergency response capability
- Interpersonal Communication and Crisis Negotiation
- Customized Safety Management Know-How by Building

**Skills to build:**

- Mechanical Security System Operation
- Fire and Disaster Prevention Practical Knowledge
- Smart Security Equipment Utilization Skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging workforce
- Expansion of unmanned security
- Enhanced facility safety
- Increase in apartments and buildings

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a security guard?**
A: Approximately 25-30 million KRW for entry-level, up to 42 million KRW with experience, night shift allowances, and special security qualifications.

**Q: Can foreigners work as security guards?**
A: Difficult with an E-9 visa; possible for holders of F-2, F-4, F-5 residence or permanent residency, and overseas Koreans (F-4).

**Q: How can a security guard get promoted?**
A: With work experience and special security or mechanical security certifications, there are opportunities for promotion to site manager (team leader/site chief).
