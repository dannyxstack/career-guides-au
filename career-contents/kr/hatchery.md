# Hatchery — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 90411 (KECO)**

A hatchery specialist is an expert who artificially breeds and raises aquatic organisms to sell to fishermen or release directly into farms.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High School Diploma | 3 years | $0~$0 (Free (compulsory education)) |
| College (Department of Aquaculture) | 2 years | $3,000,000~$5,000,000 (Annual tuition fee) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aquaculture Business License | Local Government | Required | Mandatory for operating a farm |
| Aquaculture Engineer | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Not required but advantageous for employment |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 22,000. Inland aquaculture has stable demand but faces a chronic labor shortage due to aging and lack of young workers.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Initially, you gain hands-on experience in the field; after 5–10 years, you can advance to farm manager or technical advisor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual Feed Supply
- Simple Water Quality Measurement
- Manual Sorting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Automatic Feed Dispenser Monitoring
- Water Quality Sensor Data Analysis
- Remote Aquaculture Farm Control

**Human moat:**

- Disease Diagnosis and Response
- Aquaculture Environment Control Know-How
- Seafood Quality Evaluation

**Skills to build:**

- Automated Equipment Operation
- Water Quality Data Analysis
- Aquaculture Biology Knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Eco-friendly aquaculture
- Smart aquaculture
- Increase in Seafood Exports
- Expansion of Inland Aquaculture

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary for a fish farmer?**
A: Approximately 25-30 million KRW pre-tax for entry-level, up to 60 million KRW depending on experience.

**Q: Are there visas for foreigners to work as fish farmers?**
A: Yes, the E-7 visa (Specific Activities) allows hiring skilled technicians, while the E-9 visa is limited to simple work.
