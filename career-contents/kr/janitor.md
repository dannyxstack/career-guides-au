# Janitor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 56111 (KECO)**

A janitor cleans the interiors of buildings, facilities, and offices, and collects trash, maintaining hygiene and cleanliness.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

They are usually non-regular workers or employed by outsourcing companies, and can be promoted to facility team leader or cleaning site manager based on experience.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Meticulous and diligent person
- Person who prefers working alone
- Person who does not dislike physical activity

**Who is not suited**
- Person not sensitive to hygiene
- Person who prefers static work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a cleaner?**
A: Approximately 20 million KRW for beginners, over 30 million KRW after 10+ years of experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as cleaners?**
A: E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visas have restrictions; F-2 (Residence) and F-4 (Overseas Korean) visa holders are eligible.

**Q: Are any certificates required?**
A: No mandatory certificates, but a cleaning technician certificate can help with employment.
