# Traffic Impact Assessor — career analysis · South Korea

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary level at the Traffic Impact Assessment Agency?**
A: Entry-level salary 33M~42M won, mid-level 45M~60M won, senior-level 65M~90M won.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain a Korean work visa for this occupation?**
A: Possible. You can apply with an E-7-1 visa (Specific Activities), which requires a degree in transportation engineering and certain experience.

**Q: What major should I study?**
A: A bachelor's degree or higher in transportation engineering, civil engineering, urban engineering, or related fields is required; a master's or higher is advantageous.
