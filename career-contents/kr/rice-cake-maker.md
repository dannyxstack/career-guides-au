# Rice Cake Maker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 87121 (KECO)**

A professional who makes rice cakes by kneading rice flour and steaming or baking them in various ways, producing both traditional and modern rice cakes.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 35,000. Due to a decrease in per capita rice consumption and a preference for convenient foods, mass production demand is declining, but demand for premium and traditional rice cakes persists, so employment is expected to remain stable.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with manual dexterity and attention to detail
- People interested in traditional food
- People who are early risers

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike repetitive work
- People who want a high salary

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a rice cake maker?**
A: Entry-level approximately 22 to 28 million KRW; senior with 7+ years experience up to 35 to 45 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as rice cake makers?**
A: Partially possible with E-9 visa, but limited to manufacturing; overseas Koreans (F-4/H-2) have relatively easier employment.
