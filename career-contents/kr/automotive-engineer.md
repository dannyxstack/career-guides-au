# Automotive Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15117 (KECO)**

Engineer responsible for design, development, performance testing, and production technology of automobiles and parts, researching advanced technologies like internal combustion engines, electric vehicles, and autonomous driving.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 45,000. Demand is continuously increasing due to the transition to new technologies such as electric vehicles, autonomous driving, and hydrogen cars, but the internal combustion engine field is expected to stagnate. A shortage of skilled workers is emerging.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

AI and automation enhance vehicle design, simulation, and autonomous driving development, but humans still play a major role in creative design, real vehicle testing, and integrated engineering.

Entry (Researcher) → Associate/Senior → Principal/Chief → Team Lead/CTO. Mainly in large and mid-sized companies; master's/doctoral degrees are advantageous. Career paths also include automotive parts suppliers, startups, and research institutes.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple CAD modeling
- Basic structural analysis
- Organizing standard performance test data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimal design exploration
- Autonomous driving algorithm development
- Simulation automation

**Human moat:**

- Real vehicle testing and evaluation know-how
- System integration engineering
- Safety and durability judgment experience

**Skills to build:**

- Python/C++ coding
- AI/machine learning basics
- Data analysis
- Control systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric vehicles
- Autonomous driving
- Hydrogen vehicles
- Smart mobility

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those with a strong science/engineering aptitude in math, physics, and computers
- Those who combine teamwork and meticulous design skills

**Who is not suited**
- Those who prefer repetitive, simple tasks
- Those who are passive about learning new technologies

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an automotive engineer?**
A: Entry: approx. 40 million won, mid: approx. 60 million won, senior: approx. 100 million won; higher at large companies and research institutes.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes. An E-7 visa (Specific Activities) is common, requiring a bachelor's degree and relevant experience.

**Q: Is there a required major to become an automotive engineer?**
A: Mechanical engineering, automotive engineering, electrical/electronic engineering are advantageous; a master's degree or higher is better.
