# Landscape Gardener — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 90151 (KECO)**

A professional who plants and maintains vegetation and manages landscape facilities in parks, gardens, urban green spaces, etc.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Initially learns simple maintenance tasks; with experience, can obtain landscape craftsman/technician certifications to advance into construction/design fields or start a business.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Lawn Mowing
- Simple Weeding
- Irrigation Timer Setting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Plant Growth Monitoring
- Irrigation Optimization
- Design Simulation

**Human moat:**

- On-Site Judgment
- Tree Pruning Skills
- Design Sense

**Skills to build:**

- Smart Irrigation Systems
- Drone Utilization
- Basic GIS

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban Greening
- Smart Farm
- Ecological Restoration
- Park Management

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who prefer outdoor activities
- People with dexterity and good stamina
- People interested in plants

**Who is not suited**
- Person who prefers indoor/office work
- People who want fixed working hours

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a landscaper?**
A: About 2.2 to 2.8 million won for entry-level, up to 5 million won with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as landscapers?**
A: Yes, simple labor is possible with an E-9 visa, and skilled workers can switch to an E-7 visa.
