# Tailor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 86221 (KECO)**

A tailor is a specialized professional who cuts fabric according to patterns and performs preparatory work before sewing for garment production.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school graduate | 12 years | $0~$0 (Public education cost) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 35,000. Despite automation in the Korean clothing industry, demand for custom-made and high-end clothing remains, keeping demand for skilled tailors stable but overall employment stagnant.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Starting as a skilled worker, one can advance to patter maker or tailor, with self-employment or employment at luxury brands being common career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard pattern generation
- Mass production fabric cutting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Custom pattern design using 3D scanning
- Automated cutting machine operation

**Human moat:**

- Fitting sense based on manual dexterity and experience
- Know-how in high-end repairs and custom suit making

**Skills to build:**

- Pattern CAD
- 3D virtual fitting
- Advanced sewing techniques
- Customer consultation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom suits
- Luxury repairs
- Pattern automation
- Hanbok tailoring

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are meticulous and have good dexterity
- People with a strong interest in textiles and clothing
- People who are patient and can tolerate repetitive work

**Who is not suited**
- People who find it difficult to adapt to new technological changes
- People who value interpersonal relationships

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a tailor?**
A: The annual salary for entry-level (0-3 years) is about KRW 22-28 million, increasing with skill level.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as a tailor in South Korea?**
A: It is possible to work as a skilled tailor on an E-7 visa, but it requires an employment recommendation letter and meeting qualification requirements, and is limited.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a tailor?**
A: Certifications such as Suit Craftsman or Garment Craftsman are helpful, not mandatory, but advantageous for employment.
