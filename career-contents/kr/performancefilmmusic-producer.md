# Performance·Film·Music Producer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02442 (KECO)**

A professional who oversees the planning, production, and distribution of cultural content such as performances, films, and music, managing the overall direction, schedule, and budget of works.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Creative and highly organized people
- People skilled at managing schedules under stress
- People who enjoy networking and communication

**Who is not suited**
- People who want regular and stable work
- People stronger in logical analysis than artistic sense

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: For interns or juniors, around 24-35 million KRW per year, with large entertainment companies higher than average.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in this field?**
A: E-7-1 (Specific Activities) visa is most common, requiring a degree or career evidence in the field.

**Q: Is a portfolio important?**
A: Yes, actual project planning history has a decisive impact on hiring more than a degree.
