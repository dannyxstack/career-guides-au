# Performance·Film·Music Producer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02442 (KECO)**

A professional who oversees the planning, production, and distribution of cultural content such as performances, films, and music, managing the overall direction, schedule, and budget of works.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

AI is used in the planning of performances, films, and music for data analysis (market trend prediction, target marketing), schedule management, and budget optimization, but creative planning, network-based collaboration, and intuitive decision-making remain human domains. While AI assists with some tasks, full replacement is difficult; rather, it increases efficiency, allowing planners to focus on more creative work.

Typically starting as an intern at agencies or production companies, promoted to producer after 3–5 years, then may advance to founding an independent label or becoming an executive at a large company.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Market Research and Trend Prediction
- Budget Calculation and Settlement
- Schedule Management Automation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Targeted Marketing Strategy Establishment
- Real-time Audience Response Analysis and Response
- Promotional Content Production Efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Original Planning Ability
- Industry Connections and Negotiation Skills
- Field Experience-Based Intuition

**Skills to build:**

- AI Data Analysis Tool Utilization
- Digital Marketing and SNS Strategy
- Project Management Software

**Key growth areas:**

- K-Culture Expansion
- OTT Market Growth
- Digital Marketing Enhancement
- Overseas Expansion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: For interns or juniors, around 24-35 million KRW per year, with large entertainment companies higher than average.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in this field?**
A: E-7-1 (Specific Activities) visa is most common, requiring a degree or career evidence in the field.

**Q: Is a portfolio important?**
A: Yes, actual project planning history has a decisive impact on hiring more than a degree.
