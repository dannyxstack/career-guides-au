# Automotive Sales Representative — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61231 (KECO)**

A profession that consults and sells vehicles to customers at car dealerships or agencies, handling the entire process from test drives, estimates, contracts to delivery.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school diploma or above | 0 | $0~$0 (No specific degree required, high school diploma or above preferred) |
| Automotive sales training | 3 months | $500,000~$2,000,000 (Training provided by the Korea Automobile Dealers Association or individual dealerships) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Driver's license (Class 2 or higher) | National Police Agency | Required | Essential for test drives and vehicle movement |
| Automobile Sales Certificate | Korea Automobile Industry Association | Optional | Not legally required but most dealerships require it |
| Insurance Solicitor License | Financial Supervisory Service | Optional | Advantageous for selling car insurance |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level salesperson → Mid-level (team leader/assistant manager) → Branch manager/manager → Vice president/Head. High performers can achieve faster promotions, and many move to higher salaries through job changes.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Customer basic inquiry response
- Basic vehicle specification guidance
- Test drive scheduling
- Automatic quote generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized product recommendations through customer data analysis
- Customer management using AI-based CRM
- Test drive experience through virtual simulation

**Human moat:**

- Trust and human rapport with customers
- Complex negotiations and contract processes
- Emotional empathy and sales know-how

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Online marketing
- AI tool proficiency
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric vehicle sales
- Online sales
- Vehicle subscription
- Connected cars

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy meeting others and are persuasive
- People with strong motivation from performance-based incentives
- People with interest and knowledge in automobiles

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer fixed salary and stability
- People who find it difficult to handle customer service and sales stress

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an automotive salesperson?**
A: Starting salary for new hires is approximately 28-40 million KRW (base pay + incentives), and for those with 3-7 years of experience, it is 40-70 million KRW, with top performers potentially earning over 100 million KRW.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work as a car salesperson?**
A: It is difficult to obtain an E-7 visa for general sales positions; holders of F-2 or F-4 visas have an advantage. H-2 is not possible as it does not cover this industry.

**Q: What is the career progression for a car salesperson?**
A: Junior salesperson → Intermediate (team leader/assistant manager) → Branch manager/Manager → Vice President/Head of department, with rapid promotion possible based on performance.
