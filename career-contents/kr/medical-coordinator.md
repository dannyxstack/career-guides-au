# Medical Coordinator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02922 (KECO)**

A medical administrative professional who comprehensively coordinates patient consultation, appointment scheduling, medical fee guidance, and insurance review at hospitals or medical institutions.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

| Insurance Review Specialist Certification | Korea Health Insurance Review and Assessment Association | Optional | Advantageous for insurance-related tasks |
| Medical Records Technician Certification | Korean Medical Records Association | Optional | Required for medical records management |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 35,000. This field is experiencing steady demand growth due to improved medical service quality and expansion of patient-centered care systems. Prospects are bright with aging population and medical tourism activation, and recruitment is expected to increase especially in general hospitals and large medical institutions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Initially performing administrative assistance or coordinator tasks, with career progression to managerial positions such as team leader or center director, or development into specialized fields like medical marketing or international patient coordinator.

**Skills to build:**

- Foreign Language Proficiency (English, Chinese, etc.)

**Key growth areas:**

- Medical Tourism

- Patient Experience Management

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are interested in the medical field and enjoy helping others
- Person with meticulous and systematic work processing skills
- A person who enjoys communication while collaborating with various departments

**Who is not suited**
- A person who stubbornly insists on rules without flexibility
- People who have difficulty controlling emotions in stressful situations

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a medical coordinator?**
A: Entry-level: 24–28M KRW; mid-level: 30–36M KRW; senior: 38–48M KRW; varies by hospital size and experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as medical coordinators in Korea?**
A: Possible, but proof of expertise is required to obtain an E-7-1 visa. Foreign language skills are advantageous in the medical tourism field but limited.
