# Records Manager — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41322 (KECO)**

A records manager is a professional who systematically collects, classifies, preserves, and utilizes documents and records of public institutions and enterprises, managing the entire lifecycle of records.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Careers typically start at public records management agencies, university libraries, or corporate archives. After obtaining a professional records manager certification, promotion to a managerial position is possible.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a records manager?**
A: Approximately 30-36 million KRW for entry-level, 45-60 million KRW for over 8 years of experience.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a records manager?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but cases are rare; mainly suitable for F-2 or F-5 visa holders.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a records manager?**
A: A professional records manager certification is required, and a librarian certificate helps.
