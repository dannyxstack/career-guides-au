## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary of an astronomy/meteorology researcher?**
A: For a master's degree, about 35-45 million won at national/public research institutes; for a doctorate, starting at over 45 million won.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a job in Korea in this occupation?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, requiring a relevant degree (master's or higher) and research experience. Limited hiring at government research institutes or university labs.

**Q: How can a researcher become a professor?**
A: After earning a doctoral degree, build a career as a postdoctoral researcher or research professor, then apply for a university faculty position. Research achievements and publications are crucial.
