# Office Electronic Equipment Installer & Repairer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 83211 (KECO)**

Professionals who install and repair office electronic equipment such as computers, printers, and copiers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

AI and automation play a supporting role in diagnostics and remote assistance, but physical installation and repair require human intervention, so complete replacement is difficult.

Beginners start at small service companies or as freelancers; with experience, they can move to large company partners or electronics repair centers. Starting a business is also common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple fault diagnosis
- Remote A/S reception
- Parts inventory management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Precision diagnosis and analysis
- Customer consultation and training
- Complex equipment maintenance planning

**Human moat:**

- Physical manipulation and repair
- Immediate response to on-site situations
- Building customer trust

**Skills to build:**

- AI and IoT device knowledge
- Basic network security
- Programming basics

**Key growth areas:**

- Shortage of technical personnel in SMEs
- Increase in digital multifunction devices
- Expansion of IT infrastructure
- Demand for replacing old equipment

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are dexterous and interested in electronic devices
- People who enjoy solving problems independently
- People who are good with customer service

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer repetitive document tasks
- People who want stable work in a large organization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: Starting with an annual pre-tax salary of 24-30 million KRW for small businesses, it can go up to 55 million KRW depending on experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this job?**
A: Yes, it is possible with an E-7 visa if specialized skills are recognized, and the employer must recommend it. The E-9 visa is limited.

**Q: Is there potential for promotion or growth?**
A: In small businesses, starting a business is common; in large company partners, growth to technical team leader is possible.
