# Fast Food Prep Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 53211 (KECO)**

Prepares and packages menu items (burgers, fries, drinks) and serves customers at fast food restaurants

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Can advance to store manager with experience; high turnover as most work is simple labor; often starts as part-time job

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Order entry
- Simple beverage preparation
- Frying potatoes

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized customer recommendations
- Inventory management
- Hygiene inspection

**Human moat:**

- Customer service
- Emergency response
- Cleanliness assessment

**Skills to build:**

- Basic cooking
- Food hygiene
- Customer service
- Kiosk operation

**Key growth areas:**

- Franchise expansion
- Increase in delivery and takeout
- Minimum wage increase
- Introduction of automation and kiosks

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Quick hand movements
- No aversion to customer service
- Patience for simple repetitive tasks

**Who is not suited**
- Stress from high-speed work
- Lack of service mindset

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a fast-food preparer?**
A: Entry-level: 23-26 million KRW, minimum wage applies

**Q: Can foreigners work at fast-food restaurants?**
A: Possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) or H-2 (Working Visit) visas, requires Employment Permit System
