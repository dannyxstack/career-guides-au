# Quality Control Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02842 (KECO)**

A professional who manages quality standards in the manufacturing process, analyzes inspection data, and maintains/improves product quality.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career path: New employee → Quality Control Assistant Manager → Deputy Manager → Manager → Quality Control Team Leader/Executive; ISO 9001 and Six Sigma certifications are advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual inspection records
- Simple statistical processing
- Repetitive pass/fail decisions

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Defect analysis and cause tracing
- Real-time quality monitoring
- Process optimization suggestions

**Human moat:**

- Consultations with clients
- Solving unstructured problems
- Establishing and improving quality systems

**Skills to build:**

- Statistical analysis
- Data visualization
- SAP and MES utilization
- English communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart factory
- Six Sigma
- ISO certification
- Data analysis

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a quality control clerk?**
A: Based on SMEs, pre-tax annual salary ranges from 30 to 38 million KRW; large companies offer 40 million KRW or more.

**Q: Can foreigners work as quality control clerks?**
A: It is possible, but E-7 visa issuance requires a related degree and more than one year of experience, and Korean language proficiency is required.

**Q: What is the required educational background?**
A: An associate degree or higher in engineering is common, and a quality management engineer certification is advantageous.
