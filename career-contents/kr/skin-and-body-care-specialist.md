## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a skin care specialist?**
A: For entry-level, approximately 22-28 million KRW before tax; with experience and business ownership, it can exceed 50 million KRW.

**Q: What visa is needed for a foreigner to work as a skin care specialist in Korea?**
A: E-7 visa is possible for skilled professionals, but qualifications and experience must be proven; general E-9 visa employment is difficult.

**Q: Is immigration possible as a skin care specialist?**
A: After long-term work on an E-7 visa, applying for F-2 or F-5 permanent residence is possible, but it may be disadvantageous under the Skilled Worker Immigration (E-7-4) points system.
