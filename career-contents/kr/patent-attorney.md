# Patent Attorney — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 22141 (KECO)**

A patent attorney is a professional who provides legal and technical advice regarding the filing and registration of industrial property rights such as patents, trademarks, and designs.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Obtain a Certified Patent Attorney License | 3 years | $5,000,000~$10,000,000 (Includes tuition for cram schools, textbooks, and exam fees) |
| Practical training after passing the patent attorney exam | 1 year | $5,000,000~$10,000,000 (Cost of practical training at a patent law firm) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patent Attorney Certificate | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Required | Must pass the first (multiple-choice) and second (essay) examinations |
| Bachelor's degree or higher in science/technology or law | University | Required | Eligibility to take the patent attorney exam |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

After gaining practical experience at a patent law firm or corporate IP team, one can advance to a team leader, partner patent attorney, or open a private practice. Differentiation in specialized fields (e.g., biotech, AI) is important.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patent information search and analysis
- Standard document preparation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Patent invalidity analysis
- Portfolio strategy formulation

**Human moat:**

- Courtroom advocacy
- Client trust relationships
- Technology-law integration judgment

**Skills to build:**

- Understanding of AI/machine learning
- Data analysis
- Big data processing

**Key growth areas:**

- Protection of intellectual property rights
- AI·Bio patents
- Standard Essential Patent
- IP Finance

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People interested in science, technology, and law
- Those with logical analysis and meticulous document writing skills
- Those who can withstand long periods of study and exam preparation

**Who is not suited**
- Those who prefer fast-paced changes and diverse tasks
- Those who do not dislike working alone over communicating with people

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of patent attorneys?**
A: Entry-level about 40-55 million KRW, mid-level 65-100 million KRW, senior over 100 million KRW, varying greatly with experience and performance.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a patent attorney in Korea?**
A: Mainly the E-7 (Specific Activities) visa allows employment at patent firms, and passing the Korean patent attorney exam is essential.

**Q: What education is required to become a patent attorney?**
A: After obtaining a bachelor's degree in science/technology or law, one must pass the patent attorney exam and complete a one-year practical training.
