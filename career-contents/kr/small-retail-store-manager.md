# Small Retail Store Manager — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61401 (KECO)**

A small business owner who operates small retail stores such as neighborhood supermarkets, convenience stores, and small marts, overseeing product ordering, inventory management, sales analysis, staff management, and customer service.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 250,000. Due to the expansion of large marts and online shopping, coupled with minimum wage increases, the business environment has deteriorated, leading to a decline in employment. Differentiation and efficiency are essential for survival.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

AI assists store managers with inventory management, sales forecasting, and self-checkout, but customer service and on-site decision-making still require humans for the time being. Jobs are declining due to competition from large marts and online retailers.

Career path: small store employee → store manager → district manager or headquarters supervisor, or franchise start-up/multi-store operation, but the path is narrowing due to industry downturn.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Check Inventory Quantity
- Simple Calculation and Settlement
- Store Cleaning and Organizing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Sales Data Analysis
- Customer Preference Prediction
- Promotion Planning

**Human moat:**

- Trust Relationship with Customers
- Local Community Network
- Creative Marketing

**Skills to build:**

- Digital Marketing
- Data Analysis
- Negotiation Skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Small Business Owner
- Convenience Store
- Unmanned Store
- Neighborhood Supermarket

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy interacting and communicating with customers
- Diligent and highly responsible people
- People interested in small-scale self-employment

**Who is not suited**
- People who want high profits in a short period
- People who find it difficult to adapt to change

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a small retail store manager?**
A: For entry-level, it is 24-30 million KRW per year, and for mid-level, it can go up to 30-40 million KRW depending on business performance.

**Q: Can foreigners work as small retail store managers in Korea?**
A: It is possible with an E-7 visa, but requirements are strict; long-term residents such as F-2 and F-4 are more suitable.

**Q: What are the core competencies a store manager should have?**
A: Inventory management skills, customer service mindset, basic accounting knowledge, and store management and marketing skills are important.
