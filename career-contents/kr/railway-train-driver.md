# Railway / Train Driver — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62131 (KECO)**

A professional who operates railway vehicles to safely transport passengers or freight.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school graduate | 3 years | $0~$0 (No need to major in railway-related studies after high school) |
| Associate degree | 2 years | $6,000,000~$10,000,000 (Tuition for railway operation-related departments) |
| Train driver training course | 1 year | $10,000,000~$15,000,000 (Private educational institutions or in-house training by the Korea Railroad Corporation (KORAIL)) |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression from junior driver to high-speed train driver or management position possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple driving on fixed sections
- Automatic announcement of stopping stations
- Automatic speed control

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Emergency response judgment
- Train status monitoring
- Passenger safety management

**Human moat:**

- Assuming legal responsibility

**Skills to build:**

- Understanding AI systems

- Data analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- High-speed rail
- Safe operation
- Railroad network expansion
- Digital signal

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much is the starting salary for a train driver?**
A: About 35-45 million KRW based on Korail standards, can exceed 70 million KRW with experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as train drivers?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but Korean language skills and a railroad driving license are required, along with an employer recommendation.

**Q: What educational background is needed to become a train driver?**
A: A high school diploma or higher is acceptable, and completing railroad-related courses or training programs is advantageous.
