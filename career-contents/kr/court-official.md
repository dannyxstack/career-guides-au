# Court Official — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02542 (KECO)**

A public official deployed to the National Court Administration and various levels of courts, handling administrative tasks such as case reception, trial record management, judgment writing, and witness management.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After appointment via Grade 9 open recruitment, advancement is possible through promotion exams for Grade 7 and Grade 5, or growth into specialized administrative roles through internal court training. Long-term service may lead to management positions (e.g., office director).

- Simple document classification and registration
- Basic form drafting and template filling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Security and ethical handling of confidential information

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a court official?**
A: For Grade 9 lower pay steps, annual pre-tax salary is approximately KRW 32 million to 38 million. Allowances (regular, family, etc.) are separate and increase with pay steps.

**Q: Can foreigners become court officials?**
A: Foreigners generally cannot take the court official recruitment exam. Only Korean nationals are eligible, and permanent residents (F-5) also need to meet nationality requirements. Thus, it is not suitable as an immigration path.

**Q: How does promotion work for court officials?**
A: After being appointed at Grade 9, one can be promoted to Grades 7 and 5 through years of service and promotion exams. For senior managers (e.g., bureau directors), internal promotion and passing the Court Administration Higher Civil Service Exam are advantageous.
