# Clay Product Production Machine Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 82621 (KECO)**

This occupation involves operating and managing machines that produce construction materials such as bricks, roof tiles, and tiles from clay.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with good physical strength and patience for repetitive tasks
- People interested in machine operation and manual dexterity
- People who can accommodate night shifts or shift work

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer static office jobs
- People who want creative work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beginners?**
A: For entry-level (0-3 years), pre-tax annual salary is about 24 million to 28 million won, with additional pay for night work.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: Yes. You can work in manufacturing with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, and once skilled, switch to an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa or apply for an F-2 (Residence) visa.

**Q: How can I get promoted in this occupation?**
A: Acquiring a ceramic technician or ceramic engineer certification along with experience increases opportunities for promotion to management or quality control roles.
