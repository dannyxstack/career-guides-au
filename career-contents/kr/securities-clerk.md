# Securities Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 03221 (KECO)**

An office worker who handles buying, selling, settlement, and custody of securities such as stocks and bonds at a securities company, and manages customer accounts.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Typically hired as new college graduates or experienced employees, they start with general office tasks and can later advance to manager roles such as team leader, branch manager, or head office chief, or transition to specialized positions like asset management or investment banking.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Execution of stock and bond trades
- Account balance inquiry and reconciliation
- Simple transaction entry
- Automated regular report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer consulting and portfolio recommendations
- Market analysis and investment strategy formulation
- Regulatory compliance checks
- Detection of abnormal transactions

**Human moat:**

- Ability to interpret regulations
- Understanding of complex financial products
- Communication skills

**Skills to build:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a securities clerk?**
A: Entry-level is about 35-45 million KRW, and team leaders with over 10 years of experience can earn 65-100 million KRW. Including bonuses and allowances, the range varies widely.

**Q: Can foreigners work as securities clerks?**
A: General office jobs are difficult for E-7 visa issuance. However, holders of financial professional certifications (e.g., CFA) or those assigned to overseas branches may qualify, but it is not a common immigration route.

**Q: Can a securities clerk transition to other financial roles?**
A: Yes, it's possible. To switch to asset management firms or investment banking (IB) analysts, additional certifications such as CFA or investment asset manager licenses are required.
