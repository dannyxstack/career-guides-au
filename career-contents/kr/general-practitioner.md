# General Practitioner — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30121 (KECO)**

A doctor who diagnoses and treats patients' diseases, providing comprehensive medical services at primary healthcare facilities.

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Steady demand is expected due to aging population and increasing medical needs, but competition is intensifying due to limited supply of doctors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After completing internship and residency (specialty training), either obtain a specialist license, open a private practice, or work as a salaried doctor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple image interpretation
- Primary care decision support

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Precise diagnosis
- Medical data analysis
- Patient monitoring

**Human moat:**

- Doctor-patient relationship
- Clinical judgment
- Ethical decisions

**Skills to build:**

- AI utilization interpretation
- Data literacy
- Medical communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Increase in chronic diseases
- Healthcare accessibility
- Telemedicine

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong patience and responsibility
- People interested in scientific inquiry and problem-solving

**Who is not suited**
- People who are averse to blood or body fluids
- People who struggle with quick decision-making

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a general practitioner?**
A: Based on entry-level (0-3 years), 60-80 million KRW, rising to 100-300 million KRW with experience.

**Q: What visa is needed for a foreigner to work as a general practitioner?**
A: Possible to work with an E-7 visa, with F-2 or F-5 visa paths for long-term residence.

**Q: What are the education period and costs to become a doctor?**
A: It is a total of 6 years: 2 years in pre-medical course and 4 years in medical school, with tuition fees ranging from KRW 120 million to 250 million at private medical schools.
