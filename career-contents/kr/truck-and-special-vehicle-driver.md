# Truck and Special Vehicle Driver — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62234 (KECO)**

This occupation involves driving large trucks or special vehicles to transport cargo, and is a core workforce in the logistics and transportation industry.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Obtain a driver's license | 1 year | $500,000~$1,500,000 (Cost of obtaining a Class 1 Large license) |
| Truck qualification | 3 months | $300,000~$800,000 (Preparation cost for the cargo transport worker certificate) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class 1 Large driver's license | National Police Agency | Required | Required for large truck driving |
| Cargo transport worker certificate | Korea Transportation Safety Authority | Required | Legally required for cargo transport |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Starting with general cargo truck driving, one can obtain special licenses (e.g., hazardous materials, oversized loads) to become a professional driver, or transition to self-employment by owning a vehicle.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Long-distance highway driving
- Simple repetitive transport tasks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Utilization of AI route optimization
- Real-time vehicle status monitoring

**Human moat:**

- Judgment of non-standard road conditions
- Cargo loading and safety inspection
- Communication with customers

**Skills to build:**

- Special vehicle license
- Hazardous materials transport qualification
- Understanding of logistics IT systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Logistics increase
- Aging population
- Labor shortage
- Simplification of driver qualifications

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Patient people for long-distance driving
- People confident in operating large vehicles
- Punctual and responsible individuals

**Who is not suited**
- People with health issues from long driving hours
- People who avoid night and holiday work

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of a novice truck driver?**
A: Pre-tax annual salary for entry-level (0-3 years) is approximately 30-36 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as truck drivers?**
A: Possible with E-9 visa; also possible with E-7-1 but with some restrictions.

**Q: What is the career progression path?**
A: You can move from general driving to special vehicle (hazardous materials, extra-long) driving or advance to a managerial position within a logistics company.
