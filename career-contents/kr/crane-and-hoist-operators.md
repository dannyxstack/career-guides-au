# Crane and Hoist Operators — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62301 (KECO)**

Equipment operation specialists who control cranes or hoists to lift and move heavy objects at construction sites or factories.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Starting at entry level, one can advance to operate large cranes after obtaining certification, or become a site manager.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardized loading/unloading operations
- Simple repetitive lifting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Safety monitoring
- Route planning
- Remote control

**Human moat:**

- On-site hazard judgment
- Handling atypical situations
- Tower crane assembly/disassembly

**Skills to build:**

- Remote control technology
- Understanding of smart construction systems
- AI-based Safety Diagnosis

**Key growth areas:**

- Tower crane
- Construction equipment operation
- Heavy equipment license
- Manufacturing logistics

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Meticulous and highly focused individuals
- Those unafraid of working at heights
- Those interested in machine operation

**Who is not suited**
- People with severe acrophobia
- People who prefer regular working hours

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a crane operator?**
A: Experienced operators with around 10 years of experience earn 50-70 million KRW annually, while beginners earn 28-35 million KRW.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a crane operator?**
A: Skilled workers can apply for an E-7 visa, while beginners can start with auxiliary tasks on an E-9 visa.

**Q: Can women become crane operators?**
A: Yes, they can. Precision operation is more important than physical strength, and the number of female operators is increasing.
