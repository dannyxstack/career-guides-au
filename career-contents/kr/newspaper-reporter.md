# Newspaper Reporter — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41201 (KECO)**

Newspaper reporters gather, write, and edit news and articles to deliver to readers, covering various fields such as society and politics.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 30,000. Due to digital transformation, newspaper restructuring is underway, and job opportunities are on the decline, but some demand remains through collaboration with online media.

**Competition: very high (★★★★½).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Newcomers start at local papers or internet media, build experience, then advance to central papers or specialist reporters, or move into management roles like editor-in-chief.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Organizing simple press releases
- Drafting data-based article drafts
- Photo and Video Caption Generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Interview Content Analysis
- News Curation
- Fact-checking Support

**Human moat:**

- Exclusive Source Network
- In-depth Analysis and Insights
- Ethical Judgment

**Skills to build:**

- Data Literacy
- Multimedia Storytelling
- AI Tool Proficiency

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Transformation
- Data Journalism
- Mobile Content
- Fact-Checking

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone curious with a talent for writing
- Someone resilient to stress and able to handle deadline pressure
- Someone with a strong sense of social justice

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who prefers regular working hours
- Someone who dislikes direct face-to-face interaction

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a newspaper reporter?**
A: Entry-level: approx. 30–45 million KRW, Mid-level: 45–65 million KRW, Senior: 60–90 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as reporters in Korea?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but requires Korean language skills and a media sponsor, with high entry barriers.
