## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After graduating from medical school, complete internship and residency to obtain plastic surgery specialist certification, then open a clinic or work at a hospital; with experience, can become a department head at a large hospital or open a private practice.

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a plastic surgeon?**
A: For employed physicians, junior (0-3 years) approx. 80 million to 120 million KRW; up to 500 million KRW or more with experience; income varies greatly when opening a private practice.

**Q: Can foreigners work as plastic surgeons in Korea?**
A: Obtaining a Korean medical license is mandatory; foreign medical licenses allow limited issuance via E-7 visa, but practically very difficult.

**Q: How do I become a plastic surgeon?**
A: After graduating from medical school, passing the national medical exam, completing one year of internship and four years of residency in plastic surgery, you must pass the board exam to become a specialist.
