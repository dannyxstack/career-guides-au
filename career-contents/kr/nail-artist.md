# Nail Artist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 51132 (KECO)**

Profession specializing in nail and toenail beauty care, offering services like nail care, artificial nails, and art designs.


---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Employment is stable due to Korea's growing beauty industry and increased demand for personal grooming, but competition is fierce and high self-employment rates lead to income volatility.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Nail art is a profession that AI is difficult to completely replace, where creativity and hand skills are important. However, AI can assist in some tasks such as design recommendations or customer management systems.

Career path: from junior nail artist to manager or owner. Becoming a nail instructor or starting a beauty franchise is also common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic Nail Care
- Customer Appointment Management
- Generate Basic Design Templates

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Suggest Custom Designs
- Recommend Color Combinations
- Analyze Nail Health

**Human moat:**

- Creativity
- Hand Skills
- Customer Communication
- Meticulous Work

**Skills to build:**

- Trend Analysis
- Portfolio Management
- Online Marketing
- Customer Relationship Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Nail Art
- Beauty Services
- Nail Care
- Beauty

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person with manual dexterity and excellent design sense
- Person skilled in customer service and interpersonal relations
- Person who is meticulous and patient

**Who is not suited**
- Person who finds it difficult to sit and work for long periods
- Person who reacts sensitively to customer complaints

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a nail artist?**
A: The annual salary for a beginner nail artist is approximately 18 million to 24 million KRW, increasing with experience and skill.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a nail artist in Korea?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is mainly required, and recognition as a skilled technical worker is necessary. Certain experience and certifications are also required.
