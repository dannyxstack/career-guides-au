# Nail Artist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 51132 (KECO)**

Profession specializing in nail and toenail beauty care, offering services like nail care, artificial nails, and art designs.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person with manual dexterity and excellent design sense
- Person skilled in customer service and interpersonal relations
- Person who is meticulous and patient

**Who is not suited**
- Person who finds it difficult to sit and work for long periods
- Person who reacts sensitively to customer complaints

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a nail artist?**
A: The annual salary for a beginner nail artist is approximately 18 million to 24 million KRW, increasing with experience and skill.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a nail artist in Korea?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is mainly required, and recognition as a skilled technical worker is necessary. Certain experience and certifications are also required.
