## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a railway locomotive repairman?**
A: Entry-level: about 28-34 million won, mid-level: 35-45 million won, senior: 45-55 million won, increasing with experience and certifications.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain a work visa for this job?**
A: Yes, it is possible. Employment is available under the E-7 (Specific Activities) visa, requiring relevant experience and qualifications. However, due to the nature of the railway sector, Korean language proficiency and completion of safety training are important.

**Q: What educational background is required for this job?**
A: High school graduation or higher is acceptable, but graduation from a railway-related college or holding a Railway Vehicle Maintenance Technician certificate is advantageous for employment.
