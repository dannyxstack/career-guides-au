# Cadastral and Surveying Technicians — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 14061 (KECO)**

Specialists who collect and analyze cadastral (land) survey and spatial information to create drawings, measure land boundaries and areas, and manage various cadastral records.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a cadastral/survey technician?**
A: Entry-level (0-3 years experience) approximately KRW 28-35 million, senior (10+ years) around KRW 50-70 million, with additional allowances for professional engineer certification.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a surveyor in Korea?**
A: E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is common, and relevant certification such as Land Surveyor and proof of experience are essential. It is also possible to convert to an F-2 (Points System) visa.

**Q: What education is required to obtain a Land Surveyor certification?**
A: You need to graduate from a junior college or 4-year university in a field such as cadastral studies or surveying, or complete relevant credits through a credit bank system, and then pass the written and practical exams.
