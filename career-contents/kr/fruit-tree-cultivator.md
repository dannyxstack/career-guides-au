# Fruit Tree Cultivator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 90131 (KECO)**

Professionals who cultivate and manage fruit trees such as apples, pears, and grapes for harvest. They perform tasks including fertilization, pesticide spraying, pruning, harvesting, and sorting.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After 5–10 years of farming experience, one may become an independent farm owner or advance to smart farm manager after completing training. Entry via return-to-farming programs is also common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple weeding
- Irrigation and fertigation automation
- Drone pesticide spraying

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Harvest timing decisions (AI analysis)
- Pest and disease diagnosis (image recognition)
- Smart farm remote control

**Human moat:**

- Tree pruning sense
- Harvest sorting know-how
- On-site judgment in response to climate

**Skills to build:**

- Smart farm operation
- Use of drones and ICT
- Export quarantine and GAP certification

**Key growth areas:**

- Fruit cultivation
- Smart farm
- Export expansion
- Eco-friendly certification

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average starting annual salary for a fruit tree cultivator?**
A: Approximately 22-28 million KRW for beginners, and for seasonal work, around 100,000-150,000 KRW per day.

**Q: Can foreigners work as fruit crop growers?**
A: Yes, it is possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) and Seasonal Worker visas, and smart farm technicians are eligible for E-7.

**Q: Is there a promotion path for fruit crop growers?**
A: After 5-10 years of experience, one can grow into an independent farm owner or smart farm manager, and completing a return-to-farming training is advantageous.
