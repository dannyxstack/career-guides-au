# Lighting Technician — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41671 (KECO)**

A specialized technical professional who installs and operates lighting in film, broadcasting, and stage settings to create atmosphere and visual effects.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Employment is expected to grow modestly due to increased demand for K-content and revitalization of performances and exhibitions. However, job numbers are limited as most work is in small-scale settings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Typically starts as a lighting assistant, then advances to lighting technician, senior lighting technician, and lighting director. A high proportion are freelancers, and experience and portfolio are important.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic lighting setup automation
- Repetitive lighting pattern adjustments

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Lighting design simulation
- Real-time lighting correction and monitoring
- Energy efficiency optimization

**Human moat:**

- Intuitive lighting direction based on on-site conditions
- Artistic sense and creative use of light
- Collaboration skills with stage/film production teams

**Skills to build:**

- Lighting simulation software
- Understanding DMX control systems
- LED/smart lighting technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- Video content
- Performances/festivals
- LED/smart lighting
- K-culture

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone with creativity and technical sensibility
- Someone who can accept night/weekend work and frequent travel
- Someone with excellent teamwork and quick reflexes

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who prefers a regular lifestyle and stable workplace
- Someone who finds rapid field response and physical exertion burdensome

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting annual salary for a lighting technician?**
A: For junior (0-3 years), the annual salary is about 24-30 million KRW; freelancers' pay varies greatly by project.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a lighting technician?**
A: E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is the most common, and you can apply with qualifications as a professional in culture/arts. A degree or 5+ years of experience is required.

**Q: What career paths are available after gaining experience as a lighting technician?**
A: You can advance to senior lighting technician, lighting director, or broadcast/film lighting team leader, and also work as an instructor at universities or academies.
