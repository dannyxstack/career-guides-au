# Mobile Phone Repairer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 84121 (KECO)**

A professional who diagnoses hardware failures in mobile communication devices such as smartphones and repairs them through part replacement, soldering, and software updates.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

One can start by opening a small repair shop or working as a technician at a dealership; obtaining certifications allows for a move to an authorized service center, and with experience, promotion to team leader or center manager is possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Software reset
- Initialization
- Simple parts replacement
- Warranty status check

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fault diagnosis
- Precision soldering
- Circuit analysis
- Defective part identification

**Human moat:**

- Precision manual soldering
- Micro-component replacement
- Building customer trust
- Handling non-standard failures

**Skills to build:**

- BGA Rework
- Micro Soldering
- Data Recovery
- Customer Communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Smartphone repair
- Parts procurement
- Precision soldering
- Software recovery

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent manual dexterity
- People who enjoy disassembling and assembling electronic devices
- People with patience in customer service

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer static office work
- People who easily get bored with repetitive tasks

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a mobile phone repair technician?**
A: Entry-level: 24-30 million KRW; center managers with 7+ years: 40-55 million KRW. Income varies greatly for independent repair shops.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a mobile phone repair technician?**
A: Yes. Skilled repair technicians can work on an E-7 visa; E-9 visas are limited under the Employment Permit System.

**Q: What is the outlook for this occupation?**
A: Demand for smartphone repairs is steady, but growth may stall due to large service centers and the rise of DIY repair culture.
