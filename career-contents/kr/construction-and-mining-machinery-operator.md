# Construction and Mining Machinery Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70401 (KECO)**

An occupation involving the operation of heavy construction equipment such as excavators, bulldozers, and cranes for earthmoving, excavation, and transport tasks.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who prefer outdoor work
- People who are interested in machinery operation and follow safety rules well
- People with good physical strength and patience for repetitive tasks

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer office work or static environments
- People sensitive to high-altitude work, dust, or poor environments

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary for beginner operators?**
A: About 24 to 35 million KRW for entry-level (0-3 years), with possible night/special work allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners work as construction machinery operators?**
A: Limitedly possible with E-9 visa; possible conversion to E-7 visa for skilled workers, but Korean language ability and qualifications required.

**Q: How can I get promoted in this job?**
A: After gaining experience and obtaining large equipment licenses (e.g., crane over 25 tons), you can transition to site manager or safety manager.
