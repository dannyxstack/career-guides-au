# Nursing Assistant — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30751 (KECO)**

A specialized medical professional who performs patient care, medical assistance, specimen collection, and other medical tasks under the guidance of a doctor or nurse.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 350,000. Due to aging population and increasing medical demand, employment of nursing assistants is expected to steadily grow. Demand in nursing homes and home care outside hospitals is rising.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After obtaining a nursing assistant certificate, employment is possible in long-term care hospitals, general hospitals, clinics, etc. With experience, promotion to lead nursing assistant, training supervisor, etc., is possible. Transition to registered nurse is also possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Sorting medical records
- Labeling specimens

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Patient monitoring
- Medication assistance

**Human moat:**

- Personal care
- Emotional empathy
- Physical interventions

**Skills to build:**

- Infection control
- Geriatric nursing
- Communication
- End-of-life care

**Key growth areas:**

- Long-term care hospitals
- Home nursing
- Aging population
- Nursing workforce

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are patient and interested in patient care
- People who value teamwork and have good physical strength

**Who is not suited**
- People who are uncomfortable handling blood or wounds
- People who dislike night shifts or shift work

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a nursing assistant?**
A: Entry-level about 24–30 million won; with experience, up to 45 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work as nursing assistants?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but Korean language proficiency and certification are required, and visa issuance is limited.

**Q: How do you obtain a nursing assistant certificate?**
A: Must complete a 1-year course at a designated educational institution and pass the exam of the Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute.
