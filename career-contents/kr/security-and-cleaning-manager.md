# Security and Cleaning Manager — career analysis · South Korea

A management professional responsible for overseeing security and cleaning operations in buildings or facilities, including personnel management, scheduling, quality inspection, and customer response.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Skills to build:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for security and cleaning managers?**
A: Beginner approx. 24-30 million won, intermediate 30-36 million won, advanced 36-45 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job in this occupation?**
A: E-7 visa can be used to hire skilled managers, but on-site security and cleaning are limited to E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for promotion?**
A: Certifications such as security instructor, cleaning manager, and industrial safety engineer are advantageous, and experience is important.
