# Prison Officer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 24031 (KECO)**

Supports the correction, reformation, and social reintegration of inmates in prisons and detention centers, and maintains order and safety within the facility.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 21,500. Due to policies on expanding correctional staff and a stagnant inmate population, new recruitment continues, but competition rates are high and promotion stagnation is worsening.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After appointment as a correctional public official, promotion is based on work performance and experience; transfer to the Correctional Headquarters or regional correctional agencies is possible; some specialize as professional correctional officers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Inmate information entry and classification
- Simple CCTV monitoring
- Regulatory document writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Risk assessment and behavior prediction
- Security system management
- Education program analysis

**Human moat:**

- Psychological issue response
- Crisis judgment and human network
- Trust-building counseling

**Skills to build:**

- Psychological counseling
- Data analysis
- Conflict mediation
- Emergency treatment

**Key growth areas:**

- Increasing correctional personnel
- Expanding professional correctional officers
- Recidivism reduction policy
- Strengthening inmate human rights

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with high stress tolerance and patience
- People with excellent interpersonal and conflict resolution skills
- People with a strong sense of public service

**Who is not suited**
- People sensitive to authority and who prefer a free environment
- People with insufficient physical strength or difficulty in emotional control

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting annual salary for a prison officer?**
A: Approximately 28-35 million won for Grade 7, including various allowances it is around 35 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners become prison officers?**
A: Only Korean nationals can become general public officials; permanent residents are also ineligible for regular recruitment.

**Q: Can a high school graduate become a prison officer?**
A: Yes, you can apply for the Grade 7 open competitive exam with a high school diploma or higher, but the competition rate is high and there is a physical fitness test.
