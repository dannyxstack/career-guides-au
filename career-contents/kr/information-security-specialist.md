# Information Security Specialist — career analysis · South Korea

A professional who protects information systems of companies and institutions, responds to cyber attacks, and establishes security policies.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an information security professional?**
A: Entry-level: 30-40 million KRW, mid-level: 45-65 million KRW, senior: 70 million KRW or more, with significant variation by experience and company size.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an information security professional in Korea?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is common, requiring a degree and experience. You may also enter on a D-10 (Job Seeker) visa and find employment.
