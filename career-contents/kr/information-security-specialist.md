# Information Security Specialist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 13501 (KECO)**

A professional who protects information systems of companies and institutions, responds to cyber attacks, and establishes security policies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

AI will automate some security monitoring and incident response, but strategic planning and complex responses remain the role of human experts, so overall AI will assist rather than replace.

Primarily employed by information security companies, financial institutions, and public organizations; can advance to security team leader, CISO, etc. based on experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- General Log Analysis
- Simple Penetration Test Automation
- Part of True/False Positive Classification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Threat Detection and Response
- Vulnerability Analysis
- Security Automation

**Human moat:**

- Understanding Business Context
- Legal and Regulatory Compliance
- Creative Attack Response

**Skills to build:**

- Understanding AI/ML Models
- Cloud Security
- Threat Intelligence
- Security Automation Tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Cybersecurity
- Cloud Security
- Data Privacy
- AI Security

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an information security professional?**
A: Entry-level: 30-40 million KRW, mid-level: 45-65 million KRW, senior: 70 million KRW or more, with significant variation by experience and company size.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an information security professional in Korea?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is common, requiring a degree and experience. You may also enter on a D-10 (Job Seeker) visa and find employment.
