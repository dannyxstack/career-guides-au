# Vegetable & Special Crop Grower — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 90121 (KECO)**

Cultivates, manages, and harvests vegetables and special crops (ginseng, mushrooms, etc.) in open fields or facilities, performing soil management, pest control, and post-harvest tasks.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of vegetable and specialty crop cultivators?**
A: Depending on experience, entry-level is 30-35 million won, mid-level is 35-45 million won, and senior-level is 45-60 million won, with significant variation by crop and scale.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job in this occupation in Korea?**
A: Yes. They mainly enter on an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, and after 5+ years can switch to an E-7-4 (Skilled Worker) visa. Overseas Koreans can also use an F-4 visa.

**Q: What is the future outlook for this job?**
A: Labor shortages will sustain employment, but smart farm adoption will raise skill requirements. Automation will reduce simple tasks, but skilled workers will become more valuable.
