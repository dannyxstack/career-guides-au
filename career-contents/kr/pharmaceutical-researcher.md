# Pharmaceutical Researcher — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 12212 (KECO)**

A professional who studies the efficacy and safety of new drug development and existing medications, a key workforce in the pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical industry.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a pharmaceutical researcher?**
A: For entry-level, about 35-50 million KRW; for experienced, 50-70 million KRW; and for senior researchers with over 10 years, 70-120 million KRW. It's higher at large corporations and foreign pharmaceutical companies.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job as a pharmaceutical researcher in Korea?**
A: Yes, it's possible. It qualifies for an E-7 visa in the pharmaceutical and biotech fields, and requires a pharmacist license or a relevant master's/doctoral degree. Korean language skills and research experience are important.

**Q: Is a pharmacist license absolutely necessary to become a pharmaceutical researcher?**
A: Most research institutes and pharmaceutical companies require a pharmacist license, and graduation from a college of pharmacy is basic. Some basic research fields may not require a license, but competitiveness is low.
