# Civil Engineering Supervisor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 14034 (KECO)**

A professional engineer who supervises and reviews whether the design and construction of civil engineering works comply with design documents and relevant laws, and manages quality and safety.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Promotion from junior supervisor → intermediate supervisor → senior supervisor → head of supervision office, with experience and certifications (e.g., Construction Safety Engineer) being important.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple Review of Design Documents
- Daily Safety Inspection Record Keeping
- Automation of Progress Payment Calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- On-site Safety Risk Prediction
- Real-time Quality Data Analysis
- Automatic Generation of Supervision Reports

**Human moat:**

- Intuition and Experience in On-site Situations
- Final Judgment Involving Legal Responsibility
- Stakeholder Coordination Skills

**Skills to build:**

- Utilizing AI Supervision Tools
- Data Analysis
- BIM-based Supervision
- Risk Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart Construction
- Enhanced Safety Management
- Aging Infrastructure Maintenance
- Supervisory Expertise

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented and analytical person

- Person sensitive to safety and regulations

**Who is not suited**
- Person who dislikes repetitive paperwork

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much is the annual salary for a civil engineering supervisor?**
A: Entry-level about 30–40 million KRW, mid-level 40–55 million KRW, senior 55–80 million KRW, additional allowances may apply for large projects.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a civil engineering supervisor?**
A: E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is common and requires a technical certification from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport and a sponsoring company. F-2 (Residence) visa also allows for work.

**Q: What is the outlook for this occupation?**
A: Although new SOC orders are decreasing, steady jobs are expected due to maintenance and safety management demands for existing infrastructure. Acquiring digital skills like smart construction is advantageous.
