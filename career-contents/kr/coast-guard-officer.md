## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a maritime police officer?**
A: Starting as a patrol officer, approximately 35–42 million won (including allowances); senior officers above inspector earn 60–80 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners become maritime police officers?**
A: Under current law, maritime police officers are South Korean civil servants; only Korean nationals can apply. Even permanent residents (F-5) without citizenship are ineligible.

**Q: What preparation is needed to become a maritime police officer?**
A: Applicants must pass a written exam (Korean history, English, Constitution, etc.), physical fitness test, and interview. Majors in maritime police science or related fields are advantageous.
