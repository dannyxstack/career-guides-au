# Kimchi & Side Dish Manufacturing Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 87221 (KECO)**

Skilled professionals who produce traditional fermented foods such as kimchi, pickles, and salted seafood, as well as various side dishes, working in environments ranging from small-scale cottage industries to large food factories.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 58,000. With the globalization of Korean cuisine and rising demand for home meal replacements (HMR), demand for skilled workers remains stable, but automation is expected to reduce simple labor. Attrition among older workers and insufficient new entrants persist.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

During the first 1–2 years after hiring, workers learn ingredient preparation and basic fermentation skills, then can advance to skilled positions developing recipes, managing quality, or overseeing production lines.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Material sorting and simple packaging
- Label attachment
- Quantitative input
- Raw material washing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fermentation status monitoring data analysis
- AI quality inspection assistance
- Recipe optimization simulation
- Hygiene management automation

**Human moat:**

- Sense of fermentation ripeness judgment
- Taste adjustment experience
- Traditional recipe know-how
- Ability to detect customer preferences

**Skills to build:**

- Basics of fermentation engineering
- HACCP Understanding
- Automation Equipment Operation
- Data Analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- Globalization of Korean cuisine
- Home meal replacement (HMR)
- Fermented foods
- Vegan side dishes

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are dexterous and meticulous with repetitive tasks
- People with a strong interest in fermented foods and traditional cuisine
- People who can work night shifts or weekends

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer static office work
- People sensitive to uneven work environments (humidity, odors)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a Kimchi and Side Dish Manufacturing Worker?**
A: For entry-level, about 24-30 million won; skilled workers 30-38 million won; team leaders 38-50 million won, with large gaps between SMEs and large corporations.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation with an E-9 visa?**
A: Yes, it is possible. You can work at food manufacturing companies under the Employment Permit System (E-9), and can also switch to the Skilled Worker (E-7-1) visa.

**Q: What is the career progression like in this occupation?**
A: After 2-3 years of initial experience, you can become a skilled worker, then advance to quality control or production line manager; obtaining certifications is advantageous.
