# Dietitian — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30501 (KECO)**

Dietitians are healthcare professionals who plan menus and manage nutrition for patients and meal service recipients, working in hospitals, schools, food service companies, etc.


---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dietitian License | Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute | Required | License from Minister of Health and Welfare after passing national exam |
| Clinical Dietitian Certification | Korean Dietetic Association | Optional | Optional, beneficial for hospital employment |
| Public Health Nutritionist Certification | Korean Dietetic Association | Optional | Beneficial for public health center and government careers |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a dietitian?**
A: Entry-level approximately 26-32 million KRW before tax, mid-level 32-42 million KRW, senior-level 42-55 million KRW. Varies by hospital and foodservice company.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a dietitian?**
A: Professional dietitians can work under an E-7 visa, but need a Korean dietitian license, certain experience, and employer approval from the Ministry of Labor.

**Q: What education is required to become a dietitian?**
A: You must major in food and nutrition at a junior college or 4-year university, pass the national exam, and obtain a dietitian license.
