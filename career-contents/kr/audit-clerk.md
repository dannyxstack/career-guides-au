# Audit Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02641 (KECO)**

An expert who performs internal audit tasks to check and evaluate whether an organization's financial and operational processes comply with laws and regulations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Starting as a junior audit clerk, one can advance to mid-level audit team leader and senior internal audit expert. Obtaining CPA or CIA certifications allows career moves to external audit firms or consulting.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Financial Data Collection and Classification
- Basic Anomaly Detection Report Generation
- Standardized Audit Checklist Verification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Data Sampling and Analysis
- Risk Assessment Modeling
- Audit Evidence Tracking and Visualization

**Human moat:**

- Strategic Judgment and Decision Making
- Understanding Organizational Culture and Insider Fraud Detection
- Unstructured Problem Solving and Stakeholder Consultation

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis (Python, SQL)
- IT Audit and Cybersecurity Basics
- Critical Thinking and Communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Internal Control
- Risk Management
- IT Audit
- Compliance

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for audit clerks?**
A: Entry level approximately 30~40 million KRW, mid-level 45~60 million KRW, senior 65~90 million KRW, with significant differences based on certifications and experience.

**Q: Can foreigners work as audit clerks?**
A: Generally, the E-7 visa allows professional work, but internal audit has entry barriers due to language and legal understanding requirements. Korean language skills and accounting qualifications are necessary.

**Q: What certifications are advantageous for becoming an audit clerk?**
A: Certifications like Level 1 Computerized Accounting Operator, CPA, CIA are advantageous, with CPA being preferred by large corporations.
