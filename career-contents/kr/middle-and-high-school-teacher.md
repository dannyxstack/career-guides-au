# Middle and high school teacher — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 21211 (KECO)**

A professional educator who teaches subjects, provides life guidance, and career counseling to students in middle and high schools.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| College of Education | 4 years | $4,000,000~$10,000,000 (Annual tuition: national universities at the lowest level) |
| Teaching course (general university) | 4 years | $5,000,000~$12,000,000 (No additional cost for teaching course, but tuition varies) |
| Graduate School of Education | 2 years | $6,000,000~$15,000,000 (Master's program, annual tuition) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary School Regular Teacher Grade 2 Certificate | Ministry of Education | Required | Obtained after completing College of Education or teaching course |
| Passing the appointment exam | City/Provincial Office of Education | Required | Required for public school teacher appointment |
| Teacher Certificate (Private) | Relevant school corporation | Optional | Private schools have own standards |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 420,000. Due to the sharp decline in the school-age population from low birth rates, teacher recruitment is decreasing, but some replacement demand is expected to persist due to adjustments in teacher college enrollment and retirements.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After obtaining a regular teacher certificate, one is appointed to public schools through the appointment exam. Promotion follows the order of teacher → head teacher → vice principal → principal. Private schools follow their own regulations.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple grading and grade processing
- Basic Q&A
- Searching and organizing class materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Career counseling data analysis
- Automating lesson feedback

**Human moat:**

- Unstructured problem solving

**Skills to build:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much is the annual salary of middle and high school teachers?**
A: Starting salary (Step 1) is around 35 million KRW, and over 15 years, it rises to over 60 million KRW. Private schools may have some differences.

**Q: Can foreigners become teachers in Korea?**
A: For regular middle and high schools, the employment exam must be taken in Korean, making it virtually impossible; foreign schools or international schools are possible with an E-7 visa.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a teacher?**
A: One must complete a college of education or a teaching course at a general university, obtain a secondary school teacher level 2 certificate, and pass the city/provincial education office employment exam for public schools.
