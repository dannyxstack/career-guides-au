## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a chemical product production machine operator?**
A: Depending on experience, entry-level is about 28-35 million KRW, mid-level 36-45 million KRW, and senior 47-60 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain a Korean work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes. You can enter on an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, then switch to E-7 under the skilled worker points system or apply for an F-2 (Residence) visa.

**Q: Is AI likely to replace this job?**
A: Some process automation is underway, but handling atypical situations and safety management require humans, so complete replacement is difficult.
