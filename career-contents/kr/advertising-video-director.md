# Advertising Video Director — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41614 (KECO)**

A professional who plans the concept of an advertisement, directs the overall filming, and completes the final video. Collaborates with a creative director to oversee ad production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Typically starts as an assistant director or PD, builds experience, and is promoted to advertising video director. May work as a freelancer or become a permanent director at a major production company.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic video editing
- Automatic storyboard generation
- Simple color correction
- Partial shooting scheduling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Concept planning
- Pre-production simulation
- AI-based budget management

**Human moat:**

- Creative intuition
- On-site leadership
- Human-centered emotional direction
- Client negotiation

**Skills to build:**

- AI video generation tools (e.g., Stable Video)
- Data-driven advertising effectiveness prediction
- Ability to produce short-form content in various formats

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Advertising
- Short-form Content
- Branded Content
- AI Video Production

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an advertising video director?**
A: It varies significantly by experience and employment type; for mid-level, it is about 45 to 60 million KRW.

**Q: What visa is needed for a foreigner to work as an advertising video director?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is required, and an invitation from a Korean production company along with a hiring justification letter is mandatory.

**Q: Will AI replace the job of an advertising video director?**
A: While planning and editing tasks will be partially automated, creative directing and human relationship management will remain.
