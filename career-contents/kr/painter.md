## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those passionate about original expression
- Those who can continue artistic work self-directedly
- Those who value artistic value over fixed income

**Who is not suited**
- Those who want stable income and employment
- Those who prefer a regular work environment

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a painter?**
A: Initially, it ranges from 12 to 24 million KRW per year, with significant differences depending on experience and recognition. Senior artists may earn over 100 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as painters in Korea?**
A: There is no visa specifically for pure painting activities. The E-7 visa is limited to related instructor activities, and is only possible for holders of F-2 or F-4 visas.

**Q: Do I have to graduate from an art college to become a painter?**
A: It's not mandatory, but university education and exhibition experience greatly help in art activities and networking.
