# Mail Carrier — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62421 (KECO)**

A profession that collects, sorts, and delivers mail to recipients, primarily employed by the post office.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school graduate | 0 years | $0~$0 (No separate training) |
| Mail carrier training | 3 months | $500,000~$1,000,000 (Training costs covered by Korea Post) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Driver's license | National Police Agency | Required | Must have at least a Class 2 ordinary license |
| Mail carrier certification | Korea Post | Optional | Awarded upon completion of in-house training |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 28,000. Employment is gradually decreasing due to declining mail volume and digital transformation. New hiring is limited except for replacements for retirees.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Internal promotion is possible within the Korea Post as a career position, moving from mail carrier to team leader to section chief, but competition is intense due to limited positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Address classification
- Delivery route optimization

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Delivery record management
- Customer response automation

**Human moat:**

- On-site delivery
- Weather response
- Direct response to recipients

**Skills to build:**

- AI-based delivery system operation
- Basics of data analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining mail volume
- Digital transformation
- Aging workforce
- Replacement of retirees

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: For entry-level (0-3 years), annual salary is 25-30 million KRW according to civil servant seniority-based pay.

**Q: Is it possible through immigration or work visa?**
A: Possible with E-9 visa in some areas but very limited, not a general immigration route.

**Q: What is the future outlook for this job?**
A: Employment is expected to gradually decrease due to declining mail volume, with only a few new hires.
