## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a sociology researcher?**
A: Entry-level: about 30 million KRW, mid-level: 45 million, senior: 65 million.

**Q: Can I get an employment visa as a sociology researcher?**
A: Employment at research institutions is possible with an E-7 visa, but academic competition is fierce, making it difficult. Searching for a job with a D-10 visa after studying abroad is common.

**Q: Can I become a sociology researcher without a master's degree?**
A: A research assistant position is possible, but a master's degree or higher is required for a regular researcher position.
