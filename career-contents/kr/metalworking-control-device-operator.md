# Metalworking Control Device Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 82111 (KECO)**

This is a skilled worker who operates CNC lathes, milling machines, and other numerical control devices for metalworking to produce parts.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Starting as an entry-level operator, one can advance to skilled worker, shift leader, or process manager; mastering multi-axis machining leads to higher wages.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple repetitive machining
- Manual tool change
- Basic measurement

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Machining path optimization
- Real-time quality monitoring
- Predictive equipment maintenance

**Human moat:**

- Ability to handle non-standard defects
- Micron-level tolerance adjustment
- On-site experience-based problem solving

**Skills to build:**

- CAM software (NX, Mastercam)
- Basic PLC
- Tool life management

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC automation
- Precision machining
- Aerospace and defense machining
- Mold and parts manufacturing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy precise manual work and machine operation
- People who pursue technical skill rather than simple repetitive tasks
- People who can endure night shifts and rotating shifts

**Who is not suited**
- People who find physical labor burdensome
- People who have difficulty following safety regulations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a Metal Processing Control Device Operator?**
A: Entry-level approx. 28–35 million KRW, mid-level 36–48 million KRW, senior-level 48–65 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Possible with E-7-1 (Skilled Technician) visa, but experience and qualification requirements are strict. E-9 (Non-professional Employment) is limited.

**Q: Is there a required certification?**
A: CNC lathe/milling technician certification is required; having a mechanical machining master certification is advantageous for promotion.
