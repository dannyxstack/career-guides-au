# Tailor (Custom Suit & Jacket) — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 86321 (KECO)**

Tailors (custom suit & jacket) are skilled workers who produce custom suits and jackets, performing client-specific pattern making, cutting, sewing, and finishing tasks.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary for a tailor or dressmaker?**
A: Entry-level about 24-30 million won, skilled technicians can earn over 60 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work as tailors or dressmakers in Korea?**
A: Possible with E-7 visa but requirements are strict; E-9 visa is limited to general sewing, making professional suit making difficult.

**Q: Will AI replace tailor or dressmaker jobs?**
A: Automated cutting is being introduced, but custom patterns, alterations, and high-grade finishing require human skills, so the risk of replacement is low.
