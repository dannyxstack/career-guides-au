## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a display R&D researcher?**
A: Entry-level 45-60M KRW, mid-level 70-95M KRW, senior 100-150M KRW.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a display R&D engineer in Korea?**
A: The E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is suitable, requiring a bachelor's + 5 years of experience or a master's degree or higher.

**Q: What are the prospects for this job?**
A: The outlook is good due to sustained demand for next-generation display technologies like OLED, but competition is fierce.
