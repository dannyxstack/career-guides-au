# Textile Testing Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15621 (KECO)**

A specialist who tests and analyzes the physical and chemical properties of textile raw materials and products to evaluate quality, and supports production process improvement and new material development.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

After joining as a junior testing engineer, promotion to senior or principal researcher is possible based on experience. Those with a master's or higher in textile, chemical, or polymer fields can advance to R&D centers or quality control manager positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple input of basic physical property test data
- Some repetitive sample pretreatment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Test result analysis and report writing
- Outlier detection and cause estimation
- Data analysis for process optimization

**Human moat:**

- Experience with non-standard specimen handling
- Intuitive judgment for complex defects
- Customized test design for clients

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis with Python/R
- AI-based quality prediction
- Smart textile test methods

**Key growth areas:**

- Functional textiles
- Smart textiles
- Eco-friendly dyeing
- Textile recycling

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy scientific analysis
- People who can focus on detailed work
- People with strong interest in textile and chemical materials

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike simple repetitive tasks
- People who find overtime or shift work burdensome

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a textile testing engineer?**
A: For entry-level, about 28-35 million won; mid-level, 38-50 million won; senior, 55-75 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain an employment visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, with an E-7 visa for professional talent, and domestic master's/Ph.D. graduates can use a D-10 job-seeking visa.

**Q: Are there any required certifications?**
A: No mandatory certifications, but related qualifications like Textile Engineer are advantageous for employment and promotion.
