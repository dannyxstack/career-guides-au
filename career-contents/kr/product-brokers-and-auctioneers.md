# Product brokers and auctioneers — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61251 (KECO)**

Professionals who mediate transactions or conduct auctions for various goods such as agricultural, fisheries, livestock products, used cars, and artworks; demand remains stable due to the growth of Korea's auction market and online trading platforms.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auctioneer license | Korea Agro-Fisheries & Food Trade Corporation | Required | Mandatory for agricultural and fisheries auctioneers |
| Brokerage registration | Korea Appraisal Board/Local government | Optional | Required in some fields such as real estate auctions |
| Licensed real estate agent | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Favorable for real estate brokerage |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Early career starts as a transaction or auction assistant; after 5-10 years, become an independent broker or certified auctioneer. Possible to work at large auction houses, agricultural cooperatives, fisheries cooperatives, etc., before starting a business.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Product information search and matching
- Basic pricing
- Contacting and managing auction participants

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Market analysis and forecasting
- Customer-customized recommendations
- Online auction management

**Human moat:**

- Human relationships and trust
- On-site auction know-how
- Complex negotiation skills

**Skills to build:**

- Online marketing
- Data analysis
- Digital platform utilization
- Negotiation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Online auction
- Agricultural and fisheries product distribution
- Used car trading
- Platform brokerage

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Sociable and good negotiator

- Prefers working independently

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes personal contact
- Dislikes risk-taking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: Starting annual salary is 24-35 million KRW for entry level, increasing significantly with experience and performance.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job in this profession in Korea?**
A: Professional auctioneers or brokers can apply for an E-7 visa, but Korean language skills and field experience are important, making it limited.

**Q: Are there required certifications?**
A: A certificate as an agricultural and fisheries product auctioneer is required, and additional qualifications may be needed depending on the field.
