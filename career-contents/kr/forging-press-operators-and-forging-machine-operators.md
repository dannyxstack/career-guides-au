# Forging Press Operators and Forging Machine Operators — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 82311 (KECO)**

Perform forging operations by heating and striking metal materials to shape them into desired forms, combining manual work and machine operation.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

AI and automation are gradually replacing repetitive and simple tasks in the forging process, but complete replacement is difficult for small-batch, multi-variety production or complex-shaped die work that requires human judgment and experience.

Acquire skilled techniques through field experience, then advance to supervisory roles such as foreman or plant manager, or transition to related fields like press mold.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple repetitive material input and output
- Mass forging of standardized products
- Heating furnace temperature monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based quality inspection
- Automated forging machine operation and assistance
- Process data analysis for optimization

**Human moat:**

- Experience and know-how in non-standard, complex shape forging
- Manual skills for die modification and maintenance
- Ability to handle unexpected situations

**Skills to build:**

- CNC forging machine operation
- Die design (CAD/CAM)
- Robot and automation system management
- Understanding of heat treatment and material properties

**Key growth areas:**

- Metalworking
- Shipbuilding
- Automotive Parts
- Hot Forging

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Physically fit and patient individuals
- People with mechanical interest and manual dexterity
- Those who can tolerate night shifts and shift work

**Who is not suited**
- Those who prefer creative tasks over precision work
- People who struggle with noisy, high-temp environments

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a forger?**
A: Entry-level: about 28-35 million KRW; skilled workers with 10+ years: 48-60 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as forgers?**
A: Non-professional Employment (E-9) visa allows simple forging in manufacturing, but skilled (E-7) requires career proof and is stringent.
