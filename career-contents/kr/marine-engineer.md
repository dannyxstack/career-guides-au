# Marine Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62123 (KECO)**

Operates and maintains engine, boiler, generator, and other machinery on a ship; oversees the engine room during voyages.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |

| Maritime University (Bachelor's) | 4 years | $0~$0 (National maritime universities: tuition-free or low cost) |
| Maritime Officer Training Institute (Associate's) | 2 years | $10,000,000~$20,000,000 (Private training cost) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marine Engineer License (Class 4 or higher) | Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries | Required | Separate from deck officer license; mandatory |
| STCW Basic Safety Training | Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries | Required | Mandatory training for shipboard service |
| GMDSS General Operator | Korea Coast Guard | Optional | Communication equipment operation qualification |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

AI and automation support condition monitoring and predictive maintenance of ship machinery, but complete replacement is difficult as human judgment and skill are essential for failure response and repair.

After obtaining 4th→3rd→2nd→1st class engineer licenses, one can advance to captain or shore management positions, or build a career as a maritime officer.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Regular condition monitoring
- Simple inspection report writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fault diagnosis
- Maintenance planning
- Energy efficiency optimization

**Human moat:**

- Complex machinery repair
- Emergency response
- Regulatory compliance judgment

**Skills to build:**

- Automation system operation
- Data analysis
- Understanding integrated machinery control systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Shipping industry
- Shipbuilding industry
- Eco-friendly ships
- Automated ships

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a new entry-level marine engineer?**
A: For new entry-level (junior), including boarding allowances, it is 35 million to 45 million won.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a marine engineer in Korea?**
A: If you have a professional maritime officer license, you can work under an E-7 visa (Specific Activities); simple tasks are partially permitted under an E-9 visa.

**Q: What are the required certifications to become a ship engineer?**
A: A maritime officer license issued by the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries (Engineer 4th class or higher) is mandatory, and completion of STCW basic safety training is required.
