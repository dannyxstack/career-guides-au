# Assistant Teacher — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 21291 (KECO)**

A professional who assists homeroom teachers in kindergartens, elementary, middle, high schools, and special education institutions, supporting learning guidance, life guidance, and administrative tasks.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Childcare Teacher Level 2 Certificate | Korea Childcare Promotion Agency | Required | Required for kindergarten/daycare assistant roles |
| Special Education Assistant Training Certificate | Each City/Province Office of Education | Optional | Preferred when applying as a special class assistant teacher |
| Teacher's Certificate (Level 1 or 2 Regular Teacher) | Ministry of Education | Optional | Required for transition to regular teacher |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 85,000. As the school-age population declines due to low birth rates, the hiring of assistant teachers is decreasing, but demand is expected to remain in specialized fields such as special education, multicultural education, and after-school programs.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Depending on experience, one can transition to a fixed-term teacher or regular teacher, and can advance to a regular teacher by pursuing a graduate school of education or obtaining a teacher's certificate. Some also change careers to become professional counselors or educational administrative staff within schools.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Checking and recording student attendance
- Creating simple learning materials
- Guiding basic repetitive learning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based personalized learning assistance
- Understanding students through learning data analysis
- Managing online education platforms

**Human moat:**

- Emotional support and empathy skills
- Face-to-face interaction and conflict resolution
- Ability to handle unstructured situations

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in AI educational tools
- Data literacy
- Expertise in special and multicultural education

**Key growth areas:**

- Special education assistant
- Multicultural education
- After-school programs
- Neulbom School (extended care program)

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy interacting with children and have patience
- People who are interested in education and find rewarding to support students in need
- People who can flexibly handle various administrative and support tasks in school settings

**Who is not suited**
- People who want high salary and quick promotion
- People who dislike independent work direction rather than being instructed

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an assistant teacher?**
A: Entry-level pre-tax about 22 million to 28 million KRW, varies greatly by experience and employment type

**Q: Are there visas for foreigners to work as assistant teachers?**
A: Some possible with E-7 visa, but actual cases are very rare, mainly limited to F-2 and F-4 visa holders.

**Q: Are there any certifications required to become a teaching assistant?**
A: For kindergartens and daycare centers, a Level 2 childcare teacher certificate is mandatory; for school teaching assistants, there is no separate certification, but a related degree or training is advantageous.
