# Power Generation and Distribution Equipment Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 83301 (KECO)**

This occupation involves controlling, monitoring, and operating power generation and transmission/distribution facilities at power plants or substations, ensuring a stable electricity supply.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are meticulous and have high concentration
- People interested in machinery and electricity
- People who can adapt to shift work

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike repetitive simple tasks
- People who have difficulty with night shifts and shift work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for power generation and distribution device operators?**
A: The annual salary for entry-level (0-3 years) position at public enterprises is between 30 million KRW and 40 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job in this occupation?**
A: High-skilled electrical technicians can find employment with an E-7 visa, but domestic certifications and experience are required.

**Q: What certifications are needed for promotion?**
A: An electrician certificate is essential and provides extra points in internal promotion exams at power plants.
