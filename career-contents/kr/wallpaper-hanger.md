# Wallpaper Hanger — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70251 (KECO)**

A wallpaper hanger is a specialized construction craftsperson who applies wallpaper or sheet paper to walls in residences and commercial facilities. It requires manual precision and has steady demand at new construction and remodeling sites.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 80,000. Due to increased domestic home remodeling and interior demand, demand for wallpaper hangers is steady. However, new entrants are few, and an aging workforce may lead to future labor shortages.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career advancement: Craftsman → Skilled Worker → Site Foreman. It is common to gain experience while self-employed or affiliated with an interior company. Income varies significantly with skill level.

- Basic work planning

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

**Skills to build:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with manual dexterity and attention to detail
- People capable of working independently on-site
- People interested in interior design

**Who is not suited**
- People with acrophobia
- People who dislike repetitive tasks

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a wallpaper installer?**
A: Entry-level approx. 24-30M won, mid-level 30-40M won, senior 40-55M won. Due to the nature of daily work, variation by work days is large.

**Q: Can foreigners work as wallpaper installers?**
A: Possible with E-9 visa but quota limited. Skilled workers can also use E-7 visa. Overseas Koreans (F-4) can work freely.

**Q: What is the outlook for wallpaper installers?**
A: Steady due to increasing interior demand, but aging workforce raises concerns about new labor shortage. High income possible with skill level.
