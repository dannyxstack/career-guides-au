# Ticket seller and lottery ticket seller — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61621 (KECO)**

This occupation involves selling tickets or lottery tickets at venues such as performance halls, transportation facilities, and lottery outlets, as well as handling customer service and store maintenance.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 45,000. Due to automation and the spread of kiosks, ticket seller jobs are declining, but on-site services requiring face-to-face interaction and lottery sales are expected to persist.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Starting as a ticket seller, one can gain experience in customer service or store management, but promotion opportunities are limited, and the need for career transition due to automation is growing.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Ticket Sales
- Simple Customer Response
- Cash/Card Payment Processing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Lotto Sales Strategy Development
- Store environment management
- Resolve Customer Complaints

**Human moat:**

- Building trust with customers
- Ability to flexibly respond to situations
- Understanding and resolving complex inquiries

**Skills to build:**

- Customer Service Skills
- Lottery Product Knowledge
- Basic computer skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Unmanned
- Kiosk
- In-Person Service
- Lottery Ticket Sales

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Patient people who enjoy customer service
- People who adapt well to simple repetitive tasks
- People interested in personal services

**Who is not suited**
- People not sensitive to automation and technological changes
- People seeking promotion and high income

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a ticket seller?**
A: Initial level approximately 20-24 million KRW before tax, with limited wage growth due to automation.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this job with an E-9 visa?**
A: Partially possible, but ticket seller is often excluded from E-9 visa eligible industries; mainly suitable for F-2 or F-4 visa holders.

**Q: Is career advancement possible in this job?**
A: Promotion to store manager or transfer to similar sales positions is possible, but the outlook is not bright due to job losses from automation.
