# Statistical Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02933 (KECO)**

A statistical clerk collects and analyzes various survey and administrative data to compile statistics and organize reports.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a statistical clerk?**
A: It varies by experience: entry-level about KRW 24-32 million, mid-level KRW 34-48 million, and senior-level KRW 50-70 million.

**Q: What visa is needed for a foreigner to work as a statistical clerk?**
A: An E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is suitable, requiring a specialized degree and experience in statistics. Korean language skills are also important.

**Q: Are there any required certifications?**
A: Not mandatory, but having a Social Survey Analyst Level 2 or Information Processing Engineer is advantageous, and proficiency in statistical tools like SAS, R, and Python is key.
