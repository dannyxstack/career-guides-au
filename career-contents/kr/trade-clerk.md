# Trade Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02811 (KECO)**

A professional who performs overall international trade office tasks including export/import contract signing, customs clearance, shipping, and payment settlement.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| International Trade Specialist Level 1 | Korea International Trade Association | Optional | Certifies trade practical competency, advantageous for employment |
| Trade English Level 1 | Korea Chamber of Commerce and Industry | Optional | English trade document writing ability |
| Customs Broker | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Advanced professional qualification, performs customs clearance |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 210,000. Demand is steady due to Korea's high export dependency, but it is sensitive to global economic conditions and FTA changes. Digital transformation reduces simple tasks but increases demand for expertise.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented and proficient in document work
- Interested in foreign languages and overseas cultures
- Enjoys collaborating with various departments

**Who is not suited**
- Difficulty adapting to change
- Finds repetitive document work tedious

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a trade clerk?**
A: As of 2023, it is around 35 million to 45 million KRW, varying greatly by experience and company size.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a trade clerk?**
A: An E-7 visa is required, along with a relevant degree, experience, and employer recommendation. F-2 or F-5 visa holders can freely seek employment.

**Q: How can I get promoted from a trade clerk?**
A: It is advantageous to gain practical trade experience, acquire certifications such as International Trade Specialist and Trade English, and build experience in overseas business trips/dispatches.
