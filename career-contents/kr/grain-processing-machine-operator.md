# Grain Processing Machine Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 87331 (KECO)**

This job involves operating machines to process grains such as wheat and rice flour, including milling, mixing, and packaging.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with patience for repetitive tasks
- People with a strong interest in machine operation
- People who can work night shifts or rotating shifts

**Who is not suited**
- People who get bored easily with monotonous work
- People who find it hard to tolerate noise and dusty environments

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a grain processing machine operator?**
A: Approximately 25 million to 45 million KRW depending on experience, plus shift allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: Some manufacturers may hire with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, but it is not eligible for the E-7 (Skilled Worker) visa.

**Q: How can I get promoted in this field?**
A: Obtaining a machine maintenance certificate or completing production management training can lead to promotion to a line manager.
