# Emergency Medical Technician — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30711 (KECO)**

Emergency medical technicians (EMTs) are healthcare professionals who provide on-site and in-transit emergency care, assessing and treating patients in the pre-hospital phase.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 EMT | Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute | Required | National license, mandatory qualification under the Emergency Medical Service Act |
| Level 2 EMT | Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute | Optional | Limited scope of practice compared to Level 1 |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Level 1 EMTs can work in hospital emergency rooms, fire station ambulance crews, maritime police, etc., and can advance to professors at EMT training institutions or managers after experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- recording basic vital signs
- writing ambulance activity reports
- calculating drug dosages

**Where AI augments the role:**

- emergency decision-making (AI-assisted diagnosis)
- using telemedicine guidance systems
- predicting patient condition (AI algorithms)

**Human moat:**

- on-site judgment and intuition
- emotional rapport with patients and guardians
- physical emergency care (CPR, hemorrhage control, etc.)

**Skills to build:**

- using AI-based emergency medical software
- data analysis and decision-making
- telemedicine collaboration skills

**Key growth areas:**

- aging society
- expansion of emergency medical system
- demand for firefighter-paramedics
- infectious disease response

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Calm and excellent crisis management skills
- Good physical strength and values teamwork
- Deep humanity and strong spirit of service

**Who is not suited**
- Those averse to seeing blood or injuries
- Those who have difficulty adapting to sudden changes

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an EMT?**
A: Entry-level about 24-32 million KRW, 5 years experience about 32-42 million KRW, senior 42-55 million KRW, with additional allowances for firefighters.

**Q: Can foreigners work as EMTs in Korea?**
A: Yes. Foreigners recognized by the Minister of Health and Welfare can take the national EMT exam and work under an E-7 visa.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an EMT?**
A: Graduation from a college or higher in emergency medical technology, pass the national exam, and obtain a Level 1 EMT license.
