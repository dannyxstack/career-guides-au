# Cement and Mineral Product Production Machine Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 82631 (KECO)**

An occupation operating machinery to produce mineral materials such as cement, lime, gypsum, and concrete, essential for construction and industrial production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Starting as a machine operator, one can advance to production supervisor, quality control, or process manager; often via experience at large conglomerates or specialized construction companies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Raw material input and mixing operation
- Production line monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Production data analysis
- Equipment failure prediction and preventive maintenance

**Human moat:**

- Fault diagnosis and repair
- Safety management and emergency response
- Judgment based on field experience

**Skills to build:**

- PLC programming
- Understanding of process automation systems
- Data analysis and statistical quality control

**Key growth areas:**

- Linkage to construction economy
- Automation replacement
- Strengthening safety regulations
- Decline in skilled workforce

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary and annual raise rate like?**
A: The starting salary is about 28–34 million KRW, with 5–10% raises based on experience, but varies greatly by company.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job with an E-9 visa?**
A: Yes, it is possible through the Employment Permit System, requiring the Korean language test (TOPIK) and a health checkup.

**Q: Will jobs disappear due to automation?**
A: Simple repetitive tasks will be automated, but skilled jobs like maintenance and quality control are expected to remain.
