## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 350,000. Due to labor shortages and an aging population in the food service industry, demand for kitchen assistants remains steady, but minimum wage hikes and automation are expected to slow employment growth.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

With experience, can advance to cook, head chef, or restaurant owner. Many foreign workers (E-9) enter this field, and skilled workers can move to higher-paying kitchen positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Cutting and washing ingredients
- Simple food serving
- Kitchen cleaning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Kitchen hygiene inspection
- Food ingredient inventory management
- Cooking assistant tasks requiring judgment

**Human moat:**

- Experiential judgment on hygiene and safety
- Customer response ability
- Emergency response ability

**Skills to build:**

- Cooking skills and recipe understanding
- Hygiene and safety management
- Operation of kitchen automation equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- Workforce shortage in food service industry
- Senior hiring
- Unmanned/automation
- Demand for group meals

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Physically fit and detail-oriented person
- Person who can move quickly and endure simple repetitive tasks
- Person with thorough hygiene standards

**Who is not suited**
- Person insensitive to cleanliness
- Person unable to stay calm under stress

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a kitchen assistant?**
A: Starting salary is around 24–28 million won per year, with overtime and weekend pay possible up to 30 million won or more.

**Q: Can foreigners work as kitchen assistants?**
A: Possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, and can work legally through the Employment Permit System.

**Q: How to transition from kitchen assistant to cook?**
A: Obtaining a cooking skills certificate and gaining field experience makes promotion to cook or job change easier.
