# Family Medicine Physician — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 301113 (KECO)**

A family medicine physician provides comprehensive primary care for individuals and families, including prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and management of chronic diseases.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of a family medicine doctor?**
A: Junior (resident) about ₩40-50 million, mid-level (5th-year employed doctor) about ₩120-180 million, senior (10+ years private practitioner) about ₩200-500 million, varying greatly depending on practice success.

**Q: Can a foreign doctor work as a family medicine doctor in Korea?**
A: Yes, but obtaining a Korean medical license is mandatory, and employment is possible through an E-7 visa. Additional procedures may be required for recognition of specialist qualifications.

**Q: What is the required education period to become a family medicine doctor?**
A: Medical school (6 years), internship (1 year), residency (3 years) for a total of about 10 years. After that, a fellowship program can be optionally added.
