# Principal and Vice Principal of Middle/High Schools — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 01323 (KECO)**

Top administrators who oversee or assist curriculum, staff management, and school operations in middle/high schools. Also handle student guidance and external relations.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary School Teacher Certificate (Level 2 or higher) | Ministry of Education | Required | Basic requirement for principal/vice principal qualifications |
| Principal/Vice Principal Qualification Training | Institutions designated by the Ministry of Education (e.g., Korean Educational Development Institute) | Required | Vice principal requires vice principal qualification training; principal requires principal qualification training |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 12,300. Due to declining school-age population, the quota for principals and vice principals is shrinking, and promotion stagnation is expected to worsen. On the other hand, demand for innovative schools may increase slightly due to educational autonomy.

**Competition: very high (★★★★½).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Teacher (certified) → Head Teacher → Vice Principal (qualification training + promotion) → Principal (principal qualification training + appointment). Requires at least 20 years of teaching experience, and selection is based on career evaluation separate from the teacher recruitment exam.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard Report Writing
- Timetable and Schedule Optimization
- Simple Analysis of Educational Statistics

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Teacher Evaluation and Feedback
- Curriculum Design Support
- School Budget and Resource Allocation Decisions

**Human moat:**

- Vision Setting and Organizational Culture Building
- Parent and Community Relations Management
- Crisis Judgment and Coordination

**Skills to build:**

- Educational Data Analysis
- Digital Literacy
- Change Management Leadership

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining school-age population
- Expansion of innovation schools
- Strengthening Educational Autonomy
- Reduction of Teaching Staff

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of a principal?**
A: Public school principals use a salary-by-step system, ranging from 75 million to 120 million KRW (pre-tax); private school salaries vary based on school finances but are similar.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a principal or vice principal at a Korean school?**
A: Theoretically possible with an E-1 (Invited Professor) visa, etc., but legally and practically almost impossible due to domestic personnel preferences.

**Q: What is the typical career path to become a principal or vice principal?**
A: Teacher (10+ years) → Head teacher → Vice principal qualification training + promotion → Principal qualification training + promotion, taking at least 20 years.
