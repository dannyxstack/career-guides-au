# Public Health and Medical Administrator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 01341 (KECO)**

Oversees the operation and administration of medical institutions such as hospitals and nursing homes, managing finances, personnel, and service quality as a managerial professional.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Bachelor's in Health Administration | 4 years | $4,000,000~$8,000,000 (Difference between national and private universities) |
| Master's in Hospital Management/Healthcare Management (MHA/MPH) | 2 years | $8,000,000~$20,000,000 (Based on professional graduate school standards) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 25,000. Due to aging and increasing medical demand, the demand for healthcare administrators is expected to grow steadily, with recruitment expanding mainly in large hospitals.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Typically, after graduating from graduate school (MHA/MPH), one starts as a hospital administration intern, advancing to team leader, department head, and finally administrator; experience in medical institution accreditation is advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Scheduling Algorithms
- Financial Report Generation
- Basic Patient Data Analysis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Workforce Optimization
- Financial Forecasting
- Clinical Process Improvement

**Human moat:**

- Complex Healthcare Policy Interpretation

- Ethical Decision-Making

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- Health Information System Utilization
- Change Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Medical institution accreditation
- Digital Healthcare
- Patient Experience Management
- Telemedicine Administration

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People interested in organizational management and leadership
- People with excellent understanding of healthcare systems and problem-solving skills

**Who is not suited**
- People who find repetitive administrative tasks boring
- People who prefer individual work over communication

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a healthcare manager?**
A: Entry-level 35-45 million KRW, over 80 million KRW with 10 years of experience, higher in large hospitals

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this job?**
A: E-7 visa possible but limited, requiring active sponsorship from medical institutions and relevant degrees

**Q: What is the required degree?**
A: Generally a bachelor's degree in health administration or higher, master's (MHA) advantageous
