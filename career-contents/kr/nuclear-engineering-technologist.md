## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Primarily large corporations and public enterprises (KHNP, Doosan Enerbility). With experience, can advance into nuclear decommissioning, radiation management, or regulatory bodies (NSSC). Advanced degrees preferred.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard plant performance data analysis
- Regular safety inspection reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Anomaly early warning system
- Radiation dispersion prediction modeling
- Automated maintenance planning

**Human moat:**

- Radiation Emergency Response Planning
- Nuclear Decommissioning Field Experience

**Skills to build:**

- Big Data Analysis (Python, R)

- Digital Twin Simulation

**Key growth areas:**

- Radiation safety

- Nuclear fuel

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a nuclear engineering technician?**
A: Entry-level 40-55 million KRW, mid-level 55-80 million KRW, senior 80-150 million KRW. Public companies like KHNP boast high pay and stability.

**Q: Can foreigners work as nuclear engineering technicians in Korea?**
A: It is possible with an E-7-1 visa (Specific Activities), but international nuclear safety regulations and Korean language skills are required, and employment is difficult due to preference for locals.

**Q: What is the outlook for nuclear engineering technicians?**
A: Domestic nuclear plants are in maintenance and decommissioning stages, limiting new hiring. Instead, the trend is moving into nuclear decommissioning, radioactive waste management, and SMR fields.
