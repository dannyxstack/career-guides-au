## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Starting as a researcher, progressing to team leader, department head, and lab director requires project management skills; an MBA or Master's in Technology Management (MOT) is advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Structured research data collection
- Paper search and summarization
- Research fund settlement and administration

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Research performance prediction
- Project risk analysis
- Personnel placement optimization

**Human moat:**

- Organizational management leadership
- Strategic decision-making
- Ethical judgment
- Creative research planning

**Skills to build:**

- AI literacy
- Data analysis
- Technology Management (MOT)
- Change Management

**Key growth areas:**

- R&D investment expansion
- Demand for advanced technology
- Convergence research
- Strengthened government support

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a research manager?**
A: Entry-level: 45–60 million KRW, Mid-level: 60–90 million KRW, Senior: 90 million–150 million KRW.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work as research managers?**
A: Primarily an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa, requiring research experience and a degree.

**Q: What experience is needed to become a research manager?**
A: Typically 5+ years of research experience after a master's or doctorate, with project management experience being important.
