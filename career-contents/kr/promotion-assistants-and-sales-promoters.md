## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a promotional assistant?**
A: For entry-level, pre-tax annual salary is about 20–28 million KRW, and since many positions are part-time, actual income varies greatly depending on hours worked.

**Q: Can I get a Korean work visa for this job?**
A: E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa is possible, but simple promotional work is restricted and it does not qualify for Skilled Worker (E-7) visa.

**Q: Are there opportunities for promotion or career growth?**
A: You can transition into sales/marketing or build a career as an event planner, but conversion to full-time employment is rare.
