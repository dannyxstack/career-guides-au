# Entertainment Manager — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41721 (KECO)**

A professional manager who supports the overall management of celebrities such as singers and actors, including schedule coordination, contracts, public relations, and external affairs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are resilient and flexible under stress
- People with excellent interpersonal and negotiation skills
- People who can tolerate overtime and weekend work

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer a regular lifestyle

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an entertainer manager?**
A: Entry-level 25-35 million KRW, up to 80 million KRW with experience, but varies greatly by agency size.

**Q: Can foreigners work as entertainer managers?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but language and cultural understanding are important, and competition is fierce.

**Q: Are there required certifications?**
A: A Class 1 driver's license is required; experience is valued more than related degrees or certifications.
