# Librarian — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41321 (KECO)**

A professional who collects, organizes, preserves materials in libraries and provides information services to users.

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |

| Master's in Library and Information Science | 2 years | $12,000,000~$30,000,000 (Average graduate tuition) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 2 Librarian Certificate | Korean Library Association | Required | Acquired upon completing bachelor's degree and designated courses |
| Professional Librarian | Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism | Required | Nationally recognized qualification, mandatory for public libraries |
| Level 1 Librarian Certificate | Korean Library Association | Optional | Master's degree or 3 years of experience after Level 2 certification |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a librarian?**
A: Starting salary at public libraries is around 25-32 million KRW, with 10+ years of experience at 45-60 million KRW. Private libraries may be lower.

**Q: Can foreigners work as librarians in Korea?**
A: Inviting E-7 visa holders for professional positions like university libraries is possible but rare, with Korean proficiency and certifications essential. F-2 or F-4 visa holders find it easier.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a librarian?**
A: A bachelor's degree or higher in Library and Information Science and a Level 2 Librarian Certificate are required; public libraries require a full librarian qualification.
