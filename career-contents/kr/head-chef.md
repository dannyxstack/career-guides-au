# Head Chef — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 53111 (KECO)**

A profession responsible for menu planning, cooking supervision, and kitchen management in restaurants or hotels, ensuring food quality and hygiene.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 180,000. Due to the growth and premiumization of the food service industry, demand for skilled head chefs remains steady, but employment growth is limited by labor cost burdens and automation.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Promotion path: Cook → Kitchen Section Chief → Head Chef → Executive Chef. In large hotels or franchises, experience and qualifications are important.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Ingredient Preparation
- Simple Cooking Processes (Frying, Boiling)
- Inventory Record Management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Menu Recipe Development
- Ingredient Order Optimization
- Kitchen Hygiene Monitoring

**Human moat:**

- Creative Culinary Development
- Team Leadership and Communication
- Taste Evaluation and Quality Management

**Skills to build:**

- Understanding of Food Tech
- Data Analysis
- Smart Kitchen Operation

**Key growth areas:**

- Food service industry
- Globalization of Korean cuisine
- Hotel Kitchen
- Food Tech

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented and hygiene-conscious people
- People with leadership and team-leading skills
- People who stay calm under stress

**Who is not suited**
- People indifferent to hygiene
- People who struggle with quick decisions and multitasking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a chef?**
A: Depending on experience and workplace, entry-level: 24-30 million KRW, mid-level: 32-42 million KRW, senior: 45-70 million KRW.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a chef?**
A: You can work as a professional chef with an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa, requiring skilled experience.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a chef?**
A: A cooking technician certificate is advantageous; with experience, advanced certifications like cooking industry engineer can help with promotion.
