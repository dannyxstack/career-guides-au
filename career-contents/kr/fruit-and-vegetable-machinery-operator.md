# Fruit and vegetable machinery operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 87341 (KECO)**

A production worker who operates and manages automated machines that sort, wash, cut, dry, and package fruits and vegetables.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Due to AI and automation, simple sorting and packaging tasks are being replaced by machines, but machine maintenance and quality control still require human workers.

Starting with simple operation, promotion to machine management and quality control is possible with experience; obtaining certifications is advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple sorting tasks
- Cutting to a uniform size
- Simple packaging line tasks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Machine monitoring
- Production volume data analysis
- Anomaly detection

**Human moat:**

- Machine fault diagnosis and repair
- Quality inspection judgment
- Workplace hygiene management

**Skills to build:**

- Machine maintenance
- Data analysis basics
- Hygiene management knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Food processing automation
- Non-professional Employment E-9
- Smart factory
- Fresh-cut produce

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: The annual pre-tax salary for entry-level is approximately 28-32 million KRW, with possible shift allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners also get employed?**
A: Yes, it's possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, and after a certain period of experience, you can switch to an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa for long-term stay.

**Q: Is there a required educational background?**
A: A high school diploma or higher is sufficient, and completing a one-year course at a vocational training institute is advantageous for employment.
