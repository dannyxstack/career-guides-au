# Fisherman and Haenyeo (Female Diver) — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 90421 (KECO)**

A profession that involves harvesting and capturing aquatic resources using fishing vessels or diving in coastal and offshore waters, including fishing vessel operations and diving fishing (haenyeo).

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with excellent physical strength and no aversion to working at sea
- People who adapt well to independent and changing environments
- People with high interest in fisheries and considering returning to fishing

**Who is not suited**
- People who avoid physical labor
- People who prefer stable regular employment

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a fisherman?**
A: For beginners, about 25 million to 30 million KRW; with over 10 years of experience, up to 50 million to 80 million KRW, but highly variable depending on catch volume.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a fisherman?**
A: Non-professional Employment (E-9) or Specific Activities (E-7) visas are commonly used; depending on skill level, conversion to Overseas Korean (F-4) or Residence (F-2) is possible.

**Q: Are there any educational requirements or certifications needed to become a fisherman?**
A: A small vessel operator license is required; for female divers, scuba safety training and certification are recommended. Advanced courses are available at the Korea Fisheries Technology Training Institute.
