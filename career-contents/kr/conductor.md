# Conductor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41451 (KECO)**

An artistic professional who directs instrumentalists and singers in orchestras or choirs, interpreting music to achieve a unified performance.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a conductor?**
A: For entry-level conductors, annual pre-tax salary is around 24-36 million KRW, while principal conductors of major orchestras can earn 60 million to 100 million KRW, but these are very few.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a conductor in Korea?**
A: The E-7 visa for cultural/arts is possible but issuance records are rare, and an invitation from a domestic orchestra is required. Obtaining an F-2 residence visa is possible after long-term residence but not common.

**Q: What is the future outlook for the conductor profession?**
A: The classical performance market is shrinking and digital content is reducing employment opportunities. While the risk of being replaced by AI is low, oversupply of labor is expected to persist.
