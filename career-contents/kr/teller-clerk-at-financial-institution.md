# Teller/Clerk at Financial Institution — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 03241 (KECO)**

Responsible for handling customer financial transactions such as deposits, withdrawals, and remittances at bank or other financial institution counters.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

After joining as a new hire, can be promoted to counter team leader in 3-5 years, then to PB (Private Banker) or back office/branch manager.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with meticulous and accurate numerical processing skills.
- People with patience and kindness in customer service.
- People who don't get bored with repetitive tasks.

**Who is not suited**
- People who find it difficult to adapt to digital technology.
- People who prefer creative and flexible work.

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary?**
A: Varies by bank, but the annual pre-tax salary is about 30–36 million KRW.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for this job?**
A: Generally, tellers are not recognized as professionals, making an E-7 visa difficult. However, holders of F-2, F-4, etc., residence visas are eligible.

**Q: How about promotions?**
A: Usually, you can become a team leader in 3–5 years, and after 10+ years, you can move to branch manager or back office.
