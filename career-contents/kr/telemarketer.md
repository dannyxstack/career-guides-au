# Telemarketer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 61301 (KECO)**

Telemarketers promote and sell products or services over the phone, handling both inbound and outbound calls.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

They typically start as call center agents and can advance to team leader or supervisor positions, or transition to customer management or marketing fields.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Guidance on initial consultation scripts
- Simple order processing
- Survey calls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer emotion analysis
- Real-time recommendations
- Personalized consultation support

**Human moat:**

- Empathy
- Complex problem solving
- Trust building

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- CRM system utilization
- Digital marketing
- Foreign languages

**Key growth areas:**

- Outbound telemarketing
- Inbound customer service
- AI-based call center
- Digital marketing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong persuasion and communication skills
- People who can handle stress and work steadily
- Goal-oriented and focused on results

**Who is not suited**
- People who find repetitive work boring
- People sensitive to rejection in interpersonal relationships

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a telemarketer?**
A: Approximately 24 to 30 million KRW for entry-level, up to 45 million KRW depending on experience and performance.

**Q: Can foreigners work as telemarketers?**
A: Possible on a limited basis with an E-9 visa, but Korean language proficiency is required and the employment permit system procedure must be followed.

**Q: Can telemarketers transition to other jobs?**
A: Yes. You can build a career in marketing, customer management, sales support, and related fields.
