# Recreation Facility Service Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 52401 (KECO)**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an amusement facility service worker?**
A: Approximately 24-28 million KRW for entry-level, up to 42 million KRW depending on experience.

**Q: Can I get a visa for this job?**
A: Possible in some specialized fields with an E-7 visa, but general service workers are not eligible for E-9, so it is limited.

**Q: What are the promotion opportunities?**
A: Promotion possible to facility manager, field supervisor, branch chief, etc., with more opportunities in large chains.
