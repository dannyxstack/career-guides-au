# General Machine Assembler — career analysis · South Korea

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a general machine assembler?**
A: Approximately 24–30 million KRW pretax for entry-level, up to 45 million KRW depending on experience and overtime.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a general machine assembler?**
A: Employment is possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa; if skilled, it can be converted to an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa.

**Q: Are there any required education or certifications for this job?**
A: No specific certification is mandatory, but having a Machine Assembly Technician or Welding Technician certificate is advantageous. High school graduates can learn sufficiently through on-site training.
