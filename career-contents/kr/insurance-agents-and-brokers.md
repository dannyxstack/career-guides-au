# Insurance Agents and Brokers — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 03321 (KECO)**

Insurance agents and brokers are financial professionals who connect insurance companies with customers, sell insurance products, and assist with customer risk management and claims.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insurance Solicitor License | Korea Insurance Development Institute (entrusted by Financial Supervisory Service) | Required | Required for selling insurance products |
| FP (Financial Planner) Certification | Korean FP Association | Optional | Advantageous for advanced counseling |
| Insurance Broker License | Financial Supervisory Service | Optional | Required for brokerage work |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

AI is replacing insurance counseling automation and underwriting, but complex risk analysis and building trust relationships with clients remain a human domain. Face-to-face sales jobs are decreasing, but the scope of work will expand to high-value consulting such as comprehensive financial planning.

Starting as an insurance solicitor, one can open an agency or transition to a broker, obtain FP (Financial Planner) certification, and grow to serve high-net-worth clients.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple insurance comparison/recommendation
- Contract document processing
- Basic call center counseling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Custom design through customer data analysis
- Risk diagnosis using AI simulation
- Automated underwriting review

**Human moat:**

- Building trust relationships
- Judging complex risk scenarios
- Understanding customers' non-financial needs

**Skills to build:**

- Financial planning capability
- Data literacy
- Digital marketing
- Estate Planning

**Key growth areas:**

- Online insurance
- Digital marketing
- Financial planning
- Retirement preparation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an insurance agent?**
A: Entry-level about 25-40 million won, mid-level 40-60 million won, senior 60 million to 100 million won or more. Since it is commission-based, there is a big difference depending on individual ability.

**Q: Can foreigners work as insurance agents in Korea?**
A: Limited. It is possible for professionals to work with an E-7 visa, but it is not common, and F-4 overseas Koreans can legally engage in the activity.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an insurance agent?**
A: Required is a licensed insurance solicitor qualification (entrusted exam by the Financial Supervisory Service), and additionally, having FP or insurance broker qualifications is advantageous.
