# Plating and Metal Spraying Machine Operators — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 82521 (KECO)**

These are production process specialists who apply plating or spray coating to metal surfaces to improve corrosion resistance, wear resistance, aesthetics, etc.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Starting as simple operation assistants, they can advance to line managers or quality controllers after gaining experience, and with certification, they can also move into technical supervision roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- simple dipping plating work
- metered chemical addition
- visual inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- automatic plating line monitoring
- AI-based defect prediction
- process data analysis

**Human moat:**

- complex shape plating technology
- manual finishing/polishing
- atypical quality judgment

**Skills to build:**

- PLC programming
- automation equipment operation
- understanding of surface treatment processes
- basic data analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- plating
- metal spraying
- surface treatment
- automotive parts

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those strong in performing repetitive detailed tasks accurately
- Those interested in chemicals and machine operation who strictly follow safety rules

**Who is not suited**
- Those who easily get bored with simple repetitive tasks
- Those sensitive to harmful environments like chemical odors

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a plater?**
A: Entry-level (0~3 years): approx. 28-35 million KRW; senior (8+ years): approx. 45-60 million KRW (pre-tax).

**Q: What visas can foreigners use to work as platers?**
A: E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa for manufacturing jobs; after gaining skills, can switch to E-7 (Specific Activities) visa for long-term work.

**Q: What certifications are advantageous for a plater?**
A: The basic certification is the Plating Technician (National Technical Qualification). Additional certifications like Surface Treatment Technician or Industrial Safety Engineer are advantageous for promotion or job change.
