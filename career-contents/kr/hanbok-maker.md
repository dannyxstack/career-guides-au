# Hanbok Maker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 86311 (KECO)**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a Hanbok maker?**
A: Approximately 24~30 million KRW for entry-level, and 40~55 million KRW for senior artisans with over 8 years of experience.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a Hanbok maker?**
A: E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is possible but demand is low; F-2 (Residence) or F-4 (Overseas Korean) visas are more advantageous.

**Q: Is a degree necessary to become a Hanbok maker?**
A: It is not mandatory; a Hanbok technician certification or private academy training is sufficient.
