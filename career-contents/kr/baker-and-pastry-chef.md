# Baker and Pastry Chef — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 87111 (KECO)**

A baker and pastry chef is responsible for the entire process of making bread, cakes, and pastries by kneading, shaping, and baking using ingredients such as flour, sugar, and eggs.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

AI and automation are replacing bread dough mixing and simple molding processes, but areas requiring human senses such as creative design, flavor combinations, and quality control remain, leading to job roles being reduced and advanced.

Typically, one can accumulate skills in small businesses and advance to a head pastry chef or bakery manager, or even start their own business or become a franchise branch manager.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- dough measuring and mixing
- Simple Molding
- Oven temperature control monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Recipe Development and Improvement
- Quality Inspection
- Production Process Optimization

**Human moat:**

- Taste and Creativity
- Hand skills
- Custom product manufacturing

**Skills to build:**

- AI Baking Equipment Operation
- Food Engineering Knowledge
- Dessert Trend Analysis

**Key growth areas:**

- HACCP Certification
- Premium Bakery
- Vegan Confectionery
- Online Sales

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a pastry and bakery worker?**
A: Based on entry-level (0-3 years), the pre-tax annual salary is around KRW 25 million to 32 million, varying by certification and work location.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a job as a confectionery/bakery chef?**
A: E-7 visa (Specific Activities) allows skilled workers to be employed, but requires experience, certifications, and employer recommendation is important.

**Q: Do I have to graduate from a related department?**
A: No, there is no educational restriction, but vocational college or academy training can be advantageous for employment and make it easier to obtain certifications.
