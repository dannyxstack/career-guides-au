# Architect (Architectural Designer) — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 14011 (KECO)**

Designs and supervises the construction of buildings, creating architectural plans that meet client needs and regulations.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| University (Bachelor of Architecture) | 5 years (5-year accredited architecture program) | $10,000,000~$50,000,000 (Tuition varies by university, public universities are cheaper) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architect License | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport | Required | Required for architectural design and supervision work |
| Professional Engineer (Architectural Structure, Construction, etc.) | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Recognized as advanced technical expertise, advantageous for specialization |
| BIM Specialist Certification | Korea BIM Society, etc. | Optional | Demonstrates BIM design capability in line with global trends |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Typically, graduates enter architectural firms or construction companies to learn design practices, then obtain an architect license and advance to lead designer or project manager roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic design drawing
- Applying simple regulatory checklists
- Quantitative energy simulation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Generating and optimizing alternative designs
- Coordinating structural and MEP collaboration
- Creating client presentation materials
- Eco-friendly performance analysis

**Human moat:**

- Creative design sense
- Judgment based on codes and field experience
- Trust relationships with clients
- Comprehensive architectural planning

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Building Information Modeling)
- Generative Design
- Eco-friendly architecture design
- Parametric design
- Project management

**Key growth areas:**

- Eco-friendly architecture
- BIM design
- Smart architecture
- Remodeling

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- A person with excellent aesthetic sense for space
- A person who meticulously follows laws and regulations and works systematically

**Who is not suited**
- A person who finds it difficult to adapt to repetitive design modifications and strict regulations
- A person who cannot endure the long education and training period

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an architect?**
A: Entry-level 28–35 million KRW, mid-level 40–55 million KRW, senior-level 60–90 million KRW; varies greatly depending on architect certification and experience.

**Q: What visa is needed for a foreigner to work as an architect in Korea?**
A: The E-7 (Professional) visa is typical. Requires a bachelor's degree or higher in architecture and some experience (or qualifications). Overseas Koreans can work more freely with an F-4 visa.

**Q: Do I have to graduate from a 5-year architecture program to become an architect?**
A: To obtain an architect license, an accredited 5-year architecture degree is essential, but for employment as an architectural design practitioner, a 4-year degree is possible, though it makes obtaining the license more difficult.
