## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much is the annual salary of a university president?**
A: Public university presidents typically earn 200-300 million KRW, while private universities may pay over 500 million KRW, but this varies greatly depending on the university's size and financial status.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a university president in Korea?**
A: Theoretically possible, but most universities only select Korean nationals, making it virtually impossible. You can obtain an E-7 visa as a research professor, etc.

**Q: What is the typical path to becoming a university president?**
A: After being appointed as a professor, it is common to hold positions such as department head, dean, or vice president before running for president of the university.
