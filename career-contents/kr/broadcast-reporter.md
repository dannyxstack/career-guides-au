# Broadcast Reporter — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41202 (KECO)**

This occupation is responsible for news gathering and reporting, performing article writing and video editing at broadcasting stations.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 18,000. Due to digital transformation and changes in the media environment, demand for manpower is stagnant, but demand for experienced reporters is expected to remain.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

AI can replace simple article writing or data analysis, but reporting, interviews, and judgment are advantageous to humans. AI utilization skills will become important.

Promotion from trainee reporter to regular reporter, specialist reporter (data journalism, etc.), desk/manager, or may switch to anchor or PD.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Breaking news writing
- Economic/sports data analysis
- Automated translation and summarization

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Reporter data analysis
- Video editing automation
- Reader response analysis

**Human moat:**

- On-site reporting skills
- Human relations and trust
- Ethical judgment

**Skills to build:**

- Data literacy
- AI tool utilization
- Multimedia storytelling

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Media
- Data Journalism
- Video Content
- Fact-checking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a broadcast journalist?**
A: Entry level: 35–45 million won, Mid level: 50–70 million won, Senior: 80–120 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work as broadcast journalists?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but practically difficult due to high Korean language proficiency and broadcaster hiring standards.

**Q: What preparations are needed to become a broadcast journalist?**
A: Preparing for broadcaster open recruitment (media exam), internship experience, and portfolio (articles/videos) are important.
