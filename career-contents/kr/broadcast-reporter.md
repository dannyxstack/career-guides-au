# Broadcast Reporter — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41202 (KECO)**

This occupation is responsible for news gathering and reporting, performing article writing and video editing at broadcasting stations.

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 18,000. Due to digital transformation and changes in the media environment, demand for manpower is stagnant, but demand for experienced reporters is expected to remain.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

**Skills to build:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a broadcast journalist?**
A: Entry level: 35–45 million won, Mid level: 50–70 million won, Senior: 80–120 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work as broadcast journalists?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but practically difficult due to high Korean language proficiency and broadcaster hiring standards.

**Q: What preparations are needed to become a broadcast journalist?**
A: Preparing for broadcaster open recruitment (media exam), internship experience, and portfolio (articles/videos) are important.
