# Director and Vice Director of Kindergarten — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 01321 (KECO)**

Kindergarten administrators oversee overall operations, including staff management, curriculum planning, parent consultation, and budget execution.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Acquire Kindergarten Teacher Level 2 certification (graduate from early childhood education department at a college or university). | 2-3 years | $12,000,000~$40,000,000 (National/public universities: low tuition; private universities: varying tuition.) |
| Vice Director Qualification Training (government-designated training institutions, 6 months to 1 year). | 6 months | $3,000,000~$5,000,000 (Training fees self-funded.) |
| Director Qualification Training (Ministry of Education-approved, after over 1 year of experience). | 1 year | $5,000,000~$8,000,000 (Some government support available.) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kindergarten Teacher Level 2 Certificate | Ministry of Education | Required | Obtained after graduating from an early childhood education department at a college or higher. |
| Vice Director Certificate | Ministry of Education | Required | At least 3 years of teaching experience + completion of training. |
| Director Certificate | Ministry of Education | Required | At least 2 years of vice director experience + completion of training. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI can partially replace administrative tasks (document writing, schedule management, etc.), but areas requiring human relationships and judgment such as curriculum planning, teacher coaching, and parent counseling are protected. Overall, AI's impact on this job is not significant.

Promotion from kindergarten teacher to vice director (assistant principal) to director; requires specific experience and training. Both public and private institutions have promotion structures based on experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Administrative document writing and management
- Basic statistics and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Curriculum evaluation and improvement
- Preparation of educational materials for teachers

**Human moat:**

- Understanding and counseling child development
- Organizational leadership and crisis management
- Building trust relationships with parents

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization
- Use of digital education tools
- Project management

**Key growth areas:**

- Impact of low birth rate
- Expansion of after-school programs
- Increase in public kindergarten construction
- Reform of director qualifications

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a kindergarten director?**
A: Public kindergarten directors earn approximately 50-70 million KRW through a salary step system, while private ones range from 40-80 million KRW depending on the number of children and institution size.

**Q: Can foreigners become kindergarten directors in Korea?**
A: Korean language ability and early childhood education certification are required, and it is possible with an E-7 visa, but very limited. Possibilities increase for permanent residents.

**Q: What experience is needed to become a director?**
A: After working as a kindergarten teacher for at least 3 years, obtain a vice director qualification, then work as a vice director for at least 2 years before qualifying for director training.
