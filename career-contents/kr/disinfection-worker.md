# Disinfection Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 56131 (KECO)**

A professional who controls pests and harmful organisms in various facilities and spaces, and prevents the spread of infectious diseases.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a disinfection technician?**
A: For entry-level, it is about 24 million to 30 million won, and with experience, it can rise up to 50 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work as disinfection technicians?**
A: It is possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa or E-7 (Specific Activities) visa, but Korean language skills and technical recognition are required.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a disinfection technician?**
A: It is not mandatory, but a Pest Control Technician certification is advantageous, and for business operation, registration of disinfection and sterilization services is required.
