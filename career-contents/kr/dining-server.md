## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a hall server?**
A: Approximately 24 million to 27 million KRW for beginners, up to 30 million KRW or more with experience, plus tips.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a visa for hall serving?**
A: Possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, but the scope of work is limited; mainly F-4 or H-2 visa holders are employed.

**Q: Will AI and robots replace this job?**
A: Some serving robots are being introduced, but complex service and customer interaction require humans, so full replacement will take time.
