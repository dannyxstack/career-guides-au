# Mold Maker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 81311 (KECO)**

Professional technical role that designs, processes, assembles, and tests molds to produce and maintain molds required for product manufacturing.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

On-site technician → CNC/EDM machining skilled → Mold design (CAD/CAM) → Mold team leader/technical development. Independence is possible after gaining experience at large companies or specialized firms.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple drilling and tapping
- Repetitive 2D drawing
- Visual quality inspection
- Basic NC programming

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Mold flow analysis
- Automatic tool path generation
- 3D scanning and reverse engineering
- Smart mold design

**Human moat:**

- Complex precision machining (CAM correction)
- On-site fault and anomaly judgment
- Custom mold modification for customer needs
- Injection/press test adjustment

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM (NX, Catia, Mastercam)
- Mold flow analysis (Moldflow)
- Proficient in CNC and EDM
- Python-based automation scripts

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart mold
- Ultra-precision machining
- Injection/press mold
- Mold automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a mold maker?**
A: For new hires, the annual pre-tax salary is about 24-32 million KRW, and for mid-sized/large companies, it is over 30 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa in the mold field?**
A: It is possible with an E-7-2 (Skilled Worker) visa or E-7-1 (Professional) visa. Requires at least 1 year of relevant experience and employer recommendation.

**Q: What certifications are advantageous to become a mold maker?**
A: Mold Technician (essential), Mold Industrial Engineer (promotion/extra points), and Machine Processing Master (independent/advanced) are advantageous in that order.
