# Food & Tobacco Grader — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 87231 (KECO)**

A professional who inspects and grades the quality of food and tobacco products, determining whether raw materials and finished goods meet specifications.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Starting as a junior inspector, one can advance to senior grader or quality control team leader with experience; obtaining a food engineer certificate is advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Visual inspection
- Size/weight measurement
- Package condition check

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Sensory evaluation data analysis
- Anomaly detection system monitoring
- Quality statistics reporting

**Human moat:**

- Taste and smell judgment
- Emergency response
- Ethical judgment

**Skills to build:**

- HACCP
- Data analysis
- AI Inspection Equipment Operation

**Key growth areas:**

- Food safety
- HACCP

- Automated inspection

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a food, beverage, and tobacco grader?**
A: Approximately 25~30 million KRW for 0~3 years of experience, and increases to 42~55 million KRW with over 10 years of experience.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Simple inspection work is possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, but the E-7 (Specific Activities) visa requires a food engineer certification.

**Q: Will jobs disappear due to automation?**
A: While some tasks like visual inspection are automated, sensory evaluation and judgment still require human intervention, making complete replacement difficult.
