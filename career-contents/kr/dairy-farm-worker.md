# Dairy Farm Worker — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 90211 (KECO)**

This is an agricultural occupation that involves raising dairy cows, producing milk, managing barns, and performing milking tasks.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Korea’s dairy industry is facing a worsening labor shortage due to aging and a decline in farm households; automation is being introduced, but labor demand is expected to remain for the time being.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for dairy workers?**
A: For entry-level, KRW 25 to 32 million per year, increasing with experience and scale.

**Q: Can foreigners work in dairy farming?**
A: Employment is possible via the E-9 visa under the Employment Permit System, but skilled migration is limited.

**Q: Are there required certifications?**
A: Livestock engineer certification is helpful but not mandatory; field experience is more important.
