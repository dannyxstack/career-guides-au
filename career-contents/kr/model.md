## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People confident in their appearance and expressiveness

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for models?**
A: Beginners earn around 15-30 million KRW, while top models can make over 100 million. Income varies widely due to many freelancers.

**Q: What visa is needed for a foreigner to work as a model in Korea?**
A: Mostly require an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa, which must be sponsored by the employer agency or company.

**Q: Can I switch from modeling to another occupation?**
A: Yes, it's common to transition to broadcasting, acting, or entertainment, where experience and recognition are important.
