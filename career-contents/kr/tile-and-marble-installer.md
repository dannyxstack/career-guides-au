# Tile and Marble Installer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70241 (KECO)**

A skilled worker who cuts, processes, and installs architectural finishing materials such as tile, marble, and mosaic on floors and walls.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

The impact of AI and automation is low. Robots may be introduced for material transport and cutting tasks, but precise installation, on-site adaptability, and creative design require human touch. However, new hiring may decrease and shift toward high-skilled roles.

Career advancement from entry-level worker to intermediate skilled worker to senior installer to site manager is possible; certification and experience are important.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple repetitive cutting
- Material transport

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Precision installation
- Material selection and design

**Human moat:**

- On-site intuition
- Manual finishing quality
- Custom installation per client needs

**Skills to build:**

- 3D scanning and simulation
- Eco-friendly material installation
- Automated cutter operation

**Key growth areas:**

- Labor shortage
- Aging workforce
- Construction industry trends
- Need for skilled workers

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for tile and marble installers?**
A: Depending on experience, entry-level is 28-35 million won, mid-level 36-48 million won, and senior 48-60 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: Yes, it is possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa or an E-7 (Skilled Worker) visa. It is covered by the Employment Permit System, and long-term stay is possible with skills.

**Q: Are there any required certifications?**
A: Having a tile or stone technician certification is advantageous but not mandatory. Experience is more important.
