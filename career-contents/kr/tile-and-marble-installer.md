# Tile and Marble Installer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70241 (KECO)**

A skilled worker who cuts, processes, and installs architectural finishing materials such as tile, marble, and mosaic on floors and walls.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with manual dexterity and attention to detail
- People who enjoy physical activities
- People who want to work on construction sites

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike precision work
- People uncomfortable with indoor or high-altitude work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for tile and marble installers?**
A: Depending on experience, entry-level is 28-35 million won, mid-level 36-48 million won, and senior 48-60 million won.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: Yes, it is possible with an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa or an E-7 (Skilled Worker) visa. It is covered by the Employment Permit System, and long-term stay is possible with skills.

**Q: Are there any required certifications?**
A: Having a tile or stone technician certification is advantageous but not mandatory. Experience is more important.
