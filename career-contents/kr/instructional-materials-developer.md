# Instructional Materials Developer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 21515 (KECO)**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

- text editing and proofreading
- basic image/illustration creation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- curriculum analysis and design
- personalized learning path planning

**Human moat:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone who enjoys applying creative ideas to education
- Someone interested in designing materials from a learner's perspective
- Someone proficient in using various media tools

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who dislikes repetitive paperwork
- Someone indifferent to pedagogical principles

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a textbook developer?**
A: Approximately 24-30 million KRW for entry-level, rising to 60 million KRW with experience

**Q: Can foreigners work as textbook developers?**
A: Yes, with relevant degree and experience, one can work under an E-7 visa, but Korean language skills are important

**Q: Are there any required certifications?**
A: No required certifications, but a textbook development specialist certification or e-publishing technician certification is advantageous
