# Health Teacher — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30403 (KECO)**

A professional teacher responsible for health management, first aid, health education, and infectious disease prevention for students and staff in elementary, middle, and high schools.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Bachelor's Degree in Nursing | 4 years | $20,000,000~$40,000,000 (Tuition based on national/public universities) |
| Teaching certificate or master's degree in education from a graduate school | 1-2 years | $5,000,000~$20,000,000 (Additional cost for teaching certificate) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registered Nurse License | Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute | Required | Required for health teacher qualification |
| Health Teacher Certificate (Level 2) | Ministry of Education | Required | Obtained after completing teaching certificate or graduating from a graduate school of education |
| Health Teacher Certificate (Level 1) | Ministry of Education | Optional | Upgraded after completing training with 3+ years of experience |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

The promotion system after appointment is the same as that of regular teachers; advancement to vice principal or principal, or to an education office supervisor, is possible.

- Creating regular health education materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

**Skills to build:**

- Understanding infectious disease epidemiology

**Key growth areas:**

- Enhancing school health

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those interested in student health and education
- Those who can stay calm in emergencies
- Those who enjoy preventive health activities

**Who is not suited**
- Those who find it hard to make quick decisions in emergencies
- Those who prefer administrative tasks over medical work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a school nurse?**
A: Starting around 35-45 million KRW, 5-15 years experience 45-60 million KRW, over 15 years 60-80 million KRW (public school, step-based).

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a school nurse in Korea?**
A: Generally, an E-7 visa is required, but the process is stringent, including Korean language proficiency and recognition of nursing licenses. Many work with permanent residence (F-5) or marriage immigration (F-6).

**Q: What education is needed to become a school health teacher?**
A: After obtaining a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (4 years), you must complete a teaching course or obtain a school health teacher certificate from a graduate school of education. This takes at least 5–6 years in total.
