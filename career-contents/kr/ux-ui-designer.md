## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Junior (2-5 years) → Senior (5-10 years) → Lead/Manager (10+ years). Portfolio and user research skills are key to promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic Wireframe Generation
- Basic UI Template Design

**Where AI augments the role:**

- User Test Analysis
- Design System Building
- A/B Test Design

**Human moat:**

- User Empathy
- Strategic Problem Solving
- Cross-functional Collaboration

**Skills to build:**

- User Research
- Data Visualization
- AI Design Tools (e.g., Figma AI Plugins)

**Key growth areas:**

- User Experience
- UI/UX Trends
- Service Design
- Prototyping

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Someone with strong user empathy
- Someone with both visual design and logical thinking
- Someone who enjoys collaboration and communication

**Who is not suited**
- Someone who prefers simple repetitive tasks
- Someone who cannot accept user feedback

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What to consider when negotiating UX/UI designer salary?**
A: Experience and portfolio quality are most important. Large corporations offer 50 million KRW or more, startups consider stock options.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a UX designer in Korea?**
A: E-7-1 visa is common; requires bachelor's degree and proof of experience. F-2 allows long-term stay.
