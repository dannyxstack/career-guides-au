# Construction and Mining Laborers — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70601 (KECO)**

This occupation involves performing simple support tasks at construction and mining sites, primarily physical labor such as material transport, site cleaning, and excavation assistance.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Compulsory education | 0 years | $0~$0 (No academic background required) |
| Informal on-site training | 1 month | $0~$500,000 (In-house training or construction safety education) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction safety education certificate | Ministry of Employment and Labor | Required | Mandatory at construction sites |
| Forklift Operator | Human Resources Development Service of Korea | Optional | Preferred |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 250,000. Vulnerable to construction economic fluctuations, but steady demand is expected at some sites due to labor shortages from an aging population. The mining sector is region-specific.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

With skilled training, transition to construction craftsmen (plasterers, carpenters, etc.) is possible, but most remain in unskilled labor positions with limited promotion opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Material transport and organization
- Simple excavation assistance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Heavy equipment operation
- Safety monitoring

**Human moat:**

- On-site adaptability
- Non-standard work handling
- Teamwork

**Skills to build:**

- Heavy equipment driving
- Basic electrical/welding
- Safety management

**Key growth areas:**

- Simple construction site labor
- Mining assistant
- Deepening labor shortage
- aging society

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with good physical strength who can endure outdoor work
- People who are patient with simple repetitive tasks

**Who is not suited**
- People with safety insensitivity
- People who avoid physical labor

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary of a construction laborer?**
A: About 2 to 2.5 million KRW (before tax), near minimum wage, varies by site

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Possible with E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, but requires allocation under Employment Permit System; possible conversion to E-7 based on skill level
