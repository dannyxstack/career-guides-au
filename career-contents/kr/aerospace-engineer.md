## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of an aerospace engineer?**
A: Entry-level about 35–45 million won, senior 80–120 million won.

**Q: What visa does a foreign aerospace engineer need to work in Korea?**
A: E-7 (Specific Activities) visa is required, along with aviation-related degree and experience.

**Q: What is the outlook for this job?**
A: Stable with the growth of Korea's aerospace industry, but competition is fierce due to large company-centered hiring.
