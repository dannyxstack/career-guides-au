# Wedding Ceremony Assistant — career analysis · South Korea

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a wedding staff member?**
A: Beginners 22-28 million KRW, up to 50 million KRW depending on experience.

**Q: Can foreigners get a job as a wedding staff member?**
A: Generally, an E-7 visa is required, and a high-level wedding planner qualification is needed, but cases are rare.

**Q: What education is needed to become a wedding staff member?**
A: High school diploma or higher is acceptable, and completing a 6-month course at a wedding planner academy is advantageous.
