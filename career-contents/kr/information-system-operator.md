# Information System Operator — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 13431 (KECO)**

A professional who stably operates, manages, and maintains an organization's information systems (servers, networks, databases, etc.).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Promotion path: junior operator → system administrator → senior engineer/IT manager; with expertise in cloud, security, and automation, one can advance to CTO.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Server log analysis
- Incident alert handling
- Regular inspection report writing
- Basic patch management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fault diagnosis and recovery
- Capacity planning
- Security threat analysis
- Automation script development

**Human moat:**

- Understanding business context
- Complex fault root cause analysis
- Legacy system maintenance
- Cross-team collaboration

**Skills to build:**

- Python/Go scripting
- Kubernetes
- Terraform
- Utilizing AIOps Platform
- Cloud Security

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud Native
- DevOps
- AI Infrastructure
- Security Operations

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Systematic and meticulous personality
- Interest in problem solving
- No burden with overtime/night shifts

**Who is not suited**
- Bored by repetitive tasks
- Prefers independent work over communication

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for an information system operator?**
A: Entry-level: 28-36 million KRW, mid-level: 40-55 million KRW, senior: 60-90 million KRW. Additional increases possible based on cloud/security expertise.

**Q: Can foreigners enter with an employment visa?**
A: E-7 visa required, with IT-related degree and experience. Company sponsorship is mandatory.

**Q: What is the career progression path?**
A: Operator → System Administrator → Senior Engineer → IT Manager/CTO, with DevOps/cloud certifications being advantageous.
