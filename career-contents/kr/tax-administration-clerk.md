# Tax Administration Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 02511 (KECO)**

A public official who performs tax administration tasks such as national tax collection, tax audits, and delinquent account management at tax offices. They are selected through exams and classified as a stable occupation.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 17,000. Due to aging population and diversified tax sources, national tax demand remains, but digitalization is expected to reduce simple tasks. Staff size fluctuates slightly depending on government scale.

**Competition: very high (★★★★½).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Appointed mainly through grade 7-9 civil service exams, with promotion to grade 5 or above possible through promotion exams. Some are favored from tax universities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Document Sorting and Entry
- Basic Tax Calculation
- Civil Complaint Guidance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Tax Investigation Support
- Delinquent Management Decision-making
- Tax Law Interpretation

**Human moat:**

- Complex Tax Judgment
- Face-to-face Consultation and Persuasion
- Trust and Ethics

**Skills to build:**

- Tax Law Expertise
- Data Analysis
- E-Government System Utilization

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization of National Tax Administration
- Specialization of Tax Audits
- Strengthening Delinquent Tax Management
- Freeze on Civil Service Headcount

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a tax administration clerk?**
A: Approximately 35 million KRW for entry-level, and up to 70 million KRW for senior level.

**Q: Can foreigners become tax administration clerks?**
A: Mostly limited to domestic civil service positions, making foreign employment difficult.

**Q: What is needed for promotion?**
A: Must pass performance evaluations and promotion exams; having tax accountant or certified public accountant qualifications is advantageous.
