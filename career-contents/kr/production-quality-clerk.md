## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Meticulous and systematic personality
- Person familiar with numbers and data analysis
- Person interested in collaboration with the manufacturing site

**Who is not suited**
- Person who finds repetitive office work boring
- Person who prefers independent work over interpersonal relationships

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average starting salary for a production/quality clerk?**
A: Approximately 28 to 35 million KRW for entry-level, and 35 million KRW or more at mid-to-large companies.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain a work visa for this occupation?**
A: E-7 visa is difficult due to expertise requirements, but you can work with an F-2 or F-4 visa. E-9 visa is not applicable.

**Q: Which certifications are helpful for promotion?**
A: Certifications like Quality Management Engineer or Production Management Engineer are advantageous for promotion and job change.
