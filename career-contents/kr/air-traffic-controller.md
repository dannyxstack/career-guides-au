# Air Traffic Controller — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 62141 (KECO)**

A professional who directs and controls takeoff, landing, and flight paths for safe and efficient aircraft operation.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) | National Institute for International Education | Optional | TOPIK Level 6 recommended for foreign applicants |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 2,500. Demand remains due to aviation recovery and new airport construction (2nd Gimhae/Gadeokdo), but hiring is limited as it is a quota-based system. Despite technological changes, it remains an essential occupation.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- A person with intense concentration and instantaneous decision-making
- A person who is stress-resistant and strictly follows rules and procedures
- A person with smooth English communication and values teamwork

**Who is not suited**
- A person vulnerable to multitasking and frequently makes mistakes causing incidents
- A person who has difficulty enduring night shifts and rotating shifts

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of an air traffic controller?**
A: Entry-level: 40-60 million KRW, Mid-level: 60-90 million KRW, Senior: 90-130 million KRW. Includes allowances under the civil servant pay system.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to become an air traffic controller in Korea?**
A: Controllers are civil servants, so hiring foreigners is extremely rare. Theoretically possible with an E-7 visa, but applicants need permanent residence (F-5) or residence (F-2) status, plus TOPIK level 6 and ICAO English proficiency.

**Q: What is the training process to become an air traffic controller?**
A: After graduating from a Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport designated institute (2 years) or a 4-year aviation university, you must train at the Ministry's training center (1.5 years) and pass the qualification exam. The training center is government-funded.
