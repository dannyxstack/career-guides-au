# Judicial Scrivener and Bailiff — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 22131 (KECO)**

A legal profession that involves drafting documents for submission to courts and providing legal consultation, while bailiffs are responsible for enforcing court judgments through compulsory execution.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Judicial Scrivener License | Ministry of Justice | Required | Must pass the judicial scrivener exam (1st and 2nd rounds) and complete practical training |
| Attorney License | Ministry of Justice | Optional | Can work as a judicial scrivener after graduating from law school and passing the bar exam |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 11,000. Due to Korea's aging population and increasing legal demand, the work of judicial scriveners remains stable, but simple tasks are declining due to the computerization of registration and deposit. Bailiffs operate under a quota system, limiting new hiring.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Judicial scriveners typically advance from office manager to opening their own office or joining a joint office. Bailiffs are appointed after serving as court officials, with promotions stagnating until retirement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Data entry for electronic registration and deposit applications
- Drafting basic legal document templates
- Reviewing and verifying administrative documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Legal research and case law searching
- Auto-generating document drafts
- Analyzing client consultation records

**Human moat:**

- High-level legal judgment and interpretation
- Personalized legal consultation and trust building
- Court representation and litigation procedure management

**Skills to build:**

- Utilizing electronic litigation systems
- Data analysis and management
- Document automation using AI tools
- Deepening expertise in specialized fields (family, inheritance, real estate)

**Key growth areas:**

- Real Estate Registration
- Legal Consultation
- Family·Inheritance
- Electronic Litigation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a legal scrivener?**
A: For those starting a practice or working as an office manager, the annual salary is around 30-45 million KRW, varying greatly by experience and office size.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a legal scrivener in Korea?**
A: It is possible but requires Korean language ability and extensive knowledge of Korean law; passing the exam and obtaining the qualification is very difficult, making it practically unsuitable as an immigration route.

**Q: What path is needed to become a court bailiff?**
A: You must accumulate experience as a court employee (e.g., registry or bailiff office clerk) and then pass the bailiff exam; tenure is guaranteed but recruitment numbers are very small.
