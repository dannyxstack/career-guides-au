# Dentist — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 30141 (KECO)**

A specialist who diagnoses and treats oral diseases, comprehensively managing dental and oral health including cavities, gum disease, orthodontics, and implants.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| Pre-Dentistry Program | 2 years | $7,000,000~$12,000,000 (Tuition at national universities) |
| School of Dentistry or Graduate School of Dentistry | 4 years | $16,000,000~$25,000,000 (Annual tuition varies by private universities) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dentist License | Ministry of Health and Welfare | Required | Obtained after passing the National Dental Examination |
| Dental Specialist Certification | Korean Dental Association | Optional | Obtained after internship/residency and passing the specialist exam |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 31,000. Demand remains steady due to aging population and growing cosmetic dentistry needs, but regional disparities are large due to metropolitan concentration and competition among private clinics.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Graduate from dental school → pass national dental exam → internship/residency (optional) → open a clinic or work at a hospital. Common path: become a professor at a university hospital, obtain specialist certification, then open a clinic.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple Radiograph Interpretation
- Basic Diagnosis and Consultation
- Treatment Record Writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Implant Surgery Planning
- Orthodontic Treatment Simulation
- Prosthesis Design
- Patient Data Analysis

**Human moat:**

- Complex Surgical Judgment
- Building Patient Trust
- Intraoral Fine Manipulation
- Medical Ethical Decision-Making

**Skills to build:**

- Digital Implant Design
- Use of AI Diagnostic Tools
- Big Data-Based Research Skills
- Advanced Patient Communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Geriatric Implants
- Digital Dentistry
- Cosmetic Dentistry
- Solo Practice

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Patient and dexterous people
- People who enjoy precise work and continuous learning
- People skilled in patient communication and responsible

**Who is not suited**
- People uncomfortable with blood or physical contact
- People who cannot afford long training periods and high tuition

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary of a dentist?**
A: Entry-level salaried dentists earn 60–90 million KRW; experienced clinic owners with 10+ years earn 150–400 million KRW.

**Q: Can foreigners work as dentists in Korea?**
A: A Korean dental license is mandatory; E-7 visa sponsorship is possible but strict.

**Q: How long does it take to become a dentist?**
A: It takes at least 6 years: 2 years of pre-dental and 4 years of dental school, plus 1–4 years of residency training.
