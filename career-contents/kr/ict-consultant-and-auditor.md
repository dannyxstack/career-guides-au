# ICT Consultant and Auditor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 13204 (KECO)**

A professional who provides consulting and auditing on the establishment and operation of information and communication systems, verifying project quality and compliance.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auditor Qualification (IS Auditor) | National Information Society Agency (NIA) | Required | Required for performing audits |
| Information Processing Engineer | HRD Korea | Optional | Basic ICT qualification |
| ICT Professional Engineer | HRD Korea | Optional | Advanced qualification, meets auditor requirements |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an ICT consultant?**
A: Starting salary is 35-45 million KRW, senior up to 100 million KRW, varies by experience and qualifications.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, it is possible to work as an ICT specialist through an E-7 visa, which requires a bachelor's degree and experience.

**Q: What is the minimum education required for this job?**
A: An associate degree or higher is common, and certifications like Information Processing Engineer are helpful.
