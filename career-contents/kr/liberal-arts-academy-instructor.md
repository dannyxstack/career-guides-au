# Liberal Arts Academy Instructor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 21411 (KECO)**

A profession teaching liberal arts subjects such as math and science at private academies, guiding students' learning and helping improve their grades.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High School Diploma | 0 | $0~$0 (Not required by most academies) |
| Bachelor's Degree (STEM preferred) | 4 years | $2,000,000~$10,000,000 (Tuition and living expenses separate) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 150,000. Although the school-age population is declining due to low birth rates, the demand for private education remains high, so employment is maintained but competition is expected to intensify.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Starting as a junior instructor, with experience you can advance to a subject lead instructor or academy director, or switch to freelancing.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Problem-solving guidance
- Repeated explanation of basic concepts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Student performance analysis
- Creating customized learning materials
- Diagnosing underachieving students

**Human moat:**

- Empathy
- Real-time Q&A
- Mentoring

**Skills to build:**

- Educational data analysis
- Utilizing online lecture tools
- Understanding AI tutoring systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Private education
- Test preparation
- Science-focused
- Personalized learning

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those who are good at explaining and have a talent for making students understand
- Those with a deep interest and knowledge in math and science
- Those who find reward in improving students' grades

**Who is not suited**
- Those lacking patience and disliking repetitive tasks
- Those who have difficulty working fixed hours

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a hagwon instructor?**
A: Starting salary KRW 20-30 million, with experience can reach over KRW 45 million, but varies greatly by hagwon size

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a hagwon instructor?**
A: Possible with an E-7 visa, but cases are rare; must meet Korean language and degree requirements

**Q: Can a hagwon instructor switch to another job?**
A: Possible to transition to teaching or go into education-related companies, publishing, etc.
