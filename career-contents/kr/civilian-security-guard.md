# Civilian Security Guard — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 54121 (KECO)**

Civilian security guards provide security and protection for private facilities and can exercise authority similar to national police.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (KRW) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school diploma | 0 years | $0~$0 (Free) |
| Associate degree | 2 years | $2,000,000~$8,000,000 (Police administration or related) |

|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

With experience, promotion to team leader or manager is possible, and some transition to civil service or police recruitment exams.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple surveillance tasks
- Patrol log writing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI control system monitoring
- Emergency response judgment

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment
- Physical restraint capability
- Interpersonal communication

**Skills to build:**

- CCTV control system
- Emergency response protocols
- Basic first aid

**Key growth areas:**

- Security demand
- Private security market
- Aging population
- Security services

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a petition police officer?**
A: Approximately 24-30 million KRW for entry-level, varies by allowance.

**Q: Can foreigners work as petition police officers?**
A: Currently, petition police are recruited mainly from locals, and visa issuance for foreigners is difficult.

**Q: How to get promoted in petition police?**
A: Through experience and certifications (e.g., security instructor), promotion to team leader or manager is possible.
