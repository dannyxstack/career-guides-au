# Sculptor — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 41412 (KECO)**

A sculptor is a professional artist who creates works of art using various materials such as stone, wood, and metal.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

After graduating from art college, sculptors typically operate private studios or join galleries/workshops. Common career paths include holding solo exhibitions, receiving public art commissions, or advancing to teaching positions.

- Simple sculpture production
- Basic 3D modeling tasks
- Material cutting and trimming

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Large sculpture structural calculation
- Digital sculpting and editing

**Human moat:**

- Unique artistic vision

- Communication and interpretation skills with clients

**Skills to build:**

- Digital sculpture software
- Public art planning
- Material science understanding

**Key growth areas:**

- Public art

- 3D printing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a sculptor?**
A: Entry-level sculptors earn about 20-30 million won, experienced sculptors with 8+ years earn over 50 million won, and famous artists can earn hundreds of millions.

**Q: What visa does a foreign sculptor need to work in Korea?**
A: Mainly invited on an E-7 (Specific Activities) visa for exhibitions or performances, and long-term activities are possible with an F-2 (Residence) visa, but it is strict.

**Q: How to build a career as a sculptor?**
A: After graduating from art college, winning competitions, holding solo exhibitions, and networking with galleries are important, and learning digital sculpting skills is advantageous.
