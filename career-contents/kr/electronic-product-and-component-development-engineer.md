# Electronic Product and Component Development Engineer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 15321 (KECO)**

R&D engineers responsible for designing, developing, and testing electronic products and components, playing a key role in advanced electronics sectors such as semiconductors, displays, and home appliances.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person with deep interest in electronics
- Person who solves problems persistently
- Person who values teamwork

**Who is not suited**
- Person who prefers simple repetitive tasks
- Person who dislikes rapid changes

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an electronics product development engineer?**
A: Starting at 35-50 million KRW for entry-level; over 100 million KRW possible with 10+ years of experience.

**Q: Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, skilled R&D personnel can be hired with an E-7 visa (Specific Activities), and a master's degree or higher is advantageous.
