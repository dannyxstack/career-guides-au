# Advertising, PR, and Marketing Clerk — career analysis · South Korea

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Typically start at marketing/advertising agencies or corporate marketing departments, and with experience, can advance to team leader, director, or CMO.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Writing Advertising Performance Reports
- Extracting Simple Target Lists
- Automating Social Media Posts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Creating Customized Ad Materials
- Customer Segmentation Analysis
- A/B Test Optimization

**Human moat:**

- Creative Marketing Strategy, Human-Emotion-Based Branding, Building Customer Relationships

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis, Digital Marketing Tools, AI Prompt Engineering, SQL

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Marketing
- Data-Driven Marketing
- Content Marketing
- Social Media Advertising

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average starting salary?**
A: For small and medium-sized companies, the annual salary is around 30-45 million KRW, while large companies may offer more.

**Q: What visa allows foreigners to work?**
A: Employment is possible with an E-7 visa, which requires certain experience or a degree. Holders of F-2 or F-4 visas are also eligible.

**Q: What is the outlook for this job?**
A: Employment is increasing with the growth of digital marketing, but competition is intense, so securing expertise is important.
