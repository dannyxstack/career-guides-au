# Non-commissioned Officer (NCO) — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 25031 (KECO)**

In the Republic of Korea Armed Forces, NCOs are mid-level leaders who assist officers and handle administrative, technical, and tactical duties. They exercise expertise between enlisted personnel and officers, responsible for operations and maintenance in each branch.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a non-commissioned officer?**
A: Approximately 24~30 million KRW for entry-level sergeant (excluding allowances), and approximately 40~50 million KRW for a 10-year senior sergeant. Pension and various allowances are added for long-term service.

**Q: Can foreigners become non-commissioned officers (NCOs)?**
A: Only South Korean nationals can apply, so foreigners cannot become NCOs. However, contract work in military-related technical fields through E-7 visas is possible, but very limited.

**Q: What is the promotion speed for NCOs?**
A: Generally, promotion occurs after 4 years as sergeant, 5-6 years as staff sergeant, and 7-8 years as sergeant first class. Early promotion is rare and varies by performance and position.
