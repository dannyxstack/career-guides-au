# Plasterer — career analysis · South Korea

**Occupation code: 70211 (KECO)**

A construction worker who finishes walls or floors by applying mortar, cement, etc., and is key to the interior and exterior aesthetics and durability of buildings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can advance from beginner plasterer → intermediate plasterer → senior plastering master → site manager. Experience and certifications are crucial for promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple mortar application
- Leveling work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Precision finishing
- Complex curved surface work

**Human moat:**

- On-site adaptability
- Fingertip feel
- Understanding material properties

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automatic plastering machines
- 3D scanning assistance
- Eco-friendly material application methods

**Key growth areas:**

- Increase in remodeling
- Repair of old buildings
- Shortage of skilled workers
- Mechanization and automation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Korea's E-7 skilled-worker visa is granted to qualifying professionals with a local job offer, and long-term holders may move to F-2/F-5 residence. Requirements are set by the Korea Immigration Service and change over time.

**Official immigration source:** [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are physically fit and enjoy outdoor work
- People with dexterity and attention to detail
- People who value teamwork on construction sites

**Who is not suited**
- People who lack patience for precision work
- People who do not follow safety rules

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a plasterer?**
A: For entry-level, about 24-30 million KRW; with experience, up to 50 million KRW or more. Income varies due to high proportion of daily work.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a plasterer?**
A: E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa (Employment Permit System) or E-7 (Specific Activities) visa are possible. Skilled workers may switch to F-2 (Residence) visa.

**Q: What is the career development path as a plasterer?**
A: After obtaining a technician certificate and gaining experience, you can advance to a site manager or professional master craftsman. Further training can lead to a career shift as a construction technician.
