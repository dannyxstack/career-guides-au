# Pharmacist — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 2262 (ISCO08)**

Pharmacists in Denmark are healthcare professionals specialized in the preparation, dispensing, and proper use of medications. They work in community pharmacies (apotek), hospitals, the pharmaceutical industry, and academia. The profession is regulated by the Danish Patient Safety Authority, and a master's degree in pharmacy (cand.pharm.) is required. Pharmacists advise patients on medication, ensure safe and effective drug therapy, and collaborate with doctors and other healthcare providers. With an aging population and increasing focus on medication management, pharmacists play a crucial role in the Danish healthcare system.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 4,985. The employment outlook for pharmacists in Denmark is stable to positive. The demand is driven by an aging population requiring more medications, the expansion of clinical pharmacy services, and the pharmaceutical industry's growth. However, competition for hospital positions can be stiff, and community pharmacy roles are more readily available. The profession is not on the official shortage list but remains in steady demand, especially in rural areas. EU/EEA citizens have free access, while non-EU pharmacists may find opportunities via the Positive List.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for pharmacists in Denmark typically starts in community pharmacy, with advancement to pharmacy manager (apoteker) after additional certification. Many specialize in clinical pharmacy in hospitals, or move into the pharmaceutical industry in regulatory affairs, quality assurance, or research. Further academic paths include a PhD in pharmacy leading to research or teaching roles. There is also a trend toward specialization in areas like pharmacotherapy, personalized medicine, and digital health, offering diverse career opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine prescription review and drug interaction checks
- Standardized drug dispensing and dose calculation
- Inventory management and automatic replenishment
- Drug information retrieval and simple medication consultation
- Automatic bill reconciliation with insurance systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to predict individual drug responses and adverse reaction risks
- Personalized medication regimen optimization based on clinical data
- Smart triage and remote patient medication follow-up
- Improved Vaccine Management and Promotion Efficiency
- Pharmacoeconomic analysis and real-world evidence generation

**Human moat:**

- Medication management and clinical decision-making for complex cases.
- Face-to-face patient consultation and medication adherence intervention
- Coordination and communication within multidisciplinary healthcare teams
- Ethical decision-making and pharmaceutical legal compliance judgment
- Patient education on emerging therapies (e.g., gene therapy)

**Skills to build:**

- AI-driven clinical decision support system operation
- Pharmacogenomics and precision medication analysis
- Telemedicine and digital health tool application
- Statistical data analysis and real-world research skills
- Medical ethics and privacy protection compliance knowledge
- Vaccine management and public health skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Clinical pharmacy
- Personalized medicine
- Digital health
- Medication management

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Danish Immigration Service (nyidanmark.dk)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pharmacist in Denmark?**
A: The average annual gross salary ranges from DKK 540,000 to 660,000, depending on experience and sector (community, hospital, industry).

**Q: Can non-EU pharmacists obtain a work visa for Denmark?**
A: Yes, pharmacists are on the Positive List for skilled workers, making them eligible for a work permit. Alternatively, the Pay Limit Scheme applies if the salary is above DKK 465,000 (2023).

**Q: Is Danish language proficiency required?**
A: Yes, to obtain authorization and work directly with patients, pharmacists must demonstrate Danish language skills at a high level (at least Prøve i Dansk 3).
