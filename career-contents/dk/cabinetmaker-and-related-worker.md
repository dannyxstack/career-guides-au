# Cabinet-Maker — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7522 (ISCO08)**

Cabinet-makers in Denmark are skilled craftsmen who design, build, and repair furniture and woodwork. The occupation is deeply rooted in Danish design tradition, with a steady demand for custom-made, high-quality pieces. The labour market values both traditional handcraft and modern digital fabrication skills. Employment is typically in small workshops, furniture companies, or as self-employed, with opportunities in restoration and sustainable production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a 3.5-4 year vocational apprenticeship, leading to a journeyman's certificate. Experienced cabinet-makers can become master craftsmen (Mester), allowing self-employment and apprentice training. Specialisation in restoration, design, or digital fabrication opens further opportunities. Some move into sales, project management, or teaching.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom Furniture
- Sustainable Materials
- Digital Fabrication
- Restoration

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cabinet-maker in Denmark?**
A: Salaries for cabinet-makers vary by experience and location, but pre-tax annual earnings typically range from 330,000 to 450,000 DKK.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as cabinet-makers in Denmark?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work as cabinet-makers under the Pay Limit Scheme (if salary threshold is met) or if the occupation is on the Positive List for Skilled Work. The trade certificate is required.

**Q: How do I become a cabinet-maker in Denmark?**
A: The main path is a 3.5-4 year vocational apprenticeship combining on-the-job training and school, culminating in an exam for the cabinet-maker certificate (svendebrev). EU/EEA students can attend for free; non-EU/EEA students may have tuition fees.
