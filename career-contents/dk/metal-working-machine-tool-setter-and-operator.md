# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

In Denmark, metal working machine tool setters and operators are essential in manufacturing industries such as machinery, automotive, and metal products. They set up, operate, and monitor various machine tools like lathes, milling machines, and CNC equipment. The role demands precision and familiarity with technical drawings. With a strong industrial base in regions like Central Jutland, the occupation supports Denmark's export-oriented economy. Apprenticeship-based vocational training is common.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship (erhvervsuddannelse) leading to a skilled worker certificate. Experienced operators can advance to team leader, planner, or specialist roles in CNC programming. Further education like a teknonom degree can lead to supervisory or technical sales positions. Lifelong learning is encouraged due to technological changes.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC programming
- automation
- precision engineering
- green manufacturing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for metal working machine tool setters and operators in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level around DKK 300,000-350,000 per year; experienced workers earn DKK 380,000-450,000. Overtime and shift allowances can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a metal working machine tool setter and operator in Denmark?**
A: Yes, through the Pay Limit Scheme if the annual salary exceeds the threshold (DKK 465,000 in 2024). Alternatively, if the occupation is listed on the Positive List (currently not), or through the Fast-track Scheme if employed by a certified company.

**Q: Is it easy to get a job as a metal worker in Denmark?**
A: Demand is moderate. Skilled CNC operators are sought after, especially in industrial regions. Knowledge of Danish is often required for communication, but English may suffice in some international companies.
