# Metal Polisher, Wheel Grinder and Tool Sharpener — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7224 (ISCO08)**

Metal Polishers, Wheel Grinders and Tool Sharpeners in Denmark are skilled tradespeople who finish and maintain metal surfaces and edges in industries such as manufacturing, aerospace, automotive, and tooling. They work in workshops or factories, using grinding machines, polishing tools, and sharpening equipment to achieve precise tolerances and surface finishes. The occupation requires vocational training and attention to detail.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression often involves specialization in advanced surface finishing technologies (e.g., CNC grinding) or moving into quality control, supervision, or teaching at vocational schools. With additional training, polishers can become toolmakers or precision engineers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive metal surface polishing and grinding operations
- Use automated grinding machines for batch workpiece processing.
- Grinding wheel dressing and balancing based on preset parameters
- dimensional inspection and pass/fail determination of simple workpieces
- Automatic grinding of standard tool edges.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted process parameter optimization: automatically recommends grinding parameters based on workpiece material and precision requirements
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes equipment vibration and temperature data to provide early fault warnings
- Visual inspection: AI system checks surface finish in real-time, reducing manual inspection
- Collaborative robots: human-robot collaboration to move heavy workpieces, reducing labor intensity
- Digital Twin: Simulate machining processes, shorten trial cutting cycles

**Human moat:**

- Manual polishing and finishing of non-standard workpieces
- Manual adjustment and grinding of complex-shaped molds and tools
- On-site judgment and process adjustment for material defects
- Manual repair and precision restoration of aging equipment
- Communicate with clients on customized surface treatment requirements

**Skills to build:**

- CNC grinding programming and operation
- CAD/CAM software (e.g., Mastercam)
- Fundamentals of robotics and automation system integration.
- Data analysis and quality control (SPC)
- Additive manufacturing post-processing techniques
- Electrical and mechanical maintenance skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Manufacturing
- Aerospace
- Automotive
- Surface Finishing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is salary determined for metal polishers in Denmark?**
A: Salaries are typically negotiated through collective agreements between unions and employers, varying by region, experience, and company size. For specific figures, consult Statistics Denmark's LONS20 dataset.

**Q: Can a non-EU metal polisher obtain a work visa for Denmark?**
A: The occupation is not on the Positive List for skilled shortage. Non-EU applicants may qualify under the Pay Limit Scheme if the salary exceeds the threshold (approximately 448,000 DKK in 2023), but this is rare. EU/EEA nationals have free movement rights.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for metal polishers?**
A: With experience and additional training, polishers can move into supervision, quality control, or become instructors at vocational schools. Further specialization in CNC grinding or toolmaking can lead to higher-skilled roles.
