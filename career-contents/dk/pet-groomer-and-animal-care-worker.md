# Pet Groomer and Animal Care Worker — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

Pet groomers and animal care workers in Denmark provide essential services for domestic animals, including grooming, bathing, nail trimming, and basic health checks. They work in pet salons, veterinary clinics, boarding facilities, and animal shelters. This occupation requires a strong affection for animals, patience, and good communication skills when dealing with pet owners. Many professionals are self-employed or work in small businesses. The role is increasingly valued as Danish households prioritize pet welfare and aesthetics.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for pet groomers and animal care workers in Denmark typically starts from an apprenticeship or entry-level position, advancing to senior groomer, salon manager, or business owner. Specialization in specific breeds, creative grooming, or animal behavior can enhance career prospects. Some professionals further their education to become veterinary nurses or animal trainers. Self-employment is a common path for experienced workers, allowing flexibility and higher earnings.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Client appointment and schedule management
- Basic beauty records and billing
- Standardized communication (e.g., reminders, follow-ups)
- Security monitoring (e.g., camera analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze pet fur health for care recommendations
- Use AI tools to optimize inventory and supplies management
- Use AI diagnostic tools to assist in identifying skin issues
- Using AI to generate personalized beauty solutions and recommendations

**Human moat:**

- Calming techniques for handling anxious or aggressive animals
- Adjust grooming techniques flexibly based on individual pet condition
- Building emotional trust and personalized service with customers
- Creativity and aesthetic judgment in hand styling

**Skills to build:**

- Learn animal behavior knowledge to improve soothing skills
- Master the use of AI-assisted diagnostic tools
- Enhancing advanced beauty and styling design skills
- Learn to use customer relationship management software
- Attend professional certification courses to enhance competitiveness
- develop social media marketing skills to expand client base

**Key growth areas:**

- Luxury Grooming
- Mobile Pet Services
- Pet Daycare
- Holistic Animal Care

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Danish Immigration Service (nyidanmark.dk)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who love animals and have a patient, calm demeanor.
- Individuals who enjoy hands-on work and are not afraid of physical tasks.
- Self-motivated persons interested in running their own pet care business.

**Who is not suited**
- Those with animal fur or dander allergies.
- People who prefer a quiet, low-interaction work environment.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary but typically range from 300,000 to 400,000 DKK per year for full-time employees, depending on experience and location. Self-employed individuals may earn more or less.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a pet groomer in Denmark?**
A: Non-EU citizens face significant challenges as this occupation is not on the Positive List and typical salaries are below the Pay Limit Scheme threshold. EU/EEA citizens can work freely. It is recommended to check with SIRI for any updates.

**Q: What training is required to become a pet groomer in Denmark?**
A: While not mandatory, completing a 2-year vocational education (EUD) in animal care or a specialized grooming course greatly improves job prospects. Many employers prefer certified candidates.
