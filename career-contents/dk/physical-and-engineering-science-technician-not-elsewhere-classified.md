# Physical and Engineering Science Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3119 (ISCO08)**

Physical and engineering science technicians in Denmark work across a wide range of industries including manufacturing, energy, life sciences, and environmental monitoring. They assist engineers and scientists in research, development, testing, and quality control. Typical tasks include operating laboratory equipment, collecting and analyzing data, setting up experiments, and maintaining technical systems. Many are employed in private companies, public research institutions, or consultancies. The role requires a combination of practical skills and theoretical knowledge, often obtained through vocational education or short-cycle higher education.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior technician, advancing to senior technician or specialist roles in areas like quality assurance or process optimization. Further education, such as a bachelor's in engineering, can open pathways to engineering positions. Some technicians move into project management or technical sales. Employers value continuous learning, and certifications in relevant technologies (e.g., CNC, lab automation) enhance career opportunities. The field offers clear advancement for motivated individuals.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically record experimental data and generate standardized reports
- Use AI algorithms for preliminary data analysis and anomaly detection
- Automatically calibrate and adjust experimental parameters based on historical data
- Classify samples and perform basic inspections using computer vision
- Automatically organize literature and preliminary experimental design

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using machine learning models to optimise experimental design and improve efficiency
- AI-assisted multi-variable data analysis to discover hidden patterns
- Using digital twins to simulate experimental processes and predict outcomes.
- Quick retrieval of technical documents and specifications using natural language processing
- AI-driven predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime.

**Human moat:**

- On-site equipment fault diagnosis and repair decision-making
- Innovative experimental design and non-standard testing.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration, communication, and explaining technical details
- Safety management and compliance assessment
- Customer requirement analysis and customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of Python or R programming
- Introduction to Statistics and Machine Learning
- Digital lab management software (e.g., LabVantage).
- PLC or Embedded Systems basics
- Communication and project management certifications (e.g., PMP)
- Industry-specific regulations (e.g., ISO 17025)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green Energy
- Life Sciences
- Automation
- Quality Control

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Practical problem-solvers who enjoy hands-on technical work in labs or industrial settings.
- Detail-oriented individuals who can follow precise protocols and document findings.
- Those interested in supporting engineering and research in fields like energy, pharma, or environmental tech.

**Who is not suited**
- Those seeking fully remote or desk-only roles, as most work requires on-site presence.
- Individuals uncomfortable with technical equipment or strict safety regulations.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Physical and Engineering Science Technician in Denmark?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from 300,000 to 350,000 DKK annually, while experienced technicians earn between 400,000 and 500,000 DKK. Specialists in high-demand fields like automation or biotech may exceed 550,000 DKK. Benefits often include pension and paid overtime.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign Physical and Engineering Science Technicians?**
A: EU/EEA citizens can work freely. Non-EU citizens typically use the Pay Limit Scheme (salary threshold) or the Positive List for Skilled Work if their occupation is in shortage. The Fast-Track scheme (for certified companies) may also apply. A valid job offer is required.

**Q: How can a Physical and Engineering Science Technician advance their career in Denmark?**
A: Technicians can progress by gaining experience, specializing (e.g., in quality control or lab automation), or pursuing further education such as a bachelor's in engineering. Certifications in relevant software (e.g., LIMS, CAD) or equipment enhance prospects. Some move into project management or technical consulting roles.
