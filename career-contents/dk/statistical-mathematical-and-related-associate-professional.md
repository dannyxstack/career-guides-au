# Statistical, Mathematical and Related Associate Professional — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3314 (ISCO08)**

In Denmark, statistical associate professionals support economists, data scientists, and decision-makers by collecting, processing, and analysing numerical data. They work in sectors such as public administration, market research, finance, and healthcare, applying software tools like SAS, R, or Python. The role is vital for evidence-based policy-making and business intelligence, with a strong demand for data literacy across both public and private sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression often starts from a bachelor's degree in statistics, mathematics, or economics, followed by roles as data analyst or statistical assistant. With experience and further education (e.g., master's), professionals can move into senior analyst, data scientist, or management positions. Certifications in specific software (SAS, SPSS) can boost career advancement. The trend is toward more integrated data roles, requiring both statistical and programming skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Data cleaning and preprocessing (e.g., missing value imputation, outlier detection)
- Automatic execution of basic statistical tests (e.g., t-test, chi-square test)
- Standardized report and dashboard generation (e.g., monthly sales statistics)
- Automatic fitting of simple regression models and predictions

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AutoML tools to quickly iterate complex models and improve experimental efficiency
- Query databases or statistical software via natural language, lowering technical barriers
- AI-assisted generation of data stories and visualization suggestions to enhance presentation effectiveness.
- Automated anomaly monitoring and early warning systems allowing analysts to focus on root causes.

**Human moat:**

- Business understanding: translating statistical results into actionable business recommendations
- Causal inference: designing experiments, distinguishing correlation vs. causality
- Complex model customization: adjusting algorithms or designing new metrics for non-standard problems
- Communication and persuasion: explaining analysis results to non-technical personnel and gaining trust.
- Ethics and compliance: ensuring data use and modeling comply with regulations (e.g., GDPR).

**Skills to build:**

- Python/R programming (focus on pandas, scikit-learn)
- SQL and big data platforms (e.g., Spark, BigQuery)
- Machine learning basics (classification, regression, clustering, hyperparameter tuning)
- Generative AI tools (such as ChatGPT for code assistance, report generation)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI or Python plotting libraries)
- Causal inference methods (e.g., DAG, instrumental variables)

**Key growth areas:**

- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence
- Public Sector Statistics
- Market Research

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with strong numerical and analytical skills who enjoy working with data and problem-solving.
- Detail-oriented people who thrive in structured environments (e.g., government statistics, market research).
- People interested in supporting data-driven decisions in business or public policy.

**Who is not suited**
- Those who prefer qualitative, creative, or hands-on work away from numbers and computers.
- People who dislike repetitive data cleaning or administrative statistical tasks.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a statistical associate professional in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, but entry-level positions start around 350,000-400,000 DKK annually, while experienced professionals can earn 500,000-600,000 DKK. Public sector salaries follow collective agreements; private sector may offer higher compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU/EEA citizens get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, if they meet the Pay Limit Scheme threshold (DKK 465,000/year) or are hired by a Fast-track certified company. EU/EEA citizens have free movement. The occupation may also qualify under the Positive List if shortages arise, but currently it is not listed.

**Q: Do I need a specific Danish certification to work as a statistical associate?**
A: No mandatory certification exists, but a relevant bachelor's degree (e.g., in statistics, mathematics, or economics) is typically required. Software proficiency (SAS, R, Python) and prior internship experience are highly valued.
