# Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3214 (ISCO08)**

Medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Denmark fabricate, repair, and customize dental prostheses (crowns, bridges, dentures) and medical orthotic devices. They work in dental labs, hospitals, or private clinics, using traditional craftsmanship and digital technologies like CAD/CAM and 3D printing. The role requires precision, manual dexterity, and knowledge of materials science. With Denmark's ageing population and focus on aesthetic dentistry, demand is steady. Technicians often collaborate with dentists and surgeons to ensure optimal fit and function.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a 3-4 year vocational apprenticeship (EUD) as a dental technician. After certification, technicians can specialize in areas like implant prosthetics, orthodontics, or maxillofacial prosthetics. Experienced technicians may become laboratory managers, open their own labs, or move into sales for dental material companies. Continuing education in digital dentistry and advanced materials is key for advancement. Some technicians transition to teaching at technical schools.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Dentistry
- Cad/Cam
- 3D Printing
- Implant Prosthetics

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented individuals with steady hands and spatial awareness.
- Technically-minded people interested in craftsmanship and digital workflows.
- Those who enjoy independent work in a laboratory environment and collaboration with dental professionals.

**Who is not suited**
- Individuals uncomfortable with repetitive, precision tasks under time pressure.
- Those seeking a patient-facing role (most work is lab-based, not directly with patients).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dental technician in Denmark?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around 25,000-30,000 DKK/month. With experience, salaries range from 30,000-40,000 DKK/month. Specialists or lab managers can earn over 45,000 DKK/month. Salaries are pre-tax and include pension contributions.

**Q: Can I work in Denmark as a dental technician from outside the EU?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer and the position meets the Positive List criteria (occupation on the list) or the Pay Limit Scheme. You must apply for a residence and work permit before arrival. Documentation of qualifications and a Danish employer are required.

**Q: What is the work environment like?**
A: Dental technicians typically work in private laboratories, dental clinics, or hospital dental departments. The work is lab-based, involving precision tools, computer-aided design, and material handling. Standard workweeks are 37 hours, with some overtime during peak periods.
