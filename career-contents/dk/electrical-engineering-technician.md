# Electrical Engineering Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3113 (ISCO08)**

Electrical engineering technicians in Denmark assist engineers in designing, testing, and maintaining electrical systems and equipment, working across industries such as energy, manufacturing, construction, and IT. They perform technical tasks including installation, troubleshooting, and calibration, often specializing in areas like automation, renewable energy, or smart grids. The role is critical for Denmark's green transition and digitalization efforts.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 4,358. The outlook for electrical engineering technicians in Denmark is stable, driven by the green transition, expansion of renewable energy (especially wind power), and increasing automation in industry. Smart grid development and energy efficiency projects also fuel demand. Technicians are needed for installation, maintenance, and upgrade of electrical systems. Opportunities exist in both large companies and SMEs. The occupation is on Denmark's Positive List for skilled work, indicating a recognized shortage.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a vocational education, followed by on-the-job training. Technicians can advance to senior technician, project manager, or specialist roles in automation, renewable energy, or electrical design. Further education, such as a bachelor's in electrical engineering, can lead to engineering positions. Danish employers value experience and continuous learning; certifications in specific technologies (e.g., PLC, SCADA) enhance advancement prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Circuit design draft generation and optimization based on historical data
- Automatically generate electrical system documentation with BOM (Bill of Materials) and component selection
- Routine electrical fault diagnosis and maintenance script writing (e.g., using AI-recommended troubleshooting steps).
- Automated test report generation and anomaly alert analysis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI simulation tools (e.g., ETAP, PSS/E) to quickly verify system designs and fault scenarios
- Real-time prediction of equipment health status and optimization of maintenance plans through AI-driven digital twin platforms
- Using AI-generated code snippets to accelerate PLC/SCADA system programming and debugging
- Use natural language interaction to quickly retrieve technical standards and compliance requirements
- AI-assisted multi-plan comparison and performance evaluation (e.g., harmonic analysis, energy efficiency optimization)

**Human moat:**

- Execution of on-site safety procedures and emergency response (e.g., handling of high-voltage system malfunctions)
- Understanding customer needs and effective communication with non-technical personnel
- Debugging physical interfaces and locating anomaly causes in cross-system integration
- Engineering judgment for equipment modification and on-site condition adaptation
- Creative design solutions for upgrading existing infrastructure

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using AI-assisted electrical design tools (e.g. AutoCAD Electrical AI plugin)
- Proficiency in Python/R programming for automated data analysis and digital twin development.
- Learn industrial IoT and edge computing technologies (e.g., OPC UA, MQTT)
- Develop system integration and project coordination skills (e.g., familiarity with SCADA, DCS systems)
- Obtain advanced certifications such as CEH (Cybersecurity) or industrial robot operation certification.
- Deep specialization in a vertical field (such as photovoltaic plant operation and maintenance or grid digitalization)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green transition
- Renewable energy
- Automation
- Smart grids

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Danish Immigration Service (nyidanmark.dk)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electrical engineering technician in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, industry, and location. Entry-level salaries often start below the pay limit for skilled migration, but experienced technicians can earn significantly more. For official statistics, refer to Statistics Denmark's LONS20 data.

**Q: Can I obtain a work permit as a non-EU electrical engineering technician?**
A: Yes, the occupation is on the Positive List for Skilled Work, so non-EU applicants can apply for a work permit if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold. The Pay Limit Scheme is also an option for higher-paying positions.
