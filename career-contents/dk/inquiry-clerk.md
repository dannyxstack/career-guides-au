# Inquiry Clerk — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 4225 (ISCO08)**

Inquiry clerks in Denmark respond to customer queries via phone, email, or chat, providing information about products, services, or procedures. They work in both public and private sectors, including government agencies, banks, insurance companies, and telecoms. The role requires strong communication skills, patience, and ability to multitask. While many positions require Danish language proficiency, English-speaking roles exist in international companies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression often leads to senior customer service roles, team leader, or quality assurance specialist. With further training, clerks can move into sales, marketing, or administration. Some pursue relevant vocational certifications in office administration or specialized fields like insurance or finance to enhance their career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Handle customer inquiries for general information about products or services
- Provides standardized process guidelines and frequently asked questions
- Records and transfers simple complaints to relevant departments
- Update and maintain customer information databases.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize multilingual customer communication using AI-assisted scripts
- Use automatic summarization tools to quickly record call key points

**Human moat:**

- building long-term customer trust and personalised service

**Skills to build:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for inquiry clerks in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience and sector, but are generally in line with other administrative office roles. Most positions pay below the Pay Limit Scheme threshold, making it difficult for non-EU applicants to obtain a work permit through that route.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as inquiry clerks in Denmark?**
A: Yes, but primarily through EU/EEA free movement. Non-EU citizens may qualify under the Pay Limit Scheme if the salary meets the minimum annual threshold, though this is uncommon for this occupation. Alternatively, the Fast-track Scheme applies if the employer is certified, but again salary is typically too low.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for inquiry clerks in Denmark?**
A: With experience, clerks can advance to senior customer service roles, team leadership, or specialization in areas like claims handling or technical support. Further vocational training in office administration or a related field can open doors to higher-level administrative or management positions.
