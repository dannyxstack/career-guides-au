# Education Manager — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 1345 (ISCO08)**

Education managers in Denmark oversee the planning, coordination, and evaluation of educational institutions and programs. They work in primary and secondary schools, higher education, adult education centers, and vocational training. The role requires strong leadership and understanding of the Danish education system, including the Folkeskole and gymnasium levels. Due to decentralization, many tasks involve budget management, staff development, and curriculum implementation. Danish language proficiency is essential for communication with staff, parents, and authorities.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an education manager in Denmark?**
A: Annual salaries range from 550,000 to 750,000 DKK before tax, depending on institution size, location, and experience. Top-tier can exceed 800,000 DKK.

**Q: Is there a visa option for non-EU education managers?**
A: Yes, the Pay Limit Scheme applies if the job offer salary is at least 487,000 DKK (2024). Additionally, the Fast-track Scheme for certified companies may be used. Danish language proficiency is typically required.

**Q: What is the typical career path to become an education manager in Denmark?**
A: Most start as teachers, then progress to team leader or department head, often while pursuing a leadership diploma or master's. After several years, they may become vice principal or principal. Further advancement includes municipal school consultant or director.
