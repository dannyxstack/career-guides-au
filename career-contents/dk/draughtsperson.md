# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons (Tegnere) in Denmark create detailed technical drawings for buildings, infrastructure, machinery, and products. They are essential in architecture, engineering, and manufacturing firms, working closely with engineers and architects. The Danish labour market increasingly demands proficiency in Building Information Modeling (BIM) and 3D CAD software, driven by digitalization and green transition projects. Draughtspersons are employed across sectors such as construction, energy, and industrial design.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational education, leading to junior draughtsperson roles. With experience, professionals can advance to senior draughtsperson, project coordinator, or specialize in areas like structural or MEP drafting. Further education opens paths to engineering roles or BIM management. Continuous upskilling in digital tools is key to advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- BIM
- green energy
- urban development
- industrial automation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can a non-EU draughtsperson get a Danish work visa?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer meeting the Pay Limit Scheme (salary around DKK 487,000/year) or if the occupation is on the Positive List for Skilled Work. The Positive List often includes draughtspersons (Tegnere) due to shortage. A relevant degree or vocational qualification is required.

**Q: What education do I need to work as a draughtsperson in Denmark?**
A: The typical path is a 3.5-year vocational education (EUX) or a 2.5-year short-cycle higher education (Professionsbachelor in Design and Business or similar). EU/EEA citizens can study for free and receive state grants (SU). Non-EU applicants may need a completed qualification recognized in Denmark.

**Q: Is there demand for draughtspersons specializing in BIM?**
A: Yes, BIM specialists are highly sought after in Denmark, especially in larger construction and infrastructure projects. Knowledge of software like Revit, Tekla, or Navisworks can significantly enhance job prospects and career advancement.
