# Companion and Valet — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 5162 (ISCO08)**

Companions and valets provide personal care, grooming, and companionship services to individuals, often in private households or healthcare settings. In Denmark, the occupation is niche, with demand primarily from high-net-worth families, elderly individuals seeking live-in care, or luxury hospitality establishments. The Danish labour market emphasizes formal care through public services, limiting the need for private companions, but a small niche exists for specialized butler roles in hotels and private residences. EU/EEA nationals can take up such positions freely, while non-EU/EEA candidates face significant barriers due to salary thresholds and the absence of the occupation on shortage lists.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for companions and valets in Denmark is limited. Entry-level roles may lead to senior butler positions, estate management, or personal assistant roles in affluent households. Some transition into hospitality management or care coordination. However, the lack of formal training pathways and the atomized nature of private employment mean that advancement often depends on networking and building a reputation within elite circles. Danish language proficiency and cultural familiarity are key differentiators.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary widely, but most positions pay between 250,000 and 350,000 DKK annually. Live-in arrangements often include accommodation and meals, reducing cash income. The occupation is not covered by collective agreements, so wages are negotiated privately.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA citizen get a work permit as a companion or valet in Denmark?**
A: It is very difficult. The Pay Limit Scheme requires a salary of at least 448,000 DKK, which is rare for this role. The occupation is not on the Positive List. Only if the employer is a qualifying company under the Fast-track Scheme might it be possible, but it remains exceptional.

**Q: What are the main duties of a companion and valet in Denmark?**
A: Duties include assisting with dressing, grooming, and personal hygiene; accompanying clients to appointments or social events; managing wardrobes and household errands; and providing companionship, especially for elderly or isolated individuals. Some roles also involve light domestic tasks.
