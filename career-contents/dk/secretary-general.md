# Secretary (General) — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 4120 (ISCO08)**

Secretaries (general) perform a variety of administrative and clerical tasks to support office operations. In Denmark, they are employed across sectors including private companies, public institutions, and non-profits. The role typically involves managing correspondence, scheduling meetings, handling phone calls, filing documents, and maintaining office supplies. While digitalisation has automated some tasks, secretaries remain vital for coordinating workflows and ensuring smooth communication within organisations. Danish labour market demands strong organisational skills, proficiency in office software, and fluency in Danish. Many secretaries have vocational training (e.g., kontoruddannelse) or a business academy degree. The occupation is stable but not expected to grow significantly, with opportunities in specialised executive support or administration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for secretaries in Denmark typically moves from general clerical duties to more specialised or senior roles, such as executive secretary, office manager, or personal assistant. Gaining experience in a specific industry (e.g., legal, medical, or technical fields) can lead to higher responsibilities and pay. Some secretaries pursue further education to transition into human resources, accounting, or management. Employers value digital proficiency, discretion, and the ability to handle multiple priorities. Networking and internal promotions are common paths. The role is often a stepping stone to broader administrative careers, with opportunities for those who continuously update their skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated meeting schedule coordination and conflict detection
- Call screening and standard response generation
- Document Formatting, Spell Checking, and Template Application
- Preliminary review of travel bookings and expense reimbursements
- Simple email classification and auto-reply

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing executive time allocation through AI schedule analysis
- Improving meeting minutes efficiency using voice transcription and summarization tools
- Generate administrative reports and insights using data analysis tools
- Using AI customer management system to enhance external contact management
- Automating workflow design to improve overall team efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Judgment and confidentiality skills when handling highly sensitive or confidential information
- Interpersonal coordination and decision-making in ambiguous or urgent situations
- Emotional intelligence and relationship management in cross-departmental communication
- Understanding and navigating informal power structures within an organization.
- Administrative tasks requiring creativity or personalized handling

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced office suite automation and scripting (e.g., VBA, Power Automate).
- Hands-on AI tools: meeting scheduling AI, speech-to-text, CRM system
- Data analysis basics (advanced Excel functions, BI tools)
- Project management basics (e.g., Agile methodology)
- Advanced communication and emotional intelligence (conflict resolution, executive support)
- Digital security and privacy protection awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital office skills
- Executive support
- Remote work adaptability
- Multilingual proficiency

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a secretary in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by sector, experience, and location but are generally regulated by collective agreements. Entry-level salaries for secretaries are modest, while senior executive secretaries earn higher wages. Public sector pay is standardized, while private sector offers more variation. Specific salary figures are not provided here, but sources like Statistics Denmark and unions (e.g., HK) offer annual surveys.

**Q: Can a non-EU secretary obtain a work visa for Denmark?**
A: Generally difficult unless the salary meets the Pay Limit Scheme threshold (currently around DKK 465,000 per year), which is above the typical secretary salary. The Positive List does not include secretaries. EU/EEA citizens have free movement. For others, alternative routes like family reunification or study-to-work may be possible, but employer sponsorship for a secretary role is rare.

**Q: What are the best ways to advance as a secretary in Denmark?**
A: Upskilling in digital tools, taking on administrative project management roles, or specialising in areas like legal or medical secretarial work can lead to promotion. Networking within professional associations (e.g., HK, DJØF) and pursuing further education (e.g., HD in administration) also help. Many secretaries move into office manager or executive assistant positions.
