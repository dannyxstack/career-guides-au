# Structural Metal Preparer and Erector — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Structural metal preparers and erectors assemble, install, and erect metal frameworks for buildings, bridges, and industrial structures. In Denmark, this trade is vital for the construction and infrastructure sectors, including wind energy and industrial projects. Workers interpret blueprints, cut and shape metal, and use welding and bolting techniques to secure components. The occupation requires physical stamina, precision, and adherence to strict safety protocols, especially when working at heights. Demand is driven by ongoing urban development, maintenance of existing structures, and Denmark's ambitious green energy transition.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins with a vocational apprenticeship, leading to a skilled worker role. With experience, workers can advance to team lead, foreman, or project manager. Specializations in welding, rigging, or offshore work offer higher pay. Many pursue further certifications in advanced welding or safety management. The occupation values on-the-job learning, and workers often move between companies and projects to gain diverse experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Precise dimensional measurement and calibration using BIM and laser scanning
- Optimize cutting and welding parameters for steel structures using AI software
- Drones automatically check structural alignment and bolt tightening status

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted crane and rigging configuration planning
- Risk prediction and construction plan optimization based on historical data
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted installation guidance and training
- Real-time tool status monitoring and predictive maintenance alerts.

**Human moat:**

- Physical operation and assembly in complex construction site environments
- Flexible judgment for handling non-standard or damaged materials
- Coordinate with the team and handle emergency situations
- Strict enforcement and supervision of safety regulations

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Building Information Modeling) software operation

- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a structural metal worker in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, location, and employer. Generally, entry-level workers earn around DKK 300,000–350,000 annually, while experienced workers can earn DKK 400,000–500,000 or more with overtime and specializations.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a structural metal worker in Denmark?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Danish employer. You may apply under the Pay Limit Scheme (if salary meets the threshold) or the Positive List for Skilled Work (if the occupation is listed). EU/EEA citizens can work freely.

**Q: What education is needed to become a structural metal worker?**
A: The typical path is a vocational education (EUD) as a structural metal worker, which includes an apprenticeship. Alternatively, relevant experience and certifications (e.g., welding) may be accepted by some employers.
