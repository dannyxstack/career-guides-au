# Advertising and Public Relations Manager — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 1222 (ISCO08)**

In Denmark, advertising and public relations managers lead communication strategies, brand positioning, and media campaigns for companies and agencies. This role combines creative direction with business acumen, overseeing teams that produce content, manage press relations, and analyze market trends. The Danish labour market values digital proficiency and data-driven decision-making, with a growing emphasis on sustainability and corporate social responsibility in communications.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically starts as a marketing coordinator or account executive, advancing to manager and eventually director or agency partner. In Denmark, many professionals combine formal education with hands-on agency or in-house experience. Networking through industry associations like Dansk Reklame and PR-branchen is common. Lateral moves into related fields such as brand management, digital strategy, or consulting are also viable paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate standardized press releases and social media posts
- Analyze advertising performance data and generate reports
- Monitoring media mentions and public sentiment, automatically compiling briefings
- Automate bidding and budget allocation based on historical data
- Create and test basic A/B versions of ad copy

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to predict consumer behavior and optimize marketing strategies
- Use AI tools for more precise audience segmentation and personalized targeting
- Analyze public opinion in real time through AI to quickly respond to crisis PR
- Using AI to assist creative ideation, quickly generating multiple versions of creative plans
- Using AI to automatically track competitor activities to support strategic decisions

**Human moat:**

- Senior-level client relationship and stakeholder management
- Long-term brand strategy formulation and creative direction control
- Cross-departmental coordination and team leadership
- Handle complex and sensitive public relations crises
- Innovative planning based on intuition and experience

**Skills to build:**

- Master mainstream AI marketing tools (e.g., Jasper, Crimson Hexagon)
- Data analysis and data-driven decision-making ability
- Prompt Engineering and Large Language Model Applications
- Automated operations of digital platform advertising (e.g., Google Ads Smart Bidding)
- Use of sentiment analysis tools (e.g., Brandwatch)
- Cross-cultural communication and strategic thinking

**Key growth areas:**

- digital marketing
- data-driven advertising
- content strategy
- brand management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for advertising and PR managers in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, company size, and location. The Pay Limit Scheme threshold (about 465,000 DKK/year) is a common benchmark for senior roles, with experienced managers often earning above this level.

**Q: Can a non-EU advertising and PR manager obtain a work visa for Denmark?**
A: Yes, if your salary meets the Pay Limit Scheme threshold (approx. 465,000 DKK/year) and your employer is approved, you can apply. Alternatively, if your employer is a Fast-track certified company, the process is faster.

**Q: Is a master's degree required to become an advertising and PR manager in Denmark?**
A: Not strictly mandatory, but a master's in marketing, communication, or business is highly valued. Many managers hold a bachelor's and have several years of relevant experience.
