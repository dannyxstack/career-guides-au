# Mining and Metallurgical Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3117 (ISCO08)**

Mining and metallurgical technicians in Denmark support operations in mineral extraction and metal processing, focusing on efficiency and safety in foundries, recycling plants, and engineering firms. With limited domestic mining, the role centers on metallurgy and material recycling, particularly in green transition sectors. Technicians work under engineers to oversee processes, test materials, and maintain equipment. Denmark's strong manufacturing and renewable energy industries offer niches in metal component production and battery recycling.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving from technician to senior technician, team lead, or into engineering roles after further education. Many gain experience in quality control, process optimization, or environmental compliance. Some transition into project management or consulting within the recycling and materials sector. Continuous training in automation, data analysis, and sustainability enhances advancement prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual drafting of mine design and construction drawings
- Resource reserve estimation based on experience
- Manually write routine mining plans and progress reports
- Manual monitoring of equipment status and energy consumption
- On-site Inspections for Hazard Identification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI for 3D modeling of ore deposits and resource assessment
- Optimizing autonomous truck routes and blasting plans using AI
- Using machine learning to predict rock mass stability and support requirements
- Integrating digital twin technology for real-time monitoring and adjustment of mining operations
- Quickly parse geological reports and compliance documents using natural language processing

**Human moat:**

- Decision-making under complex geological conditions
- Communication and coordination with government/community/Indigenous peoples
- Safety regulation enforcement and emergency incident command
- Multidisciplinary team collaboration integrating AI recommendations
- Comprehensive assessment of ethics and environmental impact

**Skills to build:**

- Geostatistics and machine learning applied to resource modeling
- Operation and configuration of automated scheduling systems (e.g., Minestar)
- Python/R data analysis and digital twin platform usage
- Mine simulation software (e.g., Vulcan, Surpac) with AI plugins
- Project Management and Cross-Departmental AI Solution Integration
- Data visualization and report automation tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Green transition
- Recycling
- Metal processing
- Automation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mining and metallurgical technician in Denmark?**
A: Salaries in this field vary by experience and sector. Entry-level technicians can expect a competitive wage, with senior roles earning significantly more. Exact figures depend on the employer and collective agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a mining and metallurgical technician in Denmark?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer with a salary above the Pay Limit threshold (currently DKK 448,000/year) or through the Fast-track Scheme if the employer is certified. The Positive List for skilled work may also apply if your job matches a listed occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for this role in Denmark?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior technician, team leader, or project manager. Further education in engineering or materials science opens paths to specialist or engineer roles. Continuous learning in automation and sustainability is valued.
