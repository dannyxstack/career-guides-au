# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 5152 (ISCO08)**

Domestic housekeepers perform cleaning, cooking, and household management tasks in private homes. In Denmark, this occupation is characterized by flexible, often part-time employment, with many positions filled by EU/EEA migrants due to low formal requirements. Demand is stable in urban areas, driven by dual-income households and an aging population requiring home support. The sector includes both formal employment and informal arrangements, with wages typically below the Pay Limit Scheme threshold for non-EU workers.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 77. Employment outlook for domestic housekeepers in Denmark is stable but limited in growth. The sector faces labor shortages, especially in Copenhagen and other large cities, as native workers often prefer other industries. Automation of cleaning tasks is advancing, but personalized services remain in demand. Immigration from EU/EEA countries supplies much of the workforce. The shift toward eco-friendly and specialized cleaning services may create niche opportunities.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for domestic housekeepers is limited; many remain in entry-level roles. Advancement can occur through specialization in eco-friendly cleaning, supervision in cleaning companies, or entrepreneurship by starting a housekeeping business. Some move into related fields like elderly care or property management. Vocational training in cleaning technology or service assistant programs can enhance prospects but is not mandatory.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Vacuuming/mopping floors (replaced by robotic vacuums/mops)
- Dishwashing (replaced by dishwashers)
- Bed making, clothing folding (replaced by smart bed-making robots/folding machines)
- Regular surface dust removal (replaced by automated dusting robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling tools to plan cleaning routes and task priorities
- Monitoring appliance status and scheduling repairs using smart home systems
- Remote control of cleaning equipment via voice assistant or APP
- Use data analytics to optimize cleaning supplies inventory and procurement

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex cleaning issues such as accidental damage and special material stains
- Communicate with employers about customized service needs and build trust
- Manage multiple smart devices and coordinate troubleshooting.
- Engaging in fine manual work (e.g., antique furniture maintenance)

**Skills to build:**

- Smart home device operation and basic troubleshooting
- Basic data analysis (supply inventory management)
- Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Time management and multitasking coordination
- Knowledge of eco-friendly cleaning technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- Eco-friendly cleaning
- Smart home technology
- Elderly companionship
- Organic housekeeping

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary, but average hourly wage is around 130-160 DKK. Annual full-time equivalent is about 250,000-300,000 DKK before tax.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as domestic housekeepers in Denmark?**
A: Generally difficult. EU/EEA citizens have free movement. Non-EU nationals require a work permit, usually not granted due to low salary thresholds below 465,000 DKK.

**Q: What are the main duties of a domestic housekeeper in Denmark?**
A: Tasks include cleaning, laundry, ironing, tidying, cooking, and sometimes pet care or light gardening. Elderly or child supervision may also be required.
