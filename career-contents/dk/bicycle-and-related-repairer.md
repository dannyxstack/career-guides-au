# Bicycle and Related Repairer — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Bicycle and related repairers maintain, repair, and service bicycles and similar vehicles in Denmark, a country with one of the highest cycling rates in Europe. The occupation is vital for urban mobility and the green transition, supporting both private users and bike-sharing schemes. Work typically occurs in independent repair shops, larger retail chains, or municipal workshops. Denmark has a strong vocational training system (VET) for this trade, often through the EUX or basic vocational programmes. The role requires technical skills in mechanics, hydraulics, and electronics, especially as e-bikes become common. Demand is steady but seasonal, with peaks in spring and summer. The occupation is classified under 'Trades and Construction' and is not currently on the official shortage list.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 245. Employment outlook for bicycle repairers in Denmark is positive due to increasing bicycle commuting, growth in cargo bikes, and the expansion of electric bicycles. However, the occupation is not included on the Danish Positive List for skilled work, limiting non-EU migration. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

Career progression typically starts with a vocational education programme (e.g., 2-year basic + 2-year main course at a technical college). After a few years of experience, repairers may specialize in e-bikes, cargo bikes, or classic bicycles. Some move into management roles at larger workshops or start their own business. Further education includes a diploma in mechanical engineering (via top-up programmes) or sales-oriented courses. The trade is well-regarded in Denmark and offers opportunities for entrepreneurship.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a bicycle repairer in Denmark?**
A: The typical pre-tax annual salary for a qualified bicycle repairer in Denmark ranges from DKK 280,000 to DKK 380,000, depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers earn around DKK 250,000. Salaries are regulated by collective agreements in many workshops.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work visa as a bicycle repairer in Denmark?**
A: It is very difficult because the occupation is not on the Positive List and the typical salary is well below the Pay Limit Scheme threshold (DKK 465,000 in 2024). Non-EU citizens generally need to qualify under family reunification, the researcher scheme, or have an exceptional wage offer. The Fast-track scheme may apply only if the employer is certified, but salary requirements still apply.

**Q: Is formal training required to work as a bicycle repairer in Denmark?**
A: While not legally mandatory, most employers require a completed vocational education (VET) as a bicycle mechanic. This typically takes 3-4 years and includes an apprenticeship. Without formal training, it is very difficult to find stable employment in the trade.
