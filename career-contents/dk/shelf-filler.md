# Shelf Filler — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 9334 (ISCO08)**

Shelf fillers, known as hyldefyldere in Danish, are retail workers responsible for stocking shelves, arranging products, and maintaining store displays. In Denmark, this role is common in supermarkets, discount stores, and larger retail chains. The job often involves part-time or flexible hours, making it popular among students and EU/EEA migrants. The work is physically active and requires attention to detail to ensure product availability and pricing accuracy. It is an entry-level position with low barriers to entry.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression from shelf filler typically leads to roles such as team leader, shift supervisor, or department manager within retail stores. Some move into logistics or warehouse picking positions. The job can serve as a stepping stone to more specialised retail roles like visual merchandising or purchasing. With additional training, some transition into supermarket management or supply chain coordination.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of retail automation systems
- Inventory data analysis and forecasting tools
- Operation of equipment such as forklifts and electric pallet jacks
- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling
- Basic Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Cross-functional team collaboration and coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce
- Supermarket chains
- Logistics
- Part-time workforce

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Denmark?**
A: Salaries follow collective agreements; they are generally in line with the retail sector's minimum hourly wage and vary by experience and store.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as shelf fillers in Denmark?**
A: Generally, non-EU citizens need a work permit; this occupation does not qualify for the Pay Limit Scheme or Positive List, so it is only feasible under EU free movement or specific bilateral agreements like the Working Holiday Visa.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for shelf fillers?**
A: With experience, shelf fillers can move into supervisory roles, department management, or logistics positions. Some pursue further training in retail management.
