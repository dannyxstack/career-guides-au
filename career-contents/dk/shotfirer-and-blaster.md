# Shotfirer and Blaster — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7542 (ISCO08)**

Shotfirers and Blasters handle explosives for demolition, mining, quarrying, and construction projects in Denmark. They ensure safe detonation, compliance with strict regulations, and coordination with site teams. The role requires precision, technical knowledge, and a strong safety focus. In Denmark, demand is niche, tied to infrastructure projects and resource extraction, with opportunities in tunneling, road construction, and natural stone quarrying. The occupation is not on the official shortage list but remains essential for specialized operations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a vocational education in blasting, followed by on-the-job training. Experienced shotfirers can advance to site supervisor, safety manager, or self-employment as a consultant. Some move into related fields such as demolition or tunnel boring. Further certifications in advanced blasting or environmental safety enhance prospects. The role is physically demanding and requires continuous learning to stay updated on regulations and technology, offering a clear but narrow career ladder.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Blasting design calculations: AI can automatically calculate charge weight, hole spacing, and other parameters to generate blasting plans.
- Safety monitoring and early warning: real-time detection of hazardous gases, vibrations, and alerts using sensors and visual AI
- Blast records and reports: automatically generate blasting logs and compliance reports
- Inventory management: AI optimizes inventory and usage records of explosives and detonators

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Geological analysis and blast optimization: AI-assisted analysis of rock structure to improve blast efficiency
- Remote monitoring and drone inspection: use drones and AI to identify unexploded ordnance and slope stability
- Safety training and simulations: VR/AI simulated blasting accidents to improve emergency response
- Equipment maintenance prediction: AI predicts failures of drilling machines and other equipment, reducing downtime

**Human moat:**

- Decision-making and adaptability in hazardous on-site environments
- Fine motor skills such as handheld charging and wiring
- Intuitive judgment of dynamic field conditions, such as geology and weather
- Statutory licensing responsibilities and enforcement of safety regulations
- Team coordination and leadership in emergency situations

**Skills to build:**

- Basic Programming and Data Management: Python/Excel for handling blasting data
- Drone operation and image analysis: used for site inspection before and after blasting
- Digital geological modeling: learn software such as Surpac, Datamine
- Basics of Sensors and IoT: Understanding deployment and troubleshooting
- Safety regulation updates: continuously learn automated blasting safety standards
- Collaboration software: such as BIM, project management tools

**Key growth areas:**

- construction
- demolition
- quarrying
- mining

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented individuals who thrive on technical precision and safety protocols.
- Physically fit people comfortable working outdoors in challenging conditions like tunnels or quarries.
- Those who can work independently and as part of a small team in high-risk environments.

**Who is not suited**
- People who are risk-averse or uncomfortable with loud noise and confined spaces.
- Those seeking routine desk jobs with predictable hours and minimal physical exertion.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a shotfirer in Denmark?**
A: Salaries are competitive due to the specialization and safety risks. Most shotfirers earn above the median full-time wage, and experienced professionals can qualify for the Pay Limit Scheme threshold. Exact figures depend on experience, project type, and employer.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen obtain a work visa as a shotfirer in Denmark?**
A: Yes, through the Pay Limit Scheme or Fast-track Scheme if the employer is certified. However, the occupation is not on the Positive List, so the employer must demonstrate that no EU/EEA candidate is available. The niche nature may make this challenging but not impossible.
