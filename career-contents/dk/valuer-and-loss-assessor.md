# Valuers and Loss Assessors — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3315 (ISCO08)**

Valuers and loss assessors in Denmark evaluate the value of property, assets, and insurance claims for financial, legal, and insurance purposes. They work in real estate agencies, insurance companies, banks, and public authorities. The role requires strong analytical skills, knowledge of property markets, and regulatory compliance. With Denmark's active property market and comprehensive insurance sector, valuers are essential for accurate assessments and fair claim settlements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression can start with a degree in finance, economics, or surveying, followed by on-the-job training. With experience, valuers can become senior assessors, team leaders, or independent consultants. Specialization in niche areas like agricultural or industrial property, or in loss adjusting, can enhance career prospects. Professional certifications from the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority or industry associations may be required for certain roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary data collation and analysis for automated property valuation reports
- Standard loss amount calculation based on historical data
- Online image assessment for minor vehicle damage
- Automatic Classification and Entry of Insurance Claim Documents
- Input parameters for conventional real estate valuation models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to identify anomalies and patterns in loss images.
- Leverage big data analytics to predict market value trends
- AI-assisted rapid generation of complex assessment report drafts
- Using natural language processing to automatically handle customer inquiries
- Improve valuation accuracy through machine learning models

**Human moat:**

- On-site inspection and physical access capability
- Independent judgment for complex or non-standard damage
- Legal and ethical compliance responsibilities
- Client trust building and communication negotiation
- Creativity in handling special cases

**Skills to build:**

- Machine learning fundamentals and model interpretation
- Geographic information systems and drone operation.
- Data visualization and analysis tools (e.g., Python, R)
- AI-assisted assessment software operation
- Enhance communication and client management skills
- Interdisciplinary knowledge (e.g., law, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Property Valuation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for valuers and loss assessors in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, employer, and specialization. Entry-level positions may start around 350,000 DKK annually, while experienced professionals can earn 600,000 DKK or more. Public sector salaries are often lower than private sector.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as valuers in Denmark?**
A: Yes, if they secure a job offer meeting salary thresholds under the Pay Limit Scheme or Fast-track Scheme. The occupation is not on the Positive List, but these routes are available.

**Q: What is the career progression for a valuer in Denmark?**
A: Starting as a junior valuer or assistant assessor, you can advance to senior valuer, team lead, or manager. Specializing in commercial property, agricultural land, or complex insurance claims opens further opportunities.
