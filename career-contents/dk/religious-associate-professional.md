# Religious Associate Professional — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3413 (ISCO08)**

Religious associate professionals in Denmark support the spiritual and community activities of religious organizations, including pastoral care, education, and administration. They work in churches, mosques, temples, and other faith-based institutions, primarily within the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Denmark as well as minority religious communities. The role requires strong interpersonal and organizational skills, often involving direct contact with members of the congregation. Employment is typically part-time or full-time, with many positions funded by the church or religious community. The field is characterized by stable but limited growth, with most opportunities arising from retirements or the establishment of new religious communities.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a religious associate professional in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on employer, denomination, and experience. Generally, starting salaries range from 250,000 to 350,000 DKK per year, with experienced professionals earning up to 450,000 DKK. Many positions are part-time, and benefits may include housing or vehicle allowances.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a religious associate professional in Denmark?**
A: Yes, but they need a job offer from an approved religious community and must apply for a Religious Worker Residence Permit. The job must meet the pay limit (448,000 DKK in 2025) and the religious community must be recognized. It is a restricted route, and approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What education is required to become a religious associate professional in Denmark?**
A: A bachelor's degree in theology or a related field is typically required, often combined with denomination-specific training. For some roles, a master's degree is preferred. Education is free for EU/EEA citizens at public universities, but non-EU/EEA students face tuition fees.
