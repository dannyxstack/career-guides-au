# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Meter readers and vending-machine collectors are responsible for manually recording utility meter readings (electricity, water, gas) and collecting cash from vending machines, parking meters, or similar devices. In Denmark, this occupation is typically carried out by utility companies or private contractors. The role involves outdoor work, driving between locations, and basic data entry. With the rise of smart meters and digital payment systems, demand is declining, but some manual collection persists in remote areas or older equipment.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 14. Denmark's employment outlook for meter readers and vending-machine collectors is negative due to automation and digitalization. Smart meters eliminate the need for manual readings, and cashless payments reduce coin collection. The number of positions is expected to shrink steadily over the next decade. Job opportunities will mainly arise from replacement needs, as few new positions are created. Workers may need to transition into related roles like utility technician or logistics driver.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression is limited. Some workers advance to supervisor roles overseeing collection routes or move into utility maintenance. The job is often entry-level with little formal training required. In Denmark, many in this role are part-time or temporary. With the decline, upskilling to areas like smart grid technology or logistics coordination is advisable for long-term employment.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings
- On-site inspection of measuring equipment faults (initial judgment)
- Generate manual meter reading reports.
- Reading meters along a fixed route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile apps or handheld terminals to assist with remote confirmation of abnormal readings.
- Identify potential leaks or tampering by analyzing consumption patterns via AI
- Use autonomous inspection drones or robots for meter reading in high-risk areas
- Provide energy advice to clients based on AI-generated energy saving reports.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair in complex on-site environments.
- In-person communication with clients over billing disputes or safety issues
- Decision-making ability in emergencies (e.g., gas leaks)
- Manual calibration and maintenance of old mechanical watch heads

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and anomaly detection
- Mobile device and specialized app operation
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic electrical/mechanical repair knowledge
- Drone or robot operation and maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- smart meters
- automation
- route optimization
- digitalization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a meter reader in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and region, but typically range from DKK 260,000 to 320,000 per year before tax. Many positions are part-time, so annual income may be lower.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens get a work permit as a meter reader in Denmark?**
A: Generally no. This occupation is not on the Positive List nor does it meet the Pay Limit Scheme salary threshold. Non-EU citizens would need a different visa route, which is unlikely for this role.

**Q: Is this a stable long-term career in Denmark?**
A: Not particularly. Automation is reducing demand. It can be a short-term job, but most workers transition to other roles within utilities or logistics after a few years.
