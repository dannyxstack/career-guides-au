# Livestock and Dairy Producer — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 6121 (ISCO08)**

Denmark is a leading exporter of dairy and pork. Livestock and dairy producers manage herds, oversee animal health, feeding, breeding, and ensure efficient milk and meat production. The sector is increasingly automated with robotic milking and data-driven management, with strong focus on sustainability, animal welfare, and organic production. Work is typically on family-owned farms or large cooperatives, requiring technical skills and hands-on involvement.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 99. Employment in agriculture has been declining, but the dairy and livestock sector remains stable due to high export demand. Automation reduces low-skilled jobs but increases need for skilled producers with technical knowledge. Organic farming is growing. The long-term outlook is steady with moderate replacement demand as older farmers retire.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression often starts as farm assistant, then herd manager, farm manager, or farm owner. Specialization in organic production, robotic systems, or herd health management is common. Further education can lead to agricultural consultancy or agribusiness roles. Some producers combine farming with agritourism or direct sales.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic feeding systems replace manual feeding and water management.
- Smart sensors automatically monitor animal health data and send alerts
- Milking robots replace manual milking operations.
- Automation of data recording and analysis replaces manual ledgers
- Driverless tractors replace some field labor

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted early disease warning system improves veterinary diagnostic accuracy.
- IoT optimizes pasture environment control and feed ratios on farms
- Drone inspection of large-scale pastures to improve management efficiency
- Data analysis tools assist breeding plans and yield prediction
- Blockchain technology tracks dairy supply chains to enhance trust

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment of animal welfare and emergency health conditions
- Unstructured problem-solving ability in complex environments
- Interpersonal communication and team management with veterinarians, staff, and others
- Trade-off decisions on macro factors such as ecology, market, and policy.
- Intuition and feel gained from long-term farming experience

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and sensor technology application
- Automated equipment operation and maintenance
- Use of AI diagnostic tools for animal health
- Sustainable agriculture and precision farming practices
- Digital marketing and supply chain tracking
- Change management and team digital leadership

**Key growth areas:**

- organic farming
- robotic milking
- sustainability
- precision livestock farming

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a livestock and dairy producer in Denmark?**
A: Salaries typically range from 350,000 to 500,000 DKK pre-tax, depending on experience, farm size, and region. Farm owners may have variable income based on production.

**Q: How can a non-EU citizen work as a livestock producer in Denmark?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a work permit under the Pay Limit Scheme if the salary exceeds the high-income threshold (569,000 DKK in 2024). Alternatively, the Fast-track Scheme or the Positive List may apply if the occupation is in shortage, but livestock producer is currently not on the Positive List. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: What are the main tasks of a livestock and dairy producer?**
A: Tasks include managing herd health, feeding, milking, breeding, maintaining facilities, ensuring compliance with animal welfare and environmental regulations, and often operating farm machinery.
