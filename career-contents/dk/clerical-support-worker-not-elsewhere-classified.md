# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

Clerical support workers not elsewhere classified cover a wide range of administrative roles in Denmark, from general office assistants to specialized clerks in sectors like healthcare, education, and government. These jobs often require strong organizational, communication, and digital skills. While many positions are filled locally, EU/EEA citizens can freely take up such roles. For non-EU nationals, opportunities are limited unless the job meets high salary thresholds or falls under a company's fast-track scheme. The demand for clerical workers is stable but influenced by digitalization and automation, which shift tasks toward more complex administrative functions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for clerical workers in Denmark often leads to specialized administrative roles (e.g., executive assistant, office manager) or lateral moves into HR, finance, or IT support. Many pursue further education, such as a short-cycle higher education (KVU) or vocational certificates in areas like payroll, procurement, or project management. Some transition into supervisory positions. The trend toward cross-functional teamwork and digital tools means continuous upskilling is valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Administration
- Data Management
- Remote Work
- Automation Adaptation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Danish Immigration Service (nyidanmark.dk)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for clerical support workers in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by sector, experience, and region but generally fall in the lower to middle range of Danish wages, often between 300,000 and 400,000 DKK annually.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work permit for a clerical job in Denmark?**
A: It is difficult unless the job is highly paid (Pay Limit Scheme) or through a certified company (Fast-track). Most clerical jobs do not meet the salary threshold, and the occupation is not on the Positive List for skilled shortage.

**Q: What education do I need for clerical work in Denmark?**
A: A vocational qualification (Kontoruddannelsen) or an Academy Profession degree in administration is typical. However, many employers also accept relevant experience and strong digital skills.
