# Agricultural and Industrial Machinery Mechanic and Repairer — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7233 (ISCO08)**

In Denmark, agricultural and industrial machinery mechanics and repairers are essential for maintaining and repairing equipment used in farming, forestry, and manufacturing. With a strong agricultural sector and advanced industrial base, these skilled technicians work on tractors, harvesters, and factory machinery, often using digital diagnostic tools. The role combines mechanical, hydraulic, and electronic skills, and is critical for ensuring productivity and minimal downtime. Demand is steady due to ongoing mechanization and the need for specialized knowledge in new technologies like precision agriculture and automation.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 4,197. The employment outlook for agricultural and industrial machinery mechanics in Denmark is positive, driven by the continued importance of the agricultural sector and the need to maintain increasingly complex machinery. There is a slight shortage of qualified technicians, particularly in rural areas. Automation and digitalization are increasing the demand for mechanics with electronic and software skills. However, the occupation is not expected to see dramatic growth, but rather stable demand with replacement needs as older workers retire.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship and vocational qualification. Experienced mechanics can specialize in areas like precision farming technology, industrial automation, or hydraulic systems. Many advance to workshop supervisor, service manager, or start their own repair business. Continuous education in electronics and software diagnostics is increasingly important. The shift toward sustainable agriculture and renewable energy machinery also opens new specialization paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Regular maintenance and simple troubleshooting (such as replacing filters, belts), automated system can monitor and alert
- Remote Monitoring and Predictive Maintenance, AI Analysis of Sensor Data for Automatic Alerts
- Spare parts inventory management and ordering, with system-optimized inventory
- Repair reports and document organization, AI-generated
- Basic parameter calibration, assisted by automation tools

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex fault diagnosis: AI-assisted analysis of historical data and real-time parameters to provide troubleshooting suggestions
- Maintenance decision support: augmented reality (AR) displays repair steps and hidden parts
- Training and skill enhancement: AI mentors customize learning paths and simulate fault scenarios.
- Tool and equipment operation: AI optimizes and adjusts maintenance parameters, improving precision
- Customer communication: AI generates plain-language explanations and repair plans to build trust

**Human moat:**

- Creative repair of non-standard equipment, requiring hands-on modification and adaptability
- On-site safety judgment and emergency handling
- Client relationship maintenance and communication.
- First-time fault diagnosis and maintenance manual development for new machinery
- Teamwork and cross-trade coordination

**Skills to build:**

- AI/Machine Learning Basics (for diagnostic tools)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor technology
- Augmented reality repair tool usage
- Data analysis and interpretation skills
- Knowledge of electrical and electronic controls
- Traditional skills such as advanced welding and precision assembly

**Key growth areas:**

- precision farming
- automation
- renewable energy machinery
- electronic diagnostics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level mechanics earn around 300,000-350,000 DKK annually, while experienced specialists can earn 400,000-500,000 DKK. Overtime and field work can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as an agricultural machinery mechanic in Denmark?**
A: Yes, if the occupation is on the Positive List for Skilled Work (it is). You need a vocational qualification and a job offer. The employer must meet certain conditions. Alternatively, the Pay Limit Scheme or Fast-track Scheme may apply if salary and employer qualify.

**Q: What training is required to become a machinery mechanic in Denmark?**
A: Typically a 3.5-4 year vocational training programme combining school and apprenticeship, leading to a certificate. EU/EEA citizens can study for free; others pay tuition fees.
