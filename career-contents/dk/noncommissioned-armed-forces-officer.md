# Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers (sergenter) are the backbone of the Danish Defence, responsible for leading and training soldiers in operational, technical, and administrative roles. They serve in the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Home Guard, ensuring readiness and discipline. Denmark's armed forces are transitioning to a more flexible, professional structure with increased international deployments and cyber defence focus. Officers typically enter through the Sergeant School (Sergentskolen) after basic military training, with career progression to higher non-commissioned ranks or commissioning as officers. The role demands leadership, physical fitness, and commitment to national security.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 4,320. Employment in the Danish armed forces is stable, with ongoing recruitment to maintain force levels and replace retirements. The 2023 Defence Agreement increases funding for new capabilities, including cyber and Arctic operations, sustaining demand for non-commissioned officers. Job security is high, but competition for specialist roles (e.g., intelligence, engineering) is moderate. The profession is not expected to grow significantly, but vacancies arise regularly.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned officer in Denmark?**
A: Salaries are determined by the collective agreement for public servants. A starting sergeant earns approximately 350,000–400,000 DKK annually before tax, with additional allowances for deployment, specialisation, and rank. The exact figure depends on experience and role.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a non-commissioned officer in Denmark?**
A: Generally, no. The Danish Armed Forces require Danish citizenship (or limited exceptions for NATO partners with specific bilateral agreements). Standard work permits for non-EU/EEA nationals are not available for this occupation.

**Q: How long does it take to become a non-commissioned officer?**
A: The standard path includes 4 months of basic training (compulsory for Danish citizens) followed by 12 months at the Sergeant School. Total time from enlistment to qualified sergeant is about 16 months, though prior civilian education can shorten this.
