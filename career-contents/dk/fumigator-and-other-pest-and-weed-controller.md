# Fumigator and Other Pest and Weed Controller — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7544 (ISCO08)**

Fumigators and pest and weed controllers in Denmark manage infestations through chemical, biological, and mechanical methods, ensuring compliance with strict environmental and health regulations. The role spans agriculture, food processing, urban settings, and public health, requiring knowledge of pest biology, pesticide safety, and integrated pest management (IPM). Danish regulation mandates authorization for handling certain pesticides, making professional certification essential. The occupation is vital for maintaining food safety, protecting buildings, and preserving ecological balance.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a fumigator in Denmark?**
A: Salaries for fumigators vary by experience, employer, and region, but generally fall within a competitive middle-income bracket for skilled trades. Overtime and certifications can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as pest controllers in Denmark?**
A: Yes, but the occupation is not on the Positive List for skilled shortage. Non-EU workers may apply under the Pay Limit Scheme if the job offer meets the minimum salary threshold (e.g., DKK 465,000 in 2023) and the employer sponsors the permit.

**Q: Do I need a license to use pesticides in Denmark?**
A: Yes, anyone handling professional pesticides must hold an authorization (autorisation) from the Danish Environmental Protection Agency. This requires passing a test and is renewable every five years.
