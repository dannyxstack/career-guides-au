# Medical Records and Health Information Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3252 (ISCO08)**

Medical records and health information technicians in Denmark manage patient health data, ensuring accuracy, accessibility, and compliance with regulations. They work in hospitals, clinics, and health IT firms, supporting clinical decision-making and public health reporting. The role requires knowledge of healthcare classifications (ICD-10, ICPC) and data protection laws. Denmark's fully digitized health sector offers opportunities in electronic health record (EHR) implementation and health data analysis.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with data entry and coding tasks. With experience, technicians can specialize in health informatics, data analytics, or management of health information systems. Advanced roles include chief medical information officer (CMIO) or health data consultant. Continuing education in health IT or data science enhances prospects. The Danish Health Data Authority offers specialized courses.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

- Health Informatics and HIPAA Compliance
- Data analysis tools (SQL, Python, R)
- AI/ML fundamentals (NLP, predictive modeling)
- Medical coding systems (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI).
- Project management and change management

**Key growth areas:**

- digital health
- health data analytics
- electronic health records
- telemedicine

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical records technician in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector but are generally competitive with other healthcare support roles. Public sector salaries follow collective agreements, while private sector offers higher potential.

**Q: Can I work in Denmark as a foreign medical records technician?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer and meet requirements. EU/EEA citizens have free movement. Non-EU/EEA citizens can apply under the Pay Limit Scheme if the salary meets the threshold, or via the Fast-track Scheme if the employer is certified.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience and further education, you can move into health data analysis, informatics management, or consulting. Specializing in areas like AI in healthcare or clinical data management can lead to senior roles.
