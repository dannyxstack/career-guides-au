# Building Structure Cleaner — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7133 (ISCO08)**

Building structure cleaners in Denmark specialise in cleaning exterior surfaces of buildings, including windows, facades, roofs, and monuments. They use specialised equipment like cherry pickers, scaffolding, and high-pressure washers. The occupation is essential for maintaining urban aesthetics and safety, with demand driven by new construction and renovation projects. Most workers are EU/EEA nationals employed through contractor firms.

**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically moves from cleaner to team leader or supervisor, or into specialised roles like high-rise window cleaning or industrial facade restoration. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building structure cleaners in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by region, experience, and specialisation. Entry-level cleaners earn around 23,000–28,000 DKK per month, while experienced specialists (e.g., rope access) can earn 30,000–40,000 DKK. Overtime and bonuses are common. Collective agreements set minimum rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU national become a building structure cleaner in Denmark?**
A: Non-EU nationals face challenges as the salary rarely meets the Pay Limit Scheme threshold (DKK 436,000/year). The Positive List does not include this occupation. EU/EEA free movement is the primary route; for non-EU, only special schemes (e.g., Fast-track if employer has a certified company) might apply, but demand is low.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, you can become a team leader, start your own cleaning company, or specialise in high-rise cleaning (rope access), industrial facade restoration, or conservation cleaning of historical buildings. Further training in scaffold safety or environmental cleaning methods can open supervisory roles.
