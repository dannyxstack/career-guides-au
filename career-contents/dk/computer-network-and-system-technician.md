# Computer Network and Systems Technicians — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3513 (ISCO08)**

Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Denmark install, maintain, and troubleshoot local and wide area networks, servers, and IT infrastructure. They are employed across private and public sectors, including IT service providers, enterprises, and government institutions. The role often involves supporting end-users, managing cloud services, and ensuring network security. With Denmark's strong digitalization push, demand for skilled technicians remains high, particularly in Copenhagen and Aarhus. EU/EEA nationals benefit from free movement, while non-EU applicants may qualify under the Positive List for skilled workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for network technicians in Denmark often begins with a vocational education (e.g., Data Technician) or an AP degree in computer science. Many move into specialized roles such as network architect, cybersecurity analyst, or IT project manager after gaining experience. Certifications like CCNA or CompTIA Network+ enhance advancement prospects. Some technicians transition to cloud engineering or DevOps. The field offers opportunities for freelance work and entrepreneurship, especially in IT consulting. Continuous upskilling is essential due to rapid technological change.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically diagnose common network issues and provide solutions
- Automatically generate network configuration and patch scripts
- Record work orders and categorize/escalate to senior teams
- User password reset and account management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI log analysis to quickly locate fault root causes
- Using network simulation tools to predict change impact.
- Early detection of anomalies through AI-driven monitoring.
- Using natural language queries to accelerate knowledge base resolution

**Human moat:**

- Root cause analysis and manual repair of complex faults
- Network architecture design and security policy formulation
- Effective communication with non-technical users
- Prioritizing urgent matters under pressure

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud network management (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Cybersecurity certifications (CISSP/CEH)
- Automation scripts (Python/Ansible)
- AI/ML basics and network analysis tools
- Hybrid network architecture design

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud Computing
- Cybersecurity
- Network Automation
- 5G Infrastructure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Computer Network Technician in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, location, and sector. Generally, entry-level technicians earn around 300,000-350,000 DKK/year, while experienced professionals can earn 400,000-500,000 DKK/year. Public sector salaries are fixed by collective agreements; private sector offers more variation. Overtime and on-call pay may add to earnings.

**Q: How can a non-EU national get a work visa as a network technician in Denmark?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a job offer from a Danish employer. The most common routes are the Positive List (if the occupation is on the list) or the Pay Limit Scheme (if salary is high enough). The employer must apply for a residence permit, which usually takes 1-3 months. EU/EEA nationals do not need a visa.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for network technicians in Denmark?**
A: With experience, technicians can specialize in cybersecurity, cloud architecture, or network management. Some become IT project managers or move into pre-sales roles. Certifications (e.g., CCNP, CISSP) and further education (e.g., bachelor's in IT) can accelerate growth. The field offers good mobility across sectors.
