# Legal Secretary — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3342 (ISCO08)**

Legal secretaries in Denmark provide administrative support to lawyers, law firms, courts, and corporate legal departments. They prepare legal documents, manage correspondence, schedule appointments, and maintain case files. The role requires strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and familiarity with legal terminology. Most legal secretaries have vocational training or a specialized office education with a legal focus. The profession is stable but faces moderate demand, as digitalization increases efficiency but also reduces the need for some manual tasks.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 1,392. The employment outlook for legal secretaries in Denmark is stable, with a modest decline expected due to automation of routine tasks. However, demand remains steady in law firms and the public sector, especially for bilingual secretaries handling international cases. Growth areas include legal tech, where secretaries assist with digital case management. Copenhagen and larger cities offer the most opportunities. Overall, the number of positions is expected to decrease slightly over the next decade, but turnover ensures replacement needs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Progression for legal secretaries typically involves moving from junior to senior roles, often within large law firms or in-house legal departments. With experience, some transition to paralegal positions or office management. Further education, such as a diploma in legal studies, can open doors to specialized roles in corporate law or litigation support. However, advancement may be limited without additional qualifications. The career suits those seeking a stable office environment with minimal travel.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Draft standardized letters and meeting minutes (AI-generated templates)
- Schedule and manage calendars and reminders (smart scheduling tools)
- Answer and transfer routine calls (AI voice customer service)
- Maintain and retrieve paper/electronic files (document management system)
- Data entry and organization (RPA and OCR automation)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Responsible for complex or sensitive business communication (requiring empathy and strategy)
- Coordinating multiple conflicting agendas and making trade-offs (interpersonal negotiation)
- Training or supervising AI tool output quality (human-machine collaboration)
- Handling sudden administrative crises (adaptability)
- Manage confidential or high-level affairs (trust and confidentiality)

**Human moat:**

- Situations requiring empathy and nuanced communication
- Confidentiality and high-trust working relationships
- Unstructured problem-solving and handling unexpected situations
- Cross-department coordination and power dynamics assessment

**Skills to build:**

- AI office tool usage and process optimization
- Basic data analysis and report making
- Project coordination and stakeholder management
- Social media and digital content management
- Advanced communication and writing skills
- Ability to quickly learn new software

**Key growth areas:**

- Legal Tech
- Corporate Law
- Bilingual Support
- Copenhagen

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a legal secretary in Denmark?**
A: Salaries typically range from 320,000 to 450,000 DKK annually, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 300,000 DKK.

**Q: Can a non-EU legal secretary get a work visa for Denmark?**
A: It is challenging because the salary is usually below the Pay Limit Scheme threshold (487,000 DKK). The Positive List does not include legal secretaries. EU/EEA free movement covers EU citizens; for others, the Fast-track Scheme may be possible if the employer is approved.
