# Creative and Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 2659 (ISCO08)**

Creative and Performing Artists Not Elsewhere Classified encompass a diverse range of professionals including visual artists, musicians, actors, and dancers who do not fit into more specific categories. In Denmark, this sector thrives particularly in Copenhagen and Aarhus, supported by a strong cultural policy and public funding. Many artists work freelance or on project basis, often combining multiple income streams. The Danish market values innovation and quality, with opportunities in digital media, live performances, and cultural events.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for creative artists in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary widely; many are self-employed. For employed artists in cultural institutions, average monthly salary is around 30,000-40,000 DKK before tax. Freelancers' income fluctuates greatly depending on projects.

**Q: Can non-EU artists work in Denmark?**
A: Yes, through the Pay Limit Scheme if salary exceeds 436,000 DKK/year, or the Fast-track Scheme for certified companies. Freelancers may need to apply under the Startup or Self-Employment scheme, which have stricter requirements.

**Q: What are the main challenges for artists in Denmark?**
A: High competition and limited permanent positions. Tax and administrative duties for freelancers can be complex. Networking is essential to secure projects.
