# Upholsterer — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7534 (ISCO08)**

Upholsterers in Denmark are skilled tradespeople who re-cover and repair furniture, vehicle interiors, and marine fittings using fabrics, leather, and padding materials. They work in furniture manufacturing, restoration workshops, and automotive upholstery shops. The Danish labour market values their craftsmanship, though the occupation is small and niche, with most positions filled through apprenticeship training rather than direct recruitment from abroad.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows a vocational path: apprenticeship (2–3 years) leading to a journeyman certificate, then mastery (mesterskab) after additional training and experience. Some upholsterers specialize in areas like marine, aviation, or antique furniture, which can command higher rates. Self-employment is common among experienced craftsmen, and a few advance to teaching roles at vocational schools.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software for standardized layout and cutting
- Sewing machine operation for simple straight stitching
- Mechanical cutting and forming of foam and filling materials
- Automated recording of inventory management and material ordering
- Repetitive filling and fastening tasks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software quickly generates and modifies soft package patterns
- AR/VR tools visualize final results, reducing rework
- Machine vision detects fabric defects, improving quality
- Digital cutting beds improve material utilization rates
- Smart supply chain systems optimize material procurement

**Human moat:**

- Customized complex patterns and hand-sewing techniques
- The ability to communicate with clients and understand personalized needs
- Experience handling special fabrics (e.g., antique textiles, leather)
- Flexibility for on-site installation, adjustment, and repair
- Comprehensive assessment of furniture structure and comfort

**Skills to build:**

- Learn CAD/3D modeling software (e.g., Rhino, SketchUp)
- Proficient in operating digital cutting and sewing equipment
- Familiar with AR/VR presentation tools
- Develop project management and client communication skills
- Knowledge of sustainable materials and eco-design trends
- Learn basic programming to customize automated workflows

**Key growth areas:**

- custom furniture
- marine upholstery
- automotive restoration
- heritage conservation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an upholsterer in Denmark?**
A: Earnings vary by experience and sector. Apprentices earn a training wage, while journey-level upholsterers typically earn between municipal collective agreement rates and market rates for specialized work.

**Q: Can a non-EU upholsterer get a work visa for Denmark?**
A: Upholsterer is not on the Positive List, so the main route is the Pay Limit Scheme, which requires a salary above ~480,000 DKK. This is rarely met, so non-EU migration for this occupation is uncommon. EU/EEA citizens face no barriers.

**Q: Is formal training required to work as an upholsterer in Denmark?**
A: While not legally mandatory, most employers require a journeyman's certificate obtained through a 3–4 year vocational apprenticeship. Experience may be accepted for self-employment.
