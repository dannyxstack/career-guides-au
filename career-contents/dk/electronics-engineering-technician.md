# Electronics Engineering Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3114 (ISCO08)**

Electronics Engineering Technicians in Denmark work with design, testing, installation, and maintenance of electronic systems across industries such as telecommunications, automation, renewable energy, and medical devices. They play a key role in the country's strong engineering sector and are integral to the green transition and digitalization efforts.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression often starts with vocational training or an AP degree, leading to roles such as project manager, senior technician, or specialist in areas like embedded systems or power electronics. Further education (e.g., Bachelor's in Engineering) can open doors to R&D and leadership positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated test case generation and execution
- Standard circuit board fault diagnosis report writing
- Component selection and parameter matching
- Organization and translation of production documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quickly verify circuit designs using AI simulation tools
- Assist in complex fault diagnosis using intelligent diagnostic systems.
- AI-assisted optimization of PCB layout and signal integrity
- Reliability prediction based on historical data

**Human moat:**

- Complex system-level fault analysis and debugging
- Cross-departmental communication and project coordination
- Innovative circuit architecture design
- Compliance verification of safety-critical systems

**Skills to build:**

- Python script automation testing
- AI/Machine Learning basics
- Embedded Systems Development
- Data visualization and analysis
- Advanced simulation tools (e.g., SPICE, CST)

**Key growth areas:**

- Renewable energy
- Automation
- IoT
- Telecommunications

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Danish Immigration Service (nyidanmark.dk)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Electronics Engineering Technician in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, sector, and region. Entry-level technicians can expect around 350,000 DKK annually, while experienced technicians may earn up to 500,000 DKK or more, especially with specialized skills in high-demand areas like renewable energy.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Electronics Engineering Technicians in Denmark?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer matching a Positive List occupation or if the salary exceeds the Pay Limit Scheme threshold. EU/EEA citizens enjoy free movement.

**Q: Do I need a specific education to work as an Electronics Engineering Technician in Denmark?**
A: A vocational education or an AP degree in electronics is typically required. Some employers may accept equivalent experience or a bachelor's degree in a related field.
