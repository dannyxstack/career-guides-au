# Announcers on Radio, Television and Other Media — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 2656 (ISCO08)**

Announcers on radio, television, and other media present information, music, news, and entertainment to audiences. In Denmark, they work for public service broadcaster DR, commercial stations, and online platforms. The role requires a clear voice, strong communication skills, and the ability to engage listeners or viewers. Competition is high, but opportunities exist in regional media and digital content creation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts at local or community stations, advancing to larger networks or national broadcasters. Many announcers move into hosting, production, or journalism roles. Continuous skill development in digital media and voice training is beneficial for long-term career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated news reporting (e.g., sports scores, financial reports, weather reports)
- Pre-recorded standard program introductions and linking words
- Simple on-site commentary (e.g. standardised event or ceremony procedures)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Real-time interactive script generation and adjustment (AI provides suggestions based on audience feedback)
- Personalized content recommendations (AI analyzes listener preferences, customizes broadcast highlights)
- Multilingual Simultaneous Interpretation and Dubbing (AI-assisted Real-time Translation)
- Voice cloning backup (AI-generated backup voice to prevent sudden voice loss)

**Human moat:**

- Emotional deep communication and improvisation (e.g., capturing touching moments in interviews)
- Brand persona building and trust establishment (loyalty to authentic voice of the audience)
- Complex topic debate and value judgment (content requiring stance on politics, ethics, etc.)
- Cross-platform community management and fan interaction

**Skills to build:**

- AI broadcasting tool operation and prompt design
- Multi-platform content planning and data analysis
- Video/audio editing and visual storytelling
- Social Media Operations and Personal Brand Building
- Real-time audience interaction skills and improvisation

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Media
- Podcasting
- Streaming
- Social Media

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with excellent verbal communication and a pleasant voice
- Creative personalities comfortable with live broadcasting
- People who thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic media environment

**Who is not suited**
- Those who are shy or uncomfortable speaking in front of a microphone
- Individuals who prefer routine tasks over spontaneous performance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an announcer in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start around 350,000 DKK annually, while experienced announcers at national broadcasters can earn 600,000 DKK or more.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as announcers in Denmark?**
A: Yes, through the Pay Limit Scheme if the salary exceeds the threshold (currently DKK 487,000/year). The Positive List does not include this occupation, so other routes are limited.
