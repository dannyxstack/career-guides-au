# Midwifery Associate Professional — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery associate professionals, known as midwives in Denmark, provide prenatal, childbirth, and postnatal care. They work in hospitals, birth clinics, and increasingly in home-birth settings. Denmark faces a shortage of midwives, particularly in rural areas, making this a high-demand occupation. The role requires a professional bachelor's degree in midwifery and authorization from the Danish Patient Safety Authority. Danish language proficiency is essential for patient communication.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows a path from staff midwife to senior midwife, then to clinical specialist or management roles such as ward manager. Further specialization in areas like ultrasound, lactation consultancy, or research is possible. Many midwives also move into teaching or public health. Continuous professional development is encouraged and often required for career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Auto-fill antenatal check records and birth reports
- Generates basic maternal education materials based on algorithms
- Automatically organize appointment schedules and patient reminders
- Basic postpartum follow-up data entry and analysis
- Use AI to analyze initial abnormal patterns in fetal heart rate monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted identification of early signs of high-risk pregnancy (e.g., abnormal data trends)
- Communicate efficiently with multilingual maternity patients via AI translation tools
- AI-driven patient education platform provides personalized guidance for postpartum women
- AI simulation training system enhances emergency care skills for conditions like postpartum hemorrhage
- Using predictive models to optimize postpartum home visit routes and priorities.

**Human moat:**

- Real-time palpation and manual delivery techniques during childbirth
- Empathy and decision-making in maternal emotional support and family communication
- Rapid assessment and manual intervention in emergencies (e.g., umbilical cord prolapse)
- Ability to provide personalized care considering the psychological and cultural background of the mother.
- On-site coordination across teams (doctors, nurses, anesthetists).

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and AI tool use (e.g. Excel, pregnancy risk models)
- Communication and cross-cultural nursing skills.
- Proficient in electronic health record (EHR) systems
- Emergency simulation training and crisis resource management.
- Telemedicine platform operation and patient education techniques.
- Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) interpretation and application

**Key growth areas:**

- maternity care
- home births
- health technology
- antenatal care

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Danish Immigration Service (nyidanmark.dk)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What influences salary levels for midwifery associate professionals in Denmark?**
A: Salaries are primarily determined by collective bargaining agreements, with variations based on experience, shift patterns, and location. Additional allowances for night and weekend shifts apply.

**Q: Can midwifery associate professionals get a work visa for Denmark?**
A: Yes, midwives are on the Positive List of skilled occupations, making them eligible for a streamlined work permit process. Employers must offer a job meeting salary and conditions standards.
