# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3255 (ISCO08)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Denmark support physiotherapists in delivering rehabilitation and therapeutic exercises to patients in hospitals, clinics, and nursing homes. They work under supervision, preparing treatment areas, assisting with exercises, and documenting patient progress. The role is vital in the Danish healthcare system, particularly in geriatric care, post-surgery recovery, and chronic disease management. Most employers in Denmark are public hospitals, private clinics, and municipal rehabilitation centers. The occupation requires a vocational education in Danish, and Danish language proficiency is essential due to patient communication.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves gaining experience in specialized areas such as geriatrics, sports rehabilitation, or neurological physiotherapy. Some assistants pursue further education to become fully licensed physiotherapists via a 3.5-year bachelor's degree, which opens higher-level roles and private practice. Others move into supervision or education of new assistants. Salaries increase with seniority and specialization, though advancement may require additional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patient treatment progress recording and report writing
- Basic vital signs monitoring and data entry
- Setup and calibration of routine treatment equipment
- Appointment scheduling and patient file management.
- Simple Rehabilitation Exercise Guidance (Standardized Movements)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans
- Balance and coordination training assisted by virtual reality (VR)
- Use wearable devices to monitor patient progress in real time and adjust intensity
- AI-assisted generation of treatment plan documents and patient education materials
- Smart voice assistant for patient communication and follow-up

**Human moat:**

- Manual therapy techniques (e.g., massage, joint mobilization)
- Empathy to assess patient mental state and pain
- Clinical judgment in adjusting treatment based on nonverbal cues
- Build trust with patients and motivate them to adhere to rehabilitation
- Handling emergencies (e.g., patient falls or discomfort)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools

- Virtual reality rehabilitation technology applications
- Patient communication and behavior change coaching
- Wearable device data interpretation.

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physiotherapy assistant in Denmark?**
A: Salaries are competitive with other healthcare support roles and increase with experience, specialization, and sector (public vs. private). For exact figures, refer to official labour market statistics.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a physiotherapy assistant in Denmark?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The occupation is not on the Positive List, so non-EU applicants must meet the Pay Limit Scheme (high salary) or be hired by a fast-track certified company. Danish language proficiency and a vocational diploma are required. EU/EEA citizens can work freely.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for physiotherapy assistants in Denmark?**
A: Assistants can specialize in areas like geriatrics or sports rehabilitation, or pursue a bachelor's degree in physiotherapy to become a licensed physiotherapist, allowing independent practice and higher responsibility.
