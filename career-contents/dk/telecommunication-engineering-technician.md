# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications engineering technicians in Denmark install, maintain, and troubleshoot network infrastructure for fixed and mobile communications, including fiber optics, 5G, and IoT systems. They work for telecom operators, internet service providers, and contractors, often in the field or at central offices. Denmark's high digitalization rate and ambitious connectivity goals drive steady demand for these technicians, especially in rural broadband expansion and urban 5G rollout. The role requires a mix of technical know-how and customer service skills, with frequent collaboration with engineers and end-users.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a telecommunications engineering technician in Denmark?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region, but starting pay for apprentices is around DKK 250,000-350,000 per year, while experienced technicians earn DKK 400,000-500,000. Senior specialists or those with certifications can exceed DKK 550,000. Collective agreements often set minimums.

**Q: How can a non-EU/EEA national get a work visa as a telecommunications technician in Denmark?**
A: The most common route is the Pay Limit Scheme, requiring a job offer with an annual salary above DKK 465,000. Alternatively, if the employer is certified under the Fast-track Scheme, processing is quicker. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: What education is required to become a telecommunications technician in Denmark?**
A: The standard path is a vocational education (e.g., Data Technician or Telecommunications Technician) through a 3-4 year apprenticeship. Alternatively, relevant certifications and on-the-job training can be accepted, but formal Danish qualifications are preferred.
