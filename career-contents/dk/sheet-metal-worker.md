# Sheet Metal Worker — career analysis · Denmark

**Occupation code: 7213 (ISCO08)**

Sheet metal workers in Denmark fabricate, assemble, and install metal components used in construction, HVAC systems, industrial machinery, and shipbuilding. The occupation is part of the country's strong tradition of vocational training, with apprenticeships combining classroom instruction and on-the-job learning. Demand is driven by ongoing construction activity, green retrofitting, and renewable energy projects such as wind turbines.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a 4-year vocational apprenticeship leading to a skilled worker certificate. Many then specialise in areas such as ventilation, roofing, or industrial sheet metal. Experienced workers can become foremen, project supervisors, or start their own business. Further education in structural engineering or construction management opens pathways to higher-level roles. Continuous training in new materials and digital tools is common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to generate CNC cutting paths for automatic metal sheet cutting
- Repetitive bending and punching operations done by robots
- Automated welding systems take over standard joint welding
- AI vision inspection systems replace manual surface defect inspection
- Nesting optimization algorithms automatically calculate the most material-efficient cutting layout.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complete complex installations with remote technical expert guidance using AR headsets
- AI-assisted design tools quickly generate ventilation duct layout plans
- Digital twin simulates installation outcomes to reduce onsite rework
- Real-time feedback on fastening torque and angle accuracy via smart handheld tools
- Predictive maintenance systems based on historical data optimize repair schedules.

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability to handle non-standard, irregular structures during on-site installation
- Expertise in diagnosing and manually repairing complex system faults
- Ability to communicate with clients to clarify needs and provide customized solutions
- Physical skills for flexible operation in confined or hazardous environments
- Intuitive understanding of material properties (e.g., ductility of different metals)

**Skills to build:**

- CNC machine programming (e.g., CNC press brakes, laser cutters)
- 3D CAD/CAM software (e.g., SolidWorks, AutoCAD)
- Industrial robot operation and maintenance basics
- Post-processing Techniques for Additive Manufacturing (Metal 3D Printing)
- Data analysis and digital twin tool application.
- Green building standards (e.g., LEED) and knowledge of energy-efficient materials

**Key growth areas:**

- Green Transition
- Construction
- Renewable Energy
- Shipbuilding

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Denmark does not take part in the EU Blue Card but runs its own skilled-migration schemes, such as the Pay Limit Scheme and the Positive List for occupations facing shortages. Eligibility depends on your job offer and salary, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sheet metal worker in Denmark?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary with experience, location, and specialisation. For specific figures, consult Statistics Denmark (LONS20) or the relevant trade union.

**Q: Can a non-EU sheet metal worker get a work permit in Denmark?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The most common routes are the Pay Limit Scheme (requires a high salary) or the Fast-track Scheme (if employed by a certified company). EU/EEA citizens have free movement and do not need a permit.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for sheet metal workers in Denmark?**
A: Workers can progress to foreman, project supervisor, or start their own business. Further education in structural engineering or construction management can lead to higher-level positions.
