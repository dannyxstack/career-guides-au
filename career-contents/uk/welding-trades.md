# Welding trades（Welding trades）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5213 – Welding trades。**

Welding trades workers join metal parts by welding, brazing and soldering, and cut and remove defects from metal using a variety of equipment and techniques. This occupation is in steady demand in manufacturing, construction, and repair services, and is considered a skilled trade with a shortage of workers in the UK.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a welding trades worker in the UK?**
答：Salaries range from £22,000 to £45,000 per year depending on experience and location. Those with advanced certifications (e.g., CSWIP) or in specialized fields (e.g., underwater welding) can earn significantly more.

**问：Can a welding trades worker immigrate to the UK via a work visa?**
答：Yes. Welding trades is eligible for the Skilled Worker visa, requiring employer sponsorship and a salary of at least £26,200 per year or the 'going rate'. It is on the Immigration Salary List, offering faster processing.

**问：What education is required to become a welding trades worker?**
答：No university degree is required. The most common route is through an intermediate or advanced apprenticeship (Level 2-3), combining on-the-job training with certification. Some also complete college courses at further education colleges.
