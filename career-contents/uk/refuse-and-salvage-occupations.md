# Refuse and salvage occupations（Refuse and salvage occupations）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：9225 – Refuse and salvage occupations。**

Collect and process refuse from household, commercial, and industrial premises, including driving refuse vehicles, operating recycling equipment, and street cleaning.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：高（★★★★☆）。** 全国从业人数约 75,000。随着废物管理法规日益严格和回收目标提升，该职业需求稳定。英国每年产生大量垃圾，岗位流失率低，就业机会充足。

**竞争度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**
**工作强度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 通常不适用，因为该职业不在技术移民职业清单上，薪资也难达门槛。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 毕业生路线，但该职业与学位无关，常见路径为PSW后转其他工作。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for refuse workers in the UK?**
答：Entry-level salary is around £20,000-£25,000, experienced workers earn £30,000+, and managers up to £40,000. Includes overtime and shift allowances.

**问：Can foreigners get a work visa for refuse work in the UK?**
答：Very unlikely. The occupation is not on the skilled occupation list and salaries are below the Skilled Worker threshold (£38,700). Only possible via family visas or settlement.

**问：What are the career progression options for refuse workers?**
答：Progress from collector to driver, supervisor, facilities manager, or transition to recycling/environmental roles. Requires LGV license and waste management qualifications.
