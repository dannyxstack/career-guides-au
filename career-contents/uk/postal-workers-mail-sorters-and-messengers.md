# Postal workers, mail sorters and messengers（Postal workers, mail sorters and messengers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：9211 – Postal workers, mail sorters and messengers。**

Collect, sort and deliver mail, documents and parcels to ensure efficient postal operations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 通常不符合技术工人签证要求，因不属于短缺职业且薪资门槛高。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 毕业生可从事此工作，但签证无担保优势。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for postal workers?**
答：Entry-level mail sorters earn £18,000-£22,000, while senior supervisors can earn up to £32,000 including overtime.

**问：Can overseas workers come to the UK as postal workers via the Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Generally no, as the salary is below the threshold and it's not on the shortage list, but other routes may exist.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities for postal workers?**
答：They can progress from mail sorter to delivery worker, team supervisor, or move into logistics management.
