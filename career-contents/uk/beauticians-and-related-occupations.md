# Beauticians and related occupations（Beauticians and related occupations）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：6222 – Beauticians and related occupations。**

Beauticians and related workers give facial and body beauty treatments, apply cosmetics and dress wigs. They typically work in beauty salons, spas, or self-owned studios, focusing on customer service and hygiene standards.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 50,000。英国美容行业预计将保持稳定增长，受健康意识和个人护理需求推动。但入门门槛较低，竞争激烈，自雇比例较高。

**竞争度：较高（★★★½☆）。**
**工作强度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 通常不符合要求，因为美容师不在可担保职业列表上，且薪资难以达到门槛。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 持有相关学位者可在毕业后留英工作2年，但美容师通常无需大学学位。 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在美容领域有杰出成就者，但机会极少。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for beauticians?**
答：Entry-level beauticians earn around £18,000-£22,000 per year, mid-level £22,000-£28,000, and senior beauticians can earn over £40,000. Self-employed income varies.

**问：Can beauticians migrate to the UK via the Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Generally no. Beauticians are not on the list of eligible occupations, and salaries rarely meet the minimum threshold (£26,200), making employer sponsorship difficult.

**问：What career development opportunities do beauticians have?**
答：Beauticians can progress to senior beautician, salon manager, trainer, or move into cosmetic sales, product development, or beauty writing. Opening their own salon is a common goal.
