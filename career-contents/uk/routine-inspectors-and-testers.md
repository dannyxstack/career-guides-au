# Routine inspectors and testers（Routine inspectors and testers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8143 – Routine inspectors and testers。**

Routine inspectors and testers inspect and test metal stock, electrical plant, machinery, electronic components, textiles, wood, paper, food, plastics and rubber goods to detect processing and manufacturing defects.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker） | 该职业不在标准技术工人签证的合格职业清单中，雇主难担保。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 仅适用于英国高校毕业生，可工作2年，但转技术工人签证受限。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can routine inspectors earn high salaries?**
答：Salaries are generally low; senior inspectors earn around £28,000-£35,000 per year, much less than many technical roles.

**问：Can I migrate to the UK through this occupation?**
答：No, this occupation is not on the eligible list for Skilled Worker visa, making employer sponsorship very difficult.

**问：Will it be replaced by automation?**
答：High risk; many routine inspections are automated. It is advisable to upskill to higher-level quality control roles.
