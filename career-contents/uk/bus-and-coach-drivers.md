# Bus and coach drivers（Bus and coach drivers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8212 – Bus and coach drivers。**

Drive buses, coaches, and mini-buses to transport passengers on fixed or non-fixed routes. Requires a valid passenger-carrying vehicle (PCV) license and assessment in traffic laws, passenger safety, and service skills. Typically employed by municipal transport companies or private coach operators.

**考证难度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。**

| 客运车辆驾驶执照（PCV Licence） | Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency (DVLA) | 必备 | 通过理论、危险感知及路考后颁发 |

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：较高（★★★★☆）。** 全国从业人数约 175,000。英国公交司机需求稳定，因城市公共交通扩张和老龄化驾驶员退休带来一定岗位空缺。零排放公交车普及可能要求司机接受新技术培训。整体就业前景平稳，但部分大城市出现司机短缺。

**竞争度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 可担保：公交司机被列入UK Visas and Immigration的职业清单（SOC 8212），满足薪资门槛（不低于£26,200或going rate）即可申请。但薪资通常接近下限，需确保职业代码不在短缺列表外。伦敦地区可能获得加分。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 适用于在英国完成本科或以上学位的毕业生，可在毕业后2年（博士3年）内从事任何水平的工作，包括公交司机。但需自行找到雇主支持后续工签转换。 |
| Subclass Health and Care Work（Health and Care Worker Visa） | 不适用：该职业不属于医疗或社会护理领域。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary for bus drivers?**
答：Entry-level around £22,000-£28,000, mid-level £28,000-£35,000, senior/supervisor up to £42,000. Overtime and weekend allowances can significantly increase earnings.

**问：Can overseas workers obtain a skilled worker visa for bus driving in the UK?**
答：Yes. Bus and coach drivers (SOC 8212) are on the list of eligible occupations for the Skilled Worker visa. You need a sponsor employer and meet the salary threshold (usually £26,200/year or the going rate). The occupation is not on the Immigration Salary List (shortage).

**问：What advancement opportunities do bus drivers have?**
答：Can progress to operations dispatcher, fleet supervisor, instructor, or transport manager. Also can move to long-distance coach, tour bus, or corporate shuttle services.
