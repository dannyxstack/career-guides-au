# Finance officers（Finance officers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：4124 – Finance officers。**

Finance officers oversee book-keeping, general accounting and other financial and related clerical functions mainly within local government and a variety of public sector organisations.

---

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker） | 该职业在可担保职业清单上，满足薪资门槛（一般岗位£26,200/年或市场薪资率）即可申请 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 英国毕业生签证，允许留英工作2年（博士3年），之后可转Skilled Worker |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up Worker） | 适用于快速成长企业，薪资要求£34,600/年，该职业可能符合要求 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for finance officers?**
答：Entry level: £22,000-£28,000, mid-level: £28,000-£38,000, senior: £38,000-£50,000, depending on location and experience.

**问：Can finance officers apply for a UK work visa?**
答：Yes, the occupation is on the eligible list for Skilled Worker visa, subject to meeting the salary threshold.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities for finance officers?**
答：Progression to senior finance officer, finance manager, or finance director, or transition to audit, financial management, or policy analysis.
