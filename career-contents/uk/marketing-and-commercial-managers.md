# Marketing and commercial managers（Marketing and commercial managers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2432 – Marketing and commercial managers。**

Marketing and commercial managers plan, manage and oversee the promotion of services, products, companies or brands, find new business, and negotiate contracts.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for marketing and commercial managers?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from £28,000 to £40,000, mid-level from £40,000 to £60,000, and senior roles from £60,000 to £100,000, with London premiums.

**问：How can overseas applicants obtain a UK work visa?**
答：Typically via a Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship and a minimum salary of £26,200, or a Global Talent visa for outstanding individuals.

**问：What is the career progression for this role?**
答：From specialist to manager to director, typically requiring 5-10 years, with advancement to CMO after gaining management and strategic experience.
