# Marketing and commercial managers — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 2432 (SOC)**

Marketing and business managers plan and oversee the promotion of services, products, companies, or brands, seek new business, develop project specifications, and negotiate contracts.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from marketing specialist or business analyst, advancing to manager, senior manager, director, and eventually to chief marketing officer or vice president of business.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated market trend report generation
- Basic review and compliance check of contract terms.
- Customer data cleaning and preliminary segmentation
- Meeting minutes and brief summaries
- Automatic summarization and visualization of multi-channel marketing data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI prediction models to optimize pricing and promotion strategies
- Drive personalized marketing campaigns through AI analysis of customer feedback
- Monitor competitor dynamics in real-time with AI tools and respond quickly
- Use AI to simulate ROI of different marketing plans to support decision-making
- A/B testing and effect evaluation of automated customer communication

**Human moat:**

- Complex negotiation and relationship maintenance
- Strategic brand positioning and long-term planning
- Cross-departmental coordination and team leadership
- Original conception of creative strategies
- Ethical judgment and crisis public relations handling

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in AI marketing platforms (e.g., HubSpot AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Data storytelling and visualisation (e.g., Tableau, Power BI)
- Basic programming or automation scripts (Python, RPA)
- Generative AI prompt engineering
- Programmatic buying and AI optimisation of digital advertising
- Agile project management and experimental design

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Marketing
- Brand Management
- E-commerce
- Data-Driven Marketing

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Skilled in communication and negotiation with business acumen
- Enjoys the combination of creative planning and data analysis
- Adapt to fast-paced work environment, goal-oriented

**Who is not suited**
- Dislike frequent changes and high-pressure work
- Lacks strategic thinking or digital marketing awareness

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Marketing and Business Managers?**
A: Entry-level annual salary around £28,000-£40,000, mid-level £40,000-£60,000, senior £60,000-£100,000, higher in London.

**Q: As an overseas applicant, how to obtain a UK work visa?**
A: Usually requires a Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship, minimum salary £26,200; or through a Global Talent visa (strong background required).

**Q: What is the career progression path for this occupation?**
A: From specialist to manager to director, typically takes 5-10 years; after accumulating management and strategic experience, one can be promoted to CMO.
