# Gardeners and landscape gardeners（Gardeners and landscape gardeners）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5113 – Gardeners and landscape gardeners。**

Gardeners and landscape gardeners cultivate flowers, trees, shrubs and other plants in public and private gardens, construct features to improve the appearance of existing terrain, and cut and lay turf.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 最低薪资要求通常为£26,200，但该职业不在短缺职业清单上，雇主需通过劳动力市场测试，较难获得担保。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 毕业生签证允许在英工作2年（博士3年），无需雇主担保，可从事任何工作。 |
| Subclass Youth Mobility（Youth Mobility Scheme） | 18-30岁特定国家公民可申请，有效期2年，可自由工作，适合短期经验积累。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Gardeners and landscape gardeners？**
- 热爱户外工作与植物园艺
- 愿意从事体力劳动，不介意天气变化
- 有耐心，注重细节，善于与客户沟通

**谁不适合学Gardeners and landscape gardeners？**
- 偏好办公室工作或高薪职业
- 对季节性工作或不稳定收入敏感

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for gardeners in the UK?**
答：Entry-level gardeners earn around £18,000-£22,000, mid-level £22,000-£28,000, and senior supervisors £28,000-£35,000. Salary varies by region, experience, and employer type.

**问：Can gardeners immigrate to the UK via a work visa?**
答：This occupation is not on the shortage list, and employer sponsorship requires passing the Resident Labour Market Test with a relatively high salary threshold. Skilled Worker visa route is difficult; alternatives like Graduate visa or Youth Mobility Scheme are more feasible.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities for gardeners?**
答：Gardeners can progress to senior gardener, team leader, or landscape project manager. Obtaining RHS qualifications or NVQs enhances prospects; some start their own horticulture businesses.
