# Crane drivers（Crane drivers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8221 – Crane drivers。**

Operate cranes, jib cranes, power-driven hoisting machinery and stationary engines to lift and move heavy materials, equipment and containers in construction, manufacturing, mining and logistics.

## 2. 考证难度 / 从业资质

**考证难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPCS（建筑技能认证计划）操作员卡 | CITB（建筑业培训委员会） | 必备 | 英国建筑工地操作起重机必备，通常要求通过理论和实践测试 |

| NVQ 2/3级移动式起重机操作 | City & Guilds 等 | 可选 | 国家职业资格，提升就业竞争力 |
| 塔吊操作员特定认证 | CPCS 或 NPORS | 必备 | 操作塔吊需要额外认证 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for crane drivers?**
答：Entry-level around £25,000-£30,000, experienced £30,000-£40,000, senior up to £40,000-£55,000. Offshore wind sectors pay higher.

**问：Can crane drivers migrate to the UK via skilled work visa?**
答：Yes, it's eligible for Skilled Worker visa. You need employer sponsorship, meet salary and English requirements, and can lead to settlement.

**问：What certifications are required to operate cranes in the UK?**
答：CPCS card is most common for construction sites; NPORS is also recognised. Tower crane operation needs additional endorsement. NVQ qualifications help career progression.
