# Telephone salespersons — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 7113 (SOC)**

Telephone sales operators receive, process and record orders for goods and services by phone, playing a key role in retail and customer service.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression includes senior telesales representative, team leader, sales manager, etc. After gaining customer service experience, can move to other sales or account management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically receive and record telephone orders
- Answer standard product information and price queries
- Handle simple order modifications and cancellations
- Generate order confirmation emails or text messages

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI real-time recommendation of cross-selling and upselling strategies
- Automatically records customer history and preferences to assist personalised service
- Intelligently analyze call data to optimize scripts and processes.
- Automatically generate customer follow-up reminders and notes

**Human moat:**

- Ability to handle complex complaints and negotiate pricing
- Build long-term client trust and relationship maintenance
- Emotional empathy and emotional comfort
- Cross-departmental coordination to resolve non-standard issues.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced applications of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems
- Data analysis and customer insight interpretation
- Basic configuration and monitoring of AI voice tools
- Conflict resolution and negotiation skills
- Multitasking coordination and priority management

**Key growth areas:**

- Customer service
- Retail sales
- E-commerce
- Call center operations

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong communication skills and good stress tolerance
- People with sales enthusiasm and patience

**Who is not suited**
- People who are not good at phone communication, introverted individuals
- Those who lack patience for repetitive work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a telemarketer?**
A: Junior annual salary around £18,000-22,000 plus commission; senior can reach £35,000.

**Q: Can a telemarketer immigrate to the UK through a work visa?**
A: Eligible for Skilled Worker visa with minimum salary threshold and employer sponsorship. Low salary narrows migration pathway.
