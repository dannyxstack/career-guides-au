# Weighers, graders and sorters（Weighers, graders and sorters）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8144 – Weighers, graders and sorters。**

Weighers, graders and sorters weigh, grade and sort materials, goods and products. This role is common in logistics centers, warehouses and factories, ensuring quality and accuracy in the supply chain.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for weighers, graders and sorters?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from £18,000 to £22,000; experienced workers earn £22,000-£26,000; senior supervisors can earn up to £30,000.

**问：Can weighers migrate to the UK via work visa?**
答：Generally no. This occupation is not on the Skilled Worker visa list, making employer sponsorship very difficult. Migration prospects are extremely low.
