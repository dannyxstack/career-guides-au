# Road construction operatives（Road construction operatives）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8152 – Road construction operatives。**

Road construction operatives construct, repair and maintain roads and lay paving slabs and kerbstones to form pavements and street gutters.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Road construction operatives？**
- 喜欢户外体力工作，不介意恶劣天气
- 注重实际操作技能，而非学术理论
- 愿意通过培训获取操作执照并进行重复性劳动

**谁不适合学Road construction operatives？**
- 寻求办公室工作或高度数字化的职业
- 无法适应高强度体力劳动和户外环境

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for road construction operatives in the UK?**
答：Entry-level salary is around £21,000-£26,000 per year, mid-level £27,000-£33,000, and senior level up to £34,000-£42,000.

**问：Can road construction operatives immigrate to the UK via a work visa?**
答：It is difficult. The role typically does not meet the skill and salary thresholds for a Skilled Worker visa and is not on the shortage occupation list, making employer sponsorship rare.
