# Bricklayers（Bricklayers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5313 – Bricklayers。**

Bricklayers erect and repair brick, pre-cut stone and concrete block structures such as walls, paving, chimney stacks and tunnel lining.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 需雇主担保，职业在短缺清单上，满足薪资门槛（£26,200 或行业标准） |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适用于快速发展企业，需雇主担保，满足薪资要求 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 允许毕业生工作2年，无需雇主担保，可积累经验后转 Skilled Worker |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for bricklayers?**
答：The average salary for bricklayers in the UK is around £30,000, with entry-level at £25,000 and experienced up to £45,000.

**问：How can foreigners migrate to the UK as bricklayers?**
答：Obtain employer sponsorship for a Skilled Worker visa. As the occupation is on the shortage list, the salary threshold is lower (approx. £26,200), but applicants must meet English and qualification requirements.

**问：What are the career progression paths for bricklayers?**
答：From apprentice to advanced bricklayer, foreman, project manager, or self-employed contractor.
