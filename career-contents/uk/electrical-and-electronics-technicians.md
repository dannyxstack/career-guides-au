# Electrical and electronics technicians（Electrical and electronics technicians）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3112 – Electrical and electronics technicians。**

Electrical and electronics technicians provide technical support for the design, development, installation, operation, and maintenance of electrical and electronic systems.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于获得雇主担保的电气与电子技师，需满足薪资门槛（通常£26,200/年或行业标准） |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适用于快速成长企业的高技能技师，需获得担保及高薪资 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 允许英国毕业生在完成学业后留在英国工作两年（博士三年），无需雇主担保 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for electrical and electronics technicians?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn around £22,000-£28,000; mid-level £28,000-£36,000; senior can exceed £50,000. Salaries vary by industry, location, and experience.

**问：Which UK work visas are available for electrical and electronics technicians?**
答：Main options include the Skilled Worker visa (employer sponsorship), Scale-up visa (fast-growing company), or Graduate visa (recent graduates). The occupation is on the shortage list, making sponsorship easier.
