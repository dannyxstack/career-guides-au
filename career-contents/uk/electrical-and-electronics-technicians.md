# Electrical and electronics technicians — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 3112 (SOC)**

Electrical and electronic engineering technicians assist in the design, development, installation, operation, and maintenance of electrical and electronic systems, providing technical support.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior technicians can advance to senior technician or team supervisor through experience, or move towards design engineer or project engineer. Some technicians, after further study, transition into engineering management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- repetitive circuit testing and parameter recording
- Generation of remote diagnostic reports for standard equipment faults
- Routine inspections and sensor data log sorting
- Auto-generation and verification of basic cabling plans
- Spare parts inventory management and automatic replenishment ordering

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-assisted analysis to diagnose fault modes in complex electrical systems
- Receive repair instructions and historical data via augmented reality headsets
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to optimize equipment maintenance schedules
- Quickly search technical manuals and regulations using natural language processing
- Use AI simulation tools to optimize circuit design and routing

**Human moat:**

- Temporary repair and debugging in unstructured environments
- Communicating with clients to understand customised needs
- Creative problem-solving in cross-system integration
- On-site safety judgment and emergency handling
- Experiential adjustments for old or non-standard equipment

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and sensor data analysis
- Use of AI-based predictive maintenance software
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted repair tool operation
- Automated Testing via Python or MATLAB Scripting
- Electrical system simulation software (e.g., ETAP, Simulink)
- Basic data visualization and report automation

**Key growth areas:**

- renewable energy
- smart grid
- automation
- electrification

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys hands-on work and solving technical problems

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for electrical and electronics technicians?**
A: Junior technician annual salary approx £22,000-£28,000; intermediate approx £28,000-£36,000; senior up to £50,000+. Salary varies by industry, region, and experience.

**Q: Which UK work visas can electrical and electronic technicians apply for?**
A: Mainly via Skilled Worker visa (requires employer sponsorship), Scale-up visa (high-growth companies), or Graduate visa (recent graduates). This occupation is on the shortage list, making sponsorship conditions more favorable.
