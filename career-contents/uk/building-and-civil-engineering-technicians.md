# Building and civil engineering technicians（Building and civil engineering technicians）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3114 – Building and civil engineering technicians。**

Building and civil engineering technicians provide technical support to civil and building engineers, including drawing preparation, material testing, site supervision, and quality control.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary growth potential for building and civil engineering technicians?**
答：With experience and professional qualifications, salaries can rise significantly. After achieving EngTech or CEng, annual salaries can reach £40,000-£55,000, with senior roles earning more.

**问：What visa routes are available for migrating to the UK for this occupation?**
答：The main route is the Skilled Worker Visa with employer sponsorship. Additionally, the Graduate Visa allows UK graduates to gain experience, and the Scale-up Visa is for high-growth companies.

**问：How can technicians progress to become engineers?**
答：Typically through completing a relevant degree or HND, accumulating work experience, and obtaining Chartered Engineer (CEng) or equivalent certification. Many companies offer on-the-job training and support.
