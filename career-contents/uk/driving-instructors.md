# Driving instructors（Driving instructors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8215 – Driving instructors。**

Driving instructors coordinate and undertake the instruction of people learning to drive cars, motorcycles, buses, forklifts and haulage vehicles, ensuring learners acquire safe driving skills and pass tests.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for driving instructors?**
答：Entry-level driving instructors earn around £18,000-£25,000 per year, mid-level £25,000-£35,000, and senior £35,000-£45,000, with self-employed instructors potentially earning more.

**问：Can driving instructors apply for a Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes, SOC 8215 is on the eligible occupations list, but requires employer sponsorship and a salary meeting the general threshold (usually £26,200/year) or the going rate.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a driving instructor?**
答：You must pass the DVSA approved driving instructor (ADI) qualification, including theory test, driving ability, and instructional ability assessments.
