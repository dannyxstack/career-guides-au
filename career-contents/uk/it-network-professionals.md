# IT network professionals — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 2137 (SOC)**

IT network professionals design, build and maintain computer networks, support network users and resolve faults.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior network engineers can advance to senior network engineers or network architects, and can also move toward cybersecurity or cloud networking, becoming technical managers or IT managers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine network configuration and changes (e.g., VLAN, routing table adjustments) are completed by AI automation tools
- Network monitoring alerts and log analysis are being taken over by AI systems
- Common fault diagnosis (e.g., link flapping) automatically detected and repaired by AI
- Network performance reports are automatically generated by AI
- Creation and deployment of baseline configuration templates

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based network capacity planning and optimization
- AI-assisted complex fault root cause analysis (correlating multi-system logs)
- Automated security policy generation and compliance checks
- AI-driven network automation script writing and testing
- Using AI for network architecture simulation and verification

**Human moat:**

- Strategic thinking to design cross-domain, high-availability network solutions
- Communicate requirements with business departments and translate into technical architecture
- Creative diagnosis of unknown, non-standard faults.
- Judgment and decision-making in security incident response
- Team management, project delivery and client relationships

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud network architecture (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Network automation (Python/Ansible/NETCONF)
- Cybersecurity (Zero Trust/SASE/Firewall Policy)
- AI/Machine Learning fundamentals (network log analysis)
- Software Defined Networking (SDN/NFV)
- Project Management (PMP/Agile)

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud Networking
- Network Security
- SD-WAN
- 5G

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with logical thinking skills who are good at solving complex network problems
- People who enjoy continuous learning of new technologies (e.g., cloud computing, cybersecurity)
- Preference for stable careers with a certain depth of technical expertise

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike handling frequent faults and emergencies
- Technicians who dislike communicating with end users

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for IT network professionals?**
A: Junior approx. £30,000–40,000, mid-level £45,000–60,000, senior £65,000–85,000; architect or management roles can exceed £100,000.

**Q: How can this occupation settle in the UK through skilled migration?**
A: The main route is the Skilled Worker visa, requiring employer sponsorship, salary meeting the Going Rate (approx GBP 40,000), and meeting English requirements. Transition via Scale-up or Graduate visa is also possible.

**Q: Is a university degree required?**
A: Not mandatory, but a bachelor's or master's degree often helps job applications, especially at large companies. Certifications like CCNA can compensate for lack of a degree.
