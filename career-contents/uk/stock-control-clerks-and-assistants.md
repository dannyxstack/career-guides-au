# Stock control clerks and assistants（Stock control clerks and assistants）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：4133 – Stock control clerks and assistants。**

Stock control clerks receive customer orders, prepare dispatch documents, and maintain inventory records to ensure smooth storage and shipping operations. This role requires attention to detail and organizational skills, typically working in warehouse or office settings.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for stock control clerks?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from £18,000 to £22,000, mid-level from £23,000 to £28,000, and senior roles up to £35,000. London may offer slightly higher rates.

**问：Can I migrate to the UK through this occupation?**
答：Yes, it qualifies for the Skilled Worker visa, but you must meet the minimum salary of £26,200 and the employer must be a licensed sponsor. It's not on the shortage list, so competition may be higher.

**问：How to improve career prospects as a stock control clerk?**
答：Obtain CIPS certification or a logistics diploma, gain supply chain experience, and progress to supervisory roles or transition into procurement or logistics coordination.
