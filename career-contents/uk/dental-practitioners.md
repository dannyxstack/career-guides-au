# Dental practitioners（Dental practitioners）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2253 – Dental practitioners。**

Dental practitioners diagnose and treat oral diseases and injuries, perform procedures such as fillings, extractions, and root canals, and advise patients on preventive care.

---

**职位需求量：高（★★★★☆）。** 全国从业人数约 41,000。英国牙医需求持续强劲，受老龄化人口和口腔健康意识提升驱动。NHS 牙科服务存在缺口，私营牙科市场增长，就业前景良好。

**竞争度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Health and Care Work（Health and Care Worker visa） | 适用于牙医等医疗专业人员，可从事NHS或独立医疗工作，年薪门槛£26,200或行业规定薪资，紧缺职业可降低要求。 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于受担保的牙医岗位，年薪≥£26,200（或行业标准），在移民薪资清单上可降低要求。 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在牙科领域有杰出成就或领导潜力的人士，无需雇主担保，但申请标准较高。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

- 不愿高压工作和长时间站立的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for dentists in the UK?**
答：Entry-level dentists earn around £32,000-£45,000; mid-level £50,000-£80,000; senior specialists or partners can exceed £150,000. Private practice offers higher income.

**问：How can overseas dentists migrate to the UK?**
答：Overseas dentists can migrate via the Health and Care Worker visa or Skilled Worker visa, requiring GDC registration and employer sponsorship. Shortage occupation may lower salary thresholds.

**问：Are overseas dental degrees recognized in the UK?**
答：Overseas degrees require GDC assessment (e.g., ORE or LDS exams) for registration. Some countries (e.g., EU) had mutual recognition, but post-Brexit rules have changed.
