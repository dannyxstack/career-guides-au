# Merchandisers（Merchandisers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3553 – Merchandisers。**

Ensure stores are supplied with goods, liaise with suppliers, decide on pricing and promotions, and advise retailers on optimum display of merchandise.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for merchandisers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around £18,000-£22,000, mid-level £22,000-£28,000, senior £28,000-£35,000, often with retail bonuses.

**问：Can overseas workers immigrate to the UK as a merchandiser?**
答：Yes, you can apply for a Skilled Worker visa if sponsored by an employer, with a minimum salary of £26,200/year and the role on the eligible occupation list.

**问：What are the career progression paths for merchandisers?**
答：You can advance to regional merchandising manager, category manager, or move into buying and retail management.
