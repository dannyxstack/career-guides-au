# Sales supervisors - retail and wholesale（Sales supervisors - retail and wholesale）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：7132 – Sales supervisors - retail and wholesale。**

Sales supervisors oversee operations and directly supervise and coordinate the activities of sales and related workers in retail and wholesale establishments.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for sales supervisors?**
答：Entry-level around £18,000-£22,000, mid-level £22,000-£28,000, senior up to £35,000+, depending on location and company size.

**问：Can I apply for a Skilled Worker visa for this occupation?**
答：Yes. Sales supervisors are on the eligible occupation list, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting the minimum salary threshold (£26,200 or higher).

**问：What is the career progression for sales supervisors?**
答：Opportunities include promotion to store manager, area manager, or retail operations director, as well as transitioning into buying or merchandising.
