# Travel agency managers and proprietors（Travel agency managers and proprietors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1225 – Travel agency managers and proprietors。**

Travel agency managers and proprietors plan, organise, direct and co-ordinate the resources and activities of travel agencies and booking offices to ensure efficient daily operations and high customer satisfaction.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for travel agency managers?**
答：The average annual salary for travel agency managers in the UK is around £30,000-£40,000, but entry-level roles can start at £18,000, while senior or self-employed roles can exceed £55,000.

**问：Can foreigners apply for a work visa as a travel agency manager in the UK?**
答：Yes, they can apply for a Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship, meeting skill and salary requirements (typically at least £25,600 per year). The occupation is not on the shortage list, and competition is high.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a travel agency manager?**
答：While a degree is not mandatory, a diploma in travel and tourism management (e.g., ABTA-accredited courses) can help. Industry experience, sales skills, and knowledge of travel products are more important.
