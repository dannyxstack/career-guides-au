# Managers in storage and warehousing（Managers in storage and warehousing）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1242 – Managers in storage and warehousing。**

Managers in storage and warehousing plan, organise, and co-ordinate the activities and resources necessary for the safe and efficient receipt, storage and warehousing of goods and for the maintenance of stocks at an optimal level.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 符合技术工人签证资格，需雇主担保，年薪至少 £26,200 或行业特定薪资 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适用于快速成长企业，需雇主担保，年薪至少 £33,000 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 英国毕业生可工作2年（博士3年），无需担保 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Managers in storage and warehousing？**
- 有组织协调能力，善于库存管理
- 喜欢物流和供应链行业
- 愿意接受实际工作环境（仓库）

**谁不适合学Managers in storage and warehousing？**
- 对重复性工作感到厌倦
- 不喜欢户外或体力活动

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary potential for a warehouse manager?**
答：Entry-level around £25,000-32,000, mid-level £32,000-45,000, senior can exceed £60,000.

**问：How can international applicants become a warehouse manager in the UK via work visa?**
答：Main route is Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. Scale-up visa also an option.

**问：What is the career progression for a warehouse manager?**
答：Can progress to regional operations manager or supply chain director, or move into broader logistics management.
