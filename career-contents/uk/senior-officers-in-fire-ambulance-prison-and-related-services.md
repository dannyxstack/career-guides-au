# Senior officers in fire, ambulance, prison and related services（Senior officers in fire, ambulance, prison and related services）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1163 – Senior officers in fire, ambulance, prison and related services。**

Plan, organise, direct and coordinate activities and resources of a specific area or function within fire, ambulance, or prison services to ensure efficient emergency response and public safety.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
