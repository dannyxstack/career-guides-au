# Dancers and choreographers（Dancers and choreographers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3414 – Dancers and choreographers。**

Dancers and choreographers devise, direct, rehearse, and perform classical and contemporary dance routines, playing a key role in the UK's creative industry, typically employed by performance companies, theatres, TV productions, and educational institutions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：低（★★☆☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 12,000。英国舞蹈行业竞争激烈，但现场演出、影视制作和旅游演艺的需求稳定增长。政府文化投资和大型活动（如奥运会）可能创造短期机会，长期就业增长缓慢。

**竞争度：高（★★★★½）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★½☆）。**

**AI替代风险：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent Visa） | 适用于在舞蹈领域有杰出成就或潜力的申请人，需获得艺术委员会认可 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 英国舞蹈专业毕业生可申请，允许毕业后留英工作2年 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of dancers in the UK?**
答：Income varies widely: junior dancers earn around £18,000-£25,000 per year, while senior dancers or choreographers can exceed £60,000, but most are on temporary contracts, often supplementing income with teaching.

**问：Can dancers migrate to the UK via work visa?**
答：The Skilled Worker visa is rarely used for dancers due to employer sponsorship difficulty; the Global Talent visa is the main route, but it requires proof of international excellence and is highly competitive.

**问：What are the career prospects for dancers?**
答：Prospects are unstable; most dancers have short careers (transitioning after their 30s) and need to plan early for teaching, choreography, or arts management.
