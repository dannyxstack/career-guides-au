# Housekeepers and related occupations（Housekeepers and related occupations）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：6231 – Housekeepers and related occupations。**

Housekeepers and related workers perform domestic cleaning and other housekeeping tasks within private households, hotels, schools, hostels and other non-private households, ensuring a clean and orderly environment.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for housekeepers in the UK?**
答：The median annual salary for housekeepers is around £22,000, with entry-level at £18,000 and senior positions up to £35,000+. Salary varies by region, employer type, and experience.

**问：Can overseas workers get a UK visa for housekeeper jobs?**
答：Yes, provided the employer sponsors a Skilled Worker visa and the salary meets the £25,600 threshold (or lower going rate). The occupation is eligible, but actual salaries may be low.

**问：What are the career prospects for housekeepers?**
答：With experience and additional skills (e.g., personal assistance, floristry), you can advance to head housekeeper, hotel housekeeping manager, or private butler. Self-employment is also an option.
