# Betting shop and gambling establishment managers（Betting shop and gambling establishment managers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1256 – Betting shop and gambling establishment managers。**

Betting shop and gambling establishment managers plan, organise, direct and co-ordinate the activities of betting shops and gambling establishments, ensuring compliance and customer satisfaction.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在UK独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 该职业不在通常的Skilled Worker清单上，但若雇主可证明技能稀缺，可能获得担保，难度较高。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 适合在英国完成学位的毕业生，可在任何职位工作2年（博士3年），但需后续转换。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for betting shop managers?**
答：The average annual salary for betting shop managers in the UK is around £28,000, with entry-level roles at £22,000 and senior roles up to £45,000.

**问：Is it easy for foreigners to get a work visa as a betting shop manager in the UK?**
答：This occupation is typically not on the shortage occupation list, and employers must prove they cannot recruit locally, making visa sponsorship challenging.

**问：What are the career progression paths for betting shop managers?**
答：Progression includes area manager, operations director, transitioning to online gambling management, or opening your own betting agency.
