# Sheet metal workers（Sheet metal workers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5211 – Sheet metal workers。**

Sheet metal workers mark out, cut, shape and join sheet metal using hand or machine tools to make and repair sheet metal products and components (excluding vehicle bodywork).

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 钣金工在可担保职业清单上，满足最低薪资要求（通常26,200英镑/年）即可申请。 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适用于快速增长企业雇佣的高技能工人，需获得担保。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a sheet metal worker in the UK?**
答：Entry-level: £22,000-£28,000, mid-level: £28,000-£36,000, senior/supervisor: £36,000-£45,000 per year. Overtime and bonuses can increase earnings.

**问：Can a sheet metal worker migrate to the UK via Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes. Sheet metal workers are on the eligible occupations list. You need a job offer from a sponsor, meet the minimum salary threshold (usually £26,200/year) and English language requirement.

**问：What training is required to become a sheet metal worker?**
答：Typically via an apprenticeship (3-4 years) combining on-the-job training and college study. An NVQ Level 3 in sheet metal fabrication is common. Some roles require welding certification and a CSCS card.
