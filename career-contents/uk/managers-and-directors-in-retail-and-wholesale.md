# Managers and directors in retail and wholesale（Managers and directors in retail and wholesale）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1150 – Managers and directors in retail and wholesale。**

Retail and wholesale managers and directors plan, organise, direct and co-ordinate the operations of major retail and wholesale establishments to maximise business performance and meet financial goals.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于获得英国雇主担保的零售与批发经理，需满足薪资门槛（通常£38,700以上） |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在零售领域有杰出成就的人才，无需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up Worker visa） | 适用于在快速成长的零售企业工作，需满足薪资要求 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 适用于在英国毕业的国际学生，可在零售行业工作2年（本科/硕士）或3年（博士） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary outlook for retail and wholesale managers?**
答：Entry-level managers earn around £25,000-£35,000, while senior directors can reach £90,000, depending on company size and location.

**问：How can overseas individuals become retail managers in the UK via work visa?**
答：You can apply for a Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship and a salary of at least £38,700. High achievers may qualify for Global Talent visa.

**问：What is the career progression for retail management?**
答：Typically starting as a management trainee or assistant manager, progressing to store manager, regional manager, and finally retail director or operations director.
