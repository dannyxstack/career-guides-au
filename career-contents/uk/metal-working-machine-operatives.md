# Metal working machine operatives（Metal working machine operatives）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8120 – Metal working machine operatives。**

Metal working machine operatives operate machines to cut, shape, abrade and machine metal, use hand and power tools to finish surfaces, and clean and polish metal workpieces.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 此职业不在现行可担保职业清单上，雇主一般无法提供工签担保。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 适合相关专业毕业生，可在完成学位后在英工作2-3年，但需找到雇主转换其他签证。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for metal working machine operatives?**
答：Entry-level salary is around £19,000-£24,000, mid-level £25,000-£32,000, senior £32,000-£40,000. Overtime and bonuses can increase income.

**问：Can I get a Skilled Worker visa for this occupation?**
答：This occupation is not currently eligible for sponsorship, so a Skilled Worker visa is difficult. Consider related roles like CNC programmer.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities?**
答：You can advance to senior operative, team leader, or move into CNC programming, quality control.
