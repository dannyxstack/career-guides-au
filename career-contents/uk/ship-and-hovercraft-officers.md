# Ship and hovercraft officers（Ship and hovercraft officers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3512 – Ship and hovercraft officers。**

Ship and hovercraft officers command and navigate ships, coordinate deck and engine room activities, operate communication equipment, and perform minor repairs on engines and mechanical systems.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for ship officers?**
答：Entry-level officers earn £25,000-£35,000, mid-level £40,000-£60,000, and captains up to £60,000-£90,000 per year.

**问：How can overseas workers migrate to the UK through this occupation?**
答：Apply for a Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship, meeting the salary threshold of £26,200 and English language requirements.
