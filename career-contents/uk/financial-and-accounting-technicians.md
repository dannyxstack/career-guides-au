# Financial and accounting technicians（Financial and accounting technicians）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3533 – Financial and accounting technicians。**

Financial and accounting technicians work alongside accountants and other financial professionals in managing the financial affairs of organisations, including bookkeeping, preparing accounts, and tax calculations.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 符合技术工人签证资格，需获得雇主担保且薪资不低于一般门槛（通常£26,200） |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 适合应届毕业生在英国工作2年（博士3年），之后可转Skilled Worker |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 若受雇于快速增长企业且薪资达标（通常£36,000），可申请 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for financial and accounting technicians?**
答：Entry-level around £20k-£26k, mid-level £26k-£35k, senior £35k-£45k, with London potentially 10-20% higher.

**问：Can this occupation apply for a Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes, it is on the eligible occupation list, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting the general salary threshold (usually £26,200).

**问：Is AAT qualification mandatory?**
答：Not legally required, but most employers prefer or require candidates to obtain AAT.
