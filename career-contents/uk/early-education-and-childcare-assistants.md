# Early education and childcare assistants（Early education and childcare assistants）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：6111 – Early education and childcare assistants。**

Early education and childcare assistants help care for children from birth to seven years in nurseries and similar settings, organizing play activities and supporting basic learning. They are foundational to the UK early years sector.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 该职业通常不符合技能工作签证薪资要求（年薪至少£26,200/时薪£10.75），且不在移民薪资清单上，雇主担保困难。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 本科或硕士毕业生可获2年签证，可从事该职业积累经验，但后续转长期工作签证路径受限。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for early education and childcare assistants?**
答：Entry-level assistants earn around £18,000-22,000, mid-level £22,000-26,000, and senior roles like Room Leader up to £26,000-32,000 per year.

**问：Can this occupation qualify for a Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Generally no. The salary is below the £26,200 threshold and the role is not on the Immigration Salary List, making employer sponsorship unlikely.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities?**
答：Yes. Assistants can progress to Senior Assistant, Room Leader, and further to Early Years Teacher or Nursery Manager, or transition to primary school teaching assistant or child social work.
