# Legal associate professionals（Legal associate professionals）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3520 – Legal associate professionals。**

Legal associate professionals provide administrative support for legal professionals such as solicitors and barristers, handling case files, researching legal issues, preparing legal documents, and investigating and making recommendations on legal matters outside normal court jurisdiction.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 适用于获得雇主担保的合格法律助理，需满足最低薪资要求（通常£26,200/年） |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 英国毕业生可申请，停留2年（博士3年）寻找工作，无需担保 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent Visa） | 不适用于普通法律助理，需为国际认可的法律专家 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for legal associate professionals?**
答：Entry-level legal assistants earn between £20,000 and £28,000 per year, with higher salaries in London.

**问：How can legal assistants migrate to the UK via the Skilled Worker visa?**
答：You need a job offer from a sponsor, the occupation must be eligible, and the salary must meet the threshold of £26,200/year (or relevant going rate).

**问：What is the career progression for legal assistants?**
答：Typically start as a junior assistant, progress to senior assistant or paralegal, and then qualify as a solicitor through further study.
