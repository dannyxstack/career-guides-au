# IT business analysts, architects and systems designers（IT business analysts, architects and systems designers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2133 – IT business analysts, architects and systems designers。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for IT business analysts in the UK?**
答：Entry-level around £30k-£45k, mid-level £45k-£65k, senior £65k-£90k, with architects or management roles exceeding £100k. London salaries are typically higher.

**问：How can an overseas IT business analyst immigrate to the UK?**
答：Mainly via the Skilled Worker visa requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary/skill requirements. This occupation is on the shortage list, aiding applications. The Global Talent visa is for exceptional talent.

**问：What are the career progression paths for IT business analysts?**
答：Progression to senior analyst, solutions architect, chief architect, or transition to project management, product management, IT director. Certifications like CBAP, TOGAF can accelerate advancement.
