# Personal assistants and other secretaries（Personal assistants and other secretaries）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：4215 – Personal assistants and other secretaries。**

Provide administrative and secretarial support to individuals, departmental or management teams, including diary management, meeting scheduling, document processing, and communication, ensuring efficient office operations.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for personal assistants?**
答：Entry-level £18k-£24k, mid-level £25k-£35k, senior up to £50k, with London typically 10-20% higher.

**问：Can overseas applicants become PAs in the UK via work visa?**
答：Yes, via Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship and salary threshold; not a shortage occupation, so competition is higher.

**问：What are the career progression paths for PAs?**
答：Advance to senior PA, office manager, or transition into HR, project management, etc.
