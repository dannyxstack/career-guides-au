# Insurance underwriters（Insurance underwriters）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3532 – Insurance underwriters。**

Insurance underwriters assess risks, decide whether to insure, and set policy terms. They are key to risk management in insurance companies.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for insurance underwriters in the UK?**
答：Entry level around £25k-£35k, mid-level £35k-£55k, senior up to £80k, varying by company and location.

**问：How can overseas applicants get a UK work visa as an insurance underwriter?**
答：Via Skilled Worker visa, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary threshold (typically £26,200/year or occupation-specific rate).

**问：What are the career progression paths for insurance underwriters?**
答：From junior underwriter to senior underwriter, team leader, risk manager, or pivot to actuarial or product development.
