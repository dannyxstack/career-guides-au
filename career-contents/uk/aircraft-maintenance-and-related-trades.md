# Aircraft maintenance and related trades（Aircraft maintenance and related trades）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5234 – Aircraft maintenance and related trades。**

Fit, service, repair and overhaul aircraft engines and assemblies; licensed aircraft engineers are coded to Unit Group 3113.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for aircraft maintenance technicians?**
答：Entry-level around £22,000-£28,000, licensed intermediate £30,000-£40,000, senior up to £42,000-£60,000.

**问：Is this occupation eligible for a Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes. It is on the eligible occupations list, shortage, with salary thresholds partially met; employer sponsorship is possible.

**问：Do I need a UK CAA license?**
答：Yes, an EASA Part-66 or CAA license is mandatory for certifying maintenance.
