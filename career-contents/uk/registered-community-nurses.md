# Registered community nurses（Registered community nurses）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2232 – Registered community nurses。**

Provide general nursing care and assist doctors in clinics, health centres, or patients' homes, forming a vital part of the UK community healthcare system.

---

**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Health and Care Work（Health and Care Worker visa） | 专为医疗保健人员设计，申请费较低，可家属陪同，五年后可申请永居 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于社区护士，需雇主担保，薪资不低于标准门槛 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 英国护理毕业生可申请，允许2年工作（博士3年），之后可转为技术工人签证 |

**PR难度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Registered community nurses？**
- 有耐心、同理心和沟通能力强的护理专业毕业生
- 希望在社区环境下独立工作、愿意家访的护士
- 寻求稳定职业且有移民意愿的医疗人员

**谁不适合学Registered community nurses？**
- 偏好医院快节奏工作、不愿频繁外出的人
- 对行政管理或文书工作缺乏耐心的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the salary of community nurses lower than that of hospital nurses?**
答：Community nurses' salaries are generally comparable to hospital nurses under the NHS pay scale. Senior community nurses (Band 7) can earn £41,000-£51,000, similar to hospital counterparts.

**问：How can international nurses migrate via the Health and Care Worker visa?**
答：First obtain NMC registration, then secure an employer sponsorship to apply for the Health and Care Worker visa, which has reduced fees and no IHS surcharge, leading to settlement after five years.

**问：What career progression opportunities are available for community nurses?**
答：They can advance to senior practice nurses, community nursing managers, or specialist nurses (e.g., diabetes, dementia care), and may also transition into public health or nursing education.
