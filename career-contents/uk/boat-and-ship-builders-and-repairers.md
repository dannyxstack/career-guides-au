# Boat and ship builders and repairers（Boat and ship builders and repairers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5235 – Boat and ship builders and repairers。**

Boat and ship builders and repairers construct, install and repair wooden structures and fittings, and shape, position, rivet and seal metal plates and girders to form the metal structures and frameworks for marine craft.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for boat and ship builders?**
答：Entry-level workers earn around £22,000-£28,000 per year, experienced workers £28,000-£38,000, and senior workers or supervisors up to £38,000-£50,000. Overtime and sea allowances can increase earnings.

**问：How can overseas workers migrate to the UK as boat and ship builders?**
答：This occupation is on the shortage list. You can apply for a Skilled Worker visa with a job offer from a sponsor, meeting a salary threshold (currently £26,200/year). UK qualifications or experience help.

**问：What are the career progression paths?**
答：You can progress from apprentice to skilled tradesperson, then to senior tradesperson, supervisor, or quality inspector. Some move into design or project management with further training.
