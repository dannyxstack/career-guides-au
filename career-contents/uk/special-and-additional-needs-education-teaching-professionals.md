# Special and additional needs education teaching professionals（Special and additional needs education teaching professionals）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2316 – Special and additional needs education teaching professionals。**

Special and additional needs education teaching professionals provide tailored instruction to children with emotional, behavioural, learning difficulties or physical disabilities, as well as exceptionally gifted pupils. They design individualised education plans, assess progress, and collaborate with parents and specialists to support each child's development.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 适用于受雇于英国学校或教育机构的合格教师，薪资需达到一般门槛（通常至少26,200英镑/年） |
| Subclass Health & Care（Health and Care Worker Visa） | 教育领域不直接适用，但特殊教育教师若受雇于NHS相关机构可能符合 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 在英完成学位的毕业生可申请，有效期为2年，允许从事任何工作 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for special education teachers?**
答：Entry-level teachers earn around £25,000-£32,000, while experienced SENCOs or heads can earn up to £70,000.

**问：How can international teachers obtain a UK work visa?**
答：Typically requires a Skilled Worker Visa with employer sponsorship and salary threshold. QTS and English proficiency are required.

**问：What is the career progression for special education teachers?**
答：Opportunities include becoming SENCO, school leadership, or moving into consultancy, with strong ongoing demand.
