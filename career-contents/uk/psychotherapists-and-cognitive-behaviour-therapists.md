# Psychotherapists and cognitive behaviour therapists（Psychotherapists and cognitive behaviour therapists）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2224 – Psychotherapists and cognitive behaviour therapists。**

Psychotherapists and cognitive behaviour therapists use various therapies in one-on-one or group settings to help people with mental health issues, stress, emotional and relationship problems, improving psychological well-being.

## 2. 考证难度 / 从业资质

**考证难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 英国心理学会（BPS）认证学位 | 英国心理学会（BPS） | 必备 | 通常要求本科获得BPS认证，并完成相关硕士课程 |
| 注册心理治疗师 | 英国心理治疗委员会（UKCP）或英国认知行为治疗协会（BABCP） | 必备 | 完成认证培训并积累临床时数后方可注册 |
| 健康与护理专业委员会（HCPC）注册 | 健康与护理专业委员会（HCPC） | 可选 | 若在NHS工作，需HCPC注册为从业心理学家 |

**PR友好度：较高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Health and Care Work（Health and Care Worker visa） | 适用于在NHS、社会护理机构或独立医疗提供商从事心理治疗工作，享受简化流程和较低费用 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 满足薪资门槛和技能要求，可在更广泛的医疗机构担保 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在心理治疗领域有杰出成就或潜力者，无需雇主担保 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for psychotherapists in the UK?**
答：NHS entry-level psychotherapists earn around £28,000-£35,000, senior roles can exceed £80,000. Private practice income varies widely.

**问：How can overseas psychotherapists migrate to the UK?**
答：Via Health and Care Worker or Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship, meeting salary and qualification requirements, typically requiring HCPC or UKCP registration.

**问：What certifications are required for psychotherapists in the UK?**
答：BPS-accredited undergraduate and master's degrees, plus registration with UKCP or BABCP. HCPC registration needed for NHS roles.
