# Restaurant and catering establishment managers and proprietors — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 1222 (SOC)**

Responsible for planning, directing, and coordinating operations of restaurants, hotels, and large-scale catering services, including menu design, staff management, customer service, and financial control, ensuring profitability and service quality.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Typically starting as restaurant supervisor or assistant manager, with experience advancing to regional manager, operations director, or starting own restaurant. Continuous learning of catering management trends is required.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automate employee scheduling and attendance statistics
- AI analyzes sales data to assist menu pricing and procurement decisions
- Automates customer bookings, checkouts, and FAQs
- Generate financial reports and inventory records
- Monitoring kitchen equipment and ingredient waste warnings

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze customer preferences to customize marketing campaigns and dish recommendations
- Optimize kitchen and front-of-house staffing in real-time through smart systems
- Use AI tools to predict customer flow and dynamically adjust operational strategies
- Use data dashboards to monitor costs and profits, quickly adjust menus and pricing
- AI-assisted staff training to improve service standardization

**Human moat:**

- Handle customer complaints and emergencies (e.g., dish issues)
- Shaping restaurant culture and team cohesion
- Create unique dining experiences and brand loyalty.
- Dealing with health inspections and legal compliance risks
- Make high-risk investment decisions (e.g., new store location selection, renovation)

**Skills to build:**

- Food and beverage industry data analysis tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI)
- Operation of AI-driven inventory and scheduling systems
- Advanced applications of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems
- Digital marketing and social media management
- Basic Accounting and Financial Modeling
- Project Management and Agile Iteration Methods

**Key growth areas:**

- Hospitality recovery
- Customer experience focus
- Tech integration
- Sustainable dining

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passionate about the food service industry, with strong leadership and communication skills
- adapt to fast-paced, long-hour work environment, handle high pressure
- Those aspiring to long-term development in the hotel or restaurant industry

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes direct customer interaction and handling complaints
- Unable to accept irregular hours (evenings, weekends, and holidays)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a restaurant manager?**
A: Entry-level around £22,000-£28,000, mid-level £28,000-£40,000, senior up to £60,000+, depending on experience and restaurant grade.

**Q: Can foreign chefs or managers come to the UK on a work visa?**
A: Yes, this occupation is on the Skilled Worker visa occupation list, requires employer sponsorship and meeting English and salary requirements. Shortage occupations may have lower salary thresholds.

**Q: What is the career progression path for a restaurant manager?**
A: Can progress from assistant manager to restaurant manager, then to regional manager, operations director, or start your own restaurant.
