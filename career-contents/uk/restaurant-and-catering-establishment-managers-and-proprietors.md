# Restaurant and catering establishment managers and proprietors（Restaurant and catering establishment managers and proprietors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1222 – Restaurant and catering establishment managers and proprietors。**

Plan, direct and coordinate catering services in restaurants, hotels and large-scale catering operations, managing staff, menus, finances and customer service to ensure profitability and quality.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Restaurant and catering establishment managers and proprietors？**
- 热爱餐饮服务行业，具有较强领导力和沟通能力
- 适应快节奏、长时间工作环境，能处理高压
- 有志于在酒店或餐饮行业长期发展

**谁不适合学Restaurant and catering establishment managers and proprietors？**
- 不喜直接面对客户和处理投诉
- 无法接受不定时工作（晚间、周末和假期）

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a restaurant manager?**
答：Entry-level around £22,000-£28,000, mid-level £28,000-£40,000, senior up to £60,000+, depending on experience and establishment.

**问：Can foreign chefs or managers come to the UK on a work visa?**
答：Yes, this occupation is on the Skilled Worker list. Requires employer sponsorship, English proficiency, and meeting salary thresholds; shortage roles may have lower thresholds.

**问：What is the career progression for a restaurant manager?**
答：From assistant manager to restaurant manager, then area manager, operations director, or opening own restaurant.
