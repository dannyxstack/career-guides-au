# Nursing auxiliaries and assistants（Nursing auxiliaries and assistants）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：6131 – Nursing auxiliaries and assistants。**

Nursing auxiliaries and assistants assist doctors, nurses and other health professionals in caring for the sick and injured within hospitals, homes, clinics and the wider community.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Nursing auxiliaries and assistants？**
- 有耐心、同情心，愿意帮助他人的人
- 希望快速进入医疗行业并积累经验的人
- 不介意轮班工作，能承受一定体力劳动的人

**谁不适合学Nursing auxiliaries and assistants？**
- 对血液、体液等感到不适的人
- 期望高薪资或快速晋升的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for nursing auxiliaries?**
答：Entry-level salary is around £19,000-£22,000, senior roles up to £30,000. NHS bands 2-4 apply; private sector may be lower.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for migration via work visa?**
答：Yes. Nursing auxiliaries are eligible for the Health and Care Worker visa, which has a lower salary threshold and fast-track processing for shortage occupations.

**问：Is there a pathway to become a registered nurse?**
答：Yes. Through Nursing Associate or Registered Nurse Degree Apprenticeship, you can study while working to gain a degree and qualify as a registered nurse.
