# Financial managers and directors（Financial managers and directors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1131 – Financial managers and directors。**

Plan, organise, direct and co-ordinate financial information of companies, activities of financial institutions, and advise on financial policy.

---

**学习难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用（AUD） |

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于获得雇主担保的财务经理，最低薪资门槛约£38,700/年（2024标准）。 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在金融领域有杰出成就或潜力的申请人，无需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适用于快速增长企业招聘的高技能财务人才，首两年无需持续担保。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for financial managers in the UK?**
答：Entry-level around £35k-£50k, mid-level £55k-£80k, senior £90k-£150k, CFO can exceed £200k.

**问：Can financial managers migrate to the UK via skilled migration?**
答：Yes. Skilled Worker visa is common, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary threshold. Global Talent visa for exceptional talent.

**问：What professional certifications are needed for financial managers?**
答：ACCA, CIMA, CFA, or ICAEW ACA are valued by employers. Not legally required but highly beneficial.
