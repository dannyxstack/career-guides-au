# Medical radiographers（Medical radiographers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2254 – Medical radiographers。**

Medical radiographers operate X-ray, ultrasound, MRI and other imaging devices for diagnosis and therapy, assist in injury and disease diagnosis, and perform interventional procedures. Therapeutic radiographers specialize in cancer radiotherapy planning and delivery.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在UK独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Health & Care Worker（Health and Care Worker visa） | 适用于NHS、独立医疗提供者或成人社会护理雇主担保的合格医疗专业人员，享有简化流程和较低费用 |
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于受雇于内政部许可担保方的放射技师，满足薪资和技能要求 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 允许英国毕业生在完成学业后留在英国工作或寻找工作，最长2年（博士3年） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for medical radiographers in the UK?**
答：Entry-level radiographers earn around £28,000-£35,000, while senior radiographers can earn £45,000-£60,000. NHS pay is based on Agenda for Change bands, typically starting at Band 5 and rising to Band 8 for advanced roles.

**问：How can radiographers migrate to the UK via work visa?**
答：Radiographers can apply for the Health and Care Worker visa, requiring sponsorship from the NHS or an approved employer. This visa offers reduced fees and priority processing. The Skilled Worker visa is also an option.

**问：How can overseas radiographer qualifications be recognized in the UK?**
答：Overseas qualifications must be assessed by HCPC, possibly requiring English language tests (e.g., IELTS) and additional training. Some countries' qualifications may have fast-track routes, but usually require an adaptation period or competency test.
