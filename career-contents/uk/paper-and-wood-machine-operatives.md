# Paper and wood machine operatives（Paper and wood machine operatives）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8131 – Paper and wood machine operatives。**

Operate machines to treat and cut wood, produce and process paper, paperboard, plasterboard, and assemble wooden crates and containers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

## 9. FAQ 常见问题
