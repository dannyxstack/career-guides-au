# Non-commissioned officers and other ranks（Non-commissioned officers and other ranks）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3311 – Non-commissioned officers and other ranks。**

Full-time members of the UK armed forces performing military duties with no civilian equivalent, including army, navy, and air force ranks.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在UK独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（½☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 一般不适用，军队职位通常不对外籍开放。少数技术岗位（如网络工程师）可能通过国防承包商担保。 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 不适用，军队体系不在此签证范围内。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 不适用，非高校毕业生从业路径。 |

**PR难度：极高（★★★★★）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for non-commissioned officers in the UK?**
答：Junior NCOs earn around £22,000-£28,000, senior NCOs up to £38,000-£50,000, plus free accommodation, healthcare, and pension.

**问：Can foreign nationals immigrate to the UK through this occupation?**
答：Generally no. Military roles are limited to UK/Commonwealth citizens and do not offer visa sponsorship. Some technical roles may be sponsored via defence contractors.

**问：What career opportunities exist after military service?**
答：The UK military provides resettlement training; common transitions include security, logistics, project management, and civil service.
