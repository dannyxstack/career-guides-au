# Cleaning and housekeeping managers and supervisors（Cleaning and housekeeping managers and supervisors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：6240 – Cleaning and housekeeping managers and supervisors。**

Manage and supervise cleaning and housekeeping tasks in private households, hotels, schools, hostels, and other premises, ensuring cleanliness and hygiene standards.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
