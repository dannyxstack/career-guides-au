# Construction and building trades supervisors（Construction and building trades supervisors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5330 – Construction and building trades supervisors。**

Construction and building trades supervisors oversee operations and coordinate the activities of workers in construction and building trades to ensure projects are completed on time, safely, and to quality standards.

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Construction and building trades supervisors？**
- 有5年以上建筑技工经验，希望晋升管理岗位的人
- 善于沟通、解决现场问题、具备安全意识的人

**谁不适合学Construction and building trades supervisors？**
- 不喜欢现场协调、应对突发问题的人
- 无法接受户外作业、尘土环境或体力要求高的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a construction supervisor?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from £30,000 to £40,000, mid-level £40,000–£55,000, and senior £55,000–£75,000, with potential overtime and project bonuses.

**问：How can foreign workers get a UK work visa as a construction supervisor?**
答：They need sponsorship for a Skilled Worker visa; SOC 5330 is eligible but not on the shortage list. Requirements include RQF Level 3 qualification and salary at least £26,200 or going rate.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities for a construction supervisor?**
答：Can progress to construction manager, project manager, contracts manager, or site director with further qualifications and experience.
