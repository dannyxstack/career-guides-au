# Shelf fillers（Shelf fillers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：9241 – Shelf fillers。**

Shelf fillers receive incoming goods from storage, check them for damage, and place them on the appropriate shelves in the store to ensure product displays are well-stocked and organized.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for shelf fillers?**
答：Entry-level shelf fillers earn around £18,000-£21,000 per year, mid-level up to £24,000, and senior supervisors around £28,000. Most are paid at minimum wage.

**问：Can shelf fillers apply for a Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Almost impossible. This is a low-skilled occupation with salaries well below the Skilled Worker visa threshold, and employers rarely offer sponsorship.

**问：Is there career progression for shelf fillers?**
答：Yes, they can advance to department supervisor, inventory manager, or purchasing assistant with experience and additional training.
