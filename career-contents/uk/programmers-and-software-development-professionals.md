# Programmers and software development professionals（Programmers and software development professionals）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2134 – Programmers and software development professionals。**

Programmers and software development professionals design, develop, test, implement and maintain software systems on a range of platforms to meet specifications and business objectives, also designing specialist software such as computer games.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 最常见签证，需雇主担保。职业在可担保职业清单上，薪资门槛约£26,200/年（短缺职业可降低）。 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适合在软件开发领域有突出成就或潜力的顶尖人才，无需雇主担保，可自雇。 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适合受雇于高增长企业的软件开发人员，需雇主为符合条件的Scale-up企业，首6个月后允许换雇主。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 面向英国应届留学生，毕业后可留英工作2年（博士3年），无雇主担保要求。 |

**PR难度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for software development in the UK?**
答：Junior roles £25k-£45k, mid-level £45k-£75k, senior £70k-£120k, with London commanding higher figures.

**问：How can a software developer immigrate to the UK?**
答：The most common route is the Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship. Top talent may consider the Global Talent visa.

**问：Is a computer science degree required to become a software developer?**
答：Not mandatory, but a degree (especially in computing) boosts competitiveness. Many successful developers come from coding bootcamps or self-study.
