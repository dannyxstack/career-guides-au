# Shopkeepers and owners - retail and wholesale（Shopkeepers and owners - retail and wholesale）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：7131 – Shopkeepers and owners - retail and wholesale。**

Shopkeepers and proprietors of small independent retail and wholesale establishments coordinate and direct daily operations, manage inventory, supervise staff, and engage in direct sales.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在UK技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 需雇主担保，但店主通常自雇，难以符合担保要求 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 允许毕业生在英国工作2年（博士3年），但需自寻雇主或创业 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the income of a shopkeeper stable?**
答：Income fluctuates significantly depending on store type, location, and performance. Early years may be below average, but can increase substantially with success.

**问：Is there a route for foreigners to become shopkeepers in the UK and obtain permanent residence?**
答：It is difficult to apply for a Skilled Worker visa directly as a shopkeeper due to the need for employer sponsorship. However, you can use the Innovator Founder visa or Graduate visa to gain experience and later switch to other routes.
