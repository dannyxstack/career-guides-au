# Construction project managers and related professionals（Construction project managers and related professionals）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2455 – Construction project managers and related professionals。**

Construction project managers oversee major construction and civil engineering projects, ensuring quality, safety, timeliness, and budget compliance, while developing traffic demand management strategies.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a construction project manager?**
答：The average salary ranges from £45,000 to £60,000, with entry-level at £25,000-£35,000 and senior roles over £90,000.

**问：How can overseas applicants obtain a UK work visa for construction project manager?**
答：Typically through the Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship and meeting salary threshold (usually ≥£26,200). Global Talent visa is also an option.
