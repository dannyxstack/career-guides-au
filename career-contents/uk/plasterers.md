# Plasterers（Plasterers）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5321 – Plasterers。**

Plasterers apply dry linings, plaster and cement mixtures to walls and ceilings, fix fibrous sheets and cast and fix ornamental plasterwork to the interior or exterior of buildings.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker Visa） | 抹灰工在移民薪资清单上，可通过技术工人签证来英，需雇主担保且工资不低于£30,960（2025年标准）。 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up Visa） | 申请人需有英国建筑公司工作邀请，且公司为成长型企业，工资不低于£33,000。 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate Visa） | 仅适用于在英国完成学位的国际毕业生，允许工作两年，但需转为Skilled Worker方可长期居留。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a plasterer in the UK?**
答：Entry-level plasterers earn £20,000-£28,000, mid-level £28,000-£38,000, and senior or self-employed can earn over £50,000 per year.

**问：Can a plasterer apply for a UK Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes. Plasterers are on the Immigration Salary List and can apply with a sponsor, meeting the minimum salary of £30,960 (2025) and holding relevant qualifications.

**问：What are the career progression paths for a plasterer?**
答：Progress from technician to foreman, project manager, or specialise in decorative plastering; self-employment or starting a business is also common.
