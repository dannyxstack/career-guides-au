# Planning, process and production technicians（Planning, process and production technicians）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3116 – Planning, process and production technicians。**

Planning, process and production technicians support production engineers in developing and executing production schedules, ensuring accuracy, cost-effectiveness, and efficiency in manufacturing processes.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for planning, process and production technicians?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from £24,000 to £28,000, mid-level from £30,000 to £38,000, and senior roles up to £50,000, depending on location, industry, and experience.

**问：How can overseas professionals migrate to the UK as a planning, process and production technician?**
答：Apply for a Skilled Worker visa with a sponsoring employer, meeting salary threshold of £26,200. HND or higher qualification and relevant experience are essential.

**问：What is the career progression for this role?**
答：Progress from junior to senior technician, then to production supervisor, process engineer, or production manager. Typically requires 3-5 years experience and professional certifications.
