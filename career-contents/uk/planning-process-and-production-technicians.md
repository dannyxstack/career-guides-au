# Planning, process and production technicians — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 3116 (SOC)**

Planning, process and production technicians assist production engineers in developing and executing production plans, ensuring accuracy and cost-effectiveness of manufacturing processes.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Progress from junior technician to senior technician, production supervisor, or process engineer. Further develop into production manager or operations manager, requiring project management and cross-departmental coordination experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial plan generation for production scheduling
- Documentation and standard operating procedure writing
- Routine statistical analysis and reporting of quality data
- Material requirements calculation and order reminders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to simulate different production plans and optimize resource allocation
- Early warning of production bottlenecks through AI anomaly detection
- Fine-tune process parameters with AI recommendations
- Use AI to assist in writing more accurate standard operating procedures

**Human moat:**

- Cross-department communication and coordination, and abnormal handling decision-making
- On-site equipment commissioning and fault emergency response
- Understand process principles and formulate improvement measures
- Review and evaluate AI output for accuracy and judgment

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (Python/SQL/BI tools)
- Production simulation software (e.g., AnyLogic, Simul8)
- Machine learning fundamentals and AutoML platform usage
- Lean Six Sigma and process improvement methods
- Programmable Logic Controller (PLC) basics
- Knowledge of Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data collection

**Key growth areas:**

- Manufacturing
- Automation
- Lean Production
- Digital Twin

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for planning, process, and production technicians?**
A: Junior salary around £24,000-£28,000, mid-level around £30,000-£38,000, and senior up to £50,000. Salary varies by region, industry, and experience.

**Q: How can overseas individuals become a planning, process and production technician in the UK through skilled migration?**
A: You can apply for a Skilled Worker visa, which requires a UK employer sponsor and a salary of at least £26,200. Having an HND or higher degree and relevant work experience is key.

**Q: What is the career progression path for this occupation?**
A: Can progress from junior technician to senior technician, production supervisor, and then to process engineer or production manager. Typically requires 3-5 years of experience and professional certifications.
