# IT technicians（IT technicians）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：3131 – IT technicians。**

Responsible for the day-to-day running of IT systems and networks, including preparation of backup systems and regular checks to ensure smooth functioning, providing user support.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：良好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于有雇主担保的IT技术人员，薪资需达到一般门槛（通常£26,200/年）或短缺职业较低门槛（£23,580/年） |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适用于高增长企业的IT技术人员，满足薪资要求（通常£33,000/年） |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 英国高校毕业生可留英工作2-3年，积累经验后转向技术工人签证 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学IT technicians？**
- 喜欢解决技术问题、动手能力强的人
- 善于沟通、乐于提供用户支持的人
- 对IT基础设施和网络有浓厚兴趣的人

**谁不适合学IT technicians？**
- 不适应繁琐日常维护工作的人
- 不喜欢与人打交道、偏好独立工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for IT technicians?**
答：Entry-level: £20,000-£28,000; Mid-level: £28,000-£40,000; Senior: up to £55,000. London may pay more.

**问：How can IT technicians get a UK work visa?**
答：The most common is the Skilled Worker visa with employer sponsorship. This occupation is on the shortage list, allowing lower salary thresholds.

**问：What is the career progression for IT technicians?**
答：Can advance to senior technician, network administrator, systems engineer, or specialize in cybersecurity, cloud computing, etc.
