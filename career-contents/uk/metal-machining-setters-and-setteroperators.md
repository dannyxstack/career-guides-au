# Metal machining setters and setter-operators（Metal machining setters and setter-operators）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：5221 – Metal machining setters and setter-operators。**

Operate machine tools to drill, bore, grind, cut, mill or shape metal workpieces to required specifications.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary ceiling for this occupation?**
答：Senior setters or programmer-operators can earn up to £45,000, but typically around £40,000.

**问：How to apply for a Skilled Worker visa for this occupation?**
答：Requires employer sponsorship, job at RQF3+ level, salary meeting general threshold (£26,200/year) or occupation-specific rate.

**问：Can I enter via apprenticeship?**
答：Yes, apprenticeships are common, offering paid training and qualification.
