## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for forklift drivers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around £22,000-£26,000, mid-level £26,000-£30,000, and senior up to £35,000. London rates are slightly higher, with night shift allowances.

**问：Can forklift drivers apply for a UK Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes, provided the salary meets the general threshold (typically £26,200/year) and a sponsor employer is secured. This occupation is not on the shortage list, so visa competition is moderate.

**问：Is there career progression for forklift drivers?**
答：Yes. You can progress to warehouse supervisor, logistics coordinator, or obtain higher qualifications for heavy machinery operation.
