# Elementary storage supervisors（Elementary storage supervisors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：9251 – Elementary storage supervisors。**

Elementary storage supervisors oversee daily operations in warehouses, docks, and other storage facilities, directly supervising and coordinating the activities of workers to ensure smooth goods handling, storage, and record-keeping.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for elementary storage supervisors?**
答：The annual salary for elementary storage supervisors typically ranges from £21,000 to £26,000 for entry-level, increasing to £32,000-£40,000 with experience. Overtime and performance bonuses can boost income.

**问：Can elementary storage supervisors immigrate to the UK via a Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on the eligible occupations list for the Skilled Worker visa. It requires employer sponsorship and meeting the salary threshold of £26,200 per year. However, it is not on the shortage occupation list, so finding a sponsoring employer is necessary.
