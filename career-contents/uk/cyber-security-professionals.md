# Cyber security professionals（Cyber security professionals）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2135 – Cyber security professionals。**

Design, implement, test and maintain cyber security systems, and track, investigate and analyse data linked to cybercrime.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于有雇主担保的网络安全职位，通常薪资需达到£26,200或以上（2024年标准） |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在网络安全领域有杰出成就或领导才能的个人，无需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 适用于在英国完成学位的国际学生，可在毕业后留英工作2年（博士3年） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for cybersecurity professionals?**
答：Entry-level around £25,000-£40,000, mid-level £40,000-£65,000, senior up to £65,000-£110,000+, with London typically higher.

**问：How can I migrate to the UK as a cybersecurity professional?**
答：Mainly via Skilled Worker visa (employer sponsorship, salary ≥£26,200) or Global Talent visa (proven exceptional ability). Cybersecurity is on the shortage occupation list, granting priority.

**问：What certifications are needed for cybersecurity work in the UK?**
答：A degree is not mandatory but often required. Common certifications include CompTIA Security+ (entry-level), CISSP (advanced), and CEH (penetration testing).
