# Directors in consultancy services（Directors in consultancy services）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：1258 – Directors in consultancy services。**

Directors in consultancy services plan, organise, direct and co-ordinate activities, resources and development of consultancies, liaise with clients and promote their business.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在UK独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 需雇主担保，薪资不低于标准门槛（通常£38,700/年） |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适合在咨询领域有杰出成就者，无需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Scale-up（Scale-up visa） | 适合在快速成长的咨询公司工作，薪资至少£36,000/年 |

**PR难度：高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for directors in consultancy services?**
答：Entry-level £45,000-£60,000, mid-level £60,000-£90,000, senior £90,000-£150,000 (including bonus).

**问：Is this occupation easy to migrate to the UK via skilled work?**
答：It is moderately difficult; typically requires employer sponsorship under Skilled Worker visa with a high salary threshold.

**问：How to progress from consultant to director?**
答：Typically 5-10 years of experience, building client base and project leadership, obtaining MBA or professional certifications.
