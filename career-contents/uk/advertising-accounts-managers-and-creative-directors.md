# Advertising accounts managers and creative directors（Advertising accounts managers and creative directors）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：2494 – Advertising accounts managers and creative directors。**

Plan, design, organise and direct advertising activities of an organisation, managing client relationships and creative teams to ensure alignment with brand objectives.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Skilled Worker（Skilled Worker visa） | 适用于获雇主担保的广告专业人员，需达到一般薪资门槛（2024年£38,700）或行业特定薪资标准 |
| Subclass Global Talent（Global Talent visa） | 适用于在广告领域具有杰出成就或潜力的领导者，无需雇主担保，但需获得认可 |
| Subclass Graduate（Graduate visa） | 适用于英国高校毕业生，可在完成学业后留在英国工作2年（博士3年），积累经验后转为工签 |

**PR难度：中高（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Advertising accounts managers and creative directors？**
- 富有创意且具备商业头脑的人才
- 善于沟通与谈判，能管理客户关系
- 能在高压环境下带领团队达成目标

**谁不适合学Advertising accounts managers and creative directors？**
- 不喜快节奏与多任务并行的工作
- 对数字营销与数据分析缺乏兴趣

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for advertising account managers in the UK?**
答：Junior: £22,000-£30,000; Mid: £35,000-£55,000; Senior: £60,000-£100,000+; London pays higher.

**问：Can advertising account managers migrate to the UK via Skilled Worker visa?**
答：Yes, with employer sponsorship and salary meeting the general threshold (£38,700 in 2024) or going rate. Not on shortage list; competition is high.

**问：Is the advertising industry at high risk of AI replacement?**
答：Some execution tasks may be automated, but creative strategy, client relationships and high-level decisions require human judgment; AI risk is moderate.
