# Sheet metal workers — career analysis · United Kingdom

**Occupation code: 5211 (SOC)**

Sheet metal workers use manual or machine tools to lay out, cut, shape, and join metal sheets to manufacture and repair sheet metal products and components (excluding vehicle bodies).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Entry-level panel beaters can progress to senior technicians, supervisors, or project managers. Through NVQ/SVQ certification and extensive experience, they can also move into estimating, design, or training roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use CAD/CAM software for automatic lofting and material nesting
- Programming and operation of CNC press brakes and laser cutting machines
- Batch production of standard components based on standard drawings
- Automated welding robots perform standardized welding tasks
- Uses AI visual inspection systems for quality checks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software accelerates 3D modelling and unfolding of complex sheet metal parts
- Real-time cost estimation system optimises quotes based on materials and processes
- Digital twin simulation reduces trial and error, improving first-pass yield
- AR glasses provide virtual overlay for guided installation
- AI predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime

**Human moat:**

- Precision measurement and manual adjustment of irregular components on site
- Intuition and experience with material properties (springback, stress)
- Communication and problem-solving in multi-trade coordination
- High-risk operations under safety standards (e.g., gas cutting)
- Creative realization and aesthetic control of customized customer requirements

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM (e.g., SolidWorks/AutoCAD Sheet Metal)
- CNC equipment programming and operation (e.g., Trumpf, Bystronic)
- Robot welding programming and collaborative robot operation
- Basic Python or macro language for automated nesting
- AR tool usage and digital twin platform operation
- Lean manufacturing and continuous improvement (e.g., Six Sigma)

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable demand
- Manufacturing
- Construction
- Skilled trades

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for sheet metal workers in the UK?**
A: Junior approximately £22,000-28,000/year, middle £28,000-36,000/year, senior or supervisor up to £36,000-45,000/year. Overtime and performance bonuses can increase income.

**Q: Can sheet metal workers immigrate to the UK via a skilled worker visa?**
A: Yes. Sheet metal worker is on the Skilled Worker occupation list, requiring employer sponsorship, meeting minimum salary (usually £26,200/year) and English language requirements.

**Q: What training is needed to become a panel beater?**
A: Typically learn through an apprenticeship (3-4 years) combining on-the-job training and college study. Can also obtain an NVQ Level 3 certificate. Some roles require welding certification and a CSCS card.
