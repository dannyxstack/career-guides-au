# Metal making and treating process operatives（Metal making and treating process operatives）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8115 – Metal making and treating process operatives。**

Operate furnaces, rolling mills, extruders, and other equipment to smelt, shape, coat, and treat metal products, ensuring safe and efficient production.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for metal making operatives?**
答：Entry-level around £22K-28K, mid £28K-35K, senior £35K-45K. Salaries vary by location, plant size, and skill level, often including shift allowances.

**问：Can this occupation apply for a UK work visa?**
答：Yes, Skilled Worker visa is possible, but salary may fall below the £26,200 threshold; employer may need to show shortage or use going rate.

**问：What are the future prospects for this occupation?**
答：Automation may reduce low-skilled roles, but green steel and re-industrialization create new opportunities. Higher skills ensure stability.
