# Chemical and related process operatives（Chemical and related process operatives）职业分析 · UK

**职业代码：8113 – Chemical and related process operatives。**

Operate machinery for chemical and related material processing, including chemical/heat treatment, synthetic material manufacturing, textile bleaching/dyeing, and tanning hides for leather products.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

答：Yes, it is on the eligible occupations list. Requires employer sponsorship and meeting salary threshold (typically £26,200/year or going rate).
