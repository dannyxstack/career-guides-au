# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications engineering technicians in China install, maintain, and repair equipment for telecom networks, including fiber optics, 5G base stations, and satellite systems. With the rapid rollout of 5G and expansion of IoT, demand is high in both urban and rural areas. Technicians work for state-owned carriers (China Mobile, China Unicom, China Telecom) or private contractors. The role requires technical proficiency and often involves field work. China's 'Broadband China' strategy drives continuous infrastructure upgrades, ensuring steady employment for skilled technicians.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a telecommunications engineering technician in China?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level technicians earn approximately 80,000-120,000 CNY annually, while senior technicians with 5+ years can earn 150,000-200,000 CNY. State-owned enterprises may offer slightly lower base pay but generous benefits.

**Q: Can foreign telecommunications engineering technicians get a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Most technicians qualify for a Z visa under Tier C (if with vocational qualifications and experience) or Tier B (with a degree). Employer sponsorship is mandatory, and positions must be listed on the local labor market demand list. High-level experts may qualify for Tier A or an R visa.

**Q: Is there a path to permanent residency for telecom technicians in China?**
A: Permanent residence (绿卡) is extremely rare and not points-based. It generally requires significant achievements, long-term residence, or exceptional contributions. Telecom technicians are unlikely to qualify unless they hold senior roles with national impact.
