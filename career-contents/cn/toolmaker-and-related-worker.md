# Toolmaker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7222 (ISCO08)**

Toolmakers and related workers in China manufacture precision tools, dies, jigs, and fixtures used in machining and production processes. They operate machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and grinders to create components with high accuracy. The role is concentrated in China's manufacturing hubs like Guangdong, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang, supporting industries such as automotive, electronics, and aerospace. Skilled toolmakers are essential for maintaining quality and efficiency in mass production, and the profession requires proficiency in reading technical drawings and using CAD/CAM software.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for toolmakers in China typically starts as an apprentice or junior machinist, advancing to senior toolmaker, then to supervisor or mold/die designer. Some move into quality control or production management. Specializing in high-precision industries (e.g., medical devices, aerospace) can lead to higher earning potential. Certification in CNC programming or CAD/CAM enhances mobility. The path may be linear within a firm or involve moving between manufacturing hubs. Experienced toolmakers sometimes transition into technical training or entrepreneurship in mold-making.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual CNC programming and tool path generation
- Simple repetitive machine operations (e.g., loading/unloading workpieces, starting machining cycles)
- Routine dimensional measurement and quality recording (manual measurement with calipers and micrometers)
- Paper-based drawing management and document archiving
- Basic troubleshooting (based on fixed diagnostic procedures)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted tool path optimization (automatic collision avoidance, improved machining efficiency)
- Machine learning-based online quality inspection (real-time prediction of dimensional deviations)
- Digital twin simulation of machining processes (pre-validate fixture designs)
- Smart production scheduling system integration (automatic assignment of machine tasks)
- Augmented reality-assisted assembly and debugging (overlaying virtual guidance)

**Human moat:**

- Intuitive judgment in complex fixture design and debugging
- On-site diagnosis and repair of multi-process integration issues.
- Ability to communicate non-standard requirements with clients/engineers
- Safety responsibility and process innovation decisions
- Precision manual finishing and assembly touch

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX machining modules)
- Robot-assisted loading/unloading programming and integration
- Python or MATLAB for machining data analysis
- Basics of additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and smart tool management
- Quality control data visualization (e.g., Power BI)

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Manufacturing
- Automotive Tooling
- Aerospace Components
- Smart Factory

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a toolmaker in China?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level toolmakers earn around 50,000-80,000 CNY per year; experienced ones can earn 100,000-200,000 CNY in developed manufacturing hubs. Highly skilled mold makers in aerospace or automotive may exceed 250,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreign toolmaker get a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The employer must demonstrate that the role cannot be filled by a local worker. Most toolmaker positions are classified as Tier C (普通人才), requiring a bachelor's degree and two years of relevant experience. Employers must also pay high social insurance costs.

**Q: Is there a certification requirement for toolmakers in China?**
A: Yes, the Toolmaker Vocational Qualification (工具钳工职业资格) issued by MOHRSS is mandatory for many positions, especially in state-owned enterprises. It ranges from Level 5 (entry) to Level 1 (master).
