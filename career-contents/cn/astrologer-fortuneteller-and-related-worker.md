# Astrologer, Fortune-Teller and Related Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 5161 (ISCO08)**

In China, astrologers, fortune-tellers and related workers practice traditional Chinese metaphysics, including bazi (八字), feng shui (风水), and face reading (面相). They often operate as independent consultants or in small shops, especially in cultural hubs like Beijing and Hong Kong. The occupation is not formally recognized under China's MOHRSS classification, and practitioners typically gain skills through apprenticeships or family traditions. With the rise of cultural tourism and online wellness trends, there is niche demand for such services among domestic and international clients.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an astrologer in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely from irregular earnings to over 200,000 CNY annually for established practitioners with wealthy clients. Most work on a fee-per-session basis and income is highly variable.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa to be a fortune-teller in China?**
A: It is very difficult. The occupation is not on shortage lists, and sponsorship is rare unless the foreigner has exceptional expertise and is hired by a cultural institution. A Z work visa is theoretically possible but requires proving the role cannot be filled by a Chinese national.

**Q: Is formal training in Chinese astrology available at universities?**
A: No. Training is through informal apprenticeships, private courses, or online programs. Some cultural institutes offer short-term workshops, but no accredited degrees exist.
