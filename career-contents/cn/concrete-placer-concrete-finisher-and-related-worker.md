# Concrete Placer and Finisher — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7114 (ISCO08)**

Concrete placers and finishers are skilled tradespeople who place, smooth, and finish concrete surfaces in construction projects across China. They work on residential, commercial, and infrastructure sites, ensuring concrete is properly poured, leveled, and cured to meet structural and aesthetic standards. This role is essential in China's rapidly urbanizing economy, with demand driven by massive infrastructure projects like highways, bridges, and high-speed rail, as well as ongoing real estate development. The work is physically demanding and typically requires vocational training or apprenticeship. While most workers are domestic, foreign workers are rarely sponsored for this role due to ample local labor supply.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for concrete placers typically starts from laborer to skilled finisher, then to supervisor or foreman. Experienced workers may move into quality control, site management, or start their own subcontracting businesses. Certification through vocational schools or MOHRSS skill levels (e.g., Level 5 to Level 1) can enhance income and advancement. Some workers transition to related trades like formwork or rebar installation. In China's construction ecosystem, career mobility is limited without formal training, but experienced finishers can earn higher wages on large-scale projects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a concrete finisher in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region, project type, and skill level. Entry-level workers may earn around 60,000–90,000 CNY annually, while experienced finishers on major infrastructure projects can earn 120,000–150,000 CNY. Foreign workers are rarely hired, so salary data for expats is scarce.

**Q: Can a foreign concrete placer get a work visa for China?**
A: In theory, a Z visa under Category C (ordinary worker) is possible if an employer obtains a work permit. However, approval is difficult because the role is not on any shortage list, and domestic labor is abundant. Most employers do not sponsor visas for this occupation.

**Q: Do I need a Chinese certificate to work as a concrete finisher?**
A: A safety training certificate is mandatory for all construction workers. A vocational skill certificate (e.g., from MOHRSS) is not legally required but is strongly preferred by employers and can lead to higher pay.
