# Insulation Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7124 (ISCO08)**

Insulation workers in China install and maintain thermal, acoustic, and fireproofing materials in buildings, industrial facilities, and infrastructure projects. With China's push for energy efficiency and green building standards under the 14th Five-Year Plan, demand for skilled insulation workers is growing in both new construction and retrofitting. Work is typically project-based, with roles in construction companies, specialized insulation contractors, and manufacturing plants. The occupation is classified under construction trades, requiring vocational training and national skill certifications. Foreign workers are rare, as the role is domestically served and language-intensive.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for insulation workers in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level workers may earn around 50,000-80,000 CNY annually, while experienced journeymen in major cities can make 100,000-150,000 CNY. Project bonuses and overtime are common.

**Q: Can foreign insulation workers get a work visa for China?**
A: It is very difficult. Insulation work is classified under Category C (limited supply) of the Foreigner's Work Permit system, meaning permits are only issued if no local worker is available. Practical barriers include language and certification requirements. Most insulation jobs are filled by Chinese nationals.

**Q: Is insulation work on China's shortage list?**
A: No, it is not on any major provincial or national shortage lists. The occupation is considered domestically supplied, though skilled workers with advanced certifications may be in demand in specific high-end projects.
