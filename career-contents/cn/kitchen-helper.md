# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers in China perform basic food preparation and cleaning tasks in commercial kitchens, such as washing vegetables, peeling potatoes, and washing dishes. They work under chefs in restaurants, hotels, and canteens. The role is often filled by internal migrants from rural areas or entry-level workers. Most positions are in small-to-medium eateries, with high turnover. Automation and central kitchen models are slowly changing the landscape.

**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for kitchen helpers typically leads to line cook, then chef de partie, and eventually head chef, but this requires years of experience and often formal culinary training. Many helpers remain in the role long-term if unskilled. Some transition to kitchen management or food delivery roles. Language barriers and lack of education limit mobility. In large hotel chains, internal promotion programs exist but are selective.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in China?**
A: Salaries for kitchen helpers in China range from 30,000 to 60,000 CNY annually, depending on city and establishment. First-tier cities like Beijing or Shanghai pay on the higher end, while smaller cities pay less. Overtime and meal allowances may be included.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a kitchen helper in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. Kitchen helper is considered unskilled labor (Category C). Employers must prove no suitable Chinese worker is available, which is rarely accepted. Generally, foreigners should not pursue this as a migration path.

**Q: How can a kitchen helper advance in China?**
A: Advancement requires learning culinary skills, often through apprenticeships or vocational courses. Some helpers become line cooks after 1-2 years. Formal culinary training and Mandarin fluency greatly improve prospects.
