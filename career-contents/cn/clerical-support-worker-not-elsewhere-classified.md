# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified encompass a wide range of general office roles such as filing clerks, data entry operators, and administrative assistants. In China's labour market, these positions are abundant across all industries, particularly in manufacturing, services, and government. The role typically requires basic literacy, computer skills, and attention to detail. While these jobs are numerous, they offer limited career progression without additional training or education.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression is limited without further qualifications. Many workers move into more specialized areas such as accounting, human resources, or office management after gaining experience. Some advance to supervisory roles or switch to industries with higher demand for administrative skills. Continuous learning of office software and soft skills is essential for upward mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- Administrative Automation
- Digital Office
- Data Management

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a clerical support worker in China?**
A: Salaries vary by city and industry, but entry-level clerical positions generally pay between 40,000 and 80,000 CNY annually before tax. In major cities like Beijing or Shanghai, the range is higher, around 60,000 to 100,000 CNY.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as clerical support workers in China?**
A: It is very unlikely for foreign nationals to obtain a work visa for routine clerical roles in China, as these are not considered skilled positions. Only if the role requires specific language or cultural skills (e.g., bilingual assistant) might an exception be made, but approval is difficult.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for clerical support workers in China?**
A: With additional training or further education, clerical workers can move into specialized roles such as HR assistant, accounting clerk, or office manager. Some pursue certifications in project management or business administration to move into supervisory positions.
