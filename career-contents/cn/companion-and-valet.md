# Companion and Valet — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 5162 (ISCO08)**

Companions and valets provide personal care, household management, and social support to individuals, often in private households or luxury settings. In China, this occupation is niche and primarily serves high-net-worth families, expatriates, or elderly individuals requiring assistance. The role combines domestic service with companionship, requiring discretion, cultural sensitivity, and basic caregiving skills. China's labour market for this role is small and informal, with limited formal training pathways and no significant migration channels for foreign workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for companions and valets in China is limited but can lead to roles such as household manager or senior personal assistant. Advancement typically requires building a strong reputation and a network of referrals. Training in hospitality, elderly care, or household management can enhance prospects. However, the role often remains entry-level with little formal career ladder. Some individuals may transition to related fields like hospitality or concierge services in luxury hotels.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use AI calendars and reminder systems to automatically schedule daily routines and appointments
- Query weather, flights, restaurants and other information via natural language processing.
- Use AI meeting notes tools to record client conversations and generate summaries.
- Use smart home systems to automatically control indoor environments (e.g., lighting, temperature)
- Providing basic investment advice through AI financial advisors

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI can analyze customer preference data to generate personalized service plans.
- Real-time translation tools for steward to communicate with multilingual clients and service staff
- AI security monitoring systems enhance customer privacy protection and security efficiency.
- Virtual concierge database enables fast retrieval of high-end resources (e.g., private doctors, celebrity chefs)
- AI emotion analysis assists butlers in perceiving client emotional states, improving service response.

**Human moat:**

- Ultra-high-net-worth clients require a real human butler to provide emotional companionship and trust relationships.
- Complex emergencies (e.g., medical first aid, diplomatic etiquette) require human on-the-spot judgment
- Privacy-sensitive tasks (e.g., handling confidential client documents, conversation confidentiality)
- Nuances of cultural etiquette (e.g., customs of different noble families) rely on human experience
- High-end social networking (e.g., introducing key contacts) cannot be replaced by AI

**Skills to build:**

- Psychology and Emotional Intelligence for High-End Clients
- Operation of AI lifestyle management tools (e.g., smart home, personal assistants)
- Digital privacy and cybersecurity fundamentals
- Multilingual Communication and Cross-Cultural Etiquette
- Emergency management and decision-making skills
- Data Analysis Fundamentals (Customer Preference Modeling)

**Key growth areas:**

- Elderly Care

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on employer, location, and responsibilities. For foreign workers, compensation often includes accommodation and meals, but figures are not standardized. Most earnings are negotiated privately.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a companion or valet in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. The occupation is considered low-skilled and typically does not meet the requirements for a Foreigner's Work Permit (Tier C or higher). Exceptions are rare and usually require a highly specialized skill set or sponsorship from a very wealthy employer.
