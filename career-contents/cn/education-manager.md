# Education Manager — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1345 (ISCO08)**

Education managers in China oversee the administration, curriculum, and staff of educational institutions such as schools, universities, and training centers. They are responsible for strategic planning, regulatory compliance, and quality assurance. The role is highly localized, requiring deep knowledge of China's education system, policies, and cultural norms. Foreigners are rarely hired for such positions due to the need for Chinese language proficiency and familiarity with local regulations.

Chinese citizens often transfer between public and private sectors. Foreign professionals may progress within international schools but face a ceiling unless they acquire local qualifications. Bilingual managers with cross-cultural experience are highly valued.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an education manager in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city, institution type, and experience. Public school principals earn approx. 300,000–600,000 CNY; international school directors can earn 1,000,000–2,000,000 CNY. No official average is published.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an education manager in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The employer must sponsor a Z work visa and the role must qualify for Category A or B under the Foreigner's Work Permit system. The applicant typically needs a master's degree, 5+ years of relevant experience, and Chinese language skills. Many foreign managers work in international schools under bilateral agreements.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a principal in China?**
A: Chinese nationals need a Teacher Qualification Certificate, a School Administrator Certificate, and at least 5 years of teaching experience. For international schools, foreign qualifications (e.g., PGCE, QTS) are accepted, but local certifications may still be required. A master's degree in education or management is highly recommended.
