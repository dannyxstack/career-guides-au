# Senior Official Of Special-interest Organizations — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1114 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers leaders of special-interest organizations such as trade unions, professional associations, chambers of commerce, and non-governmental organizations (NGOs) in China. These officials typically operate within the framework of China's social management system, often under the guidance of the Chinese Communist Party and government. They advocate for members' interests, coordinate with government bodies, and manage organizational operations. The role requires strong political awareness, leadership skills, and familiarity with Chinese regulations on social organizations.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a senior official of a special-interest organization in China?**
A: Salaries are generally based on civil service scales for government-affiliated roles, ranging from 200,000 to 500,000 CNY annually for mid-level to senior positions. Independent social organizations may offer higher pay, but this is less common.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a senior official of a special-interest organization in China?**
A: Rarely, as these roles often require political reliability and Chinese citizenship. A foreigner might obtain a Z visa for very specialized advisory positions in international NGOs, but leadership roles are typically restricted to Chinese nationals.

**Q: Is there a career path to government positions from this role?**
A: Yes, senior officials in government-affiliated organizations can be transferred to government positions, especially within the same sector (e.g., from trade union leadership to labor department).
