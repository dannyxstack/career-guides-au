# Midwifery Associate Professional — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery associate professionals in China provide antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care under the supervision of obstetricians or senior midwives, primarily in public hospitals and community health centers. The role is regulated by the National Health Commission, with licensure through the Nurse and Midwife Practice Qualification Certificate. China's two-child policy and later three-child policy have sustained demand for maternal services, though foreign practitioners face significant regulatory barriers due to strict recognition of overseas qualifications and Mandarin proficiency requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows a clinical ladder: from junior midwife to senior midwife, then head nurse or midwifery supervisor. Some pursue advanced practice roles (e.g., nurse-midwife) after additional training and certification. Continuing education units (CEUs) are required for license renewal. Opportunities for teaching and research exist in university-affiliated hospitals. Private high-end maternity hospitals may offer faster promotion but fewer benefits than public facilities.

- Automatically organize appointment schedules and patient reminders

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

- Empathy and decision-making in maternal emotional support and family communication

- On-site coordination across teams (doctors, nurses, anesthetists).

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwifery associate professional in China?**
A: Base salary ranges from CNY 60,000 to 120,000 annually for entry-level, with experienced midwives earning CNY 120,000–200,000. Salaries are higher in private hospitals (up to CNY 250,000). Public hospitals offer housing subsidies and bonuses.

**Q: Can a foreign midwife obtain a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need a job offer from a Chinese hospital, a recognized bachelor's degree, at least 2 years of full-time post-graduation experience, and must pass the Chinese midwifery licensure exam (in Mandarin). Most visas issued are Z (Category B).

**Q: Are foreign midwifery qualifications recognized in China?**
A: Not directly. Overseas qualifications must be evaluated by the Ministry of Education and supplemented with Chinese clinical training. Even then, you must sit for the national Nurse and Midwife licensing exams. This process typically takes 1-2 years.
