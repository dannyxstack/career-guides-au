# Garden and Horticultural Labourer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 9214 (ISCO08)**

Garden and horticultural labourers perform manual tasks in the cultivation and maintenance of gardens, parks, nurseries, and green spaces. In China, this occupation supports the country's rapid urbanization and greening initiatives, with demand driven by city landscaping projects and commercial horticulture. Most workers are domestic migrants from rural areas, with limited formal education. The work is largely seasonal and physically demanding, with wages typically around 50,000–80,000 CNY annually. Foreign workers seldom enter this role due to low skill requirements and wage levels.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garden and horticultural labourer in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salary ranges from 40,000 to 80,000 CNY, varying by region and employer. Urban areas like Beijing or Shanghai may pay slightly higher, around 60,000–90,000 CNY, but living costs are also higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a garden labourer in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. The role is classified as low-skilled, and work permits (Z visa) are typically reserved for higher-skilled occupations (Tier B or A). Employers must prove no suitable Chinese worker is available, which is almost impossible for this job.

**Q: What are the career prospects for a garden labourer in China?**
A: Prospects are limited. With experience, one may become a team leader or specialize in areas like pruning or pesticide application. However, formal advancement usually requires additional training in horticulture or landscaping design.
