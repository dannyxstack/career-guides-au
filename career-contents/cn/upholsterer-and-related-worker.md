# Upholsterer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7534 (ISCO08)**

Upholsterers in China work in furniture manufacturing, automotive interiors, and custom boat/yacht fitting, with demand concentrated in manufacturing hubs like Guangdong, Zhejiang, and Shandong. The occupation requires precision in cutting, sewing, and fitting fabrics, foams, and leathers. While largely domestic, high-end bespoke upholstery for luxury brands or historic restoration may attract foreign expertise, but most positions are filled locally through vocational training or apprenticeships. The role is classified under manual trades with limited migration prospects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Progression typically starts as an apprentice learning basic stitching and frame construction, advancing to senior upholsterer specializing in complex patterns or antique restoration. Some move into workshop management or start their own small businesses. Opportunities are better in cities with large furniture clusters like Shenzhen, Chengdu, and Qingdao. Certification through vocational schools can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software for standardized layout and cutting
- Sewing machine operation for simple straight stitching
- Mechanical cutting and forming of foam and filling materials
- Automated recording of inventory management and material ordering
- Repetitive filling and fastening tasks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software quickly generates and modifies soft package patterns
- AR/VR tools visualize final results, reducing rework
- Machine vision detects fabric defects, improving quality
- Digital cutting beds improve material utilization rates
- Smart supply chain systems optimize material procurement

**Human moat:**

- Customized complex patterns and hand-sewing techniques
- The ability to communicate with clients and understand personalized needs
- Experience handling special fabrics (e.g., antique textiles, leather)
- Flexibility for on-site installation, adjustment, and repair
- Comprehensive assessment of furniture structure and comfort

**Skills to build:**

- Learn CAD/3D modeling software (e.g., Rhino, SketchUp)
- Proficient in operating digital cutting and sewing equipment
- Familiar with AR/VR presentation tools
- Develop project management and client communication skills
- Knowledge of sustainable materials and eco-design trends
- Learn basic programming to customize automated workflows

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom Furniture
- Luxury Automotive Interiors
- Antique Restoration
- Eco-Friendly Materials

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an upholsterer in China?**
A: Entry-level upholsterers earn around 50,000–70,000 CNY annually, while experienced craftsmen in luxury sectors can earn up to 120,000 CNY. Master upholsterers in antique restoration may exceed 150,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as an upholsterer in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. Upholsterers are not on the skilled shortage list, and the Z visa Category C is very restrictive. Employers must prove no local candidate can fill the role, which is rarely accepted.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, one can become a senior upholsterer, workshop supervisor, or start a custom furniture business. Certifications help in moving to higher-end markets.
