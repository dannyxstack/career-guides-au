# Underwater Diver — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7541 (ISCO08)**

Underwater divers in China perform a range of tasks including inspection, repair, construction, and salvage in marine and freshwater environments. They are employed in industries such as offshore oil and gas, marine engineering, port construction, and underwater archaeology. The role requires specialized training and certification due to the physical demands and safety risks. China's growing marine economy and infrastructure projects along its coastline and rivers drive demand for skilled divers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with entry-level commercial diving, advancing to saturation diving, dive supervisor, or underwater welding specialist. Experienced divers may move into inspection roles or become diving instructors. The career path is physically demanding and often requires relocation to coastal areas or offshore sites. Specialization in emerging fields like underwater robotics or deep-sea exploration offers further growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.
- Routine structural measurement: ROV with sensors automatically collects data
- Underwater environment monitoring: AI analyzes parameters such as water temperature and turbidity
- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk
- Augmented reality headset: overlays structural diagrams and repair instructions to improve work accuracy
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes historical data to optimize maintenance schedules.
- Remote specialist collaboration: AI translation and 3D reconstruction help land-based experts guide

**Human moat:**

- Manual operation and quick judgment in emergency situations
- Fine tactile sensation and force feedback (e.g., valve operation)
- Adaptability in harsh environments (current, visibility)
- Legal and safety responsibility attribution (divers must sign on-site)

**Skills to build:**

- ROV/underwater robot operation and basic maintenance
- AI data analysis tools (e.g., Python image processing libraries)
- Underwater communication and AR system usage
- Advanced welding and cutting qualifications (e.g., underwater welder certificate)
- Diving medicine and physiological monitoring knowledge.
- Project management and team coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Offshore Wind
- Marine Engineering
- Salvage
- Underwater Inspection

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence an underwater diver's salary in China?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, certification level, depth of dives, and employer (offshore oil vs. inshore construction). Diving supervisors and saturation divers earn premiums.

**Q: Can a foreign diver obtain a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, through employer sponsorship for a Z visa. Applicants need at least 2 years of relevant experience and a valid commercial diving certificate. High-level divers may qualify for the R visa.

**Q: What are the most in-demand specialties for divers in China?**
A: Underwater welding, offshore wind turbine maintenance, and deep-sea salvage are particularly sought after due to major infrastructure projects.
