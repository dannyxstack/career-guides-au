# Chemical Products Plant and Machine Operator — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8131 (ISCO08)**

Operate and monitor chemical production equipment and machinery in China's extensive chemical manufacturing sector, including petrochemicals, fine chemicals, and specialty materials. These operators ensure safe and efficient processing, control reactions, and maintain quality standards in plants across industrial zones in coastal provinces and inland chemical hubs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a chemical plant operator in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level operators may earn 60,000-120,000 CNY annually, while experienced senior operators can earn 150,000-250,000 CNY, especially in coastal industrial zones. Benefits often include housing and shift allowances.

**Q: Can foreign chemical plant operators obtain a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Employers must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. Foreign operators with specialized skills or experience in high-demand processes (e.g., specialty chemicals) may qualify for a B-tier work permit. General operators (C-tier) face stricter scrutiny.

**Q: Do I need Chinese qualifications to work as a chemical plant operator?**
A: While foreign qualifications may be recognized, you will likely need to obtain a Chinese occupational skill certificate (e.g., Chemical Production Operator) through assessment or training. The Safety Production Certificate is mandatory for many roles.
