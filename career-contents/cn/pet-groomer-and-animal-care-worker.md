# Pet Groomer and Animal Care Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

Pet groomers and animal care workers in China provide grooming, bathing, and basic health care for pets, predominantly dogs and cats. With the rapid expansion of China's pet industry, especially in urban centers, demand for these services has surged. Workers may be employed in pet salons, veterinary clinics, or as self-employed mobile groomers. The role requires patience, animal handling skills, and knowledge of hygiene standards. While typically a domestic occupation, experienced professionals sometimes serve expatriate communities in first-tier cities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant groomer, advancing to senior groomer, salon manager, or entrepreneur. Many groomers eventually open their own pet shops or mobile grooming businesses. Some specialize in creative grooming (dyeing, styling) or join high-end pet hotels. The field is still informal, but formal vocational training and certifications from pet industry associations are gaining recognition.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pet groomer in China?**
A: Entry-level groomers earn around 60,000-80,000 CNY annually, while experienced groomers in top pet salons in first-tier cities can earn 120,000-150,000 CNY. Self-employed groomers may earn more depending on client volume.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a pet groomer in China?**
A: It is difficult but possible under a Z visa with a Foreigner's Work Permit (Category C). The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. High-end pet service companies in Shanghai or Beijing sometimes recruit foreign groomers for expertise in specific breeds or grooming techniques.

**Q: Is formal training required to become a pet groomer in China?**
A: No, but voluntary certification from the China Pet Grooming Association is highly recommended. Many employers prefer certified groomers, and certification can lead to higher pay and better job opportunities.
