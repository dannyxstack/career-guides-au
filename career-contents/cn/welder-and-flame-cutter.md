# Welders and Flame Cutters — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7212 (ISCO08)**

Welders and flame cutters in China are skilled tradespeople who join or cut metal parts using various welding and cutting techniques. They are essential in manufacturing, construction, shipbuilding, and automotive industries. With China's ongoing infrastructure projects and industrial expansion, demand for certified welders remains steady. Workers typically obtain vocational qualifications from technical schools or on-the-job training, and many work for state-owned enterprises or large private manufacturers. The occupation is regulated under China's vocational qualification system, with certifications required for high-quality work.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for welders in China typically starts from apprentice or helper to certified welder, then to senior welder, team leader, or inspector. Experienced welders may become workshop supervisors or quality control specialists. Many obtain vocational qualifications at levels 1-5 (highest best). Those with additional certifications in specialized welding (e.g., TIG, MIG) or underwater welding can command higher salaries and more opportunities. Some move into teaching or technical sales.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Batch standardised metal parts automatic welding (e.g., car body welding, pipe joints)
- Robot programming and path planning based on CAD models replace manual teaching
- Online weld quality inspection (visual inspection + AI defect detection)
- Automatic adjustment of welding parameters and welding process monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted welding parameter optimization (real-time recommendation of current/voltage based on material thickness and ambient temperature)
- Augmented reality (AR) welding guidance system improves novice training efficiency
- Drones/crawler robots equipped with welding modules for high-altitude or hazardous welding
- Digital twin welding simulation reduces material waste from test pieces
- AI-driven multi-robot collaborative welding group control

**Human moat:**

- On-site manual welding of non-standard workpieces and confined spaces (e.g., on ships, inside pipes)
- Welding of pressure vessels and load-bearing structures with high stress and fatigue life requirements
- Welding process development for dissimilar metals and special alloys (titanium, aluminum-magnesium alloys)
- Welding quality responsibility determination and repair decisions

**Skills to build:**

- Programming and debugging of welding robots (e.g., FANUC, KUKA, ABB)
- Welding process simulation software (e.g., Simufact Welding, SYSWELD)
- Automated welding quality control and testing methods
- Digital manufacturing basics (CAD/CAM, BOM management)
- Advanced certifications such as International Welding Engineer (IWE)
- Operation of AR/VR welding simulation training systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Manufacturing
- Infrastructure
- Shipbuilding
- Automotive

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for welders in China?**
A: Pre-tax annual salaries for certified welders in China range from approximately 80,000 to 200,000 CNY, depending on experience, certifications, and location. Senior or specialized welders can earn higher.

**Q: Can a foreign welder get a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. Foreign welders must have at least 2 years of relevant experience and a Chinese vocational qualification certificate (or equivalent). The employer must sponsor a Z work visa under Category C (ordinary). High-level welders with rare skills may qualify for Category B or even R visa if recognized as talent.

**Q: Is there a permanent residence route for welders in China?**
A: No direct route. Foreign welders can apply for permanent residence after holding a work visa for a continuous period (typically 4 years) and meeting other criteria, but it is rare and discretionary.
