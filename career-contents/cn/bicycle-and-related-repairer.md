# Bicycle and Related Repairer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Bicycle and Related Repairers maintain and repair bicycles, tricycles, and similar non-motorized vehicles in China. This occupation is vital in cities with high bike usage, including dockless bike-sharing systems and personal e-bikes. Work includes diagnosing mechanical issues, replacing parts, adjusting brakes and gears, and servicing electric bike batteries. Many repairers are self-employed or work in small shops; some are employed by bike-sharing companies for fleet maintenance. The role requires manual dexterity, mechanical knowledge, and customer service skills. While not a high-skilled profession, it supports China's green transport goals and urban mobility.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for bicycle repairers in China is limited. Many start as apprentices or shop assistants, advancing to senior repairer or shop manager over years. Some specialize in e-bike repair or open their own workshops. There is no formal ladder, and most remain in the trade long-term. The occupation does not typically lead to higher-skilled roles unless combined with business acumen or advanced mechanical training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic software to automatically identify common faults (e.g., circuit issues)
- Handle customer appointments and simple inquiries via chatbot
- Inventory management and parts ordering automated by AI system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of complex electrical faults (e.g., electric bicycle battery management systems)
- AR glasses overlay repair steps and exploded parts diagrams to improve repair accuracy
- Customer needs analysis: AI recommends personalised upgrade options (e.g. performance modifications)
- Remote expert collaboration: obtaining real-time support via AI platforms

**Human moat:**

- Manual fine-tuning (such as wheel truing, suspension adjustment)
- Assess unusual damage and creatively repair
- Face-to-face communication with clients to understand cycling preferences and customize solutions
- Handling replacement and adaptation of old or non-standard parts

**Skills to build:**

- Diagnosis and repair of electric bicycle electrical systems
- Proficient in using digital diagnostic tools and repair apps
- 3D printing part production and repair
- Customer relationship management and personalized service design
- Basic programming skills (e.g., MES system operation in repair stations)
- Sustainable repair concepts (e.g., battery recycling and upgrades)

**Key growth areas:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a bicycle repairer in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salary ranges from 40,000 to 80,000 CNY, depending on city, experience, and employer. Self-employed repairers may earn more but with variable income.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a bicycle repairer in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The occupation falls under Category C (unskilled), requiring employer sponsorship and evidence that no local worker can fill the role. Approval is not guaranteed and is rare for this low-skilled position.

**Q: Is there a career progression path for bicycle repairers in China?**
A: Progression is limited. With experience, one may become a senior repairer, shop manager, or open a workshop. Specializing in e-bike repair or adding business skills can open more opportunities, but formal advancement is rare.
