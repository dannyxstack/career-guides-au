# Water and Firewood Collector — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 9624 (ISCO08)**

Water and firewood collectors gather natural resources such as fresh water and fuel wood for household use, primarily in remote rural or mountainous areas of China where piped water and modern energy are unavailable. This occupation is informal, typically subsistence-based, and extremely rare due to rapid rural development and infrastructure expansion. It is not recognized in formal employment statistics and has effectively disappeared in most regions, with only small pockets in isolated ethnic minority villages. The work is physically demanding and carries health risks from waterborne diseases and wood smoke exposure.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in China?**
A: This occupation is not salaried; it is subsistence labor with no monetary income. Individuals collect resources for their own household use. In rare instances of informal trade, earnings are negligible and unrecorded.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa to work as a water and firewood collector in China?**
A: No. This occupation is not eligible for any Chinese work visa category. The Foreigner's Work Permit system (A/B/C talent classification) requires a formal job offer with an employer and meets specific educational/skill criteria, which this role cannot satisfy.

**Q: Will this occupation still exist in China in 10 years?**
A: Very unlikely. Continued rural development, poverty alleviation relocation, and infrastructure projects (roads, water, electricity) are rapidly eliminating the need for manual collection of water and firewood. It may persist only in the most secluded areas.
