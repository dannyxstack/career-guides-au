# Field Crop and Vegetable Grower — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 6111 (ISCO08)**

Field crop and vegetable growers in China cultivate a wide range of crops including grains, vegetables, and oilseeds, often on small family farms or increasingly in modern agricultural enterprises. With China's emphasis on food security and rural revitalization, these growers play a vital role in domestic supply chains. However, the sector faces an aging workforce and rural-to-urban migration, leading to labor shortages and a push toward mechanization and smart farming. Government subsidies and training programs aim to modernize traditional farming practices.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression traditionally starts as a farm laborer or family farmer, advancing to farm manager or cooperative leader. With modernization, pathways include specialization in crop science, agricultural machinery operation, or agribusiness management. Government programs offer certifications in modern farming techniques. However, upward mobility is limited without access to land or capital; many young workers exit the sector. High-tech agriculture and e-commerce sales of produce offer new career avenues.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using traditional tractors for repetitive field operations such as sowing and tillage
- Apply fertilizers and pesticides manually or on a fixed schedule
- Visually inspecting crop health and pests
- Manually record data such as yield and soil moisture
- Simple sorting and basic processing such as bagging grain

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using satellite or drone imagery to analyze crop growth conditions, optimizing fertilization and irrigation timing
- Develop precise irrigation and pesticide plans using AI prediction models
- Use autonomous farming equipment to improve operational accuracy and efficiency, reducing resource waste
- Use IoT sensors to monitor soil and weather data in real time to aid decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Dealing with complex mechanical breakdowns and on-site emergencies in the field
- In-depth experiential knowledge of local climate, soil, and crop varieties
- Coordinate team operations, train new workers
- Communicate with farm owners and agronomists to develop flexible production plans
- Quickly respond to bad weather or equipment anomalies

**Skills to build:**

- Skills in operating and maintaining autonomous agricultural vehicles.
- Use drones and remote sensing equipment for field monitoring
- Use data analysis and IoT platforms (e.g., AgriWebb)
- Application of precision agriculture software (e.g., Climate FieldView)
- Basic programming or debugging skills for calibrating automation equipment
- Knowledge of sustainable agricultural practices such as crop rotation and organic management

**Key growth areas:**

- Organic Farming
- Greenhouse Cultivation
- Smart Agriculture
- Vegetable Supply Chain

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for field crop and vegetable growers in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region and scale. Smallholder farmers may earn around 30,000-60,000 CNY annually, while managers of larger farms can earn 80,000-150,000 CNY. However, precise data is not provided here.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a field crop and vegetable grower in China?**
A: It is very difficult. Work permits for agricultural laborers are rarely granted, except for high-level technical experts or investors under the R visa. Most foreign agricultural workers are researchers or managers in agri-tech companies.
