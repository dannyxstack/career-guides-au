# Other Cleaning Workers — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 9129 (ISCO08)**

Other cleaning workers in China perform manual cleaning tasks in various settings such as office buildings, hotels, hospitals, and residential complexes. This occupation includes cleaners not elsewhere classified, such as window cleaners, street sweepers, and industrial cleaning staff. The role requires minimal formal education but demands physical stamina and attention to detail. In urban China, demand is steady due to rapid urbanization and a growing service sector, though wages remain low.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for cleaning workers in China is limited, with most remaining in entry-level roles. Some may advance to supervisory positions (e.g., head cleaner) after years of experience, or transition into specialized cleaning (e.g., industrial or hospital cleaning). Formal training or certification in cleaning techniques can enhance prospects, but upward mobility is generally low.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic floor scrubbers or robot vacuums for floor cleaning
- Automatic vacuuming and mopping along preset routes
- Timely automatic spraying of disinfectant for environmental disinfection.
- Use AI trash bins to automatically sort and compact waste
- Monitor cleanliness via sensors and generate cleaning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and shifts.
- Using computer vision to detect stains and guide targeted cleaning
- Use voice assistants or apps to log cleaning tasks and feedback
- Use data analysis to predict peak hours and prepare in advance
- Operate semi-automatic cleaning equipment for high-altitude or corner cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Experience in cleaning complex stains and special material surfaces.
- Communicating with clients to confirm cleaning requirements and flexibly adjusting
- Expertise in non-toxic, environmentally friendly cleaning agents
- Identify safety hazards on site and take emergency measures on the spot
- Manual cleaning in narrow, irregular spaces is irreplaceable.

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cleaner in China?**
A: Salaries for cleaning workers are low, typically ranging from 30,000 to 50,000 CNY annually, depending on location and employer. Wages are higher in tier-1 cities like Beijing or Shanghai.

**Q: Can foreigners work as cleaners in China?**
A: It is very rare. Foreigners usually require a Z visa with a work permit, and cleaning is not considered a skilled occupation. Only specialized roles (e.g., industrial cleaning) might qualify under Category C, with strict employer sponsorship and local labor market tests.

**Q: Is there room for advancement in cleaning jobs?**
A: Limited. Some workers become supervisors or start their own cleaning business, but most remain in entry-level positions. Skills training in specialized cleaning (e.g., hazardous waste) can improve prospects.
