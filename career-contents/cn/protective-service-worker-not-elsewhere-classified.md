# Protective Services Worker Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 5419 (ISCO08)**

Protective services workers not elsewhere classified encompass a range of roles in China's security and safety sectors, including private security guards, campus safety officers, and community patrol personnel. These workers are employed by security companies, property management firms, government agencies, and large enterprises. The occupation is characterized by low entry barriers, with many workers holding junior high or high school education. Demand is stable due to urbanization and public safety concerns, but the role is not considered skilled migration-friendly, with most positions filled locally. Wages are modest, and career advancement is limited without further training or certification.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for protective services workers in China is typically flat, with most remaining in entry-level guard or patrol positions. Advancement paths include becoming a shift supervisor, security team leader, or moving into related fields like fire safety or loss prevention. Obtaining higher-level qualifications, such as the Senior Security Guard certificate, can lead to better positions in high-end residential complexes or corporate security. However, the occupation is not known for upward mobility; many workers exit to other trades after gaining experience.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a protective services worker in China?**
A: Salaries are modest, typically ranging from 30,000 to 60,000 CNY annually for entry-level positions, depending on location and employer. Supervisory roles may reach 80,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a protective services worker in China?**
A: It is extremely rare. Foreign workers would need a Z visa with a Foreigner's Work Permit, but this occupation is classified as Category C (restricted) and is generally not sponsored. Most employers prioritize local hires.

**Q: Is there room for career growth in this field?**
A: Advancement is limited but possible through experience and additional certifications, such as the Senior Security Guard certificate. Some may move into safety management or event security coordination.
