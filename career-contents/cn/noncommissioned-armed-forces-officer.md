# Non-Commissioned Armed Forces Officers — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Non-commissioned officers (NCOs) form the backbone of the People's Liberation Army (PLA), responsible for leading small units, training soldiers, and maintaining discipline. This occupation is exclusively open to Chinese citizens and is not a viable career path for foreign workers in China. NCOs typically rise through the ranks after completing compulsory service or joining from civilian life. The role requires strong leadership, physical fitness, and loyalty to the state.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can a foreigner join the Chinese military as a non-commissioned officer?**
A: No, China's armed forces are exclusively for Chinese citizens. There is no work visa or residence permit for military roles.

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned officer in China?**
A: Salaries for NCOs are not publicly disclosed due to national security, but they are competitive with civil service pay, including allowances for housing, meals, and hazardous duty.

**Q: What is the path to becoming an NCO in China?**
A: Options include enlisting after compulsory military service, direct recruitment from civilian technical schools, or promotion from enlisted ranks after completing NCO training at a military academy.
