# Religious Associate Professional — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3413 (ISCO08)**

Religious associate professionals in China support the practice and administration of religion within state-authorized venues, such as temples, churches, and mosques. They lead worship, provide spiritual guidance, and organize community activities, all under the oversight of the State Administration for Religious Affairs. Foreign workers are rarely permitted, except in specific exchange programs. The role requires deep cultural and religious knowledge, as well as adherence to national regulations on religious affairs.

Movement between different religious groups is rare, and foreign-language skills may open roles in international liaison, but such positions are scarce. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can foreign religious professionals work in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. Foreigners must be invited by a state-approved religious organization and obtain a Z visa under a restricted quota. The process requires multiple government approvals and is rarely granted.

**Q: What is the typical salary for a religious associate professional in China?**
A: Salaries are modest and vary by venue. Public data is not available, but estimates range from 60,000 to 120,000 CNY annually for Chinese nationals, often including housing and meals. Foreigners are exceptionally rare.
