# Pharmaceutical Technicians and Assistants — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3213 (ISCO08)**

Pharmaceutical technicians and assistants in China support pharmacists in hospitals, retail pharmacies, and pharmaceutical companies, handling tasks like dispensing medications, managing inventory, and providing patient counsel. The role is regulated under China's National Medical Products Administration, with most practitioners holding a vocational or associate degree in pharmacy. Demand is driven by an aging population and expanding healthcare access, though automation may limit growth in basic tasks. Urban hospitals and chain pharmacies are key employers. The occupation is classified under the C-tier of the Foreigner's Work Permit system, with limited migration pathways due to domestic supply.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a pharmacy technician certificate or associate degree, advancing to senior technician, pharmacy manager, or clinical pharmacy roles with additional education. Specialization in Chinese medicine or oncology is valued. The government promotes vocational training and skills certification, enhancing job mobility. However, competition is stiff in top-tier cities, and entry-level roles may face wage stagnation.

- Standardized medication dosage calculations (e.g., dose verification algorithms)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for pharmaceutical technicians in China?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from ¥60,000 to ¥100,000 annually in most cities, with experienced technicians earning ¥120,000–¥180,000 in top-tier hospitals or chain pharmacies.

**Q: Can foreign pharmaceutical technicians get a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. The position must be on the employer's approved list, and a local hire preference applies. Most foreign technicians enter under C-category Z visas, requiring a bachelor's degree and two years of experience.

**Q: Do I need a Chinese license to work as a pharmaceutical technician?**
A: Yes, the Pharmacy Technician Qualification Certificate is mandatory for practice. Foreigners may need to pass an exam or have credentials evaluated by MOHRSS.
