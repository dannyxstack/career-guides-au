# Stationary Plant And Machine Operator, Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8189 (ISCO08)**

Stationary plant and machine operators not elsewhere classified operate and monitor various types of machinery and equipment in manufacturing, energy, and industrial settings. This broad category includes operators of crushing, grinding, mixing, and other processing machines, as well as boiler operators and similar roles. In China's vast manufacturing sector, these positions are crucial for production lines, often requiring vocational training and familiarity with automated systems. Workers are employed in factories, power plants, and construction material processing facilities. With China's push for smart manufacturing and Industry 4.0, operators increasingly need skills in digital controls and data monitoring.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from apprentice or junior operator, advancing to senior operator, team leader, then shift supervisor or production manager. Specialization in high-demand equipment like CNC machines or industrial robots can accelerate advancement. Additional vocational certifications (e.g., from MOHRSS skill levels) enhance prospects. In China, on-the-job training and internal promotions are common, though mobility between plants is possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated production quality inspection using sensors and AI vision
- Predictive maintenance scheduling for equipment based on historical data
- Standardized material handling and packaging processes.
- Adaptive adjustment and optimization of production parameters

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted predictive maintenance improves equipment reliability
- Real-time production data dashboards assist operational decisions
- Smart scheduling system optimizes multi-machine coordination efficiency
- Remote expert collaboration system enables rapid fault diagnosis

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of non-standard mechanical faults.
- Ability to flexibly switch between multiple varieties and small batch production
- Perception of subtle anomalies like unusual equipment noises and vibrations.
- Safety management and emergency shutdown procedures

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a stationary plant operator in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and industry. Entry-level operators earn around 40,000-60,000 CNY annually, while experienced ones can reach 80,000-120,000 CNY. Supervisors may earn up to 150,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a machine operator in China?**
A: Yes, but it is rare. The employer must sponsor a Z visa, and the role typically falls under Category C (limited demand). Applicants usually need at least two years of relevant experience and a vocational diploma.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: Operators can advance to senior operator, shift supervisor, or maintenance technician. Additional certifications in CNC or automation open doors to higher-paying roles in advanced manufacturing.
