# Dentist — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2261 (ISCO08)**

In China, dentists specialize in diagnosing, preventing, and treating oral diseases and conditions, including teeth, gums, and related structures. The profession is experiencing growing demand due to rising awareness of oral hygiene and an aging population. Dentists work in hospitals, private clinics, and community health centers, often integrating modern technology with traditional practices. The field is regulated by the National Health Commission, requiring rigorous education, licensing, and continuing education. Foreign dentists face significant barriers due to strict licensing exams and language requirements, limiting international mobility.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for dentists in China typically starts with a bachelor's degree in stomatology (5 years) followed by a residency and licensing exam. Many pursue master's or doctoral degrees for specialization (e.g., orthodontics, oral surgery). Experienced dentists can become chief physicians, department heads, or open private clinics. Academic tracks lead to professorship and research. Public hospital dentists enjoy stable income, while private practice offers higher earnings but requires business skills. Continuing education is mandatory for license renewal.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial screening and anomaly marking of X-rays and CT scans (AI reading)
- Administrative paperwork such as patient appointment, billing, and medical record organization
- Standardized preventive procedures like teeth cleaning and fluoride application (automated dental chair)
- Basic intraoral scanning and digital model generation
- Automated review and filling of insurance claim documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic systems provide treatment suggestions, improving diagnosis accuracy
- Simulate surgical paths via digital twin to optimize implant/orthodontic planning
- Real-time voice recording and medical record generation, freeing doctor's attention
- Personalized prevention plan recommendations based on patient historical data
- Virtual reality training systems enhance complex surgical skills

**Human moat:**

- Complex Surgical Decisions and Manual Operations (e.g., Root Canals, Tooth Extractions)
- Patient communication, empathy, and behaviour management (especially for children/anxious patients)
- Interdisciplinary Comprehensive Diagnosis (e.g., Oral-Systemic Disease Links)
- Emergency management and clinical judgement for sudden conditions
- Professional licensing and legal liability

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dentists in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city, hospital tier, and experience. Entry-level dentists in public hospitals earn around 150,000-300,000 CNY annually, while experienced specialists in private clinics can exceed 500,000-1,000,000 CNY. Cosmetic dentists may earn more. Note: Income is taxable and subject to social insurance contributions.

**Q: Can a foreign dentist get a work visa to practice in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You must secure an employer-sponsored Z visa, have a recognized dental degree, at least 2 years of experience, and pass China's Practicing Physician Qualification exam (written and practical, in Chinese). High-level specialists may qualify for Category A talent and an R visa with expedited process.

**Q: Is Chinese dental license mandatory for foreign dentists?**
A: Yes, it is mandatory. Foreign dentists must pass the National Medical Examination for foreign practitioners (subject to quota) and obtain a Practicing Physician Certificate. Without it, you cannot legally treat patients. Some international hospitals may employ as consultants without direct patient care, but this is limited.
