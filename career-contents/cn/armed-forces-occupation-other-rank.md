# Armed Forces Other Ranks — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

Armed Forces Other Ranks (ISCO 0310) include enlisted personnel in China's People's Liberation Army (PLA) and armed police, performing combat, support, and technical duties. Entry is through conscription or voluntary recruitment, with strict nationality and security clearance requirements. The role is not open to foreign workers. Training involves basic military instruction, followed by specialized skill development in areas such as logistics, engineering, or communications. The occupation is integral to China's national defense, with a focus on modernization and readiness.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Armed Forces Other Ranks in China?**
A: Salaries vary by rank and service length. A new recruit (Private) earns approximately 50,000-70,000 CNY annually, including allowances. Senior non-commissioned officers (Sergeant Major) can earn 150,000-200,000 CNY. Housing, food, and medical benefits are provided.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as an Armed Forces Other Rank in China?**
A: No. Only Chinese citizens are eligible. Military service requires Chinese nationality, and no work visa or permit is issued for this role.
