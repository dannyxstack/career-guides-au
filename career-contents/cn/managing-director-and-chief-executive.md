# Managing Director and Chief Executive — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1120 (ISCO08)**

Top executives responsible for the overall management and strategic direction of companies or organizations in China. Their role involves navigating China's unique business environment, including regulatory complexities, state-owned enterprise reforms, and rapid digital transformation. Foreigners in this role are typically found in multinational corporations and foreign-invested enterprises, while most positions are held by Chinese nationals due to cultural and linguistic barriers.

Many executives rise through subsidiaries of multinational corporations or by joining joint ventures. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Managing Director or Chief Executive in China?**
A: Salaries for this role are among the highest in China, but actual figures depend heavily on industry, company size, and location. Due to data sensitivity and extreme variability, no single figure can be provided. Compensation packages often include bonuses, stock options, and other benefits.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a Managing Director or CEO in China?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Foreigners can hold these positions in foreign-invested enterprises (FIEs) and joint ventures, subject to a work permit (Tier A or B) and approval from the State Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs (SAFEA). The role is considered high-level talent, so Tier A is typical. State-owned enterprises generally require Chinese nationals.

**Q: Is Chinese language proficiency mandatory for these roles?**
A: Legally, no. However, for effective management, especially in local teams, intermediate to advanced Chinese (HSK 4-5) is strongly recommended. For foreign-invested companies with foreign leadership, English may suffice, but Chinese speaking still enhances credibility.
