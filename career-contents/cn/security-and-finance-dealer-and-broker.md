# Securities and Finance Dealer and Broker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3311 (ISCO08)**

Securities and finance dealers and brokers in China execute buy and sell orders for stocks, bonds, foreign exchange, and other financial instruments on behalf of clients or their own firms. They operate within China's heavily regulated financial markets, including the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges, the interbank bond market, and the rapidly expanding derivatives market. Proficiency in Chinese financial regulations, particularly the CSRC rules, and fluency in Mandarin are essential. The role demands deep understanding of China's unique market dynamics, including government policy influence and retail investor behavior. Most professionals work for securities companies, fund management firms, or commercial banks' investment divisions, often in major financial hubs like Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, and Hong Kong.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

Career progression in China typically starts as a trading assistant or analyst, advancing to dealer/broker with a securities qualification. Mid-career paths include senior broker, proprietary trader, or team lead. Top performers can become branch managers or switch to asset management, investment banking, or fintech. Many obtain CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) or FRM (Financial Risk Manager) certifications to boost mobility. The regulatory environment requires continuous learning; licensed professionals must pass annual compliance exams. Networking through industry associations like the Securities Association of China (SAC) is crucial. High earners often move to private equity or hedge funds after 5-10 years.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically execute stock, bond, and commodity orders through algorithms, no manual order placement
- AI automatically generates market analysis reports and investment briefs
- AI-driven chatbots handle common customer inquiries (e.g., account inquiries, transaction confirmations)
- Rule-based system automatically identifies potential suspicious transactions and triggers alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer transaction history and risk preferences to assist in generating personalized investment portfolio recommendations
- Real-time market sentiment monitoring and event impact simulation to improve decision quality for advisors
- Automatically generate compliance checklists and regulatory reports to reduce paperwork time
- Predict upselling and cross-selling opportunities through customer behavior models

**Human moat:**

- Build deep trust relationships, understand clients' non-financial needs (e.g., family wealth inheritance, emotional needs)
- Provide qualitative judgment and real-time adjustments to investment strategies in complex market environments
- Take on personalized responsibility for one-on-one consulting and crisis communication with high-net-worth clients
- Ethical judgment and interpretation concerning regulatory grey areas and new financial products

**Skills to build:**

- Financial data analysis tools (e.g., Python, R, SQL).
- Understanding of AI/machine learning applications in portfolio optimization
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and AI-enhanced customer interaction platforms
- Regulatory technology (RegTech) and compliance automation tools
- Understanding of complex financial derivative pricing models
- Soft skills: negotiation, empathy, crisis communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Fintech
- Wealth Management
- Green Finance
- Cross-border Investment

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a securities dealer in China?**
A: Entry-level dealers earn around 150,000-300,000 CNY annually, while mid-career professionals (5-10 years) can earn 500,000-1,200,000 CNY. Bonuses often equal 50-100% of base salary.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a securities dealer in China?**
A: Yes, but it's challenging. Foreigners need a valid Z visa (employer-sponsored) and must pass the SAC securities qualification exams, which are in Chinese. Most foreign dealers work for foreign-invested securities firms in Shanghai or Hong Kong.

**Q: What are the most in-demand specialization areas for finance dealers in China?**
A: Fintech trading platforms, green bonds, cross-border stock connect, and commodity derivatives are growing rapidly. Compliance and risk management roles are also in demand due to stricter regulations.
