# Valuer and Loss Assessor — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3315 (ISCO08)**

Valuers and loss assessors estimate the value of assets, property, or losses for insurance, taxation, or financial purposes. In China, this profession is integral to the real estate market, insurance industry, and corporate finance. Practitioners often specialize in real estate appraisal, machinery and equipment valuation, or intangible assets. The role requires certification from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS) and adherence to national standards. Demand is steady, driven by urbanization, insurance penetration, and financial regulation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior appraiser in a valuation firm, insurance company, or government agency. After obtaining the Certified Asset Valuer (资产评估师) license, professionals can advance to senior appraiser, team lead, or department manager. Experienced valuers may move into consulting, financial analysis, or set up their own valuation practice. Cross-sector moves into banking, investment, or real estate development are common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary data collation and analysis for automated property valuation reports
- Standard loss amount calculation based on historical data
- Online image assessment for minor vehicle damage
- Automatic Classification and Entry of Insurance Claim Documents
- Input parameters for conventional real estate valuation models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to identify anomalies and patterns in loss images.
- Leverage big data analytics to predict market value trends
- AI-assisted rapid generation of complex assessment report drafts
- Using natural language processing to automatically handle customer inquiries
- Improve valuation accuracy through machine learning models

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a valuer in China?**
A: Salaries vary by location and experience. Entry-level valuers earn around 80,000-150,000 CNY annually; senior professionals with certification can earn 200,000-400,000 CNY. Top roles in large cities or international firms may exceed 600,000 CNY.

**Q: Can foreign valuers easily obtain a work visa in China?**
A: Not easily. The employer must demonstrate that the job cannot be filled by a local. Most valuers fall under category B (professional) or C, requiring a degree and at least 2 years of relevant experience. A recognized international certification (e.g., RICS) may help.
