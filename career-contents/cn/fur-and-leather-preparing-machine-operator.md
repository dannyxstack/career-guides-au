# Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8155 (ISCO08)**

Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operators in China operate and monitor machinery used in the processing of animal hides and furs, including fleshing, splitting, and tanning machines. This role is concentrated in manufacturing hubs such as Guangdong, Zhejiang, and Hebei provinces, where China's leather and fur processing industry is concentrated. Operators typically work in factories with moderate automation, requiring technical skill and attention to safety. The occupation is part of China's broader light manufacturing sector, which employs millions but is facing environmental pressures and gradual automation.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator in China?**
A: Entry-level operators earn around 40,000-60,000 CNY annually, while experienced operators can earn up to 80,000 CNY. Salaries vary by region and factory scale.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. This occupation is classified under Work Permit Category C, which is restricted to labor market needs. Employers must prove no local candidates are available, making it rare for foreign workers unless they have specialized experience.

**Q: What are the prospects for advancement in this field?**
A: Progression to senior operator or supervisor is possible after several years. However, further training in automation or environmental management can unlock better opportunities in larger, modern facilities.
