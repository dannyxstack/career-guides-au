# Optometrists And Ophthalmic Opticians — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2267 (ISCO08)**

Optometrists and ophthalmic opticians in China examine eyes, prescribe corrective lenses, and detect abnormalities. With rising myopia rates and an aging population, demand is growing, especially in urban areas. Many work in hospitals or private clinics, and the profession is regulated under the National Health Commission. International qualifications may not be directly recognized, requiring additional exams or training.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an optician, moving to senior optometrist or clinic manager. Opportunities exist in hospital ophthalmology departments, specialty clinics, or optical chains. Advanced roles include contact lens specialist, low-vision rehabilitation, or academic positions. Foreign professionals may advance faster in international hospital chains.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use auto-refractor for objective refraction measurement
- AI analyzes corneal topography data to generate preliminary lens fitting plans.
- Inventory lens parameter matching and automated order processing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of eye abnormalities (e.g., diabetic retinopathy screening)
- Optimize patient examination and glasses dispensing workflows through digital processes
- Virtual try-on technology improves contact lens fitting efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Requires clinical license, responsible for diagnosis and prescription
- Clinical judgment when managing patients with complex strabismus, amblyopia, etc.
- Skills such as manually adjusting frames and edging lenses
- Building long-term patient trust and personalized service

**Skills to build:**

- Learning to operate digital optometric equipment and AI-assisted diagnostic systems
- Master the use of remote eye testing platforms
- Improve communication and customer management skills
- Learn basic data analysis to interpret AI reports
- Track new eye health technologies (e.g., OCT interpretation).
- Strengthen personalized eyewear fitting and problem-solving skills.

**Key growth areas:**

- Myopia Control
- Pediatric Optometry
- Tele-Optometry
- Refractive Surgery

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an optometrist in China?**
A: Salaries vary greatly by city, experience, and employer. Entry-level optometrists may earn around 150,000–250,000 CNY annually in tier-1 cities, while experienced specialists can earn 400,000–600,000 CNY or more. Foreign professionals in international hospitals often receive higher pay.

**Q: Can I work as an optometrist in China with a foreign qualification?**
A: Yes, but you need an employer sponsor for a Z visa. Your qualifications may not be directly recognized; you may need to pass additional exams or complete a period of supervised practice. The process can be lengthy, so early preparation is advised.

**Q: Do I need a Chinese optometry license to practice?**
A: Yes, you must obtain an Optometrist Practice License from the National Health Commission. Foreign qualifications may be assessed on a case-by-case basis, and some applicants may be required to take the Chinese medical licensing exam (if holding a medical degree).
