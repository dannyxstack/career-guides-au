# Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7231 (ISCO08)**

Motor vehicle mechanics and repairers in China diagnose, maintain, and repair cars, trucks, and other vehicles. With over 300 million vehicles on the road and growing EV adoption, demand for skilled mechanics is steady. Most work in garages, dealerships, or specialized repair chains. The profession is regulated through vocational certification, and experienced technicians can specialize in electric or hybrid vehicles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or junior mechanic, advancing to senior technician, shop foreman, or service manager. Specialization in EV diagnostics, transmission systems, or vehicle electronics offers higher pay. Many pursue vocational degrees (zhongzhuan/dazhuan) and national skill certificates. Experienced mechanics may open their own repair shops or work for international dealerships.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults
- Fine disassembly and calibration requiring tactile feedback
- Comprehensive vehicle condition assessment and repair plan decision-making
- Customer trust communication and personalized service.

**Skills to build:**

- Mastery of AI diagnostic tools (e.g., Dealer-Fix, TEXA IDC5)
- Learning hybrid/electric vehicle high-voltage system repair
- Familiar with in-vehicle network diagnostics and software update processes
- Practice using AR-assisted maintenance equipment
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn the basics of data analysis and predictive maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric Vehicle Maintenance
- Diagnostic Technology
- Aftermarket Services
- Connected Vehicle Systems

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a motor vehicle mechanic in China?**
A: Salaries vary by city and experience. Entry-level mechanics earn around 40,000-60,000 CNY annually, while experienced specialists in luxury or EV repair can earn 80,000-120,000 CNY. Shop managers may exceed 150,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreign motor vehicle mechanic get a work visa for China?**
A: It is challenging. Most positions are filled locally. Foreign mechanics may qualify for a Category C work permit if they hold recognized vocational certificates and have several years of experience, but employers must prove no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: Is it possible to become an EV specialist from traditional mechanic training?**
A: Yes, many vocational schools and training centers offer EV certification programs. Mechanics with strong electrical knowledge can transition, but high-voltage safety training is mandatory.
