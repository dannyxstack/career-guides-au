# Roofer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7121 (ISCO08)**

Roofers in China install, repair, and replace roofs on residential and commercial buildings, working with materials such as tiles, metal, asphalt, and waterproof membranes. The occupation is integral to the construction sector, particularly in urban renewal and new infrastructure projects. While predominantly domestic, demand fluctuates with real estate cycles and government construction policies. Roofers typically work in teams under contractors, with limited foreign participation due to language barriers and certification requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for roofers often starts as apprentice or helper, advancing to skilled roofer, then foreman or site supervisor. Some specialize in green roofs, waterproofing, or heritage restoration. Self-employment or subcontracting is common. Vocational certification through MOHRSS (e.g., 职业技能等级证书) supports mobility. Limited formal education pathways exist; most skills are learned on the job. Foreign roofers may encounter additional barriers in certification recognition.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic calculation and estimation of roof area and material quantities.
- Automated report generation for safety inspection records and compliance checks
- Robot assistance for standard tile laying (e.g., drone pre-laying)
- AI chatbot handling of customer inquiries and quotes

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI image recognition detects roof damage and assists in diagnosing leak points.
- Drones + AI for 3D roof scanning to generate precise construction drawings
- AR glasses provide step-by-step construction guidance, improving installation efficiency for complex structures
- Smart project management software optimizes material procurement and scheduling
- AI weather prediction system assists in scheduling high-risk outdoor work time

**Human moat:**

- Custom handmade and on-site adjustments for complex waterproofing systems.
- Experience in repairing old-style roofs and aesthetic judgment
- Physical strength and risk management for working at heights
- Interpersonal trust in communicating customized solutions with clients
- Skilled mixed construction with multiple materials (tiles, metal, membrane)

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using drones and 3D scanners for roof surveying
- Master basic BIM (Building Information Modeling) operations and reading
- Learn operation and maintenance of AR-assisted construction equipment
- Basic data analysis and smart device troubleshooting
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Learn the construction techniques of new waterproof materials (e.g., PVC, TPO).

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban Renewal
- Green Roofs
- Solar Panel Integration
- Waterproofing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for roofers in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience; entry-level roofers earn around 60,000-80,000 CNY annually, while experienced roofers can earn 100,000-150,000 CNY. In major cities like Shanghai or Beijing, rates may be 20-30% higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a roofer in China?**
A: Yes, but it is very difficult. The Z visa requires a bachelor's degree or two years of relevant experience plus a work permit. Roofing is considered a low-skilled occupation, so work permits are rarely approved for C tier workers. Most foreign roofers work for international contractors on specialized projects.

**Q: Do I need a Chinese certificate to work as a roofer?**
A: Yes, to work legally on construction sites, you must hold the Construction Worker Qualification Certificate issued by MOHURD. Foreign qualifications may not be recognized, so you would need to pass local exams and training.
