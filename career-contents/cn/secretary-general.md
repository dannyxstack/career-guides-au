# Secretary (General) — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 4120 (ISCO08)**

Secretaries (general) in China provide administrative and clerical support across industries, managing correspondence, scheduling, and office coordination. This role is widespread in corporate, government, and institutional settings, with demand concentrated in large cities and multinational companies. While many positions are filled locally, foreign secretaries may find opportunities in foreign-invested enterprises, requiring Chinese language proficiency or a specific skill set.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for secretaries in China often moves from general clerical roles to executive assistant or office manager positions. With experience and additional skills (e.g., bilingualism, project management), secretaries can advance to administrative roles in HR, operations, or corporate services. Formal certification and networking are valued, and many transition to specialised administrative fields.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated meeting schedule coordination and conflict detection
- Call screening and standard response generation
- Document Formatting, Spell Checking, and Template Application
- Preliminary review of travel bookings and expense reimbursements
- Simple email classification and auto-reply

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing executive time allocation through AI schedule analysis
- Improving meeting minutes efficiency using voice transcription and summarization tools
- Generate administrative reports and insights using data analysis tools
- Using AI customer management system to enhance external contact management
- Automating workflow design to improve overall team efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Judgment and confidentiality skills when handling highly sensitive or confidential information
- Interpersonal coordination and decision-making in ambiguous or urgent situations
- Emotional intelligence and relationship management in cross-departmental communication
- Understanding and navigating informal power structures within an organization.
- Administrative tasks requiring creativity or personalized handling

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced office suite automation and scripting (e.g., VBA, Power Automate).
- Hands-on AI tools: meeting scheduling AI, speech-to-text, CRM system
- Data analysis basics (advanced Excel functions, BI tools)
- Project management basics (e.g., Agile methodology)
- Advanced communication and emotional intelligence (conflict resolution, executive support)
- Digital security and privacy protection awareness

**Key growth areas:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a secretary in China?**
A: Pre-tax annual salaries for secretaries in China vary widely by city and industry. In first-tier cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou), entry-level secretaries earn around 60,000-100,000 CNY, while experienced executive assistants can earn 120,000-200,000 CNY. Salaries in smaller cities are 20-40% lower.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a secretary in China?**
A: Yes, but it is uncommon. Employers must sponsor a Z work visa, typically under Category C (general workers). The applicant needs at least a bachelor's degree and two years of relevant work experience, plus a clean background check. Proficiency in Chinese is often required for local firms, while multinationals may accept English-only roles.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for secretaries in China?**
A: With experience and additional qualifications, secretaries can move into executive assistant, office manager, or administrative coordinator roles. Some transition into HR, operations, or project management. International certifications and bilingual skills significantly enhance prospects.
