# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

In China, metal working machine tool setters and operators set up and operate machine tools to cut, shape, or finish metal workpieces. They are essential in manufacturing hubs like Guangdong, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang, supporting industries such as automotive, aerospace, and heavy machinery. The role demands precision and adherence to safety standards, with increasing use of CNC and multi-axis machines.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression often starts from apprentice to skilled operator, then to CNC programmer or production supervisor. Certification upgrades (e.g., from intermediate to senior machinist) and cross-training in multiple machine types enhance prospects. Some move into quality control or production management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC Machining
- Precision Engineering
- Automation
- Smart Manufacturing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this role in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and skill level. Entry-level operators earn around 60,000-80,000 CNY annually, while experienced CNC machinists can earn 100,000-150,000 CNY.

**Q: Can foreign workers easily get a visa for this job?**
A: No. This role falls under Work Permit Category C (restricted), which is only available for certain nationalities or if no local talent is found. The employer must prove a genuine shortage.

**Q: What skills are most valued for progression?**
A: Proficiency in CNC programming (e.g., G-code), ability to operate multi-axis machines, and quality control certification are key for advancement.
