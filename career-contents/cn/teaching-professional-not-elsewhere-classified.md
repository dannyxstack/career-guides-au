# Teaching Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers specialized teachers not classified elsewhere, including foreign language instructors (e.g., English), special education teachers, and subject-specific tutors in China's diverse education sector. Demand is driven by international schools, bilingual programs, and vocational training, with foreign teachers often holding degrees and TEFL/TESOL certifications. Chinese nationals require a teaching license (教师资格证).

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for foreign English teachers in China?**
A: Salaries range from 15,000 to 30,000 CNY per month, depending on city, experience, and school type. Top-tier cities and international schools offer higher pay, while smaller cities may provide housing allowances.

**Q: Can I get a visa to teach in China without a degree?**
A: No, a bachelor's degree is mandatory for the Z visa. Some language schools may accept alternative qualifications, but official requirements are strict. Exceptions are rare and typically require exceptional skills.

**Q: How can I advance my teaching career in China?**
A: Consider gaining advanced certifications (e.g., DELTA, MA in TESOL), moving into academic management, or transitioning to curriculum development. Networking within international school circles and obtaining a Chinese teaching license can open more doors.
