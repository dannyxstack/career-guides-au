# Window Cleaner — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 9123 (ISCO08)**

Window cleaners in China are primarily employed by professional cleaning companies, property management firms, and commercial real estate operators. They clean windows on buildings of all heights, using tools like squeegees, ladders, and safety harnesses. The role is labor-intensive and often seasonal, with high demand in major cities like Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou due to the large number of high-rise buildings. Most workers are low-skilled migrants from rural areas, and formal training is minimal. The occupation is not considered a skilled migration pathway.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited. Workers typically start as apprentices or assistants, gaining on-the-job experience. Advancement to team leader or supervisor is possible after several years, requiring good safety records and management skills. Some experienced cleaners may start their own small cleaning businesses, but success depends on securing contracts with property management companies. Formal certification is not mandatory but can improve job prospects.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city and experience. Entry-level workers earn around 3,000-5,000 CNY per month, while experienced high-rise cleaners can earn 6,000-10,000 CNY. Payment is often hourly or per project.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a window cleaner in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. The job is not classified as skilled, and employers must prove no Chinese worker is available. Practically, foreign window cleaners are not sponsored for work visas.

**Q: Do I need a certificate to clean windows in China?**
A: For ground-level cleaning, no certificate is required. For work above 2 meters, a safety harness operation certificate (高空作业证) is mandatory by law.
