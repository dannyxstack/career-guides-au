# Weaving and Knitting Machine Operator — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8152 (ISCO08)**

Weaving and Knitting Machine Operators set up, operate, and monitor machines that weave or knit textile fabrics. In China, the textile industry remains a major employer, particularly in provinces like Zhejiang, Jiangsu, and Guangdong, though automation is gradually reducing the need for manual operators. Operators typically work in factories with shift schedules, ensuring consistent fabric quality by adjusting machine settings and identifying defects. The role requires physical stamina, attention to detail, and basic mechanical skills. While demand for traditional operators is stable, opportunities are shifting toward more skilled roles in automated textile production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with machine operation, advancing to senior operator or team leader, then to shift supervisor or production manager. Some operators transition to quality control, machine maintenance, or technician roles after acquiring additional training. Larger enterprises may offer internal promotion ladders. However, without upskilling, career growth may be limited to supervisory roles. Operators who learn programming for CNC or automated textile machines can move into higher-paid positions in industrial engineering or technical support.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically detects fabric defects and adjusts tension
- Automatically switch patterns and parameters based on production orders
- Remote diagnosis and automatic repair prompts for machine faults
- Automatic recording of production data and generation of summary reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-Assisted Optimization of Weaving Process Parameters to Improve Yield and Quality
- Using digital twin simulations to predict equipment wear and maintenance cycles
- Predictive maintenance using smart sensors and AI
- Use machine learning to analyze historical data and improve production planning

**Human moat:**

- Manual diagnosis and on-site repair of complex faults.
- Development and trial production of new fabrics or patterns
- Flexible scheduling for small-batch production of multiple varieties
- Communication with designers and clients, and understanding of custom requirements

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of industrial robots
- Basic programming (PLC, Python, C#)
- Data analysis and visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Digital twin and simulation software (AnyLogic, Simul8)
- Debugging smart sensors and IoT devices
- Basic English or Italian to read technical documentation

**Key growth areas:**

- Automation
- Smart Textiles
- Sustainability
- Supply Chain

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a weaving and knitting machine operator in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salary ranges from 50,000 to 80,000 CNY, depending on location, factory scale, and experience. Overtime pay is common.

**Q: Can foreign workers easily get a work visa for this role?**
A: No. This occupation is classified as low-skilled and is generally restricted to Chinese nationals. Foreign workers would need a rare employer sponsorship under Category C, and approval is difficult.

**Q: How can an operator advance to a better-paying role?**
A: By gaining experience and additional training in CNC or automated textile machine operation, or by moving into quality inspection, maintenance, or production supervision.
