# Plasterer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7123 (ISCO08)**

Plasterers in China apply plaster, stucco, and other materials to interior and exterior walls, ceilings, and building surfaces to create a smooth or decorative finish. They work primarily on construction sites, often as part of a team, and may specialize in traditional or modern techniques. The occupation is essential for both new builds and renovation projects across the country.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for plasterers typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, followed by years of on-site experience. With skill development, they can become foremen, supervisors, or start their own sub-contracting businesses. Certification through China's vocational qualification system can enhance opportunities and salary potential. The path is hands-on and rewards craftsmanship.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive wall construction (e.g., straight walls, standard brick walls) is taken over by bricklaying robots or automatic laying equipment.
- Material handling and mortar mixing via automated conveyor systems
- Design and layout of simple masonry structures are automatically generated by BIM software
- On-site measurement and positioning using laser scanners and AI correction, reducing manual work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI visual inspection system assists in checking masonry verticality and mortar joint uniformity, improving quality inspection efficiency
- BIM modeling tools help bricklayers precisely lay bricks in complex curved structures
- Augmented reality (AR) glasses overlay construction drawings, reducing reading errors.
- AI brick layout algorithms optimize material usage and reduce waste

**Human moat:**

- Complex irregular masonry (curved, hollow, historical building restoration) cannot be standardized
- Flexible operation in unstructured environments such as bad weather and confined spaces
- Non-standard requirements from owners or architects for aesthetics and texture, relying on craftsman experience
- On-site safety management and emergency judgment, e.g., support, temporary fixing risks

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated bricklaying equipment and collaborating with robots
- Proficiency in BIM modeling and CAD drawing interpretation.
- Learn drone surveying and laser scanning data analysis
- Proficient in green building and energy-efficient masonry material construction
- Improve project management and multi-trade coordination skills
- Familiarity with building regulations and accessible masonry standards

**Key growth areas:**

- Green Building
- Renovation
- Urban Renewal
- Construction Technology

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals who enjoy hands-on, practical work and have an eye for detail.
- Those willing to work outdoors and on construction sites.
- People who appreciate the satisfaction of creating a finished product from raw materials.

**Who is not suited**
- Desk-job seekers or those uncomfortable with physical labor.
- Individuals who dislike working at heights or in dusty environments.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: How much do plasterers earn in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region and experience, but experienced plasterers in major cities can earn a competitive living, often supplemented by project-based bonuses.

**Q: Can foreign plasterers obtain a work visa for China?**
A: It is very difficult as plasterer is not classified as high-skilled talent. Employers must prove the position cannot be filled by a local worker, and approval is rare.

**Q: What is the career outlook for plasterers in China?**
A: The outlook is stable, with opportunities in urban renewal and green building. Skilled plasterers are always in demand, but young people are less interested, leading to a potential shortage.
