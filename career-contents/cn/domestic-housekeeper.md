# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 5152 (ISCO08)**

Domestic housekeepers, also known as domestic workers, perform cleaning, cooking, and other household tasks in private residences. In China, this occupation is predominantly filled by internal migrant workers from rural areas, with very limited opportunities for foreign nationals. The role requires physical stamina, attention to detail, and often trustworthiness, as workers may have unsupervised access to homes. Demand is driven by urbanization, dual-income families, and an aging population, though wages remain relatively low and formal qualifications are not mandatory.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for domestic housekeepers in China is limited but possible. Experienced workers may become team leaders or supervisors within housekeeping agencies, or specialize in high-end services such as deep cleaning or elderly care. Some pursue formal certifications to improve employability. Others transition to related fields like hospitality or childcare. Overall, the occupation remains largely entry-level with few structured advancement paths, though professionalization efforts are gradually creating more opportunities.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic housekeepers in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city and experience, generally ranging from moderate to low compared to other occupations. In first-tier cities like Beijing or Shanghai, experienced housekeepers can earn competitive wages, but overall it is a lower-paid profession.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as domestic housekeepers in China?**
A: Generally no. Foreign domestic workers are only allowed under exceptional circumstances, such as for diplomatic staff or certain foreign executives with specific permits. The standard work visa pathways do not cover this occupation.

**Q: What is the career progression for domestic housekeepers?**
A: Limited but possible. Many stay in the role long-term, but some advance to supervisory positions in cleaning agencies, specialize in high-end services, or transition to related fields like elderly care or hospitality. Professional certifications can improve prospects.
