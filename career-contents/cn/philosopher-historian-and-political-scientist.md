# Philosopher, Historian or Political Scientist — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2633 (ISCO08)**

In China, philosophers, historians and political scientists primarily work in academic institutions, think tanks, and government research departments. They analyze social, political, and historical phenomena, often contributing to policy development and cultural preservation. The field is highly specialized, with most professionals holding doctoral degrees. Employment is concentrated in universities in Beijing, Shanghai, and other major cities, with limited opportunities in the private sector. Foreign experts are rare and typically hired for specific research projects or visiting professorships.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a philosopher or political scientist in China?**
A: Salaries for foreign professors in China range from 200,000 to 600,000 CNY annually, depending on rank and institution. Think tank researchers may earn 150,000 to 400,000 CNY. Benefits often include housing and research funds.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a foreign philosopher in China?**
A: Yes, but it is employer-sponsored. You need a job offer from a Chinese university or research institute. The employer applies for a Foreigner's Work Permit (typically B tier), then you apply for a Z visa. High-level experts may qualify for an R visa.

**Q: Is there a path to permanent residence for philosophers in China?**
A: Permanent residence is extremely rare and not points-based. It requires extraordinary contributions, long-term residence, or family ties. Most foreign academics remain on renewable work permits.
