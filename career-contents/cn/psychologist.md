# Psychologist — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2634 (ISCO08)**

Psychologists in China provide mental health services, counseling, and assessment in healthcare, education, corporate, and private practice settings, with growing demand due to increased mental health awareness and government initiatives to expand psychological services. The field is rapidly developing but remains regulated, requiring proper qualifications and licensure for practice.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for psychologists in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions may start around 100,000-150,000 CNY annually, while experienced professionals in major cities can earn significantly more.

**Q: How can a foreign psychologist obtain a work visa for China?**
A: You need a job offer from a licensed employer who will sponsor a Z visa (Work Permit). The process involves verifying your qualifications (notarized and authenticated), passing a medical exam, and applying for a Foreigner's Work Permit through the employer. High-level talent may qualify for the R visa.

**Q: Do I need to be licensed in China to practice psychology?**
A: While there is no single national license for all psychologists, most employers require relevant qualifications such as a master's degree and the National Vocational Skill Level Certificate. For clinical psychotherapy, a medical license is necessary. Foreigners should check provincial requirements.
