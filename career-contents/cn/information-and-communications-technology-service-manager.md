# Information and Communications Technology Services Managers — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1330 (ISCO08)**

Information and Communications Technology Services Managers in China plan, direct, and coordinate the provision of IT and communications services within organizations. They oversee teams responsible for network operations, software development, systems integration, and digital transformation initiatives. With the rapid digitization across industries, these managers play a critical role in aligning technology strategies with business goals. China's tech sector, particularly in hubs like Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen, offers robust opportunities for experienced professionals, though competition is intense and requires up-to-date technical and managerial skills.

In China, obtaining recognized certifications like PMP or the national 'Registered Information System Project Manager' can accelerate promotions. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Services Manager in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city (e.g., Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen pay more), experience, and company size. Entry-level managers may earn 300,000–500,000 CNY annually, while senior roles can exceed 1,000,000 CNY. Global tech firms and fintech companies often offer higher compensation.

**Q: What visa do I need to work as an ICT Services Manager in China?**
A: You need an employer-sponsored Z work visa (Foreigner's Work Permit). If you qualify as high-level talent (e.g., salary above 600,000 CNY or specialized skills), you may obtain an R visa for faster processing and longer duration. The work permit is categorized as Tier A (high-level), B (skilled), or C (ordinary).

**Q: How can I advance to a senior management role in China's ICT sector?**
A: Gain experience in leading cross-functional projects, earn recognized certifications like PMP or the national 'Registered Information System Project Manager', and build a network in China's tech hubs. Demonstrating business impact and bilingual (Chinese/English) skills are key advantages.
