# Software And Applications Developer And Analyst Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2519 (ISCO08)**

Software and applications developers and analysts not elsewhere classified design, develop, and maintain software systems, including web, mobile, and enterprise applications. In China, this occupation is in high demand, driven by digital transformation and the growth of tech hubs like Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen. Professionals work across industries such as finance, e-commerce, and manufacturing. The role requires strong programming skills, problem-solving, and often proficiency in Chinese language for local team collaboration. Foreign talents with advanced expertise may qualify for China's tiered foreign work permit system.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a software developer in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city and experience. In first-tier cities, a mid-level developer with 3-5 years of experience can earn between 250,000 to 500,000 CNY annually. Senior roles or those in AI/cloud can exceed 800,000 CNY. Benefits often include housing allowance and bonuses.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a software developer without a job offer?**
A: No, China requires an employer-sponsored work permit. You must have a confirmed job offer from a registered company. The employer applies for the Foreigner's Work Permit (Z visa) on your behalf. High-level talents (Category A) may be eligible for the R visa, but still need a sponsor.

**Q: Are Chinese qualifications necessary to work as a software developer?**
A: No, but having a local certification like the Computer Technology and Software Professional Level Examination can help with career progression and salary negotiation. International certifications like PMP are also recognized and valued.
