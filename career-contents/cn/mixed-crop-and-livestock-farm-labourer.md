# Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 9213 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop and livestock farm labourers perform various agricultural tasks on farms that combine crop cultivation and animal husbandry. In China, this role is common in small-scale family farms and larger agricultural enterprises, particularly in rural areas of provinces like Henan, Shandong, and Heilongjiang. Labourers typically engage in planting, harvesting, feeding, and basic animal care. The work is seasonal and physically demanding, with low formal education requirements. This occupation is part of China's agricultural sector, which employs a declining share of the workforce due to urbanization, but remains critical for food security and rural livelihoods.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for farm labourers in China is limited. Skilled workers may advance to supervisor or farm manager roles, often requiring experience and possibly technical training. Some labourers transition to specialized technical roles in machinery operation or crop/livestock management. Others leave agriculture for urban jobs. Formal training pathways exist through vocational schools offering agricultural programs, but on-the-job learning is more common. The occupation does not typically lead to high-income or high-status careers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using automated tractors for plowing, sowing, and harvesting
- Monitor crop growth and pests/diseases using sensors and AI systems
- Caring for livestock using automatic feeders and milking robots
- Use drones to inspect farms and produce yield maps
- Optimize irrigation and fertilization plans based on historical data and weather forecasts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operating and maintaining smart agricultural equipment
- Using data analysis to improve crop yields and herd health
- Develop comprehensive agricultural strategies to balance ecology and economy
- Collaborate with AI for precision agriculture management
- Train new employees in using digital tools

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of sudden mechanical faults
- Unstructured work in complex terrain or extreme weather conditions.
- Animal emergency medical care and behavioural intervention
- Compliance with organic certification and animal welfare standards.

**Skills to build:**

- Agricultural drone operation and data analysis certification
- Use of precision agriculture software (e.g., FarmOS, Climate FieldView)
- Basic electrical and mechanical maintenance skills
- Digital literacy: Excel/data recording apps
- Basics of crop and livestock health management
- Training for safe operation of AGV/robot equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable Agriculture
- Rural Revitalization
- Organic Farming
- Precision Agriculture

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in China?**
A: Salaries are low, typically between 30,000 and 60,000 CNY per year, varying by region and season. Accommodation and meals may be provided.

**Q: Can foreign workers get a visa for this occupation in China?**
A: Foreign workers rarely obtain visas for farm labourer roles, as these are considered low-skilled (Category C). Employers must demonstrate a specific need, which is difficult for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, labourers can become team leaders or farm supervisors. Further training can lead to roles in agricultural technology or management, but upward mobility is limited.
