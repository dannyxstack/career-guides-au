# Floor Layer and Tile Setter — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7122 (ISCO08)**

Floor layers and tile setters in China install and repair floor coverings, ceramic tiles, stone, and other materials in residential, commercial, and public buildings. The occupation is part of the construction industry, which has been driven by urbanization, real estate development, and infrastructure projects. However, with the slowdown in construction growth and increasing automation, demand for basic manual tiling has stabilized, while skilled work in high-end finishes and renovation remains steady. Most workers are domestic migrants, and foreign workers face strict visa restrictions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to journeyman tile setter, then lead installer or foreman. With experience, one can specialize in mosaic, stone, or waterproofing. Some move into project supervision, start their own small business, or train new workers. Continuous skill upgrading is needed to work with new materials and tools. Foreign workers rarely enter this field due to domestic supply.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic measurement of room dimensions and material quantity calculation, AI can quickly generate optimal layout plans
- Automatic cutting of tiles/stone, CNC waterjet or laser cutting replaces manual cutting
- Automatic detection and adjustment solution generation for ground flatness, reducing manual trial laying
- Inventory management and material ordering processes, AI analyzes project requirements to automatically place orders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to view tiling blueprints and level indicators in real time, improving accuracy
- AI-assisted design of complex patterns and parquet, generating diverse options for clients
- Work collaboratively with automatic mortar mixing and plastering robots to improve efficiency
- AI-driven project progress prediction and scheduling to optimize multi-trade collaboration

**Human moat:**

- Manual cutting and fine placement in irregular areas and corners
- Removal of old flooring, base treatment, and leveling judgment for uneven floors.
- Communicate design preferences with clients and provide personalized advice
- Flexible problem-solving ability for on-site unexpected issues.

**Skills to build:**

- Digital Measurement Tools and BIM Software Operations
- CNC cutting machine programming and maintenance
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools for construction assistance.
- Project management and client communication skills
- Knowledge of sustainable materials and environmentally friendly construction methods
- Basic electrical and mechanical troubleshooting

**Key growth areas:**

- urbanization
- renovation
- infrastructure
- green building

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a floor layer/tile setter in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and skill level. Entry-level workers earn around 60,000-80,000 CNY per year, while experienced tile setters can earn 120,000-180,000 CNY. High-end specialty work may exceed 200,000 CNY.

**Q: Can foreign floor layers get a work visa for China?**
A: It is very difficult. The occupation is not on shortage lists, and domestic labor is abundant. Most foreign workers in construction are in management or engineering. Only highly skilled with unique expertise might qualify for a Z visa, but it's rare.

**Q: Is certification required to work as a tile setter in China?**
A: While not strictly mandatory, many employers prefer or require a vocational skill certificate. For large construction sites, a construction industry qualification from MOHURD is often needed.
