# Bookmaker, Croupier and Related Gaming Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 4212 (ISCO08)**

In China, gambling is illegal on the mainland with limited exceptions in Macau and Hainan, making this occupation extremely niche and restricted. Bookmakers, croupiers, and related gaming workers primarily operate in Macau's casino industry, which is the only region with legalized gambling. The role involves managing betting operations, running table games, and ensuring compliance with strict regulations. Due to the legal constraints, employment opportunities are virtually nonexistent outside Macau, and positions are highly specialized, requiring knowledge of gaming rules and local licensing.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a croupier in Macau?**
A: Exact figures vary, but entry-level croupiers in Macau earn around CNY 150,000-250,000 annually, with experienced dealers earning up to CNY 400,000. Tokes (tips) can significantly increase income.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa for a gaming job in mainland China?**
A: No, because gambling is illegal on the mainland. Even in Macau, foreign workers require a Z visa sponsored by a casino, and the process is competitive with priority given to local residents.

**Q: Is there any career growth in this occupation in China?**
A: Only in Macau, where one can advance to supervisor, pit manager, or casino management roles. Outside Macau, there is no legal career path.
