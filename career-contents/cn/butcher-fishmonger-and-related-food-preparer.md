# Butcher, Fishmonger and Related Food Preparer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7511 (ISCO08)**

Butchers, fishmongers and related food preparers are skilled workers who slaughter, cut, trim, and prepare meat and fish for sale or consumption. In China, these workers are primarily employed in wet markets, supermarkets, and food processing plants. The role requires knowledge of animal anatomy, knife skills, and food safety regulations. While traditionally a domestic occupation, some foreign workers may be employed in high-end butchery or specialty meat shops in urban areas like Shanghai and Beijing.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for butchers in China typically starts as an apprentice in a wet market or processing plant, advancing to senior butcher or supervisor. With experience, one can open a private shop or work in high-end supermarkets. However, advancement is slow and pay increases modestly. Formal certification through the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS) can improve prospects. Foreign butchers may find limited opportunities, mainly in expat-oriented businesses.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Batch cutting and segmentation of standard parts
- Repetitive motions in deboning and skinning processes
- Basic processing like meat grinding and sausage stuffing
- Weighing, labeling, and packaging line operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI vision systems for meat grading and defect detection
- Optimizing cutting plans through data analysis to reduce waste
- Manage meat preservation and turnover using smart inventory systems
- Using augmented reality (AR) to assist training new employees in cutting techniques

**Human moat:**

- Hand-finishing special parts and custom cutting according to client requirements
- Sensory judgment of meat freshness, texture, and abnormalities
- Flexibility in handling unexpected orders and personalized needs.
- Provide cooking advice and demonstrate cutting techniques to customers

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of automated cutting equipment
- Food quality and safety management systems (e.g., HACCP)
- Basic data analysis and inventory management software
- Customer service and communication skills
- Fine hand cutting (for high-end customisation)
- Food safety regulations and traceability knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Premium meat
- Food safety
- E-commerce fresh food
- Specialty butchery

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a butcher in China?**
A: The annual pre-tax salary for butchers in China ranges from CNY 60,000 to 120,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. High-end specialty butchers in Shanghai may earn up to CNY 180,000.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a butcher in China?**
A: It is rare and difficult. Butchers fall under Category C, with strict quotas. Employers must prove no local candidate is available. Only a few expat-oriented businesses sponsor such roles.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for butchers in China?**
A: Advancement includes moving to senior butcher, shop manager, or opening a private butcher shop. However, opportunities are limited and often require Chinese language proficiency.
