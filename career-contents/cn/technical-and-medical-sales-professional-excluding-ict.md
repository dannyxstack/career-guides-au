# Technical and Medical Sales Professional — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2433 (ISCO08)**

Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in China sell complex industrial equipment, pharmaceutical products, medical devices, and other specialized goods to businesses, hospitals, and institutions. They often work for multinational corporations or large domestic firms, requiring deep product knowledge and strong client relationships. The role is prevalent in high-growth sectors such as medical devices, pharmaceuticals, renewable energy, and advanced manufacturing, particularly in major cities like Shanghai, Beijing, and Guangzhou. Foreign nationals may enter this field via employer-sponsored work permits, typically under Category B or C of the Foreigner's Work Permit system.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression often starts as a sales representative, advancing to key account manager, regional sales director, or business development head. In China, success depends on building guanxi (networks) with state-owned enterprises or hospital procurement departments. Many professionals shift toward product management or marketing after gaining technical expertise. Cross-functional moves into medical affairs or engineering sales are common. Salaries and growth opportunities are strongest in foreign-invested enterprises, but local champions like Mindray or Huawei are increasingly competitive.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in China?**
A: Entry-level positions start around 150,000 CNY annually, while experienced professionals earn 300,000–600,000 CNY, with top performers in medical devices or pharmaceuticals reaching over 1,000,000 CNY including commissions. Compensation depends on sector, company, and city tier.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a medical sales rep in China?**
A: Yes, but the employer must sponsor a Z work visa. Most roles qualify for Category B (bachelor's degree + 2 years experience). Medical product sales may require a background in medicine or pharmacy for regulatory compliance.

**Q: What are common career progressions in this field in China?**
A: Many start as sales reps, advance to senior sales or key account management, then move to regional management or product marketing. Cross-functional moves into marketing or business development are popular after 5-10 years.
