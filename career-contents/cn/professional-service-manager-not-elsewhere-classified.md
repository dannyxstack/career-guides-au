# Professional Services Manager — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1349 (ISCO08)**

This occupation involves managing teams and operations in professional service firms such as law, consulting, accounting, or engineering services. In China, demand is concentrated in first-tier cities like Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen, as well as emerging hubs like Hangzhou. These roles require technical expertise, business acumen, and often bilingual ability. The sector benefits from China's growing service economy and corporate demand for specialized advice, but competition is intense from domestic talent.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Professional Services Manager in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city and firm size. In first-tier cities, annual pre-tax salary for mid-level managers ranges from 400,000 to 800,000 CNY, with top international firms offering 1,000,000 CNY or more. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a foreign Professional Services Manager in China?**
A: Yes, via the Z work visa with a Foreigner's Work Permit. You need a job offer from a licensed employer and meet criteria for Category B (professional) or Category A (high-level). The process requires documents like degree verification and work references.

**Q: What are the main challenges for foreigners in this role in China?**
A: Language barrier, navigating local regulations, and intense competition from domestic talent. Success requires strong Chinese language skills (HSK 4+), local market knowledge, and patience with bureaucratic processes.
