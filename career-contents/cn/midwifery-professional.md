# Midwifery Professional — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery professionals in China provide prenatal, childbirth, and postnatal care in hospitals, clinics, and community health centers. They work within a hierarchical, doctor-led system, typically under the National Health Commission's regulatory framework. The role is critical to improving maternal and child health outcomes, especially in rural areas with limited access to obstetricians. Foreign midwives face strict licensing requirements and must pass Chinese medical exams or hold recognized international credentials with experience.

Experienced professionals may move into hospital administration, public health policy, or academic research. Specialization in high-risk pregnancies, neonatal care, or lactation consulting is common. ## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for midwifery professionals in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city, hospital tier, and experience. Expect competitive compensation comparable to nursing roles, with higher pay in private hospitals and tier-1 cities. Benefits often include housing and annual bonuses.

**Q: Can a foreign midwife obtain a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but the process is rigorous. You need a formal job offer from a hospital or clinic, a valid midwifery license from your home country, and must pass the Chinese midwifery licensing exam or have credentials assessed by the NHC. Employer sponsorship is mandatory via the Z visa and Foreigner's Work Permit.

**Q: What education is required to practice midwifery in China?**
A: Minimum a Bachelor's degree in Nursing or Midwifery (4 years) from a recognized institution. Foreign qualifications require credential verification by the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE) and may need supplementary coursework in Chinese medical law and traditional practices.
