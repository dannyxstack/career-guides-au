# Metal Moulder and Coremaker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7211 (ISCO08)**

Metal moulders and coremakers create moulds and cores for metal casting using sand, metal, or other materials. In China, this occupation is concentrated in the manufacturing hubs of Guangdong, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang, supporting the automotive, machinery, and aerospace industries. The role requires precision and adherence to safety standards, with increasing automation in large-scale foundries. However, many small and medium enterprises still rely on manual techniques. The workforce is aging, with younger workers less attracted to the physically demanding environment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to moulder, then senior moulder or foreman. Further paths include foundry technician or manager with additional training. Some move into quality control or process engineering. Certification in CNC or 3D printing can open roles in advanced manufacturing. The lack of young entrants may lead to higher wages for experienced workers, but physical demands limit long-term career length.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate 3D printing paths for sand moulds/cores using CAD models
- AI vision system detects casting defects in real-time and automatically sorts them
- Robots automatically complete sand mold assembly and pouring operations
- AI optimizes pouring parameters and adjusts cooling system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation software assists in predicting casting shrinkage and porosity locations, guiding process improvements.
- AR headset displays sand core assembly sequence and position calibration
- AI-driven quality analysis system generates defect root cause reports
- The digital twin system monitors the entire casting process in real time and warns of anomalies

**Human moat:**

- Process innovation capability for handling non-standard, small-batch castings
- Skills in manual repair and fine-tuning of complex sand moulds/cores
- On-site decision-making ability for multi-variety quick mold changes.
- Team coordination and safety risk management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Process parameter adjustment for 3D printed sand molds/cores
- Casting simulation software (Magma, ProCAST) operation and result interpretation
- Basic maintenance of automated production lines (robots, PLC)
- Metallurgy and Defect Analysis (metallography, X-ray)
- Data-driven process optimization (statistical process control)
- Basic operations and maintenance of digital twin systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Casting
- Automation
- Environmental Compliance
- Green Manufacturing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for metal moulders in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience, but entry-level workers earn around 60,000–80,000 CNY per year, while experienced moulders can earn 100,000–150,000 CNY. Skilled workers in advanced casting may earn more.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a metal moulder in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult as the occupation does not qualify under China's high-skilled talent categories. Work visas are rarely issued for this role unless the foreigner has exceptional skills and an employer who can justify the need. Most positions are filled by local workers.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience and additional training, metal moulders can become senior moulders, foundry supervisors, or quality inspectors. Some transition into process engineering or foundry management. Certification in new technologies like 3D sand printing can enhance prospects.
