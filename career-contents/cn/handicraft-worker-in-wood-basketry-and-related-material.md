# Handicraft Worker in Wood, Basketry and Related Materials — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7317 (ISCO08)**

Handicraft workers in wood, basketry, and related materials create functional or decorative items using traditional techniques such as carving, weaving, and joinery. In China, this occupation is concentrated in cultural heritage preservation and tourism-focused regions, with limited industrial demand. Practitioners often operate as independent artisans or in small workshops, supported by government initiatives to revive intangible cultural heritage. The role requires manual dexterity, creativity, and knowledge of material properties.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice under a master artisan, advancing to independent producer or workshop owner. Some transition into teaching at vocational schools or cultural centers. Certification through the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security's (MOHRSS) skill levels (初级/中级/高级) can enhance credibility. The field values experience over formal education, and networking with cultural institutions is crucial for recognition.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate weaving/woodworking design drawings based on customer size parameters
- Use a CNC engraving machine to cut and engrave complex patterns
- Repetitive standardized processes such as basket weaving and wood strip splicing
- Clerical work such as inventory management, order processing, and customer communication
- Product photography, description writing, and online store listing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI generative design to explore more forms and structural possibilities

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a handicraft worker in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region and skill level. Junior workers may earn around 40,000-60,000 CNY annually, while master artisans can earn over 100,000 CNY through custom work and government subsidies. Most income is project-based.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a handicraft worker in China?**
A: It is very difficult. Foreigners typically need a job offer from a registered entity and must meet work permit requirements, which usually demand a bachelor's degree and relevant experience. Few craft positions satisfy these conditions, and the R visa is rarely granted.

**Q: Is formal education required to become a handicraft worker in China?**
A: No, but vocational training or an apprenticeship is highly recommended. Many artisans learn through family traditions or informal apprenticeships. Certification can improve job prospects but is not mandatory.
