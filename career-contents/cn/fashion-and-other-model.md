# Fashion and Other Models — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 5241 (ISCO08)**

Fashion and other models in China pose for photographs, walk runways, or display products to promote brands, fashion lines, or merchandise. The role is highly competitive and concentrated in major fashion hubs like Shanghai, Beijing, and Shenzhen, with increasing demand from domestic e-commerce platforms and international luxury brands. Models may work freelance or through agencies, with income varying widely based on experience, reputation, and assignment type. The occupation is not typically a migration pathway, as foreign models require employer-sponsored work permits and the market is saturated with local talent.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fashion models in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely: entry-level models may earn CNY 50,000-150,000 annually, while top runway or commercial models can earn over CNY 500,000. Payment is often per assignment or day rate (e.g., CNY 1,000-10,000 per show or shoot).

**Q: Can foreign models get a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but it's employer-sponsored through a Z work visa (usually Category B). You need a contract with a Chinese modeling agency or brand, relevant experience, and a clean background check. The process can take 2-3 months.

**Q: What are the main challenges for foreign models in China?**
A: Intense competition from local models, language barriers, strict visa regulations, and cultural differences in beauty standards. Success often requires adapting to local trends and building strong agency relationships.
