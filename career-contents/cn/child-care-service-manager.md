# Child Care Services Manager — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1341 (ISCO08)**

Child care services managers oversee the operations of nurseries, kindergartens, and other early childhood education facilities in China. They ensure compliance with national standards, manage staff, develop curricula, and maintain parent relations. This role is critical in China's rapidly expanding early childhood education sector, driven by government policies promoting universal access and quality improvement. Managers must navigate regulatory requirements from the Ministry of Education and local bureaus, often requiring a blend of pedagogical knowledge and administrative expertise.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a child care services manager in China?**
A: Salaries range from 100,000 to 200,000 CNY annually for mid-level managers in public kindergartens, while private bilingual centers may offer 150,000–300,000 CNY. Senior directors in top-tier cities can exceed 400,000 CNY.

**Q: Can foreign child care managers work in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. Most positions require a bachelor's degree, 2+ years of experience, and Chinese proficiency. Work permits are typically Category C (limited availability) or B for high-skilled roles. Employers must demonstrate that the role cannot be filled locally.

**Q: Do I need a Chinese teaching license to be a manager?**
A: Not always, but many provinces require the Kindergarten Teacher Qualification Certificate. Some international schools waive this for foreign managers if they oversee operations rather than teach. Check local regulations.
