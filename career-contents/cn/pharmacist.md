# Pharmacist — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2262 (ISCO08)**

Pharmacists in China dispense medications, advise on drug usage, and ensure patient safety, working in hospitals, retail pharmacies, and pharmaceutical companies. The profession is regulated by the National Medical Products Administration and requires licensing as a 执业药师. With China's aging population and expanding healthcare system, demand is rising, especially in community health and clinical settings. Foreign pharmacists face language and licensure barriers, limiting direct entry.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as staff pharmacist in hospitals or retail chains, advancing to chief pharmacist, pharmacy director, or clinical specialist. Many pursue postgraduate degrees or board certifications in areas like oncology or nutrition. Pharmaceutical industry roles in R&D, regulatory affairs, or medical affairs are common career shifts. Entrepreneurship in community pharmacy or online platforms is emerging.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine prescription review and drug interaction checks
- Standardized drug dispensing and dose calculation
- Inventory management and automatic replenishment
- Drug information retrieval and simple medication consultation
- Automatic bill reconciliation with insurance systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Improved Vaccine Management and Promotion Efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Coordination and communication within multidisciplinary healthcare teams
- Ethical decision-making and pharmaceutical legal compliance judgment
- Patient education on emerging therapies (e.g., gene therapy)

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a pharmacist in China?**
A: Salaries vary by location and sector. Entry-level hospital pharmacists earn about 80,000-150,000 CNY annually; retail pharmacists 100,000-200,000 CNY; senior clinical pharmacists or industry roles can reach 300,000 CNY or more.

**Q: Can a foreign pharmacist get a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need an employer sponsor, a recognized pharmacy degree, and passage of the Chinese pharmacy licensing exam (执业药师) to practice. Most foreign pharmacists work in multinational pharmaceutical companies rather than direct patient care.

**Q: Is it necessary to be licensed as a 执业药师 to work in China?**
A: Yes, for most direct patient-care roles in hospitals and retail pharmacies. Industry roles in regulatory affairs or drug safety may accept foreign qualifications with less strict requirements.
