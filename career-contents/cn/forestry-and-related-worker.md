# Forestry and Related Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 6210 (ISCO08)**

Forestry and Related Workers in China perform tasks such as planting, maintaining, and harvesting forests, as well as fire prevention and ecological restoration, primarily in state-owned forest farms and conservation projects. The occupation supports China's reforestation and carbon neutrality goals, but faces challenges from an aging workforce and urbanization, with increasing mechanization shifting demand toward skilled roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an entry-level worker, advancing to team leader or inspector with experience and certifications. Opportunities exist in eco-tourism, state forest management, or private forestry enterprises. Further training in modern forest management, GIS, or ecological restoration can lead to higher-skilled roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using GPS and drones for forest mapping and boundary marking
- Early detection of pests and diseases based on AI image recognition
- Automated felling machinery operation and pruning
- Operation of forest fire monitoring and early warning systems
- data recording (e.g., tree growth, harvest volume)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Develop precision harvesting plans using satellite imagery and AI analysis.
- Using VR simulation for safety training and emergency drills
- AI-assisted Forest Carbon Sequestration Measurement and Reporting
- Real-time monitoring of soil moisture and wind speed via IoT sensors
- Mobile apps to track tools and resource locations

**Human moat:**

- Fine control of manually operating tools such as chainsaws in complex terrain.
- Intuition and experience-based judgment of forest ecosystems
- On-the-spot decision-making in emergencies (e.g., bushfires)
- Manual thinning and specific species management within nature reserves
- Social skills for collaborating and communicating with landowners and communities

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and maintenance
- GIS and remote sensing data analysis
- Foundational programming (Python/R) for forestry data analysis
- Use of digital maps and mobile applications
- Operation of mechanized harvesting equipment
- Ecological Restoration and Biodiversity Knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Afforestation
- Ecological Restoration
- Carbon Sink Projects
- Sustainable Forestry

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Forestry and Related Worker in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer, with state-owned forest farms offering stable income and benefits. Entry-level workers may earn around 50,000–80,000 CNY annually, while experienced workers can earn up to 120,000 CNY. Private sector compensation may be lower.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as forestry workers in China?**
A: It is uncommon. Foreign workers would need an employer-sponsored Z visa, typically classified as Tier C, requiring labor market testing. Permits are rarely issued due to local labor availability and language barriers.

**Q: What education is needed to become a forestry worker in China?**
A: A vocational high school diploma in forestry or related field is typical. Short-term training programs also qualify. Higher education (bachelor's) is not required but may lead to supervisory roles.
