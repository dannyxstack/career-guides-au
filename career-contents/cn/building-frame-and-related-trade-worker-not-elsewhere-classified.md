# Building Frame Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7119 (ISCO08)**

Building frame and related trades workers not elsewhere classified include steel fixers, concrete placers, scaffolders, and other construction workers who assemble and erect the structural framework of buildings. In China, these trades are integral to the rapidly urbanizing economy, with high demand in residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects. Workers typically gain skills through vocational training or on-the-job experience and may obtain national occupational skill certificates. Employment is project-based, with mobility across regions.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 2,692,637. China's building frame trades face steady demand driven by ongoing urbanization, large-scale infrastructure projects (such as rail and highways), and a push for green building retrofits. However, the overall construction growth is slowing compared to previous decades, and the workforce is aging. Automation and prefabrication may reduce some manual jobs, but skilled workers remain needed for complex tasks. Opportunities are stronger in tier-2 and tier-3 cities and for those willing to relocate.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from laborer to skilled tradesperson, then to team leader or site supervisor. Certification in specialized areas (e.g., scaffold erecting, steel fixing) can lead to higher pay and more stable employment. Some workers transition into construction management or start their own subcontracting businesses. However, upward mobility is limited without formal education. The industry emphasizes practical experience over credentials.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using laser rangefinders and digital levels for conventional measurement and marking
- Manually calculating material lists and cutting lists based on BIM models.
- Filling in paper work logs and progress reports
- Repetitive template setup and dismantling work (partially done by robots)
- Manually inspect and adjust component alignment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use BIM and AR glasses for precise positioning and installation guidance
- Structural health monitoring via drones and sensors
- Using AI-assisted design to optimize frame structure layout
- Use automated cutting and welding equipment to improve efficiency
- Coordinate workflows for different trades through digital platforms

**Human moat:**

- Flexible response and problem-solving in complex on-site environments
- Manual adjustments and customization for irregular or old structures
- Monitor automated equipment operation and handle anomalies
- Communication and safety supervision responsibilities in multi-trade coordination

**Skills to build:**

- BIM modeling and construction simulation software (such as Revit, Tekla)
- Operation of digital surveying and setting-out tools (e.g., total stations, laser scanners)
- Automated equipment maintenance and programming basics (e.g., robotic welding, CNC)
- Safety regulations and sustainable construction practices
- Data analysis and report generation tools (e.g. Excel, Power BI)
- Reading English technical documentation (for international projects)

**Key growth areas:**

- Urbanization
- Infrastructure
- Green Building
- Renovation

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for building frame workers in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and skill level. In major cities, monthly earnings range from 6,000 to 12,000 CNY for skilled tradespeople, with experienced scaffolders or steel fixers reaching 15,000 CNY. Salaries are project-based and often paid per day.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a building frame tradesperson in China?**
A: It is very unlikely. China's work visa system prioritizes high-skilled professionals, and manual trades are not eligible for the Foreigner's Work Permit unless you have exceptional skills or are hired as a trainer for specialized techniques. Local labor is abundant.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience, workers can become foremen, site managers, or start their own subcontracting businesses. Obtaining higher-level skill certificates and safety supervisor qualifications opens more doors.
