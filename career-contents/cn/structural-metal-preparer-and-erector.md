# Structural Metal Preparer and Erector — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

In China, structural metal preparers and erectors (ISCO 7214) are essential to the country's massive infrastructure boom, assembling steel frameworks for skyscrapers, bridges, high-speed rail stations, and industrial plants. Workers cut, shape, weld, and bolt metal components on-site, often at great heights. The occupation is dominated by domestic labour from vocational schools and rural-urban migrants; foreign workers are exceedingly rare due to ample local supply and lower skill requirements. Work is project-based, with high demand in coastal economic hubs and rapidly developing inland cities.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 847,651. China's ongoing urbanisation and Belt & Road projects sustain strong demand for structural metal erectors, particularly in large-scale public works and commercial real estate. Skilled welders and fitters with certifications in high-strength steel or seismic-resistant structures will remain employable. Overall, the outlook is stable but slowly declining for basic tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a helper or apprentice, advancing to certified welder or erector, then crew leader or supervisor. In China, experienced workers may move into site management, quality inspection, or start their own small contracting teams. Formal pathways include obtaining a 职业资格证书 (vocational qualification) at Levels 3–5. Upskilling in advanced welding techniques (e.g., for high-rise steel structures) improves earning potential. Beyond age 50, many shift to less physical roles like safety inspection or training.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a structural metal erector in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salary for experienced erectors ranges from CNY 60,000 to CNY 120,000, depending on location, skills, and project complexity. Senior welders can earn up to CNY 180,000.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a structural metal erector in China?**
A: Extremely difficult; this occupation is classified as Category C (low-skilled) and rarely meets the scarcity requirement. Most employers prefer local workers. Only possible if the employer can demonstrate a unique skill need (e.g., exotic alloy welding).

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for this job in China?**
A: Workers can become team leaders, site supervisors, or specialist welders. With additional training, they may move into quality control or safety management. Entrepreneurship (starting a small contracting business) is also common among experienced tradespeople.
