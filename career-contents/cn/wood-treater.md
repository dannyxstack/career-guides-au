# Wood Treater — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7521 (ISCO08)**

Wood treaters in China apply chemical preservatives, stains, and fire retardants to wood products to protect them from decay, insects, and fire. They work in timber yards, sawmills, and wood manufacturing plants, often operating treatment cylinders and spray booths. The occupation is part of China's vast wood processing industry, concentrated in provinces like Fujian, Zhejiang, and Jiangsu. Most workers are male and employed in small to medium-sized factories with limited formal training. Automation is gradually reducing demand, but specialty treatments for antique furniture restoration or high-end construction remain niche opportunities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited for wood treaters in China. Entry-level workers can advance to team leader or quality inspector after years of experience, but formal management roles often require additional education. Some transition into related fields like carpentry or furniture manufacturing. Vocational training certificates from MOHRSS can improve earning potential. The most successful workers might start their own small treatment workshops serving local construction or furniture markets. However, the occupation generally offers low upward mobility, and many workers exit for higher-paying manufacturing jobs.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic spraying or immersion treatment of chemical preservatives and flame retardants
- Monitoring and recording temperature, pressure, and time during processing.
- Wood treatment quality inspection and classification
- Standardized procedures for daily equipment cleaning and maintenance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize treatment formulas using AI to improve wood durability
- Use predictive maintenance to reduce equipment downtime
- Using computer vision to automatically identify wood defects and adjust processing parameters
- Digital management of batch and supply chain inventory

**Human moat:**

- Craftsmanship judgment when handling special or custom wood.
- Safety regulation compliance checks and emergency response
- Manual repair of complex equipment faults
- Communicating special handling requirements with clients

**Skills to build:**

- Automated equipment operation and programming
- Basic Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and sensor data analysis
- Knowledge of chemical safety and environmental regulations
- Use of digital recording and quality management systems
- Collaborative robot (cobot) programming and maintenance
- Lean production and process optimization

**Key growth areas:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a wood treater in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level workers earn approximately 40,000–60,000 CNY annually. Experienced workers with certificates may earn 60,000–80,000 CNY. Large factories in coastal provinces may pay higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a wood treater in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. Wood treater is not on the shortage list, and the work is considered low-skilled. Foreign workers typically need a Z visa under Category C, which requires employer sponsorship and government approval. Most applications are rejected unless the foreigner has unique expertise (e.g., antique restoration).

**Q: Is there room for advancement in this field?**
A: Advancement is limited. Most wood treaters remain operators. With additional training, one can become a team leader or quality inspector. A few start their own small workshops. The occupation is declining due to automation, so long-term prospects are not strong.
