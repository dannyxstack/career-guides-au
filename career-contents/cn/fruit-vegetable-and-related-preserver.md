# Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7514 (ISCO08)**

Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preservers in China are skilled workers engaged in the processing and preservation of fruits, vegetables, and related products through methods such canning, drying, pickling, and freezing. This occupation is primarily concentrated in food processing enterprises in agricultural regions like Shandong, Fujian, and Xinjiang, with demand driven by China's massive domestic market and export-oriented food industry. Workers often operate and monitor processing machinery, ensure quality control, and follow food safety regulations. The role is classified under the food processing trade and typically requires vocational training or on-the-job experience.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salaries range from CNY 40,000 to 80,000 for entry-level workers, with experienced supervisors earning up to CNY 120,000. Regional variations apply, with higher wages in coastal industrial areas.

**Q: Can foreigners work as a fruit and vegetable preserver in China?**
A: Yes, but it is rare. Foreign workers must obtain an employer-sponsored Z visa under the Foreigner's Work Permit system, typically Category C (low-skilled). The employer must demonstrate a genuine need and that no local candidate is available, which is challenging for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement is possible in China for this role?**
A: With experience, workers can become team leaders, quality inspectors, or shift supervisors. Further training in food science can open paths to R&D or management. Small business ownership in preserved foods is also possible.
