# Other Language Teacher — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2353 (ISCO08)**

Other Language Teachers in China instruct students in a foreign language (e.g., English, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish) or teach Chinese as a foreign language to non-native speakers. Working primarily in private language schools, international schools, universities, and increasingly through online platforms, these educators are a key part of China's expanding education sector. The demand is particularly strong for native English speakers, but opportunities exist for teachers of other languages in specialized schools and corporate training. Foreign teachers typically hold a bachelor's degree and a recognized teaching certification.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for foreign language teachers in China often starts with entry-level roles in language centers, advancing to senior teacher, academic coordinator, or curriculum developer. Many move into international schools or universities, which offer better pay and stability. Some pursue a Master's in Education to qualify for leadership roles. Others transition into EdTech, corporate training, or start their own language school. However, long-term career advancement within China may require permanent residency or a shift to other education-related fields.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a foreign language teacher in China?**
A: Salaries vary by city, employer, and experience. In major cities like Beijing and Shanghai, monthly salaries range from 15,000 to 30,000 CNY pre-tax. Lower-tier cities offer 10,000 to 20,000 CNY. Housing allowances are often provided.

**Q: What are the main visa requirements for a foreign language teacher in China?**
A: You need a job offer from a licensed school, a bachelor's degree, a TEFL/TESOL certificate (120+ hours), and at least two years of relevant work experience (or a teaching license from your home country). The employer sponsors a Z visa, which is converted to a residence permit upon arrival.

**Q: Can I transition to a university teaching position from a language center?**
A: Yes, with a master's degree and several years of experience, you can move to university roles. Many foreign teachers leverage their experience to qualify for international schools or academic management positions.
