# Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operator — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8151 (ISCO08)**

Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operators operate and monitor machinery that prepares natural or synthetic fibres, spins them into yarn, and winds yarn onto bobbins. In China, this occupation is concentrated in textile manufacturing hubs like Zhejiang, Jiangsu, and Shandong. The role is predominantly held by local workers with vocational training, as the industry faces increasing automation and pressure to reduce labour costs. Foreign workers are rarely employed in this position due to low skill requirements and language barriers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as machine operator, advancing to team leader or quality inspector. With additional training, workers can become maintenance technicians or production supervisors. The rise of smart textiles and automated factories offers opportunities for those who learn programming or mechatronics. However, without upskilling, career advancement is limited due to industry consolidation and mechanisation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual inspection of yarn tension and thickness (replaced by AI vision system)
- Manually adjust machine parameters based on raw material batch (AI auto-tuning)
- Simple fault troubleshooting for equipment anomalies (diagnostic system automatically alerts).
- Production data recording and report generation (IoT and ERP automatic collection)
- Repetitive loading, unloading, and joint operations (performed by robotic arm)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted predictive maintenance to reduce unplanned downtime.
- Optimize spinning process parameters using big data analytics to improve yield rate
- AI scheduling system enables multi-machine collaboration to improve production efficiency
- AR remote assistance for experts in complex repairs
- Intelligent material handling systems reduce manual handling and lower workplace injuries.

**Human moat:**

- Process experience with special fibers (e.g., high-end wool, carbon fiber).
- Ability to diagnose and repair multi-device linkage faults
- Flexibility in small-batch custom production
- Communication and coordination with other processes (e.g., weaving)
- Environmental and safety management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial robot operation and maintenance
- PLC and automation control system basics
- Data analysis and production process optimization
- Remote monitoring and diagnosis of smart devices
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) basics.
- 3D printing and new fiber processing technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- Automation
- Smart Textiles
- Sustainable Production
- Technical Textiles

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operator in China?**
A: The average annual pre-tax salary ranges from 40,000 to 60,000 CNY, depending on region and experience. Coastal areas may offer slightly higher pay due to cost of living.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operator in China?**
A: Foreigners are rarely hired for this role. It falls under Category C (unskilled) of the Foreigner's Work Permit, which has strict quotas and is typically reserved for jobs where domestic labour is insufficient. Practical chances are extremely low.

**Q: Is this occupation at risk of automation?**
A: Yes, automation is rapidly replacing manual fibre preparing and spinning jobs. Workers should pursue upskilling in machine maintenance or programming to stay employable.
