# Secondary Education Teacher — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2330 (ISCO08)**

Secondary Education Teachers in China typically instruct students in grades 7-12 (ages 12-18) in public, private, or international schools. The role involves subject-specific teaching, curriculum development, and student assessment. While most secondary teachers are Chinese nationals, foreign teachers are increasingly employed in international and bilingual schools, especially for English, STEM, and humanities subjects. Work authorization is employer-sponsored via the Foreigner's Work Permit, with teachers often falling under Category B (professional talent) or C (other) depending on experience and qualifications.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Can foreign teachers get a work visa for secondary schools in China?**
A: Yes, but the visa is employer-sponsored under the Foreigner's Work Permit system. Most teachers qualify for Category B, requiring a Bachelor's degree and at least 2 years of relevant teaching experience. International schools often sponsor Z visas, while public schools rarely hire foreigners for regular teaching roles.

**Q: What is the typical salary for secondary teachers in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely: public school teachers earn 150,000–250,000 CNY/year; international school teachers earn 300,000–500,000 CNY/year. Benefits may include housing allowance, flight reimbursement, and health insurance. Foreign teachers in top-tier cities like Beijing or Shanghai tend to earn higher.

**Q: What advancement opportunities are available?**
A: Teachers can move into head of department, curriculum coordinator, or principal roles. International schools value IB or Cambridge training. Some teachers transition to educational consulting or start private tutoring businesses. Government positions require Chinese citizenship.
