**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Some eventually transition to entrepreneurship or senior leadership in multinational firms.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for engineering professionals in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by industry, experience, and location. Experienced engineers in high-demand sectors like automation or renewable energy can earn competitive packages, often including housing allowances. For specific figures, refer to industry salary reports.

**Q: How do foreign engineers obtain a work visa in China?**
A: Employers must sponsor a Foreigner's Work Permit, categorized as Tier A (high-level), B (professional), or C (unskilled). Most engineers fall under Tier B. The process involves employer endorsement, health check, and application at the local entry-exit bureau.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for engineering professionals in China?**
A: Engineers can advance to senior engineering roles, project management, or technical leadership. Professional titles and certifications enhance prospects. Many foreign engineers move into management or consulting after gaining local experience.
