# Cement, Stone and Other Mineral Products Machine Operator — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8114 (ISCO08)**

Cement, stone, and other mineral products machine operators in China operate machinery to crush, grind, mix, and mold raw materials into finished products such as cement, concrete blocks, and tiles. They work primarily in factories and quarries within China's vast construction materials industry, which is driven by urbanization and infrastructure projects. Operators ensure production efficiency, monitor equipment, perform basic maintenance, and adhere to safety protocols. The role is typically found in state-owned enterprises and private manufacturing plants across provinces like Guangdong, Jiangsu, and Shandong.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a machine operator, advancing to senior operator or team leader. With experience and additional training, operators can move into maintenance technician roles or shift supervisors. Some transition to quality control or production planning. Vocational certifications and on-the-job skill upgrades improve promotion chances. The path is linear within the manufacturing hierarchy, with limited crossover to other sectors.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive tasks in cement batching and feeding.
- Simple planning of stone cutting paths
- Automatic visual inspection of finished product appearance.
- Recording and statistical reporting of production data
- Remote monitoring and alarm for equipment operation status.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience, but entry-level operators earn around 40,000-70,000 CNY annually, while experienced operators can earn 70,000-120,000 CNY. Wages are higher in coastal provinces and for those with specialized skills.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a cement machine operator in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult. Foreigners are rarely approved for such roles due to strict labor market tests and a sufficient domestic workforce. Only under exceptional circumstances (e.g., specialized equipment from abroad) might a Category C work permit be considered.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist in China for this role?**
A: Operators can progress to senior operator, shift supervisor, or maintenance technician. Further training may lead to quality control or production management. Certifications from MOHRSS enhance mobility.
