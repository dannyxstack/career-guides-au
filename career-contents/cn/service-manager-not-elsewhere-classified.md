# Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 1439 (ISCO08)**

Services managers not elsewhere classified oversee and coordinate service activities in sectors such as property management, facility management, cleaning, security, and other support services. In China, rapid urbanization and the growth of commercial real estate drive demand for these managers, who ensure efficient operations, compliance with regulations, and customer satisfaction. They typically work for property management companies, large commercial complexes, or facilities management firms. The role requires strong organizational, leadership, and problem-solving skills, often with a focus on cost control and quality service delivery.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What factors affect the salary of a services manager in China?**
A: Salaries vary by city tier, company size, and experience. Senior managers in first-tier cities like Beijing or Shanghai earn competitive packages including performance bonuses and housing allowances. Industry-specific expertise (e.g., smart buildings) can command higher pay.

**Q: How can a foreigner obtain a work visa as a services manager in China?**
A: The employer must sponsor a Foreigner's Work Permit (Z visa). Typically, a bachelor's degree and two years of work experience are required. The role is usually classified as Tier B (professional). High-level candidates may qualify for the R visa.

**Q: What are typical career advancement opportunities for a services manager in China?**
A: Managers can progress to regional manager, director of operations, or vice president of services. Specializing in areas like green building management or digital facilities can accelerate growth.
