# Commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 0110 (ISCO08)**

Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in China hold leadership and command positions within the People's Liberation Army (PLA), including its ground, naval, air, and strategic support branches. They are responsible for unit management, operational planning, training, and ensuring combat readiness. Entry is exclusively through military academies or direct recruitment from civilian universities under strict state control. The role demands high discipline, patriotism, and physical fitness. Due to national security restrictions, this occupation is closed to foreign nationals.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression follows a rigid, merit-based system from platoon leader to senior command or staff roles. Officers typically attend service academies (e.g., National Defense University) for promotion eligibility. Advancement requires performance reviews, political reliability, and time-in-grade. Specialization in emerging fields like space, electronic warfare, or AI can accelerate career growth. Retirement age varies by rank, with many transitioning to civilian government or state-owned enterprise positions post-service.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in China?**
A: Officer salaries are determined by rank, years of service, and duty position, with additional allowances for hazardous roles. Typical annual pre-tax income ranges from approximately CNY 150,000 for junior officers to over CNY 500,000 for senior officers, inclusive of housing and other subsidies.

**Q: Can foreign nationals become Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in China?**
A: No. Chinese law strictly reserves all commissioned officer positions for citizens of the People's Republic of China. Foreigners are not eligible for any military service roles in the PLA.
