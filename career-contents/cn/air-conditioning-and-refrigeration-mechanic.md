# Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7127 (ISCO08)**

Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics install, maintain, and repair HVAC and refrigeration systems in residential, commercial, and industrial settings across China. With rapid urbanization and growing demand for cold-chain logistics, these mechanics are essential for building comfort, food safety, and industrial processes. The role requires technical knowledge of electrical and mechanical systems, adherence to safety codes, and increasingly, familiarity with energy-efficient and smart technologies. In China, most workers gain skills through vocational training and nationally recognized certifications, with opportunities for advancement to technician, supervisor, or self-employed contractor.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an air conditioning mechanic in China?**
A: Salaries vary greatly by city, experience, and company type. Entry-level workers may earn around 50,000-80,000 CNY annually, while experienced mechanics in first-tier cities can exceed 150,000 CNY. Self-employed contractors often earn more depending on workload.

**Q: Can a foreign air conditioning mechanic get a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Foreign mechanics typically qualify for a B or C tier work permit, depending on qualifications and experience. The employer must sponsor the Z visa and prove the position cannot be filled locally. Having national certifications from other countries may help, but China generally requires local licensing.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for this occupation in China?**
A: Mechanics can progress to senior technician, project supervisor, or workshop manager. With further education, they may become HVAC engineers or start their own service companies. Specializing in high-demand areas like clean rooms or industrial refrigeration offers faster advancement.
