# House Builder — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7111 (ISCO08)**

House Builders in China, classified under ISCO-08 7111, are skilled workers who construct, maintain, and repair residential buildings. This occupation is integral to China's rapid urbanization and real estate development, with a strong demand in cities and rural areas. House Builders typically work for construction companies or as independent contractors, handling tasks like foundation laying, brickwork, roofing, and finishing. The Chinese construction industry has seen a shift towards green building and quality standards, requiring workers to adapt to new materials and techniques.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for House Builders typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to journeyman, then team leader or foreman. Experienced workers can become site managers or start their own contracting business. Specialization in areas like tiling, plastering, or sustainable construction can enhance prospects. Vocational training and certification are valuable for advancement, though many skills are still learned on the job. The industry offers stable income but with physical demands and project-based work.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic generation of bill of materials based on CAD drawings
- Basic building structure calculations and optimization (e.g., load-bearing analysis)
- Construction site safety monitoring and anomaly alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to generate multiple design blueprint options for client selection
- Collision detection and construction simulation optimization through BIM+AI
- Using drones and AI for rooftop inspections and damage identification
- Use machine learning to predict material waste and project delays

**Human moat:**

- On-site fine operations such as manual bricklaying, plastering, and carpentry
- Understanding of individual client needs and designing tailored solutions
- Emergency response to unexpected issues on complex construction sites

**Skills to build:**

- BIM software (e.g., Revit) operation.
- Drone operation and image analysis.
- Basic Programming & AI Collaboration Tools (e.g., ChatGPT)
- Knowledge of green building and energy-saving technologies.
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- urbanization
- green-building
- renovation
- infrastructure

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a House Builder in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salaries for House Builders range from 60,000 to 150,000 CNY, depending on experience, location, and skill level. Senior or specialized workers can earn up to 200,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a House Builder in China?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. Foreign workers need a Z work visa sponsored by an employer, typically under Category C (limited skills). The job must be on the local shortage list, and the employer must prove no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for House Builders?**
A: With experience, House Builders can become foremen, site supervisors, or project managers. Some start their own contracting firms. Additional certifications in green building or specialized trades can lead to higher pay.
