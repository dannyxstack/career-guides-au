# Glazier — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers install and replace glass in windows, doors, and other structures, working primarily in the construction and renovation sectors in China. The occupation supports the booming real estate and infrastructure development, especially in commercial high-rises and residential complexes. With increasing emphasis on energy efficiency and modern architectural design, demand for skilled glaziers remains steady in urban centers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level glaziers typically start as assistants, progressing to lead installers with experience. Career advancement includes supervisory roles, or specialization in decorative glass, curtain walls, or smart glass. Some glaziers may start their own contracting businesses. Formal vocational training and certification enhance career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- glass curtain walls
- energy-efficient windows
- smart glass
- urban renewal

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city and experience. Entry-level glaziers may earn around 60,000-90,000 CNY annually, while experienced ones can earn 120,000-180,000 CNY. Factors include project complexity and certifications.

**Q: Can a foreign glazier get a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The employer must sponsor a Z visa with a Foreigner's Work Permit, usually Category C for ordinary workers. The applicant needs at least 2 years of relevant experience and a clean record. Success is not guaranteed.

**Q: What qualifications are recognized for glaziers in China?**
A: The main qualification is the Vocational Skill Level Certificate for glass installation from MOHRSS. It is not mandatory but improves job prospects. Additionally, a Safety Production Certificate is required for onsite work.
