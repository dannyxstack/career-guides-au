# Sheet Metal Worker — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7213 (ISCO08)**

Sheet metal workers in China fabricate, assemble, install, and repair sheet metal products for construction, manufacturing, and infrastructure. They work in factories, construction sites, and workshops, using tools like shears, brakes, and welders. This trade is vital to China's booming manufacturing and construction sectors, particularly in automotive, HVAC, and building façades. Most workers are Chinese nationals; foreign workers are rare due to domestic supply and lower wage expectations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice, then journeyman, and eventually master craftsman or supervisor. Experienced workers may move into quality control, workshop management, or start their own contracting business. Some transition to related fields like welding or metal fabrication. Upskilling in CNC operation or BIM modeling can enhance prospects. Foreign workers may find it hard to advance due to language and certification barriers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to generate CNC cutting paths for automatic metal sheet cutting
- Repetitive bending and punching operations done by robots
- Automated welding systems take over standard joint welding
- AI vision inspection systems replace manual surface defect inspection
- Nesting optimization algorithms automatically calculate the most material-efficient cutting layout.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complete complex installations with remote technical expert guidance using AR headsets
- AI-assisted design tools quickly generate ventilation duct layout plans
- Digital twin simulates installation outcomes to reduce onsite rework
- Real-time feedback on fastening torque and angle accuracy via smart handheld tools
- Predictive maintenance systems based on historical data optimize repair schedules.

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability to handle non-standard, irregular structures during on-site installation
- Expertise in diagnosing and manually repairing complex system faults
- Ability to communicate with clients to clarify needs and provide customized solutions
- Physical skills for flexible operation in confined or hazardous environments
- Intuitive understanding of material properties (e.g., ductility of different metals)

**Skills to build:**

- CNC machine programming (e.g., CNC press brakes, laser cutters)
- 3D CAD/CAM software (e.g., SolidWorks, AutoCAD)
- Industrial robot operation and maintenance basics
- Post-processing Techniques for Additive Manufacturing (Metal 3D Printing)
- Data analysis and digital twin tool application.
- Green building standards (e.g., LEED) and knowledge of energy-efficient materials

**Key growth areas:**

- Manufacturing
- HVAC
- Construction
- Automotive

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sheet metal worker in China?**
A: Annual pre-tax salary ranges from 50,000 to 100,000 CNY for experienced workers, depending on location and skill level. Foreign workers may earn slightly more if sponsored, but competition is strong.

**Q: Can a foreign sheet metal worker get a Chinese work visa?**
A: Possible but difficult. The occupation is not on China's skilled shortage list, so it falls under Category C (limited quota). Employers must prove no local candidate is available. Success depends on provincial policies and employer support.

**Q: Is there career growth for sheet metal workers in China?**
A: Yes, through experience and certification (e.g., MOHRSS levels). Some move into supervision or start their own business. However, foreign workers may face language and regulatory hurdles for entrepreneurship.
