# Chemical Engineering Technician — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3116 (ISCO08)**

Chemical engineering technicians assist chemical engineers in developing, testing, and manufacturing chemical products, such as fuels, plastics, and pharmaceuticals. In China, they are employed in petrochemical, pharmaceutical, and environmental industries, supporting production and quality control. The role requires strong analytical skills and lab proficiency, with opportunities in state-owned enterprises and private firms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression often starts from technician to senior technician, then to supervisor or engineer after additional education. Certifications like the 'Chemical Engineering Technician' vocational qualification enhance prospects. In China, foreign technicians may face language barriers but can advance in multinational firms. Technical expertise in areas like energy efficiency or waste treatment is valued.

- Routine material and product batch quality testing

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for chemical engineering technicians in China?**
A: For foreign nationals, annual pre-tax salaries range from 80,000 to 150,000 CNY, depending on experience and location. Senior roles may exceed 200,000 CNY.

**Q: Can a foreign chemical engineering technician obtain a work visa for China?**
A: Yes, via an employer-sponsored Z visa and Foreigner's Work Permit. Most candidates fall under Tier B (skilled) or Tier C (general). A relevant degree and 2+ years of experience are typically required.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist in China for this role?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior technician, team leader, or engineer. Further education (e.g., bachelor's) and certifications like the Chemical Engineering Technician qualification help. Multinational companies offer clearer progression paths.
