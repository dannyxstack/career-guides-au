# Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3211 (ISCO08)**

Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians operate and maintain diagnostic imaging and radiation therapy equipment in Chinese hospitals and clinics, supporting physicians in detecting and treating diseases. This role is critical in China's rapidly modernizing healthcare system, where demand for advanced imaging services is rising due to an aging population and increasing chronic disease prevalence. Technicians must hold relevant qualifications and often work in public tertiary hospitals or specialized imaging centers.

## 6. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented individuals who thrive in a clinical environment and enjoy operating complex medical equipment.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical career progression for a medical imaging technician in China?**
A: Starting as a junior technician, one can advance to senior technician, then to chief technician or modality specialist. Further progression includes managerial roles in radiology departments or teaching positions at medical universities. Obtaining advanced certifications in specific modalities (e.g., MRI, CT) is key.

**Q: Can foreign medical imaging technicians obtain a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. Foreign technicians typically need a Bachelor's degree, at least 2 years of relevant experience, and a valid job offer from a Chinese hospital or clinic. The employer must obtain a Foreigner's Work Permit (typically Tier B). Proficiency in Chinese is often required for patient-facing roles.

**Q: What salary range can a foreign medical imaging technician expect in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by city and hospital tier. In first-tier cities (Beijing, Shanghai), experienced technicians can expect 150,000-300,000 CNY annually. Public hospitals often offer lower base pay but include housing and other benefits. Private hospitals and imaging centers may pay more.
