# Well Driller And Borer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8113 (ISCO08)**

Well drillers and borers operate and maintain drilling machinery to extract water, oil, gas, or geothermal resources, or for foundation and construction projects. In China, this occupation is primarily employed in the energy, water supply, and infrastructure sectors, with significant demand in remote regions like Xinjiang and Sichuan for oil and gas drilling, as well as in rural water well projects. The workforce is mainly domestic, with limited foreign participation due to strict work permit regulations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or roughneck, advancing to driller, then rig manager or site supervisor. Chinese workers often gain experience through state-owned enterprises (e.g., CNPC, Sinopec) or private drilling contractors. Specialization in horizontal drilling or geothermal techniques can lead to higher positions. Certification from the Ministry of Emergency Management (for safety) is common. Overseas work with Chinese companies is a possible path.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual adjustment of drilling speed and pressure is taken over by AI adaptive control systems
- Experience-based judgment of strata changes is replaced by real-time data analysis
- Record drilling logs and parameters, replaced by automated sensors and IoT.
- Repetitive maintenance checks (e.g., lubrication, bolt tightening) performed by robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes geological data to assist in optimizing drilling paths and depths
- Drone and camera AI identification of well site safety risks
- Machine learning predicts equipment failures, reducing unplanned downtime
- Augmented Reality (AR) remote guidance for complex operations

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency decision-making and troubleshooting ability
- Adaptability assessment for complex terrain and abnormal geology
- Team coordination and safety leadership responsibilities
- Fine manual skills in equipment repair

**Skills to build:**

- Geological data analysis software (e.g., Leapfrog)
- PLC and automation control system operation
- Drone operation and AI image analysis
- Equipment health monitoring and predictive maintenance
- Basic programming (Python/R) for customized reports
- Safety and compliance management (e.g., ISO 45001)

**Key growth areas:**

- Geothermal Energy
- Deep Drilling
- Water Resources
- Infrastructure

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a well driller in China?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and location. Entry-level helpers earn around 60,000-100,000 CNY annually, while experienced drillers on oil rigs can earn 150,000-300,000 CNY. Foreign workers rarely fill these roles.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa as a well driller in China?**
A: It is extremely difficult due to local labor availability. Only highly specialized roles (e.g., deep geothermal drilling experts) might qualify for Tier B or A, but most are Tier C and not sponsored.

**Q: What are the promotion opportunities for well drillers in China?**
A: Promotion from driller to rig manager or supervisor is possible with experience and certifications. China's state-owned enterprises may offer training and career ladders, especially for those with technical school backgrounds.
