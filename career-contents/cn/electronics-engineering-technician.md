# Electronics Engineering Technician — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 3114 (ISCO08)**

Electronics engineering technicians assist engineers in designing, testing, and manufacturing electronic equipment such as semiconductors, consumer electronics, and communication systems. In China, they are vital to the booming electronics industry, particularly in manufacturing hubs like Shenzhen, Shanghai, and Suzhou. They work in R&D labs, production lines, and quality control, often under the guidance of electronics engineers. The role requires hands-on skills, knowledge of circuit design, and familiarity with testing instruments. China's rapid advancement in 5G, IoT, and semiconductor production drives demand for these technicians.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with entry-level technician roles, advancing to senior technician, team lead, or specialist in areas like PCB design, test engineering, or field service. With further education or experience, technicians can move into engineering positions, such as electronics engineer or project manager. Certifications and specialization in emerging technologies (e.g., embedded systems, RF engineering) enhance prospects. Many technicians also transition into sales, technical support, or entrepreneurship within the electronics supply chain.

**Who is suited**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electronics engineering technician in China?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level technicians earn around 60,000-100,000 CNY annually, while experienced technicians in cities like Shenzhen or Shanghai may earn 120,000-200,000 CNY. Foreign technicians with specialized skills may earn higher, especially in semiconductor firms.

**Q: Can a foreign electronics engineering technician get a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but you need an employer sponsor. The role falls under Category B (skilled worker) for the Foreigner's Work Permit. You must have at least 2 years of related work experience and a relevant degree. The employer handles the application. Category A is possible if you have exceptional qualifications.

**Q: What are the main industries hiring electronics engineering technicians in China?**
A: Key industries include consumer electronics (e.g., Huawei, Xiaomi), semiconductor manufacturing (e.g., SMIC), automotive electronics, industrial automation, and telecommunications equipment. The Pearl River Delta and Yangtze River Delta are major regions.
