# Social Work and Counselling Professionals — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 2635 (ISCO08)**

Social Work and Counselling Professionals in China provide psychosocial support, case management, and community intervention within the context of a rapidly aging population and evolving social welfare system; they are employed by government civil affairs bureaus, community service centers, hospitals, schools, and NGOs, and must navigate complex local regulations and cultural norms, with Mandarin fluency and a recognized social work qualification being essential for practice.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves starting as a junior social worker, then passing the national qualification exams to become an intermediate or senior social worker. Experienced professionals may move into supervisory roles, academic research, or policy development. Many choose to specialize in child protection, mental health, or geriatric care. Advancement is often tied to government rank or NGO hierarchy.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate case reports and progress records
- Initial psychological assessment based on standardized questionnaires
- Crisis hotline initial screening and information triage
- Scheduling appointments and case management system operation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze social media data for early warning of community mental health risks
- Using virtual reality therapy to assist in PTSD treatment
- AI-supported multilingual automatic transcription and sentiment analysis improve consultation efficiency.
- Data-driven case management optimizes resource allocation.

**Human moat:**

- Establishes deep trust relationships and empathetic interaction
- Handling Ethical Dilemmas and Complex Legal Cases
- Flexible communication strategies in multicultural contexts

**Skills to build:**

- Digital literacy and use of AI tools (e.g., data analysis, VR therapy)
- Trauma-Informed Care and Crisis Intervention in Practice
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration skills (with healthcare and education sectors)
- Research evaluation and evidence-based practice methods
- Self-care and burnout management
- Multicultural competence and interpretation of Italian local policies

**Key growth areas:**

- Community Services
- Mental Health
- Child Welfare
- Elderly Care

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work professionals in China?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from 60,000 to 80,000 CNY annually, increasing to 100,000-150,000 CNY with experience and advanced qualifications. Government positions offer stability, while NGOs and private practices may vary.

**Q: Can a foreign social worker obtain a work visa in China?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. You need an employer to sponsor a Z visa, and your application will be assessed under Category C, requiring a bachelor's degree and two years of relevant work experience. Mandarin proficiency is often required by employers.

**Q: What are the main career paths for social workers in China?**
A: Common paths include working in government community service centers, hospitals, schools, or NGOs. Advancement involves obtaining higher-level qualifications (intermediate/senior) and moving into supervision, policy, or specialized areas like child protection or elderly care.
