# Electrical Line Installer and Repairer — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 7413 (ISCO08)**

Electrical Line Installers and Repairers in China are skilled tradespeople responsible for installing, maintaining, and repairing electrical power lines and cables. They work on both overhead and underground distribution systems, ensuring reliable electricity supply to residential, commercial, and industrial areas. This occupation is critical for China's expanding grid infrastructure, including smart grid projects and renewable energy integration. Workers must adhere to strict safety standards and often work in challenging outdoor conditions at heights. The role demands technical knowledge of electrical systems, physical stamina, and problem-solving skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to journeyman or senior technician after several years of experience. Experienced workers can move into supervisory roles, safety inspection, or specialized fields like underground cable splicing or live-line maintenance. Some may transition to related engineering or project management positions. Continuing education and obtaining higher-level professional qualification certificates (e.g., junior, intermediate, senior electrician) are key to advancement. The path is structured within China's vocational skill grading system.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Uses drones or AI vision systems for line inspection and preliminary fault location
- Automatic generation of predictive maintenance plans based on historical data
- Remote monitoring system automatic alarms and status reports
- AI-optimised decision-making for load dispatch and grid balancing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted fault diagnosis and repair plan recommendations to improve on-site efficiency
- Smart wearable devices provide real-time safety alerts and construction guidance
- Digital twin simulation helps plan complex line replacement or emergency repair solutions
- Collaborative robots assist with heavy lifting and precise operations

**Human moat:**

- Safety judgment and emergency response in live-line work and high-voltage environments.
- Field operations and decision-making in complex terrain (e.g., mountainous areas, adverse weather)
- Direct communication and coordination with communities, governments, and grid customers
- Team Trust and Leadership in High-Risk Environments

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for an electrical line installer in China?**
A: Entry-level workers earn around 60,000–80,000 CNY; experienced installers with advanced certifications can earn 100,000–150,000 CNY annually. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in major cities and remote areas.

**Q: Can a foreigner obtain a work visa as an electrical line installer in China?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The role qualifies under Class C of the Foreigner's Work Permit system, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of local shortage. Applicants need relevant vocational degrees and at least 2 years of experience. The process is restrictive and not a typical migration route.
