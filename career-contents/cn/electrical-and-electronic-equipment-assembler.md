# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler — career analysis · China

**Occupation code: 8212 (ISCO08)**

Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in China are skilled workers who assemble components for a wide range of products, from consumer electronics to industrial machinery. A large share of the workforce is employed in export-oriented factories in the Pearl River Delta, Yangtze River Delta, and inland manufacturing hubs. Many positions require only basic technical training, with on-the-job learning common. The role is often entry-level but can lead to supervisory or technician roles with experience.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from junior assembler to team leader, then to production supervisor or quality inspector. Some workers transition into maintenance or technician roles after acquiring additional certifications. Upskilling in automation, lean manufacturing, and quality control enhances advancement opportunities. The field offers stable employment but limited upward mobility without further education or training.

- Repetitive electronic component insertion and soldering
- Visual inspection and testing based on preset criteria
- Simple wire harness organization and connection
- Material handling and sorting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Programming and debugging of automated equipment

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for assemblers in China?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level assemblers earn about 50,000–80,000 CNY annually, while experienced workers can earn 80,000–120,000 CNY. Overtime pay is common.

**Q: Can foreign workers easily get a visa for assembly jobs?**
A: Generally, assembly jobs are not considered skilled roles, so obtaining a work visa is difficult. Employers must demonstrate that no suitable local candidate is available, and positions often fall under Tier C with strict quotas.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for assemblers?**
A: With experience and training, assemblers can become team leaders, supervisors, or quality control inspectors. Obtaining vocational certificates or studying automation can lead to technician roles.
