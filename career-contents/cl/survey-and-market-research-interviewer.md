**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for survey and market research interviewers in Chile?**
A: Entry-level interviewers earn around 450,000–600,000 CLP per month, while experienced ones can make 700,000–900,000 CLP. Pay often includes commissions or bonuses per completed survey. Rates vary by company and survey type (phone, face-to-face, or online). Full-time roles are common, but part-time and freelance positions also exist.

**Q: What skills are essential for survey interviewers in Chile?**
A: Strong communication and active listening are crucial, as interviewers must explain questions clearly and probe for accurate responses. Persistence and neutrality help when facing refusals or sensitive topics. Basic digital literacy is needed for computer-assisted interviewing. Bilingualism (Spanish/English) can be an advantage for international studies.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of survey interviewers in Chile?**
A: AI automates data collection for simple online surveys, but human interviewers remain needed for complex or sensitive topics requiring empathy and rapport. AI assists in real-time monitoring of interview quality and response patterns. The role is evolving toward higher-value tasks: validating data, handling open-ended responses, and conducting specialized studies.
