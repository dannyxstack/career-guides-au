**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using laser rangefinders and digital levels for conventional measurement and marking
- Manually calculating material lists and cutting lists based on BIM models.
- Filling in paper work logs and progress reports
- Repetitive template setup and dismantling work (partially done by robots)
- Manually inspect and adjust component alignment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use BIM and AR glasses for precise positioning and installation guidance
- Structural health monitoring via drones and sensors
- Using AI-assisted design to optimize frame structure layout
- Use automated cutting and welding equipment to improve efficiency
- Coordinate workflows for different trades through digital platforms

**Human moat:**

- Flexible response and problem-solving in complex on-site environments
- Manual adjustments and customization for irregular or old structures
- Monitor automated equipment operation and handle anomalies
- Communication and safety supervision responsibilities in multi-trade coordination

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and report generation tools (e.g. Excel, Power BI)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building frame and related trades workers in Chile?**
A: In Chile, the average monthly salary for building frame and related trades workers ranges from CLP 500,000 to CLP 800,000 depending on experience and project complexity. Skilled workers in high-demand regions like Santiago may earn higher wages, with overtime and bonuses potentially increasing total compensation.

**Q: What key skills are needed for building frame and related trades workers in Chile?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in wood, metal, and concrete framing, ability to read blueprints, and knowledge of local building codes. Physical strength, precision, and teamwork are crucial. Familiarity with power tools and safety protocols is also required. Continuous learning of new construction techniques enhances employability.

**Q: Will AI replace building frame and related trades workers in Chile?**
A: AI is unlikely to fully replace these trades soon. While automation aids in design and prefabrication, on-site assembly, customization, and problem-solving remain human tasks. Workers who adapt by learning to use AI-assisted tools may see improved efficiency, but the trade requires manual dexterity and judgment that AI cannot replicate.
