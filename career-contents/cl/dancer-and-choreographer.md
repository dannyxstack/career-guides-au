## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dancers and choreographers in Chile?**
A: Earnings vary widely due to project-based work. Dancers in established companies or TV earn roughly CLP 500,000–1,000,000 monthly. Freelance choreographers may charge CLP 30,000–80,000 per class or rehearsal. Many supplement income with teaching or other arts work. The median annual income is around CLP 6,000,000–12,000,000.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a dancer or choreographer in Chile?**
A: Technical proficiency in multiple dance styles (ballet, contemporary, urban) is essential. Choreographers need strong creative direction and communication skills. Physical fitness and stamina are crucial, as are networking abilities to secure contracts. Knowledge of music and rhythm, plus the ability to adapt to different venues (theatres, TV, events) is highly valued.

**Q: How is AI affecting dance and choreography in Chile?**
A: AI tools are emerging for motion capture and digital choreography planning, but human creativity remains central. Some dancers use AI-generated music or visuals in performances. However, AI is unlikely to replace the artistry and interpretative skills of live performers. In Chile, the impact is still minimal, with technology seen as a complementary aid rather than a threat.
