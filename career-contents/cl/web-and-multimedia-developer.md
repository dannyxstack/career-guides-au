## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for web and multimedia developers in Chile?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Junior developers in Santiago earn around 1.2–1.8 million CLP monthly, while senior roles can reach 3–4.5 million CLP. Outside the capital, pay is 10–20% lower. Benefits often include bonuses and remote work options.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for web developers in Chile?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in JavaScript frameworks (React, Angular, Vue), Python, PHP, and database management. Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, Azure) and agile methodologies is highly valued. Soft skills like communication and problem-solving are also essential for team collaboration.

**Q: How is AI impacting web development jobs in Chile?**
A: AI tools automate routine coding tasks but create demand for developers skilled in integrating AI features, like chatbots or recommendations. Developers who focus on UX, complex logic, and system architecture remain in high demand. Continuous learning is key to staying relevant.
