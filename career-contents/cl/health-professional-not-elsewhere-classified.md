## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Chile?**
A: In Chile, health professionals under this category (e.g., audiologists, dietitians) earn between CLP 800,000 and CLP 1,500,000 monthly, depending on experience and sector. Public sector roles offer additional benefits like bonuses, while private practice can yield higher incomes but less stability. Salaries vary by region, with Santiago typically paying more.

**Q: What are the typical work settings for health professionals in Chile?**
A: These professionals work in hospitals, clinics, rehabilitation centers, schools, and private practices. For example, audiologists often collaborate with otolaryngologists in clinics, while dietitians work in hospitals or nutrition counseling. Public health programs and community health centers also employ them, especially in rural areas where demand is high.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting health professionals not elsewhere classified in Chile?**
A: AI is enhancing diagnostic tools, such as automated hearing tests and dietary planning software. However, it is unlikely to replace these professionals due to the need for human interaction and clinical judgment. In Chile, AI adoption is gradual, with professionals using it to streamline administrative tasks, allowing more time for patient care.
