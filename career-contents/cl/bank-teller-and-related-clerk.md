## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bank teller in Chile?**
A: The average monthly salary for a bank teller in Chile ranges from CLP 600,000 to CLP 1,000,000 (gross), depending on experience, bank size, and location. Entry-level positions start around CLP 500,000, while senior tellers with additional responsibilities can earn up to CLP 1,200,000. Salaries are typically higher in Santiago than in regional areas.

**Q: What key skills do bank tellers need in Chile?**
A: Bank tellers in Chile require strong numerical accuracy, customer service orientation, and proficiency in banking software. Bilingual skills (English) are increasingly valued for handling international clients. Attention to detail, cash handling experience, and ability to identify fraudulent transactions are essential. Banks also seek candidates with basic financial product knowledge to cross-sell services.

**Q: How is automation affecting bank teller roles in Chile?**
A: Automation through ATMs and digital banking has reduced routine cash-handling tasks for tellers in Chile. However, tellers are shifting toward advisory roles, assisting customers with digital tools and complex transactions. Banks still value human interaction for high-net-worth clients and problem resolution. Overall, demand for tellers is gradually declining, but roles are evolving to require stronger tech and sales skills.
