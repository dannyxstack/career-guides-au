**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic floor scrubbers or robot vacuums for floor cleaning
- Automatic vacuuming and mopping along preset routes
- Timely automatic spraying of disinfectant for environmental disinfection.
- Use AI trash bins to automatically sort and compact waste
- Monitor cleanliness via sensors and generate cleaning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and shifts.
- Using computer vision to detect stains and guide targeted cleaning
- Use voice assistants or apps to log cleaning tasks and feedback
- Use data analysis to predict peak hours and prepare in advance
- Operate semi-automatic cleaning equipment for high-altitude or corner cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Experience in cleaning complex stains and special material surfaces.
- Communicating with clients to confirm cleaning requirements and flexibly adjusting
- Expertise in non-toxic, environmentally friendly cleaning agents
- Identify safety hazards on site and take emergency measures on the spot
- Manual cleaning in narrow, irregular spaces is irreplaceable.

**Skills to build:**

- Understanding the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the status of cleaning equipment
- Knowledge of Disinfection and Chemical Safety in Learning Environments
- Improve customer service and communication skills

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for other cleaning workers in Chile?**
A: In Chile, cleaning workers typically earn minimum wage, which in 2025 is around CLP 500,000 per month (approximately USD 550). Salaries can vary by employer and region, with some offering additional benefits like meal vouchers or transportation allowances. Overtime pay may apply for extra hours.

**Q: What skills are required for cleaning workers in Chile?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail, reliability, and time management. Workers must know how to use cleaning equipment and chemicals safely, often following specific protocols for sanitization. Physical stamina is important as the job involves standing, bending, and lifting. Basic communication skills are needed to coordinate with supervisors.

**Q: How is AI affecting cleaning jobs in Chile?**
A: AI and automation are gradually influencing cleaning roles in Chile, primarily through robotic vacuum cleaners and automated floor scrubbers in large facilities. However, many tasks still require human judgment, such as cleaning delicate surfaces or handling unexpected messes. The demand for human cleaners remains stable, but workers may benefit from learning to operate new technologies.
