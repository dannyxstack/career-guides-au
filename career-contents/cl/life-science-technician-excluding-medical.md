**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine sample preparation and processing (e.g., pipetting, centrifugation)
- Standard biochemical tests (e.g., ELISA, PCR result interpretation)
- Record and organize experimental data
- Reagent inventory management and ordering
- Repetitive animal husbandry and observation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- High-throughput sequencing data analysis (AI-assisted variant identification)
- Experimental protocol optimization (machine learning predicts optimal conditions)
- Literature mining and hypothesis generation (Natural Language Processing)
- Automatic analysis of microscopic images (cell counting, phenotype recognition)
- Multi-omics data integration (transcriptome and proteome correlation analysis)

**Human moat:**

- Unstructured experimental troubleshooting (e.g., investigating unexpected contamination)
- Ethical Decision-Making and Animal Welfare Assessment
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and communication (with clinicians, engineers)
- Experimental innovation design and hypothesis generation.
- Regulatory compliance and quality control responsibilities

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a life science technician in Chile?**
A: In Chile, life science technicians earn between CLP 600,000 and CLP 1,400,000 per month, depending on experience, sector (academic, biotech, environmental), and region. Entry-level positions start around CLP 550,000, while senior roles in private companies can exceed CLP 1,800,000. Benefits and bonuses may apply in larger firms.

**Q: What is the career outlook for life science technicians in Chile?**
A: The demand for life science technicians in Chile is growing, driven by expansion in biotechnology, environmental monitoring, and agricultural research. Opportunities exist in universities, government labs, and private firms. The field is moderately competitive, with good prospects for those with specialized skills in molecular biology or analytical chemistry. Job stability is favorable.

**Q: How is AI impacting the work of life science technicians in Chile?**
A: AI is automating routine data analysis and lab tasks, such as image recognition and statistical processing, allowing technicians to focus on complex procedures. In Chile, adoption varies by sector: biotech firms use AI for drug discovery, while academic labs integrate machine learning for genomics. Technicians need digital skills to operate AI-driven equipment but remain essential for hands-on work.
