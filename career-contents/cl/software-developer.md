## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Chile?**
A: In Chile, mid-level software developers earn between CLP 1.5 million and CLP 3.5 million monthly. Senior developers can earn over CLP 5 million. Salaries vary by city, with Santiago offering higher pay, and by tech stack—Java, Python, and cloud skills command premiums.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for software developers in Chile?**
A: Top technical skills include Python, JavaScript, Java, and frameworks like React or Node.js. Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), databases (SQL, MongoDB), and DevOps tools are highly valued. Strong problem-solving and communication skills are also essential for team collaboration.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting software development jobs in Chile?**
A: AI is automating repetitive coding tasks, boosting productivity, and creating demand for developers with machine learning and AI integration skills. Chilean companies are adopting AI tools, so developers who upskill in data engineering, ML frameworks, and AI ethics will have a competitive edge.
