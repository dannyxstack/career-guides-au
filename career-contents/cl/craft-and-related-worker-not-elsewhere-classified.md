## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers in Chile?**
A: In Chile, craft and related workers (ISCO 7549) earn between 700,000 and 1,200,000 CLP monthly, depending on specialization and experience. Artisans in high-demand sectors like copper or wood crafts may earn more. Self-employed workers often have variable income based on project volume and client base.

**Q: What skills are essential for craft workers in Chile?**
A: Key skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, and knowledge of traditional Chilean techniques (e.g., textiles, pottery, metalwork). Digital skills for online sales and marketing are increasingly important. Creativity and the ability to source local materials sustainably are also valued in the local market.

**Q: How is AI affecting craft and related workers in Chile?**
A: AI's impact on Chilean craft workers is currently limited but growing. Automation may streamline repetitive tasks like pattern cutting or inventory management. However, demand for unique, handmade products remains strong. Workers who blend traditional skills with digital tools (e.g., 3D modeling for prototypes) are likely to thrive.
