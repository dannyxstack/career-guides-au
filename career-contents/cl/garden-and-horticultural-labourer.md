## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Chile?**
A: In Chile, garden and horticultural labourers typically earn between 350,000 and 500,000 CLP per month, depending on experience and location. Overtime and seasonal peaks can increase earnings. Pay rates may vary between urban landscaping and agricultural settings.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for garden and horticultural labourers in Chile?**
A: Daily tasks include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, and maintaining gardens, parks, and agricultural fields. Labourers use hand tools and power equipment, apply fertilizers and pesticides, and prepare soil. Work is often outdoors and physically demanding, with seasonal variations.

**Q: How is AI impact affecting garden and horticultural labourers in Chile?**
A: AI and automation are gradually influencing horticulture in Chile through precision irrigation, drone monitoring, and robotic weeding. However, many tasks still require manual labor, so AI impact is currently low. Labourers may need to learn basic digital skills for new equipment, but job displacement is not imminent.
