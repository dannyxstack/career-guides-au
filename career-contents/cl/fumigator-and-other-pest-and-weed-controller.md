## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fumigators in Chile?**
A: In Chile, fumigators typically earn between CLP 500,000 and CLP 800,000 per month, depending on experience and employer. Those working in agriculture or specialized pest control for large companies may earn higher wages. Pay often includes bonuses for hazardous work or overtime.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for a pest controller in Chile?**
A: A typical day includes inspecting properties for pests like rodents, insects, or weeds, applying chemical or biological treatments, and ensuring safety compliance. Tasks vary from residential fumigations to large-scale agricultural spraying. Workers must document treatments and sometimes educate clients on prevention.

**Q: What is the job outlook for fumigators in Chile?**
A: Demand for fumigators is stable in Chile due to ongoing needs in agriculture, food storage, and urban pest management. Climate change may increase pest pressures, supporting steady employment. However, automation in spraying and AI-driven monitoring could reduce some manual roles, requiring workers to update skills.
