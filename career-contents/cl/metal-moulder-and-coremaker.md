**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation software assists in predicting casting shrinkage and porosity locations, guiding process improvements.

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for metal moulders and coremakers in Chile?**
A: The average monthly salary ranges from CLP 600,000 to CLP 900,000, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions start around CLP 450,000, while experienced workers in industrial sectors can earn up to CLP 1,200,000. Overtime and shift differentials may increase pay.

**Q: What skills are required for metal moulders and coremakers in Chile?**
A: Key skills include reading technical drawings, knowledge of casting processes (sand, die, investment), manual dexterity, and operating moulding machines. Physical stamina is important due to standing and lifting. Familiarity with Chilean safety regulations and basic metallurgy is expected.

**Q: What is the career outlook for metal moulders and coremakers in Chile?**
A: Demand is stable but tied to manufacturing and mining sectors. Automation reduces some manual roles, but skilled workers remain needed for complex castings. Advancement to supervisor or technician roles is possible with training. Employment is concentrated in Santiago, Valparaíso, and Biobío regions.
