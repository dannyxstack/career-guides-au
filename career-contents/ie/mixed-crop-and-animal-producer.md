# Mixed Crop and Animal Producer（Mixed Crop and Animal Producer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：6130 – Mixed Crop and Animal Producer。**

Grows multiple crops and raises livestock, managing farm operations, production planning, and resource allocation for sustainable profitability.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mixed crop and animal producers?**
答：Entry-level employees earn around €25,000-€32,000 annually, experienced managers can earn over €45,000, but income is affected by seasonality and market prices.

**问：How can foreign workers immigrate to Ireland through this occupation?**
答：This occupation is not on the shortage list. You need to apply for a General Employment Permit with a salary threshold of €30,000 and undergo a labor market test.

**问：What educational background is needed for this occupation?**
答：Formal education is not mandatory, but Teagasc offers 2-year agricultural courses that improve skills and employability.
