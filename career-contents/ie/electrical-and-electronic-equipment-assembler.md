# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 8212 (ISCO08)**

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers assemble electrical and electronic components into finished products, ensuring product quality and functionality. Requires attention to detail and operational skills.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Can advance from junior assembler to senior assembler, team leader, or quality inspector. Further study can lead to technician or engineer roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive insertion and soldering of components on PCB boards
- Use automated optical inspection equipment to replace manual visual inspection
- Mechanical arm assembly for simple components such as screws and cables
- AI vision-based defect identification and classification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AR glasses to obtain real-time overlay guidance for assembly steps
- Quick fault diagnosis through AI-assisted testing system
- Use digital twin simulation to optimize assembly processes
- Automatically generate maintenance records and reports with AI tools

**Human moat:**

- Flexible assembly of non-standard, irregular, or precision components
- Creative troubleshooting and repair of on-site equipment faults
- Collaborate with engineers to design assembly process improvements
- Customized assembly for specific client requirements

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial robot programming and debugging (e.g., ABB, Fanuc)
- PLC and automation control system basics
- AI vision system configuration and maintenance
- Knowledge of quality management systems (such as ISO 9001).
- Use of digital tools (e.g., MES, ERP)
- Troubleshooting and data analysis capabilities

**Key growth areas:**

- Manufacturing
- Electronics
- Automation
- Quality Control

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers?**
A: Junior: approx. €25,000-30,000, Intermediate: €30,000-38,000, Senior: €38,000-45,000, all before tax.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens obtain a work permit for this occupation?**
A: Typically requires a General Employment Permit, employer sponsorship, and annual salary of at least €30,000. This occupation is not a critical skills occupation and is not eligible for the Critical Skills Employment Permit.

**Q: What educational background is needed for this occupation?**
A: Usually high school diploma is sufficient, many skills gained through on-the-job training. Some employers may prefer candidates with vocational training or apprenticeships.
