# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler（Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8212 – Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler。**

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers put together components to produce finished electrical and electronic devices, ensuring quality and functionality.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非欧盟公民，需雇主担保，年薪不低于30,000欧元（通常要求） |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺职业，但该职业不在紧缺列表中，通常不适用 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers?**
答：Entry-level around €25,000-€30,000, mid-level €30,000-€38,000, senior €38,000-€45,000, gross annual.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work permit for this occupation?**
答：Typically require a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship and salary above €30,000. Not eligible for Critical Skills Employment Permit as not on shortage list.

**问：What education is needed for this occupation?**
答：Usually high school diploma; many skills learned on the job. Some employers prefer vocational training or apprenticeship.
