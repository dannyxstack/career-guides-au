# Electrical Engineering Technician（Electrical Engineering Technician）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3113 – Electrical Engineering Technician。**

Electrical engineering technicians assist engineers in designing, testing, and installing electrical systems, commonly in construction, energy, and manufacturing.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺职业，年薪门槛32,000欧元，2年后可申请Stamp 4居留 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 需要劳动力市场测试，年薪门槛30,000欧元 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能职位，年薪需达约64,000欧元（2024标准） |

**PR难度：中等（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Electrical Engineering Technician？**
- 喜欢动手操作和解决实际技术问题的人
- 对电气系统和自动化有浓厚兴趣的学生
- 希望获得稳定职业且有机会移民的人

**谁不适合学Electrical Engineering Technician？**
- 不擅长数学和物理理论的人
- 不能接受户外或工业环境工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for electrical engineering technicians?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €30,000-€40,000, mid-level €42,000-€55,000, and senior €55,000-€70,000 per year.

**问：Is this occupation easy for migration?**
答：It is on the Critical Skills list; you can apply for a Critical Skills Employment Permit and get Stamp 4 after 2 years.

**问：What education is needed to become an electrical engineering technician?**
答：Typically a 2-year higher education certificate or a relevant bachelor's degree.
