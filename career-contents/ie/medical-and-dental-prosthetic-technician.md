# Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3214 (ISCO08)**

Medical and dental prosthetic technicians design, fabricate, and repair dental prostheses, orthodontic appliances, and other medical devices, requiring fine manual skills and material knowledge.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Starting as a junior technician, gaining experience to advance to senior technician or lab supervisor; some transition to sales, R&D, or start their own labs.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Traditional plaster model making (replaced by intraoral scanning)
- Waxing and investment casting (replaced by direct CAD/CAM milling or 3D printing)
- Morphological design of standard crowns and bridges (basic form auto-generated by AI).
- Repetitive denture setup work (digital denture setup software accelerates the process)
- Some aspects of manual sanding and polishing (with automated polishing equipment becoming widespread)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use CAD software for personalized restoration design, overlaying aesthetic parameters
- Use AI scanning data to automatically generate occlusion and contact points, improving efficiency
- Using 3D printing to quickly produce trial pieces, shortening clinical feedback cycles
- Optimize color matching using digital colorimeters and AI recommendations
- Use data-driven predictive tools to optimize prosthesis lifespan design

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and adjustment for complex occlusal relationships and gnathological issues
- Communicating aesthetic requirements with dentists (e.g., personalized contours, staining features)
- Creative solutions for handling rare or unusual cases
- Refined and customized artisan techniques (e.g. ceramic layering and stacking effects)
- Experiential knowledge of materials science and biocompatibility

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in at least one CAD design software (e.g., exocad, 3Shape Dental System)
- Learn 3D printing slicer software and post-processing techniques
- Familiar with operation of intraoral scanners and digital impression processing
- Basic programming or scripting ability (for customized parameter design).
- Knowledge of digital colorimeters and color management
- Remote collaboration and digital workflow management

**Key growth areas:**

- aging population
- dental tourism
- technological advancement
- custom prosthetics
