# Beauticians and Related Workers（Beauticians and Related Workers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：5142 – Beauticians and Related Workers。**

Beauticians provide facial treatments, makeup, nail care, and body treatments in salons, spas, or self-employed.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪门槛约30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 跨国公司内部调动，需在海外公司工作6个月以上。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for beauticians in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €22,000-€28,000, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, senior up to €45,000; self-employed income varies.

**问：Can beauticians immigrate to Ireland via work visa?**
答：Beauticians are not on the critical skills list; a General Employment Permit is required with employer sponsorship and salary threshold.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a beautician in Ireland?**
答：Typically a QQI-recognized 1-2 year certificate or diploma; ITEC or CIDESCO certifications are not mandatory but boost employability.
