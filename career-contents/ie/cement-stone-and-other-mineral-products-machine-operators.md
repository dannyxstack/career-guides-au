# Cement, Stone and Other Mineral Products Machine Operators（Cement, Stone and Other Mineral Products Machine Operators）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8114 – Cement, Stone and Other Mineral Products Machine Operators。**

Operate machines for processing cement, stone, and other mineral products, including crushing, mixing, and cutting, ensuring product quality and production efficiency.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪门槛约30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能职位，但该职业通常不符合高技能要求。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for cement and stone machine operators?**
答：Entry-level operators earn around €26,000-€32,000 annually, while senior operators can earn €40,000-€50,000.

**问：Can this occupation apply for a work visa?**
答：This occupation is not on the Critical Skills list; a General Employment Permit is required, with a labor market test.

**问：What education is needed to become a machine operator?**
答：A high school diploma is usually sufficient, with on-the-job training provided. A vocational certificate can improve prospects.
