# Plasterer（Plasterer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7123 – Plasterer。**

Plasterers apply plaster, gypsum, and other materials to interior and exterior walls and ceilings to provide smooth or decorative finishes. They are typically employed by construction firms or self-employed, working on new builds and renovations.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪至少30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 抹灰工不在紧缺技能清单上，但若年薪超过60,000欧元可申请。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a plasterer in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level plasterers earn around €25,000-€35,000, experienced up to €50,000, and senior or self-employed can exceed €70,000.

**问：How can a plasterer immigrate to Ireland?**
答：Plasterers typically need a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship and minimum salary of €30,000. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：What career progression opportunities exist for plasterers?**
答：Progression from apprentice to skilled worker, then to foreman or project manager. Self-employment or specialization in decorative plastering are also options.
