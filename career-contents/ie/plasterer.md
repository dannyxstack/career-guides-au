# Plasterer — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 7123 (ISCO08)**

Plasterers apply plaster, gypsum, and other materials to interior and exterior walls to provide a smooth or decorative finish. They are usually employed by construction companies or self-employed, working on new builds and renovations.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Plasterers can progress from apprentice to tradesperson, and with experience become foremen or project managers. Some choose to start their own plastering companies. Continuous learning and mastering new techniques (e.g., decorative plastering) can enhance competitiveness.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic plastering machine for large wall surfaces
- AI monitoring system controls mortar mix ratios.
- Robots performing high-rise exterior wall plastering.
- Digital model-guided automated painting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design of complex decorative patterns
- AR glasses overlay construction instructions.
- Smart tools provide real-time feedback on coating thickness
- Drone inspection identifies wall defects
- Predictive maintenance reduces rework

**Human moat:**

- Complex curves and artistic plastering
- Craftsmanship judgment for historical building restoration
- Flexible handling of on-site unexpected issues
- Client communication and customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of automated plastering machines
- Building Information Modeling (BIM) basics
- Application of special materials (e.g., eco-friendly plaster)
- Data analysis and yield rate control.
- Basics of collaborative robot programming
- Sustainable construction practices

**Key growth areas:**

- Construction Boom
- Housing Demand
- Renovation
- Skilled Trades Shortage

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a plasterer in Ireland?**
A: Junior plasterer annual salary around EUR 25,000-35,000, skilled worker up to EUR 50,000, senior or self-employed over EUR 70,000.

**Q: How can a plasterer immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Plasterers typically need a General Employment Permit to immigrate, requiring employer sponsorship and an annual salary of at least €30,000. EU citizens can move freely.

**Q: What are the career development opportunities for plasterers?**
A: Can progress from apprentice to skilled worker, then to foreman or project manager. May also start their own business or shift to specialized areas like decorative plastering.
