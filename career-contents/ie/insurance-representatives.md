# Insurance Representatives（Insurance Representatives）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3321 – Insurance Representatives。**

Insurance representatives sell various insurance products such as life, health, and property insurance to clients and assist with claims.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 需年薪至少34,000欧元，雇主担保，不属紧缺清单，配额限制 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 通常不适用，保险代表不在紧缺职业列表 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需年薪约60,000欧元以上，高级职位可能符合 |

**PR难度：高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the compensation structure for insurance representatives?**
答：Typically a base salary plus sales commissions and performance bonuses; senior roles include team overrides.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become insurance representatives in Ireland?**
答：They need a work permit, typically a General Employment Permit, with salary at least €34,000, and the employer must demonstrate labor market need.
