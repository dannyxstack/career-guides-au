# Forestry and Related Workers — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 6210 (ISCO08)**

Engage in forest planting, maintenance, harvesting, and timber transport in Ireland, including tree planting, pruning, logging, and forest fire prevention tasks.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level workers can advance to forestry supervisor or forest manager through experience, or transition into conservation or environmental fields.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using drones for forest resource survey and mapping
- Automatically records felling volume and tree species distribution via GPS and sensors
- Using AI algorithms to optimize planting spacing and harvesting routes
- Scheduling and monitoring of automated irrigation and fertilization systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted early warning for pests and diseases and precision pesticide application
- Using satellite remote sensing data to guide selective logging and increase timber value
- Intelligent decision support system optimizes logging plans to match market demand
- Digital twin simulating the ecological impact of different harvesting plans.

**Human moat:**

- Safely operating heavy machinery in complex terrains like steep and muddy areas
- On-site decision-making in emergencies (e.g., equipment failure, forest fires)
- Intuitive knowledge of local tree species growth habits.
- Artificial intervention and adjustment of cross-species ecological balance

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and image analysis
- GIS Geographic Information System application
- Using mobile phones/tablets for mobile data collection
- Basic data analysis and Excel/dashboard operations
- Knowledge of forest ecology and sustainable management
- Communication and collaboration (working with AI advisory systems)

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable forestry
- Mechanization
- Bioenergy demand
- Carbon sequestration

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy outdoor physical work

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike outdoor or physical labor.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of forestry workers?**
A: Junior annual salary approximately 25,000-32,000 euros, senior up to 50,000 euros, but affected by seasonality and contract-based employment.

**Q: Can foreign forestry workers immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Forestry workers are not on Ireland's shortage occupation list; immigration typically requires a General Employment Permit, but the threshold is high.
