## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for Personnel and Careers Professionals in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €35,000-€45,000, mid-level €50,000-€70,000, senior up to €80,000-€110,000.

**问：Is it easy to get a work visa for HR professionals in Ireland?**
答：Not typically on the Critical Skills list; usually requires a General Employment Permit with labor market test, or EU Blue Card for high salaries.

**问：What is the career progression for Personnel and Careers Professionals?**
答：Start as HR assistant or recruiter, progress to HR manager, consultant, and eventually HR director or independent consultant.
