# Health Professional Not Elsewhere Classified（Health Professional Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2269 – Health Professional Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Includes health professionals not elsewhere classified, such as public health experts and health policy advisors, supporting Ireland's healthcare system.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺技能职业，但此类职位通常不列入列表。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 需劳动力市场测试，薪资门槛€30,000。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能职位，薪资门槛约€40,000，但该职业可能不满足条件。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level €30,000-€40,000, mid-level €40,000-€55,000, senior €55,000-€75,000.

**问：Which visas can I use to migrate to Ireland for this occupation?**
答：Mainly General Employment Permit, requiring a labor market test. Critical Skills and EU Blue Card are usually not applicable.

**问：What education background is needed to enter this occupation?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in health sciences or related field; a master's degree aids advancement.
