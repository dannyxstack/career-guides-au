# Health Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2269 (ISCO08)**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from junior researcher to senior analyst or management roles, or transition into clinical health professions or academic research.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine statistical analysis and trend forecasting of epidemiological data
- Systematic review of health policy literature and preliminary report writing
- Cross-agency data integration and standardised processing
- Data collection and preliminary analysis in health program evaluations
- Junior content generation for health promotion materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI to simulate the effects of different health policy interventions to support decision-making.
- Using natural language processing to quickly analyze large-scale public health feedback
- AI assists in identifying abnormal signals in health trends, providing early warnings.
- Automatically generate visual reports to improve communication efficiency
- Use machine learning to optimize resource allocation and project planning

**Human moat:**

- Ethical judgment and trade-offs in complex situations.
- In-depth understanding of health needs in cross-cultural communities
- Policy innovation and strategic leadership
- In-depth negotiation and trust-building with stakeholders
- On-site decision-making ability to respond to public health emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and Python/R programming
- Use of AI-assisted decision-making tools (e.g. policy simulation software)
- Health informatics and data visualization
- Cross-departmental collaboration and project management.
- Critical thinking and ethical decision-making frameworks
- Tracking latest AI application trends in public health

**Key growth areas:**

- Public Health
- Health Policy
- Data Analysis
- Digital Health

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for unclassified health professionals in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level approximately €30,000-€40,000, mid-level €40,000-€55,000, senior €55,000-€75,000.

**Q: Which visas can be used to immigrate to Ireland for this occupation?**
A: General Employment Permit (main requirement), but labor market testing needed. Critical Skills Permit and EU Blue Card are generally not applicable.

**Q: What educational background is required to enter this occupation?**
A: Typically requires a bachelor's degree in health science or a related field; a master's degree aids advancement.
