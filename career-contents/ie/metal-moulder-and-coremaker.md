# Metal Moulder and Coremaker — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 7211 (ISCO08)**

Metal casting moulders and coremakers manufacture moulds and cores for metal casting, working in foundries or metal fabrication plants. The occupation requires manual and mechanical skills, typically entered through apprenticeships or vocational training.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Entry-level workers can advance to skilled mold makers through apprenticeships, and further to supervisors or casting technicians. Some workers may transition to related manufacturing roles, such as machine operation or quality control.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual creation of sand molds/cores using modeling tools (replaced by 3D printing)
- Manually set mold parameters based on drawings (replaced by AI automatic calculation)
- Repetitive sand processing and mixing processes (replaced by automated equipment)
- Surface finishing and grinding of castings (replaced by robotic polishing)
- Visual defect recognition in quality inspection (replaced by AI vision systems)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulates casting processes, optimizing gating system design to reduce defect rates
- Laser scanning and reconstruction assist in complex mold repair.
- Real-time monitoring of sand mold quality using digital twins to improve yield rates
- AR headset guides manual die repair, reducing errors
- Intelligent scheduling system coordinates die production and casting rhythm

**Human moat:**

- Handles creative design solutions for non-standard, complex geometric molds
- Experience in on-site fault diagnosis and emergency mold repair
- Intuitive judgment of physical properties such as metal shrinkage and thermal stress
- Communication skills for discussing custom requirements with clients.
- Safe operation and responsibility for compliance with foundry industry standards

**Skills to build:**

- 3D modeling and printing (e.g. SolidWorks, ZBrush)
- CNC machining (CNC programming and operation)
- Casting simulation software (e.g., ProCAST)
- Basics of robot operation and maintenance
- Metallic Materials Science (casting properties of different alloys)
- Digital quality management (statistical process control)

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable demand
- Manufacturing
- Engineering
- Apprenticeship
