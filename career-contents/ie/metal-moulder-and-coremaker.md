# Metal Moulder and Coremaker（Metal Moulder and Coremaker）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7211 – Metal Moulder and Coremaker。**

Metal moulders and coremakers create molds and cores for metal casting, working in foundries or metal fabrication plants. The role involves manual and machine skills, typically entered via apprenticeship or vocational training.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，薪资需达到一定门槛（通常年薪至少30,000欧元）。 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 适用于跨国企业内部调动的员工，需在爱尔兰分公司工作。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for metal moulders and coremakers in Ireland?**
答：Average salary is around €32,000-€40,000 per year (pre-tax), depending on experience, location, and employer.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for immigrating to Ireland?**
答：This occupation is not on the Critical Skills list; work permit requires employer sponsorship and salary threshold, making immigration difficult.

**问：What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
答：Progression includes becoming a supervisor, foundry technician, or moving into quality control; further training can lead to engineering roles.
