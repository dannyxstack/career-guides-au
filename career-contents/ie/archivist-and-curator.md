# Archivist and Curator（Archivist and Curator）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2621 – Archivist and Curator。**

Archivists and curators manage, preserve, and display collections of historical, cultural, or artistic significance, working in museums, archives, libraries, and heritage institutions.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for archivists in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-€34,000, mid-level €35,000-€45,000, senior up to €60,000+.

**问：How can foreigners get a work visa for archivist jobs in Ireland?**
答：Typically a General Employment Permit is needed, requiring a salary over €30,000 and employer sponsorship.

**问：What education is needed to become an archivist?**
答：A bachelor's or master's degree in archival science, museum studies, or related humanities field is typically required.
