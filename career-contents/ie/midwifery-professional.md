# Midwifery Professionals — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2222 (ISCO08)**

Midwives provide antenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care to pregnant women, including newborn care and health counseling.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can progress from junior midwife to senior midwife, midwifery consultant, or management positions, or transition to academic research.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Electronic medical record entry and basic data sorting.
- Automated analysis and alerting for routine fetal heart rate monitoring
- automatic monitoring and statistics of basic parameters of labor progress
- Automated generation of standardized postpartum follow-up information.
- Inventory management and automatic replenishment reminders for pharmaceuticals and supplies.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making systems provide high-risk pregnancy warnings and obstetric complication risk predictions.
- Remote fetal monitoring combined with AI analysis improves community home care efficiency
- Personalized prenatal education content generated by AI allows midwives to focus on face-to-face consultations.
- Automatically record and structure consultation information using natural language processing to reduce paperwork time
- Augmented reality (AR)-assisted simulation training to enhance crisis response practice effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- Experience-based intuitive judgment and manual examination during labour
- Emotional support and psychological guidance during childbirth
- Rapid manual intervention and team coordination in emergencies (e.g., postpartum hemorrhage)
- Establishing deep trust relationships and personalized communication with mothers and families
- Integration of clinical decisions in interdisciplinary collaboration (e.g., with obstetricians, pediatricians)

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and Interpretation of AI-Assisted Clinical Decision Support Systems
- Remote health monitoring equipment and platform applications
- Data analysis fundamentals (e.g., understanding patient risk scoring models)
- Advanced features and efficiency tools for digital health records
- Interdisciplinary communication and team collaboration (especially with AI experts)
- Ability to apply evidence-based practice and critically evaluate AI recommendations

**Key growth areas:**

- Ageing workforce
- Maternity services expansion
- Government health investment
- Rural care demand
