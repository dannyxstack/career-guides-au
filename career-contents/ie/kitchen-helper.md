# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Assists chefs in food preparation, washing dishes, and maintaining kitchen cleanliness in commercial kitchens; a basic role in the hospitality industry.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can typically progress from kitchen hand to assistant chef or junior chef, requiring experience accumulation and relevant cooking certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic vegetable cutting machine completes food ingredient preparation
- Dishwasher automatically cleans dishes
- Automatic cleaning robots sweep kitchen floors
- Smart inventory system manages ingredient stocktaking
- Automatic cooking equipment completes simple frying and grilling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI recipe recommendation system assisting menu design and portion control.
- Smart scheduling systems optimize helper workforce arrangements

**Human moat:**

- Ability to communicate and collaborate with others (responding to chef's ad hoc instructions)
- Handling unexpected situations (e.g., equipment failure, dish rework)

**Skills to build:**

- Use digital ordering and inventory systems

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a kitchen porter in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level annual salary ranges from about 20,000 to 24,000 euros, senior from 28,000 to 32,000 euros.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland as a kitchen hand?**
A: Kitchen hand is not on the shortage occupation list; applying for a regular work permit requires an annual salary of at least 30,000 euros, which is usually hard to meet, so immigration pathways are very limited.

**Q: Are there promotion opportunities for kitchen assistants?**
A: Yes, by gaining experience and obtaining a cooking certificate, one can advance to chef assistant or junior chef.
