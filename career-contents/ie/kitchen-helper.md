# Kitchen Helper（Kitchen Helper）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9412 – Kitchen Helper。**

Assists chefs with food preparation, dishwashing, and kitchen cleaning in commercial kitchens; an entry-level role in hospitality.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，通常要求年薪不低于30,000欧元，但厨房帮工薪资较低，较难获得。 |
| Subclass EU自由流动（EU Free Movement） | 欧盟/瑞士公民可自由在爱尔兰工作，无需工作许可。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a kitchen helper in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salary is about €20,000-€24,000 per year, rising to €28,000-€32,000 for senior roles.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland as a kitchen helper?**
答：Kitchen helper is not on the critical skills list; General Employment Permit requires a salary of at least €30,000, often unattainable, so migration options are very limited.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities for kitchen helpers?**
答：Yes, with experience and culinary certificates, you can advance to chef assistant or junior chef.
