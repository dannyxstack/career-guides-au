# Building Construction Labourer（Building Construction Labourer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9313 – Building Construction Labourer。**

Building construction labourers perform a variety of physical tasks on construction sites, such as moving materials, digging, and cleaning. They provide essential support to skilled tradespeople.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪门槛30,000欧元以上。建筑工通常不属紧缺列表，因此需此许可。 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 适用于跨国调派员工，需在爱尔兰子公司工作。 |
| Subclass EU Freedom of Moveme（EU Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟/欧洲经济区公民无需工作许可，可直接工作。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for building construction labourers?**
答：Entry-level workers earn around €25,000-€32,000 per year, rising to €50,000 with experience, especially with specialized skills.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to Ireland as building labourers?**
答：They need a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship and a salary of at least €30,000. The occupation is not on the shortage list, making it more challenging.

**问：Can building labourers switch to other trades?**
答：Yes, through apprenticeships they can become skilled tradespeople like carpenters or electricians, improving income and stability.
