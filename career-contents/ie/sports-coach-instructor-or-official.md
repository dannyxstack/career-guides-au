# Sports Coach, Instructor or Official（Sports Coach, Instructor or Official）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3422 – Sports Coach, Instructor or Official。**

Sports coaches, instructors and officials in Ireland train athletes, organize training sessions and officiate competitions. They work in schools, clubs and sports bodies, promoting fitness and competitive sports.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a sports coach in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level coaches earn around €25,000-€35,000 per year, while senior coaches can earn €50,000-€70,000. Part-time positions are common, leading to variable incomes.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland as a sports coach?**
答：They need employer sponsorship for a General Employment Permit, with salary typically above €30,000. As the occupation is not on the critical skills list, immigration is challenging; however, EU family members can work freely.

**问：What certifications do sports coaches need?**
答：They must obtain a coaching qualification accredited by Sport Ireland and a first aid certificate. Specific sports may require officiating qualifications from relevant federations. A university degree can enhance competitiveness.
