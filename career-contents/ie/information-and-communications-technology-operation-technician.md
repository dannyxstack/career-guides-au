# Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3511 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for installing, maintaining, and supporting an organization's ICT systems, including networks, servers, and communication equipment. Ensure system operations and resolve technical issues.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from junior technical support to system administrator or network engineer. By obtaining certifications like CCNA or Azure, one can move into cloud architecture or IT management roles. Experienced professionals can become IT managers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Real-time monitoring of system logs and sending alerts
- Perform routine backup and recovery operations
- Troubleshoot common network/server faults according to the manual
- Configuring standard network equipment manually
- Generate routine operation and maintenance reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI to analyze logs and predict failures, enabling proactive repairs
- Use AI to generate automated deployment and configuration scripts
- Using AI to assist in diagnosing complex network performance issues
- Optimize resource allocation and capacity planning with AI
- using AI-driven chatbots to support user self-service troubleshooting

**Human moat:**

- Design high availability and disaster recovery architectures
- Creative troubleshooting and root cause analysis for unstructured faults
- Cross-team communication and emergency coordination
- Security policy customization and compliance auditing
- Manual operation and maintenance of legacy systems (no API/difficult to automate)

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud platform (AWS/Azure) operations and automation
- Infrastructure as Code（Terraform/Ansible）
- AI and Machine Learning Basics (for Log Analysis)
- Python/Go scripting
- Containerization and orchestration (Kubernetes).
- Cybersecurity fundamentals and compliance knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud Computing
- Cybersecurity
- 5G Rollout
- Remote Work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of an ICT operations technician?**
A: Junior approx. €30k-40k, Mid-level €42k-60k, Senior €60k-85k.

**Q: What work visas can ICT Operations Technicians apply for?**
A: Eligible for Critical Skills Employment Permit (salary ≥€32k) or General Employment Permit (salary ≥€30k).

**Q: What are the career prospects for this occupation?**
A: Opportunities for promotion to system administrator, network engineer, or cloud architect. With experience, can also transition to IT project management.
