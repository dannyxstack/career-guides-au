# Buyer（Buyer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3323 – Buyer。**

Buyers purchase goods and services from suppliers, negotiate contracts, manage inventory, and ensure organizations obtain needed materials at optimal costs. In Ireland, the role spans retail, manufacturing, and public sectors.

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪不低于30,000欧元的工作，需通过劳动力市场测试 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪超60,000欧元或紧缺职业（采购不在此列），但高级职位可能符合 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年薪超欧盟平均工资1.5倍（约60,000欧元），需大学学历 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for buyers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level buyers earn €30,000-€40,000, mid-level €40,000-€60,000, and senior €60,000-€85,000 annually, depending on industry and experience.

**问：Can buyers apply for work visas in Ireland?**
答：Yes, a General Employment Permit is available for salaries ≥€30,000 (labor market test required). Senior roles (≥€60,000) may qualify for Critical Skills or EU Blue Card.

**问：What is the career progression for buyers?**
答：Start as a purchasing assistant, then advance to buyer, senior buyer, procurement manager, and supply chain director. CIPS certification and international experience aid progression.
