# Electrical Line Installers and Repairers（Electrical Line Installers and Repairers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7413 – Electrical Line Installers and Repairers。**

Install, maintain, and repair electrical transmission and distribution lines and cables, ensuring safe power supply.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需年薪≥30,000欧元，雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 若列入紧缺职业可申请，年薪≥32,000欧元，简化永居路径。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能工种，年薪≥33,000欧元（2023年标准），需大学学历或5年经验。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for electrical line installers?**
答：Entry-level €30,000-€40,000, mid €45,000-€55,000, senior €60,000-€75,000 per year.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to work as electrical line installers in Ireland?**
答：Require an employer-sponsored work permit (General/Critical Skills Employment Permit), meeting salary thresholds and passing labor market test.

**问：What training is needed to become a qualified electrical line installer?**
答：A 3-4 year registered apprenticeship with classroom and on-the-job training, leading to a national certificate.
