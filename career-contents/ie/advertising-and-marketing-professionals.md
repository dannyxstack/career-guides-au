# Advertising and Marketing Professionals（Advertising and Marketing Professionals）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2431 – Advertising and Marketing Professionals。**

In Ireland, advertising and marketing professionals develop and execute marketing strategies, manage brand communications, and conduct market research and analysis. The role spans digital marketing, advertising planning, and brand management, playing a key role in business growth.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for advertising and marketing professionals in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €30,000 to €45,000, mid-level from €45,000 to €65,000, and senior roles can exceed €90,000, depending on experience, industry, and company size.

**问：How can overseas advertising and marketing professionals immigrate to Ireland?**
答：Apply for a Critical Skills Employment Permit (annual salary €38,000+ and in shortage occupation) or a General Employment Permit (salary €30,000+). Skills in digital marketing and data analytics are in demand, easing the process.

**问：What key skills are required for this occupation?**
答：Digital marketing, SEO/SEM, content creation, data analytics, social media management, communication, and project management skills are essential. Continuous learning of new technologies and trends is crucial.
