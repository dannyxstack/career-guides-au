# Print Finishing and Binding Worker（Print Finishing and Binding Worker）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7323 – Print Finishing and Binding Worker。**

Print finishing and binding workers perform post-printing operations such as cutting, folding, binding, and laminating to produce finished print products. They are employed in book publishing and commercial printing sectors in Ireland.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪通常不低于30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 跨国公司内部调动，适用于有海外关联公司的员工。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for print finishing and binding workers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €22,000 to €28,000, mid-level €28,000 to €35,000, and senior positions up to €45,000 per year.

**问：Can foreign workers immigrate to Ireland for this occupation through a work visa?**
答：This occupation is not on the Critical Skills list; a General Employment Permit is required, with a salary threshold of around €30,000 and a labor market test.
