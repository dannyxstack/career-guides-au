# Education Methods Specialist — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2351 (ISCO08)**

Education methods expert responsible for designing, evaluating, and improving teaching methods and curricula to enhance educational quality. They work in schools, universities, or educational consulting organizations, conducting research and providing teacher training.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior education methodology specialists can advance to senior specialists or educational consultants, or transition to education management roles such as curriculum directors or education policy analysts

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Develop standardized teaching plans and question banks
- Basic statistics and simple analysis of entrance exam scores.
- Editing and distribution of standard instructional videos
- Frequently asked questions in teacher training
- Initial layout and proofreading of course materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze student learning data and personalize teaching strategies
- Designing adaptive learning paths through intelligent tutoring systems
- Use natural language processing tools to quickly generate teaching cases
- Develop immersive courses using Virtual Reality/Augmented Reality
- Use AI-driven assessment tools to monitor classroom performance in real time

**Human moat:**

- Understanding Complex Educational Psychology and Child Development Theories
- Design creative and emotionally engaging teaching activities.
- Provide customized empathetic support for students with special needs
- Interdisciplinary Curriculum Integration and Value Judgment
- deep interpersonal communication with teachers and parents

**Skills to build:**

- Educational data mining and learning analytics
- AI-assisted course design tools (e.g., AI lesson planner)
- Adaptive learning system configuration and optimization
- Human-machine collaborative teaching method
- EdTech project management and ethical review
- hybrid teaching model design

**Key growth areas:**

- Educational Technology
- Curriculum Development
- Teacher Training
- Policy Analysis
