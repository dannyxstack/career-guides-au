# Mining and Quarrying Labourer（Mining and Quarrying Labourer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9311 – Mining and Quarrying Labourer。**

Mining and quarrying labourers perform physical tasks such as moving materials, cleaning equipment, and maintaining sites in mines or quarries.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for mining labourers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €25,000-€32,000, mid-level €32,000-€40,000, senior up to €50,000 including overtime.

**问：What visa is needed for mining labourers to immigrate to Ireland?**
答：Typically a General Employment Permit, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. This occupation is not on the Critical Skills list.
