# Mining and Quarrying Labourer — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 9311 (ISCO08)**

Mining and quarrying labourers perform physical labour in mines or quarries, including ore handling, equipment cleaning, site maintenance, and other basic tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Limited career progression: can advance from laborer to operator or technician with additional training. Long-term, may move to site supervision or related industries.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual ore handling and loading
- Routine site cleaning and equipment wiping.
- Inspect along a fixed route and report
- Manual operation of drilling and crushing equipment
- Simple repetitive material sorting and stacking

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using drones for slope stability inspection
- Monitor equipment health through digital twin systems
- Using wearable devices to warn of fatigue or danger
- Anomaly intervention during operation of automated drilling rigs
- Optimize transport routes based on data analysis

**Human moat:**

- Fault diagnosis and emergency handling in complex environments
- Involving supervision of safety protocols and hazard inspection
- Cross-departmental coordination with geology and engineering teams
- Fine manual operations (e.g., precision blasting charge)
- On-site repair of unforeseen mechanical failures

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial robot/remote control system operation
- Basic data analysis and sensor maintenance
- Safety plans and emergency response training
- Digital mining software (e.g., Surpac)
- Drone pilot certificate
- Communication and teamwork skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Construction
- Extractive industries
- Quarrying

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Physically fit, able to work outdoors and in harsh environments
- Seeking quick entry into the labor market without requiring high education
- Willing to do repetitive physical work with team spirit

**Who is not suited**
- Those seeking career advancement or high income
- People sensitive to safety and health risks who prefer office work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for mining laborers?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approx. €25,000–32,000, mid-level €32,000–40,000, senior up to €50,000, including overtime.

**Q: What visa is required for a mining labourer to immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Typically requires a General Employment Permit, employer sponsorship, and meeting the salary threshold. This occupation is not on the Critical Skills List.
