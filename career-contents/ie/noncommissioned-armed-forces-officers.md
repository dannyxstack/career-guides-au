# Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers（Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：0210 – Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers。**

Non-commissioned officers in the Irish Defence Forces are the backbone, leading soldiers, executing military missions, and conducting daily training. They require high discipline, leadership, and physical fitness.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

**AI替代风险：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

主要增长方向：

- Modernization
- Peacekeeping
- Cyber defense
- Leadership

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非欧盟公民，需获得国防军工作邀请，但通常只对爱尔兰或欧盟公民开放 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 国防军职位通常不符合紧缺技能清单，极少签发 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers？**
- 具有高度纪律性和团队精神的人
- 愿意从事国防事业并长期服役的人
- 身体素质优秀、能承受高强度训练的人

**谁不适合学Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers？**
- 寻求高移民便利性的人
- 偏好灵活工作时间和低风险环境的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for non-commissioned armed forces officers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-€35,000, senior up to €52,000-€65,000, plus allowances.

**问：Can foreigners apply for non-commissioned officer positions in Ireland?**
答：Generally only Irish and some EU citizens; non-EU citizens rarely receive work permits.

**问：What is the career progression?**
答：Start as a soldier, progress to corporal, sergeant, staff sergeant, and eventually company sergeant major or higher.
