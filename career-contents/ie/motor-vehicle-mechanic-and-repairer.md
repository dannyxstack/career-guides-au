# Motor Vehicle Mechanic — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 7231 (ISCO08)**

Diagnose, repair and maintain mechanical and electronic systems of cars and light commercial vehicles, including engines, transmissions, brakes and air conditioning systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior mechanics can advance to senior technicians, workshop supervisors, or open their own repair shops. Obtaining manufacturer certifications (e.g., BMW, Volkswagen) can boost salary and promotion opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults
- Fine disassembly and calibration requiring tactile feedback
- Comprehensive vehicle condition assessment and repair plan decision-making
- Customer trust communication and personalized service.

**Skills to build:**

- Mastery of AI diagnostic tools (e.g., Dealer-Fix, TEXA IDC5)
- Learning hybrid/electric vehicle high-voltage system repair
- Familiar with in-vehicle network diagnostics and software update processes
- Practice using AR-assisted maintenance equipment
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn the basics of data analysis and predictive maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric Vehicle Maintenance
- Advanced Diagnostics
- Hybrid Systems
- Telematics Integration

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys hands-on repair work and has a strong interest in automotive mechanics and electronic systems.
- Willing to undergo continuous technical training (especially in the electric vehicle sector)
- Detail-oriented, able to independently diagnose complex faults

**Who is not suited**
- Dislike of physical labor or long-term standing work
- Lack of willingness to learn rapidly changing technologies (e.g., electric systems)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for an automotive mechanic in Ireland?**
A: Average annual salary around €35,000, entry-level about €26,000, senior technicians can earn over €55,000, depending on experience and skills.

**Q: How can overseas mechanics immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Apply through General Employment Permit (requires annual salary over 30,000 euros and labor market test) or Critical Skills Employment Permit (shortage occupation, annual salary over 32,000 euros).

**Q: What training is needed to become an automotive mechanic?**
A: Typically requires a QQI Level 6 National Certificate in Automotive Repair or a 4-year apprenticeship; electric vehicle repair certification is increasingly important.
