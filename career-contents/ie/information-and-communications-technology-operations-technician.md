# Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician（Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3511 – Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician。**

Install, maintain, and support ICT systems including networks, servers, and communication equipment. Ensure system uptime and troubleshoot technical issues.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适合年薪至少€32,000的ICT技术人员，允许家庭随行并快速转永居。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪€30,000以上的岗位，需通过劳动力市场测试。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 年薪需达到€43,759（2024标准），途径灵活。 |

**PR难度：低（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an ICT Operations Technician?**
答：Junior: €30k-40k, Mid-level: €42k-60k, Senior: €60k-85k.

**问：What work visas can an ICT Operations Technician apply for?**
答：Critical Skills Employment Permit (salary ≥€32k) or General Employment Permit (salary ≥€30k).

**问：What are the career prospects for this role?**
答：Opportunities to advance to system administrator, network engineer, or cloud architect. Can also move into IT project management.
