# Spray Painter — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 7132 (ISCO08)**

A skilled tradesperson who uses spray guns and other equipment to apply paint and varnish to vehicles, furniture, building surfaces, etc.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Can advance from apprenticeship to senior technician, then to supervisor or start your own spray painting workshop.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive surface spraying operations (e.g., automotive and furniture primers) replaced by robotic automation systems
- Simple color mixing and painting parameters are automatically generated by AI software
- Spray painting processes for large-volume standardized products are taken over by integrated automatic lines
- Visual inspection of spray quality replaced by computer vision systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Intelligent spraying robot path planning (AI-optimized trajectory to reduce overspray)
- AI-based defect detection (real-time feedback to adjust spraying parameters)
- Digital workflow management (AI scheduling and paint inventory forecasting)
- Advanced colour matching system (AI quickly matches complex colour schemes)
- Remote monitoring and diagnosis (AI analysis of spraying equipment status)

**Human moat:**

- Fine spraying of non-standard surfaces (e.g., artistic painting, repairs), requiring manual adjustments
- Manual spraying of high-value custom parts (customer requires special texture or effect)
- On-site painting environment adaptation (complex curved surfaces, space constraints)
- Equipment maintenance and emergency troubleshooting (based on experience)

**Skills to build:**

- Robot programming and operation (e.g. ABB, Fanuc spray painting robots)
- AI-driven color matching software (e.g., PPG, Axalta's color systems)
- Digital painting parameter optimization (learn painting simulation software)
- Equipment maintenance and fault diagnosis (mechanical and electrical basics)
- Quality data analysis (using SPC tools to monitor coating quality)
- Safety and environmental compliance (VOC control, spray booth management)

**Key growth areas:**

- Construction
- Automotive refinishing
- Industrial maintenance
- Infrastructure renewal

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are hands-on, attentive to detail, and able to adapt to repetitive work.
- Skilled workers seeking long-term development in the automotive or construction industry.

**Who is not suited**
- People who are sensitive to chemical smells or dislike physical labor.
- For those seeking high salary or rapid promotion.
