# Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments（Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：5151 – Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments。**

Supervise cleaning staff to maintain hygiene standards in offices, hotels, and other establishments. Duties include scheduling, training, inventory management, and quality control.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于薪资低于关键技能门槛的职位，需雇主担保，劳动力市场测试。年薪通常要求€30,000以上。 |
| Subclass Intra-company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 跨国企业内部调岗，适用于管理层或专家职位，需在爱尔兰子公司工作。 |
| Subclass EU Free Movement（EU/EEA/Swiss Citizenship） | 欧盟公民无需工作许可，可自由在爱尔兰工作。 |

**PR难度：难（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a cleaning supervisor?**
答：Average salary in Ireland is around €30,000-€35,000 per year, entry-level €25,000, senior up to €45,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work permit?**
答：Requires employer sponsorship for a General Employment Permit, typically with salary above €30,000 and a labor market test.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities?**
答：Promotion to area manager, facilities manager, or operations director. Management certifications and experience help.
