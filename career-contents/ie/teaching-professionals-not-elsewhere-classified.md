## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺技能岗位，但教学专业人员通常不在紧缺清单中 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 一般工作许可，需劳动力市场测试 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能岗位，年薪需高于门槛（约33,000欧元） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for teaching professionals in Ireland?**
答：Lecturers earn around €35,000-€45,000, while senior lecturers or professors can earn €60,000-€90,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland as teaching professionals?**
答：Typically require a work permit such as a General Employment Permit (with labor market test) or EU Blue Card (meeting salary threshold).

**问：What is the career path for teaching professionals?**
答：Can progress from lecturer to senior lecturer, professor, or move into education management and policy.
