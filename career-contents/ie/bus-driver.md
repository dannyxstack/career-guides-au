# Bus Driver（Bus Driver）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8331 – Bus Driver。**

Bus drivers operate buses, coaches, or trolleybuses to transport passengers along prescribed routes and on scheduled trips, stopping at designated points. Tasks include vehicle inspection, fare collection, and ensuring passenger safety.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需年薪至少30,000欧元。雇主需进行劳动力市场测试。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 仅限爱尔兰短缺职业（如工程师等），公交车司机不符合资格。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Bus Driver？**
- 喜欢与人打交道，善于沟通
- 能够适应不规律的工作时间和交通压力
- 持有或愿意考取D类驾照

**谁不适合学Bus Driver？**
- 无法承受长时间久坐和重复性工作
- 对交通拥堵和乘客行为缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bus drivers?**
答：The average pre-tax annual salary for bus drivers in Ireland ranges from €28,000 to €48,000, depending on experience and employer.

**问：Can bus drivers immigrate to Ireland via a work visa?**
答：Bus drivers are not on the Critical Skills list; they can only apply for a General Employment Permit, requiring a salary of at least €30,000 and a labor market test.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities for bus drivers?**
答：Yes, they can advance to senior driver, trainer, dispatcher, or fleet manager, and can also obtain a coach license for long-distance routes.
