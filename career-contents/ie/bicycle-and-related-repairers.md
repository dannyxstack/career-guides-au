# Bicycle and Related Repairers（Bicycle and Related Repairers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7234 – Bicycle and Related Repairers。**

Repair and maintain bicycles, wheelchairs, and other non-motorized vehicles and related equipment.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，年薪需达3万欧元以上。 |
| Subclass Work Permit for Non-（Work Permit for Non-EEA） | 雇主担保，通常需劳动市场测试。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bicycle repairers?**
答：Entry-level around €22,000-€28,000, senior up to €45,000.

**问：Is it easy for non-EU citizens to get a work visa?**
答：Generally difficult as the occupation is not on the Critical Skills list, requiring employer sponsorship and labor market test.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities?**
答：Yes, can become senior technician or open own shop, also expand to e-bike repair.
