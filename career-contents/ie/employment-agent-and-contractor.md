# Employment Agent — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3333 (ISCO08)**

Employment consultants work in recruitment agencies, responsible for finding qualified candidates for employers and helping job seekers find suitable positions.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Can advance from junior recruitment consultant to senior consultant, team leader, or business development manager, or transition to internal HR roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated resume screening and keyword matching
- Initial candidate-job matching recommendation
- Automatic generation and publication of job advertisements
- Basic chatbot responses to common candidate questions
- Automatic interview scheduling and reminders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze big data to predict talent market trends
- Provides more precise recommendations via AI-enhanced candidate matching systems.
- Use automation tools to batch process administrative tasks, focusing on in-depth consultation.
- Personalized career development advice with the help of AI
- After initial screening with chatbots, focus on relationship maintenance with high-end candidates.

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and long-term client relationships
- Complex negotiation and persuasion skills
- Deep understanding of industry and role culture
- Handling sensitive information and ethical judgment
- Creative problem-solving and customised strategies

**Skills to build:**

- Skills in operating and optimizing AI recruitment platforms
- Data analysis and visualization (e.g., Python, Excel, Tableau)
- Communication and advanced consulting skills
- Advanced applications of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems
- Talent market trend forecasting analysis
- Social media recruitment strategies and personal branding

**Key growth areas:**

- Recruitment
- Talent Acquisition
- HR Tech
- Remote Work

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P (General Employment Permit) | Applicable for jobs with an annual income of at least €30,000; employment consultants typically meet this criterion |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl (Critical Skills Employment Permit) | If the position is on the shortage occupation list, annual salary must exceed €32,000; employment consultants are usually not on the list |
| Subclass Stamp 1G (Third Leve (Stamp 1G (Third Level Graduate)) | Can stay in Ireland to find work after graduation, allowing full-time employment |

**PR friendliness: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a careers counsellor?**
A: Entry-level approximately €28k-35k, mid-level €40k-55k, senior €60k-90k, typically including commission.

**Q: Can employment advisers migrate to Ireland with a work visa?**
A: Yes, through a general employment permit, but employer sponsorship is required and annual salary usually needs to be above €30k.
