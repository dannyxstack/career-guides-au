# Ship's Deck Crew and Related Workers（Ship's Deck Crew and Related Workers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8350 – Ship's Deck Crew and Related Workers。**

Operate deck equipment, mooring, and cargo handling in Irish ports and coastal routes, ensuring safe vessel operations.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪至少30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 跨国企业内部调派至爱尔兰，需有相关工作经历。 |
| Subclass Stamp 1G (Third Leve（Stamp 1G (Third Level Graduate)） | 毕业生可留在爱尔兰寻找工作，需相关学历。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for ships' deck crews in Ireland?**
答：Junior deckhands earn around €28,000-€35,000 per year, while senior or bosun roles can reach €45,000-€60,000.

**问：How can foreigners migrate to Ireland for this occupation?**
答：Employer sponsorship for a General Employment Permit is required, with a salary of at least €30,000 and evidence that the role cannot be filled by an EU citizen.

**问：What certifications are required to work?**
答：STCW Basic Safety Training is mandatory; Deck Watchkeeping and Proficiency in Survival Craft certificates are recommended.
