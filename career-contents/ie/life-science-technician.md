# Life Science Technician（Life Science Technician）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3141 – Life Science Technician。**

Life Science Technicians assist scientists in biological, chemical, and medical research. They prepare samples, operate laboratory equipment, record data, and maintain lab supplies. Common in pharma, biotech, food testing, and environmental monitoring.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺职业，年薪需达€32,000以上（本科）或€64,000以上（无学历）。允许快速办理家庭团聚。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪€30,000以上的职位，需劳动市场测试。配额制，非欧盟申请人需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技术人才，年薪需达€61,000（普通）或€38,000（短缺职业）。方便在欧盟内流动。 |

**PR难度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Life Science Technician？**
- 细心、有条理，喜欢实验室工作环境
- 有生命科学、化学或相关背景，愿意从基础技术工作做起
- 对制药、生物技术或食品检测行业有长期兴趣

**谁不适合学Life Science Technician？**
- 不习惯重复性实验操作或严格遵守标准流程
- 希望快速晋升或高收入，而非长期从事技术岗位

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for life science technicians in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €28,000 to €35,000 per year. Experienced technicians can earn €45,000 to €60,000. Pharma and biotech firms often pay more with bonuses.

**问：How can overseas workers migrate as life science technicians in Ireland?**
答：Typically requires a work permit. If the occupation is on the Critical Skills list, apply for a Critical Skills Employment Permit (salary €32,000+). Otherwise General Employment Permit with labor market test.

**问：What education is needed to become a life science technician in Ireland?**
答：A bachelor's degree in a relevant field (life science, biochemistry) is typical. Some roles accept a higher diploma. GMP training may be required by employers.
