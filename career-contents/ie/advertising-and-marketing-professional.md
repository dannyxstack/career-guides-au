# Advertising and Marketing Professionals — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2431 (ISCO08)**

In Ireland, advertising and marketing professionals are responsible for developing and executing marketing strategies, managing brand communications, and conducting market research and analysis. This occupation covers digital marketing, advertising planning, brand management, and is a key role in business growth.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career paths typically start as a marketing assistant or coordinator, progressing to marketing manager, brand director, or digital marketing director. Those with skills in data analysis, SEO/SEM, and content marketing are more likely to secure senior positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Write standard product descriptions and ad copy (AI-generated draft)
- Execute programmatic ad placement (AI auto-optimizes bids and audiences)
- Generate basic market research reports (AI crawls and aggregates data)
- Manage social media calendar scheduling (AI-automated posting).
- Handling customer data cleaning and preliminary analysis (AI automates processing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- In-depth consumer behavior analysis using AI insights tools
- Rapid iteration of ad creatives using AI creative generation tools
- Optimize marketing budget allocation using AI predictive models
- Use chatbots to automate customer segmentation and outreach
- Using AI to monitor competitor activity in real-time and automatically generate response strategies

**Human moat:**

- Cross-platform creative integration skills based on brand strategy
- Intuition and innovative solutions for ambiguous business problems
- Ability to build emotional trust and long-term relationships with customers.
- Ability to make decisions within complex ethical and legal frameworks
- Ability to lead cross-functional teams and coordinate multiple stakeholders

**Skills to build:**

- Prompt Engineering and AI content moderation
- Marketing analytics tools and AI predictive modeling (e.g., Python, R)
- User behavioral psychology and behavioral economics
- Data visualization and storytelling (e.g., Tableau, Power BI)
- Automation marketing platform maintenance (e.g., HubSpot, Marketo)
- AI ethics and data compliance knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Marketing
- Data Analytics
- Content Strategy
- Brand Management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for advertising and marketing professionals in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approximately €30,000-€45,000, mid-level around €45,000-€65,000, senior up to €90,000+, depending on experience, industry, and company size.

**Q: How can overseas advertising and marketing professionals immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Can apply through a Critical Skills Employment Permit (annual salary €38,000+ and in a shortage occupation) or a General Employment Permit (annual salary €30,000+). Digital marketing and data analysis skills are in high demand, which is beneficial for applications.

**Q: What key skills are needed for this occupation?**
A: Requires skills in digital marketing, SEO/SEM, content creation, data analysis, social media management, communication, and project management. Continuous learning of new technologies and trends is essential.
