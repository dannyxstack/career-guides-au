# Bicycle and Related Repairers — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Skilled worker responsible for repairing and maintaining non-motorised vehicles such as bicycles and wheelchairs and related equipment.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can progress from apprentice to senior technician, or open an independent repair shop; also transition to sports equipment or electric vehicle repair.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- basic fault diagnosis (e.g., using sensors and AI to automatically identify tire pressure, chain wear)
- Inventory management and parts procurement (AI forecasts demand and auto-orders)
- Customer appointments and follow-ups (handled by AI chatbot)
- Standardized maintenance documentation (AI-generated reports)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex fault diagnosis (AI-assisted analysis of abnormal sounds and vibration data to improve accuracy)
- Optimization of parts inventory (AI predicts seasonal demand, reducing stocking costs)
- Customer communication and education (AI generates personalized maintenance recommendations to improve customer satisfaction)
- Maintenance process training (AR/AI guides new hires, shortening learning cycle)

**Human moat:**

- Manual fine-tuning (e.g., spoke tension, brake micro-adjustments) relies on touch and experience
- Non-standard repairs (e.g., classic cars, custom vehicles) require creativity and problem-solving skills
- Client trust and relationship maintenance (face-to-face communication, on-site service)
- Safety-critical judgments (e.g., frame crack detection, comprehensive brake system inspection)

**Skills to build:**

- Repair of electric/smart bicycle electronic systems
- 3D printing part design and manufacturing
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Digital Marketing
- Basic data analysis (using AI tools to optimize inventory)
- Social media management (showcasing repair cases, attracting customers)
- Sustainability knowledge (repair and upgrade services, extending product lifespan).

**Key growth areas:**

- Green transport
- Cycling infrastructure
- E-bike maintenance
- Urban mobility

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a bicycle repairer?**
A: Entry-level about €22,000-28,000, senior up to €45,000.

**Q: Is it easy for non-EU citizens to obtain a work visa?**
A: It is difficult because this occupation is not in shortage; employer sponsorship and a labor market test are required.

**Q: Does this occupation have promotion opportunities?**
A: You can advance to senior technician or start your own business, or expand into electric vehicle repair.
