# Administrative and Executive Secretaries — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3343 (ISCO08)**

Executive and administrative secretaries provide administrative support to senior management, including calendar management, meeting scheduling, document processing, and communication coordination.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can progress from junior administrative assistant to senior executive secretary or office manager, and also transition to specialist fields such as project management and human resources.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Arrange and coordinate meetings and schedules (AI scheduling assistant can auto-optimize)
- Document archiving, formatting, and basic data processing (AI automation tools)
- Routine email replies and meeting minutes generation (AI natural language processing)
- Travel booking and expense reimbursement reconciliation (AI process automation)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze data and provide decision-support reports for executives.
- Use AI translation and cross-cultural communication tools to enhance international collaboration efficiency.
- Use AI project management tools to monitor task progress and resource allocation
- Industry trend and competitor analysis via AI-driven insight platforms
- Creating and optimizing presentations and documents using AI automation tools.

**Human moat:**

- High-level strategic communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Handling of confidential information and judgment-based decision-making
- Cross-departmental conflict mediation and emotional support
- Handling unstructured and sudden problems
- Innovative event planning and executive thinking partner

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in AI office tools (such as Copilot, Notion AI)
- Basic data analysis and visualization (Excel/Tableau)
- Project management methodologies (PMP/Agile)
- Effective communication and influencing skills
- Deployment and customization of enterprise-level AI tools
- Digital Transformation Project Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Administrative Support
- Digital Skills
- Office Management
- Executive Assistance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for administrative secretaries in Ireland?**
A: The annual salary for entry-level administrative secretaries is about 25,000-32,000 euros, while senior executive secretaries can reach 42,000-55,000 euros.

**Q: Can overseas individuals immigrate to Ireland through administrative secretary work?**
A: Administrative secretaries are not on the Critical Skills Occupation List; need a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship and meeting salary threshold; immigration is challenging.
