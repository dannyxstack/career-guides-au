# Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer（Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9213 – Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer。**

Performs manual tasks on mixed farms combining crop cultivation and livestock rearing, such as planting, harvesting, feeding animals, and cleaning enclosures; often seasonal.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪门槛€30,000（2023年），但此岗位通常低于门槛，难以获批。 |
| Subclass Seasonal Employment （Seasonal Employment Permit） | 针对临时季节性农业工作，有效期最长9个月，需雇主担保，无年薪门槛。 |
| Subclass EU/EEA Freedom of Mo（EU/EEA Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟/欧洲经济区公民可自由工作，无需签证，是主要劳动力来源。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★½）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer？**
- 喜欢户外体力劳动、不介意季节性失业的人
- 寻求临时或短期工作以积累经验的年轻人
- 欧盟公民，希望利用自由流动权利进入爱尔兰农业

**谁不适合学Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer？**
- 追求稳定高收入和职业晋升的人
- 不喜欢体力劳动或不适应爱尔兰多雨天气的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €22,000-€28,000, with hourly rates typically €11-€14. Experienced workers may earn over €30,000, but still below national average.

**问：Can non-EU nationals immigrate to Ireland through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. This job is not on the critical skills list and salary often below the €30,000 General Employment Permit threshold. Seasonal Permit is temporary (max 9 months) and does not lead to residency.

**问：Does this occupation have future prospects?**
答：Limited. Mechanisation reduces demand for unskilled labour, but organic farming and local food movements may create niche opportunities. Advancement requires additional training.
