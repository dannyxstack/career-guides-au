# Insurance Representatives — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3321 (ISCO08)**

Insurance representatives are responsible for selling various insurance products to clients, such as life, health, and property insurance, and assisting with claims.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Can progress from sales representative to team leader or regional manager, or transition to professional roles such as insurance underwriting and claims.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate policy quotes and clause explanations
- standardized paperwork (form filling, data processing)
- Handle common customer inquiries via chatbot
- Automated underwriting and updating customer information.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze customer data and recommend personalized insurance plans
- Optimizes customer follow-up strategies through AI-driven CRM systems
- Use natural language processing to generate sales scripts and emails
- Use predictive analytics to identify high-potential customers

**Human moat:**

- Build deep trust relationships and handle complex claims
- Emotional support and crisis intervention based on human empathy
- Customised financial planning and estate design
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration and long-term client relationship management

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and customer relationship management (CRM) tools
- Operation of AI-assisted sales and marketing platforms
- Digital communication and social media marketing
- In-depth knowledge of finance and insurance products
- Ethical compliance and data privacy management
- Negotiation and complex problem solving

**Key growth areas:**

- Insurance
- Financial Services
- Customer Service
- Sales

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the income structure for insurance representatives?**
A: Typically composed of a base salary plus sales commissions and performance bonuses, with senior roles also including team commissions.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens become insurance representatives in Ireland?**
A: Must obtain a work permit, usually through a General Employment Permit, with an annual salary of at least €34,000, and the employer must prove they cannot find local workers.
