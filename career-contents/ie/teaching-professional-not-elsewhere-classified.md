## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from lecturer to senior lecturer, professor, or educational management roles, or transition to education technology or policy fields.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Marking Standardized Assignments and Tests
- Create and manage course materials and lesson plans
- Answer common student questions (FAQ)
- Record and analyze student attendance, grades, and other administrative data
- Conduct basic language or skills training (e.g., language practice, programming basics).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze students' personalized learning data and develop precise teaching plans
- Use GPT to generate case discussions and simulation scenarios to enhance classroom interaction
- Use AI tools to quickly retrieve the latest educational research and optimize curriculum design
- Automatic translation and multilingual content adaptation, serving international students
- Warn of teaching issues through AI learning analytics for early intervention.

**Human moat:**

- Emotional support and psychological counseling in complex situations
- Interdisciplinary curriculum integration and innovative teaching design
- High-level communication and negotiation with stakeholders (students, industry, institutions)
- Ethical judgment and guidance on educational values
- Original knowledge creation and critical thinking cultivation

**Skills to build:**

- AI education tool deployment and data analysis (e.g., LMS AI plugins)
- Human-machine collaboration strategies in instructional design
- EdTech product evaluation and customized application
- Data privacy and AI ethics in education practice
- Interdisciplinary project-based learning design
- Facilitation and interaction techniques for online and blended teaching

**Key growth areas:**

- Higher Education
- Adult Education
- E-learning
- International Students

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for teaching professionals in Ireland?**
A: Lecturer annual salary approx. €35,000-45,000, senior lecturer or professor can reach €60,000-90,000.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland through teaching jobs?**
A: Typically, a work permit is required, such as a General Work Permit (requiring labour market testing) or an EU Blue Card (meeting salary thresholds).

**Q: What are the career development pathways for teaching professionals?**
A: Can progress from lecturer to senior lecturer, professor, or transition into education management, policy formulation, and other areas.
