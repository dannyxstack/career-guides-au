# Metal Polisher（Metal Polisher）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7224 – Metal Polisher。**

Operates polishing, grinding, and sharpening machines to finish metal surfaces, commonly used in manufacturing and repair.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for metal polishers?**
答：Entry-level: €25,000-30,000; Mid-level: €30,000-38,000; Senior: €38,000-45,000.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Ireland for this occupation?**
答：Difficult, as it's not a shortage occupation; requires a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically high school diploma plus a 3-year apprenticeship, or equivalent experience.
