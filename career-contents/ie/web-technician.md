# Web Technician（Web Technician）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3514 – Web Technician。**

In Ireland, web technicians handle website maintenance, server management, and technical support for various businesses. The role is stable due to ongoing digital transformation.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪不低于34,000欧元的工作，需获得工作邀请。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪超过32,000欧元且职业在紧缺清单上的岗位，Web Technician通常不在清单上，但若有相关经验可尝试。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Web Technician？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for web technicians in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €28,000 to €35,000, mid-level €35,000 to €45,000, and senior roles can exceed €60,000 annually, depending on experience and certifications.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation in Ireland?**
答：Typically, you need a job offer and apply for a General Employment Permit with a salary threshold of €34,000. The Critical Skills Permit is for higher-skilled roles and rarely applies to web technicians.
