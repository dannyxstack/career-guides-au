# Web Technician — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3514 (ISCO08)**

In Ireland, network technicians are responsible for website maintenance, server management, and technical support, serving various enterprises. This occupation has stable demand, benefiting from digital transformation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can advance from junior technical support to senior network administrator or IT project manager, or move into specialized fields like cybersecurity or cloud computing through certification.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine server monitoring and alert handling (AI auto-detects and fixes common faults)
- Basic website backup and recovery (via automated scripts)
- Common customer support queries (handled by AI chatbots)
- Basic network configuration and troubleshooting (AI-assisted diagnosis with solutions)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex fault diagnosis (AI log analysis for fast root cause identification)
- Performance optimization (AI predicts bottlenecks and suggests tuning).
- Security threat detection and response (AI analysis of abnormal traffic patterns)
- Document and knowledge base generation (AI-assisted writing and updates)
- Automation operation script writing (AI-assisted code generation and debugging)

**Human moat:**

- Cross-system integration and customized solution design
- High-pressure decision-making and manual recovery during emergency failures
- Collaborate with the development team to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions
- Safety auditing and compliance judgment (requires human interpretation of regulations)

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud platform management (AWS/Azure/GCP certifications)
- DevOps toolchain (Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD)
- AI/ML fundamentals and tool applications (e.g., Python, TensorFlow)
- Automation scripts and programming (Python, PowerShell)
- Cybersecurity Foundation
- Data analysis and visualization

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Transformation
- Cloud Services
- E-commerce
- Remote Work

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on technical problem-solving and are interested in networks and servers
- People who want to enter the IT industry but avoid intensive programming

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike communicating with users and providing technical support
- People seeking extremely high salaries or rapid promotion.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for network technicians in Ireland?**
A: Junior annual salary approximately 28,000-35,000 euros, intermediate 35,000-45,000 euros, senior up to 60,000 euros or more, depending on experience and certifications.

**Q: As a non-EU citizen, how can I immigrate to Ireland through this occupation?**
A: Typically requires a job offer and application for a General Employment Permit with an annual salary threshold of €34,000. Critical Skills Permit is for higher-skilled roles, but Web Technician usually does not qualify.
