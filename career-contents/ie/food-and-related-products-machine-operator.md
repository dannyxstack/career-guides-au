# Food and Related Products Machine Operator（Food and Related Products Machine Operator）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8160 – Food and Related Products Machine Operator。**

Operate food processing machinery (e.g., mixing, cutting, packaging) to ensure efficient, safe production in compliance with hygiene standards. This role is in steady demand within Ireland's food manufacturing sector.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于劳动力市场测试后雇主担保，年薪需高于€27,000（2024年标准），该职业通常满足要求。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需年薪高于€33,000（普通职业）或€43,000（紧缺职业），该职业通常不适用，因薪资偏低。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a food machine operator?**
答：Entry-level: €23,000-€28,000; mid-level: €28,000-€34,000; senior: €34,000-€40,000 (gross annual).

**问：Is this occupation easy for immigration to Ireland?**
答：Not easy. It does not require high education and has low pay, usually not meeting Critical Skills Permit criteria. General Employment Permit with labor market test is needed.

**问：What are the career development paths?**
答：Promotion to production line supervisor, quality controller, or maintenance technician, usually requiring additional training or certification.
