# Bookmaker, Croupier and Related Gaming Worker（Bookmaker, Croupier and Related Gaming Worker）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：4212 – Bookmaker, Croupier and Related Gaming Worker。**

Accept bets, operate gaming tables, and manage gaming activities in casinos and betting establishments, ensuring compliance with regulations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for bookmakers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level positions earn €22k-€28k annually, while senior supervisors can reach €35k-€45k, depending on experience and employer.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Ireland through this occupation?**
答：This occupation is not on the critical skills list; immigration is difficult. A General Employment Permit requires employer sponsorship, a salary around €34k, and a strict labor market test.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities in gaming?**
答：You can progress from table assistant to table supervisor, gaming manager, or move into online gaming operations and compliance roles.
