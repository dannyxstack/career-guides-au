# Legal Secretary（Legal Secretary）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3342 – Legal Secretary。**

Legal secretaries provide administrative support in law firms or legal departments, including document preparation, case management, client communication, and scheduling. They require familiarity with legal terminology and procedures, as well as excellent organizational and communication skills.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for legal secretaries in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level legal secretaries earn around €25,000-32,000 per year, mid-level €32,000-42,000, and senior roles up to €55,000. Salaries vary by firm size, location, and experience, with Dublin typically higher.

**问：Can a legal secretary apply for an Irish work visa?**
答：Yes, a General Employment Permit is possible if the salary exceeds €30,000 and a labor market test is passed. This occupation is not on the Critical Skills list, so a Critical Skills Permit is not applicable.

**问：What is the career progression for legal secretaries?**
答：Legal secretaries can advance to senior secretary or legal assistant, or pursue further education to become a solicitor or legal administrator. Specialization in corporate law or intellectual property is also possible.
