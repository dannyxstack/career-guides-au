# Legal Secretary — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3342 (ISCO08)**

A legal secretary provides administrative support in law firms or legal departments, including document preparation, case management, client communication, and scheduling. This role requires familiarity with legal terminology and procedures, as well as excellent organizational and communication skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior legal secretaries can advance to senior legal secretaries or legal assistants, and some may become lawyers or legal administrative managers through further study. After gaining experience, they can move into specialized areas such as corporate law or intellectual property law.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Draft standardized letters and meeting minutes (AI-generated templates)
- Schedule and manage calendars and reminders (smart scheduling tools)
- Answer and transfer routine calls (AI voice customer service)
- Maintain and retrieve paper/electronic files (document management system)
- Data entry and organization (RPA and OCR automation)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Responsible for complex or sensitive business communication (requiring empathy and strategy)
- Coordinating multiple conflicting agendas and making trade-offs (interpersonal negotiation)
- Training or supervising AI tool output quality (human-machine collaboration)
- Handling sudden administrative crises (adaptability)
- Manage confidential or high-level affairs (trust and confidentiality)

**Human moat:**

- Situations requiring empathy and nuanced communication
- Confidentiality and high-trust working relationships
- Unstructured problem-solving and handling unexpected situations
- Cross-department coordination and power dynamics assessment

**Skills to build:**

- AI office tool usage and process optimization
- Basic data analysis and report making
- Project coordination and stakeholder management
- Social media and digital content management
- Advanced communication and writing skills
- Ability to quickly learn new software

**Key growth areas:**

- Steady demand
- Legal sector growth
- Dublin concentration
- Administrative specialization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for legal secretaries in Ireland?**
A: Junior legal secretary annual salary around €25,000-32,000, intermediate around €32,000-42,000, senior up to €55,000. Salary varies by firm size, location, and experience. Dublin salaries are typically higher than other regions.

**Q: Can a legal secretary apply for an Irish work visa?**
A: Can apply for a General Employment Permit, requires an annual salary of over €30,000 and passing the labour market test. This occupation is not on the shortage list, so a Critical Skills Employment Permit is not available.

**Q: What is the career path for a legal secretary?**
A: Can advance from junior secretary to senior secretary or legal assistant, or become a lawyer (e.g., via conversion courses) or legal administrative manager. Experienced secretaries may specialize in corporate law, intellectual property, etc.
