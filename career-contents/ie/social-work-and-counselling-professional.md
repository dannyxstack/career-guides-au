# Social Work and Counselling Professional（Social Work and Counselling Professional）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2635 – Social Work and Counselling Professional。**

In Ireland, social work and counselling professionals provide psychosocial support, counseling, and interventions to help individuals, families, and communities cope with life challenges and promote well-being.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于短缺职业，要求本科以上学历且年薪≥32,000欧元，可带家属，2年后可转永居。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于普通就业，年薪≥30,000欧元，需劳动市场测试，5年后可转永居。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高素质非欧盟人才，要求≥5年经验且年薪≥33,453欧元（2024基准），可带家属，快通道永居。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for social workers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €35,000-45,000, mid-level €45,000-60,000, senior up to €60,000-80,000, depending on experience and employer.

**问：How can non-EU social workers migrate to Ireland?**
答：Via Critical Skills Employment Permit (shortage occupation, degree + salary ≥€32k) or General Employment Permit (salary ≥€30k + labour market test). CORU registration facilitates permits.

**问：What certification is required to practice in Ireland?**
答：Social workers must register with CORU; counsellors can voluntarily obtain IAHIP or NCS accreditation to enhance employability.
