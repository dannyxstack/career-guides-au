# Social Work and Counselling Professional — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2635 (ISCO08)**

In Ireland, social work and counseling professionals provide psychosocial support, counseling, and interventions to help individuals, families, and communities cope with life challenges and promote well-being.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a social worker in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level: approximately €35,000-45,000, mid-level: €45,000-60,000, senior: up to €60,000-80,000, depending on experience and employer.

**Q: How can non-EU social workers immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Can be obtained through a Critical Skills Employment Permit (shortage occupation, bachelor's degree + salary ≥ €32k) or a General Employment Permit (salary ≥ €30k + labour market test). Registration with CORU makes obtaining a permit easier.

**Q: What certifications are required to practice in Ireland?**
A: Social workers must register with CORU; psychologists can voluntarily enhance employment opportunities through IAHIP or NCS certification.
