# Supply, Distribution and Related Managers（Supply, Distribution and Related Managers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：1324 – Supply, Distribution and Related Managers。**

Plan, organize and manage supply chains, warehousing and distribution activities to ensure efficient movement of goods.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺职业，年薪超过32,000欧元且至少两年相关经验；可带家属，两年后可申请永居。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于一般就业，需劳动市场测试；年薪门槛约30,000欧元，五年后可申请永居。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能非欧盟公民，年薪门槛约54,000欧元；需高等教育学历，快速通道。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Supply, Distribution and Related Managers？**
- 具有物流、供应链或商业背景
- 善于组织协调和问题解决
- 适应快节奏工作环境

**谁不适合学Supply, Distribution and Related Managers？**
- 不擅长数据分析或流程优化
- 不喜欢应对突发状况

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for supply and distribution managers in Ireland?**
答：Mid-level managers earn around €50,000-€70,000, senior managers €75,000-€100,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Apply for a Critical Skills Employment Permit (shortage occupation) or General Employment Permit, requiring employer sponsorship.

**问：What are career progression opportunities?**
答：From warehouse supervisor to regional distribution manager, eventually to supply chain director or VP of operations.
