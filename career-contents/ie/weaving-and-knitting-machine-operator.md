# Weaving and Knitting Machine Operator（Weaving and Knitting Machine Operator）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8152 – Weaving and Knitting Machine Operator。**

Operate and monitor weaving or knitting machines to produce fabric and textiles. This role is part of Ireland's small textile manufacturing sector, with increasing automation.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪不低于30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能岗位，年薪门槛较高（通常>38,000欧元），本职业通常不满足。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for weaving machine operators?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €25,000-€30,000 per year, up to €40,000 for senior roles. Salary growth is limited.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Ireland for this role?**
答：Not easy. The role is not on the critical skills list; employers need a General Employment Permit, and salary thresholds may be challenging.

**问：What are the career prospects?**
答：Possible progression to supervisor or quality control, but the industry is small and automation may reduce positions.
