# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for reading water, electricity, and gas meter readings, as well as collecting cash and product data from vending machines. Usually works outdoors or in public areas, requiring walking and driving.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Limited career progression; can advance to senior meter reader or team supervisor, or move to other field service roles in energy companies. Vending machine collectors can advance to regional manager.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- On-site reading of water, electricity, and gas meter data and manual recording
- Collect cash from vending machines and count amounts
- Manually check vending machine stock and restock
- Drive a vehicle along a fixed route to inspect multiple meters or machines
- Manually fill in paper reports or enter data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimal route scheduling using handheld terminals or in-vehicle systems.
- Prioritizing abnormal meters or faulty machines via remote monitoring systems
- Data analysis tools help identify energy usage patterns or inventory predictions
- Mobile app real-time data upload reduces paperwork
- Integration with client/company systems for real-time reporting

**Human moat:**

- Handling complex on-site anomalies (e.g., mechanical failures, unreliable data)
- Face-to-face communication and dispute mediation with residents and businesses
- Flexible decision-making in remote or poorly-infrastructure areas.
- Scenarios that require manual confirmation due to legal or regulatory requirements
- On-site verification of high-value or confidential assets

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and reporting tools (Excel, Power BI)
- Operation of mobile devices and remote monitoring systems
- Basic GPS and route planning software
- Customer service and on-site communication skills
- Basic Equipment Maintenance and Troubleshooting
- Multitasking time management

**Key growth areas:**

- smart meter roll-out
- field service
- vending machine automation
- remote monitoring

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a meter reader?**
A: Junior meter reader annual salary about 25,000-30,000 euros, senior can reach 40,000 euros. Vending machine collector salary similar.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland through this occupation?**
A: This occupation usually does not qualify for a critical skills employment permit, only through a general employment permit, but the employer must demonstrate that no EU candidate can be found.

**Q: Are there promotion opportunities in this occupation?**
A: Limited promotion opportunities; can become a senior meter reader or transfer to customer service or maintenance roles in energy companies.
