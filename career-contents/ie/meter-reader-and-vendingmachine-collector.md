# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector（Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9623 – Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector。**

Responsible for reading utility meters and collecting cash and product data from vending machines. Typically works outdoors or in public areas, requiring walking and driving.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for meter readers?**
答：Entry-level meter readers earn around €25,000-30,000 per year, with senior roles up to €40,000. Vending machine collectors have similar pay.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Typically not eligible for Critical Skills Permit; only via General Employment Permit, requiring proof that no EU candidate is available.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities in this career?**
答：Limited; can advance to senior meter reader or transition to customer service or maintenance roles in utility companies.
