# Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified（Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：4229 – Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Client information workers n.e.c. include call center agents, receptionists, and information clerks, providing customer support, answering inquiries, and handling complaints. This occupation is in demand across various sectors in Ireland, particularly in finance, telecom, and public services.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for client information workers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €25,000 to €30,000, mid-level from €30,000 to €38,000, and senior positions can exceed €45,000. Language skills (e.g., Chinese) may attract additional allowances.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work permit for this occupation in Ireland?**
答：This occupation typically requires a General Employment Permit with a salary threshold of €34,000 and a labor market test. EU citizens can work freely.
