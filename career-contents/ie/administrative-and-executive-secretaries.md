# Administrative and Executive Secretaries（Administrative and Executive Secretaries）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3343 – Administrative and Executive Secretaries。**

Administrative and Executive Secretaries provide high-level administrative support to senior managers, managing schedules, coordinating meetings, handling correspondence, and facilitating communication.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪门槛约30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于管理或专业岗位，年薪需达到较高门槛（2024年约40,000欧元），行政秘书通常不符合。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Administrative and Executive Secretaries？**
- 细心且有组织能力，善于多任务处理
- 沟通能力强，能在压力下工作
- 熟悉办公软件和数字工具

**谁不适合学Administrative and Executive Secretaries？**
- 厌恶重复性事务性工作
- 不善于在快节奏环境中保持条理

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Administrative and Executive Secretaries in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level secretaries earn around €25,000-€32,000 per year, while senior executive secretaries can earn €42,000-€55,000.

**问：Can foreign nationals immigrate to Ireland as Administrative and Executive Secretaries?**
答：This occupation is not on the Critical Skills list; a General Employment Permit is required with employer sponsorship and a salary threshold, making immigration challenging.
