# Customs and Border Inspectors（Customs and Border Inspectors）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3351 – Customs and Border Inspectors。**

Customs and border inspectors examine people, goods, and conveyances at Irish entry points, enforcing customs, immigration, and agricultural regulations to ensure national security and tax compliance.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪通常不低于€30,000。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 仅适用于紧缺职业列表中的职业，海关检查员不在列表中。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需年薪≥€40,000及大学学历。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for customs inspectors?**
答：Entry-level: €30,000-40,000; mid-level: €40,000-55,000; senior: up to €70,000+, including allowances and bonuses.

**问：How can non-EU citizens apply for this role?**
答：Typically requires employer sponsorship via General Employment Permit with salary ≥€30,000. Critical Skills Permit not applicable as not on shortage list.

**问：What are the promotion opportunities for customs inspectors?**
答：Can advance to senior inspector, supervisor, or move into policy, training, or management roles such as customs manager.
