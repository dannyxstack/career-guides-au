# Freight Handler（Freight Handler）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9333 – Freight Handler。**

Load, unload, and sort freight in warehouses, freight terminals, or airports, ensuring efficient logistics operations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需年薪至少30,000欧元 |
| Subclass EU Freedom of Moveme（EU/EEA Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟/欧洲经济区公民可自由工作 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Freight Handler？**
- 体力充沛、能适应倒班工作者
- 寻求低门槛快速就业者
- 愿意从事重复性体力劳动者

**谁不适合学Freight Handler？**
- 对体力要求高、无法长时间站立者
- 期望高薪或晋升快者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for freight handlers?**
答：Entry-level around €25,000-€30,000, senior roles up to €35,000-€42,000 including overtime.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：This is not a shortage occupation; applicants need a General Employment Permit with a salary threshold of €30,000. Not ideal for long-term settlement.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities?**
答：Promotion to warehouse supervisor, logistics coordinator, or via training to forklift operator or transport manager.
