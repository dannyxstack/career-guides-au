# Mixed Crop Grower（Mixed Crop Grower）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：6114 – Mixed Crop Grower。**

Mixed crop growers manage the cultivation, care, and harvesting of a variety of crops such as grains, vegetables, and fruits. They plan crop rotation, manage soil fertility, control pests and diseases, and operate farm machinery.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Mixed Crop Grower？**
- 喜欢户外工作，对农业种植有热情的人
- 愿意学习现代农业技术和可持续农业方法的人
- 能够适应季节性劳动强度和体力要求的人

**谁不适合学Mixed Crop Grower？**
- 偏好城市工作和稳定规律作息的人
- 对体力劳动和气候变化敏感的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of mixed crop growers?**
答：Entry-level employees earn about €25,000-32,000 per year; experienced farm supervisors can earn up to €42,000; self-employed farmers have variable income depending on crops and market conditions.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to Ireland through agricultural work?**
答：Mixed crop growers are not on the Critical Skills list; they need a General Employment Permit with a salary of at least €30,000 and a labor market test. EU citizens can work freely.

**问：What education is needed to become a mixed crop grower?**
答：No formal degree is required, but Teagasc courses or a BSc in Agricultural Science can aid advancement. Pesticide certificate and tractor license are mandatory.
