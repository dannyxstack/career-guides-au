# Mixed Crop Grower — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 6114 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop farmers are responsible for managing the planting, care, and harvesting of various crops, including grains, vegetables, and fruits. They need to plan crop rotation, manage soil fertility, control pests and diseases, and operate agricultural machinery.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior growers can advance to farm supervisor or farm manager, or specialize in organic farming, precision agriculture, etc. Some pursue further study to become agricultural consultants or extension officers. Starting a family farm is also a common path.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Performing routine ploughing, sowing, and harvesting using tractors
- Manual or herbicide spraying for weed control
- Irrigate crops according to a fixed schedule
- Manual monitoring of crop growth and pest/disease signs
- Recording production output and inventory data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Precision planting and fertilization decisions based on multi-source data (soil sensors, satellite imagery)
- Using drones and AI vision systems for early pest and disease diagnosis
- Optimize crop rotation and variety selection through predictive models
- Automated production forecasting and market timing planning

**Human moat:**

- Handle unpredictable weather, pest outbreaks, and other emergencies
- Develop long-term crop rotation and soil health management strategies
- Complex communication with suppliers, clients, and regulatory bodies
- Trade-off Decisions for Comprehensive Ecological and Economic Benefits

**Skills to build:**

- Precision agriculture software and data analysis tools (e.g., Cropio, Climate FieldView)
- Drone operation and image analysis.
- Agricultural IoT device debugging and maintenance
- Programming and supervision of automated systems (autonomous tractors, robots)
- Sustainable farming practices and carbon footprint accounting
- Basic programming skills (Python/R for agricultural models)

**Key growth areas:**

- Organic farming
- Sustainable agriculture
- Precision farming
- Agri-tech innovation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the income of mixed crop growers?**
A: Entry-level employees earn approximately €25,000-32,000 per year, experienced farm supervisors up to €42,000, and self-employed farmers have volatile incomes depending on crops and markets.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Ireland through agricultural work?**
A: Mixed crop growers are not on the Critical Skills Occupation List and need to apply for a general work permit. The minimum annual salary is at least €30,000, and the employer must demonstrate that they cannot recruit from the EU. EU citizens can work freely.

**Q: What education background is needed to become a mixed crop grower?**
A: Typically no formal degree required, but Teagasc agricultural courses or related bachelor's degrees (e.g., Agricultural Science) help with advancement. Pesticide use certificates and tractor licenses are mandatory.
