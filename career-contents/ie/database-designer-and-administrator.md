## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于短缺职业，年薪超过32,000欧元，可快速申请永居 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非短缺职业，年薪至少30,000欧元，需劳动力市场测试 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，年薪需超过1.5倍平均工资（约42,000欧元） |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for database designers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €35,000-€50,000, mid-level €50,000-€75,000, senior €75,000-€100,000, depending on experience and certifications.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to Ireland as a database designer?**
答：Apply for a Critical Skills Employment Permit (salary ≥€32,000) or General Employment Permit (salary ≥€30,000). The former allows faster permanent residency.

**问：What are the career prospects?**
答：With digital transformation and data growth, demand is rising. Advancement to architect or data scientist roles is possible, with positive outlook.
