# Shoemaking and Related Machine Operators（Shoemaking and Related Machine Operators）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8156 – Shoemaking and Related Machine Operators。**

Operate shoemaking and related machinery to produce footwear, including cutting, stitching, forming, and assembling processes.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for shoemaking operators in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level operators earn around €22,000-€26,000 per year, while senior operators can earn up to €32,000-€38,000.

**问：Can this occupation qualify for an Irish work visa?**
答：Yes, via a General Employment Permit, but it is not on the Critical Skills list; employer sponsorship and salary thresholds apply.

**问：What career progression opportunities exist for shoemaking operators?**
答：Operators can advance to senior operator, team leader, or move into quality control or equipment maintenance roles.
