# Motor Vehicle Mechanic（Motor Vehicle Mechanic）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7231 – Motor Vehicle Mechanic。**

Diagnose, repair, and maintain mechanical and electronic systems of cars and light commercial vehicles, including engines, transmissions, brakes, and air conditioning.

## 2. 考证难度 / 从业资质

**考证难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 国家汽车维修资格证书（QQI Level 6） | QQI (Quality and Qualifications Ireland) | 必备 | 行业标准资质，需通过理论及实践考试 |
| 汽车维修技师认证（LTI） | LTI (Learning Training and Innovation) | 可选 | 额外专业认证，提升就业竞争力 |
| 电动车维修认证 | SIMI (Society of the Irish Motor Industry) | 可选 | 针对高压系统安全与维修，需求增长 |

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪30,000欧元以上的职位，需雇主担保，劳动力市场测试。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 若雇主提供年薪32,000欧元以上且职业在紧缺列表上，可申请，无需劳动力市场测试。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Motor Vehicle Mechanic？**
- 喜欢动手维修，对汽车机械与电子系统有浓厚兴趣
- 愿意接受持续技术培训（尤其是电动车领域）
- 注重细节，能够独立诊断复杂故障

**谁不适合学Motor Vehicle Mechanic？**
- 不喜欢体力劳动或长期站立工作
- 对快速变化的技术（如电动系统）缺乏学习意愿

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for motor vehicle mechanics in Ireland?**
答：Average around €35,000, entry-level about €26,000, senior mechanics can earn up to €55,000+ depending on experience and specialization.

**问：How can overseas mechanics migrate to Ireland?**
答：Via General Employment Permit (€30,000+ salary and labor market test) or Critical Skills Employment Permit (shortage occupation, €32,000+ salary, no labor market test).

**问：What training is needed to become a motor vehicle mechanic?**
答：Typically a QQI Level 6 certificate in motor mechanics or a 4-year apprenticeship; EV maintenance certification is increasingly important.
