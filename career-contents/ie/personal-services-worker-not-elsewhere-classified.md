# Personal Services Worker Not Elsewhere Classified（Personal Services Worker Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：5169 – Personal Services Worker Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Provides personal services not elsewhere classified, such as personal assistant, household manager, event companion, covering a wide range of tasks including daily assistance and personalized services.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪不低于30,000欧元的非紧缺职业，需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 跨国公司内部调派人员，需在公司工作满6个月 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：Salary ranges widely; entry-level around €22,000-€30,000, senior up to €55,000, heavily influenced by client budget and service scope.

**问：Can foreigners apply for a work visa for this occupation?**
答：Yes, via General Employment Permit with salary ≥€30,000 and employer proof that no local candidate is available.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities?**
答：Can advance to senior butler or personal service manager, or start own business via networking, but path is informal and depends on personal skills and connections.
