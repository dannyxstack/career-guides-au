# Health Care Assistant（Health Care Assistant）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：5321 – Health Care Assistant。**

Health Care Assistants support registered nurses and care staff in hospitals and care homes, providing basic patient care and assistance with daily activities.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a Health Care Assistant in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €22,000-€28,000, mid-level up to €33,000, and senior with allowances around €38,000.

**问：Can foreign Health Care Assistants immigrate to Ireland on a work visa?**
答：Yes, typically through a General Employment Permit or Critical Skills Employment Permit if the role is on the shortage list.

**问：Is there career progression for Health Care Assistants?**
答：Yes, they can advance to senior roles or become registered nurses through further study.
