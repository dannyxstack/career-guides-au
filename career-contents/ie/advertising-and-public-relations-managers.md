# Advertising and Public Relations Managers（Advertising and Public Relations Managers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：1222 – Advertising and Public Relations Managers。**

Plan and execute advertising and public relations campaigns, manage brand image, media relations, and marketing strategies to enhance organizational reputation and customer engagement.

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪超过3.2万欧元且职业在紧缺清单上的职位；广告和公关经理通常不符合，除非是技术营销角色。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪超过3万欧元的职位，需通过劳动力市场测试。适用于非紧缺职业。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年薪超过5.6万欧元的高技能职位，可快速获得居留。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for advertising and public relations managers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €35,000-45,000, mid-level €50,000-70,000, senior €80,000-120,000, depending on company size, industry, and experience.

**问：Which Irish work visa can advertising and public relations managers apply for?**
答：Typically the General Employment Permit, requiring a salary above €30,000 and a labor market test. The Critical Skills Permit is not applicable unless in a tech marketing role.

**问：What is the career progression for this profession?**
答：Start as account manager/PR executive, advance to senior manager, director, and eventually CMO or agency partner. Building client relationships and a track record of successful campaigns is key.
