# Electronics Mechanics and Servicers（Electronics Mechanics and Servicers）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7421 – Electronics Mechanics and Servicers。**

Install, maintain and repair electronic equipment and systems, including industrial controls, communications and consumer electronics. Involves fault diagnosis, component replacement and system testing.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪不低于30,000欧元的技术岗位，需获得工作offer并由雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年薪高于行业平均水平的毕业生，要求本科以上学历及工作合同。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 针对紧缺技能职业，通常年薪需达32,000欧元以上，该职业未被列为紧缺，但若满足条件可申请。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for electronics mechanics?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €25,000 to €35,000, mid-level €35,000 to €45,000, and senior up to €55,000.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Apply via General Employment Permit (salary ≥€30,000) or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship. Not a shortage occupation, so process is standard.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become an electronics mechanic?**
答：Common paths include a QQI advanced certificate or apprenticeship; some roles require RECI certification and first aid certificate.
