# Cartographer and Surveyor（Cartographer and Surveyor）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2165 – Cartographer and Surveyor。**

Cartographers and Surveyors measure and map the Earth's surface, creating maps, digital models, and supporting construction, infrastructure, and land management projects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary growth potential for cartographers and surveyors?**
答：With experience and certification, salaries can rise from €28k entry-level to over €70k senior, especially with skills in geodesy, GIS, and project management.

**问：How can overseas cartographers/surveyors migrate to Ireland?**
答：Via the Critical Skills Employment Permit (shortage occupation), requiring a job offer from an Irish employer, salary threshold (€32k+), and relevant degree.

**问：What are the main tasks of this occupation?**
答：Field surveying, data processing, map production, boundary determination, and using GIS/GPS to provide spatial data for construction, engineering, and environmental projects.
