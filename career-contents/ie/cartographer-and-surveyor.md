# Cartographer and Surveyor — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2165 (ISCO08)**

Draftspersons and surveyors measure and map earth surface features, create maps and digital models, and support construction, infrastructure, and land management projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior surveyors/cartographers can advance to project leaders, team supervisors, or move into roles such as GIS analyst, remote sensing specialist, or geographic information manager by gaining experience and obtaining professional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Conduct routine field survey data collection using total stations and GPS
- Manual drawing of topographic maps, contour lines, and other basic cartography work
- Repetitive data processing such as data entry and format conversion
- Simple spatial analysis (e.g., buffer, overlay analysis).
- Updating and maintaining traditional paper maps

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI for automatic recognition and classification of remote sensing images (e.g., feature extraction, change detection)
- Predicting terrain changes and land use trends using machine learning models
- Integrates multi-source data such as drones and LiDAR for 3D modeling
- Use GIS and AI algorithms to optimize surveying paths and resource allocation
- Use natural language processing to automatically generate survey reports and map descriptions

**Human moat:**

- Professional judgment and decision-making (e.g., measurement specification selection, boundary dispute adjudication)
- On-site measurement and emergency response skills in complex environments
- Communicate with clients and engineers to understand project requirements and coordinate progress
- Deep understanding of surveying laws, regulations, privacy, and ethics
- Interdisciplinary knowledge integration (e.g., geology, civil, environmental)

**Skills to build:**

- Remote sensing image analysis and deep learning (e.g., using PyTorch/TensorFlow for land cover classification)
- UAV surveying operation and data processing (e.g., DJI Pilot, Pix4D)
- 3D laser scanning and point cloud processing (e.g., Leica Cyclone, CloudCompare)
- Advanced geographic information system applications (e.g., ArcGIS Pro, QGIS plugin development)
- Programming skills (Python, R, or JavaScript for WebGIS)
- Cloud computing and big data processing (e.g., AWS, Google Earth Engine)

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- GIS
- Renewable Energy
- Smart Cities

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary growth potential for cartographers and surveyors?**
A: With experience and qualifications, salary can increase from €28k at junior level to over €70k at senior level, especially with skills in surveying, GIS, and project management.

**Q: How can overseas draftspersons/surveyors immigrate to Ireland?**
A: You can apply via the Critical Skills Employment Permit (for in-demand occupations); you need a job offer from an Irish employer, meet the salary threshold (€32k+), and hold a relevant degree.

**Q: What are the main tasks of this occupation?**
A: Includes site measurement, data processing, mapping, boundary determination, and spatial data support for construction, engineering, and environmental projects using GIS and GPS technology.
