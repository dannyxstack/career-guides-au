# Beauticians and Related Workers — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 5142 (ISCO08)**

Beauticians provide facial treatments, makeup, manicures, and other services, mainly in beauty salons, spas, or as self-employed.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can progress from junior beautician to senior technician, store manager, or self-employed business owner, with some transitioning to cosmetics retail or training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate facial care plans using AI skin analyzer
- Use chatbots for customer appointments, follow-ups, and product recommendations
- Using AI design software to automatically generate nail art patterns
- Using virtual try-on mirrors to preview makeup, reducing manual color testing
- Automated inventory management and consumables replenishment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Leverage AI skin diagnostic tools to obtain deep data, improving diagnostic accuracy
- Use AI recommendation systems to personalize skincare products and treatments
- Using VR/AR technology to show customers post-surgery results, enhancing sales persuasion.
- Using data analysis tools to track customer preferences and optimize service processes
- Use AI to generate teaching videos or live streaming content to expand personal brand influence

**Human moat:**

- In-depth skin problem diagnosis and personalized solution design
- Emotional companionship and customer trust building.
- Manual skills (e.g., complex makeup, massage techniques)
- Crisis management (e.g., immediate response to allergic reactions)
- Cross-Cultural Aesthetic Communication and Customized Advice

**Skills to build:**

- Operating AI skin analyzers and diagnostic software
- New media marketing and personal brand management
- Data-driven customer relationship management
- Advanced makeup techniques (such as special effects makeup for film and TV).
- Basic health and nutrition knowledge (holistic beauty)
- Multilingual service capability (international clientele)

**Key growth areas:**

- Beauty Therapy
- Wellness Tourism
- Self-Employment
- Spa Services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a beautician in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level: around €22,000-28,000; mid-level: €28,000-35,000; senior: up to €45,000; self-employment income varies widely.

**Q: Can beauticians immigrate to Ireland through a work visa?**
A: Beauticians are not in shortage occupations and usually require a General Employment Permit (GEP), with employer sponsorship and a minimum salary threshold.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a beautician in Ireland?**
A: Typically requires completing a QQI-recognised 1-2 year certificate or diploma course; ITEC or CIDESCO certificates, while not mandatory, can aid employment.
