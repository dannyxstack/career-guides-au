# Librarian — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 2622 (ISCO08)**

Librarians manage and organize library resources, assist users in finding information, and promote reading and digital literacy.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Can advance from assistant to senior librarian or department head, further to library director or information management specialist.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- In book cataloging and classification, AI can automatically identify metadata and generate classification numbers
- Common reference questions can be handled by chatbots or AI retrieval systems.
- Book borrowing, returning, inventory, and shelving can be done by RFID and automated sorting systems
- Routine communications such as overdue notices and appointment reminders can be replaced by automated email systems.
- In electronic resource ordering and renewal, AI can analyze usage data and make automatic recommendations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-enhanced search tools help users quickly locate complex information (e.g., cross-database searches).
- Data analysis tools help libraries optimize collection strategies, automatically purchasing based on borrowing trends
- AI-powered natural language processing helps librarians interpret users' deep needs and provide personalized recommendations
- AI translation and summarization tools help librarians process multilingual resources and expand service scope
- With AI-driven predictive models, librarians can plan activities and resource allocation in advance

**Human moat:**

- User inquiries requiring high-level judgment (e.g., in-depth academic research discussions).
- Interpersonal communication and training skills in community outreach and digital literacy education
- Curating local historical and cultural resources and maintaining special collections (requires expert knowledge)
- Copyright and license negotiation, information ethics and privacy protection decisions
- Cross-departmental collaboration and library strategic planning (requires comprehensive management and leadership skills)

**Skills to build:**

- Data literacy: proficiency in SQL and data analysis tools, ability to use AI for resource optimisation
- Digital content management and curation: proficient in using content management systems and metadata standards.
- Application of AI tools: learn to use library-specific AI search and analysis platforms
- Project management and change leadership: driving digital transformation in libraries
- User experience design: understanding user behavior, designing intelligent service workflows
- Information security and privacy protection: ensuring compliant use of AI systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital literacy
- Public libraries
- Academic libraries
- Information management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a librarian in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level salary approx. €28,000-35,000, mid-level €35,000-45,000, senior up to €60,000.

**Q: How can an overseas librarian immigrate to Ireland?**
A: Must obtain employer sponsorship, apply for a General Employment Permit, and meet the salary threshold.
