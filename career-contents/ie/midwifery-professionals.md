# Midwifery Professionals（Midwifery Professionals）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2222 – Midwifery Professionals。**

Midwifery professionals provide prenatal, childbirth, and postnatal care, including newborn care and health counseling.

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Does midwifery salary include overtime?**
答：Public sector pay is hourly; overtime and shift allowances are extra.

**问：How can overseas midwives register in Ireland?**
答：Requires NMBI qualification recognition, possibly language tests and adaptation courses.
