# Translator（Translator）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2643 – Translator。**

Translators and interpreters convert written or spoken material from one language to another, ensuring accurate communication of meaning.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需获得工作offer，年薪一般不低于€30,000。 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 若从事高技能翻译（如法律、技术），年薪€32,000以上可申请，转永居更快。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 要求高等教育学历及年薪≥€43,759（2024年），适用于高技能职位。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Translator？**
- 精通多语言且对文化敏感的人
- 具备良好文字功底和细节关注能力
- 能应对压力、独立工作并守时

**谁不适合学Translator？**
- 不擅长精确表达或厌恶反复校对
- 不喜欢孤独工作或频繁切换任务

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for translators in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level: €25,000-€35,000, mid-level: €35,000-€50,000, senior up to €70,000. Freelancers have variable income.

**问：How can translators apply for a work visa in Ireland?**
答：Via General Employment Permit (job offer required), Critical Skills Employment Permit (if salary ≥€32,000 and high-skilled), or EU Blue Card.

**问：What is the career progression for translators?**
答：Start as junior/freelancer, then advance to senior translator, terminology specialist, project manager, or specialize in legal/medical fields.
