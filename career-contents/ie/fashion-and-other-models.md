# Fashion and other models（Fashion and other models）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：5241 – Fashion and other models。**

Fashion models in Ireland primarily work for retail, e-commerce, and fashion brands, showcasing clothing and accessories. The small market means full-time roles are rare, with most work being freelance or part-time.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于模特与经纪公司签订全职合同，但需满足薪资门槛（年薪至少30,000欧元）。由于模特收入不稳定，申请较难。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 不适用于模特，因模特不具备高等教育学历或技能短缺。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for fashion models?**
答：Entry-level models earn around €15,000-€25,000 per year, mid-level €25,000-€40,000, and top models can earn over €70,000, though most have irregular income.

**问：Can foreign models immigrate to Ireland?**
答：It is difficult. Modeling is not on the shortage list; a General Employment Permit requires a full-time contract with salary over €30,000. EU citizens can work freely, but non-EU opportunities are scarce.
