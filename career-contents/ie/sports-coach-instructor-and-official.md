# Sports Coach, Instructor or Official — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 3422 (ISCO08)**

Sports coaches, instructors, and referees in Ireland are responsible for training athletes, organizing training sessions, and officiating matches. They work in schools, clubs, and sports organizations to promote mass participation and competitive sports.

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Start as an assistant coach or referee, gain experience, and advance to senior coach, director, or head referee. Some move into sports management or education, or open personal training studios. Career progression depends on qualifications and networking.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a sports coach in Ireland?**
A: Annual salary for junior coaches is approximately €25,000-€35,000, and for senior coaches can reach €50,000-€70,000. Part-time positions are common, and income varies greatly.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens migrate to Ireland through sports coaching work?**
A: Requires employer sponsorship to apply for a General Employment Permit. Annual salary usually needs to be above €30,000. Since this occupation is not on the shortage list, immigration is somewhat difficult, but holders of EU spouse visas can work freely.

**Q: What certifications do sports coaches need?**
A: Must obtain a coaching certification recognized by Sport Ireland and hold a first aid certificate. Specific sports also require referee or coaching qualifications from the relevant federation. A university degree can enhance competitiveness.
