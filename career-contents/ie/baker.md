# Baker（Baker）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7512 – Baker。**

Responsible for producing bread, pastries and confectionery products, typically in bakeries, supermarkets or food production facilities. Requires knowledge of dough fermentation, baking techniques and food safety.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|

| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 不适用于烘焙师，因该职业不在紧缺清单中 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for bakers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level bakers earn around 22,000-28,000 EUR per year, mid-level 28,000-35,000 EUR, and senior supervisors 35,000-45,000 EUR.

**问：How can overseas bakers immigrate to Ireland?**
答：They typically need a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship, minimum salary of 30,000 EUR, and a labor market test. This occupation is not on the critical skills list.

**问：What are the career prospects for bakers in Ireland?**
答：Yes, advancement to head baker, instructor, or opening a bakery is possible. Demand for artisan and health-conscious products is growing.
