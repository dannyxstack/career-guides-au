# Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operator（Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operator）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：8112 – Mineral and Stone Processing Plant Operator。**

Operate mineral and stone processing equipment such as crushers, mills, and screens to convert raw materials into finished products for industrial or construction use.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪门槛约30,000欧元。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能职位，年薪需高于爱尔兰平均工资的1.5倍，通常不适用于操作员岗位。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €28,000 to €35,000, while senior operators can earn up to €55,000, but overall below national average.

**问：How difficult is migration for this occupation?**
答：Non-shortage occupation, migration is difficult. Requires a General Employment Permit with a salary threshold around €30,000, and must prove the employer cannot find a suitable candidate locally.

**问：What are the career development paths?**
答：Can progress to team leader or supervisor, or transition into quality control, equipment maintenance, or production management roles.
