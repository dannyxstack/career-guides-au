# Ship's Deck Crew and Related Workers — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 8350 (ISCO08)**

Operate deck equipment, mooring, and cargo handling in Irish ports and coastal routes to ensure safe vessel operation.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Advance from ordinary seaman to senior seaman or boatswain requires accumulating sea service and obtaining advanced certificates; some can transition to shore-based management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual recording of deck logs and report generation (AI automatic recording and analysis)
- Basic navigation calculations and route planning (AI-assisted systems take over)
- Cargo Stowage Calculation (AI optimisation algorithm replacing manual calculation)
- Routine checklist inspection of ship equipment (AI visual recognition for automatic checks)
- Communication coordination and document management (AI voice assistants and automated forms)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI provides real-time tension and safety warnings during mooring operations, improving efficiency.
- AI optimizes lifting paths during loading/unloading to reduce collision risks
- Deck maintenance planning with AI predictive analysis to reduce downtime
- Emergency scenario simulation training: AI generates realistic scenes to enhance response capabilities.
- Real-time multilingual communication AI translation earphones for international port communication

**Human moat:**

- Judgment in deck operations under complex and severe weather
- Rapid emergency response and team collaboration during emergencies
- Sensory experience inspection of ship structure and equipment wear
- Interpersonal communication and coordination with port workers, pilots, etc.
- Flexibly adjust mooring and loading/unloading plans in variable environments

**Skills to build:**

- Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS) operation
- Basic programming and data analysis (Python/R for log analysis).
- Use of AI collaboration tools (such as intelligent navigation assistance systems)
- Drone and robot deck inspection equipment operation
- Cybersecurity awareness (preventing shipboard system attacks)
- Sustainable shipping practices (knowledge of low-carbon operations)

**Key growth areas:**

- Maritime trade
- Offshore wind
- Coastal shipping
- Port expansion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of deck crew on Irish ships?**
A: Junior sailor annual salary approx. €28,000-35,000; senior sailor or boatswain can reach €45,000-60,000.

**Q: How can foreigners immigrate to Ireland for this occupation?**
A: Requires employer-sponsored General Employment Permit, annual salary no less than 30,000 euros, and proof that the position cannot be filled by EU citizens.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work?**
A: STCW Basic Safety Training certificate is required; it is recommended to obtain Deck Watchkeeping Certificate and Proficiency in Survival Craft certificate.
