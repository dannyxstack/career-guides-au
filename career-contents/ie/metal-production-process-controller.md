# Metal Production Process Controller（Metal Production Process Controller）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3135 – Metal Production Process Controller。**

In Ireland, Metal Production Process Controllers monitor and optimize metal production processes such as steelmaking, ensuring efficient and safe operations. The role is vital in manufacturing, requiring automation control and metallurgical knowledge.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IE独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于多数职业，需年薪≥30,000欧元 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺技能岗位，年薪≥32,000欧元，但该职业不在紧缺列表 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能岗位，需年薪≥46,795欧元（2024年标准） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Metal Production Process Controllers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level: €28,000-€36,000; mid-level: €38,000-€50,000; senior: €50,000-€65,000, depending on experience and employer.

**问：Is it easy to obtain a work permit for this occupation in Ireland?**
答：This role is usually not on the Critical Skills list, so a General Employment Permit is needed with a salary threshold of €30,000. Competition is moderate.
