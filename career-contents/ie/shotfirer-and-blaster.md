# Shotfirer and Blaster（Shotfirer and Blaster）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：7542 – Shotfirer and Blaster。**

Responsible for safely loading, connecting, and detonating explosives in mining, quarrying, and construction sites.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪需超过3万欧元 |
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 若爆破工被列为紧缺职业，可申请此签证，年薪需超过6.4万欧元（或特定职业3.2万欧元） |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟工人，年薪需超过欧盟蓝卡门槛（约6.4万欧元），需相关学历或经验 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for shotfirers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €35,000-45,000, mid-level €45,000-60,000, senior up to €80,000 including allowances and overtime.

**问：How can non-EU citizens apply for shotfirer positions in Ireland?**
答：Typically need employer sponsorship for a General Employment Permit; if listed as a shortage occupation, Critical Skills Employment Permit is easier for permanent residency.

**问：What are the career paths for shotfirers?**
答：Can advance to blasting supervisor, blasting engineer, or safety consultant, or move into mining management or training roles.
