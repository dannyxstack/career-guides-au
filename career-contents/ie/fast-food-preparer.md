# Fast Food Preparer（Fast Food Preparer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：9411 – Fast Food Preparer。**

Fast food preparers prepare and cook food in fast-food restaurants, including grilling, frying, and assembling orders, ensuring quick service and food quality.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IE技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于非紧缺职业，需雇主担保，年薪不低于€30,000。 |
| Subclass Intra-Company Transf（Intra-Company Transfer Permit） | 跨国公司内部调动，适用于短期工作。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for fast food preparers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €21,000-€25,000, mid-level up to €30,000, and senior supervisors around €35,000.

**问：Can overseas workers obtain a work visa for this occupation in Ireland?**
答：It is unlikely, as this is not a shortage occupation. Employer sponsorship and a salary threshold of €30,000 apply, but entry-level roles are often below this.
