# Fast Food Preparer — career analysis · Ireland

**Occupation code: 9411 (ISCO08)**

Fast food preparation workers are responsible for preparing and cooking food in fast food restaurants, including grilling, frying, and assembling orders, ensuring quick service and food quality.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level staff can advance to shift supervisor or restaurant manager, or move to other management roles in the food service industry. Experienced staff can transition into training or quality control.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated ordering system handles order entry and payment
- Robots for food preparation and cooking (e.g., flipping burgers, frying fries)
- Automated equipment monitors food temperature and time
- Cleaning robot completes in-store cleaning tasks
- Smart inventory systems manage food supplies and ordering.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted order scheduling optimizes workflow
- Smart recipe recommendation system helps innovate dishes
- Data analysis tools predict peak periods and allocate staff
- Real-time quality monitoring systems ensure food consistency
- Training simulators improve new employee skills

**Human moat:**

- On-site customer service and problem-solving skills
- Handling complex special orders and non-standard requirements
- Manual repair and emergency handling during equipment failure.
- Food quality sensory inspection (e.g., taste, appearance)
- Team collaboration and leadership skills

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy: operating ordering and inventory systems.
- Equipment maintenance and troubleshooting skills
- Knowledge of food safety and hygiene management
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic data analysis skills
- Cross-role flexibility (e.g., handling both cashier and meal prep)

**Key growth areas:**

- Fast food growth
- Quick service expansion
- Urbanization
- Takeaway demand

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of a fast food preparer in Ireland?**
A: Entry-level annual salary about €21,000-25,000, mid-level up to €30,000, senior supervisor around €35,000.

**Q: Can overseas individuals obtain an Irish work visa through this occupation?**
A: Low possibility because the occupation is not in high demand; requires employer sponsorship and meeting a €30,000 annual salary threshold, but entry-level positions are typically below this standard.
