# Child Care Services Manager（Child Care Services Manager）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：1341 – Child Care Services Manager。**

Oversees and manages childcare facilities, including nurseries and daycare centers, ensuring safe, high-quality services and regulatory compliance.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for Child Care Services Managers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level: €28,000-€35,000; mid-level: €35,000-€45,000; senior: up to €60,000.

**问：How can overseas applicants obtain a work permit for Child Care Services Manager in Ireland?**
答：Apply for a General Employment Permit with employer sponsorship and salary ≥€30,000. If highly skilled, consider EU Blue Card.
