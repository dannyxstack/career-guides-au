# Industrial and Production Engineer（Industrial and Production Engineer）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：2141 – Industrial and Production Engineer。**

Industrial and production engineers optimize manufacturing processes, improve efficiency, reduce costs, and ensure quality control, playing a key role in the manufacturing sector.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于紧缺职业，年薪超过32,000欧元可申请，工作2年后可申请永居。 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 年薪30,000欧元以上可申请，劳工市场测试后获批。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 年薪至少33,000欧元，需高等教育学历，可通往永居。 |

**PR难度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for industrial and production engineers in Ireland?**
答：Entry-level around €40,000, mid-level around €55,000, senior can exceed €80,000.

**问：Is it easy to get a work visa for this occupation in Ireland?**
答：Yes, it's on the Critical Skills list; applications for a Critical Skills Employment Permit are straightforward with salary above €32,000.

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：From junior to senior engineer, production manager, or operations director; also possible to move into consulting with experience.
