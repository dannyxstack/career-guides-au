# Professional Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified（Professional Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：1349 – Professional Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Manage and direct the operations and strategy of professional service organizations (e.g., consulting, legal, accounting, architecture, engineering) to ensure business objectives are met.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Critical Skills Empl（Critical Skills Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪€38,000以上的专业服务管理岗位，可快速获得永居 |
| Subclass General Employment P（General Employment Permit） | 适用于年薪€34,000以上岗位，需劳动力市场测试 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年薪€41,808以上且持有高级学位，可享受欧盟内工作便利 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Professional Services Managers?**
答：Entry-level around €35,000-€50,000, mid-level €55,000-€85,000, senior €90,000-€150,000. Partner-level can exceed €200,000.

**问：What are the requirements for a Critical Skills Employment Permit in Ireland?**
答：Requires salary above €38,000 and occupation on the eligible list. Professional Services Managers qualify, leading to Stamp 4 and fast-tracked permanent residency.

**问：What is the career progression for Professional Services Managers?**
答：Start as consultant or analyst, progress to manager, senior manager, director, and eventually partner or CEO. MBA or professional certifications can accelerate.
