# Physical and Engineering Science Technician Not Elsewhere Classified（Physical and Engineering Science Technician Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IE

**职业代码：3119 – Physical and Engineering Science Technician Not Elsewhere Classified。**

In Ireland, these technicians support R&D, testing, and production in physical and engineering sciences, covering materials science, environmental monitoring, quality control, and other disciplines.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for this role in Ireland?**
答：Salaries range from €28,000 to €60,000 per year, with entry-level around €30,000 and senior up to €55,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate for this role?**
答：Typically require a General Employment Permit (salary ≥€30k), or EU Blue Card (salary ≥€42k) if eligible. EU citizens can work freely.

**问：What are the long-term prospects in Ireland?**
答：With growing investment in pharma and clean energy, demand is stable, with paths to engineering management or consultancy.
