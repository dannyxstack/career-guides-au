## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sweepers and related labourers in Argentina?**
A: The average monthly salary for sweepers and related labourers in Argentina is around ARS 80,000 to ARS 120,000 depending on the region and employer. Many workers are employed informally, so pay can vary. Some municipalities offer additional benefits such as health coverage or union agreements that may include overtime pay.

**Q: What is the day-to-day work like for sweepers in Argentina?**
A: Sweepers typically work outdoors in urban areas, cleaning streets, parks, and public spaces using brooms, dustpans, and sometimes mechanical sweepers. They often work early morning or night shifts to avoid traffic. Tasks include collecting litter, emptying bins, and reporting hazards. Work is physically demanding and requires standing or walking for long periods.

**Q: How is AI or automation affecting sweepers and related labourers in Argentina?**
A: Automation has limited impact on this role in Argentina due to the complexity of urban cleaning and low adoption of robotic sweepers. While some municipalities have introduced mechanical sweepers, manual sweeping remains essential for narrow streets and areas with heavy litter. AI-driven route optimization may change scheduling but won't eliminate jobs. Labour demand is expected to remain stable.
