## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for landscape architects in Argentina?**
A: As of 2025, entry-level landscape architects earn around ARS 180,000–250,000 per month. Mid-career professionals with 5–10 years of experience typically earn ARS 300,000–450,000 monthly, while senior roles or firm owners can exceed ARS 600,000. Salaries vary by region, with Buenos Aires offering higher pay due to greater project density and cost of living.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a landscape architect in Argentina?**
A: A typical day includes site analysis, client meetings, and design development using CAD and 3D modeling software. Landscape architects collaborate with architects and engineers on urban projects, parks, or residential gardens. They also prepare construction documents, oversee installations, and manage plant selection, often balancing creative design with regulatory compliance specific to Argentine municipalities.

**Q: How is AI impacting landscape architecture in Argentina?**
A: AI tools are increasingly used for site analysis, generating design alternatives, and optimizing irrigation and planting schedules. However, human expertise remains essential for contextual sensitivity and client interpretation. In Argentina, AI adoption is slower in smaller firms, but larger practices leverage parametric design and drone-based surveys. The profession is expected to evolve, not diminish, as AI handles repetitive tasks.
