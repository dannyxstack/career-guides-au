## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a ships’ deck officer in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, entry-level deck officers earn around ARS 200,000–300,000 monthly. Experienced officers on large vessels or long-haul routes can make ARS 500,000–800,000. These figures vary by company, vessel type, and rank. Salaries often include offshore bonuses and benefits. Pay is influenced by inflation, with regular adjustments expected.

**Q: What are typical daily duties for a deck officer in Argentina?**
A: Deck officers in Argentina manage navigation, safety, and cargo operations on coastal, river (e.g., Paraná), or ocean vessels. Daily tasks include watchkeeping, plotting courses, monitoring weather, communicating with ports like Buenos Aires or Bahía Blanca, supervising crew, and ensuring compliance with Argentine maritime regulations. They also maintain logs and conduct drills.

**Q: What skills are essential for deck officers in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include navigation (using radar, GPS, ECDIS), ship handling, and knowledge of Argentine waterways and port regulations. Strong communication (Spanish and English) is vital for coordinating with multinational crews and authorities. Problem-solving, decision-making under pressure, and physical stamina are critical. Certifications from the Argentine Coast Guard (Prefectura Naval) are mandatory.
