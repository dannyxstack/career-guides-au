## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for judges in Argentina?**
A: Judges in Argentina earn between ARS 500,000 and ARS 1,500,000 monthly, depending on jurisdiction and experience. Entry-level judges at lower courts start around ARS 500,000, while Supreme Court justices earn significantly more. Salaries are set by law and adjusted periodically for inflation, but have not kept pace with rising costs.

**Q: What is the career outlook for judges in Argentina?**
A: The career outlook for judges in Argentina is stable but competitive. Appointments are lifelong until retirement at age 70, with opportunities for promotion to higher courts. The number of positions is limited and depends on judicial budget and reforms. Demand is steady, but advancement is slow due to low turnover.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the role of judges in Argentina?**
A: AI is increasingly used in Argentina’s judiciary for case management, document analysis, and predictive analytics. However, it is a tool to assist judges, not replace them. AI helps reduce backlogs and streamline routine tasks, but human judgment remains essential for complex legal decisions and sentencing.
