## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, the average monthly salary for this role ranges from ARS 180,000 to ARS 300,000, varying by experience and location. Senior technicians can earn up to ARS 450,000. Salaries are often adjusted for inflation, and many positions include performance bonuses or overtime pay.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for network technicians in Argentina?**
A: Employers seek expertise in Cisco and Juniper equipment, routing and switching, firewall administration (e.g., Fortinet, Palo Alto), and wireless networking. Scripting (Python, Bash) and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) are increasingly valued. Strong troubleshooting and communication skills are essential, given the need to support diverse clients.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of computer network technicians in Argentina?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks like network monitoring and incident response, but it also creates demand for technicians skilled in AI-driven tools (e.g., SD-WAN, predictive analytics). Technicians must adapt by learning AI concepts and automation scripts, while focusing on complex problem-solving and network design where human judgment is still critical.
