## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Services Manager in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, ICT Services Managers earn between ARS 1,500,000 and ARS 3,500,000 per month, depending on experience and company size. Senior roles in multinationals or tech hubs like Buenos Aires can exceed ARS 5,000,000. Bonuses, healthcare, and stock options are common additions.

**Q: What skills are most important for ICT Services Managers in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include strategic IT planning, project management (Agile/Scrum), cybersecurity, vendor management, and leadership. Given Argentina's economic volatility, financial acumen and cost optimization are highly valued. Continuous learning in cloud technologies and AI is increasingly critical for career advancement.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of ICT Services Managers in Argentina?**
A: AI automates routine IT operations, shifting managers' focus to strategic AI integration, data governance, and ethical deployment. They oversee AI-driven tools for network management, security, and customer support. Upskilling in machine learning and data analytics is essential to maintain competitiveness, making AI literacy a core requirement.
