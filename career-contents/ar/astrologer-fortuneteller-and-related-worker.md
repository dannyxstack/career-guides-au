## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Argentina?**
A: Income varies widely. Street readers may earn ARS 15,000–30,000 per month, while established online consultants can charge ARS 1,000–3,000 per session. Top professionals in Buenos Aires or with media appearances earn up to ARS 100,000 monthly. Most income is cash-based and irregular.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for an astrologer or fortune-teller in Argentina?**
A: Many work from home, rented spaces, or street stalls in tourist areas. They spend time consulting clients via face-to-face, phone, or video calls, interpreting birth charts, tarot cards, or palms. Administration tasks include managing appointments, social media promotion, and purchasing supplies like candles or decks.

**Q: How is AI affecting astrologers and fortune-tellers in Argentina?**
A: AI-powered apps and chatbots now offer free or cheap horoscopes and tarot readings, reducing demand for low-cost services. However, many clients still seek human connection and personalized interpretation. Professionals who emphasize empathy, intuition, and interactive sessions are less affected. Adapting by offering online courses or hybrid readings helps maintain relevance.
