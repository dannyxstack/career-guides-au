## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for taxi drivers in Buenos Aires?**
A: Average monthly income for taxi drivers in Buenos Aires ranges from ARS 150,000 to ARS 250,000 depending on shifts, vehicle type, and area. Earnings fluctuate with passenger demand, and drivers often pay fuel and vehicle maintenance costs. Many drivers also receive tips, which supplement their base fare income.

**Q: What skills are required to become a taxi driver in Argentina?**
A: A valid professional driver's license (licencia de conductor profesional) and a clean driving record are mandatory. Taxi drivers need excellent knowledge of city streets and traffic patterns, good customer service skills, and basic math for fare handling. Familiarity with navigation apps and Spanish proficiency are also essential. Many drivers undergo medical and psychological evaluations.

**Q: How have ride-hailing apps affected the taxi industry in Argentina?**
A: Ride-hailing apps like Uber and Cabify have increased competition for traditional taxis, pressuring drivers to adapt. Many taxi drivers now use these apps alongside street hailing, while others stick to regulated taxi zones. The shift has encouraged better service and digital literacy, but has also led to disputes over licensing and fare structures amid regulatory changes.
