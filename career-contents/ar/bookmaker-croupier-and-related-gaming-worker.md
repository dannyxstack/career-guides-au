**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic odds calculation and dynamic adjustment
- Simple bet processing and payment (replaced by self-service terminals)
- Player Identity Verification and Credit Check Automation
- Basic customer inquiries (chatbot)
- Initial card dealing and chip counting on some tables (RFID technology)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time monitoring of abnormal betting patterns and alerts
- Using big data analytics to optimize casino table configurations based on player behavior
- Multilingual AI translation helps dealers handle international clients
- AR/VR training to upskill new croupiers

**Human moat:**

- Authentic emotional interaction and trust building with players
- Human adjudication of disputes over complex rules or cheating
- Entertainment atmosphere creation and personalised service
- Sensitive judgment on legal and regulatory compliance
- Crisis Management (e.g., Intoxicated Customers, Conflict Mediation)

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using electronic table management systems

- Multilingual ability (at least English plus Mandarin or Arabic)
- Dispute resolution and customer psychology
- Familiar with Italian gambling regulations and anti-money laundering compliance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a croupier in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, croupiers typically earn between ARS 80,000 and ARS 150,000 per month, depending on the casino's location and prestige. Tips and bonuses can significantly increase earnings, especially in high-traffic casinos in Buenos Aires. Senior dealers or those specializing in popular games like poker may earn higher wages.

**Q: What skills are essential for bookmakers and croupiers in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include strong mental arithmetic, manual dexterity for handling chips and cards, excellent customer service, and knowledge of game rules. In Argentina, casinos often require fluency in Spanish, while English is beneficial for international tourists. Integrity, attention to detail, and the ability to work under pressure are also critical.

**Q: How is AI impacting gaming workers in Argentina?**
A: AI is gradually automating administrative tasks like bet tracking and odds calculation, but croupiers and bookmakers remain essential for live table games where human interaction is valued. Some Argentine casinos use AI for surveillance and fraud detection, but it has not yet replaced dealers. Technology may shift roles toward customer-focused positions.
