## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for web and multimedia developers in Argentina?**
A: Junior web developers in Argentina earn around ARS 150,000–200,000 monthly, while senior roles can reach ARS 400,000–600,000 or more, with Buenos Aires salaries typically higher. Freelancers often charge USD 20–50 per hour, especially when working with international clients.

**Q: How is AI impacting web development jobs in Argentina?**
A: AI tools like GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT boost productivity by automating code generation and debugging, shifting focus to architecture and UX. While basic coding demand may drop, developers who leverage AI for complex problem-solving and efficient workflows remain highly valued.

**Q: What is the career outlook for web developers in Argentina?**
A: Demand stays strong thanks to digital transformation in e-commerce and fintech. Remote work with global clients is common. Continuous learning of modern frameworks (React, Node.js) and cloud services is key for growth. Full-stack and specialized roles (e.g., blockchain, AI) offer the best prospects.
