## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for police inspectors and detectives in Argentina?**
A: Salaries vary by province and seniority. Entry-level inspectors earn around ARS 80,000–120,000 per month, while experienced detectives can make ARS 150,000–250,000. Federal police roles in Buenos Aires often pay higher. Overtime and hazard pay may apply.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a police inspector or detective in Argentina?**
A: Inspectors oversee patrol operations, review reports, and coordinate investigations. Detectives conduct interviews, analyze evidence, and work with forensic teams. Both attend briefings, prepare case files, and may testify in court. Shifts can be irregular, including nights and weekends.

**Q: What key skills are required to become a police inspector or detective in Argentina?**
A: Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and written communication are essential. Physical fitness and firearm proficiency are mandatory. Investigative experience, knowledge of criminal law, and ability to work under pressure are crucial. Fluency in Spanish is required; English is a plus for federal roles.
