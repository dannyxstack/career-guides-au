## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use BIM software for formwork design optimization (AI replaces manual calculation)
- Automatic inspection of template dimensions and position accuracy through AI image recognition
- Use drones or laser scanning to automatically measure field data
- AI-generated cutting lists and material optimization plans
- Automated template layout linked with CNC cutting.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted augmented reality (AR) headsets provide real-time template installation guidance
- AI-driven project management tools optimize processes and resource scheduling
- Digital twin simulates template forces to improve safety and quality
- AI voice assistant for on-site documentation and reporting
- Automatically generate construction progress reports and cost estimates

**Human moat:**

- On-site adjustment and manual fabrication of complex irregular formwork
- Multi-trade coordination and on-site problem-solving skills
- Experiential judgment of material properties (wood defects, humidity, etc.)
- Client communication and understanding of customized needs
- Physical adaptability for working at heights and heavy manual labor

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a carpenter or joiner in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, carpenters and joiners earn an average monthly salary of ARS 120,000 to ARS 180,000, depending on experience and location. Skilled professionals in Buenos Aires or those specializing in custom furniture may earn higher rates, while apprentices start at the lower end.

**Q: What skills and certifications are required to work as a carpenter in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include proficiency with hand and power tools, ability to read blueprints, and knowledge of wood properties. While formal certification isn't mandatory, completing technical courses from institutions like the UOCRA or SENET can improve job prospects. Many carpenters learn through apprenticeships.

**Q: How is AI impacting carpentry jobs in Argentina?**
A: AI and automation are gradually influencing Argentina's carpentry industry, mainly through CNC machines and design software. However, manual skills remain essential for custom work and on-site construction. Experienced carpenters who adapt to digital tools will stay competitive, but full automation is unlikely in the near future.
