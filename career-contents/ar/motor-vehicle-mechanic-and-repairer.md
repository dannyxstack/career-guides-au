**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, motor vehicle mechanics earn between ARS 80,000 and ARS 150,000 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Specialists in luxury or imported cars may earn higher. Overtime and bonuses can increase pay.

**Q: What skills are most important for a motor vehicle mechanic in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include diagnostic ability using electronic tools, knowledge of modern engine management systems, and manual dexterity. Mechanical repair fundamentals remain essential, but familiarity with computer-based diagnostics is increasingly important due to newer vehicles.

**Q: How is the job outlook for motor vehicle mechanics in Argentina?**
A: The demand for mechanics remains stable due to the large number of vehicles on the road. However, the shift towards electric vehicles may require retraining. Basic repair skills will always be needed, but mechanics must adapt to new technologies.
