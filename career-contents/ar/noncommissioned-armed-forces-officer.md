## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned armed forces officer in Argentina?**
A: Salaries for non-commissioned officers in Argentina vary by rank and years of service. As of 2024, a junior NCO (Suboficial) earns around ARS 120,000–180,000 per month, while senior ranks (Suboficial Principal) can earn ARS 250,000–350,000. Additional allowances for deployments or special skills may apply.

**Q: What skills are required to become a non-commissioned officer in the Argentine Armed Forces?**
A: Candidates must complete secondary education and pass physical, psychological, and medical exams. Key skills include leadership, discipline, teamwork, and technical proficiency in weapons or equipment. Training at dedicated NCO schools (e.g., Escuela de Suboficiales) lasts 2–3 years, focusing on military tactics, ethics, and administration.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the role of non-commissioned officers in Argentina?**
A: AI is gradually enhancing logistics, surveillance, and data analysis in the Argentine military. NCOs now use AI-driven tools for maintenance scheduling and threat assessment, but core leadership and decision-making remain human-driven. The need for digital literacy is growing, yet no major job displacement is expected in the near term.
