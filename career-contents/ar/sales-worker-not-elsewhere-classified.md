## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sales worker in Argentina?**
A: Entry-level sales workers in Argentina earn around ARS 80,000–120,000 per month, while experienced workers can earn ARS 150,000–250,000. Commission-based roles may yield higher earnings. Salaries vary by industry, location, and employer size.

**Q: What skills are essential for a sales worker in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include strong communication and negotiation abilities, product knowledge, customer relationship management, and basic digital literacy for CRM tools. Bilingual proficiency (English) is valued in export-oriented sectors. Adaptability and resilience are important for handling rejection.

**Q: How is AI impacting sales jobs in Argentina?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks like lead generation and data entry, but human skills such as relationship-building and complex negotiation remain crucial. Sales workers who leverage AI tools for analytics and customer insights will have an advantage. The role is evolving rather than disappearing.
