## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes and related workers in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, scribes and related workers earn an average monthly salary between ARS 80,000 and ARS 130,000, depending on experience and sector. Court reporters and specialized transcriptionists may earn more. Salaries vary significantly by region and employer, with opportunities for overtime pay.

**Q: What skills are required for scribes and related workers in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include fast and accurate typing, excellent knowledge of Spanish grammar and spelling, and proficiency in digital transcription tools. Familiarity with legal or medical terminology is advantageous for specialized roles. Attention to detail and ability to work under deadlines are essential. Good listening skills and basic audio editing knowledge are also beneficial.

**Q: How is AI impacting scribes and related workers in Argentina?**
A: AI-powered speech recognition and transcription tools are automating parts of the job, reducing demand for basic transcription. However, human scribes are still needed for high-accuracy tasks, such as legal proceedings, where context and nuance matter. Many professionals are adapting by learning to review and edit AI-generated transcripts, shifting toward quality assurance roles.
