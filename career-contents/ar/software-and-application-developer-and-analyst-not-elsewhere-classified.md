## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Argentina?**
A: Salaries vary by experience. Junior developers earn around ARS 150,000-250,000 per month, mid-level ARS 300,000-500,000, and senior roles ARS 600,000-900,000. Many positions for remote work pay in USD, often doubling local rates. Bonuses and stock options are less common.

**Q: How is AI affecting software development jobs in Argentina?**
A: AI tools like GitHub Copilot assist with code generation and debugging, increasing productivity. While some routine tasks are automated, demand for developers with AI integration skills grows. Professionals focusing on architecture, security, and system design remain highly valued.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for software developers in Argentina?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in Python, JavaScript, and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure). Familiarity with AI/ML frameworks, agile methodologies, and English communication is also critical. The market favors full-stack developers who can work across frontend and backend.
