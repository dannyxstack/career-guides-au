## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a fumigator in Argentina?**
A: Entry-level fumigators earn around ARS 45,000–60,000 monthly, while experienced workers can make ARS 80,000–120,000. Pay varies by region (higher in agricultural areas) and employer (farm vs. pest control company). Overtime and travel allowances may apply.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a fumigator in Argentina?**
A: Fumigators must complete a training course and obtain a national license (e.g., from SENASA or provincial authorities). Knowledge of pest identification, safe chemical handling, and equipment operation is essential. Many employers also require a driver's license and basic English for reading product labels.

**Q: What is the job outlook for fumigators in Argentina?**
A: Demand remains steady due to Argentina's large agricultural sector (soy, corn, wheat) and urban pest control needs. Job prospects are good in rural provinces like Córdoba, Santa Fe, and Buenos Aires. Automation and drones are increasing efficiency but not replacing human workers, as regulatory oversight and precision applications still require trained operators.
