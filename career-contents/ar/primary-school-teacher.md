- Marking standardised assignments and exam papers
- Generate basic lesson plans and classroom materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI adaptive learning platforms to customize exercises and feedback for each student

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a primary school teacher in Argentina?**
A: The average monthly salary for a primary school teacher in Argentina ranges from ARS 80,000 to ARS 120,000 gross, depending on the province and public/private sector. This is relatively low compared to other professions requiring similar education, and many teachers supplement income with additional jobs.

**Q: What skills are essential for primary school teachers in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include strong classroom management, patience, creativity in lesson planning, and the ability to adapt to diverse student needs. Proficiency in Spanish is mandatory, and digital literacy is increasingly important for using educational technology. Interpersonal skills are crucial for working with parents and colleagues.

**Q: What is the career outlook for primary school teachers in Argentina?**
A: The demand for primary teachers remains stable due to population growth and retirements, but the profession faces challenges such as low pay and high stress. Opportunities exist in both public and private schools, though public positions require passing competitive exams. Career advancement may involve moving into administration or specialized education.
