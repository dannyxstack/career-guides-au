**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generating preliminary diagnostic suggestions based on symptoms and tongue/pulse images.
- Automatic recommendation of commonly used herbal formulas or acupuncture prescriptions
- Organizing and basic analysis of patient health records
- Automated responses for appointment management and patient inquiries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis: Use image recognition to analyze tongue coating, complexion, etc., to assist in syndrome differentiation.
- Personalized treatment plans: recommend optimized herbal combinations or acupuncture plans based on big data.
- Knowledge retrieval: quickly obtain the latest research literature and classic cases
- Patient education: auto-generate illustrated wellness advice and rehabilitation plans

**Human moat:**

- Manual skills (e.g., acupuncture techniques, massage force)
- Interpersonal communication skills to build trust and empathy with patients
- Holistic differentiation and individualized adjustment for complex cases.
- Ethical Judgment and Treatment Responsibility in Cross-Cultural Contexts

**Skills to build:**

- Big data analysis of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and AI tool operation
- use of digital diagnostic devices such as tongue and pulse diagnosis
- Application of telemedicine platforms and patient management
- Continuously learn the latest developments in AI-assisted diagnosis and treatment.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income range for traditional and complementary medicine professionals in Argentina?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by specialization and location. In Buenos Aires, acupuncturists and herbalists earn between ARS 80,000 and ARS 150,000 per month; in smaller cities, income is lower. Many combine private practice with work at health centers. Income depends on client volume, insurance coverage, and whether professionals offer services like homeopathy or naturopathy.

**Q: What does a typical workday look like for a traditional medicine professional in Argentina?**
A: Professionals conduct patient consultations, take health histories, and apply therapies such as acupuncture, herbal medicine, or homeopathy. They work in private clinics, wellness centers, or independently. Many also educate patients on preventive care. Collaboration with conventional doctors is growing, especially in integrative health settings. Documentation and inventory management are also part of daily tasks.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting traditional and complementary medicine in Argentina?**
A: AI is being used to analyze patient data and suggest personalized treatment plans, but it cannot replace hands-on diagnostic skills. Professionals who adopt digital tools for scheduling and record-keeping can improve efficiency. However, AI's role remains limited, and clinical judgment is still essential. The field values human touch and individualized care, which AI currently cannot replicate.
