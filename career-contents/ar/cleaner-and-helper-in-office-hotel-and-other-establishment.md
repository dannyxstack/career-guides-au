**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean floors using smart vacuum and mop robots
- Operation monitoring of automatic window cleaning robots
- Dust removal in standard areas such as corridors and halls following preset routes.
- Use automatic disinfection robots to disinfect bathrooms and kitchens.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize multi-site cleaning routes and schedules
- Use smart sensors to monitor dirt levels and precisely schedule cleaning frequency
- Receive health and safety alerts via wearable devices to enhance personal protection
- Adjusts service details using a customer review AI system to improve satisfaction
- Use digital tools to manage cleaning supplies, reducing waste

**Human moat:**

- Identification and targeted treatment of complex stubborn stains (e.g., carpet stains, oil stains).
- Communicate with clients to understand specific needs and flexibly adjust cleaning methods
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., breaking items, pet accidents)
- Fine cleaning and care of valuable items (e.g., antiques, silk)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners and helpers in offices and hotels in Argentina?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer. As of 2025, the minimum wage is around ARS 280,000 per month, but cleaners often earn between ARS 250,000 and ARS 400,000 monthly, depending on experience and hours. Tips in hotels and large offices can supplement income. Formal employment includes social security benefits.

**Q: What skills are required for cleaners and helpers in Argentina?**
A: No formal education is required, but literacy in Spanish is important for safety instructions. Key skills include attention to detail, time management, physical stamina, and the ability to use cleaning chemicals safely. Experience with industrial equipment (buffers, vacuums) is valued, especially in hotels and large offices.

**Q: What is the career outlook for cleaners and helpers in Argentina?**
A: Demand remains stable due to turnover in hospitality and commercial cleaning. Automation like robotic vacuums is limited to large hotels, so human cleaners are still needed in smaller establishments. Job growth is steady but slow; advancement to supervisory roles is possible with experience.
