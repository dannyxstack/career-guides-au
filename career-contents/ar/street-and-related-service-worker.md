## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for street and related services workers in Argentina?**
A: Street vendors and similar workers in Argentina typically earn between AR$30,000 and AR$60,000 per month. Income varies greatly by location, type of product or service, and hours worked. Most transactions are cash-based, and workers often lack formal contracts or benefits.

**Q: What are the typical daily tasks of a street services worker in Argentina?**
A: Daily tasks include setting up a sales point, arranging merchandise, interacting with customers, handling cash, and restocking. Workers often operate in busy pedestrian areas, markets, or near transit hubs. The job is physically demanding, with long hours and exposure to weather, requiring strong communication and negotiation skills.

**Q: How is AI and technology affecting street services work in Argentina?**
A: AI and automation have minimal direct impact on street vending due to its reliance on personal interaction. However, rising digital payments and e-commerce may shift customer habits. The outlook remains stable but informal, with competition from formal retail and periodic regulatory changes affecting operations.
