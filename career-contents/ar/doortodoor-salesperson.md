## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income for a door-to-door salesperson in Argentina?**
A: Income varies widely based on commission. Entry-level workers may earn around ARS 80,000–120,000 monthly, while experienced sellers can make ARS 200,000–400,000 or more. Many earn base salary plus commission. Earnings depend on product type, sales volume, and location, with higher potential in affluent neighborhoods.

**Q: What skills are essential for success in door-to-door sales in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include strong communication, persuasion, resilience, and time management. Salespersons must handle rejection confidently, adapt pitches to different audiences, and navigate varied neighborhoods. Basic math for pricing and cash handling is important. Familiarity with local products and payment methods (e.g., effective cash or card usage) adds credibility.

**Q: How is AI impacting door-to-door sales jobs in Argentina?**
A: AI mainly influences lead generation and customer management through CRM tools and data analytics, helping salespersons target prospects more efficiently. However, the core face-to-face interaction remains unaffected. AI may reduce some administrative tasks, but personal selling skills are still crucial. The role is not expected to be replaced by AI in the near term.
