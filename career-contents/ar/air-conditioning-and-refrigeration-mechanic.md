## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, entry-level mechanics earn around ARS 80,000–120,000 per month, while experienced professionals can earn ARS 150,000–250,000. Salaries vary by region and employer type (residential vs. commercial). Union-negotiated rates and overtime pay are common, especially during peak summer months.

**Q: What technical skills are most important for this role in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of refrigeration cycles, electrical troubleshooting, and handling refrigerants like R-410A and R-32. Practical experience with split systems, chillers, and HVAC controls is essential. Certification for handling flammable refrigerants (e.g., R-290) is increasingly required due to local regulations.

**Q: How is AI impacting air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Argentina?**
A: AI-powered predictive maintenance tools and smart thermostats are reducing manual diagnostics, but mechanics are still needed for installation, repair, and compliance with evolving refrigerant laws. AI handles data analysis, while on-site hands-on work and custom troubleshooting remain human-dependent, ensuring stable demand.
