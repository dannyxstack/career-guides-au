## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for software developers in Argentina?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. In Buenos Aires, junior developers earn between ARS 150,000 and ARS 200,000 monthly, while mid-level professionals earn ARS 250,000 to ARS 400,000. Senior developers and specialists can earn ARS 500,000 or more. Remote work for foreign companies often pays higher rates, sometimes in USD.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for software developers in Argentina?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in JavaScript, Python, Java, and .NET. Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), databases (SQL, NoSQL), and agile methodologies is highly valued. Soft skills like communication and teamwork are also critical, especially for remote or hybrid roles.

**Q: How is AI impacting software development careers in Argentina?**
A: AI tools like GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT are used to boost productivity in coding, testing, and debugging. While some fear job displacement, most developers find AI enhances rather than replaces their work. Demand for AI-skilled developers is growing, and adapting to these tools is key for career advancement.
