**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Environmental parameter monitoring and recording (temperature, humidity, light)
- Basic feed delivery and water supply management
- Mechanized operation of poultry house cleaning and waste disposal
- Preliminary statistics and report generation for production data
- Collection of vital signs data in routine health checks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize feed ratios and feeding times through AI analysis
- Early detection of abnormal behavior in sick poultry using computer vision
- Predicting egg production/slaughter volume based on production data to plan sales
- Efficiency improved in inspecting large poultry houses with drones or ground robots
- AI-assisted epidemic early warning and biosecurity decision support

**Human moat:**

- Acute observation and immediate handling of abnormal behavior in individual poultry.
- veterinary-level disease diagnosis and medication decisions
- Emergency management for sudden weather or equipment failure
- Animal welfare operations in special production systems like organic and free-range
- Regulatory compliance responsibilities related to government quarantine and food certification

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization tools (e.g., Excel, Tableau)
- Understanding the application of computer vision systems in farming
- Deepened knowledge of animal behavior and biosecurity

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for poultry producers in Argentina?**
A: Poultry producers in Argentina earn between ARS 80,000 and ARS 150,000 per month, depending on farm size, experience, and region. Larger operations in Buenos Aires or Córdoba tend to pay higher. Salaries may include bonuses tied to production targets. Entry-level workers earn around ARS 60,000, while senior producers with over 5 years of experience can exceed ARS 180,000 monthly.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a poultry producer in Argentina?**
A: A poultry producer starts by checking flock health, feed, and water systems. They monitor temperature and ventilation in the sheds, oversee egg collection or bird growth, and ensure biosecurity measures are followed. Administrative tasks include recording production data, ordering supplies, and coordinating with veterinarians or nutritionists. During processing or shipping, they supervise loading and quality control.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting poultry production in Argentina?**
A: AI is increasingly used in Argentine poultry farms for automated feeding, climate control, and disease detection through sensor data and cameras. Smart systems adjust conditions in real time, reducing manual labor and improving efficiency. However, AI remains less common on small farms due to cost. Producers still need hands-on skills to interpret data and handle emergencies, so human oversight remains essential.
