**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.
- Routine structural measurement: ROV with sensors automatically collects data
- Underwater environment monitoring: AI analyzes parameters such as water temperature and turbidity
- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk
- Augmented reality headset: overlays structural diagrams and repair instructions to improve work accuracy
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes historical data to optimize maintenance schedules.
- Remote specialist collaboration: AI translation and 3D reconstruction help land-based experts guide

**Human moat:**

- Manual operation and quick judgment in emergency situations
- Fine tactile sensation and force feedback (e.g., valve operation)
- Adaptability in harsh environments (current, visibility)
- Legal and safety responsibility attribution (divers must sign on-site)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for underwater divers in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, underwater divers' salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level divers earn around ARS 80,000–120,000 per month. Experienced commercial divers, especially in oil and gas, can earn ARS 200,000–400,000 monthly. Offshore or deep-sea work may offer higher rates. Pay is often project-based, with additional compensation for hazardous conditions.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for an underwater diver in Argentina?**
A: A typical workday includes inspecting underwater structures, performing repairs, or conducting surveys for harbors, offshore platforms, or aquaculture sites. Divers must complete safety drills and equipment checks before each dive. Work is project-based and may involve long, irregular hours on-site. Physical endurance and attention to safety protocols are critical.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting underwater diving in Argentina?**
A: AI is streamlining underwater inspections through remotely operated vehicles (ROVs) and autonomous drones, reducing the need for human divers in hazardous settings. However, divers remain essential for complex repairs and delicate tasks. Those skilled in operating robotic systems and interpreting AI-generated data have improved job prospects. Continuous skill updates are advised to stay relevant.
