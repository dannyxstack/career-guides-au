## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Ships' Engineer in Argentina?**
A: In Argentina, Ships' Engineers earn between ARS 150,000 and ARS 300,000 per month, depending on experience and vessel type. Senior engineers on international cargo ships may receive dollar-based pay. Salaries are typically paid in pesos for domestic routes.

**Q: What does a typical workday look like for a Ships' Engineer in Argentina?**
A: A typical day involves monitoring engine room equipment, performing preventive maintenance, and troubleshooting mechanical issues. On Argentine vessels—such as coastal tankers or river barges—engineers work 4- to 6-hour watches, with additional duties during port calls. Safety checks and logkeeping are also routine.

**Q: What skills are essential for Ships' Engineers working in Argentina?**
A: Key skills include expertise in marine diesel engines, electrical systems, and automation. Proficiency in Spanish is mandatory, while English is important for international routes. Technical certifications from the Argentine Coast Guard (Prefectura Naval) are required, along with practical experience in onboard repairs.
