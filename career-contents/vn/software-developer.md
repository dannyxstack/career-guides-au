## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a software developer in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level software developers in Vietnam earn around 10–15 million VND per month, mid-level developers 20–40 million VND, and senior developers 50–80 million VND or more. Salaries vary by experience, tech stack, and company size, with tech hubs like Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering higher pay. Specialization in AI, cloud, or mobile can further boost earnings.

**Q: What programming languages and frameworks are most sought after for developers in Vietnam?**
A: Java, JavaScript, Python, and C# are most in demand. Popular frameworks include React, Angular, Node.js, and Spring Boot. Mobile development skills in Flutter and React Native are highly valued. Employers also seek cloud experience (AWS, Azure), knowledge of microservices, and DevOps practices like Docker and Kubernetes.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting software development careers in Vietnam?**
A: AI tools like GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT boost developer productivity by automating code generation and debugging. Developers now need to focus on system architecture, AI integration, and working with large language models. Demand is rising for developers who can build AI-powered features in fintech, e-commerce, and manufacturing, reshaping skill requirements.
