## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a physiotherapist in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level physiotherapists in Vietnam earn around 8-12 million VND per month, while experienced professionals can earn 15-25 million VND. Those in private clinics or international hospitals may earn higher. Salaries vary by location, with Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering higher pay due to demand.

**Q: What are the key skills needed to succeed as a physiotherapist in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include strong knowledge of anatomy and rehabilitation techniques, patient communication in Vietnamese and ideally English, manual therapy proficiency, and the ability to design personalized treatment plans. Soft skills like empathy and patience are crucial. Familiarity with modern equipment and ongoing learning is valued.

**Q: How is AI affecting the physiotherapy profession in Vietnam?**
A: AI is enhancing physiotherapy through tools for gait analysis and virtual rehabilitation, but it is not replacing human therapists. Vietnamese practitioners can leverage AI for initial assessments and progress tracking, freeing time for hands-on care. The profession remains people-centric, with growing demand in aging population and sports medicine.
