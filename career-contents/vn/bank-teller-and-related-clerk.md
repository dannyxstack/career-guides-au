## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bank teller in Vietnam?**
A: Bank tellers in Vietnam typically earn between 7 and 12 million VND per month, depending on experience and location. In major cities like Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi, starting salaries are around 7–8 million VND, while senior tellers can earn up to 12 million VND. Benefits may include year-end bonuses and allowances.

**Q: What skills are essential for bank tellers in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include strong numeracy, attention to detail, and proficiency in Vietnamese and basic English. Tellers must be computer literate, especially in banking software, and possess excellent customer service abilities. Knowledge of local banking regulations and cash handling procedures is also important to ensure accuracy and compliance.

**Q: How is AI impacting bank teller jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI and automation are reducing routine tasks like cash counting and data entry, leading to fewer traditional teller positions. However, banks still need tellers for complex customer inquiries and relationship building. Tellers who upskill in digital banking tools and financial advisory can remain valuable, as technology complements rather than fully replaces human interaction.
