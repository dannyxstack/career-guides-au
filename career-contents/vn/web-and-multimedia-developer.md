## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for web and multimedia developers in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level developers earn around 10–15 million VND per month, while mid-level (3–5 years) earn 20–35 million VND. Senior developers in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi can earn 40–60 million VND. Bonuses and 13th-month pay are common. Freelancers may charge $20–$50 per hour depending on expertise.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for web developers in Vietnam?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in JavaScript (React, Vue, Angular), back-end languages like PHP (Laravel) or Node.js, and database management (MySQL, MongoDB). Knowledge of responsive design, RESTful APIs, and cloud platforms (AWS) is highly valued. Multimedia developers need Adobe Creative Suite, animation tools (After Effects), and video editing skills.

**Q: How is AI affecting web development jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI tools like GitHub Copilot boost productivity but do not replace developers. Demand for human creativity, UX design, and complex problem-solving remains strong. Developers who learn AI integration (e.g., chatbots, recommendation systems) gain a competitive edge. The outlook is positive, with growth in e-commerce, fintech, and startup sectors.
