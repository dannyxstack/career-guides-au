## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic Call Distribution (ACD) system takes over call routing
- Interactive Voice Response (IVR) handles simple queries and routing
- Speech recognition and natural language processing replace basic manual information consultation services
- Automated attendant software enables 24/7 unattended call handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time customer information and advice to improve service quality
- Natural language processing helps handle multilingual calls, expanding service scope
- Intelligently analyze call data to optimize call routing and staff scheduling
- Automatically record and summarize call content, reducing administrative work

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex, emotional, or contentious customer calls
- Personalized services requiring empathy and interpersonal communication
- Ability to adapt flexibly in emergency or unusual situations
- Multitasking ability (managing multiple lines and systems simultaneously)

**Skills to build:**

- Customer relationship management (CRM) system operation
- Basic data analysis and reporting
- Multilingual communication skills (especially English)
- Emotion management and conflict resolution skills

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a telephone switchboard operator in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level telephone switchboard operators in Vietnam earn around 5-7 million VND per month, while experienced operators can earn 8-12 million VND. Salaries vary by city, with higher pay in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi. Many employers also provide shift allowances and social insurance contributions.

**Q: What skills are essential for a telephone switchboard operator in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include clear verbal communication in Vietnamese and basic English, active listening, patience, and multitasking. Familiarity with PBX systems and call routing software is important. Fast and accurate typing, problem-solving under pressure, and good memory for extensions or contacts are also valued.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting telephone switchboard operator jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI-powered chatbots and automated call-routing systems are reducing demand for manual switchboard operators in large organizations. However, human operators remain essential for handling complex inquiries, multilingual calls, and personalized service. Operators who adapt by learning to manage AI tools and focus on high-value interactions will remain relevant.
