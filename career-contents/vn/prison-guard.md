**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Surveillance systems automatically analyze abnormal behavior, replacing manual monitoring
- AI-generated standardized patrol reports and prisoner files, replacing paperwork
- Smart access control and positioning systems automatically control prisoner movement range, reducing manual opening and closing
- AI-assisted prohibited item detection (e.g., X-ray image recognition) reduces manual inspection.
- Voice recognition and natural language processing automatically record summaries of inmate calls, replacing dictation.

- AI risk scoring system assists in identifying prisoners with high violence potential, enhancing early warning capability
- Smart sensor monitoring devices track inmate health status in real time, enhancing emergency response speed
- VR simulation training enhances prison officers' ability to handle emergencies such as riots and negotiations.
- AI schedule optimization tool automatically arranges shifts, reducing administrative burden
- Multilingual AI translation helps prison officers communicate with non-local inmates, reducing cultural conflicts

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a prison guard in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level prison guards in Vietnam earn around 5-8 million VND per month, increasing with experience and rank. Senior guards or those in supervisory roles can earn up to 15 million VND. Salaries are set by the Ministry of Public Security and include allowances for hazardous duty and shift work.

**Q: What skills are required to become a prison guard in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include physical fitness, situational awareness, conflict de-escalation, and first aid. Candidates must have completed military service or police training, pass a background check, and demonstrate integrity and discipline. Strong communication and teamwork are essential for managing inmates and coordinating with colleagues.

**Q: What is the career outlook for prison guards in Vietnam?**
A: The demand for prison guards remains stable due to the need for secure correctional facilities. Career advancement opportunities include promotion to senior guard, shift supervisor, or specialized units like riot control. While automation may affect some administrative tasks, direct inmate supervision will continue to require human personnel.
