## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a shelf filler in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, shelf fillers typically earn between 5 to 8 million VND per month. Entry-level positions start at minimum wage (around 4.5 million VND), while experienced workers in large supermarkets can earn up to 10 million VND. Overtime and night shifts often provide additional pay. Salary varies by location, store size, and employer.

**Q: What skills are required to be a shelf filler in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail, physical stamina, basic numeracy, and the ability to work quickly in a fast-paced environment. Familiarity with stock rotation (first-in, first-out) and product labeling is important. Good organizational skills and teamwork are valued. No formal education is required, but literacy in Vietnamese is necessary for reading labels and instructions.

**Q: How is AI affecting shelf filler jobs in Vietnam?**
A: Currently, AI has limited impact on shelf filler roles in Vietnam, as the job requires manual dexterity and adaptability to varied product shapes. However, inventory management systems and automated ordering are becoming more common, reducing manual stock counts. Full automation of shelf filling is unlikely in the near future, but technology assists with tasks like shelf labeling and inventory tracking, making the role more efficient.
