## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, hand and pedal vehicle drivers typically earn between 5 to 10 million VND per month, depending on location, hours worked, and type of vehicle. Those in major cities like Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City may earn more due to higher demand. Payments are often per trip or daily, with tips occasionally contributing.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a hand or pedal vehicle driver in Vietnam?**
A: A typical day starts early, around 5–6 AM, to catch morning commuters. Drivers navigate city streets, often using cycle rickshaws or xe ôm (motorbike taxis). They wait at stands or hail passengers, negotiating fares. Work hours can stretch 10–12 hours, with breaks during midday heat. Evening shifts cater to tourists or after-work passengers.

**Q: How is AI and technology affecting the job of hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Vietnam?**
A: AI and ride-hailing apps like Grab have impacted demand for traditional pedal drivers, especially in cities. However, many non-motorized drivers still serve tourists in historic districts. AI has not directly replaced these roles, but route optimization apps increase competition. Drivers who adopt basic smartphone use for navigation or payment can stay relevant, though long-term outlook shows gradual decline.
