## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for medical records technicians in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level medical records technicians in Vietnam earn about 8–12 million VND per month. With experience, salaries can rise to 15–25 million VND, depending on hospital size and location. Private hospitals in major cities like Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi may offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the daily responsibilities of a medical records technician in Vietnam?**
A: Daily tasks include organizing and maintaining patient health records, entering data, coding diagnoses and procedures for billing and insurance, and ensuring confidentiality. Technicians also audit records for accuracy, assist with reporting, and collaborate with healthcare staff to improve record-keeping processes.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting medical records jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI is automating routine data entry and coding, but technicians remain essential for quality control, auditing, and handling complex cases. AI tools improve efficiency, requiring technicians to develop skills in data analysis and system oversight. The role is shifting toward more analytical and supervisory tasks.
