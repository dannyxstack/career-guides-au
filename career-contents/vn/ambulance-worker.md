## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an ambulance worker in Vietnam?**
A: Ambulance workers in Vietnam earn an average monthly salary of VND 7-12 million (about $300-$520). Pay varies by experience, employer (public vs. private), and location, with urban areas like Ho Chi Minh City offering higher wages. Overtime and night shifts may add allowances.

**Q: What skills are essential for ambulance workers in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include basic life support (BLS), first aid, patient handling, and ambulance driving. Good communication and teamwork are vital, as is the ability to stay calm under pressure. Knowledge of local traffic and hospital locations is also important. Certifications from recognized training centers are often required.

**Q: What is the career outlook for ambulance workers in Vietnam?**
A: The demand for ambulance workers is growing due to an aging population and increased road traffic. Public hospitals and private ambulance services are expanding. Career advancement includes becoming a paramedic or team leader. AI may assist in dispatch but will not replace frontline roles, ensuring stable job prospects.
