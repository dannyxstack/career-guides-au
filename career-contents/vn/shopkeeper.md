## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly income for a shopkeeper in Vietnam?**
A: Income varies widely by location, product type, and shop size. Small neighborhood shops typically earn 5–10 million VND/month net profit, while shops in prime urban areas or selling high-margin goods can make 15–25 million VND. Many shopkeepers also reinvest profits into inventory.

**Q: What skills are essential for running a successful shop in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include customer service, basic financial management, inventory control, and negotiation with suppliers. Vietnamese language fluency is crucial, as is familiarity with digital tools like POS systems and e-commerce platforms to manage sales and stock efficiently.

**Q: How is AI affecting shopkeeping in Vietnam?**
A: AI is gradually influencing shopkeeping through tools like demand forecasting, automated reordering, and customer data analytics. However, most small shops still rely on manual processes. The human touch—personal recommendations and local relationships—remains vital, limiting AI's immediate disruption.
