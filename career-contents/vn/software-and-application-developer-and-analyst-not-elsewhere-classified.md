## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for software developers in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level software developers in Vietnam typically earn 10–15 million VND per month, while mid-level roles range from 20–35 million VND. Senior developers can earn 40–60 million VND or more. Salaries are higher in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, and for specialists in AI, blockchain, or cloud computing.

**Q: Which technical skills are most in demand for software developers in Vietnam?**
A: Employers in Vietnam highly value proficiency in Java, Python, JavaScript, and front-end frameworks like React or Angular. Back-end skills with Node.js, PHP, or .NET are common. Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), database management (SQL, MongoDB), and Agile practices are also key. Strong problem-solving and communication skills are essential.

**Q: What is the career outlook for software developers in Vietnam?**
A: The software development sector in Vietnam is expanding rapidly due to digital transformation and foreign investment. Demand for skilled developers is strong across fintech, e-commerce, and manufacturing. AI and automation create new opportunities but require continuous learning. Overall, the outlook is very positive for adaptable professionals.
