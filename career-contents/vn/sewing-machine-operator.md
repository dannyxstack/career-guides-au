## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a sewing machine operator in Vietnam?**
A: The average monthly salary ranges from 7 to 12 million VND, depending on experience, factory location, and skill level. Overtime pay can increase earnings significantly, especially during peak seasons in the garment industry.

**Q: What is the career outlook for sewing machine operators in Vietnam?**
A: Demand remains steady due to Vietnam's strong textile and garment sector. Experienced operators can advance to roles such as line supervisor, quality inspector, or sample maker. However, automation may reduce routine tasks, so upskilling is advised for long-term growth.

**Q: How is AI affecting sewing machine operators in Vietnam?**
A: AI-driven automation is increasingly used for cutting and simple stitching, but skilled operators are still needed for complex garments, machine maintenance, and quality control. Operators who learn to work with computerized machinery will have better job security.
