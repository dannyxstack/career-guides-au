## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a judge in Vietnam?**
A: Judges in Vietnam earn a monthly salary ranging from 8 to 20 million VND (approximately $350–$860), depending on their rank, experience, and court level. Additional allowances for seniority and responsibilities are also provided. This follows the state pay scale for civil servants.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are required to become a judge in Vietnam?**
A: To become a judge in Vietnam, candidates must hold a law degree, complete judicial training, and pass rigorous entrance exams. Essential skills include deep knowledge of Vietnamese law, analytical reasoning, impartiality, strong ethics, and the ability to manage complex cases. Experience as a prosecutor or lawyer is often required.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the role of judges in Vietnam?**
A: AI is used in Vietnamese courts for administrative tasks like case management and legal research, improving efficiency. However, judicial decision-making remains strictly human. AI tools support, not replace, judges by handling documentation and data analysis, allowing them to focus on legal reasoning and verdicts.
