## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a landscape architect in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level landscape architects earn around 8-12 million VND per month, while mid-level professionals with 3-5 years of experience can earn 15-25 million VND. Senior roles or project managers may earn 30-50 million VND or more, depending on the firm's size and project scope.

**Q: What is the career outlook for landscape architects in Vietnam?**
A: Demand is growing due to rapid urbanization and infrastructure development. Opportunities exist in public parks, resorts, residential complexes, and eco-tourism projects. Professionals with expertise in sustainable design and tropical landscapes are especially sought after. The field is competitive but offers strong potential for career advancement.

**Q: How is AI affecting the work of landscape architects in Vietnam?**
A: AI tools like generative design and parametric modeling are streamlining site analysis and conceptual planning. Software such as Land F/X and SketchUp with AI plugins help optimize irrigation and planting layouts. However, local expertise in tropical ecology and construction practices remains critical, so AI serves as a complement rather than a replacement.
