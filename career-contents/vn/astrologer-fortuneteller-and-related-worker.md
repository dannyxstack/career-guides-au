## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income for fortune-tellers and astrologers in Vietnam?**
A: Income varies widely: street fortune-tellers earn 5–15 million VND monthly, while established astrologers with private consultations can earn 20–50 million VND. Many rely on tips and donations, so earnings are irregular and depend heavily on reputation and client demand.

**Q: What skills are essential for success as a fortune-teller or astrologer in Vietnam?**
A: Deep knowledge of Vietnamese zodiac, numerology, palmistry, or tarot is required. Strong interpersonal and communication skills build client trust. Many learn through apprenticeship or family tradition. Basic business skills help manage clients and promote services, especially on social media.

**Q: What is the career outlook for fortune-telling and astrology in Vietnam?**
A: The field remains culturally popular during festivals and life events, but demand is steady rather than growing. Younger generations are more skeptical, and free online horoscopes compete with paid services. Success often depends on specialization and maintaining a loyal client base.
