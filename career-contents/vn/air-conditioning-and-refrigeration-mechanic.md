## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level mechanics earn around 8–12 million VND per month, while experienced technicians can make 15–25 million VND. Senior or specialized roles (e.g., commercial refrigeration) may reach 30 million VND or more. Salaries vary by region, with Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering higher pay, and are often supplemented by overtime or project bonuses.

**Q: What skills are essential for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in refrigerant handling, electrical troubleshooting, brazing, and system diagnostics. Familiarity with common brands like Daikin, LG, and Panasonic is important. Vocational certification (e.g., from LILAMA or HCM City Vocational College) is highly valued. Soft skills such as customer service and time management are also crucial for self-employed or service team roles.

**Q: How is AI impacting the air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic profession in Vietnam?**
A: AI-powered diagnostic tools and smart HVAC systems are becoming more common, helping mechanics identify issues faster. However, hands-on installation, maintenance, and repair remain irreplaceable. AI does not reduce demand for skilled technicians in Vietnam, as many systems require manual intervention. Continuous upskilling in smart system integration is advised to stay competitive.
