## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for film directors and producers in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, film directors earn between VND 20-60 million per month for TV and commercial work, while feature film directors may negotiate project-based fees from VND 100 million to over VND 1 billion. Producers' salaries vary widely, with experienced ones earning VND 30-80 million monthly or higher for major projects.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a film director or producer in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include strong storytelling and vision, leadership to guide cast and crew, and deep understanding of Vietnamese culture and audience preferences. Producers need negotiation, financial management, and networking abilities. Both require adaptability to tight schedules and limited budgets, as well as proficiency in Vietnamese and English for international co-productions.

**Q: How is AI affecting film and stage directing and producing in Vietnam?**
A: AI is increasingly used in script analysis, pre-visualization, and post-production tasks like editing and color grading. However, creative direction, character development, and nuanced storytelling remain irreplaceable human skills. Vietnamese directors and producers who embrace AI tools can enhance efficiency, but the profession's core demands human judgment and cultural insight.
