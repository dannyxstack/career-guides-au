## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes and related workers in Vietnam?**
A: Monthly salaries for scribes in Vietnam range from 5 to 12 million VND depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 5-7 million VND, while experienced scribes working for law firms or government offices can earn 10-12 million VND. Freelance scribes may charge per project.

**Q: What key skills do scribes need in Vietnam today?**
A: Scribes in Vietnam need excellent Vietnamese typing speed (at least 40 words per minute), strong listening and comprehension skills for accurate transcription, and attention to detail. Familiarity with legal, medical, or business terminology is valuable. Basic computer proficiency, including word processing software and transcription tools, is essential.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of scribes in Vietnam?**
A: AI-powered transcription tools are increasingly used for initial drafts, but human scribes remain essential for accuracy, especially in complex legal or medical contexts. AI helps with speed but struggles with accents, dialects, and specialized terms. Scribes who adapt by editing AI output or specializing in high-accuracy roles will remain in demand.
