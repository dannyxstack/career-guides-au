## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn about 8–12 million VND per month, while experienced professionals can earn 20–30 million VND. Salaries are higher in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi. Certifications like CCNA or CompTIA Network+ often lead to better pay.

**Q: What key skills are required for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Vietnam?**
A: Strong knowledge of network protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP), hands-on experience with routers, switches, and firewalls, and proficiency in Windows/Linux server administration are essential. Soft skills like problem-solving and communication are also valued. Certifications such as CCNA, MCSA, or CompTIA Security+ boost employability.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates routine network monitoring and fault detection, reducing manual tasks. However, technicians are increasingly needed for complex troubleshooting, security analysis, and AI system integration. Upskilling in AI-based network management tools and cybersecurity is becoming important to stay relevant in Vietnam’s evolving IT landscape.
