## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Vietnam?**
A: Monthly earnings range from 4 to 7 million VND depending on location, employer, and full-time vs. contract work. Public cleaners in cities like Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City may earn slightly more. Overtime and holiday work can increase pay, but it remains near minimum wage levels.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a sweeper in Vietnam?**
A: No formal education is needed, but physical stamina, attention to detail, and ability to work early mornings or late nights are essential. Basic safety awareness for handling waste and using cleaning tools is beneficial. Employers may provide brief on-job training for specific equipment like dustpans, brooms, or small refuse vehicles.

**Q: Will AI or automation replace sweepers in Vietnam?**
A: Automation, such as robotic street sweepers, is emerging in some modern districts but remains rare due to high costs and narrow streets. Manual sweeping is still needed for alleys, markets, and areas with heavy foot traffic. AI is unlikely to fully replace human workers in the next decade, though tasks may shift to more supervisory roles.
