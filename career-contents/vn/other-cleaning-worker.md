## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaning workers in Vietnam?**
A: The average monthly salary for cleaning workers in Vietnam ranges from 5 to 8 million VND (approximately $210 to $340 USD), depending on experience, location, and employer. Salaries are often higher in major cities like Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City, and additional benefits such as meals or transportation allowances may be provided.

**Q: What are the day-to-day tasks of a cleaning worker in Vietnam?**
A: Cleaning workers in Vietnam are responsible for sweeping, mopping, dusting, and disinfecting surfaces in offices, hotels, or public spaces. They empty trash bins, clean restrooms, and restock supplies. Tasks may also include window cleaning, carpet shampooing, and waste sorting. Work schedules can be early mornings, evenings, or weekends.

**Q: What skills are essential for cleaning workers in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include attention to detail, time management, and the ability to follow safety protocols for chemical use. Physical stamina and reliability are important. Basic literacy to read cleaning product labels and communicate with supervisors is helpful. Experience with floor care equipment or eco-friendly cleaning methods can be an advantage.
