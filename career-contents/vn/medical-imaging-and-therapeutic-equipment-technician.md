## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for medical imaging technicians in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around 8-12 million VND per month in public hospitals, while experienced professionals in private clinics can earn 15-25 million VND. Specialists in MRI or CT earn higher, up to 30 million VND. Overtime and on-call duties often add 20-30% to base pay.

**Q: What technical skills are most important for this role in Vietnam?**
A: Proficiency in operating X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound machines is essential. Radiographers must know radiation safety protocols, image post-processing software (e.g., PACS), and basic equipment maintenance. Vietnamese technicians also need competence in English for reading manuals and communicating with foreign doctors.

**Q: How is AI impacting medical imaging technicians in Vietnam?**
A: AI tools assist in image analysis, reducing time for routine scans like chest X-rays. However, technicians remain vital for positioning patients, ensuring image quality, and troubleshooting. AI reduces workload but requires tech-savvy adaption. In Vietnam, AI adoption is growing in urban hospitals, but rural areas still rely on manual expertise.
