## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for personnel and careers professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level HR specialists earn around 10-15 million VND per month, while senior managers can earn 40-60 million VND. In major cities like Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, pay is higher. Bonuses and benefits are common, especially in multinational companies.

**Q: What are the day-to-day responsibilities of a personnel and careers professional in Vietnam?**
A: Daily tasks include recruiting, interviewing, and onboarding new hires, managing employee records, processing payroll, and handling labor law compliance. They also coordinate training, performance reviews, and employee relations. Career paths lead to HR manager, talent acquisition lead, or HR director roles.

**Q: How is AI impacting personnel and careers jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates resume screening, interview scheduling, and basic HR queries, but human judgment remains key for interviewing, culture fit, and legal compliance. Professionals must adapt by learning to use HR analytics tools and AI-based recruitment platforms, enhancing efficiency and decision-making.
