## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for building architects in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level architects earn 8–15 million VND per month, mid-level 20–35 million VND, and senior architects 40–60 million VND. Compensation varies by experience, firm size, and project complexity. Overtime is common, especially during deadlines.

**Q: What key skills are essential for building architects in Vietnam?**
A: Proficiency in AutoCAD, Revit, and SketchUp is critical. Architects must understand Vietnamese building codes and regulations. Strong design creativity, project management, and communication skills are needed. Continuous learning of new software and sustainable design practices is essential.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of building architects in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates drafting and preliminary design, accelerating project timelines. Architects now need to master BIM and AI tools for efficient modeling. While AI enhances visualization and analysis, human creativity, contextual judgment, and regulatory expertise remain irreplaceable.
