## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships' deck officers in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level deck officers earn around VND 15-25 million per month, while experienced chief officers and captains can earn VND 40-80 million. Salaries vary by vessel type, company, and rank. Offshore and international routes often pay higher, with additional allowances for overtime and sea service.

**Q: What certifications and skills are required to work as a deck officer in Vietnam?**
A: Required certifications include a Certificate of Competency (COC) from the Vietnam Maritime Administration, STCW endorsements, and a valid medical fitness certificate. Key skills include navigation, ship handling, cargo operations, and English communication. Additional training in electronic chart display and information systems (ECDIS) is increasingly expected.

**Q: What is the career outlook for ships' deck officers in Vietnam?**
A: The outlook is stable due to Vietnam's growing maritime trade and shipping industry. Demand remains steady for qualified officers, though competition for senior ranks is moderate. Seafarers with advanced certifications and experience on specialized vessels (e.g., LNG tankers) have better prospects. Automation may reduce crew sizes but will not eliminate the need for skilled officers.
