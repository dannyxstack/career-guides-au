## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for door-to-door salespersons in Vietnam?**
A: Door-to-door salespersons in Vietnam often earn a base salary plus commission. Average monthly income ranges from 5–15 million VND, depending on sales performance and product type. Commission can significantly boost earnings, especially for high-value items like insurance or telecom services. Some companies also provide bonuses for meeting targets.

**Q: What skills are essential for success as a door-to-door salesperson in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include strong communication and persuasion, resilience to handle rejection, and local language proficiency (Vietnamese). Product knowledge and negotiation abilities are crucial. Salespersons should be self-motivated, organized, and comfortable with walking long distances. Adaptability to different customer attitudes is important.

**Q: How is AI impacting door-to-door sales roles in Vietnam?**
A: AI is changing customer targeting and lead generation but has not replaced face-to-face selling in Vietnam. Salespersons use AI-powered CRM tools to manage contacts and track performance. However, personal interaction remains essential, especially in rural areas where digital adoption is lower. AI enhances efficiency but human touch is still valued.
