## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for secondary education teachers in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, secondary teachers earn between 8 to 15 million VND per month, varying by experience and school type (public vs. private). Public school salaries are lower but include benefits like health insurance. Many teachers supplement income through tutoring, though regulations apply.

**Q: What skills are most important for secondary teachers in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include strong subject expertise, classroom management, and adaptability to the evolving national curriculum. Digital literacy is increasingly vital as schools adopt e-learning tools. Bilingual ability (Vietnamese and English) is highly valued, especially in international or bilingual schools.

**Q: How is AI affecting secondary education teaching in Vietnam?**
A: AI is being used for personalized learning platforms and automated grading, reducing administrative burdens. However, teachers remain essential for mentorship and interactive instruction. AI literacy is becoming a required skill, as teachers must integrate these tools effectively while preserving the human element of education.
