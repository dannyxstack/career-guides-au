## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level professionals earn around 15-20 million VND per month, while those with 5+ years of experience can earn 30-50 million VND. Senior roles in banking or insurance may exceed 70 million VND. Salaries are highest in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi.

**Q: What technical skills are most in demand for statisticians and actuaries in Vietnam?**
A: Proficiency in statistical software like R, Python, and SAS is essential. Actuaries need strong knowledge of actuarial science and passing relevant exams (e.g., SOA or CAS). Additionally, skills in machine learning, data visualization, and SQL are increasingly sought after in finance and tech sectors.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the careers of mathematicians and statisticians in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates routine data analysis and modeling, but professionals are still needed to interpret results, validate models, and provide strategic insights. The demand for hybrid skills in both statistics and AI/ML is growing, especially in fintech and banking. Continuous learning is key.
