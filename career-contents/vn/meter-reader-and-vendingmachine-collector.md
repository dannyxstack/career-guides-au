## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Vietnam?**
A: The average monthly salary ranges from 5 to 8 million VND for entry-level positions, with experienced workers earning up to 12 million VND. Pay can vary by region and employer, with urban areas often offering slightly higher wages. Additional allowances for travel or overtime may apply.

**Q: What are the typical daily duties of a meter reader or vending-machine collector in Vietnam?**
A: Duties include visiting residential or commercial premises to record utility meter readings, inspecting for damage or tampering, and collecting coins or bills from vending machines. They also handle basic maintenance, report issues, and ensure accurate data entry. The work involves walking or driving between locations, often outdoors.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Vietnam?**
A: AI and automated meter reading (AMR) systems are gradually reducing manual meter reading jobs, but many areas still rely on manual checks due to infrastructure limitations. Vending machines increasingly use cashless payments, but coin collection remains needed. Workers may need to adapt to hybrid roles involving data verification and equipment maintenance.
