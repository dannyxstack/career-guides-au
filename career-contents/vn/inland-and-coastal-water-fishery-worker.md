## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual net casting and retrieval operations: automatic nets and robotic arms can handle this
- Manual sorting and weighing of catch: machine vision and automatic grading systems
- Manual navigation and obstacle avoidance for fishing vessels: GPS and autopilot systems
- Visual monitoring of fishery resources: drones and remote sensing technology
- Hand repair of fishing gear: automatic repair equipment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using drone and satellite data to identify fish locations, improving fishing efficiency
- Real-time monitoring of environmental parameters such as water quality and temperature via IoT sensors
- Use data analysis to optimize navigation routes and working hours
- Using machine vision to assist in catch quality grading and inventory management
- Using digital platforms for sustainable fishery certification and traceability

**Human moat:**

- Ability to repair and make emergency decisions for sudden mechanical failures
- Safe judgment and operations under complex weather and sea conditions
- Local ecological knowledge and traditional fishing experience
- Compliance communication with regulatory authorities and environmental organizations
- Manual processing of high-value catch (e.g., rare fish)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for inland and coastal waters fishery workers in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level workers earn around 5–8 million VND per month, while experienced workers can earn 10–15 million VND. Earnings often depend on catch volume, season, and whether workers are self-employed or employed by a company. In peak seasons, bonuses or profit-sharing can increase total pay.

**Q: What skills are essential for fishery workers in Vietnam's inland and coastal waters?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of local fishing techniques, handling nets and traps, boat operation and basic maintenance, fish species identification, and safety procedures. Physical stamina, teamwork, and ability to work in varying weather conditions are also critical. Experience with sustainable practices is increasingly valued.

**Q: What is the career outlook for inland and coastal waters fishery workers in Vietnam?**
A: The demand remains stable due to domestic consumption and export markets, though overfishing and environmental changes pose challenges. Modernization and aquaculture expansion may reduce some traditional fishing jobs, but skilled workers in sustainable and eco-friendly fishing are likely to find steady opportunities. Government support for fisheries helps maintain the sector.
