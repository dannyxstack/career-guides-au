## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Vietnam?**
A: Monthly earnings typically range from 7 to 15 million VND, depending on experience, industry, and location. Skilled workers in specialized crafts like traditional woodworking or metal engraving may earn higher rates, especially in craft villages. Entry-level positions often start around 5-6 million VND.

**Q: What key skills are needed for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include manual dexterity, attention to detail, and knowledge of traditional or specialized techniques (e.g., lacquerware, ceramics, or silk weaving). Problem-solving, material familiarity, and basic tool maintenance are also important. Apprenticeships or training at craft villages are common pathways to develop these skills.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting craft and related workers in Vietnam?**
A: AI and automation have limited impact on highly specialized, handcrafted work. Demand for unique, culturally significant products (e.g., in tourism or export markets) remains stable. However, routine assembly tasks may face automation pressure. Skilled artisans who adapt by blending traditional techniques with modern designs can maintain strong career prospects.
