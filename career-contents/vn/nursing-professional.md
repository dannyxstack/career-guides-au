## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for nursing professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level nurses earn 6-10 million VND/month, while experienced nurses in major cities can earn 15-25 million VND. Salaries vary by location (urban vs rural), hospital type (public vs private), and specialization. Overtime and night shift allowances are common, and salaries typically increase with experience and additional certifications.

**Q: What core skills are essential for nursing professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Nurses need strong clinical skills: patient assessment, medication administration, wound care, and emergency response. Soft skills like empathy, communication, and teamwork are vital. Adaptability to high-pressure settings and basic English proficiency are increasingly valued, especially in international hospitals or roles requiring collaboration with foreign colleagues.

**Q: How is AI affecting nursing careers in Vietnam, and what is the career outlook?**
A: AI is streamlining documentation and diagnostic support, but it complements rather than replaces nurses. Demand remains strong due to an aging population and healthcare expansion. Nurses who adopt health informatics skills gain a competitive edge. Overall, the profession offers stable employment with growing recognition and opportunities for specialization.
