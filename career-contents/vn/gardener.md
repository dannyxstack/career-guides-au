## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a gardener in Vietnam?**
A: Gardeners in Vietnam earn an average monthly salary of 5-10 million VND, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 4 million VND, while skilled gardeners in urban areas or specialized gardens can earn up to 15 million VND. Many also receive bonuses during peak seasons.

**Q: What key skills are required for gardeners in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include knowledge of tropical plants, pruning, pest control, and soil management. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and familiarity with irrigation systems and basic gardening tools are crucial. Language skills (Vietnamese) and customer service are important for those working in resorts or private gardens.

**Q: How is AI impacting gardening jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI has limited impact on basic gardening tasks but is emerging in automated irrigation, drone monitoring for pest detection, and robotic lawn mowers. These tools assist rather than replace gardeners, who still require manual skills for pruning, planting, and landscape design. AI creates opportunities for tech-savvy gardeners to manage smarter systems.
