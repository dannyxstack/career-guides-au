## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fashion models in Vietnam?**
A: Earnings vary widely based on experience, agency, and project type. New models may earn 5-15 million VND per assignment, while established faces can make 30-100+ million VND for major campaigns or runway shows. Most models are freelancers paid per job, with top clients in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering higher rates. Monthly income is inconsistent.

**Q: What is the career outlook for fashion models in Vietnam?**
A: The fashion industry in Vietnam is growing rapidly, with increased demand for local models in domestic advertising, e-commerce, and international brands entering the market. Competition remains fierce, and careers are often short. Success requires a strong portfolio, networking, and adaptability. Social media presence is increasingly important for securing work.

**Q: How is AI impacting modeling jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI-generated virtual models and augmented reality are being used for e-commerce and lookbooks, reducing demand for entry-level product modeling. However, human models are still needed for high-fashion editorials, runway shows, and campaigns requiring emotional expression. AI also creates new opportunities, such as modeling for virtual fashion shows, but overall it may limit routine catalog work.
