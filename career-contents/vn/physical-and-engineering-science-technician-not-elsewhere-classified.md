## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around 10-15 million VND per month, while experienced professionals can make 20-35 million VND. Salaries vary by industry—electronics and automotive sectors tend to pay higher—and location, with Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering premium pay.

**Q: What key skills are required for Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Vietnam?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in operating and maintaining laboratory or testing equipment, knowledge of ISO quality standards, basic data analysis, and familiarity with industry software (e.g., CAD, LabVIEW). Vietnamese fluency is essential, and English skills are increasingly valued due to multinational clients.

**Q: How is AI impacting Physical and Engineering Science Technician roles in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates routine data processing and testing, reducing manual work. Technicians must adapt by learning to interpret AI-generated results and maintain advanced equipment. However, hands-on tasks like troubleshooting and calibration remain critical, so the role is evolving rather than disappearing.
