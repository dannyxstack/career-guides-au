## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for database and network professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level professionals earn VND 10-15 million/month; those with 3-5 years experience earn VND 20-35 million; senior roles can reach VND 50-70 million. Salaries vary by industry, with finance and tech firms paying higher. Certifications like AWS, CISSP, or Oracle boost earning potential.

**Q: How is AI impacting database and network roles in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates routine tasks like database tuning, anomaly detection, and network optimization, reducing manual work. Professionals now focus more on AI model integration, data governance, and complex problem-solving. Upskilling in AI/ML tools is becoming essential to stay competitive in Vietnam's evolving tech landscape.

**Q: What is the career outlook for database and network professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Demand remains strong due to digital transformation across industries like banking, e-commerce, and manufacturing. Skills in cloud services, cybersecurity, and data analytics are especially valued. Continuous learning is key, as roles evolve with technology—offering good long-term prospects for adaptable professionals.
