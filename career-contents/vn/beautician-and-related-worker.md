## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, beauticians earn between 7-15 million VND per month depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers start around 7-9 million, while senior beauticians in high-end salons in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City can earn 15-20 million. Tips and commissions on products may add 2-5 million monthly.

**Q: How is AI affecting beauticians in Vietnam?**
A: AI influences through virtual try-on tools and skin analysis apps, but face-to-face services remain essential. AI assists with personalized recommendations but cannot replace manual skills like hairstyling, makeup, or nail art. Beauticians who adopt digital tools for client management may gain an edge.

**Q: What is the career outlook for beauticians in Vietnam?**
A: The beauty industry in Vietnam is growing steadily due to rising disposable income and demand for personal grooming. Opportunities exist in salons, spas, and freelance work. Competition is high, but skilled beauticians with certifications in advanced techniques (e.g., eyelash extensions, waxing) have good prospects.
