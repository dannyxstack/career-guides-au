## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level professionals earn around 8-12 million VND per month, while experienced practitioners in private clinics can earn 15-25 million VND. Salaries vary by region, with Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering higher pay. Additional income from selling herbs or supplements is common.

**Q: What are typical daily tasks for traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Vietnam?**
A: They diagnose imbalances using pulse and tongue analysis, prescribe herbal remedies, perform acupuncture or moxibustion, and advise on diet and qigong exercises. Many work in government hospitals, private clinics, or run their own practices. Record-keeping and sourcing quality herbs are also routine tasks.

**Q: What is the career outlook for traditional and complementary medicine professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Demand is growing due to rising health awareness and government support for integrating traditional medicine into public healthcare. Opportunities exist in hospitals, wellness centers, and self-employment. However, competition is stiff in cities, and formal certification is increasingly required to practice legally.
