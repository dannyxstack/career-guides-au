**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a garment patternmaker or cutter in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, experienced patternmakers and cutters in garment factories earn between 8–15 million VND per month, depending on skill level, location, and factory size. Entry-level workers may start at 5–7 million VND. Overtime pay is common during peak production seasons, boosting total income.

**Q: What are the most important skills for garment patternmakers and cutters in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in pattern drafting software (e.g., Gerber, Lectra), manual cutting precision, fabric knowledge, and pattern grading. Attention to detail, physical stamina for long standing hours, and teamwork are crucial. Experience with industrial sewing and quality control also adds value.

**Q: How is AI impacting garment patternmaking and cutting jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI-driven tools automate pattern grading, nesting, and cutting to reduce waste and speed production. However, skilled patternmakers are still needed for complex adjustments, fitting corrections, and creative design. The outlook remains stable for workers who adapt to digital tools and focus on high-value tasks.
