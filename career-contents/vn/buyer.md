## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Buyer in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level buyers in Vietnam earn around 8-12 million VND per month, while experienced buyers can earn 20-35 million VND. Senior buyers or managers may earn 40-60 million VND monthly. Salaries vary by industry, with higher pay in manufacturing, retail, and e-commerce sectors.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as a Buyer in Vietnam?**
A: Buyers need strong negotiation and analytical skills to source quality products at competitive prices. Proficiency in English and Chinese is highly valued due to international suppliers. Knowledge of local market trends and supply chain logistics, plus proficiency with ERP systems, are also essential.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Buyers in Vietnam?**
A: AI automates tasks like data analysis, demand forecasting, and supplier comparisons, allowing buyers to focus on strategic negotiations and relationship building. While routine sourcing may decline, demand for tech-savvy buyers who can interpret AI insights and manage supplier partnerships is growing, especially in Vietnam's expanding manufacturing and retail sectors.
