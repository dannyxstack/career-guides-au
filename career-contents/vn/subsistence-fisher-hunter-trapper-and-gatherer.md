## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence fishers, hunters, trappers, and gatherers in Vietnam?**
A: Income is highly variable and often non-monetary, as these activities primarily provide food for personal consumption. Earnings from the sale of surplus fish, game, or forest products can range from VND 500,000 to 2,000,000 per month (approximately $20–80 USD), depending on location, season, and market access.

**Q: What skills are essential for subsistence fishing and gathering in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of local ecosystems, animal behavior, and seasonal patterns; proficiency with traditional tools such as nets, traps, and hunting weapons; physical stamina and survival skills; and the ability to process and preserve catch or harvest. Generational knowledge and adaptability to environmental changes are also critical.

**Q: What is the career outlook for subsistence fishers, hunters, trappers, and gatherers in Vietnam?**
A: The outlook is declining due to overfishing, habitat loss, and stricter regulations on wildlife harvesting. Many practitioners are shifting to alternative livelihoods as younger generations move to urban areas. However, in remote rural and mountainous regions, subsistence activities remain essential for food security and cultural traditions.
