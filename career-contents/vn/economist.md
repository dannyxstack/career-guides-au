## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an economist in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level economists in Vietnam earn about 15-25 million VND per month. Mid-career professionals with 5-10 years of experience make 30-50 million VND, while senior economists in finance or consulting can earn 60-100 million VND. Salaries vary by sector, with private firms and international organizations offering higher pay than academia or government.

**Q: What key skills are required for economists in Vietnam?**
A: Economists in Vietnam need strong analytical and quantitative skills, proficiency in statistical software (Stata, R, Python), and a deep understanding of local economic policies and data. Bilingual fluency in Vietnamese and English is often required. Data visualization, econometric modeling, and knowledge of behavioral economics are increasingly valued.

**Q: How is AI impacting the economics profession in Vietnam?**
A: AI is automating data collection and basic modeling, allowing economists to focus on strategic analysis and policy recommendations. Demand for machine learning and big data skills is rising. Economists who adapt to these tools can boost productivity, while traditional methods may become less competitive. Continuous upskilling is essential to stay relevant.
