## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Information and Communications Technology Services Managers in Vietnam?**
A: The average monthly salary for ICT Services Managers in Vietnam ranges from 40 to 70 million VND, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior managers at multinational corporations in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi may earn over 100 million VND per month, while those at local firms typically earn less.

**Q: What skills are essential for ICT Services Managers in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include strong technical knowledge of IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, and cloud services; project management expertise (e.g., PMP); leadership and communication abilities; and familiarity with Vietnam’s digital transformation landscape. Fluency in English is often required, especially in multinational companies. Continuous learning is vital due to rapid tech changes.

**Q: How is the career outlook for ICT Services Managers in Vietnam, considering AI impact?**
A: The outlook is positive due to Vietnam’s growing digital economy and government push for Industry 4.0. AI may automate routine tasks like network monitoring, but human oversight for strategy, vendor management, and innovation remains crucial. Managers who upskill in AI, data analytics, and cybersecurity will stay in high demand.
