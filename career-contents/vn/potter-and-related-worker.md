## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use a potter's wheel or molds for batch forming of standardized vessels
- Mechanical reproduction of basic carving and patterns
- Automated monitoring and adjustment of kiln temperature and time
- Automatic calculation and mixing of glaze formulas
- Visual inspection of dimensions and defects in product quality inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quickly design complex ceramic shapes using 3D modeling software
- Use AI to generate glaze formulas and pattern ideas
- Use AR technology to show customers virtual finished ceramic products
- Analyze market data to optimize product design and pricing

**Human moat:**

- Irreproducible personal style and artistic expression in handmade pottery
- Ability for manual adjustments to handle customized, non-standard requirements.
- Deep perception and control over clay material properties
- Inheritance and innovation of traditional craft culture

**Skills to build:**

- 3D modeling and digital sculpting (e.g., Blender, Rhino)
- AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Midjourney for creative prototyping)
- Digital manufacturing equipment operation (3D ceramic printer)
- Social media marketing and online portfolio presentation
- Deepening of traditional handicraft skills (e.g., wheel throwing, painting)
- Customer communication and customised service skills

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a potter in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, potters in traditional craft villages like Bat Trang earn an average monthly income of 8-15 million VND depending on skill level and workshop. Experienced artisans may earn up to 20 million VND. Income can vary based on product type and sales channel, with export-quality ceramics commanding higher pay.

**Q: What key skills are needed to become a successful potter in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include hand-building and wheel-throwing techniques, knowledge of local clay types and glazes, kiln operation, and design creativity. Attention to detail and patience are crucial. Familiarity with traditional Vietnamese motifs is advantageous, and increasingly, digital skills for marketing and e-commerce are valuable for reaching customers.

**Q: How is AI and technology affecting pottery work in Vietnam?**
A: AI and automation are impacting mass-produced ceramics through automated glazing and molding, but handcrafted pottery remains valued. Artisans who combine traditional skills with digital design tools or 3D printing for prototyping can innovate. The demand for unique, handmade items persists, especially in tourism and exports, so skilled potters who adapt to new tools have stable prospects.
