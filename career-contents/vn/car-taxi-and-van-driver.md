## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for car, taxi, and van drivers in Vietnam?**
A: The average monthly income ranges from 10 to 20 million VND, depending on the city, hours worked, and vehicle ownership. Drivers in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi typically earn more due to higher demand. Ride-hail drivers may earn 10-15 million VND after expenses, while taxi company drivers average 12-18 million VND.

**Q: How will self-driving cars affect driving jobs in Vietnam?**
A: Self-driving technology is still experimental in Vietnam due to chaotic traffic, narrow streets, and limited infrastructure. Widespread adoption is unlikely within the next 10–15 years. Drivers remain essential for navigating complex urban conditions. However, AI-based dispatching and navigation apps are already used, improving efficiency but not replacing drivers.

**Q: What skills are essential for car, taxi, and van drivers in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include a valid driving license for the relevant vehicle class (e.g., B2 for taxis), deep knowledge of local routes and traffic patterns, basic vehicle maintenance, customer service orientation, and good health. English proficiency is a plus for drivers serving tourists. Familiarity with ride-hailing apps is increasingly important for independent drivers.
