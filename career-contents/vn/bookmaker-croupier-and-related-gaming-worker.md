## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a croupier in Vietnam?**
A: Croupiers in Vietnam typically earn 10-15 million VND per month (about $430-650 USD), depending on experience and casino location. Larger casinos in Ho Chi Minh City or Da Nang may offer higher pay plus tips. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced dealers can earn more. Live dealer roles in online casinos also provide competitive compensation.

**Q: What skills are required to become a croupier in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include strong numeracy, manual dexterity for handling cards and chips, excellent customer service, and knowledge of casino games like blackjack, poker, and roulette. Vietnamese language fluency is essential, while English is beneficial in international casinos. Attention to detail and integrity are critical to prevent errors or fraud.

**Q: How is AI affecting croupier jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI and automation are used in online betting platforms and self-service kiosks, but live dealer games remain popular in physical casinos. AI assists with betting calculations and fairness monitoring, but human croupiers are still needed for social interaction and oversight. Job displacement is limited, though some routine tasks may become automated.
