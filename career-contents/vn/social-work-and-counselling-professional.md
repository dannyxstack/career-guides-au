## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work and counselling professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level positions earn around VND 8-12 million per month, while experienced professionals in NGOs or private practice can earn VND 20-40 million. Salaries vary by sector: government roles pay less (VND 6-10 million), and international organizations offer higher packages, often including benefits.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as a social work or counselling professional in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include active listening, empathy, cultural sensitivity to local norms, and fluency in Vietnamese (English is a plus). Knowledge of Vietnamese social welfare policies, family dynamics, and trauma-informed care is crucial. Strong communication and ethical decision-making are also vital.

**Q: What is the career outlook for social work and counselling professionals in Vietnam?**
A: Demand is growing due to rising mental health awareness, urbanization, and government focus on social welfare. Opportunities exist in NGOs, hospitals, schools, and private practice. However, the profession is still developing, and certification pathways are evolving. AI impact remains low as human interaction is core.
