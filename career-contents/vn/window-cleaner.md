## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for window cleaners in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level window cleaners in Vietnam earn around 5-8 million VND per month, while experienced cleaners in major cities like Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi can earn 10-15 million VND. Some high-rise building specialists may earn more due to risk and skill requirements. Pay often depends on the employer, location, and contract type (full-time vs. freelance).

**Q: What skills or training are required to become a window cleaner in Vietnam?**
A: Formal education is not required, but physical fitness, comfort with heights, and attention to detail are essential. On-the-job training is common. For high-rise cleaning, certification in safety harness use and scaffolding may be needed. Basic knowledge of cleaning chemicals and equipment is helpful. Many workers learn through apprenticeship or short vocational courses.

**Q: How is AI or automation affecting window cleaning jobs in Vietnam?**
A: AI impact on window cleaning in Vietnam is minimal. While automated window cleaning robots exist, they are expensive and mainly used in new high-tech buildings. Most commercial and residential cleaning still relies on manual labor. However, simple drones may reduce demand for high-risk manual cleaning on very tall structures, but overall, human cleaners remain essential for thorough, flexible work.
