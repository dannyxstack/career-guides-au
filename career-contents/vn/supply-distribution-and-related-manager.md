## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, supply and distribution managers earn an average monthly salary of 30-50 million VND (approximately $1,300-$2,200 USD), depending on experience and company size. Senior roles in multinational corporations or large logistics firms can command higher pay, reaching up to 70 million VND monthly.

**Q: What key skills are required for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Vietnam?**
A: Key skills include supply chain management expertise, proficiency in inventory planning and logistics software (e.g., SAP, Oracle), strong negotiation and vendor management abilities, and knowledge of Vietnamese customs regulations. Fluency in English is often required for roles in international firms, along with leadership and problem-solving skills.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Vietnam?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks like demand forecasting and route optimization, allowing managers to focus on strategic decisions. In Vietnam, adoption is growing in large logistics and e-commerce companies. Managers need to upskill in data analytics and AI tools to stay competitive, as AI enhances efficiency but does not replace human oversight.
