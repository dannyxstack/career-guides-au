**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted personalised learning path design and adaptive assessment

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a university lecturer in Vietnam?**
A: In Vietnam, public university lecturers earn a base salary of 5-15 million VND per month, plus allowances. Private institutions and foreign-language programs may pay 30-50 million VND. Researchers with grants can supplement income. Salaries vary by rank, experience, and institution type.

**Q: How is AI impacting university teaching in Vietnam?**
A: AI is used for tools like automated grading and personalized learning, but teachers remain essential for fostering critical thinking and mentorship. Universities are integrating digital platforms, requiring lecturers to adapt to new technologies. AI enhances efficiency but does not replace the core teaching role.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for university teachers in Vietnam?**
A: Career progression follows academic ranks: lecturer, senior lecturer, associate professor, and professor. Advancement requires publications, teaching experience, and service. Opportunities also exist in research, curriculum development, and administrative roles. The demand for higher education teachers remains stable with growing student enrollment.
