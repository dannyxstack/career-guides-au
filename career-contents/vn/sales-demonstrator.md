## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Vietnam?**
A: Sales demonstrators in Vietnam typically earn between 5-10 million VND per month, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 5 million VND, while those with 2-3 years of experience can earn up to 10 million VND. Commission and bonuses are common, especially in retail sectors like electronics or cosmetics.

**Q: What is the career outlook for Sales Demonstrators in Vietnam?**
A: The demand for sales demonstrators in Vietnam is steady, driven by retail growth and promotional events. Opportunities exist in malls, supermarkets, and brand activations. Career progression includes roles like senior demonstrator, team leader, or brand ambassador. However, automation and self-checkout may reduce demand in some areas, so adaptability is key.

**Q: How is AI impacting Sales Demonstrator roles in Vietnam?**
A: AI is expected to have limited impact on sales demonstrators in Vietnam, as the role relies on human interaction and persuasion. However, digital tools like AI-powered customer data analysis help demonstrators tailor pitches. Physical demonstration and live engagement remain irreplaceable, though automated kiosks may reduce demand for simple product showcases.
