**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Calendar management and meeting scheduling (AI scheduling tools automatically coordinate times)
- Basic document processing (e.g., formatting documents, generating email templates)
- Data entry and information retrieval (natural language queries replace manual operations)
- Meeting minutes and summary generation (speech-to-text + AI summary)
- Travel booking and expense reimbursement (automated system processing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Executive communication strategy suggestions (AI analyzes email tone, priority, aids decision-making)
- Project progress tracking and early warning (AI integrates multi-source data, automatically flags risks)
- Information organization in cross-departmental coordination (AI extracts key points, assists reporting)
- Data analysis report writing (AI generates charts and initial insights for manual optimization)
- Emergency response (AI provides historical cases and contingency plans)

**Human moat:**

- Maintaining complex interpersonal relationships and emotional support
- Judgment and political sensitivity in high-level decision-making
- Confidential information handling and ethical judgement
- Creative problem-solving for unstructured problems
- Flexible adaptability in cross-cultural and cross-level communication

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for administrative and executive secretaries in Vietnam?**
A: Salaries for administrative and executive secretaries in Vietnam vary by experience and industry. Entry-level positions earn approximately 8-12 million VND per month, while experienced secretaries in multinational companies or senior roles can earn 15-25 million VND per month. Additional bonuses and benefits may be offered.

**Q: What key skills are required to succeed as an administrative or executive secretary in Vietnam?**
A: Essential skills include strong organizational abilities, proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), excellent written and verbal communication in both Vietnamese and English, attention to detail, and discretion when handling confidential information. Time management and problem-solving skills are also highly valued.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the role of secretaries in Vietnam?**
A: AI tools are automating routine tasks like scheduling, email filtering, and document formatting, reducing clerical workload. However, secretaries are increasingly focusing on higher-value responsibilities such as project coordination and stakeholder management. Adaptability and digital literacy are becoming crucial to leverage AI effectively.
