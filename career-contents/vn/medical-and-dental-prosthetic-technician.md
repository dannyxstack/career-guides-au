**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical and dental prosthetic technician in Vietnam?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around 8-12 million VND per month, while experienced professionals can earn 15-25 million VND. Specialists in dental prosthetics or orthotics may earn higher, especially in private clinics in major cities like Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi. Salaries can vary based on qualifications, laboratory type, and patient volume.

**Q: What skills are essential for medical and dental prosthetic technicians in Vietnam?**
A: Technicians need manual dexterity, attention to detail, and knowledge of dental anatomy and materials. Proficiency in CAD/CAM software and 3D printing is increasingly important. Good communication with dentists and patients is required. Additionally, understanding Vietnamese healthcare regulations and infection control standards is crucial for safe practice.

**Q: What is the career outlook for prosthetic technicians in Vietnam?**
A: The demand for prosthetic technicians is growing due to an aging population and rising dental care awareness. Technology adoption like digital scanning and milling creates new opportunities. However, automation may streamline some tasks, requiring technicians to upgrade skills. Overall, job prospects remain stable, especially in urban areas and specialized clinics.
