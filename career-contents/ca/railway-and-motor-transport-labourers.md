# Railway and motor transport labourers（Railway and motor transport labourers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：75211 – Railway and motor transport labourers。**

Perform physical tasks like loading, unloading, and securing cargo in railway and motor transport operations to support efficient logistics.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
