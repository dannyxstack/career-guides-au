# Railway and motor transport labourers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 75211 (NOC)**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can advance from general worker to supervisor or scheduler, or transition to technical roles like truck driver or forklift operator through training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardized cargo loading and stacking
- Repetitive bundling and securing
- Vehicle cleaning and basic cleaning.
- Simple sorting and label scanning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using scheduling software to optimize loading order
- Receive and confirm work instructions via smart terminals
- Operate automated loading and unloading assistive equipment (e.g., robotic arms)
- Use mobile apps to record cargo anomalies and damages

**Human moat:**

- Temporary lifting decisions in unstructured environments (e.g., construction sites, open yards)
- Careful handling of special goods such as fragile items and dangerous goods
- Real-time communication and coordination with on-site personnel (drivers, dispatchers)
- On-site judgment when dealing with equipment failures or emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automated material handling systems (e.g., AGVs, automated sorting lines)
- Basic mechanical maintenance and fault troubleshooting
- Use warehouse management system (WMS) mobile terminal
- Read English cargo documents and safety labels
- Forklift, crane, and other heavy equipment operation certificates

**Key growth areas:**

- Logistics
- E-commerce
- Resource exports
- Transportation

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Good physical fitness, able to work outdoors and in shifts
- Those looking to enter the workforce quickly
- Those without higher education background but needing stable income

**Who is not suited**
- Unwilling to engage in repetitive physical labor
- Those seeking high salary or rapid career advancement
