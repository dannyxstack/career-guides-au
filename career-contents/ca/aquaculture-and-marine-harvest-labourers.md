# Aquaculture and marine harvest labourers（Aquaculture and marine harvest labourers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：85103 – Aquaculture and marine harvest labourers。**

Aquaculture workers in Canada are involved in raising, harvesting, and caring for fish, shellfish, and other aquatic organisms. This occupation falls under NOC 2021 TEER 5, generally requiring no post-secondary education, and may be eligible for immigration through employer-driven streams like the Atlantic Immigration Program.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋省份雇主担保，适合在新斯科舍、纽芬兰等沿海省份找到水产养殖雇主的申请人 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份（如卑诗省、新斯科舍省）的PNP有渔业相关类别，需雇主offer |
| Subclass TFWP（Temporary Foreign Worker Program） | 临时外劳，通常为季节工，可转为永久居民 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an aquaculture worker in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from CAD 28,000 to 35,000 per year, while experienced workers can earn CAD 45,000 to 55,000. Income varies by season and location.

**问：How can an aquaculture worker immigrate to Canada?**
答：Mainly through provincial nominee programs (e.g., Atlantic Immigration Program) or the Temporary Foreign Worker Program transitioning to permanent residence. Employer sponsorship is required, and minimum CLB 4 language is typical. Express Entry is not applicable for TEER 5 occupations.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Generally only high school diploma is needed; some employers may ask for a short vocational certificate (e.g., in aquaculture). Foreign credentials require ECA for immigration purposes.
