# Insurance Brokers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 6313 (NOC)**

Canadian insurance brokers mainly work at agencies or banks, assessing risks and recommending insurance products for individuals and businesses. Eligible for Express Entry and multiple PNP streams, it is a sustainable skilled migration occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for insurance brokers in Canada?**
A: Junior: approx. CAD $40-$55K; intermediate: $55-$80K; senior: $80-$120K. Commission-based bonuses are significant, with top performers earning more.

**Q: Can insurance brokers immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
A: Yes. NOC 6313 falls under TEER 3, meeting FSW or CEC requirements. However, note the licensing requirements of Canadian provinces; it is advisable to obtain qualifications in advance or choose PNP.

**Q: How can an overseas insurance broker obtain a Canadian license?**
A: Must complete Insurance Institute of Canada (CIP) courses and pass provincial exams; some experience may be eligible for credit exemptions. Requirements vary by province; recommend contacting the regulatory body first.
