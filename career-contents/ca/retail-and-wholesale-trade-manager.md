# Retail and wholesale trade managers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 60020 (NOC)**

Retail and wholesale trade managers oversee retail stores or wholesale operations, including sales, inventory, staff, and financial performance. This occupation can pursue various PNPs and some Express Entry targeted draws, requiring relevant management experience and language scores.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a retail manager in Canada?**
A: Canadian retail manager annual salary approx. CAD 35,000–110,000, median around CAD 55,000. Entry-level positions are lower; senior or large store managers can earn over 80,000. Depends on location, store size, and company.

**Q: How can a retail manager immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
A: Main pathway is Provincial Nominee Program (PNP); many provinces such as Ontario, BC, Alberta list retail manager as an eligible occupation. Can also apply through Express Entry's CEC or FSW, but must meet language, education, and work experience requirements. Generally requires IELTS CLB 7 or higher and at least 1 year of relevant experience.

**Q: What are the career development prospects for retail managers in Canada?**
A: Retail managers can advance to regional manager (annual salary AUD 80,000-120,000), operations director (AUD 100,000-150,000), or senior management positions. An MBA or relevant certifications aid promotion. Alternatively, they can start their own franchise. However, the retail industry faces e-commerce competition, slowing demand for some roles.
