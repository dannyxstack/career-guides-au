# Contractors and supervisors, other construction trades, installers, repairers and servicers（Contractors and supervisors, other construction trades, installers, repairers and servicers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72014 – Contractors and supervisors, other construction trades, installers, repairers and servicers。**

Supervise and coordinate construction trades workers, including carpenters, electricians, plumbers, etc., ensuring projects are completed on time and within budget.

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker Program） | 适合有管理经验和英语成绩的申请人，通过综合排名系统打分 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省提名的技术移民项目，针对建筑行业有优先通道 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主担保工签，需证明加拿大本地缺乏合适工人 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋省份雇主担保，适合有意在该地区工作的申请人 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for this occupation?**
答：Junior supervisors earn about $50,000-$65,000, mid-level $65,000-$85,000, and senior up to $110,000+. Self-employed or large project managers earn more.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Canada through this occupation?**
答：Apply through Federal Skilled Worker, Provincial Nominee Program, or LMIA-based work permit. Require relevant supervisory experience and meet language requirements.
