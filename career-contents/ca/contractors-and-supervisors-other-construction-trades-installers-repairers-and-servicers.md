## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for this occupation?**
A: Junior supervisor annual salary approx. CAD$50,000-65,000, mid-level CAD$65,000-85,000, senior up to CAD$110,000+. Self-employed or large project managers earn more.

**Q: How can foreigners immigrate to Canada through this occupation?**
A: Can apply through Federal Skilled Worker (FSW), Provincial Nominee Program (PNP), or employer sponsorship (LMIA). Requires relevant management experience and meeting language requirements.

**Q: What are the career advancement paths for this occupation?**
A: Can be promoted to project manager, construction manager, or self-employed contractor. With experience, can move on to large projects or senior roles in construction companies.
