# Contractors and supervisors, heavy equipment operator crews（Contractors and supervisors, heavy equipment operator crews）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72021 – Contractors and supervisors, heavy equipment operator crews。**

Supervise and coordinate crews of heavy equipment operators, ensuring safe and efficient site operations, typically promoted from experienced operators.
