# Legislators（Legislators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：00010 – Legislators。**

Legislators are elected officials who make laws, oversee government, and represent constituents. In Canada, this role is attained through federal or provincial elections, not directly through economic immigration streams, though related experience in public policy or government relations may support migration in management occupations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry） | 适合从事政策分析、管理顾问等可转入NOC 1/0类职业的申请人，通过FSW或CEC类别申请 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 若获得省级政府或相关机构雇主担保，可考虑省提名项目 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Legislators？**
- 有强烈公共服务动机和政治参与意愿的人
- 具备优秀沟通、辩论和公众演讲能力者
- 能适应高压竞选和工作环境者

**谁不适合学Legislators？**
- 对政治生态不适应或缺乏竞选耐心的人
- 追求稳定工作时间和低政治风险的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of Canadian legislators?**
答：Federal MPs earn approximately CAD 185,000 base salary (2025), plus allowances; provincial legislators earn CAD 100,000-150,000. Non-elected roles like policy advisors earn CAD 50,000-120,000.

**问：Can legislators directly immigrate to Canada?**
答：Legislators are elected positions and not directly eligible for economic immigration. However, experience in policy, management, or government relations can transfer to NOC 0 occupations for Express Entry or PNP.
