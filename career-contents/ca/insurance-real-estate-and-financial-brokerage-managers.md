# Insurance, real estate and financial brokerage managers（Insurance, real estate and financial brokerage managers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：10020 – Insurance, real estate and financial brokerage managers。**

Oversee operations of insurance, real estate, and financial brokerage firms, manage sales teams, develop business strategies, ensure compliance, and achieve performance targets.

|---|---|

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for these managers?**
答：Entry-level CAD 55k-75k, mid-level CAD 75k-110k, senior up to CAD 180k+ with commissions.

**问：Can this occupation immigrate through skilled migration?**
答：Yes, eligible under NOC 10020 via Express Entry or PNP with relevant experience and language scores.

**问：What is the career progression?**
答：Start as sales rep/broker, advance to team leader, regional manager, then VP or own brokerage.
