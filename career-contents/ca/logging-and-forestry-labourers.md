# Logging and forestry labourers（Logging and forestry labourers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：85120 – Logging and forestry labourers。**

Perform physical labor in logging and forestry operations, including site clearing, tree planting, and operating chainsaws, supporting timber processing and forest production.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labor Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主需证明无法招到本地工人，可申请LMIA支持工签，但难度较高 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 少数省份如BC省、安省可能提名林业劳工，但需满足特定条件 |
| Subclass TFWP（Temporary Foreign Worker Program） | 季节性劳工工签，通常最多6个月，需雇主申请 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for logging labourers?**
答：Logging labourers in Canada earn low salaries, entry-level around CAD 30,000-40,000 annually, but due to seasonal work, actual income may be lower. Experienced workers can earn CAD 40,000-55,000.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada as a logging labourer?**
答：Not easy. This occupation is low-skilled, typically requiring an LMIA work permit, and not eligible for Express Entry. Few PNP streams may include it, but competition is high.

**问：What career advancement opportunities exist for logging labourers?**
答：Can advance to skilled faller, equipment operator (e.g., skidder), foreman, or through training become a forestry technician, forest conservation officer, etc.
