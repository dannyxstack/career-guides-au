# Health information management occupations — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 12111 (NOC)**

Health information management professionals are responsible for maintaining, analyzing, and protecting patient health records, ensuring the accuracy, accessibility, and security of medical data. They work in hospitals, clinics, and public health organizations, supporting clinical decision-making and medical research.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Entry-level positions like health information technician can advance to health information management analyst, privacy officer, or management. Obtaining certification (e.g., CHIM) and gaining experience can lead to supervisor or director roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated coding tools replace manual ICD coding, especially for structured medical records
- Data quality check report generation, AI automatically marks outliers
- Patient information entry and retrieval, AI voice and text recognition completed
- Preliminary anomaly detection in compliance monitoring, AI scanning documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-driven clinical coding recommendations and rapid review to improve coding accuracy
- AI automatically identifies sensitive information in data governance, accelerating compliance reviews
- Machine learning for patient data analysis to support hospital performance and resource management
- AI chatbot provides doctors with real-time coding queries to improve efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Human judgment in clinical coding disputes: non-standard medical records require clinical knowledge
- Privacy compliance decisions: manual investigation and reporting of data breaches
- Standard and training development: regulatory policy updates, internal process design
- Cross-departmental coordination: communicate with doctors, IT, and management to resolve data issues

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced ICD-10-AM/ACHI coding skills
- Data governance framework (e.g., ISO 27001)
- AI coding tool operation and verification
- Basic data analysis with Python/R
- Privacy law compliance (e.g., HIPAA/Australian Privacy Act)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Health
- Aging Population
- Data Analytics
- Electronic Health Records

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for health information management careers in Canada?**
A: Junior health information technicians earn approximately CAD 45,000-60,000 annually, intermediate analysts earn CAD 60,000-80,000, and senior managers can earn CAD 80,000-100,000. Salaries vary by province and institution.

**Q: How can health information management professionals apply for permanent residency in Canada via skilled migration?**
A: This occupation is NOC TEER 1; eligible applicants can apply via Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or Provincial Nominee Program (PNP). CHIM certification and Canadian work experience improve success.

**Q: What certification is needed to work in health information management in Canada?**
A: Typically requires the CHIM certification from the Canadian Health Information Management Association (CHIMA). Some provinces also require provincial registration. Completion of a recognized diploma or bachelor's degree is a prerequisite.
