# Retail and wholesale buyers（Retail and wholesale buyers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：62101 – Retail and wholesale buyers。**

Retail and wholesale buyers purchase goods for resale, negotiating prices, managing inventory, and analyzing market trends to ensure optimal product mix and profitability.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)） | 满足工作经验、语言和教育要求，通过CRS评分获得永居邀请。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份针对商业或采购人才有优先提名通道。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA支持工签，积累加拿大经验后转永居。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the salary for retail buyers high?**
答：Moderate. Entry-level CAD 35,000-50,000, mid-level 50,000-75,000, senior 75,000-100,000. Large firms or international roles pay more but are more competitive.

**问：Is it easy for buyers to immigrate to Canada?**
答：Feasible. Typically classified under business/finance. Can apply via Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or PNP (e.g., Ontario, BC). Employer sponsorship or LMIA helps.

**问：What is the career path for buyers?**
答：From junior to senior buyer, then to purchasing manager or category manager. Can also pivot to supply chain, logistics, or retail operations.
