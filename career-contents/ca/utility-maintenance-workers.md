# Utility maintenance workers（Utility maintenance workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：74204 – Utility maintenance workers。**

Responsible for maintaining and repairing utility systems such as water, sewer, gas, and power, including pipes, valves, meters, and related equipment.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)） | 如果职业在TEER 2/3中，可申请FSW，需满足语言和学历要求。通常需雇主担保或省提名加分。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 许多省份有需求，如安省、BC省，提名后快速获得永居。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA支持工签，之后转永居。 |

**PR难度：低（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income level for utility maintenance workers?**
答：Entry-level workers earn about CAD 35,000-45,000 annually, experienced workers can earn CAD 60,000-80,000, with overtime and allowances.

**问：How to immigrate as a utility maintenance worker to Canada?**
答：Through Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario, BC) or LMIA work permit then PR, requiring provincial certification and work experience.

**问：Is there career progression?**
答：Can advance to team lead, site supervisor, or further study to become a municipal engineer.
