# Automotive Service Technician（Automotive Service Technician）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72410 – Automotive Service Technician。**

In Canada, automotive service technicians diagnose, repair and maintain various motor vehicles, working in dealerships, independent garages and fleets. This occupation is eligible for Express Entry (FSW) and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), especially for those with certification and experience.

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 需满足67分打分，至少1年NOC 72410工作经验，雅思CLB 7。通过后快速获得永居。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 安省、BC省、阿尔伯塔省等有技工优先类别，可获省提名加600分。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an automotive technician in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 35,000-45,000; Mid-level: CAD 50,000-65,000; Senior: CAD 65,000-85,000. Red Seal and EV specialization boost earnings.

**问：Is it easy for an automotive technician to immigrate to Canada?**
答：Relatively easy; it's a shortage occupation. Routes include Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or PNP (e.g., Ontario Skilled Trades). Requires IELTS CLB 5-7 and ECA.

**问：Is Canadian certification required?**
答：Yes, you need a provincial certificate through apprenticeship. Red Seal allows interprovincial mobility. Foreign experience may qualify for prior learning assessment.
