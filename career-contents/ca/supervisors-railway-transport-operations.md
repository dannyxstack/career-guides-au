# Supervisors, railway transport operations（Supervisors, railway transport operations）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72023 – Supervisors, railway transport operations。**

Supervise railway transport operations, coordinate train schedules, manage staff, and ensure safety compliance for efficient rail service.
