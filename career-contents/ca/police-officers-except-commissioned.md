# Police officers (except commissioned)（Police officers (except commissioned)）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：42100 – Police officers (except commissioned)。**

Police officers maintain public order, prevent and investigate crimes, and enforce laws. They patrol, respond to emergencies, collect evidence, and make arrests to ensure community safety.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 71,000。加拿大警察职业需求稳定，受公共安全支出和政府预算支持。随着人口增长和城市化，部分地区对警察的需求持续存在，但竞争激烈，因职位有限且招聘严格。

**竞争度：高（★★★★☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA担保外国警察，但极少见，因多数要求公民或永久居民 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a police officer in Canada?**
答：Entry-level police officers earn about CAD 60,000-80,000 annually, mid-level CAD 80,000-100,000, and senior officers CAD 100,000-120,000. Salaries vary by province and city, with overtime and allowances increasing total pay.

**问：Can foreign police officers immigrate to Canada?**
答：The police occupation generally does not support skilled migration as most forces require Canadian citizenship or permanent residency. Rare cases may involve LMIA employer sponsorship, but it's extremely difficult. It is recommended to obtain permanent residency first before applying for police positions.
