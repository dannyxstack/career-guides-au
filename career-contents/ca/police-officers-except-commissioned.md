# Police Officers (except commissioned)（Police Officers (except commissioned)）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：43200 – Police Officers (except commissioned)。**

Police officers in Canada enforce laws, prevent crime, investigate, and ensure community safety. This profession is eligible for immigration via PNP or Express Entry, with language and education requirements.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 符合NOC 43200要求，需满足CRS分数并包含在邀请中 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省指定警察职业的省提名项目，如安省、BC省 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，需有雇主支持 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for police officers in Canada?**
答：Entry-level police officers earn CAD 55,000-75,000 annually, while senior officers earn up to CAD 95,000-115,000. Salaries vary by province and police service.

**问：How can overseas police officers immigrate to Canada?**
答：Primarily via Express Entry or PNP. Need ECA for education, language score CLB 7+, and possibly Canadian police certification.

**问：Is foreign police certification recognized in Canada?**
答：Not directly. Must undergo Canadian police academy training and pass provincial exams. Some provinces require specific courses.
