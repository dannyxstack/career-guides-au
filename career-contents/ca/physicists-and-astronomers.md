# Physicists and astronomers（Physicists and astronomers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：21100 – Physicists and astronomers。**

Physicists and astronomers study the fundamental principles of the universe, including matter, energy, space, and time, conducting experiments and theoretical analyses to advance understanding of natural phenomena.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker Program） | 物理学家和天文学家通常符合FSW要求，需有至少1年连续工作经验且英语或法语达标。 |
| Subclass CEC（Canadian Experience Class） | 在加拿大获得至少1年技术工作经验后可通过CEC申请永久居留。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份如安大略、不列颠哥伦比亚省有针对学术研究人员的省提名项目。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

- 不喜欢长时间专注研究和论文写作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for physicists and astronomers?**
答：Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions range from CAD 55,000 to 75,000, mid-level from 75,000 to 100,000, and senior positions from 100,000 to 150,000. Academic salaries are lower than industry but offer benefits and stability.

**问：How can physicists and astronomers immigrate to Canada?**
答：Primarily through Express Entry (FSW or CEC) or Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario). Requires relevant education and work experience, with English or French at CLB 7 or higher. Postdoctoral experience counts as work experience.

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：Typical path: Postdoctoral Fellow → Assistant Professor/Research Scientist → Associate Professor/Senior Scientist → Full Professor/Chief Scientist. Advancement requires high-quality publications, research grants, and teaching experience.
