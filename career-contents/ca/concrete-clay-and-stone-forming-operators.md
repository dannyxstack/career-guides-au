# Concrete, clay and stone forming operators（Concrete, clay and stone forming operators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：94103 – Concrete, clay and stone forming operators。**

Operate machines and hand tools to form concrete, clay, and stone materials into bricks, pipes, tiles, and other building products. Work mainly in factories and construction sites.
