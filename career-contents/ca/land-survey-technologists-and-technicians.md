# Land survey technologists and technicians — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 22214 (NOC)**

The corresponding occupation for cartographer in Canada is land survey technician/technologist, responsible for collecting and analyzing geographic data and creating maps. This occupation is eligible for skilled migration (Express Entry/PNP), requiring relevant education and work experience.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can progress from junior technician to senior technician or team leader; with experience, can obtain a licensed surveyor certification (CLS) and move to higher-level surveying projects or management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated data collection (e.g., drone surveys, laser scanning replacing manual measurement)
- Basic map drawing and CAD drafting (AI auto-generates contours and annotations)
- Routine terrain analysis report generation (AI writes template-based documents from data)
- Coordinate conversion and basic calculations (software-automated)
- Data quality checks (AI automatic anomaly detection)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex terrain modeling and 3D visualization (AI-accelerated model construction)
- Remote sensing image interpretation and change detection (AI identifies feature changes, human verification)
- Remote control and data analysis of surveying robots (AI integrating multi-source data)
- GIS spatial analysis optimization (AI recommends optimal site selection or route)
- Client communication and needs customization (AI simulation presentation, manual decision-making)

**Human moat:**

- On-site safety and emergency response (e.g., bad weather, equipment failure)
- Regulation interpretation and surveying standard determination (e.g., boundary disputes)
- Client relations and project negotiation (trust and communication)
- On-site assessment of complex terrain (e.g., bushland, mountainous areas)
- Development of new measurement technologies and formulation of procedures.

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and laser scanning equipment maintenance
- Python/R programming (automated data processing and GIS secondary development)
- AI image recognition applications (e.g., remote sensing interpretation tools)
- 3D modeling software (e.g., Revit, Bentley)
- Data analysis and visualization (Tableau, Power BI)
- Project management and client communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- GIS Technology
- Infrastructure
- Natural Resources
- PNP

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for land survey technicians in Canada?**
A: Entry-level annual salary: about CAD 45,000–58,000; mid-level: CAD 58,000–75,000; senior: CAD 75,000–95,000. Holding a professional surveyor license (CLS) leads to higher earnings, potentially exceeding CAD 100,000.

**Q: What immigration pathways to Canada are available for cartographers?**
A: Can apply for Federal Skilled Worker (Express Entry FSW/CEC) or Provincial Nominee Program (PNP); requires at least one year of relevant work experience, CLB 7+ language scores, and suitable education. Some provinces have priority occupation lists for surveying.

**Q: What are the educational requirements for cartographers in Canada?**
A: Typically requires a 2-year college diploma or 4-year bachelor's degree in surveying, geographic information, or a related field. Overseas qualifications require ECA certification (e.g., WES).
