# Land survey technologists and technicians（Land survey technologists and technicians）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：22213 – Land survey technologists and technicians。**

Land survey technologists and technicians use specialized equipment to conduct land surveys, create maps, and collect geographic data, providing technical support for construction, engineering, and real estate development projects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for land survey technologists and technicians?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about CAD 40,000-55,000, while senior level can reach CAD 75,000-95,000, varying by experience and region.

**问：What immigration pathways are available for land survey technologists and technicians in Canada?**
答：Options include Express Entry (FSW/CEC), Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), or LMIA-based work permits, with demand in provinces like Ontario and British Columbia.

**问：What education is required to become a land survey technologist or technician?**
答：Typically a 2-year college diploma or a 4-year bachelor's degree in surveying or related field, with provincial certification required in some provinces.
