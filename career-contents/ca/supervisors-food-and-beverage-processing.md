# Supervisors, food and beverage processing（Supervisors, food and beverage processing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：92012 – Supervisors, food and beverage processing。**

Supervise workers and production lines in food and beverage processing plants, ensuring efficient, safe operations and quality standards.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Supervisor, food and beverage processing?**
答：According to Job Bank Canada, the median annual wage is around CAD 50,000. Entry-level salaries range from CAD 38,000 to CAD 45,000, while experienced supervisors can earn up to CAD 80,000, depending on experience and province.

**问：How can I immigrate to Canada as a supervisor in food processing?**
答：You can apply through Express Entry (FSW) or Provincial Nominee Programs (PNPs). Many provinces (e.g., Ontario, Alberta) have targeted draws for food processing supervisors. Meeting language, education, and work experience requirements allows you to apply for permanent residency.

**问：Which provinces in Canada have high demand for food processing supervisors?**
答：Ontario, Quebec, Alberta, and British Columbia have high demand due to concentrated food processing plants. Prairie provinces (Manitoba, Saskatchewan) also offer opportunities.
