# Steamfitters, Pipefitters and Sprinkler System Installers（Steamfitters, Pipefitters and Sprinkler System Installers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72305 – Steamfitters, Pipefitters and Sprinkler System Installers。**

Fire protection plumbers in Canada install and maintain fire sprinkler systems, suppression systems, and piping. This trade is eligible for immigration via Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) or Express Entry (with employer sponsorship or trades-specific draws).

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary for a fire protection plumber in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD $40,000-$55,000; Mid-level: CAD $60,000-$80,000; Senior: up to CAD $110,000+ depending on province and certifications.

**问：How can a fire protection plumber immigrate to Canada?**
答：Mainly through Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) like Ontario's Skilled Trades or BC's Trades, or Express Entry (Federal Skilled Trades Program) with employer sponsorship or provincial nomination.

**问：Is Chinese fire protection plumbing experience recognized in Canada?**
答：Not fully recognized. You may need to go through apprenticeship or have skills assessed by provincial trade authorities. ECA is recommended for immigration points.
