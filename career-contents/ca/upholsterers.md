# Upholsterers（Upholsterers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：63221 – Upholsterers。**

Upholsterers reupholster or replace padding and fabric on furniture, car seats, boats, etc., involving cutting, sewing, installing springs and padding. This trade requires hand skills and patience, with steady demand in custom furniture repair and restoration.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an upholsterer in Canada?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about CAD 30,000-40,000, mid-level CAD 40,000-55,000, senior up to CAD 70,000; self-employed may earn more.

**问：What are the migration paths for upholsterers to Canada?**
答：Mainly through Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) or Provincial Nominee Program (PNP). Some provinces list it as a skilled trade, or via LMIA work permit to gain experience then apply for PR.

**问：What training is required to become an upholsterer?**
答：Typically high school diploma, then apprenticeship (2-4 years) or a college certificate (1 year). Red Seal certification is voluntary.
