# Railway yard and track maintenance workers（Railway yard and track maintenance workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：74200 – Railway yard and track maintenance workers。**

Responsible for laying, inspecting, repairing and maintaining railway tracks to ensure safe rail transport. Work is outdoors, involving manual labor and machinery operation.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 需雇主获得LMIA，证明无法招聘到本地工人 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份（如安大略、BC省）提名此职业，但需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Express Entry（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 一般不直接符合，需结合LMIA获得额外加分 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level annual income is about CAD 40,000-55,000, mid-level CAD 55,000-70,000, senior up to CAD 70,000-90,000. Overtime and remote work allowances can increase income.

**问：As a foreigner, how can I immigrate to Canada for this job?**
答：This occupation usually requires LMIA or provincial nomination (PNP) sponsorship; direct Express Entry is difficult. It is recommended to obtain a Canadian job offer and apply for LMIA.

**问：What education or training is required?**
答：Usually only a high school diploma is needed, with on-the-job training provided by employers. Taking track maintenance courses at community colleges or railway associations can increase competitiveness.
