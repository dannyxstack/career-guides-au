# Public and environmental health and safety professionals — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 21120 (NOC)**

Responsible for assessing and managing public health, environmental, and occupational health risks, ensuring compliance with regulations, and preventing diseases and injuries.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from junior health and safety officer to senior consultant, manager, or specialist, or transition into policy development or research roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Auto-generate routine health and safety reports and compliance documents
- Automatically monitor environmental indicators (e.g., air quality, noise) via sensors and IoT data
- AI initial screening of occupational exposure data and identification of abnormal patterns
- Automated document management and regulatory update tracking

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted complex health risk modelling and predictive analysis
- Using machine learning to optimise survey sampling plans and exposure assessment
- Automated analysis of epidemiological data to support public health emergency response
- Smart tool-assisted training and educational material generation

**Human moat:**

- On-site safety audits and complex hazard identification
- Cross-departmental communication and regulatory interpretation (e.g., proposing corrective actions to businesses)
- Responsibility for quick judgment and decision-making in crisis situations
- Trade-off between professional ethics and public health balance

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (R/Python)
- Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and environmental modeling
- AI tool applications (e.g. natural language processing for regulatory documents)
- Remote monitoring and IoT system operation
- Communication and public advocacy skills
- Project management and interdisciplinary collaboration

**Key growth areas:**

- Environmental Health
- Public Safety
- Regulatory Compliance
- Pandemic Preparedness
