# Public and environmental health and safety professionals（Public and environmental health and safety professionals）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：21120 – Public and environmental health and safety professionals。**

Assess and manage public health, environmental, and occupational health risks to ensure regulatory compliance and prevent disease and injury.
