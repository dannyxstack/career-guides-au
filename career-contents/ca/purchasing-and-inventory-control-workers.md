# Purchasing and inventory control workers（Purchasing and inventory control workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：14403 – Purchasing and inventory control workers。**

Purchasing and inventory control workers manage supply chain activities including purchasing supplies, monitoring inventory levels, coordinating logistics, and ensuring timely material availability to support production or sales. They typically work in manufacturing, retail, or wholesale trade companies.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker (FSW)） | 如果符合FSW入池条件（如学历、工作经验、英语能力），可通过快速通道获得永久居民身份。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省提名项目针对供应链职业，需获得当地雇主offer。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请劳动力市场影响评估，支持工签，但审批严格。 |

**PR难度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is overtime common for purchasing and inventory control workers?**
答：Overtime is common during end-of-quarter and peak promotional periods, especially during inventory counts, but it is usually compensated.

**问：Is it easy for international students to immigrate after studying supply chain?**
答：If they secure full-time employment in a related field after graduation and apply through Express Entry or PNP, the success rate is relatively high. It is recommended to target positions such as purchasing assistant.

**问：Does this occupation require frequent travel?**
答：Some roles require visiting suppliers or attending trade shows, usually once or twice a month.
