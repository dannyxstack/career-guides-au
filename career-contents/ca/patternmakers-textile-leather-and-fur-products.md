# Patternmakers - textile, leather and fur products — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 53125 (NOC)**

Textile, leather, and fur product pattern makers create templates from design drawings for cutting and sewing garments, bags, etc. They need to take precise measurements, adjust patterns, and ensure specifications are met.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Usually start as an apprentice or assistant; with experience can advance to senior pattern maker, sample supervisor, or production technical manager. Can also transition to fashion design, pattern-making instructor, or entrepreneurship.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generating G-code and tool paths for simple workpieces
- Online machining parameter optimisation based on sensor feedback
- Quality inspection of standard parts and automatic defect labeling
- Processing monitoring and abnormal alarm handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted complex surface programming and simulation verification
- Optimize fixtures and cutting parameters via digital twins
- Integrate CAM with adaptive machining to improve efficiency
- Use AI to predict tool life and optimize tool change timing
- Remote collaboration and AR-assisted debugging of multi-axis machine tools

**Human moat:**

- Process design for difficult-to-machine materials like irregular and thin-walled parts
- On-site judgment and manual intervention during processing anomalies
- Jig and fixture design and non-standard part clamping solutions
- Communicate with design and assembly teams to resolve tolerance issues

**Skills to build:**

- Multi-axis CNC programming and post-processing customization
- Write automated macros using Python/Macro
- Secondary development of CAM software (e.g., Fusion 360 API)
- Industrial robot loading/unloading integration and commissioning
- GD&T geometric tolerancing and precision measurement techniques
- AI-assisted machining software (e.g., Mastercam AI Advisor)

**Key growth areas:**

- Customization
- Sustainable Fashion
- Automation Adaptation
- Niche Markets

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for textile, leather, and fur product pattern makers?**
A: This occupation has moderate to low pay, entry-level about CAD $28,000–38,000, senior up to CAD $50,000–65,000. Affected by industry and region, slightly higher in manufacturing hubs like Toronto and Montreal.

**Q: What are the pathways to immigrate to Canada as a pattern maker?**
A: Direct immigration pathways are limited as this occupation is not on the high-demand list for Express Entry or PNPs. The most common route is obtaining a work permit through LMIA employer sponsorship, then accumulating experience to apply for permanent residence.

**Q: What are the career prospects for a pattern maker?**
A: Outlook is average; affected by automation and overseas manufacturing, employment growth is slow. However, high-end custom and sustainable fashion may offer some opportunities. Career progression includes supervisor, pattern room manager, or starting a business.
