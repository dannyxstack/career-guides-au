# Patternmakers - textile, leather and fur products（Patternmakers - textile, leather and fur products）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：53125 – Patternmakers - textile, leather and fur products。**

Patternmakers for textile, leather and fur products create templates and patterns from design specifications for cutting and sewing garments, bags, and other items. They ensure accuracy, fit, and compliance with production standards.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主需证明无法招到本地员工，获得LMIA后可申请工签。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份可能将打版师列入特定需求职业，但机会较少。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for patternmakers of textile, leather and fur products?**
答：Salaries are moderate to low: 28,000-38,000 CAD for entry-level and up to 50,000-65,000 CAD for senior positions, with higher rates in manufacturing hubs like Toronto and Montreal.

**问：What immigration pathways exist for patternmakers to Canada?**
答：Direct immigration options are limited as this occupation is not in high-demand for Express Entry or most PNPs. The most common route is through an LMIA-based work permit, then applying for permanent residence after gaining Canadian experience.

**问：What are the career prospects for patternmakers?**
答：Prospects are modest due to automation and offshore manufacturing. However, niche areas like bespoke tailoring and sustainable fashion may offer opportunities. Advancement to supervisory roles or entrepreneurship is possible.
