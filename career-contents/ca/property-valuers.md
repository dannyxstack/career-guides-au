# Property Valuers（Property Valuers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：12203 – Property Valuers。**

Property valuers assess residential, commercial, and industrial properties in Canada, with stable demand and migration pathways via Express Entry or PNP.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 技术移民通道，需满足CRS分数，NOC 12203为B类职业 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省提名，如安省、BC省、阿省，可获加分 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，适合有雇主offer |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Property Valuers？**
- 有财务或房地产背景，对数字敏感
- 喜欢外勤工作，愿意实地考察物业
- 具备良好沟通能力，能撰写评估报告

**谁不适合学Property Valuers？**
- 不适应频繁外出或与客户打交道
- 对房地产市场波动敏感，抗压能力弱

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for property valuers?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 45,000-60,000; mid-level: 60,000-80,000; senior: 80,000-110,000. AIC certification may lead to higher pay.

**问：Is it easy for property valuers to immigrate to Canada?**
答：It's a TEER 2 occupation, eligible for Express Entry and PNP. Having a Canadian job offer or education helps.

**问：Are overseas qualifications and experience recognized?**
答：Require ECA for education and assessment by AIC. Experience may be partially recognized, but Canadian courses are often needed.
