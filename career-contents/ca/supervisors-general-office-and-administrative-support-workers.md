# Supervisors, general office and administrative support workers（Supervisors, general office and administrative support workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：12010 – Supervisors, general office and administrative support workers。**

Supervise and coordinate the work of general office and administrative support staff, ensuring efficient daily operations and managing documentation, records, and communication processes.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for this occupation in Canada?**
答：According to Job Bank, annual salary ranges from CAD 45,000 to 60,000, with senior roles up to CAD 80,000.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to work in this occupation?**
答：Through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or Provincial Nominee Program (PNP). Requires relevant work experience, language proficiency, and CRS score. Alternatively, LMIA employer sponsorship.

**问：Will this occupation be replaced by AI in the future?**
答：Some clerical tasks may be automated, but supervisory and coordination roles are hard to replace, so demand remains stable.
