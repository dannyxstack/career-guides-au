# Accounting and related clerks（Accounting and related clerks）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：14200 – Accounting and related clerks。**

Accounting and related clerks record financial transactions, process invoices, reconcile accounts, manage accounts payable/receivable, and provide administrative support in accounting firms or corporate finance departments.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 如具备大专以上学历及1年加拿大或海外相关工作，可通过FSW或CEC申请永久居民。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省提名项目，部分省份对会计文员有需求，可加分或无需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA支持工签，但职业通常需证明加拿大本地劳工不足。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for accounting clerks in Canada?**
答：Entry-level CAD 35,000-45,000, mid-level CAD 45,000-58,000, senior up to CAD 75,000, varying by region and industry.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada as an accounting clerk?**
答：TEER 3 occupation, eligible for Express Entry or PNP if CRS score is high; some provinces have demand.

**问：Is there career advancement for accounting clerks?**
答：Yes, to senior clerk, accounting technician, or CPA accountant; also to financial analysis or auditing.
