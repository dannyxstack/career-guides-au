# University professors and lecturers（University professors and lecturers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：41200 – University professors and lecturers。**

University professors and lecturers teach courses, supervise graduate students, conduct research, and perform administrative duties at Canadian universities and colleges. This NOC 2021 TEER 1 occupation is eligible for Express Entry (FSW) and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), requiring a doctoral degree typically.

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 55,000。加拿大大学讲师与教授就业前景因省份而异。安大略省和不列颠哥伦比亚省需求较大，但终身教职竞争激烈。预计2023-2031年岗位增长约5%，主要受退休替代和高等教育国际化推动。

**竞争度：高（★★★★☆）。**
**工作强度：中高（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学University professors and lecturers？**
- 拥有博士或顶尖硕士学位，并希望从事学术研究与教学的学者
- 具备较强英语或法语沟通能力，能适应多文化环境
- 愿意在非大城市大学就业，以降低竞争压力

**谁不适合学University professors and lecturers？**
- 偏好高薪且快速晋升的职业
- 无法承受多年博士学习和教职竞争压力

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a university lecturer in Canada?**
答：According to Job Bank, the salary range for assistant professors is CAD 60,000-80,000 per year, while full professors can earn CAD 110,000-150,000.

**问：Can a university tutor immigrate to Canada via Express Entry?**
答：Yes, university lecturers fall under NOC 41200 (TEER 1), eligible for FSW. A PhD or master's degree and good language scores are required. High CRS (470+) is competitive.

**问：What education is needed to become a university lecturer in Canada?**
答：A PhD is typically required; some colleges or part-time positions accept a master's degree. International degrees need ECA certification.
