# Electrical and electronics engineers（Electrical and electronics engineers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：21310 – Electrical and electronics engineers。**

Electrical engineers in Canada design, develop, and supervise electrical systems, electronic equipment, and communication networks. With the clean energy transition and infrastructure growth, this occupation is viable for skilled migration via Express Entry (STEM category) or Provincial Nominee Programs, with strong job prospects.

**竞争度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker / CEC)） | 通过FSW或CEC申请，STEM定向邀请分数线较低。需满足工作经验和语言要求。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 安省、BC省、阿尔伯塔省等有技术移民类别，优先邀请工程师。需获得省提名。 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，适合有工作机会的申请人。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Electrical and electronics engineers？**
- 电气工程或相关专业本科以上学历，有3年以上工作经验
- 英语达到CLB7（雅思6分）以上，愿意考取加拿大专业工程师执照
- 对清洁能源、自动化或基础设施项目感兴趣，适应团队协作

**谁不适合学Electrical and electronics engineers？**
- 无相关学历或无法通过学历认证
- 不愿接受漫长职业资格认证（P.Eng.）或语言要求

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for electrical engineers in Canada?**
答：According to Job Bank, mid-level engineers earn around CAD 75,000-100,000, senior level up to CAD 140,000+, and entry-level salaries range from CAD 55,000-75,000.

**问：What are the conditions for electrical engineers to immigrate to Canada via skilled migration?**
答：Typically require a bachelor's degree, 3+ years of work experience, language CLB7 or higher, and ECA. Options include Express Entry (STEM draws) or Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario's Tech draw).

**问：How can overseas electrical engineers obtain the P.Eng. license in Canada?**
答：Apply through the provincial engineering association (e.g., PEO in Ontario), submit ECA, pass ethics exam, and accumulate 4 years of acceptable experience (at least 1 year in Canada).
