# Correspondence, publication and regulatory clerks（Correspondence, publication and regulatory clerks）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：14301 – Correspondence, publication and regulatory clerks。**

Correspondence, publication and regulatory clerks handle incoming and outgoing correspondence, prepare and publish documents, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, including editing, proofreading, filing, and distributing official materials.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主需证明无法招到本地员工，可申请LMIA支持工签，但该职业通常不满足高技能要求 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份可能通过技能短缺类别提名，但机会有限 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for correspondence and regulatory clerks?**
答：Entry-level salary is about CAD 32,000-40,000; mid-level CAD 40,000-50,000; senior CAD 50,000-62,000. Pay varies by region, experience, and employer size.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for immigration to Canada?**
答：Not ideal. This is a low-skilled administrative role, generally ineligible for Express Entry or PNP skilled worker programs. LMIA employer sponsorship may be possible but rare.
