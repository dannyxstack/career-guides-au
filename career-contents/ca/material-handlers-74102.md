# Material handlers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 74102 (NOC)**

Material handlers load, unload, move, and store goods, including container stuffing. In Canada, this is a TEER 4 occupation, typically not directly eligible for federal skilled migration but possible through provincial nomination or employer sponsorship.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Usually starts as a general worker, can advance to team leader or warehouse supervisor, or move to roles like forklift operator or logistics coordinator. Certification can increase promotion opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use forklift or manually move standard-sized goods to designated locations
- Sorting and stacking goods by barcode or order information
- Performing repetitive inventory counting
- Operate conveyor belts or simple lifting equipment to load and unload trucks
- Move goods within a fixed warehouse route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses augmented reality (AR) glasses to receive real-time sorting instructions, reducing errors
- Optimize goods placement using AI prediction systems to improve efficiency
- Collaborative robots (cobots) handle heavy lifting; workers shift to supervision and emergency response
- Applying digital twin technology to simulate warehouse layouts, assisting in developing material handling plans
- Using wearable devices to monitor physical fatigue and prevent work injuries

**Human moat:**

- Flexibly moving non-standard goods in chaotic or dynamic environments (e.g., construction sites, renovation warehouses)
- Manual judgment and repair of damaged or abnormal goods
- Unstructured communication and coordination with colleagues, drivers, and supervisors
- Make quick decisions in emergencies (e.g., cargo collapse, equipment failure)
- Mental resilience and endurance for prolonged high-intensity physical labor

**Skills to build:**

- Operate warehouse management system (WMS) and handheld terminals
- Basic programming (e.g., controlling automation equipment parameters)
- Fault diagnosis and basic maintenance (mechanical/electrical)
- Data entry and analysis (inventory turnover, equipment efficiency)
- Team collaboration and communication (cross-departmental material coordination)
- Safety Awareness Training (Human-Robot Collaboration Scenarios)

**Key growth areas:**

- Logistics
- Warehousing
- E-commerce
- Provincial Nominee

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a material handler in Canada?**
A: According to the Canada Job Bank, the average annual salary is approximately C$39,000 (hourly wage about C$19). Entry-level positions are lower, while senior or supervisor roles can reach over C$50,000.

**Q: Can a material handler immigrate to Canada?**
A: Can immigrate through employer-sponsored programs such as Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) or Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP), but usually does not meet Federal Skilled Worker (EE) requirements due to lower TEER 4 occupation scores.

**Q: What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
A: Can advance to warehouse supervisor, logistics coordinator, or transport manager. Obtaining a forklift license or dangerous goods handling certification can improve competitiveness.
