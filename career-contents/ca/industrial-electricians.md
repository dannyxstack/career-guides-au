# Industrial electricians（Industrial electricians）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72201 – Industrial electricians。**

Industrial electricians install, maintain, and repair electrical systems in factories and industrial plants, including motors, transformers, and control panels, ensuring safe and efficient production.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an industrial electrician?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 45,000-60,000; mid-level: CAD 60,000-80,000; senior: up to CAD 100,000+ depending on province and industry.

**问：How to immigrate as an industrial electrician to Canada?**
答：Apply through Federal Skilled Trades Program or PNP. Requires a job offer or provincial certification, typically with IELTS 5+.

**问：What certifications are needed to work as an industrial electrician in Canada?**
答：Complete an apprenticeship and pass the interprovincial Red Seal exam to become a certified journeyperson.
