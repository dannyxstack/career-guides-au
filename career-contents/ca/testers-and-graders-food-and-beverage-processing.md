# Testers and graders, food and beverage processing（Testers and graders, food and beverage processing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：94143 – Testers and graders, food and beverage processing。**

Responsible for testing raw materials, intermediate and finished products in food and beverage processing, conducting sensory, chemical or physical inspections, and grading according to standards to ensure safety and quality.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 需要雇主证明无法招聘当地人，获得劳动市场影响评估后申请工签 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份（如安大略、阿尔伯塔）可能提名食品加工工人，需匹配当地需求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can this occupation immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
答：Generally no, as it is TEER 4 (low-skilled) and does not qualify for FSW or CEC. However, you can obtain a work permit through employer sponsorship (LMIA) or apply for immigration via some provincial nominee programs (e.g., Ontario Employer Job Offer).

**问：What is the average salary for testers and graders in food and beverage processing?**
答：According to Job Bank, the median hourly wage is about CAD 17.00, with annual salary around CAD 35,000. Entry-level positions start at CAD 32,000, while senior roles can reach CAD 62,000.

**问：What certifications are required for this occupation?**
答：Typically a high school diploma suffices. Employers may prefer food safety certificates (e.g., HACCP) or short training programs. Provincial food processing certificates can be beneficial.
