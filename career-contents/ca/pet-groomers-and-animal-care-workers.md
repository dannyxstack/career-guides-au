# Pet groomers and animal care workers（Pet groomers and animal care workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：65220 – Pet groomers and animal care workers。**

Pet groomers and animal care workers provide grooming, cleaning, and basic care services for pets, including bathing, trimming fur, and cleaning teeth and ears.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of pet groomers?**
答：Entry-level pet groomers earn about CAD 28,000-35,000 per year, mid-level CAD 35,000-45,000, and experienced groomers up to CAD 60,000. Self-employed can earn more but with variability.

**问：Can pet groomers immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
答：Yes, but it's challenging. Typically requires employer sponsorship (LMIA) or PNP. Express Entry demands higher education or experience; common path is vocational training then PNP.

**问：What advancement opportunities exist for pet groomers?**
答：Advance from entry-level to senior groomer, store manager, or open your own shop. Also can switch to pet training, pet product sales, etc.
