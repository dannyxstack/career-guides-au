Pet groomers (NOC 62201) have stable demand in Canada; eligible for PNP and Express Entry immigration with relevant qualifications.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 省提名项目，部分省份如BC、安省将宠物美容师列为需求职业，需雇主担保 |
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 联邦快速通道，需满足邀请分数，工作经历需符合NOC 62201要求 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋移民计划，适用于雇主担保，门槛较低 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for pet groomers in Canada?**
答：Annual salary ranges from CAD 30,000 to 65,000, with entry-level at 30K and senior at 65K; self-employed may earn more.

**问：Can pet groomers apply for skilled immigration to Canada?**
答：Yes, via PNP or Express Entry. Require 1+ year work experience, language test, ECA, and sometimes provincial certification.

**问：What certification is needed to be a pet groomer in Canada?**
答：Most provinces require a provincial certification (e.g., Skill Trade Ontario). International certifications like IPG or NDGAA help boost competitiveness.
