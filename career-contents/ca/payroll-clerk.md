# Payroll clerk（Payroll clerk）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：13102 – Payroll clerk。**

In Canada, payroll clerks calculate employee wages, deductions, and generate reports, ensuring compliance with federal and provincial labor laws. This NOC B occupation is eligible for skilled immigration, often requiring employer sponsorship or provincial nomination.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for payroll clerks in Canada?**
答：Entry-level around CAD 35,000-45,000, mid to senior up to CAD 60,000-80,000, depending on city and experience.

**问：Can payroll clerks immigrate to Canada through skilled work?**
答：Yes, as NOC B occupation, eligible for FSW or PNP, but often require employer job offer or sufficient CRS score.

**问：What certifications are required for payroll clerks in Canada?**
答：No mandatory license, but CPA's PCP certification improves competitiveness; ECA and language test needed for immigration.
