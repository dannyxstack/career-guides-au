# Light Duty Cleaners — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 65310 (NOC)**

Light-duty cleaners perform daily cleaning and maintenance in commercial premises (e.g., offices, hotels, shopping malls), including vacuuming, mopping, wiping, and garbage disposal. This occupation falls under TEER 4 in NOC 2021 and typically does not meet Express Entry skilled migration requirements, but can lead to permanent residency through Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) or employer sponsorship.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average hourly wage for a commercial cleaner in Canada?**
A: According to Canada's Job Bank, the average hourly wage for light-duty cleaners in 2024 is approximately $18, but due to provincial and employer differences, the range is $14-$24.

**Q: Can a cleaner immigrate to Canada via Express Entry?**
A: Generally no, because Express Entry requires TEER 0/1/2/3 occupations, while light-duty cleaners fall under TEER 4. However, it is possible through employer-sponsored programs such as Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Manitoba and Saskatchewan low-skilled streams) or the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP).

**Q: What certificates or qualifications are required for commercial cleaners?**
A: Most employers do not require specific certificates, but basic English communication skills (CLB 4) are usually needed. Some employers may require safety training certificates such as WHMIS. If applying through an immigration program, language scores and ECA credential assessment (if applicable) are also required.
