# Telecommunications network planner（Telecommunications network planner）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：21321 – Telecommunications network planner。**

Telecommunications network planners in Canada design, optimize, and manage mobile, fixed, and fiber network infrastructure. This occupation is eligible for Express Entry and Provincial Nominee Programs, and is a STEM shortage occupation.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Telecommunications network planner？**
- 具备电信或电子工程学历，对网络架构和优化有深入理解的技术人士
- 有3年以上相关经验，英语CLB 7或法语NCLC 7以上
- 希望在加拿大获得职业发展和移民机会的IT/电信从业者

- 不打算从事技术评优或工程认证工作，只想通过简单体力劳动移民的人
- 对频繁学习新技术（5G/6G/云计算）感到排斥的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for telecommunications network planners in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 60k-80k; Mid-level: CAD 80k-105k; Senior: CAD 105k-140k including bonuses.

**问：How to immigrate to Canada as a telecommunications network planner?**
答：Main routes: Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and PNP (e.g., Ontario Tech Draw, BC PNP Tech). Requirements: CLB 7+, ECA, and at least one year of relevant work experience.

**问：Is a professional engineer license required?**
答：For design/stamping roles usually yes, but network planning positions often don't require P.Eng. Cisco CCNP or Nokia certifications are beneficial.
