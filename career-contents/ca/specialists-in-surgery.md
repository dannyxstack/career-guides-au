# Specialists in surgery（Specialists in surgery）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：31101 – Specialists in surgery。**

Specialists in surgery are physicians who diagnose, perform operations, and provide postoperative care. Demand is stable in Canada, but entry requirements are extremely high.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主担保工作许可，需证明无法找到加拿大本地人 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份有医生类省提名，可加速永居 |
| Subclass Express Entry（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 高分候选人直接申请，但需ECA学历认证 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for specialists in surgery in Canada?**
答：Depending on experience, annual salary ranges from CAD 250,000 to 600,000, with lower pay during residency.

**问：How can foreign surgeons immigrate to Canada?**
答：Typically via LMIA or PNP, with Canadian medical credentialing and licensing exams required. The process is lengthy but feasible.

**问：What certifications are needed to practice in Canada?**
答：An MD degree, RCPSC specialization certification, and provincial license. International doctors must pass MCQE exams and residency evaluation.
