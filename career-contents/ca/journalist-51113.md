# Journalists — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 51113 (NOC)**

Journalists collect, investigate, write and report news stories for newspapers, television, radio, online and other media, requiring excellent writing, communication and critical thinking skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Junior journalists can advance to senior journalists, editors, or news directors. They can also transition to public relations, content marketing, or digital media management. Freelance and entrepreneurial opportunities are growing.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate standard news briefs (e.g., financial reports, sports scores)
- Speech-to-text and automatic subtitle generation
- Fact-checking and data reconciliation (e.g., verifying statistical data)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted data analysis and visualization (quickly identify trends)
- AI-recommended topics and audience preference prediction
- AI compiles multilingual information summaries

**Human moat:**

- Exclusive interviews and in-depth investigations (interpersonal insight and trust)
- Ethical judgment and sensitive issue gatekeeping
- Creative storytelling (features, commentary)

**Skills to build:**

- Data journalism (Python/R/data visualization)
- Multimedia production (video/podcast editing)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT-assisted writing)
- Fact-checking and information verification
- Audience Analysis and Digital Marketing

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital media
- Multimedia journalism
- Content creation
- Data journalism

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passionate about writing and journalism, with curiosity and investigative spirit
- Adapts to fast-paced environments and deadline pressure, able to multitask
- Good at interpersonal communication, able to work in different environments

**Who is not suited**
- Dislike frequent travel or irregular working hours
- Low resilience to stress, difficulty facing negative news or criticism

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a journalist in Canada?**
A: Entry-level journalist annual salary around CAD $35,000-50,000, intermediate $50,000-75,000, senior $75,000-110,000, depending on experience, location, and media type.

**Q: What are the main pathways for journalists to immigrate to Canada?**
A: Main pathways include Federal Skilled Worker (FSW), Provincial Nominee Program (PNP), and LMIA employer-sponsored work visa. Relevant education and work experience, and English or French proficiency are required.

**Q: What is the job outlook for journalists in Canada?**
A: The news industry is undergoing digital transformation, with traditional media roles declining but digital content and multimedia news growing. Competition is intense, and journalists with data journalism and multimedia skills have an advantage.
