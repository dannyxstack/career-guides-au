# Paralegals and related occupations（Paralegals and related occupations）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：42200 – Paralegals and related occupations。**

Paralegals perform legal research, draft documents, and assist with case management under lawyer supervision, serving as essential support in legal teams.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for paralegals in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 38,000-48,000; mid-level: CAD 48,000-65,000; senior: up to CAD 85,000. Higher in Ontario and large firms.

**问：How can a paralegal immigrate to Canada?**
答：Through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or PNP. Requires ECA, language test, and work experience. Ontario requires regulatory licensing.
