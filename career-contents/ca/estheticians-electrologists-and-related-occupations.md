# Estheticians, electrologists and related occupations（Estheticians, electrologists and related occupations）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：63211 – Estheticians, electrologists and related occupations。**

Estheticians, electrologists and related occupations provide skin care, hair removal, and makeup services, working mainly in salons and spas.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for estheticians in Canada?**
答：Entry-level estheticians earn around CAD 28,000-35,000 annually, while senior estheticians or salon owners can earn over CAD 60,000.

**问：Can estheticians immigrate to Canada via Express Entry?**
答：Yes, if they meet requirements such as at least 1 year of work experience and language proficiency. Some provinces offer PNP streams.

**问：What licenses are required for estheticians in Canada?**
答：Most provinces require a provincial esthetician license, and electrology requires additional certification.
