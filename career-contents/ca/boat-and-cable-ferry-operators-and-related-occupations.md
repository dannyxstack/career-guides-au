# Boat and cable ferry operators and related occupations（Boat and cable ferry operators and related occupations）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：75210 – Boat and cable ferry operators and related occupations。**

Operate and steer small ferries, cable ferries, and similar vessels to safely transport passengers and vehicles, performing basic mechanical maintenance and deck duties.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation in Canada?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from CAD 32,000 to 42,000, mid-level from 42,000 to 55,000, and captain-level from 55,000 to 70,000. Pay varies significantly by season and region.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada for this occupation?**
答：This occupation falls under NOC TEER 3, making provincial nomination (PNP) a viable route, especially with a job offer. Express Entry typically requires higher skill levels.

**问：What certifications are required for this job?**
答：A Small Vessel Operator Proficiency (SVOP) certificate and a Restricted Operator Certificate (Maritime) from Transport Canada are mandatory. Some employers also require first aid and CPR certification.
