# Managers in agriculture — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 80021 (NOC)**

Vineyard supervisors in Canada correspond to agricultural managers (NOC 80021), responsible for overseeing viticulture operations. This occupation allows for skilled migration through provincial nomination or Express Entry, especially suitable for applicants with management experience.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a vineyard supervisor in Canada?**
A: Entry-level supervisor: approximately CAD 35,000-50,000; mid-level: CAD 50,000-70,000; senior manager: up to CAD 70,000-95,000, varying by province and winery size.

**Q: What pathways are available for Vineyard Supervisors to immigrate to Canada?**
A: Main pathways include: Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) agricultural streams, Express Entry Federal Skilled Worker, and the Agri-Food Pilot (AFIP). Requires management experience, language test scores, and educational credential assessment.

**Q: Which Canadian provinces have the highest demand for vineyard supervisors?**
A: BC (Okanagan Valley), Ontario (Niagara region), and Quebec (Eastern Townships) have developed wine industries with the highest demand.
