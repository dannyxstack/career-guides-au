# Managers in natural resources production and fishing（Managers in natural resources production and fishing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：80010 – Managers in natural resources production and fishing。**

Managers who oversee and direct operations in natural resource production (mining, oil & gas, forestry) and fishing, including harvesting and processing.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for managers in natural resources production?**
答：Salaries range from CAD 75,000 to CAD 250,000 annually depending on experience, with mid-level managers earning CAD 100,000-150,000.

**问：What are the immigration pathways for this occupation to Canada?**
答：Eligible for Express Entry (FSW) and Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Alberta, BC). Employer-sponsored LMIA is also common.
