# Supervisors, mineral and metal processing（Supervisors, mineral and metal processing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：92010 – Supervisors, mineral and metal processing。**

Supervise daily operations in mineral and metal processing plants, coordinate worker activities, and ensure safe and efficient production. Handle staffing, quality control, and equipment maintenance reporting.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for supervisors of mineral and metal processing?**
答：Entry-level CAD 55,000-70,000, mid-level CAD 70,000-90,000, senior CAD 90,000-110,000, varying by province and experience.

答：Yes. This NOC B occupation qualifies for FSW or CEC. Additional points with LMIA or provincial nomination.

**问：How to advance from operator to supervisor?**
答：Typically require years of operational experience and completion of supervisory training. Some employers promote internally.
