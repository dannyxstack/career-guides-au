# Security guards and related security service occupations — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 64410 (NOC)**

Responsible for protecting property and personnel safety, conducting patrols, surveillance, and access control. Commonly found in commercial buildings, residential communities, airports, and public places.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Advancement from entry-level security guard to supervisor, security manager, or specialized fields (e.g., corporate security, investigations). Requires experience accumulation and relevant certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Real-time video surveillance analysis and anomaly alerts, automatically performed by AI vision systems
- Regular patrol and check-in, replaced by drones, robots, or fixed sensors.
- Access permission management, enforced via biometrics and automated rules
- Visitor registration and identity verification, handled by AI system at self-service kiosk

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-Assisted Crisis Event Decision-Making: System identifies threats and provides response suggestions, security officer confirms and executes
- Intelligent scheduling and resource allocation: optimize patrol routes based on crowd density and event predictions
- Real-time threat assessment and intelligence alerts: AI integrates multi-source data (sensors, social media) for early warnings.
- Multilingual interactive translation: AI voice assistant helps security guards communicate with non-English speaking visitors
- Training and simulation drills: VR/AR combined with AI to generate complex security scenarios, improving judgment

**Human moat:**

- On-the-spot judgment and decision-making in crisis situations, such as armed confrontation or first aid.
- Interpersonal conflict mediation and negotiation skills, requiring high emotional intelligence and communication skills
- Legal and compliance responsibilities, accountable for consequences of actions such as searches and arrests
- Cross-department coordination and emergency command, cannot be fully automated
- Comprehensive perception of venue physical situations, such as odours, abnormal behaviour micro-expressions

**Skills to build:**

- Security System Integration and AI Surveillance Software Operation
- Data analysis and incident report writing (including basic statistics)
- Crisis communication and conflict management (advanced)
- Basic first aid and emergency response (including CPR certification)
- Security IoT device troubleshooting
- Knowledge of privacy and information security management regulations

**Key growth areas:**

- Security
- Public Safety
- Surveillance
- Access Control

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the approximate annual salary of a security guard in Canada?**
A: Entry-level security guard annual salary about 28,000 to 36,000 CAD, supervisor up to 45,000 to 60,000 CAD.

**Q: Can security guards migrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
A: Skilled migration (Express Entry) typically requires higher-skilled occupations; security guard is low-skilled, mainly relying on LMIA work permits or very few provincial nominations.

**Q: What certificates are needed to work as a security guard?**
A: Each province requires a security guard license, which involves completing training and passing an exam; first aid certificates are also often required by employers.
