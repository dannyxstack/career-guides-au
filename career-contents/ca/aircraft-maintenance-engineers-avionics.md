# Aircraft maintenance engineers (avionics)（Aircraft maintenance engineers (avionics)）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：22313 – Aircraft maintenance engineers (avionics)。**

Inspect, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair aircraft avionics systems including navigation, communication, radar, and flight control to ensure airworthiness. Eligible for immigration via Express Entry or Provincial Nominee Programs.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics) in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salary is about CAD 45,000-60,000, mid-level CAD 60,000-80,000, and senior CAD 80,000-100,000 annually. Higher earnings are possible with an AME license and extensive experience.

**问：Is it difficult for an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics) to immigrate to Canada?**
答：This occupation is a skilled trade in high demand. Immigration via Express Entry or PNP is feasible. Requirements include a Canadian AME license or employer sponsorship, and ECA for foreign education. Overall, it's favorable, but the licensing process can be challenging for overseas applicants.

**问：How to obtain a Canadian Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics) license?**
答：Complete a Transport Canada approved training program (2-3 years), pass written and practical exams, and accumulate required work experience (minimum 48 months). Foreign experience may be assessed for equivalency.
