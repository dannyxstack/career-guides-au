# Home building and renovation managers（Home building and renovation managers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：70011 – Home building and renovation managers。**

Home building and renovation managers plan, organize, direct, and evaluate activities of residential construction and renovation projects, ensuring completion on time and within budget.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) / Canadian Experience Class (CEC)） | 通过联邦技术移民或加拿大经验类移民，需满足语言、教育及工作经验要求。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省提名项目，如安大略省、不列颠哥伦比亚省等，针对建筑管理人才。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 通过雇主担保获得工签，后续可申请永久居民。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for home building and renovation managers?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from CAD 55,000 to 75,000; mid-level CAD 75,000 to 100,000; senior CAD 100,000 to 150,000, varying by region and company size.

**问：How can I immigrate to Canada as a home building and renovation manager?**
答：You can immigrate via Express Entry, PNP, or LMIA employer sponsorship, requiring relevant education, work experience, and language proficiency.

**问：What is the career outlook for this occupation?**
答：With growing housing and renovation demand, the outlook is positive, with advancement to senior management or self-employment.
