**职业代码：64201 – Image, social and other personal consultants。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income level for image consultants in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salary is around CAD 35,000-48,000, senior can reach CAD 65,000-90,000, with freelancers experiencing more volatility.

**问：Can I immigrate to Canada as an image consultant through skilled migration?**
答：Yes, this occupation is TEER 1 and eligible for Express Entry, subject to language and education requirements.

**问：Is a university degree required for this career?**
答：No, many enter through short certification courses, but a degree can enhance competitiveness.
