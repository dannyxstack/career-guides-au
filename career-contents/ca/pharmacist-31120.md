# Pharmacist — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 31120 (NOC)**

Retail pharmacists in community pharmacies handle dispensing, health consultations, and prescription reviews. This is an in-demand occupation in Canada's healthcare system, and immigration can be achieved through Express Entry or PNP.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a retail pharmacist in Canada?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approximately CAD 70,000-90,000, mid-level CAD 90,000-110,000, senior up to CAD 110,000-140,000, may be higher in remote areas.

**Q: What are the main pathways for pharmacists to immigrate to Canada?**
A: Mainly through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) pathways, requiring educational credential assessment and language proficiency.

**Q: How can international pharmacists practice in Canada?**
A: Must pass PEBC academic and qualifying exams, obtain provincial license, and typically complete internship. Process takes 2-3 years.
