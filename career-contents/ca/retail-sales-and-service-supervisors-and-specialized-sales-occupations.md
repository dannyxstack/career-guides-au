# Retail sales and service supervisors and specialized sales occupations（Retail sales and service supervisors and specialized sales occupations）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：64100 – Retail sales and service supervisors and specialized sales occupations。**

Bank workers in Canada correspond to NOC 64100, typically performing customer service or teller duties. This occupation is non-technical and generally not eligible for Express Entry; immigration options include provincial nominee programs with employer sponsorship or LMIA-based work permits.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 银行职员通常不符合技术移民要求，NOC 64100非TEER 0/1/2/3类别 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份如安省、BC省有雇主担保类别，需获得加拿大银行offer |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA支持工签，但非永久居民途径，职业非紧缺 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a bank teller in Canada?**
答：Entry-level tellers earn about CAD 30,000-40,000 annually; senior customer service representatives may earn CAD 55,000-70,000, but overall income is relatively low.

**问：Can a bank worker immigrate to Canada?**
答：Direct Express Entry immigration is difficult because NOC 64100 is TEER 4. However, provincial nominee programs with employer sponsorship or study-then-work pathways are possible.

**问：What are the career development paths for a bank teller?**
答：Promotion to customer service manager or branch operations supervisor, or transition to personal banker or loan officer by obtaining CSC certification.
