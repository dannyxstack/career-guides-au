# Retail sales and service supervisors and specialized sales occupations — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 64100 (NOC)**

Bank tellers in Canada correspond to NOC 64100, typically working in customer service or as tellers. This occupation falls under non-skilled immigration categories and is difficult to immigrate directly through Express Entry, but possible through provincial nomination (e.g., employer sponsorship) or Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA).

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression includes: senior teller, customer service manager, branch operations supervisor. Some move into personal banking advisors or loan officers through internal training, requiring certification such as the Canadian Securities Course (CSC).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine transactions such as cash deposits and withdrawals, transfers, bill payments
- Simple account balance inquiries and transaction history queries
- Form filling guidance and basic account opening procedures
- Standard product information introduction and FAQ
- Transaction record verification and document archiving

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Identify clients' deep financial needs and recommend personalized products
- Provide investment advice through historical client data analysis
- Handling complex complaints and dispute resolution
- Using AI-assisted systems to improve cross-selling success rates
- Managing high-net-worth client relationships and wealth planning

**Human moat:**

- High-emotion customer interactions requiring empathy and trust building
- Diagnosis and solution design for complex financial issues.
- Handling emergencies and managing client emotions
- Human judgment and ethical decision-making in regulatory compliance
- Long-term client relationship maintenance and loyalty building

**Skills to build:**

- Financial planning certification (e.g., CFP)
- Digital banking tools and CRM system operation
- Data analysis and customer insight basics
- Communication and sales skills.
- Anti-fraud and compliance knowledge
- Use of AI-assisted customer service tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Customer Service
- Bank Teller
- Retail Banking
- Provincial Nominee

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of bank tellers in Canada?**
A: Entry-level teller annual salary about CAD 30,000-40,000, mid to senior customer service representatives up to CAD 55,000-70,000, but overall income level is relatively low.

**Q: As a bank teller, can I immigrate to Canada?**
A: Immigrating directly via Express Entry is very difficult because NOC 64100 is a TEER 4 category. However, it is possible through provincial nomination with employer sponsorship or by first studying then working.

**Q: What career paths are available for bank tellers?**
A: Can advance to customer service manager, branch operations supervisor, or transfer to personal banking advisor, loan officer by obtaining CSC, but requires additional qualifications.
