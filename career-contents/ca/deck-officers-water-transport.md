# Deck Officers, Water Transport（Deck Officers, Water Transport）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72601 – Deck Officers, Water Transport。**

Ship's Officers in Canada correspond to NOC 72601 Deck Officers, Water Transport. They navigate vessels, manage cargo, and ensure safety. This occupation is eligible for Express Entry and PNP programs, requiring maritime certifications.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 需符合NOC TEER 2要求，语言CLB 7，ECA学历认证，CRS分数高者获邀 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 大西洋省份、BC省PNP对海事职业有需求，可加600分 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 新斯科舍省、新不伦瑞克省等雇主担保，适合有工作offer者 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a Ship's Officer in Canada?**
答：Entry-level CAD 40,000-60,000, senior captain up to CAD 95,000-140,000, plus sea pay.

**问：Is it easy for Ship's Officers to immigrate to Canada?**
答：It is feasible via EE or PNP, but requires Canadian maritime certification. Employer sponsorship helps.
