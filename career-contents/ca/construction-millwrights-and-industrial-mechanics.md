# Construction millwrights and industrial mechanics（Construction millwrights and industrial mechanics）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：73200 – Construction millwrights and industrial mechanics。**

Install, maintain, and repair heavy machinery and rigging systems in construction and industrial settings. This occupation falls under NOC 73200 and is eligible for immigration via PNP or Express Entry.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for riggers in Canada?**
答：According to Canada's Job Bank, the median annual salary for riggers is about CAD 62,000, with senior workers earning over CAD 90,000.

**问：How can a rigger immigrate to Canada?**
答：Common pathways: 1) Federal Skilled Trades Program with a job offer; 2) Provincial Nominee Program (e.g., Alberta's skilled trades stream); 3) Work permit then permanent residence. Requires CLB 5 and relevant experience.

**问：What certifications are required to work as a rigger in Canada?**
答：Most provinces require completion of a 4-year apprenticeship and a provincial trade certificate. For inter-provincial mobility, the Red Seal endorsement is recommended.
