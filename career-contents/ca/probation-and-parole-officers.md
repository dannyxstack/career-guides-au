# Probation and parole officers（Probation and parole officers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：41311 – Probation and parole officers。**

Probation and parole officers supervise offenders placed on probation or released from prison on parole, helping them reintegrate into society and ensuring compliance with court-ordered conditions.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for probation and parole officers?**
答：Provincial officers start at CAD 50,000, mid-level around CAD 75,000, and senior supervisors can earn over CAD 100,000. Federal salaries are slightly higher.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada as a probation and parole officer?**
答：This NOC 41311 occupation is eligible for FSW and PNP. Having a job offer from a provincial government greatly increases chances.

**问：What is the career progression for probation and parole officers?**
答：Advance from entry-level to senior officer, team supervisor, or regional manager, or transition to policy, training, or forensic social work.
