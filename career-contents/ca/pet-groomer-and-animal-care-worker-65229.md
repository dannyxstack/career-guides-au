# Pet groomers and animal care workers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 65229 (NOC)**

In Canada, pet groomers provide bathing, trimming, and styling services for pets, working in pet shops, veterinary clinics, or self-employed. This occupation falls under NOC 65229, typically requires a high school diploma and on-the-job training, and may allow immigration through Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) or employer sponsorship, but note provincial licensing requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior pet groomers can advance to senior groomers or shop supervisors by gaining experience and obtaining international certifications (e.g., IPG, NDGAA). Some practitioners choose self-employment or entrepreneurship by opening pet grooming salons. Collaborating with veterinarians can expand service scope, such as pet medical care.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated online appointment booking and customer reminders
- Operation of automated equipment for basic bathing and drying processes
- Inventory management and order processing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted pet hair care and styling design suggestions
- Smart tools provide preliminary assessment of pet health status
- Generation of social media marketing content and customer management.

**Human moat:**

- Personalized styling aesthetics and creativity for different pets
- Safe handling and calming techniques for treating anxious pets
- Ability to handle complex hair matting, skin diseases and other issues on site
- Face-to-face service that builds long-term customer trust and reputation

**Skills to build:**

- Enrol in an Advanced Styling and Creative Cutting course
- Knowledge of pet first aid and basic veterinary care
- Using social media marketing tools (e.g., Canva, ChatGPT)
- Learn customer relationship management software (e.g., Mindbody)
- Improve non-English communication skills (such as Cantonese, Mandarin) to serve diverse customers
- Participate in pet grooming competitions to increase visibility

**Key growth areas:**

- Pet Industry Growth
- Provincial Nominee
- Self-Employment
- Job Bank

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the approximate annual salary for a pet groomer in Canada?**
A: Junior approx. CAD 28,000–35,000; intermediate CAD 35,000–45,000; senior CAD 45,000–60,000. Self-employment may be higher but with own expenses.

**Q: Can pet groomers immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
A: NOC 65229 falls under TEER 5 and typically does not meet FSW or CEC requirements (which require skill types 0, A, or B). However, it may be possible through provincial nomination (e.g., employer sponsorship) or self-employment immigration (requiring proof of self-employment ability); some provinces like Saskatchewan may have relevant categories. Consult an immigration advisor.

**Q: What certifications are needed to work as a pet groomer in Canada?**
A: Not mandatory, but obtaining international certifications such as IPG or NDGAA can enhance competitiveness. Self-employment or opening a shop requires applying for a provincial business license. Canada does not require a unified practice certificate, but employers may require training proof.
