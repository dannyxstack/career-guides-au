# Legal administrative assistants（Legal administrative assistants）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：13111 – Legal administrative assistants。**

Legal administrative assistants provide administrative and clerical support in law firms, corporate legal departments, and government agencies, managing documents, scheduling, and client communication to ensure efficient legal operations.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSW)） | 通过联邦技术移民快速通道，需满足工作经验、语言等要求，NOC 13111属于TEER 2，符合FSW要求。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for legal administrative assistants?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from CAD 35,000 to 45,000, mid-level from 45,000 to 58,000, and senior roles up to 72,000 annually. Larger cities like Toronto offer higher pay but also higher cost of living.

**问：Can legal administrative assistants immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. NOC 13111 falls under TEER 2, eligible for Federal Skilled Worker Program. Additionally, Provincial Nominee Programs or LMIA-based work permits can lead to permanent residence.

**问：Will this job be replaced by AI?**
答：Routine administrative tasks may be automated, but legal environments still require human interaction and judgment. Upskilling or transitioning to specialized legal assistant roles can mitigate risk.
