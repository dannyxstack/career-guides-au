# Lawyers and Quebec notaries（Lawyers and Quebec notaries）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：41101 – Lawyers and Quebec notaries。**

Canadian lawyers provide legal advice, draft documents, and represent clients in court, covering business, family, criminal law. This profession qualifies for provincial nominations (e.g., Ontario Human Capital, BC Skilled Worker) or Express Entry, but requires ECA and provincial bar admission (NCA assessment).

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for lawyers in Canada?**
答：Entry-level lawyers earn CAD 60,000-90,000; mid-level CAD 90,000-150,000; senior CAD 150,000-300,000+. However, foreign-trained lawyers may start lower due to accreditation requirements.

**问：How can a foreign lawyer immigrate to Canada?**
答：Through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or PNP (e.g., Ontario Human Capital). Requires ECA (WES) and NCA assessment to obtain Canadian bar admission. Immigration before bar certification is possible.

**问：How to get Canadian bar admission with a foreign law degree?**
答：Submit NCA assessment, complete required courses/exams, finish 12-month articling, and pass provincial bar exams. Total process typically 2-3 years.
