# Lawyers and Quebec notaries — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 41101 (NOC)**

Canadian lawyers provide legal advice, draft legal documents, and represent clients in court, covering commercial, family, criminal, and other areas. This occupation can immigrate through provincial nominations (e.g., Ontario Human Capital, BC Skilled Worker) or Express Entry, but requires overseas qualification assessment (ECA) and provincial bar qualification (NCA evaluation).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of a lawyer in Canada?**
A: Junior lawyer annual salary approx CAD $60,000-90,000, mid-level $90,000-150,000, senior $150,000-300,000+. However, overseas lawyers must undergo NCA assessment and internship, with initial salary potentially lower than expected.

**Q: How can overseas lawyers immigrate to Canada?**
A: Can apply through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario Human Capital). Requires WES ECA and plans to obtain Canadian lawyer qualification through NCA assessment. Must register with provincial law society post-migration.

**Q: How can an overseas law degree qualify for Canadian bar admission?**
A: Need to submit an assessment to NCA, take additional courses or exams, complete 12-month articling, and pass the provincial bar exam. The entire process usually takes 2-3 years.
