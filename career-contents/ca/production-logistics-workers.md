# Production logistics workers（Production logistics workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：14402 – Production logistics workers。**

Coordinate and operate material handling, loading/unloading, inventory recording, and packaging/shipping within factories and warehouses to ensure smooth production flow. Entry-level logistics role often using forklifts and conveyors.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker / Canadian Experience Class） | 若职位属于NOC TEER 4或5，通常不直接符合FSW/CEC。但可通过省提名（PNP）的特定类别申请。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份（如安大略、不列颠哥伦比亚）的省提名针对物流工人，要求雇主担保。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA支持工签，工作一年后可通过CEC或PNP申请永久居留。 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，适用于该职业。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is there significant salary growth for production logistics workers?**
答：Entry salary is about CAD 32,000-38,000, rising to CAD 46,000-58,000 as supervisor. However, ceiling is moderate; higher income requires moving into supply chain management.

**问：Is this occupation easy for immigration to Canada?**
答：Paths are limited. Usually requires LMIA work permit to gain one year Canadian experience, then apply via CEC or PNP. Some PNP streams target logistics workers but are competitive.

**问：How to advance from production logistics worker to management?**
答：Obtain a diploma in logistics/supply chain, earn certifications like forklift trainer or inventory management, gain team leadership experience, then advance to warehouse supervisor or logistics coordinator.
