# Managers in publishing, motion pictures, audio and video production, broadcasting and performing arts — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 50011 (NOC)**

This occupation is classified as a management role (TEER 0) under Canada's NOC 2021, responsible for planning, organizing, and directing the operations of arts institutions or projects, including budgeting, fundraising, marketing, and team management. As a management position, relevant experience is required. Immigration is possible through Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) or Express Entry (FSW/CEC), but usually requires employer sponsorship or in-province work experience.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for arts management or managers in Canada?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approx. CAD 40,000-55,000; mid-level CAD 55,000-75,000; senior CAD 75,000-110,000. Higher in big cities but cost of living is also high.

**Q: What immigration pathways are available for arts managers or managers to go to Canada?**
A: Main pathways include Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), such as Ontario's OINP. Typically requires a job offer or Canadian work experience, and management roles score higher.
