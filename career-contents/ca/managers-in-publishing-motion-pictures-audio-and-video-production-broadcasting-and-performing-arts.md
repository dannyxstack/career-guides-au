# Managers in publishing, motion pictures, audio and video production, broadcasting and performing arts（Managers in publishing, motion pictures, audio and video production, broadcasting and performing arts）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：50011 – Managers in publishing, motion pictures, audio and video production, broadcasting and performing arts。**

This occupation falls under NOC 2021 TEER 0, managing operations of arts organizations or projects including budgeting, fundraising, marketing, and team leadership. It is eligible for immigration via Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) or Express Entry (FSW/CEC), but often requires employer sponsorship or provincial work experience.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Arts Administrator or Manager in Canada?**
答：Entry-level CAD 40,000-55,000, mid-level CAD 55,000-75,000, senior CAD 75,000-110,000 annually. Salaries are higher in big cities but offset by living costs.

**问：What immigration pathways are available for Arts Administrator or Manager to Canada?**
答：Main pathways include Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., OINP). Employer sponsorship or Canadian work experience is often required, and managerial roles score higher.
