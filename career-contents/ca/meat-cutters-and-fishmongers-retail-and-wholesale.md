# Meat cutters and fishmongers - retail and wholesale — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 65202 (NOC)**

Cut, bone, pack meat and fish in retail or wholesale settings, serve customers, ensure product freshness and hygiene.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level cutters can advance to senior cutters, department heads, or procurement specialists; some can open their own retail stores.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using smart weighing and automatic packaging systems to replace manual weighing and packing
- Apply inventory management AI to predict demand, optimize ordering and replenishment processes
- Self-checkout kiosks and online ordering systems reduce cashier and service roles
- Automated cutting machine for batch cutting of standard parts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted quality inspection systems help identify meat texture and freshness issues.
- Data-driven cutting solution optimization to improve meat yield and profit
- Smart scheduling and task allocation systems improve overall operational efficiency
- Customer preference analysis tools, personalized recommendations and cross-selling

**Human moat:**

- Fine hand-cutting skills and customized service for cuts
- Ability to assess meat freshness, texture, and fat distribution through sensory judgment.
- Direct interaction with clients, providing personalized advice and trust-building
- Flexibility to handle special orders (e.g., whole cow deboning, irregular cuts)

**Skills to build:**

- Learn to use automated cutting equipment and smart weighing systems
- Mastery of basic inventory management and data analysis tools.
- Improve customer communication and personalised service skills
- Understanding food safety regulations and HACCP standards
- Basic knowledge of machine maintenance and troubleshooting

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable demand
- Retirement replacements
- Food retail growth
- Local food trend

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on work and attention to detail
- Able to adapt to cold storage environments and stand for long periods.
- Those seeking stable employment in retail or food industry

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike handling raw meat or fish
- Those seeking high salary or fast immigration pathways

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a meat cutter in Canada?**
A: Annual salary ranges from approximately 28,000 to 55,000 CAD depending on experience, with a median of about 38,000 CAD.

**Q: Can meat cutters immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
A: This occupation usually does not meet the requirements for Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) or Canadian Experience Class (CEC) as it is classified as low-skilled. However, PNP or LMIA work permit pathways are possible, but competition is fierce and employer support is needed.
