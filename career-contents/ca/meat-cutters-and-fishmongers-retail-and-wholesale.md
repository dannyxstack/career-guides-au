# Meat cutters and fishmongers - retail and wholesale（Meat cutters and fishmongers - retail and wholesale）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：65202 – Meat cutters and fishmongers - retail and wholesale。**

Cut, debone, and package meat and fish in retail or wholesale settings, serve customers, and ensure product freshness and hygiene.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在加拿大技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA担保临时工签，但该职业通常不被视为高技能，获批率较低。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份的PNP可能包含该职业，但通常需要雇主担保和较低技能类别。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for meat cutters in Canada?**
答：Salaries range from CAD 28,000 to CAD 55,000 per year depending on experience, with a median around CAD 38,000.

**问：Can meat cutters immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
答：This occupation is generally ineligible for FSW or CEC due to low skill level. Options include PNP or LMIA work permits, but are limited and require employer sponsorship.
