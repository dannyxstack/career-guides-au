# Light Duty Cleaners（Light Duty Cleaners）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：65312 – Light Duty Cleaners。**

Responsible for routine cleaning and sanitation in offices, malls, hospitals, etc. An entry-level labor occupation in Canada, generally not eligible for skilled immigration but may lead to permanent residence through employer sponsorship or certain low-skilled PNP streams.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP低技能通道（Provincial Nominee Program (Low Skilled)） | 部分省份（如BC、Saskatchewan、Alberta）有针对低技能职业的省提名，需雇主担保，通常要求语言CLB 4-5 |
| Subclass TFW（Temporary Foreign Worker） | 通过劳工市场影响评估（LMIA）获得工签，工作一段时间后可能经验移民 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，清洁工可申请，但企业需获指定 |

**PR难度：困难（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Light Duty Cleaners？**
- 愿意从事体力劳动、能接受夜班或灵活时间的人
- 移民初期需要快速就业、积累本地经验的人群
- 已经在加拿大、通过配偶或家庭团聚陪读的人

**谁不适合学Light Duty Cleaners？**
- 追求高薪或快速职业上升的专业人士
- 希望直接通过Express Entry获得永久居留权的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the typical annual salary for a commercial cleaner in Canada?**
答：Entry-level cleaners earn approximately CAD 28,000-34,000, experienced ones can exceed CAD 40,000, but varies by province, with higher wages in Ontario and BC.

**问：Can a cleaner immigrate to Canada through skilled immigration?**
答：Generally no via Express Entry due to low NOC skill level. But possible through Provincial Nominee Programs (low-skilled streams) or employer sponsorship (LMIA), requiring employer support.

**问：What qualifications or licenses are required for cleaners in Canada?**
答：Most provinces require no formal license, but specialized settings (e.g., hospitals) may need sanitization certification or WHMIS training. On-the-job training is common.
