# Public works maintenance equipment operators and related workers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 74205 (NOC)**

Operate and maintain public works equipment, such as street sweepers, snowplows, and garbage compactors, to ensure cleanliness and safety of urban infrastructure.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can advance from operator to senior operator, supervisor, or equipment trainer; with experience, can transition to heavy equipment operation or municipal management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Autonomous street sweeper completes street cleaning without human driving
- Automatic snow removal system starts and operates snow removal equipment based on weather data
- Waste compactors operate automatically using sensors and AI algorithms to optimise the compression process
- Drone inspection of infrastructure (e.g., bridges, roads), replacing manual visual inspection
- The dispatch system automatically plans vehicle routes and working hours, reducing manual coordination.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operators use AI-assisted monitoring systems to view real-time operation status of multiple devices, with automatic alerts for anomalies
- Simulate equipment operations using digital twin technology to improve fault diagnosis and preventive maintenance efficiency
- AI optimises fuel consumption and work routes, reducing operating costs and improving energy efficiency
- Augmented reality (AR) helmet provides equipment repair guidance, reducing training time

**Human moat:**

- Emergency judgment and on-site response to sudden incidents (e.g., complex road conditions, sudden weather changes)
- Equipment operation and adjustment in complex terrain or unconventional scenarios
- Communicate and coordinate with municipal departments and the public, handle complaints and ad hoc needs
- Perform deep repairs, modifications, and customised adjustments on equipment

**Skills to build:**

- Remote monitoring and operation of autonomous vehicles and drones
- IoT device maintenance and data analysis
- Basic programming and automation system troubleshooting
- Use of digital twins and augmented reality tools
- Emergency management and complex environment decision-making ability
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainable technology

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure renewal
- Urbanization
- Snow removal demand
- Equipment automation

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy outdoor work and hands-on operation of machinery
- For those seeking stable employment who do not mind physical labor
- Willing to gain skills through on-the-job training, no higher education required

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer indoor desk work and office environment
- Those who are not suited to high physical demands or extreme weather conditions

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a municipal maintenance equipment operator?**
A: Median annual salary in Canada is about $47,000, entry-level about $35,000, and senior can reach $75,000.

**Q: Is this occupation easy to immigrate to Canada?**
