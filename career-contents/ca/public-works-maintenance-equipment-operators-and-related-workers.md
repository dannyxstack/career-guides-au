# Public works maintenance equipment operators and related workers（Public works maintenance equipment operators and related workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：74205 – Public works maintenance equipment operators and related workers。**

Operate and maintain public works equipment such as street sweepers, snowplows, and garbage compactors to ensure clean and safe urban infrastructure.
