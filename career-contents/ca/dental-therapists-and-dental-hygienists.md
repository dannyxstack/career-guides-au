# Dental therapists and dental hygienists（Dental therapists and dental hygienists）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：32109 – Dental therapists and dental hygienists。**

Dental therapists in Canada are regulated health professionals requiring provincial licensure. This occupation is eligible for immigration via Express Entry or PNP, offering stable employment and moderate salaries.
