# Dental Therapists and Dental Hygienists（Dental Therapists and Dental Hygienists）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：32111 – Dental Therapists and Dental Hygienists。**

Dental therapists in Canada work mainly in public clinics, school oral health programs, and community health agencies, focusing on preventive care and basic restorative treatment for children and adolescents. This is a regulated occupation in provinces like Ontario and BC, eligible for PNP and Express Entry (FSW/CEC) migration, subject to interprovincial licensure recognition.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for dental therapists in Canada?**
答：According to Job Bank, the median annual salary is around CAD 75,000. Entry-level ranges from CAD 55,000 to 70,000, and senior positions can reach CAD 100,000. Remote areas offer higher pay due to incentives.

**问：How can an overseas dental therapist immigrate to Canada?**
答：Via Express Entry (requiring ECA and CLB 9+ language) or Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., BC PNP Health category). A provincial license is mandatory, often requiring bridging courses or exams. Assess your education against the NDCB exam first.

**问：Is my Australian dental therapist license valid in Canada?**
答：Not directly. You must apply for licensure through provincial regulators (e.g., CDHO in Ontario), which may require additional coursework, clinical exams, or supervised practice. BC and Alberta have partial reciprocity, but the process takes months to a year.
