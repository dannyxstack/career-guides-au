# Dental therapists and dental hygienists — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 32109 (NOC)**

Dental therapists are health care occupations in Canada, requiring provincial regulatory licensing. The occupation can immigrate via Express Entry or PNP, with stable employment and moderate salary.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can advance from junior dental therapist to senior clinician or management. Some move into dental education, public health, or private practice management, requiring continuing education for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Auto-generates patient oral examination reports and dental history records
- AI analyzes X-rays, caries detection, and preliminary periodontal disease screening
- Automated appointment management and patient reminder systems.
- Partial data recording and statistical analysis in standard dental cleaning procedures

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic tools improve early detection accuracy for cavities and periodontal disease
- Virtual patient education system enhances oral hygiene instruction effectiveness
- Intelligent treatment planning software optimizes personalized treatment plans
- Remote monitoring devices enable continuous tracking of patient oral health
- Automated note-taking tools allow health practitioners to focus more on patient communication

**Human moat:**

- Clinical manual procedures (scaling, polishing, local anesthesia injection)
- Interpersonal communication skills for building patient trust and relieving anxiety
- Comprehensive judgment and clinical decision-making responsibility for complex cases
- Adherence to practice limits and ethical requirements under provincial regulations

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic software operation (e.g., Denti.AI, Overjet)
- Digital workflow management for dental clinics (e.g., DentalXChange)
- Remote dental consultation and patient communication skills
- Data-driven preventive care plan development skills
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and electronic health record system application

**Key growth areas:**

- Healthcare Demand
- Aging Population
- Rural Health
- Preventive Care

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a dental therapist in Canada?**
A: Average annual salary approx CAD 70,000, entry level CAD 55,000-70,000, senior above CAD 100,000, depending on region and experience.

**Q: Can dental therapists immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
A: Yes. This occupation falls under NOC 32109, meeting FSW or CEC requirements, but you must first obtain an ECA and meet provincial licensing requirements.

**Q: What are the requirements to become a registered dental therapist in Canada?**
A: Must complete an accredited program (2-3 years), pass national or provincial exams, and register with the provincial regulatory body. Certification from another province may require additional training.
