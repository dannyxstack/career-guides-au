# Police Commissioned Officers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 40010 (NOC)**

A Canadian Policing Commission Officer (NOC 40010) leads police teams, develops strategies, manages budgets and resources, and is a senior management role. This occupation typically requires years of police experience and internal promotion, with limited immigration pathways mainly suitable for applicants with Canadian experience.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from constable, advancing to sergeant, inspector, superintendent and other command positions after years of experience. Promotion assessments are rigorous, requiring written exams, interviews, and assessment center tests. Officers with university degrees, management experience, and specialized training have an advantage.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of a police officer at the Canadian Police Commission?**
A: Salary range from C$60,000-C$80,000 entry-level to C$130,000-C$200,000 senior, depending on province, city, and position level. Higher salaries in big cities but also higher cost of living.

**Q: Can foreign police officers become police officers on the Canadian Police Service Council through immigration?**
A: Extremely difficult. This role typically requires Canadian citizenship, years of local police experience, and internal promotion. Even experienced foreign officers need to immigrate first and start from the bottom, as many police forces do not accept international qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a police officer on the Canadian Police Services Board?**
A: Typically requires: Canadian citizenship or permanent residency, post-secondary education (university degree preferred), completion of provincial police academy training, at least 5-10 years of police experience, no criminal record, good physical and mental fitness, and passing a rigorous selection process.
