# Material handlers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 75101 (NOC)**

In Canada, forklift operators are typically classified as material handlers (NOC 75101), working in warehouses, distribution centers, and factories. This occupation falls under TEER 5, generally requiring a high school diploma and short-term training. However, with Australian experience, you can apply for the Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) or some streams under Express Entry (EE), making it a feasible blue-collar pathway for Canadian immigration.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a forklift operator in Canada?**
A: According to Canada's Job Bank, the national average annual salary is about C$42,000, with a median hourly wage of about C$22. Entry-level positions typically earn C$32,000-44,000, while senior supervisors can earn over C$56,000. Salaries vary significantly by province and industry.

**Q: Can a forklift operator immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
A: Direct EE application is difficult as NOC 75101 is TEER 5, usually not eligible for FSW or CEC independently. But can apply through PNP (e.g., Ontario Employer Job Offer) or gain experience via LMIA work permit. Suggest seeking employer sponsorship or consulting an RCIC immigration consultant.

**Q: Is a Canadian forklift operator certificate required?**
A: Most employers mandate certification. Must complete a provincial-approved training course and pass theoretical and practical tests. Training typically lasts 1-2 weeks, costing about CAD 500-2000. Australian certificates may not be directly recognized but can exempt part of the training.
