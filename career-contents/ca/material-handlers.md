# Material handlers（Material handlers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：75101 – Material handlers。**

In Canada, forklift drivers are typically classified under material handlers (NOC 75101), working in warehouses, distribution centers and factories. This TEER 5 occupation requires high school and short training, yet Australian experience can support Provincial Nominee (PNP) or Express Entry (EE) applications, offering a viable blue-collar migration path.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary of a forklift driver in Canada?**
答：According to Canada's Job Bank, the national average salary is about CAD 42,000, with median hourly wage around CAD 22. Entry-level range is CAD 32,000-44,000, while senior supervisors can exceed CAD 56,000, varying by province and industry.

**问：Can a forklift driver immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
答：Direct EE application is challenging as NOC 75101 is TEER 5, typically not eligible for FSW or CEC alone. However, you can use Provincial Nominee (e.g., Ontario Employer Job Offer) or obtain a LMIA work visa to gain experience and later apply. Employer sponsorship is key.

**问：Do I need a Canadian forklift license?**
答：Most employers require a valid forklift certificate issued by a provincially recognized training program. Training takes 1–2 weeks and costs CAD 500–2,000. Australian certificates may not be directly recognized, but can exempt part of the training.
