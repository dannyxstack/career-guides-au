# Electronic service technicians (household and business equipment)（Electronic service technicians (household and business equipment)）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：22311 – Electronic service technicians (household and business equipment)。**

Diagnose, repair, and maintain household and business electronic equipment such as computers, printers, audio systems, and medical devices.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry (FSW)（Federal Skilled Worker） | 适合技术移民，需满足工作经验、语言等要求 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份将电子维修技师列为紧缺职业，可获省提名加分 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主担保工签，适合已有雇主offer的申请人 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary ceiling for electronic service technicians?**
答：Senior technicians can earn up to CAD 85,000 annually; self-employment or specializing in high-end equipment may yield higher.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada as an electronic service technician?**
答：You can apply through Express Entry or PNP; some provinces consider it in demand, but no special fast track.
