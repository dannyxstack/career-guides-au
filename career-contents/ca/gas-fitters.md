# Gas fitters（Gas fitters）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72302 – Gas fitters。**

Gas fitters install, maintain, and repair gas piping, appliances, and systems in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings, ensuring safety and code compliance.

|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker / Canadian Experience Class） | 如果符合技术工人要求，可通过快速通道申请永久居留；通常需要工作邀请或省级提名加分 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for gas fitters in Canada?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around CAD 35,000-45,000; licensed workers earn CAD 55,000-75,000; senior fitters can exceed CAD 95,000. Overtime and remote area premiums can boost income.

**问：How can overseas gas fitters immigrate to Canada?**
答：Common pathways include Express Entry (with PNP nomination), obtaining a work permit via LMIA and then applying for permanent residence, or direct PNP application. Provincial certification greatly improves success.
