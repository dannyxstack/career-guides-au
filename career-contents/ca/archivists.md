# Archivists（Archivists）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：51102 – Archivists。**

Archivists appraise, organize, preserve, and provide access to archival records and historical documents, ensuring the long-term conservation and usability of valuable materials in government, museums, libraries, and corporate settings.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for archivists?**
答：Entry-level archivists earn around CAD 45,000-55,000, mid-level CAD 55,000-75,000, and senior managers up to CAD 95,000 annually. Government positions often pay higher than private sector.

**问：What immigration pathways are available for archivists?**
答：Archivists can immigrate via Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario, BC). Some provinces prioritize this occupation, offering 600 CRS points. LMIA-based work permits also lead to permanent residence.

**问：What education is needed to become an archivist?**
答：A master's degree in archival studies or information management (e.g., from UBC or University of Toronto) is typically required. A bachelor's in history or library science is common. Digital archiving certifications are beneficial.
