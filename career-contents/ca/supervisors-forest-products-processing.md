# Supervisors, forest products processing（Supervisors, forest products processing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：92014 – Supervisors, forest products processing。**

Supervise daily operations in forest products processing plants (e.g., sawmills, pulp mills), coordinate worker activities, ensure production efficiency and product quality, and maintain safety standards.
