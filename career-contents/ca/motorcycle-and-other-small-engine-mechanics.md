# Motorcycle and other small engine mechanics（Motorcycle and other small engine mechanics）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72423 – Motorcycle and other small engine mechanics。**

In Canada, motorcycle mechanics repair, maintain, and modify motorcycles and similar vehicles. This occupation is eligible for immigration via Provincial Nominee Programs or employer sponsorship, but not mainstream in Express Entry.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 通过省提名（如安省技工省提名）获得永久居民身份 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省试点项目，雇主担保技工 |
| Subclass RNIP（Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot） | 偏远地区移民试点，适合技工类职业 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for motorcycle mechanics in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 35,000-45,000; mid-level: CAD 45,000-60,000; senior: CAD 60,000-80,000. Red Seal certification boosts earnings.

**问：How can motorcycle mechanics immigrate to Canada?**
答：Mainly through Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) or employer sponsorship, such as AIP or RNIP.

**问：What certifications are needed for motorcycle mechanics in Canada?**
答：Red Seal endorsement is optional but recommended; some provinces require a provincial license. Language proficiency of CLB 4 or higher is required for immigration.
