# Records management technicians（Records management technicians）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：12101 – Records management technicians。**

In Canada, records managers organize, maintain, and manage electronic and paper records, ensuring compliance, security, and accessibility. This occupation is eligible for Express Entry or PNP immigration, with stable demand.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry） | 符合FSW或CEC类别，需NOC TEER 2（12101属TEER 2），CRS分数达标即可获邀 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 多数省份将档案管理员列入技能优先或紧缺职业清单，如安省、BC省、阿省 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，适合有工作offer的申请人 |

**PR难度：中低（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Records management technicians？**
- 细心、有条理，喜欢处理文档及信息系统的人
- 有图书馆、档案或信息管理教育背景的毕业生
- 寻求稳定办公室工作且希望移民加拿大的专业人士

**谁不适合学Records management technicians？**
- 不喜欢重复性文书工作的人
- 寻求高薪或快速晋升通道的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a records manager in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from CAD 42,000 to 55,000, intermediate from CAD 55,000 to 70,000, and senior up to CAD 95,000+ with good government benefits.

**问：Is it difficult for records managers to immigrate to Canada?**
答：This occupation is NOC B (TEER 2), eligible for Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and PNP. Demand is stable; having a job offer can increase success chances.
