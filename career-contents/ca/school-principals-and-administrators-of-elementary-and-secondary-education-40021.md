# School Principals and Administrators of Elementary and Secondary Education — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 40021 (NOC)**

A regional education manager in Canada is typically classified as a primary/secondary education administrator (NOC 40021), responsible for overseeing the operations, curriculum standards, and goal achievement of regional schools. This occupation is eligible for Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) or Express Entry (requires employer support), with moderate immigration feasibility.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a regional education manager in Canada?**
A: According to Job Bank, Canadian elementary and secondary school education administrators (NOC 40021) have an average hourly wage of about CAD 45 and an annual salary of about CAD 85,000-110,000, but it varies greatly by region and experience.

**Q: How can a Regional Education Manager immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
A: Must meet FSW or CEC conditions. NOC 40021 is a TEER 1 occupation, typically requiring IELTS General Training scores of 6 or above in each band and at least 1 year of continuous work experience. If points are insufficient, can combine with a provincial nomination (+600 points).

**Q: Is a provincial teaching license mandatory to work in education management in Canada?**
A: Most provinces (e.g., Ontario, BC) mandate a teaching license or educational administrator certificate. Some provinces like Quebec have special requirements. Experience from other provinces may require recertification.
