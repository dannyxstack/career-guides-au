# Dental technologists and technicians（Dental technologists and technicians）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：32112 – Dental technologists and technicians。**

Dental technologists and technicians construct and repair dental prostheses such as dentures, crowns, and bridges. They work from dentists' prescriptions and impressions, using various materials and equipment, including CAD/CAM technology.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for dental technologists?**
答：Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level technicians earn CAD 35,000-45,000 per year, intermediate CAD 45,000-60,000, and senior technicians or lab managers CAD 60,000-80,000.

**问：What are the immigration pathways for dental technologists to Canada?**
答：Main pathways include Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP). NOC 32112 is TEER 2 and qualifies for FSW. Provinces like Ontario and BC have specific streams for healthcare occupations. Employer-sponsored LMIA can also lead to work permit and PR.

**问：Do dental technologists need provincial licensure?**
答：Most provinces require registration or licensure. For example, Ontario requires registration with the College of Dental Technologists of Ontario. Other provinces like BC require certification. Requirements typically include completing an accredited program, passing exams, and continuing education.
