# Auditors and Accountants（Auditors and Accountants）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：11100 – Auditors and Accountants。**

Auditors in Canada examine financial statements and internal controls to ensure accuracy and compliance. This TEER 1 occupation is eligible for Express Entry and PNP, offering a clear migration pathway.

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 符合条件的申请人可通过联邦快速通道获得永居，需满足CRS分数要求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for auditors in Canada?**
答：Entry-level auditors earn CAD 45,000-65,000, mid-level CAD 65,000-90,000, and senior roles CAD 90,000-140,000 annually. CPA certification and management positions significantly increase earnings.

**问：What are the main immigration pathways for auditors to Canada?**
答：Main pathways include Express Entry (FSW/CEC), Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario, BC, Saskatchewan), and the Atlantic Immigration Program. ECA and language tests are required. NOC 11100, TEER 1, offers strong immigration prospects.

**问：Do auditors need local certification in Canada?**
答：While not mandatory, obtaining the CPA Canada designation is highly recommended for job opportunities and career advancement. Many employers prefer or require it, and it is crucial for management roles.
