# Audiologists and speech-language pathologists（Audiologists and speech-language pathologists）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：31112 – Audiologists and speech-language pathologists。**

Speech-language pathologists in Canada assess, diagnose, and treat communication and swallowing disorders. The occupation sees stable demand due to aging population and increased survival rates of premature infants, making it a key target for skilled immigration (Express Entry / PNP).

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker)） | 通过联邦快速通道，满足工作经验和语言要求即可申请，适合海外申请人。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省提名，如安省、BC省、阿省均有针对医疗职业的优先类别。 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主驱动移民，适合有雇主offer的申请人。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for speech-language pathologists in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 65,000-80,000; mid-level: CAD 80,000-100,000; senior: up to CAD 120,000. Varies by province, setting, and experience.

**问：How can overseas speech-language pathologists immigrate to Canada?**
答：Primarily via Express Entry (FSW) or PNP. ECA credential assessment and language test are required. With a job offer, AIP or employer sponsorship also possible.
