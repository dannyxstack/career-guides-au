## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression from junior soldier to non-commissioned officer, officer, and senior commander, requiring continuous training, practical experience, and leadership evaluation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Fire coordination and artillery parameter calculation are automatically generated by AI.
- Battlefield intelligence fusion and threat prioritisation taken over by algorithms
- Logistics supply route planning and resource scheduling automation
- Standardized readiness reporting and log generation completed by AI

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation training system improves collaborative tactical drill efficiency
- Augmented reality headsets overlay battlefield information to aid rapid decision-making
- Real-time integration of drone and ground sensor data for situational awareness
- Smart exoskeletons reduce load and enhance individual soldier sustained combat capability
- AI-assisted rapid triage and injury prediction in healthcare

**Human moat:**

- Rapid response and adaptive judgment in extreme environments
- Fellowship trust and mental resilience in close-quarters combat
- Moral and legal autonomous decision-making when violating rules of engagement
- Complex interpersonal communication and cross-cultural peacekeeping negotiations.
- Covert mobility and intuitive experience in unstructured terrain

**Skills to build:**

- Drone and robot formation operations and coordination
- Interpretation and use of operational data platforms (e.g., COP)
- Basic AI/ML concepts and battlefield applications
- Proficient use of augmented reality and command-and-control software
- Mental health training and stress management techniques
- Digital coordination capability for cross-departmental joint operations

**Key growth areas:**

- Defense spending
- Peacekeeping
- Cyber warfare
- Arctic sovereignty

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of Canadian military personnel?**
A: Entry-level about CAD 35,000-50,000/year, senior NCOs or officers can reach CAD 80,000-120,000/year, plus allowances and benefits.

**Q: Can immigrants become combatants in the Canadian Armed Forces?**
A: No, this occupation is only open to Canadian citizens. Immigrants need to obtain citizenship first, which usually takes several years.
