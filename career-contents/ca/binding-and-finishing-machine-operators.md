# Binding and finishing machine operators（Binding and finishing machine operators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：94152 – Binding and finishing machine operators。**

Operate binding, folding, collating, cutting and other post-press finishing equipment to convert printed materials into final products such as books, magazines, and catalogs. This occupation works in printing plants and publishing companies, requiring attention to detail and basic mechanical skills.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for binding machine operators?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about CAD 32,000-40,000, mid-level CAD 40,000-50,000, and senior lead hands up to CAD 50,000-60,000. Overtime and shift differentials may increase earnings.

**问：Is this occupation easy to immigrate to Canada?**
答：This is a TEER 4 occupation, making direct Express Entry immigration challenging. It typically requires LMIA or provincial nomination. Employer sponsorship can still lead to permanent residence.
