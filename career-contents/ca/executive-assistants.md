# Executive assistants（Executive assistants）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：12100 – Executive assistants。**

Executive assistants provide comprehensive administrative support to senior executives, including schedule management, meeting coordination, document processing, and communication liaison. This role requires excellent organizational, communication, and confidentiality skills.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Executive assistants？**
- 具备优秀组织、多任务处理和沟通能力者
- 喜欢在快节奏环境中为高层管理者提供支持

**谁不适合学Executive assistants？**
- 不擅长时间管理和优先级排序者
- 应对高压环境时易焦虑者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for executive assistants in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 40,000-55,000; Mid-level: CAD 55,000-75,000; Senior: CAD 75,000-95,000. Varies by industry, location, and experience.

**问：How can executive assistants immigrate to Canada?**
答：They may apply through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or PNP, meeting requirements such as work experience and language (CLB 7). LMIA employer sponsorship is another option.

**问：What are the career prospects for executive assistants?**
答：They can advance to senior executive assistant, office manager, or administrative manager. Career shifts to HR or project management are also possible.
