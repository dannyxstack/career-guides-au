# Tour and travel guides（Tour and travel guides）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：64314 – Tour and travel guides。**

Provide guided tours, itinerary planning, and travel booking services covering city tours, ecotourism, and cultural heritage. Canada's tourism recovery boosts demand for Mandarin-speaking guides; immigration possible via PNP or EE, but limited by language and licensing.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary for tour guides in Canada?**
答：Entry-level CAD 25,000-35,000, mid-level CAD 35,000-50,000, senior CAD 45,000-65,000; tips 10-30% of income. Seasonal, possible winter unemployment.

**问：Can a tour guide immigrate via Express Entry?**
答：Yes, NOC 64314 (B category) qualifies for FSW/CEC. But CRS score needs to be high (usually 470+); recommend gaining Canadian experience or PNP.

**问：What licenses are needed to be a tour guide in Canada?**
答：Some provinces like BC require a guide license (from Tourism BC), Quebec as well. Ontario does not mandate, but certification improves job prospects.
