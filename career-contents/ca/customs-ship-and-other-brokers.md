# Customs, ship and other brokers（Customs, ship and other brokers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：13200 – Customs, ship and other brokers。**

Represent clients in customs clearance, ship brokering, and freight forwarding to ensure lawful cross-border movement of goods.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|

| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主担保工签，可加分申请PR |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for customs brokers?**
答：Entry-level CAD 40,000-55,000, mid-level CAD 55,000-75,000, senior CAD 75,000-100,000, management up to CAD 120,000.

**问：How can a foreigner immigrate to Canada through this occupation?**
答：Apply via Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or PNP, meeting work experience, language, and education requirements; alternatively, LMIA employer sponsorship.

**问：Is a Canadian customs broker license required?**
答：Yes, you must pass the CBSA exam to obtain a customs broker license to practice.
