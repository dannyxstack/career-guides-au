# Customs, ship and other brokers（Customs, ship and other brokers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：12200 – Customs, ship and other brokers。**

In Canada, customs brokers or border services officers handle import/export clearance, cargo inspection, and regulatory compliance, employed by the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) or private brokerage firms. This occupation is eligible for Express Entry or Provincial Nominee Programs, requiring relevant certification or CBSA training.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a customs officer in Canada?**
答：CBSA officer starting salary is around CAD 55,000, senior up to CAD 100,000; private brokerage start at CAD 45,000-50,000, licensed broker up to CAD 70,000+.

**问：Can a Chinese customs officer directly immigrate to Canada for the same job?**
答：Yes, but must meet Express Entry requirements (language, ECA), and may need a Canadian customs broker licence or apply for CBSA positions. Chinese experience is recognized but needs proof.

**问：Is the customs broker licence exam difficult?**
答：Pass rate is low (about 40%); requires study of customs regulations, tariff classification; training courses recommended.
