# Customs, ship and other brokers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 12200 (NOC)**

In Canada, customs brokers or border services officers handle import/export clearance, cargo inspection, and regulatory enforcement, and can be employed by the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) or private brokerage firms. This occupation can lead to immigration through Express Entry or provincial nomination, but requires relevant qualifications or completion of CBSA training.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level customs brokers or CBSA officers can advance to senior analysts, supervisors, or managers; licensed brokers (CIBS) can practice independently or open a brokerage. CBSA has enforcement, compliance, and other career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated import/export document preparation and data entry

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of Canadian customs officers?**
A: CBSA officers start at about CAD $55,000, senior up to $100,000; private customs brokers entry-level about $45,000–50,000, with license over $70,000.

**Q: Can a Chinese customs officer background directly immigrate to Canada for the same job?**
A: Yes, but must meet Express Entry conditions (language, education assessment) and may need to obtain a Canadian customs broker license or apply for CBSA positions. Domestic experience is recognized but needs proof.

**Q: Is the customs broker license exam difficult?**
A: Pass rate is low (about 40%), requires learning customs regulations, tariff classification, etc.; recommended to take training courses.
