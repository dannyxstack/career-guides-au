# Library and public archive technicians（Library and public archive technicians）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：52100 – Library and public archive technicians。**

Library and public archive technicians assist in daily operations of libraries and archives, including cataloging, circulation, materials organization, and user inquiries. They maintain accessibility of information resources, supporting learning and research needs of the public and educational institutions.
