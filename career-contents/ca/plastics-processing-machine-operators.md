# Plastics processing machine operators（Plastics processing machine operators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：94111 – Plastics processing machine operators。**

Operate plastic processing machines such as injection molders, extruders, and blow molders to manufacture plastic products. Duties include setting up machines, monitoring operations, adjusting parameters, and performing basic maintenance. Typically work in manufacturing plants, requiring physical stamina and attention to detail.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主需证明无法找到本地工人，可申请LMIA支持工签。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 某些省份如安大略、阿尔伯塔可能提名有雇主offer的技工。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for plastics processing machine operators?**
答：Entry-level operators earn about CAD 32,000 to 40,000 annually, while experienced operators can earn CAD 52,000 to 65,000. Salary depends on province, plant size, and experience.

**问：Is this occupation eligible for immigration to Canada?**
答：This occupation typically requires an LMIA employer sponsorship; direct Express Entry is difficult. However, some provinces may nominate applicants with this background through PNP.

**问：What education is required?**
答：High school diploma is usually required, with most skills learned on the job. A one-year certificate in plastics processing technology from a college can improve job prospects.
