# Social Workers（Social Workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：41300 – Social Workers。**

Social workers assess, counsel, and provide crisis intervention and referrals in fields like child protection, mental health, and elderly care. In Canada, this occupation has stable demand and is eligible for Express Entry and PNP immigration, with better prospects in shortage areas.

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|

| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省如安省、BC省、阿省有社工类别，优先邀请 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Social Workers？**
- 具备同理心和沟通能力，愿意服务弱势群体
- 有心理学、社会学或社会工作背景的留学或移民申请人
- 能适应跨文化环境，尤其是原住民社区服务者

**谁不适合学Social Workers？**
- 对高压、情感消耗性工作敏感者
- 无法接受省监管考试或注册要求者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for social workers in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salary is around CAD 42,000-55,000, mid-level CAD 55,000-72,000, and senior CAD 72,000-95,000 annually. Public sector offers stability, while private practice can earn more.

**问：How can overseas social workers immigrate to Canada?**
答：Main pathways are Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP). An ECA for your degree, language test results, and possibly provincial registration are required. Canadian authorities recognize Australian bachelor's/master's degrees.

**问：What steps are required to register as a social worker?**
答：Obtain a CSWE-accredited degree (or assessment for foreign degrees), meet work experience requirements, pass the provincial regulatory exam (e.g., OCSWSSW in Ontario), and possibly meet language benchmarks.
