# Storekeepers and partspersons — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 14401 (NOC)**

Responsible for managing inventory, receiving and storing goods, filling orders, and maintaining parts records to ensure efficient and accurate warehouse operations.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary of a warehouse manager in Canada?**
A: Entry-level: CAD 32,000-40,000; mid-level: CAD 40,000-52,000; senior: CAD 52,000-65,000, depending on province and experience.

**Q: Can a warehouse manager migrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
A: Generally not a directly in-demand occupation, with low Express Entry scores; immigration mainly through LMIA employer sponsorship or a few provincial nominations (e.g., Manitoba).

**Q: How to advance from warehouse clerk?**
A: After gaining experience, obtaining a supply chain certificate can lead to promotion to warehouse supervisor, logistics coordinator, or supply chain manager.
