# Storekeepers and partspersons（Storekeepers and partspersons）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：14401 – Storekeepers and partspersons。**

Manage inventory, receive and store goods, fill orders, and maintain parts records to ensure efficient and accurate warehouse operations.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主担保工签，需证明无法招聘本地人 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份可能将仓库管理员列为紧缺职业，可走省提名 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for storekeepers in Canada?**
答：Entry-level around CAD 32,000-40,000, mid-level CAD 40,000-52,000, senior CAD 52,000-65,000, varying by province and experience.

**问：Can storekeepers immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
答：Usually not a high-demand occupation; pathways include LMIA employer sponsorship or limited provincial nominations (e.g., Manitoba).

**问：How to advance from storekeeper?**
答：Gain experience and obtain supply chain certification to become a warehouse supervisor, logistics coordinator, or supply chain manager.
