# Dry cleaning and laundry occupations — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 67440 (NOC)**

Operating dry cleaning and laundry equipment to clean, decontaminate, and finish clothing and textiles. This occupation is typically classified as low-skilled for Canadian skilled migration, with limited immigration pathways.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a dry cleaner in Canada?**
A: Junior dry cleaner hourly wage approx. CAD 14-17, annual salary approx. CAD 28,000-35,000; mid-senior slightly higher, but cap around CAD 50,000.

**Q: Can dry cleaners immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
A: Dry cleaners are low-skilled occupations and typically do not meet Express Entry requirements. Immigration is mainly through Provincial Nominee Programs (requiring employer sponsorship) or the Atlantic Immigration Pilot.

**Q: Is there a career development path for dry cleaners?**
A: Can advance to laundry supervisor, manager, or start your own dry-cleaning business. Accumulating management experience and client base is key.
