# Dry cleaning and laundry occupations（Dry cleaning and laundry occupations）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：67440 – Dry cleaning and laundry occupations。**

Operate dry cleaning and laundry equipment to clean, stain-remove, and finish garments and textiles. This occupation is typically classified as lower-skilled in Canadian immigration, with limited pathways.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 某些省份（如萨省、曼省）的省提名项目可能包括低技能职业，但通常需要雇主担保 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋省份试点项目，接受低技能职业，需雇主offer |
| Subclass Employer-Specific（Employer-Specific Work Permit） | 通过劳动力市场影响评估获得工签，后续可申请移民 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for dry cleaners in Canada?**
答：Entry-level dry cleaners earn about CAD14-17 per hour, annual salary around CAD28,000-35,000; senior roles may reach CAD50,000.

**问：Can dry cleaners immigrate through skilled worker programs?**
答：Dry cleaners are generally considered low-skilled and ineligible for Express Entry. Immigration is possible through Provincial Nominee Programs (with employer sponsorship) or the Atlantic Immigration Program.

**问：Is there a career progression for dry cleaners?**
答：Yes, you can advance to supervisor, manager, or start your own dry cleaning business. Gaining management experience and a customer base is key.
