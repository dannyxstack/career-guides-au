# Contractors and supervisors, landscaping, grounds maintenance and horticulture services — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 82031 (NOC)**

Arborists or forestry workers in Canada are typically classified as landscape and horticulture services supervisors/contractors, responsible for tree pruning, removal, assessment, and maintenance. This occupation can immigrate through Provincial Nominee Program or Atlantic Immigration Program, with high demand especially in Nova Scotia, Ontario, and BC.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

From entry-level arborist or forestry worker, can advance to team supervisor, project manager, or obtain ISA certification for senior technical roles. Opportunities also exist to start a horticultural services company.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Plant health diagnosis report writing (auto-generated by AI image recognition)
- Irrigation system daily monitoring and scheduling (IoT+AI automatic optimization)
- Document filing and compliance records (AI auto-organizes)
- Initial client consultation and quote generation (chatbot + fee model)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Crown structure and root risk assessment (AI-assisted 3D scan analysis)
- Early pest and disease warning (AI analyzing historical and weather data).
- Pruning and removal plan optimization (AI simulates best path and tools).
- Sustainable garden design (AI recommends plant combinations and maintenance plans)

**Human moat:**

- On-site safety decisions for high-risk tree removal
- Complex negotiations and trust-building with clients and municipalities
- Improvising in response to unexpected weather or accidents
- On-site management leading teams through high-intensity physical work

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and image analysis.
- GIS and smart irrigation system configuration
- Use of AI pest and disease diagnosis tools
- Basic data analysis and reporting skills
- Customer Relationship Management and Digital Communication
- Knowledge of sustainable horticulture and ecological restoration

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban Forestry
- Provincial Nominee
- Employer Sponsorship
- Green Economy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average hourly wage for an arborist in Canada?**
A: Entry-level: ~20-25 CAD/hour, mid-level: 25-35 CAD/hour, senior supervisor: up to 40 CAD/hour.

**Q: Can forestry workers/arborists immigrate to Canada via Express Entry?**
A: Typically require a job offer from a Canadian employer with NOC 0AB to enter the EE pool. More common routes are PNP or Atlantic Immigration Pilot.

**Q: Is ISA certification required to work in Canada?**
A: Not mandatory, but obtaining the ISA International Arborist certification can significantly enhance job opportunities and salary. Provinces may have additional safety training requirements for high-altitude work.
