# Contractors and supervisors, landscaping, grounds maintenance and horticulture services（Contractors and supervisors, landscaping, grounds maintenance and horticulture services）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：82031 – Contractors and supervisors, landscaping, grounds maintenance and horticulture services。**

Arborists and forestry workers in Canada typically fall under landscaping and grounds maintenance supervisors, managing tree pruning, removal, assessment, and care. This occupation can qualify for provincial nominee or Atlantic Immigration Program, with strong demand in Nova Scotia, Ontario, and British Columbia.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average hourly wage for an arborist in Canada?**
答：Junior arborists earn around CAD 20-25/hr, mid-level CAD 25-35/hr, and senior supervisors up to CAD 40/hr.

**问：Can forestry workers/arborists immigrate via Express Entry?**
答：Generally, you need a Canadian job offer in NOC 0AB to enter the pool; PNP or AIP is more common.

**问：Is ISA certification required to work in Canada?**
答：Not mandatory, but ISA certification significantly boosts job prospects and salary. Some provinces may require additional safety training for working at heights.
