# Mathematicians, statisticians and actuaries（Mathematicians, statisticians and actuaries）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：21210 – Mathematicians, statisticians and actuaries。**

Mathematicians, statisticians and actuaries apply mathematical and statistical methods to solve practical problems in finance, insurance, government and research, involving risk assessment, data analysis and predictive modeling.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for actuaries in Canada?**
答：Entry-level actuaries earn around CAD 60,000-80,000, senior actuaries can earn over CAD 150,000, and chief actuaries can exceed CAD 200,000.

**问：How can statisticians immigrate to Canada?**
答：Statisticians are NOC A occupations and can apply for Express Entry via FSW or CEC, or through Provincial Nominee Programs.

**问：What education is needed to become an actuary?**
答：A bachelor's degree in actuarial science or related field, plus passing actuarial exams; some positions require a master's degree.
