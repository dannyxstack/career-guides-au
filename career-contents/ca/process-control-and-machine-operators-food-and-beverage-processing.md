## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

From entry-level operator to senior brewer or production manager, requiring experience accumulation and brewing certifications; can also transition to quality control or R&D roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine recording and reporting of production data
- Simple quality inspection (e.g., visual appearance check)
- Regular patrols and manual recording of equipment status
- Simple adjustment of recipe parameters (e.g., temperature, time).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Predictive maintenance planning based on sensor data
- AI optimization of brewing/fermentation process parameters.
- Automated quality control image recognition assistance
- Intelligent decision support for production scheduling and inventory
- Digital twin simulation to optimize process efficiency

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of equipment anomalies
- Subjective quality assessment of taste, flavor, etc.
- Flexibly respond to unexpected breakdowns and process adjustments.
- Interpersonal communication with suppliers and quality inspection departments

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (Excel, Power BI)
- PLC and SCADA system operation
- Python scripts for data scraping and simple modeling
- Food safety and HACCP certification
- Digital record management and electronic batch record systems.

**Key growth areas:**

- PNP
- Employer-Sponsored
- Food & Beverage
- Manufacturing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary of brewery workers?**
A: Junior annual salary: about CAD 30,000-40,000, senior: up to CAD 55,000-75,000, depending on winery size and experience.

**Q: Is it easy for distillery workers to immigrate to Canada?**
A: This occupation falls under NOC TEER 4, usually requiring employer sponsorship or provincial nomination, with limited direct Express Entry opportunities.

**Q: What are the future career prospects?**
A: Promotion to winemaker, production supervisor or quality control specialist; obtaining brewing certifications (e.g., MBAA) aids career development.
