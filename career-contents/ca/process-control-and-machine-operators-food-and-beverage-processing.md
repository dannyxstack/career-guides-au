# Process control and machine operators, food and beverage processing（Process control and machine operators, food and beverage processing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：94140 – Process control and machine operators, food and beverage processing。**

Operate equipment for brewing, fermentation, filtration, and packaging in breweries; suitable for immigration via PNP or employer sponsorship.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 通过省提名项目（如安省雇主担保、BC省技术移民）申请，需雇主offer |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋四省雇主担保，适合在海洋省份找工作 |
| Subclass TFWP（Temporary Foreign Worker Program） | 先获工签工作，再积累经验转永居 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a brewery worker?**
答：Entry-level salary is around CAD 30,000-40,000, while senior roles can reach CAD 55,000-75,000, depending on brewery size and experience.

**问：Is it easy for brewery workers to immigrate to Canada?**
答：This occupation is NOC TEER 4, usually requiring employer sponsorship or provincial nomination, with limited direct Express Entry options.

**问：What is the career development like?**
答：Opportunities to advance to brewer, production supervisor, or quality control specialist; certifications like MBAA can enhance career growth.
