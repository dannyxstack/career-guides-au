# Concrete finishers — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 73100 (NOC)**

In Canada, concrete workers are responsible for pouring, leveling, and curing concrete, commonly used in foundations, roads, and building structures. This occupation is a trade and eligible for permanent residency through Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) or Federal Skilled Trades Program (FSTP), with moderate feasibility for skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior concrete workers can gain experience to advance to foreman or contractor, or become Red Seal tradespeople through certification. Some transition to related fields like bricklaying or formworking.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Concrete strength data recording and report generation
- Curing time calculation based on weather and material parameters
- Pouring progress tracking and automatic scheduling
- Material quantity estimation and replenishment reminders

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI vision inspection systems to monitor concrete surface flatness and cracks in real time
- Optimise pouring sequence and formwork design through BIM models and AI
- Using drones and AI for large-area ground leveling measurement
- Using AI to predict concrete strength during curing and optimize formwork removal time
- Automatically adjusts water-to-cement ratio via smart devices to improve quality consistency

**Human moat:**

- Hand troweling and burnishing techniques for complex surfaces
- On-site assessment of concrete workability and timely adjustment
- Ability to work flexibly in different weather and site conditions
- Real-time communication with construction team to resolve unexpected issues

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of smart equipment like laser screeds and automatic trowels
- BIM model reading and on-site coordinate positioning
- Concrete strength prediction AI tool usage
- Drone inspection and data analysis fundamentals
- Knowledge of green concrete additives and new materials.
- Basic English communication (for international projects)

**Key growth areas:**

- Skilled Trades
- Red Seal
- Infrastructure
- PNP

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for concrete finishers in Canada?**
A: Entry-level hourly wage about CAD 17-22, annual income CAD 35,000-45,000; mid-to-senior up to CAD 65,000-85,000. Red Seal certification or becoming a foreperson increases income.

**Q: What immigration pathways to PR are available for concreters?**
A: Main pathways are Federal Skilled Trades Program (FSTP), provincial skilled trades streams (e.g., Ontario, BC, Alberta), and Atlantic Immigration Program. Most require a Canadian job offer or provincial certification.

**Q: What certificates are needed to work as a concrete worker in Canada?**
A: Most provinces require provincial trade certification, such as completing an apprenticeship program and passing an exam. Red Seal certification facilitates interprovincial work, is not mandatory but enhances employment and immigration opportunities.
