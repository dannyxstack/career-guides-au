# Concrete finishers（Concrete finishers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：73100 – Concrete finishers。**

Concrete finishers in Canada prepare, pour, level, and cure concrete for foundations, roads, and structures. This skilled trade offers immigration pathways through Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) and the Federal Skilled Trades Program (FSTP), with moderate feasibility.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass FSTP（Federal Skilled Trades Program） | 联邦技工移民，要求两年技工经验、语言CLB 5、加拿大工作offer或证书。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 各省提名，如安大略、BC省技工类别，需雇主担保或省行业认证。 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋移民计划，适合在海洋省份有雇主offer的技工。 |
| Subclass Rural and Northern（Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot） | 偏远地区试点，部分社区需要技工，可快速获得PR。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for concrete finishers in Canada?**
答：Entry-level earns CAD $17-$22/hour ($35k-$45k annual); experienced with Red Seal earns $65k-$85k; foremen can exceed $100k.

**问：What immigration pathways are available for concrete finishers?**
答：Main routes: Federal Skilled Trades Program (FSTP), Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) skilled trades streams (e.g., Ontario, BC, Alberta), and Atlantic Immigration Program. Most require a job offer or provincial certification.

**问：What certifications are needed to work as a concrete finisher in Canada?**
答：Most provinces require a provincial trade certificate after completing apprenticeship and exam. Red Seal endorsement is optional but enhances job mobility and immigration prospects.
