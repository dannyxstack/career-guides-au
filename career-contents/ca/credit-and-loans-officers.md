# Credit and Loans Officers（Credit and Loans Officers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：13201 – Credit and Loans Officers。**

Credit and loans officers work in banks, credit unions, and financial companies in Canada, evaluating loan applications, analyzing credit risk, and approving or recommending loans. This occupation is classified under NOC 13201, generally supporting immigration applications through Express Entry or Provincial Nominee Programs.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a credit and loans officer in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 35,000-45,000; Mid-level: CAD 45,000-60,000; Senior: CAD 60,000-80,000, varying by province and institution.

**问：Can credit and loans officers immigrate to Canada through Express Entry?**
答：Yes, this occupation is NOC 13201 (TEER 3), eligible for FSW or CEC streams if language, education, and work experience requirements are met.

**问：Which PNP streams are suitable for credit and loans officers?**
答：Ontario Human Capital Priorities, BC Skilled Worker, Saskatchewan or Manitoba PNP, often requiring a job offer.
