# Painters and Decorators（Painters and Decorators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：73112 – Painters and Decorators。**

Painters and decorators apply coatings to interior and exterior surfaces in Canada, requiring apprenticeship or trade certification. This skilled trade is eligible for immigration via provincial nominee or employer sponsorship.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for painters in Canada?**
答：Entry-level painters earn around CAD 35,000-45,000; experienced workers CAD 45,000-60,000; Red Seal or supervisors CAD 60,000-80,000, varying by province.

**问：What are the immigration pathways for painters to Canada?**
答：The most feasible is Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) for skilled trades, such as Ontario's Skilled Trades Stream, often requiring employer support. Express Entry can also be combined.

**问：What certifications are needed to work as a painter in Canada?**
答：Provincial apprenticeship registration is mandatory; Red Seal certification is voluntary but highly recommended for nationwide mobility.
