# Glaziers（Glaziers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：72411 – Glaziers。**

In Canada, glaziers cut, install, and replace glass in commercial and residential buildings, including curtain walls, windows, and doors. With ongoing construction demand and green retrofits, this trade is viable for skilled immigration, especially via provincial nominees.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 通过省提名（如Ontario In-Demand Skills, BC PNP Skilled Worker）申请，多数省份对建筑技工有优先通道 |
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (Federal Skilled Trades)） | 联邦技工移民（FSTP），需满足雅思、工作经验等要求，适合已有雇主offer的申请人 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋移民试点，雇主担保，适用于在新斯科舍等省份有工作机会的玻璃安装工 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Glaziers？**
- 身体强壮、动手能力强，不恐高
- 愿意从事体力劳动和户外作业
- 有志于通过技术移民加拿大的蓝领工人

**谁不适合学Glaziers？**
- 无法承受高空作业和重体力劳动
- 对玻璃切割和安装细节缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a glazier in Canada?**
答：According to recent data, the median annual salary for a glazier in Canada is about CAD 55,000, with experienced workers earning up to CAD 80,000 or more, depending on province and experience.

**问：Is it easy for a glazier to immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
答：Relatively easy. This occupation is on the in-demand lists of several provinces, allowing applications through PNPs (e.g., Ontario, BC) or Federal Skilled Trades, typically requiring a job offer or work experience.

**问：Can Chinese experience be directly used for Canadian certification?**
答：Chinese experience generally requires an ECA for degree equivalence, but trade certifications are often not directly recognized; it is advisable to obtain local credentials through apprenticeship or provincial assessment.
