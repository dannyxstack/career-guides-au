# Advertising, marketing and public relations managers（Advertising, marketing and public relations managers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：10022 – Advertising, marketing and public relations managers。**

Marketing managers in Canada develop strategies, manage teams, and drive business growth, making them key roles in the commercial and digital economy. This occupation qualifies for Express Entry and most Provincial Nominee Programs, offering high migration feasibility, especially for those with digital marketing and data analytics experience.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 通过联邦技术移民或加拿大经验类移民，满足CRS评分要求，适合有1年以上加拿大工作经验或海外高技能申请人。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 多数省份如安省、BC省、阿省等均有针对管理岗位的省提名项目，可额外加分或直接提名。 |
| Subclass Global Talent Stream（Global Talent Stream (雇主担保)） | 适用于科技公司数字营销岗位，雇主获批后两周内处理工签，快速登陆。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for marketing managers in Canada?**
答：Entry-level: CAD 55,000-75,000; mid-level: CAD 75,000-110,000; senior: CAD 110,000-160,000. Digital marketing or tech sectors may exceed CAD 200,000.

**问：What are the main immigration pathways for marketing managers to Canada?**
答：Primarily Express Entry (FSW/CEC) and Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP). If you have a Canadian employer, the Global Talent Stream can expedite a work permit.

**问：What is the job outlook for marketing managers in Canada?**
答：Positive outlook, with steady growth driven by digital transformation. Opportunities are abundant in Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal.
