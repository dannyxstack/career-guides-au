# Senior government managers and officials — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 00011 (NOC)**

Senior government managers and officials hold leadership roles in Canadian federal, provincial, or territorial governments, shaping policy, managing public resources, and overseeing government program implementation. These positions are typically filled through political appointments or senior civil service selection.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.
