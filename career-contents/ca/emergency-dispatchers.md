# Emergency dispatchers（Emergency dispatchers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：22222 – Emergency dispatchers。**

In Canada, emergency dispatchers receive emergency calls, dispatch resources, and record incident information for police, fire, and ambulance services. Classified under NOC 22222, this occupation can qualify for Provincial Nominee Programs or Express Entry, but often requires employer sponsorship or existing work permit.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 适合有雇主offer的申请人，各省紧缺列表可能包含紧急调度员。需通过省提名后申请永居。 |
| Subclass EE（Express Entry） | 若申请人有加拿大工作经验或学历，可通过CEC或FSW申请，但需CRS高分，且雇主offer可加50-200分。 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主需证明招不到本地人，获批后申请人可申请封闭式工签，之后转永居。 |
| Subclass AIP（Atlantic Immigration Program） | 大西洋省份的雇主担保项目，适合有工作offer的紧急调度员。 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of an emergency dispatcher in Canada?**
答：According to Job Bank, the median annual salary is around CAD 50,000, with entry-level earning CAD 35,000-45,000 and senior dispatchers up to CAD 80,000+ including shift premiums.

**问：What are the immigration pathways for emergency dispatchers to Canada?**
答：Main pathways include Provincial Nominee Program (with employer offer), Express Entry (if with Canadian experience or high CRS), and Atlantic Immigration Program. Employer sponsorship is crucial as this occupation is not a targeted Express Entry draw.

**问：Is local certification required for emergency dispatchers in Canada?**
答：Yes, most provinces require completion of a provincial certification program, such as the Emergency Dispatcher Certification in Ontario. Training covers first aid, communication technologies, etc.
