# Graphic designers and illustrators（Graphic designers and illustrators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：52120 – Graphic designers and illustrators。**

Graphic designers and illustrators communicate ideas through visual elements, working in advertising, publishing, and digital media.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Express Entry（Federal Skilled Worker） | 适合有1年连续工作经验、语言达标的设计师 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份有特定类别，需雇主担保或紧缺职业 |
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主可申请LMIA支持工签，后转永居 |
| Subclass CEC（Canadian Experience Class） | 适合在加拿大有1年工作经验的留学生或工签持有者 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for graphic designers in Canada?**
答：The median annual salary is around CAD 50,000, with entry-level at CAD 35,000-45,000 and senior roles up to CAD 90,000+.

**问：What immigration pathways are common for graphic designers to Canada?**
答：Mostly through Express Entry (FSW/CEC) or Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), requiring 1+ year work experience and language scores.

**问：Are there good career prospects for graphic designers in Canada?**
答：Digital transformation brings opportunities, but competition is high. Specializing in UX/UI or digital marketing design can improve competitiveness.
