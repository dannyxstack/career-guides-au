# Musicians and singers（Musicians and singers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：51121 – Musicians and singers。**

Instrumental musicians in Canada perform one or more instruments for live performances, recordings, composing, and teaching. Often self-employed, they can immigrate via Express Entry or PNP.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EE（Express Entry (FSW/CEC)） | 适合有加拿大工作经验或高学历的申请人，需综合排名系统分数。 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份如BC省、安省有文化类提名，需雇主offer或本省经验。 |
| Subclass SDS（Student Direct Stream） | 先留学，毕业后通过PGWP积累经验再移民。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for instrumental musicians in Canada?**
答：According to Canada's Job Bank, median annual wage is around CAD 50,000, but self-employed earnings vary widely, often between CAD 25,000 and CAD 70,000.

**问：Can musicians immigrate to Canada through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. NOC 51121 is TEER 2, eligible for Express Entry (e.g., FSW) or PNP. Requires ECA and language test.

**问：Which Canadian cities have high demand for musicians?**
答：Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver have the highest demand, followed by Calgary and Ottawa. These cities offer more venues, studios, and teaching opportunities.
