# Labourers in fish and seafood processing（Labourers in fish and seafood processing）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：95107 – Labourers in fish and seafood processing。**

Perform manual tasks such as cleaning, cutting, packaging, and freezing fish and seafood in processing plants, requiring no advanced education but ability to work in cold and wet conditions.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在加拿大独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：很低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass LMIA（Labour Market Impact Assessment） | 雇主需证明无法找到本地工人，可申请工签 |
| Subclass PNP（Provincial Nominee Program） | 部分省份（如新斯科舍、纽芬兰）有低技能类别，但需雇主担保 |

**PR难度：非常高（★★★★★）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level workers earn around CAD 28,000-35,000, while experienced ones may reach CAD 40,000-48,000, with seasonal and regional variations.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada as a fish processing labourer?**
答：It's difficult. This is a low-skill TEER 5 occupation, ineligible for Federal Skilled Worker programs; only a few provinces have low-skill PNP streams with LMIA support.
