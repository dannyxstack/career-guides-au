# Machining tool operators（Machining tool operators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：94106 – Machining tool operators。**

Operate metalworking machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and drills to produce precision parts.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a machining tool operator?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around CAD 35,000-45,000, mid-level CAD 45,000-60,000, and senior CAD 60,000-80,000, with overtime and bonuses.

**问：How can a machining tool operator immigrate to Canada?**
答：Through Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) or Provincial Nominee Program (PNP), with some provinces having priority for trades. LMIA employer-sponsored work permit is also common.

**问：What is the future career prospect for this occupation?**
答：With manufacturing upgrades and CNC technology adoption, operators with programming and automation skills have better advancement to senior technician or shop management.
