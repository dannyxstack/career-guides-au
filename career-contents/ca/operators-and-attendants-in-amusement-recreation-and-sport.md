# Operators and attendants in amusement, recreation and sport（Operators and attendants in amusement, recreation and sport）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：65211 – Operators and attendants in amusement, recreation and sport。**

Operate amusement rides, manage recreational activities, and provide customer service in settings like amusement parks, water parks, and sports venues.
