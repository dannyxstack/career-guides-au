# Underground mine service and support workers（Underground mine service and support workers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：83100 – Underground mine service and support workers。**

Mining machine operators in Canada work mainly in Ontario, Quebec, and Saskatchewan's metal and non-metal mines, operating drills, loaders, and other equipment. This occupation can qualify for Express Entry or PNP, but often requires employer sponsorship or provincial nomination.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mining machine operators in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from CAD 45,000 to 60,000, mid-level from 60,000 to 80,000, and senior positions can exceed CAD 100,000. Overtime and remote allowances are common.

**问：How can a mining machine operator immigrate to Canada?**
答：Mainly through Provincial Nominee Programs (e.g., Ontario, Saskatchewan) with a job offer, or via Federal Skilled Worker with high CRS or Canadian experience. Some provinces list it as a priority occupation.

**问：What education is required to become a mining machine operator?**
答：Typically a high school diploma plus provincial safety training (e.g., Common Core). Some colleges offer one-year certificate programs.
