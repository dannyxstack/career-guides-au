# Commissioned officers of the Canadian Armed Forces — career analysis · Canada

**Occupation code: 40042 (NOC)**

A commissioned officer in the Canadian Armed Forces is a military leadership role responsible for commanding troops, developing strategy, and managing defense operations. They are trained through military colleges or direct entry programs and serve in the army, navy, or air force.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary growth potential for Canadian officers?**
A: Salary steadily increases with rank and years of service, including allowances and benefits, with excellent retirement pension.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Canada through commissioned officer positions?**
A: Generally cannot; officer positions are only open to Canadian citizens. Exchange officers require special permission but are not an immigration pathway

**Q: What career transition opportunities are available after retiring as a military officer?**
A: Can transition into government management, security consulting, or private sector leadership roles, favored by employers.
