# Water and waste treatment plant operators（Water and waste treatment plant operators）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：92105 – Water and waste treatment plant operators。**

Water treatment operators monitor and maintain drinking water and wastewater treatment systems in Canada, a skilled occupation eligible for PNP or Express Entry immigration with stable employment.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for water treatment operators in Canada?**
答：Entry-level CAD 40,000-55,000, mid-level CAD 55,000-75,000, senior CAD 75,000-95,000, varying by province and certification.

**问：How can I immigrate to Canada as an Australian water treatment operator?**
答：Apply via Express Entry (FSW) or PNP. Australian experience is recognized; need ECA and CLB 7+ language test.

**问：Do I need to get recertified in Canada?**
答：Yes, each province requires local certification. You can challenge exams or apply for recognition of prior learning. Contact the relevant provincial authority.
