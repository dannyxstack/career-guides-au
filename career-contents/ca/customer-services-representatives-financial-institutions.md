# Customer services representatives - financial institutions（Customer services representatives - financial institutions）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：64400 – Customer services representatives - financial institutions。**

Provide account inquiries, transaction processing, product information, and issue resolution to customers in banks, credit unions, and other financial institutions via phone, chat, or in person.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for customer service representatives in financial institutions in Canada?**
答：Entry-level salary is around CAD 35,000-42,000 per year, senior representatives can earn CAD 52,000-65,000, and management roles are higher.

**问：Is this occupation suitable for skilled migration to Canada?**
答：It is classified as NOC TEER 4, which typically does not meet FSW/CEC 0/A/B requirements, but may be eligible through PNP or LMIA pathways. Check specific provincial requirements.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities after 5 years?**
答：Possible advancement to customer service supervisor, financial advisor, loan officer, or back-office operations roles.
