# Public works and maintenance labourers（Public works and maintenance labourers）职业分析 · 加拿大

**职业代码：75212 – Public works and maintenance labourers。**

Perform manual tasks to maintain and repair public infrastructure such as roads, sewers, and water systems, including cleaning, digging, and material handling.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for public works and maintenance labourers?**
答：Entry-level workers earn about CAD 32,000-40,000 per year, while senior workers or supervisors can earn CAD 52,000-65,000. Salaries vary by province and employer.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Canada as a public works labourer?**
答：This semi-skilled occupation typically leads to immigration via provincial nomination or employer sponsorship. Some provinces list it as in-demand, but Express Entry requires higher CRS scores.

**问：Is there career progression for this occupation?**
答：Yes, you can advance to crew leader or supervisor, or transition to a skilled trade via apprenticeship, such as plumber or road worker.
