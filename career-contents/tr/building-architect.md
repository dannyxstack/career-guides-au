## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for a building architect in Turkey?**
A: Entry-level architects earn around 8,000–12,000 TRY per month, while experienced architects can make 20,000–35,000 TRY or more. Salaries vary by city (Istanbul and Ankara higher) and project type. Senior roles in large firms or specialized sectors like sustainable design may exceed 40,000 TRY.

**Q: What is the job outlook for building architects in Turkey?**
A: Demand remains steady due to ongoing urban renewal, infrastructure projects, and earthquake-resistant building retrofits. However, competition is strong in major cities. Architects with expertise in sustainable design, BIM, and project management have better prospects. The profession is expected to grow modestly over the next decade.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting the work of building architects in Turkey?**
A: AI tools streamline design iteration, energy modeling, and code compliance checks. They automate routine drafting and clash detection in BIM, freeing architects for creative and strategic tasks. However, human judgment, local building culture, and site-specific problem-solving remain essential. AI is augmenting rather than replacing architects.
