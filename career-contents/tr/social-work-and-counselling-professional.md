## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, entry-level social workers in the public sector earn around 5,000–7,000 TRY monthly, while experienced professionals can earn 10,000–15,000 TRY. Private sector salaries vary widely, with those in NGOs or private clinics potentially earning higher. Location and specialization also affect pay.

**Q: What skills are most important for social workers and counsellors in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include empathy, active listening, crisis intervention, and cultural competence. Knowledge of Turkish social welfare laws and procedures is essential. Proficiency in English is advantageous for roles with international organizations. Continuous learning and self-care are also critical due to the emotionally demanding nature of the work.

**Q: How is AI affecting social work and counselling in Turkey?**
A: AI is primarily used for administrative tasks like client data management and scheduling. Some agencies use AI-powered tools for preliminary mental health screenings. However, direct client interaction remains human-led. Professionals must become comfortable with digital platforms, but AI is unlikely to replace the empathy and judgment required in this field.
