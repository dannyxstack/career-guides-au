- Automatically generate weaving/woodworking design drawings based on customer size parameters
- Use a CNC engraving machine to cut and engrave complex patterns
- Repetitive standardized processes such as basket weaving and wood strip splicing
- Clerical work such as inventory management, order processing, and customer communication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI generative design to explore more forms and structural possibilities
- Customer preview using 3D scanning and digital twins
- Optimise product style and pricing through social media data analysis

**Human moat:**

- The tactile feel, detail, and irreproducibility of manual craftsmanship

- Cultural heritage and innovative interpretation of traditional crafts

- Emotional value of brand story and craftsmanship spirit

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for handicraft workers in wood and basketry in Turkey?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region, but entry-level workers earn around 10,000–15,000 TRY per month, while skilled artisans can earn 20,000–30,000 TRY. Those with a strong portfolio or working in tourist areas may charge higher rates for custom pieces. Income often depends on order volume and seasonal demand.

**Q: What skills are essential to become a successful handicraft worker in wood and basketry in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in traditional techniques like carving, weaving, and joinery, as well as knowledge of wood types and natural fibers. Attention to detail, creativity, and manual dexterity are crucial. Many artisans learn through apprenticeships or vocational courses. Business acumen and digital marketing skills are increasingly important for selling products.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting the career outlook for handicraft workers in Turkey?**
A: AI and automation have limited direct impact on handmade crafts, as customers value uniqueness and artisan skill. However, AI-powered tools can assist in design and marketing. The demand for authentic, sustainable products supports steady work for skilled artisans. The outlook is stable, with growth potential through online sales and tourism-related markets.
