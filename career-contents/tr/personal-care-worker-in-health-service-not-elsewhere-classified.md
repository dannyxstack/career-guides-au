## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly salary for a personal care worker in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, entry-level personal care workers earn around 8,000–10,000 TL monthly, while experienced workers can make 12,000–15,000 TL. Salaries vary by region, employer (public vs. private), and patient needs. Some positions include housing or meal benefits.

**Q: What does a typical day involve for a personal care worker in Turkey?**
A: Daily tasks include assisting patients with bathing, dressing, and eating, monitoring vital signs, administering prescribed medications, and providing mobility support. Workers also document patient conditions, communicate with families, and coordinate with nurses or doctors in facilities or home care settings.

**Q: What skills and qualifications are needed to become a personal care worker in Turkey?**
A: Required qualifications include a high school diploma and a certified caregiver training program (usually 2–6 months). Key skills are empathy, patience, basic first aid, and communication. Experience with elderly or disabled care and knowledge of chronic conditions like diabetes or dementia are highly valued.
