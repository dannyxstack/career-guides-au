## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a croupier in Turkey?**
A: Entry-level croupiers in Turkey earn around 8,000–12,000 TRY per month, while experienced workers in major casinos can earn 15,000–25,000 TRY or more, often supplemented by tips. Salaries vary by location (e.g., Istanbul, Antalya) and employer type (land-based vs. online).

**Q: What skills are required to become a croupier in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include proficiency in games like roulette, blackjack, and poker; manual dexterity for handling chips and cards; strong mental math and attention to detail; fluency in Turkish and basic English; and excellent interpersonal skills to manage players. Licensing through Turkey’s relevant authorities is mandatory.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of croupiers in Turkish casinos?**
A: AI is automating some table-game monitoring and security, but live dealers remain essential for player interaction and game flow. Turkish casinos use AI for surveillance and fraud detection, not to replace croupiers. However, demand may shift toward tech-savvy staff who can work alongside digital systems.
