## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, elementary workers typically earn between 11,402 TL (minimum wage) and 15,000 TL per month, depending on the sector and employer. Wages in manufacturing or logistics may be slightly higher, while service roles often pay closer to minimum wage. Overtime and bonuses can increase earnings.

**Q: What skills are most important for elementary workers in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include basic literacy and numeracy, physical stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to follow instructions. Many roles require manual dexterity or familiarity with simple tools. Communication skills are beneficial for customer-facing jobs, and reliability is highly valued by employers.

**Q: How will automation and AI affect elementary workers in Turkey?**
A: Automation may reduce demand for routine manual tasks in sectors like manufacturing and warehousing. However, roles requiring flexibility, human interaction, or non-routine physical work (e.g., cleaning, delivery) are less likely to be fully automated. Upskilling in basic digital literacy may help workers adapt.
