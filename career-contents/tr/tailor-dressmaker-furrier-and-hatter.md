## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a tailor in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly salary for a tailor in Turkey ranges from TRY 7,000 to TRY 12,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Self-employed tailors can earn more, especially in high-demand areas like Istanbul, but income varies with clientele and season. Entry-level positions may start around TRY 5,000.

**Q: What skills are essential for tailors in Turkey?**
A: Essential skills include precise hand and machine sewing, pattern making, fabric knowledge, and alterations. Attention to detail and creativity are vital for custom designs. Proficiency with traditional techniques like draping and embroidery is valued, especially for cultural attire. Basic business skills help self-employed tailors manage clients and finances.

**Q: How is AI affecting the tailoring profession in Turkey?**
A: AI is primarily used for design software and automated pattern cutting, improving efficiency but not replacing skilled handwork. Custom tailoring remains artisanal, but AI assists with inventory management and client fitting through 3D scanning. Most Turkish tailors view AI as a tool rather than a threat, as bespoke craftsmanship remains in demand.
