- Instrument sterilization and inventory management (IoT + robotics)
- Patient record entry (speech-to-text + EMR system)

- AI-Assisted Dental Diagnosis (Identifying Cavities, Periodontal Disease)
- 3D printing dental crown design (AI-generated models)
- Patient education material generation (AI-customized content)
- Treatment plan simulation (AI risk prediction)

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dental assistants and therapists in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, entry-level dental assistants and therapists earn around 5,000–7,000 TRY per month, while experienced professionals can earn 10,000–15,000 TRY. Salaries vary by location, with Istanbul and Ankara offering higher pay, and by private vs. public sector. Private clinics often provide additional benefits.

**Q: What key skills are required for dental assistants and therapists in Turkey?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in dental procedures (e.g., sterilization, chairside assisting), good communication with patients and dentists, basic administrative abilities, and knowledge of Turkish dental regulations. Increasingly, digital dentistry skills (e.g., CAD/CAM software) and infection control expertise are valued.

**Q: How is the career outlook for dental assistants and therapists in Turkey?**
A: The demand for dental assistants and therapists in Turkey is stable, driven by growing dental tourism and an aging population. Job opportunities are mostly in private clinics and hospitals. While AI may automate some administrative tasks, hands-on patient care and technical skills remain in demand, ensuring steady employment.
