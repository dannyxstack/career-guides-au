## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a stonemason in Turkey?**
A: Entry-level stonemasons earn around 5,000–7,000 TRY per month, while experienced craftsmen can make 10,000–15,000 TRY monthly. Rates vary by region, with Istanbul and tourist areas offering higher pay. Self-employed stonemasons may charge 200–500 TRY per day depending on project complexity.

**Q: What key skills are required to work as a stonemason in Turkey?**
A: Essential skills include proficiency in using hand tools (chisels, hammers) and power tools (angle grinders, pneumatic hammers). Knowledge of different stone types (marble, granite, limestone) and techniques like carving, cutting, and polishing is crucial. Physical stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to read blueprints are also important.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting stonemasonry in Turkey?**
A: AI-driven CNC machines and 3D scanners are increasingly used for precise cutting and carving, especially in large-scale production. However, intricate restoration and custom artistic work still rely on human skill. Stonemasons who adapt to operating digital tools will remain in demand, as AI complements rather than fully replaces craftsmanship.
