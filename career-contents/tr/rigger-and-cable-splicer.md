## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for riggers and cable splicers in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, entry-level riggers and cable splicers earn around 8,000–12,000 TRY per month. Experienced professionals with certifications can earn 15,000–25,000 TRY. Pay varies by region, with higher wages in industrial zones and major cities like Istanbul and Ankara. Overtime and project-based bonuses are common in construction and telecom sectors.

**Q: What does a typical day look like for a rigger or cable splicer in Turkey?**
A: Daily tasks include inspecting and setting up rigging equipment for lifting heavy loads, or splicing and terminating fiber optic and copper cables. Work often involves heights, confined spaces, and outdoor environments. Riggers coordinate with crane operators, while cable splicers test connections and ensure compliance with Turkey's technical standards. Safety protocols are strictly followed.

**Q: How is AI impacting rigging and cable splicing jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI is gradually influencing the field through automated inspection drones and AI-powered diagnostic tools for cable networks. However, the hands-on nature of rigging and splicing means AI is unlikely to replace workers soon. Instead, AI assists with safety monitoring and predictive maintenance, requiring workers to adapt by learning basic digital skills to operate new tools.
