**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording work logs, material lists, and progress reports
- Performs quality checks through visual inspection or simple tools

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using BIM models and AR glasses for visual construction guidance

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a civil engineering labourer in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, civil engineering labourers typically earn between 8,000 and 15,000 Turkish Lira per month depending on experience, location, and project complexity. Overtime and hazard pay may increase earnings. Entry-level workers start around minimum wage, while experienced labourers earn more on large infrastructure projects.

**Q: What skills are required for a civil engineering labourer in Turkey?**
A: Essential skills include physical stamina, ability to operate construction tools like jackhammers and compactors, basic knowledge of construction safety, teamwork, and following instructions. Literacy and basic math are helpful. Certification such as Occupational Health and Safety training is increasingly required by employers.

**Q: What is the career outlook for civil engineering labourers in Turkey?**
A: The demand remains steady due to ongoing infrastructure projects like roads, bridges, and housing. However, automation and machinery are reducing manual labour needs. Skilled workers with safety training and equipment operation skills have better prospects. Advancement to foreman roles is possible with experience.
