**Where AI augments the role:**

- Real-time captioning assisting interpretation (AI providing background information)
- Term base management (AI automatically extracts terms)

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for translators and interpreters in Turkey?**
A: Entry-level translators in Turkey earn around 5,000-8,000 TRY per month, while experienced professionals can earn 12,000-20,000 TRY. Specialized fields like medical or legal translation pay higher. Freelance rates vary from 0.10 to 0.30 TRY per word. Salaries depend on language pair, certification, and experience.

**Q: How is AI affecting the translation profession in Turkey?**
A: AI tools like ChatGPT and Google Translate are increasingly used for initial drafts, but human linguists remain essential for nuanced, context-sensitive work. Many Turkish translators now use AI for productivity, focusing on post-editing and specialized domains like legal or literary translation where accuracy is critical.

**Q: What key skills are needed to succeed as a linguist in Turkey?**
A: Native-level proficiency in Turkish and at least one foreign language is essential. Specialization in a field (e.g., law, medicine, tech) boosts demand. Strong cultural knowledge, attention to detail, and familiarity with CAT tools like SDL Trados are important. Soft skills like time management and client communication are also valued.
