## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for scribes in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, scribes typically earn between 5,000 and 8,000 Turkish Lira monthly, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 4,000 TL, while experienced scribes in specialized fields like legal or medical transcription can earn up to 10,000 TL. Salaries may vary by sector and city.

**Q: What skills are essential for scribes in Turkey?**
A: Essential skills include fast and accurate typing (at least 60 words per minute), proficiency in Turkish grammar and spelling, and attention to detail. Familiarity with transcription software, audio equipment, and confidentiality protocols is important. Knowledge of legal or medical terminology can be advantageous in specialized roles.

**Q: How is AI impacting scribe jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI speech recognition tools are automating simple transcription tasks, reducing demand for basic scribes. However, human oversight remains crucial for complex or sensitive content, such as legal proceedings and medical records. Scribes who adapt by learning AI-assisted workflows and specializing in error-proofing or editing will remain valuable.
