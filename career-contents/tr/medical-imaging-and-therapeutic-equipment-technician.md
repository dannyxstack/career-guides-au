## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for medical imaging technicians in Turkey?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around ₺18,000–₺25,000 per month, while experienced professionals in private hospitals can earn ₺30,000–₺45,000. Public sector salaries are lower but include benefits and job security. Overtime and night shifts increase earnings. Salaries vary by city, with Istanbul and Ankara offering higher pay.

**Q: What key skills are required for medical imaging technicians in Turkey?**
A: Technicians need proficiency in operating MRI, CT, X-ray, and ultrasound devices. Strong knowledge of anatomy, patient positioning, and radiation safety is essential. Digital literacy for image processing and PACS systems is required. Soft skills like communication and empathy are valued, as is the ability to work under pressure in emergency settings.

**Q: How is AI impacting medical imaging technician jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI assists in image analysis and pattern recognition, reducing diagnostic time, but technicians remain essential for equipment operation, patient interaction, and quality control. AI may automate routine tasks like measurement, freeing technicians to focus on complex cases. Continued training in AI tools is recommended to stay competitive.
