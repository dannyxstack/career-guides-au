## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, entry-level fumigators earn around 8,000–10,000 TL per month, while experienced workers can make 15,000–20,000 TL. Salaries vary by region and employer, with higher pay in industrial settings. Overtime and hazardous duty pay may apply.

**Q: What qualifications or training are required to become a pest controller in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, pest controllers typically need a high school diploma and a certificate from the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry after completing a training course. Practical experience is valued. Knowledge of pest biology, safe chemical handling, and equipment operation is essential. Ongoing learning is required due to regulatory updates.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence impacting pest control jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI is gradually being used for pest monitoring via smart traps and drones, but hands-on fumigation and weed control still require human judgment. In Turkey, AI assists in data analysis but does not replace field workers. Demand for skilled fumigators remains stable, though efficiency gains may reduce some routine tasks.
