- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time

**Human moat:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for concrete finishers in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly salary for concrete placers and finishers in Turkey ranges from 8,000 to 14,000 Turkish Lira, depending on experience and location. Senior workers on large construction sites in major cities like Istanbul or Ankara may earn up to 18,000 TRY. Pay is typically hourly or per project.

**Q: What key skills are required for concrete placers in Turkey?**
A: Concrete placers need physical stamina, attention to detail, and knowledge of mixing ratios and curing times. Familiarity with tools like trowels, screeds, and power floats is essential. In Turkey, understanding safety regulations and ability to work outdoors in varying weather are also important. Basic math for measuring materials is required.

**Q: What is the career outlook for concrete placers in Turkey?**
A: Demand for concrete placers remains steady in Turkey due to ongoing construction and infrastructure projects. However, automation (e.g., robotic screeds) is gradually reducing need for manual finishing. Skilled workers who can operate modern equipment and adapt to new techniques will have better prospects. The outlook is stable but slow-growing.
