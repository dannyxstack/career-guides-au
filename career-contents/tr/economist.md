## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for economists in Turkey?**
A: Average monthly salary for mid-level economists in Turkey ranges from 15,000 to 30,000 TRY, varying by sector (public vs private) and location (highest in Istanbul). Senior economists can earn over 50,000 TRY, while entry-level positions start around 10,000 TRY.

**Q: What skills are most important for economists in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include strong analytical and quantitative abilities, proficiency in econometric software like Stata or R, and knowledge of Turkish economic policies. Fluency in English is beneficial. Employers also value data interpretation, report writing, and familiarity with global economic trends affecting Turkey.

**Q: How is AI affecting the economics profession in Turkey?**
A: AI is automating routine data collection and basic analysis, enabling economists to focus on strategic tasks. Demand is growing for economists skilled in machine learning and working with large datasets. Understanding AI tools is becoming essential, but human judgment remains critical for policy advice and complex modeling.
