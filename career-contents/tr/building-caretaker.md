## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building caretaker in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly salary for a building caretaker (kapıcı) in Turkey ranges between 8,000 and 12,000 Turkish Lira, depending on location, building size, and responsibilities. Salaries often include accommodation and utilities. In major cities like Istanbul and Ankara, pay tends to be higher, but cost of living is also greater.

**Q: What skills are essential for building caretakers in Turkey?**
A: Essential skills include basic plumbing, electrical repairs, cleaning, and waste management. Good communication with residents, knowledge of building systems (heating, ventilation), and ability to handle minor maintenance tasks are crucial. Physical fitness, reliability, and a working knowledge of Turkish are also important due to daily interaction with tenants.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of building caretakers in Turkey?**
A: AI is gradually automating tasks like monitoring security cameras, managing energy use, and scheduling maintenance. However, hands-on repairs, cleaning, and resident interaction remain human-dependent. AI tools can assist caretakers by providing efficient work orders or predicting equipment failures, but the role still requires physical presence and problem-solving skills.
