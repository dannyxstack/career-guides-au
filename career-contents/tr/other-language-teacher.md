## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for other language teachers in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, monthly net salaries for language teachers vary by institution and experience. Private language schools pay 8,000–15,000 TRY, while university lecturers earn 15,000–25,000 TRY. Istanbul and Ankara offer higher ranges. Freelance tutors may charge 300–600 TRY per hour.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a language teacher in Turkey?**
A: A bachelor's degree in the target language, education, or a related field is typically required. TEFL/TESOL certificates are preferred for English. For other languages, teaching experience and native-level proficiency matter. Strong communication, cultural awareness, and adaptability are essential.

**Q: How is AI impacting the language teaching profession in Turkey?**
A: AI tools like translation apps and chatbots reduce demand for basic grammar instruction. However, they cannot replace live interaction. Teachers now focus on conversation, cultural nuances, and personalized feedback. Those who integrate AI into lessons create more engaging, efficient classes.
