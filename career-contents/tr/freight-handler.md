## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a freight handler in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly salary for freight handlers in Turkey ranges between 10,000 and 14,000 Turkish Lira as of 2024, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions often start near the minimum wage, while experienced workers in major logistics hubs can earn higher. Overtime and night shift bonuses are common.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a freight handler in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include physical stamina for heavy lifting, attention to detail for accurate sorting and loading, and basic literacy for reading labels and paperwork. Familiarity with safety protocols and operation of manual pallet jacks is essential. Good teamwork and communication skills are valued due to fast-paced warehouse environments.

**Q: How is AI affecting freight handling jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI and automation are gradually transforming freight handling in Turkey, particularly in large warehouses with automated sorting systems and conveyor belts. However, many tasks still require human judgment and dexterity. While some routine jobs may decline, new roles in system oversight and maintenance are emerging, so workers adaptable to technology will remain in demand.
