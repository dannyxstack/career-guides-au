## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an applications programmer in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly salary for an applications programmer in Turkey ranges from 15,000 to 30,000 Turkish Lira, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior developers in Istanbul or Ankara can earn over 40,000 TL. Entry-level positions start around 10,000 TL. Bonuses and benefits are common in tech companies.

**Q: What programming languages and skills are most in demand for applications programmers in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, employers seek proficiency in Java, C#, Python, and JavaScript. Knowledge of frameworks like Spring, .NET, React, and Angular is highly valued. Database skills (SQL, PostgreSQL) and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) are increasingly important. Soft skills like problem-solving and teamwork are also essential.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of applications programmers in Turkey?**
A: AI is automating routine coding tasks, but it creates demand for programmers who can integrate AI services, work with machine learning models, and maintain AI-driven applications. Turkish developers now need to understand AI tools and APIs. The role is shifting toward higher-level design and problem-solving, with less emphasis on manual code writing.
