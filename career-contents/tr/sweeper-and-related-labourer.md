## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers in Turkey?**
A: As of 2025, sweepers in Turkey generally earn around 8,500–10,000 TRY per month, often at or slightly above the minimum wage. Pay may vary by employer, city, and additional responsibilities. In larger cities like Istanbul, wages tend to be higher. Overtime and holiday premiums can increase total earnings.

**Q: What skills are required to work as a sweeper in Turkey?**
A: The job primarily requires physical stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to work outdoors in all weather conditions. Basic familiarity with cleaning tools like brooms, dustpans, and trash bins is sufficient. No formal education is needed, but punctuality, reliability, and a strong work ethic are highly valued by employers.

**Q: Will AI or automation affect sweeping jobs in Turkey?**
A: Automation is unlikely to significantly replace sweepers in Turkey in the near future. While robotic cleaners may assist in indoor settings, public streets and parks still require human judgment for debris and uneven surfaces. Demand for manual sweepers remains stable, especially in municipalities and private cleaning firms.
