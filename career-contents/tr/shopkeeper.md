## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical monthly income for a shopkeeper in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly income for a shopkeeper in Turkey ranges from 8,500 TL to 15,000 TL, depending on location and store type. In large cities like Istanbul, earnings can be higher. Many shopkeepers are self-employed, so net income varies after expenses like rent and inventory.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting the role of shopkeepers in Turkey?**
A: Artificial intelligence is gradually impacting shopkeepers through automated inventory systems, self-checkout kiosks, and online platforms. However, small shops in Turkey still rely heavily on personal service. AI adoption is lower among independent stores, so the immediate threat to jobs is minimal, but tech-savvy shopkeepers may gain efficiencies.

**Q: What key skills are essential for a shopkeeper in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include customer service, product knowledge, basic accounting, and inventory management. Turkish shopkeepers must also be adept at negotiating with suppliers and handling cash. Language skills are an asset in tourist areas. Adaptability and digital literacy are increasingly important as e-commerce grows.
