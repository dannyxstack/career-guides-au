**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automatic floor scrubbers or robot vacuums for floor cleaning
- Automatic vacuuming and mopping along preset routes
- Timely automatic spraying of disinfectant for environmental disinfection.
- Use AI trash bins to automatically sort and compact waste
- Monitor cleanliness via sensors and generate cleaning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling systems to optimize cleaning routes and shifts.
- Using computer vision to detect stains and guide targeted cleaning
- Use voice assistants or apps to log cleaning tasks and feedback
- Use data analysis to predict peak hours and prepare in advance
- Operate semi-automatic cleaning equipment for high-altitude or corner cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Experience in cleaning complex stains and special material surfaces.
- Communicating with clients to confirm cleaning requirements and flexibly adjusting
- Expertise in non-toxic, environmentally friendly cleaning agents
- Identify safety hazards on site and take emergency measures on the spot

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for other cleaning workers in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, other cleaning workers typically earn between the minimum wage and slightly above, averaging around 5,500–6,500 TRY per month in 2023. Salaries vary by region and employer, with higher pay in major cities like Istanbul. Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: What are typical daily tasks for other cleaning workers in Turkey?**
A: Daily tasks include sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, and trash removal in offices, hospitals, or public spaces. They may also clean windows, sanitize surfaces, and restock supplies. Work is physical and repetitive, requiring attention to detail and adherence to hygiene standards.

**Q: How is AI affecting other cleaning jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI impact is currently low for manual cleaning roles. Automated cleaning robots are used in some large facilities, but they complement rather than replace human workers. Demand for human cleaners remains steady due to the need for nuanced tasks and flexibility. Workers may need to adapt to using new cleaning technologies.
