## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Sales Worker (not elsewhere classified) in Turkey?**
A: Entry-level sales workers in Turkey earn around 5,000–7,000 TRY per month, while experienced workers can make 10,000–15,000 TRY. Commission-based roles may yield higher earnings, varying by sector. Salaries are often supplemented with performance bonuses, especially in retail and B2B sales.

**Q: How is artificial intelligence affecting Sales Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Turkey?**
A: AI tools are automating routine tasks like data entry and lead generation, but human interaction remains crucial in Turkish sales. Workers now use CRM and analytics to personalize pitches. AI is unlikely to fully replace these roles, as local relationship-building and negotiation skills are highly valued.

**Q: What skills are essential for a Sales Worker in Turkey today?**
A: Strong communication, persuasion, and product knowledge are key. Fluency in Turkish is mandatory; English helps in export sectors. Familiarity with digital sales tools (e.g., CRM software) and social media selling is increasingly important. Adaptability and emotional intelligence are also valued for handling diverse client needs.
