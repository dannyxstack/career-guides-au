## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a systems administrator in Turkey?**
A: The average monthly salary for a systems administrator in Turkey ranges from 15,000 to 25,000 TRY, depending on experience, location, and sector. Entry-level positions may start around 10,000 TRY, while senior roles in Istanbul or at multinational companies can exceed 30,000 TRY. Certifications like CCNA or Linux can boost earnings.

**Q: What are the typical daily tasks of a systems administrator in Turkey?**
A: Systems administrators manage and maintain servers, networks, and IT infrastructure. Daily tasks include monitoring system performance, applying security patches, managing user accounts, troubleshooting hardware/software issues, performing backups, and ensuring system uptime. They also support end-users and coordinate with IT teams on upgrades or migrations.

**Q: How is AI impacting systems administration roles in Turkey?**
A: AI is automating routine tasks like log analysis, monitoring, and ticket categorization, allowing administrators to focus on complex issues and strategic projects. However, human expertise remains critical for security, incident response, and system architecture. Skills in automation tools (Ansible, Terraform) and AI-based monitoring are becoming increasingly valuable.
