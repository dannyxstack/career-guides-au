## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly wage for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Turkey?**
A: Monthly wages for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Turkey typically range from 8,000 to 12,000 Turkish Lira, depending on region, experience, and employer. Seasonal workers may earn slightly less, while those with specialized skills (e.g., tractor operation) can command higher pay. Overtime and in-kind benefits like accommodation may also apply.

**Q: What skills are essential for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include knowledge of planting, harvesting, and crop care, as well as animal feeding, milking, and basic health monitoring. Physical stamina, ability to operate farm machinery (e.g., tractors), and basic understanding of irrigation and pest control are important. Literacy for record-keeping and teamwork are also valued.

**Q: How is AI and automation affecting mixed crop and livestock farm labour jobs in Turkey?**
A: AI and automation are gradually impacting these roles, with precision farming tools (e.g., GPS-guided tractors, automated milking systems) reducing demand for manual labour. However, adoption remains low on small Turkish farms. Labourers with tech skills (e.g., operating drones for crop monitoring) may find more opportunities, but routine tasks face long-term decline.
