## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Buyer in Turkey?**
A: In Turkey, entry-level Buyers earn around 8,000–12,000 TRY per month, while mid-level professionals make 15,000–20,000 TRY. Senior Buyers can earn 25,000–35,000 TRY or more, depending on industry and experience. Bonuses and benefits are common in larger companies.

**Q: What are the key skills and daily responsibilities of a Buyer in Turkey?**
A: Buyers in Turkey source products, negotiate contracts, manage supplier relationships, and monitor market trends. They coordinate with logistics and inventory teams. Essential skills include negotiation, analytical thinking, and knowledge of Turkish trade regulations. Proficiency in English is often required.

**Q: How is AI impacting the role of Buyers in Turkey?**
A: AI is automating tasks like supplier screening, spend analysis, and demand forecasting in Turkish procurement. However, human judgment remains vital for complex negotiations and relationship management. Buyers who embrace AI tools will enhance efficiency and stay competitive in the evolving job market.
