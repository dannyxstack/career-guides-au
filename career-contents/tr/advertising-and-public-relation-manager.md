**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate standardized press releases and social media posts
- Analyze advertising performance data and generate reports
- Monitoring media mentions and public sentiment, automatically compiling briefings
- Automate bidding and budget allocation based on historical data
- Create and test basic A/B versions of ad copy

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to predict consumer behavior and optimize marketing strategies
- Use AI tools for more precise audience segmentation and personalized targeting
- Analyze public opinion in real time through AI to quickly respond to crisis PR
- Using AI to assist creative ideation, quickly generating multiple versions of creative plans
- Using AI to automatically track competitor activities to support strategic decisions

**Human moat:**

- Senior-level client relationship and stakeholder management
- Long-term brand strategy formulation and creative direction control
- Cross-departmental coordination and team leadership
- Handle complex and sensitive public relations crises
- Innovative planning based on intuition and experience

**Skills to build:**

## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary range for Advertising and Public Relations Managers in Turkey?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level managers earn around 15,000-20,000 TRY monthly, while mid-career professionals make 25,000-40,000 TRY. In Istanbul, senior managers at major agencies can earn 50,000-80,000 TRY or more. Bonuses and commissions are common in performance-driven roles.

**Q: What key skills are essential for Advertising and Public Relations Managers in Turkey?**
A: Strong communication and crisis management are vital. Proficiency in Turkish and English, digital marketing tools (e.g., Google Ads, social media platforms), and data analytics skills are increasingly important. Creativity, strategic thinking, and the ability to build relationships with media and influencers are also crucial in Turkey's competitive market.

**Q: How is the career outlook for Advertising and Public Relations Managers in Turkey?**
A: The field is growing moderately, driven by digital transformation and brand competition. AI tools are automating tasks like reporting and ad optimization, so managers must focus on strategy and creative direction. Job stability is good in major cities, but adaptability to new technologies and media trends is key for long-term success.
