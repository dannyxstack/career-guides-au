## 7. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly income for taxi drivers in Istanbul?**
A: Taxi drivers in Istanbul typically earn between 15,000 and 25,000 Turkish Lira per month after expenses, depending on the number of shifts and tips. However, income can vary widely based on location, time of day, and whether the driver owns the vehicle or rents it.

**Q: What skills are essential for becoming a taxi or van driver in Turkey?**
A: Key skills include a valid driver's license (Class B for cars/light vans), a professional driving certificate (SRC), knowledge of city routes and traffic patterns, good communication in Turkish, basic English for tourists, customer service, and patience. Familiarity with navigation apps and digital payment systems is increasingly important.

**Q: How is AI or automation expected to affect driving jobs in Turkey?**
A: Currently, AI impact is low as autonomous vehicles are not yet operational in Turkey. Ride-hailing apps use AI to match drivers with passengers, but full automation is years away. Drivers can future-proof by learning about electric vehicles (EVs) and hybrid systems, as EV adoption grows in cities like Istanbul.
