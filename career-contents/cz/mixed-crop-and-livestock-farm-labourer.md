# Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 9213 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Czechia perform manual tasks such as planting, harvesting, feeding animals, and maintaining farm equipment, often on family-run or corporate farms. This occupation is characterized by seasonal demand, with peaks during planting and harvest periods. The role requires physical stamina and adaptability to outdoor work in various weather conditions. Many workers are from EU/EEA countries, and non-EU workers face restrictions due to low skill level requirements for work permits.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression within this occupation is limited; experienced labourers may advance to team leader or specialist roles (e.g., machinery operator, animal health assistant) with additional training. Some workers transition to independent farming or agribusiness. Upskilling in precision agriculture or organic certification can improve prospects, but formal advancement often requires moving into supervisory or technical positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using automated tractors for plowing, sowing, and harvesting
- Monitor crop growth and pests/diseases using sensors and AI systems
- Caring for livestock using automatic feeders and milking robots
- Use drones to inspect farms and produce yield maps
- Optimize irrigation and fertilization plans based on historical data and weather forecasts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operating and maintaining smart agricultural equipment
- Using data analysis to improve crop yields and herd health
- Develop comprehensive agricultural strategies to balance ecology and economy
- Collaborate with AI for precision agriculture management
- Train new employees in using digital tools

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of sudden mechanical faults
- Unstructured work in complex terrain or extreme weather conditions.
- Animal emergency medical care and behavioural intervention
- Compliance with organic certification and animal welfare standards.

**Skills to build:**

- Agricultural drone operation and data analysis certification
- Use of precision agriculture software (e.g., FarmOS, Climate FieldView)
- Basic electrical and mechanical maintenance skills
- Digital literacy: Excel/data recording apps
- Basics of crop and livestock health management
- Training for safe operation of AGV/robot equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- organic farming
- sustainable agriculture
- agrotech
- seasonal labor

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by region, farm type, and season. For specific figures, consult the Czech Statistical Office or ISPV wage surveys. Generally, earnings are at or slightly above the minimum wage, with potential overtime during harvest.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a farm labourer in Czechia?**
A: Non-EU nationals face restrictions because this occupation is low-skilled. The Employee Card may be possible if the salary meets the threshold and the employer proves a labour shortage, but it is rarely approved. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: Do I need a specific certificate to work as a farm labourer?**
A: No mandatory certificate is required, but a machinery operator license may be needed for certain tasks. Optional agricultural training can improve job prospects.
