# Cabinet-Maker and Related Worker — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7522 (ISCO08)**

Cabinet-makers and related workers in Czechia design, construct, repair, and install wooden furniture and fittings, such as cabinets, shelves, and doors. The occupation is deeply rooted in a tradition of skilled craftsmanship, with many workers employed in small workshops, bespoke furniture studios, and larger manufacturing plants. The Czech furniture industry has a strong export orientation, and there is steady demand for custom woodworking, restoration of historic pieces, and modern joinery. The role requires precision, creativity, and knowledge of wood properties, tools, and finishing techniques. Automation is increasing, but hands-on skills remain essential for high-end and specialized work.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 9,260. The outlook for cabinet-makers in Czechia remains stable, driven by a sustained demand for quality furniture and interior renovations. Although the industry faces competition from imported flat-pack products, there is a growing niche for custom and sustainable woodwork. The shortage of skilled craftspeople is notable, especially for those with digital woodworking skills (CNC operation) and restoration expertise. The Czech government includes this trade on its shortage list, enabling streamlined migration. Employment is primarily in the Moravia and Bohemia regions, with opportunities in both urban and rural areas. The profession offers stable employment for those willing to adapt to modern techniques.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a three-year apprenticeship (Výuční list) followed by several years as a journeyman. Master craftsmen can open their own workshops or lead production teams. Additional specialisations include furniture design, restoration conservation, or CNC programming. Further education at secondary vocational schools with maturita allows access to supervisory roles or technical college. The industry values experience and a portfolio of work. Self-employment is common, with skilled cabinet-makers able to command premium rates for custom projects. Continuous learning of new materials and joinery methods is important for career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom furniture
- Joining systems
- Restoration
- Sustainable materials

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a cabinet-maker in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. A qualified cabinet-maker can expect between 350,000 and 550,000 CZK annually before tax. Skilled craftsmen with specialisation can earn up to 700,000 CZK.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as cabinet-makers in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card. You need a job offer, a valid work qualification (e.g., apprenticeship certificate), and meet general conditions. Since the occupation is on the shortage list, the application is expedited without a labour market test.

**Q: Is Czech language proficiency required for cabinet-making jobs?**
A: While not strictly mandatory for some employers, at least basic Czech (A2 level) is highly recommended for safety instructions, client communication, and daily cooperation. Many employers expect conversational Czech.
