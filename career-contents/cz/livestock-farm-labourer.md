# Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 9212 (ISCO08)**

Livestock farm labourers in Czechia perform manual tasks related to animal husbandry, including feeding, watering, cleaning, and assisting with veterinary care on farms raising cattle, pigs, poultry, or sheep. The role is essential to the country's agricultural sector, which employs a significant rural workforce, though many positions are seasonal or temporary. Demand is stable but subject to mechanization trends.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; most labourers remain in entry-level positions or advance to skilled livestock farmers or team leaders after several years of experience. Additional vocational training in animal science or farm management can lead to higher-level roles, but the path is not formalized. Many workers transition to other agricultural or manual labour jobs.
