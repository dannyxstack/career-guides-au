# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons in Czechia prepare technical drawings and plans for engineering, construction, and manufacturing projects, working closely with architects, engineers, and designers. They use CAD software to produce detailed blueprints for buildings, machinery, electronics, or infrastructure. The occupation is common in the industrial and construction sectors, with a steady demand for precision skills. Czechia's strong automotive, machinery, and construction industries rely on skilled draughtspersons to translate concepts into executable designs. The role is typical in both private engineering firms and public infrastructure projects.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 4,598. Employment outlook for draughtspersons in Czechia is stable to positive, driven by ongoing construction, manufacturing, and infrastructure modernisation. The shift towards Building Information Modeling (BIM) and advanced CAD tools is increasing demand for digitally proficient drafters. Green energy projects (e.g., wind, solar) and transport upgrades (railways, roads) also fuel demand. While automation poses some threat, complex design tasks still require human oversight. The occupation appears on some regional shortage lists, suggesting moderate recruitment difficulty. Overall, moderate job growth is expected, with opportunities in firms adopting BIM.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with junior drafter tasks, advancing to senior drafter or specialist (e.g., structural or electrical drafting). With experience and further education, draughtspersons can become design engineers, project coordinators, or BIM managers. Many pursue a bachelor's degree in engineering or architecture to move up. Czechia offers vocational retraining programmes (rekvalifikace) for CAD skills. Self-employment as a freelance drafter is also an option, especially for experienced professionals with a portfolio. Networking and CAD certifications (e.g., Autodesk) enhance career mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- BIM
- CAD
- construction
- manufacturing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a draughtsperson in Czechia?**
A: Salaries for draughtspersons in Czechia range from 500,000 to 700,000 CZK annually for experienced professionals, with entry-level around 350,000 CZK. Senior drafters or those with BIM skills can earn over 800,000 CZK.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to get an Employee Card as a draughtsperson?**
A: Yes, the Employee Card requires a confirmed job offer from a Czech employer. The position must meet the qualification requirements (typically secondary technical education). Draughtspersons are not on the highly skilled shortage list but are considered skilled.

**Q: Can I become a design engineer after being a draughtsperson?**
A: Yes, many draughtspersons advance to design engineer roles by gaining experience and obtaining a bachelor's or master's degree in engineering. Czechia offers part-time study options for working professionals.
