# Research and Development Manager — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1223 (ISCO08)**

Research and Development Managers in Czechia oversee teams of scientists and engineers, driving innovation in industries such as automotive, IT, pharma, and manufacturing. They plan R&D strategies, manage budgets, and coordinate projects to develop new products or improve processes, aligning with Czechia's strong industrial base and growing focus on technology and research. This role is critical in a country with a high concentration of automotive and engineering firms, as well as a expanding R&D sector supported by EU funds and foreign investment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for R&D Managers typically begins with a technical role (e.g., senior researcher or engineer) followed by promotion to team leader, then manager. Advancement to senior R&D director or chief technology officer (CTO) is common in larger firms. Mobility across industries (automotive, pharma, IT) is feasible due to transferable skills. Professionals with a PhD and international experience often have an edge. The role may also lead to executive positions in innovation or strategic management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated analysis of literature reviews and patent searches
- Recording experimental data and performing preliminary statistical analysis
- Automatic generation of project documents (e.g., progress reports, technical memos)
- Regular test case design and execution

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Feasibility analysis and solution design for R&D projects accelerated by AI
- Optimizes experimental parameters and resource allocation using AI prediction models
- AI-assisted team collaboration and knowledge management, improving cross-department communication efficiency
- Real-Time AI Market Intelligence Analysis Drives Innovation Direction Decisions

**Human moat:**

- Cross-disciplinary innovative thinking and strategic direction setting
- Complex technical decision-making and uncertainty risk management
- Deep customer needs insight and productization capability
- Team leadership and talent pipeline development

**Skills to build:**

- Deployment and use of AI tools (e.g., machine learning platforms, automated experiment systems)
- Data-driven decision making and statistical modeling
- AI project management and agile development methods
- Technology innovation commercialization and intellectual property strategy
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and systems thinking
- Basic programming (Python/R) and API integration

**Key growth areas:**

- automotive R&D
- pharmaceutical innovation
- IT and software development
- renewable energy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence the salary of an R&D Manager in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, industry (e.g., automotive vs. pharma), company size, and location. Performance bonuses and benefits are common. The role is well-compensated due to high demand and specialization.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as an R&D Manager in Czechia under the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if you have a Master's degree or higher and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average (about 60,000 CZK gross monthly in 2025). The employer must be registered. The Blue Card allows family reunification and easier mobility within the EU.

**Q: What languages are important for an R&D Manager in Czechia?**
A: Czech is important for daily operations and team communication in local firms, while English is essential for international collaboration and R&D reporting. German is an asset in automotive and engineering sectors.
