# Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 8154 (ISCO08)**

Bleaching, Dyeing and Fabric Cleaning Machine Operators in Czechia operate and monitor machinery used in textile finishing processes such as bleaching, dyeing, printing, and cleaning. They work mainly in the textile and apparel industry, which has a historical presence in regions like Liberec and Brno but has declined since the 1990s. Today, the sector focuses on technical textiles and specialty fabrics. Operators ensure quality control, maintain equipment, and follow safety protocols for handling chemicals. The role requires attention to detail and basic technical skills, often learned through vocational training or on-the-job experience.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operator in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level operators earn around 22,000–26,000 CZK per month. Experienced operators can earn up to 35,000 CZK, and supervisors or specialists in technical textiles may reach 40,000 CZK. These figures are pre-tax; add employer social/health contributions. Compare with the national median wage of ~40,000 CZK.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa for this occupation in Czechia?**
A: Yes, but it is not a high-skilled occupation. You may apply for an Employee Card if you have a job offer. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled locally. The job must meet minimum wage requirements. However, the process is restrictive, and the occupation is not on the shortage list, so approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What career advancement is possible for machine operators in this field?**
A: With experience and additional training, you can become a shift supervisor, quality controller, or process technician. Some operators move into equipment sales or technical support. Further education, such as a technician diploma, can lead to production management roles.
