# Pet Groomers and Animal Care Workers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

In Czechia, this occupation covers professionals who groom pets and care for animals in kennels, shelters, veterinary clinics, and pet shops. The growing pet ownership trend drives steady demand, especially in urban areas. The role requires patience, manual dexterity, and knowledge of animal behaviour. Entry is possible through vocational training or private courses, and many workers operate as self-employed.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically includes advancement to senior groomer, salon manager, or self-employment with own business. Specialisation in exotic animals or dog training can differentiate workers. Continuous skill development through workshops and certifications improves earning potential. Some move into veterinary assistant roles with further education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Client appointment and schedule management
- Basic beauty records and billing
- Standardized communication (e.g., reminders, follow-ups)
- Security monitoring (e.g., camera analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze pet fur health for care recommendations
- Use AI tools to optimize inventory and supplies management
- Use AI diagnostic tools to assist in identifying skin issues
- Using AI to generate personalized beauty solutions and recommendations

**Human moat:**

- Calming techniques for handling anxious or aggressive animals
- Adjust grooming techniques flexibly based on individual pet condition
- Building emotional trust and personalized service with customers
- Creativity and aesthetic judgment in hand styling

**Skills to build:**

- Learn animal behavior knowledge to improve soothing skills
- Master the use of AI-assisted diagnostic tools
- Enhancing advanced beauty and styling design skills
- Learn to use customer relationship management software
- Attend professional certification courses to enhance competitiveness
- develop social media marketing skills to expand client base

**Key growth areas:**

- Pet grooming
- Animal boarding
- Veterinary clinics
- Pet daycare

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, region, and employment type. Many groomers work on commission or are self-employed. Official wage data from the ISPV survey provide reliable averages, but overall pay is modest compared to other occupations.

**Q: Can I work as a pet groomer in Czechia under the Employee Card?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Czech employer and the position meets the minimum salary threshold. However, this occupation is not on the shortage list, and the process may be more challenging. EU/EEA nationals do not need a permit.

**Q: What language skills are required for this job?**
A: Basic Czech is usually necessary for client communication and animal handling. In international settings or large cities, English may suffice for certain roles, but Czech is preferred.
