# Education Managers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1345 (ISCO08)**

Education managers in Czechia oversee schools, universities, training centers, and other educational institutions, ensuring efficient operations, curriculum compliance, and staff management. With a strong emphasis on quality improvement and digitalization, this occupation is critical in adapting Czech education to modern demands and addressing regional shortages.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 13,020. Demand for education managers in Czechia remains stable, driven by an aging workforce, school reforms, and a push for digital transformation. **Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression often starts from teacher to deputy head to principal, or from administrative roles. Opportunities for advancement include regional inspectorates, ministry roles, or consultancy. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an education manager in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by institution type and location. Typically, school principals earn between 50,000 and 80,000 CZK monthly before tax, while university deans may earn higher.

**Q: Can a non-EU national become an education manager in Czechia?**
A: Yes, but requires a job offer, recognition of qualifications, and an Employee Card or EU Blue Card. Czech language proficiency at B2 level is usually expected.

**Q: What are the key requirements to become a school principal?**
A: A master's degree in education, at least 5 years of teaching experience, completed school management training, and passing a selection process by the school's founding body.
