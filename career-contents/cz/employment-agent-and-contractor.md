# Employment Agent — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 3333 (ISCO08)**

Employment agents and contractors in Czechia match job seekers with employers, working for recruitment agencies or as independent contractors. They operate in a dynamic labour market with strong demand for skilled workers, especially in IT, engineering, and healthcare. The industry is regulated by the Labour Code and requires a license for temporary agency work. Many agencies focus on both domestic and cross-border recruitment, leveraging Czechia's central European location and EU free movement. Digital recruitment tools and remote work trends are reshaping the role.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence salary for employment agents in Czechia?**
A: Salary depends on experience, specialization (e.g., IT pays more), agency size, and location (Prague higher). Commission or bonuses are common, often 20-40% of placement fees.

**Q: What visa is needed for a non-EU national to work as an employment agent?**
A: Typically an Employee Card is required, with a job offer from a licensed agency. EU Blue Card may apply if salary exceeds the high-skilled threshold. Intra-Company Transfer is possible for multinational transfers.

**Q: What education is required to become an employment agent in Czechia?**
A: No mandatory degree, but a bachelor's in HR, business, or psychology is advantageous. Professional certifications (e.g., from APPS) are valued but not required. A trade license is mandatory for self-employed agents.
