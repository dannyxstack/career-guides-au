# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1431 (ISCO08)**

Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers in Czechia oversee the daily operations of facilities such as sports halls, swimming pools, cultural centers, and recreational parks. They manage staff, budgets, programming, and ensure compliance with safety and regulations. In Czechia, these roles are concentrated in larger cities like Prague, Brno, and Ostrava, as well as in tourist regions such as the Krkonoše mountains or South Moravia. Many centers are operated by municipalities or private companies, with a growing emphasis on wellness, sports tourism, and cultural events. The position requires strong organizational, financial, and leadership skills.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a sports centre manager in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by facility size, location, and sector. In Prague, for a medium-sized sports centre, the average gross annual salary is around 500,000–700,000 CZK. Public-sector roles tend to be lower, while private luxury centers may offer higher. Performance bonuses are common.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a sports centre manager in Czechia?**
A: Yes, but a work permit is required. The Employee Card is the most common route. The job must meet the labour market test unless on the shortage list (this occupation is not on it). Alternatively, if part of a multinational, an Intra-Company Transfer may be possible.

**Q: Is a university degree mandatory for this role in Czechia?**
A: Not always; many managers have a bachelor's degree in management or related fields, but substantial experience in the industry can substitute. Some positions accept higher vocational education (DiS.) or relevant certifications.
