# Traditional Chief and Head of Village — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1113 (ISCO08)**

This occupation, which is virtually non-existent in the modern Czech Republic, refers to hereditary or customary leaders of traditional communities. In Czechia, local governance is handled by elected mayors (starosta) and municipal councils, with no official recognition for traditional chiefs. The role may exist informally among some ethnic minorities, such as the Roma or in isolated rural areas, but it carries no legal authority or employment status.
