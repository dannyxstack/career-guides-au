# Telecommunications Engineering Technicians — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Czechia install, maintain, and repair telecommunications systems including fiber optics, 5G networks, and broadband. They work for providers like O2, T-Mobile, and Vodafone, or for infrastructure companies. The role requires technical skills in electronics and networking, often gained through vocational training or a bachelor's degree. With Czechia's push for digitalization and smart cities, demand is steady, especially in expanding urban areas.

The government's Digital Czechia strategy and EU funding for rural connectivity drive demand. Technicians are needed for network upgrades and maintenance. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a field technician, advancing to senior technician, team leader, or network specialist. Further specialization in cybersecurity or network design opens paths to engineering roles. Some move to IT support or project management. Certifications from Huawei, Cisco, or Nokia boost prospects. Czechia offers on-the-job training and apprenticeship programs, especially in larger telecom firms.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Czechia?**
A: Annual pre-tax salaries range from approximately 350,000 CZK for entry-level to 550,000 CZK for experienced technicians. Senior roles or those with certifications may earn up to 700,000 CZK.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Czechia?**
A: Yes, through the Employee Card program. You need a job offer from a Czech employer and recognition of your qualifications (if required). The occupation is not on the highly qualified list for EU Blue Card.
