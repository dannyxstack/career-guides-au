# Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 2132 (ISCO08)**

Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Czechia provide expert guidance to agricultural, forestry, and fisheries enterprises on best practices, sustainability, regulatory compliance, and technology adoption. They work for government agencies, research institutions, private consultancies, or as independent contractors, supporting rural development and the transition to climate-smart land management. The role is essential in modernizing Czechia's primary sector.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression often starts with a technical or university degree, followed by specialization in crop protection, forestry economics, or aquaculture. Experienced advisers may become senior consultants, team leaders, or policy analysts. Continuing education in environmental management or data analytics enhances prospects. Self-employment or cooperative advisory roles are common for experienced professionals.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate standardized soil test reports and crop growth cycle analyses
- Automated fertiliser recommendation based on historical data and satellite imagery
- Draft basic sustainable forest management plans
- Automatically aggregate fishing catch data and generate compliance reports
- Answer common pest and disease control questions (knowledge base chatbot)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize precision agriculture decision-making recommendations using AI remote sensing analysis and big data
- Simulate long-term environmental impacts of different planting/breeding strategies using AI models
- Real-time monitoring of weather and market data, dynamically adjusting advisor recommendations
- Automates data collection and initial analysis, allowing consultants to focus on farmer communication and customized solutions
- AI-assisted generation of visual reports to improve communication efficiency with farmers

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and long-term relationships with farmers
- On-site problem diagnosis based on local experience and intuition
- Handling complex, unique sustainability conflicts involving multiple stakeholders
- Adaptability to unstructured, low-information-density environments (e.g., remote farms)
- Cross-disciplinary integration (agriculture, ecology, economics) providing customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Application of AI tools (e.g., remote sensing analysis platforms, agricultural decision support systems)
- Data science fundamentals (Python/R, statistical modeling, data visualization).
- Precision agriculture technology (drones, sensors, IoT data analysis)
- Environmental and social impact assessment methodology
- Communication and change management skills (to promote adoption of new technologies among farmers)
- Continuous learning and industry knowledge updates (policy, market, biotechnology).

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Farming
- Sustainable Agriculture
- Forestry Management
- Rural Development

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Junior advisers often earn 25,000–35,000 CZK monthly, while senior advisers in public service or private firms can earn 45,000–60,000 CZK. Exact figures depend on region and employer.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Czechia?**
A: Yes, through an Employee Card or EU Blue Card if they meet qualification and salary thresholds. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by an EU/EEA candidate (labor market test).

**Q: Is this occupation on the Czech shortage list?**
A: No, it is not currently on the official shortage list, but there is consistent demand for specialists, especially in sustainable practices and digital farming.
