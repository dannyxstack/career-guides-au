# Garment and Related Patternmaker and Cutter — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7532 (ISCO08)**

Garment and related patternmakers and cutters create patterns and cut fabrics or other materials for clothing, upholstery, and similar products. In Czechia, this occupation is concentrated in the textile and fashion industry, with major employers in regions like Prague, Brno, and Zlín. The sector has seen a shift towards technical textiles and workwear, while traditional garment manufacturing has declined. Workers typically hold vocational qualifications and are employed in small to medium-sized workshops or industrial settings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for garment patternmakers and cutters in Czechia typically moves from apprentice to skilled worker, then to senior cutter or pattern designer. Some advance to production manager or start their own tailoring business. Additional training in fashion design or production management can open roles in quality control or sample making. The field rewards precision, creativity, and familiarity with modern cutting machinery.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Technical Textiles
- Custom Tailoring
- CAD Pattern Making
- Sustainable Fashion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions earn around 25,000 CZK monthly, while experienced workers can earn 35,000-45,000 CZK. Senior or specialized roles may exceed 50,000 CZK. The industry is not high-paying compared to IT or engineering.

**Q: Can a non-EU patternmaker get a work visa for Czechia?**
A: Yes, with a job offer, you can apply for an Employee Card. The occupation is not on the shortage list, but the process is straightforward if you meet the qualifications (typically vocational training in garment making). Ensure your employer supports the application.

**Q: Is there demand for patternmakers in Czechia?**
A: Demand is moderate, with stable openings in custom tailoring and technical textiles. The rise of automation means fewer jobs in mass production, but skilled patternmakers remain needed for prototyping and high-end work.
