# Computer Network Professionals — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 2523 (ISCO08)**

Computer network professionals in Czechia design, implement, and manage local and wide area networks, ensuring secure and efficient data communication. With the country's strong IT sector and digital transformation across industries, these specialists are in high demand. Prague, Brno, and Ostrava are key hubs, but remote work is increasingly common. Typical employers include tech companies, telecoms, banks, and government agencies. The role requires up-to-date knowledge of cybersecurity, cloud services, and emerging technologies like 5G and IoT.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression often starts from junior network administrator or technician roles, advancing to network engineer, architect, or security specialist. With experience, professionals can move into IT management, consultancy, or solutions architecture. Certifications (CCNA, CCNP, CompTIA Network+) significantly enhance prospects. Many professionals also transition into cybersecurity or cloud infrastructure roles as the industry evolves. The Czech education system offers bachelor's and master's degrees in computer networks, but practical skills and vendor certifications are highly valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine Ops such as Auto-scaling and Load Balancing, automated by AI scripts or cloud-native tools
- Infrastructure as Code (IaC) template generation and conflict resolution; AI can automatically optimize templates
- Basic log analysis, alert response, and fault report generation; AI can handle anomalies in real-time
- Standardized cloud resource cost reports and expense forecasts; AI generates more accurate budget recommendations
- Simple cybersecurity configuration checks and compliance scans, AI can auto-fix common vulnerabilities

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Cloud Architecture Design: AI provides reference architectures, cost comparisons, and best practice recommendations to accelerate decisions
- Root cause analysis: AI correlates massive logs and metrics to intelligently locate anomaly sources
- Performance optimization: AI analyzes usage patterns to recommend instance types, storage strategies, and network configurations
- Security incident response: AI assists with threat identification and remediation recommendations, engineers focus on decision-making
- Multi-cloud management: AI uniformly monitors multiple cloud platforms and provides resource efficiency optimization recommendations

**Human moat:**

- Complex system integration and migration planning, requiring understanding of business needs and legacy systems
- Creative diagnosis of non-standard faults, requiring cross-domain knowledge
- Cost governance and business negotiation, understanding contract terms and risks
- Long-term evolution design for high-availability architecture, balancing performance, security and cost
- Team collaboration and client communication, translating technical solutions into business value

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in scripting languages like Python/Bash, able to write AI calling tools
- Master AI/ML basics, understand model deployment (MLOps) and inference optimisation
- In-depth professional certification in at least one cloud platform (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Proficiency in container orchestration (Kubernetes) and serverless architecture
- Learning AI-assisted DevOps toolchains (e.g., GitHub Copilot, CloudFormation AI)
- Strengthening network, security, and compliance knowledge to design zero-trust architectures

**Key growth areas:**

- Cybersecurity
- Cloud Networking
- IoT
- 5G

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a computer network professional in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions start around 500,000 CZK annually, mid-level ranges from 900,000 to 1,200,000 CZK, and senior/architect roles can exceed 1,800,000 CZK. Prague offers higher pay, but cost of living is also higher.

**Q: Can I work as a computer network professional in Czechia without speaking Czech?**
A: Yes, many international companies and IT firms operate in English, especially in Prague and Brno. However, learning basic Czech is helpful for daily life and integration. For employee card applications, the job offer must meet salary thresholds.

**Q: Is this occupation on the Czech shortage list?**
A: Yes, computer network professionals are in short supply in Czechia. The occupation appears on the Ministry of Labour's 'Highly Skilled Worker' list, facilitating faster processing for Employee Cards and Blue Cards.
