# Database and Network Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 2529 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a range of specialised roles in database administration, data architecture, network design, and related fields not separately classified under ISCO-08. In Czechia, these professionals are essential for managing the country's growing digital infrastructure, including corporate IT systems, cloud services, and telecommunications networks. The demand is driven by digital transformation across industries such as finance, e-commerce, manufacturing, and the public sector. Czechia has a strong tradition of IT education and a competitive job market for skilled database and network experts, with many positions concentrated in Prague, Brno, and Ostrava.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior database administrator or network technician, advancing to senior specialist, team leader, or architect roles. Many professionals move into IT project management, cybersecurity, or data science. In Czechia, large multinational companies and IT service providers offer clear career ladders, while start-ups provide broader exposure. Continuous learning is vital, with certifications (Oracle, Cisco, Microsoft) enhancing career mobility. The path often leads to roles such as IT infrastructure manager, chief data officer, or freelance consultant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine database backup, recovery, optimization and other scripted operational tasks
- Automated deployment and compliance checks for network device configurations
- Rule-based log analysis and alert generation
- Routine performance monitoring and generation of capacity planning reports
- Basic data migration and ETL workflow orchestration

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI assistance for database performance tuning (e.g., index recommendations)
- AI-driven network anomaly detection and root cause analysis
- AIOps for predictive maintenance
- Automatically generate operation reports using natural language generation technology
- AI-assisted cybersecurity threat analysis and response

**Human moat:**

- Complex system architecture design and cross-system integration decisions
- Strategic planning for high availability and disaster recovery solutions
- Business understanding and data model design
- Creative problem-solving for non-standard faults
- In-depth communication and collaboration with business departments and management

**Skills to build:**

- AI/ML fundamentals and model deployment skills
- Advanced operations of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible)
- Data security and compliance (GDPR, etc.)

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. A junior database or network professional can expect around 450,000–600,000 CZK annually. Mid-level roles often range from 700,000 to 1,000,000 CZK, while senior architects or team leads can earn over 1,200,000 CZK. These are pre-tax figures and may include bonuses. Czechia's IT sector offers competitive compensation compared to other industries.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU database/network professionals?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an Employee Card if they have a confirmed job offer from a Czech employer. The EU Blue Card is also available for highly qualified workers meeting the salary threshold (approx. 1.5x national average wage). Both permits allow work and residence, and can lead to permanent residency. EU/EEA citizens need no visa.

**Q: Is experience with cloud platforms required for this occupation?**
A: While not mandatory for all roles, cloud skills (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) are increasingly important, especially in database and network management. Many employers prefer candidates with cloud certifications, as Czech companies rapidly migrate to hybrid or cloud-native infrastructures.
