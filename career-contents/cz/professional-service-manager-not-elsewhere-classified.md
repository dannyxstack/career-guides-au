# Professional Services Manager Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1349 (ISCO08)**

Professional Services Managers oversee teams delivering expertise in fields such as legal, accounting, consulting, and business advisory. In Czechia, these roles are concentrated in Prague and Brno, driven by a strong presence of international firms and business process outsourcing centers. They require extensive professional experience, often a university degree, and strong leadership skills. The position bridges strategy and operations, ensuring high-quality client service and team performance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a specialist role (e.g., lawyer, accountant, consultant) to team leader, then to manager. Many professionals pursue an MBA or advanced certifications to accelerate advancement. International mobility within the EU is common, especially in multinational corporations. In Czechia, networking and language skills (English plus Czech) are valuable. Some managers eventually move into director or partner positions.

- Generate customer reports and market analysis drafts
- Automated compliance checks and process monitoring
- Standardized data organization and trend forecasting.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Professional Services Manager in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and location. In Prague, experienced managers can expect between 1,200,000 and 2,500,000 CZK annually, with top earners in consulting and legal services reaching over 3,000,000 CZK. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a Professional Services Manager in Czechia if I am from outside the EU?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals generally need a work permit. The Employee Card or EU Blue Card are common options. Your employer must sponsor the permit, and the position must meet salary thresholds. EU/EEA citizens can work freely without a permit.

**Q: What career progression can I expect after becoming a Professional Services Manager in Czechia?**
A: Many managers advance to senior management, director, or partner roles. Some move into executive positions such as Chief Operating Officer or Managing Director. Others specialize in niche areas like M&A advisory or risk management. Continuing education and networking are key.
