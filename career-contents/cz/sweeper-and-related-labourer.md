# Sweeper and Related Labourer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 9613 (ISCO08)**

Sweepers and related labourers in Czechia perform manual cleaning and sweeping tasks in public spaces, commercial buildings, construction sites, and industrial facilities. This low-skilled occupation is common among EU/EEA workers and some non-EU migrants, often employed through agencies or directly by municipalities and cleaning companies. The work is physically demanding and typically paid at or near the minimum wage.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; experienced workers may advance to team leader or supervisor roles, or start their own cleaning business. Some move into related fields like waste management or janitorial services. Formal training is not required, but language skills and reliability improve opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI scheduling and monitoring tools
- Automated cleaning equipment maintenance
- Basic Chemistry and Disinfectant Knowledge
- Data recording and analysis skills
- Communication and customer service skills
- Compliance and safety regulations training

**Key growth areas:**

- urban cleaning
- public hygiene
- commercial cleaning
- maintenance services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sweeper in Czechia?**
A: Salaries are generally at or slightly above the minimum wage, which in 2025 is expected to be around 20,000 CZK per month. Actual earnings vary by employer and region.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a sweeper in Czechia?**
A: Yes, but only under an Employee Card, which requires a confirmed job offer and a labour market test showing no suitable EU worker is available. Low wages may make approval harder.
