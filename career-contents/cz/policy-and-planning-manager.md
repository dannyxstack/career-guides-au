# Policy and Planning Managers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1213 (ISCO08)**

Policy and Planning Managers in Czechia are responsible for developing, implementing, and monitoring policies and strategic plans within government ministries, regional authorities, and public institutions. They analyze socio-economic trends, coordinate with stakeholders, and ensure alignment with national and EU regulations. This role is critical for effective public administration and is typically held by experienced professionals with deep knowledge of Czech legislative frameworks and policy processes.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically involves starting as a policy analyst or junior planner, advancing to senior specialist roles, then to management positions. In Czechia, progression often requires a combination of advanced degrees (master's or PhD), specialized training in public administration, and experience in the civil service. Lateral moves between ministries or to regional/local government are common. Some managers move into consulting or international organizations, but the core career path remains within state administration.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically collect and organise policy-related data (e.g., economic indicators, regulatory changes)
- Generate initial policy impact analysis report and summary
- Quantified results of simulated policy options (e.g., budget impact model)
- Monitoring news and social media trends and generating public opinion briefs
- Format and submit compliance documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI tools to quickly compare policy frameworks across countries and identify best practices
- Analyze public feedback using natural language processing tools to optimize policy communication strategies
- Use predictive models to assess long-term socio-economic benefits of policies
- Leverage collaborative AI platforms to efficiently manage cross-departmental policy-making processes
- Use AI to generate multiple versions of policy drafts for leadership review

**Human moat:**

- Making balanced and value-based judgments amidst conflicting interests of multiple parties
- Build trust and conduct political negotiations with senior officials, the public, and others.
- Deep understanding of local culture, history, and power structures
- Assume ultimate responsibility in situations of uncertainty and ethical grey areas
- Proactive policy framework for innovative design rather than optimizing existing solutions

**Skills to build:**

- AI data analysis tools (e.g. Python/R, SQL, and automated reporting platforms)
- Policy modeling and simulation software (e.g., System Dynamics)
- Stakeholder management and conflict mediation skills
- AI ethics and bias recognition capabilities
- Digital governance and e-government platform operation
- Agile Project Management and Cross-Departmental Collaboration

**Key growth areas:**

- strategic planning
- policy analysis
- digital transformation
- regional development

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Policy and Planning Managers in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by seniority and location. Entry-level managers in ministries can expect around CZK 800,000 annually, while senior directors in central state administration may earn over CZK 1,500,000. Regional and local government salaries are typically lower.

**Q: Is it possible to obtain a work visa as a Policy and Planning Manager in Czechia?**
A: Yes. For non-EU nationals, the Employee Card is a common route if you have a confirmed job offer. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so a labor market test is needed. EU Blue Card may apply if the salary meets the threshold.

**Q: What language skills are required for this occupation?**
A: Fluency in Czech is essential for mid to senior roles, as policy work involves interacting with stakeholders and drafting documents in Czech. For some international policy roles, English proficiency is also required.
