# Rigger and Cable Splicer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7215 (ISCO08)**

Riggers and cable splicers in Czechia are skilled tradespeople who erect and dismantle scaffolding, set up lifting equipment, and join electrical cables for power, telecom, and industrial installations. They work on construction sites, energy plants, and infrastructure projects, often at heights or in confined spaces. The occupation is vital for expanding renewable energy (wind turbines), upgrading telecom networks (fiber optics), and maintaining industrial facilities. With a strong tradition of vocational training, Czechia has a steady supply of apprentices, but demand often exceeds supply, especially for specialized roles like high-voltage cable splicing or heavy rigging. The job requires physical fitness, precision, and adherence to strict safety standards.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 593. Employment for riggers and cable splicers in Czechia is projected to grow steadily, driven by large infrastructure projects (highways, railways), renewable energy investments (wind and solar farms), and the rollout of 5G and fiber-optic networks. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a 3-year apprenticeship (výuční list) followed by on-the-job training. Workers can advance to foreman or supervisor roles, or specialize in areas like high-voltage cable splicing, wind turbine rigging, or telecom installation. Many become self-employed contractors, offering flexibility and higher pay. Additional certifications (e.g., in lifting operations, confined space rescue) boost earning potential. The profession also offers pathways to engineering technician roles with further education. Aging workforce in some specialties creates opportunities for younger entrants.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a rigger or cable splicer in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization, experience, and region. Entry-level workers earn around 25,000–30,000 CZK per month gross. Experienced specialists, especially in high-voltage or wind turbine work, can earn 40,000–55,000 CZK. Overtime and bonuses are common.

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a rigger or cable splicer in Czechia?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA/Swiss citizen, you do not need a work permit; only registration after 30 days. For non-EU citizens, you need an Employee Card, which requires a job offer and labor market test. The occupation is on the shortage list (as part of construction trades), which speeds up the process.

**Q: What training is required to become a rigger or cable splicer in Czechia?**
A: The standard path is a 3-year apprenticeship (výuční list) in a relevant field like cable work or structural engineering. Alternatively, adults can complete shorter retraining courses (rekvalifikace) offered by the Labour Office. Practical on-the-job training is essential, and certifications like working at heights are mandatory.
