# Fumigator and Other Pest and Weed Controller — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7544 (ISCO08)**

Fumigators and pest controllers in Czechia apply chemical and biological methods to eliminate pests, rodents, weeds, and pathogens in agricultural fields, forests, warehouses, and urban areas. They operate fumigation equipment, apply pesticides, and monitor treatment effectiveness, while adhering to strict safety and environmental regulations. The role is essential for maintaining crop yields, public health, and hygiene standards, particularly in the Moravian and South Bohemian agricultural regions, as well as in food processing facilities and municipal pest control services.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fumigator in Czechia?**
A: Salaries for fumigators and pest controllers in Czechia range from 28,000 to 35,000 CZK gross per month for entry-level positions, with experienced supervisors earning up to 45,000 CZK. Annual gross salary typically falls between 330,000 and 550,000 CZK. Pay varies by sector (agriculture vs. municipal), region, and client base.

**Q: Can a non-EU national obtain a work visa as a fumigator in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card (Zaměstnanecká karta). You need a confirmed job offer from a Czech employer, relevant qualifications (e.g., pesticide applicator certificate), health insurance, and proof of accommodation. Since fumigator is not on the shortage list, a labour market test (priority for EU workers) may apply. The card is issued for up to 2 years and can be extended.

**Q: What training is required to become a fumigator in Czechia?**
A: Most employers require a secondary vocational education in agriculture or a related field (3 years), plus a mandatory Certificate of Professional Competence for Pesticide Application from the Ministry of Agriculture. For fumigation with toxic gases, a separate Fumigation License is also mandatory. Short-term courses (2-6 months) are available for career changers.
