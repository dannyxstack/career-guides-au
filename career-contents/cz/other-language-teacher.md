# Other Language Teachers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 2353 (ISCO08)**

Other Language Teachers in Czechia deliver instruction in languages other than English, such as German, Russian, French, Spanish, or Czech for foreigners. They work in private language schools, public schools, corporate training, or as freelancers. The role is vital for integration of expats, international business, and EU mobility. Demand is steady, especially for German and less common languages. Czechia's strong industrial and trade ties with German-speaking countries boost need for German teachers. The occupation suits native speakers or highly proficient users with pedagogical training.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression often starts with entry-level teaching roles, advancing to senior teacher, coordinator, or director of studies at language schools. Experienced teachers may move to corporate training, university lecturing, or curriculum development. Freelancing and online platforms offer independence. Additional qualifications (e.g., DELTA, CELTA, or PhD) enhance prospects. In public schools, promotion to head of department or school administration is possible. Some teachers transition to translation or interpretation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic Grammar and Vocabulary Instruction
- Standardized exercise grading and feedback
- Language proficiency test score
- Simple conversation practice and correction

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized learning path design and recommendation (AI analyzes student weaknesses)
- Immersive virtual reality language environment creation (AI-generated situational dialogues)
- Real-time voice correction and pronunciation analysis
- Cultural background knowledge integration and dynamic courseware generation

**Human moat:**

- Emotional support and motivation for learning
- Cross-cultural communication and contextual interpretation
- Facilitating complex high-level discussions
- Creative writing and literary appreciation guidance.
- Emergency classroom interaction management

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI teaching tools (e.g., ChatGPT, speech recognition platforms)
- Data Analysis and Learning Outcome Evaluation
- Course design and blended teaching ability
- Knowledge of industry-specific language applications (e.g., business, law)
- Multilingual and Cross-Cultural Communication Skills
- Digital content creation (video, interactive courseware)

**Key growth areas:**

- German
- Russian
- Online Teaching
- Corporate Training

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a language teacher in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by sector: public school teachers earn around 350,000–500,000 CZK/year; private language schools pay 300,000–600,000 CZK depending on hours; experienced corporate trainers can earn 500,000+ CZK. Freelancers set their own rates, typically 400–800 CZK/hour.

**Q: What visa do non-EU language teachers need?**
A: Non-EU teachers usually apply for an Employee Card with a job offer from a Czech employer. The EU Blue Card is possible if salary meets the threshold (approx. 1.5x average wage). EU citizens need no visa.

**Q: Is language teaching considered a shortage occupation?**
A: Yes, teachers are in high demand, especially for German, French, and Russian. Some regions list language teachers on shortage lists, which can facilitate work permits.
