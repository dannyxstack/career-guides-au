# Metal Working Machine Tool Setters and Operators — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

Metal Working Machine Tool Setters and Operators in Czechia set up and operate machine tools to cut, shape, or finish metal parts. This occupation is vital in the country's strong manufacturing sector, particularly in automotive, engineering, and machinery industries. Workers typically have vocational training and work in factories across the country, with higher demand in industrial regions like Moravia-Silesia and Central Bohemia. The role requires precision and technical skills, with opportunities for advancement to CNC programming or supervisory positions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for machine tool setters often starts with apprenticeship and leads to CNC operator, then programmer, or team leader. With further education, some move into manufacturing engineering or quality control. The industry values experience and specialization, such as in 5-axis machining or CAD/CAM. Skilled workers can also advance to instructing at vocational schools. However, movement to management is limited without additional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC Machining
- Precision Engineering
- Automation
- Industry 4.0

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and shift work. Generally, entry-level positions start around minimum wage, while experienced CNC operators earn more. Overtime and bonuses for night shifts are common. The occupation is on the shortage list, which can lead to competitive offers.

**Q: Can I get a work visa for this job as a non-EU national?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an Employee Card (Zaměstnanecká karta) with a confirmed job offer from a Czech employer. Since this occupation is on the shortage list, the process is streamlined. You will need to provide proof of qualifications and meet other requirements.

**Q: Is prior experience required to start in this field?**
A: While some employers offer on-the-job training, most prefer candidates with a vocational certificate in machining. Apprenticeship programs combine classroom learning with practical experience and are the standard entry route.
