# Meter Readers and Vending-machine Collectors — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Czechia are employed by utility companies, energy suppliers, and vending machine operators to travel to residential and commercial locations to record meter readings and collect cash from vending machines. The role requires physical mobility, basic numeracy, and attention to detail. With the gradual rollout of smart meters and cashless payments, the demand for traditional meter reading is slowly declining, but these workers remain essential in many areas, especially for older infrastructure and seasonal adjustments.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression for this occupation in Czechia is limited but possible. With experience, workers may advance to team leader or supervisor roles, overseeing a group of readers or collectors. Some transition into utility administration, data analysis (meter consumption patterns), or vending machine maintenance. Others use the role as a stepping stone to more specialized positions in logistics or field service management. On-the-job training and acquisition of a driving license are common enhancements.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings
- On-site inspection of measuring equipment faults (initial judgment)
- Generate manual meter reading reports.
- Reading meters along a fixed route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile apps or handheld terminals to assist with remote confirmation of abnormal readings.
- Identify potential leaks or tampering by analyzing consumption patterns via AI
- Use autonomous inspection drones or robots for meter reading in high-risk areas
- Provide energy advice to clients based on AI-generated energy saving reports.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair in complex on-site environments.
- In-person communication with clients over billing disputes or safety issues
- Decision-making ability in emergencies (e.g., gas leaks)
- Manual calibration and maintenance of old mechanical watch heads

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and anomaly detection
- Mobile device and specialized app operation
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic electrical/mechanical repair knowledge
- Drone or robot operation and maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- smart meter transition
- field data collection
- utility services
- vending machine operations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a meter reader in Czechia?**
A: Salaries in this occupation are typically below the national average and vary by region and employer. The job is often paid on an hourly basis with additional compensation for travel.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as meter readers in Czechia?**
A: Yes, but they need an Employee Card (Zaměstnanecká karta), which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The process may involve proving the job cannot be filled by an EU citizen.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for meter readers?**
A: With experience, meter readers can move into supervisory roles, utility data analysis, or transition to related fields like logistics or field service management.
