# Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

This occupation includes non-commissioned officers and enlisted personnel in the Czech Armed Forces, performing operational, technical, and support roles. Positions range from infantry and tank crews to logistics and communications specialists. Recruitment is primarily for Czech citizens, with a focus on physical fitness and national service commitment. The Czech military is a professional force with modern equipment, and personnel serve under the Ministry of Defence.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for other ranks in the Czech armed forces?**
A: Entry-level private soldiers earn around 300,000–400,000 CZK annually, including allowances. Senior non-commissioned officers can earn up to 600,000 CZK.

**Q: Can a non-EU national join the Czech armed forces as a soldier?**
A: Generally, non-EU nationals are not eligible for enlisted roles. Only Czech citizens or EU/EEA nationals with special permits may serve, and even then opportunities are limited. Civilian specialist roles may be open via Employee Card.

**Q: What are the promotion prospects for other ranks?**
A: Promotion is based on time in service, qualifications, and performance. From private to corporal typically takes 2–4 years, with further advancement to sergeant and warrant officer possible within 10–15 years.
