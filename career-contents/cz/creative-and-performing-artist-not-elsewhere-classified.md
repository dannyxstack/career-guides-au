# Creative and Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 2659 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of artists including dancers, choreographers, circus performers, street artists, and other creative performers not classified in other specific categories. In Czechia, the field is small but vibrant, centered in Prague and other cultural hubs, with opportunities in theatres, cultural festivals, and the growing events industry. The labour market is highly competitive and often project-based, with many artists working part-time or freelance. Demand fluctuates with tourism and public funding for culture.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a creative and performing artist in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary widely; freelance artists often earn below average wage, while permanent positions in state theatres may offer 20,000–35,000 CZK/month gross. High-profile performers can earn more through multiple projects.

**Q: Can a non-EU artist get a work visa for Czechia?**
A: Yes, typically via an Employee Card if they have a job offer from a Czech employer. The job must meet qualification requirements and undergo a labour market test. EU Blue Card is possible but rare due to salary thresholds.

**Q: Is this occupation on the Czech shortage list?**
A: No, creative and performing artists are not included on the official shortage list for facilitated work permits.
