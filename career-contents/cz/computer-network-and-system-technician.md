# Computer Network and Systems Technician — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 3513 (ISCO08)**

Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Czechia install, configure, maintain, and troubleshoot local area networks (LANs), wide area networks (WANs), and computer systems. They are essential for ensuring connectivity and security in businesses, public institutions, and data centers. The role requires a mix of technical skills in hardware, software, and network protocols. With Czechia's strong industrial base and growing digital economy, demand for these technicians remains steady, particularly in IT services, manufacturing, and the public sector. Many positions are in Prague, Brno, and Ostrava, but opportunities exist nationwide.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a helpdesk or junior technician role, advancing to senior technician, network administrator, or network engineer. With further certifications (e.g., Cisco CCNP, CompTIA Network+) and experience, one can move into cybersecurity, cloud architecture, or IT management. Many technicians specialize in areas like wireless, security, or virtualization. Czechia has a vibrant IT community with training and certification opportunities. Some professionals later become freelance consultants or start their own IT service firms.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically diagnose common network issues and provide solutions
- Automatically generate network configuration and patch scripts
- Record work orders and categorize/escalate to senior teams
- User password reset and account management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI log analysis to quickly locate fault root causes
- Using network simulation tools to predict change impact.
- Early detection of anomalies through AI-driven monitoring.
- Using natural language queries to accelerate knowledge base resolution

**Human moat:**

- Root cause analysis and manual repair of complex faults
- Network architecture design and security policy formulation
- Effective communication with non-technical users
- Prioritizing urgent matters under pressure

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud network management (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Cybersecurity certifications (CISSP/CEH)
- Automation scripts (Python/Ansible)
- AI/ML basics and network analysis tools
- Hybrid network architecture design

**Key growth areas:**

- cloud computing
- cybersecurity
- network automation
- IoT infrastructure

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, location, and certifications. Entry-level positions start around 30,000 CZK/month, while experienced technicians with specialized skills can earn 60,000 CZK/month or more. Prague offers higher pay but also higher living costs.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Computer Network and Systems Technician in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card or EU Blue Card. The Employee Card is common for skilled IT roles. The position must meet the required skill level (usually ISCO 3). Czechia's shortage list includes IT occupations, which may expedite processing.

**Q: Is it necessary to speak Czech for this role?**
A: While many international companies use English as the working language, knowledge of Czech is advantageous for client-facing roles and integration. Basic Czech is recommended for daily life.
