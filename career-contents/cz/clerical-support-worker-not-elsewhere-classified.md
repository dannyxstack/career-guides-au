# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a broad range of administrative and clerical support roles in Czechia, including general office clerks, data entry operators, and assistants in various sectors. Workers perform tasks such as filing, record-keeping, mail handling, and basic customer service. In Czechia, these positions are common in all industries, often serving as entry-level roles requiring secondary education. The demand is steady but varies by region, with higher concentrations in Prague and other urban centers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving into specialized administrative roles (e.g., executive assistant, office manager) or shifting to other functions like accounting, HR, or customer service. Upskilling in digital tools, foreign languages, or industry-specific software enhances advancement prospects. Some workers pursue further education to transition into higher-skilled positions, while others remain in clerical roles long-term. The path is flexible but not highly structured.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization
- Remote Work
- Administrative Efficiency
- Industry Specialization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for clerical support workers in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by region and industry, but generally range from 25,000 to 35,000 CZK monthly pre-tax, with experienced workers earning up to 45,000 CZK. Prague and multinational companies tend to pay higher.

**Q: Can a non-EU national obtain a work visa for clerical support jobs in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card, but it is not a shortage occupation. The employer must prove the position could not be filled by an EU citizen. Success depends on labor market test and salary meeting minimum threshold.

**Q: What is the career progression for clerical workers in Czechia?**
A: Progression often leads to specialized administrative roles, team leadership, or lateral moves into HR, finance, or customer service. Additional certifications in accounting or project management can boost prospects.
