# Door-to-door Salesperson — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 5243 (ISCO08)**

Door-to-door salespersons sell products or services directly to consumers in their homes, often for energy, telecom, or charity sectors. In Czechia, this role is common in utilities and telecommunication industries, with many workers engaged on a commission basis. The occupation requires strong interpersonal skills and resilience, as face-to-face selling involves frequent rejection. It typically does not require formal education, but a trade license is mandatory. The sector is gradually shrinking due to digital alternatives, but still relevant for certain local services.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression in door-to-door sales is limited; typical paths lead to senior sales roles, team leader, or sales manager within the same company. Some individuals transition to telesales, retail, or independent entrepreneurship. In Czechia, experienced salespersons may become trainers or move into business development. The role is often a starting point for young or less educated workers, but long-term career advancement usually requires upskilling or moving to other sales channels.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial screening of potential client lists (AI automatically generates from data analysis)
- Standard product demonstration (chatbot or video alternative)
- Order collection and data entry (automated forms and CRM)
- Common Q&A in objection handling (AI knowledge base auto-reply)
- Follow-up and re-engagement (automated email/SMS sequences)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-personalized conversation scripts (real-time adjustments based on customer profiles)
- Optimize communication rhythm in real-time using emotion recognition tools
- Customer Relationship Data Analysis for Precision Recommendations and Upselling
- AI-assisted note-taking and summary generation to reduce paperwork

**Human moat:**

- Build deep trust relationships and emotional connections
- Complex negotiation and customized solutions
- Creativity & improv performance (humour, empathy)
- Handling non-standard, highly emotional rejection scenarios

**Skills to build:**

- CRM and sales automation tools (e.g., Salesforce)
- Data analysis and customer insight basics
- Video sales and remote communication skills
- AI conversation scripting tools (e.g., Gong, Chorus)

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a door-to-door salesperson in Czechia?**
A: Salary is usually commission-based, with earnings varying significantly by performance and industry. Some employers offer a small base salary plus bonuses. No official salary figures are provided here.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a door-to-door salesperson in Czechia?**
A: Generally not, as this occupation is considered low-skilled and does not qualify for an Employee Card. Most workers are EU/EEA citizens. Third-country nationals would need an exceptional justification.

**Q: Do I need any education to become a door-to-door salesperson?**
A: No formal education is required. However, a trade license is mandatory for self-employed individuals. Some employers provide basic sales training.
