# Elementary Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 9629 (ISCO08)**

Elementary workers not elsewhere classified perform simple and routine tasks in various sectors such as cleaning, hospitality, warehousing, and manufacturing, often requiring little to no formal education. In Czechia, these roles are common in the tourism, retail, and logistics industries, where they provide essential support services with on-the-job training.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for elementary workers in Czechia is limited without further training, but motivated individuals can advance to supervisory roles or move to skilled trades through vocational courses. Many workers use these positions as entry-level jobs, gaining experience before transitioning to more specialized occupations in hospitality, manufacturing, or transport.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple goods handling and stacking: replaced by autonomous mobile robots (AMRs)
- Basic cleaning (e.g., sweeping, mopping): replaced by automatic floor scrubbers or robotic vacuums
- Item classification and sorting: replaced by computer vision systems
- Mail or document distribution: replaced by digital notification systems
- Inventory counting assistance: replaced by RFID and drones

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Task scheduling and priority arrangement: AI optimizes daily work lists
- Equipment failure warning: AI analyzes sensor data to remind of maintenance
- Customer communication records: AI automatic transcription and classification of feedback
- Safety compliance checks: AI-assisted identification of hazardous areas
- Basic communication in multilingual environments: assisted by real-time translation tools

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability to unstructured environments (e.g., ad-hoc assembly tasks)
- Complex emotional communication and customer service (e.g., appeasing dissatisfied customers)
- Cross-task flexible decision-making (when AI recommendations are unclear)
- Fine manual operations (e.g., art packaging)
- Teamwork and on-site coordination skills

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (managing tasks using tablets/mobile phones)
- Operate semi-automated equipment (e.g., cleaning robots)
- Basic Data Analysis (Understanding Task Efficiency Reports)
- Interpersonal communication and customer service skills
- Basic English or a second language (to adapt to multicultural work environments)
- Safety and compliance basic training

**Key growth areas:**

- cleaning services
- warehousing
- hospitality
- manufacturing support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an elementary worker in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and region but are generally close to the national minimum wage (currently around 18,900 CZK per month pre-tax) or slightly above. Full-time workers often earn between minimum wage and 25,000 CZK per month depending on experience and employer.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa for this occupation?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card, but the employer must prove no suitable Czech/EU worker is available. Low-skilled positions are not always approved; it depends on the regional labor market. EU nationals have unrestricted access.

**Q: Is there any career progression for elementary workers?**
A: Yes, with experience and additional training, workers can become team leaders, supervisors, or move to skilled roles like kitchen chef, warehouse coordinator, or machine operator. Czechia offers vocational retraining programs through the Labour Office (Úřad práce).
