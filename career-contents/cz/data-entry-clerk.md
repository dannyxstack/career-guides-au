# Data Entry Clerk — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 4132 (ISCO08)**

Data Entry Clerks in Czechia are responsible for inputting, updating, and maintaining accurate data into computer systems, often from source documents. They work across various industries, including administration, finance, logistics, and the public sector. The role requires attention to detail, basic computer literacy, and efficient keyboard skills. In the Czech labour market, data entry positions are commonly found in larger companies and shared service centres, offering stable but often entry-level employment. While automation is gradually impacting the role, demand remains steady for those with strong accuracy and data handling skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for Data Entry Clerks in Czechia typically moves toward administrative assistant, office manager, or specialist roles in data quality, databases, or customer support. With additional training, clerks can advance into data analysis, business administration, or IT support. The role often serves as an entry point to the labour market for young workers or career changers. In larger firms, internal mobility is possible, especially for those who demonstrate reliability and acquire relevant certifications (e.g., Microsoft Office or databases). However, due to the repetitive nature, long-term career growth depends on proactive skill development.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually enter data from paper or electronic documents into databases or spreadsheets
- Check format and basic accuracy of input data
- Repetitive batch data entry, such as invoices and forms
- Basic data cleaning and deduplication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI tools to automatically extract document data, with humans handling exceptions.
- Use automated scripts to verify data consistency and improve efficiency
- Analyze data trends and generate reports through AI-assisted analysis

**Human moat:**

- Complex data judgment and anomaly handling capability
- Client communication and business understanding (e.g., explaining data deviations)
- Cross-system data integration and process optimization
- Maintain data privacy and security compliance

**Skills to build:**

- Learn Python or VBA for automated data processing
- Proficiency in advanced Excel functions (e.g., Power Query, macros)
- Familiar with OCR and RPA tools (e.g., UiPath)
- Data quality management and database basics (SQL)
- Basic data analysis and visualization (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- administrative support
- data management
- e-commerce logistics
- office automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Data Entry Clerk in Czechia?**
A: Salaries for Data Entry Clerks in Czechia typically range from 280,000 to 380,000 CZK per year gross, depending on experience, location, and sector. Entry-level positions may start around 250,000 CZK, while those with advanced skills or in specialised fields can earn up to 450,000 CZK. The median gross monthly salary is approximately 25,000 to 30,000 CZK.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa as a Data Entry Clerk in Czechia?**
A: Yes, it is possible via the Employee Card, but the employer must usually pass a labour market test (showing no suitable EU/EEA candidate). The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the process can take several months. Alternatively, if the employer has a valid work permit for the position, the test may be waived.

**Q: What training is helpful to become a Data Entry Clerk in Czechia?**
A: While no formal degree is required, completing a vocational course in administration or a certificate in keyboard typing (minimum 200 strokes per minute) improves chances. Familiarity with Microsoft Excel and data management software is also beneficial. Many employers provide on-the-job training for specific systems.
