# Bicycle and Related Repairer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Bicycle and Related Repairers maintain and repair bicycles, e-bikes, and similar vehicles. In Czechia, rising cycling popularity, investment in bike lanes, and the e-bike boom drive steady demand for skilled mechanics. Repairers work in independent shops, chains, or as self-employed. Vocational training is standard, with trade certification often required for licensed operation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship, leading to journey-level repairer. Experienced mechanics can advance to shop manager, service specialist for e-bikes, or start their own business. Continuous learning on new technologies (e.g., electric drives) is key. Some move into related fields like sports retail or technical instruction.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic software to automatically identify common faults (e.g., circuit issues)
- Handle customer appointments and simple inquiries via chatbot
- Inventory management and parts ordering automated by AI system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of complex electrical faults (e.g., electric bicycle battery management systems)
- AR glasses overlay repair steps and exploded parts diagrams to improve repair accuracy
- Customer needs analysis: AI recommends personalised upgrade options (e.g. performance modifications)
- Remote expert collaboration: obtaining real-time support via AI platforms

**Human moat:**

- Manual fine-tuning (such as wheel truing, suspension adjustment)
- Assess unusual damage and creatively repair
- Face-to-face communication with clients to understand cycling preferences and customize solutions
- Handling replacement and adaptation of old or non-standard parts

**Skills to build:**

- Diagnosis and repair of electric bicycle electrical systems
- Proficient in using digital diagnostic tools and repair apps
- 3D printing part production and repair
- Customer relationship management and personalized service design
- Basic programming skills (e.g., MES system operation in repair stations)
- Sustainable repair concepts (e.g., battery recycling and upgrades)

**Key growth areas:**

- E-bikes
- Urban cycling infrastructure
- Bicycle tourism
- Repair services

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence a bicycle repairer's salary in Czechia?**
A: Salary depends on experience, location, and employer type. Self-employed repairers may have variable income. Official wage statistics are published by ČSÚ and ISPV; typical starting salaries are below national average, but experienced e-bike specialists can earn higher.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work visa as a bicycle repairer in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card if the job is on the list of occupations in shortage (not currently for bicycle repairers) or if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. Requirements include a job offer, relevant qualifications, and meeting income threshold. The process is restrictive.
