# Concrete Placer and Finisher — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7114 (ISCO08)**

Concrete placers and finishers in Czechia work on construction sites, preparing, placing, and finishing concrete for structures such as buildings, bridges, and roads. The construction sector is a major employer, with strong demand in residential and infrastructure projects. Mobility within the EU/EEA is free for this occupation, but non-EU workers typically require an Employee Card. Although not on the official shortage list, the occupation remains in steady demand due to ongoing construction activity.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Concrete placers often start as helpers and advance to skilled finishers, then to foremen or site supervisors. Further training can lead to specialization in decorative concrete or restoration. Some move into self-employment as subcontractors. The Czech construction industry offers pathways through vocational certificates (výuční list) and professional courses. Progression may require language skills and certification in new techniques.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- infrastructure projects
- residential construction
- industrial flooring
- prefabrication adaptation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for a concrete placer in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer. Generally, they are in line with average construction wages in the country.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a concrete placer in Czechia?**
A: Yes, through an Employee Card, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so a labor market test may apply.

**Q: What training is needed to become a concrete placer?**
A: Common routes include a 3-year vocational high school apprenticeship (výuční list) or short training courses. Certification is not mandatory but can improve job opportunities.
