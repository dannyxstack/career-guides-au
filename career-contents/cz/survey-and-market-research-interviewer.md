# Survey and Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and Market Research Interviewers in Czechia collect data through face-to-face, telephone, or online interviews for opinion polls, market research, and social surveys. They work for specialized agencies, media companies, or public institutions, following structured questionnaires to ensure consistency. The role requires strong communication skills, patience, and the ability to handle rejection. Employment is often part-time or project-based, with flexible hours. While digitalization has shifted some interviews online, telephone and face-to-face methods remain important. The occupation is entry-level and accessible to individuals with secondary education, offering a stepping stone to roles in research, sales, or customer service.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression typically starts with data collection, then moves to team leader, quality control, or supervisor roles. Experienced interviewers may advance to research executive, analyst, or account manager positions within research agencies. Some transition into sales, customer service, or public relations. Additional training in survey design, statistics, or market research can enhance prospects. The field offers a clear path for those with strong communication and organizational skills. Continuous learning and adaptability to new technologies like CATI and online panels are valuable.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Online surveys
- Telephone interviewing
- Market research
- Data collection

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for Survey and Market Research Interviewers in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary widely; typically hourly or per-interview payment, often around minimum wage or slightly higher for experienced interviewers. Full-time equivalents may earn CZK 300,000 to 450,000 annually pre-tax.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a visa to work as a survey interviewer in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card if they have a job offer. However, the position must meet the required skill and salary thresholds. Due to the low skill level and variable income, it may be challenging.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: Experienced interviewers can become team leaders, quality controllers, or move into research analysis and market research management. Additional training in survey methodology or data analysis can accelerate progression.
