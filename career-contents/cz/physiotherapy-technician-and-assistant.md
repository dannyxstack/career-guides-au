# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 3255 (ISCO08)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Czechia work under physiotherapists to deliver rehabilitation and physical therapy services, with duties including preparing equipment, guiding patients through exercises, and monitoring progress. The role is vital in both public hospitals and private clinics, supported by a growing elderly population and rising demand for post-surgery and injury rehabilitation. Labour-market demand is strong, with shortages reported in many regions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins with a secondary vocational diploma (3 years) and registration. Many assistants later pursue a bachelor's degree in physiotherapy to become fully qualified physiotherapists, significantly increasing earning potential and clinical autonomy. Specialization in areas like sports therapy, geriatrics, or neurological rehabilitation is common. With experience, some move into management roles in rehabilitation departments or open private practices.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Patient treatment progress recording and report writing
- Basic vital signs monitoring and data entry
- Setup and calibration of routine treatment equipment
- Appointment scheduling and patient file management.
- Simple Rehabilitation Exercise Guidance (Standardized Movements)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans
- Balance and coordination training assisted by virtual reality (VR)
- Use wearable devices to monitor patient progress in real time and adjust intensity
- AI-assisted generation of treatment plan documents and patient education materials
- Smart voice assistant for patient communication and follow-up

**Human moat:**

- Manual therapy techniques (e.g., massage, joint mobilization)
- Empathy to assess patient mental state and pain
- Clinical judgment in adjusting treatment based on nonverbal cues
- Build trust with patients and motivate them to adhere to rehabilitation
- Handling emergencies (e.g., patient falls or discomfort)

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and use of AI tools
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) system operation.
- Virtual reality rehabilitation technology applications
- Patient communication and behavior change coaching
- Wearable device data interpretation.
- Basic programming (e.g., Python scripts for data processing)

**Key growth areas:**

- aging population
- rehabilitation services
- preventive care
- private physiotherapy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is a typical salary for a physiotherapy assistant in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level positions start around 25,000 CZK monthly, while experienced assistants in private clinics can earn up to 40,000 CZK. The average is roughly 30,000–35,000 CZK before tax.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a physiotherapy assistant in Czechia?**
A: Yes, via an Employee Card. You need a valid job offer from a Czech employer and must meet qualification requirements. The occupation is often considered a shortage field, which may expedite the process.

**Q: Can I become a physiotherapist after working as an assistant?**
A: Yes. You can pursue a bachelor's degree in physiotherapy, which typically takes 3 years of full-time study. Previous assistant experience is beneficial for admission and studies.
