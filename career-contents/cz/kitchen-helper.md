# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers in Czechia assist in food preparation, cleaning, and basic kitchen tasks under the supervision of chefs. They work in restaurants, hotels, canteens, and fast-food outlets. The role is entry-level, often filled by students, migrants, or those seeking immediate employment. Demand is steady due to high turnover and growth in the hospitality and tourism sectors, particularly in Prague and regional tourist hubs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited: kitchen helpers typically advance to commis chef or line cook after gaining experience and formal training (e.g., culinary school). Some move into supervisory roles in large kitchens or switch to related fields like catering management. Without additional qualifications, most remain in entry-level positions. The occupation is often a stepping stone for migrants to enter the Czech labour market and later transition to higher-skilled roles.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Czechia?**
A: Salaries range from 18,000 to 25,000 CZK per month gross, depending on location and employer. In Prague, it may be slightly higher, but still below the national average.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa for a kitchen helper job?**
A: Yes, through the Employee Card. However, the occupation is not on the shortage list, and employers must prove they could not fill the post locally. Approval is possible but not guaranteed.

**Q: Can I become a chef starting as a kitchen helper?**
A: Yes, with experience and additional training (e.g., apprenticeship or culinary school), advancement to commis chef or cook is possible, but it requires time and dedication.
