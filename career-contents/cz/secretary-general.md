# Secretary (General) — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 4120 (ISCO08)**

Secretaries (general) perform administrative and clerical duties, such as managing correspondence, scheduling appointments, and organizing files, within various organizations across Czechia. They are essential for ensuring smooth office operations in both private and public sectors. The role is common in all industries, with a steady demand in business services, government, and healthcare.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically moves from junior secretary to senior secretary, executive assistant, or office manager. Specialization in legal, medical, or technical fields can lead to higher pay. Continuous learning of software (MS Office, ERP systems) and language skills is key. Some transition to HR, accounting, or project coordination roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated meeting schedule coordination and conflict detection
- Call screening and standard response generation
- Document Formatting, Spell Checking, and Template Application
- Preliminary review of travel bookings and expense reimbursements
- Simple email classification and auto-reply

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing executive time allocation through AI schedule analysis
- Improving meeting minutes efficiency using voice transcription and summarization tools
- Generate administrative reports and insights using data analysis tools
- Using AI customer management system to enhance external contact management
- Automating workflow design to improve overall team efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Judgment and confidentiality skills when handling highly sensitive or confidential information
- Interpersonal coordination and decision-making in ambiguous or urgent situations
- Emotional intelligence and relationship management in cross-departmental communication
- Understanding and navigating informal power structures within an organization.
- Administrative tasks requiring creativity or personalized handling

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced office suite automation and scripting (e.g., VBA, Power Automate).
- Hands-on AI tools: meeting scheduling AI, speech-to-text, CRM system
- Data analysis basics (advanced Excel functions, BI tools)
- Project management basics (e.g., Agile methodology)
- Advanced communication and emotional intelligence (conflict resolution, executive support)
- Digital security and privacy protection awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- Administrative support
- Digitalization
- Multilingual roles
- Remote work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a general secretary in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary, but the typical annual gross salary ranges from 450,000 to 600,000 CZK depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions start lower, while senior roles in Prague can exceed 700,000 CZK.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a secretary in Czechia via an Employee Card?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5x the minimum wage, around 300,000 CZK annually) and your qualifications are recognized. However, general secretary roles are not considered highly skilled, so approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What languages are most useful for secretaries in Czechia?**
A: Czech is essential for most roles. English is widely expected in international companies, and German is valuable due to Czechia's trade ties with Germany.
