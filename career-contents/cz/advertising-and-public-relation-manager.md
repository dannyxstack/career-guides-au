# Advertising and Public Relations Manager — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 1222 (ISCO08)**

Advertising and Public Relations Managers in Czechia oversee marketing strategies, brand communication, and public image for companies, agencies, and public institutions. They develop campaigns, manage budgets, coordinate teams, and build media relations. With Czechia's integration into global markets and the rise of digital channels, demand for skilled managers is growing, especially in Prague and other regional hubs. Fluency in Czech and English is typically required, along with a strong understanding of local media landscape and consumer behavior.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★½).**

Career progression often starts from marketing specialist or account executive roles, advancing to manager, senior manager, and eventually director or head of department. Many professionals obtain degrees in marketing, communications, or business, and supplement with certifications like Google Analytics or HubSpot. Lateral moves into digital marketing, social media management, or brand strategy are common. In larger agencies or corporations, there are clear paths to leadership positions such as Chief Marketing Officer (CMO). Networking and a proven track record of successful campaigns are key for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate standardized press releases and social media posts
- Analyze advertising performance data and generate reports
- Monitoring media mentions and public sentiment, automatically compiling briefings
- Automate bidding and budget allocation based on historical data
- Create and test basic A/B versions of ad copy

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to predict consumer behavior and optimize marketing strategies
- Use AI tools for more precise audience segmentation and personalized targeting
- Analyze public opinion in real time through AI to quickly respond to crisis PR
- Using AI to assist creative ideation, quickly generating multiple versions of creative plans
- Using AI to automatically track competitor activities to support strategic decisions

**Human moat:**

- Senior-level client relationship and stakeholder management
- Long-term brand strategy formulation and creative direction control
- Cross-departmental coordination and team leadership
- Handle complex and sensitive public relations crises
- Innovative planning based on intuition and experience

**Skills to build:**

- Master mainstream AI marketing tools (e.g., Jasper, Crimson Hexagon)
- Data analysis and data-driven decision-making ability
- Prompt Engineering and Large Language Model Applications
- Automated operations of digital platform advertising (e.g., Google Ads Smart Bidding)
- Use of sentiment analysis tools (e.g., Brandwatch)
- Cross-cultural communication and strategic thinking

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital marketing
- Social media
- Data-driven advertising
- Brand strategy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence salary for Advertising and PR Managers in Czechia?**
A: Salary levels vary based on experience, company size, industry, and location. Managers in Prague typically earn more than in other regions. Additional factors include specific expertise (e.g., digital marketing, crisis PR), language skills, and the scope of responsibilities. Bonuses and benefits are common in agency and corporate settings.

**Q: Do I need a job offer before applying for a work visa in Czechia?**
A: Yes, for non-EU nationals, a confirmed job offer is essential for the Employee Card or EU Blue Card. The employer must also demonstrate that the position could not be filled by a Czech/EU citizen. EU/EEA citizens do not need a visa or work permit.

**Q: What is the typical career progression for this role?**
A: Start as a marketing specialist or account executive, then advance to manager, senior manager, and possibly director or head of department. Further progression can lead to Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) or agency partner. Continuous learning in digital tools and networking are important.
