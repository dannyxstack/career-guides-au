# Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7231 (ISCO08)**

Motor vehicle mechanics and repairers maintain, diagnose, and repair cars, vans, and light trucks. In Czechia, a robust automotive industry and high car ownership drive steady demand. Work ranges from routine servicing to complex electronic diagnostics. Employers include independent garages, dealerships, and fleet operators. The field is strongly practical, requiring manual dexterity and technical knowledge.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational school, leading to journeyman status. Experienced mechanics can become master technicians, workshop foremen, or open their own garages. Specialization in brands (e.g., Škoda, Volkswagen) or areas (e.g., electric vehicles, air conditioning) boosts earning potential. Further training in electronics and hybrid systems is increasingly important.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults
- Fine disassembly and calibration requiring tactile feedback
- Comprehensive vehicle condition assessment and repair plan decision-making
- Customer trust communication and personalized service.

**Skills to build:**

- Mastery of AI diagnostic tools (e.g., Dealer-Fix, TEXA IDC5)
- Learning hybrid/electric vehicle high-voltage system repair
- Familiar with in-vehicle network diagnostics and software update processes
- Practice using AR-assisted maintenance equipment
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn the basics of data analysis and predictive maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric Vehicle Maintenance
- Diagnostics & Electronics
- Air Conditioning Systems
- Fleet Maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can a non-EU motor vehicle mechanic get a work visa for Czechia?**
A: Yes, via the Employee Card if you have a job offer and recognized qualifications. The trade is on the shortage list, which may expedite processing.

**Q: What is the typical salary range for motor vehicle mechanics in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region, but entry-level mechanics earn around 300,000–400,000 CZK annually, while experienced specialists can earn 500,000–600,000 CZK.

**Q: How can I advance as a mechanic in Czechia?**
A: Specialize in electric vehicles or diagnostics, obtain master mechanic certification, or open your own workshop. Further education in management or technical studies also helps.
