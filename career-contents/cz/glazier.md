# Glaziers — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers in Czechia install, repair, and replace glass in windows, doors, facades, and other structures, working on both residential and commercial projects. The occupation is essential in the construction sector, particularly in new building and renovation works. Demand is stable due to ongoing construction activity and energy-efficiency upgrades requiring modern glazing systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, moving to fully qualified glazier after vocational training. Experienced glaziers can become team leaders or specialize in decorative glass, stained glass, or high-tech installations. Self-employment is common for those with a trade license. Advanced roles include project management in glazing companies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-efficient glazing
- Glass facades
- Building renovation
- Solar panel integration

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for glaziers in Czechia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for glaziers in Czechia is around 35,000–45,000 CZK (420,000–540,000 CZK per year), depending on experience, location, and employer. Self-employed glaziers may earn more but have variable income.

**Q: Can a non-EU glazier get a visa to work in Czechia?**
A: Yes, non-EU glaziers can apply for an Employee Card if they have a job offer and meet education/experience requirements (usually a vocational certificate). A labor market test is required. The position is not on the shortage list, so no special fast track.

**Q: How long does it take to become a qualified glazier?**
A: Typically 3 years of vocational training (apprenticeship) leading to a certificate. Alternatively, on-the-job training with a certified employer can qualify after a few years. A trade license is needed for self-employment.
