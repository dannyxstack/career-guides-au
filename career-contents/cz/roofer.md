# Roofer — career analysis · Czechia

**Occupation code: 7121 (ISCO08)**

Roofers install, repair, and maintain roofs on residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. In Czechia, this trade is essential given the country's active construction sector and aging building stock. Roofers work with various materials including tiles, metal, and flat roofing, and increasingly apply green roofs and solar panels. The occupation demands physical fitness, comfort at heights, and precision. With many experienced roofers nearing retirement, demand for new entrants is high, offering stable employment and opportunities for self-employment.

Job opportunities are expected to grow, especially for those trained in modern techniques such as green roofing and photovoltaic integration. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, leading to a certified roofer. Experienced roofers can advance to supervisory roles, such as foreman or project manager, or specialize in areas like waterproofing, historic restoration, or solar installation. Many roofers eventually become self-employed, subcontracting for construction firms. Continuous skill development through courses in new materials and safety regulations is common and enhances career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic calculation and estimation of roof area and material quantities.
- Automated report generation for safety inspection records and compliance checks
- Robot assistance for standard tile laying (e.g., drone pre-laying)
- AI chatbot handling of customer inquiries and quotes

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI image recognition detects roof damage and assists in diagnosing leak points.
- Drones + AI for 3D roof scanning to generate precise construction drawings
- AR glasses provide step-by-step construction guidance, improving installation efficiency for complex structures
- Smart project management software optimizes material procurement and scheduling
- AI weather prediction system assists in scheduling high-risk outdoor work time

**Human moat:**

- Custom handmade and on-site adjustments for complex waterproofing systems.
- Experience in repairing old-style roofs and aesthetic judgment
- Physical strength and risk management for working at heights
- Interpersonal trust in communicating customized solutions with clients
- Skilled mixed construction with multiple materials (tiles, metal, membrane)

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using drones and 3D scanners for roof surveying
- Master basic BIM (Building Information Modeling) operations and reading
- Learn operation and maintenance of AR-assisted construction equipment
- Basic data analysis and smart device troubleshooting
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Learn the construction techniques of new waterproof materials (e.g., PVC, TPO).

**Key growth areas:**

- green roofing
- solar panel installation
- renovation
- waterproofing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a roofer in Czechia?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience but are typically above the national average for trades due to high demand. Exact figures can be found in the ISPV wage survey; entry-level roofers earn less, while experienced specialists can command premium rates.

**Q: Can non-EU roofers work in Czechia?**
A: Yes, roofers are on the Czech shortage list, which streamlines the Employee Card process. A valid job offer and formal qualifications (or recognition thereof) are required. Retraining courses may help meet qualification standards.

**Q: Do I need to have my foreign roofer qualification recognized in Czechia?**
A: Yes, non-EU qualifications must be recognized or supplemented. The Czech system may require passing a retraining course or exam to obtain local certification, especially for trade licensing.
