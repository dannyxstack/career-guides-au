## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by region and establishment type. On average, a full-time supervisor earns between €2,300 and €3,200 gross per month. Hotels in cities like Vienna or Salzburg may offer higher pay. Collective agreements apply in hospitality, often including overtime and shift bonuses.

**Q: Can foreign workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation in Austria?**
A: Yes. For non-EU nationals, the Red-White-Red Card is the primary route for skilled workers with relevant qualifications or experience. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold (€3,900 gross/month in 2023), which is uncommon for this role. Alternatively, a job offer under the Seasonal Employment Regulation may apply for temporary positions.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Austria?**
A: Supervisors coordinate cleaning staff, inspect work quality, manage inventory of supplies, and ensure hygiene standards. They often train new employees, create schedules, and handle guest complaints in hotels. In offices, they may oversee outsourced cleaning teams and report to facility managers.
