## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dancers and choreographers in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, employer, and project. In Vienna, entry-level dancers earn around €1,800–€2,500 gross per month, while experienced choreographers can earn €3,000–€5,000 or more. State-funded theater positions follow collective agreements, with minimum roughly €2,200 monthly. Freelancers often negotiate per project.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign dancers and choreographers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with salary above threshold, or the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers with relevant qualifications. For self-employed artists, a residence permit for self-employed key workers may be available. All require proof of sufficient income and health insurance.

**Q: What employment opportunities exist for dancers and choreographers in Austria?**
A: Austria offers opportunities in state and municipal theaters (e.g., Vienna State Opera, Volksoper), independent dance companies, and commercial productions. Choreographers may work with ballet schools or as freelancers. Vienna and Salzburg are key hubs. Recognition by the Austrian Artists' Social Insurance Fund (KSV) can facilitate access to social benefits and networks.
