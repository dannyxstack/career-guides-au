## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for police inspectors and detectives in Austria?**
A: Salaries for police inspectors and detectives in Austria are based on a public service pay scale. For a starting police inspector, monthly gross salary is approximately €2,700 to €3,200, rising with experience to over €4,500. Detectives may receive additional allowances depending on their specialization and rank.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as police inspectors or detectives in Austria via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No. Police inspector and detective positions in Austria are civil service roles that require Austrian, EU/EEA, or Swiss citizenship under the Security Police Act. The EU Blue Card is not a viable route as these jobs do not qualify as highly skilled migrant occupations for non-EU citizens. Exceptions are extremely rare.

**Q: How does one advance to become a police inspector or detective in Austria?**
A: Candidates typically start as police officers after completing training at the Security Academy (SIAK). Promotion to inspector requires passing exams and accumulating service years. Detective roles are specialist positions often attained through additional training and selection. Career progression is structured within the federal police force.
