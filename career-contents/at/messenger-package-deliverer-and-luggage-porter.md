## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers, package deliverers, and luggage porters in Austria?**
A: As of 2023, collective agreements in the transport sector set a minimum monthly gross salary of around €1,800 for full-time work. Actual earnings vary by employer, location, and tips (especially for luggage porters). Experienced deliverers or those with specialized services (e.g., express couriers) may earn up to €2,500–€2,800 gross per month, with overtime and bonuses possible.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for messengers and package deliverers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically require a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers, but messengers and package deliverers are rarely on the shortage occupation list. The EU Blue Card is not feasible as it demands a university degree or high salary. Most workers in this field come from EU/EEA countries via free movement. For non-EU nationals, a specific work permit may be possible only if the job is deemed critical, which is very restrictive.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for messengers and package deliverers in Austria?**
A: Full-time employees usually work 38.5–40 hours per week, often in shifts including weekends and holidays. Luggage porters at train stations or airports may have irregular hours. Workers benefit from collective agreements ensuring minimum wage, paid leave, and social insurance. Many couriers work as independent contractors, especially bike messengers, which offers flexibility but less job security.
