## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Austria?**
A: Salaries for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Austria vary by experience and company size. The average gross monthly salary ranges from €4,500 to €6,500, with senior roles earning up to €8,000. Collective bargaining agreements and location (e.g., Vienna vs. rural areas) also influence pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers to work in Austria?**
A: Eligible managers can apply for the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers or the EU Blue Card, both requiring a valid job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds. The Red-White-Red Card also considers qualifications and work experience. Authorities process applications through Austrian embassies or the Public Employment Service (AMS).

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for Supply, Distribution and Related Manager positions in Austria?**
A: Employers usually seek a university degree in logistics, business administration, or related fields, combined with several years of experience in supply chain management. Professional certifications (e.g., CILT, APICS) and fluency in German are advantageous. Some roles accept equivalent vocational training with proven expertise.
