## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bank tellers in Austria?**
A: In Austria, bank tellers earn between €2,000 and €2,800 gross per month, depending on experience and region. Collective bargaining agreements in the banking sector set minimum salaries, with entry-level positions starting around €2,000 and experienced tellers reaching up to €3,000.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as bank tellers in Austria via a work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit such as the Red-White-Red Card, but bank teller roles may not qualify as a skilled occupation unless the applicant has specialized qualifications or the employer demonstrates a labor shortage. EU/EEA citizens have unrestricted access.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for bank tellers in Austria?**
A: Bank tellers in Austria can advance to customer advisor, account manager, or specialist roles in loans, investments, or risk management. Banks often support professional development through internal training and certifications, enabling progression to higher-level positions with increased responsibility and pay.
