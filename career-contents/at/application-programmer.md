## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for applications programmers in Austria?**
A: According to Austrian collective agreements, entry-level applications programmers earn around €2,500–€3,200 gross monthly, while experienced programmers can earn €3,800–€5,500+ depending on industry and location. Salaries are often paid 14 times a year (including holiday and Christmas bonuses). Vienna and tech sectors pay higher.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU applications programmers in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a binding job offer with a minimum annual salary (€53,514 in 2024, or less for shortage occupations), a university degree or 5 years relevant experience, and a valid passport. Another route is the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers, which has specific points-based criteria.

**Q: What is the career outlook for applications programmers in Austria?**
A: Demand for applications programmers remains strong due to digital transformation across industries like IT consulting, banking, and manufacturing. Career progression can lead to senior developer, architect, or project management roles. While many companies use English, proficiency in German is often required for senior positions. Vienna is a major tech hub with abundant opportunities.
