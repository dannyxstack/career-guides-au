## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for spray painters and varnishers in Austria?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for spray painters and varnishers in Austria ranges from €2,200 to €3,000, depending on experience, location, and industry. Entry-level positions start around €1,900, while experienced professionals in sectors like automotive or industrial finishing can earn up to €3,500. Salaries are typically negotiated based on collective agreements.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for non-EU spray painters to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU spray painters can apply for the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers with a job offer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for those with a university degree or five years of relevant experience, meeting minimum salary thresholds. Both require proof of qualifications, employer sponsorship, and compliance with labor market tests.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a spray painter in Austria?**
A: Austria typically requires a completed apprenticeship (Lehre) in painting or surface technology, or a comparable vocational qualification. For non-EU workers, formal recognition of foreign credentials through the AST (Austrian Recognition Office) is necessary. Additional certifications in industrial coatings or airless spraying can enhance job prospects.
