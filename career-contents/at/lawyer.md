## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for lawyers in Austria?**
A: In Austria, entry-level lawyers earn around €40,000–€55,000 annually. Mid-career lawyers with several years of experience can earn €60,000–€90,000, while partners in law firms or senior in-house counsel may exceed €120,000. Salaries vary by location (Vienna higher), firm size, and specialization. Public sector lawyers earn according to collective agreements.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign lawyers to work in Austria?**
A: Foreign lawyers can work in Austria via the Red-White-Red Card for highly skilled workers (points-based), the EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least €53,278 in 2025), or the company-specific short-term visa. All routes require recognized qualifications and a valid employment contract. The Austrian Bar Association may also require language proficiency and professional equivalence exams.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice law in Austria?**
A: To practice as a lawyer (Rechtsanwalt) in Austria, you must complete a law degree (Magister iuris or equivalent), a five-year court practice (Gerichtspraxis), a three-year traineeship at a law firm, and pass the bar exam. Additionally, registration with the Austrian Bar Association (ÖRAK) is mandatory. Foreign qualifications may require recognition and additional exams.
