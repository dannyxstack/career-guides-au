## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a non-commissioned armed forces officer in Austria?**
A: In Austria, non-commissioned officers (NCOs) in the Bundesheer earn a base salary ranging from approximately €2,200 to €3,500 gross per month, depending on rank, years of service, and special allowances. Additional benefits include housing, food, and family allowances. Salaries are set by collective agreement for public sector employees.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a non-commissioned armed forces officer in Austria through the EU Blue Card?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and a high salary. More importantly, non-commissioned officer positions in the Austrian Armed Forces are generally restricted to Austrian citizens due to national security requirements. Foreign nationals, including EU citizens, are not eligible for regular military service. No work visa or Blue Card route exists for this occupation.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for non-commissioned armed forces officers in Austria?**
A: NCOs in the Austrian Armed Forces can advance through ranks from Unteroffizier to higher non-commissioned officer grades such as Wachtmeister, Oberwachtmeister, and Stabsunteroffizier. Promotion depends on performance, further training, and time in service. Officers may also transition to civilian roles or specialize in technical fields. The military offers various professional development courses.
