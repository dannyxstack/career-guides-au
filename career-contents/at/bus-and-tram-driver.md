## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Austria?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for bus and tram drivers in Austria ranges from €2,200 to €3,000, depending on experience, region, and employer. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimums, with additional pay for night shifts, overtime, and weekends. Some public transport companies offer annual bonuses and benefits.

**Q: What work visa options are available for non-EU bus and tram drivers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a Red-White-Red Card under skilled worker categories if they meet criteria such as a job offer in a shortage occupation (bus/tram driving is listed). Alternatively, the EU Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold. Both allow family reunification. Applicants need recognized qualifications or equivalent experience.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Austria?**
A: To drive buses, you need a D driving license (bus) and a vocational training certificate (Berufsausbildungsnachweis). For trams, a special tram driving license is required, obtained after a training course and exam. Employers often provide training. A clean driving record and health check are mandatory. Some knowledge of German is typically required.
