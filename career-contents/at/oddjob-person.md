## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for odd-job persons in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on the type of work and employer. For unskilled or casual labor, hourly wages often range from €9 to €12 per hour, with monthly full-time equivalents around €1,600 to €2,000 gross. Collective bargaining agreements may set minimum rates, and actual pay can be higher in sectors like construction or hospitality.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as odd-job persons in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent experience in a high-demand profession. Odd-job positions typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU workers may instead qualify for a Red-White-Red Card if the job is in a shortage occupation, or for a temporary work permit for seasonal or specific labor.

**Q: What are common types of odd jobs available in Austria?**
A: Odd-job positions include cleaning, gardening, moving assistance, minor repairs, courier services, and seasonal work in tourism or agriculture. Many are part-time or freelance. Platforms like willhaben.at or local job agencies list such opportunities. Work conditions often require flexibility, and knowledge of German is helpful but not always mandatory.
