## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for ICT Sales Professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries for ICT sales professionals in Austria vary by experience and region. Entry-level roles typically earn €40,000–€55,000 gross annually, while senior professionals can earn €70,000–€100,000+. Commission and bonuses are common. Official salary data from sources like the AMS or Gehaltskompass provide more precise figures.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU ICT Sales Professionals to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU ICT sales professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card with a job offer and a university degree, meeting the salary threshold (€46,575 for shortage occupations in 2024, or €63,282 otherwise). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for key workers requires relevant qualifications and a job offer. Both routes require employer sponsorship and meet specific criteria.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ICT Sales Professionals in Austria?**
A: ICT sales professionals in Austria can advance to senior account manager, sales manager, or business development roles. The growing tech sector in cities like Vienna and Graz offers pathways into strategic consultancy or management. Continuous learning in cloud, cybersecurity, or software sales enhances advancement prospects. Many professionals also transition into product management or sales leadership.
