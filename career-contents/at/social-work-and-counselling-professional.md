## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work and counselling professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, sector, and region. Entry-level positions in social work typically earn €30,000–€38,000 gross annually. With several years of experience, salaries can rise to €45,000–€55,000. Counselling professionals in private practice may have variable incomes, while those in public institutions follow collective agreements with standardized pay scales.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for social work and counselling professionals to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary above the threshold (€43,758 gross/year in 2024). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card is for qualified workers in shortage occupations. Social work is not on the shortage list, but counselling may be considered under other criteria. All applications are processed through the Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS) and the immigration authorities.

**Q: What are the educational and licensing requirements to practice as a social worker or counsellor in Austria?**
A: Social work requires a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Social Work (Sozialarbeit) from a recognized Austrian university. Registration with the Federal Ministry of Social Affairs is mandatory. For counselling (Psychotherapie), a Master's degree in psychotherapy and a supervised internship are needed, followed by registration in the Psychotherapy Register. EU qualifications may be recognized under professional directives.
