**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Customer needs collection and preliminary plan generation: AI chatbot can automatically gather preferences and generate activity frameworks

- Meeting schedule optimization: AI algorithms automatically optimize timetables based on agendas and objectives

- Creative themes and visual design: AI generates multiple concept inspirations, planners filter and optimise

- Data-driven review: AI automatically summarizes participant feedback and social media trends to generate improvement reports

**Human moat:**

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for conference and event planners in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level planners earn around €30,000–€35,000 gross annually, while experienced planners can earn €45,000–€55,000. Vienna generally offers higher pay due to higher living costs. Collective bargaining agreements (Kollektivvertrag) may set minimums for certain sectors.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU national to work as a conference and event planner in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations or an EU Blue Card if the salary meets the threshold. Alternatively, the Rot-Weiß-Rot Karte for key workers may apply. Requirements include a job offer, relevant qualifications, and possibly recognition of credentials.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a conference and event planner in Austria?**
A: Formal qualifications vary: a degree in event management, tourism, or business is beneficial. Many start with an apprenticeship (Lehre) in event management or tourism. Recognized courses are offered by the Austrian Chamber of Commerce (WKO) or universities of applied sciences (Fachhochschulen). Practical experience is highly valued.
