## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for office supervisors in Austria?**
A: Monthly gross salaries for office supervisors in Austria typically range from €2,800 to €3,600, depending on experience, company size, and location (e.g., Vienna pays higher). Additional benefits may include holiday pay and bonuses. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum pay scales.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for office supervisors in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as office supervisors via the Red-White-Red Card or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer with a minimum salary (€3,666 gross monthly in 2024). The Red-White-Red Card uses a points system for qualifications, experience, and language skills; office supervisor is not a shortage occupation, so applicants may need points under 'other key workers'.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an office supervisor in Austria?**
A: A completed vocational training (Lehre) in office administration or a related field, combined with several years of professional experience, is typical. Alternatively, a degree in business administration may suffice. Proficiency in German (at least B2) is usually required. Further education, such as a certified business management course, can improve career prospects.
