## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for beauticians in Austria?**
A: The salary for beauticians in Austria varies by experience and location. According to the collective agreement, the minimum gross monthly salary for full-time beauticians is around €1,800 to €2,200. Experienced professionals can earn up to €2,500 or more. Income may also include tips.

**Q: How can non-EU beauticians work in Austria under the EU Blue Card or other routes?**
A: Non-EU beauticians may obtain a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers if they have a job offer and recognized qualifications. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold (typically over €60,000 annually), which is uncommon for beauticians. Alternatively, a standard employment permit may be possible if no suitable local candidate is found.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a beautician in Austria?**
A: In Austria, beauticians must provide proof of qualification (Befähigungsnachweis) under the trade regulations. This typically involves completing an apprenticeship (Lehre) or holding a diploma from a recognized school. Foreign qualifications may need to be recognized by the Wirtschaftskammer Österreich.
