## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Austria?**
A: Salaries are regulated by collective agreements. Entry-level workers earn around €1,500 to €1,800 gross per month, with increases based on experience. Benefits often include accommodation or meals. Overtime and weekend work may be compensated additionally.

**Q: What are the visa options for working as a livestock farm labourer in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically require a Red-White-Red Card for other key workers if the occupation is listed as a shortage. The EU Blue Card is not applicable due to low salary thresholds. Seasonal work permits are available for temporary stays up to six months.

**Q: What are the main duties of a livestock farm labourer in Austria?**
A: Duties include feeding and watering animals, cleaning stables and enclosures, assisting with breeding and birthing, monitoring animal health, and maintaining farm equipment. Physical fitness and animal care skills are essential. Work is often outdoors and may involve weekends or holidays.
