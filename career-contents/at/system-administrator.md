## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a systems administrator in Austria?**
A: In Austria, systems administrators typically earn between €45,000 and €70,000 gross annually, depending on experience, qualifications, and location (Vienna tends to be higher). The IT collective agreement sets minimums, but many employers offer above-tariff pay. Senior roles can exceed €80,000.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a systems administrator in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU systems administrators can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a university degree or equivalent and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least €44,352 (2024). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers is available for those with vocational training and relevant experience.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a systems administrator in Austria?**
A: Typically, a degree in computer science or a related field is expected, though vocational training with certifications (e.g., Linux, Microsoft, Cisco) can substitute. German language skills are often required, at least B1-B2 level, especially for client-facing roles. Practical experience is highly valued.
