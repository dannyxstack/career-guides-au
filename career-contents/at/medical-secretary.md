## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a medical secretary in Austria?**
A: The average gross annual salary for a medical secretary in Austria ranges from €30,000 to €38,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €27,000, while experienced secretaries in private practices or hospitals can earn up to €42,000. Salaries are typically higher in Vienna and for those with specialized medical terminology knowledge.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign medical secretaries to work in Austria?**
A: Foreign medical secretaries can apply for a Red-White-Red Card, which requires a job offer, recognized qualifications, and German language skills at B1 level or higher. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for non-EU workers with a higher education degree and a salary meeting the threshold (€48,634 in 2024). Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Public Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a medical secretary in Austria?**
A: Typically, employers require completed vocational training as a medical secretary or a related field, such as a certificate from a medical assistant program. Strong German language skills (B2 level or higher) are essential. Additional certifications in medical terminology, billing software, or office management are advantageous. A valid work permit is required for non-EU nationals.
