## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for aircraft engine mechanics in Austria?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for aircraft engine mechanics in Austria ranges from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions start around €2,200, while senior mechanics can earn up to €5,000. Salaries are typically higher in Vienna and at major airlines or maintenance organizations.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for non-EU aircraft engine mechanics to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU aircraft engine mechanics can apply for the Red-White-Red Card, which targets qualified skilled workers in shortage occupations, including aircraft mechanics. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree or equivalent and a job offer meeting salary thresholds. Both require a valid employment contract and recognition of qualifications by Austro Control.

**Q: What certifications are needed to work as an aircraft engine mechanic in Austria?**
A: To work as an aircraft engine mechanic in Austria, you need an EASA Part-66 Aircraft Maintenance License (AML) in Category B1 (mechanical) or B2 (avionics). This requires completing an approved training program, passing theoretical and practical exams, and gaining relevant experience. Certification is overseen by Austro Control, the national aviation authority.
