## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for optometrists and ophthalmic opticians in Austria?**
A: In Austria, gross monthly salaries for optometrists and ophthalmic opticians typically range between €2,500 and €3,800, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €2,200, while experienced professionals in private practice may earn over €4,000. Collective bargaining agreements in the optical trade set minimum rates.

**Q: What visa routes allow non-EU optometrists to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can work in Austria via the Red-White-Red Card if their occupation is listed as a shortage profession or they secure a job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds. The EU Blue Card applies for highly qualified roles with higher pay. Both require qualification recognition by the Austrian Chamber of Commerce and a valid employment contract.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as an optometrist in Austria?**
A: To practice as an optometrist in Austria, you need a completed apprenticeship as an optician or a relevant bachelor's degree. EU qualifications are recognized via the Professional Qualifications Directive; for non-EU qualifications, an equivalency assessment by the Austrian Health Ministry is required. Registration with the Austrian Chamber of Pharmacists or the Chamber of Commerce is mandatory.
