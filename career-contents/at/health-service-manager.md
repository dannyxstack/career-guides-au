## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Health Services Managers in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region, but a Health Services Manager in Austria typically earns between €60,000 and €90,000 per year. Public sector positions may follow collective agreements, while private sector roles can offer higher pay, especially in Vienna. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for Health Services Managers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Health Services Managers via the Red-White-Red Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer meeting salary thresholds. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for university graduates with a job paying at least €4,581 gross monthly in 2024. Relevant education and recognition of qualifications are needed.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Health Services Manager in Austria?**
A: Typically, a university degree in healthcare management, business administration, or a related field is required. Many managers also hold a clinical background (e.g., nursing) plus a postgraduate management qualification. Professional experience in healthcare settings is highly valued. Registration with the Austrian health authorities may be necessary depending on the role.
