## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Policy Administration Professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €35,000–€45,000 gross annually. Mid-career professionals earn €50,000–€65,000, while senior roles can exceed €75,000. Public sector and large private companies often offer higher pay. Figures exclude bonuses and benefits.

**Q: What work visa routes exist for non-EU Policy Administration Professionals to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with salary meeting the threshold (€45,570 in 2025). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers requires a relevant degree and at least 5 years of experience. Both permit family reunification and lead to permanent residence.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Policy Administration Professional in Austria?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in public administration, political science, law, or economics is required. Many employers prefer a master's degree. Relevant experience in government or policy analysis is valued. Knowledge of German is often essential, especially for public-sector roles. Professional certifications in project management or public policy can enhance prospects.
