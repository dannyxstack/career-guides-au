## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for university and higher education teachers in Austria?**
A: Salaries for university teachers in Austria are governed by collective agreements (Kollektivvertrag). For assistant professors (Post-Doc), the gross annual salary starts around €55,000–€65,000, while full professors earn between €80,000 and €120,000 depending on experience and prestige. Additional bonuses may apply.

**Q: What visa routes exist for non-EU nationals to work as university teachers in Austria?**
A: Non-EU university teachers can apply for the EU Blue Card (Blaue Karte EU) if they have a job offer with a salary at least €4,502 gross monthly (2025 threshold). Alternatively, the Rot-Weiß-Rot Karte is available for highly qualified workers, including university lecturers, with a valid employment contract. Both require a recognized degree and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a university teacher in Austria?**
A: Typically, a doctoral degree (PhD) is required for assistant professor or lecturer positions. For full professorships, a habilitation (postdoctoral qualification) or equivalent research and teaching experience is expected. Knowledge of German is often mandatory for teaching, while English may suffice for research-intensive roles. EU citizenship is not required, but work authorization is needed for non-EU nationals.
