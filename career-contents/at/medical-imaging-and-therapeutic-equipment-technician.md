## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician in Austria?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around €2,800 to €3,500 gross per month, increasing with experience and specialization. In public hospitals, salaries follow collective agreements, with experienced technicians earning up to €4,500. Private clinics may offer higher pay. Overtime and on-call duties add extra compensation.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign Medical Imaging Technicians in Austria?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card or the Red-White-Red Card. Requirements include a recognized degree or equivalent qualification, a job offer in a shortage occupation, and a minimum salary (e.g., approx. €3,654 gross monthly for shortage occupations in 2024). The employer must prove labor market testing unless exempt.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Medical Imaging Technicians in Austria?**
A: Technicians can specialize in areas like MRI, CT, or radiation therapy, often through further training. Advancement to senior technician or team leader roles is possible after gaining experience. Some pursue academic paths, teaching at universities of applied sciences or moving into medical technology management.
