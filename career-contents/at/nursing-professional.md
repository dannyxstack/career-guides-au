## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for nursing professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries for nursing professionals in Austria are governed by collective agreements. Entry-level nurses earn approximately €2,200–€2,500 gross per month for a 40-hour week. Experienced nurses can earn up to €3,500 or more, depending on the sector (public hospitals often pay higher). Additional allowances for night shifts and weekends apply.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nursing professionals to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nurses can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer with a salary meeting the threshold (approx. €39,000 annually). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers is available for nurses with recognized qualifications and a job offer. Both routes require recognition of the nursing diploma and German language proficiency (at least B2).

**Q: What are the requirements for foreign-trained nurses to practice in Austria?**
A: Foreign-trained nurses must have their diploma recognized by the Federal Ministry of Social Affairs, Health, Care and Consumer Protection. They need proof of German language skills at B2 level (Goethe-Zertifikat or equivalent). Depending on the assessment, they may need to complete an adaptation period or an aptitude test. Registration with the Austrian Health and Nursing Care Register is mandatory.
