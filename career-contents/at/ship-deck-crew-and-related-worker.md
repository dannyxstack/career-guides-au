## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships’ deck crew in Austria?**
A: Salaries for deck crew in Austria vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while experienced crew may earn €2,500–€3,000. Additional allowances for sea time and overtime can increase total compensation. Pay is typically based on collective agreements in inland navigation.

**Q: What visa options are available for non-EU deck crew to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Austria as deck crew via the Red-White-Red Card (for qualified workers) or the EU Blue Card (for higher-skilled roles). Both require a job offer, recognition of qualifications, and minimum salary thresholds. Seasonal work may also be possible under the Seasonal Employment Regulation. Official routes include the Red-White-Red – Key Workers and EU Blue Card.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as deck crew in Austria?**
A: For deck crew on Austrian vessels, a valid certificate of competency (e.g., Able Seafarer Deck) is required, issued by an Austrian maritime authority. Basic safety training (STCW) and a medical fitness certificate are mandatory. Knowledge of German is often required. Relevant experience and passing a practical exam may be needed.
