## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a gardener in Austria?**
A: Gardeners (ISCO 6113) in Austria earn a gross monthly salary typically between €1,800 and €2,400, depending on experience and region. Collective bargaining agreements apply. Overtime and seasonal work can increase total pay.

**Q: What visa options are available for non-EU gardeners to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU gardeners can apply for a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations. The EU Blue Card may be an option if the job meets higher salary thresholds. Both require a job offer, recognition of qualifications, and a positive labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a gardener in Austria?**
A: Becoming a gardener typically requires completing a 2-3 year vocational training (Lehre) in horticulture. There are also apprenticeship programs. Specializations in landscaping, fruit growing, or organic gardening can enhance career prospects.
