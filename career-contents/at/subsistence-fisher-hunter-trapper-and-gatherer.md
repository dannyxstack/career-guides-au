## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence fishers, hunters, trappers and gatherers in Austria?**
A: In Austria, subsistence fishing, hunting, trapping, and gathering are not commercial occupations; they are primarily recreational or traditional. No official salary data exists as practitioners usually have other employment. Income from these activities is negligible, typically only for personal consumption.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a subsistence fisher, hunter, trapper or gatherer in Austria via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals and does not cover subsistence activities. General work permits require a job offer and labor market test proving no Austrian/EU candidate is available. Due to the non-commercial nature of these occupations, such visas are extremely unlikely. Seasonal work as a fishing or hunting guide may be possible under specific permits.

**Q: Are there any legal restrictions on subsistence fishing, hunting, trapping, or gathering in Austria?**
A: Yes, strict regulations apply. Fishing requires a license obtained after an exam. Hunting requires a license after training and an exam. Trapping is heavily restricted; most methods are illegal. Gathering wild plants is allowed for personal use, but protected species may not be taken. Always comply with local laws to avoid penalties.
