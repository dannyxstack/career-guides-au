## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Professional Services Managers in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and experience, but senior managers in professional services often earn between €60,000 and €120,000 annually. Those in top management or large firms may exceed €150,000. Bonuses and benefits are common. Collective agreements and company-specific pay scales also influence earnings.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for Professional Services Managers to work in Austria?**
A: Eligible managers can apply for the Red-White-Red Card for key workers, requiring a job offer, relevant qualifications, and salary meeting minimum thresholds (typically €3,087 gross monthly in 2024). The EU Blue Card is an alternative for highly qualified roles with a salary of at least €6,300 gross monthly. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for Professional Services Manager roles in Austria?**
A: Typically, a master’s degree in business, law, or a related field is expected, along with several years of professional experience. Industry certifications (e.g., PMP, CPA) and fluency in German or English enhance prospects. Recognition of foreign qualifications may be required via the Federal Ministry of Education, Science and Research.
