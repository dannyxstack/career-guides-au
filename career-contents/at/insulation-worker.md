## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for insulation workers in Austria?**
A: Salaries for insulation workers in Austria are typically based on collective bargaining agreements. Entry-level workers earn around €2,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €3,500 or more, depending on the region and employer. Overtime and special allowances may apply.

**Q: Can insulation workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Austria?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree, so it is not applicable for insulation workers. Instead, non-EU nationals may apply for a Red-White-Red Card, which covers skilled workers in shortage occupations, if they meet specific qualification and job offer requirements.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an insulation worker in Austria?**
A: The primary route is completing a 3-year apprenticeship (Lehre) in a related trade, such as building insulation or drywall construction. Alternatively, vocational school training or prior experience can be recognized. Certification from the Austrian Chamber of Commerce (WKO) may also be required for certain tasks.
