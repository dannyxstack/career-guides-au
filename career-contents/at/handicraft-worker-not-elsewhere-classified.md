## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for handicraft workers in Austria?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for handicraft workers (ISCO 7319) in Austria ranges from €1,800 to €2,800, depending on experience, region, and industry. Collective bargaining agreements set minimum wages, with higher pay in sectors like metalworking or artisanal trades. Overtime and bonuses may apply.

**Q: How can a non-EU handicraft worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Austria?**
A: Non-EU handicraft workers typically need a Red-White-Red Card, which requires a job offer, relevant qualifications, and meeting criteria like a minimum salary threshold (€2,520 gross/month in 2023). The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; handicraft roles may qualify if the job requires a higher education degree and meets the salary threshold (€4,594 gross/month in 2023). Both require employer sponsorship and a positive labor market test.

**Q: What training is required to work as a handicraft worker in Austria?**
A: Most handicraft workers in Austria complete a 3-4 year apprenticeship (Lehre) in a specific trade, combining on-the-job training with vocational school. Formal certification or a master craftsman (Meister) title can improve prospects. Some roles may accept on-the-job learning, but recognized qualifications are essential for work permits.
