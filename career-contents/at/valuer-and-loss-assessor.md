## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Valuers and Loss Assessors in Austria?**
A: Salaries for Valuers and Loss Assessors in Austria generally range from €35,000 to €55,000 gross annually, depending on experience, qualifications, and the employer. Entry-level positions start lower, while senior roles or those in specialized fields such as real estate can exceed this range. Actual figures vary by region and company.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Valuers and Loss Assessors in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Austria if they have a university degree or equivalent qualification and a job offer with a salary meeting the annual threshold (€45,404 in 2024; or €36,323 for shortage occupations). The occupation must be recognized as highly qualified. Work experience may substitute formal education in some cases.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Valuer or Loss Assessor in Austria?**
A: Typically, a relevant degree (e.g., in economics, real estate, or engineering) or vocational training is needed. For regulated fields like real estate valuation, membership in a professional body (e.g., Austrian Real Estate Chamber) or additional certification may be required. Loss assessors often have insurance industry experience. Continuing education is common.
