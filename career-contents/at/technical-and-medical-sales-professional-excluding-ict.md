## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Technical or Medical Sales Professional in Austria?**
A: Salaries in Austria for technical and medical sales roles typically range from €45,000 to €75,000 gross per year, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Commission and bonuses are often additional. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior roles can exceed €90,000.

**Q: What are the work visa options for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary above the threshold (€45,945 in 2024) and a recognized degree. Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations may apply. Both require a specific job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Austria?**
A: Professionals can advance to senior sales roles, key account management, or sales management. Specialization in high-tech or medical devices often leads to roles like product manager or regional sales director. Continuous training in new technologies and languages (e.g., German) enhances prospects.
