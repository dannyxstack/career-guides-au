## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other cleaning workers in Austria?**
A: The salary for cleaning workers in Austria varies by region and employer. As of 2024, the statutory minimum wage for full-time employment is €2,000 gross per month. However, part-time work is common, and actual pay often ranges from €1,500 to €2,200 gross per month, depending on experience and collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: Can I work as a cleaning worker in Austria with an EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree or equivalent, typically for roles with a gross annual salary above €48,000 (2024). Cleaning work does not qualify. Third-country nationals may instead apply for a Red-White-Red Card for key workers, but cleaning is not listed as a shortage occupation. A regular employment permit with a labor market test is another option.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for cleaning workers in Austria?**
A: With experience, cleaning workers can advance to team leader or supervisor roles, managing schedules and quality control. Additional certifications in specialized cleaning (e.g., industrial or hospital hygiene) can lead to higher pay. Some workers become self-employed cleaning contractors or move into facility management, though formal training in related fields is beneficial.
