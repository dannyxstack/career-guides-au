## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fumigator or pest controller in Austria?**
A: Salaries in Austria vary by region and experience. According to collective agreements, entry-level positions in this field often start around €1,900 to €2,300 gross per month. With experience, earnings can rise to €2,800 or more. Some employers offer bonuses or overtime pay. Actual wages depend on the employer and specific job duties.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a fumigator in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is typically for highly qualified workers, and this occupation usually does not meet the salary threshold or skill level required. Non-EU citizens may instead apply for a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations, provided they have relevant qualifications and a job offer. Other routes include the Rot-Weiß-Rot – Karte for key workers.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pest controller in Austria?**
A: There is no formal compulsory education, but employers often require completion of an apprenticeship (Lehre) in a related field, such as pest control technician (Schädlingsbekämpfer/in). This lasts 3 years and combines practical training with vocational school. Alternatively, relevant experience and a certificate from the Austrian Chamber of Labour can suffice. A driving license is often required for field work.
