## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a customs and border inspector in Austria?**
A: Salaries for customs and border inspectors in Austria follow the public sector pay scale. Starting gross monthly salary is approximately €2,300 to €2,800, increasing with seniority and rank. Additional allowances for shift work or special assignments may apply. After several years, experienced inspectors can earn up to €3,500 or more, depending on classification.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a customs and border inspector in Austria through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Customs and border inspector positions in Austria are civil service roles that typically require Austrian or EU citizenship. Non-EU nationals are generally not eligible. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers but does not cover this occupation. For other border-related roles, the Red-White-Red Card may be an option, but standard customs inspector jobs remain restricted to citizens.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a customs and border inspector in Austria?**
A: To become a customs and border inspector in Austria, you need Austrian citizenship, a clean criminal record, and at least a Matura (secondary school leaving certificate) or equivalent. You must pass a competitive entry exam and complete a multi-month training program at the customs school (Zollschule). Fluency in German is mandatory. Physical fitness and good health are also required.
