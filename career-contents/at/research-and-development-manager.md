## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Research and Development Managers in Austria?**
A: The average annual gross salary for Research and Development Managers in Austria ranges from €70,000 to €100,000, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior roles in sectors like pharmaceuticals or automotive can exceed €120,000.

**Q: What are the work visa options for Research and Development Managers moving to Austria?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a recognized university degree and a job offer with a minimum gross annual salary of €66,231 (2024). Alternatively, a Red-White-Red Card is available for skilled workers meeting specific criteria, including a salary threshold and relevant qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Research and Development Manager in Austria?**
A: Typically, a master's or PhD in a relevant technical or scientific field is required, along with several years of R&D experience. Leadership skills and fluency in German are often expected, especially for roles involving team management and collaboration with local stakeholders.
