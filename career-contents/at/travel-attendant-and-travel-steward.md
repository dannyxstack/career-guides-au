## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for travel attendants in Austria?**
A: Salaries for travel attendants in Austria vary by airline and experience. Entry-level attendants earn around €1,800–€2,300 gross per month, while senior staff may earn €2,500–€3,500. Additional allowances for layovers and flight hours can increase total compensation. Collective agreements apply in many airlines.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU travel attendants to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU travel attendants can work in Austria via the EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree or equivalent and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (€43,758 in 2023). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations is possible, though travel attendant is not listed as a shortage occupation, so the Blue Card is more common.

**Q: What are the main duties of a travel attendant in Austria?**
A: Travel attendants ensure passenger safety and comfort on flights, trains, or ships. Duties include conducting safety demonstrations, serving meals, managing emergencies, and assisting passengers with special needs. In Austria, they must also comply with EU aviation regulations and often work for Austrian Airlines or other carriers based at Vienna Airport.
