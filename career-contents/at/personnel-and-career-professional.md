## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for personnel and careers professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and organisation size. Entry-level roles start around €35,000–€40,000 gross annually. Mid-career professionals earn €50,000–€65,000, while senior managers can exceed €80,000. Collective bargaining agreements may set minimums. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as personnel professionals in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. You need a university degree or equivalent and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (€43,976 gross/year in 2024 for shortage occupations; otherwise €60,880). The employer must prove labour market testing. Holders get temporary residence, family reunification, and after 21 months of employment, unrestricted EU mobility.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for personnel professionals in Austria?**
A: Typical paths include advancing to HR manager, head of personnel, or specialised roles in recruiting, training, or compensation. Further qualifications such as a HR master's or certifications from the Austrian Association of Personnel Management (VPW) can accelerate growth. Many professionals also transition into organisational development or consultancy.
