## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Teaching Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by sector, region, and experience. In public schools, entry-level teachers earn around €2,500–€3,500 gross per month (14 payments yearly). Private institutions may offer higher pay. With experience, salaries can reach €4,000–€5,000 gross monthly. Specialist roles or leadership positions may command more.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Teaching Professionals in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Red-White-Red Card for qualified workers, including teachers, or an EU Blue Card for high-skilled professionals. Both require a job offer meeting salary thresholds. A Job Search Visa allows graduates to seek employment for up to 6 months. Detailed requirements are on the Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS) website.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a Teaching Professional in Austria?**
A: Generally, a relevant university degree (Bachelor’s or Master’s) and pedagogical training are required. For public schools, completion of a teacher training program and proficiency in German (C1 level) are mandatory. Recognition of foreign qualifications by the Austrian ENIC-NARIC office is often needed. Private schools may have more flexible requirements.
