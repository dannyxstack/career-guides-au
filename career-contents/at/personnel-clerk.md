## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Personnel Clerks in Austria?**
A: The monthly gross salary for Personnel Clerks in Austria typically ranges from €2,000 to €3,000, depending on experience, sector, and region. The collective agreement (Kollektivvertrag) for administrative employees sets a minimum of around €2,100. Senior roles or those in larger firms may earn €3,500 or more.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for Personnel Clerks from non-EU countries to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU Personnel Clerks can apply for the Red-White-Red Card if they have a job offer in a shortage occupation or meet points criteria. The EU Blue Card is also an option for those earning at least €3,564 gross monthly (2024 threshold). Both require a valid employment contract and recognition of qualifications. The employer must demonstrate that no EU citizen is available for the position.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Personnel Clerk in Austria?**
A: Typically, employers require a completed vocational training in office administration (e.g., Bürokaufmann/-frau) or a related field. Some positions accept a Matura (secondary school leaving certificate) plus relevant experience. Proficiency in German (B2 level) is often required. Further certifications in payroll or HR software can improve job prospects.
