## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a handicraft worker in wood, basketry, and related materials in Austria?**
A: In Austria, handicraft workers in wood, basketry, and related materials (ISCO 7317) typically earn between €1,800 and €2,800 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and collective agreements. Apprentices earn less, while master craftspeople may earn higher wages, especially in specialized areas like carpentry or restoration.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign handicraft workers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as handicraft workers via the Red-White-Red Card, requiring a job offer, relevant qualifications (e.g., apprenticeship certificate), and meeting points criteria. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers (typically requiring a university degree), so handicraft workers without a degree may not qualify. Another option is the Skilled Worker in Shortage Occupation card if the occupation is on the shortage list.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for handicraft workers in Austria?**
A: Handicraft workers in Austria can advance to master craftsperson (Meister) by passing an exam, enabling them to train apprentices and run their own businesses. Specialization in restoration or fine woodworking can increase pay. Further education in design or engineering, or moving into vocational teaching, are also possible paths. The dual apprenticeship system provides a strong foundation for growth.
