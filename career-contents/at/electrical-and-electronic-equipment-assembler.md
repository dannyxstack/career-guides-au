## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electrical and electronic equipment assembler in Austria?**
A: The monthly gross salary for entry-level assemblers typically ranges from €1,900 to €2,500, increasing with experience. Collective bargaining agreements in the electrical and electronics industry set minimum rates, often around €2,200 for fully qualified workers. Salaries vary by region and company size, with higher pay in larger firms.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card generally requires a university degree or five years of relevant professional experience, which may not apply to most assemblers. However, non-EU nationals can qualify for the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations if they have recognized vocational training in a relevant trade. This route does not require a degree.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Austria?**
A: Assemblers work in manufacturing plants, often in clean, well-lit environments. Tasks involve reading blueprints, soldering, and assembling components. Work is typically full-time, with occasional shifts. The job requires precision and attention to detail. Many enter through a 3.5-year apprenticeship, and further training can lead to technician or team leader roles.
