## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Austria?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Austria typically earn between €1,600 and €2,200 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and employer. Collective bargaining agreements may apply. Additional pay for overtime, hazardous work, or height work can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa options for building structure cleaners in Austria?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as building structure cleaners via the Red-White-Red Card, if the occupation is in shortage and they have a job offer and recognized qualifications. The EU Blue Card is less common as it targets highly qualified workers with higher salary thresholds.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a building structure cleaner in Austria?**
A: No formal education is strictly required, but on-the-job training is typical. Safety certifications, such as working at height or handling equipment, are beneficial. Physical fitness and experience with cleaning techniques are key. Some employers may require a clean criminal record.
