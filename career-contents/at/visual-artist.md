## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for visual artists in Austria?**
A: Salaries for visual artists in Austria vary widely based on reputation, commissions, and sales. According to the collective agreement for artists, minimum monthly gross salaries start around €1,800. Established artists with gallery representation can earn significantly more, but many combine art with teaching or other work. Income is often irregular.

**Q: What work visa options are available for visual artists to work in Austria?**
A: Visual artists from non-EU/EEA countries can apply for the Red-White-Red Card for key workers if they have a job offer meeting salary thresholds. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card requires a higher salary. Self-employed artists may qualify for the Settlement Permit – Artist, which requires proof of sufficient income and recognition from Austrian authorities.

**Q: Are there any professional associations or support structures for visual artists in Austria?**
A: Yes, the Visual Artists’ Association (Bildende Künstler:innen) and IG Bildende Kunst offer advocacy, networking, and legal advice. The Austrian Artists' Social Insurance Fund (Künstler-Sozialversicherungsfonds) provides social security contributions for eligible artists. Additionally, public funding for projects is available through the Federal Ministry for Arts, Culture, the Civil Service and Sport.
