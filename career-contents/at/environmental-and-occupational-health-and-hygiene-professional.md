## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental and occupational health professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €35,000–€45,000 gross annually. Mid-career professionals earn €50,000–€65,000, while senior roles in industry or government can reach €70,000–€85,000. Public sector pay follows collective agreements (e.g., BAGS/AGS), while private sector, especially in manufacturing or consulting, may offer higher compensation.

**Q: What official work visa routes allow environmental and occupational health professionals to work in Austria?**
A: Qualified professionals can apply for the Rot-Weiß-Rot – Karte (Red-White-Red Card) for skilled workers in shortage occupations, if the role is listed. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer with a minimum salary (around €50,649 gross yearly in 2025). Both routes require employer sponsorship and recognition of qualifications by the Austrian authorities.

**Q: Which sectors employ environmental and occupational health professionals in Austria?**
A: Key employers include government agencies like the Austrian Workers' Compensation Board (AUVA) and environmental agencies, private companies in manufacturing, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and construction, as well as consulting firms. Research institutions and universities also hire for occupational health and safety roles. The profession is regulated and often requires certification for specific positions.
