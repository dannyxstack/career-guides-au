## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Austria?**
A: Salaries for Aged Care Services Managers in Austria typically range from €45,000 to €65,000 gross per year, depending on experience, qualifications, and the region. Senior roles in larger facilities may offer higher pay. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum wages in this sector.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU Aged Care Services Managers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the Red-White-Red Card (qualifying as a skilled worker in a shortage occupation) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals. Both require a job offer meeting salary thresholds and recognized qualifications. The specific requirements vary by visa type and are assessed by the Austrian Public Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Aged Care Services Manager in Austria?**
A: Typically, a degree in healthcare management, nursing, or social work is required, along with several years of experience in elderly care. Additional certifications in management or Geriatric Care are beneficial. Registration with the Austrian health authority may be necessary. Language proficiency in German (at least B2 level) is usually mandatory for daily operations.
