## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers in Austria?**
A: Meter readers in Austria earn a gross monthly salary between €1,800 and €2,500, depending on experience, employer, and collective agreements. Overtime and bonuses may increase earnings. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced workers at larger utility companies can earn toward the higher end.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU meter readers in Austria?**
A: Non-EU meter readers may qualify for the Red-White-Red Card if the job is classified as a shortage occupation or if the worker meets specific criteria (e.g., relevant qualifications). The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary (minimum €4,000/month gross), which is unlikely for this role. Alternatively, a regular employment permit may be available through the labor market test.

**Q: What training and career progression opportunities exist for meter readers in Austria?**
A: Meter readers typically receive on-the-job training; no formal education is required. A valid driving license (B category) is often necessary. Career advancement can lead to roles like supervisor, team leader, or transition to other technical positions in utility companies after gaining experience and additional training.
