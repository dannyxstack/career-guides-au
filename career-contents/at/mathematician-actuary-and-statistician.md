- Routine data cleaning and basic report generation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Austria?**
A: Salaries range from €45,000 to €80,000 annually, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level roles start around €40,000, while senior positions in finance or insurance can exceed €100,000. Public sector salaries follow collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU mathematicians/statisticians to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for the Rot-Weiß-Rot Karte (Red-White-Red Card) for qualified workers in shortage occupations, which includes statisticians and actuaries. The EU Blue Card is also available for those with a job offer earning at least €60,846 (2024 threshold) in regulated professions. Both require a recognized degree and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What is the job market outlook for mathematicians and statisticians in Austria?**
A: Demand is strong in insurance, banking, IT, and research, with Vienna and Graz as key hubs. The rise of data science and AI has created new opportunities. Proficiency in German is often required, though English-speaking roles exist in international companies. Continuous learning in data analytics is beneficial.
