**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Text Proofreading and Spell Checking with Real-time High Accuracy by AI Models
- Data Encoding and Classification: Standardized Conversion from Spreadsheets to Databases
- Standardization of format, such as layout and punctuation uniformity
- Simple document template filling and letter generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Logic and semantic proofreading of complex text; AI provides suggestions, but humans must finalize.
- Auxiliary proofreading of multilingual documents, AI draft first then human polishing
- Data quality monitoring, AI flags outliers for human review
- Quickly locate reference sources via AI search to improve proofreading efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Semantic judgment and style grasp in complex contexts
- Authoritative verification of legal or professional terminology
- Responsibility for handling data involving trade secrets or privacy
- Cross-departmental communication and requirement clarification

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced configuration of AI proofreading tools (e.g. Grammarly Business, Acrolinx)
- Basic programming (Python/Excel macros) to automate repetitive tasks
- Data visualization and basic analysis
- Project management and quality control
- Domain expertise (e.g., legal, medical knowledge)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for coding, proofreading and related clerks in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and experience. According to Austrian collective agreements, entry-level positions may start around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while experienced clerks can earn €2,500–€3,500 or more. Annual gross salaries often range between €30,000 and €45,000. Actual pay depends on the employer, region, and specific duties.

**Q: What visa routes are available for non-EU nationals to work as a coding/proofreading clerk in Austria?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for the Red-White-Red Card (for skilled workers with qualifying criteria) or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold, currently around €43,756/year). The specific requirements, such as recognized qualifications and salary, must be met. Both routes allow family reunification.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a coding or proofreading clerk in Austria?**
A: Employers typically require completed secondary education and often vocational training in office or IT-related fields. Proficiency in German (B1-B2 level) is usually necessary for proofreading roles, while coding clerks may need knowledge of specific software or databases. Relevant work experience and certifications in data entry or editing can enhance job prospects.
