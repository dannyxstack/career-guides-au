## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for personal services workers not elsewhere classified in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specific occupation within this category. Entry-level roles often start around €1,800–€2,200 gross per month, while experienced workers may earn up to €3,000 or more. Collective bargaining agreements and regional differences apply, so check industry-specific rates for precise figures.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as personal services workers in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified non-EU workers whose job meets a minimum salary threshold (€4,659 gross monthly as of 2024). Many personal services roles may not qualify due to lower pay. Other routes include the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations. Always verify official requirements.

**Q: Are personal services jobs in Austria threatened by automation or AI?**
A: Many personal services roles (e.g., funeral workers, pet sitters) require human empathy and adaptability, making them less susceptible to AI automation. Demand remains stable, particularly in childcare and event planning. However, administrative tasks may evolve. Overall, job security is moderate and field-specific.
