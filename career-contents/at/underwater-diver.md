## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for underwater divers in Austria?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Austria vary by experience and sector. Entry-level divers earn around €2,000–€2,500 gross per month. Experienced commercial divers, especially those working in hydropower or construction, can earn €3,500–€5,000 monthly. Additional pay may apply for hazardous conditions or overtime. These figures are gross and subject to Austrian income tax and social security contributions.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for underwater divers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU divers must obtain a Red-White-Red Card (RWR Card) for skilled workers. This requires a job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds and recognition of qualifications. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card applies for highly qualified roles with a salary at least €46,475/year (2024). Both require employer sponsorship, health insurance, and clean criminal records.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as a commercial diver in Austria?**
A: Commercial divers in Austria need recognized qualifications such as the IMCA, ADAS, or HSE diving certificates. Additional national approvals may be required from the Austrian Waterways Authority (via Bundesministerium für Verkehr). Training includes underwater welding, inspection, and safety procedures. Continuing education is essential for career advancement.
