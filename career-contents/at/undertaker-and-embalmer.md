## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for undertakers and embalmers in Austria?**
A: In Austria, undertakers and embalmers typically earn between €1,800 and €2,800 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and employer. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum wages, and additional benefits such as the 13th and 14th monthly salaries are standard.

**Q: What are the main work visa routes for undertakers and embalmers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU/EEA citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary meeting the minimum threshold (€42,000 in 2024). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card is available for skilled workers in shortage occupations, requiring recognized qualifications and a job offer. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Austrian Public Employment Service.

**Q: What training is required to become an undertaker or embalmer in Austria?**
A: In Austria, aspiring undertakers typically complete a 3-year apprenticeship in funeral services, combining on-the-job training with vocational school. Embalming requires specialized courses, often offered by the Austrian Chamber of Commerce. Certification from the chamber is mandatory for self-employed professionals, and continuing education is encouraged for career advancement.
