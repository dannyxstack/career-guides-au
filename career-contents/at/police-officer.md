## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a police officer in Austria?**
A: The starting salary for police officers in Austria (exekutiver Dienst) is approximately €2,300 to €2,800 gross per month, increasing with rank and service. After several years, it can reach €3,500 or more. Additional allowances apply for night shifts and special assignments.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a police officer in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not a route to becoming a police officer in Austria. Police officer positions generally require Austrian or EU/EEA citizenship due to national security and public service obligations. Non-EU nationals may be eligible only if they have permanent residency or citizenship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a police officer in Austria?**
A: Candidates must be Austrian or EU citizens, at least 18 years old, have completed compulsory education (9 years), and possess a clean criminal record. They must pass physical, psychological, and medical tests, and undergo a two-year training program at the Sicherheitsakademie. A driver's license is also required.
