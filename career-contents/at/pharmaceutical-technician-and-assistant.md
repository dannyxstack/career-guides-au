## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a pharmaceutical technician or assistant in Austria?**
A: The average gross annual salary for pharmaceutical technicians and assistants in Austria ranges from €32,000 to €45,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €28,000, while senior roles can exceed €50,000. Salaries are typically higher in private industry compared to public hospitals. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as pharmaceutical technicians in Austria through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals may be eligible for the EU Blue Card in Austria if they hold a recognized tertiary qualification and a job offer with a gross annual salary meeting the minimum threshold (€48,653 in 2024, or €39,588 for shortage occupations). Pharmaceutical technicians may fall under shortage occupations, lowering the salary requirement. Official routes include applying through the Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS) and the relevant consulate.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a pharmaceutical technician in Austria?**
A: To work as a pharmaceutical technician in Austria, you typically need a completed apprenticeship as a pharmacy assistant (Pharmazeutisch-kaufmännische Assistenz) or a specialized school diploma (Fachschule für pharmazeutisch-technische Assistenz). Alternatively, a degree in pharmacy or a related field may qualify. Registration with the Austrian Chamber of Pharmacists (Österreichische Apothekerkammer) is mandatory. Additional language requirements include German proficiency at B2 level or higher.
