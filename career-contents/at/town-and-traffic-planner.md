## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for town and traffic planners in Austria?**
A: Town and traffic planners in Austria earn a gross monthly salary between €2,800 and €4,500, depending on experience and region. Senior roles or those in Vienna may reach higher. Public sector salaries follow collective agreements (e.g., B-KUVG).

**Q: What are the visa routes for non-EU town planners to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU town planners can apply for a Red-White-Red Card for highly qualified workers if they have a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (€49,760 gross annually in 2023). Alternatively, they may qualify for an EU Blue Card with a similar threshold. Both require a recognized degree and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What education and skills are needed for town and traffic planning in Austria?**
A: A master's degree in spatial planning, civil engineering, or architecture from a recognized university is typically required. Austria also requires registration as a Ziviltechniker for independent work. Skills in GIS, urban design, and German proficiency (B2-C1) are essential. Fluency in German is often mandatory for public sector positions.
