## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. For security guards, gross monthly pay ranges from €1,800 to €2,800. Collective bargaining agreements often apply, with entry-level positions starting at the lower end. Specialized roles like event security may pay more.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as protective services workers in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€46,780 in 2024). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations may apply, depending on the exact role and qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required for protective services workers in Austria?**
A: Typically, completion of a relevant apprenticeship (e.g., security guard) or equivalent training is required. Certification from the Austrian Security Academy (Sicherheitsakademie) is mandatory for certain roles. Additional courses in first aid and conflict management are often necessary.
