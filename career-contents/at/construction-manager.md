## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for construction managers in Austria?**
A: The average gross annual salary for construction managers in Austria ranges from €50,000 to €80,000, depending on experience, project size, and location (e.g., Vienna typically pays higher). Senior managers or those on large-scale projects can earn up to €100,000. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU construction managers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU construction managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary above the threshold (€43,820 in 2024 for highly qualified workers). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for Key Workers is available for those with relevant qualifications and work experience. Both require a confirmed employment contract.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a construction manager in Austria?**
A: Typically, construction managers in Austria need a degree in civil engineering, architecture, or construction management. For regulated roles, certification (Ziviltechniker) may be needed. Practical experience is highly valued, and many managers start as site supervisors. Membership in the Austrian Chamber of Architects and Consulting Engineers is mandatory for certain projects.
