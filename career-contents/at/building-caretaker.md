## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Austria?**
A: The monthly gross salary for building caretakers in Austria typically ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience, region, and employer. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum wages, with Vienna and larger cities offering higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as building caretakers in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Austria requires a university degree or a minimum salary threshold (around €3,500 gross monthly in 2023), which building caretakers rarely meet. Non-EU citizens typically need a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations or a separate work permit if sponsored by an employer.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building caretaker in Austria?**
A: Building caretakers in Austria oversee property maintenance, including cleaning, minor repairs, heating system monitoring, waste management, and ensuring safety regulations are met. They often act as a point of contact for tenants and coordinate with external service providers.
