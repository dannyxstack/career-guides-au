## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Database and Network Professionals in Austria?**
A: Salaries for Database and Network Professionals (ISCO-08 2529) in Austria generally range between €45,000 and €70,000 gross per year. This varies based on experience, industry, and location. Senior roles or those with specialized skills can earn higher, while entry-level positions may start around €40,000.

**Q: How can non-EU professionals work in Austria as Database and Network Professionals?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Austria via the EU Blue Card, requiring a recognized university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a gross annual salary meeting the minimum threshold (€43,000 in 2024). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card applies to qualified professionals in shortage occupations, though this ISCO group is not currently listed as a shortage occupation. Employers must demonstrate labor market testing.

**Q: What are the career prospects for Database and Network Professionals in Austria?**
A: Career prospects are strong due to digital transformation across industries. Opportunities exist in IT consulting, finance, manufacturing, and public sector. Professionals can specialize in database administration, network security, or cloud computing. Continuous learning in emerging technologies is valued, and Austrian companies often offer further training. The demand is high in Vienna, Graz, and Linz.
