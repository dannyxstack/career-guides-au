## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Austria?**
A: Income varies widely due to self-employment. Average hourly rates range from €30 to €80, with annual earnings typically between €15,000 and €40,000 depending on clientele and reputation. Few hold salaried positions; most work on a commission or fee basis. Registration with the Social Security authorities (SVS) is required for self-employed practitioners.

**Q: Can non-EU astrologers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU astrologers may apply for a residence permit for self-employed key workers under the Rot-Weiß-Rot – Karte if they meet criteria for self-employment. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly qualified employees with a binding job offer, which is uncommon for this occupation. Alternatively, a regular employment visa requires a concrete employer and approval from the AMS.

**Q: What qualifications or licenses are needed to practice as an astrologer in Austria?**
A: No formal state license is required, but practitioners must comply with trade regulations if offering commercial services. Often, proof of training from recognized institutions (e.g., Vienna Astrology School) enhances credibility. Self-employed individuals must register with the Social Security Authority (SVS) and the trade office (Bezirkshauptmannschaft).
