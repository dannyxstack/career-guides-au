## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Austria?**
A: Shelf fillers in Austria typically earn between €1,500 and €1,900 gross per month for full-time work, depending on the region and employer. Collective bargaining agreements often set minimum rates, with retail sectors in Vienna and Salzburg offering slightly higher pay. Part-time and temporary positions are common.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa as a shelf filler in Austria?**
A: Shelf filler roles are considered low-skilled, so the EU Blue Card (requiring higher education) does not apply. Non-EU nationals may be sponsored for a Red-White-Red Card for other key workers, but only if the employer proves a labor shortage and no suitable EU candidate is available. Such approvals are rare for shelf filling.

**Q: What are the typical duties and career opportunities for shelf fillers in Austria?**
A: Duties include restocking shelves, rotating stock to ensure freshness, maintaining cleanliness, and assisting customers. Career progression can lead to roles like section manager or inventory specialist, especially in larger retail chains. On-the-job training and German language skills improve advancement prospects.
