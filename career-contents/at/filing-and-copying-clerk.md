## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual document sorting and filing, replaced by digital document management systems
- Copying and organizing paper documents, replaced by digital scanning and cloud storage
- Document retrieval and borrowing registration, automatically completed by AI-driven search tools
- Document version control and updates, automated process management system takeover
- Daily office supply management, automated replenishment via smart procurement system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Improve document entry efficiency using OCR and intelligent classification tools
- Use document management platforms (e.g., SharePoint) for team collaboration and permission control
- Use data analysis tools to generate document usage reports and optimize the filing system
- Apply RPA to automate repetitive tasks, freeing up time to handle exceptions or complex documents.
- Conduct document content review and compliance checks with AI assistance.

**Human moat:**

- Professional judgment for handling unstructured or ambiguous documents
- Cross-departmental communication and coordination to resolve document disputes
- Understanding tacit knowledge and unwritten rules within an organization
- Monitor automated systems operations and correct errors
- Maintain security and privacy of sensitive or classified documents

**Skills to build:**

- Document management system (e.g., Alfresco, DocuWare) operation
- Basic programming or RPA tool (e.g., UiPath) application
- Data analysis and reporting (Excel advanced features, Power BI)
- Digital literacy and information management
- English (handling multilingual documents)
- Communication and problem-solving skills.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Filing and Copying Clerks in Austria?**
A: Salaries for Filing and Copying Clerks in Austria typically range from €1,800 to €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and sector. Collective bargaining agreements set minimum wages, with Vienna often offering slightly higher pay. Entry-level positions start near the lower end, while experienced clerks may earn up to €2,800.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for Filing and Copying Clerks to work in Austria?**
A: For Filing and Copying Clerks, the main route is the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers, but this usually requires a job offer in a shortage occupation or higher qualifications. Since this role is not typically considered highly skilled, the EU Blue Card (requiring a university degree) may not apply. Alternatively, a general employment permit (Beschäftigungsbewilligung) can be sponsored by an employer if no Austrian or EU citizen is available.

**Q: Is automation or AI affecting the role of Filing and Copying Clerks in Austria?**
A: Yes, automation and digitalization are reducing demand for manual filing and copying roles in Austria. Many tasks are now handled by document management software and automated data entry. While some clerical positions remain, especially in small firms or public administration, the overall trend is toward a decline in these jobs. Upskilling in digital tools or moving to administrative support roles can improve job security.
