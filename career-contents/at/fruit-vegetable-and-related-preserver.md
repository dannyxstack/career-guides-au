## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fruit, vegetable and related preservers in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and company size. According to collective agreements, entry-level workers earn around €1,800-€2,200 gross per month. Experienced preservers can earn up to €2,800. Overtime and shift work may increase pay. Actual salaries depend on qualifications and employer.

**Q: What work visa options exist for fruit, vegetable and related preservers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the Red-White-Red Card, which requires a job offer and recognized vocational qualifications. The EU Blue Card is not suitable as it targets highly skilled workers with tertiary education. The occupation may appear on the shortage occupation list, easing eligibility. Employer sponsorship is mandatory.

**Q: What are the main tasks of a fruit, vegetable and related preserver in Austria?**
A: Tasks include sorting, washing, peeling, and cutting fruits and vegetables; operating machines for blanching, freezing, canning, or drying; monitoring production processes; ensuring quality and hygiene standards; and packaging products. Workers often need to follow strict HACCP guidelines and may work in shifts.
