## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Legal Secretary in Austria?**
A: In Austria, entry-level Legal Secretaries earn around €28,000 to €35,000 gross annually. With experience, salaries can reach €40,000 to €50,000. Larger law firms in Vienna typically pay more. The collective agreement for legal professions often sets minimum salaries, which vary by region and experience level.

**Q: Can a non-EU Legal Secretary obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Austria?**
A: Yes, but the position must meet the minimum salary threshold (currently about €43,755 gross/year in 2024, or €34,326 for shortage occupations). Legal Secretary is not listed as a shortage occupation in Austria, so the higher threshold applies. The employer must also prove the position cannot be filled by an Austrian or EU candidate. Other work permits like the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers may be an alternative.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Legal Secretary in Austria?**
A: Typically, you need completed secondary education and a vocational training certificate (Lehre) or a specialized school for legal professions. Proficiency in German is essential, often at C1 level. Knowledge of English, legal terminology, and office software is expected. Some firms require experience in a law office or a relevant apprenticeship.
