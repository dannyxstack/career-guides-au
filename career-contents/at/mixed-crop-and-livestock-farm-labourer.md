## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Austria?**
A: The minimum monthly gross wage for agricultural workers in Austria is about €1,860 as of 2024 (based on collective agreements). Actual earnings depend on region, employer, and experience. Seasonal workers may receive hourly rates around €11.60.

**Q: What work-visa options are available for non-EU nationals to work as a farm labourer in Austria?**
A: For unskilled or low-skilled farm labour, the EU Blue Card is not applicable due to its high salary threshold. Instead, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers may be an option. Seasonal workers from non-EU countries can apply for a Seasonal Employment visa under the EU Seasonal Workers Directive, valid for up to 6 months.

**Q: What are the main duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Austria?**
A: Duties include planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops; feeding and caring for livestock; maintaining farm equipment; cleaning barns and stalls; and assisting with general farm operations. Work is often seasonal and physically demanding, requiring outdoor work in varying weather conditions.
