## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a lifting truck operator in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and region. According to collective agreements, entry-level lifting truck operators earn around €2,000 gross per month, with experienced operators earning up to €2,800 or more. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total pay.

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU lifting truck operators in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers. This requires a job offer, relevant vocational training, and the occupation being listed as a shortage profession. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and generally does not apply to this occupation.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a lifting truck operator in Austria?**
A: You need a valid forklift license (Staplerführerschein) and proof of training (Befähigungsnachweis). Many employers require a certificate from a recognized course. Apprenticeship in logistics or a related field is beneficial. Safety training is mandatory and must be renewed periodically.
