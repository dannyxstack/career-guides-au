## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fashion models in Austria?**
A: Salaries for fashion models in Austria vary widely based on experience, assignments, and agency. Entry-level models may earn around €1,500–€2,500 per month, while established models can command €5,000–€10,000 or more per project. However, many models are freelance and paid per assignment, so income can be irregular. Agencies typically take a commission of 20–30%.

**Q: What is the work visa route for fashion models to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU/EEA fashion models can work in Austria through the Red-White-Red Card for key workers, which requires a valid job offer and meeting criteria (e.g., minimum salary threshold). Alternatively, the EU Blue Card may apply for highly qualified roles. For short-term assignments, a D visa for employment (up to 6 months) is possible. All routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS).

**Q: What qualifications or experience are needed to become a fashion model in Austria?**
A: Formal qualifications are not required, but a strong portfolio, professional photos, and agency representation are essential. Models should have appropriate physical measurements (height, size) and the ability to take direction. Networking and maintaining a social media presence are increasingly important. Many models start with local agencies like Wiener Models or lookbook.nu. Workshops and casting calls are common entry points.
