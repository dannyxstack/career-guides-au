**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for sweepers and related labourers in Austria?**
A: In Austria, sweepers and related labourers are typically covered by collective bargaining agreements. Gross monthly salaries for entry-level positions range from about €1,700 to €2,100, depending on the region and employer. Overtime and shift allowances may apply. Wages are often adjusted annually based on collective agreement negotiations.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a sweeper in Austria via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for sweepers, as it requires a university degree or comparable high qualification. Non-EU nationals may obtain a Red-White-Red Card for unskilled workers only in exceptional cases, such as seasonal work for up to six months, or if a labor shortage is proven. Most sweeping jobs are filled by EU/EEA citizens due to free movement.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a sweeper in Austria?**
A: No formal qualifications are required, but employers often prefer basic literacy and reliability. On-the-job training is typical, covering equipment use and safety. A valid work permit is required for non-EU nationals. Knowledge of German (A1-A2 level) is advantageous for communication and safety instructions.
