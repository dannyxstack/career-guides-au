## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Austria?**
A: Due to Austria being a landlocked country, deep-sea fishery work does not exist. For comparison, inland fishery workers earn around €1,800–€2,500 gross per month, but salaries vary by employer and experience. Deep-sea fishery roles are only possible if employed by an Austrian company operating vessels in international waters, which is extremely rare.

**Q: Can deep-sea fishery workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Austria?**
A: Deep-sea fishery workers do not qualify for the EU Blue Card in Austria, as this card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent. For non-EU nationals, a Red-White-Red Card may be possible for skilled workers in shortage occupations, but deep-sea fishery is not listed. A specific work permit would require a concrete job offer and approval from the Public Employment Service (AMS).

**Q: Are there opportunities for deep-sea fishery workers in Austria?**
A: Austria is landlocked, so deep-sea fishery as defined by ISCO-08 code 6223 does not exist. Related work may involve inland commercial fishing on lakes (e.g., Lake Constance) or aquaculture, but not deep-sea operations. Workers interested in this field would generally need to seek employment in coastal EU countries like Germany, Norway, or Spain.
