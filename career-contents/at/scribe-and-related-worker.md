- Transcribing handwritten documents into electronic text.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI-assisted transcription tools to improve efficiency
- Supervise and correct AI-generated transcription results

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes and related workers in Austria?**
A: The average gross salary for scribes and related workers (ISCO 4414) in Austria ranges from €28,000 to €36,000 per year, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €24,000, while experienced professionals may earn up to €42,000. Salaries are often governed by collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for scribes and related workers to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as scribes in Austria via the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals. Both require a job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds. The EU Blue Card mandates a gross annual salary of at least €43,584 (2024) for regulated professions. Self-employment is possible under the Settlement Permit for self-employed key workers.

**Q: What is the career outlook for scribes and related workers in Austria?**
A: The demand for scribes and related workers remains stable, with opportunities primarily in law firms, notary offices, courts, and administrative departments. Digitalization is increasing the need for workers skilled in legal transcription and document management. Career advancement may involve specialization in legal fields or progression to office management roles.
