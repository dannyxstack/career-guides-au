## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for car, taxi and van drivers in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer. According to collective agreements, gross monthly pay for taxi drivers ranges from €2,000 to €2,800. Van drivers earn similar amounts, with experienced drivers potentially reaching €3,000. Overtime, tips, and night supplements can increase total earnings. Figures are before tax and social insurance.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a car, taxi or van driver in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals and does not apply to driving occupations. Non-EU citizens must instead obtain a work permit through the Red-White-Red Card system, but driving jobs are rarely classified as shortage occupations. Employer sponsorship and a labour market test are typically required. Valid driving licences must also be recognised.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a taxi driver in Austria?**
A: You need a valid driving licence (at least B category) and a special taxi licence (Taxischein) issued by the district authority. Requirements include a clean criminal record, a medical certificate, and passing a knowledge test on local geography, regulations, and fares. Some provinces also require a psychological fitness exam.
