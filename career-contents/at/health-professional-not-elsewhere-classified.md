## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialization and experience. For example, occupational therapists earn around €2,500–€3,500 gross monthly, while speech therapists may earn €2,800–€3,800. Public sector positions follow collective agreements. The average gross monthly salary for this group is approximately €3,000, but ranges depend on sector and location.

**Q: How can non-EU health professionals obtain a work visa for Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card or a Red-White-Red Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer in a qualified health profession and meeting the annually adjusted minimum salary threshold. The Red-White-Red Card is for skilled workers in shortage occupations, including certain health roles, and requires a job offer meeting specific criteria.

**Q: What are the career prospects for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Austria?**
A: Career growth depends on the field. Many advance to senior or supervisory roles in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, or private practice. Further specialization through postgraduate training (e.g., neurorehabilitation or pediatric therapy) can lead to higher positions. The aging population ensures steady demand, especially in geriatric care, offering stable employment opportunities.
