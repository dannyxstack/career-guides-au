## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Administrative and Executive Secretaries in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. As of 2025, the gross monthly salary for administrative secretaries in Austria ranges from €2,100 to €3,200. Vienna and larger companies tend to pay higher. Collective bargaining agreements (Kollektivvertrag) set minimums; actual pay may exceed these.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for Administrative and Executive Secretaries to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers if the position meets minimum salary thresholds (€3,000+ gross monthly) and relevant qualifications. The EU Blue Card is for higher-skilled roles (salary at least €4,500). Both require a job offer and recognition of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an Administrative and Executive Secretary in Austria?**
A: A completed apprenticeship (Lehre) in office administration or a related field is common. Alternatively, a post-secondary certificate in business or secretarial studies. Fluency in German is typically required, and English is often expected. Professional experience may substitute for formal qualifications.
