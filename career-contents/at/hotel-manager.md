## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a hotel manager in Austria?**
A: Hotel managers in Austria earn between €3,000 and €6,000 gross per month, depending on factors like hotel size, location, and experience. In Vienna or luxury properties, salaries can be higher. Collective bargaining agreements (Kollektivvertrag) for the hospitality industry set minimum wages, which are generally around €2,500–€3,000 for entry-level managers.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as hotel managers in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for the EU Blue Card to work as hotel managers in Austria. Requirements include a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a gross annual salary of at least €43,120 (2024 threshold). The employer must also demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an Austrian or EU citizen. The Blue Card is valid for up to two years and renewable.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a hotel manager in Austria?**
A: Typically, hotel managers in Austria hold a degree in hospitality management or a related field, plus several years of experience. Austrian vocational training (Lehre) in hotel management or a higher-level tourism school (Höhere Bundeslehranstalt für Tourismus) is also common. Fluency in German is highly advantageous, as many roles require communication with local staff and clients.
