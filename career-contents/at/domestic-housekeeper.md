## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic housekeepers in Austria?**
A: Based on collective agreements, the minimum monthly gross salary for domestic housekeepers in Austria ranges from €1,500 to €1,800, depending on experience and region. Actual pay may be higher, especially for live-in positions or additional duties. Employers often provide in-kind benefits like meals or accommodation.

**Q: What work visa options exist for domestic housekeepers in Austria?**
A: Non-EU/EEA citizens can apply for a Red-White-Red Card for key workers if they meet specific skill criteria, though domestic housekeeping rarely qualifies. Seasonal employment visas (up to 6 months) are available for temporary work. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and higher salary, making it generally unsuitable for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for domestic housekeepers in Austria?**
A: With experience, domestic housekeepers can progress to supervisory roles in hotels or private households, or specialize in eco-friendly cleaning. Some become self-employed or start cleaning businesses. The AMS (Public Employment Service) offers professional development courses to enhance skills and qualifications.
