**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex fault diagnosis: AR overlays AI analysis of thermal imaging/sonic data to pinpoint hidden leaks

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for plumbers and pipe fitters in Austria?**
A: Entry-level plumbers earn around €2,000–€2,500 gross per month. With experience, salaries range from €2,800 to €3,800. Master plumbers can earn €4,000 or more. Actual pay varies by region, employer, and collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: Can plumbers and pipe fitters work in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Austria is for highly qualified non-EU workers with a university degree or equivalent. Plumbers typically do not meet this requirement. Non-EU plumbers may instead apply for the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations, provided they have recognized qualifications and a job offer.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a plumber in Austria?**
A: To work as a plumber in Austria, you typically need a completed apprenticeship (Lehre) in plumbing or pipe fitting, lasting three to four years. Alternatively, foreign qualifications may be recognized via the apprenticeship equivalency process (Nostrifizierung). A valid trade license (Gewerbeberechtigung) is required for self-employment.
