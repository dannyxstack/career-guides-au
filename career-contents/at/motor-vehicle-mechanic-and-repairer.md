## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for motor vehicle mechanics in Austria?**
A: Salaries for motor vehicle mechanics in Austria vary by experience, qualification, and region. Entry-level mechanics earn around €2,000 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €3,500. Master craftsmen (Kfz-Meister) may earn higher. Overtime and bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: What work visa routes exist for motor vehicle mechanics to work in Austria?**
A: Non-EU motor vehicle mechanics can apply for the Red-White-Red Card (RWR Card) if they have a recognized vocational qualification and a job offer. The EU Blue Card typically requires a university degree, which is not usually held by mechanics. Therefore, the RWR Card is the main route. No specific numbers or quotas are publicly set.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for motor vehicle mechanics in Austria?**
A: Mechanics can become master craftsmen (Kfz-Meister) after further training and exams, leading to supervisory roles, higher pay, or self-employment. Specializations in electric vehicles, diagnostics, or classic cars are growing. With experience, one may also move into service management or technical support.
