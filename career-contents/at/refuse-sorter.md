## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a refuse sorter in Austria?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a refuse sorter in Austria ranges from €1,800 to €2,400, depending on the region, employer, and experience. Collective bargaining agreements set minimum pay levels, and overtime or shift work may increase earnings. Salaries are generally lower in rural areas compared to cities like Vienna.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as refuse sorters in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent, so refuse sorters typically do not qualify. Non-EU citizens may apply for a Red-White-Red Card if the occupation is listed as a shortage occupation, which refuse sorting currently is not. Alternatively, a residence permit for employed persons can be obtained if a labor market test confirms no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a refuse sorter in Austria?**
A: Formal qualifications are usually not required, but physical fitness and reliability are essential. Many employers offer on-the-job training. Basic German language skills (A1-A2 level) are often expected for safety communication. A clean criminal record may be needed, as the job involves handling waste in public areas. Prior experience in waste management or manual work is advantageous but not mandatory.
