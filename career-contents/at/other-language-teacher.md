## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other language teachers in Austria?**
A: Salaries for language teachers in Austria vary by institution and experience. In public schools, monthly gross pay for a full-time teacher ranges from €2,500 to €3,500. Private language schools and universities may offer €3,000–€4,500. Income depends on qualifications, contract type, and location.

**Q: Can non-EU language teachers work in Austria via an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary above €43,758 (2024 threshold). Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for key workers or other employment permits may apply. Language teaching must meet specific criteria.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to teach languages in Austria?**
A: Generally, a relevant university degree (e.g., in language studies or education) and teaching experience are required. For public schools, state certification (Lehrbefähigung) is necessary. Private institutions may accept TEFL or CELTA certificates. Proficiency in German is often expected, especially for teaching in the school system.
