## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Austria?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specific trade and experience. On average, craft workers in Austria earn between €1,800 and €3,000 gross per month. Skilled trades with high demand may command higher pay. Overtime and shift work can increase earnings. For precise figures, consult the Austrian collective bargaining agreements (Kollektivvertrag) relevant to the specific occupation.

**Q: What work visa options are available for craft workers to work in Austria?**
A: Craft workers from non-EU/EEA countries typically need a Red-White-Red Card, which requires a job offer with minimum salary thresholds and recognized qualifications. The EU Blue Card is for higher-skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent. For craft occupations, the Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers in shortage occupations is most applicable. Application is made through the Austrian embassy or the responsible authority. Official details are on the Austrian immigration website.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a craft worker in Austria?**
A: For many craft trades, completion of a recognized apprenticeship (Lehre) or equivalent vocational training is required. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Austrian authorities. The process can be initiated through the ENIC-NARIC Austria office. For regulated trades, specific certifications are mandatory. It is advisable to check with the relevant professional chamber for trade-specific requirements.
