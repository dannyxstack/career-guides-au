## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for employment agents and contractors in Austria?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for employment agents and contractors in Austria ranges from €2,500 to €3,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Vienna and larger firms often pay higher. Entry-level positions start around €2,000, while experienced professionals can earn up to €4,500. These figures are based on collective agreements and market data.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for employment agents and contractors in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as employment agents or contractors via the EU Blue Card or Red-White-Red Card. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer with a minimum salary of €54,858 annually (2024). The Red-White-Red Card is for skilled workers in shortage occupations; criteria include qualifications and work experience. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Public Employment Service (AMS).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an employment agent or contractor in Austria?**
A: Typically, a completed apprenticeship in office administration or a related field is required, along with proficiency in German (B2 level or higher). Many employers also expect a degree in business, human resources, or law. Registration with the Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS) is mandatory for labour leasing agencies. Ongoing training in labour law and recruitment is advantageous.
