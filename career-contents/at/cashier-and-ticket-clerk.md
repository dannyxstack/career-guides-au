## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Austria?**
A: Salaries are governed by collective bargaining agreements. For cashiers in retail, monthly gross salary ranges from €1,800 to €2,300 for full-time. Ticket clerks in transport may earn similar. Many receive additional benefits like holiday pay. Actual pay varies by region, company, and experience.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a cashier or ticket clerk in Austria?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa. The EU Blue Card typically requires higher education or management roles, so not applicable. Instead, apply for a Red-White-Red Card if the job qualifies as skilled, or a regular employment permit after a labor market test. The employer must prove no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are typical career progression opportunities for this role in Austria?**
A: With experience, cashiers may advance to head cashier or shift supervisor roles. Ticket clerks can become team leaders or move into customer service management. Further training in retail or transport management opens doors. Some pursue additional qualifications to switch to accounting or logistics.
