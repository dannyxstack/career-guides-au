## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Austria?**
A: Salaries are regulated by collective agreements. For garden and horticultural labourers, the minimum gross monthly wage is approximately €1,500 to €1,800 for full-time work, depending on experience and region. Actual pay may be higher in specialized roles or with overtime. Additional benefits like holiday pay and bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as garden and horticultural labourers in Austria through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary threshold; it does not apply to garden and horticultural labourers. Non-EU nationals may instead qualify for a Red-White-Red Card for shortage occupations or a seasonal employment permit for temporary work. Exact eligibility depends on individual qualifications and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main duties of garden and horticultural labourers in Austria?**
A: Typical duties include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, and harvesting plants, flowers, and vegetables. They also prepare soil, operate gardening tools, maintain green spaces, and assist with landscaping. Work is often seasonal, with peak demand in spring and summer, and may be outdoors in various weather conditions.
