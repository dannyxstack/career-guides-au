## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a subsistence livestock farmer in Austria?**
A: Income is highly variable and generally low, often ranging from €1,200 to €2,500 per month net, depending on farm size and subsidies. Many farmers supplement with off-farm income. Official statistics are limited, but subsistence farming is typically not a primary source of high earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as subsistence livestock farmers in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent, so subsistence farming does not qualify. Non-EU individuals may apply for a residence permit for self-employed work if they meet specific criteria, such as sufficient capital and business viability, or a seasonal work permit. However, it is challenging due to the nature of the occupation.

**Q: What are the main challenges for subsistence livestock farmers in Austria?**
A: Key challenges include low profitability, administrative burdens from EU agricultural policies, strict animal welfare regulations, and difficulty accessing markets. Many farms are small and family-run, often relying on subsidies from the Common Agricultural Policy. Climate change and land use pressures also pose long-term risks.
