## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shopkeeper in Austria?**
A: Shopkeepers in Austria are often self-employed, so income depends on store profits. Employed shop managers earn around €1,800 to €2,500 net monthly, varying by industry and location. Self-employed shopkeepers may earn less initially but have higher potential. Average gross salary for retail managers is about €2,800 per month.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as shopkeepers in Austria via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified employees with a job offer meeting a minimum salary (€3,573 gross/month in 2024). A shopkeeper role may qualify if it involves managerial duties and meets the threshold. Alternatively, the Red-White-Red Card for self-employed key workers allows setting up a shop, requiring a business plan and sufficient funds.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become a shopkeeper in Austria?**
A: You need a trade license (Gewerbe) from the local authority. For most retail, a completed apprenticeship (Lehre) in retail or equivalent experience suffices. Some regulated trades (e.g., pharmacies, groceries) require specific certificates. Self-employed shopkeepers must register with the Social Security and tax authorities.
