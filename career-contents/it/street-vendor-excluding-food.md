# Street Vendor (excluding Food)（Street Vendor (excluding Food)）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：9520 – Street Vendor (excluding Food)。**

Sells non-food goods such as clothing, accessories, and small merchandise on streets or markets in Italy, often as self-employed or seasonal work.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Quota Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟公民，通过年度配额申请，需雇主担保，但街头贩卖自雇性质较难匹配 |
| Subclass 自雇工作签证（Lavoro Autono（Self-Employment Visa） | 适用于计划在意大利从事自雇活动的非欧盟公民，需提交商业计划并满足资金要求 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income level for street vendors in Italy?**
答：Income varies greatly; junior vendors earn about €12,000-18,000 per year, while senior vendors can reach €25,000-35,000, heavily influenced by season and location.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through street vending?**
答：Difficult. Street vending typically doesn't meet high-skilled migration criteria; self-employment visa requires significant capital and a business plan, with limited quotas.

**问：What permits are needed for street vending in Italy?**
答：A municipal stall permit and a VAT number (Partita IVA) are required, along with compliance with local commercial regulations.
