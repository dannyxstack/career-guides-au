# Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks（Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：0310 – Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks。**

Non-commissioned members of the Italian armed forces, including soldiers and non-commissioned officers, responsible for military operations, national security, and international peacekeeping missions.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for other ranks in the Italian armed forces?**
答：Junior ranks earn €18,000-24,000 per year, mid-level €25,000-35,000, and senior up to €50,000, plus benefits like housing and healthcare.

**问：Can non-EU citizens work in the Italian armed forces via a work visa?**
答：No. Military positions are reserved for Italian citizens only. Non-EU citizens cannot obtain a work visa for this purpose.

**问：What is the career progression in the Italian military?**
答：Start as a soldier, advance to non-commissioned officer through exams and training, and top performers may attend officer school. Requires long-term service.
