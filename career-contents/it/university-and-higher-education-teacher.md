# University and Higher Education Teacher（University and Higher Education Teacher）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2310 – University and Higher Education Teacher。**

University professors and lecturers teach and conduct research at universities and higher education institutions in Italy.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IT独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Blue Card UE（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能非欧盟工作者，需工作合同及最低薪资要求。 |
| Subclass Visto per lavoro sub（Work Visa (Subordinate Employment)） | 通过意大利雇主申请的工作签证，需通过劳动市场测试。 |
| Subclass Permesso di soggiorn（Research Visa） | 适用于研究活动，如博士或研究项目，可在大学申请。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for university teachers in Italy?**
答：Assistant professors earn around €35,000-€50,000, full professors up to €80,000, lower than Nordic countries but with lower living costs.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become university teachers in Italy?**
答：Usually required a PhD, apply for EU Blue Card or work visa. Start with a research visa or postdoc position.

**问：What are the challenges for academic promotion in Italy?**
答：Need to obtain national scientific qualification (abilitazione), limited positions and high competition.
