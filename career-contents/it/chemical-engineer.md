# Chemical Engineer（Chemical Engineer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2145 – Chemical Engineer。**

Chemical engineers design, develop, and optimize chemical processes for industries such as energy, pharmaceuticals, and food.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需工作合同且年薪高于门槛（约3.2万欧元） |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi（Decreto Flussi） | 年度配额工作签证，适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主申请 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta） | 工作许可，由雇主为特定岗位申请，需证明无本地候选人 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for chemical engineers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level €30k-38k, mid-level €38k-50k, senior €50k-70k, varies by industry and region.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to Italy as a chemical engineer?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (job offer and salary threshold) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa; employer must obtain Nulla Osta.

**问：Is registration as an engineer required in Italy?**
答：Not mandatory, but registration (via state exam) improves career prospects, especially for public projects and senior roles.
