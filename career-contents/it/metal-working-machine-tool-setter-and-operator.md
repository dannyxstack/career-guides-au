# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator（Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7223 – Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator。**

Sets up, operates, and maintains metalworking machine tools (e.g., lathes, milling machines, grinders) to produce metal parts according to drawings and specifications, ensuring precision and surface quality.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 非欧盟工人通过年度配额申请，需雇主担保。金属加工操作员不在优先清单，但可尝试申请。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card (Italy)） | 适用于高技能岗位，要求至少3年工作经验或高等学历，年薪门槛约30,000欧元（2025年）。通常不针对此职位。 |
| Subclass 工作居留（Permesso di Sog（Work Residence Permit） | 持有工作签证后申请，有效期通常2年，可续签。需继续受雇。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for metal working machine tool setters and operators in Italy?**
答：Entry-level: €22,000-26,000; mid-level: €27,000-32,000; senior: up to €38,000 gross per year, including shift and overtime allowances.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa for metal working jobs in Italy?**
答：Mainly through the Decreto Flussi quota system, requiring employer sponsorship. The occupation is not on shortage lists, so quotas are competitive. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：What training is needed to become a metal working operator?**
答：Typically a 3-year vocational training (IeFP) or 2-year higher technical education (ITS). CNC programming certification and a vocational qualification are beneficial.
