# Supply, Distribution and Related Managers（Supply, Distribution and Related Managers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：1324 – Supply, Distribution and Related Managers。**

Plan, supervise, and coordinate supply chain and distribution activities, including procurement, inventory management, transport and logistics to ensure efficient operations.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，要求年薪不低于约25,000欧元，有大学学位或5年经验。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi quota work visa） | 适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，每年配额有限，对供应链管理人员开放。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for supply chain managers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-35,000, mid-level €40,000-55,000, senior €60,000+.

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa for supply chain manager roles in Italy?**
答：Apply for EU Blue Card (requires minimum salary) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa with employer sponsorship.
