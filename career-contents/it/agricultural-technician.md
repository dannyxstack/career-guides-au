# Agricultural Technician（Agricultural Technician）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3142 – Agricultural Technician。**

Agricultural technicians in Italy provide technical support in agriculture, including soil analysis, crop monitoring, pest control, and guidance on agricultural machinery.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for agricultural technicians in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €18,000 to €24,000, mid-level €25,000-€32,000, and senior €33,000-€42,000. Salary varies by region and employer.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy as agricultural technicians?**
答：Via the EU Blue Card (requires higher education and high salary) or Decreto Flussi work visa (employer sponsorship, limited quotas).
