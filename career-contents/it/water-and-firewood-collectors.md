# Water and Firewood Collectors（Water and Firewood Collectors）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：9624 – Water and Firewood Collectors。**

In Italy, water and firewood collectors primarily engage in informal work in rural areas, collecting drinking water and firewood, often in remote regions or for low-income households.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of water and firewood collectors?**
答：Income is very low and unstable, typically ranging from 5,000 to 15,000 EUR annually, depending on seasonal demand and local community.

**问：Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
答：Almost impossible, as it does not qualify for any work visa category and falls under the informal economy, making legal residence unattainable.
