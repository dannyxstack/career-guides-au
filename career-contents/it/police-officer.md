# Police Officer（Police Officer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：5412 – Police Officer。**

Italian police officers maintain public order, prevent and investigate crimes, enforce laws, and provide safety and security in communities.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a police officer in Italy?**
答：Entry-level officers earn around EUR 25,000-30,000, while senior officers can earn EUR 42,000-55,000.

**问：Can non-EU citizens become police officers in Italy?**
答：Generally requires Italian or EU citizenship. Non-EU citizens face significant barriers.

**问：What is the career progression for police officers in Italy?**
答：Promotion from officer to sergeant, inspector, senior inspector requires exams and seniority.
