# Sewing Machine Operator（Sewing Machine Operator）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：8153 – Sewing Machine Operator。**

Operates sewing machines to stitch and process garments, textiles, and other materials, commonly found in manufacturing and apparel production.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 意大利年度配额工作签证，适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保 |
| Subclass 欧盟长期居留（EU Long-Term Residence） | 在意大利合法居住5年后可申请，需满足收入等条件 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Sewing Machine Operator？**
- 耐心细致，动手能力强
- 能接受重复性工作
- 愿意在制造业或服装行业长期发展

**谁不适合学Sewing Machine Operator？**
- 追求高薪和快速晋升
- 无法适应长时间坐姿操作

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for sewing machine operators in Italy?**
答：Entry-level earns around €16,000-20,000/year, mid-level €20,000-25,000/year, senior up to €30,000/year, depending on region and factory.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for sewing machine operators in Italy?**
答：Mainly via the Decreto Flussi quota work visa, requiring employer sponsorship and annual quota availability. This occupation is not in shortage, so it's challenging.

**问：Is there career progression for sewing machine operators?**
答：Limited, but can move to quality control, sample making, or production management. Automation poses risks; upskilling in high-end customization or equipment maintenance is advisable.
