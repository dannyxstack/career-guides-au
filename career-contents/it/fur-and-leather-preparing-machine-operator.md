# Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator（Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：8155 – Fur and Leather Preparing Machine Operator。**

Operate machines for preparing fur and leather, including fleshing, unhairing, and liming, to ready hides for tanning and further processing.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for fur and leather preparing machine operators?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 20,000-25,000 EUR, senior can reach 40,000 EUR, but generally below Italian average.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Italy through this occupation?**
答：Unlikely. Not a shortage occupation; Decreto Flussi work visa has limited quotas, and low salary does not meet EU Blue Card threshold.
