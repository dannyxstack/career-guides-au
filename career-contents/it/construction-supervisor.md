# Construction Supervisor（Construction Supervisor）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3123 – Construction Supervisor。**

Construction supervisors oversee daily site operations, ensuring projects proceed on schedule, within budget, and to quality standards, coordinating workers and subcontractors, and enforcing safety compliance.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for construction supervisors?**
答：Salaries range from €25,000 to €55,000 per year, with entry-level at €25,000-32,000, mid-level €32,000-42,000, and senior up to €55,000+ depending on experience and project size.

**问：How can non-EU citizens work as construction supervisors in Italy?**
答：Options include the EU Blue Card (salary threshold approx. €32,000) or the Decreto Flussi quota-based work visa. Employer sponsorship and qualification requirements apply.

**问：What certifications are needed for construction supervisors in Italy?**
答：Registration as a geometra or engineer is required for design/supervision roles; safety coordinator certification is mandatory on site.
