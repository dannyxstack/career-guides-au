# Electrical Mechanics and Fitters（Electrical Mechanics and Fitters）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7412 – Electrical Mechanics and Fitters。**

Electrical mechanics and fitters install, maintain, and repair electrical equipment such as motors, generators, and transformers, playing a key role in industry and construction.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary growth prospect for electrical mechanics and fitters in Italy?**
答：Salaries increase with experience: mid-level up to €35,000, senior or supervisor up to €45,000, plus overtime and bonuses.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to Italy through this occupation?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires high salary or degree) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa; employer must prove no EU candidate available.

**问：Are specific Italian certifications required?**
答：Electrical safety certification is mandatory; vocational qualification (Qualifica) is not compulsory but beneficial.
