# Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified（Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：4229 – Client Information Workers Not Elsewhere Classified。**

This category includes clerks handling customer inquiries, complaints, and data registration, typically in call centers, public service counters, and private company reception areas.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，年薪需达门槛（约2.4万欧元），合同至少1年。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 年度配额，适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，无特定学历要求。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (Work Authorization)） | 雇主向移民局申请工作许可，获批后申请签证，适用于各类工作。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for client information workers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level salary is about €20,000-26,000, mid-level €26,000-32,000, senior up to €40,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for client information jobs in Italy?**
答：Through Decreto Flussi quota or EU Blue Card, requiring employer sponsorship. Blue Card has a minimum salary threshold.

**问：Is there career progression in this occupation?**
答：Yes, you can advance to team leader, customer service manager, or move to training, quality analysis roles.
