# Earthmoving and Related Plant Operator（Earthmoving and Related Plant Operator）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：8342 – Earthmoving and Related Plant Operator。**

Operates bulldozers, excavators, loaders and other heavy earthmoving equipment for construction, mining and infrastructure projects.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IT独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Quota Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，每年配额有限。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能工人，需大学学历或5年经验，年薪门槛约24,000欧元。 |
| Subclass 季节性工作签证（Seasonal Work Visa） | 适用于短期建筑项目，有效期最长9个月。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for earthmoving plant operators in Italy?**
答：Entry-level around €22,000-28,000 per year, mid-level €28,000-35,000, senior up to €45,000. Salary varies by region, experience and project type.

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa as an earthmoving operator in Italy?**
答：Mainly via Decreto Flussi quota work visa requiring employer sponsorship with limited annual quotas. EU Blue Card also possible (needs high salary or degree).

**问：What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
答：From junior operator to senior operator, foreman, then move into equipment management, training or self-employment.
