# Hand Launderers and Pressers（Hand Launderers and Pressers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：9121 – Hand Launderers and Pressers。**

Hand launderers and pressers manually clean, iron, and finish garments, typically working in dry cleaners, hotels, or laundries. The role requires attention to detail and patience, with low technical barriers.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for hand launderers and pressers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level positions earn around €15,000-18,000 per year (pre-tax), mid-level €18,000-22,000, and senior €22,000-28,000. Salaries are low with limited growth.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for hand launderers in Italy?**
答：Mainly through the Decreto Flussi quota work visa, requiring employer sponsorship. EU citizens have free movement. This occupation typically does not meet EU Blue Card high salary requirements.

**问：Is there room for career advancement in this occupation?**
答：Career advancement is limited; typical progression is to laundry supervisor or dry-cleaning manager, with few starting their own business. Overall career ceiling is low.
