# Cabinet-maker and related worker（Cabinet-maker and related worker）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7522 – Cabinet-maker and related worker。**

Cabinet-makers and related workers fabricate, assemble, and install cabinets, furniture, and wooden fixtures using wood and wood-based materials, typically in workshops or construction sites.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 非欧盟劳工配额签证，需雇主担保，名额有限，每年申请窗口期短。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 高技术移民签证，要求大学学历或同等经验，薪资门槛较高，通常不适用于木工。 |
| Subclass 自雇签证（Self-Employment Visa） | 适合计划在意大利开设木工作坊的申请人，需提交商业计划并证明资金。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a cabinet-maker in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about EUR 18,000-22,000, while experienced workers can earn EUR 32,000-40,000. Self-employed may earn more but with variability.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy as a cabinet-maker?**
答：Mainly via Decreto Flussi work visa (employer-sponsored) or self-employment visa. EU Blue Card is generally not suitable.

**问：What is the job outlook for cabinet-makers in Italy?**
答：The market is stable with demand from custom furniture and renovation. Skilled workers are scarce, benefiting job seekers.
