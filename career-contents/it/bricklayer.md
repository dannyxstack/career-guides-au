# Bricklayer（Bricklayer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7112 – Bricklayer。**

Bricklayers lay bricks, concrete blocks, and other materials to build walls and structures, playing a key role in construction. In Italy, demand is stable, particularly in urban renewal and infrastructure projects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 意大利每年发布非欧盟工人配额，砌砖工在技术工种列表中，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能工人，砌砖工通常不符合薪资要求（需高于平均工资1.5倍），但若为项目经理等职位可申请。 |
| Subclass 自雇工作签证（Self-Employment Visa） | 适合作为自雇工匠申请，需证明有足够资金和专业技能。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bricklayers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level: €18,000-€24,000; mid-level: €24,000-€32,000; senior: €32,000-€42,000 per year.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy as bricklayers?**
答：Mainly via the Decreto Flussi quota work visa with employer sponsorship, or self-employment visa. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：Is there a future for bricklayers in Italy?**
答：Stable demand, driven by infrastructure and energy retrofit projects. Career progression to supervisor or self-employment is possible.
