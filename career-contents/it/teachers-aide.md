# Teacher's Aide（Teacher's Aide）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：5312 – Teacher's Aide。**

Assist teachers in classroom activities, support student learning and behavior management, and care for students with special needs.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi） | 适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，受年度配额限制。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能人才，需大学学历且薪资达标，教师助理通常不符合。 |
| Subclass 自由流动（EU Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟公民可在意大利自由工作，无需签证。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Teacher's Aide？**
- 有耐心和爱心，喜欢与儿童相处的人
- 追求稳定工作节奏、不追求高薪的人
- 希望进入教育行业但无教师资质的人

**谁不适合学Teacher's Aide？**
- 希望高收入和快速晋升的人
- 不喜欢重复性工作或纪律管理的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a teacher's aide?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €18,000-€22,000, rising to €30,000 with experience.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become teacher's aides in Italy?**
答：Employers must apply for a work permit through Decreto Flussi, but quotas are limited and competition is high.

**问：What procedures do EU citizens need to work as a teacher's aide in Italy?**
答：EU citizens can freely move and work; only need to register residence.
