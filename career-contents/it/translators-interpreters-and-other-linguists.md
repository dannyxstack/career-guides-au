# Translators, Interpreters and Other Linguists（Translators, Interpreters and Other Linguists）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2643 – Translators, Interpreters and Other Linguists。**

In Italy, translators and interpreters provide language services for business, legal, medical, and cultural events, requiring at least two languages. Demand is steady due to immigration and globalization, but competition is high.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，要求大学学历及€33,000年薪（2024门槛） |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 针对非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，受年度配额限制，适用于低技能岗位，但翻译通常不适用 |
| Subclass 自雇签证（Lavoro Autonomo（Self-Employment Visa） | 适用于计划在意大利作为自由译者注册的申请人，需证明足够财力和资质 |
| Subclass 欧盟长期居留（自由职业）（EU Long-Term Residence (Freelance)） | 在意大利合法居住5年后可申请，允许自由工作 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for translators and interpreters in Italy?**
答：Entry-level translators earn around €20,000-€30,000 per year, while senior interpreters can reach €70,000. Freelancers face income variability and must pay VAT.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for translation in Italy?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (education and salary requirements) or self-employment visa (proving qualifications and funds). Employer sponsorship is limited; freelance route is common.

**问：Will AI replace translators and interpreters?**
答：AI handles basic tasks, but specialized fields like legal/medical and conference interpreting still require humans. Mastering AI tools and specializing is recommended.
