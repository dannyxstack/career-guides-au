# Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preservers（Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preservers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7514 – Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preservers。**

Fruit, vegetable and related preservers clean, peel, cut, cook, can, freeze or dehydrate fruits and vegetables to extend shelf life. This role is common in Italy's food processing industry, particularly in industrial canning and frozen food production.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the monthly salary for fruit and vegetable preservers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level positions earn approximately €1,500-€1,800 per month before tax, while senior supervisors can earn €2,500-€2,700. Seasonal contracts may be hourly.

**问：How can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa for fruit and vegetable preserver in Italy?**
答：Primarily through the Decreto Flussi quota program. The employer must submit a hiring request and obtain Nulla Osta, then apply for a work visa. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：Is it easy to find a job as a fruit and vegetable preserver in Italy?**
答：Demand fluctuates seasonally. Northern industrial parks and southern canneries offer more opportunities during harvest season (summer/autumn), with fewer positions off-season.
