# Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers（Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3311 – Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers。**

In Italy, securities and finance dealers and brokers buy and sell financial instruments (e.g., stocks, bonds, derivatives), execute trades for clients or institutions, and provide market analysis and investment advice. They work mainly in banks, investment firms, and stock exchanges.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for securities dealers in Italy?**
答：Salaries vary by experience and firm size: junior €28k-40k, mid €45k-70k, senior up to €80k-150k (including bonuses). Milan financial hub pays higher.

**问：How can foreigners get a work visa as a securities dealer in Italy?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires salary around €40k+) or general work visa (employer applies for Nulla Osta). Finance occupations are not subject to annual quotas.

**问：What is the career path for securities dealers in Italy?**
答：Start as junior analyst or trading assistant, progress to dealer/broker, then senior dealer, portfolio manager, or department head. Obtaining CFA certification boosts advancement.
