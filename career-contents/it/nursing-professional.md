# Nursing Professional（Nursing Professional）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2221 – Nursing Professional。**

Nursing professionals in Italy provide direct patient care, health education, and preventive services, working primarily in public hospitals, clinics, and community health centers as the backbone of the healthcare system.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for nursing professionals in Italy?**
答：Entry-level nurses earn around €24,000-€30,000 gross annually, while senior nurses may earn €38,000-€45,000, with public hospitals offering additional allowances.

**问：How can non-EU nurses immigrate to Italy?**
答：Via work visa (Decreto Flussi) or EU Blue Card, requiring Italian B2, bachelor's degree, registered nurse qualification, and employer sponsorship.

**问：What education is needed to become a registered nurse in Italy?**
答：A three-year bachelor's degree in nursing (€2,000-€5,000 per year), passing a national exam, and registration with the nursing council.
