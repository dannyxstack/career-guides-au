# Food Service Counter Attendant（Food Service Counter Attendant）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：5246 – Food Service Counter Attendant。**

Food service counter attendants take orders, handle payments, prepare and serve simple food and beverages in cafes, fast-food outlets, and canteens, forming the backbone of Italy's hospitality sector.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi） | 适用于非欧盟公民通过年度配额获得工作许可，通常要求雇主担保，餐饮业常见 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 通常不适用于此低技能岗位，因其年薪门槛较高 |
| Subclass 季节性工作签证（Seasonal Work Visa） | 适用于旅游旺季短期工作，有效期最长9个月 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Food Service Counter Attendant？**
- 喜欢与人交流、服务意识强的人
- 能适应快节奏、站立工作环境的人

- 不擅长应对繁忙时段和挑剔顾客的人
- 追求高薪或长期职业稳定性的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for food service counter attendants?**
答：Entry-level positions earn around €16,000-20,000 gross annually, while senior roles can reach €30,000, with tips significantly impacting take-home pay.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for this job?**
答：Mainly through the Decreto Flussi quota work visa with employer sponsorship, or through seasonal work visas for short-term employment.

**问：Is there career progression in this occupation?**
答：Yes, advancement to supervisor, store manager, or transition to roles like chef or bartender, with some opening their own food outlets.
