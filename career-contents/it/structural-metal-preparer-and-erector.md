# Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for preparing, assembling, and installing structural steel components such as steel beams, columns, and trusses on Italian construction sites.

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Starting as an apprentice or assistant, you can advance to foreman or site supervisor with experience; or transition through training to specialized areas like welding or prefabricated metal processing.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Precise dimensional measurement and calibration using BIM and laser scanning
- Optimize cutting and welding parameters for steel structures using AI software
- Drones automatically check structural alignment and bolt tightening status

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted crane and rigging configuration planning
- Risk prediction and construction plan optimization based on historical data
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted installation guidance and training
- Real-time tool status monitoring and predictive maintenance alerts.

**Human moat:**

- Physical operation and assembly in complex construction site environments
- Flexible judgment for handling non-standard or damaged materials
- Coordinate with the team and handle emergency situations
- Strict enforcement and supervision of safety regulations

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Building Information Modeling) software operation
- Drone operation and data analysis
- Programming and maintenance of welding robots
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools
- Digital literacy and basic programming concepts
- Data analysis in safety and quality management

**Key growth areas:**

- infrastructure
- renewable energy
- steel construction
- PNRR projects

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for structural metal installers in Italy?**
A: Entry-level annual salary is about 20,000-25,000 euros, mid-level 25,000-32,000 euros, and senior or foreman up to 40,000 euros. Northern industrial areas pay more than the south; overtime and allowances can increase income.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for structural metal fitters in Italy?**
A: Primarily through Decreto Flussi quota work visa, requires employer sponsorship. If meeting high-skilled requirements (e.g., degree or years of experience), can apply for EU Blue Card. EU citizens can move freely.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a structural metal installer in Italy?**
A: Typically requires completion of 3 years of vocational high school or apprenticeship training. A high-altitude work safety certificate is a legal requirement. Welding tasks require a welding qualification. Certificates are not mandatory but beneficial for employment.
