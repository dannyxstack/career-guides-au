# Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professional（Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professional）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2263 – Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professional。**

Protect and promote health and safety in workplaces and communities by assessing and controlling environmental and occupational hazards, ensuring regulatory compliance.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需大学学历和至少1.5倍平均工资的工作合同。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for environmental and occupational health professionals in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-€35,000, mid-level €36,000-€45,000, and senior €46,000-€60,000. Public sector salaries are lower but stable, while private sector, especially large industrial companies, offers higher pay.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy for this occupation?**
答：They can apply for an EU Blue Card (requires a high-salary job offer) or a Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requires employer sponsorship). The occupation is not on the shortage list, but demand is stable, and those with relevant degrees and experience have opportunities.
