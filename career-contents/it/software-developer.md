# Software Developer（Software Developer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2512 – Software Developer。**

In Italy, software developers design, code, and maintain computer software programs. Demand is strong across finance, manufacturing, and tech sectors due to digital transformation.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card (Italy)） | 适用于高技能非欧盟员工，要求大学学历+工作合同且薪资门槛约27,000欧元（2023年）。快速通道，可带家属。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 每年配额，适用于非欧盟员工。需雇主担保，流程较长，适合非蓝卡条件者。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (Work Authorization)） | 雇主向移民局申请工作许可，是获得工作签证的前提。审批约2-4个月。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for software developers in Italy?**
答：Junior: €28k-38k, Mid: €38k-55k, Senior: €55k-80k. Higher in the North.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate as software developers to Italy?**
答：Common path is EU Blue Card, requiring degree and job with €27k+ salary. Also possible via Decreto Flussi quota visa. Employer sponsorship needed.

**问：Is demand for software developers high in Italy?**
答：Yes, digital transformation drives demand. Fintech, manufacturing, and tech hubs (Milan, Rome) urgently need talent.
