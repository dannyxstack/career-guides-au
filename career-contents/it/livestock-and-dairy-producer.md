# Livestock and Dairy Producer（Livestock and Dairy Producer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：6121 – Livestock and Dairy Producer。**

Manages breeding, feeding, and health of livestock and dairy animals, overseeing milk production and farm operations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi） | 适用于非欧盟季节性农业工人，每年配额申请 |
| Subclass 欧盟自由流动（EU Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟公民可自由在意大利从事该职业 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Livestock and Dairy Producer？**
- 有农场背景或动手能力强的人
- 热爱户外工作和动物
- 愿意接受体力劳动和早起的作息

**谁不适合学Livestock and Dairy Producer？**
- 偏好城市环境和规律工作时间
- 对动物福利不感兴趣

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of livestock and dairy producers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around EUR 18,000-24,000, senior up to EUR 50,000, but subject to agricultural price fluctuations.

**问：How can foreigners obtain work permission for this occupation in Italy?**
答：Non-EU citizens need to apply through Decreto Flussi quota, while EU citizens have free movement.

**问：Are there career advancement opportunities?**
答：Can advance from worker to farm manager or self-employed owner, or shift to agricultural advisor or organic farming.
