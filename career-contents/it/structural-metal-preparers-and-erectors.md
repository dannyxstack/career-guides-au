# Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors（Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7214 – Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors。**

Prepare, assemble, and install structural steel components such as beams, columns, and trusses on construction sites in Italy.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for structural metal erectors in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €20,000-25,000, mid-level €25,000-32,000, and senior/foreman up to €40,000. Northern industrial regions pay higher than the South.

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa for structural metal erector in Italy?**
答：Mainly through the Decreto Flussi quota work visa with employer sponsorship. If highly skilled (degree or years of experience), you may apply for an EU Blue Card. EU citizens have free movement.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a structural metal erector in Italy?**
答：Typically requires 3 years of vocational high school or apprenticeship. A safety certificate for working at height is mandatory. Welding certification is needed if welding is involved. Certificates are not mandatory but improve employability.
