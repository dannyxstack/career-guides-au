# Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates（Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3257 – Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates。**

Inspect workplaces and public environments for compliance with health and safety regulations, assess occupational health risks, and provide preventive measures to protect public health.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IT独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for environmental and occupational health inspectors?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €24,000 to €30,000, mid-level from €30,000 to €40,000, and senior can reach up to €55,000. Public sector offers good benefits but lower pay.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa as an environmental and occupational health inspector in Italy?**
答：Through EU Blue Card (requires degree and contract) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requires employer sponsorship). Italian B2 level is usually necessary.

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：From junior inspector to senior inspector, then to team supervisor or regional manager. Also possible to move to private consulting or training roles.
