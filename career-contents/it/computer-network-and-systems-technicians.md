# Computer Network and Systems Technicians（Computer Network and Systems Technicians）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3513 – Computer Network and Systems Technicians。**

Computer network and systems technicians install, configure, maintain and support computer networks and systems, including LAN, WAN and servers, ensuring efficient and secure network operations for businesses.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Computer Network and Systems Technicians？**
- 喜欢动手配置网络设备、解决网络故障的人

**谁不适合学Computer Network and Systems Technicians？**
- 不喜欢与终端用户沟通、解释技术问题的人
- 不愿意接受短期合同或驻场工作模式的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for computer network technicians in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about EUR 24,000-30,000, mid-level EUR 32,000-42,000, senior up to EUR 55,000, above the Italian average (approx. EUR 28,000).

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy for network technician jobs?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires degree and salary ≥EUR 27,000) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer sponsorship, IT often listed as shortage).

**问：What certifications are needed to work as a network technician in Italy?**
答：International certs like Cisco CCNA and CompTIA Network+ are highly valued but not mandatory. For regulated engineering activities, passing the Esame di Stato is required.
