# Shelf Fillers — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 9334 (ISCO08)**

Entry-level role responsible for restocking shelves, organizing displays, and managing inventory in retail stores, supermarkets, and warehouses.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can advance from replenishment staff to department supervisor or warehouse manager, or move to procurement or logistics roles, but requires additional training and experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of retail automation systems
- Inventory data analysis and forecasting tools
- Operation of equipment such as forklifts and electric pallet jacks
- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling
- Basic Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Cross-functional team collaboration and coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Retail
- Logistics
- Part-time
- Automation

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with good physical health who can stand for long periods and handle goods.
- Needs flexible part-time work, such as for students or those with a second career.
- Job seekers who want to enter the retail industry quickly without high qualifications

**Who is not suited**
- Those aiming for high salary or fast promotion
- People who are not good at repetitive physical labor

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary of a shelf restocker?**
A: Junior pre-tax annual salary is approximately €16,000–€20,000, increasing to €24,000–€28,000 with experience, but is often at minimum wage levels.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through this occupation?**
A: Mainly through Decreto Flussi quota work visas, but quotas are limited and low-skilled positions are difficult to obtain. EU citizens can freely move.

**Q: Are there advancement opportunities for shelf stockers?**
A: Can be promoted to department supervisor or warehouse manager, but usually require additional training and work experience; advancement paths are limited.
