# Audiologists and Speech Therapists（Audiologists and Speech Therapists）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2266 – Audiologists and Speech Therapists。**

In Italy, audiologists and speech therapists assess, diagnose, and treat hearing and speech disorders, working in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and private practices.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for audiologists and speech therapists in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €24,000-30,000, mid-level €32,000-40,000, senior €42,000-55,000. Private practice may pay higher, while public hospitals offer stable benefits.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy to work as audiologists and speech therapists?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires job offer and degree) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer sponsorship). It is recommended to obtain Italian B2 certification and professional registration.
