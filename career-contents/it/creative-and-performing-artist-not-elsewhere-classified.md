# Creative and Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified（Creative and Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2659 – Creative and Performing Artist Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Self-employed or employed artists in visual arts, music, dance, theater, etc., showcasing work through galleries, performances, and digital platforms.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于非欧盟高技能人才，需工作合同且年薪至少约4.8万欧元（2024标准），自由度较高。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 年度配额工作签证，通常在年初开放，适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Self-Employment Visa（Self-Employment Visa） | 适用于自由职业艺术家，需证明有稳定收入来源和可持续项目，需提供详细商业计划。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average income level of artists in Italy?**
答：Income varies widely: junior freelancers earn €12k-20k, mid-to-senior can reach €35k-60k, but most have unstable income.

**问：How can non-EU artists migrate to Italy?**
答：Via self-employment visa (business plan required), EU Blue Card (high-salary contract), or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer sponsorship).

**问：What are the career paths for artists in Italy?**
答：Start as freelancer, then evolve to gallery artist, art director, curator, or educator; digital media can broaden reach.
