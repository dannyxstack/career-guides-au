# Chemical Engineering Technician（Chemical Engineering Technician）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3116 – Chemical Engineering Technician。**

Professional technician assisting engineers in process design, equipment operation, quality control, and lab testing in chemical, petrochemical, and pharmaceutical industries.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card (Italy)） | 面向高技能非欧盟公民，要求本科以上学历及高于平均工资的合同，年薪门槛约25,000欧元。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 年度配额制，针对非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，适合中等技能岗位。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta (工作许可)（Nulla Osta for Employment） | 雇主向移民局申请，证明无本地人选，获批后可申请工作签证，流程约2-4个月。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for chemical engineering technicians in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €24,000-30,000, mid-level €30,000-38,000, senior up to €48,000. Higher in northern industrial areas.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through this occupation?**
答：Options include EU Blue Card (requires degree and high salary) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer sponsorship required). Recommended to secure a job first.

**问：Is there career progression for this occupation?**
答：Yes, to senior technician, process supervisor, or engineer through further education.
