# Glass Maker, Cutter, Grinder and Finisher（Glass Maker, Cutter, Grinder and Finisher）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7315 – Glass Maker, Cutter, Grinder and Finisher。**

Glass makers melt and shape glass, while cutters, grinders, and finishers perform cutting, grinding, polishing, and decorative tasks for construction, furniture, and decorative sectors.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 针对非欧盟劳工的年度配额，需雇主担保，适用于低技能岗位，玻璃行业有时列入名单。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 仅适用于高度专业化岗位，玻璃制造工通常不符合收入门槛。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for glass makers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level earns €18,000–€24,000/year, mid-level €24,000–€32,000, senior €32,000–€40,000. Varies by region, experience, and company size.

**问：How can foreigners get a work visa for glass making in Italy?**
答：Mainly via Decreto Flussi work visa with employer sponsorship within annual quotas. EU Blue Card not applicable. Check if the occupation is listed.

**问：What are the prospects for the glass making industry in Italy?**
答：The industry is slowly declining, but artistic and high-end custom glass maintains stable demand. Automation replaces some roles, but hand-finishing remains needed.
