# Financial Analyst（Financial Analyst）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2413 – Financial Analyst。**

Financial analysts in Italy provide financial analysis, investment evaluation, and market research for investment banks, asset management firms, and corporations, playing a key role in the financial sector.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Financial Analyst？**
- 对金融市场有浓厚兴趣，具备定量分析能力
- 适应高压工作环境，善于沟通
- 愿意持续学习，考取CFA等专业资格

**谁不适合学Financial Analyst？**
- 不喜欢数字和数据分析
- 难以承受长时间工作和严格截止日期

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for financial analysts in Italy?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €30,000 to €45,000, mid-level from €45,000 to €70,000, and senior roles from €70,000 to €120,000 per year, with higher pay in Milan.

**问：How can foreigners become financial analysts in Italy?**
答：Typically through the EU Blue Card (salary threshold about €27,000) or employer-sponsored work permit. A CFA or related master's degree helps.

**问：What are the career prospects for financial analysts?**
答：Prospects are good with digitalization and green finance creating opportunities. Clear progression to investment management, risk management, etc.
