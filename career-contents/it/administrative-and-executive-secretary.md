# Administrative and Executive Secretary（Administrative and Executive Secretary）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3343 – Administrative and Executive Secretary。**

Provides administrative support to senior management, including schedule management, meeting coordination, document processing, and communication liaison to ensure efficient office operations.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for administrative secretaries in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €20,000 to €26,000, mid-level from €26,000 to €33,000, and senior from €33,000 to €42,000, depending on experience and company size.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for administrative secretary in Italy?**
答：Through EU Blue Card (requires university degree and high salary) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requires employer sponsorship). Alternatively, apply for Nulla Osta work authorization.
