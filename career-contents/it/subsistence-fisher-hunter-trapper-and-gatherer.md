# Subsistence Fishers, Hunters, Trappers and Gatherers — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 6340 (ISCO08)**

This occupation involves subsistence fishing, hunting, trapping, and gathering activities, with products primarily for personal or household consumption rather than market sale.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career development paths are limited, often through family inheritance or continuation within the community. A few practitioners may transition to roles in ecotourism guiding, traditional handicraft production, or natural resource management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using satellite images and weather forecasts to plan collection routes (AI replaces manual experience-based judgment)
- Identifying edible plants and animal tracks (computer vision replacing some identification tasks)
- Record and count catch volume (automated data entry replaces manual recording)
- Basic tool maintenance reminders (IoT sensors replacing manual checks)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizes fishing and hunting time windows by analyzing historical climate data through AI.
- Use drones or remote sensing images to quickly locate fish schools or animal migration paths.
- AI assists in predicting resource distribution, reducing ineffective exploration time
- Use a mobile app to record and analyze capture efficiency, continuously optimizing strategies

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making and immediate adaptability in harsh environments
- Deep experiential knowledge of local ecosystems and animal behavior
- Physical and mental resilience for high-risk outdoor work
- Skills in manually handling catch and performing simple processing

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (using smartphones, GPS, simple apps)
- Drone operation and image interpretation
- Basic data analysis (capture records, weather patterns)
- Knowledge of sustainable resource management
- Wilderness first aid and survival skills
- Small solar/portable power maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining
- Aging workforce
- Sustainability focus
- Rural depopulation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the approximate annual income of subsistence fishers or hunters?**
A: Junior practitioners earn about 8,000-12,000 euros annually; senior practitioners (also running tourism or handicrafts) can earn 15,000-25,000 euros.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy by working in this occupation?**
A: Very difficult. This occupation is not in short supply, and self-employment visa requirements are strict, requiring proof of sufficient funds and creation of economic value.

**Q: What is the future outlook for this occupation?**
A: Due to environmental policies and an aging population, employment numbers are expected to decline slowly. A small number of practitioners may transition to ecotourism or natural resource management.
