## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for other ranks in the Italian Armed Forces?**
A: Junior enlisted annual salary approximately €18,000–24,000, intermediate around €25,000–35,000, senior up to €50,000. Plus housing, medical, and other benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens become Italian soldiers via work visa?**
A: No. Armed forces positions are only available to Italian citizens; non-EU citizens cannot apply for work visas. Must first obtain Italian citizenship.

**Q: What is the career progression path in the Italian military?**
A: Starting as a soldier, you can be promoted to non-commissioned officer through assessments and training; outstanding performers may have the opportunity to enter officer school to become officers. Requires long-term service.
