# Woodworking Machine Tool Setters and Operators（Woodworking Machine Tool Setters and Operators）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7523 – Woodworking Machine Tool Setters and Operators。**

Set up and operate woodworking machines such as saws, planers, routers, and sanders to cut, shape, and finish wood and wood-based products.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IT独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能工人，年薪需达到阈值（2024年约33,000欧元），通常要求本科学历或同等技能。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi work visa） | 年度配额，适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，通常要求高中以上学历及工作合同。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (work authorization)） | 劳工部签发的工作许可，需雇主申请，审核劳动力市场测试。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for woodworking machine operators in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €20,000-26,000, senior up to €40,000, above national blue-collar average but lower than IT sectors.

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa for woodworking operator in Italy?**
答：Via Decreto Flussi quota work visa or EU Blue Card (if salary threshold met). Employer sponsorship, contract and qualification proof required.

**问：What is the career outlook for this occupation in Italy?**
答：Slow growth due to automation and imports, but opportunities in custom furniture and green building; experienced workers can move to CNC programming or management.
