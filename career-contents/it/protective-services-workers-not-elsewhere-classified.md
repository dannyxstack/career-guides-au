# Protective Services Workers Not Elsewhere Classified（Protective Services Workers Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：5419 – Protective Services Workers Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Includes bodyguards, security patrol officers, and control room operators not classified elsewhere, working mainly for private security firms or public institutions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟公民通过年度配额申请安保类工作签证，需要雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能安保管理人员，年薪门槛约39,000欧元（2025年标准）。 |
| Subclass 自雇工作签证（Self-Employment Visa） | 适用于设立私人安保公司的创业者，需满足最低投资金额要求。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for security personnel in Italy?**
答：Entry-level security officers earn around EUR 18,000-24,000 annually, while senior roles like bodyguards can reach EUR 33,000-45,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy for security work?**
答：Mainly through the Decreto Flussi quota visa with employer sponsorship. Senior managers may apply for an EU Blue Card.

**问：What are the career advancement paths in security?**
答：From patrol officer to team leader, operations manager, or transition to bodyguard and technical security consultant.
