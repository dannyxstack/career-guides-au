# Protective Services Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 5419 (ISCO08)**

Includes security guards, patrol officers, and control room operators, among other protective service workers not elsewhere classified, primarily employed by private security companies or public agencies.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

The safety officer career shows a mixed trend: AI automated monitoring and visitor management will compress entry-level jobs but enhance advanced analysis and emergency response capabilities; professional judgment and on-site interpersonal interaction form a moat, with clear upgrade paths.

Can progress from basic security guard to team supervisor, operations manager, or personal bodyguard. Possession of a firearms license or advanced first aid certificate allows transition to high-risk security.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- AI video surveillance automatically detects abnormal events and alerts
- Automated visitor registration and identity verification systems replace manual registration
- AI-driven patrol robots replacing fixed-route patrols

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI video analysis assisting real-time threat assessment and decision-making
- AI report generation tools quickly summarize event logs
- AI voice assistant supports emergency response communication and coordination
- AI training simulators improve exercise effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency judgment and crisis intervention ability
- Interpersonal communication and persuasion with clients, visitors, and law enforcement
- Legal compliance and licensing liability (e.g., use-of-force boundaries)
- Risk assessment intuition in complex physical environments

**Skills to build:**

- AI monitoring system operation and data analysis
- Basic cybersecurity knowledge (integration of physical security and IT)
- Emergency communication and coordination software (e.g., Duress system).
- Advanced training in conflict resolution and counter-terrorism awareness.
- Data privacy regulations and compliance (Australian Privacy Act)
- Digital reporting and visualization tools (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- Private Security
- Event Security
- Surveillance
- Tourism Safety

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with good physical fitness and strong adaptability
- People who enjoy discipline and teamwork
- People willing to work shifts (including night shifts)

**Who is not suited**
- People not suited for outdoor patrols or prolonged standing
- People who are not good at handling conflicts or emergencies

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of security personnel in Italy?**
A: Junior security officer annual salary is about €18,000-24,000; with advanced certification or working as a bodyguard, it can reach €33,000-45,000.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through security jobs?**
A: Primarily through Decreto Flussi quota work visa, requiring employer sponsorship. Senior managers can apply for an EU Blue Card.

**Q: What are the career advancement paths in security?**
A: Can progress from patrol security to team supervisor, operations manager, or transition to personal bodyguard or technical security consultant.
