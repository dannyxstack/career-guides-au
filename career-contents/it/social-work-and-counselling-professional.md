# Social Work and Counselling Professional（Social Work and Counselling Professional）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2635 – Social Work and Counselling Professional。**

Social work and counselling professionals provide psychological support and crisis intervention to individuals, families, and communities, addressing social issues. They work in hospitals, schools, and public service institutions in Italy.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for social workers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level salary in public sector is around €22,000-28,000, while senior professionals can earn €35,000-45,000. Private practice incomes vary.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for social work in Italy?**
答：Options include the EU Blue Card (requires high-salary contract) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer-sponsored, occupation on shortage list). Social work is in shortage, increasing quota chances.
