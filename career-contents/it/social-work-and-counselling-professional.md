# Social Work and Counselling Professional — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 2635 (ISCO08)**

Social work and counseling professionals provide psychological support and crisis intervention to individuals, families, and communities, helping solve social issues. They work in hospitals, schools, and public service agencies in Italy.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level positions such as social assistant can advance to senior social worker or team supervisor, and further to project manager or policy advisor. Specialized counselors can establish private practices or serve as clinical supervisors.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate case reports and progress records
- Initial psychological assessment based on standardized questionnaires
- Crisis hotline initial screening and information triage
- Scheduling appointments and case management system operation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze social media data for early warning of community mental health risks
- Using virtual reality therapy to assist in PTSD treatment
- AI-supported multilingual automatic transcription and sentiment analysis improve consultation efficiency.
- Data-driven case management optimizes resource allocation.

**Human moat:**

- Establishes deep trust relationships and empathetic interaction
- Handling Ethical Dilemmas and Complex Legal Cases
- Flexible communication strategies in multicultural contexts

**Skills to build:**

- Digital literacy and use of AI tools (e.g., data analysis, VR therapy)
- Trauma-Informed Care and Crisis Intervention in Practice
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration skills (with healthcare and education sectors)
- Research evaluation and evidence-based practice methods
- Self-care and burnout management
- Multicultural competence and interpretation of Italian local policies

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Mental health awareness
- Social inclusion
- Community services expansion

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with empathy and strong communication skills.
- People who wish to work in social services and help vulnerable groups
- For those who can handle high work pressure and manage complex cases.

**Who is not suited**
- People who are not good at handling emotional conflicts or crisis situations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for social workers in Italy?**
A: Annual entry-level salary in the public sector is about €22,000–€28,000, while senior positions can reach €35,000–€45,000. Income in private organizations or clinics varies widely.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for a social worker in Italy?**
A: Available via EU Blue Card (requires high-salary contract) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requires employer sponsorship and occupation on the list). Social workers are in shortage, making quotas easier to obtain.
