# Subsistence Fishers, Hunters, Trappers and Gatherers（Subsistence Fishers, Hunters, Trappers and Gatherers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：6340 – Subsistence Fishers, Hunters, Trappers and Gatherers。**

This occupation involves subsistence fishing, hunting, trapping, and gathering activities, with products primarily for personal or family consumption rather than market sale.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Lavoratore autonomo （Self-Employment Visa） | 适用于计划在意大利从事自雇活动（如自给性渔业）的非欧盟公民，需提交商业计划并证明足够资金。 |
| Subclass Permesso di soggiorn（Seasonal Work Permit） | 适用于季节性狩猎或采集活动，有效期最长9个月，需雇主担保。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Subsistence Fishers, Hunters, Trappers and Gatherers？**
- 生活在偏远农村或岛屿地区，有传统捕猎、采集技能的人群
- 愿意接受不稳定收入和艰苦工作条件的独立从业者
- 注重自给自足和传统生活方式的人

**谁不适合学Subsistence Fishers, Hunters, Trappers and Gatherers？**
- 追求稳定高收入和职业晋升机会的人
- 不愿在恶劣天气和偏远环境中工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual income for subsistence fishers or hunters?**
答：Entry-level practitioners earn about 8,000-12,000 EUR, while advanced ones (with tourism or handicrafts) can reach 15,000-25,000 EUR.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. It is not a shortage occupation, and self-employment visas require proof of sufficient funds and economic contribution.

**问：What is the future outlook for this occupation?**
答：Due to environmental policies and an aging workforce, numbers are expected to slowly decline. A few may transition to ecotourism or resource management.
