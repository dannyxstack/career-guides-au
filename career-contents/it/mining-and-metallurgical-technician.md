# Mining and Metallurgical Technician（Mining and Metallurgical Technician）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3117 – Mining and Metallurgical Technician。**

Mining and Metallurgical Technicians in Italy assist engineers in mineral exploration, extraction, and metal refining, operating and maintaining equipment to ensure safe and efficient production.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Mining and Metallurgical Technicians in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €26,000 to €32,000, mid-level from €33,000 to €42,000, and senior from €43,000 to €55,000, depending on location and experience.

**问：How can non-EU citizens apply for a work visa as a Mining and Metallurgical Technician in Italy?**
答：They can apply via EU Blue Card (requires bachelor's degree and salary threshold) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requires employer sponsorship and annual quotas).

**问：What is the job outlook for this occupation in Italy?**
答：The outlook is stable, especially in northern industrial regions, with green transition driving demand for sustainable metallurgy and a shortage of skilled technicians.
