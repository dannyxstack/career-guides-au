# Child Care Services Manager（Child Care Services Manager）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：1341 – Child Care Services Manager。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for child care services managers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level around EUR 20,000-26,000, senior up to EUR 45,000 gross per year.

**问：What visa do foreigners need for this occupation in Italy?**
答：They can apply for the Decreto Flussi quota work visa or EU Blue Card, requiring employer sponsorship.

**问：What education is required to become a child care services manager?**
答：Typically a high school diploma plus vocational training, a bachelor's degree is advantageous.
