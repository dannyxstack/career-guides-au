# Garbage and Recycling Collector（Garbage and Recycling Collector）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：9611 – Garbage and Recycling Collector。**

Collects municipal waste and recyclables, operates garbage trucks, and ensures waste is transported to treatment facilities.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for garbage collectors?**
答：Entry-level around €18,000-22,000, senior up to €30,000, gross annual.

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa for this job?**
答：Mainly via Decreto Flussi quota work visa, requiring employer sponsorship and limited annual quotas.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities for garbage collectors?**
答：Yes, can become team supervisor or waste management coordinator with additional training and experience.
