# Gardener（Gardener）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：6113 – Gardener。**

Gardeners cultivate plants, maintain lawns, and enhance landscapes through pruning, planting, fertilizing, and pest control.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟季节性工人，需雇主担保，年度配额 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能园艺师，需大学学历和较高薪资 |
| Subclass 自由职业者签证（Freelance Visa (Lavoro Autonomo)） | 自主创业园艺师可申请，需证明经济能力 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Gardener？**
- 热爱户外工作和植物
- 体力好，能适应季节性高强度劳动
- 注重细节，有责任心

**谁不适合学Gardener？**
- 不喜户外劳动或天气变化
- 对植物过敏或体力较差

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for gardeners in Italy?**
答：Entry-level: €16,000-22,000; mid-level: €22,000-30,000; senior: up to €40,000+ per year.

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa for gardening in Italy?**
答：Typically via the Decreto Flussi quota work visa with employer sponsorship, or EU Blue Card for high-skilled roles.

**问：What is the career progression for gardeners?**
答：From entry-level to senior gardener, landscape supervisor, or self-employment.
