# Field Crop and Vegetable Growers（Field Crop and Vegetable Growers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：6111 – Field Crop and Vegetable Growers。**

Responsible for planting, managing, and harvesting field crops and vegetables, including land preparation, sowing, fertilizing, irrigation, pest control, and harvesting. In Italy, the profession is dominated by small to medium-sized family farms, with some integrating organic and sustainable practices.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Field Crop and Vegetable Growers？**
- 喜欢户外劳动和自然环境的人
- 具备一定土地或资金资源，愿意从事农业生产的人
- 对有机农业和可持续发展有热情的人

**谁不适合学Field Crop and Vegetable Growers？**
- 追求高收入和稳定办公室环境的人
- 无法适应高强度体力劳动和天气变化的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the income of field crop growers in Italy stable?**
答：Income is highly affected by climate, market prices, and EU subsidies, leading to fluctuations. Larger farms or those adopting organic practices tend to have more stable income, but overall it remains low.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through agricultural work?**
答：They can apply for a seasonal work visa under Decreto Flussi, or a self-employment visa to run their own farm. The EU Blue Card is less applicable due to lower salary levels in traditional agriculture.
