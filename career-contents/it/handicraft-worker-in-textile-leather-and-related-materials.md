# Handicraft Worker in Textile, Leather and Related Materials（Handicraft Worker in Textile, Leather and Related Materials）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7318 – Handicraft Worker in Textile, Leather and Related Materials。**

Handicraft workers in Italy create and repair products from textile, leather and related materials, preserving local traditions in small workshops.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for handicraft workers in Italy?**
答：Annual salary ranges from €16,000 to €38,000; entry-level €16,000-20,000, senior up to €38,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens work as handicraft workers in Italy?**
答：Mainly via Decreto Flussi work visa with annual quotas, requiring employer sponsorship.

**问：What qualifications are needed for this occupation?**
答：Vocational training or apprenticeship is typical; no mandatory certification, but skills certificates help.
