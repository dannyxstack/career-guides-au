# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for periodically reading utility meters such as water, electricity, and gas, as well as collecting cash or data from vending machines. The work involves outdoor activities, data recording, and basic maintenance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can progress from junior meter reader to senior technician or team supervisor, or transition to smart metering system installation and maintenance, or enter utility company data management roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings
- On-site inspection of measuring equipment faults (initial judgment)
- Generate manual meter reading reports.
- Reading meters along a fixed route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile apps or handheld terminals to assist with remote confirmation of abnormal readings.
- Identify potential leaks or tampering by analyzing consumption patterns via AI
- Use autonomous inspection drones or robots for meter reading in high-risk areas
- Provide energy advice to clients based on AI-generated energy saving reports.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair in complex on-site environments.
- In-person communication with clients over billing disputes or safety issues
- Decision-making ability in emergencies (e.g., gas leaks)
- Manual calibration and maintenance of old mechanical watch heads

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and anomaly detection
- Mobile device and specialized app operation
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic electrical/mechanical repair knowledge
- Drone or robot operation and maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart meter transition
- Data entry
- Field service
- Utility maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for meter readers in Italy?**
A: Junior annual salary is about €18,000-22,000, while mid-to-senior roles can reach €26,000-30,000. Salary growth potential is limited.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through this occupation?**
A: It is quite difficult because this occupation is not classified as a high-skilled shortage. Consider the Decreto Flussi quota work visa, but opportunities are limited.

**Q: Will this occupation be replaced by automation in the future?**
A: High possibility; smart metering systems are becoming widespread, gradually reducing demand for traditional meter reading jobs.
