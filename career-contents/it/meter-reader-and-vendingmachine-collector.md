# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector（Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：9623 – Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector。**

Responsible for periodically reading utility meters (water, electricity, gas) and collecting cash or data from vending machines. Involves outdoor work, data recording, and basic maintenance.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Quota Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟公民，需雇主担保，每年名额有限。通常要求高中以上学历。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 仅适用于高技能职业，此职业通常不符合薪资门槛（需高于意大利平均工资1.5倍）。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for meter readers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €18,000-22,000, rising to €26,000-30,000 for senior roles. Limited growth potential.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy through this occupation?**
答：Difficult, as it's not a high-skilled shortage occupation. Decreto Flussi quota work visa is possible but limited.

**问：Will this job be automated in the future?**
答：Highly likely, as smart metering expands, manual meter reading demand will decline.
