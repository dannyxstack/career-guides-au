# Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician（Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3211 – Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician。**

Operates and maintains medical imaging equipment (X-ray, CT, MRI) and therapeutic devices, ensuring safe and effective diagnostic and treatment procedures, collaborating with radiologists and healthcare teams.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for imaging technicians in Italy?**
答：Entry-level around €24,000-30,000, mid-level €32,000-40,000, senior up to €52,000 gross per year.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become imaging technicians in Italy?**
答：Obtain an Italian-recognized degree, pass the national exam for registration, and apply for a work visa such as EU Blue Card or Decreto Flussi.
