# Shop Supervisor（Shop Supervisor）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：5222 – Shop Supervisor。**

Shop supervisors oversee daily retail store operations, manage sales teams, ensure customer service quality, and assist in achieving sales targets. In Italy, this role is core to retail management.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi） | 非欧盟公民通过年度配额申请，需雇主担保，适用于零售岗位。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能工作者，要求年薪至少约3.9万欧元，主管职位通常不达标。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Shop Supervisor？**
- 喜欢与人沟通、善于管理团队的人群
- 对零售行业有热情、能适应快速节奏的工作
- 有志于在零售领域长期发展并逐步晋升的人

**谁不适合学Shop Supervisor？**
- 不善于处理顾客投诉和压力情况的人
- 追求高收入或快速移民的群体

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for shop supervisors in Italy?**
答：Pre-tax annual salary ranges from €22,000 to €45,000, with junior €22k-27k, mid €27k-35k, senior €35k-45k. Higher in Northern Italy.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa for shop supervisor in Italy?**
答：Mainly through Decreto Flussi quota work visa requiring employer sponsorship. EU Blue Card is also possible but the salary threshold is high.

**问：What are the career prospects for shop supervisors?**
答：Can advance to store manager, regional manager, or headquarters functions. However, online retail impact requires upskilling.
