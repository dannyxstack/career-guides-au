# Engineering Professional Not Elsewhere Classified（Engineering Professional Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2149 – Engineering Professional Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Covers engineering roles not elsewhere classified, including biomedical, environmental, aerospace engineers, engaged in R&D, design, consulting, or management.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能非欧盟公民，需大学学历及高于平均工资的合同，有效期1-4年，可携家属。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 年度配额，适用于非欧盟工人，需雇主担保，配额有限，适合蓝领或技术工人。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (Work Authorization)） | 雇主需向劳工局申请工作许可，审核劳动合同和资质，之后可申请工作签证。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for engineering professionals in Italy?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-35,000, mid-level €35,000-50,000, senior €50,000-70,000 gross per year, depending on sector and location.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy for work?**
答：Common paths include EU Blue Card (requires high salary and degree) and Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer-sponsored). Nulla Osta work authorization is required first.

**问：Do engineering professionals need registration?**
答：Yes, for certain regulated roles, registration with the Order of Engineers is mandatory, requiring a national exam and recognized degree.
