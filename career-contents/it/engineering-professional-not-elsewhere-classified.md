# Engineering Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Italy

**Occupation code: 2149 (ISCO08)**

Covers engineering roles not specifically classified, including but not limited to biomedical, environmental, aerospace, and other engineers engaged in R&D, design, consulting, or management.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for engineering professionals in Italy?**
A: Junior approximately €28,000–€35,000, intermediate €35,000–€50,000, senior €50,000–€70,000, depending on industry and region.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy for work?**
A: Common pathways include the EU Blue Card (requiring high salary and qualifications) and the Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requiring employer sponsorship). A work permit (Nulla Osta) must be obtained first.

**Q: Do engineering professionals need to be registered?**
A: Yes, engaging in certain regulated engineering roles requires registration with the engineering association, passing a national exam, and holding a recognized qualification.
