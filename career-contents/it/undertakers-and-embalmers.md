# Undertakers and Embalmers（Undertakers and Embalmers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：5163 – Undertakers and Embalmers。**

In Italy, undertakers and embalmers manage deceased body handling, embalming, cosmetic restoration, and funeral arrangements under strict health and safety regulations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for undertakers in Italy?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 20,000-25,000 EUR, mid-level 25,000-35,000 EUR, and senior manager or self-employed can reach 35,000-50,000 EUR.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy as undertakers?**
答：Yes, but via Decreto Flussi quota work visa (requires employer sponsorship) or EU Blue Card (requires degree and high salary, unlikely).

**问：What are the promotion opportunities in Italian funeral industry?**
答：You can advance from assistant to licensed undertaker, then to funeral home manager or start your own business.
