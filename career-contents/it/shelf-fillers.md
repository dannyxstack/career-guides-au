# Shelf Fillers（Shelf Fillers）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：9334 – Shelf Fillers。**

Workers who restock shelves, arrange merchandise, and manage inventory in retail stores, supermarkets, and warehouses.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Quota Work Visa） | 非欧盟公民通过年度配额申请，需雇主担保，适用于低技能岗位。 |
| Subclass 欧盟自由流动（EU Free Movement） | 欧盟公民可自由工作，无需额外签证。 |
| Subclass 季节性工作签证（Seasonal Work Visa） | 短期零售旺季（如圣诞）可申请，最长9个月。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Shelf Fillers？**
- 体力良好、能长时间站立和搬运货物的人
- 需要灵活兼职工作，如学生或第二职业者
- 希望快速进入零售行业、无需高学历的求职者

**谁不适合学Shelf Fillers？**
- 希望高薪或快速晋升的人
- 不擅长重复性体力劳动的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for shelf fillers?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €16,000-20,000 gross per year, increasing to €24,000-28,000 for experienced workers, often at minimum wage.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Mainly via Decreto Flussi quota work visa, but quotas are limited and low-skilled positions face challenges. EU citizens can freely move.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities for shelf fillers?**
答：Yes, to department supervisor or warehouse manager, but requires additional training and experience; career ladder is limited.
