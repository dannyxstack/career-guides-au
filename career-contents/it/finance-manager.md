# Finance Manager（Finance Manager）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：1211 – Finance Manager。**

Senior management role responsible for financial planning, budgeting, reporting, and risk management.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card (Italy)） | 适用于高技能非欧盟工作者，要求年薪达标（约2.8万欧元）。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 非欧盟工作者通过年度配额申请，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (Work Authorization)） | 雇主向劳工局申请工作许可，证明当地无合适人选。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the net salary of a finance manager in Italy?**
答：About 50-60% after taxes and social contributions for mid-level income.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become finance managers in Italy?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (high salary) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of local labor shortage.

**问：What is the prospect for finance managers in Italy?**
答：Digitalization and ESG reporting create new opportunities, but competition remains.
