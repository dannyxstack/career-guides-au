# Retail and Wholesale Trade Manager（Retail and Wholesale Trade Manager）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：1420 – Retail and Wholesale Trade Manager。**

Manage daily operations of retail or wholesale stores, including sales, inventory, staff, and customer service, ensuring profitability and efficiency.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IT独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需大学学历和高于平均工资的合同，年薪门槛约24,000欧元。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 年度配额工作签证，适用于非欧盟公民，需雇主担保，通常用于低技能或非紧缺职业。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (Work Authorization)） | 工作许可，需雇主向移民局申请，证明无法在欧盟内找到合适人选。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for Retail and Wholesale Trade Managers in Italy?**
答：Depending on experience, annual salary ranges from €25,000 (entry-level) to €80,000 (senior), with a median around €40,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for retail manager positions in Italy?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (requires degree and high salary) or Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer sponsorship). Getting a job offer first is recommended.

**问：What are the promotion opportunities for retail managers?**
答：Can advance to regional manager, operations director, or move into e-commerce management, supply chain. Starting own business is also common.
