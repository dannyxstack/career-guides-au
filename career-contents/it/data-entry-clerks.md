# Data Entry Clerks（Data Entry Clerks）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：4132 – Data Entry Clerks。**

Data Entry Clerks input data from paper or electronic sources into databases or spreadsheets, ensuring accuracy and completeness. In Italy, these roles are common in administrative, financial, and public service sectors.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟公民，需雇主担保，每年配额有限，通常针对低技能岗位。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能岗位，数据录入员通常不符合薪资门槛（年薪需约3.5万欧元以上）。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for data entry clerks in Italy?**
答：Entry-level data entry clerks earn about €18,000-22,000 annually, mid-level €22,000-28,000, and senior up to €35,000. Salaries vary by region, industry, and experience.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy as data entry clerks?**
答：The main route is the Decreto Flussi work visa, requiring employer sponsorship and annual quota availability. The EU Blue Card is not applicable due to salary thresholds.
