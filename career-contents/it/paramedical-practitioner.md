# Paramedical Practitioner（Paramedical Practitioner）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2240 – Paramedical Practitioner。**

In Italy, paramedical practitioners include physiotherapists, speech therapists, occupational therapists, etc., providing rehabilitation and therapy services in public and private healthcare settings.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for paramedical practitioners in Italy?**
答：Salaries vary by region, experience, and employer. Entry-level in public hospitals earn around €24,000-30,000 per year, while senior or private practitioners can earn €40,000-55,000.

**问：How can a non-EU citizen obtain a work permit for paramedical work in Italy?**
答：Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card (requires a degree and job offer with salary above €24,000) or the annual Decreto Flussi quota work visa (employer-sponsored).

**问：What are the requirements for foreign qualification recognition in Italy?**
答：Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Italian Ministry of Education (Dichiarazione di Valore) and pass the national licensing exam to practice.
