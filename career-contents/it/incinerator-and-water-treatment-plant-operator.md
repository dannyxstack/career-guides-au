# Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operator（Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operator）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：3132 – Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operator。**

Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operators in Italy monitor and operate waste incineration and water treatment equipment, ensuring compliance with environmental standards and public health safety. This role is vital for infrastructure maintenance.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for water treatment plant operators in Italy?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €22,000 to €26,000, mid-level €27,000–€33,000, and senior up to €40,000 per year (pre-tax). Pay varies by region, plant size, and experience.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for water treatment operator jobs in Italy?**
答：Options include the EU Blue Card (requires degree or 5 years experience) or Decreto Flussi work quota visa (needs employer sponsorship). The employer must first try to recruit locally.

**问：What certifications are required for this job in Italy?**
答：A regional professional qualification certificate and safety training certificate are mandatory. Specialized water treatment certification is optional but beneficial. High school or vocational education is typically sufficient.
