# Subsistence Livestock Farmer（Subsistence Livestock Farmer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：6320 – Subsistence Livestock Farmer。**

Raises livestock on a small scale to meet basic household needs, typically in remote mountainous or rural areas of Italy. Tasks include feeding, grazing, health care, and basic farm maintenance.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在IT技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟农业工人，需雇主担保及年度配额。自给性农民通常不满足条件，除非转为商业农场。 |
| Subclass 欧盟自由流动（EU Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟公民可在意大利自由从事自给性农业，需注册为自雇人士。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of a subsistence livestock farmer?**
答：Income is low, typically €12,000-€35,000 per year, depending on farm size and sideline activities like agritourism.

**问：Can non-EU citizens work in this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. It is not classified as high-skilled; Decreto Flussi quotas apply and self-subsistence farms rarely meet sponsorship requirements. EU citizens have free movement.
