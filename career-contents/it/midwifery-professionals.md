# Midwifery Professionals（Midwifery Professionals）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：2222 – Midwifery Professionals。**

Midwifery professionals provide prenatal, intrapartum, and postnatal care to expectant mothers, assist in natural childbirth, and handle emergencies. In Italy, it is a regulated healthcare profession, working in hospitals, clinics, and community health centers.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在IT独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能工作者，需大学学历和年薪门槛（约28,000欧元）。助产士短缺行业可放宽。 |
| Subclass Decreto Flussi（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 针对非欧盟国家配额工作签证，需雇主担保。医疗行业优先。 |
| Subclass Nulla Osta（Nulla Osta (Work Authorization)） | 雇主需向移民局申请工作许可，证明无法找到本地人胜任。 |
| Subclass Self-Employment Visa（Self-Employment Visa） | 计划开设私人助产诊所者适用，需提交商业计划。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for midwives in Italy?**
答：Entry-level midwives earn around €22,000-€28,000 annually, senior up to €50,000. Public hospitals offer stable income, private sector may pay more.

**问：How can foreign midwives immigrate to Italy?**
答：Via EU Blue Card or Decreto Flussi work visa, requiring Italian B2, degree verification, and license recognition. Rural areas have higher demand.

**问：What education is required to become a midwife in Italy?**
答：A 3-year bachelor's degree in midwifery and passing the national exam to obtain a license. Non-EU degrees must be evaluated.
