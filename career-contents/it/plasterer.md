# Plasterer（Plasterer）职业分析 · IT

**职业代码：7123 – Plasterer。**

Plasterers apply plaster, stucco, and gypsum board to walls and ceilings, playing a key role in Italy's construction sector.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Decreto Flussi 配额工作签（Decreto Flussi Work Visa） | 适用于非欧盟技术工人，每年有配额限制，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能工人，泥水工通常不满足薪资门槛，但高级技师可能适用。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average income of a plasterer in Italy?**
答：Entry-level around €16,000-€22,000/year, mid-level €22,000-€30,000/year, senior up to €40,000/year or more.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Italy as a plasterer?**
答：Mainly through the Decreto Flussi annual quota work visa, requiring an Italian employer sponsor and meeting labor market test requirements.

**问：What skills are required for a plasterer in Italy?**
答：Proficiency in plastering, gypsum board installation, and tool use; typically require middle school education and vocational training or apprenticeship.
