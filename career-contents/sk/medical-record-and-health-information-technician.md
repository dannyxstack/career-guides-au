## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Slovakia typically range from €1,200 to €1,800 gross per month. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced technicians in senior roles or larger hospitals can earn up to €2,200. Public sector salaries are often lower but include benefits like 13th salary.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Medical Records Technicians in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage (approximately €1,500/month in 2024). The employer must also obtain a labor market test.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Slovakia?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior or supervisory roles managing health information departments. Further specialization in health informatics, data analysis, or coding can lead to higher-level positions. Some pursue additional certifications from the Slovak Medical Chamber or universities to become health information managers or consultants.
