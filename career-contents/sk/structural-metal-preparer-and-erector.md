## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for structural metal preparers and erectors in Slovakia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries typically range from €1,000 to €1,400, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced workers may earn up to €1,600. Overtime and hazardous work allowances can increase total earnings.

**Q: How can a non-EU national obtain a work visa to become a structural metal preparer and erector in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a single permit (work and residence) applied for by their employer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.2 times the average gross annual wage. This occupation typically qualifies for the standard work permit route.

**Q: What is the job demand for structural metal preparers and erectors in Slovakia?**
A: Demand is steady due to ongoing construction and industrial projects. Skilled workers are sought after in automotive plants, bridge construction, and steel structures. Candidates with welding certifications have better prospects for stable employment.
