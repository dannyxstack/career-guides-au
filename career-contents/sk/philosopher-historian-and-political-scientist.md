## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. In academia, entry-level positions start around 1,000 EUR gross monthly, while senior researchers can earn over 2,000 EUR. Political scientists in public administration or NGOs may earn 1,200–1,800 EUR. Historians in museums or cultural institutions often earn between 1,000 and 1,500 EUR.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU philosophers, historians, and political scientists to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a standard work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold set by Slovak law. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the competent labor office.

**Q: What are the main employment opportunities for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Slovakia?**
A: Key employers include universities, research institutes (e.g., Slovak Academy of Sciences), public administration, NGOs, and cultural institutions like museums. Political scientists also work in government analysis or journalism. However, competition for academic posts is high, and fluency in Slovak is often required for roles involving local policy or heritage.
