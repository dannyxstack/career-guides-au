## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and region. Commissions or bonuses may be added. The minimum wage in 2024 is €750. Salaries are generally higher in Bratislava compared to other regions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Sales Demonstrators in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card targets highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (€2,189 in 2024). Sales Demonstrator roles typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU citizens may instead apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a confirmed job offer and a work permit from the competent labor office.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Sales Demonstrators in Slovakia?**
A: Experienced Sales Demonstrators can advance to senior demonstrator, team leader, or sales supervisor. Further opportunities include brand management, merchandising, or customer service management. Additional training in sales or marketing can open doors to higher roles in retail or promotional companies.
