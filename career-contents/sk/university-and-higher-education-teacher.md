## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for university teachers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for university teachers in Slovakia vary by rank and experience. Assistant professors earn around €1,200–€1,800 gross per month, while full professors can earn €2,000–€3,500. Public universities follow state pay scales, but private institutions may offer higher pay. Additional income from research grants or projects is common.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign university teachers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage in Slovakia. Alternatively, a national employment permit (temporary residence for employment) is available. Both require a university degree and a confirmed position at a recognized institution. The employer typically initiates the process.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a university teacher in Slovakia?**
A: A PhD or equivalent doctoral degree is essential for teaching at Slovak universities. For assistant professor roles, a PhD and some research activity are sufficient. Promotion to associate or full professor requires a habilitation (docentship) and further research output. Teaching experience in the relevant field and fluency in Slovak or English are also required, depending on the program.
