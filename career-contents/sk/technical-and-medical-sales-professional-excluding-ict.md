## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. Entry-level roles start around €1,800 gross per month, while senior specialists earn €4,000 or more. Commission structures can add 10-30% on top. The highest pay is in pharmaceuticals and advanced medical devices.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Technical or Medical Sales Professionals in Slovakia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, the EU Blue Card is a valid route. Requirements include a university degree (or equivalent experience) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€23,400 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit and temporary residence are possible for qualified applicants.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for Technical and Medical Sales roles in Slovakia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in a relevant field (e.g., engineering, pharmacy, or life sciences) is standard. Fluency in Slovak and English is essential; German is a plus. Prior sales experience is valued, and many employers offer product-specific training. Professional certifications (e.g., in medical sales) can boost prospects.
