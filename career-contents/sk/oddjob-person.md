## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for an odd-job person in Slovakia?**
A: Odd-job persons in Slovakia typically earn around the minimum wage, which is €750 per month in 2024, or an hourly rate of €4–€5. Pay may be slightly higher for specialized tasks but rarely exceeds €1,000 per month. Compensation is often informal and depends on the employer.

**Q: Can an odd-job person obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average wage. Odd-job persons typically do not qualify. Non-EU workers may instead apply for temporary residence for employment, which requires a job offer and a work permit obtained by the employer.

**Q: What are the working conditions for odd-job persons in Slovakia?**
A: Odd-job persons in Slovakia often work irregular hours with no fixed contract, performing tasks like cleaning, moving, or basic repairs. Employment is frequently informal, offering little job security or benefits. Workers must ensure they have proper documentation if non-EU, as undeclared work carries legal risks.
