## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a construction supervisor in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a construction supervisor in Slovakia ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, region, and project size. Senior supervisors in Bratislava may earn up to €3,000. Additional benefits such as bonuses, vehicle allowance, or accommodation are common.

**Q: What are the work visa options for construction supervisors moving to Slovakia from non-EU countries?**
A: Non-EU citizens require an employment visa or EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. €2,100/month in 2024). A standard employment visa is possible if the employer obtains a labor market test approval from the Central Office of Labour.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a construction supervisor in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a vocational diploma in construction or engineering is required, along with several years of relevant experience. Occupational safety and health (OHS) certification is often mandatory. Knowledge of Slovak building regulations and Slovak language proficiency are advantageous. Continuing education in project management can boost career advancement.
