## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Building Structure Cleaner in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for building structure cleaners in Slovakia vary by region and employer, but often range near the national minimum wage. As of 2024, the minimum monthly wage is €750. Experienced cleaners may earn slightly more, especially in larger cities like Bratislava, but wages typically remain in the lower income bracket.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Building Structure Cleaner in Slovakia through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers and requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Slovakia (approximately €2,100 per month in 2024). Building structure cleaners typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU nationals can obtain a work permit and temporary residence for employment if the position cannot be filled locally, with employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Building Structure Cleaner in Slovakia?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Slovakia perform cleaning and maintenance of exterior and interior building surfaces, including windows, facades, and roofs. They use specialized equipment like pressure washers, scaffolding, and safety harnesses. Tasks often involve removing dirt, moss, graffiti, and ensuring safety compliance. The role requires physical fitness and may include work at heights.
