## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for window cleaners in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for window cleaners in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and location. In Bratislava, wages tend to be higher. Many cleaners are paid hourly, with rates between €4 and €6 per hour. Overtime and bonus pay may apply for specialized work.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for window cleaners in Slovakia?**
A: Window cleaning is classified as a low-skilled occupation, so the EU Blue Card (for highly skilled workers) does not apply. Non-EU citizens need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The employer must obtain a labor market test and sponsorship. Citizens of EU/EEA/Switzerland require no visa.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for window cleaners in Slovakia?**
A: Experienced window cleaners can advance to supervisory roles, team leader positions, or start their own cleaning business. Specializing in high-rise or industrial cleaning may lead to higher pay. Some also transition into related fields like building maintenance or facility management with additional training.
