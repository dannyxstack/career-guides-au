## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic housekeepers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for domestic housekeepers in Slovakia are typically around the national minimum wage, which in 2023 is €700 per month for full-time work. Actual pay may vary based on employer, region, and experience, often ranging from €600 to €800 monthly.

**Q: Can a foreign domestic housekeeper obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card in Slovakia is reserved for highly skilled workers with tertiary education and higher salaries. Domestic housekeepers typically do not qualify. Instead, they may apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, requiring a job offer and work permit from the labour office.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a domestic housekeeper in Slovakia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not usually required for domestic housekeeping in Slovakia. Employers may prefer previous experience or references. Short courses in cleaning techniques or hospitality can be advantageous. Language skills in Slovak or English are often beneficial for communication.
