## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Slovakia ranges from €700 to €1,000, depending on experience and employer. Many positions are seasonal, with pay often based on hourly rates. Minimum wage for 2024 is €750 per month gross, so entry-level roles typically start at this level.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as garden and horticultural labourers in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and is not applicable for labourer roles. Non-EU nationals can apply for a work permit (temporary residence for employment) if the employer obtains a confirmation of possibility to fill a vacancy from the Central Office of Labour. Seasonal work for up to 9 months is also possible via a separate permit.

**Q: What are the main duties of garden and horticultural labourers in Slovakia?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers perform manual tasks such as planting, weeding, pruning, watering, and harvesting plants. They also prepare soil, apply fertilizers, and maintain gardens, parks, or agricultural land. In Slovakia, common tasks include working in orchards, vineyards, and nurseries, often during seasonal peaks.
