## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for lawyers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for lawyers in Slovakia vary by experience and location. Junior lawyers typically earn €1,200–€1,800 gross per month, while senior lawyers in international firms can earn €3,000–€5,000+ gross monthly. The average gross salary for lawyers is around €2,500 per month according to local job portals.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign lawyers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU lawyers can work in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card or a national work permit. Requirements include a valid job offer, a university degree in law (recognized by the Slovak Ministry of Education), and proof of Slovak or Czech language proficiency (B2 level). The Blue Card is for highly qualified positions with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly salary.

**Q: Do lawyers in Slovakia need to pass a bar exam to practice?**
A: Yes, to practice as an attorney in Slovakia, one must complete a three-year traineeship, pass the Slovak Bar Exam (advokátska skúška), and be registered with the Slovak Bar Association. EU lawyers with equivalent qualifications may have a streamlined process. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Ministry of Education.
