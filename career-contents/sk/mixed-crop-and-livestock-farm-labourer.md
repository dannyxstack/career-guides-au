## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Slovakia?**
A: The salary for farm labourers in Slovakia typically ranges from €600 to €900 per month gross, depending on region and employer. Minimum wage for 2024 is €750 per month. Seasonal workers may earn hourly rates around €4-5. Overtime and accommodation can supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Slovakia under the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card for highly skilled jobs, but farm labourer roles rarely qualify due to low skill level. More commonly, they apply for a national work permit (temporary residence for employment), which requires a labour market test. Seasonal work is possible under a short-term Schengen visa if employed by a registered Slovak employer.

**Q: What are the main duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Slovakia?**
A: Duties include planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops; feeding, herding, and monitoring livestock; cleaning animal housing and farm equipment; and maintaining fences and buildings. Work is often seasonal, requiring physical stamina. Knowledge of basic machinery operation is beneficial.
