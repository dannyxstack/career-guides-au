## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hand packers in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for hand packers in Slovakia typically ranges from €750 to €1,000, depending on location, experience, and employer. Entry-level positions start around minimum wage (€700 in 2024), while experienced packers may earn up to €1,200. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: How can I obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a hand packer in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a single permit (work permit combined with residence permit) to work as a hand packer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because it requires a higher education degree. The employer must apply for a labor market test and the permit at the local labor office. After approval, the employee applies for a temporary residence permit at the Slovak embassy.

**Q: What are the main duties of a hand packer in Slovakia?**
A: Hand packers in Slovakia typically pack products manually or with simple tools, inspect items for defects, label and seal packages, and prepare orders for shipment. They work in warehouses, manufacturing, or distribution centers. Tasks may include stacking goods, operating packing machines, and maintaining cleanliness. Attention to detail and physical stamina are required.
