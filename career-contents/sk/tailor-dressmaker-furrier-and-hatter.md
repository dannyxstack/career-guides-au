## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a tailor or dressmaker in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for tailors, dressmakers, furriers, and hatters in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while skilled professionals in custom tailoring or high-end shops can earn up to €1,500. Salaries in Bratislava tend to be higher than in other areas.

**Q: Can a foreign tailor obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: Yes, non-EU tailors can apply for an EU Blue Card in Slovakia if they have a valid job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage (approx. €2,200 as of 2024). They must hold a recognized higher vocational or university degree (or equivalent experience). Alternative routes include a work permit (for lower-skilled positions) or the Slovak National Visa for employment.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for tailors in Slovakia?**
A: Tailors in Slovakia can advance to roles such as master tailor, fashion designer, or production manager in garment factories. Many attend vocational schools or apprenticeships to specialize in bespoke tailoring, furriery, or millinery. Self-employment is common, with successful artisans opening their own boutiques. The industry is small but supports niche markets like historical costume and luxury alterations.
