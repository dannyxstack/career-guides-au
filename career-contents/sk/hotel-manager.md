## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hotel manager in Slovakia?**
A: Hotel managers in Slovakia earn an average monthly salary of €1,800 to €2,800 gross, depending on the hotel size, location, and experience. Entry-level positions may start around €1,400, while managers in luxury or large city hotels can earn over €3,500. Benefits often include accommodation and meals.

**Q: How can non-EU hotel managers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified managers with a university degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovakia. Alternatively, employers can apply for a work permit through the labor office.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a hotel manager in Slovakia?**
A: Employers typically require a degree in hospitality management or business, plus several years of experience in hotel operations. Fluency in Slovak and English is essential; German is a plus. Professional certifications like CHA (Certified Hotel Administrator) are valued. Practical skills in leadership, finance, and guest services are also crucial.
