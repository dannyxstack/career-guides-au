## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dieticians and nutritionists in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for dieticians and nutritionists in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Public sector roles offer lower pay, while private practice or specialized clinics can yield higher earnings. Salaries in Bratislava are typically higher than in other regions.

**Q: Can foreign dieticians and nutritionists work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, dieticians and nutritionists can apply for the EU Blue Card in Slovakia if they have a recognized university degree (at least three years) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (around €24,600 in 2024). The employer must register the vacancy. Alternative routes include the national work permit for non-EU citizens.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a dietician in Slovakia?**
A: To work as a dietician in Slovakia, you need a completed university degree in dietetics or nutrition (typically a 5-year master's program) from an accredited institution. Registration with the Slovak Chamber of Healthcare Professionals is mandatory. For EU nationals, diploma recognition under Directive 2005/36/EC is required. Non-EU qualifications must undergo a recognition process by the Ministry of Education.
