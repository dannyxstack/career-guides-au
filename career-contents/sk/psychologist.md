## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for psychologists in Slovakia ranges from €1,300 to €2,000, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and specialization. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior psychologists in managerial roles can earn up to €2,500.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for psychologists moving to Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU psychologists must obtain a work permit and temporary residence for employment. For the EU Blue Card, they need a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (approx. €1,800/month in 2024), a recognized university degree, and professional qualifications. Applications are submitted to the Foreign Police.

**Q: What is the typical career progression for psychologists in Slovakia?**
A: Psychologists start in entry-level roles in healthcare, education, or social services. With experience, they can specialize (e.g., clinical, counseling, neuropsychology), pursue private practice, or advance to supervisory or consulting positions. Continuing education is mandatory for license renewal, and many pursue PhDs for academia.
