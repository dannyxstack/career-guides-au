## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically diagnose common network issues and provide solutions
- Automatically generate network configuration and patch scripts
- Record work orders and categorize/escalate to senior teams
- User password reset and account management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI log analysis to quickly locate fault root causes
- Using network simulation tools to predict change impact.
- Early detection of anomalies through AI-driven monitoring.
- Using natural language queries to accelerate knowledge base resolution

**Human moat:**

- Root cause analysis and manual repair of complex faults
- Network architecture design and security policy formulation
- Effective communication with non-technical users
- Prioritizing urgent matters under pressure

**Skills to build:**

- Cloud network management (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Cybersecurity certifications (CISSP/CEH)
- Automation scripts (Python/Ansible)
- AI/ML basics and network analysis tools
- Hybrid network architecture design

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries typically range from €1,200 to €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience and location. Entry-level technicians earn around €1,200, while senior roles can reach €2,500. Bratislava offers higher pay, often 10-20% above the national average.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Computer Network and Systems Technicians to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree or 5 years of professional experience, plus a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average annual salary (approx. €1,500 gross/month). Alternatively, a standard work permit can be arranged by the employer, requiring a labor market test.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Computer Network and Systems Technicians in Slovakia?**
A: Technicians can advance to senior technician, network administrator, or IT manager roles. Certifications like Cisco CCNA or CompTIA Network+ boost prospects. Many transition into cybersecurity or cloud engineering. The demand is high, especially in Bratislava and IT hubs like Košice.
