## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Ships’ Engineer in Slovakia?**
A: Ships’ Engineers in Slovakia typically earn between €1,500 and €2,500 gross monthly, depending on experience, vessel type, and employer. Senior engineers or those on larger vessels may earn higher. Pay often includes allowances for time at sea.

**Q: What are the work-visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign Ships’ Engineers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU Ships’ Engineers need a valid job offer and can apply for an EU Blue Card if the salary meets the national threshold (currently approx. €1,500 gross monthly) and they have relevant qualifications. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible. Both routes require employer sponsorship and recognition of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a Ships’ Engineer in Slovakia?**
A: Ships’ Engineers in Slovakia must hold a valid certificate of competency (STCW III/1 or higher) and a medical fitness certificate. For inland vessels, a national license may suffice. Employers typically require relevant sea service and technical education. Additional training on specific vessel types may be needed.
