## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a software developer in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Slovakia vary by experience and location. Junior developers earn around €1,500–€2,000 gross monthly, while senior developers can earn €3,500–€5,000 or more. Bratislava offers higher wages due to demand. Total compensation may include bonuses and benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for software developers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU software developers can work in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, or a national employment permit (e.g., temporary residence for employment). The employer must obtain a confirmation of possibility to fill a vacancy from the Central Office of Labour. The EU Blue Card requires a recognised university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage.

**Q: What are the most in-demand skills for software developers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, companies seek skills in cloud technologies (AWS, Azure), modern programming languages (Python, Java, C#), and mobile development. Experience with agile methodologies and DevOps is highly valued. Specialists in cybersecurity and artificial intelligence are also increasingly sought after.
