## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly salary for gardeners in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced gardeners in urban areas like Bratislava may earn up to €1,500 gross. Seasonal variations and employer type also affect pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as gardeners in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers, typically requiring a university degree or higher qualifications. Gardening positions often do not meet the skill level for a Blue Card. However, non-EU citizens may apply for a work permit through the Act on Employment Services, which requires a labor market test and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a gardener in Slovakia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not always mandatory, but a vocational certificate in horticulture or landscaping improves job prospects. Many employers prefer prior experience. Knowledge of Slovak language is important for client interaction. Seasonally, demand peaks in spring and summer, with opportunities in municipalities, parks, and private estates.
