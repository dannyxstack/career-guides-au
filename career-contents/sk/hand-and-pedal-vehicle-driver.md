## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, hand and pedal vehicle drivers (e.g., bicycle couriers, rickshaw pullers) generally earn minimum wage or slightly above. As of 2025, the minimum monthly wage is €750. Hourly rates vary by employer, often ranging from €4.50 to €6.00. Earnings can be supplemented by tips, especially in tourist areas. Self-employed drivers may earn more but cover their own costs.

**Q: Can hand and pedal vehicle drivers obtain an EU Blue Card or work visa for Slovakia?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers, so it typically does not apply to hand and pedal vehicle drivers. For third-country nationals, a work visa (e.g., temporary residence for employment) may be possible if the employer obtains a labor market test and work permit from the Central Office of Labour. However, this occupation is rarely in shortage, making approval difficult.

**Q: What are the career prospects for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Slovakia?**
A: Career advancement is limited. Drivers may progress to supervisory roles like dispatch coordinator or operations manager within courier or logistics companies. Self-employment offers potential to grow a small delivery business. Skills gained, such as local route knowledge and customer service, can transfer to other driving or logistics positions, but formal training is minimal.
