## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwifery professional in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for midwifery professionals in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience, region, and type of employer (public hospital vs. private clinic). Senior midwives or those in specialized roles may earn higher.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for midwifery professionals to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU midwives can work in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a standard employment card (work permit) is possible, requiring a job offer and approval from the labour office.

**Q: What are the registration requirements to practice as a midwife in Slovakia?**
A: To practice as a midwife in Slovakia, you must hold a recognized midwifery qualification and register with the Ministry of Health or the Slovak Medical Chamber. Non-EU qualifications require verification (nostrification) and possibly additional exams. Proficiency in Slovak is typically required for licensing.
