- Customer needs collection and preliminary plan generation: AI chatbot can automatically gather preferences and generate activity frameworks

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for conference and event planners in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, conference and event planners typically earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross per month, with higher salaries in Bratislava. Senior planners or those in corporate events may earn up to €2,500. Salaries vary based on experience, employer size, and event complexity.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for conference and event planners to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as conference and event planners in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified roles with a salary threshold of at least 1.5 times the average wage, currently about €1,500/month) or a national employment permit, which requires employer sponsorship and a labor market test. Both routes allow family reunification.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for conference and event planners in Slovakia?**
A: Career progression includes moving into senior event manager, head of events, or roles in corporate event strategy. Specialization in luxury, international, or tech events is possible. Fluency in Slovak is often required for local roles, but international companies in Bratislava may use English. Professional certifications (e.g., CMP) can boost prospects.
