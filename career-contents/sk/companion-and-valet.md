## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a companion or valet in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Slovakia vary widely by employer and experience. Generally, they range from the national minimum wage (around €750 gross per month) to approximately €1,200 gross, with higher pay for those with language skills or specialized clients.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a companion or valet in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovakia is for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.2 times the national average. Companions and valets typically do not meet these requirements. However, non-EU nationals can obtain a temporary residence permit for employment if the employer secures a work permit from the labour office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a companion or valet in Slovakia?**
A: Formal education is not required, but employers often prefer candidates with a driver's license, first aid training, and fluency in English or German. Slovak language skills are important for local clients. Experience in hospitality or personal assistance is advantageous, and a clean criminal record is essential.
