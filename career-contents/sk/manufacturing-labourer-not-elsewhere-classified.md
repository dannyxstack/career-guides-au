## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a manufacturing labourer in Slovakia?**
A: Manufacturing labourers in Slovakia generally earn the statutory minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €750 per month. Many employers offer slightly higher rates, typically between €800 and €900 per month, depending on the region and industry.

**Q: What is the work visa route for a manufacturing labourer to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must pass a labour market test to prove no local candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is not applicable, as it requires a higher education degree or 5+ years of professional experience, which this occupation typically does not meet.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for manufacturing labourers in Slovakia?**
A: Career advancement is limited without further qualifications. With experience, workers may become team leaders or supervisors. Some employers offer internal training programs for machine operation or quality control, which can lead to semi-skilled roles.
