## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fitness instructors in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for fitness instructors in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,500, depending on qualifications, experience, and whether they work part-time or full-time. Instructors at premium gyms or with specialized certifications may earn higher rates.

**Q: Can non-EU fitness instructors obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals can apply for an employment visa or the EU Blue Card. Blue Card applicants must have a university degree or 5 years professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5x average gross wage, around €1,800/month in 2024). A work permit may also be required, processed through the Slovak Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a fitness instructor in Slovakia?**
A: Fitness instructors in Slovakia typically need a recognized certification from an accredited institution, such as the Faculty of Physical Education or international bodies like ISSA or ACE. Some employers require a bachelor's degree in sports science. Practical experience and CPR/First Aid certifications are also highly valued.
