## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Slovakia?**
A: The typical salary for a kitchen helper in Slovakia is around the national minimum wage, which is €750 per month gross as of 2024. Actual pay can vary depending on the employer, location, and experience, but rarely exceeds €900 gross per month for entry-level positions.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU kitchen helpers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens usually need a work permit, which requires a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for kitchen helpers as it requires higher education. Alternatively, a temporary residence for employment can be obtained if the employer sponsors the application and proves no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities and career advancement opportunities for kitchen helpers in Slovakia?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Slovakia assist with food preparation, cleaning, and dishwashing. They may also receive and store supplies. Career advancement is limited but possible: with experience, one can become a cook or kitchen supervisor. Vocational training programs can help improve skills and prospects, especially in larger hotels or restaurants.
