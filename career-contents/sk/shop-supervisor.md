## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Shop Supervisors in Slovakia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of Slovakia, the median gross monthly salary for shop supervisors (ISCO 5222) is approximately €1,200. Salaries range from €900 to €1,800 depending on experience, company size, and region, with higher pay in Bratislava.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Shop Supervisors in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a university degree or five years of professional experience, plus a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage (€25,258 in 2024). Shop supervisor positions typically fall below this threshold, so a standard employment permit is more common.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Shop Supervisors in Slovakia?**
A: Shop supervisors can progress to store manager, regional area manager, or operations roles. Many large retail chains in Slovakia offer internal training programs and promotion pathways, with increased responsibility and salary. Advancing often requires proven leadership and sales performance.
