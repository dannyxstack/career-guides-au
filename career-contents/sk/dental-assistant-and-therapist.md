## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dental assistant or therapist in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for dental assistants in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, with experienced dental therapists earning up to €1,500. Salaries are higher in private clinics and in Bratislava. Actual pay depends on qualifications, experience, and employer.

**Q: What are the work visa options for dental assistants and therapists from non-EU countries to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is an option for highly qualified workers (e.g., therapists with a university degree) if the job salary is at least 1.5 times the average gross wage in Slovakia. Alternatively, employers can obtain a standard work permit if the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a dental assistant or therapist in Slovakia?**
A: Dental assistants need a secondary vocational education in dental assisting. Dental therapists require a bachelor's degree in dental therapy from a Slovak institution or a recognized foreign equivalent. Both must register with the Slovak Medical Chamber. Continuous professional development is mandatory for license renewal.
