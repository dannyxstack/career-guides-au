## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary depending on specialization, experience, and employer. According to official data, the average monthly gross salary for legal professionals in Slovakia is around €2,000. Entry-level positions may start at €1,300, while senior roles can exceed €4,000. Large international law firms or corporate legal departments often offer higher pay.

**Q: How can non-EU legal professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU legal professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovakia (set annually). Alternatively, they may obtain a national employment permit (work visa) for a specific job. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Slovak labour office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a legal professional not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Most roles require a Master's degree in law (Mgr.) from a Slovak university or an equivalent foreign degree recognized in Slovakia. For unregulated positions like legal advisor or compliance officer, no additional exams are needed. However, to practice as an advocate (attorney), you must pass the bar exam and register with the Slovak Bar Association.
