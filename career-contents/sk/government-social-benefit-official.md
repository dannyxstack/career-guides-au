## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Government Social Benefits Officials in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries are set by public sector pay scales. Entry-level officials earn €800–€1,200 gross per month, while experienced officials can earn up to €1,800. Senior roles or those in higher pay grades may exceed €2,000. Actual pay depends on seniority, location, and specific responsibilities.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Government Social Benefits Officials in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if they have a university degree or 5 years of professional experience, and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average Slovak wage (approx. €24,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if the role is on the shortage list, but for public administration, EU Blue Card is more common.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Government Social Benefits Official in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a secondary education with a school-leaving exam (maturita) is required, but many positions prefer a bachelor's degree in public administration, social work, or law. Additional training in social insurance or benefits processing is provided on the job. Civil service exams may also be required for permanent roles.
