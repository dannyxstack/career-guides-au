## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental and occupational health professionals in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, typically ranging from €1,500 to €2,500 gross monthly for mid-level professionals. Senior roles or those in hazardous industries may earn higher, while entry-level positions start around €1,200. Public sector salaries are often lower than private sector. Additional benefits may include bonuses and allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU professionals in this field in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold and a recognized qualification. Alternatively, a national employment visa (povolenie na zamestnanie) is available for standard work, requiring employer sponsorship and a positive labor market test. Both routes lead to temporary residence.

**Q: Which industries in Slovakia commonly employ environmental and occupational health professionals?**
A: Key industries include manufacturing (automotive, electronics), mining, energy (including nuclear), construction, chemical production, and environmental consulting. Government agencies (e.g., Public Health Authority) and research institutions also hire these specialists. Occupational health roles are often found in large industrial companies with dedicated safety departments.
