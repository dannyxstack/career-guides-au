## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a child care worker in Slovakia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic, child care workers (ISCO 5311) earn a gross monthly salary typically ranging from 750 to 1,100 EUR. Actual pay depends on experience, employer type (public or private), and region. Entry-level positions start around 700 EUR, while experienced workers may earn up to 1,300 EUR.

**Q: Can a foreign child care worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Child care workers typically qualify for a temporary residence for employment, not the EU Blue Card, as the latter requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average wage. To work as a child care worker, apply for a single permit (work and residence) at the Slovak Foreign Police. A job offer is mandatory, and labor market testing may apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a child care worker in Slovakia?**
A: Employers usually require a secondary vocational school diploma in preschool pedagogy or a related field. Alternatively, a completed course in child care or a certificate from a recognized training program may suffice. Good communication skills and first aid training are often expected. Some positions require previous experience, especially in nursery schools or daycare centers.
