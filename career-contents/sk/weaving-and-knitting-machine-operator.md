## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Weaving and Knitting Machine Operators in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, the average monthly gross salary for Weaving and Knitting Machine Operators ranges from €800 to €1,200 depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced operators in larger textile factories may earn up to €1,400. Overtime and shift bonuses can increase total pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Weaving and Knitting Machine Operators in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or other work permits?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in this occupation through the EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and meet salary thresholds (at least 1.5x average gross salary). Alternatively, employers may obtain a work permit under the Slovakian Act on Employment Services, which requires a labor market test. The official route is through the Foreign Police for a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What are the main duties and training requirements for Weaving and Knitting Machine Operators in Slovakia?**
A: Operators set up, monitor, and maintain weaving and knitting machines, inspect fabric quality, and troubleshoot issues. In Slovakia, employers typically require a vocational certificate in textile production or related field. On-the-job training is common. Basic knowledge of Slovak is often needed, though some international companies may accept English. The job involves shift work and attention to detail.
