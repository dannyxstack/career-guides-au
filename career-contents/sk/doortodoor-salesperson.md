## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for door-to-door salespersons in Slovakia?**
A: Pay is often commission-based, with average monthly earnings between €800 and €1,500 gross. Some employers offer a low base salary (€400–€600) plus bonuses. Income depends on sales performance, product type, and company policies. Part-time roles are common.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as door-to-door salespersons in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or other routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and high salary, rarely applicable to this role. Non-EU nationals may obtain a temporary residence for employment if the employer shows the position cannot be filled locally. A work permit is needed, and quotas may apply. Self-employment is also an option.

**Q: What language skills are required for door-to-door sales in Slovakia?**
A: Fluency in Slovak is essential, as most customers are Slovak speakers. Czech or Hungarian may be useful in border regions. English is rarely needed in daily work but can help with internal company communication. Employers typically require at least B2-level Slovak.
