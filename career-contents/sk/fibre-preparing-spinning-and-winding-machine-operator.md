**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual inspection of yarn tension and thickness (replaced by AI vision system)

- Production data recording and report generation (IoT and ERP automatic collection)
- Repetitive loading, unloading, and joint operations (performed by robotic arm)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted predictive maintenance to reduce unplanned downtime.
- Optimize spinning process parameters using big data analytics to improve yield rate

- AR remote assistance for experts in complex repairs
- Intelligent material handling systems reduce manual handling and lower workplace injuries.

**Human moat:**

- Ability to diagnose and repair multi-device linkage faults

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operators in Slovakia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for these machine operators typically ranges from €900 to €1,300, depending on experience, shifts, and the employer. Entry-level positions start around minimum wage (€750 in 2025), while experienced operators in large textile factories may earn up to €1,400. Overtime and night shift bonuses can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU workers obtain an EU Blue Card for this occupation in Slovakia?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovakia is reserved for highly qualified workers with a tertiary education or at least five years of relevant professional experience. Fibre preparing and spinning machine operators typically do not meet these criteria, so most non-EU workers must instead apply for a temporary residence for employment, which requires a work permit and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What training is required to become a Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operator in Slovakia?**
A: Formal vocational training in textile production or a related field is common. Many operators complete a secondary vocational school program (e.g., in textile technology) or an apprenticeship. On-the-job training is also provided. Basic technical skills, physical stamina, and attention to detail are essential. No university degree is required.
