## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Human Resource Manager in Slovakia?**
A: Human Resource Managers in Slovakia typically earn €1,800 to €3,000 gross per month. Entry-level positions start around €1,500, while senior roles can exceed €3,500. Salaries vary by company size, location (higher in Bratislava), and experience. Bonuses are common, often 1-2 monthly salaries.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU Human Resource Managers wishing to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU HR Managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times the average wage (approx. €2,200). Alternatively, a national employment permit (temporary residence for employment) is available for roles meeting the labour market test. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are important for Human Resource Managers in Slovakia?**
A: A university degree in HR, business, or psychology is common. Professional certifications (e.g., CIPD, SHRM) are valued. Fluency in Slovak and English is essential; knowledge of German/Hungarian is an advantage. Experience with Slovak labour law, payroll, and collective bargaining is key. Digital HR skills and familiarity with HR software are increasingly important.
