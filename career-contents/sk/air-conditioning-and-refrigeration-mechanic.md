## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Senior specialists or those with certifications may earn up to €2,200. Overtime and bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: What official routes exist for non-EU nationals to work as Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit (povolenie na zamestnanie) and a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card may apply if the worker has a higher education degree or equivalent experience, though this is less common for this trade.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, completion of secondary vocational education in refrigeration or a related field, along with a professional certificate (e.g., from the Slovak Chamber of Commerce). Licenses for handling refrigerants and knowledge of Slovak safety regulations are mandatory. Practical experience is often valued by employers.
