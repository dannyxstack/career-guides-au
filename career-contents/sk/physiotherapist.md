## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a physiotherapist in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, physiotherapists earn a gross monthly salary between €1,100 and €1,800 for entry to mid-level positions, with senior roles reaching up to €2,500. Rates vary by region, with higher pay in cities like Bratislava. Public sector salaries are set by law, while private practice offers more variability.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for physiotherapists to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card (requires a university degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage) or a national employment permit (work visa) for jobs meeting labour market needs. Both require recognition of qualifications by the Slovak Ministry of Education.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements to practice as a physiotherapist in Slovakia?**
A: To practice in Slovakia, physiotherapists must have a recognized degree in physiotherapy, register with the Slovak Chamber of Physiotherapists, and meet continuous education requirements. EU/EEA qualifications are automatically recognized; non-EU qualifications undergo a recognition process by the Ministry of Education, which may include exams.
