## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a travel attendant in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a travel attendant in Slovakia ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, employer, and type of transport (e.g., airlines, train companies). Entry-level positions may start around €900, while senior roles reach up to €2,000. Overtime, allowances, and tips can increase total earnings.

**Q: How can a non-EU national secure a work visa or EU Blue Card as a travel attendant in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals must obtain a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, requiring a university degree or comparable qualifications and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (currently around 1.5× the average gross wage). Travel attendant roles may qualify if the salary threshold is met.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a travel attendant in Slovakia?**
A: Travel attendants in Slovakia ensure passenger safety and comfort on trains, buses, or aircraft. Duties include conducting safety checks, providing customer service, serving meals/drinks, assisting passengers with special needs, and handling emergencies. They also manage boarding procedures and maintain cleanliness. Good communication skills and multilingual abilities are advantageous.
