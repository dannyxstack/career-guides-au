## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Slovakia ranges from €1,000 to €1,200, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while experienced workers in specialized roles can earn up to €1,500.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign fumigators seeking employment in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign nationals typically need a temporary residence for employment, requiring a confirmed job offer and a work permit from the Central Office of Labour. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers, usually with tertiary education, and is not applicable for this occupation due to skill level requirements.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a pest controller in Slovakia?**
A: You must obtain a certificate of professional competence from the regional public health authority, which involves training in pesticide handling, safety, and regulations. The certificate is valid for five years and requires renewal. No specific higher education is mandatory, but secondary education is common.
