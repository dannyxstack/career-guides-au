## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' deck crew in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for deck crew on inland vessels in Slovakia generally range from €1,000 to €1,500 gross per month, depending on experience, vessel type, and employer. Overtime and allowances for night or weekend shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for deck crew in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. For an EU Blue Card, one must have a higher education degree and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage in Slovakia. Applications are submitted at the Slovak Foreign Police.

**Q: What are the main duties and qualifications for deck crew in Slovakia?**
A: Deck crew assist with mooring, navigation, cargo handling, and maintenance on inland vessels like those on the Danube. Qualifications include a valid watchkeeping license (e.g., STCW) and physical fitness. Relevant experience or training is often required.
