## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Buyer in Slovakia?**
A: The monthly gross salary for a Buyer in Slovakia typically ranges from €1,400 to €2,500 for junior to mid-level positions, and up to €3,500 for senior roles. Salaries vary by industry, experience, and location, with higher pay in Bratislava and in sectors like automotive or IT.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for a foreign Buyer to work in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign Buyers can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified work) or a national employment permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage. Alternatively, a temporary residence for employment is possible with a work permit.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for a Buyer in Slovakia?**
A: Buyers in Slovakia can advance to roles such as Senior Buyer, Category Manager, or Procurement Manager. With experience, they may move into strategic sourcing, supply chain management, or director-level positions. Many companies, especially in manufacturing and retail, offer training and certifications like CIPS to support career growth.
