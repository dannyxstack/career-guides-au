## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Slovakia?**
A: Earnings for subsistence farmers in Slovakia are modest and highly variable, often around the minimum wage (€750 per month in 2024). Most operate small holdings with low market integration, so income depends on season, crop yields, and livestock sales. Many supplement with off-farm work. Official statistics list average gross monthly pay for agricultural workers at approximately €1,100, but subsistence farmers typically earn less.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a work permit under the Act on Employment Services, which requires a confirmed job offer and labor market test. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because subsistence farming does not meet the required higher-education or salary thresholds (minimum €1,500 monthly gross in 2024). Seasonal work permits (up to 6 months) are also available for agricultural labor.

**Q: What does a typical workday involve for a subsistence farmer in Slovakia?**
A: Daily tasks include feeding and tending livestock (e.g., cows, pigs, poultry), milking, cleaning barns, and rotating pastures. Crop work involves planting, weeding, irrigating, and harvesting vegetables, grains, or fodder. Many farmers also maintain fences, repair machinery, and manage small orchards. Work is physically demanding and follows a seasonal rhythm, with long hours during planting and harvest.
