## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for insulation workers in Slovakia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for insulation workers in Slovakia typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,800. These figures include base pay and may vary with overtime or bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU insulation workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, requiring a confirmed job offer and a positive labor market test. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree, which typically does not apply for insulation work. Thus, a standard work permit is the usual route.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of an insulation worker in Slovakia?**
A: Insulation workers in Slovakia install and maintain thermal, acoustic, and fireproofing materials in buildings and industrial equipment. Tasks include measuring and cutting insulation, applying vapor barriers, and sealing joints. They work on new constructions, renovations, and energy-efficiency projects, often using materials like mineral wool, polystyrene, or foam.
