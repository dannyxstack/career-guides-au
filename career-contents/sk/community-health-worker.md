## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a community health worker in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for community health workers in Slovakia vary by employer and region, but typically range from €800 to €1,200 per month before tax. Public sector roles may follow standard salary tables, while NGO or private positions can differ. Experience and additional qualifications can lead to higher pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a community health worker in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovakia is generally for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage. Community health worker roles may not meet the salary threshold or qualification requirement. Alternatives include a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer and labor market test.

**Q: What are the main duties of a community health worker in Slovakia?**
A: Community health workers in Slovakia support public health initiatives, conduct health education, assist with preventive care, and link individuals to healthcare services. They may work for local governments, NGOs, or health authorities, focusing on marginalized groups like the Roma community. Tasks include home visits, organizing health campaigns, and promoting healthy lifestyles.
