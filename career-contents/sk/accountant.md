## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for accountants in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for accountants in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience, qualifications, and location. Senior accountants or those with specialized certifications may earn higher. Salaries in Bratislava are typically above the national average.

**Q: Can foreign accountants obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: Yes, foreign accountants can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree (at least three years) and a job offer in Slovakia with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times the average national salary. The application is submitted to the Slovak Foreign Police. Knowledge of Slovak language may be required by employers.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an accountant in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a university degree in accounting, finance, or economics is required. Many employers also look for professional certifications such as ACCA, CPA, or membership in the Slovak Chamber of Auditors. Proficiency in Slovak is often mandatory, and legal entities require registration with the tax office.
