## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for employment agents and contractors in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for employment agents in Slovakia vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,200 gross per month, while experienced agents can earn up to €2,500. Contractors' income fluctuates based on assignments. Additionally, performance bonuses are common in the recruitment sector.

**Q: What are the official work-visa options for employment agents wanting to work in Slovakia from non-EU countries?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as employment agents under an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. Alternatively, a temporary residence for employment is possible with a confirmed job and labor market test. Both routes require employer sponsorship and valid work permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an employment agent or contractor in Slovakia?**
A: There are no strict formal qualifications, but a university degree in human resources, business, or psychology is advantageous. Fluency in Slovak and English is essential. Practical experience in recruitment or sales is highly valued. Employment agencies must be licensed by the Central Office of Labour, so individuals typically work for licensed firms.
