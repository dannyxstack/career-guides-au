## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Building Architects in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for building architects in Slovakia typically range from €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, project size, and location (Bratislava often higher). Senior architects with 10+ years can earn up to €3,500. Additional benefits may include bonuses and paid leave.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign Building Architect to work in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign architects can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer, recognized qualifications, and salary at least 1.5x the national average gross wage. Alternatively, a standard work permit and residence permit are available for non-EU citizens. Both require employer sponsorship and registration with the Slovak Labour Office.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for Building Architects in Slovakia?**
A: Architects in Slovakia can progress from junior to project lead, senior architect, or partner in a firm. Many pursue specialization in sustainable design or BIM. After acquiring professional experience, they may register with the Slovak Chamber of Architects (SKA) for independent practice. Some move into urban planning or construction management.
