## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Research and Development Managers in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Research and Development Managers in Slovakia ranges from €3,500 to €5,000, depending on industry, experience, and company size. Senior roles in high-tech sectors can earn up to €7,000 or more. Salaries in Bratislava are typically higher than in other regions.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU Research and Development Managers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Research and Development Managers in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or a standard work permit. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (current minimum around €1,500 monthly). The standard work permit is employer-sponsored and subject to labor market testing.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a Research and Development Manager in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree or PhD in a relevant field (e.g., engineering, IT, sciences) is required, along with 5-10 years of R&D experience. Strong leadership, project management skills, and fluency in English are essential. Knowledge of Slovak or another language is an advantage but not always mandatory in multinational companies.
