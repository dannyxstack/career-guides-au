## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Slovakia?**
A: Astrologers and fortune-tellers in Slovakia often work on a freelance or informal basis, so fixed salaries are uncommon. Earnings vary widely depending on clientele and reputation. Average monthly income might range from €500 to €1,500, but many earn less due to limited demand. Official employment in this field is rare.

**Q: Can astrologers and fortune-tellers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Slovakia is designated for highly qualified workers with a valid job offer and a university degree. Astrologers and fortune-tellers typically do not meet these criteria as the occupation is not considered highly skilled and lacks formal educational requirements. Alternative residence permits for self-employment or other work visas may be possible if specific conditions are met.

**Q: What career prospects exist for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Slovakia?**
A: Career prospects are limited in Slovakia due to low demand and informal nature. Most practitioners operate independently, offering services online or through personal networks. Professional associations are scarce, and formal training is not recognized. Income instability and reliance on repeat clients are common challenges. Some combine this with other related fields like tarot reading or spiritual coaching.
