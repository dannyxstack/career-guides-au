## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sewing machine operators in Slovakia?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for sewing machine operators in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around €750, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,300. Salaries are often supplemented by overtime pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as sewing machine operators in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and is unlikely to apply due to salary thresholds. Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit obtained by an employer through a labor market test, then apply for a single permit (combined residence and work permit) at the Slovak embassy.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for sewing machine operators in Slovakia?**
A: Sewing machine operators in Slovakia usually work in garment factories with fixed shifts, often including weekends. The job involves repetitive tasks, prolonged sitting, and attention to detail. Most positions are in industrial regions like Trenčín and Prešov, with some offering piece-rate pay.
