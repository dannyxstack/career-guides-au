## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Slovakia?**
A: Shelf fillers in Slovakia usually earn the national minimum wage, which is €750 per month (full-time) in 2024. Hourly rates range from €5 to €6, with potential small increases based on experience or employer. Overtime and night shifts may offer supplements.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a shelf filler in Slovakia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for low-skilled occupations like shelf filling. Non-EU nationals must obtain a work permit (temporary residence for employment). This requires a job offer, a labor market test showing no local candidate available, and employer compliance. Certain nationalities may have simplified procedures.

**Q: What are the main duties of a shelf filler in Slovakia?**
A: Typical duties include replenishing shelves with merchandise, checking expiry dates, rotating stock, maintaining store cleanliness, and assisting customers with locating products. Shelf fillers may also handle price labeling and ensure shelves are orderly. The work is physically demanding and often involves early morning or evening shifts.
