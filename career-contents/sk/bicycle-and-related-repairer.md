## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bicycle repairers in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly salary for bicycle and related repairers in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while skilled technicians in cities like Bratislava can earn up to €1,500. Overtime and specialized skills can increase pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as bicycle repairers in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as bicycle repairers in Slovakia through a standard work visa (Zamestnanecká karta) if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree, which is not typically mandatory for this occupation, so the standard work visa is more relevant. Employers must prove the position cannot be filled by an EU citizen.

**Q: What are the career prospects for bicycle repairers in Slovakia?**
A: Career prospects are stable due to growing cycling culture and e-bike popularity. Opportunities exist in independent shops, chain retailers, and self-employment. Certification from the Slovak Chamber of Crafts (SŽČ) or courses by ZC Slovakia can enhance skills. Advancement includes shop management or specializing in high-end bikes and e-bikes.
