## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for elementary workers in Slovakia generally align with the national minimum wage, which is €750 per month in 2024. Depending on the exact role and employer, wages can range from minimum wage to around €900 per month. Overtime and shift differentials may add to earnings.

**Q: Can elementary workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card in Slovakia requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage. Elementary worker positions typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU nationals may instead apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires employer sponsorship and a labor market test.

**Q: What are common job titles for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Common jobs in this category include manual laborer, cleaner, packer, kitchen assistant, and general helper. These roles require minimal formal education and involve simple, routine tasks. Many positions are found in warehousing, retail, hospitality, and manufacturing sectors.
