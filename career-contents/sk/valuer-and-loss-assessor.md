## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Valuer or Loss Assessor in Slovakia?**
A: According to salary surveys, the average monthly gross salary for valuers in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience and specialization. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior assessors can earn up to €3,000. These figures are indicative and may vary by region and employer.

**Q: How can a foreign valuer or loss assessor obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree or five years of professional experience and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovakia (€28,000 in 2023). Alternatively, they may obtain a temporary residence permit for employment with a valid work permit. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Slovak or EU citizen.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a valuer in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in economics, finance, real estate, or a related field is required. Professional certification from the Slovak Association of Valuers (SSZ-Z) is highly recommended. For loss assessors, insurance-specific training and experience are beneficial. Continuous education is mandatory for license renewal.
