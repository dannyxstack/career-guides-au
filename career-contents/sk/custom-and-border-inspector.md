## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a customs and border inspector in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for customs and border inspectors in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, rank, and region. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior inspectors can earn up to €2,500. Additional allowances and bonuses may apply for shift work or overtime.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as customs and border inspectors in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card in Slovakia if they have a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold for highly qualified employment (currently €1,976 gross monthly). However, customs and border inspector positions are typically reserved for EU/EEA citizens due to national security and citizenship requirements, making the Blue Card route impractical for most non-EU applicants.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for customs and border inspectors in Slovakia?**
A: Customs and border inspectors in Slovakia can advance through internal exams and seniority. Promotions include moving to supervisory roles (e.g., team leader), specialized units (e.g., anti-smuggling), or administrative positions within the Financial Administration. Further education in law or customs management can accelerate career progression.
