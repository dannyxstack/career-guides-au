## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Slovakia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Slovakia typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,300, depending on experience and employer. This is below the national average wage of around €1,430. Entry-level positions may start at €900, while experienced workers in larger companies can earn up to €1,500.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The employer must first pass a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is available only if the gross monthly salary exceeds €2,145 (1.5 times average), which is uncommon for this role. Most workers therefore obtain a standard work visa with a valid job offer.

**Q: How is automation or AI affecting garment patternmaking and cutting jobs in Slovakia?**
A: Automation and CAD software are increasingly used in Slovak garment factories, reducing demand for manual patternmakers and cutters. However, skilled workers remain essential for complex designs, fitting adjustments, and quality control. Upskilling in digital pattern design and automated cutting systems can improve job security and earning potential.
