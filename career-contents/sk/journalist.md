## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for journalists in Slovakia?**
A: Journalist salaries in Slovakia vary by experience and media type. Entry-level journalists earn around €1,000–€1,300 gross per month, while senior reporters or editors can earn between €1,800–€2,800. Public broadcasters and major newspapers often pay more than regional outlets. Freelance rates are negotiated individually.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for journalists to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU journalists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times average gross wage). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible with a work permit, requiring employer sponsorship. Both routes are processed by the Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a journalist in Slovakia?**
A: A university degree in journalism, media studies, or a related field is common but not mandatory. Practical experience through internships at newspapers, TV, or online media is highly valued. Proficiency in Slovak is essential for most roles, though English-language media may require native-level English. Knowledge of media law and ethics is also beneficial.
