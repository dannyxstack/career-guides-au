## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for protective services workers (e.g., security guards) in Slovakia vary widely. Entry-level positions earn around 800–1,000 EUR gross monthly, while experienced workers or those in specialized roles may earn up to 1,500 EUR. Pay depends on employer, location, and qualifications.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for protective services workers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as protective services rarely meet the high-skill salary threshold (≥1.5x average wage). A standard employment route requires a job offer approved by the Slovak Labour Office.

**Q: What duties do protective services workers perform in Slovakia?**
A: Protective services workers in Slovakia include security guards, private investigators, and surveillance personnel. Duties involve monitoring premises, controlling access, patrolling areas, responding to alarms, and reporting incidents. Some roles require licensing under Act No. 473/2005 on Private Security Services.
