- Use AI to analyze patient movement data and optimize personalized treatment plans

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Slovakia ranges from approximately €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and the type of employer (public vs. private sector). Entry-level positions may start lower, while those in specialized clinics or with seniority can earn higher wages. Salaries are typically regulated by collective agreements in healthcare.

**Q: Can a foreign physiotherapy technician or assistant work in Slovakia under an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as physiotherapy technicians or assistants in Slovakia via a work visa or EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly salary in Slovakia. Alternatively, a standard work visa (povolenie na zamestnanie) may apply, requiring employer sponsorship and labor market testing. Both routes require recognition of qualifications by the Slovak Ministry of Health.

**Q: What are the main career paths for physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Slovakia?**
A: Physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Slovakia typically work in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, outpatient clinics, or nursing homes. With additional education and experience, they can progress to physiotherapist roles or specialize in areas such as sports rehabilitation, pediatric therapy, or neurological physiotherapy. Career advancement often involves obtaining a bachelor's or master's degree in physiotherapy, which expands opportunities for independent practice and higher salaries.
