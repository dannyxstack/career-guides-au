## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes and related workers in Slovakia?**
A: Monthly gross salaries for scribes and related workers in Slovakia typically range from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced transcribers or court reporters can earn up to €1,500. These figures are based on labor market data and may vary by region.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU scribes and related workers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU workers typically need a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer and work permit from the labor office. The EU Blue Card is generally reserved for highly skilled workers with higher education, so scribes and related workers usually do not qualify. Alternatively, the Slovak government may issue a single permit combining work and residence for eligible applicants.

**Q: Where do scribes and related workers typically find employment in Slovakia?**
A: Scribes and related workers in Slovakia are often employed in legal offices, courts (as court reporters), medical transcription services, government agencies, and administrative departments of large companies. Freelance opportunities also exist, particularly for transcription or captioning work. Language proficiency in Slovak is generally required, except for roles in international organizations.
