## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for specialist medical practitioners in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for specialist doctors in Slovakia ranges from €2,500 to €4,500, depending on specialty, experience, and region. Senior specialists in high-demand fields can earn up to €6,000. Public sector salaries are regulated, while private practice may offer higher earnings.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU specialist medical practitioners to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU doctors can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, requiring a valid work contract, recognized medical qualifications, and a minimum salary threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross wage). Alternatively, a national work permit is possible, but the Blue Card is preferred. Recognition of qualifications by the Slovak Medical Chamber is mandatory.

**Q: What is the process for recognition of medical qualifications for specialist doctors in Slovakia?**
A: Specialist medical practitioners from EU/EEA countries have automatic recognition of qualifications under EU directives. Non-EU doctors must have their diplomas recognized by the Ministry of Health and the Slovak Medical Chamber, which involves verifying education, language proficiency in Slovak, and possibly completing an adaptation period or exam.
