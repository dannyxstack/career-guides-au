## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Database and Network Professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for this role in Slovakia generally range from €1,800 to €3,200 gross per month, depending on experience, certifications, and location (Bratislava often pays higher). Senior professionals or those with specialised skills may earn more. Total compensation often includes bonuses and benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Database and Network Professionals to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly salary (approx. €1,700 in 2024). Alternatively, a national employment permit (temporary residence for employment) is possible. Both require employer sponsorship and recognition of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications and career progression opportunities exist for this occupation in Slovakia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in computer science or related field is common, but vocational training and certifications (e.g., Cisco, Oracle) are valued. Career paths include becoming a senior architect, IT manager, or consultant. The Slovak tech sector is growing, with roles in finance, telecom, and IT outsourcing. Continuous learning is key.
