## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Long-distance highway driving (autonomous truck pilot programs)
- Basic route planning and GPS navigation adjustments
- Basic vehicle status monitoring and routine inspection recording
- Standardized cargo handling (integration with automated warehousing)
- Fatigue driving risk detection (AI takeover warning)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Dynamic route optimization (real-time traffic, weather, queue times)
- Vehicle maintenance prediction (AI analysis engine data for early warning)
- Delivery confirmation and customer communication (auto-photo, e-signature)
- Multimodal transport coordination (rail, port, warehouse system integration)
- Driving safety assistance (collision warning, blind spot monitoring)

**Human moat:**

- Complex loading/unloading and on-site flexibility (e.g., construction sites, refrigerated trucks)
- Driving on unstructured roads in remote areas
- Customer relationships and non-standard delivery requirements
- Rapid decision-making in emergencies (accidents, failures)
- Compliance and safety responsibilities (cargo inspection, dangerous goods transport)

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for heavy truck and lorry drivers in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for heavy truck drivers in Slovakia ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, type of transport (national vs. international), and employer. Overtime and bonuses can increase earnings. Senior drivers with longer routes often earn at the higher end.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for non-EU heavy truck drivers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU drivers can obtain a Single Permit for employment in Slovakia, which combines work and residence. For the EU Blue Card, they need a tertiary education degree or at least five years of professional experience in truck driving. Both routes require a job offer and meet specific labor market conditions.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a heavy truck driver in Slovakia?**
A: Requirements include a valid driving license for categories C and E, a professional competence certificate (CPC), and a digital tachograph card. You must also pass a medical exam. Completing a specialized training course for international transport can improve job prospects.
