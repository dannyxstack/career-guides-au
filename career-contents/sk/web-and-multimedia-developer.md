## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for web and multimedia developers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for web and multimedia developers in Slovakia range from €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month for mid-level positions, depending on experience, skills, and location. Senior developers can earn €3,000 or more. Entry-level roles start around €1,000–€1,200 gross. The highest salaries are typically in Bratislava and for specialists in front-end frameworks or full-stack development.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for web and multimedia developers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold, set at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly salary) or through a national work permit (based on a specific job offer and labor market test). Both require employer sponsorship. The application is processed by the Slovak Foreign Police.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for web and multimedia developers in Slovakia?**
A: Employers in Slovakia seek proficiency in JavaScript frameworks (React, Angular, Vue.js), PHP, and Python. Knowledge of responsive design, CMS (WordPress, Drupal), and version control (Git) is important. For multimedia roles, skills in Adobe Creative Suite, video editing, and UX/UI design are highly valued. English fluency is required; Slovak or German is an advantage.
