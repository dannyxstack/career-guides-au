## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for vocational education teachers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for vocational education teachers in Slovakia generally range from €1,200 to €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, school type, and region. Public schools follow a state tariff system, while private institutions may offer higher pay. Additional supplements for specializations or managerial roles can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa options for vocational education teachers from non-EU countries wishing to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU vocational teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, higher education (at least 3 years), and meet salary thresholds (at least 1.5x average gross wage). Alternatively, a national employment permit (work visa) may be obtained. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Slovak labour office.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for vocational education teachers in Slovakia?**
A: Vocational teachers can advance to senior teacher, head of department, or school management roles. Continuing professional development (CPD) is encouraged through workshops, e-learning, and certification programs. Participation in EU-funded projects or dual education cooperation with companies can also enhance career prospects and specialization in emerging fields.
