## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for landscape architects in Slovakia?**
A: Entry-level landscape architects in Slovakia earn around €1,200–€1,600 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn €2,000–€3,000. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Bratislava. Senior roles in large firms or public projects may exceed €3,500. Actual figures depend on qualifications, project scope, and employer.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for landscape architects to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can work via the EU Blue Card (requires a university degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5× the average gross wage) or a national work permit (temporary residence for employment). Both require employer sponsorship. The Ministry of Interior processes applications; specific criteria are outlined on their official website.

**Q: What professional associations or certifications are important for landscape architects in Slovakia?**
A: Landscape architects in Slovakia often register with the Slovak Chamber of Architects (SKA) to offer certified services. Membership requires a master’s degree in landscape architecture and passing a professional exam. The SKA sets ethical standards and continuing education. European recognition may also allow registration via EU mutual recognition directives.
