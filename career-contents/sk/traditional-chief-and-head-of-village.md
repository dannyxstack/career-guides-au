## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a traditional chief or head of village in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, traditional chiefs and heads of villages are not typically salaried employees. These roles are often honorary or volunteer-based, with any compensation being symbolic or covering expenses. Official village leadership is held by elected mayors who receive regulated salaries, while traditional positions remain unpaid.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a traditional chief or head of village in Slovakia under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified employment and does not cover honorary or traditional roles like chiefs or heads of villages. Such positions are filled locally through custom or community selection. Foreigners seeking work in Slovakia should apply for standard work permits for occupations listed by the Central Office of Labour.

**Q: How does one become a traditional chief or head of a village in Slovakia?**
A: There is no formal career path for this role in Slovakia. Traditional chiefs or heads of villages are typically recognized through local customs, inheritance, or community acclamation. In modern Slovakia, village leadership is primarily through elected mayors (starosta), which follow political processes. The traditional title may coexist but lacks legal authority.
