## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmaceutical technicians in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for pharmaceutical technicians in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,500. Salaries vary by experience, location (higher in Bratislava), and type of employer (pharmacy chain vs. hospital). Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced technicians can earn up to €1,800.

**Q: How can foreign pharmaceutical technicians obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Foreign pharmaceutical technicians can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovakia. Alternatively, a standard work visa requires a confirmed job offer and a work permit from the competent labor office. Recognition of pharmaceutical qualifications by the Slovak Ministry of Health is mandatory.

**Q: What are typical duties of a pharmaceutical technician in Slovakia?**
A: Pharmaceutical technicians in Slovakia assist pharmacists in dispensing medications, preparing prescriptions, managing inventory, and providing customer advice on over-the-counter drugs. They also handle administrative tasks, maintain records, and ensure compliance with pharmacy regulations. Work settings include community pharmacies, hospital pharmacies, and pharmaceutical wholesalers.
