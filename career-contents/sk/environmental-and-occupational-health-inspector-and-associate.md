## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for this role in Slovakia ranges from €1,300 to €1,800, depending on experience and region. Public sector positions often follow state tariff scales, while private sector roles may offer higher pay. Additional allowances for fieldwork or hazardous duties can apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly salary in Slovakia, currently around €2,100). The employer must obtain a labor market test. Alternatively, a standard work permit and temporary residence are possible.

**Q: What are the main career paths for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Slovakia?**
A: Professionals often work for the Public Health Authority or regional offices, inspecting workplaces and ensuring compliance with health and safety regulations. Career progression can lead to senior inspector, team leader, or specialist roles in environmental health. Private sector opportunities exist in consulting or corporate safety departments.
