## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Business Services Agent in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for Business Services Agents in Slovakia vary widely by sector, but the average monthly gross salary ranges from €1,200 to €1,800. Many roles also include commission or performance bonuses, especially in sales-oriented agencies like real estate or employment services. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced agents can earn more.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as a Business Services Agent in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage in Slovakia (€2,595 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit can be obtained through a local employer, requiring a labor market test. Both routes require a residence permit.

**Q: What does a Business Services Agent typically do in Slovakia?**
A: Business Services Agents in Slovakia act as intermediaries providing specialized services not elsewhere classified. Their duties include assessing client needs, arranging contracts, and promoting services such as employment placement, real estate transactions, or insurance policies. They often work on commission and must stay updated on market trends and legal requirements.
