## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Personal Services Worker in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for personal services workers in Slovakia vary widely by role and region, but the average monthly gross wage is approximately €800–€1,200. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced workers in specialized fields can earn up to €1,500. These figures are indicative and subject to employer and location.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as a Personal Services Worker in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, but most personal services roles may not meet the salary threshold (currently €1,500/month gross). Alternatively, the Single Permit procedure combines work and residence. Job offers from Slovak employers are required.

**Q: What are common job duties and career prospects for Personal Services Workers in Slovakia?**
A: Common duties include providing non-medical care, household help, event assistance, or personal tutoring. Career progression often involves specialization (e.g., pet grooming, funeral services) or moving into supervisory roles. Skills in communication and language (Slovak) are crucial. Opportunities depend on demand in wellness, tourism, and private households.
