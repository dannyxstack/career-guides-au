## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Inquiry Clerks in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Inquiry Clerks in Slovakia ranges from €1,000 to €1,400, depending on experience, language skills, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while senior roles in Bratislava can reach €1,800. Overtime and performance bonuses are sometimes offered.

**Q: What are the work visa options for Inquiry Clerks to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Inquiry Clerks via a work permit (temporary residence for employment) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage (€1,446 in 2023 for low-skilled; for clerks, typically around €2,169). A university degree or 5 years of professional experience is also needed.

**Q: What are typical duties of an Inquiry Clerk in Slovakia?**
A: Inquiry Clerks in Slovakia handle customer inquiries by phone, email, or chat. They provide information about products or services, process orders, resolve complaints, and update databases. Fluency in Slovak and often English is required. Many work in call centers, insurance companies, or public institutions, following standardized scripts and service protocols.
