## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Social Work Associate Professional in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly salary for social work associate professionals in Slovakia ranges from €900 to €1,400 gross, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions start lower, while senior roles in larger cities may exceed €1,600. Public sector salaries are often regulated by collective agreements.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for non-EU nationals to work as a Social Work Associate Professional in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need to obtain an employment card or EU Blue Card. The employer must first register the vacancy with the Central Office of Labour. For the EU Blue Card, a university degree and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly salary are required. The process involves applying at Slovak consulates abroad.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Social Work Associate Professional in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a secondary education with a focus on social work or a related field is required, often supplemented by specialized training. Some employers prefer candidates with a bachelor's degree in social work. Registration with the Slovak Chamber of Social Workers is mandatory for certain positions. Continuous professional development is encouraged.
