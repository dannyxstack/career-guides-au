## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for undertakers and embalmers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, the average monthly gross salary for undertakers and embalmers ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, region, and employer. Public sector positions may offer slightly higher stability, while private funeral homes often provide performance-based bonuses. Overtime and on-call duties are common and can increase total earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign undertakers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as undertakers in Slovakia via a temporary residence for employment, requiring a work permit confirmed by the Central Office of Labour. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as the occupation does not meet the higher education requirement. Alternatively, intra-corporate transfers or specific bilateral agreements may offer pathways.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an undertaker in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, completion of secondary vocational education in funeral services or a related field is required. Additionally, a professional certificate from the Ministry of Interior or relevant authorities is mandatory. On-the-job training is provided by funeral homes, and knowledge of Slovak funeral customs, regulations, and hygiene standards is essential. Some positions may require a driving license.
