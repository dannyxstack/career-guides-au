## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garbage and recycling collectors in Slovakia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries typically range from €800 to €1,000, depending on experience, employer, and region. Overtime and night shift allowances may increase earnings. The minimum wage in Slovakia (2024) is €750, but collectors often earn above this due to physical demands.

**Q: How can a foreign national obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for garbage collection in Slovakia?**
A: A job offer from a Slovak employer is required. The employer applies for a work permit, after which you can apply for a temporary residence for employment. The EU Blue Card targets high-skilled workers earning at least 1.5 times the average salary; garbage collection does not meet this threshold, so standard work permit routes apply.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for garbage collectors in Slovakia?**
A: Work is full-time, often starting early morning (e.g., 4:00–6:00 AM) in all weather. Duties include lifting heavy bins, riding trucks, and sorting recyclables. Physical fitness is required. Safety gear (gloves, vests) is provided. Unions represent some municipal workers, and shift patterns vary by employer.
