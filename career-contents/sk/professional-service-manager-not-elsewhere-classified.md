## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Professional Services Managers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary by industry, experience, and location, with higher pay in IT and finance. The gross monthly salary typically ranges from €2,000 to €4,000 for mid-level positions. Senior roles in Bratislava can exceed €5,000. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU Professional Services Managers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage in Slovakia (approx. €1,900 in 2024). Alternatively, a national employment permit (e.g., temporary residence for employment) is possible for roles not meeting Blue Card criteria.

**Q: What qualifications are required for Professional Services Manager roles in Slovakia?**
A: Employers typically require a bachelor's degree in business, management, or a related field, along with 3-5 years of relevant experience. Fluency in English is essential; Slovak language skills are a plus. Professional certifications (e.g., PMP) may be advantageous. Strong analytical and leadership abilities are highly valued.
