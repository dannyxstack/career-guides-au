## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for nursing associate professionals in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for nursing associate professionals in Slovakia ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, region, and employer. Public sector wages follow state scales, with additional allowances for night shifts, weekends, and overtime. Private clinics may offer slightly higher pay.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for nursing associate professionals from outside the EU?**
A: Non-EU nursing associate professionals can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized tertiary degree (minimum 3 years) and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (currently at least 1.5 times average gross salary). Alternatively, an Employment Card is available for qualified workers. Both require employer sponsorship and a labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a nursing associate professional in Slovakia?**
A: To practice, you must complete a secondary health school (4-year program) and obtain registration with the Slovak Medical Chamber. For foreign qualifications, recognition by the competent authority is required. Continuous professional development is mandatory for license renewal. The role involves assisting registered nurses and is distinct from higher-level nursing positions.
