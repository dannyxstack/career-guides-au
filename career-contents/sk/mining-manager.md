## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Mining Managers in Slovakia?**
A: Mining Managers in Slovakia earn between €2,500 and €4,500 gross per month, depending on experience, mine size, and location. Senior roles in larger operations may exceed this range. Salaries often include bonuses linked to production targets and safety records.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Mining Managers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU mining managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage. Alternatively, a standard work permit (temporary residence for employment) is available through employer sponsorship. Both require approval from the Slovak Labour Office.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Mining Manager in Slovakia?**
A: A degree in mining engineering, geology, or a related field is typically required, along with several years of experience in mining operations. Professional certifications, such as the Slovak Mining Authority’s (Hlavný banský úrad) license for mine management, are mandatory. Continuous safety training is also essential.
