## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Slovakia?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Slovakia ranges from €500 to €800. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is €750 per month, but many cleaners earn at or slightly above this level. Salaries may vary by region, with higher pay in Bratislava. Part-time or informal work often pays less.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as a domestic cleaner in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The EU Blue Card is not available for low-skilled occupations like domestic cleaning. The main route is the 'single permit' procedure, where the employer must prove the job cannot be filled by an EU citizen. Application is made at the Slovak Foreign Police.

**Q: What are the typical duties of domestic cleaners and helpers in Slovakia?**
A: Typical duties include cleaning private homes and apartments: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, washing windows, and cleaning kitchens and bathrooms. Tasks may also involve laundry, ironing, and tidying up. Many work directly for households or through cleaning agencies. Some are self-employed, offering services to multiple clients.
