## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software
- Automated layout and grinding of restorations (e.g. CAM machine operation)
- Standardized model generation based on scan data
- Inventory management and order tracking.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design
- Smart scanning and diagnostic analysis help technicians quickly obtain patient oral data.
- AI-driven material performance simulation optimization solution selection
- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation
- Ability to communicate clinical needs directly with dentists/doctors
- Innovative Restoration Design (New Solutions Without AI Database Support)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM software operation (e.g., 3Shape, exocad)
- Knowledge of 3D printing and CNC precision machining
- Digital oral scanning and data analysis
- Materials science (bioceramics, composite resins, etc.)
- Interpersonal communication and clinical collaboration skills
- Basic programming and AI tool application

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for a Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while highly experienced technicians in private clinics can earn up to €2,200.

**Q: What work visa options are available for non-EU nationals seeking employment as a Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) if they have a university degree or relevant experience and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. Alternatively, a National Visa for employment requires a confirmed job offer and work permit from the Slovak Labour Office. Recognition of professional qualifications may be needed.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician in Slovakia?**
A: To practice as a Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technician in Slovakia, you typically need a secondary vocational education in dental technology (3-4 years) or a bachelor's degree in dental prosthetics. Registration with the Slovak Chamber of Dental Technicians is mandatory. For non-EU qualifications, recognition by the Ministry of Education is required.
