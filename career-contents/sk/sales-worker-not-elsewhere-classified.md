## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sales Worker Not Elsewhere Classified in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by industry and employer. The minimum monthly gross wage is €750 (as of 2024). For full-time sales roles, the average gross monthly salary ranges from €900 to €1,500, often supplemented by commissions or bonuses. Median earnings are around €1,100.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a Sales Worker in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, provided they have a university degree or five years of relevant professional experience, and a job offer with gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (or 1.2 times for shortage occupations). Sales Workers N.E.C. are typically not on the shortage list. Alternatively, a standard work visa or temporary residence for employment is available.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Sales Workers in Slovakia?**
A: Entry-level sales workers can advance to senior sales representative, team leader, or sales manager roles. Gaining experience in product knowledge and customer relations opens doors to specialized sales (e.g., B2B, technical sales) or related fields like marketing. Many employers offer internal training and promotion opportunities.
