## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Aged Care Services Manager in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for Aged Care Services Managers in Slovakia typically range from €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, facility size, and region. Senior managers in larger facilities or with longer tenure may earn higher. Public sector roles may follow official salary tables, while private facilities often offer additional benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for an Aged Care Services Manager to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a National Employment Visa. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (usually at least 1.5x the average gross salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if no suitable local candidate is found. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Aged Care Services Manager in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in social work, healthcare management, or a related field is required. Additional management training or a master's degree is advantageous. Slovak language proficiency is essential for regulatory compliance and daily operations. Experience in aged care settings, often 3-5 years, is expected. Some positions may require certification from the Slovak Ministry of Labour, Social Affairs and Family.
