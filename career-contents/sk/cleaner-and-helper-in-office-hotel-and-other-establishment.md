**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean floors using smart vacuum and mop robots
- Operation monitoring of automatic window cleaning robots
- Dust removal in standard areas such as corridors and halls following preset routes.
- Use automatic disinfection robots to disinfect bathrooms and kitchens.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize multi-site cleaning routes and schedules
- Use smart sensors to monitor dirt levels and precisely schedule cleaning frequency
- Receive health and safety alerts via wearable devices to enhance personal protection
- Adjusts service details using a customer review AI system to improve satisfaction
- Use digital tools to manage cleaning supplies, reducing waste

**Human moat:**

- Identification and targeted treatment of complex stubborn stains (e.g., carpet stains, oil stains).
- Communicate with clients to understand specific needs and flexibly adjust cleaning methods
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., breaking items, pet accidents)
- Fine cleaning and care of valuable items (e.g., antiques, silk)

- Learn specialized cleaning techniques (e.g., stone care, disinfection)

- Basic English proficiency to interact with smart devices or serve foreign clients
- Learn about personal protective equipment and eco-friendly cleaning agents

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cleaners and helpers in offices, hotels, and other establishments in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, the average gross monthly salary for cleaners and helpers typically ranges from €600 to €900, depending on the employer, location, and experience. The minimum wage as of 2024 is €750 per month. Actual pay may vary, with some positions offering higher rates in larger cities like Bratislava.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU nationals to work as a cleaner in Slovakia, such as the EU Blue Card?**
A: Cleaners typically do not qualify for the EU Blue Card in Slovakia, as it requires a high-skilled job with a university degree or at least 5 years of professional experience. Non-EU nationals may instead apply for a temporary residence for employment under national legislation, provided the employer has a labor market test and obtains a work permit.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions and career advancement opportunities for cleaners in Slovakia?**
A: Cleaners in Slovakia often work full-time or part-time shifts, including evenings and weekends. Employment contracts typically include benefits like paid leave and social insurance. Career advancement is limited but may include supervisory roles or specialization in industrial cleaning. Language skills (Slovak) and reliability are valued by employers.
