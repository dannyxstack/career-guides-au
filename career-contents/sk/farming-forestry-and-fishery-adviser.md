## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for these advisers in Slovakia typically range from €1,200 to €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and region. Senior roles or those in specialized sectors may earn higher. The average gross monthly salary is around €1,500, which is competitive within the agricultural advisory field.

**Q: What is the work-visa or EU Blue Card route for a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU advisers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the Slovak average wage (€23,500 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit requires a labor market test and a temporary residence permit. Both routes are processed through Slovak embassies or the Foreign Police.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Slovakia?**
A: These advisers provide expert guidance to farmers, foresters, and fishers on sustainable practices, EU subsidy applications, crop and livestock management, and environmental compliance. They may work for government agencies, private consultancies, or agricultural cooperatives. Strong knowledge of Slovak agricultural policies and Slovak language skills are essential for this role.
