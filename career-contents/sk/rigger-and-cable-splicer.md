- Image recognition-based automatic detection of cable wear

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for riggers and cable splicers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for riggers and cable splicers in Slovakia vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced workers can earn €2,000–€2,500. Overtime and shift work may increase earnings. The construction and telecommunications sectors offer the most opportunities.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for riggers and cable splicers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU workers need a work permit or EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage (€26,844 in 2024). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment can be obtained with a confirmed job. Both require employer sponsorship and meet labour market test criteria.

**Q: What are the main employment sectors for riggers and cable splicers in Slovakia?**
A: Riggers and cable splicers in Slovakia are primarily employed in construction (building bridges, towers, industrial structures) and telecommunications (laying and splicing fiber-optic cables). The energy sector also hires them for power line installation. Skills in rope access and high-voltage work are valued.
