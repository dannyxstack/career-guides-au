## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a building caretaker in Slovakia typically ranges between €800 and €1,200, depending on the location, type of building, and employer. Additional benefits such as accommodation or utilities may be provided in some cases.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for non-EU building caretakers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit, which the employer must arrange. The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified workers and is generally not applicable for building caretakers. The more common route is a temporary residence permit for employment, requiring a job offer and relevant skills.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a building caretaker in Slovakia?**
A: Formal education is not strictly required, but basic technical skills in plumbing, electricity, and general maintenance are advantageous. Employers often prefer candidates with secondary vocational education. Slovak language proficiency is essential, and good communication skills are valued.
