## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Insurance Representative in Slovakia?**
A: Average monthly gross salary for insurance representatives in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and company. Entry-level may start around €900, while senior agents can earn €2,500+ with commissions. Bonuses and performance incentives are common.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as Insurance Representatives in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, the EU Blue Card is available for non-EU nationals with a university degree or 5+ years of professional experience. The job must offer a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovakia (approx. €26,472 in 2024). Alternatively, a national employment permit may apply for lower salaries.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Insurance Representative in Slovakia?**
A: A secondary education is usually required, with higher education preferred. Licensing from the National Bank of Slovakia is mandatory – candidates must pass an exam. Language skills: Slovak is essential for client interactions; English or German may be advantageous for corporate accounts.
