## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, the average gross monthly salary for pet groomers and animal care workers typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start near the minimum wage (€816 in 2025), while experienced professionals in cities like Bratislava can earn higher. Note that tips and self-employment income can supplement base pay.

**Q: Is it possible to work as a pet groomer or animal care worker in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers and usually requires a university degree, so it is not applicable for most pet groomers or animal care workers. However, non-EU nationals can obtain a standard work permit (e.g., single permit) if an employer demonstrates that the position cannot be filled locally. Self-employment is also possible with a trade license, but visa requirements apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pet groomer in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, there is no mandatory formal education for pet grooming, but completing a recognized training course (e.g., at a vocational school or private academy) is highly recommended. Many employers prefer candidates with a certificate in animal care or grooming. Practical experience and a trade license (živnostenský list) are required for self-employment. Continuous learning is valued to keep up with breed-specific techniques.
