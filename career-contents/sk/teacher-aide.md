## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a teachers' aide in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for teachers' aides in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, region, and type of school. State school salaries follow fixed scales, often starting lower. Private schools may offer slightly higher pay.

**Q: Can a teachers' aide obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers requiring a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (around €1,900/month). Most teachers' aide positions do not meet these criteria. Non-EU nationals typically need a standard employment permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a teachers' aide in Slovakia?**
A: A secondary school diploma with a pedagogical focus or a bachelor's degree in education is typically required. Some positions accept relevant experience in childcare. Fluency in Slovak is essential. Additional training in special education or assistant pedagogy can improve job prospects.
