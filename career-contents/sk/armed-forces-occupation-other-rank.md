## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Armed Forces Other Ranks in Slovakia?**
A: Salary for other ranks in the Slovak Armed Forces depends on rank and service years. A private (vojak) typically earns around €800–€1,200 gross monthly, plus allowances. Higher ranks like sergeant can earn €1,200–€1,800. Benefits include housing, food, and special bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Armed Forces Other Ranks in Slovakia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Generally, armed forces positions are reserved for Slovak or EU/EEA citizens. Non-EU nationals cannot obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for military roles, as these require citizenship. Only civilians in support roles might be eligible, but that does not fall under ISCO 0310.

**Q: What training is required to join the Slovak Armed Forces as an other rank?**
A: Applicants must be at least 18, have completed secondary education, and pass physical, psychological, and medical tests. Basic training lasts 4–6 months at a military training center. Further specialization courses are available for career progression.
