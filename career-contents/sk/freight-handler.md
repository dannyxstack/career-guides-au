## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a freight handler in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for freight handlers in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,400. Overtime and shift allowances may increase overall pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for freight handlers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and temporary residence for employment, obtained via an employer. EU Blue Card is available for higher-skilled roles but freight handling typically requires only lower-skilled permits. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for freight handlers in Slovakia?**
A: Freight handlers load, unload, and move goods in warehouses, ports, or distribution centers using forklifts or manual handling. Physical stamina is essential. Shifts often include nights and weekends. Employment is common in logistics hubs near Bratislava, Košice, and industrial parks.
