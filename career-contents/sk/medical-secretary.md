## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical secretary in Slovakia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for medical secretaries in Slovakia typically ranges from €800 to €1,200. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced secretaries in larger hospitals or private clinics can earn up to €1,400. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Bratislava.

**Q: What work visa options are available for foreign medical secretaries in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign medical secretaries can work in Slovakia via a national employment permit (work permit) or an EU Blue Card if they meet the higher qualification criteria. The EU Blue Card requires tertiary education or at least five years of professional experience. Both routes require a job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a medical secretary in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, employers require completed secondary education (gymnázium or vocational school) with a focus on health or administration. Additional certification in medical terminology or office management is an advantage. Language skills in Slovak are essential, and knowledge of English is often preferred.
