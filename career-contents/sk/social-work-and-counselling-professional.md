## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, social workers and counsellors earn between €1,000 and €1,800 gross monthly, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and region. Public sector salaries are regulated by law, while private practice income varies. Senior roles or specialised counselling may reach higher figures.

**Q: Can non-EU social work and counselling professionals work in Slovakia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if you have a recognised university degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, updated annually). Apply through the Slovak Foreign Police. Recognition of your qualification by the Slovak Ministry of Education may be required. The Blue Card is valid for up to four years.

**Q: What are the requirements to practice as a social worker in Slovakia?**
A: You need a recognised degree in social work (or equivalent) and registration with the Slovak Chamber of Social Workers. For non-EU qualifications, official recognition by the Ministry of Education is mandatory. Continuous professional development is encouraged but not legally required.
