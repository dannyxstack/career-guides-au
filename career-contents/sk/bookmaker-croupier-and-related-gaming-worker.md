## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a croupier or gaming worker in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for croupiers and gaming workers in Slovakia vary by casino location and experience. Entry-level positions earn around €800-€1,200 gross monthly, while experienced staff can earn €1,500-€2,000. Tips often supplement income. The average hourly wage for croupiers is approximately €5-€8 before tips.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for gaming workers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a valid employment visa to work as a gaming worker in Slovakia. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation due to skill level requirements. Employers must obtain a labor market test. A temporary residence permit for employment is the standard route, requiring a job offer and work permit approval.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a croupier in Slovakia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but casinos often require completion of a croupier training course offered by licensed institutions. Basic math skills, customer service ability, and fluency in Slovak or English are essential. Some employers provide on-the-job training. A clean criminal record is also required due to gambling regulations.
