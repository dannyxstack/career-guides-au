## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Slovakia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on region and farm type. The national minimum wage in 2024 is €750 per month. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may be available during peak periods like calving or harvest.

**Q: How can a foreigner get a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a livestock farm labourer in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and salary at least 1.5× the average wage, so it is generally not applicable for labourer roles. Seasonal work permits under EU Directive 2014/36 allow up to 6 months of work.

**Q: What are the main duties of a livestock farm labourer in Slovakia?**
A: Duties include feeding, watering, and herding animals; cleaning barns and milking parlours; assisting with breeding and calving; monitoring animal health; and maintaining fences and equipment. Work often starts early, includes weekends, and requires physical stamina. Formal education is not required, but experience with livestock is valued.
