## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Health Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization and experience. On average, health professionals in this category earn between €1,200 and €2,000 per month gross. Public sector positions follow state tariff scales, while private practice incomes can be higher. Additional allowances and bonuses may apply.

**Q: How can foreign health professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Foreign health professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national employment visa. Requirements include a valid job offer, recognition of qualifications by the Slovak Ministry of Health, and proof of accommodation. The Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (approx. €1,400/month in 2023).

**Q: What are the recognition requirements for foreign health professionals in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Slovak Ministry of Health via a nostrification procedure. This involves translation, verification, and possibly additional exams. EU/EEA diplomas are generally recognized automatically. Non-EU professionals may need to complete a supplementary year or pass a language exam in Slovak.
