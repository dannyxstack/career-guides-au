## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for civil engineering technicians in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, civil engineering technicians typically earn between €1,300 and €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,100, while senior technicians can earn up to €2,500. The average gross monthly wage in the construction sector is approximately €1,600.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for civil engineering technicians to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU civil engineering technicians can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.2 times the average Slovak wage (€1,902 in 2024) and meet qualification requirements. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is available for positions not meeting Blue Card criteria. Both require employer sponsorship and recognition of professional qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a civil engineering technician in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a secondary vocational school diploma in civil engineering or a bachelor’s degree in a related field is required. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Slovak Centre of Scientific and Technical Information. Professional certification is not mandatory but is valued by employers. Practical experience and knowledge of Slovak building regulations are advantageous.
