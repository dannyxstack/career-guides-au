**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Text Proofreading and Spell Checking with Real-time High Accuracy by AI Models
- Data Encoding and Classification: Standardized Conversion from Spreadsheets to Databases
- Standardization of format, such as layout and punctuation uniformity
- Simple document template filling and letter generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Logic and semantic proofreading of complex text; AI provides suggestions, but humans must finalize.
- Auxiliary proofreading of multilingual documents, AI draft first then human polishing
- Data quality monitoring, AI flags outliers for human review
- Quickly locate reference sources via AI search to improve proofreading efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Semantic judgment and style grasp in complex contexts
- Authoritative verification of legal or professional terminology
- Responsibility for handling data involving trade secrets or privacy
- Cross-departmental communication and requirement clarification

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced configuration of AI proofreading tools (e.g. Grammarly Business, Acrolinx)

- Domain expertise (e.g., legal, medical knowledge)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for coding, proofreading and related clerks in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary ranges from €900 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €800, while experienced clerks may earn up to €1,500. Salaries in Bratislava are typically higher. Data from Platy.sk indicates these figures.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU nationals to work as a coding, proofreading clerk in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if the job is highly-skilled, requiring a university degree and a gross salary of at least €1,975 (1.5 times the average wage in 2024). For most coding clerk positions, the salary is lower, so a national single permit (work visa) is more common, requiring employer sponsorship and labour market testing.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for coding, proofreading clerks in Slovakia?**
A: Typical career progression includes moving from clerk to senior clerk, team leader, or supervisor. With additional skills in editing or data management, clerks can transition into copywriting, proofreading at higher levels, or administrative management. The role often acts as a stepping stone to editorial or communications assistant positions. Some pursue further education in linguistics or media.
