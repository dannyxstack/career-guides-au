## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for ships' deck officers in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for ships' deck officers in Slovakia vary by rank and employer. A deck officer on inland waterway vessels typically earns between €1,200 and €2,200 gross per month, while senior officers may earn up to €3,000. Pay depends on experience, vessel type, and whether working on international routes.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU deck officers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU deck officers can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers or a national work permit. Key steps: job offer from a Slovak employer, recognition of maritime qualifications by the Slovak Maritime Authority, and meeting salary thresholds. The Blue Card requires a contract of at least one year and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a deck officer in Slovakia?**
A: To become a deck officer in Slovakia, you need a valid STCW certificate of competence issued by a recognized maritime authority. For inland navigation, a Slovak or EU license for the Danube is required. Typically, candidates complete maritime academy studies, sea service, and pass examination by the Slovak Maritime Authority. Language skills in Slovak or English are advantageous.
