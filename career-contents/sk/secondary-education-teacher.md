## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for secondary education teachers in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for secondary school teachers in Slovakia ranges from €1,400 to €1,800, depending on experience, qualifications, and school location. Public sector salaries follow a state pay scale with bonuses for years of service and additional duties. Private or international schools may offer higher compensation.

**Q: What are the visa options for foreign secondary teachers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU teachers can work in Slovakia via a standard work permit and residence permit, or through the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer, a university degree, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average national salary. All applications are processed by Slovak foreign police and embassies.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a secondary teacher in Slovakia?**
A: To teach at a secondary school in Slovakia, you need a master's degree in the relevant subject and completed pedagogical training (e.g., a teacher preparation program). Foreign degrees must be recognized by the Slovak Ministry of Education. While a Slovak language certificate is often required for public schools, international schools may teach in English.
