## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Slovakia?**
A: Supply, distribution and related managers in Slovakia typically earn a gross monthly salary between €2,000 and €4,000, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior positions in larger firms or in sectors like automotive or logistics may exceed this range. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What is the official work visa route for non-EU nationals to work as a Supply, Distribution and Related Manager in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree or equivalent qualification, a binding job offer in a managerial role, and a salary meeting the statutory minimum threshold set by Slovak law. Alternatively, a national employment permit is possible if the Blue Card conditions are not met.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Slovakia?**
A: These managers plan, direct, and coordinate supply chains, inventory, and distribution strategies. Key duties include optimizing logistics, managing procurement, overseeing warehousing, and ensuring timely delivery. In Slovakia, they often work in manufacturing, retail, or logistics firms, focusing on cost efficiency and compliance with EU and national regulations.
