## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ambulance workers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, ambulance workers typically earn between €900 and €1,400 gross per month, depending on experience, region, and employer. Salaries in the capital Bratislava tend to be higher. Additional allowances for night shifts, overtime, and hazardous conditions can increase earnings. The average gross monthly salary is around €1,150.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for ambulance workers moving to Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU ambulance workers can obtain a Slovak work visa via a confirmed job offer and a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers; however, ambulance workers typically need a tertiary education or five years of experience to qualify. Both routes require employer sponsorship and meet salary thresholds, which are set annually.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an ambulance worker in Slovakia?**
A: To work as an ambulance worker in Slovakia, you need a secondary medical school diploma (higher professional education in paramedic or emergency health services). Alternatively, a bachelor's degree in health sciences with a specialization in emergency care is accepted. Registration with the Slovak Medical Chamber and completion of a state exam are required. Continuous professional development is mandatory.
