## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for stonemasons in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for stonemasons in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, region, and specialization. Restoration experts and master carvers may earn higher. Overtime and project-based contracts can provide additional income.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for foreign stonemasons in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign stonemasons can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a recognized qualification and a job offer meeting salary thresholds, or via a national employment permit (single permit) with employer sponsorship. Both routes are officially available for non-EU nationals.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a stonemason in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, stonemasons typically complete vocational training at secondary technical schools in fields like stone processing or masonry. Apprenticeships and craft certification from the Slovak Chamber of Trades are common. Continuing education in heritage restoration enhances career prospects.
