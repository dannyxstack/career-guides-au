## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for town and traffic planners in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for town and traffic planners in Slovakia typically ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, location, and sector. Senior roles in larger cities like Bratislava may earn higher. Public sector salaries follow state pay scales, while private consulting firms often offer competitive compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU town planners work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU town and traffic planners can apply for an EU Blue Card in Slovakia if they hold a university degree (at least bachelor's) and a job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times the average Slovak salary (approx. €1,950 in 2024). The employer must obtain a labor market test. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is available for lower salaries.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for town planners in Slovakia?**
A: Town and traffic planners in Slovakia work for municipal offices, regional development agencies, transport authorities, and private consultancies. The field is growing due to EU-funded infrastructure projects and urbanization. Planners can specialize in spatial planning, sustainable mobility, or urban regeneration. Career progression includes senior planner, project manager, or head of planning department.
