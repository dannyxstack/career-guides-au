## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for this category vary by sector and experience. In 2024, the average gross monthly salary for engineering professionals is around €2,000-€3,000. Entry-level positions may start at €1,500, while senior roles in automotive or IT can exceed €4,000. Bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for engineering professionals to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card or a national work visa. The Blue Card requires a university degree (or 5 years' professional experience) and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage (approximately €2,200+ in 2024). Alternatively, a work visa is available for lower-skilled roles but requires a labor market test.

**Q: How is the job market for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Demand is strong, particularly in automotive, machinery, electronics, and IT sectors. Many multinational companies operate in Slovakia, offering growth opportunities. Proficiency in English is beneficial; Slovak language skills are advantageous for local positions. Networking through professional events and online platforms can aid job search.
