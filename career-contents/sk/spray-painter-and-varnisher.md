## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a spray painter or varnisher in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for spray painters and varnishers in Slovakia ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while skilled workers with several years of experience can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and bonuses are common in manufacturing industries.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for a non-EU spray painter to work in Slovakia, including the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU spray painters typically need a work visa and a residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for this occupation, as it requires a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience at a highly skilled level. Instead, a standard work permit is obtained through a job offer, with the employer proving the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a spray painter in Slovakia?**
A: Formal training is not always mandatory, but completing a vocational school program in coating, painting, or surface treatment is common. Apprenticeships lasting 2-3 years are available. Certification in specific techniques like powder coating or automotive painting can improve job prospects. Practical skills and knowledge of safety regulations are essential.
