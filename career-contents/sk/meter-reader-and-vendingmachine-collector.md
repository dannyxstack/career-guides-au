## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a meter reader in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly salary for meter readers in Slovakia ranges from €700 to €1,100 gross, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (€700 in 2024), while experienced workers at larger utility companies can earn higher. Overtime and bonuses may increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as meter readers in Slovakia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as meter readers in Slovakia through a standard work visa, requiring a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation as it targets highly qualified workers (typically requiring a university degree or higher skill level). Applicants must meet general immigration criteria.

**Q: What are the main duties of a meter reader in Slovakia?**
A: Meter readers in Slovakia travel to residential and commercial sites to record readings from gas, water, and electricity meters. They ensure accuracy, inspect meters for damage or tampering, and report irregularities. They may also handle billing queries, collect payments from vending machines, and use handheld devices to log data.
