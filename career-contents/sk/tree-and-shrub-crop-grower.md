## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for tree and shrub crop growers in Slovakia?**
A: According to the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic, the average monthly gross wage for agricultural workers is approximately €1,000. For tree and shrub crop growers, salaries typically range from €800 to €1,200 gross per month, varying by experience, employer, and region.

**Q: What visa routes exist for non-EU nationals to work as tree and shrub crop growers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a standard work permit with a confirmed job offer and a positive labor market test from the Central Office of Labour. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a tertiary degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage, set annually by the government).

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of tree and shrub crop growers in Slovakia?**
A: Tree and shrub crop growers in Slovakia prepare soil, plant, prune, irrigate, fertilize, and apply pesticides. They monitor plant health, operate farm machinery, and manage harvesting. Work is seasonal, often outdoors, and requires knowledge of horticultural practices and safety regulations.
