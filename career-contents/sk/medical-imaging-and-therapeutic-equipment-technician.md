## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for medical imaging technicians in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for medical imaging technicians in Slovakia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and the type of healthcare facility. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior technicians in specialized clinics can earn up to €2,200. Public hospitals often offer lower base pay but include bonuses and allowances.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens work as a medical imaging technician in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens must hold a recognized tertiary qualification (at least 3 years) in radiography or a related field. The EU Blue Card requires a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.2 times the average Slovak wage (currently ~€1,775/month). The employer applies for a work permit, and then the applicant applies for the Blue Card at the Slovak embassy. Recognition of foreign qualifications by the Slovak Ministry of Health is mandatory.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of medical imaging technicians in Slovakia?**
A: Medical imaging technicians in Slovakia operate diagnostic equipment such as X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound machines. They prepare patients for procedures, ensure safety protocols, and assist radiologists with image acquisition. Their duties also include maintaining equipment, documenting patient records, and adhering to radiation protection standards. Work settings include public hospitals, private clinics, and specialized imaging centers.
