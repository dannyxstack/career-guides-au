## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a trade broker in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for trade brokers in Slovakia vary based on experience, asset class, and employer. Junior brokers earn around €1,200–€1,800 per month gross, while senior brokers can earn €3,000–€5,000 or more, often with commission. In Bratislava, salaries tend to be higher due to concentration of financial firms. Bonuses and performance-based pay are common.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for trade brokers moving to Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU trade brokers can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national employment visa. The Blue Card requires a university degree or 5+ years of professional experience and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage. Alternatively, the Single Permit combines work and residence. Employers must prove the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become a trade broker in Slovakia?**
A: Trade brokers in Slovakia typically need a bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, or business. Licenses from the National Bank of Slovakia (NBS) may be required for certain securities trading. Strong analytical skills, fluency in English, and Slovak language proficiency are advantageous. Prior experience in banking or investment firms is highly valued. Continuous professional development is expected.
