## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: Average monthly gross salary for this category is around €1,250 to €1,700, depending on experience, region, and institution. Salaries in international schools or higher education tend to be higher. Entry-level positions may start at approximately €1,000 gross per month.

**Q: How can foreign teaching professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized university degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (currently at least 1.5 times the average annual salary). Alternatively, a national employment visa (temporary residence for employment) is available with a valid work permit and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required for teaching professionals in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in the relevant subject is needed, along with recognized teaching qualifications. For public schools, fluency in Slovak or Hungarian may be required. International schools often accept international certifications (e.g., TEFL, state teaching license) and English as the medium of instruction. Recognition of foreign credentials by the Slovak Ministry of Education is often necessary.
