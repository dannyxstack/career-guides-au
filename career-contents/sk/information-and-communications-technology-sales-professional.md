## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Information and Communications Technology Sales Professional in Slovakia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for ICT sales professionals in Slovakia range from approximately €1,800 for entry-level positions to over €4,000 for senior roles, plus performance-based bonuses. Senior account managers or those in managerial roles can earn upwards of €5,000. Salaries vary by company size, location (highest in Bratislava), and language skills.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for ICT Sales Professionals moving to Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Slovakia via a work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree (or 5 years of relevant experience) and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Slovakia (approx. €25,000 in 2024). The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. Applications are processed by the Foreign Police.

**Q: What career growth opportunities exist for ICT Sales Professionals in Slovakia?**
A: ICT sales professionals in Slovakia can advance to senior account manager, key account manager, or sales manager roles. The dynamic tech sector, especially in Bratislava and Košice, offers opportunities in multinational companies and startups. Developing expertise in cloud, cybersecurity, or IT consulting can accelerate progression. Fluency in multiple European languages is highly valued.
