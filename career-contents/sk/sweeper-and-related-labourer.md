## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, sweepers and related labourers typically earn between €600 and €900 per month gross, depending on the employer and region. The national minimum wage is approximately €623 per month. Pay may increase with overtime, night shifts, or experience. Public sector employers generally follow collective agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a sweeper in Slovakia through the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and does not apply to sweepers. Non-EU citizens can work in Slovakia via a work permit or temporary residence for employment. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Official routes include employment permits under the Act on Residence of Foreigners.
