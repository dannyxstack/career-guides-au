## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for craft and related workers in Slovakia ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and region. Actual pay can vary significantly by sector and employer.

**Q: What visa options exist for foreign craft workers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign workers typically need a single permit combining work and residence. The EU Blue Card is only for highly qualified workers with a tertiary degree and a higher salary threshold, which often does not apply to craft occupations. Work visas are employer-sponsored.

**Q: Which sectors employ craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Slovakia?**
A: These workers are employed in diverse sectors such as manufacturing, construction, maintenance, and repair services. They may work in small workshops or large industrial plants. Specific roles include instrument makers, glass workers, or woodworkers, depending on specialization.
