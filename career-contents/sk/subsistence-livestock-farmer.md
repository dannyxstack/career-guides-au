## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for subsistence livestock farmers in Slovakia?**
A: Subsistence livestock farmers in Slovakia primarily produce for their own consumption, so income is minimal or non-existent. The activity is not market-oriented, and farmers often rely on other income sources. Average earnings are not reported in official statistics, as it is not a formal employment category.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as subsistence livestock farmers in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card in Slovakia is reserved for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent, typically in fields like IT or engineering. Subsistence livestock farming does not meet these criteria. Other routes, such as a work permit for agricultural jobs or a self-employment visa, may be available for non-EU citizens.

**Q: What are the main activities of subsistence livestock farmers in Slovakia?**
A: Subsistence livestock farmers in Slovakia raise animals such as cattle, pigs, sheep, goats, or poultry primarily for their own household needs, including meat, milk, eggs, and wool. They often manage small land plots, practice traditional farming methods, and may sell surplus at local markets, but do not rely on it as a primary income source.
