## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialization and experience. Entry-level positions may earn around €800-1,200 per month, while experienced professionals can earn €1,500-2,500. Self-employed practitioners have variable income. Official data from the Statistical Office of Slovakia show average monthly earnings for health associate professionals (including this group) around €1,300.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically require a national work permit (povolenie na zamestnanie) obtained by an employer. The EU Blue Card is applicable for highly qualified workers but requires a tertiary education and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (€2,000+). This occupation may not always meet the salary threshold. Recognition of qualifications by the Slovak Ministry of Health is mandatory.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Slovakia?**
A: Practitioners must have completed a recognized education program in traditional or complementary medicine, such as a bachelor's degree in acupuncture or herbal medicine. Registration with the Slovak Ministry of Health or a professional chamber is required. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain licensure. Some fields, like chiropractic, have specific legal recognition.
