## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for judges in Slovakia?**
A: Judges in Slovakia earn a gross monthly salary ranging from approximately €2,500 to €3,500, depending on experience, court level, and seniority. Supreme Court judges may earn higher. Salaries are set by law and adjusted periodically. Additional benefits include social security, health insurance, and pension contributions.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as judges in Slovakia, and is the EU Blue Card applicable?**
A: Judicial positions in Slovakia are typically reserved for Slovak citizens. Foreign lawyers from EU/EEA may work as legal advisors or in private practice after qualification recognition. Non-EU lawyers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they secure a job offer in a regulated legal profession, subject to qualification equivalency. Direct appointment as a judge requires citizenship.

**Q: What qualifications and steps are needed to become a judge in Slovakia?**
A: To become a judge in Slovakia, one must hold a Master's degree in law, pass the judicial examination, and complete a three-year judicial traineeship. Candidates must be Slovak citizens, over 30 years old, and of good repute. The President appoints judges on the recommendation of the Judicial Council.
