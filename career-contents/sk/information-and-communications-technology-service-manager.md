## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an Information and Communications Technology Services Manager in Slovakia?**
A: Based on industry surveys, the gross monthly salary for ICT Services Managers in Slovakia typically ranges from €2,500 to €4,000 for mid-level positions, with senior roles reaching up to €6,000 or more. Salaries vary by experience, company size, and location, with Bratislava offering higher pay due to cost of living.

**Q: How can an ICT Services Manager obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU ICT Services Managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a gross monthly salary at least 1.5 times the average national wage (approx. €2,000 in 2024). The employer must register the vacancy. Alternatively, a national work visa under the Act on Residence of Foreigners is possible for roles meeting labor market test criteria.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for ICT Services Managers in Slovakia?**
A: ICT Services Managers in Slovakia can progress to senior management roles such as Chief Technology Officer (CTO) or IT Director. Specialization in areas like cybersecurity, cloud services, or digital transformation also offers growth. Many professionals transition to consultancy or executive positions in multinational corporations, especially in Bratislava’s growing tech hub.
