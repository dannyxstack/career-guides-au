## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a language teacher (other than English) in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for language teachers in Slovakia vary by institution and experience. Public schools pay around €1,000–€1,400 monthly gross, while private language schools and international schools may offer €1,500–€2,200. Additional income from private tutoring is common. Rates are influenced by the language taught (e.g., German or French often higher than others) and location (Bratislava higher).

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for language teachers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU language teachers can work in Slovakia via a temporary residence permit for employment or an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified work). The employer must obtain a labor market test confirming no Slovak/EU applicant is available. For the Blue Card, a recognized teaching qualification and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross monthly wage (approx. €2,800 in 2024) are required. The standard work permit path applies for lower salaries.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to teach languages (other than English) in Slovakia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in the target language or education is required. For public schools, state-recognized teaching certification (e.g., from a Slovak pedagogical faculty) is mandatory. Private language schools often accept native speakers with a degree and a TEFL/CELTA-like certificate specific to the language. Teaching experience and CEFR C1-C2 proficiency are highly valued. Registration with the Slovak Chamber of Teachers may be needed for some positions.
