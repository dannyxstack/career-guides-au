## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for professional athletes in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sport, level, and club. Top footballers in the Slovak league can earn €5,000–€15,000 monthly, while athletes in less popular sports may earn much less, often supplemented by sponsorships or national sports funding. Many professional athletes also work part-time.

**Q: How can foreign athletes obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU athletes must secure a job offer from a Slovak club and apply for a temporary residence permit for employment. This requires a work permit from the Central Office of Labour. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers but typically requires a university degree or five years of professional experience; athletes may qualify if they meet these criteria.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for athletes in Slovakia after retirement?**
A: Retired athletes often transition into coaching, sports management, or physical education teaching. The Slovak Olympic and Sports Committee offers programs for career development. Many pursue studies in sports science or take up roles in sports administration within national federations.
