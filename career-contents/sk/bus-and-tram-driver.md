## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, bus and tram drivers earn a gross monthly salary between €1,000 and €1,500, with higher pay (up to €1,800) in Bratislava. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings. Collective agreements set minimum rates, and wages vary by employer and experience.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card process for bus and tram drivers in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens require a work permit and temporary residence for employment. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a degree and salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €2,000). Bus/tram driving typically does not meet this, so standard work permits are the main route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Slovakia?**
A: You need a professional driving license for buses (category D) or a tram driving certificate, plus medical and psychological exams. Slovak language proficiency is essential. Employers or driving schools provide training courses.
