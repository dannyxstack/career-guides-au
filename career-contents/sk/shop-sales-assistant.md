## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for shop sales assistants in Slovakia?**
A: In Slovakia, shop sales assistants typically earn between the national minimum wage (€750 per month in 2024) and around €1,000 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Some retailers offer performance bonuses or commissions, which can increase total pay. Full-time contracts often include standard benefits such as paid leave and meal vouchers.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU citizens to work as shop sales assistants in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a temporary residence for employment, requiring a confirmed job offer and a work permit obtained by the employer. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree or equivalent experience, which is uncommon for this role. For low-skilled positions, the standard employment permit route applies.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop sales assistants in Slovakia?**
A: Experienced shop sales assistants in Slovakia can advance to senior sales roles, team leader, or department manager positions. Many retailers offer internal training programs. Some assistants pursue further education in retail management or business, opening doors to higher-level roles in store management, buying, or visual merchandising.
