## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Slovakia?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Slovakia vary by experience and specialization, typically ranging from €2,000 to €4,500 gross per month. Senior developers in Bratislava may earn higher, while junior roles start lower. Benefits often include bonuses and flexible work.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU software developers to work in Slovakia?**
A: Non-EU software developers can obtain employment through the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. Both require a job offer from a Slovak employer. The EU Blue Card has specific salary criteria, while the national permit involves a labor market test. Successful applicants receive temporary residency for work.

**Q: What career growth opportunities do software developers have in Slovakia?**
A: Slovakia's tech scene is expanding, particularly in Bratislava and Košice. Developers can progress to senior, lead, or architect roles, or specialize in areas like AI, cybersecurity, or full-stack development. Many companies offer training, certifications, and paths to management. The startup ecosystem also provides entrepreneurial opportunities.
