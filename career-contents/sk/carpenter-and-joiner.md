## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a carpenter or joiner in Slovakia?**
A: The average monthly salary for carpenters and joiners in Slovakia ranges from €1,100 to €1,600 gross, depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers earn around €900, while experienced professionals can earn up to €2,000. Salaries are often higher in Bratislava and western Slovakia due to higher demand and cost of living.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a foreign carpenter to work in Slovakia?**
A: Foreign carpenters can work in Slovakia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers if they meet the salary and education requirements. Alternatively, they can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a confirmed job offer and approval from the Slovak Labour Office. Both routes are managed by the Foreign Police.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a carpenter or joiner in Slovakia?**
A: To work as a carpenter or joiner in Slovakia, vocational training in carpentry (e.g., a secondary vocational school certificate) is typically required. Apprenticeships or post-secondary courses in woodworking are also recognized. While formal qualifications are not always mandatory, employers prefer certified workers. Recognition of foreign qualifications may be needed for non-EU migrants.
