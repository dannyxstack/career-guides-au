## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work and counselling professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and experience. Entry-level positions often start around CHF 55,000–65,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn CHF 80,000–100,000 or more. Public sector roles generally follow standardised pay scales. Private sector salaries may differ. Always check local salary benchmarks for exact figures.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU/EFTA social work and counselling professionals to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals typically require a work permit through the Swiss labour market test. A valid job offer and proof that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available are needed. The EU Blue Card is not commonly issued for this occupation; instead, a B permit (residence permit for gainful employment) is the primary route, subject to cantonal and federal approval.

**Q: What are the key career prospects for social work and counselling professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Opportunities exist in child and youth welfare, healthcare, addiction counselling, and refugee support. Advanced roles require a Master's degree and often cantonal recognition. German, French, or Italian proficiency is essential depending on the region. Continuing education and specialisation (e.g., systemic therapy) can lead to supervisory or management positions.
