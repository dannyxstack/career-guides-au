## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary for members of the Swiss Armed Forces (other ranks)?**
A: Professional soldiers (other ranks) earn between CHF 4,000 and CHF 6,000 monthly, depending on rank and experience. Militia personnel (conscripts and volunteers) receive daily allowances starting at CHF 62 during basic training, increasing with rank and service days. Accommodation and meals are provided during service.

**Q: Can non-Swiss citizens serve in the Swiss Armed Forces or obtain an EU Blue Card for military roles?**
A: Only Swiss citizens may serve in the Swiss Armed Forces. Non-citizens cannot enlist, and military occupations are not eligible for the EU Blue Card, which targets highly skilled civilian professionals. Naturalized citizens or permanent residents may be called for compulsory service, but there is no direct work visa route for foreign military personnel.

**Q: What are the career prospects for other ranks in the Swiss Armed Forces?**
A: Other ranks can advance from recruit to non-commissioned officer (NCO) and senior NCO positions. Professional soldiers can pursue long-term careers in logistics, engineering, or administration. Many serve part-time as militia while holding civilian jobs. The armed forces offer vocational training and further education opportunities.
