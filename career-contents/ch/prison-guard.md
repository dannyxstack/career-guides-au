## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for prison guards in Switzerland?**
A: The gross monthly salary for prison guards in Switzerland typically ranges from CHF 5,500 to CHF 7,500, depending on the canton, experience, and shift assignments. Salaries are governed by cantonal pay scales, with additional allowances for night and weekend work. Entry-level positions start around CHF 5,000, while senior guards can earn over CHF 8,000.

**Q: Can non-Swiss citizens work as prison guards through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Prison guard positions are generally reserved for Swiss citizens or EU/EFTA nationals due to free movement agreements. Non-EU citizens may obtain a work visa only if no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card requires a tertiary degree and a minimum salary threshold (CHF 86,040 in 2024), which prison guard salaries typically do not meet. Thus, the Blue Card route is rarely applicable.

**Q: What training is required to become a prison guard in Switzerland?**
A: Candidates typically complete a 1–2 year vocational apprenticeship (Eidgenössisches Fähigkeitszeugnis) in correctional services or a specialized cantonal training program. Requirements include Swiss or EU citizenship, minimum age 21, good physical and mental health, and a clean criminal record. Training covers legal knowledge, self-defense, conflict resolution, and ethics.
