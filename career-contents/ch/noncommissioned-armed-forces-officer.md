## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for non-commissioned armed forces officers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for NCOs in the Swiss Armed Forces vary by rank and experience, typically ranging from CHF 60,000 to CHF 90,000 annually, plus allowances. Exact figures are defined by military pay scales and may include housing and family supplements.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA citizens work as non-commissioned armed forces officers in Switzerland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified non-EU workers, but military roles are generally reserved for Swiss citizens or, in limited cases, for citizens of EU/EFTA states due to security requirements. The Blue Card route is not applicable for armed forces positions; instead, employment is governed by specific military recruitment laws requiring Swiss citizenship or bilateral agreements.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for non-commissioned officers in the Swiss Armed Forces?**
A: NCOs can advance through ranks such as Sergeant, First Sergeant, and Chief NCO. Promotion depends on performance, leadership courses, and time in service. Opportunities include specialized roles in logistics, training, or military police, and further education at military academies to become an officer.
