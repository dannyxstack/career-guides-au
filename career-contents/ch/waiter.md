## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a waiter in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for waiters in Switzerland vary by canton and establishment, but the average monthly gross salary ranges from CHF 3,500 to CHF 4,500. Tips are often shared among staff and can add 10-20% to income. Minimum wages differ by canton; for example, in Geneva the minimum is around CHF 24 per hour.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EFTA national work as a waiter in Switzerland under the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Switzerland is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary threshold (at least CHF 88,000 in 2025). Waiters typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU/EFTA nationals may obtain a work permit only if the employer proves no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available, and quotas apply.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for waiters in Switzerland?**
A: Waiters can progress to head waiter, restaurant manager, or sommelier. Many attend vocational schools (e.g., École Hôtelière de Lausanne) for certifications. With experience, one can open their own restaurant or work in high-end establishments. Swiss hospitality values multilingual skills and professional training, often leading to higher positions.
