## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a police officer in Switzerland?**
A: The average starting salary for a police officer in Switzerland ranges from CHF 70,000 to CHF 90,000 per year, depending on the canton and level of experience. After several years, salaries can reach up to CHF 100,000–120,000. Additional allowances for shift work or special assignments may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as police officers in Switzerland through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Police officer positions in Switzerland are generally reserved for Swiss nationals or holders of a C permit (settlement permit). Non-EU/EFTA nationals rarely qualify as employers must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. The EU Blue Card, intended for highly qualified workers with a university degree and high salary, is not applicable as police roles typically require specific cantonal training, not academic qualifications.

**Q: What training is required to become a police officer in Switzerland?**
A: To become a police officer, candidates must complete a multi-year training program at a cantonal police academy, typically lasting 18–24 months. Requirements include Swiss or C permit status, minimum age (usually 18–20), good physical fitness, and a clean criminal record. Training covers law, self-defense, firearms, and community policing.
