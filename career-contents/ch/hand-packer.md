## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for hand packers in Switzerland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for hand packers in Switzerland ranges from CHF 3,500 to CHF 4,500, depending on experience, region, and sector. Hourly rates typically fall between CHF 18 and CHF 22. Salaries are often governed by collective bargaining agreements, and full-time positions usually include 13 monthly payments.

**Q: What are the work visa options for hand packers in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals have very limited options for hand packer roles, as these are low-skilled and subject to strict quotas. The EU Blue Card requires a tertiary qualification or equivalent experience, which typically excludes this occupation. Under the Swiss labour market test, employers must prove no suitable Swiss or EU worker is available. Short-term permits (L) or residence permits (B) may be possible if quotas allow.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for hand packers in Switzerland?**
A: Hand packers usually work full-time in warehouses, distribution centers, or manufacturing plants. Tasks include packaging, labelling, and quality checking goods. Shifts can be day, evening, or night, with overtime possible. Work is physically demanding, involving standing, lifting, and repetitive motions. Employers must adhere to strict Swiss health and safety regulations.
