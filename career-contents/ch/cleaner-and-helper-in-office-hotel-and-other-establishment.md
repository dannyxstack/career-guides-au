**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean floors using smart vacuum and mop robots
- Operation monitoring of automatic window cleaning robots
- Dust removal in standard areas such as corridors and halls following preset routes.
- Use automatic disinfection robots to disinfect bathrooms and kitchens.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize multi-site cleaning routes and schedules
- Use smart sensors to monitor dirt levels and precisely schedule cleaning frequency
- Receive health and safety alerts via wearable devices to enhance personal protection
- Adjusts service details using a customer review AI system to improve satisfaction
- Use digital tools to manage cleaning supplies, reducing waste

**Human moat:**

- Identification and targeted treatment of complex stubborn stains (e.g., carpet stains, oil stains).
- Communicate with clients to understand specific needs and flexibly adjust cleaning methods
- Ability to handle unexpected situations (e.g., breaking items, pet accidents)
- Fine cleaning and care of valuable items (e.g., antiques, silk)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners and helpers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer. Office cleaners typically earn between CHF 3,500 and CHF 4,500 per month, while hotel cleaners may earn slightly less. Hourly wages range from CHF 20 to CHF 25. Salaries often reflect the high cost of living.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa for cleaning jobs in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU nationals face very limited options. Switzerland prioritizes Swiss and EU/EFTA citizens. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and a high salary, which cleaning jobs do not meet. Work permits are rarely granted for this occupation unless the worker qualifies under a bilateral agreement or as a skilled professional in a shortage occupation.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for cleaners in Switzerland?**
A: With experience, cleaners can become team leaders or supervisors. Specializing in industrial or eco-friendly cleaning, or obtaining certifications in hygiene standards, can open doors to higher-paying roles. Some workers transition to facility management after additional training.
