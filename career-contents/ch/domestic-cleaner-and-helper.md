## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners in Switzerland?**
A: Hourly wages for domestic cleaners in Switzerland typically range from CHF 20 to CHF 30, depending on canton and employer. Some cantons have statutory minimum wages; for example, Geneva sets a minimum of CHF 24.32 per hour. Full-time annual salaries average around CHF 45,000–55,000 before tax.

**Q: What work visa options exist for domestic cleaners from non-EU countries to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals generally require a work permit. The EU Blue Card is not applicable to domestic cleaners as it targets highly qualified workers. Instead, employers must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available. For EU/EFTA citizens, free movement applies; they can work with a valid ID. Work permits are quota-limited.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for domestic cleaners in Switzerland?**
A: With experience, domestic cleaners can progress to supervisory roles in cleaning companies, specialize in green cleaning, or start their own cleaning business. Vocational training (e.g., in building cleaning) can lead to higher positions in hospitals or hotels. The Swiss system values additional certifications, offering pathways to better wages and responsibilities.
