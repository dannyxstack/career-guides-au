## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for survey and market research interviewers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and company, but entry-level positions typically pay between CHF 4,000 and CHF 5,000 per month. Experienced interviewers may earn up to CHF 6,500 monthly. Hourly rates for part-time roles often range from CHF 25 to CHF 35.

**Q: Can EU/EFTA citizens or non-EU nationals work as survey interviewers in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card or other routes?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens benefit from freedom of movement and may work without a visa. Non-EU nationals generally cannot obtain a work permit for this occupation due to low skill level. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified roles; survey interviewers do not qualify. Employer sponsorship is possible only if no Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available.

**Q: What are the career prospects for survey and market research interviewers in Switzerland?**
A: Entry-level roles often lead to senior interviewer, team leader, or research analyst positions with additional training. Progression into market research management or data analysis is possible. Fluency in German, French, or Italian is advantageous. The role is common in private research firms, NGOs, and government statistics offices.
