## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Switzerland?**
A: Entry-level labourers commonly earn between CHF 3,500 and CHF 4,500 per month. Hourly rates for seasonal work range from CHF 20 to CHF 25. Salaries may include non-cash benefits such as accommodation or meals. Pay varies by region, employer, and experience.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU/EFTA nationals to work as a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a work visa. Employers must show no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available (labour market test). The EU Blue Card is possible for roles requiring higher qualifications. Seasonal permits exist for short-term agricultural work but are restrictive.

**Q: What are the main duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Switzerland?**
A: Duties include planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops; feeding and caring for livestock; operating farm machinery; maintaining fences and buildings; and assisting with milking or shearing. Labourers work outdoors in varied weather, often on family farms or large estates, requiring physical fitness.
