## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an undertaker or embalmer in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for undertakers and embalmers in Switzerland vary by region and experience. Entry-level positions start around CHF 50,000 per year, while experienced professionals earn up to CHF 80,000. Additional certifications and employer size can influence pay.

**Q: Can foreign undertakers and embalmers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and a salary meeting minimum thresholds (currently CHF 66,000 for non-STEM fields). Bilateral agreements may also apply. Always verify with Swiss migration authorities.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as an undertaker or embalmer in Switzerland?**
A: In Switzerland, a federal vocational diploma (EFZ) in funeral services is standard, obtained through a 3-year apprenticeship. Embalmers need additional specialized training. Registration with the Swiss Funeral Directors Association (VBS) is recommended. Continuing education is required for career advancement.
