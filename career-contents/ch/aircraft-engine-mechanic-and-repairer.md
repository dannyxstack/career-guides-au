## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an aircraft engine mechanic in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for aircraft engine mechanics in Switzerland generally range from CHF 75,000 to CHF 110,000 per year, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions start around CHF 65,000, while senior specialists with certifications can earn up to CHF 120,000. These figures reflect gross annual income.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign aircraft engine mechanic to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA nationals benefit from free movement of persons and can work with a simple registration. Non-EU/EFTA nationals require a work permit via employer sponsorship; the employer must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is possible for those with a recognised tertiary qualification and a job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an aircraft engine mechanic in Switzerland?**
A: A Swiss Federal Diploma (EFZ) in aircraft maintenance, typically gained via a 4-year apprenticeship, is standard. Mechanics must also hold an EASA Part-66 license or equivalent for independent work. Continuous training is required to maintain certifications. Foreign qualifications may require recognition by the Swiss authorities.
