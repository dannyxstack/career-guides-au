## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for car, taxi, or van drivers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and employer, but full-time drivers typically earn between CHF 50,000 and CHF 70,000 gross per year. Taxi drivers often receive a base plus tips, while van drivers may have fixed contracts. Zurich and Geneva generally offer higher pay. Part-time and self-employed drivers earn less, and income depends on hours worked and client base.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-Swiss car/taxi/van drivers?**
A: EU/EFTA nationals can work as drivers under the Agreement on Free Movement. Non-EU/EFTA nationals face strict quotas and usually require a work permit obtained by their employer; the EU Blue Card is not available for driving occupations as it targets highly skilled workers. Cross-border commuters (G permit) from neighbouring EU countries are also common for this role.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a taxi or van driver in Switzerland?**
A: You need a valid Swiss driving licence (category B for cars, C1 for vans up to 3.5 tonnes, or C for heavier vehicles). For taxis, you must also obtain a professional driver's permit from the cantonal authorities, which requires a clean criminal record, a medical check, and often a local knowledge test. Some cantons require a course on passenger transport regulations.
