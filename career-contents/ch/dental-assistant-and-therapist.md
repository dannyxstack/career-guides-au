## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dental assistant in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for dental assistants in Switzerland vary by canton and experience. Entry-level assistants earn around CHF 4,000–5,000 per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to CHF 6,500. Salaries are higher in urban areas like Zurich or Geneva. Most positions include additional benefits such as pension contributions and health insurance.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as dental assistants in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer. Dental assistants typically require vocational training (EFZ) rather than a degree, so the Blue Card route is not usually applicable. Non-EU/EFTA citizens must instead obtain a work permit through a labor market test, while EU/EFTA nationals benefit from free movement.

**Q: What training is required to become a dental assistant in Switzerland?**
A: To become a dental assistant in Switzerland, you typically complete a three-year vocational apprenticeship (Eidgenössisches Fähigkeitszeugnis, EFZ) combining on-the-job training with classroom instruction. Alternatively, adults can pursue a two-year accelerated program. After qualification, assistants can specialize in areas like oral hygiene or dental prophylaxis.
