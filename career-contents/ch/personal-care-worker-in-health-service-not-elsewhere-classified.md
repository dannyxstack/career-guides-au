## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a personal care worker in health services in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and experience, but personal care workers in Switzerland earn around CHF 4,000–5,500 per month before tax. Pay scales are often regulated by cantonal collective labor agreements. Live-in care workers may receive lower cash wages but free accommodation and meals.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EFTA personal care worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not used in Switzerland. Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a Swiss work permit (B or L) sponsored by an employer. The employer must prove no Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available (labor market test). Quotas apply. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from free movement and need only registration.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to work as a personal care worker in Switzerland?**
A: Formal qualifications include a federal vocational diploma (EFZ) in health and social care (3 years) or a shorter certificate (2 years). Many employers require basic German, French, or Italian, depending on the region. Continuous education options include specialization in geriatric care or palliative care.
