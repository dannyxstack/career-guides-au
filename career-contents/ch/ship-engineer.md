## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for ships' engineers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for ships' engineers in Switzerland vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around CHF 60,000–80,000 annually, while experienced engineers can earn CHF 100,000–130,000. Factors include vessel type, operating area, and whether on inland or seagoing vessels. Salaries in Switzerland are generally high compared to other countries.

**Q: What are the official work visa options for ships' engineers from non-EU/EFTA countries to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals can obtain a Swiss work permit through the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified professionals) or a standard B permit if no suitable Swiss/EU workers are available. They must have a job offer, meet qualification requirements, and prove their salary and working conditions meet Swiss standards. Quotas apply.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as a ships' engineer in Switzerland?**
A: Ships' engineers in Switzerland must hold relevant STCW (Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping) certifications. For inland navigation, Swiss-specific licenses from the Swiss Federal Office of Transport (BAV) are required. Additional training in Swiss regulations and language skills (German, French, or Italian) may be necessary.
