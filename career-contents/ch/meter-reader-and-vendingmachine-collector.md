## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for meter readers in Switzerland generally range from CHF 50,000 to CHF 70,000 per year, depending on experience, employer, and region. Public utilities often offer higher pay and additional benefits.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU/EFTA meter readers in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA citizens can obtain a work permit only if they have specialized skills and no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available (subject to cantonal quotas). The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as this occupation does not require a university degree or meet the minimum salary threshold.

**Q: What training is required to become a meter reader in Switzerland?**
A: No formal certification is mandatory, but employers often prefer a completed vocational apprenticeship (e.g., as a utility technician) or on-the-job training. Familiarity with IT systems and a driver's license are commonly required.
