## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for aged care services managers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for aged care services managers in Switzerland generally range from CHF 80,000 to CHF 120,000 per year, depending on factors such as qualifications, experience, and the canton of employment. Additional benefits may include bonuses and pension contributions.

**Q: How can non-EU/EFTA citizens work as an aged care services manager in Switzerland? What about the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA citizens require a work permit, which involves employer sponsorship and cantonal approval. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals holding a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (CHF 85,560 in 2023). As aged care services management qualifies, this route can be pursued with an employer's support.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an aged care services manager in Switzerland?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in healthcare management, social work, or a related field is required, along with several years of experience in aged care. Additional certifications in gerontology or leadership are advantageous. Proficiency in the local language (German, French, or Italian) is essential for regulatory compliance and team management.
