## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Switzerland?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Switzerland earn between CHF 3,500 and CHF 4,500 gross per month, depending on canton, experience, and establishment. Pay is higher in cities like Zurich and Geneva. Overtime and tips may add to income, but living costs are also high.

**Q: Can non-EU kitchen helpers obtain a work visa for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA citizens face strict quotas. Kitchen helpers do not qualify for the EU Blue Card due to low skill and salary thresholds. Employers must prove no local candidate is available (labour market test) and typically need to sponsor a B permit, which is rare for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement options exist for kitchen helpers in Switzerland?**
A: With experience, kitchen helpers can advance to commis chef or cook roles. Completing vocational training (e.g., EFZ in cookery) opens doors to chef de partie or kitchen manager positions. Some also move into pastry or catering, often with higher pay.
