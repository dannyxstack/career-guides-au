## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Switzerland?**
A: In Switzerland, monthly gross salaries for electrical and electronic equipment assemblers typically range from CHF 4,500 to CHF 5,500, depending on experience, canton, and company. Some cantons have minimum wage laws (e.g., Geneva, Neuchâtel) that set a floor around CHF 3,800-4,200. Overtime and shift bonuses may apply.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU/EFTA nationals to work as an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals require a work permit (B or L). Employers must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available (labour market test). The EU Blue Card applies to highly qualified workers; for 2024, the minimum salary threshold is CHF 86,040, which exceeds typical assembler salaries. Thus, this route is rarely available for this occupation.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in Switzerland?**
A: With experience, assemblers can advance to team leader, quality inspector, or specialised assembly technician. Vocational further education (e.g., Federal Diploma of Professional Education) opens paths to production planner, mechatronics technician, or even engineering roles. Some employers offer internal training for higher-level assembly or automation tasks.
