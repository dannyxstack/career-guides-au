## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for judges in Switzerland?**
A: Judge salaries in Switzerland vary by canton and court level. Federal judges earn approximately CHF 250,000 to 300,000 per year. Cantonal judges typically earn between CHF 150,000 and 250,000 annually, depending on experience and the specific canton. Salaries are publicly regulated and adjusted periodically.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for foreign judges in Switzerland?**
A: Switzerland does not have a specific visa for judges. EU/EFTA citizens can apply for a residence permit to work. Non-EU nationals must have a job offer and the employer must prove no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is not typically used for judges; instead, a B permit for highly qualified workers may be issued.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a judge in Switzerland?**
A: To become a judge in Switzerland, one typically needs a law degree (licentiate or master's) and several years of legal practice. Judges are often elected or appointed by cantonal authorities. For federal judges, election by the Federal Assembly is required. Additional training may be provided by the Swiss Judicial Academy.
