## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electrical line installer in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for electrical line installers in Switzerland vary by experience and canton. Entry-level workers earn around CHF 50,000–60,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn CHF 70,000–90,000. The median is roughly CHF 65,000 per year. Overtime and hazard pay may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as electrical line installers in Switzerland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals may qualify for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized university degree (or equivalent) and a job offer with a salary above the threshold (CHF 85,440 in 2025). Alternatively, the Swiss work visa route requires employer sponsorship and labor market testing. Apprenticeship-based qualifications may also satisfy requirements.

**Q: What training is required to become an electrical line installer in Switzerland?**
A: Typically, a 3–4 year apprenticeship (EFZ) in electrical installation or a related field is required. This combines on-the-job training with vocational school. After completing the apprenticeship, you can specialize in line installation. Continuing education courses are available for advanced roles.
