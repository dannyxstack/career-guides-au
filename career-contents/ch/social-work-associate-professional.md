## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work associate professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton, experience, and employer. Entry-level positions often start around CHF 55,000–65,000 per year, while experienced professionals can earn CHF 75,000–90,000. Public sector roles may offer higher pay and additional benefits. Cost of living differences across cantons also affect net income.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU/EFTA social work associate professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals generally need a work permit through employer sponsorship. The employer must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available (labour market test). The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and is less common for this occupation; most permits are B or L permits subject to annual quotas.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a social work associate professional in Switzerland?**
A: Typically, a Swiss Federal Diploma of Vocational Education and Training (3–4 years) in social care or a related field is required. Alternatively, a bachelor’s degree in social work (FH or university) may be accepted. Recognition of foreign qualifications is handled by the Swiss Red Cross or cantonal authorities.
