## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bank tellers in Switzerland?**
A: Bank tellers in Switzerland earn between CHF 50,000 and CHF 70,000 per year, depending on experience, canton, and the financial institution. Entry-level positions start around CHF 45,000, while experienced tellers may reach CHF 75,000. Salaries are generally higher in cities like Zurich and Geneva due to higher cost of living.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as bank tellers in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Switzerland requires a university degree and a minimum salary threshold (CHF 85,000 in 2023). Bank teller positions typically do not meet this threshold, so non-EU nationals usually need a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and proof that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. Quotas apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bank teller in Switzerland?**
A: A commercial apprenticeship (KV) or a degree in banking/finance is common. Fluency in the local language (German, French, or Italian) is essential, along with strong customer service and numerical skills. Some employers also require a Swiss federal diploma in banking. On-the-job training is typically provided for specific tasks.
