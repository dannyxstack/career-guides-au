## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for web and multimedia developers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level developers earn around CHF 65,000–80,000 annually, while experienced developers (5+ years) earn CHF 90,000–120,000. Senior roles and specialists in Zurich or Geneva may exceed CHF 130,000. Gross salaries are subject to Swiss social security and income tax.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for web developers moving to Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a job offer from a Swiss employer, who must prove the position cannot be filled locally. The employer applies for a work permit. EU/EFTA citizens can use the EU Blue Card, requiring a valid employment contract and recognized qualifications. All applicants must meet salary thresholds and have no criminal record.

**Q: What qualifications or skills are most in demand for web developers in Switzerland?**
A: Employers seek proficiency in front-end (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React) and back-end (Node.js, Python, PHP) technologies. Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), responsive design, and agile methods is valued. A degree in computer science or equivalent experience is typical. Continuous learning is expected due to rapid tech changes.
