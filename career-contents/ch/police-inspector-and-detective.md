## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for police inspectors and detectives in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and experience. Entry-level inspectors earn around CHF 80,000–95,000 annually. Mid-career detectives can make CHF 100,000–120,000, while senior roles may exceed CHF 130,000. Pay includes allowances for shift work and specialist duties.

**Q: How can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as police inspectors or detectives in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a job offer from a Swiss employer who must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. A work permit (B permit) is then obtained. The EU Blue Card does not apply in Switzerland as it is not an EU member.

**Q: What is the career path to become a police inspector or detective in Switzerland?**
A: Candidates typically complete basic police training at a cantonal police academy, serve as patrol officers for several years, then apply for detective training. Promotion involves exams and practical assessments. Continuing education is required for specialization.
