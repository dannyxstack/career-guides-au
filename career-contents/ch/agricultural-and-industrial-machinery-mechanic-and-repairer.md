## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an agricultural and industrial machinery mechanic in Switzerland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for agricultural and industrial machinery mechanics in Switzerland ranges from CHF 5,000 to CHF 7,000, depending on experience, qualifications, and region. Entry-level positions may start around CHF 4,500, while experienced specialists can earn up to CHF 8,000 or more.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign machinery mechanics to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens have free movement and only need a residence permit. Non-EU/EFTA nationals require a work permit, granted only if no suitable Swiss/EU/EFTA candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals with a recognized university degree and a job offer meeting a minimum salary threshold (approx. CHF 85,000 in 2023).

**Q: What training or certification is needed to become a machinery mechanic in Switzerland?**
A: The standard path is a 3-4 year apprenticeship (Berufslehre) in agricultural or industrial machinery mechanics, combined with vocational school, leading to a Federal Certificate of Proficiency (EFZ). Experienced mechanics can pursue advanced certifications like master craftsman (Meister) or technician diplomas to specialize or take on supervisory roles.
