## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for these operators in Switzerland vary by experience and region, typically ranging from CHF 48,000 to CHF 65,000 per year. Entry-level positions start around CHF 45,000, while experienced operators can earn up to CHF 75,000. Wages are higher in industrial cantons like Zurich or Basel.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a university degree or equivalent high-level qualification, which this occupation typically does not require. Non-EU nationals may obtain a work permit only if the employer proves no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. The quota-based permit is subject to cantonal and federal approval.

**Q: What training or career advancement opportunities exist for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Switzerland?**
A: Most operators complete a 3-year apprenticeship (EFZ) in textile technology or dyeing. Career progression includes becoming a shift supervisor or textile technologist with further vocational education. Advanced roles in quality control or production management are possible with additional certifications.
