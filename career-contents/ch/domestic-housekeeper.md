## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic housekeepers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for domestic housekeepers in Switzerland vary by canton and employer, typically ranging from CHF 3,500 to CHF 4,500 per month gross for full-time work. Some cantons apply minimum wage laws, such as Geneva with CHF 4,300 per month. Private households may offer lower pay, while institutional roles in hotels or hospitals can pay more.

**Q: Can I work as a domestic housekeeper in Switzerland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for domestic housekeepers as it requires a higher education degree or highly qualified status. Non-EU/EFTA nationals can only obtain a work permit if a labor market test confirms no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. This is rare for housekeeping. EU/EFTA nationals benefit from freedom of movement and only need a residence permit upon finding a job.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for domestic housekeepers in Switzerland?**
A: Domestic housekeepers can advance to supervisory roles in hotels, cleaning companies, or private estates. Further training, such as a federal diploma in household management (eidg. Fachausweis Hauswirtschaft), can lead to roles like head of housekeeping. Self-employment as a cleaning service provider is also common, especially with a strong client network.
