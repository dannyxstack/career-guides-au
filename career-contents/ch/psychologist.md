## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Switzerland?**
A: The average annual salary for psychologists in Switzerland ranges from CHF 80,000 to CHF 120,000, depending on experience, specialization, and sector (public vs. private). Entry-level positions start around CHF 65,000, while senior roles in clinical or organizational psychology can exceed CHF 140,000. Salaries also vary by canton.

**Q: How can non-EU/EFTA psychologists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA psychologists must secure a job offer from a Swiss employer, who then applies for a work permit (B permit) or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a recognized degree and a salary at least 50% above the minimum threshold (approx. CHF 85,000/year). Quotas apply, and priority is given to Swiss/EU candidates. Work cannot begin until the permit is approved.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a licensed psychologist in Switzerland?**
A: To practice as a psychologist in Switzerland, you need a master's degree in psychology from a recognized university, followed by a postgraduate specialization (e.g., clinical, child, or work psychology) accredited by the Federal Office of Public Health. Registration with the Swiss Psychological Association (FSP) or cantonal health authorities is mandatory. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Swiss Red Cross or MEBEKO.
