## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a stonemason in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for stonemasons in Switzerland vary by canton and experience. Entry-level workers earn around CHF 45,000–55,000 annually, while experienced craftsmen can earn CHF 65,000–80,000. Wages are often set by collective bargaining agreements. In high-cost areas like Zurich or Geneva, pay tends to be higher.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign stonemason to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens can work freely under the Agreement on Free Movement. Non-EU nationals need a work permit through a quota system, requiring employer sponsorship and proof of labor shortage. The EU Blue Card is available only for non-EU workers with a recognized tertiary degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary (around CHF 86,000 in 2024). Stonemasons without a degree rarely qualify for the Blue Card but may obtain a standard permit if conditions are met.
