## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for manufacturing labourers in Switzerland?**
A: In Switzerland, manufacturing labourers (ISCO 9329) earn a monthly gross salary typically between CHF 4,000 and CHF 5,500, depending on region, industry, and employer. Collective labour agreements may set minimum wages in some sectors. Salaries vary by canton, with urban areas generally paying higher.

**Q: What visa routes allow non-EU/EFTA nationals to work as manufacturing labourers in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized university degree and a job offer with salary above the annual threshold (approx. CHF 86,000 for 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit requires employer sponsorship and proof that no qualified Swiss or EU worker is available. Annual quotas apply for both routes.

**Q: What are typical duties and career prospects for manufacturing labourers in Switzerland?**
A: Typical duties include assembling products, operating machinery, packing goods, and maintaining cleanliness. Career advancement can lead to positions such as machine operator, team leader, or quality inspector. Further vocational training or industry certifications enhance prospects, especially in high-precision manufacturing sectors common in Switzerland.
