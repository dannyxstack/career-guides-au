## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dietician in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for dieticians in Switzerland vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions typically earn CHF 60,000–70,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn CHF 80,000–100,000. Those in managerial roles or private practice may earn more. Salaries tend to be higher in German-speaking cantons and urban areas like Zurich or Geneva.

**Q: How can a foreign dietician obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA dieticians need a work permit, which requires a confirmed job offer and proof that no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available. The employer applies for a B permit. The EU Blue Card is rarely used for this profession; instead, standard work permits apply. EU/EFTA nationals benefit from free movement and simply register upon finding employment.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a dietician in Switzerland?**
A: You need a recognized bachelor's degree in dietetics or nutrition. Registration with the Swiss Association of Registered Dietitians (SVDE) is mandatory. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Swiss Red Cross or MEBEKO. Proficiency in a national language (German, French, or Italian) is essential for clinical practice.
