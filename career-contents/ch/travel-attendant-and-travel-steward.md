## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a travel attendant in Switzerland?**
A: Entry-level cabin crew at Swiss airlines earn about CHF 3,300–4,000 gross per month, plus flying allowances and per diems. Experienced attendants can earn CHF 4,500–6,000 monthly. Salaries vary by airline and are often governed by collective agreements.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for travel attendants in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a work permit with employer sponsorship; the EU Blue Card requires a job offer and salary at least CHF 86,040 (2023). Travel attendant roles rarely meet this threshold, so most permits are subject to the Labour Market Test. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for travel attendants in Switzerland?**
A: Travel attendants can progress to senior cabin crew, purser, or instructor roles. Swiss airlines promote internally after a few years of service. Ground positions in training, scheduling, or management are also common career paths, often requiring additional qualifications.
