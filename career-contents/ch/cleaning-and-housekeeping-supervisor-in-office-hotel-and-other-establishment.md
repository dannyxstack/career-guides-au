## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and establishment type. On average, a full-time supervisor earns between CHF 4,500 and CHF 6,000 per month before tax. Hotels in premium locations and larger offices may pay higher. Experience and language skills also influence pay. Overtime and shift work can add extra income.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a work visa, typically via the EU Blue Card. The job must be highly qualified, usually requiring a tertiary degree or equivalent experience. The employer must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. Quotas apply for third-country nationals.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Switzerland?**
A: A vocational training certificate (e.g., as a housekeeper or cleaning specialist) or several years of experience in cleaning services is common. Supervisory skills are often gained on the job or through courses. Knowledge of cleaning products, equipment, and hygiene standards is essential. German, French, or Italian language skills (typically B1/B2) are required depending on the region.
