## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for fashion and other models in Switzerland?**
A: Pay varies widely by sector and experience. Commercial models earn CHF 100–300 per hour, while high-fashion models may earn CHF 500–1,000 per show or shoot, though assignments are less frequent. Models are usually self-employed, so income is irregular. According to industry sources, average annual earnings range from CHF 30,000 to 80,000 before expenses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign models to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA models can obtain a short-term work permit (up to 120 days) if a Swiss employer sponsors them and the cantonal labour market test is passed. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles (salary threshold approx. CHF 85,000) but rarely applies to modelling. EU/EFTA nationals have free movement and need only register upon arrival.

**Q: What qualifications or skills are needed to become a model in Switzerland?**
A: No formal qualifications are required, but agencies typically require a professional portfolio, specific height and size ranges (e.g., 172–180 cm for fashion models), and proficiency in at least one national language (German, French, or Italian). Good communication, adaptability, and the ability to travel are essential. Most models start by joining a reputable agency.
