## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Switzerland?**
A: The salary for sweepers and related labourers in Switzerland varies by canton and employer. According to Swiss salary data, monthly gross wages typically range from CHF 3,500 to CHF 4,200 for full-time work. Hourly rates often fall between CHF 20 and CHF 25. Some cantons have minimum wage laws that set a floor, but no national minimum wage exists.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals obtain a work visa as a sweeper in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals face strict quotas for low-skilled occupations like sweepers. An employer must prove no Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available (priority check). The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires higher education or specialized skills. Only limited monthly quotas exist for third-country nationals in manual labour, making it very difficult.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions and career prospects for sweepers in Switzerland?**
A: Sweepers in Switzerland often work outdoors in all weather, with tasks including street cleaning, litter collection, and snow removal. Hours are usually early morning or shift work. Career progression is limited but possible into supervisory roles or specialized cleaning equipment operation. Some employers offer vocational training certificates that can improve prospects.
