## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for companions and valets in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Switzerland vary widely based on experience, employer, and region. Typically, salaries range from CHF 3,500 to CHF 5,500 per month gross. Many positions are governed by collective labor agreements, and wages may include accommodation or meals. Actual pay depends on the specific role and client profile.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU/EFTA companions and valets wanting to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals can work as companions or valets in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card if they hold a university degree or equivalent and have a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold. Alternatively, if the employer can prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available, a standard work permit may be granted. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from free movement.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required to work as a companion or valet in Switzerland?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers often seek candidates with relevant experience, discretion, and excellent interpersonal skills. Knowledge of multiple languages (German, French, or Italian depending on the region) and driving skills are advantageous. Some valets may require a chauffeuring license or security training. References from previous high-profile clients are highly valued.
