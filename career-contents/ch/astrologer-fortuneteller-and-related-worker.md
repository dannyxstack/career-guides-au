## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Switzerland?**
A: Income varies significantly as most are self-employed. Hourly rates typically range from CHF 50 to 150, depending on reputation and services. Full-time earnings are uncommon; many practice part-time. Official salary data is not available as the profession is not regulated.

**Q: Can non-EU astrologers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA face strict rules. For a work visa or Blue Card, you need a job offer with a salary meeting the threshold (approx. CHF 85,000 in 2024) and recognized qualifications. Astrology rarely qualifies as a specialized skill, so permits are seldom granted. EU/EFTA citizens can work freely.

**Q: What professional requirements exist for astrologers in Switzerland?**
A: No specific state certification is required. Practitioners often join voluntary associations like the Swiss Astrological Association (SAA) for credibility. Skills in counseling and languages (German, French, Italian) are beneficial. Competition is high, and success depends on building a client base through reputation and networking.
