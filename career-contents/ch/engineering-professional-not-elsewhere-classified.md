## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and experience. For a mid-career engineer, the gross annual salary typically ranges from CHF 80,000 to 120,000. Senior roles may exceed CHF 150,000. Higher salaries are common in pharmaceuticals, machinery, and IT sectors.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU engineering professional to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work permit through employer sponsorship after proving no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a recognized diploma and a job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds set by the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs.

**Q: Which industries in Switzerland most commonly hire engineering professionals not elsewhere classified?**
A: Engineering professionals are in high demand in Switzerland across sectors like mechanical and electrical engineering, pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, information technology, construction, and environmental technology. Many work for multinational corporations or SMEs in manufacturing and tech.
