## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, industry, and region. For a mid-level professional, expect CHF 80,000–120,000 per year. Senior roles can reach CHF 150,000 or more. Commission and bonuses are common, especially in medical sales. Zurich and Geneva tend to offer higher pay due to higher living costs.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU/EFTA Technical and Medical Sales Professionals seeking to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals typically need a job offer from a Swiss employer, who must prove that no suitable local candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is possible for highly qualified professionals, requiring a recognized degree and a minimum salary threshold (updated annually). Work permits are quota-limited and easier for senior or specialized roles.

**Q: What qualifications or skills are needed to succeed as a Technical or Medical Sales Professional in Switzerland?**
A: A bachelor’s degree in a relevant field (e.g., engineering, life sciences) is often required. Strong sales acumen, fluency in at least two Swiss national languages (German, French, or Italian) plus English, and knowledge of the local healthcare or industrial landscape are key. Experience with CRM tools and negotiation skills are highly valued.
