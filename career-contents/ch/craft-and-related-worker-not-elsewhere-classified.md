## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specific craft, experience, and location. The median annual gross salary ranges from CHF 50,000 to CHF 80,000. Entry-level workers may earn around CHF 45,000, while experienced specialists can exceed CHF 90,000. These figures are based on data from the Swiss Federal Statistical Office and industry surveys.

**Q: How can non-EU/EFTA nationals obtain a work visa for craft occupations in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals face strict quotas. Employers must prove that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available (labor market test). The EU Blue Card is generally reserved for highly qualified professionals (tertiary education or equivalent) and usually not applicable to craft occupations. EU/EFTA nationals have free movement and require only registration.

**Q: What types of jobs are classified under 'Craft and Related Workers Not Elsewhere Classified' in Switzerland?**
A: This category includes specialized craft occupations not covered by other ISCO groups, such as musical instrument makers, jewelry workers, glassblowers, engravers, and pottery artists. It also covers workers in trades like tentmakers, wigmakers, and basket weavers. These roles require specific vocational training and often an apprenticeship.
