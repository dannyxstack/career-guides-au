## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers, package deliverers, and luggage porters in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and employer. Full-time workers typically earn between CHF 3,500 and CHF 5,000 gross per month. Luggage porters at airports or train stations may earn slightly more. Zurich and Geneva tend to offer higher wages. Overtime and tips can supplement income.

**Q: Can EU/EFTA citizens work as a messenger or package deliverer in Switzerland without a visa?**
A: Yes, EU/EFTA citizens benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement of Persons and can work in Switzerland without a visa. They must register with the local commune within 14 days of arrival. Non-EU/EFTA citizens generally need a work visa, which is rarely granted for this occupation unless they qualify as highly skilled.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a messenger or package deliverer in Switzerland?**
A: No formal education is required, but a valid driving license (for motorized delivery) is often necessary. Employers value reliability, physical fitness, and language skills (German, French, or Italian, depending on region). On-the-job training is common. A clean criminal record may be required.
