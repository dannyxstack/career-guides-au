## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for economists in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for economists in Switzerland vary by experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions start around CHF 80,000 per year, while experienced economists earn CHF 120,000–150,000 or more. The median salary for economists is approximately CHF 110,000 annually, with higher pay in banking, consulting, and international organizations.

**Q: What are the work visa options for economists moving to Switzerland from abroad?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA economists typically need an employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles meeting salary thresholds (CHF 85,560 in 2024). EU/EFTA nationals benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement and only need to register with local authorities after securing a job.

**Q: Which sectors employ economists in Switzerland?**
A: Economists in Switzerland work across banking and finance (UBS, Credit Suisse), insurance, consulting, government (e.g., SECO), international organizations (WTO, WHO, ILO), and academia. The pharmaceutical and manufacturing sectors also hire economists for market analysis and forecasting.
