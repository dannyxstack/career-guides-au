## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Buyer in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for Buyers in Switzerland vary by experience and industry. Entry-level positions start around CHF 60,000–70,000 annually, while senior buyers earn CHF 90,000–120,000. The median salary is approximately CHF 80,000. Bonuses and benefits may add 10–20%. Costs of living, especially in cities like Zurich or Geneva, can be high.

**Q: How can a non-EU/EFTA national work as a Buyer in Switzerland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a job offer meeting salary thresholds (at least CHF 85,560 in 2024 for most sectors). The employer must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is issued for highly qualified roles, including Buyers with relevant experience. Work permits are canton-authorised, and quotas apply yearly.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Buyers in Switzerland?**
A: Buyers can progress to Senior Buyer, Procurement Manager, or Supply Chain Director. Many upskill in negotiation, analytics, or SAP. Multilingualism (German, French, English) is a strong advantage. Certifications like CPSM or Swiss Federal Diploma in Procurement can boost prospects. Industries such as pharmaceuticals, machinery, or retail offer senior roles.
