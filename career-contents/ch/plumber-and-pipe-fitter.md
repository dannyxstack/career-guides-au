## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for plumbers and pipe fitters in Switzerland?**
A: In Switzerland, plumbers and pipe fitters earn a gross monthly salary between CHF 5,000 and CHF 6,500, depending on experience and region. Annual salaries typically range from CHF 60,000 to CHF 78,000. Wages are higher in cities like Zurich and Geneva. Apprentices earn less, while experienced professionals with additional certifications can earn more.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for plumbers and pipe fitters moving to Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens have free movement and can work as plumbers without a visa. Non-EU/EFTA nationals typically need a work permit, which requires an employer to prove no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a university degree, while plumbing is a vocational trade. Instead, a standard work permit with proof of recognized vocational qualifications is the route.

**Q: What training or apprenticeship is required to become a plumber in Switzerland?**
A: To become a plumber in Switzerland, you must complete a 3- to 4-year federal vocational education and training (VET) apprenticeship, combining on-the-job training and classroom instruction. After earning a Federal VET Diploma, you can specialize further. Ongoing professional development is available, and experienced plumbers can become master craftsmen or open their own business.
