## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for window cleaners in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for window cleaners in Switzerland vary by experience, region, and employer. On average, a full-time window cleaner earns between CHF 3,500 and CHF 4,500 gross per month. In high-cost cantons like Zurich, wages may be higher. Additional compensation includes 13th salary and overtime pay. Salaries are subject to Swiss social deductions.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a window cleaner in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not applicable in Switzerland as it is not an EU member state. Non-EU nationals must instead apply for a Swiss work permit (typically a B permit) through an employer sponsor. The process requires proving that no suitable Swiss or EU worker is available (labor market test) and is subject to annual quotas. Self-employment routes are also possible but strict.

**Q: What training or certification is required to become a window cleaner in Switzerland?**
A: Formal education is not mandatory, but many window cleaners complete an apprenticeship (3 years) as a building cleaner (EFZ). For high-rise work, additional safety certifications (e.g., SUVA-approved rope access) are required. Continuous training in handling chemicals and working at heights is recommended. On-the-job training is common.
