## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for underwater divers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Switzerland vary by experience, sector, and project location. Entry-level divers earn around CHF 4,500–6,000 gross per month, while experienced commercial divers can earn CHF 7,000–10,000 or more. Offshore or deep-sea work often pays higher, but seasonal and contract work is common. Salaries reflect Switzerland’s high cost of living.

**Q: How can non-EU/EFTA underwater divers obtain a work visa for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA divers must secure a job offer and the employer must prove no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available (labor market test). Work permits are quota-limited and typically require a university degree or equivalent experience. The EU Blue Card is not used in Switzerland; instead, permits B or L apply. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement and only need registration.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for underwater divers in Switzerland?**
A: Underwater divers in Switzerland can advance from commercial diving to supervisory roles, diving instructor, or underwater inspection technician. Specialized certifications (e.g., in welding, photogrammetry, or hyperbaric medicine) boost prospects. Some divers transition to offshore energy, underwater construction, or scientific diving. Swiss lakes and rivers offer year-round inland work, though competition exists.
