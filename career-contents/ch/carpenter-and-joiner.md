## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for carpenters and joiners in Switzerland?**
A: The average gross annual salary for carpenters and joiners in Switzerland ranges from CHF 55,000 to CHF 75,000, depending on experience, region, and employer. Skilled professionals with several years of experience can earn up to CHF 90,000. Wages are typically higher in urban areas like Zurich, Geneva, and Basel, and are governed by collective labor agreements in some cantons.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for carpenters and joiners to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens can work in Switzerland under the Agreement on Free Movement of Persons. Non-EU/EFTA nationals require a work permit through employer sponsorship, with proof that no suitable Swiss or EU worker is available. Carpenters and joiners generally do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree or equivalent. Instead, they may obtain a residence permit under the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals if their skills are in shortage.

**Q: How do carpenters and joiners qualify and advance in Switzerland?**
A: Qualification typically requires completing a Swiss Federal VET Diploma (EFZ) through a 3-4 year apprenticeship combining on-the-job training and vocational school. Advancement options include earning a Federal Diploma of Professional Education and Training (BP) to become a foreman, or a Higher Professional Examination (HFP) to become a master carpenter. Continuing education in areas like timber construction or restoration can also enhance career prospects.
