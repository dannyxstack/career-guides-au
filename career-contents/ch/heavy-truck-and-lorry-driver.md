## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for heavy truck drivers in Switzerland?**
A: Heavy truck drivers in Switzerland earn between CHF 4,500 and CHF 6,500 gross per month, depending on experience, canton, and employer. The median salary is around CHF 5,200. Some drivers receive additional allowances for night work or long-distance routes.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as heavy truck drivers in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified non-EU/EFTA nationals. Heavy truck driving does not typically meet the skilled profession requirement. However, a work permit may be possible through the quota system for third-country nationals if the employer proves that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a heavy truck driver in Switzerland?**
A: You need a valid Swiss category C driving licence, a professional driver qualification (CZV/CPC) for trucks, and a medical certificate. For hazardous goods, an ADR certificate is required. Many employers also expect fluency in German, French, or Italian, depending on the region.
