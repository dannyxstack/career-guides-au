## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a beautician in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for beauticians in Switzerland vary by experience and canton, typically ranging from CHF 3,500 to CHF 5,000 gross per month. Skilled professionals in high-end salons or with a loyal clientele may earn more. Part-time or commission-based arrangements are common, affecting take-home pay.

**Q: Can I work as a beautician in Switzerland through the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with tertiary education, which beauticians rarely meet. Non-EU citizens must secure a work permit via employer sponsorship, requiring proof that no suitable Swiss or EEA candidate is available. Cantonal labor offices assess applications; quotas may apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a beautician in Switzerland?**
A: A federal vocational certificate (Eidgenössisches Fähigkeitszeugnis) in beauty care is standard, obtained through a 3-year apprenticeship. Alternatively, comparable foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Swiss Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SERI). Fluent German, French, or Italian is essential for client communication.
