## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for database and network professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level roles earn around CHF 75,000–90,000 annually, while senior professionals can earn CHF 110,000–140,000+ per year. Zurich and Geneva typically offer higher pay, and certifications like CISSP or AWS can boost earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU database and network professionals to Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU nationals generally need a work permit tied to a specific job. The EU Blue Card is available for those with a recognized degree and a job offer paying at least CHF 85,800 (2025). Other options include the Swiss work permit (B or L) for highly skilled roles subject to labor market testing.

**Q: What career progression is typical for database and network professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Professionals often start as administrators or engineers, progressing to senior specialist, team lead, or architect roles. Certifications such as Oracle DBA, Microsoft Azure, or Cisco CCNP are valued. Many transition into IT management, cloud architecture, or data engineering with experience.
