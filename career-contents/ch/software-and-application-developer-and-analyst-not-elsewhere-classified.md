## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a software developer in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and canton, but a mid-level software developer in Switzerland typically earns between CHF 90,000 and CHF 130,000 per year. Zurich and Geneva tend to offer higher salaries, while smaller cantons may be lower. Benefits often include pension contributions and bonuses.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU software developers to work in Switzerland, and does the EU Blue Card apply?**
A: Switzerland is not an EU member, so the EU Blue Card does not apply. Non-EU/EFTA nationals must obtain a Swiss work permit (B permit) for highly skilled workers. This requires a job offer, employer sponsorship, and often a labor market test. Quotas apply, especially for third-country nationals.

**Q: Are there growing opportunities for software developers specializing in artificial intelligence in Switzerland?**
A: Yes, Switzerland has a strong AI ecosystem, with leading research institutions like ETH Zurich and EPFL, and a growing number of AI startups. Major companies in pharma, finance, and manufacturing also invest in AI. Opportunities are concentrated in Zurich, Lausanne, and Geneva, with demand for machine learning and data engineering skills.
