## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' deck crews in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for ships' deck crews in Switzerland vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions earn around CHF 3,500–4,500 per month, while experienced crew members can earn CHF 5,000–6,500. Overtime and allowances for sea time may increase total compensation. Wages are regulated by collective labour agreements where applicable.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign ships' deck crew in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a work permit, granted only if no Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available. The EU Blue Card is not typical for deck crews as it requires a university degree. For EU/EFTA citizens, freedom of movement applies. Short-term assignments may use a 120-day permit. Employer sponsorship is required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for deck crew jobs in Switzerland?**
A: Swiss waterways (lakes and Rhine) require a Swiss Boatmaster's License (Schiffsführerausweis) for deck officers. Crew members need basic safety training (e.g., STCW). Prior experience on inland vessels is valued. Apprenticeships as a Schifffahrtsberuf are common. Language skills in German or French are often required.
