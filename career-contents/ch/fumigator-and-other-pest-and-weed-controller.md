## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and experience. Entry-level positions start around CHF 45,000–55,000 per year, while experienced professionals earn CHF 60,000–75,000. Those with specialized certifications or supervisory roles may earn upwards of CHF 80,000. These figures are gross annual salaries and may include bonuses or overtime.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as fumigators in Switzerland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation. Non-EU/EFTA nationals must obtain a Swiss work visa, which requires a confirmed job offer, labor market test proving no suitable Swiss/EU candidate, and meeting cantonal quotas. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from free movement; non-EU applicants face strict annual quotas and must demonstrate specialized skills.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a fumigator in Switzerland?**
A: You need a completed vocational training (EFZ) in a related field (e.g., agriculture, forestry) or an equivalent qualification. Specialized courses in pest control and safe chemical handling are mandatory. Certification by the Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN) is required for restricted chemicals. Ongoing education is recommended to stay updated on regulations.
