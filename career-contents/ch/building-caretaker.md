## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Switzerland?**
A: The gross annual salary for building caretakers in Switzerland typically ranges from CHF 50,000 to CHF 70,000, depending on experience, region, and employer. The median salary is around CHF 60,000 per year, according to Swiss salary data.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as building caretakers in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally reserved for highly qualified workers with a tertiary education, which may not apply to building caretakers. Non-EU/EFTA citizens typically need an employer-sponsored work permit proving that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. This occupation often falls under the quota for third-country nationals.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a building caretaker in Switzerland?**
A: A vocational apprenticeship (Berufslehre) as a property caretaker (Hauswart) or equivalent experience is common. Skills in building maintenance, cleaning, and basic technical tasks are needed. Proficiency in the local language (German, French, or Italian) is essential, and some roles require a valid driver's license.
