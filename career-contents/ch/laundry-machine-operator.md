## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for laundry machine operators in Switzerland?**
A: The average annual gross salary for laundry machine operators in Switzerland ranges from CHF 45,000 to CHF 55,000, depending on experience, canton, and employer. Hourly rates typically fall between CHF 22 and CHF 28. Wages are regulated by collective bargaining agreements in the textile services sector, and entry-level positions may start lower.

**Q: What visa routes exist for non-EU laundry machine operators to work in Switzerland?**
A: Switzerland does not use the EU Blue Card. Non-EU nationals need a work permit (B or L) sponsored by an employer, who must prove that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available. Quotas apply, and the occupation must be listed in shortage. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from free movement and need only register their residence.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for laundry machine operators in Switzerland?**
A: With experience, laundry machine operators can advance to supervisory roles, such as shift leader or laundry manager. Further training, like a Federal Diploma in Textile Cleaning, can open doors to specialist positions in industrial laundering or dry cleaning. Some operators move into quality control or machinery maintenance.
