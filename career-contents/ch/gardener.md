## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Switzerland?**
A: The average annual salary for full-time gardeners in Switzerland ranges from CHF 50,000 to CHF 70,000, depending on experience, canton, and employer. Salaries tend to be higher in urban areas like Zurich and Geneva. Entry-level positions may start around CHF 45,000, while experienced gardeners can earn up to CHF 80,000.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign gardeners to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens can work as gardeners in Switzerland under the free movement of persons agreement. For non-EU/EFTA nationals, the EU Blue Card is not typically available for gardeners due to the low salary threshold and skill requirements. Instead, a standard work permit may be issued if the employer can prove that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available. Labor market tests and quotas apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a gardener in Switzerland?**
A: In Switzerland, gardeners typically complete a three-year apprenticeship (EFZ) in horticulture or landscape gardening. Alternatively, relevant work experience and vocational training may be accepted. Certification from the Swiss Association of Professional Gardeners is advantageous. Formal qualifications are often required for senior positions or to start a business.
