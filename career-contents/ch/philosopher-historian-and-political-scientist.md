## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Switzerland?**
A: Annual salaries vary widely by sector and experience. In academia, entry-level positions start around CHF 70,000–90,000, while senior roles can exceed CHF 130,000. Outside academia, such as in public administration or think tanks, salaries range from CHF 80,000 to 120,000. Private sector political consultants may earn higher.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign philosophers, historians, and political scientists to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA nationals benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement, allowing them to take up employment without a visa but requiring registration. Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a work permit, typically through the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals, which requires a recognized degree and a job offer with a salary above the threshold (CHF 86,040 in 2024). Quotas apply.

**Q: Where do philosophers, historians, and political scientists typically find employment in Switzerland?**
A: Common employers include universities and research institutes (e.g., ETH Zurich, University of Zurich), federal and cantonal administrations, think tanks (e.g., foraus), international organizations (e.g., UN, WTO in Geneva), media, and non-profits. Job openings are concentrated in urban centers like Zurich, Geneva, Bern, and Basel.
