## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a child care worker in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton and experience. Entry-level workers earn around CHF 45,000–55,000 per year, while experienced staff can earn CHF 60,000–75,000. Public sector jobs often pay higher than private. Wages are regulated by collective agreements in some regions.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as child care workers in Switzerland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals generally face strict quotas. The EU Blue Card is not available for child care as it requires a university degree or higher qualification. Work permits are only granted if no Swiss or EU/EFTA candidate is available, and the employer must prove a labor market shortage.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a child care worker in Switzerland?**
A: A Federal VET Diploma (EFZ) in child care or a related field is standard. Alternatively, a tertiary-level qualification in social pedagogy or early childhood education may be required. Language skills (local language at B2/C1 level) are essential. Continuous training is often mandatory.
