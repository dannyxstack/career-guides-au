## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for spray painters and varnishers in Switzerland?**
A: Spray painters in Switzerland earn between CHF 55,000 and CHF 75,000 gross per year, depending on experience, canton, and company. Entry-level positions start around CHF 45,000, while experienced professionals can reach up to CHF 85,000. Wages are typically higher in major cities like Zurich and Geneva.

**Q: What are the work visa options for a non-EU spray painter to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals require a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree or equivalent professional experience. For spray painters, a recognized vocational qualification (e.g., 3-4 year apprenticeship) and a job offer are necessary. The employer must usually prove no suitable Swiss or EU candidate (labor market test). Quotas and cantonal approval apply.

**Q: What training is required to become a spray painter in Switzerland?**
A: The standard path is a 3-year vocational apprenticeship (EFZ) in surface coating technology or a related trade. This combines on-the-job training at a company with classroom instruction at a vocational school. After apprenticeship, opportunities include a master craftsman diploma (Meister) or specialization in automotive or industrial painting.
