## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sociologists and anthropologists in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around CHF 70,000–80,000 per year. Mid-career professionals earn CHF 90,000–120,000, while senior roles in research or consulting can exceed CHF 130,000. Public sector pay tends to be lower than private industry.

**Q: How can non-EU/EFTA sociologists and anthropologists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals need a job offer and work permit, subject to quotas and labor market testing. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a recognized degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. CHF 86,400 in 2024). Employers must prove no suitable Swiss/EU candidate exists.

**Q: What are typical employers for sociologists and anthropologists in Switzerland?**
A: Common employers include universities and research institutes (e.g., ETH Zurich, University of Zurich), market research firms, NGOs, government agencies (e.g., Federal Statistical Office), and private companies in sectors like HR, marketing, or international organizations. Freelance consulting is also an option.
