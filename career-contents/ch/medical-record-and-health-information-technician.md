## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for medical records technicians in Switzerland?**
A: Annual gross salaries for medical records and health information technicians in Switzerland range from approximately CHF 60,000 to CHF 85,000, depending on experience, canton, and employer type (public vs. private). Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior roles in large hospitals or specialized clinics can exceed CHF 90,000.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as medical records technicians in Switzerland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers earning at least 150% of the average Swiss wage (CHF 86,400 in 2024). For this occupation, meeting that threshold usually requires senior roles or specialized certifications. Employers must also prove no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available (labor market test).

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for medical records technicians in Switzerland?**
A: Technicians can advance to health information management roles, coding specialists, or positions in health data analytics. Further training (e.g., eHealth, informatics) and certifications like HIMSS or CHIMA certification enhance prospects. Some move into IT support for electronic health records or lead compliance teams in hospitals or insurance firms.
