**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a handicraft worker in wood, basketry, and related materials in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and canton. Entry-level workers earn around CHF 4,000–4,500 per month, while experienced artisans can reach CHF 5,500–6,500. Many are self-employed, so income depends on client base. Apprentices earn less during training.

**Q: What is the work visa route for a non-EU handicraft worker to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit tied to a specific employer, who must prove no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available (labor market test). The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers, typically requiring a university degree and higher salary threshold, which may not apply to many handicraft roles. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from free movement.

**Q: What training is required to become a handicraft worker in wood, basketry, and related materials in Switzerland?**
A: Switzerland's dual vocational system requires a 3–4 year apprenticeship (EFZ) combining on-the-job training with vocational school. Specializations include carpentry, wood carving, or basket weaving. A master craftsman diploma (Meister) enables self-employment and training apprentices.
