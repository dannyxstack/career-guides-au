## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for dancers and choreographers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, employer, and project. For dancers, monthly gross pay often ranges from CHF 3,500 to CHF 5,500, while choreographers may earn CHF 4,500 to CHF 7,000. Income is often project-based, and many work part-time or on short-term contracts. Public subsidies and grants can supplement earnings for those in state-funded institutions.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA dancers or choreographers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals typically need a work permit, which requires an employer to prove that no suitable Swiss or EU/EFTA worker is available. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and high salary, which may not apply to most dancers/choreographers. The main route is a temporary permit for cultural activities, often limited to short-term projects.

**Q: What are the career prospects for dancers and choreographers in Switzerland?**
A: Switzerland has a vibrant dance scene, with opportunities in theaters, dance companies, and independent projects. Major cities like Zurich, Geneva, and Basel offer the most possibilities. Many dancers combine performance with teaching or choreography. Competition is high, and networking is key. Career advancement often relies on building a strong portfolio and securing recurring engagements.
