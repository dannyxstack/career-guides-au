## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building structure cleaners in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer, but building structure cleaners in Switzerland typically earn between CHF 4,000 and CHF 5,000 gross per month. This can be higher in cities like Zurich or Geneva. Experience and specialization, such as working at heights, may increase pay. Social security contributions and insurance costs are deducted.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as building structure cleaners in Switzerland via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a university degree or equivalent, making it unsuitable for building cleaners without higher education. However, non-EU workers may obtain a standard Swiss work permit (B permit) if the employer proves no local or EU candidate is available and meets regional quotas. Application is employer-driven and subject to cantonal approval.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions for building structure cleaners in Switzerland?**
A: Building structure cleaners often work outdoors at heights, using specialized equipment like industrial rope access. Work is seasonal and project-based. Full-time employment is common, but many work part-time. Swiss labor laws ensure strict safety regulations, paid leave, and accident insurance. Knowledge of German, French, or Italian is advantageous.
