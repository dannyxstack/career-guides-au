**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to generate CNC cutting paths for automatic metal sheet cutting
- Repetitive bending and punching operations done by robots
- Automated welding systems take over standard joint welding
- AI vision inspection systems replace manual surface defect inspection
- Nesting optimization algorithms automatically calculate the most material-efficient cutting layout.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complete complex installations with remote technical expert guidance using AR headsets
- AI-assisted design tools quickly generate ventilation duct layout plans
- Digital twin simulates installation outcomes to reduce onsite rework
- Real-time feedback on fastening torque and angle accuracy via smart handheld tools
- Predictive maintenance systems based on historical data optimize repair schedules.

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability to handle non-standard, irregular structures during on-site installation
- Expertise in diagnosing and manually repairing complex system faults
- Ability to communicate with clients to clarify needs and provide customized solutions
- Physical skills for flexible operation in confined or hazardous environments

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sheet metal workers in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for sheet metal workers in Switzerland vary by canton and experience. Typically, entry-level workers earn around CHF 50,000 to CHF 65,000 per year, while experienced professionals can earn CHF 70,000 to CHF 85,000. Higher wages are common in urban cantons like Zurich or Geneva. Overtime and specialty skills can increase earnings further.

**Q: What visa routes are available for non-EU/EFTA sheet metal workers to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA nationals generally need a work permit sponsored by a Swiss employer. The EU Blue Card is not directly applicable as Switzerland is not in the EU but has bilateral agreements. The main route is a residence permit for skilled workers, requiring a confirmed job offer, proof of qualifications, and priority for Swiss/EEA workers. Quotas and labor market tests apply.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for sheet metal workers in Switzerland?**
A: Sheet metal workers in Switzerland can advance to foreman, workshop manager, or independent contractor. Many pursue further training as Swiss Federal VET Diploma (e.g., in metal construction) or become specialists in HVAC or roofing. Apprenticeships are common, and continuous education through professional associations or vocational schools enables specialization in automation or design.
