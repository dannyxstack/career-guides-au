## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and canton. Entry-level mechanics earn around CHF 55,000–65,000 per year, while experienced professionals can earn CHF 70,000–85,000. Those with specialized skills or lead positions may exceed CHF 90,000. Overtime and bonuses are common.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if you hold a tertiary degree or equivalent qualifications and a job offer with a salary meeting the threshold (around CHF 85,000 in 2024). Employers must prove no suitable Swiss or EU candidate exists. Alternatively, EU/EFTA citizens benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement and need only a residence permit.

**Q: What training and career opportunities exist for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Switzerland?**
A: Training typically involves a 3-4 year apprenticeship followed by a federal diploma. Mechanics can specialize in industrial refrigeration, heat pumps, or energy-efficient systems. Career progression includes foreman, technical expert, or self-employment. Continuing education courses are offered by Swiss Mechanics Associations.
