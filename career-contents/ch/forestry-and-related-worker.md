## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a forestry worker in Switzerland?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for forestry workers (ISCO 6210) in Switzerland ranges from CHF 4,500 to CHF 5,500, depending on experience, canton, and employer. Salaries are generally higher in urban areas and for specialized roles. Year-end bonuses or additional benefits may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA forestry workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU/EFTA citizens generally require a work permit with employer sponsorship and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a high salary threshold, which is rarely met for forestry roles. EU/EFTA citizens benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement of Persons.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a forestry worker in Switzerland?**
A: A completed apprenticeship as a forester (Forstwart) with a federal VET certificate is the standard entry requirement. Some positions may require a higher professional school diploma (Höhere Fachschule) or a bachelor's degree in forestry. Ongoing training in safety, machinery, and environmental management is also common.
