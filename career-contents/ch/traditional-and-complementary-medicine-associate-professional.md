## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region, but typical gross annual earnings range from CHF 60,000 to 80,000. Self-employed practitioners may earn more or less depending on client volume. Public sector or clinic roles often offer additional benefits.

**Q: What are the visa routes for foreign Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals to work in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens benefit from the Agreement on Free Movement of Persons. Non-EU/EFTA citizens need an employer-sponsored work permit; the employer must demonstrate no suitable Swiss or EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card may be an option if the salary exceeds the threshold (CHF 120,000 in 2024), though this is uncommon in this field.

**Q: What education and registration are required to practice as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Switzerland?**
A: A recognized diploma in traditional or complementary medicine (e.g., from a Swiss school like the Institute of Traditional Chinese Medicine) is essential. Registration with a professional body such as ASCA or EMR is typically required for insurance reimbursement. Some cantons impose additional licensing, especially for invasive treatments.
