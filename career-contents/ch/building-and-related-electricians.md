# Building And Related Electricians — career analysis · Switzerland

**Occupation code: 7411 (ISCO08)**

Building and related electricians install, maintain, and repair electrical wiring, fixtures, and equipment in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. They ensure electrical systems function safely and efficiently, often collaborating with construction teams and adhering to Swiss electrical standards (NIBT).

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 25,000. Switzerland's construction sector remains stable with steady demand for electricians due to ongoing building renovations, energy efficiency upgrades, and new infrastructure projects. The shift towards renewable energy and smart building technologies also drives demand. However, competition among EU/EFTA workers is moderate, and the occupation is not currently designated as a shortage profession despite consistent hiring needs.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR


| Visa | Description |
|---|---|

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals who enjoy hands-on work and problem-solving.
- People comfortable with heights and confined spaces.
- Those interested in sustainable building technologies.

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike working in varying weather conditions.
- Individuals seeking an entirely desk-based career.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical starting salary for an electrician in Switzerland?**
A: Entry-level electricians earn between CHF 48,000 and CHF 58,000 per year, depending on canton and employer.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EFTA electrician easily move to Switzerland?**
A: No, third-country nationals face tight quotas and must prove specialized skills not available locally.

**Q: Is my foreign electrician qualification recognized in Switzerland?**
A: Yes, but you must apply for recognition via SERI. Additional training or exams may be required.
