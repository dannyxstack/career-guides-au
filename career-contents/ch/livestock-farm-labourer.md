## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Switzerland?**
A: The gross monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Switzerland typically ranges from CHF 3,500 to CHF 4,500, depending on factors like canton, employer, and experience. Overtime and weekend work may be compensated extra. Collective labour agreements (GAV) apply in some cantons, setting minimum wage standards.

**Q: What are the work visa options for livestock farm labourers in Switzerland?**
A: EU/EFTA citizens can work freely under the Agreement on Free Movement. Non-EU/EFTA nationals generally cannot obtain a work permit for this occupation, as it is considered unskilled labour and not on the shortage list. The EU Blue Card requires a tertiary degree and a minimum salary threshold, which this role does not meet.

**Q: What are the typical tasks of a livestock farm labourer in Switzerland?**
A: Livestock farm labourers in Switzerland care for animals such as cattle, pigs, or sheep. Duties include feeding, cleaning stables, monitoring health, assisting with births, and maintaining fences and equipment. Work often starts early and involves physical labour in all weather. Many farms require basic German, French, or Italian depending on the region.
