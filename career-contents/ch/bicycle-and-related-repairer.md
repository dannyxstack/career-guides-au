## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bicycle repairer in Switzerland?**
A: The average monthly salary for bicycle repairers in Switzerland ranges from CHF 3,500 to CHF 5,000 gross, depending on experience and canton. Entry-level positions may start around CHF 3,000, while experienced mechanics can earn up to CHF 5,500. Salaries are typically higher in urban areas like Zurich, Geneva, and Basel.

**Q: Can a foreign bicycle repairer obtain a work visa for Switzerland?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EFTA nationals need a work permit and residence visa, typically via the EU Blue Card or a cantonal quota-based permit. A confirmed job offer and recognized qualification (e.g., vocational training) are required. Priority is given to Swiss/EU/EFTA citizens, so employers must demonstrate no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a bicycle repairer in Switzerland?**
A: While formal certification is not mandatory, employers often prefer candidates with a Federal VET Diploma (e.g., as a bicycle mechanic) or equivalent foreign training. Practical experience and knowledge of modern bike systems (e-bikes, hydraulics) are valued. Some cantons require a trade license for independent work.
