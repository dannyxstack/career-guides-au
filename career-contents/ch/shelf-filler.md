## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shelf fillers in Switzerland?**
A: Shelf fillers in Switzerland earn between CHF 3,500 and CHF 4,500 gross per month for full-time work, depending on location and employer. Salaries are higher in cities like Zurich or Geneva. Part-time or temporary roles may offer hourly rates of CHF 20–25. Collective bargaining agreements may apply in some retail chains.

**Q: Can non-EU/EFTA nationals work as shelf fillers in Switzerland via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled professionals and does not apply to shelf fillers. Non-EU/EFTA nationals generally need a work permit with employer sponsorship, but priority is given to Swiss and EU/EFTA workers. A labor market test is usually required, making it extremely difficult for low-skilled roles. EU/EFTA citizens have free movement.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shelf fillers in Switzerland?**
A: Shelf fillers can advance to roles like department manager, warehouse supervisor, or inventory planner. Retail chains such as Migros, Coop, or Aldi Suisse offer internal training programs. Further education (e.g., Swiss Federal Certificate in Retail) can lead to higher positions. Proficiency in German, French, or Italian is beneficial for progression.
