## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for ICT sales professionals in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries for ICT sales professionals in Switzerland vary by experience and performance. Entry-level roles start around CHF 80,000 per year, while senior specialists earn CHF 120,000 to CHF 160,000. Top performers with commission can exceed CHF 200,000. Figures are based on industry reports and job platforms.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as ICT sales professionals in Switzerland via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for the Swiss EU Blue Card. Requirements include a university degree or equivalent qualifications, a job offer in ICT sales, and a minimum salary threshold (currently about CHF 85,000 for most sectors). The Blue Card leads to a residence permit and potential permanent residency after 10 years.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ICT sales professionals in Switzerland?**
A: ICT sales professionals in Switzerland can advance from junior sales representative to key account manager, then to regional sales director or VP of Sales. Many transition into presales, solution architecture, or product management. Switzerland's strong tech hubs in Zurich, Geneva, and Basel offer diverse opportunities in software, cloud, and cybersecurity sales.
