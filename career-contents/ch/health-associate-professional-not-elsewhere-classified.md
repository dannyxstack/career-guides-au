## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for health associate professionals not elsewhere classified in Switzerland?**
A: Salaries vary by canton, experience, and employer. As of 2024, the median gross monthly salary for this occupational group is approximately CHF 5,500 to CHF 7,000. Entry-level positions may start around CHF 4,500, while experienced professionals can earn up to CHF 8,500 or more, especially in private clinics or hospitals.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU health associate professionals to work in Switzerland?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit through employer sponsorship, with priority given to Swiss/EU candidates. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles meeting a salary threshold (CHF 86,600 in 2024; reduced to CHF 69,280 for shortage occupations). Health associate roles may qualify as shortage occupations, easing the threshold, but a labor market test still applies.

**Q: What are common work settings for health associate professionals not elsewhere classified in Switzerland?**
A: These professionals work in hospitals, medical practices, nursing homes, rehabilitation centers, and public health institutions. Roles include medical assistants, dental hygienists, physiotherapy assistants, and laboratory technicians. Many are employed by private clinics or regional health networks, with opportunities in both urban and rural areas.
