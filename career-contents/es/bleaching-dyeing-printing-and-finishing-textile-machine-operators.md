# Bleaching, Dyeing, Printing and Finishing Textile Machine Operators（Bleaching, Dyeing, Printing and Finishing Textile Machine Operators）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：8154 – Bleaching, Dyeing, Printing and Finishing Textile Machine Operators。**

Operate machines to bleach, dye, print, and finish textiles, including setting machine parameters, monitoring production, inspecting product quality, and performing basic maintenance. Typically work in industrial textile mills, dyeing plants, often in shifts.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work contract visa (employed)） | 需获得西班牙雇主工作合同，且企业证明该职位无法在欧盟市场招到合适人选。对低技能岗位通常较难获得。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for textile machine operators in Spain?**
答：Entry-level operators earn around 16,500-19,500 EUR gross per year, mid-level up to 23,500 EUR, and senior supervisors about 28,000 EUR. Salaries are below the Spanish average with limited growth.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation is not high-skilled nor in shortage. Employers must prove they cannot find candidates in the EU, and low salaries make it hard to meet migration requirements. Consider completing vocational training in Spain then applying for a work visa.

**问：Are there promotion opportunities for this occupation?**
答：Limited. Operators can advance to shift supervisor or quality controller. With training, you could move into textile technician roles. But the industry is shrinking, so learning additional skills like machine maintenance is recommended to boost flexibility.
