# Software and Multimedia Analysts and Designers（Software and Multimedia Analysts and Designers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2719 – Software and Multimedia Analysts and Designers。**

|---|---|
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需要大学学历及工作合同，薪资门槛约€33,908（2023年） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for software analysts in Spain?**
答：Junior: €24k-34k, Mid: €35k-50k, Senior: €50k-75k; architects/managers can exceed €80k.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Apply for EU Blue Card (contract + degree) or Highly Skilled Professional visa (Ley 14/2013); shortage occupation helps.

**问：Is academic recognition (homologación) required?**
答：Not mandatory, but recommended for degree recognition to improve job prospects.
