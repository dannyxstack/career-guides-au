# Skilled Horticultural Workers in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens（Skilled Horticultural Workers in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：6120 – Skilled Horticultural Workers in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens。**

Responsible for planting, maintaining and harvesting fruit trees, vegetables, flowers and ornamental plants in orchards, greenhouses, nurseries, and gardens. Operates greenhouse equipment, manages irrigation and fertilization, and performs pest and disease control. Propagates plants in nurseries and prepares them for sale.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for horticultural workers?**
答：Entry-level workers earn around €16,000-20,000 per year, mid-level €20,000-26,000, and senior supervisors up to €34,000. Salaries vary by season, region, and skill level.

**问：How can I immigrate to Spain through horticultural work?**
答：You can apply for an employed work visa (Cuenta ajena) or a highly skilled professional visa (Ley 14/2013). Horticulture is a shortage occupation, but you need relevant training or experience. Alternatively, try a job search visa (Búsqueda de empleo) first.

**问：What education is required to work in horticulture in Spain?**
答：Typically, a vocational training certificate (FP Grado Medio or Superior) in horticulture is required. A university degree is not mandatory but helps for managerial positions.
