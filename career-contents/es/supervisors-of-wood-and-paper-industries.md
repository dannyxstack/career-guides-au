# Supervisors of Wood and Paper Industries（Supervisors of Wood and Paper Industries）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3206 – Supervisors of Wood and Paper Industries。**

Oversees daily operations in wood processing, pulp, and paper production lines, ensuring productivity, quality control, and worker safety.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 由西班牙雇主担保的受雇工作居留，需证明职位难以在当地填补。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许高学历或职业培训毕业生在西班牙居留12个月寻找工作。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a supervisor in the wood and paper industries?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €22,000-28,000, mid-level €28,000-35,000, and senior can reach €35,000-45,000, depending on region and company size.

**问：Can a foreigner immigrate as a supervisor in the wood industry in Spain?**
答：It is difficult as the occupation is not in shortage. Employers must prove inability to fill the position within the EU. Those with Spanish vocational training or degrees have better chances.

**问：What education is required for this occupation?**
答：Typically a higher vocational training (FP Grado Superior) in a related field or a bachelor's degree such as Forestry Engineering. Some companies promote experienced technicians.
