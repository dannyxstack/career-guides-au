# Waste Management and Other Services Directors and Managers（Waste Management and Other Services Directors and Managers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：1509 – Waste Management and Other Services Directors and Managers。**

Responsible for strategic planning, operations management, and business development of waste management, sanitation, and environmental services companies.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能专业人士，需大学学历和合同，薪资门槛约€34,000 |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Qualified Professional） | 依据Ley 14/2013，需学历和合同，年薪不低于西班牙平均工资 |
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 传统工作居留，需雇主担保，受限于劳动力市场测试 |
| Subclass Búsqueda de empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙居留找工作，有效期6-12个月 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for waste management managers?**
答：Entry-level: €28,000-€35,000; mid-level: €38,000-€50,000; senior: €55,000-€75,000 including bonuses.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a Spanish waste management work visa?**
答：Through the EU Blue Card (requires degree and contract >€34,000) or Highly Qualified Professional route (Ley 14/2013).

**问：What education is required for this occupation?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in environmental management or business administration; master's preferred; or FP Grado Superior.
