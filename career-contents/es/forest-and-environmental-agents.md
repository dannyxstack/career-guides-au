# Forest and Environmental Agents（Forest and Environmental Agents）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5993 – Forest and Environmental Agents。**

Professionals responsible for forest fire prevention, environmental law enforcement, wildlife protection, and natural resource supervision, key to Spain's conservation efforts.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在ES独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work permit for employed workers） | 受雇工作居留：需获得自治区林业部门工作合同，通过劳动力市场测试（若职业非紧缺） |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional (Ley 14/2013)） | 高技能专业人才签证：适用于有高等教育背景且合同薪资达到门槛者，但公共部门招聘通常优先欧盟公民 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for forest and environmental agents?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €22,000-€28,000, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior up to €45,000. Salary increases with seniority and rank, with good public sector benefits.

**问：Can a third-country national immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：It is difficult as the job is in the public sector, typically requiring Spanish or EU citizenship and passing competitive exams. Non-EU citizens must prove special skills or shortage, but this occupation is rarely on shortage lists.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically a university degree (Grado) in environmental science, forest engineering, or equivalent higher vocational training (FP Grado Superior). Foreign qualifications require homologación by the Spanish Ministry of Education.
