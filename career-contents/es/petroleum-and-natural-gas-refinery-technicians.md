# Petroleum and Natural Gas Refinery Technicians（Petroleum and Natural Gas Refinery Technicians）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3134 – Petroleum and Natural Gas Refinery Technicians。**

Monitor and operate refinery and gas processing plant equipment to ensure safe and efficient crude oil processing, distillation, conversion, and treatment.
