# Food, Beverage and Tobacco Processing Machine Operator（Food, Beverage and Tobacco Processing Machine Operator）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：8160 – Food, Beverage and Tobacco Processing Machine Operator。**

Operate and monitor machinery to produce food, beverage and tobacco products, including mixing, molding and packaging, ensuring safety and quality standards.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 需要雇主担保，适用于非欧盟公民，需证明职位无法由本地人填补。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job-Seeker Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，需有足够资金。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for food processing machine operators?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €18,000-22,000, mid-level €22,000-28,000, and senior up to €35,000, depending on experience and region.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：It's difficult as it's not a shortage occupation; employers must prove no local candidates, and salary thresholds for EU Blue Card are low but competitive.

**问：What education is needed for this occupation?**
答：Typically a vocational training (FP Grado Medio) or relevant work experience; some positions accept high school diploma plus on-the-job training.
