# Lifeguard — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 5992 (CNO)**

Responsible for safety monitoring of beaches, swimming pools, and other water bodies, preventing drowning incidents, and conducting water rescue and first aid.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Spain has a long coastline and a developed tourism industry, with a stable demand for lifeguards. The summer tourism peak season gap is obvious, but the seasonality is strong, with jobs concentrated in the southern and island regions throughout the year.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Seasonal lifeguards can be transferred to long-term contracts, promoted to lifeguard captain or water safety coordinator. Some people obtain qualifications as first aid coaches or swimming coaches, expanding their career horizons.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- The AI vision system automatically monitors the pool, identifying abnormal behaviors (such as signs of drowning) and triggering alerts
- Use drones or robots for beach patrols instead of manual patrols
- Automatically generates safety reports and accident records, replacing manual filling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time risk analysis identifies high-risk areas or populations
- AR glasses provide enhanced visibility to assist with underwater search and rescue
- The intelligent early warning system predicts weather or water flow changes in advance to assist decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Judgment and courage in rescue in complex waters
- Comfort and communication for special groups such as children and the elderly
- Humane procedures for emergency medical procedures (such as cardiopulmonary resuscitation and spinal immobilization).
- Teamwork and on-site command skills

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced First Aid and Trauma Management Certification
- AI monitoring and early warning system operation
- Drone operation and maintenance
- Risk management and emergency planning
- Communication and psychological counseling skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Tourism
- Seasonal demand
- Water safety awareness
- Coastal population

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for lifeguards in Spain?**
A: Entry-level around EUR 16,000-22,000, senior can exceed EUR 30,000, but seasonal contracts affect annual income.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through the lifeguard occupation?**
A: It is quite challenging because the profession is not in urgent demand and requires Spanish certification. You can try employer-sponsored work residency, but the position is prioritized for EU citizens.

**Q: What certifications are required to become a lifeguard?**
A: You must complete a lifesaving course recognized by the Red Cross or the autonomous region and obtain a first aid certificate. Some sea areas require a water rescue technical certificate.
