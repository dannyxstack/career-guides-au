# Mathematicians and Actuaries（Mathematicians and Actuaries）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2415 – Mathematicians and Actuaries。**

Mathematicians and actuaries use mathematical and statistical models to analyze risks and solve financial and business problems, playing a key role in insurance, banking, consulting, and the public sector.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of actuaries in Spain?**
答：Entry-level actuaries earn €30,000-€40,000, mid-level €45,000-€60,000, and senior can exceed €90,000 annually, depending on experience and certifications.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to work as an actuary in Spain?**
答：Apply for an EU Blue Card or Highly Qualified Professional visa with a job offer and a university degree or relevant experience.

**问：What education is needed to become an actuary in Spain?**
答：A bachelor's degree in mathematics, statistics, or actuarial science, preferably a master's, and certification from the Spanish Institute of Actuaries is recommended.
