# Blacksmiths And Forging Press Workers — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7321 (CNO)**

Blacksmiths and forgings process metal into tools, horseshoes, building components, and decorative items through hammering, pressing, or forging. The work includes traditional handicrafts such as heating metals, operating forging machinery, welding, and heat treatment, as well as modern techniques.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 3,500. Spain's blacksmithing industry is supported by the recovery of traditional crafts and demand for building repairs, but overall employment growth has been slow. There are fewer young workers, and aging is severe. Industrial forging relies more on automation and has limited job openings. Over the next five years, this profession may be slightly reduced, mainly focusing on custom forging and cultural heritage restoration.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Beginners usually start as apprentices and can grow into skilled smiths or adjusters. Experienced individuals can be promoted to workshop supervisor or start their own forging workshop. Some personnel can transition through training to technical positions such as precision forging or mold manufacturing, but management positions are scarce.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeated forging and stamping operations
- Metal heating and basic heat treatment monitoring
- Standard parts are mass-produced in batches
- Simple molding using traditional molds
- Foundation welding and gas cutting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted metal forging process simulation and design
- Computer vision is used for forging quality inspection
- Remote monitoring and optimization of forging parameters
- Digital twin technology optimizes heat treatment processes
- AI-generated custom styling design proposals

**Human moat:**

- Hand-forged and customized artistic engraving
- Repair and modification of complex metal components
- Communicate directly with customers to understand specific needs
- Handling non-standard materials and special processes
- Safe operation and supervision of high-risk processes

**Skills to build:**

- Programming and operation of CNC forging equipment
- 3D modeling and CAD/CAM software application
- Optimization of metal material properties and heat treatment processes
- Industrial robot collaboration skills
- Basic data analysis and quality control
- Traditional forging and restoration techniques have been deepened

**Key growth areas:**

- artisan revival
- heritage restoration
- metalworking craft
- automation adaptation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for Spanish blacksmiths?**
A: Junior annual income approx. €18,000-24,000, intermediate €24,000-32,000, senior blacksmiths or owners up to €30,000-40,000. But overall below industry average; only a few with excellent skills earn well.

**Q: Can blacksmiths go to Spain through skilled immigration?**
A: Very difficult. This occupation is not on the shortage list, and the salary is low, making it hard to meet the EU Blue Card requirement (annual salary needs about EUR 34,000). Only in very rare cases, such as as a cultural heritage artisan, might a special work permit be obtained, but the overall skilled migration path is unfriendly.

**Q: What are the promotion paths for Spanish blacksmiths?**
A: Usually, they start as apprentices (3-5 years) and then become skilled blacksmiths. Outstanding candidates can be promoted to workshop supervisor or open their own forging shop. It can also shift into specialized fields such as precision forging and mold manufacturing.
