# Blacksmiths And Forging Press Workers（Blacksmiths And Forging Press Workers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7321 – Blacksmiths And Forging Press Workers。**

Blacksmiths and forging press workers shape metal into tools, horseshoes, structural parts, and decorative items by hammering, pressing, or forging. Tasks include heating metal, operating forging presses, welding, and heat treating, blending traditional craft with modern techniques.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (Employed)） | 由雇主担保的工作居留，需证明该职位无法由欧盟公民填补。但铁匠职业不属于短缺清单，且薪资较低，通过此路径较难。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a blacksmith in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual gross salary ranges from €18,000 to €24,000, experienced from €24,000 to €32,000, and senior/self-employed up to €40,000. Overall below average, except for master smiths.

**问：Can a blacksmith immigrate to Spain as a skilled worker?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list and salaries are low, failing to meet the EU Blue Card threshold (around €34,000). Only exceptional heritage artisan cases may get a permit, but in general, the skilled migration path is unfavorable.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for blacksmiths in Spain?**
答：Start as an apprentice (3-5 years), then become a skilled smith. Outstanding workers can advance to workshop supervisor or open their own forge. Some may specialize in precision forging or mold making.
