# Product Classifiers and Testers (Except Food, Beverages, and Tobacco) — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7893 (CNO)**

Responsible for classifying, inspecting, and testing manufactured products (such as electronics, machinery, textiles, etc.) to ensure compliance with quality standards, commonly found in factories and quality inspection departments.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

You can be promoted from junior inspector to senior quality inspection technician, quality supervisor, or quality assurance engineer, with experience accumulated and professional certification obtained (such as ISO 9001 internal auditor).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeat visual inspection (such as identifying product surface defects)
- Standardized size and weight measurements
- Product classification and labeling based on fixed rules
- Generate routine quality inspection reports
- Real-time monitoring of test data and triggering alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted analysis of complex test data (such as vibration and spectroscopy) pinpoints the root causes of defects
- Automatically recommends test plans and sampling strategies
- Enhance cross-departmental quality traceability and predictive maintenance
- By combining AR glasses with test results and operation guidance
- Automatically learns new defect patterns and updates standards

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, multi-sensory judgment abnormal products
- Collaborate with production and engineering teams to solve quality issues
- Understand customer needs and adjust acceptance standards
- Develop testing plans for new materials and processes
- Bear legal responsibilities related to product quality

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical process control (SPC)
- Application of AI/Machine Learning in Quality Inspection (e.g., Image Recognition)
- Automated testing system operation and maintenance
- Cross-departmental communication and problem-solving
- Updates to quality standards and regulations (such as ISO 9001).
- Basic programming (such as Python) and sensor technology

**Key growth areas:**

- Quality control
- Manufacturing inspection
- Automation adaptation
- Industrial testing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for product classifiers in Spain?**
A: Junior annual salary is around EUR 16,000-20,000, senior up to EUR 32,000, below the national average, but acceptable in areas with lower cost of living.

**Q: Is this profession easy to immigrate to in Spain?**
A: It's not easy. This occupation is not in urgent demand and offers relatively low wages, does not meet the requirements for the EU Blue Card or Skilled Talent visa, and can only be granted residence through ordinary employment work, but the approval process is strict.

**Q: What are the future development directions?**
A: Can advance to Quality Supervisor or Quality Assurance Engineer, requiring additional quality management knowledge, or switch to production management, logistics quality inspection, etc.
