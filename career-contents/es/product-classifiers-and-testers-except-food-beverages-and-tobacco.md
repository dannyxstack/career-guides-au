# Product Classifiers and Testers (Except Food, Beverages, and Tobacco)（Product Classifiers and Testers (Except Food, Beverages, and Tobacco)）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7893 – Product Classifiers and Testers (Except Food, Beverages, and Tobacco)。**

Responsible for classifying, inspecting, and testing manufactured products (e.g., electronics, machinery, textiles) to ensure quality standards, typically employed in factories and quality control departments.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for product classifiers in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual gross salary ranges from €16,000 to €20,000, while senior positions can reach €32,000, below national average but viable in low-cost areas.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Not easy. It is not a shortage occupation and salaries are low, making it ineligible for EU Blue Card or high-skilled visa; only standard work permits apply, with strict approval.

**问：What are future career paths?**
答：Promotion to quality supervisor or QA engineer requires additional quality management knowledge; alternatively transition into production management or logistics inspection.
