# Slaughterers And Meat Packers（Slaughterers And Meat Packers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7701 – Slaughterers And Meat Packers。**

Work in slaughterhouses and meat processing plants, performing slaughtering, cutting, deboning and packing. A physically demanding job in cold and humid conditions.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a slaughterer in Spain?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €18,000 to €32,000. Entry-level around €18,000-€22,000, experienced up to €32,000. Overtime pay is common, but it is a low-wage occupation.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain as a slaughterer?**
答：Very unlikely. This occupation is not high-skilled or in shortage, and salaries usually fall below the EU Blue Card threshold (€33,908 in 2023). Employer-sponsored work permits are rarely approved for low-skilled roles.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities for a slaughterer?**
答：Can advance from assistant to skilled butcher, team leader, then quality control or maintenance. Some open their own butcher shops. However, promotion prospects are limited, and automation may reduce roles.
