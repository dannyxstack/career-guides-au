# Retail Store Section Manager（Retail Store Section Manager）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5210 – Retail Store Section Manager。**

Oversees daily operations of a specific department in a retail store, including supervising staff, managing inventory, ensuring customer service and meeting sales targets.

| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees (Cuenta Ajena)） | 由雇主担保的工作居留，需证明岗位无法在本地或欧盟找到合适人选。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a retail section manager in Spain?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from €22,000 to €28,000, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior up to €45,000 gross per year. Large companies and chain supermarkets pay more.

**问：How can non-EU nationals obtain a work permit as a retail section manager in Spain?**
答：They can apply through employer sponsorship (Cuenta ajena), as a highly qualified professional (Ley 14/2013), or via the EU Blue Card. Requirements include relevant education and salary thresholds.

**问：What is the career progression for a retail section manager?**
答：Can advance to assistant store manager, store manager, or regional manager; also can move into procurement, merchandising, or operations management.
