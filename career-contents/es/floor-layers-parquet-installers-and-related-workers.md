# Floor Layers, Parquet Installers and Related Workers（Floor Layers, Parquet Installers and Related Workers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7240 – Floor Layers, Parquet Installers and Related Workers。**

Lay and install various floor coverings including parquet, tiles, and carpets in residential and commercial buildings. Requires skills in material handling, cutting, and surface finishing.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
