# Floor Layers, Parquet Installers and Related Workers — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7240 (CNO)**

Responsible for laying various flooring materials, including parquet flooring, tiles, carpets, etc., which is a key occupation in the building decoration industry. It is necessary to master material properties, cutting techniques, and surface treatment skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

You can start as an apprentice, gain experience, and be promoted to foreman or project manager. You can also start your own renovation company or switch to positions in building materials sales, quality inspection, and similar positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual measurement using tape measure and level.
- Manual cutting of floor materials (e.g., cutting carpet, sawing flooring)
- calculate material quantities and waste
- Generate simple installation plans and quotations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR or 3D scanning tools to quickly measure room dimensions
- Generate floor laying patterns using AI design software
- Use automatic cutting machines to improve material utilization
- Use digital tools to present finished effects to clients
- Manage project progress and material inventory via mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Installation in irregularly shaped rooms and complex corners
- Fine manual skills for repairing old or damaged flooring.
- Experience in assessing ground conditions and preparing base surfaces
- Client communication and on-site problem-solving skills
- Practical knowledge of material properties and installation techniques.

**Skills to build:**

- Using laser measurement and 3D scanning equipment
- Operate CNC cutting machines and automatic cutting tools
- Learn basic CAD or interior design software
- Knowledge of floor material environmental and certification standards
- Improving customer service and quotation skills
- Understand basics of Building Information Modeling (BIM)

**Key growth areas:**

- Construction recovery
- Renovation boom
- Flooring technology
- Sustainable materials

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EU member state, Spain admits skilled foreign professionals mainly through the EU Blue Card and national work-permit routes. Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. Rules and salary thresholds change yearly, so always check the official source before making plans.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on work and attention to detail
- People willing to start as an apprentice and gradually accumulate experience
- For those who wish to develop long-term careers in the construction industry

**Who is not suited**
- Those who dislike physical labor and dusty environments
- People who expect high salaries and rapid promotions

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for flooring installers in Spain?**
A: Entry level about €18,000-22,000/year, mid-level €22,000-28,000/year, senior up to €35,000/year. Salaries vary by region and project type.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Spain through this employment?**
A: The likelihood is low; this occupation is not in urgent demand, employers must prove they cannot recruit in the EU, and academic credentials are required, which is quite challenging.
