# Pattern Maker for Textile and Leather Products（Pattern Maker for Textile and Leather Products）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7832 – Pattern Maker for Textile and Leather Products。**

Pattern makers for textile and leather products create templates based on design drawings for mass production of clothing, bags, footwear, etc. They use CAD software or manual cutting techniques to ensure accurate shapes and sizing, and participate in grading and lay planning.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of pattern makers in Spain?**
答：Junior pattern makers earn around €16,000-20,000 annually, mid-level €21,000-26,000, senior up to €27,000-34,000, varying by region and skills.

**问：Can pattern makers immigrate to Spain through skilled migration?**
答：This occupation is not on Spain's shortage list and is not highly specialized. Third-country nationals typically need employer sponsorship for a work permit, which is difficult.

**问：What education is needed to become a pattern maker in Spain?**
答：Typically, a vocational training FP Grado Superior (2 years) or a university degree (Grado, 4 years) in fashion design or textile engineering.
