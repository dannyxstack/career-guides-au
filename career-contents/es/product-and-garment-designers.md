# Product and Garment Designers（Product and Garment Designers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2482 – Product and Garment Designers。**

Product and garment designers conceive and develop styles and functions of new products, clothing, and accessories, blending aesthetics, ergonomics, and market needs from sketch to final product, in a role combining creativity and technology.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Product and Garment Designers？**
- 具备创意和审美能力，热爱时尚或产品设计
- 熟练使用设计软件（Adobe Illustrator、CLO 3D等）
- 能适应项目驱动的工作节奏，有团队协作精神

**谁不适合学Product and Garment Designers？**
- 对商业和市场敏感度低，仅追求艺术表达
- 不喜欢反复修改和客户沟通，缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for product and garment designers in Spain?**
答：Entry-level designers earn around €20,000-28,000 annually, mid-level €28,000-40,000, and senior designers can reach €60,000. Salary varies by city, company size, and experience.

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to Spain for this profession?**
答：Common routes: 1) Work permit (Cuenta ajena) sponsored by a Spanish employer; 2) Highly skilled professional visa (salary >€34,000); 3) EU Blue Card (requires degree + job offer). Degrees need homologación.

**问：What education is required for this profession?**
答：Typically a higher education in design, such as FP Grado Superior (2 years) or university degree (4 years). Private design schools are also common. Portfolio often matters more than degree.
