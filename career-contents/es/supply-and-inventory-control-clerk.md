# Supply and Inventory Control Clerk（Supply and Inventory Control Clerk）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：4121 – Supply and Inventory Control Clerk。**

Responsible for monitoring inventory levels, managing material receipts and dispatches, updating inventory records, and coordinating supply chain processes to ensure efficient material flow.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for inventory control clerks in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €18,000 to €22,000, mid-level €22,000 to €28,000, and senior up to €35,000, with higher for supervisors.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：The most common path is to obtain a work contract from a Spanish company and apply for a work permit (Cuenta ajena). Alternatively, apply for a job search visa (Busqueda empleo) and convert after finding a job. Highly skilled professionals may qualify for the EU Blue Card.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Generally high school diploma, but completing vocational training (FP Grado Medio or FP Grado Superior in logistics) significantly improves job prospects.
