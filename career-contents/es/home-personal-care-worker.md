# Home Personal Care Worker（Home Personal Care Worker）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5710 – Home Personal Care Worker。**

Provides daily living assistance, personal hygiene care, and emotional support to elderly, disabled, and chronically ill individuals in their homes.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employed Persons） | 由西班牙雇主担保的受雇工作居留，需证明岗位无法由本地人填补。 |
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 高技能工作签证，但家庭护理员通常薪资不达标，极少适用。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，需有足够资金证明。 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Home Personal Care Worker？**
- 有耐心、同理心，喜欢照顾他人的人
- 愿意从事体力劳动，能适应弹性工作时间
- 寻求稳定就业且对语言要求不高的移民

**谁不适合学Home Personal Care Worker？**
- 无法承受情感压力或体力消耗的人
- 希望快速获得高薪或高社会地位的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for home care workers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €16,000-€20,000, while experienced workers can earn €26,000-€32,000, varying by region and employer.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Spain as home care workers?**
答：The most common route is obtaining a work permit for employed persons (Cuenta ajena) sponsored by a Spanish employer, requiring proof of labor market test.

**问：Is there career progression for home care workers?**
答：Yes, you can advance to team supervisor or further study to become a registered nurse or social worker.
