# Statistician（Statistician）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2416 – Statistician。**

Statisticians collect, analyze, and interpret data to support decision-making in government, research, and business.

**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能专业人士，需大学学历和至少两年工作经验，年薪需达到西班牙平均工资1.5倍（约45,000欧元） |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional (Ley 14/2013)） | 针对高技能人才，需公司支持，学历或经验要求严格，薪资门槛低于欧盟蓝卡 |
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract (Cuenta Ajena)） | 需西班牙雇主提供工作合同并证明该职位无法由本国人填补 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for statisticians in Spain?**
答：Entry-level statisticians earn about 25,000-35,000 EUR, mid-level 35,000-50,000, and senior 50,000-70,000 EUR per year (gross).

**问：How can a foreigner become a statistician in Spain?**
答：You can migrate via EU Blue Card or the Highly Skilled Professional visa (Ley 14/2013), requiring degree homologation and employer sponsorship.

**问：What skills are needed for a statistician?**
答：Proficiency in statistical software (R, Python, SAS), database languages (SQL), plus machine learning and data visualization skills.
