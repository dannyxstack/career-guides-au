# Community Animator（Community Animator）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3715 – Community Animator。**

Plans, organizes, and promotes community activities to encourage participation and social cohesion, typically working for local governments, NGOs, or community centers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 需雇主担保，证明职位无法由本地人填补，难度较高。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6-12个月寻找工作，但需证明经济能力。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Community Animator？**
- 热爱社区服务和人际互动
- 具备组织活动和沟通协调能力
- 对社会议题有热情，愿意从事基层工作

**谁不适合学Community Animator？**
- 追求高薪和稳定职业发展
- 偏好独立工作而非团队协作

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for community animators?**
答：Entry-level around €16,000-20,000/year, mid-level €20,000-25,000, senior up to €32,000, but generally low and often temporary.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：It is difficult, as it is not a shortage occupation and requires high language and cultural skills. Usually requires employer-sponsored work permit or job search visa.

**问：What qualifications are needed for this occupation?**
答：Typically a degree in social education or related field, or an FP Grado Superior diploma. Not mandatory but helpful for job competition.
