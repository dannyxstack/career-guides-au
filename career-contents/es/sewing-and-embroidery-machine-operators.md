# Sewing and Embroidery Machine Operators — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 8153 (CNO)**

Responsible for operating industrial sewing machines and embroidery machines, sewing fabrics into clothing, textiles, or decorations. Commonly found in garment factories, home textile workshops, and custom embroidery shops, it requires hand-eye coordination and basic machine maintenance skills.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Spain's textile and apparel industry is limited in scale and heavily impacted by imports, with overall job demand remaining stable or slightly declining. With the trend of automation, simple sewing jobs are decreasing, but there is still stable demand for high-end custom embroidery and repair skills. Employment is concentrated in small businesses and family workshops.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Junior operators can be promoted to senior sewers, pattern makers, or production line leaders. Learning CAD typesetting and fashion design can lead to technical and design positions. Some experienced individuals independently open tailor shops or embroidery studios.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive straight-line suturing operations
- Automatic sewing of simple patterns
- Fabric cutting and alignment
- Defect identification in quality inspection
- Basic sewing machine maintenance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI sewing machines to program complex patterns
- Optimising sewing processes using computer-aided design (CAD)
- Monitors machine status through IoT for predictive maintenance
- Use automated systems for material management

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex, delicate special fabrics
- Custom, high-precision hand stitching
- Machine fault diagnosis and advanced repair
- Understand customer needs and provide creative solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Digital sewing machine programming and operation
- CAD/CAM software applications
- Automated system troubleshooting
- IoT fundamentals and data analysis
- Materials science knowledge
- Project management and coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining demand
- Automation impact
- Niche customization
- Stable replacement need

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for sewing machine operators?**
A: Entry-level employees earn about 16,000-20,000 euros annually, intermediate technicians 20,000-25,000 euros, and senior technicians up to 25,000-30,000 euros. Usually, it is 14 months' salary, including bonuses and overtime pay.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this profession?**
A: Difficult. This occupation is not in short supply, and employer sponsorship must prove that no EU citizen is competent. You can try a work visa (Cuenta ajena), but the success rate is low.
