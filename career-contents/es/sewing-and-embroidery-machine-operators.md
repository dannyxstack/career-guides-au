# Sewing and Embroidery Machine Operators（Sewing and Embroidery Machine Operators）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：8153 – Sewing and Embroidery Machine Operators。**

Operate industrial sewing and embroidery machines to sew fabric into garments, textiles, or decorative items. Common in apparel factories, home textile workshops, and custom embroidery shops, requiring hand-eye coordination and basic machine maintenance skills.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (Employed)） | 需雇主担保，岗位需证明无法在欧盟招到合适人员。难度大，因非紧缺职业。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，但需证明经济能力。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of sewing machine operators?**
答：Entry-level annual income is around €16,000-20,000, mid-level €20,000-25,000, and senior technicians up to €25,000-30,000. Usually 14 payments, including bonuses and overtime.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Difficult. Not a shortage occupation; employer sponsorship requires proof that no EU citizen can fill the role. Work visa (Cuenta ajena) possible but low success rate.
