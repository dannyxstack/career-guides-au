# Animal-Drawn Vehicle Driver for Passenger Transport（Animal-Drawn Vehicle Driver for Passenger Transport）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9812 – Animal-Drawn Vehicle Driver for Passenger Transport。**

Drives animal-drawn vehicles (horse-drawn carriages, donkey carts) to transport passengers, typically in tourist towns or rural areas. Duties include animal care, vehicle maintenance, and traffic rule compliance.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of an animal-drawn vehicle driver?**
答：Income is low; entry-level annual salary is about €12,000-€15,000, often seasonal part-time. Tips during peak season may supplement income.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Almost impossible. It is not on the high-skill shortage list, and the low income and seasonal nature make work visa approval very unlikely.

**问：What certifications are needed?**
答：Usually no formal certificate is required, but employers may ask for basic animal care training or a local government license for animal-drawn vehicles.
