# Animal-Drawn Vehicle Driver for Passenger Transport — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 9812 (CNO)**

Driving animal-drawn carts (horse-drawn carts, donkey carts, etc.) to transport passengers is commonly seen in short-distance travel in tourist towns or rural areas. The work includes caring for the tending animals, maintaining vehicles, and obeying traffic rules.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Usually, they start by caring for animals and assisting with driving, and after gaining experience, can independently drive or manage small fleets. A small number of people turned to tour guides or rickshaw rental businesses.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Road Navigation and Route Planning: The AI navigation system can automatically plan optimal routes
- Speed and Distance Control: The adaptive cruise system can replace manual driving
- Passenger billing and collection: Mobile payments and automatic ticket machines replace manual labor
- Vehicle Maintenance Reminders: IoT sensors automatically monitor vehicle condition and push maintenance notifications
- Basic customer service: chatbots and self-service kiosks handle common inquiries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- High-value light commentary: AI-assisted multilingual historical and cultural explanations
- Animal health monitoring: AI analyzes livestock gait and temperature data to warn of diseases
- Intelligent scheduling and order taking: AI platforms match passenger needs to improve load factors
- Safety warning system: collision warning and night vision assist enhance driving safety
- Value-added service recommendations: AI pushes attractions and dining discounts based on passenger profiles

**Human moat:**

- Understanding animal behavior and providing immediate comfort are difficult for machines to replicate
- Temporary Response to Complex Streets and Alleys (such as detouring markets or yielding to pedestrians)
- Human-centered interaction and personalized service create a travel experience
- Traditional skills passed down (such as horse training and vehicle decoration)

**Skills to build:**

- Electric/hybrid vehicle maintenance and driving
- Basic Veterinary Medicine and Nutritional Management
- Tour guide explanations and customer service skills
- Use of digital dispatch platforms
- Simple financial bookkeeping and cost accounting
- Social media promotion (such as short video marketing)

**Key growth areas:**

- Tourism
- Rural transport
- Heritage experience
- Animal care

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How do animal-drawn truck drivers earn?**
A: Low income, entry-level annual salary about €12,000-€15,000, mostly seasonal part-time. Peak season tips may increase earnings.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this profession?**
A: Almost impossible. This occupation is not on the high-skilled shortage list, has low income and is non-permanent, so immigration departments usually do not approve work visas.

**Q: What certificates are required to operate?**
A: Usually no formal certificate required, but employers may require basic animal care training or a local government-issued carriage driving license.
