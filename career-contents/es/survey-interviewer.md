# Survey Interviewer — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 4430 (CNO)**

Survey interviewers are responsible for collecting market, social, or opinion survey data via phone, online, or face-to-face methods to ensure sample representativeness and data accuracy. Flexible work schedule, multi-project work, often employed by market research firms, government agencies, or consulting firms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Junior interviewers can be promoted to team leader, project coordinator, or data analysis assistant. After gaining experience, you can move on to roles such as market research analyst or customer insight specialist. Some enter research management positions through further studies in statistics or sociology.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardized questionnaire interviews are conducted via phone or online channels
- Preliminary data entry and basic quality control inspection
- Sample screening and scheduling are performed using automated scripts
- Generate simple statistical reports and charts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI tools to analyze respondents' emotions and feedback in real time, and adjust your questioning strategy
- Use NLP techniques to quickly handle open-ended questions and extract key topics
- Optimize access times and routes with intelligent scheduling systems to improve efficiency
- Initial contact and screening through chatbots can improve response rates

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex, sensitive, or in-depth face-to-face interviews
- Establish and maintain trusting relationships with the community or special groups
- Design and culturally adapt questionnaires to avoid bias
- Manual judgment and interpretation of abnormal data and edge cases

**Skills to build:**

- Learning and using AI survey platforms (such as Qualtrics)
- Basic Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/Tableau)
- Qualitative research techniques and in-depth interview methods
- Cultural sensitivity and cross-cultural communication skills
- Project management and multitasking coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Data collection
- Market research
- Public opinion polling
- Remote interviewing

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

- People who enjoy flexible working hours and can adapt to project-based work

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for survey interviewers in Spain?**
A: Entry-level annual salary is around €14,000-€18,000, senior can reach €30,000. Salary is usually calculated hourly or per project; full-time annualized income is affected by project volume.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
A: It's very difficult. Survey interviewers are not in demand in Spain, and the approval rate for employer-sponsored work visas is low. It is recommended to consider other high-demand professions or switch to positions in data analysis, market research, and similar fields.

**Q: What career development paths are available for survey interviewers?**
A: You can be promoted to team leader, project coordinator, data analyst, or transition to market research consultant. Further studies in statistics or sociology help you enter management positions.
