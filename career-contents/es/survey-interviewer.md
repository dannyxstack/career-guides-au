# Survey Interviewer（Survey Interviewer）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：4430 – Survey Interviewer。**

Survey interviewers collect data via phone, online, or face-to-face for market, social, or opinion polls, ensuring sample representativeness and data accuracy. They work on flexible project bases for research firms, government agencies, or consultancies.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (Employer-Sponsored)） | 需西班牙雇主提供工作合同，证明岗位无法在欧盟填补。因职业非紧缺，获批难度较大。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，但需证明经济能力，且调查访问员岗位竞争激烈。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for survey interviewers in Spain?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €14,000 to €18,000 per year, while senior roles can reach €30,000. Pay is often hourly or project-based, affecting full-time annual income.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. Survey interviewer is not a shortage occupation in Spain, making employer-sponsored work permits unlikely. Consider alternative high-demand roles or transitioning to data analysis or market research.
