# Medical or Clinic Secretary（Medical or Clinic Secretary）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3614 – Medical or Clinic Secretary。**

Medical or clinic secretaries manage daily administrative tasks in healthcare settings, including patient scheduling, medical records management, billing, and communication between doctors and patients. They require strong organizational and communication skills and work in private clinics or public health centers.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for medical secretaries in Spain?**
答：Entry-level medical secretaries earn around €18,000-€22,000 gross annually, mid-level €22,000-€26,000, and senior up to €30,000. Public hospitals generally pay slightly more than private clinics.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain as medical secretaries?**
答：Medical secretary is not a shortage occupation in Spain and has low salary, making it ineligible for high-skilled visas. Non-EU citizens would need employer sponsorship for a work permit, which is difficult as the position can be easily filled by locals.

**问：What education is required to become a medical secretary?**
答：A vocational training degree (FP Grado Medio or FP Grado Superior) in administration or healthcare administration is typically required. Some employers accept high school diplomas, but vocational certification improves competitiveness.
