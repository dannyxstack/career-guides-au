# Agricultural Laborers (Except in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens)（Agricultural Laborers (Except in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens)）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9511 – Agricultural Laborers (Except in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens)。**

Agricultural laborers in Spain perform manual tasks in field crop farming such as cereals, oilseeds, and cotton, including planting, irrigation, fertilizing, weeding, and harvesting. Work is often seasonal and physically demanding.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary of an agricultural laborer?**
答：Full-time agricultural laborers earn approximately €14,000-€25,000 per year, depending on experience and skills. Entry-level: €14,000-€17,000; machinery operators: €20,000-€25,000. Seasonal workers earn €40-€60 per day.

**问：Can agricultural laborers immigrate to Spain through work?**
答：It is very difficult. Agricultural laborers are not listed as a shortage occupation. Employers must prove no EU candidates are available. Seasonal permits allow short-term work, but transitioning to permanent residence is nearly impossible due to low skills and low salary.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for agricultural laborers?**
答：Possible paths include becoming a machinery operator, agricultural technician (with training), or contract farmer. Vocational training (FP) can lead to roles in agroecology or farm management.
