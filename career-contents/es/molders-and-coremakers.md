# Molders and Coremakers（Molders and Coremakers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7311 – Molders and Coremakers。**

Molders and coremakers produce sand molds and cores for metal casting, a key trade in the foundry industry.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract (Employed)） | 需西班牙雇主提供工作合同，并证明该职位无法在欧盟内填补，难度较高。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙居留最多12个月寻找工作，但职业前景有限。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for molders in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual gross salary is around €18,000-€22,000, senior can reach €28,000-€35,000, below national average.

**问：Can a Chinese citizen migrate to Spain as a molder?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list, and employers must prove inability to hire within EU.

**问：What is the career path for molders?**
答：Start as apprentice, advance to senior technician, team leader, or quality inspector, or study to become foundry engineer.
