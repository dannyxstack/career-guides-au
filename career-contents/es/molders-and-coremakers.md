# Molders and Coremakers — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7311 (CNO)**

Mold casters and core makers are responsible for manufacturing sand molds and sand cores for metal casting, which are key technical fields in the foundry industry.


---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 4,500. Spain's foundry industry is relatively low-automation, with stable demand for high-end precision castings, but overall employment is slowly declining. The demand for renewal and replacement provides certain employment opportunities.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

You can start as an apprentice, gain experience, and be promoted to team leader or quality inspector. You can also become a mold designer or foundry engineer through further study.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive pouring and demolding operations
- Simple plaster/concrete mold making
- Standard product molding (e.g., candles, soap)
- Semi-automatic glass blowing and finishing of glassware
- Visual inspection in quality control

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use 3D printing to quickly create complex mold prototypes
- Optimize product design using AI-generated design proposals
- Automated systems assist in precise control of temperature and material ratios
- Digital twin simulation improves yield rates
- Robots collaborate to complete heavy lifting and polishing

**Human moat:**

- High-precision hand carving and finishing (e.g., for artwork)
- Deep intuition and experiential judgment of material properties
- Ability for on-site adjustments and improvisation
- Client Communication and Customized Design
- Multi-process integration and problem solving

**Skills to build:**

- Digital modeling and 3D printing operations
- Knowledge of materials science and performance optimization
- Basic programming and robot collaboration skills
- Computer-aided design and simulation (CAD/CAE)
- Fundamentals of quality control data analysis
- Client needs analysis and solution design

**Key growth areas:**

- manufacturing
- foundry
- metal casting
- industrial trades

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EU member state, Spain admits skilled foreign professionals mainly through the EU Blue Card and national work-permit routes. Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. Rules and salary thresholds change yearly, so always check the official source before making plans.

**Official immigration source:** [EU Immigration Portal](https://immigration-portal.ec.europa.eu/index_en)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- A pragmatist who enjoys hands-on operations and pays attention to detail
- People willing to work in high-temperature, dusty environments
- Strong interest in manufacturing and metal processing

**Who is not suited**
- People sensitive to their work environment and who seek comfortable office comfort
- Those seeking high salary and rapid promotion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for mold casters in Spain?**
A: Pre-tax junior approx 18,000-22,000 EUR/year, senior up to 28,000-35,000 EUR, below national average.

**Q: Can Chinese citizens become mold casters in Spain through skilled migration?**
A: Very difficult. This occupation is not on the shortage list, and employers must prove inability to recruit within the EU, leading to low visa approval probability.

**Q: What is the career development path for mold casters?**
A: You can start as an apprentice and be promoted to senior technician, team leader, or quality inspector, or further study to become a foundry engineer.
