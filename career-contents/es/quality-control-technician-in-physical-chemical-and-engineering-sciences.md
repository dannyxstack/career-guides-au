# Quality Control Technician in Physical, Chemical and Engineering Sciences（Quality Control Technician in Physical, Chemical and Engineering Sciences）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3160 – Quality Control Technician in Physical, Chemical and Engineering Sciences。**

Responsible for quality inspection, testing, and certification of products and processes in manufacturing, laboratories, and engineering sectors to ensure compliance with Spanish and EU standards.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for quality control technicians in Spain?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn around €22,000-€28,000 per year, mid-career up to €36,000, and senior managers over €48,000. Higher salaries in pharmaceutical and automotive sectors.

**问：What migration pathways exist for quality control technicians to Spain?**
答：Options include EU Blue Card (university degree and salary above €33,908), High-Skilled Professional visa (Ley 14/2013), or employed work permit, all requiring employer sponsorship.

**问：What education is required for this occupation?**
答：Typically requires Higher Vocational Training (FP Grado Superior) or a bachelor's degree in engineering or chemistry. A master's degree can help for management roles.
