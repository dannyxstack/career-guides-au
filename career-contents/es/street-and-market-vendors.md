# Street and Market Vendors（Street and Market Vendors）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5412 – Street and Market Vendors。**

Sell various goods such as food, clothing, and handicrafts at temporary markets or street stalls. Work is flexible but income is unstable, mostly self-employed or short-term.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

**职位需求量：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 45,000。西班牙街头和集市零售业因旅游业和本地消费带动，就业需求稳定，但受经济周期和季节因素影响大。数字化支付和电商虽带来挑战，但传统市场仍具文化吸引力，预计未来五年从业人数小幅增长。

**竞争度：中（★★★☆☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 从业者通常从助手起步，积累经验后可自营摊位或升级为市场管理员。部分人转向线上销售结合摆摊，或扩大规模成为小型批发商。缺乏正式晋升阶梯，收入与个人经营能力强相关。
**AI替代风险：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

主要增长方向：

- Seasonal demand
- Tourism-driven
- Self-employment

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract (Employed)） | 极少数情况，需雇主证明无法招到本地人，通常不适用于此类非技术岗位 |
| Subclass Cuenta propia（Self-Employment Visa） | 自雇摊贩可申请，需提交商业计划并证明经济能力，门槛较高 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual income of a street vendor?**
答：Entry-level vendors earn around €12,000-€18,000, self-employed vendors €18,000-€25,000, and experienced ones with multiple stalls can exceed €25,000. Income varies greatly by season, location, and product type.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain as street vendors?**
答：Very unlikely. This is a non-skilled occupation, making work visas hard to obtain. The self-employment visa requires significant investment and a business plan, usually not feasible for small-scale vendors.

**问：How can non-EU citizens legally vend on the streets in Spain?**
答：They must first obtain legal residency (e.g., student, family reunion, or work permit), then apply for a municipal vendor license. Student permits usually prohibit self-employment, and work permits require employer sponsorship, which is nearly impossible for vendors.
