# Environmental Engineer（Environmental Engineer）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2437 – Environmental Engineer。**

Environmental engineers assess and solve environmental issues such as pollution control, waste management, and sustainable design, ensuring projects comply with regulations.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Environmental Engineer？**
- 对环保有热情，愿意从事工程与法规结合的工作
- 喜欢户外作业与项目现场管理
- 具备跨学科思维和团队协作能力

**谁不适合学Environmental Engineer？**
- 不喜欢频繁更新法规知识或适应政策变化
- 对长期文书工作缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for environmental engineers?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-35,000, mid-level €36,000-50,000, senior up to €70,000 (gross), varying by sector and region.

**问：How can non-EU environmental engineers immigrate to Spain?**
答：Via the Highly Qualified Professional visa (Law 14/2013) or EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer and meet salary thresholds; also possible with a job-seeking visa.

**问：Is degree recognition needed?**
答：Yes, homologación by the Spanish Ministry of Education is required to equate a foreign degree to a Spanish degree; professional association membership may have extra requirements.
