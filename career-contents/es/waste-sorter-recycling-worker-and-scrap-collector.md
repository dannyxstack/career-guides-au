# Waste Sorter, Recycling Worker and Scrap Collector（Waste Sorter, Recycling Worker and Scrap Collector）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9442 – Waste Sorter, Recycling Worker and Scrap Collector。**

Sort, collect and classify waste materials at recycling centers, clean points and scrap yards; operate recycling equipment and manage reusable materials.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work permit for employed workers） | 需西班牙雇主担保，证明岗位无法在本地市场填补，通常不适用于此类低技能岗位。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job search visa） | 适合高学历或紧缺职业，本职业不符合条件。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €17,000-€20,000, senior up to €25,000-€30,000, varying by region and employer.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Almost impossible. It is a low-skilled job; employers struggle to obtain work permits, and it is not a shortage occupation.

**问：What training or certification is required?**
答：No formal degree needed, but some positions require waste handling certification or forklift license.
