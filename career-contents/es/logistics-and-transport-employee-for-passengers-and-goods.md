# Logistics and Transport Employee for Passengers and Goods（Logistics and Transport Employee for Passengers and Goods）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：4123 – Logistics and Transport Employee for Passengers and Goods。**

Coordinate and operate the transport of passengers and goods, including scheduling, tracking, loading/unloading, and documentation, ensuring efficient logistics chain.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 由西班牙公司担保的工作居留，需证明岗位无法由本地人填补。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，需高学历或相关经验。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for logistics and transport employees?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 18,000-22,000 EUR, mid-level 22,000-28,000, and senior up to 35,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a Spanish work visa for logistics?**
答：Usually through a company-sponsored work permit (Cuenta ajena) or a job search visa (Búsqueda de empleo).

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：Starting as an operator, you can advance to team supervisor, logistics coordinator, or move into supply chain management.
