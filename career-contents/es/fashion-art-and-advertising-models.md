# Fashion, Art and Advertising Models（Fashion, Art and Advertising Models）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5493 – Fashion, Art and Advertising Models。**

Models display clothing, accessories, or products in fashion shows, photo shoots, advertisements, and artistic projects, requiring good physique, expressiveness, and camera presence, with flexible but highly competitive work.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 需西班牙雇主提供工作合同，但模特多为自雇或短期项目，难度较大 |
| Subclass Cuenta propia（Work Permit for Self-Employed） | 自雇签证，需证明业务计划和经济能力，适合自由模特 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Fashion, Art and Advertising Models？**
- 外形出众、表现力强，适应镜头和舞台
- 心理素质好，能应对高强度竞争和频繁拒绝
- 善于自我营销，接受自由职业不稳定性

**谁不适合学Fashion, Art and Advertising Models？**
- 追求稳定收入和长期合同者
- 不擅长社交或面对镜头感到不适者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary of a model in Spain?**
答：Entry-level models earn around €5,000-15,000, mid-level €20,000-40,000, and top models over €50,000, but income is highly variable.

**问：Can foreigners migrate to Spain as models?**
答：Possibly but difficult. Usually requires employer sponsorship or self-employed visa, proving high professional capacity.

**问：How can models improve their career prospects?**
答：Build a professional portfolio, sign with reputable agencies, expand social media influence, participate in international fashion weeks, and later transition into styling or casting.
