# Earthmoving Plant Operator（Earthmoving Plant Operator）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：8331 – Earthmoving Plant Operator。**

Operates excavators, bulldozers, loaders, and similar earthmoving equipment for excavation, grading, and loading in construction, mining, and infrastructure projects.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Trabajo por cuenta a（Work for an employer） | 需雇主提供工作合同并证明无法在当地找到合适员工，经劳工部审批。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an earthmoving plant operator in Spain?**
答：The average gross annual salary ranges from €24,000 to €32,000, with entry-level lower and senior roles exceeding €35,000. Overtime and project allowances can boost income.

**问：How can a foreigner become an earthmoving plant operator in Spain?**
答：The main route is through a work contract with employer sponsorship. It's advisable to obtain training certificates and work experience. EU citizens can freely move.
