# Physiotherapy Assistant（Physiotherapy Assistant）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3325 – Physiotherapy Assistant。**

Physiotherapy assistants support physiotherapists by helping patients with exercises, applying therapeutic equipment, monitoring progress, and providing basic care in hospitals, clinics, or nursing homes.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract (employed)） | 获得西班牙雇主工作合同后申请工作居留，需证明职位无法由本地人填补。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job search visa） | 高学历者可在西班牙寻找工作6个月，但不适合该职业因学历要求不高。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary of a physiotherapy assistant in Spain?**
答：Entry-level around €18,000-€22,000, experienced up to €28,000-€34,000, depending on region and employer.

**问：Can a Chinese citizen immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Unlikely, as it does not require higher education and is not on the shortage list, making work permit sponsorship difficult.

**问：What training is required to become a physiotherapy assistant?**
答：Typically a 2-year intermediate vocational training (FP Grado Medio) in social healthcare or similar; some employers require first aid certification.
