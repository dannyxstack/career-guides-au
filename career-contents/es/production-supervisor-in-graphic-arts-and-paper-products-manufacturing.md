# Production Supervisor in Graphic Arts and Paper Products Manufacturing（Production Supervisor in Graphic Arts and Paper Products Manufacturing）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3207 – Production Supervisor in Graphic Arts and Paper Products Manufacturing。**

Oversees daily operations in printing and paper product manufacturing, ensuring quality and efficiency, coordinating staff schedules, controlling costs, and complying with safety and environmental regulations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。
