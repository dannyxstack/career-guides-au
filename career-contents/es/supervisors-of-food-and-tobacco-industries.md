# Supervisors of Food and Tobacco Industries（Supervisors of Food and Tobacco Industries）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3203 – Supervisors of Food and Tobacco Industries。**

Oversee food and tobacco production lines, ensuring safety and quality standards, coordinating workers and machinery operations.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for food industry supervisors?**
答：Entry level €22,000-€28,000, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, senior €35,000-€45,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to Spain for this occupation?**
答：Via EU Blue Card or Highly Skilled Professional visa (Law 14/2013), requiring employer sponsorship and relevant qualifications.
