# Signwriters, Glass Engravers, Decorative Painters Of Various Articles — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7616 (CNO)**

This profession includes making signs, engraving glass, and creating decorative paintings for various items, involving both handicraft and mechanical skills, commonly found in advertising, decoration, and handicraft industries.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Traditional handmade sign-making, glass engraving, and decorative painting positions have been significantly impacted by AI and automation, but personalized customization and high-end artistic demands still leave room for humans, creating significant pressure for skill transformation.

Starting as an apprentice or assistant, you can be promoted to senior craftsman or supervisor after accumulating skills. You can also start your own studio or switch to more creative roles like advertising decoration design.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use computer-aided design software to generate signage and decorative patterns
- CNC machines automatically engrave glass and cut sign materials
- Batch standardized spraying and printing of decorative paintings
- AI-generated fonts and graphic solutions are available for clients to choose from

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI tools to quickly generate creative sketches and multiple design proposals
- Preview signage and decorative effects in a virtual environment using AR/VR technology
- Leverage AI to analyze customer preferences and provide personalized recommendations
- Automated equipment is used to improve the precision and efficiency of engraving and cutting

**Human moat:**

- The irreplaceable texture and artistic expression found in hand-carved and painting
- Complex creative design and deep client communication skills
- A deep understanding of traditional craftsmanship and material properties
- Aesthetic judgment and exclusive craftsmanship in the high-end custom market

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in AI design tools (such as Adobe Firefly, Midjourney)
- Learn the operation and programming of CNC engraving machines and printers
- Enhance artistic creativity and visual design capabilities
- Master digital modeling and 3D printing technologies
- Strengthen customer communication and project management capabilities
- Learn about materials science and sustainable processes

**Key growth areas:**

- Signage
- Glass Art
- Decorative Painting
- Customization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for this profession in Spain?**
A: Junior: about 18,000-22,000 EUR/year, mid-level: 22,000-28,000, senior: up to 35,000 EUR/year.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain to work in this profession?**
A: It is quite challenging because this occupation is not in short supply and there is an abundant local labor force in Spain. Usually, a company sponsorship is required to obtain a work visa.
