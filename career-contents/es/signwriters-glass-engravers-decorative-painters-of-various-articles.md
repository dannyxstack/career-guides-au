# Signwriters, Glass Engravers, Decorative Painters Of Various Articles（Signwriters, Glass Engravers, Decorative Painters Of Various Articles）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7616 – Signwriters, Glass Engravers, Decorative Painters Of Various Articles。**

This occupation involves creating signs, engraving glass, and applying decorative painting to various articles, using both manual and mechanical techniques, commonly found in advertising, decoration, and craft sectors.
