# Plasterers and Stucco Masons — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7212 (CNO)**

Responsible for applying plaster, gypsum, or cement mortar to the interior and exterior walls of buildings, leveling, decorating, and protecting them to ensure the walls are smooth and attractive.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Plastering and plastering work are limited to AI: automated tasks such as repeated plaster application and material mixing, but precise leveling and complex shapes still require manual judgment. Moreover, digital penetration at Spanish construction sites is slow, so job openings are unlikely to be significantly reduced in the short term.

You can advance from an ordinary plasterer to foreman or self-employed contractor, or specialize in decorative plaster or restoration techniques. Those with more experience can move on to project management or quality supervision.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use an automatic sprayer to apply large-area plaster
- An AI vision system automatically detects wall flatness and generates repair plans
- The intelligent mixer automatically mixes materials according to the proportion
- The BIM model automatically generates the plaster layer thickness and construction sequence

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted measurement of wall unevenness and guidance for manual leveling
- AR glasses project plaster thickness and molding lines to improve accuracy
- Digital twins simulate the effects of different materials to assist customer decision-making
- Automatically records construction data for quality traceability and schedule optimization
- AI recommends the best material ratios and curing times

**Human moat:**

- Manual plastering and modeling capabilities for complex and irregular surfaces
- On-site assessment of the base condition and adjustment of construction techniques
- Communicate with the client to confirm the decorative effect
- Fine operation in confined or irregular spaces
- Experience handling traditional materials such as lime and hemp knives

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic spraying and leveling robots
- Read BIM models and digital construction drawings
- Use laser measuring instruments and digital levels
- Learn about eco-friendly materials and intelligent plastering systems
- Basic English terminology to operate imported equipment
- Project management and on-site coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Construction recovery
- Renovation projects
- Energy efficiency retrofits
- Specialist finishes

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for plasterers?**
A: Junior annual income approximately EUR 18,000-22,000, intermediate EUR 22,000-28,000, senior foreman up to EUR 28,000-35,000.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens obtain a Spanish plasterer work visa?**
A: You must obtain a work contract from a Spanish employer, obtain approval from the Labor Bureau, and apply for employment residence (Cuenta ajena). Foreign degrees usually do not require formal recognition, but proof of training is required.
