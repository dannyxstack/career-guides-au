# Production Support Office Clerks（Production Support Office Clerks）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：4122 – Production Support Office Clerks。**

Handle production orders, inventory records, logistics coordination, and quality documentation to support daily office operations in manufacturing.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (Employer-Sponsored)） | 由雇主担保的工作居留，需证明岗位无法在欧盟内填补。 |
| Subclass Búsqueda de empleo（Job-Seeker Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，后续转为工作居留。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Production Support Office Clerks？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for this occupation in Spain?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €18,000 to €22,000 gross annually, mid-level €22,000-€28,000, and senior roles up to €35,000.

**问：Is it easy for foreigners to immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：No, it is difficult because it is not a shortage occupation; employers must prove no EU candidate is available, and the salary is low, making skilled migration routes challenging.

**问：What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
答：Promotion to administrative supervisor, production planner, purchasing assistant, or further education into logistics and supply chain management.
