# Industrial and Production Technical Engineers（Industrial and Production Technical Engineers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2461 – Industrial and Production Technical Engineers。**

Industrial and production technical engineers design, optimize, and supervise manufacturing processes and industrial systems to ensure efficiency, quality, and safety. They typically work in factories, engineering consultancies, or public institutions, bridging engineering design and actual production.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Industrial and Production Technical Engineers？**
- 喜欢动手解决实际工程问题，注重流程优化
- 具备数学、物理基础，乐于在工业环境中工作
- 希望进入工业自动化、可持续制造等新兴领域

**谁不适合学Industrial and Production Technical Engineers？**
- 不适应工厂或车间环境，偏好纯理论工作
- 对重复性工艺改进缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for industrial technical engineers in Spain?**
答：Entry level ranges from €24,000 to €32,000, mid-level €32,000 to €45,000, and senior can exceed €65,000. Salaries vary by region, industry, and experience, with higher pay in Madrid and the Basque Country.

**问：How can a foreign engineer immigrate to Spain?**
答：Through the EU Blue Card or the Highly Skilled Professional visa (Ley 14/2013), requiring a job offer from a Spanish employer and a degree homologation.

**问：Is registration with the Spanish engineering association mandatory?**
答：Not mandatory, but joining COGITI allows signing off projects and benefits career development. Foreign degrees require homologación.
