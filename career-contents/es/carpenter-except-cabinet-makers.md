# Carpenter (except cabinet makers) — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 7131 (CNO)**

Carpenters are responsible for installing timber structures, doors and windows, flooring, and furniture on construction sites, using manual and electric tools for cutting, assembly, and fixing. They are one of the core technical trades in Spain's construction industry.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. The recovery of Spain's construction industry has driven increased demand for carpenters, especially in residential renovations and new construction projects. An aging workforce has led to a shortage of skilled workers, and employment is expected to grow steadily over the next five years.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior carpenters can be promoted to foreman or site supervisor, or specialize in ancient building restoration, furniture customization, or industrial formwork systems. After accumulating experience, they can start their own business and undertake contracting projects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use BIM software for formwork design optimization (AI replaces manual calculation)
- Automatic inspection of template dimensions and position accuracy through AI image recognition
- Use drones or laser scanning to automatically measure field data
- AI-generated cutting lists and material optimization plans
- Automated template layout linked with CNC cutting.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted augmented reality (AR) headsets provide real-time template installation guidance
- AI-driven project management tools optimize processes and resource scheduling
- Digital twin simulates template forces to improve safety and quality
- AI voice assistant for on-site documentation and reporting
- Automatically generate construction progress reports and cost estimates

**Human moat:**

- On-site adjustment and manual fabrication of complex irregular formwork
- Multi-trade coordination and on-site problem-solving skills
- Experiential judgment of material properties (wood defects, humidity, etc.)
- Client communication and understanding of customized needs
- Physical adaptability for working at heights and heavy manual labor

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of BIM software (e.g., Revit, Tekla)
- Use of AR/VR construction assistance tools
- CNC woodworking equipment programming and operation.
- Basic data analysis (e.g., material utilization optimization)
- Sustainable materials and green building practices.
- Communication and teamwork (with AI tool assistance)

**Key growth areas:**

- construction recovery
- renovation boom
- aging workforce
- green building

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EU member state, Spain admits skilled foreign professionals mainly through the EU Blue Card and national work-permit routes. Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. Rules and salary thresholds change yearly, so always check the official source before making plans.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on activities, are physically strong, and pay attention to detail
- People willing to work outdoors and on construction sites
- People with basic math and spatial thinking skills

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike physical labor and dusty environments
- People with overly high expectations for career advancement

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for carpenters in Spain?**
A: Entry-level carpenter: approximately €18,000-24,000 per year; experienced carpenters can earn €32,000-45,000; self-employed earn more but with greater fluctuation.

**Q: Can carpenters go to Spain through skilled immigration?**
A: Carpenters usually do not meet the requirements for the EU Blue Card (a bachelor's degree is required), but can obtain employment residence (Cuenta ajena) through company sponsorship, and if the occupation is on the Shortage List, it is easier to get approval.

**Q: What are the career prospects for carpentry?**
A: You can be promoted to foreman, project manager, or start your own business, or specialize in ancient building restoration or custom furniture. The aging workforce offers more promotion opportunities.
