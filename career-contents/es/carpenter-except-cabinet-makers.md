# Carpenter (except cabinet makers)（Carpenter (except cabinet makers)）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7131 – Carpenter (except cabinet makers)。**

Carpenters install wooden structures, doors, windows, and flooring on construction sites, using hand and power tools for cutting, assembling, and fixing, a key trade in Spain's construction sector.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (employed)） | 受雇工作居留，需西班牙公司提供工作合同，并证明该职位难以在本地招聘。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 求职签证，允许在西班牙停留6个月寻找工作，找到后转为工作居留。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a carpenter in Spain?**
答：Entry-level carpenters earn €18,000-€24,000 per year, experienced ones €32,000-€45,000; self-employed may earn more but with volatility.

**问：Can a carpenter migrate to Spain through skilled migration?**
答：Carpenters generally do not qualify for the EU Blue Card (requires a degree), but may obtain a work permit (Cuenta ajena) via employer sponsorship, especially if the occupation is in shortage.

**问：What are the career prospects for a carpenter?**
答：Progression to foreman, project manager, or self-employment; specialization in heritage restoration or custom furniture; aging workforce offers advancement opportunities.
