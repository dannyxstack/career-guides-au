# Cook（Cook）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5110 – Cook。**

Cooks are central to the hospitality industry, responsible for food preparation, cooking, and menu development, working in restaurants, hotels, schools, and other establishments.

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量：高（★★★★☆）。** 全国从业人数约 280,000。西班牙旅游业强劲复苏推动厨师需求增长，尤其在沿海旅游城市和马德里、巴塞罗那等大都市。预计未来5年就业增长约8%。

**竞争度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 需大学本科以上学历或5年以上经验，年薪达到西班牙平均工资1.5倍（约34,000欧元） |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional） | 适用于年收入超过40,000欧元的厨师，需高技能合同 |
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Visa (Employed)） | 需雇主担保，厨师属于短缺职业，部分省份适用简化程序 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for cooks in Spain?**
答：Entry-level cooks earn about 18,000-22,000 EUR gross annually, mid-level 22,000-30,000 EUR, and executive chefs can earn over 50,000 EUR, with higher salaries in top restaurants.

**问：How can a Chinese cook immigrate to Spain?**
答：Through the EU Blue Card (requires degree or 5 years experience + high salary) or an employed work visa (requires employer sponsorship). Cooks are in shortage, facilitating processing in some regions.

**问：What certificates are needed to work as a cook in Spain?**
答：A food safety and hygiene certificate is mandatory. An FP cooking diploma is recommended, and allergen management certificates are increasingly important.
