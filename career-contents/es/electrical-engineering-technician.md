# Electrical Engineering Technician（Electrical Engineering Technician）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2471 – Electrical Engineering Technician。**

Electrical engineering technicians design, install, and maintain power systems and equipment in industrial, building, and infrastructure settings, key in Spain's energy transition and smart grid development.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for electrical engineering technicians in Spain?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn around €22,000-€28,000 annually, mid-level €28,000-€36,000, and senior roles can exceed €45,000. Higher salaries in major energy firms and Madrid/Barcelona.

**问：How can third-country nationals immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Via EU Blue Card (bachelor degree + salary ≥€33,908) or Highly Qualified Professional visa (salary ≥€35,000). Requires a Spanish job offer and degree homologation. Listed as a shortage occupation, approval rate is favorable.

**问：Is Spanish FP qualification recognized in my home country?**
答：Spanish official FP diplomas are EU-recognized but may require credential evaluation in non-EU countries like China. For global mobility, a 4-year Grado (bachelor) is recommended.
