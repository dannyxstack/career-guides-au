# Roof Coverers（Roof Coverers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：7291 – Roof Coverers。**

Specialize in installing and repairing various roofing materials (tiles, metal sheets, asphalt, etc.) to ensure waterproofing and insulation, a key trade in construction.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（受雇工作居留） | 由西班牙雇主担保，提交工作合同和紧缺职业证明，适用于有雇主雇佣的屋顶工。 |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（高技能专业人才居留（Ley 14/2013）） | 适用于拥有高级资格（如FP Grado Superior）且工资达一定水平的屋顶工，审批较快。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（求职签证） | 允许在高技能类别下进入西班牙寻找工作6个月，适合有经验的屋顶工。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for roof coverers in Spain?**
答：Entry-level roof coverers earn €18,000-€22,000, mid-level €22,000-€28,000, and experienced up to €35,000 per year, depending on experience and qualifications.

**问：How can a foreign roof coverer immigrate to Spain?**
答：Through a work contract with employer sponsorship (Cuenta ajena) or the high-skilled professional visa (Altamente cualificado). Roof covering is a shortage occupation, facilitating work permits.

**问：What are the career progression paths for roof coverers?**
答：Start as an apprentice, advance to skilled worker, foreman, project manager, or self-employment. Learning green roofing and solar integration can broaden opportunities.
