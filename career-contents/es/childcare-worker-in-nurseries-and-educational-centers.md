# Childcare Worker in Nurseries and Educational Centers（Childcare Worker in Nurseries and Educational Centers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5721 – Childcare Worker in Nurseries and Educational Centers。**

Provides daily care, play-based activities, and basic educational support for infants and preschool children in nurseries, daycare centers, and early childhood education settings.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (Employed)） | 需雇主担保，证明岗位无法由本地或欧盟公民填补，非紧缺职业，获批难度较大。 |
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 通常要求高薪及大学学历，保育员薪资难以达标，极少数高级管理岗位可能适用。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for childcare workers in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €14,000 to €18,000, mid-level €18,000-€22,000, and supervisory roles up to €28,000. Public institutions pay more than private ones.

**问：Can foreign childcare workers work in Spain?**
答：Yes, but requires employer sponsorship and proof that no local or EU worker is available. It is not a shortage occupation, making migration difficult; homologation of foreign qualifications is mandatory.

**问：What education is required to become a childcare worker?**
答：At least a FP Grado Superior in Early Childhood Education (Técnico en Educación Infantil) or a university degree in Early Childhood Education. Foreign qualifications must undergo homologation.
