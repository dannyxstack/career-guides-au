# Miners And Other Operators In Mining Installations（Miners And Other Operators In Mining Installations）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：8111 – Miners And Other Operators In Mining Installations。**

Responsible for underground or open-pit mining operations including drilling, blasting, loading and transporting minerals, operating mining machinery and equipment, and ensuring workplace safety. Steady demand exists in Spain's major mining areas.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of miners in Spain?**
答：Entry-level miners earn around €22,000-€28,000 gross annually, mid-level €28,000-€36,000, and senior supervisors up to €45,000, with underground work allowances.

**问：Can third-country nationals immigrate to Spain as miners?**
答：Yes, but requires a Spanish employer to sponsor a work permit (Cuenta ajena), proving labor shortage. Miners are in shortage, but Spanish language skills and qualification recognition are key.

**问：What education is needed to become a miner?**
答：Typically requires completing FP Grado Medio or Superior in mining, plus a blasting safety license. Some roles only require secondary school with on-the-job training.
