# Archivists and Museum Curators（Archivists and Museum Curators）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2911 – Archivists and Museum Curators。**

Responsible for collecting, organizing, preserving, and exhibiting archival and museum collections, as well as providing public education and research support.

---

## 1. 教育路径 / 周期 / 费用

**学习难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用（AUD） |
|---|---|---:|
| 大学本科（Grado） | 4年（Grado） | $1,000~$4,000（公立大学每年学费约1000-4000欧元，私立更高） |
| 硕士（Máster） | 1-2年（Máster） | $2,000~$8,000（档案学或博物馆学硕士，部分大学费用更高） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for archivists in Spain?**
答：Entry-level about €20,000-26,000, mid-level €27,000-34,000, senior €35,000-45,000, and museum directors can earn over €50,000.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Spain as an archivist?**
答：Via EU Blue Card or Highly Qualified Professional visa, requiring a university degree and job offer. Foreign degrees must undergo Homologación.

**问：Does this career have good prospects?**
答：Digitalization and cultural heritage protection bring new opportunities, but competition for public positions is high. Digital skills and foreign languages help advancement.
