# Non-Regulated Language Teacher（Non-Regulated Language Teacher）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2322 – Non-Regulated Language Teacher。**

Professionals who teach foreign languages (mainly English) in non-regulated educational settings such as language schools and training centers. No official homologation required, but teaching skills and language proficiency are essential.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Non-Regulated Language Teacher？**
- 母语为英语或具备C1以上英语水平的人
- 希望从事非高强度教育工作的求职者
- 有TEFL/CELTA等教学证书的人

**谁不适合学Non-Regulated Language Teacher？**
- 期望高薪或稳定长期合同的人
- 不喜欢与成人或青少年互动的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for non-regulated language teachers?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €18,000 to €24,000, mid-level from €24,000 to €32,000, and senior coordinators up to €40,000 annually. Salaries are lower than in Northern Europe, but the cost of living is also lower.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa as a language teacher in Spain?**
答：The main route is the employed work permit (Cuenta ajena), requiring a job offer from a language school and proof of recruitment difficulty. The EU Blue Card is also possible with higher salary. TEFL certification helps.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a language teacher?**
答：Although not mandatory, a university degree and TEFL/CELTA certificate are commonly required. Non-regulated institutions do not require official homologation of foreign degrees.
