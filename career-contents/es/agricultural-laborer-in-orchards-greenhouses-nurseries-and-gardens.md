# Agricultural Laborer in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens（Agricultural Laborer in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9512 – Agricultural Laborer in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens。**

Performs basic agricultural tasks such as planting, harvesting, irrigating, weeding, and fertilizing in orchards, greenhouses, nurseries, and gardens; also involved in packing and loading produce.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for agricultural laborers?**
答：Agricultural laborers in Spain are typically paid at the minimum wage (SMI), which is €1,134 per month (14 payments) in 2024, amounting to an annual gross salary of around €15,876. Seasonal overtime can increase earnings.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Spain through this job?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation is not classified as high-skilled, so it is ineligible for the EU Blue Card. Employers must prove that no local or EU workers are available, and must pass a labor market test; the work visa process is cumbersome with low success rates.
