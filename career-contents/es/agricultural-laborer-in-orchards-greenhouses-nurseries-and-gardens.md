# Agricultural Laborer in Orchards, Greenhouses, Nurseries and Gardens — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 9512 (CNO)**

It is engaged in basic agricultural operations such as planting, harvesting, irrigating, weeding, and fertilizing in orchards, greenhouses, nurseries, or gardens, and is also responsible for packaging and loading harvested goods. Mostly operates in seasonal, high-intensity environments.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Seasonal workers can be promoted to fixed-contract laborers, and after gaining experience, may be promoted to foreman or shift to agricultural technical service positions. Some people gain higher positions through further studies in horticulture or agricultural technology.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive picking: Visual recognition and robotic arms will replace manual harvesting
- Irrigation monitoring: sensors and AI automatically adjust water and fertilizer
- Weeding operations: automated weeding guided by image recognition
- Production forecasting: AI models replace manual experience estimation
- Basic classification: Visual system automatically grades fruits

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Pest and disease identification: AI-assisted analysis of leaf images improves diagnostic accuracy
- Precise fertilization: data-driven optimization of fertilizer application timing and dosage
- Greenhouse environment control: AI predicts and fine-tunes temperature and humidity
- Workforce scheduling: algorithms match tasks and personnel to improve efficiency
- Sales Decision-Making: AI analysis of market trends assists pricing

**Human moat:**

- Rapid assessment and manual intervention for sudden situations (such as extreme weather or pest outbreaks).
- Manual skills and experience in fine operations (such as pruning and grafting).
- On-site communication and trust building with customers/visitors
- Overall understanding of ecosystems and non-standardized decision-making
- Flexible response in emergencies (such as equipment failures or disasters).

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of intelligent agricultural equipment
- Fundamentals of Data Analysis (Excel, Sensor Data Interpretation)
- Skills in drone and robot collaboration
- Basic AgTech software (farm management system) is used
- Knowledge of sustainable agricultural practices
- Language/communication skills (English or Spanish, connecting with technology suppliers)

**Key growth areas:**

- Seasonal labor demand
- Mechanization
- Organic farming
- Greenhouse expansion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the wage levels for agricultural workers?**
A: Spanish agricultural workers typically earn 1,134 euros per month (14 salary) in 2024 according to the minimum wage standard (SMI), with an annual salary of about 15,876 euros. Seasonal overtime can increase income.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Spain through this job?**
A: It's very difficult. This occupation does not belong to the high-skilled category and cannot apply for an EU Blue Card. Employers must prove that workers cannot be recruited locally in the EU or Spain, and must pass labor market tests. The procedures for obtaining a work visa are complicated and the success rate is low.
