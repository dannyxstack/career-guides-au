# Environmental Education Professional（Environmental Education Professional）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2326 – Environmental Education Professional。**

Environmental Education Professionals design and implement environmental awareness programs, teaching sustainable practices in schools, parks, NGOs, and government agencies through workshops, materials, and community outreach.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Environmental Education Professional？**
- 热爱自然与环保，善于沟通和教学
- 有耐心且创造力强，能设计互动教育活动

**谁不适合学Environmental Education Professional？**
- 追求高薪或快节奏商业环境
- 不擅长公众演讲或组织活动

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Environmental Education Professionals?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €18,000 to €24,000, mid-level €24,000-€32,000, and senior up to €42,000. Public sector offers stability but private NGOs may pay less.

**问：How can non-EU nationals apply for environmental education jobs in Spain?**
答：Common paths: 1) Employer-sponsored work permit; 2) EU Blue Card (requires master's degree); 3) Job-seeking visa. Degree validation (homologación) is required.

**问：Which degrees are suitable for environmental education?**
答：Bachelor's or FP in Environmental Science, Biology, Education, or Tourism Management. Master's in Environmental Education or Sustainability Education gives an edge.
