# Pawnbroker and Loan Officer（Pawnbroker and Loan Officer）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：4444 – Pawnbroker and Loan Officer。**

Works in pawnshops or lending institutions, evaluating collateral value and issuing small loans, while handling customer inquiries and transaction records.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract Visa） | 需获得西班牙雇主工作合同，且岗位需证明无法从欧盟内部招聘。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 允许在西班牙停留最多6个月寻找工作，但典当行岗位通常不满足高技能条件。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a pawnbroker and loan officer?**
答：In Spain, the gross annual salary ranges from €16,000 to €32,000, with entry-level around €16,000-€20,000 and managerial positions up to €32,000.

**问：Can third-country nationals immigrate to Spain to work as pawnbrokers?**
答：Very difficult. This is not a high-skilled occupation, so EU Blue Card or highly qualified professional visas are not available. The employer must prove the job cannot be filled from within the EU, and it is typically only suitable for those already holding Spanish residency.

**问：What is the career progression for this occupation?**
答：Progression from junior clerk to store manager or regional manager, or pivot to banking/financial customer service roles, requires accumulated appraisal experience and client networks.
