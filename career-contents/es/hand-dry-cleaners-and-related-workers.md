# Hand Dry Cleaners and Related Workers（Hand Dry Cleaners and Related Workers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9221 – Hand Dry Cleaners and Related Workers。**

Manual dry cleaning of garments, textiles, and leather goods using solvents, stain removal, ironing, and finishing. Typically employed in laundries, dry cleaners, and hotel linen services.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for hand dry cleaners?**
答：Annual gross salary ranges from €15,000 to €26,000, with entry-level around €15,000-€18,000 and senior up to €26,000, generally below national average.

**问：Can non-EU nationals immigrate via this occupation?**
答：Highly unlikely. Not a shortage occupation, low salary fails high-skilled visa requirements; only indirect routes like family reunification.

**问：Are there career advancement or training opportunities?**
答：Short courses in textile care can help, or become a store manager with experience, but overall limited progression.
