# Insurance Brokers and Agents — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 3521 (CNO)**

Insurance brokers and agents are responsible for providing individuals and businesses with insurance product consulting, risk assessment, and policy sales, facilitating insurance contracts on behalf of clients or insurance companies.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insurance Intermediary Registration (Registro administrativo de mediadores de seguros) | Spanish Directorate General of Insurance and Pension Funds (DGSFP) | Required | Registration is required to engage in insurance intermediary activities, and training or exams are required |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

They can rise from sales agents to team leaders or regional managers, with some moving into underwriting or claims roles. Senior professionals can establish independent brokerage firms or enter the bancassurance sector.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically calculate premiums and generate standardized policies
- Answering common insurance policy inquiries
- Handling claims data entry and preliminary review
- Bulk quotation emails are sent
- Automatic renewal reminders and follow-up

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted analysis of customer needs and risk exposures
- AI-generated personalized insurance plan recommendations
- AI optimizes customer segmentation and precise marketing
- AI monitors market rate changes in real time
- AI automatically generates compliance reports and logs

**Human moat:**

- Complex risk assessment (such as enterprise risk management)
- Long-term customer relationship maintenance based on trust
- Negotiation and customization of non-standardized policies
- Compliance Responsibilities and Fiduciary Duty
- Handling complex claims and disputes with humanistic care

**Skills to build:**

- Master AI tools (such as ChatGPT and insurtech platforms)
- Data analysis and interpretation skills
- Advanced customer relationship management (CRM) applications
- Digital marketing and social media operations
- Fundamentals of risk management and actuarial science
- Updated regulatory compliance knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Insurance
- Digitalization
- Aging population
- Risk management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Does insurance broker income mainly come from commissions?**
A: Yes, the base salary is usually low, with the main income coming from sales commissions, so income fluctuates greatly, but those with excellent performance can earn high salaries.

**Q: How can foreigners work as insurance brokers in Spain?**
A: You need to obtain work residency and register as an insurance intermediary. Usually applying for a Cuenta ajena or EU Blue Card through employer sponsorship. Language proficiency (Spanish B2 or above) and local certification are key.
