# Insurance Brokers and Agents（Insurance Brokers and Agents）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3521 – Insurance Brokers and Agents。**

Insurance brokers and agents advise individuals and businesses on insurance products, assess risks, and sell policies, representing either clients or insurance companies.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employed Persons） | 由西班牙雇主担保申请工作居留，适用于受雇于保险公司的员工 |
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 适合高学历（本科以上）且薪资达到门槛的受雇人员，可快速获得居留 |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional Visa） | 适用于高管或专业人才，薪资门槛较高，申请流程简化 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Do insurance brokers earn mainly from commissions?**
答：Yes, the base salary is often low, and the main income comes from sales commissions, so earnings can fluctuate, but top performers can earn high salaries.

**问：How can foreigners work as insurance brokers in Spain?**
答：They need a work permit and must register as an insurance intermediary. Usually, an employer sponsors a Cuenta ajena or EU Blue Card. Spanish language proficiency (B2 or above) and local certification are key.
