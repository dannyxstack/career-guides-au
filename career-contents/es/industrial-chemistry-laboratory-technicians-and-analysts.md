# Industrial Chemistry Laboratory Technicians and Analysts（Industrial Chemistry Laboratory Technicians and Analysts）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3127 – Industrial Chemistry Laboratory Technicians and Analysts。**

Technical professionals who perform analytical tests, quality control, sample preparation, and instrument operation in industrial chemistry laboratories, supporting production, R&D, and environmental monitoring.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for industrial chemistry laboratory technicians in Spain?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn around €22,000-€28,000 gross annually, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior or supervisory roles up to €45,000. Salaries increase with experience and qualifications.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Via the EU Blue Card (requires university degree and sufficient salary) or the Highly Skilled Professional visa (annual salary ≥€40,000). Employer-sponsored work permits are also possible but require a labor market test.

**问：What education is required to become an industrial chemistry laboratory technician?**
答：Typically a university degree (Grado) in chemistry or analytical chemistry, or a higher vocational training diploma (FP Grado Superior). Some employers also require safety training certificates.
