# Customer Service Administrative Assistants（Customer Service Administrative Assistants）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：4500 – Customer Service Administrative Assistants。**

Administrative staff who handle public inquiries, provide information, and process requests in person or by phone, typically in government offices, utilities, and private companies.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract (Employed)） | 需获得西班牙公司工作合同，满足最低工资要求，通过劳工局审核。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this occupation in Spain?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €18,000-22,000, mid-level €22,000-28,000, and senior up to €28,000-35,000, varying by region and sector.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to work in this occupation?**
答：It's difficult as the occupation is not in shortage and salaries are low, rarely meeting high-skilled visa criteria. Usually requires pre-existing residency.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically FP Grado Medio (intermediate vocational training) or high school diploma; some employers offer on-the-job training.
