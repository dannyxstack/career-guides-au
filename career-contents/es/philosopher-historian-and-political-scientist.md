# Philosophers, Historians and Political Scientists — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 2822 (CNO)**

Engaged in research, teaching, and analysis in philosophy, history, and political science, usually working at universities, research institutions, or think tanks, focusing on theoretical exploration and policy analysis in socio-cultural fields.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Academic positions in Spain's humanities and social sciences are highly competitive, with limited faculty gaps at public universities. However, in recent years, demand in emerging fields such as public policy research and digital transformation ethics has slightly increased, making it a stable but slow-growing profession.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career development usually starts as a doctoral research assistant, gradually advancing to assistant professor, associate professor, full professor, or shifting to think tanks and government agencies as policy analysts or researchers. Continuously publish high-level research results.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic literature retrieval and abstract (AI-generated reviews)
- Historical event timeline compilation and statistics
- Preliminary comparative analysis of policy texts
- Automatic transcription and keyword extraction of oral history recordings

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quantitative analysis of large-scale historical texts (such as corpus analysis)
- Simulation and Prediction of Political Theory (such as game theory models)
- A rapid cross-linguistic comparison of classic philosophical literature
- Policy effect simulation and causal inference

**Human moat:**

- Value judgments and ethical stance choices (such as definitions of justice)
- A deep interpretation of ambiguity and contradictory texts
- Original theoretical construction (such as proposing new concepts)
- Interdisciplinary integration and inspirational integration
- The persuasiveness of public communication and education

**Skills to build:**

- Programming (Python/R) and data analysis
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) tool usage.
- Statistical modeling and causal inference
- AI ethics and algorithmic bias analysis
- Digital Humanities Methods (Text Mining, Network Analysis)
- Data visualization and narrative communication

**Key growth areas:**

- stable
- academic
- policy analysis
- research

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EU member state, Spain admits skilled foreign professionals mainly through the EU Blue Card and national work-permit routes. Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. Rules and salary thresholds change yearly, so always check the official source before making plans.

**Official immigration source:** [EU Immigration Portal](https://immigration-portal.ec.europa.eu/index_en)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passionate about theoretical research and academic writing, possesses critical thinking
- Willing to pursue long-term doctoral programs and face fierce academic competition
- Strong interest in history, philosophy, or political science, and a desire to pursue education or research

**Who is not suited**
- Those seeking high salary or rapid career advancement
- Applicants who wish to immigrate to Spain with ease

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels of philosophers, historians, and political scientists?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approximately €20,000-28,000, professor or senior researcher up to €45,000-60,000, but salary increases are slow and limited by position.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
A: Yes, but it's quite challenging. Non-EU applicants must first obtain a work contract with a Spanish university or research institution and pass academic credential verification. EU citizens can move directly.
