# Philosophers, Historians and Political Scientists（Philosophers, Historians and Political Scientists）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2822 – Philosophers, Historians and Political Scientists。**

Engage in research, teaching, and analysis in philosophy, history, and political science, typically at universities, research institutes, or think tanks, focusing on theoretical inquiry and policy analysis in social and cultural fields.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在ES独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional Visa） | 适用于高学历专业人才，需获得高校或研究机构的工作合同。 |
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Employed Work Visa） | 普通受雇工作签证，需雇主担保，适合从事非学术岗位。 |
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 欧盟蓝卡，需高薪（通常为西班牙平均工资1.5倍以上）和高等教育背景。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Philosophers, Historians and Political Scientists？**
- 热爱理论研究与学术写作，具备批判性思维
- 愿意攻读长期博士课程并面对激烈学术竞争
- 对历史、哲学或政治学有深厚兴趣，希望从事教育或研究

**谁不适合学Philosophers, Historians and Political Scientists？**
- 追求高薪或快速职业晋升者
- 希望轻松移民西班牙的申请人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for philosophers, historians and political scientists?**
答：Entry-level positions earn €20,000-28,000 annually; professors or senior researchers can reach €45,000-60,000, but salary growth is slow and positions are limited.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Yes, but it is challenging. Non-EU applicants need a job contract from a Spanish university or research institute and must have their degree homologated. EU citizens can freely move.
