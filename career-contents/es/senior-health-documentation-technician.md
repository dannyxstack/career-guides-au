# Senior Health Documentation Technician（Senior Health Documentation Technician）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3322 – Senior Health Documentation Technician。**

Senior health documentation technicians manage medical records, code diagnoses and procedures, ensure data accuracy and confidentiality, and support healthcare information systems.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Senior Health Documentation Technicians?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about 22,000-28,000 EUR, mid-level 28,000-35,000, and senior up to 45,000 EUR; public sector offers lower pay but stable benefits.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Via work contract visa (Cuenta ajena) or EU Blue Card. Must secure a job offer; qualifications may require homologación. The occupation is listed as shortage, facilitating the process.

**问：What education is required to become a Senior Health Documentation Technician?**
答：Typically a 2-year advanced vocational training (FP Grado Superior) or a 4-year university degree in health information management; Spanish B2 is required.
