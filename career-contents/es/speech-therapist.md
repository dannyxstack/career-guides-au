# Speech Therapist — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 2154 (CNO)**

Speech therapists assess, diagnose, and treat speech, language, communication, and swallowing disorders, serving people of all ages, including developmental delays in children and post-stroke rehabilitation in adults.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. With the intensifying aging population in Spain and growing attention to children's language development, the demand for speech therapists continues to grow. The public healthcare system offers stable recruitment but fierce competition for positions; There are many opportunities in private clinics and schools, especially in major cities like Madrid and Barcelona.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI has an overall amplifying effect on speech-language pathologists' work. While automating documentation and basic assessment tasks, it greatly enhances diagnosis, personalized treatment, and remote service capabilities, making career prospects positive.

Junior speech therapists can work in clinical positions in general hospitals, rehabilitation centers, or schools; After gaining experience, you can specialize in a specific field (such as neurorehabilitation, children's language), or be promoted to department supervisor or open a private clinic; It can also be turned into academic research or teaching.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated speech recognition and natural language processing for initial screening and standardized test scoring
- AI generates basic treatment plans and exercise materials
- Automatic transcription and document filling of electronic health records
- Automatic detection of speech disorder features through voice analysis
- Basic interactive Q&A in remote therapy is handled by chatbots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis: analyzing large amounts of voice data to identify subtle patterns
- Personalised treatment: using machine learning to optimise treatment plans and provide real-time feedback
- Telemedicine: expanding service scope through AI-driven platforms.
- Effect tracking: automatically analyzes patient progress data and adjusts plans
- Research acceleration: AI processes large-scale corpora to discover new treatments

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and empathetic communication for complex cases
- Interdisciplinary team collaboration and patient education
- Design and implement alternative communication systems (e.g., AAC)
- Handling ethical and legal issues (e.g., informed consent)
- Humanistic care and therapeutic relationship building.

**Skills to build:**

- Use and interpretation of AI-assisted diagnostic tools
- Data analysis and statistics (Python/R)
- Remote therapy platform operation and optimization
- Natural language processing basics
- Human-computer interaction design (for therapeutic tools)
- Agility for continuous learning and adapting to new technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- ageing population
- early intervention
- teletherapy
- neurorehabilitation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for speech therapists in Spain?**
A: Entry-level speech therapist pre-tax annual salary about 24,000-30,000 EUR, mid-level about 30,000-38,000 EUR, senior up to 50,000 EUR. Private clinics and senior experts earn more.

**Q: Can third-country speech therapists immigrate to Spain through skilled migration?**
A: Yes, but you must first complete the homologación (academic qualification verification) before applying for an EU Blue Card or ordinary work residence. The credential verification process is complex and may take about one year.

**Q: What certifications do Spanish speech therapists need?**
A: You must hold a Spanish recognized bachelor's degree in speech therapy (Grado en Logopedia) and be recognized by academic credentials (such as foreign qualifications). No additional national exams are required to practice.
