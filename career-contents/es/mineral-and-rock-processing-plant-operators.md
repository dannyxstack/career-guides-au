# Mineral and Rock Processing Plant Operators — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 8112 (CNO)**

This profession is responsible for operating equipment such as crushers, mills, and screeners in mining and quarrying plants, processing ore and rock into aggregates or concentrates of various specifications. Work environments are mostly outdoors or in factory workshops, requiring physical strength, mechanical knowledge, and safety awareness.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Usually, they start as assistants and, after gaining experience, can be promoted to equipment operation team leader or plant director. You can also transition to mechanical maintenance or quality control positions by participating in FP (vocational training). Some personnel enter mine management or technical sales.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual scheduled inspections of equipment status are replaced by sensors and automatic monitoring systems
- Manually adjust crusher and mill parameters, automatically adjusted by the AI optimization system
- Production data is recorded on paper and automatically collected by the digital management system
- Physical troubleshooting of simple faults is identified and warned by predictive maintenance AI

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Remote monitoring of multiple device operations through digital twins and real-time data dashboards
- AI analyzes ore characteristics and automatically optimizes crushing and grinding parameters
- Use AR headset receiver maintenance guidance to improve fault handling efficiency
- Coordinate transportation and processing processes with automated scheduling systems

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment capability to handle sudden mechanical failures and abnormal working conditions
- An empirical intuition about equipment wear and material properties
- Cross-device collaboration and emergency risk avoidance decision-making capabilities
- Communication and coordination skills with maintenance teams and geological engineers

**Skills to build:**

- Basic operation of industrial automation systems (PLC/SCADA).
- Data analysis and visualization (e.g., Tableau, Power BI)
- Maintain basic knowledge of sensors and IoT devices
- Use of predictive maintenance software (e.g., IBM Maximo)
- Basic programming (Python for data recording and analysis)
- Safety Production and Digital Security Standards

**Key growth areas:**

- Mining automation
- Construction materials demand
- Safety regulations
- Quarry operations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for this profession in Spain?**
A: Beginners earn about 19,000-24,000 euros, experienced players can earn up to 40,000 euros, but most pay between 25,000 and 30,000 euros.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this profession?**
A: It is quite challenging; this occupation is not listed as a shortage occupation and requires employer certification that it cannot recruit local workers. The skilled migration pathway (such as the EU Blue Card) usually requires a bachelor's degree or above, and the occupation is mainly for skilled workers, which does not meet the requirements.
