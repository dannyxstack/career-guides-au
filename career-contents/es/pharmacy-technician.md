# Pharmacy Technician — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 5621 (CNO)**

Under the guidance of pharmacists, pharmacy technicians are responsible for drug dispensing, inventory management, and customer consultation, serving as important auxiliary personnel in community and hospital pharmacies.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

You can advance to pharmacist status through further study with FP Grado Superior or a university degree, or move into hospital pharmacy, pharmaceutical industry QA, or drug procurement positions. Gaining experience can lead to promotion to pharmacy manager or technical supervisor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic drug inventory counting and replenishment alerts
- Standardized prescription review and drug interaction testing
- AI customer service answers for routine customer inquiries (such as usage and usage).
- Handling and classification of electronic prescriptions
- Automatic generation of pharmaceutical labels and packaging

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted drug interaction early warning and dosage calculation
- Use data analytics to optimize drug inventory and procurement planning
- AI-driven tracking and reminders of customer medication adherence
- Automated document management frees up more patient communication time
- AI assists in recording and sharing medication records in telemedicine

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment in complex drug formulation and dose adjustments
- Empathy in patient medication education and personalized counseling
- Comply with local pharmaceutical regulations and quality control responsibilities
- Professional communication in collaboration with pharmacists and doctors
- Rapid response and error handling in emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI drug management software (such as PillPack, TruMed).
- Fundamentals of Data Analysis (Excel, Tableau)
- Advanced use of electronic health record (EHR) systems
- Doctor-patient communication and customer service skills
- Basic pharmacology and prescription review knowledge
- Cross-departmental collaboration and project management

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Community pharmacy expansion
- Hospital pharmacy
- Pharmaceutical logistics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmacy technicians in Spain?**
A: Pre-tax annual salary is about 18,000-25,000 euros, with entry-level salaries lower; experienced players can earn up to 28,000 euros, and supervisors earn over 30,000 euros.

**Q: How can foreigners become pharmacy technicians in Spain?**
A: You need to complete Spain's FP Grado Medio or complete academic certification (homologación), then apply for an employer-sponsored work visa (Cuenta ajena). Non-EU citizens face greater challenges due to the need for labor market testing.
