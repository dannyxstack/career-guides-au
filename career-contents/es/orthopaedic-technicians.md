# Orthopaedic Technicians（Orthopaedic Technicians）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3315 – Orthopaedic Technicians。**

Orthopaedic technicians design, fabricate, fit, and repair orthoses, prostheses, and other assistive devices to help patients regain mobility. In Spain, this regulated healthcare profession requires higher vocational training (FP Grado Superior) and official certification.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在ES独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 由雇主担保的工作居留，需证明职位无法在欧盟内填补 |
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能专业人员，需满足薪资门槛（约年薪33,908欧元） |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional Visa） | 针对高技能专业人才，流程较快，需学历认证 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for orthopaedic technicians in Spain?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn €18,000-€24,000 annually, mid-level €25,000-€35,000, and senior roles up to €50,000+, depending on location and employer (public/private).

**问：How can third-country nationals migrate to Spain through this profession?**
答：They must first obtain homologation of their foreign qualifications equivalent to FP Grado Superior, then secure a job offer for a work permit (Cuenta ajena) or EU Blue Card. The occupation is on the shortage list, which may facilitate sponsorship.

**问：Is an official license required for this profession in Spain?**
答：Yes, a qualification as Técnico Superior en Ortoprótesis issued by the Ministry of Education is mandatory, along with registration with the regional health authority.
