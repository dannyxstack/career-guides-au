# Non-Regulated Music and Dance Teacher（Non-Regulated Music and Dance Teacher）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：2323 – Non-Regulated Music and Dance Teacher。**

Teaches music or dance in non-formal settings (e.g., private music schools, dance studios, community centers) to children or adults, focusing on skill development and artistic expression.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在ES独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

**竞争度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit (Employed)） | 受雇于西班牙教育机构，需雇主担保，符合劳动力市场测试 |
| Subclass Cuenta propia（Self-Employed Work Permit） | 自雇开设工作室，需商业计划及投资证明，审批严格 |

**PR难度：较高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for non-regulated music and dance teachers in Spain?**
答：Entry-level teachers earn €15,000-22,000 annually, mid-level €22,000-30,000, and senior teachers €30,000-40,000. Many work part-time, so income varies.

**问：How can third-country nationals migrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Usually requires a job offer from a Spanish educational institution for an employed work permit. Self-employment (opening a studio) is possible but more challenging. Degree recognition (Homologación) is optional but beneficial.

**问：What educational background is needed for this occupation?**
答：No mandatory degree, but a bachelor's (Grado) or higher vocational training (FP Grado Superior) in music or dance greatly improves competitiveness.
