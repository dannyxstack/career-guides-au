# Ship Engineers（Ship Engineers）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3151 – Ship Engineers。**

Operate, maintain, and repair ship propulsion systems, machinery, and electrical equipment, ensuring navigation safety and efficiency. Serve as senior technical officers on merchant, fishing, and special-purpose vessels.

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Tarjeta azul UE（EU Blue Card） | 适用于持有本科或同等学历、获得西班牙公司工作合同且薪资达到门槛（2024年约33,908€）的技术人才。轮机员收入通常远超该标准。 |
| Subclass Altamente cualificad（Highly Skilled Professional (Ley 14/2013)） | 面向高级技术人才，公司可快速办理居留。需提供工作合同与学历证明，适合拥有工程师学位或STCW一级证书的轮机长。 |
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 标准工作居留，需西班牙雇主担保，劳工市场测试（紧缺职业可豁免）。适用于持有STCW证书的轮机员。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 毕业生或高技能人才可获得最长1年居留许可在西班牙寻找工作，需提供学位或证书与资金证明。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Ship Engineers？**
- 拥有机械、电气或船舶工程背景的求职者
- 适应长期海上工作、耐得住寂寞且纪律性强
- 希望在国际环境中工作、英语良好并追求高薪者

**谁不适合学Ship Engineers？**
- 无法承受长时间离家（数月航程）或海上颠簸
- 对强体力劳动与高压工作环境敏感

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of marine engineers in Spain? Does it include overtime and allowances?**
答：Junior engineers earn €35,000-50,000 annually, while chief engineers can reach €80,000-120,000. Salaries generally include base pay and sea service allowances (extra daily rates), with overtime paid separately. LNG carriers and large container ships pay higher.

**问：How can third-country marine engineers obtain work and residence in Spain through skilled migration?**
答：Common paths: 1) Secure a job contract from a Spanish shipowner and apply for the EU Blue Card or Highly Skilled Professional visa; 2) Use STCW certificates and sea service records for homologación (qualification recognition) then apply directly; 3) Come through a vocational training program and later undergo homologación. The shortage occupation status exempts labor market testing.

**问：What education and training are required to become a marine engineer in Spain?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in marine engineering (Grado, 4 years) or advanced vocational training (FP Grado Superior, 2 years), plus STCW certification exams to obtain a certificate of competency. All programs are in Spanish; B2 Spanish is recommended.
