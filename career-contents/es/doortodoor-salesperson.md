# Door-to-Door Salesperson — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 5491 (CNO)**

Door-to-door salespeople are responsible for selling products or services directly from customers' homes, such as utility contracts, telecom packages, or household items. Usually paid on a commission, flexible work but unstable income.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 12,000. In Spain, door-to-door sales positions are gradually decreasing, driven by the rise of e-commerce and telemarketing. However, there is still limited demand in the energy and telecommunications sectors. The number of employees is expected to remain stable or slightly decline over the next five years.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Promotion paths are limited; usually, you can become a sales supervisor or regional manager, or move to positions such as telemarketing or retail sales. Some senior salespeople can be self-employed or establish small sales agency companies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial product introductions and quote demonstrations are replaced by chatbots and automated presentation tools
- Customer information and preference collection is being replaced by automated CRM data collection and AI analysis
- Simple contract signing and order processing are completed through electronic contracting and self-service portals
- Routine follow-ups and repeated visits are executed by automated email/SMS marketing systems
- Market intelligence gathering and competitor comparisons are replaced by AI crawlers and real-time reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI customer scoring tools help filter high-intention families and improve visit efficiency
- Mobile sales assistant tools (AR/VR) remotely showcase products, enhancing on-site demonstration effects
- Real-time voice translation and sentiment analysis improve cross-cultural communication
- The personalized recommendation engine pushes discounts based on historical data, increasing conversion rates

**Human moat:**

- Building deep trust and personal relationships, suitable for high-value or customized products
- Handling complex objections and negotiations on site requires flexibility and emotional resonance
- Identify hidden needs and pain points, and propose creative solutions
- Cross-cultural sensitivity and localized communication skills

**Skills to build:**

- Learn to use CRM and sales automation tools (such as Salesforce)
- Master basic data analysis and identify patterns from customer data
- Enhancing remote presentation and virtual sales capabilities (AR/VR tools)
- Learn AI-assisted communication tools (intelligent script recommendations, sentiment analysis)
- Strengthen interpersonal communication and negotiation skills, specializing in complex scenarios

**Key growth areas:**

- declining
- commission-based
- high turnover
- telemarketing competition

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong communication skills and stress resistance
- Those who prefer flexible working hours and commission-based high returns
- Those who want to quickly enter the labor market without a formal degree

**Who is not suited**
- People seeking stable income and job security
- People who are not good at unfamiliar visits or environments with high rejection rates

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for door-to-door salespeople?**
A: The average annual salary for Spanish door-to-door salespeople is about 18,000 euros, but their income mainly comes from commissions, with a relatively low base salary. Outstanding earners can earn over 30,000 euros, but income fluctuates greatly.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
A: It is very difficult because the profession is not considered a high-skilled or scarce occupation. To apply for work residence (Cuenta ajena), you need to prove that no suitable candidate can be found in the EU, and usually only large companies are willing to sponsor you.

**Q: Will door-to-door salespeople be replaced by artificial intelligence?**
A: The risks are higher because telemarketing and online marketing are gradually replacing in-person visits. However, interpersonal negotiation and trust-building still require manual intervention, with a medium probability of complete replacement.
