# Door-to-Door Salesperson（Door-to-Door Salesperson）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：5491 – Door-to-Door Salesperson。**

Door-to-door salespersons sell products or services directly to customers in their homes, such as utility contracts, telecom packages, or household goods. Typically paid on commission with flexible hours but unstable income.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景：差（★½☆☆☆）。** 晋升路径有限，通常可转为销售主管或区域经理，或转至电话销售、零售销售等岗位。部分资深推销员可自雇或成立小型销售代理公司。
**AI替代风险：高（★★★★☆）。**

主要增长方向：

- declining
- commission-based
- high turnover
- telemarketing competition

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work permit for employees） | 雇主担保的工作居留，但申请难度大，因该职业非高技能且非紧缺。 |
| Subclass Autónomo（Self-employed visa） | 自雇工作签证，需提供商业计划和充足资金，获批率较低。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Door-to-Door Salesperson？**
- 具有较强沟通能力和抗压能力的人
- 偏好弹性工作时间和佣金制高回报的人
- 希望快速进入劳动力市场无需学历的人

**谁不适合学Door-to-Door Salesperson？**
- 追求稳定收入和职业保障的人
- 不擅长陌生拜访或拒绝率高的环境的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for door-to-door salespersons?**
答：In Spain, the average annual salary for door-to-door salespersons is around €18,000, but income heavily relies on commissions. Top performers can earn over €30,000, but income is volatile.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult, as it is not a high-skilled or shortage occupation. Applying for a work visa requires proving no suitable EU candidate, and usually only large companies sponsor.

**问：Will door-to-door salespersons be replaced by AI?**
答：High risk, as telemarketing and online marketing are replacing door-to-door visits. However, human negotiation and trust-building remain valuable, so full replacement is moderate.
