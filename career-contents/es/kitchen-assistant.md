# Kitchen Assistant（Kitchen Assistant）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9310 – Kitchen Assistant。**

Kitchen assistants support chefs in food preparation, cleaning, and basic cooking tasks in restaurants, hotels, and other food service establishments.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Contract (Cuenta Ajena)） | 需西班牙雇主提供工作合同，并证明职位无法由本地人填补，通常适用于高技能岗位，但厨房助手难以符合条件。 |
| Subclass Busqueda empleo（Job Search Visa） | 针对高学历或高技能人才，厨房助手不适用。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Kitchen Assistant？**
- 喜欢团队合作和快节奏工作环境的人
- 对餐饮业有兴趣，愿意从基础做起的人
- 不介意体力劳动和轮班制的人

**谁不适合学Kitchen Assistant？**
- 希望高薪或快速晋升的人
- 无法适应高温、潮湿和高压厨房环境的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a kitchen assistant?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €16,000 to €20,000, mid-level €20,000-€25,000, and senior supervisor up to €30,000. Salary varies by region and restaurant type.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain as a kitchen assistant?**
答：Very difficult. This is a low-skilled job and typically does not qualify for work visas. EU citizens can work freely, but third-country nationals have almost no chance of obtaining residency through this occupation.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities for a kitchen assistant?**
答：With experience, you can advance to chef assistant, second chef, or become a professional chef through vocational training. Some also move into food service management.
