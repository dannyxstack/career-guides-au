# Wholesale Trade Managers And Directors（Wholesale Trade Managers And Directors）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：1431 – Wholesale Trade Managers And Directors。**

Plan, direct and coordinate the operations and strategy of wholesale companies, including purchasing, warehousing, sales and customer relationship management.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for wholesale trade managers?**
答：Entry-level around €28,000-€36,000/year, mid-level €36,000-€50,000/year, senior can exceed €80,000/year.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa as a wholesale trade manager in Spain?**
答：Apply for a Cuenta ajena work permit with employer sponsorship, or meet salary/education requirements for EU Blue Card or Highly Qualified Professional visa.

**问：What is the career path for a wholesale trade manager?**
答：Promote from department manager to regional director or CEO, or start own business. MBA or cross-border trade experience fast-tracks promotion.
