# Mechanical Engineering Technician（Mechanical Engineering Technician）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3126 – Mechanical Engineering Technician。**

Mechanical engineering technicians assist in designing, testing, and manufacturing machinery, equipment, and engines. They work in industrial, manufacturing, and engineering sectors to ensure mechanical systems function efficiently.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Mechanical Engineering Technician？**
- 喜欢动手操作、解决机械问题的技术人员
- 具备机械原理基础、有意从事工业制造者

**谁不适合学Mechanical Engineering Technician？**
- 不喜欢体力劳动或车间环境的人
- 期望快速晋升至管理岗位者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mechanical technicians in Spain?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €22,000 to €28,000 gross per year, mid-level up to €36,000, and senior or management roles up to €48,000. Lower than engineers but stable.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to Spain through this occupation?**
答：Apply for EU Blue Card (requires higher salary) or work contract residence. Advanced vocational training or bachelor's degree is common.

**问：What education is needed to become a mechanical technician?**
答：Common paths are 2-year advanced vocational training (FP Grado Superior) or 4-year bachelor's in mechanical engineering. FP focuses on practice.
