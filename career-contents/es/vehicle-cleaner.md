# Vehicle Cleaner（Vehicle Cleaner）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：9222 – Vehicle Cleaner。**

Vehicle cleaners wash, clean, and detail the interior and exterior of various vehicles (e.g., cars, trucks, buses), including body washing, vacuuming, waxing, and sanitizing. The occupation has stable demand at car rentals, used car dealers, car washes, and auto repair shops.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在ES技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

**竞争度：高（★★★★☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★½☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a vehicle cleaner?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from €16,000 to €30,000 in Spain. Entry-level positions are near the minimum wage, while senior or supervisor roles can reach €25,000-€30,000. Overtime can increase earnings.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to Spain as a vehicle cleaner?**
答：Very difficult. This occupation is not on the shortage list, and the labor market test prioritizes EU citizens. Work visa approval for third-country nationals is unlikely unless there is exceptional employer support.

**问：What certifications or training are required?**
答：Generally no formal certification is required; on-the-job training is provided. Some positions require a B driver's license. Voluntary FP courses in auto detailing (1-2 years, €500-€2,000) can be advantageous for senior roles.
