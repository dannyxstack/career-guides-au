# Vehicle Cleaner — career analysis · Spain

**Occupation code: 9222 (CNO)**

Vehicle cleaners are responsible for washing, cleaning, and maintaining the interior and exterior of various vehicles (such as cars, trucks, buses), including body cleaning, internal vacuuming, waxing, and disinfection. This profession is in stable demand at places such as car rentals, used car dealerships, car washes, and auto repair shops.

Junior vehicle cleaners can be promoted to senior cleaners or team leaders through accumulated experience, while some may move on to higher-skilled roles such as vehicle detailing, painting, or repair. A few people can start their own businesses to open car wash shops or mobile cleaning services.

- Tire cleaning: Automated cleaning device specifically for wheel hubs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is the salary for vehicle cleaners?**
A: In Spain, vehicle cleaners typically earn between 16,000 and 30,000 euros before tax, with entry-level positions close to the minimum wage, while senior or supervisor positions can earn 25,000-30,000 euros. Salary levels are lower, but overtime pay can increase income.

**Q: Can foreigners immigrate to Spain through vehicle cleaners?**
A: It's very difficult. This occupation is not considered a shortage occupation in Spain and has low skill requirements; labor market tests prioritize EU citizens. The likelihood of third-country nationals being granted a work visa is very low, unless there is a special relationship or special support from the employer.

**Q: What certificates or training are required?**
A: Usually, no formal certificate is required; employers provide on-the-job training. Some positions require a Class B driver's license, and some advanced cleaning jobs may voluntarily take automotive detailing-related FP courses (about 1-2 years, costing 500-2000 euros).
