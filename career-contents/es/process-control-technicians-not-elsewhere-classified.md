# Process Control Technicians Not Elsewhere Classified（Process Control Technicians Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · ES

**职业代码：3139 – Process Control Technicians Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Process control technicians monitor and optimize industrial processes, ensuring production meets quality, safety, and efficiency standards. They operate control panels and adjust parameters in manufacturing, chemical, energy, and other sectors.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Cuenta ajena（Work Permit for Employees） | 受雇工作居留，需西班牙公司提供工作合同并证明无法在欧盟内找到合适人选。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for process control technicians?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €22,000-€28,000, mid-level €28,000-€36,000, and senior up to €48,000, depending on industry, region, and experience.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to Spain for this occupation?**
答：They can apply for a work permit for employees (Cuenta ajena) with a job offer, or a Highly Qualified Professional visa or EU Blue Card requiring a degree and meeting salary thresholds.
