# Meat Packer — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 832114 (ANZSCO)**

A meat packer works in abattoirs or meat processing plants, weighing, packing, labelling and boxing fresh or processed meat products. The role typically involves physical labour and meticulous attention to detail to ensure products meet hygiene and quality standards.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Entry-level meat packers can advance to packaging line supervisor or quality inspector, or move into meat inspection, cold chain logistics and other roles through training. Experienced workers can also enter processing plant management, but advancement opportunities are limited.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automated weighing and packaging systems to weigh, seal, and box fresh meat.
- AI visual inspection system replaces manual packaging label checking and appearance quality inspection
- Robot-guided automatic sorting and palletizing replace manual handling of heavy objects.
- AI automatically updates packaging material consumption and replenishment in inventory management systems.
- Automated temperature monitoring and sanitation record-keeping reduces manual log entry.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI to predict production volume and packaging needs, optimizing scheduling and material preparation.
- Access packaging specifications and equipment maintenance guidance through AR smart glasses.
- AI data analysis assists in improving packaging processes, reducing waste
- Inspecting Cold Storage and Production Lines with Drones or AI Vision to Enhance Safety Monitoring

**Human moat:**

- Ability to handle complex health compliance and emergency troubleshooting
- Fine hand-packaging of specially cut meat products (e.g., shaped meat cuts)
- Communicate with regulatory authorities, handle temporary audits and traceability responsibilities
- Team coordination and training new staff to adapt to automated workflows

**Skills to build:**

- Basic mechanical maintenance and automated equipment operation
- Application of AI-assisted quality inspection software
- Data analysis and Excel skills for monitoring packaging efficiency metrics
- Knowledge of food safety regulations (HACCP/ISO)
- Problem-solving and emergency response skills
- Basic understanding of cold storage warehouse robotic systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Manual Labour
- Food Processing
- Export Oriented
- Regional Employment

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for meat packers?**
A: Entry-level hourly rate approx. AUD 23-28, annual income approx. AUD 45,000-55,000; senior roles can reach AUD 65,000-75,000. Overtime and shift allowances are common.

**Q: Are there immigration opportunities for meat packers?**
A: This occupation is usually not on the regular skilled migration list, but can be applied via employer sponsorship (482/494) or regional DAMA programs. Must meet relevant labor agreement requirements.
