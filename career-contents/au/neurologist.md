# Neurologist（Neurologist）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：253318 – Neurologist。**

Neurologists are medical specialists who diagnose and treat disorders of the nervous system, including stroke, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis. They work in hospitals, specialized clinics, and academic settings, often collaborating with neurosurgeons and other healthcare professionals.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：How much do neurologists earn?**
答：Neurologists in Australia earn well, with entry-level salaries around AUD 150,000-200,000 and experienced specialists up to AUD 300,000-500,000. Private practice can yield higher income but involves overhead costs.

**问：Is it easy for neurologists to migrate to Australia?**
答：Neurologists are in shortage and on the MLTSSL, eligible for various visas like 189, 190, 482, 186. You need skills assessment and English test, but with sufficient quotas, it is relatively straightforward.

**问：What are the career prospects for neurologists in Australia?**
答：Excellent prospects due to aging population and rising neurological disorders. Telehealth expands opportunities in rural areas, ensuring stable demand and high income.
