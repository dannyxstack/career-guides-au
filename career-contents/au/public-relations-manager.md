# Public Relations Manager（Public Relations Manager）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：131114 – Public Relations Manager。**

Public Relations Managers plan, direct and coordinate communication between an organisation and the public, managing media relations, brand image and crisis communications to maintain and enhance organisational reputation.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 针对在MLTSSL列表上的职业，可独立申请永久居留，无需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 需获得州政府担保，在州内居住和工作，可获额外5分加分。 |
| Subclass 482（Skills in Demand Visa） | 临时技术短缺签证，需雇主担保，工作满3年可转186永居。 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永久居留签证，需满足职业评估和语言要求。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Public Relations Manager？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Public Relations Manager in Australia?**
答：The average salary for a PR Manager in Australia ranges from AUD 100,000 to 130,000, with entry-level around AUD 60,000-80,000 and senior roles up to AUD 140,000-200,000, depending on city and company size.

**问：Can a Public Relations Manager migrate to Australia via skilled migration?**
答：Yes. PR Managers are on the MLTSSL, eligible for 189, 190, 482 visas, requiring a Vetassess skills assessment.

**问：What are the career prospects for a PR Manager?**
答：PR Managers can advance to Director of Communications or CMO, or move into consulting and entrepreneurship, with growing opportunities in digital PR and social media.
