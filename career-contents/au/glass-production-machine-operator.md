# Glass Production Machine Operator（Glass Production Machine Operator）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：711113 – Glass Production Machine Operator。**

Operates glass production machinery, including batching, melting, forming, and annealing, to produce glass products to specifications.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for glass production machine operators?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around AUD 50,000-60,000, mid-level 60,000-70,000, and senior up to 85,000. Overtime and shift allowances can increase income.

**问：Are there skilled migration opportunities for this occupation?**
答：Almost none. This occupation is not on the skilled occupation list, and only possible through employer-sponsored temporary visa (482) with high requirements.

**问：How to advance from entry-level operator?**
答：With experience, you can be promoted to senior operator or team leader, or transition to quality control or maintenance roles by learning new skills.
