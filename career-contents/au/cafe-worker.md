# Cafe Worker（Cafe Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：431211 – Cafe Worker。**

Cafe workers take orders, prepare beverages and food, handle payments, and maintain a clean dining area, forming the backbone of Australia's hospitality industry.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage） | 仅适用于高端咖啡师或管理岗，普通服务员不符合 |
| Subclass DAMA（Designated Area Migration Agreement） | 部分偏远地区DAMA可能涵盖，但极少见 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average hourly wage for a cafe worker?**
答：Under the Fair Work Act, the minimum hourly rate is around AUD 24-28 (including casual loading), with actual pay reaching AUD 25-35.

**问：Can a barista apply for skilled migration?**
答：General cafe workers are not on the skilled occupation list and cannot apply for 189/190/482 visas. High-end baristas with managerial or specialized skills may be eligible via DAMA or employer sponsorship (482), but it is very restrictive.

**问：How to advance from cafe worker to manager?**
答：Typically requires 2-3 years of experience, completion of management training courses (e.g., coffee shop operations), and demonstrated leadership. Some employers offer internal promotion pathways.
