# Project Builder（Project Builder）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：133112 – Project Builder。**

Project Builders plan, manage and oversee construction projects from concept to completion, ensuring they meet budget, timeline and quality requirements. They coordinate contractors, designers and clients, and handle permits, compliance and site safety.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 需雇主担保，职业在CSOL上（如项目经理） |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居，需通过技能评估 |
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 若职业在MLTSSL上，可通过打分系统申请 |

**PR难度：中（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Project Builders in Australia?**
答：Entry-level: AUD 75k-90k, Mid-level: AUD 100k-130k, Senior: AUD 140k-180k. Experience and certifications matter.

**问：Which skilled migration visas can a Project Builder apply for?**
答：Typical options include the 482 Temporary Skill Shortage visa, 186 Employer Nomination Scheme, and possibly the 189 Skilled Independent visa if the occupation is on the MLTSSL.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a Project Builder?**
答：A bachelor's degree in construction management or a related field, plus a White Card. Project management certification (e.g., AIPM's CPP) is beneficial.
