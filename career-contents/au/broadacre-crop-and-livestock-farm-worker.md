# Broadacre Crop and Livestock Farm Worker（Broadacre Crop and Livestock Farm Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：842411 – Broadacre Crop and Livestock Farm Worker。**

Performs manual tasks such as planting, harvesting, feeding livestock, and irrigation on broadacre crop and livestock farms, supporting Australia's agricultural sector.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for farm workers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about AUD 45,000-55,000, mid-level AUD 55,000-65,000, and senior supervisors up to AUD 80,000; however, work is seasonal and income varies.

**问：Which visas can farm workers use to migrate to Australia?**
答：Mainly through the 482 employer-sponsored visa, 494 regional sponsored visa, and 403 seasonal worker visa, with some pathways to permanent residency.

**问：What education or training is required to become a farm worker?**
答：Most positions do not require formal education, but a Certificate III in Agriculture and relevant licenses can improve employability.
