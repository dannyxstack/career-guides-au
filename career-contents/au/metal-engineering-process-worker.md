# Metal Engineering Process Worker（Metal Engineering Process Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：839111 – Metal Engineering Process Worker。**

Metal engineering process workers operate and monitor metalworking machinery for cutting, shaping, assembling, and welding, ensuring smooth production in manufacturing and construction.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage） | 需雇主担保，但该职业通常不在短期职业名单上，申请难度大 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)） | 偏远地区州担保，但需在CSOL上，该职业通常不包含 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for metal engineering process workers?**
答：Entry-level workers earn AUD 45,000-55,000 annually; experienced workers up to AUD 65,000-75,000, with overtime potential.

**问：Is migration to Australia possible for this occupation?**
答：Rare. This occupation is typically not on skilled occupation lists, and employer sponsorship is difficult, except under some DAMA agreements.

**问：How can one advance from a metal engineering process worker?**
答：By obtaining higher certifications (e.g., Certificate IV) or completing apprenticeships, one can advance to production supervisor, quality inspector, or specialized welder.
