# Respiratory Technician（Respiratory Technician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：311217 – Respiratory Technician。**

Respiratory Technicians assist in diagnosing and treating respiratory disorders, operating pulmonary function testing equipment, managing oxygen therapy and respiratory support devices, and collaborating with doctors in Australian hospitals and clinics.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Respiratory Technician in Australia?**
答：Annual salary ranges from AUD 65,000 to 90,000; senior technicians or those in regional areas can earn over AUD 100,000.

**问：Can a Respiratory Technician migrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on the CSOL, eligible for 482→186 or 190/491 state-nominated visas, requiring relevant diploma and English proficiency.
