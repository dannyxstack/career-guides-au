# Marine Transport Professionals nec — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 231299 (ANZSCO)**

Maritime professionals handle ship operations, cargo management, port operations, and marine logistics coordination to ensure safe and efficient shipping. This career covers crew management, route planning, and regulatory compliance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry-level positions such as deck crew, progressing to senior officers (Chief Mate, Captain) or shore-based management roles (Port Manager, Operations Director). Requires accumulating sea experience and obtaining advanced certificates.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine voyage data recording and report generation
- Basic meteorological route planning and optimization
- Marine machinery condition monitoring and early warning
- Cargo loading calculation and balance adjustment
- Communication relay and standard message forwarding

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted navigation decisions to improve flight safety and fuel efficiency
- Real-time weather and sea condition forecasting, supporting dynamic adjustment of speed and route
- Automated fault diagnosis and predictive maintenance to reduce downtime
- Intelligent Cargo Monitoring Systems to Improve Stowage Accuracy and Safety
- Augmented reality (AR) assisted navigation at night and in adverse weather

**Human moat:**

- Ship handling and emergency response capabilities in crisis situations
- Team management and crew leadership (especially captain duties)
- Special cargo handling and complex port operations experience
- Regulatory compliance and maritime safety inspection responsibilities
- Flexible judgment in unpredictable marine environments

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI navigation and decision support systems
- Data analysis and interpretation of predictive models
- Remote operation and automated system monitoring
- Cybersecurity basics (ship system protection)
- STCW certification renewal (including E-navigation module)
- Cross-cultural communication and team management

**Key growth areas:**

- Maritime Logistics
- Port Management
- Offshore Energy
- Coastal Shipping

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for maritime professionals?**
A: Salary varies by position and experience: entry-level around AUD 55,000-75,000; mid-level AUD 80,000-110,000; senior captain or manager can reach AUD 120,000-180,000. Sea-going positions often include allowances.

**Q: Can maritime professionals immigrate to Australia?**
A: Yes. This occupation is on the CSOL and can be used for migration via the 482 employer sponsorship, 186 permanent residence, 190 state nomination, or 491 regional nomination visa. It requires a Vetassess assessment and meeting English language requirements.

**Q: What qualifications are required?**
A: Usually requires a maritime-related degree (4 years) or diploma (2 years), and hold an AMSA certificate of competency. Shore-based management roles may accept relevant field degrees.
