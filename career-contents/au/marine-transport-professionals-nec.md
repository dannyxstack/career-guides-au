# Marine Transport Professionals nec（Marine Transport Professionals nec）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：231299 – Marine Transport Professionals nec。**

Marine transport professionals manage ship operations, cargo logistics, port coordination, and regulatory compliance to ensure safe and efficient maritime transport.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for marine transport professionals?**
答：Salaries vary: entry-level AUD 55k-75k, mid-level AUD 80k-110k, senior (captain/manager) AUD 120k-180k. Sea-going roles include allowances.

**问：Can marine transport professionals immigrate to Australia?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on CSOL, eligible for 482, 186, 190, 491 visas. Requires Vetassess skills assessment and English proficiency.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically a maritime bachelor degree (4 years) or diploma (2 years) plus AMSA certificate of competency. Shore-based roles may accept related degrees.
