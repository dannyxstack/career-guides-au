# Tree Worker（Tree Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：362512 – Tree Worker。**

Tree workers prune, remove, and maintain trees to ensure safety and health. They use specialized tools and equipment, working in residential, commercial, and public spaces.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 适用于雇主担保的技术工人，需有雇主提名。树木工人在短期职业列表上。可后续转186。 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居签证，需通过技能评估并满足英文要求。树木工人符合条件。 |
| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 偏远地区雇主担保签证，后可转191永居。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for tree workers?**
答：Entry level: AUD 55,000-70,000; mid-level: AUD 70,000-90,000; senior: AUD 90,000-120,000. Self-employed or supervisors earn more.

**问：How can tree workers migrate to Australia?**
答：Via employer sponsorship (482 or 186 visa). Need relevant qualifications (e.g., Cert III) and skills assessment. Tree worker is on the short-term list.
