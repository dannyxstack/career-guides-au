# Stonemason — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 331112 (ANZSCO)**

Stonemasons cut, shape, and install natural or artificial stone for building structures, monuments, and decorative projects, requiring precise manual skills and expertise.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Stonemasons can progress from apprentice to journeyman, then to foreman, project manager, or self-employment. With experience, they can specialise in carving, restoration, or commercial stone installation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use CAD software for stone cutting and layout design
- Operating automatic stone cutting machines and CNC engraving machines
- Material quantity calculation and layout using BIM models
- Use robots for repetitive stone handling and laying

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using 3D scanning and digital twins for precise stone structure installation
- Use AR glasses to overlay design drawings on construction sites, reducing errors
- Using AI to optimize stone splicing plans, improving aesthetics and strength
- Conduct drone inspections of large stone structures and remotely monitor maintenance needs.

**Human moat:**

- Artistic judgment for complex stone carving and hand modeling
- Adaptive skills for integrated on-site stone and structure installation
- Empirical judgment of stone texture, color, and physical properties
- Ability to communicate customized design solutions with clients

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/BIM software operation (e.g., AutoCAD, Revit)
- CNC stone processing equipment programming and operation
- 3D scanning and digital modeling skills
- Engineering surveying and precision installation technology
- Drone operation and image analysis basics

**Key growth areas:**

- Strong infrastructure investment
- Heritage restoration demand
- Residential construction boom
- Skilled labour shortage

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on and physical work
- People with an artistic interest in construction and stone
- People willing to work outdoors and in various weather conditions

**Who is not suited**
- People who cannot endure long periods of physical labor
- Those sensitive to dust and noise

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How are the salary levels for stonemasons?**
A: Entry-level approx. AUD $50,000-65,000; mid-level AUD $70,000-90,000; senior AUD $95,000-120,000; overtime and project bonuses can add extra.

**Q: How can stonemasons migrate to Australia?**
A: Eligible via 189/190 skilled migration (if on MLTSSL/STSOL), or 482/186 employer sponsorship. Requires skills assessment by Vetassess.

**Q: What are the career prospects for a stonemason?**
A: Driven by construction and infrastructure investment, demand for stonemasons is stable, especially for restoration and high-end projects, with expected growth over the next five years.
