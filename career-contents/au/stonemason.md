# Stonemason（Stonemason）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：331112 – Stonemason。**

Stonemasons cut, shape, and install natural or artificial stone for buildings, monuments, and decorative projects, requiring precise manual skills and technical knowledge.

---

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 独立技术移民，需在MLTSSL清单上，通过技能评估和EOI打分 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保技术移民，需获得州政府提名，加分容易 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，需有雇主担保，可转186永居 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居签证，需通过雇主提名和技能评估 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Stonemason？**
- 喜欢动手操作和体力工作的人
- 对建筑和石材有艺术兴趣的人
- 愿意在户外和不同天气条件下工作的人

**谁不适合学Stonemason？**
- 不能承受长时间体力劳动的人
- 对粉尘和噪音敏感的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a stonemason?**
答：Entry-level around AUD 50k-65k, mid-level AUD 70k-90k, senior AUD 95k-120k, with overtime and bonuses.

**问：How can a stonemason migrate to Australia?**
答：Via 189/190 skilled migration (if on MLTSSL/STSOL) or 482/186 employer sponsorship. Requires skills assessment by Vetassess.

**问：What is the job outlook for stonemasons?**
答：Steady demand driven by construction and infrastructure investment, especially in restoration and high-end projects, with growth expected over the next five years.
