# Pharmacy Technician（Pharmacy Technician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：311215 – Pharmacy Technician。**

Pharmacy technicians assist registered pharmacists in dispensing medications, managing inventory, processing prescriptions, and providing customer service, playing a vital support role in pharmacy teams.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary ceiling for pharmacy technicians?**
答：Senior pharmacy technicians earn around AUD 75,000 per year (including superannuation), typically requiring many years of experience or supervisory roles.

**问：Can pharmacy technicians migrate to Australia?**
答：This occupation is typically not on the skilled occupation list, with very limited migration pathways, mainly through employer sponsorship (e.g., 482) or regional DAMA.

**问：How to become a pharmacist from a pharmacy technician?**
答：You need to complete an accredited Bachelor of Pharmacy (typically 4 years) and pass the registration exam of the Australian Pharmacy Council to become a registered pharmacist.
