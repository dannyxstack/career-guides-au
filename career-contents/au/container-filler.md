# Container Filler（Container Filler）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：832112 – Container Filler。**

Container fillers load goods into shipping containers safely and efficiently, ensuring proper securing and weight distribution for transport.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 此职业通常不符合482签证职业清单要求。 |
| Subclass 187（Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme） | 偏远地区雇主担保，但仅限高级主管等特殊情况，一般不可用。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for container fillers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around A$45,000-55,000, mid-level A$55,000-65,000, and senior/supervisor A$65,000-75,000. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**问：Are there skilled migration pathways for container fillers?**
答：Virtually none. The occupation is not on the skilled occupation list, so it does not qualify for visas like 189, 190, or 482. Transition to a higher role such as warehouse supervisor may help.

**问：Will this occupation be automated?**
答：There is some risk. Automated container handling and robotics are advancing, but many complex tasks still require human labor. Some roles may decline within the next decade.
