# Fruit Farm Worker（Fruit Farm Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：842212 – Fruit Farm Worker。**

Fruit farm workers pick, grade, and pack fruit, and perform field tasks like pruning and fertilizing. This seasonal job requires physical stamina.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 417（Working Holiday Visa） | 打工度假签证，允许短期工作，但不能转为永久居留。 |
| Subclass 462（Work and Holiday Visa） | 类似417，适用于特定国家公民。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for fruit farm workers?**
答：Average annual salary is around AUD 50,000, but work is seasonal. Hourly rate is about AUD 25-30.

**问：Can fruit farm workers immigrate to Australia?**
答：Almost no pathway. The occupation is not on the skilled occupation list; work visa (e.g. 482) requires two years experience and employer sponsorship, but is very restrictive. Mainly suitable for working holiday visas.

**问：Is demand high for this occupation in Australia?**
答：Seasonal demand is high, especially during harvest. Long-term demand is unstable, affected by backpacker and international worker supply.
