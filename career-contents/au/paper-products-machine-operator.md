# Paper Products Machine Operator（Paper Products Machine Operator）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：711311 – Paper Products Machine Operator。**

Paper Products Machine Operators set up, operate, and monitor machines that produce paper products such as paper sheets, cardboard, tissues, and packaging materials. They perform routine maintenance, troubleshoot issues, and ensure production quality and efficiency.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 纸制品机器操作员不在CSOL/MLTSSL列表上，通常无法申请雇主担保482签证。 |
| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 仅极少数偏远地区DAMA协议可能涵盖该职业，但通常不适用。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Paper Products Machine Operator？**
- 喜欢动手操作机械设备、不介意重复性工作的人
- 愿意在工厂环境工作，能适应轮班制
- 寻求稳定就业且不追求高薪者

**谁不适合学Paper Products Machine Operator？**
- 希望快速移民澳洲的人（此职业无技术移民途径）
- 厌恶噪音、粉尘和体力劳动的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Paper Products Machine Operator in Australia?**
答：Entry-level operators earn around AUD 45,000-55,000 per year, while experienced operators can earn up to AUD 65,000-75,000. Salaries vary by location, employer, and shift work.

**问：Can a Paper Products Machine Operator migrate to Australia via skilled migration?**
答：No. This occupation is not on any skilled occupation list (CSOL/MLTSSL), so it is ineligible for skilled migration visas like 189, 190, 491, or 482. Only very remote DAMA agreements may cover it, but opportunities are extremely limited.

**问：What are the career prospects for a Paper Products Machine Operator?**
答：Career progression is limited, typically to senior operator or supervisor. Automation may reduce job numbers, but demand from the packaging industry remains stable.
