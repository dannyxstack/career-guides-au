# Meat Boner and Slicer（Meat Boner and Slicer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：831211 – Meat Boner and Slicer。**

Boners and slicers use knives to bone, trim, and slice meat from carcasses (beef, lamb, pork) in red meat processing plants. The job is physically demanding, performed in cold, wet conditions.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Skills in Demand Visa） | 雇主担保工作签证，需获得肉厂担保，职业在CSOL上 |
| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 偏远地区雇主担保，3年后可转191永居 |
| Subclass DAMA（Designated Area Migration Agreement） | 部分偏远地区DAMA提供更宽松的英语和年龄要求 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme Visa） | 直接永居签证，需雇主提名及通过技能评估 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary of a meat boner and slicer?**
答：Entry-level around AUD 48,000-58,000; experienced workers can earn AUD 70,000-85,000. Overtime and piece rates can increase earnings.

**问：How can a meat boner get Australian PR?**
答：Mainly through employer-sponsored visas: 482 Temporary Skill Shortage, 494 Regional Sponsored, or DAMA. Requires TRA skills assessment and usually 2+ years experience.

**问：What is the working environment like?**
答：Work is usually in refrigerated or freezer rooms (0-10°C), standing, using sharp knives. Protective gear is required; repetitive motions can cause injuries.
