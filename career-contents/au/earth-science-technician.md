# Earth Science Technician（Earth Science Technician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：311412 – Earth Science Technician。**

Earth Science Technicians assist geologists and earth scientists with field surveys, sample collection, data analysis, and laboratory testing, providing technical support for mineral exploration, environmental assessment, and engineering geology.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Earth Science Technicians?**
答：Entry-level AUD 55,000-70,000; mid-level AUD 70,000-90,000; senior AUD 90,000-120,000. Mining companies offer additional allowances.

**问：Is it easy to migrate to Australia as an Earth Science Technician?**
答：The occupation is on the CSOL, eligible for 189/190/491/482 visas. Requires VETASSESS assessment and relevant qualifications/experience. Mining states like WA offer advantages.

**问：What are the career progression paths for Earth Science Technicians?**
答：Advance to senior technician or lab supervisor; pursue degree to become geoscientist or geological engineer; transfer to exploration or operations management within mining companies.
