# Earth Science Technician — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 311412 (ANZSCO)**

Geoscience technicians assist geologists and earth scientists in field surveys, sample collection, data analysis, and lab testing, providing technical support for mineral exploration, environmental assessment, and engineering geology.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Typically starts as a junior technician, can advance to senior technician or lab supervisor, or transition to geoscientist or geological engineer through further study. Salary increases significantly with field experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine geological data compilation and report generation
- Simple ore body modeling based on historical data
- Routine reading and recording of automated measurement instruments
- Standardized geological mapping

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted complex geological modeling and resource estimation
- Using machine learning for ore body prediction and grade analysis
- Real-time monitoring data analysis to optimize mining processes
- Multi-source data fusion (remote sensing, geophysics) improves exploration efficiency

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency judgment and decision-making (e.g., slope stability)
- Effective communication and coordination with mining engineers and operators
- Experience in field surveying under complex geological conditions
- Implementation of safety protocols and risk management

**Skills to build:**

- Python or R programming (for geological data analysis)
- Application of machine learning in geological modeling.
- Automated measurement and drone surveying operations
- Advanced analysis in Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Digital mining software (e.g., Surpac, Datamine)
- Data visualization and report automation

**Key growth areas:**

- Mining
- Resource Exploration
- Environmental Monitoring
- Geotechnical Services

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy outdoor work and field investigation
- People interested in geology, environmental science, or mining
- People who are hands-on, detail-oriented, and good at data analysis

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for geoscience technicians?**
A: Entry-level approximately $55,000-$70,000 AUD, mid-level $70,000-$90,000, senior $90,000-$120,000. Mining companies often provide additional allowances, with salaries above average.

**Q: Is it easy to immigrate to Australia as a geoscience technician?**
A: This occupation is on the CSOL list and eligible for 189/190/491/482 visas. However, VETASSESS assessment is required, along with relevant qualifications or work experience. Mining states like Western Australia offer additional advantages.

**Q: What are the career development paths for geoscience technicians?**
A: Can advance to senior technician or lab supervisor, or pursue a degree to become a geoscientist or geological engineer. Internal transfers to exploration or operations management within mining companies are also common.
