# Commissioned Police Officer（Commissioned Police Officer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：139113 – Commissioned Police Officer。**

Commissioned Police Officer (ANZSCO 139113) is a senior-ranked officer in the Australian police force, responsible for leading police teams, managing resources, enforcing laws, and maintaining public safety. This role typically requires extensive policing experience and promotion through rigorous assessment, forming a key management position in the government and public sector.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景：稳定（★★★☆☆）。** 通常从普通警员（Constable）起步，经过多年执勤和内部考试晋升至高级警员（Senior Constable）、警官（Sergeant），进而竞争 Commissioned Officer 职位（如 Inspector, Superintendent）。后续可通过担任地区指挥官、专业部门主管或进入联邦警察高层实现持续发展。
**AI替代风险：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

主要增长方向：

- Public safety
- Law enforcement management
- Community policing
- Counter-terrorism

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 通常不适用于该职业，因为Commissioned Officer属于公共部门内部岗位，一般不向海外申请人开放 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 极少数情况下，州警察机构可能担保高级专家，但通常要求申请人已具备永久居民身份 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Commissioned Police Officer？**
- 具备领导力和决策能力，希望在公共安全领域担任管理职务
- 有多年警务工作经验且通过内部晋升考核的现职警员
- 适应高强度工作环境，愿意应对危机事件

**谁不适合学Commissioned Police Officer？**
- 不打算从基层警员做起或无兴趣从事执法工作
- 偏好低压力、规律作息的白领岗位

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a Commissioned Police Officer?**
答：Entry-level Commissioned Officer (e.g., Inspector) earns AUD 80,000-100,000 per year; mid-level (Superintendent) AUD 110,000-140,000; senior (Assistant Commissioner and above) AUD 150,000-200,000. Salary increases with rank and experience.

**问：Can overseas applicants become a Commissioned Police Officer in Australia through skilled migration?**
答：Almost impossible. This role generally requires Australian citizenship or permanent residency and promotion from within the police force. Overseas migrants cannot directly apply; only very few senior experts may obtain employer sponsorship under special circumstances.

**问：What is the typical career path to become a Commissioned Police Officer?**
答：Typically, one starts as a constable in a state police force, serves for several years, then passes internal exams to become a sergeant, and later competes for commissioned officer positions (e.g., Inspector). The entire process takes about 5-10 years, requiring demonstrated leadership and management potential.
