# Commissioned Police Officer — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 139113 (ANZSCO)**

Commissioned Police Officer (ANZSCO 139113) is a senior police officer in the Australian policing system, responsible for leading police teams, managing resources, enforcing the law, and maintaining public safety. This role typically requires extensive policing experience and passing promotion assessments, serving as a core management role in the government public sector.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

**Who is suited**
- Possesses leadership and decision-making skills, aspiring to take on management roles in public safety

- Able to adapt to high-intensity work environments and willing to handle crisis events.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for a Commissioned Police Officer?**
A: Entry-level Commissioned Officer (e.g., Inspector) salary about 80,000-100,000 AUD, mid-level (Superintendent) about 110,000-140,000 AUD, senior (Assistant Commissioner and above) up to 150,000-200,000 AUD. Salary increases with rank and seniority.

**Q: Can overseas individuals migrate to Australia as a Commissioned Police Officer through skilled migration?**
A: Almost impossible. This position is usually limited to Australian citizens or permanent residents and requires progression from a junior police officer. Overseas migrants cannot apply directly; only a very few senior experts may obtain special arrangements through employer sponsorship.

**Q: What is the typical path to become a Commissioned Police Officer?**
A: typically join a state police force as a regular constable, after several years of service pass internal exams to become a Sergeant, then compete for Commissioned Officer positions (e.g., Inspector). The entire process takes about 5-10 years, requiring demonstration of leadership and management potential.
