# Electorate Officer（Electorate Officer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：224911 – Electorate Officer。**

Electorate officers provide administrative and policy support to elected representatives, manage electorate office operations, handle constituent inquiries, and facilitate community engagement, serving as a key link between government and the public.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 需雇主担保，但该职业通常不在短缺清单，极少适用。 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa (State Sponsored)） | 需州担保，但选区官员极少在技术职业清单上，路径困难。 |
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 职业不在MLTSSL上，无法申请。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for electorate officers?**
答：Entry-level positions earn AUD 55,000-70,000; mid-level AUD 70,000-90,000; senior AUD 90,000-110,000, depending on experience, state, and representative level.

**问：Can electorate officers obtain permanent residence through skilled migration?**
答：Highly unlikely. The occupation is not on the skilled occupation list, making 189/190/491 visas inapplicable, and employer sponsorship is extremely rare.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become an electorate officer?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in political science or public administration, strong communication and organizational skills, and sometimes a security clearance and driver's license.
