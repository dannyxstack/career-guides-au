# Mine Deputy — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 312913 (ANZSCO)**

The mine deputy in Australian underground coal mines supervises mining operations, ensuring compliance with safety regulations and production targets. This role is key to mine management and typically requires extensive underground experience.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Usually progresses from mining operator or worker, obtaining supervisor certification to become assistant supervisor, then can advance to mine manager or safety supervisor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automate generation of routine safety inspection and compliance reports
- Real-time monitoring of environmental parameters like gas and ventilation via sensors with automatic alarms
- Automated dispatching of mining equipment (e.g., autonomous trucks, drills) to reduce manual intervention
- Use AI to analyze historical data to predict equipment failures and schedule preventive maintenance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI integrates multi-source data to provide real-time decision support (e.g., optimal mining paths, risk heat maps)
- Enhance on-site inspection efficiency by overlaying underground maps and hazard tips via AR headsets
- AI-driven training simulator helps assistant supervisors practice emergency handling.
- Automating administrative tasks frees up time to focus on team management and safety culture

**Human moat:**

- On-site Judgment and Emergency Command in Critical Situations (e.g., gas explosion, collapse)
- Building Trust with Miners, Motivating Teams, and Managing Interpersonal Conflicts
- Intuition and experience with abnormal environmental cues (e.g., sensing pressure changes)
- Take legal responsibility and make ethical decisions that cannot be programmed
- Integrated decision-making in complex underground environments with multiple variables and uncertainties

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (e.g., Python, Power BI)
- Automated monitoring system operation and troubleshooting
- AI/Machine Learning fundamentals and model application understanding
- Digital twin and simulation software usage
- Change Management and Digital Leadership
- Cybersecurity fundamentals (protecting OT systems)

**Key growth areas:**

- Safety regulations
- Automation
- Coal mining
- Mine management

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Miners or operators with underground coal mining experience
- People who prioritize safety and can handle high-pressure environments
- Those willing to work in remote mining areas and adapt to shift work

**Who is not suited**
- Those who dislike underground work or confined spaces
- Those unable to adapt to high-intensity physical labor and irregular hours

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a deputy mine supervisor in Australia?**
A: Average annual salary between A$120,000 and A$150,000, including allowances and shift premiums, depending on experience and region.

**Q: Can an assistant mine manager migrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
A: This occupation is not on the MLTSSL or CSOL, and applications are typically only possible through employer-sponsored visas (e.g., 494 or 482), requiring employer support.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a mine deputy?**
A: A university degree is not usually required, but specific training courses and a deputy supervisor certificate must be completed, along with at least 2-3 years of underground work experience.
