# Transport Operations Inspector（Transport Operations Inspector）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：599518 – Transport Operations Inspector。**

Transport Operations Inspectors oversee and inspect public transport and freight systems to ensure compliance with safety regulations and operational standards. They audit driver qualifications, vehicle conditions, and trip records, and investigate incidents.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Transport Operations Inspector?**
答：Entry-level earns AUD 65,000-80,000, mid-level AUD 80,000-100,000, senior AUD 100,000-130,000 per year. Regional areas may offer additional allowances.

**问：What migration pathways are available for Transport Operations Inspectors?**
答：Eligible for state-nominated visas (190/491) if on CSOL. States like NSW and QLD may sponsor. Employer-sponsored 482 visa is also an option.

**问：How to become a Transport Operations Inspector?**
答：Typically requires transport industry experience, completion of a relevant certificate or diploma, and obtaining a state-issued inspector certificate and heavy vehicle license.
