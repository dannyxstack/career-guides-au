# Electronic Engineering Technician（Electronic Engineering Technician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：312412 – Electronic Engineering Technician。**

Electronic Engineering Technicians assist engineers in designing, testing, and maintaining electronic equipment and systems, focusing on practical troubleshooting and implementation in fields like communications, control, and medical devices.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Electronic Engineering Technician？**
- 喜欢动手操作、解决硬件故障，对电子电路有浓厚兴趣的人
- 愿意接受TAFE职业培训，希望快速进入澳洲就业市场的人

**谁不适合学Electronic Engineering Technician？**
- 厌恶枯燥的测试和重复性维修工作的人
- 追求高薪且快速晋升，缺乏耐心积累经验的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for an Electronic Engineering Technician?**
答：Entry-level salary is around AUD 55,000–70,000 per year, mid-level AUD 75,000–95,000, and senior AUD 100,000–130,000. Mining and defence sectors offer higher pay, with regional allowances.

**问：What are the requirements for an Electronic Engineering Technician to migrate to Australia?**
答：You need a positive skill assessment from Engineers Australia (classified as Engineering Technologist or Technician), typically requiring a Diploma and relevant work experience. Options include 190/491 state sponsorship or 482 employer sponsorship.

**问：What education is required to become an Electronic Engineering Technician?**
答：A minimum of a Certificate IV in Electronics (1-2 years) is required, but a Diploma (2 years) is more common. TAFE courses are affordable, with international student fees around AUD 20,000–40,000.
