# Other Wood Processing Machine Operator（Other Wood Processing Machine Operator）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：711314 – Other Wood Processing Machine Operator。**

Operates wood processing machines other than sawing or planing, such as sanders, moulders, and borers, to produce furniture, building components, and other wooden products.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，本职业不在中长期清单，需通过DAMA或特殊协议 |
| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 偏远地区雇主担保，部分DAMA地区可能覆盖本职业 |
| Subclass DAMA（Designated Area Migration Agreement） | 特定地区协议，可能列入职业清单，需雇主担保 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for wood processing machine operators?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges from AUD 45,000 to 55,000 per year; mid-level from 55,000 to 65,000; senior/supervisor from 65,000 to 78,000. Overtime can boost earnings.

**问：Can this occupation lead to Australian permanent residency?**
答：Generally not on skilled occupation lists; migration is very difficult. Some regional DAMA agreements or specific employer sponsorship may be possible but are rare.

**问：How to enter this occupation without experience?**
答：Enroll in TAFE short courses or apprenticeships, obtain a Certificate III in Timber and Wood Processing, and get a White Card and Forklift Licence to improve job prospects.
