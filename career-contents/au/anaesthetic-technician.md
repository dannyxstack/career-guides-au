# Anaesthetic Technician — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 311211 (ANZSCO)**

Anaesthetic technicians assist anaesthetists in preparing and maintaining anaesthesia equipment, monitoring patient vital signs, and ensuring safe and effective anaesthesia in the operating theatre.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After gaining experience as an Anaesthetic Technician, you can advance to senior technician or team leader, or move into perioperative care, equipment management, or anaesthesia assistance. Some further study to become an anaesthetic nurse or doctor.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically records patient vital signs data (e.g., blood pressure, heart rate, blood oxygen saturation)
- Generate anesthesia records and reports.
- Routine equipment self-checks and fault log recording
- Drug dosage calculation and infusion rate adjustment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time monitoring of abnormal vital signs with early warning
- Predictive maintenance and fault diagnosis for smart devices
- AI-driven anesthesia depth and drug response models assist decision-making
- VR simulation training for new technicians
- Big Data Analytics for Optimized Anesthesia Plans

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and manual intervention in emergencies
- Real-time communication and collaboration with anesthesiologists and surgical teams
- Quick troubleshooting and manual operation for equipment failures
- Perception of individual patient differences and humanistic care
- Statutory signature and legal liability requirement

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted monitoring system operation and anomaly interpretation
- Basic data analysis and medical statistics
- Smart medical device maintenance and fault diagnosis
- Human-machine collaboration team communication skills
- Emergency simulation and crisis resource management
- Critical evaluation of evidence-based medicine and AI recommendations

**Key growth areas:**

- Healthcare
- Aging Population
- Surgical Services
- Medical Technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for anaesthetic technicians?**
A: Full-time average annual salary approx. AUD $70,000-90,000, senior up to $110,000; overtime and night shift allowances can increase income.

**Q: Is it difficult for anaesthesia technicians to immigrate to Australia?**
A: This occupation is on the MLTSSL and eligible for 189/190/482/186 visas, but requires a VETASSESS skills assessment and meeting English requirements.

**Q: Are anaesthetic technicians in demand in Australia?**
A: Occupation in demand, especially in remote areas, with recruitment in both public and private hospitals.
