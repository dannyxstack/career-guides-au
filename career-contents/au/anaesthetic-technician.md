# Anaesthetic Technician（Anaesthetic Technician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：311211 – Anaesthetic Technician。**

Anaesthetic Technicians assist anaesthetists in preparing and maintaining anaesthesia equipment, monitoring patient vitals, and ensuring safe anaesthesia delivery during surgeries.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 需雇主担保，职业在CSOL上，可转186 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居，需职业评估和三年工作经验 |
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 职业在MLTSSL上，通过打分系统，无需担保 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保永居，需获得州政府提名 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for anaesthetic technicians?**
答：Full-time average salary is around AUD 70,000–90,000, senior up to AUD 110,000, with overtime and shift allowances.

**问：Is it difficult for anaesthetic technicians to immigrate to Australia?**
答：The occupation is on MLTSSL, eligible for 189/190/482/186 visas, but requires VETASSESS skills assessment and English proficiency.

**问：Are anaesthetic technicians in demand in Australia?**
答：Yes, a shortage occupation, especially in regional areas, with vacancies in public and private hospitals.
