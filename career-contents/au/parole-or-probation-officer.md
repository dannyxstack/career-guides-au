# Parole or Probation Officer（Parole or Probation Officer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：411714 – Parole or Probation Officer。**

Parole or Probation Officers supervise and counsel offenders under court orders, including parolees and probationers, to facilitate rehabilitation and reduce recidivism. Duties include case management, risk assessment, report writing, and collaboration with the justice system.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a Parole or Probation Officer?**
答：In Australia, the starting salary is around AUD 68,000-78,000 inclusive of superannuation, with senior roles reaching over AUD 100,000. Salary growth is moderate.

**问：Can a Parole or Probation Officer migrate to Australia via skilled migration?**
答：Generally, this occupation is not on the skilled occupation list, so migration pathways are very limited. Employer sponsorship (482/186) is possible but rare.

**问：What are the career development opportunities for a Parole or Probation Officer?**
答：Progression from entry-level to senior officer, team leader, regional manager, or into policy, training, and assessment roles.
