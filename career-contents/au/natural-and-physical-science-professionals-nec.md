# Natural and Physical Science Professionals nec（Natural and Physical Science Professionals nec）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：234999 – Natural and Physical Science Professionals nec。**

Covers natural and physical science professionals not elsewhere classified, including biologists, chemists, environmental scientists, conducting research, analysis, experiments, and consultancy.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 技术独立签证，无需担保，需满足分数及职业清单要求 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保签证，需获得州政府提名 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，需有雇主提名 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居签证，需雇主提名且通过职业评估 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Natural and Physical Science Professionals?**
答：Entry-level AUD 60-75k, mid-level AUD 80-100k, senior AUD 110-140k. Varies by industry and location.

**问：Which skilled migration visas are available for this occupation?**
答：Eligible for 189, 190, 482, and 186 visas, requiring VETASSESS assessment.

**问：What is the career progression?**
答：Start as junior researcher or lab assistant, advance to senior scientist, project lead, or manager, or move into policy, consulting, or management.
