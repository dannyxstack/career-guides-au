# Tourist Information Officer（Tourist Information Officer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：451611 – Tourist Information Officer。**

Provides travel information, itinerary planning, ticket booking and related services at visitor centres or information kiosks to enhance tourist experiences and promote local attractions.

**竞争度：中（★★★☆☆）。**
**工作强度：中（★★½☆☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)） | 偏远地区州担保技术移民签证，适合在指定区域工作 |
| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional)） | 偏远地区雇主担保临时签证，需雇主提名 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage） | 短期紧缺技能签证，需雇主担保，可能转186 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated (Permanent)） | 州担保永居签证，需州政府提名，部分州开放此职业 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Tourist Information Officer？**
- 喜欢与人沟通、乐于助人的人
- 对旅游行业有热情，熟悉当地资源
- 能多任务处理、适应灵活排班

**谁不适合学Tourist Information Officer？**
- 不喜欢应对游客投诉和压力
- 希望高薪或快速晋升

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Tourist Information Officer?**
答：The average full-time salary is around AUD 55,000-68,000, with senior roles up to AUD 80,000. Remote areas may offer additional allowances.

**问：Can a Tourist Information Officer apply for skilled migration?**
答：Yes, the occupation is on some state nomination lists. Usually through 491 (regional) or 494 (regional employer sponsored) visas. Opportunities in non-regional areas are limited.

**问：Is there career progression for this role?**
答：Yes, from frontline officer to centre supervisor, or move into tourism marketing, event coordination, etc.
