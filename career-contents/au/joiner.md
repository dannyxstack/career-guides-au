# Joiner（Joiner）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：331213 – Joiner。**

Joiners manufacture and install wooden components such as doors, windows, cabinets, and furniture, working in workshops or on construction sites, and are key tradespeople in the building industry.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Skills in Demand (Temporary Skill Shortage) Visa） | 适用于有雇主担保的细木工，需三年相关工作经验。 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) Visa） | 雇主担保永久居留，需通过技能评估和满足英语要求。 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 州担保偏远地区临时签证，可通过转191获PR。 |
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 技术独立签证，细木工在MLTSSL上，需达到65分以上。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of a joiner in Australia?**
答：Entry-level joiners earn AUD 50,000-65,000, mid-level earn AUD 65,000-85,000, and senior/self-employed earn AUD 85,000-110,000 annually, with overtime and piecework increasing income.

**问：Can a joiner obtain Australian PR through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. Joiner is on the MLTSSL, eligible for 189, 190, 491, or employer-sponsored 482/186 visas. Requires TRA skills assessment and sufficient English.

**问：Which regions in Australia have the highest demand for joiners?**
答：New South Wales, Queensland, and Victoria have high demand due to construction projects. Regional areas like Tasmania and South Australia also offer opportunities due to building growth.
