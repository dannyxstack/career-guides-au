# Dairy Products Maker（Dairy Products Maker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：831114 – Dairy Products Maker。**

Dairy Products Makers operate equipment to process milk into cheese, butter, yogurt and other dairy products, ensuring quality and hygiene standards.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

|---|---|

| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional） | 偏远地区雇主担保签证，需在指定地区工作 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a Dairy Products Maker?**
答：Entry-level earns AUD 45,000-55,000; mid-level AUD 55,000-65,000; senior up to AUD 80,000. Regional allowances may apply.

**问：Can a Dairy Products Maker migrate to Australia via skilled visa?**
答：This occupation is not on CSOL/MLTSSL, so 189/190/186 are not possible. Employer sponsorship via 482 or 494 is possible but limited.

**问：What are the career paths for a Dairy Products Maker?**
答：Advance to production supervisor, quality controller, or plant manager. Also possible to move into dairy R&D or equipment maintenance.
