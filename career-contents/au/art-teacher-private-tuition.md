# Art Teacher (Private Tuition)（Art Teacher (Private Tuition)）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：249211 – Art Teacher (Private Tuition)。**

Art Teachers (Private Tuition) instruct students in visual arts such as drawing, painting, and sculpture in non-school settings like private studios or community centers. A relevant degree and teaching skills are typically required.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 需在MLTSSL列表上，通过AITSL评估，EOI打分获邀 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保提名，部分州可能要求当地工作 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 需雇主担保，艺术教师岗位较少 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional Visa） | 偏远地区担保，分数要求较低 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Art Teacher (Private Tuition)？**
- 热爱艺术且善于教学的自由职业者
- 希望兼职或灵活安排时间的艺术专业毕业生
- 已有稳定学生资源的经验艺术家

**谁不适合学Art Teacher (Private Tuition)？**
- 追求高薪和稳定全职收入者
- 不愿自雇或缺乏自我推广能力者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the income of private art teachers stable?**
答：Income fluctuates depending on student numbers and rates. Part-time is common; full-time requires a steady client base, typically AUD 40,000-70,000 per year.

**问：Can Art Teachers (Private Tuition) migrate to Australia?**
答：Yes, it is on the MLTSSL, eligible for 189/190/491 visas. Requires AITSL skills assessment, English, and points. Competition is moderate but state nomination places are limited.
