# Whitewater Rafting Guide（Whitewater Rafting Guide）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：452217 – Whitewater Rafting Guide。**

Whitewater Rafting Guide leads clients on whitewater rafting trips, ensuring safety, providing instruction, and enhancing the outdoor adventure experience.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Whitewater Rafting Guide？**
- 热爱户外运动、善于沟通、有团队合作精神的人
- 具备良好体能、能在激流环境中保持冷静的人
- 希望从事旅游行业、享受季节性工作节奏的人

**谁不适合学Whitewater Rafting Guide？**
- 不喜欢户外体力劳动、怕水或不擅长游泳的人
- 追求稳定办公室工作和固定收入的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Whitewater Rafting Guide?**
答：Entry-level salary is around AUD 45,000–55,000, mid-level AUD 55,000–65,000, and senior guides can earn AUD 65,000–80,000, with income affected by seasonality and tips.

**问：Can a Whitewater Rafting Guide migrate to Australia?**
答：This occupation is not on the skilled occupation list, so standard skilled migration (189/190/491) is not possible. Options are limited to working holiday visas or employer sponsorship, but long-term migration is difficult.
