# Glass Processing Worker（Glass Processing Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：839916 – Glass Processing Worker。**

Operates machines to process glass products, including cutting, grinding, drilling, and polishing, widely used in construction and automotive industries.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Glass Processing Worker？**
- 喜欢动手操作、注重细节的人
- 愿意在工厂或工地工作，能适应噪音和尘土环境

**谁不适合学Glass Processing Worker？**
- 不擅长体力劳动或长时间站立的人
- 对玻璃粉尘或高温环境敏感者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the salary high for glass processing workers?**
答：Starting salary is around AUD 45,000-55,000, and experienced workers can earn AUD 70,000-88,000, above the average for trades.

**问：Can glass processing workers migrate to Australia?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on the short-term skilled list, eligible for 482, 186 (employer-sponsored), or state-nominated 190/491 visas.

**问：What are the career progression paths for glass processing workers?**
答：Can advance to senior technician, production supervisor, quality inspector, or start own glass processing business.
