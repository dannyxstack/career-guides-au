# Vineyard Worker（Vineyard Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：842222 – Vineyard Worker。**

Vineyard workers perform tasks such as planting, pruning, irrigating, pest control, and harvesting grapes. They are essential to Australia's wine industry. Typical duties include operating farm machinery, applying fertilizers, and maintaining trellis systems.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 需雇主担保，职业需在短期清单上（如农业技师），葡萄园工人作为基层岗位通常不符合 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 偏远地区州担保，若职业在州担保清单上（如农业工人），可凑分申请 |
| Subclass 494（Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) Visa） | 雇主担保偏远地区签证，需职业在特定清单上 |
| Subclass DAMA（Designated Area Migration Agreement） | 特定区域协议，可能开放农业工人岗位，需雇主担保 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★½）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Vineyard Worker？**
- 喜欢户外体力劳动，适应季节性工作和农场生活
- 愿意在偏远地区工作，积累农业经验

- 不擅长体力劳动或对农业工作无兴趣
- 希望快速获得永居且不了解农业签证限制

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the hourly wage for vineyard workers?**
答：Typically AUD 23-28 per hour, depending on experience, location, and certifications. Seasonal casual workers may earn higher rates.

**问：Can vineyard workers get permanent residency through skilled migration?**
答：Unlikely, as this occupation is not on the core skills list. Some DAMA agreements may include agricultural workers, but it usually requires employer sponsorship and leads to temporary visas only.

**问：Are there career progression opportunities for vineyard workers?**
答：Yes, workers can advance to team leader, farm manager, or viticulture specialist. Completing TAFE courses (e.g., Certificate III in Agriculture) improves prospects.
