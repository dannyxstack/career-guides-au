# Winery Cellar Hand — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 831118 (ANZSCO)**

Perform physical tasks such as cleaning, bottling, and grape processing at a winery; an entry-level role in wine production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Can progress from temporary worker to senior cellarman or winemaking assistant, then move into winemaker or production management with experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual cleaning of fermentation tanks and pipelines (replaced by automatic CIP system)
- Bottle labeling and stacking operations (robotic labeling/palletizing)
- Record fermentation temperature and density (IoT sensor automatic collection)
- Repetitive grape pressing and conveying (automated pressing machine).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI fermentation monitoring system assists in assessing wine maturity, reducing the frequency of sample testing
- Cleaning robot path planning and task scheduling to optimize efficiency
- Predictive maintenance alerts (equipment sensor data warning of failures)
- Data visualization analysis of inventory and production output to assist production scheduling decisions.
- AR glasses for remote expert guidance in maintenance and quality inspection

**Human moat:**

- Handle non-standard issues such as unexpected equipment failures and pipe blockages
- Wine tasting and sensory evaluation (e.g., detecting mustiness, oxidation levels)
- Experience-based judgment in adjusting process parameters in collaboration with winemakers
- On-site sorting and disposal of complex raw materials (such as moldy grapes)

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and basic programming of automated cleaning systems
- IoT sensor data interpretation and anomaly alarm handling
- Basic mechanical maintenance and HMI interface operation
- Basics of brewing processes and knowledge of quality testing
- Use of digital tools (Excel, winery management software)
- Reading English technical documents (equipment manuals, safety regulations)

**Key growth areas:**

- Seasonal
- Wine Industry
- Manual Labor
- Agriculture

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Good physical fitness, able to adapt to seasonal high-intensity work
- Interested in winemaking, willing to start from the ground up

**Who is not suited**
- Seeking long-term stability and high salary
- Dislikes outdoor or repetitive physical labour
