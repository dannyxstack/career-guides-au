# Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General)（Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General)）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：342314 – Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General)。**

Electronic Instrument Trades Workers (General) install, test, maintain, and repair electronic instruments and control systems, including industrial automation equipment, medical electronics, and communication systems.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Electronic Instrument Trades Workers?**
答：Entry-level salary is about AUD 55,000-70,000 per year, mid-level AUD 75,000-95,000, and senior AUD 100,000-130,000, depending on industry and location.

**问：What visas can Electronic Instrument Trades Workers use to migrate to Australia?**
答：They can apply for the 482 Temporary Skill Shortage visa, 186 Employer Nomination Scheme, 189 Skilled Independent visa, or 190 Skilled Nominated visa. TRA skills assessment and English requirements apply.

**问：What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
答：Advance to senior technician, supervisor, or project manager, or become an engineer through further study, or specialize in areas like automation and renewable energy.
