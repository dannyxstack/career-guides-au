# Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General) — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 342314 (ANZSCO)**

Electronic instrument trade worker (general) installs, tests, maintains and repairs electronic instruments and control systems, including industrial automation equipment, medical electronic devices and communication systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career development paths include promotion to senior technician, supervisor, or project manager, or transitioning via additional training to engineer or system designer.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate circuit schematics and PCB layouts
- Perform standard electrical calculations and simulation verification
- Writing and testing routine control logic code
- Automatically generate test reports and documentation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted rapid generation of multiple design options and performance comparison
- Using machine learning to predict equipment failures and perform preventive maintenance
- Quickly retrieving technical documentation and standards through natural language interaction
- AI optimizes power system load distribution and energy efficiency
- Automated checking of design compliance with safety standards

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard or highly customized system integration needs
- Innovative design and decision-making under uncertainty
- Take on legal and ethical responsibilities as project lead
- Cross-team communication coordination and client needs insight
- Experience-based fault diagnosis and on-site debugging

**Skills to build:**

- AI tool applications (e.g., Altair AI, Ansys AI)
- Data analytics and machine learning basics (Python/TensorFlow)
- Digital twin and simulation technology
- Renewable energy system design (e.g., photovoltaic, energy storage)
- Embedded systems and IoT development
- Project management and interdisciplinary communication

**Key growth areas:**

- Instrumentation
- Automation
- Renewable Energy
- Mining Support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for electronic instrument trade workers?**
A: Junior annual salary approx. AUD 55,000-70,000, mid-level AUD 75,000-95,000, senior up to AUD 100,000-130,000, depending on industry and location.

**Q: Which visas can electronic instrument trade workers use to immigrate to Australia?**
A: Eligible via 482 employer-sponsored temporary visa, 186 employer-sponsored permanent residency, 189 Skilled Independent visa, or 190 state-nominated visa. Requires TRA skills assessment and English proficiency.

**Q: What are the career development paths for this occupation?**
