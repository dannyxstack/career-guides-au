# Nut Farm Worker — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 842216 (ANZSCO)**

Agricultural workers responsible for planting, cultivating, harvesting, and initial processing of nuts (e.g., almonds, macadamia nuts), playing an important role in the Australian agricultural sector. Typically involved in seasonal work, relying on physical labor, and needing to adapt to outdoor environments and machinery operation.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Starting as a general farm worker, one can progress to team supervisor or machine operator with experience, and a few may become farm managers or operate small farms. Requires skills in picking, pruning, irrigation, and basic management abilities

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using AI vision systems to automatically sort nut grades and remove defective nuts

- Automated crop health inspection using drones and satellite imagery
- Use AI to predict optimal harvest times and schedule harvesters

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal coordination and on-site management with seasonal temporary worker teams

**Skills to build:**

- Enhance skills in mechanical maintenance and automation equipment debugging

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a nut farm worker?**
A: Junior workers earn about A$45,000-55,000 per year, intermediate A$55,000-65,000, senior up to A$75,000. Usually paid hourly at A$25-30/hour, with higher overtime rates.

**Q: Can nut farm workers immigrate to Australia?**
A: This occupation usually does not meet skilled migration visa requirements as it is not on the occupation list. However, temporary work is possible through seasonal worker programs or working holiday visas (subclass 417). In rare cases, employer-sponsored visas (like DAMA) can lead to permanent residency, but with high thresholds.

**Q: How to advance from a nut farm worker?**
A: With experience, can advance to team supervisor, machinery operator, or learn farm management to become a farm manager. Some workers transition to related fields like orchard management or horticulture.
