# Nut Farm Worker（Nut Farm Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：842216 – Nut Farm Worker。**

Responsible for planting, cultivating, harvesting and primary processing of nuts (e.g., almonds, macadamias) in Australian agricultural sector. Involves seasonal manual labor, outdoor work and machinery operation.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a nut farm worker?**
答：Entry-level workers earn AUD 45,000-55,000 per year, mid-level AUD 55,000-65,000, and senior up to AUD 75,000. Usually paid hourly at AUD 25-30, with overtime premiums.

**问：Can a nut farm worker migrate to Australia?**
答：This occupation is generally not eligible for skilled migration. However, temporary work via Seasonal Worker Programme or Working Holiday visa (subclass 417) is possible. In limited cases, DAMA agreements may offer PR pathways.

**问：How to advance from nut farm worker?**
答：With experience, you can become a team supervisor, machinery operator, or farm manager by learning management skills. Some transition to related fields like orchard management or horticulture.
