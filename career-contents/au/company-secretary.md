# Company Secretary — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 221211 (ANZSCO)**

The company secretary is responsible for ensuring the company complies with legal and regulatory requirements, maintaining corporate records, managing board meetings, and acting as a communication bridge between shareholders and the board. In Australia, this role typically plays a key governance role in senior management.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

- Document archiving and retrieval: AI automatically classifies, stores, and retrieves company records

- Risk management monitoring: AI monitors compliance risk points in real time and alerts potential issues.
- Shareholder communication: AI-generated personalized reports to improve communication efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Strategic advice and governance: providing board with combined qualitative and quantitative insights.
- Interpersonal trust and confidentiality: trust and confidentiality when handling sensitive information
- Cross-departmental coordination: mediating conflicts between different stakeholders

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What are the salary levels for company secretaries in Australia?**
A: Entry-level 70k-95k AUD, mid-level 100k-140k AUD, senior 150k-250k AUD. Varies by company size and industry; higher in finance and mining.

**Q: can company secretaries immigrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
A: Yes. Company secretary is on the MLTSSL list, applicable for 189/190/482/186 visas. Requires relevant skills assessment (e.g., VETASSESS), usually a bachelor's degree or higher in a closely related field, and at least one year of work experience.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a company secretary?**
A: Typically need a bachelor's degree in law, accounting or business. Obtaining a Chartered Secretary qualification from the Governance Institute of Australia can boost competitiveness. Some employers also accept relevant work experience.
