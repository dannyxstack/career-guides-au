# Company Secretary（Company Secretary）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：221211 – Company Secretary。**

Company Secretaries ensure corporate compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, maintain company records, manage board meetings, and act as a communication bridge between shareholders and the board. In Australia, this role is a key governance position in senior management.

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 适用于由雇主担保的中期清单职位，公司秘书在MLTSSL上，可转186。 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居签证，需通过职业评估，适用于有3年相关工作经验的申请人。 |
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 技术独立永居签证，公司秘书在MLTSSL上，可通过EOI打分申请。 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保永居签证，需获得州政府提名，部分州对公司秘书有需求。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Company Secretaries in Australia?**
答：Entry-level AUD 70k-95k, mid-level AUD 100k-140k, senior AUD 150k-250k. Varies by company size and industry, with finance and mining paying more.

**问：Can a Company Secretary migrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on the MLTSSL, eligible for 189/190/482/186 visas. Requires a positive skills assessment (e.g., VETASSESS), typically a bachelor's degree in a highly relevant field and at least one year of work experience.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a Company Secretary?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in law, accounting, or business. Obtaining the Chartered Secretary qualification from the Governance Institute of Australia is highly valued. Some employers may accept relevant work experience.
