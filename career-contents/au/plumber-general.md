# Plumber (General)（Plumber (General)）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：334116 – Plumber (General)。**

Plumbers install, repair, and maintain water, drainage, gas, and heating systems including pipes, fixtures, and equipment. They work in residential, commercial, and industrial settings to ensure plumbing systems are safe, reliable, and code-compliant.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 技术独立签证，需在MLTSSL列表上，水管工在MLTSSL中，可申请189永居 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保技术移民，各州均担保水管工，获担保可加5分 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，水管工在短单上，可转186永居 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居签证，可通过482转186或直接申请（需职业评估） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of plumbers in Australia?**
答：Entry-level plumbers earn AUD 55,000-70,000 per year, mid-level licensed plumbers AUD 75,000-95,000, and senior or supervisors AUD 100,000-130,000. Self-employed plumbers can earn more but bear business costs.

**问：How can a plumber migrate to Australia?**
答：Plumbers are on the MLTSSL, eligible for 189 Skilled Independent, 190 State Nominated, and 482/186 employer-sponsored visas. Must pass TRA skills assessment and meet English requirements.

**问：What are the career progression paths for plumbers?**
答：Progress from apprentice to licensed plumber, then to supervisor, project manager, or self-employment. Specializations in gas, fire, solar hot water, or green plumbing can increase earnings.
