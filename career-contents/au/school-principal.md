# School Principal — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 134311 (ANZSCO)**

The principal is responsible for overall school management, including curriculum, staff leadership, student welfare, and budget control, ensuring educational quality and school development goals.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for school principals in Australia?**
A: Entry-level principal salary about AUD 130,000-150,000, mid-size school 150,000-180,000, large or elite school up to 180,000-220,000.

**Q: Can a school principal immigrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
A: Yes. School principals are on the MLTSSL and can immigrate via visas such as 189, 190, 482, and 186. Applicants must meet education, teacher registration, and English requirements.

**Q: What steps are needed to become a principal?**
A: Usually obtain an education degree and become a registered teacher, accumulate teaching experience, then be promoted to deputy principal, and finally complete principal qualification certification.
