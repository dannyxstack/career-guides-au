# School Principal（School Principal）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：134311 – School Principal。**

School principals oversee all aspects of school operations, including curriculum, staff leadership, student welfare, and budget management, ensuring educational quality and school development.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for school principals in Australia?**
答：Entry-level principals earn AUD 130,000-150,000, mid-level AUD 150,000-180,000, and senior principals at large schools up to AUD 220,000.

**问：Can school principals migrate to Australia via skilled migration?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on the MLTSSL, eligible for visas 189, 190, 482, and 186. Requirements include relevant degree, teacher registration, and English proficiency.

**问：What are the steps to become a school principal?**
答：Typically: obtain an education degree, become a registered teacher, gain teaching experience, advance to deputy principal, and complete principal certification.
