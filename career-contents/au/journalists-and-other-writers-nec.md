# Journalists and Other Writers nec — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 212499 (ANZSCO)**

Includes freelance writers, columnists, content creators, etc., producing articles, reviews, or feature content for newspapers, magazines, or online platforms, not limited to specific categories.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Entry-level can work as freelance writers or content editors, building a portfolio to become columnists or editors; experienced can advance to content director or transition into public relations/marketing communications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate standard news briefs (e.g., financial reports, sports scores)
- Speech-to-text and automatic subtitle generation
- Fact-checking and data reconciliation (e.g., verifying statistical data)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted data analysis and visualization (quickly identify trends)
- AI-recommended topics and audience preference prediction
- AI compiles multilingual information summaries

**Human moat:**

- Exclusive interviews and in-depth investigations (interpersonal insight and trust)
- Ethical judgment and sensitive issue gatekeeping
- Creative storytelling (features, commentary)

**Skills to build:**

- Data journalism (Python/R/data visualization)
- Multimedia production (video/podcast editing)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT-assisted writing)
- Fact-checking and information verification
- Audience Analysis and Digital Marketing

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital media
- Content marketing
- Freelance economy
- Specialized journalism

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- For those passionate about writing and content creation, with excellent written expression skills
- Freelancers who can handle publishing deadlines and flexible working hours (including weekends/nights)
- Professional writer with deep interest and willingness to specialize in a specific field (e.g., technology, sports, culture)

**Who is not suited**
- Those seeking a stable 9-to-5 work routine.
- Those who cannot accept low starting salaries and highly competitive environments

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for journalists and authors in Australia?**
A: Entry-level positions approx. AUD 45,000–60,000, mid-level AUD 60,000–85,000, senior up to AUD 120,000+, but freelance writers have more variable income.

**Q: Can journalists and writers migrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
A: This occupation is not on the Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL); skilled migration (189/190) is nearly impossible. Only regional employer sponsorship (494) or DAMA pathways exist, but opportunities are scarce.

**Q: What career development paths are available for journalists or writers?**
A: Can start as a freelance writer or junior journalist, build a portfolio to become a columnist or editor; can also transition into content marketing, PR, or publishing management.
