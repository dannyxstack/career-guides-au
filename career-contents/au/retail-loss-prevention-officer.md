# Retail Loss Prevention Officer（Retail Loss Prevention Officer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：442215 – Retail Loss Prevention Officer。**

Retail Loss Prevention Officers monitor theft, fraud, and shrinkage in retail environments, using security measures, investigations, and staff training to protect store assets and reduce losses.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Retail Loss Prevention Officer?**
答：Entry-level officers earn around AUD 50,000-60,000 annually; senior or management roles can reach AUD 75,000-90,000, depending on location and employer.

**问：Can a Retail Loss Prevention Officer migrate to Australia through skilled migration?**
答：This occupation is not on any skilled occupation list, so migration opportunities are very limited, usually only via employer sponsorship or related security roles.

**问：What are the career prospects for a Retail Loss Prevention Officer?**
答：Employment is stable but slow-growing; advancement to loss prevention management or corporate security is possible.
