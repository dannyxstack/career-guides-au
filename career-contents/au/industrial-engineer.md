# Industrial Engineer（Industrial Engineer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：233511 – Industrial Engineer。**

Industrial Engineers optimize production processes, improve efficiency, reduce costs, and ensure quality control and safety management. They work in manufacturing, logistics, consulting, and apply systems engineering to solve complex problems.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Skills in Demand Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，需2年相关工作经验和雇主担保 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居签证，需职业评估和3年工作经验 |
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 独立技术移民永居签证，需高分EOI和职业评估 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保永居签证，需获得州政府提名 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Industrial Engineer？**
- 喜欢系统性思考和优化流程的人
- 具备数学和工程分析能力，善于沟通协调
- 追求稳定职业发展并愿意在制造业/物流领域深耕

**谁不适合学Industrial Engineer？**
- 对重复性工作缺乏耐心的人
- 不擅长与不同部门协作沟通的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for Industrial Engineers in Australia?**
答：Entry-level AUD 65k-80k, mid-level AUD 85k-110k, senior AUD 115k-150k. Senior managers can earn over AUD 180k.

**问：What are the requirements for Industrial Engineer skilled migration to Australia?**
答：Require positive skills assessment from Engineers Australia, IELTS 6.0 each band, EOI score at least 65 (but higher needed for invitation), and occupation on MLTSSL.

**问：What are the career progression paths for Industrial Engineers?**
答：Can advance to Lean Production Manager, Supply Chain Director, Operations Manager, or Consultant. Specialize in data analytics, automation, or sustainability.
