# Vegetable Grower (Aus) / Market Gardener (NZ)（Vegetable Grower (Aus) / Market Gardener (NZ)）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：121616 – Vegetable Grower (Aus) / Market Gardener (NZ)。**

Vegetable growers plan, plant, cultivate and harvest vegetable crops, manage farm operations, and ensure product quality and yield. In Australia, this role typically involves large-scale commercial or small-scale organic farming.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage） | 蔬菜种植者不在MLTSSL上，但可能通过特定雇主担保申请482签证，需证明技能和英语 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)） | 部分偏远地区州担保可能包含该职业，需获得州政府担保 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the income of a vegetable grower stable?**
答：Income is greatly affected by season, weather, and market prices. Entry-level positions may have minimum wage guarantees, but farm owner income fluctuates significantly.

**问：Can a vegetable grower immigrate to Australia?**
答：This occupation is not on the MLTSSL, so independent skilled migration (189/190) is unlikely. Employer sponsorship (482) or regional visas (491) are possible but require meeting skill and English requirements.
