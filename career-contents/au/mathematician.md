# Mathematician（Mathematician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：224112 – Mathematician。**

Mathematicians apply advanced mathematical and statistical methods to solve complex problems in science, engineering, business, and government, developing models and analyzing data.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Mathematician？**
- 对数学和逻辑有浓厚兴趣，具备抽象思维和问题解决能力
- 喜欢数据分析和建模，愿意持续学习新方法
- 适合在学术、金融、科技或政府研究部门发展的职业人士

**谁不适合学Mathematician？**
- 不喜欢长时间面对复杂数学公式和抽象概念
- 偏好实践操作而非理论分析，不适应高压研究环境

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for mathematicians?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from AUD 65,000 to 85,000; mid-level from AUD 85,000 to 110,000; senior positions can exceed AUD 150,000.

**问：Can mathematicians migrate to Australia via skilled migration?**
答：Yes. Mathematician (ANZSCO 224112) is on the MLTSSL, eligible for 189/190/482/186 visas, requiring VETASSESS skills assessment.

**问：What does a mathematician do?**
答：Mathematicians apply advanced mathematics and statistical modeling to solve real-world problems such as data mining, financial risk analysis, operations research, and algorithm design.
