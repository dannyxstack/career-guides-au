# Dairy Cattle Farmer（Dairy Cattle Farmer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：121313 – Dairy Cattle Farmer。**

Manage daily operations of dairy cattle farms, including feeding, breeding, health management and milk production, ensuring profitability and animal welfare.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of a dairy cattle farmer?**
答：Entry-level AUD 50k-65k, mid-level 65k-85k, senior 85k-120k; farm owners earn more but vary.

**问：How can a dairy cattle farmer migrate to Australia?**
答：Via employer sponsorship (482/186) or state nomination (190/491), requiring VETASSESS skills assessment and English proficiency.

**问：Is there career progression for this occupation?**
答：Yes, from farm hand to manager, or pivot to agri-consulting, dairy processing, or starting own farm.
