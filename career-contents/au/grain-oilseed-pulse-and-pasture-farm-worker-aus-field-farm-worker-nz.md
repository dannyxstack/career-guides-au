# Grain, Oilseed, Pulse and Pasture Farm Worker (Aus) / Field Farm Worker (NZ) — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 842214 (ANZSCO)**

Involves planting, managing, harvesting, and primary processing of grains, oilseeds, legumes, and pastures, including tillage, sowing, fertilizing, irrigating, spraying pesticides, and harvesting.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

General farm workers can advance to senior farm operators, farm foremen, or production supervisors. With experience, they may become agricultural contractors or farm owners, requiring precision agriculture skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using traditional tractors for repetitive field operations such as sowing and tillage
- Apply fertilizers and pesticides manually or on a fixed schedule
- Visually inspecting crop health and pests
- Manually record data such as yield and soil moisture
- Simple sorting and basic processing such as bagging grain

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using satellite or drone imagery to analyze crop growth conditions, optimizing fertilization and irrigation timing
- Develop precise irrigation and pesticide plans using AI prediction models
- Use autonomous farming equipment to improve operational accuracy and efficiency, reducing resource waste
- Use IoT sensors to monitor soil and weather data in real time to aid decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Dealing with complex mechanical breakdowns and on-site emergencies in the field
- In-depth experiential knowledge of local climate, soil, and crop varieties
- Coordinate team operations, train new workers
- Communicate with farm owners and agronomists to develop flexible production plans
- Quickly respond to bad weather or equipment anomalies

**Skills to build:**

- Skills in operating and maintaining autonomous agricultural vehicles.
- Use drones and remote sensing equipment for field monitoring
- Use data analysis and IoT platforms (e.g., AgriWebb)
- Application of precision agriculture software (e.g., Climate FieldView)
- Basic programming or debugging skills for calibrating automation equipment
- Knowledge of sustainable agricultural practices such as crop rotation and organic management

**Key growth areas:**

- Seasonal labour
- Broadacre cropping
- Precision agriculture
- Farm mechanisation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for farm workers in Australia?**
A: Entry-level farm worker annual salary approx. A$48,000-58,000, but income is highly seasonal. Overtime during peak seasons can significantly increase earnings. Some employers provide free accommodation.

**Q: Can this occupation apply for Australian skilled migration?**
A: This occupation is usually not on the skilled migration occupation list, but permanent residency can be obtained through regional employer sponsorship (494/482) or DAMA programs, depending on the region and employer qualifications.

**Q: What are the common risks of farm work?**
A: Mainly includes machinery operation accidents, chemical exposure, heat stroke, and back strain. Employers must provide safety training and protective equipment, and comply with work health and safety regulations.
