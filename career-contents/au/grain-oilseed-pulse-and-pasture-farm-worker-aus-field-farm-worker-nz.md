# Grain, Oilseed, Pulse and Pasture Farm Worker (Aus) / Field Farm Worker (NZ)（Grain, Oilseed, Pulse and Pasture Farm Worker (Aus) / Field Farm Worker (NZ)）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：842214 – Grain, Oilseed, Pulse and Pasture Farm Worker (Aus) / Field Farm Worker (NZ)。**

Performs tasks related to planting, cultivating, harvesting, and primary processing of grains, oilseeds, pulses, and pasture, including plowing, sowing, fertilizing, irrigation, spraying, and operating farm machinery.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

|---|---|

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for farm workers in Australia?**
答：Entry-level farm workers earn around AUD 48,000-58,000 per year, but income varies seasonally with overtime opportunities. Some employers provide free accommodation.

**问：Can this occupation lead to Australian skilled migration?**
答：This occupation is generally not on the skilled occupation list, but permanent residence may be possible via regional employer sponsorship (494/482) or DAMA programs, subject to location and employer eligibility.

**问：What are common risks in farm work?**
答：Risks include machinery accidents, chemical exposure, heat stress, and back injuries. Employers must provide safety training and protective equipment, complying with work health and safety regulations.
