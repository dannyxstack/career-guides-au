# Retail Pharmacist（Retail Pharmacist）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：251513 – Retail Pharmacist。**

Retail pharmacists work in community pharmacies, dispensing prescriptions, providing health advice, managing medicine stock, and recommending over-the-counter products, playing a key role in primary healthcare.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 独立技术移民，需在MLTSSL上，满足分数（目前约85+） |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，需找到雇主，可转186永居 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保永居签证，各州配额，额外加5分 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主直接担保永居，需通过职业评估 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income prospect for retail pharmacists in Australia?**
答：Entry-level salary is around AUD 65,000-80,000; mid-level AUD 80,000-100,000; experienced pharmacy managers can earn AUD 100,000-130,000. Income grows with experience but is lower than doctors.

**问：How can retail pharmacists migrate to Australia via skilled visa?**
答：Retail pharmacists are on MLTSSL. Options include 189 Skilled Independent (high points required), 190 State Nominated, or 482 Temporary Skill Shortage leading to 186 ENS. Must pass APC skills assessment and AHPRA registration.

**问：What are the career progression paths for retail pharmacists?**
答：Can advance to pharmacy manager, regional supervisor, or specialize in clinical pharmacy, medication review, or aged care pharmacy. Also options to own a pharmacy or work in pharmaceutical wholesale.
