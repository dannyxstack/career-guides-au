# Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioner（Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioner）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：252214 – Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioner。**

Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioners diagnose and treat diseases using acupuncture, herbal medicine, and tuina, registered under CMBA in Australia as a complementary health profession.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a TCM practitioner in Australia?**
答：Entry-level around AUD 55,000-70,000, mid-level 70,000-90,000, senior 90,000-120,000. Self-employed income varies.

**问：Can a TCM practitioner migrate to Australia via skilled migration?**
答：Yes. TCM is on MLTSSL, eligible for 189, 190, or 491 visas, requiring CMBA registration and skills assessment.

**问：What are the registration requirements for TCM in Australia?**
答：You need a CMBA-approved degree, English proficiency (e.g., IELTS 7.0), and pass the registration exam.
