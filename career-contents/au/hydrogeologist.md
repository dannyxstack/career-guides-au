# Hydrogeologist（Hydrogeologist）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：234413 – Hydrogeologist。**

Hydrogeologists study the distribution, movement, and quality of groundwater, assessing water resource sustainability for mining, environmental remediation, and water supply projects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 技术独立签证，需通过技能评估并在MLTSSL清单上 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保签证，需满足州政府提名要求 |
| Subclass 482（Skills in Demand Visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，工作3年后可转186永居 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Hydrogeologist？**
- 具备地质学、水资源或环境科学背景且乐于户外工作
- 喜欢数据分析和实地调查
- 有志于在矿业、环境或水务行业长期发展

**谁不适合学Hydrogeologist？**
- 不擅长物理、化学和数学
- 不喜欢野外工作和偏远地区

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a hydrogeologist?**
答：Entry-level AUD 70,000-90,000, mid-level AUD 95,000-130,000, senior AUD 140,000-180,000; mining sector pays higher.

**问：Is it easy for a hydrogeologist to migrate to Australia?**
答：Yes, it's on MLTSSL, eligible for 189/190/482 visas, with high demand, but requires EA skills assessment.

**问：What is the job outlook for hydrogeologists in Australia?**
答：Growing demand in mining, environmental consulting, and water management, especially in resource-rich states like WA and QLD.
