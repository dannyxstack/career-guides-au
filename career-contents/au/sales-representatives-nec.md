# Sales Representatives nec — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 611399 (ANZSCO)**

Sales Representative (Other) covers unclassified sales positions, responsible for promoting and selling products or services to businesses or consumers, maintaining customer relationships, and achieving sales targets.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Junior sales can advance to senior sales representative, sales supervisor, or regional manager, and then to sales director. Accumulating industry experience and customer resources is key.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated outbound calling and initial client screening (AI phone robot)
- Customer information entry and CRM data organisation (automation tools)
- Standardized product introductions and FAQ responses (chatbot)
- Sales email writing and basic follow-up (AI copywriting)
- Sales forecasting and report generation (AI analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer behavior analysis and precision recommendations (AI-driven needs mining)
- Personalized plan design and presentation (AI-generated draft)
- Sales Pitch Optimization & Real-time Prompts (AI Coach)
- Multi-channel customer reach strategy (AI predicts best channel)
- Smart review of contract terms and risk reminders (AI legal assistance)

**Human moat:**

- Building long-term client trust and emotional connection
- Complex B2B negotiation and objection handling
- Cross-departmental coordination and internal resource allocation
- Creative design of non-standard solutions
- Industry networking and relationship maintenance

**Skills to build:**

- CRM and sales automation tools (such as Salesforce Einstein)
- Data analysis and visualization (Python/SQL/Tableau)
- AI marketing copy generation and optimization (ChatGPT/Jasper)
- Video conference presentations and virtual sales techniques
- Social Media Sales and Personal Branding
- Advanced customer psychology and negotiation strategies.

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital sales
- B2B sales
- Customer relationship management
- E-commerce

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Good at communication and persuasion, with stress tolerance
- Goal-oriented, enjoys challenges and commission-based income
- Have a passion for sales and willingness to build long-term customer relationships.

**Who is not suited**
- Not good at dealing with people or rejection
- Prefer stable base salary over performance-based income.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the income of a sales representative?**
A: Income typically consists of base salary plus commission. Entry-level annual income approx. AUD 55,000-75,000; senior can exceed AUD 100,000.

**Q: Can a sales representative immigrate to Australia?**
A: ANZSCO 611399 is not on the CSOL or MLTSSL list, so it cannot qualify for mainstream skilled migration via 189/190/482. It may only be possible through DAMA or employer-sponsored individual cases.
