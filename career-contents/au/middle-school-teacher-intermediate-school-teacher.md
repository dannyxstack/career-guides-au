# Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher（Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：241311 – Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher。**

Middle school teachers educate students in grades 7-12, designing curricula, assessing learning outcomes, and supporting holistic development. The profession faces persistent skills shortages in Australia, offering stable employment.

## 2. 考证难度 / 从业资质

**考证难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 教学学士或硕士 | 澳大利亚大学 | 必备 | 需包含至少1年学科教学法学习 |

| 职业评估 | AITSL | 可选 | 移民必需，评估教学资质 |

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent Visa） | 适用于MLTSSL列表，无需担保，可永久居留 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 需州担保，多数州将中学教师列为优先职业 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 雇主担保，2-4年，可转186永居 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional Visa） | 偏远地区担保，转191永居，额外加分 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher？**
- 热爱青少年教育，有耐心和沟通能力
- 追求稳定职业，愿意在偏远地区任教
- 具备学术能力和跨文化适应力

**谁不适合学Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher？**
- 不喜与人互动或课堂管理
- 抗压能力弱，无法应对繁重文书工作

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：How does the salary progression work for middle school teachers?**
答：Salaries typically increase 2-4% annually per enterprise agreements. After reaching the top pay scale, significant raises require moving into leadership roles.

**问：What are the requirements for migrating to Australia as a middle school teacher?**
答：You need a positive AITSL skills assessment, IELTS 7.5 (L&S 8.0), an accredited teaching degree, and state nomination or skilled migration. Regional work offers additional points.

**问：What non-teaching career options are available for middle school teachers?**
答：Options include educational consultancy, curriculum development, edtech, teacher training, school administration, or roles in government education departments and NGOs.
