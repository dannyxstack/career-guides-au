# Wood and Wood Products Factory Worker（Wood and Wood Products Factory Worker）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：839413 – Wood and Wood Products Factory Worker。**

Performs sawing, planing, joining, and packing tasks in wood processing factories, operating woodworking machinery and handling raw materials and finished products.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在澳大利亚技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for wood factory workers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about AUD 42,000-50,000, senior up to AUD 60,000-70,000, with hourly rates around AUD 21-30.

**问：Can wood factory workers immigrate to Australia?**
答：Very unlikely. This occupation is not on any skilled migration list and cannot lead to PR via employer sponsorship, except possibly through DAMA in very remote areas.

**问：Are there career advancement opportunities for wood factory workers?**
答：Limited. Progression to machine operator, team leader or quality inspector is possible with experience. Transition to carpentry requires formal apprenticeship.
