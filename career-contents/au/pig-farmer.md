# Pig Farmer（Pig Farmer）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：121318 – Pig Farmer。**

Responsible for pig breeding, rearing, herd health management, and farm operations to ensure efficient pork production.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Pig Farmer？**
- 喜欢户外和体力劳动，愿意在偏远地区工作
- 对动物科学和养殖管理有热情

**谁不适合学Pig Farmer？**

- 对动物福利和生物安全要求缺乏耐心

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a pig farmer in Australia?**
答：Entry-level AUD 50,000-65,000, mid-level AUD 65,000-85,000, senior manager AUD 85,000-120,000, higher on large farms or self-owned.

**问：Which visas can a pig farmer use to migrate to Australia?**
答：Main pathways: state-nominated 190/491 (on CSOL with state nomination), employer-sponsored 482 to 186, and DAMA in some regions.

**问：Is there career progression for pig farmers?**
答：Yes, from stockperson to supervisor, farm manager, or specialization in nutrition, breeding, or biosecurity.
