# Medical Laboratory Technician（Medical Laboratory Technician）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：311213 – Medical Laboratory Technician。**

Medical Laboratory Technicians perform diagnostic tests in pathology, hematology, microbiology labs, assisting doctors in disease diagnosis and treatment monitoring. A vital role in Australia's healthcare system with stable demand.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 189（Skilled Independent visa） | 独立技术移民，需在MLTSSL列表上，且分数达标。医学实验室技师在MLTSSL上。 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated visa） | 州担保技术移民，获州政府担保可加分。大多数州担保此职业。 |
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage visa） | 雇主担保临时签证，需有雇主支持，可转186永居。 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme visa） | 雇主担保永居签证，通过直接申请或482转186。 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Medical Laboratory Technicians?**
答：Entry-level salary is around AUD 55,000-65,000, mid-level 65,000-80,000, senior up to 100,000. Public hospitals offer stability; private sector may pay more but competitive.

**问：Is it easy for Medical Laboratory Technicians to migrate to Australia?**
答：This occupation is on the MLTSSL, eligible for visas 189, 190, 482, 186. Requires AIMS skills assessment, IELTS 7.0. Currently in shortage, favorable migration prospects.

**问：What are the career progression paths?**
答：Technicians can advance to senior roles, lab supervisors or quality managers. Further study can lead to medical scientist or lab researcher positions.
