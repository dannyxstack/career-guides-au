# Cabler (Data and Telecommunications)（Cabler (Data and Telecommunications)）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：342411 – Cabler (Data and Telecommunications)。**

Cablers (Data and Telecommunications) install, maintain and repair telecommunications networks, data communication lines and fiber optic systems, including structured cabling in buildings.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 482（Temporary Skill Shortage Visa） | 需雇主担保，职业在CSOL上，可转186 |
| Subclass 186（Employer Nomination Scheme） | 雇主担保永居，需通过技能评估及三年工作经验 |
| Subclass 190（Skilled Nominated Visa） | 州担保永居，部分州对电信技工有需求 |
| Subclass 491（Skilled Work Regional Visa） | 偏远地区临时签证，满足居住要求后可转191永居 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for data and telecommunications cablers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about AUD 55k-70k, mid-level 70k-90k, senior up to 90k-120k, with overtime and remote projects increasing income.

**问：What are the skilled migration pathways for this occupation?**
答：Common pathways include 482 employer sponsorship leading to 186 PR, or 190/491 state nomination. Requires TRA skills assessment, English proficiency and qualifications.

**问：What is the future outlook for this occupation?**
答：Driven by 5G, NBN and data centre construction, demand continues to grow. Requires constant learning of fiber optics, wireless and other new technologies.
