# Cabler (Data and Telecommunications) — career analysis · Australia

**Occupation code: 342411 (ANZSCO)**

Data and telecommunications cable installers install, maintain, and repair telecom networks, data communication lines, and fiber optic systems, including structured cabling in buildings.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Start as an apprentice or assistant, gain experience, then advance to senior cabler, project supervisor, or move into higher technical roles such as network engineering or telecommunications engineering.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic fusion splicing and testing of fiber optic cables
- Use drones for line inspections
- AI-assisted fault diagnosis and localization
- Remote automatic configuration and debugging

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for line inspection guidance
- Using AI to analyze historical failure data to optimize maintenance plans
- Using digital twins to simulate construction impacts
- Enhance working at height safety through smart wearable devices

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency response (e.g., repairing downed lines after typhoon)
- Communicate and coordinate construction schedules with clients
- Manual wiring decisions in complex terrain
- Safety and compliance regulatory responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of automatic fiber fusion splicers
- Drone inspection and data analysis
- Use of AR/VR-assisted maintenance systems
- Application of AI diagnostic tools for network faults
- Digital Twin and BIM fundamentals.
- Communication security and regulatory certification

**Key growth areas:**

- 5G deployment
- NBN upgrades
- Data centre construction
- Smart building technologies

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on work and are willing to do outdoor/indoor wiring.
- Skilled trade professionals seeking stable skilled migration pathways
- Job seekers willing to develop long-term in the telecommunications industry and learn new technologies

**Who is not suited**
- Those who dislike physical labor or working at heights
- Those who want to completely avoid on-site work and prefer purely office-based jobs

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for data and telecommunications cablers?**
A: Entry-level annual salary: AUD 55,000-70,000; mid-level: AUD 70,000-90,000; senior: AUD 90,000-120,000; overtime and remote area projects can increase income.

**Q: What are the skilled migration pathways for this occupation?**
A: Common pathways include 482 employer sponsorship to 186 PR, or 190/491 state sponsorship. Requires TRA skills assessment, meeting English and academic requirements.

**Q: What is the future outlook for this occupation?**
A: Driven by 5G, NBN, and data center construction, demand continues to grow, technology updates quickly, requiring continuous learning of new technologies such as fiber optics and wireless.
