# Records Manager（Records Manager）职业分析 · 澳大利亚

**职业代码：224214 – Records Manager。**

Responsible for designing, implementing, and managing organizational records and information management systems to ensure compliance, efficiency, and secure document storage and retrieval.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Records Manager？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Records Managers?**
答：Salaries in Australia range from AUD 60,000 to 120,000 per year, with junior at 60-75k, mid at 75-95k, and senior at 95-120k.

**问：Can Records Manager migrate to Australia?**
答：Yes. It is on the CSOL, eligible for employer-sponsored visas (482/186) and state-nominated visa (190), subject to skills assessment.
