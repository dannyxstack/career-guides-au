# Professional Services Manager — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1349 (ISCO08)**

Professional Services Managers N.E.C. oversee operations and strategy in fields such as consulting, legal, accounting, architecture, and engineering. In Sweden, they typically lead teams, manage client relationships, and drive business development within firms that offer specialised expertise. These managers are crucial for maintaining service quality and profitability, and they often possess deep industry knowledge combined with strong leadership skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a relevant bachelor's or master's degree, followed by experience in a specialised area. Many professionals advance to managerial roles after several years of consulting or senior analysis. Further growth may lead to partner or director positions within firms, or to executive roles such as CEO. Continuous professional development and networking are essential for progression, and many pursue certifications like PMP or CFA.

- Generate customer reports and market analysis drafts
- Automated compliance checks and process monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Professional Services Manager in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary significantly by industry, location, and experience. Typically, managerial roles in professional services offer competitive compensation in line with collective agreements. For exact figures, consult industry surveys or job listings.

**Q: Can I migrate to Sweden as a Professional Services Manager?**
A: Yes, but requirements depend on your nationality. EU/EEA citizens can work freely. Non-EU/EEA citizens need an employer-tied work permit with a job offer meeting prevailing wage conditions. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles with a higher salary threshold. A job offer is mandatory.

**Q: Is a Swedish degree necessary for this role?**
A: No, foreign degrees are accepted if they are equivalent. However, understanding of Swedish business culture and language can be advantageous. Professional certifications may also boost employability.
