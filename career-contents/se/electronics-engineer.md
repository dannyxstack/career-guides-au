# Electronics Engineer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2152 (ISCO08)**

Electronics engineers in Sweden design, develop, and test electronic systems and components for a wide range of industries including telecommunications, automotive, medical technology, and industrial automation. The Swedish labour market offers strong opportunities due to a robust high-tech sector and ongoing digitalisation. Engineers often work in R&D departments, engineering consultancies, or manufacturing firms. The profession is regulated through collective agreements, and demand is particularly high for specialists in embedded systems, IoT, and renewable energy applications.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a bachelor's degree in electronics engineering, leading to junior design or testing roles. With experience, engineers can move into senior technical positions, project management, or specialised consulting. Many pursue a master's degree (Civilingenjör) to enhance their qualifications. The field offers opportunities in both large corporations (Ericsson, Volvo) and innovative startups. Continuous learning in areas like AI, IoT, and power electronics is essential for long-term career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use EDA software for schematic and PCB layout design and verification.
- Perform standardized electronic component testing and parameter verification
- Generate engineering documents and reports that meet standards
- Simple troubleshooting and repetitive debugging

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI simulation tools to quickly optimize circuit design parameters
- Predict product reliability based on ML analysis of test data
- Collaborates with digital twin systems for virtual prototype validation.
- Assisted technical literature search and patent analysis via NLP tools

**Human moat:**

- Innovative system architecture design and high-complexity mixed-signal circuit design
- Interdisciplinary integration (e.g., synergy between RF and embedded systems)
- Engineering judgment and trade-offs in extreme environments or reliability requirements
- High-level technical negotiations and decision-making with clients, suppliers, and regulators

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced simulation tools (e.g., ANSYS HFSS, Keysight ADS)
- Python scripts and AI models applied to design optimization
- Digital twin and system modeling methods
- Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and signal integrity design knowledge
- Project Management and Agile Development Practices

**Key growth areas:**

- Embedded Systems
- IoT
- Medical Electronics
- Renewable Energy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electronics engineer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are competitive and determined by collective agreements. They generally increase with experience, specialisation, and employer. Entry-level engineers earn a baseline set by unions, while senior roles command higher pay. The average is above the national median.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as an electronics engineer?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA engineers can obtain an employer-tied work permit if they have a job offer that meets the prevailing wage and terms set by collective agreements. EU Blue Cards are also available for those with a higher degree and higher salary. EU/EEA nationals have free movement.

**Q: Do I need to speak Swedish to work as an electronics engineer?**
A: Many companies, especially in tech, use English as their working language. However, learning Swedish is beneficial for integration and career advancement, particularly in smaller firms or client-facing roles.
