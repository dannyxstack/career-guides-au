# Health Care Assistant — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5321 (ISCO08)**

Health Care Assistants (undersköterskor) are integral to Sweden's healthcare system, providing direct patient care in hospitals, nursing homes, home care, and other settings. With an aging population, the demand for these workers is consistently high, and the occupation is classified as a shortage profession by the Swedish Public Employment Service. The role involves assisting with personal hygiene, medication administration, and monitoring patient well-being, often under the supervision of nurses. The profession offers stable employment, but requires formal training and licensing.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 227,370. Employment outlook for Health Care Assistants in Sweden remains very strong due to demographic trends and an increasing elderly population. The Swedish Public Employment Service forecasts a significant shortage over the next five to ten years, particularly in rural areas and in-home care services. The government has implemented initiatives to recruit and train more Undersköterskor, including fast-track vocational programs. However, the working conditions and pay are often cited as challenges, leading to high turnover. Overall, the sector offers job security for those entering the field.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for Health Care Assistants in Sweden typically involves specialization in areas such as dementia care, palliative care, or rehabilitation. Many choose to continue their studies to become registered nurses (sjuksköterskor) through bridging programs. Leadership roles as team leaders or unit supervisors are possible with experience and further education. Some assistants move into administrative or educational roles within healthcare. The profession also offers flexibility with part-time and shift work, though it can be physically and emotionally demanding.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic monitoring and recording of patient vital signs.
- Basic health data entry and electronic medical record updates
- Use AI tools for medication reminders and simple checks
- Partial patient communication and information inquiry (e.g., AI chatbot)
- Scheduling and materials management (optimized by AI systems)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic tools help identify early symptoms
- Smart wearable devices remotely monitor patients, allowing assistants to focus on emergencies
- AI translation tools improve communication with multilingual patients
- Automated document generation reduces paperwork time, increasing direct care
- Virtual reality simulation training enhances nursing skills.

**Human moat:**

- Providing emotional support and human care
- Performing complex personal care tasks such as bathing and feeding
- Quick decision-making and flexible response in emergency situations
- Builds trust relationships and team collaboration
- Handling unstructured patient needs and discomfort

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in AI-powered nursing record and monitoring systems
- Learn basic data analysis and health information technology
- Enhancing Communication and Cross-Cultural Nursing Skills
- Participating in virtual reality simulation training
- Obtain certification in aged care or rehabilitation nursing
- Learn psychological support and crisis intervention skills

**Key growth areas:**

- elder care
- home health care
- hospital care
- specialized dementia care

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Health Care Assistant in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by region and sector but generally follow collective agreements. Starting salaries are around 25,000 SEK per month, with increases for experience, night shifts, and specialization.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA citizen get a work permit as a Health Care Assistant?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer meeting collective agreement pay and terms. The occupation is on Sweden's shortage list, which may expedite the process. You must also have the required qualifications (e.g., recognized nursing assistant training).

**Q: What are the opportunities for career advancement?**
A: Many Health Care Assistants go on to study nursing via bridging programs, or specialize in areas like dementia care, home care, or rehabilitation. Leadership roles as team leaders are also possible with experience.
