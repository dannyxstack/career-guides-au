# Research and Development Manager — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1223 (ISCO08)**

Research and Development Managers (FoU-chefer) in Sweden lead teams that drive innovation across industries such as pharmaceuticals, automotive, ICT, and green technology. They are responsible for strategic planning, project management, budgeting, and aligning R&D activities with business goals. Sweden’s strong innovation ecosystem and high R&D expenditure create a steady demand for experienced managers who can navigate both technical and commercial aspects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically begins with a strong scientific or engineering background, often a PhD or Master’s plus industrial experience. Step-up roles include senior scientist, project manager, then R&D manager. Further advancement leads to director of R&D or CTO. Cross-functional experience and international mobility are valued. Many managers stay in the private sector, but some move to academia or government research agencies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated analysis of literature reviews and patent searches
- Recording experimental data and performing preliminary statistical analysis
- Automatic generation of project documents (e.g., progress reports, technical memos)
- Regular test case design and execution

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Feasibility analysis and solution design for R&D projects accelerated by AI
- Optimizes experimental parameters and resource allocation using AI prediction models
- AI-assisted team collaboration and knowledge management, improving cross-department communication efficiency
- Real-Time AI Market Intelligence Analysis Drives Innovation Direction Decisions

**Human moat:**

- Cross-disciplinary innovative thinking and strategic direction setting
- Complex technical decision-making and uncertainty risk management
- Deep customer needs insight and productization capability
- Team leadership and talent pipeline development

**Skills to build:**

- Deployment and use of AI tools (e.g., machine learning platforms, automated experiment systems)
- Data-driven decision making and statistical modeling
- AI project management and agile development methods
- Technology innovation commercialization and intellectual property strategy
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and systems thinking
- Basic programming (Python/R) and API integration

**Key growth areas:**

- digitalization
- sustainability
- life sciences
- green technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for R&D managers in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are competitive and vary by sector, company size, and location. They generally exceed the national average for managerial roles, with higher pay in pharmaceuticals and tech industries.

**Q: Can non-EU residents obtain a work visa as an R&D manager?**
A: Yes, through an employer-tied work permit or the EU Blue Card. The job must meet collective agreement standards and salary thresholds. The Blue Card requires a salary above approximately SEK 534,000 per year (2024) and a relevant university degree.

**Q: Is there a shortage of R&D managers in Sweden?**
A: Yes, particularly in high-tech sectors like ICT, pharmaceuticals, and green technology. This makes the occupation favorable for skilled migration, though competition for senior roles is significant.
