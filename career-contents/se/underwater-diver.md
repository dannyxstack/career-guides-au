# Underwater Diver — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7541 (ISCO08)**

Underwater divers perform tasks such as inspection, maintenance, construction, and salvage in underwater environments. In Sweden, the occupation is niche but vital for maritime industries, including offshore energy, port infrastructure, and aquaculture. Divers often work in cold waters and require specialized certifications. The labour market is stable but small, with most job openings in coastal regions like Gothenburg and Stockholm.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins with commercial diver training and certification, followed by specialization in areas like underwater welding, inspection, or ROV operation. Experienced divers may advance to supervisor or team leader roles, or transition into related fields such as hyperbaric medicine or diving instruction. Continuous physical fitness and recertification are required. The path is linear with limited vertical mobility; most divers remain in operational roles throughout their careers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.
- Routine structural measurement: ROV with sensors automatically collects data
- Underwater environment monitoring: AI analyzes parameters such as water temperature and turbidity
- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk
- Augmented reality headset: overlays structural diagrams and repair instructions to improve work accuracy
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes historical data to optimize maintenance schedules.
- Remote specialist collaboration: AI translation and 3D reconstruction help land-based experts guide

**Human moat:**

- Manual operation and quick judgment in emergency situations
- Fine tactile sensation and force feedback (e.g., valve operation)
- Adaptability in harsh environments (current, visibility)
- Legal and safety responsibility attribution (divers must sign on-site)

**Skills to build:**

- ROV/underwater robot operation and basic maintenance
- AI data analysis tools (e.g., Python image processing libraries)
- Underwater communication and AR system usage
- Advanced welding and cutting qualifications (e.g., underwater welder certificate)
- Diving medicine and physiological monitoring knowledge.
- Project management and team coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- Offshore Wind
- Underwater Inspection
- Salvage Operations
- Aquaculture

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an underwater diver in Sweden?**
A: Salaries for commercial divers vary by experience and sector. Entry-level divers may earn around 350,000–450,000 SEK annually, while experienced divers in offshore or inspection roles can earn 500,000–700,000 SEK. Overtime and hazardous pay can increase earnings. Always check current collective agreements for exact rates.

**Q: Can non-EU divers get a work permit for Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets prevailing wages. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the permit process is not prioritized. The employer must show that the position could not be filled locally. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: What training is required to become a diver in Sweden?**
A: Commercial divers need a recognized license (e.g., IMCA or equivalent) and must complete a certified training program. Swedish programs often include theory and practical dives. Medical fitness is mandatory. Some diving schools accept students with no prior experience.
