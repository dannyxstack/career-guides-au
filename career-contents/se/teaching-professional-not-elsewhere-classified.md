# Teaching Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

This category includes teaching professionals in Sweden who do not fit into more specific groups, such as vocational trainers, adult education instructors, and special education teachers in non-school settings. They work in folk high schools, municipal adult education (Komvux), vocational colleges, and private training providers. The role requires adapting teaching methods to diverse adult learners and often involves curriculum development. Employment is typically under collective agreements, with stable demand driven by lifelong learning policies and labor market retraining needs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a teaching professional in this category in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience but typically range from 320,000 to 450,000 SEK per year. Vocational teachers with in-demand skills may earn higher. Public sector roles follow collective agreements.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as a non-EU teaching professional?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer meeting collective agreement pay. The occupation is on the shortage list, which facilitates processing. An EU Blue Card may be possible if salary exceeds 540,000 SEK/year.

**Q: Do I need Swedish teaching credentials to work?**
A: A teaching license is mandatory for municipal adult education if you teach theoretical subjects, but for vocational subjects, relevant work experience and a vocational teacher qualification are often sufficient.
