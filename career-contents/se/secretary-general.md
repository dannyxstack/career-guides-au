# Secretary (General) — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4120 (ISCO08)**

General secretaries perform a variety of clerical and administrative tasks to support office operations. In Sweden, they work across all sectors, including business, government, and healthcare. The role involves managing correspondence, scheduling, record-keeping, and coordinating meetings. While automation has reduced demand for routine duties, secretaries remain essential for organizational efficiency and are often the first point of contact for clients and visitors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for secretaries in Sweden often involves moving into specialized assistant roles (e.g., executive assistant, legal secretary) or office management. With additional training, some transition to human resources, project coordination, or procurement. Digital skills and proficiency in Swedish are key for advancement. Many secretaries gain experience in one sector and then move to more senior administrative positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated meeting schedule coordination and conflict detection
- Call screening and standard response generation
- Document Formatting, Spell Checking, and Template Application
- Preliminary review of travel bookings and expense reimbursements
- Simple email classification and auto-reply

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing executive time allocation through AI schedule analysis
- Improving meeting minutes efficiency using voice transcription and summarization tools
- Generate administrative reports and insights using data analysis tools
- Using AI customer management system to enhance external contact management
- Automating workflow design to improve overall team efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Judgment and confidentiality skills when handling highly sensitive or confidential information
- Interpersonal coordination and decision-making in ambiguous or urgent situations
- Emotional intelligence and relationship management in cross-departmental communication
- Understanding and navigating informal power structures within an organization.
- Administrative tasks requiring creativity or personalized handling

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced office suite automation and scripting (e.g., VBA, Power Automate).
- Hands-on AI tools: meeting scheduling AI, speech-to-text, CRM system
- Data analysis basics (advanced Excel functions, BI tools)
- Project management basics (e.g., Agile methodology)
- Advanced communication and emotional intelligence (conflict resolution, executive support)
- Digital security and privacy protection awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalized workplaces
- Specialized sectors (legal, medical)
- Remote work
- Administrative coordination

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for secretaries in Sweden?**
A: Salaries for general secretaries in Sweden vary by sector, experience, and location, but typically align with collective agreement minimums. Entry-level roles may start around the threshold for work permits, while experienced secretaries in specialized fields can earn higher.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a secretary in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. A job offer with terms meeting collective agreements and a salary above the threshold (about 22,000 SEK/month) is needed. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so employers may need to show that no suitable EU candidate was found. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation.
