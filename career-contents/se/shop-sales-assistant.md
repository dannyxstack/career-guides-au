# Shop Sales Assistant — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5223 (ISCO08)**

Shop sales assistants, known as butikssäljare in Sweden, work in retail stores assisting customers with purchases, restocking shelves, and handling payments. The occupation is a common entry point into the labour market, often filled by students and part-time workers. The retail sector employs a significant share of the workforce, but faces structural changes due to e-commerce growth and digitalization. Despite these challenges, traditional stores remain central to Swedish commerce, especially in specialized retail. The role demands strong customer service skills, flexibility with working hours, and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 127,877. The employment outlook for shop sales assistants in Sweden is stable but not growing. E-commerce expansion continues to reduce demand in some segments, while physical stores adapt with omnichannel strategies. Opportunities are more favorable in specialized retail and larger cities, with a high turnover rate creating openings. The occupation is not on any shortage list, and competition for full-time positions can be moderate. Overall, the number of jobs is expected to remain relatively constant with a slight decline over the long term.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for shop sales assistants in Sweden typically involves moving into supervisory roles such as department manager or store manager. Some transition to central functions like purchasing, merchandising, or e-commerce roles with additional training. Others may build careers in customer service or logistics within retail companies. However, many positions are part-time or seasonal, limiting upward mobility without further education. Vocational qualifications in retail can enhance prospects, and some employers offer internal advancement programs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a shop sales assistant in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary by sector and employer. The average monthly salary is around 23,000–27,000 SEK for full-time work, but part-time pay is pro-rated. Entry-level positions may start lower.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit for a shop sales assistant role?**
A: Generally no, because the salary does not meet the work permit requirement (at least 28,480 SEK/month in 2024). Exceptions might apply for shortages, but retail is not on the shortage list.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop sales assistants?**
A: With experience, you can progress to team leader, department manager, or store manager. Some move into purchasing, visual merchandising, or e-commerce roles with additional training.
