# Clearing And Forwarding Agent — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3331 (ISCO08)**

Clearing and forwarding agents coordinate and manage the shipment of goods through customs and transportation networks, ensuring compliance with import/export regulations. In Sweden, this occupation supports the country's heavy reliance on international trade, with major ports like Gothenburg and Stockholm as key hubs. Agents work for freight forwarders, logistics firms, or manufacturing companies, handling documentation, customs clearance, and route planning. The role requires knowledge of Swedish customs regulations, EU trade policies, and digital tracking systems. Demand is steady due to Sweden's export-oriented economy, with opportunities in both urban centers and regional logistics clusters.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a junior agent or administrative role, advancing to senior agent or team leader, then to logistics manager, branch manager, or supply chain director. Many professionals gain specialized expertise in commodity sectors such as automotive, pharmaceuticals, or forest products. Further certifications, such as a customs broker license or supply chain management degrees, enhance mobility. Switching to carrier-side roles or consulting is also common. Digitalization and sustainability compliance create new niches for experienced agents.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate and check standardized documents such as customs declarations, bills of lading, commercial invoices, etc.
- Use OCR and NLP technologies to automatically extract and input cargo information and document data
- Basic trade compliance checks (e.g., automatic HS code classification, rules of origin verification)
- Real-time tracking of cargo status with automatic system updates and notifications to relevant parties
- Generate standardised transport reports, statistical statements and trade data summaries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI tools to quickly retrieve and interpret the latest trade regulations, tariff changes, and sanction lists of various countries
- AI-assisted analysis of supply chain risks (e.g., port congestion, route disruptions) and recommendation of alternatives
- Use smart chatbots to efficiently handle routine inquiries from customers/freight forwarders about document status and customs clearance progress.
- Use AI to optimize multimodal transport route combinations, balancing cost, time, and compliance requirements

**Human moat:**

- Skills in handling complex, non-standard, or disputed customs clearance anomalies (e.g., customs checks, classification disputes), including negotiation and resolution.
- Maintain long-term trust relationships and industry networks with freight forwarders, clients, and customs officials
- Intuitive judgment and experiential reliance on hidden risks in international trade (e.g., political sanctions, exchange rate fluctuations).
- Develop and implement enterprise-level import/export compliance strategies, train teams to adapt to new regulatory changes

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using AI modules in mainstream international trade management software (e.g., Descartes, eCustoms)
- Master the basics of EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) system configuration and troubleshooting
- New ability to interpret regulatory compliance data (e.g., deciphering customs AI pre-ruling logic)
- Operation and alert interpretation of supply chain visibility platforms (e.g. Project44, FourKites)
- Basic Python or RPA skills for customized data integration
- AI-assisted negotiation and conflict resolution skills (using AI to simulate customs clearance scenarios)

**Key growth areas:**

- digitalization
- e-commerce logistics
- green supply chains
- customs automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a clearing and forwarding agent in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. According to collective agreements, entry-level agents earn around 24,000–27,000 SEK/month, while senior agents with 5+ years can earn 30,000–35,000 SEK/month. Additional overtime and bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as a clearing and forwarding agent without a university degree?**
A: Yes, based on the occupation's skill level (SSYK 2012 level 3), a vocational education in logistics or equivalent experience is typically accepted. The employer must offer at least the minimum salary required by the work permit rules.

**Q: Is clearing and forwarding agent on Sweden's shortage list?**
A: The Swedish Public Employment Service does not currently list this occupation as a national shortage, but regional shortages exist, especially in ports and logistics hubs. Employers may still recruit from abroad if unable to find local candidates.
