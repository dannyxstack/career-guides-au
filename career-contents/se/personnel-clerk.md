# Personnel Clerk — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4416 (ISCO08)**

Personnel clerks handle administrative tasks in human resources, such as maintaining employee records, processing payroll, and assisting with recruitment. In Sweden, they are employed across all sectors, often in larger firms or public agencies. The role requires accuracy and familiarity with Swedish labour law and collective agreements.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a personnel clerk in Sweden?**
A: Salaries for personnel clerks in Sweden vary by sector, experience, and location. Generally, they are determined by collective agreements, with entry-level positions earning around 25,000–30,000 SEK per month before tax. Experienced clerks may earn 30,000–38,000 SEK. Always check the relevant collective bargaining agreement for exact figures.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA citizen get a work permit as a personnel clerk in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but the employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA candidate. The job offer must meet the terms of the applicable collective agreement, including salary and insurance. The permit is tied to that employer.

**Q: What training is needed to become a personnel clerk in Sweden?**
A: Upper secondary education in administration is common, but many enter via adult education (Komvux) or vocational courses. A background in business administration or HR is advantageous. Formal certifications are not mandatory but can improve job prospects.
