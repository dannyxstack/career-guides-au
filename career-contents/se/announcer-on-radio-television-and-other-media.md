# Announcers On Radio, Television And Other Media — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2656 (ISCO08)**

Announcers on radio, television and other media present programs, read news, conduct interviews, and host shows across various platforms in Sweden. The role is primarily concentrated in public service broadcasters like Sveriges Radio and SVT, as well as commercial stations and digital media companies. Swedish-language fluency is essential, and many positions are in Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö. The field is competitive, with a mix of permanent and freelance roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts with smaller local stations or community radio, moving to larger public or commercial broadcasters. Experienced announcers may advance to program manager, editor, or move into digital content strategy. Many also diversify into podcasting, voice-over work, or event hosting. Continuous development in social media, audio editing, and live production is valuable. Swedish media unions (e.g., SJF) offer support and training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated news reporting (e.g., sports scores, financial reports, weather reports)
- Pre-recorded standard program introductions and linking words
- Simple on-site commentary (e.g. standardised event or ceremony procedures)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Real-time interactive script generation and adjustment (AI provides suggestions based on audience feedback)
- Personalized content recommendations (AI analyzes listener preferences, customizes broadcast highlights)
- Multilingual Simultaneous Interpretation and Dubbing (AI-assisted Real-time Translation)
- Voice cloning backup (AI-generated backup voice to prevent sudden voice loss)

**Human moat:**

- Emotional deep communication and improvisation (e.g., capturing touching moments in interviews)
- Brand persona building and trust establishment (loyalty to authentic voice of the audience)
- Complex topic debate and value judgment (content requiring stance on politics, ethics, etc.)
- Cross-platform community management and fan interaction

**Skills to build:**

- AI broadcasting tool operation and prompt design
- Multi-platform content planning and data analysis
- Video/audio editing and visual storytelling
- Social Media Operations and Personal Brand Building
- Real-time audience interaction skills and improvisation

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Media
- Podcasting
- Streaming
- Local Content

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an announcer in Sweden?**
A: Starting salaries for announcers at public service broadcasters are around SEK 25,000–30,000 per month. Experienced announcers may earn SEK 35,000–50,000, depending on employer and platform. Freelancers vary widely.

**Q: Can I work as an announcer in Sweden with a work permit tied to one employer?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA citizens can apply for an employer-tied work permit if they have a job offer that meets collective agreement standards. The permit is tied to that employer, and changing jobs requires a new permit.
