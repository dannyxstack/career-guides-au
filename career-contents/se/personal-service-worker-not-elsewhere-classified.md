# Funeral Director — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5169 (ISCO08)**

This occupation involves arranging and overseeing funeral services, providing emotional support to bereaved families, and handling administrative and ceremonial tasks. In Sweden, funeral directors must comply with strict regulations and health standards. The role requires a blend of empathy, organizational skills, and attention to detail. The labour market is stable due to consistent demand from an aging population, though regional variations exist.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a funeral director in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions typically range from 25,000 to 30,000 SEK per month, while experienced directors can earn 35,000–45,000 SEK. Additional benefits may include pension and insurance.

**Q: Can a non-EU funeral director work in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but they need a work permit sponsored by a Swedish funeral home. The job must meet collective agreement standards, and the employer must show that the position cannot be filled from within the EU/EEA. The permit is tied to the employer.

**Q: Is the funeral director profession regulated in Sweden?**
A: Yes, funeral directors must hold a license from the Swedish Funeral Directors Association (SBF). They must complete vocational training or a relevant degree, pass an exam, and fulfill practical experience requirements.
