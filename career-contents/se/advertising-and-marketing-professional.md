# Advertising and Marketing Professional — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2431 (ISCO08)**

Advertising and Marketing Professionals in Sweden develop and implement strategies to promote products and services, manage brand reputation, and drive customer engagement. The role spans digital marketing, content creation, market research, and campaign management. In Sweden, the profession is concentrated in Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö, with growing demand for digital and data-driven skills. Most positions require a bachelor's degree in marketing, business, or communication. The labour market values proficiency in Swedish and English, as well as experience with analytics tools and CRM systems. The sector is dynamic, with a shift toward online channels and personalized marketing.

Competition for roles is strong in major cities, but opportunities exist in B2B and tech companies. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts from marketing coordinator or assistant roles, advancing to specialist positions (e.g., digital marketer, brand manager), then to manager or director levels. Many professionals specialize in areas like SEO, paid advertising, or analytics. Some move into consultancy or start their own agencies. Continuous learning is crucial due to evolving tools and platforms. Advanced degrees or certifications (e.g., Google Analytics, HubSpot) enhance prospects. Networking and a strong portfolio are key for upward mobility.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Advertising optimization and bid adjustment based on historical data
- Social Media Content Scheduling and Basic Posting
- Structured data collection and preliminary analysis reports in market research
- A/B testing plan execution and basic result statistics
- Automated email marketing sending and basic performance tracking

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI prediction models for consumer behavior insights and market trend forecasting
- Use generative AI to rapidly iterate multiple versions of creative assets and perform sentiment analysis
- Design cross-channel user journeys via AI-driven personalization engines
- Monitor real-time AI delivery system decisions and adjust brand strategy boundaries
- Integrate multi-source data to build a brand health dashboard to assist high-level decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Brand narrative and emotional resonance strategy development
- Cross-cultural market insights integrated with localized creativity
- Ethical judgment and brand value balance in high-risk decisions.
- Build long-term trust relationships with C-level clients.
- Global strategies integrating non-quantifiable factors (e.g., social sentiment)

**Skills to build:**

- Python or R for data analysis and automated script writing
- Operations on major AI marketing platforms (e.g., Google AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Prompt engineering and fine-tuning large models for creative content generation
- Data visualization tools (Tableau/Power BI) and reporting skills
- Basic machine learning concepts and model evaluation methods
- Omnichannel marketing attribution modeling and experimental design skills

**Key growth areas:**

- digital marketing
- content marketing
- data-driven marketing
- brand strategy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Advertising and Marketing Professional in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level positions (0–2 years) start around 300,000–400,000 SEK/year. Mid-level professionals (3–5 years) earn 400,000–500,000 SEK/year, and senior roles (6+ years) can reach 500,000–600,000 SEK/year or more. Salaries are highest in Stockholm and in tech/consulting firms.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to immigrate as an Advertising and Marketing Professional?**
A: Yes, for non-EU/EEA citizens a valid job offer with a salary meeting the collective agreement (minimum around 30,000 SEK/month) is required for a work permit. The employer must advertise the position in Sweden and the EU first. EU/EEA citizens have free movement and do not need a permit.

**Q: Is advertising and marketing on Sweden's shortage list?**
A: Marketing professionals are not currently on Sweden's official shortage list for work permits. However, digital marketing specialists with technical skills (e.g., SEO, analytics) are in high demand and may face less competition.
