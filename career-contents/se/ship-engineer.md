# Ships' Engineer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3151 (ISCO08)**

Ships' Engineers operate, maintain, and repair the engines, boilers, and mechanical systems of ships. In Sweden, this occupation is vital to the maritime sector, encompassing ferries, cargo vessels, and cruise ships in the Baltic Sea. The role requires technical expertise and certification under the STCW convention. The labour market is niche but stable, with demand driven by an aging workforce and increasing maritime trade. Automation and green shipping are shaping the occupation, requiring continuous skill updates.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant engineer, advancing to engineer, then chief engineer. Experienced professionals may transition to shore-based roles such as technical superintendent, marine surveyor, or instructor. Specialization in areas like automation or green technologies can enhance prospects. Continuous professional development is essential, and many engineers pursue further education in naval architecture or marine management to move into leadership or consultancy.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine voyage data recording and report generation
- Basic meteorological route planning and optimization
- Marine machinery condition monitoring and early warning
- Cargo loading calculation and balance adjustment
- Communication relay and standard message forwarding

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted navigation decisions to improve flight safety and fuel efficiency
- Real-time weather and sea condition forecasting, supporting dynamic adjustment of speed and route
- Automated fault diagnosis and predictive maintenance to reduce downtime
- Intelligent Cargo Monitoring Systems to Improve Stowage Accuracy and Safety
- Augmented reality (AR) assisted navigation at night and in adverse weather

**Human moat:**

- Ship handling and emergency response capabilities in crisis situations
- Team management and crew leadership (especially captain duties)
- Special cargo handling and complex port operations experience
- Regulatory compliance and maritime safety inspection responsibilities
- Flexible judgment in unpredictable marine environments

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI navigation and decision support systems
- Data analysis and interpretation of predictive models
- Remote operation and automated system monitoring
- Cybersecurity basics (ship system protection)
- STCW certification renewal (including E-navigation module)
- Cross-cultural communication and team management

**Key growth areas:**

- green shipping
- automation
- offshore wind
- digitalization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Ships' Engineer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary widely by experience, vessel type, and rank. Entry-level engineers earn around 25,000 SEK/month, while chief engineers can earn over 50,000 SEK/month. Average annual salary ranges from 420,000 to 600,000 SEK.

**Q: Do I need a work visa to work as a Ships' Engineer in Sweden?**
A: EU/EEA citizens do not need a visa. Non-EU citizens require a job offer and a work permit. The process is typically straightforward due to the occupation being on Sweden’s shortage list.

**Q: What education is required to become a Ships' Engineer in Sweden?**
A: A vocational diploma (2 years) or a bachelor's degree in marine engineering (3 years) is required, along with STCW certification. Programs are tuition-free for EU/EEA students.
