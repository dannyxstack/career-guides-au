# Data Entry Clerk — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4132 (ISCO08)**

Data entry clerks in Sweden primarily work in administrative roles across both public and private sectors, entering and updating information in databases, spreadsheets, and other systems. The role is often entry-level or part-time, with many positions in temporary staffing. Automation and digitalisation are reducing demand for pure data entry, but the occupation still exists in niche areas like healthcare records, logistics, and customer databases. Most clerks work in offices, but remote work is growing. Swedish language skills are usually required.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for data entry clerks in Sweden typically involves moving into more administrative or specialised roles, such as accounting assistant, office coordinator, or IT support, after gaining experience and additional training. Some may advance to supervisor positions in data processing teams. Upskilling in software (e.g., Excel, SAP) and learning Swedish administrative routines are common steps. However, many use this role as a stepping stone to higher education or other fields, as the job itself offers limited advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually enter data from paper or electronic documents into databases or spreadsheets
- Check format and basic accuracy of input data
- Repetitive batch data entry, such as invoices and forms
- Basic data cleaning and deduplication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses AI tools to automatically extract document data, with humans handling exceptions.
- Use automated scripts to verify data consistency and improve efficiency
- Analyze data trends and generate reports through AI-assisted analysis

**Human moat:**

- Complex data judgment and anomaly handling capability
- Client communication and business understanding (e.g., explaining data deviations)
- Cross-system data integration and process optimization
- Maintain data privacy and security compliance

**Skills to build:**

- Learn Python or VBA for automated data processing
- Proficiency in advanced Excel functions (e.g., Power Query, macros)
- Familiar with OCR and RPA tools (e.g., UiPath)
- Data quality management and database basics (SQL)
- Basic data analysis and visualization (e.g., Tableau)

**Key growth areas:**

- Administrative automation
- Digital record-keeping
- Healthcare data entry
- Remote clerical work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a data entry clerk in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are modest, typically starting around the lowest decile for clerical workers, with limited progression. Most positions pay between the minimum collective agreement levels and the median for administrative assistants. Overtime and bonuses are rare.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a data entry clerk?**
A: It is possible but difficult because the wage often falls below the threshold for permanent residence permit (currently around 80% of median wage). The job must meet collective agreement pay, and the employer must advertise within the EU first. Many data entry roles are part-time or temporary, which complicates permit renewal.

**Q: What career progression is possible for data entry clerks?**
A: Common next steps are office coordinator, accounting assistant, or IT support roles. Some move into data quality or database management after additional training. Formal education in business or IT can accelerate advancement.
