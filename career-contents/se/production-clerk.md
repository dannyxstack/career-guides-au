# Production Clerk — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4322 (ISCO08)**

Production clerks coordinate and monitor manufacturing processes, ensuring production targets are met while maintaining quality and safety standards. In Sweden, they are employed across diverse sectors such as automotive, pharmaceuticals, electronics, and food processing, often working in teams with engineers and supply chain specialists.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically leads to roles such as production manager, supply chain planner, or logistics coordinator. Many production clerks enhance their skills through further vocational training or higher education in industrial engineering. Mobility across industries is common, and experience in lean production or ERP systems is highly valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual entry of production data and inventory updates
- Routine order processing and document verification
- Basic report generation and ledger maintenance
- Standard production progress tracking and recording
- Basic calculation of material requirements

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI prediction tools to optimise production scheduling and material allocation
- Quickly query historical production data via natural language interface
- Use anomaly detection systems to warn supply chain risks in advance
- Adjusting production parameters using digital twin simulations
- Automated generation of multilingual compliance documents and reports

**Human moat:**

- Cross-departmental communication and supplier negotiation skills
- Emergency coordination for unexpected issues on the production floor
- Experience-based judgment and decision-making for non-standard anomalies
- Teaching and guiding new employees on manual processes.
- Understand hidden production bottlenecks and propose improvement plans

**Skills to build:**

- Intermediate or higher Excel/Google Sheets automation skills
- Advanced functions of enterprise resource planning systems (e.g., SAP, Oracle)
- Basic Python/SQL for data cleaning and reporting
- Supply chain management basics (e.g., inventory turnover, safety stock setting)
- Process improvement methodologies (Lean/Six Sigma basics).
- Operation of mainstream AI scheduling tools (e.g., Planview, Siemens Opcenter)

**Key growth areas:**

- automation
- digitalization
- lean production
- sustainability

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a production clerk in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by industry and experience, but are generally set by collective agreements. Entry-level positions start around 25,000 SEK/month, while experienced clerks earn up to 35,000 SEK/month.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA national work as a production clerk in Sweden?**
A: Yes, via an employer-tied work permit if the job offer meets collective-agreement pay. The EU Blue Card is also an option if the salary threshold is met. Annual quotas and processing times apply.

**Q: Is the demand for production clerks expected to decrease due to automation?**
A: While some routine tasks may be automated, the role is evolving to require more analytical and supervisory skills. Demand remains stable in advanced manufacturing sectors.
