# Electronics Engineering Technician — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3114 (ISCO08)**

Electronics engineering technicians assist engineers in designing, testing, and maintaining electronic systems and equipment. In Sweden, they are employed in industries such as telecommunications, automotive, industrial automation, and green technology. The role requires practical skills in troubleshooting, soldering, and using measurement instruments. With the growth of IoT and digitalization, demand is stable in tech clusters like Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö. Many technicians have vocational or higher education qualifications, and Swedish language is often required for client-facing roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from a vocational or associate degree, advancing to senior technician, team leader, or project coordinator roles. Further education, such as a bachelor's in electronics engineering, can lead to design or management positions. Specializations in automation, embedded systems, or renewable energy enhance prospects. Certifications from authorities like Swedish Certification Board are valued. Some technicians transition into sales or technical support.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated test case generation and execution
- Standard circuit board fault diagnosis report writing
- Component selection and parameter matching
- Organization and translation of production documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quickly verify circuit designs using AI simulation tools
- Assist in complex fault diagnosis using intelligent diagnostic systems.
- AI-assisted optimization of PCB layout and signal integrity
- Reliability prediction based on historical data

**Human moat:**

- Complex system-level fault analysis and debugging
- Cross-departmental communication and project coordination
- Innovative circuit architecture design
- Compliance verification of safety-critical systems

**Skills to build:**

- Embedded Systems Development

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an electronics engineering technician in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level positions start around 28,000 SEK per month, with experienced technicians earning 35,000–40,000 SEK. Senior or specialized roles can reach 45,000 SEK or more. Salaries vary by region and industry, with higher pay in Stockholm and Gothenburg.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as an electronics technician without a university degree?**
A: Yes, vocational training or relevant work experience suffices. The job offer must meet collective-agreement wages, and the employer typically handles the application. Being on the shortage list may speed up the process.

**Q: Is electronics technician listed on Sweden's shortage occupation list?**
A: Yes, it frequently appears due to demand in tech industries. This does not guarantee a permit but can streamline applications and exempt from labor market testing.
