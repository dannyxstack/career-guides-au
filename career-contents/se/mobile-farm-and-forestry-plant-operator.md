# Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operator — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 8341 (ISCO08)**

Mobile farm and forestry plant operators in Sweden handle tractors, harvesters, forwarders, and other machinery for agricultural and forestry operations. The work is seasonal, often in rural areas, and requires physical stamina and mechanical skill. Many operators are employed by large farms, forestry companies, or contractors. The sector is increasingly adopting precision agriculture and automated systems, but traditional operator roles remain vital for Swedish food and timber production.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 4,610. Employment in this occupation is stable with moderate demand due to mechanization and retirements. The Swedish Forest Agency and agricultural organizations report a need for skilled operators, especially those familiar with modern GPS-guided machinery. However, automation may reduce long-term demand. Seasonal variations and regional shortages exist, particularly in northern Sweden. Overall outlook: balanced, with some openings for experienced operators.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an assistant or trainee, advancing to independent operator of larger machinery. Experienced operators can become team leaders, machinery managers, or start their own contracting businesses. Specialization in forestry versus farming is common. Further training in precision agriculture or heavy vehicle mechanics opens additional opportunities. Many operators take short courses to operate specific brands or new technology.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Path planning and driving control for autonomous tractors or harvesters
- Automatic seeding and fertilizing operations based on GPS and sensor data
- Use AI visual systems to automatically identify crop maturity and perform harvesting
- Use drones to automatically inspect farmland and generate work instructions

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted fault diagnosis system helps identify mechanical abnormalities and suggests repair steps
- Real-time data analytics to optimise fuel consumption and work routes
- Remote expert support system guides operations with AR overlay information
- Predictive maintenance alerts reduce unplanned downtime

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment and decision-making in complex terrain and unexpected weather
- Manual skills for on-site equipment repair and mechanical adjustments
- Unstructured communication and coordination skills with base and team
- Intuition and experience in dealing with biological factors (such as pests and diseases, livestock behavior)

**Skills to build:**

- Agricultural drone operation and data analysis
- GPS/autonomous driving system calibration and maintenance.
- Basic programming and data interpretation (e.g., Python/R)
- Operation of precision agriculture software (e.g., Farm Management System)
- Mechatronic repair and troubleshooting
- Use of digital twins and remote collaboration tools

**Key growth areas:**

- forestry
- sustainable agriculture
- automation
- bioenergy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mobile farm and forestry plant operator in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and region, but for 2025 the median monthly salary is around SEK 28,000–32,000, with experienced operators earning up to SEK 38,000. Forestry operators often earn slightly more than agricultural ones. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU migrants get a work permit as a mobile farm and forestry plant operator?**
A: Yes, but it is not a shortage occupation, so the employer must show that the job was advertised in Sweden/EU first. The job offer must meet Swedish collective agreement wages (minimum SEK 28,000/month in 2025). Residence permit is granted for the duration of the employment.
