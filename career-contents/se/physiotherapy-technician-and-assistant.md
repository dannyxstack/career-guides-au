# Physiotherapy Technician and Assistant — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3255 (ISCO08)**

Physiotherapy technicians and assistants in Sweden work under the supervision of licensed physiotherapists to support patient rehabilitation, exercise programs, and therapeutic treatments. They are employed in hospitals, primary care centers, rehabilitation clinics, and elderly care facilities. The role requires strong communication skills and physical stamina. The occupation is part of the healthcare sector, with growing demand due to an aging population and increased focus on preventive care and rehabilitation.

Career progression typically involves gaining experience and specializing in areas such as geriatrics, sports medicine, or neurology. ## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals with a strong interest in healthcare and helping others recover from injuries or illnesses.
- Team players who can follow instructions and work closely with physiotherapists and other healthcare professionals.
- Physically active people comfortable with manual tasks, standing for long periods, and assisting patients with mobility.

**Who is not suited**
- Those preferring sedentary desk jobs or minimal patient interaction.
- Individuals uncomfortable with handling bodily fluids or working with elderly or disabled patients.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a physiotherapy technician and assistant in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer, but as a guide, entry-level positions pay around 25,000-30,000 SEK per month according to Statistics Sweden (SCB). Experienced assistants can earn up to 35,000 SEK. This occupation is not subject to salary thresholds for work permits, but the offer must meet collective agreement levels.

**Q: Can non-EU/EEA nationals get a work permit as a physiotherapy technician and assistant in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is not a fast-tracked occupation. The employer must advertise the job in Sweden and EU/EEA first (labor market testing). The job offer must provide at least the collective agreement salary and terms. Work permits are issued for the duration of employment, typically 1-2 years, renewable.

**Q: What training is required to work as a physiotherapy technician and assistant in Sweden?**
A: Typically a 1-2 year vocational program in healthcare with a focus on physiotherapy assistance. Programs are offered at upper secondary level or as post-secondary vocational education (Yrkeshögskola). Some employers provide on-the-job training, but formal qualifications are preferred.
