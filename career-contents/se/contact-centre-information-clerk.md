# Contact Centre Information Clerks — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4222 (ISCO08)**

Contact centre information clerks in Sweden handle customer inquiries, provide information, and resolve issues via phone, email, and chat. They work in various sectors including telecommunications, banking, and public services. The role requires strong communication skills and often Swedish language proficiency. The Swedish labour market for this occupation is stable, with many positions concentrated in large cities and some remote opportunities.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression from contact centre information clerk often leads to team leader, quality assurance, or training roles. Some move into customer relationship management, sales, or IT support. With additional education, one can advance to specialist roles like complaint handling or technical support. Swedish language skills are crucial for advancement. Many employers offer internal training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Answer standard FAQ questions (e.g. account balance, business hours)
- Handling simple order changes, returns, and exchange requests
- Transfer complex complaints to human agents
- Record and update customer information in the CRM system
- Send standard confirmation emails or SMS notifications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time translation, supporting multilingual customer service
- Emotion recognition alerts to help agents adjust communication strategies
- Smart knowledge base recommendations for quickly locating solutions to complex issues
- Automatic call summary generation reduces post-call documentation time
- Predict customer intent and prepare response scripts in advance

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly emotional, sensitive, or sudden complaints
- Coordinate across departments to solve complex non-standard issues.
- Build customer trust and maintain long-term relationships
- Respond to unforeseen emergencies (e.g., system failures)
- Deliver personalized, empathetic service experiences

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in major AI customer service platforms (e.g., Zendesk AI, Salesforce Einstein)
- Learn basic data analysis (SQL/Excel) to analyze customer trends.
- Enhance conflict management and emotional communication skills
- Master the principles of Conversational Design
- Understand automation process building tools (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere)
- Develop multilingual skills (especially English) to serve international clients

**Key growth areas:**

- omnichannel support
- customer experience
- remote work
- AI-assisted service

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a contact centre information clerk in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary by sector and experience. Starting salaries are usually around the national minimum for the industry, with increases based on tenure and performance.

**Q: Can I get a work permit for this job?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA citizens can obtain an employer-tied work permit if they have a job offer that meets the collective agreement salary and conditions. A labour market test may be required.

**Q: Do I need to speak Swedish?**
A: Swedish is generally required for customer-facing roles, though some international companies may accept English only for certain positions.
