# Driving Instructor — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5165 (ISCO08)**

Driving instructors in Sweden teach traffic theory, defensive driving, and practical skills to learner drivers, primarily for category B licenses. They work at driving schools or as freelancers and must hold a state-issued license from Transportstyrelsen. The occupation offers stable demand due to high licensing rates among young adults and immigrants, with moderate competition in urban areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression includes specialization in truck, bus, or motorcycle instruction, or eco-driving. Many instructors open their own driving schools or become examiners at Transportstyrelsen. Continuing education is available for those seeking advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic driving operation instruction (e.g., starting, gear shifting) is automated by simulators.
- Theoretical exam question bank explanations replaced by AI interactive system
- Basic assessment of student performance (e.g., speed control) generates reports automatically via sensors and algorithms.
- Standard driving test route teaching is covered by GPS and virtual environments

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyse learner driving data, providing personalised weak spot training plans
- Using augmented reality (AR) to simulate complex road conditions (e.g., rain, snow, night) for safety training
- AI-assisted real-time feedback system to enhance trainees' perception and reaction training
- Optimize teaching processes with big data, predict student pass rates and adjust strategies

**Human moat:**

- Non-verbal communication and emotional guidance to alleviate student anxiety and build trust
- Immediate judgment and intervention in emergencies (e.g., sudden operational errors by trainees)
- Teaching defensive driving and ethical decision-making (e.g., accident avoidance ethics)
- Unpredictable interactive training in real road environments (e.g., pedestrians, animals)

**Skills to build:**

- AI teaching platforms and data interpretation skills
- Operating virtual reality (VR/AR) teaching tools
- Behavioral psychology and personalized instructional design
- Advanced driving safety certification (e.g., defensive driving expert)
- Digital content creation (creating interactive tutorials)
- Remote teaching and online instruction techniques.

**Key growth areas:**

- Eco-driving
- Electric vehicle instruction
- Simulator training
- Multilingual instruction

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is the salary for driving instructors determined?**
A: Salaries are negotiated individually based on experience, qualifications, and region. A collective agreement provides a minimum floor for employees at driving schools.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens become driving instructors in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but they need a work permit sponsored by a driving school and must meet certification requirements, including obtaining a Swedish driving instructor license.

**Q: What training is required to become a driving instructor?**
A: The standard route is a two-year vocational program (yrkeshögskola) in traffic instruction, followed by a license from Transportstyrelsen.
