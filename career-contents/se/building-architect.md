# Building Architect — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2161 (ISCO08)**

Building architects in Sweden design and plan buildings, ensuring functionality, safety, aesthetics, and compliance with strict building codes. They work in private architectural firms, government agencies, or as self-employed professionals. The profession is regulated, requiring a Master's degree in Architecture from a recognized university, followed by a period of supervised practice and registration with the Swedish National Board of Housing, Building and Planning (Boverket) to use the protected title 'arkitekt'. The Swedish market is shaped by urbanization, sustainability demands, and digitalization (BIM), with opportunities in renovation, green building, and urban densification. Employment is sensitive to construction cycles, but long-term demand remains stable due to infrastructure needs and climate adaptation.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 3,947. The employment outlook for architects in Sweden is stable but competitive, with moderate demand linked to construction activity. The Swedish Public Employment Service reports no general shortage, but there is demand for specialists in sustainable design, energy-efficient buildings, and BIM expertise. Urban growth in Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö drives projects, while renovation and retrofitting of older buildings offer steady work. Digital skills and knowledge of Swedish building regulations (BBR) are critical. Entry-level competition is high, but experienced architects with project management skills are sought after.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for architects in Sweden typically starts with an assistant role, advancing to registered architect after completing two years of supervised practice and registration. Senior architects can become project managers, design team leaders, or partners in firms. Many move into specialized roles like urban planning, conservation architecture, or sustainability consulting. Self-employment is common, and further education in areas like BIM or energy efficiency can enhance prospects. Registration (legitimation) is essential for higher responsibility and salary.

- Engineering quantity estimation and preliminary cost calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an architect in Sweden?**
A: Starting salaries for architects range from 33,000 to 37,000 SEK per month. Experienced architects with registration can earn 45,000 to 60,000 SEK per month, and senior roles or partners may exceed 70,000 SEK.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to apply for a work permit as an architect?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA architects must have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement wages. The permit is employer-tied, but switching employers after two years is possible.

**Q: Is the title 'arkitekt' protected in Sweden?**
A: Yes, the title 'arkitekt' is protected by law. Only individuals registered with Boverket, after completing an approved degree and two years of supervised practice, may use the title.
