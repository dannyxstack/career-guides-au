# Midwifery Professionals — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2222 (ISCO08)**

Midwives in Sweden provide comprehensive prenatal, childbirth, and postnatal care, often working in hospitals, maternity clinics, or as independent practitioners. They are highly trained professionals and key to Sweden's maternal healthcare system, with a strong emphasis on continuity of care and patient autonomy.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 3,863. The demand for midwives in Sweden remains strong due to an aging workforce and regional variations in birth rates. The Swedish Public Employment Service forecasts good opportunities, especially in rural areas and smaller hospitals. Shortages are reported in several counties, making midwifery a promising career choice.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression includes specialization in areas like ultrasound, breastfeeding support, or management roles. Many midwives advance to senior positions, or pursue research and teaching at universities. Independent practice is also an option, offering flexibility and entrepreneurial opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic entry and organization of routine prenatal electronic records
- Guideline-based routine pregnancy risk assessment report generation
- Automatic Analysis of Birth Monitoring Data and Anomaly Alerts
- AI voice automated outbound calls and basic screening for postpartum follow-up
- Automatic Filling and Coding of Standardized Descriptions in Medical Records

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI assisted fetal monitoring interpretation to improve anomaly recognition accuracy
- AI-assisted measurement and preliminary diagnosis of prenatal ultrasound images
- AI recommendation for personalized birth plans based on historical data
- AI-driven continuous obstetric education simulation training and skill assessment
- Real-time AI translation and information integration in telemedicine consultations

**Human moat:**

- Manual assistance maneuvers and emergency judgment in complex deliveries
- Emotional communication, psychological comfort, and education with mothers and families
- Clinical Leadership and Decision-Making in Cross-Disciplinary Team Collaboration
- Rapid clinical reasoning in unpredictable obstetric emergencies
- Sensitive understanding of maternal cultural background and personal preferences

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI-assisted diagnostic tools in obstetrics (e.g., smart monitors)
- Clinical data analysis and information system management
- Telehealth consultation and digital health education skills
- Interventional prenatal examination techniques (e.g., ultrasound-guided)
- Cross-cultural communication and psychological support skills
- Simulation training for maternal and infant emergencies and team collaboration

**Key growth areas:**

- maternal health
- neonatal care
- rural healthcare
- telemedicine

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwife in Sweden?**
A: The average monthly salary for a midwife in Sweden is around 37,000 SEK (pre-tax). Entry-level may be slightly less, while experienced midwives can earn 45,000+ SEK.

**Q: Can a non-EU midwife get a work permit in Sweden?**
A: Yes, midwives are on the shortage list, so work permits are generally available with a job offer that meets collective agreement standards.
