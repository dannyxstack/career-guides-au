# Security Guard — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5414 (ISCO08)**

Security guards in Sweden are licensed professionals responsible for protecting property, people, and premises. They work in diverse settings such as shopping centers, corporate offices, government buildings, ports, and events. The occupation is regulated by the Swedish Police Authority, requiring a personal security guard license (personligt skyddsvaktsbevis). Demand is stable, driven by increased security concerns and commercial growth. Many guards work for private security firms on contract assignments. The role typically involves surveillance, access control, patrol, and incident response, often during non-standard hours.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression for security guards in Sweden typically begins with entry-level guarding and moves toward supervision or specialized roles. With experience and further training, guards can become site supervisors, operations managers, or security consultants. Specializations include technical security (CCTV, alarm systems), executive protection, or event security. Some pursue further education in law enforcement, criminology, or security management. License renewal and continued training are required. The role offers stable employment but limited upward mobility without additional qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically detect intrusions and abnormal behavior via video analysis, replacing long-term human monitoring.
- Automatic access control system (facial recognition/QR code) replaces manual identity verification and registration
- Robots patrol electronically along preset routes, replacing some manual patrols.
- Automatically generate patrol logs and incident reports, replacing manual records

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted analysis of surveillance video, quick event playback, key frame identification, improving investigation efficiency
- Smart scheduling system optimizes patrol routes based on real-time risk, enhancing manual patrol accuracy
- AI voice assistants help handle visitor inquiries, improving service response speed and professionalism.
- Receive AI alerts on mobile terminals, guiding security personnel to prioritize high-risk areas and improve emergency response.

**Human moat:**

- Handling physical conflicts, violent incidents, and other high-risk interventions requires human judgment and physical strength
- Situational understanding and flexible communication for suspicious behavior (e.g., dispute mediation)
- Humanized reassurance and emotional interaction, such as calming public sentiment.
- Legal compliance and ethical judgment, such as reasonable use of force boundaries.

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of smart security systems (video analysis, access control, alarm platforms)
- Fundamental data analysis and report interpretation (e.g., event statistics, risk heat maps)
- Human-machine collaboration communication skills (coordinating with AI scheduling and robots)
- Emergency Management and Crisis Intervention (Conflict Resolution, First Aid)
- Service Awareness in Digital Transformation (Visitor Self-Service System Guidance)
- Continuous learning and tool adaptability

**Key growth areas:**

- Event security
- Retail security
- Public sector
- Technical security

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a security guard in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements. Entry-level guards earn around 23,000–28,000 SEK per month. Experienced guards or those with specialized duties can earn 30,000–35,000 SEK. Overtime and night shift supplements add to income. Exact figures vary by region and employer.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a security guard in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. You need a job offer from a Swedish employer, and the job must meet the terms of the applicable collective agreement (minimum salary and conditions). After obtaining a work permit, you must apply for and pass the security guard license from the Police. Language skills (Swedish) are often required for the training.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for security guards in Sweden?**
A: With experience, guards can become site supervisors, operations managers, or security coordinators. Specializing in technical security (alarms, CCTV) or working at high-security sites (airports, nuclear) offers higher pay. Some pursue further education like a university degree in security management or criminology.
