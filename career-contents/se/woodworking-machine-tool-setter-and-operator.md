# Woodworking Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7523 (ISCO08)**

Woodworking machine tool setters and operators set up, adjust and operate woodworking machines to shape and finish wood products in Sweden's sawmills, furniture and construction material industries. The role requires technical skill and precision, with steady demand tied to the housing and renovation markets.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with entry-level operator roles, advancing to senior operator or machine setup specialist. With additional training, operators can move into production planning, quality control, or supervisory positions. Many employers support further education in wood technology or automation, enabling transition to maintenance technician or engineer roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic programming and path optimization for CNC machines
- Repetitive wood cutting and shaping operations
- Vision-based automatic quality inspection
- Machine status monitoring and basic fault diagnosis
- Automated scheduling of production planning and material handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to optimise cutting paths to reduce waste
- Overlay real-time operational guides through augmented reality
- AI predictive maintenance reduces downtime
- Debug new programs using digital twin simulation
- Collaborative robots assist with heavy object handling to improve efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Real-time decision-making ability to handle natural defects in wood.
- Non-standard process adjustments for custom furniture
- On-site repair experience for mechanical faults in machine tools
- Intuitive judgment of wood characteristics (moisture content, grain)
- Communicate customization needs with designers and clients

**Skills to build:**

- CNC programming (G-code, CAD/CAM software)
- Industrial robot operation and maintenance
- Sensor and IoT device integration
- Data analysis and predictive maintenance basics
- Digital twin and simulation technology
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing in woodworking)

**Key growth areas:**

- Furniture manufacturing
- Sawmill technology
- Automated wood processing
- CNC woodworking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a woodworking machine operator in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region but typically follow collective agreement levels, with entry-level around 24,000–28,000 SEK per month and experienced operators earning 30,000–35,000 SEK for full-time work.

**Q: Can I get a Swedish work permit as a woodworking machine operator?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the minimum wage (around 80% of median) and the terms of the applicable collective agreement. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled within the EU/EEA.

**Q: Is there demand for woodworking machine operators in Sweden?**
A: Demand is moderate and regionally dependent, with better prospects in forestry-intensive areas like Småland and Dalarna. Automation is reducing demand for basic operators, but skilled workers with CNC experience are sought after.
