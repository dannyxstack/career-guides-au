# Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 8212 (ISCO08)**

Electrical and electronic equipment assemblers in Sweden work in manufacturing industries, assembling components for products such as computers, control panels, and communication devices. The role requires precision, manual dexterity, and familiarity with technical drawings. Employment is concentrated in regions with strong industrial sectors like Västsverige and Östra Mellansverige.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression often involves moving from assembly to team leader, quality inspector, or technician roles after gaining experience and additional training. Further education in electronics engineering or automation can lead to higher-skilled positions. Some assemblers specialize in specific products like medical devices or automotive electronics.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive electronic component insertion and soldering
- Visual inspection and testing based on preset criteria
- Simple wire harness organization and connection
- Material handling and sorting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted complex fault diagnosis and troubleshooting
- Assembly guidance and real-time verification using AR headsets
- Predictive maintenance data analysis
- Programming and debugging of automated equipment

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, flexible assembly requirements
- Understanding of new circuit design and hands-on prototype verification
- Cross-system integration and process optimization
- On-site emergency handling and customer communication

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial Robot Programming and Debugging

- SMT patch and advanced packaging technology

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electrical and electronic equipment assembler in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by region and experience. Entry-level positions start around 24,000 SEK/month, while experienced assemblers earn up to 30,000 SEK/month. Collective agreements set minimum wages; actual pay may include shift allowances.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work permit as an electrical and electronic equipment assembler in Sweden?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the salary threshold (about 28,000 SEK/month) and terms of collective agreements. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA candidate.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for assemblers in Sweden?**
A: With experience, assemblers can become team leaders, quality inspectors, or production supervisors. Further education at vocational college can lead to roles as electronics technicians or automation specialists.
