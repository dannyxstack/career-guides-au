# Buyers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3323 (ISCO08)**

Buyers in Sweden are responsible for purchasing goods, services, and raw materials for organizations, often specializing in areas such as manufacturing, retail, or the public sector. The role involves supplier evaluation, contract negotiation, and ensuring cost-effective procurement. Sweden's strong export-oriented economy and focus on sustainable supply chains drive demand for skilled buyers who can navigate global markets and implement green procurement strategies.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts from junior buyer or procurement assistant, advancing to senior buyer, category manager, or procurement manager. Many move into supply chain management or strategic sourcing. Larger firms offer clear hierarchies, while startups may require broader responsibilities. Professional certifications, such as those from SILF (Swedish Association of Purchasing and Logistics), can enhance prospects. Networking and continuous learning in digital procurement tools are key for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically Analyze Public Property Data, Generate Market Reports and Comparable Sales Analysis
- Automatically filtering property listings that match client criteria.
- Basic clerical work, such as contract drafting and document filing
- Initial customer inquiries and communication via chatbots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI tools for in-depth data analysis to quickly identify investment opportunities and market trends
- Using AI to generate personalized property valuation models and investment reports
- Enhance client communication efficiency and follow-up automation through CRM systems and AI chatbots
- Virtual property tours and remote assessments to expand service coverage

**Human moat:**

- Build long-term client trust and reputation through relationship-based referral business
- Complex negotiation strategies and psychological tactics, especially in multi-party bidding or difficult deals
- On-site property inspection and detail insights (structure, community factors)
- Experience-based judgment for non-standard properties (such as inherited properties, auction properties)

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualisation (Python, Tableau, Power BI)
- AI tool applications (AI-driven valuation models, chatbots)
- Customer relationship management (CRM systems and automated marketing)
- Advanced negotiation skills and conflict resolution
- Legal and compliance knowledge (state conveyancing regulations)
- Digital marketing and social media customer acquisition skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable procurement
- Digital supply chains
- E-commerce
- Strategic sourcing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much do buyers earn in Sweden?**
A: Salaries for buyers in Sweden vary by experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions typically start around 30,000 SEK per month, while senior buyers can earn over 50,000 SEK. Collective agreements set minimums, and actual pay depends on negotiation and qualifications.

**Q: Can a non-EU buyer get a work permit in Sweden?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer that meets the collective agreement salary and conditions. Your employer must first advertise the position within the EU/EEA. The permit is employer-specific. For senior roles with high pay (over 54,000 SEK/month), an EU Blue Card may be an option.

**Q: What is the career progression for a buyer in Sweden?**
A: Many start as junior buyers or assistants, then move to buyer, senior buyer, category manager, or procurement manager. With experience, some transition into supply chain management, logistics, or strategic sourcing roles. Professional certifications and language skills can accelerate advancement.
