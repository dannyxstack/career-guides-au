# Other Artistic and Cultural Associate Professionals — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3435 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of creative roles such as graphic designers, interior designers, photographers, and decorators. In Sweden, these professionals work in advertising agencies, design studios, media companies, or as freelancers. The labour market is competitive with a strong demand for digital skills, but many positions are project-based or part-time.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an artistic and cultural associate professional in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary widely by role, experience, and sector. Entry-level graphic designers may earn around 25,000 SEK/month, while experienced freelancers can earn more but have irregular income. Interior designers and photographers often have project-based pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU photographer or designer get a work permit in Sweden?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement pay (often around 27,000-30,000 SEK/month) and the employer can prove the position could not be filled locally. Freelance work is not covered; self-employment permits exist but are stricter.

**Q: Is a university degree necessary to work in this field in Sweden?**
A: No, many professionals succeed with a strong portfolio and vocational training. However, a degree can help with job applications and salary negotiations, especially for larger companies.
