# Health Associate Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3259 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of health associate professionals in Sweden, such as audiologists, orthoptists, and other specialized clinical support roles not classified elsewhere. These professionals work in hospitals, clinics, and primary care settings, often requiring post-secondary education between two and three years. Strong demand is driven by an aging population and expanding healthcare services, with good prospects for those with specific specializations. Swedish language skills and, in many cases, a license from the National Board of Health and Welfare (Socialstyrelsen) are required for practice.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization and region, but entry-level positions often start around 30,000–35,000 SEK per month, with experienced professionals earning 40,000–50,000 SEK. Public sector pay is regulated by collective agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a health associate professional in Sweden?**
A: Yes, via a work permit with a job offer that meets collective agreement wages. Many specializations also require a Swedish license, which involves recognition of foreign qualifications and language tests. EU Blue Card is possible for higher-paying roles.

**Q: Is a Swedish license mandatory for all occupations in this group?**
A: No, not all roles require a license. However, for regulated professions like audiologist or orthoptist, a license from Socialstyrelsen is mandatory. For unregulated roles, the employer sets requirements.
