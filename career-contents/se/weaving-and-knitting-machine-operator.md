# Weaving and Knitting Machine Operator — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 8152 (ISCO08)**

Weaving and knitting machine operators set up, operate, and monitor looms and knitting machines to produce textiles. In Sweden, the textile industry is concentrated in the Sjuhärads region around Borås, with a strong focus on technical textiles, sustainable production, and automation. The workforce is aging, and while overall employment is declining due to automation and offshoring, replacement needs create steady opportunities for skilled operators. The role increasingly demands digital skills to manage computer-controlled machinery.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically moves from operator to senior operator, team lead, or technician responsible for machine maintenance and programming. With further vocational training, operators can specialize in automation or quality control, or move into textile engineering roles. The industry values operators who adapt to digitalization and sustainable production methods, opening paths to roles in production development or supply chain management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically detects fabric defects and adjusts tension
- Automatically switch patterns and parameters based on production orders
- Remote diagnosis and automatic repair prompts for machine faults
- Automatic recording of production data and generation of summary reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-Assisted Optimization of Weaving Process Parameters to Improve Yield and Quality
- Using digital twin simulations to predict equipment wear and maintenance cycles
- Predictive maintenance using smart sensors and AI
- Use machine learning to analyze historical data and improve production planning

**Human moat:**

- Manual diagnosis and on-site repair of complex faults.
- Development and trial production of new fabrics or patterns
- Flexible scheduling for small-batch production of multiple varieties
- Communication with designers and clients, and understanding of custom requirements

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of industrial robots
- Basic programming (PLC, Python, C#)
- Data analysis and visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Digital twin and simulation software (AnyLogic, Simul8)
- Debugging smart sensors and IoT devices
- Basic English or Italian to read technical documentation

**Key growth areas:**

- technical textiles
- sustainable production
- automation
- digitalization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a weaving and knitting machine operator in Sweden?**
A: According to Statistics Sweden (SCB), the median monthly salary is around 27,000 SEK. However, salaries vary by region, experience, and employer. The minimum for a work permit is set by collective agreements, typically 25,000–30,000 SEK per month.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a weaving machine operator in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it requires an employer-tied work permit. The employer must offer a job with terms at least equal to Swedish collective agreements (including salary) and prove that the position could not be filled within Sweden/EU. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the process may be more challenging.

**Q: What skills are most in demand for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: Employers increasingly seek operators with digital literacy to handle computer-controlled looms and knitting machines. Knowledge of technical textiles and sustainable production methods is also valued. Swedish language skills are often required for communication and safety.
