# Valuer and Loss Assessor — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3315 (ISCO08)**

Valuers and loss assessors in Sweden work mainly in real estate, insurance, and banking. They assess property values for mortgages, insurance claims, and damage compensation. The role demands strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and knowledge of market conditions. Many are employed by valuation firms, insurance companies, or work as independent contractors. The profession is regulated through voluntary certifications, with demand tied to property market activity and insurance cycles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically moves from junior valuer or loss assessor to senior roles, team leadership, or independent expert. Many start in insurance or real estate companies, then move to consultancies or start their own business. Obtaining a licensed real estate valuer certification (auktoriserad fastighetsvärderare) significantly enhances prospects. Continued professional development in areas like international valuation standards or green building assessment is beneficial.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary data collation and analysis for automated property valuation reports
- Standard loss amount calculation based on historical data
- Online image assessment for minor vehicle damage
- Automatic Classification and Entry of Insurance Claim Documents
- Input parameters for conventional real estate valuation models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to identify anomalies and patterns in loss images.
- Leverage big data analytics to predict market value trends
- AI-assisted rapid generation of complex assessment report drafts
- Using natural language processing to automatically handle customer inquiries
- Improve valuation accuracy through machine learning models

**Human moat:**

- On-site inspection and physical access capability
- Independent judgment for complex or non-standard damage
- Legal and ethical compliance responsibilities
- Client trust building and communication negotiation
- Creativity in handling special cases

**Skills to build:**

- Machine learning fundamentals and model interpretation
- Geographic information systems and drone operation.
- Data visualization and analysis tools (e.g., Python, R)
- AI-assisted assessment software operation
- Enhance communication and client management skills
- Interdisciplinary knowledge (e.g., law, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Real Estate Valuation
- Insurance Claims
- Sustainable Valuation
- Digital Analysis

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a valuer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around the median for business graduates, while senior valuers can earn significantly more. Official statistics from Statistics Sweden (SCB) provide detailed salary data.

**Q: Can a non-EU valuer get a work permit for Sweden?**
A: Yes, with a valid job offer that meets collective agreement pay and terms. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. EU/EEA citizens do not need a permit.

**Q: What education is needed to become a valuer in Sweden?**
A: A bachelor's degree in real estate, economics, or finance is common. Vocational training in property valuation is also an option. Professional certifications like auktoriserad fastighetsvärderare are highly recommended for career advancement.
