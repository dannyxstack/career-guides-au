# Elementary Worker Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9629 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a wide range of simple and routine tasks across various sectors in Sweden, such as manual labour in warehouses, cleaning, or basic manufacturing. Workers typically perform duties that require minimal training or skill, often on a temporary or part-time basis. The role is common in industries like logistics, construction, and services, but because it is a catch-all category, specific duties vary greatly. Employment conditions are generally low-paid and may involve shift work.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; most workers remain in similar roles or move to slightly more skilled elementary jobs. Some may transition to semi-skilled positions through on-the-job training or short courses, e.g., forklift certification. However, without further education, advancement is rare. The occupation is often a stepping stone for newly arrived immigrants or those with low formal qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple goods handling and stacking: replaced by autonomous mobile robots (AMRs)
- Basic cleaning (e.g., sweeping, mopping): replaced by automatic floor scrubbers or robotic vacuums
- Item classification and sorting: replaced by computer vision systems
- Mail or document distribution: replaced by digital notification systems
- Inventory counting assistance: replaced by RFID and drones

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Task scheduling and priority arrangement: AI optimizes daily work lists
- Equipment failure warning: AI analyzes sensor data to remind of maintenance
- Customer communication records: AI automatic transcription and classification of feedback
- Safety compliance checks: AI-assisted identification of hazardous areas
- Basic communication in multilingual environments: assisted by real-time translation tools

**Human moat:**

- Adaptability to unstructured environments (e.g., ad-hoc assembly tasks)
- Complex emotional communication and customer service (e.g., appeasing dissatisfied customers)
- Cross-task flexible decision-making (when AI recommendations are unclear)
- Fine manual operations (e.g., art packaging)
- Teamwork and on-site coordination skills

**Skills to build:**

- Basic digital literacy (managing tasks using tablets/mobile phones)
- Operate semi-automated equipment (e.g., cleaning robots)
- Basic Data Analysis (Understanding Task Efficiency Reports)
- Interpersonal communication and customer service skills
- Basic English or a second language (to adapt to multicultural work environments)
- Safety and compliance basic training

**Key growth areas:**

- Logistics
- Cleaning
- Manufacturing
- Seasonal Work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an elementary worker not elsewhere classified in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are low, often around 20,000–25,000 SEK per month before tax, depending on sector and region. This is below the threshold for many work permits.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit for this occupation?**
A: It is very difficult. The job must meet collective agreement pay (which is low but still above permit threshold), but the Swedish Migration Agency often rejects such applications due to the low skill level and availability of EU labour.

**Q: Are there any training programs to advance from this role?**
A: Yes, short vocational courses (e.g., forklift, warehouse safety) can lead to slightly better roles, but formal education like Komvux (adult education) is needed for significant progression.
