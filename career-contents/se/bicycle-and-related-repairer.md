# Bicycle and Related Repairers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Bicycle and related repairers maintain, repair, and customize bicycles, e-bikes, and similar vehicles. In Sweden, cycling is a key part of urban transport and recreation, with growing demand for e-bike services. The occupation is typically accessed via vocational training (gymnasium or yrkeshögskola) and offers stable employment, especially in cities with strong cycling infrastructure.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior mechanic, advancing to senior mechanic, workshop manager, or specialist (e.g., e-bike technician). Many repairers become self-employed, opening their own shops. Additional certifications from brands (e.g., Bosch, Shimano) boost prospects. Some transition to sales or parts distribution.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic software to automatically identify common faults (e.g., circuit issues)
- Handle customer appointments and simple inquiries via chatbot
- Inventory management and parts ordering automated by AI system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnosis of complex electrical faults (e.g., electric bicycle battery management systems)
- AR glasses overlay repair steps and exploded parts diagrams to improve repair accuracy
- Customer needs analysis: AI recommends personalised upgrade options (e.g. performance modifications)
- Remote expert collaboration: obtaining real-time support via AI platforms

**Human moat:**

- Manual fine-tuning (such as wheel truing, suspension adjustment)
- Assess unusual damage and creatively repair
- Face-to-face communication with clients to understand cycling preferences and customize solutions
- Handling replacement and adaptation of old or non-standard parts

**Skills to build:**

- Diagnosis and repair of electric bicycle electrical systems
- Proficient in using digital diagnostic tools and repair apps
- 3D printing part production and repair
- Customer relationship management and personalized service design
- Basic programming skills (e.g., MES system operation in repair stations)
- Sustainable repair concepts (e.g., battery recycling and upgrades)

**Key growth areas:**

- E-bikes
- Urban mobility
- Sustainability
- Cycling infrastructure

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bicycle repairer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. Entry-level mechanics earn around 22,000-25,000 SEK/month, while experienced specialists can earn up to 30,000 SEK/month. Self-employed income depends on client base.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens get a work permit as a bicycle repairer in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so employers must conduct a labour market test. The job must offer at least collective agreement pay (around 25,000 SEK/month). EU/EEA citizens do not need a permit.

**Q: What education is needed to become a bicycle repairer?**
A: A vocational program in bicycle mechanics (gymnasium or yrkeshögskola) is common. Apprenticeships are also available. No formal degree is mandatory, but employers often prefer certified training.
