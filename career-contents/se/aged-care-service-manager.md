# Aged Care Services Manager — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1343 (ISCO08)**

Aged care services managers in Sweden oversee residential care homes, home care services, and day centres for older adults. They are responsible for staff management, budget planning, quality assurance, and compliance with national care standards. With Sweden's rapidly ageing population, demand for these managers is high, particularly in municipalities with growing elderly demographics. The role requires a combination of social work, nursing, or healthcare management qualifications, along with strong leadership and administrative skills. Swedish language proficiency is essential.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 6,537. Employment outlook for aged care services managers is strong due to Sweden's demographic shift—by 2030, over 25% of the population will be 65+. Municipalities are expanding home care and specialised dementia units, creating new managerial positions. However, recruitment is challenging due to region-specific shortages, particularly in rural areas. The occupation is on the Swedish Public Employment Service's shortage list, indicating long-term demand. Budget constraints in some municipalities may slow hiring, but overall growth is steady.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a social worker, nurse, or unit manager within elderly care. With experience and further education (e.g., a master's in health management or gerontology), one can advance to senior manager roles overseeing multiple facilities. Some move to consultancies or national agencies like the National Board of Health and Welfare. Continuous professional development in digital health and person-centred care is valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate shift schedules and personnel assignments
- Automated nursing documentation and review
- Budget and Resource Allocation Report Based on Data Analysis
- Compliance monitoring and automatic alarm system
- Standardized customer satisfaction and service quality statistics

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to predict occupancy and care needs, optimizing bed and staff allocation
- Use analytical tools to improve care quality monitoring and improvement decisions
- Conduct risk management and emergency drills through digital twin simulations
- AI-assisted personalized care plan creation and dynamic adjustment
- Automatically generate compliance reports, freeing up human resources to focus on strategic planning.

**Human moat:**

- Handling complex interpersonal interactions and empathetic communication such as sudden behaviors in dementia patients
- Lead teams and mediate conflicts between staff and families
- Making human judgments and assuming responsibility in ethical dilemmas
- Cross-departmental coordination (medical, government, family) negotiation skills.

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (e.g., Power BI, Tableau)
- Application of AI tools in nursing management (e.g., predictive modeling)
- Digital project management and remote collaboration tools
- Basic programming skills (Python for data cleaning)
- Ethical and compliance knowledge updates (data privacy regulations)
- Change management and digital training skills for employees

**Key growth areas:**

- ageing population
- home care
- digital care solutions
- specialized dementia care

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an aged care services manager in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by municipality and experience, typically ranging from 45,000 to 60,000 SEK per month (pre-tax annual ~540,000–720,000 SEK). Managers in larger cities or with specialized roles may earn more.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens qualify for a work permit as an aged care manager?**
A: You need a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective-agreement pay and benefits. The employer must advertise the position within EU/EEA first. Swedish language skills (at least B2) are almost invariably required.

**Q: What qualifications are mandatory for aged care managers in Sweden?**
A: A Swedish socionom (social work) degree or equivalent is typically mandatory. Some municipalities accept a nursing degree combined with management training. Foreign qualifications may be assessed by the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR).
