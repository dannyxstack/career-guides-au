# Structural Metal Preparer and Erector — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Structural metal preparers and erectors in Sweden cut, shape, assemble, and install steel and other metal components in buildings, bridges, wind turbines, and industrial facilities. The occupation is closely tied to large infrastructure projects and the manufacturing sector, with a strong demand for certified welders and erectors. Sweden's ongoing investments in renewable energy and transport infrastructure sustain consistent job opportunities.

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 1,051. Many experienced workers are retiring, creating replacement demand. Mobility within the EU/EEA and targeted work permits for skilled tradespeople facilitate recruitment from abroad.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, leading to a journeyman role. Experienced workers can advance to team leader, site supervisor, or specialist in welding techniques (e.g., underwater welding, certifying in EN 1090). Formal certification (e.g., IFP certified welder) is increasingly valued and can command higher wages. Some move into project management or start their own contracting business.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Precise dimensional measurement and calibration using BIM and laser scanning
- Optimize cutting and welding parameters for steel structures using AI software
- Drones automatically check structural alignment and bolt tightening status

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted crane and rigging configuration planning
- Risk prediction and construction plan optimization based on historical data
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted installation guidance and training
- Real-time tool status monitoring and predictive maintenance alerts.

**Human moat:**

- Physical operation and assembly in complex construction site environments
- Flexible judgment for handling non-standard or damaged materials
- Coordinate with the team and handle emergency situations
- Strict enforcement and supervision of safety regulations

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Building Information Modeling) software operation
- Drone operation and data analysis
- Programming and maintenance of welding robots
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools
- Digital literacy and basic programming concepts
- Data analysis in safety and quality management

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Renewable energy
- Offshore
- Steel construction

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a structural metal preparer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary with experience and certifications. The occupation is considered well-paid for a skilled trade, with collective agreements setting minimum rates. Experienced certified welders can earn above average.

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a structural metal preparer in Sweden?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA citizen, no permit is needed. Non-EU/EEA nationals need a job offer meeting collective agreement pay and must apply for a work permit before entering Sweden.

**Q: What certifications improve job prospects?**
A: A welding certificate (e.g., EN 1090) and a work-at-height certificate are highly beneficial. Additional certifications in specialist welding (e.g., TIG, MIG) can open up advanced roles.
