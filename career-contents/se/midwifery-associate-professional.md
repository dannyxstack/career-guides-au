# Midwifery Associate Professional — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3222 (ISCO08)**

Midwifery associate professionals assist registered midwives in providing care to pregnant women, women in labour, and new mothers. In Sweden, they work in maternity wards, prenatal clinics, and sometimes in home care settings. Their duties include monitoring vital signs, assisting during deliveries, providing breastfeeding support, and educating new parents. This role is part of the Swedish healthcare system, which emphasizes evidence-based practice and patient-centered care. With a growing population and an aging workforce of midwives, the demand for support staff is increasing, particularly in urban areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves gaining experience in maternity care and possibly pursuing further education to become a registered midwife (Barnmorska), which requires a university degree. Some associate professionals specialize in areas such as neonatal care or contraceptive counseling. Others move into leadership roles as team leaders or coordinators in maternity units. The role is often a stepping stone for those aiming for higher qualifications in nursing or midwifery, and many employers offer study support.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Auto-fill antenatal check records and birth reports
- Generates basic maternal education materials based on algorithms
- Automatically organize appointment schedules and patient reminders
- Basic postpartum follow-up data entry and analysis
- Use AI to analyze initial abnormal patterns in fetal heart rate monitoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted identification of early signs of high-risk pregnancy (e.g., abnormal data trends)
- Communicate efficiently with multilingual maternity patients via AI translation tools
- AI-driven patient education platform provides personalized guidance for postpartum women
- AI simulation training system enhances emergency care skills for conditions like postpartum hemorrhage
- Using predictive models to optimize postpartum home visit routes and priorities.

**Human moat:**

- Real-time palpation and manual delivery techniques during childbirth
- Empathy and decision-making in maternal emotional support and family communication
- Rapid assessment and manual intervention in emergencies (e.g., umbilical cord prolapse)
- Ability to provide personalized care considering the psychological and cultural background of the mother.
- On-site coordination across teams (doctors, nurses, anesthetists).

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and AI tool use (e.g. Excel, pregnancy risk models)
- Communication and cross-cultural nursing skills.
- Proficient in electronic health record (EHR) systems
- Emergency simulation training and crisis resource management.
- Telemedicine platform operation and patient education techniques.
- Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) interpretation and application

**Key growth areas:**

- Maternity care
- Postnatal support
- Reproductive health
- Breastfeeding counseling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwifery associate professional in Sweden?**
A: Salaries follow collective agreements in the healthcare sector. Entry-level monthly pay ranges around 24,000–28,000 SEK, increasing with experience and shift work.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as a midwifery associate professional from outside the EU?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the terms of collective agreements and the salary threshold (approx. 13,000 SEK/month for 2023). The role is considered skilled, but you must also have relevant education.

**Q: Is it possible to advance from this role to become a registered midwife?**
A: Yes, many use this position as a stepping stone. You can apply for the midwifery program (Barnmorskeutbildning) at university, which is a 2-year master's-level program after a nursing degree. Some employers offer study support.
