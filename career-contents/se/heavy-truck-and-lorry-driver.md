# Heavy Truck and Lorry Drivers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 8332 (ISCO08)**

Heavy truck and lorry drivers operate large vehicles to transport goods over short or long distances. In Sweden, this occupation is critical to logistics and supply chains, with high demand due to a growing e-commerce sector and an aging driver workforce. Drivers often work for transport companies, construction firms, or retail distributors, and must hold a valid CE license and professional competence certificate. The job involves long hours, time-sensitive deliveries, and adherence to strict safety regulations, including digital tachograph usage.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 35,402. The employment outlook for heavy truck drivers in Sweden is very positive. Many current drivers are nearing retirement, creating a steady flow of vacancies. E-commerce growth and increased demand for just-in-time delivery drive recruitment. The Public Employment Service projects good opportunities nationwide, with regional shortages in northern Sweden. However, the job requires flexibility regarding irregular hours and overnight stays, which may deter some candidates.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with obtaining a CE license and gaining experience. Drivers can specialize in areas like hazardous materials (ADR), refrigerated goods, or heavy haulage. Experienced drivers may become instructors, fleet managers, or start their own small transport businesses. There is also a path to supervisory roles such as transport coordinator or logistics manager, often with further training in supply chain management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Long-distance highway driving (autonomous truck pilot programs)
- Basic route planning and GPS navigation adjustments
- Basic vehicle status monitoring and routine inspection recording
- Standardized cargo handling (integration with automated warehousing)
- Fatigue driving risk detection (AI takeover warning)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Dynamic route optimization (real-time traffic, weather, queue times)
- Vehicle maintenance prediction (AI analysis engine data for early warning)
- Delivery confirmation and customer communication (auto-photo, e-signature)
- Multimodal transport coordination (rail, port, warehouse system integration)
- Driving safety assistance (collision warning, blind spot monitoring)

**Human moat:**

- Complex loading/unloading and on-site flexibility (e.g., construction sites, refrigerated trucks)
- Driving on unstructured roads in remote areas
- Customer relationships and non-standard delivery requirements
- Rapid decision-making in emergencies (accidents, failures)
- Compliance and safety responsibilities (cargo inspection, dangerous goods transport)

**Skills to build:**

- Autonomous driving system operation and monitoring
- Use of logistics scheduling software and multimodal transport platforms
- Basic Maintenance of New Energy Trucks (Electric/Hydrogen)
- Safety compliance and digital document management
- Customer communication and on-site problem solving
- Basic data analysis (fuel efficiency, route optimization)

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce logistics
- Hazardous materials transport
- Last-mile delivery
- Refrigerated transport

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Individuals who enjoy long-distance driving and independence.
- People comfortable with irregular hours, including nights and weekends.
- Those who can manage physical loading/unloading and adhere to tight schedules.

**Who is not suited**
- Those who dislike being away from home for extended periods.
- People who feel stressed in heavy traffic or large vehicle operation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a heavy truck driver in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary by experience, employer, and type of transport. The level generally exceeds median wages for manual occupations, with additional pay for overtime, night work, and specialized loads.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a truck driver in Sweden?**
A: Yes, with a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement pay and conditions. Since the occupation is on the shortage list, application processing may be faster. The permit is tied to that employer.

**Q: Do I need Swedish language skills to work as a truck driver?**
A: While not legally required, basic Swedish is often needed for communication with dispatchers, customers, and authorities. Some employers accept English, but language skills improve job prospects significantly.
