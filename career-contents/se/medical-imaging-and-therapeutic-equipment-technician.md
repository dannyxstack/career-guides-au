# Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3211 (ISCO08)**

Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians operate X-ray, MRI, CT, and radiation therapy machines in Swedish hospitals and clinics. They work under radiologists to produce diagnostic images and administer radiation treatments. The role requires strong technical skills, attention to safety, and patient interaction. In Sweden, the profession is regulated by the National Board of Health and Welfare, and all practitioners must hold a license. Demand is stable due to an ageing population and increasing reliance on advanced imaging, though shortages persist in rural areas.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Medical Imaging Technician in Sweden?**
A: The typical pre-tax monthly salary ranges from 35,000 to 45,000 SEK (420,000–540,000 SEK annually), depending on experience, region, and specialization. Shift allowances and overtime can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a Medical Imaging Technician?**
A: Yes, but the profession is regulated. You need a job offer that meets collective agreement pay and a license from Socialstyrelsen. License recognition requires evaluation of your education and possibly supplementary courses. The occupation is on the shortage list, which may facilitate the permit process.

**Q: Can I work with a foreign degree in radiography?**
A: Yes, but your qualifications must be assessed by the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR) and then approved by Socialstyrelsen. You may need to complete bridging courses or a language test. Once licensed, you can apply for jobs.
