# Dairy Products Maker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7513 (ISCO08)**

Dairy products makers operate machinery and processes in industrial dairies to produce cheese, yogurt, butter, and other dairy goods, ensuring quality and hygiene standards in Sweden's modern food manufacturing sector.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a production operator, advancing to team leader or shift supervisor with experience. Further specialization in quality control, dairy technology, or automation maintenance opens paths to technician or management roles. Further education in food science can lead to product development.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeating the feeding and mixing steps according to the recipe
- Routine temperature and humidity monitoring records.
- Packaging, labeling, and palletizing
- Simple sensory inspection (e.g., color, texture)
- Generate batch production reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI vision system assists with foreign object detection and ripeness assessment
- Data-driven optimization of fermentation parameters and energy consumption
- Smart sensors predict equipment failures
- Automated supply chain and inventory management
- Customized flavor formulation recommendations for clients

**Human moat:**

- Traditional cheese/yogurt artisan craftsmanship
- Experience in flavor fine-tuning and sensory evaluation
- Emergency handling of sudden equipment failures
- Small-batch specialty product innovation development
- Food safety and HACCP compliance assessment.

**Skills to build:**

- Automated equipment programming and maintenance
- Food data analysis and IoT applications
- Operation and calibration of AI quality inspection tools
- Sustainable processing technology
- Value-added product development (e.g., functional dairy products)
- Updates on interpretation of food safety regulations

**Key growth areas:**

- organic dairy
- automation
- quality control
- sustainability

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Detail-oriented and methodical in following production procedures.
- Comfortable working in a hygienic, temperature-controlled environment.
- Enjoys hands-on work with machinery and quality checks.

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes repetitive tasks and shift work.
- Allergic to dairy products or lactose intolerant.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dairy products maker in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary by experience and region, generally competitive with other industrial production roles. Entry-level wages start around the manufacturing median, with increases for night shifts and specialized skills.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a dairy products maker?**
A: It is possible but difficult, because the occupation is not on Sweden's shortage list and the salary may fall below the work permit threshold. A job offer with collective agreement pay is required, but approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What education is needed to become a dairy products maker in Sweden?**
A: A vocational upper secondary education in food processing (Livsmedelsprogrammet) or adult education (Komvux) is common. On-the-job training is also provided.
