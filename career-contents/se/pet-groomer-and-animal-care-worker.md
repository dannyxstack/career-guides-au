# Pet Groomer and Animal Care Worker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5164 (ISCO08)**

Pet groomers and animal care workers in Sweden provide grooming, care, and supervision for animals in settings such as kennels, pet shops, veterinary clinics, and farms. The occupation does not require formal certification but practical experience and animal handling skills are essential. With rising pet ownership, demand for these services remains steady, though competition for jobs can be high in urban areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving from assistant to senior groomer or animal care supervisor. Some professionals become self-employed, opening their own grooming salons or boarding kennels. Further education in animal nursing or ethology can lead to roles as veterinary assistants or animal welfare inspectors. The path is largely experience-based.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Client appointment and schedule management
- Basic beauty records and billing
- Standardized communication (e.g., reminders, follow-ups)
- Security monitoring (e.g., camera analysis)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to analyze pet fur health for care recommendations
- Use AI tools to optimize inventory and supplies management
- Use AI diagnostic tools to assist in identifying skin issues
- Using AI to generate personalized beauty solutions and recommendations

**Human moat:**

- Calming techniques for handling anxious or aggressive animals
- Adjust grooming techniques flexibly based on individual pet condition
- Building emotional trust and personalized service with customers
- Creativity and aesthetic judgment in hand styling

**Skills to build:**

- Learn animal behavior knowledge to improve soothing skills
- Master the use of AI-assisted diagnostic tools
- Enhancing advanced beauty and styling design skills
- Learn to use customer relationship management software
- Attend professional certification courses to enhance competitiveness
- develop social media marketing skills to expand client base

**Key growth areas:**

- pet grooming
- animal welfare
- veterinary assistant
- kennel management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pet groomer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary with experience. They are generally competitive within the service sector.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as a pet groomer?**
A: It is possible if you have a job offer that meets the wage requirements, but the occupation is not on the shortage list, so the employer must show that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: Do I need formal education to work as an animal care worker in Sweden?**
A: No formal education is mandatory, but completing a gymnasium animal care program or a vocational course significantly improves your chances.
