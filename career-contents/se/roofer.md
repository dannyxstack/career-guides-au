# Roofer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7121 (ISCO08)**

Roofers install, repair, and replace roofs on buildings using materials such as tiles, slate, metal, and bitumen. In Sweden, the occupation is essential for both new construction and the renovation of the large stock of older buildings. Weather conditions play a significant role, with seasonal fluctuations affecting workload. The profession requires physical fitness, comfort with heights, and meticulous attention to detail. Roofers often work in small teams and may specialize in areas like green roofs or solar panel integration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to experienced roofer, then foreman or site supervisor. Many roofers eventually start their own businesses. Specialization in green roofing, solar panel installation, or heritage roofing can lead to higher demand and rates. Continuing education in new techniques and materials is important for staying competitive. Union membership is common and provides access to further training and collective bargaining.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic calculation and estimation of roof area and material quantities.
- Automated report generation for safety inspection records and compliance checks
- Robot assistance for standard tile laying (e.g., drone pre-laying)
- AI chatbot handling of customer inquiries and quotes

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI image recognition detects roof damage and assists in diagnosing leak points.
- Drones + AI for 3D roof scanning to generate precise construction drawings
- AR glasses provide step-by-step construction guidance, improving installation efficiency for complex structures
- Smart project management software optimizes material procurement and scheduling
- AI weather prediction system assists in scheduling high-risk outdoor work time

**Human moat:**

- Custom handmade and on-site adjustments for complex waterproofing systems.
- Experience in repairing old-style roofs and aesthetic judgment
- Physical strength and risk management for working at heights
- Interpersonal trust in communicating customized solutions with clients
- Skilled mixed construction with multiple materials (tiles, metal, membrane)

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using drones and 3D scanners for roof surveying
- Master basic BIM (Building Information Modeling) operations and reading
- Learn operation and maintenance of AR-assisted construction equipment
- Basic data analysis and smart device troubleshooting
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Learn the construction techniques of new waterproof materials (e.g., PVC, TPO).

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-efficient roofing
- Green roofs
- Solar panel installation
- Renovation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is the salary for roofers determined in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements between employer organizations and trade unions, varying by region, experience, and employer. Typical pay includes a base monthly salary plus additional compensation for overtime or hazardous conditions.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA roofer get a work permit for Sweden?**
A: Yes, if you have a concrete job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the pay and conditions of the relevant collective agreement. The employer must first try to recruit locally or within the EU/EEA. The permit is tied to that specific employer.
