# Statistical, Finance and Insurance Clerk — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4312 (ISCO08)**

Statistical, finance and insurance clerks in Sweden handle data recording, processing insurance claims, financial transactions, and statistical reporting. They work in banks, insurance companies, government agencies, and financial departments. The role requires accuracy, numeracy, and familiarity with office software and databases. Digitalization is changing tasks, but demand remains stable for skilled clerks who can manage complex data and customer queries. Many positions are concentrated in Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö, with opportunities in regional offices. The occupation offers entry points for gymnasium graduates and further career development through internal training or higher education.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for statistical, finance and insurance clerks in Sweden often involves moving into specialist roles such as financial analyst, underwriter, or data analyst, typically requiring further education or certifications. Many employers offer internal training programs. With experience, clerks can advance to team leader or supervisor positions. Some transition to related fields like accounting or risk management. The occupation serves as a solid foundation for a career in the financial or insurance sectors, with clear pathways to more senior roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Extracting and entering data from formatted tables or documents
- Preliminary review and basic information verification of insurance claims
- Generate standard statistical reports and financial summaries
- Handling routine premium calculations and claim amount verification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Utilize AI for automatic flagging and preliminary analysis of abnormal transactions or claims
- Quickly retrieve historical cases and policy clauses using natural language query tools
- Generating dynamic visual dashboards through automated reporting platforms
- Using RPA to connect multiple systems for cross-platform data validation and reconciliation
- Use machine learning to predict risk scores, assisting human decision-making

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, ambiguous, or anomalous data situations, such as non-compliant claims
- Communicate complex issues with clients or internal departments, providing explanations and recommendations.
- Understand and comply with constantly changing financial and insurance regulations
- Judging business substance behind data anomalies based on industry experience

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and automation scripting in Python or R
- Process design using low-code/RPA tools (e.g. UiPath)
- SQL data querying and database management
- Basic machine learning concepts and model applications (e.g., anomaly detection)
- Knowledge of financial and insurance products and compliance interpretation
- Data visualisation tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization
- Data Analytics
- Insurance Technology
- Risk Management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a statistical, finance and insurance clerk in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions typically start at collective agreement levels, with increases based on seniority and performance. For current figures, consult collective agreements or Statistics Sweden (SCB).

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a statistical, finance and insurance clerk in Sweden?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an employer-tied work permit if they have a job offer that meets collective agreement salary and conditions. The EU Blue Card is less common for this occupation due to salary thresholds.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: With experience and further training, clerks can advance to specialist roles like financial analyst, underwriter, or data analyst. Some move into management or related fields such as accounting or risk management.
