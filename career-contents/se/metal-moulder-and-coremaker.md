# Metal Moulder and Coremaker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7211 (ISCO08)**

Metal moulders and coremakers create metal castings by shaping molten metal in moulds and making cores for hollow sections. In Sweden, this trade is vital for industries such as automotive, energy, and general engineering, with foundries concentrated in regions like Småland and Västsverige. The occupation requires precision, manual skill, and knowledge of materials and processes. Employment is stable due to ongoing demand for custom cast parts, though automation is gradually changing job tasks.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 500. The Swedish foundry sector is facing a moderate labour shortage as experienced workers retire and fewer young people enter the trade. Digitalisation and automation of casting processes are increasing, but manual skill remains essential for complex and low-volume production. Over the next five years, employment is expected to remain steady with slight growth, driven by demand from the green transition (e.g., wind turbine components) and reshoring of manufacturing. Opportunities exist mainly in specialised foundries.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a vocational education (gymnasial or adult education) followed by apprenticeships. Experienced moulders can advance to team leader, foundry technician, or quality inspector. Some move into pattern-making or foundry engineering with further training. The role offers a clear path to specialist positions in areas like simulation-based casting or additive manufacturing. Mastering digital tools and automation can enhance career prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate 3D printing paths for sand moulds/cores using CAD models
- AI vision system detects casting defects in real-time and automatically sorts them
- Robots automatically complete sand mold assembly and pouring operations
- AI optimizes pouring parameters and adjusts cooling system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation software assists in predicting casting shrinkage and porosity locations, guiding process improvements.
- AR headset displays sand core assembly sequence and position calibration
- AI-driven quality analysis system generates defect root cause reports
- The digital twin system monitors the entire casting process in real time and warns of anomalies

**Human moat:**

- Process innovation capability for handling non-standard, small-batch castings
- Skills in manual repair and fine-tuning of complex sand moulds/cores
- On-site decision-making ability for multi-variety quick mold changes.
- Team coordination and safety risk management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Process parameter adjustment for 3D printed sand molds/cores
- Casting simulation software (Magma, ProCAST) operation and result interpretation
- Basic maintenance of automated production lines (robots, PLC)
- Metallurgy and Defect Analysis (metallography, X-ray)
- Data-driven process optimization (statistical process control)
- Basic operations and maintenance of digital twin systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Additive Manufacturing
- Green Casting
- Automation
- Foundry Technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a metal moulder in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer. Entry-level monthly pay is around 26,000–30,000 SEK before tax, while experienced moulders can earn 32,000–38,000 SEK. Collective agreements set minimum rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU metal moulder get a work permit for Sweden?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the collective agreement terms (salary, insurance, etc.). The occupation is not on the 'shortage list' but demand for skilled moulders is recognised, though the employer must still show the job was advertised to EU/EEA workers first.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for metal moulders?**
A: With experience, you can become a team leader, foundry technician, or quality control specialist. Further training in CAD/CAM or additive manufacturing can lead to roles in process development or foundry engineering.
