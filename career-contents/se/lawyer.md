# Lawyers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2611 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, lawyers (jurister) are legal professionals working in private law firms, corporate legal departments, government agencies, and courts. The profession is highly regulated, with the title 'advokat' reserved for members of the Swedish Bar Association. The market is concentrated in major cities like Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö, with stable demand but intense competition for entry-level positions due to a high number of law graduates each year.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 3,087. The employment outlook for lawyers in Sweden is stable. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically begins as a junior associate at a law firm or legal advisor in a corporate setting. After several years, one can advance to senior associate, partner, or move in-house. To become an 'advokat,' three years of supervised practice, courses, and a bar exam are required. Many jurists choose to specialize in niche areas such as tax, intellectual property, or contract law.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated review and keyword extraction of contracts and legal documents
- Automatic generation of legal case search and precedent summaries.
- Drafting standardized legal documents (e.g., wills, contract templates)
- Classification and initial analysis of large volumes of electronic documents during the forensic phase.
- Automatic summarization of factual sections in legal memos

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based case prediction and litigation strategy optimization
- Intelligent contract review system assists in identifying hidden risk clauses
- AI-driven mock court and debate training
- Legal knowledge graph assists in quickly cross-referencing regulations across fields
- AI chatbot screening initial legal needs in customer service

**Human moat:**

- On-the-spot adaptability and persuasion skills in courtroom argument and negotiation
- Comprehensive judgment and ethical decision-making for complex cross-disciplinary legal issues
- Empathy to build trust with clients and understand deep needs
- Maintaining lawyer-client privilege in the public interest
- Unstructured, high-uncertainty exploration of emerging legal fields.

**Skills to build:**

- AI legal tools (e.g., Relativity, Kira Systems) operation
- Data analysis and visualization (for predictive legal analysis)
- Algorithm bias identification and compliance review
- Human-machine collaborative workflow design (legal tech project management)
- Knowledge of cybersecurity and privacy regulations
- Advanced negotiation and complex dispute resolution

**Key growth areas:**

- commercial law
- EU law
- IT law
- compliance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a lawyer in Sweden?**
A: Starting salary for junior lawyers is around 35,000-45,000 SEK/month. Mid-level lawyers earn 50,000-70,000 SEK/month, and senior lawyers or partners can earn 80,000 SEK/month or more.

**Q: Can I work as a lawyer in Sweden with a foreign law degree?**
A: Yes, but you may need to have your degree validated by the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR). For non-EU/EEA nationals, a work permit is required, and the job offer must meet collective agreement standards.
