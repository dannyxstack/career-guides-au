# Health Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2269 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse group of health professionals in Sweden, including audiologists, dietitians, occupational therapists, and others not classified elsewhere. They work in hospitals, primary care, rehabilitation centres, and private practice. The Swedish healthcare system faces high demand due to an aging population and a focus on preventive care, creating a stable job market for these specialists.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression often involves specialization through further education or research. Many professionals advance to senior clinical roles, become team leaders, or move into healthcare management. Others pursue academic careers or private practice. Continuous professional development is mandatory for license renewal. Interdisciplinary collaboration is increasingly valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate nutrition assessment reports and meal plans
- Preliminary analysis and classification of hearing test results
- Standard pronunciation practice and feedback in speech therapy
- Medical documentation organisation and medical summary generation
- Preliminary screening and sorting of some diagnostic information

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic tools improve accuracy of hearing and speech disorder identification
- Smart nutrition analysis platform optimizes personalized dietary plans
- Voice synthesis and recognition technology enhances language therapy interaction.
- AI-driven dynamic adjustment of rehabilitation plans
- Big data analytics supports epidemiology and efficacy studies

**Human moat:**

- Clinical judgment and comprehensive decision-making for complex cases
- Patient Emotional Support and Human Care
- Multidisciplinary team coordination and communication
- Personalized adjustment of treatment plans and ethical judgment
- Flexibility to handle unexpected situations

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted Diagnostic Tool Operation and Result Interpretation
- Health data analysis and statistics
- Digital health platforms and telemedicine applications
- Interdisciplinary Collaboration and Communication Skills
- Ethics and privacy protection knowledge
- Continuous learning and adapting to new technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- aging population
- rehabilitation
- preventive care
- digital health

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for health professionals in this category in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by profession and experience. For example, a newly licensed audiologist or dietitian can expect around 30,000–35,000 SEK per month. Experienced specialists in clinical roles may earn 40,000–50,000 SEK. Public sector pay is slightly lower than private but includes benefits.

**Q: Do I need a job offer before applying for a Swedish work permit as a health professional?**
A: Yes, for non-EU/EEA citizens, a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement conditions is required. The occupation is on the shortage list, which can speed up processing.

**Q: How long does it take to get a Swedish license for health professionals from outside the EU?**
A: The process can take 6–12 months, sometimes longer. It involves submitting credentials, language tests, and possibly supplementary courses or internships. Start early.
