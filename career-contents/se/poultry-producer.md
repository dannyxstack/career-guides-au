# Poultry Producer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 6122 (ISCO08)**

Poultry producers in Sweden manage egg and meat production on farms, overseeing breeding, feeding, health, and welfare of chickens, turkeys, and other fowl. The sector is heavily regulated, with increasing focus on animal welfare, organic production, and sustainability. Most farms are concentrated in southern Sweden, and the industry faces pressure from imports and rising costs. Automation is growing in larger operations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a farm worker, advancing to supervisor or farm manager with experience and further training. Some producers specialize in breeding, nutrition, or animal health. Self-employment is possible, but capital-intensive. The trend is toward larger, automated facilities, reducing the need for manual labor. Certification in animal welfare or organic farming can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Environmental parameter monitoring and recording (temperature, humidity, light)
- Basic feed delivery and water supply management
- Mechanized operation of poultry house cleaning and waste disposal
- Preliminary statistics and report generation for production data
- Collection of vital signs data in routine health checks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize feed ratios and feeding times through AI analysis
- Early detection of abnormal behavior in sick poultry using computer vision
- Predicting egg production/slaughter volume based on production data to plan sales
- Efficiency improved in inspecting large poultry houses with drones or ground robots
- AI-assisted epidemic early warning and biosecurity decision support

**Human moat:**

- Acute observation and immediate handling of abnormal behavior in individual poultry.
- veterinary-level disease diagnosis and medication decisions
- Emergency management for sudden weather or equipment failure
- Animal welfare operations in special production systems like organic and free-range
- Regulatory compliance responsibilities related to government quarantine and food certification

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and basic troubleshooting of agricultural IoT devices
- Data analysis and visualization tools (e.g., Excel, Tableau)
- Understanding the application of computer vision systems in farming
- Deepened knowledge of animal behavior and biosecurity
- IT fundamentals and automated systems management skills
- Food safety regulations and sustainable farming practices

**Key growth areas:**

- Automation
- Animal Welfare
- Organic Production
- Sustainability

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a poultry producer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries for poultry producers vary by experience and farm size, but entry-level workers often earn around 25,000-30,000 SEK per month. Farm managers may earn 35,000-45,000 SEK per month. Collective agreements set minimums.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a poultry producer in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The job must meet collective agreement pay and terms, and the employer must advertise to EU/EEA workers first. Poultry producer is not on the shortage list, so permits are not prioritized.

**Q: What are the opportunities for advancement?**
A: With experience, you can become a farm supervisor, manager, or start your own farm. Further training in animal science or business can lead to roles in agribusiness or consultancy.
