# Business Services Agent Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3339 (ISCO08)**

Business services agents in Sweden act as intermediaries for a variety of services, including real estate, insurance, and other business-to-business transactions. They facilitate connections between buyers and sellers, requiring strong communication and negotiation skills. The Swedish labour market for these agents is influenced by economic cycles, with demand fluctuating based on business activity and investment levels. Many agents are employed by specialized agencies or work as independent contractors. Digitalization is reshaping the field through online platforms and data-driven matchmaking, while specialization in sectors like commercial property or corporate insurance remains key to success.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression for business services agents often involves moving from general roles to specialized areas, such as commercial real estate or marine insurance. Experienced agents may advance to team leader or branch manager positions, or start their own agencies. Continuous professional development and networking are important for advancement. Some agents transition to related fields like business consulting or sales management. The field offers flexibility, but success heavily depends on building a strong client base and reputation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a business services agent in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector, experience, and commission structure. Entry-level positions often have a base salary plus commission, while experienced agents can earn significantly more. Collective agreements set minimum levels in many industries.

**Q: Can I get a work visa as a business services agent in Sweden?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA nationals can apply for a work permit if they have a job offer that meets the terms of Swedish collective agreements. The employer must first advertise the position within the EU/EEA. The role does not currently qualify for a fast-track or shortage list visa, but it is eligible for the standard work permit route.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for business services agents in Sweden?**
A: Agents can progress to senior agent roles, team leadership, or branch management. Specialization in high-demand niches like commercial real estate or corporate insurance offers better prospects. Some agents eventually start their own agencies or move into related fields like business consulting.
