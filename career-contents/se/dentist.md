# Dentists — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2261 (ISCO08)**

Dentists in Sweden diagnose and treat oral conditions, performing procedures like fillings, extractions, and root canals, while promoting preventive care. The profession is regulated by the National Board of Health and Welfare (Socialstyrelsen), requiring a five-year university degree and a state licence. The Swedish healthcare system includes both public (Folktandvården) and private dental clinics. Due to an ageing population and increased emphasis on oral health, demand for dentists remains high, particularly in rural and northern regions. The occupation is classified as a shortage profession, making it attractive for foreign-trained professionals who meet licensing requirements.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 4,442. According to the Swedish Public Employment Service, the outlook for dentists is good, with shortages in most counties. Competition is higher in Stockholm, but overall demand exceeds supply, driven by demographic shifts and expanded preventive care. The occupation is on the Swedish Migration Agency's shortage list, facilitating work permits for non-EU professionals. EU/EEA dentists can practice after qualification recognition. However, immigration is subject to language and clinical skill assessments.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression often begins in the public sector before moving to private practice or specialization (e.g., orthodontics, oral surgery). Many dentists pursue further training in digital dentistry, implantology, or aesthetic procedures. Leadership roles in public dental health administration or academic positions are possible. With experience, dentists may open their own clinics, offering higher earning potential but also business risks.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial screening and anomaly marking of X-rays and CT scans (AI reading)
- Administrative paperwork such as patient appointment, billing, and medical record organization
- Standardized preventive procedures like teeth cleaning and fluoride application (automated dental chair)
- Basic intraoral scanning and digital model generation
- Automated review and filling of insurance claim documents

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted diagnostic systems provide treatment suggestions, improving diagnosis accuracy
- Simulate surgical paths via digital twin to optimize implant/orthodontic planning
- Real-time voice recording and medical record generation, freeing doctor's attention
- Personalized prevention plan recommendations based on patient historical data
- Virtual reality training systems enhance complex surgical skills

**Human moat:**

- Complex Surgical Decisions and Manual Operations (e.g., Root Canals, Tooth Extractions)
- Patient communication, empathy, and behaviour management (especially for children/anxious patients)
- Interdisciplinary Comprehensive Diagnosis (e.g., Oral-Systemic Disease Links)
- Emergency management and clinical judgement for sudden conditions
- Professional licensing and legal liability

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted Diagnostic Tool Operation and Result Interpretation
- Digital oral scanning and 3D printing design
- Patient interaction via telehealth platforms
- Basic data analysis and clinical research
- Patient communication and digital health education
- Continuous learning of automated workflow integration

**Key growth areas:**

- geriatric dentistry
- implantology
- aesthetic dentistry
- digital dentistry

Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for dentists in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level salaries start around 30,000 SEK/month, while experienced dentists can earn up to 60,000 SEK/month. Dentists in private practice or with specializations may earn higher.

**Q: Can non-EU dentists easily get a work visa for Sweden?**
A: Yes, because dentistry is on the shortage list. You need a job offer with adequate salary (matching collective agreements) and a valid license approved by Socialstyrelsen.

**Q: How long does it take to become a dentist in Sweden?**
A: A 5-year university program leading to a degree in dentistry (tandläkarexamen). After that, you must apply for a license, which can take a few months.
