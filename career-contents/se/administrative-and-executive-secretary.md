# Administrative and Executive Secretary — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3343 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, administrative and executive secretaries play a vital role in managing office operations, supporting executives, and handling communication in both private companies and public agencies. The role requires strong organizational skills, digital literacy, and fluency in Swedish. With increasing digitalization, secretaries are expected to handle more complex tasks, making the profession adaptable.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 77,682. The labor market for administrative secretaries in Sweden is stable but slowly declining due to automation and digital tools. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression often leads to roles such as office manager, executive assistant, or project coordinator. Many advance by gaining specialized knowledge in areas like HR, finance, or IT, or by pursuing higher education in business administration. Continuous learning in digital tools and languages is key for long-term growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Calendar management and meeting scheduling (AI scheduling tools automatically coordinate times)
- Basic document processing (e.g., formatting documents, generating email templates)
- Data entry and information retrieval (natural language queries replace manual operations)
- Meeting minutes and summary generation (speech-to-text + AI summary)
- Travel booking and expense reimbursement (automated system processing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Executive communication strategy suggestions (AI analyzes email tone, priority, aids decision-making)
- Project progress tracking and early warning (AI integrates multi-source data, automatically flags risks)
- Information organization in cross-departmental coordination (AI extracts key points, assists reporting)
- Data analysis report writing (AI generates charts and initial insights for manual optimization)
- Emergency response (AI provides historical cases and contingency plans)

**Human moat:**

- Maintaining complex interpersonal relationships and emotional support
- Judgment and political sensitivity in high-level decision-making
- Confidential information handling and ethical judgement
- Creative problem-solving for unstructured problems
- Flexible adaptability in cross-cultural and cross-level communication

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced application of AI office tools (e.g., Copilot, Notion AI)
- Data visualization and basic data analysis
- Operation of project management systems (e.g., Asana, Jira)
- Emotional intelligence and conflict management training
- Basic programming (Python script for automating repetitive tasks)
- Information security and compliance awareness

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization
- Remote work
- Executive support
- Office automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What factors influence the salary of an administrative secretary in Sweden?**
A: Salary depends on experience, sector (private vs public), and location. Stockholm and private companies often pay higher.

**Q: Is it easy to get a work permit for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: EU/EEA citizens need no permit. Non-EU citizens can apply if they have a job offer meeting collective agreement pay, but the occupation is not on the shortage list, so processing may be slower.

**Q: What are common career advancement opportunities?**
A: Many move into executive assistant, office manager, or specialized administrative roles in HR or finance.
