# Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1431 (ISCO08)**

Sports, recreation and cultural centre managers in Sweden oversee facilities such as sports halls, swimming pools, libraries, and cultural centres, ensuring efficient operations, staff management, and visitor satisfaction. The role involves budgeting, programming, and compliance with safety regulations. The sector is heavily municipal-led, with many centres run by local governments. Demand is stable, with opportunities in growing areas like wellness and multi-purpose event venues.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from supervisory roles in leisure facilities or event coordination, advancing to centre manager, then area manager or director of cultural/recreation services in larger municipalities. Specialisation in sports development, culture programming, or commercial operations is common. Continuous professional development in leadership, project management, and digital tools is valued. Some managers transition to consulting or entrepreneurship in the wellness and event sectors.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by sector (municipal vs private), experience, and location. Expect a range roughly between 30,000 and 50,000 SEK per month. Entry-level may start lower; senior managers in large cities earn more.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a sports centre manager?**
A: Yes, through employer-tied work permit if you have a job offer meeting collective-agreement pay. The employer must first advertise the position in Sweden and EU/EEA. The EU Blue Card is possible if you hold a university degree and the salary threshold is met.

**Q: Do I need a university degree to become a centre manager?**
A: Not mandatory, but a bachelor's in sports management, leisure studies, or business is common. Vocational education or substantial experience can also qualify.
