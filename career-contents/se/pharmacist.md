# Pharmacists — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2262 (ISCO08)**

Pharmacists in Sweden are highly trusted healthcare professionals responsible for dispensing medications, counselling patients on safe drug use, and collaborating with physicians to optimize pharmacotherapy. Working primarily in community pharmacies (Apotek), hospital pharmacies, and the pharmaceutical industry, they play a critical role in public health, supported by a strong regulatory framework from the Medical Products Agency and the National Board of Health and Welfare.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 1,743. The employment outlook for pharmacists in Sweden remains favourable due to an aging population, increasing prevalence of chronic diseases, and the expansion of pharmaceutical care. While the number of pharmacy graduates is stable, regional shortages persist—especially in northern and rural areas. The growing emphasis on clinical pharmacy and e-health services creates new opportunities, but competition in urban centres like Stockholm may intensify.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically follows a path from staff pharmacist to pharmacy manager, or into specialised roles such as clinical pharmacy, hospital pharmacy, or regulatory affairs. Many pharmacists also transition to the pharmaceutical industry (e.g., research, marketing, pharmacovigilance) or academia. Advanced degrees (e.g., PhD) can lead to senior positions in drug development or health authorities. Continuous professional development is encouraged and often mandatory for license renewal.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine prescription review and drug interaction checks
- Standardized drug dispensing and dose calculation
- Inventory management and automatic replenishment
- Drug information retrieval and simple medication consultation
- Automatic bill reconciliation with insurance systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to predict individual drug responses and adverse reaction risks
- Personalized medication regimen optimization based on clinical data
- Smart triage and remote patient medication follow-up
- Improved Vaccine Management and Promotion Efficiency
- Pharmacoeconomic analysis and real-world evidence generation

**Human moat:**

- Medication management and clinical decision-making for complex cases.
- Face-to-face patient consultation and medication adherence intervention
- Coordination and communication within multidisciplinary healthcare teams
- Ethical decision-making and pharmaceutical legal compliance judgment
- Patient education on emerging therapies (e.g., gene therapy)

**Skills to build:**

- AI-driven clinical decision support system operation
- Pharmacogenomics and precision medication analysis
- Telemedicine and digital health tool application
- Statistical data analysis and real-world research skills
- Medical ethics and privacy protection compliance knowledge
- Vaccine management and public health skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Clinical Pharmacy
- E-Health
- Specialist Medication
- Patient Counselling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a pharmacist in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary by sector and experience. A newly licensed pharmacist typically earns around the median for all professionals (approx 35,000 SEK/month), while experienced pharmacists in industry may earn significantly more.

**Q: Do I need to speak Swedish to work as a pharmacist in Sweden?**
A: Yes, fluency in Swedish (minimum C1 level) is mandatory for licensure and effective communication with patients and healthcare staff. Language exams are required.

**Q: Can I work as a pharmacist in Sweden with a degree from another EU country?**
A: Yes, but you must apply for recognition of your professional qualifications from Socialstyrelsen and demonstrate Swedish language proficiency. EU automatic recognition rules may apply for degrees from other EU/EEA states.
