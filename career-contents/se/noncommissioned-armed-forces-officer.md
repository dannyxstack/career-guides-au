# Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 0210 (ISCO08)**

Non-commissioned armed forces officers, known as specialist officers in Sweden, are technical experts and leaders within the Swedish Armed Forces. They serve in roles such as logistics, intelligence, communications, and weapon systems, providing critical support to commissioned officers and enlisted personnel. This career requires Swedish citizenship, extensive military training, and security clearance, with positions concentrated at military bases nationwide. The role combines tactical leadership with advanced technical skills, and is central to Sweden's defense capabilities, especially following recent NATO accession.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins with recruit training followed by a 1.5-year specialist officer program at the Swedish Defence University. Officers advance through ranks (e.g., Sergeant to Master Sergeant) with increasing responsibility and specialization. Experienced officers may move into training, planning, or international missions. Further education can lead to commissioning as an officer.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary for a specialist officer?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary by rank, experience, and specialisation, typically starting at around 30,000–35,000 SEK per month.

**Q: Can non-Swedish citizens become specialist officers?**
A: Extremely difficult; virtually all positions require Swedish citizenship due to security clearance requirements. Non-citizens may serve in limited roles if they obtain citizenship first.
