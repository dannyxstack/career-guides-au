# Shelf Filler — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9334 (ISCO08)**

Shelf fillers, known in Sweden as hyllaplockare, are responsible for restocking merchandise on store shelves, ensuring products are neatly arranged and easily accessible to customers. This entry-level retail role involves unloading deliveries, checking stock levels, and rotating products to maintain freshness. In Sweden, shelf fillers are common in grocery stores, supermarkets, and large retail chains. The job requires basic numeracy and physical stamina, as it involves standing for long periods and lifting heavy items. It is often a stepping stone for individuals entering the labour market, with many positions being part-time or seasonal.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for shelf fillers in Sweden is limited without additional training. Many advance to roles such as department manager or store supervisor after gaining experience. Some transition into logistics or warehouse coordination. The job is often part-time and may not lead to a full-time career by itself, but it can provide a foundation in retail operations. Workers who take initiative in learning inventory systems or forklift operation may improve their prospects. For those seeking a long-term path, further education in business or logistics is advisable.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of retail automation systems
- Inventory data analysis and forecasting tools
- Operation of equipment such as forklifts and electric pallet jacks
- Knowledge of fresh food preservation and handling
- Basic Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Cross-functional team collaboration and coordination

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce logistics
- Food retail
- Seasonal employment
- Urban retail centres

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Sweden?**
A: The monthly salary for shelf fillers in Sweden ranges from 20,000 to 25,000 SEK, depending on the employer and location. This is below the threshold for work permits, making it difficult for non-EU/EEA citizens to qualify for a visa.

**Q: Can non-EU/EEA citizens get a work permit as a shelf filler in Sweden?**
A: It is highly unlikely. Work permits require the salary to be at least the median income in Sweden (around 28,000 SEK per month). Shelf filler positions typically pay less than that, and the occupation is not considered a shortage occupation.

**Q: Is there room for career growth as a shelf filler?**
A: Yes, but limited. Some workers move into supervisory roles, logistics, or retail management after gaining experience. However, formal education is often needed for significant advancement.
