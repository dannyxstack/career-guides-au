# House Builder — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7111 (ISCO08)**

House builders in Sweden construct, renovate, and repair residential buildings, working with wood and other materials. The occupation is in high demand due to a persistent housing shortage and an aging building stock. Most workers are employed by construction companies or work as self-employed contractors. The sector is cyclical but long-term prospects are supported by urbanization and sustainability goals.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to journeyman status. Experienced house builders can become foremen, site managers, or specialize in areas like timber framing or energy efficiency. Many eventually start their own firms. Continuous learning in sustainable building techniques is increasingly important for career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic generation of bill of materials based on CAD drawings
- Basic building structure calculations and optimization (e.g., load-bearing analysis)
- Construction site safety monitoring and anomaly alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to generate multiple design blueprint options for client selection
- Collision detection and construction simulation optimization through BIM+AI
- Using drones and AI for rooftop inspections and damage identification
- Use machine learning to predict material waste and project delays

**Human moat:**

- On-site fine operations such as manual bricklaying, plastering, and carpentry
- Understanding of individual client needs and designing tailored solutions
- Emergency response to unexpected issues on complex construction sites

**Skills to build:**

- BIM software (e.g., Revit) operation.
- Drone operation and image analysis.
- Basic Programming & AI Collaboration Tools (e.g., ChatGPT)
- Knowledge of green building and energy-saving technologies.
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable building
- Renovation
- Modular construction
- Energy-efficient homes

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a house builder in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary with experience, region, and employer. For up-to-date figures, consult Statistics Sweden (SCB) or the relevant trade union. Typically, entry-level workers earn around 25,000–30,000 SEK per month, while experienced journeymen can earn 35,000–40,000 SEK.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a house builder in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement wages and conditions. The occupation is on the shortage list in some regions, which may streamline the process. However, the permit is employer-tied and does not directly lead to permanent residency.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for house builders in Sweden?**
A: With experience, you can become a foreman, site manager, or specialize in areas like sustainable construction. Starting your own construction company is also a common path. Further education in construction engineering or project management can open higher-level roles.
