# Creative and Performing Artist (Not Elsewhere Classified) — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2659 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, this occupation covers a diverse range of performing and creative artists—from musicians, dancers, and actors to comedians and circus performers—who often work on project basis or are self-employed. The labour market is highly competitive with many seeking limited public grants and commercial engagements, though cultural consumption and live events support steady demand in major cities like Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for a creative and performing artist in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary enormously: freelancers may earn from 0 to 400,000 SEK per year depending on engagements, while employed artists at institutions (e.g., orchestra members) earn around 300,000–450,000 SEK. Many rely on multiple income sources and grants.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as a non-EU artist without a permanent contract?**
A: Yes, but you need a concrete job offer from a Swedish employer covering the entire permitted period. Project-based work is possible if the aggregate duration and pay meet requirements. Self-employment is not covered by the standard work permit route.

**Q: Is a formal degree necessary to work as an artist in Sweden?**
A: No, many successful artists lack formal degrees. However, a degree from a respected school can help access networks and grants. Practical experience and a strong portfolio are paramount.
