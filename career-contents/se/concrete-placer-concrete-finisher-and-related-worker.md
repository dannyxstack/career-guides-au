# Concrete Placer and Finisher — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7114 (ISCO08)**

Concrete placers and finishers in Sweden work mainly in construction, preparing and placing concrete for foundations, floors, bridges, and other structures. The role is physically demanding and requires precision to ensure durability and finish quality. With a strong construction sector driven by urbanisation and infrastructure projects, demand for skilled workers remains high, particularly in larger cities and regions with major building projects. Work is often seasonal and project-based, with opportunities in both residential and commercial construction.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to skilled finisher or foreman. With experience, one can specialise in decorative concrete, structural repair, or precast elements. Further training can lead to site management or quality control roles. Some workers become self-employed contractors. Certification in areas like concrete repair or safety can enhance prospects. The trend towards sustainable building practices may create roles in green concrete technologies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mechanical troweling and polishing of large concrete surfaces
- Laser or GPS-based automatic leveling
- Spraying curing agent on standard floors
- Precast production of simple concrete components.
- Surface quality inspection via drones or robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use tablets or AR glasses to view design elevations and construction drawings in real time
- Using algorithms to optimize concrete mix ratios and additive dosages
- Remotely monitor the automatic troweling machine and adjust parameters.
- Sensor-based defect prediction and preventive maintenance
- Digital project management and coordination of multi-trade progress

**Human moat:**

- Manual finishing of complex curves, artistic shapes, or special textures
- Making real-time decisions on unpredictable construction sites
- Experiential perception of concrete setting status
- Directly communicate aesthetic requirements with clients and architects
- Fine operations when machines cannot be used in narrow or high-risk areas

**Skills to build:**

- Operate automatic power trowels and concrete 3D printers
- Use BIM software and digital construction management tools
- Basic programming and sensor debugging
- Read structural drawings and construction specifications
- Knowledge of high-performance concrete and special additives
- Safety awareness and automated equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Green Building
- Infrastructure
- Renovation
- Prefabrication

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a concrete placer in Sweden?**
A: The average annual salary for concrete placers and finishers in Sweden ranges from approximately 350,000 to 450,000 SEK, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level workers may earn around 310,000 SEK, while experienced finishers can earn up to 520,000 SEK.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a concrete placer in Sweden?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for a work permit if they have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the collective agreement pay and insurance requirements. The occupation is not on the shortage list for a simplified process, but permits are granted if the job cannot be filled locally.
