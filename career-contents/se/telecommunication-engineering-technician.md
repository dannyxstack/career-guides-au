# Telecommunications Engineering Technician — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3522 (ISCO08)**

Telecommunications engineering technicians in Sweden install, maintain, and repair telecom networks and equipment, including fiber optics, 5G infrastructure, and broadband systems. They work for telecom operators, network providers, and installation companies, often combining technical expertise with field assignments. The role requires knowledge of network protocols, hardware configuration, and troubleshooting skills. Sweden's strong telecom sector, home to Ericsson, and its rapid digitalization drive demand for these professionals, especially in rural broadband expansion and urban network upgrades.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a telecommunications engineering technician in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary with experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions typically start around the industry minimum, while experienced technicians can earn higher. For current figures, consult arbetsformedlingen.se or union agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a telecommunications engineering technician in Sweden?**
A: Yes, by obtaining a work permit tied to a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement pay and conditions. EU/EEA citizens do not need a permit.

**Q: What education is needed to become a telecommunications engineering technician?**
A: A vocational diploma in telecommunications technology from a YH school or a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering. Certifications like CCNA are also beneficial.
