# Electronics Mechanics and Servicers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7421 (ISCO08)**

Electronics mechanics and servicers in Sweden install, maintain, and repair electronic equipment such as industrial control systems, medical devices, and telecommunications gear. They work across manufacturing, service, and public sectors, often in teams with engineers. The role requires precision and knowledge of circuit diagrams, soldering, and testing. Swedish employers value relevant vocational training and practical experience. The occupation is part of the broader electrical and electronics trades, with good opportunities in regions with strong industrial bases like Västra Götaland and Stockholm.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from vocational training or apprenticeship. After a few years, mechanics can specialise in areas like medical electronics or robotics, or become team leaders. Further education, such as a higher vocational diploma (KYH), opens paths to technician or engineer roles. Mobility between sectors is common. Many experienced workers move into service management or entrepreneurship.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive electronic fault diagnosis (e.g., using automated test equipment)
- Standard circuit board soldering and component replacement
- Routine equipment inspection and recording (replaced by IoT sensors and AI analysis).
- Simple parameter calibration (done by self-calibrating systems)
- Spare parts inventory management (handled by automated inventory systems)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use augmented reality (AR) headsets to remotely receive expert guidance for complex repairs
- Use AI diagnostic tools to quickly identify atypical faults
- Optimizes maintenance plans by simulating equipment performance through digital twin systems
- Use machine learning to analyze historical maintenance data to predict equipment failure
- Collaborate with collaborative robots for precision assembly or repair

**Human moat:**

- Creative diagnosis of non-standard, atypical faults
- Manual operations required in hazardous environments or confined spaces
- Communicate with clients or operators to understand complex requirements
- Ability to quickly learn and adapt to new electronic systems.
- Responsibility for repair decisions involving safety-critical systems

**Skills to build:**

- Basic programming in Python or C (for automation scripts and data analysis)
- Construction and debugging of Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor networks
- Augmented Reality (AR) Maintenance Tool Operation
- Use of Data Analytics and Predictive Maintenance Tools
- Collaborative robot programming and integration
- Linux system basics and network configuration

**Key growth areas:**

- Green energy
- Automation
- Telecom
- Defence

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an electronics mechanic in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level salary is around 26,000–30,000 SEK per month, while experienced mechanics earn 32,000–38,000 SEK. Annual pre-tax is roughly 310,000–460,000 SEK.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as an electronics mechanic?**
A: Yes, if an employer offers a job that meets the pay and insurance requirements. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the employer must show recruitment efforts within Sweden/EU first. After four years, permanent residence may be possible.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities?**
A: With experience, you can become a service technician, team leader, or specialist in areas like robotics or medical electronics. Further studies can lead to an engineering degree.
