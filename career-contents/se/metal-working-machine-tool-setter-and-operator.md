# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, metal working machine tool setters and operators are essential to the manufacturing sector, particularly within engineering, automotive, and industrial production. They set up, program, and operate computer numerical control (CNC) and conventional machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and grinding machines to produce precision metal components. The occupation faces regional skill shortages, offering stable employment for skilled operators, especially those with CNC programming abilities. Demand is driven by Sweden's strong export-oriented industrial base.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically begins as an operator, advancing to setup technician, CNC programmer, or team leader. Many enhance their skills through post-secondary vocational education (Yrkeshögskola) or employer-sponsored training. Additional certifications in quality control, specific machinery brands, or lean production can accelerate advancement. Experienced operators may move into production planning or technical sales.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC programming
- automation
- precision engineering
- digital manufacturing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a metal working machine tool setter/operator in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from 24,000 to 28,000 SEK/month. Experienced operators earn 30,000–35,000 SEK/month, with shift allowances common.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as metal working machine tool setters/operators in Sweden?**
A: Yes, via an employer-tied work permit if the employer offers collective-agreement-level pay and conditions. The occupation is not on a specific shortage list, so a Labour Market Test may apply.

**Q: What training is needed to become a metal working machine tool setter/operator?**
A: Most complete upper secondary vocational education in industrial technology or a post-secondary CNC operator course (Yrkeshögskola). Apprenticeships are also available.
