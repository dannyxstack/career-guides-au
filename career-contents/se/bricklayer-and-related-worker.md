# Bricklayer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7112 (ISCO08)**

Bricklayers in Sweden lay bricks, concrete blocks, and stone to construct or repair walls, arches, and other structures. They work primarily in the construction industry, focusing on residential housing, commercial buildings, and infrastructure projects. The role requires precision, physical stamina, and knowledge of materials and techniques. In Sweden, bricklayers often work in teams and may specialize in restoration or energy-efficient construction.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for bricklayers in Sweden includes advancement to foreman, site supervisor, or specialist in restoration and heritage work. Many pursue further vocational training to become construction managers or start their own companies. Apprenticeships are common, combining on-the-job learning with formal education. Continuous skill development in green building techniques enhances career opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive wall construction (e.g., straight walls, standard brick walls) is taken over by bricklaying robots or automatic laying equipment.
- Material handling and mortar mixing via automated conveyor systems
- Design and layout of simple masonry structures are automatically generated by BIM software
- On-site measurement and positioning using laser scanners and AI correction, reducing manual work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI visual inspection system assists in checking masonry verticality and mortar joint uniformity, improving quality inspection efficiency
- BIM modeling tools help bricklayers precisely lay bricks in complex curved structures
- Augmented reality (AR) glasses overlay construction drawings, reducing reading errors.
- AI brick layout algorithms optimize material usage and reduce waste

**Human moat:**

- Complex irregular masonry (curved, hollow, historical building restoration) cannot be standardized
- Flexible operation in unstructured environments such as bad weather and confined spaces
- Non-standard requirements from owners or architects for aesthetics and texture, relying on craftsman experience
- On-site safety management and emergency judgment, e.g., support, temporary fixing risks

**Skills to build:**

- Operating automated bricklaying equipment and collaborating with robots
- Proficiency in BIM modeling and CAD drawing interpretation.
- Learn drone surveying and laser scanning data analysis
- Proficient in green building and energy-efficient masonry material construction
- Improve project management and multi-trade coordination skills
- Familiarity with building regulations and accessible masonry standards

**Key growth areas:**

- Renovation
- Sustainable building
- Urban development
- Restoration

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bricklayer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are governed by collective agreements and vary with experience and location. Entry-level bricklayers earn a rate that increases with skill and seniority, typically above the industry minimum.

**Q: Can non-EU bricklayers get a work visa for Sweden?**
A: Yes, but the employer must obtain a work permit on your behalf. The job must meet collective agreement terms, and the employer must show that the position could not be filled from within the EU/EEA. The process is restrictive and requires a formal job offer.

**Q: Is there a shortage of bricklayers in Sweden?**
A: Yes, in many regions there is a moderate shortage, especially for skilled bricklayers. However, demand fluctuates with the construction cycle.
