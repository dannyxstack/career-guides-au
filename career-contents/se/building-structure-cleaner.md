# Building Structure Cleaner — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7133 (ISCO08)**

Building structure cleaners in Sweden perform cleaning and maintenance of building exteriors, including windows, facades, roofs, and other surfaces. This role often involves working at heights and using specialized equipment such as lifts and scaffolding. The occupation is common in both urban and rural areas, with demand driven by property maintenance and construction projects. While entry-level positions exist, experience and certifications can lead to higher-skilled roles. The Swedish Public Employment Service categorizes this occupation as having moderate employment opportunities, with a stable demand due to ongoing urbanization and property upkeep needs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for building structure cleaners in Sweden typically moves from entry-level cleaner to team leader or supervisor with experience and further training. Some specialize in high-rise window cleaning or facade restoration, which can command higher pay. Additional certifications in work-at-height safety, scaffolding, or environmental cleaning can enhance prospects. Many workers transition into related fields such as property maintenance or janitorial services management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Clean exterior walls using high-pressure water jets or chemical agents
- Clean glass curtain walls with high-altitude rope work
- Roof debris removal and simple maintenance
- Periodic repetitive facade cleaning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI plans cleaning routes and optimizes chemical ratios.
- UAV inspection to identify dirty areas
- Digital twin simulation of cleaning effectiveness
- Precise application of cleaning agents by automatic spraying equipment
- Real-time sensor feedback adjusts cleaning intensity.

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling complex stains (e.g., bird remains, oil stains)
- Safety risk assessment and emergency rescue capabilities
- Communicating with customers to customize cleaning solutions
- Professional responsibility requiring a high-altitude work permit

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and maintenance
- Fundamentals of programming smart cleaning equipment
- AI image recognition for stain analysis
- High-altitude work safety regulations updated
- Low-voltage fault troubleshooting and basic repairs.

**Key growth areas:**

- Green cleaning
- High-rise maintenance
- Sustainable materials
- Work-at-height safety

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and generally range from SEK 25,000 to 30,000 per month for experienced workers, with entry-level positions lower. Exact figures vary by region and employer.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a building structure cleaner in Sweden?**
A: It is challenging, as the occupation is considered low-skilled and the salary often falls below the work permit threshold (around SEK 30,000 per month in 2025). EU/EEA citizens can work freely, but non-EU applicants rarely qualify.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist for building structure cleaners?**
A: With experience and additional certifications, one can become a team leader, supervisor, or specialist in high-rise cleaning, facade restoration, or green cleaning techniques.
