# Forestry Worker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 6210 (ISCO08)**

Forestry workers in Sweden are primarily engaged in planting, tending, and harvesting forests, as well as maintaining forest roads and trails. The occupation is vital to Sweden's large forest industry, which is a major export sector. Work is often seasonal and physically demanding, requiring outdoor work in all weather conditions. Many workers are employed by large forestry companies or contractors, and the industry is increasingly mechanized, with tree harvesting machines operated by skilled workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a manual forestry worker, then moves to operating forest machines (e.g., harvesters, forwarders) with additional training. Experienced workers can become team leaders, supervisors, or forestry consultants. Further education in forestry engineering or management can lead to higher-level positions in planning and administration. Some workers specialize in areas like silviculture or forest conservation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using GPS and drones for forest mapping and boundary marking
- Early detection of pests and diseases based on AI image recognition
- Automated felling machinery operation and pruning
- Operation of forest fire monitoring and early warning systems
- data recording (e.g., tree growth, harvest volume)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Develop precision harvesting plans using satellite imagery and AI analysis.
- Using VR simulation for safety training and emergency drills
- AI-assisted Forest Carbon Sequestration Measurement and Reporting
- Real-time monitoring of soil moisture and wind speed via IoT sensors
- Mobile apps to track tools and resource locations

**Human moat:**

- Fine control of manually operating tools such as chainsaws in complex terrain.
- Intuition and experience-based judgment of forest ecosystems
- On-the-spot decision-making in emergencies (e.g., bushfires)
- Manual thinning and specific species management within nature reserves
- Social skills for collaborating and communicating with landowners and communities

**Skills to build:**

- Drone operation and maintenance
- GIS and remote sensing data analysis
- Foundational programming (Python/R) for forestry data analysis
- Use of digital maps and mobile applications
- Operation of mechanized harvesting equipment
- Ecological Restoration and Biodiversity Knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Sustainable forestry
- Mechanization
- Bioenergy
- Digitalization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can I work as a forestry worker in Sweden without a work permit?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA citizen, you have free movement and can work without a permit. Non-EU/EEA citizens require a work permit, but due to the low skill level, approval is not guaranteed unless the occupation is on a shortage list or you have a specialized role.

**Q: What mandatory qualifications do I need to work as a forestry worker in Sweden?**
A: A chainsaw license (Motorsågskörkort) is mandatory if you use a chainsaw. Many employers also require first aid training. Additional certificates for forest machine operation are beneficial but not required for entry-level positions.

**Q: Can I advance to operating heavy forestry machinery?**
A: Yes, with additional training (typically 6 months to 1 year) you can become a forest machine operator. This role is in higher demand and offers better pay. Many employers provide on-the-job training.
