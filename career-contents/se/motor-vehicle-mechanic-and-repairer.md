# Motor Vehicle Mechanic — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7231 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, motor vehicle mechanics are essential for maintaining the country's extensive vehicle fleet, with employment concentrated in workshops and dealerships, and growing demand for skills in electric and hybrid vehicle repair.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for motor vehicle mechanics in Sweden typically involves moving from general repair to specialized areas such as diagnostics, electric vehicles, or heavy trucks. With experience, one can become a workshop foreman, technical specialist, or start an independent garage. Continuing education is valued, especially in modern vehicle technologies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults
- Fine disassembly and calibration requiring tactile feedback
- Comprehensive vehicle condition assessment and repair plan decision-making
- Customer trust communication and personalized service.

**Skills to build:**

- Mastery of AI diagnostic tools (e.g., Dealer-Fix, TEXA IDC5)
- Learning hybrid/electric vehicle high-voltage system repair
- Familiar with in-vehicle network diagnostics and software update processes
- Practice using AR-assisted maintenance equipment
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn the basics of data analysis and predictive maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric vehicles
- Diagnostics
- Hybrid technology
- Connected vehicles

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a motor vehicle mechanic in Sweden?**
A: Monthly salaries typically range from 28,000 to 33,000 SEK (pre-tax), depending on experience and specialization. Annual income is around 336,000 to 396,000 SEK.

**Q: How can a non-EU motor vehicle mechanic get a work visa for Sweden?**
A: Non-EU mechanics need a job offer from a Swedish employer who offers terms at or above the collective agreement level. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled within the EU/EEA. The work permit is tied to the employer.

**Q: Are Swedish qualifications recognized for foreign-trained mechanics?**
A: Foreign qualifications may be assessed by the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR). Practical experience is highly valued; additional training may be needed to meet Swedish standards.
