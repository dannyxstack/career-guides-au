# Landscape Architect — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2162 (ISCO08)**

Landscape architects in Sweden design outdoor environments such as parks, gardens, urban squares, and green infrastructure. They integrate ecological sustainability, aesthetics, and functionality, often working on projects related to climate adaptation, urban densification, and public health. The profession is regulated through higher education and professional experience, with most practitioners holding a master's degree. Employment is found in municipalities, architectural firms, government agencies, and consultancy. Demand is driven by urban development, environmental goals, and investments in green spaces.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior landscape architect, advancing to project leader, senior landscape architect, or head of department. Many professionals specialize in urban planning, ecological design, or landscape management. Membership in Sveriges Arkitekter (the Swedish Architects' Association) can enhance credibility. Experienced landscape architects may become partners in firms or establish their own consultancies. Continuing education in GIS, BIM, and sustainable design is valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use AI to generate initial landscape design drafts
- Automatically adjust irrigation schedules based on weather sensors
- Customer inquiries pre-processed by chatbots for common questions
- Automatically generate standardized instructions for pruning and fertilization

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AR/VR for on-site design effect previews
- Drone inspection of large green areas with disease marking
- Smart devices collect soil moisture data to assist precision fertilization
- Use management software to optimize construction scheduling and resource allocation

**Human moat:**

- On-site manual trimming and detailed horticultural operations
- Subjective experience in judging abnormal plant growth
- Face-to-face client interaction to understand needs and provide personalized advice
- Real-time decision-making for sudden weather or plant diseases

**Skills to build:**

- Master AI tools for garden and landscape architectural design (Python/3D modeling)
- Learn to debug and maintain intelligent irrigation systems
- Obtain precision agriculture sensor application certification
- Enhance customer service and project communication skills
- Obtain Qualifications in Tree Pathology and Native Plant Application
- Proficient in landscaping management software (SaaS platforms)

**Key growth areas:**

- urban development
- green infrastructure
- sustainability
- climate adaptation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a landscape architect in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, sector, and location, but are governed by collective agreements. Entry-level landscape architects typically earn around SEK 30,000–35,000 per month, while senior professionals can earn SEK 45,000–55,000 or more. For precise figures, consult Sveriges Arkitekter.

**Q: Is it easy for a non-EU landscape architect to get a work permit in Sweden?**
A: Yes, because landscape architect is on Sweden's shortage list, which streamlines the process. You need a valid job offer meeting collective agreement terms and a recognized degree. The employer must first advertise the position in Sweden and EU. A work permit is issued for the duration of the employment.

**Q: What education do I need to work as a landscape architect in Sweden?**
A: Typically, a five-year master's degree in landscape architecture from a Swedish university or an equivalent foreign degree recognized by the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR). Shorter master's programs (2 years) after a relevant bachelor's may also be accepted.
