# Baker, Pastry-cook and Confectionery Maker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7512 (ISCO08)**

Bakers, pastry-cooks and confectionery makers in Sweden work in traditional bakeries, patisseries, supermarkets, and industrial bakeries, producing bread, pastries, cakes, and confectionery. The profession blends craft tradition with modern food trends, and is closely tied to Sweden's fika culture. Employment is concentrated in urban areas, with a steady demand for artisanal and specialty products. The sector includes both small-scale artisan bakeries and large-scale industrial production.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 2,765. The employment outlook for bakers in Sweden is stable, with consistent demand for bread and pastries. However, automation and centralization may reduce jobs in industrial baking, while artisanal and specialty bakers remain sought after, especially in cities. The shortage of skilled bakers has been noted by the Swedish Public Employment Service, indicating good opportunities for qualified candidates. Seasonal variations exist, but permanent positions are common.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to journeyman (gesäll) and then master baker (mästare). Bakers can become head bakers, production managers, or open their own bakeries. Specialization in artisan bread, gluten-free, or organic products offers growth. Continuous learning and creativity are key, with opportunities in product development and teaching.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically weigh and dispense ingredients based on fixed recipes
- Automatic dough kneading, proofing time monitoring, and oven temperature control
- Mass production of standardized bread and pastries
- Inventory counting and automatic raw material ordering
- Basic bread and pastry shaping (e.g., dough dividing, rounding)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Developing new flavors and nutritional combinations using AI recipe optimization software
- Forecast daily sales through data analysis to reduce waste
- Enhance creative baking using 3D cake design and AR decoration
- AI-assisted marketing and customer customization for social media and e-commerce platforms
- Intelligent temperature control and quality sensors for consistent output

**Human moat:**

- Artisan Decoration and Custom Cake Craft
- New product R&D and recipe innovation without BOM
- On-site customer interaction and understanding of customized requirements
- Manage and train kitchen team
- Flexibly adjust processes in non-standard environments (e.g., temperature/humidity changes)

**Skills to build:**

- AI formulation software and data analysis tools
- 3D cake printer and digital decoration equipment operation
- Use of inventory management and forecasting systems
- Social media marketing and e-commerce platform operation
- Food safety and automated equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Artisan baking
- Organic products
- Plant-based pastries
- Gluten-free products

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a baker in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level bakers earn around 22,000–26,000 SEK per month, while experienced bakers can earn 28,000–35,000 SEK. Salaries vary by region, employer, and skill level. Collective agreements often set minimum pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU baker get a work permit for Sweden?**
A: Yes, if you have a confirmed job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective agreement standards for pay and terms. Bakers are on the shortage list, which may streamline the process. You must also have relevant training or experience.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities for bakers in Sweden?**
A: You can progress from trainee to head baker, specialize in artisan or health-oriented baking, open your own bakery, or move into product development or teaching. Further certifications like gesällbrev or mästare can boost your career.
