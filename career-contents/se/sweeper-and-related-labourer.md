# Sweepers And Related Labourers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9613 (ISCO08)**

Sweepers and related labourers in Sweden perform manual cleaning and maintenance of public spaces, streets, parks, and buildings. They work for municipalities, cleaning companies, and property management firms, often in entry-level positions. The role involves using brooms, leaf blowers, and small vehicles to keep urban areas tidy, with tasks varying by season. This occupation provides initial access to the Swedish labour market for those with limited formal qualifications.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 6,178. Employment outlook for sweepers is stable due to ongoing urban maintenance needs, but growth is limited by automation and mechanization (e.g., street sweeping machines). Many positions are part-time or seasonal, with high turnover. The sector sees steady demand in larger municipalities and during winter months for snow removal, but competition can be high for full-time roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited without further training. Opportunities include advancement to team leader or supervisor roles within cleaning companies, or specialization in industrial or hazardous waste cleaning. Some workers transition to related trades (e.g., janitorial services) or start their own cleaning businesses. Language skills and a driving license are valuable for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection
- Surface wiping with fixed cleaning agents
- Repeatedly patrol and clean along predetermined routes
- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI scheduling and monitoring tools
- Automated cleaning equipment maintenance
- Basic Chemistry and Disinfectant Knowledge
- Data recording and analysis skills
- Communication and customer service skills
- Compliance and safety regulations training

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban cleaning
- Street maintenance
- Public hygiene
- Waste management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are set by collective agreements and vary by employer and region. Entry-level wages are moderate but increase with experience and specialization. Exact figures depend on the sector (municipal vs private) and working hours.

**Q: Can non-EU migrants get a work permit for a sweeper job in Sweden?**
A: It is difficult because the job is considered low-skilled and does not meet the minimum salary threshold required for a work permit (around 27,000 SEK per month in 2024). EU/EEA citizens have easy access, but non-EU nationals typically need a higher-skilled job or a job on the shortage list.

**Q: What are the working conditions for sweepers?**
A: Work is often physically demanding and outdoors in varying weather. Shifts may include early mornings, evenings, or weekends. Seasonal peaks occur during autumn leaf fall and winter snow removal. Personal protective equipment is provided.
