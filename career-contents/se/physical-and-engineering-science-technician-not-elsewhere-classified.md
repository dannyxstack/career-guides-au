# Physical and Engineering Science Technician — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3119 (ISCO08)**

Physical and engineering science technicians in Sweden support engineers in research, development, testing, and production across industries such as manufacturing, energy, and construction. They work with instruments, prototypes, and quality control, often using CAD or other technical software. The role is essential in translating engineering concepts into practical applications. Sweden's strong industrial base, particularly in automotive, telecommunications, and clean tech, drives demand for these technicians. Many are employed by engineering consultancies or in-house R&D departments. The occupation is classified under SSYK 3119 and often requires vocational training or an associate's degree.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves advancing to senior technician roles, project coordination, or specialization in areas like automation or materials testing. With additional education, technicians may qualify as engineers. Continuing professional development in CAD, PLC programming, or project management enhances prospects. Many employers offer internal training and support for further studies. The gig economy is less prevalent, but temporary contracts are common in project-based industries. Union membership is frequent among technicians, and collective agreements ensure competitive pay and working conditions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically record experimental data and generate standardized reports
- Use AI algorithms for preliminary data analysis and anomaly detection
- Automatically calibrate and adjust experimental parameters based on historical data
- Classify samples and perform basic inspections using computer vision
- Automatically organize literature and preliminary experimental design

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using machine learning models to optimise experimental design and improve efficiency
- AI-assisted multi-variable data analysis to discover hidden patterns
- Using digital twins to simulate experimental processes and predict outcomes.
- Quick retrieval of technical documents and specifications using natural language processing
- AI-driven predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime.

**Human moat:**

- On-site equipment fault diagnosis and repair decision-making
- Innovative experimental design and non-standard testing.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration, communication, and explaining technical details
- Safety management and compliance assessment
- Customer requirement analysis and customized solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of Python or R programming
- Introduction to Statistics and Machine Learning
- Digital lab management software (e.g., LabVantage).
- PLC or Embedded Systems basics
- Communication and project management certifications (e.g., PMP)
- Industry-specific regulations (e.g., ISO 17025)

**Key growth areas:**

- automation
- renewable energy
- quality control
- digitalization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level technicians earn around 28,000–32,000 SEK/month; experienced technicians can earn 35,000–45,000 SEK/month. Collective agreements set minimums.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as a physical and engineering science technician?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA nationals can obtain an employer-tied work permit if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 27,360 SEK/month in 2024) and collective agreement conditions. The occupation is not on the shortage list, but demand is high in some sectors.

**Q: Is fluency in Swedish required for this role?**
A: Many employers require Swedish language skills, especially for client-facing or documentation roles. However, in research-intensive companies or international firms, English may suffice. Learning Swedish is highly recommended for career advancement.
