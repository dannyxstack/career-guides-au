# Mining Manager — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1322 (ISCO08)**

Mining managers in Sweden oversee the planning, coordination, and direction of mining operations, ensuring safety, production targets, and environmental compliance. They work primarily in northern Sweden (e.g., Kiruna, Malmberget) in iron ore, copper, and zinc mines. The role demands strong leadership, technical expertise, and familiarity with Swedish mining regulations. Sweden's mining sector is a key export industry, with ongoing investments in automation and sustainability.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Further upward moves may include regional or corporate management roles. Swedish employers value a Master's in Mining Engineering and often require a Bergmästare license for certain operations. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Mining Manager in Sweden?**
A: The average monthly salary for a Mining Manager in Sweden is approximately 80,000 SEK before tax, with experienced managers earning up to 100,000 SEK or more. Salaries vary by mine size, location, and company.

**Q: Can a non-EU Mining Manager get a work visa for Sweden?**
A: Yes, non-EU Mining Managers can apply for a work permit with a job offer that meets collective agreement pay and conditions. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles with a salary above ~53,000 SEK/month. EU/EEA citizens do not need a permit.

**Q: Do I need a Swedish license to work as a Mining Manager?**
A: Yes, in many cases a Bergmästare license from Bergsstaten is mandatory. It requires a relevant degree, documented experience, and passing an exam. This license is legally required for certain mining operations in Sweden.
