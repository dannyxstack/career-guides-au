# Door-to-door Salesperson — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5243 (ISCO08)**

Door-to-door salespersons sell goods or services by making unsolicited visits to homes or businesses. In Sweden, this occupation is declining due to digital marketing and strict consumer protection laws. Work is often commission-based and common in sectors like energy, telecom, or charity fundraising. The role requires strong persuasion skills and resilience to rejection. Most positions are part-time or temporary, and employment is concentrated in larger cities.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 1,268. Employment outlook for door-to-door salespersons in Sweden is negative. The profession is shrinking as companies shift to online and phone sales, and new regulations (e.g., 'Nej tack till reklam' signs) limit access. Automation and do-not-contact lists further reduce demand. The number of active workers is very low, and future opportunities will be scarce, mostly in niche areas like political campaigning or small local businesses.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression is limited. Most door-to-door salespersons move into other sales roles (e.g., retail, telesales) or marketing within a few years. Some advance to team leader or area manager positions in larger sales organizations. The role is often a stepping stone for students or those seeking flexible work, but long-term career prospects are poor without additional education or networking.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Initial screening of potential client lists (AI automatically generates from data analysis)
- Standard product demonstration (chatbot or video alternative)
- Order collection and data entry (automated forms and CRM)
- Common Q&A in objection handling (AI knowledge base auto-reply)
- Follow-up and re-engagement (automated email/SMS sequences)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-personalized conversation scripts (real-time adjustments based on customer profiles)
- Optimize communication rhythm in real-time using emotion recognition tools
- Customer Relationship Data Analysis for Precision Recommendations and Upselling
- AI-assisted note-taking and summary generation to reduce paperwork

**Human moat:**

- Build deep trust relationships and emotional connections
- Complex negotiation and customized solutions
- Creativity & improv performance (humour, empathy)
- Handling non-standard, highly emotional rejection scenarios

**Skills to build:**

- CRM and sales automation tools (e.g., Salesforce)
- Data analysis and customer insight basics
- Video sales and remote communication skills
- AI conversation scripting tools (e.g., Gong, Chorus)
- Introduction to digital marketing
- Emotional intelligence and resilience training

**Key growth areas:**

- Charity fundraising
- Energy sales
- Telecom services
- Local marketing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for door-to-door salespersons in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are often commission-based, so income varies greatly. Average monthly earnings are around 20,000–25,000 SEK but can be lower for part-time. Many workers rely on bonuses or piece rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit for door-to-door sales in Sweden?**
A: It is highly unlikely. Work permits require the job to offer terms at least as good as Swedish collective agreements, which most door-to-door positions cannot guarantee due to low and variable pay. The occupation is not shortage-listed.
