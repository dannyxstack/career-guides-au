# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers assist in food preparation, cleaning, and basic cooking tasks in restaurants, cafeterias, and institutional kitchens. In Sweden, this role is common in the hospitality sector, often filled by young workers, students, or immigrants seeking initial entry into the labour market. The job requires little formal education and is typically low-paid but offers flexible hours.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements, typically around the lowest pay scales in the hospitality sector. Entry-level pay may be around SEK 20,000 per month before tax, but can vary by employer and region. Overtime and shift allowances may apply.

**Q: Can a non-EU/EEA national get a work permit for a kitchen helper job?**
A: It is possible but difficult. The job must be advertised in Sweden and EU/EEA first, and the salary and terms must meet collective agreement standards. Kitchen helper is not on the shortage list, so the Swedish Migration Agency may reject applications unless the employer proves acute labour shortage.

**Q: What career progression is possible for a kitchen helper in Sweden?**
A: With experience and vocational training, kitchen helpers can become cooks or chefs. Many adult education centres offer programmes. Some may move into management in institutional kitchens. However, without further education, long-term advancement is limited.
