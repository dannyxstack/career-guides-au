# Legal Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2619 (ISCO08)**

Legal professionals in Sweden, excluding judges, prosecutors, and advocates, work across law firms, corporate legal departments, government agencies, and non-profits. They provide legal advice, draft contracts, handle litigation support, and ensure regulatory compliance. The role requires a Master of Laws (juristexamen) and strong analytical skills. Sweden's stable economy and increasing regulatory demands in sectors like tech, finance, and sustainability ensure steady demand. Knowledge of EU law and Swedish legal systems is essential, and foreign qualifications may require supplementary courses.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically moves from junior legal advisor or associate to senior counsel, partner, or head of legal. Specializing in high-demand areas accelerates advancement. Many professionals also transition between private practice, in-house roles, and public sector. Continuous education in emerging fields like AI law or sustainability is valued. Leadership roles require strong client management and business development skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic contract review and clause comparison
- Template-based drafting of legal documents
- Legal regulation retrieval and case summary generation.
- Information extraction and classification in due diligence.
- Automated generation of standard compliance reports.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Constructing Strategies for Complex Legal Arguments
- Scenario simulation in negotiation and mediation
- Compliance Risk Assessment and Early Warning
- Intellectual property layout analysis
- Customer legal risk education and training

**Human moat:**

- Discretion involving ethics, reason, and public interest
- Cross-domain strategic decision-making and business intuition
- Building trust and persuasion between people
- Creative interpretation of ambiguous rules.
- Responsibility and correction for AI output results

**Skills to build:**

- Knowledge of data privacy and AI governance laws
- Legal Process Automation (LPA) Design and Project Management
- Cross-cultural communication and negotiation skills

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for legal professionals in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Junior legal advisors earn around 35,000–45,000 SEK/month, while senior counsels can earn 60,000–80,000 SEK/month or more, especially at large law firms or in corporate roles.

**Q: Can a foreign legal professional obtain a work visa for Sweden?**
A: Yes, via an employer-tied work permit or EU Blue Card. You need a job offer meeting collective agreement pay. EU/EEA citizens have free movement. Foreign qualifications may require validation by the Swedish Bar Association or a university.

**Q: Is it necessary to speak Swedish to work as a legal professional in Sweden?**
A: For most roles, fluent Swedish is required due to legal documents and court proceedings. However, some international firms or in-house roles may accept English-only positions, especially in niche areas like EU law or arbitration.
