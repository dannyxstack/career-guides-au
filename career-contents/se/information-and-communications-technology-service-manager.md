# Information and Communications Technology Services Managers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1330 (ISCO08)**

Information and communications technology (ICT) services managers plan, direct, and coordinate the operations of ICT service delivery and support functions. In Sweden, they are key in driving digital transformation across industries, overseeing teams that manage networks, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and enterprise applications. They work in both private and public sectors, with strong demand in tech hubs like Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö. The role requires a mix of technical expertise and leadership skills, often with responsibility for budgets, vendor management, and strategic planning.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 17,480. The Swedish labour market shows robust demand for ICT managers, driven by rapid digitalization, cloud migration, and cybersecurity needs. The Swedish Public Employment Service forecasts a significant shortage over the next five years, especially in metropolitan areas. Growth is fueled by AI adoption, IoT, and remote work infrastructure. Job openings often require several years of experience, but career changers with strong technical backgrounds also find opportunities. ## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts from senior technical roles (e.g., system developer, network engineer) moving into team lead, then manager. Many ICT managers hold a bachelor's or master's degree in computer science or related fields, complemented by certifications like PMP, ITIL, or CISSP. In Sweden, many pursue further leadership training through universities or specialized institutes. The role can branch into CTO, IT director, or consultant. Continuous learning is key due to fast technology changes. Networking within the Swedish IT community is beneficial for advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate project progress reports and status updates
- Initial code review and test case generation
- Automated resource scheduling and budget tracking
- Automated diagnosis and repair of common operational issues
- Routine compliance checks and document archiving

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze project data to predict risks and optimize decisions
- AI-assisted system architecture design and capacity planning
- Quickly generate technical proposals and documentation using natural language interfaces
- AI-based automated testing and CI/CD pipeline management
- Improving system reliability with AI-driven monitoring tools

**Human moat:**

- Decision-making ability for complex system integration and cross-team collaboration
- Deep understanding and strategic alignment with business needs
- Crisis Management and Customer Relationship Maintenance
- Diagnosis and repair of unforeseen system faults
- Team leadership, talent development, and cross-cultural communication

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary of an ICT services manager in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by location, experience, and sector. The median monthly salary is around 65,000 SEK, with a typical range of 55,000–80,000 SEK. In Stockholm, it can be higher, often 70,000–90,000 SEK.

**Q: Can I get a work permit as an ICT manager without a degree?**
A: A degree is not mandatory but strongly preferred. The Swedish Migration Agency requires the job offer to meet collective-agreement standards. Experience can substitute for formal education in some cases, especially with certifications.

**Q: What languages are needed for ICT manager roles in Sweden?**
A: English is widely used in tech companies. For roles in Swedish-speaking organizations, proficiency in Swedish is often required, especially for communication with local teams and clients.
