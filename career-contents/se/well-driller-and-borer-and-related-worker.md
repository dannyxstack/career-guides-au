# Well Driller and Borer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 8113 (ISCO08)**

Well drillers and borers operate drilling rigs to create wells for water, geothermal energy, or construction foundations. In Sweden, this occupation is vital for infrastructure projects, geothermal heating systems, and rural water supply. The work involves setting up equipment, drilling through various ground conditions, and maintaining machinery. Employment is concentrated in construction companies, drilling contractors, and mining firms. The role demands mechanical aptitude, physical stamina, and attention to safety protocols. With Sweden's focus on renewable energy and infrastructure renewal, demand remains steady, particularly in regions with ongoing construction and energy projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a helper or trainee, advancing to driller operator after gaining experience and certifications. Experienced drillers can become supervisors, site managers, or start their own drilling businesses. Additional training in specialized techniques (e.g., directional drilling, geothermal) can lead to higher pay. Some move into related fields like construction supervision or mining engineering. Union representation is common, and continuous skill development is valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual adjustment of drilling speed and pressure is taken over by AI adaptive control systems
- Experience-based judgment of strata changes is replaced by real-time data analysis
- Record drilling logs and parameters, replaced by automated sensors and IoT.
- Repetitive maintenance checks (e.g., lubrication, bolt tightening) performed by robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes geological data to assist in optimizing drilling paths and depths
- Drone and camera AI identification of well site safety risks
- Machine learning predicts equipment failures, reducing unplanned downtime
- Augmented Reality (AR) remote guidance for complex operations

**Human moat:**

- On-site emergency decision-making and troubleshooting ability
- Adaptability assessment for complex terrain and abnormal geology
- Team coordination and safety leadership responsibilities
- Fine manual skills in equipment repair

**Skills to build:**

- Geological data analysis software (e.g., Leapfrog)
- PLC and automation control system operation
- Drone operation and AI image analysis
- Equipment health monitoring and predictive maintenance
- Basic programming (Python/R) for customized reports
- Safety and compliance management (e.g., ISO 45001)

**Key growth areas:**

- Geothermal drilling
- Infrastructure projects
- Water well drilling
- Environmental investigation drilling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a well driller in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from 25,000 to 30,000 SEK per month. Experienced drillers can earn 35,000 to 40,000 SEK per month, with potential for higher pay in specialized roles like geothermal drilling.

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as a well driller in Sweden?**
A: EU/EEA citizens do not need a visa. Non-EU/EEA nationals require an employer-tied work permit, which requires a job offer that meets collective agreement wages (minimum around 27,000 SEK/month) and standard working conditions.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for well drillers?**
A: With experience and additional training, well drillers can become supervisors, site managers, or start their own drilling companies. Specializing in geothermal or directional drilling can lead to higher earnings.
