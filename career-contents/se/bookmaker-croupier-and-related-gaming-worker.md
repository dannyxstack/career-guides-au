# Bookmakers, Croupiers and Related Gaming Workers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4212 (ISCO08)**

Bookmakers, croupiers and related gaming workers operate games of chance in casinos, bingo halls, and online gambling platforms in Sweden. The industry is strictly regulated by the Swedish Gambling Authority (Spelinspektionen). Physical casinos exist in major cities like Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö, while online gambling has grown. The labour market is small but stable, with limited turnover. Most roles require evening and weekend shifts, and a license or background check is mandatory for land-based casinos. Average salaries are modest compared to other service sectors.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a dealer or croupier, advancing to pit boss, table games supervisor, or casino manager. Alternatively, workers can transition into online gaming operations, responsible for live dealer studios or game quality. Some move into gambling compliance or regulation. Continuous training in new games and security procedures is common. The career path is linear within the casino industry but limited outside it.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic odds calculation and dynamic adjustment
- Simple bet processing and payment (replaced by self-service terminals)
- Player Identity Verification and Credit Check Automation
- Basic customer inquiries (chatbot)
- Initial card dealing and chip counting on some tables (RFID technology)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time monitoring of abnormal betting patterns and alerts
- Using big data analytics to optimize casino table configurations based on player behavior
- Multilingual AI translation helps dealers handle international clients
- AR/VR training to upskill new croupiers

**Human moat:**

- Authentic emotional interaction and trust building with players
- Human adjudication of disputes over complex rules or cheating
- Entertainment atmosphere creation and personalised service
- Sensitive judgment on legal and regulatory compliance
- Crisis Management (e.g., Intoxicated Customers, Conflict Mediation)

**Skills to build:**

- Proficient in using electronic table management systems
- Basic data analysis and betting pattern recognition
- Multilingual ability (at least English plus Mandarin or Arabic)
- Dispute resolution and customer psychology
- Familiar with Italian gambling regulations and anti-money laundering compliance
- AI tool operation (e.g., self-service terminal maintenance, chatbot monitoring)

**Key growth areas:**

- Casino Operations
- Online Gaming
- Entertainment
- Hospitality

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for a croupier in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level croupiers earn around 300,000 SEK per year, while experienced supervisors can earn up to 400,000 SEK.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a croupier in Sweden?**
A: It is possible but difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list, and work permits require proof that no EU/EEA worker can fill the role. Most casinos prefer EU citizens.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for croupiers?**
A: Croupiers can advance to pit boss, casino manager, or move into online gaming operations such as live dealer supervision or game quality control.
