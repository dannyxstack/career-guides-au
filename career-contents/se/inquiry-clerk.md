# Inquiry Clerk — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4225 (ISCO08)**

Inquiry clerks, also known as customer service representatives, handle incoming queries from customers by phone, email, or in person, providing information, resolving complaints, and processing orders or requests. In Sweden, they are employed across sectors such as banking, insurance, telecommunications, government agencies, and retail. The role demands strong communication skills, patience, and the ability to multitask. While many tasks are being automated, Swedish employers still value human interaction for complex issues, and the occupation offers a stable entry point into the labour market, particularly in larger organisations.

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 23,713. The employment outlook for inquiry clerks in Sweden is stable but with a slight downward trend due to increasing digitalisation and self-service channels. The Swedish Public Employment Service expects limited demand growth, as routine queries are automated. However, there will continue to be opportunities for clerks handling specialised or sensitive matters, especially in public sector and healthcare administration. The occupation is not on the shortage list, and competition for entry-level positions is moderate. Jobseekers with multilingual skills or experience in complex case handling may have an advantage.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression for inquiry clerks in Sweden typically involves moving into team leader or supervisory roles, or specialising in areas like complaints handling, claims processing, or technical support. Many use the role as a stepping stone into other departments such as sales, marketing, or operations. Internal mobility is common in large firms, and some employers offer training for higher-level administrative qualifications. With experience and additional education, clerks can advance to customer service manager, quality assurance specialist, or process development roles. The occupation also provides a foundation for those pursuing work in finance or insurance sectors.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Handle customer inquiries for general information about products or services
- Provides standardized process guidelines and frequently asked questions
- Records and transfers simple complaints to relevant departments
- Update and maintain customer information databases.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI real-time knowledge bases to quickly retrieve answers to complex questions.
- Using sentiment analysis tools to identify customer emotions and adjust communication strategies
- Optimize multilingual customer communication using AI-assisted scripts
- Use automatic summarization tools to quickly record call key points

**Human moat:**

- Handle highly complex or emotionally charged customer complaints
- Creative problem-solving in uncertain situations
- building long-term customer trust and personalised service
- Cross-departmental coordination and internal process optimization.

**Skills to build:**

- Emotional intelligence and conflict resolution skills
- AI customer service system operation and tuning
- Data analysis basics (customer trend identification).
- Multilingual ability (for serving international clients)
- Advanced communication and negotiation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- customer experience
- digital transformation
- multilingual support
- remote work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an inquiry clerk in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience but are generally aligned with collective agreements for service workers. Entry-level positions may start around the national minimum for the sector, while experienced clerks can earn higher rates, especially in specialised roles. Exact figures are available in the relevant collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as an inquiry clerk in Sweden?**
A: Generally, it is difficult because the occupation does not meet the salary threshold for a work permit unless the role is specialised or involves multilingual support. EU/EEA citizens can work freely. Non-EU applicants may have better chances if they have unique language skills or if the employer can demonstrate a shortage of qualified workers.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for inquiry clerks in Sweden?**
A: Inquiry clerks can progress to team leader, customer service manager, or specialist roles in areas like claims, complaints, or technical support. Many move into sales, marketing, or operations. Internal training and further education (e.g., in business administration) can accelerate advancement.
