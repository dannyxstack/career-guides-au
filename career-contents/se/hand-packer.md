# Hand Packer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9321 (ISCO08)**

Hand packers in Sweden work primarily in manufacturing, warehousing, and logistics, performing manual packing, sorting, and labeling of goods. The role is often entry-level with high turnover, many temporary or seasonal positions, and is common in sectors like e-commerce, food processing, and pharmaceuticals. Work is typically physically demanding and repetitive, with on-the-job training provided. EU/EEA citizens can work without restrictions, but for third-country nationals, obtaining a work permit is difficult due to low wages often below the threshold.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited without additional training. Experienced hand packers may advance to team leader, warehouse supervisor, or machine operator roles, often requiring forklift certification or vocational courses. Some move into quality control or logistics coordination. Continuous learning in warehouse management systems can improve prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual product packing, sealing, and packaging
- Labeling and scanning operations
- Visual inspection and sorting
- Simple quality records and counts.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI vision systems to assist in complex defect detection
- Use AR glasses to get packaging process guidance.
- Optimize packaging materials and process efficiency through data analysis

**Human moat:**

- Fine packaging for irregular, fragile, or high-value items
- On-site rapid diagnosis and resolution of packaging equipment faults
- Custom packaging solution design
- Teamwork and communication skills

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and maintenance of automated packaging equipment
- basic programming (PLC or simple robot teaching)
- Use of data analysis and quality monitoring systems
- Supply Chain Management Fundamentals
- Forklift and other logistics equipment operation certificate
- Basic English (for reading equipment manuals)

**Key growth areas:**

- e-commerce fulfillment
- warehouse automation
- food packaging
- pharmaceutical logistics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand packer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary by sector, region, and experience. They are generally at the lower end of the wage scale, often just above the minimum for work permit thresholds. For precise figures, refer to industry collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: Can a non-EU national obtain a work permit as a hand packer?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. The job offer must meet collective agreement pay and the salary must reach at least 80% of the median wage (around 25,000 SEK/month). Many hand packer positions fall below this, making permits rare. EU/EEA citizens can work freely.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for hand packers?**
A: Advancement is limited without further training. With experience, you can become a team leader, machine operator, or warehouse supervisor. Obtaining a forklift license or logistics certifications can open up better roles.
