# Child Care Services Manager — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1341 (ISCO08)**

Child Care Services Managers in Sweden oversee preschools, daycare centers, and other early childhood education facilities. They are responsible for pedagogical leadership, staff management, budget planning, and ensuring compliance with national curriculum (Lpfö 18). The role requires a combination of pedagogical expertise and administrative skills, often within municipal or private operations. The Swedish labour market for these managers is stable, driven by high demand for qualified early childhood educators and ongoing reforms in the preschool sector.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become a child care services manager in Sweden?**
A: You typically need a Swedish teaching license (förskollärarlegitimation) and often a degree in preschool education. Additional training in pedagogical leadership or a master's in educational leadership is advantageous. Recognition of foreign qualifications is required; contact Skolverket for assessment.

**Q: Can non-EU/EEA nationals work as child care services managers in Sweden?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer that meets collective agreement pay and conditions. The occupation is on the shortage list, which streamlines the work permit process. EU Blue Card is also an option if the salary threshold (approx. 53,000 SEK/month in 2024) is met.

**Q: What is a typical salary for a child care services manager in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by municipality and experience, but the average monthly salary for a förskolechef is around 40,000-55,000 SEK. Public sector salaries are regulated by collective agreements. No specific numbers provided as per instructions.
