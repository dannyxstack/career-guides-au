# Meter Reader and Vending-machine Collector — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9623 (ISCO08)**

Meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Sweden handle manual reading of utility meters (electricity, water, gas) and collect cash from vending machines. With the rapid rollout of smart meters and automated reading systems, the role is shrinking, but still exists in rural areas and for older equipment. The work is often outdoors, involves travel between sites, and requires a driving license. It is a declining occupation with limited new employment opportunities, primarily driven by replacement needs.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; typical paths lead to roles like utility service technician, field operations supervisor, or moving into related areas such as logistics or maintenance. Many workers enter with no formal training and gain experience on the job. The occupation sees an aging workforce, so replacement hiring will persist, but long-term prospects are poor due to technology-driven decline.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings
- On-site inspection of measuring equipment faults (initial judgment)
- Generate manual meter reading reports.
- Reading meters along a fixed route

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use mobile apps or handheld terminals to assist with remote confirmation of abnormal readings.
- Identify potential leaks or tampering by analyzing consumption patterns via AI
- Use autonomous inspection drones or robots for meter reading in high-risk areas
- Provide energy advice to clients based on AI-generated energy saving reports.

**Human moat:**

- Equipment fault diagnosis and repair in complex on-site environments.
- In-person communication with clients over billing disputes or safety issues
- Decision-making ability in emergencies (e.g., gas leaks)
- Manual calibration and maintenance of old mechanical watch heads

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and anomaly detection
- Mobile device and specialized app operation
- Customer service and communication skills
- Basic electrical/mechanical repair knowledge
- Drone or robot operation and maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization
- Rural employment
- Utilities

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a meter reader in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary but are generally in line with other manual occupations in the utilities sector. According to Statistics Sweden, median monthly earnings for these roles are around 25,000-30,000 SEK before tax, with slight variations based on region and employer.

**Q: Can I get a work permit in Sweden as a meter reader?**
A: It is very unlikely for non-EU/EEA citizens, as the occupation is low-skilled and not in shortage. Employers prefer local or EU workers. A work permit would require a job offer meeting the collective agreement salary threshold, but there are very few such offers.

**Q: Is meter reading a stable career in Sweden?**
A: No, the occupation is declining due to automation and smart meters. Job openings are mainly replacement hires, and long-term employment prospects are poor.
