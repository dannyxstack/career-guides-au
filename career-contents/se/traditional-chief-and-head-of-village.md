# Traditional Chiefs and Heads of Villages — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1113 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, the occupation of traditional chief or head of village does not exist as a formal employment category. Local governance is structured through democratic municipalities and county councils, with no recognition of hereditary or traditional leadership roles. This ISCO code is primarily used in other national contexts where such positions are part of local administration or customary law. In Sweden, comparable functions are performed by elected officials or community representatives, but these are classified under other occupations such as legislators or senior officials.
