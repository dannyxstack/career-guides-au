# Database and Network Professional — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2529 (ISCO08)**

Database and network professionals in Sweden design, implement, and maintain databases and computer networks for organizations across all sectors. They ensure data integrity, security, and efficient network performance, supporting digital infrastructure and business operations. The role often involves working with cloud services, cybersecurity, and data management systems. Demand is strong due to ongoing digitalization and the need for robust IT systems.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically starts with roles such as database administrator or network technician. With experience and certifications, professionals can advance to senior specialist, architect, or team lead roles. Many transition into IT project management, cybersecurity, or cloud architecture. Continuous learning is essential, and specialization in areas like cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) or security can accelerate career growth. The path often includes moving between companies or sectors.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine database backup, recovery, optimization and other scripted operational tasks
- Automated deployment and compliance checks for network device configurations
- Rule-based log analysis and alert generation
- Routine performance monitoring and generation of capacity planning reports
- Basic data migration and ETL workflow orchestration

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI assistance for database performance tuning (e.g., index recommendations)
- AI-driven network anomaly detection and root cause analysis
- AIOps for predictive maintenance
- Automatically generate operation reports using natural language generation technology
- AI-assisted cybersecurity threat analysis and response

**Human moat:**

- Complex system architecture design and cross-system integration decisions
- Strategic planning for high availability and disaster recovery solutions
- Business understanding and data model design
- Creative problem-solving for non-standard faults
- In-depth communication and collaboration with business departments and management

**Skills to build:**

- AI/ML fundamentals and model deployment skills
- Advanced operations of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible)
- Data security and compliance (GDPR, etc.)
- AIOps tools (e.g. Datadog, Splunk) usage
- Communication and project management skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud Computing
- Cybersecurity
- Big Data
- Network Automation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EU member state, Sweden admits skilled foreign professionals mainly through the EU Blue Card and national work-permit routes. Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. Rules and salary thresholds change yearly, so always check the official source before making plans.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Analytical thinkers who enjoy solving complex technical problems.
- Individuals who like working with both hardware (networks) and software (databases).
- Team players who can collaborate with IT and business departments.

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer routine tasks and minimal continuous learning.
- Those who dislike troubleshooting and pressure from system outages.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a database and network professional in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and specialization, but are generally competitive and above the national average for IT professionals. For precise data, consult official statistics from Statistics Sweden or relevant collective agreements.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to get a work permit as a database and network professional in Sweden?**
A: Yes, for non-EU/EEA citizens, a job offer meeting the terms of the collective agreement is required. The employer must also advertise the position within the EU. For EU/EEA citizens, no work permit is needed.
