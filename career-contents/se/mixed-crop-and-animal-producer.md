# Mixed Crop and Animal Producer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 6130 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop and animal producers manage farms that combine crop cultivation and livestock rearing. In Sweden, these farmers often operate family-run, medium-sized holdings focused on sustainable practices. The sector is traditionally important but faces challenges from ageing farmers and urbanisation. Many producers diversify into organic farming, agro-tourism, or niche products. The profession is outdoors-oriented and requires versatility in business management, animal husbandry, and crop science.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 3,908. Employment in mixed farming is stable but slowly declining due to mechanisation and structural change. The workforce is ageing, creating replacement demand but few new positions. Growth is expected in organic and small-scale farming, driven by consumer demand for local and sustainably produced food. Seasonal labour needs persist, but permanent roles are limited. Overall, the outlook is moderate with regional variation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression often starts as a farm worker or assistant, advancing to farm manager or owner-operator after gaining experience and often taking over a family farm. Formal education in agriculture or vocational training is common. Many farmers also engage in related sectors like machinery contracting, food processing, or rural tourism. Entrepreneurship and diversification are key to long-term success.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated collection and report generation of crop growth data
- Irrigation and fertilisation scheduling based on sensors and satellite imagery
- Basic financial records and inventory management
- Path planning for automated farm machinery (e.g., autonomous tractors)
- Automatic tracking and alerts for weather forecasts and market prices

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyse soil, meteorological, and historical data to optimise crop rotation and planting decisions
- Using drones and computer vision to quickly detect pests and diseases and apply precise treatments
- Financial forecasting for farm expansion or transition using AI simulation tools
- Using intelligent scheduling systems to optimize labor, equipment, and transportation arrangements
- Automatically generate compliance reports and subsidy applications through natural language processing.

**Human moat:**

- Dynamic response capabilities for handling sudden natural disasters (e.g., drought, floods, fires)
- Managing interpersonal relationships in coordinating cross-cultural temporary labor (e.g., backpackers, migrants)
- Subtle judgments based on local experience and intuition (e.g., irrigation timing adjustments)
- Build long-term trust with supply chain partners
- On-site oversight of animal welfare and ethical standards

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (Python/R, Power BI)
- Drone operation and image interpretation
- Precision agriculture software (e.g., Climate FieldView)
- Basic programming (automation scripts, API integration)
- Project management and digital transformation management
- Agricultural economics and risk assessment

**Key growth areas:**

- organic farming
- precision agriculture
- local food production
- agro-tourism

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and animal producer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries depend on farm size, ownership, and experience. For employees, monthly pay usually follows collective agreements; farm owners' income varies greatly. Check with the Swedish Board of Agriculture (Jordbruksverket) and the relevant union for current figures.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a mixed crop and animal producer in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but requires a work permit based on a job offer. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled from within EU/EEA (labor market test). Since the occupation is not on the shortage list, approval may be challenging.

**Q: What education is needed to become a mixed crop and animal producer?**
A: A typical path is the three-year upper secondary agriculture programme (Naturbruksgymnasium). University degrees in agriculture are also common for farm management or for those planning to take over a farm. Many practical skills are learned on the job.
