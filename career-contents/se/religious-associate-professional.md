# Religious Associate Professional — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3413 (ISCO08)**

Religious Associate Professionals in Sweden support clergy and religious communities in pastoral care, education, and worship. They work in churches, mosques, synagogues, and other faith-based organizations, often focusing on youth work, charity, or interfaith dialogue. The role requires strong interpersonal skills and knowledge of religious texts and practices. Employment is primarily within religious institutions, which are often funded by member contributions or state support via the Swedish Church (Svenska kyrkan). The occupation is not regulated by government but requires endorsement from the relevant religious body.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Religious Associate Professional in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary widely by employer and role. For a full-time position, monthly pre-tax salary often ranges from SEK 25,000 to 35,000, which is below the high-skilled work permit threshold (SEK 35,600 in 2025). Many roles are part-time or stipend-based.

**Q: Can non-EU religious workers get a work permit in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is challenging. The job offer must meet collective agreement pay (minimum SEK 25,000/month) but for work permits, the salary requirement is SEK 35,600/month (2025). Many religious associate jobs fall below this, so employer-tied permits are rare. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Religious Associate Professional?**
A: A degree in theology or religious studies is common but not always mandatory. Most employers require endorsement from a religious body (e.g., ordination). For roles involving teaching in schools, a Swedish teaching license is necessary.
