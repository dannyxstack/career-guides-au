# Survey and Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and market research interviewers in Sweden conduct interviews to collect data for public and private sector research. They work in various settings, including telephone, face-to-face, and online. Employment is often project-based, with demand fluctuating based on research cycles. The occupation is not large but provides entry-level opportunities for those with good communication skills. Proficiency in Swedish is typically required for domestic surveys, while English may suffice for some international projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression for interviewers in Sweden often leads to supervisory roles, project management, or specialization in data analysis. With experience, one can become a senior interviewer, team leader, or research coordinator. Further education in statistics or market research can open doors to analyst positions. However, many use it as a stepping stone to other roles in marketing or social research.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- digital transformation
- qualitative research
- public opinion polling
- customer experience

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a survey interviewer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries in Sweden are determined by collective agreements. For survey interviewers, the average monthly salary is comparable to other entry-level administrative roles, but actual figures depend on experience, employer, and sector. Many positions are part-time or hourly.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens get a work permit as a survey interviewer?**
A: It is very difficult as the occupation is not considered high-skilled. Work permits are typically granted for occupations on the shortage list or with higher skill levels. EU/EEA citizens have free movement.

**Q: Do I need to speak Swedish to work as a survey interviewer in Sweden?**
A: Yes, for most domestic survey roles, fluency in Swedish is required. However, some international research projects may accept English.
