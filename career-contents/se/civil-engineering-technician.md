# Civil Engineering Technician — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3112 (ISCO08)**

Civil engineering technicians in Sweden support civil engineers in designing, planning, and supervising construction projects such as roads, bridges, and buildings. They work on site surveys, material testing, and project documentation. The role is vital in Sweden's ongoing infrastructure investments and urban development, with demand concentrated in expanding regions like Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö. Technicians often collaborate with architects, surveyors, and construction managers, ensuring projects meet safety and environmental standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a junior technician assisting with calculations and inspections. With experience, technicians can advance to project coordinator, site manager, or senior technician. Many specialize in areas like structural design, geotechnics, or sustainability. Further education (e.g., a Master's) can lead to a civil engineer title. Certification through bodies like the Swedish Construction Federation can enhance prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generates standard construction drawings and 2D/3D models
- Automatically calculate material quantities and cost estimates.
- Monitor on-site construction progress and generate reports
- Inspect structural integrity and mark issues

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Structural analysis and simulation testing using AI
- Automated inspection via drones and sensors
- Optimize construction scheduling and resource allocation
- Quickly generate design options for multi-scenario comparison
- Real-time monitoring of construction quality and early warning of anomalies.

**Human moat:**

- Site safety supervision and emergency decision-making
- Coordination with engineers, clients, and construction crews
- Handling complex working conditions and non-standard designs
- Responsible for legal compliance and professional ethics

**Skills to build:**

- In-depth application of BIM software (e.g., Revit, Navisworks)
- Basic data analysis with Python or R
- GIS Geographic Information System Operation
- AI-assisted design tools (such as Autodesk Forma)
- IoT and sensor integration knowledge
- Project management and communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Green Buildings
- Renovation
- Transportation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How is the salary for a civil engineering technician determined in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are typically set by collective agreements between unions and employer associations. They vary by experience, region, and sector, with higher pay in larger cities and specialized roles.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU civil engineering technicians?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an employer-tied Work Permit with a job offer that meets collective agreement terms. The EU Blue Card is less common because the education and salary requirements are higher.

**Q: Is a university degree mandatory to work as a civil engineering technician in Sweden?**
A: No, a degree is not mandatory. Many technicians enter via vocational training (yrkeshögskola) or through long experience in the construction industry.
