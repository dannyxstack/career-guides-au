# Armed Forces Other Ranks — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 0310 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers enlisted personnel and non-commissioned officers (NCOs) in the Swedish Armed Forces, responsible for defense, peacekeeping, and national security operations. Roles include soldiers, sailors, and junior commanders in all branches. Work involves combat readiness, maintenance of equipment, and participation in domestic and international missions. It is a public-sector profession limited to Swedish citizens, requiring completion of basic military training (värnplikt or GMU) and often additional specialization. The occupation offers a structured career with clear ranks, extensive benefits, and a strong sense of purpose.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a soldier or NCO in the Swedish Armed Forces?**
A: Starting salary for enlisted personnel is approximately 20,000–25,000 SEK per month before taxes, depending on role and location. NCOs earn higher, around 26,000–35,000 SEK. Additional allowances for deployments and specialized skills apply.

**Q: Can I work as a soldier in Sweden if I am not a Swedish citizen?**
A: No, you must be a Swedish citizen. The Armed Forces generally do not hire non-citizens for enlisted or NCO roles. Very rare exceptions exist for certain specialist positions, but these require long-term residence and security approvals.

**Q: What training is required to become a soldier in Sweden?**
A: All recruits must complete basic military training (grundläggande militär utbildning, GMU) lasting 3–12 months. After that, specialized training depending on branch and role (e.g., engineer, communications, medic) typically takes another 6–24 months. Many also pursue further education to become NCOs or officers.
