# Dancer and Choreographer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 2653 (ISCO08)**

Dancers and choreographers in Sweden work in theatres, dance companies, TV, film, and as freelancers. The labour market is competitive with many temporary and project-based contracts. Public funding supports some institutions, but private sector opportunities are limited. Many professionals combine performance with teaching or choreography to sustain their careers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a dancer, moving into choreography, teaching, or artistic direction. Many dancers supplement income by teaching at dance schools or offering workshops. Freelancing is common, and building a strong network is essential. Some advance to lead positions in companies or become self-employed choreographers.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic dance teaching: movement breakdown and correction
- Group choreography (automatically generated based on music)
- Replacing real human extras in virtual scenes
- Automatic editing and publishing of dance videos
- Automatic dance notation generation and translation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Dance creation AI assistant (generating movement sequences, exploring styles)
- VR/AR rehearsal and stage effect simulation
- Data analysis optimizes performance choreography (audience preferences, spatial layout)
- AI-driven clothing/lighting design integration
- Personalized Online Interactive Course Tutoring

**Human moat:**

- Live improvisation and emotional expression
- Unique artistic style and innovation
- Preservation of cultural rituals and traditional dances
- Real-time interaction and empathy with audiences
- Complex narrative and cross-media fusion creation

**Skills to build:**

- AI choreography tools (e.g., ChoreoAI, Choreography)
- Motion Capture and Virtual Production Technology
- Basic programming (Python, Web development)
- Digital Content Operations and Social Media Marketing
- Cross-media creation (VR/AR, interactive installations)
- Data analysis and visualization

**Key growth areas:**

- Contemporary dance
- Choreography
- Freelance
- Digital performance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a dancer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on employer and role. Employed dancers typically earn between 20,000 and 35,000 SEK per month before tax. Freelancers' income is irregular and often lower.

**Q: Can foreign dancers get a work permit in Sweden?**
A: Yes, non-EU dancers can obtain a work permit if they have a job offer that meets the collective agreement salary and conditions. The permit is tied to the employer.

**Q: Do I need a formal degree to work as a dancer in Sweden?**
A: Not mandatory, but most employers expect professional training from a recognized dance school or a university degree in dance. Experience and portfolio are also valued.
