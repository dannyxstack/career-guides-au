# Stonemason — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7113 (ISCO08)**

In Sweden, stonemasons work with natural stone in construction, restoration, and artistic carving. The occupation is small and specialized, with most employment in restoration of historic buildings, monuments, and landscaping. Demand is tied to the preservation of Sweden's built heritage and custom stonework for high-end construction. The labour market is limited, with few new entrants, making experienced stonemasons valued in niche segments. Most work is project-based, often through small firms or self-employment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a stonemason in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Entry-level stonemasons typically earn around 25,000–30,000 SEK per month, while experienced specialists can earn 35,000–45,000 SEK. Wages are set by collective agreements. Always check current industry rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU stonemason get a work permit for Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. You need a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective-agreement pay and conditions. Since the occupation is not on the shortage list, the Swedish Public Employment Service will check if any EU worker is available. A positive decision is not guaranteed, and the permit is tied to that employer.

**Q: What training is required to become a stonemason in Sweden?**
A: The typical path is a 3-year vocational programme in 'Stenindustri' at upper secondary school (gymnasium), or a 3-4 year apprenticeship with a certified firm. Many stonemasons also study at folk high schools specializing in stone. Practical experience is key.
