# Legal Secretary — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3342 (ISCO08)**

Legal secretaries in Sweden provide administrative and clerical support in law firms, courts, and corporate legal departments. Their duties include document preparation, case management, scheduling, and client communication. The role requires strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and familiarity with Swedish legal terminology. Most legal secretaries hold a vocational college (yrkeshögskola) or adult education (Komvux) certificate. Employment is concentrated in larger cities, with stable demand driven by replacement needs and digitalization of legal services.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving into senior secretary, document manager, or paralegal roles after gaining experience and additional training. Some transition to legal technology specialist positions. Lateral moves to administrative roles in the public sector are common. Proficiency in legal software and e-services enhances advancement prospects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Draft standardized letters and meeting minutes (AI-generated templates)
- Schedule and manage calendars and reminders (smart scheduling tools)
- Answer and transfer routine calls (AI voice customer service)
- Maintain and retrieve paper/electronic files (document management system)
- Data entry and organization (RPA and OCR automation)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Responsible for complex or sensitive business communication (requiring empathy and strategy)
- Coordinating multiple conflicting agendas and making trade-offs (interpersonal negotiation)
- Training or supervising AI tool output quality (human-machine collaboration)
- Handling sudden administrative crises (adaptability)
- Manage confidential or high-level affairs (trust and confidentiality)

**Human moat:**

- Situations requiring empathy and nuanced communication
- Confidentiality and high-trust working relationships
- Unstructured problem-solving and handling unexpected situations
- Cross-department coordination and power dynamics assessment

**Skills to build:**

- AI office tool usage and process optimization
- Basic data analysis and report making
- Project coordination and stakeholder management
- Social media and digital content management
- Advanced communication and writing skills
- Ability to quickly learn new software

**Key growth areas:**

- Legal tech
- E-services
- Digital case management
- Compliance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a legal secretary in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements. Entry-level salaries range from 25,000 to 30,000 SEK per month, with experienced secretaries earning up to 35,000-40,000 SEK. Stockholm and large firms pay slightly higher.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a legal secretary in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it requires an employer to sponsor a work permit. The employer must offer terms at least as good as the collective agreement. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so labour market test applies. EU Blue Card is unlikely due to salary requirements.

**Q: What education is needed to become a legal secretary in Sweden?**
A: The most common path is a 1-2 year vocational college (yrkeshögskola) program in legal secretary or legal administration. Adult education (Komvux) offers shorter programs. Private courses also exist but are less common. Fluency in Swedish and English is essential.
