# Clerical Support Worker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

Clerical support workers not elsewhere classified perform a variety of routine administrative tasks such as filing, data entry, photocopying, mail handling, and telephone reception. In Sweden, these roles are found across all sectors, including private companies, public agencies, and non-profits. The occupation typically requires upper secondary education and basic computer literacy. Due to digitalization, demand for pure clerical tasks is gradually declining, but the role remains an entry point into the labour market for many, especially in offices and service centers.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 48,304. The Swedish Public Employment Service projects a balanced market for clerical support workers over the next five years. Automation of routine tasks may reduce demand in large departments, but roles in smaller offices and those combining administrative with customer-facing duties will persist. Competition is moderate, with more applicants in urban areas. Employers increasingly require familiarity with digital tools and basic office software.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression often leads to specialized assistant roles (e.g., HR assistant, accounting assistant) or office management. Some workers advance through further education to become administrative coordinators or executive assistants. Others transition to customer service or sales. Overall, the role is seen as a stable, low-risk entry position, but long-term career growth depends on upskilling and taking on additional responsibilities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- office administration
- digitalization
- customer service
- data entry

Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: Entry-level salaries are moderate and increase with experience, and are regulated by collective agreements. Exact figures vary by sector and region.

**Q: What visa do I need to work as a clerical support worker in Sweden?**
A: EU/EEA citizens can work freely. Non-EU/EEA citizens need a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets pay and conditions set by collective agreements for a work permit; however, this occupation rarely qualifies due to low skill level.
