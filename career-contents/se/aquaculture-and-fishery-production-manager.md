# Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1312 (ISCO08)**

Aquaculture and fisheries production managers oversee the operations of fish farms, hatcheries, and fishing enterprises in Sweden. They plan production, manage staff, ensure compliance with environmental regulations, and optimize output. The sector is relatively small but growing due to increased focus on sustainable seafood and export opportunities. Managers often combine hands-on work with administrative duties, working in coastal or inland areas. Sweden's strict environmental standards require managers to stay updated on regulations and best practices.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from technical roles such as fish farm technician or biologist, moving into supervisory and then managerial positions. Further education in aquaculture science or business management can accelerate advancement. Some managers eventually start their own aquaculture businesses or move into consulting for sustainable practices. Networking within industry associations and staying updated on technological innovations are key for senior roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manually record farming data (water temperature, feeding amounts, etc.)
- Generate daily production reports and compliance documents
- Image recognition of initial disease symptoms
- Inventory counting and feed order calculation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Uses sensors and AI to predict optimal feeding times and amounts
- Monitor fish health and abnormal behavior through machine vision.
- Simulate the impact of different stocking densities and feed plans on yield
- Real-time water quality monitoring and automatic adjustment of oxygenation and filtration equipment

**Human moat:**

- On-site decision-making for responding to sudden disease outbreaks or environmental disasters
- Experience-based biosecurity management and overall disease prevention planning.
- Negotiations and relationship management with regulators, suppliers, and sales channels
- Assess and implement new sustainable aquaculture technologies

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and statistical software (Python/R)
- Internet of Things (IoT) and sensor system management
- Operation and troubleshooting of automated control systems
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and sustainability certification standards
- Inspection skills for drones or underwater robots

**Key growth areas:**

- sustainable aquaculture
- fish health management
- automated feeding systems
- export markets

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Manager in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, location, and company size. According to SCB statistics, median monthly salaries range from approximately 40,000 to 55,000 SEK. For exact figures, refer to official SCB data.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work permit for this occupation?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for a work permit if they have a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the collective agreement pay and conditions. The employer must advertise the position in Sweden and EU/EEA first. An EU Blue Card is possible if the salary exceeds the threshold (around 54,000 SEK/month in 2024).

**Q: Is this occupation in shortage in Sweden?**
A: Aquaculture and fisheries production managers are not currently on the official shortage list, but there is a moderate demand due to the growing sustainable aquaculture sector.
