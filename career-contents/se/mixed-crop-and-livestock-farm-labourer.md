# Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 9213 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop and livestock farm labourers perform a variety of manual tasks on farms that combine crop cultivation and animal husbandry. In Sweden, this occupation is common in rural areas, especially in the south and central regions. The work is seasonal and often involves planting, harvesting, feeding animals, cleaning stables, and maintaining equipment. Many workers are employed on a temporary or part-time basis, with a significant share being EU/EEA migrants, particularly from Poland and the Baltic states. The labour market is highly dependent on agricultural cycles and weather conditions.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited for farm labourers; many remain in entry-level positions. With experience, workers can move into supervisory roles (e.g., farm foreman) or specialise in areas like animal care or machinery operation. Further education in agriculture (e.g., a vocational qualification) can lead to positions as farm managers or agricultural technicians. However, many labourers leave the sector for other manual jobs in construction or logistics.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using automated tractors for plowing, sowing, and harvesting
- Monitor crop growth and pests/diseases using sensors and AI systems
- Caring for livestock using automatic feeders and milking robots
- Use drones to inspect farms and produce yield maps
- Optimize irrigation and fertilization plans based on historical data and weather forecasts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operating and maintaining smart agricultural equipment
- Using data analysis to improve crop yields and herd health
- Develop comprehensive agricultural strategies to balance ecology and economy
- Collaborate with AI for precision agriculture management
- Train new employees in using digital tools

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of sudden mechanical faults
- Unstructured work in complex terrain or extreme weather conditions.
- Animal emergency medical care and behavioural intervention
- Compliance with organic certification and animal welfare standards.

**Skills to build:**

- Agricultural drone operation and data analysis certification
- Use of precision agriculture software (e.g., FarmOS, Climate FieldView)
- Basic electrical and mechanical maintenance skills
- Digital literacy: Excel/data recording apps
- Basics of crop and livestock health management
- Training for safe operation of AGV/robot equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- organic farming
- automation skills
- animal welfare
- seasonal harvest

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a farm labourer in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by region and task, but the average monthly wage is around 23,000–28,000 SEK before tax. Collective agreements set minimums, but many workers receive hourly wages of 130–150 SEK.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work permit as a farm labourer?**
A: Generally no, because the occupation is considered low-skilled. Non-EU workers would need a job offer with salary and conditions meeting collective agreement levels, but the Swedish Migration Agency rarely grants permits for this type of work. EU/EEA free movement is the main route.
