# Veterinary Technicians and Assistants — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3240 (ISCO08)**

Veterinary technicians and assistants in Sweden support veterinarians in animal healthcare, performing tasks such as preparing animals for surgery, taking X-rays, collecting samples, and providing nursing care. They work in animal hospitals, clinics, and research facilities. The profession is part of the broader animal care sector, which has grown due to increasing pet ownership and a focus on animal welfare. While not a licensed profession, many hold post-secondary vocational qualifications. The role often involves shift work and direct contact with animals and owners.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving from assistant to senior technician roles, possibly with specialization in areas like surgery, dentistry, or laboratory work. Further education can lead to veterinary nursing or even veterinary medicine studies. Experience and additional certifications enhance opportunities. Some technicians advance to supervisory roles in animal clinics or move into sales of veterinary products. The field offers diverse pathways within animal healthcare, but management roles often require further formal education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary interpretation and report generation of routine imaging (X-ray, ultrasound)
- Data analysis for basic laboratory tests (blood, urine analysis)
- Standardized administrative tasks like vaccination reminders and client follow-ups
- Routine symptom recognition and classification in pet health monitoring.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI image analysis to improve diagnostic accuracy in complex cases
- Remote monitoring and wearable devices enable continuous chronic disease management
- Personalized treatment plans based on big data analysis (e.g., dose optimization)
- AI-assisted surgical navigation and robotic operations improve surgical precision

**Human moat:**

- On-the-spot judgment and hand-eye coordination in complex surgeries
- Cross-species clinical reasoning and comprehensive diagnosis of rare cases
- Client communication requiring empathy, end-of-life care, and psychological support.
- Practitioner responsibility: bear ultimate decision-making and legal consequences for treatment plans
- Ergonomic adjustment and real-time titration of drugs and anesthetics.

**Skills to build:**

- Use and result verification of AI imaging diagnostic tools
- Telehealth platform operation and data analysis
- Basic data science (Python/R) integrated with clinical data
- Animal welfare and ethical decision-making skills
- Advanced cross-species pathophysiology knowledge
- Customer relationship management (CRM) and digital communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Animal care
- Pet industry
- Biosecurity
- Veterinary technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a veterinary technician in Sweden?**
A: Salaries follow collective agreements in the animal healthcare sector. Typical monthly pay for entry-level positions is around 25,000-28,000 SEK, increasing with experience and specialization. Exact figures vary by employer and region.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen obtain a work permit as a veterinary technician in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but a job offer is required, and the employer must prove the position could not be filled locally. The job must meet collective agreement standards. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so work permit approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: Is there a mandatory license for veterinary technicians in Sweden?**
A: No, veterinary technicians and assistants are not a licensed profession. However, employers often require relevant vocational education or experience. Certification from a recognized vocational school is advantageous.
