# Services Manager Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 1439 (ISCO08)**

Services managers not elsewhere classified oversee operations in various service sectors such as cleaning, security, property management, and hospitality support in Sweden. They coordinate staff, ensure service quality, manage budgets, and handle customer relations. The role requires strong leadership, organizational, and communication skills. In Sweden, these managers are found in both private companies and public institutions, with demand driven by the growing service economy and outsourcing trends.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Services Manager in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience, but due to the managerial level, they are generally above the national average and typically meet the threshold for work permits. Exact figures depend on the specific industry and collective agreements.

**Q: Do I need a work visa to work as a Services Manager in Sweden?**
A: If you are an EU/EEA citizen, you can work freely. Non-EU/EEA nationals need a job offer from a Swedish employer and must apply for an employer-tied work permit. The job must meet the collective agreement salary and conditions.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Services Managers in Sweden?**
A: With experience, Services Managers can progress to senior management, regional manager, or director roles. Some move into consulting or start their own service companies. Additional certifications in leadership or project management can accelerate advancement.
