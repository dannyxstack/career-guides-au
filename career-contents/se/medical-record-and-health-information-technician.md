# Medical Records and Health Information Technician — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3252 (ISCO08)**

Medical Records and Health Information Technicians, known in Sweden as medicinska sekreterare, manage patient records, code diagnoses and procedures, and ensure accurate health data. They work in hospitals, clinics, and health authorities, supporting healthcare professionals with documentation and administration. The role is increasingly digitalized, requiring proficiency in electronic health record systems and classification standards like ICD-10-SE. The occupation demands attention to detail and knowledge of medical terminology.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically involves specialization in health informatics, classification coding, or health data management. Experienced technicians can advance to team leader or coordinator roles, or move into health IT system administration. Further education in health informatics or public health can open doors to higher-level positions in healthcare administration. Some professionals transition to roles in health data analysis or health policy.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically extract and assign diagnosis and procedure codes from electronic health records.
- Generate standardized medical reports and statistical summaries
- Perform data integrity checks and quality audits
- Process information requests and issue authorizations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze health data trends using AI to support clinical decisions
- Improving coding accuracy through NLP and machine learning
- Build and maintain health information data warehouse
- Implement and optimize AI-assisted coding systems

**Human moat:**

- Deep knowledge of medical processes and clinical terminology
- Coding judgments for complex or ambiguous cases
- Responsibility for ensuring data privacy and regulatory compliance
- Direct communication and collaboration with clinicians

**Skills to build:**

- Health Informatics and HIPAA Compliance
- Data analysis tools (SQL, Python, R)
- AI/ML fundamentals (NLP, predictive modeling)
- Medical coding systems (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS)
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI).
- Project management and change management

**Key growth areas:**

- e-Health
- Digitalization
- Patient Record Systems
- Health Informatics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical records technician in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions start around 25,000 SEK per month, while experienced technicians can earn up to 35,000 SEK or more. For the most current figures, refer to Statistics Sweden (SCB) data.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a medical records technician in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets collective-agreement pay and a work permit. Since the occupation is on the shortage list, the process may be somewhat streamlined, but Swedish language proficiency is typically required.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, you can move into health informatics, become a senior coding specialist, or take on administrative management roles. Further education in health IT or public health can lead to positions in health data analysis or project management.
