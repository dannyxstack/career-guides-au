# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5152 (ISCO08)**

Domestic housekeepers in Sweden provide cleaning, tidying, and light household maintenance in private homes. The profession is small-scale and often informal, with many workers employed part-time. Demand is driven by an aging population and dual-income households seeking help with chores. However, wages are relatively low, and the occupation is not considered skilled migration-friendly.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 2,506. Employment prospects for domestic housekeepers in Sweden are stable but limited. The occupation is small and highly competitive, with many positions being part-time or informal. There is no expected significant growth, as most households prefer to use formal cleaning services or do the work themselves. Automation and smart home technology may reduce demand for basic cleaning tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for domestic housekeepers is limited. Some may move into supervisory roles in cleaning companies or become self-employed, offering premium services. Others transition to related fields like elderly home care or hotel housekeeping. Formal training can improve opportunities but is not required.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Vacuuming/mopping floors (replaced by robotic vacuums/mops)
- Dishwashing (replaced by dishwashers)
- Bed making, clothing folding (replaced by smart bed-making robots/folding machines)
- Regular surface dust removal (replaced by automated dusting robots)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling tools to plan cleaning routes and task priorities
- Monitoring appliance status and scheduling repairs using smart home systems
- Remote control of cleaning equipment via voice assistant or APP
- Use data analytics to optimize cleaning supplies inventory and procurement

**Human moat:**

- Handle complex cleaning issues such as accidental damage and special material stains
- Communicate with employers about customized service needs and build trust
- Manage multiple smart devices and coordinate troubleshooting.
- Engaging in fine manual work (e.g., antique furniture maintenance)

**Skills to build:**

- Smart home device operation and basic troubleshooting
- Basic data analysis (supply inventory management)
- Customer Service and Communication Skills
- Time management and multitasking coordination
- Knowledge of eco-friendly cleaning technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- Elderly home help
- Professional cleaning services
- Household management
- Green cleaning

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary widely but typically follow collective agreements for cleaning staff, which are lower than median wages. Actual figures depend on employer and region.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as domestic housekeepers in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. A work permit requires a job offer with salary meeting the collective agreement, which is often below the permit's minimum threshold. Most domestic workers are EU/EEA citizens or work informally.
