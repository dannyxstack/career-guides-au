# Cabinet-maker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 7522 (ISCO08)**

Cabinet-makers and related workers in Sweden specialize in crafting, repairing, and installing custom wooden furniture and fittings. This trade is integral to the construction and renovation sector, with demand driven by housing development, interior design, and commercial projects. Many work in small workshops or for construction firms, often combining traditional craftsmanship with modern CNC machinery. The profession offers stable employment but requires technical skill and physical stamina.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with a vocational education or apprenticeship, leading to a journeyman or master craftsman status. Experienced cabinet-makers can become supervisors, start their own workshops, or specialize in high-end restoration or design. Continued learning in CNC programming or green materials enhances prospects. The path is hands-on but offers entrepreneurship opportunities for those with business acumen.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD/CAM software to automatically generate cutting paths and material lists
- Batch assembly and cutting of standardized cabinets (done by CNC robots)
- Finished product quality inspection via AI vision detection system
- BIM-based automatic measurement and adjustment planning for on-site installation dimensions
- Automated inventory management and replenishment system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI-driven custom design software to quickly generate client proposals (e.g. space fitting and style recommendations)
- AR/VR-assisted customer preview of finished product results, improving communication efficiency
- AI optimizes material usage and reduces waste
- Smart tools (e.g., electric screwdrivers with automatic torque adjustment) improve precision in manual installation
- Project management platform automates scheduling and progress tracking.

**Human moat:**

- Hand carving and antique finishing techniques for high-end custom cabinets
- Creativity and experience judgment for solving non-standard installation issues on site
- Ability to communicate aesthetic needs with clients and translate them into unique designs
- Machining and assembly techniques for complex curved and irregularly shaped parts
- Touch-dependent skills such as wood property identification and defect repair

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in CAD/CAM software (e.g., TopSolid, WoodWop)
- Learn AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Improve client communication and project management skills
- Operate CNC machining centers and basic robot maintenance.
- Learn BIM modeling (e.g., Revit) for building integration
- Digital manufacturing (3D printing molds, laser cutting)

**Key growth areas:**

- sustainable furniture
- interior renovation
- kitchen design
- CNC woodworking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cabinet-makers in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, region, and employer. Entry-level cabinet-makers typically earn around 25,000-30,000 SEK per month before tax, while experienced masters can earn 35,000-45,000 SEK. Pay is determined by collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: Can I obtain a work permit as a cabinet-maker in Sweden?**
A: Yes, non-EU/EEA citizens can apply for an employer-tied work permit if they have a concrete job offer that meets the prevailing wage (currently around 27,000 SEK/month) and other terms. The occupation is on the shortage list for some regions, which may facilitate the process.

**Q: Do I need formal education to work as a cabinet-maker in Sweden?**
A: Formal education is not mandatory but highly recommended. Most employers prefer vocational training or an apprenticeship. A journeyman's certificate can enhance career prospects but is not required by law.
