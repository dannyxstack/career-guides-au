# Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector and Associate — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 3257 (ISCO08)**

Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates in Sweden ensure compliance with environmental and occupational health regulations through inspections, investigations, and advisory services. They are employed primarily by municipalities, county administrative boards, and private consulting firms, playing a key role in public health and environmental protection. The work involves assessing workplaces, food establishments, and environmental sites for adherence to laws on chemicals, noise, air quality, and safety. Swedish language proficiency is typically required due to the need for communication with Swedish-speaking stakeholders and interpretation of national regulations.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary with experience and employer. Entry-level inspectors earn in the lower range, while senior inspectors earn higher. The salary generally meets the threshold for a work permit. Exact figures are best obtained from relevant trade unions or job advertisements.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but they need a work permit. The employer must first advertise the position locally to show no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. Swedish language skills are typically required due to interaction with Swedish authorities and regulations. The job offer must meet collective agreement pay.

**Q: What is the career advancement potential for this role?**
A: With experience and further education, inspectors can advance to senior inspector, team leader, or specialist roles in areas like chemical control or radiation protection. Some move into policy-making or consulting. Additional certifications can open doors.
