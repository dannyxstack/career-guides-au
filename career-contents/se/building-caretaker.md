# Building Caretaker — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 5153 (ISCO08)**

Building caretakers (fastighetsskötare) are responsible for the daily maintenance, cleaning, and minor repairs of residential and commercial properties. In Sweden, this role is essential for ensuring buildings remain safe, functional, and aesthetically pleasing. Tasks include snow removal, lawn care, painting, plumbing fixes, and overseeing heating systems. The occupation is part of the broader property management sector, with many employed by municipal housing companies, private landlords, or property management firms.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression for building caretakers in Sweden often involves specialization in areas like energy efficiency, heating systems, or green building maintenance. Some advance to facility manager or property manager roles after further education. The job is typically entry-level with opportunities for on-the-job learning, but formal vocational training (e.g., gymnasium fastighetsprogram) improves prospects. Union membership is common, with collective agreements governing wages and conditions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use automated cleaning robots for floor cleaning
- Security patrols using smart sensors and AI monitoring systems
- Manual record keeping of maintenance logs replaced by automated IoT data collection
- Simple fault diagnosis overtaken by AI predictive maintenance systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Monitor building system status using mobile apps and dashboards
- Optimizing energy consumption and cleaning schedules through AI-assisted decision-making
- Use AR glasses for complex equipment repair guidance

**Human moat:**

- On-site judgment and response to emergencies
- Communication and coordination with tenants and property owners, and customer service
- Hands-on ability for complex non-standard repairs
- Overall management responsibility for building operations

**Skills to build:**

- Smart Building Management System (BMS) operation and maintenance
- Basic IoT device troubleshooting
- Data analysis and reporting (energy, cleaning efficiency)
- Customer service and conflict resolution
- Skills in minor repairs and preventive maintenance
- Learn to use AR-assisted repair tools

**Key growth areas:**

- property maintenance
- sustainable buildings
- energy efficiency
- facility management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Sweden?**
A: Salaries are determined by collective agreements and vary by experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions pay lower while experienced caretakers earn more; overall, it is a moderately paid trade.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a building caretaker in Sweden?**
A: Yes, but they need a job offer from a Swedish employer that meets the collective agreement wage, and the employer must show the position was advertised in EU/EEA first. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so approval is not guaranteed.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for building caretakers?**
A: With experience and further education, building caretakers can move into supervisory roles, facility management, or specialize in energy systems or sustainable building maintenance.
