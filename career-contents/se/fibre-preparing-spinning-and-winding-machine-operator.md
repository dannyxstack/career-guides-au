# Fibre Preparing, Spinning and Winding Machine Operator — career analysis · Sweden

**Occupation code: 8151 (ISCO08)**

Fibre preparing, spinning and winding machine operators set up, operate and monitor machines that prepare fibres, spin yarn and wind yarn onto bobbins. In Sweden, this occupation is primarily found in the textile industry, which has declined significantly due to automation and offshoring. Most jobs are concentrated in a few remaining textile mills, often in rural areas. Operators ensure production quality, perform basic maintenance, and adhere to safety standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited. Operators typically start with basic tasks and can advance to senior operator or shift supervisor after several years. Further advancement into production management or quality control is possible but requires additional education. Many workers leave the industry for other manufacturing roles. Specialized training in automated equipment can improve prospects, but the overall trend is toward workforce reduction.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual inspection of yarn tension and thickness (replaced by AI vision system)
- Manually adjust machine parameters based on raw material batch (AI auto-tuning)
- Simple fault troubleshooting for equipment anomalies (diagnostic system automatically alerts).
- Production data recording and report generation (IoT and ERP automatic collection)
- Repetitive loading, unloading, and joint operations (performed by robotic arm)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted predictive maintenance to reduce unplanned downtime.
- Optimize spinning process parameters using big data analytics to improve yield rate
- AI scheduling system enables multi-machine collaboration to improve production efficiency
- AR remote assistance for experts in complex repairs
- Intelligent material handling systems reduce manual handling and lower workplace injuries.

**Human moat:**

- Process experience with special fibers (e.g., high-end wool, carbon fiber).
- Ability to diagnose and repair multi-device linkage faults
- Flexibility in small-batch custom production
- Communication and coordination with other processes (e.g., weaving)
- Environmental and safety management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial robot operation and maintenance
- PLC and automation control system basics
- Data analysis and production process optimization
- Remote monitoring and diagnosis of smart devices
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) basics.
- 3D printing and new fiber processing technologies

**Key growth areas:**

- automation
- technical textiles
- recycling
- quality control

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for this occupation in Sweden?**
A: Salaries vary by region and employer, but the average monthly salary for textile machine operators is around 25,000–30,000 SEK before tax. Experienced operators may earn slightly more, but the range is narrow due to the declining industry.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen get a work visa for this job in Sweden?**
A: It is possible but difficult. The job must offer a salary at the collective agreement level, which is typically met. However, the low demand for this occupation and the availability of EU workers mean that employers rarely sponsor non-EU applicants. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary and is not applicable.
