# Other Social Welfare Professional Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 169 (JSCO)**

These are professionals who provide life support, consultation, and care plan creation for the elderly and disabled at welfare facilities and community comprehensive support centers.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Vocational school (Social Worker Training Course) | 2 years | $2,000,000~$3,000,000 (Tuition: approx. 1-1.5 million yen per year) |
| University (Department of Social Welfare) | 4 years | $4,000,000~$6,000,000 (National/public: approx. 500,000 yen/year, Private: approx. 1 million yen/year or more) |
| Practical experience route (Social Worker National Exam Eligibility) | 5 years | $0~$500,000 (Requires practical experience and designated training) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social Worker (Certified Social Worker) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Required | National qualification. Required for consultation and assistance work. |
| Care Worker (Certified Care Worker) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | National qualification. Essential in care settings. |
| Long-Term Care Support Specialist (Care Manager) | Each prefecture | Optional | Eligible with 5+ years of practical experience |
| Mental Health Social Worker (Psychiatric Social Worker) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | National qualification. Useful in the mental health field. |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 280,000. Welfare demand is increasing with the aging population. Particularly with the promotion of community-based integrated care systems, demand in home support and prevention fields is rising.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career paths: accumulate field experience and advance to chief/manager. Obtaining certifications as a social worker or care worker is advantageous for promotion. Paths to care manager or counselor also exist.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Record keeping and document creation
- Data entry
- Creating standard parts of care plans

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Client assessment
- Care plan formulation (AI suggests candidates)
- Risk prediction and preventive intervention

**Human moat:**

- Empathy
- Complex interpersonal coordination
- Ethical judgment
- Flexible on-site response

**Skills to build:**

- Digital literacy
- Data analysis
- Interpersonal communication
- Team collaboration

**Key growth areas:**

- Community-based integrated care
- In-home support
- Support for independence of persons with disabilities
- Dementia care

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level?**
A: Starting monthly salary is about ¥200,000–250,000, annual income ¥3,000,000–4,000,000. With experience, it rises to ¥4,000,000–6,000,000, but slightly lower than other occupations.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need for this occupation?**
A: With a certified social worker license, 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' is common; for care work, 'Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Care)' is typical. Japanese proficiency (N2 or above) is required.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: Apart from aiming for care manager or management positions, obtaining a certified social worker or psychiatric social worker license and specializing in counseling is another path.
