# Personnel Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 252 (JSCO)**

An administrative role handling general HR tasks such as recruiting, payroll, attendance management, and labor procedures. Often works in a company's HR department.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★★☆☆☆).**

Many start as general clerks, advancing to HR chief, section manager, and HR director with experience. Specialization can lead to a career as an HR/labor consultant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Payroll Calculation
- Attendance Aggregation
- Primary Processing of Recruitment Application Data
- Document Creation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Scheduling and Analysis of Interview Appointments
- HR Data Analysis
- Employee Survey Analysis
- Initial Response to Labor Consultation

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal Negotiation Skills
- Legal Interpretation and System Design
- Handling Confidential Information
- Understanding of Corporate Culture

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- AI Tool Utilization
- Communication Skills
- Legal Knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- HR and Labor Affairs
- Recruitment Strategy
- Payroll Calculation
- Human Resource Development

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of a personnel clerk?**
A: Starting salary is around 3–4 million yen, mid-career 4–5.5 million yen, and managerial positions 5.5–8 million yen. Varies by qualifications and company size.

**Q: How can a foreigner work as a personnel clerk in Japan?**
A: A 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' residence status is required. Conditions include university graduation or 10+ years of practical experience.

**Q: How to advance your career as a personnel clerk?**
A: Obtaining a Social Insurance Labor Consultant qualification or specializing in recruitment, labor, payroll etc. opens paths to management or HR consultant roles.
