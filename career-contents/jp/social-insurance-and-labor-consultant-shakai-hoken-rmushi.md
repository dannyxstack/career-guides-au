# Social Insurance and Labor Consultant (Shakai Hoken Rōmushi) — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 183 (JSCO)**

As an expert in social insurance and labor-related laws, this certified professional handles corporate labor management, procedural representation, and consultation. A specialist in HR and labor affairs.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a Labor and Social Security Attorney?**
A: For employed attorneys, about 5–7 million yen; independent practitioners can earn over 10 million yen, but income varies.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a Labor and Social Security Attorney?**
A: No nationality restrictions for exam eligibility, but Japanese language ability and knowledge of Japanese law are essential. After qualification, work is typically under the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa.
