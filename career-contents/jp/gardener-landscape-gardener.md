# Gardener / Landscape Gardener — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 463 (JSCO)**

A professional who creates and maintains beautiful landscapes by managing planting, pruning, weeding, lawn care, stonework, and paving in gardens, parks, etc.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| High School (Horticulture Course) | 3 years | $0~$300,000 (Public schools nearly free; private schools vary) |
| Vocational Training School (Landscaping) | 1–2 years | $200,000~$500,000 (Some programs free via Hello Training etc.) |
| Specialized School (Landscaping / Greening) | 2 years | $1,500,000~$3,000,000 (Annual tuition approx.) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landscape Gardener Certification (Grade 1–3) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Obtained through skills exam, work experience, or training |
| Civil Engineering Construction Management Engineer | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism | Optional | Advantageous for site management; enables career advancement |
| Landscape Construction Management Engineer | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism | Optional | Required as lead engineer for landscaping projects |

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Demand is stable due to urban greening and environmental awareness. Employment opportunities are good due to ongoing labor shortages from aging workforce. New entrants are welcome.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Start as an apprentice, gain skills through practical experience and obtain certification (Landscape Gardener Certification) to become a team leader or site supervisor. Self-employment is possible, offering diverse career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple grass cutting and cleaning (partially automated)
- Manual tree list management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Drone-based tree diagnostics
- AI proposals for pruning and felling locations
- Garden design with 3D CAD

**Human moat:**

- Judgment based on tree species knowledge and experience
- Delicate pruning techniques
- Client interaction and design skills
- On-site adaptability

**Skills to build:**

- Arborist and tree diagnostic skills
- Drone operation skills
- Landscape CAD and 3D modeling
- Management and Sales Skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Urban greening
- Environmental conservation
- Satoyama restoration
- Public green space management

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for someone starting from scratch?**
A: Around 2.5 to 3.5 million yen in the first year. With experience and a skilled worker certification, it's possible to earn over 5 million yen.

**Q: What residence status does a foreigner need for this job?**
A: Generally, Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Agriculture/Construction) or Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services. The Specified Skilled Worker status requires passing an exam.

**Q: Is it possible to start an independent business?**
A: Yes. After gaining practical experience and obtaining certifications like Skilled Worker or Construction Management Engineer, many people start their own garden management company as sole proprietors.
