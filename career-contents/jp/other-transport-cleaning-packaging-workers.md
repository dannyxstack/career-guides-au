# Other Transport, Cleaning, Packaging Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 739 (JSCO)**

A broad support role in logistics and manufacturing, including product transport, packing, and cleaning in factories and warehouses.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Due to AI and automation, simple tasks like sorting and packaging are decreasing. However, flexibility and adaptive decision-making for diverse situations still depend on humans, making complete replacement difficult.

Easy to enter without experience, but obtaining forklift and hazardous materials licenses helps career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Picking and sorting products
- Routine packaging
- Cleaning in warehouses

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Monitoring and troubleshooting automation systems
- Operating inventory management systems

**Human moat:**

- Adaptive decision-making ability
- Experiential knowledge to detect abnormalities on site

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of machinery such as forklifts
- Basic knowledge of inventory management systems
- Communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Logistics/warehousing
- E-commerce logistics
- Automation/robotics
- Cold/refrigerated storage

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income?**
A: Starting at around ¥2.8 million in the first year, ¥3.5-4.5 million for intermediate level, and over ¥6 million for management or special skills.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: You can work if the job falls under a field covered by Specified Skilled Worker (i) (e.g., building cleaning, manufacturing). However, 'other transportation, cleaning, packaging, etc.' is not covered, so you need to confirm if the actual job type qualifies.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: Common paths include obtaining qualifications like forklift licenses or hazardous materials handler certificates, and promotion to team leader or management positions.
