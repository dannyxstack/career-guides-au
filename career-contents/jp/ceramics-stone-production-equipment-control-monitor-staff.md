# Ceramics/Stone Production Equipment Control & Monitor Staff — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 502 (JSCO)**

Professional who controls and monitors equipment for firing, crushing, mixing, etc., in the manufacturing process of ceramics and stone products. Quality control and process optimization are important.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career path starts from production floor operator, leading to roles in equipment management, quality control leader, plant manager, etc. Obtaining skill certifications aids promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Kiln temperature monitoring
- Crusher operation
- Routine control panel monitoring
- Product visual inspection (replaced by image processing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Identifying cause and responding to anomalies
- Quality improvement through production data analysis
- Planning preventive maintenance schedules
- Parameter adjustment for AI monitoring systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to assess situations and respond quickly to anomalies
- Quality judgment based on five senses cultivated through years of experience
- Troubleshooting equipment failures and repair skills
- Flexible changeover for high-mix low-volume production

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis (Statistical Quality Control)
- Basic Design of Automation Systems
- Machine Maintenance (Electrical/Mechanical)
- Basic Operation of PLC and SCADA

**Key growth areas:**

- Energy-saving firing technology
- Automation and robot introduction
- Recycling material utilization
- Specialty ceramics demand

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for a ceramic and stone product production equipment control and monitoring operator?**
A: Expect around 3-4 million yen in the first year, 4-5.5 million yen after 10 years, and 5-7 million yen for management positions. Passing skill certification exams can lead to salary increases.

**Q: What visa is required for foreigners to work in this occupation?**
A: Generally, a 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is required, with conditions such as a university degree or over 10 years of relevant work experience. Since this field is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker, simple labor is difficult.

**Q: What is the future prospect of this occupation?**
A: Due to declining domestic demand and automation advances, the number of workers is on the decline. However, acquiring skills in equipment operation, maintenance, and improvement can lead to career advancement to plant management positions.
