# General/Purpose/Industrial Machinery Assembly Equipment Control/Monitoring Operator — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 511 (JSCO)**

An occupation that controls and monitors assembly equipment for general, production, and industrial machinery on factory production lines to maintain normal operation.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for this occupation?**
A: Varies by experience: junior ¥3.5–4.5M, mid ¥4.5–6M, senior ¥5.5–7.5M. Overtime and shift allowances are added.

**Q: Can I get a Specified Skilled Worker visa for this job?**
A: Yes. Since it falls under the fields covered by the Specified Skilled Worker (i) visa (primary metal products, industrial machinery, and electrical/electronic information-related manufacturing), it can be obtained by passing the skills test and meeting at least JLPT N4 Japanese proficiency.

**Q: What is the path to career advancement?**
A: Career changes to equipment maintenance, production management, etc. are possible. Additionally, by acquiring knowledge of control programming and robot implementation, you can advance to more specialized roles.
