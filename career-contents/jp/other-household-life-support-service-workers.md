# Other Household Life Support Service Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 359 (JSCO)**

Occupations providing household life support services such as housekeeping, babysitting, and personal care for the elderly.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home Helper (Level 2) | Prefectural Government | Required | Basic qualification for home visit care |
| Certified Care Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | National qualification, essential in caregiving settings |
| Babysitter Certification (Private) | Japan Babysitter Association | Optional | Voluntary but effective for enhancing trust |
| Certified Social Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Handles advanced counseling tasks |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 320,000. Demand for household life support services is expanding due to aging population and increase in dual-income households. Also noted as a focus area under Specified Skilled Worker (caregiving, housekeeping support).

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Entry possible without experience. Acquiring qualifications (e.g., Certified Care Worker) and enhancing expertise can lead to management or self-employment. Practical experience and training are key for career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine cleaning
- Checking monitoring cameras
- Simple cooking

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing visit schedules
- Care proposals based on health data analysis
- Operating communication robots

**Human moat:**

- Emotional empathy
- Delicacy in physical assistance
- Building trust with families

**Skills to build:**

- AI/IoT device operation
- Data analysis basics
- Foreign language support (multicultural families)

**Key growth areas:**

- Housekeeping
- Babysitting
- Elderly care
- Disability support

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the median annual income?**
A: Depending on experience and employment type, the median annual income for full-time regular employees is around 3.5 million yen.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work?**
A: In the caregiving field, Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Caregiving) or Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services (for certified care workers) is common. Housekeeping support falls under Designated Activities.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: By obtaining qualifications such as Certified Care Worker or Social Worker, you can aim for positions like facility manager or care manager. There is also the option of becoming an independent housekeeping business owner.
