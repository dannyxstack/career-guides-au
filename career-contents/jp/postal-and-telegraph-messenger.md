# Postal and Telegraph Messenger — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 701 (JSCO)**

Occupation involving collecting and delivering mail, parcels, and telegrams. Plays a vital role in supporting the communication infrastructure of local communities.

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 280,000. Mail volume is declining due to digitalization, but parcel volume is increasing with e-commerce expansion, and labor shortages persist due to an aging population, so demand is expected to remain stable.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a mail carrier/telegraph messenger?**
A: For a regular employee of Japan Post, starting salary is about ¥200,000, with annual income around ¥3-5.5 million, varying by region and overtime.

**Q: Can foreigners work as mail carriers?**
A: Yes. From 2024, the transport sector has been added for Specified Skilled Worker (i), and applicants with around JLPT N4 Japanese can apply. However, a driver's license is required for pickup and delivery work.

**Q: How can I advance my career from this occupation?**
A: You can aim to become a section chief or branch manager within the post office, or change jobs to a distribution management position at a delivery company. Some people also obtain a large vehicle license and switch to becoming a truck driver.
