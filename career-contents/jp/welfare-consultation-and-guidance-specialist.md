# Welfare Consultation and Guidance Specialist — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 161 (JSCO)**

A professional who provides life consultations, support plan formulation, and coordination with related organizations for welfare service users such as the elderly and disabled.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consultation Support Specialist | Prefectural Government | Required | Obtainable after practical experience. Required for creating plan consultations. |
| Certified Social Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | National qualification. Not mandatory but strongly recommended. |
| Psychiatric Social Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Advantageous in the mental disability field. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Demand is steady due to an aging population and the expansion of the Comprehensive Support for Persons with Disabilities Act. Employment in local governments and social welfare corporations is stable, and labor shortages are expected to continue.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

AI supports the creation of consultation records and information organization, but cannot replace the core of interpersonal counseling. AI will streamline operations, allowing focus on high-quality consultations.

At the beginning of a career, gain experience as a consultation support specialist, with promotion possible to chief consultation support specialist or manager. Obtaining qualifications such as Certified Social Worker can enhance career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Creation of consultation records
- Information collection and organization

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Assessment analysis
- Support for care plan development
- Risk prediction

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal empathy
- Complex coordination skills
- Ethical judgment

**Skills to build:**

- AI and data analysis literacy
- Interpersonal communication
- Care management

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Disability support
- Community-based integrated care
- Shortage of care workers

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with a strong interest in helping others

- People who can persistently tackle welfare issues

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a welfare counseling guidance specialist?**
A: After graduating from university and obtaining a Certified Social Worker license, the monthly salary is about ¥200,000–250,000, with an annual income of around ¥3–4 million.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work in this occupation?**
A: This applies to the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa, but requires qualifications such as social worker and Japanese language ability. Only the caregiving field is excluded under Specified Skilled Worker.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: You can advance by becoming a chief consultation support specialist or manager, or by obtaining higher qualifications such as social worker or psychiatric social worker to enhance your expertise.
