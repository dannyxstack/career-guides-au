# Bathhouse Attendant — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 384 (JSCO)**

Service role handling reception, cleaning, facility management, and customer service at public baths or super sento.

|---|---|---:|

| Vocational Training (Customer Service) | 1 year | $100,000~$500,000 (Public Vocational Training) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 35,000. Employment is declining due to aging and successor shortages, though demand persists in tourist areas and hot spring facilities.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career advancement is limited; some move to managerial roles or independent business ownership.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Payment at automated ticket vending machines
- Floor cleaning by cleaning robots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based congestion prediction and staff allocation
- Service improvement through customer analysis

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal customer service skills
- Facility safety management and emergency response

**Skills to build:**

- Customer service skills
- Machine operation (e.g., cleaning robots)
- Multilingual support

**Key growth areas:**

- Hot spring facilities
- Tourist areas
- Labor shortage
- Aging population

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People confident in physical strength
- People who enjoy interacting with others
- People good at cleaning and organizing

**Who is not suited**
- People averse to cleaning work
- People who want to avoid manual labor

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of bathhouse workers?**
A: Approximately ¥2.5-3.5 million for entry-level, ¥3.5-4.5 million for intermediate, and ¥4.5-5.5 million for advanced.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work as bathhouse employees?**
A: General bathhouse work is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker or Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visas, making it difficult to obtain residence status.
