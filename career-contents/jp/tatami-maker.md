# Tatami Maker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 665 (JSCO)**

Artisan who produces, installs, and repairs traditional Japanese tatami mats. Main tasks include replacing tatami surfaces, making new mats, and laying tatami on flooring.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 14,000. The number of artisans is declining due to reduced demand for Japanese-style rooms, but steady demand remains for high-end Japanese architecture and cultural property restoration. Aging workforce and lack of successors are notable.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a tatami mat maker?**
A: During apprenticeship, annual income is typically 2.5–3.5 million yen. After skill acquisition, 3.5–5 million; after independence, 5–7 million or more.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to become a tatami mat maker?**
A: The most realistic route is Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Construction). You need a skills test and Japanese N4 or above, with a maximum stay of 5 years. Those with exceptional skills may qualify for a Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa, but require 10+ years of experience.

**Q: What is the future of tatami making?**
A: Demand for tatami rooms is declining, but there is steady demand in high-end Japanese-style architecture, cultural property restoration, and renovation. With a shortage of young successors, skilled craftsmen can become independent and earn stable income.
