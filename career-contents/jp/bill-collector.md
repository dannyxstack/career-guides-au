# Bill Collector — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 291 (JSCO)**

A job that involves visiting customers' homes or businesses to collect payments for utilities such as gas, electricity, and water, as well as various other charges. Also responsible for issuing invoices and managing collected funds.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 20,000. Demand is declining due to the spread of cashless payments. However, there remains a certain level of demand from elderly customers and those who prefer cash payments.

**Competition: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

With experience, one can advance to managing collection routes or overseeing collection operations. Some companies offer career paths to sales or accounting positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Issuing and mailing invoices
- Confirming payments and reconciliation
- Door-to-door collection and cash handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Providing in-person services for the elderly
- Guiding non-cash payment methods
- Collecting and analyzing customer data

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal communication skills
- Reliability in cash handling
- Ability to respond flexibly

**Skills to build:**

- IT literacy
- Customer management skills
- Accounting knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Spread of cashless payments
- Aging society
- Declining need for cash collection
- Need for operational efficiency

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a bill collector?**
A: Entry level: ¥2.8-3.8M, mid-level: ¥3.8-4.8M, senior: ¥4.5-5.5M. Commission-based income varies.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a bill collector?**
A: Bill collecting is low-skilled, often ineligible for Engineer/Humanities/Intl Services visa. Not covered by Specified Skilled Worker; immigration routes are limited.

**Q: Can you advance your career as a bill collector?**
A: With experience, you can move into route management, accounting, or sales. Acquiring related certifications also enables career shifts.
