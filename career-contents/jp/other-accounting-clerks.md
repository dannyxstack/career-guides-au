# Other Accounting Clerks — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 269 (JSCO)**

Clerical role using accounting software for slip organization, bookkeeping, and invoice processing, also assisting tax accountants and CPAs.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school diploma | 3 years | $0~$0 (Essentially free at public high schools) |
| Vocational school (Accounting/Bookkeeping) | 2 years | $1,000,000~$2,500,000 (Tuition only. Additional materials cost.) |
| University (Economics, Management, Commerce) | 4 years | $2,500,000~$4,000,000 (For national/public universities. Private universities cost more.) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nissho Bookkeeping Level 2 | Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry | Optional | Basic qualification for accounting roles, recommended by many companies. |
| Nissho Bookkeeping Level 1 | Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry | Optional | Partial qualification for CPA exam; highly regarded. |
| CPA exam subject pass | National Tax Agency Examination Commission | Optional | Path to becoming a CPA; can pass subjects individually. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Gain experience at accounting firms or corporate accounting departments; career advancement via CPA exam or Level 1 Bookkeeping.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Slip entry
- Bookkeeping
- Invoice processing
- Automatic classification of journal entries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Data analysis
- Management advice
- Tax filing strategy planning

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal skills
- Complex tax judgment
- Advice tailored to client situations

**Skills to build:**

- Tax knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud accounting
- RPA implementation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for accounting work?**
A: Starting annual income around 3 million yen, mid-career 4-5 million yen, managerial 6 million yen or more. Significant increase possible with tax accountant certification.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain a work visa for accounting jobs?**
A: May qualify for the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa, but accounting knowledge and Japanese language ability are required. Simple data entry is not approved, so expertise is essential.
