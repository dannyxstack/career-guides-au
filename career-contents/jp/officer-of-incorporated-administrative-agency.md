# Officer of Incorporated Administrative Agency — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 022 (JSCO)**

Top management role overseeing organizational strategy and governance, such as chairman, director, or auditor of incorporated administrative agencies and special corporations.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public Service Comprehensive Exam (National Public Service) | National Personnel Authority | Optional | Gateway to career bureaucracy. Not a mandatory path to agency officers but a strong foundation. |
| MBA (Master of Business Administration) | Graduate school | Optional | Domestic/international business schools. Useful for governance and management strategy knowledge. |
| Certified Public Accountant | CPA Exam (Financial Services Agency) | Optional | Expertise in finance and accounting. Advantageous for auditor or CFO-like roles. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 12,000. While mergers of administrative agencies progress amid the aging population, demand for DX and operational efficiency talent remains steady. Managerial positions in the public sector are declining.

**Competition: very high (★★★★½).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After passing the National Public Service Comprehensive Exam, gain experience in Kasumigaseki and local entities, reach bureau chief level, then become an officer. Career paths are diversifying due to stricter amakudari regulations.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine report creation
- Data aggregation and analysis
- Preliminary legal compliance checks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Risk assessment and predictive analysis
- Performance data dashboard management
- Stakeholder-oriented document preparation

**Human moat:**

- Final decision-making as organizational head
- Liaison role with politics and administration
- Crisis management and emergency response
- Staff leadership and motivation management

**Skills to build:**

- Deep knowledge of finance and accounting
- AI literacy
- Leadership and communication skills
- Understanding of public policy

**Key growth areas:**

- Incorporated Administrative Agency Reform
- Governance Enhancement
- DX Promotion
- Accountability Improvement

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with long-term national civil service careers
- People with strong interest in organizational reform and governance
- People with high ethical standards and strategic thinking

**Who is not suited**
- People seeking rapid career advancement
- People who prefer the free management environment of private companies

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of an incorporated administrative agency executive?**
A: Chairperson: 20-30 million yen, Director: 15-20 million yen. Linked to national civil servant salary levels, including bonuses.

**Q: Can a foreigner become an executive of an incorporated administrative agency?**
A: Extremely rare. In principle, Japanese nationality or permanent residence is required; almost no track record even with Highly Skilled Professional visa.
