# Other Agricultural Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 469 (JSCO)**

Engaged in cultivation management and harvesting of vegetables, fruit trees, flowers, etc., supporting diverse agricultural sites.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 1,750,000. Aging and shortage of successors are severe; robots and AI are being introduced but manual labor remains. A field covered by Specified Skilled Worker.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Automated harvesting robots and AI-based growth management are being introduced, but many tasks require diverse judgment, making full replacement difficult. Human roles remain, but simple tasks will be mechanized.

Transitioning from family-run to corporate farming. Smart agriculture skills can open paths to management and business participation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Field vegetable harvesting
- Weeding
- Simple sorting
- Transplanting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Growth monitoring
- Pest and disease diagnosis
- Irrigation and fertilization control
- Yield prediction

**Human moat:**

- Adaptation judgment to microclimates
- Sense of unity with the land
- Cultivation planning reflecting consumer preferences
- Trial cultivation of new crops

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Drone operation
- Maintenance and operation of automated machinery
- Agricultural Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart agriculture
- Facility horticulture
- Organic farming
- Production for export

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of agricultural workers?**
A: Beginner: approx. ¥2.5–3.5M, Intermediate: ¥3.5–4.8M, Advanced: ¥4.8–6M. Higher if business owner.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in agriculture?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Agriculture) is representative. Technical Intern Training is also common, while the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is limited to highly skilled work.

**Q: What skills are needed in agriculture?**
A: Cultivation knowledge, machinery operation, pest and disease control, and recently, ICT utilization is also important. Skills in autonomous tractors and drone operation are advantageous.
