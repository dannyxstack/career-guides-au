# General Office Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 257 (JSCO)**

General office position in companies/organizations handling diverse clerical tasks such as general affairs, document management, data entry, and phone correspondence.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a general office clerk?**
A: Depending on experience, entry level about ¥2.5-3.5M, mid level ¥3.5-4.8M, senior ¥4.8-6M.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a general office clerk in Japan?**
A: Need Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services status. University degree or 10+ years experience required. Direct application difficult, often needs N2+ Japanese.

**Q: Can I become one with no experience?**
A: Yes, many companies have job offers for inexperienced workers. However, basic PC operation skills (Word, Excel) are required.
