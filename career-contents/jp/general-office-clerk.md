# General Office Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 257 (JSCO)**

General office position in companies/organizations handling diverse clerical tasks such as general affairs, document management, data entry, and phone correspondence.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Routine tasks such as data entry, document processing, and schedule management are being automated by AI and RPA, so demand for general office clerks is declining. However, tasks requiring interpersonal skills and judgment will remain, so the role won't completely disappear.

Start as a beginner, advance to lead clerk or section chief with experience. Career advancement possible with specialized certifications (e.g., bookkeeping).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Data Entry
- Filing Documents
- Automated Email Responses
- Schedule Coordination

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer Service
- Complaint Handling
- Planning and Coordination
- Data Analysis and Reporting

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal Negotiation Skills
- Situational Judgment
- Handling Confidential Information
- Flexible Response

**Skills to build:**

- Bookkeeping
- PC Skills (e.g., Excel Macros)
- Language Skills
- Project Management

**Key growth areas:**

- General Office Clerk
- General Affairs
- Data Entry
- Document Management

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a general office clerk?**
A: Depending on experience, entry level about ¥2.5-3.5M, mid level ¥3.5-4.8M, senior ¥4.8-6M.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a general office clerk in Japan?**
A: Need Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services status. University degree or 10+ years experience required. Direct application difficult, often needs N2+ Japanese.

**Q: Can I become one with no experience?**
A: Yes, many companies have job offers for inexperienced workers. However, basic PC operation skills (Word, Excel) are required.
