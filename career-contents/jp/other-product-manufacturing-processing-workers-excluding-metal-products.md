# Other product manufacturing/processing workers (excluding metal products) — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 539 (JSCO)**

Occupations involved in the manufacturing/processing of non-metal products such as plastic, rubber, paper, and wood. Responsible for machine operation, manual parts processing, assembly, and finishing.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 1,200,000. Long-term employment in domestic manufacturing is declining due to industry contraction and overseas relocation, but certain sectors like food and medical products continue to face labor shortages. Foreign worker acceptance is increasing as this field is covered by Specified Skilled Worker categories (e.g., casting, industrial machinery, electrical/electronic).

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Entry-level workers can start with no experience and advance to team leader or line manager by honing skills. Multi-skilling and quality control knowledge boost career growth. Independent work is possible depending on the job.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple parts attachment and assembly
- Routine processing work (deburring, cutting)
- Product sorting and packaging
- Supplying materials to machines

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Assistance in creating NC programs
- Anomaly detection and quality prediction
- Production plan optimization

**Human moat:**

- Anomaly response and troubleshooting
- Quality inspection using senses like touch and sound
- Workflow changeover know-how

**Skills to build:**

- Automation equipment operation/maintenance skills
- Quality control knowledge (QC certification, etc.)
- Process improvement thinking (IE, etc.)
- Multi-skilled worker (mastery of multiple processes)

**Key growth areas:**

- Plastic processing
- Rubber product manufacturing
- Paper product processing
- Non-metallic material processing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income? Does it increase with years of service?**
A: First year is approximately 2.8-3.6 million yen, intermediate 3.6-4.8 million yen, and advanced 4.8-6 million yen. It can increase significantly by passing skill tests or promotion to management.

**Q: Is there a visa for foreigners to work in this job?**
A: Yes, Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Metal Molding, Industrial Machinery, Electrical/Electronic, etc.) applies. A transfer from Technical Intern Training is also possible. Specified Skilled Worker (ii) is only for some fields.

**Q: Can it be replaced by AI or robots?**
A: Simple assembly and processing steps are expected to become automated, but tasks requiring human judgment like high-mix low-volume production, anomaly response, and quality checks will remain. Becoming multi-skilled or acquiring maintenance skills increases job security.
