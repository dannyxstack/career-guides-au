# Warehouse Worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 704 (JSCO)**

Performs receiving, inspection, sorting, and inventory management of goods in the warehouse. Operates forklifts and handheld scanners to handle items accurately and efficiently.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 1,100,000. Demand for warehouse work remains steady with EC market growth. However, automation and robots are reducing simple tasks. Labor shortages persist, and it's a target field for Specified Skilled Worker status.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

The introduction of AI and automation technologies (automated warehouses, AGVs, picking robots) makes simple sorting and moving tasks easily replaceable. However, tasks requiring complex judgment and flexible responses will remain.

Start with no experience; by obtaining a forklift license and inventory management skills, you can advance to team leader or warehouse manager.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Moving pallets
- Sorting products
- Inventory check via barcode scanning

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimal layout suggestions by inventory management systems
- Receiving and shipping instructions linked with automated warehouses

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment (e.g., handling damaged items)
- Complex picking (irregular items)
- Human communication (team coordination)

**Skills to build:**

- Forklift operation
- Inventory management system (WMS) operation
- Basic PC skills
- Operation and maintenance of robots and automated warehouses

**Key growth areas:**

- E-commerce logistics
- Automated warehouse
- Robot implementation
- Inventory management system

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for warehouse workers?**
A: The average annual income is about 3.7 million yen (from the 2022 Basic Survey on Wage Structure by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare). It varies depending on experience and qualifications.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in warehouse jobs?**
A: Currently, the logistics field for Specified Skilled Worker is not established, but Technical Intern Training or Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visas may apply. Check with the Immigration Services Agency for details.

**Q: How can I advance my career from warehouse work?**
A: By obtaining a forklift license or warehouse manager certification, and acquiring skills in inventory management systems, you can open the path to warehouse manager or logistics management positions.
