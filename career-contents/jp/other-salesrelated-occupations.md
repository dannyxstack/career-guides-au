# Other Sales-Related Occupations — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 339 (JSCO)**

Occupations engaged in sales-like activities such as product explanations, sales promotions, and telemarketing. Mainly door-to-door sales and face-to-face customer service.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| High School | 3 years | $0~$0 (Subject to free public high school) |
| Vocational School (Sales) | 2 years | $1,000,000~$2,000,000 (Includes tuition and materials) |

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retail Marketing Specialist (Sales) | National Association of Retail Marketing Specialists | Optional | From Grade 3 to 1, proof of customer service and sales knowledge |
| Practical English Proficiency Test (Eiken) | Japan English Proficiency Test Association | Optional | Advantageous for dealing with foreign tourists |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 180,000. Due to population decline and the spread of online sales, demand for face-to-face sales positions is shrinking. However, niche areas like door-to-door sales for the elderly retain some demand.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can start with no experience. Career path includes promotion to sales chief or store manager with experience. Specialized knowledge can lead to a shift to sales roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Reading product lists
- Inventory checking
- Simple order processing
- Handling inquiries that can be automated

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer needs analysis
- Cross-sell and upsell proposals
- Handling non-standard complaints
- Event planning and execution

**Human moat:**

- Advanced negotiation skills
- Building trust with customers
- Flexible proposals based on the situation

**Skills to build:**

- Digital tool proficiency
- Data analysis skills
- Language skills
- Problem-solving skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Mail order
- Door-to-door sales
- Telemarketing
- Event promotion

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy interacting with others
- Gentle-mannered people good at explaining
- Patient people

**Who is not suited**
- People not good at communicating
- People who dislike physical fatigue

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for this occupation?**
A: Entry-level: 2.5-3.5 million yen, mid-level: 3.5-5 million yen, advanced: 4.5-6 million yen. Income may be unstable at commission-based companies.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: Obtaining an Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is difficult, and it is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker. Short-term work via Working Holiday is the main route.

**Q: How to advance your career?**
A: Common paths include obtaining a sales clerk certification, switching to a sales role, or promotion to store manager or area manager.
