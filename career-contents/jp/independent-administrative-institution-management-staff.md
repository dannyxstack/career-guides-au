# Independent Administrative Institution Management Staff — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 032 (JSCO)**

Senior management who oversee and manage the overall operations of independent administrative institutions and national R&D institutions. Responsible for budget planning, HR strategy, and business plan formulation.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 18,000. Due to aging population and administrative reform progress, consolidation of independent administrative institutions is advancing, and management positions are expected to remain flat or slightly decrease. However, some R&D institutions have demand for new business creation.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of managers at incorporated administrative agencies?**
A: Approximately 9-12 million yen for section chief level, and 14-20 million yen for department head level, varying by organization size and performance.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a manager at an incorporated administrative agency?**
A: While it is theoretically possible under statuses like Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, actual hiring cases are extremely rare due to the public nature of organizations. Advanced Japanese proficiency and deep understanding of Japan's administrative system are essential.

**Q: How long does it take to progress from a general position to a management role?**
A: For internal promotions, it typically takes 15–20 years to reach section chief and 25–30 years for department head. High-potential individuals may achieve early promotion.
