# Road and Park Cleaner — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 713 (JSCO)**

Work involves cleaning public spaces such as roads and parks to maintain aesthetics and hygiene. Primarily outdoor work, contributing to the upkeep of residents' living environment.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 350,000. Labor shortages due to an aging population persist, with many job openings especially in local governments and contracted companies. Acceptance of foreign workers is expected to increase.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career paths are limited, but opportunities exist for promotion to leader or site supervisor, or management roles in cleaning contracting companies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- cleaning fixed routes
- partial automation of garbage collection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- monitoring and maintenance of AI-equipped cleaning robots
- optimization of cleaning plans through data analysis

**Human moat:**

- flexible response
- handling fine dirt
- communication with citizens

**Skills to build:**

- robot operation skills
- knowledge of cleaning chemicals
- site management skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Labor shortage
- aging population
- public facilities
- cleaning industry

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**
- Not physically confident

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a road/park cleaner?**
A: Depending on experience and region, entry-level approx. 2.5-3.5 million yen, intermediate 3.5-4.5 million yen.

**Q: Can foreigners work as road/park cleaners?**
A: It falls under Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Building Cleaning) but may exclude road/park cleaning, so check in advance.
