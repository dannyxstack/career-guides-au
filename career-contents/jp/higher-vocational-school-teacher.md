# Higher Vocational School Teacher — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 197 (JSCO)**

A teacher who specializes in technical subjects at a higher vocational school (KOSEN). Responsible for practical education in engineering and technology, as well as research and student guidance.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 8,000. While the number of students is declining due to the falling birthrate, KOSEN remains relatively popular, and demand for specialized teachers in engineering and IT fields is stable. A certain number of replacement hires due to retirement are expected.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression from assistant, lecturer, associate professor to professor. A doctoral degree, research achievements, and teaching performance are important. Opportunities also exist for administrative roles (e.g., principal, vice principal).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Research data analysis
- Curriculum design

**Human moat:**

- Research ideas and supervision

**Skills to build:**

- Data science

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for KOSEN faculty?**
A: For national KOSEN, salaries follow national university corporation rules: professors typically earn 8–11 million JPY/year, associate professors 6–8 million JPY/year.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreign KOSEN faculty?**
A: A Professor visa (Status of Residence 'Professor') is required. The Highly Skilled Professional visa is also an option, but permanent residency requires track record and years.

**Q: What is the career path to become KOSEN faculty?**
A: After completing a master's program, the typical route is to be hired as an assistant or lecturer, then accumulate a doctorate and research achievements to advance to associate professor and professor.
