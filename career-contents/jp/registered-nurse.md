# Registered Nurse — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 133 (JSCO)**

A professional who assists in patient care and provides nursing care under physician instructions. Includes Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN).


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Nursing School (3-year program) | 3 years | $2,000,000~$5,000,000 (Tuition varies greatly by region and school.) |
| Bachelor of Nursing (4-year program) | 4 years | $4,000,000~$8,000,000 (National universities are affordable, private universities are expensive.) |
| Licensed Practical Nurse Training (2-year program) | 2 years | $1,000,000~$3,000,000 (LPNs can advance to become Registered Nurses.) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Nursing Examination | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Required | Mandatory qualification to become a Registered Nurse. |
| Licensed Practical Nurse Exam | Prefecture | Required | Licensed Practical Nurse License |
| Certified Nurse | Japanese Nursing Association | Optional | Demonstrates advanced skills in specialty fields. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★★).** National workforce approx. 1,230,000. Demand is stable due to aging population, but expected to increase further as baby boomers enter late elderly stage after 2025.

**Competition: medium (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career path: Licensed Practical Nurse → Registered Nurse → Specialist Nurse/Certified Nurse/Management. Graduate school is common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated vital sign measurement
- Charting and recording
- Simple interview responses

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Diagnostic support
- Medication management optimization
- Patient monitoring

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal communication
- Clinical judgment
- Physical care

**Skills to build:**

- AI tool utilization skills
- Data literacy
- Specialized nursing skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Home nursing
- Visiting nursing
- Specified medical practice training
- Elderly care

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy interacting with others
- People with strong sense of responsibility
- People confident in their physical strength

**Who is not suited**
- People who find night shifts or irregular hours difficult
- People who find it hard to face life and death

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a nurse?**
A: It depends on experience and workplace; starting salary is about 3-4 million yen, mid-career 4-5.5 million, veteran 5-7 million. Night shift and qualification allowances cause variation.

**Q: What visa do foreign nurses need to work in Japan?**
A: The 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is common. A Japanese nursing license is required, and there is a system under EPA. Note that it is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker.

**Q: How can nurses advance their careers?**
A: Options include obtaining certified nurse or specialist nurse qualifications, graduate school, promotion to management. Completing specified medical practice training expands roles.
