# Agricultural, Forestry and Fisheries Engineer — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 061 (JSCO)**

Engineers dedicated to improving productivity and sustainability in agriculture, forestry, and fisheries. They handle tasks such as variety improvement, cultivation/breeding techniques, and environmental management.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 55,000. Due to severe aging and shortage of successors, technology transfer and labor-saving are urgent. Conversion to smart agriculture is progressing, and demand for engineers remains stable.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI and robotics aid in automation and data analysis in farming, but human experience and insight are essential for cultivation decisions and environmental adaptation. AI complements engineers, boosting productivity.

After specialized education at agricultural or fisheries universities, careers are built at research institutions, agricultural cooperatives, and private companies. Mastery of specialized skills enables management positions or self-employment.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine farm work (harvesting, weeding)
- Simple aquaculture tasks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Growth data collection and analysis
- Early detection of pests and diseases
- Planning optimal fertilization schedules

**Human moat:**

- On-site observation and judgment
- Regionally adapted techniques
- Intuition based on long experience

**Skills to build:**

- Data science
- Drone operation
- Biotechnology
- Environmental monitoring

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart agriculture
- Gene editing
- Aquaculture technology
- Organic farming

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of agricultural and fishery engineers?**
A: Entry-level: approx. 3-4 million yen; mid-level: 4-6 million yen; senior: 6-8 million yen. Stable at research institutes and government positions, but varies at private agricultural corporations.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in this occupation?**
A: After university graduation, 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is common. Also, 'Specified Skilled Worker (i)' for agriculture is available, requiring passing skills and Japanese language tests.
