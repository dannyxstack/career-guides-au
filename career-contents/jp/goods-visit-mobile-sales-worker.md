# Goods Visit/Mobile Sales Worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 324 (JSCO)**

A job that involves visiting customers or patrolling with a mobile sales vehicle to sell food and daily necessities. Characterized by community-based sales.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 250,000. Demand for mobile sales is stable due to aging population and measures for shopping refugees in rural areas. However, door-to-door sales are declining due to the expansion of e-commerce.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

AI has low direct replacement risk but may enhance work through customer management and route optimization. Interpersonal skills are key.

With experience, promotion to sales office manager or area manager is possible. There is also a path to start an independent mobile sales business.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple goods handover
- Routine phone sales

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Sales route optimization
- Customer data analysis

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal communication
- Trust building
- On-site responsiveness

**Skills to build:**

- Customer service skills
- Local network building
- Product knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Mobile sales
- Door-to-door sales
- Shopping support
- Community-based

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy talking to others
- People who want to work closely with the local community
- People who enjoy driving

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer desk work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for product visit/mobile sales?**
A: Beginner: 3–3.8 million JPY, Intermediate: 3.8–5 million JPY, Advanced: 5–6.5 million JPY.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work in this occupation?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Food Service) or Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa applies, but requires work experience or academic background.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: With experience, paths to branch manager or independent business exist. Qualifications like sales expert are advantageous.
