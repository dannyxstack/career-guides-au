# Automotive Engineer (Development) — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 074 (JSCO)**

Engineer responsible for designing and developing engines, chassis, electrical systems, etc. Contributes to performance improvement and environmental compliance from research to mass production.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

After joining a manufacturer as a new graduate, accumulate OJT in assigned department and advance to project leader or specialist engineer. Later career options include management or consulting.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple CAD drawing creation
- Routine strength calculations
- Template document creation for designs

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Development of control algorithms for autonomous driving
- Automatic generation and evaluation of design proposals
- Predictive analysis of quality anomalies

**Human moat:**

- Creative design ideas
- Leadership to manage teams
- Interpretation of regulations and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

- Machine learning
- Control engineering
- Project management
- Software development

**Key growth areas:**

- Autonomous driving
- Electrification software
- Lightweight materials
- AI control

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of an automotive engineer?**
A: Starting annual salary is around 3.5-4.5 million yen; mid-career: 5-7 million yen; veteran managers: 8-12 million yen. Differences between major OEMs and suppliers.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an automotive engineer in Japan?**
A: Mainly requires the "Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services" residence status. A university degree in engineering or equivalent work experience is required. It may also qualify for the Highly Skilled Professional visa.

**Q: How can I advance my career in this field?**
A: In addition to deepening expertise (e.g., in EVs, autonomous driving, software), project management skills and English proficiency are important. Obtaining certifications such as Professional Engineer is also valued.
