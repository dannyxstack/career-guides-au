# Merchandise Procurement Sales Representative — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 326 (JSCO)**

This sales position involves visiting manufacturers and wholesalers to propose product purchases and take orders from retail stores, playing a crucial role in the retail industry.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Due to structural changes in retail and the expansion of e-commerce, demand is flat to slightly decreasing. While there is a continued shortage of experienced workers, new entrants remain limited.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Routine tasks like order processing and inventory management are easily automated by AI, but human negotiation and new client acquisition will remain human roles for now.

Starting from scratch, you can advance your career by increasing your assigned area and clients. Opportunities to become a buyer or sales manager are also available.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Order Processing
- Inventory Check
- Simple Sales Record Keeping

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer Data Analysis
- Demand Forecasting
- Sales Support Tool Utilization

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal Negotiation Skills
- Trust Building
- Market Knowledge

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- CRM Utilization
- Presentations

**Key growth areas:**

- EC shift in retail industry
- Sales DX
- Data Utilization
- Sales Efficiency

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a merchandise purchasing agent?**
A: Depends on experience: entry level ¥3-4M, mid level ¥4-5.5M, advanced ¥5.5-7M.

**Q: Can foreigners get a visa for this occupation?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa applies, but requires expertise and Japanese ability. Pure sales may be difficult.

**Q: Is it covered by the Specified Skilled Worker visa?**
A: Currently not included in the specified skilled fields. However, future expansion is possible.
