## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Start as an assistant with no experience, advance to lead designer or art director. Freelancing is also common.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Mass production of banner ads
- Creating simple logo proposals
- Template-based document creation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Visualizing ideas and rapid prototyping
- Automatic color variation generation
- Data-driven design proposals

**Human moat:**

- Client dialogue and intent understanding
- Overall brand strategy planning
- Original concept creation

**Skills to build:**

- AI tool operation skills
- UX research and data analysis
- Project management
- Presentation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Web design
- UI/UX design
- Graphic Design
- Product Design

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong curiosity and problem-solving skills for design.
- People who value self-expression while collaborating in a team.
- People who actively engage with new tools and technologies.

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer routine work and dislike change.
- People who cannot tolerate detailed revisions or client demands.

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary for someone with no experience?**
A: Starting salary for graduates of vocational schools with no experience is around 3 million yen per year. It increases with experience and results.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work as a designer in Japan?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa applies. Requires a vocational school degree or higher and design-related work experience.

**Q: How to become a freelancer?**
A: First, gain experience at a company, build a portfolio and network. Then, it is common to go independent and take on client work.
