# Delivery Person — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 705 (JSCO)**

A job that delivers orders from restaurants and retail stores to customers by motorcycle or bicycle; demand has surged in recent years.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Compulsory Education | 9 years | $0~$0 (No educational background required) |
| Driver's License | 1 month | $100,000~$300,000 (moped license: tens of thousands of yen; regular license: 100,000–300,000 yen) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular Automobile License | Public Safety Commission | Optional | Required for four-wheeled delivery |
| Moped License | Public Safety Commission | Optional | Required for moped delivery |
| Large Motorcycle License | Public Safety Commission | Optional | Advantageous when delivering on large motorcycles |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 280,000. Demand is increasing due to the expansion of EC and food delivery markets. Barriers to entry are low, but there is a medium-to-long-term risk of decline with the introduction of AI and drone delivery.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Can start with no experience. With experience, can become a delivery leader or manager. Also possible to start an independent business or change careers to logistics/transportation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Route delivery
- Simple pickup and delivery tasks
- In-warehouse movement

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Delivery route optimization (AI route suggestions)
- Customer interaction (AI translation, chatbots)
- Real-time delivery status management

**Human moat:**

- Flexible response
- Judgment in complex traffic situations
- Interpersonal service

**Skills to build:**

- Operations management
- Logistics system knowledge
- Customer service

**Key growth areas:**

- Food delivery
- Last mile
- Same-day delivery
- E-commerce logistics

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People confident in physical strength
- People who enjoy cycling or riding motorcycles
- People who are not time-lax

**Who is not suited**
- People who want to work quietly
- People who dislike jobs affected by weather

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a delivery driver?**
A: For full-time work, annual income is around 2.5–3.5 million yen. With commission, monthly income can exceed 400,000 yen during busy periods, but is unstable.

**Q: What visa is needed to work in Japan as a delivery driver?**
A: Currently, simple delivery tasks are not covered by residence status; work requires advanced fields like Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services.

**Q: Can delivery workers advance their careers?**
A: It is possible to become a delivery manager, logistics company operations manager, or start your own business. Obtaining qualifications (e.g., operations manager) is advantageous.
