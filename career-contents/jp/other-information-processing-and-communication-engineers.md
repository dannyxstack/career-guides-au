# Other Information Processing and Communication Engineers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 109 (JSCO)**

Specialists handling a wide range of tasks in information processing and communication, such as system development, network construction, and data analysis.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for this occupation?**
A: Depends on experience and skills, median around 5-6 million JPY. Can exceed 10 million at large/foreign companies.

**Q: Which visa do foreigners need to work in this occupation?**
A: Generally, an 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is required. Specified Skilled Worker (i) (IT) also became eligible from 2024.

**Q: What are the key points for career advancement?**
A: Acquiring certifications (e.g., Applied Information Technology Engineer) and skills in specialized fields (AI, security) is effective.
