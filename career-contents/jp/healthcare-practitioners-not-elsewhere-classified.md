# Healthcare practitioners (not elsewhere classified) — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 159 (JSCO)**

Health and medical professionals other than doctors and nurses, including dietitians, clinical laboratory technicians, physical therapists, speech-language therapists, and various other occupations.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After obtaining qualifications at vocational schools or universities, gain experience in hospitals/facilities, and can advance to managerial positions, specialist instructors, or private practice.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine data entry
- Primary screening of images
- Automated judgment of simple test results

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Diagnostic support
- Optimization of rehabilitation plans
- Efficient patient monitoring
- Speech analysis assistance

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal communication
- Complex clinical judgment
- Ethical judgment
- Coordination in team-based healthcare

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Operation of AI tools
- Communication skills
- Multidisciplinary collaboration

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Home healthcare
- Rehabilitation needs
- Preventive medicine

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for this occupation?**
A: Varies by occupation, but for clinical lab technicians about 4.5 million yen, for physical therapists about 4-5 million yen as a guide.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work in Japan?**
A: A representative visa is the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services. For occupations requiring national licenses, obtaining them is a prerequisite.

**Q: Are there opportunities for promotion?**
A: With experience, you can advance to managerial roles, become a leader in a specialized field, or even start your own business. Medical technologists can advance by obtaining certification.
