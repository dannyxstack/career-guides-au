# Receptionist/Information Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 254 (JSCO)**

An administrative role at the entrance of companies or public facilities, welcoming visitors, handling phone calls, and providing guidance. As the front line of customer service, politeness and multitasking are required.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for reception and guidance clerks?**
A: The average annual salary is around 3 to 4 million yen, varying by experience and workplace. Bonuses depend on performance.

**Q: Is there a visa for foreigners to work in reception?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is common. A university degree or work experience is required. For hotel work, Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Accommodation) is also an option.

**Q: Are there any required qualifications?**
A: No mandatory qualifications, but having a Business Manner Certification or Secretary Certification is advantageous.
