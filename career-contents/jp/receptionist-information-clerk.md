# Receptionist/Information Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 254 (JSCO)**

An administrative role at the entrance of companies or public facilities, welcoming visitors, handling phone calls, and providing guidance. As the front line of customer service, politeness and multitasking are required.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

The introduction of AI chatbots and automated reception systems is progressing, and simple response tasks are being replaced. Meanwhile, complex complaint handling and high-level hospitality are still required from humans, leading to collaboration between AI and humans.

After joining, acquire customer service skills and business etiquette. With experience, can advance to chief or front manager.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- First-level phone response
- Reservation reception
- Simple guidance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Multilingual interpretation support
- Visitor data management
- Reservation system management

**Human moat:**

- Advanced communication skills
- Complaint handling
- Attentiveness through facial expressions and voice tone

**Skills to build:**

- Customer service etiquette
- Complaint handling
- OA skills (Excel, Word)
- English conversation

**Key growth areas:**

- Customer service
- Multitasking
- OA skills
- Hospitality

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for reception and guidance clerks?**
A: The average annual salary is around 3 to 4 million yen, varying by experience and workplace. Bonuses depend on performance.

**Q: Is there a visa for foreigners to work in reception?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is common. A university degree or work experience is required. For hotel work, Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Accommodation) is also an option.

**Q: Are there any required qualifications?**
A: No mandatory qualifications, but having a Business Manner Certification or Secretary Certification is advantageous.
