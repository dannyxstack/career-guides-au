Engaged in various service tasks such as inn/hotel cleaning, waste disposal, and building maintenance.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

While robots and AI are replacing some cleaning and waste treatment tasks, areas requiring complex judgment and meticulous work remain for humans. Overall, automation is progressing gradually.

Can start with no experience. Career advancement possible through qualifications, promotion, or specialized skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple floor cleaning robot tasks
- Routine waste sorting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-based cleaning route optimization
- Sensor-based cleaning quality control

**Human moat:**

- Customer trust
- Judgment on complex stains
- Emergency response

**Skills to build:**

- AI management tools
- Environmental hygiene knowledge
- Management

**Key growth areas:**

- Labor shortage
- Cleaning
- Waste treatment
- Automation

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Those confident in physical strength
- Those who don't mind simple tasks
- Those interested in cleaning and maintenance

**Who is not suited**
- Those who value interpersonal communication
- Those who want to avoid jobs with low raises

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income?**
A: Starting salary is about 2.5-3.5 million JPY, with management roles around 5 million JPY.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work?**
A: Cleaning may fall under Specified Skilled Worker (i), but as of 2024, the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa covers limited roles.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: Options include obtaining building cleaning certification, promotion to management, or switching to a specialized cleaning company.
