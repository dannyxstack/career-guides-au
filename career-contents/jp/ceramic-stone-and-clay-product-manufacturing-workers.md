# Ceramic, stone and clay product manufacturing workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 532 (JSCO)**

This occupation involves manufacturing ceramic, stone and clay products such as pottery, glass, cement, and bricks. Tasks include molding, firing, and finishing.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Employment is flat due to construction demand fluctuations and competition from foreign products. Some demand exists for energy-saving and eco-friendly products, but overall trend is declining.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of ceramics and stone product manufacturing workers?**
A: The average annual income is around 4 million yen, varying by experience and region.

**Q: How can foreigners work in this occupation?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing 3 fields) is available. Requires JLPT N4 or higher and passing the skills test (manufacturing field).

**Q: How to advance your career?**
A: By obtaining qualifications through skills certification or acquiring eco-friendly product manufacturing techniques, you can open a path to management or specialized technical roles.
