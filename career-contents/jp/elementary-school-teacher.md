# Elementary school teacher — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 192 (JSCO)**

A professional who instructs children as a homeroom teacher in public elementary schools, conducting subject lessons, life guidance, and parent communication, and implementing education based on the curriculum guidelines.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| University (teacher training course) | 4 years | $4,200,000~$5,000,000 (National university annual tuition about ¥530,000. Private schools ¥1,000,000–1,500,000/year.) |
| Graduate school of education | 2 years | $2,000,000~$3,000,000 (National graduate school tuition (approx. ¥800,000/year). Private schools ¥1,000,000–1,500,000/year.) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elementary school teaching license (Type 1) | Prefectural Board of Education | Required | Obtained by completing required credits at university. Mandatory. |
| Elementary school teaching license (Specialized) | Prefectural Board of Education | Optional | Obtainable upon completing graduate school. Salary advantage. |
| Special needs school teaching license | Prefectural Board of Education | Optional | Advantageous when assigned to special needs classes. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career advancement from homeroom teacher to grade head, academic affairs head, vice principal, and principal. Graduate study for specialized licenses or management training is effective for career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Test grading tasks
- Attendance management
- Document creation for parents
- Lesson preparation (material search/creation assistance)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-powered individualized learning drills
- Support for foreign-national children using translation tools
- Data analysis for learning achievement tracking

**Human moat:**

- Instruction that empathizes with each child's heart
- Building trust with parents
- Creative lesson design

**Skills to build:**

- Ability to utilize ICT in instruction
- Knowledge of special needs education
- Cross-cultural understanding and foreign language skills
- Data-driven lesson improvement

**Key growth areas:**

- Increase in foreign-national children
- Work style reform for teachers
- Enhanced special needs education
- Promotion of ICT education

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary of an elementary school teacher?**
A: Average annual salary is about 6.8 million yen (public elementary school teachers, 2022 salary data). Starting salary is around 200,000 yen, increasing with years of service; management positions can exceed 10 million yen.

**Q: What visa is required for a foreigner to become an elementary school teacher in Japan?**
A: Primarily requires 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa. Conditions include teaching license and Japanese proficiency (N1 level). Highly Skilled Professional visa also possible via points system.

**Q: What is the career path for elementary school teachers?**
A: Homeroom teacher → Subject head/Grade head → Academic affairs head → Vice principal → Principal. Advantageous to earn a specialized license via graduate school or undergo management training.
