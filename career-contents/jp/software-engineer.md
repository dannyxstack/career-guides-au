# Software Engineer — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 104 (JSCO)**

A professional involved in designing, developing, testing, and maintaining computer software. Active as system engineers or programmers in fields like web, mobile, and embedded systems.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| University Faculty of Information Science | 4 years | $4,000,000~$8,000,000 (Approx. ¥2.4 million/year at national universities, ¥1.5 million/year at private universities. Total tuition.) |
| Vocational School IT Department | 2 years | $2,000,000~$4,000,000 (Daytime courses around ¥1 million/year.) |
| Programming Bootcamp | 6 months | $500,000~$1,500,000 (Online courses are popular. Many include job placement support.) |

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |

| Oracle Certified Java Programmer | Oracle | Optional | A vendor certification beneficial for Java developers. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 1,000,000. With a continued shortage of IT personnel and rising demand for DX and AI, the need for software developers is expected to remain strong. Shortages are especially notable in cloud and AI areas.

**Competition: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career path progresses from junior to senior, tech lead, architect, and manager. A specialist track also exists, with higher earnings possible by deepening expertise in fields like AI or security.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple web page coding
- Creating routine test code
- Generating basic data processing scripts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing architecture
- Identifying and fixing complex bugs
- Requirements definition and client communication

**Human moat:**

- Complex system-wide design ability
- Planning skills to solve business problems with tech
- Understanding and migrating legacy systems

**Skills to build:**

- AI/machine learning basics
- Cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Security
- Domain Knowledge
- Communication Skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Artificial intelligence
- Cloud computing
- Cybersecurity
- Mobile app development

---

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Can think logically
- Can maintain interest in new technologies
- Can develop collaboratively in a team

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes change
- Not good at detailed work

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a software engineer?**
A: Entry-level: ~4 million JPY, mid-level: 6-8 million JPY, senior: over 10 million JPY. Varies greatly by skill, experience, and employer.

**Q: How can foreigners work as software engineers in Japan?**
A: The 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' status is common. Requires a university degree or 10+ years of work experience. The 'Highly Skilled Professional' visa is also an option.

**Q: Which fields will grow in the future?**
A: AI, cloud, security, data science. These skills are in high demand.
