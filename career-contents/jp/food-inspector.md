# Food inspector — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 573 (JSCO)**

In food factories and production sites, inspect product quality, foreign objects, and packaging by sight and equipment, ensuring safe food shipment.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 45,000. Food demand is stable; stricter quality control maintains demand for inspection work. However, AI image inspection is reducing simple inspections.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

With experience, can advance to quality control leader or QC specialist. Knowledge of HACCP or ISO22000 boosts career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Visual foreign matter inspection
- Periodic appearance checks
- Part of packaging inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Verification of AI inspection results
- Analysis and root cause investigation of defective items
- Inspection data management and improvement

**Human moat:**

- Experience-based rules for abnormal judgment
- Subtle judgments using the five senses
- Flexible on-site responsiveness

**Skills to build:**

- Basics of AI/machine learning
- Statistical quality control
- Knowledge of HACCP and food safety
- Communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Quality Control
- Food Safety
- Automated Inspection
- HACCP

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Meticulous and good at detailed work
- Committed to quality
- Able to work following rules

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes monotonous work
- Enjoys talking with people and seeks change

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of food inspection workers?**
A: Approximately 2.8–3.6 million yen for entry level, 3.6–4.5 million yen for intermediate level, and 4.5–5.5 million yen for advanced level.

**Q: What visa is required for foreigners to work in this occupation?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Food and Beverage Manufacturing sector) or Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa are common.
