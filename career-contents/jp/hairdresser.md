# Hairdresser — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 382 (JSCO)**

A professional dedicated to enhancing customers' appearance through cutting, perming, coloring, etc. Requires skill acquisition and national certification.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Beauty vocational school | 2 years | $2,000,000~$4,000,000 (Varies by day and evening courses) |
| Beauty correspondence education | 3 years | $800,000~$1,500,000 (Requires in-person practical sessions) |
| National exam eligibility | 2-3 years | $500,000~$1,000,000 (Separate from tuition fees) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Hairstylist License | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Required | Required to work in a salon |
| Management Hairstylist License | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Required to manage a salon (after practical experience) |
| Beauty Skills Certification | Japan Beauty Skills Certification Association | Optional | Advantageous for employment and promotion |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 532,000. The beauty industry has stable demand, but competition is fierce and the rate of opening new salons is high. Labor shortages persist, but there are many job openings for beginners.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Start as an apprentice, then move from assistant to stylist. After working at a salon, you can open your own business or specialize in techniques like makeup or kimono dressing.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Appointment and customer information management
- Simple shampooing
- Mixing colorants (partial)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Hairstyle suggestions (AI simulation)
- Scalp diagnosis and AI counseling
- Inventory management and ordering

**Human moat:**

- Cutting techniques
- Interpersonal communication
- Creativity
- Adaptability during treatments

**Skills to build:**

- Counseling skills
- Trend analysis
- Social Media Marketing
- Advanced Skills (Kimono Dressing, Makeup)

**Key growth areas:**

- Hair and scalp care
- Men's grooming
- Gray hair dye and anti-aging care
- Bridal and kimono dressing

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

**Who is suited**
- Person who enjoys interacting with people

**Who is not suited**
- Person lacking physical stamina
- Person who dislikes standing work or weekend shifts

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for a hairdresser?**
A: Starting salary is around ¥160,000-200,000 per month, with annual income of ¥2-3 million. In commission-based salons, it varies by performance.

**Q: Can foreigners work as hairdressers in Japan?**
A: A national hairdressing license is required, and the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa rarely covers hairdressing, making it challenging. It is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker.

**Q: How can hairdressers advance their careers?**
A: From stylist to top stylist to salon manager. Alternatively, specialize in hair makeup, wigs, or beauty instruction, or open your own salon.
