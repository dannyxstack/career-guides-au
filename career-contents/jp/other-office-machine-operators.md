# Other Office Machine Operators — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 319 (JSCO)**

Specialist who operates and manages office equipment such as copiers, printers, and mail processing machines to support operational efficiency.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Document management knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for this occupation?**
A: Average annual income is around 3.5-4.5 million JPY with 3-7 years of experience. Senior roles can exceed 5 million JPY, but slightly lower than general clerical work.

**Q: Can foreigners obtain a work visa for this occupation?**
A: It may qualify for the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa, but requires a vocational school degree or higher and knowledge/experience in office equipment operation. It is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker, so advanced expertise is needed.

**Q: How can one advance their career?**
A: By expanding skills into IT support and office management, you can transition to more specialized roles. Obtaining certifications such as MOS or IT Passport is effective.
