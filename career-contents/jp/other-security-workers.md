# Other Security Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 459 (JSCO)**

This occupation includes a variety of security duties such as museum/gallery monitors and parking lot attendants, excluding guards, police officers, and Self-Defense Forces personnel.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Compulsory Education | 9 years | $0~$0 (No educational background required. No special education needed.) |
| Security Guard Training | 1-2 weeks | $0~$50,000 (Basic training is a statutory 20-hour course upon hiring. Paid courses are also available.) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security Guard Certification (Class 1 & 2) | Prefectural Public Safety Commission | Optional | Not mandatory for security work but advantageous for promotion and salary increase. |
| Traffic Control Security (Class 2) | Prefectural Public Safety Commission | Optional | Specialized certification for traffic control. High demand at construction sites. |
| Disaster Prevention Manager | Fire and Disaster Management Agency | Optional | Required for large facilities. Acquiring it opens paths to management positions. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 450,000. Demand is stable due to aging and labor shortages, but the security industry is advancing AI and sensor technology for labor-saving, leading to a gradual decline in hiring.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

You can start with no experience and aim for team leader or management roles through on-site experience. Career paths include specializing in facility security or traffic control.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Constant monitoring duties
- Parking gate management
- Patrol checkpoint confirmation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- On-site confirmation after anomaly detection
- Emergency response
- Security consulting

**Human moat:**

- Flexible decision-making
- Interpersonal communication
- Coordination and guidance during emergencies

**Skills to build:**

- Abnormal situation response training
- Basic data analysis
- Security risk management

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging facility security
- Traffic guidance
- Surveillance camera systems
- Security consulting

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are physically confident and willing to work night shifts
- People who follow rules and can handle tasks patiently
- People with a sense of mission to protect others' safety

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike physical labor or cannot tolerate monotonous tasks
- People who are extremely poor at communication

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the median annual income?**
A: Median annual income is around 4 million yen after about 5 years of experience. Varies by qualifications and employer.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation in Japan?**
A: Japanese language skills are essential for security work, and currently it is practically difficult to work as a security guard under existing residence statuses (e.g., Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services). It is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities?**
A: It is common to obtain the Security Guard Class 1 certification and aim to become a field leader or manager. Additionally, acquiring a Disaster Prevention Manager qualification can lead to promotion to security supervisor at large facilities.
