# Bus Driver — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 611 (JSCO)**

A profession that drives route buses and tour buses to safely transport passengers to their destinations. Plays a vital role in supporting regional transportation.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Large Vehicle License Acquisition | 2 months | $300,000~$500,000 (Training school fees, with package options) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large Vehicle License | Public Safety Commission | Required | Mandatory. Class 1 license required for bus driving. |
| Large Vehicle Class 2 License | Public Safety Commission | Required | Mandatory for passenger transport. Many employers support acquisition after hiring. |
| Operations Manager Qualification | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism | Optional | For management, optional but beneficial for promotion. |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 210,000. A serious shortage of drivers due to an aging population, especially in rural areas where job openings are increasing. While it's covered by Specified Skilled Worker status, hiring is limited due to the need for a large vehicle license.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

After obtaining a large vehicle license, start on route buses, gain experience, then move to tour or express buses. Career advancement to management or operations manager is possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Operational driving of scheduled routes
- Simple route bus driving

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Efficiency of operations management
- Safe driving support systems

**Human moat:**

- Decision-making in emergencies
- Customer service skills
- Local knowledge

**Skills to build:**

- Operations management
- Safety management
- Understanding of autonomous driving systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Driver shortage
- Autonomous driving technology
- Aging countermeasures
- Wage increase

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People eager to obtain large vehicle license
- People healthy enough to endure long hours of driving
- People who don't mind customer service

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike irregular work hours
- People not confident in safe driving

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a bus driver?**
A: Around ¥3.5 million in first year, up to ¥6 million with experience. Varies by bonuses and allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners become bus drivers?**
A: Not eligible for Specified Skilled Worker, Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services also difficult. Possibilities limited if fluent in Japanese and have large vehicle license.

**Q: How to advance a career as a bus driver?**
A: Obtain a license as a transportation manager, aiming for a branch office manager or administrative position. Or advance to large tour buses.
