# Inspectors of General-Purpose, Production, and Industrial Machinery Equipment — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 581 (JSCO)**

A specialist who performs quality inspections and tests on machinery such as machine tools and industrial robots to ensure product reliability.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

The introduction of automatic inspection using AI and image recognition is advancing, but human experience is necessary for judging complex abnormal sounds, minute scratches, and inspections involving machine adjustment. AI and human collaboration will continue.

Career advancement is possible by acquiring skills certification or QC certification. Paths include promotion to inspection team leader or quality control management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Dimensional Measurement
- Visual Inspection
- Product Sorting
- Simple Function Testing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Abnormal Sound Diagnosis
- Complex Defect Analysis
- Inspection Device Tuning
- Verification of AI Judgment Results

**Human moat:**

- Auditory Judgment of Abnormal Sounds
- Experience-Based Cause Identification
- Fine Sensation of Machine Adjustment
- Customer Negotiation and Reporting

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- Programming (Python/R)
- Quality Control Methods
- Machine Learning Basics

**Key growth areas:**

- Smart Factory
- Automated Dimension Measurement
- Non-Destructive Testing
- Quality Control DX

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the first-year salary for someone starting without experience?**
A: First-year annual income is typically around 3-3.5 million yen, and can reach 3.5-4 million yen including overtime.

**Q: What visa is required for a foreigner to work in this occupation in Japan?**
A: If you have a bachelor's degree or higher, you can get the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa; if you pass a skills test, you can get 'Specified Skilled Worker (i)'. Specified Skilled Worker (ii) is not yet available in manufacturing.

**Q: Are there any required qualifications?**
A: There are no mandatory qualifications, but having a Mechanical Inspection Technician certification or QC exam can be advantageous for promotion or job change.
