# Wood and Paper Product Manufacturing Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 536 (JSCO)**

Skilled workers who manufacture furniture, building materials, and paper products from wood and paper. They handle a wide range of processes, from machine operation to manual finishing.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 380,000. The market is shrinking due to sluggish housing demand and digitalization, but there is a chronic labor shortage in high-value-added products requiring craftsmanship. This is a field covered by the Specified Skilled Worker status.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Although automation via AI and robots is advancing, human judgment is essential for high-end furniture making and finishing processes requiring craftsmanship. Simple cutting and polishing are mechanized.

Entry-level workers start as machine operators; with skill development, they can advance to pattern making, quality control, or production management. Independent work as a craftsman is also an option.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic plate cutting
- Polishing process
- Simple assembly

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design and styling
- Sensor inspection for wood sorting

**Human moat:**

- Skilled hand-finishing techniques
- Wood grain reading and processing adjustment

**Skills to build:**

- AI design tool operation
- NC machine tool programming
- Quality control

**Key growth areas:**

- Wooden building demand
- Paper packaging
- Furniture craftsman
- Eco-material processing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of wood/paper product manufacturing workers?**
A: Average annual income is about 3.8 million yen. Ranges from 2.5 million yen for entry-level to 6 million yen for senior-level.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in this occupation?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing 3 fields) is the most realistic. Experienced workers can also qualify for the 'Engineer' visa, but at least 10 years of experience is required.
