# Pawnshop Owner/Staff — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 334 (JSCO)**

Pawnshops lend money against items deposited as collateral, and sell items if loans are not repaid. Requires appraisal skills and customer service.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| High School | 3 years | $100,000~$300,000 (Public high school tuition) |
| Vocational School (e.g., Gem Appraisal) | 2 years | $1,000,000~$2,000,000 (Private vocational school tuition) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondhand Dealer Permit | Prefectural Public Safety Commission | Required | Required for business |
| Gem Appraiser | Japan Gem Appraisal Association | Optional | Optional but advantageous |
| Brand Item Appraiser | Japan Brand Item Appraisal Association | Optional | Optional but advantageous |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★☆☆).** National workforce approx. 15,000. Demand is stable due to growth in the reuse market, but the number of stores is slightly declining. Demand is high for staff with appraiser qualifications.

**Competition: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**Work intensity: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

First, gain experience as a staff member and learn appraisal techniques. Then there is a career path to store manager or independent owner. Qualifications in gem or brand item appraisal are advantageous.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple appraisal tasks
- Automation of inventory management
- Data entry tasks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Improvement of appraisal accuracy
- Rapid acquisition of market information
- Efficiency in customer management

**Human moat:**

- In-person customer service skills
- Appraisal know-how
- Experience in authenticating luxury brands and jewelry

**Skills to build:**

- Appraisal techniques
- Customer service skills
- Market knowledge
- Utilization of AI tools

**Key growth areas:**

- Expansion of the reuse market
- Demand for appraisal skills
- Integration with EC sales
- Demand for inheritance and estate sorting

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People interested in item appraisal
- People who enjoy communication with others

**Who is not suited**
- People who prefer routine work
- People not confident in physical strength

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual salary at a pawnshop?**
A: Entry-level: 2.5–3.5M yen, mid-level: 3.5–5M yen, senior: 5–8M yen. Shop owners can earn more.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work at a pawnshop?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is common. Appraisal and management skills are required, and Specified Skilled Worker does not apply.

**Q: How can I advance my career at a pawnshop?**
A: Generally, one obtains certifications such as jewelry or brand item appraiser, builds a track record, and aims to become a store manager or start their own business.
