# Metal product manufacturing and processing worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 529 (JSCO)**

A profession that manufactures and processes various metal products by cutting, welding, bending, and finishing metal materials. Experience in factories or work sites is valued.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skill Certification (Metal Processing) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Grade 1 and 2; advantageous for promotion and salary increase |
| Welding Skill Qualification | Japan Welding Engineering Society | Optional | Qualifications for various welding methods; may be required for employment |
| Forklift Operation Skills Training | Prefectural Labor Bureau | Optional | Necessary for cargo handling work in factories |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 650,000. While automation is progressing throughout the manufacturing industry, demand for skilled metalworking remains high, especially in small and medium-sized enterprises facing labor shortages. This field also sees many technical intern and specified skilled worker acceptances.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Starting as an apprentice, one can advance to a mid-level worker after acquiring skills in a few years. Career paths include becoming a site leader through skill certification or multi-skilling, and further to foreman or production management positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple deburring
- Routine cutting/drilling
- Inspection of mass-produced items

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Creating machining programs with CAD/CAM
- Teaching welding robots
- Quality data analysis and process improvement

**Human moat:**

- Advanced hand finishing and welding skills
- Judgment during abnormalities
- Custom one-off machining per customer requirements

**Skills to build:**

- Operating digital machine tools
- Quality control methods
- Maintenance techniques
- Process Design

**Key growth areas:**

- Metalworking
- Automotive parts
- Precision machinery
- Welding technology

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for metalworking positions?**
A: Entry-level about ¥3.2-4M, mid-level ¥4-5.2M, expert ¥5-6.5M, varies with overtime and certifications.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work in this occupation?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (manufacturing) is common. Requires N4+ Japanese and skills exam pass. Technical Intern Training (ii) also possible.

**Q: How can one advance their career?**
A: Obtain skill certifications and become multi-skilled to become a team leader. Further paths include production management or quality control.
