# Food Sales Occupation Practitioner — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 341 (JSCO)**

In food manufacturers or wholesale companies, sells food products to retailers and the food service industry. Also responsible for sales planning and inventory adjustment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After joining, learn customer handling by accompanying seniors, then take charge of assigned areas or clients after a few years. Advancement to team leader or sales office manager is possible based on performance. Transfer to food manufacturers is also possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Inventory checks and order processing
- Sales report creation
- Simple customer list management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Demand-based sales strategy planning
- AI-based prioritization of negotiation partners

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face trust building
- Negotiation skills
- Food knowledge and proposal abilities

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- CRM operation
- English proficiency
- Presentations

**Key growth areas:**

- Health foods
- Home delivery for seniors
- Food exports
- E-commerce market

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy talking with others

**Who is not suited**
- People not good with numbers

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the median annual income?**
A: The national median is around ¥4.3 million (company average). At major food manufacturers, it can exceed ¥5 million.

**Q: Is it difficult for foreigners to obtain a visa for this occupation?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa applies. Requires a university degree or equivalent experience, and Japanese proficiency. Difficulty is moderate.

**Q: Are there any qualifications advantageous for career advancement?**
A: Food Sales Technician or Food Sanitation Manager certifications are valued. High TOEIC scores open doors to export roles.
