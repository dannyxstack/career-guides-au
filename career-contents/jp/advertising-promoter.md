# Advertising Promoter — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 424 (JSCO)**

A broad role responsible for promoting a company's products or services, from planning ad campaigns and media negotiations to organizing sales promotion events.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of an advertising promoter?**
A: Entry level: 3.2-4.2 million yen, mid-level: 4.5-6 million yen, senior: 6.5-9 million yen. May be higher at major agencies or based on performance.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an advertising promoter in Japan?**
A: The 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' status is common. Requires university degree and advertising-related work experience, with duties falling under humanities.

**Q: How to become an advertising promoter with no experience?**
A: The common path is to study advertising/marketing at vocational school or university, then join an agency through internships or new graduate hiring.
