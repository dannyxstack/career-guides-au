# Advertising Promoter — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 424 (JSCO)**

A broad role responsible for promoting a company's products or services, from planning ad campaigns and media negotiations to organizing sales promotion events.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 230,000. With the expansion of digital advertising, demand is growing for talent skilled in data analysis and social media. Meanwhile, traditional ads like print and TV commercials are declining.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

AI automates ad delivery optimization and performance measurement, but strategy planning and creative planning remain human roles.

With experience, one can advance to project leader or manager at ad agencies or corporate marketing departments. Specializing may lead to independent roles as creative director or planner.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated Ad Bidding & Delivery Optimization
- Automated Generation of Performance Measurement Reports
- Routine Copywriting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Predictive Analysis of Ad Effectiveness
- Improved Targeting Accuracy
- Creation of Personalized Ads

**Human moat:**

- Creative Planning Ability
- Building Client Trust
- Intuitive Insight into Market Trends

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- Digital Marketing
- Social Media Management
- Storytelling

**Key growth areas:**

- Digital Marketing
- Social Media Promotion
- Data Analysis
- Influencer Marketing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of an advertising promoter?**
A: Entry level: 3.2-4.2 million yen, mid-level: 4.5-6 million yen, senior: 6.5-9 million yen. May be higher at major agencies or based on performance.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an advertising promoter in Japan?**
A: The 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' status is common. Requires university degree and advertising-related work experience, with duties falling under humanities.

**Q: How to become an advertising promoter with no experience?**
A: The common path is to study advertising/marketing at vocational school or university, then join an agency through internships or new graduate hiring.
