# Textile, Apparel, and Fiber Product Manufacturing Workers — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 535 (JSCO)**

Occupations involved in the entire process of manufacturing textile products such as spinning, weaving, knitting, apparel, and bedding from fiber raw materials.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 250,000. The domestic textile industry is on a declining trend, but demand for high-value-added products and functional materials remains steady. Labor shortages persist, making it a target field for the Specified Skilled Worker program.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career advancement is possible from skill acquisition at factories to process management and quality control. Accumulating experience can open paths to becoming a team leader or production manager.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple sewing processes
- Visual fabric inspection
- Routine dyeing work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Quality control (assisting AI visual inspection)
- Design support (AI pattern generation)
- Production plan optimization

**Human moat:**

- Advanced hand-sewing techniques
- Sensitivity to fabric texture
- Custom-made responses to client needs

**Skills to build:**

- AI/IoT device operation skills
- High-value-added product manufacturing skills
- Quality control and analysis skills
- Ability to handle high-mix, low-volume production

**Key growth areas:**

- Functional fibers
- Smart textiles
- Recycled fibers
- Automated sewing technology

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with dexterous hands
- People with concentration skills
- People who like detailed work

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike monotonous work
- People who dislike a lot of standing work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for this occupation?**
A: Entry-level: ¥2.5-3.5M, mid-level: ¥3.5-5M, advanced: ¥4.5-6.5M. Varies by company size & region.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work in this occupation?**
A: After completing Technical Intern Training (ii), you can obtain Specified Skilled Worker (i). Skilled workers may bring family under Specified Skilled Worker (ii). University graduates are eligible for the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa.

**Q: What is the future employment outlook?**
A: The domestic textile industry is shrinking, but high-performance materials and recycled fibers are expected to grow. Due to ongoing labor shortages, demand will remain steady.
