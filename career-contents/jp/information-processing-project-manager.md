# Information Processing Project Manager — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 103 (JSCO)**

Specialist responsible for IT project planning, progress management, quality/delivery control, and team leadership. Also handles client coordination and risk management, impacting system development success.

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

AI assists PMs with task management and progress prediction, but human judgment remains essential for stakeholder coordination and team management. Consequently, AI enhances PM work and improves productivity.

Promotion from programmer via project leader. PMI or IPA certifications are effective for career advancement. Agile PM demand rising in recent years.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Schedule Updates
- Automated Progress Report Generation
- Resource Allocation Optimization Proposals

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Risk Prediction
- Data-Driven Decision Making
- Communication Optimization

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal Coordination Skills
- Decision-Making Under Uncertainty
- Negotiation Skills
- Team Building

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- AI Tool Utilization
- Agile/Scrum
- Cloud Knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- System Integration
- Cloud Migration Support
- AI Project Management
- DX Promotion

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the expected annual salary for IT project managers?**
A: Entry-level: 4-6M yen, mid-level: 6-9M yen, senior: 9-14M yen. Varies by experience, certs, project scale.

**Q: How can foreigners work in Japan in this role?**
A: Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is common. Requires degree or 10+ years exp. Highly Skilled Professional visa also possible.

**Q: Are certifications mandatory?**
A: Although not required, obtaining PMP or project management certification is advantageous for job changes and promotions.
