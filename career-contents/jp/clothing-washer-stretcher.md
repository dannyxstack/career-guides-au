# Clothing Washer/Stretcher — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 386 (JSCO)**

A specialist who washes, starches, and stretches kimonos and Japanese clothing to shape them. Requires traditional techniques and craftsmanship.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for a kimono refitter?**
A: For beginners, about 2.5 to 3.5 million yen; intermediate: 3.5 to 5 million; advanced: 4.5 to 7 million. Self-employment can yield higher income, but the market is shrinking.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as a kimono refitter in Japan?**
A: The standard 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is difficult. The 'Technical Skills' visa for traditional crafts may apply, but few have it, and advanced skills are required. It's not covered by Specified Skilled Worker.

**Q: What are the routes to becoming a kimono refitter?**
A: Study at vocational school or training center, or apprentice under a master. Gain practical experience and obtain a cleaning technician certification.
