# Textile, Apparel & Fabric Product Inspection Worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 575 (JSCO)**

Responsible for quality inspection of textile products (clothing, bedding, curtains, etc.), professionally checking appearance, dimensions, strength, etc.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career typically progresses from on-site experience to inspection leader or quality control manager. Workers who can handle diverse products are valued as factories consolidate.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automation of visual inspection (scratches, dirt)
- Automation of dimensional measurement

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI assistance for color tone judgment
- AI utilization for quality trend analysis

**Human moat:**

- Sensory inspection of touch and texture
- Identification of defect causes and improvement proposals

**Skills to build:**

- Sensory inspection ability
- Data analysis skills
- Quality control knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Quality Control
- Textile Product Inspection
- Apparel Inspection
- High Value-Added Textiles

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the median annual income for this occupation?**
A: Industry average is about 3.5-4 million yen, varying by experience and employer.

**Q: Can foreigners work in this occupation in Japan?**
A: Possible with Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa or Specified Skilled Worker (i) in textiles, requiring Japanese ability and skills.

**Q: What is needed for career advancement?**
A: Obtaining related certifications (e.g., Textile Product Quality Manager) and gaining experience inspecting diverse products is effective.
