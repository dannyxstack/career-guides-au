# Operations Management Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 302 (JSCO)**

Administrative role creating bus/truck operation plans, managing crew, monitoring operations. Vital role supporting safety and efficiency in transport industry.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After obtaining Operations Manager qualification (national license), specialize in operations management. Career path to office manager or logistics management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual entry of operation records
- Scheduling regular inspections
- Tallying check-in data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimizing dispatching plans
- Real-time monitoring of operations
- Automatic driver shift creation

**Human moat:**

- On-site coordination and complaint handling
- Decision-making based on transportation management qualification
- Building trust with drivers

**Skills to build:**

- AI dispatching system operation
- Data analysis
- Communication skills
- Transportation management qualification

**Key growth areas:**

- Logistics DX
- AI dispatching
- Driver shortage
- 2024 Problem

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of an operations management clerk?**
A: Varies by experience and qualifications, but average is around 4 to 5 million yen. Managerial positions can earn over 6 million yen.

**Q: Can foreigners work as operations management clerks?**
A: Yes, it is possible. The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa requires a university degree or relevant work experience. Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Transport) is also eligible from 2024.

**Q: How can I advance my career?**
A: Obtain a transportation management qualification and become a specialist in operations management. Then, you can advance to positions such as depot manager or logistics management role.
