# House cleaning worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 712 (JSCO)**

A professional who cleans and organizes homes and offices to maintain a hygienic environment. Demand is stable.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school diploma | 3 years | $0~$0 (Free after compulsory education) |
| Vocational school (House Cleaning Department) | 1-2 years | $500,000~$1,500,000 (Including tuition and materials) |

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| House Cleaning Skilled Worker | Japan House Cleaning Association | Optional | Voluntary private certification |
| Building Cleaning Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Not a national qualification but public certification |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 450,000. Due to aging population and increase in dual-income households, demand for professional cleaning services is steady. Labor shortage is chronic in this field.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

House cleaning is hard to fully replace with AI and robots, as they play a supporting role but humans handle detailed work and customer service.

Starting from scratch, you can advance to chief or manager by honing skills. Self-employment is also an option.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- routine floor cleaning
- large-scale waste collection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- cleaning plan optimization
- customer data analysis

**Human moat:**

- attention to detail
- adaptability
- customer trust

**Skills to build:**

- advanced cleaning techniques
- management skills
- customer service skills

**Key growth areas:**

- aging population
- dual-income households
- hygiene awareness
- outsourcing

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Physically confident
- Methodical and enjoys detailed work
- Wants to learn cleaning techniques

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes physical labor
- Easily bored with monotonous work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for house cleaning jobs?**
A: Entry-level: 2.5–3.5 million yen, mid-level: 3.5–4.5 million yen, advanced: 4.5–6 million yen. Self-employed can earn more.

**Q: Can foreigners work in house cleaning jobs?**
A: Yes. It is a target field for Specified Skilled Worker (i) and (ii). Requires JLPT N4 level and passing a skills test. Also available via Technical Intern Training.

**Q: How to advance your career?**
A: Get certified, gain experience, and become a chief or manager. Alternatively, start your own business and take orders directly.
