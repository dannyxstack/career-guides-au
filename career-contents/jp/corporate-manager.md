# Corporate Manager — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 031 (JSCO)**

A profession that formulates management policies and oversees departments in companies and organizations. Includes directors, executive officers, and division heads responsible for overall organizational performance.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 2,300,000. Demand for managers remains stable due to labor shortages from an aging population, but personnel capable of handling DX and globalization are sought. Demand is expected to rise for managers with IT or international experience.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: very high (★★★★½).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Typically, after joining as a general employee, one gains field experience, takes on subordinates, and is promoted to section chief or department head. An MBA or advanced professional qualifications aid career advancement.

**Key growth areas:**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of corporate managers?**
A: Typical salaries: section manager 7–10 million yen, department manager 12–18 million yen, executive officer over 20 million yen. Varies by industry and company size.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to become a manager in Japan?**
A: Generally, a 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is required. If you have advanced management knowledge or experience, a 'Highly Skilled Professional' visa is advantageous. If you are starting your own company, a 'Business Manager' visa applies.

**Q: What experience is needed to become a manager?**
A: Typically, 10-20 years of practical experience and experience managing subordinates are required. Qualifications like an MBA are advantageous for promotion.
