# Tax Accountant — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 182 (JSCO)**

A specialist who handles tax filing, tax consultation, and bookkeeping guidance. They manage taxes for companies and individuals and respond to tax audits.

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tax Accountant | National Tax Agency | Required | Register after passing the exam and gaining practical experience, etc. |
| Certified Public Accountant | Financial Services Agency | Optional | Can also perform tax work; exam difficulty is high. |
| Bookkeeping Level 1 (Nissho) | Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry | Optional | May exempt some subjects of the Tax Accountant exam. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After passing the Tax Accountant exam, work at a tax corporation or accounting firm. Career paths include gaining experience and starting your own practice, or aiming to become a partner within a firm.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated Preparation of Tax Returns
- Automation of Tax Calculations
- Data Entry and Classification
- Simple Tax Advisory Chatbots

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Tax Research (AI-based Legal and Precedent Search)
- Fraud Detection (AI-based Anomaly Analysis)
- Client Tax Report Creation Support
- Tax Planning Simulations

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal Negotiation and Persuasion
- Tax Audit Responses
- Complex Tax Judgments (Gray Areas)
- Building Trust Relationships

**Skills to build:**

- Deepening Tax and Accounting Knowledge
- AI Literacy
- Consulting Skills
- English (International Taxation)

**Key growth areas:**

- Digitalization of tax
- M&A Support
- International Taxation
- Inheritance Tax Planning

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much does a tax accountant earn?**
A: It depends on the employer and experience, but generally 3-5 million yen in the first year, 5-8 million yen for mid-career, and 8-15 million yen for veterans or independent practitioners.

**Q: How can a foreigner become a tax accountant in Japan?**
A: You need to pass the Japanese tax accountant exam, gain practical experience, and register. However, while the residence status 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' may apply, whether tax accountant work is recognized as the main activity is subject to screening.
