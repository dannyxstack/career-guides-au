# Planning and Administrative Assistant — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 253 (JSCO)**

An administrative role in charge of planning, document creation, data analysis, and meeting coordination within a company. Supports management strategy and new business initiatives.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a planning clerk?**
A: The average annual income is about 4.5 million yen, and with experience it can exceed 6 million yen.

**Q: How can a foreigner work as a planning clerk?**
A: An Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is required, along with a university degree or higher and Japanese language ability (N2 or above).

**Q: How to advance your career?**
A: Expanding your career through an MBA, acquiring qualifications such as a Small and Medium Enterprise Diagnostic Consultant, or transferring to another department is effective.
