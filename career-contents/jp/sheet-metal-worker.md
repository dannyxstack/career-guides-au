# Sheet Metal Worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 526 (JSCO)**

A skilled trade that cuts, bends, and welds metal sheets to manufacture automobile and aircraft parts, building materials, etc. Reads blueprints and finishes precision products through manual and machine processing.

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gas Welder Certification | Safety and Health Technical Examination Association | Required | Required for welding work. |
| Arc Welding Special Education | Industrial Safety and Health Act | Required | Mandatory for arc welding workers. |
| Certified Skilled Worker (Sheet Metal) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Levels 1, 2, and 3. Proof of advanced skills. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 68,000. The labor shortage due to the aging population is severe, and this is a target industry for Specified Skilled Worker (ii). There is high demand for skilled workers in the automotive industry and construction sites, and securing young workers is a challenge.

**Competition: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Start as an apprentice and obtain higher-level skills certification. Possible career paths include factory manager or independent business owner. Career advancement can be achieved by learning CAD/CAM and robot operation.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple sheet metal cutting
- Standard bending
- Deburring mass-produced parts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Developing developed view drawings with CAD
- Robot teaching
- AI-assisted quality inspection

**Human moat:**

- Manual processing of complex 3D shapes
- On-site flexible adjustments
- Repair and maintenance skills

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM
- Robot programming
- Heterogeneous material joining techniques
- 3D scanning
- Inheritance of craftsmanship

**Key growth areas:**

- Sheet metal processing
- Technical Intern Training (ii)
- Construction sheet metal
- CAD/CAM

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of sheet metal workers?**
A: Approx. 3-4M JPY for beginner, 4-5.5M for intermediate, 5.5-8M for advanced. Varies with overtime pay and skill allowances.

**Q: Can foreigners get jobs in sheet metal?**
A: Possible with Specified Skilled Worker (i)/(ii) or technical intern. Requires N4 Japanese. Must pass construction field specific skill exam.

**Q: How to go independent as a sheet metal worker?**
A: Obtain Grade 1 skill certification, secure capital and clients. File business registration. Common as sole proprietor or corporation.
