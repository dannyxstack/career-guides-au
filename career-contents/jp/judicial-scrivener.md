# Judicial Scrivener — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 175 (JSCO)**

Judicial scriveners are legal professionals specializing in procedures related to registration and deposits, as well as document preparation for courts, and they also handle representation in summary courts.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a judicial scrivener?**
A: It varies by experience; roughly 4-6 million yen for junior, 6-10 million for mid-level, and 10-15 million for senior levels. Independent practice can yield higher but variable income.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a judicial scrivener in Japan?**
A: Obtaining judicial scrivener qualification requires Japanese language ability and passing the exam. It may qualify for Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa, but foreign judicial scriveners are extremely rare.

**Q: How do I become a judicial scrivener?**
A: After acquiring basic academic skills at university etc., you must pass the judicial scrivener exam. Then register with the Japan Federation of Judicial Scrivener Associations to start practice. The exam is difficult; preparatory schools are common.
