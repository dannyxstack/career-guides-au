# Surveying Technician — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 093 (JSCO)**

A surveying technician is a professional who measures the shape and location of land, providing basic data for map creation and construction work.


---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assistant Surveyor | Geospatial Information Authority of Japan | Required | Required for assisting in surveying work |
| Surveyor | Geospatial Information Authority of Japan | Required | Required as a manager of surveying work |
| Surveyor Business Registration | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism | Optional | Registration required to operate a surveying business |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Advancement from assistant surveyor to surveyor is common. Career progression is possible through gaining experience, mastering skills, or shifting to management.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- GNSS Survey Data Collection
- Simple Surveying with Total Station

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Aerial Photogrammetry with Drones
- Point Cloud Data Processing with 3D Laser Scanners
- AI-based Feature Recognition

**Human moat:**

- On-site Judgment
- Survey Planning Based on Laws
- Quality Control
- Client Negotiation

**Skills to build:**

- Drone Piloting Skills
- 3D Data Analysis
- Automation Script Creation (Python, etc.)
- Project Management

**Key growth areas:**

- i-Construction
- Drone Surveying
- 3D Laser Scanner
- Disaster Prevention and Mitigation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a surveying technician?**
A: Entry-level: 3-4 million yen; veteran: 6-8 million yen. Varies by qualifications and experience.

**Q: How can foreigners work as surveyors in Japan?**
A: You need a status of residence for Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services. Often, qualifications like Assistant Surveyor or higher and Japanese language ability are required. Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Construction) is also an option.

**Q: Is it possible to change careers from surveyor to other occupations?**
A: You can leverage related skills in construction consulting, GIS-related fields, drone piloting, etc.
