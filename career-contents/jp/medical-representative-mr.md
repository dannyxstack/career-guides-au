# Medical Representative (MR) — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 343 (JSCO)**

A professional who bridges medical institutions and pharmaceutical companies through drug information provision and sales activities. Requires medical knowledge and communication skills.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| University (Pharmacy, Science, Medical) | 4 years | $4,000,000~$6,000,000 (Large difference between national/public and private) |
| MR Certification Exam Prep Course | 6 months | $300,000~$600,000 (Optional but effective for passing rate) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| MR Certification | MR Accreditation Center | Optional | Industry standard qualification, renewal required |
| Registered Salesperson | Prefectural Government | Optional | Mandatory for OTC drug sales |

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People interested in medicine or pharmacy
- People good at interpersonal communication
- People who want to leverage sales experience

**Who is not suited**
- People who feel stressed by numbers or quotas
- People not good at public speaking

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of an MR?**
A: Approximately 4-5 million JPY for entry-level, 6-8 million for mid-level, and 9-12 million for senior level.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an MR?**
A: An Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is required, with a university degree or higher and a job offer from a pharmaceutical company.

**Q: How can an MR advance their career?**
A: Obtaining certified MR status, moving to marketing, or getting an MBA can broaden career options.
