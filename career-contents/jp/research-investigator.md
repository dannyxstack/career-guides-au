# Research Investigator — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 292 (JSCO)**

A professional who collects and organizes data through questionnaire and statistical surveys to understand social and economic conditions.

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 250,000. Employment remains stable due to ongoing government statistics and market research demand, but face-to-face surveys are declining with the spread of web surveys.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career advancement to survey design, analysis, or management is possible with experience; obtaining qualifications and skills can lead to a consultant role.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Questionnaire distribution and collection
- Basic tabulation
- Part of field surveys

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Survey design
- Data analysis
- Report writing

**Human moat:**

- Survey design know-how
- Interpersonal skills
- Specialized knowledge

**Skills to build:**

- Statistical analysis
- Data visualization
- Questionnaire design
- Presentations

**Key growth areas:**

- Statistical survey
- Market research
- Social research
- Data analysis

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People good at detailed work
- People with communication skills
- People interested in statistical data

**Who is not suited**
- People who are not sociable
- People who dislike being pressed for deadlines

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a investigator?**
A: As a guide, entry-level: 3-4 million yen, mid-level: 4-5.5 million yen, advanced: 5.5-7 million yen.

**Q: Can foreigners work as investigators?**
A: It is possible if you have high Japanese proficiency and specialized knowledge, but obtaining a visa is not easy.
