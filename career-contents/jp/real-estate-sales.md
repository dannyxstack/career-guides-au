# Real Estate Sales — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 347 (JSCO)**

Sales role handling real estate sales and leasing brokerage. Responsible for proposing properties matching client needs, contract procedures, and after-sales follow-up.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| High school graduate | 3 years | $0~$0 (For public high school) |
| University graduate (optional) | 4 years | $2,500,000~$5,000,000 (For private liberal arts) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real Estate Transaction Specialist | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism | Required | Mandatory. Must attend training every 3 years. |
| Rental Real Estate Management Specialist | Japan Rental Real Estate Management Specialist Council | Optional | Advantageous for rental management work |
| Condominium Management Specialist | Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism | Optional | For condominium owners' associations |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 650,000. Due to population decline and rising vacancies, demand for second-hand properties and renovations is expanding. Meanwhile, AI-driven property matching is advancing, reducing pure brokerage work.

**Competition: high (★★★★☆).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Even beginners can obtain the Real Estate Transaction Specialist license and advance from lead to store manager. Major firms offer career paths to management or business development.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Collecting and organizing property information
- Scheduling property viewings
- Creating standard documents
- Handling simple customer inquiries

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Automatic extraction of potential clients
- Market data analysis
- Initial response via chatbots
- VR property viewing systems

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal negotiation skills
- Local network
- Trust building
- Complex real estate transaction coordination

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- Negotiation skills
- Finance knowledge
- Digital marketing

**Key growth areas:**

- Resale housing distribution
- Renovation
- Rental management
- Vacant house measures

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for real estate sales?**
A: First year around 3-4.5 million yen, but commission-based can exceed 10 million yen. Large firms may have stable salary systems.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in real estate sales?**
A: You need 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' status. Requires university degree or 10+ years experience, and Japanese ability.

**Q: Is the Real Estate Transaction Specialist license mandatory?**
A: Although not essential for working in sales, obtaining a real estate transaction specialist license is strongly recommended since it's required for important matters explanation and contract procedures.
