# Accounting Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 263 (JSCO)**

A clerical position responsible for cash and deposit management, voucher processing, bookkeeping, invoice issuance, and other accounting tasks in companies and organizations.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nissho Bookkeeping Level 2 | The Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry | Optional | Basic qualification for accounting clerks; recommended by many companies. |
| Nissho Bookkeeping Level 1 | The Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry | Optional | Advanced qualification that also serves as eligibility for the tax accountant exam. |
| Tax Accountant | National Tax Agency | Optional | National qualification allowing tax-related work; offers significant career advancement. |
| Certified Public Accountant | Financial Services Agency | Optional | The highest-level qualification for auditing work; extremely challenging. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

Start as an accounting clerk, then advance to accounting supervisor, and section manager. Career advancement is possible by acquiring qualifications as a tax accountant or certified public accountant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Data entry
- Automation of journal entries
- Issuing invoices
- Reconciliation of transaction data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Management analysis reports
- Strategic tax filing planning
- Fraud detection monitoring
- System implementation consulting

**Human moat:**

- Complex tax law interpretation
- Financial analysis directly linked to management decisions
- External negotiations (tax audit response)
- Establishment of internal rules

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced accounting knowledge (tax accountant/certified public accountant)
- Data analysis skills
- Operation of RPA and cloud accounting tools
- Communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Cloud accounting
- RPA
- Electronic Bookkeeping Act
- Invoice system

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of an accounting clerk?**
A: The average annual income is about 4-5 million yen, but varies by experience, qualifications, and company size. With Bookkeeping Level 1 or tax accountant qualifications, it can reach 7 million yen or more.

**Q: What visa does a foreigner need to work as an accounting clerk in Japan?**
A: A 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is required. A university degree or relevant work experience is necessary, and Japanese language ability is important. It is not covered by the Specified Skilled Worker visa.

**Q: What qualifications are recommended for career advancement?**
A: The Nissho Bookkeeping Level 2 (essential), plus Level 1, tax accountant, and certified public accountant are effective for career advancement. In particular, becoming a tax accountant opens up the possibility of independence from accounting.
