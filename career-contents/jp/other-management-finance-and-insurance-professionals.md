# Other Management, Finance, and Insurance Professionals — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 189 (JSCO)**

Professionals engaged in corporate strategy planning, financial analysis, risk management, etc. Includes actuarial and investment analysis work.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for this occupation?**
A: Entry-level: ¥4-6 million, mid-level: ¥7-12 million, senior: ¥15-25 million. Varies greatly by experience, qualifications, and employer.

**Q: How can a foreigner get this job in Japan?**
A: After graduating from university, it is common to join a Japanese financial institution and acquire a 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa status. It's also possible to obtain a visa through the Highly Skilled Professional Point System.

**Q: Are there any required qualifications?**
A: Not required, but qualifications such as Certified Public Accountant, Chartered Member of the Securities Analysts Association (CMA), or actuary are advantageous for your career.
