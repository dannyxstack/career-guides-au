# Metal Press Worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 524 (JSCO)**

A job that punches and forms metal sheets using press machines. Important in the manufacturing of auto parts and home appliances.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Automation via AI and robots is progressing. Mass-produced simple pressing is easily replaced, but multi-variety small-lot, die adjustment, and troubleshooting depend on skilled human workers.

Career progression: apprentice → intermediate press worker → skilled worker/multi-process operator → production leader or die adjustment specialist. Skill certification exams are useful for career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine press processing
- Simple material loading/unloading
- Simple visual quality inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- CAD support for die design
- Use of AI-based quality prediction and anomaly detection systems
- Optimization of process scheduling

**Human moat:**

- Advanced die adjustment and repair skills
- Troubleshooting
- Prototyping and improvement per customer requests

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM skills
- Programming (robots, PLC)
- Quality control/process improvement (QC methods)
- English/Chinese (overseas transactions)

**Key growth areas:**

- Automotive parts
- Die and mold technology
- Precision press processing
- Robot integration

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical annual salary for metal press workers?**
A: Entry-level ¥2.5-3.5M, mid ¥3.5-4.8M, skilled ¥4.8-6.2M. Management may be higher.

**Q: What visa do foreign workers need?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing) is most common, requiring N4 or higher Japanese and passing a skills test. The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa requires a university degree.

**Q: What qualifications are advantageous for career advancement?**
A: Metal Press Processing Technician (Grade 1/2) is valid. Also, Press Machine Operator Supervisor is a legally required qualification and often mandatory in manufacturing sites.
