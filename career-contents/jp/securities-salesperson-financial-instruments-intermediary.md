# Securities Salesperson/Financial Instruments Intermediary — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 333 (JSCO)**

Financial professionals who trade or intermediate securities such as stocks and bonds. At major securities firms and financial institutions, they support clients' asset management.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| University (Economics, Commerce) | 4 years | $4,000,000~$6,000,000 (National university: approx. 4 million yen; private liberal arts: approx. 6 million yen) |
| Vocational school (Finance) | 2 years | $2,000,000~$3,000,000 (Curriculum specialized in financial practice) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Securities Sales Representative License (Type 1) | Japan Securities Dealers Association | Required | A mandatory national qualification for securities sales. Type 1 allows handling all products. |
| Financial Planner (AFP/CFP) | Japan Association for Financial Planners | Optional | Advantageous for asset management advice. CFP is a higher-level qualification. |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 120,000. Due to population decline and the spread of AI/robo-advisors, traditional face-to-face sales are shrinking. Demand remains in specialized areas such as high-net-worth individuals and corporate clients.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

After joining, through in-house training and obtaining qualifications, career progression: sales representative → senior sales → deputy branch manager → branch manager. Career advancement possible by obtaining CFP, securities analyst, or other certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Accept stock buy/sell orders
- Provide routine market information
- Create simple asset diagnostic reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze and optimize client portfolios
- Run risk management simulations
- Analyze market trends and formulate investment strategies

**Human moat:**

- Build trust with clients
- Hear and propose solutions for complex needs
- Provide advanced financial advice

**Skills to build:**

- Financial planning
- Data analysis skills
- Advanced communication skills
- English proficiency (foreign-affiliated companies)

**Key growth areas:**

- Asset Management Advisor
- Sustainable Finance
- Fintech
- Wealth Management Sales

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for securities sales?**
A: It depends on years of service and performance, but generally 3.5-5 million yen in the first year, 6-10 million yen for mid-career, and over 10 million yen for veterans. Commission rates are high, making it a job with large individual differences.

**Q: How can foreigners obtain a work visa for this occupation?**
A: An Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is required. You need to have graduated from a Japanese university or have practical experience in specialized knowledge (usually over 10 years). Also, Japanese language ability (around N1 level) is effectively necessary.
