# Metal Welding/Cutting Worker — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 528 (JSCO)**

Skilled professional who welds and cuts metal parts and structures. High demand in manufacturing and construction, where expertise is valued.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gas Welding Technician | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Required | Required for gas welding work. |
| Welding Skill Evaluation Test (JIS) | Japan Welding Engineering Society | Optional | Evaluates welding skills per JIS standards. Widely recognized in the industry. |
| Special Welding Technician | Japan Welding Engineering Society | Optional | Proof of advanced welding skills. Advantageous for promotion and career growth. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 275,000. Due to labor shortages in manufacturing and demand for repairing aging infrastructure, stable employment is expected. However, automation is reducing simpler tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Start as an apprentice, advance by passing skill tests. Multi-skilling and promotion to site supervisor possible. Specified Skilled Worker (ii) enables long-term work.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple repetitive welding on automated lines
- Mass welding of standard products

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI optimization support for welding conditions
- Remote monitoring and operation systems

**Human moat:**

- Welding of complex shapes
- Flexible on-site response
- Final quality inspection judgement

**Skills to build:**

- Robot programming
- Dissimilar material welding techniques
- Knowledge of non-destructive testing

**Key growth areas:**

- Welding robots
- Infrastructure aging
- Technical Intern Training
- Specified Skilled Worker

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a welder?**
A: Entry-level: 3–4 million JPY, mid-career: 4–5.5 million, veteran: 5–7 million. Increases with qualifications & experience.

**Q: How can foreigners work as welders in Japan?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing) is common. Must pass skills test and Japanese test (approx. N4). Spouse/family can accompany for Specified Skilled Worker (ii).

**Q: What is the career path for a welder?**
A: From apprentice, obtain qualifications like JIS Welding Specialist, step up as skilled worker. Paths also include site supervisor or robotic welding operator.
