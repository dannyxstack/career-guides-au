# Rubber and Plastic Products Inspector — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 578 (JSCO)**

Production process worker who inspects appearance, dimensions, and quality of rubber and plastic products. Main tasks involve visual inspection and use of measuring instruments on the production line.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career path from inspector to team leader or quality assurance department is possible. Recognition as a skilled worker and promotion to production management or quality control positions can be expected.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Visual inspection by sight
- Simple dimensional measurement
- Pass/fail sorting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Verification of AI inspection system results
- Root cause analysis of anomalies
- Statistical analysis of inspection data

**Human moat:**

- Fine judgment of anomalies
- Adjustment and maintenance of inspection equipment
- Guidance for workers

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of AI and machine learning
- Non-destructive testing techniques
- Statistical Quality Control (SQC)
- Equipment maintenance skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Quality Control
- Automated Flaw Detection
- Resin Molding
- Visual Inspection AI

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for rubber/plastic product inspection?**
A: Entry-level: 2.8–3.5 million yen, mid-level: 3.5–4.5 million yen, veteran: 4.5–5.5 million yen. Slightly low for manufacturing, but varies with overtime and bonuses.

**Q: What visa is needed for foreigners to work in this occupation?**
A: Generally, the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa (university degree or higher) or Technical Intern Training visa applies. Specified Skilled Worker (i) may cover manufacturing, but inspection-only work has limited applicability.

**Q: Will AI and automation eliminate this job?**
A: Some visual inspection is being replaced by AI image inspection, but complex judgments and sampling inspections require human decision. Job content will change with AI adoption.
