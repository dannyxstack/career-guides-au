# Judge — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 171 (JSCO)**

An independent judicial profession that adjudicates civil and criminal cases in courts. As a core member of the legal triad, duties are performed based on the Court Act.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Law School (Advanced Course) | 2 years | $3,000,000~$5,000,000 (Private: approx. ¥1 million/year (National: approx. ¥1.5 million)) |
| Legal Apprenticeship (Shihō Shūshū) | 1 year | $0~$0 (Stipend provided (approx. ¥200,000/month)) |

---

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passing the National Bar Exam | Ministry of Justice | Required | Those who have passed the Bar Exam and completed legal apprenticeship |
| Appointment as Judge | Supreme Court | Required | Appointed by the Supreme Court; after appointment, status as a judge is guaranteed. |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★☆☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 4,000. The quota for judges is set by law, with only about 20-30 hired annually, making it a highly competitive field. Although the number of lawsuits is declining due to the falling birthrate, no major fluctuations are expected due to judicial stability.

**Competition: very high (★★★★★).**
**Work intensity: high (★★★★☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After completing legal apprenticeship, one is hired as an assistant judge and promoted to judge after 10+ years of experience. Further career paths include High Court judge and Supreme Court judge.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Case law research
- Format creation
- Data entry

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fact-finding assistance
- Providing reference information for sentencing
- Proposing settlement plans between parties

**Human moat:**

- Human ethical judgment
- Legal interpretation
- Consideration of social norms
- Trust and authority

**Skills to build:**

- Legal tech
- Data analysis
- Psychology
- Communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Judicial system reform
- IT adoption in court procedures
- Increase in family cases
- International adjudication

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

---

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with meticulous analytical skills
- People who are fair and independent
- People who can maintain focus for long periods

**Who is not suited**
- People who are weak under pressure
- People who lack flexibility

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a judge?**
A: About 6-8 million yen for assistant judges, 12-15 million for judges, and over 20 million for the Supreme Court.

**Q: Can a foreigner become a judge in Japan?**
A: It is generally impossible as Japanese nationality is required. However, it is possible to work at a Japanese law firm with qualifications such as a foreign lawyer.
