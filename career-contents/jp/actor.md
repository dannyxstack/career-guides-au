# Actor — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 233 (JSCO)**

A profession that performs roles based on scripts in film, television, stage, etc., delivering emotion and empathy to audiences and viewers.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium (★★½☆☆).** National workforce approx. 12,000. Demand is stable due to the expansion of digital distribution, but competition is extremely intense, and regular employment is rare.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of an actor?**
A: Beginner: around 1-3 million yen. Intermediate: 3-8 million yen. Advanced: over 8 million yen.

**Q: Can foreigners work as actors in Japan?**
A: Possible with an entertainment visa, but requires performance contracts and is very challenging.

**Q: Is a training school necessary to become an actor?**
A: Not mandatory, but it's common to learn basics at vocational schools or theater companies.
