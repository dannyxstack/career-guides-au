# Machinery/Equipment Sales — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 344 (JSCO)**

A profession responsible for sales of industrial machinery and machine tools, proposing and selling to corporate clients. A specialized sales role that bridges manufacturers and users.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

After gaining sales experience, career advancement to managerial positions such as chief or section manager, or as a specialized salesperson. Deepening technical knowledge opens paths to consulting sales.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine quotation creation
- Customer list management
- Simple inquiry handling

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Customer need analysis
- Proposal creation
- Sales strategy formulation

**Human moat:**

- Proposal skills based on technical understanding
- Building long-term trust relationships
- On-site coordination skills

**Skills to build:**

- Basic knowledge of mechanical engineering
- Data analysis skills
- Presentation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Manufacturing industry
- Capital Investment
- Automation
- Labor Saving

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for machinery equipment sales?**
A: Beginner: 3.5-4.5 million yen, Mid-level: 5-7 million yen, Veteran: 7-10 million yen. Higher income possible with commission-based companies.

**Q: What visa is required for foreigners to work in machinery equipment sales?**
A: Mainly the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa. Mechanical knowledge and sales experience required, with company sponsorship. Specified Skilled Worker (i) is limited to support tasks in manufacturing.
