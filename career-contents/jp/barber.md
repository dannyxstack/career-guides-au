# barber — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 381 (JSCO)**

A barber is a professional who grooms customers' appearance through haircuts, shaving, and hair care. A national license is required, and technical and customer service skills are needed.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Barber License | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Required | Mandatory. Written and practical exams |
| Barber Practical Exam | Each prefecture | Required | Cutting, shaving, etc. |
| Managing Barber License | Each prefecture | Optional | Required for shop management |

---

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 210,000. The barber industry is expected to see stable demand due to an aging population, but competition is somewhat intense due to an increase in new openings. It is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker visas, so labor shortages are moderate.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Starting as an apprentice, after obtaining a license, independent practice or managing a chain store is common. Advanced technical skills and business knowledge are advantageous for career advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple cutting tasks
- Appointment reception
- Inventory management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Style suggestions (AI image analysis)
- Customer management and analysis
- Appointment optimization

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal communication
- Manual techniques (shaving, etc.)
- Creative styling

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced cutting techniques
- Customer service skills
- Hair care knowledge
- Digital marketing

**Key growth areas:**

- Hair care demand
- Men's grooming
- Elderly barbering
- Smart barbering

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a barber?**
A: According to a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare survey, the average is about 3.8 million yen. Varies by experience and work style.

**Q: Can foreigners work as barbers?**
A: In principle, it is not permitted under the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa. Possible only in exceptional cases such as special skills or Business Manager visa.

**Q: How to become independent?**
A: After obtaining a national license, gain practical experience, learn management knowledge, and start a business. Capital about 5 million yen ~.
