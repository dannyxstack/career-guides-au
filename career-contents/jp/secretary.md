# Secretary — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 255 (JSCO)**

Administrative role that supports executives and managers by handling scheduling, document preparation, phone correspondence, and information organization.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression from general secretary to executive assistant or board secretary; in large companies, secretarial certification and language skills aid promotion.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated schedule adjustment
- Standard document creation and formatting
- Initial screening of phone calls
- Data entry and information organization

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Efficient meeting scheduling (using AI assistants)
- Decision support for prioritization
- Information analysis and summarization (AI-assisted)
- Multilingual support (translation AI)

**Human moat:**

- Advanced interpersonal skills
- Deep understanding of organizational culture
- Handling confidential information
- Adaptability and judgment

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced Excel/PowerPoint Skills
- AI/RPA Business Application Skills
- Foreign Languages (English, Chinese, etc.)
- Project Management Skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Secretary
- Executive Assistant
- Work Efficiency
- AI Utilization

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a secretary?**
A: Entry-level is about 2.8–3.5 million yen, mid-level 3.5–4.5 million yen, and senior-level 4.5–6 million yen. For executive secretaries or foreign-affiliated companies, it can be higher.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a secretary in Japan?**
A: For general secretarial work, it is difficult to obtain the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa, but if it includes interpretation/translation or involves advanced duties at an international company, it may be possible.

**Q: How can a secretary advance their career?**
A: It is common to improve language and business skills to aim for executive assistant or senior secretary roles. Transfer to other departments or management positions is also possible.
