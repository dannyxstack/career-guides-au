# Cleaning occupation — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 385 (JSCO)**

A profession involving washing and finishing clothes and textile products. It requires specialized skills such as dry cleaning and stain removal, and demand is high in facilities like hotels and hospitals.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Compulsory education | Not required | $0~$0 (Educational background irrelevant; can be learned through OJT.) |
| Vocational training school (Cleaning department) | 6 months to 1 year | $100,000~$300,000 (May be free through Hello Training and other programs.) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cleaner (National Qualification) | Prefectural governor | Optional | National qualification. Not mandatory for work, but required to establish a business office. |
| Dry Cleaning Skill Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Skills test. Levels 1 and 2. Proof of skill. |
| Stain Removal Skill Worker | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | Optional | Skills test. Highly specialized. |

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 350,000. Chronic labor shortage persists, and demand is expected to increase due to an aging population. Meanwhile, automation is progressing, and simple tasks are on the decline.

**Competition: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Can be started without experience; with skill development, one can become a factory manager or open an independent business. Dry cleaning skills and customer service abilities are valued.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Washing machine operation and monitoring
- Simple ironing
- Sorting work (e.g., RFID tags)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Stain removal judgment (with AI image recognition support)
- Inventory management and demand forecasting
- Quality check (camera + AI)

**Human moat:**

- Sensory-based finish judgment
- Understanding customer needs through dialogue
- Identifying stain causes and chemical treatment

**Skills to build:**

- Dry cleaning techniques
- Stain removal skills
- Fabric knowledge
- Customer service and communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Dry cleaning
- Stain removal
- Wet cleaning
- Factory management

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for cleaning jobs?**
A: Entry-level: ¥2.5-3.5M, intermediate: ¥3.5-4.5M, senior management: ¥4.5-6.0M. Varies by employer and region.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need for cleaning jobs?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Building Cleaning Field) is common. Requires JLPT N4+, max 5 years stay.

**Q: How to advance in a cleaning career?**
A: There is a path to becoming a factory manager or starting your own business by obtaining certifications like the Dry Cleaning Skill Certification.
