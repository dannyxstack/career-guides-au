## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Can start with no experience and advance from general clerical to secretary/assistant, or to sales/accounting clerical. Professional qualifications broaden opportunities for raises and job changes.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People good at meticulous and accurate work
- People who can perform basic computer operations

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike monotonous tasks
- People who value creativity

---

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for general clerical work?**
A: 250-350 JPY for 0-3 years experience, 350-450 JPY for mid-career, 450-600 JPY for management.

**Q: Can foreigners work in general clerical jobs in Japan?**
A: General clerical is often excluded from the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa. It may be possible if the role involves specialized knowledge/experience, such as sales clerical.
