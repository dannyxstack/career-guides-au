# Metal Processing/Welding/Cutting Inspector — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 562 (JSCO)**

A profession that inspects welding, cutting, and processing quality of metal parts using visual checks or measuring instruments to ensure compliance with standards.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welding Skill Evaluation Test (JIS certification) | Japan Welding Engineering Society | Required | Types include covered arc, semi-auto, TIG, etc. Required certification. |
| Non-Destructive Testing Technician (UT, RT, MT, etc.) | Japanese Society for Non-Destructive Inspection | Optional | Levels 1-3. Preferred for inspection work. |
| Skill Certification (Welding) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (Prefectural Vocational Development Association) | Optional | Class 1 & 2. Useful for career advancement. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

From entry-level, one can obtain skill certifications and advance to site supervisor or inspection leader. Certifications like Welding Skill Test or NDT technician benefit career growth.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple manual welding (standardized products)
- Visual inspection (partially image recognition AI)
- Standardized cutting operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Non-destructive inspection data analysis
- Welding robot teaching and maintenance
- Optimization of advanced welding conditions
- Integration with quality management systems

**Human moat:**

- Non-standard/complex shape welding
- Advanced non-destructive inspection judgment (defect type/depth evaluation)
- Troubleshooting based on field experience
- New material response and prototyping processes

**Skills to build:**

- Welding robot programming
- Non-destructive inspection data analysis
- 3D scanning and measurement technology
- Operation and verification of AI inspection systems

**Key growth areas:**

- Automotive parts
- Construction steel frames
- Non-destructive testing
- Factory automation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income for this job?**
A: It varies by experience and qualifications: entry-level 2.8~3.8M JPY, mid-level 3.8~5M JPY, senior-level 5~7M JPY. Overtime and skill allowances cause variation.

**Q: Is there a visa for foreigners to do this job?**
A: Typical examples include Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing). Skill Visa (Welding) and Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services (Technology) are also applicable.

**Q: What qualifications are required?**
A: Not mandatory, but passing the JIS Welding Skill Evaluation Test is strongly recommended. Holding Non-Destructive Testing Engineer or Skill Test (Welding) qualifications is advantageous.
