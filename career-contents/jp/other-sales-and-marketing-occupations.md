# Other Sales and Marketing Occupations — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 349 (JSCO)**

Sales occupations broadly encompassing specialized sales roles such as insurance agents, real estate agents, and route sales. Main duties include agency sales and face-to-face selling.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 900,000. Phone and visit-based sales are declining due to digitalization, but demand for specialized product sales remains steady. Labor shortages persist.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

With the spread of AI and CRM tools, routine sales (calls, visits) are decreasing, but sales requiring interpersonal negotiation and specialized knowledge remain. Overall, sales positions are trending downward.

For career advancement, customer management skills and proposal abilities are important. Options include starting a business, promotion to management, or obtaining an MBA or other qualifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Telemarketing Script Creation
- Simple quote creation
- Customer Data Entry

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Sales Talk Analysis
- Optimal Visit Route Suggestion
- Cross-sell Recommendation

**Human moat:**

- Human relationship building skills
- Complex business negotiations
- Long-term trust building

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis
- Presentation
- Consultative Proposals

**Key growth areas:**

- Insurance Sales
- Real Estate Sales
- Corporate Sales
- B2B Sales

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Is commission-based pay common for sales jobs?**
A: Depends on the industry. In insurance and real estate, there are various models from full commission to fixed + commission. Many companies offer a fixed salary in the first year.

**Q: Can foreigners work in sales jobs in Japan?**
A: Yes. An Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is required, along with a university degree and Japanese proficiency (N2 or above). Specialized knowledge (e.g.,宅建) is advantageous.

**Q: How can I advance from sales to management?**
A: Build a track record and highlight experience in mentoring subordinates and team management. Internal/external training and obtaining an MBA also aid in promotion.
