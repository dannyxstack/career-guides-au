# Assembler of Measuring Instruments and Optical Machinery — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 545 (JSCO)**

Skilled worker who assembles precision parts of measuring instruments and optical devices according to drawings. Requires quality control and fine adjustments.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 45,000. Manufacturing industry faces challenges in skill transfer; high-precision assembly is difficult to automate. Not covered by Specified Skilled Worker but supplemented by Technical Intern Training. Long-term demand is stable.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**Work intensity: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Start as apprentice, become multi-skilled and acquire inspection/adjustment skills to become expert. Career path to leader or process management exists.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive simple assembly
- Simple inspection tasks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Fine-tuning and correction work
- Quality data analysis
- Assembly sequence optimization

**Human moat:**

- Subtle tactile sensitivity
- Adjustment know-how for optical components
- Abnormal Sound/Vibration Discrimination

**Skills to build:**

- Precision Measurement Techniques
- Digital Tool Operation
- QC Methods
- Robot Programming Basics

**Key growth areas:**

- High-precision assembly
- Optical equipment
- Inspection process
- Skill transfer

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for beginners?**
A: Around 2.8–3.5 million yen annually (monthly salary 230,000–290,000 yen). Graduates from technical high schools may also receive skill allowances.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work in this occupation?**
A: The Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is typical. However, work involving only assembly is difficult; applications are combined with design/development duties. Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing) also covers this.

**Q: Will AI or robots take over this job?**
A: High-precision adjustments and inspections require human senses, making full automation difficult. Instead, the trend is toward collaboration with AI, such as analyzing inspection data.
