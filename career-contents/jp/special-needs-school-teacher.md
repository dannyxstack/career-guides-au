# Special Needs School Teacher — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 196 (JSCO)**

Provides specialized instruction and support to students with disabilities based on individualized education plans.


---

## 1. Education path / duration / cost

| Stage | Duration | Cost (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Bachelor's (Teacher Training Program) | 4 years | $2,500,000~$5,000,000 (Approx. ¥2.5 million at national universities, ¥5 million at private universities) |
| Teacher Training Course (Special Needs School Teacher License) | 1–2 years | $500,000~$1,500,000 (Additional coursework via correspondence universities or graduate schools) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Special Needs School Teacher License | Prefectural Boards of Education | Required | Mandatory license; obtained in addition to a kindergarten/elementary/junior high/high school license |
| Kindergarten, Elementary, Junior High, High School Teacher License | Prefectural Boards of Education | Required | Required as prerequisite for special needs school teacher license |
| Teacher Recruitment Exam (Public Schools) | Prefectural Boards of Education | Required | Mandatory for public school employment |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

After obtaining a teaching license, public school teachers pass recruitment exams and advance to vice-principal/principal roles or specialist positions like special needs coordinator.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Record Keeping
- Data Analysis
- Automatic Generation of Teaching Materials

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Developing Individualized Education Plans
- Customization of Teaching Materials
- Progress Management

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal Empathy
- Non-Verbal Communication
- Flexible Responses Based on Disability Characteristics

**Skills to build:**

- Assessment Techniques
- Behavior Analysis
- Specialized Knowledge of Special Needs Education
- Parent Collaboration Skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Special Needs Education
- Disability Support for Developmental Disorders
- Inclusive Education
- Individualized Education Plan (IEP)

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a special needs school teacher?**
A: Depending on experience and region, starting salary at public schools is about 3.5-4.5 million yen, with experienced teachers earning 6-8 million yen.

**Q: Can foreigners become special needs school teachers?**
A: A Japanese teaching license is required, and advanced Japanese skills are needed, making it a difficult immigration route.

**Q: What educational background is needed to become a special needs school teacher?**
A: Complete a teacher training course at a 4-year university and obtain a special needs school teaching license. Then, for public schools, pass the recruitment exam.
