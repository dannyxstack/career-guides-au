# Civil Engineer — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 092 (JSCO)**

Specialist in planning, design, and construction management of social infrastructure such as roads, bridges, tunnels, and dams. High demand due to national resilience and disaster prevention measures.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income of a civil engineer?**
A: Entry-level: ¥3.5-4.5M, mid-level: ¥5-7M, senior: ¥7-10M. Varies by certifications and employer.

**Q: How can foreigners work as civil engineers in Japan?**
A: A university degree and civil engineering experience qualify for the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa. 'Specified Skilled Worker (i) and (ii)' are also available in construction.

**Q: Key points for career advancement?**
A: Obtaining certifications like 'Grade 1 Civil Engineering Construction Management Engineer' or 'Professional Engineer' is key. You can advance from construction management to design/project management.
