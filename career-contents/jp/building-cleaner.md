# Building Cleaner — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 711 (JSCO)**

This occupation involves cleaning floors, windows, and restrooms in office buildings, commercial facilities, and public institutions to maintain a clean environment.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 600,000. Labor shortages due to aging are severe; demand is stable, but long-term employment is declining due to population decrease. It is a target occupation for Specified Skilled Worker.

**Competition: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career paths include starting with no experience, gaining on-site experience, and advancing to chief or managerial positions. Management roles at building maintenance companies or self-employment are possible.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Floor cleaning
- Window cleaning (high-rise)
- Garbage collection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimization of cleaning plans
- Remote monitoring of robots
- Integration with dirt detection sensors

**Human moat:**

- Detailed manual work
- Communication with customers
- Flexible response

**Skills to build:**

- Cleaning techniques
- Hygiene management knowledge
- Machine operation skills
- English (for foreign workers)

**Key growth areas:**

- Labor shortage
- Aging
- Cleaning robots
- Rising hygiene awareness

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike monotonous work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual income of a building cleaner?**
A: The average annual income is about 3.5 million yen, ranging from 2.5 million to 5.5 million yen depending on experience and position.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need to work as cleaners in Japan?**
A: Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Building Cleaning) or Technical Intern Training (ii) (Building Cleaning) are standard. Japanese Language Proficiency Test N4 or higher and passing a skills test are required.
