# Sales Clerk — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 289 (JSCO)**

A role supporting sales operations through document creation, data entry, and customer correspondence. An unsung hero ensuring smooth corporate sales activities.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Sales clerical work is highly likely to be replaced by AI-driven data entry automation and advanced CRM, with routine tasks particularly shrinking.

Many start in general clerical positions, with opportunities to advance into sales or sales planning roles by deepening sales knowledge.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Slip processing
- Data entry
- Simple customer support (chatbots)
- Document preparation assistance

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Sales data analysis
- Customer management system operation
- Complex complaint handling

**Human moat:**

- Interpersonal skills
- Understanding of sales processes
- Adaptability

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis
- CRM operation
- Sales planning ability
- Communication skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Sales support system
- Customer management CRM
- Data entry efficiency
- Sales assistant

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person who can work steadily and accurately
- Person good at multitasking
- Person who finds fulfillment in sales support

**Who is not suited**
- Person who likes creative work
- Person who likes speaking in public and aims for a sales role

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a sales assistant?**
A: Varies by experience; entry-level around ¥3 million, mid-level ¥4 million, senior ¥5-6 million.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a sales assistant?**
A: Regular sales assistant positions are generally ineligible for the Engineer/Specialist visa, making work visas difficult. However, if combined with interpretation/translation or international business, the visa may be possible.

**Q: What skills are required for a sales assistant?**
A: Basic operation of office software such as Excel and Word, accurate data entry, communication skills, multitasking required.
