A role supporting sales operations through document creation, data entry, and customer correspondence. An unsung hero ensuring smooth corporate sales activities.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Person who can work steadily and accurately
- Person good at multitasking
- Person who finds fulfillment in sales support

**Who is not suited**
- Person who likes creative work
- Person who likes speaking in public and aims for a sales role

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a sales assistant?**
A: Varies by experience; entry-level around ¥3 million, mid-level ¥4 million, senior ¥5-6 million.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a sales assistant?**
A: Regular sales assistant positions are generally ineligible for the Engineer/Specialist visa, making work visas difficult. However, if combined with interpretation/translation or international business, the visa may be possible.

**Q: What skills are required for a sales assistant?**
A: Basic operation of office software such as Excel and Word, accurate data entry, communication skills, multitasking required.
