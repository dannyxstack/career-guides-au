# Assemblers of General, Production, and Business Machinery — career analysis · Japan

**Occupation code: 541 (JSCO)**

Specialist in the production process who assembles, adjusts, and inspects parts for industrial and business machinery. Supports the core of manufacturing.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

Japan issues status-of-residence work visas by field, and high-scoring professionals can use the Highly Skilled Professional route, which offers a faster path to permanent residence. Eligibility depends on your qualifications and employer, not on any single occupation.

**Official immigration source:** [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://www.isa.go.jp/en/)

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy manufacturing and have dexterous hands
- People with patience to repeat same tasks accurately
- People who can work cooperatively in a team

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike monotonous tasks
- People who prefer creative work

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the annual income for machine assembly?**
A: Entry-level: ¥2.8-3.7M, mid-level: ¥3.7-4.8M, senior: ¥4.8-6.0M. Varies with overtime and certifications.

**Q: What visa do foreigners need for this occupation?**
A: The most common is Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Manufacturing sector). Requires skill test and JLPT N4 or higher. May also qualify for Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa.

**Q: What are the career advancement paths?**
A: Obtain skill certification (e.g., Mechanical Assembly Skill Test) or become multi-skilled to move into leadership or quality control roles. Career change to production engineering or equipment maintenance is also possible.
