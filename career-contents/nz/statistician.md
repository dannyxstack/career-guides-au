# Statistician（Statistician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：224212 – Statistician。**

Statisticians are in high demand in New Zealand, eligible for Green List Direct Residence with a bachelor's degree or higher, employed in government, health, finance, offering competitive salaries and strong immigration prospects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：极好（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 满足学历与薪资要求（中位数工资$31.61/小时以上），无需工作经历即可直接申请居留 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 6分制：硕士学历+技能工作经验等可累积6分申请居留 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 用于持工作许可就业，后转绿名单或SMC |

**PR难度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Statistician？**
- 喜欢数据分析和解决实际问题的人
- 具备数学或统计学背景的毕业生

**谁不适合学Statistician？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for statisticians in New Zealand?**
答：In 2024, median salary is around $80,000, entry-level $55,000-$70,000, senior up to $130,000.

**问：How can statisticians immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Statisticians are on Green List Tier 1; with a bachelor's degree and meeting salary threshold, they can apply for Straight to Residence Visa; also eligible for SMC 6-point system.

**问：Do statisticians need registration?**
答：No mandatory registration, but NZSA certification enhances job prospects.
