## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is the income of an insurance broker mainly from commissions?**
答：Yes, junior brokers usually have a base salary plus commissions; as experience grows, commission proportion increases, and top brokers may earn 50%-70% from commissions.

**问：Does Insurance Broker qualify for Green List direct residence?**
答：Currently not on Green List Tier 1, but it is a Tier 2 work-to-residence occupation; after 2 years with an accredited employer, you may apply for residence.

**问：What local licenses are required to become an insurance broker in NZ?**
答：Must register with the Financial Markets Authority (FMA), complete NZ Certificate in Financial Services Level 5 or equivalent, and pass ethics and regulatory exams.
