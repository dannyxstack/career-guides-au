# Electrical Engineering Technician（Electrical Engineering Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：312312 – Electrical Engineering Technician。**

Assist electrical engineers in designing, testing, installing, and maintaining electrical systems and equipment, covering power distribution, control systems, and industrial automation. This occupation is eligible for residence under New Zealand's Skilled Migrant Category and Green List (Tier 2), with stable employment prospects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for electrical engineering technicians in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 50,000-65,000, mid-level NZD 70,000-90,000, senior NZD 95,000-120,000. Varies by region and industry, with Auckland typically higher.

**问：How can an electrical engineering technician migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Via Skilled Migrant Category (SMC 6-point system) or Green List Tier 2 Work to Residence. Requires a job offer from an accredited employer, salary at median (approx. NZD 67,000), and meeting English requirements.

**问：Are there many job opportunities for this occupation in New Zealand?**
答：Steady opportunities, especially in construction, infrastructure, and renewable energy. Competition exists; local experience and registration are advantageous.
