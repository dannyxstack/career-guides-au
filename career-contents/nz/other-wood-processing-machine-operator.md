# Other Wood Processing Machine Operator（Other Wood Processing Machine Operator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：711314 – Other Wood Processing Machine Operator。**

Operates wood processing machines like sanders and moulders in New Zealand to produce building components and furniture; typically via AEWV, not on Green List.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a wood processing machine operator in NZ?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around NZD 45,000-53,000, senior levels up to NZD 63,000-72,000, slightly below the median wage.

**问：Can this occupation lead to NZ residency?**
答：Primarily via AEWV work visa; Skilled Migrant Category possible with points (e.g., bachelor's + 3 years experience), but not on Green List, making residency challenging.

**问：What qualifications or training are required?**
答：Usually no formal education needed, but a NZ Certificate in Wood Processing (Level 4) or apprenticeship improves job prospects; workplace safety training is common.
