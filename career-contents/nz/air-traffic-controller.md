# Air Traffic Controller（Air Traffic Controller）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：231112 – Air Traffic Controller。**

Air traffic controllers monitor and direct aircraft takeoffs, landings, and flight paths to ensure safe and efficient airspace operations. This occupation is on New Zealand's Green List Tier 1, offering a direct residence pathway.

---

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Air Traffic Controller？**
- 抗压能力强、能在高压下快速决策者
- 具备出色空间想象和多任务处理能力者
- 愿意接受严格培训和定期考核，纪律性强者

**谁不适合学Air Traffic Controller？**
- 难以适应轮班制和不规律作息者
- 对高噪音、长时间注视雷达屏幕感到不适者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can I apply for skilled migration without a New Zealand local license?**
答：No. Air traffic controllers must hold a license from the Civil Aviation Authority of New Zealand (CAA). You must complete training with Airways New Zealand (paid) to obtain the license before applying for Green List Tier 1 residence.

**问：What is the salary range for air traffic controllers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level (0-3 years) earns NZD 75,000-100,000; mid-level (3-7 years) NZD 100,000-140,000; senior or area control roles NZD 140,000-180,000 annually.

**问：Which employers hire air traffic controllers in New Zealand?**
答：The main employer is Airways New Zealand (the national air navigation service provider). Other employers include major airports (Auckland, Christchurch) and the New Zealand Defence Force. Airways runs annual trainee intakes.
