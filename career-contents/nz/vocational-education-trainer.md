# Vocational Education Trainer（Vocational Education Trainer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：242211 – Vocational Education Trainer。**

Vocational education trainers teach industry skills in New Zealand, with stable demand. They can apply for residency via the Skilled Migrant Category or Green List, requiring qualifications and relevant experience.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Work to Residence） | 从事绿色清单相关行业（如建筑、ICT）的培训师，工作2年后可申请居留。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 满足6分制要求，学历+工作经验或新西兰工作可申请。 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 获得认证雇主offer，从事培训师工作，可续签并转居留。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for vocational education trainers?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-65,000; experienced: NZD 65,000-80,000; senior: NZD 80,000-100,000.

**问：Can vocational education trainers immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Yes, typically via SMC or Green List Tier 2 if the industry is on the list, meeting qualification and work requirements.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a vocational education trainer?**
答：Typically a Level 3+ industry certificate and a teaching qualification (e.g., NZQF Level 4+ diploma); some require IELTS 7.0.
