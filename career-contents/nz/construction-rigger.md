# Construction Rigger（Construction Rigger）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：821711 – Construction Rigger。**

Dogmen direct crane operators to move and position heavy equipment; Riggers attach and secure lifting gear. Occupation on Green List Tier 1, eligible for direct residence, in high demand for large-scale projects in New Zealand.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 直接居留签证，无需工作两年，符合绿色清单Tier 1要求即可申请 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 6分制技术移民，符合职业要求并满足积分可申请居留 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，适合未直接满足居留条件的申请人，积累经验后转居留 |

**PR难度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Construction Rigger？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for a construction rigger in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level salary is around NZD 50,000-65,000 per year, hourly rate NZD 25-32; senior can reach NZD 85,000-110,000.

**问：Can a rigger immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Yes. It is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa if requirements are met.
