# Mental Health Nurse（Mental Health Nurse）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：254423 – Mental Health Nurse。**

Mental health nurses in New Zealand assess, treat, and support patients with mental health conditions. Registered and experienced, this role is on the Green List Tier 1, offering a direct pathway to residency.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 符合资格可直接申请居留，无需先工作。需满足注册、经验等要求 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa） | 6分制下，注册护士凭学历、经验等可累积分数，达到6分后申请居留 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 如未达居留条件，可通过认证雇主获得临时工签，工作2年后可转居留（属Green List T2路径） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for mental health nurses in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 60,000-72,000; Mid-level: NZD 73,000-92,000; Senior: NZD 94,000-120,000 annually, varying by region and employer.

**问：How can mental health nurses migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：This role is on Tier 1. If you meet Nursing Council registration and have relevant experience, you can apply for a Straight to Residence Visa.

**问：What are the steps to register as a mental health nurse in New Zealand?**
答：Complete a recognized nursing degree, achieve IELTS 7.0 (or equivalent), pass NCNZ capability assessment. Overseas nurses need a temporary practicing certificate.
