# Retail Loss Prevention Officer（Retail Loss Prevention Officer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：442215 – Retail Loss Prevention Officer。**

Retail Loss Prevention Officers monitor theft, fraud, and shrinkage in retail environments, using security measures, investigations, and staff training to protect store assets and reduce losses. This occupation is not on skill shortage lists but can access work via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) and may qualify for Skilled Migrant Category if remuneration thresholds are met.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Retail Loss Prevention Officers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from NZD 42,000 to 50,000 per year, mid-level NZD 55,000-65,000, and senior roles NZD 70,000-90,000. Many positions are part-time, so actual earnings may be lower.

**问：Can Retail Loss Prevention Officers migrate to New Zealand via skilled migration?**
答：It's generally difficult. This occupation is not on the Green List. For SMC 6-point system, the wage must reach 3 times the median (approx. NZD 89/hour), usually only senior management roles qualify. Most can only obtain an AEWV and then gain experience for other visa pathways.

**问：What are the career development paths for a Loss Prevention Officer?**
答：Starting as a junior loss prevention officer, you can advance to supervisor, regional manager, or security director. Transition to consulting or corporate risk management is also possible, with higher salary and migration prospects.
