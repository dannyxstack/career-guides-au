# Scaffolder（Scaffolder）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：821712 – Scaffolder。**

Scaffolders erect and dismantle temporary work platforms for safe access at height, a critical role in New Zealand's construction industry with high migration feasibility due to skill shortage.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for scaffolders?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$50,000-65,000; Intermediate: NZ$65,000-80,000; Advanced: NZ$80,000-100,000. Overtime and remote work can increase pay.

**问：How can scaffolders migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Scaffolders are on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa. Also can apply under Skilled Migrant Category with qualification and English test. Employer sponsorship may be needed.

**问：What certifications are required?**
答：Mandatory: Site Safety card. Recommended: BCITO scaffolding certificate. For immigration: IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.
