# Cartographer（Cartographer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：232213 – Cartographer。**

Cartographers in New Zealand collect and analyze geographic data to create digital or paper maps for urban planning, environmental management, and navigation. This occupation is eligible for Skilled Migrant Category and Green List pathways, subject to qualification and skill requirements.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for cartographers in New Zealand?**
答：Junior: NZD 50,000-65,000; intermediate: NZD 65,000-85,000; senior: NZD 85,000-100,000. Exact depends on experience, location, and certification.

**问：Can cartographers migrate through the Green List?**
答：Yes, cartographers are on Green List Tier 2. Must work for an accredited employer for 2 years, earning at least NZD 29.66 per hour, then apply for residence.

**问：What qualification is needed to work as a cartographer?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in geography, cartography, or GIS. A postgraduate diploma can enhance prospects. No mandatory registration, but NZGIS certification is beneficial.
