# Golfer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 452412 (ANZSCO)**

Professional golfers earn prize money and sponsorship income by participating in professional tournaments, requiring top-tier skills, psychological resilience, and event management abilities. This occupation is not on New Zealand's skilled migration list, but high-income earners (2x median annual salary or more) can apply for an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) and transition to residence through long-term stay.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a golfer in New Zealand?**
A: Salaries vary greatly by tournament results and sponsors. Junior golfers earn NZD 30k–60k/year, intermediate NZD 60k–150k, top players over NZD 150k but income is volatile.

**Q: Can a golfer immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: This occupation is not on the Skilled Migration or Green List, but can work via an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV); if meeting high income (over 2x median annual salary) and long-term residence conditions, may apply for residency.

**Q: How to become a professional golfer in New Zealand?**
A: Typically requires NZ PGA professional qualification, amateur and secondary tour ranking building, then join the Australia-New Zealand tour. Focus on golf skills, psychology, and fitness training.
