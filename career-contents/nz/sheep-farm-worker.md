# Sheep Farm Worker（Sheep Farm Worker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：842317 – Sheep Farm Worker。**

Performs manual tasks on sheep farms such as feeding, shearing, tagging, and fence maintenance, managing flock health and wool production. This role may qualify for AEWV or SMC visa pathways in NZ, subject to wage or skill thresholds.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 适用于有认证雇主担保的工人，需工资达到中位数（约29.66纽币/小时） |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 如达到技术移民薪资门槛（中位数1.5倍），可能符合要求，但本职业通常不被视为技术性 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 本职业不在绿色清单中，除非有特殊技能或高薪 |
| Subclass Working Holiday（Working Holiday Visa） | 短期打工度假签证常见于季节性农场工作 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the typical salary for a sheep farm worker?**
答：Entry-level hourly rate is about NZD 20-25, annual salary NZD 42,000-52,000; intermediate NZD 52,000-62,000; senior NZD 62,000-75,000, depending on experience and skills.

**问：Can a sheep farm worker migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Generally not directly via Skilled Migrant Category (not on Green List or high-skilled). However, you can work on an AEWV, then later apply for residence if you meet higher wage thresholds or have employer sponsorship.

**问：What is the career progression for a sheep farm worker?**
答：Start as a general worker, progress to senior shepherd, farm supervisor, or manager. With training, you can become a shearer, vet assistant, or agricultural advisor.
