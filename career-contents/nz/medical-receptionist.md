# Medical Receptionist（Medical Receptionist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：542114 – Medical Receptionist。**

Medical Receptionists in New Zealand handle patient bookings, registrations, billing, and electronic medical records (e.g., Best Practice, MediTech) in clinics, hospitals, and specialist centers. They need familiarity with the NZ public health system (e.g., PHO/ACC). Due to lower skill levels, this role typically does not qualify for skilled migration, but can work via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) and later explore other pathways.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 适合持有认证雇主工作邀请的申请人，需满足工资要求（时薪至少为中位数29.66纽元，2024年标准）。该职业通常符合AEWV资格。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 医疗前台通常不属于技能职业，但若雇主愿意支持且工资达到中位数两倍（约年薪6.1万+），可能符合较高技能等级要求。但实际可能性极低。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a Medical Receptionist in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level hourly rate NZ$20-23, annual NZ$42,000-48,000; intermediate NZ$23-26, annual NZ$48,000-55,000; senior or supervisor NZ$26-31, annual NZ$55,000-65,000. Below the median wage with limited growth.

**问：Can Medical Receptionist apply for Skilled Migrant Category residence in New Zealand?**
答：Generally no. The role is ANZSCO skill level 4 or 5, requiring wages three times the median ($90k+) to qualify. Realistic path is via AEWV then trying to switch categories with employer support, but very difficult.

**问：What are the career prospects for Medical Receptionist?**
答：Can advance to reception supervisor, medical office manager, or health information manager via further study (e.g., Diploma in Medical Administration). Lateral moves to specialist clinics or hospital admin possible, but slow progression.
