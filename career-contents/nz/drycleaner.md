# Drycleaner（Drycleaner）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：811512 – Drycleaner。**

Drycleaners operate dry cleaning and washing machines in dry-cleaning shops or laundry plants, cleaning, stain-removing, ironing, and finishing garments and textiles. In New Zealand, this occupation is accessible via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but is not on the Green List or shortage list; Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) requires higher wages or points.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在新西兰独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签：需获得认证雇主的工作offer，薪资不低于中位数（29.66纽元/小时，2024年标准）或行业协议工资。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民：需符合ANZSCO技能等级（Level 4），薪资达到中位数1.5倍（约44.49纽元/小时）才能申请技术移民，且需满足6分制积分要求（例如学历+工作经验）。 |
| Subclass Working Holiday（Working Holiday Visa） | 打工度假签证：允许从事该职业，但通常为短期。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Drycleaner？**
- 注重细节、耐心细致者
- 动手能力强、不怕劳作和化学品者
- 寻求低门槛快速就业者

**谁不适合学Drycleaner？**
- 追求高薪或快速移民者
- 对化学气味敏感或不愿体力劳动的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for drycleaners in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level salary is around NZD 38,000-45,000 per year; intermediate NZD 45,000-52,000; senior/supervisor up to NZD 52,000-60,000. Salaries are low and do not meet the 1.5x median wage requirement for Skilled Migrant Category (about NZD 44.49/hour).

**问：Can a drycleaner migrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is not on the Green List or shortage list. Skilled Migrant Category requires wages at 1.5x median and 6-point system, which is difficult. Main pathway is Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but residence transition is challenging.

**问：Which cities have more job opportunities for drycleaners?**
答：Major cities like Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, and tourist towns have more positions in dry-cleaning shops and hotels. However, overall positions are limited.
