# Warehouse Supervisor（Warehouse Supervisor）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：741111 – Warehouse Supervisor。**

Warehouse Supervisors in New Zealand have stable demand in logistics; experienced individuals can apply for immigration via Skilled Migrant Category or Green List Tier 2, driven by e-commerce and export growth.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主担保，工作3年后可申请居留 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 6分制技术移民，需有相关工作经验或学历 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 工作2年后申请居留，需满足薪资要求（约中位数） |
| Subclass Partnership（Partner of a New Zealander） | 如配偶为新西兰公民或居民，可用伴侣工签 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Warehouse Supervisor？**
- 有物流或仓储经验，寻求稳定管理岗位者
- 具备基本的数字和团队管理技能
- 愿意在非办公环境（仓库）工作，能接受体力劳动

**谁不适合学Warehouse Supervisor？**
- 不喜欢重复性工作和体力劳动
- 无法适应仓库的噪音和温度变化

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for a Warehouse Supervisor in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 45,000-55,000, mid-level 55,000-70,000, senior 70,000-90,000, depending on experience and employer.

**问：Can a Warehouse Supervisor apply for Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Yes. Although ANZSCO skill level 3, you can accumulate points via experience, qualifications, or salary (e.g., above median) for SMC; also eligible for Green List Tier 2 Work to Residence.

**问：Is Warehouse Supervisor on the shortage list in New Zealand?**
答：Not on the official shortage list, but demand is stable, especially in Auckland and Christchurch.
