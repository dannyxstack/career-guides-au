# Retail Manager (General)（Retail Manager (General)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：142111 – Retail Manager (General)。**

Retail managers oversee daily store operations, inventory, staff scheduling, and sales targets, requiring leadership and customer service skills. In New Zealand, this occupation is eligible for Skilled Migrant Category or Green List Tier 2 work-to-residence pathway with employer sponsorship.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa） | 技术移民类别，需满足6分制（学历+工作经验+薪资），职业在ANZSCO Skill Level 1-3可申请 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 Work to Residence） | 绿色清单Tier 2，从事零售经理（ANZSCO 142111）工作满2年，薪资达中位数（约NZD29.66/小时），可申请居留 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，需雇主获认证，提供合同和薪资达标，最长3年，可转居留 |
| Subclass Partnership（Partner of a New Zealander Resident Visa） | 配偶担保移民，若配偶为新西兰公民或居民，可申请居留 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for retail managers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 48,000-58,000/year; Mid-level: NZD 58,000-75,000/year; Senior: NZD 75,000-95,000/year. Large chains or regional managers can earn NZD 100,000+.

**问：How can a retail manager immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Main pathways: 1) Work on AEWV for 2 years then apply for Green List Tier 2 residence with median wage; 2) Apply directly for SMC with 6-point system (qualifications + experience + salary), occupation at ANZSCO Skill Level 2.

**问：What training is required to become a retail manager?**
答：Typically a business or management degree, e.g., Bachelor of Commerce or Diploma in Retail Management. Short courses like First Aid help. On-the-job training is common, with large employers offering internal promotion programs.
