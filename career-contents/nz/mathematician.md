# Mathematician（Mathematician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：224112 – Mathematician。**

Mathematicians in New Zealand apply advanced mathematics and statistics to model and optimize solutions in research, business, and engineering. Listed on the Green List Tier 1, this role offers strong migration prospects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 满足年龄、学历和薪资要求（中位数工资以上），可直接申请居留，无需工作担保。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 6分制技术移民，需满足学历、工作经验和薪资积分要求，通常需工作担保。 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，作为临时工作签证，可为后续居留积累资格。 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for mathematicians in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level mathematicians earn NZD 60,000-80,000, mid-level NZD 85,000-110,000, and senior NZD 120,000-160,000 per year, depending on industry and location.

**问：What are the fast-track immigration options for mathematicians in New Zealand?**
答：Mathematicians are on the Green List Tier 1 and can apply for the Straight to Residence Visa, requiring a relevant qualification and a salary at or above the median wage.

**问：Which industries have high demand for mathematicians in New Zealand?**
答：Research institutions, financial services, IT companies, and government agencies have significant demand, especially in data analytics and operations research.
