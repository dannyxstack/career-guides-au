# Ship's Officer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 231214 (ANZSCO)**

Ship pilots are responsible for driving and operating vessels, ensuring navigational safety, and must hold maritime qualifications in New Zealand. With Green List and shortage skill status, this occupation has a clear immigration pathway, suitable for experienced seafarers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Promotion from Third Officer to Chief Officer or Captain requires accumulating sea service time and passing exams. Some shift to port management or maritime training, with income rising significantly at higher ranks.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine voyage data recording and report generation
- Basic meteorological route planning and optimization
- Marine machinery condition monitoring and early warning
- Cargo loading calculation and balance adjustment
- Communication relay and standard message forwarding

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted navigation decisions to improve flight safety and fuel efficiency
- Real-time weather and sea condition forecasting, supporting dynamic adjustment of speed and route
- Automated fault diagnosis and predictive maintenance to reduce downtime
- Intelligent Cargo Monitoring Systems to Improve Stowage Accuracy and Safety
- Augmented reality (AR) assisted navigation at night and in adverse weather

**Human moat:**

- Ship handling and emergency response capabilities in crisis situations
- Team management and crew leadership (especially captain duties)
- Special cargo handling and complex port operations experience
- Regulatory compliance and maritime safety inspection responsibilities
- Flexible judgment in unpredictable marine environments

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI navigation and decision support systems
- Data analysis and interpretation of predictive models
- Remote operation and automated system monitoring
- Cybersecurity basics (ship system protection)
- STCW certification renewal (including E-navigation module)
- Cross-cultural communication and team management

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 1
- Skilled Migrant Category
- Coastal Shipping
- Maritime Industry

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a ship pilot in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level approx. NZD 55,000–75,000; mid-level NZD 80,000–110,000; senior (captain) up to NZD 120,000–180,000. Varies by vessel type and route; larger offshore vessels pay more.

**Q: How can a ship pilot immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: This occupation is on the Green List Tier 1; meeting the median salary (about NZD 29.66/hour) qualifies for a direct residence visa; alternatively, SMC skilled migration or AEWV work visa transition are options.

**Q: Does New Zealand recognize Chinese ship driving licenses?**
A: Chinese certificates need to be assessed by Maritime New Zealand, may need supplementary STCW subjects or conversion training, and must meet English requirements such as IELTS 6.0.
