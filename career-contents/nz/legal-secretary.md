# Legal Secretary（Legal Secretary）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：599411 – Legal Secretary。**

Legal secretaries and paralegals assist lawyers with documents, case management, and court preparation. The legal market in New Zealand is stable; entry is possible via Skilled Migrant Category or AEWV, but not on the Green List, offering moderate migration feasibility.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a legal secretary in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level NZD 55,000-70,000; senior NZD 70,000-90,000 per year, lower than Australia but with lower cost of living.

**问：Can a legal secretary migrate to New Zealand under the Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Yes, but requires meeting the 6-point system (qualifications/work experience) and a salary at the median (approx. NZD 29.66/hour). The occupation is not on the Green List, but AEWV is possible with employer sponsorship.

**问：What are the career prospects for a legal secretary?**
答：Promotion to senior secretary or transition to training/compliance roles. Some pursue LLB to become lawyers, requiring three more years of study.
