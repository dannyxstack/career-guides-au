# Diesel Motor Mechanic（Diesel Motor Mechanic）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：321212 – Diesel Motor Mechanic。**

Diesel Motor Mechanics are in high demand in New Zealand, diagnosing, repairing, and maintaining heavy diesel vehicles and equipment. Listed on Green List Tier 1, it offers a direct residence pathway, making it a top choice for skilled migration.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Diesel Motor Mechanic in New Zealand?**
答：The median salary is around NZD 70,000, with entry-level around NZD 50,000 and experienced up to NZD 90,000. Overtime and bonuses can add 10-20%.

**问：How can I immigrate to New Zealand as a Diesel Motor Mechanic?**
答：If you already have a NZ qualification (e.g., NZ Certificate), you can apply for Green List Tier 1 Direct Residence. Otherwise, you can first get an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), work for 2 years, then apply for Tier 2 Work to Residence. Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) is also possible under the 6-point system.

**问：What is the career progression for this job in New Zealand?**
答：Start as an apprentice or junior mechanic, complete a 4-year national certificate to become a senior technician. Then advance to supervisor, diagnostic specialist, or trainer, or move into sales or parts management. Many also run their own workshop.
