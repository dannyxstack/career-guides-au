# Communications Operator（Communications Operator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：342312 – Communications Operator。**

Communications Operators handle emergency calls, dispatch resources, and ensure rapid response in police, fire, and ambulance services. Migration via AEWV or SMC is possible, subject to wage thresholds and employer accreditation.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需认证雇主雇佣且工资达中位数(NZD29.66/小时)，可续签 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 需6分制且为ANZSCO Level 3职业，工资达中位数或更高 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 部分通信角色如紧急服务调度可能符合，工作2年后可申请居留 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Communications Operators?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 50,000-62,000; mid-level: NZD 62,000-75,000; senior: NZD 75,000-90,000. Varies by employer, experience, and region.

**问：How feasible is it to migrate as a Communications Operator?**
答：Possible via AEWV or SMC with employer sponsorship and meeting wage thresholds. Green List Tier 2 applies to niche roles; overall medium difficulty.

**问：How to become a Communications Operator?**
答：Typically need high school diploma, employer-provided training, and possibly a certificate in emergency communication. Strong English and stress tolerance required.
