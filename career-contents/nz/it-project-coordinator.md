# IT Project Coordinator（IT Project Coordinator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：224211 – IT Project Coordinator。**

IT Project Coordinators coordinate IT project progress, resources, and communication, serving as key liaisons between project teams and departments. In New Zealand, this role is generally eligible for immigration via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), but is not on the Green List for direct residence.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for IT Project Coordinators in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-70,000; Mid-level: NZD 75,000-95,000; Senior: NZD 100,000-130,000. Salary varies with experience, certifications, and location; Auckland often pays more.

**问：Can IT Project Coordinators immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. This role is eligible for skilled migration via SMC (6-point system) or AEWV. It is recommended to have at least 3 years of work experience and meet English language requirements.

**问：What are the career prospects for IT Project Coordinators?**
答：They can advance to IT Project Manager or PMO Manager. With experience and certifications like PMP, salary and position have significant growth potential.
