# Tax Accountant（Tax Accountant）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：221113 – Tax Accountant。**

Tax advisors provide tax planning, filing and compliance services for individuals and businesses, specializing in IRD regulations, GST, corporate and international tax. This occupation is on New Zealand's Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence without a job offer. High demand and strong migration pathway.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for tax advisors in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-75,000; mid-level: NZD 75,000-100,000; senior: NZD 100,000-140,000. Holding CA certification can increase salary.

**问：How can tax advisors migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Tax advisors are on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa (no job offer required). Also can apply via SMC (6-point system) or use AEWV as a stepping stone.

**问：Do tax advisors need New Zealand local qualifications?**
答：Not mandatory, but holding CA (NZICA) or CPA/ACCA greatly improves competitiveness. Many employers prefer certified candidates.
