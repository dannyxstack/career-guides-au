# Respiratory Scientist（Respiratory Scientist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：234612 – Respiratory Scientist。**

Respiratory Scientists diagnose and treat respiratory diseases using lung function tests and sleep studies. In New Zealand, this occupation is on Green List Tier 1, offering direct residency pathway, highly feasible for immigration.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for respiratory scientists in NZ?**
答：Entry-level $65k-$80k, mid-level $80k-$100k, senior up to $130k+, depending on experience and certification.

**问：Is it easy for respiratory scientists to immigrate to NZ?**
答：Yes, Green List Tier 1 allows direct residence. Requires NZQA qualifications assessment and ANZSRC registration.

**问：What education is needed to become a registered respiratory scientist in NZ?**
答：A bachelor's in health science (3 years) plus a postgraduate diploma in respiratory science (1 year), or equivalent overseas qualification.
