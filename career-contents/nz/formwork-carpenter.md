# Formwork Carpenter（Formwork Carpenter）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：331212 – Formwork Carpenter。**

Formwork carpenters erect and dismantle temporary formwork for concrete pouring, a critical trade in construction. Due to skill shortages in New Zealand, this role is on the Green List, offering a fast-track to residence.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a formwork carpenter in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 45,000–60,000; experienced: NZD 60,000–80,000; senior/foreman: NZD 80,000–105,000. Green List roles often pay above median (NZD 65,000).

**问：How can a formwork carpenter immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：This occupation is on Tier 1 (Straight to Residence), allowing direct residence application without a job offer. Alternatively, work 2 years on an AEWV and then apply for residence.

**问：What training or qualifications are required to become a formwork carpenter?**
答：Typically complete an apprenticeship (3–4 years) for the New Zealand Certificate in Carpentry, or a short certificate course. Site Safe card and driver's license are also required.
