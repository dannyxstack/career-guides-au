# Procurement Manager（Procurement Manager）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：133612 – Procurement Manager。**

Procurement Managers develop strategies, evaluate suppliers, negotiate contracts, and manage categories to secure goods/services at best cost. Stable demand in NZ government and private sectors. Eligible for Skilled Migrant Category and Green List.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Procurement Managers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level NZ$65k-85k, mid-level NZ$85k-120k, senior NZ$120k-170k. CIPS certification or extensive experience can lead to higher pay.

**问：Can Procurement Managers migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Yes. Procurement Manager is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa.

**问：What certifications are needed to become a Procurement Manager in New Zealand?**
答：Not mandatory, but CIPS certification is highly recommended. A relevant bachelor's degree and experience are common.
