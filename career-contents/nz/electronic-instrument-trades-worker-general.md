# Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General)（Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：342314 – Electronic Instrument Trades Worker (General)。**

Install, test, maintain and repair electronic instruments and control systems for industrial automation, medical equipment, and communications. This occupation is on New Zealand's Green List, offering direct residence or work-to-residence pathways.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for electronic instrument trades workers?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-70,000; mid-level: NZD 70,000-90,000; senior: NZD 90,000-120,000. Holding an EST license boosts earnings.

**问：How to migrate under the Green List for this occupation?**
答：This role is on Green List Tier 1. With a salary at median wage ($29.66/hr) and relevant qualifications, you can apply for Straight to Residence; or under Tier 2, work for 2 years then apply.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically a NZQF Level 4 certificate in electronic engineering (2 years) or higher. Overseas qualifications must be assessed by NZQA, possibly requiring bridging courses.
