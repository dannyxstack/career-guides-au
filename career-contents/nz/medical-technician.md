# Medical Technician（Medical Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311299 – Medical Technician。**

Medical technicians assist with diagnostic tests and equipment operation in labs, hospitals, and clinics, handling specimen processing, instrument calibration, and quality control. This role is generally not eligible for direct Skilled Migrant Category, but can work via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) and potentially transition later.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 适用于获得认证雇主offer的海外申请者，非直接移民路径，可累积工作经历 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 理论上如满足ANZSCO技能等级和薪资中位数，可申请，但实际因职业不在优先列表，EOI筛选率低 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Medical Technician？**
- 具备生物或医学背景，愿意从基层做起的人
- 希望在新西兰医疗行业稳定就业，不急于移民的人
- 能接受实验室重复性工作，注重细节的人

**谁不适合学Medical Technician？**
- 追求高薪或快速移民路径者
- 不习惯轮班或长时间站立工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for medical technicians in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level: NZD 55,000-70,000; senior: NZD 70,000-85,000. Below the national median but stable.

**问：Can medical technicians get New Zealand residency through skilled migration?**
答：Direct Skilled Migrant Category is difficult as the occupation is not on the Green List or shortage lists. Common route is AEWV then SMC after meeting wage and experience thresholds.
