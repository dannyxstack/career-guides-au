# Aircraft Maintenance Engineer（Aircraft Maintenance Engineer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：321111 – Aircraft Maintenance Engineer。**

Aircraft Maintenance Engineers inspect, repair, and maintain aircraft mechanical and electronic systems to ensure flight safety. This occupation is on New Zealand's Green List Tier 1, offering direct residence pathway with excellent immigration prospects.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Aircraft Maintenance Engineer？**
- 喜欢动手维修，对航空机械和电子系统有浓厚兴趣
- 注重安全规范，做事细致、条理清晰
- 愿意接受持续培训和考证

**谁不适合学Aircraft Maintenance Engineer？**
- 对高空工作恐惧或适应不了轮班制
- 不善于枯燥的检查流程和繁琐的文书工作

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer in NZ?**
答：Entry-level NZD 50k-65k, mid-level NZD 65k-85k, senior NZD 85k-110k; some senior or supervisor roles may exceed NZD 120k.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate to NZ as an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence if meeting median wage, with low immigration difficulty.

**问：What qualifications are required for Aircraft Maintenance Engineers?**
答：You need a CAA Aircraft Maintenance Engineer License, usually after completing accredited training (e.g., diploma) and gaining practical experience.
