# Home Care Package Coordinator（Home Care Package Coordinator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：134215 – Home Care Package Coordinator。**

Home Care Package Coordinators manage government-funded home care packages for the elderly, assessing needs, arranging services, and managing budgets. This occupation is eligible for AEWV employment in New Zealand but is not on the Green List or skilled migration occupation lists, limiting immigration pathways.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在新西兰独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 通过认证雇主获得工作签证，适合有雇主担保者，但需满足工资中位数要求（目前29.66纽元/小时），该职业初级工资可能低于中位数。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 该职业不在技能职业列表中，通常无法直接申请SMC，除非有高薪或特殊技能。 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 该职业不在绿色清单中，无法通过此路径移民。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★½）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for a Home Care Package Coordinator in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level salary is around NZD 45,000-55,000 per year, mid-level NZD 55,000-65,000, and senior up to NZD 75,000. Wages may be below the AEWV median requirement (NZD 29.66/hour), but some employers pay higher.

**问：Can this occupation lead to skilled migration in New Zealand?**
答：No. Home Care Package Coordinator is not on New Zealand's skill shortage list or Green List, so direct skilled migration is not possible. Typically, only AEWV employment is available, requiring median wage compliance, which entry-level roles often do not meet.

**问：What are the job prospects in New Zealand?**
答：Job prospects are stable due to an aging population driving demand for community care. However, salary growth is limited, and promotion to management can boost income. Note that migration policies are unfriendly, and positions are mostly held by residents.
