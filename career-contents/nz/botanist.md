# Botanist（Botanist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：234515 – Botanist。**

Botanists study plant classification, distribution, physiology and ecology, supporting agriculture, conservation and biotechnology. This occupation is on Green List Tier 1 in New Zealand, enabling a direct residence pathway with strong job prospects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：很高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 直接居留签证，植物学家在绿色清单Tier 1，满足薪资中位数（时薪29.66纽元）即可申请，无需工作两年 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民6分制，需满足学历、工作经验及薪资要求，植物学家通常可获高学历加分 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，如未直接满足绿色清单，可先持此签证工作，后续申请居民签证 |

**PR难度：较低（★★☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Botanist？**
- 热爱植物学，有科研热情和扎实的生物学基础
- 适应户外工作，愿意在田间或偏远地区收集样本
- 有志于为农业可持续发展或生态保护做出贡献

**谁不适合学Botanist？**
- 不喜欢重复性实验或数据收集工作
- 无法接受相对较低的初级薪资（低于新西兰中位数）

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a botanist in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level botanists earn NZ$45,000-55,000, mid-level NZ$60,000-75,000, and senior NZ$80,000-100,000 per year. Salaries are lower than IT but cost of living is moderate.

**问：How can a botanist immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Botanist is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa if earning at least NZ$29.66/hour and holding a bachelor's degree. Also can apply through Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) with points for qualification and experience.

**问：What are the job prospects for botanists in New Zealand?**
答：Job prospects are good, particularly in agritech, environmental consulting and biosecurity. Government agencies like DOC and CRIs frequently hire, though competition exists.
