# Product Grader（Product Grader）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：839312 – Product Grader。**

Product Graders in New Zealand perform visual and mechanical inspection of raw materials or finished products in mining, agriculture, and manufacturing, grading by size, quality, purity, etc., to ensure products meet export or processing standards. This occupation is generally eligible for work visas (AEWV) and skilled migration (SMC).

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需获得认证雇主的工作邀请，职位需在ANZSCO技能等级1-3级。产品分级员通常为技能等级4-5级，但若雇主证明技能短缺，仍可申请。 |

| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2） | 产品分级员不在绿色清单直接居留名单中，但若从事乳制品加工等特定行业并满足条件，可走工作转居留。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Product Grader in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level workers earn around NZD 45,000-55,000 per year, while experienced graders can earn NZD 68,000-80,000. Salaries vary by industry, region, and experience.

**问：Is it easy for Product Graders to immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is not on the Green List, but you can gain experience via an AEWV and later apply under the Skilled Migrant Category, provided you meet the wage threshold (currently ~NZD 29.66/hour) and qualification requirements.

**问：What is the job outlook for Product Graders in New Zealand?**
答：Outlook is stable, with demand driven by primary industry exports (dairy, meat, fruit). Automation may affect some roles, but skilled graders remain needed.
