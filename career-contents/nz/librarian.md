## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for librarians in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level $50,000–$60,000 NZD, mid-level $65,000–$80,000, senior $85,000–$100,000; public libraries pay slightly less than academic/special libraries.

**问：Can librarians immigrate through the Green List?**
答：Librarians are on Green List Tier 2, requiring 2 years of work before residency, or can apply directly via SMC 6-point system.

**问：Is registration required to work as a librarian in New Zealand?**
答：No mandatory registration, but LIANZA professional registration enhances competitiveness; some employers require it.
