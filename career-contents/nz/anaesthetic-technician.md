# Anaesthetic Technician（Anaesthetic Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311211 – Anaesthetic Technician。**

Anaesthetic Technicians assist anaesthetists in preparing and maintaining anaesthetic equipment, monitoring vital signs, and ensuring safe anaesthesia delivery. This role is on Green List Tier 1, offering a direct residency pathway.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an Anaesthetic Technician in New Zealand?**
答：As of 2024, the median annual salary is around NZD 70,000, with entry-level at NZD 55,000 and senior roles exceeding NZD 100,000.

**问：How can an Anaesthetic Technician migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Anaesthetic Technician is on Green List Tier 1. With a job offer from an accredited employer, you can apply for the Straight to Residence Visa directly.

**问：How can overseas Anaesthetic Technicians register in New Zealand?**
答：Apply to MSCNZ for qualification assessment, possibly requiring additional training or exams. Typically need IELTS 6.5 and completion of a recognized New Zealand programme.
