# Anaesthetic Technician — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 311211 (ANZSCO)**

Anaesthesia technicians assist anaesthetists in preparing and maintaining anaesthesia equipment, monitoring patient vitals, and ensuring safe anaesthesia delivery. This occupation is on New Zealand's Green List Tier 1, eligible for a direct residence pathway.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior anaesthesia technicians can advance to senior technician, team leader or trainer; some may transition to anaesthesia assistant or medical equipment management. With experience, opportunities exist in management or academia.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically reads and records patient vital signs data (e.g., heart rate, blood pressure)
- Generate standard anaesthesia records and surgical reports
- automated equipment self-check and fault alarm
- Basic gas supply and medication pump management

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI alert system assists in prioritizing abnormal patient conditions
- Real-time dose recommendations support precise anesthesia control
- AI cameras in operating rooms monitor patient positioning and tube placement
- Historical data predicts postoperative complication risk

**Human moat:**

- Immediate response and decision-making in emergencies (e.g., airway spasm, major bleeding)
- Non-verbal coordination with anesthesiologists and surgeons
- Non-standard rapid troubleshooting for equipment failures
- Experience-based judgment of patient individual differences (e.g., elderly, children, obesity).

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and understanding of AI-assisted decision systems
- Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) and other emergency certifications
- In-depth interpretation of multi-parameter monitors
- Use of telemedicine collaboration tools
- Data analysis and basic statistics (for quality control)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 1
- Skilled Migrant Category
- Healthcare Shortage
- Population Aging

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for anaesthetic technicians in New Zealand?**
A: According to 2024 data, median annual salary for anaesthetic technicians in New Zealand is approx NZ$70,000, junior approx NZ$55,000, senior up to NZ$100,000+.

**Q: How can anaesthetic technicians immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
A: Anaesthetic Technicians are on Green List Tier 1; with a job offer from an accredited employer, they can directly apply for a Straight to Residence Visa without working for 2 years first.

**Q: How can an overseas anaesthetic technician register in New Zealand?**
A: Must apply to MSCNZ for a qualification assessment; may need to take additional courses or exams. Usually requires IELTS 6.5 and completion of NZ-recognized training.
