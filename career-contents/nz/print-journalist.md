# Print Journalist — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 212413 (ANZSCO)**

Print journalists write news and features for newspapers, magazines and other print media, requiring interviews, research, and fact-checking. Migration opportunities are limited; focus on industry transformation and digital media skills.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Junior reporters can advance to senior reporters, editors, or feature editors, with a few entering media management. Cross-skills transitions (e.g., digital content, public relations) are common career paths.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate standard news briefs (e.g., financial reports, sports scores)
- Speech-to-text and automatic subtitle generation
- Fact-checking and data reconciliation (e.g., verifying statistical data)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted data analysis and visualization (quickly identify trends)
- AI-recommended topics and audience preference prediction
- AI compiles multilingual information summaries

**Human moat:**

- Exclusive interviews and in-depth investigations (interpersonal insight and trust)
- Ethical judgment and sensitive issue gatekeeping
- Creative storytelling (features, commentary)

**Skills to build:**

- Data journalism (Python/R/data visualization)
- Multimedia production (video/podcast editing)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT-assisted writing)
- Fact-checking and information verification
- Audience Analysis and Digital Marketing

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining
- Digital Transition
- Content Marketing
- Low Demand

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Passionate about writing and investigation, able to handle tight deadlines.
- Possess digital media skills such as SEO, data analysis
- Willing to accept part-time or freelance opportunities

**Who is not suited**
- Those seeking stable, high salaries
- Those who reject new media transformation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for print journalists?**
A: Junior reporter annual salary approximately NZ$45,000-55,000, senior or editor up to NZ$75,000-100,000, overall lower than industries like IT.

**Q: Can print journalists obtain New Zealand residency through skilled migration?**
A: Generally not possible. This occupation is not on the Green List and the market is saturated. If you have a certified employer offer and meet the salary requirements, you could try SMC, but it would be difficult.

**Q: What is the future outlook for print journalists?**
A: Traditional print media roles continue to decline; transitioning to digital content, PR, or freelance writing is the trend. Those with multimedia skills are more competitive.
