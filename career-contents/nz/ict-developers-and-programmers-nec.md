**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence） | 直接居留签证，满足薪资中位数（约29.66 NZD/小时）且从事DevOps工程师工作，可立即申请。 |

| Subclass Green List T2（Work to Residence） | 工作转居留，从事DevOps工程师工作满2年并满足薪资中位数即可申请，但T1更直接。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What are the immigration advantages for DevOps engineers in New Zealand?**
答：DevOps engineers are on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence visa without needing to work for two years first. As long as the salary meets the median wage (approx. 29.66 NZD/hour) and you have a job offer from an accredited employer, you can apply directly.

**问：What is the salary range for DevOps engineers in New Zealand?**
答：Junior: NZD 70,000-95,000; Mid: NZD 95,000-130,000; Senior: NZD 130,000-170,000+. Salaries vary by experience, certifications, and location, with Auckland and Wellington typically higher.
