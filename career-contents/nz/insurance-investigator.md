# Insurance Investigator（Insurance Investigator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：599611 – Insurance Investigator。**

Insurance Investigators in New Zealand probe suspicious claims, collect evidence, interview witnesses, and write reports to combat fraud. This occupation is eligible for the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) and Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but not on the Green List, requiring competitive salary.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for an Insurance Investigator in New Zealand?**
答：The median salary is around NZD 75,000, with entry-level earning NZD 55,000-70,000 and senior roles up to NZD 130,000. Salary depends on experience and certifications.

**问：Can an Insurance Investigator immigrate through the Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Yes. This occupation is at ANZSCO skill level 2-3, must meet the SMC 6-point system, and salary must be at or above median wage.

**问：Is a license required to work as an Insurance Investigator in New Zealand?**
答：Yes. A Private Investigator License from the Ministry of Justice is mandatory for paid work, involving training, background check, and exam.
