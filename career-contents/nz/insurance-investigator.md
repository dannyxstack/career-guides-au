# Insurance Investigator — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 599611 (ANZSCO)**

Insurance investigators in New Zealand investigate suspicious claims, gather evidence, interview witnesses, and write reports to combat fraud. This occupation can lead to migration under the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but is not on the Green List and must meet salary requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior investigators can advance to senior investigator, team leader, or anti-fraud manager. With experience, they can move into forensic accounting or risk management consulting. Some investigators obtain certification from the New Zealand Insurance and Financial Services Association to enhance their value.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Preliminary document review and fraud detection (AI automatically scans for abnormal patterns)
- Routine report writing and template filling (AI generates first draft)
- Database search and background checks (AI rapid retrieval)
- Schedule management and task reminders (AI-automated management)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- In-depth analysis of complex claims patterns (AI provides data insights)
- Interview strategy optimisation (AI real-time prompts and risk scoring)
- Evidence relevance modeling (AI visual relationship network)
- Fraud report quality control (AI proofreading and compliance checks)

**Human moat:**

- Face-to-face interviews and body language interpretation
- Ethical judgment and decision-making in complex fraud cases
- Cross-agency coordination and courtroom testimony
- Creative reasoning from unstructured evidence.

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and fraud detection tools (e.g., SAS, Python)
- AI-assisted investigation platform operation (e.g., Palantir)
- Psychology and negotiation skills
- Legal and compliance knowledge updates
- Data visualization (Tableau/Power BI)
- Cybersecurity Basics (Responding to Digital Evidence)

**Key growth areas:**

- Skilled Migrant Category
- Accredited Employer Work Visa
- Fraud Prevention
- Digital Forensics

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of an insurance investigator in New Zealand?**
A: Median salary is about NZD 75,000, entry-level NZD 55,000-70,000, senior up to NZD 130,000. Salary depends on experience and certifications.

**Q: Can an insurance investigator immigrate to New Zealand through the skilled migration category?**
A: Yes. This occupation is at ANZSCO skill level 2-3, meets SMC 6-point requirements, and the salary meets or exceeds the median.

**Q: Do insurance investigators need a license in New Zealand?**
A: Yes. Conducting paid investigations requires a private investigator license issued by the Ministry of Justice, requiring training, background checks, and exams.
