# Dental Therapist — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 411214 (ANZSCO)**

Dental therapists focus on children and adolescent oral health, performing preventive treatments, fillings, and extractions. In New Zealand, this occupation is usually employed by District Health Boards (DHBs) or school oral health services. Due to high demand, it is on the Green List Tier 2, allowing eligible applicants to apply for work-to-residence.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Dental therapists can advance to senior therapist, clinical tutor, or oral health coordinator. Some become dentists or oral health therapists through further study. New Zealand offers progressive registration and continuing education support.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Caries detection and basic X-ray interpretation
- Basic Treatment Plan Generation (e.g., filling plans)
- Patient Appointment Scheduling and Medical Record Organization
- Drug dosage calculation and side effect alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted image analysis improves diagnostic accuracy
- Digital treatment planning to improve filling/restoration outcomes
- Smart patient management system optimizes appointments and follow-ups
- Virtual assistant provides personalized oral health education

**Human moat:**

- Clinical operation skills (e.g., fillings, sealants)
- Ability to build trust and communicate with patients
- Clinical judgment for handling complex cases
- Fine hand movements and hand-eye coordination
- Techniques for soothing children and anxious patients

**Skills to build:**

- Master AI-assisted diagnostic software (e.g., DEXIS, Pearl)
- Digital impressions and CAD/CAM operation
- Data analysis to interpret oral health trends in patient populations
- Remote dental consultation and collaboration skills
- Interdisciplinary collaboration (with dentists, hygienists)
- Lifelong learning and adaptability to new technologies

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much do dental therapists earn in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level about NZD 65,000-75,000, mid-level 75,000-90,000, senior 90,000-110,000. Public system offers stable pay with annual increments and remote area allowances. Private is less common but some have higher caps.

**Q: Can dental therapists immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
A: Yes. This occupation is on the Green List Tier 2. After working for 24 months and meeting the salary requirement (median $29.66/hour), you can apply for work-to-residence visa. It also qualifies under the SMC 6-point system.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a dental therapist in New Zealand?**
A: Typically requires the University of Otago's Bachelor of Oral Health (BOH) 3-year program, or a related postgraduate diploma. International students need IELTS 7.0 (Academic) and registration with the Dental Council.
