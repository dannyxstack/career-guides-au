# Renderer (Solid Plaster) — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 333212 (ANZSCO)**

In New Zealand, solid plasterers apply cement, lime, or gypsum-based materials to plaster and finish interior and exterior walls and ceilings of buildings, making them a key skilled trade in the construction industry. Benefiting from ongoing residential and commercial building demand, this occupation offers migration opportunities under the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) and Green List, requiring relevant qualification certification.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior plasterers can work on basic construction, advance to senior plasterer or foreman after gaining experience, responsible for team management and complex projects. Further development to construction project manager or self-employed contractor, requiring a New Zealand Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP) license to undertake higher-value projects.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual measuring and setting out: AI vision + laser automatic surveying
- Mortar mix ratio calculation: AI automatically optimizes based on weather/substrate.
- Repetitive large-area plastering: spray robot completes initial work

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses to overlay design drawings for precise plaster thickness positioning
- AI defect detection: real-time scanning of walls to indicate hollow/delamination
- Smart scheduling: AI automatically adjusts entry order based on weather/progress.

**Human moat:**

- Handcrafting and Restoration of Complex Finish Textures
- Judgment of on-site emergencies (substrate defects, structural deviations)
- Multi-trade coordination and ultimate responsibility for construction quality

**Skills to build:**

- Reading and Collaborating on BIM Models
- Operation and programming of spray painting robots.
- Use of AR measurement/alignment tools
- Basic data analysis (field logs and quality traceability)
- Knowledge of sustainable materials (low-carbon plastering products)
- Digital communication tools (e.g., Fieldwire reporting)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 1
- Skilled Migrant Category
- Construction Skills Shortage
- Residential Building

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary for a solid plasterer in New Zealand?**
A: Depending on experience level, entry-level around NZ$45,000-55,000, mid-level NZ$55,000-75,000, senior NZ$75,000-95,000. Foremen or self-employed can exceed NZ$100,000. Salaries vary by region, employer, and project.

**Q: Can solid plasterers apply for residence directly through the Green List?**
A: Yes, this occupation is on the Green List Tier 1. If conditions are met (e.g., holding a relevant New Zealand national certificate, having an accredited employer, and salary reaching the median), you can directly apply for the Straight to Residence Visa without needing to work first and then apply for residence.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to work as a solid plasterer in New Zealand?**
A: Usually requires completion of the New Zealand National Certificate in Construction (Level 4) or an approved apprenticeship, and holding a site safety card. LBP registration is not mandatory but beneficial. Overseas qualifications need NZQA assessment. English must meet immigration requirements (e.g., IELTS 6.5).
