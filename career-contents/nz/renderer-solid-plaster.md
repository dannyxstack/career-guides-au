# Renderer (Solid Plaster)（Renderer (Solid Plaster)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：333212 – Renderer (Solid Plaster)。**

In New Zealand, solid plasterers apply cement, lime, or gypsum-based materials to interior and exterior walls and ceilings, finishing surfaces smoothly. Skilled demand driven by residential and commercial construction; immigration pathway via Skilled Migrant Category and Green List, requiring relevant qualifications and registration.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a solid plasterer in New Zealand?**
答：Depending on experience: entry-level NZ$45,000-55,000, intermediate NZ$55,000-75,000, senior NZ$75,000-95,000. Foreman or self-employed can exceed NZ$100,000. Varies by region, employer, and project.

**问：Can solid plasterers apply for residence directly through the Green List?**
答：Yes, the occupation is on Green List Tier 1. If you meet criteria such as holding a relevant New Zealand qualification, having an accredited employer, and earning at least median wage, you can apply for the Straight to Residence Visa directly without first working for a period.

**问：What qualifications do I need to work as a solid plasterer in New Zealand?**
答：Typically: a New Zealand Certificate in Construction Trades (Level 4) or an approved apprenticeship, and a Site Safe card. LBP registration is optional but beneficial. Overseas qualifications must be assessed by NZQA. English proficiency to immigration standard (e.g., IELTS 6.5) if migrant.
