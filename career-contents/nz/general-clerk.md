# General Clerk（General Clerk）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：532111 – General Clerk。**

General clerks in New Zealand handle administrative support, data entry, filing, and basic office tasks. This is a low-skilled occupation, generally not eligible for skilled migration, but accessible via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV).

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 普通文员可通过认证雇主工签在新西兰工作，需雇主担保且工资达中位数 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 仅当工资达中位数的1.5倍且工作经验丰富时，可能通过SMC申请居留，但难度极高 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the typical salary for a general clerk in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 38,000-45,000; mid to senior NZD 45,000-65,000, often below the median wage.

**问：Can a general clerk immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Generally no. The occupation is not on the Green List, and SMC requires a salary 1.5x the median, making AEWV the main pathway.
