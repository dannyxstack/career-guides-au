# Child Care Worker（Child Care Worker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：421111 – Child Care Worker。**

Child Care Workers are in steady demand in New Zealand. Suitable for those with care or education background. Entry via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), with some meeting Green List criteria for residence.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 适合获得认证雇主工作offer者，可申请工签，工作2年后可转居留。 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 早教教师（需注册）在Green List上，工作2年后可申请居留。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 满足6分制要求（学历+经验），可申请技术移民。 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Child Care Workers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from NZD 42,000 to 50,000, mid-level 50,000 to 60,000, senior up to 75,000. Hourly rates typically above minimum wage.

**问：Is it easy for Child Care Workers to immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Enter via AEWV, work for 2 years, then apply for residence. Registered ECE teachers qualify for Green List.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a Child Care Worker?**
答：Minimum National Certificate Level 3, typically 2-year diploma or 3-year degree, plus teacher registration.
