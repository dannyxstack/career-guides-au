# Child Protection Officer / Caseworker（Child Protection Officer / Caseworker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：411713 – Child Protection Officer / Caseworker。**

Child Protection Officers in New Zealand primarily work for Oranga Tamariki (Ministry for Children), investigating abuse allegations, developing safety plans, and managing cases. This role is eligible for migration via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) but is not on the Green List or Skilled Migrant Category.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在新西兰独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Do Child Protection Officers get overtime pay?**
答：Typically no overtime pay, but Oranga Tamariki offers allowances for some extra duties. Starting salary is about NZD 55,000-65,000, rising to 80,000 at mid-level.

**问：How can a Child Protection Officer immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Primarily via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), requiring a job offer from an accredited employer like Oranga Tamariki. This role is not on the Green List, so direct residence is not available, but you may later apply under the Skilled Migrant Category if salary thresholds are met.

**问：What educational background is required for a Child Protection Officer?**
答：A Bachelor's degree in Social Work (or equivalent) accredited by SWRB is mandatory. Those without a social work background may complete a 2-year Master's diploma.
