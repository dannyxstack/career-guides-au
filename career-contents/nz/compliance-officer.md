# Compliance Officer（Compliance Officer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：221214 – Compliance Officer。**

Compliance Officers ensure organizations adhere to laws and regulations; demand grows in NZ's finance, healthcare, and construction sectors. Skilled Migrant Category and Green List offer residency pathways.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 需满足6分制，包含学历、工作经验和新西兰工作offer，合规官员属于技能职业，可申请 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2） | 合规官员不在T1直接居留名单，但可能符合T2条件，工作2年后转居留（需雇主支持） |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 适用于有认证雇主offer的合规官员，可工作3年，后续可转居民 |

**PR难度：中等，需工作offer（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a compliance officer in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$55,000–70,000; mid-level: $75,000–95,000; senior: $110,000–150,000. Finance sector pays higher.

**问：How can a compliance officer migrate to New Zealand via skilled migration?**
答：Apply under Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) requiring bachelor's + 3 years experience + job offer, or Green List Tier 2 pathway after 2 years work.

**问：What career progression opportunities exist for compliance officers in NZ?**
答：Advance to compliance manager, director, or move into risk management, audit. Certifications like CAMS boost prospects.
