# Painter and Decorator（Painter and Decorator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：332211 – Painter and Decorator。**

Painters in New Zealand have steady demand, responsible for surface coating in residential and commercial buildings. The occupation is not on the Green List but can be migrated via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) with salary and employer sponsorship requirements.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for painters in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level hourly rate is 22-26 NZD, intermediate 26-33 NZD, senior up to 35+ NZD. Self-employed can earn more.

**问：How can painters migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Main routes: work on AEWV for 2 years with accredited employer then apply for residence; or SMC 6-point system (work experience points) meeting salary threshold.

**问：What qualifications are needed?**
答：New Zealand National Certificate in Painting and Decorating L3/L4 and a health and safety certificate.
