# Pharmacy Technician（Pharmacy Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311215 – Pharmacy Technician。**

Pharmacy technicians assist registered pharmacists in dispensing medications, managing inventory, processing prescriptions, and providing customer service. This occupation is eligible for immigration via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), but not on the Green List, requiring employer sponsorship.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Pharmacy Technician？**
- 细心、有条理的人，适合重复性工作并关注细节
- 希望在医疗领域从事辅助工作，且不在高压力临床角色的人

**谁不适合学Pharmacy Technician？**
- 寻求高薪或快速晋升的人
- 不愿受严格监管或无法通过注册考试的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary range for a pharmacy technician in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level: 55,000-68,000; senior/supervisor: up to 80,000. Hospital roles pay higher than community.

**问：How can a pharmacy technician immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Main pathway is AEWV: secure a job offer from an accredited employer, work, then apply for residence. SMC is also possible but requires points. Not on Green List, so no direct T1/T2.

**问：Do pharmacy technicians need to be registered?**
答：Yes, registration with the Pharmacy Council of New Zealand is mandatory after completing an accredited course and exam. Overseas applicants need qualification assessment and may need bridging studies.
