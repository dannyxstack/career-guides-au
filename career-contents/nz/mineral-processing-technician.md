# Mineral Processing Technician（Mineral Processing Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311411 – Mineral Processing Technician。**

Mineral Processing Technicians monitor and optimize mineral processing operations such as flotation, leaching, filtration, and metallurgy in processing plants. In New Zealand, the industry is small, with limited roles in critical mineral processing; immigration pathways require employer accreditation or high salary thresholds.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在新西兰独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需认证雇主提供全职工作，时薪至少达中位数，最长3年，可续签，满足条件可转居留 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 分数制，需时薪达1.5倍中位数（约31.61纽币）或拥有研究生学历，职业在ANZSCO Skill Level 3-5 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Work to Residence） | 职业不在绿色清单直接居留列表，但满足薪资要求（1.5倍中位数）并工作2年可申请居留 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

- 不愿轮班或长时间偏远驻场者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can a Mineral Processing Technician apply for residence via Green List?**
答：No. This occupation is not on the Green List Direct Residence pathway, but if the salary reaches 1.5 times the median wage (approx. $31.61/hr), you may qualify for Work to Residence (Tier 2) after 2 years of work.

**问：What is the average annual salary for a Mineral Processing Technician in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-70,000; Mid-level: NZD 70,000-90,000; Senior: NZD 90,000-110,000. Salaries vary by location and employer, with mining companies often providing accommodation allowances.

**问：Which regions in New Zealand offer jobs for Mineral Processing Technicians?**
答：Opportunities exist mainly in West Coast (gold), Waikato (ironsand), and Bay of Plenty (geothermal) mines and processing plants. Most roles are FIFO or remote residential.
