# Contract Administrator（Contract Administrator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：511112 – Contract Administrator。**

Contract Administrators draft, negotiate, execute, and monitor commercial contracts to ensure project compliance and cost control. New Zealand's construction boom fuels demand, offering a pathway via Green List Tier 2 work-to-residence.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 Work to Residence） | 从事合同管理员工作满2年，可申请居留权，需满足薪资中位数要求 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 满足6分制（学历+经验），职业在ANZSCO技能等级，有工作offer可申请 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需认证雇主担保，从事合同管理员职位，可续签 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Contract Administrator？**
- 具备良好沟通和谈判能力的人
- 注重细节、有组织能力的人

**谁不适合学Contract Administrator？**
- 不喜欢文书工作和重复性任务的人
- 不善于处理压力和多方协调的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Contract Administrator?**
答：Entry-level NZD 55,000-70,000, mid-level NZD 75,000-95,000, senior NZD 100,000-130,000, depending on experience and industry.

**问：How can a Contract Administrator migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Via Green List Tier 2 Work to Residence (after 2 years) or Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system), meeting qualifications, experience, and salary thresholds.

**问：What are the job prospects for Contract Administrators?**
答：Strong demand due to ongoing construction and infrastructure investment, especially in Auckland and Christchurch, with solid growth over the next five years.
