# Earthmoving Labourer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 821113 (ANZSCO)**

Earthmoving workers operate heavy equipment such as excavators and bulldozers for land excavation, leveling, and material handling, and are key labor in construction and infrastructure projects. This occupation can immigrate to New Zealand via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but is not on the Green List or shortage skill list; skilled migration requires meeting higher conditions.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior earthmoving workers can gain operating experience, obtain heavy vehicle licenses (e.g., Class 2/4), and advance to senior operator or foreman. Some move into project management or start small earthmoving contracting companies; career progression depends on experience and certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive excavation and loading operations
- Simple GPS-based path planning for grading
- Basic equipment fault diagnosis
- Documentation and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Optimize excavation paths and earthwork volume calculations
- Remote operation with automation and human collaboration
- Automated reminders for safety compliance checks
- Training simulation and skill enhancement.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in manual handling of sudden equipment failures
- Team communication and safety supervision
- Multi-process coordination and decision making

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for earthmoving labourers?**
A: Entry-level annual salary about NZD 45,000–55,000, increasing to NZD 70,000–90,000 with experience. However, overall below median pay for skilled trades.

**Q: How can earthmoving workers immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: The main pathway is through the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) to work in New Zealand, but directly applying for the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) is difficult because the occupation is low-skilled (ANZSCO Level 4) and requires 1.5 times the median wage to be eligible. It is not on the Green List.
