# Earthmoving Labourer（Earthmoving Labourer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：821113 – Earthmoving Labourer。**

Earthmoving labourers operate heavy machinery such as excavators and bulldozers for earth excavation, levelling and material handling, playing a key role in construction and infrastructure projects. This occupation is eligible for migration via AEWV, but is not on the Green List or shortage list, requiring higher criteria for skilled migration.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在新西兰独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，需雇主担保，通常为短期工签，可续签但移民路径有限。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民类别，但土方工程工人ANZSCO技能等级为4（低技能），需满足更高薪酬或工作经验要求，通常需达到中位数工资的1.5倍。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for earthmoving labourers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around NZD 45,000-55,000, rising to NZD 70,000-90,000 for experienced workers. However, it is below the median for skilled trades.

**问：How can earthmoving labourers migrate to New Zealand?**
答：The main pathway is the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) to work in NZ, but direct skilled migration (SMC) is difficult as it is low-skilled (ANZSCO 4) and requires 1.5 times the median wage. It is not on the Green List.
