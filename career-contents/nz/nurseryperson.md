# Nurseryperson（Nurseryperson）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：362411 – Nurseryperson。**

In New Zealand, Nurserypersons cultivate and sell plants, a low-skilled role eligible for Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) but not for Skilled Migrant Category or Green List residency unless promoted to Horticulturist or Farm Manager.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，适用于有雇主担保的苗圃工作，可续签，但需达到工资中位数（目前29.66纽元/小时） |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民类别，通常要求职业为ANZSCO技能等级1-3，园艺工（等级4）不符合，除非晋升为园艺师（Level 3） |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 绿色清单二级，园艺工不直接包含，但相关职业如苗圃经理（Nursery Manager）可能符合条件 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Nurseryperson in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level Nurseryperson earns NZD 42,000-48,000 per year, near minimum wage. Experienced workers earn NZD 48,000-55,000, and supervisors up to NZD 55,000-65,000. This is below the median wage required for Skilled Migrant Category.

**问：Can a Nurseryperson immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：No, as an ANZSCO Skill Level 4 occupation, it is not eligible for Skilled Migrant Category or Green List. However, advancing to Horticulturist (Level 3) via a diploma can qualify for residency pathways.
