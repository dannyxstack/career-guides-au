# Plumber (General) — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 334116 (ANZSCO)**

Plumbers are in high demand in New Zealand, on the Green List, and can achieve skilled migration through Direct Residence or Work to Residence. Main duties include installing, repairing, and maintaining water supply, drainage, gas, and heating systems, ensuring compliance with building codes.

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Zealand National Certificate in Plumbing (Level 5) | Plumbers, Gasfitters and Drainlayers Board (PGDB) New Zealand | Required | Requirements for becoming a licensed plumber: complete apprenticeship and pass exams. |
| Licensed plumber registration | PGDB | Required | All workers in plumbing must be registered, with levels for apprentices, technicians, and licensed practitioners |
| Worker Safety Certificate (Site Safe) | Site Safe New Zealand | Optional | Construction site safety training, mandatory for many employers |
| English proficiency (IELTS 6.5) | Department of Home Affairs recognized body | Optional | Skilled migration visa requirement, required if native language not English |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Usually starts as an apprentice, obtains a national certificate, and becomes a licensed plumber. Can advance to senior technician, project supervisor, or self-employed contractor. Experienced individuals can enter construction management or specialized technical fields.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Simple leak detection and pipe blockage diagnosis (replaced by AI with acoustic/thermal imaging sensors).
- Inventory management and parts ordering (AI automatic replenishment system)
- Routine water system maintenance reminders (AI predictive maintenance).
- Partial client communication and quoting (AI chatbots handle initial inquiries).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted fault diagnosis: quickly locate complex pipeline issues via sensor data and historical cases.
- AR helmet overlays building structural drawings to precisely locate buried pipes.
- Smart scheduling systems automatically plan optimal routes, reducing commute time.
- AI training simulator: practice high-risk operations (e.g., gas pipeline repair) in virtual scenarios.

**Human moat:**

- Physical handling feel and fine craftsmanship (e.g., copper pipe welding, threaded connections).
- On-site safety risk assessment and emergency response (gas leaks, water pressure hazards).
- Non-standard repair experience for unique piping systems in old buildings
- Ability to build long-term trust and communicate on-site with clients.

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI-assisted diagnostic tools (e.g., smart detection cameras, data analysis platforms).
- AR augmented reality installation and maintenance guidance software operation.
- Basic data analysis ability: understanding predictive maintenance reports.
- Digital workflow management (e.g., mobile job dispatch and acceptance systems).
- Continuously learn new eco-friendly plumbing technologies (heat pumps, solar hot water systems).
- Advanced communication and project management skills (for complex renovation projects).

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 1
- Skilled Migrant Category
- Construction Boom
- Aging Infrastructure

## 6. Migration pathways / PR


| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1 (Straight to Residence Visa) | This occupation is on the Green List Tier 1, allowing direct application for residence without needing a job first. Requires PGDB registration and salary at the median wage. |
| Subclass Green List T2 (Work to Residence Visa) | Eligible for permanent residence after 2 years of work. Must hold PGDB registration and earn at least the median wage. |
| Subclass SMC (Skilled Migrant Category) | Six-point skilled migration system, scoring based on occupation, education, income, etc. Plumber is a licensed occupation and can get extra points. |
| Subclass AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa) | If you do not meet the Green List criteria, you can get a work visa through an accredited employer and apply for residence after working for 3 years. |

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much do plumbers earn in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level approx. NZ$55,000-70,000, mid-level $75,000-95,000, senior $100,000-130,000. Self-employed income higher, up to $150,000+.

**Q: Is it easy for a plumber to immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: Relatively easy. This occupation is on the Green List Tier 1. Meeting registration and salary requirements qualifies for direct residence application. Immigration also accepts apprenticeship experience.

**Q: How to become a licensed plumber in New Zealand?**
A: Requires completion of a 4-year apprenticeship, obtaining the New Zealand Certificate in Plumbing Level 5, and passing the PGDB exam for registration. Overseas qualifications need assessment.
