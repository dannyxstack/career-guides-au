# Cabler (Data and Telecommunications) — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 342411 (ANZSCO)**

Data and telecommunications cablers in New Zealand install, maintain, and repair telecommunications networks, data communication lines, and fiber optic systems, including structured cabling in buildings. Demand is stable, driven by digital transformation and fiber network expansion. They can immigrate via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or the Green List direct residence pathway, requiring relevant industry certifications.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for data and telecommunications cablers in New Zealand?**
A: Junior approx. 50,000-60,000 NZD, mid-level 65,000-80,000, senior 85,000-100,000. Those with registered electrician license or advanced certifications (e.g., fiber splicing) may earn more, and some project managers earn over 120,000 annually.

**Q: How can data and telecommunications cablers apply for New Zealand residence via the Green List?**
A: This occupation is on the Green List Tier 1 (Direct Residence). Requires New Zealand telecommunications cabling certification or registered electrician license, and an accredited employer, meeting the median wage (approx. NZD 29.66/hour) to directly apply for residence. No 2-year work buffer needed.

**Q: Is a New Zealand registered electrician license required for data cabling?**
A: If only engaged in low-voltage wiring (e.g., communication cables and fiber optics), an electrician license is usually not required, but holding telecom industry certifications (e.g., TCF Cabler registration) is preferred by employers. If involving power cables (e.g., installing power for network switches), a registered electrician license is mandatory.
