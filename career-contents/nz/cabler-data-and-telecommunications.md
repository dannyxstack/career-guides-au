# Cabler (Data and Telecommunications)（Cabler (Data and Telecommunications)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：342411 – Cabler (Data and Telecommunications)。**

Cablers (Data and Telecommunications) install, maintain, and repair telecom networks, data communication lines, and fiber optic systems, including structured cabling in buildings. Demand is steady due to digital transformation and fiber network expansion. Immigration is possible via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Green List direct residence, requiring relevant industry certifications.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Cabler (Data and Telecommunications) in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 50,000-60,000; mid-level: NZD 65,000-80,000; senior: NZD 85,000-100,000. Those with electrician registration or advanced certifications (e.g., fiber splicing) can earn more, up to NZD 120,000+ for project managers.

**问：How can a Cabler apply for New Zealand residence through the Green List?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 1 (Straight to Residence). You need a New Zealand cabling certification or electrician registration, a job offer from an accredited employer, and a wage at least median (approx. NZD 29.66/hr). You can apply directly without waiting 2 years.

**问：Do I need a New Zealand registered electrician license to work as a cabler?**
答：For low-voltage work (e.g., telecom or fiber cabling), an electrician license is not mandatory, but a telecom industry certification (e.g., TCF Cabler registration) is preferred. If work involves mains power (e.g., powering network switches), an electrician license is required.
