# Migration Agent（Migration Agent）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：224913 – Migration Agent。**

In New Zealand, Licensed Immigration Advisers (LIAs) assist clients with visa applications, strategy, and compliance through Immigration New Zealand. They must be registered with the Immigration Advisers Authority (IAA), which has about 2,500 licensees in 2025. This occupation can lead to Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but requires mandatory licensing.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Migration Agent？**
- 熟悉新西兰移民法规和签证流程，尤其是技术移民和AEWV路径的人士
- 具备良好英语沟通能力和咨询服务经验，乐于为客户提供定制化签证方案
- 有法律或商业背景，愿意持续学习移民政策变化并考取IAA执照

**谁不适合学Migration Agent？**
- 不擅长法规研究和细节处理，对复杂移民案件缺乏耐心
- 无法承受工作压力，尤其是面对客户签证被拒时的情绪管理

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for immigration advisers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level (0-3 years) NZD 55,000-70,000, mid-level (3-6 years) NZD 75,000-95,000, senior (6+ years) NZD 100,000-140,000. Salaries vary by location, firm size, and performance, with higher rates in Auckland and Christchurch.

**问：Can overseas immigration advisers migrate directly to New Zealand via Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Yes, but they need a local IAA license or obtain an AEWV to gain local experience. SMC requires ANZSCO skill level 1-3 and meeting median wage, with overseas qualifications evaluated by NZQA. It's advisable to work on an AEWV first before applying for residence.

**问：What steps are required to become a Licensed Immigration Adviser in New Zealand?**
答：Complete an NZQA-recognized Graduate Diploma in NZ Immigration Advice (1-1.5 years), pass the CAB exam, meet English requirements (IELTS Academic 7.0 or OET B), and apply for a license from IAA. The process takes 1.5-2 years and costs NZD 18,000-31,000.
