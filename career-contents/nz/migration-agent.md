# Migration Agent — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 224913 (ANZSCO)**

In New Zealand, a Licensed Immigration Adviser submits visa applications to Immigration New Zealand on behalf of clients, providing visa strategy, application preparation, and compliance services. Licensed Immigration Advisers (LIAs) must be registered with the Immigration Advisers Authority (IAA); as of 2025, there are about 2,500 licensed advisers. This occupation can use the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but note the immigration adviser license requirement.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Familiar with New Zealand immigration regulations and visa processes, especially skilled migration and AEWV pathways
- Possess good English communication skills and consulting service experience, willing to provide customized visa solutions for clients
- Has legal or business background, willing to continuously learn immigration policy changes and obtain IAA license

**Who is not suited**
- Not good at legal research and detail handling, lacking patience for complex immigration cases
- Cannot handle work pressure, especially emotional management when facing client visa rejections

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for New Zealand immigration consultants?**
A: Junior immigration advisor (0-3 years) annual salary approximately NZD 55,000-70,000, intermediate (3-6 years) approximately NZD 75,000-95,000, senior (6+ years) up to NZD 100,000-140,000. Salary is affected by region, company size, and performance; Auckland and Christchurch are higher.

**Q: Can overseas migration advisors directly immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
A: Yes, but need to obtain a New Zealand IAA license or switch to an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) to gain local experience. Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) requires meeting ANZSCO skill level 1-3 and salary threshold, but overseas qualifications need NZQA assessment. It is recommended to first enter on an AEWV and work before applying for residence.

**Q: What are the steps to becoming a licensed immigration adviser in New Zealand?**
A: Must complete a NZQA-recognized Graduate Diploma in NZ Immigration Advice (1-1.5 years), pass the CAB exam, meet English requirements (IELTS Academic overall 7.0 or OET B), and apply to the IAA for a license. The entire process takes about 1.5-2 years, total cost approximately NZD 18,000-31,000.
