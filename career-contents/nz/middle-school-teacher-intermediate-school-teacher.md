## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a middle school teacher in New Zealand?**
A: Under the collective agreement, new teachers earn approximately NZ$54,000-60,000 per year, experienced teachers NZ$75,000-95,000, and senior teachers or heads of department up to NZ$110,000+.

**Q: How can secondary school teachers immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
A: If you are registered with the Teaching Council of New Zealand and hold a recognized teaching qualification and English test score (IELTS 7.0), you can apply for the Green List Tier 1 Straight to Residence Visa without waiting two years of work.

**Q: Do I need to obtain New Zealand teacher registration overseas?**
A: Yes, overseas qualifications require assessment by the New Zealand Teaching Council and may require additional courses. Teaching qualifications from some countries (e.g., UK, US, Australia) may be partially exempt, but typically require provisional registration while working.
