# Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher（Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：241311 – Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher。**

Middle school teachers instruct students in years 7-13 in public or private schools; it's a Green List occupation with stable demand and skilled migration pathways.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher？**
- 热爱教学，喜欢与青少年互动，有耐心和沟通能力
- 拥有教育相关学位并愿意在新西兰注册和任教
- 希望在紧缺领域（如STEM、毛利语）获得快速移民通道

**谁不适合学Middle School Teacher / Intermediate School Teacher？**
- 不喜欢课堂管理或高压工作环境
- 缺乏英语能力或不愿通过严格的语言考试

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a middle school teacher in New Zealand?**
答：Starting salary is around NZ$54,000-60,000; experienced teachers earn NZ$75,000-95,000; senior roles can exceed NZ$110,000.

**问：How can a middle school teacher migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：If you are registered with the Teaching Council and meet English requirements (IELTS 7.0), you can apply for the Straight to Residence Visa directly.

**问：Do I need to get New Zealand teacher certification from overseas?**
答：Yes, overseas qualifications must be assessed by the Teaching Council. Some countries have partial exemptions, but provisional registration is common.
