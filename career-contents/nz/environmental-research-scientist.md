# Environmental Research Scientist（Environmental Research Scientist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：234313 – Environmental Research Scientist。**

Environmental research scientists use scientific methods to study pollution, climate change, and ecosystem health, providing data-driven environmental management advice to the New Zealand government and businesses. This occupation is on the Green List, offering a clear pathway to residence.

- 对环境科学有浓厚兴趣，喜欢户外和实验室工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for environmental research scientists in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level scientists earn NZD 60,000-75,000, mid-level NZD 75,000-95,000, senior NZD 95,000-130,000, with higher for PhDs or managers.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate as an environmental research scientist?**
答：Yes, it's on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa, also via SMC or AEWV. Immigration is relatively straightforward.

**问：What education is required to become an environmental research scientist?**
答：A bachelor's degree in environmental science or related field is required, master's or PhD preferred. NZ universities like University of Auckland and University of Canterbury offer relevant programs.
