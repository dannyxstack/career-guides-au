# Fire Fighter — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 441212 (ANZSCO)**

Firefighters in New Zealand are responsible for firefighting, emergency rescue, hazardous materials handling, and fire prevention education, forming a vital emergency service occupation. This occupation is not currently on the skilled migration shortage list, with primary employment at Fire and Emergency New Zealand (FENZ), and limited immigration pathways.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 2,300. Firefighter employment in New Zealand is stable; due to ongoing public safety needs, job numbers remain steady. However, competition is fierce, especially in urban areas, due to high career appeal and limited FENZ recruitment quotas. Growth will be slow over the next decade, mainly constrained by government budgets.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for firefighters in New Zealand?**
A: Junior firefighter: approximately NZD 56,000-65,000 per year; mid-level: NZD 65,000-80,000; senior or supervisor: NZD 80,000-110,000. Salaries are standardized by FENZ, including shift and danger allowances.

**Q: Can firefighters immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
A: No. Firefighter is not on New Zealand's Green List or skill shortage list, nor can they directly apply for Skilled Migrant Category (SMC). Only possible via an AEWV visa working for FENZ, but FENZ rarely sponsors overseas applicants.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a firefighter?**
A: Must hold NCEA Level 2 qualification, a valid first aid certificate, Class 2 driver's license, and pass FENZ physical fitness test, psychological assessment, and background check. After induction, complete 16 weeks of paid training to obtain professional qualifications.
