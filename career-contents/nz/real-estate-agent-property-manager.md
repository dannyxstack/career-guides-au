# Real Estate Agent / Property Manager（Real Estate Agent / Property Manager）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：612112 – Real Estate Agent / Property Manager。**

Real estate agents facilitate property sales and rentals; property managers oversee rental properties. This occupation is eligible for immigration via Skilled Migrant Category or AEWV, subject to salary and employer accreditation requirements.

|---|---|
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 职业在ANZSCO Skill Level 2-4，需时薪不低于中位数（2024年约$29.66），建议匹配长期短缺列表或高分池 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income level for real estate agents in New Zealand?**
答：Income is mostly commission-based: entry level $50,000-$70,000, mid-level $70,000-$100,000, senior $100,000-$150,000+, but volatile.

**问：Can real estate agents immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：Yes, via SMC with salary ≥ median wage and accredited employer; not on Green List, competition high.

**问：What are the career prospects for this profession in New Zealand?**
答：Market cycles affect demand; opportunities in cities like Auckland but competition intense; can start own agency after experience.
