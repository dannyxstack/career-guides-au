# Police Officer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 441111 (ANZSCO)**

The New Zealand Police is the primary law enforcement agency responsible for maintaining public safety, preventing and investigating crime, traffic enforcement, and emergency response. This occupation is in high demand in New Zealand; although not on the Green List in 2024, you can apply for residence via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), requiring New Zealand citizenship or residency.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa) | Requires a job offer from an accredited employer (New Zealand Police). Valid for 3 years, renewable, eligible to apply for residence. |
| Subclass SMC (Skilled Migrant Category) | Requires meeting the 6-point system: qualifications (3-5 points) + work experience (1-3 points) + New Zealand job offer (1-3 points). Police occupation is ANZSCO Level 2, requires salary at median ($29.66/hour). |
| Subclass Citizen or Resident (New Zealand Citizenship or Permanent Residency) | Police positions are typically limited to New Zealand citizens or permanent residents. Immigrants must first obtain residency through other pathways. |

**PR friendliness: low (★½☆☆☆).**

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: How much do New Zealand police earn?**
A: Junior police officers earn approximately NZD 65,000-75,000 per year, senior sergeants can reach NZD 100,000, and senior roles like superintendents exceed NZD 110,000.

**Q: Can foreign police officers immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: Police positions typically require New Zealand citizenship or residency. However, you can first obtain residency through other skilled migration pathways (e.g., SMC) and then apply.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a New Zealand police officer?**
A: No university degree required, but must complete 18 weeks of paid training at police academy. A bachelor's degree is beneficial for promotion and competition.
