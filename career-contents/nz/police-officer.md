# Police Officer（Police Officer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：441111 – Police Officer。**

New Zealand Police is the primary law enforcement agency, responsible for maintaining public safety, preventing and investigating crime, traffic enforcement, and emergency response. This occupation is in high demand in New Zealand but is not on the Green List as of 2024. Immigration pathways include the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), subject to holding New Zealand citizenship or residency.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需获得认证雇主（新西兰警方）的工作邀请。有效期为3年，可续签，符合条件可申请居留。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 需满足6分制：学历（3-5分）+工作经验（1-3分）+新西兰工作offer（1-3分）。警察职业属ANZSCO Level 2，需薪资达到中位数（$29.66/小时）。 |
| Subclass Citizen or Resident（New Zealand Citizenship or Permanent Residency） | 警察职位通常仅限新西兰公民或永久居民。移民者需先通过其他途径获得居留权。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a New Zealand Police Officer?**
答：Entry-level constables earn NZD 65,000-75,000 per year, senior sergeants up to NZD 100,000, and inspectors above NZD 110,000.

**问：Can foreign police officers immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Police roles generally require New Zealand citizenship or residency. One may first obtain residency via other skills pathways (e.g., SMC) before applying.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a New Zealand Police Officer?**
答：A university degree is not required, but completion of the paid 18-week Police College training is mandatory. A bachelor's degree is advantageous for promotion.
