# Teacher Aide（Teacher Aide）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：422115 – Teacher Aide。**

Teacher Aides in New Zealand have stable demand in the education sector, typically requiring an early childhood education or related certificate. They can work in public and private schools. Immigration pathways include the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but it is not on the Green List or skill shortage list; Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) requires meeting salary and points criteria.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the hourly wage range for a Teacher Aide in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level Teacher Aides earn about NZD 20-25 per hour, experienced ones NZD 25-30, and senior/supervisor roles NZD 30-35. Salaries are generally lower than teachers.

**问：Can Teacher Aides immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Yes, but pathways are limited. Common route is through an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) leading to residence. Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) requires median wage ($29.66/hr) and points but is not on the Green List.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a Teacher Aide?**
答：Typically a New Zealand Certificate in Supporting Learning (Level 3-4) is required. Some employers accept relevant experience. No mandatory degree required.
