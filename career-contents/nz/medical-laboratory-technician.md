# Medical Laboratory Technician（Medical Laboratory Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311213 – Medical Laboratory Technician。**

Medical Laboratory Technicians perform diagnostic tests in NZ healthcare settings, aiding disease diagnosis and treatment monitoring. Listed on Green List Tier 1, it offers direct residency pathway with strong job stability.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Medical Laboratory Technician？**
- 细心且有条理，能在压力下准确执行实验操作
- 对医学和实验室科学有浓厚兴趣，愿意终身学习
- 希望在医疗领域获得稳定高需求职业及移民机会

**谁不适合学Medical Laboratory Technician？**
- 厌恶重复性工作或不愿意处理生物样本
- 对严格的质量控制和法规要求不耐受

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Medical Laboratory Technicians in NZ?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around NZD 60,000-75,000, mid-level NZD 75,000-90,000, and senior can exceed NZD 110,000. Salary increases with experience and qualifications.

**问：How can Medical Laboratory Technicians immigrate to NZ?**
答：This role is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa, or Skilled Migrant Category with a job offer. IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 6) is commonly required.

**问：What are the recognition requirements for overseas Medical Laboratory Technicians in NZ?**
答：Require NZIMLS competency assessment, possibly bridging courses or internships. Overseas qualifications need NZQA evaluation, and non-native English speakers need IELTS/PTE scores.
