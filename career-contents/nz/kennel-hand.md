# Kennel Hand（Kennel Hand）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：361115 – Kennel Hand。**

A New Zealand occupation involving daily care of dogs in breeding kennels, boarding facilities or working dog training centers, with low to median wages. Not on the skilled migration list, only feasible via employer-sponsored work visas.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 最常见途径，需获得认证雇主的工作offer，无技术移民直接通道，时薪需达到中位数（约29.66纽币/小时，2024年）或按行业协议 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民通常不适合，因该职位ANZSCO技能等级为5（低技能），除非能证明薪资达中位数1.5倍（约44.50纽币/小时）以上，但实际困难 |
| Subclass WTR（Work to Residence） | 绿名单相关路径不适用，此职业不在绿名单上 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Kennel Hand?**
答：Entry level NZ$40,000-48,000 per year (approx NZ$22-24/hr), mid-level NZ$48,000-55,000, senior/supervisor NZ$55,000-65,000. Salary growth is limited, rarely exceeding NZ$70,000.

**问：Can a Kennel Hand obtain New Zealand residency via Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Generally no. The occupation is ANZSCO skill level 5 (low-skilled), not meeting the 'highly skilled' threshold for SMC. Only if the wage reaches 1.5 times the median (approx NZ$44.50/hr), which is unrealistic. The only feasible pathway is the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) for temporary work, then upgrading to a higher-skilled role for residency.

**问：What are the career progression options for a Kennel Hand?**
答：You can advance to kennel supervisor or operations manager, or transition to veterinary nurse (requires additional study). Some start their own pet boarding or training business. However, the role itself has limited growth; it's better seen as a stepping stone.
