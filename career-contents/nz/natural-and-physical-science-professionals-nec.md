# Natural and Physical Science Professionals nec（Natural and Physical Science Professionals nec）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：234299 – Natural and Physical Science Professionals nec。**

Food technologists work in food companies, research institutes, and government agencies, developing new products, improving processes, and ensuring food safety. The food tech sector in New Zealand has stable demand, with residency possible via skilled migration or Green List pathways.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary progression for food technologists?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from NZD 50,000 to 65,000, mid-level from 65,000 to 85,000, and senior roles like R&D manager can reach 85,000 to 110,000. Those with a PhD or management experience earn more.

**问：How can a food technologist migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Food technologists are on the Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence). You need a job with an accredited employer earning at least NZD 29.66 per hour, then apply for residence after 2 years of work.

**问：Which cities in New Zealand have the highest demand for food technologists?**
答：Auckland, Christchurch, and Wellington are the main hubs due to large food processing plants and R&D centers. Some entry-level roles also exist in Dunedin and Hamilton.
