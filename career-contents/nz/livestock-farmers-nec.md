# Livestock Farmers nec（Livestock Farmers nec）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：121399 – Livestock Farmers nec。**

Manage livestock on farms, including feeding, health monitoring, breeding and pasture management, ensuring animal welfare and productivity. This occupation is on New Zealand's Green List for skilled migration, subject to salary and skill requirements.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for livestock farmers?**
答：Entry-level NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level NZD 55,000-70,000; senior up to NZD 90,000+. Farm managers can earn more.

**问：Is it easy to migrate to New Zealand as a livestock farmer?**
答：This occupation is on the Green List, allowing work-to-residence after two years. It is relatively straightforward provided salary and employer requirements are met.

**问：Is an English test required?**
答：Immigration applications generally require IELTS 6.5 overall or equivalent, depending on the visa category.
