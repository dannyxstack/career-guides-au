# Landscape Gardener — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 362711 (ANZSCO)**

Landscape horticulturists in New Zealand design, build and maintain outdoor landscapes; employment is stable driven by residential and infrastructure demand. Immigration via AEWV or Green List Tier 2 (work to residence).

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can advance from entry-level laborer to senior landscape gardener, site supervisor, or project manager. With experience and certification, can start own business or transition to landscape designer.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use AI to generate initial landscape design drafts
- Automatically adjust irrigation schedules based on weather sensors
- Customer inquiries pre-processed by chatbots for common questions
- Automatically generate standardized instructions for pruning and fertilization

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AR/VR for on-site design effect previews
- Drone inspection of large green areas with disease marking
- Smart devices collect soil moisture data to assist precision fertilization
- Use management software to optimize construction scheduling and resource allocation

**Human moat:**

- On-site manual trimming and detailed horticultural operations
- Subjective experience in judging abnormal plant growth
- Face-to-face client interaction to understand needs and provide personalized advice
- Real-time decision-making for sudden weather or plant diseases

**Skills to build:**

- Master AI tools for garden and landscape architectural design (Python/3D modeling)
- Learn to debug and maintain intelligent irrigation systems
- Obtain precision agriculture sensor application certification
- Enhance customer service and project communication skills
- Obtain Qualifications in Tree Pathology and Native Plant Application
- Proficient in landscaping management software (SaaS platforms)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 2
- AEWV
- Infrastructure Boom
- Residential Construction

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa) | Most common work visa, requires certified employer offer, can accumulate experience to transition to residency |
| Subclass Green List T2 (Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)) | Can apply for residence after 2 years of work, meeting the median wage and occupation requirements |
| Subclass SMC (Skilled Migrant Category) | If the salary reaches twice the median or with a higher degree, can apply with additional points. |

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of a landscape gardener?**
A: Entry-level salary is approximately NZD 48,000-58,000 per year, mid-level NZD 60,000-75,000, and senior up to NZD 95,000. Self-employed or project managers may earn more.

**Q: What are the pathways to apply for a green card for landscape gardeners from New Zealand?**
A: Mainly gain experience through AEWV work visa, then apply for Green List Tier 2 (work 2 years to residency). If salary or qualifications meet requirements, can also use SMC.

**Q: What are the job prospects for landscape gardeners?**
A: New Zealand's infrastructure and housing demand is stable, with good employment prospects, but competition is moderate, and senior positions are more in demand.
