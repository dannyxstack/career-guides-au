# Helicopter Pilot（Helicopter Pilot）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：231114 – Helicopter Pilot。**

Helicopter pilots operate helicopters for passenger transport, freight, rescue, surveillance, and agricultural spraying. New Zealand has ongoing demand, but overseas qualifications require CAANZ conversion; skilled migration is possible but challenging.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary range for helicopter pilots in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level NZ$50,000–70,000, experienced NZ$70,000–100,000, senior NZ$100,000–150,000 or more, depending on role and hours.

**问：Can helicopter pilots migrate to New Zealand under Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Yes, but challenging. Overseas licenses need CAANZ conversion. Usually start with AEWV, then apply for Green List or SMC after gaining local experience.

**问：How to become a licensed helicopter pilot in New Zealand?**
答：Complete CAANZ commercial helicopter training (12-18 months, NZ$80k-150k), build flight hours (e.g., as instructor), then secure work visa and eventually apply for residence.
