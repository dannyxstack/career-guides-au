# Fencer（Fencer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：821311 – Fencer。**

Fencers in New Zealand install and repair various fences, a skilled trade in high demand. Eligible for Green List direct residence or Skilled Migrant Category, offering stable employment.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 围栏工在绿色清单第一级，可直接申请居留，无需工作满2年 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for fencers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZ$50,000-60,000, mid-level NZ$60,000-75,000, senior up to NZ$90,000+. Self-employed can earn more.

**问：How can fencers immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Fencers are on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa. Also can use Skilled Migrant Category or AEWV.

**问：What qualifications do fencers need?**
答：While not mandatory, NZ Certificate in Fencing (Level 4) is recommended. Site Safe card is often required, plus a valid driver's license.
