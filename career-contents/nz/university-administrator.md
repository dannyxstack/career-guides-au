# University Administrator（University Administrator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：511114 – University Administrator。**

University administrators handle student services, course scheduling, admissions, and research management in universities. In New Zealand, public universities offer stable positions, but it is not a skilled migration occupation, primarily accessible via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV).

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，适合有新西兰大学offer的申请人，需雇主担保，最长5年 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民类别，但该职业不在绿色清单，需满足高薪或额外条件，一般不适用 |
| Subclass Post-study Work Visa（Post-study Work Visa） | 毕业后工签，适合新西兰毕业的国际生，可积累经验后转AEWV |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for university administrators in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-65,000, mid-level: NZD 65,000-80,000, senior manager: up to NZD 100,000. Universities offer additional benefits like superannuation and health insurance.

**问：Can university administrators immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is not on the Green List, so direct skilled migration is not typical. However, you can work via AEWV and potentially apply for residence if earning at least twice the median wage, though it's challenging.

**问：What qualifications are needed for university administration in New Zealand?**
答：A bachelor's degree, preferably in education or business, is typically required. A master's degree enhances competitiveness. Some roles may need project management or Māori cultural certificates.
