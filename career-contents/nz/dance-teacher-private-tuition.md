# Dance Teacher (Private Tuition)（Dance Teacher (Private Tuition)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：249212 – Dance Teacher (Private Tuition)。**

Teach ballet, modern, Latin, street dance etc. in New Zealand dance studios or private tuition, catering to children and adults. This occupation can immigrate via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but is not on Green List, requiring meeting Skilled Migrant Category salary threshold.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Dance Teacher (Private Tuition)？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a dance teacher in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around NZD 40,000-50,000, mid-level NZD 50,000-65,000, senior NZD 65,000-80,000. Income depends on teaching hours and student base; part-time is common.

**问：Can a dance teacher immigrate to New Zealand via Skilled Migrant Category?**
答：Yes, via Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system), but salary must reach 1.5x median (approx. NZD 44.50/hour), which is challenging. Alternatively, gain experience on AEWV then apply for residence.

**问：Is there a professional association for dance teachers in New Zealand?**
答：Yes, DanceNZ provides professional development, certification, and networking. Joining can enhance professional image and job opportunities.
