# Dance Teacher (Private Tuition) — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 249212 (ANZSCO)**

Teaching ballet, modern dance, Latin, street dance, etc. to children and adults at dance studios or private training institutions in New Zealand. This occupation can lead to immigration via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but is not on the Green List; must meet skilled migration salary requirements.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the income of dance instructors in New Zealand?**
A: Junior coaches earn about 40,000-50,000 NZD per year, mid-level 50,000-65,000, senior 65,000-80,000. Income is strongly related to teaching hours and student numbers; part-time is common.

**Q: Can dance instructors immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
A: Yes, but must go through Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system), salary must reach 1.5 times median (about NZ$44.50/hour), which is quite difficult. Alternatively, gain experience via AEWV work visa before applying for residence.

**Q: Are there professional associations for dance instructors in New Zealand?**
A: Yes, DanceNZ offers professional development resources, certification, and industry networking. Joining can enhance professional image and job opportunities.
