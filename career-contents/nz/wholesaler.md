# Wholesaler（Wholesaler）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：133312 – Wholesaler。**

Wholesalers act as key intermediaries between producers and retailers, managing bulk purchasing, inventory, and distribution. In New Zealand, this occupation is eligible for immigration via the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), subject to ANZSCO skill level requirements.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for wholesalers?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$50,000-65,000; Mid-level: NZ$65,000-85,000; Senior: NZ$85,000-110,000. Varies by industry and experience.

**问：Can wholesalers obtain New Zealand residency through skilled migration?**
答：Yes, via Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) or after working on an Accredited Employer Work Visa. Pay and skill level requirements apply.

**问：What is the future job outlook for wholesalers?**
答：Positive, especially in supply chain and e-commerce. However, adapting to digital transformation is essential.
