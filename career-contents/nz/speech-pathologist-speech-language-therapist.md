# Speech Pathologist / Speech Language Therapist（Speech Pathologist / Speech Language Therapist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：252712 – Speech Pathologist / Speech Language Therapist。**

Speech language therapists in NZ diagnose and treat speech, language, communication, and swallowing disorders. Listed on Green List Tier 1, offering direct residence pathway with excellent migration prospects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a speech language therapist in NZ?**
答：Entry-level NZ$58k-68k, mid-career NZ$70k-90k, senior NZ$95k-120k; private practice may pay more.

**问：How can overseas speech therapists immigrate to NZ?**
答：Green List Tier 1 Straight to Residence: requires NZSTA registration, recognized qualification, salary at median (approx. NZ$68k). SMC 6-point system also available.

**问：Which NZ universities offer speech pathology master's programs?**
答：University of Auckland, University of Canterbury, Massey University offer 2-year master's, requiring relevant bachelor's.
