# Aquaculture Farmer（Aquaculture Farmer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：121111 – Aquaculture Farmer。**

Manages fish, shellfish, or aquatic plant farms in New Zealand, including feeding, water quality monitoring, disease control, and harvesting. Listed on Green List Tier 1, eligible for direct residence, offering strong immigration prospects.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Green List Straight to Residence） | 直接居留路径，需满足职位在绿色清单且达到工资中位数（或更高），无需先在新西兰工作。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Aquaculture Farmer？**
- 对水产养殖和海洋生物有浓厚兴趣，愿意在户外/偏远地区工作
- 具备吃苦耐劳精神，能适应潮湿、寒冷或海上作业环境
- 希望获得新西兰快速移民机会，且职业在绿色清单上

**谁不适合学Aquaculture Farmer？**
- 无法接受乡村或孤岛生活、频繁出差或季节性工作节奏
- 对高体力消耗、长时间站立或冷水作业抵触

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for aquaculture farmers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level: NZD 55,000-70,000; senior managers: NZD 70,000-90,000. Some large operations may offer higher.

**问：How can aquaculture farmers immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 1. If you meet the median wage (approx. NZD 29.66/hr), you can apply for straight-to-residence. Also possible via Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) or AEWV.

**问：What qualifications are needed for aquaculture farming?**
答：A New Zealand Certificate in Aquaculture (Level 4) or a related diploma/degree is common. Hands-on experience is highly valued; formal qualifications boost competitiveness.
