# Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics)（Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：323111 – Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics)。**

Aircraft Maintenance Engineers (Avionics) inspect, maintain, and repair aircraft avionics systems like navigation and communication. Listed on New Zealand's Green List, offering direct residence or work-to-residence migration paths.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics) in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$55,000-70,000; intermediate: NZ$70,000-90,000; senior: NZ$90,000-120,000, depending on experience and license grade.

**问：How can an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics) migrate to New Zealand?**
答：This role is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa; Tier 2 allows Work to Residence after 2 years. Skilled Migrant Category also available with 6-point system.

**问：What licenses are required to work as an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (Avionics) in New Zealand?**
答：Must hold a CAA AME license in Avionics category, obtained through exams and practical experience.
