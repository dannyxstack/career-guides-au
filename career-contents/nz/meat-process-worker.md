# Meat Process Worker（Meat Process Worker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：362111 – Meat Process Worker。**

Works in abattoirs and meat processing plants, involved in slaughtering, boning, trimming, and packing. New Zealand's export-oriented meat industry faces skill shortages, eligible for Green List Tier 1 direct residence.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★½）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Green List Tier 1 Direct Residence） | 符合绿色清单第一级职业，可直接申请居留权，需有认可雇主offer。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民类别，需满足6分制（如工作经验、学历等），当前职业有额外加分。 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，适用于无绿名单职位但雇主担保的情况。 |

**PR难度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Meat Process Worker？**
- 身体健康、能适应低温潮湿环境的人。
- 愿意学习屠宰或剔骨技术、不怕血腥的人。
- 希望通过雇主担保或绿名单快速移民新西兰的人。

**谁不适合学Meat Process Worker？**
- 对血腥和动物内脏极度不适者。
- 期望高薪且轻松工作的人。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a meat process worker?**
答：Entry-level earns NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level boners and slaughterers NZD 55,000-70,000; senior supervisors NZD 70,000-90,000. Pay increases with skill.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate as a meat process worker?**
答：Yes, it is on Green List Tier 1 (direct residence). With a job offer from an accredited employer, you can apply for residence immediately. Skilled Migrant Category also applies.
