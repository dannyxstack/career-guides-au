# Nutritionist（Nutritionist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：251112 – Nutritionist。**

Nutritionists use nutrition science to improve dietary and health outcomes for individuals and populations, working in public health, community, food industry, and private practice. Skilled migration is challenging, requiring at least a bachelor's degree and often NZ Nutritionist Association registration. The occupation is not on the Green List but may qualify for SMC or AEWV visa pathways.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（非绿清单，需达到中位数薪资）（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 若满足学历、雅思6.5分及薪资中位数（2025年约NZD 61,800）以上，可通过6分制申请，但非紧缺职业，排期较长。 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 获得认证雇主offer后申请，薪资需达到中位数，工作满3年可申请居留。适合积累本地经验。 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 营养师目前不在绿色清单上，但若从事公共卫生或社区营养相关岗位且雇主支持，可能凭个别职位申请。 |

**PR难度：中等偏高（非紧缺职业）（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Nutritionist？**
- 对营养与健康促进有热情，愿意帮助个人与社区改善饮食
- 具备良好沟通与教育能力，乐于从事公共卫生或社区工作
- 能够接受非临床工作，喜欢研究健康饮食与疾病预防

**谁不适合学Nutritionist？**
- 期望高薪资与快速晋升者
- 不愿投入多年教育或注册认证者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is it easy for nutritionists to immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Not easy. Nutritionist is not on the Green List; pathways include SMC or AEWV. SMC requires a bachelor's degree, IELTS 6.5, and income above median wage. Competition is high; local experience helps.

**问：What is the salary level for nutritionists in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 50,000-65,000; mid-level: NZD 65,000-85,000; senior: NZD 85,000-110,000. Lower than medical practitioners but provides a stable living.

**问：Do nutritionists need registration?**
答：Not mandatory, but membership in NZ Nutritionist Association can help employment. For dietitians, registration with Dietitians Board is required, requiring an accredited course.
