# Agricultural Technician（Agricultural Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311111 – Agricultural Technician。**

Agricultural Technicians/Agronomists in New Zealand provide technical support for agriculture, including soil analysis, crop management, irrigation, and pest control. This occupation is on the Green List, offering migration pathways via Skilled Migrant Category or work-to-residence, addressing skill shortages in NZ's agricultural sector.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an Agricultural Technician in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around NZD 45,000–55,000, mid-level NZD 55,000–75,000, and senior can exceed NZD 95,000. Varies by region and experience.

**问：How can an Agricultural Technician immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Through the Green List Work to Residence (Tier 2) pathway after 2 years of work, or via Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) under the 6-point system, meeting salary and skill requirements.

**问：Is there high demand for this occupation in New Zealand?**
答：Demand is stable as agriculture is a key industry; it's on the Green List shortage list, with higher demand in farming regions.
