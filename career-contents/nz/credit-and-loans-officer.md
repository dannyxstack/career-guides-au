# Credit and Loans Officer（Credit and Loans Officer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：552112 – Credit and Loans Officer。**

Credit and Loans Officers assess loan applications, verify financial information, and determine credit limits. In NZ, this occupation is not on the Green List, but migration is possible via AEWV or SMC under high-skilled conditions.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需获得认证雇主担保，签证有效期最高3年，可续签。适合无技术移民直通路径的申请人。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for Credit and Loans Officers in NZ?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 50,000-65,000; Mid-level: NZD 65,000-85,000; Senior: NZD 85,000-110,000, excluding bonuses.

**问：Can Credit and Loans Officers immigrate to NZ via skilled migration?**
答：Yes, but typically via AEWV work visa or SMC with high salary (1.5x median wage, approx. NZD 36/h). Not on Green List.

**问：What education is required for Credit and Loans Officers in NZ?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, or business. Relevant qualifications are advantageous.
