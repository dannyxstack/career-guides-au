# Paper and Pulp Mill Operator（Paper and Pulp Mill Operator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：712916 – Paper and Pulp Mill Operator。**

Operate machinery in pulp and paper mills to monitor production and ensure quality. This occupation is not on New Zealand's Green List or Long Term Skill Shortage List, but can gain employment via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) and may qualify for Skilled Migrant Category if salary and skill thresholds are met.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a paper and pulp mill operator in New Zealand?**
答：Annual salary ranges from NZD 55,000 to 70,000 (hourly $28-$36) depending on experience and location. Entry-level around 45,000-55,000, senior up to 70,000-90,000.

**问：How can a paper and pulp mill operator migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Obtain an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) with a salary at or above the median wage ($29.66/hour or about $61,700/year). After 3 years of work, you may apply for Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) under the 6-point system. This occupation is not on the Green List, so no direct residence pathway.

**问：What are the job prospects for this occupation in New Zealand?**
答：Limited; the industry is slowly declining with few employers such as OJI Fibre Solutions. Consider other industrial operator roles with higher demand.
