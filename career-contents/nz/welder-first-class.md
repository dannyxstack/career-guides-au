# Welder (First Class)（Welder (First Class)）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：322313 – Welder (First Class)。**

Welders are in high demand in New Zealand's construction, manufacturing, and infrastructure sectors, listed on the Green List for direct residence (Tier 1) via the Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system).

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Do welders in New Zealand earn at least the median wage?**
答：Yes. Entry-level welders earn around NZD 22-26 per hour, slightly below the median (approx. NZD 29.66), but experienced certified welders typically earn over NZD 30 per hour, meeting immigration salary requirements.

**问：How can welders apply for residence through Green List Tier 1?**
答：Get a full-time job offer from an accredited employer with salary meeting the median wage (NZD 29.66/hour), then apply directly for the Straight to Residence Visa without needing a prior work visa.

**问：Do welders need a New Zealand qualification?**
答：Yes, typically a New Zealand Certificate in Welding Level 4 or equivalent is required. Overseas qualifications may be accepted subject to NZQA assessment.
