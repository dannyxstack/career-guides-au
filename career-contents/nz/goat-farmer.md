# Goat Farmer（Goat Farmer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：121315 – Goat Farmer。**

Goat Farmer plans, organizes, and manages daily operations of a goat farm, including breeding, feeding, health management, and production/marketing of milk, fiber, and meat. This occupation is eligible for Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) and Green List Tier 2 (work-to-residence) in New Zealand, offering a clear migration pathway.

**PR友好度：较高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa） | 满足6分制要求（如学历+工作经验），职业在ANZSCO技能等级中，可获得居留权。 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 Work to Residence） | 从事该职业工作2年，且雇主为认证雇主，可申请居留。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a Goat Farmer in New Zealand?**
答：The average annual salary is around NZD 55,000-70,000, with senior farmers earning up to NZD 90,000, though income varies significantly with farm size and product type.

**问：How can a Goat Farmer migrate through the Green List?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 2; you need an offer from an accredited employer, work for 2 years, then apply for residence. Alternatively, you can apply directly under SMC if you meet the points threshold.
