# Money Market Clerk — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 552313 (ANZSCO)**

Money market clerks handle money market transactions, including recording, reconciling accounts, and preparing reports. This occupation falls under the business, finance, and law sector and can work in New Zealand through skilled migration or an accredited employer work visa pathway.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior money market clerks can advance to senior clerks or trading assistants, and then to traders or investment analysts. With experience, they can also move into risk management, compliance, and other fields. Holding a CFA or related certification aids advancement.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Enter and verify money market transaction data
- Automatically generate daily fund flow reports
- Reconcile differences between bank statements and internal accounts
- Trade booking and position updates

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to warn of liquidity risks and suggest hedging strategies
- Quickly review transaction compliance through natural language queries
- Use predictive models to optimize short-term capital allocation
- Automate the first draft of regulatory reports with manual review of anomalies

**Human moat:**

- Judgment in handling non-standard transactions or market anomalies
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation with counterparties, banks, and regulatory agencies
- Deep understanding and risk assessment of complex financial products
- Ability to make real-time decisions under high pressure

**Skills to build:**

- Automate reports using Python or VBA scripts.
- Use Power BI/Tableau for data visualization
- Understand machine learning basics to evaluate AI model outputs
- Learn RPA tools (e.g., UiPath) to handle repetitive tasks
- Knowledge of money market derivatives and compliance regulations

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable Demand
- Fintech Growth
- Not on Green List
- Skilled Migrant Eligible

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a money market clerk in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level approx NZD 45,000-55,000, mid-level NZD 60,000-80,000, senior NZD 85,000-110,000.

**Q: Can Money Market Clerks Migrate to New Zealand?**
A: Yes, but not a Green List occupation. Can apply for migration through Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) pathway, subject to ANZSCO skill level and salary requirements.

**Q: What is the career development path for this occupation?**
A: Junior clerks can advance to senior clerk, trading assistant, trader, or investment analyst, and may also move into risk management or compliance.
