# Money Market Clerk（Money Market Clerk）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：552313 – Money Market Clerk。**

Money Market Clerks handle money market transactions, including recording, reconciling accounts, and preparing reports. This role falls under Business, Finance & Legal and is eligible for skilled migration via SMC or AEWV pathways in New Zealand.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Money Market Clerk in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 45,000-55,000, mid-level NZD 60,000-80,000, senior NZD 85,000-110,000.

**问：Can a Money Market Clerk migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Yes, but not on the Green List. Eligible via Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), meeting ANZSCO skill level and salary requirements.

**问：What is the career progression for this role?**
答：Junior clerk can advance to senior clerk, trading assistant, trader, or investment analyst, or transition to risk management or compliance.
