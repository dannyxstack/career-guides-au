# Geotechnical Engineer（Geotechnical Engineer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：233215 – Geotechnical Engineer。**

Geotechnical engineers in New Zealand analyze soil, rock, and groundwater to design foundations, roads, tunnels, and slopes. Listed on Green List Tier 1, it offers direct residence pathway with strong migration prospects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for geotechnical engineers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$60k-80k; Mid-level: NZ$85k-110k; Senior: NZ$120k-160k. CPEng holders can earn over NZ$180k.

**问：Can geotechnical engineers migrate directly under the Green List?**
答：Yes. It is on Green List Tier 1 (Straight to Residence). Eligible applicants can apply for residence immediately without waiting years.

**问：Is local registration required to work as a geotechnical engineer?**
答：Registration as a Registered Engineer (REA) is mandatory for design sign-off. Many entry-level roles do not require it, but CPEng is beneficial for career.
