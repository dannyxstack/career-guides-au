# Sports Umpire — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 452322 (ANZSCO)**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income of a sports referee in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level referees earn approximately NZD 20-25 per hour, with annual income around NZD 30,000-45,000; senior referees can reach NZD 65,000-90,000. Mostly part-time income.

**Q: Can Sports Referees Migrate to New Zealand via Skilled Migration?**
A: No. Sports referees are not on New Zealand's Skilled Migrant Occupation List or Green List. Immigration pathways are very limited.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a sports referee?**
A: Must obtain referee certification from relevant sports associations, such as New Zealand Rugby, New Zealand Football, etc. Some programs require first aid certificates.
