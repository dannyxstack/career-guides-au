# Sports Umpire（Sports Umpire）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：452322 – Sports Umpire。**

Sports umpires enforce rules and make decisions in various sports events to ensure fair play. This occupation in New Zealand is typically non-skilled migrant, with limited immigration pathways, often seasonal or temporary work.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 如被认证雇主雇佣，可申请此工签。但体育裁判通常不符合技能要求，获批难度大。 |
| Subclass Specific Purpose Wor（Specific Purpose Work Visa） | 可用于短期体育赛事执裁，需雇主支持，有效期不超过12个月。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average income for sports umpires in New Zealand?**
答：Junior umpires earn about NZD 20-25 per hour, annual income NZD 30,000-45,000; senior umpires can earn NZD 65,000-90,000. Often part-time.

**问：Can sports umpires immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：No. Sports umpires are not on the skilled migration occupation list nor the Green List. Immigration pathways are very limited.

**问：What qualifications do sports umpires need?**
答：They need certification from relevant sports associations, e.g., New Zealand Rugby, Football NZ. Some sports require a first aid certificate.
