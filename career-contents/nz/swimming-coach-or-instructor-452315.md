# Swimming Coach or Instructor — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 452315 (ANZSCO)**

Swim coaches or instructors fall under the Education and Community category in the Australia and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations (ANZSCO). In New Zealand, this occupation can be employed under the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but it is not on the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Green List, limiting skilled migration pathways. Practitioners need relevant swimming teaching certifications.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for swimming instructors in New Zealand?**
A: Junior instructors earn NZ$45,000-55,000 per year, intermediate NZ$55,000-70,000, senior NZ$70,000-90,000. Part-time work is common, with hourly pay.

**Q: Can swimming instructors migrate to New Zealand?**
A: Swimming instructors are not on the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Green List, primarily working via an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV). Migration opportunities are limited, requiring other pathways (e.g., partner migration) or transitioning to management roles.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a swimming instructor?**
A: Must obtain Swimming NZ swim teacher certification and a children's work safety check; some employers also require a lifeguard qualification.
