# Dental Therapist（Dental Therapist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：411111 – Dental Therapist。**

Dental therapists in New Zealand focus on preventive and basic dental care for children and adolescents. This occupation is on the Green List, offering direct residence or skilled migration pathways with stable job prospects.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for dental therapists in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$55,000-70,000; mid-level: $70,000-85,000; senior: $85,000-100,000. Public sector offers stability, while private practice may pay more.

**问：How can dental therapists obtain residency in New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa. Alternatively, skilled migrant under 6-point system with registration and qualifications is also straightforward.

**问：What are the career progression options for dental therapists?**
答：Clinical progression to senior or chief therapist, or move into oral health promotion, management, or education. Some pursue further study to become dentists or specialists.
