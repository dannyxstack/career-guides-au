# Mobile Developer（Mobile Developer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：261319 – Mobile Developer。**

Develop mobile apps for iOS and Android, a high-demand IT role in New Zealand eligible for Green List or Skilled Migrant residence pathways.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Work to Residence） | 从事该职业2年后可申请居留，需满足薪资中位数要求 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 6分制下，学历+工作经验可用，薪资达中位数1.5倍可加分 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需认证雇主担保，通常为临时工作签证 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a mobile developer in New Zealand?**
答：Junior: NZD 55-75k, Mid: NZD 75-100k, Senior: NZD 100-140k. Senior or architect can exceed NZD 150k.

**问：How can a mobile developer immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This role is on Green List Tier 2, eligible for residence after 2 years of work. Also qualifies for Skilled Migrant Category 6-point system.

**问：What is the job market like for mobile developers in NZ?**
答：High demand in Auckland and Wellington, with rising remote roles. React Native and Flutter are top skills; fintech and health tech are growing.
