# Wine Maker（Wine Maker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：234213 – Wine Maker。**

Wine makers manage the entire winemaking process in New Zealand, from grape cultivation and harvesting to fermentation, aging, and bottling, ensuring product quality. This occupation is eligible for direct residence via the Green List, offering strong immigration prospects.

**竞争度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 直接居留签证，酿酒师在绿色清单一级职业中，满足薪资中位数（时薪NZD 29.66）即可申请，无需工作经验要求（但需有雇主offer）。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for wine makers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level wine makers earn around NZD 45,000-60,000, mid-level NZD 60,000-85,000, and senior (head winemaker) up to NZD 85,000-120,000, depending on the winery size and region.

**问：Is it easy for wine makers to immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Relatively easy. Wine makers are on the Green List Tier 1, eligible for direct residence if meeting the median wage. Skilled Migrant Category also applies, with overall low barriers.

**问：What are the career progression paths for wine makers?**
答：From assistant winemaker to winemaker, senior winemaker, winery manager, or chief winemaker. Also opportunities in viticulture, quality control, winery start-ups, or consultancy.
