# Courier（Courier）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：561211 – Courier。**

Couriers collect, transport, and deliver packages, documents, and goods, typically using motorcycles or small vans within local or regional areas. In New Zealand, this occupation is eligible for the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) but is not a skilled migration occupation, with limited immigration pathways.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，可从事快递员工作，但需雇主认证，职位不属于技能移民范畴 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民，但不适用（快递员为ANZSCO Level 4-5，薪资未达中位数两倍时无资格） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the typical annual salary for a courier in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level couriers earn around NZD 45,000–55,000 per year, while experienced or owner-operators can earn NZD 60,000–80,000. Self-employed operators may have variable income.

**问：Can a courier obtain New Zealand residency through skilled migration?**
答：No. Couriers are classified as ANZSCO Skill Level 4-5, not eligible for Skilled Migrant Category or Green List. They can only work on an AEWV, and residency requires transitioning to a higher-skilled role.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities for couriers?**
答：Advance to dispatcher, area manager, or become a franchise owner (e.g., NZ Post franchise). Alternatively, study logistics management to transition into logistics planner or transport manager roles.
