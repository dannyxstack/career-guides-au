# Composer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 211211 (ANZSCO)**

Composers in New Zealand can work in film, game, and music industries creating original compositions. Skilled migration pathways are limited, primarily through Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or the Talent (Exceptional Talent) category, but competition is intense and income is unstable.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

**This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa) | A composition job contract from an accredited employer is required, with the role meeting ANZSCO skill level requirements (Level 1-3); composers are typically Level 1 but need to demonstrate skills and market demand. |
| Subclass SMC (Skilled Migrant Category) | Theoretically feasible, but must meet the 6-point system: NZ qualification (e.g., master's 3-5 points) + work experience (1-3 points). Composers generally struggle to reach immigration points and are not in shortage occupations. |
| Subclass Green List T1 (Green List Tier 1 (Direct Residence)) | Composer is not on the Green List occupation, cannot apply directly. |
| Subclass Green List T2 (Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)) | Also not applicable. |

**PR friendliness: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the income level of composers in New Zealand?**
A: New Zealand composers are mostly freelancers with varied annual income: junior (0-3 years) about NZ$30,000-50,000; mid-level (3-7 years) about NZ$50,000-70,000; senior (7+ years) about NZ$70,000-100,000. Some top composers earn over NZ$150,000, but that's extremely rare.

**Q: Can composers immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
A: Very difficult. Composers are not on the Green List shortage occupations, and must meet the SMC 6-point system (e.g., master's degree plus high salary plus work experience), but the occupation has a small market and job offers are hard to obtain, making the immigration pathway unfriendly. More commonly, experience is accumulated via an AEWV work visa before attempting to apply, but success rates are low.

**Q: What is the career outlook for composers in New Zealand?**
A: Limited prospects but creative industries have potential. Digital platforms (e.g., Spotify, film/game demand) create opportunities, but AI composition tools increase competition. Suggest combining music production, recording technology, or expanding into education and advertising to increase income.
