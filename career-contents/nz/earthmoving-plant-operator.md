## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for an earthmoving plant operator in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 50,000-65,000; Mid-level: NZD 65,000-85,000; Senior: NZD 85,000-110,000. Overtime and remote site allowances may boost income.

**问：How can an earthmoving plant operator migrate via the Green List?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 2. You need to work for an accredited employer under an AEWV for 2 years, earning at least the median wage (currently NZD ~62,000), then apply for residence.

**问：Where in New Zealand can I get operator certification?**
答：Polytechnics (e.g., MIT, Unitec) and private training providers offer short courses. Training costs NZD 3,000-6,000. You also need a driver's license and Site Safe certificate.
