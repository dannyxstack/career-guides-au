# Customs Officer（Customs Officer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：441312 – Customs Officer。**

Customs Officers in New Zealand enforce customs, immigration, and biosecurity laws at airports, seaports, and mail centers. This occupation is on Green List Tier 1 for direct residence, subject to specific employment conditions with the New Zealand Customs Service.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Customs Officer in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level NZ$55,000-65,000, experienced NZ$70,000-90,000, and senior/team leader NZ$95,000-120,000.

**问：Can overseas applicants use the Customs Officer role to immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Theoretically possible via Green List Tier 1, but roles typically require NZ citizenship or permanent residency and strict security clearance, making it extremely difficult for offshore applicants.

**问：What is the career progression for a Customs Officer?**
答：Start as Customs Officer, progress to Senior Officer, Team Leader, or specialist roles like investigation or intelligence, requiring internal applications and training.
