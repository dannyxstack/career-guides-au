# Metal Fabricator — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 322311 (ANZSCO)**

Metal fabricators are key skilled tradespeople in New Zealand's construction and manufacturing sectors, responsible for fabricating, assembling, and installing steel structures and metal products according to drawings. This occupation is on the Green List, offering a direct residency pathway, skilled migration friendly, and with steady demand for skilled workers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression can advance from apprentice to senior technician, foreman, supervisor, or workshop manager. With experience, one can also start a business and take on subcontracts. Holding a New Zealand accredited qualification enhances competitiveness and supports immigration applications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual cutting and grinding of metal parts (replaced by CNC cutting machines and robots)
- Repetitive welding operations (performed by automated welding arms)
- Manually draw and distribute drawings (AI generates drawings and imports directly into equipment)
- Simple quality inspection (automated machine vision)
- Manual inventory counting of materials (real-time tracking by RFID and AI systems)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to optimize cutting paths and welding parameters to save materials and time
- Precise positioning by overlaying drawings onto workpieces via AR.
- Use AI to predict tool wear and replace proactively
- Use digital twins to simulate assembly processes, reducing trial and error
- AI-assisted cost estimation and project quoting.

**Human moat:**

- Custom manufacturing and repair of complex non-standard components on site
- On-site decision-making and communication in multi-trade coordination
- Safety supervision and emergency response skills
- Process debugging for new materials (e.g., high-strength alloys)
- Interpretation of client needs and assessment of design feasibility

**Skills to build:**

- CNC programming (e.g., G-code, CAM software)
- Welding robot operation and programming
- BIM (Building Information Modelling) Reading Comprehension
- Basic Python scripting for data processing
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Project management and cost estimation basics

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 1
- Skilled Migrant Category
- Construction Boom
- Regional Demand

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Metal Fabricator in New Zealand?**
A: According to Stats NZ, the median annual salary for metal fabricators is around NZD 60,000, with entry-level at NZD 45,000-55,000 and senior roles reaching NZD 70,000-90,000. The median hourly wage is about NZD 29, above the minimum wage.

**Q: Can metal fabricators directly apply for residence in New Zealand?**
A: Yes. Metal fabricators are on the Green List Tier 1 (Straight to Residence). Meeting the median wage and qualification requirements (e.g., New Zealand Level 4 certificate or equivalent Australian qualification) allows direct residence application without prior work. They can also take the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC 6-point system).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a metal fabricator?**
A: Usually need to complete a New Zealand apprenticeship and obtain a National Certificate in Metal Fabrication (Level 4), about 4 years. Alternatively, a pre-apprenticeship certificate (Level 3, 1 year) can be taken first. Overseas qualifications must be assessed by NZQA for equivalency.
