# Metal Fabricator（Metal Fabricator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：322311 – Metal Fabricator。**

Metal Fabricators are key trade workers in New Zealand's construction and manufacturing sectors, responsible for fabricating, assembling, and installing structural steel and metal products per drawings. This occupation is on the Green List, offering a direct residence pathway, and is immigration-friendly with steady demand for skilled workers.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Metal Fabricator in New Zealand?**
答：According to Stats NZ, the median annual salary for Metal Fabricators is around NZD 60,000; entry-level earns NZD 45,000-55,000, while senior roles can reach NZD 70,000-90,000. The median hourly wage is about NZD 29, above the minimum wage.

**问：Can Metal Fabricators directly apply for residence in New Zealand?**
答：Yes. Metal Fabricator is on the Green List Tier 1 (Straight to Residence). Meeting the median wage and qualification requirements (e.g., NZ Level 4 certificate or equivalent Australian qualification) allows direct residence application without prior work experience in NZ. It also qualifies under the Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system).

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a Metal Fabricator in New Zealand?**
答：Typically, completion of a New Zealand apprenticeship and obtaining a National Certificate in Metal Fabrication at Level 4 (about 4 years) is required. Alternatively, a Level 3 pre-apprenticeship certificate (1 year) can start the path. Overseas qualifications need NZQA assessment for equivalency.
