# Teacher of the Sight Impaired — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 241513 (ANZSCO)**

Vision Impairment Teachers provide specialized instruction to students with vision loss or blindness, teaching braille, assistive technology, and life skills, working in mainstream or special education settings. This occupation is on New Zealand’s Green List Tier 2, eligible for a work-to-residence visa pathway, and requires completion of New Zealand teacher registration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Primary vision impairment teachers can start as resource teachers or teaching assistants, then progress to senior teacher, special education coordinator (SENCO), or education consultant. They may also move into policy development or training roles. Continuous professional development is required to maintain registration.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- AI voice synthesis replacing some Braille reading instruction
- Automatically generate personalized learning materials (such as text with audio)
- AI-assisted assessment of progress for students with visual impairments

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-enhanced assistive technologies (e.g., real-time object recognition) for life skills training
- Smart voice assistants for lesson preparation and classroom interaction
- Adaptive learning system provides personalized practice.

**Human moat:**

- Emotional support and psychological counseling skills
- Non-verbal communication in one-on-one interactions
- Flexibly respond to individual special needs of students
- Interdisciplinary collaboration and family communication

**Skills to build:**

- Assistive technology tools (e.g., screen readers, BrailleNote)
- AI teaching platform usage and customization
- Data analysis to assess student progress
- Emotional Intelligence and Support Skills
- Disability Education Policy and Ethics

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 2
- Work to Residence
- Special Education Demand
- Steady Growth

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for vision impairment teachers in New Zealand?**
A: Salary depends on experience and position. Junior teachers earn approximately NZD 55,000-65,000 per year, intermediate teachers NZD 65,000-80,000, and senior teachers or SENCOs can earn NZD 80,000-100,000. Refer to the school teachers' collective agreement for details.

**Q: How can an overseas vision impairment teacher immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: This occupation is on Tier 2 of the Green List. You must first secure a job offer from an accredited employer and work in the occupation for 2 years before applying for a work-to-residence visa. Must hold New Zealand teacher registration and meet the salary requirement (currently ≥ $29.66 NZD per hour).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a teacher of the vision impaired in New Zealand?**
A: Must hold New Zealand teacher registration (accredited by Teaching Council), usually require completion of a recognized teacher education program and a postgraduate qualification in special education/visual impairment. Non-native English speakers must provide IELTS scores (overall 7.0, no band below 7.0).
