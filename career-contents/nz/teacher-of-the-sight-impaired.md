# Teacher of the Sight Impaired（Teacher of the Sight Impaired）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：241513 – Teacher of the Sight Impaired。**

Teachers of the Sight Impaired provide specialist instruction for visually impaired or blind students, teaching braille, assistive technology, and life skills in mainstream or special education settings. This occupation is on the Green List Tier 2, offering a work-to-residence pathway, and requires New Zealand teacher registration.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Teacher of the Sight Impaired in New Zealand?**
答：Salaries vary by experience: entry-level NZD 55,000-65,000, mid-level NZD 65,000-80,000, senior/SENCO NZD 80,000-100,000. Details refer to primary/secondary teachers' collective agreements.

**问：How can an overseas Teacher of the Sight Impaired migrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 2. You need a job offer from an accredited employer, work for 2 years, then apply for Work to Residence. You must hold NZ teacher registration and meet the median wage (currently NZD 29.66/hour).

**问：What qualifications are required to work as a Teacher of the Sight Impaired in New Zealand?**
答：You must hold NZ teacher registration (via Teaching Council), typically a recognized teaching qualification plus a postgraduate qualification in special education/vision impairment. Non-native English speakers need IELTS 7.0 overall with 7.0 in each band.
