# Regional Education Manager — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 134412 (ANZSCO)**

A Regional Education Manager oversees the operations of educational institutions in a specific region, ensuring educational standards are met. This occupation can immigrate to New Zealand via the Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) or Green List Tier 2 (work to residence) pathway, requiring employer sponsorship.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of a Regional Education Manager in New Zealand?**
A: Junior approx. NZD 75,000-90,000, mid-level 90,000-120,000, senior 120,000-150,000. Actual figures vary by region, institution size, and experience.

**Q: How to immigrate to New Zealand through this occupation?**
A: Eligible via SMC 6-point system (qualifications + experience/wage) or Green List Tier 2 (apply for residence after 2 years of work); requires employer sponsorship and wage at 1.5 times the median.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for this occupation?**
A: Typically requires a bachelor's degree in education or a related field; a master's degree is advantageous. Years of managerial experience may compensate for lower qualifications.
