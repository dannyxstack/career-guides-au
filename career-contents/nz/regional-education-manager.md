# Regional Education Manager（Regional Education Manager）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：134412 – Regional Education Manager。**

Regional Education Managers oversee the operations of multiple educational institutions within a region, ensuring educational standards. This occupation qualifies for immigration under the Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) or Green List Tier 2 (work to residence), requiring employer sponsorship.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a Regional Education Manager in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level NZD 75,000-90,000, mid-level NZD 90,000-120,000, senior NZD 120,000-150,000. Actual depends on region, institution size, and experience.

**问：How can I immigrate to New Zealand through this occupation?**
答：Via Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system: qualifications + experience/salary) or Green List Tier 2 (work for 2 years then apply for residence). Requires employer sponsorship and salary at 1.5x median.

**问：What qualifications are needed for this occupation?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree in education or related field; a master's is advantageous. Extensive management experience may substitute.
