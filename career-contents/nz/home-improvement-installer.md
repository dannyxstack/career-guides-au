# Home Improvement Installer（Home Improvement Installer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：821412 – Home Improvement Installer。**

Home Improvement Installers in NZ install doors, windows, cabinets, countertops, and flooring; this occupation is in demand and eligible for Green List Tier 2 work-to-residence pathway.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can a Home Improvement Installer apply for NZ residency directly?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 2; you need to work for at least 2 years on a work visa (e.g., AEWV) and meet salary and health requirements to apply for residence.

**问：What is the salary for a Home Improvement Installer in NZ?**
答：Entry level NZD 45,000-55,000/yr; intermediate NZD 60,000-75,000; senior NZD 80,000-100,000 (including overtime). Hourly rate NZD 25-40.

**问：What core skills are needed for this occupation?**
答：Skills in installing doors, windows, cabinets, flooring, countertops; proficiency with power tools; ability to read blueprints; safety awareness; communication skills for client interaction.
