# Home Improvement Installer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 821412 (ANZSCO)**

Home renovation installers in New Zealand install doors, windows, cabinets, countertops, flooring, etc. This is a shortage occupation in the construction industry, eligible for migration via the Green List Tier 2 work-to-residence pathway.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can a home renovation installer directly apply for a New Zealand green card?**
A: This occupation is on the Green List Tier 2. Requires holding a work visa (e.g., AEWV) for at least 2 years before meeting salary and health requirements to apply for residency.

**Q: What is the salary for home improvement installers in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level: approx NZD 45,000-55,000/year, mid-level: NZD 60,000-75,000, senior: up to NZD 80,000-100,000 (including overtime). Market hourly rate: approx NZD 25-40.

**Q: What are the core skill requirements for this occupation?**
A: Requires skills in installing doors, windows, cabinets, flooring, countertops, etc.; familiarity with manual/power tools; ability to read blueprints; and safety awareness. Communication is important for customer service.
