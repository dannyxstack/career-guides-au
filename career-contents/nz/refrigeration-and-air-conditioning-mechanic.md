# Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic（Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：342112 – Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic。**

Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanics in New Zealand install, maintain, and repair commercial air conditioning, refrigeration, and industrial cooling systems. This occupation is on Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence, making migration highly feasible.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for refrigeration and air conditioning mechanics?**
答：Entry-level salary is around NZD 50,000-65,000, mid-level NZD 65,000-85,000, and senior can reach over NZD 110,000 per year, with overtime and weekend work paid extra.

**问：How can a refrigeration and air conditioning mechanic immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa. It can also apply via Skilled Migrant Category 6-point system or AEWV work visa leading to residence.

**问：What qualifications are required?**
答：A New Zealand Certificate in Refrigeration and Air Conditioning (Level 4) is mandatory, typically obtained through apprenticeship. An electrical registration (EWRB) is beneficial but not mandatory.
