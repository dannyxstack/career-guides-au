# Nut Farm Worker — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 842216 (ANZSCO)**

Nut farm workers are responsible for planting, cultivating, harvesting, and initial processing of nut crops (such as almonds, macadamias, etc.), forming an important labor force in New Zealand's primary industry. The work is typically seasonal and physically demanding, requiring adaptation to outdoor environments; skilled migration pathways are narrow, mainly through the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but this occupation is not on the Green List.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of nut farm workers?**
A: The hourly wage for nut farm workers in New Zealand is approximately NZ$21-27, with an annual salary of about NZ$42,000-65,000, depending on experience and position. Entry-level workers are usually paid at or slightly above minimum wage, while senior workers or supervisors earn more.

**Q: Can nut farm workers immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: This occupation is not on the Green List, and its ANZSCO skill level is Level 5, making it very difficult for Skilled Migrant Category (SMC). The main pathways are through an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or the Recognised Seasonal Employer (RSE) scheme, but transitioning to residency is very difficult unless meeting high income or qualification requirements.

**Q: What skills are needed to work on a nut farm?**
A: Basic requirements include physical fitness, ability to work outdoors, basic English communication; holding a New Zealand driver's license and awareness of machinery operation safety are key. An agricultural certificate or Growsafe pesticide certificate will enhance job opportunities.
