# Nut Farm Worker（Nut Farm Worker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：842216 – Nut Farm Worker。**

Nut farm workers are responsible for planting, cultivating, harvesting, and initial processing of nuts (e.g., almonds, macadamias). They are vital to New Zealand's primary sector, typically seasonal and physically demanding. Migration options are limited; mostly through Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) as the role is not on the Green List.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 主要工作签证；需获得认证雇主offer，可续签，但无直接移民路径 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa） | 极难达到，因为职业ANZSCO技能等级低（Level 5），需高收入或高学历才可能 |
| Subclass RSE（Recognised Seasonal Employer Scheme） | 针对太平洋岛国季节性工人，适合短期工作，非移民路径 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Nut Farm Worker？**
- 喜欢户外劳作、体格强壮、能适应新西兰乡村生活的人
- 愿意积累农业经验、将来转入其他园艺或农场管理岗位的人
- 计划通过季节性工作获得新西兰体验、不追求永久居留的人

**谁不适合学Nut Farm Worker？**
- 对高强度体力劳动和户外天气不适应者
- 希望快速获得新西兰永久居留权、或寻求高薪技术职业者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for nut farm workers?**
答：Hourly wages range from NZD 21 to 27, with annual salaries around NZD 42,000 to 65,000, depending on experience and role. Entry-level workers earn near minimum wage, while senior roles pay more.

**问：Can nut farm workers immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is not on the Green List and is ANZSCO Skill Level 5, making Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) very difficult. Main route is via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or RSE scheme, but residence is unlikely unless high income or qualifications are met.

**问：What skills are needed to be a nut farm worker?**
答：Key skills include physical fitness, outdoor work tolerance, basic English; a driver's license and safety awareness are crucial. Agricultural certificates or Growsafe pesticide certification can improve job prospects.
