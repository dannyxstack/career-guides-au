# Arborist / Forestry Worker（Arborist / Forestry Worker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：362211 – Arborist / Forestry Worker。**

Arborists maintain urban trees through pruning, removal, assessment, and care, essential for green infrastructure; forestry workers handle harvesting, planting, and forest management in commercial forests. High demand in New Zealand with Green List fast-track immigration.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of an arborist in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level arborists earn NZD 50,000–70,000, mid-level NZD 70,000–95,000, and senior (consultants or team leaders) NZD 95,000–130,000. Overtime and contract work can boost earnings.

**问：Is it easy to immigrate as an arborist to New Zealand?**
答：Arborist is on Green List Tier 1 (Straight to Residence). Meeting the median wage (approx. NZD $33.08/h) qualifies you for residency immediately, making it one of the most immigration-friendly occupations.
