# Agricultural and Agritech Technician（Agricultural and Agritech Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311112 – Agricultural and Agritech Technician。**

Agricultural and Agritech Technicians in New Zealand assist agricultural research, conduct soil, crop, and livestock experiments, and apply precision agriculture techniques to boost productivity. This role is on the Green List, enabling direct residence or work-to-residence migration pathways.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for agricultural technicians in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from NZD 50,000 to 65,000, mid-level from 65,000 to 85,000, and senior roles can exceed 110,000, depending on experience and employer.

**问：Is it easy for agricultural technicians to immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is on the Green List Tier 2, allowing residence after 2 years of work, and is also eligible for SMC 6-point system, making migration relatively straightforward.
