# Driving Instructor（Driving Instructor）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：451511 – Driving Instructor。**

Driving instructors teach vehicle control and road rules for license tests, requiring a New Zealand driving instructor license, often self-employed or with a school, limited skilled migration eligibility, primarily via AEWV.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 受雇于认证驾校或培训机构可申请，需有雇主担保，年薪需达到中位数 |
| Subclass Self-employed（Self-Employed Visa） | 需满足特定条件，如投资额、创造就业，非常规路径 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 通常不符合技术移民要求，ANZSCO等级为C级，需高薪或额外认可 |
| Subclass Green List（Green List Tier 2） | 驾驶教练不在绿色清单上，无直接居留通道 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Driving Instructor？**
- 喜欢与人交流、有耐心，善于教学的人
- 有灵活时间，愿意自雇或兼职的人

**谁不适合学Driving Instructor？**
- 追求高薪或快速移民的人
- 缺乏耐心或不喜欢长时间在车内工作的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the income of a driving instructor in New Zealand?**
答：Annual income ranges from NZD 35,000 to 70,000 (pre-tax), higher for heavy vehicle instructors up to 80,000+, variable for self-employed due to seasonal demand.

**问：Can a driving instructor apply for New Zealand skilled migration?**
答：Generally ineligible for direct skilled migration; the role is ANZSCO Level C and not on Green List. Only possible via AEWV with median wage, but no residence pathway.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a driving instructor in New Zealand?**
答：Must hold a full NZ driver license for at least 2 years, pass NZTA driving instructor license test (theory & practical), no degree required, minimum age 18.
