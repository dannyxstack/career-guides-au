# Surveyor's Assistant（Surveyor's Assistant）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：821915 – Surveyor's Assistant。**

Surveyor's assistants support registered surveyors in land surveying tasks, common in construction and infrastructure projects. New Zealand has steady demand for this role, with migration possible via AEWV or Skilled Migrant Category, but advancement to registered surveyor is recommended for better immigration outcomes.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 需找到认证雇主，时薪达到中位数（$29.66/小时，2024年），可续签，后续可转技术移民 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 满足6分制（如学历+工作经验），ANZSCO Level 4-5需时薪达$29.66，但助理级别通常分数不足，建议晋升后申请 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 测量员助理本身不在绿色清单中，但测量技术员（ANZSCO 312212）在T2，需工作2年后转居留 |
| Subclass CSLB Registered（Cadastral Surveyor Licensing） | 获得注册后可直接申请技术移民，享优先审理 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a surveyor's assistant in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level: NZD 55,000-70,000; senior: NZD 70,000-90,000. Registered surveyors earn over NZD 100,000.

**问：Can a surveyor's assistant immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：Yes, but as an ANZSCO Level 4 role, you need a wage of at least NZD $29.66/hour and relevant experience. A better path is to advance to survey technician (Green List Tier 2) or become a registered surveyor.

**问：What education is needed to become a surveyor's assistant?**
答：Typically a New Zealand Diploma in Surveying (2 years) or a relevant bachelor's degree. Some employers offer on-the-job training.
