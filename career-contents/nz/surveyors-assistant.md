# Surveyor's Assistant — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 821915 (ANZSCO)**

Surveyor assistants work under registered surveyors on land surveying and measurement support tasks, commonly in building and infrastructure projects. New Zealand's labour market has steady demand for such skilled workers. Immigration is possible via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), but progressing from assistant to registered surveyor offers higher immigration advantage.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Surveyor assistants can progress to registered surveyors by completing a NZ Diploma in Surveying (2 years) and accumulating experience, then passing the Cadastral Surveyors Licensing Board exam. They can also transition to geographic information systems (GIS) or geotechnical engineering, with salary and position significantly improving with experience.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual entry and preliminary sorting of field survey data
- Basic coordinate calculation and simple drawing generation
- Automated reporting of routine calibration records for measuring equipment
- Fill standardized templates for measurement reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted real-time data analysis enhances on-site decision speed
- Automated generation of high-precision 3D models using drone and laser scanning data
- Smart voice assistant assists in recording measurement notes and site logs
- AI model predicts measurement errors and optimizes observation plans

**Human moat:**

- Flexible surveying and handling of anomalies in complex terrain
- Efficient communication and collaboration with registered surveyors and on-site teams.
- On-site fault diagnosis and emergency repair of measuring equipment
- Manual compliance review and risk assessment of measurement results

**Skills to build:**

- Operate drones and laser scanners and other automated surveying equipment
- Uses GIS and BIM software to process spatial data.
- Basic Python or R scripts for processing measurement data
- Understand the input and output of AI tools (e.g., automated modeling software)
- Enhance on-site safety and emergency response capabilities
- Learn digital measurement regulations and data privacy knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Steady demand
- Infrastructure projects
- Green List Tier 2
- Skilled Migrant Category

## 6. Migration pathways / PR


| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa) | Need to find an accredited employer, hourly wage at median ($29.66/hour, 2024), renewable, can later transition to skilled migration |
| Subclass SMC (Skilled Migrant Category) | Meet the 6-point system (e.g. education + work experience); ANZSCO Level 4-5 requires hourly wage of $29.66, but assistant levels often have insufficient points; recommend applying after promotion. |
| Subclass Green List T2 (Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)) | Surveyor Assistant is not on the Green List itself, but Surveying Technician (ANZSCO 312212) is on T2, requiring 2 years of work before transitioning to residency. |
| Subclass CSLB Registered (Cadastral Surveyor Licensing) | After obtaining registration, can directly apply for skilled migration with priority processing |

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys outdoor work, good physical fitness, able to adapt to various weather conditions.
- Detail-oriented, organized, skilled in operating instruments and recording data
- Willing to start from the basics and gradually advance to become a professional surveyor

**Who is not suited**
- Those who dislike outdoors, frequent travel, or construction site environments
- Cannot accept low starting salary

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for surveyor assistants in New Zealand?**
A: Junior: NZD 45,000-55,000, intermediate: NZD 55,000-70,000, senior: NZD 70,000-90,000 (pre-tax annual salary). Registered surveyors can earn over NZD 100,000.

**Q: Can a surveyor assistant immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
A: Yes, but the assistant ANZSCO level is lower (Level 4), requiring an hourly wage of $29.66 and accumulation of experience. A more secure path is to advance to a survey technician (Green List T2) or registered surveyor.

**Q: What education is required to become a surveyor assistant?**
A: Typically requires a New Zealand Diploma in Surveying (2 years) or a related bachelor's degree. Some employers accept apprenticeship training.
