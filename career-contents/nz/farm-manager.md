# Farm Manager（Farm Manager）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：121311 – Farm Manager。**

Farm managers in New Zealand oversee daily operations of sheep, cattle, dairy, or horticultural farms, including livestock/crop management, staff supervision, and financial planning. The role is in steady demand in NZ's agricultural economy and qualifies for Green List direct residence.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Farm Manager？**
- 热爱户外工作，动手能力强，能适应体力劳动和天气变化

- 偏好城市办公环境，无法接受偏远地区生活
- 对动物/作物管理无兴趣，不能适应季节性强的工作节奏

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for farm managers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level managers earn around NZD 45,000-55,000 annually, mid-level NZD 60,000-80,000, and senior managers NZD 90,000-120,000. Larger farms or dairy sectors offer higher pay, often with accommodation subsidies.

**问：How can a farm manager immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This role is on the Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence. Requirements include meeting the median salary (approx. NZD 61,700) and occupation criteria. Alternatively, SMC 6-point system or AEWV leading to residence.

**问：What are the career prospects for farm managers?**
答：Start as assistant, progress to manager of large farms, then regional director or farm owner. Transition to agricultural consulting, supply chain management, or training. Many entrepreneurs start their own farms.
