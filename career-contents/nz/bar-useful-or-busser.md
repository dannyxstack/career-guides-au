# Bar Useful or Busser — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 431911 (ANZSCO)**

Back-of-house role in bars, restaurants, or clubs handling cleaning, restocking, and assisting bartenders. In New Zealand, this role is typically on an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), but does not qualify for Skilled Migrant or Green List occupations, offering low immigration feasibility.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Junior utility workers can advance to bar assistants or bartenders, requiring experience and obtaining a liquor license (LCQ). Some move into hospitality management or food and beverage supervisory roles, but additional training is needed.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using automated cleaning robots for floor and tabletop disinfection
- Automate inventory of beverages and supplies using sensors and RFID systems
- Use AI inventory management systems to automatically generate orders and replenishment plans
- Using an automatic dispenser to mix cleaning and disinfectant solutions
- Record cleaning task completion via voice-activated devices

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI scheduling systems optimize cleaning routes and timetables, improving efficiency
- Smart devices provide real-time supply shortage alerts to assist proactive replenishment
- Data analysis tools to identify peak hours and optimize staffing
- Mobile terminals receive automated task assignments, reducing communication costs
- AI training simulates optimal cleaning processes to improve service quality.

**Human moat:**

- Ability to handle emergencies (such as liquid spills, equipment malfunctions).
- Non-verbal communication and emotional support in interactions with colleagues and customers
- Manual dexterity to maintain fine cleanliness in complex environments (e.g., wine stains, glassware)
- Adaptability to uncertain tasks (e.g., temporary venue setup)

**Skills to build:**

- Operate and maintain cleaning robots and automated equipment
- Basic data analysis and inventory software usage
- Multitasking coordination and time management
- Food safety and hygiene certification
- Basic English communication and customer service

**Key growth areas:**

- Low-skill
- High turnover
- Seasonal demand
- AEWV eligible

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bar attendant in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level hourly wage about NZD 17-19, annual salary around NZD 33,000-38,000 (based on 40 hours/week), senior up to NZD 22-27/hour.

**Q: Can bar attendants obtain New Zealand residency through skilled migration?**
A: No. This occupation is low-skilled (ANZSCO Skill Level 5) and does not meet requirements for skilled migration (SMC) or Green List. Usually only eligible for AEWV temporary work visa, extremely difficult to obtain residency.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities does this occupation offer?**
A: Can advance to bartender or supervisor, requiring LCQ liquor license and experience. Some move into hospitality management but need a hospitality management diploma.
