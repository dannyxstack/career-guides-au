# Bar Useful or Busser（Bar Useful or Busser）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：431911 – Bar Useful or Busser。**

A support role in bars, restaurants, or clubs responsible for cleaning, restocking, and assisting bartenders. In New Zealand, this role is typically filled via the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) but is not eligible for Skilled Migrant or Green List pathways, with low immigration feasibility.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a bar useful in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level hourly wage is around NZD 17-19, annual salary NZD 33,000-38,000 (based on 40 hrs/week); senior can reach NZD 22-27/hr.

**问：Can a bar useful gain New Zealand residency through skilled migration?**
答：No. This role is low-skilled (ANZSCO Skill Level 5), not eligible for Skilled Migrant Category or Green List. Usually only possible via AEWV temporary work visa, with very limited residency options.

**问：What career progression opportunities exist for this role?**
答：Advancement to bartender or supervisor requires LCQ license and experience. Some transition into hospitality management but need a diploma.
