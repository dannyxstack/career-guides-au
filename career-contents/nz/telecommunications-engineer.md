# Telecommunications Engineer（Telecommunications Engineer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：263311 – Telecommunications Engineer。**

Telecommunications engineers design, deploy, and optimise network infrastructure (5G, fibre, IoT). This is a skilled migration and Green List occupation in New Zealand, requiring assessment by Engineering New Zealand.

---

**学习难度：较高（★★★★☆）。**

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用（AUD） |

**考证难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 直接居留签证，符合指定职业和薪资要求（中位数工资1.5倍），申请人可直接申请居民签证。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民类别，满分6分制。通信工程师通常可获得学历（3分）+经验（3分）满分，需薪资达中位数。 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 认证雇主工签，适合暂不满足直接居留条件的申请人，雇主需认证，工作满2年后可申请居留。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Telecommunications Engineer？**
- 喜欢数学、物理，擅长编程和网络技术的人
- 愿意持续学习新通信技术（如5G、卫星通信）的人
- 目标移民新西兰，追求稳定高薪职业的人

**谁不适合学Telecommunications Engineer？**
- 不喜欢频繁出差或现场工作的人
- 对硬件/网络不感兴趣，偏好纯软件开发的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is it easy for telecommunications engineers to migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Relatively easy, as the occupation is on Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence. You need a positive International Qualification Assessment from Engineering New Zealand and meet the salary threshold (1.5x median wage, ~88,000 NZD/year).

**问：What is the average salary for telecommunications engineers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 65,000-85,000; Mid-level: NZD 90,000-120,000; Senior: NZD 130,000-160,000. Highly skilled specialists can exceed NZD 180,000.

**问：What is the career progression for telecommunications engineers?**
答：Start as a junior engineer on project execution. After 2-5 years, become senior engineer or tech lead. Then progress to project manager, network architect, or R&D specialist.
