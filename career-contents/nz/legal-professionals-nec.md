# Legal Professionals nec（Legal Professionals nec）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：271299 – Legal Professionals nec。**

Property conveyancer handles legal aspects of property transactions, ensuring compliance and smooth transfers. Stable demand with pathways through skilled migration or employer sponsorship.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a conveyancer in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZD 55,000-75,000; mid-level: NZD 75,000-95,000; senior: NZD 100,000-140,000. Varies by experience and location.

**问：Can a conveyancer immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：Yes. The occupation is on the SMC list. You need NZ admission and meet language requirements for the 6-point system.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a conveyancer in New Zealand?**
答：A Bachelor of Laws (LLB) or Graduate Diploma in Law, plus Professional Legal Studies (Profs) and admission to the bar.
