# Gas Fitter（Gas Fitter）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：334112 – Gas Fitter。**

Gas Fitters have high migration feasibility to New Zealand due to sustained shortage of licensed gas workers, especially in cities like Auckland. This occupation qualifies for Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Green List Direct Residence, subject to obtaining New Zealand registration.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：友好（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 获得新西兰燃气工注册后，可直接申请居留，无需工作两年 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 通过6分制打分（如学历+工作经验+薪资）申请技术移民，需有雇主担保 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 适用于未取得注册的申请人，先通过认证雇主工作2年，再转居留 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Gas Fitter in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level licensed gas fitters earn NZD 55,000-70,000, experienced ones NZD 70,000-90,000, and senior/self-employed over NZD 90,000 per year.

**问：How can a Gas Fitter migrate to New Zealand?**
答：Main pathways: 1) Obtain NZ registration and apply for Green List Straight to Residence Visa; 2) Skilled Migrant Category with 6 points (requires employer offer and salary threshold); 3) Work 2 years on AEWV then apply for residence.

**问：How can overseas Gas Fitters get NZ registration?**
答：Australian holders can use Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition; others need NZQA assessment and pass theory/practical exams, or complete an apprenticeship top-up.
