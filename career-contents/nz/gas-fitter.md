# Gas Fitter — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 334112 (ANZSCO)**

Gas fitters have high feasibility for immigrating to New Zealand; New Zealand has a continuous shortage of licensed gas fitters, especially in cities like Auckland. This occupation can apply for Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Green List direct residence pathway, provided New Zealand registration is obtained.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gas fitters in New Zealand?**
A: Entry-level certified workers earn approximately NZD 55,000-70,000 per year, experienced tradespeople NZD 70,000-90,000, and senior supervisors or self-employed individuals over NZD 90,000.

**Q: How can gas fitters immigrate to New Zealand?**
A: Main pathways: 1) Obtain New Zealand registration, apply for Green List direct residence visa; 2) Via Skilled Migrant 6-point system (requires employer offer and meeting salary threshold); 3) First hold an accredited employer work visa for 2 years, then transfer to residence.

**Q: How can an overseas gasfitter obtain registration in New Zealand?**
A: Holders of Australian qualifications can convert directly through the Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition Agreement; others need to undergo NZQA qualification assessment followed by theory and practical exams, or supplementary training through apprenticeships.
