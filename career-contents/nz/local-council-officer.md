# Local Council Officer — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 599613 (ANZSCO)**

Local council staff work in New Zealand city/district councils in administration, planning, community services, compliance, and facility management. As this occupation is not on the skilled migration shortage list, migration pathways are limited, usually through Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or partner visas.

> **This is a non-skilled-migration occupation.** It is generally not on the skilled occupation lists and cannot be used for independent skilled migration.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Can local council staff immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
A: No. This occupation is not on the Green List Tier 1 or Tier 2, so it cannot directly lead to residency. Usually requires accumulating experience on an AEWV work visa, then trying SMC, but it is difficult to get enough points under the 6-point system.

**Q: What is the salary level of a New Zealand local council staff member?**
A: Junior about NZ$45,000-$55,000, intermediate NZ$60,000-$80,000, senior NZ$90,000-$110,000. Salary affected by council size and region; Auckland slightly higher, but overall lower than private sector.

**Q: What are the career prospects for local council staff in New Zealand?**
A: Development is stable but promotion is slow. Internal training can lead to planning or environmental management roles, but competition for senior positions is fierce. It is recommended to obtain relevant certifications (e.g., project management) to enhance competitiveness.
