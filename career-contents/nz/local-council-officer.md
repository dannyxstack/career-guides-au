# Local Council Officer（Local Council Officer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：599613 – Local Council Officer。**

Local Council Officers in New Zealand handle administrative, planning, community service, compliance, and facility management tasks within city/district councils. This occupation is not on the skilled migration shortage list, limiting immigration pathways mainly to Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or partner visas.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在新西兰技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 若获得认证雇主雇佣，可申请该工签，但职业不在绿色清单上，后续移民需通过SMC或配偶途径。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 需满足6分制要求，但该职业非注册职业，通常需高薪（中位数3倍）或新西兰学历加分，难度较大。 |
| Subclass Partner of a Worker（Partner of a Worker Visa） | 若配偶持有技术工签或居民签证，可作为附属申请人工作。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Can a Local Council Officer immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：No. This occupation is not on the Green List Tier 1 or Tier 2, so it does not qualify for direct residence. The typical route is via AEWV work visa, then attempt SMC, but scoring enough points under the 6-point system is challenging.

**问：What is the salary range for Local Council Officers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 45,000-55,000, mid-level NZD 60,000-80,000, senior NZD 90,000-110,000. Salaries vary by council size and location, slightly higher in Auckland but generally lower than private sector.

**问：What are the career prospects for Local Council Officers in New Zealand?**
答：Stable but slow progression. Internal training can lead to planning or environmental management roles, but senior positions are competitive. Obtaining additional certifications like project management can enhance competitiveness.
