# Pet Groomer（Pet Groomer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：361113 – Pet Groomer。**

Pet groomers bathe, clip, style, and trim nails for pets in shops or clinics. This role qualifies for AEWV but not on shortage or Green List, limiting skilled migration; suitable for experienced workers or self-employment.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 最常见的工签路径，需获得认证雇主offer，要求技能匹配，非长期居民途径 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 技术移民，但该职业不属于技能短缺列表，需雇主支持且薪资达标（约中位数以上），6分制下可直接申请，但获批难度较大 |
| Subclass Entrepreneur（Entrepreneur Work Visa） | 适合自雇开设宠物美容店，需商业计划和资金证明，可转居留 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a pet groomer in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 35,000-45,000, experienced up to NZD 58,000-75,000 annually; self-employed income varies.

**问：Can a pet groomer immigrate to New Zealand through skilled migration?**
答：It's challenging. Not on Green List; possible via AEWV or SMC with high salary (e.g., double median) but competitive. Entrepreneur visa is alternative for self-employment.

**问：What recognized pet grooming training is available in New Zealand?**
答：NZPGI Level 3 Certificate (3-6 months, NZD 3,000-8,000) and NZ Certificate in Animal Technology (1 year, NZD 12,000-20,000). Apprenticeships also common.
