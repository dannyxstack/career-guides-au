# Pet Groomer（Pet Groomer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：361311 – Pet Groomer。**

Pet groomers in New Zealand provide washing, trimming, and styling services for pets, with stable demand. They can immigrate via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) or Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), though the occupation is not on the Green List.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for pet groomers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZ$40,000-50,000, mid-level NZ$50,000-65,000, senior NZ$65,000-80,000. Self-employed can earn over NZ$100,000 in peak seasons.

**问：Can pet groomers immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Yes, mainly via Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) with a pathway to residence after 3 years, or via Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) if earning median wage. Not on the Green List, so moderate difficulty.

**问：What training is needed to become a pet groomer in New Zealand?**
答：Usually a short course of 3-6 months or 1-2 year apprenticeship. A NZQA Level 4 certificate in pet grooming is not mandatory but recommended.
