# Crane, Hoist and Lift Operator（Crane, Hoist and Lift Operator）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：721111 – Crane, Hoist and Lift Operator。**

Crane operators in New Zealand operate tower cranes, mobile cranes, and gantry cranes in construction, infrastructure, and ports. Migration via AEWV is available with a license, while SMC/Green List options are limited.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等偏低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 获得认证雇主担保，职位需满足工资中位数（约NZD 29.66/小时），持有起重机执照。 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 需满足6分制：学历（如Level 4证书）+工作经验积累分数，但该职业不在绿色清单，需高薪或本地经验。 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence)） | 目前该职业不在绿色清单，但若未来列入，工作2年后可申请居留。 |

**PR难度：中等偏难（★★★½☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Crane, Hoist and Lift Operator？**
- 有操作重型机械经验或机械背景的人
- 愿意在户外和高空工作，体格良好
- 能接受偏远地区项目（如矿区）者

**谁不适合学Crane, Hoist and Lift Operator？**
- 恐高或无法适应长时间高空作业者
- 不擅长团队协作和严格安全规定者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a crane operator?**
答：Entry-level NZD 55,000-70,000, intermediate NZD 75,000-95,000, senior NZD 100,000+ depending on experience and license.

**问：Can a crane operator immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：Not on Green List currently, but possible via AEWV work visa, then apply for SMC residence with competitive salary or long-term employment.

**问：What license is required to operate a crane in New Zealand?**
答：A crane license from WorkSafe New Zealand and Site Safe Passport. Separate endorsements for different crane types.
