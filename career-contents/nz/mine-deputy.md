# Mine Deputy — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 312913 (ANZSCO)**

An underground mine deputy supervisor oversees mining operations in New Zealand underground coal mines, ensuring safety compliance and production targets. This occupation may qualify for residence via skilled migration or Green List pathways, but opportunities are limited.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Junior mine deputy can advance to senior deputy, mine manager, or move into safety advisory or training roles. With experience, can obtain advanced management certifications.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Data entry and report generation (automated monitoring system takeover)
- Routine ventilation and gas detection (sensors + AI early warning)
- Production progress tracking (replaced by automated scheduling systems)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI predictive maintenance alerts miners to repair equipment before breakdown
- Real-time risk maps assist safety inspection decisions
- Automated scheduling optimization improves ore extraction efficiency
- Digital twin simulation for emergency drill scenarios

**Human moat:**

- Quick decision-making and leadership in on-site emergencies.
- Interpersonal coordination with miners, contractors, and regulatory bodies
- Unstructured problem-solving in complex mine environments
- On-site implementation of compliance and safety culture

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and visualization (interpreting AI monitoring reports)
- Automated system operation and troubleshooting.
- Digital security protocols and emergency response
- Human-Machine Collaboration Management
- Advanced studies in mining engineering or safety management
- Green Mining and Sustainable Practices

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable demand
- Limited vacancies
- Health & safety focus
- Regional employment

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average annual salary of a deputy mine manager in New Zealand?**
A: Average annual salary approx. NZD 100,000-130,000, junior approx. 80,000, senior up to 160,000.

**Q: Can a deputy mine manager apply for immigration to New Zealand?**
A: Yes, mainly through Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) or Green List Tier 2 pathway. Must meet qualifications, experience, and job offer requirements.

**Q: What certificates must you hold to work in New Zealand?**
A: Must obtain the National Coal Manager's Certificate (Underground) issued by WorkSafe NZ, and pass relevant exams and assessments.
