# Mine Deputy（Mine Deputy）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：312913 – Mine Deputy。**

Mine Deputies supervise underground coal mining operations in New Zealand, ensuring safety compliance and production targets. The occupation offers limited migration pathways via Skilled Migrant Category or Green List.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a Mine Deputy in New Zealand?**
答：Average annual salary ranges from NZD 100,000 to 130,000, with entry-level around NZD 80,000 and senior up to NZD 160,000.

**问：Can a Mine Deputy apply for New Zealand residence?**
答：Yes, via Skilled Migrant Category or Green List Tier 2 (Work to Residence). Requirements include qualification, experience, and job offer.

**问：What certifications are required to work as a Mine Deputy in New Zealand?**
答：Must hold the National Certificate in Coal Mine Management (Underground) from WorkSafe NZ, obtained through exams and assessments.
