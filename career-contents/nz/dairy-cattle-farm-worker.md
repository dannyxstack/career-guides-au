# Dairy Cattle Farm Worker（Dairy Cattle Farm Worker）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：842313 – Dairy Cattle Farm Worker。**

Dairy cattle farm workers in New Zealand perform milking, feeding, herd health checks, cleaning, and basic equipment maintenance. This occupation is in chronic shortage and eligible for Green List Tier 2 (work to residence) pathway, requiring employer support and meeting salary thresholds.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for dairy cattle farm workers?**
答：Entry-level NZD 45,000-55,000; mid-level NZD 55,000-65,000; senior NZD 65,000-85,000. Some farms provide free accommodation.

**问：How can I immigrate through this occupation?**
答：The occupation is on Green List Tier 2. After working 2 years at median wage (NZD 29.66/hr) you can apply for residence. You'll first need an AEWV.

**问：What training or certification is needed?**
答：No formal qualification required, but a 1-year agriculture certificate is recommended. Mandatory: IELTS 5.0 for migration. Some farms require Growsafe.
