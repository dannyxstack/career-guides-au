# Interior Designer（Interior Designer）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：232511 – Interior Designer。**

Interior designers plan functional layouts and aesthetic designs for residential, commercial and hospitality spaces, coordinating material procurement and construction supervision. With growing building projects in Auckland and Christchurch, designers can apply for residence via Green List or Skilled Migrant Category; proficiency in SketchUp/Revit/BIM is highly valued.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Interior Designer？**

- 对可持续设计、绿色建筑缺乏兴趣

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for interior designers in New Zealand?**
答：Junior (0-3 yrs) earns NZD 55,000-70,000, mid-level (3-6 yrs) NZD 72,000-90,000, senior (6+ yrs) NZD 95,000-120,000. Salaries in Auckland are generally higher than in Christchurch.

**问：Can interior designers apply for residence via Green List?**
答：Yes. Interior designer is on Green List Tier 2, allowing work-to-residence after 2 years of employment. Alternatively, they can apply under Skilled Migrant Category (6-point system) based on qualifications, income, and experience.

**问：Is registration required for interior designers in New Zealand?**
答：Registration is not mandatory by law, but a relevant bachelor's degree and DINZ membership enhance competitiveness. Skills assessment for immigration typically requires a Level 7 qualification or higher.
