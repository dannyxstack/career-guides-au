# Teacher of English to Speakers of Other Languages（Teacher of English to Speakers of Other Languages）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：249311 – Teacher of English to Speakers of Other Languages。**

TESOL teachers in New Zealand teach English to non-native speakers in language schools, universities, or private institutions. This occupation is eligible for Green List Tier 2 (work 2 years then residence) or Skilled Migrant Category, but requires relevant qualifications and employer sponsorship.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for TESOL teachers in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level teachers earn NZ$45,000-55,000, mid-level NZ$55,000-70,000, and senior teachers up to NZ$90,000. However, many positions are part-time or hourly, so actual income may be lower.

**问：Can TESOL teachers migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Yes, this occupation is on Green List Tier 2. After working for 2 years, you can apply for residence, requiring employer sponsorship and a wage at or above the median (currently NZ$29.66/hour).

**问：What qualifications are required for this occupation?**
答：Typically a bachelor's degree and a TESOL certificate (e.g., CELTA). Teaching in universities or secondary schools also requires New Zealand teacher registration.
