# School Principal（School Principal）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：134311 – School Principal。**

School principals manage the overall operations of schools, including curriculum, staff, student welfare, and budget. This occupation has stable demand in New Zealand and can lead to residence via Skilled Migrant Category or Green List pathways, requiring NZ teacher registration.

---

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1（Straight to Residence Visa） | 如果职位在绿色清单一级列表（中学教师/校长），可直接申请居留，无需工作2年 |
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 满足6分制（如薪资分数+学历/工作经验），可申请居留 |
| Subclass AEWV（Accredited Employer Work Visa） | 如未直接符合绿色清单，可先持AEWV工作2年后申请居留 |
| Subclass Green List T2（Work to Residence Visa） | 如果职位在二级列表，工作2年后可申请居留（目前校长不在T2，但相关教育管理可能在） |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学School Principal？**
- 有10年以上教学经验并担任过副校长或中层管理者
- 具备领导力、决策力和跨文化沟通能力
- 愿意在新西兰偏远地区（如乡村学校）工作以获得更快移民
- 持有新西兰教师资格注册或愿意完成转换课程

**谁不适合学School Principal？**
- 无教学或学校管理经验者
- 不适应高压、长工作时间（如50小时/周）
- 缺乏教育领导专业背景或语言成绩不足（IELTS 7.0以下）

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is School Principal on the Green List?**
答：Yes, Secondary School Principal is on Tier 1 Green List, eligible for Straight to Residence. Primary School Principal may fall under education management and need SMC or AEWV.

**问：What is the average salary of a school principal in NZ?**
答：Entry-level (small schools): NZD 90,000-110,000; Mid-level: NZD 120,000-140,000; Senior (large secondary): NZD 150,000-180,000. Private schools may offer higher.

**问：Is overseas principal experience recognized in NZ?**
答：Partially. NZ Teacher Registration is mandatory. Overseas experience can be assessed by NZQA for credit, but local experience is highly valued.
