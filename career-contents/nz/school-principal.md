# School Principal — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 134311 (ANZSCO)**

The principal is responsible for the overall management of the school, including curriculum, staff, student welfare, and budget. This occupation has stable demand in New Zealand and can apply for residency through skilled migration or the Green List pathway, requiring New Zealand teacher registration.

## 6. Migration pathways / PR


| Visa | Description |
|---|---|
| Subclass Green List T1 (Straight to Residence Visa) | If the position is on Tier 1 of the Green List (secondary school teacher/principal), you can apply for residence directly without working for 2 years. |
| Subclass SMC (Skilled Migrant Category) | Meet the 6-point system (e.g., salary points + education/work experience), can apply for residency |
| Subclass AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa) | If not directly meeting the Green List, can first work on an AEWV for 2 years before applying for residency |
| Subclass Green List T2 (Work to Residence Visa) | If the position is on the secondary list, you can apply for residency after 2 years (currently principals are not on T2, but related education management may be) |

**PR friendliness: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Over 10 years of teaching experience and have served as deputy principal or middle management
- Possessing leadership, decision-making, and cross-cultural communication skills
- Willing to work in remote areas of New Zealand (e.g., rural schools) for faster immigration
- Hold New Zealand teacher registration or be willing to complete conversion courses

**Who is not suited**
- No teaching or school management experience
- Unable to handle high pressure, long working hours (e.g., 50 hours/week)
- Lack of educational leadership background or insufficient language scores (IELTS 7.0 or below)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Is the principal on New Zealand's Green List?**
A: Yes, Secondary School Principal is on the Green List Tier 1 and can directly apply for residence. Primary School Principal is usually classified under education management and may need to go through the SMC or AEWV pathway.

**Q: What is the average salary of a New Zealand principal?**
A: Entry level (small schools) approx. NZD 90,000-110,000, intermediate (medium) NZD 120,000-140,000, senior (large secondary) NZD 150,000-180,000, some private schools higher.

**Q: Is overseas principal experience recognized in New Zealand?**
A: Partial recognition, but New Zealand teacher registration is usually required, and assessment by the Teaching Council is necessary. Overseas management experience may gain points after NZQA evaluation.
