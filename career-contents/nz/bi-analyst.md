## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for a BI Analyst in NZ?**
答：Entry-level NZD 60k-80k, mid NZD 85k-110k, senior NZD 120k-150k, depending on skills.

**问：How can a BI Analyst migrate to NZ?**
答：This role is on Green List Tier 1, eligible for Straight to Residence Visa, or SMC 6-point system.

**问：What certifications are required?**
答：Power BI or Tableau certifications preferred, basic SQL/Python; no local NZ qualification needed.
