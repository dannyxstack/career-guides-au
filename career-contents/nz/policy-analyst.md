# Policy Analyst（Policy Analyst）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：224412 – Policy Analyst。**

Policy analysts in New Zealand research, develop and evaluate policies for government, think tanks and private organizations, requiring strong analytical and communication skills. This occupation is not on the Green List but can apply for immigration through the Skilled Migrant Category, requiring relevant qualifications and work experience.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary progression for a policy analyst?**
答：Junior policy analysts earn around NZ$55,000-75,000, mid-level NZ$75,000-110,000, and senior up to NZ$150,000+. Management roles offer higher pay.

**问：Can a policy analyst immigrate through the Green List?**
答：Policy analyst is not on the Green List, but can immigrate via the Skilled Migrant Category if they meet qualification and experience requirements and earn at least the median wage.

**问：What qualification is needed to become a policy analyst?**
答：A bachelor's degree in public policy, political science, economics or related field is typically required. A master's degree can improve competitiveness.
