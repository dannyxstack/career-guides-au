# Wall and Floor Tiler（Wall and Floor Tiler）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：333111 – Wall and Floor Tiler。**

Wall and Floor Tilers are in steady demand in New Zealand, a key trade in construction. For immigration, this occupation is on Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence, and also via Skilled Migrant or Accredited Employer Work Visa.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary of a wall and floor tiler in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level tilers earn around NZ$45,000-55,000 annually, experienced tilers NZ$55,000-70,000, and senior tilers NZ$70,000-90,000. Self-employed tilers can earn more.

**问：How can a Chinese wall and floor tiler immigrate to New Zealand?**
答：This occupation is on Green List Tier 1, allowing direct residence application. You need relevant qualifications or experience, and NZQA assessment. Alternatively, you can come on an AEWV and later apply for residence.

**问：Do wall and floor tilers need registration or license in New Zealand?**
答：Registration is not mandatory, but having the New Zealand Certificate in Wall and Floor Tiling (Level 4) greatly improves job and immigration prospects. A Site Safe Passport is required.
