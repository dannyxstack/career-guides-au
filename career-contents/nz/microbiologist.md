# Microbiologist（Microbiologist）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：234517 – Microbiologist。**

In New Zealand, microbiologists study bacteria, viruses and other microbes, working in healthcare, agriculture and food industries. The occupation is on the Green List, offering direct residence or Skilled Migrant Category pathways.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for microbiologists in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level: NZ$55,000–70,000; intermediate: NZ$75,000–95,000; senior: NZ$100,000–130,000 per annum.

**问：How can a microbiologist immigrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Microbiologists are on the Green List Tier 1, eligible for the Straight to Residence Visa without needing a job offer, if they meet qualification and registration requirements.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a microbiologist in New Zealand?**
答：A bachelor's degree in microbiology or related field is typically required; research roles may require a master's or PhD.
