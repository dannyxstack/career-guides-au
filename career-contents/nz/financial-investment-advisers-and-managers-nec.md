# Financial Investment Advisers and Managers nec（Financial Investment Advisers and Managers nec）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：222299 – Financial Investment Advisers and Managers nec。**

Financial analysts evaluate investment opportunities and analyze financial data in banks, investment firms, insurance, and corporate finance. This occupation is eligible for skilled migration in New Zealand via SMC (6-point system) or Green List (Tier 2), requiring a salary above the median.

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass SMC（Skilled Migrant Category） | 6分制，需中位数薪资（2024年约$55,844以上）及6分（学历+新西兰工作经验） |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What are the starting and maximum salaries for financial analysts in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level around NZD 65,000-85,000; senior roles NZD 120,000-180,000 (incl. bonuses). CFA holders earn significantly more.

**问：Can financial analysts migrate to New Zealand via the Green List?**
答：Yes, some roles like Financial Investment Adviser are on Green List Tier 2, requiring 1.5x median salary and 2 years work before residence.

**问：What are the main certifications for financial analysts in New Zealand?**
答：CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) and CA (Chartered Accountant). CFA is globally recognized for investment analysis; CA suits corporate finance.
