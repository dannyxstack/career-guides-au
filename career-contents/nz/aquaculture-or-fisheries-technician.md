# Aquaculture or Fisheries Technician（Aquaculture or Fisheries Technician）职业分析 · 新西兰

**职业代码：311114 – Aquaculture or Fisheries Technician。**

Assist with daily aquaculture farm operations, including water quality monitoring, feeding, disease control, and harvest recording. Steady demand in New Zealand, eligible for Green List or Skilled Migrant Category residence pathways.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary for an aquaculture technician in New Zealand?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn about NZD 45,000-55,000 per year, hourly rate NZD 22-27, rising to over NZD 70,000 with experience.

**问：Is aquaculture technician on the Green List for immigration?**
答：Yes, it is a Green List Tier 2 occupation. After working in New Zealand for 2 years with a salary above the median, you can apply for residence.

**问：Can I immigrate with experience but no aquaculture qualification?**
答：Yes. With over 5 years of relevant experience, you may qualify for Skilled Migrant Category, or work on an AEWV visa and later apply under the Green List.
