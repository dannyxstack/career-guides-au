# Aquaculture or Fisheries Technician — career analysis · New Zealand

**Occupation code: 311114 (ANZSCO)**

Assist with daily operations of aquaculture farms, including water quality monitoring, feeding, disease prevention, and harvest recording. New Zealand has a steady demand for aquaculture technicians, who can apply for residency via the Green List or skilled migration pathways.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Promote from junior technician to senior technician or farm manager, or develop into specialist in aquaculture disease control and water quality management. With experience, suitable for starting a business or becoming a consultant.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual water quality parameter recording and basic analysis (automated by sensors + AI)
- Regular feeding schedule and quantitative calculation (replaced by automatic feeding system + algorithm)
- Paper harvest records and report generation (automatically generated by digital systems)
- Image recognition of simple disease symptoms (replaced by AI visual inspection)
- Routine inspection of farming environment (replaced by drones/underwater robots + AI anomaly detection)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI models to predict water quality fluctuations and algal blooms, adjusting management measures in advance.
- Optimize feeding strategies and harvest timing based on real-time data and growth models
- Assisted rapid diagnosis of fish diseases and parasites through computer vision
- Automatically generate compliance reports and sustainable certification documents to improve efficiency.
- Integrating meteorological and oceanographic data with AI to optimize farming layout and risk assessment

**Human moat:**

- On-site intuitive judgment of fish behavior and health status
- Emergency decision-making ability for handling sudden environmental incidents (e.g., oxygen deficiency, disease outbreaks)
- Responsibility for communicating and coordinating with farm teams, veterinarians, and regulatory agencies
- Direct operational and supervisory responsibility for biosecurity and animal welfare
- Adjustment of farming strategies based on experience and local ecology

**Skills to build:**

- Basic data analysis and Excel/Python scripting
- Using livestock management software and IoT platforms
- Understand basic concepts of machine learning and its application in fisheries
- Drone or underwater robot operation and maintenance
- Environmental DNA (eDNA) sampling and analysis skills.
- Knowledge of sustainable aquaculture certification standards (e.g., ASC/MSC)

**Key growth areas:**

- Green List Tier 2
- Skilled Migrant Category
- Aquaculture expansion
- Sustainable aquaculture

## 6. Migration pathways / PR


| Visa | Description |
|---|---|

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys outdoor and hands-on work, interested in aquatic organisms
- Pursue a stable migration path, willing to gain experience from the grassroots

**Who is not suited**
- Dislikes dampness, cold, or physical labour
- Expecting high salary and quick returns

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the starting salary for an Aquaculture Technician in New Zealand?**
A: Junior technician annual salary about NZ$45,000-55,000, hourly NZ$22-27, can reach over NZ$70,000 with experience.

**Q: Do aquaculture technicians meet Green List immigration requirements?**
A: This is a Green List Tier 2 occupation; after working in New Zealand for 2 years with a salary above the median, you can apply for a work-to-residence visa.

**Q: No aquaculture degree but rich experience – can I immigrate?**
A: Yes. With over 5 years of relevant work experience, can apply for skilled migration, or work under employer sponsorship on an AEWV visa to gain experience before transitioning to the Green List.
