# Assemblers Not Elsewhere Classified（Assemblers Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：8219 – Assemblers Not Elsewhere Classified。**

This occupation covers various assembly tasks not elsewhere classified, involving electronics, machinery, metal products, and other manufacturing sectors. Workers assemble components into finished products according to drawings and procedures, and perform basic testing and inspection.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Kennismigrant） | 适用于高技术移民，但装配工通常不满足薪资门槛（2025年需至少5,688欧元/月），除非晋升至管理岗。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year  Zoekjaar） | 适用于荷兰毕业的国际学生，可在一年内寻找工作，但装配工岗位对技能要求不高，签证转换难度较大。 |
| Subclass EU自由流动（EU/EEA Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟/欧洲经济区公民可自由在荷兰工作，无需工作许可。装配工岗位对语言要求较低，适合欧盟劳动力。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for assemblers in the Netherlands?**
答：Junior assemblers earn around €26,000-32,000 annually, mid-level €32,000-38,000, and senior €38,000-45,000. Salaries vary by industry, experience, and region.

**问：How can non-EU citizens work as assemblers in the Netherlands?**
答：Low-skilled assemblers typically cannot obtain a work visa as wages fall below the Highly Skilled Migrant threshold. EU citizens are free to work; non-EU citizens need employer sponsorship and a labor market test.

**问：What career progression is available for assemblers?**
答：They can advance to senior assembler, team leader, or quality inspector; with training, move into technical sales, equipment maintenance, or production management.
