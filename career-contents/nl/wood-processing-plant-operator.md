# Wood Processing Plant Operator（Wood Processing Plant Operator）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：8172 – Wood Processing Plant Operator。**

Operate wood processing machinery such as saws, planers, and sanders to convert logs into lumber or panels, ensuring efficient and safe production.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于年收入高于阈值（2024年约€4,684/月）的技术工人，需雇主担保 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等职业以上学历，薪资门槛约€5,331/月（2024年），需雇主担保 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year (Zoekjaar)） | 荷兰或世界前200高校毕业生可在毕业后一年内申请，自由求职 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for a Wood Processing Plant Operator?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €26,000 to €32,000, mid-level €32,000-€38,000, senior up to €45,000, typically including 8% holiday allowance.

**问：What are common migration paths for this occupation to the Netherlands?**
答：Mainly through Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant) or EU Blue Card, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. Graduates from Dutch universities can use the Orientation Year visa.

**问：Are there advancement opportunities for this occupation?**
答：Yes, can advance to production supervisor, quality controller, or maintenance technician; further training in automation leads to senior technical roles.
