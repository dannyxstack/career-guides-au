# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

In the Netherlands, drafters use CAD software to create technical drawings for architecture, engineering, and manufacturing projects, serving as key technical support in design teams.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Draftspersons can advance from junior draftsperson to senior draftsperson, project coordinator, or design manager. By obtaining professional certifications (e.g., AutoCAD, Revit) and accumulating industry experience, they can move into more specialized areas such as Building Information Modeling (BIM).

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repetitive 2D/3D standard part drawing
- Automatic dimensioning and annotation based on specification parameters
- Automated building model generation from point cloud data
- Automatic compliance checking of construction drawings (e.g., clash detection)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI for rapid iteration and comparison of multiple solutions
- Generate complex curved surfaces or topologically optimized structures with AI assistance
- Multi-party collaborative review and annotation on an AI real-time collaboration platform
- AI-driven automatic generation of bill of materials and cost optimization

**Human moat:**

- Understanding the trade-offs between design intent and engineering specifications
- Handle customized drawing requirements for non-standard and special working conditions
- Cross-disciplinary communication and coordination (structure, mechanical, electrical, HVAC, etc.)
- Final review responsibility for drawing safety, compliance, and constructability

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced application of BIM software (Revit, Navisworks)
- AI-assisted design tools (e.g., Autodesk Forma, Dall-E generative concept diagrams)
- Basic programming (Python script for automating repetitive tasks)
- Engineering coordination and project management skills
- Knowledge of sustainable design and green building standards
- Point cloud processing and reverse engineering skills

**Key growth areas:**

- BIM
- CAD
- Sustainable Design
- Infrastructure

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are detail-oriented, patient, and good at technical communication
- People with a strong interest in the construction, engineering, and manufacturing industries
- People who enjoy using software tools for precise drafting

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike long hours in front of screens and repetitive work
- Individuals lacking spatial imagination and mathematical foundations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of drafters?**
A: Junior drafter annual salary approx. €28,000-€35,000, mid-level €35,000-€45,000, senior €45,000-€60,000, typically including an 8% holiday allowance.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens migrate to the Netherlands as a drafter?**
A: Eligible via high-skilled migration visa (requires employer sponsorship and meeting salary threshold) or EU Blue Card. Recent graduates can first apply for a job-seeking visa to look for work.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a drafter?**
A: Typically requires an MBO (Intermediate Vocational Education) drafting diploma or an HBO (Higher Professional Education) engineering technology bachelor's degree; CAD certification (e.g., AutoCAD) is also an important plus.
