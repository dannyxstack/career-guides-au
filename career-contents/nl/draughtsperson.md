# Draughtsperson（Draughtsperson）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3118 – Draughtsperson。**

In the Netherlands, draughtspersons use CAD software to create technical drawings for construction, engineering, and manufacturing projects, acting as key technical support within design teams.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Draughtsperson？**
- 注重细节、有耐心、善于技术沟通的人
- 对建筑、工程和制造行业有浓厚兴趣的人
- 喜欢使用软件工具进行精确制图的人

**谁不适合学Draughtsperson？**
- 不喜欢长时间面对电脑和重复性工作的人
- 缺乏空间想象力和数学基础的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for draughtspersons?**
答：Entry-level draughtspersons earn €28,000-€35,000, mid-level €35,000-€45,000, and senior €45,000-€60,000 per year, typically including 8% holiday allowance.

**问：How can non-EU nationals migrate to the Netherlands as a draughtsperson?**
答：They can apply for a Highly Skilled Migrant visa (employer-sponsored with salary threshold) or EU Blue Card. Graduates can first obtain an Orientation Year visa to search for a job.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become a draughtsperson?**
答：Typically an MBO diploma in drafting or a HBO bachelor's in engineering technology, plus CAD certifications (e.g., AutoCAD) are highly valued.
