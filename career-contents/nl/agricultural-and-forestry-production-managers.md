# Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers（Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：1311 – Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers。**

Plan, organize, and manage agricultural, horticultural, or forestry production activities, including crop cultivation, livestock farming, or forest management, ensuring efficiency and sustainability.

**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于年薪达到门槛的高技能人才，需获得认可雇主担保，门槛约为€5,000/月（30岁以上）。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等教育背景且年薪达到€58,400以上的非欧盟公民，可快速获得居留权。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year） | 荷兰大学毕业或全球前200高校硕士毕业生可申请为期一年的求职签证，寻找高技能工作。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers?**
答：The average annual salary in the Netherlands ranges from €45,000 to €60,000, with entry-level around €35,000 and senior roles exceeding €80,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to work in this occupation?**
答：Via the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme or EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer from a recognized sponsor and meeting salary thresholds.

**问：What education is required for this occupation?**
答：A bachelor's degree in agriculture, forestry, or a related field from a university of applied sciences (HBO) or research university (WO) is typically required.
