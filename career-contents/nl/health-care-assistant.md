# Health Care Assistant（Health Care Assistant）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：5321 – Health Care Assistant。**

In the Netherlands, Health Care Assistants support registered nurses by providing basic care, personal hygiene, and daily living assistance in hospitals, nursing homes, and home care settings, playing a vital role in the long-term care system.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于获得工作合同且薪资达到门槛的高技能移民，但护理助理通常薪资较低，需雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 要求高等教育学历及高于平均水平的薪资，护理助理较难满足。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year (Zoekjaar)） | 在荷兰完成学位的毕业生可申请一年找工作时间，找到工作后转换工作签证。 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

- 愿意从事轮班工作，能承受一定体力劳动

- 不善于团队协作和多任务处理的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Health Care Assistants?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €22,000 to €26,000, mid-level €26,000 to €31,000, and senior €31,000 to €37,000, often including shift allowances.

**问：How can foreigners migrate to the Netherlands as a Health Care Assistant?**
答：Most come through employer-sponsored Highly Skilled Migrant visa, but salary thresholds are high; alternatively, study MBO program and use Orientation Year visa to find a job.

**问：What certifications are required?**
答：Typically an MBO diploma in care assistance, and some employers require BIG registration and first aid certificate.
