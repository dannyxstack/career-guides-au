# Receptionist (General)（Receptionist (General)）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：4226 – Receptionist (General)。**

Handles visitor greeting, phone calls, mail, and basic administrative tasks, serving as the face of an organization.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant Permit） | 适用于年薪达标的高技能移民，前台通常不满足薪资门槛。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需年薪高于荷兰平均工资（约5.6万欧元），前台薪资不达标。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year Permit） | 在荷毕业的留学生可申请一年找工作时间，可从事前台工作。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a receptionist in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level: €24,000-€28,000; mid-level: €28,000-€34,000; senior: €34,000-€40,000 (gross annual, incl. holiday allowance).

**问：How can a foreigner become a receptionist in the Netherlands?**
答：Obtain a work visa; however, the salary typically does not meet the Highly Skilled Migrant threshold. Options include the Orientation Year permit or employer sponsorship after learning Dutch.

**问：What are the career progression opportunities for receptionists?**
答：Advance to administrative supervisor, office manager, customer service manager, or pivot to HR/marketing departments.
