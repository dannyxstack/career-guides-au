# Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmer（Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmer）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：6330 – Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmer。**

Engages in subsistence mixed farming, growing crops and raising livestock primarily for own consumption, with occasional surplus sold.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmer？**
- 热爱传统农业和自给自足生活方式的人
- 希望避免商业压力、追求田园生活的人

**谁不适合学Subsistence Mixed Crop and Livestock Farmer？**
- 追求高收入和职业发展的人
- 对农业商业化、规模化缺乏兴趣的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：How much can a subsistence farmer earn in the Netherlands?**
答：Income is very volatile, typically below minimum wage, around €15,000-€25,000 annually, mainly from small sales and subsidies.

**问：Can non-EU citizens become subsistence farmers in the Netherlands?**
答：Very difficult. Non-EU citizens need a self-employment visa and must demonstrate substantial economic contribution, which subsistence farming typically does not meet.
