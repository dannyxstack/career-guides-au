# Commissioned Armed Forces Officers — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 0110 (ISCO08)**

Serve in command, management, and professional technical roles in the Dutch armed forces, responsible for leading military operations, personnel training, and strategic planning.

**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for military officers?**
A: Junior officers earn approximately €38,000-48,000 per year, while senior officers can earn up to €120,000, including various allowances.

**Q: Can non-Dutch citizens become officers in the Netherlands?**
A: No. Officer positions are limited to Dutch citizens and require passing a strict security clearance.

**Q: Is it easy for military officers to obtain permanent residency in the Netherlands?**
A: Officers as civil servants can apply after 5 years of service, but must maintain citizenship. Non-citizens do not have this pathway.
