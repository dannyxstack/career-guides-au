# Building Caretaker（Building Caretaker）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：5153 – Building Caretaker。**

Responsible for daily maintenance, cleaning, minor repairs, and tenant services to ensure safe and orderly operation of buildings.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★☆☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需高等教育且薪资达标，但此职业通常不符合蓝卡薪资要求。 |
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需薪资达到门槛，此职业通常不易满足。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year） | 适用于在荷兰毕业的留学生，可求职一年，找到工作后可转为工作居留。 |
| Subclass EU Freedom of Moveme（EU/EEA Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟/欧洲经济区公民可自由在荷兰工作，无需额外签证。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for building caretakers?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is about €24,000-30,000, mid-level €30,000-37,000, and senior €35,000-43,000 (pre-tax).

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate as building caretakers to the Netherlands?**
答：This occupation usually does not meet the salary threshold for highly skilled migration. EU citizens can move freely; non-EU citizens need other routes like family reunification or refugee status.

**问：What certifications are required to work as a building caretaker?**
答：No mandatory requirements, but an MBO diploma or SVGB certification improves job prospects.
