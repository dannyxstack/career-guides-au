# Vocational Education Teacher（Vocational Education Teacher）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2320 – Vocational Education Teacher。**

Vocational Education Teachers instruct students in trade, technical, or professional subjects at secondary or post-secondary level, combining theory with practical skills.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于从非欧盟招聘的专业教师，需与认可担保机构签订合同，薪资需达到最低标准（2024年约€5,008/月）。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合高技能非欧盟教师，需高等教育学历及高薪（至少€5,331/月），可携家属并快速获得欧盟长居。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year for Graduates） | 荷兰毕业生或全球前200大学毕业者可申请1年求职签证，找到教师工作后可转为工作居留。 |
| Subclass EU Freedom of Moveme（EU/EEA Freedom of Movement） | 欧盟/瑞士公民可自由在荷兰工作，无需签证，直接注册即可。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for vocational education teachers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level teachers earn around €30,000-€38,000 annually, mid-level €38,000-€48,000, and senior up to €60,000. Salaries vary by education level and region.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa as a vocational education teacher in the Netherlands?**
答：Apply for a Highly Skilled Migrant visa or EU Blue Card with a job offer from a recognized sponsor meeting the salary threshold. The Orientation Year visa is also an option for graduates.

**问：What qualifications are required to become a vocational education teacher?**
答：You need a second-degree or first-degree teaching qualification, plus relevant work experience. Foreign qualifications must be recognized via NLQF or supplemented with pedagogical training.
