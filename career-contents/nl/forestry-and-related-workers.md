# Forestry and Related Workers（Forestry and Related Workers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：6210 – Forestry and Related Workers。**

Perform manual and technical tasks in forest planting, maintenance, harvesting, and timber transport, including reforestation, thinning, logging, and forest road upkeep.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant)） | 需符合薪资门槛（2025年约€5,239/月），通常需高等教育背景，本职业不符合 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需高等教育及高薪，本职业不符合 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year (zoekjaar)） | 面向全球毕业生，允许在荷兰居留一年找工作，但不直接适用于本职业 |
| Subclass Seasonal Work（Seasonal Labour Visa） | 适用于短期林业季节工，需雇主担保，最长6个月 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Forestry and Related Workers？**
- 喜欢户外体力劳动，不介意天气变化
- 动手能力强，愿意操作机械设备
- 注重生态保护，愿意从事可持续林业

**谁不适合学Forestry and Related Workers？**
- 不适应体力劳动和户外艰苦环境
- 寻求高薪或快速职业晋升者

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for forestry workers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €22,000 to €28,000, mid-level €28,000-€35,000, and senior up to €42,000, below national average.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to the Netherlands through forestry work?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation does not meet requirements for Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card; only seasonal work visa possible, which does not lead to permanent residence.

**问：What certifications are needed to become a forestry worker?**
答：Chainsaw operation certificate and forestry machinery certificate are legally required; first aid and general forestry worker certificate are recommended.
