# Typist — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 4131 (ISCO08)**

Typists are responsible for accurately and quickly entering and editing documents based on audio recordings, handwritten notes, or verbal instructions using word processing software. This occupation is common in administrative, legal, medical, and other industries, but demand is declining due to automation.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: low (★½☆☆☆).** National workforce approx. 8,000. The job outlook for typists in the Netherlands continues to shrink, with automated speech recognition and AI tools replacing much manual data entry. The number of positions is expected to decrease by about 5% annually over the next five years. Existing jobs are concentrated in specialized fields requiring low speed and high accuracy (e.g., legal or medical transcription). It is recommended that practitioners transition to more comprehensive administrative roles or digital document management.

**Competition: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**Work intensity: medium (★★½☆☆).**

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Junior typists can advance to senior typists or word processing supervisors, or transition to roles such as administrative assistant, secretary, or data entry. Obtaining certificates in business English or medical terminology can enhance competitiveness. Some typists transition into legal assistants or medical coders by accumulating industry knowledge.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Transcribe audio recordings into text documents
- Transcribe handwritten or printed documents
- Create standard format letters/reports from verbal instructions
- Basic text proofreading and formatting adjustments
- Data entry into tables/databases

**Where AI augments the role:**

- After speech recognition, AI automatically generates a first draft, with typists only performing final quality checks.
- AI-assisted real-time caption generation, with typists correcting complex terminology.
- AI provides grammar and spelling suggestions to expedite final editing
- Quickly template repetitive documents using AI tools

**Human moat:**

- Understanding terminology in highly specialized fields (e.g., medicine, law)
- Processing audio with heavy accents or poor recording quality
- Contextual editing of documents (e.g., adjusting tone, style).
- Complex non-standard or table-based formatting

**Skills to build:**

- Medical/Legal Terminology Knowledge
- Advanced shorthand (e.g., 220+ words per minute)
- AI text processing tool operation (e.g., Otter.ai, Rev.com)
- Basic editing and proofreading skills
- Voice engine quality evaluation and calibration
- Multilingual dictation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Declining demand
- Automation
- Voice recognition
- Administrative consolidation

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

As an EU member state, Netherlands admits skilled foreign professionals mainly through the EU Blue Card and national work-permit routes. Eligibility depends on your qualifications, a qualifying job offer and salary threshold — not on any single occupation. Rules and salary thresholds change yearly, so always check the official source before making plans.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who are fast, accurate, and careful typists
- Those who don't mind repetitive office work, seek stability, but don't require higher education
- Those seeking part-time or remote work

**Who is not suited**
- Those pursuing high salary or long-term career development
- Those looking to avoid the risk of automation replacing jobs

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a typist in the Netherlands?**
A: Junior typist annual salary approx €22,000-€28,000, intermediate approx €28,000-€35,000, senior up to €42,000. Salary includes 8% holiday allowance but overall is a low-paid occupation.

**Q: Can foreigners apply for a work visa as a typist in the Netherlands?**
A: Extremely difficult. Typist is not a high-skilled shortage occupation and the salary is below the high-skilled migration threshold (€5,008/month). Unless you can obtain company sponsorship and meet other conditions, it is nearly impossible to get a work permit.

**Q: What are the career advancement paths for typists?**
A: Can advance to senior typist, administrative assistant, or office manager. By gaining professional certifications (e.g., in legal or medical terminology), can transition to specialist transcription or data management roles.
