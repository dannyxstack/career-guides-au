# Dairy Products Maker（Dairy Products Maker）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7513 – Dairy Products Maker。**

Operates machinery to produce cheese, butter, yogurt and other dairy products, handling processing, fermentation, packaging and quality control.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Dairy Products Makers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €24,000 to €30,000, mid-level €30,000 to €38,000, senior €38,000 to €45,000. Below national average but with good benefits.

**问：Can non-EU citizens apply for a visa as a Dairy Products Maker?**
答：Salaries are usually below the Highly Skilled Migrant threshold, but EU Blue Card (managerial salary required) or targeted recruitment schemes may apply. Partnership or refugee status more common.

**问：What is the career path for a Dairy Products Maker?**
答：Can advance to production supervisor, quality control technician, or R&D assistant, some move into management. Requires continuous learning of automation and food safety regulations.
