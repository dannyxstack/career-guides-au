# Car, Taxi and Van Drivers（Car, Taxi and Van Drivers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：8322 – Car, Taxi and Van Drivers。**

Drive cars, taxis, or light vans to transport passengers or goods, playing a key role in urban mobility and local delivery in the Netherlands.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：低（★★☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于高收入司机，如物流公司高管或专车司机，需满足工资门槛 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于非欧盟高技能司机，需大学学历和高薪，通常不适用于普通司机 |
| Subclass Self-employed visa（Self-Employed Residence Permit） | 适用于自营出租车或货运司机，需通过点数评估 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for car/taxi/van drivers?**
答：Entry-level drivers earn €25,000-€30,000, while experienced drivers can make €38,000-€45,000 per year, depending on the type of work and hours.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as a driver?**
答：It is challenging for regular drivers; possible options include self-employment visa or highly skilled migrant (e.g., for fleet managers). Language and license requirements apply.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for drivers?**
答：Drivers can advance to fleet dispatcher, transport manager, or start their own business with multiple vehicles.
