# Domestic Housekeeper — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 5152 (ISCO08)**

Housekeeping staff are responsible for cleaning, organizing, and daily maintenance in private residences or hotels, ensuring a tidy and orderly environment.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Junior housekeepers can gain experience and advance to senior butlers or housekeeping supervisors, or transition to hotel cleaning management or start their own business.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Regular vacuuming and mopping of floors (replaced by robot vacuum cleaners)
- Window cleaning (replaced by window cleaning robots)
- Clothing folding and storage (replaced by automatic folding machines)
- Simple item placement and organization (smart system scheduling)
- Basic disinfection and dust mite removal (automated equipment)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use app to dispatch multiple cleaning robots for collaborative work
- Use voice assistants to record customer preferences and create cleaning plans
- Monitor and adjust indoor air quality using smart sensors
- Use online platforms to manage appointments, payments, and customer feedback
- Operate specialized equipment (e.g., steam cleaners) for deep cleaning

**Human moat:**

- Emotional companionship and psychological support
- Handling complex unexpected situations (e.g., breaking an antique vase)
- Personalized services (e.g., care for special fabric garments)
- Sensitive safety environments (e.g., caring for elderly living alone)
- Social etiquette and customer relationship maintenance

**Skills to build:**

- Smart home device operation and basic maintenance
- Use of cleaning management software (e.g., Cleantelligent).
- First Aid and Safety Awareness Certification
- Knowledge of high-end fabrics and item care.
- English or local language communication skills
- Social Media and Online Marketing Basics

**Key growth areas:**

- Aging population
- Dual-income households
- Self-employment
- Cleaning technology

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a domestic worker in the Netherlands?**
A: Full-time pre-tax annual salary approx. €25,000-30,000, part-time charged hourly, typically €10-15 per hour.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as domestic workers?**
A: Domestic service workers are not classified as highly skilled occupations and are generally ineligible for the EU Blue Card; however, they may work legally through self-employment visas or family reunion visas.

**Q: Is there room for advancement for domestic workers in the Netherlands?**
A: Advance to butler or housekeeping supervisor through experience, or expand into hotel cleaning management or start a cleaning company.
