# Domestic Housekeeper（Domestic Housekeeper）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：5152 – Domestic Housekeeper。**

Domestic housekeepers perform cleaning, organizing, and routine maintenance tasks in private homes or hospitality settings to ensure a tidy and orderly environment.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a domestic housekeeper in the Netherlands?**
答：Full-time pre-tax annual salary is around €25,000-30,000, while part-time workers are paid hourly, typically €10-15 per hour.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as a domestic housekeeper?**
答：Domestic housekeeping is not a highly skilled occupation and does not qualify for the EU Blue Card; however, self-employment or family reunification visas may allow work.

**问：Are there career advancement opportunities for domestic housekeepers in the Netherlands?**
答：With experience, one can become a butler or housekeeping supervisor, or transition into hotel cleaning management or start a cleaning business.
