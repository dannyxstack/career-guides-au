# Generalist Medical Practitioner（Generalist Medical Practitioner）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2211 – Generalist Medical Practitioner。**

Generalist Medical Practitioners are the cornerstone of Dutch primary care, providing first-contact diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and health management in community practices and hospital outpatient settings.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Generalist Medical Practitioner？**
- 具备出色沟通和同理心，善于与各类患者交流
- 能承受工作压力，适应不规律工作时间和紧急情况
- 对持续学习和更新医学知识保持热情

**谁不适合学Generalist Medical Practitioner？**
- 讨厌与人面对面交流，偏好独立工作
- 无法接受高强度的责任和突发状况

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a general practitioner in the Netherlands?**
答：Self-employed GPs earn a pre-tax average of €80,000–€130,000 annually, depending on patient volume, contract type, and location.

**问：How can foreign doctors obtain a license to practice in the Netherlands?**
答：Foreign doctors must apply for BIG registration, prove Dutch language proficiency (NT2 or higher), and may need to complete additional training or internships before entering GP specialization.

**问：What are the career progression paths for a GP?**
答：GPs can progress from junior to senior partner or practice owner, move into teaching or research, or transition into public health management or policy.
