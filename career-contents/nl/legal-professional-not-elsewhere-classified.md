# Legal Professional Not Elsewhere Classified（Legal Professional Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2619 – Legal Professional Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Includes legal assistants, advisors, and other legal professionals not elsewhere classified, handling legal research, contract drafting, and compliance advisory in the Netherlands.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：较高（★★★½☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于年薪达门槛的技术移民，需雇主担保，可带家属 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高学历高薪职位，年薪需高于约€60,000，快速居留路径 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year） | 适用于荷兰高校毕业生或全球前200大学毕业生，一年内求职 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for legal professionals in the Netherlands?**
答：Junior: €35k-€45k, mid-level: €50k-€70k, senior: €75k-€100k (gross annual), varying by experience, firm size, and specialization.

**问：How can foreign legal professionals immigrate to the Netherlands?**
答：Via the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship; a Dutch law degree and Dutch language skills are advantageous.

**问：What are the career paths for legal professionals in the Netherlands?**
答：From legal assistant to senior advisor or partner, or specialize in corporate compliance, intellectual property, etc.
