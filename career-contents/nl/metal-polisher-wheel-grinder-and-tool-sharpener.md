# Metal Polisher, Wheel Grinder and Tool Sharpener（Metal Polisher, Wheel Grinder and Tool Sharpener）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7224 – Metal Polisher, Wheel Grinder and Tool Sharpener。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for metal polishers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges EUR 24,000-28,000, mid-level EUR 30,000-36,000, senior EUR 36,000-44,000, depending on skills and industry.

**问：Is it easy to migrate to the Netherlands as a metal polisher?**
答：Difficult. The Highly Skilled Migrant scheme requires a salary above approx. EUR 44,000, which this occupation typically does not meet. EU free movement applies; non-EU candidates need employer sponsorship but often fail the salary threshold.

**问：What education is required for this occupation?**
答：Typically requires MBO (secondary vocational education) or apprenticeship in metalworking. VCA safety certificate is a plus.
