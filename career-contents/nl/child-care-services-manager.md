# Child Care Services Manager（Child Care Services Manager）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：1341 – Child Care Services Manager。**

Manages daily operations of child care centers, preschools, or after-school programs, including staff management, curriculum development, budgeting, and regulatory compliance.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for Child Care Services Managers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level managers earn €30k-€38k, mid-level €38k-€48k, and senior up to €60k per year, including holiday allowance.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
答：Via the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme (employer-sponsored, salary threshold ~€60k/year) or EU Blue Card (€56.8k/year).

**问：What education is required to become a Child Care Services Manager?**
答：Typically a 4-year HBO bachelor's degree in childcare management, with tuition fees around €2000-€4000 per year.
