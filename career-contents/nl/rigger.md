# Rigger（Rigger）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7215 – Rigger。**

Installs, adjusts, and maintains lifting equipment, cables, and rigging systems for construction, maritime, and industrial settings.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Rigger？**
- 喜欢体力活动和户外工作的人
- 注重安全规范、动手能力强的人
- 愿意接受高空、海上等特殊环境的人

**谁不适合学Rigger？**
- 畏惧高空或对体力要求敏感的人
- 偏好办公室或低风险环境的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for riggers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level earns €28,000-35,000, senior up to €55,000 per year, plus overtime and allowances.

**问：Can foreign riggers migrate to the Netherlands via highly skilled migrant scheme?**
答：Yes, if salary meets thresholds (€31,891-€45,715 in 2024), employer sponsorship required.
