# Lawyer（Lawyer）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2611 – Lawyer。**

Lawyers in the Netherlands provide legal advice and representation, primarily employed by law firms, corporations, and government agencies. Fluency in Dutch and solid knowledge of Dutch law are required.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant Permit） | 适用于受雇于认可担保人的高技能移民，需满足薪资门槛（2024年约5,331欧元/月，30岁以上） |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需合同至少1年且薪资达门槛（2024年约5,331欧元/月） |
| Subclass Orientation Year (zo（Orientation Year for Graduates） | 针对荷兰或世界top200高校毕业生，可在毕业后1年内求职，有效期1年 |
| Subclass Self-employed (zzp)（Self-employed Residence Permit） | 适用于作为独立律师（advocaat-zzp）工作，需通过荷兰经济事务部评估 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：How can a non-EU lawyer practice in the Netherlands?**
答：Non-EU lawyers must pass an assessment by the Dutch Bar (NOvA), possibly take additional Dutch law courses, pass a Dutch language test, and complete a 3-year traineeship.

**问：What is the average salary of a lawyer in the Netherlands?**
答：Junior lawyers earn around €45,000-60,000; mid-level €60,000-90,000; senior up to €150,000+, with partners earning more.

**问：What is the career path for a lawyer in the Netherlands?**
答：Typically starts as a trainee lawyer, completes a 3-year traineeship to become a registered lawyer, then advances to senior lawyer, salary partner, or equity partner.
