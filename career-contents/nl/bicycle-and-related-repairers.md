# Bicycle and Related Repairers（Bicycle and Related Repairers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7234 – Bicycle and Related Repairers。**

Responsible for repairing and maintaining bicycles, e-bikes, and related equipment, including adjusting brakes, chains, tires, and gear systems. In the Netherlands, bicycles are a primary mode of transport, ensuring steady demand.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |

| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant Scheme） | 适用于有雇主担保的高技能移民，但该职业通常不符合薪资门槛（2025年最低约€3,600/月）。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for bicycle repairers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level repairers earn €25,000-30,000 annually, while senior technicians can earn up to €38,000-45,000. Salaries vary by location and experience, with slightly higher pay in cities like Amsterdam.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as bicycle repairers?**
答：This occupation generally does not meet the salary threshold for the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme, making it mainly accessible to EU citizens. Non-EU immigrants need alternative routes like family reunification or investment visas.

**问：How to become a bicycle repairer in the Netherlands?**
答：Typically through MBO vocational training (3 years) or on-the-job apprenticeship. Courses cover mechanical and electrical repair, and some employers offer in-house training. Certification is not mandatory but helpful.
