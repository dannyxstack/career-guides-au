# Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver（Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7514 – Fruit, Vegetable and Related Preserver。**

Responsible for cleaning, sorting, pickling, and canning fruits, vegetables, and related products, ensuring food safety and quality in the Netherlands' food processing industry.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant)） | 适用于高技能移民，但对学历和薪资有要求，此职业通常薪资水平不满足门槛，较难申请 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需高学历和高薪资，此职业一般不适用 |
| Subclass Orientation Year (Zo（Orientation Year） | 适用于在荷兰毕业的留学生寻找工作，但此职业非高技能岗位，可能不易利用该签证 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for fruit and vegetable preservers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level positions earn about €24,000-€30,000 per year, while experienced workers can earn over €38,000. Salaries vary by region, factory size, and experience.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation in the Netherlands?**
答：This occupation usually does not meet the salary threshold for highly skilled migrants and is not in shortage, making immigration difficult. EU citizens have free movement; non-EU citizens need other routes.

**问：What are the future prospects for this occupation?**
答：The role faces automation, but demand for artisanal and organic products may offer opportunities. Career advancement is limited but possible into quality control or production management.
