# Bricklayer（Bricklayer）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7112 – Bricklayer。**

Bricklayers construct walls, chimneys, arches and other structures using bricks, concrete blocks and similar materials. They prepare materials, mix mortar, lay bricks to precise specifications and ensure structural alignment.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能非欧盟人才，年薪门槛约€56,000（2024年），砌砖工通常达不到，需雇主证明紧缺 |
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant)） | 雇主担保高技术移民，年薪门槛随年龄调整（2024年30岁以上约€6,000/月），砌砖工较少适用 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year (Zoekjaar)） | 荷兰毕业非欧盟学生可申请一年求职签证，砌砖工MBO毕业生可用此路径找工作 |
| Subclass EU自由流动（EU Free Movement） | 欧盟/EEA公民无需工作许可，可直接在荷兰从事砌砖工，非欧盟公民需雇主担保 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a bricklayer in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level bricklayers earn €28,000-€34,000, mid-level €35,000-€42,000, and senior bricklayers can earn up to €52,000 or more. Salaries vary by experience, region and project type.

**问：How can a non-EU citizen get a work visa as a bricklayer in the Netherlands?**
答：Usually requires employer sponsorship under the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme, but salaries are often below threshold. Alternatives include EU Blue Card (high salary requirement) or Orientation Year visa for graduates, then switching to a regular work permit.

**问：What are the future prospects for bricklayers in the Netherlands?**
答：Positive. Housing shortages and renovation demand drive employment, with government plans for 100,000 new homes annually. Sustainable construction (e.g., energy-efficient blocks) creates new opportunities. Watch for economic cycles and material costs.
