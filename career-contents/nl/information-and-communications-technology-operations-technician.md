# Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician（Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3511 – Information and Communications Technology Operations Technician。**

Monitor, maintain and optimize ICT systems daily operations, ensuring high availability and security of networks, servers and databases.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于年薪超过€3,909/月（30岁以下）或€5,008/月（30岁以上）的技术人才，由雇主担保。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于年薪不低于€5,331/月的高技能非欧盟人才，工作满18个月可自由流动至其他欧盟国家。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year (Zoekjaar)） | 适用于在荷兰完成学位的毕业生，给予一年时间寻找工作，找到工作后可转为工作居留。 |

**PR难度：低（★½☆☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for an ICT operations technician in the Netherlands?**
答：Junior: €30k-€42k, Mid: €42k-€55k, Senior: €55k-€75k (gross annual).

**问：How can non-EU nationals immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
答：Via the Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant) or EU Blue Card route, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds.

**问：Is there career advancement potential for this occupation?**
答：Yes, to senior engineer, system architect, IT manager, or transition to DevOps/cloud architect.
