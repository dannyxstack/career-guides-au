# Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers（Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7114 – Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers。**

Place, finish, and repair concrete surfaces in construction projects, ensuring proper leveling, texture, and curing.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers？**
- 喜欢户外体力劳动、动手能力强的人
- 注重细节、有耐心完成抹平和修整工作
- 愿意通过学徒制学习并接受在岗培训

**谁不适合学Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers？**
- 对粉尘、噪音和寒冷/炎热天气敏感者
- 偏好室内办公或不喜重复性体力劳动

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for concrete workers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level: €25,000-32,000; mid-level: €32,000-40,000; senior: up to €50,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can non-EU citizens get a work visa for concrete work in the Netherlands?**
答：Find a Dutch employer to sponsor a Highly Skilled Migrant visa, or meet EU Blue Card requirements (high salary + degree).

**问：Is Dutch language required for work?**
答：Basic Dutch (A2/B1) is often needed for safety communication, but English may suffice on international projects.
