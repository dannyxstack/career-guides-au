# Construction Manager（Construction Manager）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：1323 – Construction Manager。**

Oversees construction projects from planning to completion, managing budgets, schedules, safety, and team coordination to ensure high-quality delivery within deadlines.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Construction Manager？**
- 具备工程或建筑背景，组织协调能力强
- 能承受高压工作，喜欢户外与现场管理
- 熟悉项目管理软件和建筑法规

**谁不适合学Construction Manager？**
- 不善于处理复杂人际沟通
- 讨厌不确定性或频繁变更的日程

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for construction managers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level around €40,000-€55,000, mid-level €55,000-€75,000, senior up to €100,000+, usually including holiday allowance and bonuses.

**问：How can a foreigner work as a construction manager in the Netherlands?**
答：Via Highly Skilled Migrant visa or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship and salary threshold. Graduates from Dutch or top 200 global universities can apply for Orientation Year visa first.

**问：What is the career path for a construction manager?**
答：Typically starts as site supervisor or foreman, advances to project manager, then senior PM or director, with options to become a consultant or start own business.
