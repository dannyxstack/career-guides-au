# Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional（Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3230 – Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional。**

In the Netherlands, Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals assist in providing non-mainstream healthcare such as acupuncture, herbal medicine, or chiropractic. They typically work in integrative clinics or private practices, adhering to industry standards.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €24,000 to €30,000, mid-level €30,000-€40,000, and senior up to €55,000 annually. Pay depends on experience, location, and employment type.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to the Netherlands for this profession?**
答：Options include the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme (salary threshold approx. €4,500/month) or EU Blue Card (annual salary approx. €58,000). Graduates of Dutch universities can apply for the Orientation Year visa.

**问：What educational background is required for this profession?**
答：A relevant MBO (secondary vocational) or HBO (applied sciences bachelor) degree, e.g., in massage or acupuncture, is typical. Certification is not mandatory but beneficial.
