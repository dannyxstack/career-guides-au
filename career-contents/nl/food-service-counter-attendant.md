# Food Service Counter Attendant — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 5246 (ISCO08)**

Taking orders, serving, and handling payments at food service counters, common in fast-food restaurants, cafes, and canteens. Duties include preparing simple food, cleaning, and maintaining counter areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Entry-level positions can advance to supervisor or store manager, or transition to food and beverage management or hospitality. With experience, can apply for higher-level food service positions.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Receiving customer orders via self-service kiosks or mobile apps
- Handle standardized cashier and change transactions
- Generating and passing simple orders (e.g., pre-packaged food)
- Answering common questions (e.g., business hours, menu prices).

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI recommendation system assists personalized ordering suggestions and meal combinations.
- Real-time inventory management systems help remind about restocking and expiration dates
- AI-driven dynamic pricing and promotional suggestions.
- Smart scheduling system optimizes staff allocation

**Human moat:**

- Handling complex or special order requests (allergies, customizations).
- Provide personalised service (smiling, handling complaints, building customer relationships)
- Coordinate multiple tasks and customer demands during busy periods
- Maintain a clean and hygienic physical work environment

**Skills to build:**

- Troubleshooting digital ordering systems and POS systems
- Basic data analysis and inventory management software operation
- Interpersonal communication and conflict resolution skills
- Food safety and allergen management knowledge
- Multitasking coordination and time management skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Fast food expansion
- Customer service focus
- Hospitality growth
- Part-time employment

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a food service counter attendant in the Netherlands?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approx. €22,000-28,000, mid-level €28,000-35,000, senior up to €42,000. Salary includes tips.

**Q: Can non-EU individuals immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
A: Very difficult. This occupation does not meet the high-skilled immigration salary threshold and typically only allows entry via partner visas or EU free movement.
