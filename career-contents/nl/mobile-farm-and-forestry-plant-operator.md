# Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operator（Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operator）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：8341 – Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operator。**

Operates mobile agricultural or forestry machinery such as tractors, harvesters, and forwarders for plowing, harvesting, afforestation, and timber transport. Usually employed by farms, forestry companies, or contractors.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于年收入达标的高技能工人，该职业通常薪资不足以满足门槛，但若受雇于大型农业企业可能适用。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 薪资门槛较高，该职业通常不满足要求。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year (zo（Orientation Year） | 适用于在荷兰毕业的留学生，可自由工作一年，易于进入本行业。 |
| Subclass EU Free Movement（EU Free Movement） | 欧盟/EEA公民无需工作许可，可直接在荷兰从事该职业。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level annual salary ranges from €24,000 to €32,000, mid-level €32,000 to €42,000, and senior up to €52,000+.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to the Netherlands for this occupation?**
答：The most feasible path is EU free movement (if holding EU nationality) or the Orientation Year visa for Dutch graduates. The Highly Skilled Migrant salary threshold often exceeds this occupation's range. It is recommended to obtain a Dutch degree or seek employment with a large agricultural company.

**问：What certifications are needed?**
答：A T-license for driving agricultural vehicles on public roads, and specialist certifications for forestry machinery. MBO agricultural programs and apprenticeships are common entry paths.
