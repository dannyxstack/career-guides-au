# Stock Clerk（Stock Clerk）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：4321 – Stock Clerk。**

Stock clerks receive, store, and record goods in warehouses and retail stores, ensuring accurate inventory and smooth supply chain operations.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于年薪超过门槛的熟练工人，通常需要高等教育背景，但可能不适用于初级库存管理员。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高等教育学历且年薪达阈值的高技能工人，库存管理员通常不符合薪资要求。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year） | 荷兰毕业生或全球前200大学毕业生可申请，一年内寻找工作。 |
| Subclass EU自由流动（EU Free Movement） | 欧盟公民可自由在荷兰工作，无需签证。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★½☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a stock clerk in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level €24,000-€30,000, mid €30,000-€38,000, senior up to €45,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can a foreigner migrate as a stock clerk to the Netherlands?**
答：Usually requires employer sponsorship for Highly Skilled Migrant visa, but salary may not meet threshold. EU citizens can work freely.

**问：What education is needed to become a stock clerk?**
答：MBO logistics qualification or high school diploma with on-the-job training; no university degree required.
