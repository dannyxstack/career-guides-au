# Prison Guard — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 5413 (ISCO08)**

Prison guards are responsible for supervising inmates, maintaining prison security and order, conducting searches and patrols, and ensuring prison facilities operate normally.


---

| Stage | Duration | Cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---:|
| Intermediate Vocational Education (MBO) | 2-4 years | $0~$2,000 (Tuition is government-funded, and students only need to pay a registration fee of around a few hundred euros per year.) |
| Higher vocational education (HBO) | 4 years (bachelor's degree) | $2,000~$10,000 (Tuition varies by school; EU students: about €2,000-4,000/year, non-EU students: higher.) |

## 2. Qualifications & licensing

| Qualification | Issuer | Required | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Training Certificate | Ministry of Justice and Security of the Netherlands | Required | Complete mandatory training courses provided by the prison department, including legal, security, communication, and self-defense skills. |
| MBO Diploma (Safety and Supervision) | Secondary vocational education institutions | Optional | Many employers prefer candidates with a relevant MBO diploma covering the basics of prison work. |
| First aid and CPR certificate | Dutch Red Cross or equivalent organization | Optional | Some prisons require a valid first aid certificate to handle medical emergencies. |

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Prison guards can progress from junior officer to senior officer, supervisor, or specialist roles (e.g., Special Intervention Team). Through internal training and certification, they can move into prison management or other positions in the justice system.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine surveillance video patrol and abnormal alarm screening.
- Fixed-route patrol tasks (replaced by drones/robots)
- Daily prisoner roll call and basic data entry
- Non-emergency access control and entry verification
- Basic report writing and record filing

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted threat identification and risk warning (e.g., behavior analysis).
- Virtual reality scenario simulation training to improve adaptability
- Real-time data analysis assists patrol route optimization
- Smart wearable devices improve health and safety monitoring efficiency
- Automating administrative processes frees up time for frontline interactions

**Human moat:**

- Use of force and physical conflict management
- Crisis negotiation and psychological counseling
- Prisoner Rehabilitation and Personalized Management Strategies
- Immediate decision-making and leadership in emergencies
- Judgment in legal and ethical compliance

**Skills to build:**

- AI monitoring system operation and abnormal behavior analysis
- Data-driven security posture assessment
- Crisis intervention and conflict resolution techniques
- Basic programming or robotic collaborative maintenance
- Psychological counseling and correctional education methods
- Digital reporting and cross-departmental data integration

**Key growth areas:**

- Stable demand
- Rehabilitation focus
- Security modernization
- Staff retention issues

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Possess strong communication and conflict resolution skills, able to work under high pressure
- Values rules and procedures, responsible and a team player.
- Strong interest in criminal justice and rehabilitation

**Who is not suited**
- Unable to accept shift work and confined environments
- Extremely sensitive to violence or conflict, lacking self-control.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of prison guards in the Netherlands?**
A: Entry-level approximately €28,000-34,000, mid-level approximately €34,000-42,000, senior up to €52,000 (gross annual salary).

**Q: Can non-EU citizens become prison guards in the Netherlands?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a high-skilled migration visa, but prison guard salaries may not meet the threshold. EU citizens can work freely but need fluent Dutch.

**Q: What certifications are needed to become a prison guard?**
A: Must complete the training course approved by the Ministry of Justice and obtain the basic training certificate. Diploma in MBO security direction and first aid certificate are plus points.
