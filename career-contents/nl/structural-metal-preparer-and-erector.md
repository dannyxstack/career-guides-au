# Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for preparing, assembling, and installing steel structures and metal components on construction sites, including steel beams, columns, and trusses. Typically involves reading blueprints, operating lifting equipment, and performing welding and bolting.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Can progress from apprentice to senior installer, foreman or site supervisor. With experience, can move into structural steel detailer, project coordinator or self-employment.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use AI to read BIM models and automatically generate cutting and drilling plans
- Automated robots for welding and bolting standard steel structural components
- AI-driven drones or cameras for on-site progress monitoring and quality inspection

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted drawing reading, automatically flagging conflicts and potential installation issues
- AR glasses overlay construction information, improving assembly precision and efficiency
- Digital twin technology simulates lifting paths to optimize crane plans.

**Human moat:**

- Safety judgment and emergency response in complex field environments
- Manual adjustment and temporary solutions for non-standard components
- Coordinate and communicate with multiple disciplines such as civil and electrical engineering.

**Skills to build:**

- BIM Model Operation and Collision Detection
- Robotic welding and automation equipment programming
- Use of AR/VR construction assistance tools
- Advanced security management and risk assessment
- Materials science knowledge (high-strength steel, composite materials)
- Project Management Fundamentals (Agile Construction Methods)

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure
- Housing
- Green transition
- Steel construction

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for structural metal installers in the Netherlands?**
A: Entry-level approximately €28,000-€34,000, mid-level approximately €34,000-€42,000, senior up to €52,000 (annual salary before tax). Overtime and allowances can increase income.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands for this occupation?**
A: Can be accessed via the highly skilled migrant (kennismigrant) or EU Blue Card visa, requiring employer sponsorship. The Dutch construction industry has a shortage of this occupation, but the visa has salary requirements. Alternatively, can enter the Netherlands first through the orientation year visa (Orientation Year).

**Q: What certifications are needed to be qualified?**
A: A VCA basic safety certificate is mandatory. Welding certificates (e.g., NIL) and working at height certificates improve job opportunities. Apprenticeship completion certificates are also common requirements.
