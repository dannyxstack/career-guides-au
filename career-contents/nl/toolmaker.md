# Toolmaker — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 7222 (ISCO08)**

Tool makers use machine tools and hand tools to manufacture and repair precision metal molds, jigs, and tools to support manufacturing production.

> **This is a restricted skilled-migration occupation (employer-sponsored / regional agreements only).** It is not on the independent skilled lists (e.g. 189/190).

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Toolmakers face complex impacts from AI and automation: some precision machining tasks may be replaced by AI-driven machine tools, but maintenance, debugging, and complex mold design still require human experience, and AI tools can significantly improve design efficiency.

May start as an apprentice or technician, progress to senior toolmaker, production supervisor, or technical specialist. Also possible to move into design or quality control roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Conventional CNC machine programming and operation (AI auto-generates G-code)
- CAD/CAM modeling and simulation verification of standard molds
- Repetitive measurement and quality inspection (AI vision system)
- Tool path optimization and parameter adjustment (machine learning algorithms)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Complex mold design and reverse engineering (AI-generated design suggestions)
- Process monitoring and predictive maintenance (IoT + AI analysis)
- Multi-axis machine tool programming and simulation optimization (AI path planning)
- Collaborative robot-assisted clamping and loading/unloading
- Automatically generate processing reports and process documents

**Human moat:**

- Creative design and on-site commissioning of non-standard molds
- Precision repair and hand grinding (tactile and experience)
- Multi-trade coordination and production anomaly handling
- Safety compliance and process improvement decisions

**Skills to build:**

- AI-assisted design (e.g., generative design software)
- Advanced CNC programming and CAM software applications
- Industrial robot operation and programming
- Data analysis and process monitoring (SPC, IoT)
- Basics of additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision engineering
- Automation in manufacturing
- CAD/CAM integration
- Sustainable production

## 6. Migration pathways / PR

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who enjoy hands-on work, precision, and attention to detail
- Individuals with a strong interest in mechanical manufacturing and metalworking

**Who is not suited**
- People who dislike repetitive physical labor

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for Toolmakers?**
A: Entry-level annual salary approx. €28k–35k, senior up to €45k–55k.

**Q: Can foreign toolmakers migrate to the Netherlands?**
A: Can apply through the Highly Skilled Migrant visa or EU Blue Card, but requires employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. This occupation is not in shortage, with medium competition.
