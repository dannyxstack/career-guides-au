# Accounting and Bookkeeping Clerk（Accounting and Bookkeeping Clerk）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：4311 – Accounting and Bookkeeping Clerk。**

Record financial transactions, reconcile accounts, prepare financial statements and tax returns, serving as a key support role in organizational financial management.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for accounting and bookkeeping clerks in the Netherlands?**
答：Junior: €28,000-€35,000; Mid-level: €35,000-€45,000; Senior: €45,000-€60,000. Certifications like CPA or MBA can increase pay.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
答：Via Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card, requiring a job with a recognized employer meeting minimum salary thresholds. Dutch language skills and local certifications improve employability.

**问：Will AI replace accounting and bookkeeping clerks?**
答：AI and automation will replace routine tasks, but complex analysis, tax planning, and compliance still require human judgment. Upskilling to management or analytical roles reduces risk.
