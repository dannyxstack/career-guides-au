# Town and Traffic Planners（Town and Traffic Planners）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2164 – Town and Traffic Planners。**

Town and Traffic Planners design and manage urban space layout and transport systems, ensuring sustainability, efficient mobility, and livability. They analyze data, develop plans, and collaborate with stakeholders.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level of town and traffic planners in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level planners earn €35,000-45,000, mid-level €45,000-60,000, senior up to €85,000+, plus 8% holiday allowance.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to work as urban planners in the Netherlands?**
答：Via Highly Skilled Migrant (employer sponsorship, salary threshold ~€5,000/month) or EU Blue Card (salary threshold ~€58,000/year). Graduates from Dutch universities can apply for the Orientation Year visa.

**问：Are town and traffic planners in shortage in the Netherlands?**
答：Yes, due to housing shortages and transport issues, demand is high in both public and private sectors, making it a shortage occupation with good prospects.
