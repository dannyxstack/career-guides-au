# Mechanical Engineering Technician（Mechanical Engineering Technician）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3115 – Mechanical Engineering Technician。**

Assist mechanical engineers in designing and testing machinery, performing drafting, calculations, experiments, and on-site maintenance as key technical support in engineering teams.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Mechanical Engineering Technician？**
- 喜欢动手操作和解决技术问题的人
- 具有机电或机械基础知识，关注细节的人
- 愿意在工程团队中协作并能适应现场工作环境的人

**谁不适合学Mechanical Engineering Technician？**
- 不喜欢重复性技术检查或文书工作的人
- 希望完全独立工作、无需团队沟通的人

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for mechanical engineering technicians in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level technicians earn around €30k-40k, mid-level €40k-55k, and senior up to €70k+ annually. Salary depends on experience, industry, and region.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate through this occupation?**
答：The most common route is the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme (kennismigrant), requiring employer sponsorship and meeting income thresholds. Alternatively, use the Orientation Year visa to search for a job in the Netherlands, then switch to a work visa.

**问：What are the future career prospects?**
答：Driven by smart manufacturing, energy transition, and automation, demand for mechanical engineering technicians is positive. Continuous upskilling in areas like 3D printing and robotics enhances competitiveness.
