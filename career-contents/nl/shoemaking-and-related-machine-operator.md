# Shoemaking and Related Machine Operator（Shoemaking and Related Machine Operator）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：8156 – Shoemaking and Related Machine Operator。**

Operates shoemaking machinery to produce, assemble, and finish footwear, including cutting, stitching, and bonding. Typically works in a factory setting.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for shoemaking machine operators?**
答：Gross annual salary ranges from EUR 24,000 to 45,000, with entry level at 24k-30k and senior up to 45k. Below national average but moderate for MBO-level jobs.

**问：Can I immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
答：Difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list and salary usually does not meet Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card thresholds. Orientation Year visa is possible for graduates.
