# Messenger, Package Deliverer and Luggage Porter（Messenger, Package Deliverer and Luggage Porter）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：9621 – Messenger, Package Deliverer and Luggage Porter。**

Responsible for short-distance delivery of letters, small packages, and luggage, often using bicycles, e-bikes, or vans in urban areas.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：低（★½☆☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant Scheme） | 适用于年收入高于门槛的专业人才，此职业通常不满足收入要求。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高技能非欧盟公民，收入门槛较高，此职业不适用。 |
| Subclass EU Free Movement（EU/EEA/Swiss citizens free movement） | 欧盟/欧洲经济区/瑞士公民可在荷兰自由工作，无需工作许可。 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a messenger/package deliverer in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level: €25,000-€30,000; mid-level: €30,000-€36,000; senior: up to €42,000 per year before tax.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as a messenger/package deliverer?**
答：Generally no. The salary does not meet the thresholds for Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card. Only EU/EEA/Swiss citizens can work freely.
