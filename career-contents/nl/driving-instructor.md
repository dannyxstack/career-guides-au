# Driving Instructor（Driving Instructor）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：5165 – Driving Instructor。**

Driving instructors teach driving skills and traffic rules to students, preparing them for license exams. Demand is stable but influenced by pass rates.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：一般（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant Permit） | 适用于高技能移民，要求年薪至少€5,331每月（2025年标准），驾驶教练通常达不到该门槛。 |
| Subclass Self-Employment Visa（Self-Employed Person Permit） | 适用于自雇驾驶教练，需通过荷兰企业局（RVO）的审核，满足积分制要求。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 要求年薪至少€5,331每月（2025年标准），驾驶教练通常不符合。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year Permit） | 适用于在荷兰毕业的外国学生，可在一年内自由工作，寻找就业机会。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a driving instructor in the Netherlands?**
答：The average annual salary for a full-time driving instructor is around €35,000, but self-employed instructors have variable incomes, typically between €20,000 and €60,000.

**问：What visa is needed to become a driving instructor in the Netherlands as a foreigner?**
答：Most driving instructors cannot meet the salary threshold for a highly skilled migrant visa, so self-employment visa or orientation year permit are common options.

**问：What qualifications are required to become a driving instructor in the Netherlands?**
答：You must pass the theoretical and pedagogical exams from the CBR (Central Bureau for Driving Licenses), obtain a instructor certificate, and have a clean criminal record (VOG).
