# Philosopher, Historian and Political Scientist（Philosopher, Historian and Political Scientist）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2633 – Philosopher, Historian and Political Scientist。**

Conduct research, teach, and write in the fields of philosophy, history, or political science, analyzing social phenomena, historical events, and political systems. Employed mainly in universities, research institutes, and think tanks.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for philosophers/historians in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level postdoc salaries range from €30,000 to €45,000 per year gross, assistant professors from €45,000 to €60,000, and full professors can earn €80,000 to €100,000. Salaries vary by university and seniority.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate through this occupation in the Netherlands?**
答：Mainly via the Highly Skilled Migrant visa (kennismigrant), requiring a job offer from a Dutch employer (e.g., a research position at a university). PhD graduates can apply for the Orientation Year visa.

**问：What non-academic careers are possible after graduation?**
答：Graduates can enter policy research, government think tanks, journalism, cultural institutions, or international organizations, working as analysts, consultants, or managers.
