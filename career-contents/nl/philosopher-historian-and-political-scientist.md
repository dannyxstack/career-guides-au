# Philosopher, Historian and Political Scientist — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 2633 (ISCO08)**

Conduct research, teaching, and academic writing in philosophy, history, or political science, analyzing social phenomena, historical events, and political systems. Typically work in universities, research institutions, or think tanks.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of Dutch philosophers/historians?**
A: Gross annual salary for junior postdocs is approximately €30,000-€45,000, assistant professors €45,000-€60,000, and professors can reach €80,000-€100,000. Salaries vary by university and years of service.

**Q: How can foreigners immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
A: Primarily via the Highly Skilled Migrant visa (kennismigrant), requiring a job contract with a Dutch employer (e.g., a university research position). PhD graduates can apply for the Orientation Year visa.

**Q: What non-academic jobs can I do after graduation?**
A: Can enter policy research, government think tanks, news media, cultural institutions, or international organizations, working in analysis, advisory, or management roles.
