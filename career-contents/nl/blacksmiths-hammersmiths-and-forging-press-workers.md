# Blacksmiths, Hammersmiths and Forging Press Workers（Blacksmiths, Hammersmiths and Forging Press Workers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7221 – Blacksmiths, Hammersmiths and Forging Press Workers。**

This occupation involves manual or mechanical forging of metal to produce tools, weapons, decorative items, etc. In the Netherlands, traditional blacksmiths work on restoration and artistry, while forging press workers operate in manufacturing.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for blacksmiths in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level: €25,000-€32,000, mid-level: €32,000-€40,000, senior: €40,000-€50,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to work as a blacksmith in the Netherlands?**
答：The most common route is the Highly Skilled Migrant visa, requiring employer sponsorship and salary threshold. Alternatively, study (MBO) followed by Orientation Year.
