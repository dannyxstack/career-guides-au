# Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 7231 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for diagnosing, repairing and maintaining mechanical and electronic systems of motor vehicles including cars, trucks and motorcycles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from junior technician to senior technician, workshop supervisor, or independent auto repair shop owner. Obtaining BOVAG certification or EV high-voltage certification enhances competitiveness.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use diagnostic tools to read standard fault codes and provide common solutions
- Automatically generate standard maintenance work orders and parts lists from repair database.
- Automatically generate routine maintenance reminder reports from vehicle sensor data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI diagnostic systems to quickly locate complex electrical or mechanical faults
- Overlay maintenance steps and component disassembly instructions via AR glasses
- Use predictive maintenance algorithms to detect potential vehicle issues early
- Using natural language processing tools to automatically record maintenance logs

**Human moat:**

- Creative troubleshooting and on-site debugging of non-standard faults
- Fine disassembly and calibration requiring tactile feedback
- Comprehensive vehicle condition assessment and repair plan decision-making
- Customer trust communication and personalized service.

**Skills to build:**

- Mastery of AI diagnostic tools (e.g., Dealer-Fix, TEXA IDC5)
- Learning hybrid/electric vehicle high-voltage system repair
- Familiar with in-vehicle network diagnostics and software update processes
- Practice using AR-assisted maintenance equipment
- Improve customer service and communication skills
- Learn the basics of data analysis and predictive maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Electric vehicle repair
- Advanced diagnostics
- Hybrid systems
- Safety compliance

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- Enjoys hands-on repair work with a strong interest in automotive mechanics and electronic systems.
- Problem-solving skills and attention to detail, adaptable to learning new technologies
- Willing to undergo on-the-job training and obtain professional certifications such as electric vehicles

**Who is not suited**
- Not accustomed to physical labour or standing for long periods
- Resistance to technology updates, unwilling to learn electric vehicle repair

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level of auto mechanics in the Netherlands?**
A: Pre-tax annual salary for junior technicians is about €25,000-32,000, intermediate €32,000-42,000, and senior up to €55,000.

**Q: How Can Foreign Auto Mechanics Immigrate to the Netherlands?**
A: Can be obtained through the highly skilled migrant (kennismigrant) scheme or EU Blue Card, requiring employer sponsorship. Master's graduates can apply for the Orientation Year visa.

**Q: Are there shortages of automotive technicians in the Netherlands?**
A: Yes, especially electric vehicle repair technicians. The BOVAG report indicates the industry is facing a skills shortage.
