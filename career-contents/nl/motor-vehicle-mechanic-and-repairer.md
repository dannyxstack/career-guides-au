# Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer（Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7231 – Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer。**

Diagnose, repair, and maintain mechanical and electronic systems of motor vehicles, including cars, trucks, and motorcycles.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer？**
- 喜欢动手维修，对汽车机械和电子系统有浓厚兴趣
- 具有问题解决能力和细致态度，能适应学习新技术
- 愿意接受在职培训并获取电动车等专业认证

**谁不适合学Motor Vehicle Mechanic and Repairer？**
- 不习惯体力劳动或长时间站立工作
- 对技术更新持排斥态度，不愿学习电动车维修

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for motor vehicle mechanics in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level mechanics earn around €25,000-32,000 gross annually, mid-level €32,000-42,000, and senior up to €55,000.

**问：How can a foreign motor vehicle mechanic immigrate to the Netherlands?**
答：Via the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship. Graduates can use the Orientation Year visa.

**问：Is there a shortage of motor vehicle mechanics in the Netherlands?**
答：Yes, particularly for EV repair. BOVAG reports a talent shortage in the sector.
