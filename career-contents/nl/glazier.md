# Glaziers — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers install, replace, and repair glass windows, curtain walls, skylights, etc. in buildings, involving measuring, cutting, fixing, and sealing glass, typically serving residential, commercial, and industrial construction projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Glass workers can start as apprentices and gradually advance to skilled workers, foremen, or project managers; they can also further their studies in construction technology or business management to pursue self-employment or business ownership.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic optimization calculation of glass dimensions and layout
- BIM-based glass installation sequence planning
- Automated processing of inventory management and material procurement.
- Automatic generation and archiving of installation reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AR glasses for installation positioning and verification
- AI-assisted glass cutting optimization to reduce waste
- Drone or Robot-Assisted Glass Installation on High-Rise Buildings
- Smart tools for real-time monitoring of installation quality

**Human moat:**

- Physical tasks: on-site glass handling, positioning, and fixing
- Handling custom installation of non-standard and shaped glass
- Ability to handle emergencies such as glass breakage or structural deviations.
- Coordinate on-site with architects, contractors, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Reading BIM models and collaboration
- Drone/robot-assisted installation operations
- Use of AR/VR assisted construction tools
- Basic Python for automated reporting
- Green energy-efficient glass performance evaluation
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- energy-efficient glass
- renovation
- sustainable building
- curtain wall systems

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a glass worker in the Netherlands?**
A: Junior glazier annual salary about EUR 24,000-30,000, intermediate about EUR 30,000-38,000, senior up to EUR 45,000. Salaries are influenced by experience, qualifications, and region.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands through the glass worker occupation?**
A: Difficult. Glass worker is not on the Netherlands' shortage occupation list, typically requires employer sponsorship and proof that no EU citizen can be recruited. It is recommended to first obtain an EU qualification or find a job through the orientation year.

**Q: Is there room for advancement for a glazier?**
A: Yes. Can advance from skilled worker to supervisor, project manager, or self-employed entrepreneur. Obtaining additional qualifications such as the SVIB certificate can enhance competitiveness.
