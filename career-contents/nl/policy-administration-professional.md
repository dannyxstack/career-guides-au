# Policy Administration Professional（Policy Administration Professional）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2422 – Policy Administration Professional。**

Policy administration professionals analyze and implement government policies, oversee administrative procedures, and ensure compliance with regulations.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for policy administration professionals?**
答：Entry-level salaries range from €35,000 to €45,000, mid-level from €45,000 to €60,000, and senior from €60,000 to €80,000 per year. Public sector salaries are determined by collective labor agreements (CAO) and adjusted annually.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a work visa for policy administration positions in the Netherlands?**
答：Non-EU citizens can apply for the Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant) visa, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. Dutch graduates can use the Orientation Year visa without employer sponsorship. EU Blue Card is another option.

**问：What are the career advancement paths for policy administration?**
答：Common path: Policy Assistant → Policy Advisor → Senior Policy Officer → Department Head. Advancement can be accelerated through internal training or a Master of Public Administration (MPA).
