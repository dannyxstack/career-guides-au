# Health Associate Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified（Health Associate Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3259 – Health Associate Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Includes dental assistants, medical laboratory technicians, dietitians, and other allied health professionals supporting diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation in Dutch healthcare.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for this profession in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level annual gross salary ranges from €28,000 to €36,000, mid-level from €36,000 to €48,000, and senior up to €60,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa for the Netherlands?**
答：Via the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme or EU Blue Card, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. Alternatively, use the Orientation Year permit for job search.

**问：What education is required?**
答：Typically an MBO (secondary vocational) or HBO (higher professional education) degree, with foreign qualifications assessed by Nuffic.
