# Companions and Valets（Companions and Valets）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：5162 – Companions and Valets。**

Companions and Valets provide daily living assistance, companionship, and light household services for elderly, ill, or high-net-worth individuals. This niche personal service occupation in the Netherlands sees modest demand growth due to an aging society.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Orientation Year (Zo（Orientation Year） | 适用于在荷兰毕业的留学生，可在毕业后一年内自由求职。 |
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 需雇主担保，且工资达到门槛（2025年约€5,008/月），该职业很难达到。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需高等学历且工资门槛较高，不适合此职业。 |
| Subclass Self-Employed (ZZP)（Self-Employed Residence Permit） | 自雇从业者可通过“自雇居留许可”在荷工作，需证明对荷兰经济有贡献。 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Companions and Valets？**
- 有耐心、善解人意，喜欢与人相处的人。
- 愿意从事非正式、灵活工作方式的人。
- 对薪资要求不高，追求工作生活平衡的人。

**谁不适合学Companions and Valets？**
- 追求高薪和正式职业发展路径的人。
- 希望在荷兰获得工作签证和长期居留的外国人。

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for companions and valets in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level full-time positions pay around €20,000-€25,000 gross per year, while senior roles can reach €38,000. However, many work part-time or self-employed with irregular income.

**问：Can foreigners immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
答：Very difficult. The occupation is not on the shortage list and salaries are well below the Highly Skilled Migrant threshold. Work permits are unlikely unless via self-employment or partner visa.

**问：How to become a companion/valet in the Netherlands?**
答：No formal degree required, but training in domestic service or first aid helps. Most find clients through agencies or self-employment.
