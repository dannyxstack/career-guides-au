# Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 1311 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for planning, organizing, and managing agricultural, horticultural, or forestry production activities, including crop cultivation, livestock farming, or forest management, ensuring production efficiency and sustainable development.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Career paths typically start as farm supervisors or technicians, progressing to production managers, regional managers, or agribusiness executives; also possible to move into consulting or policy roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Crop growth monitoring: big data collation and visual report generation
- Develop and implement standardized irrigation and fertilization schedules
- Automated labeling of logging areas based on satellite data in forestry harvesting plans.
- Automate inventory and financial records for daily farm operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using drones and AI vision for early pest and disease diagnosis and precision spraying
- Crop yield prediction and planting optimization based on soil sensors and weather data
- Simulate the impact of different management strategies on ecology and economy through digital twins
- AI-assisted livestock behavior analysis for early warning of disease and stress
- Automated reporting and visualization tools improve efficiency for upward reporting and compliance documentation.

**Human moat:**

- Making intuitive, experience-based judgments in complex biological systems (e.g., responding to sudden pests and diseases)
- Communicating and coordinating with farm workers, community and environmental departments, managing interpersonal relationships and expectations
- Make dynamic risk decisions for unforeseen weather and market changes
- Assumes legal responsibility for production safety and food quality

**Skills to build:**

- Basics of remote sensing and drone data analysis
- AI/ML fundamentals (for yield prediction tools)
- Operation of digital farm management systems (e.g., FarmOS)
- Knowledge of sustainable agriculture and carbon emissions accounting
- Data visualization and report automation skills

**Key growth areas:**

- Precision Agriculture
- Sustainability
- Digital Farming
- Supply Chain Optimization

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for agricultural and forestry production managers?**
A: The average annual salary for agricultural/forestry production managers in the Netherlands is approximately €45,000-€60,000, entry-level around €35,000, and senior can reach over €80,000.

**Q: How can a non-EU citizen immigrate to the Netherlands for this occupation?**
A: Can apply through the Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card pathways, requiring an approved employer sponsor and meeting salary thresholds.

**Q: What educational background is needed for this occupation?**
A: A bachelor's degree in agriculture, forestry, or a related field is usually required, from either a University of Applied Sciences (HBO) or a research university (WO).
