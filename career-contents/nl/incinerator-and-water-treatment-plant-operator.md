# Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operator（Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operator）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3132 – Incinerator and Water Treatment Plant Operator。**

Operates and monitors incinerator and water treatment plant equipment to ensure waste processing, energy recovery, and water quality meet environmental standards.

|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant Permit） | 适用于年收入高于门槛（2025年约€4,500/月）的技术人员，雇主担保，30%税收优惠。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary for this occupation?**
答：Entry-level salary ranges €28k-35k, mid-level €35k-45k, senior €45k-55k gross per year including holiday allowance.

**问：How can overseas workers obtain a work visa for the Netherlands?**
答：Common routes are the Highly Skilled Migrant permit (employer-sponsored, salary threshold ~€4,500/month) or EU Blue Card (salary threshold ~€5,500/month).

**问：Are there career advancement opportunities?**
答：Yes, you can advance to senior operator, supervisor, or move into process optimization, environmental compliance roles.
