# University and Higher Education Teacher（University and Higher Education Teacher）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2310 – University and Higher Education Teacher。**

University teachers deliver lectures and seminars, conduct research, publish findings, supervise students, and contribute to academic governance. They work in universities and higher education institutions, combining teaching with research responsibilities.

> 注：本职业为**受限技术移民职业**，不在NL独立技术移民清单（189/190）上，但可通过雇主担保（482/494）、偏远地区指定协议（DAMA）或劳务协议移民——移民通道受限。

**职位需求量：中（★★½☆☆）。** 全国从业人数约 50,000。荷兰高等教育行业稳定，但教职竞争激烈。由于学术职位有限，获得永久教职（tenure）需要较强科研产出。数字化教学和跨学科研究是增长点，但预算削减可能影响招聘。

**竞争度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景：中（★★½☆☆）。** 典型路径：博士后研究员 → 助理教授（Universitair Docent, UD）→ 副教授（Universitair Hoofddocent, UHD）→ 正教授。晋升主要依赖科研产出、教学评估和第三方资金获取能力。多数初级职位为临时合同。
**AI替代风险：低（★☆☆☆☆）。**

主要增长方向：

- Digital education
- Interdisciplinary research
- Internationalization
- Open science

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / DAMA）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（189 / 190 / 491）上，无法直接申请普通技术移民；但可通过雇主担保（482 / 494）、偏远地区指定移民协议（DAMA）或劳务协议等通道移民——通道与名额受限，具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定及 CSOL 清单为准。
**PR友好度：高（★★★★☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于有工作聘书的博士生或研究人员，薪资门槛较低（约€3,000/月） |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高薪职位（2024年门槛约€5,331/月），可带家属并快速申请永居 |

**PR难度：中（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for university teachers in the Netherlands?**
答：Assistant professors start at around €4,500/month, while full professors can earn up to €150,000 annually. Salaries follow the collective agreement CAO NU, including 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus.

**问：How can non-EU citizens become university teachers in the Netherlands?**
答：A PhD and job offer are required. Apply for a Highly Skilled Migrant visa with salary threshold around €3,000/month. After 5 continuous residence, you can apply for permanent residence or citizenship.

**问：What are the conditions for promotion for university teachers in the Netherlands?**
答：Promotion depends on research publications, teaching evaluations, societal impact, and securing research grants. Advancement from assistant to associate professor typically requires about 6 years of achievement. Tenure is granted after a successful assessment.
