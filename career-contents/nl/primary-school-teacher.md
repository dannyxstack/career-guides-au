# Primary School Teacher — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 2341 (ISCO08)**

Primary school teachers teach multiple subjects to children aged 6 to 12, promoting their cognitive, social, and emotional development, covering language, mathematics, science, etc.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★½☆☆☆).**

Can progress from teacher to senior teacher, subject coordinator, deputy principal or principal, or move into education policy or teacher training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Basic assignment grading and feedback generation
- Standardized answer technique training
- Classroom discipline monitoring and attendance recording
- Basic knowledge instruction (e.g., arithmetic, spelling)
- Student academic data organization and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized Learning Path Planning and Resource Recommendation
- Intelligent analysis of academic progress and customized tutoring programs
- Design and implementation of interdisciplinary project-based courses
- Improved parent communication and collaboration efficiency
- Teaching creative content generation (stories, experiments, games, etc.)

**Human moat:**

- Student mental and emotional wellness care
- Classroom atmosphere creation and real-time interactive guidance
- Teaching morals and values by example
- Handling emergencies and conflict mediation
- In-depth support for students with complex family backgrounds

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI educational tools (such as adaptive learning platforms)
- Data analysis and personalized student assessment
- Educational psychology and communication skills
- Interdisciplinary curriculum design skills
- Digital content creation (multimedia courseware)
- Ethics and data privacy knowledge

**Key growth areas:**

- Teacher shortage
- Rising enrollment
- Government investment
- Urban-rural gap

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People who love children and education, with patience and empathy
- Those who are good at communication and teamwork
- Creative and organised individuals

**Who is not suited**
- People who cannot tolerate noisy environments and high pressure
- Those who dislike interacting with parents or the community

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a primary school teacher in the Netherlands?**
A: Junior teachers earn approximately €35,000–42,000 per year, while senior teachers can earn €55,000–70,000 (pre-tax).

**Q: How can non-EU citizens become primary school teachers in the Netherlands?**
A: Must obtain a recognized Dutch education degree and pass language exams; after employer sponsorship, can apply for highly skilled migration or EU Blue Card.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a primary school teacher in the Netherlands?**
A: Must complete the PABO course and obtain a bachelor's degree, as well as pass the Dutch language proficiency test (B2 or above) and a criminal record check.
