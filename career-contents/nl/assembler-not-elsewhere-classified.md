# Assemblers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 8219 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers various assembly work not listed in other categories, involving multiple manufacturing sectors such as electronics, machinery, and metal products. Workers assemble parts into finished products according to drawings and operating procedures, and perform basic testing and inspection.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: high (★★★★☆).**

Junior assemblers can advance to senior assemblers, assembly team leaders, or quality inspectors. Through further study, they can transition to technical sales, equipment maintenance, or production management roles. Some workers enter more specialized technical trades through vocational training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Perform repetitive assembly of parts according to drawings (screws, cables, small parts)
- Basic functional testing and visual inspection (e.g., connections, power-on)
- Simple fault diagnosis and rework
- Material handling and sorting
- Operate fixed power tools (e.g., electric drills, screwdrivers)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI vision inspection systems to assist quality inspection and improve defect identification
- Guiding complex assembly processes via AR/VR to improve accuracy
- Using collaborative robots for heavy or precision component handling, reducing labor intensity.
- Optimize equipment commissioning and maintenance using data analysis and predictive maintenance tools
- Use digital manufacturing execution systems (MES) to track production progress and quality data

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment skills for handling non-standard, small-batch, or custom assembly
- On-site diagnosis and repair decisions for complex mechanical failures
- Interpersonal communication and team coordination skills (collaborating with designers and engineers)
- Strict adherence to safety regulations and response in emergency situations

**Skills to build:**

- Industrial robot programming and debugging (e.g., KUKA, ABB)
- Basic PLC programming and maintenance
- Fundamentals of Data Analysis and Intelligent Manufacturing Systems
- AR/VR-assisted work skills
- Lean production and continuous improvement methods
- Basic English for reading technical documents

**Key growth areas:**

- Electronics assembly
- Precision manufacturing
- Automation adaptation
- Quality control

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary level for assemblers in the Netherlands?**
A: Annual salary for junior assemblers is about 26,000-32,000 euros, intermediate 32,000-38,000 euros, and senior 38,000-45,000 euros. Salaries are affected by industry, experience, and region.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens work as assemblers in the Netherlands?**
A: Low-skilled assembly workers often struggle to obtain work visas because wages do not meet high-skilled immigration thresholds. EU citizens can work freely; non-EU citizens require employer sponsorship and often need to prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What are the career development paths for assemblers?**
A: Can advance to senior assembler, team leader, or quality inspector; transition to technical sales, equipment maintenance, or production management through training.
