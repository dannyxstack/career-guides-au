# Bicycle and Related Repairers — career analysis · Netherlands

**Occupation code: 7234 (ISCO08)**

Responsible for repairing and maintaining bicycles, e-bikes and related equipment, including adjusting brakes, chains, tires and gear systems. In the Netherlands, bicycles are the main mode of transport, and this occupation has stable demand.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Start as a junior mechanic, can advance to senior technician or store manager. Accumulate experience to start your own repair shop. Some practitioners become certified e-bike or racing bike specialists, significantly increasing salary.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Fault diagnosis: AI image recognition and sensors can quickly detect issues, replacing manual inspection.
- Inventory management: AI system automatically orders replacement parts, reducing manual stocktaking
- Maintenance records: AI automatically generates maintenance reports and customer history
- Routine maintenance: robots or automated tools can handle tire replacement and chain lubrication

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI diagnostic tools assist in identifying rare faults, improving repair efficiency
- AR headsets provide real-time repair guides, reducing training time.
- AI predicts maintenance needs, proactively contacts customers, increasing business volume
- Data-driven pricing strategies to optimize service fees and parts inventory

**Human moat:**

- Fine manual adjustments (e.g., wheel alignment, welding repair)
- Handle non-standard, old, or modified bicycles
- Customer communication and trust building (e.g., explaining repair plans)
- On-site emergency repairs (e.g., roadside breakdowns)

**Skills to build:**

- Master diagnostic and repair skills for e-bike electronic systems
- Learning to use AI diagnostic software and digital repair manuals
- Improve customer service and sales skills
- Understand maintenance of light electric vehicles (e.g., scooters).
- Learn basic programming to customize diagnostic tools
- Mastering 3D printing parts fabrication

**Key growth areas:**

- E-bike repair
- Cargo bike services
- Sustainable transport
- Mobility transition

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary of a bicycle mechanic in the Netherlands?**
A: Entry-level mechanic salary is about €25,000-30,000 per year, senior technicians can reach €38,000-45,000. Salaries vary by region and experience, with slightly higher pay in cities like Amsterdam.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as bicycle mechanics?**
A: This occupation usually does not meet the high-skilled immigration salary requirements and is mainly suitable for EU citizens. Non-EU immigrants need to take other paths, such as marriage to a Dutch person or investment visa.

**Q: How to become a bicycle mechanic in the Netherlands?**
A: typically through MBO vocational training (3 years) or on-the-job apprenticeships. Curriculum covers mechanical and electrical repairs, with some employers offering internal training. No mandatory certification needed, but certificates help with employment
