# Database and Network Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified（Database and Network Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：2529 – Database and Network Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified。**

Covers database administrators, network administrators, and other IT professionals not elsewhere classified, responsible for designing, maintaining, and managing database and network systems.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for database and network professionals in the Netherlands?**
答：Salaries increase with experience: entry-level €35k-€45k, mid-level €45k-€60k, senior €60k-€85k, above national average.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands for database and network jobs?**
答：Through the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme or EU Blue Card, requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds. Dutch graduates can use the Orientation Year visa.

**问：What is the job market for database and network professionals in the Netherlands?**
答：Strong demand, especially in data management and cybersecurity. Digital transformation drives sustained growth, with low unemployment.
