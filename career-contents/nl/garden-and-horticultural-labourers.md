# Garden and Horticultural Labourers（Garden and Horticultural Labourers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：9214 – Garden and Horticultural Labourers。**

Garden and horticultural labourers perform planting, maintaining and harvesting plants, including flowers, vegetables and fruit trees, doing physical work in nurseries, greenhouses and gardens.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★½☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于与雇主签约的高技能工人，需满足工资门槛（2024年约3,500欧元/月） |
| Subclass Seasonal Labor Schem（Seasonal Work Visa） | 适用于短期季节性园艺工作，最长6个月 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适合高学历且薪资达标者（通常高于中位数），园艺工人较少适用 |

**PR难度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Garden and Horticultural Labourers？**
- 喜欢户外体力劳动且不介意季节性工作
- 有园艺基础或农业经验
- 愿意学习温室技术和可持续农业

**谁不适合学Garden and Horticultural Labourers？**
- 偏好室内稳定办公环境
- 无法适应重复性体力劳动

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for garden and horticultural labourers?**
答：Entry-level about EUR 22,000-28,000 per year, senior up to EUR 42,000.

**问：How can foreigners obtain a visa for horticultural work?**
答：Via Seasonal Work Visa (up to 6 months) or Highly Skilled Migrant visa (employer sponsorship, salary threshold).

**问：Is there a shortage of garden and horticultural labourers in the Netherlands?**
答：Yes, especially in spring and autumn, employers often face labor shortages.
