# Glaziers（Glaziers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7125 – Glaziers。**

Glaziers install, replace, and repair glass panes, curtain walls, and skylights in buildings, involving measuring, cutting, fixing, and sealing glass for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于高技能非欧盟移民，需雇主担保且薪资达到门槛（2025年约€5,008月薪）。玻璃工通常不符合条件。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 针对高等教育以上学历的高技能专业人士，薪资门槛较高（2025年约€5,688月薪），玻璃工一般难以达到。 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year） | 为荷兰大学毕业的非欧盟毕业生提供一年找工作时间，适合相关专业毕业生。 |
| Subclass Work permit (GVVA)（Work Permit (GVVA)） | 雇主需证明无法在欧盟内招聘到合适人才，玻璃工市场通常不紧缺，获批较难。 |

**PR难度：中等（★★★☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for glaziers in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level glaziers earn €24k-30k, mid-level €30k-38k, and senior up to €45k gross per year, depending on experience, certifications, and location.

**问：Can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as a glazier?**
答：It is challenging. Glaziers are not on the shortage list; employers must prove no suitable EU candidates. A EU education or an orientation year might help.

**问：Are there career advancement opportunities for glaziers?**
答：Yes. Can progress to foreman, project manager, or self-employment. Additional certifications like SVIB can enhance prospects.
