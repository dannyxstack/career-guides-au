# Food and Beverage Taster and Grader（Food and Beverage Taster and Grader）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7515 – Food and Beverage Taster and Grader。**

Taste and evaluate the quality, flavor, aroma, and appearance of food and beverages, grading them according to standards to ensure safety and quality.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，需满足薪资门槛（2024年约€5,331/月）。 |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高学历非欧盟公民，薪资门槛约€5,008/月（2024年），工作满21个月可申请永居。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for food tasters in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level salary is around €25k-€32k, mid-level €32k-€42k, senior up to €55k per year, depending on industry and region.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands for food taster jobs?**
答：Via Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship and salary threshold. Not a shortage occupation, migration difficulty is moderate.
