# Pharmaceutical Technician（Pharmaceutical Technician）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3213 – Pharmaceutical Technician。**

Pharmaceutical technicians assist pharmacists in preparing and dispensing medications, managing inventory, and providing customer service in pharmacies, hospitals, or pharmaceutical companies.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for a pharmaceutical technician in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level pharmaceutical technicians earn €25,000-€32,000 gross annually, mid-level €32,000-€40,000, and senior up to €40,000-€50,000.

**问：How can non-EU citizens migrate to the Netherlands as a pharmaceutical technician?**
答：Non-EU citizens typically need a job offer from a Dutch employer and can apply for a residence permit under the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme or EU Blue Card. BIG registration is mandatory.

**问：What career advancement opportunities are there for pharmaceutical technicians?**
答：Pharmaceutical technicians can advance to senior technician, pharmacy manager, or move into quality control or production management at pharmaceutical companies, or further study to become a pharmacist.
