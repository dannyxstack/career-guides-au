# Apiarist（Apiarist）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：6123 – Apiarist。**

Apiarists manage bee colonies for honey, royal jelly, beeswax production and crop pollination services. The job requires outdoor work and knowledge of bee biology and colony management.

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Apiarist？**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：Is an apiarist's income stable?**
答：Income fluctuates significantly, depending on honey yield, pollination contracts, and weather. Entry-level earn around €22,000-€28,000, experienced beekeepers can exceed €45,000 but face business risks.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as a beekeeper?**
答：It is challenging. Regular highly skilled migrant salary thresholds are high and rarely met. Options include a self-employment visa (requires a business plan assessment) or an Orientation Year visa, but success rates are low.

**问：What are the career progression paths for beekeepers?**
答：Start as a beekeeper assistant, progress to independent apiary owner or pollination contractor. Specializations include disease control, queen breeding, or honey processing. Some move into research or education.
