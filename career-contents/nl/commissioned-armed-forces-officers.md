# Commissioned Armed Forces Officers（Commissioned Armed Forces Officers）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：0110 – Commissioned Armed Forces Officers。**

Commissioned officers in the Dutch armed forces lead military operations, manage personnel, and provide strategic direction across land, air, and sea branches.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

**竞争度：中等偏高（★★★½☆）。**
**工作强度：高（★★★★☆）。**

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 公民身份（Dutch Citizenship） | 军官职位只对荷兰公民开放，无法通过工作签证担任 |
| Subclass 其他（No alternative） | 非荷兰公民无工作或居留路径 |

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合学Commissioned Armed Forces Officers？**
- 有志于军事领导和国防事业的荷兰公民
- 身体素质优秀、心理抗压能力强
- 愿意接受频繁调动和海外部署

**谁不适合学Commissioned Armed Forces Officers？**
- 非荷兰公民
- 拒绝国际任务或偏远地区部署

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for officers?**
答：Junior officers earn about €38,000-€48,000 per year, while senior officers can earn up to €120,000 including allowances.

**问：Can non-Dutch citizens become Dutch officers?**
答：No. Officer positions are restricted to Dutch citizens who pass stringent security vetting.

**问：Is it easy for officers to get permanent residence?**
答：As civil servants, officers can apply after 5 years, but must retain citizenship. No path for non-citizens.
