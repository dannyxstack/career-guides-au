# Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks（Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：0310 – Armed Forces Occupations, Other Ranks。**

Non-commissioned members of the Netherlands Armed Forces, including soldiers, corporals, and sergeants, responsible for defense and security operations.

> 注：本职业为**非技术移民职业**，不在NL技术移民职业清单上，以下内容主要面向本地就业与职业了解。

主要增长方向：

- Cybersecurity

- Technology integration

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在澳洲技术移民职业清单（MLTSSL / STSOL / CSOL）上，不能作为独立的技术移民或雇主担保提名职业。如以移民为目标，建议考虑清单上的相关职业，或通过学生、毕业生工作、家庭团聚等其他签证类别。具体以 Department of Home Affairs 最新规定为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 适用于高技能非欧盟公民，但武装部队通常仅限荷兰公民，不适用。 |
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 适用于高技术移民，但武装部队岗位一般不对非欧盟公民开放。 |

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level in the Netherlands Armed Forces?**
答：Entry-level soldiers earn around €25,000-35,000 annually, increasing with rank and tenure, but generally lower than IT sectors.

**问：Can non-Dutch citizens join the Netherlands Armed Forces?**
答：Typically requires Dutch citizenship; non-citizens have very limited opportunities and are nearly impossible.

**问：Can military service lead to civilian jobs?**
答：Yes, skills can transfer to civilian roles in the Ministry of Defense or other government agencies.
