# Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors（Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7214 – Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors。**

Prepare, assemble, and install steel structures and metal components on construction sites, including beams, columns, and trusses. Tasks include reading blueprints, operating cranes, and welding/bolting.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary for structural metal preparers and erectors in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level approx. €28,000-€34,000, mid-career €34,000-€42,000, senior up to €52,000 (pre-tax annual). Overtime and allowances can add extra.

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands for this occupation?**
答：Via Highly Skilled Migrant or EU Blue Card with employer sponsorship. The sector faces shortages, but visa salary thresholds apply. Alternatively, use the Orientation Year visa if eligible.

**问：What certifications are needed?**
答：VCA basic safety certificate is mandatory. Welding certificates (e.g., NIL) and working at height certificate are beneficial. An apprenticeship diploma is also common.
