# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator（Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：7223 – Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator。**

Sets up, operates and maintains metal working machine tools such as lathes, milling machines and drills to produce precision metal parts, serving as a core role in manufacturing.

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary level for metal working machine tool setters and operators in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level annual salary is around €28,000-35,000, mid-level €35,000-45,000, and senior up to €55,000, including holiday allowance and overtime.

**问：How can foreigners immigrate to the Netherlands through this occupation?**
答：The main route is the Highly Skilled Migrant scheme (salary threshold €3,672/month, employer sponsorship, MBO diploma qualifies). EU Blue Card is less common due to higher salary requirements.

**问：What are the career progression paths for this occupation?**
答：Operators can advance to CNC programmer, production supervisor, or quality inspector; further education enables roles like mold designer or process engineer.
