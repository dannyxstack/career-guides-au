# Forestry Technician（Forestry Technician）职业分析 · NL

**职业代码：3143 – Forestry Technician。**

Forestry technicians in the Netherlands manage forests, plan reforestation and timber harvesting, monitor ecosystems, and ensure sustainable forestry practices using GIS and remote sensing.

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**PR友好度：中等（★★★☆☆）。** 签证路径需按具体职责匹配对应 ANZSCO，并以 Department of Home Affairs 最新职业清单及相关评估机构评估结果为准。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass Highly Skilled Migra（Highly Skilled Migrant） | 需与认可雇主签约，年薪门槛约€5,008/月（30岁+）或€3,672/月（30岁以下） |
| Subclass EU Blue Card（EU Blue Card） | 需高等教育学历，年薪门槛约€5,900/月，较适合高级技术员 |
| Subclass Orientation Year（Orientation Year） | 荷兰毕业非欧盟学生可申请一年内自由求职，无薪资门槛 |

**PR难度：中等（★★½☆☆）。**

## 9. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the salary range for forestry technicians in the Netherlands?**
答：Entry-level €28,000-35,000, mid-level €35,000-45,000, senior €45,000-55,000 (gross annual).

**问：How can non-EU citizens immigrate to the Netherlands as forestry technicians?**
答：Via Highly Skilled Migrant visa requiring employer sponsorship and meeting salary thresholds, or EU Blue Card.

**问：What education is needed to become a forestry technician?**
答：Typically an MBO or HBO degree in forestry, including internships.
