## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for underwater divers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Greece vary widely by sector. Commercial divers in offshore oil and gas or underwater construction can earn €2,500–€5,000 per month. Scientific or tourism-related divers (e.g., underwater guides) typically earn €1,200–€2,500 monthly. Experience, certifications, and project complexity significantly affect pay.

**Q: Can non-EU underwater divers work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU divers can apply for the EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a recognized higher education degree (or equivalent) and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). For regulated professions like diving, recognition of professional qualifications may be required. The employer must initiate the process.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as an underwater diver in Greece?**
A: In Greece, commercial divers need recognized certifications such as those from the International Diving Schools Association (IDSA) or equivalent. Recreational divers typically require CMAS, PADI, or SSI instructor-level qualifications. All divers must hold a valid medical certificate. For specific sectors (e.g., scientific diving), additional training may be mandated by employers or Greek regulations.
