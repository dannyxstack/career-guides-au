## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for policy administration professionals in Greece?**
A: Entry-level positions start around €1,200 per month gross, while experienced professionals can earn up to €2,500 per month. Public sector roles follow national pay scales, with additional allowances. Senior specialists in private sector may exceed this range.

**Q: Can non-EU policy administration professionals work in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€32,986 annually in 2024) and a recognized university degree. Alternatively, a national work visa is available for lower salaries, requiring employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for policy administration professionals in Greece?**
A: Professionals can progress to senior policy officer, team leader, or manager roles. Specialization sectors like insurance, government, or consulting offer further advancement. Continuous education in public policy or law enhances prospects.
