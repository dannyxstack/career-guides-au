## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a door-to-door salesperson in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on commission. Entry-level positions may offer a base of €700-900 per month plus commissions, while experienced salespersons can earn €1,200-2,000 monthly. Commission structures often include a percentage of sales, so income is performance-dependent. Minimum wage in Greece is €830 per month as of 2024.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as door-to-door salespersons in Greece through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is intended for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent, typically in regulated professions. Door-to-door sales generally does not qualify as a highly skilled occupation. Non-EU nationals may apply for a national work visa if they have a job offer and meet criteria, but the Blue Card route is not applicable for this role.

**Q: What are the career prospects for door-to-door salespersons in Greece?**
A: Career growth includes moving into team leader roles, area sales manager positions, or transitioning to inside sales or retail management. With proven performance, opportunities arise in training, recruitment, or business development. Digitalization is changing the field, with many companies integrating online lead generation, but face-to-face skills remain valued in sectors like energy, telecoms, and insurance.
