## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an education methods specialist in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions in public schools follow state pay scales, starting around €1,100 gross monthly. Experienced specialists in private institutions or international schools can earn €1,800–€2,500 gross monthly. Private sector roles may include additional benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU education methods specialists work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary. Alternatively, a national work permit for highly qualified personnel is available. Both require employer sponsorship and compliance with Greek immigration procedures.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for education methods specialists in Greece?**
A: Specialists can advance to senior curriculum designer, department head, or educational consultant roles. In public education, promotions follow seniority and additional qualifications. Private sector offers faster advancement, with potential to lead training departments or become independent consultants for schools and educational organizations.
