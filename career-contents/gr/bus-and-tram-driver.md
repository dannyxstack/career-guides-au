## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, bus and tram drivers typically earn between €1,000 and €1,400 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. Public sector drivers may receive slightly higher pay and additional benefits. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings. Salaries are subject to collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU bus and tram drivers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree or five years of professional experience, a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, and valid travel documents. Alternatively, a national work visa (D-type) requires a confirmed job offer and a labor market test showing no local candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Greece?**
A: Bus and tram drivers need a valid driving license category D (for buses) and a professional driver's certificate (PES). They must also pass medical exams, complete training programs, and obtain a certificate of professional competence (CPC). For tram drivers, additional specific training on tram operation is required.
