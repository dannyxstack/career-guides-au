## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in Greece?**
A: The typical monthly salary for a glazier in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,400, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €700–€900, while experienced glaziers can earn up to €1,500 or more. Overtime and specialized skills, such as working with tempered glass, may increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU glaziers work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Glaziers typically do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which is reserved for highly skilled professionals with a university degree and a higher salary threshold. Non-EU glaziers may obtain a Greek work permit if an employer sponsors them and shows that no EU candidate is available. Self-employment is also possible via a business visa or residence permit for entrepreneurs.

**Q: What training or certifications are needed to become a glazier in Greece?**
A: Formal training is not mandatory, but vocational programs in glass installation offered by public IEK (Vocational Training Institutes) or private schools are beneficial. Apprenticeships under experienced glaziers are common. Certification in safety standards, such as working at heights, may be required. The Greek Ministry of Labour recognizes certain professional qualifications for those with prior experience.
