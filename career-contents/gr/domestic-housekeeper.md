## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a domestic housekeeper in Greece?**
A: In Greece, domestic housekeepers typically earn the statutory minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €780 per month for full-time work. Actual pay may vary by region and employer, with part-time or live-in positions sometimes including accommodation. Overtime and additional benefits are uncommon but legally mandated for extra hours.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a domestic housekeeper in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly-skilled workers with tertiary education and high salary thresholds, making it unsuitable for domestic housekeepers. Non-EU nationals can instead apply for a national work visa requiring a job offer from a Greek employer, who must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally. The process involves a labor market test and residence permit application.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a domestic housekeeper in Greece?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory for domestic housekeepers in Greece. Employers often seek reliability, cleaning experience, and good references. Knowledge of Greek or English is beneficial. Some vocational training courses in housekeeping or cleaning may improve employability. Background checks and health certificates may be required by some employers.
