## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other cleaning workers in Greece?**
A: The typical monthly salary for cleaning workers in Greece ranges from €650 to €850 gross, depending on experience and location. The statutory minimum wage for full-time workers is €713 per month as of 2024. Overtime and night-shift work may provide additional pay. Many positions offer seasonal or part-time contracts.

**Q: Can non-EU workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for cleaning jobs in Greece?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a national work visa (Type D) for employment in Greece, but the EU Blue Card is not typically available for cleaning occupations as it requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold. Employers must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by local or EU workers. Work permits are tied to specific employers and regions.

**Q: What are the career prospects for other cleaning workers in Greece?**
A: Career advancement for cleaning workers in Greece is limited but possible. With experience, one can move into supervisory roles or start a small cleaning business. Additional certifications in specialized cleaning (e.g., industrial or biohazard) may improve opportunities. However, the sector is largely informal with high turnover, and formal training programs are scarce.
