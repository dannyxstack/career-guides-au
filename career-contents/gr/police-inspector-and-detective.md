## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for police inspectors and detectives in Greece?**
A: Starting salary for police inspectors in Greece is around €1,100-€1,300 gross per month, with experienced detectives earning up to €2,000 or more depending on rank and service years. Additional allowances are provided for night shifts, overtime, and hazardous duty.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU citizens to work as police inspectors or detectives in Greece?**
A: Police inspector and detective positions in Greece are restricted to Greek citizens; non-EU citizens are not eligible via work visas or the EU Blue Card. The only possible route involves obtaining Greek citizenship through naturalization, which does not guarantee employment in these roles.

**Q: What are the educational requirements to become a police inspector in Greece?**
A: Candidates must be Greek citizens aged 18-26, hold at least a high school diploma, and pass physical and psychological exams. After completing the Police Academy's 2-year program, they become police officers. Promotion to inspector requires further training, experience, and competitive exams.
