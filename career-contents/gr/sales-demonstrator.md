## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sales Demonstrator in Greece?**
A: Sales Demonstrators in Greece typically earn between €700 and €1,200 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Many positions offer the statutory minimum wage (currently €830 per month) plus commissions or bonuses. Pay is often hourly or project-based, with variations across retail sectors.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Sales Demonstrator in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is designed for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€36,262 in 2024). Sales Demonstrator roles rarely meet this threshold. Instead, non-EU workers typically need a work visa sponsored by an employer, subject to labor market tests.

**Q: What are the career prospects for Sales Demonstrators in Greece?**
A: Sales Demonstrators often gain skills in customer engagement and product knowledge. In Greece, career advancement can lead to roles like sales representative, brand ambassador, or retail supervisor. The job is common in electronics, cosmetics, and food sectors. Seasonal and tourism-related positions are frequent in islands and major cities.
