## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry workers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, forestry workers earn an average gross monthly salary of approximately €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced workers can reach up to €1,500. Wages are often based on collective bargaining agreements.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU forestry workers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU forestry workers can apply for the EU Blue Card if they hold a university degree or five years of professional experience and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (about €24,000 in 2023). Alternatively, a national work permit may be available for seasonal or labor shortages, but requirements vary.

**Q: What are the main duties of forestry workers in Greece?**
A: Forestry workers in Greece perform tasks such as planting, thinning, and harvesting trees; maintaining forest roads and trails; preventing and fighting forest fires; and monitoring forest health. They work for public forestry services, private companies, or as self-employed contractors. Seasonal work is common, especially during summer fire-prevention activities.
