## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Professional Services Managers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Professional Services Managers in Greece vary by sector and experience. Typically, annual gross salaries range from €30,000 to €60,000. Senior roles in consulting, legal, or accounting firms may exceed this range. Bonuses and benefits are common. Note that salaries are influenced by company size and location, with higher pay in Athens and Thessaloniki.

**Q: What are the work visa options for Professional Services Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece). Alternatively, a national work visa (type D) requires employer sponsorship and approval from the Greek authorities. Both routes lead to a residence permit. Self-employment is possible under specific conditions.

**Q: What qualifications do Professional Services Managers typically need in Greece?**
A: In Greece, Professional Services Managers usually hold a university degree in business, finance, law, or a related field. Many also have professional certifications (e.g., ACCA, PMP) or a master’s degree. Fluency in Greek and English is often required. Experience in the relevant service sector (consulting, IT, HR) is essential. For regulated professions, additional licensing may be necessary.
