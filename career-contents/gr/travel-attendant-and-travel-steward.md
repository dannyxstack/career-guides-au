## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for travel attendants in Greece?**
A: In Greece, travel attendants earn an average monthly salary of €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and airline. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior staff or those on international routes can earn more. Additional allowances for layovers and flight hours are common.

**Q: What are the work visa options for travel attendants moving to Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as travel attendants in Greece via an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree or five years' professional experience. Alternatively, a national work permit requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. Both routes need Ministry of Labor approval.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for travel attendants in Greece?**
A: Travel attendants in Greece can advance to senior cabin crew, purser, or in-flight manager positions. Additional opportunities include training roles, ground operations, or transitioning to airline management. Greek airlines often promote based on seniority and performance.
