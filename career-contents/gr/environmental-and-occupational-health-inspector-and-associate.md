## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an environmental and occupational health inspector in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. In the public sector, entry-level net monthly salary ranges from €800 to €1,000, increasing with seniority. Private sector roles may offer higher pay. Gross annual salaries typically fall between €18,000 and €25,000, depending on qualifications and responsibilities.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as environmental health inspectors in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in this profession through the EU Blue Card, which requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece. Alternatively, a national work permit can be obtained via employer sponsorship through the Ministry of Migration and Asylum. Fluency in Greek is typically required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an environmental health inspector in Greece?**
A: A university degree in environmental health, public health, occupational hygiene, or a related field is required. Additional specialized training or certification may be necessary. Fluency in Greek is essential, as the role involves interacting with local authorities and the public. The profession is regulated by the Ministry of Health.
