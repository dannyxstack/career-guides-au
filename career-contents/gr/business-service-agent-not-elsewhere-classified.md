## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services Agents in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Business Services Agents (ISCO 3339) in Greece vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €10,000–€14,000 annually, while experienced agents earn €18,000–€30,000. The average monthly net salary is roughly €1,300–€1,800, with potential bonuses in sales-linked roles.

**Q: How can non-EU professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card as a Business Services Agent in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals may apply for a Greek work permit sponsored by an employer, requiring a labor market test. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled roles with a salary threshold (currently €24,000–€28,000 gross/year). Business services agents may qualify if their position requires specialized knowledge or higher education.

**Q: What are the main duties of Business Services Agents in Greece?**
A: Business Services Agents in Greece perform tasks such as debt collection, business brokerage, or contract negotiation. They act as intermediaries between companies, handle client accounts, and ensure compliance with Greek tax and commercial laws. Fluency in Greek is typically required, along with knowledge of local market practices.
