## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for web and multimedia developers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average salary for web and multimedia developers (ISCO 2513) ranges from €18,000 to €30,000 per year, depending on experience, location (higher in Athens), and company size. Entry-level positions start around €14,000, while senior developers can earn up to €40,000. Salaries are lower than in Western Europe but reflect the local cost of living.

**Q: How can non-EU web or multimedia developers work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU developers can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card (for highly skilled workers) or a national work visa. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (around €28,000 in 2024). Alternatively, employers can sponsor a work visa for specific roles. Both routes require a labor market test and university degree or equivalent experience.

**Q: What are the job prospects for web developers in Greece?**
A: Job prospects for web and multimedia developers in Greece are growing, driven by digital transformation, tourism tech, and startup ecosystems, especially in Athens and Thessaloniki. Skills in JavaScript, React, and WordPress are in high demand. Remote work options are increasing, but local competition can be stiff for entry-level positions. Networking and upskilling are key to securing roles.
