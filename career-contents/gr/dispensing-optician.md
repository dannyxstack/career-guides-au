## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dispensing optician in Greece?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for dispensing opticians in Greece ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while experienced professionals in private practice can earn up to €1,800. Salaries in Athens and other urban centers tend to be higher.

**Q: Can non-EU dispensing opticians work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as dispensing opticians in Greece through the EU Blue Card if they have a binding job offer and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Alternatively, they may apply for a national work permit, which requires employer sponsorship and proof of qualifications. Both routes require recognition of their optician degree by Greek authorities.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a dispensing optician in Greece?**
A: To practice as a dispensing optician in Greece, you must hold a recognized degree in optometry or optics and be registered with the Hellenic Association of Opticians and Optometrists. Licensing from the Ministry of Health is also required. Foreign qualifications must be evaluated by the Hellenic National Academic Recognition and Information Center (DOATAP).
