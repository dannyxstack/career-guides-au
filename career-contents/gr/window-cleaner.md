## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for window cleaners in Greece?**
A: Window cleaners in Greece typically earn the statutory minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 per month gross. Experienced cleaners in cities like Athens may earn between €900 and €1,100 monthly. Pay is often hourly or per job, and self-employed cleaners can set their own rates.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens get a work visa or EU Blue Card for window cleaning in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a Greek work visa for window cleaning if they have a job offer and the employer secures a work permit. The EU Blue Card is not applicable because it requires a higher education degree or a high salary threshold, which window cleaning does not meet.

**Q: What are the career prospects for window cleaners in Greece?**
A: Window cleaning in Greece offers stable work in urban areas, with opportunities for self-employment. No formal qualifications are needed, but physical fitness and safety training are important. Career advancement may involve starting a cleaning company or specializing in high-rise building cleaning.
