## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a tailor or dressmaker in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience and location. As of 2024, the monthly minimum wage in Greece is €830. Starting tailors may earn around €800–€1,000, while skilled professionals in high-end shops or ateliers can earn €1,200–€1,500. Self-employed tailors set their own rates.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for tailors to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU tailors need a job offer from a Greek employer to apply for a national work visa (Type D). The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average; tailors typically do not meet these criteria. Seasonal work permits may be available for temporary positions.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a tailor in Greece?**
A: Formal training is available through public Vocational Training Institutes (IEK) offering 2-year programs in garment making. Apprenticeships with experienced tailors are common. While certification is not mandatory, it improves job prospects. Continuing education in fashion trends and sewing technology is beneficial.
