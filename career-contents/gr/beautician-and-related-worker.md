## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for beauticians in Greece?**
A: Entry-level beauticians in Greece earn around €600–€800 per month, while experienced professionals can make €1,000–€1,500. Salaries vary by location, with higher pay in Athens and tourist areas. Many beauticians also receive tips, which can significantly boost income. Self-employed workers set their own rates, typically charging €20–€50 per treatment.

**Q: Can non-EU beauticians obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU beauticians usually cannot qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree and a high-skilled position. However, they may obtain a Greek national work visa if sponsored by an employer. The employer must prove that no EU candidate is available. After five years of legal residence, a long-term residence permit may be possible.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a beautician in Greece?**
A: To work as a beautician in Greece, you typically need a vocational training diploma from a recognized school (IEK or private institute) or an apprenticeship certificate. Registration with the local Chamber of Professions (Epimelitirio) is required for self-employed workers. Licenses are valid nationwide, but continuous education is recommended to stay updated on techniques and products.
