## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for personal services workers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for personal services workers in Greece vary widely by occupation and experience. Entry-level positions earn around €700–€900 per month, while experienced workers in sectors like tourism or wellness can earn €1,000–€1,200. Many jobs pay the national minimum wage (€830/month in 2024) and may include tips or commission.

**Q: Can non-EU personal services workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Most personal services roles are considered low-skilled, making them ineligible for the EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree and a minimum gross salary of €3,425/month (2024). Non-EU workers typically need a Greek work permit sponsored by an employer, which is subject to labor market tests and quotas. Highly skilled exceptions exist for niche roles.

**Q: What career prospects exist for personal services workers in Greece?**
A: Greece's tourism and hospitality sectors offer opportunities for personal services workers such as tour guides, fitness instructors, and wedding planners. Self-employment is common. Demand fluctuates seasonally, with peak work in summer. Upskilling in languages, digital marketing, or niche services (e.g., pet care) improves prospects.
