**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standard conference interpreting (e.g., business negotiations)
- Simple telephone interpreting or community interpreting
- Basic subtitling/transcription proofreading
- Standardized oral exam scoring

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Real-time captioning assisting interpretation (AI providing background information)
- Term base management (AI automatically extracts terms)
- AI speech-to-text note-taking assistance during simultaneous interpretation
- Remote interpreting platforms (AI optimizes audio/video).
- Quality self-check (AI playback comparison against standard translation)

**Human moat:**

- Cultural context understanding and emotional conveyance
- Rapid decision-making under high pressure
- Confidentiality and ethical judgment
- Interpretation of Non-Verbal Signals (Tone, Body Language)
- Transcreation and rhetorical refinement

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for translators and interpreters in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely depending on language pair, specialization, and experience. Entry-level translators earn around €15,000-€20,000 annually, while experienced interpreters in high-demand languages (e.g., English, German) can earn €25,000-€40,000. Freelance rates range from €20-€50 per hour. Full-time positions in EU institutions or large corporations may offer higher pay with benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for linguists to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU linguists can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals, requiring a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €24,000). Alternatively, a national work permit is available for roles meeting labor market needs; the employer must prove no local candidate is suitable. Both require valid employment contract and residence permit.

**Q: Is there high demand for translators and interpreters in Greece?**
A: Demand is moderate but steady, particularly for Greek-English and other EU language pairs. The tourism, legal, and EU sectors offer opportunities. Localization for digital content and remote interpreting are growing fields. Fluency in English plus another European language, especially German or French, improves prospects. Professional certification from the Hellenic Translation Society can boost credibility.
