## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions may start around €18,000–€24,000 gross annually, while mid-level professionals earn €30,000–€45,000. Senior roles, especially in insurance or finance, can exceed €60,000. Public sector and academia tend to offer lower pay but greater stability.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Alternatively, a national work visa may be possible for positions on the shortage list. Both require a recognized degree and employer sponsorship. Specific conditions are detailed by the Greek Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

**Q: What are the main employers for mathematicians and statisticians in Greece?**
A: Key employers include banks, insurance companies, consulting firms, and tech companies. The Hellenic Statistical Authority (ELSTAT) and academic institutions also hire. Additionally, the growing data science and fintech sectors in Athens offer opportunities. Many professionals work in actuarial roles for insurance firms or as data analysts in large corporations.
