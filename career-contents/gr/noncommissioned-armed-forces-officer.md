## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary range for Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Greece?**
A: Salaries are set by the Greek military pay scale. A Sergeant (OR-5) earns around €1,100 gross monthly, plus allowances (housing, family). With rank progression (e.g., Master Sergeant), gross pay can reach €1,800. Senior non-commissioned officers with decades of service may exceed €2,000. Exact amounts depend on rank, years, and family status.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No. The EU Blue Card applies only to highly skilled civilian jobs, not military roles. Greek Armed Forces generally hire only Greek citizens for non-commissioned officer positions. Non-EU nationals cannot obtain a work visa or Blue Card for this occupation; exceptional access is limited to bilateral defense agreements, which are rare.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a Non-commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Greece?**
A: Candidates must be Greek citizens, aged 18–28, with completed secondary education. They must pass medical, psychological, and physical fitness tests, plus competitive entrance exams. Successful applicants attend the Non-Commissioned Officers School for training. A minimum service commitment (typically 5–10 years) is required after graduation.
