## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Sales and Marketing Managers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Sales and Marketing Managers in Greece vary by experience, industry, and company size. Entry-level managers may earn around €30,000-€40,000 annually, while senior managers in multinational firms can exceed €80,000. The average gross salary typically falls between €45,000 and €65,000 per year, with bonuses and commissions common in sales roles.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for Sales and Marketing Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece (approx. €28,000 in 2023, so about €42,000), a recognized higher education degree, and valid work contract. Employer must prove the position couldn't be filled by a local. The standard work visa also requires a job offer and employer sponsorship, processed through Greek consulates.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for Sales and Marketing Managers in Greece?**
A: Greece has a strong tourism and shipping sector, offering roles in hospitality, maritime, and retail. Digital marketing and export-oriented companies are growing, particularly in Athens and Thessaloniki. Managers may advance to director-level positions or specialize in sectors like tech or pharmaceuticals. Proficiency in Greek and English is essential, while knowledge of other European languages is advantageous.
