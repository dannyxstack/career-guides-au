## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for legal and related associate professionals in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level legal associates (e.g., paralegals) earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals can reach €1,800–€2,500. Salaries vary by region, with Athens and Thessaloniki offering higher pay, and by employer type (law firms vs. corporate). Bonuses are uncommon.

**Q: What is the work-visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU legal associate professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer as a legal associate, a recognized degree, and a salary at least 1.5 times the Greek average gross annual salary. Alternatively, a national work permit may be obtained for specific positions, requiring employer sponsorship and labor market testing.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for legal associate professionals in Greece?**
A: Legal associates can advance to senior paralegal or legal manager roles with experience. Many pursue additional qualifications to become fully licensed lawyers (dikigoros) after passing the bar exam, opening paths to private practice, in-house counsel, or judicial positions. Specialization in areas like corporate or maritime law enhances prospects.
