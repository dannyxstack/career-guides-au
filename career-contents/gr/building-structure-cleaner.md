## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building structure cleaners in Greece?**
A: Salaries for building structure cleaners in Greece generally align with the national minimum wage, which is €780 gross per month as of 2024. Experienced cleaners or those working for larger companies may earn between €800 and €1,000 per month for full-time work. Overtime and hazardous pay may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as building structure cleaners in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as building structure cleaners in Greece through the standard national work permit process, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable for this occupation, as it targets highly skilled workers with higher education or high salaries, not low-skilled roles.

**Q: What are the main tasks and requirements for building structure cleaners in Greece?**
A: Building structure cleaners in Greece clean exteriors like windows, facades, and roofs, often working at heights with harnesses and lifts. No formal education is required, but safety training and a certificate for working at height are common. Physical stamina, comfort with heights, and basic knowledge of cleaning agents are essential.
