## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ambulance workers in Greece?**
A: Ambulance workers in Greece earn a gross monthly salary typically between €800 and €1,200. Public sector wages follow standardized scales, while private employers may offer slightly higher pay. Shift differentials and overtime can increase earnings. Overall, the occupation is not high-paying compared to other roles in healthcare.

**Q: Can non-EU ambulance workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals must secure a job offer from a Greek employer, who then applies for a work permit on their behalf. The EU Blue Card is generally not available for ambulance workers, as it is reserved for highly qualified professionals. Applicants should verify current requirements with the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What are the typical requirements and duties for ambulance workers in Greece?**
A: Ambulance workers in Greece require a high school diploma and completion of a specialized ambulance training course. Duties include providing emergency first aid, transporting patients, and assisting paramedics. They often work for public hospitals or private services. Continuous training in CPR and advanced techniques is expected.
