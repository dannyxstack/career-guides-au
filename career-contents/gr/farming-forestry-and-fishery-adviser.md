## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Greece vary by sector and experience. According to Greek labor data, the average gross monthly salary ranges from €1,200 to €1,800. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior advisers can earn up to €2,500 or more, depending on the employer and region.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work as advisers in Greece via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (set annually by Greek law). Alternatively, a national work permit sponsored by an employer is possible; both routes involve approval from the Greek Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Farming, Forestry or Fisheries Adviser in Greece?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in agricultural science, forestry, fisheries, or a related field is required. Many employers prefer a master's degree or specialized training. Practical experience in the sector and knowledge of Greek agricultural policies is advantageous. Registration with the Greek Geotechnical Chamber (GEOTEE) may be necessary for certain advisory roles.
