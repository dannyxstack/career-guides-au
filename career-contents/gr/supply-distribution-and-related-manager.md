## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Greece vary based on experience and company size. Entry-level managers earn around €24,000–€30,000 annually, while senior managers can earn €40,000–€60,000 per year. Bonuses and benefits may apply. Major cities like Athens and Thessaloniki often offer higher pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Greece via an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified employment) or a national work visa. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, a recognized university degree, and a contract of at least one year. The employer must apply to the Greek authorities. Other routes include intra-company transfers.

**Q: What skills and qualifications are most valued for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Greece?**
A: Employers look for a bachelor's degree in logistics, supply chain management, or business administration. Knowledge of Greek and English is essential. Experience with ERP systems, strong analytical skills, and familiarity with Greek transportation networks and customs regulations are highly valued. Professional certifications like CSCP or CPIM can boost career prospects.
