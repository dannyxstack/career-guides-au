## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for ships’ deck officers and pilots in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by rank and experience. A junior deck officer may earn €1,200–€1,800 per month, while a captain can earn €3,000–€5,000. Pilots in Greek ports earn around €2,000–€4,000 monthly, depending on seniority and port traffic. Overtime and sea service bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU deck officers work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU deck officers can apply for a Greek EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, a recognized degree or equivalent qualification (e.g., STCW certification), and meet salary thresholds of at least 1.5 times the national average salary. The employer must prove the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA national. Recognition of seafarer credentials is required from the Greek maritime authorities.

**Q: What are the career prospects for deck officers in Greece?**
A: Career progression typically starts as a deck cadet, advancing to third officer, second officer, chief officer, and captain. Greek shipping companies offer opportunities on bulk carriers, tankers, and passenger vessels. With experience, officers can specialize as harbor pilots, maritime inspectors, or shore-based fleet managers. Continuous training and STCW endorsements enhance prospects.
