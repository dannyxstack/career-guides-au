## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, vessel size, and catch. Entry-level workers may earn around €800-€1,200 per month, while experienced fishermen can earn €1,500-€2,500 or more, often with profit-sharing. Income is seasonal and depends on fishing quotas and market prices.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as deep-sea fishery workers in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Greek national work visa (type D) if they have a job offer from a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers; deep-sea fishery work does not typically qualify due to skill level requirements. Employer must demonstrate labor market needs.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a deep-sea fishery worker in Greece?**
A: No formal education is required, but basic maritime training certificates (e.g., STCW) are often mandatory. Practical experience on fishing vessels is crucial. Knowledge of Greek waters, fishing techniques, and safety procedures is essential. Knowledge of Greek language is highly beneficial for communication.
