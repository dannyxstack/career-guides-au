## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Greece?**
A: Monthly gross salaries for Aged Care Services Managers in Greece typically range from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, location, and facility size. According to national salary surveys, managers in Athens or private institutions may earn higher. Salaries often include bonuses or benefits like accommodation in some cases.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Aged Care Services Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a Greek work permit through employer sponsorship after a labor market test. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (approximately €1,800 gross monthly). Both routes require a recognized degree and relevant experience.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an Aged Care Services Manager in Greece?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in nursing, social work, health management, or a related field is required. Many employers prefer a master's in healthcare administration. Professional experience of at least 3–5 years in elderly care settings is essential. Registration with relevant Greek professional bodies may be mandatory for regulated roles.
