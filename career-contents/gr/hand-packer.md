## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for hand packers in Greece?**
A: Hand packers in Greece typically earn the national minimum wage, which as of 2023 is €780 per month for full-time work. Wages may vary slightly by region and employer, with some offering up to €850 per month. Overtime and bonuses are uncommon in this entry-level role.

**Q: What are the work visa options for hand packers in Greece, such as the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a high-skilled job, so hand packers generally do not qualify. The main route is a national work visa for low-skilled workers, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. Third-country nationals can apply through the Greek Manpower Employment Organization (OAED) for seasonal or permanent labor permits.

**Q: What are common working conditions for hand packers in Greece?**
A: Hand packers usually work in warehouses, factories, or farms, often on fixed-term or seasonal contracts. Shifts can be day or night, and work is physically demanding with repetitive tasks. Employers must provide health and safety training, but conditions vary. Some workers are hired through temporary agencies.
