## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Greece?**
A: The minimum wage in Greece is €830 per month (as of 2024), and livestock farm labourers typically earn this amount or slightly higher. Some employers provide accommodation or meals. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may apply. Pay can vary by region and farm size.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as livestock farm labourers in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Greek national visa for employment (type D) with a valid job offer. Seasonal work permits are available for temporary agricultural work, often under bilateral agreements with countries like Albania and Pakistan. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and is not applicable for this occupation. Always consult the Greek embassy or consulate for current procedures.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for livestock farm labourers in Greece?**
A: With experience, labourers can become team leaders or farm supervisors. Some pursue further training in animal husbandry or agricultural management. Opportunities to own or manage a small livestock farm exist but require capital and knowledge. Continuous learning through vocational programs can enhance skills and lead to higher-paying roles.
