## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a gardener in Greece?**
A: In Greece, gardeners typically earn between €700 and €1,200 per month, depending on experience, location, and whether they are self-employed or employed by a company. Seasonal work may offer lower pay, while specialized gardeners (e.g., in landscaping) can earn higher rates. Net income varies after taxes and social security contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU gardeners work in Greece via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU gardeners may work in Greece through a national work visa for low-skilled or seasonal labor, as the EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and specific salary thresholds, typically not met by gardeners. Employers must apply for a work permit, and quotas may apply. Seasonal work permits are available for up to 9 months.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a gardener in Greece?**
A: Formal qualifications are not always required, but a vocational training certificate in agriculture or horticulture is beneficial. Knowledge of local plants and climate is essential. Many gardeners learn through apprenticeship or experience. Self-employment requires registration with the Greek tax authority (AADE) and a business license.
