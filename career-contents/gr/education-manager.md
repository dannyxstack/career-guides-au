## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Education Manager in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Education Managers in Greece vary by experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions earn around €20,000-€28,000 annually, while experienced managers in private schools or higher education can earn €35,000-€50,000. Public sector salaries are lower and follow state pay scales. Benefits often include 13th and 14th month pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for an Education Manager to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Education Managers in Greece via the EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approximately €28,000/year in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit requires employer sponsorship and labor market test. Both routes lead to residence permits.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Education Manager in Greece?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in education, administration, or related field is required, with many employers preferring a master's. Relevant experience in teaching or school management is essential, often 5+ years. Additional certification in educational leadership or school management is advantageous. Knowledge of Greek education laws and curriculum is important for public schools.
