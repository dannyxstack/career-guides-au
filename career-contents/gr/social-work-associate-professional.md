## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Social Work Associate Professional in Greece?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a Social Work Associate Professional in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, sector (public or private), and location. Public sector roles may offer slightly higher pay and benefits, while entry-level positions in NGOs or private clinics tend to start at the lower end.

**Q: Can non-EU Social Work Associate Professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card if they hold a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece. Alternatively, a national work permit is available for lower-skilled roles, requiring employer sponsorship. Both routes require compliance with Greek immigration laws.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Social Work Associate Professional in Greece?**
A: A recognized degree in social work or a related field from an accredited institution is mandatory. Professionals must register with the Social Work Association of Greece (SWAG) to practice legally. Some roles may require additional certifications, such as in counseling or child protection. Continuous professional development is encouraged for career advancement.
