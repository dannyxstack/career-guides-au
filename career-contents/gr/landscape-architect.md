## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for landscape architects in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level landscape architects earn around €12,000-€18,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €25,000-€40,000. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Athens and for those working in private firms or large projects. Public sector positions may offer lower starting salaries but greater job security.

**Q: What are the official work routes for non-EU landscape architects to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a valid job offer, a contract of at least 12 months, and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. Alternatively, a national work visa (D type) is available for specific employment, subject to a labor market test.

**Q: What are the main job prospects for landscape architects in Greece?**
A: Landscape architects in Greece find opportunities in private design firms, construction companies, and government agencies involved in urban planning and environmental projects. Growing demand exists in sustainable tourism and coastal development. Fluency in Greek is often required, and knowledge of Greek landscape regulations is highly beneficial.
