## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Human Resource Manager in Greece?**
A: The average annual salary for a Human Resource Manager in Greece ranges from €35,000 to €55,000, depending on experience, company size, and location (higher in Athens). Senior roles in large corporations can exceed €70,000. Bonuses and benefits are common. Entry-level or junior positions start around €25,000.

**Q: What are the work-visa or EU Blue Card options for a Human Resource Manager to work in Greece from a non-EU country?**
A: Non-EU HR Managers can obtain a Greek work visa through employer sponsorship, following a labor market test. Alternatively, they may qualify for the EU Blue Card (for high-skilled roles) requiring a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €33,000 in 2024). The employer must prove the role cannot be filled locally. Both routes lead to temporary residency.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are important for a Human Resource Manager career in Greece?**
A: A bachelor’s degree in HR, business, or psychology is typically required; a master’s in HR or an MBA is advantageous. Professional certifications like SHRM or CIPD are valued. Key skills include labor law knowledge (Greek), communication, conflict resolution, and familiarity with Greek business culture. Fluency in Greek is often essential, though English is widely used in multinational firms.
