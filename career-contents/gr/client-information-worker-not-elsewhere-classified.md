## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Client Information Workers in Greece?**
A: For Client Information Workers not elsewhere classified in Greece, gross monthly salaries typically range from €800 to €1,400, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start around the minimum wage (€830 per month in 2024), while senior roles in large companies can exceed €1,500. Collective bargaining agreements in sectors like call centers often set specific pay scales.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as a Client Information Worker in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a national work visa (type D) if they have a job offer from a Greek employer. The employer must prove no suitable EU candidate is available. For highly qualified roles, the EU Blue Card is an option, requiring a bachelor's degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€28,056 in 2024). Both routes require a valid residence and work permit.

**Q: What are the main duties and sectors for Client Information Workers in Greece?**
A: Client Information Workers in Greece handle inquiries via phone, email, or chat, providing product or service information. They often work in call centers, customer service departments of banks, telecoms, tourism, and e-commerce companies. Duties include explaining company policies, processing orders, resolving complaints, and updating databases. Fluency in Greek and English is typically required, with additional languages being advantageous.
