## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for vocational education teachers in Greece?**
A: In public vocational schools (EPAL, IEK), entry-level teachers earn around €1,100 gross monthly, rising to €1,800 with seniority. Private institutes offer similar ranges. Supplemental pay for overtime or specializations can increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for vocational education teachers in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work visa sponsored by a Greek employer, or an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for vocational education teachers in Greece?**
A: Dual system reforms and EU-funded programs have increased demand in tourism, IT, and healthcare. Public hiring through ASEP exams offers permanent positions, while private IEKs and colleges provide additional roles. Continuous professional development is supported through state training programs.
