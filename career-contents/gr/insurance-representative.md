## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an insurance representative in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for insurance representatives in Greece ranges from €1,000 to €1,800, depending on experience, company size, and sales performance. Entry-level positions start around €800, while experienced representatives can earn up to €2,500 including commissions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as insurance representatives in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as insurance representatives in Greece through the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold (currently €35,300 annually for regulated professions). Alternatively, a standard Greek work permit is available for those with a job offer, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from the Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an insurance representative in Greece?**
A: Typically, a high school diploma is required, but a bachelor's degree in business or finance is advantageous. Completion of training courses accredited by the Bank of Greece and registration with the Insurance Salespeople Registry are mandatory. Fluency in Greek is essential, and some companies prefer prior sales experience.
