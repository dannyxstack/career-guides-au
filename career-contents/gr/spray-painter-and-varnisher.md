## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a spray painter and varnisher in Greece?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for spray painters and varnishers in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced professionals in specialized sectors can earn higher wages. Salaries are often based on collective bargaining agreements in the construction or manufacturing industries.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a spray painter to work in Greece?**
A: The primary route is a Greek national visa for employment (Type D), requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is available only for highly skilled workers with a university degree or high salary; spray painting typically does not meet these criteria. Third-country nationals must also obtain a residence permit through their employer, with procedures handled by the Greek Migration Ministry.

**Q: Are there specific certifications needed to work as a spray painter in Greece?**
A: Yes, a vocational training certificate (e.g., from IEK or OAED) or apprenticeship in industrial painting or varnishing is often required. For automotive painting, a certificate from the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport may be needed. Additionally, health and safety training (e.g., handling hazardous materials) is strongly recommended and sometimes mandatory in larger firms.
