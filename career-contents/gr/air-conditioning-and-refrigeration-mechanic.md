## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Greece?**
A: In Greece, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics typically earn between €1,200 and €1,500 gross per month. Experienced professionals or those in specialized roles can earn up to €1,800 or more. Salaries vary by region, employer, and level of certification.

**Q: Can a non-EU air conditioning mechanic work in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, but the EU Blue Card in Greece requires a job offer with a gross annual salary of at least €35,000, a university degree (or equivalent professional experience), and a valid work contract. Alternatively, a national work visa may be available for positions below the Blue Card threshold, typically through employer sponsorship.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as an air conditioning mechanic in Greece?**
A: In Greece, mechanics typically need a vocational training certificate from recognized institutions (e.g., IEK or OAED), and often a license for handling refrigerants (F-gas certification). Many employers also require specific manufacturer training. Continuous learning is important due to evolving environmental regulations.
