## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cement and stone machine operators in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average monthly gross salary for cement and stone machine operators ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and region. Overtime and shift work can increase earnings. Salaries are typically paid 14 times per year including holiday and Christmas bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU nationals to work as cement or stone machine operators in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (currently at least €19,208 annually). Alternatively, a Greek national visa for employment (Type D) requires a confirmed job offer and employer-initiated work permit. Employers must demonstrate that no EU candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for cement and stone machine operators in Greece?**
A: Experienced operators can advance to supervisor or quality control roles. Additional certifications in safety, machinery maintenance, or concrete technology can lead to higher positions. Larger companies like Titan Cement offer internal training programs, and some operators transition into related fields such as construction or quarry management.
