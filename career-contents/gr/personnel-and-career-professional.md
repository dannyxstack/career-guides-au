## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for personnel and careers professionals in Greece?**
A: Salary varies by experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions generally earn €18,000–€25,000 annually, while mid-level professionals make €25,000–€40,000. Senior roles, such as HR directors, can exceed €50,000. Performance bonuses and benefits are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU personnel professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain an EU Blue Card (for highly skilled workers with a degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds) or a national work visa (employer-sponsored, subject to labor market testing). Both require approval from the Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for personnel professionals in Greece?**
A: Career paths include moving from HR assistant or recruiter to specialist roles (e.g., training, compensation), then to HR manager, director, or Chief HR Officer. Many professionals also transition to consulting or specialize in talent acquisition, organizational development, or HR analytics.
