## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a lifting truck operator in Greece?**
A: Salaries for lifting truck operators in Greece vary by experience and region, typically ranging from €800 to €1,200 gross per month. Entry-level positions may start near the national minimum wage (€830 in 2024), while experienced operators in industrial zones like Attica or Thessaloniki can earn higher.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a lifting truck operator in Greece through the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Greece as lifting truck operators through a national work permit if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree or equivalent skills, which typically does not apply to this occupation. Alternatively, the Greek Ministry of Migration issues permits for low- and medium-skilled workers under quotas.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a lifting truck operator in Greece?**
A: Operators need a valid license (e.g., for forklifts) from an accredited school, and often a certificate of vocational training. On-the-job training is common. Knowledge of Greek language is usually required. Certifications from organizations like IEK or OAED are recognized.
