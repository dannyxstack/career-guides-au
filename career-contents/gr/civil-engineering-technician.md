**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generates standard construction drawings and 2D/3D models
- Automatically calculate material quantities and cost estimates.
- Monitor on-site construction progress and generate reports
- Inspect structural integrity and mark issues

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Structural analysis and simulation testing using AI
- Automated inspection via drones and sensors
- Optimize construction scheduling and resource allocation
- Quickly generate design options for multi-scenario comparison
- Real-time monitoring of construction quality and early warning of anomalies.

**Human moat:**

- Site safety supervision and emergency decision-making
- Coordination with engineers, clients, and construction crews
- Handling complex working conditions and non-standard designs
- Responsible for legal compliance and professional ethics

**Skills to build:**

- In-depth application of BIM software (e.g., Revit, Navisworks)
- Basic data analysis with Python or R
- GIS Geographic Information System Operation
- AI-assisted design tools (such as Autodesk Forma)

- Project management and communication skills

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for civil engineering technicians in Greece?**
A: In Greece, civil engineering technicians earn between €18,000 and €30,000 annually, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €18,000, while senior technicians with specialized skills can reach €30,000. Public sector jobs may offer lower base pay but include benefits.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU civil engineering technicians to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU civil engineering technicians can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card if they have a recognized degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Greek average gross salary (approx. €37,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work visa (type D) requires employer sponsorship, a labor market test, and a residence permit.

**Q: How is AI affecting the role of civil engineering technicians in Greece?**
A: AI tools are increasingly used in Greece for structural analysis, BIM modeling, and project management. Technicians may need to upskill in software like Revit or CAD with AI plugins. While AI automates routine drafting, it enhances precision and efficiency, requiring technicians to focus on interpretation and quality control.
