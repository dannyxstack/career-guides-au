## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for environmental engineers in Greece?**
A: Entry-level environmental engineers in Greece earn around €18,000–€24,000 annually, while experienced professionals can make €35,000–€50,000. Salaries vary by sector: private consulting firms and energy companies tend to pay higher than public agencies. Regional differences also apply, with Athens and Thessaloniki offering higher pay than smaller cities.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU environmental engineers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU environmental engineers can apply for a Greek national work visa (type D) if they secure a job offer and meet salary thresholds. For highly qualified professionals, the EU Blue Card (Greece) is available, requiring a valid employment contract, recognized qualifications, and a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Greece. Applications are processed through Greek consulates and the Ministry of Migration.

**Q: Which sectors employ environmental engineers in Greece?**
A: Environmental engineers in Greece work in consulting firms (environmental impact assessments), renewable energy companies (solar, wind), waste management and water treatment facilities, construction (green building), and government agencies (ministries, regional authorities). The growing focus on sustainability and EU-funded projects drives demand, especially in consulting and energy sectors.
