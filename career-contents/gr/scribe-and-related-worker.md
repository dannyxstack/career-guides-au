## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for scribes and related workers in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for scribes and related workers in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,500, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced workers in Athens or with specialized skills can earn up to €1,800. Part-time and freelance roles are common.

**Q: Can scribes and related workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: Yes, scribes and related workers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a university degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. Alternatively, they may obtain a National Visa for employment if the job is classified as a shortage occupation. Applications are processed by the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a scribe in Greece?**
A: To work as a scribe in Greece, a high school diploma is typically required, along with specialized training in shorthand, transcription, or office administration. Fluency in Greek is essential, and knowledge of English or other languages is advantageous. Certification from vocational institutes or previous experience in legal or medical transcription can improve job prospects.
