## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a freight handler in Greece?**
A: The gross monthly salary for freight handlers in Greece typically ranges from €780 to €1,100, often based on the statutory minimum wage. Overtime, night shifts, and collective agreements may increase earnings. Pay varies by employer, region, and experience, with some large logistics companies offering slightly higher rates.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a freight handler in Greece via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Freight handlers generally do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher-education degree or highly skilled position. Non-EU nationals can apply for a National Visa (Type D) for employment if a Greek employer sponsors them. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally, and the worker must meet salary thresholds (e.g., at least minimum wage) and legal requirements.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for freight handlers in Greece?**
A: With experience, freight handlers can move into supervisory roles such as warehouse shift manager or logistics coordinator. Further training in supply chain management or forklift operation can lead to higher-paying positions. Some pursue certifications in logistics or transport management to advance within large distribution companies in Greece.
