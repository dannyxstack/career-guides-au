**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

- Non-standard problem solving: improvisational modification skills for old houses and non-standard materials
- On-site customer trust: direct communication with clients, calming emotions, providing reliable service

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for plumbers and pipe fitters in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for plumbers and pipe fitters in Greece ranges from €900 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Experienced professionals in high-demand areas like Athens may earn higher wages, while entry-level positions start at lower rates. Overtime and specialized skills can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for plumbers and pipe fitters to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU plumbers can work in Greece via an employer-sponsored work visa or the EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized university degree or at least five years of professional experience. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Greek or EU worker. Self-employment requires a separate permit.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a plumber in Greece?**
A: In Greece, plumbers typically need a vocational training certificate (e.g., from IEK or EPAS) or an apprenticeship. Professional experience is highly valued. Registration with the Greek Chamber of Tradesmen may be required for self-employment. Continuous learning of new technologies and regulations is essential for career advancement.
