## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. In the public sector, entry-level psychologists earn around €1,000–€1,200 per month, while private practice can yield €1,500–€2,500. Senior roles in hospitals or academia may reach €3,000. Private practice income depends on client base and location.

**Q: How can foreign psychologists work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Psychologists are a regulated profession in Greece. To work under the EU Blue Card, you need a valid job offer, a recognized university degree in psychology, and recognition of your qualifications by the Greek National Organization for the Certification of Qualifications (EOPPEP). The employer must offer at least the national salary threshold for the Blue Card (€1,352 in 2024).

**Q: What are common career paths for psychologists in Greece?**
A: Psychologists in Greece work in public hospitals, private clinics, schools, and as independent practitioners. Many specialize in clinical, counseling, or organizational psychology. Others enter academia or research. The profession is growing, with increased demand in mental health services and HR departments.
