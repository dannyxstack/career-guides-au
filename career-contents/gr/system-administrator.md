## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Systems Administrator in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Systems Administrators in Greece vary by experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €18,000-€24,000 annually, while mid-level roles earn €28,000-€40,000. Senior positions or those in Athens may reach €45,000-€60,000. Additional certifications and skills in cloud or security can increase pay.

**Q: What work visa options are available for foreign Systems Administrators wanting to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card if they have a recognized degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece (approx. €28,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit (e.g., for highly qualified workers) requires employer sponsorship and approval from the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications are most in demand for Systems Administrators in Greece?**
A: Employers seek expertise in Linux/Windows server administration, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), networking (Cisco), and cybersecurity. Certification such as CompTIA Server+, MCSA, or RHCE is valued. A bachelor's degree in IT or related field is typical. Fluency in English is often required; Greek language skills are advantageous for local teams.
