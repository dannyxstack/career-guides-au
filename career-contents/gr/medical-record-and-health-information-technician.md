## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical records and health information technician in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions in public hospitals start around €13,000–€15,000 annually, while experienced technicians in private clinics can earn up to €22,000. Public sector salaries follow national collective agreements, with additional allowances for shift work.

**Q: Can non-EU medical records and health information technicians work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU technicians may apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Greek average gross annual salary. They must also hold a recognized qualification. The application is submitted to the Greek Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a medical records and health information technician in Greece?**
A: Typically, a post-secondary diploma in health information management or a related field from a recognized Greek institution is required. Certification from the Hellenic Association of Medical Records and Health Informatics may be beneficial. Continuing education is encouraged for career advancement.
