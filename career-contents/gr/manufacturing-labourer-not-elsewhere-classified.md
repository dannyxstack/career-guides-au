## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a manufacturing labourer in Greece?**
A: Manufacturing labourers in Greece typically earn around the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 per month (14 payments) or €968 per month (12 payments). Actual pay can vary by region and employer, with some offering slightly higher wages. Overtime may increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a manufacturing labourer in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is designed for highly skilled workers and requires a higher education degree or equivalent professional experience plus a salary threshold. Manufacturing labourers (ISCO 9329) typically do not meet these requirements. Non-EU workers may instead apply for a national work visa based on an employer-sponsored permit, subject to a labour market test.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for manufacturing labourers in Greece?**
A: Manufacturing labourers in Greece perform simple tasks such as sorting, packing, loading, and cleaning in factories or workshops. They may operate basic machinery and assist skilled workers. Working conditions often involve shifts, repetitive tasks, and physical effort. The job requires no formal education, but on-the-job training is provided.
