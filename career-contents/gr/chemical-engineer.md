## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for chemical engineers in Greece?**
A: Entry-level chemical engineers in Greece earn around €18,000–€24,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €30,000–€45,000. Salaries vary by industry, location, and company size, with higher pay in petrochemicals and pharmaceuticals.

**Q: How can a non-EU chemical engineer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Non-EU chemical engineers can apply for a Greek work visa or the EU Blue Card by securing a job offer from a Greek employer meeting the minimum salary threshold. The employer must obtain a work permit, after which the engineer applies for a visa at the Greek consulate. The Blue Card requires a recognized university degree and a contract of at least one year.

**Q: Which industries employ chemical engineers in Greece?**
A: Chemical engineers in Greece find opportunities in petrochemicals (e.g., Hellenic Petroleum), pharmaceuticals, food and beverage processing, energy (including renewables), and environmental consulting. The shipping and mining sectors also hire for roles in fuel and materials processing. Many work in Athens, Thessaloniki, or near industrial zones.
