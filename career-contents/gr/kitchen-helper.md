## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for kitchen helpers in Greece?**
A: The typical monthly salary for kitchen helpers in Greece ranges from €600 to €900 gross, depending on the region and establishment. In Athens, it may be slightly higher. Part-time or casual workers often earn minimum wage (currently €713 per month for full-time). Tips are common in some restaurants but not guaranteed.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as kitchen helpers in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as kitchen helpers through the EU Blue Card only if the job requires high skills, which this role typically does not. For unskilled positions, employers must apply for a national work permit, proving they cannot find local workers. The process involves labor market tests and long processing times. Seasonal work visas are also available for short-term roles.

**Q: What are the main duties of a kitchen helper in Greece?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Greece assist with food preparation, washing dishes, cleaning kitchen areas, and storing supplies. They may peel vegetables, prepare basic ingredients, and maintain cleanliness. In busy Greek tavernas, they also help with plating and serving. The role is physically demanding and often requires flexibility with hours, including weekends and holidays.
