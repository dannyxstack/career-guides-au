## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for forestry technicians in Greece?**
A: Entry-level forestry technicians in Greece earn around €12,000–€15,000 annually. Experienced technicians can earn €18,000–€24,000 per year, with senior roles in public forestry services or private companies reaching up to €30,000. Salaries vary by region, employer (public vs. private), and seasonality of work.

**Q: Can a non-EU forestry technician work in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals can apply for a national work visa (Type D) if they have a job offer from a Greek employer. For the EU Blue Card, you need a university degree (or equivalent) and a binding job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece (approximately €26,000 in 2023). The employer must prove no suitable local candidate.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for forestry technicians in Greece?**
A: Forestry technicians in Greece work in public forest services, private forestry companies, NGOs, and research institutes. Key tasks include forest inventory, fire prevention, reforestation, and land management. Career progression can lead to supervisory roles, specialization in GIS or environmental assessment, or advancement to forestry engineer after further studies.
