## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mining managers in Greece?**
A: Mining managers in Greece earn between €45,000 and €75,000 per year, depending on experience, company size, and location (e.g., lignite mines in Western Macedonia or metal mines in Chalkidiki). Senior managers or those overseeing large operations may earn higher, including bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU mining managers work in Greece through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU mining managers can apply for the EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a university degree or five years of relevant experience, a binding job offer with a salary meeting the national threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary), and meet other requirements like valid travel documents and health insurance.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a mining manager in Greece?**
A: Typically, a degree in mining engineering, geology, or a related field is required, along with several years of experience in mining operations. Licensing from the Technical Chamber of Greece (TEE) may be necessary. Many managers also hold an MSc or MBA, and familiarity with Greek mining regulations and safety standards is essential.
