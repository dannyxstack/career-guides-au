## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a refuse sorter in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for refuse sorters in Greece ranges from €700 to €900 gross, depending on the employer and region. This is typically based on the national minimum wage (€780 per month in 2024) with some variation. Overtime or additional bonuses are rare in this role.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a refuse sorter in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other visa?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is designed for highly qualified workers with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (€2,160 in 2024), which refuse sorters do not meet. Instead, a standard work permit for low-skilled labor may be possible if the employer proves no local candidates are available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for refuse sorters in Greece?**
A: Refuse sorters can advance to team leader or supervisor roles with experience. Some may transition to recycling facility operators or waste management technicians after training. On-the-job training is common, and vocational programs in environmental management or recycling technologies can improve promotion prospects.
