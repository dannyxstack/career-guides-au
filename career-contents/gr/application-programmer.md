## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for applications programmers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, applications programmers earn between €25,000 and €45,000 annually on average, varying by experience, location, and company size. Junior roles start around €18,000–€22,000, while senior positions can exceed €50,000. Athens offers higher salaries, and benefits often include bonuses and health insurance.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU applications programmers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU applications programmers can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a recognized degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €28,000). Alternatively, a national work permit sponsored by an employer is possible. Both require employer sponsorship and valid documentation.

**Q: How is the job market for applications programmers in Greece?**
A: The demand for applications programmers in Greece is stable, driven by a growing tech sector and digitalization. Hubs like Athens and Thessaloniki offer many opportunities. English is common in tech firms, but Greek is beneficial. Remote work is increasing. Proficiency in modern languages (Java, Python, JavaScript) and continuous learning are key for career growth.
