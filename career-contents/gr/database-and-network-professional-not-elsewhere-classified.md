## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for database and network professionals in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, location, and company. Entry-level positions may start around €1,200 gross monthly, while experienced professionals can earn €2,500 or more. Senior roles or those in Athens may pay higher. Bonuses and benefits are less common than in some other EU countries.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for database and network professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary (updated by the Hellenic Statistical Authority). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Alternatively, a national work visa may be obtained for other qualified roles.

**Q: What are the key skills and qualifications needed for database and network roles in Greece?**
A: Employers typically seek a bachelor’s degree in computer science or related field, plus certifications like Oracle, Cisco, or Microsoft. Fluency in English is often required, and Greek is helpful. Experience with cloud services, cybersecurity, and specific database systems (SQL, NoSQL) is increasingly valued. The demand is robust in Athens and tech hubs.
