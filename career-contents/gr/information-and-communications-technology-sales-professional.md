## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Sales Professionals in Greece?**
A: Salaries for ICT sales professionals in Greece vary by experience and employer. Entry-level roles may start around €18,000–€25,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €40,000–€70,000, often with commission. Major tech hubs like Athens and Thessaloniki offer higher pay. Multinational companies and startups in sectors like SaaS and cloud services tend to provide competitive compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU professionals work as ICT Sales Professionals in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can apply for the EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a valid job offer in a highly skilled occupation, including ICT sales, and meet salary thresholds. The minimum gross annual salary for 2025 is set by Greek law (currently around €32,000). Applicants must hold a recognized tertiary degree or five years of professional experience. The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What career growth opportunities exist for ICT Sales Professionals in Greece?**
A: ICT sales professionals in Greece can advance to senior sales roles, account management, or sales management. The growing tech ecosystem, especially in Athens' 'Thermi' Valley and Thessaloniki's tech hubs, offers paths into business development, partnerships, or product management. Multinational companies provide international mobility, while local startups offer rapid leadership growth. Continuous learning in cloud, AI, and cybersecurity sales is key.
