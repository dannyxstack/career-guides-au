**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Greece?**
A: In Greece, salary for garment patternmakers and cutters ranges from €800 to €1,300 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start near the minimum wage (€780 per month in 2024), while experienced professionals in Athens or with specialized skills can earn higher. Overtime and bonuses may supplement base pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a garment patternmaker or cutter in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is available for highly skilled non-EU workers with a job offer and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. For garment patternmakers and cutters, this is unlikely as the occupation typically does not meet the required salary threshold or higher education requirement. Other routes, such as a national work visa for specialized employment, may be possible if a labor market test shows no suitable EU candidate.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a garment patternmaker or cutter in Greece?**
A: In Greece, garment patternmakers and cutters typically complete vocational training at IEK (Vocational Training Institutes) in fashion design or garment technology, or apprenticeship programs. A secondary education diploma is usually required. Practical experience is highly valued, and proficiency in computer-aided design (CAD) software like Gerber or Lectra is increasingly important for employment in larger firms.
