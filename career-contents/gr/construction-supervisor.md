## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for construction supervisors in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for construction supervisors in Greece ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience, project size, and location. Senior supervisors or those on large infrastructure projects may earn higher. Salaries are typically higher in Athens and Thessaloniki.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU construction supervisors in Greece?**
A: Non-EU construction supervisors can apply for a national work visa (type D) sponsored by a Greek employer, or the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and the job meets the salary threshold. Both require a valid employment offer and compliance with Greek labor regulations.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a construction supervisor in Greece?**
A: Generally, a technical diploma or bachelor's degree in civil engineering or construction management is preferred. Practical experience in construction site supervision and knowledge of Greek building regulations and safety standards are essential. Certification from the Technical Chamber of Greece (TEE) may be required for certain roles.
