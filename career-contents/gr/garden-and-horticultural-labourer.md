## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Greece?**
A: Garden and horticultural labourers in Greece usually earn the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 per month (14 payments) or about €7.83 per hour. Actual wages may vary depending on the employer, region, and individual experience, but generally remain at or slightly above the minimum wage.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for garden and horticultural labourers in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit to work as garden or horticultural labourers in Greece. This occupation is typically low-skilled, so the EU Blue Card, reserved for highly qualified workers, does not apply. The main route is a residence permit for employment under Greek Law 4251/2014, requiring a job offer and labor market test. Employer sponsorship is essential.

**Q: What are the typical duties of a garden and horticultural labourer in Greece?**
A: Typical duties include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, and harvesting plants, trees, and crops in gardens, parks, or farms. They also prepare soil, apply fertilizers, and operate hand tools or light machinery. Labourers may assist with landscape maintenance, pest control, and irrigation systems under the supervision of gardeners or horticulturists.
