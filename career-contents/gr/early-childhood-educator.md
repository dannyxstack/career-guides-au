## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for early childhood educators in Greece?**
A: Salaries for early childhood educators in Greece depend on the sector. In the public sector, pay follows the state salary scale, starting around €830 gross per month (the national minimum wage). Private sector salaries vary but often start at similar levels. Experienced educators may earn up to €1,200 gross monthly.

**Q: Can non-EU early childhood educators work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU early childhood educators can apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and the job offer meets the Greek salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Alternatively, a national work permit is available through a job offer and approval from the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an early childhood educator in Greece?**
A: A university degree in early childhood education from an accredited institution (AEI or TEI) is required. For public schools, educators must also pass exams by the Supreme Council for Civil Personnel Selection (ASEP). Registration with the relevant professional body is mandatory.
