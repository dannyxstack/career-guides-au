## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for motorcycle drivers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for motorcycle drivers in Greece vary by employer and location, but typically range from €700 to €1,200 per month gross. Many drivers work on commission or per-delivery basis, especially in food delivery. Overtime and tips can increase earnings. Minimum wage in Greece as of 2024 is €830 per month for full-time work.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as motorcycle drivers in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and is not applicable to motorcycle drivers, which is a low-skilled occupation. Non-EU nationals may obtain a Greek national work visa (type D) if they have a job offer and a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by local workers. Self-employment as a motorcycle driver is generally not a route to residency.

**Q: What licenses or qualifications are required to work as a motorcycle driver in Greece?**
A: To work as a motorcycle driver in Greece, you must hold a valid motorcycle driving license (category A, A1, or A2 depending on engine size). For courier or delivery work, many employers also require a clean driving record and familiarity with local areas. No specific vocational training is mandatory, but experience and knowledge of traffic rules are essential. Professional driving may require registration with the relevant municipal authorities.
