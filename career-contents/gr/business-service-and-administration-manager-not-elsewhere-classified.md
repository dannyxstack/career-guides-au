## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services and Administration Managers in Greece?**
A: Monthly gross salaries range from €1,800 to €4,500, depending on sector, experience, and company size. Senior roles in multinationals or large Greek firms can exceed €6,000, often including bonuses and benefits. Public sector pay is generally lower but offers stability.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Business Services and Administration Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a national visa (Type D) for employment or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Requirements include a valid job offer, relevant qualifications, and meeting salary thresholds set by Greek law. Employers must secure a work permit from the Ministry of Labour.

**Q: Which skills are most valued for Business Services and Administration Managers in Greece?**
A: Employers prioritize leadership, strategic planning, financial management, and digital transformation skills. Proficiency in Greek and English is often required. Experience with Greek bureaucracy, EU regulations, and local market dynamics gives candidates a competitive edge.
