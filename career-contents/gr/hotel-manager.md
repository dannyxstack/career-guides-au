## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Hotel Managers in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Hotel Managers in Greece ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on factors such as hotel size, location, and experience. Senior managers at luxury resorts may earn higher, while entry-level positions start lower. Bonuses and benefits can also affect total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Hotel Managers in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as Hotel Managers in Greece through the EU Blue Card, which requires a valid job offer, a recognized higher education degree (or equivalent), and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a national work permit may be obtained if the Blue Card criteria are not met.

**Q: What is the typical career progression for Hotel Managers in Greece?**
A: Hotel Managers in Greece often start as department heads (e.g., front office, housekeeping) and advance to assistant manager or hotel manager. With experience, they may move to larger hotels, luxury resorts, or regional management roles. Continuous training in hospitality management and languages supports career growth.
