## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for lawyers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, lawyers' salaries vary widely by experience, specialization, and location. Junior lawyers earn around €1,200–€1,800 per month gross, while mid-level lawyers earn €1,800–€2,500. Senior lawyers at top firms can earn €3,000–€5,000 or more. Partners have significantly higher incomes.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign lawyers to work in Greece?**
A: Foreign lawyers can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €2,500/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work visa requires a work permit obtained by the employer. Lawyers must have their qualifications recognized by the Greek Bar Association.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements to practice law in Greece?**
A: To practice law in Greece, one must be a member of a local Bar Association. This requires a law degree from a Greek university or recognized foreign equivalent, an 18-month traineeship at a law firm or court, and passing the Bar exams. Foreign lawyers may need degree recognition and possibly additional exams.
