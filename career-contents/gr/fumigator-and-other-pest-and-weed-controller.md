## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fumigators and pest control workers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, fumigators and pest controllers typically earn between €700 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience and employer. Senior specialists or those in managerial roles may earn higher. Salaries can vary by region and whether self-employed.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as a fumigator in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a national visa for employment if sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers but requires a university degree or higher qualification, which may not apply here. Typically, a work permit through employer sponsorship is the route, with a labor market test to prove no EU candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a fumigator in Greece?**
A: Fumigators in Greece must hold a certificate of professional competence (Pistopiitiko Epangelmatikis Eparkeias) from the Ministry of Rural Development, which requires passing exams on pest control methods, pesticide use, and safety. Continuous training is needed for license renewal. A driving license is often required.
