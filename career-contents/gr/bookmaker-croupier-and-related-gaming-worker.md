## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for croupiers and gaming workers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for croupiers and gaming workers in Greece vary by employer and experience. Entry-level positions often start at the national minimum wage (approximately €830 per month in 2024), plus tips. Experienced croupiers in major casinos may earn higher, reaching €1,200–€1,500 monthly. Overtime and shift differentials may apply. Tips can significantly increase total income.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as croupiers in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the national average salary. Croupiers and gaming workers typically do not meet these criteria. Non-EU nationals can instead obtain a national work permit if a Greek employer sponsors them. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA national. The permit is issued for up to two years and can be renewed.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for gaming workers in Greece?**
A: Starting as a croupier, workers can advance to senior croupier, pit boss, or floor manager with experience and additional training. Some move into casino management, surveillance, or gaming regulation. In Greece, the Hellenic Gaming Commission oversees licensing, and courses in hospitality or gaming management can aid progression. Fluency in Greek and English is beneficial. Some workers transition to online gaming platforms as they expand.
