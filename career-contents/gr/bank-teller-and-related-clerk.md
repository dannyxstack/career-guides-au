## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bank tellers in Greece?**
A: Bank teller salaries in Greece are set by collective agreements and vary by bank and experience. A typical gross monthly salary ranges from €1,000 to €1,400, plus bonuses and benefits like meal vouchers. Seniority and performance can increase pay, but actual figures depend on the employer and location.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as a bank teller in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as bank tellers via the EU Blue Card, requiring a bachelor's degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, a national work visa requires employer sponsorship and a labor market test showing no suitable EU candidate. Both lead to a residence permit typically valid for one to two years.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for bank tellers in Greece?**
A: Bank tellers in Greece can progress to senior teller, customer service officer, or branch supervisor with experience and training. Many banks offer internal courses for roles in lending, investments, or digital banking. Pursuing certifications like the Hellenic Bank Association diploma enhances promotion prospects, especially as banks emphasize advisory services over routine transactions.
