## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building frame and related trades worker in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average monthly salary for these trades ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers earn around the minimum wage (€830/month as of 2024), while skilled tradespeople with several years of experience can earn up to €1,500. Overtime and project-based bonuses may increase earnings.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU nationals to work as a building frame trades worker in Greece?**
A: Non-EU workers require a national work visa sponsored by a Greek employer. The employer must prove no suitable EU candidate is available (labor market test). The EU Blue Card, designed for highly skilled roles, typically requires a salary above €40,000/year, which these trades rarely meet. Instead, the standard work permit is appropriate.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for building frame trades in Greece?**
A: Formal vocational training (e.g., IEK) or apprenticeship is preferred. Certification from the Greek Manpower Employment Organization (OAED) or relevant trade unions is beneficial. While no mandatory license exists, employers often require proven experience or a recognized diploma. Knowledge of Greek language and safety regulations is essential.
