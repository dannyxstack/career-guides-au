## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mechanical engineers in Greece?**
A: The gross annual salary for mechanical engineers in Greece typically ranges from €25,000 to €50,000, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions start around €20,000, while senior roles in sectors like energy or shipping can exceed €60,000.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for mechanical engineers in Greece?**
A: Non-EU mechanical engineers can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a recognized university degree and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the national average (set by Greek law). Alternatively, a national work visa is possible for sponsored positions. Both routes need employer sponsorship and approval from Greek authorities.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for mechanical engineers in Greece?**
A: Mechanical engineers in Greece can progress from junior to senior engineer, project manager, or technical director. Specialization in high-demand sectors like maritime, renewable energy, or manufacturing is common. Registration with the Technical Chamber of Greece (TEE) is mandatory for practicing engineers and enhances career mobility.
