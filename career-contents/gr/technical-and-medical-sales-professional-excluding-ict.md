## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for technical and medical sales professionals in Greece?**
A: Entry-level positions typically earn €20,000-€25,000 annually. Experienced professionals earn €30,000-€45,000, while senior roles with large accounts can reach €60,000. Commission and bonuses vary, often adding 10-30%. Salaries are higher in multinational companies and in Athens/Thessaloniki.

**Q: What is the route for non-EU professionals to work in Greece as a technical or medical sales specialist?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary meeting the minimum threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, adjusted annually). Employer sponsorship is required. Alternatively, a national work visa may apply, but technical/medical sales are not listed as shortage occupations.
