## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an electrical line installer in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average gross monthly salary for electrical line installers and repairers typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior roles in major cities like Athens can exceed €2,000. Adjustments for overtime and hazardous conditions may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU electrical line installers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for a Greek national work visa or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (around €2,000/month in 2024) and a recognized university degree or relevant vocational qualification. Alternatively, a Greek work permit may be employer-sponsored without the degree requirement but has a separate process.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an electrical line installer in Greece?**
A: Typically, completion of a secondary technical school in electrical engineering or a related field is required. Many installers also hold a vocational training certificate from IEK (Vocational Training Institutes) or have completed an apprenticeship. Licensing from the Hellenic Ministry of Environment and Energy is mandatory for high-voltage work. Continuous professional development is recommended.
