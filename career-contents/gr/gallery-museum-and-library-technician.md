## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a gallery, museum or library technician in Greece?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around €700-900 per month, while experienced professionals in major institutions like the National Archaeological Museum can earn €1,200-1,800. Public sector roles follow collective agreements; private galleries may offer lower but variable pay. Salaries are often supplemented by project-based contracts or grants.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a gallery, museum or library technician in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €26,000 in 2024). Technician roles may not always meet the salary threshold unless senior or specialized. Alternatively, a national work permit (e.g., for dependent employment) is possible if an employer proves the position cannot be filled by an EU/EEA national.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a gallery, museum or library technician in Greece?**
A: Typically, a degree in archaeology, history, art history, library science, or a related field is required. Many positions also require postgraduate specialization in museology, conservation, or archival studies. Practical experience through internships (often offered by major museums like the Acropolis Museum) is highly valued. Knowledge of Greek is essential; English is a plus.
