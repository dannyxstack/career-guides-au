## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Greece?**
A: The typical monthly salary for farm labourers in Greece is around the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 gross. Wages can vary depending on the region, type of farm, and experience, with some employers offering additional benefits like accommodation or meals. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may apply.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU workers to work as mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Greece?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a Greek work permit under Law 4251/2014 for seasonal employment in agriculture, typically valid up to six months. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as this occupation does not meet the high-skill threshold. Employers must demonstrate labour market shortages. Residence permits for long-term seasonal work also exist.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Greece?**
A: Main duties include planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops, as well as feeding, watering, and monitoring livestock. Workers may operate machinery, maintain equipment, and perform general farm maintenance. Working conditions involve long hours outdoors in varying weather, often seasonal. Physical stamina is required, and many positions are located in rural areas.
