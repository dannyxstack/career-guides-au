## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a dental assistant in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for a dental assistant in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience, location, and type of practice. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced assistants in private clinics can earn up to €1,500. Salaries are often supplemented by bonuses or overtime pay.

**Q: Can a non-EU dental assistant work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU dental assistants can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized higher education qualification (or 5 years of professional experience) and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece. Alternatively, a national work visa for highly skilled workers may be available. Check with Greek authorities for current thresholds.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a dental assistant in Greece?**
A: To work as a dental assistant in Greece, you typically need a secondary education diploma and completion of a specialized vocational training program (e.g., from IEK or EPAL). Registration with the Ministry of Health may be required. Knowledge of Greek is essential, and certification in dental radiography or sterilization practices can be advantageous for employment.
