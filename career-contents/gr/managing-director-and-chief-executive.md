## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for managing directors and chief executives in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary significantly based on company size, sector, and location. In larger corporations, annual gross earnings can exceed €100,000, while SMEs offer lower compensation. Many executives receive additional benefits and performance bonuses. Exact figures depend on negotiations and economic conditions.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign managing directors moving to Greece?**
A: Foreign executives can apply for an EU Blue Card if their job meets the salary threshold for highly qualified workers. Alternatively, they may obtain a national work visa sponsored by their Greek employer. Both routes require a valid employment contract and proof of qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a managing director in Greece?**
A: Typically, extensive senior management experience (10+ years) and a master's degree in business or a related field are expected. Industry-specific expertise, leadership skills, and fluency in Greek and English are highly advantageous. Many top executives have a track record of driving organizational growth.
