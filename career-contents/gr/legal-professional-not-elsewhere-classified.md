## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization and experience. Entry-level positions typically start around €18,000-€24,000 annually, while senior professionals can earn €40,000-€60,000 or more. In-house legal roles in companies often pay higher than law firms. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: Can legal professionals obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: Yes, legal professionals may qualify for the EU Blue Card in Greece if they hold a recognized university degree (at least bachelor’s level) and have a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (adjusted periodically). The employer must apply, and the candidate must meet other general requirements.

**Q: What are the career prospects for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Opportunities exist in corporate law, compliance, legal consultancy, and the public sector. Specialization in areas like data protection, maritime law, or EU law can enhance prospects. Networking and fluency in Greek are essential for domestic roles. International firms offer additional mobility.
