## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a secondary education teacher in Greece?**
A: Secondary teachers in Greece are public servants. Starting net monthly salary is approximately €1,200, increasing with years of service and advanced degrees. Additional allowances for family status or remote postings may apply. Salaries are set by collective agreements and have faced cuts in recent years due to fiscal measures.

**Q: Can non-EU secondary teachers work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU teachers can work in Greece through the EU Blue Card if they have a job offer, recognized teaching qualifications, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage (or lower for regulated professions). Alternatively, a national work permit for shortage occupations is possible. Both routes require employer sponsorship and meet national labour market tests.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for secondary teachers in Greece?**
A: Teachers can progress through a fixed pay scale based on seniority and additional academic qualifications. Opportunities include becoming a school principal, deputy principal, or moving to administrative roles in local education offices or the Ministry of Education. Participation in professional development and training programs also supports career growth.
