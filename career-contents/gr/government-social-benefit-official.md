## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Government Social Benefits Official in Greece?**
A: The annual salary for a Government Social Benefits Official in Greece typically ranges from €15,000 to €25,000, depending on experience, rank, and seniority. Entry-level positions start around €15,000, while senior officials may earn up to €25,000. Additional benefits such as allowances and bonuses can apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Government Social Benefits Officials in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is intended for highly skilled non-EU workers in the private sector. Public sector roles like Government Social Benefits Officials generally require Greek citizenship or EU/EEA nationality. Thus, the EU Blue Card is not a viable route for this specific occupation; other work permits may apply for non-EU nationals only in exceptional cases.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Government Social Benefits Official in Greece?**
A: Candidates typically need a university degree in social sciences, public administration, or related fields. Entrance is via competitive public exams (ASEp). Additional requirements include Greek citizenship, clean criminal record, and proficiency in Greek. On-the-job training covers social security law, case management, and digital systems.
