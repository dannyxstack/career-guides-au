## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Services Managers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Services Managers in Greece vary by sector and experience. Entry-level positions typically start around €1,200 per month gross, while experienced managers can earn up to €3,500 per month. In high-demand sectors like tourism or IT, compensation may be higher, often including bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a Services Manager in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Services Managers via the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a gross salary of at least 1.5 times the average national wage (approx. €2,000/month). The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU candidate is available. Both routes grant residence and work rights.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Services Manager in Greece?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor's degree in business, hospitality, or a related field. Several years of relevant experience are essential. Fluency in Greek and English is standard, and additional certifications (e.g., project management) may be advantageous. Strong leadership and communication skills are highly valued.
