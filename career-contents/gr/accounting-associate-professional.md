## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an accounting associate professional in Greece?**
A: According to recent data, the average gross monthly salary for accounting associate professionals in Greece ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and company size. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior roles can exceed €2,000. Salaries in Athens tend to be higher than in other regions.

**Q: What are the visa requirements for non-EU nationals to work as an accounting associate professional in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Greece as an accounting associate professional. The EU Blue Card requires a university degree, a job offer for at least one year, and a gross annual salary meeting the national threshold (approximately 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, a national work visa requires a confirmed job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an accounting associate professional in Greece?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or a related field is required. Professional certifications such as ACCA or OEEK (Greek accounting body) can enhance prospects. Proficiency in Greek is often essential for client-facing roles, though international companies may accept English. Experience with Greek tax law and accounting software (e.g., Epsilon, Softone) is highly valued.
