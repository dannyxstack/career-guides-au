## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a customs and border inspector in Greece?**
A: Customs and border inspectors in Greece are civil servants. Starting net monthly salary ranges from €1,100 to €1,400, depending on rank and allowances. With seniority and promotions, salaries can reach €2,000–€2,500 net. Additional benefits include paid overtime and holiday bonuses.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign national to work as a customs and border inspector in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals may apply for an EU Blue Card if offered a position, but public sector jobs typically require Greek citizenship. EU nationals can work without a visa. Candidates must pass competitive civil service exams and language proficiency tests. Specific qualifications include a relevant degree and clean criminal record.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for customs and border inspectors in Greece?**
A: Inspectors can advance to supervisory roles such as team leader or section head. Specialization areas include anti-smuggling, customs fraud investigation, or risk analysis. With experience and exams, one can reach higher administrative grades. Continuous training is provided for promotion eligibility.
