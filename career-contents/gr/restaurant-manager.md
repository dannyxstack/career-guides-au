- Automated scheduling and personnel optimization (AI analyzes traffic flow, employee preferences)

- Basic customer complaint classification and automated responses (AI handling common issues)

- AI monitors kitchen and service efficiency in real time, assisting managers in making quick decisions.
- AI analyzes customer feedback and social media reviews to provide personalized service suggestions
- AI-driven dynamic pricing and menu optimization to increase profits
- AI training system simulates complex scenarios to accelerate management skill improvement

**Human moat:**

- Handling customer complaints, employee disputes, and other complex interpersonal interactions requiring empathy and negotiation skills

- Shape restaurant culture, motivate teams, foster employee loyalty

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a restaurant manager in Greece?**
A: Salaries for restaurant managers in Greece vary by location and establishment type. In Athens and tourist areas, monthly gross pay ranges from €1,800 to €3,200. Annual salaries typically fall between €22,000 and €38,000, with experienced managers in upscale venues earning on the higher end. Benefits may include tips, accommodation, or meals.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a restaurant manager in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can obtain an EU Blue Card for restaurant management if the job meets the salary threshold (applicable to highly skilled workers). Greece sets this threshold at a percentage of the national average gross salary, updated annually. If the position does not meet the Blue Card criteria, a national work permit may be an alternative route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a restaurant manager in Greece?**
A: While formal education like a degree in hospitality management is advantageous, extensive experience in the restaurant industry is often valued more. Many employers require at least 3-5 years of supervisory experience, fluency in Greek and English, knowledge of Greek food and beverage regulations, and strong leadership skills. Knowledge of local market trends is also beneficial.
