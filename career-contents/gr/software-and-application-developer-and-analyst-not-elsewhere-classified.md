## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level software developers earn around €20,000–€25,000 annually, while experienced developers can earn €40,000–€60,000 or more. Salaries vary by location (Athens typically higher) and company size. The tech sector has seen growth, but wages remain below EU averages.

**Q: What visa options are available for software developers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU software developers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times Greece's average gross annual salary (approx. €33,000 in 2024). Alternatively, the Greek National Visa for highly skilled workers requires a similar salary threshold. Both lead to residency and family reunification.

**Q: What are the career prospects for software developers in Greece?**
A: The Greek tech sector is expanding, with increasing demand for developers in fintech, tourism tech, and startups. Many companies offer remote work, and developers can advance to senior, lead, or manager roles. Lifelong learning is key due to rapid technological change. The 'Tech4Greece' initiative supports talent development.
