## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for judges in Greece?**
A: Judicial salaries in Greece vary by rank and years of service. As of 2024, beginning judges earn around €1,800–€2,200 gross monthly, while senior judges in higher courts can earn over €4,500 gross monthly. Additional allowances may apply for specialized roles or tenure.

**Q: Can foreign judges work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is generally for highly skilled non-EU workers in regulated professions. For judges, recognition of foreign legal qualifications is required by the Greek Ministry of Justice. Alternatively, non-EU nationals may apply for a national work visa if appointed by the Greek government, but judicial positions are typically restricted to EU nationals due to citizenship requirements.

**Q: What are the typical career progression steps for judges in Greece?**
A: Judges in Greece start at courts of first instance and advance through promotions based on seniority, evaluations, and exams. Progression includes moving to appellate courts, the Supreme Court (Areios Pagos), or specialized courts (e.g., administrative). The Supreme Judicial Council oversees promotions, which occur every few years.
