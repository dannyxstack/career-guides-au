## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an ICT Services Manager in Greece?**
A: Salaries for ICT Services Managers in Greece vary widely based on experience, company size, and sector. As of 2024, the average gross monthly salary ranges from €3,500 to €6,000, with senior roles in multinational companies or Athens-based firms earning higher. Benefits often include bonuses and health insurance.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for ICT Services Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU ICT Services Managers can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €2,000/month in 2024), plus recognized qualifications. Alternatively, a Greek national work visa (Type D) is available for specific roles, with employer sponsorship and valid residence permit.

**Q: What career opportunities are available for ICT Services Managers in Greece?**
A: ICT Services Managers in Greece find opportunities in IT consultancies, banking, telecoms (e.g., Cosmote), and the growing startup ecosystem in Athens and Thessaloniki. The shipping sector also demands ICT leadership. Career progression includes CTO or IT Director roles, especially in multinational companies or digital transformation projects.
