## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Finance Managers in Greece?**
A: Finance Managers in Greece typically earn between €40,000 and €80,000 annually, depending on experience, company size, and location. Salaries in Athens and multinational firms tend to be higher. Bonuses and benefits may also apply.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Finance Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU Finance Managers can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a university degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Alternatively, a Greek national work visa for highly skilled workers is available, with employer sponsorship and residence permit.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Finance Managers in Greece?**
A: Finance Managers in Greece can advance to roles such as Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Financial Director, or Regional Finance Head. Progression often involves gaining certifications like CFA or ACCA, and experience in multinational companies or the shipping/finance sectors. Networking within Athens' business community is key.
