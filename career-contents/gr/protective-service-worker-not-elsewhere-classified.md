## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for protective services workers in Greece?**
A: Monthly gross salaries for protective services workers in Greece typically range from €830 (minimum wage) to around €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and specific duties. Overtime and night shift allowances can increase earnings. Senior roles or specialized security positions may command higher pay.

**Q: What are the routes to work in Greece as a protective services worker for non-EU citizens?**
A: Non-EU citizens require a work visa sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as protective services roles rarely meet the required skill or salary thresholds. Alternatively, third-country nationals could apply for a national visa for seasonal or short-term work if eligible. EU/EEA citizens have free movement and can work without a visa.

**Q: What is the career progression for protective services workers in Greece?**
A: Career progression often starts as a security guard or patrol officer. With experience and certifications (e.g., in first aid or surveillance), workers can advance to supervisor, shift manager, or specialized roles like close protection. Additional training or a degree in security management may lead to managerial positions in private security firms or public safety agencies.
