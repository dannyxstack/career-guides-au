## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Greece?**
A: In Greece, building caretakers typically earn around the minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 per month gross. Experienced caretakers in larger buildings or with additional responsibilities may earn slightly more, up to €1,000–€1,200 per month. Salaries can vary by region and employer.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as building caretakers in Greece via the Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as building caretakers through an employer-sponsored work visa (Type D). The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation because it requires a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, which building caretakers rarely meet.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building caretaker in Greece?**
A: Building caretakers in Greece are responsible for cleaning common areas, minor repairs, waste management, and ensuring building security. They may also handle boiler maintenance, gardening, and liaison with service providers. The role often requires flexibility and basic technical skills.
