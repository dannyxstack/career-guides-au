## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an accountant in Greece?**
A: Salaries for accountants in Greece vary by experience and location. Entry-level accountants earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross monthly, while senior accountants with several years of experience can earn €2,000–€3,000. Large multinational firms and Athens-based roles tend to offer higher pay, plus potential bonuses.

**Q: Can foreign accountants obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: Yes, foreign accountants can apply for an EU Blue Card in Greece if they hold a recognized university degree in accounting or finance and have a job offer from a Greek employer. The job must meet the national minimum salary threshold (approximately 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an accountant in Greece?**
A: To practice as an accountant in Greece, a bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or economics is typically required. Membership in the Economic Chamber of Greece (OEE) is mandatory for signing financial statements. Additional certifications such as ACCA or CPA may enhance career prospects, especially in international firms. Fluency in Greek is often necessary for local roles.
