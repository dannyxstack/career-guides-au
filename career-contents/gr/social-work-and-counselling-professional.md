## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work and counselling professionals in Greece?**
A: Social work and counselling professionals in Greece typically earn between €12,000 and €20,000 annually for entry-level positions, with experienced professionals earning up to €30,000 or more in senior roles. Salaries vary by sector (public vs. private) and location, with higher pay often found in Athens and Thessaloniki.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign social work professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a recognized university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a national work visa (Type D) is possible for regulated professions after recognition of qualifications and licensing by Greek authorities.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a social worker or counsellor in Greece?**
A: To practice, you need a recognized bachelor’s or master’s degree in social work or counselling from an accredited institution. Registration with the relevant Greek professional body (e.g., the Social Workers' Association) is mandatory. For foreign qualifications, you must obtain recognition from the Hellenic National Academic Recognition and Information Center (DOATAP) and meet any additional licensing requirements.
