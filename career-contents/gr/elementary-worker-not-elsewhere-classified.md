## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers in Greece?**
A: Elementary workers in Greece generally earn at or near the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 per month gross for full-time employment. Actual pay varies by sector and region, with some industries offering slightly higher wages. Overtime and bonuses are uncommon.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa for elementary jobs in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically cannot obtain an EU Blue Card for elementary work, as this requires a higher skills level. However, they may apply for a national work visa if an employer sponsors them for a specific job, subject to labor market tests and quotas. Seasonal work permits are also available for agriculture and tourism.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for elementary workers in Greece?**
A: Career advancement is limited for elementary workers due to the low-skill nature of the jobs. However, gaining experience and on-the-job training can lead to supervisory roles or specialization in certain fields. Some workers may transition to skilled occupations by completing vocational training programs offered by Greek institutions.
