## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pharmacist in Greece?**
A: Entry-level pharmacists in Greece earn around €1,200-€1,500 gross per month. With experience, salaries can reach €1,800-€2,200 in community pharmacies. Hospital or industry roles may offer higher pay, often up to €2,500. Self-employed pharmacists in their own pharmacy have variable income based on turnover.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for pharmacists to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU pharmacists can apply for a national work visa (type D) if sponsored by a Greek employer. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals with a job offer meeting salary thresholds (currently around €24,000/year). Recognition of pharmacy qualifications by the Greek authorities is required before applying.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for pharmacists in Greece?**
A: Pharmacists in Greece primarily work in community pharmacies, which are highly regulated and often family-owned. Other options include hospital pharmacy, pharmaceutical industry (production, quality control, regulatory affairs), academia, and public health roles. Employment prospects are moderate, with many new graduates choosing to open their own pharmacy.
