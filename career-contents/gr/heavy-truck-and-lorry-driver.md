## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for heavy truck drivers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, heavy truck drivers typically earn between €1,200 and €1,800 gross per month, depending on experience, route type, and employer. Long-haul and international drivers may receive additional allowances, bringing total compensation higher. Overtime and night shifts often increase monthly earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU heavy truck drivers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Yes, non-EU drivers can apply for a national work visa (type D) or an EU Blue Card if hired by a Greek company. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience, along with a job offer meeting the national salary threshold (currently around €30,000 annually). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to drive heavy trucks in Greece?**
A: Drivers must hold a valid category C+E driving licence, a professional driver qualification (CPC) card, and a digital tachograph card. Additional requirements include a medical certificate from an approved doctor and a psychological aptitude test. For international routes, a Driver Attestation Certificate and knowledge of tachograph rules are necessary.
