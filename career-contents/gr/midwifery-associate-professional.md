## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Midwifery Associate Professional in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a midwifery associate professional in Greece ranges from €900 to €1,400, depending on experience, location, and employer. Public sector roles may offer higher pay and benefits. Private clinics and hospitals also employ associate professionals, with salaries often starting at the lower end.

**Q: Can a non-EU Midwifery Associate Professional obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. Midwifery associate professionals typically hold a two-year diploma, not a university degree, so they generally do not qualify for the Blue Card. Instead, they may apply for a standard national work visa, which has different requirements.

**Q: What are the requirements to practice as a Midwifery Associate Professional in Greece?**
A: To practice, you need a recognized diploma in midwifery from a Greek or EU institution, or a non-EU diploma recognized by the Hellenic National Academic Recognition Information Center (DOATAP). Registration with the relevant professional body (e.g., Midwives' Association) is mandatory. Non-EU nationals must also obtain a work visa and meet language requirements.
