## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fitness and recreation instructor in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the monthly gross salary for fitness and recreation instructors typically ranges from €800 to €1,500 depending on experience, employer, and location. Public sector positions may offer slightly higher pay and benefits, while private gyms and freelance instructors earn variable income. Salary growth is possible with advanced certifications or management roles.

**Q: What are the visa routes for non-EU fitness instructors to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Greece via an EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The Blue Card requires a job offer, a higher education degree (or equivalent professional experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. Alternatively, a national work permit may apply for roles below the threshold, subject to labor market tests.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a fitness instructor in Greece?**
A: To work as a fitness instructor in Greece, you typically need a recognized certification from a body like the Panhellenic Association of Gymnastics and Sports or an international equivalent. Many employers also require a first aid certificate. For recreation programme leaders, experience in organizing activities and group management is important. Continuing education is valued for career advancement.
