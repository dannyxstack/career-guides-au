## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for administrative and executive secretaries in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for administrative and executive secretaries in Greece ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions start around €800, while senior roles in Athens or multinational companies can reach €2,000. Benefits often include 13th and 14th month salaries.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for administrative and executive secretaries to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU administrative secretaries can obtain a work visa if they have a job offer and the employer obtains a work permit from the Greek authorities. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified roles requiring a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €1,800/month in 2024). Actual rates and quotas apply.

**Q: What qualifications are required for administrative and executive secretary roles in Greece?**
A: Most employers require at least a high school diploma, though a bachelor's degree in business administration or a related field is advantageous. Fluency in Greek and English is essential; additional languages (e.g., German) are a plus. Proficiency in MS Office and secretarial skills is expected. Certification from the Greek Organization for Vocational Education & Training (OEEK) can enhance prospects.
