## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for pet groomers and animal care workers in Greece typically range from €700 to €1,200 per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Many workers are paid hourly or per animal, with rates often around €10–€20 per groom. Full-time positions may include benefits like social security contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as pet groomers or animal care workers in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers, so it generally does not apply to pet groomers or animal care workers. However, non-EU citizens can obtain a Greek work visa if they have a job offer and the employer proves the position could not be filled by an EU worker. Other routes include the seasonal work permit or a residence permit for employment.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a pet groomer in Greece?**
A: Formal qualifications are not legally required, but many employers prefer candidates with vocational training or certification from recognized schools like the Hellenic Grooming Academy or similar programs. Practical experience, animal handling skills, and knowledge of breed-specific grooming are essential. Continuing education in pet first aid or hygiene standards can improve job prospects.
