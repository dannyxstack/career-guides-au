## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Greece?**
A: Domestic cleaners in Greece often earn at or slightly above the national minimum wage, which is approximately €780 monthly gross for full-time work. Hourly rates range from €5 to €7, depending on location and employer. Many cleaners work part-time or multiple jobs, so actual earnings vary widely.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for domestic cleaning jobs in Greece?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers and does not apply to domestic cleaners. Non-EU nationals can enter Greece for this occupation through a standard work permit, which requires employer sponsorship. However, demand for such permits is low due to the availability of local and EU workers.

**Q: Are there career advancement opportunities for domestic cleaners in Greece?**
A: Advancement is limited but possible. Experienced cleaners may progress to supervisory roles in hotels, hospitals, or large cleaning firms. Others start their own cleaning businesses. Some pursue additional training in specialized cleaning (e.g., post-construction) to increase income.
