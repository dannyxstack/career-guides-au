## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the salary for armed forces other ranks in Greece?**
A: Pay varies by rank and service years. A recruit earns around €600–800 monthly, while senior non-commissioned officers can earn €1,500–2,000. Benefits include free housing, meals, and healthcare. Additional allowances for special duties or deployments may apply.

**Q: Can foreign nationals join the Greek armed forces as other ranks through an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: The Greek armed forces generally require Greek citizenship for other ranks. EU nationals may serve in limited advisory roles but cannot join as enlisted personnel via the EU Blue Card, which is reserved for highly skilled civilian workers. No specific work visa route exists for foreign military enlistment.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for other ranks in the Greek military?**
A: Other ranks can advance through promotion exams and time in service, moving from private to non-commissioned officer ranks. Specialized training in fields like engineering or communications accelerates progression. Commissioned officer paths are possible after completing officer candidate school for those meeting education requirements.
