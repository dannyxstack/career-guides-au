## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Research and Development Managers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, R&D Managers typically earn between €35,000 and €70,000 annually, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior roles in large multinationals or high-tech sectors may exceed €80,000. Salaries are often supplemented with bonuses and benefits. Figures are gross and before taxes.

**Q: What are the official work-visa or EU Blue Card routes for Research and Development Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as R&D Managers in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €26,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work visa (type D) applies for longer stays, subject to employer sponsorship and labor market test. Both require recognized qualifications and valid passport.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a Research and Development Manager in Greece?**
A: Typically, a Master's or PhD in a relevant scientific or engineering field is required. Strong technical expertise, project management skills, and fluency in English are essential. Greek language proficiency is often preferred. Experience in leading cross-functional teams and knowledge of EU funding programs (e.g., Horizon Europe) are advantageous.
