## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an optometrist in Greece?**
A: The average salary for an optometrist in Greece ranges from €1,200 to €1,800 per month before taxes, depending on experience, location, and whether working in the private or public sector. Starting salaries are lower, while senior optometrists or those owning their practice can earn more.

**Q: What work-visa routes exist for non-EU optometrists to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer, recognized qualifications, and salary above the threshold. Alternatively, employers can apply for a standard work permit under national law. Registration with the Panhellenic Association of Opticians and Optometrists is mandatory.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as an optometrist in Greece?**
A: To practice as an optometrist or ophthalmic optician in Greece, you need a degree in optometry from a recognized institution and registration with the Panhellenic Association of Opticians and Optometrists (PAOO). For non-EU degrees, equivalence from the National Organization for the Certification of Qualifications (EOPPEP) may be required.
