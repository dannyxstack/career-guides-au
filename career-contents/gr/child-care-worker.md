## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for child care workers in Greece?**
A: The gross monthly salary for child care workers in Greece typically ranges from €700 to €1,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Salaries are often based on the national collective agreement for private sector employees. This role is generally considered low-wage, and pay may be higher in private nurseries or for those with additional qualifications.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU child care workers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals must secure a work visa or permit sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€25,700 in 2023), which child care roles rarely meet. Instead, a standard national work permit is typical, requiring employer proof of labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a child care worker in Greece?**
A: To work as a child care worker in Greece, a minimum of a high school diploma is required, though many employers prefer vocational training (e.g., from IEK) or a university degree in early childhood education. Certification in first aid is often expected. Registration with the relevant municipal authority may be needed for public-sector roles.
