## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fashion models in Greece?**
A: Pay varies widely based on experience, project type (e.g., runway, print), and client. Beginner models may earn €150–€400 per day, while established names can command €1,000+ per assignment. Many models work freelance, with irregular income. Agencies typically take a 20–30% commission.

**Q: What work-visa or EU Blue Card routes exist for non-EU models to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU models typically need a Greek work visa (type D) sponsored by a local employer. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average; modeling contracts rarely meet this threshold, so the standard work visa is the common route.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a fashion model in Greece?**
A: Key requirements include appropriate height (usually 173–183 cm for women, 180–190 cm for men), good proportions, clear skin, and strong portfolio. Most models sign with a licensed agency in Athens or Thessaloniki. Fluency in English is important, while Greek is beneficial for local bookings.
