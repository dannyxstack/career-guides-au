## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a midwifery professional in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for midwives in Greece ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and location. Senior midwives in hospitals can earn up to €2,200. However, salaries are lower in rural areas and may include additional allowances for on-call duties.

**Q: What are the official routes for a midwife to work in Greece as a non-EU professional?**
A: Non-EU midwives can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national work visa. Requirements include a recognized degree in midwifery, at least three years' professional experience (or a higher qualification), and a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. €31,000/year). Licensure from the Greek Nursing and Midwifery Council (ESNE) is also mandatory.

**Q: Is there a demand for midwives in Greece currently?**
A: Yes, Greece faces a shortage of midwives, especially in public hospitals and rural areas. The aging workforce and increased birth rates in some regions drive demand. However, competition exists in urban centers like Athens. Proficiency in Greek is essential for most roles. Opportunities also exist in private clinics and community health programs.
