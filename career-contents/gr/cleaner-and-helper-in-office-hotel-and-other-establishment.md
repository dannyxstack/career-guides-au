## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners and helpers in offices, hotels, and other establishments in Greece?**
A: As of 2024, the statutory minimum wage in Greece is €830 per month gross, which is commonly applied for cleaners and helpers. Many are paid hourly at rates around €4.50-€5.50. Salaries may be slightly higher in hotels during peak seasons or in larger cities like Athens, but overall pay is near minimum wage.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a cleaner in Greece through the EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, so it does not apply to cleaners. Non-EU cleaners may obtain a national work permit if an employer sponsors them and passes a labor market test proving no EU candidate is available. Seasonal work permits exist for tourism but are temporary.

**Q: What are common career advancement opportunities for cleaners and helpers in Greece?**
A: With experience, cleaners may become supervisors or team leaders in larger hotels or cleaning companies. Additional training in specialized cleaning (e.g., industrial or eco-friendly) can lead to higher pay. Some transition to related roles like housekeeping management in hotels or self-employment by starting a cleaning business. However, advancement often requires language skills and local contacts.
