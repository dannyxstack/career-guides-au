## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shop sales assistant in Greece?**
A: Shop sales assistants in Greece typically earn between €700 and €900 per month, depending on experience and location. The national minimum wage for full-time workers is €780 per month as of 2024. Many positions also include benefits such as meal vouchers or bonus payments.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU shop sales assistants in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as shop sales assistants in Greece through employer-sponsored work permits, which require demonstrating that no EU candidate is available. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers, typically requiring a university degree and higher salary thresholds, which this occupation rarely meets. Seasonal work permits may also apply in tourism areas.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shop sales assistants in Greece?**
A: Shop sales assistants can advance to senior sales roles, department manager, or store manager. Many employers offer internal training programs. Experience can lead to roles in retail buying, merchandising, or visual merchandising. Bilingual skills improve prospects in tourist-heavy areas like Athens, Thessaloniki, or island resorts.
