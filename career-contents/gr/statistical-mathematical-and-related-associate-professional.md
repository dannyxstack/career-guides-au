## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for statistical and mathematical associate professionals in Greece?**
A: The annual gross salary for these professionals in Greece typically ranges from €18,000 to €30,000, depending on experience, sector, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €15,000, while senior roles or those in high-demand industries like finance or tech can exceed €35,000. Public sector positions offer lower pay but greater stability.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU statistical associate professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized university degree and a job offer with a salary meeting the national minimum threshold (updated annually). Alternatively, they can obtain a national work permit through a Greek employer, which requires a labor market test proving no suitable EU candidate is available. Both routes require a valid residency permit.

**Q: Which industries in Greece employ statistical, mathematical, and related associate professionals?**
A: Key employers include the Hellenic Statistical Authority (ELSTAT), banks, insurance companies, market research firms, and consulting companies. Increasingly, tech startups, logistics firms, and energy companies hire these professionals for data analysis, risk assessment, and forecasting. The tourism sector also uses statistical associates for market analysis and demand forecasting.
