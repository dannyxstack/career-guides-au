## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Greece?**
A: Salaries for commissioned officers in the Greek Armed Forces vary by rank and years of service. A new Second Lieutenant earns approximately €1,200–€1,500 per month gross, while senior officers can earn €2,500–€3,500. Additional allowances for housing, family, and special duties apply, and salaries are subject to Greek income tax and social security contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Commissioned Armed Forces Officers in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card in Greece is for highly skilled non-EU workers in civilian professions. Commissioned Armed Forces Officer positions are reserved for Greek citizens or, under specific bilateral agreements, citizens of other EU/EEA states. Non-EU nationals generally cannot join the Greek military, and no work visa path exists for this occupation.

**Q: What are the entry requirements for becoming a Commissioned Armed Forces Officer in Greece?**
A: To become a commissioned officer in the Greek military, one must be a Greek citizen aged 17–21, hold a high school diploma, and pass physical and psychological exams. Candidates attend the Evelpidon Military Academy or equivalent naval/air force academies. Graduates earn a bachelor's degree and commit to a minimum service period, typically 8–12 years.
