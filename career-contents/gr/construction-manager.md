## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a construction manager in Greece?**
A: The average annual salary for construction managers in Greece ranges from €25,000 to €45,000, depending on experience, project size, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €20,000, while senior managers can earn up to €60,000. Bonuses and benefits are sometimes included.

**Q: Can non-EU construction managers work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Non-EU construction managers can work in Greece through the EU Blue Card, which requires a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a national work visa applies for specific contracts. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications and career progression are typical for construction managers in Greece?**
A: Most construction managers in Greece hold a civil engineering or architecture degree and are registered with the Technical Chamber of Greece (TEE). Career progression often starts as site engineer or supervisor, advancing to project manager, then senior construction manager. Professional experience and ongoing training are key for advancement.
