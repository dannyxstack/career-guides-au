## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for clerical support workers not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for this occupation in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while senior roles can reach €1,500. Salaries are typically higher in Athens and for roles in larger companies or multinationals.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a clerical support worker in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Greece for this occupation if they hold a university degree or at least 5 years of relevant professional experience and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €38,854 gross annually for 2024). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for clerical support workers in Greece?**
A: Clerical support workers can advance to senior administrative roles, office manager, or executive assistant positions. With additional training, they may specialize in fields like accounting or human resources. Some pursue further education to become administrative coordinators or move into public sector positions through competitive exams.
