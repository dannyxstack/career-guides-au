## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Government Tax and Excise Official in Greece?**
A: Entry-level tax and excise officials in Greece earn a gross monthly salary of approximately €1,200 to €1,500. With seniority and promotions, salaries can reach €2,500 or more. Additional allowances for specific duties or locations may apply. Total compensation includes social security contributions and benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Government Tax and Excise Officials in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No. Public sector positions, including tax and excise officials, are generally reserved for Greek or EU/EEA nationals. The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled private-sector employment and does not apply to these roles. Non-EU citizens may explore alternative work permits for private-sector positions only.

**Q: What career progression steps are typical for Government Tax and Excise Officials in Greece?**
A: Officials typically start as entry-level inspectors under the Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE). Advancement to senior inspector, team leader, or management roles occurs through exams, performance evaluations, and years of service. Specializations in customs, tax audits, or excise duties are common career paths.
