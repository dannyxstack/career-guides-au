## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for accounting and bookkeeping clerks in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level accounting clerks earn around €800-€1,000 gross per month, while experienced clerks can earn €1,200-€1,600. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Athens and Thessaloniki. Public sector positions often offer additional benefits.

**Q: What work visa options are available for foreign accounting clerks in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work visa sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified professionals requiring a university degree and a salary threshold (currently around €1,500/month); accounting clerks may not always meet this. Standard work permits are more common for this role.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an accounting clerk in Greece?**
A: A degree in accounting, finance, or business is preferred, but vocational training or certification (e.g., from IEK or OAED) can suffice. Proficiency in Greek tax law, familiarity with software like Epsilon Net or Softone, and knowledge of Greek accounting standards (ELP) are essential.
