## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for companions and valets in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average monthly salary for companions and valets ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience and employer. Private household workers may earn at the minimum wage (€830 per month in 2024) plus benefits. Salaries can be higher in luxury hotels or for high-net-worth individuals.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as a companion or valet in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as companions or valets in Greece through employer-sponsored work permits under national law. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers, but this occupation typically does not meet the high-skill criteria. The most common route is a temporary residence permit for employment, requiring a job offer and labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a companion or valet in Greece?**
A: Typically, no formal education is required, but experience in hospitality or personal service is valued. Language skills (Greek and English) are important. Training in etiquette, first aid, or security may be advantageous. Employers often prioritize reliability and discretion.
