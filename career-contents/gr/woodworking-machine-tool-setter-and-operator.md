## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for woodworking machine tool setters and operators in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Overtime and shift work can increase earnings. Salaries in Athens and industrial zones tend to be higher than in rural areas.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as woodworking machine tool setters in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa sponsored by a Greek employer. For the EU Blue Card, you need a university degree or five years of relevant professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the salary threshold (set at 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What training is required to become a woodworking machine tool setter in Greece?**
A: Vocational training through OAED (Manpower Employment Organization) or private technical schools is common. Apprenticeships offer hands-on experience. While no formal license is mandatory, certifications in CNC operation and safety (e.g., from ELOT) boost employability.
