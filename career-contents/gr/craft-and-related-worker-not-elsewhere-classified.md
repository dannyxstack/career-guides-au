## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely by skill and sector. Entry-level workers may earn around €800–€1,000 monthly, while experienced specialists can earn €1,200–€1,500. Many are employed in small workshops or self-employed. The national minimum wage (2024) is €830 per month, which often applies to unskilled positions.

**Q: Can craft and related workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: Yes, provided the occupation qualifies as a high-skilled role. The EU Blue Card requires a valid employment contract with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approximately €21,600 in 2024). Applicants must hold relevant qualifications or experience and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the typical career prospects for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Career progression often includes specialization (e.g., in restoration, fine carpentry, or metalwork) or self-employment. Many workers train through vocational schools or apprenticeships. Demand is steady in tourism-related crafts, construction specialties, and artisanal goods. With experience, some become supervisors or open their own workshops.
