## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a cleaning and housekeeping supervisor in Greece?**
A: In Greece, cleaning and housekeeping supervisors typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month, depending on experience, establishment size, and location. Salaries are often based on sectoral collective agreements, with additional benefits such as holiday pay and bonuses. In major cities like Athens, wages may be slightly higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU nationals to work as cleaning and housekeeping supervisors in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a Greek national work visa if they secure a job offer from a Greek employer. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled locally. For highly qualified roles, the EU Blue Card may apply, but cleaning supervisors typically do not meet the salary threshold (€1,410 gross monthly). Other routes include intra-corporate transfer or seasonal work permits, but these are less common.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for cleaning and housekeeping supervisors in Greece?**
A: Experienced supervisors can advance to managerial positions such as facilities manager, operations manager in hospitality, or executive housekeeper in large hotels. Additional training in hospitality management or business administration can open doors. Some pursue entrepreneurship by starting their own cleaning companies. The Greek tourism sector offers growth, especially in high-end resorts and boutique hotels.
