## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bicycle repairer in Greece?**
A: Salaries for bicycle repairers in Greece vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions often earn around the national minimum wage (approximately €830 per month gross in 2024). Experienced repairers or those in tourist areas may earn €1,000–€1,500 monthly. Overtime and tips from high-season work can supplement income.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for bicycle repairers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU bicycle repairers can apply for a Greek national work visa (Type D) if they have a job offer from a Greek employer. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. An alternative is the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher-skilled job with a salary at least 1.5 times the Greek average gross annual salary (around €25,000 in 2024). Both routes require a valid work contract and relevant qualifications.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for bicycle repairers in Greece?**
A: Bicycle repairers can advance to shop manager, open their own repair workshop, or specialize in electric bikes and high-end racing models. With additional training, they may work in bicycle assembly for manufacturers or as mobile repair technicians. The growing cycling tourism sector in Greece also offers seasonal roles in rental shops and tour companies.
