## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fruit and vegetable preservers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, fruit and vegetable preservers usually earn between €700 and €1,200 per month, depending on experience and employer. Many are covered by collective labor agreements in the food processing sector. Overtime and shift allowances may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU fruit and vegetable preservers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a national work permit if hired by a Greek employer, or the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold (approx. €21,000/year as of 2025). Both require a job offer and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main duties of fruit and vegetable preservers in Greece?**
A: Fruit and vegetable preservers in Greece prepare and process produce for canning, freezing, drying, or pickling. Tasks include sorting, washing, cutting, cooking, and packaging. They also monitor quality, follow hygiene standards, and operate processing machinery in facilities like agri-food cooperatives or industrial plants.
