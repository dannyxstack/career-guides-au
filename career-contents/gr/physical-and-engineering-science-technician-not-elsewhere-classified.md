## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Greece?**
A: Entry-level technicians earn around €12,000–€15,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €20,000–€30,000. Salaries vary by sector (e.g., energy, manufacturing) and location (Athens higher than rural areas). Overtime and shift allowances may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU Physical and Engineering Science Technicians work in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU technicians can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer for highly qualified work, a recognized degree or relevant experience, and meet salary thresholds (official minimum set by Greek law, updated annually). The employer must apply to the Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What are the main industries hiring Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Greece?**
A: Key industries include renewable energy (solar, wind), construction, telecommunications, and manufacturing. Technicians are also employed in research labs, quality control, and technical support. The tourism sector occasionally needs technicians for facilities maintenance.
