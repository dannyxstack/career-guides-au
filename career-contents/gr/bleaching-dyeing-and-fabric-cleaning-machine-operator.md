## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Greece?**
A: In Greece, monthly gross salaries for this role range from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and location. Entry-level operators earn around €800, while those with several years in industrial zones like Attica or Thessaloniki may reach €1,200. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total compensation.

**Q: How can non-EU workers obtain a work visa for this occupation in Greece?**
A: Non-EU workers need a job offer from a Greek employer who applies for a work permit (e.g., special permit for seasonal or permanent work). The EU Blue Card is not typical for this role as it requires a higher education degree. The employer must demonstrate that no EU/EEA candidate is available.

**Q: What are common workplaces for these operators in Greece?**
A: Operators are primarily employed in textile factories, dyeing plants, and industrial laundry services. Major industrial zones near Athens (e.g., Elefsina) and Thessaloniki (e.g., Sindos) host many such facilities. Work involves operating machinery and handling chemicals, with safety gear required.
