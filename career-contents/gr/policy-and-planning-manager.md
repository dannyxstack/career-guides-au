- Automatically collect and organise policy-related data (e.g., economic indicators, regulatory changes)
- Generate initial policy impact analysis report and summary
- Quantified results of simulated policy options (e.g., budget impact model)
- Monitoring news and social media trends and generating public opinion briefs

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI tools to quickly compare policy frameworks across countries and identify best practices
- Analyze public feedback using natural language processing tools to optimize policy communication strategies
- Use predictive models to assess long-term socio-economic benefits of policies
- Leverage collaborative AI platforms to efficiently manage cross-departmental policy-making processes
- Use AI to generate multiple versions of policy drafts for leadership review

**Human moat:**

- Making balanced and value-based judgments amidst conflicting interests of multiple parties
- Build trust and conduct political negotiations with senior officials, the public, and others.
- Deep understanding of local culture, history, and power structures
- Assume ultimate responsibility in situations of uncertainty and ethical grey areas
- Proactive policy framework for innovative design rather than optimizing existing solutions

**Skills to build:**

- Policy modeling and simulation software (e.g., System Dynamics)
- Stakeholder management and conflict mediation skills

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Policy and Planning Manager in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Policy and Planning Managers in Greece vary by sector and experience. In the public sector, gross monthly earnings range from €1,800 to €3,500, while in private companies, they can reach €4,000–€6,000 for senior roles. Bonuses and allowances may apply in large organizations or multinational firms.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Policy and Planning Manager in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals can apply for the EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a valid job offer as a Policy and Planning Manager with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the Greek average (approx. €34,000 in 2024). The employer must prove the position couldn't be filled by an EU national. Work permits are issued by the Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are required for Policy and Planning Managers in Greece?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in public policy, business administration, or a related field is required, along with at least 5–7 years of experience in strategic planning or policy development. Fluency in Greek is often mandatory for public sector roles, while English is essential in international organizations. Professional certification in project management is an advantage.
