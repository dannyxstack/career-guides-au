## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for civil engineers in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level civil engineers earn around €14,000-€18,000 annually, while experienced engineers (5-10 years) earn €22,000-€35,000. Senior positions or project managers can reach €40,000-€55,000. Salaries vary by region, with higher pay in Athens and Thessaloniki, and by sector, such as construction or infrastructure projects.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU civil engineers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU civil engineers can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a recognized degree, a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, and a valid work contract. Alternatively, a national work permit for shortage occupations may apply. Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: Do civil engineers in Greece need a professional license to practice?**
A: Yes, civil engineers in Greece must be registered with the Technical Chamber of Greece (TEE) to practice professionally. Registration requires a recognized engineering degree, passing professional exams, and meeting continuing education requirements. Licensed engineers can sign off on projects and access certain roles in public and private sectors.
