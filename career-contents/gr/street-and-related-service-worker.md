- Parking meter collection (replaced by mobile payments and automatic billing systems)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for street and related services workers in Greece?**
A: Pay for street vendors and related workers in Greece varies, but the average gross monthly salary is around €700–€900. Many are self-employed, so earnings depend on sales, location, and season. Minimum wage in Greece is currently €830 per month, but street workers often earn less or more based on demand.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU street vendors in Greece?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and does not apply to street vendors. Non-EU citizens need a work permit sponsored by an employer or a residence permit for self-employment. This requires registering with Greek authorities, proving business viability, and meeting income thresholds. No specific visa route exists for low-skilled street work.

**Q: What are the main requirements to become a street vendor in Greece?**
A: No formal education is required, but you must obtain a municipal license for street vending. Self-employed workers must register with the tax office (ΑΦΜ) and social security (ΕΦΚΑ). Knowledge of Greek is helpful for customer interaction. The job is often seasonal, and competition varies by location and product type.
