## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for specialist medical practitioners in Greece?**
A: Salaries for specialist doctors in Greece vary by experience and sector. In the public healthcare system, gross monthly earnings range from €2,500 to €4,500, while private practice can yield higher incomes, often exceeding €5,000 per month. Senior consultants and those in high-demand specialties may earn up to €8,000 monthly.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU specialist doctors to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU medical specialists can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card, requiring a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, a recognized medical degree, and proof of language proficiency. Alternatively, they may apply for a national work visa under the Greek immigration law for highly qualified professionals, subject to labor market tests.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for specialist medical practitioners in Greece?**
A: Specialist doctors can progress to senior consultant roles, head of department, or clinical director in hospitals. Many pursue academic careers as university professors or researchers. Others open private practices or specialize further through subspecialty fellowships. The Greek healthcare system also offers opportunities in public health administration.
