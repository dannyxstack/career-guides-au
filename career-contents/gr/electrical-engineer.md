## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for electrical engineers in Greece?**
A: The average annual salary for electrical engineers in Greece ranges from €24,000 to €35,000, varying with experience, sector, and location. Higher pay is common in Athens and Thessaloniki, as well as in the renewable energy and telecommunications sectors. Entry-level positions typically start lower, while senior roles in large firms or public projects can exceed €40,000.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU electrical engineers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU electrical engineers can work in Greece via two main official routes: the EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, a recognized degree, and a contract of at least one year) or a national work visa (employer-sponsored, subject to labor market testing). Both require a valid employment contract and proof of qualifications.

**Q: Which industries employ the most electrical engineers in Greece?**
A: Electrical engineers in Greece are primarily employed in the energy sector (especially renewables like solar and wind), telecommunications, construction, manufacturing, and public infrastructure projects. The growing renewable energy sector offers increasing opportunities, along with consulting firms and freelance work in automation and electrical design.
