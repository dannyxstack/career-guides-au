## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a laundry machine operator in Greece?**
A: Salaries for laundry machine operators in Greece vary by region and employer. Entry-level positions typically earn around €700-€900 per month, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,200. Wages are often based on the national collective labor agreement for the cleaning and related industries. Overtime and night shifts may offer additional compensation.

**Q: Can a foreigner work as a laundry machine operator in Greece through the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers and is not applicable for laundry machine operators, as the occupation does not meet the skill requirement. Non-EU nationals can apply for a national work visa (e.g., for dependent employment) if they have a job offer from a Greek employer. The employer must prove that no local candidate was available. Residence and work permits are issued by the Greek Migration Policy Ministry.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for laundry machine operators in Greece?**
A: Career progression may include roles such as supervisor or manager in large laundries or dry-cleaning businesses. With additional training, operators can specialize in industrial laundry processes or textile maintenance. Some advance to self-employment by opening their own laundromat. Formal vocational training in textile care or business management can improve prospects.
