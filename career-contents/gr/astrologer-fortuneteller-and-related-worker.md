## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Greece?**
A: Income varies widely as many work independently. Average monthly earnings range from €800 to €1,500, depending on clientele and reputation. Some may earn less, especially starting out. It is often cash-based and not formally reported.

**Q: What are the work visa options for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Greece?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a university degree and high-skilled employment, so it does not apply. Non-EU nationals may seek a self-employment visa or a general work permit if sponsored. Alternatively, a digital nomad visa may suit those working remotely for foreign clients.

**Q: Are there any professional associations or licensing requirements for astrologers in Greece?**
A: Astrology and fortune-telling are not regulated in Greece. No official license is required. However, some practitioners join informal networks or online platforms. Formal training is available at private schools but not state-recognized.
