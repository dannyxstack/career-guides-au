## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for stonemasons in Greece ranges from €1,000 to €1,800 gross, depending on experience and region. Wages are often higher in tourist areas like Crete or the Cyclades due to restoration work. Self-employed stonemasons may charge daily rates of €80–€150. Official figures are available from the Greek Ministry of Labour and ELSTAT.

**Q: Can a non-EU stonemason work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Non-EU stonemasons may obtain a Greek work visa if they have a job offer and the employer proves a labour market test. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree or equivalent; it typically requires a salary threshold (e.g., 1.5 times average gross salary). Stonemasons without higher education may apply for a national work permit under Law 4251/2014.

**Q: What training is required to become a stonemason in Greece?**
A: Formal training is not mandatory but vocational schools (IEK) offer 2-year programs in stone carving and masonry. Apprenticeships under experienced stonemasons are common. Certificates from the Organisation for Vocational Education and Training (OEEK) can improve job prospects. The profession is in moderate demand, especially for heritage restoration projects.
