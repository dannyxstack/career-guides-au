## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Buyer in Greece?**
A: The average annual salary for a Buyer in Greece ranges from €18,000 to €30,000, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions start around €15,000, while senior buyers in larger firms can earn up to €40,000 or more. Benefits may include bonuses and commission.

**Q: What are the official work visa and EU Blue Card routes for working as a Buyer in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as a Buyer in Greece via a standard work visa, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a contract meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Both require application through Greek consulates and residence permit procedures.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Buyers in Greece?**
A: Buyers in Greece can advance to senior buyer, purchasing manager, or supply chain director roles. Gaining expertise in specific sectors like retail, manufacturing, or logistics enhances prospects. Professional certifications (e.g., from the Hellenic Supply Chain Institute) and language skills (English, German) improve mobility and earning potential.
