## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a software developer in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and location. A junior developer may earn €18,000–€25,000 annually, while mid-level roles range €30,000–€45,000. Senior developers can earn €50,000–€70,000 or more. Major companies in Athens or tech hubs offer higher pay, and benefits like bonuses or stock options are common.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU software developer to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU developers can work in Greece via an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national work visa. The Blue Card requires a job offer meeting salary thresholds (approx. 1.5x the average gross salary), a recognized degree, and a contract of at least one year. Alternatively, a national visa for dependent employment is possible if the employer proves no suitable EU candidate is available.

**Q: What are the main career growth opportunities for software developers in Greece?**
A: Software developers in Greece can advance to senior, lead, or architect roles, or specialize in fields like AI, cloud, or cybersecurity. Many companies support continuous learning through certifications and conferences. Remote work with international teams is also common, broadening exposure. Startups and established tech firms offer paths to CTO or technical management positions.
