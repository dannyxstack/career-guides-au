## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for athletes and sports players in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on sport, level, and club. In popular team sports like football, top-tier players can earn €100,000–€500,000 annually, while lower-league players may earn €15,000–€30,000. Individual sports and amateur levels generally offer lower or no guaranteed income, often relying on sponsorships or bonuses.

**Q: What visa routes allow foreign athletes to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU athletes can obtain a Greek work visa (Type D) sponsored by a Greek sports club or organization. This requires a contract, approval from the relevant sports federation, and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not typically used for athletes; instead, specific national procedures for highly qualified athletes apply.

**Q: What are common career paths for Greek athletes after retiring from competition?**
A: Many Greek athletes transition into coaching, sports management, or physical education. Some pursue roles in sports marketing, event organization, or become sports commentators. Government programs and the Hellenic Olympic Committee offer support for career development, including entrepreneurship and further education.
