## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for undertakers and embalmers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for undertakers and embalmers in Greece vary by experience and location, with entry-level positions earning around €800–€1,200 per month. Experienced professionals in private funeral homes may earn €1,500–€2,500 monthly, while those in managerial roles or larger cities like Athens can expect higher pay. Overtime and on-call duties may provide additional income.

**Q: Can non-EU undertakers and embalmers work in Greece via an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as undertakers and embalmers in Greece through a national work permit sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds; however, this occupation may not always qualify. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible if the employer proves that no EU/EEA candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an undertaker or embalmer in Greece?**
A: In Greece, undertakers and embalmers typically need a secondary education diploma and may complete vocational training in funeral services. While formal licensing is not mandatory, many employers prefer candidates with a certificate from a recognized school or apprenticeship. Practical experience is crucial, and knowledge of Greek funeral customs and laws is essential for career advancement.
