## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a traditional and complementary medicine associate professional in Greece?**
A: Salaries for traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Greece typically range between €800 and €1,500 per month, depending on experience, specialization, and whether they work privately or in a clinic. Earnings can increase with reputation and client base.

**Q: Can non-EU professionals work as traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card to work in Greece if they have a valid job offer and meet the national salary threshold (updated annually, currently around €25,000). Qualifications must be recognized by the relevant Greek authority. Alternatively, a national work permit is available for lower-paid positions.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a traditional and complementary medicine associate professional in Greece?**
A: Practitioners typically need a recognized diploma or degree in their field (e.g., acupuncture, herbal medicine) and must register with professional associations. For example, osteopaths require a four-year program from an accredited institution. Regulation varies by modality, so checking with the competent Greek authority is advisable.
