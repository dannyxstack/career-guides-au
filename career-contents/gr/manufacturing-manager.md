## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Manufacturing Managers in Greece?**
A: The average annual salary for Manufacturing Managers in Greece ranges from €35,000 to €55,000, depending on industry, company size, and experience. Senior roles in larger firms or specialized sectors like pharmaceuticals or automotive may exceed €70,000. Bonuses and benefits are common, particularly in multinational companies.

**Q: Can Manufacturing Managers from non-EU countries work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU Manufacturing Managers can apply for the EU Blue Card in Greece. Requirements include a valid employment contract or binding job offer for at least one year, meeting the salary threshold (currently around €25,000–€30,000, varying by profession), and having recognized higher professional qualifications. The employer must apply to the relevant Greek authorities.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Manufacturing Managers in Greece?**
A: Manufacturing Managers in Greece can progress to roles such as Plant Manager, Operations Director, or Vice President of Manufacturing. Experience in lean management, automation, and supply chain optimization is valued. Opportunities exist in industries like food processing, textiles, pharmaceuticals, and automotive manufacturing, with potential to move into consultancy or international roles.
