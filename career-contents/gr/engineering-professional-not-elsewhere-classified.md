**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU engineering professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for a national visa for employment or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting salary thresholds). The Blue Card requires a recognized degree and a contract of at least one year. In-country work permits are employer-sponsored. Seasonal or short-term work may have separate provisions.
