## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary based on employer and region, but entry-level positions typically range from €700 to €1,000 gross per month. Experienced workers may earn up to €1,200 monthly. Overtime and bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as meter readers or vending-machine collectors in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Greece if they secure a job offer and the employer obtains a work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers, but these occupations typically do not meet the salary threshold. General work visas require employer sponsorship and approval from the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications or skills are needed to become a meter reader or vending-machine collector in Greece?**
A: No formal education is required beyond compulsory schooling. Employers prefer candidates with a valid driver's license, basic math skills, and physical fitness. On-the-job training is provided. Knowledge of Greek is essential for communication with customers and reading local meters.
