## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sales workers in Greece?**
A: Sales workers not elsewhere classified in Greece typically earn between €830 (minimum wage) and €1,200 gross per month, with potential for higher earnings through commissions. Pay varies by experience, sector, and performance bonuses.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU sales workers in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified roles (requires degree and salary threshold) or a national work permit for other sales positions. Both require a verified job offer and employer sponsorship, with preference given to local hires.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for sales workers in Greece?**
A: Sales workers can advance to senior sales, team leader, or key account manager roles. With experience, paths include business development or sales management. Proficiency in Greek and English, plus continuous training, enhances mobility. Some may transition to self-employment or entrepreneurship.
