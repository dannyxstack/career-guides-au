## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shelf filler in Greece?**
A: In Greece, shelf fillers typically earn the national minimum wage, which as of 2024 is €830 per month gross for a full-time position. Some employers may offer slight increases based on experience or store size. Overtime and night shifts often attract additional pay of 20-40% above the base rate.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as shelf fillers in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Greece is intended for highly skilled workers with a university degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds, typically above €30,000/year. Shelf filler positions rarely qualify due to low skill requirements and pay. Non-EU workers may instead seek a standard work permit through employer sponsorship for lower-skilled roles, subject to labor market tests.

**Q: What are common career progression opportunities for shelf fillers in Greece?**
A: Shelf fillers in Greece can advance to positions such as team leader, assistant manager, or department manager in retail stores. With experience, some move into logistics or warehouse supervision. Retail companies often offer internal training programs. Progression depends on store size and company policy; larger chains provide more opportunities.
