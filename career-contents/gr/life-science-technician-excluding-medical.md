## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Life Science Technicians in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Life Science Technicians in Greece typically range from €14,000 to €24,000 per year, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €12,000–€14,000, while senior technicians can earn up to €28,000. Public sector roles may offer additional benefits, but private sector salaries are often higher.

**Q: Can non-EU Life Science Technicians work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU Life Science Technicians can apply for an EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a valid job offer and meet the minimum salary threshold set by national law. The employer must sponsor the application. Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if the position cannot be filled by a Greek or EU citizen. Both routes require a recognised tertiary qualification or equivalent experience.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Life Science Technician in Greece?**
A: Life Science Technicians in Greece assist in research and analysis within biology, chemistry, or environmental labs. Typical duties include preparing samples, operating laboratory equipment, recording data, and maintaining lab safety. They often collaborate with scientists in universities, research institutes, or private companies, and may specialise in fields like microbiology, biochemistry, or plant science.
