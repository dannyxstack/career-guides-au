## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Text Proofreading and Spell Checking with Real-time High Accuracy by AI Models
- Data Encoding and Classification: Standardized Conversion from Spreadsheets to Databases
- Standardization of format, such as layout and punctuation uniformity
- Simple document template filling and letter generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Logic and semantic proofreading of complex text; AI provides suggestions, but humans must finalize.
- Auxiliary proofreading of multilingual documents, AI draft first then human polishing
- Data quality monitoring, AI flags outliers for human review
- Quickly locate reference sources via AI search to improve proofreading efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Semantic judgment and style grasp in complex contexts
- Authoritative verification of legal or professional terminology
- Responsibility for handling data involving trade secrets or privacy
- Cross-departmental communication and requirement clarification

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced configuration of AI proofreading tools (e.g. Grammarly Business, Acrolinx)

- Data visualization and basic analysis

- Domain expertise (e.g., legal, medical knowledge)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for coding, proofreading and related clerks in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average monthly gross salary for coding and proofreading clerks ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and location (higher in Athens). Entry-level positions may start around €700, while senior clerks can earn up to €1,400. Part-time roles are common.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as coding or proofreading clerks in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically apply for a national work permit (visa type D) after securing a job offer. The employer must prove no suitable EU candidate exists. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable, as it requires a university degree and a salary threshold of at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, which clerical roles rarely meet.

**Q: How is AI impacting coding and proofreading clerical jobs in Greece?**
A: AI tools for proofreading and basic coding are becoming common in Greek companies, especially in publishing and IT. While some routine tasks are automated, demand remains for clerks who can review AI output and handle nuanced work. Upskilling in AI-assisted workflows is advisable to remain competitive.
