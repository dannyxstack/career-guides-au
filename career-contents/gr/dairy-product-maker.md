## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dairy products makers in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for dairy products makers in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer size. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced workers in larger dairies may earn up to €1,500. Overtime and bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: Can foreign dairy products makers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Yes, foreign workers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a university degree or at least 5 years of professional experience and a job offer with a salary above the Greek threshold (€28,000 gross annually in 2025). Alternatively, a national work visa under Law 4251/2014 requires a labor market test and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions for dairy products makers in Greece?**
A: Dairy makers usually work in production facilities with shifts (including nights and weekends). The job involves standing for long periods, handling raw milk, operating machines, and maintaining hygiene standards. Seasonal peak demand occurs in summer. Training is often on-the-job, but vocational certifications in dairy technology are valued.
