## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sewing machine operators in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the average monthly salary for sewing machine operators ranges from €700 to €1,000 gross, depending on experience and employer. Many positions are paid at or near the national minimum wage (€830 per month as of 2025). Overtime and piece-rate work can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as sewing machine operators in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Greece under a national work visa, typically requiring a job offer and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers, but sewing machine operators usually do not meet the salary threshold (€46,000 in 2025). The simpler route is the national work permit for low-skilled labor, subject to annual quotas.

**Q: What are the working conditions for sewing machine operators in Greece?**
A: Sewing machine operators in Greece typically work in garment factories, often on a full-time basis. The job involves repetitive tasks, standing for long hours, and meeting production targets. Working hours are usually 8 hours per day, 5 days a week, with occasional overtime. Safety regulations apply, and training is provided on the job.
