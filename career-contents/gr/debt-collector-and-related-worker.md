## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for debt collectors in Greece?**
A: Debt collectors in Greece typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start near the minimum wage (€830/month as of 2024), while experienced collectors can earn higher through commissions and performance bonuses.

**Q: Can non-EU debt collectors obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Greece?**
A: Non-EU debt collectors may qualify for the EU Blue Card if they hold a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Greek national average gross salary. Alternatively, a national work visa is possible if the employer proves no suitable EU candidate is available. Debt collection roles may not always meet skill shortage criteria; always consult the Greek Ministry of Migration.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a debt collector in Greece?**
A: A high school diploma is the minimum requirement, though experience in customer service or finance is highly valued. Fluency in Greek is essential, and English is beneficial. Employers often prefer candidates with degrees in economics, business, or law. Strong communication, negotiation, and computer skills are key, with on-the-job training commonly provided.
