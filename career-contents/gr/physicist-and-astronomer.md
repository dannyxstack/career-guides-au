## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for physicists and astronomers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for physicists and astronomers in Greece vary by sector and experience. In academia, entry-level positions pay around €1,200–€1,500 gross monthly, while senior roles reach €2,000–€2,500. Private sector jobs in tech or energy can offer up to €2,500. Public research institutes provide stable but lower pay, with additional benefits.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU physicists and astronomers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a binding job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €1,800/month in 2024), a recognized university degree, and a one-year contract. Alternatively, a national work permit (for lower salaries) is possible if the employer proves no suitable EU candidate.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for physicists and astronomers in Greece?**
A: Career opportunities include research at institutes like NCSR Demokritos, universities, and the National Observatory of Athens. Private sector roles in renewable energy, data science, and semiconductor industries are growing. Fluency in Greek is often required for academic positions, while English may suffice in international companies. Networking and publications enhance prospects.
