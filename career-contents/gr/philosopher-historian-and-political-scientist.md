## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level salaries in academia or research typically range from €800–€1,200 net per month. Senior professionals or those in government policy roles may earn €1,500–€2,500 net. Salaries vary significantly by sector, with public sector positions often lower than private think tanks or international organizations.

**Q: Can the EU Blue Card help non-EU philosophers, historians, and political scientists work in Greece?**
A: Yes. Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a binding job offer with a gross annual salary of at least €33,000 (2024 threshold) and relevant qualifications. For occupations listed as shortage, a lower salary may apply. Work permits are employer-sponsored and require recognition of degrees by DOATAP.

**Q: What are the main employers for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Greece?**
A: Main employers are universities (as researchers or lecturers), research institutes (e.g., Hellenic Foundation for European & Foreign Policy), government ministries, and think tanks. NGOs, cultural foundations, and media also hire for policy analysis, historical research, and editorial roles. Many professionals work on project-based contracts rather than permanent positions.
