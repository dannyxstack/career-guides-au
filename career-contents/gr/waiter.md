## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a waiter in Greece?**
A: The wage for waiters is governed by the national collective labor agreement. As of 2024, the official minimum monthly salary is €780 gross. Actual pay often includes tips and may be higher in upscale establishments. Overtime and holiday bonuses are mandated by law, but individual contracts vary.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a waiter in Greece via an EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers requiring a university degree or equivalent experience, which waiters typically lack. Instead, non-EU nationals can obtain a Greek national work visa if sponsored by an employer and if the position cannot be filled by a Greek or EU citizen. The employer must apply for a work permit through the Greek authorities.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for waiters in Greece?**
A: Experienced waiters can advance to head waiter, supervisor, or restaurant manager. Some pursue sommelier or bartender roles with specialized training. The tourism season in Greece offers temporary but valuable experience. Further education in hotel management or hospitality can open doors to higher positions in hotels and resorts.
