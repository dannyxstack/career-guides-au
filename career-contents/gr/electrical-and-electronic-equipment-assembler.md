## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start at around €700, while experienced assemblers can earn up to €1,500. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total compensation.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: To work as an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Greece via the EU Blue Card, a non-EU national must have a valid job offer from a Greek employer that meets the minimum salary threshold required by Greek law. The applicant must also hold a recognized tertiary education qualification (at least bachelor's degree) relevant to the field. The employer must prove they could not find a suitable EU candidate.

**Q: What are the main duties of an Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assembler in Greece?**
A: Electrical and Electronic Equipment Assemblers in Greece are responsible for assembling components such as circuit boards, wiring harnesses, and control panels according to technical specifications. They use hand tools and soldering equipment, test finished products for functionality, and ensure compliance with quality standards. Work typically occurs in manufacturing plants, often with repetitive tasks requiring precision and attention to detail.
