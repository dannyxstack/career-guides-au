## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for nursing professionals in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level registered nurses in the public sector earn approximately €1,100–€1,400 per month gross, while experienced nurses can earn up to €1,800. Private sector salaries vary, often lower in smaller clinics. Overtime and shift differentials may increase take-home pay. Overall, nursing salaries in Greece are modest compared to other EU countries.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign nursing professionals to work in Greece via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nurses can work in Greece through the national work visa (type D) if they have a job offer and meet qualification requirements. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers with a recognized degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece. Both routes require recognition of nursing qualifications by the Greek authorities.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for nurses in Greece?**
A: Nurses in Greece can advance to supervisory roles like head nurse or nursing director in hospitals, often requiring postgraduate qualifications. Specialization in areas such as intensive care, oncology, or pediatrics is possible through additional training. Some pursue academic careers in nursing education or research. Private healthcare and home care also offer growth with experience.
