## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Greece ranges from €700 to €1,000 gross, depending on experience and location. Many positions are paid at or near the national minimum wage, which is €830 per month as of 2024. Overtime and tips may supplement income in some establishments.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as cashiers or ticket clerks in Greece through the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, cashiers and ticket clerks are not eligible for the EU Blue Card, which is reserved for highly skilled positions requiring higher education or specialized experience. Non-EU workers generally need a regular work visa sponsored by an employer, but these roles are typically filled by Greek or EU citizens due to local labor availability.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for cashiers in Greece?**
A: With experience, cashiers can move into supervisory roles such as head cashier or shift manager. Some transition to retail management, inventory control, or customer service positions. Further education in accounting or business can lead to roles in finance or administration, though formal training is often required.
