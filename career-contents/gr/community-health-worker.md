## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a community health worker in Greece?**
A: Community health workers in Greece earn between €800 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience, location, and whether they work in the public or private sector. Public sector positions may offer additional benefits, while private sector roles can vary. Entry-level workers typically start at the lower end.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for community health workers from non-EU countries to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU community health workers can apply for a national work visa (Type D) sponsored by a Greek employer. The EU Blue Card is an option for those with a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently around €28,500 per year), but this typically requires higher-skilled roles. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with Greek labor laws.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for community health workers in Greece?**
A: Community health workers can progress to roles such as health visitor, public health coordinator, or program manager after gaining experience and additional training. Further education, such as a bachelor's or master's in public health, opens more senior positions in hospitals, NGOs, or government agencies. The field is growing due to increased focus on primary care and health promotion.
