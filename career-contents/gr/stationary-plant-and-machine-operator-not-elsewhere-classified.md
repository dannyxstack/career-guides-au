## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for stationary plant and machine operators in Greece?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for this occupation in Greece ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €850, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,800. Salaries can be higher in sectors like manufacturing and energy.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as stationary plant and machine operators in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a valid job offer in this occupation. Requirements include a higher education degree or five years of relevant professional experience, plus a gross annual salary of at least €31,960 (2024 threshold). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for stationary plant and machine operators in Greece?**
A: Operators can progress to supervisory roles like shift supervisor or plant manager after gaining experience. Additional training in automation, maintenance, or safety can lead to specialized positions. Certification programs offered by OAED (Manpower Employment Organization) and technical schools enhance skills, enabling moves into higher-paid roles in energy, manufacturing, or logistics.
