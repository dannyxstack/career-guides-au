## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Advertising and Public Relations Managers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for Advertising and PR Managers in Greece vary widely based on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions start around €25,000–€35,000 annually, while mid-career managers earn €40,000–€60,000. Senior roles in major agencies or multinational firms can exceed €80,000 per year, often with performance bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Advertising and PR Managers to work in Greece?**
A: Foreign professionals can apply for a Greek work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross annual salary (approximately €48,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national visa for dependent employment is possible if the employer files a request and the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for Advertising and PR Management roles in Greece?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, business, or a related field. A master's degree (e.g., MBA or MSc in Marketing) can be advantageous. Fluency in Greek and English is essential. Professional experience of 5+ years in advertising or PR, along with a portfolio of successful campaigns, is typically expected for management roles.
