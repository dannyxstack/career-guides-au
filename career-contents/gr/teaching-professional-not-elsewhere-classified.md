## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely by sector and experience. In public schools, entry-level pay is around €1,100–€1,300 per month, while private institutions may offer more. Private tutoring or specialized training roles can range from €1,500 to €2,200 monthly. Overall, earnings depend on qualifications, hours, and employer.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for teaching professionals to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU teaching professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece (approx. €34,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work permit (e.g., for dependent employment) requires employer sponsorship and labor market test. Both routes lead to residency and work authorization.

**Q: What is the career outlook for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Demand exists in private language schools, vocational training centers, and as private tutors. Public sector positions require certification and Greek language proficiency, limiting access for foreigners. Opportunities may grow with digital education and lifelong learning programs, but competition is moderate. Continuing education and specialization can enhance prospects.
