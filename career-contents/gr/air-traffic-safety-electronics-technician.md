## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Greece?**
A: Salaries for these technicians in Greece are based on public sector pay scales. Entry-level positions start around €20,000 per year, while experienced technicians earn up to €35,000 annually. Additional allowances for night shifts or overtime may apply. The Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA) is the main employer.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU national to work as an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa sponsored by an employer or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approximately €30,000-€33,000 per year). Both routes require employer sponsorship and are processed through Greek consulates and the Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Greece?**
A: Candidates typically need a degree or diploma in electronics, telecommunications, or a related engineering field. After recruitment by the Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA), they must complete specialized training and pass exams to obtain an HCAA license. Continuous professional development and periodic recertification are required.
