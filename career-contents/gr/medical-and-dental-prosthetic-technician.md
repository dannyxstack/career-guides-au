- Digital design of standard dentures and crowns using CAD software

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software improves the efficiency and accuracy of complex restoration design

- Enhanced communication with dentists and prosthetists via remote collaboration platforms

**Human moat:**

- Personalised aesthetic judgment and manual fine adjustment (e.g., biomimetic colouring)
- Handling complex maxillofacial defects, biomechanical adaptation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical and dental prosthetic technician in Greece?**
A: In Greece, entry-level technicians earn around €700-€900 per month, while experienced professionals can earn €1,200-€1,800. Those with specialized skills or running their own lab may earn higher. Salaries vary by region, with Athens and Thessaloniki offering higher pay.

**Q: How can a foreign medical and dental prosthetic technician work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for non-EU highly skilled workers. You need a recognized degree (at least bachelor's level) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece (approx. €28,000/year in 2023). The employer applies for a work permit, then you apply for the Blue Card at the Greek consulate.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a medical and dental prosthetic technician in Greece?**
A: In Greece, you typically need a diploma from a public or private vocational school (IEK) in dental technology, followed by a certification exam. Alternatively, a university degree in dental technology. Registration with the Hellenic Dental Association may be required. For medical prosthetics, a degree in orthotics/prosthetics is needed.
