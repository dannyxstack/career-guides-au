## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' engineers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for ships' engineers in Greece vary based on experience, vessel type, and rank. Entry-level engineers may earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month, while chief engineers on large vessels can earn €3,500–€5,000 or more. Additional allowances for sea service and overtime are common. Compensation is often governed by collective agreements.

**Q: What work visa routes exist for ships' engineers to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU ships' engineers can work in Greece via a national visa for seafarers or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Both require a valid job offer, recognized qualifications (e.g., STCW certification), and meeting salary thresholds. The Blue Card is for engineers with higher education and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a ships' engineer in Greece?**
A: Ships' engineers in Greece must hold STCW (Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping) certification, typically starting with a junior engineer license. Formal education from an accredited maritime academy (e.g., Aegean Maritime College) is required for higher ranks. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Greek Ministry of Maritime Affairs. Additional sea service and exams are needed for promotion.
