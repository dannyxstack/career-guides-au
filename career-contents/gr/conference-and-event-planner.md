- Customer needs collection and preliminary plan generation: AI chatbot can automatically gather preferences and generate activity frameworks

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Creative themes and visual design: AI generates multiple concept inspirations, planners filter and optimise

- Real-time event monitoring and adjustment: AI sensors analyze foot traffic and temperature to assist on-site decisions

- Data-driven review: AI automatically summarizes participant feedback and social media trends to generate improvement reports

**Human moat:**

- Cultural sensitivity: understanding and integrating customs and preferences from different regions
- Branding and Storytelling: Deeply link events with corporate culture and social issues.

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for conference and event planners in Greece?**
A: Salaries for conference and event planners in Greece vary by experience and location, with entry-level positions earning around €15,000-€20,000 annually. Mid-level planners can expect €25,000-€35,000, while senior roles in major cities like Athens may reach €45,000 or more. Freelancers often charge daily rates between €200-€500.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for foreign conference and event planners to work in Greece?**
A: Foreign conference and event planners can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card for highly skilled workers, requiring a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, the Greek National Visa for highly qualified employees or the seasonal work permit for event-specific roles may apply. Employer sponsorship is mandatory.

**Q: What are the career prospects for conference and event planners in Greece?**
A: Career prospects in Greece are promising due to growing tourism and MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) sectors. Professionals can advance to senior planner, event director, or start their own agency. Networking and certifications like CMP (Certified Meeting Professional) boost opportunities. Seasonal peaks in summer and conference seasons offer varied work.
