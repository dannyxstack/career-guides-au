## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garbage and recycling collectors in Greece?**
A: In Greece, garbage and recycling collectors are typically paid based on collective labor agreements. Entry-level monthly salaries range from €700 to €900 gross, while experienced workers may earn up to €1,100. Pay often includes allowances for shift work, overtime, and hazardous conditions. Public sector workers may have slightly different scales.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as garbage and recycling collectors in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Greece through the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a national work visa. However, garbage and recycling collecting is classified as low-skilled work, so the EU Blue Card typically does not apply. The national work visa requires a job offer and labor market test proving no EU candidate is available. Third-country nationals may also apply for a seasonal worker permit.

**Q: What are the main duties and working conditions for garbage and recycling collectors in Greece?**
A: Garbage and recycling collectors in Greece collect waste from residential and commercial areas, operate collection vehicles, and ensure proper disposal. Work is physically demanding, often starting early morning, and involves exposure to weather and odors. Workers use protective gear. Many are employed by municipalities or private waste management companies. Training is usually on the job.
