## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a house builder in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a house builder in Greece ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced builders can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and specialized skills may increase pay. Salaries are often more competitive in major cities like Athens and Thessaloniki.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a house builder in Greece under the EU Blue Card or another visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as house builders in Greece through the National Visa for dependent employment (type D), which requires a confirmed job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is generally reserved for highly skilled positions requiring a university degree, so it is rarely applicable to house builders. Alternatively, seasonal work permits exist for temporary construction projects.

**Q: What are the main duties of a house builder in Greece?**
A: House builders in Greece erect and repair residential structures using materials like concrete, bricks, and wood. Tasks include laying foundations, framing walls, installing roofs, and finishing interiors. They must understand Greek building regulations and often work with teams on site. Physical stamina and familiarity with traditional masonry techniques are valued.
