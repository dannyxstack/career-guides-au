## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for real estate agents and property managers in Greece?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, location, and commission structures. Entry-level agents may earn around €12,000–€18,000 annually, while experienced professionals can earn €25,000–€40,000 or more. Property managers often have fixed salaries plus bonuses, typically ranging from €15,000 to €30,000 per year. Major cities like Athens and Thessaloniki offer higher earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as real estate agents or property managers in Greece via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in these roles if they secure a job offer from a Greek employer. The employer must apply for a work permit, and for highly qualified positions, an EU Blue Card may be available if the job meets salary thresholds (usually at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece). Alternatively, a national work visa can be obtained for specific employment. Self-employment options exist via a business visa or residence permit for entrepreneurs.

**Q: What qualifications or licenses are required to become a real estate agent or property manager in Greece?**
A: While no formal degree is mandatory, professional certifications from recognized bodies like the Hellenic Real Estate Federation (EOMEX) are highly recommended. Real estate agents must register with the local Chamber of Commerce and obtain a professional license. For property management, additional knowledge of Greek property law and rental regulations is essential. Fluency in Greek is typically required for local transactions, though English may suffice for expat-focused roles.
