- Image recognition-based automatic detection of cable wear

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AR headset displays complex rigging connection steps and safety annotations

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for riggers and cable splicers in Greece?**
A: Salaries for riggers and cable splicers in Greece vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around the national minimum wage (€830 per month gross in 2024). Experienced professionals can earn €1,500 to €2,000 per month, especially in specialized sectors like telecommunications or industrial projects.

**Q: Can non-EU riggers and cable splicers work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a binding job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Greece, along with recognized higher education or five years of professional experience. Alternatively, a national work permit for skilled labor is available, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from local authorities.

**Q: What is the job outlook for riggers and cable splicers in Greece?**
A: Job prospects are moderate, driven by infrastructure projects, renewable energy installations, and telecom network expansions. Demand is steady in urban centers and industrial zones. Knowledge of safety standards and certifications (e.g., for working at height) can improve employability. Seasonal fluctuations may occur in some sectors.
