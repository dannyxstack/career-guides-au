## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Production Clerk in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the gross monthly salary for a production clerk typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions may start near the minimum wage (€830 in 2024), while experienced clerks in larger firms can earn up to €1,500.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Production Clerk in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Greece if they have a valid job offer, a university degree (or 5+ years of relevant professional experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. For 2024, this threshold is approximately €24,000 annually. Alternatively, a national work permit may be possible for lower-skilled positions.

**Q: What qualifications or experience are needed to become a Production Clerk in Greece?**
A: Most employers require a high school diploma (Apolytirio) and basic computer literacy, especially in office software. Vocational training in logistics or administration is advantageous. Experience in manufacturing or warehouse environments is highly valued. Language skills: Greek is essential; English may be required in international companies.
