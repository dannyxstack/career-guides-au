## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for creative and performing artists not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Income varies widely due to project-based work. In Greece, entry-level artists may earn around €10,000–€15,000 annually, while experienced professionals can reach €25,000–€40,000. Public funding, EU grants, and private commissions supplement income. Salaries are often lower than the EU average, reflecting Greece's economic conditions.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU artists to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU artists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the Greek average (approx. €24,000 gross/year). Alternatively, a national work visa requires employer sponsorship and labor market test. Self-employed artists may apply for a residence permit for independent economic activity. No specific artist visa exists.

**Q: What are the main employment opportunities for creative and performing artists not elsewhere classified in Greece?**
A: Opportunities include freelance work in advertising, film, theater, and cultural events, plus roles in museums, galleries, and tourism. Public sector positions exist in state-funded arts organizations, but competition is high. Digital platforms and EU cultural programs offer remote and project-based work. Many artists combine multiple income streams due to limited full-time roles.
