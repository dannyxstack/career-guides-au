## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for sweepers and related labourers in Greece is around the national minimum wage, approximately €830 gross per month for full-time employment. Pay can vary by employer, region, and type of contract. Some workers are paid hourly, often at rates between €4–6 per hour. Overtime or additional bonuses are uncommon.

**Q: Can non-EU workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for sweeper jobs in Greece?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a Greek work visa and residence permit through employer sponsorship under the national quota system. However, the EU Blue Card is not applicable as this occupation typically does not meet the high-skilled salary threshold. Official routes include the general work permit for third-country nationals; seasonal work permits may also be available for temporary positions.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for sweepers in Greece?**
A: Sweepers and related labourers in Greece usually work outdoors or in indoor public spaces, often for municipalities or private cleaning companies. Shifts can include early mornings, evenings, or weekends. The work is physically demanding, involving manual sweeping, litter collection, and operating simple cleaning equipment. Contracts may be part-time or seasonal, especially in tourist-heavy regions.
