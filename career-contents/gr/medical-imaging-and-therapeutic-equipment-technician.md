## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for medical imaging technicians in Greece?**
A: In Greece, medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians earn between €1,200 and €1,800 gross per month. Public hospitals offer lower starting salaries but include benefits, while private clinics often pay higher. Experienced specialists in MRI or CT may earn up to €2,000. Overtime and on-call hours can increase total compensation.

**Q: What is the work visa route for non-EU technicians to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU medical imaging technicians can obtain an EU Blue Card for Greece. Requirements include a recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent, a binding job offer, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. The employer must apply to the Greek Ministry of Labour, and the card is valid for up to two years, renewable.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for medical imaging technicians in Greece?**
A: Technicians can specialise in advanced modalities like MRI, CT, mammography, or nuclear medicine through postgraduate certificates or on-the-job training. Career paths include senior technician, department supervisor, or educator in vocational institutes. Some transition into medical equipment sales or healthcare management, especially after obtaining a bachelor's degree in healthcare administration.
