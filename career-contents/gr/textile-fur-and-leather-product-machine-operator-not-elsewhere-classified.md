## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Textile, Fur and Leather Products Machine Operator in Greece?**
A: Salaries for these operators in Greece vary by experience, location, and employer, but generally range near the national minimum wage (approximately €830 gross monthly as of 2024). Experienced operators in specialized sectors may earn up to €1,200 per month. Overtime and shift allowances can supplement base pay.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a Textile, Fur and Leather Products Machine Operator in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card to work in this occupation in Greece. Requirements include a valid job offer of at least one year, a contract meeting the national salary threshold (typically 1.5 times the average gross annual salary), and recognized qualifications or relevant experience. The employer must prove the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Textile, Fur and Leather Products Machine Operator in Greece?**
A: Operators set up, operate, and monitor machines that cut, sew, or finish textiles, fur, and leather goods. Tasks include adjusting machine settings, feeding materials, inspecting output for defects, and performing basic maintenance. In Greece, operators often work in factories producing clothing, upholstery, or accessories, requiring attention to detail and adherence to safety standards.
