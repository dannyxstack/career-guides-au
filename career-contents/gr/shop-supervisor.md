## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a shop supervisor in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a shop supervisor in Greece ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and company size. In major cities like Athens or Thessaloniki, salaries may be higher, while smaller towns offer lower pay. Overtime and bonuses can supplement income, but overall compensation is moderate compared to other European countries.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a non-EU citizen to become a shop supervisor in Greece?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as shop supervisors in Greece via the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The Blue Card requires a university degree or 5+ years of professional experience, a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (approx. €24,000 annually). Alternatively, employers can sponsor a work permit under Greek immigration law, which involves a labor market test and quota availability.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of a shop supervisor in Greece?**
A: Shop supervisors in Greece oversee daily store operations, manage staff schedules, ensure customer service standards, handle inventory and stock replenishment, and enforce health and safety regulations. They also process sales transactions, resolve customer complaints, and collaborate with management to achieve sales targets. Strong organizational and leadership skills are essential, as well as fluency in Greek for effective communication with customers and colleagues.
