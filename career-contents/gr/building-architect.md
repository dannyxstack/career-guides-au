## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for building architects in Greece?**
A: Entry-level building architects in Greece earn around €18,000–€24,000 annually. With 5–10 years of experience, salaries range from €25,000–€35,000. Senior architects or project managers can earn €40,000 or more, depending on the firm and location. Salaries are higher in Athens and for those registered with the Technical Chamber of Greece.

**Q: How can a foreign building architect obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU architects can work in Greece via the EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized university degree and a job offer meeting the national salary threshold (currently determined by Greek law). Alternatively, a national work visa requires employer sponsorship and a labor market test. Both routes require registration with the Technical Chamber of Greece (TEE) and recognition of qualifications by the Hellenic Ministry of Education.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for building architects in Greece?**
A: Building architects in Greece typically start as junior designers, advancing to project architects, then senior architects or team leaders. Many pursue specialization in restoration, sustainable design, or urban planning. Experienced architects often open private practices or become partners in firms. Registration with the Technical Chamber of Greece is mandatory for independent work and signing off on projects.
