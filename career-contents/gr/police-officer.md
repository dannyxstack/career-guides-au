## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a police officer in Greece?**
A: A police officer in Greece starts with a gross monthly salary of approximately €900 to €1,100, depending on rank and service years. Salaries increase with seniority, and additional allowances for night shifts, holidays, or hazardous duties may apply. Higher ranks and specialized positions earn more.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as police officers in Greece via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Police officer positions in Greece are generally restricted to Greek or EU citizens due to nationality requirements. Non-EU nationals may only enter via a work visa or EU Blue Card for support roles (e.g., administrative or technical staff) if no local candidates qualify. Direct law enforcement roles typically require Greek citizenship.

**Q: What are the main career progression steps in the Greek police force?**
A: Career advancement requires passing competitive exams for higher ranks (e.g., from officer to lieutenant). Entry-level officers undergo training at the Police Academy. To become an officer, candidates need a university degree and pass physical and written tests. Promotions to senior ranks like commander involve further exams and service experience.
