## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an insulation worker in Greece?**
A: The average monthly salary for insulation workers in Greece ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Overtime and specialized tasks like fireproofing or acoustic insulation can increase pay. Entry-level workers typically earn closer to the minimum wage, while senior or certified workers may earn higher rates.

**Q: Can insulation workers from non-EU countries work in Greece via the EU Blue Card or other visas?**
A: Non-EU insulation workers generally need a job offer and an employer-sponsored work permit to work in Greece. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled professionals with a university degree or equivalent; insulation work typically does not qualify unless the role involves advanced engineering or management. Other routes include seasonal work permits or bilateral agreements for temporary projects.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for insulation workers in Greece?**
A: Insulation workers can advance to supervisory roles, estimators, or project managers. Gaining certifications in energy efficiency, fireproofing, or green building materials can lead to higher pay and specialization. Experienced workers may also start their own contracting businesses, especially with Greece's focus on building energy upgrades under EU renovation targets.
