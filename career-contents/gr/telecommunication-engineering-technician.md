## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a telecommunications engineering technician in Greece?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for telecommunications engineering technicians in Greece ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Athens offers higher pay. Most companies provide 14 salaries per year, including holiday and Christmas bonuses.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for telecommunications engineering technicians to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approx. €33,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a national visa for highly qualified workers is available. Employers must initiate the process.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for telecommunications engineering technicians in Greece?**
A: Telecommunications engineering technicians in Greece work for major providers like Cosmote, Vodafone, and Wind, or as freelancers. Roles involve installing, maintaining, and troubleshooting network equipment, with growing demand in 5G expansion. A technical diploma or equivalent is typically required.
