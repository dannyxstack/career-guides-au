## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for weaving and knitting machine operators in Greece?**
A: In Greece, the gross monthly salary for weaving and knitting machine operators typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and the employer. Entry-level positions may start near the national minimum wage (€780–€830), while skilled operators in industrial regions can earn higher. Overtime and shift allowances may supplement base pay.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a weaving machine operator to work in Greece?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Greece via an employer-sponsored national work permit, requiring a job offer and proof that the position could not be filled locally. The EU Blue Card is primarily for highly skilled workers with a tertiary degree; weaving operators typically do not qualify unless they hold a relevant degree. Both routes require the employer to file with Greek authorities.

**Q: What career progression or additional skills can weaving and knitting machine operators pursue in Greece?**
A: Operators can advance to supervisory roles or specialize in maintenance and repair of textile machinery. Technical training in automation or digital textile production may improve prospects. Some operators move into quality control or textile design with further education. The Greek textile industry, though smaller than in the past, still offers opportunities in niche or high-end production.
