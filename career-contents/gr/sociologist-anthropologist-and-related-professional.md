## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sociologists and anthropologists in Greece?**
A: Salaries for sociologists and anthropologists in Greece vary by sector. In the public sector and academia, entry-level gross monthly salary is around €1,200–€1,500, while in private research or consulting firms it can reach €2,000–€2,500. Experienced professionals may earn higher, especially in senior roles.

**Q: Can non-EU sociologists and anthropologists work in Greece via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Greece as sociologists or anthropologists via the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary) and a recognized higher education qualification. The employer must apply to the competent Greek authority.

**Q: What are typical career paths for sociologists and anthropologists in Greece?**
A: Typical employers include universities, research institutes, non-profits, government agencies, and market research firms. Career progression often leads to roles such as senior researcher, policy analyst, or project manager. Some pursue PhDs for academic careers, while others move into consulting or international organizations.
