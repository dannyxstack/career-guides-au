## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a travel attendant in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for travel attendants in Croatia vary by employer and experience. Flight attendants typically earn between €800 and €1,200 net per month, while train or bus stewards may earn slightly less. Senior positions or those with international airlines can command higher pay.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as a travel attendant in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is an option if the gross annual salary meets the threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia). However, most travel attendant positions do not reach this level, so the standard work permit route is more common.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a travel attendant in Croatia?**
A: Employers typically require a high school diploma, fluency in English and Croatian, and a certificate of good health. For flight attendants, completion of a safety training course approved by the Croatian Civil Aviation Agency is mandatory. Customer service experience is an advantage.
