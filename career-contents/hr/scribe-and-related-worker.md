## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes and related workers in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for scribes and related workers in Croatia ranges from €900 to €1,200, depending on experience, sector (e.g., legal or administrative), and region. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while experienced professionals in specialized fields can earn up to €1,500. These figures are based on official labor market data.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as scribes in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers requiring a tertiary degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (about €1,800 gross monthly). Scribe roles typically do not meet these criteria. Instead, non-EU citizens need a standard work permit, which requires employer sponsorship and a positive labor market test. Residence permits are issued after approval.

**Q: Do I need to speak Croatian to work as a scribe in Croatia?**
A: Yes, fluent Croatian is essential for most scribe positions, as tasks involve drafting and processing official documents, court records, or administrative materials in Croatian. Some international companies or organizations may accept English, but local language proficiency is strongly preferred and often a legal requirement for roles interacting with public institutions.
