## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sweepers and related labourers in Croatia?**
A: As of 2024, the gross monthly salary for sweepers and related labourers in Croatia typically ranges from the minimum wage (€840) to around €1,000. Actual pay depends on the employer, region, and experience. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as sweepers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and requires a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience. Sweepers and related labourers are low-skilled, so this route does not apply. Non-EU nationals may obtain a standard work permit if an employer sponsors them, subject to labour market testing.

**Q: What are the main duties of sweepers and related labourers in Croatia?**
A: Duties include sweeping streets, parks, and public spaces, collecting litter, emptying bins, and cleaning gutters. They may also remove snow and leaves, maintain cleaning equipment, and report hazards. Work is often outdoors and physically demanding, typically for municipal companies or private cleaning firms.
