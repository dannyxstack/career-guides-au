## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a vocational education teacher in Croatia?**
A: The average net monthly salary for vocational education teachers in Croatia ranges from HRK 7,000 to HRK 9,000 (approx. €930–€1,200), depending on experience and institution. Public-sector teachers follow a state pay scale, with increases for seniority and specialization. Private institutions may offer slightly different rates.

**Q: Can non-EU vocational education teachers work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card to work as a vocational education teacher in Croatia if they have a valid job offer, a recognized university degree, and meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia). The employer must first obtain a work permit, after which the Blue Card is issued for up to two years.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a vocational education teacher in Croatia?**
A: To teach vocational subjects in Croatia, you typically need a master's degree in the relevant field and a pedagogical qualification (e.g., from a teacher training program). For public schools, you must pass a professional exam and register with the Croatian Employment Service. Language proficiency in Croatian is usually required, as instruction is in Croatian.
