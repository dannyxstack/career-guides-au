## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for social work and counselling professionals in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for social work and counselling professionals in Croatia generally range from 7,000 to 11,000 HRK gross per month, depending on experience and sector (public or private). Senior or specialized roles can earn up to 14,000 HRK. Public sector positions often include additional benefits like paid leave and pension contributions.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU social work professionals to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a recognized degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times Croatia's average gross salary (approx. 1,600 EUR/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit is available with employer sponsorship, requiring a labor market test. Both routes lead to a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What are the licensing or registration requirements for social workers in Croatia?**
A: Social workers in Croatia must be registered with the Croatian Chamber of Social Workers (Hrvatska komora socijalnih radnika). Requirements include a recognized master's degree in social work, passing a professional exam, and fulfilling continuous education points. Registration is mandatory for practicing in public and private sectors.
