## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an underwater diver in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Croatia vary by experience and sector. Entry-level commercial divers earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross monthly, while experienced divers can earn €2,000–€3,000. Offshore and scientific divers may earn higher rates, but recreational diving instructors often earn less, around €800–€1,200.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for foreign underwater divers in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA divers need a work and residence permit. The employer must obtain a labor market test through the Croatian Employment Service. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, which diving roles rarely meet.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as an underwater diver in Croatia?**
A: Commercial divers need an internationally recognized certification (e.g., IMCA or ADCI) and a Croatian medical certificate. For recreational diving, PADI or CMAS instructor certifications are standard. All foreign certifications must be recognized by the Croatian Ministry of the Sea, Transport and Infrastructure.
