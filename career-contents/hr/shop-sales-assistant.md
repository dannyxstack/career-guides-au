## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a shop sales assistant in Croatia?**
A: The monthly gross salary for shop sales assistants in Croatia typically ranges from €700 to €1,100, depending on experience, location, and employer. The minimum wage is around €700 gross per month (2023). Overtime and commissions may increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as shop sales assistants in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree or high salary, which shop sales assistants typically do not meet. Non-EU nationals must instead obtain a work and residence permit through employer sponsorship, with a labor market test proving no suitable local candidate.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for shop sales assistants in Croatia?**
A: Shop sales assistants can progress to senior sales roles, team leader, or store manager positions. Additional training in customer service or product knowledge can lead to specialized roles. Some workers transition to retail buying, visual merchandising, or e-commerce with further education.
