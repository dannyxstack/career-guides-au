## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bank tellers in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for bank tellers in Croatia ranges from €1,100 to €1,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €900, while senior tellers can earn up to €1,700. Salaries are typically higher in Zagreb compared to other regions.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as bank tellers in Croatia through the EU Blue Card or other work visas?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as bank tellers in Croatia through the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold (currently around 1.5x the average gross salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if the employer demonstrates a labor market test. Both routes require a job offer.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bank teller in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a high school diploma is required, though a degree in finance or economics is advantageous. Employers often provide on-the-job training. Fluency in Croatian is essential, and English is useful. Strong numerical, communication, and customer service skills are key. Experience with banking software is a plus.
