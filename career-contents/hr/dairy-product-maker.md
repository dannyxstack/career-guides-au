## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a dairy products maker in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for dairy products makers in Croatia ranges from 800 to 1,200 EUR, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level workers earn near the minimum wage (approx. 700 EUR gross), while skilled makers in larger dairies may earn higher. Net pay is typically 600–900 EUR.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU dairy products maker to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and residency permit. The employer must obtain a labor market test. For lower-skilled roles like dairy products maker, the EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires higher education and a salary above 1.5 times the national average. A standard work permit is the main route.

**Q: What are the main duties of a dairy products maker in Croatia?**
A: Dairy products makers operate machinery to process milk into cheese, butter, yogurt, and other products. Tasks include pasteurizing, culturing, churning, and packaging. They monitor quality, maintain hygiene standards, and clean equipment. In Croatia, workers may also handle traditional cheese production in small dairies.
