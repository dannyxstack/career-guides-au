- Visual quality inspection: AI vision systems replace manual inspection, identifying defects more accurately

- Production data recording: IoT sensors automatically collect and upload data
- Simple fault diagnosis: AI system quickly locates common faults through pattern recognition

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Equipment condition monitoring: AI-assisted analysis of sensor data to predict maintenance needs
- Process optimization: AI suggests parameter adjustments to improve yield rate and energy efficiency

**Human moat:**

- Complex fault repair: requires comprehensive understanding of mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic systems
- Mold debugging: hand-eye coordination and experiential judgment are difficult to be fully replaced by AI

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for rubber products machine operators in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for rubber products machine operators in Croatia typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and the specific employer. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,400. Overtime and shift allowances can increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for non-EU rubber products machine operators in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals must obtain a work permit sponsored by a Croatian employer. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree or equivalent experience; rubber products machine operators typically do not meet these criteria. Therefore, the standard temporary residence and work permit is the applicable route.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a rubber products machine operator in Croatia?**
A: Typically, employers require completed secondary education, preferably with a focus on technical or vocational training. On-the-job training is provided. Some employers may also accept candidates with basic mechanical skills and a willingness to learn. Knowledge of safety standards and basic machine operation is an advantage.
