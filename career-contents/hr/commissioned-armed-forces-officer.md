## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for commissioned armed forces officers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for commissioned officers in the Croatian Armed Forces vary by rank and experience. A newly commissioned officer may earn around HRK 8,000–10,000 gross monthly (approx. €1,060–1,330). Senior officers with higher ranks can earn significantly more. Additional allowances for special duties, deployments, and years of service apply. Salaries are determined by state regulations and subject to periodic adjustments.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as commissioned armed forces officers in Croatia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled non-EU workers but does not apply to military personnel because armed forces positions are typically reserved for Croatian citizens due to national security requirements. Foreign nationals may be eligible for other work permits if they possess specific skills needed by the military, though such cases are rare and require special approval from the Ministry of Defense.

**Q: What are the basic requirements to become a commissioned armed forces officer in Croatia?**
A: To become a commissioned officer in Croatia, you must be a Croatian citizen, at least 18 years old, have completed secondary education, and pass medical, psychological, and physical fitness tests. Officer candidates typically attend the Croatian Military Academy 'Petar Zrinski' for university-level education. Additional requirements include no criminal record and loyalty to the constitutional order.
