## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical income for an astrologer or fortune-teller in Croatia?**
A: Income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Croatia is highly variable and unregulated, as most are self-employed. Earnings depend on client base, reputation, and services offered (e.g., consultations, events, online content). There is no official salary data; practitioners may charge per session or receive donations. Many supplement income with related work like tarot reading or holistic services.

**Q: Can an astrologer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Croatia?**
A: Standard work routes apply: a Croatian employer can sponsor a work permit if the job meets local labor market tests. The EU Blue Card requires a high-skilled position with a higher education degree and a salary above the national threshold (approx. €2,700/month in 2025). Astrology is unlikely to qualify as high-skilled; self-employed individuals may apply for a temporary stay permit for business purposes.

**Q: What career paths are available for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Croatia?**
A: Practitioners typically work as self-employed consultants, offering in-person or online readings (astrology, tarot, palmistry). Some diversify into event entertainment, workshops, or content creation (blogs, social media, YouTube). A few may work part-time in esoteric shops or holistic centers. Formal employment is rare; most build their own clientele through word-of-mouth and marketing.
