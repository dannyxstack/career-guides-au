## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Professional Services Managers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for Professional Services Managers in Croatia vary by sector, experience, and location. Entry-level positions start around €2,000 gross per month, while senior managers can earn €5,000 or more. The average monthly gross salary in Zagreb is higher than in other regions, typically between €3,000 and €4,000.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for Professional Services Managers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, requiring a valid job offer, a recognized university degree, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) can be obtained through the Croatian Employment Service. EU/EEA nationals have free movement and require no work permit.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a Professional Services Manager in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a university degree in a relevant field (e.g., law, economics, engineering) is required. Most positions demand at least 5 years of professional experience in the specific service area, fluency in Croatian and English, and strong leadership skills. Professional certifications (e.g., CFA, ACCA) can enhance prospects.
