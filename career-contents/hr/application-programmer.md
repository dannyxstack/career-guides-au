## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for applications programmers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for applications programmers in Croatia range from €1,300 to €2,500 net per month for mid-level positions, with senior developers earning up to €3,500 net. Rates vary by experience, location (Zagreb pays higher), and company size. The average gross salary in IT is around €2,000–€3,000 monthly.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals work as an applications programmer in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card after securing a job offer for a highly qualified position. The job must pay at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia (currently around €2,200 monthly). The employer must apply for the work permit; afterward, the worker applies for the Blue Card at the Croatian embassy.

**Q: What programming languages are most in demand for applications programmers in Croatia?**
A: Employers in Croatia seek proficiency in Java, C#, JavaScript (including frameworks like React and Angular), and Python. SQL and knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure) are also highly valued. For mobile development, Kotlin (Android) and Swift (iOS) are common. Adaptability to new technologies is key.
