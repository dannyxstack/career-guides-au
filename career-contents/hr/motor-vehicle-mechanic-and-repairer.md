## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for motor vehicle mechanics and repairers in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, the average gross monthly salary for motor vehicle mechanics and repairers is around €900 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Mechanics in larger cities like Zagreb or on the coast may earn higher wages. Overtime and specialization in high-demand areas, such as electric vehicle repair, can increase pay.

**Q: Can motor vehicle mechanics from non-EU countries work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU motor vehicle mechanics can apply for an EU Blue Card in Croatia if they have a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. The employer must demonstrate the position cannot be filled locally. The Blue Card allows work and residence, and after 18 months, holders can move to other EU countries under certain conditions.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a motor vehicle mechanic in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, becoming a motor vehicle mechanic typically requires completing a three-year secondary vocational school program in car mechanics or a related field. Alternatively, one can complete a shorter vocational program or apprenticeship. Licensing is not mandatory, but certification from manufacturers or bodies like Hrvatska obrtnička komora can enhance job prospects. Ongoing training is beneficial.
