## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a sales worker not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by industry and experience. Entry-level positions typically earn around €800–€1,000 gross per month, while experienced sales workers can earn up to €1,500–€2,000. Commission-based roles may yield higher earnings. Official data from the Croatian Bureau of Statistics shows median gross monthly earnings for retail and wholesale sales workers at approximately €1,100.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as a sales worker in Croatia via the work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit, typically obtained through an employer with a local labor market test. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers earning at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (approx. €2,100/month in 2024). Seasonal work permits exist for temporary sales roles. Applications are filed with the Croatian police or diplomatic missions.

**Q: What are the main career advancement opportunities for sales workers in Croatia?**
A: Sales workers can progress to team leader, regional sales manager, or key account manager. Additional certifications in sales techniques or product specialization can boost advancement. Industries like IT, pharmaceuticals, and tourism offer higher-level roles. Language skills (English, German) are advantageous for international firms based in Zagreb or on the coast.
