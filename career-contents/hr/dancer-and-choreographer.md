## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dancers and choreographers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for dancers and choreographers in Croatia vary widely. Dancers employed by national theatres or dance companies typically earn between €800 and €1,200 net per month. Choreographers may earn €1,000–€1,500 net, but many work freelance on project-based contracts, leading to income fluctuations. Experienced professionals in prestigious ensembles can earn higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU dancers and choreographers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work and residence permit sponsored by a Croatian employer. The EU Blue Card is an option for highly skilled workers, requiring a higher education degree and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average national gross salary (threshold set annually). Dancers without a degree typically use standard work permits.

**Q: What training is required to become a professional dancer or choreographer in Croatia?**
A: Most professional dancers graduate from state-accredited dance academies, such as the Academy of Dramatic Art in Zagreb or the Osijek Ballet School. Choreographers often hold degrees in choreography or related arts. Private dance schools and international workshops provide additional training. Continuous practice, networking, and auditions are essential for career progression.
