## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Croatia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for advisers in Croatia typically range from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, employer (public vs. private sector), and specialization. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior advisers with expertise in EU funding or sustainable practices may earn up to €3,000.

**Q: Can foreign Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers work in Croatia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia as advisers by obtaining a work permit or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The employer must sponsor the application, and the candidate must have a relevant university degree and a valid job offer. The EU Blue Card requires a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Croatia.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Croatia?**
A: Advisers in Croatia help farmers, foresters, and fishers improve productivity and sustainability. Key tasks include advising on EU Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) subsidies, organic certification, crop rotation, pest management, and rural development projects. They also conduct field visits, organize training, and assist with business planning and compliance with environmental regulations.
