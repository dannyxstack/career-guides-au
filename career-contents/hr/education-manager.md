## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Education Manager in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for Education Managers in Croatia vary by sector and experience, typically ranging from €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month. Public schools follow state-regulated pay scales, while private institutions may offer higher compensation. Bonuses and allowances may apply depending on the institution and location.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Education Managers in Croatia through the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, including Education Managers. Requirements include a valid employment contract, recognized higher education qualifications, and a salary meeting the national threshold. The employer must also obtain a work permit from the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Education Manager in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in education, management, or a related field is required, along with several years of teaching or administrative experience. Additional certifications in school leadership or management are advantageous. Proficiency in Croatian is often mandatory, particularly for roles in public institutions.
