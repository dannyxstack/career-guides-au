## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for personnel and careers professionals in Croatia?**
A: As of 2023, the average gross monthly salary for HR professionals in Croatia ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior specialists or managers can earn €3,000 or more. Salaries are higher in Zagreb and in multinational companies.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as personnel and careers professionals in Croatia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Croatia as personnel and careers professionals through the EU Blue Card if they have a recognized university degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approximately €1,500+ gross monthly in 2023). Alternatively, a standard work permit is available, requiring a labor market test showing no suitable EU candidate. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and career progression exist for personnel and careers professionals in Croatia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in human resources, psychology, or business is typically required. Professional certifications like CIPD, SHRM, or local equivalents are advantageous. Career progression starts as an HR assistant or recruiter, advancing to specialist, then HR manager or director. Continuous learning in labor law and digital HR tools is important for growth.
