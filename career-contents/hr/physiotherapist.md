## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a physiotherapist in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for physiotherapists in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and sector (public vs. private). Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles in private clinics can exceed €2,000. Overtime and specializations may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the visa routes for foreign physiotherapists to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified professionals) or a national work permit. Requirements include a recognized degree, employment contract, and proof of qualifications. The employer must obtain a work permit from the Croatian Employment Service. EU/EEA nationals have free movement.

**Q: What is the career outlook for physiotherapists in Croatia?**
A: The demand for physiotherapists in Croatia is steady, driven by an aging population and growing focus on rehabilitation. Opportunities exist in public hospitals, private clinics, sports clubs, and wellness centers. Specializations in sports physiotherapy or neurological rehabilitation are valued. Continuous education is encouraged.
