## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' deck officers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for ships' deck officers in Croatia vary by rank and vessel type. A third officer may earn around €1,500 gross per month, while a chief officer on a large ship can earn €3,000–€4,000. Captains' salaries are higher. Overtime, bonuses, and onboard benefits are common.

**Q: Can non-EU deck officers work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU deck officers can obtain an EU Blue Card in Croatia if they have a valid job offer and meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit can be arranged by an employer. Qualifications must be recognized by the Croatian Ministry of the Sea.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for deck officers in Croatia?**
A: Deck officers in Croatia can progress from third officer to chief officer and captain. Advancement requires additional certifications, sea time, and examinations. The University of Dubrovnik and maritime schools offer specialized training. Career growth also depends on vessel type, with opportunities on commercial, passenger, or fishing vessels.
