## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Croatia?**
A: Average gross monthly salaries typically range from HRK 10,000 to HRK 20,000 (approx. €1,330–€2,660), varying with experience and sector. Senior roles in finance or insurance can earn higher. Salaries in Zagreb are generally above the national average.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can obtain an EU Blue Card (requiring a job offer meeting a salary threshold) or a standard work and residence permit. The employer must secure a work permit from the Croatian Employment Service. The Blue Card offers a simplified process for highly qualified roles.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Croatia?**
A: Career opportunities exist in finance, insurance, IT/data analytics, research institutes, and academia. The growing tech sector demands data scientists and machine learning experts. Actuaries are needed in pension funds, while statisticians work in government, market research, and healthcare. English proficiency is often required.
