## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Sales and Marketing Managers in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Sales and Marketing Managers in Croatia ranges from HRK 12,000 to 18,000, depending on experience and company size. Senior roles can exceed HRK 25,000. Performance bonuses and incentives are common, and salaries in Zagreb and coastal tourist areas tend to be higher.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Sales and Marketing Managers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment in Croatia. Requirements include a valid job offer, recognized higher education, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. The employer must also prove the position could not be filled locally. Applications are processed by the Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed for Sales and Marketing Manager roles in Croatia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in business, marketing, or a related field is typically required, with many employers preferring a master's degree (e.g., MBA). At least 5 years of relevant experience is expected. Fluency in Croatian and English is essential; additional languages like German or Italian are advantageous. Strong digital marketing skills and knowledge of the local market are highly valued.
