## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for this role in Croatia ranges from HRK 8,000 to 12,000 (approximately €1,060–€1,600), depending on experience, location, and the size of the facility. Managers in larger cities like Zagreb or on the coast may earn higher salaries, especially in private or high-end centers.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a non-EU citizen to work as a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a standard work permit, which requires a positive labour market test. Alternatively, they may qualify for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and have a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (currently around 1.5 times the national average). Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Croatia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in sports management, recreation, business administration, or a related field is typically required. Relevant work experience, often 3–5 years, and knowledge of Croatian language are usually expected. Certifications from recognized sports organizations or management institutes can enhance prospects.
