## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for cashiers and ticket clerks in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for cashiers and ticket clerks in Croatia typically range from HRK 4,500 to HRK 6,500 net per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced staff in larger cities or tourist areas can earn more. Overtime and tips may supplement income in some settings.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa for cashier or ticket clerk jobs in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer from a Croatian employer. The employer must apply for a work permit through the Croatian Employment Service, demonstrating that no local candidate is available. After approval, the worker applies for a temporary residence and work permit. EU Blue Card is not applicable as these jobs are low-skilled; standard work permits are used instead.

**Q: Which types of employers commonly hire cashiers and ticket clerks in Croatia?**
A: Cashiers are hired by retail stores, supermarkets, gas stations, and pharmacies. Ticket clerks work in cinemas, theaters, museums, public transport companies (buses, trains), and at tourist attractions like national parks. Seasonal hiring is common in coastal tourism areas during summer.
