## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Croatia?**
A: The net monthly salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Croatia typically ranges from €1,300 to €1,800, depending on experience, facility size, and location. Senior managers in larger facilities may earn up to €2,200 net. These figures are indicative and subject to change.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU nationals to work as an Aged Care Services Manager in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a standard work permit, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals, requiring a contract, higher education, and salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (approx. €2,200 gross).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Aged Care Services Manager in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in healthcare management, social work, or nursing is required, along with several years of experience in aged care. Additional certifications in healthcare administration and knowledge of Croatian regulations are beneficial. Fluency in Croatian is usually mandatory.
