## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic housekeepers in Croatia?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Croatia generally earn between 700 and 900 EUR net per month, often at or slightly above the minimum wage. Actual pay depends on experience, location, hours worked, and whether it's a private household or agency employment. Some employers also provide accommodation or meals.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as domestic housekeepers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified professionals with a university degree or equivalent, so it does not apply to domestic housekeepers. Non-EU citizens must instead obtain a standard work permit and temporary residence permit through an employer, following Croatian labor migration rules.

**Q: What are the main duties and requirements for domestic housekeepers in Croatia?**
A: Duties include cleaning, laundry, ironing, and maintaining household order. Some employers also require basic cooking or childcare. No formal education is mandatory, but prior experience is valued. Knowledge of English or German is a plus, especially in tourist regions. Employers often seek reliability and trustworthiness.
