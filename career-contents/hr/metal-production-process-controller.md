## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Metal Production Process Controller in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, the average gross monthly salary for a Metal Production Process Controller ranges from €1,100 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Net pay typically falls between €800 and €1,100. Larger companies in industrial regions like Sisak may offer higher compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU Metal Production Process Controllers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia via an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience in metal production, and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. 1.5x average gross salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit based on employer sponsorship and a positive labor market test is possible.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a Metal Production Process Controller in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a secondary technical education in metallurgy, mechanical engineering, or a related field is required. Vocational training or apprenticeships in metal processing are highly valued. Employers often seek candidates with knowledge of industrial automation, quality control, and safety regulations. Relevant work experience starts at 2–3 years.
