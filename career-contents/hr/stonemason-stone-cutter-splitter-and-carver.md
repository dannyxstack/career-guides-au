**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Use CAD software for stone cutting and layout design
- Operating automatic stone cutting machines and CNC engraving machines
- Material quantity calculation and layout using BIM models

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using 3D scanning and digital twins for precise stone structure installation
- Use AR glasses to overlay design drawings on construction sites, reducing errors
- Using AI to optimize stone splicing plans, improving aesthetics and strength
- Conduct drone inspections of large stone structures and remotely monitor maintenance needs.

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for stonemasons in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. In tourist-heavy regions like Dalmatia, wages may be higher due to demand. Overtime and specialized carving skills can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU stonemason to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU stonemasons can obtain a standard work permit (dozvola za rad) sponsored by a Croatian employer, or apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and meet salary thresholds. Both require a labor market test proving no local worker is available.

**Q: What training is required to become a stonemason in Croatia?**
A: Becoming a stonemason in Croatia typically requires completing a three-year vocational secondary school program in stone processing or masonry. Apprenticeships are common. Continuing education in stone conservation or digital carving can improve job prospects, especially for restoration work on historic buildings.
