## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Croatia?**
A: The average net monthly salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Croatia ranges from €700 to €950, depending on experience, employer, and region. Seasonal workers may receive additional benefits like accommodation. Pay is often hourly or based on completed tasks, with overtime potentially increasing earnings.

**Q: What official routes exist for non-EU citizens to work as garden and horticultural labourers in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a seasonal work permit under the EU Seasonal Workers Directive, valid for up to 9 months. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as it targets highly skilled workers. Labour market tests may be required. Employers must obtain a work permit through the Croatian Employment Service before hiring.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a garden and horticultural labourer in Croatia?**
A: No formal qualifications are mandatory, but basic knowledge of plants, tools, and safety practices is advantageous. Many employers provide on-the-job training. A secondary school diploma in agriculture or horticulture can be beneficial but is not required. Physical fitness and willingness to work outdoors in various weather conditions are essential.
