- Long-distance highway driving (autonomous truck pilot programs)
- Basic route planning and GPS navigation adjustments
- Basic vehicle status monitoring and routine inspection recording
- Standardized cargo handling (integration with automated warehousing)

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

- Customer relationships and non-standard delivery requirements

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for heavy truck drivers in Croatia?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for heavy truck drivers in Croatia ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, company size, and routes (international vs. domestic). Overtime and per diems may increase net income. Entry-level drivers often start around €900 gross.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU citizens to work as heavy truck drivers in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit (obtained by an employer) and a temporary residence permit. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as it requires a university degree or five years of professional experience, which is uncommon for truck drivers. A seasonal work permit is also available for short-term employment.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a heavy truck driver in Croatia?**
A: You need a valid Croatian driving license for category C+E, a professional driver qualification card (CPC), and a digital tachograph card. Additionally, a basic health certificate and, for international routes, a Driver Attestation from the Ministry of the Interior may be required.
