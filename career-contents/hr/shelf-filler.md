## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for shelf fillers in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, shelf fillers typically earn minimum wage or slightly above, averaging around €700–800 gross per month. Hourly rates range from €4 to €5. Actual pay depends on the employer, location, and hours worked. Some retailers offer performance bonuses or night shift supplements.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU shelf fillers in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit to work as shelf fillers in Croatia. The most common route is employer-sponsored work permit. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold (usually above €2,500 per month), making it generally unsuitable for shelf fillers. Standard residence and work permits are processed through employer applications.

**Q: What education or skills are required for shelf fillers in Croatia?**
A: Formal education is not required for shelf fillers in Croatia. Employers typically provide on-the-job training. Key skills include physical stamina, attention to detail, and basic numeracy. Bilingualism (Croatian and English) is an advantage. Career progression may lead to supervisor or stock manager roles with experience.
