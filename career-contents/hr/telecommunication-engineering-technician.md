## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians in Croatia ranges from 1,200 to 1,500 EUR, depending on experience, location, and employer. Net salary is typically around 900 to 1,100 EUR. Senior technicians or those in Zagreb may earn higher.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Telecommunications Engineering Technicians to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work permit requires a labor market test. Both require relevant qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Telecommunications Engineering Technician in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a secondary vocational education in telecommunications or electrical engineering is required, or a bachelor's degree in telecommunications engineering. Practical experience with networking, fiber optics, and wireless systems is valued. Certification from the Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Industries (HAKOM) may be advantageous.
