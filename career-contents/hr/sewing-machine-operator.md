## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for a sewing machine operator in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sewing machine operators in Croatia is between €800 and €1,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,200. Salaries are typically higher in the Zagreb region and in larger manufacturing companies.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU sewing machine operator to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as sewing machine operators in Croatia via a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation, as it targets highly qualified workers with at least a bachelor's degree. The employer must demonstrate a labor market need and obtain approval from the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions and tasks for a sewing machine operator in Croatia?**
A: Sewing machine operators in Croatia work in textile and garment factories, often in production lines. Tasks include operating industrial sewing machines, stitching fabric pieces, and inspecting finished products. Work is typically full-time, with shift work common. The job requires attention to detail, manual dexterity, and the ability to meet production quotas. Most operators receive on-the-job training.
