## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a croupier in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for croupiers in Croatia ranges from HRK 5,500 to HRK 8,500 (approx. EUR 730–1,130), depending on experience and casino location. Tips, especially in tourist areas like Dubrovnik or Zagreb, can significantly boost total earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as croupiers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable for croupiers, as it requires a higher education degree or equivalent. Non-EU nationals must instead obtain a standard work and residence permit. The employer must apply for a work permit after a labor market test, demonstrating no suitable local candidate.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a croupier in Croatia?**
A: A high school diploma is the minimum requirement. Most casinos provide on-the-job training, but prior completion of a croupier school is advantageous. Key skills include fluency in English, strong numeracy, and manual dexterity. A clean criminal record is mandatory, and knowledge of Croatian is a plus.
