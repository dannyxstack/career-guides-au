## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmaceutical technicians in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for pharmaceutical technicians in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior technicians in pharmacies or pharmaceutical companies may earn up to €1,800. Salaries are higher in larger cities like Zagreb, Split, and Rijeka.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign pharmaceutical technicians to work in Croatia?**
A: Foreign pharmaceutical technicians can work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card (for non-EU nationals with a recognized diploma and a job offer) or a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad). Both require employer sponsorship. The Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold, while the standard permit is based on labor market tests. EU/EEA nationals have free movement.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pharmaceutical technician in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, a pharmaceutical technician typically requires a secondary school diploma in pharmacy (four-year program) or a related field, followed by passing a professional exam (stručni ispit) for licensure. Some positions may prefer a bachelor's degree in pharmacy technology. Registration with the Croatian Chamber of Pharmacists is mandatory for practice.
