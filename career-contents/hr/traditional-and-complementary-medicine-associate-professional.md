## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Croatia?**
A: There is no publicly available official salary data specific to this occupation in Croatia. Many professionals work on a fee-for-service basis, so income can fluctuate.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Croatia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card with a valid job offer. However, they must have qualifications recognized by the Croatian Ministry of Health or relevant professional body, and any required licences or registrations. The employer applies for a work permit; the Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold.

**Q: How is the profession of Traditional and Complementary Medicine regulated in Croatia?**
A: Croatia regulates certain complementary medicine practices, such as acupuncture and homeopathy, which require registration with the Croatian Chamber of Health Professionals. Other modalities may be unregulated but must comply with general business regulations. The field is evolving, and practitioners should verify current requirements with the relevant authorities.
