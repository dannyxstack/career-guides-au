## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for debt collectors in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, debt collectors typically earn a gross monthly salary between €800 and €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Larger cities like Zagreb may offer higher pay. Commissions or bonuses based on recovery rates can increase total earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU debt collectors obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a salary at least 1.5 times the Croatian average gross salary. Debt collector roles typically do not meet this threshold, so standard work permit procedures apply via the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for debt collectors in Croatia?**
A: Debt collectors in Croatia can advance to senior collector, team leader, or manager roles with experience and performance. Additional training in negotiation or finance may lead to positions in credit management or collections strategy within banks or specialized agencies.
