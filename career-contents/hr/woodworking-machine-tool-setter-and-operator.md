- Automatic programming and path optimization for CNC machines

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to optimise cutting paths to reduce waste
- Overlay real-time operational guides through augmented reality

**Human moat:**

- Real-time decision-making ability to handle natural defects in wood.
- Non-standard process adjustments for custom furniture
- On-site repair experience for mechanical faults in machine tools

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a woodworking machine tool setter or operator in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for woodworking machine tool setters and operators in Croatia typically range from €1,200 to €1,600 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and company size. Overtime and shift work may increase earnings. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced operators in larger firms can earn more.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a woodworking machine tool setter or operator in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment in Croatia. The job must require a higher education qualification or relevant professional experience. The salary must meet the threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia). A valid job offer and residence permit application are required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a woodworking machine tool setter or operator in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a completed secondary vocational education in woodworking or a related field is required. Some employers accept equivalent experience. On-the-job training is common. Certifications in CNC operation or safety standards can be advantageous. Knowledge of Croatian is usually necessary for communication in the workplace.
