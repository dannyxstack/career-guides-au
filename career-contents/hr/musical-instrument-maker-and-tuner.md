## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for musical instrument makers and tuners in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for musical instrument makers and tuners in Croatia vary based on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may earn around €700–€900 per month, while experienced professionals can expect €1,200–€1,500 monthly. Freelance tuners or craftspeople with a reputation may charge higher rates. These figures are gross amounts before taxes.

**Q: Can non-EU musical instrument makers work in Croatia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for a work permit based on a job offer from a Croatian employer. For the EU Blue Card, they must hold a higher education diploma (or five years of relevant experience) and meet a salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, currently around €1,350/month). The employer must demonstrate the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What training is available for musical instrument makers in Croatia?**
A: Formal training is offered at vocational schools like the School of Applied Arts and Design in Zagreb, or through apprenticeships with master craftsmen. The University of Zagreb's Academy of Music also offers courses in instrument maintenance. Since the field is niche, practical experience and specialized workshops are highly valued. Licensing is not mandatory, but certification can improve job prospects.
