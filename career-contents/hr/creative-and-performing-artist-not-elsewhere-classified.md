## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for creative and performing artists in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary widely. Freelance artists typically earn €800–€1,500 per month, while those employed in cultural institutions may earn €1,200–€2,000. Income is project-based and supplemented by grants, commissions, and performance fees.

**Q: Can foreign creative and performing artists work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card applies to highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5x average gross salary). Artists may alternatively obtain a temporary residence permit for work based on an employment contract or a self-employment permit.

**Q: What are the main employers for creative and performing artists in Croatia?**
A: Artists commonly work freelance or for cultural institutions such as theaters, orchestras, galleries, and museums. The tourism sector also provides seasonal opportunities in entertainment, festivals, and events, particularly along the Adriatic coast.
