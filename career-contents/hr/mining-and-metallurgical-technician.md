- Manual drafting of mine design and construction drawings

- Manual monitoring of equipment status and energy consumption
- On-site Inspections for Hazard Identification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI for 3D modeling of ore deposits and resource assessment
- Optimizing autonomous truck routes and blasting plans using AI
- Using machine learning to predict rock mass stability and support requirements
- Integrating digital twin technology for real-time monitoring and adjustment of mining operations
- Quickly parse geological reports and compliance documents using natural language processing

**Human moat:**

- Decision-making under complex geological conditions
- Communication and coordination with government/community/Indigenous peoples

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mining and metallurgical technician in Croatia?**
A: According to local job portals, salaries for mining and metallurgical technicians in Croatia typically range from €1,200 to €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior technicians can earn above this range.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for a mining and metallurgical technician moving to Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia through a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and employer demonstration of labor market need. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers, provided the job meets the minimum salary threshold (set annually by the government) and the applicant has relevant qualifications. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for a mining and metallurgical technician in Croatia?**
A: Mining and metallurgical technicians in Croatia assist engineers in extracting and processing minerals and metals. Duties include operating equipment, monitoring production, performing quality control, and ensuring safety. Career progression may lead to senior technician, shift supervisor, or specialized roles in process optimization. Relevant experience and certifications enhance advancement opportunities.
