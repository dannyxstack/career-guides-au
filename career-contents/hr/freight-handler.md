- Repetitive lifting and stacking in warehouses

- Use manual forklifts for short-distance transfers

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI scheduling systems to optimize material handling routes and sequences
- Operate automated equipment (e.g., AGVs, robotic arms) for loading and unloading
- Receive and feedback cargo information via smart terminals

**Human moat:**

- Handling non-standard, irregular, or fragile goods
- Flexibly adapt to complex environments (e.g., temporary space adjustments)
- Monitor and intervene in automation system anomalies
- Assume safety responsibilities and implement emergency plans

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a freight handler in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a freight handler in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced workers in major ports or logistics hubs may earn up to €1,500. Overtime and night shifts can increase pay.

**Q: How can a non-EU citizen obtain a work visa to work as a freight handler in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work and residence permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) based on a job offer. The employer must prove there are no suitable EU candidates (labor market test). The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree; freight handlers typically do not qualify. The permit is issued for up to one year and renewable.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a freight handler in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a secondary school diploma (four-year or three-year vocational program) in logistics or a related field is preferred. On-the-job training is common. Additional certifications in forklift operation, warehouse safety, or hazardous materials handling improve job prospects. Physical fitness and basic computer skills are also beneficial.
