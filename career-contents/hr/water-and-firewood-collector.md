## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in Croatia?**
A: Pay for water and firewood collectors in Croatia is generally low and often informal. Workers may earn around the minimum wage (€700-800 gross per month in 2024) or be paid per task, such as per cubic meter of firewood. Actual earnings vary by region and employer, with many workers in rural areas paid in cash.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card as a water and firewood collector in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a standard Croatian work permit if an employer sponsors them and passes a labor market test. However, the EU Blue Card is not available for this low-skilled occupation, as it requires a higher education degree and a high-skilled job. Seasonal work permits may be an alternative for short-term stays.

**Q: What are the working conditions for water and firewood collectors in Croatia?**
A: Work is physically demanding, often outdoors in rural areas, and may involve carrying heavy loads. Hours can be irregular, with peak seasons in autumn and winter. Many workers are self-employed or employed informally. Safety risks include injury from tools or terrain, and access to social benefits is limited for informal workers.
