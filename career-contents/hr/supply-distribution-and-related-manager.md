## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for supply and distribution managers in Croatia vary by experience and company size. Entry-level positions start around €1,200-€1,500 gross per month, while experienced managers can earn €2,500-€4,000+. Senior roles in larger firms may exceed €5,000. Bonuses and benefits are often included.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU citizens to work as a supply/distribution manager in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or a national work permit. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer, higher education qualifications, and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Alternatively, a standard work permit is employer-sponsored and quota-dependent. Both routes require employer application and approval.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for supply and distribution managers in Croatia?**
A: Supply and distribution managers in Croatia can advance to senior management roles like Director of Supply Chain or Operations Director. Further training in logistics or certification (e.g., APICS) enhances prospects. The growing e-commerce sector offers additional opportunities.
