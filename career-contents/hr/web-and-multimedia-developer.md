## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for web and multimedia developers in Croatia?**
A: Entry-level web developers earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross monthly, while experienced developers can earn €2,000–€3,500 gross. Salaries vary by company size, location (Zagreb higher), and specialization (e.g., front-end vs full-stack). Many companies also offer bonuses and remote work options.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card options for web developers moving to Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit, often employer-sponsored. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer, higher education (or 5+ years experience), and a salary at least 1.5x the national average gross salary (~€1,900/month in 2023). Alternative: seasonal or temporary residence permits for digital nomads are also available.

**Q: What are the most in-demand skills for web developers in Croatia?**
A: Croatian employers seek expertise in JavaScript (React, Angular, Vue), PHP (Laravel), Python, and CMS like WordPress. Full-stack capabilities, cloud (AWS/Azure), and DevOps knowledge boost employability. Soft skills like English proficiency and remote collaboration are also highly valued due to Croatia's growing IT outsourcing sector.
