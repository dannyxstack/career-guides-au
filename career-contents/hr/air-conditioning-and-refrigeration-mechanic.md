## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics typically earn a gross monthly salary between €1,000 and €1,500, depending on experience, qualifications, and location. Entry-level workers may start around €800, while experienced professionals in larger cities like Zagreb can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and specializations can increase earnings.

**Q: How can a foreign worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation in Croatia?**
A: Foreign workers need a work permit or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a recognized diploma (or equivalent experience), a binding job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €1,500/month in 2023), and valid health insurance. Employers must obtain a labor market test. Work permits are processed via the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What are the career prospects for Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics in Croatia?**
A: Career prospects are positive due to growing demand in construction, commercial buildings, and industrial cooling. Mechanics can advance to team leader, service manager, or start their own business. Specializations in heat pumps, solar cooling, or ammonia systems offer higher pay. Continuous training is valued, and certifications from manufacturers improve job opportunities.
