## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for cleaners and helpers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for cleaners and helpers in Croatia usually range from the national minimum wage (around €700 gross per month in 2024) to about €900 gross, depending on employer, location, and experience. Overtime and night shifts may offer additional pay. Some employers provide meal allowances or transportation subsidies.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as cleaners in Croatia through an EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Cleaners and helpers typically do not qualify for an EU Blue Card, as that scheme targets high-skilled workers. Non-EU nationals can obtain a work permit and residence permit for employment if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. The process involves a labor market test and meeting specific criteria set by the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What are the main duties of a cleaner in Croatian hotels and offices?**
A: Duties include sweeping, mopping, vacuuming floors, cleaning windows, dusting furniture, emptying trash bins, and sanitizing restrooms. In hotels, they may also change linens and restock amenities. Some establishments require use of industrial cleaning equipment. Attention to detail and ability to follow safety protocols are important.
