## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships’ deck crew in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and vessel type. Entry-level deck ratings earn around €800–€1,200 net per month, while able seamen with experience can earn €1,500–€2,000. Additional overtime and bonuses may apply, and income from international voyages can be tax-free.

**Q: How can foreign deck crew obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit or EU Blue Card. For the Blue Card, you require a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. 1.5× average wage). Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for lower-skilled roles, requiring an employment contract with a Croatian shipping company and relevant seafarer certifications.

**Q: What certifications are required to work as deck crew on Croatian ships?**
A: Deck crew must hold STCW (Standards of Training, Certification, and Watchkeeping) certificates, including basic safety training. Higher ranks require additional endorsements. Croatian-flagged ships also require recognition of foreign certificates by the Ministry of the Sea. Valid medical fitness and seafarer's book are mandatory.
