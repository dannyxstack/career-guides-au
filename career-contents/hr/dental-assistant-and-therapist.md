## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a dental assistant in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for dental assistants in Croatia ranges from 6,500 to 8,500 HRK (approximately €860 to €1,130), depending on experience, location, and whether they work in public or private clinics. Overtime and performance bonuses may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign dental assistant to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work and residence permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) arranged by an employer. The EU Blue Card is available for those with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (around 1.5 times the national average gross salary). EU/EEA nationals have free movement and can work without a permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a dental assistant in Croatia?**
A: To work as a dental assistant in Croatia, you must complete a four-year secondary vocational program in dental assisting or a related field, pass a state professional exam, and register with the Croatian Chamber of Dental Medicine. Continuing education is required for license renewal.
