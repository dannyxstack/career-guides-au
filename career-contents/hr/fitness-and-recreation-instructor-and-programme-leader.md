## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fitness instructors and recreation programme leaders in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary by location and employer. In Zagreb, fitness instructors typically earn between €800 and €1,200 gross per month, while senior programme leaders may earn up to €1,500. Private studios and luxury hotels often offer higher pay. Rates for self-employed instructors are usually higher but depend on client base.

**Q: Can a non-EU fitness instructor work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU fitness instructors may qualify for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia (approx. €1,800 monthly in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit can be obtained through an employer, who must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a fitness instructor in Croatia?**
A: There is no single mandatory certification, but most employers require a recognized fitness instructor diploma (e.g., from the Croatian Fitness Association) or an international certification like ACE, NASM, or ISSA. A relevant university degree in kinesiology is highly regarded. First aid certification is often required, and registration with the Croatian Employers' Association is advisable.
