## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Office Supervisor in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, the gross monthly salary for an Office Supervisor typically ranges from 1,200 to 1,500 EUR, depending on experience, company size, and location. Entry-level positions start around 1,000 EUR, while senior supervisors can earn up to 1,800 EUR. These figures are based on industry data and may vary.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as an Office Supervisor in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card for an Office Supervisor position in Croatia if they have a valid job offer, a higher education degree (or equivalent experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Detailed requirements are available from the Croatian Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities of an Office Supervisor in Croatia?**
A: Office Supervisors in Croatia oversee administrative staff, manage office workflows, coordinate meetings, handle correspondence, and ensure efficient office operations. They may also be responsible for budgeting, procurement, and compliance with local labor laws. Strong organizational and communication skills are essential, as well as proficiency in Croatian and often English.
