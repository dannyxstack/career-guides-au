## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hunters and trappers in Croatia?**
A: Hunters and trappers in Croatia typically earn between 800 and 1,200 EUR net per month, though income can vary based on seasonality, game abundance, and employment type. Many work part-time or on a contract basis for hunting associations or private landowners, and some earn additional income from selling game meat or trophies.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as hunters and trappers in Croatia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals generally cannot work as hunters or trappers via the EU Blue Card, as this scheme is reserved for highly qualified workers with a university degree or five years of professional experience. Instead, they would need a standard work permit, which requires a Croatian employer to prove no suitable domestic worker is available. Self-employment is also possible but subject to strict conditions.

**Q: What licenses or qualifications are required to become a hunter in Croatia?**
A: To become a hunter in Croatia, one must pass a hunting exam and obtain a valid hunting license from the Ministry of Agriculture. Additionally, aspiring hunters must join a registered hunting association or secure permission from a hunting ground owner. Non-residents can obtain a temporary hunting permit for guided hunts without taking the full exam.
