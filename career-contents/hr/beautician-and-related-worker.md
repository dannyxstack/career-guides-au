## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Croatia?**
A: The average net monthly salary for beauticians in Croatia typically ranges between 5,500 and 7,500 HRK (approx. 730–1,000 EUR), depending on experience, location, and employer. Earnings can increase with a loyal client base, additional services, or working in upscale salons in larger cities like Zagreb, Split, or Rijeka.

**Q: Can beauticians from non-EU countries work in Croatia via an EU Blue Card or other visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for beauticians as it targets highly skilled workers with higher education degrees. Non-EU citizens must obtain a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable EU candidate is available. After one year of legal work, you may apply for a temporary residence permit.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a beautician in Croatia?**
A: To work as a beautician in Croatia, you typically need a completed secondary vocational education in cosmetology or a related field, plus a recognized certificate. Many practitioners also complete additional courses in specialized treatments. The Croatian Chamber of Trades and Crafts (HOK) oversees licensing. EU qualifications are recognized, while non-EU ones may require equivalency assessment.
