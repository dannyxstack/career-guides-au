## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an ambulance worker in Croatia?**
A: Salary varies by experience, region, and employer. Entry-level ambulance workers earn around 6,500–8,500 HRK net per month (~€860–€1,130). Those with advanced certifications or in urban areas may earn up to 10,000 HRK net. Public sector positions often include benefits.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for ambulance workers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit obtained by an employer, which involves a labor market test. Alternatively, if you have a recognized higher education qualification, you may qualify for the EU Blue Card, though ambulance roles typically require vocational training. The work permit is initially temporary and renewable.

**Q: What training is required to become an ambulance worker in Croatia?**
A: Ambulance workers must complete a secondary medical or paramedic program, followed by licensing exams. The Croatian Institute of Emergency Medicine oversees training. Continuous education is mandatory. For foreign qualifications, validation through the Agency for Science and Higher Education is needed.
