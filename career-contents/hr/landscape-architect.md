## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Landscape Architect in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Landscape Architect in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while senior professionals can earn up to €2,500. Salaries are typically higher in cities like Zagreb, Split, and Rijeka.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign Landscape Architects to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU Landscape Architects can work in Croatia via a work permit based on a labor market test, or the EU Blue Card (if earning at least 1.5x the average gross salary). For both, a valid employment contract and recognition of qualifications (via the Croatian Employment Service) are required. EU citizens need only registration of residence.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Landscape Architect in Croatia?**
A: To practice, you need a recognized master's degree in landscape architecture (e.g., from University of Zagreb Faculty of Agriculture). After graduation, completion of a professional internship and passing the state professional exam (under the Croatian Chamber of Architects) is required for licensure. Continuous professional development is also mandatory.
