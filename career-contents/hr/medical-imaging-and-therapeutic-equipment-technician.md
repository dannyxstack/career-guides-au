## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a medical imaging technician in Croatia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians in Croatia typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Public hospitals may offer additional benefits and shift allowances. Experienced professionals in private clinics can earn up to €2,200.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU medical imaging technicians to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU technicians can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid employment contract and meet salary thresholds. Alternatively, a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) can be obtained through employer sponsorship. Both require recognition of your qualifications by the Croatian Ministry of Health and registration with the competent chamber.

**Q: What education and licensing are required to work as a medical imaging technician in Croatia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in radiology technology or a related field from an accredited institution is required. Graduates must pass a state professional exam (stručni ispit) and register with the Croatian Chamber of Health Professionals. Continuing education is mandatory to maintain licensure.
