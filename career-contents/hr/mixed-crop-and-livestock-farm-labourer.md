## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Croatia?**
A: The minimum gross monthly wage in Croatia is around €700 (as of 2024), and farm labourers often earn at this level or slightly more, depending on experience, region, and farm type. Overtime and seasonality may affect total pay.

**Q: Can third-country nationals work as mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold of at least 1.5 times the average gross wage. For farm labourers, third-country nationals can obtain a standard work and residence permit subject to a labor market test.

**Q: What are the main duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Croatia?**
A: Duties include planting, harvesting, feeding livestock, cleaning animal housing, operating basic farm machinery, and maintaining equipment. Tasks vary seasonally, with a focus on crop rotation and animal care typical of mixed farms in Croatia.
