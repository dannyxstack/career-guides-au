## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a Telecommunications Engineer in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for Telecommunications Engineers in Croatia typically range from €1,500 to €2,800 gross per month for mid-level positions, depending on experience and company size. Senior roles or specialists may earn up to €3,500 gross. Entry-level positions start around €1,200 gross. These figures are indicative and can vary by region and sector.

**Q: How can a non-EU Telecommunications Engineer obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Non-EU engineers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work and residence permit is available if the employer demonstrates that the position could not be filled locally. The employer initiates the process at the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are most in demand for Telecommunications Engineers in Croatia?**
A: Employers seek a bachelor's or master's degree in electrical engineering, telecommunications, or related fields. Key skills include knowledge of network protocols (TCP/IP, LTE, 5G), experience with network design and optimization, and familiarity with Cisco or Huawei equipment. Proficiency in English is essential, and Croatian language skills are advantageous for local integration.
