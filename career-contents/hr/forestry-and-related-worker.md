## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for forestry workers in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for forestry workers in Croatia ranges from €900 to €1,300, depending on experience and region. Entry-level positions start around €800, while experienced workers in state forests or private companies may earn up to €1,500. Overtime and seasonal bonuses can increase earnings.

**Q: How can foreign forestry workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Non-EU forestry workers need an employment permit, typically obtained by an employer through the Croatian Employment Service. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; forestry workers generally do not qualify as they are not considered highly skilled. Instead, a standard work permit for seasonal or permanent employment is required.

**Q: What are the main job duties of forestry workers in Croatia?**
A: Forestry workers in Croatia perform tasks like planting, thinning, and harvesting trees; maintaining forest roads; controlling pests; and preventing wildfires. They operate chainsaws, tractors, and other machinery. Work often occurs in mountainous or remote areas, requiring physical fitness. Employment can be with the state forest company Hrvatske šume or private logging firms.
