## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwife in Croatia?**
A: Midwives in Croatia earn an average gross monthly salary of €1,300 to €1,800, depending on experience and sector. Public healthcare salaries are set by collective agreement; private practice may offer higher pay. Additional benefits include shift allowances and overtime.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for midwives in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU midwives can work in Croatia via a work permit or EU Blue Card. Requirements include a valid employment contract, recognized qualifications (diploma approval by the Croatian Health Ministry), and meeting Croatian language proficiency (usually B2). The employer must perform a labor market test. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold.

**Q: What are the career prospects for midwives in Croatia?**
A: Midwives are in demand in Croatia due to an aging workforce and shortages in rural areas. Career advancement includes specialization in clinical midwifery, management roles, or teaching positions. Continuing professional development is mandatory for license renewal. Public hospitals and health centers are primary employers.
