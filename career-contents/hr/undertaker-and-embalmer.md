## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for undertakers and embalmers in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for undertakers and embalmers in Croatia ranges from 800 to 1,200 EUR, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage, while experienced professionals in larger cities can earn higher. Overtime and additional services like embalming can increase overall pay.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for non-EU nationals to work as an undertaker or embalmer in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a valid job offer from a Croatian employer to apply for a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary (currently around 2,100 EUR). This salary threshold may be challenging for this profession, so the standard work permit route is more common.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an undertaker or embalmer in Croatia?**
A: To work as an undertaker in Croatia, you need completed secondary education and a specialized vocational course in funeral services, followed by a professional exam. Registration with the Croatian Chamber of Funeral Services is mandatory. For embalming, additional training and certification in mortuary science are required. Continuing education is recommended to stay updated on regulations.
