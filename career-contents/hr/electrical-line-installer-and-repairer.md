## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for electrical line installers and repairers in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, electrical line installers and repairers earn a net monthly salary between HRK 7,000 and HRK 10,000 (approximately €930–€1,330). Pay varies with experience, certifications, and employer. Overtime and hazardous work supplements are common in this field.

**Q: Can non-EU electrical line installers work in Croatia via an EU Blue Card or other permit?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized higher professional qualification and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times Croatia's average gross salary. Alternatively, employers can sponsor a standard work and residence permit through the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an electrical line installer in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a vocational secondary school diploma in electrical engineering (4 years) is required, followed by an apprenticeship. Some positions demand additional certifications for high-voltage work. Major employers include Hrvatska elektroprivreda (HEP) and private electrical contracting firms.
