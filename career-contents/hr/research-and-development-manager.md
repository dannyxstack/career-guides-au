## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Research and Development Managers in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, Research and Development Managers typically earn between HRK 20,000 and HRK 35,000 gross per month. Salaries vary by industry, company size, and experience. Senior roles in multinational firms or high-tech sectors may exceed this range. Benefits often include bonuses and allowances.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Research and Development Managers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia via an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified jobs) or a standard work permit. The Blue Card requires a valid employment contract, higher education, and salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a work permit application is employer-sponsored. Both routes involve approval from the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications or experience are needed to become a Research and Development Manager in Croatia?**
A: Most employers require a master's or PhD in a relevant technical field (e.g., engineering, natural sciences) plus 5-10 years of R&D experience. Demonstrated leadership in project management, knowledge of EU research funding, and fluency in English are crucial. Croatian language skills are an advantage but not mandatory for international firms.
