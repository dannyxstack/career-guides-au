## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for gardeners in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, region, and employer. Seasonal workers may earn on an hourly basis, typically around €5–€7 per hour. Salaries in tourist-heavy coastal areas might be higher.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU gardeners to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU gardeners can work in Croatia via employer-sponsored work permits or the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers meeting salary thresholds). Seasonal work permits for up to 6 months are also available. Self-employment registration is possible but requires a valid business permit.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a gardener in Croatia?**
A: While formal qualifications are not mandatory, a vocational certificate in horticulture, landscaping, or agriculture is advantageous. Practical experience and knowledge of local plant species are highly valued. Basic Croatian language skills are often required for client interaction.
