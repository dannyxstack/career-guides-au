## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand packer in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, hand packers typically earn the statutory minimum wage, which in 2024 is approximately €700 gross per month. Some employers may offer slightly higher rates for overtime or specialized tasks. Wages vary by region and company size.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as hand packers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals seeking to work as hand packers in Croatia generally require a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Instead, employers must apply for a standard work permit through the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for hand packers in Croatia?**
A: Hand packers in Croatia can advance to supervisory roles like packing line supervisor or quality control inspector with experience. Further training in logistics or warehouse management may lead to positions such as warehouse manager. Some workers transition to machine operation or logistics coordination with additional certifications.
