## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for companions and valets in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Croatia vary by employer and region, but the average monthly gross salary ranges from €800 to €1,200. For private care of elderly or disabled individuals, rates may be higher. Social security contributions and taxes apply, so net pay is typically 20-30% lower.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as companions or valets in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers with higher education and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, which companions/valets rarely meet. Non-EU nationals may instead apply for a standard work permit (dozvola za rad) if sponsored by an employer, but this occupation may not qualify under shortage lists. Always check with Croatian embassy for current rules.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to work as a companion or valet in Croatia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but employers often prefer a high school diploma and experience in caregiving, hospitality, or personal assistance. Certificates in first aid, elderly care, or language skills (Croatian or English) improve prospects. Some private agencies provide training, and a clean criminal record is usually required.
