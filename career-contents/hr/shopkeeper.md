## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for shopkeepers in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly net salary for shopkeepers in Croatia typically ranges between €800 and €1,200, depending on location, shop size, and experience. In major cities like Zagreb or Split, salaries may be higher. Additional income may come from performance bonuses.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for shopkeepers moving to Croatia?**
A: Non-EU shopkeepers need a work and residence permit, which their employer must apply for. For highly qualified roles, the EU Blue Card is available if you have a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold. Self-employed individuals require a business visa and registration with Croatian authorities.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shopkeepers in Croatia?**
A: Experienced shopkeepers can advance to store manager, area supervisor, or open their own business. Many attend retail management courses. In Croatia, speaking Croatian is crucial for customer-facing roles. With experience, some move into wholesale or franchise ownership.
