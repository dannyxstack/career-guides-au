## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bicycle repairer in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, bicycle repairers typically earn a net monthly salary between €800 and €1,200, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €800, while skilled mechanics in Zagreb or tourist areas can earn up to €1,500. Overtime and specializations (e.g., e-bike repair) may increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a bicycle repairer to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as bicycle repairers via a standard work and residence permit, which requires a job offer and a positive labor market test. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable for this occupation as it demands a higher education degree. Self-employment is possible with a special permit after registering a business.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a bicycle repairer in Croatia?**
A: Formal training includes completing a secondary vocational program in bicycle mechanics or related fields. Alternatively, on-the-job learning with a certified mechanic is common. Certifications from manufacturers (e.g., Shimano, Bosch for e-bikes) enhance career prospects. No mandatory license, but experience is highly valued.
