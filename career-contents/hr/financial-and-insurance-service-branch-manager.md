## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Financial and Insurance Services Branch Manager in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for this role in Croatia ranges between €2,500 and €4,000, depending on experience, company size, and location (higher in Zagreb). Bonuses and benefits such as company car or health insurance are common. Salaries are typically higher in insurance than in other financial services.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for a non-EU Financial and Insurance Services Branch Manager to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia via a standard work permit (tied to a specific employer) or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a university degree or 5+ years of professional experience, and a job offer meeting the national salary threshold (typically 1.5x the average gross salary). Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Croatian authorities.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become a Financial and Insurance Services Branch Manager in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, or business is required, along with 5–10 years of experience in banking, insurance, or financial services. Leadership skills and knowledge of Croatian financial regulations (HANFA) are essential. Professional certifications such as CFA or insurance intermediary licenses are highly valued.
