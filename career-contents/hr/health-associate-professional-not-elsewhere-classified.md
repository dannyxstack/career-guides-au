## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Health Associate Professionals not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: Entry-level salaries range from €800 to €1,200 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €1,500. Public healthcare sector salaries may differ slightly and are set by collective agreements. Earnings vary by specialization, employer, and region.

**Q: Can Health Associate Professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Croatia?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for a standard work permit (dozvola za rad) requiring employer sponsorship and a labour market test. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified professionals meeting a salary threshold (currently around €2,100 gross monthly). Both routes require a valid job offer.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for Health Associate Professionals in Croatia?**
A: Professionals can advance through specialization programs (e.g., becoming a radiologic technologist or physiotherapist) or by pursuing higher education to qualify as a health professional. Supervisory roles in clinics or hospitals are also possible. Career growth is regulated by the Ministry of Health and professional chambers.
