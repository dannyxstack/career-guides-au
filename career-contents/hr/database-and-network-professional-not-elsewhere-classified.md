## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for database and network professionals in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for database and network professionals in Croatia vary with experience and company size. Mid-level roles typically earn between €1,200 and €2,500 net per month. Senior specialists or those in managerial positions may earn up to €3,500 net monthly. The IT sector in Zagreb and coastal cities tends to offer higher salaries.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU database professionals to work in Croatia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Croatia if they have a valid job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary, a recognized higher education degree or equivalent experience, and a work contract lasting at least one year. The Blue Card grants residence and work rights, including family reunification.

**Q: What certifications are beneficial for database professionals in Croatia?**
A: Croatian employers value certifications from Oracle (OCP), Microsoft (MCSA/MCSE), and AWS (Solutions Architect). Additionally, proficiency in Croatian or English is important, as many international companies operate in English. Local IT associations like HrOpen also offer networking opportunities and skill development.
