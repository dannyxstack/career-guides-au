## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Croatia?**
A: Hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Croatia typically earn around the minimum wage, approximately €4.50–€5.50 per hour (gross) in 2024. For full-time work (40 hours/week), monthly net earnings range from €800 to €1,000. Earnings may be higher in tourist-heavy seasons or with tips, especially for pedicab or delivery cyclists.

**Q: What are the work visa requirements for non-EU citizens to work as hand or pedal vehicle drivers in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and a residence permit for employment. The employer must apply for a labor market test to show no suitable Croatian/EU worker is available. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as this occupation does not require higher qualifications. Seasonal work permits (up to 90 days) may be an option for short-term roles.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Croatia?**
A: Work is often seasonal and concentrated in tourist areas like Dubrovnik, Split, or Zagreb. Drivers may be employed by delivery companies, bike taxi services, or work as independent contractors. Physical fitness is essential due to long hours cycling or pedaling. Work schedules vary; pay may be per delivery, hourly, or with tips. Accidents and weather exposure are common risks.
