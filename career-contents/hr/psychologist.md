## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly salary for psychologists in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €2,000 gross, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and location. Senior roles or specialized fields like clinical psychology may earn higher, up to €2,500 gross. Salaries are generally lower in the public sector.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for psychologists moving to Croatia?**
A: Non-EU psychologists can work in Croatia via a standard work permit, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card applies for highly qualified workers with a degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Both routes require recognition of qualifications by the Croatian Psychological Chamber.

**Q: What is the process for licensing as a psychologist in Croatia?**
A: To practice as a psychologist in Croatia, you must register with the Croatian Psychological Chamber (Hrvatska psihološka komora). This requires a recognized master's degree in psychology, completion of a one-year supervised internship, and passing a professional exam. EU qualifications may be recognized under Directive 2005/36/EC.
