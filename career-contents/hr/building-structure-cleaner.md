## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for building structure cleaners in Croatia generally align with the national minimum wage, which is around 700 euros gross per month. Experienced cleaners may earn slightly more, but wages vary by employer and contract terms.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as building structure cleaners in Croatia through a work visa?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as building structure cleaners in Croatia via a standard work permit, as the role does not qualify for the EU Blue Card. The employer must apply to the Croatian Employment Service, demonstrating that no suitable local worker is available.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a building structure cleaner in Croatia?**
A: No formal qualifications are mandatory, but employers often require physical fitness and reliability. For tasks like high-rise window cleaning, a safety course or experience at heights may be needed. A clean criminal record is sometimes requested.
