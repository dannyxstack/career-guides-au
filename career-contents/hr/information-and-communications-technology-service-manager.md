## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Services Managers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for ICT Services Managers in Croatia vary widely depending on experience, company size, and industry. They are generally among the highest in the country, often exceeding the national average gross salary. For accurate, up-to-date figures, consult official sources such as the Croatian Bureau of Statistics or specialized salary surveys from recruitment agencies.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for ICT Services Managers moving to Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card, requiring a valid employment contract, higher professional qualifications (e.g., a university degree), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work permit is available, which the employer must apply for through the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What key skills are essential for ICT Services Managers in Croatia?**
A: Successful ICT Services Managers in Croatia need strong leadership, strategic planning, and project management abilities. Proficiency in English and Croatian is crucial, along with knowledge of EU data protection regulations (GDPR). Technical expertise in areas like cybersecurity or cloud computing, plus experience in cross-cultural team management, are highly valued.
