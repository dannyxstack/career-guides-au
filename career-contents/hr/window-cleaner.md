## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a window cleaner in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly net salary for window cleaners in Croatia ranges from approximately 700 to 900 euros, depending on experience and location. Many work on minimum wage (around 700 EUR net). Overtime and tips may increase income. Employers in larger cities may offer higher pay.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for window cleaners to work in Croatia?**
A: Window cleaners typically do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires higher education or highly skilled status. Instead, non-EU nationals need a standard work permit sponsored by an employer. The employer must demonstrate that no local worker is available. After obtaining a work permit, apply for a temporary residence permit at the Croatian embassy. Annual quotas for foreign workers may apply.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for window cleaners in Croatia?**
A: Experienced window cleaners may advance to supervisory roles or start their own cleaning business. Additional certifications in safety equipment or handling hazardous materials can enhance prospects. Some specialize in high-rise building cleaning, which requires additional training and can lead to higher pay. Language skills (Croatian) are important for client interaction.
