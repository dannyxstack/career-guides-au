## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a sports coach in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly salary for sports coaches in Croatia ranges from €1,000 to €1,800 gross, depending on experience, sport, and employer. Public sector coaches may earn less, while those in professional clubs or private academies can earn higher. Additional income from bonuses or private lessons is common.

**Q: What are the visa requirements for foreign sports coaches to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU sports coaches need a work permit and temporary residence permit, or an EU Blue Card if meeting salary and qualification criteria. The employer must obtain a labor market test. Coaches from EU/EEA countries can work freely. Specific quotas may apply. Official routes are via the Croatian Employment Service or diplomatic missions.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a sports coach in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a degree in kinesiology, sports science, or physical education is required. Many sports also require a national coaching license from the Croatian Olympic Committee or respective sports federation. Experience and first aid certification are often recommended. For school coaching, a teaching qualification may be needed.
