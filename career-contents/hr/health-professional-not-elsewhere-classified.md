## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specialization, experience, and employer (public vs. private sector). Entry-level positions typically earn around 1,000–1,200 EUR net per month, while experienced professionals can earn 1,500–2,000 EUR net. Specialists or those in managerial roles may earn more.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for foreign health professionals in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for a standard work and residence permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) with a job offer, or an EU Blue Card if the gross annual salary is at least 1.5 times the national average. Both require employer sponsorship, health insurance, and recognition of professional qualifications by relevant Croatian authorities.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: Typical employers include public hospitals, private clinics, rehabilitation centers, research institutes, and public health agencies. Roles may involve specialized clinical practice, health management, public health projects, or medical research. Career advancement often depends on further specialization and experience.
