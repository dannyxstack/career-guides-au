## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an administrative secretary in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for administrative and executive secretaries in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles in multinational companies can exceed €2,000. Salaries in Zagreb are typically higher than in other regions.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as administrative secretaries in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work in Croatia as administrative secretaries through the EU Blue Card if they have a higher professional qualification (typically a bachelor's degree) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary). Alternatively, they may obtain a standard work and residence permit for employment, which requires a labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an administrative secretary in Croatia?**
A: Most employers require at least a high school diploma, but a bachelor's degree in business administration or a related field is increasingly preferred. Proficiency in English and Croatian, plus computer skills (Microsoft Office), are essential. Knowledge of additional languages and experience in a specific industry can enhance job prospects.
