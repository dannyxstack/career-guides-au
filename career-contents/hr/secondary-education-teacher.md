## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for secondary education teachers in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for secondary education teachers in Croatia ranges from approximately €1,300 to €1,800, depending on experience, qualifications, and location. Salaries are set by collective agreements and increase with seniority. Additional pay may come from extracurricular activities or school-specific bonuses.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a secondary education teacher in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as secondary teachers in Croatia through the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (at least 1.5 times average gross salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible after a labor market test. Recognition of teaching credentials by the Croatian Ministry of Science and Education is mandatory.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a secondary education teacher in Croatia?**
A: To teach at secondary level in Croatia, one typically needs a master's degree in the subject area plus a completed pedagogical-psychological-didactic education program (often part of the degree). Teachers must also pass the professional examination (stručni ispit) after a year of induction. Registration with the Croatian Chamber of Teachers is required for state schools.
