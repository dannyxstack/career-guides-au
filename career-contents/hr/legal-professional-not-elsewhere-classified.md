## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for legal professionals not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary ranges from €1,200 to €2,500, depending on experience, sector, and location. Private practice and corporate legal departments tend to offer higher pay, while public sector roles are generally lower.

**Q: Can non-EU legal professionals work in Croatia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if you hold a recognized higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for positions not meeting Blue Card criteria.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a legal professional in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in law (magistar prava) from a recognized university is required. Many roles also necessitate passing the Croatian bar exam. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the competent authority.
