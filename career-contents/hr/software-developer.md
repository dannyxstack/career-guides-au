## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a software developer in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a software developer in Croatia ranges from €1,500 for juniors to over €3,500 for seniors, with median around €2,200. Salaries are higher in Zagreb and for specialized roles like AI or cloud. Net pay is lower after taxes and contributions.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU software developers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU software developers can obtain a standard work and residence permit based on a job offer, requiring employer proof of labor market need. Alternatively, they may qualify for an EU Blue Card if holding a higher education degree and meeting the salary threshold (currently around 1.5x average gross wage). EU Blue Card holders benefit from simplified family reunification and intra-EU mobility.

**Q: What is the current demand for software developers in Croatia?**
A: Demand for software developers in Croatia remains very high, especially for front-end, back-end, mobile, and DevOps roles. The IT sector is growing rapidly with many startups and multinational companies. Proficiency in English is essential, and remote work opportunities are common. The shortage of skilled developers gives candidates strong negotiation power.
