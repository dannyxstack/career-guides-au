## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CAD drafting and 2D drawing generation
- Standard finite element analysis (FEA) and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations
- Generate Standard BOMs and Technical Documentation
- Routine tolerance analysis and dimensional inspection
- Predictive maintenance planning based on historical data

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-driven generative design (e.g., topology optimization)
- Machine learning-based failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)
- Digital twin real-time simulation and optimization
- Smart materials selection and supply chain optimization
- Write automated test and verification scripts

**Human moat:**

- Cross-system physical intuition and innovative design
- Complex nonlinear problem diagnosis and on-site debugging
- Ethical and responsible decision-making for safety-critical systems
- Unstructured communication with clients and suppliers
- Multidisciplinary integration and system architecture decision-making

**Skills to build:**

- AI-enhanced design tools (e.g., Autodesk Fusion 360 generative design)
- Python/R for data analysis and automation scripts
- Digital Twin and IoT Integration Knowledge
- Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing) Design Optimization

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a mechanical engineer in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a mechanical engineer in Croatia ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior engineers with over 10 years of experience can earn €3,000 or more. Major employers include the automotive, energy, and manufacturing sectors.

**Q: Can a foreign mechanical engineer work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, a foreign mechanical engineer can apply for an EU Blue Card in Croatia if they have a valid employment contract with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average national salary. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. The Blue Card allows work and residence, with a path to permanent residency after 33 months of legal stay in the EU.

**Q: What are the career prospects for mechanical engineers in Croatia?**
A: Mechanical engineers in Croatia find opportunities in automotive, shipbuilding, energy, and manufacturing industries. Major companies like Rimac Automobili, Končar, and Brodogradilište Uljanik offer roles in design, production, and maintenance. With EU integration, mobility is enhanced. Continuing education in CAD/CAM or renewable energy can improve prospects. Professional certification through Croatian Chamber of Engineers is recommended.
