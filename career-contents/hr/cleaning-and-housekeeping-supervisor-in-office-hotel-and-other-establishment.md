## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, gross monthly salaries for cleaning and housekeeping supervisors typically range between 700 and 1,100 EUR, depending on experience, location, and type of establishment (e.g., hotels, offices). Entry-level positions may start near minimum wage, while experienced supervisors in upscale hotels can earn higher. Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Croatia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia via a standard employment permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers (typically requiring a higher education degree and higher salary threshold). As cleaning supervisory roles usually do not meet these criteria, the regular work permit is the relevant route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisor in Croatia?**
A: Formal education is not always required, but a high school diploma is common. Employers often prefer candidates with prior cleaning or housekeeping experience and supervisory skills. Vocational courses in hospitality or facility management can be advantageous. On-the-job training is typical, and knowledge of cleaning chemicals and equipment is useful.
