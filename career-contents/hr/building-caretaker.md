## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Croatia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for building caretakers in Croatia typically ranges from 700 to 900 EUR, depending on location, employer, and experience. This often aligns with or slightly exceeds the national minimum wage. Net pay may be lower after taxes and social contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as building caretakers in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers with higher education qualifications and a salary threshold. For building caretakers, non-EU nationals must obtain a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) based on a labor market test, requiring employer sponsorship and proof that no local candidate is available.

**Q: What are the main duties and requirements for building caretakers in Croatia?**
A: Building caretakers in Croatia perform cleaning, minor repairs, and maintenance of common areas, as well as monitoring access and waste disposal. No formal education is required, but basic knowledge of Croatian language and practical skills are essential. Prior experience in property maintenance is advantageous.
