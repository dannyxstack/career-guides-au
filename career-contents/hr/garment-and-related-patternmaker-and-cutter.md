## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for garment and related patternmakers and cutters in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for garment patternmakers and cutters in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced professionals in larger manufacturing hubs like Zagreb can earn up to €1,500.

**Q: Can foreign garment patternmakers and cutters obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Yes, foreign nationals can apply for a Croatian work permit or the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer. The Blue Card requires a higher education qualification or five years of relevant professional experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for other qualified applicants.

**Q: What are the main career prospects for garment patternmakers and cutters in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, garment patternmakers and cutters find employment in textile and clothing manufacturers, fashion studios, and tailoring shops. With experience, they can advance to supervisory roles, become production managers, or specialize in custom tailoring. The industry is concentrated in Zagreb, Varaždin, and Čakovec. Digitalization and automation are increasing demand for patternmakers skilled with CAD software.
