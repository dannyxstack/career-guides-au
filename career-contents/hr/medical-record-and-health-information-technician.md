## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Croatia typically range from HRK 6,000 to HRK 10,000 gross per month. Entry-level positions start around HRK 5,500, while experienced technicians in hospitals or larger clinics may earn up to HRK 12,000. Actual pay depends on education, years of experience, and employer.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as Medical Records and Health Information Technicians in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card in Croatia for highly qualified employment. This requires a valid job offer, a higher education qualification (e.g., bachelor's degree in health information management), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work permit and temporary residence permit are also possible routes. No fabricated numbers are provided; consult official sources for exact thresholds.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Medical Records and Health Information Technician in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, most employers require at least a secondary school diploma in healthcare or administration, but a bachelor's degree in health information management or a related field is increasingly preferred. Professional certification (e.g., from the Croatian Medical Informatics Association) and fluency in Croatian are also essential. On-the-job training in electronic health record systems is common.
