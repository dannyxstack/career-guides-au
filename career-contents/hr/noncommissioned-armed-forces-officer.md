## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for non-commissioned officers in the Croatian Armed Forces?**
A: Salaries are determined by rank and years of service, with additional allowances for special duties and deployments. Exact figures are classified, but typical net monthly pay ranges from €800 to €1,500, with higher ranks and longer service receiving more.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals become non-commissioned officers in Croatia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No. The Croatian Armed Forces only employ Croatian citizens. Non-EU nationals cannot apply for non-commissioned officer positions via work visa or EU Blue Card. To join, one must first obtain Croatian citizenship through naturalization, which generally requires at least 8 years of residence.

**Q: What is the career progression for non-commissioned officers in Croatia?**
A: NCOs start at the rank of Corporal and can advance through Sergeant, Staff Sergeant, Sergeant First Class, Master Sergeant, and Sergeant Major. Promotions depend on time in service, performance evaluations, and completion of mandatory courses at the Croatian Military Academy or specialized schools.
