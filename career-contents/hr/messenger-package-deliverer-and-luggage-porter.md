## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers and package deliverers in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for messengers and package deliverers in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200. Many earn the minimum wage (€700 gross as of 2025) or slightly above. Luggage porters in tourism hubs may receive tips, boosting total earnings. Salaries vary by employer, location, and full-time vs. part-time status.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a messenger or package deliverer in Croatia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in this occupation via a standard work visa if a Croatian employer sponsors them and passes the labour market test. The EU Blue Card is not applicable, as it requires a higher education degree and a higher salary threshold (typically €1.5x the average salary). This occupation generally does not meet those criteria.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for messengers and deliverers in Croatia?**
A: Career progression is limited but possible. Workers can advance to team leader or dispatcher roles in larger courier companies. Self-employed couriers may expand their client base. Additional qualifications like a heavy goods vehicle license or logistics certification can lead to jobs in transport and warehousing, which offer higher pay and responsibility.
