## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for domestic cleaners and helpers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for domestic cleaners and helpers in Croatia vary by employer and region but generally range from the minimum wage (approximately €700 gross per month in 2025) to around €1,000 gross. Many positions are part-time or paid hourly, often at rates between €4 and €6 net per hour. Overtime and tips can supplement income.

**Q: Can foreign workers obtain an EU Blue Card or work visa for domestic cleaner jobs in Croatia?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for domestic cleaners as it requires a higher education qualification and a high-skilled job. However, foreign workers can obtain a work permit and temporary residence for employment in Croatia if their employer demonstrates a labor market need. This requires a valid employment contract and meeting legal requirements set by the Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What are the typical duties and working conditions for domestic cleaners in Croatia?**
A: Domestic cleaners in Croatia perform tasks such as sweeping, mopping, dusting, vacuuming, cleaning kitchens and bathrooms, and taking out trash. They may work in private homes, hotels, or offices. Working conditions vary; many work part-time or irregular hours, including weekends. Physical stamina and attention to detail are important, and some employers provide cleaning supplies.
