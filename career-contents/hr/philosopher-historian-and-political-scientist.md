## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary by sector. In academia, entry-level assistant professors earn around €1,300–€1,600 gross monthly, while senior professors may earn €2,000–€2,500. Public administration roles offer salaries comparable to the national average (approx. €1,200 gross). Private sector positions like policy analysis may pay higher but are limited. Overall, these professions generally align with national averages for university-educated workers.

**Q: What are the official work-visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU citizens to work as philosophers, historians, or political scientists in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens require a valid work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (currently around €1,650 per month). Alternatively, a standard work permit can be obtained if the employer proves no suitable local candidate is available. Both routes require a higher education degree and a binding employment contract.

**Q: What are typical career paths for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Croatia?**
A: Common paths include academia (universities, research institutes), public administration, journalism, NGOs, and cultural institutions. Teaching at secondary schools is possible with additional pedagogical qualifications. Competition for academic positions is high, leading many to pursue interdisciplinary roles in policy analysis, consulting, or archival work. Career progression often depends on specialized expertise and networking within relevant sectors.
