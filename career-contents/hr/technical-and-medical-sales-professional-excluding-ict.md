## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, the gross monthly salary for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals typically ranges from 1,300 to 2,800 EUR, depending on experience and industry. Entry-level positions start around 1,100 EUR, while senior roles may exceed 3,000 EUR. Commission and bonuses are common, often adding 10–30% to base pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card options for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Croatia via a work and residence permit, requiring a job offer and a labor market test. For the EU Blue Card, applicants need a valid employment contract with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the Croatian average (approx. 1,950 EUR in 2025) and a recognized higher education degree. Both routes are administered by the Ministry of Interior and local police.

**Q: What key skills and career progression opportunities exist for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Croatia?**
A: Essential skills include strong technical knowledge of medical devices or industrial equipment, fluency in English (often German or Italian), and advanced negotiation abilities. Career progression typically moves from Junior Sales Representative to Key Account Manager, then to Sales Manager or Regional Director. Continuous learning in digital sales tools and market trends is highly valued.
