## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bus and tram drivers in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, bus and tram drivers earn a gross monthly salary typically ranging from 1,000 to 1,400 EUR, depending on experience and employer. Additional allowances may apply for night shifts or overtime. Net pay after taxes and contributions is usually between 800 and 1,100 EUR.

**Q: What is the work visa process for non-EU citizens to become bus or tram drivers in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens must obtain a work and residence permit through their employer. The standard procedure requires a labor market test by the Croatian Employment Service. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as this occupation does not require a university degree. The employer sponsors the permit, which is issued for a specific job and employer.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a bus or tram driver in Croatia?**
A: You need a valid driving license for the respective vehicle (D category for buses), a professional competence certificate (CPC), and medical fitness. Training is provided by authorized driving schools. Additionally, fluency in Croatian is essential for communication with passengers and understanding traffic regulations.
