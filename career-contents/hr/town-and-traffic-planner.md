## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a town and traffic planner in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions often start around €1,200 gross per month, while senior planners in public administration or private firms can earn €2,500 or more. Bonuses and allowances may apply. The average gross salary in Croatia as of 2024 is approximately €1,300, so planners generally earn above average.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card process for non-EU town planners to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit or EU Blue Card. For the Blue Card, you require a valid job offer, a recognized university degree (or five years of professional experience), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. The employer sponsors the application. Croatian citizens do not need a visa.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a town planner in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in spatial planning, architecture, civil engineering, or related field is required. Registration with the Croatian Chamber of Architects and Civil Engineers (HKAIG) may be necessary for certain roles. Work experience in urban development projects is highly valued. Continuous professional development is encouraged.
