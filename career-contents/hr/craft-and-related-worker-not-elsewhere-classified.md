## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for craft and related workers in Croatia?**
A: According to the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, the average monthly net salary for craft and related workers varies by sector but typically ranges between €800 and €1,500. Earnings depend on experience, specialization, and region, with higher pay in tourism-heavy areas like the coast.

**Q: Can non-EU craft workers obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU craft workers rarely qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree or equivalent for highly skilled occupations. Instead, they typically apply for a standard work permit based on a labor market test. Croatia also issues seasonal work permits for temporary positions, especially in tourism-related crafts.

**Q: What types of jobs are included in this occupational category in Croatia?**
A: ISCO 7549 covers manual craft trades not listed elsewhere, such as musical instrument makers, boat builders, and watchmakers. In Croatia, these crafts are often found in small family-run workshops, cultural tourism, and restoration of historical artifacts. The tourism industry also provides opportunities for specialized crafts like lace-making or pottery.
