## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Inquiry Clerk in Croatia?**
A: The monthly net salary for an Inquiry Clerk in Croatia typically ranges from €700 to €1,200, depending on experience, location (higher in Zagreb), and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior clerks with language skills can earn more.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as an Inquiry Clerk in Croatia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals need an employer-sponsored work permit to work as an Inquiry Clerk in Croatia. The EU Blue Card is unlikely for this role as it requires a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the Croatian average gross salary (approx. €2,000+ monthly). Most Inquiry Clerk positions do not meet this threshold.

**Q: What are the main duties of an Inquiry Clerk in Croatia?**
A: Inquiry Clerks in Croatia handle customer requests via phone, email, or in person. They provide information about products or services, process orders or complaints, and maintain records. Fluency in Croatian is essential, and English is often required for international clients.
