- Basic subtitling/transcription proofreading

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Real-time captioning assisting interpretation (AI providing background information)

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for translators and interpreters in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on language pair, specialization, and experience. Entry-level translators typically earn around €800–€1,200 gross per month. Experienced conference interpreters can earn €2,000–€3,000 or more. Freelance rates range from €0.05–€0.15 per word for translation and €200–€500 per day for interpretation. Note that salaries in Croatia are subject to income tax and social contributions.

**Q: Can non-EU translators and interpreters obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, a higher education qualification, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Croatia. Alternatively, a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) is available through employer sponsorship. Both routes require meeting specific conditions set by the Croatian Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a translator or interpreter in Croatia?**
A: While no formal license is legally required, most employers prefer candidates with a bachelor's or master's degree in translation, interpreting, linguistics, or a related field. Professional certifications (e.g., from the Croatian Association of Translators) and proven language proficiency (C2 level) are highly valued. Specialization in legal, medical, or technical domains can enhance career prospects.
