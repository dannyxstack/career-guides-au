## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for door-to-door salespersons in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary widely as compensation is often commission-based. The minimum gross monthly wage in Croatia is around €700, but door-to-door salespersons may earn between €500 and €1,500 gross per month, depending on sales performance and the employer's pay structure. Some positions offer a base salary plus commission.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as door-to-door salespersons in Croatia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and higher salary threshold, which this occupation may not meet. A standard work permit is more common, requiring a labor market test unless the occupation is on the shortage list.

**Q: What qualifications or skills are needed to become a door-to-door salesperson in Croatia?**
A: No formal qualifications are legally required, but employers often prefer strong communication skills, persistence, and a good sales record. Fluency in Croatian is essential. Some companies provide training. Experience in sales or customer service is advantageous. The role may suit those seeking flexible hours or commission-based income.
