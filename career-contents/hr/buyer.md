## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Buyer in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Buyer in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Entry-level positions start around €900, while senior buyers can earn up to €2,500. Salaries are higher in Zagreb and for roles in international trade or manufacturing.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for foreign Buyers moving to Croatia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work as Buyers in Croatia through a work permit based on a labor market test or via the EU Blue Card if they have a university degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Residence permits are issued for one year and renewable. No specific quotas for buyers exist.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are needed to become a Buyer in Croatia?**
A: Employers typically require a bachelor's degree in economics, business, or supply chain management. Relevant experience in procurement or logistics is highly valued. Fluency in English and Croatian is often essential, and knowledge of ERP systems like SAP improves prospects. Professional certifications (e.g., CIPS) can boost career advancement.
