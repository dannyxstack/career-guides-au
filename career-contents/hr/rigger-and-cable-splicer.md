## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for riggers and cable splicers in Croatia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries typically range from 900 to 1,500 EUR, depending on experience, specialization, and employer. Entry-level positions start around 750–900 EUR, while experienced senior riggers or splicers with certifications can earn over 1,500 EUR. Overtime and project-based work may increase total earnings.

**Q: Can riggers and cable splicers from non-EU countries obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for an EU Blue Card or a standard work permit. The Blue Card requires a valid employment contract, qualifications (e.g., vocational diploma or equivalent), and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia. Alternatively, employers can sponsor a work permit under specific shortage occupations, which may include rigging or cable splicing.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a rigger or cable splicer in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a vocational qualification in electrical engineering, telecommunications, or mechanics is preferred. Practical training or an apprenticeship is common. For cable splicers, knowledge of fiber optics and safety standards is essential. Riggers often need certifications in rigging and lifting operations. Licensing from the Croatian employers or trade associations may be required.
