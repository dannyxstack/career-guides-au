## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical secretary in Croatia?**
A: Medical secretaries in Croatia earn between €700 and €1,200 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. Public sector salaries are often lower but include benefits, while private clinics may pay slightly more. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced secretaries can reach €1,200.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU medical secretaries in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as medical secretaries in Croatia via a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, though the latter typically requires higher education. The employer must prove no local candidate is available. After five years of legal residence, permanent residence may be possible.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a medical secretary in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a secondary school diploma in healthcare administration or a related field is required. Many employers prefer candidates with a bachelor's degree in health management or business. Proficiency in English and basic medical terminology are essential, while knowledge of Croatian medical software is an advantage.
