## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Electronics Engineering Technician in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for an Electronics Engineering Technician (ISCO 3114) in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions start around €900, while senior technicians can earn up to €2,200. Salaries are typically higher in Zagreb and in the IT or manufacturing sectors.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for a non-EU Electronics Engineering Technician to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a standard work permit. The EU Blue Card requires a valid employment contract, higher education or 5+ years of experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. Standard work permits are employer-sponsored and subject to a labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an Electronics Engineering Technician in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a secondary vocational diploma in electronics or electrical engineering is required, often followed by a professional bachelor's degree (stručni prvostupnik) in electronic engineering. Practical experience or apprenticeships are valued. Registration with the Croatian Chamber of Engineers may be needed for certain roles, especially those involving public infrastructure.
