## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for fashion models in Croatia?**
A: Model salaries in Croatia vary widely. Average monthly earnings range from HRK 5,000 to HRK 15,000 (approx. €660–€2,000), depending on experience, project type, and agency. Top models may earn significantly more. Most models work freelance or on short-term contracts.

**Q: Do fashion models need a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA models require a work permit and temporary residence permit for employment to work legally in Croatia. The EU Blue Card is intended for highly qualified workers and is not typically applicable for modeling roles. The standard route involves an employer sponsoring the work permit.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a fashion model in Croatia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not required. Key requirements include appropriate physical attributes, a strong portfolio, and confidence. Many aspiring models attend modeling schools or workshops to improve skills. Networking with reputable agencies in Zagreb or Split is essential, and English fluency is advantageous.
