## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an aircraft engine mechanic in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for aircraft engine mechanics in Croatia ranges from €1,400 to €2,200, depending on experience, employer, and certifications. Senior mechanics or those with specialized EASA Part-66 licenses can earn higher. Salaries in major cities like Zagreb or near airports tend to be at the upper end.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign aircraft engine mechanics to work in Croatia?**
A: Foreign nationals can work as aircraft engine mechanics in Croatia via the EU Blue Card for highly skilled workers, requiring a valid job offer with salary meeting the national threshold. Alternatively, a standard work and residence permit can be obtained through employer sponsorship, provided the applicant meets qualification and labor market test requirements.

**Q: What training and certification are required to become an aircraft engine mechanic in Croatia?**
A: To work as an aircraft engine mechanic in Croatia, you typically need an EASA Part-66 Aircraft Maintenance License (AML) in category B1 (turbine or piston engines). Training involves completing an approved Part-147 maintenance training course and accumulating practical experience. Croatian regulations require recognition of foreign licenses through the Croatian Civil Aviation Agency.
