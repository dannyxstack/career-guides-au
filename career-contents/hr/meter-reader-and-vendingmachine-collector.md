## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly salary for this occupation in Croatia ranges from HRK 5,000 to 6,500 (approximately €660–€860). Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced workers in certain regions or companies can earn somewhat more. Salaries are typically higher in larger cities like Zagreb.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as a meter reader or vending-machine collector in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a standard work permit through employer sponsorship and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because this occupation does not require the higher education or high salary threshold. The employer must prove no local candidate is available.

**Q: What are the main duties of meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Croatia?**
A: Meter readers visit homes and businesses to record consumption from gas, water, or electricity meters, and report discrepancies. Vending-machine collectors empty cash boxes, restock products, and perform basic maintenance. Both roles require physical stamina, attention to detail, and reliable transportation.
