## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for managing directors and chief executives in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for top executives in Croatia vary significantly by industry and company size. In 2023, gross monthly salaries for managing directors ranged from approximately €3,500 to over €10,000, with average annual earnings around €60,000–€120,000. Bonuses and profit-sharing often form a substantial part of total compensation.

**Q: Can foreign nationals work as managing directors in Croatia under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: Yes, highly qualified non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Croatia, provided they have a valid employment contract or binding job offer for a highly qualified position, including senior management roles. The salary must be at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Croatia. Other routes include work permits based on a local employer's sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are typically required for managing director roles in Croatia?**
A: Most managing director positions in Croatia require extensive senior management experience, often 10+ years, with a proven track record in strategic leadership. A university degree in business, economics, or a relevant field is common, and an MBA is highly valued. Industry-specific expertise and fluency in Croatian and English are often essential.
