## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary depending on the catch volume and vessel type, but deep-sea fishery workers in Croatia typically earn between €800 and €1,200 net per month. Income can be higher during peak fishing seasons or on larger vessels, and share-based compensation models are common.

**Q: What is the work-visa route for non-EU deep-sea fishery workers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU workers need a work and residence permit sponsored by a Croatian employer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as deep-sea fishery work is not considered highly skilled. Employers must demonstrate a labor market test and obtain a work permit quota allocation. Seasonal work permits for up to 6 months are also available.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities and working conditions for deep-sea fishery workers in Croatia?**
A: Workers operate fishing vessels in the Adriatic Sea, handling nets, lines, and catches, and maintaining equipment. Shifts can be long and physically demanding, often requiring extended periods at sea. Basic seamanship skills and a valid medical certificate are required; experience is preferred but on-the-job training is common.
