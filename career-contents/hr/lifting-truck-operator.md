## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for lifting truck operators in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for lifting truck operators in Croatia ranges from €1,000 to €1,300, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced operators can earn up to €1,500. Overtime and night shifts may increase total earnings. Salaries are typically paid 12 times per year, with mandatory social contributions deducted.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as lifting truck operators in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a Croatian work permit for lifting truck operator roles. The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable as it requires a higher education degree, which this occupation does not demand. Instead, employers must apply for a standard work permit through the Croatian Employment Service, proving no suitable EU candidate is available. Long-stay visa (Type D) follows for entry.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a lifting truck operator in Croatia?**
A: A valid forklift or lifting truck operator license is required, typically obtained after a training course approved by the Croatian Ministry of Labour. Basic Croatian language skills are often demanded for safety communication. Employers may also require a medical certificate and a clean driving record. On-the-job training is common, but prior experience is preferred.
