- Automated monitoring and adjustment of kiln temperature and time

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for potters in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly net salary for potters in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Skilled artisans working in specialized studios or tourism-related crafts may earn higher rates. Salaries in larger cities like Zagreb or coastal tourist hubs are typically at the higher end.

**Q: How can a foreign potter obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Croatia?**
A: A foreign potter needs a job offer from a Croatian employer, who then applies for a work permit on their behalf. For the EU Blue Card, the job must require higher qualifications and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage. Applications are processed by the Ministry of the Interior (MUP).

**Q: What are the main employment opportunities for potters in Croatia?**
A: Potters in Croatia can find work in craft workshops, ceramic studios, and pottery factories. Many are self-employed, selling directly at markets or to tourism businesses. Opportunities also exist in restoration of historic ceramics and teaching workshops. The tourism sector on the coast and islands provides a steady demand for handmade pottery.
