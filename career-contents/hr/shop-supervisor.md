## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shop supervisors in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, location, and company size. In Croatia, shop supervisors typically earn above the national average but less than store managers. Collective bargaining agreements in retail set minimum wage levels. For current estimates, consult official statistics or job advertisements.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU citizens to work as a shop supervisor in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit obtained by their employer, which requires a labor market test. The EU Blue Card may be an option if the applicant has a higher education degree and meets salary thresholds, but shop supervisor roles typically do not qualify. Seasonal work permits for up to 9 months are also available. Official routes are via the Croatian Employment Service.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a shop supervisor in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a secondary education is required, with preference for vocational training in retail or business. Relevant experience is crucial. Many employers offer internal training. Fluency in Croatian is essential. Additional certifications in sales or customer service can be advantageous. Continuous professional development is encouraged through industry associations.
