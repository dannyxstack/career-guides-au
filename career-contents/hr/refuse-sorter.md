## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for refuse sorters in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for refuse sorters in Croatia ranges from approximately €700 to €900, depending on employer and region. Minimum wage laws apply, but many workers earn slightly above minimum. Overtime and shift differentials may increase pay. Salaries are typically lower than the national average.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as refuse sorters in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers. Refuse sorters (ISCO 9612) are low-skilled occupations. Non-EU nationals can obtain a standard work permit if the employer proves no local worker is available. The process includes a labor market test and applying for a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What are the main duties of a refuse sorter in Croatia?**
A: Refuse sorters in Croatia manually separate recyclable materials such as paper, plastic, glass, metal, and organics at sorting facilities or landfills. Duties include identifying and sorting waste, operating basic machinery, and maintaining cleanliness. The work is physically demanding and often in outdoor or industrial settings. No formal education is required; on-the-job training is provided.
