- Repeated on-site demonstrations of product standard functions
- Using pre-recorded videos or AI-generated content to replace on-site explanations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer data to assist in customizing personalized presentation scripts
- Real-time speech transcription and translation, supporting multilingual audiences

- Sales data dashboard helps presenters adjust strategies in real time

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sales demonstrator in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a sales demonstrator in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €700, while experienced demonstrators can earn up to €1,400. Commissions and bonuses are often additional.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a sales demonstrator in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Croatia as sales demonstrators through a standard work permit, requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is available for higher-skilled roles with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average; sales demonstrator positions typically do not meet this threshold, so the standard work permit route is more common.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for a sales demonstrator in Croatia?**
A: Sales demonstrators in Croatia demonstrate products at retail stores, trade shows, or events, engaging customers to increase sales. Duties include setting up displays, explaining features, and handling inquiries. Career progression may lead to roles like sales representative, brand ambassador, or team leader, with opportunities in retail, consumer goods, and promotional agencies.
