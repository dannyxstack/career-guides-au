## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Croatia?**
A: The average net monthly salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Croatia is approximately 950 EUR, according to official statistics. However, earnings can vary significantly depending on hours worked, tips, and employer. Taxi drivers often earn more in tourist areas, while van drivers may have fixed salaries.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a driver in Croatia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as drivers in Croatia via a standard work permit, which requires employer sponsorship and a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because it requires a higher education degree and a salary threshold higher than typical driver earnings. The employer must prove no suitable Croatian or EU candidate is available.

**Q: What licenses or certifications are required to become a taxi or van driver in Croatia?**
A: To drive a taxi, you need a valid B category driving license, a clean criminal record, and a taxi driver license issued by the local city authority. Van drivers require a B license, and for vehicles over 3.5 tons, a C1 or C license plus a Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC) is mandatory.
