## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, salaries for this role vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around HRK 7,000–9,000 monthly, while experienced inspectors in public health institutions or private companies earn HRK 10,000–15,000. Senior roles or those with specialized certifications may exceed HRK 18,000. These figures are gross amounts before taxes.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for foreign Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors seeking employment in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (approx. 1.5x average gross salary) and relevant qualifications. Alternatively, a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) can be obtained through an employer. EU/EEA citizens do not require a visa but must register residence if staying over 90 days.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in public health, environmental engineering, occupational safety, or related fields is required. Additional certifications from the Croatian Institute of Public Health or Ministry of Health may be necessary. Practical experience through internships or prior work in health inspection is highly valued. Proficiency in Croatian is essential for regulatory roles.
