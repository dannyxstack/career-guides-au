- Routine ordering and checkout (using self-service kiosks and mobile payments)

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Personalized customer service and advice (AI-assisted analysis of customer preferences)

- Inventory management and supply chain optimization (AI demand forecasting)
- Customer Relationship Management and Loyalty Programs (AI-driven CRM Systems)

**Human moat:**

- Complex emotional interaction and empathy (handling drunk customers or special requests)
- Ability to handle emergencies (e.g., food allergies, medical emergencies).
- Teamwork and on-site coordination (supporting kitchen and front-of-house during peak periods)

- Enhances multilingual communication skills (especially English).

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for waiters in Croatia?**
A: The net monthly salary for waiters in Croatia ranges from approximately €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and establishment type. Tips can significantly increase total earnings, especially in tourist-heavy areas like Dubrovnik or Split.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as waiters in Croatia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not applicable for waiters as it requires a higher education degree or high-skilled qualification. Non-EU citizens can work as waiters in Croatia via a standard work permit, which requires an employer to pass a labor market test. The employer sponsors the permit.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for waiters in Croatia?**
A: Waiters in Croatia can progress to head waiter, restaurant supervisor, or manager. With specialized training, they may become sommeliers, bartenders, or event coordinators. Experience in high-end establishments or tourism can lead to roles in international hotels or cruise ships.
