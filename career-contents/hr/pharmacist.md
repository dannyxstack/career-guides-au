## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pharmacists in Croatia?**
A: The average net monthly salary for pharmacists in Croatia ranges from 1,200 to 1,500 EUR, depending on experience, location, and whether they work in a public pharmacy or hospital. Gross salaries typically range from 1,600 to 2,000 EUR. Experienced pharmacists or those in managerial roles may earn higher.

**Q: Can non-EU pharmacists work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU pharmacists can apply for an EU Blue Card to work in Croatia. Requirements include a valid employment contract with a salary meeting the legal threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) and recognition of their pharmacy degree by the Croatian Chamber of Pharmacists. The employer must also sponsor the application.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements to practice as a pharmacist in Croatia?**
A: To practice as a pharmacist in Croatia, you must complete a Master's degree in pharmacy (5 years) and pass the state professional exam. Additionally, you must register with the Croatian Chamber of Pharmacists. For EU graduates, qualifications are automatically recognized. Non-EU graduates need a recognition procedure from the Agency for Science and Higher Education.
