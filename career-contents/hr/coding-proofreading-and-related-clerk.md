## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average gross monthly salary for a coding, proofreading or related clerk in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for coding, proofreading and related clerks in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Salaries in Zagreb and larger companies tend to be higher. Entry-level positions start around €700, while experienced clerks can earn up to €1,500.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a non-EU national to work as a proofreader or clerk in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work permit and residence permit, sponsored by a Croatian employer. The EU Blue Card is not available for this occupation, as it is not considered highly skilled. Instead, apply for a standard work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad) through the employer. The process requires a job offer and meeting salary requirements set by Croatian law.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for coding and proofreading clerk roles in Croatia?**
A: Most employers require at least a high school diploma, with preference for candidates with a bachelor's degree in linguistics, languages, or a related field. Proficiency in multiple languages, especially English and German, is highly valued. Relevant experience in editing, data entry, or administrative support is also beneficial. Specific coding skills may be required for roles involving markup languages.
