## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for language teachers in Croatia?**
A: The salary for language teachers in Croatia varies by employer and experience. In public schools, the net monthly salary ranges from €800 to €1,200. Private language schools and international schools may offer €1,000 to €2,000 net, depending on qualifications and demand for specific languages.

**Q: Can non-EU language teachers work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card or other work permits?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as language teachers in Croatia through a standard work permit or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher-education degree and a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (approx. €25,000). Alternatively, employers can sponsor a regular temporary residence permit for work.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to teach languages in Croatia?**
A: To teach languages in Croatia, a bachelor's degree in the target language or education is typically required. Public schools require a pedagogical qualification (e.g., integrated BA+MA in teaching). Private institutions often accept a TEFL, CELTA, or equivalent certification, plus native-level fluency. Registration with the Croatian Employment Service may be necessary.
