## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a customs and border inspector in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for customs and border inspectors in Croatia are determined by public sector pay scales. According to the Croatian Ministry of Finance, the average gross monthly salary ranges from €1,500 to €2,000, depending on rank and years of service. Additional allowances may apply for shift work and hazardous duties.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a customs and border inspector in Croatia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Customs and border inspectors are typically civil servants requiring Croatian citizenship. Non-EU nationals may only work in such roles if they hold permanent residence or Croatian citizenship. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation due to public sector restrictions. Check with the Ministry of the Interior for rare exceptions.

**Q: What are the typical requirements to become a customs and border inspector in Croatia?**
A: Candidates must have completed at least secondary education, be Croatian citizens, and pass a rigorous selection process including physical fitness tests, psychological evaluation, and background checks. Relevant work experience or a degree in customs, law, or economics is advantageous. Training is provided by the Croatian Customs Administration.
