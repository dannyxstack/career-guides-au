## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for optometrists in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for optometrists in Croatia ranges from €1,200 to €2,000, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles or those in private practices can exceed €2,500. Salaries are often supplemented by bonuses and commission on sales of eyewear.

**Q: How can a non-EU optometrist obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Croatia?**
A: Non-EU optometrists can obtain a work visa via a job offer and work permit from the Croatian Employment Service. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a valid employment contract meeting the salary threshold (1.5 times the average gross salary). Recognition of professional qualifications by the Croatian Chamber of Health Professionals is required.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements for optometrists in Croatia?**
A: Optometrists must complete a recognized degree in optometry (typically a bachelor's or master's) and register with the Croatian Chamber of Health Professionals. Additional requirements include passing a professional exam, obtaining a license to practice, and continuous education credits. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the competent authority.
