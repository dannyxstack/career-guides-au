## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for subsistence livestock farmers in Croatia?**
A: Earnings for subsistence livestock farmers in Croatia are generally low and vary widely. In 2024, the average net monthly salary in agriculture is about €1,000, but subsistence farmers often earn less, sometimes below minimum wage, with income supplemented by own consumption of produce.

**Q: Can I obtain an EU Blue Card to work as a subsistence livestock farmer in Croatia?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree or equivalent experience. Subsistence livestock farming does not meet these criteria. Non-EU nationals may instead apply for a standard work permit sponsored by an employer or a seasonal work permit for temporary agricultural labor.

**Q: Are there any training programs or support for subsistence livestock farmers in Croatia?**
A: Yes, the Croatian Ministry of Agriculture offers advisory services and training through the Agricultural Advisory Service. Programs cover animal husbandry, EU funding for small farms, and sustainable practices. Local associations also provide workshops for improving productivity and market access.
