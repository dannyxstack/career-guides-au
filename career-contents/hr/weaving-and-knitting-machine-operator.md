## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Croatia ranges from €800 to €1,000, depending on experience, region, and employer. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings. Entry-level positions typically start lower, while experienced operators can earn higher wages.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for non-EU weaving and knitting machine operators in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a work permit to work as a weaving or knitting machine operator in Croatia. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it targets highly qualified workers. Instead, employers must apply for a standard work permit through the Croatian Employment Service. EU/EEA citizens can work without a visa.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for weaving and knitting machine operators in Croatia?**
A: With experience, operators can advance to senior machine operator, shift supervisor, or quality control inspector. Additional training can lead to roles in production management or textile engineering. Major employers include textile and carpet manufacturers in regions like Zagreb and Varaždin.
