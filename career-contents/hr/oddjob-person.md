## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for odd-job persons in Croatia?**
A: Odd-job persons in Croatia often earn the minimum gross monthly salary, which is approximately 700 EUR as of 2024. However, many are paid per task or hourly, with rates varying from 5 to 10 EUR per hour depending on the job complexity and location. Earnings can be irregular due to the nature of casual work.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as odd-job persons in Croatia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is intended for highly skilled workers and is not applicable to odd-job roles. Non-EU citizens can work in Croatia via a standard work and residence permit, which requires an employer sponsor and a labor market test. Seasonal work permits may be available for temporary odd-job positions.

**Q: What tasks do odd-job persons typically perform in Croatia?**
A: Odd-job persons in Croatia handle diverse manual tasks such as cleaning, home repairs, gardening, moving furniture, or assisting in construction sites. Work is often informal, part-time, or seasonal. No formal qualifications are required, but physical fitness and reliability are key for securing repeat assignments.
