**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign Services Managers NEC to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a work permit through the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers (requires a contract with salary at least 1.5x average gross salary, currently approx. €2,475/month). Alternatively, a standard work permit based on a job offer from a Croatian employer is possible, subject to a labor market test. The process involves employer application at the Croatian Employment Service.
