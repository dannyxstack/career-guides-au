## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Other Ranks in the Croatian Armed Forces?**
A: Monthly net salaries for other ranks range from approximately €800 for recruits to €1,200 for senior non-commissioned officers, depending on rank, tenure, and special allowances. Additional benefits include housing, food, and healthcare. Pay scales are set by the Ministry of Defence and may adjust annually.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work in the Croatian Armed Forces via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled non-EU workers in civilian professions requiring a university degree. It does not apply to military service. Non-EU citizens may only join the Croatian Armed Forces if they obtain Croatian citizenship. No other work visa route exists for foreign nationals to serve as regular military personnel.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Other Ranks in Croatia?**
A: Other ranks can advance through promotions to non-commissioned officer ranks (e.g., sergeant, staff sergeant) based on experience, exams, and performance. They may also specialize in technical fields or undergo officer training. The Croatian Armed Forces offer continuous professional development courses and opportunities for international missions.
