## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a glazier in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for glaziers in Croatia vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions typically earn around €700–€900 per month, while experienced glaziers can earn €1,200–€1,500 monthly. Overtime and specialized work may increase pay. These figures are gross amounts; net pay is lower after taxes and contributions.

**Q: Can a non-EU glazier work in Croatia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, glaziers may qualify for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for skilled workers without a degree. Applications require employer sponsorship and must comply with Croatia's labor market test.

**Q: What are the qualifications needed to become a glazier in Croatia?**
A: In Croatia, completing a secondary vocational school program in construction or glass-related trades is common. Alternatively, apprenticeships under a licensed glazier provide hands-on training. Certification from the Croatian Chamber of Trades and Crafts (HOK) may be required for independent work. Ongoing training for safety and new techniques is recommended.
