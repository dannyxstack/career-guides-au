## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for police inspectors and detectives in Croatia?**
A: The average monthly net salary for a police inspector in Croatia is approximately 1,200–1,600 EUR, depending on rank, experience, and location. Entry-level detectives earn around 1,000 EUR net, while senior inspectors can earn up to 2,000 EUR net. Additional allowances for overtime, shift work, and special duties may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a police inspector in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU citizens generally cannot work as police inspectors via standard work visas or the EU Blue Card because law enforcement positions typically require Croatian citizenship. The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly-skilled non-EU workers, but police roles are excluded. Alternative legal routes include obtaining permanent residence or citizenship through naturalization, which takes years.

**Q: What is the career progression for police detectives in Croatia?**
A: After graduating from the Police Academy, officers begin as patrol officers. To become a detective, they must pass competitive exams and gain field experience. Detectives can advance to senior detective, inspector, and chief inspector. Further specialization in criminal investigation, cybercrime, or forensics is possible. Promotions depend on performance, seniority, and additional training.
