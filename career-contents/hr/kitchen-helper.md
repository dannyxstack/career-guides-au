## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Croatia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a kitchen helper in Croatia ranges from HRK 5,500 to HRK 7,000 (approximately €730 to €930). Actual pay varies by region, employer, and experience. The statutory minimum wage is around HRK 4,500 gross.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a kitchen helper in Croatia through the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is reserved for highly skilled workers with a university degree or equivalent qualifications. Kitchen helpers typically do not meet these requirements. Non-EU nationals can work in this role via a standard work permit, which requires employer sponsorship and labour market testing.

**Q: What are the main duties and career prospects for a kitchen helper in Croatia?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Croatia assist with food preparation, cleaning utensils and workstations, and maintaining kitchen hygiene. Entry-level roles often lead to advancement to cook or chef after gaining experience and possibly completing vocational training. The hospitality sector offers steady demand, especially in tourist regions.
