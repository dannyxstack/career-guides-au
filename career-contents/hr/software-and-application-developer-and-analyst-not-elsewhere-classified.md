## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Croatia?**
A: Entry-level software developers earn around 8,000-12,000 HRK (€1,060-€1,590) gross monthly, while mid-level developers earn 12,000-18,000 HRK (€1,590-€2,390). Senior developers can earn 18,000-25,000+ HRK (€2,390-€3,320+). Salaries are higher in Zagreb and for specialized skills like AI or cybersecurity.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for software developers to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU software developers can work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card (requires a university degree or 5 years of experience, and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary) or a standard work/residence permit based on a labor market test. Both require employer sponsorship and valid health insurance.

**Q: What is the current demand for software developers in Croatia?**
A: Demand for software developers in Croatia is high, especially in Zagreb, Split, and Rijeka. Key sectors include IT outsourcing, fintech, and gaming. English proficiency is often required. The government offers tax incentives for IT professionals to attract foreign talent, and the tech industry is growing rapidly.
