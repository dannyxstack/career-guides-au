## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: Salaries vary by institution and experience. In public schools, teachers earn around €1,000-€1,500 gross monthly. Private institutions and international schools may offer €1,500-€2,500. Additional income from tutoring or project work is common. Cost of living in Zagreb is higher than in smaller towns.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU teaching professionals to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work permit and temporary residence permit. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified positions requiring higher education and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross salary. For teaching, the Croatian Employment Service must assess the labor market. Sponsored by employer.

**Q: What qualifications are required for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Croatia?**
A: Typically a degree in education or relevant field, plus pedagogical training. For higher education, a master's or doctorate is needed. Foreign teachers must have their diplomas recognized by the Agency for Science and Higher Education. Proficiency in Croatian is often required, except in international schools.
