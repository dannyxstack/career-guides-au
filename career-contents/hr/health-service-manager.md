## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Health Services Managers in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for health services managers in Croatia vary by experience, sector (public vs private), and location. According to official statistics, gross monthly salaries typically range between €1,500 and €2,500. Senior managers in private hospitals or specialized clinics may earn higher, while entry-level positions in public healthcare start lower.

**Q: Can non-EU Health Services managers work in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU health services managers can apply for an EU Blue Card in Croatia. Requirements include a valid employment contract (at least one year), higher professional qualifications, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Croatia (threshold adjusted annually). The application is submitted to the Croatian Ministry of Interior.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a Health Services Manager in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in healthcare management, public health, or a related field is required. Many managers also have clinical backgrounds (e.g., doctors or nurses) with additional management training. Relevant experience in healthcare administration is essential, and proficiency in Croatian is usually mandatory for patient-facing roles.
