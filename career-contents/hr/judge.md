## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a judge in Croatia?**
A: Judges in Croatia earn a gross monthly salary ranging from approximately 1,800 EUR for a basic court judge to over 3,500 EUR for Supreme Court justices. Salaries are set by law and include allowances for seniority and court level. Net income varies after deductions.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign judges in Croatia?**
A: Foreign nationals seeking to work as judges in Croatia must typically be EU/EEA citizens, as Croatian citizenship or EU citizenship is required for judicial office. Non-EU citizens may consider EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, but judgeships are public service positions with nationality restrictions. Alternatively, permanent residence or naturalization after five years of residency can lead to eligibility.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a judge in Croatia?**
A: To become a judge in Croatia, one must hold a law degree (Master's level), pass the bar exam, complete a 3-year traineeship as a judicial trainee, and pass the state judicial exam. After that, candidates apply for vacant positions through the State Judicial Council. Proficiency in Croatian and EU citizenship are required.
