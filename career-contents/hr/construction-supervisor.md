## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for construction supervisors in Croatia?**
A: Salaries for construction supervisors in Croatia typically range from €1,200 to €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, project size, and location. Senior supervisors or those on large infrastructure projects may earn up to €2,800. Net pay after taxes and contributions is approximately €900 to €1,500.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as construction supervisors in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, provided they have a valid work contract for at least one year and meet the salary threshold (typically 1.5 times the average gross salary in Croatia). Alternatively, a standard work permit (dozvola za rad) may be obtained if the employer sponsors a labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a construction supervisor in Croatia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in civil engineering, construction management, or a related field is required. Relevant work experience (3-5 years) is highly valued. Professional certifications in occupational safety (e.g., certified safety coordinator) are often mandatory. Fluency in Croatian is usually necessary for communicating with workers and authorities.
