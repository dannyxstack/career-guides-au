## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for journalists in Croatia?**
A: According to the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, the average gross monthly salary for journalists (ISCO 2642) is approximately €1,300. Entry-level positions may earn around €900, while experienced journalists in national media can earn up to €2,000. Salaries vary by employer, location, and specialization.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for journalists to work in Croatia?**
A: Non-EU journalists can work in Croatia via a standard work permit, requiring a job offer and employer-sponsored application. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers, including journalists, if the gross annual salary meets the threshold (currently around €30,720, i.e., 1.5 times the average gross salary). Both routes require a valid employment contract.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for journalists in Croatia?**
A: Journalists in Croatia commonly work for newspapers, TV and radio stations, online news portals, or as freelancers. Major employers include national broadcasters like HRT and private media houses. Career progression may lead to editor, correspondent, or media management roles. Proficiency in Croatian is essential for most positions, though English-language media offer opportunities for expats.
