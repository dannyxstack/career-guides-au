## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Government Licensing Officials in Croatia?**
A: In the Croatian public sector, licensing officials earn between €1,000 and €1,600 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and specific ministry. Entry-level salaries start around €900, while senior officials may reach €2,000. Additional allowances and bonuses can apply for specialized roles.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a Government Licensing Official in Croatia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as licensing officials under the EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, higher education qualifications (at least three years), and a salary meeting the threshold (approx. 1.5 times the national average). Alternatively, a standard work permit applies for lower-skilled roles. All routes require employer sponsorship and police registration.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Government Licensing Official in Croatia?**
A: Candidates typically need a bachelor's degree in law, public administration, or a relevant field. Fluency in Croatian (C1 level) is mandatory. Some positions require passing the state professional exam for civil servants. Prior experience in administrative or regulatory roles is advantageous. Internal promotions occur through competitive exams.
