## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for judges in Estonia?**
A: Judges in Estonia earn a monthly gross salary ranging from approximately €3,000 to €5,000, depending on experience and court level. Additional benefits include allowances for seniority and special duties. Total compensation is regulated by the state budget and legal statutes.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as judges in Estonia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No, under Estonian law, only Estonian citizens are eligible to serve as judges. Therefore, non-EU nationals cannot obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this profession. The judiciary is a reserved occupation for citizens.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a judge in Estonia?**
A: To become a judge in Estonia, candidates must hold a master's degree in law (MA or equivalent), complete a judges' training programme, and pass aptitude tests. They must be Estonian citizens, proficient in Estonian, and have at least five years of legal experience. Appointment is made by the President on the proposal of the Council of Courts.
