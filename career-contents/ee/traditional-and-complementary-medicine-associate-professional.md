## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Estonia ranges from approximately €1,500 to €2,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. Private practice earnings may vary. This is comparable to other healthcare associate professionals.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for traditional and complementary medicine associate professionals in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a job offer and employer-sponsored residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is also available if the job requires higher professional qualifications and meets the salary threshold (currently €1,573 per month in 2024). Applicants must have recognized qualifications in traditional or complementary medicine.

**Q: Is traditional and complementary medicine regulated in Estonia?**
A: Yes, certain practices are regulated. For example, acupuncturists and naturopaths must register with the Health Board and meet specific training requirements. Unregulated practices exist but cannot claim medical benefits. Professional certification from relevant Estonian associations is recommended for credibility.
