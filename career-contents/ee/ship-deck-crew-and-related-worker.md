## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships' deck crew in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for ships' deck crew (e.g., able seamen) in Estonia ranges from €1,500 to €2,000, depending on experience, employer, and vessel type. Overtime and sea allowances can increase total earnings.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign ships' deck crew in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work in Estonia as deck crew via a D visa for employment (long-term visa) or an EU Blue Card if the job meets the salary threshold (€1,484 gross monthly in 2024). The employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for deck crew in Estonia?**
A: Deck crew can advance from ordinary seaman to able seaman, then to boatswain or deck officer after completing STCW certifications and sea service. Further training leads to navigation officer ranks, such as second mate, chief mate, or captain.
