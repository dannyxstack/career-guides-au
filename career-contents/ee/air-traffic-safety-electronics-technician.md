## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technicians in Estonia vary based on experience and employer, but typically range from €2,000 to €3,500 gross per month. The Estonian Air Navigation Services (EANS) is the primary employer. Entry-level positions may start around €1,800, while senior technicians can earn up to €4,000.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Estonia via the EU Blue Card or a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer, higher education or 5+ years of professional experience, and a salary at least 1.5 times Estonia's average gross wage. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible with a job offer meeting local labour market needs.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Estonia?**
A: To become an Air Traffic Safety Electronics Technician in Estonia, you typically need a degree in electronics, telecommunications, or a related engineering field. Specific training in air traffic control systems is provided by the employer, often involving on-the-job training and certification. Knowledge of English is essential, and proficiency in Estonian may be required for some positions. Prior experience with radar, navigation aids, or communication systems is advantageous.
