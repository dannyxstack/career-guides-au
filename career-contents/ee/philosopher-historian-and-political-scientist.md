## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average monthly gross salary for social science professionals (including these occupations) is around €2,000–€2,500, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions may start at approximately €1,500 per month.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign philosophers, historians, and political scientists to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross yearly wage). Alternatively, a standard work visa requires employer sponsorship and a valid job contract.

**Q: What are the typical employers for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Estonia?**
A: Common employers include universities (e.g., University of Tartu, Tallinn University), research institutes, government agencies, think tanks, and media organizations. Some professionals also work as independent consultants or writers.
