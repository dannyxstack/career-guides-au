## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Education Managers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for education managers ranges from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on institution type and experience. Private schools and higher education institutions tend to offer higher pay. Benefits often include additional paid leave and professional development opportunities.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Education Managers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as education managers via the EU Blue Card (requires a master's degree and salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average) or a long-term residence permit for employment (requires a job offer and salary meeting legal minimum). A short-term work D visa is also possible for up to 365 days.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Education Manager in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in education or a related field, along with several years of teaching or administrative experience. Fluency in Estonian is often required for public institutions, though international schools may accept English. Registration with the Estonian Education Information System may be necessary.
