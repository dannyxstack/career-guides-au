## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a lawyer in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for lawyers in Estonia ranges from €3,000 to €5,000, depending on experience and specialization. Senior lawyers in top firms can earn upwards of €7,000 per month. Public sector positions may offer lower salaries but include additional benefits such as job security and pension schemes.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for lawyers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU lawyers can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals, which requires a valid employment offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary (approximately €2,116 per month in 2024). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment can be obtained through an employer-sponsored application, demonstrating that the role cannot be filled by local candidates.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a lawyer in Estonia?**
A: To practice law in Estonia, you need a Master of Laws (LL.M.) or equivalent, complete a three-year traineeship at a law firm, court, or prosecutor's office, pass the bar exam administered by the Estonian Bar Association, and be registered as a member. EU lawyers may have their qualifications recognized under mutual recognition directives, but additional language proficiency in Estonian is typically required.
