## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical pay for odd-job persons in Estonia?**
A: Odd-job persons in Estonia usually earn between €5 and €10 per hour, depending on the task's complexity and location. Many are paid at or slightly above the national minimum wage, which is €4.86 per hour (2024). Earnings can vary seasonally, with higher rates in summer for tourism-related tasks.

**Q: Can an odd-job person obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Estonia?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card in Estonia is for highly skilled non-EU workers with a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Odd-job persons typically do not meet these criteria. Instead, they may apply for a short-term employment visa or a residence permit for seasonal work, if eligible.

**Q: What types of work do odd-job persons typically do in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, odd-job persons often perform temporary tasks such as cleaning, gardening, moving services, petty repairs, and assistance at events. Demand is highest in urban areas like Tallinn and during the summer tourist season. Many find work through online platforms or local job boards, and skills in multiple trades can increase opportunities.
