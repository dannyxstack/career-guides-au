## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a construction manager in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a construction manager in Estonia ranges from €2,500 to €4,500, depending on experience, project size, and location. Senior managers or those on large infrastructure projects can earn higher, sometimes exceeding €6,000. Salaries in Tallinn tend to be above the national average.

**Q: What are the official work-visa or EU Blue Card routes for a non-EU construction manager to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU construction managers can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross wage. Alternatively, a standard work permit can be obtained if the employer demonstrates that no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required to become a construction manager in Estonia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in civil engineering, construction management, or a related field is usually required. Professional certification from the Estonian Association of Construction Entrepreneurs (EKEL) or similar bodies can be advantageous. Relevant work experience, often 5+ years, and knowledge of local building regulations are also essential. Continuous professional development is encouraged.
