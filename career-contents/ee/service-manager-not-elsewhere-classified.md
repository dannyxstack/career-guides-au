## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Services Managers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for Services Managers in Estonia typically range from €2,500 to €4,500 gross per month, depending on experience, sector, and company size. Senior managers in larger firms may earn more. According to Statistics Estonia, the average monthly gross salary for managers was around €3,200 in 2023.

**Q: How can a foreign Services Manager obtain a work visa for Estonia?**
A: Foreign Services Managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a binding job offer, higher education (or 5 years of experience), and meet the salary threshold (1.5x average wage). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible if the job offer meets the minimum salary requirement (at least 1.24x average wage).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Services Manager in Estonia?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor's degree in business administration, management, or a related field. Proven experience in service management, leadership skills, and proficiency in English (and often Estonian) are essential. Additional certifications in project management or customer service can be advantageous.
