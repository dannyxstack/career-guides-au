## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for employment agents in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for employment agents in Estonia ranges from €1,800 to €2,200, with variations based on experience, location (e.g., Tallinn higher), and company size. Performance-based bonuses are common, and senior agents may earn above €3,000.

**Q: What are the visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as employment agents in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain an EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary) or a national D-visa for employment. The employer must secure a work permit approval from the Estonian Police and Border Guard Board. A valid residence permit is then required.

**Q: What career progression is typical for employment agents in Estonia?**
A: Entry-level employment agents often start as junior recruiters, progressing to senior agent, team lead, or branch manager. Specializing in sectors like IT, engineering, or healthcare can accelerate growth. Additional certifications in HR or labor law enhance prospects.
