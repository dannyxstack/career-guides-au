## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for animal producers in Estonia?**
A: Based on Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for agricultural workers is around €1,300. For animal producers, pay varies by farm type and experience, ranging from €1,100 to €1,600. Overtime and bonuses may increase earnings on larger commercial farms.

**Q: What work visa options are available for non-EU animal producers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as animal producers via the EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€1,656 gross monthly in 2023). Alternatively, a national D visa for employment suits shorter contracts. The employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What are the main duties of an animal producer in Estonia?**
A: Animal producers in Estonia manage breeding, feeding, and health of livestock such as cattle, pigs, or poultry. Tasks include monitoring animal welfare, organizing housing, keeping records, and ensuring compliance with EU regulations. They may also oversee farm workers and coordinate with veterinarians.
