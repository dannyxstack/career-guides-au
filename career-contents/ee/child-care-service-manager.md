## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Child Care Services Manager in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for Child Care Services Managers in Estonia vary by experience and region, typically ranging from €1,500 to €2,200 gross per month. According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary in education management was around €1,900 in 2023.

**Q: What are the work visa options for a Child Care Services Manager to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times average gross salary). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible with a job offer from an Estonian employer. Both require a valid work contract and approval from the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Child Care Services Manager in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in early childhood education or a related field is required, along with relevant work experience. Registration with the Estonian Education Information System (EHIS) may be necessary. Additionally, B2-level Estonian language proficiency is often required for managing local staff and communicating with authorities.
