## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a landscape architect in Estonia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for landscape architects in Estonia typically ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience, project complexity, and sector (public vs private). Senior professionals or those leading large projects may earn higher, while entry-level positions start around €1,500. Rates vary by region, with Tallinn offering more opportunities.

**Q: What work visa options exist for landscape architects moving to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can work in Estonia via a temporary residence permit for employment or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (currently at least €2,698/month gross). Both routes require employer sponsorship and approval from the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a landscape architect in Estonia?**
A: To practice as a landscape architect in Estonia, you typically need a master's degree in landscape architecture or a related field from an accredited university. Professional licensure is not mandatory but is recommended for independent responsibility. Registration with the Estonian Association of Landscape Architects may enhance credibility. International qualifications are often recognized after evaluation.
