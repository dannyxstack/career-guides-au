## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a shop supervisor in Estonia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for a shop supervisor in Estonia typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and company size. Larger retail chains in Tallinn may offer higher pay. Overtime and bonuses can supplement income.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU shop supervisors in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a job offer and employer sponsorship to apply for a temporary residence permit for employment in Estonia. The EU Blue Card is generally for highly skilled workers with a higher education degree and a salary above the national threshold; shop supervisor roles may not qualify unless the job meets these criteria.

**Q: What are common career advancement opportunities for shop supervisors in Estonia?**
A: Shop supervisors can progress to store manager, district manager, or regional manager roles. Some move into retail buying, visual merchandising, or operations. Large retail chains often offer internal training programs and clear promotion paths based on performance.
