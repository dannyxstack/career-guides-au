## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for signwriters and decorative painters in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, signwriters, decorative painters, engravers, and etchers typically earn a gross monthly salary between €1,200 and €1,800. Pay varies based on experience, employer, and location, with Tallinn offering higher rates. Freelancers may earn more but with irregular income.

**Q: What official routes allow non-EU citizens to work as a signwriter or engraver in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card (for high-skilled roles) or a national D-visa/work permit. For signwriters, the employer must prove the role cannot be filled locally, and the applicant must meet salary thresholds. Long-term residence is possible after 5 years.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a signwriter in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, signwriters typically complete vocational training in painting or graphic design, often through a 3-4 year program at a vocational school. Apprenticeships are common. Certification from the Estonian Qualifications Authority may enhance job prospects. Practical skills and a portfolio are key.
