## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, the gross monthly salary for fumigators and pest controllers typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced specialists can earn up to €2,200. Overtime and hazardous work allowances may apply.

**Q: How can a foreign national obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for pest control work in Estonia?**
A: To work as a fumigator in Estonia, non-EU nationals typically need a long-term (D) visa or temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must register the job with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers; pest control usually does not meet the salary threshold (≥€2,208/month in 2024).

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a fumigator in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, fumigators must have vocational training in pest control or a related field, often through a professional qualification certificate issued by the Estonian Qualifications Authority. A license from the Health Board is required for handling biocides. Knowledge of safety regulations and practical experience are essential.
