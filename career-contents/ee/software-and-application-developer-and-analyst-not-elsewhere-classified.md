## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for software developers in Estonia is around €3,500, with junior developers earning approximately €2,500 and senior developers up to €6,000. Salaries vary by company size, location (Tallinn higher), and specialization. The tech sector offers competitive pay, often including stock options and bonuses.

**Q: What are the main work visa routes for software developers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU software developers can work in Estonia via an EU Blue Card, requiring a relevant degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average (around €2,715/month gross in 2024). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment applies if the employer registers the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund. Short-term D-visas also exist for up to 365 days.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for software developers in Estonia?**
A: Estonia's thriving tech ecosystem, including over 1,500 startups and scale-ups like Bolt and TransferWise, offers rapid career progression. Developers can advance to senior, lead, or architect roles, or move into management. The strong e-Estonia infrastructure also provides niche opportunities in cybersecurity, data science, and AI.
