## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for journalists in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for journalists in Estonia ranges from €1,500 to €2,000. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while experienced journalists at major outlets can earn over €2,500. Salaries vary by media type (print, digital, broadcast) and location, with Tallinn offering higher pay.

**Q: Can journalists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Estonia?**
A: Yes, non-EU journalists can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average Estonian gross annual salary). An employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund first.

**Q: What language skills are needed to work as a journalist in Estonia?**
A: Fluency in Estonian is essential for most journalism roles, as content is primarily produced for local audiences. However, international outlets or English-language media in Estonia may hire journalists with strong English skills. Russian-language media also exists, catering to the Russian-speaking minority.
