## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for supply, distribution, and related managers in Estonia typically range from €2,500 to €4,500 per month gross, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior roles in larger logistics firms or Tallinn may exceed €5,000. Additional benefits like bonuses and health insurance are common.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU national to work as a Supply, Distribution and Related Manager in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, or a national D-visa for temporary work. The employer must obtain a work permit approval from the Police and Border Guard Board. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Supply, Distribution and Related Managers in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, these managers can advance to roles such as Head of Logistics, Supply Chain Director, or Operations Manager. The growing e-commerce and manufacturing sectors offer paths to executive positions. Professional certifications like CILT or APICS can enhance prospects. Networking within the local logistics hub is also beneficial.
