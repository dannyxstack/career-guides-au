## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sociologists and anthropologists in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for social scientists and related professionals in Estonia ranges from €2,000 to €2,500, according to Statistics Estonia. Entry-level positions start around €1,500, while senior roles in research or consultancy can exceed €3,000. Salaries in private sector market research firms tend to be higher than in public institutions.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for sociologists to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment or an EU Blue Card. The employer must usually register the vacancy and pass a labour market test. For the Blue Card, you need a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.2 times the Estonian average gross wage (currently ~€2,100). Both routes require a job offer from an Estonian employer.

**Q: What types of employers hire sociologists and anthropologists in Estonia?**
A: Typical employers include universities (e.g., University of Tartu, Tallinn University), research institutes, government ministries (e.g., Ministry of Social Affairs), and private market research firms. Opportunities also exist in NGOs, cultural heritage institutions, and international organizations based in Estonia. The field is small but growing, with demand for data-driven social analysis.
