## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Buyer in Estonia?**
A: Buyers in Estonia earn a gross monthly salary ranging from €1,800 to €3,500, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while senior buyers with specialized skills can earn over €3,500. The median salary is approximately €2,400 per month.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Buyers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU buyers can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers (requires a job offer meeting the salary threshold, at least 1.5 times average gross salary) or a standard long-term (D) visa for employment, which requires an employer-sponsored work permit. Both routes need employer applications and compliance with Estonian immigration rules.

**Q: What qualifications and skills are important for Buyers in Estonia?**
A: Buyers in Estonia typically need a bachelor's degree in business, supply chain, or a related field. Strong negotiation, analytical, and communication skills are essential. Proficiency in English is mandatory, and knowledge of Estonian or Russian can be advantageous. Experience with procurement software and understanding of local market dynamics are highly valued.
