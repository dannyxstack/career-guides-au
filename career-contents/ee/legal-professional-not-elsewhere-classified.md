## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a legal professional in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for legal professionals in Estonia vary by experience, specialization, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €1,500–2,000 per month, while senior lawyers in corporate firms can earn €4,000–6,000+ monthly. Public sector roles typically pay less than private practice.

**Q: Can non-EU legal professionals work in Estonia through the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU legal professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer and meet salary thresholds (currently at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary, roughly €3,250/month in 2024). Alternatively, a long-term work visa is possible for qualified positions. Both require recognition of qualifications.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements to practice law in Estonia?**
A: To practice as a lawyer (advokaat) in Estonia, you must have a Master of Laws (MA) degree, pass the bar exam, and register with the Estonian Bar Association. Foreign-qualified lawyers may need to complete additional exams or undergo recognition of professional qualifications, especially for EU/EEA qualifications under the mutual recognition directive.
