## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Estonia?**
A: Most astrologers and fortune-tellers in Estonia are self-employed, so income varies widely. Typical earnings range from €500 to €1,500 per month, depending on client base and service type. Few work full-time; many combine it with other jobs. Official salary statistics are not available for this niche.

**Q: Can astrologers and fortune-tellers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: These occupations are not eligible for the EU Blue Card, which requires a highly skilled job with a specific salary threshold. However, a standard work visa (D visa) may be possible if an Estonian employer offers a contract, though such positions are rare. Self-employment is an option if you meet the business plan and investment criteria for a start-up visa.

**Q: What are the career prospects for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Estonia?**
A: Career prospects are limited. Most practitioners work independently, offering consultations online or at fairs. The market is small, and demand is seasonal. Some expand into related areas like tarot reading or spiritual coaching. Networking and online presence are crucial for building a client base.
