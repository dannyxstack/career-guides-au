## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Database and Network Professionals in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for Database and Network Professionals (ISCO 2529) in Estonia ranges from €3,000 to €4,500, depending on experience, certifications, and industry. Senior specialists with expertise in cloud or cybersecurity can earn €5,000 or more. Entry-level roles start around €2,500.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU professionals in this field to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting at least 1.5 times Estonia's average gross salary (approx. €3,000/month in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work visa (D-visa) requires a valid employment contract and employer sponsorship. Shortage occupations may have simplified procedures.

**Q: Which skills are most in demand for Database and Network Professionals in Estonia?**
A: Estonian employers highly value expertise in database management (SQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB), network security, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), and automation (Ansible, Python). Certifications like Cisco CCNA or AWS Solutions Architect boost employability. Familiarity with Estonian e-governance systems is also a plus.
