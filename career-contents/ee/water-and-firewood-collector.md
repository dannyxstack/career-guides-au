## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for water and firewood collectors in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for water and firewood collectors in Estonia are generally low, as the work is unskilled and often seasonal or informal. Monthly earnings typically range from €600 to €900 gross, depending on region and employer. Minimum wage (€725 per month in 2024) is common. Overtime or piece-rate pay may increase earnings slightly.

**Q: Can foreign workers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for water and firewood collection in Estonia?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers and does not apply to this occupation. Third-country nationals may apply for a temporary residence permit for employment if an employer proves a labour market need. The process requires a registered job offer, and the occupation must be on Estonia's list of shortage occupations (which this is not), making approval unlikely without special circumstances.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions for water and firewood collectors in Estonia?**
A: Work is physically demanding, often outdoors in remote areas with limited facilities. Tasks include collecting firewood from forests or fetching water from natural sources for households without utilities. Hours vary seasonally; winter requires more firewood work. Safety risks include weather exposure and manual handling injuries. Most positions are temporary or part-time.
