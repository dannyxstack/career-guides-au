## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for deep-sea fishery workers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for deep-sea fishery workers in Estonia vary based on experience and vessel type. Entry-level workers earn around €1,200–€1,500 per month, while experienced crew can earn €1,800–€2,500. Income often includes bonuses tied to catch volume. According to Statistics Estonia, average monthly earnings in fishing and aquaculture were €1,436 in 2022.

**Q: Can deep-sea fishery workers obtain an EU Blue Card or work visa for Estonia?**
A: Estonia offers a work visa for non-EU nationals under the Aliens Act. The EU Blue Card, requiring a higher education degree and a high salary threshold (€1,578/month in 2024), is typically not suited for fishery workers, who usually qualify for a standard long-term employment visa (D-visa) based on a job offer and employer sponsorship. No specific quota limits apply.

**Q: What are the main requirements to work as a deep-sea fishery worker in Estonia?**
A: Key requirements include a valid seaman's certificate (STCW basic safety training), good physical fitness, and often Estonian or Russian language skills. While formal education is not mandatory, experience is valued. Employers typically require a medical fitness certificate and a clean criminal record. Non-EU workers need a work visa and employer sponsorship.
