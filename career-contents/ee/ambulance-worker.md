## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ambulance workers in Estonia?**
A: According to the Estonian Health Board and labor statistics, ambulance workers (including paramedics) earn an average gross monthly salary of approximately €1,500–€2,000. Salaries vary by experience, qualifications, and region. Additional allowances may apply for night shifts, weekends, and overtime.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as ambulance workers in Estonia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as ambulance workers in Estonia through the EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary, currently around €2,100 gross monthly) and have a higher education degree. Alternatively, a long-term visa (D-visa) or temporary residence permit for employment is possible if a job offer from an Estonian employer is obtained and no local applicants are available.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an ambulance worker in Estonia?**
A: To work as an ambulance worker (e.g., paramedic) in Estonia, you need a vocational or applied higher education diploma in pre-hospital emergency care (e.g., from Tallinn Health Care College). Registration with the Estonian Health Board as a healthcare professional is mandatory. For foreign qualifications, recognition and language proficiency (Estonian at B2 level) are required.
