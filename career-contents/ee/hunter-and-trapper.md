## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for hunters and trappers in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, hunters and trappers often work freelance or seasonally, with income varying widely based on game species and contracts. Full-time professionals may earn between €1,200 and €2,000 per month. Many supplement income with wildlife management or guiding. Official average salary data is not specifically published for this occupation.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign hunters and trappers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU hunters/trappers can apply for a residence permit for employment if sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is available for those with higher education and meeting salary thresholds. Alternatively, short-term work may be permitted via a visa for seasonal or specific projects. Official routes include the Estonian residence permit for employment and the EU Blue Card.

**Q: What are the main duties and career opportunities for hunters and trappers in Estonia?**
A: Hunters and trappers in Estonia manage game populations, control pests like beavers, and provide game meat. They must pass hunting exams and join a hunting club. Opportunities include contract work for landowners, game management, or guided hunts. The profession is regulated by the Environmental Board, and many work part-time alongside other jobs.
