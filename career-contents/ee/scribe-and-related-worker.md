## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Transcribing handwritten documents into electronic text.
- Copying and transcribing manuscripts
- Simple proofreading and formatting adjustments
- Document archiving and numbering
- Fill in table content following a template

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI-assisted transcription tools to improve efficiency
- Supervise and correct AI-generated transcription results
- Processing complex handwritten or non-standard format documents
- Manage multilingual document translation and localization
- Use OCR and NLP tools to batch process historical documents

**Human moat:**

- Ability to interpret complex, blurred, or damaged manuscripts
- Precise understanding of terminology in legal, medical, or other professional fields
- Client communication and document quality control
- Cross-platform Document Management System Configuration and Maintenance
- Legal compliance checks (e.g., evidence chain completeness)

**Skills to build:**

- Operation of AI transcription and OCR tools (e.g., ABBYY, Transkribus)
- Document management systems (e.g., SharePoint, M-Files)
- Basic programming (Python scripting for batch document processing)
- Knowledge of terminology in the legal or medical field
- Data quality audit and correction techniques
- Cross-cultural communication and multilingual ability

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for scribes in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for scribes and related workers in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, sector (e.g., legal or medical), and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles may exceed €2,000. Self-employed scribes often charge per project.

**Q: What visa options exist for non-EU scribes to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU scribes can obtain a long-term D-visa or an employment residence permit if they have a job offer from an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary (€2,040/month in 2024). Scribes' typical salaries usually do not meet this threshold, making the standard work permit the more common route.

**Q: How is technology affecting scribe jobs in Estonia?**
A: Automation and speech-to-text software are reducing demand for manual transcription, but human scribes remain valued for accuracy in legal and medical contexts requiring certification. Many scribes adapt by specializing in fields like court reporting or medical transcription, which are less automated. Upskilling in digital tools is recommended for career longevity.
