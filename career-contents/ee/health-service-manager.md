## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Health Services Managers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for Health Services Managers in Estonia vary based on experience, qualifications, and the size of the healthcare facility. Typically, the monthly gross salary ranges from €3,000 to €5,000. Public sector salaries may be lower than private sector. Bonuses and benefits may also be included.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for Health Services Managers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU Health Services Managers can work in Estonia via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree and a job offer. Alternatively, a long-term D visa for employment is possible if the job meets labor market needs. Both require employer sponsorship and compliance with Estonian migration laws.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Health Services Manager in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in health administration, public health, or a related field is required. Relevant work experience in healthcare management is essential. Registration with the Health Board (Terviseamet) may be needed for certain roles, and proficiency in Estonian language is often required for public sector positions.
