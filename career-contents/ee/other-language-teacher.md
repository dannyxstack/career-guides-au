## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for other language teachers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for other language teachers in Estonia ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, institution (public school, private academy, or university), and location. Senior teachers or those in international schools may earn higher.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU citizens to work as a language teacher in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €1,575/month gross). Alternatively, a long-term D-Visa or temporary residence permit for employment is possible, requiring employer sponsorship and relevant qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to teach languages in Estonia?**
A: To work as a language teacher in Estonia, a bachelor's degree is typically required, preferably in the target language or education. For public schools, registration with the Estonian Education Ministry may be necessary. Private language schools often accept TEFL, CELTA, or equivalent certificates.
