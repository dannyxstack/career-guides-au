## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for other ranks in the Estonian Defence Forces?**
A: The salary for other ranks in the Estonian Defence Forces varies by rank and experience. As of 2024, a private (soldier) earns approximately €1,200–€1,500 per month gross, while non-commissioned officers may earn €1,500–€2,200. Additional allowances apply for deployments, special skills, and tenure.

**Q: Is there a work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU citizens to work as other ranks in the Estonian armed forces?**
A: Generally, positions in the Estonian Defence Forces are reserved for Estonian citizens. The EU Blue Card and standard work visa routes are not applicable for military roles due to citizenship requirements. Only in rare cases, such as specialists in niche fields, might a temporary residence permit for employment be considered, but this is not common for other ranks.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for other ranks in the Estonian Defence Forces?**
A: Other ranks can advance through non-commissioned officer (NCO) ranks, such as corporal, sergeant, and higher. Promotion is based on performance, training, and time in service. Many pursue specialized courses in leadership, technical fields, or attend the NCO School. Long-term careers may lead to warrant officer positions or transition to officer roles via military academies.
