## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Estonia?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Estonia usually earn between €700 and €1,200 per month gross, depending on experience and employer. The national minimum wage is €784 per month as of 2024. Salaries are often negotiated per hour, ranging from €4.50 to €7.50. Some positions include accommodation and meals.

**Q: Can a non-EU domestic housekeeper work in Estonia through the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Domestic housekeepers are not eligible for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree and a high salary. However, they can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must register the vacancy with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund and show that no suitable local or EU worker is available. The job offer must meet the minimum salary requirement, currently €1,484 per month for 2024.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a domestic housekeeper in Estonia?**
A: No formal qualifications are required, but employers often prefer previous experience and references. Basic knowledge of cleaning products, equipment, and safety practices is helpful. Language skills are not mandatory but knowledge of Estonian or Russian can be an advantage. Some agencies offer short training courses. Attention to detail and reliability are key traits.
