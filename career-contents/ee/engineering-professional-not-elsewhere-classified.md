## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,800–€2,200 gross monthly, while senior roles can reach €3,500–€4,500. The average gross monthly salary for this group is approximately €2,700–€3,200, according to recent statistics from Statistics Estonia.

**Q: What work visa options exist for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross annual salary (€28,752 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard long-stay (D) visa for employment is available for jobs meeting the minimum salary threshold (€1,656 gross monthly in 2024).

**Q: What are the career prospects for engineering professionals not elsewhere classified in Estonia?**
A: Estonia’s growing tech and industrial sectors offer strong demand for multidisciplinary engineers. Career advancement often leads to senior specialist or project manager roles. Continuing education in fields like automation, renewable energy, or digital engineering can enhance prospects. Professional registration with the Estonian Association of Engineers is not mandatory but improves credibility.
