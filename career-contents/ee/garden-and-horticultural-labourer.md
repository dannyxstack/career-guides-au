## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, garden and horticultural labourers typically earn between €1,200 and €1,600 gross per month, depending on experience and employer. Hourly wages often range from €6 to €8. Salaries may vary by region and season, with overtime and piecework rates sometimes applying.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for garden and horticultural labourers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a short-term employment visa (D-visa) for seasonal work up to 9 months. EU Blue Cards are not typically issued for this occupation due to skill level requirements. Alternatively, a long-term residence permit for employment may be possible if the employer passes a labour market test and obtains work permission from the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What are the main duties of garden and horticultural labourers in Estonia?**
A: Duties include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, and harvesting plants. They prepare soil, apply fertilizers and pesticides, and maintain gardens, parks, or greenhouses. Tasks also involve operating hand tools and machinery, and ensuring cleanliness of work areas. Seasonal work is common in agriculture and landscaping.
