## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for building caretakers in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, building caretakers (ISCO-08 5153) earn a gross monthly salary of approximately €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience, location, and employer. Many positions include in-kind benefits such as reduced rent or utility allowances.

**Q: What official work routes exist for non-EU building caretakers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as building caretakers through a temporary residence permit for employment (Töötamiseks), requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is typically not available as it requires higher education or a higher salary threshold not met by this occupation.

**Q: What are the main duties of a building caretaker in Estonia?**
A: Building caretakers in Estonia handle cleaning, minor repairs, heating/ventilation checks, and waste management in apartment buildings or offices. They often manage key access, coordinate with service providers, and ensure compliance with local building regulations.
