## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a window cleaner in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a window cleaner in Estonia ranges from €900 to €1,400, depending on experience, employer, and location (Tallinn generally higher). Hourly rates for self-employed cleaners are typically €10–€20. Overtime and weekend work may increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU window cleaner obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: Non-EU workers need a long-term (D) visa or temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card typically requires higher education, so it's usually not applicable for window cleaners. Instead, the employer must apply through the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund to confirm no suitable local candidates, then the worker can apply for a residence permit for employment.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for window cleaners in Estonia?**
A: Experienced window cleaners can specialize in high-rise or industrial cleaning, requiring additional safety training and certification. Others start their own cleaning business, subcontracting for companies. Some move into supervisory roles or combine window cleaning with other cleaning services.
