## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a handicraft worker in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for handicraft workers in Estonia vary widely by specialization and experience. As of 2024, the average gross monthly wage is around €1,400–€1,800. Entry-level positions may start at €1,000–€1,200, while experienced artisans can earn €2,000 or more. Many workers are self-employed, so income can fluctuate based on project demand and client base.

**Q: Can a non-EU handicraft worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: Yes, non-EU workers can apply for a long-term (D) visa or a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €2,139/month gross in 2024). Handicraft workers must demonstrate relevant qualifications or experience. The employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund first.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to work as a handicraft worker in Estonia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not always mandatory, but vocational training in fields like woodworking, textiles, or metalcraft is highly valued. Many workers complete courses at vocational schools or through apprenticeships. Some crafts require a certificate or license (e.g., for traditional crafts). The Estonian Chamber of Crafts offers professional certification for master artisans.
