## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for accountants in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for accountants in Estonia vary based on experience and qualifications. Entry-level accountants earn around €1,400–€1,700 gross per month, while senior accountants or those with certifications (e.g., CPA) can earn €2,000–€3,000. The average gross monthly salary for accountants is approximately €1,800, but larger firms in Tallinn may offer higher packages.

**Q: Can non-EU accountants work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes. Non-EU accountants can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. The Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold (at least 1.5 times Estonia's average gross salary) and a relevant higher education degree. The standard work visa does not have a salary floor but must meet general labour market needs.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an accountant in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or business is required. Professional certifications such as CPA (Auditiorkogu), ACCA, or CIMA enhance prospects. Work experience is valued, and proficiency in Estonian is often necessary for local roles, though international companies may use English. Continuous learning is expected due to evolving tax laws and digital reporting systems.
