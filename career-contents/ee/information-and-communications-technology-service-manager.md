## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ICT Services Managers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for ICT Services Managers in Estonia ranges from €4,500 to €6,500, depending on experience, company size, and location. Senior roles in Tallinn typically pay higher. Salary data is available from the Estonian Tax and Customs Board and employer surveys.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for ICT Services Managers moving to Estonia?**
A: ICT Services Managers can apply for an EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross wage) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must register the vacancy with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund. No numerical quotas are specified by law.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ICT Services Managers in Estonia?**
A: ICT Services Managers in Estonia can advance to senior management roles such as Chief Information Officer (CIO) or Director of IT. The growing tech sector offers opportunities in startups, large enterprises, and government digital projects. Professional certifications (e.g., PMP, ITIL) and continuous learning in emerging technologies like AI and cybersecurity enhance career growth.
