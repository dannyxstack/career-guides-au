## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a medical secretary in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a medical secretary in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Salaries are often determined by collective agreements in the healthcare sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior secretaries in larger hospitals may earn up to €2,200.

**Q: What official work visa routes are available for medical secretaries to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Estonia as medical secretaries through the EU Blue Card, requiring a higher professional qualification and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, or via a temporary residence permit for employment, which needs a job offer and a positive labor market test. The specific route depends on qualifications and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a medical secretary in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a secondary education with vocational training in office work or healthcare administration is required. Many medical secretaries complete a professional qualification in health services administration (tase 4-5). Proficiency in Estonian is essential, and knowledge of medical terminology, electronic health record systems, and basic accounting is often expected by employers.
