## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for travel guides in Estonia?**
A: Travel guides in Estonia typically earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, language skills, and seasonality. Senior guides or those specializing in niche tours may earn higher, especially during peak tourist seasons like summer.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for travel guides to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU travel guides can work in Estonia via a short-term employment (D) visa for up to 365 days, or a long-term (D) visa for work. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified jobs, but travel guiding typically does not meet the salary threshold (€1,558/month in 2024) and may require a job offer from an Estonian employer.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a travel guide in Estonia?**
A: While formal certification is not mandatory, many employers prefer guides with a tour guide qualification from institutions like the Estonian Tourism Board or vocational schools. Fluency in English and Estonian, plus strong knowledge of Estonian history and culture, are essential. Guides often complete first aid training and a guiding exam.
