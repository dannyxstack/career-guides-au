## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for building structure cleaners in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for building cleaners (including building structure cleaners) is approximately €1,400. However, wages can vary depending on the employer, specific job tasks, and the worker's experience. Some employers may offer additional pay for work at heights or hazardous conditions.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a building structure cleaner in Estonia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and typically requires a higher education degree and a salary of at least 1.5 times the national average, which is unlikely for building cleaners. Non-EU nationals can apply for a residence permit for employment. The employer must prove that the job could not be filled by a local or EU worker, usually via the unemployment insurance registry. The application is submitted to the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are the typical duties and requirements for building structure cleaners in Estonia?**
A: Typical duties include cleaning exterior surfaces of buildings such as facades, windows, roofs, and gutters using tools like pressure washers, chemical cleaners, and rope access equipment. Requirements often include physical fitness, comfort working at heights, and relevant safety certifications. In Estonia, employers may require a rope access certificate or training for high-altitude work.
