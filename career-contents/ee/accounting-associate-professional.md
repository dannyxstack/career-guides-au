## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for accounting associate professionals in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for accounting associate professionals in Estonia typically ranges between €1,500 and €2,200, depending on experience, industry, and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior roles can reach €2,800. These figures are indicative and can vary.

**Q: How can a foreign national obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as an accounting associate professional in Estonia?**
A: Foreign nationals can work in Estonia by applying for a temporary residence permit for employment or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian gross salary (as of 2024, about €2,700). The employer must register the job offer with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an accounting associate professional in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a vocational education in accounting or a bachelor's degree in finance or business administration is required. Professional certifications such as ACCA or CIMA are highly valued. Practical experience with Estonian accounting software (e.g., Merit, HansaWorld) and knowledge of local tax laws are also important for job seekers.
