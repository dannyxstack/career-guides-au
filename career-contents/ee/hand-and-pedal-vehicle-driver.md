## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for hand and pedal vehicle drivers in Estonia?**
A: Earnings vary widely depending on employer, hours, and delivery volume. Monthly gross salary typically ranges from €700 to €1,200. Some drivers are paid per delivery or per hour, while others receive a fixed monthly wage. The average is around minimum wage or slightly above, with no formal industry standard.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU citizens to work as a hand or pedal vehicle driver in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment if they have a job offer and the employer proves no suitable local candidates. The EU Blue Card is generally not viable as it requires a high salary (at least 1.5 times the Estonian average, about €2,550 gross monthly) and higher education, which this occupation rarely meets. A short-term D visa for up to 365 days may be possible but also requires a salary meeting the national average (around €1,700 gross monthly), which is often not attained.

**Q: Do I need a special license to work as a hand or pedal vehicle driver in Estonia?**
A: For bicycles and other non-motorized vehicles, no special license is required. However, if the vehicle is electric (e.g., e-bike or e-scooter), you may need a category A or B license depending on speed and power. Additionally, employers often require a clean criminal record and basic road safety knowledge. Registration or insurance requirements vary by municipality.
