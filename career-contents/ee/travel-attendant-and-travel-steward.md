## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for travel attendants in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for travel attendants in Estonia vary by employer and experience. Entry-level positions may earn around €1,200-€1,500 gross per month, while experienced attendants can earn €2,000-€2,500 gross. Additional allowances for flight hours and per diems may apply. Actual pay depends on the airline and contract type.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for travel attendants in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA citizens need a valid work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a travel attendant in Estonia. The employer must obtain a labor market test and register the vacancy with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund. The EU Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold and a relevant degree or five years of professional experience.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a travel attendant in Estonia?**
A: Travel attendants in Estonia typically need a secondary education, fluency in English and Estonian, and a valid cabin crew attestation from the Estonian Transport Administration. Many airlines prefer candidates with customer service experience and first aid training. Additional languages and EU citizenship may be advantageous.
