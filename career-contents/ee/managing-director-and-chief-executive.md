## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Managing Directors and Chief Executives in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for managing directors and chief executives in 2022 was approximately €6,000–€7,000, varying by company size and sector. Top earners in large firms may exceed €15,000, while smaller companies pay less.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for Managing Directors and Chief Executives to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average (€2,520/month in 2024). Alternatively, a long-term D visa for employment is possible if the role meets labor market needs. Applications are processed by the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are the key qualifications and career prospects for Managing Directors in Estonia?**
A: Most managing directors hold a master's degree in business, finance, or a relevant field, along with extensive experience in senior management. In Estonia, strong digital literacy and adaptability to flat organizational structures are valued. Career advancement typically involves moving to larger corporations or international companies operating in Estonia.
