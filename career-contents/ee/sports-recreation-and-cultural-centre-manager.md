## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Estonia?**
A: According to Estonian labour statistics, the average monthly gross salary for this role (ISCO 1431) ranges from €2,500 to €3,500. Salaries vary based on region, years of experience, and whether the centre is public or private. Performance bonuses may also apply.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for foreign Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Managers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment or the EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold and a relevant higher education degree. A job offer from an Estonian employer is mandatory. For details, consult the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Sports, Recreation and Cultural Centre Manager in Estonia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in sports management, cultural management, business administration, or a related field is typically required. Relevant experience in event planning and team leadership is highly valued. For public sector roles, fluency in Estonian is often essential, while private international centres may accept English.
