- Use handheld terminals or AR glasses to receive digital instructions, improving accuracy

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for manufacturing labourers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for manufacturing labourers in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while experienced workers may earn up to €1,800. Overtime and shift work can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as manufacturing labourers in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience. For manufacturing labourers, the standard route is a long-term (D) visa or temporary residence permit for employment, requiring a job offer and approval from the Estonian Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What skills are in demand for manufacturing labourers in Estonia?**
A: Employers seek workers with basic technical skills, reliability, and ability to operate machinery. Knowledge of Estonian or Russian is beneficial. There is demand in food processing, electronics, and wood industries. On-the-job training is common, and experienced labourers can advance to team leader or technician roles.
