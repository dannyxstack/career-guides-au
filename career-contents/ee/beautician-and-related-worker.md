## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for beauticians in Estonia typically ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, location, and employer. Salaries in Tallinn tend to be higher. Additional income from tips and commission can supplement earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU beauticians work in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU beauticians can obtain a D-visa for long-term work or an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €1,482 monthly gross). For lower-skilled positions, employers must obtain a work permit through the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund's labor market test.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a beautician in Estonia?**
A: Formal qualifications such as a vocational diploma in beauty therapy are typically required. Estonian regulation mandates that beauticians complete a recognized vocational training program (e.g., Estonian Qualifications Framework level 4). Licenses from the Health Board may be needed for certain services, like tattooing or permanent makeup.
