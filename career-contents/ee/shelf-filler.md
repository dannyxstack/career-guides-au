**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display
- Repeated counting and checking of goods
- Moving standard-size cargo boxes
- Print and attach price tags

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices
- Adjust replenishment priorities based on sales forecasts
- Real-time synchronization and query using inventory management app
- Use AI assistance to plan efficient replenishment routes

**Human moat:**

- Fine handling of fragile, irregularly shaped, or fresh products
- resolving sudden issues such as shelf blockages and fallen merchandise
- Interacting with customers to provide product information and recommendations
- Coordinates non-standard processes such as returns and near-expiry items
- Maintain overall store environment and safety standards

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shelf fillers in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for shelf fillers in Estonia ranges from €900 to €1,100, often at or slightly above the national minimum wage (€820 as of 2024). Additional pay may apply for night shifts or overtime.

**Q: Can shelf fillers get an EU Blue Card to work in Estonia?**
A: Shelf fillers generally do not qualify for an EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree or a salary at least 1.5 times Estonia's average gross salary. Instead, a standard long-stay (D) visa for employment is the typical route, requiring employer sponsorship and a labor market test.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shelf fillers in Estonia?**
A: Shelf fillers can advance to roles like shift supervisor or department manager after gaining experience. Some move into logistics or warehouse operations. Language proficiency in Estonian and reliability are key for promotion, with on-the-job training often provided.
