## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a building architect in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for building architects ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience and project complexity. Senior architects or those with specialization can earn higher, while entry-level salaries start around €1,500.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign building architects in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU architects can work in Estonia via a long-stay D-visa for employment or the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers. Both require a valid job offer and relevant qualifications. Additionally, a temporary residence permit for employment is available. Official routes are processed through the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for building architects in Estonia?**
A: Building architects in Estonia find opportunities in private architectural firms, construction companies, and public sector projects. The growing focus on sustainable design and digitalization (e.g., BIM) creates demand. Registration with the Estonian Association of Architects is recommended for professional credibility and networking.
