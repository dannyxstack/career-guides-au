## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for farm labourers in Estonia ranges from 1000 to 1400 EUR, depending on experience, region, and employer. Many positions offer additional benefits such as accommodation or meals. Overtime pay may increase earnings during peak seasons. Minimum wage regulations apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for mixed crop and livestock farm labourer jobs in Estonia?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not available for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree or high salary. Non-EU citizens can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment if the employer proves a labor market shortage, or a seasonal work visa for up to 9 months. Official routes are through the Estonian Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are typical working conditions and career prospects for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Estonia?**
A: Work involves physical tasks like planting, feeding animals, and operating machinery. Hours vary by season, often including weekends. While advancement to supervisor roles is possible, many workers use this as a stepping stone to specialized agricultural jobs. Some employers offer training for equipment operation.
