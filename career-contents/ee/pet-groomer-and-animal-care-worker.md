## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers in Estonia?**
A: Pet groomers in Estonia typically earn between €900 and €1,500 gross per month, varying with experience, location, and employer. Some self-employed groomers may earn more. Income can be supplemented by tips and commission.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a pet groomer in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian wage, which pet groomers rarely meet. Instead, non-EU citizens can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, contingent on a job offer and a positive labor market test. A short-term D-visa may be possible for stays up to one year.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pet groomer in Estonia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not legally required, but vocational training or apprenticeships are common. The Estonian Chamber of Commerce and Industry offers skill certification. Practical experience and a portfolio are key. Some groomers attend courses abroad or online.
