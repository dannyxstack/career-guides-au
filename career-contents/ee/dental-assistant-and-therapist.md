## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dental assistant or therapist in Estonia?**
A: Dental assistants and therapists in Estonia typically earn between €1,200 and €1,800 per month gross, depending on experience, location, and private vs. public sector. Salaries may be higher in private clinics, especially in Tallinn. Overtime and bonuses can add to total compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for dental assistants and therapists to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as dental assistants/therapists via an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €1,578/month). Alternatively, a long-term (D) visa for employment requires a registered job offer from an Estonian employer and approval from the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a dental assistant or therapist in Estonia?**
A: To work as a dental assistant in Estonia, you typically need a secondary medical education or a vocational qualification in dental assisting. Dental therapists require a bachelor's degree in dental therapy from an accredited institution. All non-EU qualifications must be recognized by the Estonian Health Board before practicing.
