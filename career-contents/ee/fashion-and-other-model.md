## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fashion and other models in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience and assignment. Entry-level models may earn €500-€1,500 per month, while established models can earn €2,000-€5,000 or more. Many models are freelancers, so income is project-based. Fashion shows and commercial shoots pay per assignment, typically €100-€500 per day.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU models to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU models need a temporary residence permit for employment, sponsored by an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary, which is rarely applicable to models. Alternatively, short-term work (up to 365 days) may be possible under a D-visa for specific contracts.

**Q: What are the typical requirements to become a model in Estonia?**
A: Agencies usually require a portfolio, a minimum height for runway (175 cm for women, 185 cm for men), and a specific look. No formal education is needed, but good communication skills and reliability are essential. Many models start by submitting photos to agencies in Tallinn, such as Baltic Models or Elite Model Look Estonia.
