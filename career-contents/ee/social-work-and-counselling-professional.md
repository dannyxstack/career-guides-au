## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average monthly gross salary for social work and counselling professionals is approximately 1,500–2,000 euros. This varies by experience, sector (public vs. private), and specialization. Private practitioners and specialized counsellors often earn higher incomes, while public sector roles may include additional benefits.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for social work and counselling professionals in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary as set annually by Statistics Estonia. A temporary residence permit for employment may also be available after a labor market test. Recognition of professional qualifications is mandatory.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a social work or counselling professional in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a higher education degree in social work, psychology, or a related field is required. Many positions require a master's degree. Additionally, registration with the Estonian Health Board or the Ministry of Social Affairs may be necessary for certain roles. Proficiency in Estonian is often required, especially for client-facing roles.
