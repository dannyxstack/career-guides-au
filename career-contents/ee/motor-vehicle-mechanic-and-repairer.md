## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for motor vehicle mechanics and repairers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for motor vehicle mechanics and repairers is around €1,400–€1,800. Entry-level positions may start at €1,100, while experienced professionals can earn up to €2,200. Earnings vary by location, employer, and specialization (e.g., electric vehicles or diagnostics).

**Q: Can a non-EU motor vehicle mechanic work in Estonia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU mechanics can work in Estonia via a long-term (D) visa or EU Blue Card. The process requires a job offer from an Estonian employer, a valid employment contract meeting the minimum salary threshold (€1,484/month for Blue Card in 2024), and relevant qualifications. The employer typically handles the application with the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a motor vehicle mechanic in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, formal vocational education in automotive mechanics (e.g., a 3-year programme) is typically required. Professional certification from the Estonian Qualifications Authority (Kutsekoda) is advantageous. Knowledge of modern diagnostic tools, electric vehicle systems, and Estonian/English language skills improve employability. On-the-job training is common.
