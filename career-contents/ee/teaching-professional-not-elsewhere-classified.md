## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and experience. In public schools, teachers follow a national pay scale starting around €1,400 gross per month. Private language schools or specialized training centers may offer €1,800–€2,500. Overall, the average gross monthly wage for education professionals in Estonia is approximately €1,800, according to Statistics Estonia.

**Q: What are the work visa and EU Blue Card options for teaching professionals in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a long-stay visa or temporary residence permit for employment. An EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher-education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5× average Estonian salary, around €2,250 per month). For other teaching roles, the employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a teaching professional in Estonia?**
A: A relevant bachelor's or master's degree is typically required. For public school teaching, Estonian language proficiency and recognition of qualifications by the Ministry of Education are mandatory. Private institutions may have more flexible requirements, but a teaching certificate or relevant experience is often expected.
