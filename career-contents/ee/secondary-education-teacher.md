## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for secondary education teachers in Estonia?**
A: Secondary education teachers in Estonia earn a gross monthly salary of €1,700–€2,300 on average, depending on qualifications and experience. Salaries are set by the state and increase with seniority. Additional pay may come from extracurricular duties or teaching specialized subjects.

**Q: How can a foreign secondary education teacher obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: Foreign teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized qualification and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times average gross salary). Alternatively, a residence permit for employment is possible. Proficiency in Estonian is typically required. The employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available for secondary education teachers in Estonia?**
A: Teachers can progress to roles such as head teacher, subject coordinator, or education advisor. They may also specialize in curriculum development or teacher training. Further education, like a master's degree in education or leadership, can open doors to higher positions and increased responsibilities.
