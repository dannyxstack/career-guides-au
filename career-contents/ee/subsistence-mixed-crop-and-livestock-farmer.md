## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Estonia?**
A: Earnings for subsistence farmers in Estonia are highly variable and often below the national average. Many operate on small plots with limited market sales, resulting in income around the minimum wage (€725/month in 2024) or lower. Subsistence farming typically provides for personal consumption rather than significant cash income.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Estonia?**
A: Subsistence farming generally does not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian wage. A standard residence permit for employment may be possible if a registered employer offers a full-time job in agriculture, but subsistence farming itself is not a recognized basis for work migration.

**Q: What are the main duties of a subsistence mixed crop and livestock farmer in Estonia?**
A: Daily tasks include planting and harvesting crops (e.g., potatoes, grains, vegetables), tending to livestock (e.g., cows, pigs, sheep), maintaining fences and buildings, and managing feed and water supplies. Work is seasonal and physically demanding, often relying on traditional methods with minimal mechanization.
