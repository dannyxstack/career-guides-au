## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a companion or valet in Estonia?**
A: The typical monthly gross salary for companions and valets in Estonia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience and employer. Many positions are part-time. The minimum wage is €725, but most employers offer slightly more.

**Q: What visa options are available for companions and valets to work in Estonia?**
A: Companions and valets typically qualify for a national D visa for short-term work (up to 1 year) or a long-term residence permit for employment, if sponsored by an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as this occupation is considered low-skilled.

**Q: What are typical duties of a companion or valet in Estonia?**
A: Companions provide personal care, companionship, and assistance with daily tasks for elderly or disabled individuals. Valets attend to personal needs such as dressing, bathing, and household chores. Fluency in Estonian or Russian is often required. Work may be in private homes or care facilities.
