## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for door-to-door salespersons in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary widely due to commission-based nature. Entry-level earnings may be around €900-1,200 gross per month, while experienced salespersons can earn €1,500-2,500 or more including commissions. Many positions offer base pay plus performance bonuses. Income depends heavily on sales volume and product type.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card options for door-to-door salespersons in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need an employer-sponsored work visa or EU Blue Card. The employer must prove the position couldn't be filled locally. The Blue Card requires a higher salary threshold (at least 1.5x average gross wage) and a valid employment contract. Both routes require a residence permit application.

**Q: What are typical career progression opportunities for door-to-door salespersons in Estonia?**
A: Experienced door-to-door salespersons may advance to team leader, sales manager, or area supervisor roles. Others transition into retail sales or customer service management. Continuous sales training and proven results can lead to higher commission rates or permanent in-store positions.
