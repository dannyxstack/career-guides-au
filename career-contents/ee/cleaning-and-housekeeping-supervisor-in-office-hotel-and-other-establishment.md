## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cleaning and housekeeping supervisors in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for cleaning supervisors in Estonia vary based on experience, location, and establishment size. According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for supervisors in this field typically ranges between €1,200 and €1,700. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced supervisors in larger hotels or offices may earn higher wages.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as cleaning supervisors in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer from an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€2,100+ gross/month in 2024). Cleaning supervisors typically do not meet this salary threshold, so the standard work visa route is more applicable.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for cleaning supervisors in Estonia?**
A: Cleaning supervisors can progress to facility management, operations management, or specialize in areas like eco-cleaning. With experience, they may oversee larger teams or multiple sites. Further education in hospitality or business management, available at Estonian vocational schools, can enhance promotion prospects. Some supervisors advance to regional manager roles in cleaning companies.
