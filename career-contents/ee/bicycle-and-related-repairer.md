## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bicycle repairers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for bicycle repairers in Estonia vary by experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around the minimum wage (€725 gross per month in 2024), while experienced repairers can earn between €1,200 and €1,600 gross monthly. Larger cities like Tallinn may offer higher pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as bicycle repairers in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Estonia under a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is typically not applicable as bicycle repair is not classified as highly skilled. The employer must prove the vacancy could not be filled locally and offer a salary meeting the national average.

**Q: What training or certification is required to become a bicycle repairer in Estonia?**
A: Formal education is not mandatory, but vocational training in bicycle mechanics is offered by schools like Tallinn Polytechnic. On-the-job learning is common. Employer-based certification (e.g., from the Estonian Qualification Authority) can enhance credibility. Knowledge of Estonian is often required for customer interaction.
