## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for psychologists in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for psychologists ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, varying by experience and sector. Public sector roles may start lower, while private practice or specialized clinical positions can pay higher. Source: Statistics Estonia and salary surveys.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for psychologists moving to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU psychologists can work in Estonia via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers or a temporary residence permit for employment. The Blue Card requires a higher-education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross wage). The employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice as a psychologist in Estonia?**
A: To practice as a psychologist in Estonia, you typically need a master's degree in psychology from a recognized university. Licensure is not mandatory for all roles, but clinical work may require registration with the Estonian Health Board. Proficiency in Estonian is often needed for client-facing positions, though some international organizations use English.
