## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average monthly gross salary for environmental and occupational health inspectors ranges from €1,800 to €2,200, depending on experience and sector. Senior inspectors in the public sector or private consulting can earn up to €3,000.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a residence permit for employment or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a valid job offer meeting the salary threshold (approximately 1.5 times the average gross salary, around €2,700/month in 2024). Applications are processed by the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an Environmental and Occupational Health Inspector in Estonia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in environmental health, occupational safety, or a related field is typically required. Registration with the Health Board is mandatory. Opportunities exist in government agencies, such as the Health Board, and private consulting firms. Continuing education is encouraged.
