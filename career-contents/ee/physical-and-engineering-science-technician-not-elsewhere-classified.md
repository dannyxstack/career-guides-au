## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for physical and engineering science technicians in Estonia vary by sector and experience. Typical gross monthly earnings range from €1,500 to €3,000, with higher pay in specialised fields like IT or electronics. According to Statistics Estonia, the median monthly salary for similar technician roles was around €1,800 in 2023. Overtime and shift work may increase total compensation.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU Physical and Engineering Science Technicians to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as a technician in Estonia via the EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least 1.2 times the average Estonian salary) or a temporary residence permit for employment (employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund for at least 2 weeks). Both routes require a valid work contract and recognised qualifications.

**Q: What education and career progression is typical for Physical and Engineering Science Technicians in Estonia?**
A: Most technicians in Estonia hold a vocational or applied higher education degree in engineering, physics, or a related field. Career advancement includes becoming a senior technician, project supervisor, or moving into engineering roles with additional studies. Key employers are manufacturing, energy, and R&D companies. Professional certification from the Estonian Qualifications Authority can enhance opportunities.
