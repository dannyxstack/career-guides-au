## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for motorcycle drivers in Estonia?**
A: Motorcycle drivers in Estonia typically earn a gross monthly salary between €1,200 and €1,500, depending on experience, employer, and whether pay is per delivery or hourly. According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for motor vehicle drivers was around €1,400 in recent years. Overtime and tips can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the visa options for working as a motorcycle driver in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a job offer from an Estonian employer to apply for a long-stay (D) visa for employment. The employer must register the position with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund and obtain permission from the Police and Border Guard Board. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and typically does not apply to motorcycle drivers due to skill requirements.

**Q: What are typical working conditions for motorcycle drivers in Estonia?**
A: Motorcycle drivers in Estonia are often employed by food delivery platforms or courier companies. Work can be on a freelance or employment basis, requiring a valid motorcycle license (category A) and sometimes own motorcycle. The job involves navigating city traffic and variable weather, with peak demand during lunch and dinner hours. Self-employed drivers must manage their own taxes.
