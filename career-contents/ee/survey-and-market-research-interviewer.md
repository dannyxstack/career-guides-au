## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for survey and market research interviewers in Estonia?**
A: Survey and market research interviewers in Estonia typically earn between €1,200 and €1,500 gross per month for full-time work, or an hourly rate of €7–€9. Pay varies by agency, project complexity, and experience. Some positions offer performance-based bonuses or per completed interview rates.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as survey interviewers in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can obtain a temporary residence permit for employment or a long-stay D-visa, requiring a job offer and a positive labour market test. The EU Blue Card is only possible if the role qualifies as highly skilled, with a gross monthly salary of at least €1,500 and a relevant higher education degree, which is uncommon for this occupation.

**Q: What language skills are needed for survey and market research interviewer jobs in Estonia?**
A: Fluency in Estonian is essential for most positions, as interviews target local respondents. Russian is also valued for projects requiring Russian-speaking respondents. English alone is rarely sufficient, except for niche international surveys. Basic computer literacy and good communication skills are standard requirements.
