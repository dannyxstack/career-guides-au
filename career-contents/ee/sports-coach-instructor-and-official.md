## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for sports coaches in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, sports coaches earn a gross monthly salary ranging from €1,200 to €2,500, depending on experience, qualifications, and level of sport. Top-level coaches working with professional athletes or national teams may earn significantly more.

**Q: How can non-EU citizens work as sports coaches in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as sports coaches via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, or a temporary residence permit for employment. The process requires a job offer from an Estonian employer, relevant qualifications, and meeting the salary threshold. Specific conditions apply per permit type.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a sports coach in Estonia?**
A: Sports coaches in Estonia typically need a coaching license from a recognized body, such as the Estonian Olympic Committee or respective sports federation. A degree in sports science or physical education is advantageous but not always mandatory. Continuous professional development is required to maintain certification.
