## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Policy Administration Professionals in Estonia?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for Policy Administration Professionals in Estonia ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience, sector, and location. Senior roles in government or large organizations may exceed €4,000. These figures are based on current market data and can vary.

**Q: What visa routes are available for non-EU Policy Administration Professionals to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.2 times Estonian average wage). Alternatively, a long-term (D) visa or temporary residence permit for employment may be obtained if the employer registers the position with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Policy Administration Professional in Estonia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in public administration, political science, law, or a related field is typically required. Many positions prefer a master's degree. Fluency in English is essential, and knowledge of Estonian is often necessary for roles involving local policy. Relevant experience and analytical skills are highly valued.
