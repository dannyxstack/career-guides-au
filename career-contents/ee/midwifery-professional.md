## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwifery professional in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for midwives in Estonia ranges from €1,800 to €2,400, depending on experience and location. Public sector pay is set by collective agreements with additional allowances for night shifts and overtime. Private clinics may offer higher rates.

**Q: What work visa options exist for midwives wishing to immigrate to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU midwives can apply for a long-term employment visa (D-visa) or an EU Blue Card if they have a valid job offer, recognized qualifications, and meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Estonia). Professional qualifications must be recognized by the Estonian Health Board. Applications are submitted via the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are the educational requirements to become a midwife in Estonia?**
A: To practice as a midwife in Estonia, one must complete a Bachelor's degree in midwifery (3.5 years) at a recognized university such as Tallinn University of Technology or the University of Tartu. Registration with the Health Board is mandatory. Continuing education credits are required for license renewal.
