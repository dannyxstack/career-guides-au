## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a telecommunications engineer in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for telecommunications engineers in Estonia ranges from €3,000 to €4,500, depending on experience and company. Senior roles can exceed €5,000. Salaries are competitive within the EU, with bonuses common in larger firms like Telia or Elisa.

**Q: What are the work visa routes for telecommunications engineers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU telecommunications engineers can work in Estonia via an EU Blue Card (requires a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross salary) or a temporary residence permit for employment (requires an employer-sponsored application with proof of qualifications and a job contract). Both routes are official.

**Q: What is the career outlook for telecommunications engineers in Estonia?**
A: Estonia's strong digital infrastructure and telecom sector, led by companies like Telia and Elisa, create steady demand for telecommunications engineers. Opportunities exist in 5G rollout, network security, and IoT. The field offers career progression to senior engineering or management roles, with many engineers moving into cybersecurity or software-defined networking.
