**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Daily floor cleaning and garbage collection

- Simple waste sorting and disposal

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI scheduling system to optimize cleaning routes
- Identify high-pollution areas using sensor data
- Using robots to assist in large-area cleaning
- Report facility issues using a mobile app

**Human moat:**

- Cleaning strategies for complex stains such as grease and bloodstains
- Safe handling of hazardous waste (e.g., needles)
- Etiquette and adaptability for interacting with the public
- Careful work maintaining precision equipment surfaces
- Flexible maneuvering in narrow or confined spaces

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sweepers and related labourers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for cleaners, helpers, and related labourers (ISCO 9613) ranges from approximately €800 to €1,000. This varies by region, employer, and experience. The national minimum wage (as of 2024) is €820 per month, which many in this occupation earn.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for sweeper jobs in Estonia?**
A: Sweepers and related labourers are considered low-skilled occupations. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree or a highly qualified salary threshold. Non-EU nationals may apply for a short-term employment visa for temporary or seasonal work, subject to a labour market test showing no suitable local candidates.

**Q: What are the main duties of sweepers in Estonia?**
A: Sweepers and related labourers in Estonia maintain cleanliness in public areas such as streets, parks, and squares. Tasks include manual sweeping, litter collection, emptying bins, and removing snow in winter. They often work for municipal waste management companies or private cleaning contractors. The job requires physical stamina and the ability to work outdoors in all weather conditions.
