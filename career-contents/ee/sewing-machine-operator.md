## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a sewing machine operator in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sewing machine operators in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced operators in larger textile factories can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and piece-rate pay can increase earnings.

**Q: How can a non-EU sewing machine operator get a work visa for Estonia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment if the employer has registered the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund and no suitable local candidate is found. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable due to salary requirements. Alternatively, a short-term employment visa (up to 365 days) is possible for seasonal work. All applications are processed by the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for sewing machine operators in Estonia?**
A: Sewing machine operators in Estonia can advance to positions such as senior operator, team leader, or quality inspector. With experience, they may move into pattern making or sample production. Some operators specialize in industrial embroidery or upholstery. Continuing education through vocational schools can lead to roles in fashion design or textile technology.
