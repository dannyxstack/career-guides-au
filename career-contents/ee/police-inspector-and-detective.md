## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for police inspectors and detectives in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for police inspectors in Estonia ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on rank, experience, and location. Detectives with specialized skills may earn higher. Salary is determined by the Police and Border Guard Board pay scale, with additional allowances for shift work, overtime, and hazard pay.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for foreign police inspectors and detectives in Estonia?**
A: Foreign nationals can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment if they have a job offer from the Police and Border Guard Board. The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable as police roles require Estonian citizenship or at least permanent residency. Proficiency in Estonian at B2 level or higher is mandatory. Work permits are granted only in exceptional cases, such as for experts in specific fields.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for police inspectors in Estonia?**
A: Police inspectors may advance to senior inspector, chief inspector, or detective superintendent roles. Specialization in areas like cybercrime, forensics, or criminal analysis leads to higher ranks. Further promotions include district police chief or positions within the Police and Border Guard Board headquarters. Continuing education at the Estonian Academy of Security Sciences is often required.
