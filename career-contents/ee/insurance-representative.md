## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for insurance representatives in Estonia?**
A: Insurance representatives in Estonia typically earn between €1,600 and €2,400 gross per month, depending on experience, performance, and company. Many roles also include commission or bonuses, which can significantly increase total compensation. Salaries tend to be higher in larger cities like Tallinn and Tartu.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU citizens to work as an insurance representative in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment or a short-term D visa for work up to one year. For the EU Blue Card, you need a higher education degree (or five years of professional experience) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5 times Estonia's average gross salary). The employer must also register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for insurance representatives in Estonia?**
A: Insurance representatives in Estonia can advance to senior agent, team leader, or branch manager roles. Many pursue further qualifications in insurance or finance. With experience, you may specialise in areas like underwriting or risk assessment. The sector also offers opportunities to move into related fields such as banking or real estate.
