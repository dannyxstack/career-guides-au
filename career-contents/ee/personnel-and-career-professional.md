## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for personnel and careers professionals in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average monthly gross salary for human resources professionals is around €2,200–€3,000, depending on experience and industry. Entry-level positions start at approximately €1,500, while senior roles can exceed €4,000. Bonuses and benefits can also significantly increase total compensation.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for personnel professionals moving to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals need a job offer from an Estonian employer, who must apply for a work permit or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card is for highly skilled workers with a salary at least 1.5 times Estonia's average gross wage. The employer initiates the process; the employee then applies for a D-visa or temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a personnel professional in Estonia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in human resources, psychology, business administration, or a related field is typically required. Professional certifications such as CIPD or SHRM are valued. Fluency in Estonian is often necessary, though some multinational companies accept English. Experience in recruitment or employee relations is highly regarded.
