## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physicists and astronomers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia and industry surveys, the average gross monthly salary for physicists and astronomers ranges from €2,500 to €3,500. Salaries vary by experience and sector: academic researchers may earn less, while those in private R&D or senior positions can earn higher, especially in optics, electronics, and data science.

**Q: What are the official routes for foreign physicists and astronomers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU physicists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross wage. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is available for qualified specialists, processed by the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are the main employment sectors for physicists in Estonia?**
A: Physicists in Estonia primarily work in academia at universities like the University of Tartu and Tallinn University of Technology, or at research institutes such as the National Institute of Chemical Physics and Biophysics. Private sector opportunities exist in optics, electronics, renewable energy, and increasingly in data science and software development.
