**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for agricultural, forestry, and fisheries advisors is approximately 1,800–2,200 EUR, depending on experience, specialization, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around 1,400 EUR, while senior advisers can earn over 2,500 EUR.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for non-EU Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (about 2,600 EUR per month in 2023). Alternatively, a national D-visa for temporary employment is possible for qualified advisers, requiring a valid job offer and relevant qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor’s degree in agriculture, forestry, environmental science, or a related field is required. Practical experience and knowledge of EU and Estonian regulations are valued. Professional certifications, such as those from the Estonian Agricultural Board, can enhance career prospects. Many advisers also pursue master's degrees for specialized roles.
