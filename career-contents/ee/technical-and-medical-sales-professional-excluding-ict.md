## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Technical and Medical Sales Professionals in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, industry, and performance, but the average monthly gross salary for technical and medical sales representatives in Estonia typically ranges from €2,500 to €4,000. Top performers often earn higher bonuses, and starting salaries for less experienced roles may be around €1,800–€2,200.

**Q: What are the official routes to work in Estonia as a Technical or Medical Sales Professional from outside the EU?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card, requiring a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross annual salary (updated annually). Alternatively, a long-term D visa or temporary residence permit for employment may be possible if the employer proves a labor market need. The employer must register the vacancy with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for Technical and Medical Sales roles in Estonia?**
A: Employers typically require a bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as engineering, life sciences, or pharmacy. Previous sales experience, fluency in Estonian and English, and knowledge of the local market are often essential. Professional certifications in sales or specific product areas can be advantageous.
