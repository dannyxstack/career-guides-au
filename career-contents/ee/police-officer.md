## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for police officers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for police officers in Estonia ranges from €1,800 to €2,200, depending on rank, experience, and additional allowances for overtime, night shifts, or specialization. Senior officers or those in specialized units may earn higher.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as police officers in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Police officer positions in Estonia typically require Estonian citizenship or permanent residency. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as police work is not considered highly skilled employment under the scheme. Non-EU citizens may obtain a work visa for specific support roles, but recruitment for sworn officers is generally limited to EU/EEA nationals.

**Q: What career development opportunities are available for police officers in Estonia?**
A: Police officers in Estonia can advance through ranks from constable to senior officer and specialize in areas such as criminal investigation, traffic, or border patrol. The Estonian Police and Border Guard Board provides continuous training and supports higher education in law or public administration, enabling career progression into leadership or specialist roles.
