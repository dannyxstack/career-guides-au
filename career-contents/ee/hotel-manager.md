## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Hotel Manager in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for hotel managers in Estonia ranges from approximately €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience, location, and hotel size. Tallinn generally offers higher pay. Bonuses and performance incentives are common, especially in larger hotel chains.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for a foreign Hotel Manager in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as hotel managers in Estonia via a long-stay (D) visa for employment or an EU Blue Card. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a valid job offer, higher education degree, and salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (€1,605 in 2025). An employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund before hiring a third-country national.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are typically required for a Hotel Manager position in Estonia?**
A: Most hotel managers in Estonia hold a bachelor's degree in hospitality management or a related field, plus relevant work experience (usually 3–5 years) in hotel operations. Fluency in English is essential, and knowledge of Estonian or Russian is a strong advantage. Practical skills in budgeting, staff management, and customer service are crucial.
