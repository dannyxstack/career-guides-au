## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a secretary in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a secretary (general) in Estonia is approximately €1,200–€1,400. This can vary based on experience, location, and industry. Tallinn and larger companies may offer higher pay. Income tax and social contributions apply.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a secretary in Estonia and what visa is needed?**
A: Yes, but a work visa or residence permit for employment is required. The employer must apply and typically demonstrate a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not suitable for secretaries due to the high salary threshold (about €40,000 annually in 2024). The standard D-visa or long-stay visa for employment is the common route.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for secretaries in Estonia?**
A: Secretaries can advance to executive assistant, office manager, or administrative specialist roles. With additional training, they may move into human resources, project coordination, or customer service management. Proficiency in Estonian and English, plus digital skills, significantly enhance prospects.
