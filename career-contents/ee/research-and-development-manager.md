## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Research and Development Managers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia and recent salary surveys, the gross monthly salary for R&D managers typically ranges from €3,500 to €5,500, depending on experience, industry, and company size. Senior positions in ICT and biotechnology may exceed €6,000.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Research and Development Managers in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €1.5 times the average annual gross wage). The employer may also apply for a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: Which industries in Estonia employ the most Research and Development Managers?**
A: Key industries include information and communication technology (ICT), biotechnology, renewable energy, and engineering. Estonia's strong startup ecosystem and e-governance sector also drive demand for R&D managers, particularly in Tallinn and Tartu.
