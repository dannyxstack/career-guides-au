## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for other arts teachers in Estonia?**
A: Other arts teachers in Estonia earn between €1,500 and €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, institution type, and location. Salaries in Tallinn and for higher education positions tend to be at the upper end. Freelance rates vary by lesson type and duration.

**Q: Can foreign arts teachers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to teach in Estonia?**
A: Yes, non-EU arts teachers can work in Estonia via a work visa (D visa) or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian wage. An employer must apply for a work permit through the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to teach arts in Estonia?**
A: A bachelor's or master's degree in the relevant art field is typically required. For formal education settings, a teaching qualification or pedagogical training may be needed. For private lessons, professional experience and a strong portfolio can suffice. Language proficiency in Estonian or English is often expected.
