## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for nursing professionals in Estonia?**
A: Gross monthly salary for nurses typically ranges from 1,500 to 2,000 EUR, depending on experience, specialisation, and workplace. Hospital nurses with seniority may earn up to 2,500 EUR. Additional pay for night shifts, overtime, and on-call duties is common. The average Estonian gross wage is around 1,700 EUR, so nursing salaries are competitive.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU nursing professionals to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU nurses can apply for a long-term (D) visa or an EU Blue Card. For the Blue Card, a valid employment contract with salary meeting the national threshold (at least 1.5 times average gross wage for shortage occupations) is required. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible if the employer obtains a labour market test approval from the Estonian Labour Board.

**Q: What career development opportunities exist for nursing professionals in Estonia?**
A: Nurses can pursue specialisations in intensive care, emergency, paediatrics, or mental health. Advanced degrees (master's, PhD) are offered at the University of Tartu and Tallinn Health Care College. Experienced nurses may become clinical preceptors, nurse managers, or lecturers. The Estonian health system supports continuing professional development through workshops and conferences.
