## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Community Health Workers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for Community Health Workers in Estonia vary by experience and employer, typically ranging from €1,400 to €2,200 gross per month. According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for health associate professionals is around €1,800, with higher pay in larger cities like Tallinn.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for Community Health Workers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU Community Health Workers can obtain a work visa (long-stay D visa) or an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and meet the salary threshold (approx. €38,000/year). The employer must register the position with the Unemployment Insurance Fund and apply for a residence permit for employment via the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Community Health Workers in Estonia?**
A: Community Health Workers can progress to supervisory roles, team leadership, or specialize in areas like elderly care or public health. Many pursue further education to become registered nurses or health promotion specialists. The Estonian health system supports continuous professional development through training programs and qualification upgrades.
