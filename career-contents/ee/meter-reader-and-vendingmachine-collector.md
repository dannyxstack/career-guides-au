## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for this occupation in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions may start around minimum wage (€820 gross in 2024), while experienced collectors in urban areas can earn up to €1,700. Overtime and bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as meter readers or vending-machine collectors in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a temporary residence permit for employment, sponsored by an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card is unlikely as its salary threshold (€3,210 gross/month in 2024) exceeds typical pay for this role. A regular work permit requires the employer to prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. No specific shortage designation exists for this occupation.

**Q: What are the main duties of meter readers and vending-machine collectors in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, meter readers travel to residential and commercial sites to record utility usage (electricity, water, gas) using handheld devices. Vending-machine collectors restock products, collect cash, and perform basic maintenance. The role requires driving for field visits, accuracy in data entry, and occasional customer interaction. Work is often outdoors or in machine locations.
