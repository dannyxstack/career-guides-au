## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an accounting and bookkeeping clerk in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for bookkeeping and accounting clerks is around €1,300. Entry-level positions may start near €1,000, while experienced clerks can earn up to €1,600. Salaries vary by location, with Tallinn typically offering higher pay.

**Q: What visa options are available for foreign accounting clerks to work in Estonia?**
A: Accounting clerks can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment if they have a job offer from an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card is another route but requires a higher salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary), which may exceed typical clerk pay. Both routes require a valid work contract and meeting legal conditions.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for accounting clerk positions in Estonia?**
A: Employers usually require at least secondary education with a focus on accounting or finance, and often a vocational diploma or bachelor's degree in accounting. Proficiency in Estonian is essential for most roles, though some international companies may accept English. Relevant software skills (e.g., SAP, ERP) and prior experience are highly valued.
