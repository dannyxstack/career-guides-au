## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technician in Estonia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries typically range between €1,500 and €2,200, depending on experience, qualifications, and the healthcare facility. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total earnings. Public sector pay is standardized, while private clinics may offer higher rates.

**Q: Can foreign Medical Imaging Technicians work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. The EU Blue Card requires a valid job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average (approx. €33,000 in 2024). This occupation usually qualifies. Applicants need a recognized higher education diploma or equivalent professional experience. The employer must demonstrate that the vacancy could not be filled locally. The Blue Card grants temporary residence and work rights.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for this profession in Estonia?**
A: Technicians can progress to senior or lead radiographer roles, specialize in MRI, CT, or nuclear medicine, or move into equipment management, training, or quality assurance. With additional education, some transition to radiologist assistant positions or healthcare management. Continuous professional development is encouraged and often supported by employers.
