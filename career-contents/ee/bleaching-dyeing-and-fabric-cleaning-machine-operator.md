## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary by employer, experience, and location. Average monthly gross earnings for textile machine operators in Estonia range around €1,200–€1,600. Entry-level pay may start near the national minimum wage (€725 in 2024). Collective agreements or company policies can influence actual compensation.

**Q: What work visa options exist for non-EU citizens seeking this job in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically require a temporary residence permit for employment or an EU Blue Card. The employer must first register a job vacancy with Estonia's Unemployment Insurance Fund. The Blue Card requires a higher education (or equivalent) and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary (approx. €2,615 in 2024). Without a degree, a standard work permit is possible if the employer justifies the need.

**Q: How is automation affecting the role of bleaching, dyeing and fabric cleaning machine operators in Estonia?**
A: Automation is gradually being adopted in Estonian textile manufacturing, with some processes becoming computer-controlled. However, many operations still require manual oversight. Operators who upskill in digital control systems and maintenance experience better job security. Estonia's strong IT sector may accelerate automation, but demand for skilled operators remains steady in textile hubs like Narva.
