## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for tailors and dressmakers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for tailors and dressmakers in Estonia vary by experience and employer. Entry-level positions may earn around €1,200 gross per month, while experienced professionals can earn up to €1,800. The average monthly salary in this occupation is approximately €1,500 before tax, according to national statistics.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as tailors in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a highly qualified job with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€2,187 in 2024). Tailor positions rarely meet this threshold. Non-EU citizens typically need a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer and employer application to the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a tailor in Estonia?**
A: Formal vocational training in garment making or tailoring is offered at institutions like the Tallinn School of Services. Apprenticeships are also common. While not always mandatory, a certificate or diploma improves job prospects. Continuing education and specialization in areas like fur or hats can advance a career.
