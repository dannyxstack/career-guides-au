## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for weaving and knitting machine operators in Estonia is around €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and shift work. Entry-level positions start lower, while experienced operators in larger textile firms may earn up to €1,800. Salaries are generally in line with the Estonian manufacturing sector average.

**Q: Can a foreign weaving or knitting machine operator obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: Yes, foreign workers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least €1,428.83 gross monthly). Alternatively, a standard work visa (D visa) is available for skilled workers without a degree, provided the employer obtains a labour market test from the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What qualifications or training are needed to become a weaving or knitting machine operator in Estonia?**
A: Most employers in Estonia require at least a vocational secondary education in textile technology or a related field. On-the-job training is common, but formal programmes at vocational schools, such as the Tallinn University of Applied Sciences, offer courses in textile machinery. Basic knowledge of machine maintenance and quality control is also expected.
