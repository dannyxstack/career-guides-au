## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for announcers in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for announcers in Estonia ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience, employer, and media type. Public broadcasters like ERR may offer slightly higher pay, while private stations often provide additional performance bonuses. Entry-level positions typically start around €1,200.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for announcers moving to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU announcers can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified positions) or a long-term (D) visa for employment. A short-term (C) visa is also possible for temporary assignments. Employers must register the job with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund. Relevant qualifications and experience are required.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an announcer in Estonia?**
A: A degree in media, communications, or journalism is common but not mandatory. Fluency in Estonian or Russian (depending on the target audience) is essential. Practical experience through internships at broadcasters like ERR or Radio Sky Plus is highly valued. Voice training and technical skills in sound editing are advantageous.
