## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by role. For security guards, average monthly gross pay is €1,200–€1,600. Private investigators can earn €1,500–€2,000. Experience, employer, and specific duties influence earnings. Overtime and night shifts may add bonuses.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU protective services workers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens generally need a temporary residence permit for employment. For most protective service roles (e.g., security guard), a long-stay D-visa or residence permit based on a job offer applies. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers (university degree + higher salary threshold) and may be less relevant for this category. Always apply via the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for protective services jobs in Estonia?**
A: Requirements depend on the role. Security guards need a security license (involving a background check and training). Private investigators may require relevant experience or certification. Many positions require Estonian language skills. Vocational training or on-the-job training is common. Check specific employer requirements.
