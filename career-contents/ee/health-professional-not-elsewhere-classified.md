## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Health Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for health professionals in this category in Estonia ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, depending on experience and sector. Public sector roles often pay less than private clinics. According to Statistics Estonia, the median monthly gross wage for health professionals was approximately €2,400 in 2023.

**Q: What visa options are available for health professionals to work in Estonia?**
A: Health professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average gross annual salary (approx. €31,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a long-term D visa for employment is possible. All applicants must have their qualifications recognised by the Estonian Health Board.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a health professional not elsewhere classified in Estonia?**
A: You need a recognised degree in a relevant health field and must register with the Estonian Health Board. For most roles, fluency in Estonian is required, though some international clinics may accept English. Additional specialisation or licensing may be needed depending on the specific profession.
