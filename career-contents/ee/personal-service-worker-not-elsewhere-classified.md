## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for personal services workers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary by specific role and experience. As of 2024, the average monthly gross salary in Estonia's personal services sector is approximately €1,200–€1,600. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while experienced workers can earn up to €2,000. Note that these figures are indicative and subject to change.

**Q: How can I obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a personal services worker in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a residence permit for employment. The employer must register the job with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified roles requiring higher education and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€2,082 gross/month in 2024). For lower-skilled jobs, a standard long-stay D visa may apply if a labour shortage is proven.

**Q: What qualifications are needed for personal services jobs in Estonia?**
A: Requirements depend on the specific occupation. Many roles require vocational training or a certificate in the relevant field, such as hairdressing or beauty therapy. Language skills in Estonian or English are often necessary. Some positions may accept relevant work experience instead of formal education. Always confirm with the employer.
