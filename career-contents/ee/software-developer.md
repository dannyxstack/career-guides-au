## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for software developers in Estonia vary by experience and location. Junior developers earn around €2,000–€3,000 gross monthly, mid-level €3,500–€5,000, and seniors €5,000–€7,000 or more. Tallinn offers higher pay due to concentration of tech firms. Stock options and bonuses may add 10–20%.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for software developers moving to Estonia?**
A: Software developers can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card (for high-skilled jobs with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average), a D visa (long-stay for employment), or a short-term employment visa (up to 365 days). A work permit is required for non-EU citizens; the employer must register the position with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund first.

**Q: What career prospects exist for software developers in Estonia?**
A: Estonia has a thriving tech ecosystem with over 1,500 startups, including unicorns like Bolt and Wise. Developers can advance to senior, lead, or architect roles, or transition into product management. Continuous learning is supported by coding bootcamps and meetups. Remote work is common, and many companies sponsor professional certifications.
