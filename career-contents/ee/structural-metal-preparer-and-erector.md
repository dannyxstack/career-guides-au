## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for structural metal preparers and erectors in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for structural metal workers (ISCO-08 7214) ranges from €1,600 to €2,000. Experienced professionals in high-demand areas may earn up to €2,500. Salaries vary by location, with higher pay in Tallinn and major industrial centres.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for structural metal preparers and erectors in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a job offer from an Estonian employer to apply for a temporary residence permit for employment. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree (or equivalent professional experience of at least 5 years) and a salary meeting the national threshold (1.5 times the average gross annual wage). No specific shortage list status for this occupation.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for structural metal preparers and erectors in Estonia?**
A: With experience, workers can advance to team leader, site supervisor, or welding inspector roles. Additional vocational certifications from Estonian authorities (e.g., in advanced welding or blueprint reading) boost employability and pay. Some professionals move into project management or start their own contracting businesses.
