## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for bank tellers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for cashiers and related clerks was around €1,200–€1,500 in 2023. Bank tellers specifically earn between €1,300 and €1,800 per month before taxes, depending on experience and employer.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for bank tellers in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a temporary residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average annual wage (≈€25,000 in 2023), which bank tellers typically do not meet. Instead, employers must register the vacancy and demonstrate no suitable local candidates.

**Q: What career progression opportunities are available for bank tellers in Estonia?**
A: Bank tellers can advance to senior teller, customer service representative, or branch operations roles. With further education, they may move into financial advisory, loan processing, or back-office positions. Major Estonian banks (e.g., Swedbank, SEB) offer internal training and support for professional certifications.
