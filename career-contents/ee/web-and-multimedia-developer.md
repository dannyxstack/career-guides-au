## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for web and multimedia developers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for web and multimedia developers in Estonia ranges from €2,500 for junior roles to €4,000 for senior positions. Salaries vary based on experience, location (Tallinn typically pays higher), and company size. The tech sector offers competitive compensation, often with additional benefits like health insurance.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for web developers moving to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU web developers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree or five years of professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5x average gross monthly salary, currently about €2,100). Alternatively, a short-term D visa allows work for up to 365 days. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What skills are in high demand for web developers in Estonia's tech scene?**
A: Estonia's tech sector values full-stack skills, especially in JavaScript (React, Node.js), Python, and PHP. Cloud platforms (AWS, Google Cloud), mobile development (React Native, Flutter), and cybersecurity awareness are also sought. Proficiency in English is essential; Estonian language skills are a bonus but not mandatory for most tech roles.
