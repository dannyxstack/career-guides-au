## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a hand packer in Estonia?**
A: Hand packers in Estonia typically earn between €700 and €1,200 gross per month, depending on experience, location, and employer. The average hourly wage ranges from €4.50 to €6.50. Overtime and shift work may increase pay. Most positions are full-time, with some offering benefits such as meal allowances.

**Q: What work visa options are available for hand packers moving to Estonia?**
A: Non-EU hand packers may apply for a short-term employment visa (D visa) valid up to 365 days, or a long-term (D) visa for seasonal work. The EU Blue Card requires higher education or 5+ years of experience, rare for this role. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible if the employer obtains a labor market test approval from the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for hand packers in Estonia?**
A: Hand packers can advance to team leader, quality control inspector, or warehouse supervisor with experience. Some move into logistics coordination or machine operation. Employers often provide on-the-job training. Knowledge of Estonian or Russian can improve prospects. The role is common in food production, logistics, and manufacturing companies across Estonia.
