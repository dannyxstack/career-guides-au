## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for messengers, package deliverers, and luggage porters in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for messengers, package deliverers, and luggage porters in Estonia is around €1,200 to €1,500, depending on employer and location. Entry-level positions may start at minimum wage (€820 per month in 2024), while experienced workers can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and tips may supplement income.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU citizens to work as a messenger or package deliverer in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a standard work visa (D visa) and a residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable because it requires higher education or high-skilled work. For low-skilled roles like messengers, employers must obtain a labour market test, proving no suitable local worker is available. The application is processed through the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for messengers and package deliverers in Estonia?**
A: With experience, messengers and package deliverers can advance to supervisory roles like dispatch coordinator or logistics team leader. Further opportunities include moving into courier company management, starting a small delivery business, or gaining certifications for specialized transport (e.g., hazardous materials). Additional language skills and driving licenses can enhance prospects.
