## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for sales workers not elsewhere classified in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for sales workers in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced professionals in specialized sales can earn up to €2,500. Commission and bonuses often supplement base pay.

**Q: What work visa options exist for sales workers not elsewhere classified to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU sales workers can apply for a short-term employment visa or a long-term residence permit for employment. If offered a highly qualified position with a salary at least 1.5 times the Estonian average (€1,884 gross/month in 2024), they may qualify for an EU Blue Card. Other routes include intra-company transfer or a start-up visa if self-employed.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions for sales workers in Estonia?**
A: Sales workers in Estonia often work in retail, telemarketing, or business-to-business sales. Hours may include weekends and shifts. Estonian employers value language skills; Estonian and Russian are common, while English is important for export roles. Career progression can lead to sales management or account executive positions.
