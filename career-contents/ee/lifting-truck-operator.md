## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for lifting truck operators in Estonia?**
A: As of 2024, lifting truck operators in Estonia earn an average gross monthly salary between €1,300 and €1,700, depending on experience and employer. Hourly rates range from €7 to €9. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total pay. Salaries are higher in industrial hubs like Tallinn.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as lifting truck operators in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary (approx. €2,100/month in 2024), which lifting truck operator roles typically do not meet. Instead, non-EU workers can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, provided the job cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to operate lifting trucks in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, lifting truck operators must hold a valid operator’s license (tõstuki juhiluba) issued by the Estonian Labour Inspectorate. Training courses cover safety, load handling, and machine controls. The license is valid for five years. Prior experience is often preferred but not mandatory; employers may provide on-the-job training.
