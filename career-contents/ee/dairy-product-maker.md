## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for dairy products makers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the average monthly gross salary for food products makers (including dairy) is approximately €1,200–€1,500. Entry-level earners may start around €1,000, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,800. Actual pay depends on the employer, region, and specific role.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as dairy products makers in Estonia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU dairy products makers can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (or equivalent professional experience) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €2,100 monthly, 1.5 times average). Alternatively, a standard temporary residence permit for employment may be available for lower-skilled roles if the employer demonstrates a labor shortage.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a dairy products maker in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a vocational education in food technology or dairy processing is beneficial, with programs offered at schools like Väike-Maarja or Olustvere. On-the-job training is common. Employers value experience in food hygiene, quality control, and knowledge of dairy production processes.
