## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physiotherapists in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for physiotherapists in Estonia ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and work setting. Private practice may offer higher pay, while public healthcare salaries are typically at the lower end. Overtime and bonuses can increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU physiotherapists to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Estonia via a long-term (D) visa for employment or an EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary. The employer must obtain a work permit or register short-term employment.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to practice physiotherapy in Estonia?**
A: Physiotherapists need a recognized higher education degree in physiotherapy. Non-EU qualifications must be assessed by the Estonian Health Board for equivalency. Proficiency in Estonian is typically required for patient communication, though some private clinics may accept English speakers.
