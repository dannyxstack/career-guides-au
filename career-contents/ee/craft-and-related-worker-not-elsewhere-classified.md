**Q: What is the typical salary for Craft and Related Workers Not Elsewhere Classified in Estonia?**

**Q: Can a foreign national obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for this occupation in Estonia?**
A: Yes, foreign workers can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally. For the EU Blue Card, the job requires higher professional qualification (e.g., higher education or 5+ years experience) and a salary at least 1.5 times Estonia’s average gross annual salary (€12,324 as of 2023).
