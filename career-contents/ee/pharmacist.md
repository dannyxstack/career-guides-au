## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pharmacist in Estonia?**
A: Pharmacist salaries in Estonia vary by experience and sector. The average gross monthly salary ranges from €2,000 to €3,500, with higher pay in hospitals or managerial roles. Entry-level positions start around €1,800, while experienced pharmacists in leadership can earn over €4,000. Salaries are subject to income tax and social contributions.

**Q: How can non-EU pharmacists obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: Non-EU pharmacists must secure a job offer from an Estonian employer. For a work visa, the employer registers the position with the Unemployment Insurance Fund. The EU Blue Card requires a recognized pharmacy degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (€2,244 in 2024). Applications are submitted to the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements for pharmacists in Estonia?**
A: Pharmacists need a university degree in pharmacy recognized by the Estonian Health Board. Non-EU diplomas may require additional courses or exams. Registration with the State Agency of Medicines is mandatory. Proficiency in Estonian is typically required for patient interaction. License renewal involves continuing professional education every five years.
