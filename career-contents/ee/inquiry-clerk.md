## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for inquiry clerks in Estonia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for inquiry clerks in Estonia typically ranges from €1,300 to €1,900, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,200, while experienced clerks in larger cities like Tallinn can earn up to €2,000. Bonuses and benefits vary by company.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU inquiry clerks in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a job offer and employer-sponsored residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times Estonia's average gross salary (€2,700+ in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit has no specific salary threshold but requires a labor market test. Both routes lead to temporary residence.

**Q: What are the main duties of an inquiry clerk in Estonia?**
A: Inquiry clerks in Estonia handle customer inquiries via phone, email, or chat, providing information on products, services, or procedures. They record requests, resolve complaints, and forward complex issues to specialists. Clerks often work in call centers, government offices, or service companies, requiring strong communication skills and basic computer proficiency.
