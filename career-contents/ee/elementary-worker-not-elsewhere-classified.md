## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for elementary workers not elsewhere classified in Estonia?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and location. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is €820 per month. Many elementary roles, such as cleaners or kitchen helpers, pay between €800 and €1,100 monthly. Some positions in construction or logistics may offer higher wages. For precise figures, consult job advertisements or Statistics Estonia data.

**Q: Can an elementary worker obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Estonia?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary above the threshold, so it is generally not available for elementary workers. However, a temporary residence permit for employment is possible if an Estonian employer sponsors the worker and demonstrates a labor market need. The employer must prove that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available.

**Q: What are typical job titles or career paths for elementary workers in Estonia?**
A: Common job titles include cleaner, packer, laborer, street vendor, and kitchen assistant. These roles require no formal education and are found in retail, hospitality, and construction. Career advancement often involves gaining specific skills or language proficiency, leading to supervisory roles or specialized positions like forklift operator or head cleaner.
