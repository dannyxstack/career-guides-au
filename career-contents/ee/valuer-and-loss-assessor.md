## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for valuers and loss assessors in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for valuers and loss assessors in Estonia ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and qualifications. Entry-level positions may start around €1,400, while senior professionals can earn over €3,000. Salaries are often supplemented with performance bonuses.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options are available for valuers and loss assessors moving to Estonia?**
A: Qualified valuers and loss assessors can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently €1,668.70 gross monthly in 2024). Alternatively, a long-term D visa for employment is available for specific positions. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What are the career prospects for valuers and loss assessors in Estonia?**
A: Career prospects are positive due to growing real estate and insurance markets. Professionals can advance to senior appraiser, team leader, or open their own valuation firm. Certification through the Estonian Association of Appraisers or similar bodies is beneficial for credibility and higher pay. Ongoing training in EU valuation standards is recommended.
