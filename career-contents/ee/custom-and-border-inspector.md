## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Customs and Border Inspectors in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for customs and border inspectors in Estonia is approximately €1,800 to €2,400, depending on experience and specific duties. Entry-level positions start around €1,500, while senior inspectors can earn up to €3,000. Salaries are determined by collective agreements and public sector pay scales, with additional allowances for shift work and overtime.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for joining this profession in Estonia?**
A: To work as a customs or border inspector in Estonia, non-EU citizens typically need a long-term employment visa (D-visa) or an EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary). Additionally, employers must obtain a work permit from the Police and Border Guard Board. Citizenship or permanent residency may be required for certain positions due to security clearance.

**Q: What are the main duties of customs and border inspectors in Estonia?**
A: Customs and border inspectors in Estonia control the movement of goods and people across borders, enforce customs regulations, prevent smuggling, and collect duties. They inspect documents, luggage, and cargo using x-ray scanners and physical checks. They also work at border checkpoints, airports, and seaports, cooperating with the Tax and Customs Board and the Police and Border Guard Board.
