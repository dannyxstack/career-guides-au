## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Information and Communications Technology Sales Professionals in Estonia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for ICT sales professionals in Estonia typically range from €2,500 to €5,000, with senior roles or those earning commission reaching over €6,000. Entry-level positions start around €1,800–€2,000. Figures are based on market data and vary by experience, company size, and performance.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for ICT Sales Professionals to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card or a long-term (D) visa for employment. Requirements include a valid job offer, a salary at least 1.2 times the average Estonian salary (approx. €1,800/month in 2024), and relevant qualifications. Applications are processed by the Police and Border Guard Board.

**Q: What is the typical career progression for ICT Sales Professionals in Estonia?**
A: Career paths often progress from Sales Representative to Key Account Manager, then to Sales Director or Head of Sales. Some transition into product management or business development. Building expertise in cloud, cybersecurity, or SaaS and obtaining certifications like AWS or Cisco can accelerate advancement. Estonian e-residency also supports entrepreneurial sales ventures.
