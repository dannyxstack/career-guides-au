## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Government Regulatory Associate Professionals in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for this role in Estonia typically range from €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month, depending on experience, sector, and specific agency. The median gross salary is around €1,800. Public sector positions may include additional benefits such as health insurance and pension contributions.

**Q: What are the work visa options for non-EU professionals in this occupation in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the qualifying salary threshold. Alternatively, a long-term D visa is available for employment exceeding one year. The employer must register the vacancy with the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund if recruiting from outside the EU/EEA.

**Q: What are the typical duties of a Government Regulatory Associate Professional in Estonia?**
A: They assist in developing and enforcing regulations in sectors like energy, transport, and finance. Tasks include conducting inspections, processing license applications, monitoring compliance, and preparing reports. They work under senior officials and collaborate with stakeholders to implement policies.
