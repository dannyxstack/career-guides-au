## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Estonia?**
A: The typical gross monthly salary for gardeners in Estonia ranges from €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while experienced gardeners can earn up to €2,000 or more, especially in high-end landscaping or horticulture.

**Q: Can foreign gardeners work in Estonia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Foreign gardeners typically need a work visa (D visa) sponsored by an Estonian employer. The EU Blue Card is only for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a higher salary threshold, which gardening may not meet. The employer must prove no suitable local candidate is available.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for gardeners in Estonia?**
A: Gardeners can advance to senior gardener, landscape manager, or horticultural technician roles by gaining experience and certifications. Some pursue further education in landscape architecture or agronomy. Opportunities exist in public parks, private estates, or starting a landscaping business.
