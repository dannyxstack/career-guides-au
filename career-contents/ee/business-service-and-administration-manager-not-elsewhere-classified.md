## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services and Administration Managers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the median gross monthly salary for managers in business services and administration ranges from €3,500 to €4,500, with senior roles exceeding €6,000. Salaries vary by experience, company size, and location, with higher pay typically in Tallinn and for specialized skills.

**Q: How can a foreign national work as a Business Services and Administration Manager in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens usually need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The employer must register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund. For the EU Blue Card, the job must be highly qualified with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Estonian salary (approximately €2,100 gross/month in 2024). A short-term D-visa for up to 365 days is also an option.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for Business Services and Administration Managers in Estonia?**
A: Employers typically require a bachelor's degree in business administration or a related field, plus several years of managerial experience. Proficiency in English is essential; Estonian language skills are beneficial but not mandatory in many international firms. Professional certifications, such as PMP, can also enhance prospects.
