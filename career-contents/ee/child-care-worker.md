## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for child care workers in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for child care workers in Estonia is between €1,000 and €1,300, depending on experience, region, and employer. Public-sector workers may earn slightly less, while private care centers may offer higher pay. This is lower than the national average wage.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU child care workers to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment after the employer registers the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund. The EU Blue Card is typically not available for this occupation due to the low salary, which usually does not meet the threshold (1.5 times the average Estonian salary).

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a child care worker in Estonia?**
A: A secondary education is typically required, along with a vocational qualification in child care (e.g., from a vocational school). Many employers also require first aid training and a clean criminal record. Experience with children is highly valued. Registration with the Estonian Health Board may be needed for certain roles.
