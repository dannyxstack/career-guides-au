## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Professional Services Managers in Estonia?**
A: According to Statistics Estonia, the median gross monthly salary for managers in professional, scientific and technical activities is around €4,500. Salaries vary by sector and experience, with top earners in management consulting and legal services often exceeding €6,500 per month.

**Q: What are the visa options for Professional Services Managers moving to Estonia from outside the EU?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (or 5 years experience) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 1.5 times Estonia's average gross salary, updated annually). Alternatively, the national D visa for long-term employment may be used; both require employer sponsorship and residence registration.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Professional Services Managers in Estonia?**
A: Estonia's dynamic business environment, especially in technology and consulting, offers pathways to senior leadership roles such as Partner or Director. Many professionals also transition to entrepreneurship or C-suite positions in multinationals. Continuous learning in digital transformation and EU regulations can accelerate advancement.
