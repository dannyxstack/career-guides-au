## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for telecommunications engineering technicians in Estonia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for telecommunications engineering technicians in Estonia ranges from €1,800 to €2,500, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,400, while senior technicians can earn over €3,000. Salaries are competitive within Estonia's ICT sector.

**Q: What visa routes are available for non-EU telecommunications engineering technicians to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Estonia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a job offer, relevant qualifications, and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a long-term D visa for employment is possible. The employer must first register the vacancy with the Unemployment Insurance Fund.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for telecommunications engineering technicians in Estonia?**
A: Telecommunications engineering technicians are in high demand in Estonia due to 5G expansion and digital infrastructure projects. Major employers include telecom operators like Telia, Elisa, and Tele2, as well as IT service companies. Career progression can lead to roles such as senior technician, network specialist, or engineering manager.
