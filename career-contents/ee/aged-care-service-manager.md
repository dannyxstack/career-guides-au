## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Estonia?**
A: Salaries for Aged Care Services Managers in Estonia vary by experience, facility size, and location. The gross monthly salary typically ranges between 2,500 and 3,500 EUR. According to Estonian labour statistics, managers in human health and social work activities earned an average of around 2,800 EUR gross per month in 2023.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for an Aged Care Services Manager to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross wage). Alternatively, a national D-visa for long-term employment is available for those who do not qualify for the Blue Card. Work permit sponsorship by the employer is required for both routes.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an Aged Care Services Manager in Estonia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in social work, healthcare management, nursing, or a related field is required. Many employers prefer a master's degree. Relevant work experience in aged care, leadership skills, and knowledge of Estonian regulations are essential. Professional certification in social care management is advantageous but not mandatory.
