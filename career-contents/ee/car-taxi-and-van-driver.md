## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Estonia?**
A: In Estonia, the average gross monthly salary for car, taxi, and van drivers ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, according to Statistics Estonia. Taxi drivers often earn per trip or commission, while van drivers may receive a fixed salary. Actual earnings depend on hours worked, location (e.g., Tallinn vs. rural areas), and employer.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as drivers in Estonia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as drivers via an employment-based long-stay D visa or temporary residence permit for work, requiring employer sponsorship and a labour market test proving no suitable EU/EEA candidate. The EU Blue Card rarely applies, as its salary threshold (€1,459/month in 2023) typically exceeds average driver pay, and the profession usually requires no higher education.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a taxi driver in Estonia?**
A: To be a taxi driver in Estonia, you need a valid B-category driver’s license, a taxi driver permit (taksojuhtimise luba) from the local municipality, and a clean criminal record. Additional requirements may include a health certificate. For van driving, a C1 or C license is needed if the vehicle exceeds 3.5 tonnes. No formal education is required, but customer service skills are valued.
