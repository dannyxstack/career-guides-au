## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for shop sales assistants in Estonia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for shop sales assistants in Estonia typically ranges from 1,300 to 1,600 euros, depending on experience and location. The national minimum wage is 820 euros per month (2024), but many employers offer higher pay, especially in larger cities like Tallinn.

**Q: Can a foreigner get a work visa or EU Blue Card to work as a shop sales assistant in Estonia?**
A: The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average, which is usually unattainable for shop assistants. However, a standard temporary residence permit for employment is possible if an Estonian employer offers a job that meets the local labor market needs and salary threshold (currently at least 1.24 times the national average wage).

**Q: What language skills are needed to work as a shop sales assistant in Estonia?**
A: Estonian language proficiency at least at B1 level is typically required for direct customer interaction. In some tourist areas or international stores, English may suffice, but basic Estonian is often expected for daily operations. Knowledge of Russian can also be beneficial in certain regions.
