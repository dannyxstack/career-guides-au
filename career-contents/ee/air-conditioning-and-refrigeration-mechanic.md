## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Estonia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Estonia ranges from €1,400 to €2,000, depending on experience and employer. Experienced specialists or those with supervisory roles may earn up to €2,500. Overtime and on-call work can increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics to work in Estonia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least €2,184 monthly gross). Alternatively, a national D visa for long-term employment is possible for those with vocational qualifications, provided the employer proves the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Estonia?**
A: Typically, employers require a vocational secondary education in refrigeration or air conditioning, or relevant professional certification. Practical experience and knowledge of Estonian standards (e.g., handling refrigerants) are essential. Registration with the Estonian Environmental Board may be needed for handling fluorinated greenhouse gases.
