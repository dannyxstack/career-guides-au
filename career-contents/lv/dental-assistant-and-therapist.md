## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a dental assistant or therapist in Latvia?**
A: As of 2023, the average gross monthly salary for dental assistants and therapists in Latvia ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Public sector positions may be lower, while private clinics typically offer higher pay. Entry-level assistants earn around minimum wage, while experienced therapists can earn up to €1,500 or more in specialized practices.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for foreign dental assistants and therapists to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals can work in Latvia as dental assistants or therapists via the EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers) or a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must obtain a work permit approval from the State Employment Agency. Requirements include a valid employment contract, recognized qualifications, and proof of health insurance. EU citizens have free movement and do not need a work visa.

**Q: What qualifications and duties are required for dental assistants and therapists in Latvia?**
A: Dental assistants in Latvia typically need a secondary education and on-the-job training. Dental therapists require a professional bachelor’s degree in oral hygiene or dental therapy (3 years). Duties include patient preparation, infection control, assisting during procedures, taking X-rays, and providing preventive care. Licensure is mandatory for therapists, overseen by the Health Inspectorate. Continuing education is required for license renewal.
