## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for beauticians in Latvia?**
A: The average monthly salary for beauticians in Latvia ranges from €700 to €1,200 gross, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions typically pay around the minimum wage (€700 in 2024), while experienced professionals in Riga can earn higher. Self-employed beauticians may have variable income.

**Q: Can beauticians work in Latvia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Beauticians can obtain a Latvian work visa if an employer sponsors them and proves that the position cannot be filled locally. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (€2,157 monthly in 2024), which is rarely met in this occupation. Therefore, the standard work visa is the most feasible route.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a beautician in Latvia?**
A: To work as a beautician in Latvia, you typically need a vocational education certificate in cosmetology or a related field, obtained from a recognized school. Licensing from the Health Inspectorate may be required for certain services like tattooing or microneedling. Additional courses in hygiene and safety are often mandatory.
