## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for window cleaners in Latvia?**
A: Window cleaners in Latvia typically earn between €800 and €1,200 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and location. Riga often offers higher pay. Entry-level positions start around minimum wage, while experienced cleaners or those working at height may earn more. Overtime and tips can supplement income.

**Q: Can window cleaners obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Latvia?**
A: Window cleaning is a low-skilled occupation and does not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires higher education. However, third-country nationals may work in Latvia via a standard work permit obtained by an employer after a labour market test. Seasonal work permits may also be available for short-term contracts.

**Q: What are the career advancement opportunities for window cleaners in Latvia?**
A: Experienced window cleaners can progress to supervisory roles, start their own cleaning businesses, or specialize in high-rise or rope-access cleaning. Additional training in safety and equipment use can lead to higher-paying positions. The occupation offers a path to entrepreneurship or team management.
