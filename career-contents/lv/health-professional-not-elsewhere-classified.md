## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for health professionals not elsewhere classified in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary by specialization and experience. For example, dietitians and audiologists typically earn between 1,200 and 1,800 EUR gross monthly in the public sector, with higher pay in private practice. The average gross salary in Latvia is around 1,500 EUR per month, but professionals with advanced qualifications may earn up to 2,500 EUR.

**Q: How can non-EU health professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU health professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a recognized higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia. Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) is available for those who meet specific criteria, including employer sponsorship and recognition of qualifications by the Latvian health authorities.

**Q: Do health professionals need to have their qualifications recognized to work in Latvia?**
A: Yes, recognition of professional qualifications is mandatory. For EU/EEA citizens, automatic recognition applies for some regulated professions under EU directives. Non-EU professionals must have their diplomas assessed by the Academic Information Centre (AIC) and may need additional exams or training. Registration with the relevant professional association is also required.
