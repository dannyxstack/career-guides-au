## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a secondary education teacher in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for secondary teachers in Latvia is approximately €1,300–€1,500, depending on experience and location. This figure is below the national average for all occupations, which is around €1,500. Salaries can increase with seniority and additional responsibilities.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as a secondary teacher in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU teachers can obtain a standard work permit if they have a job offer and recognised qualifications. The EU Blue Card is also available but requires a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia (threshold set by law and updated annually). As teacher salaries often fall below this threshold, the standard work permit is the more common route.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a secondary school teacher in Latvia?**
A: Becoming a secondary teacher in Latvia typically requires a master's degree in the subject to be taught, plus a professional qualification in pedagogy (e.g., a one- to two-year programme). Fluency in Latvian is mandatory, as classes are conducted in Latvian. Degrees must be recognized by the Academic Information Centre (AIC), and a teaching certificate is required.
