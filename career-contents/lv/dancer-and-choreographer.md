## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for dancers and choreographers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, employer, and project. According to data from the State Revenue Service, choreographers in Latvia earn an average gross monthly salary between €1,000 and €2,000. Dancers often have project-based or part-time contracts, leading to irregular income. Many also rely on multiple engagements or teaching.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for a non-EU dancer or choreographer to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card or a national work visa (D visa) if they have a valid employment contract from a Latvian employer. The Blue Card requires higher professional qualifications or at least five years of relevant experience. Alternatively, performers may qualify for a short-term C visa for specific engagements, following the Immigration Law.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for dancers and choreographers in Latvia?**
A: Dancers and choreographers in Latvia work at national institutions like the Latvian National Opera and Ballet, contemporary dance companies, and independent projects. Teaching at the Latvian Academy of Culture or private schools is common. Many also freelance for events, TV, and music videos. Competitions and international collaborations offer further growth.
