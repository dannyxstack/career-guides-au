## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specific trade, but median gross monthly earnings for this broad group range from 1000 to 1500 EUR. Minimum wage is 700 EUR per month. Actual pay depends on experience, employer, and region, with skilled trades like bakers or butchers often earning closer to the upper end.

**Q: Can third-country nationals work in this occupation in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, if the role requires a higher education degree and the gross annual salary exceeds 1.5 times Latvia’s average (approximately 24,000 EUR in 2024). The employer must have a job offer, and the applicant must meet Blue Card criteria. This route is for highly qualified positions within the category.

**Q: What industries employ craft and related workers not elsewhere classified in Latvia?**
A: Workers in this category are found in food processing (bakers, butchers), woodworking (carpenters, cabinet makers), and construction (plasterers, painters). Tourism and handicraft sectors also employ such trades. Apprenticeships and vocational schools provide pathways, with demand steady in manufacturing and services.
