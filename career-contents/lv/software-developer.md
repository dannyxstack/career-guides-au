## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for software developers in Latvia?**
A: Entry-level software developers earn around €1,500–€2,000 gross monthly, while mid-level professionals earn €2,500–€3,500. Senior developers and specialists can exceed €4,500. Salaries vary by company, city (Riga higher), and tech stack. Stock options or bonuses are less common than in Western Europe.

**Q: How can a foreign software developer obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Latvia?**
A: To qualify for an EU Blue Card in Latvia, you need a recognized higher education degree (or 5+ years of relevant experience), a binding job offer as a software developer, and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia (approx. €2,000/month in 2024). Apply at the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What are the career prospects for software developers in Latvia?**
A: Latvia has a thriving tech sector with many startups, global companies (e.g., Accenture, Evolution), and remote work options. Demand for developers in AI, cybersecurity, and fintech is growing. English is widely used in tech. Career growth includes roles like team lead, architect, or specialist in high-demand niches. Professional development is supported through meetups and certifications.
