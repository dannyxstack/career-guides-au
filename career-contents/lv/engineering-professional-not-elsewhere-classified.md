**Q: What is the typical salary for Engineering Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified in Latvia?**

Public sector and research roles may offer lower pay, but private companies, especially in IT and manufacturing, often provide competitive compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU Engineering Professionals obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Latvia?**
A: Yes. Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (or 5 years’ experience) and a job offer paying at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia. Alternatively, a standard national D visa or temporary residence permit for employment is available, provided the employer obtains a work permit. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

Knowledge of local language is not always mandatory in international firms. Continuing education and certifications can enhance career growth. The job market is competitive, but skilled professionals are in demand.
