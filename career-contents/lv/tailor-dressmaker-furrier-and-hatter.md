## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a tailor in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for tailors, dressmakers, and furriers in Latvia ranges from 900 to 1,400 EUR, depending on experience and location. Entry-level positions may start around 700 EUR, while experienced professionals in custom tailoring can earn up to 1,800 EUR.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for tailors to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU tailors can work in Latvia via a standard work permit, obtained through an employer who proves a labor market need. The EU Blue Card is an option only for highly qualified workers (higher education and salary at least 1.5x national average), which tailors with vocational training may not meet. Self-employment is also possible with a business visa.

**Q: What are the career opportunities for tailors in Latvia?**
A: Tailors in Latvia can work in garment factories, alteration studios, or bespoke tailoring shops. With experience, they may become master tailors, open independent businesses, or teach at vocational schools. The industry is niche but stable, with opportunities for skilled artisans, though competition is limited due to low demand.
