**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatically generate 3D printing paths for sand moulds/cores using CAD models
- AI vision system detects casting defects in real-time and automatically sorts them
- Robots automatically complete sand mold assembly and pouring operations
- AI optimizes pouring parameters and adjusts cooling system

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI simulation software assists in predicting casting shrinkage and porosity locations, guiding process improvements.
- AR headset displays sand core assembly sequence and position calibration
- AI-driven quality analysis system generates defect root cause reports
- The digital twin system monitors the entire casting process in real time and warns of anomalies

**Human moat:**

- Process innovation capability for handling non-standard, small-batch castings
- Skills in manual repair and fine-tuning of complex sand moulds/cores
- On-site decision-making ability for multi-variety quick mold changes.
- Team coordination and safety risk management responsibilities

**Skills to build:**

- Process parameter adjustment for 3D printed sand molds/cores
- Casting simulation software (Magma, ProCAST) operation and result interpretation
- Basic maintenance of automated production lines (robots, PLC)
- Metallurgy and Defect Analysis (metallography, X-ray)
- Data-driven process optimization (statistical process control)
- Basic operations and maintenance of digital twin systems

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for metal moulders and coremakers in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, metal moulders and coremakers (ISCO 7211) typically earn between €900 and €1,400 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and location. Entry-level positions start around €900, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,400 or more. Overtime and shift allowances may increase total pay.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for metal moulders and coremakers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as metal moulders or coremakers in Latvia via a work visa (D visa) based on a specific employment offer, or through the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers (requires a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage, around €2,000/month in 2024). Both routes require employer sponsorship and Labour Office approval.

**Q: What are the main job requirements and working conditions for metal moulders and coremakers in Latvia?**
A: Metal moulders and coremakers in Latvia typically need vocational training or an apprenticeship in metalworking or foundry technology. The job involves physical stamina, attention to detail, and the ability to work in noisy, hot, and dusty environments. Shift work is common. Certification or prior experience is preferred by employers. Career progression can lead to supervisory roles or specialist positions.
