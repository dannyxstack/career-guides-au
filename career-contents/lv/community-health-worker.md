## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a community health worker in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for community health workers in Latvia ranges from €900 to €1,300, depending on experience, employer (public or private), and region. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while senior roles can reach €1,500. Public sector salaries are often lower but include benefits.

**Q: How can non-EU nationals obtain a work visa as a community health worker in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a job offer and employer-sponsored work permit. The employer applies to the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. The EU Blue Card is possible only if the role meets the salary threshold (generally at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, around €1,900 per month) and requires higher education, which community health worker positions may not fulfill.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a community health worker in Latvia?**
A: A secondary education in healthcare or social work is typically required, along with completing a community health worker training program. Some employers may prefer a bachelor's degree in public health or nursing. Registration with the Health Inspectorate or relevant professional body is often mandatory, as well as certification in first aid.
