## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Customs and Border Inspectors in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Customs and Border Inspectors in Latvia ranges from €1,500 to €2,200, depending on experience and rank. Entry-level inspectors start around €1,200, while senior officers can earn up to €2,800. Additional allowances for shift work and overtime may apply.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for foreign nationals to become Customs and Border Inspectors in Latvia?**
A: Foreign nationals may apply for an EU Blue Card or a national work visa (D visa) if they secure a job offer meeting salary and qualification criteria. However, positions as Customs and Border Inspectors are typically reserved for Latvian citizens due to national security and legal requirements, making these routes rarely applicable.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Customs and Border Inspectors in Latvia?**
A: Customs and Border Inspectors in Latvia can advance to senior inspector, team leader, or specialist roles in customs control, risk analysis, or investigation. Further promotion to management positions, such as head of department or regional customs office, requires additional training and years of service. Lateral moves to other state institutions are also possible.
