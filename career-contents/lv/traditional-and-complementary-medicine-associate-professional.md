## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary based on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may earn around €800-€1,000 per month, while experienced professionals in private practice can earn €1,500-€2,500 or more. Public sector roles often follow state-defined pay scales.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professionals to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals typically need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The work visa requires a job offer and approval from the State Employment Agency. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers with a valid employment contract meeting the salary threshold (€2,374 per month in 2024) and higher education qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a Traditional and Complementary Medicine Associate Professional in Latvia?**
A: Practitioners must have relevant education and registration. The Ministry of Health regulates some modalities like acupuncture and homeopathy. Typically, a recognised diploma or certification is needed, and practitioners must register with the Health Inspectorate. Unregulated practices may have fewer requirements.
