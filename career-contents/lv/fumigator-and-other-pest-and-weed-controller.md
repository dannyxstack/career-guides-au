## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fumigators and pest controllers in Latvia?**
A: According to the State Revenue Service, the average gross monthly salary for pest control workers (ISCO 7544) in Latvia ranges from approximately 900 to 1,300 euros, depending on experience, region, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around 800 euros, while experienced professionals can earn up to 1,500 euros or more, especially in specialized sectors like industrial fumigation.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for non-EU fumigators wanting to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as fumigators in Latvia by obtaining a work permit and temporary residence permit, requiring a job offer from a Latvian employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers but typically requires a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience; this occupation often does not meet the education threshold, so the standard work permit route is most common.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a fumigator in Latvia?**
A: To work as a fumigator in Latvia, you must obtain a professional qualification certificate from the State Plant Protection Service (VAAD). This involves completing a training course and passing an exam on pest control methods, safety, and chemical use. A basic education and knowledge of Latvian are typically required. Some employers may also require a driver's license and physical fitness.
