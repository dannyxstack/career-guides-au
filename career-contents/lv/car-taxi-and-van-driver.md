## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for car, taxi, and van drivers in Latvia?**
A: As of 2023, the average gross monthly salary for drivers in Latvia is around €1,000 to €1,200, varying by experience and region. Taxi drivers may earn less due to variable income, while van drivers for logistics companies often receive fixed pay. The minimum wage is currently €620 per month.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as car, taxi, or van drivers in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as drivers in Latvia only through a standard work visa (D visa) if offered a job by a Latvian employer. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, which is unlikely for this occupation. Therefore, the standard work permit route is typical.

**Q: What are the licensing requirements to become a taxi or van driver in Latvia?**
A: To drive professionally, you need a valid driving license (category B) and, for taxis, a special permit (taxi license) issued by the municipality. Van drivers may require a category C license if operating vehicles over 3.5 tonnes. All drivers must undergo a medical check and obtain a driver's qualification card (code 95) for commercial transport.
