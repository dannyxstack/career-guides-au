## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Services Manager in Latvia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for Services Managers in Latvia ranges from €1,500 to €3,500, with a median around €2,200 according to the Central Statistical Bureau. Higher pay is common in finance, IT, and international companies. Entry-level positions start lower, and bonuses or benefits can increase total compensation.

**Q: What are the work visa options for a Services Manager moving to Latvia?**
A: The main route is the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (approximately 1.5 times the national average gross salary). Alternatively, a national D visa with a work permit is possible if the employer obtains a labor market opinion.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Services Manager in Latvia?**
A: Most employers require a bachelor’s degree in business administration, management, or a related field, along with several years of relevant experience. Proficiency in Latvian and English is often expected. Professional certifications in service management and networking skills can enhance prospects. For senior roles, a master’s degree may be preferred.
