## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for garden and horticultural labourers in Latvia ranges from €700 to €1,000, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start at the national minimum wage (€700 per month in 2024), while experienced workers can earn higher rates, especially during peak seasons.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as garden and horticultural labourers in Latvia through the EU Blue Card or other work-visa routes?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work visa or residence permit for employment. The EU Blue Card is aimed at highly qualified workers and usually requires a higher education degree, so it may not apply to this role. Instead, employers can sponsor a temporary residence permit for employment, which is the standard route for low- or medium-skilled labour. Official details are available from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP).

**Q: What are the main duties of a garden and horticultural labourer in Latvia?**
A: Duties include planting, watering, weeding, pruning, and harvesting plants, flowers, fruits, and vegetables. Workers also prepare soil, operate basic gardening tools, maintain lawns and hedges, and assist with pest control. Tasks are often seasonal, with peak work in spring and summer. Employers include farms, nurseries, public parks, and private gardens.
