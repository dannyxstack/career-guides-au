## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for social work and counselling professionals in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and sector. Public sector roles may start lower, while private practice or specialized counselling can reach higher. Salaries in Rīga tend to be above the national average. Note that actual pay varies by employer and qualifications.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for social work professionals moving to Latvia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 1.5x average wage). Alternatively, a national D visa for employment is possible if the employer obtains a work permit. Both routes require employer sponsorship and recognition of your qualifications by the Latvian authorities.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as a social work or counselling professional in Latvia?**
A: A bachelor's or master's degree in social work, psychology, or a related field is typically required. For regulated roles like social worker, registration with the Latvian Association of Social Workers or other professional bodies may be necessary. Practical experience and Latvian language proficiency are often essential, especially for client-facing positions.
