## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Sales Professional in Latvia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for ICT Sales Professionals in Latvia typically range from €2,000 to €4,500, depending on experience, employer, and sales performance. Senior roles or those in high-demand sectors may exceed this range. Additional commission or bonuses are common, significantly increasing total compensation.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for ICT Sales Professionals in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can work in Latvia via the EU Blue Card for highly qualified employment, requiring a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience, and a job offer meeting the salary threshold. Alternatively, a standard work permit may be obtained with a Latvian employer sponsorship, following the annual quota system.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ICT Sales Professionals in Latvia?**
A: ICT Sales Professionals in Latvia can advance to senior sales roles, team lead, or sales manager positions. With experience, opportunities include key account management, business development, or transitioning to product or marketing roles. The growing ICT sector in Latvia also offers pathways into consultancy or entrepreneurship.
