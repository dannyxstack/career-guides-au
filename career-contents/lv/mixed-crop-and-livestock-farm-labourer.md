## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Latvia?**
A: The monthly gross salary for mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Latvia typically ranges from €800 to €1,200, depending on the region, employer, and season. This is around or slightly above the national minimum wage. Additional bonuses may be provided during harvest periods.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU nationals to work as mixed crop and livestock farm labourers in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain a Latvian work permit if they have a confirmed job offer. The employer must prove a shortage of local labour. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires higher education. Seasonal work permits are available for temporary (up to 9 months) agricultural labour.

**Q: What are the main duties of a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Latvia?**
A: Duties include planting, cultivating, and harvesting crops; feeding, watering, and herding livestock; cleaning animal housing; maintaining fences and equipment; and assisting with general farm operations. Work is physically demanding and often seasonal, with early starts. No formal education is required, but on-the-job training is provided.
