## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Aged Care Services Manager in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for Aged Care Services Managers in Latvia vary by experience and employer, but typically range from €1,500 to €2,500 gross per month. Senior positions may exceed this. Additional bonuses or benefits are occasionally offered, especially in private care facilities.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for aged care managers moving to Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting salary thresholds (about 1.5x average salary). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment requires a work permit from the State Employment Agency. Both routes need employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as an Aged Care Services Manager in Latvia?**
A: Typically, a degree in healthcare management, social work, or nursing is required. Relevant experience in elder care and management is essential. Registration with the Health Inspectorate or relevant professional body may be needed. Knowledge of Latvian language is often necessary for direct client interaction.
