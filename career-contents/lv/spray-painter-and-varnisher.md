## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a spray painter in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for spray painters in Latvia generally range from €800 to €1,500 gross per month, depending on experience, employer, and sector. Entry-level positions start around minimum wage (€700/month in 2024), while experienced professionals in industries like automotive or furniture finishing can earn higher. Overtime and bonuses may also apply.

**Q: Can spray painters from outside the EU get a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Latvia?**
A: Yes. Non-EU spray painters must obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card. The standard route requires a job offer and employer sponsorship, followed by a work permit from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree or five years of professional experience and meets a minimum salary threshold set by Latvian law.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a spray painter in Latvia?**
A: Formal vocational education in surface treatment or painting is common, but on-the-job training and apprenticeships are also accepted. Important skills include knowledge of paint types, mixing, and application techniques, as well as occupational safety. Certification in specific methods (e.g., powder coating) can improve job prospects. The profession has steady demand in manufacturing and construction.
