- Transcribing handwritten documents into electronic text.

- Using AI-assisted transcription tools to improve efficiency
- Supervise and correct AI-generated transcription results

- Use OCR and NLP tools to batch process historical documents

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for scribes and related workers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for scribes and related workers in Latvia is approximately €900-€1,100, depending on experience and employer. This figure is based on data from the State Revenue Service and job portals. Salaries tend to be higher in Riga and lower in other regions.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for working as a scribe in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit and residence permit. The EU Blue Card is not typically applicable for scribe roles due to salary requirements. Standard work permits require a job offer and employer sponsorship. EU/EEA citizens can work freely without a visa. Consult the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs for official procedures.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a scribe in Latvia?**
A: Typically, secondary education is required along with proficiency in Latvian and possibly other languages. Skills in typing, document formatting, and attention to detail are essential. Vocational training in administration is an advantage. Certification in legal or medical transcription may be beneficial for specialized roles.
