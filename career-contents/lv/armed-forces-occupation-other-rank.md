## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average monthly salary for other ranks (enlisted personnel) in the Latvian armed forces?**
A: Monthly salary for other ranks in Latvia starts around €800–€1,200 gross for new recruits, increasing with rank and experience. Senior non-commissioned officers may earn €1,500–€2,200. Additional allowances for specializations, deployments, and tenure apply. Figures are subject to annual adjustments and are tax-exempt for basic pay.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work in armed forces other ranks in Latvia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not applicable to military positions. Non-EU nationals may join the Latvian armed forces only under specific bilateral agreements or as part of international military cooperation, typically requiring permanent residency or citizenship. Work permits for foreign military personnel are issued case-by-case by the National Armed Forces, not through standard employment visa routes.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for other ranks in the Latvian armed forces?**
A: Other ranks can advance through non-commissioned officer (NCO) ranks, from recruit to senior NCO (sergeant major). Promotions are based on time in service, completion of professional courses, and performance evaluations. Opportunities include specialization in technical, combat, or support roles, and participation in international missions like NATO operations.
