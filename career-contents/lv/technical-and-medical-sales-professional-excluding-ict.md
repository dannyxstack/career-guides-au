## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for technical and medical sales professionals in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, technical and medical sales professionals typically earn between €1,500 and €3,000 gross per month. Salaries vary based on experience, industry, and company size. Commission and bonuses can significantly increase total compensation, especially for those achieving sales targets in the medical or technical sectors.

**Q: What are the visa options for non-EU professionals seeking to work as technical or medical sales professionals in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold set by Latvian law. Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) may be obtained for employment. Both routes require employer sponsorship and compliance with immigration regulations.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a technical or medical sales professional in Latvia?**
A: A bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as engineering, life sciences, or medicine is typically required. Strong communication, negotiation skills, and proficiency in Latvian and English are essential. Previous sales experience and knowledge of the local market are advantageous. Certifications in sales or specific product areas may also enhance prospects.
