## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors and Associates in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for this role in Latvia typically range from €1,200 to €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience and sector (public vs. private). Public sector positions with the State Labour Inspectorate or Health Inspectorate offer stable pay, while private sector roles may vary. Entry-level associates earn around €1,000, while senior inspectors can earn €2,500 or more.

**Q: What work visa routes are available for foreign Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors to work in Latvia?**
A: Foreign professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary in Latvia). Alternatively, a standard work permit (D visa) is available for those with a valid employment contract. Both require employer sponsorship and approval from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What are the main duties of Environmental and Occupational Health Inspectors in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, these professionals inspect workplaces, public facilities, and environmental sites to enforce health, safety, and environmental regulations. They assess risks, investigate complaints and accidents, issue permits, and recommend corrective measures. They work for agencies like the State Labour Inspectorate or Health Inspectorate, focusing on occupational safety, food safety, or environmental health compliance.
