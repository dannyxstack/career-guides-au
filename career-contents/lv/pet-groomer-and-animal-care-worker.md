## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for pet groomers and animal care workers in Latvia?**
A: According to Latvia's State Revenue Service data, the average gross monthly salary for animal care workers (including pet groomers) is approximately €900–€1,200. Earnings vary based on experience, location, and whether the groomer is self-employed or works at a salon.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as pet groomers in Latvia via the EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes. Non-EU citizens can obtain a work visa through employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card is an option if you have a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience, along with a job offer meeting the minimum salary threshold (currently €1,455 per month gross). No specific quotas for this occupation.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a pet groomer in Latvia?**
A: No formal state regulation exists, but vocational schools offer short courses in pet grooming. Practical apprenticeships with experienced groomers are common. Joining the Latvian Pet Grooming Association provides networking and training opportunities, which can boost credibility and job prospects.
