## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for physiotherapists in Latvia?**
A: According to data from the State Revenue Service and job portals, the average gross monthly salary for physiotherapists in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Private practice may yield higher earnings.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for physiotherapists to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU physiotherapists can work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card (for highly qualified workers with a job offer and recognized qualifications) or a national D visa for employment. The employer must apply for a work permit. Recognition of professional qualifications is required through the Health Inspectorate.

**Q: What are the main employment settings for physiotherapists in Latvia?**
A: Physiotherapists in Latvia work in public and private hospitals, rehabilitation centers, sports facilities, and private clinics. Some are self-employed. The profession is regulated; registration with the Latvian Society of Physiotherapists and the Health Inspectorate is mandatory.
