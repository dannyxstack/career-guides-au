## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for street and related services workers in Latvia?**
A: The average salary for street and related services workers in Latvia is typically around the national minimum wage, which is €700 per month (2024). Earnings can vary depending on location, sales volume, and tips, but most workers in this occupation earn at or slightly above minimum wage due to the low-skilled nature of the work.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a street vendor in Latvia under the EU Blue Card scheme?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is not applicable for street and related services workers, as it requires a highly qualified job with a university degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Instead, third-country nationals can apply for a temporary residence permit for employment, which requires a job offer, a positive labor market opinion, and compliance with annual quotas for low-skilled workers.

**Q: What are the typical working conditions and legal requirements for street vendors in Latvia?**
A: Street vendors in Latvia must register as self-employed or work for a registered employer. They need a municipal permit for specific sales locations and must comply with hygiene and product safety regulations. Working conditions often involve outdoor exposure, irregular hours, and income based on daily sales or commission. Many work in markets, fairs, or busy pedestrian areas.
