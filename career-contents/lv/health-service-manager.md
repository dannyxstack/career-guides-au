## 8. FAQ

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a Health Services Manager in Latvia?**
A: Typically, a master's degree in healthcare management, public health, or business administration is required. Professional experience in healthcare settings and knowledge of Latvian healthcare regulations are essential. Fluency in Latvian is often mandatory, especially for public sector roles.

**Q: What is the average salary for Health Services Managers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for health services managers in Latvia ranges from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience, location (Riga vs. other regions), and the size of the healthcare facility. Senior managers in large hospitals may earn more.

**Q: Can foreign Health Services Managers work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU health services managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia. Alternatively, a work permit under the national Labour Migration Framework is possible for those not meeting Blue Card criteria.
