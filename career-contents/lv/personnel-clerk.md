## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Personnel Clerk in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for personnel clerks in Latvia generally range from €900 to €1,400 gross per month, depending on experience, employer size, and location. Entry-level positions may start around €800, while senior clerks in Riga or large companies can earn up to €1,600. The average is approximately €1,100 gross monthly.

**Q: What work visa options exist for Personnel Clerks moving to Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if the job meets the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary, currently around €2,400 monthly). Alternatively, a national D-type visa for employment is available for positions not meeting that threshold, requiring a work permit and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are typical duties of Personnel Clerks in Latvia?**
A: Personnel clerks in Latvia handle administrative tasks such as maintaining employee records, processing payroll data, managing attendance, assisting with recruitment paperwork, and ensuring compliance with Latvian labour laws. They often work in HR departments and use software like Microsoft Office and local HR systems. Fluency in Latvian is typically required.
