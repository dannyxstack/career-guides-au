## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a landscape architect in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for landscape architects in Latvia vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions typically earn €1,200–€1,500 gross monthly, while mid-career professionals can expect €1,800–€2,500. Senior specialists or those in private practice may earn over €3,000. Public sector roles often have lower but stable pay.

**Q: What are the official routes for a non-EU landscape architect to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU landscape architects can work in Latvia via a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and employer sponsorship. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (≈€2,100/month in 2024). Both routes require positive labor market opinion.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a landscape architect in Latvia?**
A: A master's degree in landscape architecture or a related field is typically required. Graduates must also register with the Latvian Association of Landscape Architects (LALA) to practice professionally. Continuing education is encouraged. Foreign qualifications must be recognized by the Academic Information Centre (AIC) for equivalency.
