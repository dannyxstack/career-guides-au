- Automatically replenish orders based on inventory data
- Regular product shelving and display

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze high-demand areas and optimize product layout
- Quickly locate item positions through AR devices

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for shelf fillers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for shelf fillers in Latvia ranges from €700 to €1,000, depending on the employer, region, and experience. Large retail chains like Rimi or Maxima may offer slightly higher pay. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as shelf fillers in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is typically not available for shelf fillers, as it requires higher education qualifications and a salary above a set threshold (€2,391.99 gross monthly in 2024). Non-EU citizens may instead apply for a national work permit (D visa) sponsored by an employer, subject to labour market tests.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for shelf fillers in Latvia?**
A: With experience, shelf fillers in Latvia can advance to roles like department supervisor, store manager, or logistics coordinator. Many retailers offer internal training programmes. Career growth often depends on performance, willingness to take responsibility, and completion of management courses.
