- Manual drafting of mine design and construction drawings
- Resource reserve estimation based on experience

- On-site Inspections for Hazard Identification

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI for 3D modeling of ore deposits and resource assessment
- Optimizing autonomous truck routes and blasting plans using AI
- Using machine learning to predict rock mass stability and support requirements
- Integrating digital twin technology for real-time monitoring and adjustment of mining operations
- Quickly parse geological reports and compliance documents using natural language processing

**Human moat:**

- Communication and coordination with government/community/Indigenous peoples

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mining and metallurgical technicians in Latvia?**
A: According to official data, the average gross monthly salary for mining and metallurgical technicians in Latvia ranges from €1,400 to €2,000, depending on experience, qualifications, and the specific sector (e.g., metal processing or mining). Entry-level positions may start lower, while senior technicians or those in supervisory roles earn higher.

**Q: What are the official routes to work in Latvia as a mining and metallurgical technician for non-EU nationals?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as a technician via a work visa sponsored by a Latvian employer, requiring a positive labor market opinion. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for those with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the annual salary threshold set by the Ministry of Economics. Both routes require employer sponsorship and valid residence permits.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are there for mining and metallurgical technicians in Latvia?**
A: Technicians can progress to senior technician, shift supervisor, or production manager. Further education, such as a bachelor's in engineering, opens roles in quality control, research and development, or process optimization. The Latvian industry, including companies in metalworking and mineral extraction, offers pathways to specialized technical or management positions.
