## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a bicycle repairer in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for bicycle repairers in Latvia typically ranges between €900 and €1,200, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start lower, while experienced mechanics in Riga can earn closer to the upper end. Overtime and bonuses may add to total compensation.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a bicycle repairer in Latvia via the EU Blue Card or other work visa?**
A: Bicycle repairers generally do not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times Latvia's average gross wage. However, non-EU citizens can obtain a work visa through employer sponsorship for a fixed-term employment contract. The employer must apply for a work permit with the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to become a bicycle repairer in Latvia?**
A: Formal certification is not mandatory, but vocational training in bicycle mechanics or automotive repair is highly valued. Many employers prefer candidates with a basic technical education or relevant work experience. On-the-job training is common, and proficiency with bike tools, diagnosing mechanical issues, and performing repairs is essential.
