## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for cashiers and ticket clerks in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, the average gross monthly salary for cashiers and ticket clerks ranges from €800 to €1,000, depending on experience, location, and employer. The national minimum wage is €620 per month (as of 2024). Overtime and shift premiums may increase earnings, especially in retail and transport sectors.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as cashiers or ticket clerks in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work in Latvia under a work permit or EU Blue Card. For cashier/ticket clerk roles, a standard work permit is more common, as the Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (€28,368 in 2024), which these roles typically do not meet. Employers must apply for a work permit on the candidate's behalf.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for cashiers and ticket clerks in Latvia?**
A: Cashiers and ticket clerks in Latvia can advance to senior cashier, shift supervisor, or team leader roles. With additional training, they may move into retail management, customer service, or administrative positions. Some transition to finance or logistics roles. Proficiency in Latvian and English, along with digital skills, enhances advancement prospects.
