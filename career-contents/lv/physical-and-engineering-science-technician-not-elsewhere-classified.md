## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Physical and Engineering Science Technician in Latvia?**
A: Gross monthly salaries for physical and engineering science technicians in Latvia typically range from €1,300 to €2,000, depending on experience, industry, and location. Senior roles or specialized sectors like manufacturing or IT may offer higher pay. Entry-level positions usually start around €1,000 gross per month.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign Physical and Engineering Science Technician to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree or at least 5 years of professional experience and receive a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Alternatively, a standard work permit (C visa) is available if the employer proves no local candidate could fill the position.

**Q: What are typical tasks for a Physical and Engineering Science Technician in Latvia?**
A: These technicians assist engineers in research, development, testing, and production. Duties include calibrating and maintaining laboratory equipment, conducting experiments, analyzing data, preparing technical reports, and ensuring quality control. They work in industries such as manufacturing, construction, IT, and environmental services.
