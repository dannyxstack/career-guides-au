## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for personal services workers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary widely by specific role, but average monthly gross pay for personal service workers in Latvia typically ranges between €700 and €1,100. Experience, location, and employer influence earnings. The national minimum wage is €700 per month as of 2025.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as personal services workers in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card targets highly qualified workers, requiring a higher education degree and salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage. Personal services roles rarely meet these criteria. Non-EU workers may obtain a work visa if the employer secures a labor market opinion from the State Employment Agency demonstrating a shortage.

**Q: What are typical career advancement opportunities for personal services workers in Latvia?**
A: Career paths include specialization in areas like beauty, tourism, or funeral services, or moving into supervisory or self-employment roles. Acquiring additional certifications and proficiency in Latvian can improve prospects. Many workers find opportunities in hospitality, personal care, or event services.
