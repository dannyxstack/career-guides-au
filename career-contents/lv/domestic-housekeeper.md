## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a domestic housekeeper in Latvia?**
A: The salary for domestic housekeepers in Latvia varies by employer and location, but the minimum monthly wage is €700 (as of 2024). Many earn between €700 and €1,200 gross per month for full-time work, with higher pay possible in Riga or for experienced staff. Overtime and benefits may add to compensation.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as domestic housekeepers in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as domestic housekeepers in Latvia via a national work visa (D visa). The employer must obtain a work permit from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. The EU Blue Card is not typically available for this occupation as it requires higher education, but a standard work permit is a viable route.

**Q: What are the career prospects for domestic housekeepers in Latvia?**
A: Domestic housekeepers in Latvia can advance to supervisory roles in hotels, cleaning companies, or private households. Additional training in hospitality or cleaning services may lead to higher positions. The demand for domestic help is stable, especially in urban areas, with opportunities for self-employment or agency work.
