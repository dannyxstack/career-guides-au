## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a judge in Latvia?**
A: Judges' salaries in Latvia are set by the Judicial Remuneration Law. A first-instance judge earns approximately €3,500–€4,000 gross per month. Salaries rise with court level: appellate judges earn around €4,500–€5,500, and Supreme Court judges receive up to €6,500. These figures are gross and subject to income tax.

**Q: Can foreign judges obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU judges can work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (1.5 times average gross annual salary). Alternatively, a national long-stay D visa for employment is possible. Recognition of foreign legal qualifications is required. Note: Latvian citizenship is typically required for permanent judicial appointments.

**Q: What are the requirements to become a judge in Latvia?**
A: To become a judge in Latvia, one must be a Latvian citizen, have a master's degree in law, pass the judicial qualification exam, and complete a three-year judicial assistantship. Candidates then apply for appointment by the President upon recommendation of the Judicial Council. A clean criminal record and impeccable reputation are mandatory.
