## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for tree and shrub crop growers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for tree and shrub crop growers in Latvia vary by experience and employer, typically ranging from €800 to €1,500 gross per month. Seasonal workers may earn minimum wage (€620/month in 2025) plus bonuses. Specialized roles in fruit orchards or nurseries can reach higher due to demand for skilled labor.

**Q: Can tree and shrub crop growers obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU workers can apply for a Latvian D visa for employment if they have a job offer. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and higher salary; it is not typical for crop growers. Instead, seasonal work permits (up to 9 months) are common. Official routes include the Labour Market Test and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What are the main duties and entry requirements for tree and shrub crop growers in Latvia?**
A: Duties include planting, pruning, irrigating, fertilizing, pest control, and harvesting trees and shrubs. Formal education is not mandatory, but vocational training in horticulture or agriculture is advantageous. Practical experience and physical fitness are essential. Proficiency in Latvian or Russian is often required for communication with employers.
