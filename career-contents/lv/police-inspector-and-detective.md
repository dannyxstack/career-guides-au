## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a police inspector or detective in Latvia?**
A: According to the State Police, the average gross monthly salary for a police inspector in Latvia ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,600, depending on rank, experience, and location. Detectives earn similar amounts. Additional allowances may apply for special duties or overtime.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as police inspectors or detectives in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: No. Police inspector and detective positions in Latvia require Latvian citizenship and fluency in the Latvian language. Therefore, non-EU nationals cannot obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for these roles, as they are restricted to citizens and, in limited cases, other EU/EEA nationals with language proficiency.

**Q: What training is required to become a police inspector in Latvia?**
A: Candidates must complete a 3-year professional bachelor's programme at the State Police College in Riga, or hold a university degree and pass a competitive entry exam. After recruitment, they undergo practical on-the-job training and must demonstrate proficiency in Latvian law and investigative procedures.
