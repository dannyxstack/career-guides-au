## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Policy Administration Professionals in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for policy administration professionals in Latvia vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,200 gross per month, while mid-career roles average €1,800. Senior professionals in government or EU institutions can earn €2,500 or more. Public sector roles may have additional benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as Policy Administration Professionals in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work in Latvia through the EU Blue Card or a national D visa. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (approx. €2,100/month in 2024). The national D visa is for other qualified positions, subject to labour market tests.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for Policy Administration Professionals in Latvia?**
A: Policy administration professionals in Latvia can advance to senior policy advisor, team lead, or managerial roles in government ministries, EU institutions, or NGOs. Specialising in areas like EU funds, public health, or digital policy opens further opportunities. Continuing education in public administration or policy analysis is valued.
