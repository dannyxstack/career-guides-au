## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for Social Work Associate Professionals in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for social work associate professionals in Latvia ranges from €900 to €1,300, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions start around €800, while senior roles can exceed €1,500. Salaries may be higher in Riga compared to rural areas.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for foreign Social Work Associate Professionals to work in Latvia?**
A: Foreign social work associate professionals can work in Latvia via an employer-sponsored work visa (national visa D) or the EU Blue Card if they meet the salary threshold (approx. €1,500 gross monthly) and have a recognized qualification. The employer must prove labor market shortage. Professional recognition may be required for regulated roles.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for Social Work Associate Professionals in Latvia?**
A: Social work associate professionals can advance to social work manager, team leader, or specialized roles (e.g., child welfare, addiction counseling). With further education, they may qualify as social workers (ISCO 2635). Continuous professional development is encouraged through courses offered by the Latvian Association of Social Workers.
