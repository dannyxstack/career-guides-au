## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a bank teller in Latvia?**
A: Bank tellers in Latvia typically earn a gross monthly salary between €800 and €1,200, depending on experience, bank size, and location (Riga pays higher). The national average for cashiers and related clerks is approximately €950 per month, according to the Central Statistical Bureau of Latvia.

**Q: What visa or EU Blue Card options are available for foreign bank tellers in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU/EEA nationals need a work visa or residence permit sponsored by a Latvian employer. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable as bank teller salaries (€800–€1,200) fall below the threshold (around €1,500+). Instead, a national D visa for employment can be obtained if the job meets labour market test criteria.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for bank tellers in Latvia?**
A: Bank tellers in Latvia can advance to customer service advisors, personal bankers, or branch managers with experience and additional training. Proficiency in Latvian and Russian is essential. However, digitalization is reducing teller roles, so upskilling in sales or financial advisory is recommended for long-term growth.
