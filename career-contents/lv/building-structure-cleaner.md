## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building structure cleaner in Latvia?**
A: The gross monthly salary typically ranges from €900 to €1,200, with an hourly wage of €5 to €7. Overtime and night shifts may increase earnings. Salaries vary by region and employer.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for building structure cleaners in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals require a work permit and a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must prove a labor market test. The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this low-skilled occupation. There is no specific fast-track scheme for cleaners.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a building structure cleaner in Latvia?**
A: No formal education is required. Basic safety training is often provided by the employer. Knowledge of Latvian or Russian is useful for communication. On-the-job training is standard, and prior experience in cleaning is an advantage.
