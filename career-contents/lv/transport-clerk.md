## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a transport clerk in Latvia?**
A: The average monthly gross salary for transport clerks in Latvia ranges from 1000 to 1500 EUR, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions start around 900 EUR, while senior clerks may earn up to 1800 EUR. Bonuses and overtime pay may also apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as transport clerks in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is reserved for highly qualified professionals with a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia. Transport clerks typically do not meet these requirements. Instead, non-EU citizens must obtain a standard work visa through employer sponsorship for a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What are the career prospects for transport clerks in Latvia?**
A: Transport clerks can advance to roles such as logistics coordinator, freight forwarder, or supply chain manager with experience and additional certifications in logistics or transportation. Opportunities exist in shipping companies, freight forwarding firms, and large retailers, especially in Riga and key port areas.
