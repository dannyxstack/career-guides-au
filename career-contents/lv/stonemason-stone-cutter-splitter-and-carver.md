## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a stonemason in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for stonemasons in Latvia ranges from €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €900, while skilled carvers in high demand can earn up to €2,000. Wages are typically higher in Riga and for specialized restoration work.

**Q: How can non-EU stonemasons obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Latvia?**
A: Non-EU stonemasons can apply for a Latvian national D visa for employment, requiring a job offer and work permit. The EU Blue Card is available for those with a higher education degree (or 5+ years relevant experience) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold set by Latvian law. For stonemasons without a degree, a standard work permit is the primary route.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a stonemason in Latvia?**
A: While formal qualifications are not always mandatory, vocational training in stonework or construction is highly valued. Apprenticeships and certificates from Latvian vocational schools improve job prospects. For heritage restoration, additional training in traditional techniques is required. Licensing is not needed, but employers often prefer candidates with proven skills and safety certifications.
