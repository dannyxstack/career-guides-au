## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for buyers in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, the average gross monthly salary for buyers ranges from €1,500 to €2,500, depending on experience and industry. Entry-level positions start around €1,200, while senior buyers can earn up to €3,500. Bonuses and benefits may be additional.

**Q: What are the work visa options for foreign buyers in Latvia?**
A: Foreign buyers can work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary (approximately €28,000 in 2024). Alternatively, a standard work permit is possible if the employer demonstrates a labor market test. Both require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for buyers in Latvia?**
A: Buyers in Latvia can advance to senior buyer, procurement manager, or supply chain director. Specializing in sectors like IT, manufacturing, or retail accelerates growth. Professional certifications (e.g., CIPS) are valued. Many companies in Riga offer structured career development programs.
