## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and employer. Entry-level advisers earn around €1,200-€1,500 gross per month, while senior specialists can earn €2,000-€2,800. Public sector roles may offer lower base pay but include benefits. Private consulting firms or EU-funded projects often provide higher compensation.

**Q: What is the work-visa or EU Blue Card route for non-EU Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a work visa or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card is for highly qualified workers (salary at least 1.5x average gross wage, currently ~€1,800/month). This occupation may qualify if the employer proves the role cannot be filled locally. A work permit (temporary residence permit for employment) is another option for jobs meeting minimum salary thresholds.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Adviser in Latvia?**
A: Typically a bachelor's or master's degree in agriculture, forestry, environmental science, or a related field is required. Practical experience and knowledge of EU agricultural policy are highly valued. Proficiency in Latvian is often necessary for client communication, though English may suffice for international projects.
