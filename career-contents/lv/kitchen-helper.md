## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a kitchen helper in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a kitchen helper in Latvia ranges from €500 to €900, depending on experience, location, and employer. Entry-level positions typically start around €500, while experienced helpers in Riga may earn up to €900. Overtime and tips can add to earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a kitchen helper in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as kitchen helpers in Latvia with a national D visa for employment, requiring a job offer and approval from the State Employment Agency. The EU Blue Card is not applicable as it requires a higher education degree and a higher salary threshold than kitchen helper roles typically meet.

**Q: What are the main duties of a kitchen helper in Latvia?**
A: Kitchen helpers in Latvia assist chefs by washing dishes, cleaning kitchen equipment, preparing basic ingredients (e.g., peeling vegetables), and maintaining hygiene standards. They may also help with food storage and simple cooking tasks, such as boiling pasta or assembling salads.
