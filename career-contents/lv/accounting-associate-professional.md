## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for an Accounting Associate Professional in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for Accounting Associate Professionals in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior roles can exceed €2,000. Salaries are higher in Riga and for those with ACCA or similar certifications.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreigner to work as an Accounting Associate Professional in Latvia, such as the EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia (currently €24,636). Alternatively, a work visa can be obtained if the employer obtains a work permit from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What qualifications are typically required for an Accounting Associate Professional role in Latvia?**
A: Employers usually require at least a bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or a related field. Professional certifications like ACCA, CIMA, or Latvian certified accountant status are highly valued. Proficiency in Latvian and English is often needed, along with experience in accounting software such as Horizon or Microsoft Dynamics.
