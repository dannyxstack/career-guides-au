## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for sociologists and anthropologists in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, sociologists and anthropologists typically earn between €1,200 and €2,000 gross per month, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and employer. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior roles in research institutions or NGOs can exceed €2,500.

**Q: Can non-EU sociologists and anthropologists work in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Latvia if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently at least 1.5 times the average gross salary, around €2,000+ per month). The employer must first register the vacancy. A work permit and residence permit are also required.

**Q: What are the main employers for sociologists and anthropologists in Latvia?**
A: Sociologists and anthropologists in Latvia are mainly employed by universities, research institutes, government agencies (e.g., Central Statistical Bureau), and NGOs. Private sector roles exist in market research and consulting firms. International organizations like the OECD also offer opportunities, particularly in policy analysis and social research.
