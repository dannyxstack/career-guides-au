## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics in Latvia typically range from €1,200 to €1,800 gross per month, depending on experience, certifications, and employer location. Entry-level workers may start around €1,000, while senior technicians can earn up to €2,200. Overtime and specializations can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card for highly qualified professionals, requiring a higher education degree or 5+ years of experience. Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment applies if the employer proves a labor market shortage. EEA nationals have free movement.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic in Latvia?**
A: Most mechanics complete a vocational secondary education program in refrigeration or HVAC, typically 3-4 years. Apprenticeships and professional certificates from the Latvian Chamber of Crafts or vocational schools are common. Continuing education is required for handling refrigerants, and State Labour Inspectorate approval may be needed.
