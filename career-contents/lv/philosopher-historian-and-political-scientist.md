## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Latvia vary by employer and experience. Entry-level positions in academia or research typically start around €1,000–€1,200 gross per month, while senior roles or jobs in international organizations may reach €2,000–€3,000 gross. Public sector salaries follow state wage scales.

**Q: Can foreign philosophers, historians, and political scientists work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes. Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times average gross salary, adjusted annually). Alternatively, a standard work permit is available for positions that cannot be filled locally, requiring employer sponsorship and approval from the State Employment Agency.

**Q: What are the main employers for philosophers, historians, and political scientists in Latvia?**
A: Key employers include universities (e.g., University of Latvia, Riga Stradiņš University), research institutes (e.g., Institute of Philosophy and Sociology), government ministries and agencies, think tanks, NGOs, media outlets, and international organizations. Some also work as independent consultants or in cultural heritage institutions.
