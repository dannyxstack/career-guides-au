## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for construction managers in Latvia?**
A: Construction managers in Latvia earn between €1,500 and €3,000 gross per month, depending on experience, project size, and location. Senior managers or those on large international projects may earn higher, while entry-level positions start at the lower end.

**Q: What are the official work-visa routes for construction managers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU construction managers can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (typically at least 1.5 times the average gross wage). Alternatively, a Latvian work permit is available for sponsored employment, requiring a valid job offer and employer support.

**Q: What are the main responsibilities and career prospects for construction managers in Latvia?**
A: Construction managers oversee project planning, budgets, timelines, and team coordination, ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards. Career prospects include advancement to senior project director, regional manager, or specialization in green building or infrastructure, with demand driven by Latvia's growing construction sector.
