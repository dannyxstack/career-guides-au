## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for this profession vary by experience and sector. According to the Central Statistical Bureau, the average gross monthly salary for professionals in this field is approximately €1,800, with entry-level positions around €1,200 and senior roles reaching €2,500 or more. Public sector positions may have lower starting pay but offer stability.

**Q: Can non-EU professionals work as Environmental and Occupational Health and Hygiene Professionals in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes. Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average. The exact threshold is set annually by the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. The employer must also prove the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What are the main employers and career prospects for this profession in Latvia?**
A: Key employers include the State Labour Inspectorate, the Health Inspectorate, private companies in manufacturing and construction, and environmental consulting firms. Opportunities also exist in research and academia. Career advancement often leads to managerial roles or specialization in industrial hygiene or environmental health. The field is growing due to EU regulatory requirements.
