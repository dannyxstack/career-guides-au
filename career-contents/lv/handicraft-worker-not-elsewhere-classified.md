## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for handicraft workers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for handicraft workers in Latvia vary widely by specialization and experience. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is €700 per month gross. Skilled handicraft workers often earn between €900 and €1,500 gross per month, with master craftspeople in high-demand trades earning more. Income can also depend on self-employment or piecework arrangements.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as handicraft workers in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is generally for highly qualified workers requiring a higher education degree and meeting a salary threshold (€2,082 per month in 2024). Most handicraft positions do not qualify. Instead, non-EU nationals may apply for a national D visa for employment, which requires a work permit obtained by the employer after demonstrating that the vacancy cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What career prospects exist for handicraft workers in Latvia?**
A: Handicraft workers in Latvia can advance by becoming master craftspeople or starting their own businesses. The Latvian Chamber of Crafts offers certification programs. Demand exists in construction, woodworking, and traditional crafts like pottery. Government support for vocational training may help upskill. Retirement age is 64 years and 3 months (2024), with opportunities for part-time work.
