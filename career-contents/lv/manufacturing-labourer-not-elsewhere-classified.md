## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a manufacturing labourer in Latvia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for manufacturing labourers in Latvia typically ranges from €800 to €1200, depending on experience and employer. The national minimum wage is €620 as of 2024. Overtime and shift bonuses may apply. Actual pay varies by region and industry.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as a manufacturing labourer in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Latvia is for highly skilled workers, requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the national average. Manufacturing labourer positions typically do not meet these criteria. However, non-EU citizens may apply for a temporary residence permit for employment under the Latvian Labour Law, subject to a labour market test.

**Q: What are the main duties of a manufacturing labourer in Latvia?**
A: Manufacturing labourers perform simple tasks in factories, such as material handling, machine operation, assembly, packaging, and cleaning. They must follow safety regulations and work under supervision. Duties vary by industry, including food processing, metalworking, or woodworking. No formal qualifications are required, but on-the-job training is provided.
