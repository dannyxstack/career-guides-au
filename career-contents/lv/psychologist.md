## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a psychologist in Latvia?**
A: The average monthly salary for psychologists in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800 gross, depending on experience, sector (public vs. private), and specialization. Entry-level positions may start around €1,000, while senior psychologists in private practice or managerial roles can earn up to €2,500 or more.

**Q: Can psychologists from non-EU countries work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, psychologists from non-EU countries can apply for an EU Blue Card in Latvia if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia). The employer must demonstrate the position could not be filled locally.

**Q: What qualifications are required to practice as a psychologist in Latvia?**
A: To practice as a psychologist in Latvia, you need a master's degree in psychology from an accredited university and registration with the Latvian Association of Psychologists or the Health Inspectorate for clinical roles. Additional certification is required for specific fields like clinical or school psychology. Recognition of foreign qualifications may require a nostrification process.
