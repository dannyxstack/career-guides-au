## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for messengers, package deliverers, and luggage porters in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for these occupations in Latvia ranges from €900 to €1,200, depending on experience, employer, and location (Rīga vs. regions). Part-time or seasonal roles may pay at the minimum wage level (€700 per month in 2025). Overtime and tips can supplement income.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as messengers or package deliverers in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: This occupation is classified as elementary (ISCO 9621) and does not qualify for the EU Blue Card, which requires a higher education degree or a high salary threshold. Non-EU citizens may obtain a short-term employment visa (D visa) or a residence permit for employment if a Latvian employer sponsors them and proves the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Employer must register the vacancy with the State Employment Agency.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for messengers and package deliverers in Latvia?**
A: With experience, workers can advance to senior courier roles, dispatch coordinators, or logistics supervisors. Some may transition into delivery fleet management or start their own courier business. Additional training in logistics, driving licenses (e.g., for vans or trucks), or customer service skills can open up higher-paying positions in supply chain companies.
