## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a medical imaging technician in Latvia?**
A: The gross monthly salary for medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians in Latvia typically ranges between €1,200 and €1,800, depending on experience, workplace (public vs private sector), and location. Senior technicians in Riga may earn higher. Additional shift allowances or on-call pay may apply.

**Q: Can foreign medical imaging technicians work in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, foreign technicians with a recognized qualification can apply for an EU Blue Card in Latvia. Requirements include a valid job offer in the field, a salary meeting the national threshold (usually at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary), and proof of qualifications. The employer must first register the vacancy with the State Employment Agency.

**Q: What are the main duties and education requirements for this role in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians operate diagnostic imaging equipment (X-ray, CT, MRI) and administer radiation therapy. They ensure equipment safety, position patients, and maintain records. Education typically requires a secondary vocational education in radiology technology or a bachelor's degree in medical engineering. Certification from the Health Inspectorate is mandatory.
