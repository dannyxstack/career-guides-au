## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for nursing professionals in Latvia?**
A: Average gross monthly salary for nurses in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience and location. Public sector nurses earn slightly less than private. Additional allowances for night shifts and overtime apply. Note that wages are lower than EU average but cost of living is lower too.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign nurse to work in Latvia via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Non-EU nurses can apply for a work visa requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. For the EU Blue Card, a higher education degree (or equivalent) and a salary at least 1.2 times the average gross annual wage in Latvia are needed. Nursing qualifications must be recognized by the Latvian healthcare authorities (the Health Inspectorate). The employer submits the application.

**Q: What qualifications do I need to work as a nurse in Latvia?**
A: To work as a nurse in Latvia, you need a recognized nursing diploma or degree. Non-EU qualifications must be evaluated and recognized by the Academic Information Centre and the Health Inspectorate. Language proficiency in Latvian (at least B2 level) is required for patient communication. Registration with the Latvian Nurses’ Association is also mandatory.
