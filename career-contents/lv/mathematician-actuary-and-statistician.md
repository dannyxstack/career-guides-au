## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for mathematicians, actuaries, and statisticians in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector. Entry-level positions may start around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced professionals in banking or insurance can earn €2,500–€4,000 or more. Actuaries and statisticians in high-demand sectors often receive above-average pay. All figures are gross monthly.

**Q: How can a foreign mathematician, actuary, or statistician obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Latvia?**
A: Non-EU professionals can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual wage in Latvia (currently around €34,000). Alternatively, a standard work permit (term residence permit for employment) can be obtained if the employer proves the position cannot be filled locally. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: Are there specific industries in Latvia that highly demand mathematicians, actuaries, or statisticians?**
A: Yes, the financial sector (banks and insurance companies) is the largest employer, especially for actuaries and risk analysts. IT and fintech companies also hire for data science roles. Additionally, government agencies and research institutions employ statisticians for economic and demographic analysis. Demand is moderate but growing with data-driven business trends.
