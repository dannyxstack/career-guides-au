## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Education Managers in Latvia?**
A: Education Managers in Latvia earn a gross monthly salary typically ranging from €2000 to €3500, depending on the institution type (public vs. private), location, and experience. Senior positions at universities or large schools may exceed this range.

**Q: What are the visa or EU Blue Card requirements for non-EU Education Managers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can obtain an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and have a job offer with a salary meeting the annual minimum threshold (e.g., €29,736 in 2024). Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) applies for temporary employment. Both require employer sponsorship and registration with the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What qualifications and experience are needed to become an Education Manager in Latvia?**
A: Most Education Manager roles require a master's degree in education management, pedagogy, or a related field. Proven experience in teaching or school administration is essential, typically 3-5 years. In public institutions, proficiency in Latvian is mandatory. Private international schools may accept English-only candidates.
