## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ships' deck crew in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for deck crew (ISCO 8350) in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, vessel type, and contract terms. Overtime and sea bonuses can increase total pay. Wages are often negotiated per voyage or contract period.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU deck crew to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU deck crew need a work visa sponsored by a Latvian employer who obtains a work permit from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. The EU Blue Card is for highly qualified workers and typically requires a higher education degree and salary threshold; many deck crew roles may not meet these criteria. A national long-stay visa (D visa) for employment is the common route.

**Q: What qualifications are required to work as deck crew in Latvia?**
A: Required qualifications include a valid STCW Basic Safety Training certificate. For advanced roles, Able Seaman or Deck Officer certification is needed. Proficiency in English is essential. All crew must be registered with the Latvian Maritime Administration.
