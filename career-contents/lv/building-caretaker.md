## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a building caretaker in Latvia?**
A: Building caretakers in Latvia typically earn a gross monthly salary between €700 and €1,000, depending on experience and employer. The minimum wage in 2024 is €700, so entry-level positions start there. Overtime and housing benefits may supplement pay in some cases.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a building caretaker in Latvia? What visa options are available?**
A: Yes, but a work visa (D visa) is required for non-EU citizens. The employer must obtain a work permit from the State Employment Agency. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable to this occupation as it requires higher education or equivalent for high-skilled roles, which caretaking does not meet. Alternative: a temporary residence permit for employment.

**Q: What are the typical career progression opportunities for building caretakers in Latvia?**
A: With experience, building caretakers can advance to senior caretaker or facility manager positions. Additional certifications in building maintenance or property management can lead to roles in larger residential or commercial properties. Some also start their own property maintenance businesses.
