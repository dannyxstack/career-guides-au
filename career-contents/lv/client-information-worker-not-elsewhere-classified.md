## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Client Information Worker (not elsewhere classified) in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for client information workers in Latvia vary by experience and industry. Entry-level positions may start around €800 gross per month, while experienced workers can earn up to €1,400. The median salary is approximately €1,000. These figures are indicative and may change based on employer and location.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes to work as a Client Information Worker in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a work permit (D visa), sponsored by an employer. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia). For this occupation, the Blue Card may be less common due to typical salary levels.

**Q: What are the main duties of a Client Information Worker (not elsewhere classified) in Latvia?**
A: These workers handle customer inquiries via phone, email, or in person, provide information on products or services, process orders, resolve complaints, and update records. They often work in call centers, government agencies, or private companies. Strong communication and problem-solving skills are essential.
