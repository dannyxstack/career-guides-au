## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a shop supervisor in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, shop supervisors generally earn a gross monthly salary between €1,000 and €1,500, depending on experience, location, and employer size. Larger retail chains in Riga may offer higher wages. Overtime and performance bonuses can increase total earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a shop supervisor in Latvia through a work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU citizens can work as shop supervisors in Latvia via a work visa or EU Blue Card. The employer must secure a work permit from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. For the EU Blue Card, the role must be highly qualified, requiring a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia.

**Q: What career progression is typical for a shop supervisor in Latvia?**
A: Shop supervisors in Latvia often advance from sales associate roles. They oversee daily store operations, manage teams, and handle customer service. With experience, they can move up to store manager, area manager, or regional supervisor positions within retail chains.
