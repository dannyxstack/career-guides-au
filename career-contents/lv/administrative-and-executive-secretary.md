## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for administrative and executive secretaries in Latvia?**
A: According to the State Revenue Service and labor market surveys, the gross monthly salary for entry-level administrative secretaries in Latvia typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,500. Experienced executive secretaries in Riga or with specialized skills may earn €1,500 to €2,200 or more. Salaries vary by industry, company size, and location.

**Q: What are the official routes for a foreign administrative secretary to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as administrative secretaries via a D visa (long-term) or EU Blue Card, provided the gross monthly salary meets the Blue Card threshold (€1,800 as of 2025). The employer must obtain a work permit from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. Short-term C visas are for stays up to 90 days.

**Q: What qualifications are most valued for administrative secretaries in Latvia?**
A: Employers in Latvia prioritize fluency in Latvian and English, strong Microsoft Office skills, organizational ability, and discretion. A bachelor's degree in business administration or a related field is beneficial but not always required. Experience with event coordination or accounting software can give candidates a competitive edge.
