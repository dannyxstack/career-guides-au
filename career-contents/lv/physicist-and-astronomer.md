## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for physicists and astronomers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for physicists and astronomers in Latvia vary by sector. In research institutions and universities, entry-level salaries range from €1,200 to €1,800 per month gross. Experienced professionals in private R&D or high-tech industries can earn €2,500 to €3,500 monthly. Salaries are generally lower than in Western Europe but offset by lower living costs.

**Q: What are the official visa routes for non-EU physicists and astronomers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU physicists and astronomers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia. Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) under the Immigration Law is possible if the job is in short supply. Both routes require employer sponsorship.

**Q: What career opportunities exist for physicists and astronomers in Latvia?**
A: Physicists and astronomers in Latvia work primarily in universities (University of Latvia, Riga Technical University), research institutes (Institute of Solid State Physics, Institute of Astronomy), and private sector R&D (electronics, optics, energy). Government space programs are limited, but EU research grants provide project-based opportunities. Many specialists also pursue academic careers or move into data science and engineering.
