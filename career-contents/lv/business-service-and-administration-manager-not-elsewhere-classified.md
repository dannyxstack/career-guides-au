## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for Business Services and Administration Managers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for managers in this category vary widely based on experience and company size. In Latvia, gross monthly salaries typically range from €2,500 to €5,000, with senior roles in larger firms reaching higher figures. According to official statistics, the average gross monthly wage for managers was around €3,500 in 2023.

**Q: What official routes exist for foreign managers to work in Latvia via work visa or EU Blue Card?**
A: Foreign managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the average Latvian wage. Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) can be obtained through a company sponsorship for a specific position. Both routes require approval from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What are typical duties and qualifications for Business Services and Administration Managers in Latvia?**
A: These managers oversee business services like HR, finance, or facilities, and may manage cross-functional teams. Typical duties include strategic planning, budget management, and process optimization. Employers usually require a master's degree in business administration or related field, plus 3-5 years of managerial experience. Proficiency in English and Latvian is often needed.
