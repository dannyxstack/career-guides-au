## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a Forestry Technician in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for a Forestry Technician in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,800, depending on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions start around €1,000, while senior technicians can earn up to €2,200. Salaries in public sector forestry enterprises may be slightly lower than in private companies.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU Forestry Technicians to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU nationals can work as Forestry Technicians in Latvia via two main routes: the EU Blue Card for highly qualified workers, requiring a university degree or 5 years’ professional experience; or a National Work Permit (D visa) sponsored by a Latvian employer. Both require a job offer meeting minimum salary thresholds (for Blue Card, at least 1.5× average gross annual salary).

**Q: What are the main duties of a Forestry Technician in Latvia?**
A: Forestry Technicians in Latvia assist forest managers in planning sustainable harvesting, reforestation, and conservation. They collect field data on tree species, health, and growth; mark trees for cutting; supervise logging crews; and ensure compliance with Latvian forestry laws. They often use GPS and GIS tools to map forest stands and monitor biodiversity.
