## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for signwriters, decorative painters, engravers, and etchers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for these trades in Latvia is approximately €1,000 to €1,500, depending on experience and location. Entry-level workers may earn around €800, while skilled professionals in Riga can earn up to €1,800. Overtime and specialized skills can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work in Latvia as signwriters, decorative painters, engravers, or etchers via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Non-EU citizens typically need a work permit or EU Blue Card. The Blue Card requires a higher education degree or 5 years of professional experience, plus a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€2,022 gross monthly in 2024). Since these occupations often pay below this threshold, a standard work permit is more common, which requires employer sponsorship and labor market testing.

**Q: What training or qualifications are needed to become a signwriter or decorative painter in Latvia?**
A: Vocational education in arts, design, or decorative painting is common. Programs at state-accredited technical schools last 2–3 years. Apprenticeships with experienced craftspeople also provide entry. Certification is not mandatory but improves job prospects. Continuous learning in digital tools (e.g., vinyl cutting, CNC engraving) is increasingly important.
