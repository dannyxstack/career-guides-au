## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a companion or valet in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for companions and valets in Latvia vary by employer and experience, typically ranging from €700 to €1,200 per month gross. Wages often depend on responsibilities, such as elderly care or household management. Part-time or live-in positions may offer lower base pay but include accommodation or meals.

**Q: What work visa or EU Blue Card options exist for companions and valets in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens can work as companions or valets in Latvia via a work permit sponsored by a local employer. The EU Blue Card requires a higher education degree and a salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage, which this occupation rarely meets. A standard temporary residence permit for employment is the usual route.

**Q: What are the typical duties of a companion or valet in Latvia?**
A: Typical duties include assisting with personal care, household chores, organizing schedules, accompanying clients to appointments, and providing light administrative support. In Latvia, companions often work with elderly or disabled individuals, while valets focus on wardrobe management, errands, and driving. Flexibility and discretion are essential.
