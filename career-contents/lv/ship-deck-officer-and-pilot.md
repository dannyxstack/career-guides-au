## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for ships’ deck officers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for deck officers in Latvia vary by rank and employer, ranging from €2,000 to €3,000 gross monthly for junior officers and up to €4,500 for senior captains. These figures are averages; actual pay can be higher on international vessels or with overtime. Salary negotiations often include sea service allowances and benefits.

**Q: Can non-EU deck officers work in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can apply for an EU Blue Card in Latvia if they have a higher education degree (or equivalent) and a valid job offer from a Latvian employer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average gross wage (approx. €1,920 per month in 2024). The employer must obtain a work permit, and the profession requires recognition of qualifications by the Maritime Administration of Latvia.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a deck officer in Latvia?**
A: To work as a deck officer in Latvia, you must hold the appropriate STCW (International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) certificate. For unlimited voyages, a Master Mariner or Deck Officer license from an IMO-approved institution is required. Non-EU qualifications must be recognized by the Maritime Administration. Proficiency in English and Latvian (or Russian) is often required.
