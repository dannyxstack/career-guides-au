## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for odd-job persons in Latvia?**
A: Odd-job persons in Latvia usually earn per task or hourly. Hourly rates range from €5 to €10, depending on job complexity and region. Monthly earnings vary but often align with the national minimum wage of €700 for full-time work. Many work informally, so income can be inconsistent.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as odd-job persons in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card targets highly skilled workers with a university degree and a high salary, which odd-job persons typically do not meet. Non-EU nationals can instead apply for a work permit and residence permit for employment, provided the employer proves the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Short-term seasonal permits may also be available.

**Q: What are common tasks for odd-job persons in Latvia?**
A: Odd-job persons perform temporary tasks such as cleaning, gardening, moving furniture, minor repairs, painting, and household chores. They are often hired by private individuals or small businesses for short-term projects. Skills are typically gained through experience rather than formal training.
