**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Marking standardised assignments and exam papers
- Generate basic lesson plans and classroom materials
- Record and analyze student attendance and performance data
- Answering common knowledge questions (e.g., math calculations, spelling)
- Managing classroom multimedia equipment and basic IT troubleshooting

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI adaptive learning platforms to customize exercises and feedback for each student
- Use AI to analyze student behavior and performance data to early identify learning difficulties or social-emotional issues
- Quickly generate differentiated teaching resources (e.g., reading materials at varying difficulty levels) using AI tools
- Using AI assistants to plan course progress and evaluate teaching effectiveness

**Human moat:**

- face-to-face emotional communication and building trust between teachers and students
- Managing unexpected classroom situations and behavior
- Tailored teaching based on deep understanding of individual students
- Passing on values, critical thinking, and social skills
- Complex communication and collaboration with parents

**Skills to build:**

- Use of AI educational tools (e.g., adaptive learning platforms, intelligent grading systems)
- Data interpretation and student assessment analysis
- Methods for integrating AI resources in instructional design
- Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) and counselling skills
- Interdisciplinary project-based learning (PBL) design
- Digital Literacy and Cybersecurity Awareness

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a primary school teacher in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for primary school teachers in Latvia ranges from €1,200 to €1,500, depending on experience and school location. Salaries are set by the state and can include additional pay for extracurricular activities or special education needs. Teachers often receive bonuses for performance and may have opportunities for increased earnings through overtime or leadership roles.

**Q: What are the official work visa or EU Blue Card routes for non-EU primary school teachers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU teachers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer meeting the salary threshold (€1,503 gross monthly in 2024). Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) and temporary residence permit require an employer-sponsored job offer and approval from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. Qualifications must be recognised by Latvia's academic information centre.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become a primary school teacher in Latvia?**
A: To teach at primary level in Latvia, you need at least a bachelor's degree in education or a related field, plus a teaching qualification. Foreign qualifications must be recognised by the Academic Information Centre. Teachers must also be proficient in Latvian (C1 level) and register with the Register of Pedagogues. Continuous professional development is mandatory to maintain certification.
