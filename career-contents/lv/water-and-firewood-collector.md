## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for water and firewood collectors in Latvia?**
A: Water and firewood collectors in Latvia typically earn around the national minimum wage, which in 2024 is €700 per month gross. Actual earnings may vary depending on the employer, region, and hours worked. This occupation is often informal, so pay may be below official minimum in some cases.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as water and firewood collectors in Latvia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not applicable for this occupation as it requires a higher education degree and higher salary threshold. Non-EU nationals can obtain a temporary residence permit for employment if the employer proves the vacancy cannot be filled locally. Work visas require employer sponsorship and employer compliance with Latvian labor laws.

**Q: What are the main duties of water and firewood collectors in Latvia?**
A: Water and firewood collectors gather natural resources for household or small-scale use. Duties include collecting firewood from forests, transporting it, and fetching water from wells or natural sources. The work is physically demanding, often in rural areas, and may involve manual labor without modern equipment.
