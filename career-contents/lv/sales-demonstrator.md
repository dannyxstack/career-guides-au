## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Repeated on-site demonstrations of product standard functions
- Using pre-recorded videos or AI-generated content to replace on-site explanations
- Frequently asked customer questions; replaced by chatbots
- Product parameter query and comparison, replaced by self-service terminals or AR applications

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI analyzes customer data to assist in customizing personalized presentation scripts
- Real-time speech transcription and translation, supporting multilingual audiences
- AR/VR technology enhances the immersion and interactivity of product displays
- Sales data dashboard helps presenters adjust strategies in real time

**Human moat:**

- On-site engagement and improvisation skills
- Soft skills for building emotional trust and client relationships
- Creativity and narrative skills in complex product experiences
- Flexible adaptation for cross-cultural and non-standard scenarios

**Skills to build:**

- Digital storytelling and multimedia content production
- AR/VR demonstration tool operation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a sales demonstrator in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for sales demonstrators in Latvia vary by experience and employer, but they often align with the national minimum wage (€700 per month in 2024) plus performance bonuses. Full-time demonstrators may earn between €800 and €1,200 gross monthly, depending on the industry, such as electronics or cosmetics.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a sales demonstrator in Latvia via the EU Blue Card or other routes?**
A: The EU Blue Card is typically for highly qualified professionals requiring higher education and higher salary thresholds, which sales demonstrators generally do not meet. Non-EU nationals must obtain a standard work permit, requiring employer sponsorship and a labor market test to prove the vacancy cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for sales demonstrators in Latvia?**
A: Experienced sales demonstrators can advance to supervisory roles, brand ambassador positions, or transition into sales management. Specialization in high-demand product categories like electronics or cosmetics can lead to roles such as merchandiser or retail trainer, offering higher pay and more responsibility.
