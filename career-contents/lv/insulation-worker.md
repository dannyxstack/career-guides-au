## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for insulation workers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for insulation workers in Latvia ranges from €1,000 to €1,400, depending on experience and employer. Entry-level positions start around €900, while skilled workers with certifications may earn up to €1,600. Overtime and project-based bonuses can increase total pay.

**Q: What is the work visa or EU Blue Card route for insulation workers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU insulation workers can obtain an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree or at least five years of professional experience, and a job offer with a salary at least 1.5 times the national average (currently around €1,800 gross monthly). Alternatively, a temporary residence permit for employment is available with a valid work contract and employer sponsorship.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an insulation worker in Latvia?**
A: To work as an insulation worker in Latvia, you typically need a vocational education in construction or a related field, often completed at a professional school. Apprenticeships or on-the-job training are common. Certification in specific insulation materials or safety standards can improve job prospects. Knowledge of Latvian or Russian is beneficial.
