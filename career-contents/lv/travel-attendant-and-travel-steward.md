## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for travel attendants in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for travel attendants in Latvia typically range from €900 to €1,500 gross per month, depending on the airline, experience, and flight hours. Entry-level positions start around €900, while senior attendants can earn up to €1,500. Additional allowances for overnight stays and language bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as travel attendants in Latvia via an EU Blue Card or work visa?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can work as travel attendants in Latvia if they secure a job offer from a Latvian employer. A standard work visa is an option. The EU Blue Card may be applicable for those with a higher education degree and a salary meeting the national threshold, which is updated annually by the Latvian government. Specific requirements include a valid employment contract and qualifications.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become a travel attendant in Latvia?**
A: To become a travel attendant in Latvia, you need at least a secondary education, fluency in English and preferably another language, and a certificate of no criminal record. Most airlines require completion of their own training program, covering safety procedures and customer service. A medical fitness certificate and ability to swim are also typically mandatory.
