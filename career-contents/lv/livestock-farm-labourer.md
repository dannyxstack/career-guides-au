## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for livestock farm labourers in Latvia ranges from €700 to €1,000, often near the national minimum wage (€700 as of 2024). Pay may vary based on farm size, region, and overtime. Some employers offer accommodation or meals as part of the compensation package.

**Q: Can a foreign national work as a livestock farm labourer in Latvia under the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card is not typically available for livestock farm labourers as it requires a high-skilled position with a higher education degree or at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary. For low-skilled farm work, non-EU nationals may enter via a national work visa (D visa) with employer sponsorship and Labour Market test proof.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for livestock farm labourers in Latvia?**
A: Starting as a general labourer, one can advance to specialised roles like milking or feeding supervisor, then to farm manager with experience and training. Agricultural vocational schools offer short courses. Some workers move to machinery operation or start their own small farm. Skills in herd health monitoring and modern farming technology are valued.
