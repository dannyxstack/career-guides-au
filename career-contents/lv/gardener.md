## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for gardeners in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for gardeners in Latvia typically start at the national minimum wage, which is set annually by the government. According to the State Revenue Service, actual earnings vary by employer, experience, and location, but no specific average is published. Seasonal or part-time work is common.

**Q: What work visa options exist for foreign gardeners in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU gardeners can work in Latvia via a standard work visa (D visa), requiring a job offer and employer sponsorship. The EU Blue Card, intended for highly skilled professionals with a university degree and higher salary threshold, is generally not applicable for gardeners.

**Q: What qualifications are required to be a gardener in Latvia?**
A: Formal qualifications are not mandatory, but vocational training in horticulture or landscaping is valued. Many employers prefer candidates with practical experience or a relevant certificate from a Latvian vocational school. Some positions require a driver's license or pesticide application permit.
