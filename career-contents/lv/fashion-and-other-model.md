## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for fashion and other models in Latvia?**
A: Salaries vary widely based on experience, client, and project type. Entry-level models may earn between €500 and €1,000 per assignment, while established professionals can earn €2,000–€5,000 monthly. Payment is often project-based or hourly, with top models commanding higher rates for commercial or runway work.

**Q: Can non-EU models work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card or work permit?**
A: Non-EU models can work in Latvia through a standard work permit, which requires a job offer and labor market test. The EU Blue Card is available for highly skilled workers meeting a salary threshold (currently €2,143 monthly gross), but modeling may not always qualify unless the role involves specialized skills or management. Consult Latvia's Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs.

**Q: What are the main career opportunities for models in Latvia?**
A: Opportunities include fashion shows, editorial shoots, commercial advertising, and e-commerce catalog modeling. Many models work through agencies in Riga, collaborating with local designers and international brands. Career progression can lead to international assignments or roles in related fields like acting or influencer marketing.
