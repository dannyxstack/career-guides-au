## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for protective services workers in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, protective services workers (e.g., security guards, private investigators) earn between €800 and €1,200 gross per month. Experienced or specialized roles may reach €1,500. Minimum wage is €500 (2023). Overtime and shift differentials can increase earnings.

**Q: What are the work visa options for protective services workers in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU workers need a work permit and residence permit. Employers must pass a labor market test. The EU Blue Card requires a salary of at least 1.5x the average gross wage (~€1,600/month in 2023), which may be challenging for typical protective roles. Lower-skilled positions may qualify for other work permits.

**Q: What career progression is available for protective services workers in Latvia?**
A: Protective services workers can advance to supervisor or security manager roles. Additional training in cybersecurity, fire safety, or first aid improves prospects. Some transition to law enforcement or private investigation. Large security firms and government contracts offer stable careers.
