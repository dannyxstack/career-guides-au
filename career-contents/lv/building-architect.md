## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for building architects in Latvia?**
A: Entry-level building architects in Latvia earn around €1,200–€1,800 gross per month, while experienced architects can earn €2,000–€3,500. Senior architects or those in managerial roles may earn €4,000 or more. Salaries vary by experience, project scope, and location (Riga typically offers higher pay).

**Q: How can a non-EU architect obtain a work visa or EU Blue Card for Latvia?**
A: Non-EU architects must secure a job offer from a Latvian employer. For an EU Blue Card, the position must require higher education and meet the Latvian salary threshold (e.g., at least 1.5× the average gross annual salary). Alternatively, a national work visa (D visa) and temporary residence permit are available, requiring employer sponsorship and compliance with labour market tests.

**Q: What are the licensing and professional requirements for architects in Latvia?**
A: To practice as an architect in Latvia, you must be registered with the Latvian Association of Architects and obtain a professional practice certificate. This requires a recognized university degree in architecture, at least two years of supervised experience, and passing a professional exam. Continuous professional development is mandatory to maintain certification.
