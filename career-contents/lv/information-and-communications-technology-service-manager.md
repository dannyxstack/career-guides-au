## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Information and Communications Technology Services Manager in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for ICT Services Managers in Latvia ranges from €2,500 to €4,000, depending on experience and company size. Senior managers in larger firms or multinationals can earn €5,000 or more. These figures are based on data from the State Revenue Service and industry surveys.

**Q: What are the official work visa routes for non-EU ICT Services Managers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU ICT managers can apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and a job offer with a gross annual salary at least 1.5 times the national average (€21,600 in 2023). Alternatively, a work permit is possible after a labor market test demonstrating no suitable local candidate.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for ICT Services Managers in Latvia?**
A: ICT Services Managers in Latvia can advance to Chief Technology Officer (CTO), IT Director, or Head of Digital Transformation roles. The growing tech sector, especially in Riga, offers paths into consulting or entrepreneurship. Skills in cybersecurity, cloud computing, and agile management are highly valued for progression.
