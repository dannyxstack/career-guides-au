**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Underwater visual inspection and video recording: AI image recognition automatically labels defects.
- Routine structural measurement: ROV with sensors automatically collects data
- Underwater environment monitoring: AI analyzes parameters such as water temperature and turbidity
- Simple salvage operation: remote-controlled robotic arm performs standard operations

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted decision-making: real-time analysis of diver physiological data to warn of decompression sickness risk
- Augmented reality headset: overlays structural diagrams and repair instructions to improve work accuracy
- Predictive maintenance: AI analyzes historical data to optimize maintenance schedules.
- Remote specialist collaboration: AI translation and 3D reconstruction help land-based experts guide

**Human moat:**

- Manual operation and quick judgment in emergency situations
- Fine tactile sensation and force feedback (e.g., valve operation)
- Adaptability in harsh environments (current, visibility)
- Legal and safety responsibility attribution (divers must sign on-site)

**Skills to build:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for underwater divers in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for underwater divers in Latvia vary by experience and specialization. Entry-level divers earn around €1,200–€1,500 gross per month, while experienced commercial divers can earn €2,000–€3,500. Additional pay may be provided for hazardous or deep-sea work, but overall compensation is lower than in Western Europe.

**Q: What are the official routes for non-EU underwater divers to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU divers can apply for a work visa or an EU Blue Card. For the Blue Card, they need a job offer meeting Latvia’s minimum salary threshold, a higher education degree or five years of professional experience, and a recognized qualification. Alternatively, employers can sponsor a standard work visa for highly skilled or seasonal roles.

**Q: What qualifications are required to become an underwater diver in Latvia?**
A: To work as an underwater diver in Latvia, you need a recognized commercial diving certificate, such as those from the Professional Association of Diving Instructors (PADI) or equivalent. Additionally, a valid medical fitness certificate and, for certain jobs, a separate qualification in underwater welding or inspection are required.
