## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a midwife in Latvia?**
A: Midwives in Latvia earn a gross monthly salary typically between €1,200 and €1,800, depending on experience and workplace (public hospital vs private clinic). Senior midwives or those with specialized skills may earn higher. Additional on-call allowances and benefits may apply.

**Q: What are the visa options for a non-EU midwife to work in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU midwives can apply for an EU Blue Card if they hold a higher education degree and have a job offer meeting the salary threshold (currently about 1.5x average gross salary, approx. €1,800/month). Alternatively, a national work permit (D visa) is possible after obtaining recognition of midwifery qualifications from the Latvian Health Inspectorate.

**Q: What is the demand for midwives in Latvia?**
A: Latvia faces a shortage of midwives, particularly in rural areas. The profession is in high demand due to an aging workforce and low numbers of new graduates. Career opportunities exist in hospitals, maternity clinics, and community health centers. Continuing education is encouraged.
