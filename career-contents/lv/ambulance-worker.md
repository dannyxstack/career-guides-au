## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for ambulance workers in Latvia?**
A: The average gross monthly salary for ambulance workers in Latvia ranges from approximately €1,200 to €1,600, depending on experience and shift patterns. Additional allowances may apply for night shifts and overtime. Salaries are determined by collective agreements and public sector pay scales.

**Q: What is the work visa process for non-EU ambulance workers in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU citizens must obtain a work permit and a residence permit. The employer applies for a work permit with the State Employment Agency, confirming the vacancy cannot be filled locally. After approval, the worker applies for a national D visa at a Latvian embassy. The EU Blue Card is available for highly qualified workers meeting higher salary thresholds, but ambulance workers typically do not meet that criterion.

**Q: What career progression opportunities exist for ambulance workers in Latvia?**
A: Ambulance workers can advance to senior paramedic roles, shift supervisors, or instructor positions. Additional training enables specialization in emergency medicine or disaster response. Some pursue studies to become emergency doctors or nurses. Public sector experience is valued for leadership roles in the health emergency service.
