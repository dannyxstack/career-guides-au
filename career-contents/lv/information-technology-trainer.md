- Write and update standard training manuals and documentation
- Create and score basic quizzes and assessments

- Record and edit basic training videos
- Track student progress and generate learning reports

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Provides instant, personalized learning support through AI-driven virtual assistants
- Use AI to generate interactive simulations and virtual laboratory environments
- Expand training audiences with AI translation and multilingual support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an Information Technology Trainer in Latvia?**
A: Salaries for IT trainers in Latvia vary by experience and employer, typically ranging from €1,500 to €3,000 gross per month. Senior trainers or those with specialized certifications may earn higher. Public and private institutions offer different scales. Always check current labor market data for updated figures.

**Q: What are the work visa or EU Blue Card requirements for IT Trainers in Latvia?**
A: Non-EU IT trainers can work in Latvia via an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree and a job offer with salary at least 1.5 times the average gross wage (approx. €2,000/month in 2023). Alternatively, a national D visa for employment is possible. Both require employer sponsorship and meeting labor market tests.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to become an IT Trainer in Latvia?**
A: Typically, a bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field is required, along with teaching or training experience. Certification in specific technologies (e.g., Microsoft, Cisco) is advantageous. Fluency in Latvian and English is often necessary. Some employers may accept equivalent practical experience.
