## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for meter readers in Latvia?**
A: Meter readers in Latvia typically earn between €800 and €1,200 per month before taxes. Salaries vary based on employer, experience, and location, with urban areas like Riga offering slightly higher pay. Overtime and bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as meter readers in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: No, the EU Blue Card is for highly skilled workers, which this occupation does not qualify for. Non-EU citizens must obtain a temporary residence permit for employment. The employer must secure a work permit from the State Employment Agency, which involves a labor market test to prove no local candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for meter readers in Latvia?**
A: Career progression is limited but possible. Experienced meter readers may become team leaders, supervisors, or move into related roles such as billing, data analysis, or customer service within utility companies. Additional training in energy efficiency or management can open further doors.
