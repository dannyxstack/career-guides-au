## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average income for astrologers and fortune-tellers in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, astrologers and fortune-tellers typically earn between €500 and €1,500 per month, depending on reputation, client base, and services offered. Many work as self-employed individuals, so income varies. The average is around €800–€1,000 monthly, but official statistics are scarce as this occupation often operates informally.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as astrologers in Latvia via the EU Blue Card?**
A: The EU Blue Card in Latvia is reserved for highly qualified workers with a higher education degree and a job offer meeting a salary threshold (e.g., 1.5 times the average gross annual salary). Astrologers and fortune-tellers generally do not qualify, as the occupation is not considered highly skilled. Non-EU nationals may instead apply for a temporary residence permit for self-employment or employment through standard work visa routes.

**Q: Is any official licensing or certification required to practice astrology in Latvia?**
A: In Latvia, astrologers and fortune-tellers are not regulated by the state. No official license or certification is required to practice. However, to operate legally, practitioners must register as self-employed or establish a business, and comply with tax regulations. Many choose to obtain voluntary certifications from private associations to enhance credibility, but these are not legally mandatory.
