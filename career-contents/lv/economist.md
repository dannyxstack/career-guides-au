## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for economists in Latvia?**
A: Economists in Latvia typically earn a gross monthly salary between €1,800 and €2,500, depending on experience and sector. Entry-level positions start around €1,400, while senior roles in finance or government can exceed €3,000. Salaries in Riga are generally higher than in other regions.

**Q: Can economists obtain an EU Blue Card to work in Latvia?**
A: Yes, economists can apply for an EU Blue Card if they have a higher education degree (at least 3 years) and a job offer with a gross salary at least 1.5 times the average gross annual salary in Latvia (approximately €24,000 in 2024). The Blue Card is initially valid for up to 2 years and renewable.

**Q: What is the job outlook for economists in Latvia?**
A: The job outlook is favorable, with demand in banking, public sector, and private companies. Economists are needed for analysis, forecasting, and policy advice. Growing fields include data analysis and fintech. Fluency in Latvian and English is often required, and skills in econometrics and statistical software are highly valued.
