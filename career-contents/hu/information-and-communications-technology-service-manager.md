# Information and Communications Technology Services Manager — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 1330 (ISCO08)**

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) Services Managers plan, direct, and coordinate the provision of IT and telecommunications services within organizations. In Hungary, this role is critical as the country rapidly digitizes its economy, with strong demand in Budapest and other urban centers. Managers oversee teams, budgets, and service-level agreements, ensuring reliable and secure technology infrastructure. The role requires a blend of technical expertise and business acumen, often involving vendor management and strategic planning. Hungary's growing tech sector, including software development, cloud services, and cybersecurity, drives demand for these managers, who typically command above-average salaries and work in dynamic environments.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for an ICT Services Manager in Hungary?**
A: Salaries for ICT Services Managers in Hungary are competitive, typically ranging from HUF 12,000,000 to HUF 24,000,000 per year depending on experience, company size, and location (Budapest pays higher). Senior roles can exceed HUF 30,000,000.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to apply for an EU Blue Card in Hungary?**
A: Yes, you need a binding job offer from a Hungarian employer with a gross annual salary meeting the EU Blue Card threshold (currently approximately HUF 9,000,000). The employer must also prove the position cannot be filled locally.

**Q: What is the career progression for ICT Services Managers in Hungary?**
A: Typically, you start as an IT project manager or service desk lead. With 5-10 years of experience, you can become an ICT Services Manager, then advance to IT Director or CIO. Specializations in cloud, cybersecurity, or data analytics boost prospects.
