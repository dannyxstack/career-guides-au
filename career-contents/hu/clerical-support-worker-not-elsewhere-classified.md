# Clerical Support Workers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 4419 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of clerical support tasks not specifically classified elsewhere, including filing, data entry, mail handling, and general office assistance. In Hungary, these roles are found across all sectors, from large corporations to small businesses and government institutions. The demand is steady, with many positions requiring only secondary education, making it an accessible entry point into the labour market. However, the role is increasingly affected by digitalisation and automation, which may reduce the need for routine clerical tasks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★½☆).**

Career progression typically involves moving from entry-level clerical roles to senior administrative assistant, office manager, or specialist positions in areas like accounting or human resources. With additional training or education, workers can advance to executive assistant or administrative coordinator roles. Hungarian employers value reliability and proficiency in office software, and fluency in English can open doors in multinational companies.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual data entry and copy-paste operations
- Document Classification, Filing, and Retrieval
- Standardized email and notification template generation
- Schedule planning and meeting coordination
- Simple tables and report generation

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to analyze document content and extract key information
- Dictation and transcription of documents using voice assistants
- Use smart tools for multilingual translation and proofreading
- Automate repetitive approval processes using RPA
- Using AI to assist in generating complex report drafts

**Human moat:**

- Flexible judgment in handling ambiguous, non-standard requests
- Cross-departmental communication and complex interpersonal coordination
- Understanding and interpretation of policies and regulations
- Discretion and ethical judgment when handling sensitive or confidential information
- Integrated decision-making combining outputs from different AI tools

**Skills to build:**

- RPA tool usage (e.g., UiPath, Blue Prism)
- AI document processing tools (e.g., Azure Cognitive Services)
- Basic data analysis and advanced Excel functions
- Project management and collaboration software (e.g., Jira, Trello)
- Basic knowledge of digital ethics and data privacy
- Basic programming or scripting (Python basics)

**Key growth areas:**

- digitalisation
- customer service
- office management
- multilingual support

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a clerical support worker in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary by sector and region, but generally range from the national minimum wage to slightly above average. For specific figures, consult recent KSH data or job advertisements.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work permit for a clerical support job in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but the employer must obtain a labour market test (unless the occupation is on the shortage list, which clerical support is not). The process is lengthy and not guaranteed, making this a less favourable migration route.
