# Teaching Professionals Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 2359 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a diverse range of teaching roles not specifically classified elsewhere, including special education teachers, adult educators, and private tutors. In Hungary, demand for such professionals is moderate, with opportunities in both public and private sectors. The labour market is characterised by an ageing workforce in education, creating gradual replacement demand. However, competition exists in urban areas, and salaries are generally lower than in other professional fields. Many positions require fluency in Hungarian, limiting opportunities for non-native speakers.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for teaching professionals not elsewhere classified in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary greatly by institution type, experience, and location. Public sector teachers earn according to a government pay scale, which has been gradually increasing but remains below the national average for professionals. Private tutors and adult educators often charge hourly rates. Official statistics show median annual earnings for this group are around 3-4 million HUF, but this is subject to change.

**Q: How can a non-EU national work as a teaching professional in Hungary?**
A: Non-EU nationals must secure a job offer and apply for a Work and Residence Permit. The employer must prove that the position could not be filled by an EU citizen. Additionally, most teaching roles require high proficiency in Hungarian, which is a significant barrier. Some international schools or language institutions may offer English-medium positions, but competition is strong. The EU Blue Card is generally not applicable due to salary thresholds.
