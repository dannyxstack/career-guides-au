# Education Manager — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 1345 (ISCO08)**

Education managers in Hungary oversee operations at educational institutions ranging from kindergartens to universities, encompassing staff management, curriculum development, regulatory compliance, and strategic planning. The role is pivotal in adapting to national education reforms, including digitalization and competency-based teaching. With a centralized public education system, managers navigate state frameworks while private and international schools offer more autonomy. Demand is steady, driven by retirements and the expansion of early childhood and vocational education.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an education manager in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary widely; in public schools, a principal's gross monthly salary ranges from 500,000 to 700,000 HUF, while in private international schools it can be 800,000 to 1,200,000 HUF or more, depending on experience and institution.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as education managers in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but typically require a work and residence permit with a job offer. The EU Blue Card is also possible if the salary threshold is met. The employer must prove the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA national. Some positions in public schools may be restricted to Hungarian citizens.

**Q: Is a teaching background mandatory for education managers?**
A: In most cases, yes. Public school principals must have a teaching degree and at least 5 years of teaching experience. Private institutions may accept related degrees, but a teaching background is highly valued.
