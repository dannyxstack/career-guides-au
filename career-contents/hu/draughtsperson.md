# Draughtsperson — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 3118 (ISCO08)**

Draughtspersons in Hungary prepare detailed technical drawings for construction, manufacturing, and engineering projects, using CAD software and BIM tools. They collaborate closely with architects, engineers, and designers to convert rough sketches and specifications into precise plans. The Hungarian labour market offers steady demand due to ongoing infrastructure investments, EU-funded development projects, and a growing manufacturing sector. While not classified as a shortage occupation, skilled draughtspersons with CAD expertise are valued, particularly in Budapest and in regions with active industrial parks.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for draughtspersons in Hungary typically starts with entry-level drafting and moves toward senior draughtsperson, team leader, or specialized roles in BIM coordination. Many advance by gaining expertise in specific software (e.g., AutoCAD, Revit) or by moving into design engineering, project management, or quality assurance. Continuing education through vocational courses or acquiring engineering technician qualifications (e.g., OKJ certificate) is common. There is a clear path to self-employment as a freelance drafter, especially for those with industry contacts and German/English language skills.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software to create standard 2D technical drawings
- Convert hand-drawn sketches or scanned drawings into digital drawings.
- Check drawing dimensions, annotations, etc., for compliance with industry standards
- Generating repetitive design variants with standard parts libraries
- Basic 3D model to 2D projection views

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI design assistants to quickly generate preliminary plans, focusing on creative adjustments
- Use AI simulation analysis to identify structural conflicts in drawings early
- Quickly Modify Drawing Parameters Using Natural Language Commands
- Integrate Building Information Modeling (BIM) with AI to check collision issues
- Automatically generate multiple design options for client or engineer evaluation

**Human moat:**

- Understand design intent and engineering constraints, making creative trade-offs
- Interpersonal communication and negotiation skills when collaborating with engineers, architects, etc.
- Handling non-standard, highly customized special structures.
- Perform quality audit and reliability assessment on AI output
- Tasks requiring physical presence, such as site inspections and coordination with construction parties.

**Skills to build:**

- Proficiency in BIM software (e.g., Revit) and AI design plugins
- Grasp basic programming (Python, Dynamo) to customize automated workflows
- Learning AI-assisted generative design (Generative Design)
- Strengthen engineering mechanics and materials knowledge to review AI solutions
- Enhance cross-departmental communication and project management skills
- Familiar with point cloud scanning and reverse engineering tools

**Key growth areas:**

- BIM
- Infrastructure Development
- Manufacturing Automation
- Green Construction

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary range for a draughtsperson in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and region. Gross monthly earnings for entry-level draughtspersons typically start around 300,000-400,000 HUF, while experienced professionals can earn 500,000-700,000 HUF. In Budapest and with BIM skills, salaries may be higher. Exact figures depend on the employer and project complexity.

**Q: Can a non-EU draughtsperson obtain a work permit in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but it is not a priority occupation. The employer must demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a Hungarian or EU/EEA worker. A job offer is required, and the process involves obtaining a work permit from the immigration office. The EU Blue Card is an option if the applicant holds a university degree and meets the salary threshold.

**Q: Is knowledge of Hungarian language necessary for draughtspersons?**
A: Yes, for the majority of roles, Hungarian language skills are required to understand local building codes, communicate with colleagues, and read technical specifications. However, some multinational companies or BIM-focused positions may accept English, especially in Budapest.
