# Mining Supervisor — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 3121 (ISCO08)**

Mining supervisors in Hungary oversee underground or surface mining operations, coordinating teams of miners and ensuring compliance with safety regulations. They work primarily in the coal, bauxite, and construction materials sectors, with major operations in the Mecsek Mountains and Bakony region. The role requires strong leadership, practical mining experience, and knowledge of Hungarian mining law. Despite Hungary's declining coal mining industry, supervisors remain needed for quarrying and special projects.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a mining supervisor in Hungary?**
A: Salaries for mining supervisors are competitive, often exceeding the national average due to shift work and hazardous conditions. According to 2023 KSH data, gross earnings for supervisors in mining and quarrying range typically between 6 and 8 million HUF annually, depending on experience and location.

**Q: Can a non-EU national become a mining supervisor in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but they typically need a Work and Residence Permit with a job offer. The employer must demonstrate a shortage of qualified local workers. EU Blue Card is also possible for those with a relevant degree and higher salary. Hungarian language skills are crucial, as many safety regulations and team interactions are in Hungarian.

**Q: What are the main safety certifications required?**
A: All mining supervisors must hold a valid Occupational Safety and Health Certificate from the National Labour Office and a Mining Supervisor License from the Hungarian Mining Authority. These require passing exams and periodic renewals.
