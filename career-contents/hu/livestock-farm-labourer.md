# Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 9212 (ISCO08)**

Livestock farm labourers in Hungary perform manual tasks related to the care and feeding of animals, cleaning of stables and pens, and assisting with breeding and milking operations. The role is crucial in Hungary's agricultural sector, which contributes significantly to the economy, especially in dairy, pig, and poultry farming. Labour demand is steady, particularly on larger farms, but the work is physically demanding and often seasonal. Many workers are employed on fixed-term contracts, and the job requires no formal education, though practical experience is valued.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: medium-high (★★★½☆).** National workforce approx. 14,071. The employment outlook for livestock farm labourers in Hungary is stable but with limited growth, as the sector faces mechanisation and consolidation. Demand for labour persists due to a shortage of local workers willing to do manual agricultural work, leading to increasing reliance on migrant labour from neighbouring countries. However, job opportunities may decline in the long term as farms adopt automation and precision livestock technologies. Seasonal fluctuations and dependence on EU agricultural subsidies also affect job stability.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for livestock farm labourers in Hungary is typically limited without further training. Starting positions involve basic care and cleaning tasks. With experience, workers can become senior stockmen, herd managers, or specialists in artificial insemination or animal health. Hungarian agricultural apprenticeships or vocational courses (e.g., in animal husbandry) can improve advancement prospects. Some labourers transition into supervisory roles or start their own small farms, though this requires capital and land.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a livestock farm labourer in Hungary?**
A: Gross monthly earnings are generally around the national minimum wage (2023: approx. 232,000 HUF) or slightly higher, depending on experience and type of livestock. Overtime and seasonal bonuses may apply.

**Q: Can a non-EU citizen work as a livestock farm labourer in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but it is restricted. The employer must obtain a work permit and the worker a residence permit. Quotas apply for low-skilled roles, and priority is given to EU/EEA workers.

**Q: What career progression is possible for a livestock farm labourer?**
A: With experience, you can advance to senior stockman, team leader, or farm supervisor. Further vocational training in animal husbandry or veterinary assistance can open up higher-skilled roles.
