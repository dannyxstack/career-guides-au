# Structural Metal Preparer and Erector — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 7214 (ISCO08)**

Structural metal preparers and erectors cut, shape, assemble, and install metal components for buildings, bridges, and industrial structures. In Hungary, the construction sector is a key economic driver, with high demand for skilled workers due to numerous infrastructure projects and industrial investments. This occupation requires physical stamina, precision, and familiarity with tools and blueprints. Workers often operate in teams and at heights, adhering to strict safety standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, advancing to journeyman and then to foreman or supervisor roles. Experienced workers may specialize in areas like welding, crane operation, or project management. Some pursue further education to become construction technicians or engineers. The trade offers opportunities for self-employment, especially in small-scale projects and renovation works.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Precise dimensional measurement and calibration using BIM and laser scanning
- Optimize cutting and welding parameters for steel structures using AI software
- Drones automatically check structural alignment and bolt tightening status

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted crane and rigging configuration planning
- Risk prediction and construction plan optimization based on historical data
- Augmented Reality (AR)-assisted installation guidance and training
- Real-time tool status monitoring and predictive maintenance alerts.

**Human moat:**

- Physical operation and assembly in complex construction site environments
- Flexible judgment for handling non-standard or damaged materials
- Coordinate with the team and handle emergency situations
- Strict enforcement and supervision of safety regulations

**Skills to build:**

- BIM (Building Information Modeling) software operation
- Drone operation and data analysis
- Programming and maintenance of welding robots
- Use of augmented reality (AR) tools
- Digital literacy and basic programming concepts
- Data analysis in safety and quality management

**Key growth areas:**

- Infrastructure projects
- Industrial construction
- Steel structures
- Renovation

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a structural metal preparer and erector in Hungary?**
A: As of 2023, the average gross monthly salary ranges from 350,000 to 450,000 HUF, depending on experience, location, and certifications. Overtime and allowances can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as structural metal preparers in Hungary?**
A: Yes, non-EU nationals can obtain a work and residence permit with a job offer. Since the occupation is on Hungary's shortage list, the application process is expedited. Guest worker permits may also be available for certain countries.
