# Deep-sea Fishery Worker — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 6223 (ISCO08)**

Deep-sea fishery workers operate fishing vessels and equipment to catch fish and other seafood in deep ocean waters. However, Hungary is a landlocked country with no access to the sea, so this occupation is virtually non-existent in the Hungarian labour market. Related roles exist in freshwater fishing and aquaculture, but deep-sea fishing is not practiced. The Hungarian fishing industry is limited to inland waters and fish farming, with very few workers engaged in offshore activities.

- Fishing vessel navigation and route planning (AIS + autopilot)
- Fish catch classification and initial weighing (computer vision)

**Human moat:**
