# Kitchen Helper — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 9412 (ISCO08)**

Kitchen helpers assist in food preparation, cleaning, and maintaining hygiene in commercial kitchens, typically in restaurants, hotels, and catering services. In Hungary, this occupation is part of the hospitality sector, with many positions being part-time or seasonal. It requires minimal formal qualifications and is often an entry-level job for students or job changers.

Employers often prefer candidates with food safety certification.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a kitchen helper in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary, but gross monthly earnings typically range from HUF 230,000 to HUF 350,000, depending on location and employer. It is often close to the minimum wage.

**Q: Can a non-EU national get a work visa as a kitchen helper in Hungary?**
A: It is possible but difficult. A work permit requires a job offer and a positive labour market test. The employer must prove the position cannot be filled locally. Additionally, quotas may apply.
