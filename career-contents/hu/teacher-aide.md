# Teachers’ Aide — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 5312 (ISCO08)**

Teachers’ aides support classroom teachers in Hungarian primary and secondary schools, assisting with lesson preparation, student supervision, and administrative tasks. They work under the guidance of qualified teachers, often in inclusive or special education settings. The role is essential for managing larger class sizes and supporting students with diverse needs, particularly in public education. Demand is steady due to ongoing teacher shortages and increasing focus on inclusive education.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for teachers’ aides often involves obtaining additional qualifications to become a teaching assistant or, with further study, a certified teacher. Many start with a secondary vocational certificate and later pursue a bachelor’s degree in education. Experienced aides may move into coordination roles or specialize in areas like special needs support. Mobility is relatively low, but the role offers a stable entry point into the education sector.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using AI to generate class notes, student attendance reports, and transcripts
- Automated scheduling and classroom resource allocation
- Answer common parent questions (e.g., school calendar, policies) via chatbot
- Automatically Organize Student Files and IEP (Individualized Education Program) Data
- Recording meeting minutes using voice-to-text tools

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI to Analyze Student Academic Data to Precisely Identify Students Needing Extra Tutoring
- Assist non-English speaking students and parents using AI translation tools
- Generate personalized learning materials via AI (e.g., differentiated exercises)
- Using AI to simulate classroom management scenarios to enhance behavioral intervention strategies
- Using AI to optimize teacher workflows, freeing up more time for instructional support

**Human moat:**

- Empathy and real-time adaptability in handling student emotional and behavioral issues
- One-on-one physical assistance and emotional connection with students with special needs
- Interpersonal communication in unstructured situations (e.g., sudden conflicts, parent meetings)
- Coordination work requiring flexible judgment in cross-departmental collaboration
- Recognition and response to non-verbal signals in classroom management

**Skills to build:**

- Operation and data interpretation of AI education tools (e.g., Classcraft, Knewton)
- Basic data analysis (intermediate+ Excel/Google Sheets)
- Digital content creation (Canva/Adobe Express to produce learning materials)
- AI-assisted communication tools (e.g., ChatGPT generating parent letter templates)
- Classroom behavior management techniques (positive discipline, trauma-informed practices)
- Project management tools (Asana/Trello) for tracking student support plans

**Key growth areas:**

- inclusive education
- special needs
- early childhood
- digital tools

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a teachers’ aide in Hungary?**
A: Salaries are determined by the public education salary scale, generally ranging from near the minimum wage to slightly above, with additional allowances for special needs or location. Annual gross earnings are modest, typically below the national average.

**Q: Do I need a visa to work as a teachers’ aide in Hungary?**
A: EU/EEA nationals can work freely. Non-EU nationals need a work permit based on a job offer, subject to labour market testing. The employer sponsors the permit. If the role is not on the shortage list, approval may be challenging.
