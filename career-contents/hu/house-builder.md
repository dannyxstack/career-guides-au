# House Builder — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 7111 (ISCO08)**

House builders in Hungary construct, renovate, and repair residential buildings using traditional and modern techniques. They work primarily outdoors on construction sites, handling tasks such as bricklaying, plastering, and concreting. The occupation is in high demand due to ongoing housing projects and EU-funded infrastructure developments, but faces a persistent labor shortage, especially of skilled craftsmen.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an apprentice or helper, advancing to skilled builder after 2-4 years, then to site supervisor or self-employed contractor. Continuing training in green building techniques, insulation, and energy-efficient construction can improve prospects. Experienced builders may transition to project management or start their own construction firm.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automatic generation of bill of materials based on CAD drawings
- Basic building structure calculations and optimization (e.g., load-bearing analysis)
- Construction site safety monitoring and anomaly alerts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Use AI to generate multiple design blueprint options for client selection
- Collision detection and construction simulation optimization through BIM+AI
- Using drones and AI for rooftop inspections and damage identification
- Use machine learning to predict material waste and project delays

**Human moat:**

- On-site fine operations such as manual bricklaying, plastering, and carpentry
- Understanding of individual client needs and designing tailored solutions
- Emergency response to unexpected issues on complex construction sites

**Skills to build:**

- BIM software (e.g., Revit) operation.
- Drone operation and image analysis.
- Basic Programming & AI Collaboration Tools (e.g., ChatGPT)
- Knowledge of green building and energy-saving technologies.
- Project management and communication coordination skills

**Key growth areas:**

- housing
- home renovation
- green building
- infrastructure

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a house builder in Hungary?**
A: Gross earnings are competitive with the national average for skilled trades and vary based on qualifications, experience, and region. Check the latest KSH data for precise figures.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a house builder in Hungary?**
A: Yes, through a Work and Residence Permit (job offer) or the Guest Worker Permit if the occupation is on the shortage list. EU/EEA citizens can work freely.

**Q: What qualifications are needed to work as a house builder in Hungary?**
A: A Craftsman Certificate in Construction and occupational safety training are mandatory. Secondary vocational education is the standard path.
