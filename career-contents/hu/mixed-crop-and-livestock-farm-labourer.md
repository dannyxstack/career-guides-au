# Mixed Crop And Livestock Farm Labourer — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 9213 (ISCO08)**

Mixed crop and livestock farm labourers perform a variety of manual tasks on farms that combine both crop cultivation and animal husbandry. In Hungary, this occupation is concentrated in rural areas with a strong presence of family farms and agricultural cooperatives. Workers assist with planting, harvesting, feeding, and general farm maintenance. The role is physically demanding and often seasonal, with demand fluctuating according to harvest cycles and livestock schedules. Despite agricultural modernisation, manual labour remains essential for many small-to-medium operations. EU/EEA citizens have full access to this job market, while non-EU nationals face restrictions due to Hungary's Guest Worker permit limitations for low-skilled work.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is very limited for this role. Labourers may advance to team leader or supervisor after several years of experience, or specialise in either crop or livestock management. Some workers transition to running their own small farm or cooperative. Formal agricultural education (e.g., vocational school) can accelerate advancement, but most remain in labourer positions. The work is typically manual and low-paid, with little upward mobility without significant investment in training.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using automated tractors for plowing, sowing, and harvesting
- Monitor crop growth and pests/diseases using sensors and AI systems
- Caring for livestock using automatic feeders and milking robots
- Use drones to inspect farms and produce yield maps
- Optimize irrigation and fertilization plans based on historical data and weather forecasts

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Operating and maintaining smart agricultural equipment
- Using data analysis to improve crop yields and herd health
- Develop comprehensive agricultural strategies to balance ecology and economy
- Collaborate with AI for precision agriculture management
- Train new employees in using digital tools

**Human moat:**

- On-site diagnosis and repair of sudden mechanical faults
- Unstructured work in complex terrain or extreme weather conditions.
- Animal emergency medical care and behavioural intervention
- Compliance with organic certification and animal welfare standards.

**Skills to build:**

- Agricultural drone operation and data analysis certification
- Use of precision agriculture software (e.g., FarmOS, Climate FieldView)
- Basic electrical and mechanical maintenance skills
- Digital literacy: Excel/data recording apps
- Basics of crop and livestock health management
- Training for safe operation of AGV/robot equipment

**Key growth areas:**

- seasonal labour
- organic farming
- niche livestock
- local supply chains

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Hungary?**
A: Salaries are modest and often near the minimum wage (gross around 232,000 HUF per month in 2023), but may vary by region and seasonal demand. Additional benefits like accommodation or meals are sometimes provided.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as a mixed crop and livestock farm labourer in Hungary?**
A: It is very difficult. Hungary's guest worker scheme favours higher-skilled occupations, and this role is low-skilled. EU/EEA nationals have full access.

**Q: What are the advancement opportunities?**
A: Limited. With experience, you might become a team leader or farm manager. Vocational training in agriculture can open doors to specialised roles.
