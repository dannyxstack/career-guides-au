# Nursing Professional — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 2221 (ISCO08)**

Nursing professionals in Hungary are registered nurses providing direct patient care, health education, and support in hospitals, clinics, and community settings. The occupation is in high demand due to an ageing population, a shortage of healthcare workers, and ongoing healthcare reforms. Nurses work under the supervision of physicians but also perform independent tasks. The profession is regulated by the Hungarian Medical Chamber, and practice requires a valid license. Hungary faces a chronic nursing shortage, making it a target for both domestic and international recruitment, with efforts to improve working conditions and salaries.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression for nurses in Hungary typically starts with a BSc in Nursing and registration. Experienced nurses can specialise in areas such as intensive care, paediatrics, or geriatrics, often through postgraduate courses. Opportunities exist to move into management (head nurse), education (instructor), or advanced practice (nurse practitioner). The public sector offers stable employment, while private clinics and international organisations provide higher salaries. With additional training, nurses can also transition to roles in public health or research.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is a typical gross salary for a registered nurse in Hungary?**
A: Based on 2023 data, nurses in Hungary earn a starting gross salary of around HUF 400,000-600,000 per month in the public sector, with higher pay for specialists or in private clinics. Salaries have been increasing due to government wage hikes, but are still below EU averages.

**Q: Can a non-EU nurse get a work visa for Hungary?**
A: Yes, if they have a job offer and their qualifications are recognised. The employer must apply for a work permit (Single Permit) on their behalf. Nursing is a shortage occupation, which can bypass the labour market test. The process takes several months, and Hungarian language proficiency is often required.

**Q: What are the steps to have my nursing diploma recognised in Hungary?**
A: Non-EU nurses must submit their documents to the Hungarian Health Registration and Training Centre (ETT). They will verify the curriculum and may require additional exams or a bridging course. After recognition, they can apply for a license with the Medical Chamber (MESZK) and then seek employment.
