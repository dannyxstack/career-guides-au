# Stationary Plant and Machine Operator Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 8189 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers a broad range of stationary plant and machine operators not classified elsewhere, including operators of mixing, crushing, packaging, labeling, and other industrial machinery. In Hungary, these roles are prevalent in manufacturing sectors such as automotive components, chemicals, food processing, and logistics. Workers are responsible for setting up, monitoring, and maintaining equipment to ensure efficient production. The position often requires shift work and adherence to strict safety protocols.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★½☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts with entry-level operator roles, advancing to senior operator, team leader, or maintenance technician. With additional training, operators can move into quality control, production planning, or shift management. Many workers begin with vocational training (OKJ) and subsequently specialize in specific machinery or industries. Continuing education and certifications can lead to higher pay and greater job security.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a stationary plant operator in Hungary?**
A: Gross monthly salary typically ranges from 300,000 to 450,000 HUF, depending on industry, experience, and shift work. Overtime and bonuses can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as a stationary plant operator in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but they need a work and residence permit. This requires a concrete job offer and a labor market test. The process can take several months, and the occupation is not currently on the shortage list.

**Q: What training is needed to become a stationary plant operator?**
A: Vocational training in machine operation (2-3 years) or a secondary school diploma with specialized courses. On-the-job training is also common.
