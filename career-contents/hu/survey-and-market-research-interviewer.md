# Survey and Market Research Interviewer — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 4227 (ISCO08)**

Survey and market research interviewers in Hungary conduct face-to-face, telephone, or online interviews with respondents to collect data for market research, opinion polls, and official statistics. They are employed by market research agencies, the Hungarian Central Statistical Office (KSH), and polling firms. The role requires clear communication, active listening, and accuracy in recording responses. Interviewers often work on project-based assignments, with variable hours. Hungarian language fluency is essential, as most surveys target the local population. The job offers entry-level opportunities in the research sector, with potential for advancement to supervisory or analytical roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very high (★★★★½).**

Career progression for survey interviewers typically starts with entry-level data collection roles. With experience, interviewers can move to senior interviewer positions, team leader, or fieldwork supervisor. Further advancement may involve transitioning into research analysis, project management, or specialized roles in business intelligence, social research, or public opinion polling. Additional training in data analysis, statistics, or market research methodologies enhances career prospects. Some interviewers pursue certifications from professional bodies like the European Society for Opinion and Marketing Research (ESOMAR) to boost their credentials.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Standardised questionnaire surveys conducted by phone or online
- Data entry and basic cleaning
- Initial screening and recruitment of respondents
- Execution of interviews with preset scripts
- Summary statistics of simple opinion polls

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Designing more precise questionnaires and sampling plans
- Use AI analysis tools to mine deep insights from data
- Handling complex, semi-structured interviews and sensitive topics.
- Sentiment analysis using natural language processing techniques
- Provide training data and quality verification for AI systems

**Human moat:**

- Ability to build trust and rapport
- Understand and manage complex, emotional respondent reactions
- Cross-cultural, multilingual communication skills
- Strategic thinking for project design and result interpretation.
- Data quality ethical judgment and compliance awareness

**Skills to build:**

- Data Analysis and Visualization (Python/R, Tableau)
- AI tool applications (e.g., ChatGPT, SurveyMonkey AI)
- Questionnaire design and experimental methods
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Basic programming and automation scripts
- Domain knowledge (e.g., healthcare, finance)

**Key growth areas:**

- Market research
- Data collection
- Consumer insights
- Opinion polling

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a survey interviewer in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary by employer and project, but typically range from the national minimum wage (around HUF 232,000 per month in 2023) to slightly above average for full-time positions. Part-time or project-based roles may be paid per interview.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as survey interviewers in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but it is not a typical migration route. Non-EU nationals need a work permit and a job offer from a Hungarian employer. The employer must prove that no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available. Hungarian language fluency is usually required, making it challenging for non-native speakers.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities are available?**
A: With experience, interviewers can progress to senior interviewer, team leader, or fieldwork supervisor. Further training can lead to roles in research analysis, project management, or data processing. Some move into specialized fields like social research or consumer insights.
