# Employment Agent and Contractor — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 3333 (ISCO08)**

Employment agents and contractors in Hungary act as intermediaries between job seekers and employers, handling recruitment, contract staffing, and temporary placements. The occupation is crucial in a tight labour market, where demand for skilled workers outstrips supply, particularly in sectors like IT, engineering, and healthcare. Agencies specialise in matching candidates with positions, managing payroll, and ensuring compliance with Hungarian labour law. With a growing emphasis on flexible work and digital recruitment, this role supports mobility and efficiency in the Hungarian economy.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from junior recruiter or headhunter, advancing to senior consultant, team leader, or agency manager. Specialisation in high-demand sectors like IT, healthcare, or engineering offers faster growth. Experienced agents may transition to HR management, start their own recruitment firm, or move into corporate talent acquisition. Continuous learning in labour law, recruitment technology, and soft skills is valued.

## 7. Who is / isn't suited

**Who is suited**
- People with strong interpersonal and sales skills who enjoy matching people with jobs.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for an employment agent in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary by experience, sector, and location. Agents often earn a base salary plus commission, with total compensation above the national average, especially in specialised recruitment for IT or healthcare.

**Q: Can a non-EU national work as an employment agent in Hungary?**
A: Yes, with a valid job offer and a Work and Residence Permit (Single Permit). The employer must prove the position cannot be filled by an EU/EEA national. Alternatively, the EU Blue Card is available for high earners.

**Q: Is a university degree mandatory to become a recruitment agent in Hungary?**
A: No, it is not mandatory, but most employers prefer a bachelor's degree, especially in HR, business, or psychology. Relevant experience and certifications can substitute.
