# Glazier — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 7125 (ISCO08)**

Glaziers in Hungary install, repair, and replace glass in buildings, windows, doors, and facades. The occupation is part of the construction sector, with demand driven by new building projects and renovation activities, especially in urban areas like Budapest and growing regional centers. Skills include cutting, fitting, and sealing glass using hand and power tools. The trade requires precision and safety awareness. Hungary's construction industry has seen steady growth, creating opportunities for skilled glaziers. Most workers are employed by construction firms or specialized glass companies, and self-employment is also common.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (½☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression for glaziers in Hungary typically starts with an apprenticeship or vocational training, leading to a journeyman position. With experience, workers can become master glaziers, qualify for supervisory roles, or start their own business. Specialization in areas like structural glass, solar panel integration, or restoration of historic windows can enhance career growth. Continuing education and obtaining additional certifications in safety and new technologies are beneficial. Some glaziers move into related fields such as facade engineering or construction management.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a glazier in Hungary?**
A: Salaries for glaziers in Hungary vary based on experience, employer, and region. Entry-level positions may start near the minimum wage, while experienced glaziers can earn significantly more, especially in Budapest. Self-employed glaziers have variable income. Overall, it is a competitive trade with above-average earnings potential for skilled workers.

**Q: Can a non-EU glazier get a work visa for Hungary?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer from a Hungarian employer who must obtain a work permit. The employer must prove that the position could not be filled by an EU citizen. The glazier needs a recognized vocational qualification. The process can take several months, and the permit is typically valid for up to two years and renewable.

**Q: How long does it take to become a qualified glazier in Hungary?**
A: Typically, vocational training takes 2-3 years through a secondary school or apprenticeship program. Adult training courses may be shorter (1-2 years). After passing the exam, you receive a vocational certificate recognized by the Hungarian Chamber of Commerce.
