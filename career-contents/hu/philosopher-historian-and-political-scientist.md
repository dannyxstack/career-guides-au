## 8. FAQ

**Q: Do I need a work permit to work as a philosopher, historian, or political scientist in Hungary?**
A: EU/EEA nationals do not need a permit. Non-EU nationals require a work and residence permit (job offer) or an EU Blue Card if the salary exceeds the high-income threshold (approx. 7.8 million HUF gross annually in 2023).

**Q: What is the typical salary for a philosopher or historian in Hungary?**
A: Salary figures vary widely by sector and experience. Entry-level academic researchers may earn around 400,000 HUF gross monthly, while senior policy advisors can earn 800,000 HUF or more. Official KSH data for ISCO 2633 is not published separately, but averages for social science professionals are around 550,000 HUF gross monthly.

**Q: What are the main career paths for political scientists in Hungary?**
A: Common paths include academic research (university or think tanks), policy analysis in government ministries or EU institutions, and advocacy roles in NGOs. Progression often requires a PhD and a strong publication record.
