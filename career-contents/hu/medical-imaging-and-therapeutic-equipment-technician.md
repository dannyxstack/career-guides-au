# Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 3211 (ISCO08)**

Medical imaging and therapeutic equipment technicians in Hungary operate and maintain diagnostic imaging devices (X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound) and radiation therapy equipment. They work in hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centers, often collaborating with radiologists and oncologists. The role requires technical precision, patient care skills, and adherence to strict safety protocols. Hungary's healthcare sector, particularly in Budapest and regional centers, demands skilled technicians due to an aging population and increased cancer screening. EU/EEA nationals can work freely; non-EU applicants need a work permit, typically via a job offer.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 4,924. Employment outlook is positive due to Hungary's growing healthcare investment and replacement demand from retiring technicians. The government is expanding cancer screening programs and upgrading equipment, boosting demand for qualified technicians. Shortages exist in rural areas, but Budapest remains the primary job market. EU Blue Card holders may fill high-skill posts, while non-EU technicians face a competitive permit process. Overall, job growth is steady, with a focus on digital imaging and radiotherapy.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★★☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from an associate degree and clinical practice. Technicians can specialize in modalities like MRI, CT, or radiation therapy, leading to higher pay. Experience and additional certifications (e.g., in dosimetry or PACS administration) open supervisory roles or teaching positions. Some advance to medical physics or healthcare management, though this requires further education. Private clinics and international hospital chains offer faster advancement. Contract mobility within the EU is possible.

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical gross salary for a medical imaging technician in Hungary?**
A: According to KSH 2023 data, the median annual gross salary is around 4,500,000 HUF for entry-level, rising to 7,000,000 HUF with experience. Overtime and on-call pay can increase earnings.

**Q: Can a non-EU qualified technician get a work visa for Hungary?**
A: Yes, if you have a job offer and your qualification is recognized by the Hungarian health authority. The employer applies for a work permit; processing takes 2-3 months. The EU Blue Card is an option if your salary meets the threshold (approx. 6,000,000 HUF annually).
