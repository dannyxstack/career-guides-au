# Fashion Model — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 5241 (ISCO08)**

Fashion models in Hungary primarily work in Budapest, the main hub for fashion shows, photo shoots, and commercial advertising. The industry is niche and highly competitive, with many models working on freelance or part-time contracts for local designers, e-commerce brands, and occasionally international clients. Earnings vary significantly based on experience, assignment type, and agency representation.

Successful models may advance to higher-profile runway shows, editorial work, or international contracts. ## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a fashion model in Hungary?**
A: Earnings vary widely by experience and assignment. Beginners may earn 10,000 to 30,000 HUF per hour for commercial shoots or runway shows. Established models can earn 50,000 to 100,000 HUF per hour or more for high-profile work. Many models earn irregularly due to freelance nature.

**Q: Can non-EU citizens work as fashion models in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but they need a work and residence permit sponsored by a Hungarian employer. The job must be advertised locally, and the employer must prove no EU candidate is available. The permit is typically tied to the employer and valid for up to 2 years.

**Q: What are the career progression opportunities for models in Hungary?**
A: Models can move into senior modeling roles, become brand ambassadors, or transition to modeling agency work, stylist positions, or social media influencing. Some pursue further education in fashion or business to expand options.
