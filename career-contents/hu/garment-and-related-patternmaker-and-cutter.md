# Garment and Related Patternmaker and Cutter — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 7532 (ISCO08)**

Garment and related patternmakers and cutters in Hungary create patterns and cut fabric or other materials for clothing, upholstery, and similar products. They work primarily in the textile and fashion industry, often for small workshops or larger manufacturers. The Hungarian garment sector has declined over the past decades but maintains a niche presence in technical textiles, workwear, and high-end tailoring. Most positions require vocational training, and employment is concentrated in Budapest and regional industrial zones. The profession is not on the official shortage list, and automation poses a moderate risk to manual cutting roles.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression in Hungary often starts with vocational training, followed by roles as junior cutter or patternmaker. Advancement to senior positions or master tailor is possible with experience and additional certifications. Some workers move into production management, quality control, or teaching at vocational schools. Self-employment or opening a small bespoke workshop is a common path, especially in Budapest's fashion scene. Digital skills (e.g., Gerber, Lectra) increasingly differentiate candidates for higher-level roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Generate tool paths using CAM software
- Operating CNC machine tools for blank processing
- Manual grinding and polishing of mold surfaces
- Measure and record mold dimensional accuracy
- Maintain and refurbish used moulds

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using AI mold design software to quickly generate solutions
- Use simulation software to predict casting defects and optimize processes
- Mold assembly and debugging assisted by AR
- Using data analysis platforms to monitor mold life and quality
- Use 3D scanning and reverse engineering to quickly replicate molds.

**Human moat:**

- Experience in designing and commissioning gating systems for complex castings
- On-site diagnosis of mold failure causes (e.g., thermal cracking, deformation)
- Collaborate with foundry engineers to solve process problems
- Flexibility to handle non-standard, small-batch urgent orders
- Deep understanding of material properties (shrinkage, thermal expansion)

**Skills to build:**

- CAD/CAM 3D modeling and programming (e.g., SolidWorks, Mastercam)
- Operation and setup of multi-axis CNC machining centers
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing molds)
- Mold flow simulation analysis (e.g., ProCAST, Moldflow)
- Industrial robot-assisted grinding and assembly
- Data-driven preventive equipment maintenance

**Key growth areas:**

- Technical textiles
- Sustainable fashion
- Protective clothing
- CAD pattern making
