# Inland and Coastal Waters Fishery Worker — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 6222 (ISCO08)**

This occupation involves catching fish and other aquatic organisms in inland waters such as lakes, rivers, and ponds, as well as coastal areas. In Hungary, fisheries are primarily concentrated around Lake Balaton, the Tisza River, and various fish farms. Workers use nets, traps, and lines, and may also maintain equipment and process catches. The sector is small but supports local food supply and tourism, particularly in rural areas.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: low (★☆☆☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression is limited; workers typically start as assistants and advance to skilled fishermen or crew leaders. Some become self-employed or start small aquaculture operations. Additional training in fish stock management or environmental monitoring can open paths to supervisory roles in fish farms or water management agencies. Mobility to related fields like aquatic ecology or tourism is possible with further education.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Manual net casting and retrieval operations: automatic nets and robotic arms can handle this
- Manual sorting and weighing of catch: machine vision and automatic grading systems
- Manual navigation and obstacle avoidance for fishing vessels: GPS and autopilot systems
- Visual monitoring of fishery resources: drones and remote sensing technology
- Hand repair of fishing gear: automatic repair equipment

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Using drone and satellite data to identify fish locations, improving fishing efficiency
- Real-time monitoring of environmental parameters such as water quality and temperature via IoT sensors
- Use data analysis to optimize navigation routes and working hours
- Using machine vision to assist in catch quality grading and inventory management
- Using digital platforms for sustainable fishery certification and traceability

**Human moat:**

- Ability to repair and make emergency decisions for sudden mechanical failures
- Safe judgment and operations under complex weather and sea conditions
- Local ecological knowledge and traditional fishing experience
- Compliance communication with regulatory authorities and environmental organizations
- Manual processing of high-value catch (e.g., rare fish)

**Skills to build:**

- Automated fishing gear and drone inspection operations
- Fisheries Data Collection and Analysis (Python/R)
- Applications of GIS and remote sensing technology in fisheries
- Fisheries machinery repair and basic electrical skills
- Knowledge of sustainable fisheries management and regulations
- Aquaculture and Resource Monitoring Technology

**Key growth areas:**

- Aquaculture expansion
- Sustainable fishing practices
- Lake management
- Fish processing

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for inland and coastal waters fishery workers in Hungary?**
A: Salaries in this occupation are relatively low compared to the national average, typically below the median gross earnings. Exact figures vary by experience and region.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals get a work visa for fishery worker jobs in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but it requires a job offer and employer sponsorship for a Work and Residence Permit. Since the occupation is not on the shortage list, the employer must conduct a labour market test to show no suitable EU/EEA candidate is available.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for fishery workers?**
A: With experience, workers can become crew leaders, supervisors, or start their own small-scale fishing or aquaculture business. Further training in fish management or environmental protection may lead to roles in water management agencies.
