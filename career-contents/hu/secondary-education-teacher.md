# Secondary Education Teacher — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 2330 (ISCO08)**

Secondary education teachers in Hungary provide subject instruction to students aged 14-18 in general and vocational schools. The profession faces a chronic shortage due to low salaries and an aging workforce, prompting government initiatives like salary hikes and scholarship programs to attract new teachers. Demand is particularly high for STEM, foreign language, and ICT teachers.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: high (★★★★☆).** National workforce approx. 72,037. Employment outlook for secondary education teachers in Hungary is moderately positive, driven by retirements and the need to reduce class sizes. The government aims to increase teacher salaries to match the national average, but budgetary constraints may slow progress. The number of students is declining slightly, but shortages persist in rural areas and specialized subjects. Overall, job opportunities are expected to remain stable with a slight increase in demand for digital skills.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★★☆).**

Career progression typically starts as a classroom teacher, advancing to head of department, deputy head, or school principal. Experienced teachers can become subject advisors, curriculum developers, or teacher trainers. The new career model (Pedagógus I-II, Master Teacher) offers salary increments and professional recognition. Many teachers also pursue roles in educational administration or policymaking.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Automated grading of standardized assignments and multiple-choice questions
- Generate basic lesson plans and course draft materials
- handling student attendance and grade records
- Provide standardized FAQ services

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Analyze student learning data with AI to achieve personalized teaching
- Using AI to generate differentiated practice problems and extension materials
- Enhance classroom interaction and lab simulation with AI virtual assistants
- Using natural language processing tools to quickly search teaching materials
- Use AI to monitor student participation and adjust teaching strategies in a timely manner.

**Human moat:**

- Classroom management and student behavior guidance
- Emotional support and psychological counseling
- Complex teaching decisions and curriculum design
- In-depth communication with parents and the community
- Cultivate students' critical thinking and creativity

**Skills to build:**

- Data analysis and learning situation diagnosis.
- AI teaching tool applications (e.g., Knewton, Carnegie Learning)
- Digital content creation and multimedia teaching
- Programming and STEM education basics
- Online teaching and blended learning design
- AI ethics and data privacy protection

**Key growth areas:**

- STEM education
- digital literacy
- foreign language teaching
- special education

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a secondary education teacher in Hungary?**
A: Gross annual salary for a beginning secondary teacher is around 4-5 million HUF (approx. €11,000-14,000), increasing with experience and seniority. After 10 years, it may reach 6-7 million HUF. Additional bonuses exist for head teachers and those in shortage subjects.

**Q: Can I teach in Hungary with a non-EU teaching degree?**
A: Yes, but you must have your degree recognized by the Hungarian Equivalency and Information Centre (HEIC). You also need a job offer, a valid work visa/permit, and demonstrate Hungarian language proficiency at B2 level or higher. EU/EEA citizens have simplified procedures.

**Q: What subjects are most in demand for secondary teachers in Hungary?**
A: STEM subjects (mathematics, physics, chemistry), foreign languages (English, German), information technology, and special education are in high shortage. Schools in rural areas and vocational institutions have the greatest need.
