# Health Associate Professional Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 3259 (ISCO08)**

This occupation covers various health associate professionals not elsewhere classified, such as dental assistants, medical assistants, and optometric assistants. In Hungary, these professionals support physicians and nurses in hospitals, clinics, and private practices. The role is vital for the efficiency of the healthcare system, with stable demand driven by an aging population and ongoing healthcare reforms.
