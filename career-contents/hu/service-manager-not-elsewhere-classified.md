# Services Managers Not Elsewhere Classified — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 1439 (ISCO08)**

Services managers not elsewhere classified oversee operations in a variety of service industries, such as cleaning, travel agencies, waste management, and personal services. In Hungary, this occupation is part of a growing service sector, with demand driven by tourism, business services, and facility management. Managers are responsible for planning, organizing, and coordinating service delivery, ensuring customer satisfaction, and meeting financial targets. The role requires strong leadership, multitasking, and industry-specific knowledge. Typical employers include travel companies, cleaning firms, and event organizers.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★★☆☆).**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a Services Manager in Hungary?**
A: Salaries are competitive and depend on the specific service sector, experience, and location; generally above the national average due to managerial responsibilities.

**Q: How can a non-EU citizen obtain a work visa as a Services Manager in Hungary?**
A: Non-EU citizens need a concrete job offer from a Hungarian employer who obtains a work permit. The employer must demonstrate the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA national. Alternatively, if the salary meets the high-skilled threshold, the EU Blue Card is an option.

**Q: What career growth opportunities are there for Services Managers in Hungary?**
A: Growth can lead to senior management roles within large service companies, or entrepreneurship. Additional certifications in management or sector-specific skills enhance prospects.
