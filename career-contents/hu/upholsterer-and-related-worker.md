# Upholsterer — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 7534 (ISCO08)**

Upholsterers in Hungary are skilled tradespeople who refurbish and create upholstered furniture, car interiors, and other padded items. The craft combines traditional hand techniques with modern materials and machinery. Demand is steady, driven by the domestic furniture industry, automotive sector (especially in Győr and Debrecen), and the restoration of historic pieces. Apprenticeships through vocational schools and on-the-job training are common. The role is physically demanding and requires precision.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically moves from apprentice to journeyman to master upholsterer. Experienced workers can specialise in antique restoration, automotive upholstery, or marine interiors. Self-employment is common, with many running small workshops. Further training in industrial design or business management opens doors to supervisory or entrepreneurial roles.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Using CAD software for standardized layout and cutting
- Sewing machine operation for simple straight stitching
- Mechanical cutting and forming of foam and filling materials
- Automated recording of inventory management and material ordering
- Repetitive filling and fastening tasks.

**Where AI augments the role:**

- AI-assisted design software quickly generates and modifies soft package patterns
- AR/VR tools visualize final results, reducing rework
- Machine vision detects fabric defects, improving quality
- Digital cutting beds improve material utilization rates
- Smart supply chain systems optimize material procurement

**Human moat:**

- Customized complex patterns and hand-sewing techniques
- The ability to communicate with clients and understand personalized needs
- Experience handling special fabrics (e.g., antique textiles, leather)
- Flexibility for on-site installation, adjustment, and repair
- Comprehensive assessment of furniture structure and comfort

**Skills to build:**

- Learn CAD/3D modeling software (e.g., Rhino, SketchUp)
- Proficient in operating digital cutting and sewing equipment
- Familiar with AR/VR presentation tools
- Develop project management and client communication skills
- Knowledge of sustainable materials and eco-design trends
- Learn basic programming to customize automated workflows

**Key growth areas:**

- Custom furniture
- Automotive interiors
- Restoration
- Sustainable materials

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the gross annual salary for an upholsterer in Hungary?**
A: As of 2023, the median gross annual salary for upholsterers (ISCO-08 7534) is approximately 3,500,000 HUF, according to KSH data. Entry-level workers may earn around 2,800,000 HUF, while experienced specialists can reach 5,000,000 HUF or more.

**Q: Can a non-EU national easily get a work visa as an upholsterer?**
A: Non-EU nationals need a concrete job offer and the employer must apply for a work permit. The occupation is not on the shortage list, so the process is standard but not restricted. An EU Blue Card is unlikely due to the salary threshold. A Guest Worker permit may be possible if the employer participates in specific bilateral agreements, but it is less common.

**Q: Is upholsterer considered a shortage occupation in Hungary?**
A: No, upholsterer is not currently on Hungary's national shortage list (hiányszakma). However, skilled craftspeople in certain regions may still be in demand.
