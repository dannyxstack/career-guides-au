# Protective Services Worker — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 5419 (ISCO08)**

Protective services workers not elsewhere classified include security guards, private investigators, and other personnel ensuring safety of people and property. In Hungary, this occupation spans private security firms, corporate security, event protection, and public safety support. The sector employs a significant workforce, with demand driven by retail, industrial sites, and public events. Entry requires a security guard license (személy- és vagyonőr igazolvány) and often a basic first aid certificate. Workers typically earn around the minimum wage or slightly above, with limited career progression without further education.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★★☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-low (★★☆☆☆).**

Career progression for protective services workers in Hungary typically starts as a security guard and may advance to supervisor, security manager, or private investigator. Gaining additional certifications (e.g., in risk assessment or CCTV operation) can lead to better positions. Some workers move into corporate security or law enforcement. However, without further education or specialized training, upward mobility is limited. The field offers stable employment but few high-salary opportunities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the average salary for a protective services worker in Hungary?**
A: Based on 2023 KSH data, the average gross monthly salary for protective services workers (ISCO 541) is approximately 250,000–300,000 HUF, varying by experience and employer. Overtime and night shifts can increase earnings.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as security guards in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but it is difficult. Non-EU nationals need a job offer and a work permit, and the employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EU/EEA worker. The occupation is not on the preferential shortage list, so obtaining a permit is subject to strict labor market testing.

**Q: What career advancement opportunities exist for security guards?**
A: With experience, guards can become supervisors, security managers, or move into specialized roles like event security or private investigation. Additional certifications, such as in risk management, can lead to higher-level corporate security positions.
