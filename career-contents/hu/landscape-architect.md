# Landscape Architect — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 2162 (ISCO08)**

Landscape architects in Hungary design outdoor spaces such as parks, gardens, urban plazas, and green infrastructure, balancing aesthetics, ecology, and functionality. They work on public and private projects, including urban regeneration, environmental restoration, and recreational areas. The profession requires a master's degree in landscape architecture and registration with the Hungarian Chamber of Landscape Architects. Employment opportunities exist in design firms, government agencies, and research institutions, with growing demand driven by EU-funded green city initiatives and climate adaptation projects.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium-high (★★★½☆).**
**AI replacement risk: medium (★★½☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts from junior designer or assistant, advancing to project lead, senior landscape architect, or office director. Many professionals later specialize in areas like urban ecology, green building certification, or master planning. Some pursue self-employment or consultancy, especially in niche fields like therapeutic gardens or green roof design. Continuous learning in sustainable design and latest software is essential for advancement.

**Human moat:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a landscape architect in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary with experience and sector: junior landscape architects earn less than the national average, while senior professionals with project leadership roles can earn well above average. Private sector and Budapest-based positions typically pay higher than public sector or regional roles.

**Q: Do I need a job offer to immigrate as a landscape architect to Hungary?**
A: For non-EU nationals, a job offer is required for both the Work and Residence Permit and the EU Blue Card. The employer must demonstrate that the position could not be filled by an EU citizen. EU/EEA citizens do not need a job offer for free movement.

**Q: Is a Hungarian license mandatory for landscape architecture work?**
A: Yes, for independent practice and for projects requiring public authority approval, you must be registered with the Hungarian Chamber of Landscape Architects. You can work under supervision without a license for some roles.
