# Pharmacists — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 2262 (ISCO08)**

Pharmacists in Hungary are highly skilled healthcare professionals responsible for dispensing medications, providing pharmaceutical care, and ensuring the safe use of drugs. They work in community pharmacies, hospitals, and the pharmaceutical industry. The role requires a Master's degree in pharmacy and registration with the Hungarian Chamber of Pharmacists. With an aging population and increasing demand for healthcare services, pharmacists are essential to the Hungarian healthcare system.

## 3. Job demand / competition / intensity

**Job demand: very high (★★★★½).** National workforce approx. 6,633. Employment outlook for pharmacists in Hungary is stable with moderate growth. The healthcare sector faces a shortage of qualified pharmacists, especially in rural areas. The government has initiatives to retain professionals and attract foreign-trained pharmacists, but the process for non-EU nationals is stringent due to language and qualification recognition requirements.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: very high (★★★★½).**
**AI replacement risk: medium-high (★★★☆☆).**

Career progression for pharmacists typically starts in community or hospital pharmacy, moving to senior pharmacist, pharmacy manager, or clinical specialist roles. Opportunities exist in the pharmaceutical industry, academia, or regulatory affairs. Further specialization (e.g., clinical pharmacy, oncology) can lead to higher salaries and responsibilities.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- Routine prescription review and drug interaction checks
- Standardized drug dispensing and dose calculation
- Inventory management and automatic replenishment
- Drug information retrieval and simple medication consultation
- Automatic bill reconciliation with insurance systems

**Where AI augments the role:**

**Human moat:**

- Medication management and clinical decision-making for complex cases.

**Skills to build:**

**Key growth areas:**

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a pharmacist in Hungary?**
A: Salaries vary by experience and sector, but pharmacists generally earn above the national average, with community pharmacists earning around 500,000–800,000 HUF gross per month.

**Q: Can non-EU pharmacists work in Hungary?**
A: Yes, but they need a job offer, work permit, and recognition of their pharmacy degree by the Hungarian Chamber of Pharmacists. The process can be lengthy and requires Hungarian language proficiency.

**Q: Is Hungarian proficiency mandatory?**
A: Yes, for patient-facing roles, as communication with patients and doctors is in Hungarian. Exceptions may apply for industry or research positions.
