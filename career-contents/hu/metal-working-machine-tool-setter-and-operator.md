# Metal Working Machine Tool Setter and Operator — career analysis · Hungary

**Occupation code: 7223 (ISCO08)**

Metal working machine tool setters and operators set up and operate machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and grinders to shape metal parts. In Hungary, this occupation is vital to the manufacturing sector, especially in automotive, machinery, and precision engineering industries. The role requires technical skills, attention to detail, and adherence to safety standards.

## 5. Future outlook / AI exposure

**Future prospect: medium (★★½☆☆).**
**AI replacement risk: very low (★☆☆☆☆).**

Career progression typically starts as an operator, advancing to setter, then CNC programmer, production supervisor, or quality inspector. Gaining CNC skills and certifications opens higher-paid positions. Continuous learning is valued due to technological advancements.

**Tasks most exposed to AI:**

- CNC machine programming (G-code/M-code) automatically generated by AI
- Real-time monitoring of processing and tool life prediction
- Automatic measurement and feedback compensation of workpiece dimensions
- Optimization of batch processing paths for standard parts.
- AI-assisted equipment fault diagnosis

**Where AI augments the role:**

- Simulation and optimization of complex part machining processes using AI.
- Using augmented reality (AR) for equipment debugging and maintenance
- AI-driven quality prediction system improves yield rate
- Digital twin technology enables virtual commissioning and training
- Smart scheduling system improves efficiency in high-mix, low-volume production

**Human moat:**

- Experience in debugging non-standard, high-precision parts
- Understand drawing design intent and propose process improvement suggestions
- Hands-on ability to troubleshoot on-site sudden failures
- Programming and operating combined machining (turning, milling, drilling) on a single workpiece
- Cross-departmental collaboration and communication with designers, quality engineers, etc.

**Skills to build:**

- Advanced CAD/CAM software applications (e.g., Mastercam, NX)
- Robot loading/unloading and automation cell integration
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) data acquisition and analysis
- Python/MATLAB basics for processing parameter optimization
- CNC multi-axis (5-axis) programming
- Technical English reading (equipment manuals, international standards)

**Key growth areas:**

- CNC programming
- automation
- automotive parts
- precision engineering

## 8. FAQ

**Q: What is the typical salary for a metal working machine tool setter and operator in Hungary?**
A: Gross monthly salaries range from 350,000 to 500,000 HUF for entry-level, with experienced CNC operators earning up to 600,000 HUF or more.

**Q: Can non-EU nationals work as metal working machine tool setters and operators in Hungary?**
A: Yes, through a work and residence permit or guest worker permit if the occupation is on the shortage list.

**Q: What advancement opportunities exist?**
A: With experience, one can become a CNC programmer, production supervisor, or quality control inspector.
