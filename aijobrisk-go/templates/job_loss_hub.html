Method in one line

jobs lost = workforce × GenAI exposure × automation share × realisation rate. Realisation rates (low/mid/high) reflect how much of the theoretically-automatable exposure actually converts to job loss by 2030 — adoption is partial. Full detail on the methodology page.

Not a prediction. A scenario range for orientation; real outcomes depend on adoption, policy, and job creation that this figure does not net out.
