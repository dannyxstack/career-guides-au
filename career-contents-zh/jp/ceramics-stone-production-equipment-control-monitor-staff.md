# Ceramics/Stone Production Equipment Control & Monitor Staff 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 502 (JSCO)**

Professional who controls and monitors equipment for firing, crushing, mixing, etc., in the manufacturing process of ceramics and stone products. Quality control and process optimization are important.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

AI and automation are expected to centralize equipment monitoring and control operations in central control rooms and advance autonomous operation, leading to staff reductions. However, human judgment is needed for anomaly response and process improvement.

Career path starts from production floor operator, leading to roles in equipment management, quality control leader, plant manager, etc. Obtaining skill certifications aids promotion.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Kiln temperature monitoring
- Crusher operation
- Routine control panel monitoring
- Product visual inspection (replaced by image processing)

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Identifying cause and responding to anomalies
- Quality improvement through production data analysis
- Planning preventive maintenance schedules
- Parameter adjustment for AI monitoring systems

**人类护城河:**

- Ability to assess situations and respond quickly to anomalies
- Quality judgment based on five senses cultivated through years of experience
- Troubleshooting equipment failures and repair skills
- Flexible changeover for high-mix low-volume production

**需构建的技能:**

- Data Analysis (Statistical Quality Control)
- Basic Design of Automation Systems
- Machine Maintenance (Electrical/Mechanical)
- Basic Operation of PLC and SCADA

**主要增长方向:**

- Energy-saving firing technology
- Automation and robot introduction
- Recycling material utilization
- Specialty ceramics demand

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual income for a ceramic and stone product production equipment control and monitoring operator?**
答：Expect around 3-4 million yen in the first year, 4-5.5 million yen after 10 years, and 5-7 million yen for management positions. Passing skill certification exams can lead to salary increases.

**问：What visa is required for foreigners to work in this occupation?**
答：Generally, a 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa is required, with conditions such as a university degree or over 10 years of relevant work experience. Since this field is not covered by Specified Skilled Worker, simple labor is difficult.

**问：What is the future prospect of this occupation?**
答：Due to declining domestic demand and automation advances, the number of workers is on the decline. However, acquiring skills in equipment operation, maintenance, and improvement can lead to career advancement to plant management positions.
