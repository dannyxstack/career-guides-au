# Other Judicial Police Officers 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 449 (JSCO)**

A profession engaged in maintaining order and safety in judicial institutions, including guarding court-related facilities, escorting detainees, and managing evidence.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中低（★★☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

With the introduction of AI and mechanical security, some patrol and monitoring tasks may be automated. However, human judgment is still needed for interpersonal response and emergency decisions, so the occupation is overall shrinking.

Career paths are based on a rank system, with promotion to sergeant, assistant inspector, etc., requiring exam success. Long-term stability can be expected as a Metropolitan Police Department or court employee.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Patrol security
- Checking surveillance cameras
- Managing entry/exit records

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Data analysis for patrol planning
- AI-based facial recognition and suspicious behavior detection
- Remote monitoring via drones

**人类护城河:**

- Human judgment in emergencies
- Counseling and persuasion of detainees
- Complex interpersonal coordination within courts

**需构建的技能:**

- Crisis management skills
- Psychology and counseling skills
- Legal knowledge
- Leadership

**主要增长方向:**

- Courthouse security
- Civil servant stability
- Police officer shortage
- Mechanical security substitution

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary?**
答：For civil servant hires, the starting salary is about ¥200,000–250,000 per month, or ¥3,500,000–4,500,000 annually, depending on region and education.

**问：Is it possible for foreigners to work in this occupation in Japan?**
答：Judicial police officers in Japan are mainly civil servants, and Japanese nationality is usually required. It is extremely difficult for foreigners to work in this field, and there is no standard residence status route.

**问：How can I advance my career?**
答：You need to pass promotion exams to raise your rank as a police officer or take exams to increase your specialization as a court staff member.
