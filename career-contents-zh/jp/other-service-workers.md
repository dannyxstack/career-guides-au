# Other Service Workers 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 429 (JSCO)**

Engaged in various service tasks such as inn/hotel cleaning, waste disposal, and building maintenance.

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中低（★★☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

While robots and AI are replacing some cleaning and waste treatment tasks, areas requiring complex judgment and meticulous work remain for humans. Overall, automation is progressing gradually.

Can start with no experience. Career advancement possible through qualifications, promotion, or specialized skills.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple floor cleaning robot tasks
- Routine waste sorting

**AI 增强的环节:**

- AI-based cleaning route optimization
- Sensor-based cleaning quality control

**人类护城河:**

- Customer trust
- Judgment on complex stains
- Emergency response

**需构建的技能:**

- AI management tools
- Environmental hygiene knowledge
- Management

**主要增长方向:**

- Labor shortage
- Cleaning
- Waste treatment
- Automation

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual income?**
答：Starting salary is about 2.5-3.5 million JPY, with management roles around 5 million JPY.

**问：What visa do foreigners need to work?**
答：Cleaning may fall under Specified Skilled Worker (i), but as of 2024, the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa covers limited roles.

**问：How can I advance my career?**
答：Options include obtaining building cleaning certification, promotion to management, or switching to a specialized cleaning company.
