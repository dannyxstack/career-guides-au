# Ironworker / Steel Fabrication Worker 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 525 (JSCO)**

Cuts, bends, and welds steel to produce structures and tanks; a skilled trade in high demand in manufacturing and construction.

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**

Full automation with AI and robots is difficult, but NC cutting and automated welding robots are reducing simple cutting and welding processes. Advanced skills and on-site adaptability will remain human tasks.

Starts as apprentice, becomes independent in 3–5 years; further welding certifications lead to leadership roles. Factory management or self-employment options.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Routine straight-line cutting
- Simple fillet welding
- Marking and scribing

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Simulation of complex bending
- Precision control with 3D measurement
- Education using datafied expert movements

**人类护城河:**

- Adaptive on-site responses
- Experience-based judgment of distortion and deformation
- Beautiful finish by manual welding

**需构建的技能:**

- NC machine tool operation
- CAD/CAM skills
- Welding robot teaching
- Non-destructive testing

**主要增长方向:**

- Steel frame fabrication
- Shipbuilding
- Plant piping
- Automotive bodywork

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual income of ironworkers and sheet metal workers?**
答：Depending on experience, starting around 3 million JPY, mid-level 4 to 5.5 million JPY, and skilled 5.5 to 7 million JPY. It varies based on certifications and workplace size.

**问：Is it difficult for foreigners to obtain a Japanese visa for this occupation?**
答：Work under the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa is possible but requires practical experience and expertise. The Specified Skilled Worker visa currently excludes this field, limiting transfer routes from technical intern training.

**问：What are the career advancement opportunities in this occupation?**
答：By acquiring advanced welding certifications (e.g., Welding Management Engineer), one can become a site supervisor, quality control manager, or even start their own steel fabrication shop. There are also paths to management roles at construction companies or plant manufacturers.
