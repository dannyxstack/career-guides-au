# Production Similar Worker 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 592 (JSCO)**

In manufacturing processes, occupations involving mechanical operation or manual work for product assembly, processing, and inspection. Ranges from simple repetitive tasks to skilled work.

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量: 较高（★★★★☆）.** 全国从业人数约 2,500,000. The manufacturing industry faces severe labor shortages, especially for skilled trades like welding, sheet metal, and painting. However, there is some risk of replacement by AI and robots.

**竞争度: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**
**工作强度: 中高（★★★½☆）.**

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中低（★★☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中（★★½☆☆）.**

AI and robotics automation is advancing, likely replacing simple assembly and inspection. However, high-mix low-volume production and complex skills will still require human input.

After joining, skills are acquired through OJT, with career progression from skilled worker to leader to foreman. Multi-skilling and certification enable career advancement.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple assembly work
- Routine inspection
- Manual transport

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Use of AI inspection support systems
- Design of collaborative robot work
- Use of digital work instructions

**人类护城河:**

- Advanced manual skills (welding, polishing)
- On-site tacit knowledge
- Troubleshooting ability

**需构建的技能:**

- Robot operation and programming
- Data analysis
- Multi-process handling

**主要增长方向:**

- Labor shortages due to declining birthrate
- Skill succession
- Coexistence with automation
- Utilization of foreign human resources

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度


| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 特定技能1号 (Specified Skilled Worker (Manufacturing 3 fields)) | Obtainable by passing skills and Japanese tests. Max 5 years. No family accompaniment. |
| Subclass 技術・人文知識・国際業務 (Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services) | For specialized/junior college graduates or above in technical/knowledge-based work. Family accompaniment allowed. |
| Subclass 技能 (Skilled Labor Visa) | Covers skilled trades (e.g., cooking, construction). Not suitable for production-like work. |

**PR友好度: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

**PR难度: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual income for production-related workers?**
答：Average is around 3.5 to 5.5 million yen. With skills or certifications, over 6 million yen is possible.

**问：What visa do foreigners need for this job?**
答：Specified Skilled Worker (i) in manufacturing is most realistic. Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services requires at least vocational school graduation.

**问：How can I advance my career?**
答：Obtain skill certifications, multi-skilling, or leadership training for promotion. Opportunities include management or production control roles.
