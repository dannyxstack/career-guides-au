# Postal and Telegraph Messenger 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 701 (JSCO)**

Occupation involving collecting and delivering mail, parcels, and telegrams. Plays a vital role in supporting the communication infrastructure of local communities.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量: 较高（★★★★☆）.** 全国从业人数约 280,000. Mail volume is declining due to digitalization, but parcel volume is increasing with e-commerce expansion, and labor shortages persist due to an aging population, so demand is expected to remain stable.

**竞争度: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**
**工作强度: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较低（★★☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★½☆）.**

If autonomous driving and drone delivery technology advances, there is a high possibility that some collection and delivery tasks will be replaced by AI and robots. Particularly affected by the decline in mail volume in rural areas, long-term staff reductions are expected.

With experience, one can advance to managerial positions or delivery planning roles. Promotions within post offices and job changes to transport companies are common.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Route Planning for Standard Delivery/Pickup
- Sorting luggage
- Delivery completion confirmation

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Dynamic optimization of delivery routes
- Customer Communication (AI Translation)
- Delivery decision during abnormal weather conditions

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual income of a mail carrier/telegraph messenger?**
答：For a regular employee of Japan Post, starting salary is about ¥200,000, with annual income around ¥3-5.5 million, varying by region and overtime.

**问：Can foreigners work as mail carriers?**
答：Yes. From 2024, the transport sector has been added for Specified Skilled Worker (i), and applicants with around JLPT N4 Japanese can apply. However, a driver's license is required for pickup and delivery work.

**问：How can I advance my career from this occupation?**
答：You can aim to become a section chief or branch manager within the post office, or change jobs to a distribution management position at a delivery company. Some people also obtain a large vehicle license and switch to becoming a truck driver.
