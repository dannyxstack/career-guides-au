# Secretary 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 255 (JSCO)**

Administrative role that supports executives and managers by handling scheduling, document preparation, phone correspondence, and information organization.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中（★★½☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

Routine tasks like schedule management and document creation are being replaced by AI and RPA. However, tasks requiring high-level interpersonal coordination, confidentiality, and situational judgment will remain, so overall employment will shrink but persist.

Career progression from general secretary to executive assistant or board secretary; in large companies, secretarial certification and language skills aid promotion.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Automated schedule adjustment
- Standard document creation and formatting
- Initial screening of phone calls
- Data entry and information organization

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Efficient meeting scheduling (using AI assistants)
- Decision support for prioritization
- Information analysis and summarization (AI-assisted)
- Multilingual support (translation AI)

**人类护城河:**

- Advanced interpersonal skills
- Deep understanding of organizational culture
- Handling confidential information
- Adaptability and judgment

**需构建的技能:**

- Advanced Excel/PowerPoint Skills
- AI/RPA Business Application Skills
- Foreign Languages (English, Chinese, etc.)
- Project Management Skills

**主要增长方向:**

- Secretary
- Executive Assistant
- Work Efficiency
- AI Utilization

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for a secretary?**
答：Entry-level is about 2.8–3.5 million yen, mid-level 3.5–4.5 million yen, and senior-level 4.5–6 million yen. For executive secretaries or foreign-affiliated companies, it can be higher.

**问：Can a foreigner work as a secretary in Japan?**
答：For general secretarial work, it is difficult to obtain the 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' visa, but if it includes interpretation/translation or involves advanced duties at an international company, it may be possible.

**问：How can a secretary advance their career?**
答：It is common to improve language and business skills to aim for executive assistant or senior secretary roles. Transfer to other departments or management positions is also possible.
