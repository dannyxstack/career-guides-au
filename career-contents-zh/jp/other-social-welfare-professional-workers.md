# Other Social Welfare Professional Workers 职业分析 · 日本

**职业代码: 169 (JSCO)**

These are professionals who provide life support, consultation, and care plan creation for the elderly and disabled at welfare facilities and community comprehensive support centers.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。


---

## 1. 教育路径 / 周期 / 费用

| 阶段 | 周期 | 费用 (JPY) |
|---|---|---:|
| Vocational school (Social Worker Training Course) | 2 年 | $2,000,000~$3,000,000（Tuition: approx. 1-1.5 million yen per year） |
| University (Department of Social Welfare) | 4年 | $4,000,000~$6,000,000（National/public: approx. 500,000 yen/year, Private: approx. 1 million yen/year or more） |
| Practical experience route (Social Worker National Exam Eligibility) | 5年 | $0~$500,000（Requires practical experience and designated training） |

## 2. 考证难度 / 从业资质

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social Worker (Certified Social Worker) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | 必备 | National qualification. Required for consultation and assistance work. |
| Care Worker (Certified Care Worker) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | 可选 | National qualification. Essential in care settings. |
| Long-Term Care Support Specialist (Care Manager) | Each prefecture | 可选 | Eligible with 5+ years of practical experience |
| Mental Health Social Worker (Psychiatric Social Worker) | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare | 可选 | National qualification. Useful in the mental health field. |

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量: 很高（★★★★½）.** 全国从业人数约 280,000. Welfare demand is increasing with the aging population. Particularly with the promotion of community-based integrated care systems, demand in home support and prevention fields is rising.

**竞争度: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**
**工作强度: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较高（★★★★☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

AI promotes operational efficiency (care plan creation support, document management, etc.), but the core of human empathy and interpersonal support is difficult to replace. AI supplementation is expected to improve service quality.

Career paths: accumulate field experience and advance to chief/manager. Obtaining certifications as a social worker or care worker is advantageous for promotion. Paths to care manager or counselor also exist.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Record keeping and document creation
- Data entry
- Creating standard parts of care plans

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Client assessment
- Care plan formulation (AI suggests candidates)
- Risk prediction and preventive intervention

**人类护城河:**

- Empathy
- Complex interpersonal coordination
- Ethical judgment
- Flexible on-site response

**需构建的技能:**

- Digital literacy
- Data analysis
- Interpersonal communication
- Team collaboration

**主要增长方向:**

- Community-based integrated care
- In-home support
- Support for independence of persons with disabilities
- Dementia care

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass 技術・人文知識・国際業務 (Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services) | Applicable with social worker or care worker certification; requires university degree. |
| Subclass 特定技能1号（介護） (Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Care)) | Obtainable after completing caregiver training or passing exam; max stay 5 years. |
| Subclass 特定技能2号（介護） (Specified Skilled Worker (ii) (Care)) | Eligible from 2023; requires skilled expertise; allows family accompaniment and permanent residency application. |

**PR友好度: 中（★★½☆☆）.**

**PR难度: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：薪资水平如何？**
答：Starting monthly salary is about ¥200,000–250,000, annual income ¥3,000,000–4,000,000. With experience, it rises to ¥4,000,000–6,000,000, but slightly lower than other occupations.

**问：What visa does a foreigner need for this occupation?**
答：With a certified social worker license, 'Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services' is common; for care work, 'Specified Skilled Worker (i) (Care)' is typical. Japanese proficiency (N2 or above) is required.

**问：How can I advance my career?**
答：Apart from aiming for care manager or management positions, obtaining a certified social worker or psychiatric social worker license and specializing in counseling is another path.
