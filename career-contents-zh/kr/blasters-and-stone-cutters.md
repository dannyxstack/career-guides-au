# Blasters and Stone Cutters 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 70511 (KECO)**

This occupation involves extracting and cutting ore or stone in mines or quarries, and processing construction stone, primarily requiring physical labor and machine operation.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

AI and automation technology can replace some stone cutting and processing tasks, but tasks requiring on-site judgment and manual labor still need humans. Automation is expected to reduce simple tasks and increase high-value tasks like machine maintenance.

Starting as a beginner, one can obtain heavy equipment licenses such as excavators and cranes with experience and advance to a crew leader, but large-scale promotion paths are limited.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Manual stone cutting
- Simple transport tasks
- Basic mining processes

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Automated cutting machine operation
- Remote equipment monitoring
- Precision processing control

**人类护城河:**

- On-site safety judgment
- Work in irregular environments
- Repair skills for machine breakdowns

**需构建的技能:**

- Heavy equipment operation (excavators, etc.)
- Automated equipment operation
- Safety management
- Machine Maintenance

**主要增长方向:**

- Stone demand
- Infrastructure maintenance
- Automation adoption
- Aging workforce

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary for stone cutting workers?**
答：For entry level, approximately 28 to 36 million KRW; with experience, up to 42 to 55 million KRW, though it varies by company size and region.

**问：Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
答：E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visas allow hiring in some mining and manufacturing companies, but mostly in manufacturing. Skilled workers may apply for E-7 (Specific Activities) visas, but the process is strict.

**问：Are there mandatory certifications?**
答：No legally required certificates, but having a excavator operation license or blasting management certificate is advantageous for employment and wages.
