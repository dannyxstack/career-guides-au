# 急救医疗技师（Emergency Medical Technician）职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 30711 (KECO)**

Emergency medical technicians (EMTs) are healthcare professionals who provide on-site and in-transit emergency care, assessing and treating patients in the pre-hospital phase.

## 2. 考证难度 / 从业资质

| 资质 | 发证机构 | 是否必备 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 EMT | Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute | 必备 | National license, mandatory qualification under the Emergency Medical Service Act |
| Level 2 EMT | Korean Health Personnel Licensing Examination Institute | 可选 | Limited scope of practice compared to Level 1 |

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中高（★★★★☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

AI cannot fully replace paramedics' core tasks of on-site judgment and treatment, but AI-based decision support tools are being introduced to increase efficiency. Some paperwork and basic information collection can be automated, but human empathy and physical ability remain essential.

Level 1 EMTs can work in hospital emergency rooms, fire station ambulance crews, maritime police, etc., and can advance to professors at EMT training institutions or managers after experience.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- recording basic vital signs
- writing ambulance activity reports
- calculating drug dosages

**AI 增强的环节:**

- emergency decision-making (AI-assisted diagnosis)
- using telemedicine guidance systems
- predicting patient condition (AI algorithms)

**人类护城河:**

- on-site judgment and intuition
- emotional rapport with patients and guardians
- physical emergency care (CPR, hemorrhage control, etc.)

**需构建的技能:**

- using AI-based emergency medical software
- data analysis and decision-making
- telemedicine collaboration skills

**主要增长方向:**

- aging society
- expansion of emergency medical system
- demand for firefighter-paramedics
- infectious disease response

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合**
- Calm and excellent crisis management skills
- Good physical strength and values teamwork
- Deep humanity and strong spirit of service

**谁不适合**
- Those averse to seeing blood or injuries

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of an EMT?**
答：Entry-level about 24-32 million KRW, 5 years experience about 32-42 million KRW, senior 42-55 million KRW, with additional allowances for firefighters.

**问：Can foreigners work as EMTs in Korea?**
答：Yes. Foreigners recognized by the Minister of Health and Welfare can take the national EMT exam and work under an E-7 visa.

**问：What qualifications are needed to become an EMT?**
答：Graduation from a college or higher in emergency medical technology, pass the national exam, and obtain a Level 1 EMT license.
