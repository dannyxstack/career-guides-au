# 营养师（Dietitian）职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 30501 (KECO)**

Dietitians are healthcare professionals who plan menus and manage nutrition for patients and meal service recipients, working in hospitals, schools, food service companies, etc.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**

AI can automate some tasks like meal analysis and nutrition calculation, but patient counseling and personalized prescriptions require human judgment, so it has a mixed impact.

Dietitians start as institutional dietitians in hospitals, schools, or industrial food services, and can advance to head dietitian or nutrition team leader, or specialize as clinical dietitians or public health nutritionists.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Meal nutrient calculation
- Food ingredient inventory management
- Standardization of meal recipes

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Patient counseling record writing
- Nutritional status assessment
- Meal plan optimization

**人类护城河:**

- In-person patient counseling and education
- Personalized nutrition intervention
- Collaboration and communication with medical staff

**需构建的技能:**

- Data analysis
- Nutrition informatics
- Communication skills
- AI tool utilization

**主要增长方向:**

- Aging
- Health foods
- Clinical nutrition
- Dietary management

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a dietitian?**
答：Entry-level approximately 26-32 million KRW before tax, mid-level 32-42 million KRW, senior-level 42-55 million KRW. Varies by hospital and foodservice company.

**问：What visa does a foreigner need to work as a dietitian?**
答：Professional dietitians can work under an E-7 visa, but need a Korean dietitian license, certain experience, and employer approval from the Ministry of Labor.

**问：What education is required to become a dietitian?**
答：You must major in food and nutrition at a junior college or 4-year university, pass the national exam, and obtain a dietitian license.
