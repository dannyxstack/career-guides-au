# Sports Referee 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 42031 (KECO)**

A professional responsible for applying rules and making decisions in sports games, ensuring fair play and operating across various sports disciplines.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

AI assists referees through semi-automated decision systems (VAR, tennis Hawk-Eye, etc.), but full replacement is difficult. Complex decisions and situational judgments still require human referees.

Typically affiliated with sports associations; promotion to international referee is possible based on experience and qualifications. After retirement, can transition to referee educator or administrator.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Repetitive offside decisions
- Ball position tracking
- Simple out/save decisions (tennis, etc.)

**AI 增强的环节:**

- VAR review decisions
- Real-time statistics provision
- Training and education analysis

**人类护城河:**

- On-site atmosphere control
- Ambiguous situation interpretation
- Referee-player communication
- Emotional judgment

**需构建的技能:**

- AI analysis tool proficiency
- Video review collaboration skills
- Deepened rule interpretation

**主要增长方向:**

- Professional sports
- Community sports
- Esports
- Female referees

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary of a sports referee?**
答：For beginners about 24-35 million KRW, senior international referees can earn over 100 million KRW. However, full-time referee positions are rare, most work part-time.

**问：Can foreigners work as sports referees in Korea?**
答：With an E-7 visa, invitations for professional sports referees are possible but extremely rare, requiring Korean language proficiency and qualifications in the sport.

**问：What training is needed to become a sports referee?**
答：A degree in physical education is advantageous, and you must obtain referee training and certification from each sport's association. Typically, training takes 6 months to 2 years.
