# Office Electronic Equipment Installer & Repairer 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 83211 (KECO)**

Professionals who install and repair office electronic equipment such as computers, printers, and copiers.

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中（★★½☆☆）.**

AI and automation play a supporting role in diagnostics and remote assistance, but physical installation and repair require human intervention, so complete replacement is difficult.

Beginners start at small service companies or as freelancers; with experience, they can move to large company partners or electronics repair centers. Starting a business is also common.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple fault diagnosis
- Remote A/S reception
- Parts inventory management

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Precision diagnosis and analysis
- Customer consultation and training
- Complex equipment maintenance planning

**人类护城河:**

- Physical manipulation and repair
- Immediate response to on-site situations
- Building customer trust

**需构建的技能:**

- AI and IoT device knowledge
- Basic network security
- Programming basics

**主要增长方向:**

- Shortage of technical personnel in SMEs
- Increase in digital multifunction devices
- Expansion of IT infrastructure
- Demand for replacing old equipment

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary?**
答：Starting with an annual pre-tax salary of 24-30 million KRW for small businesses, it can go up to 55 million KRW depending on experience.

**问：Can foreigners work in this job?**
答：Yes, it is possible with an E-7 visa if specialized skills are recognized, and the employer must recommend it. The E-9 visa is limited.

**问：Is there potential for promotion or growth?**
答：In small businesses, starting a business is common; in large company partners, growth to technical team leader is possible.
