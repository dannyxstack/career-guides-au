# Civilian Security Guard 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 54121 (KECO)**

Civilian security guards provide security and protection for private facilities and can exercise authority similar to national police.

> **本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在技术移民职业清单上，不能作为独立技术移民提名职业。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中（★★★☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

The impact of AI and automation is limited. AI CCTV, access control systems, etc., play a supporting role, but on-site response and judgment remain human tasks.

With experience, promotion to team leader or manager is possible, and some transition to civil service or police recruitment exams.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple surveillance tasks
- Patrol log writing

**AI 增强的环节:**

- AI control system monitoring
- Emergency response judgment

**人类护城河:**

- On-site judgment
- Physical restraint capability
- Interpersonal communication

**需构建的技能:**

- CCTV control system
- Emergency response protocols
- Basic first aid

**主要增长方向:**

- Security demand
- Private security market
- Aging population
- Security services

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a petition police officer?**
答：Approximately 24-30 million KRW for entry-level, varies by allowance.

**问：Can foreigners work as petition police officers?**
答：Currently, petition police are recruited mainly from locals, and visa issuance for foreigners is difficult.

**问：How to get promoted in petition police?**
答：Through experience and certifications (e.g., security instructor), promotion to team leader or manager is possible.
