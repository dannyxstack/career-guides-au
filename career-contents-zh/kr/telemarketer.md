# Telemarketer 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 61301 (KECO)**

Telemarketers promote and sell products or services over the phone, handling both inbound and outbound calls.

> **本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在技术移民职业清单上，不能作为独立技术移民提名职业。

**职位需求量: 中（★★½☆☆）.** 全国从业人数约 120,000. Due to digital marketing and AI-based call center technology, telemarketer employment is declining, and simple consultation tasks are likely to be automated.

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较高（★★★★☆）.**

With the introduction of AI and automation technologies, simple repetitive telemarketing tasks are decreasing, and counselors are shifting to focus on high-level counseling or complex problem solving.

They typically start as call center agents and can advance to team leader or supervisor positions, or transition to customer management or marketing fields.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Guidance on initial consultation scripts
- Simple order processing
- Survey calls

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Customer emotion analysis
- Real-time recommendations
- Personalized consultation support

**人类护城河:**

- Empathy
- Complex problem solving
- Trust building

**需构建的技能:**

- Data analysis
- CRM system utilization
- Digital marketing
- Foreign languages

**主要增长方向:**

- Outbound telemarketing
- Inbound customer service
- AI-based call center
- Digital marketing

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a telemarketer?**
答：Approximately 24 to 30 million KRW for entry-level, up to 45 million KRW depending on experience and performance.

**问：Can foreigners work as telemarketers?**
答：Possible on a limited basis with an E-9 visa, but Korean language proficiency is required and the employment permit system procedure must be followed.

**问：Can telemarketers transition to other jobs?**
答：Yes. You can build a career in marketing, customer management, sales support, and related fields.
