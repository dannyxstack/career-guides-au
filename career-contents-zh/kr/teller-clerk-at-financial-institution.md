# Teller/Clerk at Financial Institution 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 03241 (KECO)**

Responsible for handling customer financial transactions such as deposits, withdrawals, and remittances at bank or other financial institution counters.

> **本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在技术移民职业清单上，不能作为独立技术移民提名职业。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中低（★★☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★★☆）.**

Tellers are seeing their roles significantly reduced by AI and digital banking. Automated deposit, withdrawal, and transfer systems are replacing simple counter tasks, leading to a decline in full-time jobs.

After joining as a new hire, can be promoted to counter team leader in 3-5 years, then to PB (Private Banker) or back office/branch manager.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Cash Deposit and Withdrawal Processing
- Account Transfer
- Check Processing
- Balance Inquiry

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Customer Consultation
- Financial Product Recommendation
- Fraud Detection
- CRM Data Analysis

**人类护城河:**

- Customer Empathy
- Complex Complaint Resolution
- Regulatory Compliance Judgment
- Building Face-to-Face Trust

**需构建的技能:**

- Digital Tool Utilization
- Financial Product Knowledge
- Basic Data Analysis
- Communication

**主要增长方向:**

- Digital Transformation
- Face-to-Face Demand for Financial Services
- Response to Aging Population
- Complex Financial Consulting

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting salary?**
答：Varies by bank, but the annual pre-tax salary is about 30–36 million KRW.

**问：Can a foreigner get a work visa for this job?**
答：Generally, tellers are not recognized as professionals, making an E-7 visa difficult. However, holders of F-2, F-4, etc., residence visas are eligible.

**问：How about promotions?**
答：Usually, you can become a team leader in 3–5 years, and after 10+ years, you can move to branch manager or back office.
