# Prison Officer 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 24031 (KECO)**

Supports the correction, reformation, and social reintegration of inmates in prisons and detention centers, and maintains order and safety within the facility.

> **本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在技术移民职业清单上，不能作为独立技术移民提名职业。

## 3. 职位需求量 / 竞争度 / 工作强度

**职位需求量: 中（★★★☆☆）.** 全国从业人数约 21,500. Due to policies on expanding correctional staff and a stagnant inmate population, new recruitment continues, but competition rates are high and promotion stagnation is worsening.

**竞争度: 较高（★★★★☆）.**
**工作强度: 较高（★★★★☆）.**

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中（★★½☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**

AI can assist in inmate risk assessment and monitoring, but the core human-facing correctional work is hard to replace. Simple paperwork and CCTV analysis may decrease.

After appointment as a correctional public official, promotion is based on work performance and experience; transfer to the Correctional Headquarters or regional correctional agencies is possible; some specialize as professional correctional officers.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Inmate information entry and classification
- Simple CCTV monitoring
- Regulatory document writing

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Risk assessment and behavior prediction
- Security system management
- Education program analysis

**人类护城河:**

- Psychological issue response
- Crisis judgment and human network
- Trust-building counseling

**需构建的技能:**

- Psychological counseling
- Data analysis
- Conflict mediation
- Emergency treatment

**主要增长方向:**

- Increasing correctional personnel
- Expanding professional correctional officers
- Recidivism reduction policy
- Strengthening inmate human rights

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

**本职业为非技术移民职业。** 通常不在技术移民职业清单上，不能作为独立技术移民提名职业。

| 签证类别 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| Subclass E-9 (E-9 (Non-professional Employment)) | Not applicable; prison officers are public officials and foreigners cannot be employed |
| Subclass F-2 (F-2 (Residence)) | Career special recruitment as a public official is extremely limited to permanent residents |
| Subclass F-5 (Permanent residence) | Permanent residents are not eligible for regular prison officer recruitment; professional contract positions may be possible |

**PR友好度: 中低（★★☆☆☆）.**

**PR难度: 很高（★★★★½）.**

## 7. 适合人群 / 不适合人群

**谁适合**
- People with high stress tolerance and patience
- People with excellent interpersonal and conflict resolution skills
- People with a strong sense of public service

**谁不适合**
- People sensitive to authority and who prefer a free environment
- People with insufficient physical strength or difficulty in emotional control

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the starting annual salary for a prison officer?**
答：Approximately 28-35 million won for Grade 7, including various allowances it is around 35 million won.

**问：Can foreigners become prison officers?**
答：Only Korean nationals can become general public officials; permanent residents are also ineligible for regular recruitment.

**问：Can a high school graduate become a prison officer?**
答：Yes, you can apply for the Grade 7 open competitive exam with a high school diploma or higher, but the competition rate is high and there is a physical fitness test.
