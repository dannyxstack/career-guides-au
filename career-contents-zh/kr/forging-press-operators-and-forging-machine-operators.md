# Forging Press Operators and Forging Machine Operators 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 82311 (KECO)**

Perform forging operations by heating and striking metal materials to shape them into desired forms, combining manual work and machine operation.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中高（★★★☆☆）.**

AI and automation are gradually replacing repetitive and simple tasks in the forging process, but complete replacement is difficult for small-batch, multi-variety production or complex-shaped die work that requires human judgment and experience.

Acquire skilled techniques through field experience, then advance to supervisory roles such as foreman or plant manager, or transition to related fields like press mold.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple repetitive material input and output
- Mass forging of standardized products
- Heating furnace temperature monitoring

**AI 增强的环节:**

- AI-based quality inspection
- Automated forging machine operation and assistance
- Process data analysis for optimization

**人类护城河:**

- Experience and know-how in non-standard, complex shape forging
- Manual skills for die modification and maintenance
- Ability to handle unexpected situations

**需构建的技能:**

- CNC forging machine operation
- Die design (CAD/CAM)
- Robot and automation system management
- Understanding of heat treatment and material properties

**主要增长方向:**

- Metalworking
- Shipbuilding
- Automotive Parts
- Hot Forging

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

| 签证类别 | 说明 |

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a forger?**
答：Entry-level: about 28-35 million KRW; skilled workers with 10+ years: 48-60 million KRW.

**问：Can foreigners work as forgers?**
答：Non-professional Employment (E-9) visa allows simple forging in manufacturing, but skilled (E-7) requires career proof and is stringent.
