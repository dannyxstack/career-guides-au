# Metalworking Control Device Operator 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 82111 (KECO)**

This is a skilled worker who operates CNC lathes, milling machines, and other numerical control devices for metalworking to produce parts.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中低（★★☆☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 中（★★½☆☆）.**

AI and automation are replacing simple data input and tool change tasks, but precision machining and error handling still rely on human technicians.

Starting as an entry-level operator, one can advance to skilled worker, shift leader, or process manager; mastering multi-axis machining leads to higher wages.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple repetitive machining
- Manual tool change
- Basic measurement

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Machining path optimization
- Real-time quality monitoring
- Predictive equipment maintenance

**人类护城河:**

- Ability to handle non-standard defects
- Micron-level tolerance adjustment
- On-site experience-based problem solving

**需构建的技能:**

- CAM software (NX, Mastercam)
- Basic PLC
- Tool life management

**主要增长方向:**

- CNC automation
- Precision machining
- Aerospace and defense machining
- Mold and parts manufacturing

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary of a Metal Processing Control Device Operator?**
答：Entry-level approx. 28–35 million KRW, mid-level 36–48 million KRW, senior-level 48–65 million KRW.

**问：Can foreigners get a work visa for this occupation?**
答：Possible with E-7-1 (Skilled Technician) visa, but experience and qualification requirements are strict. E-9 (Non-professional Employment) is limited.

**问：Is there a required certification?**
答：CNC lathe/milling technician certification is required; having a mechanical machining master certification is advantageous for promotion.
