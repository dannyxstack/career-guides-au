# 牙科技师（Dental Technician）职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 30631 (KECO)**

A dental technician is a healthcare professional who manufactures dental prosthetics, orthodontic appliances, and implants according to a dentist's prescription, restoring the patient's oral function and aesthetics.

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中高（★★★½☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**

AI and automation can assist dental technicians in designing and producing prosthetics using CAD/CAM, and replace some simple tasks, but high-difficulty work requiring final adjustments and aesthetic judgment still needs human skill, making complete replacement difficult.

Most dental technicians work in dental laboratories. After 5–10 years of experience, they can advance to head technician or open their own laboratory. Specializing in prosthetic areas (crowns, implants, orthodontics, etc.) is advantageous for career development.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple model making
- Basic wax-up
- Investing and casting

**AI 增强的环节:**

- CAD design
- 3D printing production
- Shade matching and esthetic evaluation

**人类护城河:**

- Precise manual skills
- Esthetic sense and artistic judgment
- Custom adjustment based on patient oral conditions

**需构建的技能:**

- Digital dental laboratory (CAD/CAM) software
- 3D printing operation
- Implant dental laboratory technology

**主要增长方向:**

- Implants
- Digital dental technology (CAD/CAM)
- Orthodontic devices
- Demand related to aging

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the annual salary of a dental technician?**
答：Approximately 25-35 million KRW for beginners, and 50-70 million KRW for those with over 10 years of experience. Income can increase significantly when opening a practice.

**问：Can foreigners work as dental technicians in Korea?**
答：Yes, it's possible. Through the E-7 visa (Specific Activities), skilled dental technicians can work, requiring Korean language skills and relevant certifications.

**问：What education is needed to become a dental technician?**
答：You need to graduate from a junior college's dental technology department (3 years) or a 4-year university's dental technology department and pass the national licensing exam.
