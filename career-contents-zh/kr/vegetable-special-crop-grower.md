# Vegetable & Special Crop Grower 职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 90121 (KECO)**

Cultivates, manages, and harvests vegetables and special crops (ginseng, mushrooms, etc.) in open fields or facilities, performing soil management, pest control, and post-harvest tasks.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中（★★½☆☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★½☆☆☆）.**

AI and automation support irrigation, environmental control, and pest/disease diagnosis in open-field and facility cultivation, boosting productivity. Full replacement is unlikely, but smart farm tech will greatly improve work efficiency.

Begins with simple cultivation tasks, then may transition to crop switching, farm management, self-employment, or smart farm technician roles with experience.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple weeding tasks
- Manual harvesting
- Irrigation scheduling

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Pest/disease detection
- Growth monitoring
- Environmental control
- Yield prediction

**人类护城河:**

- Subtle growth assessment
- Adaptive cultivation strategies
- Soil microbiome management
- Field experience-based decision making

**需构建的技能:**

- Smart farm system operation
- Data Analysis
- Eco-friendly Cultivation Technology
- Agricultural Drone Operation

**主要增长方向:**

- Smart farm
- Eco-friendly cultivation
- Export agriculture
- Open-field smart agriculture

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average annual salary of vegetable and specialty crop cultivators?**
答：Depending on experience, entry-level is 30-35 million won, mid-level is 35-45 million won, and senior-level is 45-60 million won, with significant variation by crop and scale.

**问：Can foreigners get a job in this occupation in Korea?**
答：Yes. They mainly enter on an E-9 (Non-professional Employment) visa, and after 5+ years can switch to an E-7-4 (Skilled Worker) visa. Overseas Koreans can also use an F-4 visa.

**问：What is the future outlook for this job?**
答：Labor shortages will sustain employment, but smart farm adoption will raise skill requirements. Automation will reduce simple tasks, but skilled workers will become more valuable.
