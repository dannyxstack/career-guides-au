# 潜水员（Diver）职业分析 · 韩国

**职业代码: 70592 (KECO)**

Specialized divers performing underwater rescue, construction, inspection, installation, and other marine tasks, playing a key role in the construction and maritime sectors.

> **本职业为受限技术移民职业（仅雇主担保 / 偏远地区协议）。** 不在独立技术移民清单（如 189/190）上，可通过雇主担保（482/494）、DAMA 或劳务协议移民，通道受限。

## 5. 未来趋势 / AI替代概率

**发展前景: 中高（★★★½☆）.**
**AI替代风险: 较低（★☆☆☆☆）.**

AI and automation technology can replace some underwater tasks (remote monitoring, ROV), but diving functions requiring high-level judgment and physical work remain human-led.

Career progression: entry-level diver → intermediate technician → senior supervisor; acquiring special diving certifications (saturation diving, ROV operation) opens overseas project opportunities.

**最易被 AI 替代的任务:**

- Simple underwater photography and monitoring
- Basic visual inspection of structures

**AI 增强的环节:**

- Underwater welding and cutting
- Precision underwater installation and maintenance
- Structural damage assessment and reporting

**人类护城河:**

- Complex underwater environment judgment
- 突发情况应变能力
- Key tasks with legal liability

**需构建的技能:**

- ROV operation
- Underwater ultrasonic inspection
- Digital work log writing

**主要增长方向:**

- Marine Construction
- Underwater Structure Inspection
- Offshore Wind Power Installation
- Port Maintenance

## 6. 移民路径 / PR难度

## 8. FAQ 常见问题

**问：What is the average salary of a diver?**
答：From about 35 million KRW for entry-level to 90 million KRW for senior, with additional field and hazard pay.

**问：Can foreigners work as divers in Korea?**
答：Possible with an E-7 visa, but a Korean diving license and experience are required, and the employer must obtain approval from the Ministry of Labor.

**问：How do I get a diver's license?**
答：You must pass the written and practical exams administered by the Human Resources Development Service of Korea, usually prepared with 6 months of academy training.
