# 建筑管理员（Building Caretaker）职业分析 · 墨西哥

**职业代码：5153 (ISCO08)**

建筑管理员，也称为保管员或门卫，负责住宅和商业物业的清洁、维护和安全。他们执行扫地、拖地、垃圾处理、小修等任务，并确保建筑系统（如暖通空调、管道）正常运行。在墨西哥，他们通常在公寓小区、办公楼、学校和酒店工作。该职位需要体力、注意细节和基本的问题解决能力。虽然不需要正规教育，但常见在职培训。这一职业广泛分布于城市和农村地区，提供稳定的就业但晋升机会有限。

Career progression for building caretakers is limited. Most start as cleaners and may advance to head caretaker or supervisor in larger buildings. Some transition into property management or start their own cleaning services with experience. Additional certifications (e.g., in basic electrical or plumbing) can improve prospects, but formal advancement is rare. The role offers steady work, especially in urban centers, but is often considered entry-level with low mobility.
