**职业代码：9311 (ISCO08)**

In Singapore, the mining and quarrying industry is virtually non-existent due to the country's small size and lack of mineral resources. Consequently, the occupation of Mining and Quarrying Labourer is not a recognized or employed role in the local labour market. Any similar manual labour tasks are subsumed under construction or general labourer positions, which are subject to strict foreign worker policies. This occupation is absent from official occupational surveys, and there are no training programs or career pathways associated with it in Singapore.
